A Novel Dataset and Framework for Schenkerian Optical Music Recognition

申克分析的光學樂譜辨識：47 頁手寫申克圖（HandSchA，30,780 個符號、128 類）、YOLOv8L 方塊切片＋RF-DETR 整列橫條的偵測集成、讓「共用同一符號的候選連結」彼此交換訊息的拓撲神經網路，以及把語意圖轉成排版 JSON 的規則演算法——語法連結 F1 86.48 對 GraphSAGE 75.49，但最後的排版結果沒有任何量化評估

為什麼挑這篇：第一篇把 OMR 搬到申克分析（Schenkerian analysis）圖上的論文：資料集、偵測、連結組裝到排版轉換整條管線都有。今天是週二，三個主列表最新到 11-23（週一）的公告，比上週落後的情況好；cs.SD 裡跟音樂有關的未讀論文只有這篇，其餘都是語音、深偽偵測與一般音訊。選它是因為它屬於使用者在追的 `omr`，而且方法本身有新東西：連結預測不再逐對獨立判斷，而是讓競爭同一個符號的候選連結先互相「看見」再打分。標成深度分析，評估重點放在那個拓撲網路到底比既有的邊更新式 GNN 多了什麼，以及 47 頁資料撐不撐得起這些數字。Why this one: The first paper to bring OMR to Schenkerian analysis graphs, covering the whole pipeline: dataset, detection, link assembly and conversion to typesetting. Today is Tuesday; the three main lists reach the 11-23 (Monday) announcements, a smaller lag than last week, and the only unread music-related paper in cs.SD is this one, the rest being speech, deepfake detection and general audio. I picked it because it belongs to `omr`, a subfield the user is tracking, and because the method has something new: link prediction no longer judges each pair independently but lets the candidate links competing for the same symbol see each other before scoring. It is marked as a deep dive, and the review focuses on what the topological network adds over existing edge-updating GNNs and whether 47 pages can support these numbers.

動機Motivation

申克分析是調性音樂教學與研究的核心工具，把樂曲拆成多層的聲部進行與和聲結構。但要產出一張排版好的申克圖非常痛苦：先在紙上畫，再一個一個符號輸入 Dorico 或 Sibelius，匯出 SVG，最後在 Illustrator 裡手動調版面。更糟的是，一旦匯出成 SVG 或 PNG，記譜軟體裡原本有的音高、符號類別等語意就全部消失，電腦讀不懂。

作者想要的是一條「手寫申克圖 → 機器可讀語意圖 → 自動排版」的流程。現成的手寫 OMR 做不到，原因很具體：申克記譜借用了一般五線譜（CWMN）的符號，但意義不同——符桿長短代表結構層級而不是時值，連線可以橫跨整頁，符槓會彎曲表示和聲展開，還有一般記譜沒有的 ^ 級數記號與中斷記號 ||。一般 OMR 模型靠局部視窗偵測與兩兩邊關係，正好在「超大符號」與「依賴整頁脈絡的關係」這兩處失敗。

Schenkerian analysis is a core tool in teaching and researching tonal music, decomposing a piece into layered voice-leading and harmonic structure. Producing a typeset Schenkerian graph, however, is painful: sketch it on paper, enter each symbol into Dorico or Sibelius, export SVG, and fix the layout by hand in Illustrator. Worse, once exported to SVG or PNG, the semantics the notation software held (pitches, symbol classes) are gone and no machine can read them.

The authors want a pipeline from handwritten Schenkerian graph to machine-readable semantic graph to automatic typesetting. Existing handwritten OMR cannot do this, for concrete reasons: Schenkerian notation borrows symbols from common Western music notation (CWMN) but changes their meaning. Stem length marks structural level rather than duration, slurs can span a whole page, beams bend to show a harmony unfolding, and there are marks CWMN lacks, such as scale-degree carets and the interruption sign ||. Ordinary OMR models rely on local-window detection and pairwise edge decisions, which fail exactly on very large symbols and on relations that depend on page-wide context.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

資料面最接近的是 MUSCIMA++（Hajič jr. 與 Pecina，2017）：手寫五線譜、符號層級標註、用 MuNG 圖記錄符號關係；這篇沿用 MuNG 格式並擴充了申克符號類別，標註工具也是在既有的 MuNG Studio 上加功能。偵測端的對照是 completeOMR（Yang 等人）用 YOLOv8 在切片上偵測、MuSViT（Penarrubia 等人）用 IMSLP 970 萬頁預訓練的 ViT。這篇的「方塊切片＋整列橫條」集成，本質上是 SAHI（Akyon 等人，2022）那類切片推論的延伸，差別在多加一條保留水平脈絡、專抓長連線與長符槓的分支。

組裝端，Pacha 等人（2019）把 MuNG 組裝當成逐對分類，Efficient Notation Assembly 與 completeOMR 也都逐對打分；MusiGraph（Baró 等人）則用 GNN。論文指出 GraphSAGE 類模型只有節點有表徵、候選連結本身沒有，這個批評成立。但它宣稱的「拓撲神經網路」其實最像 Battaglia 等人（2018）Graph Network 架構裡的邊更新：邊有自己的狀態，先聚合到節點、再回寫到邊。這篇的新意在於用注意力在「共用同一符號的候選連結」之間做競爭式聚合，並配上按對方類別分組的計數損失與排序損失，而不是發明了一種全新的訊息傳遞形式。

On the data side the closest precedent is MUSCIMA++ (Hajič jr. and Pecina, 2017): handwritten CWMN, symbol-level annotation, and symbol relations recorded as a MuNG graph. This paper keeps the MuNG format and adds Schenkerian classes, and its annotation tool extends the existing MuNG Studio. For detection the comparisons are completeOMR (Yang et al.), which runs YOLOv8 on tiles, and MuSViT (Penarrubia et al.), a ViT pretrained on 9.7 million IMSLP pages. The tile-plus-strip ensemble is essentially an extension of slicing-aided inference in the style of SAHI (Akyon et al., 2022); the difference is an extra branch that keeps horizontal context to catch long slurs and beams.

For assembly, Pacha et al. (2019) treat MuNG construction as pairwise classification, and Efficient Notation Assembly and completeOMR also score pairs independently, while MusiGraph (Baró et al.) uses a GNN. The paper's point that GraphSAGE-style models give nodes but not candidate links a representation is fair. But what it calls a topological neural network is closest to the edge update in the Graph Network framework of Battaglia et al. (2018): edges carry their own state, are aggregated into nodes and written back to edges. The novelty is the attention-based competitive aggregation among candidates sharing a symbol, plus count and ranking losses grouped by the other endpoint's class, rather than a new form of message passing.

方法Method

資料集 HandSchA。 3 位專家分析者手寫的 47 頁申克圖，涵蓋 Monteverdi、Pachelbel、巴赫、舒伯特、拉赫曼尼諾夫、Berg 共 12 首作品，30,780 個標註符號、128 類。標註採模型輔助的迴圈：先用 MUSCIMA++ 預訓練偵測器，把五線譜類別映射到申克類別，再讓標註者修正；每修好一頁就加進訓練集、重訓四個模型，產生下一頁的初始預測。實驗用八組隨機頁面切分，另有一組把拉赫曼尼諾夫《鐘》整首留作測試。

符號偵測。 YOLOv8L 在重疊的 1216×1216 方塊上抓小符號，RF-DETR 在全寬、高 768 的重疊橫條上抓長邊大於 400 像素的大符號。合併時先各自做 NMS，再丟掉「落在同類 RF-DETR 大框裡、且靠近切片邊緣」的 YOLO 碎片，最後一起做 NMS。

語法連結。 每個符號取 64 個最近鄰組成候選對。每對先用 Pacha 等人的雙遮罩 CNN 得到視覺嵌入與一個信心 logit，加上表 III 的幾何特徵與類別嵌入。接著把符號當 0-cell、候選連結當 1-cell：每條候選在兩個端點各形成一個「入射 token」，用 K=4 個注意力通道在同一符號的所有候選之間做 softmax 加權，得到該符號的脈絡；再用脈絡更新符號，用更新後的符號與脈絡更新候選，共兩輪。最終分數是基礎 logit 加上一個拓撲修正項。損失除了 BCE，還有按「對方類別」分組的計數損失（例如一條連線應連到幾個符頭）與排序損失（組內最低的正例要高於最高的負例）。

時間群組與先後連結。 這兩個關係在一般五線譜可以從時值推出，但申克圖的符頭符桿不代表時值，所以另外用直方圖梯度提升（HGB）各訓練一個分類器：時間群組先估兩兩符頭同時發聲的機率再做平均連結分群，先後連結則對每個符號只保留分數最高的後繼者。最後演算法 1 用規則把語意圖轉成 Ni-Hahn 等人先前定義的申克 JSON。

主要數字。 偵測（八組隨機切分）：w-mAP50 93.47，對 MuSViT 91.03、completeOMR 87.77；但不加權的 mAP50 只有 62.89 對 MuSViT 62.81，幾乎打平，差距集中在大符號——長邊超過 400 像素的 w-mAP50 是 84.56 對 71.13。值得注意的是 RF-DETR 單獨在超過 800 像素的符號上（67.75）反而比集成（59.07）好，合併規則有損失。語法連結在 HandSchA 上 86.48，對 HGB 76.61、GraphSAGE 75.49；留出作品上 85.70 對 69.64。先後連結 74.70 對最近同族規則 57.01。時間群組的提升最小：94.98 對規則 92.92，只多 2.06 點。從偵測結果出發做端到端，三項分別掉到 80.79、95.04、71.38。

Dataset HandSchA. 47 pages of handwritten Schenkerian graphs by 3 expert analysts, covering 12 works by Monteverdi, Pachelbel, Bach, Schubert, Rachmaninov and Berg, with 30,780 annotated symbols in 128 classes. Annotation uses a model-in-the-loop cycle: a detector pretrained on MUSCIMA++ has its CWMN classes mapped to Schenkerian ones, annotators correct its output, and each corrected page joins the training set, the four models are retrained, and they produce initial predictions for the next page. Experiments use eight random page splits plus one split that holds out all of Rachmaninov's The Bells.

Symbol detection. YOLOv8L detects small symbols on overlapping 1216×1216 tiles, and RF-DETR detects symbols whose longest side exceeds 400 pixels on overlapping full-width strips 768 pixels high. Merging applies NMS within each branch, drops YOLO fragments near tile edges that lie inside a same-class RF-DETR box, and then applies NMS jointly.

Syntax links. Each symbol takes its 64 nearest neighbours as candidate pairs. Each pair gets a visual embedding and a confidence logit from the two-mask CNN of Pacha et al., plus the geometry features of Table III and class embeddings. Symbols are then treated as 0-cells and candidate links as 1-cells: each candidate forms an incidence token at each endpoint, and K=4 attention channels take a softmax over all candidates at the same symbol to give that symbol a context. The context updates the symbol, and the updated symbols and contexts update the candidates, for two rounds. The final score is the base logit plus a topology correction. Besides BCE, the loss includes a count loss grouped by the other endpoint's class (e.g. how many noteheads a slur should join) and a ranking loss (the lowest positive in a group should beat the highest negative).

Time groups and precedence links. In CWMN both follow from durations, but in Schenkerian graphs noteheads and stems do not encode duration, so each gets a histogram gradient-boosting (HGB) classifier: time groups estimate pairwise simultaneity and then use average-linkage clustering, and precedence keeps only the top-scoring successor per symbol. Algorithm 1 then converts the semantic graph by rules into the Schenkerian JSON previously defined by Ni-Hahn et al.

Main numbers. Detection (eight random splits): w-mAP50 93.47 versus MuSViT 91.03 and completeOMR 87.77, but unweighted mAP50 is 62.89 versus MuSViT's 62.81, essentially a tie; the gap is concentrated in large symbols, where w-mAP50 for symbols longer than 400 pixels is 84.56 versus 71.13. Notably, RF-DETR alone beats the ensemble on symbols longer than 800 pixels (67.75 versus 59.07), so the merge rule loses something. Syntax links on HandSchA reach 86.48 versus HGB 76.61 and GraphSAGE 75.49, and 85.70 versus 69.64 on the held-out work. Precedence links reach 74.70 versus 57.01 for the nearest-same-family rule. Time groups improve least: 94.98 versus 92.92 for a rule, only 2.06 points. Starting end to end from detected symbols, the three drop to 80.79, 95.04 and 71.38.

限制Limitations

資料量與切分。 47 頁、3 位分析者是這篇最根本的限制。八組隨機切分是按「頁」切，同一首作品、同一位分析者的筆跡會同時出現在訓練與測試；真正的泛化證據只有留出一首作品的那組，測試只有 7 頁，而且只跑兩次。留出作品上偵測的 mAP50（73.53）反而比隨機切分（62.89）高，說明單一作品的難度差異大到足以蓋過切分方式的影響，這種情況下很難說數字代表一般表現。

最後一哩沒有評估。 論文標題與摘要的賣點之一是第一個把語意申克圖轉成排版格式的演算法，但演算法 1 完全沒有量化結果：沒有轉出來的音高、聲部、層級對了多少，也沒有跟人工排版比較。而它的規則很脆弱——聲部歸屬看第一個空心符頭的符桿方向，結構層級看符桿尖端的相對高度——再加上前一步的先後連結 F1 只有 74.70，約四分之一的連結是錯的，聲部序列一斷，後面的排版就跟著錯。

拓撲網路的消融不夠。 對照組有 pair MLP（沒有脈絡）與 GraphSAGE（只有節點脈絡），這兩個算是間接消融；但沒有直接拿掉計數損失、排序損失或注意力聚合各自掉多少。在 HandSchA 上 HGB 76.61 與 GraphSAGE 75.49 幾乎一樣，而這篇模型多了好幾個成分，到底是拓撲訊息傳遞、雙遮罩 CNN 特徵還是那兩個分組損失在出力，讀者無從分辨。

範圍。 只處理「純」申克圖，混合記譜（直接畫在總譜上）明說留待未來。標註者是三位專家，但論文沒說他們之間的一致性，也沒說同一頁讓兩人標會差多少；先後連結這種帶有詮釋成分的關係，標註本身的不確定性可能不小。

Data size and splits. 47 pages from 3 analysts is the fundamental limitation. The eight random splits are by page, so the same work and the same analyst's handwriting appear in both training and test; the only real generalization evidence is the held-out work, with 7 test pages and two runs. Detection mAP50 on the held-out work (73.53) is actually higher than on the random splits (62.89), which shows that difficulty varies enough between works to outweigh the split protocol, making it hard to read these numbers as typical performance.

The last mile is unevaluated. One selling point in the title and abstract is the first algorithm to convert semantic Schenkerian graphs into typeset notation, yet Algorithm 1 has no quantitative result at all: no accuracy for the recovered pitches, voices or levels, and no comparison with manual typesetting. Its rules are also fragile (voice assignment follows the stem direction of the first hollow notehead, and structural level follows relative stem-tip height), and the preceding precedence step reaches only 74.70 F1, so roughly a quarter of the links are wrong; once a voice sequence breaks, the typesetting downstream breaks with it.

Thin ablation of the topological network. The pair MLP (no context) and GraphSAGE (node context only) act as indirect ablations, but there is no direct removal of the count loss, ranking loss or attention aggregation to show what each contributes. On HandSchA, HGB (76.61) and GraphSAGE (75.49) are nearly identical, while this model adds several components, so readers cannot tell whether the topological message passing, the two-mask CNN features or the two grouped losses do the work.

Scope. Only pure Schenkerian graphs are handled; mixed notation drawn on the score is explicitly left for future work. The three annotators are experts, but the paper reports no inter-annotator agreement or how much two people would differ on the same page; for interpretive relations such as precedence, annotation uncertainty itself may be substantial.

討論Discussion

這篇最值得記住的是連結組裝的設計思路，而不是那個 47 頁的資料集。在一般五線譜上它已經站得住：MUSCIMA++ 96.68、DoReMi 94.11，都贏過調得不錯的通用基線，而且優勢在排除譜線類連結後還變大（90.95 → 94.69）。這代表「讓競爭同一符號的候選互相看見」對任何 MuNG 式的組裝都有用，不限於申克分析。下一步自然是把它接到 OmniOMR 那類有先後連結標註的大型資料上，看邊數多一個量級時注意力聚合的成本與效果。

至於申克 OMR 本身，論文誠實地承認偵測在全部訓練頁時還在上升，目前的瓶頸是資料。我會想看的是：與其擴充手寫頁面，能不能先用已有的 Ni-Hahn 等人的申克 JSON 資料反向渲染出合成頁面來預訓練——正如 Smashcima 對五線譜做的那樣——因為三位分析者的筆跡很難代表整個學界的手寫習慣。另一個值得注意的細節是附錄寫明 AI 助理參與了程式撰寫、訓練排程與結果計算，作者聲明已人工檢查；對一篇以小資料、多組切分為主的論文，公開的程式碼庫會是驗證數字最直接的方式。

What is worth remembering here is the design idea for link assembly rather than the 47-page dataset. It already holds up on CWMN: 96.68 on MUSCIMA++ and 94.11 on DoReMi, beating well-tuned general baselines, and the lead grows once staff-related links are excluded (90.95 to 94.69). That means letting candidates that compete for one symbol see each other helps any MuNG-style assembly, not only Schenkerian analysis. The natural next step is applying it to large datasets with precedence annotation, such as OmniOMR's, to see the cost and benefit of attention aggregation when there are an order of magnitude more edges.

As for Schenkerian OMR itself, the paper honestly admits that detection is still improving at the full training set, so data is the bottleneck. What I would want to see is whether, instead of only adding handwritten pages, the existing Schenkerian JSON of Ni-Hahn et al. could be rendered back into synthetic pages for pretraining, as Smashcima does for CWMN, since three analysts' handwriting can hardly represent the field. One more detail: the acknowledgment states that AI assistants helped write code, schedule training and compute results, which the authors say they checked manually. For a small-data paper built on many splits, the public repository is the most direct way to verify the numbers.

The review focuses on how many of its wins over Legato 2 are measured with the same ruler, and on how low the layout metrics themselves are.

動機Motivation

一份可編輯的樂譜不只是音符序列：歌詞音節掛在音符上、小節組成譜行、換頁決定演奏者翻頁的位置。端到端 OMR 把影像翻成符號序列時，翻成什麼格式就決定了哪些資訊能留下來。作者指出目前的選擇都有缺口：ABC 能表達音符、歌詞與部分排版指令，但轉錄時省略的屬性要由渲染程式補預設值；Legato 1 把文字標註換成佔位符，Legato 2 的訓練資料乾脆不含歌詞。

MusicXML 能明確寫出符桿方向、符頭樣式、小節寬度與換行換頁，但直接預測完整標記會重複大量元素與屬性名稱，序列長到不可行——在 1,000 份完整樂譜上，MusicXML 平均 352,228 個 token。這篇要解的就是這個兩難：能不能保留 MusicXML 的資訊量，又讓序列長度接近 ABC。

An editable score is more than a note sequence: lyric syllables attach to notes, measures form systems, and page breaks decide where a performer turns the page. When end-to-end OMR translates an image into a symbolic sequence, the target format decides which of these survive. The authors argue that current choices all have gaps: ABC can express notes, lyrics and some formatting directives, but attributes omitted in transcription must be filled with renderer defaults; Legato 1 replaces text annotations with placeholders, and Legato 2's training data excludes lyrics altogether.

MusicXML can state stem direction, notehead style, measure width and system or page breaks explicitly, but predicting the full markup repeats element and attribute names so often that sequences become infeasibly long: across 1,000 complete scores, MusicXML averages 352,228 tokens. The paper addresses this dilemma: keep the information of MusicXML while bringing the sequence length close to ABC.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的對照是 Legato 1 與 Legato 2（Yang 等人，2026），它們用 ABC 當輸出目標，在 OpenScore 上建立了大規模印刷 OMR 的基準；這篇完全沿用它們的評估設定。另一條線是 Sheet Music Transformer（Ríos-Vila 等人，2024）及其全頁鋼琴譜版本，輸出 Humdrum kern 系的序列，同樣不保留版面。

論文宣稱 Cecilia 開啟了新方向，但最像它的既有工作其實是 Mayer 等人（2024）的線性化 MusicXML（LMX）：同樣是把 MusicXML 的樹壓成適合序列模型的 token，論文只在相關研究裡一句帶過。兩者真正的差別在取捨：LMX 刻意丟掉版面與呈現屬性以縮短序列，Cecilia 反其道而行，保留座標、換行換頁與歌詞，並宣稱在 1,000 份樂譜上可以逐位元組還原規範化後的樹。另外「把長輸出拆成多段、各段共用同一份影像編碼」的做法，概念上接近 Nougat、Pix2Struct 這類文件理解模型處理長文件的分塊策略，只是這裡把切點對齊到小節邊界，並另加一個預測段數的分類頭。

The most direct comparisons are Legato 1 and Legato 2 (Yang et al., 2026), which use ABC as the output target and established large-scale typeset OMR benchmarks on OpenScore; this paper reuses their evaluation setup wholesale. Another line is the Sheet Music Transformer (Ríos-Vila et al., 2024) and its full-page pianoform version, which emit Humdrum kern-style sequences and likewise discard layout.

The paper says Cecilia opens a new direction, but its closest precedent is the Linearized MusicXML (LMX) of Mayer et al. (2024), which also compresses the MusicXML tree into tokens suited to sequence models and gets only a single sentence in the related work. The real difference is the trade-off: LMX deliberately drops layout and presentation attributes to shorten sequences, while Cecilia keeps coordinates, breaks and lyrics and claims exact reconstruction of the canonicalised tree on 1,000 scores. Splitting a long output into segments that share one image encoding is conceptually close to how document models such as Nougat and Pix2Struct chunk long documents; here the cuts are aligned to measure boundaries and a separate head predicts the number of segments.

方法Method

Cecilia 記法。 先把 MusicXML 規範化：移除來源資訊與部分 MIDI 播放欄位，保留音符、歌詞、標題、版權文字與版面（包含不列印的元素）。常見元素與屬性換成固定短碼，罕見結構走通用的括號形式；水平座標改存為與同譜表前一音的差值，垂直座標只有在能從譜表基線與音高精確推回時才省略。同一個 BPE 分詞器下，平均長度 ABC 11,176、Cecilia 12,702、MusicXML 352,228 個 token，也就是 Cecilia 是 ABC 的 1.14 倍、MusicXML 是 Cecilia 的 27.73 倍。1,000 份樂譜（895 首藝術歌曲、105 首四重奏）全部能逐一還原。

模型。 約 20 億參數：12 層 ViT 編碼 1024×1024 輸入，投影成 4096×2048 的視覺記憶；28 層、寬 2048 的解碼器。輸出分成「後設資料」與「音樂」兩種單元，音樂按小節邊界切段，每段最多 8,192 個 token、帶有自己的 divisions 邊界狀態；段數由一個 128 類的分類頭先預測。每段都從 BOS 開始、有獨立的自注意力快取，但交叉注意力共用同一份視覺記憶。

訓練與解碼。 三階段：先在 NotaGen 的約 160 萬首預訓練語料（轉成 Cecilia，保留歌詞）上預訓練，再用 OpenScore 與 CVC-MUSCIMA 做監督式微調（SFT），最後做 GRPO，獎勵是 0.95×記譜正確度＋0.05×格式有效率。推論時同時產生無約束候選與「文法前綴」約束候選（每個 token 都必須延伸出合法的前綴），以「能否編譯成合法 XML」挑選，再做確定性的結構修復。這個挑選步驟本身就把 OpenScore 平均 OMR-NED 從 0.2591 降到 0.2232。

主要數字。 相機四重奏 0.3160 → 0.2307、渲染藝術歌曲 0.2760 → 0.1839、相機藝術歌曲 0.4360 → 0.2270（前者是 Legato 2），四條件平均 0.2998 → 0.2232。版面方面，渲染四重奏的換行 F1 0.6829 對直接 XML 的 Astra 0.4404，小節 IoU 0.2340 對 0.0193。

Cecilia notation. MusicXML is first canonicalised: provenance and some MIDI playback fields are removed, while notes, lyrics, titles, credits and layout (including non-printing elements) are kept. Common elements and attributes become fixed short codes, and rare structures use a generic parenthesised form; horizontal positions are stored as differences from the previous note on the same staff, and vertical positions are omitted only when they can be recovered exactly from the staff baseline and pitch. Under one BPE tokeniser, mean lengths are 11,176 tokens for ABC, 12,702 for Cecilia and 352,228 for MusicXML, so Cecilia is 1.14 times ABC and MusicXML is 27.73 times Cecilia. All 1,000 scores (895 Lieder, 105 quartets) reconstruct exactly.

Model. About 2 billion parameters: a 12-block ViT encodes a 1024×1024 input into a 4096×2048 visual memory, followed by a 28-block decoder of width 2048. Output is split into metadata and music units; music is cut at measure boundaries into segments of at most 8,192 tokens, each carrying its own divisions boundary state, and a 128-class head predicts the segment count first. Each segment starts at BOS with its own self-attention cache, while cross-attention reuses the same visual memory.

Training and decoding. Three stages: pretraining on the roughly 1.6 million pieces of the NotaGen corpus (converted to Cecilia with lyrics kept), supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on OpenScore and CVC-MUSCIMA, then GRPO with reward 0.95 × notation accuracy + 0.05 × format validity. At inference the model produces an unconstrained candidate and a grammar-prefix candidate (every token must extend a valid prefix), selects by whether the output compiles to valid XML, and applies deterministic structural recovery. This selection step alone lowers OpenScore macro OMR-NED from 0.2591 to 0.2232.

Main numbers. Camera Quartets 0.3160 to 0.2307, Rendered Lieder 0.2760 to 0.1839 and Camera Lieder 0.4360 to 0.2270 (the first figure is Legato 2's), for a four-condition macro of 0.2998 to 0.2232. For layout, system-break F1 on Rendered Quartets is 0.6829 against 0.4404 for direct-XML Astra, and measure IoU is 0.2340 against 0.0193.

限制Limitations

贏 Legato 2 的那把尺。 Legato 2 的四個數字是從論文抄來的，沒有重跑，也沒有預測檔可以算其他指標；這次比較只在 OMR-NED 一項、四個條件上成立。在四個條件裡，渲染弦樂四重奏 Legato 2 是 0.1710、這篇是 0.2513，明顯較差。另外三個 API 基線（GPT-6 Astra、Claude Fable 5.1、Gemini 4 Argon）作者自己在附錄承認沒有固定的模型快照、完整提示與重試紀錄，GPT-6 Astra 的直接 XML 基線還是透過一個第三方轉接端點呼叫的，這些數字最多只能當參考。

手寫與歌詞。 手寫 CVC-MUSCIMA 上這篇 0.5257、直接輸出 XML 的 Astra 0.3297，差距很大；而 CVC-MUSCIMA 是 50 位抄譜者各抄同一批 20 頁，論文雖然按作品切分，同一批人的筆跡仍會同時出現在訓練與測試。保留歌詞的條件下，相機影像的歌詞 CER 是 50.61%，等於一半的字元錯；渲染影像也有 21.03%。

版面指標絕對值很低。 換行 F1 與小節 IoU 有贏，但渲染四重奏的譜表 IoU 只有 0.0240、文字 IoU 0.0466，相機四重奏的換頁 F1 只有 0.1039。也就是說，模型常常能把換行放在對的小節，但譜表與文字在頁面上的位置幾乎都對不上。表 3 也顯示預測的版面讓換頁 F1 從 0.6667 掉到 0.5417、譜表 IoU 從 0.1043 掉到 0.0802——版面指令不是全面勝出。

GRPO 與獎勵研究的樣本太小。 獎勵設計比較每條件只有 8 頁、只做 7 次更新，作者自己也說只是探索性。附錄表 S6 還顯示一個正文沒強調的現象：渲染藝術歌曲在預訓練基底模型是 0.1308，SFT 後變 0.2187、GRPO 後 0.1839，都比基底差；渲染四重奏從 SFT 的 0.2372 經 GRPO 變成 0.2513。另外無效輸出不少：相機四重奏 252 頁有 22 頁、相機有歌詞藝術歌曲 64 頁有 13 頁無法被評估器使用。

The ruler behind the Legato 2 win. Legato 2's four numbers are copied from its paper, not rerun, and there are no prediction files for other metrics, so the comparison holds only for OMR-NED on four conditions. Within those four, Legato 2 scores 0.1710 on Rendered String Quartets against this paper's 0.2513, clearly better. For the three API baselines (GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Gemini 4 Argon), the appendix itself admits there are no fixed model snapshots or complete prompt and retry records, and the direct-XML Astra baseline was called through a third-party relay endpoint, so these numbers are at best indicative.

Handwriting and lyrics. On handwritten CVC-MUSCIMA this paper scores 0.5257 against 0.3297 for direct-XML Astra, a large gap. CVC-MUSCIMA consists of the same 20 pages copied by each of 50 writers, so even though the paper splits by work, the same writers' hands appear in training and test. With lyrics retained, lyric CER on camera images is 50.61%, meaning half the characters are wrong; on rendered images it is 21.03%.

Low absolute layout scores. System-break F1 and measure IoU improve, but on Rendered Quartets staff IoU is only 0.0240 and text IoU 0.0466, and page-break F1 on Camera Quartets is only 0.1039. In other words, the model often puts a system break at the right measure, yet staff and text positions on the page almost never line up. Table 3 also shows predicted layout lowering page-break F1 from 0.6667 to 0.5417 and staff IoU from 0.1043 to 0.0802, so layout instructions do not win across the board.

GRPO and reward studies are too small. The reward comparison uses 8 pages per condition and 7 updates, which the authors call exploratory. Supplementary Table S6 also shows something the main text does not stress: Rendered Lieder scores 0.1308 with the pretrained base model, 0.2187 after SFT and 0.1839 after GRPO, both worse than the base; Rendered Quartets goes from 0.2372 after SFT to 0.2513 after GRPO. Invalid outputs are also common: 22 of 252 Camera Quartets pages and 13 of 64 Camera Lieder-with-lyrics pages cannot be used by the evaluator.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的不是贏了誰，而是表 3 那個乾淨的對照：同一批 48 頁、音符與歌詞完全相同，只差有沒有預測出來的版面指令，換行 F1 就從 0.2063 變成 0.6917。這證明了版面資訊值得放進 OMR 的輸出目標，而且 Cecilia 的長度代價很小（只比 ABC 長 14%）。對想做「掃描樂譜 → 可直接印給演奏者的版本」的人，這是少見有實際數字支撐的論證。

但它還離「讀者看到的頁面一樣」很遠：作者自己也說版面重建還有很大進步空間。我會想看兩件事。第一，把 Cecilia 記法和模型拆開評估——拿同一個模型分別訓練 ABC、LMX 與 Cecilia 目標，才知道 0.2232 有多少來自表示法、多少來自 20 億參數與 160 萬首預訓練曲目。第二，GRPO 的效果應該用全部頁面而不是每條件 8 頁來判斷；目前看來它在某些條件有幫助，在另一些條件讓分數退步，作為訓練流程的必要步驟證據還不夠。

The most valuable thing here is not who it beats but the clean comparison in Table 3: on the same 48 pages, with identical notes and lyrics and only the predicted layout instructions differing, system-break F1 goes from 0.2063 to 0.6917. That shows layout information is worth putting into the OMR target, and Cecilia's length cost is small (only 14% longer than ABC). For anyone wanting to go from scanned score to an edition that can be handed straight to performers, this is a rare argument with actual numbers behind it.

It is still far from reproducing the page a reader sees, as the authors themselves acknowledge. I would want two things. First, evaluate the notation and the model separately: train the same model on ABC, LMX and Cecilia targets to learn how much of 0.2232 comes from the representation and how much from 2 billion parameters and 1.6 million pretraining pieces. Second, judge GRPO on all pages rather than eight per condition; so far it helps some conditions and hurts others, which is not yet enough evidence that it belongs in the training recipe.

I chose it because it asks the most common practical question: when a pretrained model meets a new collection and there is only enough labour to annotate a few dozen items, which ones should be annotated? That is a different angle from the other two papers (a new notation system, a new output format), so together the three cover data, representation and annotation cost in OMR. The method is a systematic comparison of existing active-learning strategies with no new architecture, so it is not marked as a deep dive; the review focuses on how many numbers back the claim that confidence-based selection is comparable to selecting by true error.

動機Motivation

端到端 OMR 模型在沒見過的館藏上表現會大幅退步，常見解法是拿目標館藏的標註資料微調。問題是標註很貴：古譜、手稿的字形、墨跡、版面每一批都不同，而且需要懂記譜的人來標。合成資料與資料增強可以減輕需求，但作者認為要做到可用的準確度，終究還是需要真實的標註樣本。

所以這篇把問題換成：既然一定要標，能不能挑「最有用」的那幾份來標？這就是主動學習（active learning）。作者想知道在 OMR 這種序列輸出的任務上，常見的抽樣策略哪些有效，以及在單譜表與全頁兩種主流管線上答案是否一樣。

End-to-end OMR models degrade sharply on collections they have not seen, and the usual fix is fine-tuning on annotated data from the target collection. Annotation is expensive, though: glyphs, ink and layout differ from one batch of early music or manuscripts to the next, and labelling requires people who read the notation. Synthetic data and augmentation reduce the need, but the authors argue that usable accuracy still requires real annotated samples.

The paper therefore reframes the question: if annotation is unavoidable, can we pick the most useful items to annotate? That is active learning. The authors want to know which common sampling strategies work for a sequence-output task like OMR, and whether the answer is the same for the two dominant pipelines, staff-level and full-page.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

這篇用到的兩個模型都是作者群自己的既有系統：單譜表用 CRNN＋CTC（Calvo-Zaragoza 等人一路發展下來的架構），全頁用 Sheet Music Transformer（Ríos-Vila 等人）。信心的定義沿用 Ayllon 等人（2024）在手寫文字辨識校準研究中的做法：取每個預測 token 機率的平均。多樣性抽樣則直接採用 Xie 等人（2023）的 ActiveFT：在特徵空間找一組彼此分散又能代表整體的中心點，再取離每個中心最近的樣本。

論文最主要的發現是「挑中等不確定的樣本比挑最不確定的好」，並把它解讀為最不確定的樣本多半是離群值。這其實跟 Hacohen 等人（2022）的 TypiClust 很接近：他們在影像分類上證明，標註預算很低時應該優先挑「典型」、容易的樣本，預算變大後才輪到不確定的樣本，論文沒有引用這條線。另外在 OMR 圈內，MuRET（Rizo 等人）這類人機協作標註工具早已用「模型先轉、人再修」的方式降低標註量，這篇的貢獻是第一次系統地比較「先標哪幾份」，而不是發明新的抽樣方法。

Both models are the authors' group's existing systems: a CRNN with CTC for staff-level input (the line of work developed by Calvo-Zaragoza and colleagues) and the Sheet Music Transformer (Ríos-Vila et al.) for full pages. Confidence follows Ayllon et al. (2024), from their calibration study of handwritten text recognition: the mean probability of the predicted tokens. Diversity sampling directly adopts ActiveFT by Xie et al. (2023): find centroids in feature space that are mutually spread out yet representative, then take the sample nearest each centroid.

The main finding is that moderately uncertain samples beat the most uncertain ones, which the paper attributes to the most uncertain samples being outliers. This closely matches TypiClust by Hacohen et al. (2022), which showed on image classification that at low budgets one should favour typical, easy samples and only turn to uncertain ones as the budget grows; the paper does not cite that line of work. Within OMR, human-in-the-loop tools such as MuRET (Rizo et al.) have long reduced annotation by letting the model transcribe first and humans correct. The contribution here is the first systematic comparison of which items to annotate first, not a new sampling method.

方法Method

流程。 一輪式、池式（pool-based）主動學習：先用合成資料預訓練（單譜表用 Primens 的 4,923 張白符記譜單譜表，全頁用 GrandStaff），在目標館藏上用預訓練模型推論一次、依策略挑出 B 個樣本、取得真實標註，然後微調一次。預算 B 為 10、25、50，單譜表另加 100。每個館藏先留 10% 當測試集。

七個評估館藏。 單譜表五個：Capitan（704，手寫）、Guatemala（3,263，手寫）、Magnificat（1,361）、Mottecta（1,845）、Seils（1,067），都是 16 至 18 世紀的白符記譜；全頁兩個：Mozarteum（101 頁）與 Polish Scores（117 頁）現代記譜鋼琴譜。

策略。 不確定性抽樣有五種：最低信心、最接近第三四分位、最接近平均、最接近第一四分位、最高信心；每種都另有一個用真實 SER 取代信心的「神諭」版本當上界參考。多樣性抽樣用編碼器特徵跑 ActiveFT。隨機抽樣重複 10 次取平均當基線。

主要數字。 單譜表最佳策略是「最接近第三四分位」，全頁是「最接近第一四分位」；最高與最低兩個極端都與隨機抽樣差不多甚至更差。多樣性抽樣在單譜表幾乎與隨機打平，在全頁則先差後追上第一四分位策略，但計算成本高得多。

Pipeline. One-round, pool-based active learning: pretrain on synthetic data (4,923 mensural single-staff images from Primens for staff level, GrandStaff for full pages), run the pretrained model once on the target collection, select B samples by the strategy, obtain their true labels, and fine-tune once. Budgets B are 10, 25 and 50, plus 100 for staff level. Each collection first reserves 10% as a test set.

Seven evaluation collections. Five at staff level: Capitan (704, handwritten), Guatemala (3,263, handwritten), Magnificat (1,361), Mottecta (1,845) and Seils (1,067), all 16th- to 18th-century mensural notation. Two at full page: Mozarteum (101 pages) and Polish Scores (117 pages), pianoform music in modern notation.

Strategies. Five uncertainty criteria: lowest confidence, nearest the third quartile, nearest the mean, nearest the first quartile, and highest confidence, each with an oracle version that uses true SER in place of confidence as an upper-bound reference. Diversity sampling runs ActiveFT on encoder features. Random sampling averaged over 10 repetitions serves as the baseline.

Main results. The best staff-level strategy is nearest the third quartile, and the best full-page strategy is nearest the first quartile; both extremes perform about as well as random sampling or worse. Diversity sampling roughly ties random at staff level, and at full page starts worse before catching up with the first-quartile strategy, at a much higher computational cost.

限制Limitations

「跟神諭相當」這句話太寬。 表 3 的差值顯示，在最不確定那一端，信心版本比真實錯誤版本好上 8.68（B=10）、7.22（25）、10.49（50）個 SER 百分點；在最確定那一端則是錯誤版本較好，差 1.46 到 2.75 點。與其說「信心跟神諭相當」，更準確的說法是「用真實錯誤挑最難的樣本反而更糟」——這正支持了離群值的解讀，但也說明「神諭」在這裡不是上界。

表 2 是事後挑最佳。 每個預算報的是所有策略裡最好的那個結果，等於用測試集選策略；實際使用時你事先不知道該用哪一種，而兩種情境的最佳策略還不一樣。隨機抽樣基線的數字只畫在圖上、沒有表格，讀者無法精確比較「最佳策略比隨機好多少」。

標註成本的帳不完整。 挑完樣本後，池子裡剩下的樣本被當成驗證集使用，而驗證需要真實標註——在真正的主動學習情境裡這些標註並不存在，模型選擇與早停其實用到了預算以外的標籤。全頁的設定更明顯：Mozarteum 只有 101 頁，扣掉 10% 測試只剩約 90 頁，B=50 已經超過一半的可用池，難怪 50 頁的 18.04% 已經很接近全部標註的 16.36%。

一輪、一個種子。 微調只用種子 42 跑一次，沒有變異數；而表 3 很多差值只有 1 到 2 個 SER 點，可能在微調隨機性的範圍內。論文結論段還有一處與結果不一致：結果節寫全頁最佳是第一四分位，結論段卻寫成第三四分位。另外預算以「樣本數」計，但一個單譜表平均 26 個 token、一頁平均 2,108 個，兩種情境的標註成本差了約 80 倍，作者也承認兩者不能直接比較。

"Comparable to oracle" is too loose. The differences in Table 3 show that at the most-uncertain end, the confidence version beats the true-error version by 8.68 (B=10), 7.22 (25) and 10.49 (50) SER points, while at the most-certain end the error version is better by 1.46 to 2.75 points. Rather than saying confidence matches the oracle, it is more accurate to say that picking the hardest samples by true error is even worse, which supports the outlier interpretation but also shows the oracle is not an upper bound here.

Table 2 is best-in-hindsight. Each budget reports the best result across all strategies, which amounts to choosing the strategy on the test set; in practice you do not know in advance which to use, and the best strategy differs between the two settings. The random baseline appears only in figures, with no table, so readers cannot say precisely how much the best strategy beats random.

Incomplete cost accounting. After selection, the remaining pool samples serve as the validation set, and validation needs true labels; in a real active-learning setting those labels do not exist, so model selection and early stopping use labels outside the budget. The full-page setting makes this sharper: Mozarteum has only 101 pages, about 90 after the 10% test split, so B=50 is already more than half the available pool, which explains why 18.04% at 50 pages is so close to 16.36% with full annotation.

One round, one seed. Fine-tuning runs once with seed 42, with no variance reported, while many differences in Table 3 are only 1 to 2 SER points and may fall within fine-tuning noise. The conclusion also contradicts the results: the results section says the best full-page strategy is the first quartile, but the conclusion says the third. Finally, the budget counts samples, yet a staff averages 26 tokens and a page 2,108, about an 80-fold difference in annotation cost, which the authors acknowledge makes the two settings not directly comparable.

討論Discussion

撇開策略之爭，這篇對實務者最有用的其實是表 2 的學習曲線：單譜表平均 SER 從零樣本 61.67% 開始，標 10 個降到 32.08%、25 個 23.51%、50 個 16.24%、100 個 11.54%，全部標註是 4.55%；全頁則是 68.40% → 39.87%（10 頁）→ 22.64%（50 頁），全部標註 20.61%。對一個新館藏，前 10 個標註樣本帶來的下降（29.59 點）比之後一路標到全部所帶來的（27.53 點）還多，這個「先標少量再說」的建議不需要任何花俏的抽樣也成立。

至於該怎麼挑，我會讀成「避開兩個極端」而不是「用第三四分位」：單譜表偏中高不確定、全頁偏低不確定，與 TypiClust 的「低預算挑典型」大方向一致，但兩條管線的最佳點不同，代表這個結論還依賴模型。真正值得做的下一步是多輪主動學習與多個種子，看中間門檻在第二輪之後是否仍然有效；以及把預算改用 token 數或實際修正時間來計，才能讓單譜表與全頁的結果放在同一把尺上比較。

Setting aside the strategy debate, the most useful part for practitioners is the learning curve in Table 2: average staff-level SER starts at 61.67% zero-shot and falls to 32.08% with 10 labels, 23.51% with 25, 16.24% with 50 and 11.54% with 100, against 4.55% with full annotation; full-page goes from 68.40% to 39.87% (10 pages) to 22.64% (50 pages), against 20.61% with full annotation. For a new collection, the first 10 labelled samples bring a larger drop (29.59 points) than labelling everything after them (27.53 points), and that advice to label a little first holds without any clever sampling.

As for how to choose, I would read it as avoid both extremes rather than use the third quartile: staff level favours moderately high uncertainty and full page favours low uncertainty, broadly consistent with TypiClust's advice to favour typical samples at low budgets, but the two pipelines peak at different points, so the conclusion still depends on the model. The next steps worth doing are multi-round active learning with several seeds, to see whether the middle thresholds still work after the second round, and measuring the budget in tokens or actual correction time so staff-level and full-page results share one ruler.

動機Motivation

作者的出發點是一個研究型圖書館的實際處境：OMR 研究在單一語料上常報告「好到很好」的結果，但一座館藏從中世紀記譜、共通西方記譜到非歐洲記譜都有的圖書館，沒有任何一套 OMR 能涵蓋全部。而且 OMR 只是第一步，後面還要把資料接進館藏目錄、做查詢介面、做比對與排序，最後還得有機構願意長期維護。

具體數字讓這個落差很清楚：BSB 約有 50 萬冊樂譜印本，數位化約 2 萬冊（4%），其中做過 OMR 的約 3,000 冊、約 16 萬頁，只占全部印本的 0.6%。2024–2026 年的新計畫要再加約 2,000 冊 16–17 世紀早期印本、約 35 萬頁，以及 133 本手抄合唱譜。這篇文章想回答的是：在這種規模與異質性下，什麼樣的 OMR 與索引設計是可行、可維護的。作者明說方法本身不新，文章定位是給實務者、研究者與學生的教學資源。

The authors start from the practical situation of a research library. OMR research often reports good to very good results on a single corpus, but a library whose holdings span medieval notation, Common Western Music Notation and non-European notation has no single OMR system that covers everything. OMR is also only the first step: the data must then be integrated into catalogs, given a query interface and matched and ranked, and some institution has to be willing to maintain all of it.

The numbers make the gap concrete. BSB holds about 500,000 music prints; about 20,000 (4%) are digitized, and about 3,000 of those, roughly 160,000 pages, have been through OMR, which is only 0.6% of all prints. A 2024–2026 project adds about 2,000 early prints from the 16th and 17th centuries (about 350,000 pages) plus 133 handwritten choirbooks. The question the article addresses is which OMR and indexing designs are feasible and maintainable at this scale and heterogeneity. The authors state plainly that the methods are not new; the article is meant as a teaching resource for practitioners, researchers and students.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

文章本身的相關研究寫得很誠實：檢索核心是 Downie（1999）把旋律切成 n-gram 當文字處理的做法；Hankinson 等人（2012）在 SIMSSA 框架下首次把 OMR 管線接上 n-gram 檢索，但只用在《Liber Usualis》這種單一同質語料，而且存了五種特徵，BSB 只存其中兩種（音高與音程）。F-TEMPO（Crawford 等人，2023）做的是跨館早期印本的「整頁比對」，OmniOMR（Hajič jr. 等人，2023）則是捷克兩個機構把 OMR 檢索加進大型圖書館館藏，作者自己也說它是最接近的對照。

文章沒提、但最值得對照的是 Peachnote（Viro，2011）：它早在十幾年前就把 IMSLP 數十萬頁樂譜跑過 OMR，再用旋律 n-gram 建跨館藏的檢索與統計，規模與想法都跟 musiconn.scoresearch 非常接近。另外「每個譜表只保留最高音」其實就是 Uitdenbogerd 與 Zobel（1998）的 skyline 演算法，用來從複音音樂抽出旋律的經典捷徑，文中只把它描述成實作上的簡化。所以這篇的貢獻不在方法，而在把這些既有技巧放進一個真實、仍在運作的圖書館服務裡，並記錄整合時遇到的問題。

The article's own related-work section is honest. The retrieval core is Downie's (1999) approach of cutting melodies into n-grams and treating them as text. Hankinson et al. (2012), within SIMSSA, first connected an OMR pipeline to n-gram search, but only on a single homogeneous corpus (the Liber Usualis) and with five stored features, of which BSB stores only two (pitches and intervals). F-TEMPO (Crawford et al., 2023) does page-wise comparison of early prints across libraries, and OmniOMR (Hajič jr. et al., 2023), a collaboration of two Czech institutions adding OMR-based search to a large library's holdings, is described by the authors themselves as the closest point of comparison.

The most relevant comparison the article does not mention is Peachnote (Viro, 2011), which more than a decade ago ran OMR over hundreds of thousands of IMSLP pages and built cross-collection search and statistics on melodic n-grams, close to musiconn.scoresearch in both scale and idea. Likewise, keeping only the highest note per staff is essentially the skyline algorithm of Uitdenbogerd and Zobel (1998), the classic shortcut for extracting a melody from polyphonic music, which the article only describes as an implementation simplification. So the contribution is not the method but placing these existing techniques in a real, running library service and documenting the integration problems.

方法Method

一個索引，三條管線。 所有資料最後都進 Apache Solr，以音高序列與音程序列兩種形式儲存；查詢切成 3 到 8 個音的重疊 n-gram 後比對。為了效率與穩健度刻意大幅簡化：每個譜表獨立處理、和弦只取最高音、索引與查詢都不考慮節奏。作者引用 Padilla 等人（2015）的觀察支持這個取捨：OMR 最常錯的正是節奏、漏掉的音與休止符，丟掉節奏等於繞過最不可靠的部分。

第一條：SmartScore。 2017 年起用商用軟體 SmartScore 批次處理 19 世紀全集印本（貝多芬、韓德爾、李斯特、孟德爾頌、舒伯特、舒曼全集等），平均每小時 70 張影像，輸出 MusicXML，原本只取每個譜表的第一聲部。

第二條：MuRET。 SmartScore 處理不了定量記譜（mensural notation），所以 16–17 世紀語料改用阿利坎特大學的 MuRET（以 YOLO 偵測譜表，再用 CRNN＋CTC 辨識符號）。訓練用 21 份樣本、共 5,136 張影像，分兩階段：先辨識並人工校正前 20 張，再從每份剩下的影像均勻抽 10% 重跑並再校正一次；整個模型反覆訓練校正花了一年半以上。MuRET 輸出 MEI，所以整條管線改成 MEI，舊的 SmartScore 資料也用 Verovio 批次轉成 MEI 重建索引，順便讓多聲部可以分開索引。

第三條：RISM。 約 250 萬筆以 Plaine & Easie Code 人工輸入的單聲部樂曲開頭，本來就是單旋律，可以直接轉成音高序列；節奏有記錄但只用來顯示。查詢介面是兩個服務共用的：點選式鋼琴鍵盤、電腦鍵盤對應鋼琴黑白鍵（例如 D5=W、D♯5=3），或直接打 C5F5G5G#5，並有即時播放與五線譜顯示，可選完全比對或移調比對。

One index, three pipelines. All data end up in Apache Solr, stored both as pitch sequences and as interval sequences; queries are cut into overlapping n-grams of 3 to 8 notes and matched. The simplification is deliberate, for efficiency and robustness: each staff is processed separately, only the highest note of a chord is kept, and neither indexing nor querying considers rhythm. The authors cite Padilla et al. (2015) in support: wrong rhythms and missing notes and rests are among the most frequent OMR errors, so dropping rhythm sidesteps the least reliable part.

Pipeline one: SmartScore. From 2017, the commercial SmartScore batch-processed 19th-century complete editions (Beethoven, Handel, Liszt, Mendelssohn, Schubert, Schumann and others) at an average of 70 images per hour, producing MusicXML, originally keeping only the first voice per staff.

Pipeline two: MuRET. SmartScore cannot handle mensural notation, so the 16th- and 17th-century corpus uses MuRET from the University of Alicante (YOLO for staff detection, then a CRNN with CTC for symbols). Training used 21 sample sources with 5,136 images in two phases: first the initial 20 images were recognized and manually corrected, then an evenly distributed 10% of each source's remaining images were processed and corrected again; the training-and-correction cycle took over 1.5 years. MuRET outputs MEI, so the whole pipeline moved to MEI and the old SmartScore data were batch-converted with Verovio and reindexed, which also lets multiple voices be indexed separately.

Pipeline three: RISM. About 2.5 million monophonic incipits entered by hand in Plaine & Easie Code can be turned directly into pitch sequences; rhythm is stored but used only for display. The query interface is shared by both services: a clickable piano keyboard, computer-keyboard rows mapped to piano keys (e.g. D5=W, D♯5=3), or typed text such as C5F5G5G#5, with live playback and notation display, and a choice of exact or transposed matching.

限制Limitations

沒有任何量化評估。 全文沒有 OMR 的符號錯誤率、沒有檢索的準確率或召回率、沒有查詢延遲，也沒有「丟掉節奏與和弦內聲」讓錯誤匹配增加多少的估計。作者主張簡化「抹平了部分 OMR 錯誤」，但這是一個可以量的宣稱（例如在人工校正過的子集上比對 OMR 前後的檢索結果），文章沒有量。使用者回饋在致謝裡被明說是「非正式收集」，所以「有些使用者覺得有用」的兩個用例（追溯改編、辨認佚名作品）只是軼事。

簡化的代價沒有被正面討論。 只取最高音意味著內聲、低音聲部的主題在共通西方記譜的鍵盤與管弦樂譜中幾乎找不到；不看節奏則讓短的 3 音 n-gram 在 16 萬頁裡會有大量偶然命中。這些是設計上的取捨，但讀者拿不到任何數字判斷它划不划算。

很多東西還沒公開。 MEI 解析器、VuFind 輸入元件都寫「完成後會釋出」；MuRET 模型與訓練印本則要向阿利坎特大學「申請」；大量 OMR 資料也是「依申請提供」。對一篇以教學與可重現為定位的文章，這讓其他機構暫時無法照做。

There is no quantitative evaluation. The article reports no OMR symbol error rate, no retrieval precision or recall, no query latency, and no estimate of how many false matches dropping rhythm and inner voices adds. The authors claim the simplification smooths over some OMR errors, but that is a measurable claim (for example, comparing retrieval on a manually corrected subset before and after OMR) and it is not measured. User feedback is stated in the acknowledgments to have been gathered informally, so the two use cases some users find helpful (tracing adaptations, identifying anonymous works) are anecdotes.

The cost of the simplification is not discussed head-on. Keeping only the highest note means themes in inner or bass voices of CWMN keyboard and orchestral scores are nearly unfindable, and ignoring rhythm means short 3-note n-grams will produce many chance hits across 160,000 pages. These are design trade-offs, but the reader gets no numbers to judge whether they pay off.

Much is not yet released. The MEI parser and the VuFind input components are to be released once finished; the MuRET model and training prints are available on request from the University of Alicante; larger sets of OMR data are also on request. For an article positioned as educational and reproducible, this means other institutions cannot yet follow it.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的不是技術，而是第 6 節講的「作品與版本」問題。用旋律查詢時，使用者心裡想的是抽象的「作品」，但館藏目錄描述的是特定版本與實體。例子很具體：查貝多芬「命運動機」g′–g′–g′–e♭′，貝多芬本人的命中顯示 1862–1865 年，因為它繼承的是 Breitkopf & Härtel 全集的書目紀錄；Samuel Scheidt（1587–1654）的《Tabulatura nova》則顯示 1892 年，是《德意志音樂文獻》那一冊的出版年。想用旋律檢索研究風格演變的人，會被這個日期落差直接誤導。

這點對 MIR 研究者是個提醒：OMR 論文的評估幾乎都停在符號層級，但真正決定一個檢索服務能不能用的，常常是 OMR 之外的詮釋資料。作者的建議方向（把 OMR、OCR 與版面辨識整合起來抽出作曲家與歌詞開頭，再接權威檔與 linked data）跟最近 OMR 界談的「全頁理解」一致，也是 RISM 已經靠幾十年人工編目做到的事。

整體而言，這是一份有用的實務紀錄：它清楚說明為什麼要用 MEI 當共同格式、為什麼要容許多套 OMR 並存，以及一個橫跨 Java／PHP／JavaScript 開發與音樂學的團隊花了多少時間。但若要當成其他機構的設計依據，還缺一組最基本的數字——簡化後的檢索到底找回了多少該找到的東西。

The most valuable part is not the technology but the work-versus-edition problem in Section 6. A user searching by melody is thinking of an abstract work, but library catalogs describe a particular edition and physical item. The examples are concrete: searching Beethoven's fate motif g′–g′–g′–e♭′ returns Beethoven's own match dated 1862–1865, because it inherits the record of the Breitkopf & Härtel edition, and Samuel Scheidt's (1587–1654) Tabulatura nova appears as 1892, the publication year of the Denkmäler deutscher Tonkunst volume. Anyone using melodic search to study stylistic change would be misled by these date gaps.

This is a useful reminder for MIR researchers: OMR evaluation almost always stops at the symbol level, but what decides whether a search service is usable is often the metadata outside OMR. The authors' proposed direction (combining OMR, OCR and layout recognition to extract composers and text incipits, then linking to authority files and linked data) matches the recent OMR push toward full-page understanding, and it is what RISM already achieves through decades of manual cataloging.

Overall this is a useful practical record: it explains clearly why MEI serves as the common format, why several OMR systems must coexist, and how much time a cross-disciplinary team spanning Java, PHP, JavaScript and musicology needed. But as a design basis for other institutions it still lacks the most basic number: how much of what should be found the simplified search actually retrieves.
