MI-MIDI: Mechanistic Interpretability of Text-to-MIDI Generation Models via Probing, Lenses and Steering

MI-MIDI：透過探針、透鏡與操控技術對文字轉 MIDI 生成模型做機制可解釋性分析

Jakub Poćwiardowski, Mateusz Modrzejewski
arXiv:2608.06638 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：機制可解釋性（mechanistic interpretability）這套從語言模型研究發展出來的工具——線性探針、logit/tuned lens、activation patching、方向性操控——過去幾乎只用在音訊音樂模型（例如 MusicGen），這篇是少數把整套工具搬到符號音樂（text-to-MIDI）模型上的研究，而且刻意選了架構迥異的兩個模型（cross-attention 的 text2midi 與把 MIDI token 塞進 Llama 的 MIDI-LLM）對照分析，發現兩者形成音樂預測的方式截然不同——這個架構決定可操控性的發現，對想要控制生成模型輸出（例如調音域、調複音密度）的人有直接的工程意義，所以標記為深度分析。Why this one: Mechanistic interpretability tooling developed for language models — linear probes, logit/tuned lenses, activation patching, directional steering — has almost exclusively been applied to audio music models (e.g. MusicGen). This paper is one of the first to bring the full toolkit to symbolic, text-to-MIDI models, and deliberately contrasts two architecturally different systems (the cross-attention-based text2midi versus MIDI-LLM, which grafts MIDI tokens onto a Llama backbone) to find that they form musical predictions in fundamentally different ways. That architecture-determines-controllability finding has direct engineering relevance for anyone trying to steer generation output (register, polyphony density, etc.), which is why it's flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

近年語言模型的機制可解釋性研究發展出一整套成熟工具：用線性探針檢驗某個概念是否可以從內部激活值線性解碼出來；用 logit lens／tuned lens 觀察模型在每一層「如果現在就輸出，會輸出什麼」；用 activation patching 找出資訊在哪一層被因果性地綁定到輸出；用方向性操控（steering）驗證某個方向的激活加法能不能可控地改變輸出。這些工具在音樂生成模型上的應用幾乎全部集中在音訊模型（如 MusicGen），符號音樂生成——尤其是最近因為 MIDICaps 這類資料集興起的文字轉 MIDI 模型——完全沒人碰過。作者提出三個核心問題：這些模型內部到底編碼了哪些音樂概念（音高、樂器、和聲）？這些概念在網路的哪一層、什麼時候形成最終預測？以及這些被解碼出來的資訊是不是「因果上真的有用」，能不能拿來操控生成結果？這篇論文的目標就是把語言模型可解釋性的方法論第一次系統性地搬到符號音樂生成模型上，並且刻意選兩個架構差異很大的模型做對照，檢驗「架構設計」本身如何決定音樂概念的表徵方式與可操控性。

Mechanistic interpretability research on language models has matured into a full toolkit: linear probes test whether a concept is linearly decodable from internal activations; logit/tuned lenses reveal what a layer would output if generation stopped there; activation patching locates where information becomes causally bound to the output; and directional steering tests whether adding an activation vector can controllably shift the output. Applied to music generation, this toolkit has almost exclusively targeted audio models like MusicGen — symbolic music generation, especially the recent wave of text-to-MIDI models enabled by datasets like MIDICaps, has gone entirely unexamined. The authors pose three core questions: what musical concepts (pitch, instrument, harmony) are actually encoded internally; at which layer and when do these concepts crystallize into a final prediction; and is the decoded information causally useful — can it actually steer generation? The paper's goal is to bring language-model interpretability methodology to symbolic music generation systematically for the first time, deliberately contrasting two architecturally distinct models to examine how architecture itself determines how musical concepts are represented and how controllable they are.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景涵蓋三條線：文字條件式音樂生成（特別是 MIDICaps 這類資料集，讓端到端的文字轉 MIDI 模型變得可行）；機制可解釋性技術本身（線性探針、logit/tuned lens、activation steering，均源自語言模型研究）；以及既有的音樂模型可解釋性工作（過去幾乎只做音訊模型如 MusicGen，符號系統幾乎是空白）。研究對象選了兩個代表性但架構迥異的系統：text2midi 是編碼器-解碼器架構，用凍結的 Flan-T5 當文字編碼器，透過 cross-attention 把文字資訊持續餵給 18 層、768 維隱藏層的解碼器，用 REMI+ 做符號音樂的 tokenization；MIDI-LLM 則是把 Llama 3.2 1B（16 層、2048 維隱藏層）這個純解碼器語言模型的詞表直接擴充，塞進 MIDI token，用 context 方式（而不是 cross-attention）做文字條件，tokenization 用 AMT 格式。這個選擇本身就是研究設計的核心：一個是「音樂 token 和文字 token 從一開始就在共享的殘差流裡競爭」的架構，一個是「文字資訊透過獨立通道持續注入」的架構,這兩種條件方式理論上應該導致完全不同的資訊處理模式。

The related work spans three threads: text-conditioned music generation, particularly datasets like MIDICaps that made end-to-end text-to-MIDI models feasible; the mechanistic interpretability techniques themselves (linear probes, logit/tuned lenses, activation steering), all originating in language-model research; and prior music-model interpretability work, which has almost exclusively targeted audio models like MusicGen, leaving symbolic systems essentially unexamined. The two studied systems are chosen to be architecturally distinct: text2midi is an encoder-decoder model with a frozen Flan-T5 text encoder feeding a REMI+-tokenized, 18-layer, 768-hidden-dimension decoder via cross-attention conditioning; MIDI-LLM instead extends a Llama 3.2 1B decoder-only language model (16 layers, 2048 hidden dimensions) with an expanded vocabulary that includes MIDI tokens, conditioning on text via context rather than cross-attention, and using AMT-style tokenization. This choice is itself the core of the research design: one architecture has music and text tokens competing in a shared residual stream from the start, while the other injects text information through a persistently separate channel — two conditioning strategies that should, in principle, produce very different information-processing patterns.

方法Method

方法分四層。線性探針：在每一層的激活值上訓練 L2 正則化邏輯回歸探針，檢驗音高類別、樂器家族、和弦性質、和聲功能、推估調性等概念能不能線性解碼，同時對真實生成內容與來自 SynTheory 資料集的受控合成範例都做探測，並用打亂標籤的對照探針確認結果反映的是真實表徵而非探針本身的容量。Logit lens 與 tuned lens：經典 logit lens 直接看每一層「現在停下來會輸出什麼」；對於詞表混雜文字與音樂 token 的 MIDI-LLM，額外分析限制在音樂詞表內的分佈；tuned lens 額外訓練一個小型仿射轉換器，把中間層表徵映射到最終層的座標系，用來區分「預測在後段真的發生劇烈變化」還是「只是座標系旋轉」這兩種可能。Activation patching：把某一層的激活值替換成對比提示詞（例如鋼琴 vs. 小提琴）產生的激活值，量測樂器資訊在哪一層被因果性地「綁定」進生成內容，藉此定位資訊整合發生的深度。方向性操控：用對比提示詞的激活值算出「均值差」方向，加回殘差流裡，並設計了一個新穎的雙向協議——分別檢驗「往正向加」與「往負向加」是否會產生方向相反的效果（真正的方向性控制）還是只是對稱的漂移，追蹤的指標包括音密度（代表節奏/能量）、平均 MIDI 音高（代表音域）、同時發聲的音符數（代表複音密度）。實驗規模包括 1000 個隨機生成提示用於完整生成探測、200 個序列子樣本用於逐 token 探測、5 折分組交叉驗證、tuned lens 用 6 萬個 token 位置訓練、5 萬個位置留作驗證，操控實驗涵蓋 10680 個序列，並用 2000 次自助抽樣算信賴區間。

The methodology has four layers. Linear probing: L2-regularized logistic regression probes are fit on activations at each layer to test whether pitch class, instrument family, chord quality, harmonic function, and estimated key are linearly decodable, run on both realistic full generations and controlled synthetic examples from the SynTheory dataset, with shuffled-label control probes confirming results reflect genuine representational content rather than probe capacity. Logit and tuned lens: the classic logit lens shows what each layer would predict if generation stopped there; for MIDI-LLM, whose vocabulary mixes text and music tokens, the analysis is additionally restricted to the musical-vocabulary distribution; the tuned lens trains a small affine translator mapping intermediate representations into the final layer's basis, distinguishing genuine late-stage prediction refinement from mere basis rotation. Activation patching: activations at a given layer are swapped with those from a contrasting prompt (e.g. piano vs. violin) to measure at which layer instrument information becomes causally bound into generation, localizing the depth of information integration. Directional steering: difference-in-means vectors computed from contrastive prompts are additively injected into the residual stream, using a novel bidirectional protocol that separately tests whether adding the vector in the positive versus negative direction produces genuinely opposite effects (true directional control) versus merely symmetric drift, tracking note density (tempo/energy proxy), mean MIDI pitch (register), and simultaneous note count (polyphony). Experimental scale includes 1,000 randomly generated prompts for full-generation probing, 200-sequence subsamples for token-level probing, 5-fold grouped cross-validation, a tuned lens trained on 60,000 token positions with 50,000 held out for evaluation, steering experiments spanning 10,680 sequences, and 2,000 bootstrap resamples for confidence intervals.

限制Limitations

作者列出五點限制。均值差方向的品質問題：對比提示詞在詞彙和音樂內容上同時有差異，方向的「乾淨程度」直接取決於對比組設計得好不好，混雜的對比可能讓操控方向不夠純粹。指標間的相依性：音符密度（用來代表節奏/能量）本身跟複音密度高度相關，兩者不是完全獨立的測量，操控其中一個難免影響另一個，讓因果解讀打了折扣。標籤品質：序列層級的調性估計用 Krumhansl-Schmuckler 演算法，本身就有誤差，一些較弱的高層概念表徵結果可能只是反映標籤噪音而非模型真的沒學到。抽象概念難以量化：更抽象的音樂性質（例如整體樂句結構、情緒）在多軌 MIDI 裡難以明確定義與測量，完全排除在量化分析之外。模型範圍：只研究了兩個模型，無法把「條件機制的差異」和「其他架構差異（模型大小、tokenization、訓練資料）」完全切開，觀察到的差異有可能部分來自條件機制之外的因素。

The authors list five limitations. Difference-in-means direction quality: contrastive prompts vary both lexically and musically, so the cleanliness of a steering direction depends heavily on how well-matched the contrast set is, and mixed contrasts can leave the direction impure. Metric interdependence: note density (used as a tempo/energy proxy) correlates strongly with polyphony, so the two are not fully independent measurements, and steering one inevitably affects the other, weakening causal interpretation. Label quality: sequence-level key estimates rely on the Krumhansl-Schmuckler algorithm, which itself carries error, so some weak higher-level concept representations may reflect label noise rather than a genuine gap in what the model learned. Hard-to-quantify abstraction: more abstract musical properties (overall phrase structure, mood) are difficult to define and measure unambiguously in multi-track MIDI and are excluded from the quantitative analysis entirely. Model scope: only two models are studied, so differences attributable to the conditioning mechanism cannot be fully separated from other architectural differences (model size, tokenization, training data) — the observed contrasts may partly reflect factors beyond conditioning alone.

討論Discussion

最核心的發現是兩個模型形成音樂預測的方式截然不同，而這個差異可以直接追溯到條件機制的設計。text2midi 用 cross-attention 讓解碼器在每一層都能重新查閱編碼過的文字提示，結果是預測在各層逐步、漸進地精煉，沒有單一層出現劇烈跳躍，樂器資訊的整合也是分散在各層，沒有集中的深度峰值——這種「隨時可查閱提示」的設計讓操控也變得穩定：在所有層同時注入操控向量依然穩定，且方向性最乾淨。MIDI-LLM 則呈現截然不同的兩階段模式：前 12 層幾乎完全停留在繼承自文字模型的表徵基底裡，音樂詞表的預測準確率接近零，詞表機率質量維持在跟詞表大小成比例的約 30%；到第 13 層開始劇烈轉向，第 15 層音樂詞表機率質量衝到約 95%，這個轉向還有明確的順序：先是特殊符號、再是音符、最後才是時間/音長token。用 tuned lens 檢驗發現這個劇烈跳躍有一部分只是座標系旋轉（不是真正新資訊的產生），但排除座標系效應後仍有實質的預測精煉在發生。這個「資訊晚期才突然綁定」的模式直接反映在操控實驗上：MIDI-LLM 只有單層注入才穩定，多層同時注入操控向量會累積干擾，操控強度被限制在 α=0.75 這個上限；而 text2midi 全層注入依然穩定。這給出一個可以直接落地的工程結論：用 cross-attention 條件的模型，操控時可以放心用全層注入；用 LLM 改造、共享殘差流條件的模型，操控必須挑單一層小心注入，貪心地全層加會讓效果崩掉。這種「架構設計決定表徵形成路徑，進而決定可操控性上限」的因果鏈，是這篇論文對可解釋性研究和實務操控應用都有貢獻的地方。

The central finding is that the two models form musical predictions in fundamentally different ways, traceable directly to their conditioning mechanism. text2midi's cross-attention lets the decoder re-consult the encoded text prompt at every layer, producing gradual, layer-by-layer prediction refinement with no single sharp jump, and instrument-information integration is distributed across depth with no concentrated peak — this "always-available prompt" design also makes steering stable: injecting a steering vector at every layer simultaneously remains stable and yields the cleanest directional control. MIDI-LLM shows a starkly different two-stage pattern: the first 12 layers remain almost entirely within a basis inherited from the underlying text model, with near-zero musical-vocabulary prediction accuracy and vocabulary probability mass sitting at roughly 30% — proportional to vocabulary size — before rotating sharply starting at layer 13, reaching roughly 95% musical-vocabulary mass by layer 15, in an ordered sequence: special tokens first, then notes, then timing/duration tokens. The tuned lens shows part of this sharp jump is merely basis rotation rather than genuinely new information, though substantial real prediction refinement remains even after accounting for that. This late, sudden-binding pattern shows up directly in the steering results: MIDI-LLM is stable only with single-layer injection, while injecting the steering vector across all layers accumulates disruptive interference, capping usable strength at α=0.75; text2midi, in contrast, remains stable with all-layer injection. This yields directly usable engineering guidance: for cross-attention-conditioned models, all-layer steering injection is safe; for LLM-adapted models sharing a residual stream between prompt and generation, steering must target a single carefully chosen layer, since greedy all-layer injection collapses performance. This causal chain — architecture determines representation-formation pathway, which in turn determines the ceiling on controllability — is the paper's core contribution to both interpretability research and practical steering applications.

Equivariant Music Transformer

等變音樂 Transformer

Zixun Guo, Simon Dixon
arXiv:2608.03920 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇提出「等變性不會隨著模型變大或訓練變久而自動出現」的實證證據，並且用一個乾淨的自蒸餾式輔助損失函式把等變性直接塞進訓練目標裡，而不是像過去那樣靠資料增強或架構技巧繞路。研究設計成三組對照——不加輔助損失、資料增強、模型層級正則化——讓因果關係看得很清楚。Why this one: It gives concrete evidence that equivariance does not emerge from scale or longer training, then folds it directly into the training objective via a self-distillation auxiliary loss instead of leaning on data augmentation or architectural tricks. The three-way comparison (no auxiliary loss vs. augmentation vs. model-level regularization) isolates the causal claim cleanly.

動機Motivation

人類聽音樂時，一段旋律不管移調多少半音、往前或往後平移多少拍，聽起來都還是「同一個東西」——這是一種等變性（equivariance）：輸入做了某種變換，輸出應該跟著做出可預期的對應變換。symbolic music transformer 理論上該有這個特性，但作者觀察到一個不尋常的現象：目前主流的自迴歸 transformer，只用 next-token cross-entropy 訓練，並不會自動學到這個特性，而且模型越大、訓練越久，反而越不等變——模型把容量拿去記憶絕對位置模式，而不是共享的音樂結構。這篇論文的出發點就是要質疑「等變性會隨著規模湧現」這個常見假設，並且直接在訓練目標裡加入監督訊號，強迫模型學到它。

When a listener hears a melody transposed up a few semitones or shifted a few beats in time, it still registers as "the same" musical idea — a property called equivariance: a transformation of the input should produce a predictable, corresponding transformation of the output. Symbolic music transformers ought to have this property, but the authors report something counter-intuitive: today's standard autoregressive transformers, trained purely with next-token cross-entropy, do not acquire it automatically — and larger models trained longer become *less* equivariant, apparently spending their extra capacity memorizing absolute patterns rather than shared musical structure. The paper's starting point is to question the common assumption that equivariance emerges from scale, and to instead inject it directly into the training objective as an explicit supervisory signal.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

過去讓模型「感知」平移不變性的做法大致三類：一是輸入端做特徵工程（例如用相對音高、相對時值編碼取代絕對值）；二是架構上動手腳，像相對位置編碼（relative position embedding）；三是資料增強，把同一段音樂做隨機移調、平移後都丟進去訓練。這三種做法在音高估計、速度估計、表徵學習等 MIR 任務上都證實有效，但作者指出一個共同盲點：不管輸入端或架構端做了什麼調整，幾乎所有生成模型的訓練目標最後都還是標準的 next-token cross-entropy，從來沒有人在「模型層級」明確要求等變性作為訓練目標的一部分。這篇論文接的正是這個空白：把等變性從「盼望架構設計帶來的副產物」提升成「顯式的訓練目標」。

Prior attempts to give models translation-invariant perception of music fall into three camps: input-level feature engineering (relative rather than absolute pitch/duration encodings), architectural modifications such as relative position embeddings, and data augmentation via random transposition and time-shifting during training. Each has shown benefits for MIR tasks like pitch estimation, tempo estimation, and representation learning. But the authors point to a shared blind spot: regardless of what happens at the input or architecture level, nearly every generative model's training objective still defaults to plain next-token cross-entropy — no one has explicitly supervised equivariance as a model-level training objective. This paper fills exactly that gap, promoting equivariance from a hoped-for side effect of architecture choices to an explicit training target.

方法Method

EMT 用雙分支架構同時優化兩個目標。主分支就是標準自迴歸 next-token 預測，用 cross-entropy loss。輔助分支做的是「自蒸餾式」等變性正則化：對輸入序列 x 套用一個隨機的音樂變換 T（音高平移、時間平移），丟進模型得到變換後的預測分佈 p^T，再把預測分佈套用反變換 T^-1，拿去跟原始輸入（不做變換）得到的預測分佈 p 做 KL 散度比較——理論上，如果模型真的等變，「先變換輸入再反變換輸出」應該要等於「完全不變換」。這裡的關鍵設計是對原始預測 p 做 stop-gradient，避免模型學到讓兩邊都塌縮成同一個平凡解來騙過損失函式。總損失是兩者加權和，權重 λ 設得很小（0.001），等變損失是輔助角色而非主導。

架構本身建立在 Moonbeam 之上，做了幾個 MIR 特化的修改：改用絕對 onset time 而不是相對 delta，方便變換能均勻套用到輸入輸出兩端；把八度與音高類別合併成一本統一的 pitch 字典，簡化變換操作；用 token delay pattern 取代原本的 GRU 解碼器來捕捉 onset、pitch、duration、instrument、velocity 五個屬性之間的相依關係；embedding 用直接相加而非串接，增加每個屬性的表達力。此外還疊加了兩個既有的等變性技巧作為對照與互補：Fundamental Music Embedding（讓輸入層本身帶有音樂距離感）和 Multi-Dimensional Relative Attention（用相對位置而非絕對位置編碼）。

訓練資料是 LakhMIDI：消融實驗用 LMD-clean 的 7.9 萬條序列（約 8000 萬 token），主觀聆聽評測用完整 LMD。模型不大，12 層、hidden size 768，約 9600 萬參數，兩張 A100/H100 分散式訓練。變換範圍是 ±12 半音、0 到 5 秒（0.25 秒為單位），測試時故意用更大範圍（±24 半音、0 到 10 秒）做分佈外評估。

EMT jointly optimizes two branches. The main branch is a standard autoregressive next-token predictor trained with cross-entropy loss. The auxiliary branch implements equivariance as a self-distillation regularizer: a random musical transformation T (pitch shift, time shift) is applied to the input sequence x, the model's prediction on the transformed input p^T is computed, an inverse transform T^-1 is applied to that prediction, and the result is compared via KL divergence against the model's prediction on the untransformed input p. In a perfectly equivariant model, "transform the input then invert the output" should match "never transforming it at all." A stop-gradient on the untransformed anchor prediction prevents the trivial collapse where both branches converge to the same degenerate output just to cheaply minimize the loss. The total loss is a weighted sum with a small λ=0.001, keeping the equivariance term auxiliary rather than dominant.

The backbone builds on Moonbeam with several MIR-specific modifications: absolute onset times instead of relative deltas so transformations apply uniformly across input and output; a unified pitch dictionary merging octave and pitch-class tokens to simplify transformation; a token delay pattern replacing the original GRU decoder to capture dependencies among onset, pitch, duration, instrument, and velocity; and additive rather than concatenative embeddings for greater per-attribute expressiveness. Two existing equivariance techniques are layered on as complementary feature-level engineering: Fundamental Music Embedding (a music-distance-aware input layer) and Multi-Dimensional Relative Attention (relative rather than absolute positional encoding).

Training uses the LakhMIDI dataset: ablations use LMD-clean (79k sequences, ~80M tokens), and the subjective listening study uses the full LMD. The model is modest — 12 layers, hidden size 768, ~96M parameters, trained on 2 A100/H100 GPUs. Transformations span ±12 semitones and 0-5 seconds (0.25s steps) during training, with deliberately wider out-of-distribution ranges (±24 semitones, 0-10s) at test time.

限制Limitations

論文本身很誠實地把「等變性不會隨規模湧現」講成一個負面結果，但這個結論的證據鏈其實依賴特定的架構家族（Anticipatory Music Transformer、MIDI-LLM）和特定資料集（LakhMIDI），換一種完全不同的 tokenization 或訓練語料，趨勢是否一樣穩健並未驗證。其次，λ=0.001 是憑經驗選出來的單一數值，論文沒有做完整的敏感度分析說明這個超參數在不同資料規模或模型大小下要怎麼調，若 λ 選得不好，等變損失可能反過來傷害生成品質。第三，主觀聆聽測試只找了 12 位受試者評估，樣本數偏小，雖然用 Wilcoxon 檢定確認顯著性，但外部效度有限。最後，變換只涵蓋音高平移與時間平移這兩種最基本的音樂對稱性，速度伸縮（tempo rubato）、調式轉換等其他音樂上有意義的變換完全沒有討論，等變性框架能否推廣到這些變換也是未知數。

The paper is admirably honest in framing "equivariance does not emerge from scale" as a negative result, but the evidence chain rests on a specific architecture family (Anticipatory Music Transformer, MIDI-LLM) and a specific corpus (LakhMIDI); whether the trend holds under a different tokenization scheme or training corpus is untested. Second, λ=0.001 is a single empirically chosen value without a full sensitivity analysis across model or dataset scale — a poorly chosen λ could let the equivariance term actively hurt generation quality. Third, the subjective listening study used only 12 participants; while a Wilcoxon test confirms statistical significance, the external validity of such a small panel is limited. Finally, the transformations studied are limited to the two most basic musical symmetries — pitch shift and time shift — leaving tempo rubato, mode changes, and other musically meaningful transformations entirely unaddressed, so it remains open whether the equivariance framework generalizes to them.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有意思的地方不是「加了一個新 loss 讓模型變好」這種常見套路，而是它先做了一個扎實的診斷實驗，證明業界預設「規模夠大自然會學到好的歸納偏誤」這個信念在等變性這件事上是錯的——甚至是反過來的。這對整個 MIR 領域的啟示可能比 EMT 本身的架構更重要：如果連「移調不變性」這種音樂裡最基本、最容易驗證的對稱性都不會隨規模自然浮現，那麼其他更複雜的音樂結構性質（比如曲式的層級性、和聲功能的等價關係）恐怕更不會白白出現，勢必要靠明確的訓練目標或架構偏誤去誘導。另外，EMT 用比 Anticipatory 模型小得多的參數量就達到相近的生成品質，同時等變性大幅提升，這說明「顯式正則化」有時候比「單純擴大模型規模」更划算——對計算資源有限的研究者是個實用的訊息。比較可惜的是論文沒有討論等變損失對訓練時間、記憶體開銷的實際代價，這在評估「值不值得採用」時是缺一角的資訊。

The paper's most interesting contribution isn't "add a new loss and things get better" — it's the diagnostic work up front, which shows that the field's implicit assumption, that sufficient scale naturally yields good inductive biases, is wrong, even inverted, for equivariance. That may matter more for MIR broadly than EMT's architecture itself: if even the most basic, easily verifiable musical symmetry (transposition invariance) doesn't emerge from scale alone, more complex structural properties of music (hierarchical form, functional harmonic equivalence) are unlikely to emerge for free either, and will likely need explicit training objectives or architectural biases to induce. It's also notable that EMT matches Anticipatory-scale generation quality with far fewer parameters while gaining substantially in equivariance — suggesting explicit regularization can sometimes be a better bargain than simply scaling up, a practically useful signal for compute-constrained researchers. One gap: the paper doesn't discuss the actual training-time or memory overhead of the auxiliary equivariance branch, which matters for judging whether the tradeoff is worth adopting.

HARP: Harmonic-Aware Residual Partitioning for Neural Audio Codecs

HARP：神經音訊編解碼器的諧波感知殘差分割

Qiaoyu Yang, Lixing He, Binyue Deng, Weifeng Zhao
arXiv:2607.16657 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-07-18
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：架構完全不動、只改訓練損失，就讓 RVQ 的每一階對應到一個頻帶——這種「零推論成本」的novel技術在表徵學習裡很少見，而且對音樂特別有意義：泛音與基頻的關係正是它想保住的東西。Why this one: It changes only the training loss, not the architecture, yet makes each RVQ stage correspond to a frequency band — a genuinely novel zero-inference-cost technique, and one that matters especially for music, since the fundamental-overtone relationship is exactly what it preserves.

動機Motivation

RVQ 音訊 codec（SoundStream、EnCodec、DAC 一系）預設對所有頻率一視同仁，結果是 codebook 在頻譜上糾纏：每一階量化器學到的是頻率的不可預測混合，而非某個特定頻帶。這造成一個實務痛點——低位元率時我們會截斷後面幾階，但截掉的是「一團說不清是哪些頻率的東西」，退化行為因此不可控、產生的失真也不穩定。

既有的補救是平行頻帶分解：把音訊切成獨立頻帶各自編碼。這確實建立了頻率階層，卻付出另一個代價——潛空間被切碎，跨頻帶的相干性消失。對音樂而言這特別致命：一個 200 Hz 的基頻與它在 800 Hz 的泛音是同一個發聲事件的兩個面向，把它們丟給兩個互不通訊的編碼器，重建出來的諧波關係就會失真。

作者把這兩者定調為互補的失效模式：標準 RVQ 有諧波脈絡但沒有頻率特化，平行分解有頻率階層但破壞跨頻帶諧波。HARP 想同時要兩者。

RVQ audio codecs (the SoundStream / EnCodec / DAC lineage) treat all frequencies uniformly, so codebooks become spectrally entangled: each quantiser stage learns an unpredictable mixture of frequencies rather than a specific band. This creates a practical problem — at low bitrates we truncate later stages, but what gets removed is an indeterminate mix of frequencies, so degradation is uncontrolled and the resulting distortion is erratic.

The existing remedy is parallel band decomposition: split the audio into independent bands and encode each separately. That does establish a frequency hierarchy, but at another cost — the latent space fragments and cross-frequency coherence is lost. For music this is especially damaging: a 200 Hz fundamental and its 800 Hz partial are two facets of one sounding event, and handing them to two encoders that never communicate distorts the reconstructed harmonic relationship.

The authors frame these as complementary failure modes: standard RVQ has harmonic context but no frequency specialisation; parallel decomposition has frequency hierarchy but breaks cross-band harmonics. HARP wants both.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

定位很清楚：這不是新架構，是訓練策略。對照組一邊是標準 RVQ codec，另一邊是 BSCodec 這類平行頻帶分解方法。論文的三項貢獻宣稱依序為：把上述兩種失效模式指認為互補問題；提出僅靠監督訊號就在單一 RVQ 內部建立頻率階層的訓練法；以及在語音、音樂、一般音訊上勝過兩邊基線，並釋出模型、訓練腳本與音檔範例。

值得注意的是它的工程賣點——推論路徑與標準 RVQ 完全相同。這代表任何已經在用 RVQ codec token 的下游系統（音樂 LM、TTS、音訊理解）都能直接換上，不必改任何介面。

The positioning is explicit: this is not a new architecture but a training strategy. It is compared against standard RVQ codecs on one side and parallel band decomposition methods such as BSCodec on the other. The three claimed contributions are: identifying the two failure modes above as complementary; proposing a training method that imposes a frequency hierarchy inside a single RVQ through supervision alone; and beating both baselines on speech, music and general audio, with public models, training scripts and audio samples.

The engineering selling point is worth noting — the inference path is identical to standard RVQ. Any downstream system already consuming RVQ codec tokens (music LMs, TTS, audio understanding) can swap it in without changing any interface.

方法Method

三個機制疊起來構成 HARP。

1. 頻率有序分組。 把 L 個 RVQ 階段分成 K 個依頻率排序的組。預設 K=4、L=9：第 0 組（低音）階段 1–3，對應 0–1 kHz；第 1 組（中低）階段 4–5，1–4 kHz；第 2 組（中高）階段 6–7，4–10 kHz；第 3 組（高音）階段 8–9，10–22 kHz。注意分配是不均勻的——低頻拿到最多階段，符合聽覺與音樂訊號的能量分布。

2. 累積解碼（Cumulative Decoding）。 這是保住諧波脈絡的關鍵。第 k 組的重建使用所有累積的低頻潛表示 ẑ≤k = Σ_{j≤k} Σ_{ℓ∈S_j} q^(ℓ)，也就是說解碼高頻時，解碼器看得到所有已重建的低頻內容——200 Hz 的基頻可以指引 800 Hz 泛音的重建。接著用 stop-gradient 把第 k 組自己的貢獻隔離出來：x̂_k = G(sg[ẑ≤k−1] + ẑ_k) − sg[x̂≤k−1]。stop-gradient 在這裡是防止梯度回流污染前面的組，讓每組專注在自己的增量上。

3. 子帶監督與軟頻帶加權。 每組的波形增量對一個加權 mel 頻譜目標計算 L1：L_band^(k) = Σ_m Σ_t w_k(m) |M_{m,t}(x̂_k) − M_{m,t}(x)|。權重 w_k 不是硬切的二值遮罩，而是可學習的高斯：w̃_k(m) = β + (1−β)·exp(−(m−μ_k)²/2σ_k²)，其中 β=0.3 是地板值（確保組外頻率不會被完全忽略），μ_k（中心）與 σ_k（頻寬）可學習，初始化為 σ_k = M/(3K)、M=80 個 mel bin 涵蓋 0–22 kHz，權重再正規化以維持損失量級一致。作者明講頻帶邊界是「透過軟監督施加的近似目標，不是剛性的二值約束」。

總損失只是在標準 codec 損失後面加一項：L = L_rec + λ_adv·L_adv + λ_fm·L_fm + λ_vq·L_vq + λ_band·Σ_k L_band^(k)。就這樣——沒有新模組、沒有新參數（除了 K 組的 μ、σ），推論時完全等同標準 RVQ。

Three mechanisms stack to make HARP.

1. Frequency-ordered grouping. The L RVQ stages are split into K frequency-ordered groups. The default is K=4 over L=9 stages: group 0 (bass) stages 1–3 for 0–1 kHz; group 1 (low-mid) stages 4–5 for 1–4 kHz; group 2 (high-mid) stages 6–7 for 4–10 kHz; group 3 (treble) stages 8–9 for 10–22 kHz. Note the allocation is deliberately uneven — low frequencies get the most stages, matching both perception and the energy distribution of musical signals.

2. Cumulative decoding. This is what preserves harmonic context. Group k's reconstruction uses all accumulated lower-frequency latents, ẑ≤k = Σ_{j≤k} Σ_{ℓ∈S_j} q^(ℓ), so when decoding high frequencies the decoder can see everything already reconstructed below — a 200 Hz fundamental guides reconstruction of its 800 Hz partial. Group k's own contribution is then isolated with a stop-gradient: x̂_k = G(sg[ẑ≤k−1] + ẑ_k) − sg[x̂≤k−1]. The stop-gradient prevents gradient bleed into earlier groups so each group focuses on its own increment.

3. Subband supervision with soft band weighting. Each group's waveform increment is scored by an L1 loss against a weighted mel-spectrogram target: L_band^(k) = Σ_m Σ_t w_k(m) |M_{m,t}(x̂_k) − M_{m,t}(x)|. The weights w_k are not hard binary masks but a learnable Gaussian: w̃_k(m) = β + (1−β)·exp(−(m−μ_k)²/2σ_k²), where β=0.3 is a floor (so out-of-band frequencies are never fully ignored), and μ_k (centre) and σ_k (bandwidth) are learned, initialised at σ_k = M/(3K) with M=80 mel bins spanning 0–22 kHz, then normalised to keep loss magnitudes consistent. The authors are explicit that band boundaries are "approximate targets enforced through soft supervision, not rigid binary constraints."

The total loss simply appends one term to the standard codec objective: L = L_rec + λ_adv·L_adv + λ_fm·L_fm + λ_vq·L_vq + λ_band·Σ_k L_band^(k). That is all — no new modules, no new parameters beyond K pairs of μ and σ, and inference identical to standard RVQ.

限制Limitations

作者沒有寫明確的 limitations 段落，這本身是這篇最明顯的弱點；以下是我讀後的判斷，請視為我的評論而非作者陳述。

數據透明度不足：論文正文我能取到的部分並未完整報出客觀指標的數值（如 SI-SNR、PESQ、ViSQOL），也沒有 MUSHRA 的受試者人數、分數與信賴區間，只說「在兩個位元率條件下有感知改善」。訓練資料僅以「語音、音樂、一般音訊」帶過，未指名資料集，這讓與 DAC／EnCodec 的比較難以校準。

設計上的疑問：K=4 與四個頻帶切點（1 / 4 / 10 / 22 kHz）是手工設定，論文未報告對 K 或切點的敏感度分析；高斯權重可學習雖然優雅，但 μ_k 在訓練中可能漂移甚至跨組重疊，論文沒有展示最終學到的 μ、σ 值——這是我最想看到的一張圖。此外，β=0.3 的地板意味著每組其實仍看到全頻譜的 30% 權重，所謂「特化」是程度問題而非二分。

最關鍵的空缺是動機與驗證之間的落差：論文以「截斷 codebook 時退化不可控」作為主要動機，卻（就我取得的內容而言）沒有把「不同截斷階數下的品質曲線」當作核心實驗來報告。這正是 HARP 應該最發光的場景。另外，完全沒有下游任務評估——沒有測這些頻率有序的 token 在音樂 LM、轉譜或分離上是否更好用。

The authors provide no explicit limitations section, which is itself the paper's most visible weakness. What follows is my assessment, not the authors' statements.

Reporting transparency is thin: the portion of the text I could retrieve does not give numeric values for objective metrics (SI-SNR, PESQ, ViSQOL), nor MUSHRA participant counts, scores or confidence intervals — only that perceptual improvements hold "at two bitrate conditions." Training data is described only as "speech, music, and general audio" without named datasets, which makes calibration against DAC or EnCodec difficult.

Design questions: K=4 and the band cutoffs (1 / 4 / 10 / 22 kHz) are hand-set, and no sensitivity analysis over K or the cutoffs is reported. The learnable Gaussian weighting is elegant, but μ_k may drift or even overlap across groups during training, and the paper does not show the learned μ and σ values — that is the one figure I most want to see. The β=0.3 floor also means every group still receives 30% weight across the full spectrum, so "specialisation" is a matter of degree rather than a clean split.

The most consequential gap is the distance between motivation and validation: uncontrolled degradation under stage truncation is the headline motivation, yet (in the content I could retrieve) quality-versus-truncation-depth curves are not reported as a central experiment. That is precisely where HARP should shine. There is also no downstream evaluation — nothing testing whether these frequency-ordered tokens are more useful for music LMs, transcription or separation.

討論Discussion

這篇對你的價值不在音質數字，而在它讓 codec token 有了語意上可讀的結構。

最直接的推論：如果階段 1–3 就是低頻、8–9 就是高頻，那麼建立在這種 token 上的音樂語言模型，其自迴歸順序就同時是一個頻率上的粗到細順序——先決定低音與基頻骨架，再逐步補上泛音與空氣感。這與人類編曲的思考順序意外地接近，也讓「階層式生成」有了物理意義而非只是工程分層。上一篇（2607.20253）的 hybrid-LM 正好把 c₀ 交給 8B 大模型、c₁–c₇ 交給 0.4B 小模型；若底層 codec 換成 HARP，那個 5:1 的損失加權就從「結構比細節重要」變成了「低頻比高頻重要」——兩篇擺在一起讀會很有意思。

對 MIR 更具體的機會：這是一個現成、低成本的 probing 實驗題目。取 HARP 與標準 RVQ 的 token，在相同下游任務（和弦辨識、多音高估計、樂器辨識、節拍追蹤）上做 linear probing，比較各階段 token 的資訊量分布。假設很明確——HARP 的低頻組應該在低音線與和聲根音上資訊量顯著更高。這個實驗不需要重訓 codec（作者已釋出模型），一台 GPU 就能做完，而且無論結果正反都有話可說。

另一條線是把「累積解碼 + 軟子帶監督」的想法搬到音源分離：分離模型同樣面臨頻帶特化與跨頻帶相干性的取捨，而 stop-gradient 隔離增量的技巧是可移植的。最後提醒一點，這篇仍是預印本、且缺乏完整數字報告，在你自己引用時建議標註「訓練策略層面的貢獻，實驗細節待補」，等 peer-reviewed 版本出來再更新。

The value here for you is not the audio-quality numbers but that it gives codec tokens a semantically legible structure.

The immediate consequence: if stages 1–3 are low frequency and 8–9 are treble, then a music language model built on these tokens has an autoregressive order that is simultaneously a coarse-to-fine order in frequency — settle the bass and fundamentals first, then fill in partials and air. That is unexpectedly close to how humans arrange music, and it gives hierarchical generation a physical meaning rather than merely an engineering one. The previous paper (2607.20253) assigns c₀ to an 8B model and c₁–c₇ to a 0.4B one; swap HARP in underneath and that 5:1 loss weighting shifts from "structure over detail" to "low frequencies over high" — the two papers are interesting read side by side.

A concrete MIR opportunity: this is a ready-made, cheap probing experiment. Take HARP and standard RVQ tokens, run linear probing on the same downstream tasks (chord recognition, multi-pitch estimation, instrument recognition, beat tracking), and compare how information is distributed across stages. The hypothesis is sharp — HARP's low-frequency group should carry markedly more information about bass lines and harmonic roots. The experiment needs no codec retraining (the authors released models), fits on a single GPU, and is publishable either way the result falls.
