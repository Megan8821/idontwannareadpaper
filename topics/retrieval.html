What Are You Listening to? Temporal Music Grounding for Audio-to-Text Large Language Models

你在聽什麼？音頻轉文字大型語言模型的音樂時序定位

Kun Fang, Ziyu Wang, Ichiro Fujinaga
arXiv:2608.29480 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-31
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：提出「音樂時序定位」任務與 MusicGroundingBench 基準，用可控生成的鋼琴 MIDI 音訊測試模型能不能把答案釘在真正的時間區段上，而不是只看最後文字答對沒；結果顯示 Music Flamingo 與 Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct 這兩個現成音頻語言模型在零樣本下幾乎定位失敗（音符級 IoU≥0.5 僅 0.0842 與 0.0006），但針對任務微調過的自製模型能拉到 0.57–0.81，顯示問題出在訓練資料而非任務本身做不到。Why this one: Introduces temporal music grounding and the MusicGroundingBench benchmark, using controlled synthetic piano MIDI audio to test whether models can localize their answers to real time spans rather than just getting the final text answer right; results show two off-the-shelf audio-language models, Music Flamingo and Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct, largely fail to ground in zero-shot settings (note-level IoU>=0.5 of only 0.0842 and 0.0006), while task-specifically fine-tuned custom models reach 0.57-0.81, indicating the gap is about training data rather than the task being inherently unsolvable.

動機Motivation

大型音頻語言模型能生出聽起來很像那麼回事、音樂上也說得通的回答，但論文指出這不代表回答真的「紮根」在音訊輸入上——現有的音樂評測大多只看最終文字輸出對不對，不會去檢查模型的答案是不是真的對應到一段具體的時間區間。這件事在音樂上特別重要，因為要判斷的目標常常是關係性的、重複出現的，或由結構定義出來的，而不是單一、可以一眼抓到的聲音事件，模型很容易講出聽起來合理、其實沒有真正「聽」過的答案。

視覺與影片領域的定位任務已經研究很久，但現有的音訊定位工作幾乎都只做環境音、語音事件，或很粗略的時間對齊——音樂需要的精細時序定位是一塊沒人碰過的空白，這篇論文要填的正是這個洞。

Large audio-language models can produce responses that sound fluent and musically plausible, but the paper argues this doesn't mean the responses are actually grounded in the audio input -- existing music evaluations mostly check whether the final text answer is correct, without testing whether that answer corresponds to a specific, real time span. This matters especially for music, where the relevant target is often relational, repeated, or structurally defined rather than a single, easily-spotted acoustic event, making it easy for a model to produce a plausible-sounding answer without genuinely perceiving it.

Grounding has been studied extensively in vision and video, but existing audio grounding work focuses almost entirely on environmental sounds, speech events, or coarse temporal alignment -- the fine-grained temporal localization music requires is an unaddressed gap, and this paper sets out to fill it.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

先前的定位系統多半來自視覺與影片領域，論文點名了 TALL、Proposal-Free Video Grounding、UniVTG、SlowFocus 等具體方法，以及 BLIP-2 這類具備定位能力的多模態 LLM。音訊定位方面，論文點名 AudioTime 跟時序音訊問答的既有工作，但指出它們鎖定的是環境音事件或粗粒度對齊，不是音樂。

音樂專用的音頻語言模型方面，論文點名 Audio Flamingo 系列（1–3 代）、MusiLingo、Music Understanding LLaMA、MuMu-LLaMA、LLARK，以及專門評測音樂理解的基準 MuChoMusic——MuChoMusic 已經指出「現有音樂導向的音頻語言模型在感知與概念層級的理解上仍然有限」，但它評的一樣是最終答案對不對，不是答案有沒有對應到正確的時間區段。這篇論文跟以上所有工作的差異在於：明確要求模型輸出時間區間、而不只是答對最終問題，並藉此打造出第一個針對音樂的受控定位基準。

Prior grounding systems mostly come from vision and video; the paper names concrete methods such as TALL, Proposal-Free Video Grounding, UniVTG, and SlowFocus, plus grounding-capable multimodal LLMs like BLIP-2. On audio grounding it names AudioTime and prior temporal audio QA work, but notes these target environmental sound events or coarse alignment, not music.

On music-specific audio-language models it names the Audio Flamingo series (1-3), MusiLingo, Music Understanding LLaMA, MuMu-LLaMA, and LLARK, plus the MuChoMusic benchmark for evaluating music understanding -- MuChoMusic already showed that "current music-oriented audio-language models remain limited in perceptual and concept-level understanding," but it still only evaluates whether the final answer is correct, not whether that answer maps to the right time span. This paper's difference from all of the above is requiring models to output actual time spans rather than just a correct final answer, building the first controlled grounding benchmark specifically for music.

方法Method

任務定義是：給模型一段音訊跟一個自然語言查詢，模型要回傳對應到查詢音樂事件或型態的時間區間。MusicGroundingBench 分成兩個子集。MGBench-3N 是音符級定位：用 PianoTeq 9 Stage 把 MIDI 合成成最多三個連續音符的單聲部鋼琴片段，涵蓋全部 88 個琴鍵，訓練/驗證/測試各 4200/900/900 段，查詢分成絕對音高、相對比較、序數指涉、型態關係四類，共 102900 組問答對，並刻意維持 15% 的空答案比例。

MGBench-2B 是結構化定位與理解：用演算法生成、具備精確符號-音訊對齊的單聲部兩小節片段（訓練/驗證/測試 9007/1128/1124 段），控制拍號、速度、調性、音域、和聲內容、重複型態等變數，定位問答對 63240 組（涵蓋節奏、調性、音程、小節比較、和弦、重複、和聲型態七類），另有 82204 組理解問答對。比較對象包含用 MERT 音訊特徵搭配 FiLM/XAttn 的傳統條件機制、外部音頻 LLM 基線 Music Flamingo 與 Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct，以及論文自製、用 LoRA 微調且同樣接上 MERT 特徵的 LLaMA-3-8B 與 Qwen3.5-9B-Base。

The task is defined as: given an audio clip and a natural-language query, the model returns a time span corresponding to the queried musical event or pattern. MusicGroundingBench has two subsets. MGBench-3N is note-level grounding: monophonic piano clips of up to three sequential notes, synthesized from MIDI via PianoTeq 9 Stage across all 88 keys, split 4,200/900/900 for train/val/test, with queries in four families (absolute pitch, relative comparison, ordinal reference, pattern relation) totaling 102,900 QA pairs, deliberately keeping a 15% empty-answer ratio.

MGBench-2B is structured grounding and understanding: algorithmically generated monophonic two-bar excerpts with exact symbolic-to-audio alignment (9,007/1,128/1,124 for train/val/test), controlling meter, tempo, tonality, register, harmonic content, and repetition type, with 63,240 grounding QA pairs across seven categories (rhythm, tonality, interval, bar comparison, chord, repetition, harmonic pattern) plus 82,204 understanding QA pairs. Comparisons include classic conditioning mechanisms (FiLM, XAttn) on MERT audio features, external audio-LLM baselines Music Flamingo and Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct, and the paper's own LoRA-tuned LLaMA-3-8B and Qwen3.5-9B-Base, both also built on MERT features.

限制Limitations

基準完全來自演算法生成的合成 MIDI 音訊，不是真實錄音，論文自己承認「這不是要解決音樂定位問題的完整方案，而是一個受控的第一步」。MGBench-3N 的音樂內容刻意稀疏、幾乎沒有樂句結構；所有音訊都是單聲部鋼琴，不涵蓋複音音樂、管弦編制或不同樂器；就連 MGBench-2B 也只涵蓋兩小節的片段，不是完整曲式或長篇結構。

理解類問題的正確性評分是二元的（對或錯），可能掩蓋部分理解的情況；而且理解評測完全依賴單一個 LLM（Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct）當裁判，可能帶入系統性偏誤。

The benchmark relies entirely on algorithmically generated synthetic MIDI audio rather than real recordings, and the authors acknowledge "this design is not intended as a comprehensive solution to music grounding, but as a carefully controlled first step." MGBench-3N's musical content is sparse and tightly controlled, with minimal phrase structure; all audio is strictly monophonic piano, not representative of polyphonic music, orchestration, or diverse instruments; and even MGBench-2B covers only two-bar excerpts, not full compositions or longer-form structure.

Correctness scoring for understanding questions is binary (right or wrong), which can mask partial understanding, and the understanding evaluation relies entirely on a single LLM judge (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), which may introduce systematic bias.

討論Discussion

零樣本下的兩個現成模型幾乎定位失敗：Music Flamingo 在 MGBench-3N 上音符級 IoU≥0.5 只有 0.0842、F1 僅 0.0342，Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct 更是幾乎掛零（IoU≥0.5 = 0.0006，F1 = 0.0000）；在 MGBench-2B 上兩者也只有 0.1118 與 0.0647。

相對地，經過任務專屬微調的自製模型表現明顯不同：LLaMA-3-8B 在 MGBench-3N 拿到 IoU≥0.5 = 0.5796、F1 = 0.7569，起訖點的平均絕對誤差只有 17.93/51.87 毫秒；Qwen3.5-9B-Base 在結構更複雜的 MGBench-2B 上更進一步衝到 IoU≥0.5 = 0.8107、F1 = 0.9221，起訖誤差壓到 9.34/13.28 毫秒。這說明現成音頻語言模型的失敗主要是訓練資料沒教過這個任務，不是任務本身做不到。

定位訓練跟理解表現之間的關係則因模型而異、沒有一致方向：對 LLaMA-3-8B，加入定位資料並不會提升整體理解準確率（反而從 0.6726 微降到 0.6646）；但對 Qwen3.5-9B-Base，混合定位資料反而讓整體準確率從 0.6512 進步到 0.6868。論文因此把這個基準定位成「用來檢驗音頻語言模型是否真的把回答釘在時間證據上的受控測試場」，並承諾釋出資料集、程式碼與模型。

The two off-the-shelf models nearly fail at zero-shot grounding: Music Flamingo reaches only IoU>=0.5 = 0.0842 and F1 = 0.0342 on MGBench-3N, while Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct is essentially at zero (IoU>=0.5 = 0.0006, F1 = 0.0000); on MGBench-2B both remain low at 0.1118 and 0.0647. In contrast, task-specifically fine-tuned custom models perform very differently: LLaMA-3-8B reaches IoU>=0.5 = 0.5796 and F1 = 0.7569 on MGBench-3N, with onset/offset mean absolute error of just 17.93/51.87 ms; Qwen3.5-9B-Base goes further on the more complex MGBench-2B, reaching IoU>=0.5 = 0.8107 and F1 = 0.9221, with onset/offset error down to 9.34/13.28 ms. This suggests the off-the-shelf models' failure is mainly about training data never covering this task, not the task being unsolvable.

The relationship between grounding training and understanding performance is model-dependent with no consistent direction: for LLaMA-3-8B, adding grounding data doesn't improve overall understanding accuracy (it slightly drops from 0.6726 to 0.6646), but for Qwen3.5-9B-Base, mixing in grounding data raises overall accuracy from 0.6512 to 0.6868. The paper positions this benchmark as "a controlled testbed for assessing whether audio-language models ground their responses in temporally localized musical evidence," and commits to releasing the datasets, code, and models.

On the Human and Computer Alignment of Attribute-Based Music Matches

屬性層級的音樂相似度判斷：人類與電腦指標的對齊程度

Roser Batlle-Roca, Woosung Choi, Joan Serrà, Fabio Morreale, Wei-Hsiang Liao, Xavier Serra, Emilia Gómez, Yuki Mitsufuji
arXiv:2609.00987 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-01
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：建立 MATCHA 資料集，讓 83 位專業音樂人針對「旋律、和聲、節奏、聲部、音色」五個屬性做三選一的音樂相似度判斷；人類對人類作曲的翻唱／抄襲案例判斷一致度高（如旋律 Fleiss' κ≈0.60），但對 AI 生成音樂（Stable Audio Open）的判斷幾乎降到機會水準（κ≤0.19），而四個常見計算相似度指標沒有一個能同時對齊全部五個屬性。Why this one: Introduces MATCHA, having 83 expert musicians make three-way forced-choice similarity judgments across five attributes (melody, harmony, rhythm, voice, timbre); human agreement on human-composed cover/plagiarism cases is high (e.g. melody Fleiss' kappa ~=0.60) but collapses to near-chance on AI-generated (Stable Audio Open) cases (kappa <=0.19), and none of four common computational similarity metrics aligns with all five attributes.

動機Motivation

生成式 AI 音樂系統越來越容易做出跟人類作品高度相似的曲子，這牽動著訓練資料是否被複製、創作者的著作權與生計等爭議。過去評估「這首生成音樂是不是抄了某首訓練資料」的做法多半用單一整體相似度分數，但論文指出「音樂相似度本質上是主觀的，而且取決於多個音樂維度」——把旋律、和聲這些不同屬性混在一個分數裡，會掩蓋掉評審者之間真正的分歧點在哪裡。

論文的核心假設是：如果把「整體像不像」拆成「旋律像不像」「節奏像不像」等屬性層級的判斷，人類評審之間的一致性應該會提高，因為每次只需要針對單一維度做判斷。論文因此設計了一個三選一的三元組比較實驗，同時想知道：人類在屬性層級上的判斷一致嗎？現有的計算相似度指標，能不能對齊人類在各個屬性上的判斷？

Generative AI music systems increasingly produce tracks highly similar to human-composed music, raising disputes over training-data replication, creator copyright, and livelihoods. Prior work assessing whether a generated track replicates a specific training example has typically used a single overall similarity score, but the paper argues "musical similarity is inherently subjective, and depends on multiple music dimensions" -- collapsing melody, harmony, and other distinct attributes into one number obscures where evaluators actually disagree.

The paper's core hypothesis is that breaking "how similar overall" down into attribute-level judgments -- how similar is the melody, the rhythm, and so on -- should raise agreement among human raters, since each judgment only concerns one dimension. It therefore designs a three-way forced-choice triplet comparison experiment to ask two questions: do humans agree at the attribute level, and do existing computational similarity metrics align with human judgments on each attribute?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文採用既有的「音樂複製評估」（MiRA）框架作為對照的計算指標，框架本身包含四個具體方法：CoverID（翻唱曲辨識，鎖定旋律與和聲層面的對應）、以 PaSST 音訊分類器為基礎的 KL 散度、CLAP（對比式語言-音訊預訓練嵌入）、以及 Discogs-EffNet（捕捉更廣的語意、風格與音色關係）。這四個指標各自針對複製偵測設計，但論文指出它們幾乎都沒有拿真人評審的屬性層級判斷驗證過。

論文也點名了翻唱辨識（version identification）文獻——即便樂器編制、速度、音色、錄音條件不同，也要偵測出是同一首作品——以及用 CLMR（音樂對比式表徵學習）與 CLAP 這類嵌入方法做相似度比對的既有做法。這篇論文跟它們的差異，不是提出新的相似度指標，而是先建立一個「人類在屬性層級上到底同不同意」的行為學基準，再回頭檢驗 MiRA 這四個既有指標跟這個基準對不對得上——換句話說，論文的定位是評測既有指標，而不是取代它們。

The paper adopts an existing computational framework, Music Replication Assessment (MiRA), as its comparison point; it comprises four concrete metrics: CoverID (cover-song identification, focused on melodic and harmonic correspondence), KL divergence based on the PaSST audio classifier, CLAP (Contrastive Language-Audio Pretraining embeddings), and Discogs-EffNet (capturing broader semantic, stylistic, and timbral relationships). Each was designed for replication detection, but the paper notes none had been validated against human attribute-level judgments before.

It also cites version-identification literature -- detecting the same underlying work despite differing instrumentation, tempo, timbre, or recording conditions -- and embedding-based similarity approaches using CLMR (Contrastive Learning of Musical Representations) and CLAP. This paper's difference from all of them is not proposing a new similarity metric, but first establishing a behavioral baseline of whether humans even agree at the attribute level, then checking whether MiRA's four existing metrics track that baseline -- its role is evaluating existing metrics, not replacing them.

方法Method

MATCHA 資料集由 300 組三元組構成，每組由 83 位受試者中的部分人評分，共收集 1105 筆評分（每組平均 3.7 筆）。受試者以專業或半專業音樂人為主（63.8% 是有經驗的專業音樂人），並用 30 組（10%）品質控制題篩掉隨意作答者（得分 ≤0.25 者被排除）。三元組分四類：人類抄襲案例（HP，75 組，來自 SMP 資料集）、人類翻唱版本（HV，75 組，來自 Discogs-VI）、AI 生成案例（AS，120 組，來自 Stable Audio Open）、以及媒體報導過的 AI 抄襲爭議案例（AM，30 組）。

AI 生成案例的產生分兩種：一種是「較不相似」樣本，純粹用文字描述（先用 Music Flamingo 抽取描述、再用 QWEN 3.5 摘要）從隨機噪聲生成；另一種是「較相似」樣本，用 DDIM 反演（把參考音訊反推回噪聲、再重新取樣生成，中間加入受控隨機性）來刻意製造跟參考曲目更接近的版本，取樣時的無分類器引導強度為 -6.0（反演）與 6.0（生成），共跑 100 步擴散。受試者針對每個三元組，在旋律、和聲、節奏、聲部、音色五個屬性上分別做三選一判斷（比較樣本 A、比較樣本 B，或都不像）。

The MATCHA dataset comprises 300 triplets, rated by a subset of 83 participants, collecting 1105 total ratings (3.7 per triplet on average). Participants are mostly professional or semi-professional musicians (63.8% experienced professionals), with 30 triplets (10%) used as attention checks to filter careless raters (those scoring <=0.25 were excluded). The triplets split into four categories: Human-Plagiarism (HP, 75 cases, from the SMP dataset), Human-Versions (HV, 75 cases, from Discogs-VI), AI-generated (AS, 120 cases, from Stable Audio Open), and AI cases documented in media reports (AM, 30 cases).

AI-generated cases come in two kinds: "less similar" samples generated from random noise conditioned purely on text descriptions (extracted via Music Flamingo, summarized by QWEN 3.5), and "more similar" samples produced via DDIM inversion -- inverting the reference audio back to noise and resampling with controlled stochasticity to deliberately produce a closer match, using classifier-free guidance scales of -6.0 (inversion) and 6.0 (generation) over 100 diffusion steps. For each triplet, participants made a three-way forced choice (excerpt A, excerpt B, or neither) separately for melody, harmony, rhythm, voice, and timbre.

限制Limitations

人類一致度的數字本身就分裂成兩個世界：在人類抄襲案例（HP）上，旋律屬性的一致度約 88%、Fleiss' κ≈0.60，屬於中等偏高的一致；但在 AI 生成案例（AS）上，所有屬性的 κ 值都 ≤0.19，而且有 52–64% 的評分選了「都不像」（相較其他子集只有 17–34%），論文形容這個結果「近乎不可解讀」。這代表整份資料集裡，人類創作內容跟 AI 生成內容的相似度判斷，其實是兩種不同的評測情境，不能一概而論。

在人類-計算對齊的部分，論文自己承認「沒有任何一個指標能對齊全部屬性，不同指標捕捉的是互補的音樂維度」——CoverID 對旋律與和聲對齊較好，DEfNet 在節奏結構上稍微一致，CLAP 擅長聲部（人聲）相似度，KL 散度則完全抓不到旋律或和聲層面的相似性。論文也承認研究範圍侷限在西方音樂傳統、僅涵蓋五個屬性（不含歌詞、結構、力度動態、情緒等），且刺激樣本是從「已知高度相似」的來源挑出來的，不能代表隨機配對曲目時的一致度。

Human agreement itself splits into two different worlds. On human-plagiarism cases (HP), melody agreement is around 88% with Fleiss' kappa ~=0.60, a moderate-to-high level. On AI-generated cases (AS), every attribute's kappa is <=0.19, and 52-64% of ratings chose "neither" (versus 17-34% in other subsets) -- the paper describes this as "near-uninterpretable." In other words, whatever consistency exists for human-composed similarity judgments does not carry over to AI-generated content; the two cannot be treated as the same evaluation task.

On human-computational alignment, the paper concedes "no single metric aligns with all attributes, and different metrics capture complementary musical dimensions" -- CoverID aligns better with melody and harmony, DEfNet is somewhat more consistent for rhythmic structure, CLAP excels at vocal/voice similarity, and KL divergence fails to capture melodic or harmonic similarity at all. The authors also acknowledge the study is limited to Western musical traditions, covers only five attributes (excluding lyrics, structure, dynamics, or emotional content), and drew stimuli from sources with pre-established strong similarity, so results may not generalize to randomly paired tracks.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有價值的發現，不是「屬性層級判斷比整體判斷一致度高」這個直覺上的假設被證實（雖然確實如此），而是它意外揭露了一個評測方法論的斷層：目前用來偵測 AI 音樂複製的計算指標（CoverID、CLAP、KL 散度、DEfNet），全部都是拿人類創作內容的相似度直覺校準出來的，但人類評審面對 AI 生成內容時，判斷本身就近乎隨機（κ≤0.19），這代表現在拿這些指標去審查 AI 生成內容是否抄襲，其實缺乏一個站得住腳的人類判斷基準可以對照。

換句話說，論文標題問的是「人類跟電腦對不對得齊」，但更根本的問題其實是「人類自己對 AI 生成內容的相似度判斷，能不能形成一個可靠的基準」——如果連人類評審對著 AI 生成的三元組都經常選「都不像」而不是明確判斷，那麼任何跟這類人類評分做比對的計算指標，都繼承了同一個不穩定的地基。論文對這一層自我指涉的困境著墨不多，只在討論裡簡短帶過「相似性本身不等於抄襲或侵權」，值得更進一步追問。

The most valuable finding here isn't the confirmed hypothesis that attribute-level judgments raise agreement over holistic ones -- it's an unintended methodological gap this exposes: the computational metrics currently used to detect AI music replication (CoverID, CLAP, KL divergence, DEfNet) are all calibrated against human intuitions about human-composed similarity, yet when the same human raters judge AI-generated content, their agreement is itself near chance (kappa <=0.19). That means there's no solid human-judgment baseline to check these metrics against when the content in question is AI-generated in the first place.

The paper's title asks whether humans and computers align, but the more fundamental question is whether human judgments of AI-generated similarity form a reliable baseline at all -- if human raters facing AI-generated triplets frequently pick "neither" rather than a clear match, any computational metric benchmarked against those ratings inherits the same instability. The paper touches this self-referential problem only briefly, noting in passing that "similarity alone is not directly equivalent to plagiarism or copyright infringement," a point that deserves more direct follow-up.

動機Motivation

Though the piano is a single instrument, its polyphony conveys rich semantics spanning genre, mood, and style. Yet existing piano-specific representation models, such as MidiBERT-Piano and PianoBART, handle only the symbolic (MIDI) modality and never integrate audio or textual semantics, while general-purpose cross-modal joint-embedding models cover audio, symbolic, and text but lack discriminative power for the homogeneous solo-piano domain, since their training data skews toward multi-instrument or vocal-centric music. The question the authors target: can a piano-specific joint-embedding model integrating audio, MIDI, and text be trained effectively with far less data than general-purpose models, on a domain that is inherently homogeneous?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景一節點名了具體的既有系統,並分成兩條路線比較。符號音樂路線包括 PianoTree VAE、CollageNet 這類專注拆解節奏/和聲/織體的可控生成模型,以及 MidiBERT-Piano 與 PianoBART 這兩個基於 Transformer 的大規模鋼琴 MIDI 預訓練模型——但它們全部停留在符號模態,不涉及音訊或文字。跨模態聯合嵌入路線則點名 MuLan(4400 萬筆音訊-文字配對)、MusCALL(音訊-文字雙編碼器)、CLAP、TTMR/TTMR++,以及符號-文字方向的 CLaMP 系列(CLaMP、CLaMP2、CLaMP3),其中 CLaMP3 首次做到音訊、符號、文字三模態對齊,是本文在表 2 中直接對照的最強基準。

The background section names concrete prior systems along two lines. The symbolic-music line includes PianoTree VAE and CollageNet, which decompose rhythm/harmony/texture for controllable generation, plus MidiBERT-Piano and PianoBART, Transformer-based large-scale piano MIDI pretraining models -- but all of these stay purely symbolic, with no audio or text. The cross-modal joint-embedding line names MuLan (44 million audio-text pairs), MusCALL (an audio-text dual encoder), CLAP, TTMR/TTMR++, and the symbolic-text CLaMP series (CLaMP, CLaMP2, CLaMP3), where CLaMP3 is the first to align audio, symbolic, and text trimodally and serves as the strongest baseline directly compared against in Table 2.

PianoBind's real departure from these systems isn't a new encoder architecture -- its audio encoder reuses MusCALL's ResNet-50, its symbolic encoder is an off-the-shelf MidiBERT, and its text encoder an off-the-shelf RoBERTa; none of the three is a new design. The actual difference is in training strategy: for piano music's specific situation of small, homogeneous data, PianoBind systematically compares two multi-source training strategies (combined training vs. pre-train-then-fine-tune), and aligns all three modalities using the average of the audio-text and MIDI-text contrastive losses, rather than CLaMP3's staged saas (symbolic-to-audio-to-audio-to-symbolic) alignment. The paper's own ablation (Table 3) shows that swapping CLaMP3's saas alignment for this paper's averaged loss lifts in-domain R@10 from 13.56% to 52.76%.

方法Method

PianoBind 的架構本身沒有新元件:音訊走 ResNet-50 處理 128 維梅爾頻譜(沿用 MusCALL),符號走 MidiBERT 處理 Compound Word 編碼的 MIDI(取最後一層隱藏狀態的平均池化),文字走 RoBERTa(同樣用平均池化取代 BERT 式的 [CLS] pooling)。三個編碼器分別投影到 512 維共享空間,用 InfoNCE 對比損失對齊音訊-文字與 MIDI-文字兩對,再取兩者損失的平均作為總損失——這一步刻意不加入音訊-MIDI 損失,因為 MIDI 本身是從音訊轉譜出來的,直接對齊不會帶來額外語意訊息。

訓練資料是 PIAST 資料集:7,367 首、約 900 小時、文字為自動蒐集(YouTube 標題/描述,經 LLM 清理)的 PIAST-YT,以及 1,986 首、約 17 小時、專家標註文字的 PIAST-AT。表 1 比較兩種多來源訓練法:混合訓練(兩來源按 7:3 比例混合)vs. 先在 PIAST-YT 預訓練、再用 PIAST-AT 微調。結果是後者全面勝出——三模態設定下,域內 Median Rank 從混合訓練的 17 降到 10,域外(EMOPIA-Caps,88 首)R@10 從混合訓練的 46.15% 升到 51.28%。跟表 2 列出的通用模型比較,PianoBind 域內 Median Rank(10)遠優於最強對手 CLaMP3(symbolic 版,Median Rank 39);域外 R@10(51.28%)也優於 CLaMP3 的 46.15%。

PianoBind's architecture has no genuinely new component: audio goes through a ResNet-50 over 128-band mel-spectrograms (reusing MusCALL), symbolic input goes through MidiBERT over Compound-Word-encoded MIDI (mean-pooled over the last hidden layer), and text goes through RoBERTa (also mean-pooled rather than using BERT-style [CLS] pooling). All three encoders project into a shared 512-dim space, aligned via InfoNCE contrastive loss on audio-text and MIDI-text pairs, with the total loss being their average -- deliberately excluding an audio-MIDI loss, since MIDI here is transcribed directly from audio and wouldn't add new semantic signal.

Training data is the PIAST dataset: PIAST-YT (7,367 tracks, ~900 hours, text auto-collected from YouTube titles/descriptions and cleaned by an LLM) and PIAST-AT (1,986 tracks, ~17 hours, expert-annotated text). Table 1 compares two multi-source training strategies: combined training (a 7:3 mix of the two sources) versus pre-training on PIAST-YT then fine-tuning on PIAST-AT. The latter wins across the board -- in the trimodal setup, in-domain Median Rank drops from 17 (combined) to 10 (pre-train+fine-tune), and out-of-domain (EMOPIA-Caps, 88 tracks) R@10 rises from 46.15% to 51.28%. Against the general-purpose models in Table 2, PianoBind's in-domain Median Rank (10) far beats the strongest competitor CLaMP3 (symbolic variant, Median Rank 39), and its out-of-domain R@10 (51.28%) also beats CLaMP3's 46.15%.

限制Limitations

論文自己在結論明確承認限制:評測資料集(域內 199 首、域外 88 首)規模都偏小,且訓練與評測資料主要集中在流行鋼琴曲風(new-age、鋼琴改編、爵士),缺乏古典或其他多元鋼琴曲風的代表性,論文原文承認這會限制研究結論的可推廣性。除此之外,MIDI 資料並非人工採譜,而是用自動轉譜工具從音訊產生,轉譜誤差會直接帶進符號模態的訓練訊號,論文沒有報告轉譜品質或誤差率。另外,論文比較的 CLaMP3 是套用作者自行實作的「多模態項目嵌入」(把音訊、符號特徵做平均融合)去跟 PianoBind 比較,而不是 CLaMP3 原始論文的設定——公平地說,這代表表 2 的部分對照組是本文作者的重新實作,而非完全復現原作者的最佳結果。

The paper explicitly acknowledges its own limitation in the conclusion: the evaluation datasets are small (199 tracks in-domain, 88 out-of-domain), and both training and evaluation data skew toward pop-piano genres (new-age, piano covers, jazz), lacking classical or other diverse piano styles -- the paper itself admits this constrains how generalizable the findings are. Beyond that, the MIDI data isn't human-transcribed but generated by automatic piano transcription from audio, so transcription errors feed directly into the symbolic modality's training signal, and the paper reports no transcription quality or error rate. Also, the CLaMP3 comparison in Table 2 uses the authors' own reimplementation of a "multimodal item embedding" (averaging audio and symbolic features) rather than CLaMP3's original setup -- to be fair, some of Table 2's baselines are this paper's own reimplementation rather than the original authors' best reported results.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最值得留意的地方,是它示範了一個資料量遠小於業界標準(17 小時精標 vs. MuLan 的 4400 萬筆配對)也能打贏通用模型的具體路徑——關鍵不在架構創新,而在「先廣後專」的訓練順序這個看似平常的策略選擇。這對其他資料稀缺的垂直 MIR 領域(例如特定樂器、特定文化圈的音樂)有直接的參考價值:與其硬湊更大的通用資料集,不如先用弱標註資料建立廣泛表徵,再用少量精標資料校準到目標領域。同時,論文也留下一個尚待驗證的問題——三模態訓練依賴的 MIDI 是從音訊自動轉譜而來,如果換成人工核對過的高品質 MIDI,三模態的優勢會不會被進一步放大,論文沒有做這個對照實驗。

The most notable takeaway here is a concrete demonstration that a dataset far smaller than industry norms (17 hours of expert annotation vs. MuLan's 44 million pairs) can beat general-purpose models -- and the key isn't architectural novelty but the seemingly mundane choice of training order: broad first, then specialized. That has direct relevance for other data-scarce vertical MIR domains (a specific instrument, a specific musical culture): rather than forcing a bigger general-purpose dataset, build broad representations from weakly-labeled data first, then calibrate to the target domain with a small expert-annotated set. The paper also leaves one question untested: since the trimodal setup relies on MIDI auto-transcribed from audio, would the trimodal advantage grow further with human-verified MIDI instead? No such comparison is run.

動機Motivation

現有的音樂 CLAP 模型讓文字查詢跟音樂嵌入對齊,支援用文字檢索音樂,大多靠 LLM 把標籤或網路搜尋結果轉寫成訓練用字幕,這些字幕「準確但表達範圍狹窄」——描述的是客觀的曲風、樂器、情緒標籤,卻缺少「敘事框架」(像是「聽起來就像……」)與「評價性形容詞」(像是「沙啞的人聲」)這類複雜查詢真正會用到的語言。這篇論文要解決的問題,是既有訓練資料的文字風格太單一,導致模型面對表達比較口語、比較主觀的查詢時檢索效果變差。

Existing music CLAP models, which align text queries with music embeddings to support text-based retrieval, mostly rely on an LLM rewriting tags or web-search results into training captions. Such captions are "accurate but expressively narrow": they capture objective genre, instrument, and mood tags but miss the narrative framing (e.g. "feels like...") and evaluative adjectives (e.g. "raspy vocals") that more complex, conversational queries actually use. The problem this paper targets is that the text register of existing training data is too uniform, which hurts retrieval when a real query is phrased more subjectively or narratively.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名幾個具體的既有音樂 CLAP:MusCALL 用私有目錄裡的專業字幕、MuLan 用 YouTube 描述╱標籤╱播放清單、TTMR 用音樂標記資料集、CLaMP3 則是拿歌名與歌手名去網路搜尋再用 LLM 後製。這些系統的共通點是,「大規模使用專輯樂評文字」這件事目前沒有人做過。字幕生成這邊,主流做法是 LP-MusicCaps 開創的「用 LLM 把標籤集合轉寫成自然語言字幕」,後續工作多半是改進提示詞或加上網路檢索增強生成——這篇論文的差異在於把底層文字來源換成專業樂評人寫的長文,而不是標籤或開放網路檢索結果。

訓練目標上,論文把 LeJEPA 的 SigReg 正規化項加進標準的 InfoNCE 對比損失,聲稱這樣可以在不犧牲檢索表現的前提下,拿回等向高斯嵌入原本才有的探測優勢——這是把兩個各自獨立的既有技術組合起來,而不是提出全新的訓練目標。

The paper names specific prior music CLAPs: MusCALL uses professional captions from private catalogs, MuLan uses YouTube descriptions/tags/playlists, TTMR uses music-tagging datasets, and CLaMP3 web-searches song title and artist name and LLM-post-processes the results. Their shared gap, per the paper, is that "album-review text has not been explicitly used at scale" by any of them. On the training objective, the paper adds LeJEPA's SigReg regularizer on top of the standard InfoNCE contrastive loss, claiming this recovers the probing advantage of isotropic Gaussian embeddings without costing retrieval performance -- combining two existing techniques rather than proposing a new objective.

方法Method

資料上,論文先把 94,041 篇 AllMusic 專輯樂評,依作者╱專輯名稱比對到 Discogs 的發行後設資料,再擴充到 245,346 首曲目(含 YouTube 曲目層級後設資料)。字幕生成有兩條管線:AMCQuotes 先用 Qwen 2.5-32B 從樂評裡萃取「事實性描述」的原文引句(限定曲風、製作風格、樂器編制、情緒、節奏,排除主觀評論與傳記資訊),再用第二個 LLM 把引句、Discogs 標籤與 YouTube 後設資料合併改寫成短句字幕;AMCStruct 則是單次呼叫 Llama 3-70B,依固定欄位(曲風、情緒、能量、速度、樂器編制、製作風格)產生結構化字幕,覆蓋率較高但幻覺風險也較高。

Track identification (TI) finds the exact recording matching a query; version identification (VI) finds different performances, covers, or remixes of the same underlying work. The two have always been handled separately, but the authors point out a logical fact: every recording is, trivially, its own closest "version," so a VI method should in principle subsume TI. The catch is that existing VI systems deliberately suppress acoustic detail to bridge different versions, which works against recognizing a recording as its own nearest version; existing TI systems, meanwhile, are rarely trained against pitch- or tempo-altering signal manipulation. Both sides have a real gap.

這篇論文的做法不是重新設計一個嵌入架構,而是先建一個能同時評量 TI 與 VI 準確度與強健性的統一基準,發現沒有一個現有模型同時在兩項任務上既準確又強健,包括通用音語模型 CLAP 也失敗——它在乾淨 TI 上 hit@1 達 89.9%,但在 VI 幾乎完全失效,證明通用音訊嵌入不一定包含「作品身份」這種結構。

Specialized track-ID models -- NMFP, GraFPrint, PeakNetFP -- use short (roughly 1-second) fingerprint embeddings and are hardened via self-supervised training only against recording degradation (noise, reverberation), rarely against signal manipulation. Specialized version-ID models -- CLEWS, ByteCover3, CoverHunter -- use long (20-45 second) embeddings and are trained against signal manipulation to bridge version differences, but only CoverHunter adds noise, and none has been systematically tested for robustness across both. The authors call this the first systematic robustness study of version identification.

Rather than proposing a new embedding architecture, this paper first builds a unified benchmark measuring both accuracy and robustness across both tasks, and finds no existing model is both accurate and robust on both -- including CLAP, a general audio-language model, which reaches 89.9% clean track-ID hit@1 but fails almost entirely at version identification, showing general audio embeddings don't necessarily encode work identity.

方法Method

基準模擬一條現實中的「剪輯-播放-錄音」鏈,將信號操控(音高移調±12半音、時間拉伸×0.5~2.0、播放速度×0.5~2.0)與錄音劣化(限制器、高通濾波、靜態非線性失真、背景噪聲+房間衝激回應、麥克風響應)串接在一起,在 Discogs-VI(116k 首)、SHS100K(8k 首)、NMFP(95k 首)三個資料庫上產生超過一百萬筆查詢,遠超過先前工作只用幾百個片段的規模。

作者提出的 Fish 模型用 CLEWS 架構為底,核心新技巧是建構「片段團(segment cliques)」訓練資料:將 Discogs-VI 切成 20 秒片段並用預訓練 CLEWS 嵌入,計算版本對間的片段相似度矩陣,以每矩陣第 5 百分位數當動態阀值拒絕假陽性,再用洪泛填充(flood-fill)找相似度盆地並取局部極小值作代表對,最後將重疊的對合併成片段團(共 644k 團,來自 317k 首曲)。訓練用三元損失(邊界 0.2)加困難負樣本挖掘,4 張 L40S GPU 跑約 4 天。消融實驗顯示,困難負樣本挖掘比半困難負樣本對最終資料集的效果進步很大,但作者誠實寫道這下的訓練收斂但推理不穩定,目前無法定位根本原因。

The benchmark models a realistic "editing-playback-capture" chain, chaining signal manipulation (pitch shift ±12 semitones, time stretch ×0.5-2.0, playback speed ×0.5-2.0) with recording degradation (limiter, high-pass filter, static nonlinearity, background noise plus room impulse response, microphone response). Applied across Discogs-VI (116k tracks), SHS100K (8k), and NMFP (95k), it generates over one million queries, far beyond prior work's scale of a few hundred segments.

The authors' Fish model builds on the CLEWS architecture; its main new technique is constructing "segment cliques" for training. Discogs-VI is cut into 20-second segments, embedded with a pretrained CLEWS, and segment-level similarity matrices are computed between version pairs; a dynamic threshold (5th percentile per matrix) rejects false positives, a flood-fill algorithm finds connected similarity basins, and local minima serve as representative pairs, finally merging overlapping pairs into segment cliques (644k cliques from 317k tracks). Training uses triplet loss (margin 0.2) with hard negative mining, on 4 L40S GPUs for about 4 days. Ablation shows hard negative mining substantially outperforms semi-hard mining on the final dataset, though the authors candidly note training converges but inference is unstable on non-final datasets, and they cannot yet pin down the root cause.

The authors flag several limitations. The query length is fixed at 10 seconds, leaving shorter-query performance unstudied. Segment duration trades off intrinsically: 10-second segments give 92.7% track-ID hit@1 but only 0.529 version-ID MAP@10k; switching to 20-second segments lifts version-ID MAP@10k to 0.780 but slightly drops track-ID hit@1 to 91.9% -- no single duration optimizes both. The authors also diagnose that Fish's remaining gap against the specialized NMFP model on track ID stems partly from the retrieval mechanism itself rather than pure semantic understanding: when database segment length matches query length exactly with zero misalignment, Fish reaches 99.9% track-ID hit@1, showing much of the real-world gap is a retrieval-setup artifact, not a model blind spot.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最強的證據是一組對比數字:在 Discogs-VI 信號操控查詢上,Fish 的曲識別 hit@1 69.7% 遠高於專門模型 NMFP 的 48.7%;在乾淨查詢上,Fish 的版本識別 MAP@10k 0.687 也高於專門模型 CLEWS 的 0.628,在混合失真的情況下差距拉得更開(0.612 對 0.523)。一個模型同時靠近或超越兩個專門模型,支持了作者「VI 可以包容 TI」的中心主張。

方法Method

架構分兩塊:CNN 特徵萃取模組把梅爾頻譜圖逐層卷積、批次正規化、最大池化,輸出攤平成一組 patch embedding,加上可學習的位置編碼後,送進一個雙頭(2-head)自注意力機制,計算 softmax(QK^T/√d_k)V,再用一個可學習的加權向量做「注意力式序列池化」取代常見的全域平均池化,取得最終表示後接全連接層分類。訓練用 AdamW、學習率 0.001、批次 128、100 epoch(patience=10 提前停止),另外套用時間伸縮(0.9-1.1 倍)、音高偏移(±1 半音)、20dB 訊噪比高斯噪音、動態範圍壓縮四種資料增強。

The architecture has two blocks: a CNN feature extractor applies convolution, batch normalization, and max-pooling to mel-spectrograms, flattens the output into patch embeddings, adds a learnable positional encoding, and feeds this into a two-head self-attention block computing softmax(QK^T/sqrt(d_k))V; a learnable weighting vector then does "attention-based sequence pooling" in place of standard global average pooling before a fully-connected classifier head. Training uses AdamW at learning rate 0.001, batch size 128, 100 epochs with early stopping (patience 10), plus four augmentations: time-stretching (0.9-1.1x), pitch-shifting (±1 semitone), 20 dB SNR Gaussian noise, and dynamic-range compression.

Both benchmark datasets are segmented into 30/15/10/6/3-second clips from the original 30-second tracks, with 3-second segments giving the most training instances (10,000 segments for GTZAN, 18,000 for IMG-Amrita, both at an 80/20 track-level split) and, per the authors, the best accuracy: they attribute this to shorter clips both increasing effective sample count and avoiding the "structural transitions and intra-class variability" that dilute discriminative signal in longer segments.

限制Limitations

作者對兩個資料集的品質問題相當坦白:GTZAN 本身「含有重複片段、偶爾標錯、曲風分布不均」,是這個資料集眾所皆知的老問題;IMG-Amrita 雖然是論文的新貢獻,但只由單一位印度音樂專家標注,且「尚未涵蓋印度音樂完整的地域與風格多樣性」。方法論上,論文承認評測只用了統一編碼的 WAV 檔案,「對不同音訊格式與失真壓縮的穩健性尚待驗證」。更根本的問題是論文自己也點出:「曲風本身是主觀且受文化影響的概念,常與情緒、樂器編制、演奏風格等概念重疊」,尤其當代混合曲風的邊界更模糊——這意味著即使準確率數字亮眼,分類目標本身的定義就不完全穩固。

The authors are fairly candid about quality issues in both datasets: GTZAN itself "contains duplicate excerpts, occasional mislabelling, and imbalances in genre representation," a well-known long-standing problem with that benchmark, while IMG-Amrita, though a new contribution, was annotated by a single Indian-music specialist and "does not yet capture the full regional and stylistic diversity of Indian music traditions." Methodologically, the paper admits evaluation only used uniformly encoded WAV files, so "robustness to diverse audio formats and lossy compression schemes remains to be explored." More fundamentally, the authors themselves note that "genre itself is an inherently subjective and culturally influenced concept, often overlapping with mood, instrumentation, and performance style," especially blurred for contemporary hybrid genres — meaning even a strong accuracy number sits on top of a target label that isn't fully stable.

討論Discussion

這篇論文比較能推廣的觀察,其實是一個看起來反直覺的實證結果:3 秒短切片在兩個資料集、三種架構上全部贏過 30 秒完整片段,推翻了「上下文越長分類越準」的預設。但作者對這個現象的解釋(短切片增加樣本數、降低估計變異)只是假設,論文沒有做進一步的控制實驗(例如固定樣本數、只變長度)去驗證到底是樣本量效應還是切片長度本身的效應,這是一個值得注意但論文沒有完全處理掉的因果混淆。跟三個近期方法比較(Hybrid CNN-RNN 89.30%、CNN-Transformer 87.41%、Compact Convolutional Transformer 94.00%),AECNN 的 96.02% 確實領先,但這些對照組是從各自論文的報告數字引用而來,不是在同一套資料切分與訓練條件下重新跑過,所以「贏過轉換器」這個結論的可信度,不如論文自己內部消融實驗來得紮實。

The paper's most transferable observation is a genuinely counterintuitive empirical result: 3-second clips beat full 30-second clips across both datasets and all three architectures, overturning the assumption that longer context always helps classification. But the authors' explanation (shorter clips increase sample count and reduce estimation variance) is only a hypothesis; the paper runs no controlled follow-up (e.g., holding sample count fixed while varying only clip length) to disentangle whether this is a sample-size effect or an effect of clip length itself — a real confound the paper doesn't fully resolve. Against three recent methods (Hybrid CNN-RNN at 89.30%, CNN-Transformer at 87.41%, Compact Convolutional Transformer at 94.00%), AECNN's 96.02% is indeed ahead, but those baselines are quoted from their respective papers rather than re-run under the same data split and training conditions, so the "beats transformers" claim rests on weaker footing than the paper's own internal ablations.

為什麼挑這篇：使用者常常想對檢索結果做細緻調整——「再環境一點、少一點失真、拿掉吉他」——但現有的稠密音樂檢索系統缺乏這種細粒度控制。這篇論文的關鍵洞察是：過去用「哪個神經元的解碼方向跟文字概念的嵌入最像」來挑要操控的稀疏特徵，這套文字側的啟發式方法在多模態情境下會系統性失準，因為文字空間裡跟某個概念最對齊的神經元，不見得是真正在處理該概念的音訊範例裡被啟動的神經元。作者把選特徵這件事重新定義成一個純幾何的「稀疏反演」問題，完全不需要成對的音訊-文字監督或重新訓練稀疏自編碼器，方法簡潔但背後的診斷（模態落差、特徵分裂與吸收）具有超越這篇論文本身的參考價值，因此標記為深度分析。Why this one: Users often want fine-grained control over retrieval results — more ambient, less distorted, no guitar — but existing dense music retrieval systems lack this granularity. This paper's key insight is that the standard approach to picking which sparse feature to edit — ranking neurons by how closely their decoder direction aligns with a concept's text embedding — fails systematically in multimodal settings, because the neurons most aligned with a concept on the text side are not necessarily the neurons that actually activate for audio examples expressing that concept. The authors reframe feature selection as a purely geometric "sparse inversion" problem, requiring no paired audio-text supervision and no sparse-autoencoder retraining. The method itself is simple, but the underlying diagnosis — modality gap, feature splitting, and feature absorption — has reference value beyond this one paper, which is why it's flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

使用者在做音樂檢索時常常想做細緻調整——「再環境一點」「少一點失真」「拿掉吉他但保留其他特質」——這需要對嵌入空間裡的個別語意概念做精準的加強或抑制，但現有的稠密檢索系統缺乏這種細粒度控制介面。作者指出問題的根源在於「概念歸因」（concept attribution）：要操控一個概念，得先找到嵌入空間裡對應這個概念的方向或神經元，而既有方法幾乎都靠文字側的對齊啟發式——用一個概念的文字嵌入去跟稀疏自編碼器（SAE）裡每個解碼器原子算餘弦相似度，挑最像的那個。但這套方法在多模態情境下有一個根本性的缺陷：文字空間裡跟某個概念最對齊的神經元，不保證是音訊範例裡真正被啟動、真正在編碼這個概念的神經元——這就是常說的「模態落差」（modality gap），加上概念在音訊端經常分散在多個神經元上（而非集中在單一神經元），也加上文字對齊的原子容易吸收多個語意因子（feature splitting/absorption），三個因素疊加讓文字側啟發式方法在音訊端變得不可靠。論文用實驗證實了這個診斷：用餘弦相似度選出的「支持集」（support）不論在結構上還是功能上都表現不佳,跟真正的正例音訊支持集重疊度很低。

Users doing music retrieval often want fine-grained adjustments — more ambient, less distorted, no guitar but otherwise similar — which requires precisely amplifying or suppressing individual semantic concepts within an embedding space, an interface existing dense retrieval systems lack. The authors trace the root cause to concept attribution: to steer a concept, you first need to locate the direction or neurons in embedding space that correspond to it, and existing methods almost universally rely on a text-side alignment heuristic — computing cosine similarity between a concept's text embedding and every decoder atom in a sparse autoencoder (SAE), then picking the closest match. This approach has a fundamental flaw in multimodal settings: the neurons most aligned with a concept on the text side are not guaranteed to be the neurons actually activated by, and genuinely encoding, that concept in audio examples — the well-known "modality gap" — compounded by concepts often being distributed across multiple neurons on the audio side rather than localized to one, and by text-aligned atoms tending to absorb multiple semantic factors at once (feature splitting/absorption). Together these make text-side heuristics unreliable on the audio side. The paper backs this diagnosis experimentally: cosine-similarity-selected "supports" perform poorly both structurally and functionally, overlapping only weakly with the true positive audio support set.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景涵蓋三條線。稀疏自編碼器（SAE）本身：近年在語言模型、多模態系統與生成模型的機制可解釋性研究裡，SAE 被廣泛用作把稠密表徵拆成可解讀的稀疏特徵、進而做表徵操控的介面。概念歸因方法：手動或範例式方法（人工檢視神經元、挖掘範例、事後命名）成本高；幾何式探測方法如「Discover-Then-Name」（DTN）把概念的文字嵌入跟 SAE 解碼器原子做對齊，選出最相關的神經元，是目前最接近本篇方法的既有做法。多模態嵌入模型：CLAP 與 MuQ 被列為目前最先進的音樂-文字聯合編碼器，也是這篇論文實驗中拿來抽取音訊嵌入的骨幹。這篇論文與 DTN 這類既有方法的關鍵差異在於問題的定義本身：不把概念歸因當成「獨立地依文字相似度替每個神經元打分排序」，而是重新定義成「稀疏反演問題」——找一組稀疏碼，使其解碼後的表徵既能重建這個概念、又能維持在音訊嵌入空間的幾何結構裡；這個定義本身不需要成對的音訊-文字監督，也不需要重新訓練 SAE，是純粹的推論時（inference-time）方法。

The related work spans three threads. Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) themselves: recent mechanistic-interpretability research on language models, multimodal systems, and generative models has widely used SAEs as an interface for decomposing dense representations into interpretable sparse features and then manipulating them. Concept attribution methods: manual or example-based approaches (inspecting neurons by hand, mining exemplars, post-hoc naming) are costly; geometric probing methods such as Discover-Then-Name (DTN), which align a concept's text embedding to SAE decoder atoms and select the most-aligned neurons, are the closest prior approach to this paper's method. Multimodal embedding models: CLAP and MuQ are cited as state-of-the-art joint music-text encoders and serve as the backbones from which this paper extracts audio embeddings for its experiments. The key difference from DTN-style prior work lies in how the problem itself is framed: rather than independently ranking neurons by text-side similarity, this paper reframes concept attribution as a sparse inversion problem — finding a sparse code whose decoded representation both reconstructs the concept and remains geometrically consistent with the audio embedding space. This framing requires neither paired audio-text supervision nor SAE retraining; it is a purely inference-time method.

方法Method

SAE 訓練設定：音訊嵌入取自 JamendoMaxCaps 資料集，分別用 CLAP 與 MuQ 的音訊塔（皆輸出 512 維嵌入）抽取；SAE 採用 BatchTopK 變體，字典大小 m=4096（擴張倍率 8），測試了五種稀疏度 L0∈{5,10,20,50,100}；訓練用 AdamW（學習率 1e-4）在單張 L40 GPU 上跑 5 萬步，解碼器是綁定編碼器權重的線性層。既有基線 Discover-Then-Name（DTN）的做法是：對每個概念的文字嵌入 z_c，計算它跟每個解碼器原子 d_i 的餘弦相似度，直接選相似度最高的那個原子；作者實測顯示這樣選出來的「支持集」在不同稀疏度下基數變化很大，本質上是挑出一整批神經元的「集合」（bundle）而非單一神經元。核心方法「稀疏模態反演」把問題定義成一個最佳化：在連續空間裡最佳化一個潛在的前激活向量 u，讓它經過訓練時同樣的稀疏化算子 Π 、再經解碼器 D 得到的表徵，跟概念的文字嵌入 z_c 之間的餘弦距離最小。但無約束的反演容易漂移到不像音訊的方向，於是加入一個「流形正則化」項：用馬氏距離衡量反演結果跟從 1 萬筆 MaxCaps 測試集嵌入估計出來的音訊經驗分佈之間的距離，正則化係數固定為 γ=1e-4，確保反演出的概念仍落在合理的音訊空間內。最佳化用兩種策略：一是 Adam 梯度下降（從語意最接近 z_c 的音訊鄰居的稀疏碼開始初始化，避免收斂到爛的局部解）；二是利用解碼器是線性的這個性質，用 FISTA（快速迭代收縮閾值演算法），在 CPU 上單次反演只要約 20 毫秒（沒有正則化項時要約 20 秒）。最後再做一次 IDF 加權後處理，用 log(N/(該神經元在多少樣本中被啟動+ε)) 當權重，抑制過於通用、頻繁啟動的神經元，取加權後啟動值最高的幾個座標當作最終的概念支持集。

The SAE training setup: audio embeddings are extracted from the JamendoMaxCaps dataset using both CLAP and MuQ audio towers (each producing 512-dimensional embeddings); the SAE uses a BatchTopK variant with a dictionary size of m=4096 (expansion factor 8), tested across five sparsity levels L0∈{5,10,20,50,100}; training uses AdamW (learning rate 1e-4) for 50k steps on a single L40 GPU, with the decoder instantiated as a linear layer tied to the encoder weights. The existing Discover-Then-Name (DTN) baseline works by computing cosine similarity between a concept's text embedding z_c and every decoder atom d_i, then selecting the single highest-similarity atom; the authors show empirically that the resulting "support" varies substantially in cardinality across sparsity levels — it is really selecting a bundle of neurons rather than one clean unit. The core method, sparse modality inversion, frames the problem as an optimization: optimize a latent pre-activation vector u in continuous space so that, after passing through the same sparsifying operator Π used during SAE training and then the decoder D, the resulting representation has minimum cosine distance to the concept's text embedding z_c. Unconstrained inversion drifts toward non-audio-like directions, so a manifold regularization term is added: the Mahalanobis distance between the inversion result and an empirical audio distribution estimated from 10k MaxCaps test-set embeddings, with a fixed regularization coefficient γ=1e-4, keeping the recovered concept within plausible audio space. Two optimization strategies are tested: Adam gradient descent (initialized from the sparse code of the nearest audio neighbor to z_c, to avoid converging to a bad local solution), and FISTA (fast iterative shrinkage-thresholding algorithm), which exploits the decoder's linearity to run a single inversion in roughly 20ms on CPU (versus roughly 20 seconds without the regularization term). A final IDF-weighting post-processing step down-weights overly generic, frequently active neurons using log(N/(number of samples in which that neuron activates + ε)) as a weight, with the top weighted-activation coordinates forming the final concept support.

限制Limitations

作者自陳的限制集中在方法對上游元件的依賴：整套方法的效果被底層的文字-音樂嵌入空間品質與 SAE 重建保真度上限卡住，如果一個概念在嵌入空間裡本身就對齊得很弱、很罕見、很抽象，或者在兩個模態間的表徵很分散，反演就可能失敗；SAE 本身固有的重建誤差、特徵分裂、特徵吸收問題,也會一路傳導、疊加到概念歸因的結果上；方法對稀疏度與流形正則化的超參數選擇有一定敏感度,雖然論文說實務上相對穩定；更根本的是,直接套用在稠密嵌入上的 SAE 本質上還是一種「準線性」的操控介面,能做的編輯精細度有其上限。論文自己也承認,在某些概念、支持集大小與編輯強度的組合下仍然存在效果不好的區域，可能來自概念本身的歧義、嵌入空間品質不足，或 SAE 重建保真度不夠這三個原因中的一個或多個，但論文並未進一步拆解是哪一個在主導。此外，評估侷限在 20 個高頻標籤,對稀有或全新概念的表現如何仍是未知；也沒有跟其他近期多模態概念歸因方法做比較；流形正則化的具體設計選擇看起來偏經驗性,對這個選擇的敏感度分析也不完整。

The authors' own stated limitations center on dependency on upstream components: the method's effectiveness is bounded by the quality of the underlying text-music embedding space and the fidelity of the SAE reconstruction — if a concept is weakly grounded, rare, abstract, or diffusely represented across modalities, inversion may fail; inherent SAE issues (reconstruction error, feature splitting, feature absorption) propagate directly into concept-recovery failures; the method shows some sensitivity to sparsity and manifold-regularization hyperparameters, though the paper reports it is reasonably stable in practice; and more fundamentally, SAEs applied directly to dense embeddings remain a quasi-linear control interface, which caps how fine-grained an edit can be. The paper also acknowledges that poor-performing regions still exist for some combinations of concept, support size, and edit strength, attributing this to some mix of concept ambiguity, embedding-space quality, or SAE reconstruction fidelity, without fully disentangling which factor dominates. Beyond that, evaluation is restricted to 20 high-frequency tags, leaving behavior on rare or novel concepts unclear; there is no comparison against other recent multimodal concept-attribution methods; and the specific design choice behind the manifold regularization appears empirically motivated, without a full sensitivity analysis of that choice.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最重要的貢獻與其說是一個新方法,不如說是一個重新框架問題的視角：概念控制的可靠性,關鍵不在於找到「個別文字對齊的神經元」，而在於恢復「一個概念在目標模態裡真正對應的稀疏支持集」——這句話直接點出過去做多模態可解釋性時常見的一個盲點：把文字空間的幾何結構當成音訊空間的代理，而兩者未必一致。實驗結果支持這個框架的價值：在概念歸因分析中，反演方法在不同稀疏度下的支持集召回率比餘弦探測基線高出 10% 到 70%，用限制在選中神經元上的分類器做 AUROC 評估時，反演方法達到 65% 到 80%，而基線只有大約 50%（等同隨機）；在可操控檢索實驗中，反演方法在「編輯強度」與「保留度」之間取得的權衡曲線系統性地優於 DTN 基線——在相同的偏離程度下能取得更大的目標概念變化，而且反演方法產生的編輯結果在馬氏距離意義上明顯更貼近真實音訊流形，DTN 基線則會把嵌入拉向文字流形而非音訊流形，這正好印證了論文開頭診斷的問題。更值得注意的是這套方法完全是推論時（inference-time）操作，不需要成對音訊-文字監督也不需要重新訓練 SAE，讓它可以直接接上既有系統，實現「使用者即時做概念級編輯來精修檢索結果，不用重新訓練嵌入模型或提供新範例」這種互動式音樂檢索場景。作者也誠實地把這個方法定位成一個起點而非終點：未來想做更平滑、更像滑桿一樣精細的編輯，需要把稀疏分解跟更具表現力或個體自適應的檢索機制結合起來——這代表目前的操控粒度仍然停留在「支持集開關」層級，還沒到真正連續可調的程度。

This paper's most important contribution is less a new method than a reframing of the problem: reliable concept control depends less on finding individually text-aligned neurons than on recovering the sparse support that actually instantiates a concept in the target modality — directly naming a common blind spot in multimodal interpretability work, where text-space geometry gets treated as a proxy for audio-space geometry when the two need not agree. The results back up this framing: in the concept-attribution analysis, inversion achieves 10–70% better bundle recall than the cosine-probing baseline across sparsity levels, and a classifier restricted to the selected neurons reaches 65–80% AUROC under inversion versus roughly 50% (chance level) under the baseline; in the steerable-retrieval experiments, inversion achieves a systematically better edit-strength-versus-preservation tradeoff curve than DTN — larger target-concept gains at matched levels of off-target drift — and inversion-edited embeddings stay measurably closer to the true audio manifold in Mahalanobis-distance terms, while the DTN baseline pulls embeddings toward the text manifold instead, directly confirming the problem diagnosed at the outset. Equally notable is that the whole method is inference-time only, requiring neither paired audio-text supervision nor SAE retraining, meaning it can be dropped into existing systems to enable interactive music retrieval where users make real-time, concept-level edits to refine results without retraining the embedding model or supplying new examples. The authors are candid that this is a starting point rather than an endpoint: achieving smoother, slider-like fine-grained edits will require combining sparse factorization with more expressive or instance-adaptive retrieval mechanisms — meaning the current granularity of control is still closer to a support-set on/off switch than a truly continuous dial.

動機Motivation

音樂串流平台長期面對「冷啟動」問題：一首新上架的歌沒有任何互動紀錄，純靠協同過濾完全無法推薦，這時音訊本身的內容資訊就是唯一可用的信號。近年出現大量以分類或對比學習訓練的預訓練音訊模型（語音領域的 Wav2Vec 2.0、HuBERT，音樂領域的 Music2Vec、MERT，語言-音訊對齊的 CLAP），它們在分類、音樂標記等任務上表現很好，但作者指出一個常被忽略的落差：這些模型的訓練目標（遮蔽預測、對比學習、音訊-文字對齊）本質上是為了讓「同類的聲音靠近」，而推薦系統需要的是「使用者會喜歡的東西靠近」——這是由主觀、行為驅動的偏好決定的相似性，兩者不必然一致。更進一步，近期生成式推薦系統（把嵌入量化成離散的 Semantic ID，再用自迴歸方式生成推薦序列，例如 TIGER 架構）興起後，音訊嵌入在這個新範式下到底有沒有用、該怎麼設計量化方式，幾乎完全沒有系統性研究。這篇論文就是要把這塊空白填上。

Music streaming platforms face a persistent cold-start problem: a newly uploaded track has no interaction history, so collaborative filtering has nothing to work with, and audio content becomes the only usable signal. A wave of pretrained audio models trained via classification or contrastive objectives has emerged — speech-domain Wav2Vec 2.0 and HuBERT, music-domain Music2Vec and MERT, and language-audio-aligned CLAP — all strong on classification and tagging benchmarks. The authors flag an overlooked gap: these training objectives pull acoustically similar sounds together, but recommendation needs items a given listener would enjoy pulled together — a subjective, behavior-shaped notion of similarity that doesn't automatically follow from acoustic similarity. Compounding this, the recent rise of generative recommender systems — which quantize embeddings into discrete Semantic IDs and generate recommendation sequences autoregressively, as in the TIGER architecture — raises an almost entirely unstudied question: do audio embeddings even help in this new paradigm, and how should the quantization be designed? This paper sets out to close that gap systematically.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景橫跨三條線：(1) 預訓練音訊模型——語音導向的 Wav2Vec 2.0、HuBERT 用遮蔽預測，音樂領域的 Music2Vec、MERT 針對音樂結構調整目標，CLAP 則用對比學習把音訊與文字描述對齊；(2) 音訊感知推薦系統——過去已有內容基礎與神經網路方法把音訊特徵餵進推薦模型，但多半針對單一任務手工設計編碼器，鮮少系統比較多個現成編碼器；(3) 生成式推薦——以 TIGER 為代表，用殘差量化自編碼器（RVQ）把連續嵌入轉成一串離散的 Semantic ID token，再用類似語言模型的方式自迴歸生成推薦。這篇論文的定位很清楚：不做新編碼器、不做新推薦演算法，而是把「現有的六個編碼器 × 現有的三種推薦介面」全部交叉組合起來測，並且進一步拆解 RVQ 的寬度（codebook width）、深度（quantization depth）、保留前綴長度這三個設計選項對推薦效果的影響。

The related work spans three threads: (1) pretrained audio models — speech-oriented Wav2Vec 2.0 and HuBERT trained via masked prediction, music-domain Music2Vec and MERT with objectives adapted to musical structure, and CLAP, which uses contrastive learning to align audio with text descriptions; (2) audio-aware recommendation — prior content-based and neural approaches have fed audio features into recommenders, but typically with encoders hand-designed for one task, rarely comparing several off-the-shelf encoders systematically; and (3) generative recommendation — exemplified by TIGER, which uses a residual-quantization autoencoder (RVQ) to convert continuous embeddings into a sequence of discrete Semantic ID tokens, then generates recommendations autoregressively in a language-model-like fashion. The paper's positioning is deliberate: it proposes no new encoder and no new recommendation algorithm, but instead cross-tests six existing encoders against three existing recommendation interfaces, and further dissects how RVQ's codebook width, quantization depth, and retained prefix length each shape recommendation quality.

方法Method

實驗設計成三層。第一層是「推薦介面」：KNN 直接用嵌入的餘弦相似度做檢索，等於直接檢驗原始嵌入幾何空間的品質；SASRec 是序列推薦器，用音訊向量初始化可訓練的物品嵌入，再靠使用者行為序列微調，等於檢驗「嵌入被下游任務適應後」的效果；TIGER 則是生成式推薦，先用 RVQ 把嵌入量化成離散 Semantic ID，再自迴歸生成序列，檢驗嵌入在離散化與生成式框架下還剩多少資訊。第二層是「六個編碼器」：Wav2Vec 2.0、HuBERT（語音導向）、Music2Vec、MERT（音樂領域）、CLAP-General、CLAP-Music（語言-音訊對齊，一般版與音樂特化版）。第三層是針對 TIGER 的消融：改變 RVQ 的 codebook 寬度（64 到 4096）、量化深度（1 到 12 層），以及推薦時只保留前幾層 Semantic ID 前綴的效果。資料集用 LFM1b 與 Music4All-Onion 各抽約 5000 首歌的子集，分別對應約 8000 位使用者、190 萬與 200 萬筆互動紀錄，評估指標是 Recall@50、NDCG@50、MRR@50，總共形成六個問題（RQ1 到 RQ6）逐一回答編碼器選擇、介面交互、預訓練目標、預訓練資料領域、量化寬度/深度、RVQ 各層貢獻度的影響。

The experiment is structured in three layers. The first is the recommendation interface: KNN retrieves directly via cosine similarity on raw embeddings, testing the quality of the pretrained geometry itself; SASRec is a sequential recommender that initializes trainable item embeddings from audio vectors and fine-tunes them against user behavior sequences, testing how much adaptation can compensate for the starting embedding; TIGER is the generative recommender, quantizing embeddings into discrete Semantic IDs via RVQ before autoregressive generation, testing how much information survives discretization and the generative framing. The second layer is the six encoders: Wav2Vec 2.0 and HuBERT (speech-oriented), Music2Vec and MERT (music-domain), and CLAP-General and CLAP-Music (audio-text alignment, general and music-specialized). The third layer is a TIGER-specific ablation varying RVQ codebook width (64 to 4096), quantization depth (1 to 12 levels), and how many leading Semantic-ID prefix levels are retained at recommendation time. Two datasets — roughly 5,000-track subsets of LFM1b and Music4All-Onion, with about 8,000 users and 1.9M/2.0M interactions respectively — are evaluated with Recall@50, NDCG@50, and MRR@50, organized around six research questions (RQ1–RQ6) covering encoder choice, interface interaction, pretraining objective, pretraining domain, quantization width/depth, and per-RVQ-layer contribution.

限制Limitations

作者自己點出的限制很直接：兩個資料集都只抽了約五千首歌的子集，且評估只用離線指標（Recall、NDCG、MRR），沒有真人聽感或滿意度資料，離線指標高不代表使用者真的會喜歡；音訊只是偏好訊號的一種，藝人、曲風標籤、流行度等其他常見推薦特徵完全排除在分析之外，所以「音訊嵌入的貢獻」實際上是在一個被簡化的情境裡測出來的，換到含有更多特徵的正式系統，結論未必等比例成立。另外，六個編碼器都是直接拿現成的、未針對推薦任務微調的版本，等於只回答了「哪個現成模型的嵌入幾何比較適合推薦」，而不是「音訊模型的推薦上限在哪」；如果之後有人針對推薦目標微調這些編碼器，排名很可能改變。

The authors' own limitations are stated plainly: both datasets are ~5,000-track subsets evaluated purely offline (Recall, NDCG, MRR), with no listener-satisfaction data, so strong offline numbers don't guarantee real listener preference. Audio is treated as the only signal, excluding artist identity, genre tags, and popularity — features that typically matter in production recommenders — so the measured "contribution of audio embeddings" is bounded by this simplified setting and may not scale proportionally to a full-featured system. All six encoders are used off-the-shelf, without any fine-tuning toward the recommendation objective, meaning the study answers "whose pretrained geometry happens to suit recommendation better" rather than "what is the ceiling for audio-based recommendation" — fine-tuning any of these encoders toward the task could plausibly reorder the rankings.

討論Discussion

整體結論可以濃縮成三句可操作的建議。第一，如果要直接用嵌入做檢索（KNN），選音樂領域、語言對齊的 CLAP-Music 通常最好；但如果推薦器本身有訓練（SASRec），下游監督訊號可以大幅拉平不同編碼器的差距，代表「起點嵌入的品質」在有充分行為資料微調的情境下沒那麼關鍵。第二，音樂領域預訓練（Music2Vec、MERT、CLAP-Music）系統性優於語音或通用音訊預訓練，這點在 KNN 與 TIGER 下特別明顯，替「該不該花資源訓練音樂專用編碼器」這個常見工程決策提供了實證支持。第三，也是最違反直覺的一點：在 TIGER 這類生成式推薦裡，把 RVQ 加深、Semantic ID 容量拉大，並不會穩定提升效果，反而經常在深度超過三層之後開始不穩定或退化，第三層殘差幾乎沒有額外的推薦相關資訊。這對正在設計 Semantic-ID 系統的工程團隊是直接可用的指引：資源該分配到 codebook 寬度而不是深度。整篇論文的價值不在提出新方法，而在把業界普遍靠直覺做的選型決策，換成了有對照組的系統性證據。

The findings distill into three actionable takeaways. First, for direct retrieval on raw embeddings (KNN), the music-domain, text-aligned CLAP-Music generally wins; but once the recommender itself is trained (SASRec), downstream supervision closes much of the gap between encoders, meaning the quality of the starting embedding matters less once there's enough behavioral data to adapt on. Second, music-domain pretraining (Music2Vec, MERT, CLAP-Music) systematically beats speech or general-audio pretraining, especially under KNN and TIGER — empirical support for a common but often intuition-driven engineering decision about whether to invest in music-specific encoders. Third, and most counter-intuitively, deepening RVQ quantization and expanding Semantic-ID capacity in TIGER-style generative recommenders does not reliably help, and often becomes unstable or degrades performance beyond three levels, with the third residual layer carrying almost no additional recommendation-relevant information. That's directly actionable guidance for teams designing Semantic-ID systems: spend capacity on codebook width, not depth. The paper's value isn't a new method — it's replacing intuition-driven encoder and quantization choices with controlled, systematic evidence.

How Much AI Is in This Track? Quantifying the Proportion of AI-Generated Stems in Hybrid Music Mixtures

這首歌裡有多少 AI 成分？量化混音中 AI 生成音軌的比例

Fernando Garcia de la Cruz, David López-Ayala, Pablo Zinemanas, Emilio Molina, Martín Rocamora
arXiv:2608.07285 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：過去 AI 音樂偵測幾乎都假設「這首歌不是純 AI 就是純人類」的二分法，但實務上混音才是常態——鼓是 AI 生成、人聲是真人錄的。這篇把偵測問題重新定義成「AI 能量佔比是多少」的迴歸問題，而不是分類問題，並且順手發現原本的二元分類器拿來當佔比估計器用其實嚴重失準，是少見把 evaluation methodology 本身講清楚、講誠實的論文。Why this one: Prior AI-music detectors almost universally assume a track is either fully AI or fully human, but real-world production is hybrid — AI drums under a human vocal is the norm, not the exception. This paper reframes detection as a regression problem (what fraction of the acoustic energy is AI-generated) rather than binary classification, and shows along the way that reusing an existing binary classifier as an implicit ratio estimator is badly miscalibrated — an unusually candid piece of evaluation methodology work.

動機Motivation

業界報告指出，平台上每天上傳的音樂有超過一半含有 AI 生成成分，但同一份調查也顯示 97% 的聽眾根本聽不出來哪些是 AI 生成的。歐盟 AI 法案第 50 條這類法規開始要求 AI 生成內容要能被標示，這把「偵測」從學術問題變成合規需求。問題是，目前的偵測系統幾乎都是在「純 AI vs. 純人類」這種乾淨的二分類情境下訓練跟評估，現實中的混音場景——AI 鼓組搭配真人主唱、AI 貝斯配上真人吉他——完全沒被觸及過。作者的核心觀察是：如果一首歌 49% 的能量來自 AI 生成的鼓組，把它硬塞進「AI/人類」二元標籤本身就是概念上的錯誤，真正該問的問題是「這首歌裡有多少比例是 AI 做的」，這需要一個連續的迴歸目標，而不是離散分類。

Industry reports suggest more than half of daily music uploads on some platforms already contain AI-generated content, while the same reports find that 97% of listeners cannot tell which tracks these are. Regulation such as Article 50 of the EU AI Act is starting to require AI-generated content to be disclosed, turning detection from an academic curiosity into a compliance requirement. The trouble is that virtually all existing detectors are trained and evaluated on a clean binary setting — fully AI versus fully human — and never touch the realistic hybrid case where AI-generated drums sit under a human vocal, or an AI bassline under a human guitar part. The authors' core observation is that forcing a track whose energy is 49% AI-generated drums into an "AI or human" binary label is a category error to begin with; the right question is what fraction of the track is AI-generated, which calls for a continuous regression target rather than a discrete class.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

現有的二元 AI 音樂偵測器靠的是類神經音訊 codec 解碼時，transposed convolution 層會留下特定的頻譜偽影（spectral artifact），偵測器就是在抓這個偽影的痕跡，在純 AI 或純人類的音軌上可以做到超過 99% 準確率。但這類偵測器已知有兩個弱點：一是有很強的平台偏誤（不同 generator 留下的偽影不一樣，換一個生成器準確率就崩掉），二是遇到後製處理（壓縮、broadcast 級的音質轉換）準確率會大幅下降。作者也指出 deepfake 偵測這個鄰近領域近年已經從「這張圖是不是假的」二分類，轉向「圖裡哪個區域是被竄改的」像素級定位，但音樂偵測領域完全沒有類似的演進——沒有任何先前工作研究過 AI 生成內容以不同比例混入人類創作內容時要怎麼偵測。這篇論文要補的正是這塊空白，某種意義上是把 deepfake 領域「從偵測到定位」的思路搬到音樂上，只是定位的維度從空間換成了「能量佔比」。

Existing binary AI-music detectors exploit spectral artifacts left by transposed-convolution layers during neural audio codec decoding, achieving over 99% accuracy on purely-AI or purely-human tracks. But such detectors have two known weaknesses: strong platform-specific bias (artifacts differ across generators, and accuracy collapses when the generator changes), and steep degradation under post-processing such as compression or broadcast-quality transcoding. The authors also note that the adjacent field of deepfake detection has already moved from binary "is this image fake" classification toward pixel-level localization of which regions were manipulated — but music detection has seen no equivalent evolution. No prior work has studied detection when AI-generated content is mixed with human-created content at varying proportions. This paper fills exactly that gap, in effect porting the deepfake field's "from detection to localization" shift into music, except the localization axis becomes an energy proportion rather than a spatial region.

方法Method

核心度量是「AI 能量佔比」α，定義成所有 AI 生成音軌（stem）的均方根能量平方和，除以整首混音裡所有音軌的均方根能量平方和——用能量而不是單純算「有幾軌是 AI 生成的」，是因為一個很輕的 AI 合成弦樂鋪底，跟一個很吵的 AI 鼓組，對整首歌可辨識度的貢獻天差地遠，用能量加權比較符合實際聽感上「AI 成分佔多少」的直覺。

資料建構分三步：先用 MoisesDB 這個含 2 到 10 軌的 240 首多軌資料集，取出每一軌原始的人聲音軌；每一軌都額外經過 EnCodec（3kbps）編碼再解碼一次，刻意把 codec 偽影「烙印」進去，模擬 AI 生成的頻譜特徵，同時保留原本的音樂內容——這一步的用意是把偵測任務限定在「架構偽影能不能被抓到」，排除掉「AI 生成的音樂內容本身聽起來就比較假」這個干擾因子。接著對每首歌的 n 軌音源，窮舉所有 2^n 種「這一軌是真的還是編碼過的」組合，依照牽涉到幾軌分層，確保 α 在整個 [0,1] 區間都有密集的取樣點，總共產生 21,212 個混音樣本。

比較的模型有兩個：一個是複現既有論文的二元 CNN 分類器（在純真/純 AI 音軌上有 99.97% 準確率），另一個是用完全相同的 CNN 骨幹、但改成直接對 α 做迴歸的 regressor。兩者都在 5 秒窗口上評估，也測試了 1 秒窗口的效果差異。

The core metric is the AI energy ratio α: the sum of squared RMS energy across all AI-processed stems, divided by the summed squared RMS energy of every stem in the mix. Using energy rather than a simple stem count matters because a quiet AI-synthesized string pad and a loud AI drum kit contribute very differently to how "AI" a track sounds, and energy-weighting matches that intuition better than treating every stem as equally important.

Data construction proceeds in three steps. Starting from MoisesDB (240 tracks, 2-10 stems each), every stem is additionally round-tripped through EnCodec (3 kbps) encode-decode to imprint codec artifacts that mimic AI-generation signatures while preserving the underlying musical content — this isolates the detection task to "can the architectural artifact be found," removing the confound of "AI-generated content simply sounding different." For each track's n stems, all 2^n real/AI combinations are enumerated and organized by how many stems are affected, ensuring dense coverage of α across the full [0,1] range — 21,212 mixtures in total. Two models are compared: a binary CNN classifier reproduced from prior work (99.97% accuracy on pure tracks) and a regressor with an identical backbone trained directly to predict α, both evaluated at 5-second windows, with 1-second windows tested as a contrast.

限制Limitations

論文自己講得很清楚：整個實驗只用了 EnCodec 一種 codec、固定在 3kbps 這個位元率，現實中的商業生成器（Suno、Udio）用的架構、位元率完全不同，偽影特徵不一定相同，結論能不能直接搬過去用是未知數。混音樣本也完全沒有做專業後製（等化、壓縮），跟真實發行的音樂在音質上有落差，作者自己承認這是「方法學上的可控基礎，而不是可以直接部署的偵測器」。另外，α 的定義需要拿到每一軌的原始 stem 才算得出來，但實務上拿到的往往只是已經混好的成品，沒有 stem-level 的存取權限，這代表這個方法在真實場景下的可操作性其實有限，需要額外的音源分離步驟才能用，而分離本身也會引入誤差。最後，不同樂器的可偵測性差異很大（鼓、吉他容易分辨，人聲、貝斯幾乎分不出來），這代表 α 的估計精確度其實會隨著混音的樂器組成系統性地偏移，論文雖然指出這點但沒有進一步提出校正方案。

The paper is upfront about its limits: the entire study uses only EnCodec at a fixed 3 kbps, while commercial generators (Suno, Udio) use different architectures and bitrates with potentially different artifact signatures, so it's unclear how well the findings transfer. The mixtures also have no professional post-processing (EQ, compression), leaving a gap versus real released music — the authors themselves call this "a controlled methodological foundation rather than a deployment-ready detector." Computing α also requires access to individual original stems, but real-world deployment usually only has the finished mix, meaning the method needs an additional source-separation step to be usable in practice, which would itself introduce error. Finally, detectability varies sharply by instrument (drums and guitar separate cleanly; vocals and bass barely separate at all), meaning α estimates will be systematically biased depending on a mix's instrumentation — a point the paper flags but does not propose a correction for.

討論Discussion

這篇論文的價值不完全在偵測準確率本身，而在於它把一個常被簡化成「二選一」的問題重新框架成連續量測，這個框架轉換其實對整個 AI 內容治理的討論有啟發性——不只是音樂，任何「混合創作」的媒介（共同寫作的文字、人機協作的圖像）都可能面臨同樣的問題：與其問「這是不是 AI 做的」，更誠實的問法或許是「這裡面有多少是 AI 做的」。論文裡最讓人意外的發現是，原本號稱 99.97% 準確率的二元分類器，拿來當作 α 的隱式估計器時表現比瞎猜（用平均值當預測）還差（R²=-0.85）——這給整個領域一個警訊：一個在自己訓練分佈（純 AI/純人類）上表現極好的分類器，套用到分佈外的中間地帶時，校準品質可能完全不能信任，論文用具體數字把這個常被忽略的問題攤開來看，是很紮實的貢獻。比較意外的是作者對「樂器可分性差異」只當成限制帶過，其實這個發現本身滿有意思的——它暗示未來的偵測器設計應該要針對不同樂器分開建模，而不是用單一模型硬套所有音軌，這方向論文自己點出來了卻沒有再往下推。

This paper's value isn't primarily about detection accuracy — it's the reframing of a problem usually flattened into a binary choice as a continuous measurement, and that reframing has implications beyond music: any hybrid-creation medium (human-AI co-written text, human-AI collaborative images) faces the same question, where "how much of this is AI" may be the more honest question than "is this AI." The most striking finding is that the celebrated 99.97%-accurate binary classifier, when repurposed as an implicit α estimator, performs worse than simply predicting the mean (R²=-0.85) — a cautionary result for the field: a classifier that excels on its own training distribution (pure AI/pure human) may be completely uncalibrated once pushed into the out-of-distribution middle ground, and this paper puts concrete numbers behind a problem that's often glossed over. It's a bit surprising the authors treat the instrument-dependent separability gap as merely a limitation rather than pursuing it further — the finding itself suggests future detectors should be modeled per-instrument rather than applying one model uniformly across all stems, a direction the paper identifies but doesn't chase.

Towards Robust Version Identification in the Wild: A Dataset, Benchmark, and Fine-Tuning Study

邁向真實環境下穩健的版本辨識：資料集、基準與微調研究

Simon Hachmeier, R. Oguz Araz, Dmitry Bogdanov, Robert Jäschke, Xavier Serra
arXiv:2608.04543 · cs.SD; cs.IR · 投稿submitted 2026-08-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：指出既有版本辨識資料集全部偏向專業錄音，與真實世界的業餘、使用者生成內容存在領域落差，然後用 110 萬個版本把這個落差補上。難得的是它同時報告了微調帶來的退步，而不只報告進步。Why this one: It identifies a domain mismatch — every existing version-identification dataset is dominated by professional recordings, while the real world is full of amateur and user-generated content — and closes it with 1.1 million versions. Unusually, it reports where fine-tuning makes things worse, not only where it helps.

動機Motivation

版本辨識（version identification，VI）要做的是：給一堆音軌，自動找出哪些是同一首作品的不同演繹。這包含翻唱、現場、器樂版等等。

問題出在訓練資料。現有的資料集幾乎都衍生自人工策展的線上資料庫——SecondHandSongs 與 Discogs——因此以專業錄音為主。以 Discogs-VI-YT（DVI）為例，它有將近 50 萬個版本，是目前 SOTA 系統的訓練與評估基礎，但它刻意限定在 YouTube 的官方上傳，以確保資料乾淨。

這個設計選擇的代價是：把實務上真正大量存在的東西排除掉了。非官方、使用者生成的上傳才是平台上的常態，而它們帶來的挑戰是真實的——業餘翻唱的音質參差、使用者自錄現場的環境噪音，還有更麻煩的：音樂根本不是以演出形式呈現的內容，例如反應影片、教學影片，中間穿插著解說與非音樂片段。已有研究顯示，在 SHS100K 上訓練的 VI 系統在 YouTube 上找到的各種版本上表現明顯下滑。那份研究雖然處理了部分限制，但資料集規模太小，不足以訓練 VI 系統。

所以缺口很具體：既要有真實環境的多樣性，又要有足以訓練的規模。

Version identification (VI) asks which tracks in a collection are different renditions of the same musical work — covers, live performances, instrumental versions and so on.

The problem is the training data. Nearly all existing datasets derive from manually curated online collections, SecondHandSongs and Discogs, and are therefore dominated by professional recordings. Discogs-VI-YT (DVI), with nearly 500,000 versions, underpins the current state of the art, but it is deliberately restricted to official YouTube uploads to keep the data clean.

The cost of that design choice is that it excludes what actually dominates in practice. Unofficial, user-generated uploads are the norm on the platform, and they bring real difficulties: variable audio quality in amateur covers, environmental noise in user-recorded live performances, and — harder still — music presented in formats that are not performances at all, such as reaction videos and tutorials, interrupted by commentary and non-musical content. Prior work has shown that VI systems trained on the SHS-based SHS100K underperform on versions found on YouTube. That study addressed some of the limitations but its dataset was too small to train VI systems with.

So the gap is specific: real-world diversity together with enough scale to train on.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

VI 的技術路線近年有明顯轉向。早期方法多半使用手工設計的音訊描述子，近期則以直接從常數 Q 變換（CQT）學習緊緻表徵為主流。這類方法對資料量的需求很大，所以資料集才會集中在 SHS 與 Discogs 這些人工策展來源。

作者的做法是建立在 DVI 之上而不是另起爐灶：不新增任何作品（work），保留 DVI 原本對作品的訓練／驗證／測試劃分，只針對這些既有作品去野外尋找新的版本。他們把能從 YouTube 下載到的 96% 版本記為 DVI*，作為基礎。

這個設計決定很關鍵——它讓新資料集與既有的 Discogs-VI-YT、SHS100K、Da-TACOS 保持相容，可以直接比較，而不是製造出又一個彼此不可比的孤島。

除了版本層級的標註之外，資料集另外提供兩種自動標註：類別標籤（例如 instrumental、live）與片段層級的「有無音樂」預測。前者用來支援分層評估，因為既有研究已經顯示 VI 系統對這些屬性存在偏誤；後者則是因為這份資料預期會比 DVI* 含有更多非音樂內容。

The technical line in VI has shifted. Early approaches used hand-crafted audio descriptors; recent ones learn compact representations directly from the constant-Q transform. These are data-hungry, which is why datasets cluster around curated sources like SHS and Discogs.

The authors build on DVI rather than starting over: they add no new works, keep DVI's original partition of works into train, validation and test, and search only for new versions of those existing works. The 96% of versions they could download from YouTube is denoted DVI* and serves as the base.

That decision matters. It keeps the new dataset compatible with Discogs-VI-YT, SHS100K and Da-TACOS so results remain comparable, rather than producing yet another mutually incomparable island.

Beyond version-level annotation, the dataset provides two automatic labellings: categorical tags (instrumental, live and so on) and segment-level music/non-music predictions. The first supports stratified evaluation, since prior work has shown VI systems carry biases with respect to such properties; the second exists because this data is expected to contain more non-musical content than DVI*.

方法Method

尋找野外版本。 以 DVI* 的作品為基礎在 YouTube 搜尋，查詢字串是正規化後的曲名（轉小寫、去除變音符號、去除開頭冠詞與標點、去掉括號內可能表示編輯資訊的文字如「Radio Edit」）。每個查詢取前 500 筆搜尋結果的 metadata。

過濾流程層層收緊：排除時長小於 10 秒的候選（因為既有研究顯示短於此長度時 VI 效能會下滑，可能是這個任務的自然最小長度），排除超過 20 分鐘的，排除 YouTube ID 已出現在 DVI、SHS100K、Da-TACOS 任一資料集中的（確保找的是新東西且維持相容性）。為了刻意偏向非官方版本，再排除符合官方版本條件的候選，以及標題含 lyric video 或 remaster 的。之後每個查詢只留前 100 個候選。

模糊比對。 要求 DVI* 的作品 metadata（正規化曲名、演出者名）出現在候選的 YouTube metadata（影片標題與描述）中；一個作品關聯的所有演出者名中命中任一個即可。用 Rapidfuzz 的 token ratio，門檻設在 80%。

去重。 為避免撈到的其實只是另一個官方版本，用建立在 Chromaprint 之上的 soundalike 系統做去重，相似度門檻 0.8，在完整音軌層級比對——也就是說兩者必須整段音訊都超過門檻才算重複。重複組內優先保留來自 DVI* 的版本。這一步丟掉 31% 的比對候選，最終得到 629,536 個新發現的版本（記為 YVI-L），與 DVI* 合併成 DiVers-L，共 1,102,317 個版本。

品質驗證。 人工標註 320 組隨機抽出的配對，96.25% 的指派是正確的。作者也追查了錯誤來源：多半發生在演出者正確但查詢曲名對上的其實是影片標題裡提到的專輯名，或是 DVI* 本身就有指派錯誤的版本。正確配對中有 6.82% 是近似重複——作者指出可以透過調低 soundalike 的比對時長門檻來排除，但那也可能一併排除掉有價值的案例（例如反應影片）。

片段層級音樂偵測。 用 PANN 做聲音事件分類，10 秒滑動窗、1 秒 hop，每段取前五個預測類別，只要有任一個屬於音樂類就標記為音樂。全資料集含 64,349 小時音樂與 2,426 小時非音樂；每軌最長音樂片段平均 194 秒，最長非音樂片段平均 15 秒。

標籤比對。 合併 MTG-Jamendo 與 MagnaTagATune 兩個自動標註資料集的標籤（去除 68 個重疊後得 315 個），再加入 14 個 YouTube 特有標籤，共 329 個。為提高覆蓋率，用 GPT-5 把標籤翻譯成法、德、義、葡、西五種語言並人工校對。比對只針對影片標題（使用者提供的 YouTube 標籤與描述中的標籤被發現不可靠，故忽略），且採精確比對而非模糊比對（因為翻譯已經提供了彈性），並排除出現在曲名或演出者名中的比對以聚焦於脈絡資訊。標籤品質驗證方式是對 15 個最常見標籤各標註 30 支影片、共 450 支，兩位標註者獨立標註，Cohen's κ 有報告；除了 rock、blues、solo 三個之外，幾乎所有標籤的精確率都超過 80%。

實驗設定。 兩條路線：從頭訓練與微調。從頭訓練的是 DVINetX——採用 DVINet+ 架構並擴容到 2070 萬參數（通道數 48→64，嵌入維度 512→1024），用三元組損失搭配困難樣本挖掘。微調的是 CLEWS（近 2 億參數），在 DiVers-S 上用 bpwr-5 策略。評估同時報告 MAP 與 NAR，後者捕捉的是超越前段排名的整體排序品質。

Finding versions in the wild. Starting from DVI*'s works, they search YouTube with a normalised track title as the query — lowercased, diacritics removed, leading articles and punctuation stripped, parenthetical editing information such as "Radio Edit" removed — and retrieve metadata for the top 500 results per query.

Filtering tightens in stages: candidates shorter than 10 seconds are dropped (prior work shows VI performance falls below this length, suggesting a natural minimum for the task), as are those over 20 minutes and those whose YouTube identifiers already appear in DVI, SHS100K or Da-TACOS — keeping the search to genuinely new material and preserving compatibility. To bias towards unofficial versions, candidates meeting the criteria for an official version are excluded, as are titles containing "lyric video" or "remaster". The top 100 remaining candidates per query are kept.

Fuzzy matching. The work metadata from DVI* (normalised title, performer name) must appear in the candidate's YouTube title and description; matching any one of the performers associated with a work suffices. They use Rapidfuzz's token ratio with a threshold of 80%.

Deduplication. To avoid retrieving merely another official version, they deduplicate with a soundalike system built on Chromaprint at a similarity threshold of 0.8, applied at full-track level — two items count as duplicates only if their complete recordings exceed the threshold. Within a duplicate set, DVI* versions are retained preferentially. This drops 31% of matched candidates, yielding 629,536 newly discovered versions (YVI-L); combined with DVI* this gives DiVers-L at 1,102,317 versions.

Quality validation. Manual annotation of 320 randomly selected pairs finds 96.25% of assignments correct. The authors trace the errors: mostly cases where the artist is right but the queried title matches an album name mentioned in the video title, or where DVI* itself contains a wrongly assigned version. Among correct matches, 6.82% are near-duplicates — removable by lowering the soundalike matching duration, but that would also exclude interesting cases such as reaction videos.

Segment-level music detection. PANN performs sound event classification over 10-second sliding windows with a 1-second hop; the top five predicted classes per segment are kept, and a segment is musical if any of them is a music class. The dataset holds 64,349 hours of music and 2,426 hours of non-music; the mean longest music segment per track is 194 seconds against 15 seconds for non-music.

Tag matching. Tags from MTG-Jamendo and MagnaTagATune are merged (315 after removing 68 overlaps) plus 14 YouTube-specific tags, for 329 total. To widen coverage, GPT-5 translates the tags into French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Spanish, followed by manual curation. Matching targets video titles only — user-provided YouTube tags and description tags were found unreliable and are ignored — and is exact rather than fuzzy, since the translations already supply flexibility; matches falling in track titles or performer names are excluded to focus on contextual information. Tag quality is checked by annotating 30 videos for each of the 15 most frequent tags, 450 in total, by two independent annotators with a reported Cohen's κ. Precision exceeds 80% for nearly every tag except rock, blues and solo.

Experimental setup. Two routes: training from scratch and fine-tuning. From scratch is DVINetX, the DVINet+ architecture scaled to 20.7M parameters (channels 48→64, embedding 512→1024), trained with triplet loss and hard triplet mining. Fine-tuning targets CLEWS (nearly 200M parameters) on DiVers-S with the bpwr-5 strategy. Both MAP and NAR are reported, the latter capturing overall ranking quality beyond the top results.

限制Limitations

作者自己揭露的最重要一點，是微調帶來的是取捨而非單純的改善。CLEWS 微調後在 YVI 資料集、SHS100K* 與 DiVers-L 上表現最佳，但在乾淨的 DVI* 上，MAP 反而低於未微調版本。嵌入空間分析給出了對應的證據：微調後 YVI 上的類間／類內距離比從 1.52 升到 1.60，DVI* 上卻從 1.72 降到 1.65。也就是說，對野外版本的可分性改善，是拿乾淨資料上的可分性換來的。

作者對此提出了部分解釋——DVI* 與 SHS100K* 的中位類別大小分別是 3 與 12，類別小的時候 MAP 對個別檢索失敗特別敏感——但這只解釋了指標的敏感度，沒有消除那個實質的退步。

從頭訓練那條線的結論也不是全面勝利。DVINetX 在 DVI* 上訓練時，在 DVI* 自己的測試子集上表現最好；改用 DiVers-S 訓練雖然在所有其他評估集上改善了 NAR，但 MAP 的增益只出現在野外子集 YVI-S 與 YVI-L 上，在 SHS100K* 與合併子集上仍略低於 DVI* 訓練的模型。要到擴大成 DiVers-L 才在兩個指標與每個評估集上都超越 DiVers-S，並且除了 DVI* 自己的測試子集外全面勝過 DVI* 訓練的模型。

資料建構本身也有幾處已知的不確定性。標籤是自動指派的「銀標籤」代理，作者明確承認仍有不確定性存在，而且標註協定只估計精確率、不估計召回率——所以我們不知道有多少該被標上 live 的影片沒被標到。solo 這個標籤的錯誤分析尤其說明了問題：有四支影片含有由其他樂器伴奏的獨奏段落，嚴格說不是純獨奏，但那些樂段確實使標籤有其道理，顯示這個類別本質上就模稜兩可。

作者在倫理聲明裡也主動指出：資料來自 YouTube 使用者生成內容，語言、曲風、演出風格的分布會受到上傳行為與平台搜尋推薦機制影響，因此在 DiVers 上訓練的模型可能繼承甚至放大這些偏誤。這個提醒對一份宣稱要「更貼近真實世界」的資料集來說特別重要——更貼近的是 YouTube 的真實世界，不等於音樂的真實世界。

The most important thing the authors disclose is that fine-tuning is a trade-off, not a clean improvement. Fine-tuned CLEWS performs best on the YVI datasets, SHS100K* and DiVers-L, but on the clean DVI* its MAP falls below the un-fine-tuned model. The embedding analysis supplies the matching evidence: after fine-tuning the inter/intra-class distance ratio rises on YVI from 1.52 to 1.60 but falls on DVI* from 1.72 to 1.65. Separability on in-the-wild versions improves at the cost of separability on cleaner, cataloged ones.

The authors offer a partial explanation — median class sizes of 3 for DVI* and 12 for SHS100K*, with MAP more sensitive to individual retrieval failures when classes are small — but that explains the metric's sensitivity rather than removing the regression.

The from-scratch results are not a clean sweep either. DVINetX trained on DVI* performs best on DVI*'s own test subset. Training on DiVers-S improves NAR everywhere else, but the MAP gains are confined to the in-the-wild subsets YVI-S and YVI-L; on SHS100K* and the combined subsets, MAP stays slightly below the DVI*-trained model. Only scaling to DiVers-L improves on DiVers-S across both metrics and every evaluation set, and beats DVI* training everywhere except DVI*'s own test subset.

The dataset construction carries acknowledged uncertainty. Tags are automatically assigned silver-label proxies, and the authors state plainly that uncertainty remains; the annotation protocol estimates precision but not recall, so how many videos that should carry a "live" tag went unlabelled is unknown. The error analysis for "solo" is telling: four videos contain solo passages accompanied by other instruments, which are not strictly solo performances but where the highlighted passages nevertheless justify the tag — an inherently ambiguous category.

The ethics statement raises another point unprompted: the data is YouTube user-generated content, so the distribution of languages, genres and performance styles is shaped by upload behaviour and by the platform's search and recommendation mechanisms, and models trained on DiVers may inherit or amplify those biases. That caveat matters especially for a dataset claiming to be closer to the real world — it is closer to YouTube's real world, which is not the same as music's.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值不在於任何架構創新——它沒有提出新模型——而在於把一個大家隱約知道但沒有量化的領域落差，做成可以訓練、可以比較的東西。「策展資料集偏向專業錄音」這件事本身不新，先前研究已經指出過；這篇的貢獻是把規模做到能真正拿來訓練，而且維持與既有資料集的相容性。

分層評估的結果比整體數字更有資訊量。微調的增益在 tutorial 這個標籤上最大且最一致，作者的解釋是這類內容噪音水準較高——含有口說段落、重複段落與不穩定的節奏；相對地 live 的改善較小且較不一致，因為候選的噪音水準本來就較低。這條「增益隨噪音程度上升」的規律，在非音樂比例的分析裡也重現：NAR 的差距隨非音樂比例加大而擴大。這比單一個平均分數更能說明微調到底改善了什麼。

有一個實務上很有用的發現容易被略過：全軌嵌入比對雖然比片段層級比對便宜得多，但微調後的 CLEWS 用全軌比對在每個非音樂比例下都能匹敵或超越用片段層級比對的未微調版本。作者的解讀是，微調帶來的穩健性有一部分來自更好的時間軸聚合能力，讓模型能在單一音軌內部消化非音樂內容與局部失真。對要部署檢索系統的人來說，這意味著查詢時的計算成本可以省下來。另外，L2 正規化對全軌嵌入有明顯幫助，對片段層級比對則幫助有限甚至偶爾有害。

還有一個對照值得記下來：DVINetX 只有 2000 萬參數，CLEWS 接近 2 億，但在 SHS-YT+ 上以 NAR 衡量，DVINetX 反而勝過 CLEWS 的各個變體。作者只用「這很值得注意」帶過，沒有深究——但這暗示在這個任務上，資料的分布可能比模型容量更關鍵。

作者對未來的建議是探索能更好地強化類間分離的方法，以及整合音訊嵌入之外的多視角訊號例如歌詞。從前面那個「改善野外、犧牲乾淨」的取捨來看，前者確實是最該解的問題。

The value here is not architectural — no new model is proposed — but in turning a domain gap everyone vaguely knew about into something trainable and comparable. That curated datasets skew professional is not itself new; prior work said so. The contribution is scale sufficient for training while preserving compatibility with existing datasets.

The stratified results carry more information than the headline numbers. Fine-tuning gains are largest and most consistent for the "tutorial" tag, which the authors attribute to higher noise — spoken segments, repeated sections, variable pacing — while "live" shows smaller and less consistent improvement because candidate noise is lower there. The same pattern, gains rising with noise, recurs in the non-music-ratio analysis, where the NAR gap widens as the non-music ratio grows. That says far more about what fine-tuning actually fixed than any single average.

One practically useful finding is easy to miss: full-track embedding comparison is substantially cheaper than segment-level matching, yet fine-tuned CLEWS with full-track retrieval matches or beats the un-fine-tuned model with segment-level matching at every non-music ratio. The authors read this as the robustness gain coming partly from better aggregation along the temporal axis, letting the model absorb non-music and local distortion within a track. For anyone deploying a retrieval system, that is query-time compute saved. Relatedly, L2 normalisation helps full-track embeddings substantially but offers little and occasionally hurts for segment-level matching.

One comparison is worth recording: DVINetX has 20M parameters against CLEWS's nearly 200M, yet on SHS-YT+ measured by NAR the smaller system outperforms every CLEWS variant. The authors note this is notable and leave it there — but it hints that on this task the training distribution may matter more than model capacity.

Their suggested directions are methods that better enforce inter-class separation and multi-view signals beyond audio embeddings, such as lyrics. Given the improve-the-wild-at-the-cost-of-the-clean trade-off above, the former is indeed the problem most in need of solving.
