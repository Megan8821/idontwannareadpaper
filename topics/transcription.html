Beyond Piano: Cross-Instrument MIDI Velocity Estimation via Differentiable SoundFont Proxies

不只是鋼琴：透過可微分 SoundFont 代理進行跨樂器 MIDI 力度估計

Zhanhong He, Hanyu Meng, David Defeng Huang, Roberto Togneri
arXiv:2608.08985 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：大部分公開 MIDI 檔案的力度（velocity）都是常數 64，因為只有裝了感測器的 Yamaha Disklavier 鋼琴才有真人演奏的力度標籤，吉他、小提琴等樂器完全沒有對應的標註資料。這篇論文不是硬套用鋼琴模型，而是先在鋼琴上用真實標籤訓練一個力度估計器 VeloEst，再設計了兩條跨樂器「調適」路徑：一條在波形空間比對可微分合成器（Diff-Synth），另一條是這篇論文提出的、在聲學參數空間比對的可微分 SoundFont 代理（Diff-SFProxy）。實驗直接顯示波形空間監督在跨樂器遷移上會失敗甚至比什麼都不做更差，而參數空間監督穩定勝出，這個「哪一種監督訊號在遷移時比較穩健」的對照做得乾淨，故列為深度分析。Why this one: Most public MIDI files carry a constant velocity of 64, because only sensor-equipped Yamaha Disklavier pianos produce real performance velocity labels — guitar, violin, and most other instruments have no comparable ground truth. Rather than forcing a piano-trained model onto other instruments, this paper first trains a velocity estimator (VeloEst) on piano with real labels, then designs two cross-instrument adaptation paths: one matching in waveform space via a differentiable synthesizer (Diff-Synth), and the paper's proposed alternative matching in acoustic-parameter space via a differentiable SoundFont proxy (Diff-SFProxy). The experiments cleanly show waveform-space supervision can fail on cross-instrument transfer — sometimes underperforming doing nothing at all — while parameter-space supervision holds up robustly. That clean contrast in which supervision signal survives domain transfer is why it's flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

MIDI 的力度數值控制著音符渲染出來的音量與音色細節，是表達性演奏的核心參數之一，但絕大多數公開釋出的 MIDI 檔案裡，這個欄位根本不是真人彈奏的紀錄，而是被匯出工具寫死成一個常數（通常是 64）。學習式的力度估計之所以在鋼琴上做得起來，完全是託了 Yamaha Disklavier 這類裝了力度感測鍵盤的錄音設備的福——彈奏當下就同步記錄了每個音符的真實力度，形成乾淨的監督訊號。但吉他、小提琴這些樂器沒有這種感測基礎設施，換句話說，「怎麼在完全沒有真人力度標籤的樂器上，訓練一個可信的力度估計器」本身就是一個標籤稀缺問題。作者的切入點是：與其硬造合成資料去訓練（會引入合成與真實錄音之間的落差），不如先在有標籤的鋼琴上訓練好模型，再想辦法把這個模型「調適」到目標樂器上——調適的判準是，估計出來的力度值，拿去渲染出的音訊要跟真實演奏音訊的動態聽起來一致。

MIDI velocity governs a note's rendered loudness and timbral detail, making it one of the central parameters of expressive performance — yet in most publicly released MIDI files, the field isn't a recording of a real performance at all, but a constant baked in by an export tool (typically 64). Learning-based velocity estimation works for piano only because sensor-equipped instruments like the Yamaha Disklavier capture each note's true velocity as it's played, yielding a clean supervision signal. Guitar, violin, and most other instruments have no comparable sensing infrastructure, so training a trustworthy velocity estimator for them is fundamentally a label-scarcity problem. The authors' angle: rather than manufacturing synthetic training data (which introduces a synthetic-to-real gap), first train a solid model on labeled piano data, then adapt that model to a target instrument — where "adapted correctly" is judged by whether the estimated velocities, once rendered, reproduce the dynamics of real performance audio.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把相關工作放在兩個脈絡下看。第一是既有的力度估計工作幾乎全部綁死在鋼琴上，原因正是前述的感測器標籤可得性，這使得「跨樂器」本身變成一塊沒什麼人碰過的空白。第二是可微分數位訊號處理（differentiable DSP）與可微分合成器的路線：這條路線的優勢是可以把「渲染出的音訊跟真實音訊的差距」直接寫成可以反向傳播的損失函數，讓模型透過梯度下降去調整輸入（這裡是力度）。論文選擇站在這條路線上，但同時指出一個問題：如果比對的對象是完整波形，那麼跟力度無關的因素（錄音環境、麥克風特性、合成器音色跟真實樂器的音色落差）全部會混進損失函數裡，變成雜訊甚至誤導梯度方向。这正是論文提出 Diff-SFProxy、把監督訊號從「完整波形」降到「跟力度直接相關的少數聲學參數」的動機所在。

The paper situates related work along two threads. First, prior velocity-estimation work is almost entirely piano-bound, precisely because of the sensor-label availability described above — leaving cross-instrument estimation a largely untouched gap. Second is the line of differentiable digital signal processing and differentiable synthesizers: its appeal is that the gap between rendered and real audio can be written directly as a backpropagatable loss, letting the model adjust its input (here, velocity) via gradient descent. The paper adopts this line but flags a problem: if the comparison target is the full waveform, then everything unrelated to velocity — recording environment, microphone characteristics, the timbral mismatch between a synthesizer and a real instrument — gets folded into the loss too, becoming noise or even misleading the gradient. That is exactly the motivation behind Diff-SFProxy: reducing the supervision signal from the entire waveform down to a small set of acoustic parameters directly tied to velocity.

方法Method

兩條調適路徑：第一條 Diff-Synth 用凍結權重的樂器專屬可微分合成器（鋼琴用 DDSP-Piano、吉他用 DDSP-Guitar-Synth），把音符事件（音高、起始時間、時長）跟預測出的力度一起餵進去渲染出音訊 ã，再用多解析度的 STFT 頻譜損失去比對 ã 跟真實演奏音訊 a：ℒ_audio = Σ_ω∈Ω(‖|STFT_ω(ã)|−|STFT_ω(a)|‖₁ + ‖log(|STFT_ω(ã)|+ε)−log(|STFT_ω(a)|+ε)‖₁)，其中 Ω={2048,1024,512,256,128,64} 涵蓋六種不同的視窗大小。

第二條、也是這篇論文的核心創新，是 Diff-SFProxy：不去比對整段波形，而是設計了兩個跟力度直接相關的聲學參數，從真實演奏音訊裡萃取出來當監督目標。第一個是「音高條件諧波能量」PHE：PHE_n = log(1+Σ_{h=1}^{H_n} 1/(|ℬ_{n,h}||𝒯_n|) Σ_{k∈ℬ_{n,h}}Σ_{t∈𝒯_n} X(k,t)²)，在音符起始後120毫秒的窗口𝒯_n內，沿著該音符最多5個保留諧波(H_n≤5)附近±1個頻帶ℬ_{n,h}去累加能量。第二個是「起始窗頻譜通量」OSF：OSF_n = log(1+1/|𝒪_n| Σ_{t∈𝒪_n}Σ_k[X(k,t)−X(k,t−1)]₊)，量測起始前20毫秒到起始後80毫秒這個窗口𝒪_n內的寬頻能量增幅。這兩個參數合起來組成目標向量 Z。

代理模型架構：一個6層、d=256、8個注意力頭、前饋層寬1024、dropout 0.1的Transformer編碼器，把一段5秒內最多N_max=64個音符的序列，映成預測出的(PHE_n, OSF_n)配對Z̃。每個音符token由三部分組成：可學習的音高嵌入、把起始時間/時長/力度映射成加性token方向的共享可學習基底、以及用MLP做的位置編碼；輸出頭是d→d→2維。調適損失用Huber loss（β=1.0，對離群值穩健）：ℒ_params = Huber_β(Z̃−Z)。

兩條路徑共用的正則化項：一個「反崩塌」正則化ℒ_anti = (v̄−μ)²+max(0,σ²_min−Var(v̂))（μ=0.5，σ²_min=0.01），防止模型為了討好損失函數把所有力度都預測成同一個值；另一個是「軟飽和懲罰」ℒ_satu = (1/N)Σ_{n=1}^N[max(0,v̂_n−τ)]²（τ=0.95），懲罰預測值逼近上限。總損失是ℒ=ℒ_main+λ_antiℒ_anti+λ_satuℒ_satu，三者權重比為1:0.2:0.4。

資料與實驗設定：來源域是鋼琴MAESTRO V3（198.7小時，有真實力度標籤），目標域是吉他GAPS資料集（14小時，尼龍弦古典吉他）；額外用SMD（4.7小時鋼琴）跟François Leduc FL資料集（4.0小時、混合各種吉他）做跨資料集驗證。SoundFont選用Salamander Grand Piano（鋼琴）與Spanish Classical Guitar（吉他），另外拿YDP Grand Piano做敏感度測試。

診斷實驗（力度復原）：在合成音訊上測試「代理模型的梯度方向是否真的指向正確的力度」——把MIDI用SoundFont渲染成音訊，把力度重設成常數64，再用梯度下降去優化力度，直到代理模型的預測跟目標PHE/OSF吻合。5秒切片下，鋼琴的每音符MAE從初始37.9降到12.2（域內，改善67.8%）、壓力測試下從38.5降到17.7（改善54.0%）；吉他從34.6降到9.1（域內，改善73.7%）、壓力測試下從33.8降到10.7（改善68.3%）。5秒切片被選為預設設定，10秒切片沒有帶來額外增益。

跨樂器主結果：基線包括「常數力度64」、「VeloEst零樣本套用（鋼琴預訓練、不調適）」、「VeloEst+Diff-Synth」、「VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy」。評估指標用Bark尺度特定響度相關係數r_BSSL、Bark尺度總響度相關係數r_BSTL（沒有真實標籤的資料集只能用這兩個），以及有標籤的鋼琴資料集才能算的每音符力度MAE_Velo。鋼琴（MAESTRO測試集）：常數力度MAE_Velo=14.8、r_BSSL=0.757、r_BSTL=0.622；VeloEst預訓練MAE_Velo=3.9、r_BSSL=0.878、r_BSTL=0.892；VeloEst+Diff-Synth反而惡化到MAE_Velo=19.2、r_BSSL=0.776、r_BSTL=0.752；VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy是MAE_Velo=10.5、r_BSSL=0.869、r_BSTL=0.881，比Diff-Synth全面好但仍不及鋼琴原生的預訓練模型（畢竟這裡鋼琴本身就是源域，不需要調適）。吉他（GAPS測試集）：常數力度r_BSSL=0.769、r_BSTL=0.798；VeloEst零樣本套用（不調適）分數剛好跟常數力度一樣（因為零樣本沒有用到吉他資料校準）；VeloEst+Diff-Synth大幅退步到r_BSSL=0.669、r_BSTL=0.624，比什麼都不做的常數力度還差；VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy則是這四者中最好的r_BSSL=0.794、r_BSTL=0.860。跨資料集：SMD鋼琴上Diff-SFProxy的MAE_Velo=11.3、r_BSSL=0.821、r_BSTL=0.806；FL吉他上r_BSSL=0.777、r_BSTL=0.788。

關鍵發現：在FL吉他資料集上，5秒切片的Diff-Synth（r_BSSL=0.646）甚至低於常數力度基線（r_BSSL=0.714）；而且切片長度拉長時，Diff-SFProxy幾乎不受影響（變化僅±0.006），Diff-Synth卻掉了0.083，說明波形空間監督對切片長度這種無關因素非常敏感，參數空間監督穩健得多。

消融：拿掉反崩塌與飽和正則化，MAESTRO上r_BSSL從0.869掉到0.789，輸出會塌縮成接近常數（但MAE因為塌縮點剛好接近中位數而變化不大，這是MAE這個指標的盲點）。拿掉鋼琴預訓練權重直接調適，MAESTRO上MAE_Velo惡化到20.5（相對10.5），吉他四項指標裡有三項都比常數力度基線還差，證明鋼琴預訓練提供的先驗知識是不可或缺的一環，不是可有可無的暖身。

指標選擇驗證：另外用MAESTRO的真實力度標籤，比較Pearson相關係數、餘弦相似度、MAE三種指標在「動態敏感度」（真實標籤與常數64之間的差距Δ）跟「SoundFont穩健度」（換一套SoundFont後分數的變化量）這兩個性質上的表現，結果Pearson相關係數在動態敏感度上表現最好（BSSL上+0.118、BSTL上+0.263），同時對換SoundFont最穩健（BSSL上只差0.034、BSTL上只差0.011），這是論文選用Pearson相關係數當主要評估指標的依據。

Two adaptation paths: Diff-Synth feeds note events (pitch, onset, duration) plus predicted velocities into a frozen, instrument-specific differentiable synthesizer (DDSP-Piano for piano, DDSP-Guitar-Synth for guitar) to render audio ã, then compares ã against real performance audio a with a multi-resolution STFT spectral loss: ℒ_audio = Σ_ω∈Ω(‖|STFT_ω(ã)|−|STFT_ω(a)|‖₁ + ‖log(|STFT_ω(ã)|+ε)−log(|STFT_ω(a)|+ε)‖₁), with Ω={2048,1024,512,256,128,64} spanning six window sizes.

The second path — and the paper's core contribution — is Diff-SFProxy: instead of comparing full waveforms, it extracts two velocity-related acoustic parameters from real performance audio as supervision targets. The first, Pitch-Conditioned Harmonic Energy (PHE): PHE_n = log(1+Σ_{h=1}^{H_n} 1/(|ℬ_{n,h}||𝒯_n|) Σ_{k∈ℬ_{n,h}}Σ_{t∈𝒯_n} X(k,t)²), accumulating energy within a 120ms post-onset window 𝒯_n around up to H_n≤5 retained harmonics, each within a ±1-bin band ℬ_{n,h}. The second, Onset-Window Spectral Flux (OSF): OSF_n = log(1+1/|𝒪_n| Σ_{t∈𝒪_n}Σ_k[X(k,t)−X(k,t−1)]₊), measuring broadband energy increase in a window 𝒪_n spanning 20ms before to 80ms after onset. Together these form a target vector Z.

Proxy architecture: a 6-layer Transformer encoder (d=256, 8 heads, FFN width 1024, dropout 0.1) maps a 5-second segment of up to N_max=64 notes to predicted (PHE_n, OSF_n) pairs Z̃. Each note token combines a learnable pitch embedding, a shared learnable basis mapping onset/duration/velocity to an additive token direction, and an MLP-based positional encoding; the output head goes d→d→2. The adaptation loss uses a Huber loss (β=1.0, robust to outliers): ℒ_params = Huber_β(Z̃−Z).

Shared regularizers across both paths: an anti-collapse term ℒ_anti = (v̄−μ)²+max(0,σ²_min−Var(v̂)) (μ=0.5, σ²_min=0.01) preventing the model from collapsing all predictions to one convenient value, and a soft saturation penalty ℒ_satu = (1/N)Σ_{n=1}^N[max(0,v̂_n−τ)]² (τ=0.95) discouraging near-ceiling predictions. The combined loss is ℒ=ℒ_main+λ_antiℒ_anti+λ_satuℒ_satu, weighted 1:0.2:0.4.

Data and setup: the source domain is piano MAESTRO V3 (198.7 hours, real velocity labels), the target domain is guitar GAPS (14 hours, nylon-string classical guitar), with SMD (4.7 hours piano) and the François Leduc FL dataset (4.0 hours, mixed guitar types) used for cross-dataset validation. SoundFonts: Salamander Grand Piano for piano, Spanish Classical Guitar for guitar, plus YDP Grand Piano for a sensitivity check.

Diagnostic (velocity recovery): tests on synthetic audio whether the proxy's gradient actually points toward the correct velocity — render MIDI via SoundFont, reset velocity to a constant 64, then optimize velocity via gradient descent until the proxy's prediction matches the target PHE/OSF. At 5-second segments, piano per-note MAE drops from 37.9 to 12.2 in-domain (67.8% gain) and 38.5 to 17.7 under stress testing (54.0% gain); guitar drops from 34.6 to 9.1 in-domain (73.7% gain) and 33.8 to 10.7 under stress testing (68.3% gain). 5-second segments were chosen as default since 10-second segments gave no additional gain.

Main cross-instrument results: baselines are constant velocity 64, zero-shot VeloEst (piano-pretrained, no adaptation), VeloEst+Diff-Synth, and VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy. Metrics are Pearson correlation on Bark-scale specific loudness (r_BSSL) and total loudness (r_BSTL) — the only options for datasets lacking ground truth — plus per-note velocity MAE_Velo where labels exist. Piano (MAESTRO test): constant velocity MAE_Velo=14.8, r_BSSL=0.757, r_BSTL=0.622; VeloEst pretrained MAE_Velo=3.9, r_BSSL=0.878, r_BSTL=0.892; VeloEst+Diff-Synth actually worsens to MAE_Velo=19.2, r_BSSL=0.776, r_BSTL=0.752; VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy reaches MAE_Velo=10.5, r_BSSL=0.869, r_BSTL=0.881 — beating Diff-Synth across the board though still short of the native piano-pretrained model (unsurprising, since piano is the source domain here and needs no adaptation). Guitar (GAPS test): constant velocity r_BSSL=0.769, r_BSTL=0.798; zero-shot VeloEst matches constant velocity exactly (zero-shot uses no guitar calibration); VeloEst+Diff-Synth degrades sharply to r_BSSL=0.669, r_BSTL=0.624 — worse than doing nothing; VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy is best of the four at r_BSSL=0.794, r_BSTL=0.860. Cross-dataset: on SMD piano, Diff-SFProxy gets MAE_Velo=11.3, r_BSSL=0.821, r_BSTL=0.806; on FL guitar, r_BSSL=0.777, r_BSTL=0.788.

Key finding: on FL guitar, 5-second Diff-Synth (r_BSSL=0.646) falls below the constant-velocity baseline (r_BSSL=0.714), and while Diff-SFProxy is nearly insensitive to crop length (±0.006 change), Diff-Synth drops 0.083 as crop length increases — showing waveform-space supervision is highly sensitive to factors unrelated to velocity, while parameter-space supervision is far more robust.

Ablations: removing the anti-collapse and saturation regularizers drops MAESTRO r_BSSL from 0.869 to 0.789, with outputs collapsing toward near-constant values (MAE barely moves because the collapse point happens to sit near the median — a blind spot of the MAE metric itself). Removing piano-pretrained weights and adapting from scratch worsens MAESTRO MAE_Velo to 20.5 (versus 10.5), and three of four guitar metrics fall below the constant-velocity baseline — proving piano pretraining supplies indispensable prior knowledge rather than being an optional warm start.

Metric justification: using MAESTRO's ground-truth labels, the paper compares Pearson correlation, cosine similarity, and MAE on two properties — dynamics sensitivity (the gap Δ between ground truth and constant-64) and SoundFont robustness (score change when swapping SoundFonts). Pearson correlation scores best on dynamics sensitivity (+0.118 on BSSL, +0.263 on BSTL) while remaining most robust to SoundFont swaps (only 0.034 difference on BSSL, 0.011 on BSTL) — the basis for choosing it as the primary metric.

限制Limitations

作者自己指出，復原出來的力度值本質上是「受限於所選渲染器」的：它被定義成「能讓渲染音訊最接近原始音訊感知動態的那個力度值」，而不是絕對意義上的真實演奏力度。這一點很重要，因為真實錄音裡影響響度的因素不只有力度——踏板、連續控制器、殘響等等都會混進響度變化裡，而這些因素目前完全沒有被模型獨立建模，換句話說，復原出來的「力度」有可能悄悄吸收了這些其他因素的影響，卻仍然被記錄成一個力度數值。另外，論文目前只驗證了鋼琴到吉他這一組遷移，作者自己也把「推廣到小提琴、管樂器等其他樂器」、「自動化SoundFont挑選（目前靠人工挑選音色相近的SoundFont）」、以及「復原其他表達性參數（踏板延音、運弓力度）」列為明確的未來工作，這些都還是未解決的問題，不是本文已經處理掉的範圍。

The authors themselves note that the recovered velocity is fundamentally "renderer-conditioned": it's defined as whichever velocity value makes the rendered audio best match the original audio's perceptual dynamics, not an absolute ground truth of the actual performance. This matters because real recordings' loudness is shaped by more than velocity alone — pedaling, continuous controllers, reverb all contribute — and none of these are modeled independently here, meaning the recovered "velocity" may silently absorb the influence of these other factors while still being recorded as a single velocity number. The paper also only validates one transfer pair, piano-to-guitar; the authors explicitly list generalizing to violin, wind instruments, automating SoundFont selection (currently done manually by picking a timbrally similar SoundFont), and recovering other expressive parameters (sustain pedal, bow pressure) as future work — these remain open problems, not something the paper has already resolved.

討論Discussion

把整篇論文的三組實驗放在一起看，講的其實是同一件事：監督訊號的「粒度」比監督訊號的「豐富度」更重要。波形層級的監督理論上包含比兩個聲學參數多得多的資訊，但這篇論文的結果反覆顯示，這個「多」反而是負擔——完整波形裡混雜著跟力度無關的錄音環境、合成器音色落差等雜訊，這些雜訊在跨樂器遷移時被放大，導致Diff-Synth在吉他上不但沒有改善、反而比什麼都不做（常數力度）還差。相對地，Diff-SFProxy把監督訊號主動限縮到跟力度直接掛勾的兩個聲學參數（諧波能量與起始頻譜通量），代價是理論上的資訊量變少，換來的卻是穩健的跨樂器與跨資料集遷移能力，而且對切片長度這種無關的實驗設定幾乎不敏感。消融實驗進一步補強這個結論：拿掉正則化項會讓模型走捷徑塌縮成常數輸出（而且MAE這個指標還看不太出來，凸顯了論文選用Pearson相關係數當主要指標的必要性）；拿掉鋼琴預訓練則證明了「先在有標籤的鄰近域打好基礎，再遷移」這個策略本身的價值，而不只是靠調適損失就能憑空生出正確答案。對MIR領域而言，這篇論文提供的不只是一個吉他力度估計器，更是一個可以複製到其他標籤稀缺任務上的方法論：當目標任務缺乏真實標籤、但可以定義出「哪些聲學參數跟目標變數直接相關」時，把可微分渲染的監督訊號限縮到這些參數上，可能會比端到端比對整段波形更可靠。

Taken together, the paper's three experimental threads make a single point: the granularity of a supervision signal matters more than its richness. Waveform-level supervision in principle carries far more information than two acoustic parameters, but the results repeatedly show that this abundance becomes a liability — the full waveform mixes in noise unrelated to velocity (recording environment, synthesizer-versus-real-instrument timbral mismatch), and that noise gets amplified under cross-instrument transfer, to the point where Diff-Synth on guitar doesn't merely fail to help but underperforms doing nothing at all. Diff-SFProxy, by contrast, deliberately narrows the supervision signal to two acoustic parameters directly tied to velocity (harmonic energy and onset spectral flux); the theoretical information loss buys back robust cross-instrument and cross-dataset transfer, and near-insensitivity to an unrelated experimental setting like crop length. The ablations reinforce this: removing the regularizers lets the model take the shortcut of collapsing to near-constant output (something MAE barely detects, underscoring why Pearson correlation was chosen as the primary metric), while removing piano pretraining demonstrates that starting from a solid labeled neighboring domain is itself valuable — the adaptation loss alone cannot conjure correct answers out of nothing. For MIR broadly, the paper offers not just a guitar velocity estimator but a transferable methodology: when a target task lacks ground truth but a handful of acoustic parameters can be identified as directly tied to the variable of interest, constraining differentiable-rendering supervision to those parameters can be more reliable than matching the entire waveform end to end.

為什麼挑這篇：大部分符號音樂分析都預設音樂可以拆成離散的「音符」單位，但盤索里（pansori）這類韓國傳統音樂的裝飾唱腔「시김새（sigimsae）」是連續滑動的音高輪廓，套用音符級轉譜會直接抹掉這些裝飾的細節。這篇論文不套用既定的音高格點，而是設計了一個VQ-VAE，搭配一個刻意迴避「切割位置敏感」問題的轉換最小化重建損失（同時搜尋時間偏移、時間縮放、音高偏移、音高縮放四種變換取最小MSE），從無標籤音訊裡直接學出一組256個離散音高輪廓的詞彙表。學出來的token不但能穩定地對應到專家定義的sigimsae類別，還能在完全沒有調式標籤的情況下，把兩個盤索里主要調式（계면조Gyemyeonjo、우조Ujo）的裝飾音差異分出來，是一篇把「轉換不變性」這個工程細節做得很扎實、又直接連到音樂學結論的論文，故列為深度分析。Why this one: Most symbolic music analysis assumes music can be split into discrete "note" units, but the ornamented vocal technique sigimsae in Korean pansori is a continuously sliding pitch contour that note-level transcription simply erases. Rather than imposing a predefined pitch grid, this paper builds a VQ-VAE with a transformation-minimized reconstruction loss purpose-built to dodge segmentation-position sensitivity (searching over temporal offset, temporal scale, pitch offset, and pitch-range scale jointly for the minimum MSE), learning a 256-entry vocabulary of discrete pitch-contour tokens directly from unlabeled audio. The resulting tokens not only align stably with expert-defined sigimsae categories, but — with zero mode supervision — separate the ornamental differences between pansori's two principal modes (Gyemyeonjo and Ujo). It's a rare case where a carefully engineered invariance detail connects directly to a musicological finding, hence flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

把音樂表示成一串離散單位，是音樂分析裡最核心的抽象之一，西方符號音樂的「音符」正是這種抽象的具體體現。但作者指出，許多音樂傳統並不完全符合這個前提：像韓國盤索里這類以連續音高滑動為核心的唱腔，裝飾音「시김새（sigimsae）」本身就是滑音、抖音、轉音等連續運動，如果硬套用音符級的轉譜，這些裝飾細節會被直接抹平消失。要分析這類「以輪廓為核心」的音樂傳統，需要一種既能捕捉連續音高運動、又能提供離散、可計數、可統計分析單位的表徵方式——這正是論文要解決的問題：如何從無標籤音訊裡，直接學出一組屬於這個音樂傳統自己的離散音高輪廓詞彙，而不是借用西方音符或印度音樂那種人工定義的音高格點。

Representing music as a sequence of discrete units is one of the central abstractions in music analysis, with the Western "note" being its most familiar instantiation. But the authors point out that many musical traditions don't fully conform to this premise: Korean pansori singing, built around continuous pitch movement, expresses its core ornamentation — sigimsae — as sliding, vibrato-like, or turning pitch gestures that are inherently continuous. Forcing note-level transcription onto this repertoire simply erases that ornamental detail. Analyzing such contour-centric traditions calls for a representation that captures continuous pitch movement while still providing discrete, countable units suitable for statistical analysis — which is exactly what this paper sets out to build: a vocabulary of discrete pitch-contour tokens learned directly from unlabeled audio, native to the tradition itself, rather than borrowed Western notes or the hand-designed pitch grids used in prior Indian-music work.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

相關研究分兩條線。第一條是既有的音高輪廓抽象法：過去在印度藝術音樂上的研究，要嘛用音樂理論定義好的音高格點，要嘛把輪廓細切成10音分一格的精細量化區間；另一路則是用DTW相似度或矩陣輪廓（matrix profile）去挖掘反覆出現的旋律動機，但這類方法產生的是長度不一的片段實例，而這篇論文的做法是對整條輪廓產生「密集」的定長token序列，用固定詞彙表覆蓋每一段。第二條是VQ-VAE與離散表徵學習：標準VQ-VAE透過重建損失加向量量化懲罰項學離散潛碼，過去在音樂與音訊上的向量量化主要用在生成與壓縮，而這篇論文把它轉向「可解讀的分析單位」這個不同的目標——這個目標轉向，正是這篇論文在既有VQ-VAE應用光譜裡的定位。

Related work splits into two threads. The first is prior pitch-contour abstractions: earlier work on Indian art music either used music-theoretically defined pitch grids or finely quantized contours into 10-cent bins, while a separate line mined recurring melodic motifs via DTW similarity or matrix profiles — but that produces variable-length instances rather than a dense token sequence. This paper instead produces a dense, fixed-length token sequence over the entire contour using one shared codebook. The second thread is VQ-VAE and discrete representation learning: standard VQ-VAE learns discrete latent codes via reconstruction loss plus a vector-quantization penalty; in music and audio, vector quantization has previously been used mainly for generation and compression, whereas this paper redirects it toward interpretable analytical units — that redirection of purpose is exactly where this paper sits in the existing spectrum of VQ-VAE applications.

方法Method

架構基礎：模型用1D卷積層，搭配兩個關鍵設計。第一是接受域分離：輸入先被切成互不重疊的128幀窗口，確保token跟輪廓片段之間是1對1對應，並且每個片段都先減去自身的音高中位數，只捕捉相對音高運動（而非絕對音高），藉此避免解碼時各token之間互相依賴。第二、也是核心創新，是轉換最小化重建損失：標準的逐幀MSE會失效，因為即使兩段輪廓對應到同一種裝飾音型態，只要時間上有一點點錯位，逐幀MSE就會被放大成一個很大的誤差。這篇論文的損失函數改成：在時間偏移τ、時間縮放s、音高偏移b、音高範圍縮放a四個變換維度上，找出讓重建誤差最小的那一組變換參數，只懲罰「最佳對齊後仍然存在」的誤差，而不是無腦懲罰所有逐幀差異。實際搜尋的變換範圍是：時間縮放{1, 1.08, 1.2}，時間偏移取重新取樣後所有合法位置，音高範圍縮放{0.85, 1.0, 1.15}，音高偏移{-0.1, 0.0, 0.1}。

實驗資料與設定：主要資料是約280小時的盤索里錄音，先用HT-Demucs分離人聲，再用CREPE做F0抽取（10毫秒解析度，附信心分數），信心過低的幀被濾掉，間隙在2幀以內的用線性內插補上，含有大量無聲/無音高區間的片段整段捨棄（因為模型沒辦法表示缺失的音高值），訓練/驗證/測試切分為8:1:1。編碼器與解碼器都是6層堆疊的1D卷積，接受域128幀，詞彙表256個條目，訓練5萬步，AdamW，學習率0.001，批次大小1024，量化損失係數β=2.0，選驗證重建損失最低的checkpoint。

評估一：切割位移一致性：測試token指派在切割起始位置偏移時是否穩定，分別在偏移0-60幀（小於接受域一半）與0-124幀（幾乎涵蓋整個相位）兩種範圍下，量測token分布的KL散度（越低越好）與token匹配準確率（越高越好）。結果（Table 1）：自編碼器基線的KLD高達1.747(0-60)/2.085(0-124)，準確率只有0.100/0.115；VQ-VAE大幅改善到KLD 0.657/0.859、準確率0.462/0.488；只加時間對齊在這個指標上表現最好（KLD 0.531/0.735、準確率0.519/0.561）；但加上全部四種變換反而在這個特定指標上略微變差（KLD 0.652/0.881、準確率0.456/0.495）——因為音高變換讓「形狀相似但音高不同」的輪廓也能被匹配到同一個token，這對下游任務有利，但對這個嚴格要求「同一段輪廓要對應同一個token」的一致性指標而言是不利的。

評估二：시김새（sigimsae）分類：用NIA AI-Hub標籤的韓國傳統音樂資料，「全部」設定有34.0小時、42.3k段、6個sigimsae類別，「純人聲」設定有10.0小時、13.5k段、5個類別，以8:1:1按曲目切分。分類器是用token到標籤的頻率做最大後驗估計（MAP），溫度參數τ用驗證集調校，這是刻意設計成「探測任務」，測試無監督學到的token本身有沒有帶標籤資訊，而不是要打造一個強力分類器。另外拿一個直接用sigimsae全監督訓練的ED-TCN基線做對照（不完全可比，但可看出上限）。結果（Table 2）：自編碼器F1-macro為0.284（全部）/0.324（人聲），VQ-VAE略降到0.266/0.319，加上時間對齊回升到0.285/0.320，加上全部變換達到最佳的0.285/0.331，mAP在全部變換下也是四者中最高（0.278/0.324）；監督式ED-TCN則達到0.463/0.541，顯示無監督token跟全監督基線之間仍有明顯落差，但作者強調「單一token在人聲情境下，不靠序列建模或標籤監督，就能拿到大約五分之三的F1」。質性上，加上全部變換的VQ-VAE學出六個視覺上清楚可分的類別代表token，而自編碼器基線把四個類別混進同一個token裡，說明轉換損失確實把「跟形狀無關的因素」從訓練訊號裡拿掉了，讓輪廓形狀成為詞彙表的主要區辨依據。

評估三：盤索里調式分析：用標籤調式的盤索里子集（26.7小時계면조Gyemyeonjo、8.7小時우조Ujo），統計每個token在各調式片段裡出現的次數，找出對每個調式而言P(調式|token)最高的token。結果：token 87對應到「上揚轉音」手法，token 141對應到「下滑輪廓」，兩者都是盤索里研究裡被記錄為계면조典型特徵的裝飾唱法；token 171對應到우조特有的樂句。特別值得一提的是token 54：它代表「向主音下行大二度」的動作，兩個調式都有，但主要出現在우조，區分兩者的關鍵是「上方音在解決到主音之前所加的寬幅顫音」——這代表在完全沒有調式監督的情況下，tokenizer把兩個調式的差異，沿著「裝飾音」這個維度分開了，而這正是音符級轉譜會直接丟掉的訊息。

Architectural foundation: the model uses 1D convolutional layers with two key design choices. First, receptive-field separation: input is pre-segmented into non-overlapping 128-frame windows, ensuring a 1:1 correspondence between tokens and contour segments, with each segment's median pitch subtracted so the model captures relative rather than absolute pitch movement and avoids inter-token dependencies during decoding. Second, and the core innovation, is a transformation-minimized reconstruction loss: standard frame-level MSE fails because even when two contours correspond to the same local ornamental pattern, a small temporal misalignment inflates the frame-level error. This paper's loss instead searches over four transformation dimensions — temporal offset τ, temporal scale s, pitch offset b, and pitch-range scale a — for whichever combination minimizes reconstruction error, penalizing only the error that remains after optimal alignment rather than blindly penalizing every frame-level discrepancy. The searched ranges are: temporal scales {1, 1.08, 1.2}, all valid temporal offsets from the resampled output, pitch-range scaling {0.85, 1.0, 1.15}, and pitch shifting {-0.1, 0.0, 0.1}.

Data and setup: the primary dataset is roughly 280 hours of pansori recordings, with vocals isolated via HT-Demucs and F0 extracted via CREPE (10ms resolution with confidence scores); low-confidence frames are dropped, gaps of ≤2 frames linearly interpolated, and segments with extensive unvoiced regions discarded entirely (since the model cannot represent missing pitch values); the split is 8:1:1 train/validation/test. Encoder and decoder are each 6 stacked 1D conv layers with a 128-frame receptive field, a 256-entry codebook, 50K training updates via AdamW at learning rate 0.001, batch size 1024, quantization loss coefficient β=2.0, selecting the checkpoint with lowest validation reconstruction loss.

Evaluation 1: segmentation-shift consistency tests whether token assignments stay stable as the segmentation start position shifts, measured via token-distribution KLD (lower is better) and token-matching accuracy (higher is better) across offset ranges of 0-60 frames (under half the receptive field) and 0-124 frames (near-full phase coverage). Results (Table 1): the autoencoder baseline scores a poor KLD of 1.747(0-60)/2.085(0-124) with accuracy of only 0.100/0.115; VQ-VAE improves substantially to KLD 0.657/0.859, accuracy 0.462/0.488; adding temporal alignment alone performs best on this specific metric (KLD 0.531/0.735, accuracy 0.519/0.561); but adding all four transformations performs slightly worse here (KLD 0.652/0.881, accuracy 0.456/0.495) — because pitch transformations let shape-similar-but-differently-pitched contours match the same token, which helps downstream tasks but hurts this strict same-contour-same-token consistency measure.

Evaluation 2: sigimsae classification uses NIA AI-Hub-annotated Korean traditional music: a "full" setting of 34.0 hours/42.3k segments/6 sigimsae classes, and a "vocal-only" setting of 10.0 hours/13.5k segments/5 classes, split 8:1:1 at the track level. The classifier is a token-to-label frequency-based MAP estimator with a validation-tuned temperature τ — deliberately built as a probing task to check whether unsupervised tokens carry label information, not to build a strong classifier — compared against a fully supervised ED-TCN baseline trained directly on sigimsae (not strictly comparable, but indicating an upper bound). Results (Table 2): the autoencoder scores F1-macro 0.284 (full)/0.324 (vocal); VQ-VAE dips slightly to 0.266/0.319; adding temporal alignment recovers to 0.285/0.320; the full transformation setting achieves the best 0.285/0.331, with the highest mAP among the four (0.278/0.324); the supervised ED-TCN reaches 0.463/0.541, showing a clear gap remains between unsupervised tokens and a fully supervised baseline — though the authors note "a single token recovers roughly three-fifths of its F1 on the vocal setting without sequence modeling or label supervision." Qualitatively, the full-transformation VQ-VAE learns six visually distinct class-representative tokens, while the autoencoder baseline conflates four classes into a single token — confirming the transformation loss removes shape-irrelevant factors from the training signal, leaving contour shape as the codebook's main basis for discrimination.

Evaluation 3: pansori mode analysis uses a mode-annotated pansori subset (26.7 hours Gyemyeonjo, 8.7 hours Ujo), counting token occurrences within each mode's segments to find, for each mode, the token with the highest P(mode|token). Results: token 87 captures an "upper turn gesture" and token 141 a "downward gliding contour," both documented in pansori scholarship as defining Gyemyeonjo ornaments; token 171 corresponds to phrases characteristic of Ujo. Notably, token 54 represents a "descending major-second motion to the tonic" shared by both modes but occurring predominantly in Ujo — the distinguishing factor being the wide vibrato applied to the upper note before it resolves to the tonic. Without any mode supervision, the tokenizer separated the two modes along exactly this ornamental dimension — a distinction note-level transcription would simply discard.

限制Limitations

作者明確承認的限制集中在資料涵蓋範圍上。第一，無法處理無聲區間：這個1D-CNN tokenizer假設輸入是密集連續的輪廓，沒辦法直接建模無聲片段，因此訓練與評估都被限縮在「無聲幀很少」的片段裡——這意味著目前這套方法還不能直接套用在完整錄音上，必須先手動篩掉問題片段。第二，無法端對端處理完整錄音：正因為無聲區間處理不了，論文自己承認「要讓模型能端對端地把整段長錄音token化，還需要明確處理無聲區間」，這是被列為未來工作的必要步驟，不是已解決的問題。第三，跟監督式基線之間仍有明顯落差：sigimsae分類的F1在兩種設定下都比ED-TCN低了大約0.2到0.25，說明無監督學到的token雖然帶有標籤資訊，但拿來單獨做分類還遠遠比不上專門訓練的監督式模型——這套方法目前的定位比較像「音樂學分析的輔助工具」，而不是要取代監督式分類器。

The authors' explicitly acknowledged limitations concentrate on data coverage. First, unvoiced regions cannot be handled: the 1D-CNN tokenizer assumes a dense, continuous contour and cannot directly model unvoiced segments, so training and evaluation were restricted to segments with few unvoiced frames — meaning the method cannot yet be applied directly to full recordings without first manually filtering out problematic segments. Second, no end-to-end handling of full recordings: precisely because unvoiced regions aren't handled, the authors concede that "extending the model to handle unvoiced regions explicitly is necessary" for end-to-end tokenization of entire recordings — an acknowledged future-work requirement, not a solved problem. Third, a clear gap remains versus supervised baselines: sigimsae classification F1 trails the ED-TCN baseline by roughly 0.2 to 0.25 in both settings, showing that while unsupervised tokens do carry label information, they're far from matching a purpose-trained supervised classifier on their own — positioning this method more as a musicological analysis aid than a supervised-classifier replacement.

討論Discussion

三組評估各自驗證了論文的一個主張。切割位移一致性實驗證明「轉換最小化損失」確實解決了它想解決的問題——單純的VQ-VAE已經比自編碼器穩定得多，而額外加上溫和的時間對齊（不含音高變換）在這個指標上表現最好，顯示問題的核心確實出在「時間錯位」而非別的因素。sigimsae分類的探測結果證明，即使沒有任何標籤監督，學出來的token詞彙表仍然帶有跟專家標籤高度相關的資訊，而且質性分析清楚看到VQ-VAE把六個裝飾音類別分進六個視覺上可辨識的token，自編碼器卻把其中四類混成一團——這代表「轉換不變性」這個工程細節，直接轉譯成了「詞彙表更貼近音樂學意義上的分類」這個具體收穫。最有說服力的是調式分析：在完全沒有調式標籤的情況下，tokenizer學出的token不只能對應到既有音樂學研究記錄的裝飾唱法（如계면조的上揚轉音、下滑輪廓），還能在共享同一個「主音下行大二度」動作的兩個調式之間，靠「顫音寬窄」這個純粹裝飾性的維度把它們分開——這正是音符級轉譜會直接丟棄、卻是音樂學家長期關注的細節。作者把這個結果放在更大的脈絡下：西方符號音樂長期享有「切成音符、做n-gram統計、算轉移機率」這類分析工具，而以輪廓為核心的音樂傳統（不只是韓國傳統音樂，也包括印度藝術音樂或流行歌曲的人聲滑音）一直缺少對應的工具，這篇論文的token化方法提供了一條可行路徑，讓這些傳統也能享有同等級的計算分析，雖然距離「端對端處理任意錄音」還有段路要走。

The three evaluations each validate a different piece of the paper's argument. Segmentation-shift consistency confirms the transformation-minimized loss solves the exact problem it targets — plain VQ-VAE is already far more stable than the autoencoder, and adding mild temporal alignment alone (without pitch transformations) performs best on this metric, showing the core issue really is temporal misalignment rather than something else. The sigimsae classification probe shows that even with zero label supervision, the learned token vocabulary carries information highly correlated with expert annotations, and the qualitative analysis clearly shows VQ-VAE separating six ornament classes into six visually distinguishable tokens while the autoencoder conflates four of them — meaning the transformation-invariance engineering detail translates directly into a vocabulary that better matches musicologically meaningful categories. The most persuasive result is the mode analysis: with zero mode supervision, the learned tokens not only correspond to ornamental techniques already documented in pansori scholarship (Gyemyeonjo's upper-turn gesture, downward glide), but also separate two modes that share the same "descending major second to the tonic" gesture, using purely the width of the vibrato as the distinguishing dimension — precisely the kind of detail note-level transcription discards but musicologists have long cared about. The authors situate this in a larger context: Western symbolic music has long enjoyed tools for splitting into notes, computing n-gram statistics, and transition probabilities, while contour-centric traditions (not only Korean traditional music but also Indian art music or vocal glides in pop singing) have lacked equivalent tools — this tokenization method offers a workable path toward giving these traditions the same tier of computational analysis, though there's still distance to travel before it can handle arbitrary full recordings end to end.

為什麼挑這篇：光學音樂辨識（OMR）在單聲部與鋼琴譜上已經有明顯進展，但「多聲部樂譜」——像弦樂四重奏這種四個聲部同時演奏、疊在同一個系統上——一直因為缺資料集而停滯不前。這篇論文花了超過一百小時手工把OpenScore String Quartet corpus的樂譜跟IMSLP掃描本逐音符對齊，產出第一個專門給多聲部OMR用的資料集，並用兩種代表性架構、三種編碼格式（LMXE、**kern、ABC）跑出完整的基準結果。它把「編碼格式的選擇」這個常被忽略的變因量化到具體數字，是扎實但不追求架構新穎性的資料集論文，因此不列為深度分析。Why this one: Optical music recognition (OMR) has advanced substantially on monophonic and piano-form scores, but multi-part scores — like string quartets, where four independent voices stack on a single system — have stalled for lack of a suitable dataset. It quantifies the often-overlooked effect of encoding-format choice with concrete numbers — a solid dataset-and-benchmark contribution rather than a novel-architecture one, so it isn't flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

光學音樂辨識（OMR）把樂譜影像轉成結構化的數位格式，對保存音樂文化遺產、以及後續用MIR技術分析符號化樂譜都很關鍵。深度學習近年大幅推進了這個領域，但幾乎所有成果都集中在單聲部樂譜（因為歷史文獻豐富、任務形式單純）和鋼琴譜（如GrandStaff資料集）。「多聲部樂譜」——多個聲部同時由不同樂器演奏——的進展則相對有限，主因是缺乏合適的資料集。弦樂四重奏是這類樂譜的典型代表：一個系統裡四個獨立聲部並排，比鋼琴譜的複雜度更高，還帶來音域寬廣、頻繁的加線、以及樂章內外的譜號變化等額外挑戰。這篇論文正是為了填補這個資料缺口而生。

Optical music recognition (OMR) transcribes score images into structured digital formats, playing a key role in preserving musical cultural heritage and enabling downstream MIR analysis of symbolic scores. Deep learning has advanced the field substantially in recent years, but almost all progress concentrates on monophonic scores (due to abundant historical sources and a simple task formulation) and piano-form scores (as in the GrandStaff dataset). Multi-part scores — where multiple voices are performed simultaneously by different instruments — have seen comparatively limited progress, largely for lack of a suitable dataset. String quartets are a prototypical case: four independent parts sit side by side on a single system, substantially increasing complexity beyond piano-form notation and introducing further challenges such as wide pitch ranges, frequent ledger lines, and clef changes across and within movements. This paper exists to close that data gap.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者把OMR的既有研究分成三個世代。單聲部OMR是最成熟的一支，代表資料集有PrIMUS、Capitan、Seils、FMT、Guatemala，主要處理素歌等歷史文獻。鋼琴譜（grand staff）OMR近年由GrandStaff系列資料集帶動，發展出Sheet Music Transformer等端對端的編碼器—解碼器架構，把卷積視覺編碼器（如ConvNeXt）跟序列解碼器（RNN、Transformer）結合，直接輸出線性化的符號序列而不需要先做符號切割。多聲部OMR則基本上是空白，這也是這篇論文鎖定的缺口。論文選擇弦樂四重奏作為切入點，是因為它有現成、品質良好的數位樂譜語料庫可以擴充——OpenScore String Quartet corpus，收錄116首曲子、由47位作曲家橫跨1773到1994年寫成，並已提供乾淨的MuseScore編碼與渲染圖像。作者要做的，是把這份「乾淨版」樂譜跟IMSLP上「真實掃描版」的歷史印刷本或手稿逐音符對齊，讓兩者在token層級完全對應。

The authors divide prior OMR work into three generations. Monophonic OMR is the most mature strand, with representative datasets including PrIMUS, Capitan, Seils, FMT, and Guatemala, mostly targeting historical sources such as plainchant. Piano-form (grand staff) OMR has been driven in recent years by the GrandStaff dataset family, producing end-to-end encoder-decoder architectures such as Sheet Music Transformer, which pair convolutional visual encoders (e.g. ConvNeXt) with sequential decoders (RNN, Transformer) to directly output linearized symbolic sequences without upfront symbol segmentation. Multi-part OMR is essentially blank, and that is precisely the gap this paper targets. The authors chose string quartets as the entry point because a ready-made, high-quality digital score corpus already exists to extend — the OpenScore String Quartet corpus, comprising 116 pieces by 47 composers spanning 1773 to 1994, already offering clean MuseScore encodings and rendered images. Their job is to align this "clean" digital score against its "real scanned" historical print or manuscript counterpart on IMSLP, note by note, so the two match at the token level.

方法Method

掃描本蒐集與分類：團隊從IMSLP取回對應的掃描樂譜，分成三類：全譜（93首，80%，四個聲部同時印在一個系統上，絕大多數是排版印刷版）、分譜本（17首，15%，反映18、19世紀把室內樂拆成單一樂器分冊出版的習慣）、以及手稿（6首，5%，全部出自女性作曲家之手，品質參差不齊）。

視覺對齊與校正：OpenScore的編輯流程會把原始掃描本裡的印刷錯誤、過時的刻版慣例等不一致之處「撫平」成乾淨、適合演出的版本，但OMR需要的是渲染影像跟掃描本在token層級完全吻合的編碼。因此作者針對93份全譜掃描逐一手動重新編輯MuseScore檔案，讓渲染結果在視覺上對齊掃描本，這個過程耗時超過100小時，並把每一次編輯都記錄成Git版本歷史以便追溯。校正分成版面編輯（調整系統、頁面、段落斷點）與記譜編輯（修改譜號、符桿方向、圓滑線、連結線、顫音、符尾樑、演奏記號等音樂內容）。93份全譜裡有78份同時需要版面與記譜編輯，只有1份完全不用修改；最常見的編輯類別是音符（音高）修正，共7,183處。

影像切割：用三階段的YOLOv8模型管線，依序做系統邊界框偵測、五線譜高度回歸（把譜表縮放到讓符頭約佔4-5像素高，符合下游OMR模型的輸入尺度）、以及單一五線譜邊界框偵測，合成影像與掃描影像共用同一套管線。

三種符號編碼格式：LMXE（Extended Linearized MusicXML）是作者擴充既有Linearized MusicXML而來，加入明確的聲部token與譜型token（單聲部、多聲部、大譜表），並採用「小節內巢狀各聲部」的編碼順序，讓部分輸出即使解碼中斷也還能重建；kern用tab分隔的「軌道」（spine）組織聲部與譜表，結構限制較嚴格；ABC用ASCII助憶符號，結構鬆散但文字形式簡單，適合一般語言模型。所有格式都從同一條MuseScore匯出管線衍生，並用OMR-NED做往返轉換驗證，任何在往返過程中出現結構性或重大音樂錯誤（如小節插入/刪除、譜號錯誤等）的樣本會被剔除——kern與ABC轉換分別損失約5%與21%的系統級樣本。

最終規模：24,544張系統級影像、98,172張譜表級影像，來自116份合成樂譜與93份掃描樂譜。基準測試用Zeus（LSTM序列到序列模型，加大隱藏層到256維）與Sheet Music Transformer（SMT，ConvNeXt視覺編碼器+Transformer解碼器）兩個代表性架構，在四種樂譜層級的隨機切分（測試集互斥）上、對九種tokenization方案分別訓練到收斂，共136次獨立訓練跑，用OMR-NED（在music21符號空間裡計算插入與刪除數的正規化編輯距離）評分。

Scan collection and categorization: The team retrieved corresponding scanned scores from IMSLP and grouped them into three categories: full scores (93 pieces, 80%, all four parts on one system, predominantly typeset editions), part-books (17 pieces, 15%, reflecting 18th/19th-century chamber-music publishing as separate single-instrument booklets), and manuscripts (6 pieces, 5%, all by women composers, of varying quality).

Visual alignment and correction: OpenScore's editorial process smooths inconsistencies in the original scans — print errors, outdated typesetting conventions — into a clean, performance-ready edition, but OMR needs encodings whose rendered image matches the scan at the token level. The authors therefore manually re-edited the MuseScore files for all 93 full-score scans so the rendering visually aligns with the scan, a process spanning over 100 hours, with every edit recorded as an auditable Git commit history. Corrections split into layout edits (system, page, section breaks) and notation edits (clefs, stem direction, slurs, ties, tremolos, beaming, articulations). Of the 93 full scores, 78 needed both kinds of edits and only 1 needed none; the most common edit category was pitch correction, at 7,183 instances.

Image segmentation: A three-stage YOLOv8 pipeline sequentially performs system bounding-box detection, staff-height regression (resizing so noteheads are roughly 4-5 pixels tall, matching downstream OMR model input scale), and individual staff bounding-box detection — the same pipeline is shared across synthetic and scanned images.

Three symbolic encoding formats: LMXE (Extended Linearized MusicXML) extends existing Linearized MusicXML with explicit part tokens and a score-type token (single, multi, grandstaff), using a measure-wise nesting order across parts so partial decoder output remains reconstructable even if decoding breaks off early. kern organizes voices and staves into tab-separated "spines," a comparatively rigid structural format; ABC uses loosely structured ASCII mnemonics whose textual simplicity suits general-purpose language models. All formats derive from the same MuseScore export pipeline and are verified via round-trip conversion checked with OMR-NED — any sample showing structural or major musical errors on round-trip (measure insert/delete, wrong clef, etc.) is excluded from release, with kern and ABC conversion losing roughly 5% and 21% of system-level samples respectively.

Final scale: 24,544 system-level images and 98,172 staff-level images, drawn from 116 synthetic and 93 scanned scores. The benchmark trains two representative architectures — Zeus (an LSTM sequence-to-sequence model with hidden size increased to 256) and Sheet Music Transformer (SMT, a ConvNeXt visual encoder plus Transformer decoder) — across four mutually-exclusive-test-set random score-level splits and nine tokenization schemes, 136 independent training runs total, scored with OMR-NED (a normalized edit distance over insertions and deletions computed in music21's symbolic space).

限制Limitations

論文本身把限制講得相當清楚。首先，架構本身沒有創新：兩個基線Zeus與SMT都是既有的piano-form OMR架構直接拿來訓練，貢獻在於資料與基準，而不是模型設計，這也是這篇沒被列為「深度分析」的原因。其次，SMT對真實掃描本的遷移能力明顯較弱：從合成到掃描的表現下滑（domain shift）在全部34種設定裡都存在，但Zeus平均只退化39%（3.6個百分點），SMT卻退化100%（6.7個百分點）——論文坦承「這個落差的架構性根源，在這些實驗裡並未被單獨釐清」，也就是說作者知道SMT明顯較不穩健，但沒有進一步拆解原因。第三，外部基準Legato（在PDMX上預訓練的ABC模型）在這個資料集上表現遠差於論文自己訓練的基線（系統級36.1%對比自家SMT-CABC的6.0%），顯示光靠泛用的ABC預訓練不足以應付弦樂四重奏這種多聲部任務，但這也代表現有的通用OMR模型在遇到新譜面型態時遷移能力有限。第四，範圍侷限在弦樂四重奏（固定四聲部），論文自己在結論裡承認要往更大編制（如管弦樂總譜）推進，還需要更豐富的多聲部基準資料，目前的方法能否直接擴展到八件、十幾件樂器的總譜還不確定。第五，全部評估都在系統或譜表切好的影像上進行，還沒有處理「整頁」端對端辨識，論文把這列為未來方向而非本篇已解決的問題。

The paper is quite transparent about its own limitations. First, there's no architectural novelty: both baselines, Zeus and SMT, are existing piano-form OMR architectures trained as-is, with the contribution lying in the data and benchmark rather than model design — which is also why this entry isn't flagged as a deep dive. Second, SMT transfers noticeably worse to real scans: synthetic-to-scanned degradation appears across all 34 configurations, but Zeus degrades by only 39% (3.6 percentage points) on average while SMT degrades by 100% (6.7 points) — the paper candidly states "the architectural source of this gap is not isolated by these experiments," meaning the authors know SMT is markedly less robust but haven't diagnosed why. Third, the external Legato baseline (an ABC-based model pretrained on PDMX) performs far worse on this dataset than the paper's own trained baselines (36.1% system-wise vs. their own SMT-CABC at 6.0%), showing that general-purpose ABC pretraining alone is insufficient for multi-part string-quartet OMR — which also implies limited transfer for existing general OMR models when faced with an unfamiliar score layout. Fourth, the scope is fixed at string quartets (always four parts); the authors acknowledge in the conclusion that scaling to larger ensembles (e.g. orchestral scores) will require richer multi-part benchmarks, and whether the current approach extends directly to scores with eight or more instruments is unclear. Fifth, all evaluation happens on pre-segmented system- or staff-level images rather than full-page end-to-end recognition, which the paper explicitly lists as future work rather than something this study has already solved.

討論Discussion

基準測試最重要的發現是：表徵選擇比模型架構更重要。LMXE在兩個模型、兩種切割層級上都穩定拿下最低的OMR-NED，其次是字元層級的ABC（CABC）；kern系的編碼落後一截；用byte-pair encoding壓縮過的ABC反而是最差的——而且Zeus的表現隨著BPE詞彙表變大而單調惡化，顯示縮短序列長度並不能換來更好的辨識準確率。另一個發現是譜表層級的切割一律優於整個系統層級：理論上系統層級的輸入應該能利用聲部間的上下文線索（例如從其他聲部推斷拍號、從縱向對齊推斷音符時值），但作者檢查了跟這類線索直接相關的錯誤類別（音符、符頭），發現系統層級的錯誤率並沒有比譜表層級低，代表這兩個基線架構其實沒有真的利用到這些跨聲部線索——這暗示了未來要設計「真正能整合多聲部上下文」的架構，還有明顯的進步空間。最佳設定（Zeus + LMXE + 譜表層級）在合成輸入上達到3.6% OMR-NED，在掃描輸入上是5.9%，證明多聲部OMR這件事在技術上是可行的，但掃描本上仍有清楚的進步空間。整體而言，這篇論文的價值在於把「多聲部OMR」從一個沒有資料集、無從量化的空白，變成一個有明確基準數字、可以追蹤進步的研究題目，這對MIR領域的樂譜數位化與後續分析都是紮實的基礎建設。

The most important finding from the benchmark is that representation choice matters more than model architecture. LMXE consistently achieves the lowest OMR-NED across both models and both segmentation levels, followed closely by character-level ABC (CABC); kern-based encodings trail a step behind; and byte-pair-encoded ABC is the weakest of all — Zeus's performance degrades monotonically as the BPE vocabulary grows, showing that shortening sequences doesn't buy better recognition accuracy. Another finding is that staff-level segmentation beats system-level segmentation across the board: in principle, system-level input should benefit from cross-part context (inferring time signatures from concurrent voices, note durations from vertical alignment), but the authors checked error categories directly tied to such cues (note, notehead) and found system-level error rates were no lower than staff-level ones — meaning neither baseline architecture actually exploits this cross-part context, which suggests real room for future architectures explicitly designed to integrate multi-part context. The best configuration (Zeus + LMXE + staff-level) reaches 3.6% OMR-NED on synthetic input and 5.9% on scanned input, confirming multi-part OMR is technically feasible while leaving clear headroom on scanned sources. Overall, this paper's value lies in turning multi-part OMR from a blank spot with no dataset and no way to quantify progress into a research topic with concrete benchmark numbers that future work can track — solid infrastructure for score digitization and downstream analysis in MIR.

A Dual Evaluation for Music Transcription

arXiv:2608.04511 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：自動音樂轉譜系統輸出的樂譜同時要服務兩個目的：給音樂家讀的記譜、以及可以重新演奏播放的內容，但過去的評估幾乎都把這兩者混為一談。這篇論文第一次系統性地把「記譜相似度」（用光學樂譜辨識文獻裡的編輯距離）與「播放相似度」（用 14 種音訊特徵、DTW／TWED、CLaMP 3、CLEWS、甚至讓 Gemini 當裁判）分開驗證，並找 106 位真人做 3180 次成對比較當黃金標準，結果發現兩軸經常給出完全不同的系統排名——這代表過去只看單一指標的轉譜論文，排名本身可能就是誤導的。因為方法論本身的嚴謹度與對整個領域評估慣例的挑戰性，標記為深度分析。Why this one: Automatic music transcription systems output sheet music that must serve two purposes at once — readable notation for musicians, and content that can be re-played as audio — yet prior evaluation has almost always conflated the two. This paper is the first to systematically separate notation similarity (an edit-distance metric borrowed from optical music recognition) from playback similarity (validated across 14 audio features, DTW/TWED, CLaMP 3, CLEWS, and even Gemini as an audio judge), anchored by 106 human listeners making 3,180 pairwise judgments as ground truth. It finds the two axes frequently rank systems completely differently — meaning transcription papers that report only one metric may be reporting a misleading ranking. Flagged as a deep dive for its methodological rigor and its direct challenge to a field-wide evaluation convention.

動機Motivation

自動音樂轉譜（AMT）系統的輸出——可讀寫的樂譜——天生要同時服務兩種完全不同的使用情境：音樂家要拿它來讀、理解、甚至列印演奏；另一方面它也可以被重新播放，變成一段可聽的音訊。作者主張這兩個目的分別對應「記譜與參考樂譜的相似度」和「播放與原始演奏的相似度」兩種本質不同的評估,理由是：記譜精確不代表播放起來像原曲（例如演奏者的表情性節奏與原始拍點不同步）；反過來,播放聽起來很像,也可能是犧牲了記譜的可讀性或符號結構換來的。但目前 AMT 評估幾乎都是逐幀的音高-起始-結束指標,這套指標本來是為 MIDI 系統設計的,並不適合評估完整的「音訊轉樂譜」管線；既有的記譜導向指標（Cogliati、MV2H、MUSTER）要嘛沒有互斥的懲罰項、要嘛偏重樂曲結構而非視覺工程排版,而且作者發現 MUSTER 的各項子分數彼此之間的排名並不一致——有的子分數跟記譜相似度高度相關,有的卻跟播放偏好高度相關,混在一個平均分數裡反而模糊了兩者的差異。

The output of an Automatic Music Transcription (AMT) system — readable, editable sheet music — inherently serves two distinct use cases: musicians read, understand, and perform from it, and it can also be re-rendered into audio for playback. The authors argue these two purposes call for two fundamentally different evaluations: notation similarity to a reference score, and playback similarity to the original performance — because notation accuracy doesn't guarantee playback resembles the original (expressive performance timing may not align with the notated beat), and conversely, playback that sounds close to the original may have sacrificed notational readability or symbolic structure to get there. Yet current AMT evaluation is dominated by frame-level pitch-onset-offset metrics designed for MIDI-output systems, which are ill-suited to evaluating a complete audio-to-score pipeline; existing notation-focused metrics (Cogliati, MV2H, MUSTER) either lack disjoint penalty terms, emphasize musical structure over visual engraving quality, or — as the authors demonstrate for MUSTER — produce subscores that disagree with each other, with some tracking notation similarity and others tracking playback preference, a distinction a single averaged score obscures entirely.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

記譜側既有工作：Cogliati 等人用跨多個記譜維度（音符、時值、休止符、小節線、聲部分配）的編輯距離,再迴歸對齊人類評分,但沒有互斥懲罰；MV2H 對多音高、聲部分離、拍子、音值、和聲設計了互斥懲罰,但偏重樂曲結構甚於視覺可讀性,而且要先轉成 MIDI 才能比較,等於繞過了記譜本身；MUSTER 提供多個獨立錯誤率（音高、漏音、多音、起始、結束、聲部、手部分配、音階錯誤）,但官方彙總分數 MeanER 忽略了樂譜組織相關的屬性,作者實測顯示 MUSTER 子分數之間的相關性並不一致（VoiceER 跟光學樂譜辨識編輯距離的相關係數高達 0.94,但 OnsetER 跟人類播放偏好的相關係數只有 0.84,兩者衡量的其實是不同的東西）。播放側既有工作：Ycart 等人用成對聆聽測試驗證鋼琴轉譜指標,並從人類判斷訓練學習型指標,但比較的是合成 MIDI 對合成 MIDI,不是把樂譜重新演算成音訊去對照真實錄音；Simonetta 等人證明標準 MIDI 層級指標跟聽眾對「演奏詮釋是否保留」的判斷相關性很弱,這正是這篇論文動機的一部分來源。這篇論文的獨特之處在於：第一次把「用光學樂譜辨識編輯距離比較 MusicXML 樂譜」與「把樂譜重新演算成音訊再跟原始錄音比較播放相似度」完全分開驗證,並用 106 位真人、3180 次成對判斷做為黃金標準校準。

On the notation side, prior work includes Cogliati et al., who compare edit distance across multiple notation dimensions (notes, durations, rests, barlines, staff assignment) regression-mapped to human ratings but without disjoint penalties; MV2H, which enforces disjoint penalties across multi-pitch, voice separation, meter, note value, and harmony but emphasizes musical structure over visual readability and converts scores to MIDI for comparison, effectively bypassing notation itself; and MUSTER, which provides separate error rates (pitch, miss, extra, onset, offset, voice, hand, scale) but whose official aggregated MeanER omits score-organization properties — the authors show MUSTER's own subscores disagree with each other, with VoiceER correlating strongly with OMR edit distance (ρ=0.94) while OnsetER correlates with human playback preference (ρ=0.84), meaning the subscores are tracking genuinely different things. On the playback side, Ycart et al. validate piano-transcription metrics through pairwise listening tests and train learned metrics from human judgments, but compare synthesized MIDI against synthesized MIDI rather than audio rendered from a score against an actual recording; Simonetta et al. show standard MIDI-level metrics correlate weakly with listener judgments of preserved interpretation, part of the motivation for this work. This paper's distinguishing move is fully separating OMR-edit-distance comparison of MusicXML scores from audio-rendered-score-versus-original-recording playback comparison, calibrated against 106 human listeners and 3,180 pairwise judgments as ground truth.

方法Method

記譜相似度用 OMR-NED（光學樂譜辨識正規化編輯距離）：把樂譜拆成音高、升降記號、符頭、符桿、圓滑線、連結線、演奏記號、譜號、調號、拍號、力度、歌詞等符號,計算插入與刪除操作數,除以兩份樂譜符號總數正規化（取代視為一次刪除加一次插入）,數值越低代表需要人工修改的量越少。播放相似度則是一整套多方法評估：先用固定管線把候選 MusicXML 經 MuseScore 轉 MIDI、再經 FluidSynth 配 SGM 音色庫合成 44.1kHz 音訊並做峰值正規化,確保渲染步驟本身不含可學習成分、不會偷偷影響評估。接著在 14 種音框級音訊特徵（Chroma、HPCP、Chroma CENS、Tonnetz、CQT、STFT 半音、音高顯著度、Mel 頻譜、MFCC、PCEN-Mel、Log-STFT、頻譜通量、起始強度、節奏圖）上,分別測試 DTW（用餘弦距離逐幀比對,路徑長度正規化）與 TWED（額外用 ν 控制時間彈性懲罰、λ 控制編輯懲罰,測了四組參數）；同時測了三種模型／嵌入式評估：讓 Gemini（2.0 到 3.1 版）以 ABX 格式直接聽兩個候選片段判斷哪個更像參考錄音（並用雙向評估校正約 59% 的「偏好選第一個」位置偏誤）、CLaMP 3 多模態嵌入的音訊-音訊／音訊-symbolic 跨模態相似度、以及 CLEWS 學習型音訊嵌入評估器（測了距離、相似度、全域餘弦、片段中位數等聚合方式,最後選片段平均相似度）。人類黃金標準則用參考錨定的二選一強迫選擇（ABX）,106 位參與者各做 30 次試驗,共 3180 次有效判斷,用 Bradley–Terry 模型從成對勝負估計每個系統的潛在偏好分數。評測資料是從 ATEPP 抽出的鋼琴演奏錄音,230 段錄音涵蓋 23 首作品、30 位演奏者、6 位作曲家（貝多芬、莫札特、蕭邦、巴哈、布拉姆斯、李斯特）,每段截到三分鐘。系統組合方面,把 8 個音訊轉 MIDI 模型（MT3、ISMIR 2021、ISMIR 2022 small/base、Basic Pitch、位元組跳動鋼琴模型、Transkun、Aria-AMT）分別接上 3 個 MIDI 轉樂譜轉換器（music21、MuseScore、MIDI2ScoreTransformer）,組成 24 套模組化管線,並額外納入論文完成後才發布的端到端系統 Rubato 作個案研究。

Notation similarity is measured with OMR-NED (OMR Normalized Edit Distance): a score is decomposed into pitch, accidentals, note heads, beams, ties, slurs, articulations, clefs, key/time signatures, dynamics, and lyrics as discrete symbols; insertions and deletions between candidate and reference are counted and normalized by total symbol count (a substitution counts as one deletion plus one insertion), with lower values meaning less manual correction needed. Playback similarity is a full multi-method evaluation: candidate MusicXML is rendered through a fixed pipeline — MuseScore to MIDI, then FluidSynth with an SGM soundfont to 44.1kHz audio, peak-normalized — ensuring the rendering step itself contains no learned component that could quietly bias evaluation. Fourteen frame-level audio features (Chroma, HPCP, Chroma CENS, Tonnetz, CQT, semitone-binned STFT, pitch salience, mel spectrogram, MFCC, PCEN-Mel, log-STFT, spectral flux, onset strength, tempogram) are each tested with DTW (frame-wise cosine-distance alignment, path-length normalized) and TWED (adding a ν parameter for temporal-gap penalty and λ for edit penalty, four configurations tested); three model/embedding-based evaluators are also tested: Gemini (versions 2.0 through 3.1) judging pairwise ABX comparisons by ear to pick which candidate better matches a reference recording (bidirectionally evaluated to correct a roughly 59% first-candidate position bias), CLaMP 3's multimodal audio-audio and audio-symbolic cross-modal similarity, and CLEWS, a learned audio-embedding evaluator (distance, similarity, global-cosine, and segment-median aggregations were tested, with segment-mean similarity selected as best). The human gold standard uses reference-anchored two-alternative forced choice (ABX): 106 participants each completed 30 trials, yielding 3,180 valid judgments, aggregated via a Bradley–Terry model that estimates each system's latent preference score from pairwise wins. The evaluation set draws piano performance recordings from ATEPP — 230 recordings spanning 23 works, 30 performers, and 6 composers (Beethoven, Mozart, Chopin, Bach, Brahms, Liszt), each capped at three minutes. On the system side, 8 audio-to-MIDI models (MT3, ISMIR 2021, ISMIR 2022 small/base, Basic Pitch, ByteDance's piano model, Transkun, Aria-AMT) are each paired with 3 MIDI-to-score converters (music21, MuseScore, MIDI2ScoreTransformer) to form 24 modular pipelines, with the end-to-end system Rubato — released after this study — added as a separate case study.

限制Limitations

作者明確列出範圍限制：整套研究只涵蓋西方古典鋼琴演奏、且只評估每段錄音的前三分鐘，結論不一定能推廣到其他樂器、合奏、人聲、非古典曲風或更長的樂曲結構。播放側結果依賴固定的渲染管線,雖然所有系統都用同一套渲染流程確保公平,但渲染器、音色庫與正規化方式本身可能同時影響自動指標與真人聽感的判斷方向。OMR-NED 只衡量符號層級的相似度,並沒有驗證「記譜品質」本身是否符合人類讀譜與編輯的實際感受——換句話說,兩份符號距離相近的樂譜,人類讀起來的難易度不一定相同。個案研究的端到端系統 Rubato 是在人類聆聽實驗結束後才發布的,所以它的播放品質完全沒有真人判斷可以佐證,目前只能用自動指標推測。作者也坦言未來需要加入樂譜可讀性的真人視覺評估,並測試這套指標選擇方法在不同曲目、不同參與者、不同系統組合下是否依然穩定。

The authors explicitly scope their limitations: the study covers only Western classical piano performance and evaluates only the first three minutes of each recording, so conclusions may not transfer to other instruments, ensembles, voice, non-classical genres, or longer musical structures. Playback-side results depend on a fixed rendering pipeline; although every system is rendered identically for fairness, the renderer, soundfont, and normalization choices could themselves influence both automatic metrics and listener judgments in the same direction. OMR-NED measures only symbol-level similarity and does not validate whether "notation quality" as measured actually matches human readability or editing effort — two scores with similar symbol-level distance to a reference need not be equally easy for a human to read. The end-to-end case-study system, Rubato, was released after the human listening study concluded, so its playback quality has no human judgment to corroborate it and can currently only be inferred from automatic metrics. The authors acknowledge that future work should add human visual evaluation of notation readability and test whether this metric-selection methodology remains stable across held-out pieces, participants, and system combinations.

討論Discussion

核心發現用一句話講完就是：記譜側與播放側常常青睞不同的轉換器家族,系統排名不是單一維度的事。具體來說,MIDI2ScoreTransformer 這個轉換器在記譜相似度上系統性最強（OMR-NED 落在 86 到 90 之間）,MuseScore 在播放相似度上最強（CLEWS 超過 0.82）,而 music21 在兩個維度都表現平庸；更有意思的是,當轉換器本身較弱（music21）時,上游用哪個音訊轉 MIDI 模型幾乎不影響結果（八個模型全部擠在 OMR-NED 96.1 到 96.9 的窄範圍內）,但換成強的轉換器（MuseScore 或 MIDI2ScoreTransformer）,上游模型的選擇會讓分數相差超過 5 分——這代表「評估轉譜系統」如果只看整條管線的單一分數,很可能把差異歸因錯了環節。人類驗證的結果同樣值得注意：在測試的所有自動指標裡,CLEWS 片段平均相似度（ρ=0.971）與 Gemini 3.1 Pro 當裁判（ρ=0.970）跟人類判斷相關性最高,幾乎打平,但 CLEWS 的每千次評估成本只要 0.37 美元,Gemini 裁判要 5.10 美元,人類本身則要 333.33 美元——這給出一個非常直接的實務建議：播放相似度評估首選 CLEWS,不建議用昂貴又跟模型版本綁定的語言模型當裁判。端到端系統 Rubato 的個案研究進一步印證了論文的核心論點：它在記譜相似度上是全部 25 套系統裡最好的（OMR-NED=72.30）,播放相似度也有競爭力但非最佳（CLEWS=0.749,排名第 6）,同時贏過 24 套模組化管線中的 19 套（兩個維度合計）,顯示端到端設計確實有機會繞開模組化管線「記譜好就播放差、播放好就記譜差」的結構性折衷——但它的播放品質目前完全沒有真人聽感驗證,這個開放問題也呼應了論文一開始就強調的立場：轉譜評估不能只看一個數字,而且新系統發布得比評估基礎設施還快的落差,本身就是這個領域需要正視的問題。

The core finding in one sentence: notation-side and playback-side evaluation frequently favor different converter families, so system ranking is not a single-dimensional question. Concretely, MIDI2ScoreTransformer is systematically strongest on notation similarity (OMR-NED in the 86–90 range), MuseScore is strongest on playback similarity (CLEWS above 0.82), and music21 is mediocre on both; more interesting still, when the downstream converter is weak (music21), the choice of upstream audio-to-MIDI model barely matters — all eight models cluster in a narrow OMR-NED range of 96.1–96.9 — but with a strong converter (MuseScore or MIDI2ScoreTransformer), the upstream model choice spreads scores by more than 5 points, meaning that evaluating a transcription system by a single end-to-end score risks attributing differences to the wrong stage of the pipeline. The human-validation results are equally noteworthy: among all automatic metrics tested, CLEWS segment-mean similarity (ρ=0.971) and Gemini 3.1 Pro as judge (ρ=0.970) correlate best with human judgment, essentially tied — but CLEWS costs $0.37 per 1,000 evaluations versus $5.10 for the Gemini judge and $333.33 for humans, giving a direct practical recommendation: default to CLEWS for playback evaluation rather than an expensive, model-version-dependent LLM judge. The end-to-end system Rubato's case study further supports the paper's central argument: it is the best of all 25 systems on notation similarity (OMR-NED=72.30), competitive but not best on playback similarity (CLEWS=0.749, ranked 6th), and beats 19 of 24 modular pipelines combined across both dimensions — evidence that end-to-end design can genuinely sidestep the structural notation-versus-playback tradeoff inherent to modular pipelines. But its playback quality currently has zero human-listening validation, an open question that echoes the paper's opening argument: transcription evaluation cannot rest on a single number, and the fact that new systems are now being released faster than the evaluation infrastructure to properly assess them is itself a problem the field needs to reckon with.

動機Motivation

把一段錄音變成音樂家能直接看懂、演奏的樂譜（Audio-to-Score, A2S），跟一般說的自動音樂轉錄（AMT）不完全一樣：AMT 通常只求輸出 MIDI 或 piano-roll 這種中介表示，A2S 則要求輸出符合記譜規範的樂譜，需要處理調號、和弦標記、節奏量化等更貼近人類閱讀習慣的資訊。作者指出這個領域被兩個問題卡住：一是配對的音訊-樂譜資料稀缺，過去幾乎所有 A2S 資料集都侷限在古典音樂，而且音訊多半是合成音（MIDI 渲染出來的），不是真實錄音；二是儘管預訓練模型與資料增強已經在 AMT 領域證明有效，A2S 研究卻很少系統性採用這些技巧。流行音樂本身結構更多樣（主旋律加和弦的 lead sheet 形式很常見，來源多是社群手抄的樂譜網站），如果能把 A2S 擴展到流行音樂並用上真實錄音，會是這個領域第一次真正貼近實際使用場景（例如自動生成一首歌的主旋律和弦譜）。

Converting a recording into a score a musician can read and play (Audio-to-Score, A2S) is not quite the same task as standard Automatic Music Transcription (AMT): AMT typically targets an intermediate representation like MIDI or piano-roll, while A2S must produce notation-compliant output — key signatures, chord labels, rhythmic quantization — matching how humans actually read scores. The authors identify two problems holding the field back. First, paired audio-score data is scarce, and nearly all prior A2S datasets are confined to classical music with synthetic (MIDI-rendered) audio rather than real recordings. Second, even though pretrained models and data augmentation have proven effective in AMT, A2S research has rarely adopted them systematically. Popular music has more structural variety — lead-sheet-style melody-plus-chords notation is common, often sourced from community-transcribed sheet-music sites — so extending A2S to popular music with real audio would bring the field, for the first time, close to an actual use case: automatically generating a lead sheet for a song.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

過去的 A2S 系統分兩派：模組化方法先做 AMT 得到 MIDI，再用規則或另一個模型把 MIDI 轉成樂譜；端到端方法直接用 CNN 加 RNN 把音訊映射到符號序列。但現有資料集幾乎全部侷限古典音樂並使用合成音訊，作者認為這既限制了任務難度的代表性（合成音訊沒有真實錄音的噪音、混音、人聲等複雜度），也限制了模型的泛化能力。同時，AMT 領域近年已經證明「用預訓練音訊特徵取代從頭訓練的 CNN 編碼器」與「資料增強（變調、變速）」都能明顯提升效果，但 A2S 的既有工作大多還停留在從零訓練的小模型。這篇論文就是把這兩條線接起來：一方面提供真實錄音、跨曲風的新資料集，另一方面把 AMT 領域已驗證有效的技巧系統性搬進 A2S。

Prior A2S systems split into two camps: modular pipelines that first run AMT to obtain MIDI, then convert MIDI to score via rules or a second model; and end-to-end approaches that map audio directly to symbol sequences via CNN-RNN architectures. Existing datasets, however, are almost entirely confined to classical music with synthetic audio, which the authors argue both understates task difficulty (synthetic audio lacks the noise, mixing, and vocal complexity of real recordings) and limits model generalization. Meanwhile, AMT research has already shown that replacing from-scratch CNN encoders with pretrained audio features, and adding data augmentation (pitch/tempo shifting), both meaningfully improve results — yet prior A2S work has largely stuck with small, from-scratch-trained models. This paper connects the two threads: providing a new dataset of real, cross-genre recordings, and systematically porting AMT's proven techniques into A2S.

方法Method

資料集建構分三階段。第一階段抓取：從 26175 次嘗試中成功下載 20072 段 YouTube 音訊，配上原始 SheetSage 標註提供的節拍-時間對齊資訊。第二階段是最棘手的「八度推斷」：原始標註只有相對音高、沒有絕對八度，作者用 Mel-band Roformer 抽出人聲幹軌，再用 RMVPE 做音高估計得到 72 個半音間距的機率箱，接著窮舉 12 種假設（6 種八度位移 × 2 種起始時間偏移），挑信心最高且超過 0.2 門檻的組合，最終得到 9468 個八度可信的片段（98.66% 落在第 3、4 八度，符合流行音樂主旋律的常見音域）。第三階段是把 JSON 標註轉成 kern：包含依調號推斷正確的音名拼法、用 Harte 語法標和弦、時間對齊、節奏量化，再合併主旋律譜線與和弦譜線。模型架構方面，基線是 CNN 編碼器加 Transformer 解碼器，作者做了三處改動：把解碼器前饋層維度從 256 擴到 1024 並改用 pre-norm（訓練更穩定）；把 CNN 換成凍結的 MuQ（自監督音樂特徵抽取器，每秒輸出 25 個特徵向量，比原本的 5.38 個密得多，再用線性投影加 LayerNorm 把維度從 1024 壓到 256，並改用一維位置編碼）；以及做離線資料增強（變調 ±1~3 半音、變速 0.9~1.1 倍，每個訓練樣本產生 6 個增強版本，資料量擴大 7 倍）。評估指標用基於 Levenshtein 距離的符號錯誤率（SER），推論階段用貪婪解碼。

Dataset construction has three stages. Acquisition: 20,072 YouTube audio clips were successfully downloaded out of 26,175 attempts, paired with beat-to-time alignment from the original SheetSage annotations. The hardest stage, octave inference, addresses that the original annotations carry relative pitch only, with no absolute octave: the authors extract a vocal stem via Mel-band Roformer, run RMVPE pitch estimation to get 72 semitone-spaced probability bins, then exhaustively test 12 hypotheses (6 octave shifts × 2 start-time offsets), keeping the highest-confidence combination above a 0.2 threshold — yielding 9,468 clips with confident octave assignment (98.66% falling in octaves 3–4, consistent with typical pop-melody range). The final stage converts JSON annotations to kern: inferring correct note spelling from the key signature, labeling chords with Harte syntax, aligning timing, quantizing rhythm, and merging melody and chord spines. On the model side, the baseline is a CNN encoder with a Transformer decoder; the authors make three changes: expanding the decoder feedforward dimension from 256 to 1024 with pre-norm (more stable training); replacing the CNN with a frozen MuQ self-supervised music feature extractor (25 feature vectors/second versus the baseline's 5.38, projected down from 1024 to 256 dimensions via a linear layer plus LayerNorm, with 1D rather than 2D positional encoding); and offline data augmentation (pitch shifts of ±1–3 semitones, tempo scaling of 0.9–1.1×, generating 6 augmented variants per example for a 7× data expansion). Evaluation uses Levenshtein-distance-based Symbol Error Rate (SER) with greedy decoding at inference.

限制Limitations

作者誠實列出了資料本身的雜訊來源：SheetSage-A2S 的原始標註來自社群手抄樂譜，和弦標記的細緻程度不一致（有人每次和聲變化才標一次，有人每個節奏重音或每個小節都標一次），主旋律的音節、滑音、裝飾音處理也不統一，還有部分片段標註提早結束、或人聲靜默時卻標了伴奏音，這些都會人為拉高在這個資料集上量出來的錯誤率，代表 20.92% SER 這個數字本身帶有資料品質的噪音，不能直接等同於模型能力的天花板。模型本身則在複雜和弦（七和弦、擴展和聲）上表現差、容易混淆和弦轉位，最大弱點是音符時長判斷：容易把連續音符合併或把單一音符切開，尤其在饒舌等重複音高密集的段落特別明顯，另外偶有不超過八分音符的細微時間偏差。整體而言，這篇論文承認自己是在一個標註品質參差不齊的新資料集上打地基，後續需要專門處理標註不一致與建立更細緻的評估指標。

The authors are candid about noise inherent to the data itself: SheetSage-A2S annotations come from community-transcribed sheet music, and chord-labeling granularity is inconsistent (some annotators mark chords only at harmony changes, others at every rhythmic strum or every measure); melodic treatment of syllables, pitch bends, and grace notes is likewise inconsistent; and some clips have annotations that end early or label accompaniment during vocal silence. All of this artificially inflates the measured error rate on this dataset, meaning the 20.92% SER figure carries data-quality noise and shouldn't be read as a hard ceiling on model capability. The model itself struggles with complex chords (7ths, extended harmonies), confuses chord inversions, and its biggest weakness is note-duration prediction — merging consecutive notes or splitting a single note, especially in pitch-repetitive passages like rap verses — plus occasional minor timing deviations rarely exceeding an eighth note. Overall, the paper is upfront that it's building on a new dataset with uneven annotation quality, and follow-up work needs to tackle annotation inconsistency and finer-grained evaluation metrics directly.

討論Discussion

三項貢獻疊加出的結果相當扎實：第一個真實錄音的流行音樂 A2S 資料集、驗證了 MuQ 這類自監督音樂特徵在轉錄任務上遠優於從零訓練的 CNN（在新資料集上帶來最大單項提升，相對誤差降低 53%）、以及資料增強帶來的最後一段提升，三者疊加把古典音樂基準的 SER 從既有最佳的 15.3% 打到 4.98%（相對誤差降低 67.5%），流行音樂基準則建立在 20.92% SER。值得注意的是消融實驗顯示的優先順序：架構調整（pre-norm、擴大前饋層）本身就貢獻了古典音樂上 44% 的誤差降低，MuQ 帶來最大的單項跳躍，資料增強是錦上添花——這個順序對之後想在類似任務上省算力的研究者是有用的參考。作者也誠實地把「哪些錯誤是模型的鍋、哪些是資料標註不一致造成的」分開講清楚，沒有把數字灌水成模型能力的全部功勞，這種對資料侷限的坦白在轉錄類論文裡不算常見，也是這篇值得肯定的地方。後續方向包括擴展到樂器演奏的旋律（不只是人聲主旋律）、修正標註不一致、以及設計比 SER 更貼近音樂可讀性的評估指標。

The three stacked contributions add up to something solid: the first real-recording popular-music A2S dataset, evidence that self-supervised music features like MuQ substantially outperform a from-scratch-trained CNN for transcription (the single largest gain on the new dataset, a 53% relative error reduction), and a final increment from data augmentation — together driving the classical-music benchmark from the prior best of 15.3% SER down to 4.98% (a 67.5% relative reduction), while establishing a 20.92% SER benchmark for popular music. The ablation ordering is worth noting: architectural changes alone (pre-norm, wider feedforward) already account for a 44% error reduction on classical music, MuQ delivers the single biggest jump, and augmentation is the final polish — a useful prioritization for anyone trying to conserve compute on a similar task. The authors are also unusually candid about separating which errors are the model's fault versus artifacts of inconsistent annotation, rather than inflating the numbers as pure model capability — a transparency about data limitations that's not that common in transcription papers, and worth crediting. Future directions include extending to instrument-performed melodies (not just vocal lines), resolving annotation inconsistencies, and designing evaluation metrics closer to musical readability than raw SER.

Masked diffusion enables coherent beat tracking

遮罩擴散讓節拍追蹤產生前後一致的輸出

Francesco Foscarin, Filip Korzeniowski, Richard Vogl
arXiv:2608.04624 · cs.SD; cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-08-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把「模型為什麼會吐出自相矛盾的節拍」這個老問題重新診斷成機率建模的缺陷，而不是後處理不夠強，然後用遮罩擴散給出對症的解法。三個為節拍任務量身改造的設計細節都寫得很誠實，包含哪些嘗試失敗了。Why this one: It re-diagnoses a long-standing failure of beat trackers as a probabilistic modelling defect rather than a post-processing shortfall, then answers it with masked diffusion. The three task-specific modifications are reported honestly, including the variants that did not work.

動機Motivation

神經網路節拍追蹤器會產生「不合法」的輸出：連續兩個小節線落在相鄰拍上、速度突然倍增或減半——即使訓練資料裡從來沒有這種東西。長期以來業界的處理方式是套一層動態貝氏網路（DBN）後處理，用「每小節固定幾拍」「速度變化有限」這類硬性約束把輸出掰回合理範圍。

這篇的出發點是：DBN 治的是症狀，不是病因，而且病因從來沒被講清楚過。作者提出的假設是——問題出在單步前饋這個範式本身隱含了「給定輸入，只有唯一一個正確輸出」的假設。但節拍本來就有歧義：同一首曲子，不同聽者可能採用不同的節律層級（metrical level），數成 4/4 或數成 2/2 都說得通，而且都是風格上合理的解讀。當模型面對這種曲子時，它不是選一個解讀，而是把幾個互相競爭的解讀混在一起輸出，於是產生了任何單一詮釋下都不成立的東西。

這個診斷有個直接的推論：既然病因是「沒有建模多個合法輸出」，那真正的解法就該是一個能表達多重詮釋、並在推論時一致地選定其中一個的模型，而不是在事後用規則把混合物擦乾淨。

Neural beat trackers emit invalid outputs: consecutive downbeats on adjacent beat positions, abrupt tempo doubling or halving — none of which appear in the training data. The standard remedy has been a Dynamic Bayesian Network (DBN) post-processor that forces the output back into shape with hard constraints such as a fixed number of beats per bar and bounded tempo variation.

The paper's starting point is that the DBN treats symptoms, and that the underlying cause has never been articulated. Their hypothesis is that the fault lies in the single-forward-pass paradigm itself, which implicitly assumes exactly one valid output per input. Beat tracking violates that assumption: listeners may legitimately adopt different metrical levels for the same piece, and alternative beat placements are stylistically plausible. Faced with such a piece, the network does not choose an interpretation — it emits a mixture of competing ones, producing something that is coherent under none of them.

The diagnosis carries a direct implication. If the cause is a failure to model multiple valid outputs, the fix should be a model that represents several interpretations and commits to one consistently at inference, not a rule-based cleanup of the mixture after the fact.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

節拍與小節線追蹤已經被研究了數十年，近年的工作在架構、損失函數、資料增強、預訓練與微調上都有大量嘗試，但幾乎全部共用同一個推論範式：對每個時間框，單次前饋預測一個節拍活化值。

作者盤點了「讓模型考慮多種輸出」的既有路線與各自的代價。自迴歸模型天然能建模整段輸出的聯合分布，但實作上都有麻煩：逐框自迴歸解碼需要每框一次前饋，30 秒 50 fps 就是 1500 次，推論慢到不可用；改成 [節拍類型, 節拍時間] 的緊湊 token 化雖然避開這點，但絕對時間編碼需要大量時間 token 且無法處理長片段，相對時間編碼則在生成過程中會累積誤差。

先前工作裡只有 Gagneré 等人正面處理過多重合法輸出的問題，他們用多個預測頭去捕捉不同的階層層級——但那只用在自監督預訓練階段，微調與推論時仍然回到單頭架構，所以最終輸出照樣有這篇要解的毛病。

這篇的基底是 Beat This（Foscarin 等人），一個權重、訓練程式與資料全部開源的模型。作者沿用它的資料、增強方法、評估指標與大部分超參數，只把架構與損失改造成能配合遮罩擴散。遮罩擴散這邊則主要參照 LLaDA，理由是它在訓練與推論都用線性遮罩，實作最單純。

Beat and downbeat tracking has been studied for decades, with recent work exploring architectures, losses, augmentation, pre-training and fine-tuning — but nearly all of it shares one inference paradigm: a single forward pass predicting a beat activation per time frame.

The authors survey the existing routes to modelling multiple outputs and the cost of each. Autoregressive models capture the joint distribution over the full output sequence, but every implementation has a drawback. Frame-wise autoregressive decoding needs one pass per frame — 1500 passes for 30 seconds at 50 fps — which is prohibitively slow. Compact [beat-type, beat-time] tokenisation avoids that, but absolute-time encoding needs many time tokens and breaks down on longer excerpts, while relative-time encoding accumulates error during generation.

Among prior work, only Gagneré et al. confront the multiple-valid-output problem directly, using multiple heads to capture different hierarchical levels. That idea is confined to their self-supervised pre-training stage; fine-tuning and inference revert to a single head, so their predictions still exhibit the pathology this paper targets.

The base here is Beat This (Foscarin et al.), whose weights, training code and data are fully open. The authors reuse its data, augmentation, metrics and most hyperparameters, changing only what masked diffusion requires. For the diffusion side they follow LLaDA, chosen because its linear masking in both training and inference makes for the simplest implementation.

方法Method

核心設定是遮罩擴散模型（MDM）：把節拍序列 y 視為要被逐步遮罩與還原的對象，而音訊 x 在整個前向與反向過程中始終完全可見，也就是建模條件分布而非邊際分布。推論從全遮罩序列開始，每一步做一次前饋、揭露一部分框，直到整段填滿。這樣模型可以在早期步驟先選定一個合法的詮釋，再前後一致地把剩下的填完。

作者刻意把架構改動壓到最小，好繼續沿用既有的研究成果。改動有三處：加入節拍與小節線兩張可學習的 embedding 表，詞彙為 {MASK, NO_EVENT, EVENT, PAD}，在 6 層 RoFormer 之前才加入（前端只處理音訊），並乘上 √d 放大權重，這個簡單做法在初步實驗裡就有效；把原本的 sum head 換成兩個獨立線性頭，因為 sum head 會在小節線 logit 上製造反向尖峰，單步預測時無害，但這裡遮罩排程要靠 logit 信心度決定揭露順序，被污染的信心度會出問題；把 FFN 的 GELU 換成 SwiGLU，這不是必要的，只是讓設計向現代網路對齊並順帶改善結果。

真正把通用 MDM 變成能用的節拍追蹤器的，是三個非顯而易見的設計決定：

第一，節拍與小節線獨立遮罩。訓練時兩個通道各自抽自己的遮罩比例與遮罩，所以某一步可能節拍遮 30%、小節線遮 80%。這是推論時基於信心度的揭露策略能正確運作的前提——因為兩個通道的信心度必須各自校準到自己的稀疏度。作者試過各種在揭露前先合併兩通道信心度的做法，也試過先揭節拍再揭小節線或反過來，全都讓效能下降。理由是有些曲子節拍比小節線好抓（節奏穩定但拍號會變），有些反過來（縹緲的音樂但和聲在小節線上變化清楚），而且小節線大約比節拍稀疏四倍，logit 量級本來就不在同一個尺度上。

第二，平衡揭露排程。負 logit（非事件框）天生比正 logit 更有信心，所以純粹按信心度排序會先把非事件全部揭完，造成訓練與推論的不匹配——訓練時模型看到的永遠是事件與非事件混合的脈絡。在這個設定下做迭代推論反而比單步更差。解法是每一步先計算當前遮罩框中預測的正負比例，再按這個比例把揭露預算分配給正例與負例。

第三，步間峰值挑選。逐框網路傾向在每個真實節拍附近產生跨數框的活化叢集。原本 Beat This 是在最後做一次 7 框滑動窗的非極大值抑制，但在迭代推論裡，相鄰的峰會被揭露成相鄰的 EVENT token——這種情況訓練資料裡從未出現過（每個事件都是單一脈衝）。作者的做法是每一步推論後就做一次峰值挑選：只要有新的 EVENT 被揭露，鄰近窗內的所有框也一併被揭露並強制設為 NO_EVENT。同時順手加上一條音樂上必然成立的規則：揭露一個小節線 EVENT 時，同位置也揭露一個節拍 EVENT。作者特別指出，這組規則遠比 DBN 少，而且對所有音樂都普遍成立。

最後還加了模型集成：對多個模型的輸出 logit 取平均後再算信心度。神經網路本來就不擅長估計機率、傾向過度自信，平均能得到較可靠的信心度。這在節拍追蹤是成熟技術，但在 MDM 文獻裡不常見，因為大模型推論成本太高——25M 參數的模型則可以接受。

The core setup is a Masked Diffusion Model. The beat sequence y is what gets progressively masked and recovered, while the audio x stays fully visible throughout the forward and reverse processes — the model learns a conditional distribution, not a marginal one. Inference starts from a fully masked sequence and iteratively reveals frames over a handful of forward passes, so the model can commit to a valid interpretation in the early steps and then fill in the rest coherently.

Architectural changes are deliberately minimal, so prior work on architectures, losses and post-processing still applies. There are three. Two learnable embedding tables (beats, downbeats) over the vocabulary {MASK, NO_EVENT, EVENT, PAD} are added just before the 6-layer RoFormer — the frontend handles audio only — and scaled by √d, a simple trick that raises the weight of the beat input and proved effective in preliminary experiments. The original sum head is replaced by two independent linear heads, because the sum head sometimes produces inverted spikes in the downbeat logits; harmless for one-step prediction, but here the masking scheduler relies on logit confidence to order the reveals, and contaminated confidences break it. GELU in the FFN is swapped for SwiGLU — not required for the method, just alignment with modern practice that also helps results.

What actually turns a generic MDM into a working beat tracker is three non-obvious choices:

Independent masking of the two channels. During training each channel draws its own masking ratio and its own mask, so a step may show 30% of beat frames masked and 80% of downbeat frames masked. This is the precondition for confidence-based unmasking to work per channel at inference. The authors tried aggregating confidences across channels before deciding what to reveal, and tried revealing beats first then downbeats and vice versa; all of it lowered performance. Their explanation is that some pieces make beats easier than downbeats (stable pulse, changing time signature) and others the reverse (ethereal music with clear harmonic change at downbeats), and that downbeats are roughly four times sparser, putting the two channels' logit magnitudes on different scales.

A balanced unmasking schedule. Negative logits — non-event frames — are systematically more confident than positive ones, so pure confidence ordering reveals all the non-events first. That creates a train/inference mismatch, since during training the model always sees a balanced mix, and iterative inference in this regime performs worse than a single step. The fix computes the current positive-to-negative ratio among the masked predictions at each step and splits the unmasking budget accordingly.

Peak picking between steps. Frame-wise networks spread activation across several frames near each true beat. Beat This suppresses non-maxima within a 7-frame window once, at the end. Under iterative inference, adjacent peaks become adjacent EVENT tokens — a configuration that never occurs in training, where every event is a single impulse. So peak picking is applied after every inference step: whenever an EVENT is revealed, all frames in its proximity window are also revealed and forced to NO_EVENT. The same hook enforces one musically universal rule: revealing a downbeat EVENT also reveals a beat EVENT at that position. The authors are explicit that this is a far smaller rule set than the DBN's, and one that holds across all music.

Finally, model ensembling: output logits are averaged across models before confidence is computed. Networks estimate probabilities poorly and tend to be overconfident, and averaging yields a more reliable confidence signal. Ensembling is well established in beat tracking but rare in the MDM literature because of inference cost at scale — at 25M parameters the authors find it acceptable.

限制Limitations

作者自己列出的代價很清楚：訓練時間加倍（150 epoch 增為 300），推論時間大約是單步的數倍——雖然他們指出前端只需算一次，還有優化空間。模型集成則讓這兩項成本再乘上模型數量。

更根本的一個限制被作者誠實地點出來：他們讓模型能在多個合法詮釋中消歧義了，但評估指標仍然假設只有一個正確答案。AMLt 部分處理了這件事（容許二分之一與二倍速），但預設設定不含三分法細分，也無法處理複雜拍號、拍號變化與本質上模稜兩可的節拍位置。換句話說，這篇最想證明的那個能力，現有指標其實測不太到。

實驗設計上也有幾點值得留意。測試集是 GTZAN，而 GTZAN 以 3 拍與 4 拍曲子為主，只有少數拍號變化——這正好是 DBN 假設成立得最好的場景，所以「不用 DBN 也能打平」這個結論在更多樣的音樂上會如何，是外推而非實測。與 SOTA 系統的比較也不是嚴格的對照：Gagneré 等人的程式與模型未公開，無法做更深入的評估或統計顯著性檢定；Ru 等人微調的是參數量十倍以上的 MusicFM。作者自己也說直接比較不是目的，只是提供脈絡。

此外，訓練超參數有一部分是被硬體逼出來的：因為 GPU 限制只能用 fp16 而非 bf16 混合精度，所以必須調低學習率、加大 weight decay、加梯度裁剪，並把遮罩比例下限 ε 設得比語言 MDM 高得多（後者約 0.001）。這些不是方法本身的需求，但確實混在結果裡。

The costs the authors themselves list are clear: training time doubles (150 epochs to 300) and inference is several times slower than a single pass, though they note the frontend need only be computed once, leaving room for optimisation. Ensembling multiplies both by the number of models.

A deeper limitation is stated honestly: they have given the model the ability to disambiguate among valid interpretations, but the metrics still assume exactly one is correct. AMLt partially accommodates this by allowing half and double tempi, but its default configuration excludes ternary subdivisions and cannot handle complex time signatures, time-signature changes, or genuinely ambiguous beat positions. The capability the paper most wants to demonstrate is the one current metrics measure least well.

Several aspects of the experimental design deserve attention. The test set is GTZAN, which is dominated by pieces in 3 and 4 beats per bar with few time-signature changes — precisely the regime where the DBN's assumptions hold best. So the claim that a DBN-free system can match DBN-based ones on more diverse music is an extrapolation, not a measurement. The SOTA comparison is not a controlled one either: Gagneré et al.'s code and models are not public, precluding deeper evaluation or significance testing, and Ru et al. fine-tune MusicFM, a model more than ten times larger. The authors say directly that head-to-head comparison is not their objective and the numbers are context.

Some hyperparameters are also forced by hardware rather than by the method: GPU constraints meant fp16 rather than bf16 mixed precision, which required a lower learning rate, higher weight decay, gradient clipping, and a masking-ratio floor ε far above what language MDMs use (around 0.001). These are not requirements of the approach, but they are mixed into the results.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的地方不在數字，而在它示範了一種診斷方式：當模型持續產生訓練資料裡不存在的錯誤時，先問這是不是機率建模層次的問題，而不是急著加後處理。作者提出的證據鏈很有說服力——最引人注意的是，即使只用「單步」推論，MDM 訓練目標也已經勝過非擴散版本。這代表好處不只來自迭代推論，也來自訓練目標本身；作者的假設是部分遮罩的脈絡減少了「訓練混淆」，也就是當網路看到節律層級互相衝突的相似曲目時不會被逼著平均掉。

連貫性的量化結果也具體：8 步推論下，每首曲子的連續小節線平均從 0.25 降到 0.02，速度倍增／減半事件從 0.75 降到 0.119（20 步時再降到 0.06）。作為對照，GTZAN 標註本身是 0 與 0.033。這是直接對著病因量測，而不是只看整體 F1。

更值得注意的是這個架構順帶打開的應用面。模型接受部分輸出作為輸入，這是單步系統做不到的：使用者可以先手動標定幾個節拍位置，讓模型把其餘補完，對困難曲目的標註工作是實質的加速。同樣的機制不需任何訓練修改就支援自迴歸與區塊自迴歸推論——把前一片段輸出的尾端當成永遠不遮罩的條件，就能讓長曲子在片段之間保持一致，而不是像現在這樣各段獨立處理。

作者也指出這個思路不限於節拍：和弦辨識、結構分段同樣有「多個合法輸出」的性質，可能適用同一套處理。這個推論合理，但也提醒我們這篇的貢獻主要是方法論上的可移植性，而非節拍追蹤這個任務本身的突破。

The paper's most valuable contribution is not a number but a way of diagnosing: when a model persistently produces errors absent from its training data, ask first whether the fault is at the level of probabilistic modelling rather than reaching for more post-processing. The evidence is persuasive, and the striking part is that the MDM training objective beats the non-diffusion version even at a single inference step. The benefit therefore is not only from iterative inference but from the objective itself; the authors hypothesise that partially masked context reduces training "confusion" when the network sees similar pieces annotated at conflicting metrical levels.

The coherence measurements are concrete. At 8 inference steps, consecutive downbeats per track drop from 0.25 to 0.02 and tempo doubling/halving events from 0.75 to 0.119 (0.06 at 20 steps), against 0 and 0.033 in the GTZAN annotations themselves. This measures the diagnosed cause directly rather than reading it off an aggregate F1.

More notable still is what the architecture opens up. The model accepts partial outputs as input, which one-step systems cannot: a user can fix a handful of beat positions and have the model complete the rest, a real speedup for annotating difficult pieces. The same mechanism supports autoregressive and block-autoregressive inference with no training changes — conditioning on the tail of the previous snippet's output, always unmasked, would keep long pieces consistent across snippets instead of processing each independently.

The authors note the idea is not specific to beats: chord recognition and structure segmentation share the multiple-valid-output property and may admit the same treatment. That is a reasonable extrapolation, and also a reminder that the contribution here is mainly methodological portability rather than a breakthrough in beat tracking as such.

Dense-Sparse Dynamic Time Warping for Customizing Piano Concerto Accompaniments

用於客製化鋼琴協奏曲伴奏的稠密—稀疏動態時間規整

TJ Tsai, Kavi Dey, Yigitcan Özer, Meinard Müller
arXiv:2607.18189 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-07-20
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：在人人都堆神經網路的當下，這篇用一個很輕的 DTW 變體打贏了 HDemucs 與 Spleeter，而且釋出了標註資料集與程式碼。方法簡單到你今天下午就能拿去試在別的對齊任務上。Why this one: In a moment when everyone stacks neural networks, this beats HDemucs and Spleeter with a very light DTW variant, and releases both an annotated dataset and code. The method is simple enough to try on another alignment task this afternoon.

動機Motivation

Music Minus One（MMO）錄音讓演奏者能跟著伴奏練習，但那個伴奏是死的——速度、彈性速度、呼吸點都固定，演奏者只能遷就它。理想情況是反過來：讓伴奏跟著你這一次的演奏走。

問題在於既有的自動伴奏系統幾乎都預設拿得到符號樂譜（MIDI）。作者直接點出這個假設在協奏曲上不成立：「對某些曲類（例如鋼琴協奏曲），符號樂譜並不普及，而且製作起來非常耗時。」於是本文的設定改為只用三份音訊：使用者的獨奏鋼琴錄音、純管弦樂錄音、以及鋼琴加管弦樂的混音錄音。

Music Minus One (MMO) recordings let a performer practise against an accompaniment, but that accompaniment is fixed — tempo, rubato and breathing points are frozen, and the player has to accommodate it. The desirable arrangement is the reverse: the accompaniment should follow this particular performance.

The obstacle is that existing automatic accompaniment systems almost all assume access to a symbolic score. The authors state plainly why that assumption fails for concertos: "for certain genres of music (such as piano concertos), symbolic scores are not widely available and are very time-consuming to create." So the setup here uses only three audio recordings: the user's solo piano performance, an orchestra-only recording, and a mixed piano-plus-orchestra recording.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

如果不用樂譜，直覺的做法是先用音源分離把混音中的鋼琴拿掉、留下管弦樂，再與純管弦樂錄音對齊。這條路的問題是分離本身會引入殘響與偽影，對齊模組拿到的是被污染的訊號。

作者的觀察更根本：真正的困難是頻譜不匹配——混音裡有鋼琴、純管弦樂錄音裡沒有，兩者的頻譜統計天生就對不起來，而 DTW 的成本矩陣對這種不匹配相當敏感。與其花大力氣去「移除」鋼琴，不如讓對齊演算法只看那些不受污染、且含有明確時間線索的幀。這個轉念是整篇的核心。

Without a score, the intuitive approach is to strip the piano from the mix with source separation, then align the residual orchestra against the orchestra-only recording. The problem is that separation introduces artefacts and bleed, so the alignment module receives a contaminated signal.

The authors' observation is more fundamental: the real difficulty is spectral mismatch — the mix contains piano and the orchestra-only recording does not, so their spectral statistics simply do not correspond, and a DTW cost matrix is quite sensitive to that. Rather than working hard to *remove* the piano, let the alignment algorithm look only at frames that are uncontaminated and carry clear timing cues. That reframing is the core of the paper.

方法Method

管線共四步：(1) 用 chroma 特徵與 subsequence DTW 把使用者的鋼琴錄音 P_query 對齊到混音 M_ref；(2) 用 Dense-Sparse DTW 把純管弦樂 O_acc 對齊到 M_ref；(3) 合成兩組對齊，推得 P_query 與 O_acc 之間的時間對應；(4) 對 O_acc 做時間尺度修改（TSM）以同步。

Dense-Sparse DTW 本身分兩件事。

*稀疏幀選取*：計算 chroma 特徵的通量 f_t = ‖o_t − o_{t+1}‖₁，也就是相鄰幀的變化量——變化大的幀通常對應音符起始、和聲轉換這類明確的時間錨點。選一個門檻 τ 使保留比例為 γ（例如 γ=0.2，只留兩成的幀），保留超過門檻者，並記錄被保留幀之間的間隔 g。

*成本與動態規劃*：對 L 個被選中的幀與 N 個混音幀建立 L×N 成本矩陣，距離用 cosine。DP 依間隔大小切換兩種模式——間隔為 1 時走稠密模式，使用標準 DTW 轉移 {(1,1), (1,2), (2,1)}；間隔 g>1 時走稀疏模式，允許水平移動落在 ⌈g/2⌉ 到 2g 之間。這個設計很直觀：跳過了 g 幀，就該允許在參考序列上前進大約 g 幀的量，上下各留一倍的彈性。回溯時取最後一列的最小值，沿 backpointer 回推並映射回稠密座標。

*TSM*：以諧波—打擊源分離為基礎做時間伸縮，實驗中使用的因子為 {0.8, 0.9, 1.0, 1.11, 1.25}。

The pipeline has four steps: (1) align the user's piano recording P_query to the mix M_ref with subsequence DTW on chroma features; (2) align the orchestra-only recording O_acc to M_ref with Dense-Sparse DTW; (3) compose the two alignments to infer the P_query–O_acc correspondence; (4) time-scale modify O_acc to synchronise.

Dense-Sparse DTW itself does two things.

*Sparse frame selection*: compute chroma flux f_t = ‖o_t − o_{t+1}‖₁, the frame-to-frame change — high-flux frames typically correspond to note onsets and harmonic changes, i.e. clear temporal anchors. Choose a threshold τ retaining a fraction γ of frames (e.g. γ=0.2, keeping one frame in five), keep those above threshold, and record the gaps g between retained frames.

*Cost and dynamic programming*: build an L×N cost matrix between the L selected frames and the N mix frames using cosine distance. The DP switches between two modes according to gap size — with a gap of 1 it runs in dense mode using standard DTW transitions {(1,1), (1,2), (2,1)}; with a gap g>1 it runs in sparse mode, allowing horizontal movement between ⌈g/2⌉ and 2g. The intuition is direct: having skipped g frames, the path should be allowed to advance roughly g frames in the reference, with a factor-of-two margin either side. Backtracking takes the minimum of the final row and follows backpointers, mapping back to dense coordinates.

*TSM*: time-scale modification based on harmonic-percussive source separation, with factors {0.8, 0.9, 1.0, 1.11, 1.25} in the experiments.

限制Limitations

作者明講的限制有兩點：本研究只處理離線任務（「其框架設計上可系統性研究離線與線上兩種協奏曲伴奏生成，但本初步研究僅聚焦離線任務」），以及只評估了對齊準確度，輸出音訊的主觀品質評估留待未來——考慮到 TSM 在 ±25% 的伸縮下會產生明顯偽影，這個空缺不小。

數據上還有幾點值得留意。資料規模是四個樂章、總長 2:51:05、約 150 個情境（靠 TSM 資料增強），以樂譜小節的下拍為標註，且只在鋼琴與管弦樂同時發聲的小節上評估。這是很紮實的標註工作，但四個樂章、四位作曲家的規模仍屬小型，跨曲目的泛化未經檢驗。

最該注意的是結果的細節：在嚴格容忍度下 DS-DTW 確實最好（0.1 秒：53.1% vs HDemucs 55.4%；0.2 秒：29.9% vs 33.0%；0.5 秒：10.0% vs 10.7%），但在寬鬆容忍度下反而輸給 HDemucs（1.0 秒：2.9% vs 2.1%；2.0 秒：0.6% vs 0.3%）。這個交叉很有訊息量——它暗示稀疏選幀提升了局部精度，卻在缺乏明顯時間線索的段落（長音、持續和聲、漸慢）更容易發生大幅偏移。論文把重點放在嚴格容忍度的優勢上，但這個 trade-off 值得被明說。另外，所有系統在 0.1 秒容忍度下錯誤率都超過 50%，代表幀級精度的對齊仍遠未解決。最後一個實務限制：整套方法要求存在一份純管弦樂錄音，而那本身就是 MMO 商品，並非隨手可得。

The authors name two limitations: the study covers the offline task only ("designed to allow for systematic study of both offline and online concerto accompaniment generation, though in this initial study we focus only on the offline task"), and only alignment accuracy is evaluated — subjective audio quality of the output remains future work, a non-trivial gap given that TSM at ±25% produces audible artefacts.

A few notes on the data. The corpus is four movements totalling 2:51:05 across roughly 150 scenarios (augmented via TSM), annotated at measure downbeats, with evaluation restricted to measures where piano and orchestra are both active. That is solid annotation work, but four movements by four composers is small, and cross-repertoire generalisation is untested.

The detail most worth noting is in the results: at tight tolerances DS-DTW is indeed best (0.1 s: 53.1% vs HDemucs 55.4%; 0.2 s: 29.9% vs 33.0%; 0.5 s: 10.0% vs 10.7%), but at loose tolerances it loses to HDemucs (1.0 s: 2.9% vs 2.1%; 2.0 s: 0.6% vs 0.3%). That crossover is informative — it suggests sparse frame selection improves local precision but is more prone to large excursions in passages lacking clear timing cues (sustained notes, held harmonies, ritardandi). The paper emphasises the tight-tolerance win, but the trade-off deserves stating. Note too that every system exceeds 50% error at 0.1 s tolerance, meaning frame-accurate alignment remains far from solved. A final practical constraint: the method requires an orchestra-only recording, which is itself an MMO product rather than something freely available.

討論Discussion

這篇最值得帶走的不是那張表，而是方法論上的姿態：面對頻譜不匹配，作者沒有加一個更大的分離網路，而是縮小問題——只對齊那些本來就可靠的幀。結果是一個更簡單、更快、且在關鍵區間更準的系統。在目前的研究氛圍下，這是很好的提醒，也是審稿時會加分的敘事。

可移植性很高。稀疏—稠密的切換邏輯不預設任何音樂類型或樂器，凡是「兩個版本音色差很多但事件時間對得起來」的對齊任務都能試：跨版本的錄音對齊、翻唱歌曲對齊、樂譜—音訊對齊（合成音訊 vs 真實演奏的頻譜差距同樣巨大）、甚至是不同錄音條件下的田野錄音比對。γ 只有一個超參數，你可以在一個下午內把它接到自己既有的 DTW 管線上做消融。

延伸的研究缺口也清楚。作者自己指出的線上（即時）版本是最直接的一條——稀疏選幀在因果設定下如何做，是個真問題，因為 flux 門檻需要未來幀。另一條是自適應 γ：既然失效似乎集中在時間線索稀疏的段落，那 γ 或許不該全曲固定，而應隨局部 flux 分布調整。第三條是把選幀準則從 chroma flux 換成 onset strength 或學習得到的顯著度，看看是否能同時修好寬容忍度下的大幅偏移。此外，作者釋出了四個樂章的小節標註與程式碼（HMC-MIR），這對缺乏標註的協奏曲對齊研究來說本身就是有價值的貢獻，可以直接當成你自己方法的比較基準。

The most transferable thing here is not the results table but the methodological posture: faced with spectral mismatch, the authors did not add a bigger separation network — they shrank the problem, aligning only the frames that were reliable to begin with. The result is simpler, faster and more accurate in the range that matters. In the current research climate that is a useful reminder and a narrative that tends to earn goodwill in review.

It transfers well. The sparse-dense switching logic assumes nothing about genre or instrumentation, so any alignment task where two versions differ sharply in timbre but correspond in event timing is fair game: cross-version recording alignment, cover-song alignment, score-to-audio alignment (synthesised versus performed audio has an equally large spectral gap), even comparing field recordings made under different conditions. With γ as essentially the only hyperparameter, you could bolt it onto an existing DTW pipeline and run an ablation in an afternoon.

The open gaps are clear too. The online (real-time) version the authors flag is the most direct — and a genuine research question, since a flux threshold needs future frames and must be rethought in a causal setting. A second is adaptive γ: if failures concentrate in passages with sparse timing cues, γ arguably should not be fixed across a movement but should track the local flux distribution. A third is swapping the selection criterion from chroma flux to onset strength or a learned saliency, to see whether the large excursions at loose tolerances can be fixed at the same time. Finally, the released measure-level annotations for four movements plus code (HMC-MIR) are a genuine contribution in their own right for a task starved of annotated data, and make a ready baseline for your own methods.
