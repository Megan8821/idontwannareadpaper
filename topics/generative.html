Agogic: Performance-Timed Music Tokens for LLM-Native Text-to-Symbolic-Music Generation

Agogic:給 LLM 原生文字轉符號音樂生成用的演奏時間音樂 token

Agogic authors
arXiv:2608.03999 · cs.SD, cs.CL · 投稿submitted 2026-08-04
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文做了一個乾淨的控制變因實驗:固定同一個預訓練 Qwen3.5 骨幹(0.8B 到 27B)、固定資料與訓練預算,只換符號音樂的 token 化方式。結果 0.8B 模型用作者提出的 PMT(Performance-timed Music Tokens,10 毫秒精度)拿到 Fréchet Music Distance 159,而 27B 模型用節拍網格(beat grid)只拿到 272——骨幹放大 34 倍換來的進步,遠比不上換一種 token 化方式帶來的進步(FMD 直接砍半)。更犀利的是,作者對既有系統 MIDI-LLM 與 text2midi 做了診斷測試,發現它們對不同文字描述輸出的和弦時間分布幾乎不變(分別是 72% 與 66% 的和弦時間匹配率,近乎跟提示詞無關),暗示這兩個系統可能只是在複誦訓練分布,而不是真的跟著文字描述生成。Why this one: This paper runs a clean controlled experiment: fix the same pretrained Qwen3.5 backbone (0.8B to 27B), fix data and training budget, and only swap the symbolic-music tokenization scheme. An 0.8B model using the authors' PMT (Performance-Timed Music Tokens, 10 ms resolution) reaches Frechet Music Distance 159, while a 27B model using a beat grid only reaches 272 -- scaling the backbone 34x buys far less than switching representation, which halves FMD outright. More pointedly, the authors run a diagnostic on existing systems MIDI-LLM and text2midi and find their chord-time output distribution barely shifts across different text prompts (72% and 66% chord-time match respectively, nearly prompt-invariant), suggesting these systems may be reciting their training distribution rather than actually following the text description.

動機Motivation

文字轉符號音樂生成領域普遍假設「模型越大,生成品質越好」,但很少有人控制其他變因、單獨檢驗「符號音樂怎麼被序列化成 token」這件事本身的影響力。現有的節拍網格(beat grid)、REMI、ABC 記譜這幾種常見的 token 化方式,普遍只用小節或拍子當時間單位(相當於 63 到 125 毫秒的解析度),這對古典或爵士這種需要精細演奏時機(踏板延遲、搶拍、彈性速度)的音樂來說解析度太粗。這篇論文要回答的問題是:如果把時間解析度做到 10 毫秒、逐音符記錄力度與多音軌資訊,這樣的 token 化方式能不能在同樣的模型規模與訓練預算下,產出更貼近真實音樂分布的生成結果?

Text-to-symbolic-music generation generally assumes bigger models generate better music, but few studies hold everything else fixed to isolate the effect of how symbolic music is serialized into tokens in the first place. Common tokenization schemes -- beat grids, REMI, ABC notation -- typically quantize time to the bar or beat level (roughly 63-125 ms resolution), which is too coarse for genres like classical or jazz that depend on fine performance timing: rubato, pedal decay, notes pushed ahead of or behind the beat. This paper asks: if timing resolution is pushed to 10 ms with per-note velocity and multi-track information preserved, can that tokenization scheme produce generations closer to the real music distribution under the same model size and training budget?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

相關研究點名了兩個既有的文字轉符號音樂系統:MIDI-LLM 與 text2midi,以及 REMI、ABC 記譜這兩種代表性的 token 化基準。這篇論文跟它們最關鍵的差異,不是又做了一個新模型,而是先做了一個「想像的複製測試」——把同一批文字描述餵給 MIDI-LLM 與 text2midi,檢查輸出的和弦節奏分布會不會因為提示詞不同而改變。結果發現兩者的和弦時間匹配率分別固定在 72% 與 66% 附近、最大複音數固定在 17.3 附近,幾乎不隨提示詞變化,這是一種「印記診斷」(imprinting diagnostic):模型很可能只是在重現訓練資料的統計特徵,而不是真正回應文字描述。這個發現直接說明了作者為什麼要提出 PMT——如果連現有系統對文字的服從度都值得懷疑,那麼比較生成品質時,至少要先把 token 化方式這個變因徹底控制乾淨。

The related-work section names two existing text-to-symbolic-music systems, MIDI-LLM and text2midi, along with REMI and ABC notation as representative tokenization baselines. The key difference from this prior work isn't another model -- it's a diagnostic test run first: feeding the same batch of text captions to MIDI-LLM and text2midi and checking whether their output chord-rhythm distribution shifts with the prompt. It doesn't: chord-time match rates sit fixed around 72% and 66% respectively, and max polyphony sits fixed around 17.3, nearly invariant to the caption -- an "imprinting diagnostic" suggesting these models likely reproduce their training distribution's statistics rather than truly responding to the text. That finding is the direct motivation for PMT: if even existing systems' prompt adherence is questionable, then before comparing generation quality at all, the tokenization variable needs to be controlled cleanly.

方法Method

PMT(Performance-timed Music Tokens)把每個音符表示成一小串可解讀的參數 token:時間位移(量化到 10 毫秒、最多 99 格)、音軌與樂器編號、音高、音長(量化到 10 毫秒、限制在 1 到 200 格)、力度(量化成 32 級)。整個詞彙表由音高 128 類、音長 200 類、力度 32 類、時間位移 100 類、音軌 16 類、樂器編號 129 類,加上 4 個特殊符號組成,總共 609 個符號,平均每個音符只需要約 4 個 token。訓練上,PMT 的詞彙表以平均初始化的方式接到 Qwen3.5 的既有詞彙表後面,用「標題遮罩式」監督微調——損失只算在音樂 token 上、不算在文字標題上,解碼時把 logits 限制在音樂詞彙範圍內,訓練跑 1 萬步、有效批次大小 16、學習率 8×10⁻⁵、序列區塊長度 1024。

評估方法刻意設計成「天花板校正」:每種 token 化方式都先做一次「編碼再解碼回真實音樂」的迴圈,量出這種格式本身能保留多少表現力當作上限,再用這個上限去校正模型生成的分數,這樣才能把「格式本身的表達力」跟「模型有沒有學好」這兩件事分開來看。作者也釋出兩個資料集:一個是 8 萬 6598 首歌的對齊語料庫(同時有文字標題、MIDI、ABC 記譜與算圖音訊),取材自民謠、古典、Lakh 與爵士;另一個是 625 萬首歌的大規模標題語料庫,規模是既有 MidiCaps 資料集的 37 倍,是目前符號或音訊音樂裡最大的標題資料集。

PMT (Performance-Timed Music Tokens) represents each note as a short sequence of interpretable parameter tokens: a time-shift (quantized to 10 ms, capped at 99 steps), track and instrument program number, pitch, duration (quantized to 10 ms, clipped to 1-200 steps), and velocity (quantized to 32 levels). The vocabulary is built from 128 pitch classes, 200 duration bins, 32 velocity levels, 100 time-shift bins, 16 tracks, 129 instrument programs, plus 4 special tokens, for 609 symbols total, averaging about 4 tokens per note. For training, PMT's vocabulary is appended to Qwen3.5's existing vocabulary with mean-initialized embeddings, using caption-masked supervised fine-tuning -- loss is computed only on music tokens, not on the caption -- with decoding logits restricted to the music vocabulary; training runs 10k steps, effective batch size 16, learning rate 8e-5, 1024-token blocks.

The evaluation protocol is deliberately ceiling-anchored: each tokenization scheme is first round-tripped (encode then decode) on real music to measure how much expressiveness the format itself can preserve, and generation scores are normalized against that ceiling -- separating format expressiveness from whether the model actually learned to use it. The authors also release two datasets: an aligned 86,598-piece corpus (with caption, MIDI, ABC notation, and rendered audio together) drawn from folk, classical, Lakh, and jazz sources, and a 6.25-million-piece captioned corpus, 37x larger than the existing MidiCaps dataset and the largest captioned corpus for symbolic or audio music to date.

限制Limitations

在固定骨幹、資料與預算的對照實驗裡(表一),0.8B 模型用 PMT 拿到 Fréchet Music Distance(FMD)159±8,同樣 0.8B 但用節拍網格(Beat-TSD)是 286±1、REMI 是 285±1、ABC 記譜是 406,而 27B 模型用 PMT 是 156、用 REMI 是 272。把 FMD 對模型參數量取對數畫圖,每一條線的斜率都在雜訊範圍內、幾乎是平的,PMT 跟節拍網格之間穩定維持約 125 分的差距,不會隨著模型放大而縮小——也就是說骨幹放大 34 倍幾乎不影響 FMD,但換一種 token 化方式就能讓 FMD 砍半。和弦時間匹配率上,PMT 在 0.8B 是 36.2%、27B 是 35.8%,而節拍網格與 REMI 都停在 30% 出頭、ABC 更只有 16.2%。

遷移到既有的 MidiCaps 標準評測集上(表二),用 8.66 萬筆訓練資料訓出的 4B 版 PMT 拿到 FMD 196,對照 MIDI-LLM 的 314 與 text2midi 的 421,PMT 的分布保真度明顯最接近真實音樂,但在屬性服從度上並不領先:PMT 的正確調性比例只有 0.10,而 MIDI-LLM 是 0.32;PMT 的樂器 F1 是 0.46,MIDI-LLM 是 0.63。前面提到的「印記診斷」測試顯示 MIDI-LLM 與 text2midi 的和弦時間匹配率(72%、66%)與最大複音數(17.3)幾乎不隨提示詞改變,暗示這兩者的高屬性分數有一部分只是複誦訓練分布、而非真的服從文字指令。

消融實驗顯示,若移除力度資訊,從頭在 MAESTRO 上訓練的 FMD 會從 106.1 惡化到 697;分來源看,PMT 對民謠題材會過度和聲化(生成 25% 有和聲的段落,參考音樂只有 13%),對古典題材則填音不足(生成 50% 對參考音樂的 82%)。作者也承認,一個簡單的解碼期屬性限制就能把樂器 F1 從 0.46 拉到 0.60、正確調性從 0.10 拉到 0.35,顯示 PMT 目前的屬性服從度並非上限,只是還沒做這一步優化。

In the controlled experiment holding backbone, data, and budget fixed (Table 1), the 0.8B model with PMT reaches Frechet Music Distance (FMD) 159±8, versus 286±1 for the same 0.8B model on a beat grid (Beat-TSD), 285±1 on REMI, and 406 on ABC notation; the 27B model reaches 156 with PMT and 272 with REMI. Plotting FMD against log-parameters, every arm's slope is within noise of flat, leaving a near-constant ~125-point PMT-versus-beat-grid gap that scale does not erode -- scaling the backbone 34x barely moves FMD, while switching representation halves it. On chord-time match, PMT reaches 36.2% at 0.8B and 35.8% at 27B, while beat grid and REMI stay just above 30% and ABC manages only 16.2%.

Transferred to the existing MidiCaps benchmark (Table 2), a 4B-parameter PMT trained on the 86.6k-piece corpus reaches FMD 196, versus 314 for MIDI-LLM and 421 for text2midi -- PMT is clearly closest to the real music distribution, but it doesn't lead on attribute adherence: PMT's correct-key rate is only 0.10 versus MIDI-LLM's 0.32, and PMT's instrument-F1 is 0.46 versus MIDI-LLM's 0.63. The imprinting diagnostic mentioned above shows MIDI-LLM and text2midi's chord-time match (72%, 66%) and max polyphony (17.3) barely shift with the prompt, suggesting part of their attribute scores may come from reciting the training distribution rather than genuinely following text instructions.

An ablation shows that removing velocity information degrades FMD from 106.1 to 697 when training from scratch on MAESTRO; broken down by source, PMT over-harmonizes folk material (25% of generated passages carry harmony versus 13% in the reference) and under-fills classical material (50% versus 82% in the reference). The authors also note that a simple decode-time attribute constraint lifts instrument-F1 from 0.46 to 0.60 and correct-key from 0.10 to 0.35, showing PMT's current attribute adherence isn't a hard ceiling, just an optimization not yet applied.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最重要的貢獻不是 PMT 這個具體的 token 化方案,而是它證明了一個可以推廣到其他模態的原則:對於「要求生成模型輸出一個有結構的產物」這種任務,資料怎麼被序列化,可能比模型規模更決定生成品質的上限,而且這件事可以被一個乾淨的控制變因實驗量出來、而不是用主觀感受主張。

作者也老實揭露了 PMT 目前的弱點:屬性服從度(正確調性只有 0.10)反而不如既有系統,對民謠與古典這兩種風格有系統性的和聲填充偏差,聆聽測試也還在收集中、目前的自動化代理指標「尚無定論」,27B 這個規模只跑了兩個隨機種子、35B-A3B 更只有一個種子,統計把握度有限。這些限制加總起來,讓「PMT 全面優於既有系統」這個結論還不能下,作者自己給的定位也更保守——是提供一套可重複驗證的評測框架與資料集,讓「換一種 token 化表示能不能改善生成品質」這種宣稱,以後都可以被實際測量,而不是單憑生成範例的觀感去主張。

The most important contribution here isn't PMT itself but the transferable principle it demonstrates: for tasks that ask a generative model to output a structured artifact, how the data is serialized may bound generation quality more than model scale does -- and this can be measured with a clean controlled experiment rather than asserted from listening impressions.

The authors are also candid about PMT's current weaknesses: attribute adherence (correct-key rate of only 0.10) actually trails existing systems, there's a systematic harmonic-filling bias on folk and classical material, the listening study is still being collected with the automatic proxy metric currently "inconclusive," and statistical power is limited -- the 27B scale uses only two random seeds and 35B-A3B just one. Taken together, these limitations mean "PMT is strictly better than existing systems" isn't yet a conclusion the paper can support, and the authors position it more conservatively: as a reproducible evaluation harness and dataset release so that future claims about whether a new tokenization scheme improves generation quality can actually be measured, not just asserted from generated examples.

Drive-to-Music: Context-Aware Generative Audio for In-Vehicle Experiences

Drive-to-Music:面向車內體驗的情境感知生成式音訊

Cosmin Dragoiu, Nooshin Nabizadeh
arXiv:2608.12615 · cs.SD, cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-08-12
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Drive-to-Music 是一套把行車紀錄器影像與車輛遙測資料(車速、車外溫度)轉換成即時生成音樂的系統管線:先用視覺語言模型描述行車場景,再用 LLM 把場景描述與車輛資料轉成音樂描述詞,最後餵給生成式音訊模型(選定 Stable Audio 2.5)合成配樂,同時生成專輯封面與車內氣氛燈光。跟既有車內音訊系統相比——SoundsRide(2021)是即時混音既有音效、非 AI 生成也非情境化音樂,既有的情緒感知車內音樂推薦系統則是「挑選」既有曲庫而非生成新曲——這篇論文的差異在於直接生成前所未有的原創配樂,而不是挑選或混音。但論文本身的驗證相當薄弱:模型選型每個候選只測約十個樣本,「有限的使用者研究」也沒有公布參與人數、方法或任何量化數字。Why this one: Drive-to-Music is a system pipeline that turns dashcam imagery and vehicle telemetry (speed, exterior temperature) into real-time generated music: a vision-language model describes the driving scene, an LLM converts that description plus telemetry into musical descriptors, and a generative audio model (Stable Audio 2.5, as selected) synthesizes the soundtrack, alongside generated cover art and cabin ambient lighting. Compared with prior in-vehicle audio systems -- SoundsRide (2021) remixes existing sound effects in real time rather than generating AI music, and prior mood-aware in-vehicle recommenders select from an existing music library rather than generate new tracks -- this paper's difference is generating genuinely novel soundtracks rather than selecting or remixing. But the paper's own validation is thin: model selection is based on roughly ten samples per candidate, and the "limited user studies" report no participant count, methodology, or quantitative figures at all.

動機Motivation

車內音樂長期以來只是「被動的旅伴」——播放清單或電台,跟駕駛當下的路況、天氣、車速沒有任何連動。論文主張,音樂其實可以做成一種情境感知的介面,依照即時的行車狀況調整,藉此提升駕駛體驗、專注力與身心舒適度。這篇論文要解決的問題是:現有的 AI 音樂生成技術已經廣泛用在內容創作、素材庫音樂等領域,唯獨車用場景還沒有人做過——既有的車內音訊系統要嘛是挑選或混音既有曲目,要嘛評估只做在模擬器裡,沒有真正部署到實車上。

In-vehicle music has long been a passive travel companion -- a playlist or radio station with no connection to the current road, weather, or speed. The paper argues music could instead be an adaptive, context-aware interface that responds to real-time driving conditions to improve driver experience, attention, and well-being. The gap it targets: AI music generation is already widely used in content creation and stock-music libraries, but the automotive domain remains untouched -- existing in-vehicle audio systems either select or remix existing tracks, and where AI generation has been tested at all, evaluation stayed in a simulator rather than an actual vehicle.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名兩個具體的既有車內音訊系統。SoundsRide(Koutsogeorgis 等人,2021,曾獲 UIST 2021 最佳論文獎)是一套車內音訊擴增實境系統,把環境線索連結到音樂事件,靠即時預測與混音把既有音效素材接起來播放——重點是混音既有素材,不是生成新內容。另一個是 Çelik 等人(2020)的情緒感知車內音樂推薦系統,靠推斷駕駛情緒去挑選預期能改善舒適度與駕駛行為的既有曲目。

這篇論文最像的既有工作其實就是這兩個系統目標的組合:同樣是想讓車內音訊回應駕駛情境,但做法從「即時混音既有素材」或「挑選既有曲庫」換成「即時生成全新的原創配樂」。論文也引用了一般性的 AI 音樂生成文獻,但坦承這些系統過去從未被應用在車用場景,也沒有實車部署跟評估的先例。

The paper names two specific prior in-vehicle audio systems. SoundsRide (Koutsogeorgis et al., 2021, winner of a UIST 2021 Best Paper Award) is an in-vehicle audio augmented-reality system that links environmental cues to musical events, using continuous prediction and real-time mixing to stitch together existing sound material -- the operative word being mixing existing material, not generating new content. The other is Celik et al.'s (2020) mood-aware in-vehicle music recommender, which infers driver mood and selects existing tracks expected to improve comfort and driving behavior.

The closest prior work is effectively the combination of these two systems' goals -- making in-vehicle audio responsive to driving context -- but with the mechanism swapped from real-time remixing of existing material, or selecting from an existing library, to real-time generation of an entirely new soundtrack. The paper also cites general AI-music-generation literature but concedes none of it had previously been applied to the automotive setting, nor tested in an actual deployed vehicle.

方法Method

系統管線分六個階段。第一階段用視覺語言模型(選定 LiquidAI LFM 2.5 VL 1.6B)描述行車鏡頭畫面(周遭環境、地標、植被、天空與天氣);第二階段用 LLM(選定 LiquidAI LFM 2.5 1.2B Instruct)把場景描述跟車速、車外溫度合併成音樂描述詞(情緒、氛圍、風格);第三階段平行生成專輯封面(Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large Turbo)與曲名;第四階段用直方圖分析遮蔽天空╱道路╱建築等低資訊區域後,萃取主色調來調整車內氣氛燈光;第五階段對所有輸出做安全檢查(音樂品質評分、響度、真峰值、突然音量跳動、噪音底床,以及封面與曲名的內容審查),沒過的部分會重新生成。所有生成模型都跑在雲端,車機只負責蒐集資料上傳、接收生成結果播放,只需要間歇性網路連線。

模型選型是拿約十個樣本比較延遲與品質。生成音樂這一步比較了 ElevenLabs(15.7 秒延遲、MuQ-MuLan 語意對齊分數 0.09)、Mubert(7.4 秒、0.29)與 Stable Audio 2.5(6.2 秒、0.36),最後選 Stable Audio 2.5,因為它延遲最低且語意對齊分數最高;要求輸出是不帶人聲的純演奏音樂,長度至少 90 秒。

The system pipeline has six stages. 方法Method

MusPyExpress 擴充的是 MusPy 既有的通用 Music 物件，底層格式不變，但標註系統大幅擴增，新增了 28 種表情文字類別，分成文字類（如 ChordSymbol、RehearsalMark）、結構控制類（如 Dynamic、HairPinSpanner、TempoSpanner）、音符層級標註（如 Articulation、SlurSpanner、VibratoSpanner）與通用類別。標註分兩個層級存放：跟時間相關的整體記號與排練記號放在樂曲層級，力度與個別文字指示則放在聲部層級。

MusPyExpress extends MusPy's existing general-purpose Music object; the underlying format is unchanged, but the annotation system is substantially expanded with 28 new expression-text classes, grouped into text-based (e.g. ChordSymbol, RehearsalMark), structural-control (e.g. Dynamic, HairPinSpanner, TempoSpanner), note-level (e.g. Articulation, SlurSpanner, VibratoSpanner), and general-purpose categories. Annotations are stored at two levels: temporally-related marks and rehearsal marks at the score level, dynamics and individual text instructions at the track level.

A key design is the 'spanner' concept, for expression text that spans an explicit duration -- an accelerando is a TempoSpanner, distinct from the Tempo object used for a single-point marking like vivace. The library also implements dual timing: converting between a tempo-agnostic metrical time-step unit and a seconds-based real-time unit that reflects tempo changes, and it actually renders these markings in MIDI/audio output -- for example, notes in a slurred passage have their duration extended to the next note, and accented notes get a marked loudness boost, controlled by configurable constants (e.g. slowing tempo 3x for fermatas, raising velocity 1.5x for accents).

Music-driven character animation has achieved solid results for dance and melodic-instrument performance, but drumming introduces obstacles that dance generation does not face. First, drum audio consists of noisy transient bursts whose timbres are similar across different drum components, making discriminative feature extraction difficult and hindering generalization across kits and environments. Third, the same acoustic event could have been struck by either hand, a one-to-many problem requiring not just precise motion but a high-level coordination decision. The authors argue existing generative frameworks "remain ill-equipped" to bridge noisy percussive signals and high-velocity physical execution -- they excel at melodic instruments' smooth, low-frequency trajectories but cannot resolve drum-onset ambiguity or stay stable under extreme accelerations.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

動作生成技術本身經歷了從姿態混合、狀態機、動作圖到現在業界標準的動作比對（motion matching），再到近年的資料驅動生成模型（擴散模型因為能捕捉動作分布的多樣性，被認為優於 GAN 與 VAE）。音訊驅動動作生成這條線上，Tseng et al. 的 EDGE 用擴散模型做舞蹈生成，是這篇論文直接沿用並改造的架構基礎——但作者強調 EDGE 的設計「無法直接套用」到打擊樂，因為舞蹈動作平滑連續，鼓點動作卻是離散的瞬間爆發。

真正跟這篇做同一件事的先前系統只有兩個：Shahid et al.（2025）用強化學習訓練人形機器人以 MIDI 輸入打鼓，結果因為機械限制而缺乏人類的表現力；Kyriakou et al.（2025）的 DRUMS 系統用 LSTM 驅動手臂、動作比對處理軀幹與臉部、決定性函式處理腿部，同樣吃 MIDI 輸入，但受限於資料庫搜尋與啟發式規則，動作多樣性與物理合理性都有限，訓練資料也只有約 35 分鐘，雜訊高且常有腳滑問題。這篇論文跟這兩者的差異很明確：改用原始音訊而非 MIDI 輸入，讓系統能泛化到沒有對齊 MIDI 的音樂；設計新的雙目標損失函式同時兼顧骨架合理性與鼓棒末端精準度；並提出兩個新的評測指標（擊點偏差 IPD 與打擊對齊分數 PAS），讓這個子領域第一次有辦法系統性比較不同方法。

Motion-synthesis technology evolved from pose blending and state machines through motion graphs to today's industry-standard motion matching, and more recently to data-driven generative models, with diffusion favored over GANs and VAEs for capturing the full diversity of plausible motion. Within audio-conditioned motion synthesis, Tseng et al.'s EDGE uses diffusion for dance generation and is the architecture this paper directly adapts -- but the authors stress EDGE's design "does not translate" to percussion, since dance motion is smooth and continuous while drum motion is discrete and bursty.

Only two prior systems actually attempt the same task: Shahid et al. (2025) trained a humanoid robot via reinforcement learning to play drums from MIDI, but results "lack human-like expressivity due to mechanical constraints"; and Kyriakou et al.'s (2025) DRUMS drives arms with an LSTM, torso and face with motion matching, and legs with deterministic functions, also from MIDI, but is limited by database search and heuristics constraining diversity and plausibility, with training data of only about 35 minutes, noisy and prone to foot sliding. This paper's departure from both is explicit: raw audio instead of MIDI so it generalizes to unaligned music; a new dual-objective loss balancing skeletal plausibility against stick-tip precision; and two new metrics (Impact Point Deviation, Percussive Alignment Score) enabling systematic comparison for the first time.

方法Method

動作以每秒 120 幀表示（對應鼓棒可能超過每秒 10 公尺的速度）：身體用 27 個關節的連續 6D 旋轉表示，鼓棒方向另用 6D 旋轉表示，鼓棒尖端則額外用明確的三維卡氏座標表示。訓練資料是一位專業爵士鼓手在 Roland TD-25KV 電子鼓組上錄製的 3 小時 30 分 53 秒動作捕捉（120 Hz，約 151.8 萬幀），涵蓋基本功、17 種常見節奏型態（搖滾、迪斯可、放克、爵士、雷鬼、金屬等，三種速度）與即興演奏，刻意排除跨手打法。資料再用兩階段方式擴增：先用不同鼓組音色把動作對應的 MIDI 重新合成成音訊，再疊加 13 種隨機音訊效果（殘響、雜訊、等化、壓縮、變調等，強度從 [0,1] 均勻取樣），每個動作片段擴增出 50 種音訊版本。

音訊特徵是手工設計的 44 維向量，包含鼓件起始偵測、節拍追蹤、振幅包絡、頻譜重心與 40 維 MFCC，作者認為這比套用 Jukebox 之類的預訓練音訊編碼器更可解釋、更好維護。模型主幹沿用 EDGE 的音訊條件化 Transformer 擴散架構，重點創新在損失函式：標準做法用正向運動學把旋轉誤差轉成關節位置來監督，但旋轉誤差會沿著運動鏈累積，導致鼓棒末端精度不夠——這篇改用 ℒ = 0.5‖r-r̂‖² + 1‖p_sticks-p̂_sticks‖²，把身體姿態用旋轉監督、鼓棒尖端額外用卡氏座標直接監督，理由是「幾公分的誤差就決定打中還是打空」。訓練用 3×10⁻⁴ 學習率、batch size 128，在單張 RTX 3090 上跑 6000 個 epoch、約 48 小時。

Motion is represented at 120 Hz to capture stick velocities exceeding 10 m/s: the body uses continuous 6D rotations across 27 joints, stick orientation uses 6D rotation, and stick tips additionally carry explicit 3D Cartesian coordinates. Training data is 3h30m53s of 120 Hz motion capture (about 1.52 million frames) from a professional jazz drummer on a Roland TD-25KV kit, covering fundamentals, 17 common grooves at three tempos, and improvised songs, deliberately excluding cross-armed playing. Data is augmented in two stages: audio is re-synthesized from paired MIDI using randomized drum-kit timbres, then 13 randomized effects (reverb, noise, EQ, compression, pitch shift, strength sampled from [0,1]) are layered on, producing 50 audio variants per motion clip.

Audio features are a hand-designed 44-dimensional vector -- onset detection, beat tracking, amplitude envelope, spectral centroid, and 40-dimensional MFCCs -- which the authors argue is more explainable and maintainable than pretrained encoders such as Jukebox. The backbone reuses EDGE's audio-conditioned Transformer diffusion architecture; the key innovation is the loss: standard practice supervises joint position via forward kinematics from rotation, but rotation error accumulates along the kinematic chain, leaving the stick tip imprecise. This paper instead uses ℒ = 0.5‖r-r̂‖² + 1‖p_sticks-p̂_sticks‖², supervising body pose by rotation while supervising stick tips directly by Cartesian coordinates, reasoning that "a few centimeters of error determines hitting the drum versus missing." Training uses a 3×10⁻⁴ learning rate, batch size 128, for 6000 epochs (~48 hours) on one RTX 3090.

限制Limitations

論文承認系統只吃單軌鼓聲，遇到混音完成的音樂必須先用 Demucs 或 Spleeter 之類的音源分離工具抽出鼓軌，生成品質會受限於分離品質（作者的實驗顯示現有分離器「已經夠穩健」，但仍是外部依賴）；模型假設固定的鼓組空間配置，換一組配置不同的鼓需要額外資料或幾何條件化，留給未來工作；120 Hz 的高幀率是精準度的前提，降低取樣率會讓時間量化誤差變大；訓練資料只來自一位專業爵士鼓手，論文沒有明確討論換成不同鼓手風格時的泛化情形。

The authors acknowledge the system only takes isolated drum audio; polyphonic mixes must first go through source-separation tools such as Demucs or Spleeter to extract the drum stem, and output quality depends on separation quality (their experiments suggest current separators are "sufficiently robust," but it remains an external dependency). The model assumes a fixed drum-kit spatial configuration, and generalizing to a different kit layout is left as future work requiring additional capture or geometric conditioning. The 120 Hz frame rate is a precondition for accuracy; a lower sampling rate would increase temporal-quantization error. Training data comes from a single professional jazz drummer, and the paper does not explicitly address generalization to other drummers' styles.

討論Discussion

作者坦承跟既有方法做直接的量化比較「並不容易」，因為資料集、輸入模態（音訊 vs. MIDI）與評測方式都不一樣，而且發表當時沒有公開的程式碼或標準化精度指標可以對照——這也是為什麼這篇特別強調自己提出的 PAS 與 IPD 指標的價值：讓這個子領域第一次有辦法系統性比較。論文也指出 PAS 背後「離散音訊事件與加速度峰值之間做軟性對應」的核心概念可以推廣到打字、拍手、節奏性踏步等其他需要緊密音訊-動作同步的任務。

未來方向包括加入手指與臉部動作以提升視覺真實感、透過風格遷移處理龐克 vs. 慵懶爵士這類風格差異、開發手動調整左右手指派與時間偏移的創作工具，以及參考近期大提琴合成與人物-物件互動研究，探索兩階段模型架構。整體而言，這篇論文用一個相對成熟的擴散骨幹加上針對性的損失設計，把「音訊驅動鼓手動作」這個長期被 MIDI 綁住的子問題，第一次做到接近人類難以分辨真假的精度。

The authors admit direct quantitative comparison to prior work "remains non-trivial" because datasets, input modality (audio vs. MIDI), and evaluation methods all differ, and no public codebase or standardized precision metric existed at the time of writing -- which is why the paper leans on its own PAS and IPD metrics as a contribution: they let this sub-area be systematically benchmarked for the first time. The authors note that PAS's underlying idea, a soft correspondence between discrete audio events and acceleration peaks, generalizes to other tasks needing tight audio-motion synchrony, such as typing, clapping, or rhythmic footstepping.

Future directions include finger- and facial-level motion for visual realism, style transfer to capture differences such as punk energy versus laid-back jazz, manual authoring tools for overriding hand assignment and timing offsets, and exploring two-stage architectures following recent cello-synthesis and human-object-interaction work. Overall, by pairing a fairly established diffusion backbone with a targeted loss redesign, the paper pushes audio-driven drummer motion synthesis -- long tied to MIDI input -- to a precision level close to indistinguishable from real performance for the first time.

為什麼挑這篇：這篇的核心手法不是新的擴散或影片生成骨幹，而是把「演唱時的嘴型同步」重新定義成一個可以獨立操控的語意角色欄位（畫面中人是聲音的來源，還是純粹伴舞的聆聽者），藉此把「純舞蹈」「純說話」「聆聽反應」跟從未在訓練資料裡出現過的「邊唱邊跳」四種行為，用同一套共享的語音表徵與注入路徑串起來。跟先前的音樂驅動舞蹈系統（如 EDGE、X-Dancer、OmniDance）或語音驅動人物動畫系統（如 Wan-S2V、InfiniteTalk）不同的地方在於：這篇從頭到尾沒看過任何一段「邊唱邊跳」的真實影片，純靠角色欄位切換就在推論時零樣本合成出來。用 InfiniteTalk 三分之一左右的參數量（5.63B vs 18.88B），SingDance 在嘴型同步準確度上仍拿到有競爭力的數字（LSE-C 7.995 vs InfiniteTalk 8.535），而在美感與畫質指標上反而是五個基準裡最高的（Aesthetic 0.593、Imaging 0.722），使用者偏好調查裡動作節奏對齊的偏好率是 61-68% 對 32-39%，大幅贏過同樣做語音驅動影片的 Wan-S2V。Why this one: SingDance's core move isn't a new diffusion or video backbone -- it's reframing lip-sync articulation as an explicit, independently controllable semantic role (whether the on-screen subject is the vocal source or merely a listener dancing along), letting a single shared speech representation and injection pathway cover speaking, listening/reacting, dancing-only, and the never-jointly-observed singing-and-dancing behavior. Unlike prior music-driven dance systems (EDGE, X-Dancer, OmniDance) or speech-driven avatar systems (Wan-S2V, InfiniteTalk), SingDance never sees a single real singing-and-dancing video during training -- it composes that behavior zero-shot at inference purely by flipping the role field. Using roughly a third of InfiniteTalk's parameters (5.63B vs 18.88B), SingDance still achieves competitive lip-sync accuracy (LSE-C 7.995 vs. InfiniteTalk's 8.535) while topping all five baselines on aesthetic and imaging quality (0.593 and 0.722). In human preference studies for motion-beat alignment, it wins 61-68% of votes against Wan-S2V's 32-39%.

動機Motivation

個人化舞蹈影片生成其實藏著兩種不同的行為，過去的研究把它們當成兩個獨立問題處理：音樂驅動的舞蹈生成方法只管編舞，完全不建模嘴型與發聲；語音驅動的人物動畫方法則預設畫面裡的人就是聲音的來源。但同一位使用者、同一首歌，創作者有時想要「邊唱邊跳」的影片，有時只想要「純伴舞」的影片——如果一個框架能同時支援兩種形式，就能大幅簡化個人化影片創作的流程。這篇論文的關鍵洞見是把「畫面中人是否在發聲」重新定義成一個明確的語意角色：畫面中人要嘛是聲音的來源（source），要嘛是接收畫面外聲音的聆聽者（listener）。

Personalized dance-video generation actually conflates two distinct behaviors that prior work always treated as separate problems: music-conditioned dance methods handle choreography only and never model vocal articulation, while speech-driven avatar methods assume the on-screen person is the one producing the voice. But given the same subject and the same song, a creator might sometimes want a video where the subject sings and dances, and other times just a dancing-along video with no lip-sync -- a single framework supporting both would substantially simplify personalized video creation. This paper's key insight is reframing whether the on-screen subject is vocalizing as an explicit semantic role: the visible subject is either the source producing vocals, or a listener receiving vocals from off-screen.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂驅動的舞蹈生成這條線上，從早期的 FACT、Bailando 到用擴散模型的 EDGE、精細編舞資料集 FineDance，再到近期把音樂對齊到 2D 姿態 token 的 X-Dancer、拆分動作與外觀專家的 MACE-Dance，以及把文字生成模型改造成音樂條件化的 MusicInfuser、結合圖像/文字/MERT 特徵的 5B 骨幹 OmniDance、階層式全域到局部音樂條件化的 Wan-Dancer——這些系統全都只處理編舞，完全不涉及畫面中人是否在唱歌。

另一條線是語音驅動的人物影片生成，例如用「幀對齊語音注入」做時間條件化的 Wan-S2V、多角色動畫的 HY-Avatar、稀疏幀配音的 InfiniteTalk，以及專門處理說話者/聆聽者互動的 DualTalk、UniLS、LPM 1.0 與 StreamAvatar——但這些系統要嘛預設畫面中人就是說話者，要嘛用完全獨立的路徑分別處理說話與聆聽兩種行為。SingDance 跟這些系統最大的不同，是刻意讓來源角色與聆聽角色共用同一套語音表徵與注入路徑，只透過角色條件的切換去改變行為，而不是像 DualTalk 等系統那樣為兩種角色設計不同的處理管線；跟音樂驅動舞蹈系統的差異則是額外引入了角色感知的語音控制，把「會不會對嘴」變成一個可獨立操縱的軸。

Within music-conditioned dance generation, the lineage runs from early FACT and Bailando through the diffusion-based EDGE and the fine-grained FineDance dataset, to systems that align music to 2D pose tokens (X-Dancer), split motion and appearance experts (MACE-Dance), adapt text-to-video backbones with music conditioning (MusicInfuser), combine image/text/MERT features in a 5B backbone (OmniDance), and use hierarchical global-to-local music conditioning (Wan-Dancer) -- all handling choreography only, never whether the on-screen subject is singing.

A separate lineage is speech-driven human video generation: Wan-S2V's frame-aligned speech injection, HY-Avatar for multi-character animation, InfiniteTalk for sparse-frame dubbing, and systems modeling speaker/listener interaction such as DualTalk, UniLS, LPM 1.0, and StreamAvatar. But these systems either assume the on-screen subject is always the speaker, or use entirely separate pathways for speaking versus listening, as DualTalk does. SingDance's central departure is deliberately sharing one speech representation and injection pathway across source and listener roles, changing only the role condition to switch behavior, rather than building distinct pipelines per role; its departure from the dance lineage is adding role-aware speech control, turning "whether lips are synced" into an independently controllable axis.

方法Method

語音走 Wav2Vec 2.0 做逐層加權聚合後映射成全域語音特徵與逐幀對齊的局部語音 token；音樂則走 MuQ（音樂導向的聲學/結構特徵）映射成逐幀對齊的局部音樂 token。角色控制透過五欄位提示結構（語音角色、風格、動作、首幀、鏡位）實現，標準化的「語音角色」欄位決定訊號要指派給畫面中人（來源）還是畫面外（聆聽者），文字提示裡的肢體動作描述跟發聲行為描述分開寫，並搭配一組全域角色嵌入與局部角色 token 貫穿訓練與推論。

針對五種任務（說話、聆聽反應、純伴舞-器樂、純伴舞-歌曲、邊唱邊跳）設計了「硬性精簡路由」，依任務決定要把哪些 token 送進交叉注意力——關鍵是「歌曲+來源角色」這個組合（對應邊唱邊跳）在訓練時完全沒有出現過，是推論時才第一次被組合出來的零樣本配置。

訓練分兩階段：語音階段用約 30 萬段五秒影片（HuMoSet 加自建網路影片，約 1.2 萬段是真正的畫面外聆聽反應，其餘是畫面內說話），此階段不涉及音樂路徑；舞蹈階段用約 4.8 萬段五秒影片（清洗過的 MA-Data 加自建舞蹈資料，1.8 萬段器樂、3 萬段有歌曲），器樂範例只有音樂做時變音訊輸入，歌曲範例保留語音加音樂（即使沒有對嘴，歌聲的分句與樂句邊界仍能影響編舞），並保留 20% 的說話範例避免遺忘，訓練目標是標準的流匹配損失，沒有額外的對嘴或節拍對齊損失。

文字-音訊分類器自由引導採三段式，分別用文字引導強度與音訊引導強度組合；骨幹初始化自 Wan2.2-TI2V-5B，語音階段在 480p 訓練 8 個 epoch，舞蹈階段先在 480p 訓練 8 個 epoch、再於 704×1280 訓練約 3000 步，用 64 張 A100、batch size 64、AdamW 學習率 1×10⁻⁵。

Speech uses Wav2Vec 2.0 with layer-wise weighted aggregation, mapped into global speech features and frame-aligned local speech tokens; music uses MuQ (music-oriented acoustic/structural features) mapped into frame-aligned local music tokens. Role control uses a five-field prompt structure (vocal role, style, action, first frame, camera); the standardized vocal-role field assigns the signal to the on-screen subject (source) or off-screen (listener), body-motion text is kept separate from vocal-behavior text, and a paired global role embedding plus local role token run through training and inference. A hard-compact routing scheme picks which tokens enter cross-attention for each of five tasks (speaking, listening/reacting, dancing-only instrumental, dancing-only song, singing-and-dancing) -- critically, the song+source combination for singing-and-dancing is never observed during training and is composed zero-shot only at inference.

Training runs in two stages: a speech stage on roughly 300,000 five-second clips (HuMoSet plus proprietary web video, about 12,000 genuine off-screen listening/reacting, the rest on-screen speaking) with no music pathway; and a dance stage on roughly 48,000 clips (cleaned MA-Data plus proprietary dance data, 18,000 instrumental and 30,000 with songs), where instrumental examples use music as the only time-varying audio, song examples retain both speech and music since vocal phrasing still informs choreography without lip-sync, and speech examples make up 20% of dance-stage data to prevent forgetting; training uses a standard flow-matching loss with no dedicated lip-sync or beat-alignment loss. Text-audio classifier-free guidance uses a three-pass scheme with separate text and audio guidance scales; the backbone initializes from Wan2.2-TI2V-5B, training 8 epochs at 480p in the speech stage, then 8 epochs at 480p followed by ~3000 steps at 704×1280 in the dance stage, on 64 A100 GPUs, batch size 64, AdamW at 1×10⁻⁵.

限制Limitations

論文自己承認 SingDance 目前只處理短片段、單人物、且角色是整段影片二元固定的：它還無法處理片段內的角色轉換（例如一開始聆聽、後來自己開口唱）、多人重唱、多重語音角色，也沒有自動判斷角色歸屬的機制。要把這種角色感知的組合能力延伸到更長、多人的表演場景，論文明白說是留給未來工作。

The authors acknowledge SingDance currently targets short, single-person clips with a binary, clip-level-fixed vocal role: it does not yet model within-clip role transitions (e.g., starting as a listener and later becoming the singer), duets, multiple simultaneous vocal roles, or automatic role attribution. Extending this role-aware compositional approach to longer, multi-person performances is explicitly left as future work.

討論Discussion

Current video-to-music systems take different approaches: VidMuse is a MusicGen-backbone autoregressive model with a local visual branch built on CLIP frame embeddings; GVMGen also uses a MusicGen backbone but compresses video features with a Q-Former followed by mean-pooling; Diff-V2M is a Stable Audio Open-based latent diffusion model using temporal-grid cross-attention. This paper's difference isn't a new generative backbone — all three baseline architectures are kept unchanged — but an added dialogue-aware conditioning signal: dialogue is source-separated the same way as music, then converted frame-by-frame into FiLM (feature-wise linear modulation) parameters that modulate the existing video conditioning vector, with the module's final layer zero-initialized so the model is identical to the unmodified baseline at initialization. Since GVMGen originally collapses the entire time axis via Q-Former plus mean-pooling, the authors add back frame-level positional embeddings so dialogue conditioning has a time axis to align against.

方法Method

資料集 OSSL-v2 收錄 1,886 部公版電影裡的 34,343 個片段,共 246.4 小時,平均每段 28.6 秒。建置流程是:先用開源的電影音源分離模型(選品質較高但速度慢三倍的設定)把音樂從電影混音裡分出來;再用開源的事件偵測模型判斷 527 個聲音類別,人工從中選出 157 個屬於音樂的類別(例如「trance music」),抓出「音樂機率連續超過非音樂機率至少十秒」的片段,並排除非音樂機率超過 0.05 這個門檻的片段。

對話感知模組的數學形式很單純:把逐畫格的影片條件向量記為 v_i,對白同樣經過音源分離、再用一維卷積編碼成 u_i,一個雙層 MLP φ 把 u_i 映射成 FiLM 參數 (γ_i, β_i),最後用 ṽ_i = (1+γ_i)⊙v_i + β_i 調變原本的影片向量。訓練片段長度統一裁成 10 秒(OSSL-v2 裡最短片段的長度),推論時則生成完整長度的配樂。

OSSL-v2 contains 34,343 clips from 1,886 public-domain films, totaling 246.4 hours with an average clip length of 28.6 seconds. The pipeline first applies an open-source cinematic source-separation model (using its slower, higher-quality setting) to pull music out of the film mix, then an open-source event-detection model scores 527 sound categories, from which 157 music-related categories (e.g. "trance music") are manually selected; segments are kept where music probability exceeds non-music probability for at least 10 consecutive seconds, excluding any segment where non-music probability exceeds an empirically set 0.05 threshold.

The dialogue-aware module is mathematically simple: let the per-frame video conditioning vector be v_i; dialogue, separated the same way as music, is encoded via a 1-D convolution into u_i; a two-layer MLP φ maps u_i to FiLM parameters (γ_i, β_i); and the video vector is modulated as ṽ_i = (1+γ_i)⊙v_i + β_i. Training segments are cropped to a uniform 10 seconds (the length of OSSL-v2's shortest clips), while inference generates full-length soundtracks.

限制Limitations

論文並沒有專闢一節寫限制,但從結果本身就能看出侷限:訓練片段被裁成 10 秒(受限於 OSSL-v2 最短片段的長度),這可能限制模型捕捉較長時間結構的能力;對話訊號的品質完全依賴上游音源分離模型分得乾不乾淨。更直接的侷限是效果不穩定——GVMGen 加了對話調變之後,同分布測試集(OSSL-v2)的表現不升反降(CLAP 從 0.43 掉到 0.39,KL 從 0.72 微幅惡化到 0.73),意味著這個模組在訓練資料上可能有過度正則化的傾向;Diff-V2M 不管加不加對話,Precision 都固定在 0.00,FAD 都在 119 以上,對話調變在這個基準線上幾乎起不了作用,論文也沒有解釋為什麼。

The paper has no dedicated limitations section, but the limits show up in the results themselves: training segments are cropped to 10 seconds because that's the length of OSSL-v2's shortest clips, which may cap the model's ability to capture longer-range structure, and dialogue-signal quality depends entirely on how cleanly the upstream source-separation model isolates it. More directly, the effect is unstable — adding the dialogue adapter to GVMGen makes in-distribution performance on OSSL-v2 worse rather than better (CLAP drops from 0.43 to 0.39, KL edges up from 0.72 to 0.73), suggesting the module may overregularize on training data; and Diff-V2M's Precision stays fixed at 0.00 with FAD above 119 whether or not dialogue is added, meaning the adapter does almost nothing for that baseline, a gap the paper doesn't explain.

這篇論文比較值得談的地方,其實是資料集本身的定位選擇,而不是對話調變的效果:作者刻意用「自我託管的公版電影片段」取代「網址清單」,用工程手段解決學術基準會隨時間腐朽的問題,這比大多數方法論文更關心可重現性這件無聊但重要的事。

至於對話調變本身,論文的說法是「主要在配對層面的忠實度(paired fidelity,例如 CLAP、KL)上看到穩定進步,而不是整體生成分布的改變(distributional fidelity,例如 FAD、Precision)」,而且效果在商業片測試集(跨域)比在 OSSL-v2(同域)上更明顯——作者把這解釋成對話調變像是一種正則化,但反過來看,這也代表這個訊號目前只能微調生成結果貼合既有對白的程度,還沒有辦法改變模型本身「該生成什麼樣的音樂」的整體判斷。三個基準模型裡效果最不穩定的 GVMGen,剛好就是同分布測試集上表現最好的模型,兩者放在一起看,說明對話這個訊號目前更像是錦上添花,而不是解決生成品質的根本手段。

What's more worth discussing here is the dataset's own framing choice rather than the dialogue adapter's effect: the authors deliberately replace a list of URLs with self-hosted public-domain film clips, using an engineering fix for the problem of academic benchmarks decaying over time — a concern for reproducibility that most modeling papers treat as an afterthought.

As for the dialogue adapter itself, the paper's own framing is that gains show up mainly in paired fidelity (CLAP, KL) rather than distributional fidelity (FAD, Precision), and are more pronounced on the commercial-film (out-of-distribution) test set than on OSSL-v2 (in-distribution) — the authors read this as dialogue acting like a regularizer. But read the other way, it also means the signal currently only nudges generated output to better match existing dialogue, without changing the model's overall judgment of what music to generate. GVMGen, the baseline whose dialogue effect is least stable, happens to be the one that performs best in-distribution; taken together, dialogue conditioning here reads more as icing than as a fix for the underlying generation quality problem.

動機Motivation

神經音樂演奏合成過去幾乎都繞著鋼琴打轉,原因是鋼琴的表現力主要集中在觸鍵瞬間(onset),之後音符的走向相對固定。小提琴不是這樣:每個音符從發聲到結束,音高、音量、頻譜內容與時間包絡都持續被演奏者塑形,不是敲一下鍵就結束。

作者也點出現況的實務問題:專業音樂製作現在仍高度仰賴取樣音源庫與虛擬樂器(VI),這些音源庫儲存成本高,而且需要大量人工程式設計才能做出細膩的演奏變化。他們借用歌聲合成(如 TechSinger)與 TTS 領域已經驗證過的做法——同時對離散屬性(演奏技巧種類)與連續時間軌跡(動態)做條件化——把這個思路搬到小提琴合成上,提出 VIOLET:一個對演奏技巧與動態都有顯式控制的潛在擴散(latent-diffusion)合成框架,並同時釋出一個新資料集 CSV-TD,含 39 小時 48kHz 的小提琴演奏音訊,附帶 MIDI、技巧與動態曲線標註。

Neural music performance synthesis has mostly orbited around piano, because piano's expressiveness is concentrated at the note onset and the note's trajectory afterward is largely fixed. Violin is different: pitch, amplitude, spectral content, and the temporal envelope are all continuously shaped by the player throughout each note's duration, not settled at a single strike.

The authors also point to a practical gap: professional music production still relies heavily on sample libraries and virtual instruments (VIs), which carry large storage costs and demand labor-intensive manual programming to produce nuanced performance variation. They borrow an approach already validated in singing voice synthesis (TechSinger) and TTS — conditioning jointly on discrete attributes (technique) and a continuous temporal trajectory (dynamics) — and bring it to violin, proposing VIOLET: a latent-diffusion framework with explicit control over technique and dynamics, alongside a new dataset, CSV-TD, with 39 hours of 48 kHz violin audio annotated with MIDI, techniques, and dynamics curves.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

鋼琴這條線上,既有系統多半用 CNN 或 Transformer 做譜到音訊的映射、DDSP 式的分層合成、或狀態空間模型,搭配 MAESTRO、ATEPP 這類資料集,但這些方法的假設是「表現力主要在音符起音就決定了」,搬到小提琴上並不成立。

小提琴與弦樂合成這邊,業界實務靠的是拼接式合成(concatenative synthesis)與商用虛擬樂器(VI)——後者把大量錄好的取樣對應到密集的按鍵切換點上,本質上是資料庫查表而非生成。神經網路方法裡最直接的前作是 ViolinDiff(Kim et al., 2025),用兩階段擴散框架先預測音高彎曲(pitch-bend)的走勢、再合成梅爾頻譜,證明了顯式建模音高輪廓能改善小提琴合成品質,但論文指出這類方法「音質仍然比不上取樣音源庫,而且缺乏對演奏技巧與動態的精細顯式控制」。

VIOLET 與 ViolinDiff 的差別很明確:不是換一種擴散做法,而是新增了技巧與動態這兩條可以獨立調節的顯式條件通道,並且用 classifier-free guidance 讓這兩條通道在推論時能分開加權。作者也自陳這是「第一個同時對音符、演奏技巧與連續動態做時間對齊條件化」的音樂生成系統。

On the piano side, existing systems mostly map score to audio via CNN or Transformer architectures, DDSP-style hierarchical synthesis, or state-space models, trained on datasets like MAESTRO and ATEPP — all premised on expressiveness being decided mostly at note onset, an assumption that breaks down for violin.

On the string-synthesis side, industry practice relies on concatenative synthesis and commercial virtual instruments (VIs), which map large sample libraries to dense keyswitches — essentially database lookup rather than generation. The most direct neural precedent is ViolinDiff (Kim et al., 2025), a two-stage diffusion framework that predicts pitch-bend contours before synthesizing a mel-spectrogram, demonstrating that explicit pitch-contour modeling improves violin synthesis quality; but the paper notes such methods "still suffer from lower audio quality compared to sample-based libraries and lack explicit, fine-grained control over playing techniques and dynamics."

VIOLET's difference from ViolinDiff is specific: not a different diffusion recipe, but two new, independently adjustable explicit conditioning channels for technique and dynamics, combined with classifier-free guidance that lets these channels be weighted separately at inference. The authors describe VIOLET as "the first music generative system that takes time-aligned conditioning of notes, playing techniques and continuous dynamics."

方法Method

音訊表徵用微調過的 DACVAE(Descript Audio Codec 的 VAE 版本),在小提琴錄音上微調解碼器,潛在幀率為 25Hz(每 40 毫秒一幀),微調後對顫音音符的高頻諧波細節重建更準。

三路控制訊號經過時間對齊後同時輸入:MIDI 音符(以鋼琴捲簾的二值矩陣表示,音域涵蓋小提琴的 G3 到 A7)、演奏技巧(12 種技巧、音符層級的二值矩陣,每個技巧持續時間跟著對應音符走)、以及動態(從 MIDI 的 CC1 控制器值取出,正規化到 0–1 並做零階保持插值,產生逐幀對齊的分段常數動態曲線)。骨幹是加了 AdaLN(自適應層正規化)的 DiT(Diffusion Transformer),三種條件各自有獨立的嵌入器,最終調變參數是全域(來自時間步)與各條件局部貢獻的加總,套用在自注意力與前饋層上。

訓練目標是修正流(rectified flow):z_t 是 z0、z1 的線性內插,損失是預測速度場與真實位移的均方誤差;訓練時會隨機把各條件替換成一個學到的空嵌入,做組合式的無分類器引導(compositional classifier-free guidance),推論時可以分別調整技巧引導權重與動態引導權重,同時保持 MIDI 條件全程有效。長篇生成用 50% 重疊的滑動視窗,在時域用 Hann 窗做重疊相加。骨幹規模是 12 層、12 個注意力頭、隱藏維度 768,兩張 A100 訓練 10 萬步、批次 32(用梯度累積),約四天;推論時在單張 A100 上生成 10 秒音訊要 2.3 秒(RTF=0.23),用 30 步 Euler 積分,推論引導權重 w_tech = w_dyn = 1。

Audio is represented via a DACVAE (a VAE variant of the Descript Audio Codec) with its decoder fine-tuned on violin recordings, giving a 25 Hz latent frame rate (one frame per 40 ms) and better reconstruction of the high-frequency harmonic detail in vibrato notes.

Three time-aligned control signals feed in together: MIDI notes as a binary piano-roll spanning the violin's playable range (G3 to A7), 12 playing techniques as a note-level binary piano-roll where each technique's duration matches its note, and dynamics extracted from the MIDI CC1 controller, min-max normalized to [0,1] with zero-order-hold interpolation to produce a frame-aligned, piecewise-constant curve. The backbone is a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) with AdaLN, with separate embedders per condition; the final modulation parameters sum a global (timestep-derived) term and each condition's local contribution, applied to both self-attention and feed-forward layers.

Training uses a rectified-flow objective — z_t linearly interpolates z0 and z1, and the loss is the squared error between predicted and true velocity — with each condition randomly replaced by a learned null embedding during training to enable compositional classifier-free guidance, which at inference lets technique and dynamics guidance weights be tuned independently while MIDI stays active throughout. Long-form generation uses 50%-overlapping windows, overlap-added with Hann windows in the time domain. The backbone is 12 blocks, 12 attention heads, 768 hidden dimensions, trained for 100,000 steps on 2 A100 GPUs at batch size 32 (with gradient accumulation) over about four days; inference takes 2.3 seconds to generate 10 seconds of audio on one A100 (RTF=0.23), using 30 Euler steps with guidance weights w_tech = w_dyn = 1.

限制Limitations

訓練資料裡真正同時有技巧與動態標註的只有 CSV-TD,而這批資料的音訊是用商用虛擬樂器(VI)算圖算出來的,標註時的技巧指派也不是照音樂性去設計,而是用「音符長短」的機率式啟發法決定(短音配撥弦、頓弓、泛音,長音配圓滑、顫音、泛音);換句話說,訓練訊號裡最完整的那一塊本身就帶著合成資料的偏差。

測試集同樣是合成的,論文自己承認這代表評估「可能偏向本來就在合成資料上訓練的模型」,結果應該被理解成「有沒有把輸入 MIDI 的時間與音高對準」這種基本正確性的量測,而不是「聽起來像不像真人演奏」的證據。真實錄音資料(MOSA、MUSC 共約 49.8 小時)雖然加進訓練,但論文自陳「動態與起音音高 F1 只有小幅改善」,推測是這批真實資料規模仍不夠大、錄音條件也比較吵。主觀聽測裡,VIOLET 在音質與動態貼合度兩項顯著輸給商用 VI(p<0.05、p<0.01),距離取代商用音源庫還有落差。

Among the training data, only CSV-TD carries both technique and dynamics annotations, but its audio was rendered through a commercial virtual instrument, and technique assignment during that rendering used a duration-based probabilistic heuristic (short notes get pizzicato/staccato/harmonic, long notes get legato/trill/harmonic) rather than musically deliberate choices — meaning the most complete slice of the training signal already carries synthetic-data bias.

The test set is likewise synthetic, and the paper itself acknowledges this "may favor models trained primarily on synthetic data," so results should be read as measuring basic correctness — pitch and timing alignment with the input MIDI — rather than evidence of sounding like a real performance. Real recordings (MOSA and MUSC, about 49.8 hours combined) were added to training, but the paper reports only "slight improvement" in dynamics and onset-pitch F1, attributed to the real corpora still being limited in scale and noisier in recording condition. In subjective listening tests, VIOLET scores significantly below the commercial VI on both audio quality (p<0.05) and dynamics matching (p<0.01), leaving a real gap before it could replace a commercial sample library.

討論Discussion

VIOLET 值得討論的地方在於它把「能不能執行對的演奏動作」跟「聽起來夠不夠真」這兩件事乾脆地分開評估,而不是把兩者混在一個總分裡模糊帶過:多技巧片段的技巧清晰度上顯著贏過 ViolinDiff(p<0.001),單一技巧的辨識準確率甚至在撥弦、頓弓上超過商用 VI,但音質與動態貼合度在統計上仍然輸給商用系統。

這代表擴散模型加顯式技巧/動態條件這條路,目前解決的是「控制力」的問題,還沒解決「音色說服力」的問題,兩者未必用同一種模型容量或同一批資料就能一起補齊。另一個值得注意的地方是動態相關係數從無條件化的 0.025 跳到有條件化的 0.631——這個對照組(w/o Cond)刻意設計成跟 ViolinDiff 的輸入條件對齊,是全文少數乾淨的消融實驗,證明「動態曲線有沒有被顯式建模」造成的差距,而不是被訓練資料或架構其他部分的差異混淆。

What's worth discussing about VIOLET is that it keeps "can it execute the right playing gesture" and "does it sound convincing" as separate, explicitly reported axes rather than folding both into one blurred score: on multi-technique clips it beats ViolinDiff significantly on technique clarity (p<0.001), and on single-technique identification it even beats the commercial VI on pizzicato and staccato, yet it still loses to the commercial system on audio quality and dynamics matching in statistical terms.

That suggests diffusion plus explicit technique/dynamics conditioning currently solves the controllability problem, not yet the timbral-conviction problem, and the two may not be closed by the same model capacity or the same training data. Also worth noting: the dynamics correlation jumps from 0.025 (unconditioned) to 0.631 (conditioned) — this ablation (w/o Cond) is deliberately set up to match ViolinDiff's input conditions, making it one of the paper's few clean ablations, isolating the gap caused specifically by explicit dynamics modeling rather than confounding it with other architectural or data differences.

Paired with full-song non-causal attention, independently-encoded lyrics/captions fed directly to the renderer, and a four-way CFG that separates codec/lyric/caption guidance, the ablations cleanly isolate each component's contribution and the complete system is competitive with commercial systems on a real leaderboard. 動機Motivation

混合式音樂生成系統把工作拆成兩半：一個語言模型負責用離散 codec token 規劃長程結構，一個擴散/flow-based 的渲染器負責把這些 token 轉成音訊。這種拆法在訓練時運作良好，因為渲染器每次看到的都是與目標音訊完美配對的 ground-truth token。但部署上線後，渲染器必須吃語言模型自己預測出來的 token——而這些 token 在某個不容小覷、且隨 codebook 深度而異的比例下是錯的（論文報告最深的 RVQ 第 7 層 top-1 錯誤率高達 89.2%）。一個只看過乾淨 token 的渲染器沒有理由對這種錯誤具有韌性，而推論時的錯誤會以訓練損失函數從未懲罰過的方式拖垮音質。論文把這個現象稱為 codec-interface 曝光偏差，類比自迴歸文字生成裡經典的曝光偏差問題，並主張這個問題之所以長期被忽略，是因為多數對混合式生成器的評測都是端對端打分，沒有把「渲染器對輸入條件的韌性」單獨切出來看。

Hybrid music generation systems split the job into a language model that plans long-range structure via discrete codec tokens, and a diffusion/flow-based renderer that turns those tokens into audio. This split works well at training time, when the renderer always sees the ground-truth tokens paired with their target audio. But at deployment, the renderer has to work from the LM's own predicted tokens — which are wrong at some nontrivial, codebook-dependent rate (the paper reports up to 89.2% top-1 error for the deepest codebook, RVQ level 7). A renderer that has only ever seen clean tokens has no reason to be robust to that error, and mistakes at inference degrade acoustic quality in ways the training loss never penalized. The paper calls this codec-interface exposure bias, drawing an analogy to the classic exposure-bias problem in autoregressive text generation, and argues it has been under-addressed because most evaluations of hybrid generators score the whole pipeline end-to-end without isolating the renderer's condition-robustness.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把 FullDiT 放在三種既有音樂生成典範的對照之下。直接擴散/flow 模型一次生成完整的聲學序列，沒有明確的離散規劃階段，在長程結構上容易吃虧。自迴歸 codec 建模直接生成 token 再解碼，音質同時取決於預測準確度與解碼器品質，兩邊的誤差會疊加。FullDiT 所屬的「token 規劃 + 渲染」混合典範，把兩者解耦：語言模型在 token 空間規劃，另一個獨立的渲染器把 token 轉成音訊，原則上兩邊可以各自獨立改進。論文主張，既有混合式系統缺少的是一個 (a) 透過非因果注意力一次看到整首歌的 token，而不是只看短的局部窗口、(b) 歌詞與文字說明是以獨立、高保真的方式直接餵給渲染器，而不是只靠語言模型隱含編碼進 token 預測裡、(c) 被明確訓練成能容忍上游語言模型實際會犯的那種 token 錯誤的渲染器。

The paper positions FullDiT against three existing paradigms for music generation. Direct diffusion/flow models generate the complete acoustic sequence in one shot without an explicit discrete planning stage, which can struggle with long-range structure. Autoregressive codec modeling generates tokens directly and decodes them, so quality depends jointly on prediction accuracy and decoder quality, with errors compounding through both. The hybrid token-planning-plus-rendering paradigm — which FullDiT belongs to — decouples the two: an LM plans in token space, and a separate renderer turns tokens into audio, in principle allowing each half to be improved independently. What the paper argues is missing from prior hybrid systems is a renderer that (a) sees the entire song's tokens at once via non-causal attention rather than a short local window, (b) gets lyrics and captions as direct high-fidelity conditioning signals rather than only whatever the LM implicitly encodes into its token predictions, and (c) is explicitly trained to tolerate the kind of token errors the upstream LM will actually make.

方法Method

FullDiT 是一個 80 億參數的擴散 transformer，接在一個具語意感知能力的 RVQ tokenizer 與一個階層式自迴歸語言模型（80 億參數的全域模型加上 4 億參數的局部模型，外加一個可選的旋律模組）下游。它吃進語言模型輸出的八條逐幀對齊的 RVQ token 串流，以及獨立編碼的文字說明（透過 Qwen3 embedding，帶有曲風/情緒資訊）與歌詞（帶有音素與人聲結構資訊），用非因果自注意力機制、以逐幀方式跟目標 VAE latent 串流融合、且一次涵蓋整首歌——音訊位置扮演 query，去注意歌詞、文字說明與 codec 這些 context。訓練用的是標準 flow-matching 目標（從雜訊到目標 latent 之間預測向量場），但刻意加了一道破壞機制：Error-Matched Distractor Conditioning（EMDC）。對每一幀、八個 codebook 中的每一個，EMDC 以機率 p_k = 1 − Acc@1_k 替換掉 ground-truth token，也就是校準到該 codebook 在上游語言模型底下、teacher-forcing 時實際的 top-1 錯誤率（所以層數越深、越難預測的 codebook 被破壞得越頻繁——最高到前面提到的 89.2%）。關鍵是，替換用的 token 不是隨機雜訊，而是從該 codebook 嵌入空間裡以 cosine 相似度取最近鄰的集合中取樣（鄰域大小 K_k 依 codebook 調整，介於 20 到 180 之間），讓破壞的樣態貼近語言模型實際會犯的「差一點」錯誤，而不是無結構的雜訊。EMDC 完全不動乾淨的音訊目標——只動 codec 這個條件輸入——所以模型真正的訓練訊號維持正確。破壞比例的排程沿著訓練課程漸進：0 到 3 萬步保持乾淨、3 萬到 4 萬步線性拉高、4 萬到 15 萬步維持在目標比例。推論階段，論文提出四路 classifier-free guidance：不是慣用的單一條件/無條件對照，而是計算四種預測——無條件、只有 codec、codec 加歌詞、codec 加歌詞加文字說明——再用三個各自獨立可調的引導強度（對應每一層額外條件）組合起來，讓使用者（或自動化策略）能權衡要多信任 codec 規劃、還是要更強調歌詞或文字說明/風格的貼合度。

FullDiT is an 8B-parameter diffusion transformer sitting downstream of a semantic-aware RVQ tokenizer and a hierarchical autoregressive LM (an 8B global model plus a 0.4B local model, with an optional melody module). It takes the LM's eight frame-aligned RVQ token streams plus independently-encoded captions (via Qwen3 embeddings, carrying genre/mood) and lyrics (carrying phonetic/vocal structure), and fuses them frame-wise with the target VAE latent stream via non-causal self-attention over the whole song — audio positions act as queries attending to lyric, caption, and codec context. Training uses a standard flow-matching objective, but with one deliberate corruption: Error-Matched Distractor Conditioning (EMDC). For each frame and each of the eight codebooks, EMDC replaces the ground-truth token with probability p_k = 1 − Acc@1_k, matched to that specific codebook's actual teacher-forced top-1 error rate under the upstream LM (so deeper, harder-to-predict codebooks get corrupted more often — up to the 89.2% rate mentioned above). Crucially, the replacement token is sampled from a cosine-similarity nearest-neighbor set in the codebook's embedding space (neighborhood size K_k tuned per codebook, from 20 to 180), so the corruption resembles the kind of near-miss the LM actually produces rather than unstructured noise. EMDC never touches the clean audio target — only the codec conditioning input. The corruption schedule ramps in over a training curriculum: 0–30k steps clean, 30k–40k linear ramp-up, 40k–150k steps at full target rate. At inference, the paper introduces Four-Way Classifier-Free Guidance: it computes four predictions — unconditional, codec-only, codec+lyrics, and codec+lyrics+captions — and combines them with three independently tunable guidance scales, letting a user trade off how much to trust the codec plan versus emphasize lyrics or caption/style adherence.

限制Limitations

論文明顯沒有寫一個明確的「限制」章節——大部分的缺口得從對照實驗沒有涵蓋到的地方去推。EMDC 的鄰域大小 K_k 被描述成「工程上的頻寬啟發式選擇」，選定時並沒有給出嵌入空間 KNN 半徑跟語言模型預測的困惑度/不確定性之間的理論連結——實務上有效，但不是從第一原理推出來的。三階段課程（乾淨期/斜坡期/全速期各自的步數）也是以固定排程呈現，沒有敏感度分析告訴讀者換一組步數或不同的斜坡形狀會讓表現差多少。評測大量倚賴自動化、無參考的指標（ViSQOL、log-mel L1，以及 SongBench/SongEval/Audiobox/CMI-RM 這類框架分數），CFG 調參只做了一輪盲聽；由於所有對照都用同一個固定的語言模型，沒有辦法切割出端對端品質提升裡有多少歸功於渲染器本身、多少歸功於上游語言模型自己的品質。跟商用系統的比較用的是內部 500 首歌的集合，加上另一個資料與方法都不同的外部排行榜，所以「前三名」這個說法在兩種評測之間並不是同一把尺。

The paper is notably light on an explicit limitations section — most gaps have to be inferred from what the ablations don't cover. The EMDC neighborhood sizes K_k are described as an "engineering bandwidth heuristic," chosen without a stated theoretical link between embedding-space KNN radius and the actual perplexity/uncertainty of the LM's predictions. The three-stage curriculum is also presented as a fixed schedule without a sensitivity analysis showing how much performance would change under different step counts or ramp shapes. Evaluation leans heavily on automatic and reference-free metrics plus one blind-listening pass for CFG tuning; there's no isolation of how much of the end-to-end quality gain traces to the renderer specifically versus the upstream LM's own quality, since all comparisons use the same fixed LM. The commercial-system comparison uses a 500-song internal set plus a separate external leaderboard with different data and methodology, so the "top-three" claim isn't apples-to-apples across the two evaluations.

討論Discussion

FullDiT 最核心的貢獻，是重新框定了「聲學渲染」這件事：不再問「渲染器從完美 token 重建音訊的能力有多好」，而是問「面對上游模型實際會交出來、帶著錯誤的規劃，渲染器生成合理音訊的能力有多好」。這個重新框定的意義超越這一個系統本身——任何一個上游預測器與下游生成器之間存在訓練/推論分佈落差的管線（不只是音樂，也不限於 codec token）都會遇到同樣結構的曝光偏差問題，而 EMDC 的做法——依照實測的上游錯誤率去校準破壞比例，並用語意上說得通的替代品而非隨機雜訊——是一個相當通用、可能遷移到其他串接式生成系統（例如語音合成管線，或任何「語言模型 + 渲染器」形式的影像/影片系統）的技巧。論文自己的對照實驗（RQ1–RQ3）是它最強的資產：每一個設計選擇都對照一個只改變單一因子的控制組去測試，這種乾淨的因果切割，在往往一次改動所有東西的端對端生成模型論文裡相當少見。比較弱的一環，是論文主張自己能與商用系統匹敵的證據，建立在外部讀者難以獨立驗證或重現的指標與排行榜上，所以那些自成一體、方法嚴謹的對照實驗結果，其實比排行榜名次更值得信賴，是這篇論文真正貢獻之所在的證據。

FullDiT's core contribution is reframing acoustic rendering: instead of asking "how well does the renderer reconstruct audio from perfect tokens," the right question is "how well does it generate plausible audio from the plan the upstream model will actually hand it, errors and all." That reframing matters beyond this one system — any pipeline with a training/inference distribution mismatch between an upstream predictor and a downstream generator faces the same exposure-bias structure, and EMDC's recipe is a fairly general technique that could transfer to other cascaded generation systems. The paper's own ablations are its strongest asset: each design choice is tested against a matched control that changes only that one factor, which is exactly the kind of clean causal isolation that's rare in end-to-end generative-model papers where everything tends to be changed at once. The weaker link is that the paper's claim of competitiveness with commercial systems rests on metrics and leaderboards that are hard for outside readers to independently verify or reproduce, so the ablation results are more trustworthy evidence of the paper's actual contribution than the leaderboard placement.

為什麼挑這篇：文字轉音樂模型常常拿到很高的「準確率」——但這篇論文指出一個關鍵陷阱：如果一個模型不管有沒有被要求，本來就傾向於生成同一種常見輸出（比如四拍分組），那麼「有指定就對」的準確率其實測不出模型有沒有真的在聽指令，只是測到了它的預設偏好。論文設計了一組配對反事實實驗——同一個隨機種子下，比較「中性提示（不提屬性）」跟「明確指定某個屬性值」兩種輸出的差異，把「有出現目標」「比中性提示更好」「換一個目標值會不會真的換輸出」這三件事分開量測。最戲劇性的發現是Stable Audio 3有96.9%的中性輸出本來就是四拍分組，但明確要求四拍時反而只有56.3%命中——高準確率掩蓋了「模型根本沒在聽你要求四拍還是三拍」的事實。這種把評估方法論本身當成研究對象、專門拆穿「準確率高不代表真的可控」這個陷阱的做法很少見，故列為深度分析。Why this one: Text-to-music models often report high "accuracy" scores — but this paper flags a critical trap: if a model tends to produce the same common output (say, four-beat grouping) regardless of what it's asked for, then "correct when instructed" accuracy doesn't actually measure whether the model is listening to the instruction at all — it just measures the model's default preference. The paper designs matched counterfactual experiments — comparing, under the same random seed, a neutral prompt (no attribute stated) against explicit attribute-value treatments — and separates three distinct questions: does the target appear at all, does it improve beyond the neutral baseline, and does swapping the target value actually redirect the output. The most striking finding: Stable Audio 3 produces four-beat grouping in 96.9% of its neutral outputs, yet hits only 56.3% when four-beat is explicitly requested — high raw accuracy masking the fact that the model isn't really listening to whether you asked for three beats or four. Treating evaluation methodology itself as the object of study, specifically to expose the trap of "high accuracy doesn't mean genuinely controllable," is rare enough to warrant a deep dive.

動機Motivation

文字轉音樂系統常常會標榜自己能依照文字指令控制調性、節奏等結構性屬性，評估方式也大多是「給定一個屬性指令，檢查輸出是否符合」的單點準確率。但這個評估方式有一個結構性的漏洞：如果模型本來就傾向於輸出某種常見的預設值（比如流行音樂裡最常見的四拍分組、或是某個常見調性），那麼即使模型完全沒有理解、甚至完全忽略了文字指令，只要這個指令剛好要求的是模型的預設偏好，準確率照樣會很高。換句話說，高準確率有可能只是「模型的先驗偏好剛好跟指令對上了」，而不是「模型真的因為看懂了指令才調整輸出」。作者想解決的問題是：如何設計一套評估方法，能把「指令真的有起作用」跟「模型本來就傾向這樣輸出」這兩件事分開量測，而不是被一個容易被先驗偏好污染的單一準確率數字混為一談。

Text-to-music systems often claim to control structural attributes like key and rhythm via text instructions, and evaluation typically reduces to single-point accuracy: give an attribute instruction, check whether the output matches. But this evaluation has a structural blind spot: if a model has a strong default tendency toward some common value (say, four-beat grouping, the most common pattern in pop music, or some particular key), then even a model that completely ignores the text instruction will score high accuracy whenever the instruction happens to ask for that default. In other words, high accuracy could simply reflect "the model's prior preference happened to match the instruction," rather than "the model genuinely adjusted its output because it understood the instruction." The authors set out to design an evaluation method that separates whether an instruction is genuinely having an effect from whether the model was just going to produce that output anyway — rather than conflating the two into a single accuracy number that a strong prior can silently contaminate.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把自己定位在音樂生成評估方法論這條線上，跟既有做法最大的差異在於「反事實」設計：不是只看「指定了A、輸出是不是A」這一種條件下的結果，而是同時準備「中性提示（完全不提這個屬性）」跟「明確指定值A」「明確指定值B」三種條件，在同一個隨機種子下平行生成，再比較三者之間的差異。這樣一來就能把答案拆成三個獨立的問題：（1）出現度（Occurrence）——指定A之後，輸出裡真的有A嗎；（2）增益度（Enhancement，Δ）——指定A之後的表現，是不是真的比完全不提這個屬性時更好；（3）區辨度（Margin）——把指令從A換成B，輸出是不是真的跟著換了。論文選擇調性（key）與拍號分組（beat grouping，三拍vs四拍）作為兩個測試屬性，用S-KEY模型做調性判定、用Beat This節拍偵測器判斷每小節拍數，並且用GiantSteps、GTZAN、Ballroom、RWC Classical等既有標準資料集驗證這兩個偵測器本身的準確性，確保評估方法論不是建立在一個本身就不可靠的偵測器上。

The paper positions itself within music-generation evaluation methodology, and its key departure from prior work is the counterfactual design: rather than looking only at "asked for A, got A" under one condition, it prepares three parallel conditions — a neutral prompt (the attribute unmentioned), an explicit value-A instruction, and an explicit value-B instruction — generated under the same random seed, then compares across them. This splits the question into three independent measurements: (1) Occurrence — does the output actually contain A after A was requested; (2) Enhancement (Δ) — is performance after requesting A genuinely better than under the neutral prompt; (3) Differentiation (Margin) — does swapping the instruction from A to B actually redirect the output. The paper picks key and beat grouping (three-beat vs. four-beat) as its two test attributes, using an S-KEY model for key detection and a Beat This detector for beats-per-bar, and validates both detectors themselves against established benchmarks (GiantSteps, GTZAN, Ballroom, RWC Classical) to ensure the evaluation methodology isn't built on an unreliable detector.

方法Method

核心框架——三個互補指標：出現度（Occurrence, Acc）判斷目標是否出現在該條件的輸出裡；增益度（Enhancement, Δ）判斷指令是否讓輸出往目標方向移動、超過中性提示的表現；區辨度（Margin）判斷把目標值互換是否真的重新導向輸出。用公式表示：Δf = ½{s(y_f^a,a_f)−s(y_f^0,a_f)+s(y_f^b,b_f)−s(y_f^0,b_f)}，其中s(y,t)∈[0,1]是輸出y對目標t的評分函數，y_f^0是中性提示輸出，y_f^a、y_f^b分別是指定值a、b的輸出。

基準結構：每個系統跑256組完整「家族」（調性192組、拍號分組64組），每組家族包含中性+值A+值B三個條件，總共768個首輪輸出；三個條件在家族內共用同一組隨機種子，確保比較的公平性；另外抽出48組家族當作哨兵子集，用兩組額外的隨機種子重複測試，檢驗結果對種子的穩健度。評估視窗設定在偵測到音樂起始後的前30秒，短於15秒的片段直接判0分。

評分方法：調性用S-KEY模型判斷精確的主音-調式一致性（主要指標），並輔以MIREX式的分級給分（五度關係給0.5分、關係大小調給0.3分、平行大小調給0.2分）；拍號分組用Beat This偵測器追蹤每個小節內的拍點數，用眾數決定該片段是三拍還是四拍分組。

統計方法：主要估計量是「家族在固定基準情境下的平均反應」，用2,000次自助重抽（種子0）算百分位信賴區間；跨模型比較用20,000次配對重抽（種子20260721）；並對類型（genre）分層重抽以維持各分層樣本數。判定「有效控制」的規則是：兩個95%信賴區間都必須排除零——同時要求相對中性提示的增益度為正、以及目標值互換後的區辨度為正（拍號分組則用該目標值專屬的Δ）。

外部驗證：調性偵測器在GiantSteps EDM上精確準確率0.506、MIREX式準確率0.626；在GTZAN多曲風上精確準確率0.656、MIREX式準確率0.765。拍號偵測器在Ballroom舞曲資料集上平衡準確率達0.998、RWC Classical上0.734、GTZAN-Rhythm上0.806。另外做了60個片段、每片段3位評分者的人工聽測稽核：調性判斷跟多數決的一致率是0.796（精確）/0.850（MIREX式），拍號分組一致率0.745；並抽出28組家族（84個片段）重新讓人工標註，比較自動評分跟人工評分算出的Δ差異——調性的差異是−0.143 [−0.250,−0.036]，拍號分組的差異則剛好是零。

安慰劑對照：用跟屬性完全無關的指令去重新評分同一段音訊（比如拿「三拍」偵測器去評分「四拍指令」產生的音訊），三拍的安慰劑效應只有+0.003到+0.008（相對真實效應的+0.250到+0.469），四拍的安慰劑效應是−0.060（相對真實效應的−0.406），證明前述的效應大小不是偵測器雜訊造成的假象。

三個受測系統：ACE-Step 1.5用原生結構化欄位輸入調性/拍號；Stable Audio 3跟LeVo2用開放文字輸入，在基礎提示詞後面附加「A major」「4/4」這類文字。全部系統輸出都是純器樂、30秒片段。

Core framework — three complementary metrics: Occurrence (Acc) checks whether the target appears in that condition's output; Enhancement (Δ) checks whether the instruction moves the output toward the target beyond the neutral-prompt baseline; Differentiation (Margin) checks whether swapping the target value genuinely redirects the output. Formally: Δf = ½{s(y_f^a,a_f)−s(y_f^0,a_f)+s(y_f^b,b_f)−s(y_f^0,b_f)}, where s(y,t)∈[0,1] scores output y against target t, y_f^0 is the neutral-prompt output, and y_f^a, y_f^b are the outputs under explicit values a and b.

Benchmark structure: each system runs 256 complete "families" (192 for key, 64 for beat grouping), each family containing neutral + value-A + value-B conditions, for 768 first-pass outputs total; the three conditions within a family share one random seed to keep the comparison fair; a stratified 48-family sentinel subset is additionally repeated with two extra seeds to check robustness. The evaluation window is the first 30 seconds after the detected musical onset; clips shorter than 15 seconds score zero automatically.

Scoring: key uses an S-KEY model for exact tonic-mode agreement (the primary metric), supplemented by MIREX-style graded scoring (0.5 for fifth relations, 0.3 for relative major/minor, 0.2 for parallel major/minor); beat grouping uses a Beat This detector tracking beats per bar, with the modal count determining three- vs. four-beat grouping.

Statistics: the primary estimand is the family's mean response over the frozen benchmark contexts, using 2,000 bootstrap resamples (seed 0) for percentile confidence intervals; cross-model comparisons use 20,000 paired resamples (seed 20260721); genre-stratified resampling preserves stratum sizes. The rule for "effective control" requires both 95% confidence intervals to exclude zero — positive enhancement over neutral AND positive value differentiation (or the target-specific Δ, for beat grouping).

External validation: the key detector scores 0.506 exact / 0.626 MIREX-style on GiantSteps EDM, and 0.656 exact / 0.765 MIREX-style on GTZAN multi-genre. The beat detector scores 0.998 balanced accuracy on Ballroom, 0.734 on RWC Classical, and 0.806 on GTZAN-Rhythm. A human audit of 60 clips (3 raters each) found agreement with majority vote of 0.796 (exact) / 0.850 (MIREX-style) for key and 0.745 for beat grouping; a follow-up re-labeling of 28 families (84 clips) compared automatic vs. human-derived Δ — the difference was −0.143 [−0.250,−0.036] for key and exactly zero for beat grouping.

Placebo controls: rescoring the same audio with an instruction irrelevant to the attribute being measured (e.g. scoring "four-beat instruction" audio with a three-beat detector) yields placebo effects of only +0.003 to +0.008 for three-beat (versus the real +0.250 to +0.469) and −0.060 for four-beat (versus the real −0.406), confirming the reported effect sizes aren't detector-noise artifacts.

Three tested systems: ACE-Step 1.5 uses native structured fields for key/beat input; Stable Audio 3 and LeVo2 use open text, appending phrases like "A major" or "4/4" after a base prompt. All systems output instrumental-only, 30-second clips.

Main results (key, 192 families): ACE-Step 1.5 — treatment agreement 0.667, Δ=0.612 [0.563,0.659], Margin=0.628; Stable Audio 3 — treatment agreement 0.674, Δ=0.646 [0.599,0.693], Margin=0.635; LeVo2 — treatment agreement only 0.055, Δ=0.026 [0.003,0.049], Margin=0.021, essentially no key control at all.

Main results (beat grouping, 64 families): three-beat target — ACE-Step neutral 0.016 / treatment 0.266 (Δ=0.250 [0.156,0.344]); Stable Audio 3 neutral 0.016 / treatment 0.484 (Δ=0.469 [0.344,0.594]); LeVo2 neutral 0.047 / treatment 0.031 (Δ=−0.016 [−0.063,0.031], effectively no response). Four-beat target — ACE-Step neutral 0.812 / treatment 0.859 (Δ=0.047 [0.000,0.109], weakly positive); Stable Audio 3 neutral 0.969 / treatment 0.563 (Δ=−0.406 [−0.516,−0.297] — explicit instruction actively makes the output worse!); LeVo2 neutral 0.906 / treatment 0.859 (Δ=−0.047 [−0.109,0.016]).

Cross-genre results: ACE-Step's key Δ is fairly even across genres — classical 0.625, jazz 0.573, pop 0.563, rock 0.688; Stable Audio 3 shows classical 0.729, jazz 0.635, pop 0.698, rock 0.521, notably weaker on rock; LeVo2's key Δ sits between 0.010 and 0.042 across every genre, confirming its near-total loss of key control isn't genre-specific.

Three-seed sentinel: key Δ preserves the same ordering across all three seeds — Stable Audio 3 > ACE-Step > LeVo2; Stable Audio 3's target-specific beat Δ keeps opposite signs in every seed (roughly +0.417 for three-beat, −0.479 for four-beat), confirming the main results aren't an artifact of a single seed.

限制Limitations

作者自己列出六項限制。第一是因果範圍：這套方法評估的是系統釋出時的端對端使用者介面行為，並不能推論到模型內部架構層面的因果機制。第二是「載體」混淆：中性提示不是單純把屬性資訊拿掉，同時也改變了文字或欄位裡承載這個資訊的方式，這個「載體與數值之間的交互作用」目前沒有被因子化實驗設計隔離開來。第三是偵測誤差：拍號分組的效應接近零時，有可能只是偵測靈敏度的天花板，而不是模型真的完全沒反應（相對地，調性的判斷結果在多種驗證方法下都很穩健，比較不受這個問題影響）。第四是主要結果只用單一種子跑完整基準矩陣，哨兵子集提供的是「已觀測到的重複樣本」的穩健性檢驗，不是對所有可能種子的全面平均。第五是屬性範圍侷限：只評估了全域大小調跟三/四拍分組，沒有涵蓋調式轉換、局部修改、複合或不規則拍號、和弦進行等更複雜的結構屬性。第六，中性提示的建構方式對「類別型」屬性（比如大調/小調）很自然，但如果要評估連續型的控制變數，可能需要另外設計參照點（比如鄰近數值或分布配對的載體），這套方法目前沒有處理連續變數的版本。

The authors list six explicit limitations. First, causal scope: the method evaluates end-to-end released-interface behavior, not internal architectural causal mechanisms. Second, carrier confounding: the neutral prompt doesn't merely remove attribute information — it also changes how that information would have been carried in the text or field, and this carrier-value interaction hasn't been isolated via a full factorial design. Third, recognition error: near-zero beat-grouping effects could reflect the detector's sensitivity floor rather than genuine model non-response (key judgments, by contrast, stay robust across multiple validation methods and are less exposed to this issue). Fourth, the full benchmark matrix uses a single seed as its primary run; the sentinel subset provides a robustness check on observed replicates, not a comprehensive average over all possible seeds. Fifth, attribute scope: only global major/minor key and three-vs-four-beat grouping were evaluated, excluding modulation, local edits, compound or irregular meters, and chord progressions. Sixth, the neutral-prompt construction is natural for categorical attributes (e.g. major vs. minor) but evaluating continuous control variables would likely require an alternative reference point (a neighboring value or a distribution-matched carrier) — the method has no version for continuous variables yet.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有力的貢獻不是「哪個模型比較好」的排名，而是拆穿了一個評估方法論的陷阱：Stable Audio 3在四拍目標上呈現的高原始命中率（0.812中性→0.859指定，看似很正常），跟同一個模型在四拍上的真實表現（0.969中性→0.563指定，Δ=−0.406，指定反而讓命中率暴跌）完全是兩碼事。如果評估者只看「指定A之後輸出裡有沒有A」這個單一數字，Stable Audio 3看起來在四拍控制上做得不錯；但反事實設計一揭穿，事實是這個模型在完全沒被要求的情況下，96.9%的時候就已經自然生成四拍分組——所以「指定後有0.563命中」不是因為模型聽懂了指令，而是即使指令把它導向別的方向，它仍然有一半以上的機率因為強烈的預設偏好而生成四拍。這正是論文開頭想解決的問題的具體實例：高準確率可以完全是先驗偏好的產物，跟「真的有在聽指令」毫無關係。三個模型之間的對比也很有教育意義：ACE-Step跟Stable Audio 3在調性上都展現出紮實的、通過「增益度」與「區辨度」雙重門檻的控制能力，但LeVo2的調性Δ在全部曲風下都逼近零，說明它的調性控制基本上是裝飾性的、對文字指令幾乎無感。安慰劑對照、外部偵測器驗證、人工聽測稽核、跨種子哨兵這四層驗證疊在一起，讓論文能夠很有信心地說「這些效應量是真的，不是量測雜訊」——這種把評估方法論本身武裝到近乎無懈可擊的程度、再拿去戳破業界常見的「準確率高=可控」直覺的做法，對後續任何想評測生成式音樂模型可控性的工作，都提供了一套可以直接套用的檢驗清單。

The paper's strongest contribution isn't a leaderboard ranking but exposing an evaluation-methodology trap: Stable Audio 3's raw hit rate on the four-beat target (0.812 neutral → 0.859 treatment, which looks unremarkable) is a completely different story from the same model's real behavior on four-beat (0.969 neutral → 0.563 treatment, Δ=−0.406 — explicit instruction actively tanks the hit rate). If an evaluator looked only at "does A appear after A was requested," Stable Audio 3 would look like it handles four-beat control reasonably well; but the counterfactual design reveals that this model already produces four-beat grouping 96.9% of the time with zero instruction — so a 0.563 hit rate after explicit instruction isn't evidence of listening to the instruction, it's evidence that even when the instruction points elsewhere, the model's strong prior still wins the coin flip more than half the time. This is precisely the concrete instance of the problem the paper opens with: high accuracy can be purely a product of prior preference, unrelated to whether the instruction is actually being heard. The cross-model contrast is instructive too: both ACE-Step and Stable Audio 3 show genuine key control that clears both the enhancement and differentiation bars, while LeVo2's key Δ sits near zero across every genre — its key control is essentially decorative, near-insensitive to the text instruction. Stacking placebo controls, external detector validation, human listening audits, and cross-seed sentinels lets the paper state with real confidence that these effect sizes are genuine rather than measurement noise — and armoring an evaluation methodology this thoroughly, specifically to puncture the common industry intuition that "high accuracy equals controllability," hands anyone evaluating generative-music controllability a directly reusable checklist.

為什麼挑這篇：完整歌曲生成系統（DiffRhythm、YuE、LeVo、ACE-Step等）近年愈趨成熟，但既有評分模型大多把音訊直接映成分數，看不出判斷依據。MuseCritic反過來要求模型先寫出涵蓋五個美學面向的自然語言評語，再用評語當中介表徵去預測分數，並用兩階段訓練（教師示範評語做SFT，再用自我生成的評語做獎勵學習）解決評語分布偏移問題。消融實驗把「拿掉評語」「用外部教師評語而非自我生成」「拿掉SFT」三種變體都跑過，分別讓MSE從0.2316惡化到0.5005、0.5481、0.7541，把「評語為何有用」量化到具體數字，是一篇把可解釋性跟獎勵建模數字扎實地綁在一起的論文，故列為深度分析。Why this one: Full-song generation systems (DiffRhythm, YuE, LeVo, ACE-Step, etc.) have matured quickly, but existing scoring models mostly map audio straight to a number with no visible reasoning. MuseCritic instead makes the model write a natural-language critique across five aesthetic dimensions first, then uses that critique as an intermediate representation to predict scores, using a two-stage pipeline (SFT on teacher critiques, then reward learning on the model's own self-generated critiques) to fix a critique-distribution-shift problem. Three ablations — removing the critique, using an external teacher's critiques instead of self-generated ones, and removing the SFT stage — degrade macro MSE from 0.2316 to 0.5005, 0.5481, and 0.7541 respectively, turning "why does the critique help" into concrete numbers. That's a rare case of interpretability being tied directly to reward-modeling metrics, hence flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

隨著完整歌曲生成系統（DiffRhythm、YuE、LeVo、ACE-Step、Muse等）日趨成熟，能生成長達數分鐘、具備段落結構與人聲協調的完整歌曲，「怎麼幫這些歌曲打分數」變成核心瓶頸，尤其是要用來做強化學習的獎勵訊號時更是如此。現有評分器（MuLan、MuQ-MuLan做文字-音樂對應，PAM做音質，MusicEval，Audiobox Aesthetics，以及SongEval定義的五個美學面向）幾乎都是「音訊進、分數出」的黑箱，即使SongEval把評分拆成五個面向，也僅止於分數預測，看不出模型是聽到了什麼具體現象才給出這個分數。作者認為，如果評分模型能像人類樂評一樣先講出「這裡的問題是什麼」再打分，不但分數本身更可信，這段評語還能反過來當作預測分數的證據，讓整個獎勵訊號變得可檢視。

As full-song generation systems (DiffRhythm, YuE, LeVo, ACE-Step, Muse, etc.) mature to the point of producing multi-minute songs with coherent structure and vocal coordination, how to score these songs becomes the central bottleneck — especially when the score is meant to serve as a reinforcement-learning reward signal. Existing evaluators (MuLan/MuQ-MuLan for text-music correspondence, PAM for audio quality, MusicEval, Audiobox Aesthetics, and SongEval's five aesthetic dimensions) are almost all audio-in-score-out black boxes; even SongEval, which splits scoring into five dimensions, still only predicts numbers with no visibility into what specific audible phenomenon drove the score. The authors argue that if a scoring model first states, like a human critic, "here's what's going on" before assigning a number, the score becomes more trustworthy and the critique itself can serve as inspectable evidence behind the reward signal.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把相關研究分成兩條線。第一條是文字轉歌曲生成：近年的系統能一次生成涵蓋主歌副歌、具備人聲與伴奏協調的完整曲目，隨著這條線愈趨成熟，「怎麼可靠評分、怎麼提供適合強化學習用的獎勵訊號」變成核心挑戰。第二條是獎勵建模與音樂美學評估：MuLan、MuQ-MuLan關注文字與音樂的對應程度，PAM評的是音質，MusicEval跟Audiobox Aesthetics涵蓋更廣的美學面向，SongEval則定義了五個美學面向的評分標準，但這些方法「仍然只做分數預測」。MuseCritic把「評語生成」的能力接到音訊語言模型上，讓明確的美學證據能參與到獎勵預測的過程裡，這是它相對於SongEval等既有方法的核心差異。

The paper divides related work into two threads. The first is text-to-song generation: recent systems can generate a full track in one pass, spanning verse-chorus structure with coordinated vocals and accompaniment; as this thread matures, reliable scoring and RL-suitable reward signals become the central challenge. The second is reward modeling and music aesthetic evaluation: MuLan/MuQ-MuLan target text-music correspondence, PAM targets audio quality, MusicEval and Audiobox Aesthetics cover a broader range of aesthetic dimensions, and SongEval defines a five-dimension aesthetic rubric — but these methods "still target score prediction alone." MuseCritic attaches critique-generation capability to an audio language model so that explicit aesthetic evidence can participate in reward prediction, which is its core departure from SongEval and similar prior approaches.

方法Method

問題設定：模型輸入是歌曲音訊x、評估提示p，輸出是涵蓋K=5個面向、範圍[1,5]的專家標註分數y*。模型分成共用骨幹參數θ的兩個部分：評語生成器g_c = h_LM∘f_θ，美學評分器g_s = h_RM∘f_θ。跟直接把音訊映成分數不同，MuseCritic的評分器同時以歌曲、評分準則、以及評語c為條件：ŷ = g_s(x,p,c)，訓練目標是最小化MSE：ℒ_MSE = (1/|S|K)Σ‖g_s(x,p,c)−y*‖²。

兩階段訓練管線：第一階段（SFT）先用Gemini-3-Pro觀看每首歌、評分準則與專家平均分數，產生離線評語D_off，模型透過交叉熵損失ℒ_SFT學習複製這些教師評語。訓練設定：學習率5×10⁻⁵，在2,199首歌上跑一個epoch，四張H200，批次大小32（梯度累積）。第二階段（獎勵學習）讓SFT後的模型自己對所有訓練歌曲生成評語D_on，獎勵頭與骨幹的LoRA（rank 8、scaling 32）一起訓練，目標是ℒ_MC = ℒ_MSE(D_on; g_s)。訓練設定：學習率2×10⁻⁴，權重衰減0.1，十個epoch，硬體同上。作者強調用「模型自己生成的評語」而非外部教師評語來訓練獎勵頭，是為了緩解「訓練時看到的評語分布」跟「推論時模型自己會產生的評語分布」之間的落差。

實驗設定：骨幹是MOSS-Audio-8B-Instruct，訓練用2,199首SongEval歌曲（種子42打亂），留200首做域內測試，並用Music Arena（733組偏好配對）做域外測試。基線包含Gemini-3.1-Pro（LLM當裁判）、用相同資料切分重訓的SongEval（UTMOS架構）、以及Music Arena上的SongEval官方權重、Audiobox Aesthetics、Qwen3-Omni。評估指標：域內用MSE、Pearson LCC、Spearman SRCC、Kendall's τ（逐面向計算）；域外用嚴格意義的成對準確率（平手算錯）；下游則用GRPO訓練Muse-0.6B（500筆多輪範例，305英文、195中文），獎勵函數是r(x̃) = (1/K)Σŷ_k(x̃)，在SongEval五個面向與Audiobox Aesthetics四個面向共九項指標上量測生成品質的變化。

域內結果（Table 1）：MuseCritic在五個面向的MSE都低於SongEval，例如Coherence從0.2605降到0.2058、Musicality從0.2913降到0.2202，巨集平均MSE從SongEval的0.2875降到0.2316；巨集平均Pearson LCC達0.9068、Spearman SRCC達0.8838、Kendall's τ達0.7178。Gemini-3.1-Pro的巨集MSE高達1.3061，說明「沒有經過調適的LLM當裁判」沒辦法可靠地複製五維專家判斷。域外Music Arena準確率：MuseCritic 71.35%、SongEval 70.80%、Audiobox Aesthetics 68.49%、Qwen3-Omni 53.75%。下游GRPO訓練後，Muse-GRPO在全部九項Audiobox Aesthetics與SongEval指標上都優於原始Muse（例如Audiobox Content Enjoyment從7.33升到7.45、SongEval Coherence從4.01升到4.07）。

Problem formulation: the model takes song audio x and an evaluation prompt p, and outputs expert-annotated scores y* over K=5 dimensions in [1,5]. It splits into two heads sharing backbone parameters θ: a critique generator g_c = h_LM∘f_θ and an aesthetic scorer g_s = h_RM∘f_θ. Unlike mapping audio directly to a score, MuseCritic's scorer is jointly conditioned on the song, the rubric, and a critique c: ŷ = g_s(x,p,c), trained to minimize ℒ_MSE = (1/|S|K)Σ‖g_s(x,p,c)−y*‖².

Two-stage training pipeline: Stage I (SFT) uses Gemini-3-Pro to observe each song, rubric, and expert mean rating and produce offline critiques D_off; the model learns to reproduce these teacher critiques via cross-entropy loss ℒ_SFT. Settings: learning rate 5×10⁻⁵, one epoch over 2,199 songs, four H200 GPUs, batch size 32 (accumulated). Stage II (reward learning) has the SFT'd model generate its own critiques D_on for every training song, and jointly trains a reward head with backbone LoRA (rank 8, scaling 32) on ℒ_MC = ℒ_MSE(D_on; g_s). Settings: learning rate 2×10⁻⁴, weight decay 0.1, ten epochs, same hardware. The authors emphasize training the reward head on the model's own self-generated critiques, rather than the external teacher's, specifically to close the gap between the critique distribution seen during training and the one the model itself produces at inference.

Experimental setup: the backbone is MOSS-Audio-8B-Instruct, trained on 2,199 SongEval songs (shuffled, seed 42), with 200 held out for in-domain testing and Music Arena (733 preference pairs) for out-of-domain testing. Baselines: Gemini-3.1-Pro (LLM-as-a-Judge), a SongEval (UTMOS architecture) retrained on the same split, and on Music Arena, SongEval's official weights, Audiobox Aesthetics, and Qwen3-Omni. Metrics: in-domain MSE/Pearson LCC/Spearman SRCC/Kendall's τ per dimension; out-of-domain strict pairwise accuracy (ties count as errors); and a downstream GRPO run training Muse-0.6B on 500 multi-turn examples (305 English, 195 Chinese) with reward r(x̃) = (1/K)Σŷ_k(x̃), measured across nine SongEval/Audiobox Aesthetics metrics.

In-domain results (Table 1): MuseCritic beats SongEval's MSE on all five dimensions (e.g. Coherence 0.2605→0.2058, Musicality 0.2913→0.2202), with macro MSE dropping from SongEval's 0.2875 to 0.2316; macro Pearson LCC reaches 0.9068, Spearman SRCC 0.8838, Kendall's τ 0.7178. Gemini-3.1-Pro's macro MSE is 1.3061, showing an unadapted LLM-as-a-Judge cannot reliably reproduce five-dimensional expert judgments. Out-of-domain Music Arena accuracy: MuseCritic 71.35%, SongEval 70.80%, Audiobox Aesthetics 68.49%, Qwen3-Omni 53.75%. After downstream GRPO training, Muse-GRPO beats the original Muse on all nine Audiobox Aesthetics/SongEval metrics (e.g. Audiobox Content Enjoyment 7.33→7.45, SongEval Coherence 4.01→4.07).

限制Limitations

作者自己列出的限制集中在兩處。第一，計算成本：「先生成評語再預測分數」的流程需要自迴歸地生成評語，比直接做分數回歸昂貴得多，這對要大量取樣的強化學習訓練迴圈是個實際負擔。第二，資料侷限：同時具備音訊與專家美學評分的長篇歌曲資料集非常稀缺，MuseCritic主要在SongEval上訓練，而SongEval以中文與英文人聲歌曲為主，這限制了模型對「更廣泛語言與曲風」的類推能力——換句話說，這套方法目前驗證過的範圍，基本上就是中英文流行人聲歌曲，遇到器樂曲、其他語言曲種或非流行風格，表現如何完全沒有被檢驗過。消融實驗雖然把「評語」「自我生成」「SFT」三個因素的貢獻都拆解得很清楚，但這些拆解本身都是在同一個SongEval資料分布內做的，沒有回答「換一個完全不同的評分準則或音樂文化，這套流程還能不能成立」這個更大的問題。

The authors' own limitations concentrate on two points. First, computational cost: the critique-then-score procedure requires autoregressive critique generation before reward prediction, making it substantially more expensive than direct score regression — a real burden for RL training loops that need heavy sampling. Second, data scarcity: long-form song datasets pairing audio with expert aesthetic ratings remain scarce, so MuseCritic is trained primarily on SongEval, which itself skews toward Chinese and English vocal songs — limiting how well the model's judgments generalize to a broader range of languages and genres. In other words, everything validated here is essentially Chinese/English pop vocal music; how it performs on instrumental pieces, other languages, or non-pop genres is untested. And while the ablations cleanly isolate the contribution of the critique, self-generation, and SFT, all three ablations still run inside the same SongEval data distribution — they don't answer the larger question of whether this pipeline holds up under a completely different rubric or musical culture.

討論Discussion

三組消融實驗把「評語為什麼有用」拆解成具體數字。拿掉評語（直接從音訊預測分數）讓巨集MSE從0.2316惡化到0.5005，而且拿掉評語後的預測「會擠在一個狹窄的高分區間」，顯示評語迫使模型真正區辨出歌曲之間的差異，而不是無腦地給出接近滿分的分數。把自我生成的評語換成外部教師（Gemini-3-Pro）的離線評語，巨集MSE從0.2316惡化到0.5481，LCC/SRCC/KTAU從(0.9068, 0.8838, 0.7178)掉到(0.8154, 0.7585, 0.5685)，驗證了「評語分布偏移」確實是真實存在、而且代價不小的問題。拿掉SFT階段直接做自我生成，巨集MSE惡化到0.7541，LCC/SRCC/KTAU掉到(0.6695, 0.6660, 0.5008)，說明「單靠自我生成、沒有教師示範打底」不足以產生可靠的中介表徵——SFT階段提供的並不只是起點，而是讓後續自我生成評語具備品質下限的必要條件。整體來看，MuseCritic在域內（SongEval）、域外（Music Arena）、以及下游GRPO訓練三種情境下都一致地優於或至少持平既有方法，證明「把自然語言評語當成分數預測的中介表徵」這個做法本身是有效的，而不只是換了個更大的骨幹模型帶來的提升。它把「可解釋性」跟「獎勵訊號品質」直接綁在同一組數字上，對後續想用LLM當音樂評審或獎勵模型的工作，提供了一個具體可比較的參照點。

The three ablations decompose "why does the critique help" into concrete numbers. Removing the critique (predicting scores directly from audio) worsens macro MSE from 0.2316 to 0.5005, and without it predictions cluster in a narrow high-score region — showing the critique forces the model to genuinely discriminate between songs rather than defaulting to near-perfect scores. Swapping self-generated critiques for the external teacher's (Gemini-3-Pro) offline critiques worsens macro MSE to 0.5481 and drops LCC/SRCC/KTAU from (0.9068, 0.8838, 0.7178) to (0.8154, 0.7585, 0.5685), confirming that critique-distribution shift is a real and costly problem. Removing the SFT stage and relying on self-generation alone worsens macro MSE to 0.7541 and LCC/SRCC/KTAU to (0.6695, 0.6660, 0.5008), showing that self-generation without a teacher-demonstrated starting point cannot produce a reliable intermediate representation — SFT isn't just a warm start, it's a precondition for the self-generated critiques that follow to meet a quality floor. Taken together, MuseCritic consistently matches or beats existing methods across in-domain (SongEval), out-of-domain (Music Arena), and downstream GRPO training settings, demonstrating that treating natural-language critique as an intermediate representation for score prediction is itself the source of the gain, not merely a bigger backbone. By tying interpretability directly to reward-signal quality in one set of numbers, it offers a concrete, comparable reference point for future work using LLMs as music judges or reward models.

為什麼挑這篇：生成式音樂模型幾乎都用西方等律音樂訓練，遇到阿拉伯調式（maqam）這種微分音、以呼應式（call-and-response）為核心的傳統就顯得力不從心；串流式文字轉音樂模型（如Google Lyria RealTime）雖然能即時生成，卻沒有精準的控制介面。這篇論文不去微調模型，而是設計一個知識庫驅動的「演奏→提示詞」即時編譯器，把MIDI、手勢、推斷出的和聲即時編譯成不斷更新的文字提示，用純提示工程去引導一個完全沒改動過的商用生成模型演奏六種阿拉伯調式。它的評估相當紮實：量測了逐次重新提示的延遲（中位數263毫秒）、8.5分鐘實測session零掉線、還做了「有無調式知識」的消融實驗直接量化微分音的呈現率，是少見把即時互動系統的工程細節攤開來檢驗的論文，因此標記為深度分析。Why this one: Generative music models are trained almost entirely on Western equal-tempered music, leaving them ill-equipped for Arabic maqam traditions built on microtonal intervals and call-and-response phrasing; streaming text-to-music models (like Google Lyria RealTime) can generate in real time but lack precise control interfaces. Rather than fine-tuning the model, this paper builds a knowledge-based "performance-to-prompt" real-time compiler that turns live MIDI, gesture, and inferred harmony into continuously updated text prompts, steering an entirely unmodified commercial generator through six Arabic maqamat using pure prompt engineering. The evaluation is unusually rigorous: it measures re-prompting latency (263ms median), reports zero dropped connections across an 8.5-minute live session, and runs a grounding-on/off ablation that directly quantifies how much microtonal content actually appears in the output — a rare case of an interactive real-time system's engineering details being laid bare for scrutiny, hence flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

阿拉伯調式（maqam）音樂具有微分音（例如bayati調式裡「半降B」的第二音級，被作者稱為「這個調式表現力的靈魂」）、調式性、以及裝飾性的呼應式（call-and-response）結構，但生成式音樂模型的訓練框架幾乎全部建立在西方、等律的音樂傳統上，是目前最被忽視的音樂傳統之一。即時伴奏更放大了這個落差：一個AI夥伴必須即時聆聽、動態適應，同時尊重這種微分音的調式結構。串流式文字轉音樂模型（如Google Lyria RealTime）提供了強大的生成能力，卻缺乏精準的控制介面——使用者能做的往往只是換一句提示詞，無法逐拍、逐樂句地即時調整。作者選擇的路線很清楚：不對模型微調（訓練資料有限、算力成本高），而是把自然語言本身當成一個即時控制迴路的「致動器」（actuator），透過持續即時編譯的文字提示去操控一個完全沒動過的既有生成模型。

Arabic maqam music is microtonal, modal, and built on ornamented call-and-response phrasing — for instance, the half-flat second degree in bayati, which the authors call "the expressive soul" of that mode — yet generative music models' training frameworks are built almost entirely on Western, equal-tempered traditions, making maqam one of the most underserved musical traditions today. Real-time accompaniment sharpens this gap further: an AI partner must listen and adapt dynamically while still respecting these microtonal modal structures. Streaming text-to-music models (like Google Lyria RealTime) offer strong generative capability but lack a precise control interface — users can typically only swap in a new prompt, with no way to adjust phrase-by-phrase or beat-by-beat in real time. The authors' chosen path is deliberate: rather than fine-tuning the model (limited training data, high compute cost), they treat natural language itself as the actuator of a real-time control loop, steering an entirely unmodified existing generator through continuously and dynamically compiled text prompts.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把自己放在五條研究脈絡的交會點上。第一是知識型符號系統（如CHORAL）：把音樂專業知識編碼成明確規則、確定性地套用，MazzikaAI保留了這種「可檢視」的特性，但把符號式的合成換成了神經網路生成。第二是互動式與共同創作系統（Voyager、The Continuator）：透過輪流交替協商演奏的主導權，MazzikaAI用語言媒介的控制複製了這個模式。第三是神經音樂生成：涵蓋符號模型（Music Transformer）、音訊模型（MusicLM、MusicGen）、以及串流變體（Lyria RealTime）——這些串流模型雖然接受session中途更新提示，卻沒有自動的即時操控機制。第四是「提示詞作為控制」：在NLP脈絡下這通常是一次性的操作，MazzikaAI把它擴充成「連續閉迴路」的音訊生成控制，提示詞的角色從靜態查詢變成即時致動器。第五是非西方調式音樂的電腦運算研究：過去多半聚焦在分析而非生成，微分音音程與調式結構在訓練資料與控制詞彙裡都嚴重不足。這五條線索加總起來，定義了MazzikaAI試圖填補的具體缺口：一個不需要訓練資料、不需要微調、卻能精準控制非西方調式即時生成的方法。

The paper positions itself at the intersection of five research threads. First, knowledge-based symbolic systems (e.g. CHORAL) encoded musical expertise as explicit rules applied deterministically; MazzikaAI keeps this inspectability while swapping symbolic synthesis for neural generation. Second, interactive and co-creative systems (Voyager, The Continuator) negotiated performance agency through turn-taking; MazzikaAI replicates this pattern using language-mediated control. Third, neural music generation spans symbolic models (Music Transformer), audio models (MusicLM, MusicGen), and streaming variants (Lyria RealTime) — these streaming models accept mid-session prompt updates but lack any automatic steering mechanism. Fourth, prompting as control typically operates one-shot in NLP contexts; MazzikaAI extends this into continuous closed-loop audio-generation control, turning the prompt from a static query into a real-time actuator. Fifth, computational work on non-Western modal music has focused mostly on analysis rather than generation, with microtonal intervals and modal structure badly underrepresented in both training data and control vocabularies. Together these five threads define the specific gap MazzikaAI targets: a method that needs no training data and no fine-tuning, yet still precisely controls real-time generation of non-Western modal music.

方法Method

系統架構分三層：瀏覽器端（React/TypeScript）擷取MIDI鍵盤輸入、手勢（用MediaPipe偵測）、語音指令、以及樂譜（用Gemini做OMR），並在本地無延遲地渲染演奏者自己的鋼琴音；FastAPI後端維持一個128個音符事件的滾動視窗，負責編譯提示詞、協調兩個獨立的Lyria RealTime串流session；雲端生成模型端則用兩個解耦的串流引擎——一個負責旋律的伴奏（關閉鼓組、不設固定BPM），另一個是節奏固定的打擊樂層。作者的理由是「旋律伴奏跟節奏層遵循不同的時鐘」：旋律引擎需要動態地重新提示，打擊樂則要維持節拍固定的一致性，硬把兩者塞進同一個提示詞會強迫兩邊互相妥協。

狀態機與控制流程：伴奏遵循四狀態策略——Supporting（安靜地在獨奏者底下伴奏，提示詞服從度最高）、Responding（樂句結束後開啟0.3到2.5秒的回應窗口，逐字複誦獨奏者剛才彈的音高）、Sustain（單一音符持續超過2.5秒觸發最小化伴奏）、LongIdle（超過5秒靜默且有段落啟用時，樂隊自由主導）。兩個閘門管理狀態轉換：暖機閘門要求段落啟用後至少彈兩個音符才會發聲；靜默停止閘門在超過4秒安靜後停掉旋律串流。

表演狀態估計（工作記憶）：後端維護的特徵包含音域/力度（音區高低、織度提示、運動方向、演奏速度、力度弧線）、樂句結構（用靜默驅動分段、計算樂句輪次、記錄上一句音高供回應使用）、以及和聲（即時和弦模板比對、十二小節藍調段落推斷、以2秒視窗、2半音顆粒度計算「和聲指紋」）。這個和聲指紋是專門設計來穩定閘門的：它取代了原本「每個音符觸發就改變」的舊版key，那個版本在旋律快速跑動時會不斷重新提示、造成串流不穩定。

控制器與生成參數：每個伴奏狀態對應一組風格化的參數向量（溫度、top-k、guidance、密度、亮度）。以藍調Supporting狀態為例：溫度0.60、top-k 12、guidance 7.0、密度0.22；LongIdle用最高的溫度與密度（樂隊自由即興），Supporting則用最低的溫度與密度、最高的guidance（樂隊保持忠實而不搶戲）。

知識庫：整個系統把手動編寫的音樂知識完全以可讀的文字與調校過的常數呈現，編譯器本身有2,434行Python程式碼，包含15種調式描述（6種核心阿拉伯調式：bayati、rast、hijaz、nahawand、saba、kurd，加上9種西方音階，每個條目詳述音名拼寫、特徵音級、裝飾音、終止音、色彩描述、以及「避免落入西方調性」的負面引導）、38個樂隊段落（每段落3種角色，共114種角色描述）、9種曲風設定（段落順序、樂器主導偏好、打擊樂型態）、135組生成參數（9種曲風 × 3狀態 × 4個旋鈕 + 3個亮度值）、以及和聲規則（音程模板式和弦辨識、十二小節段落推斷、四層藍調和聲鎖優先序）。

提示詞合成：編譯器把提示詞組裝成有序的子句串接：[樂器規則/靜默] | [禁止打擊樂] | [特定標頭] | [場次階段] | [調式描述] | [和聲脈絡/回應] | [角色分配] | [音區] | [力度] | [空間子句]。調式描述會明確地把微分音音級用拼音方式寫出（例如「E半降」），並附上負面引導：「避免西方大小調性，維持在bayati調式世界裡」。角色分層與回應機制會把上一句的音高轉譯成具體的回應指令：「用相同的音高回應、帶藍調風格：滑進第一個音、用藍調式的推弦」。

System architecture has three tiers: a browser client (React/TypeScript) captures MIDI keyboard input, hand gestures (via MediaPipe), voice commands, and sheet music (via Gemini OMR), rendering the performer's own piano locally with no network latency; a FastAPI backend maintains a rolling 128-note-event window, compiles prompts, and orchestrates two independent Lyria RealTime sessions; and on the cloud generation side, two decoupled streaming engines run — one melodic companion (drums muted, no fixed BPM) and one tempo-locked percussion bed. The authors' rationale: "melodic accompaniment and rhythmic bed obey different clocks" — the melodic engine needs dynamic re-prompting while the percussion needs metronomic consistency, and forcing both into one prompt would force a compromise on each.

State machine and control flow: accompaniment follows a four-state policy — Supporting (quiet comping beneath the soloist at highest prompt adherence), Responding (a 0.3-2.5s answer window opens after a phrase ends, literally echoing the soloist's just-played pitches), Sustain (a single note held over 2.5s triggers minimal accompaniment), and LongIdle (after more than 5s of silence with sections active, the ensemble leads freely). Two gates manage transitions: a warmup gate requiring at least two notes after a section activates before the ensemble sounds, and a silence-stop gate that halts the melodic stream after 4s of quiet.

Performance-state estimation (working memory): the backend tracks features including register/dynamics (register, texture hint, motion direction, playing speed, dynamic arc), phrase structure (silence-driven segmentation, phrase-count tracking, last-phrase pitches for echo), and harmony (real-time chord-template matching, twelve-bar blues inference, a "harmony fingerprint" computed over a 2-second window at 2-semitone granularity). This harmony fingerprint was specifically designed to stabilize gating — it replaced an earlier key that changed on every note-on and re-prompted the model continuously during fast melodic runs, destabilizing the stream.

Controller and generation parameters: each accompaniment state maps to a genre-conditioned parameter vector (temperature, top-k, guidance, density, brightness). A representative blues Supporting-state example: temperature 0.60, top-k 12, guidance 7.0, density 0.22; LongIdle uses the highest temperature and density (the band improvises freely) while Supporting uses the lowest temperature and density with the highest guidance (the band stays literal and unobtrusive).

Knowledge base: the entire system embeds hand-authored musical knowledge as readable prose and tuned constants, with the compiler itself running 2,434 lines of Python, including 15 mode/scale descriptions (6 core Arabic maqamat — bayati, rast, hijaz, nahawand, saba, kurd — plus 9 Western scales, each specifying note spelling, characteristic degree, ornaments, resting tones, color description, and negative guidance discouraging Western tonality), 38 ensemble sections (3 role registers each, 114 role descriptions total), 9 genre configurations (section ordering, lead-instrument preference, percussion patterns), 135 generation-parameter sets (9 genres x 3 states x 4 knobs + 3 brightness values), and harmonic rules (interval-template chord recognition, twelve-bar region inference, a four-tier blues harmonic-lock priority).

Prompt synthesis: the compiler assembles prompts as an ordered concatenation of clauses: [instrument rule/silence] | [no-percussion] | [specific header] | [session stage] | [maqam description] | [harmonic context/echo] | [role assignment] | [register] | [dynamics] | [space clause]. Maqam descriptions explicitly spell out microtonal degrees phonetically (e.g. "E half-flat") with negative guidance such as "avoid western major or minor tonality, stay in bayati modal world." Role-layering and echo translate the last phrase's pitches into concrete response instructions: "answer using those same pitches with blues character: slide into the first note, bend with blues phrasing."

限制Limitations

作者列出的限制相當具體且誠實。第一，缺乏拍點層級的同步：系統沒有節拍追蹤或速度重新錨定機制，兩位測試音樂家一致的觀察是「樂隊維持自己的節拍」，而不是跟著演奏者的實際脈動呼吸——系統做到的是「語意層級的回應」（追蹤音區、密度、力度、和聲、樂句邊界），但不是「節拍層級的回應」（節拍追蹤、速度重新錨定），作者把這列為未來工作議程的首要項目。第二，樂器抑制機制不可靠：消融實驗顯示，光靠提示詞裡的負面指令（例如「以下樂器絕對不能出現」）並沒有可量測地抑制被排除的樂器，用AudioSet標籤器分析後，作者直言「提示詞層級的禁止,無法取代stem層級的API控制」——也就是說負面提示反而容易「喚起」它想避免的東西。第三，微分音的穩定性有限：提示詞式的調式描述確實能引出四分音的旋律「傾向」，但作者承認「要讓半降音級穩定地成為一個調準確的音階音級，光靠提示詞層級的控制還做不到」。第四，系統完全依賴一個沒被改動過的商用串流模型（Lyria RealTime），沒辦法在串流中途改變BPM，速度變化必須整個斷線重連。第五，和聲推斷主要只在藍調、爵士這類有明確和弦進行的曲風裡可靠，對非和聲導向的曲種適用性較低。第六，也是最根本的一點：整個評估規模很小——只有兩位專業音樂家的體驗回饋，加上一場8.5分鐘的儀器化session，作者自己承認「這只構成一次小規模的專家型感知評估，不是有較大參與者樣本的受控聆聽研究，後者仍是未來工作」。

The authors list quite specific and honest limitations. First, no beat-level synchronization: the system has no beat tracking or tempo re-anchoring, and both test musicians consistently observed that "the ensemble kept its own clock" rather than breathing with the performer's actual pulse — the system achieves semantic responsiveness (tracking register, density, dynamics, harmony, phrase boundaries) but not metric responsiveness (beat tracking, tempo re-anchoring), which the authors place at the head of their future-work agenda. Second, instrument suppression is unreliable: an ablation shows that negative prompt-level directives alone (e.g. "the following must not appear") do not measurably suppress excluded instruments — analyzed via an AudioSet tagger, the authors state plainly that "prompt-level prohibition is therefore not a substitute for stem-level API control," meaning negative prompting tends instead to evoke what it names. Third, microtonal stability is limited: prompt-level maqam grounding does elicit a quarter-tone melodic inflection, but the authors admit "rendering the half-flat second as a stably tuned scale degree remains beyond prompt-level control alone." Fourth, the system depends entirely on an unmodified commercial streaming model (Lyria RealTime), cannot change BPM mid-stream, and requires a full disconnect-and-reconnect for tempo changes. Fifth, harmonic inference is reliable mainly in blues/jazz-style genres with clear chord progressions, and less applicable to non-harmonically-driven genres. Sixth, and most fundamentally: the evaluation scale is small — just perceptual feedback from two professional musicians plus one 8.5-minute instrumented session, and the authors themselves acknowledge this "constitute[s] a small-scale perceptual evaluation by domain experts... not a controlled listening study with a larger participant pool; the latter remains future work."

討論Discussion

這篇論文的說服力主要來自它願意把即時系統的工程細節攤開來量化，而不是只給一段示範影片。在延遲與穩定性方面：知識層（狀態更新加提示詞編譯）花費「即使在p99也只要大約半毫秒」，端對端從按鍵到可聽見聲音的延遲中位數是263毫秒（p99為945毫秒），落在控制器開啟的0.3至2.5秒回應窗口的前段；在8.5分鐘的主要測試session裡，1,517次閘門觸發、166次每分鐘的API推送，零重連、零串流中斷、零因佇列滿而丟棄的資料，即使關掉閘門機制、把推送頻率拉到每分鐘202次，串流依然穩定——這說明「這套管線能承受的重新引導頻率，比設計時假設的還要高一個數量級」。在調式落地的消融實驗裡，有無調式知識造成的差異相當明確：有調式知識時，主音D在三組實驗裡有兩組拿到最高能量佔比（0.175、0.163），沒有調式知識時則從未排到第一（合併後排第四，0.094）；逐幀分析顯示，有調式知識時22.4%的有聲幀偏離十二平均律網格35音分以上（沒有時是14.9%）；當旋律線經過D上方的第二音級區域時，有調式知識時有59.4%的幀落在半降音的125-175音分帶內，沒有時只有24.1%。這些數字證明了「用自然語言描述微分音特徵」確實能統計上顯著地把生成內容推往調式方向，即使沒辦法讓每個音符都精準地調在正確頻率上。整體而言，作者認為這個架構的五個特性——模型無關、不需訓練、可檢視可編輯、確定性、效能極佳（知識層成本比生成器預算小三個數量級）——共同證明了「確定性的知識規則」可以有效地橋接專業、非西方的音樂傳統跟一個沒被微調過的基礎模型。這對資源有限、訓練資料稀缺的非西方音樂傳統來說，提供了一個不需要龐大訓練語料就能做出可用系統的可行路徑，但論文也誠實地把「節拍同步」與「大規模聆聽評估」列為尚未跨過的門檻。

This paper's persuasiveness comes largely from its willingness to lay real-time system engineering details bare and quantify them, rather than offering only a demo video. On latency and stability: the knowledge layer (state update plus prompt compilation) costs "roughly half a millisecond even at p99," with end-to-end key-press-to-audible latency at a 263ms median (945ms p99), sitting at the front of the 0.3-2.5s answer window the controller opens. Across the 8.5-minute primary test session: 1,517 gate fires, 166 API pushes per minute, zero reconnects, zero stream terminations, and zero bounded-queue drops — and even with the gate disabled and push frequency raised to 202/minute, the stream stayed stable, establishing that "the pipeline tolerates an order of magnitude more re-steering than its design assumed." In the maqam-grounding ablation, the difference with and without grounding is clear-cut: with grounding, the tonic D achieves the highest energy share in two of three arms (0.175, 0.163), while without grounding it never ranks first (pooled fourth at 0.094); frame-level analysis shows 22.4% of voiced frames land 35+ cents off the 12-TET grid with grounding versus 14.9% without; and when the melodic line visits the second-degree region above D, 59.4% of frames fall within the half-flat band (125-175 cents) with grounding versus only 24.1% without. These numbers demonstrate that describing microtonal features in natural language does statistically significantly push generated content toward the intended mode, even without pinning every note to an exactly correct frequency. Overall, the authors argue that five properties of the architecture — model-agnostic, requiring no training, inspectable and editable, deterministic, and highly performant (the knowledge layer costs three orders of magnitude less than the generator's budget) — together demonstrate that deterministic knowledge-based rules can effectively bridge expert, non-Western musical traditions and an unfine-tuned foundation model. For resource-limited, data-scarce non-Western traditions, this offers a workable path to a usable system without a massive training corpus, though the paper is also honest that beat-level synchronization and large-scale listening evaluation remain thresholds not yet crossed.

動機Motivation

文字轉音樂生成近年進步很快，但主流系統（MusicGen、AudioLDM 2、MeLoDy 等）幾乎都只靠一句全域文字提示決定整首曲子，提示可以描述風格、情緒、樂器編制或速度，卻無法指定「段落怎麼組織、素材什麼時候重複或變化、織度怎麼演變、樂器何時加入或退出」這類長程結構安排。既有的可控生成方法通常是額外加一個屬性通道（旋律參考、鼓點、和弦、力度），這些控制仍然是局部的、隱式的，或需要額外的符號輸入，沒有一個統一、隨時間對齊的表示可以同時描述整首曲子的段落組織與編制安排。作者借用影像生成領域的想法：像 PlanGen 這類方法透過在生成內容前先暴露一層顯式的高階規劃，明顯提升了可控性；這篇論文把同樣的原則搬到音樂上,只是把「沿著空間規劃」換成「沿著時間規劃」。

Text-to-music generation has advanced quickly, but mainstream systems (MusicGen, AudioLDM 2, MeLoDy, and similar) rely almost entirely on a single global text prompt to determine an entire piece. A prompt can describe genre, mood, instrumentation, or tempo, but it cannot specify how sections are organized, when material recurs or varies, how texture evolves, or when instruments enter and exit — the long-range structural decisions that shape a song. Existing controllable-generation methods typically bolt on a single attribute channel (a melody reference, drum pattern, chords, dynamics), and these controls remain local, implicit, or dependent on extra symbolic input; no unified, time-aligned representation exists that describes both the sectional organization and the instrumental arrangement of an entire piece at once. The authors borrow an idea from image generation, where methods like PlanGen expose an explicit high-level plan before content generation and measurably improve controllability; this paper adapts the same principle to music, simply replacing planning-over-space with planning-over-time.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景涵蓋兩條線。文字轉音樂生成方法上：自迴歸系統（Jukebox、MusicLM、MusicGen）直接預測離散音訊 token；擴散式系統（Noise2Music、Moûsai）用逐步去噪合成；混合式系統（AudioLDM 2、MeLoDy）把語言模型式的語意建模與擴散合成結合；較新的 Stable Audio 3 支援變長生成，ACE-Step 1.5 則結合語言模型規劃與 DiT 合成。可控生成方面：多數工作是在全域提示外再補一個額外控制通道，例如旋律、節奏、力度、和弦、鼓點；Seed-Music 用符號化的 lead-sheet 管線；ACE-Step 1.5 本身已經納入 metadata 與隱式的「歌曲藍圖」規劃。作者指出這些既有做法的共同侷限：控制是逐項的、外加的、任務特定的，或者只到粗粒度的 metadata 層級，沒有一個表示能同時、顯式地暴露段落、織度、重複變化關係與樂器編制,並且讓使用者在生成音訊前就能檢視與修改。這正是 MusicLayout 想填的空白。

The related work spans two threads. On text-to-music generation architectures: autoregressive systems (Jukebox, MusicLM, MusicGen) predict discrete audio tokens directly; diffusion-based systems (Noise2Music, Moûsai) synthesize via iterative denoising; hybrid systems (AudioLDM 2, MeLoDy) combine language-model-style semantic modeling with diffusion synthesis; and more recent systems like Stable Audio 3 support variable-length generation while ACE-Step 1.5 combines LM-based planning with DiT synthesis. On controllable generation: most work supplements the global prompt with a single extra control channel — melody, rhythm, dynamics, chords, or drums; Seed-Music uses a symbolic lead-sheet pipeline; ACE-Step 1.5 itself already incorporates metadata and an implicit song-blueprint plan. The authors point to a shared limitation across this prior work: control is piecemeal, bolted-on, task-specific, or limited to coarse metadata, with no single representation that explicitly exposes sections, texture, repetition/variation relationships, and instrumental arrangement together, in a form a user can inspect and edit before audio is generated. That is the gap MusicLayout is built to fill.

方法Method

MusicLayout 本身是一套結構化表示：沿著共用時間軸,同時描述「整首曲子層級」與「片段層級」的資訊。整首曲子層級用「家族」（family）把曲子內部素材相近的片段分組（fam_0、fam_1...），並記錄片段之間的重複／變化關係。片段層級每個片段對應一段時間區間，帶有：段落標籤（11 類，如 intro、verse、prechorus、chorus、bridge、breakdown、outro、transition、hook、solo、build）、織度（10 類，如 layered、rhythm_driven、melodic_front、percussive、build_up、sparse_pulse 等）、變化強度（4 級：unique、same、light_var、strong_var，用該片段與所屬家族原型的 Jaccard 距離與能量/密度/角色數差異加權計算後分級）、樂器角色（25 類，如 acoustic_piano、electric_guitar、bass、strings、synth_pad、drums 等）以及每個角色的音域、密度、能量屬性。整份 layout 序列化成一段類 XML 的純文字格式，方便人類閱讀與編輯。生成架構建立在 ACE-Step 1.5（17 億參數語言模型）之上，音訊 tokenizer（5Hz）與 Diffusion Transformer 保持凍結不動，模型被擴充成先在同一個自迴歸序列裡生成 layout token,再以 layout 為條件生成音訊 token,形式化為聯合分布 p(l,a|c)=p(l|c)p(a|c,l)。訓練分兩階段：第一階段只更新新增詞彙對應的 embedding 與輸出層權重（凍結其餘部分），跑 10 個 epoch；第二階段完整微調整個語言模型,同時最佳化「layout 規劃」與「layout 條件下的音訊生成」兩個損失,跑 100 個 epoch。訓練用 FreeMIDI（24474 筆訓練樣本，MIDI 經 FluidSynth 合成音訊，提示語由另一個語言模型生成）,並在域外資料 MidiCaps（1040 筆）與完全域外的真實中文人聲伴奏資料 MuChin（1000 筆，人聲已用 BS-RoFormer 分離掉）上做評估,藉此檢驗跨資料集甚至跨音色型態的泛化能力。

MusicLayout is itself a structured representation describing both piece-level and segment-level information along a shared timeline. At the piece level, "families" group segments that share similar musical material within a piece (fam_0, fam_1, ...) and record repetition/variation relationships across segments. At the segment level, each segment spans a time interval and carries: a section label (11 categories — intro, verse, prechorus, chorus, bridge, breakdown, outro, transition, hook, solo, build), a texture label (10 categories — layered, rhythm_driven, melodic_front, percussive, build_up, sparse_pulse, and others), a variation strength (4 levels — unique, same, light_var, strong_var, computed from a weighted combination of Jaccard distance and differences in energy/density/role count relative to the segment's family prototype), instrument roles (25 categories — acoustic_piano, electric_guitar, bass, strings, synth_pad, drums, and others), and register/density/energy attributes per role. The whole layout is serialized into a human-readable, XML-like plain-text format for inspection and editing. The generation architecture builds on ACE-Step 1.5 (a 1.7B-parameter language model), keeping its 5Hz audio tokenizer and Diffusion Transformer frozen, and extends it to generate layout tokens first within the same autoregressive sequence, then condition audio-token generation on that layout — formalized as the joint distribution p(l,a|c)=p(l|c)p(a|c,l). Training runs in two stages: stage one updates only the embedding and output-layer rows for newly introduced vocabulary (everything else frozen) for 10 epochs; stage two fully fine-tunes the language model, jointly optimizing a layout-planning loss and a layout-conditioned audio-generation loss for 100 epochs. Training uses FreeMIDI (24,474 examples, MIDI synthesized to audio via FluidSynth, prompts generated by another language model), with evaluation on out-of-domain MidiCaps (1,040 examples) and a fully out-of-domain real-recording set, MuChin (1,000 examples of Chinese vocal-plus-accompaniment audio with vocals separated out via BS-RoFormer) — testing generalization across both dataset and, critically, timbral realism.

限制Limitations

作者自陳的限制相當明確。方法只能在生成前規劃,無法對已存在的音訊做局部編輯或重新生成某一段同時保留其餘部分不變；樂器表示停在「類別」層級,不描述精確音色、演奏細節或後製效果；訓練資料完全來自 MIDI 合成音訊,聲學多樣性與真實感有限,論文也直接承認 layout 「只保證是一個結構完整的請求,不保證每個指定屬性都會被完美實現」。最值得注意的是實測到的失敗模式：在域內 FreeMIDI 上,加了 layout 的模型幾乎全指標勝出;換到域外 MidiCaps 時,生成式 layout 的結構複雜度指標（SCM）從參考 layout 的 0.087 惡化到 0.804,顯示模型自己生成的 layout 已經偏離真實結構分佈；換到真實錄音的 MuChin 時情況更明顯,FAD 從基線的 2.480 惡化到 3.456、SCM 從 0.186 惡化到 0.655,作者將此歸咎於訓練（MIDI 合成音訊）與評估（真實人聲伴奏錄音）之間的分佈落差——這代表這套方法目前驗證的可控性,主要成立在「音色本身也是合成出來」的封閉世界裡,離真實錄音場景還有明顯距離。

The authors state their limitations plainly: the method only plans before generation and cannot edit existing audio locally or regenerate one region while preserving the rest; instrument representation stops at the categorical level, not exact timbre, performance nuance, or production effects; training data is entirely MIDI-synthesized audio with limited acoustic diversity and realism, and the paper is explicit that a valid layout "is a well-formed structural request, not a guarantee that every specified attribute will be realized perfectly." The most notable limitation is an observed failure mode rather than a stated caveat: on in-domain FreeMIDI, the layout-conditioned model wins on nearly every metric; but moving to out-of-domain MidiCaps, the generated-layout condition's structural-complexity metric (SCM) degrades from the reference layout's 0.087 to 0.804, indicating the model's own generated layout has drifted from the true structural distribution; moving further to the real-recording MuChin set, this worsens sharply — FAD degrades from the baseline's 2.480 to 3.456, SCM from 0.186 to 0.655 — which the authors attribute to a distribution gap between MIDI-synthesized training audio and real vocal-plus-accompaniment evaluation recordings. This means the controllability validated here mainly holds within a closed world where the timbre itself is also synthetic, and there remains a clear gap before it transfers to real recorded audio.

討論Discussion

整篇論文最有意思的地方不是「加了 layout 就變好」這個直覺結論,而是它系統性地拆解了這個結論在哪裡成立、在哪裡崩潰。在域內合成資料上,不論用參考 layout 還是模型自己生成的 layout,幾乎所有聲學與結構指標都優於沒有 layout 的匹配資料對照組（同樣資料訓練但拿掉 layout）,證明結構規劃本身確實提供了額外訊號,而不只是資料量增加的效果——這一點透過刻意設計的「打亂 layout」消融組得到驗證：把 layout 換成不相關的另一份 layout,效果介於有 layout 與無 layout 之間,說明「layout 形狀本身」與「layout 跟內容是否對應」都各自有貢獻。更值得工程實務參考的是細粒度編輯實驗：直接在 240 秒的曲子中段替換樂器角色、調降某段的密度與能量、或重寫段落順序,都能在生成音訊中量到對應的、方向正確的變化（例如密度調降段落的均方根音量確實下降約 15%）,證明這層表示不是裝飾性的,而是真的能被人類編輯並傳導到最終音訊。但作者對域外與真實錄音結果的坦白同樣重要：一旦音色從合成 MIDI 換成真實錄音,模型生成的 layout 品質與最終音訊品質同時崩壞,這說明目前的收穫主要來自「在訓練分佈內把隱式規劃變顯式」,還沒有解決「顯式規劃遇到分佈外音色時該如何維持有效性」這個更難的問題,也是這條研究路線接下來最值得攻克的地方。

The most interesting part of this paper isn't the intuitive headline that adding a layout helps — it's the systematic dissection of exactly where that claim holds and where it breaks. On in-domain synthetic data, both the reference layout and the model's own generated layout beat a matched-data control (same training data, layout removed) on nearly every acoustic and structural metric, showing that structural planning contributes real signal rather than just benefiting from more effective use of the same data — confirmed by a deliberately designed shuffled-layout ablation: pairing training examples with an unrelated layout lands performance between the with-layout and without-layout conditions, indicating both the shape of a layout and its correspondence to content each contribute independently. More practically relevant is the fine-grained editing experiment: directly swapping instrument roles mid-piece, lowering density and energy in a section, or rewriting section order in a 240-second track each produces measurable, correctly-directed changes in the generated audio (e.g. RMS level in a density-reduced section drops by roughly 15%) — evidence that the representation is not merely decorative but genuinely editable by a human and does propagate through to the final audio. Equally important is the authors' candor about the out-of-domain and real-recording results: once timbre shifts from synthesized MIDI to real recordings, both the quality of the model's generated layout and the quality of the final audio degrade together, indicating that the current gains mainly come from making an implicit plan explicit within the training distribution, and have not yet solved the harder problem of keeping explicit planning effective under out-of-distribution timbre — arguably the most important direction for this line of research to tackle next.

為什麼挑這篇：大多數符號音樂生成論文一股腦把「和弦圖」跟「彈奏節奏」綁在同一個生成步驟裡，這篇反其道而行，先把兩者拆開——和弦位置的選擇跟彈奏型態（picking pattern）的生成分開處理——讓每個子問題都可以獨立進步，而且用 54 位吉他手做的使用者研究老實揭露了一個尷尬的事實：量化指標判定比較好的模型，人類反而覺得比較不好用。Why this one: Most symbolic-guitar generation work bundles chord-diagram choice and rhythmic playing pattern into a single generative step; this paper deliberately separates them — chord-position selection from picking-pattern generation — so each subproblem can improve independently, and a 54-guitarist user study honestly surfaces an uncomfortable finding: the model that scores better on quantitative metrics is the one guitarists find less usable.

動機Motivation

節奏吉他的譜寫，不只是決定「彈哪個和弦」，還要決定「怎麼彈」——用什麼樣的節奏型態去撥弦、哪幾條弦要一起響、哪幾條弦要跳過。市面上的 Guitar Pro 之類的譜曲軟體要靠人工一小節一小節慢慢複製貼上調整，效率很低。過去嘗試用 AI 輔助生成完整 tablature 的作法，通常是把「彈哪個和弦」跟「怎麼彈」這兩件事綁在同一個生成步驟裡直接輸出 fret/string 的組合，但這樣做等於把兩個性質完全不同的子問題疊在一起硬 train 一個模型——選和弦位置比較像離散的樂理決策，彈奏型態則更接近節奏與觸弦質地的風格選擇。這篇論文的出發點就是把兩者拆開：先確定和弦的把位圖，再獨立生成「picking pattern」——一種只描述「哪幾條弦在什麼時間點被撥動」、完全不涉及品格資訊的中介表示法，讓兩個子任務都可以各自被改進、被評估。

Composing rhythm guitar parts isn't just choosing which chord to play — it's also deciding how to play it: what rhythmic pattern to strum with, which strings sound together, which get skipped. Notation software like Guitar Pro currently makes composers copy-paste and adjust bar by bar, which is slow. Prior AI-assisted tablature generation typically bundles "which chord" and "how to play it" into one generative step that outputs fret/string combinations directly — forcing a single model to jointly learn two qualitatively different subproblems, since chord-position selection is closer to a discrete harmonic decision while picking pattern is more a stylistic choice about rhythm and texture. This paper's starting point is to split the two apart: fix the chord diagram first, then independently generate the "picking pattern" — an intermediate representation describing only which strings are struck at which moments, entirely decoupled from fret information — so each subtask can be improved and evaluated on its own terms.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

過去的符號吉他生成研究大多直接對完整的 tablature（同時包含弦、品、時間資訊）做序列生成，這篇論文引用的相關工作顯示，這類端對端做法在生成多樣性與可彈奏性之間常常顧此失彼。作者選擇的切入點，是先定義一套「texture feature」來描述彈奏型態的統計特性：橫向特徵包括每拍平均幾個 onset、onset 間隔的變異程度；縱向特徵包括每次撥弦同時響幾條弦（thickness）、涉及弦號的變異程度、弦的重心位置。這套特徵不只用來當生成時的條件輸入，也直接拿來當評估指標——這種「用同一套特徵語言同時做 conditioning 跟 evaluation」的設計在符號音樂生成論文裡不算常見，好處是評估的維度直接對應到模型被要求優化的維度，不會有評估標準跟訓練目標脫節的問題。訓練資料用 DadaGP，這是一個含超過 2.6 萬個 Guitar Pro 檔案的資料庫，涵蓋各種流行音樂的吉他編曲，是目前吉他 tablature 生成領域少數規模夠大的公開資料集。

Prior work on symbolic guitar generation mostly generates complete tablature (string, fret, and timing jointly) as a single sequence. The related work cited here suggests such end-to-end approaches often trade off diversity against playability. This paper's angle is to first define a set of "texture features" describing the statistical properties of a picking pattern: horizontal features (average onsets per beat, variation in inter-onset interval) and vertical features (thickness — how many strings sound per onset — string-number variation, and string centroid position). These features serve double duty as both generation conditioning and evaluation metrics — a design choice not common in symbolic music generation, whose benefit is that the evaluation axes directly match what the model is asked to optimize, avoiding a mismatch between training objective and evaluation criteria. Training data comes from DadaGP, a corpus of over 26,000 Guitar Pro files spanning popular-music guitar arrangements, one of the few sufficiently large public datasets for guitar tablature generation.

方法Method

系統分成兩個互補的模型。第一個是規則式（rule-based）模型，單純模仿吉他手在編曲軟體裡複製貼上的工作流程：根據和弦圖做弦的對應規則，優先保留貝斯音（低音弦），並且複製既有的琶音型態；當前後兩個和弦圖用到的弦數不同時，設計了專門的映射函式來處理弦數不匹配的狀況。這個模型完全確定性，沒有隨機性，輸出穩定但缺乏變化。

第二個是類 GPT-2 架構的解碼器，參數量只有 84 萬（比一般語言模型小非常多），使用 155 個 token 的詞彙表涵蓋弦、onset、時值與 metadata。條件化的方式是在 attention 之前把 texture control、和弦圖資訊、結構訊號一起餵進去（pre-attention conditioning）。訓練分兩階段：第一階段用全部的 picking pattern（不論有沒有和弦圖標註）做基礎訓練；第二階段只用有明確和弦圖標註的 3 萬 1596 個樣本做精修。這個模型還設計了一個專門的「吉他和弦圖損失函式（guitar chord diagram loss）」，對彈到和弦圖之外、不被允許的弦（out-of-diagram note）的 token 額外懲罰。

資料前處理從 DadaGP 的 290 萬個小節裡篩出 230 萬個判定為節奏吉他的小節，組成 260 萬個四小節序列，去重後剩下 100 萬個獨特 pattern。選四小節為單位，是因為過去研究顯示這個時間尺度剛好能同時捕捉到一致的音樂特徵跟對比性的段落變化。

The system pairs two complementary models. The first is a rule-based model that mimics a guitarist's copy-paste workflow in notation software: it maps strings from chord-diagram rules, prioritizes retaining bass notes on low strings, and replicates existing arpeggio patterns, with dedicated mapping functions handling the case where consecutive chord diagrams use different numbers of strings. This model is fully deterministic — stable output, but no variation.

The second is a GPT-2-style decoder with only 0.84M parameters (far smaller than typical language models), using a 155-token vocabulary covering strings, onsets, durations, and metadata. Conditioning is applied pre-attention, combining texture controls, chord-diagram information, and structural signals. Training happens in two phases: a base phase on all picking patterns regardless of chord-diagram annotation, followed by a refinement phase on only the 31,596 samples with explicit chord-diagram annotations. A custom "guitar chord diagram loss" additionally penalizes tokens that strike strings not permitted by the diagram (out-of-diagram notes).

Preprocessing filters DadaGP's 2.9M bars down to 2.3M bars judged to be rhythm guitar, forming 2.6M four-bar sequences that deduplicate to 1M unique patterns. Four bars was chosen because prior work shows this window captures both consistent musical characteristics and contrastive section changes.

限制Limitations

使用者研究只用了 5 個測試提示（prompt）去產生延續段落讓 54 位吉他手評分，測試場景數量偏少，能不能涵蓋不同曲風、不同難度的節奏型態是存疑的。而且這是非互動式的評測設計——受試者只是聽/看已經生成好的段落打分，跟真實使用情境（邊生成邊調整、反覆嘗試）有落差，論文自己也承認這點。另一個問題出在評估基準本身：參考資料集（DadaGP）裡本來就有 4.1% 的「out-of-diagram note」，包括裝飾音、悶音（dead note）跟單純的轉譜錯誤，這代表用「跟參考資料的差距」當作品質指標時，參考答案本身就不乾淨，量化指標的可信度打了折扣。最後，整個方法目前只在 4/4 拍上驗證過，論文自己列為未來工作要擴展到其他拍號，代表目前的技術泛化性有限。

The user study used only 5 test prompts to generate continuations rated by 54 guitarists — a small number of scenarios, leaving open whether the findings cover the range of genres and pattern difficulties in practice. It's also a non-interactive evaluation design: participants rated pre-generated segments rather than interacting with the system in a real compose-and-adjust loop, a gap the authors acknowledge themselves. A second issue lies in the evaluation baseline: the reference dataset (DadaGP) itself contains 4.1% out-of-diagram notes — ornaments, dead notes for muting, and outright transcription errors — meaning the "ground truth" used to compute distance-based metrics is itself noisy, undercutting the reliability of the quantitative scores. Finally, the whole method has only been validated in 4/4 time; the authors list extending to other time signatures as future work, meaning current generalizability is limited.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最值得注意的地方，不是提出了哪個新穎的模型架構（用的其實是很小、很傳統的 GPT-2 解碼器），而是評估設計本身帶出的張力：量化指標判定 Transformer 模型比規則式模型更好（edit distance 更低、texture distance 更貼近參考資料），但使用者研究裡，吉他手反而覺得規則式模型的輸出更可彈、更一致、不需要修改就能直接上場演奏，Transformer 只在「有趣程度」這個主觀維度上勝出。論文自己點出癥結：時值 token 的些微差異就能大幅改變彈奏的律動感，而弦的選擇差異一旦造成「彈到和弦圖外的弦」，會直接衝擊可彈奏性——但這些對彈奏體驗至關重要的細節，edit distance 這種逐 token 比對的指標根本測不出來。這對整個符號音樂生成領域是個提醒：光靠自動化指標去斷定「哪個模型比較好」可能得到跟實際使用體驗完全相反的結論，尤其是牽涉到「這東西人類要不要拿去用」的創作輔助工具，使用者研究不是錦上添花，而是必要的驗證手段。可惜的是論文沒有進一步嘗試「規則式生成 + Transformer 局部修飾」這種混合式方案，兩個模型目前是平行對照，而不是互補使用，這其實是很自然的下一步。

This paper's most notable contribution isn't a novel model architecture — the generative model is in fact a small, conventional GPT-2 decoder — but the tension its evaluation design surfaces: quantitative metrics judge the Transformer better (lower edit distance, closer texture-distance alignment to reference data), yet in the user study, guitarists found the rule-based model's output more playable and consistent, usable for performance without editing, while the Transformer won only on the subjective "interest" dimension. The paper pinpoints why: small differences in duration tokens can substantially change a pattern's groove, and string-choice differences that produce out-of-diagram notes directly hurt playability — details that token-level edit distance simply cannot capture. That's a broader caution for symbolic music generation: automated metrics alone can rank models in the opposite order from how they'll actually be experienced, and for creative tools whose whole point is whether a human will actually use the output, user studies aren't a nice-to-have but a necessary check. It's a shame the paper doesn't explore a hybrid — rule-based generation refined locally by the Transformer — since the two models are currently treated as parallel alternatives rather than complementary; that seems like a natural next step.

為什麼挑這篇：把擴散模型的品質優勢與自迴歸的長度優勢接在一起，關鍵在於用前一段的潛在變數當條件而不是硬拼接。作者明確寫出了自己先試過的失敗做法與失敗原因，那段分析比最終方法更有參考價值。Why this one: It joins diffusion's quality advantage to autoregression's length advantage, the key being to condition on the previous segment's latent rather than concatenating segments. The authors report the approach they tried first and why it failed, and that analysis is more instructive than the final method.

動機Motivation

文字控制的符號音樂生成有四個彼此拉扯的要求：品質、多樣性、長度、文字可控性。作者的觀察是現有方法各自只顧得上其中一半。

Transformer 解碼器路線（MuseCoco、MMT）能靠自迴歸產生較長的音樂，但品質與多樣性輸給擴散模型。擴散模型路線（Polyffusion）在品質與多樣性上有優勢，卻只能生成固定長度——因為長度被 VAE 綁死，要生成 64 小節就得訓練一個能編碼 64 小節的 VAE，而過長的音樂會讓計算成本上升、VAE 重建品質變差。

為什麼是符號而不是波形，作者也講得直接：波形裡的音樂元素是隱含的、難以調整，這限制了實用性；符號音樂是離散音樂元素的序列，速度、音高、力度、配器都可以在高低兩個層次上精確修改。對作曲者、音樂教育者、或想做高度客製化作品的人來說，這種控制力才是關鍵。

另外一個現實障礙是資料：文字與符號音樂配對的資料集根本不足，這是這個方向「還在非常早期階段」的一部分原因。

Text-controlled symbolic music generation has four requirements that pull against each other: quality, diversity, duration and text controllability. The authors observe that existing methods manage only half of them each.

The Transformer-decoder line (MuseCoco, MMT) generates longer music autoregressively but loses to diffusion on quality and diversity. The diffusion line (Polyffusion) wins on quality and diversity but produces only fixed-length output, because length is bound to the VAE: generating 64 bars requires training a VAE that encodes 64 bars, and longer spans raise compute cost while degrading reconstruction.

On why symbolic rather than waveform, the authors are direct: musical elements in a waveform are implicit and hard to adjust, which limits practical use, whereas symbolic music is a sequence of discrete elements where tempo, pitch, dynamics and instrumentation can be edited precisely at both low and high levels. For composers, educators, or anyone building highly customised work, that controllability is the point.

A second practical obstacle is data: paired text–symbolic-music datasets barely exist, which is part of why this direction remains at a very early stage.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者把相關工作分成兩條線來盤點。

文字到音訊的音樂生成已經相當成熟，典型結構有三個部件：文字編碼器（T5、CLAP、MuLan）把詞映射到潛在空間；自編碼器把波形壓成離散聲學 token（殘差向量量化）或連續聲學嵌入；聲學建模把文字嵌入轉成聲學 token。MusicGen 與 MusicLM 用自迴歸方式建模，MAGNET 用遮罩語言建模，AudioLDM 系列、Mustango、Stable Audio 系列則走擴散。

文字到符號音樂則明顯落後。BUTTER 基於 VAE 做音樂表徵解耦與跨模態對齊，能從文字生成 ABC 記譜，但描述被限制在三個確切關鍵字。GPT-4 也能生成 ABC 記譜，但缺乏任何有意義的和聲——它本來就是為理解與生成文字設計的，不是音樂。Wu 與 Sun 在超過 20 萬對文字-ABC 資料上微調，模型仍難以準確對應文字裡的音樂屬性，且只能生成單軌。MuseCoco 把任務拆成兩階段：先用 BERT 從文字預測音樂屬性，再把屬性當前綴 token 用 Transformer 解碼器自迴歸生成——能對上文字描述，但長度受限且訓練成本可觀。

擴散應用於符號音樂這一支，Mittal 等人首次把擴散用在離散符號音樂上（用 2 小節的 MusicVAE），Polyffusion 把樂譜當成鋼琴捲影像來生成，Wang 等人用級聯擴散生成整首歌。潛在擴散（LDM）則在影像、影片、音訊各領域廣泛成功。作者的定位很明確：現有 LDM 工作無法在文字條件下生成長篇符號音樂，這正是他們要補的洞。

The authors survey two lines of prior work.

Text-to-audio music generation is comparatively mature, typically with three components: a text encoder (T5, CLAP, MuLan) mapping words into latent space; an autoencoder compressing waveforms into discrete acoustic tokens via residual vector quantisation or into continuous embeddings; and acoustic modelling from text embeddings to acoustic tokens. MusicGen and MusicLM model autoregressively, MAGNET uses masked language modelling, and the AudioLDM series, Mustango and the Stable Audio series use diffusion.

Text-to-symbolic lags clearly behind. BUTTER disentangles music representations with a VAE and aligns them cross-modally, generating ABC notation from text, but its descriptions are limited to three exact keywords. GPT-4 can also produce ABC notation but with no meaningful harmony, having been built to understand and generate text rather than music. Wu and Sun fine-tuned on over 200k text–ABC pairs and still struggled to match the musical attributes in the text, with output limited to solo tracks. MuseCoco splits the task in two — BERT predicts musical attributes from text, then those attributes serve as prefix tokens for autoregressive decoder-only generation — which does follow text descriptions, but with limited length and considerable training cost.

On the diffusion side, Mittal et al. first applied diffusion to discrete symbolic music using a 2-bar MusicVAE, Polyffusion generates polyphonic scores treated as piano-roll images, and Wang et al. use cascaded diffusion for whole songs. Latent diffusion has succeeded broadly across image, video and audio. The authors' position is precise: no existing LDM work generates long-duration symbolic music conditioned on text, and that is the gap they target.

方法Method

整體架構由三塊組成：音樂資訊編碼器、Multi-view MidiVAE、以及 Transformer 為骨幹的擴散模型。訓練分兩階段——先訓練 LDM 生成符合文字描述的音樂，再加入音樂脈絡模組做全參數微調，讓模型能自迴歸地生成長篇。

資料建構。 沒有合用的文字-符號音樂配對資料集，所以自己造。作者把音樂屬性分成客觀與主觀兩類：樂器、速度這類客觀屬性可以直接從符號音樂抽出，音域之類需要用規則計算，主觀屬性則只能取自資料集既有標籤。他們為每個音樂屬性設計單句模板，再用 GPT-4 把模板改寫成 3 到 6 句意思相同的自然語言描述，並對每一種屬性組合都做一次，最終得到涵蓋所有組合的 19,345 個文字模板。

音樂資訊編碼器（MI Encoder）。 預訓練 BERT 雖然能抽語意特徵，但後續擴散模型的計算成本隨文字長度平方成長。作者的做法是：凍結 BERT，加上注意力機制與 m 個可學習參數（m 是預定義音樂屬性總數）。這些 query 先過自注意力層，再作為交叉注意力層的 query，以 BERT 抽出的語意表徵當 key 與 value，最後每個 query 的輸出經 softmax 分類器去分類音樂屬性（例如有沒有「鋼琴」、拍號是多少）。用這個分類任務訓練編碼器，等於逼它把與音樂無關的語意資訊濾掉。訓練這個編碼器時可以隨機生成大量屬性組合來造文字，不必真的從 MIDI 抽屬性，資料建構更靈活。

LDM 本體。 用 Multi-view MidiVAE 把符號音樂編成潛在變數，骨幹採 Transformer Encoder 架構以捕捉音樂的時序動態。訓練時用 classifier-free guidance，以 20% 的固定機率丟棄文字條件，同時訓練條件與非條件 LDM。

長度問題與失敗的第一次嘗試——這段是全文最有價值的部分。 作者先照 Mittal 等人的做法試：把長曲切成多段、各自編碼、把潛在變數串接起來代表整首的分布。結果不理想。無條件生成把 16 小節切成兩個 8 小節時，段落之間內容明顯改變，尤其是樂器會換掉；加上文字描述當控制條件也沒能完全解決；把 32 小節切成四段時，段數增加讓突兀變化更嚴重。

作者給出的解釋是機率上的：若把樂曲切成 n 段編碼得到 n 個分布，單純串接等於要求擴散模型去擬合一個複雜的聯合新分布 p(z₁,…,z_n)；加入文字條件 c 後模型擬合的是更具體的 p(z₁,…,z_n|c)，這確實在一定程度上緩解了樂器特性捕捉的問題，但隨段數增加，整體分布的複雜度上升，去噪過程的偏差累積，問題重新惡化。他們也指出為什麼 Mittal 等人沒遇到這個問題——因為那份工作根本沒考慮樂器這類音樂資訊。

實際採用的自迴歸做法。 不再一次生成多段，而是一次只生成一段，靠串接延長總長度。關鍵是用音樂脈絡的內容資訊來引導新段落：對相鄰兩段 s_{i-1} 與 s_i，用 VAE 編碼器抽出潛在變數，前者作為後者的脈絡條件。實作上設計了一個音樂脈絡模組，在每個 Transformer block 裡加交叉注意力層，訓練時把前一段的潛在變數當作 key 與 value。由於 LDM 已經學會擬合 p(z|c)，這裡做全參數微調讓它改去擬合 p(z_i|c, z_{i-1})。推論時先用未微調的 LDM 生成第一段，再用微調後的模型逐段往下生成，最後把各段解碼並按時序串接。

同一個機制順帶支援音樂續寫：把既有樂曲用 VAE 編碼器取得潛在表徵當脈絡條件，再結合文字模板構造出想要的描述，就能讓模型接著寫下去。

The architecture has three parts: a music information encoder, a Multi-view MidiVAE, and a Transformer-backbone diffusion model. Training runs in two stages — first an LDM that generates music matching a text description, then full-parameter fine-tuning with a musical context module added, enabling autoregressive long-form generation.

Data construction. With no suitable paired dataset, they build one. Musical attributes split into objective and subjective: instruments and tempo can be extracted directly from symbolic music, things like pitch range require rule-based computation, and subjective attributes must come from pre-existing labels. They write a single-sentence template per attribute, use GPT-4 to rewrite each into 3–6 natural-language paraphrases, and repeat for every attribute combination, yielding 19,345 templates covering all combinations.

Music Information Encoder. Pre-trained BERT extracts semantics, but downstream diffusion cost grows quadratically with text length. Their encoder freezes BERT and adds an attention mechanism plus m learnable queries, where m is the number of predefined musical attributes. The queries pass through self-attention, then act as queries in a cross-attention layer with BERT's representations as keys and values; each query's output is classified for a musical attribute (is "piano" present, what is the time signature) through a softmax classifier. Training on this classification task forces the encoder to strip semantics unrelated to music. Notably, training data for the encoder can be generated by randomly sampling attribute combinations and synthesising the corresponding text — no MIDI extraction required — which makes construction more flexible.

The LDM. Multi-view MidiVAE encodes symbolic music into a latent variable; the backbone is a Transformer Encoder chosen to capture music's temporal dynamics. Training uses classifier-free guidance, dropping the text condition with fixed probability 20% to train the conditional and unconditional models together.

The length problem and the first attempt that failed — the most instructive part of the paper. They first tried the Mittal et al. approach: split a long piece into segments, encode each, and concatenate the latents to represent the whole. It did not work. Unconditional generation splitting 16 bars into two 8-bar segments produced conspicuous content changes between segments, instrumentation especially. Adding text conditioning did not fully resolve it. Splitting 32 bars into four segments made the abrupt changes worse.

Their explanation is probabilistic: encoding n segments gives n distributions, and naive concatenation asks the diffusion model to fit a complex joint p(z₁,…,z_n). Text conditioning narrows this to p(z₁,…,z_n|c), which does help the model capture instrument characteristics, but as segment count grows so does the complexity of the overall distribution, bias accumulates through denoising, and the problem returns. They also explain why Mittal et al. did not hit it: that work did not consider musical information such as instrumentation at all.

The autoregressive approach they adopt. Rather than generating many segments at once, generate one at a time and extend by concatenation. The key is conditioning each new segment on the content of its predecessor: for adjacent segments s_{i-1} and s_i, the VAE encoder extracts latents and the former conditions the latter. A musical context module adds a cross-attention layer inside each Transformer block, taking the previous segment's latent as key and value during training. Since the LDM already fits p(z|c), full-parameter fine-tuning retargets it to p(z_i|c, z_{i-1}). At inference the un-fine-tuned LDM generates the first segment, the fine-tuned model generates each subsequent one, and the decoded segments are concatenated in temporal order.

The same mechanism supports music continuation: encode an existing piece with the VAE to obtain the context condition, construct a text description from attributes and templates, and let the model continue.

限制Limitations

評估的規模與方法都偏薄。主觀實驗只邀請 20 位「音樂愛好者」（不是受過訓練的音樂家）以 1-5 分評分，GPT-4 那組基線更是「用官方網頁手動」以 20 條隨機測試文字生成——這種手動、小樣本、且對 GPT-4 明顯不利的設定，很難支撐嚴謹的比較。作者自己也承認基線少，因為文字到符號音樂的工作本來就不多。

消融研究相當有限。MI Encoder 那一項實際上只比較了「BERT 有沒有微調」對 ASA 的影響，並沒有真正隔離出 MI Encoder 這個設計相對於直接用 BERT 輸出的貢獻——而降低計算成本正是它被提出的主要理由之一，但論文沒有給出計算成本的實際數字。CFG 的結論是提升文字可控性但略微降低音樂品質，這與既有認識一致，沒有新資訊。

最關鍵的是，那個核心的自迴歸脈絡機制只在「相鄰兩段」的條件下訓練——微調資料是 16 小節切成兩個 8 小節、且屬性一致的片段（256,154 個）。但推論時要一路生成到 32 小節（四段）甚至更長。條件只看前一段而非全部歷史，代表這是一階馬可夫的近似；32 小節的實驗結果雖然正面，但作者沒有報告更長篇幅下一致性是否會漂移，而「幾分鐘的一致音樂」正是摘要裡的主張。

另外，訓練資料只保留「相鄰兩段屬性一致」的片段（從 224,928 個 MIDI 檔切出的片段中篩選），這等於系統性地把真實音樂裡的段落轉換、配器變化排除在訓練之外。模型學到的是保持一致，而非在該變的時候變——對於需要結構對比的長篇音樂，這可能是個內建的限制。

Evaluation is thin in both scale and method. The subjective study uses 20 "music enthusiasts" — not trained musicians — rating on a 1–5 scale, and the GPT-4 baseline was produced "manually via the official web page" from 20 randomly selected test texts. A manual, small-sample setup that clearly disadvantages GPT-4 cannot support a rigorous comparison. The authors concede the baselines are few, since little prior text-to-symbolic work exists.

The ablation is limited. The MI Encoder entry actually compares only whether BERT is fine-tuned, measured by ASA; it does not isolate what the MI Encoder design contributes over using BERT's output directly — and reducing computational cost was one of its stated motivations, yet no compute figures are reported. The CFG finding, better text controllability at slight cost to quality, matches existing understanding and adds nothing new.

Most importantly, the central autoregressive context mechanism is trained only on adjacent pairs: fine-tuning data is 16-bar spans split into two 8-bar segments, filtered to those with consistent attributes (256,154 of them). Inference then runs out to 32 bars (four segments) and beyond. Conditioning on only the previous segment rather than the full history makes this a first-order Markov approximation. The 32-bar results are positive, but the paper does not report whether consistency drifts over longer spans — and "consistent music up to several minutes" is the claim in the abstract.

Relatedly, training keeps only segments whose adjacent halves share attributes, filtered from spans cut out of 224,928 MIDI files. That systematically removes sectional transitions and instrumentation changes — real features of real music — from training. The model learns to stay consistent, not to change when change is called for, which may be a built-in limitation for long-form music that needs structural contrast.

討論Discussion

這篇的貢獻與其說是模型，不如說是一個接合方式：擴散模型的長度限制來自 VAE，而 VAE 的限制無法靠切段串接繞過——因為串接改變的是要擬合的分布本身。真正的解法是不要一次擬合聯合分布，而是把長度問題轉成逐段的條件生成問題。這個轉換乾淨且可移植。

讓這篇特別值得讀的是作者把失敗過程寫出來了。他們先試了文獻裡既有的做法（串接潛在變數）、觀察到具體症狀（段落間樂器突變）、給出機率上的解釋（分布複雜度隨段數上升、去噪偏差累積）、並解釋了為什麼前人沒遇到（前人沒建模樂器）。這條推理鏈比最終那個交叉注意力模組更有參考價值——它告訴讀者在什麼條件下前人的做法會失效。

實務上有兩點值得留意。一是 MI Encoder 的訓練資料可以完全合成——隨機組合屬性再生成對應文字，不需要真實 MIDI——這對任何缺乏配對資料的條件生成任務都是可借鑑的技巧。二是刻意在訓練時隨機選 3 到 5 個屬性、每個屬性再以 5% 機率刪掉，用來模擬使用者不會把所有屬性都寫進提示的真實情況。這種對「輸入不完整」的顯式建模，比事後才發現模型在真實輸入上崩掉要好得多。

至於「這是首個把 LDM 與脈絡學習用於多軌文字到符號音樂生成的工作」這個定位，考慮到這個子領域確實還很稀疏，作為先行工作的價值主要在於劃出可行路線，而不在於它現在的絕對效能。

The contribution is less a model than a way of joining things: diffusion's length limit comes from the VAE, and that limit cannot be worked around by splitting and concatenating, because concatenation changes the distribution being fitted. The real fix is to stop fitting a joint distribution at all and recast length as segment-wise conditional generation. The reframing is clean and portable.

What makes the paper worth reading is that the authors write up the failure. They tried the existing approach (concatenated latents), observed a specific symptom (instrumentation flipping between segments), gave a probabilistic account (distribution complexity rising with segment count, denoising bias accumulating), and explained why their predecessors did not hit it (that work never modelled instrumentation). That chain of reasoning is more useful than the cross-attention module it leads to, because it tells the reader under what conditions the prior approach breaks.

Two practical points are worth carrying away. First, the MI Encoder's training data can be fully synthetic — sample attribute combinations, generate matching text, no real MIDI needed — a trick applicable to any conditional generation task short on paired data. Second, training deliberately samples 3–5 attributes and then drops each with 5% probability, simulating users who do not spell out every attribute in a prompt. Modelling incomplete input explicitly beats discovering after deployment that the model collapses on it.

As for the claim of being the first to apply LDM and contextual learning to multi-track text-to-symbolic generation: given how sparse this subfield genuinely is, the value of a pioneering work lies in marking out a viable route rather than in its absolute performance today.

Pushing the Frontier of Full-Song Generation: Hierarchical Autoregressive Planning Meets Flow-Matching Rendering

推進全曲生成的前沿：階層式自迴歸規劃結合流匹配渲染

Junyu Dai, Xinyue Fan, Weiqin Li, Xiangang Li, Yunjia Li, Bin Ma, Yukun Ma, Chongjia Ni, Yufei Shi, Biao Tian, Haoxu Wang, Menglin Wu, Jianwei Yu, Huaicheng Zhang, Han Zhao, Shengkui Zhao, Haina Zhu
arXiv:2607.20253 · cs.SD; cs.AI; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-07-22 (v2: 07-23)
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這是目前少數把「離散 token 自迴歸」與「連續潛空間流匹配」在單一系統裡分工清楚的全曲生成架構，而且三個任務（詞到歌、純樂器、翻唱）共用同一套骨幹。翻唱那條路徑本質上就是 MIR 問題：旋律抽取的品質直接決定生成品質。Why this one: One of the few full-song systems that cleanly divides labour between discrete-token autoregression and continuous-latent flow matching, with one backbone serving three tasks. The cover-song path is essentially an MIR problem: melody extraction quality directly bounds generation quality.

動機Motivation

全曲生成的難處不在於「生出好聽的三十秒」，而在於同時滿足兩個彼此拉扯的需求：長程結構一致性（主歌、副歌、橋段之間的調性與動機要連得起來，長度動輒五分鐘）與聲學保真度（人聲質地、立體聲場、樂器分離度）。

作者指出既有做法各偏一邊。走離散 token 自迴歸的系統（RVQ token + LM）擅長長程規劃，但 token 壓縮掉的細節無法還原，音質天花板被 codec 綁死；走擴散／流匹配的系統音質好，但實務上多採 chunk-wise 合成，段落接縫處的全域一致性會崩掉。這篇的立場是：兩者不該二選一，而該分工——LM 負責「規劃」，flow matching 負責「渲染」。

The hard part of full-song generation is not producing a pleasant 30 seconds; it is satisfying two requirements that pull against each other: long-range structural coherence (key and motif continuity across verse, chorus and bridge over five-plus minutes) and acoustic fidelity (vocal texture, stereo image, instrument separation).

The authors argue prior work leans one way or the other. Discrete-token autoregressive systems (RVQ tokens plus an LM) plan well over long horizons, but detail discarded by the tokenizer cannot be recovered, so the codec caps audio quality. Diffusion and flow-matching systems sound better but in practice synthesise chunk-wise, and global consistency degrades at the seams. The paper's position is that these are not alternatives but a division of labour: the LM plans, flow matching renders.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

定位上這是一份工業技術報告（作者群十七人，資源規模為業界等級），不是傳統學術論文。它對照的是 MusicGen 一系的 token-LM 路線、以及近年 DiT／flow matching 的音訊生成路線，主張把兩者串成上下游而非競爭關係。

三個支援任務——Lyrics-to-Song、Instrumental Music Generation、Cover Song Generation——共用同一骨幹，差別只在條件訊號。這個設計本身值得注意：翻唱任務靠的是從參考音訊抽出旋律線並離散化，等於把一個 MIR 前端（旋律抽取／F0 估計）直接掛進生成管線當控制器。

This is positioned as an industrial technical report (seventeen authors, industry-scale resources) rather than a conventional academic paper. It situates itself against the MusicGen-style token-LM line and the more recent DiT / flow-matching line for audio generation, arguing the two should be chained upstream-downstream rather than treated as competitors.

The three supported tasks — lyrics-to-song, instrumental generation, cover-song generation — share one backbone and differ only in conditioning. That design is itself notable: the cover task works by extracting and discretising a melody line from reference audio, effectively bolting an MIR front end (melody extraction / F0 estimation) onto the generation pipeline as a controller.

方法Method

系統分四塊，值得逐塊拆。

1. 語意感知 tokenizer。 24 層 Conformer 編碼器接 8 個 codebook 的 RVQ，每個 codebook 8,192 entries，frame rate 25 Hz（即每 40 ms 一幀）。訓練分三階段：先用 BEST-RQ 做遮罩音訊預測的自監督預訓練，再以多任務微調（ASR、重建、chroma 預測）注入語意與音高資訊，最後凍結底層 12 層訓練 RVQ token。chroma 這個任務很關鍵——它是明確把和聲資訊寫進 token 的手段，也是這套 token 之所以「semantic-aware」的來源。

2. hybrid-LM（階層式自迴歸）。 一個 8B 的 global LLM 在 frame 層級運作，每幀只預測第一個 codebook 的 token c₀，負責長程依賴；另一個 0.4B 的 local LLM 在幀內自迴歸補完殘差 codebook c₁–c₇。損失加權為 global : local = 5 : 1，等於明說「結構比細節重要」。條件輸入包含結構化音樂標註、帶段落標記的歌詞，以及可選的旋律 token；訓練時 10% 無條件 dropout 以支援 classifier-free guidance。這個 8B + 0.4B 的切法是效率關鍵：把 8 倍的序列長度壓在小模型上。

3. FullDiT（流匹配渲染器）。 8B 的 DiT，在連續 VAE 潛空間做非因果流匹配，context window 8,192 幀，25 Hz 下約 5.5 分鐘——足以一次涵蓋整首歌，這是「full-song」名稱的實質來源。條件為幀對齊的 codec embedding 融合進 hidden states，歌詞與 caption 各自獨立編碼後以全域 self-attention 處理。這裡有個實作上的巧思：EMDC（Error-Matched Distractor Conditioning）——訓練時以「與上游實際錯誤率相符的機率」把條件 token 換成干擾項，緩解訓練用完美 token、推論用 LM 生成（帶錯）token 的暴露偏差。另有四路 classifier-free guidance，把 codec、歌詞、caption 三種條件的貢獻拆開獨立調整。

4. 兩級旋律模組。 粗層是 MIDI 音符序列（128 級 token，依音符時值對齊），細層是幀級 F0 在 log-pitch 域量化為 128 級。粗層保輪廓、細層保表情（滑音、抖音），供翻唱任務控制。

後訓練。 對 hybrid-LM 比較了 DPO（用 SongBench 評分建偏好對，分差需超過門檻 δ 才保留，以濾掉模稜兩可的比較）、GRPO（on-policy 獎勵優化）與 OPD（on-policy distillation，用凍結 teacher 提供稠密的 token 級訊號，補序列級獎勵過於稀疏的問題）；對 FullDiT 則用 flow-based GRPO，目標是人聲與伴奏的平衡以及立體聲渲染。

資料。 預訓練語料為「數千萬首歌」，以 metadata 完整度過濾，再用 SongEval、Audiobox Aesthetics、SongBench 三套自動評分做品質篩選；中期訓練依 SongBench Musicality 分數在 Pop／Rock／Folk／Hip-Hop／R&B／Soul／EDM 之間重新平衡風格；最後在高品質子集上做 SFT。FullDiT 另用一份帶寬、立體聲品質、位元率更嚴格的高保真語料。

The system has four parts, each worth unpacking.

1. Semantic-aware tokenizer. A 24-layer Conformer encoder feeds an 8-codebook RVQ with 8,192 entries per codebook at a 25 Hz frame rate (one frame per 40 ms). Training runs in three stages: BEST-RQ self-supervised pretraining with masked audio prediction; multi-task finetuning on ASR, reconstruction and chroma prediction to inject semantic and pitch information; then RVQ token training with the bottom 12 layers frozen. The chroma task matters — it is the explicit mechanism for writing harmonic information into the tokens, and the reason these tokens count as semantic-aware.

2. Hybrid-LM (hierarchical autoregressive). An 8B global LLM operates at frame level, predicting only the first-codebook token c₀ per frame and carrying long-range dependencies; a 0.4B local LLM autoregressively fills the residual codebooks c₁–c₇ within each frame. Losses are weighted 5:1 in favour of the global objective, an explicit statement that structure outranks detail. Conditioning includes structured music annotations, lyrics with section tags, and optional melody tokens; 10% unconditional dropout during training supports classifier-free guidance. The 8B + 0.4B split is the efficiency lever: the eight-times-longer token stream is handled by the small model.

3. FullDiT (flow-matching renderer). An 8B DiT performing non-causal flow matching in a continuous VAE latent space, with an 8,192-frame context window — about 5.5 minutes at 25 Hz, enough to cover a whole song in one pass, which is what makes "full-song" literal here. Conditioning fuses frame-aligned codec embeddings into the hidden states; lyrics and caption are encoded independently and handled by full-context self-attention. One neat implementation idea: EMDC (Error-Matched Distractor Conditioning) replaces conditioning tokens with distractors at a probability matched to the upstream model's actual error rate, mitigating the exposure bias between training on ground-truth tokens and inference on LM-generated (error-prone) ones. A four-way classifier-free guidance scheme decomposes the codec, lyric and caption contributions so each can be scaled independently.

4. Two-level melody module. The coarse level is a MIDI note sequence (128-level tokens aligned to note durations); the fine level is frame-level F0 quantised to 128 levels in the log-pitch domain. Coarse preserves contour, fine preserves expression (slides, vibrato), together controlling the cover-song task.

Post-training. For hybrid-LM the authors compare DPO (preference pairs from SongBench scores, kept only when the score gap exceeds a threshold δ, filtering ambiguous comparisons), GRPO (on-policy reward optimisation) and OPD (on-policy distillation, where a frozen teacher supplies dense token-level signal to supplement sparse sequence-level rewards). FullDiT is tuned with flow-based GRPO targeting vocal-accompaniment balance and stereo rendering.

Data. Pretraining uses "tens of millions of songs" filtered by metadata completeness and scored by SongEval, Audiobox Aesthetics and SongBench; mid-training rebalances style across Pop, Rock, Folk, Hip-Hop, R&B, Soul and EDM using SongBench Musicality scores; a high-quality subset is used for SFT. FullDiT trains on a separate corpus with stricter bandwidth, stereo-quality and bitrate constraints.

限制Limitations

作者自己承認的限制不多，但有兩點明講：「純樂器與翻唱生成的專門量化評估仍是未來工作」，也就是三個宣稱支援的任務裡有兩個沒有嚴謹的數字；以及較高層 codebook「較難準確預測」。

讀完後我認為還有幾個該記住的問題。第一，結果宣稱其實相當克制：在 Artificial Analysis 榜上 Elo 1,129、官方排名區間 2–3，95% 信賴區間 [1,116–1,144] 與排名第二的 Mureka V8 [1,134–1,150] 重疊——所以摘要寫的是「competitive performance」而非 SOTA，這個措辭是誠實的，讀者不該過度解讀。第二，主要的自動評估基準是內部的 500 條多語言 benchmark，未公開，外部無法複現。第三，資料來源僅描述為「數千萬首歌」，未交代授權與來源，這在版權與可複現性上都是空白。第四，用 SongBench／Audiobox 這類自動評分當 RL 獎勵，天然帶有 reward hacking 風險——模型可能學會取悅評分器而非人耳，而論文並未報告針對這點的對照分析。第五，EMDC 本質上是對暴露偏差的補丁，它假設上游錯誤率可估且分布可模擬，這個假設在分布外的風格上是否成立沒有驗證。第六，8B + 8B 的規模與 5.5 分鐘的 context，對學術實驗室而言基本無法複現。

The authors state few limitations explicitly, but two are named: "dedicated quantitative evaluation of instrumental and cover song generation remains future work" — two of the three claimed tasks lack rigorous numbers — and that higher codebooks are "more difficult to predict accurately."

Several further caveats are worth carrying away. First, the results claim is notably restrained: Elo 1,129 on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard with an official rank range of 2–3, and a 95% confidence interval of [1,116–1,144] that overlaps the rank-2 system, Mureka V8 at [1,134–1,150]. The abstract's "competitive performance" rather than "state of the art" is honest phrasing and should not be over-read. Second, the primary automatic benchmark is an internal 500-example multilingual set that is not public, so external replication is impossible. Third, training data is described only as "tens of millions of songs" with no account of licensing or provenance — a gap for both copyright and reproducibility. Fourth, using automatic scorers such as SongBench and Audiobox as RL rewards carries inherent reward-hacking risk: the model may learn to please the scorer rather than the ear, and no control analysis addressing this is reported. Fifth, EMDC is fundamentally a patch for exposure bias; it assumes the upstream error rate is estimable and its distribution simulable, an assumption not validated on out-of-distribution styles. Sixth, 8B + 8B parameters with a 5.5-minute context is effectively out of reach for an academic lab.

討論Discussion

對 MIR 研究者，我認為這篇的價值在三個地方。

一、「離散規劃 + 連續渲染」正在成為共識架構。 這不是這篇獨有的想法，但它是把分工講得最清楚的版本之一：token 端負責可控性與長程結構，連續端負責音質。如果你要做音樂生成的控制研究，值得直接站在這個分層上思考——你的控制訊號該進哪一層？結構性的（段落、和弦進行）進 LM，音色性的（音場、質地）進 renderer。

二、MIR 前端正在變成生成系統的控制介面。 兩級旋律模組（MIDI 輪廓 + 幀級 F0）就是傳統 melody extraction 的產物。這代表旋律抽取、F0 估計、和弦辨識這些「老」MIR 任務的精度，現在直接是生成品質的上界——一個很好的論證，可以拿去支持你自己的研究動機。反過來也成立：翻唱生成的失敗案例，可以當成旋律抽取模型的壓力測試集。

三、評估是最大的空缺，也是最好的切入點。 這篇用 SongEval、Audiobox Aesthetics、SongBench 做資料篩選、風格平衡、偏好對建構與 RL 獎勵——整條管線都建立在這些自動評分器之上，但它們與人類判斷的相關性、以及被優化時的退化行為，論文沒有檢驗。對學術實驗室來說，複現這個 8B 系統不可行，但檢驗這些評分器完全可行，而且是目前最缺的工作之一。順帶一提，chroma 預測作為 tokenizer 的多任務目標，也暗示了一個可測的假設：這類 semantic token 在下游和弦／調性 probing 上應該優於純重建 token，這是個小而乾淨的實驗。

For an MIR researcher I see value in three places.

1. "Discrete planning plus continuous rendering" is becoming the consensus architecture. The idea is not unique to this paper, but this is one of the clearest statements of the division: tokens carry controllability and long-range structure, the continuous stage carries audio quality. If you work on control for music generation, it is worth reasoning directly on top of this split — which layer should your control signal enter? Structural signals (sections, chord progressions) belong in the LM; timbral ones (stereo image, texture) in the renderer.

2. MIR front ends are becoming the control interface for generative systems. The two-level melody module (MIDI contour plus frame-level F0) is exactly the output of classical melody extraction. That means the accuracy of melody extraction, F0 estimation and chord recognition now upper-bounds generation quality — a useful argument to motivate your own work. The converse also holds: failure cases in cover generation make a good stress-test set for melody extraction models.

3. Evaluation is the biggest gap and the best point of entry. The paper uses SongEval, Audiobox Aesthetics and SongBench for data filtering, style balancing, preference-pair construction and RL rewards — the entire pipeline rests on these automatic scorers, yet their correlation with human judgement and their degradation under optimisation go unexamined. Reproducing an 8B system is infeasible for an academic lab, but auditing those scorers is entirely feasible and is among the most-needed work right now. Relatedly, chroma prediction as a tokenizer multi-task objective suggests a testable hypothesis: such semantic tokens should outperform reconstruction-only tokens on downstream chord and key probing — a small, clean experiment.
