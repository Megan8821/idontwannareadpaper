What Makes a Good Layer? Assessing the Layer-Wise Intrinsic Properties of Music Foundation Models

什麼樣的層才夠好?音樂基礎模型的逐層內在特性評估

Angelos-Nikolaos Kanatas, Yuexuan Kong, Pablo Alonso-Jiménez, Xavier Serra, Dmitry Bogdanov
arXiv:2608.14819 · cs.SD, cs.LG, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-18
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇的核心主張——「用免訓練的內在指標挑層,可以取代耗資源的逐層 probing」——立得住是因為有數字撐著:拿內在指標選出的前三候選層做加權平均,離最佳單層(oracle)只差 0.3 個百分點,還贏過所有需要訓練的融合方法(加權和 1.8pp、HConv 2.9pp、注意力融合 2.7pp),資料量低於一千筆時差距更大(HConv 掉到 7.7pp)。但這套指標分工明確、不是萬用尺:有效秩、內在維度在調性任務(調性估計、和絃辨識)上幾乎抓不到訊號,論文新提出的「音高移調等變性」(PTE)才是調性任務上唯一穩定為正的指標,平均相關係數達 0.80(音高 0.87、調性 0.83、和絃根音/三和絃 0.59/0.63)。Why this one: The paper's central claim — that label-free intrinsic metrics can replace exhaustive layer-wise probing — holds up because it's backed by numbers: averaging the top-3 proxy-ranked layers with no training closes to within 0.3 percentage points of the oracle best single layer, beating every trainable fusion method (weighted sum 1.8pp, HConv 2.9pp, attentive fusion 2.7pp), with the gap widening further below 1,000 training clips (HConv drops to 7.7pp). But the metrics split labor rather than working universally: effective rank and intrinsic dimension barely track tonal tasks (key estimation, chord recognition), where the paper's new pitch-transposition equivariance (PTE) metric is the only one that stays reliably positive, reaching a mean correlation of 0.80 (pitch 0.87, key 0.83, chord root/triad 0.59/0.63).

動機Motivation

音樂基礎模型(如 MERT、MusicFM、MusicGen)訓練完成後,幾乎都是被凍結起來當特徵萃取器用,但要從哪一層抽取特徵目前「多半是憑經驗決定」——固定用某個深度,或乾脆把好幾層融合起來,背後沒有一套原則性的理解去說明為什麼某些層轉移效果比較好。

作者認為,單純看下游任務的 probing 分數不夠——這種分數主要反映「跟任務相關的訊息是不是線性可分」,而不是描述表徵空間本身是怎麼被組織起來的;而且 probe 的容量、模型架構本身都會影響排名,所以下游表現本身無法完整刻畫表徵品質。他們想問的問題是:能不能找到一組不需要標籤、不需要訓練的「內在指標」,直接從表徵的幾何特性去預測哪一層會是好的特徵萃取層?

Music foundation models such as MERT, MusicFM, and MusicGen are almost always frozen after pretraining and used purely as feature extractors, but which layer to extract from is, in the authors' words, largely heuristic — a fixed depth or an ad hoc multi-layer fusion, with no principled account of why some layers transfer better than others.

The authors argue that downstream probing scores alone are insufficient: they mostly reflect whether task-relevant information happens to be linearly separable, not how the representation space itself is organized, and probe capacity plus architecture choices can distort the ranking. Their question is whether a set of label-free, training-free intrinsic metrics can predict, directly from a layer's geometric properties, which layer will make a good feature extractor.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

內在指標這條路子在音樂領域之外已經有人做過:電腦視覺的 RankMe 把嵌入的有效秩跟下游遷移能力連結起來;LiDAR 針對聯合嵌入架構把秩的評估做得更細緻;語音與音訊領域也有以秩為基礎的模型評估方法;幾何分析(內在維度、各向異性、曲率)則揭露了不同訓練目標下,表徵性質隨深度變化的階段性轉折。

音樂領域本身也有零星觀察:Jukebox 的中間層在 MIR 任務上表現最好;MusicFM、MuQ 呈現「淺層偏聲學、深層偏語意」的趨勢,而且中間層常常打得贏加權融合;MERT、MusicGen 上,內容屬性(音高、和絃根音)隨深度愈來愈容易被線性探測到,風格屬性則不然。這些過去的工作大多是針對特定任務做 probing、做局部介入,或做事後的特徵探勘,並沒有系統性地刻畫表徵幾何本身。

這篇論文的差異在於它是「第一個」對 12 個音樂基礎模型、橫跨三種預訓練典範(遮罩建模、自迴歸、對比學習)做系統性逐層內在特性分析,而且直接去測試這些免標籤指標能不能當作實務上的選層依據,而不是像過去那樣停留在觀察層級。

Intrinsic-metric approaches predate this outside music: in vision, RankMe links an embedding's effective rank to downstream transfer; LiDAR refines rank assessment for joint-embedding architectures; rank-based measures also support model assessment in speech and audio; and geometric analyses of intrinsic dimension, anisotropy, and curvature reveal objective-dependent phase transitions across depth.

Music-specific observations exist too: Jukebox's intermediate layers peak on MIR tasks; MusicFM and MuQ show an acoustic-to-semantic trend across depth, with intermediate layers often beating weighted fusion; and on MERT and MusicGen, content attributes like pitch and chord root become more linearly accessible with depth while style attributes do not. Prior work here mostly relied on task-specific probing, targeted interventions, or post-hoc feature discovery rather than systematically characterizing representation geometry itself.

This paper's difference is being the first systematic layer-wise analysis of intrinsic representation properties across 12 music foundation models spanning three pretraining paradigms (masked modeling, autoregressive, contrastive), and the first to directly test whether these label-free metrics work as a practical layer-selection tool rather than staying at the level of observation.

方法Method

評測對象是 12 個音樂基礎模型,分三種預訓練典範:遮罩建模(MERT-95M/330M、MusicFM_MSD、MuQ_iter、OMAR-RQ,12 到 24 層、95M 到 580M 參數)、自迴歸(MusicGen-S/M/L、YuE-0.5B/7B,24 到 48 層、0.3B 到 7B 參數)、對比學習(LAION-CLAP、Myna-Base,12 到 21 層、22M 到 73M 參數)。所有模型凍結,表徵接一個 512 維隱藏層的淺層 MLP 去做 15 個下游任務(調性、節奏、音色、語意、相似度五大類),沿用 MARBLE 的受限賽道設定。

內在指標包含:內在維度(用 TwoNN 估計)、有效秩(RankMe,對奇異值分布算 Shannon 熵再取指數)、各向異性(最大奇異值平方佔總變異的比例)、時間曲率(相鄰時間步表徵位移向量的夾角期望值)、LiDAR(把原始片段當類別、增強視圖當同類樣本,算 LDA 矩陣的有效秩)、InfoNCE(對增強視圖的對比損失)。

最關鍵的新指標是「音高移調等變性」(PTE),改編自 STONE 的交叉功率頻譜密度目標:線性探針把序列層級嵌入映射到 12 維調性剖面(投影到五度圈上),同一個探針分別用在原始片段與升 k 個半音的版本上,若探針具備移調等變性,交叉功率的相位應該剛好是 -2πωk/12;論文用 10,000 段 MTG-Jamendo 音檔(70/15/15 切分)訓練,PTE 分數定義為 1 減去均方根和絃距離的一半,愈接近 1 代表 12 平均律的移調結構愈容易被解碼出來。

The evaluation covers 12 music foundation models across three pretraining paradigms: masked modeling (MERT-95M/330M, MusicFM_MSD, MuQ_iter, OMAR-RQ; 12–24 layers, 95M–580M parameters), autoregressive (MusicGen-S/M/L, YuE-0.5B/7B; 24–48 layers, 0.3B–7B parameters), and contrastive learning (LAION-CLAP, Myna-Base; 12–21 layers, 22M–73M parameters). All models stay frozen, with representations fed into a shallow MLP with a single 512-unit hidden layer for 15 downstream tasks across five families — tonal, rhythm, timbre, semantic, similarity — following MARBLE's constrained-track protocol.

The intrinsic metrics are: intrinsic dimension (estimated via TwoNN), effective rank (RankMe — the exponentiated Shannon entropy of the singular-value distribution), anisotropy (the fraction of total variance in the dominant singular direction), temporal curvature (the expected angle between consecutive representation-displacement vectors), LiDAR (treating each original clip as a class and its augmented views as within-class samples, then computing the effective rank of the resulting LDA matrix), and InfoNCE (a contrastive loss over augmented views).

The key new metric is pitch-transposition equivariance (PTE), adapted from STONE's cross-power spectral density objective: a linear probe maps a sequence-level embedding to a 12-dimensional key-signature profile projected onto the circle of fifths, and the same probe is applied to both the original clip and a version transposed up k semitones — an equivariant probe should show a cross-power phase of exactly -2πωk/12. Trained on 10,000 MTG-Jamendo clips (70/15/15 split), PTE is scored as 1 minus half the RMS chordal distance, with values near 1 meaning the 12-tone transposition structure is easily decoded.

限制Limitations

論文自己點名的限制是:這些指標與下游表現之間只是「相關」不是「因果」——「這些指標找出的是跟遷移能力較強相關聯的表徵特性,但並沒有證實直接去操弄這些特性就會改變下游表現」。訓練目標、監督方式、資料、架構、規模在現有模型之間本來就是糾纏在一起的,要真正拆解各自的貢獻需要做控制變因的預訓練實驗,而這篇沒有做。

部分指標本身就是「典範相依」的——像 LiDAR、InfoNCE 這類增強條件式的指標,原本就是針對特定訓練典範提出並驗證,拿到別的典範上可能失準;而且跨模型比較時,原始指標數值不能直接拿來比,因為架構本身異質性太高(有效秩的跨模型相關係數只有 0.02,幾乎跟哪一層最好無關),只有 PTE 在跨模型上仍然保有穩定的調性訊號。

The authors' own stated limitation is that the metrics are only correlational, not causal: "these metrics identify representation properties associated with stronger transfer, but do not establish whether directly manipulating them would affect downstream performance." Objective, supervision, data, architecture, and scale all co-vary across the models tested, and disentangling their individual contributions would require controlled pretraining experiments, which this paper does not run.

Some metrics are themselves paradigm-dependent — augmentation-conditioned metrics like LiDAR and InfoNCE were proposed and validated within specific training paradigms and may not transfer to others — and raw metric values are not directly comparable across architecturally heterogeneous models (effective rank's cross-model correlation with which layer is actually best drops to just 0.02). Only PTE retains a stable tonal signal across models.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最值得討論的地方,是它明確拆開了「一體適用」的幻覺:沒有任何單一內在指標可以同時預測所有 MIR 任務的表徵品質——語意任務靠內在維度,節奏任務靠曲率(愈平滑的時間軌跡遷移愈好),調性任務則非得靠論文自己造的 PTE 不可,標準的秩、維度指標在調性任務上幾乎失效。這種「按任務家族分工」的結論,比起宣稱找到一個萬用指標,誠實得多也更有操作性。

這套方法的實務價值在於把逐層 probing 需要跑的次數砍到四分之一到十六分之一,而且低資料量情境下(例如 GTZAN-Genre 只有 442 筆)所有需要訓練的融合方法都輸給 oracle 十個百分點以上,免訓練的 proxy-guided 方法反而穩得多——這對資源有限、無法把每個下游任務都跑一輪完整 probing 的研究者來說,是比再訓練一個更好的融合層更划算的路。

The most discussion-worthy part of this paper is how cleanly it dismantles the one-size-fits-all fantasy: no single intrinsic metric predicts representation quality across all MIR tasks — semantic tasks track intrinsic dimension, rhythm tasks track curvature (smoother temporal trajectories transfer better), and tonal tasks require the paper's own PTE metric, since standard rank and dimension metrics nearly fail there. A conclusion organized by task family is more honest and more actionable than claiming to have found one universal proxy.

The practical payoff is cutting the number of layer-wise probing runs needed by 4 to 16 times, and in low-data settings (GTZAN-Genre has only 442 clips) every trainable fusion method falls more than 10 percentage points behind the oracle while the training-free proxy-guided approach stays far more stable — a better use of limited compute than training yet another fusion layer, for researchers who can't afford to run full probing on every downstream task.

Dialogue-Aware Video-to-Music Generation Using Public Domain Film Collections

對話感知的影片轉音樂生成:以公版電影收藏建構可重現資料集

Haven Kim, Zachary Novack, Julian McAuley, Hao-Wen Dong
arXiv:2608.11576 · cs.SD, cs.CV, cs.MM · 投稿submitted 2026-08-13
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文最踏實的地方不是「對話能幫助配樂生成」這句主張本身,而是它老實攤開了成效因模型而異的全貌:在 VidMuse 上加入對話調變後,KL 散度在兩個測試集上都明顯下降(商業片測試集 OES-Com 從 1.47 降到 0.85,自建的 OSSL-v2 測試集從 0.94 降到 0.82),但在 GVMGen 上卻是「跨域表現變好、同域表現變差」——OES-Com 上的 KL 從 0.97 降到 0.87,OSSL-v2 上的 CLAP 相似度反而從 0.43 掉到 0.39。三個基準模型裡,Diff-V2M 的 Precision 在兩個測試集都是 0.00、FAD 高達 119 到 130,說明這個基準線本身生成的音樂幾乎完全落在參考分布之外,對話調變在這種情況下能做的有限。Why this one: The most grounded part of this paper isn't the claim that dialogue helps soundtrack generation, but its honest admission that the effect is model-dependent: adding the dialogue adapter to VidMuse clearly lowers KL divergence on both test sets (from 1.47 to 0.85 on the commercial-film set OES-Com, from 0.94 to 0.82 on the self-hosted OSSL-v2 set), but on GVMGen the effect flips — out-of-distribution performance improves (OES-Com KL: 0.97 to 0.87) while in-distribution performance degrades (OSSL-v2 CLAP similarity drops from 0.43 to 0.39). Of the three baselines, Diff-V2M's Precision is 0.00 on both test sets with FAD as high as 119–130, meaning that baseline's generated music falls almost entirely outside the reference distribution — there's only so much a dialogue adapter can do there.

動機Motivation

影片轉音樂生成這個領域長期缺乏可重現的訓練資料集。多數系統依賴從網路爬來的語料庫,發布時只給 YouTube 或 TikTok 的網址清單——作者觀察到,前人整理的一個 300 支影片的基準資料集,兩年內就有超過一成的影片下架或連結失效;就算連結還在,平台的速率限制也讓重新下載變得曠日費時。而先前少數自建、自我託管的資料集規模又太小,像 Open Screen Soundtrack Library 只有 736 個片段、共 36.5 小時。

音樂在電影裡承擔情緒傳達與觀眾詮釋引導的功能。作者想解決的不只是「資料集會腐朽」這個工程問題,還想問一個內容問題:電影配樂跟畫面上的對白之間,是不是存在值得建模的時間對齊關係?他們發現逐畫格的對白與音樂響度(LUFS)之間有微弱負相關(Pearson r = -0.11),這暗示對白或許值得當成一個明確的、逐畫格的條件訊號,而不只是被埋在整體畫面特徵裡。

Video-to-music generation has long lacked reproducible training datasets. Most systems depend on web-scraped corpora distributed only as lists of YouTube or TikTok URLs — the authors observed that more than 10% of the videos in one prior 300-video benchmark had gone offline or had broken links within two years, and even when links survive, platform rate limits make re-downloading prohibitively slow. The few prior self-hosted datasets, meanwhile, are too small — the Open Screen Soundtrack Library holds only 736 clips totaling 36.5 hours.

Film music also carries emotional and interpretive weight for viewers. Beyond the engineering problem of link rot, the authors ask a content question: is there a modelable temporal relationship between film music and on-screen dialogue? They find a weak negative correlation between per-frame dialogue and music loudness in LUFS (Pearson r = -0.11), suggesting dialogue may deserve explicit, frame-local conditioning rather than being buried inside global visual features.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

目前主流的影片轉音樂系統各有取徑:VidMuse 是以 MusicGen 為骨幹的自迴歸模型,靠 CLIP 畫格嵌入建立局部視覺分支;GVMGen 同樣以 MusicGen 為骨幹,但用 Q-Former 壓縮影片特徵再做平均池化;Diff-V2M 則是以 Stable Audio Open 為基礎的潛在擴散模型,用逐時間網格的交叉注意力機制。另外還有 Sonique 這類使用不成對資料訓練的系統,因為訓練資料形式不同,這篇論文的比較裡把它排除在外。

這篇論文的差異不在提出新的生成骨幹,三個基準模型的架構都原封不動保留,新意在於額外注入一個「對話感知」的條件訊號:把對白同樣做音源分離抽取出來,逐畫格轉成 FiLM(feature-wise linear modulation)參數去調變原有的影片條件向量,而且模組的最後一層做零初始化,保證加了這個模組之後,模型在初始化時等同於沒加。GVMGen 原本會把時間軸靠 Q-Former 加平均池化整個壓掉,論文為了讓對話調變有時間軸可以對齊,額外幫它補回逐畫格的位置編碼。

Current video-to-music systems take different approaches: VidMuse is a MusicGen-backbone autoregressive model with a local visual branch built on CLIP frame embeddings; GVMGen also uses a MusicGen backbone but compresses video features with a Q-Former followed by mean-pooling; Diff-V2M is a Stable Audio Open-based latent diffusion model using temporal-grid cross-attention. Sonique, which trains on unpaired data, is excluded from this paper's comparisons because of that mismatched data format.

This paper's difference isn't a new generative backbone — all three baseline architectures are kept unchanged — but an added dialogue-aware conditioning signal: dialogue is source-separated the same way as music, then converted frame-by-frame into FiLM (feature-wise linear modulation) parameters that modulate the existing video conditioning vector, with the module's final layer zero-initialized so the model is identical to the unmodified baseline at initialization. Since GVMGen originally collapses the entire time axis via Q-Former plus mean-pooling, the authors add back frame-level positional embeddings so dialogue conditioning has a time axis to align against.

方法Method

資料集 OSSL-v2 收錄 1,886 部公版電影裡的 34,343 個片段,共 246.4 小時,平均每段 28.6 秒。建置流程是:先用開源的電影音源分離模型(選品質較高但速度慢三倍的設定)把音樂從電影混音裡分出來;再用開源的事件偵測模型判斷 527 個聲音類別,人工從中選出 157 個屬於音樂的類別(例如「trance music」),抓出「音樂機率連續超過非音樂機率至少十秒」的片段,並排除非音樂機率超過 0.05 這個門檻的片段。

對話感知模組的數學形式很單純:把逐畫格的影片條件向量記為 v_i,對白同樣經過音源分離、再用一維卷積編碼成 u_i,一個雙層 MLP φ 把 u_i 映射成 FiLM 參數 (γ_i, β_i),最後用 ṽ_i = (1+γ_i)⊙v_i + β_i 調變原本的影片向量。訓練片段長度統一裁成 10 秒(OSSL-v2 裡最短片段的長度),推論時則生成完整長度的配樂。

OSSL-v2 contains 34,343 clips from 1,886 public-domain films, totaling 246.4 hours with an average clip length of 28.6 seconds. The pipeline first applies an open-source cinematic source-separation model (using its slower, higher-quality setting) to pull music out of the film mix, then an open-source event-detection model scores 527 sound categories, from which 157 music-related categories (e.g. "trance music") are manually selected; segments are kept where music probability exceeds non-music probability for at least 10 consecutive seconds, excluding any segment where non-music probability exceeds an empirically set 0.05 threshold.

The dialogue-aware module is mathematically simple: let the per-frame video conditioning vector be v_i; dialogue, separated the same way as music, is encoded via a 1-D convolution into u_i; a two-layer MLP φ maps u_i to FiLM parameters (γ_i, β_i); and the video vector is modulated as ṽ_i = (1+γ_i)⊙v_i + β_i. Training segments are cropped to a uniform 10 seconds (the length of OSSL-v2's shortest clips), while inference generates full-length soundtracks.

限制Limitations

論文並沒有專闢一節寫限制,但從結果本身就能看出侷限:訓練片段被裁成 10 秒(受限於 OSSL-v2 最短片段的長度),這可能限制模型捕捉較長時間結構的能力;對話訊號的品質完全依賴上游音源分離模型分得乾不乾淨。更直接的侷限是效果不穩定——GVMGen 加了對話調變之後,同分布測試集(OSSL-v2)的表現不升反降(CLAP 從 0.43 掉到 0.39,KL 從 0.72 微幅惡化到 0.73),意味著這個模組在訓練資料上可能有過度正則化的傾向;Diff-V2M 不管加不加對話,Precision 都固定在 0.00,FAD 都在 119 以上,對話調變在這個基準線上幾乎起不了作用,論文也沒有解釋為什麼。

The paper has no dedicated limitations section, but the limits show up in the results themselves: training segments are cropped to 10 seconds because that's the length of OSSL-v2's shortest clips, which may cap the model's ability to capture longer-range structure, and dialogue-signal quality depends entirely on how cleanly the upstream source-separation model isolates it. More directly, the effect is unstable — adding the dialogue adapter to GVMGen makes in-distribution performance on OSSL-v2 worse rather than better (CLAP drops from 0.43 to 0.39, KL edges up from 0.72 to 0.73), suggesting the module may overregularize on training data; and Diff-V2M's Precision stays fixed at 0.00 with FAD above 119 whether or not dialogue is added, meaning the adapter does almost nothing for that baseline, a gap the paper doesn't explain.

討論Discussion

這篇論文比較值得談的地方,其實是資料集本身的定位選擇,而不是對話調變的效果:作者刻意用「自我託管的公版電影片段」取代「網址清單」,用工程手段解決學術基準會隨時間腐朽的問題,這比大多數方法論文更關心可重現性這件無聊但重要的事。

至於對話調變本身,論文的說法是「主要在配對層面的忠實度(paired fidelity,例如 CLAP、KL)上看到穩定進步,而不是整體生成分布的改變(distributional fidelity,例如 FAD、Precision)」,而且效果在商業片測試集(跨域)比在 OSSL-v2(同域)上更明顯——作者把這解釋成對話調變像是一種正則化,但反過來看,這也代表這個訊號目前只能微調生成結果貼合既有對白的程度,還沒有辦法改變模型本身「該生成什麼樣的音樂」的整體判斷。三個基準模型裡效果最不穩定的 GVMGen,剛好就是同分布測試集上表現最好的模型,兩者放在一起看,說明對話這個訊號目前更像是錦上添花,而不是解決生成品質的根本手段。

What's more worth discussing here is the dataset's own framing choice rather than the dialogue adapter's effect: the authors deliberately replace a list of URLs with self-hosted public-domain film clips, using an engineering fix for the problem of academic benchmarks decaying over time — a concern for reproducibility that most modeling papers treat as an afterthought.

As for the dialogue adapter itself, the paper's own framing is that gains show up mainly in paired fidelity (CLAP, KL) rather than distributional fidelity (FAD, Precision), and are more pronounced on the commercial-film (out-of-distribution) test set than on OSSL-v2 (in-distribution) — the authors read this as dialogue acting like a regularizer. But read the other way, it also means the signal currently only nudges generated output to better match existing dialogue, without changing the model's overall judgment of what music to generate. GVMGen, the baseline whose dialogue effect is least stable, happens to be the one that performs best in-distribution; taken together, dialogue conditioning here reads more as icing than as a fix for the underlying generation quality problem.

H2H Music Improv: A Communication Model and Audio-Visual Dataset for Music Improvisation

H2H 音樂即興:即興演奏的溝通模型與影音資料集

Aleksandra Teng Ma, Anthony Cammarota, Jiayi Wang, Alexandria Smith, Cheng-Zhi Anna Huang, Jeffrey Albert, Alexander Lerch
arXiv:2608.13957 · cs.SD, cs.MM, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-17
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文最有意思的證據不是「即興演奏需要溝通模型」這句主張,而是雙方標註不吻合的程度本身就是資料:當兩位樂手都同意自己身處「穩定」狀態時,標註重疊的中位數高達 81.3%,但一旦進入「提案」狀態,重疊中位數只剩 23.6%,「協商」狀態更誇張——37 段錄音裡有 27 段,雙方標註完全零重疊。這組數字反過來證成了論文的核心設計:意圖與感知之間的落差不是雜訊,而是即興演奏裡真實存在、值得用資料捕捉下來的現象。Why this one: The most interesting evidence here isn't the claim that improvisation needs a communication model, but the degree of disagreement between the two musicians' annotations, which is itself the finding: when both musicians agree they're in a "stability" state, the median annotation overlap is 81.3%, but that drops to a median of 23.6% during "proposal," and further collapses during "negotiation" — 27 of the 37 recorded clips show zero overlap between the two musicians' annotations. That gap is what validates the paper's core design choice: the mismatch between intention and perception isn't noise, it's a real phenomenon in improvisation worth capturing as data.

動機Motivation

現有的即時人機即興系統,大多把「溝通」當成事後補上去的功能,靠明確的控制項與預先設計好的互動模式疊加在演算法之上,而不是把溝通當成演算法設計本身的核心考量。目前也還沒有一套形式化、機器可讀的溝通模型,能夠捕捉真正的樂手在互動時實際上是怎麼運作的。

作者認為,要讓人類與機器學習系統之間真正產生創意上的化學反應,雙方必須能夠即時、有意義地溝通。自由即興——不受既定曲風或事前約定限制——提供了研究溝通最原始形式的絕佳場域,因為樂手之間除了聲音本身,沒有其他共享的框架可以依靠。

Most real-time human-AI improvisation systems treat communication as something bolted on afterward — explicit controls and pre-designed interaction modes layered on top of an algorithm — rather than as a core concern of the algorithm's design itself. No formalized, machine-readable communication model yet exists that captures how expert musicians actually interact.

The authors argue that genuine creative chemistry between humans and ML systems requires real-time, meaningful communication. Free improvisation — unconstrained by pre-existing style or prior agreement — offers an ideal setting for studying communication in its least constrained form, since musicians have nothing but sound itself as a shared frame of reference.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

過去的 AI 即興系統各有各的做法:IRCAM 的 Somax2 用針對特定語料訓練的 factor oracle,靠明確的參數控制做出「相互影響」;Spire Muse 靠回饋按鈕(表示贊同或不贊同)去自動切換互動策略;jam_bot 微調基於 transformer 的音樂語言模型,用固定時間間隔觸發生成;George Lewis 在一九八零年代做的 Voyager 是早期「非階層式、主體對主體」的模型,完全靠聲音溝通,但骨子裡還是規則導向;Heretic 把機器聆聽綁定在特定演奏者的框架上,用神經網路把演奏分類進 Anthony Braxton 的 Language Music 分類系統裡。

這些系統的共通點,是把互動模式當成一組可以切換的「策略」或「模式」去設計,沒有一個是先去研究真人樂手怎麼溝通、再把那套溝通邏輯形式化。這篇論文的差異在於:它是第一個從專家即興樂手的實際互動歸納出正式溝通模型的研究,也是第一個提供帶標註的影音即興資料集(六小時),而且用雙邊標註同時記錄「自己的意圖」與「感知到對方的意圖」,直接把過去被忽略的「意圖與感知落差」變成資料的一部分。

Prior AI improvisation systems each took a different approach: IRCAM's Somax2 uses factor oracles trained on a specific corpus with explicit parameter controls to create mutual influence; Spire Muse uses feedback buttons signaling approval or disapproval to automatically switch interaction strategies; jam_bot fine-tunes transformer-based music language models, triggering generation at fixed time intervals; George Lewis's 1980s Voyager was an early nonhierarchical, subject-to-subject model communicating entirely through sound, but remained fundamentally rule-based; and Heretic grounds machine listening in a specific practitioner's framework, classifying playing into Anthony Braxton's Language Music categories via neural networks.

What these systems share is treating interaction as a set of switchable strategies or modes designed in, rather than starting from how real musicians actually communicate and formalizing that logic. This paper's difference is being the first to derive a formal communication model from expert improvisers' actual interactions, and the first to provide an annotated audio-visual improvisation dataset (six hours) with dual-sided annotation that separately records "my own intention" and "my perception of my partner's intention" — turning the previously overlooked intention-perception gap directly into data.

方法Method

溝通模型是七人跨領域團隊(即興演奏、音樂資訊檢索、機器學習、人機互動)花六個月、每兩個月開會一次,在「演奏場次」與「跨領域討論」之間反覆迭代出來的。前導階段找兩位專業即興樂手試玩,發現一個關鍵洞察:樂手對「同一個當下」常有不同的詮釋,「做出的意圖」跟「被對方感知到的意圖」之間存在落差。

模型分成兩層:「時間點動作」有兩種——「發起」(引入新的音樂想法,或宣告進入新的聲響空間,標誌著偏離目前軌跡的任何姿態)與「回應」(接住對方發起的想法,依定義一定跟在對方的發起之後);「狀態」則是動作序列組成的——「提案」(從發起開始到獲得回應為止的這段時間)、「穩定」(獲得回應之後,雙方一起發展這個想法,或各自維持平行的聲響線)、「協商」(一連串發起都沒有得到回應,雙方還在找共識)。

錄音安排是兩位樂手在隔音的房間裡,靠低延遲的個人監聽系統維持聲音溝通,並透過窗戶保持視覺接觸;音訊以 48kHz/16bit 單聲道用 Pro Tools 錄製,兩台同步的 SONY HXR-NX3 攝影機拍攝影像。每次演奏事前沒有約定曲風、速度、調性或角色分工。標註是演奏結束後才做,兩位樂手各自獨立在 Reaper 裡標記時間點,同時標出「自己的意圖」跟「感知到對方的意圖」,而且只標「宣告新聲響空間開始」的發起,不標既有空間裡的發展。

The communication model was developed by a seven-person interdisciplinary team (improvisation, MIR, ML, HCI) meeting bimonthly over six months, iterating between playing sessions and interdisciplinary discussion. Pilot sessions with two expert improvisers surfaced a key insight: musicians often hold different interpretations of the same moment, and a gap exists between the intention produced and the intention perceived by the partner.

The model has two layers. Point-in-time actions are of two kinds: initiate (introducing a new musical idea or signaling a new sonic space — any gesture marking a departure from the current trajectory) and acknowledge (taking up a preceding initiation; by definition it follows the partner's initiation). States are sequences of actions: proposal (the stretch from an initiation's start until it receives acknowledgment), stability (the stretch after acknowledgment during which both musicians develop the idea together or maintain parallel streams), and negotiation (one or more initiations in search of alignment, none met with acknowledgment).

Recording paired two musicians in acoustically isolated rooms, maintaining sound communication via a low-latency personal monitoring system and visual contact through a window; audio was recorded mono at 48kHz/16-bit via Pro Tools, video by two synchronized SONY HXR-NX3 camcorders. No style, tempo, key, or role division was agreed beforehand. Annotation happened after playing: each musician independently placed timemarks in Reaper marking both their own intentions and their perception of the partner's intentions, annotating only initiations that signal the start of a new sonic space, not development within an established one.

限制Limitations

論文自己點出模型抓不到的兩種邊緣案例:「快速回應但沒有真的接住」(樂手短暫回應一下,但很快又回到自己原本的素材)以及「漸進式收斂」(兩條平行的聲響線不是靠一次明確的發起或回應事件,而是隨時間慢慢融合)。另外,連續五場一小時的演奏場次出現「創作疲勞」,樂手回報長時間待在同一個音樂環境裡會影響創意狀態。

樣本規模與代表性也有限:只找了五位專業即興樂手(六種配對組合),而且不具人口代表性——樂手性別偏男性,全數受過正規訓練,且都在美國亞特蘭大。銅管樂器因為動態範圍寬,錄音裡偶爾出現削波(clipping)的音質問題。

The paper names two edge cases its model doesn't capture: rapid acknowledgment without uptake (a musician briefly responds but quickly returns to their own independent material) and gradual convergence (two parallel streams slowly merge over time rather than through one discrete initiate or acknowledge event). Separately, the last five one-hour sessions showed creative fatigue, with musicians reporting that sustained exposure to the same musical environment affected their creative state.

Sample size and representativeness are also limited: only five expert improvisers across six pairings, and the pool is not demographically representative — it skews male, is entirely formally trained, and all based in Atlanta, Georgia. Horn instruments, due to their wide dynamic range, occasionally show clipping artifacts in the recordings.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最耐人尋味的地方,是它把「意圖與感知不一致」這件事從噪音重新定位成研究對象:穩定狀態下 81.3% 的高度一致,對照協商狀態下 37 段裡有 27 段完全零重疊,兩個數字放在一起,直接證明了樂手之間的溝通落差不是隨機誤差,而是即興演奏的結構性特徵——這也是為什麼論文堅持要做「雙邊標註」而不是找第三方來標一個「客觀」答案的原因。

論文裡樂手自陳的觀察也提供了具體的信號線索,比方吉他手一號提到「音色才是不變的參數,音高才是可變的」,而且「沉默可靠地預示接下來會有發起」,這類質性描述雖然沒有統計檢定支撐,但配上前述的量化落差數字,共同構成一份給 AI 即興系統設計者的具體參考清單——而不是像過去的系統一樣,自己假設一套互動模式,再回頭看樂手買不買單。角色動態部分也有一個量化觀察:吉他手一號在感知到的發起裡平均佔了約 63%,顯示即使在「非階層式」的自由即興裡,主導權分布仍然可能不均等。

The most intriguing part of this paper is how it reframes the intention-perception mismatch from noise into the object of study: 81.3% agreement during stability, against zero overlap in 27 of 37 clips during negotiation — put side by side, these numbers show the communication gap between musicians isn't random error but a structural feature of improvisation itself, which is exactly why the paper insists on dual-sided annotation rather than having a third party mark one "objective" answer.

The musicians' own reported observations also supply concrete signal cues — one guitarist noted that "texture is the immutable parameter, pitch is mutable," and that "silence reliably predicts a forthcoming initiation." These qualitative notes aren't backed by statistical tests on their own, but paired with the quantitative gap above, they form a concrete reference list for AI improvisation system designers, rather than the older pattern of a system assuming an interaction mode first and checking afterward whether musicians accept it. There's also a quantitative note on role dynamics: one guitarist accounted for roughly 63% of perceived initiations, showing that even in "nonhierarchical" free improvisation, leadership can still be unevenly distributed.
