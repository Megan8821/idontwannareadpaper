FUSEMOS: Perceptual Evaluation of Text-to-Music Generation with Dual-Encoder Fusion and Ranking-Aware Composite Loss

FUSEMOS：CLAP 與 MERT 兩條分支各自預測分數、再用一層線性層合併，並以截斷迴歸加成對排序損失訓練的文字生成音樂主觀分數預測器

Jing Yang, Haoyu Wang, Ningning Pan, Zhao Wang, Jianxuan Yang, Gongping Huang
arXiv:2611.07562 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：MusicEval 的官方基線只用 CLAP 一個編碼器預測整體音樂印象（OMI）和文字對齊（TA）兩種 MOS；這篇加上凍結的 MERT-v1-95M 分支，兩條分支各自出分數，再用一層線性層合併，並把 L1 換成截斷迴歸加成對排序的複合損失。選它是因為自動評分器正在變成文字生成音樂論文的預設評估工具，它到底多準直接影響後續論文的結論。在 MusicEval 官方切分上，OMI 的句子層級 SRCC 從 0.637 升到 0.811，TA 從 0.495 升到 0.644。但拆開看，光換架構（仍用 L1）就把 OMI SRCC 拉到 0.797，複合損失只再加 0.014；結論裡「U-MSE 降五成以上、S-MSE 降八成以上」也只對 OMI 成立，TA 分別是 31% 與 72%。Why this one: The official MusicEval baseline predicts both MOS targets, overall musical impression (OMI) and textual alignment (TA), from a single CLAP encoder; this paper adds a frozen MERT-v1-95M branch, lets each branch output its own scores, merges them with one linear layer, and replaces L1 with a composite of truncated regression and pairwise ranking losses. I picked it because learned scorers are becoming the default evaluation tool in text-to-music papers, so how accurate they are directly affects later papers' conclusions. On the official MusicEval split, utterance-level SRCC rises from 0.637 to 0.811 for OMI and from 0.495 to 0.644 for TA. Broken down, though, the architecture change alone (still trained with L1) already brings OMI SRCC to 0.797, and the composite loss adds only 0.014 more; the conclusion's claim of over 50% lower U-MSE and over 80% lower S-MSE also holds only for OMI, where TA gets 31% and 72%.

動機Motivation

文字生成音樂（text-to-music, TTM）系統的主觀聽測（MOS）是公認的黃金標準，但又貴又難重現；FAD 這類客觀指標便宜，卻和人類偏好相關性弱。MusicEval（Liu 等，ICASSP 2025）提供了 31 個生成系統、384 條文字提示、2748 段音樂的專家評分，分成 OMI 與 TA 兩個分數，並附一個只用 CLAP 的基線：音訊嵌入接 MLP 預測 OMI，音訊與文字嵌入串接後預測 TA。

作者的出發點有兩個。CLAP 主要為跨模態對齊而訓練，對音色、表現力這類細部音樂屬性抓得不夠；而 MSE 只管絕對誤差，不管人類評分本質上是排序判斷。這兩個觀察分別對應到論文的兩個改動：加一個音樂專用的自監督編碼器，以及加一個排序導向的損失。

Listening tests (MOS) are the accepted gold standard for text-to-music (TTM) systems, but they are expensive and hard to reproduce; objective metrics such as FAD are cheap but correlate weakly with human preference. MusicEval (Liu et al., ICASSP 2025) supplies expert ratings for 2,748 clips from 31 generation systems under 384 text prompts, split into an OMI score and a TA score, together with a CLAP-only baseline: an MLP on the audio embedding predicts OMI, and the concatenated audio and text embeddings predict TA.

The authors start from two observations. CLAP is trained mainly for cross-modal alignment and captures fine musical attributes such as timbre and expressiveness poorly; and MSE only penalises absolute error, while human ratings are inherently ordinal judgments. These map onto the paper's two changes: adding a music-specific self-supervised encoder, and adding a ranking-oriented loss.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的比較對象是 MusicEval 自己的 CLAP 基線，它沿用語音 MOS 預測那一路的做法：SSL-MOS 與 UTMOS（Saeki 等，2022）都是在自監督語音模型上接一個迴歸頭。這篇的成對排序損失其實就是 UTMOS 的 contrastive loss 原封不動搬過來（論文有引用 UTMOS 與 SESQA），截斷迴歸損失則來自 MBNet（Leng 等，2021），兩者都不是新設計，新的是把它們組合用在音樂 MOS 上。

「雙編碼器晚期融合」說穿了是兩個預測器的分數層級集成：最後那層線性層只吃兩個純量，等於學一組加權平均，和 VoiceMOS Challenge 上常見的多 SSL 模型集成、以及 UTMOS 本身的 stacking 是同一類技巧。音樂側相關的有 Audiobox Aesthetics（Tjandra 等，2025）與 SongEval（Yao 等，2025），它們只評音質或美學、不看文字對齊。另外，AudioMOS Challenge 2025 的第一軌就是在 MusicEval 上預測這兩種分數，論文沒有引用，也沒有和參賽系統比較，所以「超越 SOTA」只是相對於 MusicEval 基線而言。

The most direct point of comparison is MusicEval's own CLAP baseline, which follows the speech MOS prediction line: SSL-MOS and UTMOS (Saeki et al., 2022) both attach a regression head to a self-supervised speech model. The pairwise ranking loss here is in fact UTMOS's contrastive loss carried over unchanged (the paper cites UTMOS and SESQA), and the truncated regression loss comes from MBNet (Leng et al., 2021); neither is a new design, and what is new is combining them for music MOS.

The dual-encoder late fusion is, at bottom, a score-level ensemble of two predictors: the final linear layer takes just two scalars, so it learns a weighted average, the same family of tricks as the multi-SSL ensembles common in the VoiceMOS Challenge and the stacking inside UTMOS itself. On the music side, Audiobox Aesthetics (Tjandra et al., 2025) and SongEval (Yao et al., 2025) rate quality or aesthetics only, without text alignment. Also, Track 1 of the AudioMOS Challenge 2025 predicts exactly these two scores on MusicEval; the paper neither cites it nor compares with its entries, so its claim to beat the SOTA is relative to the MusicEval baseline only.

方法Method

CLAP 分支用 LAION 的音樂版 CLAP 檢查點並做微調。TA 的粗估直接取文字與音樂嵌入的餘弦相似度，不經任何可學參數；OMI 的粗估則把音樂嵌入送進三層 MLP。MERT 分支凍結 MERT-v1-95M，取最後一層隱藏狀態，用一個可學查詢向量做多頭注意力池化成單一向量；OMI 由此向量接 MLP 預測，TA 則把它和 CLAP 文字嵌入串接、過 LayerNorm 再接 MLP。作者特意不在 MERT 與 CLAP 文字之間算餘弦，因為兩者不在同一個空間。

融合層把兩條分支的 OMI（或 TA）各一個純量串成二維向量，送進一層線性層得到最終分數。損失是 β·L_con + γ·L_trun：L_con 對批次內每一對樣本，懲罰「真實分差」與「預測分差」之差超過邊界 α=0.2 的部分；L_trun 是忽略小於 τ=0.3 誤差的 L1。β=0.5、γ=1.0，以 SGD（學習率 5e-4、動量 0.9）在單張 H800 上訓練，訓練批次大小只有 4。

The CLAP branch starts from LAION's music CLAP checkpoint and is fine-tuned. The coarse TA estimate is simply the cosine similarity between the text and music embeddings, with no learnable parameters; the coarse OMI estimate passes the music embedding through a three-layer MLP. The MERT branch freezes MERT-v1-95M, takes the last-layer hidden states, and pools them into one vector with multi-head attention against a learnable query; OMI is predicted from this vector by an MLP, while for TA it is concatenated with the CLAP text embedding, layer-normalised and fed to an MLP. The authors deliberately avoid a cosine between MERT and CLAP text, since the two do not share a space.

The fusion layer stacks the two branches' OMI (or TA) scalars into a two-dimensional vector and applies one linear layer to get the final score. The loss is β·L_con + γ·L_trun: L_con penalises, for every pair in the batch, the part of the gap between true and predicted score differences that exceeds a margin α = 0.2; L_trun is an L1 loss that ignores errors below τ = 0.3. With β = 0.5 and γ = 1.0, training uses SGD (learning rate 5e-4, momentum 0.9) on a single H800, with a training batch size of only 4.

限制Limitations

主結果把三個改動綁在一起：微調過的音樂 CLAP、新增的 MERT 分支、換掉的損失。基線是作者自己重新實作的，論文沒交代重實作的基線是否也從同一個音樂 CLAP 檢查點微調，所以 0.637→0.811 裡有多少來自 CLAP 本身無法分離。消融表也顯示，換回 L1 的 FUSEMOS-L1 在 OMI U-SRCC 已達 0.797、TA 0.576，損失的貢獻分別只有 0.014 與 0.068，而且全部是單次訓練、沒有變異數。

成對排序損失在批次大小 4 時，每批只有 12 個有序配對可比，排序訊號很稀薄。只用排序損失時 MSE 飆到 7.887（OMI）與 12.50（TA），這是因為該損失對整體平移不變，本來就可預期，論文卻把它當成發現來寫。系統層級的 S-SRCC 0.977 是在至多 31 個系統上算的，幾個系統排名互換就會大幅變動。最後，結論的「U-MSE 降五成以上、S-MSE 降八成以上」只對 OMI 成立，TA 是 31% 與 72%。

The main result bundles three changes: a fine-tuned music CLAP, the added MERT branch, and the new loss. The baseline was re-implemented by the authors, and the paper does not say whether that baseline was fine-tuned from the same music CLAP checkpoint, so how much of 0.637→0.811 comes from CLAP itself cannot be separated. The ablation table also shows that FUSEMOS-L1, trained with plain L1, already reaches 0.797 OMI U-SRCC and 0.576 for TA, so the loss contributes only 0.014 and 0.068 respectively; every number is a single training run with no variance.

With a batch size of 4, the pairwise ranking loss has only 12 ordered pairs per batch to compare, a thin ranking signal. Using the ranking loss alone sends MSE to 7.887 (OMI) and 12.50 (TA); that is predictable because the loss is invariant to a global offset, yet the paper writes it up as a finding. The system-level S-SRCC of 0.977 is computed over at most 31 systems, where swapping a few ranks moves it a lot. Finally, the conclusion's over 50% lower U-MSE and over 80% lower S-MSE hold only for OMI; for TA they are 31% and 72%.

討論Discussion

消融表有一個讀法上的陷阱。論文寫「所有消融都用 L1 訓練，以便和 FUSEMOS-L1 公平比較」，但正文把每個變體都拿去和用複合損失的完整版（OMI U-SRCC 0.811、TA 0.644）比。如果照字面以 FUSEMOS-L1（0.797、0.576）為基準，晚期融合對特徵串接（FUSEMOS-concat：0.785、0.605）的優勢在 OMI 只剩 0.012，TA 反而是串接高 0.029；把餘弦換成 MLP 的 w/o-cos（0.786、0.583）在 TA 也比 FUSEMOS-L1 高。只有注意力融合（FUSEMOS-atten：0.726、0.551）不論怎麼比都明顯較差。

所以「晚期融合優於早期融合」與「餘弦優於學出來的映射」這兩個結論，取決於那句話涵蓋哪些變體，而論文沒講清楚。比較站得住的是：MERT 單獨（OMI 0.669、TA 0.522）只比 CLAP 基線（0.637、0.495）高一點，增益主要來自兩者合併。對想拿它當評估工具的人，TA 句子層級 SRCC 0.644 代表逐段判斷文字對齊仍不可靠，系統層級（0.940）才比較能用。要說服人，下一步應該是放到 AudioMOS Challenge 2025 的同一測試集上與參賽系統比較。

The ablation table has a reading trap. The paper says all ablations are trained with L1 for a fair comparison with FUSEMOS-L1, yet the text compares every variant with the full model trained with the composite loss (0.811 OMI U-SRCC, 0.644 TA). Taking FUSEMOS-L1 (0.797, 0.576) as the reference instead, late fusion's lead over feature concatenation (FUSEMOS-concat: 0.785, 0.605) shrinks to 0.012 on OMI, and on TA concatenation is actually 0.029 higher; w/o-cos, which replaces the cosine with an MLP (0.786, 0.583), also beats FUSEMOS-L1 on TA. Only attention fusion (FUSEMOS-atten: 0.726, 0.551) is clearly worse under either reading.

So the conclusions that late fusion beats early fusion and that the cosine beats a learned mapping depend on which variants that sentence covers, which the paper does not make clear. What does hold is that MERT alone (0.669 OMI, 0.522 TA) is only a little above the CLAP baseline (0.637, 0.495), and the gain comes mainly from combining the two. For anyone planning to use it as an evaluation tool, a clip-level TA SRCC of 0.644 means per-clip judgments of text alignment are still unreliable, and only the system-level ranking (0.940) is more usable. To be convincing, the next step would be to run it on the AudioMOS Challenge 2025 test set and compare with the entries.

FFMT: Frequency-Factorized Multi-Rate Tokenization for Music-to-Dance Generation

FFMT：用固定高斯低通把舞蹈動作拆成低頻骨架與高頻殘差，分別以低速率小碼本與高速率大碼本量化，再先生成低頻、後以低頻為條件生成高頻的音樂驅動舞蹈生成

Qirong Guo, Ruijie Li, Zeyan Li, Sicheng Shu, Guanzhi Ding, Weihang Ding
arXiv:2611.07384 · cs.SD; cs.CV · 投稿submitted 2026-11-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Bailando、TokenDance 這類「VQ-VAE 加自迴歸」的音樂生成舞蹈系統，所有動作 token 都用同一個時間速率和碼本大小；FFMT 先用固定高斯濾波把相對根節點的關節軌跡拆成低頻與殘差兩股，低頻每 0.2 秒一個 token、碼本 128，高頻每 0.1 秒一個、碼本 512，生成時先出低頻再出高頻。選它是因為「哪一股訊號該分到多少 token 與碼本容量」這個問題在音樂 codec 上也一樣存在，而這篇的消融把它拆得很乾淨。AIST++ 上 FID_k 是 9.91，作者重跑的 Bailando++ 是 21.29；遮掉高頻對低頻的注意力，FID_k 劣化到 55.27，是所有消融裡最大的。不過 tokenizer 的高頻重建誤差 E_H 仍有 0.55，也就是誤差能量仍是參考高頻能量的 55%。Why this one: Music-to-dance systems built as a VQ-VAE plus an autoregressive model, such as Bailando and TokenDance, give every motion token the same temporal rate and codebook size; FFMT first uses a fixed Gaussian filter to split root-relative joint trajectories into a low band and a residual, with one low-band token per 0.2 s from a 128-entry codebook and one high-band token per 0.1 s from a 512-entry codebook, and generates the low band before the high band. I picked it because the question of how many tokens and how much codebook capacity each stream should get exists just the same for music codecs, and this paper's ablations take it apart cleanly. On AIST++ its FID_k is 9.91 against 21.29 for the authors' rerun of Bailando++; masking high-to-low attention degrades FID_k to 55.27, the largest of all ablations. Still, the tokenizer's high-band reconstruction error E_H is 0.55, meaning the error energy is still 55% of the reference high-band energy.

動機Motivation

舞蹈同時有兩種時間尺度：身體姿態與走位緩慢變化，手腕、腳步的快速動作則疊加在上面。以離散 token 為基礎的音樂生成舞蹈系統，tokenizer 決定了生成器學習音樂與動作關係的單位；token 速率太低會漏掉快速變化，太高則會重複編碼緩慢的部分，碼本太小會把不同的動作合併，太大則浪費容量。

作者把這個問題稱為「時間容量分配」：該怎麼在不同頻率成分之間分配時間解析度與碼本容量，同時讓快速動作的時間點與幅度和緩慢的身體配置保持協調。這和既有的「粗細兩層 token」不同，因為光有不同的時間解析度，並沒有規定每一層要裝哪些頻率內容。

Dance has two time scales at once: body configuration and position change slowly, while fast movements of wrists and feet sit on top. In music-to-dance systems built on discrete tokens, the tokenizer defines the units over which the generator learns music-motion relationships; too low a token rate misses fast changes, too high a rate re-encodes slow motion redundantly, too small a codebook merges distinct movements, and too large a codebook wastes capacity.

The authors call this a temporal capacity allocation problem: how to allot temporal resolution and codebook capacity across frequency components while keeping the timing and amplitude of fast movements coordinated with the slowly evolving body configuration. This differs from existing coarse-and-fine token hierarchies, since different temporal resolutions alone do not specify which frequency content each level should hold.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接的前作是 Bailando（Li 等，2022）與 Bailando++：用 VQ-VAE 把上半身與下半身各自量化成「編舞記憶」碼本，再用 GPT 生成；FFMT 沿用了上下半身分開碼本，以及旋轉／根速度解碼器的做法。TokenDance 是表中最強的 token 式基線，EDGE（Tseng 等，2023）與 Lodge（Li 等，2024）則是擴散式。DanceMeld 與 DuetGen 已經在學不同時間解析度的粗細兩層碼，DuetGen 也讓細層以粗層為條件；ExpressiveBailando 用傅立葉域損失監督解碼，InfiniteDance 對參考動作分頻後當條件。FFMT 和它們的差別在於量化的目標本身就是明確的頻帶：低頻是高斯平滑後的軌跡，高頻是精確互補的殘差。

論文沒有點出的是，「低速率粗 token 先生成、高速率細 token 以粗 token 為條件」這個階層，在音訊生成裡早有前例：Jukebox（Dhariwal 等，2020）用三個不同步長的 VQ-VAE 層級，上層先生成、下層用條件上取樣器補細節；VQ-VAE-2（Razavi 等，2019）在影像上也是同一結構。FFMT 真正多出來的是兩點：每一層的目標由固定低通濾波明確界定，而不是讓網路自己決定什麼放上層；以及不對稱地給高頻更多 token 與更大碼本。拆解方式本身則接近只有一層的拉普拉斯金字塔。

The direct predecessors are Bailando (Li et al., 2022) and Bailando++: a VQ-VAE quantises the upper and lower body into separate choreographic memory codebooks, and a GPT generates over them; FFMT keeps the separate upper/lower-body codebooks and the rotation/root-velocity decoder. TokenDance is the strongest token-based baseline in the table, while EDGE (Tseng et al., 2023) and Lodge (Li et al., 2024) are diffusion-based. DanceMeld and DuetGen already learn coarse and fine codes at different temporal resolutions, with DuetGen conditioning the fine level on the coarse one; ExpressiveBailando supervises decoding with a Fourier-domain loss, and InfiniteDance band-splits reference motions for conditioning. FFMT's difference is that the quantisation targets are explicit frequency bands: the low band is the Gaussian-smoothed trajectory, the high band its exactly complementary residual.

What the paper does not point out is that the hierarchy of low-rate coarse tokens generated first, with high-rate fine tokens conditioned on them, has clear precedent in audio generation: Jukebox (Dhariwal et al., 2020) uses three VQ-VAE levels with different strides, generating the top level first and filling in detail with conditional upsamplers; VQ-VAE-2 (Razavi et al., 2019) has the same structure for images. What FFMT really adds is two things: each level's target is pinned down by a fixed low-pass filter rather than left to the network, and the high band gets asymmetrically more tokens and a larger codebook. The decomposition itself is close to a Laplacian pyramid with a single level.

方法Method

分頻與量化。 先去掉全域根平移，得到相對根節點的關節位置，再沿時間做固定的高斯低通（核長 31 幀、σ=6 幀、邊界複製）：低頻 P^L 是平滑結果，高頻 P^H 是原訊號減 P^L，所以兩者相加精確等於原訊號。每個頻帶再按關節分成上半身與下半身，共四個 VQ-VAE 分支，各有自己的編碼器、碼本與位置解碼器。兩個資料集都用 4 秒視窗，低頻每 0.2 秒、高頻每 0.1 秒一個 token（AIST++ 60 fps 時步長 12/6，FineDance 30 fps 時 6/3），所以每視窗每個身體部位是 20/40 個 token；碼本大小 K_L=128、K_H=512，嵌入 512 維，低／高分支寬 512/1024、深 3/6。

訓練。 每個分支用位置、速度、加速度三階差分的 L1 重建損失，加上 commitment 與潛在正則；另外再對低高頻重建相加後的完整動作加一組同樣的差分損失，讓四個分支透過重組結果聯合最佳化。位置 VQ 訓練完後凍結編碼器與碼本，另訓練一個解碼器：每個身體部位把低、高頻碼解成逐幀特徵、串接後經時間融合網路，預測關節旋轉，下半身另外預測根速度並積分出全域平移。

先規劃、後細化的生成。 低頻與高頻各用一個自迴歸 Transformer（AIST++ 上 12 與 14 個區塊，512 維、8 頭），每一步預測上下半身一對 token。音樂先經局部上下文的音樂 Transformer，降採樣到兩個 token 速率。高頻第 t 步透過 π(t) 對到低頻索引（T_H=2T_L 時每兩個高頻步共用一個低頻步），取周圍 7 個低頻 token 當條件；動作注意力是因果的，音樂注意力可以看有限的未來。推論時再疊一個不需訓練的解碼先驗：候選集是 logit 前 64 名加上訓練資料中出現過的所有後繼對，對訓練中出現過的轉移加一個固定的有號偏移 α，並依該步是否落在拍點加上 log p(c|beat) − log p(c) 的修正，乘以權重 β。

Band splitting and quantisation. Global root translation is removed to get root-relative joint positions, which are then low-pass filtered along time with a fixed Gaussian (kernel 31 frames, σ = 6 frames, replicated boundaries): the low band P^L is the smoothed result and the high band P^H is the original minus P^L, so the two sum exactly to the original. Each band is further split by joints into upper and lower body, giving four VQ-VAE branches, each with its own encoder, codebook and position decoder. Both datasets use 4-second windows with one low-band token per 0.2 s and one high-band token per 0.1 s (strides 12/6 for AIST++ at 60 fps, 6/3 for FineDance at 30 fps), i.e. 20/40 tokens per body part per window; codebooks are K_L = 128 and K_H = 512 with 512-D embeddings, and the low/high branches are 512/1024 wide and 3/6 deep.

Training. Each branch uses L1 reconstruction losses on position, velocity and acceleration (three orders of differencing) plus commitment and latent regularisation; a second set of the same difference losses is applied to the full motion obtained by summing the low- and high-band reconstructions, so the four branches are optimised jointly through the recombined result. After position-space VQ training, the encoders and codebooks are frozen and a separate decoder is trained: for each body part, the low- and high-band codes are decoded into frame-level features, concatenated and passed through a temporal fusion network to predict joint rotations, with the lower body also predicting root velocity, integrated into global translation.

Plan-then-articulate generation. The low and high bands each get an autoregressive Transformer (12 and 14 blocks on AIST++, 512-D, 8 heads), predicting an upper/lower-body token pair per step. Music first goes through a local-context music Transformer and is downsampled to the two token rates. High-band step t maps to a low-band index through π(t) (with T_H = 2T_L, every two high-band steps share one low-band step) and takes the 7 surrounding low-band tokens as conditioning; motion attention is causal, while music attention may look a bounded distance ahead. At inference a training-free decoding prior is added: the candidate set is the top 64 pairs by logit plus every successor pair seen in training, observed transitions get a fixed signed offset α, and a correction of log p(c|beat) − log p(c), weighted by β, depends on whether the step falls on a beat.

限制Limitations

高頻其實還原得不好。 Tokenizer 重建表裡，完整模型在 AIST++ 的 E_H 是 0.5514、FineDance 是 0.3190，也就是高頻誤差能量仍是參考高頻能量的 55% 與 32%。和去掉分頻的版本（0.7338、0.4280）比確實降了約 25%，但 MPJPE 在 AIST++ 上只從 96.6 降到 94.5 mm，整體姿態誤差將近 10 公分。更要注意的是，E_H 用的高通 H=I−G 正是完整模型訓練時用的同一個高斯核，評估指標和完整模型的分解方式是對齊的，對其他變體天生不利。

基線與指標的選擇。 EDGE、Lodge、TokenDance 的數字直接抄自 TokenDance 論文，只有 AIST++ 上的 Bailando++ 是作者自己重跑的。論文把多樣性和 BAS 都定義成「越接近真實資料越好」，而不少前作把 BAS 當成越高越好；照後者的讀法，AIST++ 上 BAS 最高的是 Lodge（0.2423）而不是 FFMT（0.2374）。全篇沒有任何觀眾主觀評測，音樂特徵用的是什麼也沒寫。

解碼先驗的調參。 先驗權重 (α_L, α_H, β_L, β_H) 在 AIST++ 是 (0.25, −0.30, 0.12, 0)，在 FineDance 是 (0, 0.05, 0.06, 0)，連高頻轉移偏移的正負號都不同，論文只說推論時固定不變，沒說是在哪個切分上選的。若是在測試集上調，AIST++ 上 FID_k 從 13.14 降到 9.91 的那 3.23 就不能全算進方法。所有結果也都是單次執行。

The high band is still poorly recovered. In the tokenizer reconstruction table, the full model's E_H is 0.5514 on AIST++ and 0.3190 on FineDance, meaning the high-band error energy is still 55% and 32% of the reference high-band energy. That is indeed about 25% lower than the variant without band splitting (0.7338, 0.4280), but MPJPE on AIST++ only falls from 96.6 to 94.5 mm, so overall pose error is close to 10 cm. More importantly, E_H uses the high-pass H = I − G built from the very Gaussian kernel the full model was trained with, so the metric is aligned with the full model's decomposition and inherently disadvantages the other variants.

Choice of baselines and metrics. The EDGE, Lodge and TokenDance numbers are copied from the TokenDance paper; only Bailando++ on AIST++ was rerun by the authors. The paper defines both diversity and BAS as better when closer to the ground truth, whereas many prior papers treat BAS as higher-is-better; under that reading the best AIST++ BAS belongs to Lodge (0.2423), not FFMT (0.2374). There is no subjective evaluation with viewers at all, and the music features used are not described.

Tuning of the decoding prior. The prior weights (α_L, α_H, β_L, β_H) are (0.25, −0.30, 0.12, 0) on AIST++ and (0, 0.05, 0.06, 0) on FineDance, with even the sign of the high-band transition offset differing; the paper only says they stay fixed during inference, not which split they were chosen on. If they were tuned on the test set, the 3.23 drop in AIST++ FID_k from 13.14 to 9.91 cannot be credited entirely to the method. All results are also single runs.

討論Discussion

消融是這篇最有說服力的部分，而且每次只動一個元件。AIST++ 上完整模型 FID_k 9.91；拿掉分頻變 28.74，token 速率統一（每股 30 個 token、總數不變）變 18.92，碼本統一成 256 變 15.16，遮掉高頻對低頻的注意力變 55.27，拿掉解碼先驗變 13.14。FineDance 上的順序一樣：35.21 對 56.82、48.01、39.46、140.77、35.39。換句話說，最關鍵的是「高頻要看得到低頻」，其次是分頻本身，而先驗在 FineDance 上幾乎沒有作用（只差 0.18）。

「Same Rate」這組對照特別值得注意：總 token 數固定在每視窗 60 個，只改變它們在兩股之間的分配，FID_k 在 AIST++ 差了 9 點、在 FineDance 差了 12.8 點；tokenizer 端的 E_H 也從 0.7175 降到 0.5514。這是少數在固定預算下量出「容量該給誰」的證據，比單純加大模型的比較乾淨。

對 MIR 讀者來說，這個問題可以直接搬到音樂 token 上：MusicGen 用的 EnCodec RVQ 是按殘差層級分容量，每層同一速率、同一碼本大小，而不是按明確的頻帶或時間尺度；SubRoformer 一類的分頻前端則顯示固定濾波器組在音訊上也可行。FFMT 的結果暗示，用固定低通明確界定每一股的內容、再不對稱地分配速率，可能比讓網路自己學出層級更好訓練。但它的高頻誤差在 AIST++ 上仍有 55%，又缺主觀評測，現階段比較像一個乾淨的設計論證，而不是已經解決細節還原的系統。

The ablations are the most convincing part, and each changes one component at a time. On AIST++ the full model's FID_k is 9.91; removing band splitting gives 28.74, a uniform token rate (30 tokens per stream, same total) gives 18.92, a uniform codebook of 256 gives 15.16, masking high-to-low attention gives 55.27, and removing the decoding prior gives 13.14. FineDance follows the same order: 35.21 against 56.82, 48.01, 39.46, 140.77 and 35.39. In other words, what matters most is that the high band can see the low band, then the band split itself, while the prior does almost nothing on FineDance (a difference of only 0.18).

The Same Rate comparison deserves special attention: with the total fixed at 60 tokens per window and only their split between the two streams changed, FID_k differs by 9 points on AIST++ and 12.8 on FineDance, and tokenizer-side E_H falls from 0.7175 to 0.5514. This is rare evidence, under a fixed budget, of who should get the capacity, and cleaner than comparisons that simply enlarge the model.

For MIR readers the question transfers directly to music tokens: the EnCodec RVQ used by MusicGen allots capacity by residual level, with every level at the same rate and codebook size, not by explicit band or time scale, while band-split frontends such as SubRoformer show that fixed filter banks are workable on audio. FFMT's results hint that pinning down each stream's content with a fixed low-pass and then allotting rates asymmetrically may train more easily than letting the network learn the hierarchy. But with high-band error still at 55% on AIST++ and no subjective evaluation, it currently reads more as a clean design argument than as a system that has solved detail recovery.

Latent Terrain: Adapting Neural Audio Autoencoders as Design Materials in NIME

Latent Terrain：用加上傅立葉特徵的座標 MLP，把使用者畫的二維軌跡對應到神經音訊自編碼器的潛在軌跡，做成可在 Max/MSP 裡即時訓練與演奏的聲音地形

為什麼挑這篇：「只用解碼器」的潛在空間漫遊已是 NIME 裡用 RAVE 做樂器的常見手法，Vigliensoni 與 Fiebrink（2023）用 MLP 把控制平面上的點對應到潛在向量；這篇把對應對象從單點擴大成整段音訊的潛在軌跡，並在 MLP 前加傅立葉特徵來對付頻譜偏差，包成 Max/MSP 套件 nn.terrain~，再和四位藝術家合作做出四件作品。選它是因為它是少數同時交代技術量測與實際創作使用的工具論文。鼓聲資料上，傅立葉特徵把 Stable Audio Open 自編碼器潛在軌跡的 PSNR 從 21.7 提高到 32.1 dB，FAD 從 28.16 降到 2.39，幾乎追平直接編碼再解碼的 2.32。但測試座標只比訓練座標多了 N(0, 1e−6) 的偏移，這組數字量到的主要是擬合能力，不是在沒畫過的區域上的泛化。Why this one: Decoder-only latent space walking is already a common way to build RAVE-based instruments in NIME, and Vigliensoni and Fiebrink (2023) used an MLP to map points on a control plane to latent vectors; this paper widens the mapping target from single points to whole latent trajectories of audio, adds Fourier features before the MLP to counter spectral bias, packages it as the Max/MSP package nn.terrain~, and then makes four pieces with four artists. I picked it because it is one of the few tool papers that reports both technical measurements and actual creative use. On drum data, Fourier features raise the PSNR of Stable Audio Open autoencoder latent trajectories from 21.7 to 32.1 dB and cut FAD from 28.16 to 2.39, nearly matching the 2.32 of direct encode-decode. But the test coordinates differ from the training ones only by an offset of N(0, 1e−6), so these numbers mainly measure fitting ability, not generalisation to regions never drawn.

動機Motivation

神經音訊自編碼器（RAVE、Stable Audio Open 的 VAE 等）在 NIME（New Interfaces for Musical Expression）社群越來越常見，其中一個做法是只用解碼器：把使用者的連續輸入覆寫成潛在向量，讓解碼器當合成器用。問題是潛在空間不以容易理解的方式編碼音樂控制，維度又常超過一個樂器能用的控制數，演奏者往往只能「盲目」摸索。

作者另外指出三個阻礙藝術家親手使用這些模型的因素：技術研究多半位在音訊製作與創意程式環境之外、文件與支援不足、參數高維而不透明。論文的研究問題是：把神經音訊自編碼器當成設計材料，對 NIME 創作者有哪些挑戰與機會？技術上要解決的則是如何用低維控制空間存取高維潛在空間，同時不把聲音抹得太平。

Neural audio autoencoders (RAVE, the VAE in Stable Audio Open and others) are increasingly common in the NIME community, and one approach uses only the decoder: the user's continuous input overrides the latent vectors so the decoder acts as a synthesiser. The trouble is that latent spaces do not encode musical controls in easy-to-understand ways, and their dimensionality often exceeds the number of controls an instrument can use, so players are often left exploring blindly.

The authors also name three obstacles to artists working with these models hands-on: technical research mostly sits outside audio production and creative coding environments, documentation and support are thin, and the parameters are high-dimensional and opaque. The research question is what challenges and opportunities arise when neural audio autoencoders are used as design materials by NIME makers. Technically, the problem is how to reach a high-dimensional latent space from a low-dimensional control space without smoothing the sound flat.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的前作是 Vigliensoni 與 Fiebrink（2023）：使用者在潛在空間裡找到喜歡的點，再在控制平面上指定對應位置，用互動式機器學習（Wekinator 那一路）訓練 MLP 做座標到潛在向量的對應。Latent Terrain 的差別是訓練資料變成成對的軌跡：使用者在平面上畫的一條路徑，配上一段音訊編碼出來的整串潛在向量，因此一個地形可以容納較長的樂句。音樂互動那一側另有 CataRT（Schwarz）一類的語料庫式聲音空間，用描述子加降維把聲音片段排在平面上；這篇刻意不做聚類式的降維，而是讓地形保留潛在軌跡的快速變化。命名本身則借自 wave terrain synthesis。

技術核心最像的其實是電腦視覺的隱式神經表示：Tancik 等（2020） 的傅立葉特徵與 NeRF 的位置編碼，論文有清楚引用，並把這類模型稱為座標 MLP 或 CPPN。換句話說，這是把「用 MLP 擬合一張圖」換成「用 MLP 擬合一段潛在訊號」，傅立葉特徵的作用和在影像上相同。新意不在演算法，而在把它放進 Max/MSP、可以邊畫邊在一兩分鐘內重訓的工作流程。

The most direct predecessor is Vigliensoni and Fiebrink (2023): the user finds favourite points in the latent space, assigns them positions on a control plane, and trains an MLP from coordinates to latent vectors through interactive machine learning in the Wekinator tradition. Latent Terrain's difference is that its training data are paired trajectories: a path the user draws on the plane, matched with the whole sequence of latent vectors encoded from a piece of audio, so one terrain can hold longer phrases. On the musical-interaction side there are corpus-based sound spaces such as CataRT (Schwarz), which lay sound segments out on a plane using descriptors and dimensionality reduction; this paper deliberately avoids clustering-style reduction and lets the terrain keep the rapid changes of latent trajectories. The name itself is borrowed from wave terrain synthesis.

The technical core is closest to implicit neural representations from computer vision: the Fourier features of Tancik et al. (2020) and NeRF's positional encoding, which the paper cites clearly, calling such models coordinate MLPs or CPPNs. In other words, it swaps fitting an image with an MLP for fitting a latent signal with an MLP, and Fourier features do the same job they do for images. The novelty is not the algorithm but putting it into Max/MSP in a workflow that can retrain within a minute or two while drawing.

方法Method

設定是：一個 k 維潛在空間的自編碼器把音訊 x 編成潛在序列 Z={z_1,…,z_t}，使用者在控制平面上畫出等長的座標序列 V={v_1,…,v_t}，要訓練 f_θ: R²→R^k。座標先經傅立葉特徵映射 γ(v)=[sin(2πBv), cos(2πBv)]，B 從 N(0,σ²) 抽樣，再送進 MLP。σ 是關鍵：太小則地形過平（欠擬合），太大則雜訊多、在沒有訓練資料的區域泛化差，作者經驗上建議 σ 在 0.1 到 1 之間，並把它開放給使用者調。

量測用了四個自編碼器：RAVE（三個預訓練模型，潛在 4 或 16 維）、Music2Latent（64 維、壓縮比 4096）、Stable Audio Open 1.0 的 VAE（64 維、2048）、FlowDec（NDAC-25，128 維離散碼、1764）。實驗其實是一維時間軸：每段 5 秒音訊各訓練一個 MLP，資料是 MUSDB18 鼓軌、AAM 的弦樂與吉他、VCTK 語音三組，每組各做超參數搜尋。指標是潛在軌跡的 PSNR，以及用 VGGish 嵌入算的 FAD。

效率方面，在 M4 Max 的 CPU 上訓練 1000 步在所有設定下都不到 2 秒；10 秒音訊編成 215 個潛在碼，批次 16 時一個 epoch 是 14 步，500 個 epoch 約 1.2 分鐘。加上 MLP 後解碼器的即時率只略為上升。套件 nn.terrain~ 拆成編碼、訓練與 GUI 等數個 Max 物件，即時解碼則交給 ACIDS-IRCAM 既有的 nn~。

The setup: an autoencoder with a k-dimensional latent space encodes audio x into a latent sequence Z = {z_1, …, z_t}, the user draws a coordinate sequence V = {v_1, …, v_t} of the same length on a control plane, and f_θ: R² → R^k is trained. Coordinates first pass through the Fourier feature mapping γ(v) = [sin(2πBv), cos(2πBv)], with B sampled from N(0, σ²), and then into the MLP. σ is the key setting: too small and the terrain is too flat (underfitting), too large and it gets noisy and generalises poorly where there is no training data; empirically the authors recommend σ between 0.1 and 1 and expose it to users.

Four autoencoders are measured: RAVE (three pretrained models, 4 or 16 latent dimensions), Music2Latent (64 dimensions, compression ratio 4096), the Stable Audio Open 1.0 VAE (64, 2048) and FlowDec (NDAC-25, 128-dimensional discrete codes, 1764). The experiment is in fact a one-dimensional timeline: one MLP is trained per 5-second segment, on three collections (MUSDB18 drum stems, AAM bowed and guitar tracks, VCTK speech), with a hyperparameter search per collection. Metrics are latent-trajectory PSNR and FAD computed on VGGish embeddings.

For efficiency, 1,000 training steps take under 2 seconds in every setting on an M4 Max CPU; 10 seconds of audio encodes to 215 latent codes, an epoch at batch size 16 is 14 steps, and 500 epochs take about 1.2 minutes. Adding the MLP raises the decoder's real-time factor only slightly. The package nn.terrain~ is split into several Max objects for encoding, training and the GUI, with real-time decoding handed to the existing nn~ from ACIDS-IRCAM.

限制Limitations

技術量測幾乎只證明了「傅立葉特徵讓 MLP 擬合得更好」。測試時間軸只是訓練時間軸加上 N(0, 1e−6) 的偏移，幾乎就是訓練點本身，而且每段音訊各有一個 MLP、每組資料各做超參數搜尋，所以 PSNR 與 FAD 量的是記憶能力。真正讓樂器有趣的是在沒畫過的區域會發生什麼，這部分只有圖 3 的定性視覺化與「σ 太大會過擬合」的描述，沒有數字。實驗也停在一維時間軸，二維平面沒有量化評估；其中一組資料還是 VCTK 語音。

FAD 也有不一致之處：在弦樂資料上，傅立葉特徵版的 RAVE（4.783）與 SAO（4.023）明顯比直接編碼再解碼（2.718、2.642）差，M2L 在 VCTK 上是 7.562 對 5.439，並不是正文說的「只略為劣化」，那句話只在鼓聲資料上大致成立。創作評估那一側，四位藝術家同時是共同作者，訪談對象就是作者群自己，倫理聲明也說沒有其他受試者參與；這是實踐研究的慣例，但「挑戰與機會」的結論因此缺少外部使用者的檢驗。

The technical measurements show little more than that Fourier features help the MLP fit better. The test timeline is just the training timeline plus an offset of N(0, 1e−6), essentially the training points themselves, and there is one MLP per segment and a hyperparameter search per collection, so PSNR and FAD measure memorisation. What makes an instrument interesting is what happens in regions never drawn, and for that there is only the qualitative visualisation in Figure 3 and the remark that too large a σ overfits, with no numbers. The experiments also stop at a one-dimensional timeline, with no quantitative evaluation of the two-dimensional plane; one of the collections is VCTK speech.

FAD is also inconsistent: on the string collection the Fourier-feature RAVE (4.783) and SAO (4.023) are clearly worse than direct encode-decode (2.718, 2.642), and M2L on VCTK gives 7.562 versus 5.439, which is not the minimal degradation the text describes; that statement roughly holds only on drums. On the creative side, the four artists are also co-authors, so the interviewees are the author group itself, and the ethics statement says no other participants were involved; that is customary in practice research, but the conclusions about challenges and opportunities thus lack testing by outside users.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值在工作流程而不在演算法，論文自己也這麼說：它不是要發明新的自編碼器，也不是要提出勝過 Vigliensoni 與 Fiebrink（2023）、Demerlé 等（2024）的操控方法，而是把自編碼器放進 NIME 的手作空間。三個介面的演進（2024 年 12 月到 2025 年 6 月）很具體：第一版要在 Python 裡訓練、匯出 TorchScript 再載入 Max，等待時間長，創作時間多半花在畫板上標記好聽的區域；第二版把訓練搬進 Max；第三版把輸入輸出都開放，做成「二維取樣器」，在潛在空間裡取樣時 MLP 可以內插填滿整個畫布，這是波形域取樣做不到的。

四件作品（EEG 驅動的冥想聲景、北京胡同錄音構成的虛擬藝廊、用 boids 演算法在 RAVE 地形上製造微變化的現場演出、把潛在取樣混入一般製作流程的聲景作品）顯示同一個地形工具可以落到很不一樣的實踐裡。訪談歸納出的三個主題——不確定性被當成創作來源、能動性分散在人與材料之間、自編碼器只是材料組合中的一員——比較像是對既有 NIME 論述的印證，而不是新發現。

對 MIR 研究者，最實際的啟示是：生成模型的潛在空間若要給人演奏，「平滑內插」與「保留快速變化」是需要明確取捨的旋鈕，σ 就是那顆旋鈕。下一步若能量化二維地形在未訓練區域的聲音品質與多樣性，這個工具的主張會紮實得多。

The paper's value is in the workflow rather than the algorithm, and it says so itself: the aim is not to invent new autoencoders or to propose steering methods that surpass Vigliensoni and Fiebrink (2023) or Demerlé et al. (2024), but to place autoencoders in a NIME crafting space. The evolution of the three interfaces (December 2024 to June 2025) is concrete: the first required training in Python, exporting to TorchScript and loading into Max, with long waits, so creative time went mostly into marking good-sounding zones on the drawing pad; the second moved training into Max; the third exposed inputs and outputs and became a two-dimensional sampler, where sampling in latent space lets the MLP interpolate across the whole canvas, something waveform-domain sampling cannot do.

The four pieces (an EEG-driven meditative soundscape, a virtual gallery built from Beijing hutong recordings, a live performance that uses a boids algorithm to create micro-variations over a RAVE terrain, and a soundscape that mixes latent sampling into an ordinary production workflow) show the same terrain tool landing in very different practices. The three interview themes—uncertainty as a creative source, agency distributed between people and materials, and the autoencoder as one member of a material assemblage—read more as confirmation of existing NIME discourse than as new findings.

For MIR researchers, the most practical lesson is that if a generative model's latent space is to be played, smooth interpolation and preserving rapid change are a trade-off that needs an explicit knob, and σ is that knob. If a next step quantified the sound quality and diversity of two-dimensional terrains in untrained regions, the tool's claims would be much firmer.
