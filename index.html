MeloCodec: Harnessing Melodic Priors for High-Fidelity Singing Voice Representation

MeloCodec:用旋律先驗打造高保真度歌聲表徵編解碼器

MeloCodec authors (accepted at IEEE ICME 2026)
arXiv:2608.03021 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-04
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：神經音訊編解碼器(如 X-Codec、SpeechTokenizer、BiCodec)大多把重心放在語義先驗上以保證可懂度,卻很少顯式處理旋律這種聲學先驗。這篇論文示範了「直接把旋律特徵跟聲學特徵融合」這個看似合理的做法其實會整個崩掉——消融實驗顯示直接融合(Direct Fusion)版本的 ViSQOL 從 3.38 掉到 2.34、F0-RMSE 從 1.64 惡化到 5.95,碼本使用率不到 5%,而作者提出的「先量化再融合」兩階段訓練法把這個問題解決掉,同時把音高偏移的音高相關係數從 0.40(±2 半音)拉到 0.72。Why this one: Neural audio codecs like X-Codec, SpeechTokenizer, and BiCodec lean heavily on semantic priors for intelligibility but rarely handle melody as an explicit acoustic prior. This paper shows that the intuitive fix -- fusing melodic and acoustic features directly -- collapses outright: the Direct Fusion ablation drops ViSQOL from 3.38 to 2.34 and worsens F0-RMSE from 1.64 to 5.95, with codebook utilization under 5%. The proposed tokenize-then-fuse two-stage training fixes this and lifts pitch-shift correlation from 0.40 to 0.72 at +/-2 semitones.

動機Motivation

神經音訊編解碼器是 LLM 式音訊生成的基礎分詞器,但現有系統普遍偏重語義先驗以保住語言可懂度,對旋律這類明確的聲學先驗著墨很少,導致在唱歌這種需要精準音高與可控音高操作的場景下,合成保真度不足。作者鎖定的問題很具體:如果把旋律特徵當成一般先驗,直接跟聲學特徵融合會發生什麼事?

Neural audio codecs are the foundational tokenizers for LLM-based audio generation, but existing systems lean on semantic priors to preserve intelligibility while leaving explicit acoustic priors like melody largely unexploited. That limits synthesis fidelity for singing voice, where precise and controllable pitch matters. The paper's target question is concrete: what actually happens when melodic features are fused directly with acoustic features?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

相關研究一節點名了 X-Codec、SpeechTokenizer、BiCodec 這幾個既有神經編解碼器,指出它們的共同特徵是重語義、輕聲學。基準對照組還包含 DAC 與 Encodec 這兩個通用音訊編解碼器。

這篇論文跟這些既有系統最大的差異,不是又提出一個新的量化器,而是先做了一個對照實驗去證明「把旋律特徵跟聲學特徵直接串接」這條最直覺的路會失敗:失敗有兩個具體機制——一是捷徑學習(shortcut learning),因為聲學分支資訊量是旋律分支的超集,解碼器學會直接忽略顯式旋律特徵;二是最佳化不穩定,因為旋律先驗是離散且確定的,跟聲學那邊連續且有彈性的神經潛在表示放在一起訓練時梯度會互相打架。MeloCodec 的解法是把旋律分支先獨立訓練成一個 RVQ 離散碼本(Stage 1),再把這組離散 token 投影進聲學空間去跟聲學流融合(Stage 2),藉由離散化這道資訊瓶頸擋掉捷徑學習。

The related-work discussion names X-Codec, SpeechTokenizer, and BiCodec as existing neural codecs that share the same bias toward semantic priors over acoustic ones, with DAC and Encodec used as general-purpose codec baselines.

MeloCodec's real contribution isn't another quantizer -- it's a controlled experiment showing that the obvious approach, concatenating melodic and acoustic features directly, fails for two identifiable reasons: shortcut learning, where the decoder ignores the explicit melody branch because the acoustic branch's information is a superset of it, and optimization instability, where the melodic prior is discrete and deterministic while the acoustic latent is continuous and flexible, so their gradients conflict during joint training. MeloCodec's fix is to pretrain the melody branch independently as a discrete RVQ codebook (Stage 1), then project those discrete tokens into acoustic space to fuse with the acoustic stream (Stage 2) -- the discretization itself is the information bottleneck that blocks the shortcut.

方法Method

系統分成兩條分支。Tokenize 分支先用 STFT 加音高類別投影把波形轉成 12 維色度圖(chromagram),再用旋律編碼器壓成連續特徵,最後用殘差向量量化(RVQ)離散化成旋律 token,這一段單獨用 VQ-VAE 目標函數訓練,建立一個穩健的旋律碼本。Fuse 分支則是聲學編碼器處理原始波形,把 Stage 1 訓練好的旋律 token 投影後跟聲學特徵串接,並加一個輔助投影頭從融合後的 token 反推旋律色度圖,用這個輔助損失強迫融合後的表示仍保留旋律資訊。訓練上採兩階段策略:Stage 1 只訓練旋律分支;Stage 2 凍結旋律編碼器與 RVQ、只微調旋律解碼器去適應分布偏移,聲學流則整段端對端訓練,損失是重建損失加對抗損失加旋律輔助損失的加權和。

The system has two branches. The Tokenize branch converts the waveform into a 12-dimensional chromagram via STFT plus pitch-class projection, compresses it with a melody encoder into continuous features, then discretizes it with residual vector quantization (RVQ) into melody tokens -- trained alone with a VQ-VAE objective to build a robust melody codebook first. The Fuse branch runs an acoustic encoder over the raw waveform, projects the Stage-1 melody tokens into acoustic space, concatenates them with acoustic features, and adds an auxiliary head that predicts the melody chromagram back from the fused tokens, forcing the fused representation to keep melodic information. Training is two-stage: Stage 1 trains only the melody branch; Stage 2 freezes the melody encoder and RVQ, fine-tunes only the melody decoder to absorb distribution shift, and trains the acoustic stream end-to-end on a weighted sum of reconstruction, adversarial, and melody auxiliary losses.

限制Limitations

評估在高位元率(約 6.0 kbps,8 層 RVQ)與低位元率(約 1.5 kbps,2 層 RVQ)兩種設定下進行。高位元率下 MeloCodec 的 ViSQOL 是 4.08、STOI 是 0.85、F0-RMSE 是 0.96,對照 DAC 的 ViSQOL 3.97、STOI 0.83、F0-RMSE 1.23,全面領先。低位元率的落差更明顯:MeloCodec 的 F0-RMSE 是 1.64,而 Encodec 的 F0-RMSE 是 4.87,幾乎是三倍差距;MeloCodec 的 ViSQOL 是 3.38,Encodec 只有 2.81。

消融實驗把落差拆解得很清楚:純聲學基準的 F0-RMSE 是 4.12,加上旋律輔助損失(單流)降到 2.10,但改成雙流直接融合,F0-RMSE 反而惡化到 5.95、ViSQOL 掉到 2.34、色度相似度只剩 0.81,碼本使用率不到 5%,且輔助驗證損失持續發散——這是捷徑學習的直接證據。完整的先量化再融合方法把 F0-RMSE 壓到 1.64、ViSQOL 拉到 3.38、色度相似度到 0.97,是四種設定中最好的。

論文沒有寫獨立的限制章節,但數字裡藏著兩個實際取捨。第一,音高偏移的可控性有上限:MeloCodec 在 ±2 半音下相關係數 0.72、F0-RMSE 3.85,但偏移到 ±4 半音時 F0-RMSE 升到 4.50,作者承認絕對音高精度仍受限於固定不動的聲學分支——音高輪廓能整體移動,但不是每個音準都精準跟上。第二,即使在最佳設定下,1.5 kbps 這種極低位元率的整體音質(ViSQOL 3.38)仍明顯低於高位元率的 4.08,壓縮率與保真度的取捨並沒有被解決,只是把旋律這一項的損耗壓到最小。

主觀 MUSHRA 測試找了 15 位專家聽 30 個樣本,高位元率下 MeloCodec 分數最接近參考音訊,低位元率下聽者回報 X-Codec 與 Encodec 出現明顯音高不穩定,MeloCodec 則保留清楚的旋律走向。

Evaluation covers a high-bitrate setting (~6.0 kbps, 8 RVQ levels) and a low-bitrate one (~1.5 kbps, 2 levels). At high bitrate MeloCodec scores ViSQOL 4.08, STOI 0.85, F0-RMSE 0.96, versus DAC's ViSQOL 3.97, STOI 0.83, F0-RMSE 1.23 -- a clean win across the board. The gap widens at low bitrate: MeloCodec's F0-RMSE is 1.64 versus Encodec's 4.87, nearly 3x, and MeloCodec's ViSQOL (3.38) beats Encodec's 2.81.

The ablation breaks this down precisely: an acoustic-only baseline scores F0-RMSE 4.12; adding a melody auxiliary loss in a single stream drops it to 2.10; but direct dual-stream fusion makes it worse, at F0-RMSE 5.95, ViSQOL 2.34, chroma similarity only 0.81, with codebook utilization under 5% and the auxiliary validation loss diverging -- direct evidence of shortcut learning. The full tokenize-then-fuse method reaches F0-RMSE 1.64, ViSQOL 3.38, chroma similarity 0.97, the best of the four configurations.

The paper has no standalone limitations section, but two real trade-offs show up in the numbers. First, pitch-shift controllability has a ceiling: correlation reaches 0.72 with F0-RMSE 3.85 at +/-2 semitones, but F0-RMSE rises to 4.50 at +/-4 semitones, and the authors note absolute pitch precision stays partly constrained by the frozen acoustic branch -- the contour moves, but not every pitch tracks it exactly. Second, even under the best configuration, overall quality at the extreme 1.5 kbps bitrate (ViSQOL 3.38) still trails the high-bitrate setting (4.08) -- the compression-fidelity trade-off isn't solved, only the melodic component of that loss is minimized.

A MUSHRA test with 15 experts across 30 samples found MeloCodec closest to reference at high bitrate, while listeners reported audible pitch instability in X-Codec and Encodec at low bitrate, with MeloCodec retaining a clear melodic contour.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有意思的地方不是提出了一個新架構,而是把「這個看起來理所當然的做法為什麼會失敗」講清楚了:直接融合不是普通地變差,是災難性地變差(ViSQOL 掉超過 30%、碼本幾乎不被使用),而且失敗機制可以被具體診斷出來(碼本利用率、輔助驗證損失發散)。這給後面想把任何離散先驗(節奏型態、動態走向)塞進神經編解碼器的人一個明確的教訓:確定性的離散先驗不能跟連續神經潛在表示天真地拼在一起訓練,而應該先獨立量化、再作為條件接進去。作者也把這個方向寫進未來工作,打算把節奏與動態這些額外的顯式聲學先驗一併加進來。

The most interesting part of this paper isn't the architecture itself but the clear diagnosis of why the obvious approach fails: direct fusion doesn't degrade gracefully, it collapses (ViSQOL drops over 30%, the codebook goes nearly unused), and the failure mode is diagnosable in concrete terms (codebook utilization, diverging auxiliary validation loss). That's a transferable lesson for anyone trying to inject another discrete prior -- rhythmic patterns, dynamic contour -- into a neural codec: a deterministic discrete prior cannot be naively co-trained with a continuous neural latent, it needs to be quantized independently first and then supplied as a condition. The authors themselves flag this as future work, planning to add rhythm and dynamics as further explicit acoustic priors.

Separate-and-Detect: Unified Drum Transcription and Stem Generation via Latent Diffusion

Separate-and-Detect:用潛在擴散模型統一鼓組轉譜與音軌生成

Wei-Han Hsu, Chih-Cheng Chang, Bo-Yu Chen, Li Su, Yi-Hsuan Yang
arXiv:2608.01093 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：自動鼓組轉譜(ADT)一般走「端對端事件偵測」這條路,例如 ADTOF;這篇論文反過來,先用 HT-Demucs 抽出鼓組主導訊號,再用五音軌潛在擴散模型把它拆成大鼓、小鼓、落地鼓、腳踏鈸、鈸片五個音軌,最後才在每個音軌上做起始點偵測。統計檢定顯示這個做法在大鼓與小鼓上顯著贏過 LarsNet(Wilcoxon 符號檢定 p<0.01),整體 F1 從 LarsNet 的 0.613(MDB)/0.493(ENST)提升到 0.707/0.640,但代價是單樣本要跑 25 秒的擴散取樣,比端對端模型慢得多,而且鈸片與腳踏鈸這種寬頻、持續音的類別轉譜效果依然不好。Why this one: Automatic drum transcription (ADT) usually goes the end-to-end event-detection route, as in ADTOF; this paper inverts that: it first extracts a drum-dominant signal with HT-Demucs, decomposes it into five stems (kick, snare, toms, hi-hats, cymbals) via a five-stem latent diffusion model, and only then runs onset detection per stem. A Wilcoxon signed-rank test shows this significantly beats LarsNet on kick and snare (p<0.01), lifting overall F1 from LarsNet's 0.613 (MDB) / 0.493 (ENST) to 0.707 / 0.640 -- at the cost of about 25 seconds of diffusion sampling per 10-second clip, far slower than end-to-end models, and hi-hats/cymbals -- broadband, sustained classes -- remain hard to transcribe well.

動機Motivation

多數自動鼓組轉譜系統直接從混音訊號預測鼓組事件,輸出只有符號化的起始點時間戳,對音樂製作沒有太大幫助——製作人真正想要的往往是可以個別編輯的音軌(大鼓、小鼓分開),而不只是一串時間點。這篇論文想同時解決轉譜與可編輯音軌生成這兩件事,而且特別聚焦在大鼓與小鼓,因為這兩者是節奏律動(groove)的核心,對編曲與混音最有實用價值。

Most automatic drum transcription systems predict drum events directly from the mixture and output only symbolic onset timestamps, which isn't very useful for music production -- producers usually want individually editable stems (kick and snare separated out), not just a list of timestamps. This paper targets both transcription and editable stem generation at once, with particular focus on kick and snare since those form the core of the rhythmic groove and carry the most practical value for arrangement and mixing.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

相關研究點名了三條既有路線:鼓組音源分離方面有 HT-Demucs、Open-Unmix、LarsNet;轉譜方面 Riley 與 Dixon 的做法是把 ADTOF 加上鼓組音軌分離當作輔助步驟,Inverse Drum Machine 則是用分析合成(analysis-by-synthesis)框架處理;而端對端的代表則是 ADTOF 本身。這篇論文的差異在於分離不再只是輔助或後處理,而是被拉到推論的主要路徑上——先分離出五個音軌,再對每個音軌單獨偵測起始點,分離品質直接決定轉譜品質。骨幹是 MSG-LD 這個多軌潛在擴散分離框架,在其上加了兩個只在訓練時使用、推論時捨棄的輔助分支:一個起始點分支(OB)、一個音色分支(TB),分別預測粗粒度的音軌起始點圖與 DrumGAN 定義的 7 維音色描述子(明亮度、硬度、深度、粗糙度、隆隆感、溫暖度、銳利度)。

The related-work section names three existing lines: drum source separation systems HT-Demucs, Open-Unmix, and LarsNet; on the transcription side, Riley and Dixon augment ADTOF with drum stem separation as an auxiliary step, while the Inverse Drum Machine uses an analysis-by-synthesis framework; and ADTOF itself represents the end-to-end baseline. This paper's difference is that separation is no longer auxiliary or a post-processing step -- it becomes the primary inference path: five stems are separated first, and onset detection then runs per stem, so separation quality directly determines transcription quality. The backbone is MSG-LD, a multi-track latent diffusion separation framework, augmented with two training-only auxiliary branches discarded at inference: an onset branch (OB) predicting coarse per-stem onset maps, and a timbre branch (TB) predicting 7-dimensional DrumGAN descriptors (brightness, hardness, depth, roughness, boominess, warmth, sharpness).

方法Method

分離器建立在 MSG-LD 框架上,用一個 3.0517 億參數的 U-Net 去噪器搭配凍結的 VAE 骨幹(1.2812 億參數),在壓縮的 VAE 潛在空間裡做標準的 DDPM 去噪目標,並用五個可學習的音軌 token 讓模型分別合成五個鼓組音軌。起始點分支用 1×1×1 的三維卷積加輕量上採樣模組,把頻率維度壓掉、只留幀級別的起始點邏輯值,損失函數用 Focal BCE(α=0.25、γ=2.0);音色分支則用 L2 損失去逼近 DrumGAN 的 7 維描述子。

訓練資料混合了超過 1200 小時的合成資料集 StemGMD 與 2.1 小時的真實錄音 IDMT-SMT-Drums,評估則用 MDB Drums 與 ENST-Drums 兩個真實錄音資料集。前處理用 16 kHz 取樣率、64 維梅爾頻譜、FFT 大小 1024、跳躍步長 160 樣本(對應 10 毫秒幀跳),訓練用 AdamW、基礎學習率 3×10⁻⁵、批次大小 3,起始點分支的損失權重從 0 線性暖身到 0.05(前 2000 步),音色分支權重固定 0.5。最終轉譜階段套用 madmom 的 CNN 起始點偵測器,每個鼓組類別各自的觸發閾值介於 0.35 到 0.60、最小音符間隔介於 30 到 50 毫秒,這些數字是在保留的開發集上調出來的。

The separator builds on MSG-LD, using a 305.17M-parameter U-Net denoiser paired with a frozen 128.12M-parameter VAE backbone, running a standard DDPM denoising objective in the compressed VAE latent space, with five learnable track tokens letting the model synthesize each drum stem separately. The onset branch uses 1x1x1 3D convolutions plus lightweight upsampling to collapse the frequency dimension down to frame-level onset logits, trained with Focal BCE (alpha=0.25, gamma=2.0); the timbre branch uses L2 loss against 7-dimensional DrumGAN descriptors.

Training data mixes over 1,200 hours of synthesized StemGMD with 2.1 hours of real recordings from IDMT-SMT-Drums, evaluated on the real-recording sets MDB Drums and ENST-Drums. Preprocessing uses 16 kHz sampling, 64-bin mel spectrograms, FFT size 1024, hop size 160 samples (a 10 ms frame hop); training uses AdamW at base learning rate 3e-5, batch size 3, with the onset loss weight linearly warmed up to 0.05 over the first 2,000 steps and the timbre loss weight fixed at 0.5. The final transcription stage applies madmom's CNN onset processor, with per-class activation thresholds between 0.35 and 0.60 and minimum inter-onset intervals of 30-50 ms, tuned on held-out development data.

限制Limitations

分離品質(梅爾頻譜 MSE)上,MSG-LD 原始版整體是 1.88,遠優於 LarsNet 的 5.17;加上起始點分支後整體 MSE 略升到 2.12,但 Fréchet 音訊距離(FAD)反而從 0.41 降到 0.33,顯示加輔助分支雖然犧牲了逐點重建精度,分布層級的音質反而更好。

轉譜 F1 上,MDB Drums 上 ADTOF 整體是 0.795、LarsNet 是 0.613、本文 MSG-LD(+OB)是 0.707;ENST-Drums 上 ADTOF 是 0.709、LarsNet 0.493、MSG-LD(+OB)0.640。拆到類別看:大鼓上 MSG-LD(+OB)在 MDB 拿到 F1 0.931,超過 ADTOF(0.851)與 LarsNet(0.899);但腳踏鈸與鈸片這種寬頻類別,MSG-LD(+OB)雖遠贏 LarsNet(腳踏鈸 0.650 對 0.410、鈸片 0.663 對 0.217),卻仍落後端對端的 ADTOF(腳踏鈸 0.773、鈸片 0.849)。統計檢定確認 MSG-LD(+OB)在兩個資料集整體 F1 都顯著贏過 LarsNet(p<0.01)。

限制主要有三個。第一是取樣速度:擴散式生成需要迭代取樣,一段 10 秒音訊要花約 25 秒生成,比 ADTOF 這類單次前向模型慢得多,不適合即時應用。第二是取樣率上限:16 kHz 省算力但限制可還原的高頻內容,直接影響腳踏鈸與鈸片這類高頻能量集中的類別,前面轉譜 F1 落後端對端基準就是具體表現。第三是類別混淆:寬頻、持續音的鼓組成分(尤其鈸片類)彼此頻譜邊界模糊,論文承認這類事件「仍然難以區分」,分離出來的音軌會互相滲漏。

On separation quality (mel-spectrogram MSE), vanilla MSG-LD scores an overall 1.88, far better than LarsNet's 5.17; adding the onset branch nudges overall MSE up to 2.12, but Frechet Audio Distance (FAD) drops from 0.41 to 0.33 -- the auxiliary branch trades point-wise reconstruction accuracy for better distributional audio quality.

On transcription F1: MDB Drums has ADTOF at overall 0.795, LarsNet at 0.613, this paper's MSG-LD (+OB) at 0.707; ENST-Drums has ADTOF at 0.709, LarsNet 0.493, MSG-LD (+OB) 0.640. Per class: on kick, MSG-LD (+OB) hits F1 0.931 on MDB, beating ADTOF (0.851) and LarsNet (0.899); but on broadband classes like hi-hats and cymbals, MSG-LD (+OB) massively beats LarsNet (hi-hats 0.650 vs 0.410, cymbals 0.663 vs 0.217) yet still trails end-to-end ADTOF (hi-hats 0.773, cymbals 0.849). A Wilcoxon signed-rank test confirms MSG-LD (+OB) significantly beats LarsNet in overall F1 on both datasets (p<0.01).

There are three main limitations. First, sampling speed: diffusion generation requires iterative sampling, taking about 25 seconds to generate a 10-second clip, far slower than single-pass models like ADTOF, ruling out real-time use. Second, the sampling-rate ceiling: 16 kHz saves compute but limits recoverable high-frequency content, directly affecting hi-hats and cymbals -- exactly the classes where transcription F1 lagged the end-to-end baseline. Third, class confusion: broadband, sustained drum components (cymbals especially) have blurry spectral boundaries with each other, and the paper acknowledges these events "remain difficult to distinguish," with separated stems bleeding into one another.

討論Discussion

這篇論文的核心賣點不是刷新單一指標,而是證明「先生成可編輯音軌、再轉譜」這條路徑,在大鼓與小鼓這兩個對混音最重要的類別上確實能同時交出比端對端模型更好的音軌與有競爭力的轉譜結果,而且差異通過了統計檢定而不只是數字比較。但它同時老實地把代價攤開來看:換來的是遠高於即時需求的推論延遲,以及在寬頻類別上依然打不過端對端模型的轉譜表現。這說明「生成式分離當轉譜前端」目前比較適合對音軌可編輯性有強烈需求、能接受離線運算的音樂製作場景,而不是即時鼓組辨識或現場應用。未來工作明確指向更高解析度的潛在表示、其他音訊編解碼器與加速取樣技術,顯示作者自己也把取樣速度視為最需要解決的瓶頸。

The core selling point here isn't topping a single metric but showing that "generate editable stems first, then transcribe" can genuinely deliver both better stems and competitive transcription on the two classes that matter most for mixing -- kick and snare -- with the improvement backed by a statistical test rather than just a number comparison. But the paper is equally honest about the cost: inference latency far above real-time requirements, and transcription on broadband classes that still trails end-to-end models. That suggests generative separation as a transcription front end currently fits music-production workflows that value editable stems and can tolerate offline computation, not real-time drum recognition or live use. Future work explicitly points to higher-resolution latents, alternative audio codecs, and faster sampling, which is the authors' own signal that sampling speed is the bottleneck most in need of a fix.

為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文做了一個乾淨的控制變因實驗:固定同一個預訓練 Qwen3.5 骨幹(0.8B 到 27B)、固定資料與訓練預算,只換符號音樂的 token 化方式。結果 0.8B 模型用作者提出的 PMT(Performance-timed Music Tokens,10 毫秒精度)拿到 Fréchet Music Distance 159,而 27B 模型用節拍網格(beat grid)只拿到 272——骨幹放大 34 倍換來的進步,遠比不上換一種 token 化方式帶來的進步(FMD 直接砍半)。更犀利的是,作者對既有系統 MIDI-LLM 與 text2midi 做了診斷測試,發現它們對不同文字描述輸出的和弦時間分布幾乎不變(分別是 72% 與 66% 的和弦時間匹配率,近乎跟提示詞無關),暗示這兩個系統可能只是在複誦訓練分布,而不是真的跟著文字描述生成。Why this one: This paper runs a clean controlled experiment: fix the same pretrained Qwen3.5 backbone (0.8B to 27B), fix data and training budget, and only swap the symbolic-music tokenization scheme. An 0.8B model using the authors' PMT (Performance-Timed Music Tokens, 10 ms resolution) reaches Frechet Music Distance 159, while a 27B model using a beat grid only reaches 272 -- scaling the backbone 34x buys far less than switching representation, which halves FMD outright. More pointedly, the authors run a diagnostic on existing systems MIDI-LLM and text2midi and find their chord-time output distribution barely shifts across different text prompts (72% and 66% chord-time match respectively, nearly prompt-invariant), suggesting these systems may be reciting their training distribution rather than actually following the text description.

動機Motivation

文字轉符號音樂生成領域普遍假設「模型越大,生成品質越好」,但很少有人控制其他變因、單獨檢驗「符號音樂怎麼被序列化成 token」這件事本身的影響力。現有的節拍網格(beat grid)、REMI、ABC 記譜這幾種常見的 token 化方式,普遍只用小節或拍子當時間單位(相當於 63 到 125 毫秒的解析度),這對古典或爵士這種需要精細演奏時機(踏板延遲、搶拍、彈性速度)的音樂來說解析度太粗。這篇論文要回答的問題是:如果把時間解析度做到 10 毫秒、逐音符記錄力度與多音軌資訊,這樣的 token 化方式能不能在同樣的模型規模與訓練預算下,產出更貼近真實音樂分布的生成結果?

Text-to-symbolic-music generation generally assumes bigger models generate better music, but few studies hold everything else fixed to isolate the effect of how symbolic music is serialized into tokens in the first place. Common tokenization schemes -- beat grids, REMI, ABC notation -- typically quantize time to the bar or beat level (roughly 63-125 ms resolution), which is too coarse for genres like classical or jazz that depend on fine performance timing: rubato, pedal decay, notes pushed ahead of or behind the beat. This paper asks: if timing resolution is pushed to 10 ms with per-note velocity and multi-track information preserved, can that tokenization scheme produce generations closer to the real music distribution under the same model size and training budget?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

相關研究點名了兩個既有的文字轉符號音樂系統:MIDI-LLM 與 text2midi,以及 REMI、ABC 記譜這兩種代表性的 token 化基準。這篇論文跟它們最關鍵的差異,不是又做了一個新模型,而是先做了一個「想像的複製測試」——把同一批文字描述餵給 MIDI-LLM 與 text2midi,檢查輸出的和弦節奏分布會不會因為提示詞不同而改變。結果發現兩者的和弦時間匹配率分別固定在 72% 與 66% 附近、最大複音數固定在 17.3 附近,幾乎不隨提示詞變化,這是一種「印記診斷」(imprinting diagnostic):模型很可能只是在重現訓練資料的統計特徵,而不是真正回應文字描述。這個發現直接說明了作者為什麼要提出 PMT——如果連現有系統對文字的服從度都值得懷疑,那麼比較生成品質時,至少要先把 token 化方式這個變因徹底控制乾淨。

The related-work section names two existing text-to-symbolic-music systems, MIDI-LLM and text2midi, along with REMI and ABC notation as representative tokenization baselines. The key difference from this prior work isn't another model -- it's a diagnostic test run first: feeding the same batch of text captions to MIDI-LLM and text2midi and checking whether their output chord-rhythm distribution shifts with the prompt. It doesn't: chord-time match rates sit fixed around 72% and 66% respectively, and max polyphony sits fixed around 17.3, nearly invariant to the caption -- an "imprinting diagnostic" suggesting these models likely reproduce their training distribution's statistics rather than truly responding to the text. That finding is the direct motivation for PMT: if even existing systems' prompt adherence is questionable, then before comparing generation quality at all, the tokenization variable needs to be controlled cleanly.

方法Method

PMT(Performance-timed Music Tokens)把每個音符表示成一小串可解讀的參數 token:時間位移(量化到 10 毫秒、最多 99 格)、音軌與樂器編號、音高、音長(量化到 10 毫秒、限制在 1 到 200 格)、力度(量化成 32 級)。整個詞彙表由音高 128 類、音長 200 類、力度 32 類、時間位移 100 類、音軌 16 類、樂器編號 129 類,加上 4 個特殊符號組成,總共 609 個符號,平均每個音符只需要約 4 個 token。訓練上,PMT 的詞彙表以平均初始化的方式接到 Qwen3.5 的既有詞彙表後面,用「標題遮罩式」監督微調——損失只算在音樂 token 上、不算在文字標題上,解碼時把 logits 限制在音樂詞彙範圍內,訓練跑 1 萬步、有效批次大小 16、學習率 8×10⁻⁵、序列區塊長度 1024。

評估方法刻意設計成「天花板校正」:每種 token 化方式都先做一次「編碼再解碼回真實音樂」的迴圈,量出這種格式本身能保留多少表現力當作上限,再用這個上限去校正模型生成的分數,這樣才能把「格式本身的表達力」跟「模型有沒有學好」這兩件事分開來看。作者也釋出兩個資料集:一個是 8 萬 6598 首歌的對齊語料庫(同時有文字標題、MIDI、ABC 記譜與算圖音訊),取材自民謠、古典、Lakh 與爵士;另一個是 625 萬首歌的大規模標題語料庫,規模是既有 MidiCaps 資料集的 37 倍,是目前符號或音訊音樂裡最大的標題資料集。

PMT (Performance-Timed Music Tokens) represents each note as a short sequence of interpretable parameter tokens: a time-shift (quantized to 10 ms, capped at 99 steps), track and instrument program number, pitch, duration (quantized to 10 ms, clipped to 1-200 steps), and velocity (quantized to 32 levels). The vocabulary is built from 128 pitch classes, 200 duration bins, 32 velocity levels, 100 time-shift bins, 16 tracks, 129 instrument programs, plus 4 special tokens, for 609 symbols total, averaging about 4 tokens per note. For training, PMT's vocabulary is appended to Qwen3.5's existing vocabulary with mean-initialized embeddings, using caption-masked supervised fine-tuning -- loss is computed only on music tokens, not on the caption -- with decoding logits restricted to the music vocabulary; training runs 10k steps, effective batch size 16, learning rate 8e-5, 1024-token blocks.

The evaluation protocol is deliberately ceiling-anchored: each tokenization scheme is first round-tripped (encode then decode) on real music to measure how much expressiveness the format itself can preserve, and generation scores are normalized against that ceiling -- separating format expressiveness from whether the model actually learned to use it. The authors also release two datasets: an aligned 86,598-piece corpus (with caption, MIDI, ABC notation, and rendered audio together) drawn from folk, classical, Lakh, and jazz sources, and a 6.25-million-piece captioned corpus, 37x larger than the existing MidiCaps dataset and the largest captioned corpus for symbolic or audio music to date.

限制Limitations

在固定骨幹、資料與預算的對照實驗裡(表一),0.8B 模型用 PMT 拿到 Fréchet Music Distance(FMD)159±8,同樣 0.8B 但用節拍網格(Beat-TSD)是 286±1、REMI 是 285±1、ABC 記譜是 406,而 27B 模型用 PMT 是 156、用 REMI 是 272。把 FMD 對模型參數量取對數畫圖,每一條線的斜率都在雜訊範圍內、幾乎是平的,PMT 跟節拍網格之間穩定維持約 125 分的差距,不會隨著模型放大而縮小——也就是說骨幹放大 34 倍幾乎不影響 FMD,但換一種 token 化方式就能讓 FMD 砍半。和弦時間匹配率上,PMT 在 0.8B 是 36.2%、27B 是 35.8%,而節拍網格與 REMI 都停在 30% 出頭、ABC 更只有 16.2%。

遷移到既有的 MidiCaps 標準評測集上(表二),用 8.66 萬筆訓練資料訓出的 4B 版 PMT 拿到 FMD 196,對照 MIDI-LLM 的 314 與 text2midi 的 421,PMT 的分布保真度明顯最接近真實音樂,但在屬性服從度上並不領先:PMT 的正確調性比例只有 0.10,而 MIDI-LLM 是 0.32;PMT 的樂器 F1 是 0.46,MIDI-LLM 是 0.63。前面提到的「印記診斷」測試顯示 MIDI-LLM 與 text2midi 的和弦時間匹配率(72%、66%)與最大複音數(17.3)幾乎不隨提示詞改變,暗示這兩者的高屬性分數有一部分只是複誦訓練分布、而非真的服從文字指令。

消融實驗顯示,若移除力度資訊,從頭在 MAESTRO 上訓練的 FMD 會從 106.1 惡化到 697;分來源看,PMT 對民謠題材會過度和聲化(生成 25% 有和聲的段落,參考音樂只有 13%),對古典題材則填音不足(生成 50% 對參考音樂的 82%)。作者也承認,一個簡單的解碼期屬性限制就能把樂器 F1 從 0.46 拉到 0.60、正確調性從 0.10 拉到 0.35,顯示 PMT 目前的屬性服從度並非上限,只是還沒做這一步優化。

In the controlled experiment holding backbone, data, and budget fixed (Table 1), the 0.8B model with PMT reaches Frechet Music Distance (FMD) 159±8, versus 286±1 for the same 0.8B model on a beat grid (Beat-TSD), 285±1 on REMI, and 406 on ABC notation; the 27B model reaches 156 with PMT and 272 with REMI. Plotting FMD against log-parameters, every arm's slope is within noise of flat, leaving a near-constant ~125-point PMT-versus-beat-grid gap that scale does not erode -- scaling the backbone 34x barely moves FMD, while switching representation halves it. On chord-time match, PMT reaches 36.2% at 0.8B and 35.8% at 27B, while beat grid and REMI stay just above 30% and ABC manages only 16.2%.

Transferred to the existing MidiCaps benchmark (Table 2), a 4B-parameter PMT trained on the 86.6k-piece corpus reaches FMD 196, versus 314 for MIDI-LLM and 421 for text2midi -- PMT is clearly closest to the real music distribution, but it doesn't lead on attribute adherence: PMT's correct-key rate is only 0.10 versus MIDI-LLM's 0.32, and PMT's instrument-F1 is 0.46 versus MIDI-LLM's 0.63. The imprinting diagnostic mentioned above shows MIDI-LLM and text2midi's chord-time match (72%, 66%) and max polyphony (17.3) barely shift with the prompt, suggesting part of their attribute scores may come from reciting the training distribution rather than genuinely following text instructions.

An ablation shows that removing velocity information degrades FMD from 106.1 to 697 when training from scratch on MAESTRO; broken down by source, PMT over-harmonizes folk material (25% of generated passages carry harmony versus 13% in the reference) and under-fills classical material (50% versus 82% in the reference). The authors also note that a simple decode-time attribute constraint lifts instrument-F1 from 0.46 to 0.60 and correct-key from 0.10 to 0.35, showing PMT's current attribute adherence isn't a hard ceiling, just an optimization not yet applied.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最重要的貢獻不是 PMT 這個具體的 token 化方案,而是它證明了一個可以推廣到其他模態的原則:對於「要求生成模型輸出一個有結構的產物」這種任務,資料怎麼被序列化,可能比模型規模更決定生成品質的上限,而且這件事可以被一個乾淨的控制變因實驗量出來、而不是用主觀感受主張。

作者也老實揭露了 PMT 目前的弱點:屬性服從度(正確調性只有 0.10)反而不如既有系統,對民謠與古典這兩種風格有系統性的和聲填充偏差,聆聽測試也還在收集中、目前的自動化代理指標「尚無定論」,27B 這個規模只跑了兩個隨機種子、35B-A3B 更只有一個種子,統計把握度有限。這些限制加總起來,讓「PMT 全面優於既有系統」這個結論還不能下,作者自己給的定位也更保守——是提供一套可重複驗證的評測框架與資料集,讓「換一種 token 化表示能不能改善生成品質」這種宣稱,以後都可以被實際測量,而不是單憑生成範例的觀感去主張。

The most important contribution here isn't PMT itself but the transferable principle it demonstrates: for tasks that ask a generative model to output a structured artifact, how the data is serialized may bound generation quality more than model scale does -- and this can be measured with a clean controlled experiment rather than asserted from listening impressions.

The authors are also candid about PMT's current weaknesses: attribute adherence (correct-key rate of only 0.10) actually trails existing systems, there's a systematic harmonic-filling bias on folk and classical material, the listening study is still being collected with the automatic proxy metric currently "inconclusive," and statistical power is limited -- the 27B scale uses only two random seeds and 35B-A3B just one. Taken together, these limitations mean "PMT is strictly better than existing systems" isn't yet a conclusion the paper can support, and the authors position it more conservatively: as a reproducible evaluation harness and dataset release so that future claims about whether a new tokenization scheme improves generation quality can actually be measured, not just asserted from generated examples.
