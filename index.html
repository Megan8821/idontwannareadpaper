KRAISLER: A Multi-Track Dataset of Piano and Violin Duet Recordings for Music Information Retrieval Research

KRAISLER：面向音樂資訊檢索的鋼琴與小提琴二重奏多軌資料集

Hyemi Kim, Jiyun Park, Sein Lee, Taegyun Kwon, Sunjae Won, Juhan Nam
DOI:10.5334/tismir.338 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2025-09-01
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇的價值不在單一模型，而在一個把六項 MIR 任務串起來的乾淨對照組:同一批 Transkun 轉譜模型,在乾淨獨奏鋼琴上 note onset F1 有 98.5%,換到鋼琴小提琴二重奏混音,沒做資料增強前掉到 58.3%,加上音高與環境噪音增強後拉回 95.0%。這個近 37 個百分點的落差,加上小提琴轉譜在 KRAISLER 上 F1 僅 40.9%(遠低於 URMP 的 84.6% 與 Bach10 的 64.8%),把「模型在乾淨資料上表現好」跟「模型能處理真實二重奏」這兩件事的落差用數字釘死了,而不是空泛地說「更有挑戰性」。Why this one: The value here isn't a single model but a clean six-task benchmarking pass: the same Transkun transcriber gets 98.5% note-onset F1 on clean solo piano, drops to 58.3% on piano-violin duet mixtures without augmentation, and recovers to 95.0% with pitch/noise augmentation added. That near-37-point swing, plus violin transcription F1 of only 40.9% on KRAISLER versus 84.6% on URMP and 64.8% on Bach10, pins down the gap between clean-data performance and real-duet robustness with actual numbers instead of a vague claim of "more challenging."

動機Motivation

現有的獨奏鋼琴資料集(MAPS、SMD、MAESTRO)規模可觀,但都侷限在獨奏情境;既有的多樂器合奏資料集要嘛不含鋼琴,要嘛依賴粗略的樂譜對音訊對齊而非直接演出擷取,時間精度不足以支撐轉譜、對齊這類需要精細標注的任務。鋼琴與小提琴二重奏是室內樂裡最常見的編制之一,作者指出卻沒有一個資料集同時提供分離音軌、直接從 Disklavier 擷取的高精度鋼琴 MIDI、樂譜對齊,以及音符與節拍層級標注。KRAISLER 想補的是這個組合式的缺口:不是單純多錄一批二重奏音訊,而是把「乾淨分離的音軌」跟「真實演出時常見的彈性速度與微觀不同步」同時捕捉下來。

Existing solo-piano datasets (MAPS, SMD, MAESTRO) are substantial but confined to solo playing; existing multi-instrument ensemble datasets either exclude piano or rely on coarse score-to-audio alignment rather than direct performance capture, leaving timing precision too weak for tasks like transcription and alignment that need fine-grained labels. Piano-violin duet is one of the most common chamber configurations, yet the authors note no dataset combines separated audio stems, high-precision piano MIDI captured directly from a Disklavier, score alignment, and note/beat-level annotation all at once. KRAISLER targets exactly that combination gap: not just more duet audio, but clean separated stems captured alongside the expressive tempo rubato and micro-asynchrony typical of real duet performance.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景章節點名了三群既有資源。獨奏鋼琴這邊,MAPS 靠 MIDI 合成或 Disklavier 錄製、約 65 小時但已知有標注誤差;MAESTRO 約 200 小時、來自國際鋼琴電子大賽,MIDI 精度高但仍是獨奏;ASAP 在 MAESTRO 之上加了樂譜對齊。非鋼琴的多樂器資料集裡,URMP 提供多軌古典合奏加樂譜對齊與音高標注,Bach10 是四部巴哈聖詠的分軌錄音。含鋼琴的合奏資料集則有 MusicNet(11 種樂器、音訊只釋出混音單軌,對齊品質早期不佳,後來靠 MusicNetEM 修正)、TRIOS(五首三重奏)、以及最相近的 Piano Concerto Dataset(PCD,81 段選自 15 首鋼琴協奏曲、鋼琴與管弦樂分開錄製)。

The related-work section names three clusters of prior resources. On the solo-piano side: MAPS (~65 hours via MIDI synthesis or Disklavier, known to contain annotation errors), MAESTRO (~200 hours from the International Piano-e-Competition, high-precision MIDI but still solo), and ASAP (adds score pairing on top of MAESTRO). Among non-piano multi-instrument sets: URMP provides multitrack classical ensembles with score alignment and pitch annotation, and Bach10 offers four-part Bach chorale stems. Piano-inclusive ensemble datasets include MusicNet (11 instruments, audio released as a single mixed file, with alignment quality later fixed via MusicNetEM), TRIOS (five trio recordings), and the closest prior work, the Piano Concerto Dataset (PCD: 81 excerpts from 15 piano concertos, piano and orchestra recorded separately).

Against this backdrop, the authors' claimed novelty is specific: KRAISLER is positioned as the first public dataset combining separately recorded piano and violin stems, Disklavier-precision piano MIDI, score alignment, and both note- and beat-level annotation in a live duet setting — closest in spirit to PCD, but PCD pairs piano against a full pre-fixed-tempo orchestra, whereas KRAISLER's two-performer duet setting captures the flexible, mutually negotiated tempo coordination that larger fixed-tempo ensembles don't need to produce.

方法Method

錄音安排是兩間以隔音窗相連、聲學隔離的房間,小提琴用 Shure SM57,鋼琴用一對電容式麥克風立體收音,同時用 Yamaha Disklavier 直接擷取鋼琴的音符起訖、力度與踏板 MIDI 訊號。20 段選自 13 位作曲家(1775 年莫札特到 1940 年克萊斯勒)、涵蓋 Henle 難度 4 到 9 級的小提琴協奏曲與奏鳴曲樂段,每段 1 到 2 分鐘,總長約 25 分鐘。每段都提供乾聲、錄音室、音樂廳三種混音版本(用 Altiverb 8 的 Paramount Stage 與倫敦 Wigmore Hall 預設分別套用混響),鋼琴與小提琴以 0dB 訊噪比混合。

Recording used two acoustically isolated rooms connected by a soundproof window, with a Shure SM57 on violin and a stereo condenser pair on piano, while a Yamaha Disklavier directly captured piano note onset/offset, velocity, and pedal MIDI. The dataset covers 20 excerpts spanning 13 composers (Mozart in 1775 to Kreisler in 1940) at Henle difficulty levels 4-9, drawn from violin concerto and sonata movements, 1-2 minutes each, about 25 minutes total. Each excerpt ships in three mixed acoustic versions — dry, studio, and concert-hall reverb (Altiverb 8's Paramount Stage and London Wigmore Hall presets) — with piano and violin combined at 0 dB SNR.

Piano note alignment uses DualDTWNoteMatcher (a DTW-based matcher from the parangonar library) against the Disklavier MIDI, while violin note annotation uses the NoteEM expectation-maximization framework with DTW-guided score warping and pitch-shift augmentation, followed by manual correction of clear onset/offset errors by a trained annotator; violin velocity is fixed at 127 since offset estimates for a bowed instrument are inherently less reliable than pitch and onset. Beat annotation is semi-automated, extracted first from the piano MIDI and then manually corrected against explicit guidelines covering ensemble entrances, melody/accompaniment role changes, and grace notes, because tempo coefficient-of-variation across the 20 pieces ranges from 0.05 (steady Beethoven sonata movement) to 0.29 (rubato-heavy Kreisler Preghiera).

限制Limitations

作者自己列出的落差,在鋼琴獨奏轉譜上已經清楚可見:Transkun(有增強)在 MAESTRO 上 note onset F1 是 98.3%,在 KRAISLER 獨奏鋼琴上是 98.5%,數字相近,但把 offset 也算進去的 note F1 落差就明顯拉開,顯示 KRAISLER 作為跨資料集測試集確實抓得住 MAESTRO 訓練模型沒見過的變異。真正的弱點是小提琴標注:offset 靠自動對齊加人工校正而非直接擷取,力度全部固定填 127 而非真實演出動態,作者也坦言小提琴的表現式演奏(大量抖音、圓滑奏)讓 offset 判定天生不可靠。規模上,資料集僅 25 分鐘、20 段,只有一位小提琴家與一位鋼琴家,結果可能帶有特定演奏者的個人風格,無法推論到其他二重奏編制。

The gap the authors themselves surface is already visible in solo piano transcription: Transkun-with-augmentation gets 98.3% note-onset F1 on MAESTRO versus 98.5% on KRAISLER solo piano — close numbers — but once offsets are folded into the metric the gap widens noticeably, confirming KRAISLER captures cross-dataset variation MAESTRO-trained models haven't seen. The real weak point is violin annotation: offsets come from automatic alignment plus manual correction rather than direct capture, velocity is fixed at 127 rather than reflecting real dynamics, and the authors acknowledge that violin's expressive playing (heavy vibrato, legato bowing) makes offset determination inherently unreliable. In scale, the dataset is only 25 minutes across 20 excerpts with a single violinist and pianist, so results may carry performer-specific idiosyncrasies that don't generalize to other duet pairings.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有用的地方,是把「模型在乾淨獨奏資料上表現優異」跟「模型能不能處理真實二重奏」這兩件事的落差用具體數字釘住,而不是停留在「合奏比較難」的直覺陳述。以 Transkun 為例,note onset F1 從獨奏鋼琴的 98.5% 掉到未增強二重奏的 58.3%,是超過 40 個百分點的崩壞;而增強(音高偏移 ±20 音分加環境噪音)把它拉回 95.0%,證明問題主要出在訓練資料的分佈,不是模型容量不足。小提琴轉譜的 40.9% F1(對比 URMP 的 84.6%)進一步指出,即使是相對簡單的雙樂器混音,只要涉及高度表現式的演奏(而非 URMP、Bach10 那種節奏規整的旋律線),既有模型的落差仍然巨大。這對任何想把獨奏模型直接套用到室內樂情境的人,是一個具體、可量化的警訊,而不是模糊的提醒。

This paper's most useful contribution is pinning the gap between "performs well on clean solo data" and "can actually handle real duets" to concrete numbers, rather than resting on the intuition that ensembles are harder. Transkun's note-onset F1 falls from 98.5% on solo piano to 58.3% on unaugmented duet mixtures — a collapse of over 40 points — and augmentation (±20-cent pitch shift plus environmental noise) recovers it to 95.0%, indicating the problem is mainly training-distribution mismatch rather than insufficient model capacity. Violin transcription's 40.9% F1 (versus 84.6% on URMP) further shows that even a relatively simple two-instrument mixture produces a large gap once genuinely expressive playing (rather than URMP/Bach10's rhythmically regular lines) is involved. That's a concrete, quantified warning for anyone assuming solo-trained transcription models transfer to chamber-music settings.

Attention-Enhanced Convolutional Neural Network for Music Genre Classification

注意力強化卷積神經網路於音樂曲風分類

M. Pushparajan, K. T. Sreekumar, K. I. Ramachandran, C. Santhosh Kumar
DOI:10.5334/tismir.268 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2025-04-10
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇的對照實驗做得算乾淨,拆解掉「加了注意力就變好」這種模糊說法:在 GTZAN 3 秒切片上,拿掉整個注意力模組準確率從 96.02% 掉到 89.11%(-6.91 個百分點),是消融實驗裡掉最多的單一因素,比拿掉批次正規化(-2.49)或砍掉一層卷積(-3.23)都明顯。作者也另外貢獻了一個 12 類印度音樂曲風的新資料集 IMG-Amrita(1,800 首),同一套架構在上面達到 98.94% 準確率,比對照的 CNN(95.55%)與 CNN-LSTM(96.67%)都高,但增幅只有 3.39 個百分點,遠小於在 GTZAN 上的 8.97 個百分點,這個資料集間差異本身就是一個值得注意的觀察,論文卻沒有特別討論。Why this one: The ablations here are reasonably clean and pin down what a vague "adding attention helps" claim actually means: on GTZAN 3-second clips, removing the entire attention module drops accuracy from 96.02% to 89.11% (-6.91 points), the single largest drop in the ablation table, bigger than removing batch normalization (-2.49) or cutting a convolutional layer (-3.23). The paper also contributes a new 12-genre Indian-music dataset, IMG-Amrita (1,800 tracks), where the same architecture reaches 98.94% accuracy versus 95.55% (CNN) and 96.67% (CNN-LSTM) — but the gain there is only 3.39 points, far smaller than the 8.97-point gain on GTZAN, a cross-dataset discrepancy the paper doesn't really dwell on.

動機Motivation

音樂曲風分類支撐的不只是娛樂用的推薦系統,論文特別點出版權管理與音樂治療(例如舒緩曲風用於減壓、明快曲風用於提振情緒)也依賴可靠的分類。作者的問題意識是既有兩條路線都有明顯瓶頸:早期靠手工特徵(MFCC、chromagram)配合 SVM、HMM、KNN 的方法在任務複雜度提高後就顯得吃力;後來的 CNN 雖然能在梅爾頻譜圖上抓到局部樣式,但論文引述指出 CNN「難以無縫整合空間與時間特徵」,而彌補這點的 CNN-LSTM 混合架構又需要「複雜的架構與仔細的超參數調校」,才勉強兼顧局部特徵與長程時間依賴。

Music genre classification supports more than entertainment recommendation systems; the paper specifically points to copyright management and music therapy (calming genres for stress reduction, upbeat genres for mood elevation) as applications that depend on reliable classification. The authors' framing is that both existing lines of work hit clear ceilings: early hand-crafted features (MFCCs, chromagrams) paired with SVM, HMM, or KNN struggled as task complexity grew, and later CNNs, while good at capturing local patterns in mel-spectrograms, are quoted as "struggling to integrate spatial and temporal features seamlessly," with the CNN-LSTM hybrids meant to fix this requiring "complex architectures and careful hyperparameter tuning" just to balance local features against long-range temporal dependency.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景章節把 Tzanetakis 與 Cook(2002)提出的音色、節奏、音高統計特徵框架列為手工特徵時代的奠基工作,接著是 CNN 直接吃梅爾頻譜圖的階段,再到 CNN-LSTM 混合。論文也提到近期以轉換器(transformer)處理音樂任務的研究(引述 Zhao 等人 2022 年關於自注意力機制的工作),指出轉換器在複雜多樣的音樂資料集上表現優於 CNN 與 RNN,但需要遠比中等規模 MIR 資料集更大量的訓練資料。作者聲稱自己的定位跟這些既有系統的差別在於:AECNN 把注意力機制接在 CNN 特徵萃取之後,而不是像完整轉換器那樣整個取代卷積,目的是「在保有表達能力與運算效率之間取得平衡」,適用於 GTZAN(1,000 首)、IMG-Amrita(1,800 首)這種中等規模、無法支撐大型轉換器訓練的資料集。

The related-work section credits Tzanetakis and Cook (2002) and their timbral/rhythmic/pitch statistical feature framework as the founding hand-crafted-feature work, then traces the progression to CNNs operating directly on mel-spectrograms, then to CNN-LSTM hybrids. The paper also cites recent transformer-based work on music tasks (Zhao et al., 2022, on self-attention), noting transformers outperform CNNs and RNNs on complex, diverse music datasets but require far more training data than moderate-scale MIR datasets can supply. The authors position AECNN's difference as attaching attention on top of CNN feature extraction rather than replacing convolution entirely as a full transformer would, aiming to "balance representational expressiveness with computational efficiency" for datasets the scale of GTZAN (1,000 tracks) or their own IMG-Amrita (1,800 tracks), which are too small to train a large transformer from scratch.

方法Method

架構分兩塊:CNN 特徵萃取模組把梅爾頻譜圖逐層卷積、批次正規化、最大池化,輸出攤平成一組 patch embedding,加上可學習的位置編碼後,送進一個雙頭(2-head)自注意力機制,計算 softmax(QK^T/√d_k)V,再用一個可學習的加權向量做「注意力式序列池化」取代常見的全域平均池化,取得最終表示後接全連接層分類。訓練用 AdamW、學習率 0.001、批次 128、100 epoch(patience=10 提前停止),另外套用時間伸縮(0.9-1.1 倍)、音高偏移(±1 半音)、20dB 訊噪比高斯噪音、動態範圍壓縮四種資料增強。

The architecture has two blocks: a CNN feature extractor applies convolution, batch normalization, and max-pooling to mel-spectrograms, flattens the output into patch embeddings, adds a learnable positional encoding, and feeds this into a two-head self-attention block computing softmax(QK^T/sqrt(d_k))V; a learnable weighting vector then does "attention-based sequence pooling" in place of standard global average pooling before a fully-connected classifier head. Training uses AdamW at learning rate 0.001, batch size 128, 100 epochs with early stopping (patience 10), plus four augmentations: time-stretching (0.9-1.1x), pitch-shifting (±1 semitone), 20 dB SNR Gaussian noise, and dynamic-range compression.

Both benchmark datasets are segmented into 30/15/10/6/3-second clips from the original 30-second tracks, with 3-second segments giving the most training instances (10,000 segments for GTZAN, 18,000 for IMG-Amrita, both at an 80/20 track-level split) and, per the authors, the best accuracy: they attribute this to shorter clips both increasing effective sample count and avoiding the "structural transitions and intra-class variability" that dilute discriminative signal in longer segments.

限制Limitations

作者對兩個資料集的品質問題相當坦白:GTZAN 本身「含有重複片段、偶爾標錯、曲風分布不均」,是這個資料集眾所皆知的老問題;IMG-Amrita 雖然是論文的新貢獻,但只由單一位印度音樂專家標注,且「尚未涵蓋印度音樂完整的地域與風格多樣性」。方法論上,論文承認評測只用了統一編碼的 WAV 檔案,「對不同音訊格式與失真壓縮的穩健性尚待驗證」。更根本的問題是論文自己也點出:「曲風本身是主觀且受文化影響的概念,常與情緒、樂器編制、演奏風格等概念重疊」,尤其當代混合曲風的邊界更模糊——這意味著即使準確率數字亮眼,分類目標本身的定義就不完全穩固。

The authors are fairly candid about quality issues in both datasets: GTZAN itself "contains duplicate excerpts, occasional mislabelling, and imbalances in genre representation," a well-known long-standing problem with that benchmark, while IMG-Amrita, though a new contribution, was annotated by a single Indian-music specialist and "does not yet capture the full regional and stylistic diversity of Indian music traditions." Methodologically, the paper admits evaluation only used uniformly encoded WAV files, so "robustness to diverse audio formats and lossy compression schemes remains to be explored." More fundamentally, the authors themselves note that "genre itself is an inherently subjective and culturally influenced concept, often overlapping with mood, instrumentation, and performance style," especially blurred for contemporary hybrid genres — meaning even a strong accuracy number sits on top of a target label that isn't fully stable.

討論Discussion

這篇論文比較能推廣的觀察,其實是一個看起來反直覺的實證結果:3 秒短切片在兩個資料集、三種架構上全部贏過 30 秒完整片段,推翻了「上下文越長分類越準」的預設。但作者對這個現象的解釋(短切片增加樣本數、降低估計變異)只是假設,論文沒有做進一步的控制實驗(例如固定樣本數、只變長度)去驗證到底是樣本量效應還是切片長度本身的效應,這是一個值得注意但論文沒有完全處理掉的因果混淆。跟三個近期方法比較(Hybrid CNN-RNN 89.30%、CNN-Transformer 87.41%、Compact Convolutional Transformer 94.00%),AECNN 的 96.02% 確實領先,但這些對照組是從各自論文的報告數字引用而來,不是在同一套資料切分與訓練條件下重新跑過,所以「贏過轉換器」這個結論的可信度,不如論文自己內部消融實驗來得紮實。

The paper's most transferable observation is a genuinely counterintuitive empirical result: 3-second clips beat full 30-second clips across both datasets and all three architectures, overturning the assumption that longer context always helps classification. But the authors' explanation (shorter clips increase sample count and reduce estimation variance) is only a hypothesis; the paper runs no controlled follow-up (e.g., holding sample count fixed while varying only clip length) to disentangle whether this is a sample-size effect or an effect of clip length itself — a real confound the paper doesn't fully resolve. Against three recent methods (Hybrid CNN-RNN at 89.30%, CNN-Transformer at 87.41%, Compact Convolutional Transformer at 94.00%), AECNN's 96.02% is indeed ahead, but those baselines are quoted from their respective papers rather than re-run under the same data split and training conditions, so the "beats transformers" claim rests on weaker footing than the paper's own internal ablations.

Beethoven Symphony Excerpt Dataset (BSED): An Evaluation Dataset for Orchestral Music Transcription

貝多芬交響曲樂段資料集（BSED）：管弦樂自動轉譜評測資料集

Hans-Ulrich Berendes, Abhirup Saha, Ben Maman, Vlora Arifi-Müller, Meinard Müller

為什麼挑這篇：這篇的核心貢獻是資料集,但作者沒有停在「我們釋出了資料」就收工,而是把三種訓練標籤(訊號處理式序列對齊、轉譜模型式序列對齊、精細音符級對齊)分別拿去訓練同一個 Onsets & Frames 架構,再交叉評測到 BSED 與外部的 PHENICX 資料集上做對照。結果乾淨地分出高下:用最粗的訊號處理對齊訓練,在 BSED-Synth 上只有 36.5% F1;換成音符級對齊訓練,拉到 71.3%;而拿這個在貝多芬交響曲上訓練出來的模型去測完全獨立的 PHENICX,F1 有 65.7%,比在通用的 MusicNet 上訓練的模型(64.9%)還高一點點,顯示管弦樂領域專用資料確實比規模更大但跨領域的資料更有效。Why this one: The core contribution here is a dataset, but the authors don't stop at releasing it — they train the same Onsets & Frames architecture on three different alignment-derived label qualities (signal-processing sequence alignment, transcription-model sequence alignment, and fine-grained note-level alignment) and cross-evaluate on both BSED and the independent PHENICX dataset. The comparison cleanly separates the options: training on the coarsest signal-processing alignment gets only 36.5% F1 on BSED-Synth; switching to note-level alignment pushes that to 71.3%; and the model trained on Beethoven-only orchestral data reaches 65.7% F1 on the fully independent PHENICX set, edging out a MusicNet-trained model's 64.9% there, evidence that domain-specific orchestral training data beats larger but out-of-domain data even on transfer.

動機Motivation

自動音樂轉譜(AMT)在獨奏樂器與小型室內樂已有相當進展,靠的是 MAESTRO、ASAP 這類高品質對齊資料集撐起訓練與評測。管弦樂轉譜卻嚴重落後,作者指出關鍵瓶頸是「同時具備真實管弦樂錄音、完整多樂器樂譜、且兩者精確對齊」的公開資源極度稀缺。既有選項要嘛是合成音訊(缺乏真實演出的細微變化),要嘛侷限在標注相對容易的小型室內樂編制。管弦樂本身的密集複音、多樣音色、不同指揮與錄音場地帶來的變異,讓這個對齊問題比獨奏或室內樂困難得多,這正是作者選擇貝多芬九大交響曲作為切入點的原因——曲目公開、錄音數量龐大、且橫跨近一世紀的錄音技術。

Automatic music transcription (AMT) has made real progress on solo instruments and small chamber ensembles, propped up by high-quality aligned datasets like MAESTRO and ASAP. Orchestral transcription lags far behind because, as the authors put it, publicly available resources combining real orchestral recordings with complete multi-instrument scores that are accurately aligned are extremely scarce. Existing options are either synthetic audio (missing the nuance of real performance) or restricted to small chamber ensembles where annotation is more tractable. Orchestral music's dense polyphony, wide timbral diversity, and the variability introduced by different conductors and recording venues make this alignment problem much harder than for solo or chamber settings, which is why the authors chose Beethoven's nine symphonies as an entry point: public-domain, recorded prolifically, and spanning nearly a century of recording technology.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景章節點名的獨奏鋼琴資料集陣營包括 MAESTRO、MAPS、SMD、ASAP,以及較新的 PiJAMA(爵士鋼琴)與 ATEPP(表現式鋼琴演奏)。室內樂陣營則有 MusicNet、Bach10、URMP、ChoraleBricks,以及大規模合成資料集 Slakh。管弦樂這一塊,作者點名 PHENICX Anechoic(分開錄製的分軌,總長僅十到二十分鐘)、Beethoven Anechoic(規模有限的控制條件錄音),以及 SynthSOD(大規模但為合成資料)。作者明確指出:「至今沒有任何資料集把既有的真實管弦樂錄音跟對齊的樂譜資訊結合起來」,這正是 BSED/BSD 要填補的具體空缺,而不是泛泛地說「管弦樂資料不夠多」。

The related-work section names the solo-piano camp — MAESTRO, MAPS, SMD, ASAP, plus newer PiJAMA (jazz piano) and ATEPP (expressive piano performance) — and the chamber-ensemble camp — MusicNet, Bach10, URMP, ChoraleBricks, and the large synthetic Slakh dataset. On the orchestral side specifically, the authors name PHENICX Anechoic (separately recorded stems, only 10-20 minutes total), Beethoven Anechoic (limited controlled-condition recordings), and SynthSOD (large-scale but purely synthetic). The authors state plainly that "to date, no dataset has combined existing real orchestral recordings with aligned score information" — a specific, named gap that BSED/BSD is built to fill, not a vague complaint that orchestral data is scarce.

方法Method

BSED 是 20 段選自貝多芬九首交響曲的樂段(10 段人工挑選的知名主題、10 段隨機挑選以確保多樣性),每段提供 5 種音訊版本(3 種來自不同指揮/樂團的歷史錄音、1 段固定用 Blomstedt 的高品質 CD 錄音、1 段用 NotePerformer 合成),總長約 37 分鐘,共 31,710 個標注音符事件。對齊分兩階段:第一階段用 Sync Toolbox 的動態時間規整(DTW),但特徵不是傳統訊號處理特徵,而是用 Maman 與 Bermano(2022)在 MusicNet 上訓練的轉譜模型活化值,所有 80 段真實錄音都經過人工聽感覆核,估計最差情況對齊誤差約 150 毫秒,多數在 50 毫秒以內;第二階段在 ±150 毫秒窗口內把每個音符起始點「吸附」到轉譜模型輸出的活化峰值,解決樂譜上同時值音符實際演出時的微觀不同步。

BSED consists of 20 excerpts drawn from Beethoven's nine symphonies (10 manually chosen for iconic themes, 10 randomly chosen for diversity), each provided in 5 audio versions — 3 historical recordings from different conductors/orchestras, 1 fixed high-quality Blomstedt CD recording, and 1 NotePerformer synthetic rendering — totaling about 37 minutes and 31,710 annotated note events. Alignment runs in two stages: stage one uses DTW from the Sync Toolbox, but with features drawn not from traditional signal processing but from the activations of a transcription model (Maman and Bermano, 2022, trained on MusicNet), with all 80 real recordings manually verified by ear, giving a worst-case estimated alignment error of about 150 ms and typically under 50 ms; stage two "snaps" each note onset within a ±150 ms window to the nearest transcription-model activation peak, resolving the micro-asynchrony between notes that share the same notated onset but are performed slightly apart.

The companion training corpus, BSD, scales this up to 63 hours across 415 movement recordings covering all nine symphonies, using the same two-stage alignment but with a wider 1-second snapping window (versus 150 ms for BSED) and symbolic scores from the pre-existing MuseData repository rather than manually re-created scores, since MuseData annotations skip grace notes and default velocity to a flat value of 64. Because some BSD recordings overlap with BSED's source recordings, the authors define five different train/test split strategies of varying strictness and recommend a "Version Split — Single Movement" as the balanced default for cross-dataset evaluation.

限制Limitations

作者自己指出「樂器無關轉譜」對管弦樂而言可能是個不良定義的問題:多個聲部同時演奏相同音高時,現有指標會把它們當成單一音符處理,可能簡化了真實情況。BSD 因規模較大,人工覆核程度遠低於 BSED,標注品質有落差要靠使用者自行取捨(論文提供 quality_ratings_BSD.csv 標示每筆錄音的 Fréchet Audio Distance 分數)。範疇上,資料集只涵蓋貝多芬交響曲,不含《第九號》第四樂章(因為含人聲與無固定音高的打擊樂,轉譜模型無法處理),也不含豎琴、管風琴等特殊編制樂器,無法直接推論到其他作曲家或編制。連結度上,note-level 對齊終究仰賴轉譜模型是否能在對應時間點提供足夠強的起始線索,對於弱起奏或音色混濁難以分辨音高的段落,精度上限受模型本身限制。

The authors themselves note that instrument-agnostic transcription may be an ill-posed problem for orchestral music: when multiple sources produce overlapping notes at identical pitch, current metrics treat them as a single note, potentially oversimplifying the real situation. Because of its larger scale, BSD received far less manual verification than BSED, and the authors leave that quality trade-off to users via a provided quality_ratings_BSD.csv file scoring each recording with Fréchet Audio Distance. In scope, the dataset covers only Beethoven symphonies, excludes the Ninth's choral fourth movement entirely (its singing and unpitched percussion can't be handled by the transcription model used for alignment), and omits specialty instruments like harp or organ, so findings don't directly generalize to other composers or instrumentations. Note-level alignment ultimately depends on the transcription model providing a sufficiently strong onset cue at the right time, so precision is capped by that model's own limits on soft attacks or timbrally blended passages.

討論Discussion

最能推廣的發現是關於「訓練標籤品質」而非模型架構:同一個 Onsets & Frames 架構,只換訓練用的對齊標籤,BSED-Synth 上的 F1 就從訊號處理對齊的 36.5% 一路拉到轉譜式序列對齊的 69.2%、再到音符級對齊的 71.3%,顯示對齊標籤本身的精細度,可能比換一個更大的模型架構帶來更直接的效益。跨資料集遷移的結果也支持一個具體結論:只在貝多芬交響曲上訓練的模型(TBSD),在完全獨立的 PHENICX 資料集上達到 65.7% F1,已經追平甚至略勝在規模相近的通用資料集 MusicNet 上訓練的模型(64.9%),而把兩者混合訓練(TBSD+MN)進一步推到 69.0%,說明管弦樂領域專用資料與跨領域資料是互補的,而非互斥。

跟兩個公開的預訓練轉譜系統相比,BasicPitch 在 BSED 上只有 24.9% F1、YMT3+ 是 42.3%,遠低於本文自己訓練的 71.1%,這個落差本身就是「通用轉譜系統不足以處理管弦樂」最直接的量化證據。

The most transferable finding is about training-label quality rather than model architecture: the same Onsets & Frames architecture, with only the alignment label swapped, goes from 36.5% F1 on BSED-Synth with signal-processing alignment to 69.2% with transcription-based sequence alignment to 71.3% with note-level alignment, suggesting that alignment label precision may deliver more direct benefit than a bigger model architecture would. The cross-dataset transfer results support a specific conclusion too: a model trained only on Beethoven symphonies (TBSD) reaches 65.7% F1 on the fully independent PHENICX dataset, already matching or slightly beating a model trained on the similarly-sized but generic MusicNet (64.9%), and combining both (TBSD+MN) pushes further to 69.0% — indicating domain-specific orchestral data and cross-domain data are complementary rather than redundant.

Against two public pretrained transcribers, BasicPitch gets only 24.9% F1 on BSED and YMT3+ gets 42.3%, both far below this paper's own 71.1%, a gap that is itself the most direct quantified evidence that general-purpose transcription systems aren't yet adequate for orchestral music.
