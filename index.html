Generative AI Training and Copyright Law

生成式 AI 訓練與著作權法：主張 GenAI 訓練不屬於歐盟 DSM 指令的文字與資料探勘（TDM）例外、美國合理使用也靠不住，記憶化另外構成「權重內重製」的獨立風險，並提出以 URL／ISRC·ISWC／AcoustID 指紋分三層的訓練資料文件化框架——一篇立場鮮明但自承屬少數說、且美國判決敘述前後不一致的綜述

Sebastian Stober, Tim W. Dornis
DOI:10.5334/tismir.258 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-10-29
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：一位 MIR 機器學習研究者（Stober）與一位著作權法學者（Dornis）合寫，濃縮他們 2024 年的德文開放取用專書，主張 GenAI 訓練不是 TDM，因此歐盟研究機構引以為據的 DSM 指令第 3、4 條例外不適用。今天是週日，依週末規則只從先前看過的列表挑：本週 cs.SD／eess.AS／cs.IR 已沒有未讀的 MIR 論文，所以改從週一掃過的 TISMIR 清單裡挑未讀的三篇。選它是因為它直接碰到 MIR 社群的日常操作——資料集再利用、分享模型權重、甚至 EnCodec 這類神經編解碼器——而且它把結論寫得很硬（「一般而言建議事先取得明確授權」），值得檢查論證撐不撐得住。它沒有任何實驗數字，評估的重點是論證與事實敘述。Why this one: Co-written by an MIR machine-learning researcher (Stober) and a copyright scholar (Dornis), it condenses their 2024 German open-access book and argues that GenAI training is not TDM, so the DSM Directive's Articles 3 and 4 exceptions that EU research institutions rely on do not apply. Today is Sunday, so by the weekend rule I only pick from listings already seen: this week's cs.SD/eess.AS/cs.IR lists have no unread MIR papers left, so I took three unread articles from the TISMIR list scanned on Monday. I picked this one because it touches everyday MIR practice (reusing datasets, sharing model weights, even neural codecs such as EnCodec) and states its conclusion firmly ("it is generally advisable to seek prior and express permission"), so it is worth checking whether the argument holds. It has no experimental numbers; what gets evaluated here is the argument and its factual claims.

動機Motivation

生成式音樂模型的品質靠的是訓練資料的規模，而這些資料大多受著作權保護。論文開頭引了一份具體證據：在 RIAA 控告 Suno AI 的訴訟裡，Suno 自己承認訓練資料包含「開放網路上基本上所有品質尚可的音樂檔案」，並主張這是合理使用。作者認為 MIR 社群一直把著作權當成外部問題，但它已經直接影響到研究者能用哪些資料集、能不能公開模型，以及該接受誰的贊助。

文章要回答三件事：一，現有的 TDM 例外（歐盟）與合理使用（美國）是否涵蓋 GenAI 訓練；二，訓練資料記憶化（memorization）在法律上意味著什麼，為什麼它跟資料取得是否合法無關；三，在法律未定之前，訓練資料該怎麼文件化，才能同時照顧權利人、研究者與開發者。作者也事先聲明，法律上合法與倫理上正當要分開看，兩者不互相推導。

The quality of generative music models depends on the scale of their training data, most of which is copyright protected. The paper opens with a concrete piece of evidence: in the RIAA lawsuit against Suno AI, Suno itself stated that its training data includes "essentially all music files of reasonable quality that are accessible on the open Internet" and argued that this is fair use. The authors think the MIR community has treated copyright as an external issue, but it now directly shapes which datasets researchers can use, whether models can be released, and whose sponsorship to accept.

The article addresses three questions: first, whether existing TDM exceptions (EU) and fair use (US) cover GenAI training; second, what training-data memorization means legally, and why it is independent of whether the data were lawfully obtained; third, how training data should be documented, before the law is settled, in a way that serves rightholders, researchers and developers alike. The authors also state up front that legal permissibility and ethical defensibility must be kept apart and do not imply each other.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

法學與倫理脈絡。 作者點名了一串前人：Scherzinger（2014）批評音樂智慧財產權的擴張造成文化共有資源的「圈地」；Drott（2019）分析音樂 AI 產業如何從訓練資料抽取價值；Caldwell（2023）主張 AI 藝術的作者應是終端使用者；Morreale 等人（2023）調查 MIR 資料集，指出它們多半是為傳統分析任務建立的。技術面則引 Carlini 等人（2023）的 LLM 抽取實驗與 Somepalli 等人（2022）在 Stable Diffusion 上觀察到的影像複製。

這篇最接近、卻沒有引用的是資料文件化那條線。 第 4 節的三層文件化框架，本質上是 Gebru 等人的 Datasheets for Datasets（2018 年提出）加上 Longpre 等人 Data Provenance Initiative（2023）那種逐筆來源與授權稽核，再換成音樂業的識別碼（ISRC、ISWC）與 MIR 自己的音訊指紋工具。新的部分是「用指紋反查來源」這一步，以及把 MIR 既有工具定位成合規基礎設施；框架本身不是新發明，文中沒有交代它和這些既有規範的關係。

Legal and ethical context. The authors name a series of predecessors: Scherzinger (2014) criticizes the expansion of musical IP as an "enclosure" of shared cultural resources; Drott (2019) analyzes how the music-AI industry extracts value from training data; Caldwell (2023) argues that the author of AI art should be the end user; Morreale et al. (2023) survey MIR datasets and note that most were built for traditional analytical tasks. On the technical side they cite Carlini et al.'s (2023) LLM extraction experiments and Somepalli et al.'s (2022) observations of image replication in Stable Diffusion.

The closest line of work, and one the paper does not cite, is dataset documentation. The three-tier framework in Section 4 is essentially Gebru et al.'s Datasheets for Datasets (proposed in 2018) combined with the per-item source and license auditing of Longpre et al.'s Data Provenance Initiative (2023), translated into music-industry identifiers (ISRC, ISWC) and MIR's own audio-fingerprinting tools. What is new is the step of using fingerprints to recover provenance, and the framing of existing MIR tools as compliance infrastructure; the framework itself is not new, and the paper does not explain how it relates to these existing norms.

方法Method

這是一篇法學論證加技術綜述，沒有實驗。核心論證在 2.4 節：DSM 指令第 2(2) 條把 TDM 定義為「以產生資訊（包括模式、趨勢與相關性）為目的的自動化分析技術」。作者承認學習到的機率分佈本身也是一種「資訊」，也承認「以合成來分析」（analysis-by-synthesis）在訊號處理裡由來已久，但主張關鍵在目的：TDM 的目的是取得關於資料的資訊，GenAI 訓練的目的是產出與訓練資料相似的表現性內容，所以落在 TDM 例外的立法目的之外。

依這條線，他們把 MIR 任務分成兩邊：調性、和弦、節拍、樂器辨識、旋律與人聲擷取、音源分離、轉譜、對齊、指紋、哼唱檢索都算 TDM；注腳特別說明，就算音源分離用到生成模型，只要目的是分析，仍算 TDM。合理使用的部分，作者逐一走過四個要件，並引 Bartz v. Anthropic 與 Kadrey v. Meta 兩件 2025 年的判決，結論是第一要件（轉化性）與第四要件（市場影響）是決勝點，而且答案取決於模型拿去做什麼。

This is a legal argument plus a technical review, with no experiments. The core argument is in Section 2.4: Article 2(2) of the DSM Directive defines TDM as "any automated analytical technique aimed at analyzing text and data in digital form in order to generate information which includes but is not limited to patterns, trends and correlations." The authors concede that a learned probability distribution is itself a kind of "information," and that analysis-by-synthesis has a long history in signal processing, but argue that what matters is purpose: TDM aims to obtain information about the data, whereas GenAI training aims to produce expressive content resembling the training data, which falls outside the purpose of the TDM exception.

Following this line, they sort MIR tasks: key, chord, beat and instrument recognition, melody and vocal extraction, source separation, transcription, alignment, fingerprinting and query by humming all count as TDM; a footnote adds that source separation remains TDM even when it uses generative models, as long as its purpose is analytical. For fair use, the authors walk through the four factors and cite two 2025 decisions, Bartz v. Anthropic and Kadrey v. Meta, concluding that the first factor (transformativeness) and the fourth (market effect) are decisive, and that the answer depends on what the model is used for.

限制Limitations

核心立場自承是少數說。 2.4.3 節作者明白寫出：歐洲多數學說與幾個德國法院採相反見解，AI Act 第 53(1)(c) 條要求通用模型提供者遵守 DSM 第 4(3) 條的保留聲明，等於預設 TDM 例外適用於 GenAI 訓練；漢堡法院的 LAION 案二審也採此見解。論文的實務建議（「一般而言建議事先取得明確授權」）建立在這個尚待歐盟法院（CJEU）裁決的少數立場上，讀者應該把它當成一方主張，而不是現行法的描述。

美國判決的敘述前後不一致。 2.4.2 節開頭說兩個地方法院對 Anthropic 與 Meta 作出了有利的即決判決（summary judgment），後文卻說市場損害主張「足以在即決判決階段撐過駁回」，小結又說兩院「拒絕在訴狀階段決定合理使用」——三種程序狀態互相矛盾。據我所知，Bartz 案法官認定訓練本身屬合理使用，但不承認「訓練授權市場」是原告可主張的市場；論文寫成法院認為這類主張有理，方向可能相反。另外文中說 Suno 與 Udio 在「2024 年底」與唱片公司和解；據我所知這些和解是 2025 年下半年才陸續宣布的，這個年份可能有誤。

音樂記憶化的證據很薄。 第 3 節的技術依據幾乎都來自文字與影像（Carlini 等人的「模型越大記得越多，約二到五倍」、50 個 token 的上下文、Stable Diffusion 的沙發例子）；音訊只引了 Bralios 等人與 Rahman 等人（2024）兩篇「初步跡象」，作者自己也說這方面研究「仍在起步」。據此推論「很可能是普遍問題」，是外推而不是證據。

The core position is, by the authors' own account, a minority view. Section 2.4.3 states plainly that much of the European literature and several German courts take the opposite view; Article 53(1)(c) of the AI Act requires general-purpose model providers to honor opt-outs under Article 4(3) of the DSM Directive, which presupposes that the TDM exception applies to GenAI training; and the Hamburg LAION appeal adopted that view. The paper's practical advice ("generally advisable to seek prior and express permission") rests on this minority position, which awaits a ruling by the CJEU, so readers should treat it as one side's argument, not a description of current law.

The account of the US decisions is internally inconsistent. Section 2.4.2 opens by saying two district courts granted summary judgment to Anthropic and Meta, later says the market-harm allegations were "plausible enough to survive dismissal at the summary judgment stage," and then concludes that both courts "declin[ed] to resolve fair use at the pleading stage"; these three procedural states contradict each other. To my knowledge, the Bartz court held that training itself was fair use but did not accept a market for training licenses as one the plaintiffs could claim; the paper says the court found such claims plausible, which may be backwards. The paper also dates the Suno and Udio settlements with record labels to "late 2024"; to my knowledge these settlements were announced in the second half of 2025, so the year may be wrong.

The evidence for memorization in music is thin. Section 3's technical support comes almost entirely from text and images (Carlini et al.'s finding that larger models memorize roughly two to five times more, 50-token contexts, the Stable Diffusion sofa example); for audio it cites only two papers on "initial signs," Bralios et al. and Rahman et al. (2024), and the authors themselves say the research is "still in its infancy." Concluding that it is "very likely a general problem" is extrapolation, not evidence.

討論Discussion

最有份量的論點其實在記憶化那節。 作者把問題拆得很清楚：記憶化（memorization）是權重內部的儲存，反芻（regurgitation）是輸出端可觀察到的近乎逐字重現；前者是後者的必要條件但不充分。法律上的推論是：即使訓練時的複製被 TDM 或合理使用涵蓋，那份許可也不延伸到「權重裡留著一份重製」以及之後散布模型或輸出。這直接打到開源模型權重的做法，而且與 2025 年 11 月慕尼黑地方法院在 GEMA 控告 OpenAI 案中「歌詞記憶化構成重製」的判決一致。

神經編解碼器那段對 MIR 最有新意，但它也鬆動了作者自己的分界。 作者主張 EnCodec、DAC 這類編解碼器的解碼器會從權重裡補出輸入沒有的資訊，因此適用同樣的記憶化分析。可是同一個邏輯也適用於 MERT 之類用重建或遮罩預測訓練的自監督表徵模型——它們的訓練目的接近生成，用途卻是分析。作者用「目的」劃分 TDM 與 GenAI，同一套訓練過程卻能同時服務兩種目的，這條線在 MIR 最常見的預訓練實務上反而最模糊，文中沒有處理。

文件化建議的可行性沒有數字。 三層框架用 GTZAN、MUSDB18（150 首）、MTG-Jamendo（超過 55,000 首、CC 授權）與 AudioSet（超過 200 萬段 10 秒片段，2023 年重新下載只取回約 84%）舉例，但沒有估計 AcoustID／MusicBrainz 指紋比對在這些資料集上能涵蓋多少比例。MTG-Jamendo 被放在「進階層」，理由其實是授權先天清楚，不是做了指紋比對，例子和層級定義對不太起來。結尾拋出的問題（該不該接受大規模侵權公司的贊助、該不該拒收資料來源不明的論文）比整篇的法律分析更直接，也更值得 ISMIR 社群正面回應。

The most substantial argument is actually in the memorization section. The authors draw a clean distinction: memorization is storage inside the weights, regurgitation is observable near-verbatim reproduction in outputs, and the former is necessary but not sufficient for the latter. The legal inference is that even if copying during training is covered by TDM or fair use, that permission does not extend to retaining a reproduction in the weights or to later distributing the model or its outputs. This goes straight at the practice of releasing open model weights, and it matches the Munich Regional Court's November 2025 ruling in GEMA v. OpenAI that memorized lyrics constitute reproduction.

The neural-codec passage is the most novel part for MIR, but it also loosens the authors' own boundary. They argue that codec decoders such as EnCodec and DAC fill in information absent from the input from their weights, so the same memorization analysis applies. Yet the same logic applies to self-supervised representation models such as MERT trained by reconstruction or masked prediction: their training objective is close to generative while their use is analytical. The authors divide TDM from GenAI by purpose, but one training process can serve both purposes, and this line is blurriest exactly in MIR's most common pretraining practice, which the paper does not address.

The feasibility of the documentation proposal has no numbers. The three tiers are illustrated with GTZAN, MUSDB18 (150 songs), MTG-Jamendo (over 55,000 CC-licensed tracks) and AudioSet (over 2 million 10-second clips, of which a 2023 redownload recovered only about 84%), but there is no estimate of how much of these datasets AcoustID/MusicBrainz fingerprint matching could cover. MTG-Jamendo is placed in the "advanced" tier because its licensing is clear from the start, not because fingerprinting was done, so the example does not quite match the tier definition. The closing questions (whether to accept sponsorship from companies engaged in mass infringement, whether to refuse papers built on undocumented datasets) are more direct than the legal analysis and more deserving of an open answer from the ISMIR community.

Stravinsky in Urbana: The Case for the Evolution of Digital Library Software Design to Support Musicological Scholarship

Stravinsky in Urbana：在不改核心程式的前提下擴充 Greenstone3 數位圖書館——Wavesurfer 播放加 Tartan-ify 自相似熱圖、Mirador＋IIIF＋三元組庫的掃描文件標註，以及唯一破例改核心的正規表示式詮釋資料規則——記錄史特拉汶斯基父子 1949–1978 年在伊利諾大學的足跡；n-of-1 個案，沒有任何正式評估，也沒報收藏件數

Joseph G. Goldstein, David Bainbridge, Christina Bashford, Rachel E. Cowgill, Michael Twidale, J. Stephen Downie, Alan J. Dix, Maureen Reagan, Frankie Perry, Rupert Ridgewell
DOI:10.5334/tismir.320 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-10-14
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：InterMusE 計畫（2021 年起，英國 AHRC 資助）的一個聚焦個案：把音樂會節目單、口述歷史、錄音與書信做成一個以事件為中心的數位館藏，記錄 Igor Stravinsky 與兒子 Soulima 在伊利諾大學厄巴納—香檳分校一帶 1949 到 1978 年的活動，並說明如何在 Greenstone3 上擴充出音樂學者需要的功能。同樣依週末規則從週一看過的 TISMIR 清單挑。選它是因為它提出一個值得記下的區分：「檢索音樂」（M-IR）與「檢索關於音樂的資訊」（MI-R），後者正是 ISMIR 主流較少照顧的使用者；同時它也是一個典型的「只有展示、沒有評估」的系統論文，作者自己承認沒有做任何正式評估，也沒有報告收藏規模。Why this one: A focused case study within the InterMusE project (UK AHRC-funded, since 2021): concert programs, oral histories, recordings and correspondence are assembled into an event-centered digital collection documenting Igor Stravinsky and his son Soulima around the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign from 1949 to 1978, and the paper explains how Greenstone3 was extended with the functions music scholars needed. Also picked under the weekend rule from the TISMIR list seen on Monday. I chose it because it offers a distinction worth recording, between retrieving music (M-IR) and retrieving information about music (MI-R), the latter being users the ISMIR mainstream serves less; it is also a typical "demonstration without evaluation" systems paper, and the authors themselves admit to no formal evaluation and report no collection size.

動機Motivation

表演史研究要處理的證據天生零散：節目單、海報、手寫信、報紙評論、錄音、口述訪談，格式各異，也散落在不同館藏。作者認為現有的通用數位圖書館（DL）平台，例如 DSpace、Fedora、ePrints 與 Greenstone，擅長的是穩定的詮釋資料綱要、線性匯入與「已知項目」搜尋；人文研究則是反覆修改描述框架、跨媒體追蹤人與事件的關係，兩者的工作方式對不上。

文章提出三個研究問題：通用 DL 系統能如何讓記錄不足的文化史浮上檯面（RQ1）；在這個個案與這個平台上，學者與 DL 的互動如何形塑詮釋資料結構（RQ2）；能從中歸納出哪些擴充開源 DL 平台的設計原則（RQ3）。個案本身是史特拉汶斯基父子在一個美國中西部大學城的足跡——1949、1950、1963 年三屆當代藝術節，以及 1969 年 Krannert 中心開幕演出——主流研究多聚焦在倫敦、巴黎與紐約，這段歷史幾乎沒有被記錄。

The evidence that performance history works with is inherently scattered: programs, posters, handwritten letters, newspaper reviews, recordings and oral interviews, all in different formats and held in different collections. The authors argue that existing general-purpose digital library (DL) platforms such as DSpace, Fedora, ePrints and Greenstone are good at stable metadata schemas, linear ingestion and known-item search, whereas humanities research repeatedly revises its descriptive frameworks and traces relationships between people and events across media; the two ways of working do not fit.

The article poses three research questions: how general-purpose DL systems can surface under-documented cultural histories (RQ1); in this case and on this platform, how interactions between scholars and the DL shape metadata structures (RQ2); and what design principles for extending open-source DL platforms can be derived (RQ3). The case itself is the presence of Stravinsky and his son in a Midwestern college town (the 1949, 1950 and 1963 Festivals of Contemporary Arts, and the 1969 opening of the Krannert Center), a history almost entirely undocumented because mainstream scholarship focuses on London, Paris and New York.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

M-IR 與 MI-R。 作者以 Lee（2010）的已知項目搜尋研究與 Lewis 等人（2015）為起點，區分「檢索音樂本身」（music-(information retrieval)）與「檢索關於音樂的資訊」（(music information)-retrieval），並把自己的工作對齊 Gotham 等人（2025）的「Everything Corpus」構想——語料庫不只收音訊，也收創作過程的文件、相關人物與場地。方法上，「切片史」（slice history）沿用 Bashford 等人 1997 年起的 Concert Life in Nineteenth-Century London 資料庫。

最接近、卻沒被拿來比較的是 Omeka。 文中比較的平台是 DSpace、Fedora、ePrints，但數位人文領域最常用來做「研究者自己策展、帶 IIIF 檢視器與標註的館藏」的平台是 Omeka S，這篇做出來的東西（展示導向的館藏、Mirador 檢視、標註）跟它幾乎同一類，文中沒有提。另外兩個元件也有明確前身：Tartan-ify 畫的是 Foote（1999）提出的音訊自相似矩陣，正規表示式詮釋資料規則則和 OpenRefine 這類資料清理工具做的是同一件事。這篇的新意不在任何單一元件，而在把它們組進一個讓音樂學者自己擔任館員的建置流程。

M-IR versus MI-R. Starting from Lee's (2010) work on known-item search and from Lewis et al. (2015), the authors distinguish retrieving music itself (music-(information retrieval)) from retrieving information about music ((music information)-retrieval), and align their work with Gotham et al.'s (2025) "Everything Corpus" vision, in which a corpus holds not only audio but also documents of the creative process, the people involved and the venues. Methodologically, "slice history" is borrowed from the Concert Life in Nineteenth-Century London database that Bashford et al. began in 1997.

The closest system, and one that is not compared, is Omeka. The platforms the paper compares are DSpace, Fedora and ePrints, but the platform digital humanities most often uses for researcher-curated collections with IIIF viewers and annotation is Omeka S, and what this paper builds (an exhibition-oriented collection, Mirador viewing, annotation) is very much the same kind of thing; the paper does not mention it. Two components also have clear predecessors: Tartan-ify draws the audio self-similarity matrix introduced by Foote (1999), and the regular-expression metadata rules do the same job as data-cleaning tools such as OpenRefine. The novelty lies not in any single component but in assembling them into a build workflow in which the musicologist acts as the librarian.

方法Method

第一階段是音樂學者自己用 Greenstone Librarian Interface（GLI）建館藏。GLI 有六個分頁（Download、Gather、Enrich、Design、Create、Format），已從 Java 桌面程式移植到 WebSwing，可直接在瀏覽器使用。工作模式是迭代的：先有一個團隊共用的雲端硬碟當「粗剪」，再逐步轉成詮釋資料豐富的館藏；學者讀材料越深，就越會設計新的詮釋資料欄位（例如 siu.Organization），館藏結構跟著改，論文寫作與館藏互相遞迴推進。

第二階段請一位 Greenstone3 開發者進場，規定不准改核心程式，只能靠設定、客製與館藏層級設計。做了三件事：一，音訊文件頁嵌入 Wavesurfer（播放、波形、圖形等化器）與 Tartan-ify（在瀏覽器裡算自相似熱圖），兩者播放游標同步；二，沿用姊妹館藏 British Music Society Concert Ephemera 已整合好的 Mirador 檢視器，背後用 Cantaloupe 提供 IIIF 影像服務、Apache Jena Fuseki 三元組庫存 OpenAnnotation 標註，使用者可修正 OCR 文字並加標註，標註會變成可搜尋的詮釋資料。

第三件事打破了不改核心的規定：在 Greenstone3 裡實作規則式詮釋資料處理，因為檔名常常藏著年份與地點（例如 SoM 代表 School of Music）。規則寫在 CSV 裡，借用 awk 依序比對的作法，每條規則有來源欄位、正規表示式守衛（m 代表比對後從頭組新值、s 代表在原值上做替換）、新值、修飾符與目的欄位；以 _transient 開頭的欄位只在規則執行期間存在。表 1 列出的規則把檔名逐步整理成標題（去副檔名、拆 CamelCase、修正「UISo M」這類打字錯誤），並抽出年份與所屬單位。

In the first phase the musicologist built the collection with the Greenstone Librarian Interface (GLI). GLI has six tabs (Download, Gather, Enrich, Design, Create, Format) and has been ported from a Java desktop application to WebSwing, so it runs directly in the browser. The work is iterative: a shared cloud drive serves as a "rough cut" that is gradually transformed into a metadata-rich collection; the deeper the scholar reads the material, the more new metadata fields they design (for example siu.Organization), the collection structure changes accordingly, and writing the article and building the collection advance by mutual recursion.

In the second phase a Greenstone3 developer was brought in under the rule that the core software must not be modified; everything had to be done through configuration, customization and collection-level design. Three things were built. First, audio document pages embed Wavesurfer (playback, waveform, graphic equalizer) and Tartan-ify (a self-similarity heatmap computed in the browser), with synchronized playback cursors. Second, the Mirador viewer already integrated for the sister collection, British Music Society Concert Ephemera, was reused, backed by Cantaloupe for IIIF image serving and an Apache Jena Fuseki triplestore for OpenAnnotation; users can correct OCR text and add annotations, which become searchable metadata.

The third broke the no-core-changes rule: rule-based metadata processing was implemented inside Greenstone3, because filenames often encode the year and place (for example SoM for School of Music). Rules live in a CSV and borrow awk's sequential matching; each rule has a source field, a regular-expression guard (m builds a new value from captures, s performs substitutions on the original), a value, modifiers and a destination field, and fields prefixed _transient exist only while the rules run. The rules in Table 1 turn filenames into titles step by step (strip the extension, split CamelCase, fix typos such as "UISo M") and extract the year and organization.

限制Limitations

沒有評估，作者自己也這樣說。 4.1 節明白承認這項工作沒有經過 HCI 研究通常要求的正式評估或驗證，是在學者與開發者緊密合作下以實務帶動、反覆修改出來的。全文沒有任何量化資料：沒有團隊以外的使用者、沒有任務完成時間、沒有和其他平台的比較，甚至沒有報告館藏有多少件文件、多少段錄音。讀者看得到的證據是九張截圖和一張規則表。作者提出的下一步（讓一群研究生各自建同主題館藏再比較）是合理的設計，但尚未執行。

n-of-1 的代價。 作者把「為單一真實使用者設計」當成方法優勢，並強調這樣做的生態效度高；但這個個案有一位專職開發者支援，作者自己也說這是「資源密集、必然少見」的機會。從這裡歸納出的設計原則（詮釋資料綱要要能臨時增減、建置工具要交到學者手上、介面要能客製、伺服器端要能擴充）相當通用，很難說是從這個個案特別得出的。另外，詮釋資料品質、版本與出處目前完全沒有功能支援，只能靠「不同學者用不同欄位前綴」這種非正式慣例。

There is no evaluation, and the authors say so. Section 4.1 openly concedes that the work has not undergone the formal evaluation or validation usually expected in HCI research and was developed through a practice-led, iterative process of close collaboration between scholars and developers. The paper contains no quantitative data at all: no study participants beyond the team, no task-completion times, no comparison with other platforms, and not even a count of how many documents or recordings the collection holds. The evidence available to the reader is nine screenshots and one table of rules. The proposed next step (a cohort of graduate students each building a collection on a shared subject, then compared) is a reasonable design but has not been carried out.

The cost of n-of-1. The authors present designing for a single real user as a methodological strength with high ecological validity, but this case had a dedicated developer, which the authors themselves call a "resource-intensive and inevitably rare" opportunity. The design principles derived from it (metadata schemas that can be extended ad hoc, building tools in scholars' hands, customizable interfaces, extensible server-side functions) are generic enough that it is hard to say they follow from this case in particular. Moreover, metadata quality, versioning and provenance currently have no functional support at all and rely on informal conventions such as different scholars using different field prefixes.

討論Discussion

自相似熱圖的論述停在定性。 討論節用《普爾欽奈拉》組曲開頭當例子，說熱圖能看出弦樂段落的重複與法國號的插入，並說它「不需要聽力訓練就能辨識」，是聽覺分析的「強大中介」。這個說法合理，但它描述的是 Foote 以來 MIR 用了二十多年的自相似矩陣，沒有展示熱圖幫學者找到任何原本沒注意到的東西。把圖形等化器當成研究工具（壓低高音木管來聽低音弦樂）也很誠實地附上「代價是音色品質」——這其實點出瀏覽器內工具和 MIR 現成的音源分離之間還有一大段距離，接上分離模型會是更實在的下一步。

這篇真正的貢獻是 M-IR／MI-R 這個框架與「學者即館員」的流程。 對 ISMIR 讀者來說，它提醒的是另一類使用者：他們要的不是找到一首最對的歌，而是把零散、不完整的證據連起來。文中最具體的發現其實是社會性的：節目單、宣傳冊這些被視為次要的材料一旦數位化，就讓厄巴納這樣的地方性音樂生活變得可見。可惜這些主張都沒有被量化——連「這個館藏讓學者找到了哪些原本找不到的連結」都沒有舉出清單——所以它比較像一份設計立場聲明，而不是一份證據報告。

The argument for the self-similarity heatmap stays qualitative. The discussion uses the opening of the Pulcinella Suite as an example, saying the heatmap shows the repetition of the string passage against the intrusion of the horn line, that it can be read "without ear training," and that it is a "powerful intermediary" for aural analysis. That is reasonable, but it describes the self-similarity matrix MIR has used for over twenty years since Foote, and the paper does not show the heatmap helping a scholar find anything they had not already noticed. Using the graphic equalizer as a research tool (suppressing the upper winds to hear the lower strings) is honestly qualified as coming "at the expense of tone quality," which actually shows how far in-browser tools are from MIR's off-the-shelf source separation; hooking up a separation model would be a more substantive next step.

The real contribution is the M-IR/MI-R framing and the "scholar as librarian" workflow. For ISMIR readers it points to a different kind of user, one who wants not the single best song but to connect scattered, incomplete evidence. The paper's most concrete finding is actually social: once programs and brochures, usually treated as minor material, are digitized, local musical life in a place like Urbana becomes visible. Unfortunately none of these claims is quantified (there is not even a list of connections the collection let scholars find that they could not find before), so the paper reads more as a statement of design position than as a report of evidence.

動機Motivation

MIR 從一開始就把音樂學者當成核心使用者，但對音樂學的實際影響一直有限。作者引 Laplante 與 Sauvé（2023）的調查：音樂學者自評的技術能力差異很大，常常難以把軟體整合進研究，甚至有人把數位方法視為對傳統音樂學的威脅；「支援傳統方法」的工具比「替學者做遠距分析」的工具更容易被接受。Borsan 等人（2023）的統計更直接：2012–2021 年 1,055 篇 ISMIR 論文裡，只有 28 篇的成果後來出現在音樂學文獻中。

因此這個計畫刻意不走「遠距聆聽」（distant listening），而是用對齊與輔助介面讓近距離聆聽在大型錄音集上變得可行。研究對象是維也納愛樂的新年音樂會：核心問題是幾十年下來、換了這麼多指揮，是否仍有一種可辨識的「招牌音色」，以及它如何嵌在歷史與機構脈絡裡。附帶目標是讓整套語料與分析結果符合 FAIR 原則（可尋、可及、互通、可重用），供專案外的人重用。

MIR has treated musicologists as a core audience from the start, yet its actual impact on musicology has remained limited. The authors cite Laplante and Sauvé's (2023) survey: musicologists vary widely in self-assessed technical skill, often struggle to integrate software into their research, and some even see digital methods as a threat to traditional musicology; tools that support traditional methods are accepted more readily than tools that do distant analysis on the scholar's behalf. Borsan et al.'s (2023) count is more direct: of 1,055 ISMIR papers from 2012–2021, only 28 produced results that later appeared in the musicology literature.

The project therefore deliberately avoids "distant listening" and instead uses alignment and assistive interfaces to make close listening feasible over large recording collections. The object of study is the Vienna Philharmonic's New Year's Concert: the core question is whether, across decades and many conductors, there is still a discernible "signature sound," and how it sits within historical and institutional contexts. A further aim is to make the whole corpus and the analytical results conform to FAIR principles (findable, accessible, interoperable, reusable) for reuse beyond the project.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者自己點名的前人很完整。 鏈結資料這條線從 OMRAS2 與 Music Ontology（Raimond 與 Sandler）開始，經過 Transforming Musicology、FAST、AcousticBrainz、CALMA，到 CompMusic 的 Dunya（這篇的數位化與詮釋資料管理流程直接參考它）、TROMPA、Polifonia 與用大型語言模型做自然語言查詢的 LinkedMusic。樂譜端用 MEI 與 Verovio；最接近的同類平台是 Dezrann（Ballester 等人，2025），差別在 Dezrann 把各種資料轉成自訂的統一格式來整合，這篇則讓獨立工具透過共享的鏈結資料「在資料層」互通。

最該拿來比、卻沒被提到的是表演分析那條線。 Listen Here! 的核心功能——多個錄音垂直排列、點另一條波形就跳到對齊後的同一位置繼續播、疊上相對速度曲線——和 Müller 團隊在波昂做的 SyncPlayer／Interpretation Switcher 幾乎是同一個設計，而後者在 2000 年代後期就已經在 ISMIR 發表；用速度曲線比較幾十個錄音，則是 CHARM 計畫 Mazurka Project（Sapp、Cook 等人）的招牌方法。文中只引了 Sonic Lineup。這篇真正新的不是對齊或切換播放，而是把它們放到瀏覽器裡，並把標註做成可引用、跨工具共享的鏈結資料。

The predecessors the authors name are thorough. The Linked Data line runs from OMRAS2 and the Music Ontology (Raimond and Sandler) through Transforming Musicology, FAST, AcousticBrainz and CALMA, to CompMusic's Dunya (whose digitization and metadata workflow this project follows directly), TROMPA, Polifonia, and LinkedMusic with its LLM-based natural-language querying. On the score side they use MEI and Verovio; the closest comparable platform is Dezrann (Ballester et al., 2025), the difference being that Dezrann integrates datasets by converting them to custom unified representations, whereas this project lets independent tools interoperate "on the data" through shared Linked Data.

The line that most needs comparing, and is not mentioned, is performance analysis. Listen Here!'s core features (recordings stacked vertically, clicking another waveform to jump to the aligned position and keep playing, relative tempo curves overlaid) are nearly the same design as the SyncPlayer/Interpretation Switcher built by Müller's group in Bonn, published at ISMIR in the late 2000s; comparing dozens of recordings through tempo curves is the signature method of the CHARM Mazurka Project (Sapp, Cook and others). The paper cites only Sonic Lineup. What is genuinely new is not alignment or switched playback but moving them into the browser and making annotations citable Linked Data shared across tools.

方法Method

語料。 團隊收集 CD、DVD、LP 等實體媒體：新年音樂會錄音只有 1987 年以後的還買得到，所以也去二手店與跳蚤市場找，並收其他樂團演奏常見曲目的錄音。合計超過 80 張專輯，涵蓋 61 場新年音樂會（43 場「完整」錄音加上精選輯），另有 30 張其他樂團的專輯。音軌存成未壓縮 WAV，用 AcoustID 指紋辨識、以 MusicBrainz Picard 標上唱片資料，再和實體內頁對照驗證，錯誤或缺漏回饋給 AcoustID 與 MusicBrainz。

發布。 Picard 外掛為每張錄音產生 cuesheet，自寫的 cueToRdf 腳本再轉成符合 Music Ontology 的 RDF（mo:Record、mo:Track、mo:Signal、mo:Release、mo:Performance），並抽取音訊特徵。資料不放資料庫，而是以靜態檔存在 GitHub，透過 w3id.org/ssv 的永久代理網址提供內容協商（同一網址可取 RDF-XML、JSON-LD 或 Turtle），並為這篇論文維護一個 TISMIR 分支，確保引用時的版本固定。受著作權限制的錄音只公開衍生特徵與波形包絡。

對齊與工具。 用 SyncToolbox 的記憶體受限多尺度 DTW（以 chroma onset 特徵），把同一首曲子的所有演出對齊到一個參考錄音，再把參考時間軸切成 20 毫秒的等距格線投射到每個錄音；參考錄音也對齊到樂譜編碼，反覆與尾聲的結構差異用 MEI 的 expansion 元素記錄。三個工具：mei-friend 是瀏覽器 MEI 編輯器（可從 OMR 或 MuseScore 匯入、XML 與樂譜同步選取、GitHub 協作）；Listen Here! 是對齊錄音的比較聆聽環境；Primal 把標註鏈結資料渲染成人看得懂的超媒體頁面。三者透過 Solid 個人資料艙（Pod）與 Music Annotation Ontology 在資料層交換，不需要程式層整合。

Corpus. The team collected physical media (CDs, DVDs, LPs): only New Year's Concert recordings from 1987 onward are still for sale, so they also searched second-hand shops and flea markets and added other orchestras' recordings of frequently performed works. In total there are more than 80 albums covering 61 New Year's Concerts (43 "complete" concert recordings plus compilations) and 30 albums by other orchestras. Tracks were captured as uncompressed WAV, identified with AcoustID fingerprints, labeled with discographic metadata via MusicBrainz Picard and validated against the physical liner notes, with corrections and missing records contributed back to AcoustID and MusicBrainz.

Publication. A Picard plugin generates a cuesheet per recording, and a custom cueToRdf script converts it into RDF following the Music Ontology (mo:Record, mo:Track, mo:Signal, mo:Release, mo:Performance) while extracting audio features. Data are not kept in a database but stored as static files on GitHub, served through w3id.org/ssv permanent proxy URIs with content negotiation (one URI returns RDF-XML, JSON-LD or Turtle), and a TISMIR branch is maintained for this paper so that cited versions stay fixed. For copyright-restricted recordings only derived features and waveform envelopes are published.

Alignment and tools. SyncToolbox's memory-restricted multi-scale DTW (with chroma onset features) aligns every performance of a piece to a reference recording, and the reference timeline is divided into an isometric 20-ms grid projected onto each recording; the reference is also aligned to the score encoding, and structural differences in repeats and codas are recorded with MEI expansion elements. The three tools: mei-friend is a browser MEI editor (import from OMR or MuseScore, synchronized selection between XML and score, GitHub collaboration); Listen Here! is a comparative listening environment for aligned recordings; Primal renders annotation Linked Data as human-readable hypermedia pages. The three exchange data through Solid Pods and the Music Annotation Ontology, with no code-level integration.

限制Limitations

工具沒有評估，對齊也沒有。 作者在第 7 節坦承無法宣稱嚴格評估過工具的使用性，因為個案研究是在共同設計、反覆修改的過程中進行的；Listen Here! 與 Primal 也是隨這篇論文才首次公開。更根本的是，整套近距離聆聽建立在 DTW 對齊上，但全文沒有報告任何對齊準確度——沒有誤差毫秒數，也沒有說明在錄音品質很差的早期錄音上表現如何。切換錄音時「跳到對應位置」是否真的落在同一個音，是讀者無法從文中判斷的。

發現是定性的，分母常常缺席。 五個發現都很具體（例如《天體之音》第 253–258 小節的弱奏記號，在早期錄音裡完全被忽略，直到 1987 年卡拉揚才落實，之後所有維也納愛樂錄音都跟進），但多半沒給出樣本數。最清楚的《藍色多瑙河》例子說「幾乎普遍」不照譜、語料中只有 4 個錄音照譜（3 個 K&K Philharmonie／Kendlinger、1 個紐約愛樂／伯恩斯坦 1982 年），卻沒說這首曲子語料裡總共有幾個錄音，讀者算不出比例。

語料本身也是靠購買與跳蚤市場湊出來的便利樣本，「維也納以外的詮釋差異較大」這類比較會受收得到哪些錄音影響。另外，語料建置與對齊仍需要命令列操作，作者也承認 Essentia 與 SyncToolbox 的預設參數不見得適用其他音樂情境。

The tools are not evaluated, and neither is the alignment. In Section 7 the authors concede they cannot claim to have rigorously evaluated the tools' usability, since the case study was carried out within the co-creative, iterative design process; Listen Here! and Primal are also made public for the first time with this paper. More fundamentally, the whole close-listening setup rests on DTW alignment, yet the paper reports no alignment accuracy at all: no error in milliseconds, nothing on how it performs on poor-quality early recordings. Whether "jumping to the corresponding position" when switching recordings actually lands on the same note is something readers cannot judge from the paper.

The findings are qualitative, and the denominator is often missing. All five findings are concrete (for example, the piano marking in bars 253–258 of Sphärenklänge was completely ignored in early recordings until Karajan realized it in 1987, after which every Vienna Philharmonic recording followed), but most give no sample size. The clearest one, the Blue Danube example, says the score is "almost universally" not followed and only 4 recordings in the corpus follow it (3 by the K&K Philharmonie under Kendlinger, 1 by the New York Philharmonic under Bernstein in 1982), without saying how many recordings of that piece the corpus holds, so readers cannot compute a proportion.

The corpus itself is a convenience sample assembled through purchases and flea markets, so comparisons such as "interpretations outside Vienna vary more widely" depend on which recordings happened to be obtainable. Corpus building and alignment also still require the command line, and the authors admit that the default parameters for Essentia and SyncToolbox will not suit all musical contexts.

討論Discussion

五個發現本身就是這篇最好的論據。 《藍色多瑙河》開頭：所有校訂版裡第一長笛在第 4 小節末從 E 上行到第 5 小節正拍的升 F，但實際演出幾乎都改成下行到 D，好呼應第一圓舞曲的旋律輪廓——傳統壓過了文本。《天體之音》的例子更有意思：卡拉揚 1971 年和柏林愛樂已經這樣處理，所以這個詮釋是「輸入」維也納愛樂的，不是樂團自己演化出來的，直接回應了「招牌音色到底屬於樂團還是指揮」這個核心問題。其餘三例分別是《蝙蝠》序曲第 79–83 小節的彈性速度慣例、《匈牙利萬歲》波卡的三種反覆結構，以及《遊覽列車》第 94 小節 Boskovsky 擔任首席期間（1955–1979）的即興搞笑傳統。

但工具與發現之間的因果沒有被證明。 這些觀察原則上一個耐心的聽者也能做到，論文主張的是工具讓它在幾十個錄音上「變得可行」，卻沒有任何耗時或錯漏的對照。反過來看，這套設計最站得住的貢獻是工程上的：靜態檔加永久網址的極簡發布（不依賴機構典藏庫）、每個發現都有可解析的網址（例如 w3id.org/ssv/TISMIR/annot/oa/ExDonau），讀者能直接點進去看到被標註的小節與對應的錄音片段。這讓音樂學主張第一次能像程式碼一樣被引用和檢查——比起再做一個對齊工具，這才是它對 MIR 與音樂學之間那道鴻溝真正的推進。作者也誠實地提到 GitHub 與 Solid 的長期可持續性風險。

The five findings are the paper's best argument. In the opening of the Blue Danube, every critical edition has the first flute rising from E at the end of bar 4 to F-sharp on the downbeat of bar 5, but performances almost always descend to D instead, echoing the melodic contour of the first waltz: tradition overriding the text. The Sphärenklänge example is even more interesting: Karajan had already used the same reading with the Berlin Philharmonic in 1971, so the interpretation was imported into the Vienna Philharmonic rather than evolved within it, which speaks directly to the core question of whether a signature sound belongs to the orchestra or the conductor. The other three are the rubato convention in bars 79–83 of the Fledermaus overture, three competing repeat structures in the polka Éljen a Magyar!, and the improvised comic tradition at bar 94 of Vergnügungszug during Boskovsky's tenure as concertmaster (1955–1979).

But the causal link between tools and findings is not demonstrated. A patient listener could in principle make these observations, and the paper's claim is that the tools make it "tractable" over dozens of recordings, yet there is no comparison of time spent or errors missed. Seen the other way, the design's most defensible contribution is engineering: minimal publication via static files plus permanent URIs (no institutional repository needed), and a resolvable URI for every finding (for example w3id.org/ssv/TISMIR/annot/oa/ExDonau) that lets readers click through to the annotated bars and the corresponding recording segments. This lets musicological claims be cited and checked like code for the first time, which advances the bridge between MIR and musicology more than yet another alignment tool would. The authors also honestly note the long-term sustainability risks of GitHub and Solid.
