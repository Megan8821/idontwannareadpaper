Copying Versus Randomization in Lempel-Ziv Music Synthesis

Lempel-Ziv 音樂合成中的複製與隨機化

Nadav Mishan, Ram Zamir
arXiv:2609.11353 · cs.SD, cs.IT · 投稿submitted 2026-09-11
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文把音樂生成用的 Lempel-Ziv 演算法(LZ78)加上兩個可調參數——限制每棵樹的訓練資料量,以及新提出的「steps」參數(延後幾步才把指標帶回樹根)——用來控制生成過程「照抄訓練資料」與「隨機亂走」之間的平衡;論文引用 Cover 與 Thomas 的 LZ 漸進片語數定理推導出平均序列長度的理論公式(L_s ≈ log2(n)/H(x)),並用兩張圖驗證兩個參數確實能讓平均序列長度朝理論預期的方向移動,但除了這兩張趨勢圖之外沒有任何準確率、使用者評分或與最相近前作(Ding et al. 2025 的 LzMidi)的直接比較數字,只有作者自己聽過生成結果後寫下的兩點定性觀察(節奏僵硬、序列長度與「連貫性其實是抄襲」之間的正相關)。Why this one: The paper adds two tunable knobs to LZ78-based music generation -- capping the training data each dictionary tree sees, and a new "steps" parameter that delays the return-to-root move -- to control the trade-off between verbatim copying and randomized generation, citing Cover and Thomas's asymptotic LZ phrase-count theorem to derive a heuristic for average sequence length (L_s ≈ log2(n)/H(x)); two figures show both knobs move L_s in the predicted direction, but beyond those two trend plots the paper reports no accuracy, listener-rating, or head-to-head numbers against its closest prior work (Ding et al.'s 2025 LzMidi), only two qualitative listening observations (rigid rhythm, and a direct trade-off between sequence coherence and verbatim copying).

動機Motivation

生成式 AI 音樂目前由擴散模型、transformer 與 GAN 主導,但這些方法運算成本高、生成邏輯也不透明。作者主張以 Lempel-Ziv(LZ)通用壓縮演算法作為替代路徑:LZ 能即時運算、又能從資料學出統計模型,先前已有 Dubnov 與 Assayag(2002)以及 Ding 等人(2025)的 LzMidi 探索過用 LZ 做符號音樂生成。

但 LZ 演算法的核心邏輯——盡量偵測並重複使用已出現過的長序列以最大化壓縮率——正好與音樂創作的期待相衝突:新作品通常是「受既有作品啟發」而非「逐字複製既有作品」,標準 LZ 演算法的輸出卻容易變成訓練資料的逐字片段。這篇論文要解決的正是這個「過度複製」與「隨機化不足或過度」之間如何拿捏的問題,提出兩個控制平均序列長度的方法:限制每棵樹的訓練資料量,以及新提出的 steps 參數,讓演算法延伸序列時不必每一步都回到樹根。

Generative AI for music is currently dominated by diffusion, transformer, and GAN-based models, which are computationally expensive and opaque in how they arrive at an output. The authors instead build on Lempel-Ziv (LZ) universal compression, which runs in real time and learns a statistical model directly from data; prior work explored LZ-family methods for symbolic music generation, including Dubnov and Assayag (2002) and, more recently, Ding et al.'s 2025 LzMidi.

The catch is that LZ's core mechanism -- detecting and reusing long previously-seen sequences to maximize compression -- runs directly against what creative composition wants: new music is expected to be inspired by prior works, not copied verbatim, yet standard LZ output tends to consist of literal excerpts from the training corpus. This paper targets exactly that over-copy/under-copy trade-off, proposing two controls over the average sequence length retained in the dictionary: capping how much training data each tree sees, and a new "steps" parameter that lets the algorithm extend a branch for several steps before resetting to the root.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

LZ 家族用於音樂生成並非新事:Dubnov 與 Assayag(2002)的「風格化音樂生成的通用預測」就已經用通用預測(與 LZ 同一系譜的統計壓縮技術)學習音樂風格模型,概念上與可變記憶長度、後綴樹式生成方法(如 factor oracle)相近——本質上都是靠限制或延伸「上下文記憶長度」來控制生成的忠實度與原創性,這篇論文提出的「steps」參數其實就是這個既有想法的一個具體化版本,而不是全新的機制。

跟這篇論文最直接相關的前作是 Ding 等人 2025 年發表於 ISIT 的 LzMidi,同樣是用壓縮為基礎的符號音樂生成,但論文從頭到尾沒有把自己的輸出拿去跟 LzMidi 做任何直接比較(無論是量化指標或聽感 A/B),只在文獻回顧提到它的存在。論文也引用 Lu 等人 2025 年的 MelodySim 作為未來可以拿來檢測「生成內容是否抄襲」的工具,但目前完全沒有實作或使用,只是留在未來工作。整體而言,背景章節點名了兩個具體前作,但沒有清楚說明自己的兩個控制參數跟這些既有的可變記憶長度技巧比起來,量化差異究竟在哪裡。

Using the LZ family for music generation is not new: Dubnov and Assayag's 2002 "Universal Prediction Applied to Stylistic Music Generation" already used universal prediction -- a statistical-compression technique in the same lineage as LZ -- to learn style models, conceptually close to variable-memory-length, suffix-tree-style generation methods (e.g., factor oracle): all of these control generation fidelity versus originality by capping or extending how much context history the model keeps. The "steps" parameter proposed here is essentially a concrete instance of that existing idea rather than a new mechanism.

The most directly relevant prior work is Ding et al.'s 2025 LzMidi (ISIT), also a compression-based symbolic music generator, but the paper never runs any direct comparison against LzMidi's output, quantitative or listening-based -- it is only cited in the literature review. The paper also cites Lu et al.'s 2025 MelodySim as a tool that could test generated output for plagiarism, but this is left entirely to future work, not implemented or used here. On the whole, the background section names two concrete prior systems but does not spell out how the two proposed control knobs quantitatively differ from these existing variable-memory techniques.

方法Method

訓練資料是 Google Magenta 的 MAESTRO 資料庫(約 200 小時鋼琴演奏 MIDI),只取音高資訊,忽略力度與音符起訖事件;為了讓不同調性、八度的作品也能對齊重複型態,先把絕對音高轉成「相對音程」(前後音符的差值),LZ 樹學到的其實是音程結構而非絕對音高。

演算法採用 LZ78 分支,把已見過的序列存成一棵樹:遇到已存在的子序列就把該節點出現頻率加一並往下走;遇到新序列則在當前節點新增子節點、並把指標移回樹根——這裡是論文唯一偏離標準 LZ78 的地方:提出「steps」參數,讓演算法延伸序列若干步之後才回到樹根,藉此拉長平均序列長度 L_s。

另一個控制 L_s 的方式來自理論推導:引用 Cover 與 Thomas 資訊理論教科書定理 13.5.2,LZ 壓縮下字典片語數 c(n) 滿足 limsup[c(n)log2 c(n)/n] ≤ H(x),搭配 c(n) 的上界估計式,推出平均序列長度的啟發式公式 L_s ≈ log2(n)/H(x)——顯示 L_s 會隨訓練資料量 n 增加而變長。因此論文改用「樹的集成」:把訓練資料切給多棵各自獨立的樹,藉由縮小每棵樹看到的 n 來縮短 L_s。生成時先均勻隨機選一棵樹,依照子節點出現頻率機率式地輸出符號直到走到葉節點,再換一棵樹重複,直到湊滿要求的音符數。

Training data is Google Magenta's MAESTRO database (~200 hours of piano performance MIDI), using only pitch information and discarding velocity and note-on/off events; to let repeated patterns line up across pieces in different keys and octaves, absolute pitches are first converted to relative intervals (the difference between consecutive notes), so the LZ tree actually learns interval structure rather than absolute pitch.

The algorithm is the LZ78 branch of the LZ family, storing seen sequences as a tree: an already-seen subsequence increments that node's frequency and moves the pointer down; a new subsequence adds a child node and resets the pointer to the root -- this reset step is the one place the paper departs from standard LZ78, introducing a "steps" parameter that lets the algorithm extend a branch for a set number of steps before resetting, lengthening the average sequence length L_s.

The second control over L_s comes from theory: citing Cover and Thomas's theorem 13.5.2, the number of dictionary phrases c(n) under LZ compression satisfies limsup[c(n)log2 c(n)/n] ≤ H(x); combined with an upper bound on c(n), this yields a heuristic L_s ≈ log2(n)/H(x), showing L_s grows with training data size n. The paper therefore uses a tree ensemble -- splitting data across several independent trees so each sees a smaller n, shortening L_s. Generation uniformly picks a tree, probabilistically emits symbols by child-node frequency until a leaf is reached, then switches trees and repeats until the requested note count is reached.

限制Limitations

整篇論文除了兩張顯示「L_s 隨每棵樹訓練歌曲數增減」與「L_s 隨 steps 增減」的趨勢圖之外,沒有任何準確率、困惑度或聽眾評分等量化指標,也完全沒有跟最相近的前作 LzMidi 或 Dubnov 與 Assayag 的通用預測方法做直接比較,論文自己也只用「範例可在 GitHub 上聽」帶過評估,沒有做正式的聽感實驗。

訓練與生成階段只用了相對音程,力度、音符時值、休止符等節奏資訊完全沒有建模,論文自己承認生成結果因此「節奏僵硬」、聽起來像固定拍子的機械輸出。論文也觀察到 L_s 越大,生成的和聲進行與調性聽起來越連貫,但這個連貫感很可能只是演算法直接複製了訓練資料裡的長段落(即過擬合),論文雖提出未來可用 Lu 等人的 MelodySim 做抄襲偵測,但這篇報告本身完全沒有做這個檢測,所以「連貫性提升」與「原創性下降」孰輕孰重目前沒有數字佐證。

訓練資料也只涵蓋 MAESTRO 的鋼琴獨奏曲目,尚未驗證能否推廣到其他樂器或多聲部編制;這是一篇 3 頁的 IEEE 會議短篇論文,篇幅本身也限制了理論與實驗的完整度。

Beyond the two trend figures showing L_s move with per-tree training-song count and with the steps parameter, the paper reports no accuracy, perplexity, or listener-rating numbers, and runs no direct comparison against its closest prior work, LzMidi, or Dubnov and Assayag's universal-prediction method; evaluation is limited to pointing readers to audio examples on GitHub rather than a formal listening study.

Both training and generation operate on relative pitch intervals only -- velocity, note duration, and rests are not modeled at all, and the paper itself admits the output is consequently "rhythmically rigid" and sounds mechanically metronomic. The paper also observes that larger L_s produces more coherent-sounding chord progressions and tonality, but that coherence plausibly comes from the algorithm directly copying long training passages (overfitting); it proposes using Lu et al.'s MelodySim for plagiarism detection as future work but never runs this check itself, so there is no number weighing the coherence gain against the originality loss.

Training data is also limited to MAESTRO's solo piano repertoire, and generalization to other instruments or polyphonic ensembles is untested; this is a 3-page short IEEE conference paper, and its length constrains how thoroughly the theory and experiments are developed.

討論Discussion

相較於擴散模型、transformer 或 GAN,這個方法運算成本低、邏輯透明(整套演算法就是一棵頻率樹加機率抽樣),適合放進即時、低資源或需要可解釋性的場景,這是它真正的定位優勢,論文的兩個控制參數也確實有堅實的理論根據——Cover 與 Thomas 的 LZ 漸進片語數定理直接推導出 L_s 的公式,兩張圖顯示實測結果跟理論預期的方向一致。

但把「靠限制記憶長度控制複製與原創的平衡」講成一個全新貢獻,說服力有限:這個想法在風格模仿類的音樂生成研究裡早有前例(Dubnov 與 Assayag 的可變記憶馬可夫/後綴結構方法,以及同樣用 LZ 家族的 Ding 等人的 LzMidi),這篇論文真正新增的,窄化到「steps」這個具體參數與「用資料量控制集成樹平均序列長度」這個具體做法,而不是控制複製/隨機化平衡這個大方向本身。

考量到評估的單薄程度,這篇論文讀起來更像一篇驗證概念可行的技術短記,而非一套已被驗證好用的生成系統;它最扎實的部分是把兩個控制參數跟資訊理論公式對上,其餘結論(節奏僵硬、連貫性與抄襲的取捨)都停留在作者自己聽過之後的定性觀察。

Compared with diffusion, transformer, or GAN models, this method is computationally cheap and mechanistically transparent -- the whole algorithm is a frequency tree plus probabilistic sampling -- which is a genuine advantage for real-time, low-resource, or interpretability-sensitive settings, and the two control knobs do rest on solid theory: Cover and Thomas's asymptotic LZ phrase-count theorem directly yields the L_s formula, and both figures show the empirical trend matching the theoretical direction.

Framing "controlling the copy/originality balance by limiting memory length" as a wholly new contribution is less convincing, though: the idea has clear precedent in style-imitation music generation research (Dubnov and Assayag's variable-memory Markov/suffix-structure methods, and Ding et al.'s LzMidi, itself also LZ-family). What this paper actually adds narrows down to the specific "steps" parameter and the specific data-limited-ensemble technique for tuning L_s, not the broader idea of controlling copy-versus-randomization itself.

Given how thin the evaluation is, the paper reads more as a proof-of-concept technical note than a validated generation system; its most solid contribution is tying the two control knobs to an information-theoretic formula, while the remaining conclusions (rigid rhythm, the coherence-versus-plagiarism trade-off) stay at the level of the authors' own qualitative listening impressions.

Project Qualia: Recovering Experiential Music Structure from Session Co-occurrence Data

Project Qualia:從聆聽 Session 共現資料中回收「體驗式」音樂結構

Nizam Mohammed, Abu B. S. Rahman, Dimuthu D. K. Arachchige
arXiv:2609.10862 · cs.SD, cs.IR, cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-09-09
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這份報告(Project Qualia 第一階段)從 9,396 位重度 Last.fm 使用者收集了 12.92 億筆 scrobble,清理後留下 5.316 億筆訓練 scrobble、2,859 萬個聆聽 session,拿去訓練一個 skip-gram Word2Vec(Song2Vec)嵌入;跨藝人的原始嵌入平均餘弦相似度是 0.2487(顯示被藝人身分主導),扣掉每位藝人自己的嵌入中心點之後(殘差法)這個數字掉到 0.0005,但在 5 萬首取樣歌曲的最近鄰裡仍有 4,577 對跨藝人歌曲殘差餘弦相似度 ≥0.70(比隨機基準高約 8 個標準差),並形成 trip-hop、90 年代另類搖滾、2020 主流流行、跨作曲家古典鋼琴等可辨識的風格群集(蕭邦與巴赫的一對最高到 0.941);但論文自己強調這只是「訊號是否存在」的存在性檢定,沒有任何留出的量化評估,也完全無法從現有資料排除「自動播放」污染的可能性。Why this one: This first-stage Project Qualia report collected 1.292 billion scrobbles from 9,396 heavy Last.fm users, cleaned them down to 531.6 million training scrobbles across 28.6 million sessions, and trained a skip-gram Word2Vec embedding (Song2Vec); raw cross-artist cosine similarity averaged 0.2487 (dominated by artist identity), and after subtracting each artist's embedding centroid (the residual method) this dropped to 0.0005, yet among the ten nearest neighbors of 50,000 sampled tracks, 4,577 cross-artist pairs still retained residual cosine similarity >=0.70 (roughly 8 standard deviations above the random baseline), forming recognizable genre clusters -- trip-hop, 90s alternative rock, 2020 mainstream pop, cross-composer classical piano (the top Chopin-Bach pair reaching 0.941); the paper itself is careful to call this only an existence check for whether the signal is present, with no held-out quantitative validation and no way, from the data collected, to rule out autoplay contamination.

動機Motivation

現代音樂推薦系統——協同過濾、內容式音訊分析、深度學習、混合排序——工程上已經很成熟也相當準確,但一直有個共通的落差:推薦出來的是「統計上接下來很可能播放」的歌,而不是「跟聽者當下體驗一致」的歌,這個落差在好幾代系統演進後都沒有明顯縮小。作者從 LeCun 的立場論文《邁向自主機器智慧》出發,主張這個落差可能不是資料不夠,而是系統架構的問題:既有系統學的是類型、速度、藝人偏好、共同聆聽統計這些「體驗的代理指標」,而不是體驗本身的學習表徵。

Project Qualia 把這個想法當成可證偽的假說來測試:聆聽 session(聽者連續播放的一串歌曲)是否帶有一種既有的類型/藝人 metadata 都抓不到、但仍可從真實聆聽資料中回收的「體驗結構」?整個專案分階段推進,這份報告只處理第一階段——先確認這個訊號是否存在、能不能被回收出來——而不是去建構或評估後續要拿來用這個訊號做推薦的預測架構,那要留到假說通過驗證之後。

Modern music recommendation systems -- collaborative filtering, content-based audio analysis, deep learning, hybrid ranking -- are technically mature and largely accurate, yet a persistent gap remains: recommendations reflect what is statistically likely to play next, not what is consistent with the listener's current experience, and that gap has not visibly closed across successive generations of these systems. The authors take Yann LeCun's position paper "A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence" as their starting point, arguing the gap may reflect a limitation of system architecture rather than of available data: existing systems learn from genre, tempo, artist affinity, and co-listening statistics -- proxies for listening experience -- rather than a learned representation of the experience itself.

Project Qualia treats this as a falsifiable hypothesis: does a listening session (the sequence of songs a listener plays consecutively) carry an experiential structure that no existing genre or artist metadata captures, yet that is recoverable from real listening data? The project is staged, and this report covers only Stage 1 -- establishing whether the signal exists and can be recovered at all -- not building or evaluating the predictive architecture that would eventually use it, which is deferred until after the hypothesis survives this test.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

推薦系統的協同過濾/矩陣分解(Koren、Bell 與 Volinsky,2009)是這個領域最基礎的既有方法,作者認為這類方法的問題在於學的是「共同出現的統計相關性」而非體驗本身。至於公開的大規模 scrobble 語料,最有名的是 Schedl 的 Last.fm-1B(2016),但作者批評這份資料集已經過時,而且收集與前處理的決策細節完全不透明——這是這篇論文自己重新收集一份新資料集、而非直接沿用 Last.fm-1B 的主要理由。

比較需要點出來的是:這篇論文用來訓練嵌入的 Song2Vec,其實就是把 Word2Vec skip-gram 直接套用在「session 當句子、歌曲當詞」上,這個做法本身並不是新東西——Barkan 與 Koenigstein 2016 年的 Item2Vec 早就是同一個想法(把 Word2Vec 套用在協同過濾的物品序列上)。這篇論文真正新增的方法論貢獻,是後面的「藝人殘差分析」這個診斷步驟,以及借用 LeCun 的 JEPA/世界模型框架(I-JEPA、V-JEPA/V-JEPA2)規劃、但尚未實作的第 1 層架構。

論文也誠實地引用 Schedl 等人 2018 年討論的音樂推薦系統「循環污染」問題(平台自己的推薦邏輯會反過來污染訓練資料),並在第 5 節承認這個問題對本研究同樣適用。

The most basic existing method the authors position against is collaborative filtering / matrix factorization (Koren, Bell, and Volinsky, 2009), which the paper argues learns statistical co-occurrence rather than experience itself. For public large-scale scrobble corpora, the best known is Schedl's Last.fm-1B (2016), but the authors criticize it as outdated with no visibility into its collection or preprocessing decisions -- the stated reason for collecting a fresh dataset here rather than reusing LFM-1b.

Worth flagging directly: the Song2Vec embedding used here is literally skip-gram Word2Vec applied to "session as sentence, track as token," which is not itself new -- Barkan and Koenigstein's 2016 Item2Vec is the same idea (Word2Vec applied to item sequences for collaborative filtering). What this paper actually contributes methodologically is the artist-residual analysis that follows, plus a proposed, not-yet-built Layer 1 architecture borrowing LeCun's JEPA/world-model framework (I-JEPA, V-JEPA/V-JEPA2).

The paper is also candid about the recommender-system "circularity" problem discussed by Schedl et al. (2018) -- a platform's own recommendation logic contaminating the training data it later feeds -- and acknowledges in Section 5 that this concern applies here too.

方法Method

資料收集:透過 Last.fm 公開 API,鎖定「重度使用者」(多年聆聽紀錄、數萬到數十萬筆 scrobble),從 9,396 個帳號共收集 12.92 億筆 scrobble,存成約 222.7GB 的本地 SQLite 資料庫(以 zstd 壓縮到 59GB)。200 人小樣本前測發現三件事:字串比對的歌曲識別會因為大小寫、「ft.」/「feat.」、現場版等因素讓詞彙量膨脹約 4.6 倍(MBID 並非每筆 scrobble 都有);77.9% 的獨立曲目只被一位使用者播放過(長尾對共現模型完全沒用);用 30 分鐘間隔切分 session,中位數間隔約 207 秒、平均長度 23.9 首、53.2% 的 session 有 8 首以上。同時也抓到 12 個疑似機器人帳號與 BTS 生態圈佔樣本 5.8% scrobble 的集中問題。

轉成 188 個 ZSTD 壓縮的 Parquet 檔(共 15GB)後用 DuckDB 做全量分析,169 秒內掃完全部資料。全量統計:排除 2002 年前的時間戳後剩 12.53 億筆 scrobble、3,044 萬個字串曲目識別、656 萬個 MBID;用 MBID 去重只讓詞彙量減少 1.7%,顯示大部分字串膨脹發生在冷門、沒有 MBID 的曲目上——因此改用「至少 10 個不同使用者聽過」的門檻做詞彙縮減,能把詞彙量從 3,044 萬砍到 220 萬(縮減 93%),只犧牲 16.4% 的覆蓋率。

六階段清理管線:(1)30 分鐘間隔切 session,得到 53,735,946 個原始 session;(2)用「間隔均勻度」抓自動播放/機器人 session,移除 12,697 個、178.6 萬筆 scrobble;(3)比較四種詞彙門檻,選「中等」門檻(≥10 位使用者、≥25 次播放),保留 1,960,021/28,820,633 首曲目(6.80%),覆蓋 84.1% 的 scrobble;(4)收合連續重播,scrobble 數降到 979,448,409(減少 20.1%);(5)每位使用者同一首歌播放上限 30 次,再降到 591,679,449(減少 39.6%);(6)丟棄少於 5 首歌的 session,最終語料 2,859 萬個 session、5.316 億筆 scrobble(平均 18.6 首、中位數 11 首),詞彙量 196 萬首。

嵌入模型:用 Gensim 訓練帶負採樣的 skip-gram Word2Vec,256 維、上下文窗口 10、負樣本 15 個、5 個訓練週期,產出 196 萬×256 的嵌入矩陣。核心診斷實驗(藝人殘差分析):對詞彙庫裡至少有 5 首歌的藝人(52,445 位、涵蓋 90.7% 詞彙量),計算該藝人所有歌曲嵌入的平均值作為「藝人中心點」,把每首歌的嵌入減去該中心點並做 L2 正規化,得到的「殘差向量」若在不同藝人之間出現非隨機對齊,就代表嵌入空間裡存在藝人身分之外的訊號。

Data collection: via the public Last.fm API, the authors targeted "heavy users" (multi-year histories, tens to hundreds of thousands of scrobbles), collecting 1.292 billion scrobbles from 9,396 accounts into a ~222.7GB local SQLite database (compressed to 59GB with zstd). A 200-user pilot found string-based track identity inflates vocabulary by ~4.6x due to case, "ft."/"feat.", and live-version variants (MBIDs aren't on every scrobble); 77.9% of unique tracks were played by exactly one user; a 30-minute gap rule gave a median gap of ~207 seconds, mean session length 23.9 tracks, 53.2% of sessions with 8+ tracks. It also caught 12 suspected bot accounts and a BTS-driven concentration (5.8% of sample scrobbles).

After converting to 188 ZSTD-compressed Parquet files (15GB) and moving to DuckDB, full scans completed in 169 seconds. Full-scale totals: after excluding pre-2002 timestamps, 1.253 billion scrobbles, 30.44 million string track identities, 6.56 million MBIDs; MBID deduplication cut vocabulary by only 1.7%, so the paper instead used a "heard by >=10 distinct users" threshold, cutting vocabulary from 30.4 million to 2.2 million tracks (93% reduction) while sacrificing only 16.4% of scrobble coverage.

Six-stage cleaning pipeline: (1) 30-minute-gap sessionization yielding 53,735,946 raw sessions; (2) a gap-uniformity heuristic removing 12,697 automated-playback sessions (1,785,673 scrobbles); (3) choosing a "moderate" vocabulary threshold (>=10 users, >=25 plays) among four candidates, retaining 1,960,021 of 28,820,633 tracks (6.80%) covering 84.1% of scrobbles; (4) collapsing consecutive repeats, -20.1% scrobbles; (5) capping per-user per-track plays at 30, a further -39.6%; (6) discarding sessions under 5 tracks, leaving 28.59 million sessions and 531.6 million scrobbles (mean length 18.6, median 11), vocabulary 1.96 million tracks.

Embedding: a skip-gram Word2Vec model with negative sampling, trained via Gensim at 256 dimensions, window 10, 15 negative samples, 5 epochs, producing a 1.96M x 256 embedding matrix. The core diagnostic (artist-residual analysis): for every artist with >=5 tracks in vocabulary (52,445 artists, 90.7% of vocabulary), an "artist centroid" is the mean of that artist's track embeddings; each track's embedding minus its centroid, L2-normalized, gives a residual vector -- non-random alignment between different artists' residuals would indicate a signal beyond artist identity.

限制Limitations

作者自己在第 5 節講得很清楚:這個結果只回答一個窄但必要的問題——session 層級的共現資料是否帶有藝人身分之外的東西——回答是有,但這只是後續架構的必要條件,不是那套架構表現如何的證據。「體驗相似」這個詞本身也做了不少工作:殘差空間找到的群集確實符合類型與年代的一致性,但類型一致不等於體驗相似,群集也可能只是共享了製作年代、編制或速度這些跟類型高度相關但不完全相同的屬性,論文承認這超出目前分析範圍。

證據本身只有兩種:一個是門檻式的整體統計(4,577 對跨藝人歌曲餘弦相似度 ≥0.70,對照隨機基準 0.0005),另一個是對 20 首種子曲目做最近鄰檢視,但這 20 首是作者手選、非盲測也非系統抽樣,能否代表整個 4,577 對的集合是存疑的;論文完全沒有做任何留出的量化驗證(沒有人工判斷的相似度基準,也沒有下游任務的成功指標)。藝人殘差法本身也假設「藝人中心點」的線性平均能代表該藝人的典型位置,這個假設在 Frank Ocean、Taylor Swift、Kendrick Lamar 這幾位風格特別統一的藝人身上就不成立(最近鄰幾乎全落在自己的作品裡),論文承認無法分辨這是藝人的真實特性、還是中心點相減法本身的限制。

更根本的問題是自動播放污染:清理管線的「間隔均勻度」啟發式只能抓機器人式的機械均勻間隔,抓不到串流平台自己的自動接續播放,而 Last.fm API 用的方法根本不會回傳播放來源欄位,論文明白寫出「這個效應的量級無法從現有資料估計」。資料本身也只反映重度使用者這個特定母體,不能代表一般聽眾;BTS 一個生態圈就佔全量語料 5.7%,更廣的 K-pop(49 組藝人)佔 6.5%,由僅 356 位重度聽眾撐起,這種集中程度是否也發生在其他類型上完全沒有檢查。最後,Word2Vec 架構上不處理序列順序、也沒有內建區分「身分」與「感覺」的機制,更沒有對 session 整體結構建模——這正是論文提出改用 JEPA 式架構的理由,但第 6 節的五層架構完全沒有實作,也沒有任何結果可以評估。

The authors are explicit in Section 5: the result answers only a narrow but necessary question -- does session-level co-occurrence carry anything beyond artist identity -- and the answer is yes, but that is a precondition for the proposed architecture, not evidence of its performance. "Experiential similarity" is itself doing real work: the residual-space clusters are genre- and era-coherent, but genre coherence is not the same claim as experiential similarity, since clusters could equally form from shared production era, instrumentation, or tempo -- a question the paper admits is outside its current scope.

The evidence itself is only two things: an aggregate threshold statistic (4,577 cross-artist pairs at cosine >=0.70 against a 0.0005 random baseline) and a nearest-neighbor inspection of 20 seed tracks that were hand-picked rather than blind or systematically sampled, so how representative they are of the full 4,577-pair set is unclear; there is no held-out quantitative validation at all (no human-judged similarity benchmark, no downstream success metric). The artist-residual method also assumes an artist's linear-mean centroid represents their "typical" position, an assumption that fails for unusually cohesive catalogs like Frank Ocean, Taylor Swift, and Kendrick Lamar (whose nearest neighbors stay almost entirely within their own catalog); the paper cannot tell whether that is a real artist property or a limitation of centroid subtraction.

A more fundamental problem is autoplay contamination: the gap-uniformity heuristic only catches mechanically uniform bot-like timing, not a streaming platform's own autoplay continuation, and the Last.fm API method used does not return a playback-source field at all -- the paper states outright that "the magnitude of this effect cannot be determined from the present dataset." The data also reflects only the heavy-user population, not general listeners; BTS alone accounts for 5.7% of the full corpus and the broader K-pop category (49 acts) for 6.5%, driven by just 356 heavy listeners, and whether similar concentration exists for other genres was never checked. Finally, Word2Vec has no notion of sequence order, no built-in mechanism separating identity from feel, and no session-level structure model -- the stated reason for proposing a JEPA-style architecture instead, but the five-layer system in Section 6 is entirely unimplemented, with no results to evaluate.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最扎實的地方,是把一個聽起來合理但難以驗證的假說(「session 共現帶有藝人/類型之外的體驗結構」)變成一個有明確證偽條件、有具體門檻的實驗,而且實驗結果是正面的:殘差法讓跨藝人餘弦相似度從 0.2487 掉到 0.0005(幾乎完全去除藝人身分訊號),同時還能在對照基準之上找到 8 個標準差外的高相似度配對,這種「先講清楚什麼結果會推翻假說、再去看資料」的實驗設計本身值得肯定。

但用來訓練嵌入的技術(skip-gram/Item2Vec)完全是現成的,這裡沒有任何架構創新;真正的新意在方法論的「殘差相減」診斷步驟,以及規劃中但完全未驗證的 JEPA 路線圖。論文裡幾個具體案例也值得注意:跨作曲家的古典鋼琴配對(蕭邦—巴赫 0.941)之所以是比較有力的證據,是因為這類曲目在商業自動播放佇列裡幾乎不可能被排在一起,相對地,2020 主流流行的群集正好是自動播放污染跟真實相似度最難分辨的案例——論文自己誠實區分了哪些發現比較可信、哪些不行。

作為「五階段中的第一階段」報告,論文的結論刻意收得很窄:它只證成了值得投入資源去建 Layer 1,並沒有宣稱這套方法已經能改善推薦品質。

The paper's strongest contribution is turning a plausible-sounding but hard-to-verify hypothesis -- that session co-occurrence encodes experiential structure beyond artist or genre -- into a test with an explicit falsification criterion and concrete thresholds, and the test came back positive: the residual method drops cross-artist cosine similarity from 0.2487 to 0.0005 (near-total removal of the artist-identity signal), while still surfacing high-similarity pairs 8 standard deviations above that baseline. That discipline -- stating in advance what result would refute the hypothesis before looking at the data -- is worth crediting on its own.

But the embedding technique used (skip-gram/Item2Vec) is entirely off-the-shelf; there is no architectural novelty here. The real methodological contribution is the residual-subtraction diagnostic, plus a planned but entirely unvalidated JEPA roadmap. A few specific findings stand out: the cross-composer classical pairs (Chopin-Bach at 0.941) are relatively strong evidence precisely because such catalogs are unlikely to co-occur in a commercial autoplay queue, whereas the 2020 mainstream-pop cluster is exactly the case where autoplay contamination and genuine similarity are hardest to separate -- the paper is honest about which findings are more and less trustworthy.

As a "Stage 1 of 5" report, its conclusions are deliberately narrow: it justifies investing in building Layer 1, not a claim that this approach already improves recommendations.

Learned Continuous Synthesis of Quadratic Difference Tone Spectra

用神經網路學出連續可控的二次差音頻譜(QDTS)合成法

Esteban Gutiérrez, Behzad Haki, Christopher Haworth, Xavier Serra, Rodrigo F. Cádiz

為什麼挑這篇：二次差音(QDT)是耳蝸非線性產生的「幻音」,聲譜儀上量不到但聽者確實聽得到;QDTS 合成法用一組真實正弦波(carrier)去誘發出目標頻譜的幻音,但求解「目標頻譜對應到哪組 carrier」這個逆問題在 5 個以上諧波時沒有解析解,前作 Gutiérrez 等人(2023、2024)的隨機 Newton-Raphson 解法雖能處理任意諧波數,卻會在目標頻譜隨時間變化時,因為逆問題有多組解而在不同分支間跳動,產生聽得到的不連續雜訊。這篇論文把逆問題重新框成一個自編碼器——已知的失真函數 D 當成固定不可訓練的解碼器,學一個小型 MLP 當編碼器,並用 S(t) = sqrt(||t||)·s(t/||t||) 這個結構直接保證對應的 2 次齊次縮放關係,連續性因此是架構保證而非事後修補;在 N=5 到 16 諧波的重建誤差上,神經網路解法平均誤差都低於 0.04(仍高於 Newton-Raphson 解法,但作者認為在感知上可接受),線性內插響應幾乎貼著理想對角線,而且在 Apple M4 Pro 上每次推論都低於 0.25 毫秒,遠比 Newton-Raphson 解法的高變異運算時間穩定。Why this one: Quadratic difference tones (QDTs) are cochlear-nonlinearity "phantom" tones, inaudible to a spectrogram but clearly audible to listeners; QDTS synthesis drives a carrier complex of real sinusoids to evoke a target phantom spectrum, but solving the inverse problem -- which carrier produces a given target -- has no closed form beyond 4 harmonics, and the prior stochastic Newton-Raphson solver of Gutierrez et al. (2023, 2024), while handling arbitrary harmonic counts, jumps between different valid solution branches as the target spectrum evolves in time, producing audible discontinuities. This paper reframes the inverse problem as an autoencoder -- the known distortion function D as a fixed, non-trainable decoder, with a small learned MLP as the encoder -- and forces the required degree-2 homogeneity into the architecture itself via S(t) = sqrt(||t||)*s(t/||t||), so continuity is guaranteed by construction rather than patched after the fact; across N=5 to 16 harmonics the neural solver's mean reconstruction error stays below 0.04 (higher than Newton-Raphson, but judged perceptually acceptable), its response to linear interpolation tracks the ideal diagonal closely, and on an Apple M4 Pro it runs under 0.25ms per call, far more stable than the Newton-Raphson baseline's high-variance timing.

動機Motivation

二次差音(QDT)是聽覺失真產物(ADP)的一種——一種只存在於聽者主觀感知、聲譜分析裡完全看不到的「幻音」,成因是耳蝸外毛細胞與基底膜交互作用產生的非線性,健康聽力的常見特徵,也是聽力檢測的常用工具。QDT 從小提琴家 Tartini 於 1754 年發現起就有豐富的音樂應用史,從即興演奏家 John Butcher、Evan Parker 的瞬態「鬼音」,到 Maryanne Amacher、Phill Niblock 等人的實驗噪動音樂(drone)。

單純用兩個正弦波誘發 QDT 需要相當高的音壓,對多數聽者來說不舒服。Haworth(2011)提出用一組多音複合體(carrier complex)取代雙音刺激,讓每對相鄰正弦波都產生同一個 QDT,藉此提高失真增益並降低單一音量;Kendall 等人(2014)進一步把這個做法形式化為 QDTS(二次差音頻譜)合成模型。真正的合成需求是逆向問題:給定想要的目標頻譜,要用哪組 carrier 才能誘發出來?這在諧波數超過 4 時沒有解析解,前作 Gutiérrez 等人(2023、2024)雖然用隨機 Newton-Raphson 解法解決了任意諧波數的問題,卻因為逆問題本身有多組解,導致目標頻譜隨時間變化時,解法在不同分支間跳動、產生聽得見的不連續雜訊,把應用範圍侷限在近乎靜態的聲音。這篇論文要解決的正是這個連續性問題。

Quadratic difference tones (QDTs) are a type of auditory distortion product (ADP) -- phantom tones audible only subjectively, invisible to spectrogram analysis, arising from cochlear nonlinearity in the outer hair cells and basilar membrane, a marker of healthy hearing and a common diagnostic tool. QDTs have a rich musical history since violinist Tartini's 1754 discovery, from the transient "ghost tones" of improvisers John Butcher and Evan Parker to the drone works of Maryanne Amacher and Phill Niblock.

Evoking a QDT with just two sinusoids requires uncomfortably high sound pressure. Haworth (2011) proposed using a complex of many tones instead, so each adjacent pair produces the same QDT, boosting distortion gain while lowering individual tone levels; Kendall et al. (2014) formalized this into the QDTS (Quadratic Difference Tone Spectrum) synthesis model. Actual synthesis requires the inverse problem: given a desired target spectrum, which carrier produces it? This has no closed form beyond 4 harmonics, and while the prior stochastic Newton-Raphson solver of Gutierrez et al. (2023, 2024) handled arbitrary harmonic counts, the inverse problem's multiple solutions meant it jumped between solution branches as the target spectrum varied in time, producing audible discontinuities that confined prior tools to near-static sounds. Fixing this continuity problem is exactly what this paper sets out to do.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景章節明確點名了三個具體前作,而不是只轉述自己的分類:Kendall、Haworth 與 Cádiz(2014,Computer Music Journal)提出的原始符號解法,受限於諧波數 N≤4 才有解析解;Gutiérrez、Haworth 與 Cádiz(2023 ICMC、2024 Computer Music Journal)用隨機 Newton-Raphson 數值解法把上限解除到任意 N,但代價是每個時間點各自獨立選解、沒有跨時間一致性的機制;Haworth(2011,ICMC)則是提出多音複合體這個基礎技巧本身(建立在 Pressnitzer 與 Patterson 2001 的心理聲學研究之上)。

這篇論文跟 Gutiérrez 等人的關係是直接的技術延續而非全新方向:同一個失真函數 D、同一個逆問題,差別在於把「用隨機重啟的 Newton-Raphson 找根」換成「訓練一個確定性的神經網路直接學逆映射」,並且把失真函數本身當成自編碼器裡固定、已知、不需訓練的解碼器——這個「已知解析函數當固定解碼器、只學逆映射」的自編碼器框架,是 Kendall 或 Gutiérrez 的論文裡都沒有用過的設計選擇,也是這篇論文相對於前作最明確的差異所在。

The background section names three concrete prior systems rather than just restating its own taxonomy: Kendall, Haworth, and Cadiz's (2014, Computer Music Journal) original symbolic solution, limited to closed-form solutions at N<=4 harmonics; Gutierrez, Haworth, and Cadiz's (2023 ICMC, 2024 Computer Music Journal) stochastic Newton-Raphson numerical solver, which lifted that cap to arbitrary N at the cost of choosing an independent solution at each time step with no cross-time consistency; and Haworth (2011, ICMC), who introduced the multi-tone carrier complex itself, building on Pressnitzer and Patterson's 2001 psychoacoustics.

This paper's relationship to Gutierrez et al. is a direct technical continuation rather than a new direction: same distortion function D, same inverse problem, but swapping the stochastic restart-based Newton-Raphson root-finder for a deterministic neural network that learns the inverse mapping directly, framing the analytically known distortion function itself as a fixed, non-trainable decoder inside an autoencoder. That "known analytic function as a frozen decoder, learn only the inverse encoder" framing is a design choice neither Kendall's nor Gutierrez's papers used, and is the clearest point of difference from prior work.

方法Method

QDTS 模型:carrier 複合體是 N+1 個正弦波,頻率為 C, C+F, ..., C+NF;目標頻譜是 N 個諧波,頻率為 F, 2F, ..., NF。失真函數 D 把 carrier 振幅 (a0,...,aN) 映射到目標振幅 (t1,...,tN),每個 t_k 是特定 a_i·a_j 乘積項的和,且 D 對輸入是 2 次齊次:D(λa) = λ²D(a)。因此若 S 是 D 的左逆,S 必須滿足 S(λt) = sqrt(λ)·S(t)。

這篇論文的核心設計,是把這個縮放關係直接刻進網路架構,而不是靠訓練去逼近:S_θ(t) = sqrt(‖t‖)·s_θ(t/‖t‖),讓小型神經網路 s_θ 只需要學習單位球面上的「方向」部分,振幅縮放交給解析的 sqrt 函數處理,這個構造同時保證了齊次縮放關係與連續性。實測發現三層 MLP 效果最好。訓練把整個逆問題框成自編碼器:失真函數 D 是固定、已知、不參與訓練的「解碼器」,S_θ 是要學的「編碼器」,損失函數是重建誤差 ‖D(S_θ(t)) − t‖²,不需要任何「正確答案」carrier 的標籤。

由於逆問題在某些區域有多組解,訓練採用課程式策略:從中心 (0.5,...,0.5)、半徑 0.1 的小球開始取樣(每步 10,000 批、每批 512 個目標向量),半徑每次增加 0.1 一路加到 1.0,讓模型先學到一個局部一致的逆解、再逐步擴展到整個定義域。針對每個 N∈{5,...,16},各跑 8 次不同初始化、再加 8 次搭配 a0≈t1 正則項(沿用 Gutiérrez 等人的做法)的訓練,總共 16 個模型版本;最終匯出成 ONNX,透過自製 Max 外掛 qdts.solver_nn 即時運行,讓作曲家可以互動調整目標振幅分布。

The QDTS model: the carrier complex is N+1 sinusoids at C, C+F, ..., C+NF; the target spectrum is N harmonics at F, 2F, ..., NF. The distortion function D maps carrier amplitudes (a0,...,aN) to target amplitudes (t1,...,tN), each t_k a sum of specific a_i*a_j products, and D is homogeneous of degree 2: D(lambda*a) = lambda^2*D(a). A left inverse S must therefore satisfy S(lambda*t) = sqrt(lambda)*S(t).

The paper's central design choice is baking that scaling relation directly into the architecture rather than hoping training approximates it: S_theta(t) = sqrt(||t||)*s_theta(t/||t||), so the small network s_theta only has to learn the "direction" on the unit sphere, with magnitude handled analytically by the sqrt term -- this construction guarantees both the homogeneity relation and continuity by design. A three-layer MLP worked best empirically. Training frames the whole inverse problem as an autoencoder: the distortion function D is a fixed, known, non-trainable "decoder," S_theta the learned "encoder," and the loss is the reconstruction error ||D(S_theta(t)) - t||^2, requiring no ground-truth carrier labels at all.

Because the inverse problem admits multiple solutions in places, training uses a curriculum: sampling from a small sphere (radius 0.1 around center (0.5,...,0.5), 10,000 batches of 512 target vectors) and growing the radius by 0.1 up to 1.0, so the model first learns one locally consistent inverse before extending to the full domain. For each N in {5,...,16}, 8 runs with different initializations plus 8 more with an a0=t1 regularizer (following Gutierrez et al.) are trained, 16 model variants total; the result is exported to ONNX and run in real time through a custom Max external, qdts.solver_nn, letting composers interactively adjust the target amplitude distribution.

限制Limitations

神經網路解法在每一個 N 值上的重建誤差都高於 Newton-Raphson 基準(圖 4),平均誤差低於 0.04 被作者稱為「感知上可接受」,但這個門檻是作者自己的判斷,論文並沒有做獨立的聽感實驗去驗證 0.04 這個誤差量級實際上聽不聽得出來。訓練用的課程式取樣策略也只是經驗法則,論文自己承認「沒有理論上的保證」,連續性雖然由架構保證,但收斂到哪一個局部解分支、是否每次都是「好」的那個分支,同樣沒有形式化的證明。

這個模型只處理 carrier 的振幅關係,相位完全沒有被學習或納入模型——但論文自己在第 5.4 節展示,固定振幅、只改變相位配置,就能讓 QDTS 的感知強度從「加強」變成「抑制」,代表決定幻音聽不聽得到的一部分因素目前只能靠 Max 介面手動調整,還沒被學進模型。此外每個模型只支援固定的 N∈{5,...,16},16 個版本各自獨立訓練,無法在不同諧波數之間連續內插或推廣;論文提供的聲音範例都是作者自己的示範,沒有做任何盲測或 A/B 聽感比較去驗證「音樂實用性」的宣稱,而且這整套 QDTS 框架限定在二次差音,論文自己在第 7 節承認立方差音(感知上更豐富、也是臨床耳聲傳射檢測的主要訊號)因為比值相關的非單調性,無法直接套用同一套方法,仍是未解問題。

The neural solver's reconstruction error is higher than the Newton-Raphson baseline at every tested N (Figure 4); a mean error below 0.04 is called "perceptually acceptable," but that threshold is the authors' own judgment call -- no independent listening study is run to confirm that an error of this magnitude is actually inaudible. The curriculum sampling strategy is also purely empirical, and the paper itself states there is "no theoretical guarantee"; continuity is architecturally guaranteed, but which local solution branch training converges to, and whether it is always the "good" one, is not formally proven either.

The model only handles the carrier's amplitude relationship -- phase is neither learned nor part of the model at all -- yet the paper's own Section 5.4 shows that holding magnitude fixed and only changing phase configuration flips the QDTS between perceptually enhanced and suppressed, meaning part of what actually determines audibility is currently exposed only for manual tweaking in the Max interface, not learned. Each model also only supports a fixed N in {5,...,16}, with the 16 variants trained independently and no continuous interpolation or generalization across harmonic counts; the sound examples are the authors' own demonstrations, with no blind test or A/B listening comparison to back the claimed "musical usefulness," and the whole framework is confined to quadratic difference tones -- Section 7 concedes that cubic difference tones, perceptually richer and the dominant signal in clinical otoacoustic-emission testing, cannot be directly ported to the same method because of the non-monotonic ratio-dependence of the cubic distortion, and remain unsolved.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最核心的技術動作——把逆網路寫成 S_θ(t) = sqrt(‖t‖)·s_θ(t/‖t‖),把 2 次齊次縮放(因此也是連續性)直接刻進架構,而不是指望訓練自己逼近出來——是一個目標明確、直接對症下藥的設計:它針對的正是 Gutiérrez 等人 Newton-Raphson 解法之所以受限的那個具體失效模式(不同解分支之間跳動造成的不連續)。因為失真函數 D 本身是已知且固定的解析函數,把它當成凍結的「解碼器」、只訓練一個小型「編碼器」,本質上是把一個逆問題求解器改寫成不需要標籤的自監督學習任務——想法一旦框架對了其實相當簡潔。

對作曲家而言,這是實際可用的升級:每次推論低於 0.25 毫秒、可以即時整合進 Max、參數響應接近線性,換來的是連續音色漸變、即時重合成這些「前作做不到」的能力,代價是比不即時安全的 Newton-Raphson 基準略高的重建誤差。論文的宣稱也拿捏得恰當——作者從未主張這個方法比前作更準,只主張用可界定的精度損失去換取連續性與速度,而且明確承認相位控制、立方差音延伸、重建精度都還是開放問題,這種宣稱幅度跟論文實際證明的東西相當貼合,在同批論文裡算是相對誠實克制的一篇。

The paper's central technical move -- writing the inverse network as S_theta(t) = sqrt(||t||)*s_theta(t/||t||) to bake degree-2 homogeneity (and hence continuity) directly into the architecture rather than hoping training approximates it -- is well targeted: it addresses exactly the specific failure mode (jumping between solution branches) that limited the Gutierrez et al. Newton-Raphson approach. Because the distortion function D is analytically known and fixed, treating it as a frozen "decoder" and training only a small "encoder" against it effectively turns an inverse-problem solver into a label-free self-supervised task -- a simple idea once framed correctly.

For composers, this is a genuine practical upgrade: sub-0.25ms inference, real-time Max integration, near-linear parameter response, buying continuous timbral morphing and live resynthesis that prior solvers could not do, at the cost of somewhat higher reconstruction error than the non-real-time-safe Newton-Raphson baseline. The paper's claims are also appropriately scoped -- the authors never claim their method is more accurate than prior work, only that it trades a bounded accuracy loss for continuity and speed, and they explicitly flag phase control, the cubic extension, and reconstruction accuracy as open. That match between claim strength and demonstrated result makes this one of the more restrained, honest papers in this batch.
