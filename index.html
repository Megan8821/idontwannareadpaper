Evaluation Platform and Benchmarks for Music Source Separation

MVSep Quality Checker：一個收上傳分離結果、在伺服器端用私有參考音軌打分的音源分離公開排行榜，附 Multisong、樂器專用與人聲專用測試集，以及把 mel-dB 差值拆成正負兩半的 Bleedless／Fullness 指標

Roman Solovyev, Tatiana Habruseva, Jeremy Bonnot, Alexander Stempkovskiy, Ferdinand van Wijnen
DOI:10.5334/tismir.366 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-11-04
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：社群裡被大量引用的 MVSep 排行榜終於有了正式描述：100 段一分鐘的 Multisong 測試集（參考音軌不公開）、吉他／鋼琴／弦樂／管樂／DrumSep 等樂器專用集，以及兩個新指標 Bleedless 與 Fullness。選它是因為現在很多分離模型（尤其是 Hugging Face 上的 Roformer 微調版）主要在這個平台上報數字，讀者需要知道那些數字怎麼來的。最有用的結果在聽測：人聲上 SDR 與整體偏好的相關 ρ=0.68，伴奏上卻是 −0.03；Fullness 和「哪個比較飽滿」的相關則在人聲 0.84、伴奏 0.71。但這只是 60 組配對、485 票，而且 48 位投票者裡有 5 位貢獻了大部分票數。Why this one: The MVSep leaderboard, widely cited in the community, finally gets a formal write-up: a 100-excerpt, one-minute Multisong test set with private reference stems, instrument-specific sets for guitar, piano, bowed strings, wind and DrumSep, and two new metrics, Bleedless and Fullness. I picked it because many separation models today, especially the Roformer fine-tunes on Hugging Face, report numbers mainly on this platform, so readers need to know where those numbers come from. The most useful result is the listening test: on vocals SDR correlates with overall preference at ρ = 0.68, but on the instrumental stem at −0.03; Fullness correlates with which stem sounds fuller at 0.84 for vocals and 0.71 for instrumental. That rests on only 60 pairs and 485 votes, though, and 5 of the 48 voters cast most of them.

動機Motivation

音源分離的評估很分散：各篇論文用不同資料集、不同 SDR 實作、不同模型版本報數字，彼此難以直接比較。標準基準幾乎都停在四軌（人聲、貝斯、鼓、其他），吉他、鋼琴、管樂、主唱／和聲、男聲／女聲這類細分任務沒有共同測試集；MUSDB18 的測試集只有 50 首，又被反覆使用多年，難以判斷模型是否只是熟悉這批素材。

作者的回應是一個長期開放的平台：使用者下載混音、在本地跑分離、把結果上傳，伺服器端用不公開的參考音軌計分並更新排行榜。論文同時想解決另一個問題：單一 SDR 無法區分「漏進別的聲部」和「目標聲部被削掉」兩種錯誤，而社群裡調模型的人恰恰最在意這兩者的取捨，因此提出 Bleedless 與 Fullness。

Source separation evaluation is fragmented: papers report numbers on different datasets, with different SDR implementations and model versions, which makes direct comparison hard. Standard benchmarks almost all stop at four stems (vocals, bass, drums, other), so fine-grained tasks such as guitar, piano, wind, lead versus backing vocals and male versus female vocals have no shared test set; the MUSDB18 test set has only 50 songs and has been reused for years, making it hard to tell whether models are just familiar with that material.

The authors respond with a long-running open platform: users download mixtures, run separation locally, upload the results, and the server scores them against private reference stems and updates the leaderboard. The paper also targets a second problem: a single SDR cannot tell leakage from other sources apart from missing target content, yet that trade-off is exactly what practitioners tuning models care about most, hence Bleedless and Fullness.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最接近的前身是 SiSEC MUS 與 AIcrowd 上的 MDX21／SDX23 挑戰賽：它們同樣用隱藏測試集與伺服器端計分，SDR 公式也是這篇直接沿用的版本。差別在於挑戰賽有截止日、限制提交次數、要求提交程式碼或模型，這個平台則是常年開放、只收分離後的音訊，所以門檻低，但也拿不到模型細節。資料方面，MUSDB18（Rafii 等）是四軌標準，MedleyVox（Jeon 等，2023）是主唱與其他人聲分離的前例；論文對後者寫了一段具體比較：MedleyVox 只有人聲音軌，這裡直接提供含伴奏的完整混音。

Bleedless／Fullness 最像的既有方法是 BSS Eval（Vincent 等，2006）把誤差拆成干擾（SIR）與人工雜訊（SAR）的做法。差別在於 BSS Eval 需要所有聲部的參考訊號做投影，這裡只比對目標聲部的 mel-dB 頻譜，正差值算「多出來的」、負差值算「不見的」。代價是漏音和模型自己產生的雜訊都落在正差值，論文的說明也承認這一點（bleed、artifacts 並列）。

The closest predecessors are SiSEC MUS and the MDX21/SDX23 challenges on AIcrowd: they also used hidden test sets with server-side scoring, and the SDR formula here is taken directly from them. The difference is that challenges have deadlines, cap submissions and require code or models, whereas this platform is permanently open and accepts only separated audio, which lowers the barrier but yields no model details. On the data side, MUSDB18 (Rafii et al.) is the four-stem standard and MedleyVox (Jeon et al., 2023) is the precedent for separating a main vocal from other vocals; the paper gives a concrete comparison with the latter: MedleyVox contains vocal stems only, while this set provides full mixtures with accompaniment.

The closest existing method to Bleedless/Fullness is the BSS Eval (Vincent et al., 2006) split of error into interference (SIR) and artifacts (SAR). The difference is that BSS Eval projects onto references for all sources, while this compares only the target stem's mel-dB spectrum, counting positive differences as added content and negative ones as missing content. The cost is that leakage and the model's own artifacts both land in the positive side, which the paper's description itself concedes by listing bleed and artifacts together.

方法Method

Multisong MVSep 由 100 段一分鐘片段組成，取自 Epidemic Sound 曲庫，由一位職業音樂人依「人聲與伴奏音軌間無可察覺的串音」和「挑最熱鬧段落」兩個原則手選，涵蓋 40 多種曲風、1960 到 2020 年代，48 kHz 轉 44.1 kHz／16-bit，每首以混音峰值正規化。樂器專用集都是約一分鐘的片段：吉他 30 首、鋼琴 50 首、弦樂 30 首、管樂 35 首；DrumSep 有 150 段短曲，用 Superior Drummer 與鼓機取樣製作，因此各鼓件之間沒有收音串音；主唱／和聲 48 首、每首 40 秒；男聲／女聲 25 首。

Bleedless／Fullness 的算法：對參考與估計訊號做 STFT、套 mel 濾波器、轉 dB 並限制在 80 dB 動態範圍，逐格相減得 Δ。正值格的平均為 Δ̄+、負值格的平均為 Δ̄−，Bleedless = 100/(Δ̄+ + 1)、Fullness = 100/(−Δ̄− + 1)，兩者都是越高越好、上限 100。平台另外報 SDR、SI-SDR、L1Freq、LogWMSE、AuraSTFT、AuraMRSTFT。

Multisong MVSep consists of 100 one-minute excerpts from the Epidemic Sound catalogue, hand-picked by a professional musician under two rules: no perceptible leakage between vocal and instrumental stems, and the most active sections preferred. It spans over 40 genres from the 1960s to the 2020s, is converted from 48 kHz to 44.1 kHz/16-bit, and each track is normalised by the mixture peak. The instrument sets are roughly one-minute excerpts: 30 tracks for guitar, 50 for piano, 30 for bowed strings and 35 for wind; DrumSep has 150 short tracks built with Superior Drummer and drum-machine samples, so there is no microphone bleed between kit pieces; lead/back vocals has 48 tracks of 40 seconds each, and male/female has 25.

Bleedless/Fullness work as follows: take the STFT of reference and estimate, apply a mel filterbank, convert to dB clipped to an 80 dB range, and subtract bin by bin to get Δ. The mean over positive bins is Δ̄+ and over negative bins Δ̄−; Bleedless = 100/(Δ̄+ + 1) and Fullness = 100/(−Δ̄− + 1), both higher-is-better with a ceiling of 100. The platform also reports SDR, SI-SDR, L1Freq, LogWMSE, AuraSTFT and AuraMRSTFT.

限制Limitations

指標本身有幾個從公式就看得出的問題，論文沒有討論。第一，它只取正值格（或負值格）的平均，不管這類格子占多少比例，所以「少數格子多很多」和「大量格子多一點」可能得到同樣分數。第二，它不是尺度不變的：論文沒有提到計分前做增益對齊，若估計整體大 1 dB，幾乎每格都變正差值，Fullness 趨近 100、Bleedless 約掉到 50，也就是單純把輸出調大聲就能換到 Fullness。這正好可能和 Table 9 裡「偏重飽滿」的模型糾纏在一起。

聽測規模小：每種音軌 30 組配對、485 票，許多票是平手，48 位投票者中 5 位貢獻多數票，作者自己也說樣本不獨立。Multisong 對 MUSDB18 的比較寫著平均 SDR「一致較低」，但同一張表裡 SCNet Masked Small 是 8.81 對 8.86，Multisong 反而略高；多數模型的差距也只有 0.1 到 0.3 dB。平台由作者營運、排行榜上標著「by MVSep」的模型也是他們的，公開排行榜是否限制重複提交，論文沒有說明。

The metrics have problems visible from the formula alone that the paper does not discuss. First, they average only over positive (or negative) bins regardless of what fraction of bins that is, so a few bins with a lot added and many bins with a little added can score the same. Second, they are not scale-invariant: the paper mentions no gain alignment before scoring, and if the estimate is 1 dB louder overall nearly every bin becomes positive, so Fullness approaches 100 and Bleedless drops to about 50; simply turning the output up buys Fullness. That may well be entangled with the fullness-tuned models in Table 9.

The listening test is small: 30 pairs per stem and 485 votes, many of them ties, with 5 of 48 voters casting most votes, which the authors themselves say makes the samples non-independent. The Multisong-versus-MUSDB18 comparison says average SDR is consistently lower, yet in the same table SCNet Masked Small scores 8.81 versus 8.86, slightly higher on Multisong, and most gaps are only 0.1 to 0.3 dB. The platform is run by the authors and the models labelled by MVSep on the leaderboards are theirs; the paper does not say whether repeated submissions to the public leaderboards are limited.

討論Discussion

Table 9 是這篇最有說服力的一張表：SDR 相近的模型在兩個新指標上差很多。例如 MVSep SCNet XL 與其高飽滿版本，人聲 SDR 是 10.96 對 10.40，Fullness 卻是 17.96 對 23.50；偏伴奏調校的 Unwa v1e，伴奏 Fullness 38.85 遠高於 Kimberley Jensen 版的 27.71，Bleedless 則從 46.72 掉到 35.68。這和聽測裡伴奏 Bleedless 與「哪個比較飽滿」呈 −0.63 相關一致：兩個指標確實在量一組取捨。

比較值得帶走的反而是 SDR 的那一列：伴奏音軌上 SDR 與整體偏好、與「哪個雜訊少」的相關都接近零（−0.03、+0.02），代表在伴奏上用 SDR 選模型可能跟聽感無關。但要把 Bleedless／Fullness 當成標準指標，至少得先處理尺度問題（例如先做 SI-SDR 那樣的最佳增益對齊），再做作者自己規劃的 MUSHRA 測試。在那之前，比較合理的定位是：它們是描述模型風格的診斷量，不是品質分數。

Table 9 is the paper's most convincing table: models with similar SDR differ a lot on the two new metrics. MVSep SCNet XL and its high-fullness variant, for example, score 10.96 versus 10.40 vocal SDR but 17.96 versus 23.50 Fullness; the instrumental-tuned Unwa v1e reaches 38.85 instrumental Fullness against 27.71 for the Kimberley Jensen model, while Bleedless falls from 46.72 to 35.68. This matches the listening test, where instrumental Bleedless correlates at −0.63 with which stem sounds fuller: the two metrics really do measure a trade-off.

The more important takeaway is actually the SDR row: on the instrumental stem SDR correlates near zero with overall preference and with fewer artifacts (−0.03 and +0.02), meaning that choosing instrumental models by SDR may have nothing to do with how they sound. Before Bleedless/Fullness can serve as standard metrics, though, the scale issue needs handling (for instance an optimal gain alignment as in SI-SDR), followed by the MUSHRA test the authors themselves plan. Until then they are better read as diagnostics of a model's style than as quality scores.

轉譜與音高分析Transcription & Pitch2026-11-09

Semi-Automatic Pipeline for the Transcription of Mensural Polyphony into Symbolic Interpreted Scores

把 MuRET 光學樂譜辨識、MP Editor 內的定量記譜節奏解讀（scoring-up）專家系統，與 humlib 文藝復興不協和音過濾器串成一條流程，把分聲部抄寫的定量記譜複音手稿轉成對齊的 Mensural MEI 總譜，並以瓜地馬拉合唱書 GCA-Gaha 1 為案例

Martha E. Thomae
DOI:10.5334/tismir.292 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-07-15
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：中世紀晚期到文藝復興的複音音樂以定量記譜（mensural notation）寫成，而且各聲部分開抄寫；音符時值不只看形狀，還取決於拍制記號與上下文（不完全化、加倍），所以光是辨識符號，還得不到可以對齊的總譜。這篇把三個既有工具串起來：MuRET 做光學樂譜辨識（OMR），MP Editor 裡的 scoring-up 專家系統解讀時值並對齊聲部，humlib 的不協和音過濾器把對位上說不通的地方標出來，幫人找出抄寫或辨識錯誤。選它是因為它處理的是 MIR 少見、但對音樂學很實際的轉譜問題。要先說清楚的是，這是一篇系統整合與經驗報告，全文沒有在 GCA-Gaha 1 上報告任何辨識錯誤率、曲目數或工時；唯一的量化說法「校正時間減半」引自作者 2022 年的 ISMIR 論文。Why this one: Polyphony from the late Middle Ages and Renaissance is written in mensural notation, with each voice copied separately; a note's duration depends not only on its shape but on the mensuration sign and context (imperfection, alteration), so recognising symbols alone does not yield an alignable score. This paper chains three existing tools: MuRET for optical music recognition (OMR), the scoring-up expert system inside MP Editor to interpret durations and align the voices, and humlib's dissonance filter to flag contrapuntally implausible spots and help locate scribal or recognition errors. I picked it because it tackles a transcription problem rare in MIR but very practical for musicology. To be clear up front, this is a systems-integration and experience report: it reports no recognition error rate, piece count or labour time on GCA-Gaha 1, and its only quantitative claim, halved correction time, is cited from the author's 2022 ISMIR paper.

動機Motivation

定量記譜有三層拍制：長音符對短音符（modus）、短音符對半短音符（tempus）、半短音符對最短音符（prolatio），每層可以是三分（完全）或二分（不完全）。在三分拍制裡，同一個音符形狀可能因上下文被「不完全化」（三拍變兩拍）或「加倍」（alteration），還有塗色、分割點與附加點（兩者外觀相同、要靠上下文判斷）等規則。再加上各聲部分開抄寫在跨頁的四個角落或不同分譜，讀者看不到垂直的和聲關係，必須先把時值解讀出來才能排成總譜。

現有 OMR 系統能把符號辨識出來、輸出 MEI，卻停在「每個聲部一串符號」，沒有做時值解讀與對齊。作者的另一個動機是資源：這份合唱書收藏在缺乏數位化設備的機構，所以整條流程刻意只用免費、開放的工具，並以重用既有工具為原則，而不是另寫一套一體化軟體。

Mensural notation has three levels of mensuration: longa to brevis (modus), brevis to semibrevis (tempus), and semibrevis to minima (prolatio), each either ternary (perfect) or binary (imperfect). In ternary mensurations the same note shape can be imperfected (three units become two) or altered (doubled) by context, and further rules govern coloration and dots of division versus dots of addition, which look identical and must be told apart from context. On top of that, the voices are copied separately in the four quadrants of an opening or in separate partbooks, so the reader cannot see the vertical sonorities; durations must be interpreted before the voices can be scored up.

Existing OMR systems can recognise the symbols and export MEI, but stop at one symbol string per voice, without duration interpretation or alignment. The author's other motivation is resources: the choirbook is held at an institution lacking digitisation infrastructure, so the pipeline deliberately uses only free, open tools and favours reusing existing ones over writing a new monolithic application.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名了幾個前例。Huang 等（ISMIR 2015）做過從手寫定量記譜到 MIDI 的系統，但只處理二分拍制，也就是沒有上下文依賴的簡單情況；Rizo 等（2017）提出可從音樂學者修正中學習的定量轉現代記譜轉換器，但沒有報告效能；MeRIT（Plaksin 與 Lewis，2022）則依 Tinctoris 的理論寫成瀏覽器端的節奏解讀工具。OMR 端的替代方案有只支援印刷譜、已不支援新版 macOS 的 Aruspix，以及主要為紐姆記譜設計的 SIMSSA 流程。

要注意「第一條半自動流程」這個說法的範圍。流程中最核心的兩塊都是作者自己先前發表過的：scoring-up 工具（Thomae 等，2019）與在 MP Editor 中加入不協和音標示並量測校正時間（Thomae 等，ISMIR 2022）；MEI 到 mens 再到 kern 的轉換流程也已見於 Desmond 等（2021）。這篇新增的是把 MuRET 的輸出接進 MP Editor，以及為此擴充 mens 格式與 humlib 的轉換工具，所以它的貢獻主要在整合，而不在新演算法。

The paper names several precedents. Huang et al. (ISMIR 2015) built a system from handwritten mensural notation to MIDI, but only for binary mensurations, the case without context dependence; Rizo et al. (2017) proposed a mensural-to-modern transducer meant to learn from musicologists' corrections, but reported no performance; MeRIT (Plaksin and Lewis, 2022) is a browser-side rhythm interpreter built on Tinctoris's rules. Alternatives on the OMR side are Aruspix, which handles printed sources only and no longer runs on recent macOS, and the SIMSSA workflow, designed mainly for neumatic notation.

The scope of the first semi-automatic pipeline claim deserves care. The two core pieces were published earlier by the author: the scoring-up tool (Thomae et al., 2019) and the integration of dissonance labels into MP Editor with a measurement of correction time (Thomae et al., ISMIR 2022); the MEI to mens to kern conversion workflow already appears in Desmond et al. (2021). What is new here is feeding MuRET's output into MP Editor, plus the mens format and humlib converter extensions this required, so the contribution lies mainly in integration rather than in a new algorithm.

方法Method

第一步在 MuRET：先用選擇性自編碼器偵測譜表區域，人工把譜表指派給聲部；再用以卷積遞迴網路為基礎的整行辨識模型產生「agnostic」表示（只記符號類別與在譜表上的線／間位置），人工修正後，以規則式轉換器轉成「semantic」表示，也就是 Humdrum mens。MuRET 原本就有針對西班牙手稿訓練過的模型，因為伊比利半島手稿的水滴形音符頭和這份瓜地馬拉手稿相近，可以當起點。

第二步把 MuRET 匯出的分聲部 Mensural MEI 上傳到 MP Editor。作者把原本的 Python 命令列 scoring-up 工具改寫成 JavaScript 嵌進 MP Editor，並補上黑色定量記譜（Ars antiqua）、拍制變換、塗色音符等支援；它會把每個音標成完全、不完全或加倍，把每個點標成分割點或附加點，輸出對齊後的總譜 MEI。第三步把總譜依序轉成 mens、kern，在保留 MEI XML ID 的前提下跑 humlib 的不協和音過濾器，再把標籤以 JSON 回貼到 MP Editor 的音符上：可用對位規則解釋的不協和（經過音、鄰音、掛留）標藍色，解釋不了的標橘色。

The first step is in MuRET: a selectional auto-encoder detects staff regions, which a person assigns to voices; a staff-level recognition model based on a convolutional recurrent network then produces an agnostic representation (only symbol class and line/space position on the staff), which is corrected by hand and converted by a rule-based transducer into a semantic representation, Humdrum mens. MuRET already had models trained on Spanish manuscripts, whose droplet-shaped note heads resemble this Guatemalan source, so they served as a starting point.

The second step uploads MuRET's parts-based Mensural MEI export into MP Editor. The author rewrote the Python command-line scoring-up tool in JavaScript inside MP Editor and added support for black Ars antiqua notation, mensuration changes and colored notes; it labels each note perfect, imperfect or altered and each dot as division or addition, and outputs a scored-up MEI. The third step converts that score to mens and then kern, keeps the MEI XML IDs throughout, runs humlib's dissonance filter, and pastes the labels back onto MP Editor's notes as JSON: dissonances explainable by counterpoint rules (passing tones, neighbour tones, suspensions) in blue, unexplainable ones in orange.

限制Limitations

最大的限制是沒有量化評估。全文沒有報告 GCA-Gaha 1 有多少首曲子、多少頁，OMR 的符號錯誤率是多少，人工修正花了多少時間，scoring-up 在多少處需要手動推翻；唯一的數字「校正時間減半」來自 2022 年的前作，不是在這次的案例上量的。結論說這項轉譜工作「大致可以自動化」，但讀者沒有數字可以判斷「大致」是多少。

流程本身的限制，論文寫得很坦白：整行辨識模型是為單聲部譜表設計的，疊在音符上下的延長記號與臨時記號處理不了；所有連結音符（ligature）都被辨識成同一個類別，要逐音手動輸入；MuRET 沒有歌詞辨識，所以這份聲樂語料目前沒有歌詞。MuRET 到 MP Editor 要經過一次 mens 往返，符桿方向、延長記號與逐符號的影像框都會遺失。Table 2 還寫著 scoring-up 不適用於文藝復興晚期、加倍規則漸少使用的三拍子作品，整條流程適用 13 到 15 世紀的主要記譜，但論文沒有說明這份合唱書在這方面落在哪裡、這個限制在案例上影響多大。

The biggest limitation is the absence of quantitative evaluation. The paper does not report how many pieces or pages GCA-Gaha 1 contains, the OMR symbol error rate, how long manual correction took, or how often scoring-up had to be overridden by hand; the only figure, halved correction time, comes from the 2022 predecessor and was not measured on this case. The conclusion says the transcription task can be largely automated, but readers have no numbers to judge what largely means.

The pipeline's own limits are stated candidly: the staff-level recognition model was designed for monophonic staves and cannot handle fermatas or accidentals stacked above or below notes; every ligature is recognised as one generic class and must be entered note by note; MuRET has no text recognition, so this vocal corpus currently lacks lyrics. Going from MuRET to MP Editor requires a mens round trip that discards stem direction, fermatas and per-symbol image boxes. Table 2 also says scoring-up does not work well for late-Renaissance triple-meter pieces, when alteration falls out of use, and the pipeline covers the main notations of the 13th to 15th centuries, yet the paper does not say where this choirbook falls in that respect or how much the limit affected the case.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值不在演算法，而在它把一個長期卡在「每個聲部一串符號」的工作流程接通了，而且接法可以被別人複製：每個工具只做自己擅長的事，彼此之間靠 MEI 與 Humdrum 這兩種開放格式交換，順帶把 mens 補上了加倍（+）、拍制語義、分割點（:）與塗色（~）的編碼。最終的 Mensural MEI 總譜放在 Zenodo 與 GitHub，可在 mei-friend 裡對照原始影像播放，這對想做文藝復興對位語料分析的人是可直接使用的資料。

用不協和音過濾器當錯誤偵測器是個巧妙的想法：OMR 錯誤與抄寫錯誤在單一聲部裡常常看起來合理，排成總譜後才會變成對位上說不通的不協和，而橘色標籤正好把搜尋範圍縮小到第一個無法解釋的不協和之前。但它能抓到的只是「會產生錯位或怪異不協和」的錯誤，音高錯一個音但仍落在協和音程上的情況就會漏掉。下一步若能補上符號錯誤率與逐步工時，這條流程才能和 Ríos-Vila 等（ISMIR 2022）那類端到端整頁辨識做實際比較。

The paper's value lies not in an algorithm but in connecting a workflow long stuck at one symbol string per voice, in a way others can replicate: each tool does what it is good at, and they exchange data through two open formats, MEI and Humdrum, extending mens along the way with encodings for alteration (+), mensuration semantics, dots of division (:) and coloration (~). The final Mensural MEI scores are on Zenodo and GitHub and can be played back against the source images in mei-friend, which is directly usable data for anyone doing corpus analysis of Renaissance counterpoint.

Using a dissonance filter as an error detector is a clever idea: OMR and scribal errors often look plausible within a single voice and only become contrapuntally unexplainable dissonances once scored up, and the orange labels narrow the search to the stretch before the first unexplainable one. But it catches only errors that produce misalignment or odd dissonances; a pitch wrong by a step that still lands on a consonance slips through. If the next step adds symbol error rates and per-stage labour times, the pipeline could finally be compared in practice with end-to-end full-page recognition such as Ríos-Vila et al. (ISMIR 2022).

Spatial DJ: Spatial Audio Control for Arbitrary Loudspeaker Layouts

Spatial DJ：用 Apple Vision Pro 把虛擬喇叭標記對準實體喇叭來取得場地喇叭座標，再以高斯距離核加等功率正規化即時渲染 Demucs 分出的人聲與伴奏音軌，並以 18 人聽測比較擴散與環繞軌道兩種空間配置

為什麼挑這篇：物件式空間音訊號稱「到哪都能渲染」，但換一個場地還是得先量出喇叭在哪裡，而且創作者通常是戴耳機聽雙耳預覽在做混音，不是聽現場的喇叭。這篇用頭戴 AR 把虛擬標記對準實體喇叭來取得座標，再讓人站在場地裡用手勢移動 Demucs 分出的人聲與伴奏音軌。選它是因為它把音源分離的輸出拿去當現場空間混音的素材，題目在 MIR 的邊緣，卻是很具體的應用。在 18 支喇叭的房間裡，校準誤差中位數 5.51 公分；聽測中，人聲環繞比全擴散的沉浸感高 2.11 分（七點量表）。但對照組是兩軌都均勻分散到所有喇叭，沒有「靜止點聲源」條件，所以分不出加分來自「動」還是來自「有定位」。Why this one: Object-based spatial audio promises render-anywhere playback, but each new venue still requires measuring where the loudspeakers are, and creators usually mix on headphones with a binaural preview rather than through the venue's speakers. This paper acquires loudspeaker coordinates by aligning virtual markers to physical speakers with a head-worn AR device, then lets a person standing in the room move Demucs-separated vocal and instrumental stems by hand gestures. I picked it because it uses source-separation output as material for live spatial mixing, a topic at the edge of MIR but a concrete application. In an 18-loudspeaker room, median calibration error is 5.51 cm; in the listening test, the vocal orbit raises immersion by 2.11 points on a seven-point scale over the fully diffuse baseline. But the baseline spreads both stems evenly over all speakers and there is no static point-source condition, so it cannot tell whether the gain comes from motion or from localisation.

動機Motivation

空間音訊的製作流程有一個落差：作品在螢幕上編排、用耳機的雙耳預覽監聽，最後卻要在擁有自己喇叭配置、房間聲學與聽眾位置的實體場地播放。物件式格式（如 MPEG-H）讓場景描述與播放系統脫鉤，渲染器理論上能適應各種喇叭配置，但實際部署仍有兩件事很少被一起處理：在現場取得喇叭的實際幾何座標，以及讓創作者站在場地裡、聽著真正的喇叭來反覆調整。

作者提出兩個研究問題。RQ1：這套系統能否在不同喇叭配置下做到可重複的現場佈局取得與即時渲染？RQ2：用它渲染時，套在不同音樂層上的不同空間配置如何影響聽者感受？前者是工程可行性，後者是知覺研究，論文把兩者放在同一個 18 支喇叭的房間裡回答，另外附上四個場地的部署紀錄。

Spatial audio production has a gap: work is arranged on a screen and monitored through a binaural headphone preview, but ultimately played in a physical venue with its own loudspeaker layout, room acoustics and audience positions. Object-based formats such as MPEG-H decouple the scene description from the playback system, so a renderer can in principle adapt to many layouts, but real deployments still face two tasks rarely handled together: acquiring the actual loudspeaker geometry on site, and letting creators iterate while standing in the venue listening to the real speakers.

The authors pose two research questions. RQ1: can the system support repeatable on-site layout acquisition and real-time rendering across changing loudspeaker layouts? RQ2: when rendered with it, how do different spatial configurations applied to different musical layers shape listeners' perception? The first is engineering feasibility and the second a perception study; the paper answers both in the same 18-loudspeaker room and adds deployment notes from four venues.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名的最接近系統有三個。AudioMiXR 讓戴頭顯的使用者在 6DoF 下抓取並擺放虛擬音源，但輸出是單人頭部追蹤的雙耳音訊；ARCube 用 AR 中的虛擬立方體做 3D 聲像控制，但綁定在一個固定的 28 支喇叭裝置；boomRoom 用波場合成（WFS）陣列與 OptiTrack 光學追蹤做房間尺度的身體操控，同樣是專門搭建的固定系統。這篇和它們的差別在於喇叭佈局是現場取得的變數，而不是寫死的前提。

渲染端最該點出的是：論文的增益映射（以喇叭到物件的距離算高斯權重，再做 L2 正規化讓平方和固定）本質上就是 DBAP（距離式振幅聲像，Lossius 等，2009）的一個變體，只是把 DBAP 原本的反距離冪律換成高斯核、把核寬度綁到使用者的「擴散半徑」。論文在相關研究裡介紹了 DBAP，卻沒有明說自己的渲染器就屬於這一類。這不是缺點（DBAP 本來就是為不規則佈局設計的），但意味著渲染上沒有新東西，貢獻在 AR 校準與工作流程。

The paper names three closest systems. AudioMiXR lets a headset user grab and place virtual sources in 6DoF, but outputs single-user head-tracked binaural audio; ARCube does 3D panning with a virtual cube in AR, but is tied to a fixed 28-loudspeaker installation; boomRoom does room-scale embodied control with a wave field synthesis (WFS) array and OptiTrack optical tracking, also a purpose-built fixed system. The difference here is that the loudspeaker layout is a variable acquired on site rather than a hard-coded premise.

On the rendering side, the point most worth making is that the paper's gain mapping (Gaussian weights from speaker-to-object distance, then L2 normalisation to keep the squared sum constant) is essentially a variant of DBAP (distance-based amplitude panning, Lossius et al., 2009), replacing DBAP's inverse-distance power law with a Gaussian kernel whose width is tied to the user's spread radius. The paper introduces DBAP in its related work but never says its own renderer belongs to that family. That is not a flaw, since DBAP was designed for irregular layouts, but it means nothing is new in rendering; the contribution lies in AR calibration and workflow.

方法Method

系統分三塊。前端有桌面 GUI 與 Apple Vision Pro 上的 AR 控制器（Swift／visionOS 2.0）；桌面端可載入音檔並用 Demucs 分離成音軌，每個音軌是一個單聲道音訊物件，有 3D 位置與擴散半徑。AR 端進入校準模式後，操作者把每個虛擬喇叭標記拖到實體喇叭低音單體的中心並確認，座標存進資料庫供下次重用；平時則以手勢拖曳物件、雙手捏合調整擴散，狀態以約 30 Hz 經 OSC／UDP 傳給後端。

後端是 Python Flask 服務，在每個音訊回呼中以最新狀態計算增益：點聲源物件的權重 w = exp(−d²/2σ²)，σ = max(r, σ_min)，再做 L2 正規化；擴散半徑達上限 r_max = 2.5 時物件變成「環境」物件，以 1/√|Ω| 平均分配到指定喇叭，並在上限前 0.2 的區間平滑過渡。輸出經 AoIP 介面一路一聲道送到喇叭，超低音等可排除在正規化之外。

The system has three parts. The front end is a desktop GUI plus an AR controller on Apple Vision Pro (Swift/visionOS 2.0); the desktop loads audio and separates it into stems with Demucs, each stem becoming a mono audio object with a 3D position and spread radius. In calibration mode the operator drags each virtual speaker marker onto the centre of the physical speaker's woofer and confirms, and the coordinates are stored for reuse; otherwise gestures drag objects and a two-hand pinch adjusts spread, with state sent to the back end over OSC/UDP at about 30 Hz.

The back end is a Python Flask service that computes gains from the latest state in each audio callback: a point-source object's weight is w = exp(−d²/2σ²) with σ = max(r, σ_min), followed by L2 normalisation; when spread reaches r_max = 2.5 the object becomes ambient, distributed at 1/√|Ω| over a chosen speaker set, with a smooth blend over the last 0.2 below the maximum. Output goes through an AoIP interface, one channel per speaker, and outputs such as subwoofers can be excluded from normalisation.

限制Limitations

技術評估的數字要看清楚量的是什麼。校準誤差中位數 5.51 公分（IQR 3.62–8.78），兩位操作者各做 10 次全房校準，兩人重心差距中位數 4.25 公分；但誤差主要在水平面（|Δx| 4.10、|Δz| 2.51 公分，|Δy| 只有 0.59），而喇叭箱體本身寬 24.8 公分，所以這比較像「對準了箱體的哪一點」，而不是聲學中心的定位精度。控制延遲中位數 8.07 毫秒，但只量到更新進入音訊回呼為止，不含手勢辨識、AoIP 傳輸與輸出緩衝；p95 是 27.40 毫秒，用「低於 10 毫秒的音樂互動準則」來形容並不完整。

聽測的設計問題更大。C1 是人聲與伴奏都以相同增益送進全部 18 支喇叭，坐在正中央的聽者很可能聽到明顯的梳狀濾波染色，有受試者形容它「悶」；其餘三個條件都有點聲源。因此「環繞提高沉浸感」混雜了「動 vs. 靜」與「定位 vs. 全擴散」兩個因素，論文在討論裡也承認位置與擴散參數在這個設計中是綁在一起的。另外受試者事先被告知會聽到「不同的空間效果」，只有 18 人、每人只聽 4 段曲子中的 2 段，且全部坐在同一個中央位置。

The technical numbers need reading for what they measure. Median calibration error is 5.51 cm (IQR 3.62–8.78), with two operators each doing 10 full-room calibrations and a median gap of 4.25 cm between their centroids; but the error lies mostly in the horizontal plane (|Δx| 4.10 and |Δz| 2.51 cm, |Δy| only 0.59), and the cabinets are 24.8 cm wide, so this is more about which point on the cabinet was targeted than about locating the acoustic centre. Median control latency is 8.07 ms, but it is measured only until the update enters the audio callback, excluding gesture recognition, AoIP transport and output buffering; the p95 is 27.40 ms, so describing it via the under-10 ms guideline for musical interaction is incomplete.

The listening-test design has bigger issues. C1 sends both vocal and instrumental at equal gain to all 18 speakers, so a listener at the exact centre is likely to hear noticeable comb-filter coloration, and one participant called it muffled; the other three conditions all contain a point source. Higher immersion from orbiting therefore mixes motion versus stasis with localisation versus full diffusion, and the paper's discussion concedes that position and spread are coupled in this design. Participants were also told beforehand they would hear different spatial effects; there were only 18 of them, each heard only 2 of the 4 excerpts, and all sat at the same central position.

討論Discussion

效果量本身很大：沉浸感上 C2、C3、C4 分別比 C1 高 2.11、2.45、2.28 分，能量感高 1.17、1.62、1.60 分，且不適感沒有顯著差異（F(3,115.51)=2.32，p=.079）。比較有意思的是兩個「沒有」：雙軌道 C4 被最多人聽出「兩個在動的層」（63.9%），但在動態清晰度、沉浸、能量、新奇感上都沒有顯著勝過單軌道；而且 C1 有 22.2% 的試次被說成「水平移動」，代表聽者描述的運動和程式設定的軌跡並不一一對應。

論文最反直覺的結果是：伴奏環繞、人聲保持擴散（C3）時，「人聲聚焦」評分反而最高，比人聲自己環繞的 C2 高 0.69 分（p=.009）。但比 C1 高 0.57 分時，95% 信賴區間半寬是 0.56，p=.044，幾乎貼在門檻上。比較穩的結論是「不同音樂層可以獨立指定空間行為，而且彼此會互相影響」，這正好支持用音源分離拆出音軌再分別空間化的做法。下一步該補的是靜止點聲源條件、多個聽位，以及真正讓創作者自己用 AR 寫軌跡的研究，因為這次的軌跡都是預先設定好的，系統標榜的「現場編排」其實還沒被評估。

The effect sizes themselves are large: immersion for C2, C3 and C4 exceeds C1 by 2.11, 2.45 and 2.28 points, energy by 1.17, 1.62 and 1.60, with no significant difference in discomfort (F(3,115.51) = 2.32, p = .079). The more interesting results are two non-findings: dual orbit C4 was most often heard as two moving layers (63.9%), yet did not significantly beat single orbits on motion clarity, immersion, energy or novelty; and 22.2% of C1 trials were described as horizontal movement, so the motion listeners report does not map one-to-one onto the programmed trajectories.

The paper's most counterintuitive result is that vocal focus is rated highest when the instrumental orbits and the vocal stays diffuse (C3), 0.69 points above C2 where the vocal itself orbits (p = .009). But its 0.57-point lead over C1 has a 95% CI half-width of 0.56 and p = .044, right at the threshold. The safer conclusion is that musical layers can be given independent spatial behaviour and that they influence one another, which supports separating stems with source separation and spatialising them individually. Next steps should add a static point-source condition, several listening positions, and a study in which creators author trajectories themselves in AR, since every trajectory here was predefined and the system's advertised on-site authoring has not yet been evaluated.
