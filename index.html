Computational Raga Recognition: A Theory-Driven Approach

理論驅動的計算拉格辨識：以 22 個 shruti 延伸成 44 格的音高框架，從 pYIN 音高曲線抽出 Nyasa、Purvang–Uttarang、Vadi–Samvadi、Alpatva/Varjit、上下行轉移與 Chalan 樂句六個樂理特徵，各自與 Yaman 參考模型比對後合成相似度分數（Yaman 84.0%、Shuddh Kalyan 64.9%、Marwa 0%）

Janay Sukheja, Shailaja D Sharma
arXiv:2611.15242 · cs.SD; cs.HC; cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-11-11
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：多數拉格辨識是把音高分布或學到的表徵直接丟進分類器。這篇反過來，逐條把印度斯坦古典樂理對拉格的定義（停留音、主副音、上下行、特徵樂句）寫成可計算的特徵，各自對 Yaman 的參考模型打分：Yaman 84.0%、音材最接近的 Shuddh Kalyan 64.9%、Bageshri 18.9%、Miyan Malhar 13.5%、Marwa 0%。選它是因為每個分數都對得回一個樂理概念，可以看出哪些樂理能從音訊中抽出來、哪些不行——例如資料抽出的主副音是 S–P，而樂理上 Yaman 的 Vadi–Samvadi 是 G–N。要注意整個評估只有一個參考拉格、11 段錄音，且論文沒有說測試錄音是否與參考錄音分開。Why this one: Most raga recognition feeds pitch distributions or learned representations straight into a classifier. This paper goes the other way: it turns each clause of the Hindustani theoretical definition of a raga (resting notes, principal notes, ascent/descent, characteristic phrases) into a computable feature and scores each against a Yaman reference model: Yaman 84.0%, the closest-in-pitch-material Shuddh Kalyan 64.9%, Bageshri 18.9%, Miyan Malhar 13.5%, Marwa 0%. I picked it because every score maps back to a theoretical concept, so one can see which parts of theory are recoverable from audio and which are not; for instance, the data yields S–P as the principal pair, whereas theory gives G–N as Yaman's Vadi–Samvadi. Note that the whole evaluation has one reference raga and 11 recordings, and the paper does not say whether test recordings were held out from the reference set.

動機Motivation

拉格（raga）不只是一組音。兩個拉格可以用幾乎一樣的音材，卻因為「哪個音要停留、哪個音要強調、上行下行怎麼走、慣用哪些樂句」而截然不同。Yaman 與 Shuddh Kalyan 就是這種例子。只看音高分布的方法在這種成對拉格上最容易混淆。

作者想問的是：樂理書上對一個拉格的描述，能不能逐條從錄音的音高曲線裡抽出來？抽出來之後，用這些特徵組成的相似度分數能不能把目標拉格和其他拉格分開？這個問題的好處是可解釋：如果分數低，可以直接說是哪一條樂理特徵不吻合，而不是一個黑箱機率。

A raga is not just a set of notes. Two ragas can share nearly the same pitch material and still differ sharply in which notes are rested on, which are emphasized, how ascent and descent move, and which phrases are idiomatic. Yaman and Shuddh Kalyan are such a pair, and methods that look only at pitch distributions are most easily confused by pairs like this.

The authors ask whether a textbook description of a raga can be extracted clause by clause from the pitch contour of a recording, and whether a similarity score built from those features can separate the target raga from others. The appeal is interpretability: when a score is low, one can name which theoretical feature failed to match, instead of reading a black-box probability.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

分布派。 Chordia & Rae（2007）用音級分布加上雙音（dyad）分布做拉格分類；Koduri 等（2012）系統比較了各種音高分布方法。本文的 Purvang–Uttarang 比例與前六大音位，本質上就是音高分布的兩種粗粒度摘要；上下行轉移機率則等於 Chordia & Rae 的雙音分布，只是限定在「穩定音之間」計算。

樂句派。 Tansen（Pandey 等 2003）是最接近本文精神的前例：它用 HMM 建模音序，再加上 pakad（拉格的招牌樂句）比對來辨識拉格——也就是本文 Chalan 特徵的原型，但本文沒有引用它。Gulati 等（2016）的 TDMS（time-delayed melody surfaces）與向量空間樂句模型，在公開的 CompMusic 印度斯坦資料集上做到多拉格分類；Dutta & Murthy（2015）以最長共同片段集合做拉格驗證，本文的加權編輯距離比對與它屬同一類。停留音偵測也有前例：Gulati 等（2014）專門做過 nyas 片段偵測。

本文的差別在於把六個樂理概念各自實作、各自打分，而不是混成一個特徵向量交給分類器。但這也讓它比上述系統更像「單一拉格的驗證器」：只建了 Yaman 一個參考模型，沒有在任何公開資料集上和上述方法比較。

Distribution methods. Chordia & Rae (2007) classified ragas with pitch-class distributions plus dyad distributions; Koduri et al. (2012) systematically compared pitch-distribution methods. This paper's Purvang–Uttarang proportion and top-six note positions are essentially two coarse summaries of a pitch distribution, and its ascent/descent transition probabilities amount to Chordia & Rae's dyad distribution restricted to transitions between stable notes.

Phrase methods. Tansen (Pandey et al. 2003) is the closest precedent in spirit: it modelled note sequences with an HMM and added pakad (signature-phrase) matching, which is the prototype of this paper's Chalan feature, yet it is not cited. Gulati et al. (2016) used time-delayed melody surfaces (TDMS) and vector-space phrase models for multi-raga classification on the public CompMusic Hindustani dataset; Dutta & Murthy (2015) verified ragas via longest common segment sets, the same family as this paper's weighted edit-distance matching. Resting-note detection also has precedent: Gulati et al. (2014) specifically addressed nyas segment detection.

The difference here is that six theoretical concepts are implemented and scored separately rather than merged into one feature vector for a classifier. That also makes it more of a single-raga verifier than the systems above: only a Yaman reference model is built, and there is no comparison with those methods on any public dataset.

方法Method

資料與音高。 11 段 Yaman 演唱錄音、總長約 6 小時，先用 RipX DAW 分離出主唱以去除伴奏，再用 librosa 的 pYIN 以 10 ms 解析度抽音高，範圍 C1–C6。主音 Sa 由人工標定，而且近似到最接近的西方音名（C、C# 或 D）。

44 格音高框架。 以 Oak（2025）的 22 shruti 系統為基礎（相鄰 shruti 間隔為 256/243、81/80、25/24 三種比例），再在每兩個 shruti 之間依 25%–50%–25% 切出中間位置，得到 44 格；每個音高值對到最近的格。

六個特徵。 Nyasa：同一格持續至少 100 幀（約 1 秒）算一次停留，統計 44 維分布。Purvang–Uttarang：在主要八度內計算下半（Sa–Ma）與上半（Pa–上 Sa）的比例。Vadi–Samvadi：由停留分布、上下半區偏重與出現頻率前六名的音位推得候選主副音。Alpatva/Varjit：若樂理上應少用或避用的音出現在前六名就扣分。上下行：持續至少 0.75 秒的音算穩定音，5 秒內的穩定音轉移分成上行與下行。Chalan：把序列降到 250 ms 一格，以 4–7 秒滑動窗（16–28 格）切出樂句，用依格距加權的 Levenshtein ratio 比對，相似度 0.9 以上歸為同組，每種長度保留出現最多的前 10 組。

打分。 Nyasa 用 44 維向量與參考平均方向的夾角，門檻取 11 段參考的中位數加一個標準差；Purvang–Uttarang 用參考中夾角最大的兩個向量界定範圍再放寬 5%；上下行把出現少於 5 次或機率低於 0.1 的轉移濾掉、每個起始音保留前 3 名，測試錄音中命中的轉移把參考機率加總；Chalan 計算四種長度的命中樂句數。六個分數如何加權合成最後的百分比，論文沒有給出公式。

Data and pitch. Eleven Yaman vocal recordings, about 6 hours in total, had the lead voice isolated with RipX DAW to remove accompaniment, then pitch was extracted with librosa's pYIN at 10 ms resolution over C1–C6. The tonic Sa was set manually and approximated to the nearest Western pitch (C, C# or D).

44-position framework. Starting from Oak's (2025) 22-shruti system (adjacent shrutis separated by ratios of 256/243, 81/80 or 25/24), intermediate positions are inserted between each pair of shrutis using a 25%–50%–25% split, giving 44 positions; each pitch value maps to the nearest one.

Six features. Nyasa: a position held for at least 100 frames (about 1 s) counts as a rest, yielding a 44-dimensional distribution. Purvang–Uttarang: the proportion of notes in the lower (Sa–Ma) and upper (Pa–upper Sa) halves of the main octave. Vadi–Samvadi: candidate principal notes derived from the rest distribution, the half-octave emphasis and the six most frequent positions. Alpatva/Varjit: a penalty when a note that theory says is rare or avoided appears in the top six. Ascent/descent: notes held at least 0.75 s are stable; transitions between stable notes within 5 s are split into ascending and descending. Chalan: the sequence is downsampled to 250 ms steps, phrases are cut with 4–7 s sliding windows (16–28 steps), compared with a Levenshtein ratio weighted by position distance, grouped at similarity 0.9 or above, and the 10 most frequent groups per length are kept.

Scoring. Nyasa uses the angle between a 44-dimensional vector and the mean reference direction, with a threshold at the median plus one standard deviation over the 11 references. Purvang–Uttarang bounds a region by the two most widely separated reference vectors, widened by 5%. Ascent/descent drops transitions seen fewer than 5 times or with probability below 0.1, keeps the top 3 per starting note, and sums the reference probabilities of transitions matched in the test recording. Chalan counts matched phrases across the four lengths. The paper gives no formula for how the six scores are weighted into the final percentage.

限制Limitations

沒有說清楚測試集。 Nyasa 門檻是用「全部 11 段」參考錄音算出來的，論文也沒有說 Yaman 的 84.0% 是在哪段錄音上得到的、它是否在參考集之外。如果測試錄音就是參考錄音之一，84.0% 量的是自我一致性，不是辨識能力。其他拉格各用了幾段錄音也沒有交代；引言列了 Bhoop 為測試拉格，結果卻沒有它的分數。

評估規模與比較都不足。 只有一個參考拉格、五個對照拉格，沒有混淆矩陣、沒有多拉格分類準確率，也沒有和 Koduri 等（2012）的音高分布法或 Gulati 等（2016）的 TDMS 在同一批資料上比較。現成的公開資料集（CompMusic 印度斯坦拉格資料集）沒有被使用，作者自己的錄音也沒有釋出。

樂理與資料不吻合的地方被輕輕帶過。 抽出的主副音候選是 S–P，其次才是 G–N，但樂理上 Yaman 的 Vadi–Samvadi 是 G–N。Sa 與 Pa 佔了停留的 57.56% 與 27.73%，這幾乎是所有拉格都會有的現象（Sa、Pa 是持續音的固定錨點），所以 Nyasa 特徵的鑑別力本來就可疑。作者承認需要加入樂句位置等上下文，但沒有量化這對分數的影響。

流程依賴人工。 主音人工標定且四捨五入到西方半音，對一個以 shruti 為單位的框架而言，這個近似本身就可能把音推到錯的格。主唱分離用的是商業軟體 RipX，可重現性有限。最後的 84.0% 和 64.9% 只有約 19 個百分點的差距，而 Shuddh Kalyan 正是最需要被分開的那個。

The test set is not specified. The Nyasa threshold is computed from all 11 reference recordings, and the paper does not say which recording produced Yaman's 84.0% or whether it lay outside the reference set. If the test recording is one of the references, 84.0% measures self-consistency, not recognition. The number of recordings per other raga is also not given; the introduction lists Bhoop as a test raga, yet no score for it appears in the results.

Scale and comparison are both thin. There is one reference raga and five contrast ragas, with no confusion matrix, no multi-raga accuracy, and no comparison with Koduri et al.'s (2012) pitch-distribution methods or Gulati et al.'s (2016) TDMS on the same data. The existing public dataset (the CompMusic Hindustani raga dataset) is not used, and the authors' recordings are not released.

Mismatches between theory and data are passed over lightly. The extracted principal-note candidates are S–P, then G–N, whereas Yaman's theoretical Vadi–Samvadi is G–N. Sa and Pa account for 57.56% and 27.73% of rests, which nearly every raga would show, since Sa and Pa anchor the drone, so the discriminative power of the Nyasa feature is doubtful to begin with. The authors concede that context such as phrase position is needed but do not quantify its effect on the score.

The pipeline depends on manual steps. The tonic is set by hand and rounded to a Western semitone, which, for a framework built on shrutis, can itself push notes into the wrong position. Lead isolation uses the commercial RipX software, limiting reproducibility. Finally, 84.0% versus 64.9% is a gap of only about 19 points, and Shuddh Kalyan is exactly the raga that most needs separating.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的不是辨識數字，而是逐條對照樂理的結果。上下行轉移抽出 N→R 比 S→R 更常見，符合 Yaman 慣用的 N-R 起音；前十大 Chalan 樂句裡找得到樂理書上 N R S、N D N、M D N 這些招牌動作。這說明「樂句與轉移」這一層的樂理可以相當直接地從音高曲線讀出來，而「主副音」這一層不行——它需要知道音在樂句中的位置，而不只是出現頻率。

作者也觀察到 Nyasa 對演出差異最敏感，Chalan 與上下行轉移在 11 段錄音間較穩定；慢板的 alap、vilambit 段落提供較多穩定音，快板 drut 段落則可能抽不出足夠的停留音與樂句。這對任何以停留音為基礎的方法都是實用提醒：分數有一部分反映的是錄音裡演了什麼段落，而不只是哪個拉格。

作者正確地把分數定位為「與參考拉格的吻合度」，而非屬於該拉格的機率。要讓這個框架成為真正的辨識系統，最少需要：每個拉格各建參考模型、留出測試錄音、在公開資料集上與 TDMS 等方法比較，並把六個分量的加權方式寫清楚。目前它比較像一份可計算的樂理檢核表，作為可解釋性的分析工具已經有用，作為辨識器還沒有被驗證。

The most valuable part is not the recognition number but the clause-by-clause comparison with theory. The transition analysis finds N→R more probable than S→R, consistent with Yaman's idiomatic N-R approach, and the top-ten Chalan phrases contain the textbook signature movements N R S, N D N and M D N. This shows that the phrase-and-transition layer of theory can be read fairly directly from a pitch contour, while the principal-note layer cannot: it needs a note's position within the phrase, not just its frequency.

The authors also observe that Nyasa is the most sensitive to performance variation, while Chalan and ascent/descent transitions are more stable across the 11 recordings; slow alap and vilambit sections offer more stable notes, whereas fast drut passages may not yield enough rests and phrases. That is a practical warning for any rest-based method: part of the score reflects which sections were performed, not only which raga.

The authors rightly frame the score as agreement with a reference raga rather than a probability of membership. To become a real recognizer, the framework needs at least a reference model per raga, held-out test recordings, a comparison with methods such as TDMS on a public dataset, and an explicit weighting of the six components. For now it is a computable theory checklist: useful as an interpretable analysis tool, not yet validated as a recognizer.

Evaluating AI Musical Creativity: Towards a Quantitative Multi-Dimensional Framework Based on Value, Novelty, and Surprise

評估 AI 音樂創造力：把 Boden 的價值／新穎／驚奇三分法改寫成 M-value、M-surprise、M-novelty，提出 25 個指標（情緒軌跡、旋律／和聲資訊量、頻譜通量 z 分數、IOI 熵、雙模型貝氏 HC-surprise、嵌入最近鄰距離等）的概念框架，作者明言未做任何對人類判斷的實證驗證

Hindy Rossignol, Giorgio Franceschelli, Mirco Musolesi
arXiv:2611.14634 · cs.SD; cs.AI; cs.CY · 投稿submitted 2026-11-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：生成音樂的評估大多量音質、文字對齊或與訓練資料的分布距離，很少有人正面處理「創造力」。這篇把 Boden 的價值、新穎、驚奇三分法改寫到音樂上，整理成 25 個可計算的指標，並把驚奇拆成機率型、隱含內容型與典範轉移型三種。選它是因為它把「新穎」（與語料的距離）和「驚奇」（違反預期）在計算上分開，這是生成音樂評估常混為一談的兩件事。要注意這是一篇純概念論文：作者明言沒有對任何指標做人類判斷的驗證，全文沒有一個實驗數字，三種驚奇裡也只有機率型真的能算。Why this one: Generative music evaluation mostly measures audio quality, text alignment or distributional distance from training data; few papers address "creativity" head-on. This paper adapts Boden's value–novelty–surprise triad to music, organizes it into 25 computable metrics, and splits surprise into probabilistic, hidden-content and paradigm-shifting kinds. I picked it because it separates novelty (distance from a corpus) from surprise (violated expectation) computationally, two things generative music evaluation often conflates. Note that this is a purely conceptual paper: the authors state that no metric is validated against human judgment, the paper contains no experimental numbers, and of the three kinds of surprise only the probabilistic one is actually computable.

動機Motivation

作者認為音樂生成相較文字、影像生成受到的關注較少，原因之一是缺少評估框架：沒有共識的尺，就很難衡量進步或比較系統。現有評估多半在量音質（MUSHRA、FAD）、條件遵循或與訓練資料的相似度，而「創不創新」這件事要嘛交給 Likert 量表的主觀評分，要嘛乾脆不量。

他們想問四個問題：AI 音樂創造力能否嚴格定義？Boden 的價值／新穎／驚奇框架能幫上多少？哪些指標能量這些成分？這些成分能被多客觀地量化？更遠的目標是讓這些指標成為生成模型的訓練目標，讓模型不只最小化重建損失，也被鼓勵去「有創意」。

The authors argue that music generation has received less attention than text or image generation partly because it lacks an evaluation framework: without an agreed yardstick, progress is hard to measure and systems hard to compare. Existing evaluation mostly measures audio quality (MUSHRA, FAD), conditioning adherence or similarity to training data, while whether a piece is creative is either left to Likert-scale ratings or not measured at all.

They pose four questions: can AI musical creativity be rigorously defined; how far does Boden's value–novelty–surprise framework help; which metrics can measure its components; and how objectively can those components be quantified? The longer-term aim is to make these metrics training objectives, so that models are encouraged to be creative rather than only to minimize reconstruction loss.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

創造力框架。 Jordanous（2012）的 SPECS 讓評估者沿著技巧、新穎、價值等維度評分，但需要人參與；Colton 等（2011）的 FACE／IDEA 模型形式化了創作行為，但難以落地。最直接的前身是第二、三作者自己的 DeepCreativity（Franceschelli & Musolesi 2022），它已在十九世紀美國詩上把價值、新穎、驚奇做成不需人工判斷的計算量。本文本質上是把 DeepCreativity 的思路移到音樂，並把每個成分拆到旋律、和聲、音色、節奏四個維度。

機率型驚奇其實已有成熟工具。 本文的「旋律／和聲資訊量（IC）」——以 n-gram 估計事件機率、取負對數——正是 Pearce（2005）IDyOM 的核心量。IDyOM 已有近二十年對聽者預期判斷與腦電反應的驗證，是本框架中唯一已被充分驗證的成分，但論文沒有點名它。「頻譜通量 z 分數」則與音訊新穎度曲線（Foote 2000 的自相似矩陣新穎度）及一般起音偵測同源。

新穎度。 以嵌入空間最近鄰距離量新穎，與生成音樂的訓練資料複製偵測（論文引用的 Batlle-Roca 等 2024）是同一個量、方向相反：一個想讓距離大，一個怕距離太小。論文也正面回應了 Salamanca 等（2023）的發現：43 位聽者對共創系統半成品的評分中，新穎與驚奇在統計上無法區分。作者的回應是那是人類對單一總分的內省限制，計算上兩者可以分開——但這個主張本文沒有檢驗。

Creativity frameworks. Jordanous's (2012) SPECS has evaluators score along dimensions such as skill, novelty and value, but requires humans; Colton et al.'s (2011) FACE/IDEA models formalize creative acts but are hard to apply. The most direct predecessor is DeepCreativity by the second and third authors (Franceschelli & Musolesi 2022), which already computed value, novelty and surprise without human judgment on nineteenth-century American poetry. This paper essentially moves the DeepCreativity idea to music and splits each component across melody, harmony, timbre and rhythm.

Probabilistic surprise already has a mature tool. The paper's melodic/harmonic information content (IC), negative log-probability of an event under an n-gram model, is exactly the core quantity of Pearce's (2005) IDyOM. IDyOM has nearly two decades of validation against listener expectancy ratings and EEG responses and is the only thoroughly validated component of this framework, yet the paper does not name it. The spectral-flux z-score belongs to the same family as audio novelty curves (Foote's 2000 self-similarity novelty) and ordinary onset detection.

Novelty. Measuring novelty by nearest-neighbour distance in embedding space is the same quantity as training-data replication detection in generated music (Batlle-Roca et al. 2024, which the paper cites), with the opposite sign: one wants the distance large, the other fears it is too small. The paper does engage with Salamanca et al. (2023), who found that among 43 listeners rating unfinished pieces from a co-creative assistant, novelty and surprise were statistically indistinguishable. The authors reply that this reflects the limits of human introspection on a single global score and that the two separate computationally, but this claim is not tested here.

方法Method

前提：聽者經驗（PME）。 驚奇與價值都相對於聽者的預期，所以作者先定義「先前音樂經驗」，並以「先前聆聽」近似：對某年出生的一群聽者，取其出生前 δ 年到現在所有發行的音樂（預設 δ = 50 年，涵蓋父母與祖父母輩的音樂），再加上 Vivaldi《四季》這類跨時代經典。實作上建議用 Million Song Dataset 或 Discogs 按年份篩選，經典部分用串流平台的精選歌單。

M-value（4 個指標）。 用逐段估計的效價–喚醒值，計算離中性點的平均距離、其標準差、變化量總和與轉折點數，分別代表情緒強度、表現力、起伏與情緒軌跡複雜度。作者也提到 Zipf 定律與歌詞分析，但沒有把它們列進指標表。他們自己承認，Yang 等（2008）的效價／喚醒回歸只解釋了 0.28／0.58 的變異，以此為基礎的價值指標可靠度有限。

M-surprise（17 個指標）。 機率型驚奇按四個維度各給一組：旋律與和聲用 n-gram 下的資訊量、平均資訊量與熵（熵分「單一作品內的分布」與「模型預測分布」兩種）；音色用 Mel 頻譜通量，對 PME 語料的平均與標準差算逐幀與整體 z 分數，再加通量熵；節奏用起音間隔（IOI）的資訊量（含微時序容差）、熵、速度導數形成的速度偏移驚奇，以及用 Jensen–Shannon 散度量不同聲部（先以 Demucs 等分離）IOI 分布的錯位。隱含內容型驚奇定義為兩個模型貝氏驚奇（後驗對先驗的 KL）之差：第一個模型大幅更新信念、第二個模型幾乎不更新，代表「先驚訝、回頭一想又合理」；另給了以描述長度下降表示「頓悟」的版本。典範轉移型驚奇則只列出三個必要條件（極高新穎、極高機率型驚奇、既有模型無法表徵），作者明說無法直接量測。

M-novelty（4 個指標）。 在 MERT 或 CLAP 等嵌入空間中，取生成作品對訓練集的平均與最小餘弦距離，並可按旋律、和聲、音色、節奏分開計算。作者刻意不用 KL 散度量新穎，理由是那量的是在參考分布下的意外程度，屬於驚奇而非新穎。

組合。 論文不給總分公式，只以一張六種情境的表定性描述三成分高低組合對創造力的影響，並指出簡單加權和忽略了 Wundt 曲線式的非線性交互。

Premise: prior musical experience (PME). Surprise and value are relative to a listener's expectations, so the authors define prior musical experience and approximate it by prior listening: for listeners born in a given year, all music released from δ years before their birth to now (default δ = 50 years, covering parents' and grandparents' music), plus timeless canonical works such as Vivaldi's Four Seasons. In practice they suggest filtering the Million Song Dataset or Discogs by year and taking the canon from curated streaming playlists.

M-value (4 metrics). From per-segment valence–arousal estimates: mean distance from the neutral point, its standard deviation, summed absolute change and inflection count, standing for emotional intensity, expressivity, volatility and trajectory complexity. Zipf's law and lyric analysis are discussed but not entered in the metric table. The authors concede that Yang et al.'s (2008) valence/arousal regression explained only 0.28/0.58 of the variance, limiting any value metric built on such estimates.

M-surprise (17 metrics). Probabilistic surprise gets a set per dimension. Melody and harmony: IC under an n-gram model, mean IC and entropy (artifact-derived over one piece, or model-derived from its predictive distribution). Timbre: Mel spectral flux, frame-level and global z-scores against the PME corpus mean and standard deviation, plus flux entropy. Rhythm: inter-onset-interval (IOI) IC with a microtiming tolerance, IOI entropy, tempo-deviation surprise from the tempo derivative, and Jensen–Shannon divergence between IOI distributions of separated streams (e.g. via Demucs).

Hidden-content surprise is defined as the difference between two models' Bayesian surprise (posterior-versus-prior KL): one model updates strongly, the other barely, standing for "surprising at first, coherent in retrospect"; a variant expresses the "aha" as a drop in description length. Paradigm-shifting surprise gets only three necessary conditions (extreme novelty, extreme probabilistic surprise, irrepresentability by existing models), and the authors say it cannot be measured directly.

M-novelty (4 metrics). Mean and minimum cosine distance from a generated piece to the training set in an embedding space such as MERT or CLAP, optionally per melody, harmony, timbre and rhythm. KL divergence is deliberately avoided for novelty, on the grounds that it measures unexpectedness under a reference distribution, which belongs to surprise.

Combination. No overall formula is given; a six-case table describes qualitatively how high or low combinations of the three components affect creativity, noting that a simple weighted sum ignores Wundt-curve-style nonlinear interactions.

限制Limitations

沒有任何實證。 作者在引言末明說貢獻是概念性的，沒有對人類判斷做驗證。全文沒有一個由本文產生的實驗數字：沒有對任何生成模型計算過這 25 個指標，也沒有檢查它們彼此是否相關、是否與聽者的創造力評分相關。唯一的圖是用三首第一作者自己發行的歌曲畫的 CLAP 嵌入示意圖。

「新穎與驚奇可分」是核心主張，卻沒有被檢驗。 這正是 Salamanca 等（2023）的 43 位聽者資料所質疑的，作者以概念論證回應，並承認兩者在實務上能否分開仍是開放問題。如果在真實資料上兩者高度相關，框架的三分結構就只剩兩分。

三種驚奇中只有一種可算。 作者自己說，以現有生成架構評估隱含內容型驚奇「在概念上與技術上都不可行」，典範轉移型驚奇「無法直接量測」。所以 M-surprise 的 17 個指標中，真正能算的 15 個全屬於機率型——而那一部分大多是 IDyOM、頻譜通量、IOI 統計等既有工具的重新包裝。

價值與 PME 的假設很重。 M-value 依賴效價／喚醒估計，而作者引用的估計只解釋 0.28–0.58 的變異；PME 假設同一社會文化群體共享預期模型，δ = 50 年的預設沒有經驗依據。框架不給組合公式，也就無法對系統排名，這與引言「讓系統可比較」的動機有落差。

There is no empirical evidence. The authors state at the end of the introduction that the contribution is conceptual and not validated against human judgment. The paper contains no experimental number of its own: none of the 25 metrics is computed on any generative model, and there is no check of whether they correlate with each other or with listener creativity ratings. The only figure is an illustrative CLAP embedding plot of three songs released by the first author.

The claim that novelty and surprise are separable is central but untested. It is exactly what Salamanca et al.'s (2023) 43-listener data calls into question; the authors answer with a conceptual argument and concede that whether the two separate in practice is an open question. If they correlate strongly on real data, the three-part structure collapses to two.

Only one of the three kinds of surprise is computable. The authors themselves say that evaluating hidden-content surprise with current generative architectures is "both conceptually and technically unfeasible" and that paradigm-shifting surprise cannot be measured directly. So the 15 computable metrics among M-surprise's 17 are all probabilistic, and that part largely repackages existing tools such as IDyOM, spectral flux and IOI statistics.

The value and PME assumptions are heavy. M-value relies on valence/arousal estimates, and the estimates the authors cite explain only 0.28–0.58 of the variance; PME assumes a shared expectation model within a sociocultural group, and the δ = 50-year default has no empirical basis. With no combination formula, the framework cannot rank systems, which sits uneasily with the introduction's motivation of making systems comparable.

討論Discussion

這篇的用處在於當作一份整理過的地圖，而不是一把可以直接拿來用的尺。它把散落在音樂認知（IDyOM 的資訊量）、MIR（頻譜通量、IOI、速度追蹤）、表徵學習（嵌入距離）與計算創造力（Boden、SPECS、DeepCreativity）裡的工具，按「價值／驚奇／新穎」與「旋律／和聲／音色／節奏」排成一張表，而且每個成分都區分了「單一作品內」與「模型輸出整體」兩個層級。對想設計生成音樂評估的人來說，這張表是好的起點檢核清單。

最有意思的兩個想法是：第一，把新穎定義為與訓練集的嵌入距離、把驚奇定義為在聽者預期模型下的資訊量，並刻意不用 KL 量新穎，讓兩者在定義層面就不重疊；第二，隱含內容型驚奇的「兩個模型一個大幅更新、一個幾乎不更新」，以及「描述長度下降＝頓悟」（呼應 Schmidhuber 2010 的壓縮進步理論），雖然無法實作，卻點出了現有指標完全沒碰到的一類現象。

但要把它用在生成音樂評估上，下一步非常明確：選幾個生成模型，把可算的指標都算一遍，看它們之間的相關結構，再對照 Grötschla 等（2025）那種上萬筆兩兩比較的人類偏好資料。另一個作者自己提到的風險也值得記下：如果把這些指標當作強化學習的獎勵，模型很可能學會刷高資訊量與嵌入距離——那是「隨機」，不是「創意」。框架裡 M-value 正是要擋住這件事的成分，而它恰恰是最難量的。

The paper is useful as a curated map, not as a ready-made yardstick. It takes tools scattered across music cognition (IDyOM's information content), MIR (spectral flux, IOIs, tempo tracking), representation learning (embedding distances) and computational creativity (Boden, SPECS, DeepCreativity) and arranges them in one table by value/surprise/novelty and melody/harmony/timbre/rhythm, distinguishing for each the single-artifact level from the model-output level. For anyone designing a generative-music evaluation, that table is a good starting checklist.

Two ideas stand out. First, novelty is defined as embedding distance from the training set and surprise as information content under a listener expectation model, with KL deliberately excluded from novelty so that the two do not overlap by definition. Second, hidden-content surprise as "one model updates strongly, the other barely", and the "aha" as a drop in description length (echoing Schmidhuber's 2010 compression-progress theory), cannot be implemented, but names a class of phenomena no existing metric touches.

To apply it to generative-music evaluation, the next step is clear: pick several generative models, compute every computable metric, examine their correlation structure, and compare against human preference data such as Grötschla et al.'s (2025) tens of thousands of pairwise comparisons. A risk the authors themselves raise is also worth recording: used as RL rewards, these metrics may teach a model to inflate information content and embedding distance, which is randomness, not creativity. M-value is the component meant to prevent that, and it is precisely the hardest to measure.

Real-Time Arbitrary Resampling for Phase-Continuous Microtuning in Audio Streams

音訊串流中相位連續微分音調整的即時任意重取樣：以輸出驅動的分數位置遞增 q = (F_in/F_out)·p 同時處理音高比例與輸入輸出取樣率，兩點線性內插、拉式環形緩衝與保持讀取位置的音高漸變；唯一的模擬是 1 kHz 正弦在 4410 幀內上升 50 cents，影像旁瓣約 −26 dB

為什麼挑這篇：把整條錄音的音高微調幾個 cents（例如對齊 A=440 與 A=432 的版本）是很常見的需求。這篇把它寫成一個輸出驅動的重取樣器：每個輸出樣本按 q = (F_in/F_out)·p 在輸入上前進一段分數距離，取兩個相鄰樣本做線性內插，調音改變時保持目前讀取位置以維持相位連續。選它是因為它把「調音解析度、數值精度、內插品質、串流正確性」這四件常被混為一談的事分開講清楚，而且誠實地說線性內插不保證音質。要注意方法本身就是取樣器的變速播放（varispeed），作者也承認任意時間重取樣並不新；唯一的實驗是 1 kHz 正弦在 0.1 秒內上升 50 cents，影像雜訊約 −26 dB，遠遜於作者自己提到的 FIR 多相濾波器 >90 dB。Why this one: Retuning an entire recording by a few cents (say, to align versions at A=440 and A=432) is a common need. This paper writes it up as an output-driven resampler: each output sample advances a fractional distance q = (F_in/F_out)·p through the input, linearly interpolates two neighbouring samples, and preserves the current read position when the tuning changes so that phase stays continuous. I picked it because it cleanly separates four things that are often conflated (tuning resolution, numerical precision, interpolation quality and streaming correctness) and states honestly that linear interpolation guarantees no particular quality. Note that the method is essentially sampler varispeed, and the author concedes arbitrary-time resampling is not new; the only experiment is a 1 kHz sine rising 50 cents within 0.1 s, with imaging at about −26 dB, far worse than the >90 dB of the FIR polyphase filters the author mentions.

動機Motivation

情境很具體：一條錄好的音軌要對齊另一個調音基準，或要從同一個來源產生幾個音高略有差異的版本。這裡的「微分音調整」是指對整條串流乘上一個接近 1 的音高比例，不是對複音錄音裡的個別音符做修正，也不做音高偵測——來源與目標頻率都是使用者給定的參數。

作者要解決的是工程問題：當比例非常接近 1（例如 1 cent ≈ 1.000578），若堅持用有理數 L/M 表示，分子分母會變得很大，傳統「先升取樣 L 倍再降 M 倍」的作法就變得昂貴。他想示範可以不必真的產生那個中間取樣率，而是每個輸出樣本直接計算它對應的輸入位置，而且在串流中途改變比例時不產生相位跳動。

The scenario is concrete: a recorded track must be aligned with another tuning reference, or several slightly detuned versions must be produced from one source. "Microtuning" here means multiplying the whole stream by a pitch ratio close to 1, not correcting individual notes in a polyphonic recording, and no pitch detection is involved: source and target frequencies are user parameters.

The problem is an engineering one. When the ratio is very close to 1 (one cent is about 1.000578), insisting on a rational L/M representation produces large integers, making the classic upsample-by-L, downsample-by-M approach expensive. The author wants to show that the intermediate rate never needs to be materialized: each output sample computes its input position directly, and the ratio can change mid-stream without phase jumps.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

任意時間重取樣早就存在。 Smith & Gossett（1984）的頻寬受限內插可以在任意分數位置取值、也支援平滑變化的比例，作者自己也引用它並承認「任意時間重取樣本身並不新」。Farrow（1988）的可變延遲結構與 Laakso 等（1996）的分數延遲濾波器設計，是實作同一件事的標準工具。開源的 libsamplerate（Secret Rabbit Code）與 SoX 的重取樣器，都已提供可在串流中途改變比例的任意比例轉換。

本文最像的其實是取樣器的變速播放。 用一個分數相位累加器在樣本表中前進、以線性內插取值、改變步長就改變音高——這正是硬體與軟體取樣器、波表合成器幾十年來的標準作法（drop-sample 或線性內插的 varispeed）。本文的「輸出驅動、分數遞增、保持位置的比例更新」與它在演算法上是同一件事，差別只在於把它寫成音高比例與兩個取樣率的統一公式，並延續作者自己 2018 年 ISSPIT 論文的按需取樣想法。

它不是音高移位。 Rubber Band、相位聲碼器或 PSOLA 這類方法會保持長度只改音高；本文明確說音高與播放速度會一起變，長度變為原來的 1/p，要保持長度得另外處理。

Arbitrary-time resampling has long existed. Smith & Gossett's (1984) bandlimited interpolation evaluates at arbitrary fractional positions and supports smoothly varying ratios; the author cites it and concedes that arbitrary-time resampling is not inherently novel. Farrow's (1988) variable delay structure and Laakso et al.'s (1996) fractional-delay filter design are the standard tools for the same task. The open-source libsamplerate (Secret Rabbit Code) and SoX resamplers already offer arbitrary-ratio conversion with ratio changes mid-stream.

What this most resembles is sampler varispeed. Advancing through a sample table with a fractional phase accumulator, reading by linear interpolation, and changing pitch by changing the step is what hardware and software samplers and wavetable synthesizers have done for decades (drop-sample or linear-interpolation varispeed). The paper's output-driven, fractional-increment, position-preserving update is algorithmically the same thing; the difference is that it is written as one formula combining the pitch ratio with two sample rates, continuing the on-demand sampling idea of the author's own 2018 ISSPIT paper.

It is not pitch shifting. Methods such as Rubber Band, phase vocoders or PSOLA preserve duration while changing pitch; this paper states explicitly that pitch and playback speed change together, duration becomes 1/p of the original, and preserving it requires a separate process.

方法Method

核心公式。 令音高比例 p = f_target / f_source（或由 cents 給定，p = 2^(c/1200)），輸入位置遞增 q = (F_in / F_out)·p。比例不變時，第 n 個輸出樣本位於輸入座標 n·q；比例會變時改為逐樣本累加 x[n+1] = x[n] + q[n]。p = 1 時就是單純的取樣率轉換；F_in = F_out 時音高與速度一起改變。作者也給了數值例子：在 440 Hz 上升 1 cent 約是 0.254 Hz，而 0.01 Hz 約是 0.039 cents。

內插與頻寬。 預設用兩點線性內插：取位置兩側的樣本，按小數部分加權。作者反覆強調線性內插頻率響應不平坦、影像抑制不完美，也不能取代抗混疊濾波；升音高時輸入頻率乘上 p，超過輸出 Nyquist 的成分會混疊，必須另行濾波。

精度與有理數表示。 調音誤差只取決於實際使用的遞增 q̂ 與理想 q 的差，可換算成 1200·log2(q̂/q) cents。若想用整數，可選分母 B、取 A = round(q·B)，並檢查誤差是否在容差內；文中 3333/3332 只是示例。約分不會改善近似。

串流實作。 輸入存在長度為 2 的冪的環形緩衝，讀取位置逼近寫入位置時以阻塞式回呼向上游拉一塊資料；定期從位置座標扣掉整塊長度以避免長時間累積的數值漂移。調音改變時保持目前讀取位置不動，只改之後的遞增；漸變可選擇比例線性或 cents 線性——作者提醒兩者不同，分別內插分子分母也不會得到比例線性。

另一層：音名對應與正弦合成。 頻率與「音名＋八度＋殘餘 cents」之間的雙向換算是獨立的表示層，參考頻率與命名方式都是參數；直接正弦合成則以保留相位的相位累加器生成，頻率改變只改相位增量。

Core formula. With pitch ratio p = f_target / f_source (or from cents, p = 2^(c/1200)), the input-position increment is q = (F_in / F_out)·p. For a constant ratio, output sample n sits at input coordinate n·q; for a changing ratio the position accumulates per sample, x[n+1] = x[n] + q[n]. With p = 1 this is plain sample-rate conversion; with F_in = F_out, pitch and speed change together. A worked example: one cent up at 440 Hz is about 0.254 Hz, and 0.01 Hz is about 0.039 cents.

Interpolation and bandwidth. The default kernel is two-point linear interpolation, weighting the two neighbouring samples by the fractional part. The author repeatedly stresses that linear interpolation has a non-flat response and imperfect image suppression and is no substitute for an antialias filter; raising pitch multiplies input frequencies by p, and anything above the output Nyquist aliases unless filtered.

Precision and rational representation. Tuning error depends only on the difference between the realized increment q̂ and the ideal q, expressible as 1200·log2(q̂/q) cents. For integers, choose a denominator B, set A = round(q·B), and check the error against a tolerance; 3333/3332 is only an example, and reduction by a common divisor does not improve the approximation.

Streaming. Input sits in a power-of-two ring buffer; when the read position nears the write head, a blocking callback pulls a block from upstream, and whole block lengths are periodically subtracted from the position to avoid long-run drift. On a tuning change the read position is kept and only subsequent increments change; ramps can be ratio-linear or cents-linear, and the author notes these differ, and that ramping numerator and denominator separately is not ratio-linear.

Separate layers: note mapping and sine synthesis. Conversion between frequency and note name, octave and residual cents is a separate representation layer with the reference frequency and naming as parameters; direct sine synthesis uses a phase accumulator that retains phase, so a frequency change only alters the phase increment.

限制Limitations

實驗只有一個。 唯一的評估是模擬：1 kHz 正弦、F_in = 44.1 kHz，在 N = 4410 個輸出幀（約 0.1 秒）內以 cents 線性從 +0 上升到 +50 cents，然後對整段做 Hann 視窗 DFT 看頻譜。輸出取樣率沒有寫明，沒有任何真實音樂訊號、沒有聽測、沒有 THD+N 或 SNR 數字，也沒有和 libsamplerate、SoX 或任何 FIR 重取樣器比較。

結論的措辭超出證據。 文中說頻譜「以絕對清晰度」追蹤目標、「零頻率模糊」，並稱這是「實證證明」。但 50 cents 在 1 kHz 約是 29 Hz 的掃頻，而 4410 點 DFT 的頻率解析度約 10 Hz（假設輸出同為 44.1 kHz），對一段掃頻做單一 DFT 本來就會把能量攤在一段頻帶上，這張圖無法證明「零模糊」，也無法和有相位跳動的版本區分——論文沒有放對照組。同樣，「長時間座標漂移被完全消除」也沒有任何多小時串流的量測。

品質代價被自己的數字點出。 影像旁瓣約 −26 dB，而作者提到的 FIR 多相濾波器有 >90 dB 的阻帶抑制。對「微調幾個 cents」這種要求音質無損的應用，−26 dB 的影像雜訊在高頻豐富的錄音上很可能聽得到。論文說線性內插是「可接受時的低成本選項」，但沒有給出何時可接受的任何量測。

寫作重複且有斷層。 前半篇大量篇幅在反覆聲明「3332 與 3333 只是示例」「不構成保證」，後半篇第 X 節卻改用「絕對」「證明」的強語氣，兩者風格明顯不一致。參考文獻列了 Oster（1973）的「腦中的聽覺拍頻」，正文卻沒有引用，也沒有說明用途。

There is a single experiment. The only evaluation is a simulation: a 1 kHz sine at F_in = 44.1 kHz, ramped cents-linearly from +0 to +50 cents over N = 4410 output frames (about 0.1 s), then a Hann-windowed DFT of the whole segment. The output sample rate is not stated; there is no real music signal, no listening test, no THD+N or SNR figure, and no comparison with libsamplerate, SoX or any FIR resampler.

The wording of the conclusions exceeds the evidence. The text says the spectrum tracks the target "with absolute clarity" and "zero frequency-smearing", calling this "empirical proof". But 50 cents at 1 kHz is a sweep of about 29 Hz, while a 4410-point DFT resolves about 10 Hz (assuming a 44.1 kHz output); a single DFT over a sweep necessarily spreads energy across a band, so this figure can neither prove zero smearing nor distinguish the method from a version with phase jumps, and no such control is shown. Likewise, the claim that long-run coordinate drift is "entirely eliminated" has no multi-hour streaming measurement behind it.

The paper's own number exposes the quality cost. Image side-lobes sit near −26 dB, whereas the FIR polyphase filters the author mentions reach >90 dB of stopband rejection. For retuning by a few cents, an application that expects transparent quality, −26 dB imaging is likely audible on recordings rich in high frequencies. The paper calls linear interpolation a low-cost option "when its measured distortion is acceptable" but provides no measurement of when that is.

The writing is repetitive and uneven. The first half spends much space repeating that 3332 and 3333 are only illustrations and that nothing is guaranteed, while Section X switches to strong words such as "absolute" and "proof", an obvious change of register. The references list Oster (1973), "Auditory beats in the brain", which is never cited in the text or explained.

討論Discussion

這篇的貢獻主要在「規格書」層面，而不是演算法。它把一個常被寫得很混亂的問題拆成清楚的幾層：音高比例與 cents 的換算、比例與兩個取樣率合成單一遞增、遞增的數值精度與調音誤差的關係、內插核的品質、抗混疊的責任歸屬、串流緩衝與漂移、漸變軌跡的選擇。特別是「比例線性與 cents 線性不同」「分別漸變分子分母不等於比例線性」「約分不改善近似」這幾點，是實作者真的會踩到的坑。

對 MIR 研究者來說，它的實用場景是資料處理：例如把不同調音基準的錄音對齊到同一個參考頻率再做翻唱辨識或轉譜，或用小幅變速做資料增強。這時保持相位連續的串流重取樣確實方便，但大多數離線場景直接用 libsamplerate 或 SoX 的高品質模式即可，品質更好、而離線處理時計算成本不是瓶頸。本文的兩點內插只在極度在意每樣本計算量與確定性記憶體存取的即時嵌入式情境才有優勢。

要讓這篇的主張站得住，最需要的是三件事：在真實音樂上量測線性內插相對於 FIR 重取樣的失真與聽感差異；用短時頻譜（而非單一 DFT）對照有與沒有位置保持時的相位連續性；以及長時間串流的漂移量測。目前它更適合被讀成一份謹慎的工程筆記，而非經過驗證的方法論文。

The contribution is mainly at the specification level, not the algorithm. It splits a problem that is often described messily into clean layers: converting between pitch ratio and cents, combining the ratio and two sample rates into one increment, relating increment precision to tuning error, kernel quality, responsibility for antialiasing, stream buffering and drift, and the choice of ramp trajectory. Points such as "ratio-linear differs from cents-linear", "ramping numerator and denominator separately is not ratio-linear" and "reduction does not improve the approximation" are pitfalls implementers really hit.

For MIR researchers, the practical use is data processing: aligning recordings made at different tuning references before cover-song identification or transcription, or small speed changes for data augmentation. Phase-continuous streaming resampling is convenient there, but most offline cases are better served by the high-quality modes of libsamplerate or SoX, with better quality and no meaningful compute bottleneck offline. The two-point kernel pays off only in real-time embedded settings that care intensely about per-sample cost and deterministic memory access.

To make its claims hold, three things are needed most: measured distortion and perceptual differences of linear interpolation versus FIR resampling on real music; short-time spectra (rather than a single DFT) comparing phase continuity with and without position preservation; and drift measurements over long streams. For now it reads better as a careful engineering note than as a validated methods paper.
