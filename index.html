Singer-Informed Vocal Source Separation for Multi-Singer Music Mixtures

以歌手嵌入引導的多人聲部混音人聲分離

Jocelyn Xu, Minje Kim
arXiv:2608.14516 · cs.SD, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-14
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇的核心賣點——SI-SDR 從 0.33 dB 拉到 5.58 dB——聽起來像十幾倍的進步,但要注意 0.33 dB 是基準模型 Open-Unmix 被拿去對到「單一目標歌手」這把尺上量出來的分數;同一顆模型若換算對「兩位歌手混在一起的人聲」評分,其實有 15.88 dB,代表它把人聲從伴奏裡分得很乾淨,只是分不出哪個人聲是你要的。真正該看的數字是條件化模型彼此之間的差距:FiLM 版本目標人聲 3.39 dB,串接加雙重損失版本拉到 5.58 dB,這才是「哪種條件化方式有效」的證據,搭配 FAD 從 2.28 降到 0.37,兩個指標方向一致。Why this one: The headline number — SI-SDR climbing from 0.33 dB to 5.58 dB — sounds like an order-of-magnitude gain, but 0.33 dB is the baseline Open-Unmix scored against a single target singer it was never asked to isolate; scored against both singers' combined vocal, the same baseline gets 15.88 dB, meaning it cleanly separates vocals from accompaniment, just without knowing which voice you wanted. The number that actually matters is the gap between conditioning variants: FiLM reaches 3.39 dB on the target vocal, concatenation plus a dual loss term reaches 5.58 dB, and FAD drops from 2.28 to 0.37 in the same direction.

動機Motivation

現有的音樂人聲分離系統,不管是 Open-Unmix 這類基於時頻遮罩的模型,還是 Demucs 這種直接在原始波形上運作的編碼器-解碼器架構,都只把「人聲」當成單一分離目標:輸出的人聲軌是混音中所有歌手疊在一起的結果。這在獨唱情境下沒問題,但真實錄音裡二重唱、重唱很常見,多個歌手的音高與頻率會互相重疊,傳統模型無法把個別歌手分開,更遑論指定「只要某一位」。

作者把語音處理裡常見的目標語者萃取(target speaker extraction, TSE)概念搬過來:給一小段目標歌手的預錄音檔(enrollment)當條件,分離模型就只保留這位歌手,其餘歌手與伴奏一律當成干擾聲。他們點出三個難處:同時有乾淨獨唱與多人混音的資料集稀少、真實場景裡乾淨的單一歌手預錄音檔本來就難取得,以及歌聲在音高、音色、表現力上的變化比語音大得多。

Standard music vocal separation systems, whether mask-based ones like Open-Unmix or waveform encoder-decoder architectures like Demucs, treat "vocals" as a single target: the output vocal stem is whatever all singers together produced. That works for solo recordings, but duets and ensembles are common in real music, where multiple singers overlap in pitch and frequency and conventional models cannot isolate one singer from another, let alone extract a specific one on request.

The authors import target speaker extraction (TSE) from speech processing: a short enrollment recording of the target singer conditions the separation model to keep only that singer and treat everyone else, plus the accompaniment, as interference. They flag three obstacles: datasets pairing clean solo vocals with multi-singer mixtures are scarce, clean single-singer enrollment clips are hard to obtain in the wild, and singing varies more in pitch, timbre, and expression than speech does.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂人聲分離這條線上,Open-Unmix 靠時頻遮罩、Demucs 靠端到端波形建模,近期的 Moises-Light 與 Task-Aware Unified Source Separation 持續推進品質,但這些系統把人聲當成單一來源,MedleyVox、IdolSongsJP 這類多重唱分離(MSVS)基準資料集也只驗證「能不能把多個人聲切開」,切開之後哪一段屬於哪位歌手仍是盲的,選不出「這位」。

真正示範「用一小段登記音檔挑出目標對象」的是語音的目標語者萃取(TSE)家族:SpeakerBeam、VoiceFilter、SpEx+ 都是拿說話者嵌入去條件化分離網路,做法上不是對比學習就是從內部表徵取嵌入,再用特徵串接或 FiLM(feature-wise linear modulation)這類更靈活的方式把嵌入注入分離模型。

這篇論文老實說沒有提出新的分離機制或新的條件化手段——串接與 FiLM 都是 TSE 文獻裡已經在用的做法,骨幹分離網路直接沿用 Open-Unmix。它的貢獻是把這整套「登記音檔 + 嵌入條件化」的組合,從語音領域原封不動搬到歌聲分離,並且證明搬過來之後在二重唱場景真的能用。

On the music side, Open-Unmix relies on time-frequency masking and Demucs on end-to-end waveform modeling, with recent systems like Moises-Light and Task-Aware Unified Source Separation pushing quality further, but all of them treat vocals as one source. Benchmark datasets for multi-singer separation, MedleyVox and IdolSongsJP, only test whether singers can be split apart, not which split belongs to which singer — the output stays blind to identity.

The actual precedent for "use a short enrollment clip to pick a target" comes from speech: target speaker extraction (TSE) methods SpeakerBeam, VoiceFilter, and SpEx+ already condition a separation network on a speaker embedding, obtained via contrastive learning or from internal representations, and inject it through feature concatenation or the more flexible FiLM (feature-wise linear modulation).

This paper does not introduce a new separation mechanism or a new conditioning trick — concatenation and FiLM are both established TSE techniques, and the backbone separator is Open-Unmix unchanged. The contribution is porting the entire "enrollment clip plus embedding conditioning" recipe from speech to singing voice separation and showing it works in duet mixtures.

方法Method

分離模型分兩塊:一是歌手嵌入模型,用兩層 GRU(隱藏層 32 維)吃登記音檔的振幅頻譜圖,取最後一個隱藏狀態當固定維度的嵌入,訓練時用對比學習拉近同一歌手、拉遠不同歌手;二是以 Open-Unmix 為骨幹的分離網路,一樣吃振幅頻譜圖、輸出頻譜遮罩,但把歌手嵌入用兩種方式之一注入:沿時間重複後直接與輸入特徵串接,或是用 FiLM 對中間層特徵做逐元素的縮放與平移調變(FiLM(h, z) = γ(z)⊙h + β(z))。

訓練用 6 秒片段,Adam 最佳化器,學習率 1e-3,權重衰減 1e-5,主要損失是尺度不變訊噪比(SI-SDR),部分版本另外加一個監督殘餘訊號(其他歌手加伴奏)的雙重損失項。

The system has two parts: a singer embedding model — a two-layer GRU with hidden size 32 — that reads the enrollment clip's magnitude spectrogram and takes the final hidden state as a fixed-size embedding, trained contrastively so same-singer embeddings pull together and different-singer ones push apart; and an Open-Unmix-backbone separator that reads the mixture's magnitude spectrogram and predicts a spectral mask, injecting the singer embedding either by repeating it across time and concatenating with input features, or via FiLM, which scales and shifts intermediate features per element (FiLM(h, z) = γ(z)⊙h + β(z)).

Training uses 6-second segments, Adam at learning rate 1e-3 with weight decay 1e-5, and a scale-invariant SDR (SI-SDR) loss, with some variants adding a dual loss term that also supervises the residual signal (other singers plus accompaniment).

限制Limitations

論文自己承認的未來方向已經點出限制所在:目前只處理最多兩位歌手(二重唱),沒有驗證三人以上的合唱;登記音檔假設是乾淨、只含目標歌手的錄音,現實中要找到這種素材本身就是論文開頭點名的三個難題之一,等於方法論成立的前提還沒被方法本身解決。

測試集只有 559 首原始二重唱錄音,規模偏小。獨唱情境下基準模型(未條件化的 Open-Unmix)反而拿下最高分(人聲 17.80 dB、伴奏 16.68 dB)——這說明條件化機制在不需要挑選目標時沒有帶來好處,只是不需要用而已,但也代表這套機制目前無法在「不知道現場有幾位歌手」的通用場景裡自動判斷該不該啟用。

The paper's own future-work list points at where it falls short: only two-singer duets are handled, with no validation on ensembles of three or more; enrollment clips are assumed to be clean recordings containing only the target singer, which is exactly one of the three obstacles the paper opens by naming, so the method's own precondition remains unsolved by the method itself.

The test set covers only 559 original duet recordings, a modest scale. In the solo setting, the unconditioned Open-Unmix baseline scores highest (17.80 dB vocal, 16.68 dB background) — conditioning brings no benefit when there is nothing to select against, but it also means the system has no built-in way to decide whether to engage at all in a general setting where the number of singers present is unknown.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最值得討論的地方不是分離效果本身,而是它把「登記音檔式條件化」這套語音領域已經成熟的做法,幾乎原封不動地驗證了一次可以套用到歌聲上——這比發明新架構更接近工程驗證的性質。

它也暴露一個評估框架上的問題:把「未條件化的通用分離模型」當作目標歌手萃取任務的基準線,本質上是拿一個沒被要求做這件事的系統來比較,0.33 dB 到 5.58 dB 的落差有多少是「條件化真的有效」、多少只是「基準線的任務設定本來就不對」,論文沒有拆開來談。FAD 從基準線 2.28 降到最佳模型 0.37,是比 SI-SDR 更能說服人的證據,因為它衡量的是感知層面的音色貼近程度,而不只是訊號重建誤差。

The most discussion-worthy aspect here isn't the separation quality itself but the fact that this is essentially an engineering validation — porting an already-mature speech-domain technique, enrollment-conditioned extraction, onto singing with minimal modification, rather than a new architectural idea.

It also exposes a framing issue in the evaluation: using an unconditioned general-purpose separator as the baseline for a target-singer extraction task compares against a system that was never asked to do this job, and the paper never separates how much of the 0.33-to-5.58 dB gap reflects genuine conditioning benefit versus a mismatched baseline task. The FAD drop from 2.28 (baseline) to 0.37 (best model) is more persuasive than the SI-SDR numbers, since it captures perceptual timbral closeness rather than just signal-reconstruction error.

The MPB Corpus: A Dataset of Melody, Rhythm, Harmony, and Melody-Harmony Relationships in Brazilian Popular Music

MPB 語料庫:巴西流行音樂旋律、節奏、和聲與旋律和聲關係的資料集

Carlos de L. Almada, Hugo T. de Carvalho, Felipe D. Martins
arXiv:2608.13842 · cs.SD, cs.IR · 投稿submitted 2026-08-14
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文最紮實的證據不是「巴西流行音樂很多元」這種印象式描述,而是一個排列檢定:把十位作曲家的節奏字母(r-words)分布拿去做一萬次隨機排列,觀察到的統計量 R_obs = 1.072 沒有被任何一次隨機排列超過,p < 10^-4,把「作曲家風格差異不是隨機噪音」這個主張釘在數字上。CIEI(旋律穩定性指標)在十位作曲家間只落在 0.856 到 0.898 之間,MAI(旋律和聲貼合度)則從 0.582 拉開到 0.741,離散程度差了將近三倍,說明和聲層面的個人風格差異遠比旋律輪廓層面明顯——這是資料集本身算得出來的發現,不是作者自己宣稱的。Why this one: The strongest evidence here isn't an impressionistic claim about Brazilian music's diversity but a permutation test: shuffling the ten composers' rhythmic-letter (r-word) distributions 10,000 times, the observed statistic R_obs = 1.072 was never exceeded by any shuffle, giving p < 10^-4 and pinning down "composer style differences aren't random noise" with an actual number. CIEI (a melodic-stability index) spans only 0.856 to 0.898 across the ten composers, while MAI (melody-harmony anchoring) spreads from 0.582 to 0.741 — nearly three times the range — showing individual style diverges far more at the harmonic level than at the melodic-contour level, a finding the dataset itself computes rather than one the authors merely assert.

動機Motivation

巴西流行音樂(MPB)在二十世紀的巴西音樂與文化史裡佔有核心地位,但作者指出一個矛盾:對它的結構性、量化研究出奇地少。既有的流行音樂語料庫研究多半集中在和聲一項,而且幾乎只處理英語系流行樂(搖滾、流行、爵士、藍調);少數處理巴西音樂的研究也只挑單一面向下手——有的只看和聲、有的只看節奏,沒有人同時處理旋律輪廓、旋律節奏、和聲、以及旋律與和聲的互動關係這四個維度。

這篇論文要回答的問題是:哪些風格標記(節奏與旋律輪廓、和聲、以及和聲與旋律的互動)是這個音樂美學裡共享的規範,哪些又是偏離規範、進而構成個別作曲家風格的東西?作者選了十位作曲家(Tom Jobim、Ivan Lins、Chico Buarque、Edu Lobo、Caetano Veloso、Djavan、João Bosco、Milton Nascimento、Gilberto Gil、Rita Lee),時間橫跨 1955 到 1990 年代,MPB 的和聲複雜多變、旋律與和聲關係精細、節奏帶有巴西音樂特有的切分,正是值得系統性研究的對象。

Brazilian Popular Music (MPB) holds a central place in twentieth-century Brazilian musical and cultural history, yet the authors point to a paradox: there is a surprising lack of structural, quantitative research on it. Existing popular-music corpus studies concentrate almost entirely on harmony, and almost exclusively on anglophone popular music — rock, pop, jazz, blues. The handful of studies on Brazilian music each isolate a single dimension: some cover only harmony, others only rhythm, none the four together.

The paper's driving question is which stylistic markers — rhythmic and melodic contour, harmony, and the melody-harmony relationship — function as shared norms within this aesthetic, and which diverge from those norms to mark an individual composer's style. The authors select ten composers spanning 1955 to the 1990s (Tom Jobim, Ivan Lins, Chico Buarque, Edu Lobo, Caetano Veloso, Djavan, João Bosco, Milton Nascimento, Gilberto Gil, Rita Lee), whose harmony is sophisticated and chromatic, whose melody-harmony relationships are intricate, and whose rhythms carry syncopation distinctive of Brazilian genres.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

流行音樂語料庫研究這條線上,deClercq & Temperley(2011)、Mauch et al.(2015)、Serrà et al.(2012)、White & Quinn(2018)等人的工作幾乎都鎖定和聲一項,對象也幾乎都是英語系流行樂。拉丁美洲音樂這邊,Cano et al.(2021)做過哥倫比亞 bambuco、Rocamora et al.(2015)做過烏拉圭 candombe、Gómez-Marín et al.(2024)做過騷莎(salsa)。

專門處理巴西音樂的研究則是:Moss et al.(2020)只分析巴西 choro 的和聲,Maia et al.(2018、2019、2024)一系列研究只處理森巴(samba)的節奏,Davies et al.(2020)與 Fonseca et al.(2021)只看 maracatu 的節奏面向。

這篇論文與它們的差別很直接:前面所有工作都只挑一個維度下手,MPB Corpus 是第一個把旋律輪廓、旋律節奏、和聲、旋律和聲關係四個維度同時、對同一批 500 首作品做完整標註的資料集,而且自創了三套分析模型(和弦類型譜系 Genera of Chord Types、旋律過濾模型 Melodic Filtering Model、旋律和聲關係模型)去產生可比較的量化指標。

In the popular-music corpus literature, deClercq & Temperley (2011), Mauch et al. (2015), Serrà et al. (2012), and White & Quinn (2018) all concentrate on harmony alone and almost exclusively on anglophone repertoire. On Latin American music specifically, Cano et al. (2021) studied Colombian bambuco, Rocamora et al. (2015) Uruguayan candombe, and Gómez-Marín et al. (2024) salsa.

Work on Brazilian music itself is single-dimension too: Moss et al. (2020) covers only choro harmony, the Maia et al. (2018, 2019, 2024) line covers only samba rhythm, and Davies et al. (2020) and Fonseca et al. (2021) cover only maracatu rhythm.

The gap this paper fills is direct: every prior study picks one dimension, while the MPB Corpus is the first to annotate melodic contour, melodic rhythm, harmony, and melody-harmony relationships together across the same 500 pieces, backed by three original analytical models it introduces for the purpose — Genera of Chord Types, the Melodic Filtering Model, and a melody-harmony relationship model.

方法Method

和弦類型譜系(GCT)把和弦歸到 10 個「原型和弦」(大小三和弦與七和弦),再用三種變換——加音(ADD,如加九音、十一音、十三音)、替代(SUB,如三音換成四音)、變化音(ALT)——衍生出總共 161 種變體,分佈在最多六個世代裡,並用規則限制哪些組合合法。

旋律過濾模型(MFM)把音高濃縮成七種輪廓手勢(u 同音、P/p 級進上下行、A/a 琶音式上下行、S/s 大跳上下行),把節奏濃縮成 26 個字母組成的節奏字母表,對應以 12 個單位切分一拍內的起音時間點;由此衍生出兩個指標:CIEI(補償式音程經濟指數,量化旋律穩定度,0–1)與 CMI(反拍指數,量化切分程度,0–1)。

旋律和聲關係則靠和弦音階理論建立音符與和弦的層級關係,並用 NF 網(五圈同心圓 × 12 道半音方向、共 60 個位置的視覺化)呈現,搭配 MAI(旋律錨定指數,音符功能的加權平均,根音權重最高、變化張力音權重最低,正規化到 0–1)。整套標註流程裡,樂譜校對、MIDI 轉換、旋律切分、節奏標註、和聲分析、旋律和聲關係標註全部或部分靠人工完成,只有音高的字母化是自動化的,由單一標註者(第一作者)花了約五年完成十位作曲家、共 500 首作品的標註。

The Genera of Chord Types (GCT) sorts chords into 10 "protochords" (basic major/minor triads and seventh chords), then derives 161 total variants across up to six generations via three transformations — ADD (adding ninths, elevenths, thirteenths), SUB (substitution, e.g. swapping the third for the fourth), and ALT (chromatic alteration) — with rules constraining which combinations are valid.

The Melodic Filtering Model (MFM) reduces pitch to seven contour gestures (u for unison, P/p for stepwise up/down, A/a for arpeggio-range up/down, S/s for leap up/down) and rhythm to a 26-letter alphabet keyed to onset positions within a beat divided into 12 units, from which two metrics follow: CIEI (a compensated intervallic economy index measuring melodic stability, 0-1) and CMI (a countermetricity index measuring syncopation, 0-1).

Melody-harmony relationships use chord-scale theory to build a hierarchy between notes and chords, visualized as an "NF web" (five concentric circles by 12 chromatic rays, 60 positions total), summarized by MAI (a melodic anchoring index, a weighted average of note functions with the root weighted highest and altered tensions lowest, normalized to 0-1). The annotation pipeline — score proofing, MIDI conversion, melodic segmentation, rhythm labeling, harmonic analysis, and melody-harmony labeling — is manual except for the automated pitch-letter step, carried out by a single annotator (the first author) over roughly five years across the ten composers' 500 pieces.

限制Limitations

作者自己列出的限制相當坦白:十位作曲家幾乎全是男性,女性創作者(Gal Costa、Elis Regina、Dona Ivone Lara、Maysa、Joyce 等)被排除,原因是本研究只採計有正式出版樂譜的作曲家,而這些女性歌手的貢獻主要留存在演出而非出版樂譜裡——這代表資料集的取樣邏輯本身就系統性地偏向某一種創作角色。

標註流程裡有三個步驟(旋律切分、節奏標註、和聲分析)完全靠人工,作者提到嘗試過自動化節奏標註但受限於 MIDI 協定本身的限制而失敗;旋律切分規則也還沒標準化,是被作者點名優先要處理的一項。單一標註者五年才完成十人份,若要如作者規劃的擴大到約 50 位作曲家,標註產能會是明顯瓶頸。

The authors' own limitations are stated plainly: the ten composers are overwhelmingly male, with female artists (Gal Costa, Elis Regina, Dona Ivone Lara, Maysa, Joyce, and others) excluded because the study only includes composers with formally published scores, and these singers' contributions survive mainly through performance rather than published composition — meaning the dataset's sampling logic is itself systematically skewed toward one kind of creative role.

Three annotation steps — melodic segmentation, rhythm labeling, and harmonic analysis — remain entirely manual; the authors report that an attempt to automate rhythm labeling failed due to limitations in the MIDI protocol itself, and melodic segmentation rules are not yet standardized, which the authors flag as a priority to fix. A single annotator took roughly five years to cover ten composers, so annotation throughput will be a clear bottleneck for the stated plan to expand to around 50 composers.

討論Discussion

這篇論文的價值比較接近「建立可信賴的量化工具」而非「提出新發現」——GCT、MFM、MAI 這幾套模型本身就是可以拿去套用在其他音樂傳統上的方法論貢獻,論文特別提到這是這些原本以葡萄牙文發表的分析模型第一次有英文版本,這件事本身被作者當成一項貢獻列出來,值得留意的是這代表語言隔閡曾經讓這套方法論在國際 MIR 社群裡不可見。

排列檢定證明作曲家之間風格有統計上的差異(p < 10^-4),但這只回答「有沒有差異」,回答不了「差異的大小在音樂上算不算重要」——CIEI 從 0.856 到 0.898 只有 0.042 的全距,統計顯著不代表這個差距在聽感上站得住腳,論文對這一點沒有深入討論。作者規劃的控制組語料庫(各 50 首 choro、森巴、爵士)完成後,才真正能回答「MPB 作曲家彼此的差異」和「MPB 與其他曲風的差異」哪個比較大這個更有意思的問題。

This paper's value sits closer to building a trustworthy quantitative toolkit than to reporting a new finding — GCT, MFM, and MAI are methodological contributions that could transfer to other musical traditions, and the paper notes this is the first English-language publication of analytical models originally written up in Portuguese, itself listed as a contribution, which is worth flagging: it means a language barrier had kept this methodology invisible to the international MIR community.

The permutation test establishes that composers differ statistically (p < 10^-4), but that only answers whether a difference exists, not whether its size is musically meaningful — CIEI spans only 0.042 (0.856 to 0.898) across all ten composers, and statistical significance doesn't by itself establish that this gap is audible or musically salient, a point the paper doesn't dig into. The more interesting question — whether MPB composers differ more from each other or from other genres — will only be answerable once the planned control corpus (50 pieces each of choro, samba, and jazz) is complete.

VIOLET: High-Fidelity Violin Synthesis with Techniques and Dynamics

VIOLET:具技巧與動態控制的高擬真度小提琴合成

Baotong Tian, Cynthia Lu, Vincent K.M. Cheung, Ting-Kang Wang, Jonathan Churchill, Zhiyao Duan
arXiv:2608.07944 · cs.SD, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-08
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇的說服力來自把「技巧辨識準確率」跟「聽感評分」分開來看:VIOLET 在顫音、泛音、圓滑奏這類長音技巧上辨識準確率是 100%,撥弦(pizzicato)92.9% 對商用音源庫 86.2%、頓弓(staccato)95.7% 對 85.2%,兩項還贏過商用系統;但主觀聽測裡音質與動態貼合度兩項,VIOLET 顯著輸給商用 VI(p<0.05、p<0.01)。也就是說,VIOLET 在「有沒有做出正確的演奏動作」這件事上追平甚至超車,但在「聽起來夠不夠真」這件事上還沒追上——論文用不同統計檢定把這兩把尺分開報告,沒有含糊帶過。動態控制的效果最乾脆:拿掉條件化後動態相關係數只有 0.025,加回去之後跳到 0.631,是全文唯一一個「有沒有加這個機制」差距達到量級的數字。Why this one: The persuasive part is that the paper separates "did it perform the right gesture" from "does it sound convincing." VIOLET hits 100% identification accuracy on long-note techniques like trill, harmonic, and slur legato, and even beats the commercial virtual instrument on pizzicato (92.9% vs. 86.2%) and staccato (95.7% vs. 85.2%). But in subjective listening tests, VIOLET scores significantly lower than the commercial VI on both audio quality (p<0.05) and dynamics matching (p<0.01) — two different yardsticks the paper keeps separate with distinct statistical tests rather than blurring together. The dynamics-conditioning result is the cleanest number in the paper: the correlation between synthesized loudness and the input dynamics curve is 0.025 without conditioning and jumps to 0.631 with it — the one place where the gap is an order of magnitude.

動機Motivation

神經音樂演奏合成過去幾乎都繞著鋼琴打轉,原因是鋼琴的表現力主要集中在觸鍵瞬間(onset),之後音符的走向相對固定。小提琴不是這樣:每個音符從發聲到結束,音高、音量、頻譜內容與時間包絡都持續被演奏者塑形,不是敲一下鍵就結束。

作者也點出現況的實務問題:專業音樂製作現在仍高度仰賴取樣音源庫與虛擬樂器(VI),這些音源庫儲存成本高,而且需要大量人工程式設計才能做出細膩的演奏變化。他們借用歌聲合成(如 TechSinger)與 TTS 領域已經驗證過的做法——同時對離散屬性(演奏技巧種類)與連續時間軌跡(動態)做條件化——把這個思路搬到小提琴合成上,提出 VIOLET:一個對演奏技巧與動態都有顯式控制的潛在擴散(latent-diffusion)合成框架,並同時釋出一個新資料集 CSV-TD,含 39 小時 48kHz 的小提琴演奏音訊,附帶 MIDI、技巧與動態曲線標註。

Neural music performance synthesis has mostly orbited around piano, because piano's expressiveness is concentrated at the note onset and the note's trajectory afterward is largely fixed. Violin is different: pitch, amplitude, spectral content, and the temporal envelope are all continuously shaped by the player throughout each note's duration, not settled at a single strike.

The authors also point to a practical gap: professional music production still relies heavily on sample libraries and virtual instruments (VIs), which carry large storage costs and demand labor-intensive manual programming to produce nuanced performance variation. They borrow an approach already validated in singing voice synthesis (TechSinger) and TTS — conditioning jointly on discrete attributes (technique) and a continuous temporal trajectory (dynamics) — and bring it to violin, proposing VIOLET: a latent-diffusion framework with explicit control over technique and dynamics, alongside a new dataset, CSV-TD, with 39 hours of 48 kHz violin audio annotated with MIDI, techniques, and dynamics curves.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

鋼琴這條線上,既有系統多半用 CNN 或 Transformer 做譜到音訊的映射、DDSP 式的分層合成、或狀態空間模型,搭配 MAESTRO、ATEPP 這類資料集,但這些方法的假設是「表現力主要在音符起音就決定了」,搬到小提琴上並不成立。

小提琴與弦樂合成這邊,業界實務靠的是拼接式合成(concatenative synthesis)與商用虛擬樂器(VI)——後者把大量錄好的取樣對應到密集的按鍵切換點上,本質上是資料庫查表而非生成。神經網路方法裡最直接的前作是 ViolinDiff(Kim et al., 2025),用兩階段擴散框架先預測音高彎曲(pitch-bend)的走勢、再合成梅爾頻譜,證明了顯式建模音高輪廓能改善小提琴合成品質,但論文指出這類方法「音質仍然比不上取樣音源庫,而且缺乏對演奏技巧與動態的精細顯式控制」。

VIOLET 與 ViolinDiff 的差別很明確:不是換一種擴散做法,而是新增了技巧與動態這兩條可以獨立調節的顯式條件通道,並且用 classifier-free guidance 讓這兩條通道在推論時能分開加權。作者也自陳這是「第一個同時對音符、演奏技巧與連續動態做時間對齊條件化」的音樂生成系統。

On the piano side, existing systems mostly map score to audio via CNN or Transformer architectures, DDSP-style hierarchical synthesis, or state-space models, trained on datasets like MAESTRO and ATEPP — all premised on expressiveness being decided mostly at note onset, an assumption that breaks down for violin.

On the string-synthesis side, industry practice relies on concatenative synthesis and commercial virtual instruments (VIs), which map large sample libraries to dense keyswitches — essentially database lookup rather than generation. The most direct neural precedent is ViolinDiff (Kim et al., 2025), a two-stage diffusion framework that predicts pitch-bend contours before synthesizing a mel-spectrogram, demonstrating that explicit pitch-contour modeling improves violin synthesis quality; but the paper notes such methods "still suffer from lower audio quality compared to sample-based libraries and lack explicit, fine-grained control over playing techniques and dynamics."

VIOLET's difference from ViolinDiff is specific: not a different diffusion recipe, but two new, independently adjustable explicit conditioning channels for technique and dynamics, combined with classifier-free guidance that lets these channels be weighted separately at inference. The authors describe VIOLET as "the first music generative system that takes time-aligned conditioning of notes, playing techniques and continuous dynamics."

方法Method

音訊表徵用微調過的 DACVAE(Descript Audio Codec 的 VAE 版本),在小提琴錄音上微調解碼器,潛在幀率為 25Hz(每 40 毫秒一幀),微調後對顫音音符的高頻諧波細節重建更準。

三路控制訊號經過時間對齊後同時輸入:MIDI 音符(以鋼琴捲簾的二值矩陣表示,音域涵蓋小提琴的 G3 到 A7)、演奏技巧(12 種技巧、音符層級的二值矩陣,每個技巧持續時間跟著對應音符走)、以及動態(從 MIDI 的 CC1 控制器值取出,正規化到 0–1 並做零階保持插值,產生逐幀對齊的分段常數動態曲線)。骨幹是加了 AdaLN(自適應層正規化)的 DiT(Diffusion Transformer),三種條件各自有獨立的嵌入器,最終調變參數是全域(來自時間步)與各條件局部貢獻的加總,套用在自注意力與前饋層上。

訓練目標是修正流(rectified flow):z_t 是 z0、z1 的線性內插,損失是預測速度場與真實位移的均方誤差;訓練時會隨機把各條件替換成一個學到的空嵌入,做組合式的無分類器引導(compositional classifier-free guidance),推論時可以分別調整技巧引導權重與動態引導權重,同時保持 MIDI 條件全程有效。長篇生成用 50% 重疊的滑動視窗,在時域用 Hann 窗做重疊相加。骨幹規模是 12 層、12 個注意力頭、隱藏維度 768,兩張 A100 訓練 10 萬步、批次 32(用梯度累積),約四天;推論時在單張 A100 上生成 10 秒音訊要 2.3 秒(RTF=0.23),用 30 步 Euler 積分,推論引導權重 w_tech = w_dyn = 1。

Audio is represented via a DACVAE (a VAE variant of the Descript Audio Codec) with its decoder fine-tuned on violin recordings, giving a 25 Hz latent frame rate (one frame per 40 ms) and better reconstruction of the high-frequency harmonic detail in vibrato notes.

Three time-aligned control signals feed in together: MIDI notes as a binary piano-roll spanning the violin's playable range (G3 to A7), 12 playing techniques as a note-level binary piano-roll where each technique's duration matches its note, and dynamics extracted from the MIDI CC1 controller, min-max normalized to [0,1] with zero-order-hold interpolation to produce a frame-aligned, piecewise-constant curve. The backbone is a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) with AdaLN, with separate embedders per condition; the final modulation parameters sum a global (timestep-derived) term and each condition's local contribution, applied to both self-attention and feed-forward layers.

Training uses a rectified-flow objective — z_t linearly interpolates z0 and z1, and the loss is the squared error between predicted and true velocity — with each condition randomly replaced by a learned null embedding during training to enable compositional classifier-free guidance, which at inference lets technique and dynamics guidance weights be tuned independently while MIDI stays active throughout. Long-form generation uses 50%-overlapping windows, overlap-added with Hann windows in the time domain. The backbone is 12 blocks, 12 attention heads, 768 hidden dimensions, trained for 100,000 steps on 2 A100 GPUs at batch size 32 (with gradient accumulation) over about four days; inference takes 2.3 seconds to generate 10 seconds of audio on one A100 (RTF=0.23), using 30 Euler steps with guidance weights w_tech = w_dyn = 1.

限制Limitations

訓練資料裡真正同時有技巧與動態標註的只有 CSV-TD,而這批資料的音訊是用商用虛擬樂器(VI)算圖算出來的,標註時的技巧指派也不是照音樂性去設計,而是用「音符長短」的機率式啟發法決定(短音配撥弦、頓弓、泛音,長音配圓滑、顫音、泛音);換句話說,訓練訊號裡最完整的那一塊本身就帶著合成資料的偏差。

測試集同樣是合成的,論文自己承認這代表評估「可能偏向本來就在合成資料上訓練的模型」,結果應該被理解成「有沒有把輸入 MIDI 的時間與音高對準」這種基本正確性的量測,而不是「聽起來像不像真人演奏」的證據。真實錄音資料(MOSA、MUSC 共約 49.8 小時)雖然加進訓練,但論文自陳「動態與起音音高 F1 只有小幅改善」,推測是這批真實資料規模仍不夠大、錄音條件也比較吵。主觀聽測裡,VIOLET 在音質與動態貼合度兩項顯著輸給商用 VI(p<0.05、p<0.01),距離取代商用音源庫還有落差。

Among the training data, only CSV-TD carries both technique and dynamics annotations, but its audio was rendered through a commercial virtual instrument, and technique assignment during that rendering used a duration-based probabilistic heuristic (short notes get pizzicato/staccato/harmonic, long notes get legato/trill/harmonic) rather than musically deliberate choices — meaning the most complete slice of the training signal already carries synthetic-data bias.

The test set is likewise synthetic, and the paper itself acknowledges this "may favor models trained primarily on synthetic data," so results should be read as measuring basic correctness — pitch and timing alignment with the input MIDI — rather than evidence of sounding like a real performance. Real recordings (MOSA and MUSC, about 49.8 hours combined) were added to training, but the paper reports only "slight improvement" in dynamics and onset-pitch F1, attributed to the real corpora still being limited in scale and noisier in recording condition. In subjective listening tests, VIOLET scores significantly below the commercial VI on both audio quality (p<0.05) and dynamics matching (p<0.01), leaving a real gap before it could replace a commercial sample library.

討論Discussion

VIOLET 值得討論的地方在於它把「能不能執行對的演奏動作」跟「聽起來夠不夠真」這兩件事乾脆地分開評估,而不是把兩者混在一個總分裡模糊帶過:多技巧片段的技巧清晰度上顯著贏過 ViolinDiff(p<0.001),單一技巧的辨識準確率甚至在撥弦、頓弓上超過商用 VI,但音質與動態貼合度在統計上仍然輸給商用系統。

這代表擴散模型加顯式技巧/動態條件這條路,目前解決的是「控制力」的問題,還沒解決「音色說服力」的問題,兩者未必用同一種模型容量或同一批資料就能一起補齊。另一個值得注意的地方是動態相關係數從無條件化的 0.025 跳到有條件化的 0.631——這個對照組(w/o Cond)刻意設計成跟 ViolinDiff 的輸入條件對齊,是全文少數乾淨的消融實驗,證明「動態曲線有沒有被顯式建模」造成的差距,而不是被訓練資料或架構其他部分的差異混淆。

What's worth discussing about VIOLET is that it keeps "can it execute the right playing gesture" and "does it sound convincing" as separate, explicitly reported axes rather than folding both into one blurred score: on multi-technique clips it beats ViolinDiff significantly on technique clarity (p<0.001), and on single-technique identification it even beats the commercial VI on pizzicato and staccato, yet it still loses to the commercial system on audio quality and dynamics matching in statistical terms.

That suggests diffusion plus explicit technique/dynamics conditioning currently solves the controllability problem, not yet the timbral-conviction problem, and the two may not be closed by the same model capacity or the same training data. Also worth noting: the dynamics correlation jumps from 0.025 (unconditioned) to 0.631 (conditioned) — this ablation (w/o Cond) is deliberately set up to match ViolinDiff's input conditions, making it one of the paper's few clean ablations, isolating the gap caused specifically by explicit dynamics modeling rather than confounding it with other architectural or data differences.
