Small Data Generative AI for Culturally Specific Artistic Creation: A Case Study on the Sonic Space of sarīr

以小資料生成式 AI 做文化特定的藝術創作：波斯書法蘆葦筆聲 sarīr 的個案——自錄 220 分鐘、切成 1787 段 6 秒單筆畫音訊，訓練四種 RAVE 設定與頻譜圖版 StyleGAN2（FID 9.37），再用 RAVE 風格轉換清掉 StyleGAN2 重建出來的偽影，並對照三個文字轉音訊模型的完全失敗

Arshia Sobhan, Paul Triana, Philippe Pasquier
arXiv:2611.11374 · cs.SD; cs.CY · 投稿submitted 2026-11-08
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：sarīr 是波斯書法蘆葦筆劃過紙面的聲音，在伊朗文化裡有名字、有詩意，卻幾乎不存在於任何訓練資料。作者先用 AudioLDM2、Stable Audio Open、AudioGen 三個文字轉音訊模型試各種提示詞，產出全是無關聲音；再自錄 220 分鐘、1787 段 6 秒片段，從頭訓練 RAVE（四種設定，每種單張 H100 約 11 小時）與在 1024×1024 頻譜圖上的 StyleGAN2（FID 9.37）。選它是因為它把「大模型對非西方素材的盲區」做成一個具體、可聽的例子，而且 RAVE 當 StyleGAN2 後處理濾波器這招有實用價值。要注意：除了一個影像 FID 之外沒有任何量化或聽測評估，「捕捉到細緻音色」全是作者自己聽的判斷；對照組也只給大模型文字提示，沒試過微調或音訊提示。Why this one: sarīr is the sound of a Persian calligraphy reed pen moving across paper; it has a name and a poetic meaning in Iranian culture, yet is practically absent from any training data. The authors first prompt three text-to-audio models (AudioLDM2, Stable Audio Open, AudioGen) with many prompt variants and get only unrelated sounds; they then record 220 minutes, cut into 1787 six-second clips, and train RAVE from scratch (four configurations, about 11 hours each on one H100) plus a StyleGAN2 on 1024×1024 spectrograms (FID 9.37). I picked it because it turns "large models are blind to non-Western material" into a concrete, audible example, and using RAVE as a post-processing filter for StyleGAN2 is a practically useful trick. Caveat: apart from one image FID there is no quantitative or listening-test evaluation; "captures the subtle timbre" is entirely the authors' own listening judgment, and the baseline gives the large models only text prompts, never fine-tuning or audio prompts.

動機Motivation

作者的出發點是對大型生成模型的兩層批評。第一層是常見的：作者性被稀釋、驚喜感降低、得把默會的藝術意圖翻成文字提示、美學同質化、資料抓取的倫理。第二層是文化：文字轉圖已被多次指出會複製對非西方文化的刻板印象，作者推論，到了承載大量默會知識與身體技法的「特定藝術形式」，問題只會更嚴重。

sarīr（صریر）被選作試金石。它是書法蘆葦筆沾墨劃過紙面時的細微吱嘎共鳴，在長筆畫時加強；波斯文裡這個詞本身就帶有「筆在紙上歌唱」的意思，論文也連到魯米《瑪斯納維》開篇的「聽那蘆葦訴說」。從聲音生態學的角度，它是一種 soundmark——只在特定社群裡被辨認與珍視的聲音。作者指出目前沒有任何 sarīr 資料集或系統性研究。

論文的主張是 small-data 路線：由藝術家自己錄製、挑選、塑形資料，並只在這份資料上從頭訓練，而不是去改造大型預訓練模型。作者把資料策展本身視為一種「文化對齊」的勞動。第一作者受過傳統波斯書法訓練，論文明白說它不想提出通用框架，只想證明這條路對文化特定素材可行。

The starting point is a two-layer critique of large generative models. The first layer is familiar: diluted authorship, less surprise, the need to translate tacit artistic intent into text prompts, aesthetic homogenisation, and data-scraping ethics. The second is cultural: text-to-image models have repeatedly been shown to reproduce stereotypes of non-Western cultures, and the authors reason that for specific art forms carrying tacit knowledge and embodied technique the problem should be worse.

sarīr (صریر) is chosen as the test case. It is the fine creaking resonance of an inked reed pen drawn across paper, intensifying along elongated strokes; the Farsi word itself means roughly "the singing of the pen on paper", and the paper links it to the opening of Rumi's Masnavi ("Listen to the reed"). In acoustic-ecology terms it is a soundmark, a sound recognised and valued within one community. The authors note that no sarīr dataset or systematic study exists.

The paper argues for a small-data route: the artist records, selects and shapes the data, and models are trained from scratch on that data alone rather than adapting large pretrained models. Dataset curation is framed as an act of cultural alignment. The first author has formal training in traditional Persian calligraphy, and the paper explicitly says it does not aim at a generalisable framework, only at showing that this route is feasible for culturally specific material.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

small-data 的理論來源。 論文主要依靠 Vigliensoni、Perry 與 Fiebrink 的小資料藝術創作論點（小資料讓藝術家能反覆調整資料與架構），以及 Abuzuraiq & Pasquier、Bryan-Kinns 等人關於個人化與低資源 AI 的論述。這條線更早的源頭是 Fiebrink 的 Wekinator：用少量示範即時訓練模型、讓創作者與模型互動。本文是把同一種精神從「映射控制」搬到「生成音色」。

它實際用的兩個系統都不新。 RAVE（Caillon & Esling 2021）本來就是設計給單一音色、數小時規模語料的即時神經合成器，IRCAM 的 nn~ 外掛讓它能在 Max/MSP 當樂器演奏——本文的 RAVE 部分基本上就是 RAVE 的標準用法，套在一個新的語料上。把影像 GAN 拿去生成頻譜圖再重建音訊也有前例：SpecGAN（Donahue 等 2019）、GANSynth（Engel 等 2019）與 DrumGAN（Nistal 等 2020）都處理過頻譜圖生成，以及相位遺失造成的重建偽影；本文沒有引用這些前例，而是把偽影當成自己的觀察。

對照的大模型。 AudioLDM2、Stable Audio Open 與 AudioGen 被選為基準，作者指出它們都以英文、偏西方的資料訓練（AudioSet、AudioCaps、VGGSound、WavCaps 等）。

本文的差別不在技術，而在於把「文化專門知識」放進資料設計的每一步（什麼算一個 sarīr、哪些變化可以收、哪些增強會破壞聲音），並用同一個素材把大模型和小模型並排。

Where small-data comes from. The paper leans mainly on Vigliensoni, Perry and Fiebrink's argument for small-data artistic creation (small corpora let artists iterate on data and architecture), and on Abuzuraiq & Pasquier and Bryan-Kinns et al. on personalisation and low-resource AI. An earlier root of this line is Fiebrink's Wekinator: training models live from a few demonstrations so the creator interacts with the model. This paper carries the same spirit from mapping and control over to timbre generation.

Neither system it uses is new. RAVE (Caillon & Esling 2021) was designed precisely as a real-time neural synthesiser for a single timbre and a corpus of a few hours, and IRCAM's nn~ plugin lets it be played as an instrument in Max/MSP; the RAVE part of this paper is essentially standard RAVE use on a new corpus. Using an image GAN to generate spectrograms and then reconstructing audio also has precedent: SpecGAN (Donahue et al. 2019), GANSynth (Engel et al. 2019) and DrumGAN (Nistal et al. 2020) all dealt with spectrogram generation and with artefacts from lost phase. The paper cites none of these and presents the artefact as its own observation.

The large-model baselines. AudioLDM2, Stable Audio Open and AudioGen are chosen; the authors note that all are trained on English, Western-leaning data (AudioSet, AudioCaps, VGGSound, WavCaps and so on).

The difference here is not technical. It lies in putting cultural expertise into each step of data design (what counts as one sarīr, which variations to keep, which augmentations would break the sound) and in setting large and small models side by side on the same material.

方法Method

大模型基準。 提示詞包括「Persian calligraphy sarīr」、「the sound of a reed pen writing in Persian calligraphy」、更描述性的「the creaking sound of a reed pen…」，並把 Persian 換成 Arabic 擴大搜尋。三個模型產出的是無意義的人聲與歌聲、音樂紋理、機械噪音、打字機聲等；少數樣本有一點鉛筆快速劃紙的痕跡，但沒有蘆葦筆的音色、壓力調變與緩慢長筆畫。這部分沒有任何量化指標，完整提示詞與音檔放在專案網頁。

資料集。 以伊朗主流的 nastaliq 書體為依據，錄的不是完整字母，而是組成字母的基本筆畫動作；一張參考紙從傳統筆畫漸進到實驗性用法（把筆當發聲樂器）。變因包括五支不同筆幅的蘆葦筆、手壓與速度、筆畫形狀。比較了動圈心型麥克風、立體聲現場錄音機與接觸式麥克風，依比較聆聽與頻譜分析選了心型麥克風。共 220 分鐘，做基本降噪與音量正規化，依最長筆畫切成 6 秒、每段一個筆畫，最後 1787 段。

訓練。 RAVE 用官方程式庫訓練 v1、v2、v2_small、v2+prior 四種設定，採用建議的資料增強，依對抗階段的驗證損失選檢查點，每種在單張 H100 上約 11 小時；作者認為 v2 與 v2+prior 最好，並透過 IRCAM 的 Max/MSP 外掛即時操作潛空間。StyleGAN2 把每段 6 秒音訊轉成正規化的 1024×1024 頻譜圖，在兩張 H100 上以 batch 16 訓練 800 kimg；第一次訓練出現模式崩潰，改用輕量的音訊層增強（平移靜音段、小幅隨機增益）後穩定，依最低驗證 FID 選模型（9.37）。

兩個模型的串接。 StyleGAN2 頻譜圖重建成音訊後有一個原始錄音裡沒有的不自然音色成分，作者歸因於相位遺失，於是把它送進 RAVE 的風格轉換模式當後處理濾波器。另外在 StyleGAN2 潛空間連續插值、把逐步變化的頻譜圖幀串接起來，合成任意長度的音訊。

Large-model baseline. Prompts included "Persian calligraphy sarīr", "the sound of a reed pen writing in Persian calligraphy" and the more descriptive "the creaking sound of a reed pen…", with Persian swapped for Arabic to widen the search. The three models produced gibberish speech and singing, musical textures, mechanical noise, typewriter sounds and so on; a few samples carried faint traces of a pencil moving quickly over paper, but none had the reed pen's timbre, pressure modulation or slow elongated strokes. There is no quantitative metric for this part; full prompts and audio are on the project webpage.

Dataset. Grounded in nastaliq, the dominant style in Iran, the recordings are not whole letters but the basic stroke gestures from which letters are built; a reference sheet moves gradually from traditional strokes to experimental uses (the pen as a sound-making instrument). Variables include five reed pens of different nib widths, hand pressure and speed, and stroke form. A dynamic cardioid microphone, a stereo field recorder and a contact microphone were compared, and the cardioid was chosen by comparative listening and spectral analysis. The total is 220 minutes, with basic noise reduction and amplitude normalisation, cut into 6-second clips (the longest stroke), one stroke per clip, giving 1787 clips.

Training. RAVE is trained from the official repository in four configurations (v1, v2, v2_small, v2+prior) with the recommended augmentation, checkpoints chosen by validation loss in the adversarial phase, about 11 hours each on one H100; the authors judge v2 and v2+prior best and play the latent space in real time through IRCAM's Max/MSP plugin. For StyleGAN2, each 6-second clip becomes a normalised 1024×1024 spectrogram, trained on two H100s for 800 kimg at batch size 16; the first run mode-collapsed, which a lightweight audio-level augmentation (shifting silent regions, small random gains) fixed, and the model is chosen by lowest validation FID (9.37).

Chaining the two models. StyleGAN2 spectrograms reconstructed to audio carry an unnatural timbral component absent from the recordings, which the authors attribute to lost phase, so they pass them through RAVE's style-transfer mode as a post-processing filter. They also interpolate continuously in StyleGAN2's latent space and concatenate the evolving spectrogram frames to synthesise audio of any length.

限制Limitations

評估幾乎全是作者自己聽。 「RAVE 可靠地學到 sarīr 的細緻音色」「v2 與 v2+prior 表現較好」「StyleGAN2 產出高品質且多樣的頻譜圖」都沒有聽測、沒有 FAD 之類的音訊指標，也沒有和原始錄音做任何距離比較。唯一的數字是 FID 9.37，但它是在頻譜圖影像上算的，而且論文給這個指標的引用是 [6]（Epstein 等的藝術與生成式 AI 綜論），不是提出 FID 的 Heusel 等人，看起來是引用錯誤。

對照組不對等。 大模型只拿到文字提示；論文沒試過任何一種「給大模型看 sarīr」的作法，例如用這 220 分鐘去微調 Stable Audio Open，或用音訊提示／風格轉換。所以實驗能說明的是「大模型的零樣本文字生成碰不到 sarīr」，而不是「小資料從頭訓練優於大模型」——作者自己也把混合式微調列為未來工作。

泛化範圍。 只有一個聲音素材、一位錄音者兼評估者，作者也坦言不追求可推廣的框架。sarīr 是單一音源、短事件、紋理型的聲音，正好落在 RAVE 最擅長的範圍；這個成功能否推到有旋律、節奏結構的文化特定音樂，論文沒有證據。

Evaluation is almost entirely the authors' own listening. "RAVE reliably learns the subtle timbre of sarīr", "v2 and v2+prior performed best" and "StyleGAN2 produces high-quality, diverse spectrograms" come with no listening test, no audio metric such as FAD and no distance comparison against the original recordings. The only number is FID 9.37, computed on spectrogram images, and the paper cites it as [6] (Epstein et al.'s overview of art and generative AI) rather than Heusel et al., who introduced FID; this looks like a citation error.

The baseline is not like-for-like. The large models get only text prompts; the paper tries no way of showing them sarīr, such as fine-tuning Stable Audio Open on the same 220 minutes or using audio prompts or style transfer. So the experiment shows that zero-shot text generation from large models cannot reach sarīr, not that small-data training from scratch beats large models. The authors themselves list hybrid fine-tuning as future work.

Scope. There is one sound material and one person who both recorded and judged it, and the authors openly disclaim generalisability. sarīr is a single-source, short-event, texture-like sound, exactly where RAVE is strongest; the paper offers no evidence on whether this success carries over to culturally specific music with melodic or rhythmic structure.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的地方是把一個常被抽象討論的問題（大模型的文化盲區）縮到一個可以聽的具體點：連「波斯書法的筆聲」這種有名字、有文獻的聲音，三個開源文字轉音訊模型都完全產不出來。比起廣泛的偏見統計，這種細粒度的個案確實更能說明「沒被收進語料」是什麼意思，作者在討論裡也正是這樣主張的。

技術上最可帶走的是 RAVE 當後處理濾波器：StyleGAN2 的頻譜圖在重建時因相位遺失出現不自然的音色成分，把它丟進同一個語料訓練的 RAVE 做風格轉換，因為輸入已在 RAVE 學過的音色範圍內，RAVE 會保留結構、去掉偽影。這等於用一個領域內的神經聲碼器取代 Griffin-Lim 類的相位重建，論文沒有說原本用什麼重建，但這個組合對其他頻譜圖 GAN 也適用。另一個是 StyleGAN2 的潛空間插值再串接，能產出任意長度、資料裡沒有的時間軌跡，補足了每段只有 6 秒的限制。

但要小心把結論讀太大。這篇證明的是「為一個窄素材自建資料、用現成的小型架構從頭訓練，可以做出藝術上可用的樂器」，這在 RAVE 社群其實是常態。「小資料優於大模型」需要一個有微調組、有聽測的對照才站得住。作者提到的訓練門檻（11 小時一種設定、需要除錯能力）也提醒：這條路目前仍偏向技術素養高的藝術家。

The paper's most valuable move is shrinking an often abstract problem (the cultural blind spots of large models) to one concrete, audible point: even a named, documented sound like the Persian calligraphy pen cannot be produced at all by three open-source text-to-audio models. Compared with broad bias statistics, a fine-grained case like this shows more clearly what "not in the corpus" means, which is exactly what the authors argue in their discussion.

The most portable technical idea is RAVE as a post-processing filter. StyleGAN2 spectrograms reconstruct with an unnatural timbral component from lost phase; feeding them through RAVE's style transfer, trained on the same corpus, keeps the structure and drops the artefact because the input already lies within the timbre space RAVE has learned. In effect this replaces Griffin-Lim-style phase reconstruction with an in-domain neural vocoder. The paper does not say which reconstruction it used, but the combination would apply to other spectrogram GANs. Another is StyleGAN2 latent interpolation plus concatenation, which yields audio of any length with temporal trajectories absent from the data, getting around the 6-second clips.

The conclusion should not be read too broadly, though. What is shown is that building your own data for a narrow material and training an off-the-shelf small architecture from scratch can produce an artistically usable instrument, which is already routine in the RAVE community. "Small data beats large models" would need a comparison with a fine-tuned arm and a listening test. The training barrier the authors mention (11 hours per configuration, plus debugging skill) is also a reminder that this route still favours technically literate artists.

An Audio-Perspective on the Divergent Paths of Techno in Germany and the United States

從音訊看德美 techno 的分岔：以 9,029 首 1984–1994 年 house/techno 曲目的四個錄音室特徵（bpm、PhaseSpace、聲道相關、峰值因子）做雙因子 MANOVA、自組織映射圖與隨機森林，檢驗場景人物的七條說法

Tim Ziemer, Simon Linke
DOI:10.5334/tismir.324 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-07-09
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：techno 在德國成為大眾現象、在美國卻一直是地下場景，紀錄片與傳記對原因有很多說法，但從沒有人用音訊去對照。這篇用 HOTGAME 語料（4,667 首德國、4,362 首美國，1984–1994）的四個錄音室特徵，做 MANOVA、SOM 與隨機森林：德國曲目在 SOM 上從 1992 年開始離開美國的區域，德國九種風格的分類準確率 0.515、美國九種只有 0.369。選它是因為它示範了 MIR 工具怎麼回答音樂學與場景史的問題，而且它也拿數據挑戰了一條說法（「大型銳舞讓所有音樂聽起來一樣」）。要注意：只有四個特徵、每首只取中位數；隨機森林訓練準確率 0.98 以上、測試只有 0.37–0.52，過擬合明顯；「多樣化是突破的原因而非結果」只靠時間先後推論。Why this one: Techno became a mass phenomenon in Germany but stayed underground in the United States; documentaries and biographies offer many explanations, but nobody had checked them against audio. This paper takes four recording-studio features from the HOTGAME corpus (4,667 German and 4,362 US tracks, 1984–1994) and runs MANOVA, a self-organising map and a random forest: German tracks leave the US region of the SOM from 1992 on, and the classifier reaches 0.515 accuracy on nine German styles versus only 0.369 on nine US styles. I picked it because it shows how MIR tools can answer questions from musicology and scene history, and because it uses the data to challenge one claim ("megaraves made all music sound the same"). Caveats: only four features, each reduced to a per-track median; the random forest has training accuracy above 0.98 but test accuracy of only 0.37–0.52, a clear overfit; and "diversification caused the breakthrough rather than resulting from it" rests only on temporal order.

動機Motivation

2024 年柏林 techno 文化被列入 UNESCO 無形文化遺產，同一時間密西根州立大學博物館的展覽是在「回顧」底特律曾經的地位。techno 的發源地在美國，卻在德國開花：1994 年多特蒙德 Mayday 有超過 25,000 人、柏林 Loveparade 超過 100,000 人。

解釋這個落差的說法大多來自紀錄片、自傳與場景人物訪談。音樂學界一致認為這類敘述要批判地看，也有人（Volkwein 2003）明確呼籲去找「人物說法」與「音樂分析」之間的一致性，但實際的研究要嘛只分析一兩首曲子，要嘛完全不碰音訊。另一方面，MIR 對 EDM 做了大量音訊分析，卻很少把結果連回場景史。

這篇想填的就是這個縫：用 MIR 的大規模音訊分析，去對照並檢驗場景人物的說法。作者把可檢驗的說法整理成七條，例如「早期德國 house 模仿美國」「德國人在 1992 年找到自己的 techno」「美國沒能建立非傳統分支」「柏林圍牆倒塌是催化劑」。

In 2024 Berlin's techno culture was listed as UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, while an exhibition at the Michigan State University Museum was looking back at the role Detroit used to play. Techno originated in the US but flourished in Germany: the 1994 Mayday rave in Dortmund drew over 25,000 people and Berlin's Loveparade over 100,000.

Most explanations for this gap come from documentaries, autobiographies and interviews with scene protagonists. Musicologists agree such accounts must be read critically, and Volkwein (2003) explicitly called for seeking agreement between protagonists' statements and music analysis, yet existing studies either analyse one or two tracks or ignore audio altogether. Meanwhile MIR has done extensive audio analysis of EDM but rarely ties it back to scene history.

This paper aims at that gap: use MIR-style large-scale audio analysis to check protagonists' statements. The authors condense the testable claims into seven, such as "early German house emulated US house", "Germans found their techno in 1992", "the US failed at establishing unconventional branches" and "the fall of the Berlin Wall was a catalyst".

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

MIR 端最接近的前例是 Collins（2012）與 Mauch 等（2015）。 Collins 用預測模型與音色特徵分析 248 首 1980 年代底特律 techno 與芝加哥 house，發現兩者很像、都繼承 disco 與 synth-pop——本文對美國風格「彼此很像」的結論與它一致，但規模大了三十多倍。Mauch 等用音訊特徵與自相似矩陣分析 1960–2010 年 Billboard Hot 100，找出三次「革命」年；本文把德國 1992 年稱為類似的革命，方法精神相同，只是把「年代斷點」換成「國別分岔」。

EDM 分類的前例。 Caparrini 等（2020）用 92 個音訊特徵與隨機森林分 10 種 EDM 子類，最高 59%，bpm 是最重要特徵，且 house 的召回率低；本文同樣發現 bpm 主導、美國的 house 類彼此混淆，結果相當一致。Ziemer 等（2020）用同一組錄音室特徵預測 10 位 DJ 會放哪首歌，準確率 0.63，這是本文特徵的直接前身。

本文的差別有兩點：一是把分析結果系統性地對到場景人物的具名說法並標出支持、反駁或無法判斷；二是語料規模（9,029 首、97 個德國與 85 個美國廠牌）。方法本身——少量手工特徵、SOM 探索、RF 驗證——是作者團隊既有的套路，Ziemer 等（2024）分析嘻哈製作人時已用過。

On the MIR side the closest precedents are Collins (2012) and Mauch et al. (2015). Collins analysed 248 1980s Detroit techno and Chicago house tracks with predictive models and timbre features and found the two very similar, sharing a disco and synth-pop heritage; this paper's conclusion that US styles are much alike agrees, at more than thirty times the scale. Mauch et al. analysed the Billboard Hot 100 from 1960 to 2010 with audio features and a self-similarity matrix and identified three revolution years; this paper calls Germany's 1992 a similar revolution, in the same spirit, swapping a break in time for a split between nations.

EDM classification precedents. Caparrini et al. (2020) classified 10 EDM subgenres with 92 audio features and a random forest, reaching up to 59%, with bpm as the most important feature and low recall for house; this paper likewise finds bpm dominant and US house styles confused with each other, a consistent result. Ziemer et al. (2020) used the same recording-studio features to predict which of 10 DJs played a track, with 0.63 accuracy; that is the direct predecessor of this paper's features.

The difference here is twofold: the results are mapped systematically onto named protagonists' statements and marked as supported, challenged or undecided; and the corpus is large (9,029 tracks, 97 German and 85 US labels). The method itself (a few hand-crafted features, SOM exploration, RF confirmation) is the authors' established toolkit, already used by Ziemer et al. (2024) on hip-hop producers.

方法Method

語料。 HOTGAME 語料來自第一作者與其單位的 CD 與唱片，4,667 首德國、4,362 首美國，全部發行於 1984–1994，國別依製作人成長的國家判定。只收 house 分支衍生的音樂（各種 house、techno、trance、ambient、eurodance、hardcore、schranz、gabber、drum and bass、tekkno）。標註由第一作者依唱片內頁、文獻與 discogs 完成，學生做隨機抽查。語料公開的是特徵而不是音檔。

特徵。 只有四個錄音室特徵：bpm、PhaseSpace（以盒計數把相位示波器的點雲濃縮成一個值，反映音量、聲像與立體聲分布）、ChannelCorrelation（左右聲道相關，越低代表聲場越寬）、CrestFactor（峰值對 RMS 之比，鼓多則高、壓縮多則低）。每首取各特徵的中位數，再全語料 z 標準化。作者的理由是：這些是製作與母帶時真的會看的儀表、數值可直接解讀、彼此沒有顯著相關。

三種分析。 雙因子 MANOVA（國別 × 年份），作者承認常態與同質變異的假設不成立，所以搭配盒鬚圖、SOM 與 RF 交叉看。SOM 用全部曲目訓練，不給任何國別、年份或風格資訊，事後再依標籤著色，並計算每年 SOM 座標的變異與曲目間平均距離。RF 用 100 棵樹，對德國與美國各自的九種風格做 100 折交叉驗證。

Corpus. HOTGAME comes from CDs and records owned by the first author and his institution: 4,667 German and 4,362 US tracks, all released between 1984 and 1994, with nation assigned by where the producer grew up. Only music from the house branch is included (house variants, techno, trance, ambient, eurodance, hardcore, schranz, gabber, drum and bass, tekkno). Labels were assigned by the first author from booklets, literature and discogs, with random checks by students. The corpus publishes features, not audio.

Features. Just four recording-studio features: bpm; PhaseSpace (a phase-scope point cloud distilled to one value by box counting, reflecting level, panning and stereo spread); ChannelCorrelation (left–right correlation, lower meaning a wider image); and CrestFactor (peak-to-RMS ratio, high for drums and lowered by compression). Each track is represented by the median of each feature, z-normalised across the corpus. The rationale: these are meters actually watched in production and mastering, their values are directly interpretable, and they are not significantly correlated with each other.

Three analyses. A two-way MANOVA (nation × year), whose normality and homoscedasticity assumptions the authors admit do not hold, so it is cross-checked with boxplots, the SOM and the RF. The SOM is trained on all tracks with no nation, year or style information and coloured by labels afterwards; per-year variance of SOM coordinates and mean inter-track distances are also computed. The RF uses 100 trees with 100-fold cross-validation over nine styles each for Germany and the US.

限制Limitations

特徵太少，而且主要是 bpm 在說話。 作者自己承認四個特徵幾乎不含音色與節奏資訊：acid house 的身分在 TB-303 共振濾波，特徵抓不到，所以 acid house 在 SOM 上不成群、分類召回率不到機率水準 11%；breakbeat 的身分在節奏，同樣認不出來。downbeat 的召回率 98%（德）與 99%（美）幾乎全靠 bpm 偏低。每首只取中位數，也抹掉了一首曲子內部的段落變化。

分類結果的過擬合很嚴重。 德國訓練準確率 0.983、測試 0.515；美國訓練 0.991、測試 0.369。「德國風格較可分」的結論依賴這兩個測試數字的差距（0.515 對 0.369），但兩國的風格類別不同、各類樣本數論文沒有並列，差距有多少來自風格定義本身並不清楚。

語料與標註的來源單一。 曲目來自一位作者的收藏，風格與國別由同一人標註、學生抽查；代表性的論證在另一篇文章（Ziemer 2025b）。MANOVA 的 nation*year 自由度寫成 F(21, 9005)，與兩個主效果的 F(4, 9008) 格式不一致，論文沒有解釋。

因果推論太快。 「1991 年開始分岔、1992 年明顯多樣化，比 1994 年的突破早兩年，所以多樣化可能是原因而非結果」只是時間先後；1992 年德國已有大型銳舞與商業廠牌，突破的過程本身可能早於 1994。

Too few features, and mostly bpm doing the talking. The authors admit the four features carry almost no timbre or rhythm information: acid house is defined by the TB-303 resonant filter, which the features miss, so acid house does not cluster on the SOM and its recall falls below the 11% chance level; breakbeat is defined by rhythm and is likewise unrecognised. Downbeat's recall of 98% (Germany) and 99% (US) rests almost entirely on its low bpm. Reducing each track to medians also erases variation between sections within a track.

The classifier overfits badly. Germany: training accuracy 0.983, test 0.515; US: training 0.991, test 0.369. The claim that German styles are more separable rests on the gap between those test numbers (0.515 vs 0.369), but the two nations have different style categories and the paper does not list per-class sample counts side by side, so how much of the gap comes from the style definitions themselves is unclear.

Single-source corpus and labels. The tracks come from one author's collection, and style and nation were labelled by the same person with student spot checks; the representativeness argument lives in another article (Ziemer 2025b). The MANOVA's nation*year term is reported as F(21, 9005), inconsistent in form with the F(4, 9008) of the two main effects, without explanation.

The causal inference is quick. "Divergence starts in 1991 and diversification is clear in 1992, two years before the 1994 breakthrough, so diversification may be a cause rather than a result" is only temporal order; Germany already had megaraves and commercial labels by 1992, so the breakthrough process itself may predate 1994.

討論Discussion

三個主要結果。 一、德美曲目可區分：MANOVA 在國別上 F(4, 9008) = 303、partial η² = 0.12，年份 partial η² = 0.20；SOM 在沒給國別標籤的情況下把兩國大致分在不同區域。二、美國的風格彼此很像：garage、Chicago、deep、hip house 與第一波底特律 techno 在 SOM 上幾乎重疊，只有第二波底特律 techno、hardcore 與 downbeat 例外；分類器測試準確率 0.369 對德國的 0.515，作者另一篇用 9 個 MFCC 的研究得到相近的 54% 對 39%。三、美國十年間幾乎沒變，德國在 1990 年前與美國重疊、1992 年起大量曲目離開美國區域，SOM 上的 x 座標變異逐年上升。

對七條說法的判定。 支持的有：早期德國 house 模仿美國、德國人 1992 年找到自己的 techno、德國場景較有活力、美國沒能建立非傳統分支、第二波底特律 techno 聽起來像歐洲（它在 SOM 上擴散進德國區域，與 Underground Resistance 面向歐洲市場的說法吻合）。被挑戰的是 Wolle XDP 的「大型銳舞讓音樂都一樣」——德國音樂在 1991 年後反而更分散。無法判斷的是柏林圍牆：1989–1990 年特徵沒有明顯變化，作者給了三種解釋（影響的是場景組織而非音樂、效果延遲到 1992、或改變不在這四個特徵裡），沒有選邊，這是誠實的處理。

值得帶走的方法論。 把「場景人物的說法」整理成表、逐條標成支持／反駁／無法判斷，是讓音樂學與 MIR 對話很好用的格式，比籠統宣稱「音訊分析與史料一致」可檢驗得多。最後提到用「偏離起源並多樣化」去預測新潮流會不會突破，目前只有德國一個案例，作者也說需要其他國家（英國、荷蘭）與其他類型（dubstep、美國 EDM）來驗證。

Three main results. First, German and US tracks are separable: MANOVA gives F(4, 9008) = 303, partial η² = 0.12 for nation and partial η² = 0.20 for year, and the SOM, given no nation labels, places the two nations largely in different regions. Second, US styles are much alike: garage, Chicago, deep and hip house and first-wave Detroit techno almost coincide on the SOM, with only second-wave Detroit techno, hardcore and downbeat as exceptions; classifier test accuracy is 0.369 versus Germany's 0.515, and the authors' separate study with nine MFCCs finds a similar 54% versus 39%. Third, the US barely changes over the decade, while German tracks overlap with the US until 1990 and from 1992 many leave the US region, with the variance of SOM x-coordinates rising year by year.

Verdicts on the seven claims. Supported: early German house emulated the US; Germans found their techno in 1992; the German scene was more dynamic; the US failed at unconventional branches; second-wave Detroit techno sounded European (it spreads into the German region on the SOM, matching accounts of Underground Resistance targeting the European market). Challenged: Wolle XDP's "megaraves made all music sound the same", since German music became more spread out after 1991. Undecided: the Berlin Wall, as the features show no clear change in 1989–1990; the authors offer three explanations (it affected scene organisation rather than the music, its effect was delayed until 1992, or the change lies outside these four features) without choosing, which is an honest treatment.

The portable methodology. Tabulating protagonists' statements and marking each as supported, challenged or undecided is a useful format for dialogue between musicology and MIR, far more checkable than a blanket claim that audio analysis agrees with history. The closing idea of predicting whether a trend will break through from its divergence and diversification has only the German case behind it, and the authors themselves say other nations (the UK, the Netherlands) and other genres (dubstep, US EDM) are needed to test it.

為什麼挑這篇：西方音樂有 Lerdahl 的張力模型與一系列聽者實驗，土耳其 makam 音樂卻沒有可計算的張力模型。這篇把 makam 的「旋律核」理論（暫時中心與相對階層）接上 Erlich 的調和熵：每個音對它所屬的暫時中心算音程，查表得到熵值當張力，回到中心時歸零。選它是因為它是少數把 makam 的樂理概念寫成明確演算步驟的工作，而且附了調和熵的程式碼。要注意：整篇只示範兩段旋律（主要是 14 個事件的 Hüseyni Sofyan Şarkı），沒有任何聽者資料驗證；切分與階層樹由作者「聽」出來，並坦承主觀；所謂「熵」是音程的協和度，不是旋律的可預測性。Why this one: Western music has Lerdahl's tension model and a line of listener experiments, but Turkish makam music has no computable tension model. This paper connects makam's "melodic nucleus" theory (temporary centres and relative hierarchy) to Erlich's harmonic entropy: each note's interval to its temporary centre is looked up as an entropy value and used as tension, which drops to zero on returning to the centre. I picked it because it is one of the few works to write makam theory as explicit computational steps, and it ships code for harmonic entropy. Caveats: the whole paper demonstrates only two melodies (mainly the 14-event Hüseyni Sofyan Şarkı) with no listener data; segmentation and the hierarchy tree are "heard" by the authors, who admit this is subjective; and the "entropy" is the consonance of an interval, not the predictability of a melody.

動機Motivation

張力與解決是調性音樂理論的核心題目，從 Lerdahl & Jackendoff 的 GTTM 到 Lerdahl（1996）的調性張力計算、Lerdahl & Krumhansl（2007）的四成分模型，都已有可計算的形式。但這些模型都建立在西方調性與和聲上，無法直接搬到以單音旋律、微分音程與暫時中心為特色的土耳其 makam 音樂。

在 makam 理論裡，張力與解決其實一直是核心概念。作曲家與音樂學者 Kemal İlerici 依每個 makam 的「動音」與「靜音」討論張力；較新的「旋律核」（melodic nucleus）理論（Bayraktarkatal & Güray 2023）更明說，旋律核由三到四個音組成，建立在「旋律張力—解決」造成的穩定與不穩定平衡上。也就是說，樂理早就有張力的概念，只是沒有量化方法。

作者想把這種「直覺感受到」的張力變成可量化的曲線，用途包括認知音樂學、電腦輔助 makam 分析與演算法作曲（論文出自第一作者以熵控制 makam 作曲的博士論文）。作者也預先聲明：張力是多維的，預期、記憶與文化熟悉度都有影響，本文只處理心理聲學這一面。

Tension and resolution are central to tonal theory, and from Lerdahl & Jackendoff's GTTM to Lerdahl's (1996) tonal tension calculation and Lerdahl & Krumhansl's (2007) four-component model they already have computable forms. But these models rest on Western tonality and harmony and do not transfer directly to Turkish makam music, which is built on monophonic melody, microtonal intervals and temporary centres.

Within makam theory, tension and resolution have always been central. The composer and musicologist Kemal İlerici discussed tension in terms of each makam's moving and stationary notes, and the newer melodic nucleus theory (Bayraktarkatal & Güray 2023) states outright that a nucleus of three or four notes rests on stable and unstable equilibrium arising from melodic tension and resolution. In other words, the theory already has the concept; it lacks a way to quantify it.

The authors want to turn this intuitively felt tension into measurable curves, for cognitive musicology, computer-aided makam analysis and algorithmic composition (the paper derives from the first author's PhD thesis on entropy-controlled makam composition). They state up front that tension is multidimensional, with expectation, memory and cultural familiarity all playing a role, and that this paper addresses only the psychoacoustic side.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

張力模型的前例。 Lerdahl（2001）的 Tonal Pitch Space 用音高空間距離加上延長結構樹計算張力，Lerdahl & Krumhansl（2007）再以聽者的即時張力評分驗證；Farbood（2012）的參數化模型與 Herremans & Chew（2016）基於螺旋陣列的 tension ribbons 也都對過聽者資料。本文的「相對階層樹 + 每個音對其中心的距離」在結構上最像 Lerdahl 的延長樹加音高空間距離，只是把「距離」換成調和熵、把「調性中心」換成 makam 的暫時中心；但上述模型都有聽者驗證，本文沒有。

「熵」在音樂裡的兩種用法要分清楚。 Pinkerton（1956）、Gündüz & Gündüz（2005）分析土耳其旋律、以及 Pearce 的 IDyOM 用的是 Shannon 熵衡量「下一個音有多難預測」，這才是音樂認知裡與預期、張力相連的熵。本文用的 Erlich 調和熵是另一回事：它衡量一個音程被聽成哪個簡單整數比的不確定性，本質上是一種協和度曲線，跟 Plomp–Levelt 粗糙度或 Sethares 的感官失協和屬於同一族。論文在背景節把兩條線並列，容易讓讀者以為兩者是同一種「熵」。作者也承認換成粗糙度模型同樣可行，選 Erlich 主要因為它叫「熵」且能逐 cent 給值。

本文的差別在於把旋律核理論的「暫時中心」與 Öztürk（2014）的定位／定向音型正式寫成決定「每個音該對誰算距離」的規則，這一步過去只存在於樂理描述。

Precedents for tension models. Lerdahl's (2001) Tonal Pitch Space computes tension from pitch-space distance plus a prolongational tree, and Lerdahl & Krumhansl (2007) validated it against listeners' continuous tension ratings; Farbood's (2012) parametric model and Herremans & Chew's (2016) spiral-array tension ribbons were also checked against listener data. This paper's relative hierarchy tree plus each note's distance to its centre is structurally closest to Lerdahl's prolongational tree plus pitch-space distance, with harmonic entropy in place of distance and makam's temporary centres in place of the tonal centre; but those models have listener validation and this one does not.

Two uses of "entropy" in music need separating. Pinkerton (1956), Gündüz & Gündüz (2005) on Turkish melodies, and Pearce's IDyOM use Shannon entropy to measure how hard the next note is to predict, which is the entropy that music cognition links to expectation and tension. Erlich's harmonic entropy, used here, is different: it measures the uncertainty of which simple integer ratio an interval is heard as, essentially a consonance curve in the same family as Plomp–Levelt roughness or Sethares' sensory dissonance. The background section sets the two lines side by side, which invites readers to think they are the same entropy. The authors concede a roughness model would work equally well and chose Erlich mainly because it is called entropy and gives a value per cent.

The difference here is that melodic nucleus theory's temporary centres and Öztürk's (2014) positional and orientational figures are written down as explicit rules for deciding which centre each note is measured against, a step that previously existed only as theoretical description.

方法Method

四個步驟。 一、旋律切分：依 Öztürk（2014）定義的音型，把旋律分成定位音型（圍繞某音循環，例如重複、上下行複合）與定向音型（從一個中心走向另一個中心，例如上行級進、上行跳進、下行級進）；切分由作者聆聽並依 makam 知識判定，作者稱之為「知覺分組」，並承認主觀。二、相對階層樹：定位音型的循環中心是暫時中心，其他音掛在它下面；定向音型的音同時掛在前後兩個中心；所有音最終接到主中心。

三、熵計算：每個音與其所屬中心的頻率比換成 cent，查 Erlich 調和熵曲線（0–1200 cent 每 cent 一個值）；若掛在兩個中心，就取兩個熵值的算術平均（作者說因為不知道哪個中心影響較大，假設權重相等）。四、畫圖：事件軸的熵與累積熵，再把每個音的熵乘上它包含的時間單位數（例如 32 分音符）轉成時間軸。

累積熵的規則是：從第一個音開始加總，遇到熵為零（回到主中心）就歸零。

Hüseyni 示範。 旋律取自 Hacı Arif Bey 的 Hüseyni Sofyan Şarkı 開頭，分成七個音型，14 個事件。以第 4 個事件 neva 為例：它同時掛在 dügah（330 Hz）與 hüseyni（495 Hz），neva 取 440 Hz；對 dügah 是 1.33 即 498 cent、熵 1.83，對 hüseyni 是 0.89 即 204 cent、熵 3.39，平均 2.61，累積到 7.91。最高的單一熵值是 çargah 對 hüseyni 的 408 cent，熵 4.00；回到 hüseyni 的事件熵為 0。

Four steps. One, segmentation: the melody is divided into Öztürk's (2014) figures, positional ones (circling a note, e.g. repetition or ascending–descending compound) and orientational ones (moving from one centre to another, e.g. ascending step, ascending leap, descending step); segmentation is decided by the authors' listening and makam knowledge, which they call perceptual grouping and admit is subjective. Two, the relative hierarchy tree: the circling note of a positional figure is a temporary centre and the other notes hang under it; notes of an orientational figure hang under both the preceding and following centres; everything ultimately connects to the main centre.

Three, entropy: each note's frequency ratio to its centre is converted to cents and looked up on Erlich's harmonic entropy curve (one value per cent over 0–1200); a note attached to two centres takes the arithmetic mean of the two values (the authors assume equal weights because they do not know which centre matters more). Four, plotting: event-based entropy and cumulative entropy, converted to a time axis by multiplying each note's entropy by its count of time units (e.g. 32nd notes).

The cumulative rule is to sum from the first note and reset to zero whenever entropy is zero (a return to the main centre).

Hüseyni example. The melody is the opening of Hacı Arif Bey's Hüseyni Sofyan Şarkı, split into seven figures and 14 events. Take event 4, neva: it hangs under both dügah (330 Hz) and hüseyni (495 Hz), with neva at 440 Hz; against dügah the ratio is 1.33, or 498 cents, entropy 1.83; against hüseyni it is 0.89, or 204 cents, entropy 3.39; the mean is 2.61 and the cumulative total reaches 7.91. The highest single value is çargah against hüseyni at 408 cents, entropy 4.00; events that return to hüseyni have entropy 0.

限制Limitations

沒有驗證。 整篇的「結果」是兩段旋律的曲線，以及「張力在離開中心時上升、回到中心時解決」的觀察——但這個觀察是模型定義直接推出來的（回到中心的音程是 0 cent，熵被設為 0），不是被資料檢驗出來的。沒有聽者評分、沒有和 Lerdahl 類模型或樂手標註比較，也沒有在多首曲子上看曲線是否穩定。

主觀步驟正好在最關鍵的地方。 切分與「每個音掛在哪個中心」決定了要算哪個音程，作者明說這由他們聆聽判定、不同人可能有不同結果；連續定向音型時更「模稜兩可」。雙中心取算術平均也是無依據的假設。換句話說，模型的自由度大多在人手上，熵查表只是最後一步。

音高是離散化的理論值。 示範用 dügah 330、neva 440、hüseyni 495 Hz 這類簡單整數比的頻率，得到 204、498、702、408、181、294 cent 等值；作者自己承認模型不處理實際演奏的音高變異（Bozkurt 等的研究早已指出理論與實踐差距），只適用於符號資料。表 1 裡 eviç 與 acem 兩個不同的音對 hüseyni 都記成 181 cent、熵 3.53；依 AEU 體系 acem 約在 hüseyni 上方 90 cent、eviç 約 181 cent，兩者不該相同，這看起來是表格錯誤，論文沒有說明。

累積熵的意義不明。 把逐音的協和度加總稱為「累積能量」、回到中心時「全部釋放」，是比喻而不是被驗證的心理量；旋律越長、離中心越久，數字就越大，這與聽感的張力是否成比例沒有證據。

No validation. The paper's "results" are the curves for two melodies and the observation that tension rises when moving away from the centre and resolves on returning. But that observation follows directly from the model's definition (the interval back to the centre is 0 cents, whose entropy is set to 0); it is not tested against data. There are no listener ratings, no comparison with Lerdahl-style models or musicians' annotations, and no check of curve stability across many pieces.

The subjective steps sit exactly where it matters. Segmentation and the choice of which centre each note hangs under determine which interval is measured, and the authors say these are decided by their listening and may differ between people, becoming more ambiguous for consecutive orientational figures. Averaging two centres with equal weights is also an unsupported assumption. Most of the model's degrees of freedom are in human hands; the entropy lookup is only the last step.

Pitch is discretised theory. The example uses simple-ratio frequencies such as dügah 330, neva 440 and hüseyni 495 Hz, yielding 204, 498, 702, 408, 181 and 294 cents; the authors admit the model ignores pitch variation in performance (Bozkurt et al. have long documented the gap between theory and practice) and applies only to symbolic data. In Table 1, eviç and acem, two different notes, are both listed at 181 cents against hüseyni with entropy 3.53; in the AEU system acem lies about 90 cents above hüseyni and eviç about 181 cents, so the two should not coincide; this looks like a table error, and the paper does not explain it.

The meaning of cumulative entropy is unclear. Summing per-note consonance into "accumulated energy" that is "fully discharged" on returning to the centre is a metaphor, not a validated psychological quantity; the longer a melody stays away from the centre the larger the number, and there is no evidence that this is proportional to perceived tension.

討論Discussion

這篇的貢獻是概念上的形式化，而不是實證結果。它把 makam 樂理裡口語式的「旋律核」「暫時中心」「動音／靜音」寫成一組可以一步步執行的規則，並附了 Matlab／Python 的 Erlich 調和熵實作（Zenodo 公開），這對想做 makam 電腦分析的人是可以直接拿來用的起點。兩段旋律的比較也給了直觀的對比：Sofyan Şarkı 開頭很快回到中心、短暫建立張力後再全部釋放；Peşrev 的段落張力一路累積、到最後才解決，累積值也比較高。

讀者最該抓住的是：這個模型的張力完全由「音程對中心的協和度」決定，所以它本質上是 Krumhansl 式音級階層的一種心理聲學版本——離中心的五度（702 cent）熵 0.96，比二度（204 cent）的 3.39 低，就是「五度比二度穩定」的量化。這和樂理直覺一致，但也意味著它無法表達同一音程在不同旋律位置的張力差異，例如預期落空造成的張力；作者在討論裡提到要加入 Narmour 的 Implication–Realization 模型，正是在補這一塊。

要讓它成為可用的分析工具，至少需要三件事：把切分與中心指派自動化或做多位標註者一致性分析；收集 makam 聽者的張力評分來驗證曲線；以及接上 Bozkurt 等的音高分布與 AEU 的 comma 體系，處理真實的微分音高。在那之前，它比較適合當作曲系統的設計工具（作者的博士論文方向），而不是對既有曲目的分析結論。

The contribution is a conceptual formalisation, not an empirical result. It writes makam theory's informal notions of melodic nucleus, temporary centre and moving versus stationary notes as rules that can be executed step by step, and it releases Matlab and Python implementations of Erlich's harmonic entropy on Zenodo, a ready starting point for anyone doing computational makam analysis. The two-melody comparison also gives an intuitive contrast: the Sofyan Şarkı opening returns to the centre quickly, builds tension briefly and then discharges it all; the Peşrev passage accumulates tension throughout and resolves only at the end, with a higher cumulative value.

The key point for readers is that tension here is determined entirely by an interval's consonance relative to the centre, so the model is essentially a psychoacoustic version of a Krumhansl-style pitch-class hierarchy: a fifth from the centre (702 cents) has entropy 0.96, lower than a second (204 cents) at 3.39, which quantifies "a fifth is more stable than a second". That agrees with theoretical intuition, but it also means the model cannot express how the same interval carries different tension in different melodic positions, such as tension from violated expectation; the authors' plan to add Narmour's Implication–Realization model in their discussion addresses exactly this gap.

To become a usable analytical tool it needs at least three things: automating segmentation and centre assignment, or measuring agreement across multiple annotators; collecting tension ratings from makam listeners to validate the curves; and connecting to Bozkurt et al.'s pitch distributions and the AEU comma system to handle real microtonal pitch. Until then it is better suited as a design tool for composition systems (the direction of the author's PhD) than as an analytical conclusion about existing repertoire.
