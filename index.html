Silent Metronome: Rhythmic Grounding for Live Music Accompaniment

無聲節拍器：為即時音樂伴奏建立節奏基準

Kevin Bretz, Derya Soydaner, Aske Plaat
arXiv:2609.07688 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-09
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文認為即時音樂伴奏不必從音訊裡『推斷』節拍，而是可以像人類樂手看節拍器一樣，把節拍相位（正弦/餘弦編碼）、速度、拍號等 24 維訊號透過 AdaLN-Zero 逐幀注入串流生成模型的每一層，這個訊號完全獨立於自回歸生成過程、因此不會隨時間漂移；在 Slakh2100 上，完整方法把 Beat-F 從無條件基準的 0.133 推高到 0.432（3.25 倍），甚至比能看到未來 50 個音框的『非因果』對照組（0.269）還高 60.6%，20 秒長生成的漂移率也只有 5%，遠低於基準的 14–16%；不過打擊樂（達真實值 93.8%）比和聲樂器（僅 71.0%）受益明顯更多，而且目前的節拍格全部來自 MIDI 合成資料的完美標註，作者自己承認還沒測試過真實演出裡節奏會漂移的情況。Why this one: The paper argues live music accompaniment need not infer rhythm from audio at all -- instead, the way a human musician watches a metronome, it injects a 24-dimensional signal (sine/cosine-encoded beat and bar phase, tempo, time signature) per frame into every layer of a streaming generation model via AdaLN-Zero, a signal fully independent of the autoregressive process and therefore immune to drift; on Slakh2100, the full method raises Beat-F from an unconditioned baseline of 0.133 to 0.432 (3.25x), even beating a non-causal reference that sees 50 frames ahead (0.269) by 60.6%, with only a 5% drift rate over 20-second generations versus 14-16% for the baseline; however, percussion (93.8% of ground truth) benefits far more than harmonic instruments (only 71.0%), and the current metrical grid comes entirely from perfect MIDI-synthesized annotations -- the authors themselves acknowledge real performances where rhythm drifts remain untested.

動機Motivation

即時音樂伴奏系統必須在聽到最新一段輸入音訊之前，就先產生輸出音框，也就是嚴格因果、沒有時間參照的生成方式。作者把這個問題定義為一種暴露偏誤（exposure bias）：「模型有耳朵卻沒有時間感」，只能從有雜訊、不完整的過去音訊裡推斷速度、拍號與節拍相位，一旦推斷有誤，誤差會隨時間累積，變成聽得出來的節奏錯位。他們提出的研究問題是：節拍結構是不是一定要從音訊裡推斷出來，還是可以像人類樂手跟著節拍器演奏一樣，用外部訊號直接提供、而且這個訊號本身不會隨時間漂移？

Live music accompaniment systems must commit to an output frame before hearing the latest incoming audio -- strictly causal generation with no external time reference. The authors frame this as an exposure-bias problem: "the model has ears but no sense of time," forced to infer tempo, meter, and metrical phase from imperfect past audio, with errors compounding into audible rhythmic misalignment. Their research question: must metrical structure be inferred from audio at all, or can it be supplied externally as a separate, drift-resistant signal -- the way human musicians follow a shared metronome?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者點名了具體的先前系統。ReaLchords 與部署它的 ReaLJam 是符號域（symbolic）的線上和弦伴奏，因為直接操作符號而非音訊，對齊問題本來就簡單很多；Wu 等人的 SMG 是第一個直接在音訊上做串流生成的框架，這篇論文延伸的正是它的因果基準；LiveBand 在音質、拍點對齊、連貫性上改進了 SMG，並且明確量測了節奏隨時間的漂移，但它的做法是「整個拿掉回饋」來防止漂移，代價是犧牲自回歸生成本身。這些系統的共同點，是沒有一個提供「獨立於自回歸生成過程、不會被帶偏」的週期性節拍相位條件訊號。

另外，暴露偏誤與自回歸漂移這條文獻裡，排程取樣（scheduled sampling）、雜訊增強、逐 token 雜訊排程、用模型自己的滾動輸出訓練，都是訓練時的解法，而不是像這篇論文一樣在推論時提供外部訊號。條件生成方面，MusicGen 靠文字與旋律條件化、Stable Audio Open 提供時間控制、Music ControlNet 能做隨時間變化的節奏控制，但只能離線（非因果）使用；Beat This!、madmom 這類拍點偵測器可以提供節拍格，但都是事後分析、不是即時串流。這篇論文與它們的關鍵差異，在於把週期性相位條件訊號，逐幀（而非逐樣本）注入嚴格因果的串流生成過程。

The authors name specific prior systems. ReaLchords and its live-jamming deployment ReaLJam operate in the symbolic domain, which trivializes alignment since chords rather than audio are manipulated directly; Wu et al.'s SMG is the first streaming generation framework operating directly on audio, and this paper extends exactly its causal baseline; LiveBand improves on SMG in audio quality, beat alignment, and coherence, and explicitly measures rhythmic drift over time, but does so by removing feedback entirely, which prevents drift at the cost of sacrificing autoregressive generation itself. None of these systems supplies a periodic metrical-phase conditioning signal that stays independent of the autoregressive process and therefore cannot itself drift.

In the exposure-bias and autoregressive-drift literature, scheduled sampling, noise augmentation, per-token noise schedules, and training on the model's own rollouts are all training-time fixes rather than an externally supplied inference-time signal like this paper's. On conditional generation, MusicGen conditions on text and melody, Stable Audio Open offers timing controls, and Music ControlNet supports time-varying rhythm control for diffusion, but only offline (non-causal); beat trackers like Beat This! and madmom can supply a metrical grid, but only through after-the-fact analysis, not live streaming. This paper's key departure is injecting a periodic phase conditioning signal per frame, rather than per sample, directly into strictly causal streaming generation.

方法Method

架構直接建立在 Wu 等人的串流框架（前綴解碼器 transformer）之上，保留原本的架構、（因果版本的）DAC 編解碼器與訓練流程，運作在 50Hz 的音框率、四個殘差碼本上。核心設計「沉默節拍器」（Silent Metronome）是一個 24 維的條件向量：包含節拍相位與小節相位各自的正弦／餘弦編碼（避免相位在 0 與 1 之間跳變的問題）、逐幀與逐視窗的對數速度、速度／拍號變化旗標，以及拍號分子分母的 8 維學習嵌入，全部投影到模型維度後，透過 AdaLN-Zero 機制注入每一層 transformer 區塊，用零初始化的線性層預測每個分支的縮放與門控殘差係數，讓調製強度可以逐幀特化。

除了條件訊號本身，模型還加了三個推論時會拿掉的輔助損失頭：多音高預測（128 個二元音高活躍度）、Constant-Q 頻譜預測，這兩個是既有的音樂結構目標；第三個是這篇論文提出的新目標——預測未來 δ∈{10,25,40} 個音框（也就是 0.2、0.5、0.8 秒後）的輸出 DAC token。條件訊號機制讓模型多了約 2.78 億參數（合計 4.97 億，未加條件的基準是 2.19 億），但因為調製只依賴提前一個區塊就知道的外部節拍格，可以平行預先算好，實測在 A100 上每個區塊生成時間只多了約 5%。訓練用 Slakh2100（2,100 首多軌合成歌曲）、10 秒視窗、20 萬步、批次量 16，peak 學習率 10⁻⁴。

The architecture is built directly on Wu et al.'s streaming framework (a prefix-decoder transformer), keeping its architecture, causal DAC codec variant, and training recipe, operating at a 50Hz frame rate over four residual codebooks. The core design, "Silent Metronome," is a 24-dimensional conditioning vector: sine/cosine encodings of beat phase and bar phase each (avoiding phase wraparound between 0 and 1), per-frame and per-window log-tempo, tempo/meter change flags, and 8-dimensional learned embeddings for the time-signature numerator and denominator, all projected to model width and injected into every transformer block via AdaLN-Zero, with zero-initialized linear layers predicting per-branch scale and gated-residual coefficients so modulation strength can specialize per frame.

Beyond the conditioning signal itself, the model adds three auxiliary loss heads, all dropped at inference: multi-pitch prediction (128 binary pitch-activity values) and Constant-Q spectrum prediction, both established music-structure objectives, plus a novel third objective that predicts the model's own future output DAC tokens at delta in {10, 25, 40} frames ahead (0.2, 0.5, 0.8 seconds). The conditioning mechanism adds roughly 278M parameters (497M total versus a 219M unconditioned baseline), but because modulation depends only on an external grid known one chunk ahead, it can be precomputed in parallel, adding only about 5% per-chunk generation overhead measured on an A100. Training uses Slakh2100 (2,100 MIDI-synthesized multitrack songs), 10-second windows, 200k steps, batch size 16, and a peak learning rate of 1e-4.

限制Limitations

作者列出的限制包括：目前的節拍格是從 MIDI 合成資料裡直接拿到的完美標註，「節拍器與輸入一致，是同步時鐘的前提，用 MIDI 合成資料時這件事是天生成立的，但沒有測試過真實演出裡搭檔偏離既定節奏時模型會怎麼反應」；不同樂器類別的表現並不平均，打擊樂能達到真實值的 93.8%，但和聲樂器只有 71.0%，作者的非正式聽測也確認節拍訊號對打擊樂幫助最大，和聲面向的處理相對不足。

另外，作者也坦承「我們的模型收到一個對照組沒有的訊號」，並主張這個訊號「便宜、外部、不會漂移，很適合 DAW、錄音室、舞台這些本來就有共用時鐘的串流系統」來自我辯護；目前的實驗全部假設拿到完美的節拍格標註，還沒測試用即時拍點追蹤器產生節拍格、也還沒處理真實系統需要的延遲補償（f_v<0 的情境）。

The authors list several limitations: the current metrical grid is a perfect annotation taken directly from MIDI-synthesized data -- "the metronome agreeing with the input is the premise of a synchronized clock, and is inherently given when using MIDI-generated data, yet it leaves untested how the model responds when a live partner drifts from the set rhythm"; performance is uneven across instrument classes, with percussion reaching 93.8% of ground truth versus only 71.0% for harmonic instruments, and the authors' informal listening confirms the rhythm signal helps percussion far more than harmony.

The authors also acknowledge that "our models receive a signal the references do not," defending this by arguing the signal is "cheap, external, and drift-free, ideal for streaming systems with access to a shared clock, the norm in DAW, studio, and stage setups"; current experiments assume a perfect oracle grid throughout, leaving live tracker-derived grid estimation untested, and delay compensation for the latency real systems require (the f_v<0 case) is not addressed either.

討論Discussion

主要結果（表一，1,024 筆測試樣本，指標是 Beat-F、CoCoLa 諧和/打擊一致性、FAD 音質，真實值 Beat-F 為 0.570）：完全不加條件的基準只有 0.133；只加輔助頭（不加節拍條件）也還是 0.133，證明光靠輔助任務沒有用；只給速度與拍號、不給相位訊號，只小幅提升到 0.141；完整的「沉默節拍器」條件（含相位）大幅跳到 0.380，是基準的 2.86 倍；再加上音高與頻譜輔助頭提升到 0.411；最後加上預測未來 token 的輔助頭達到最高的 0.432，是基準的 3.25 倍，而且比非因果（能看到未來 50 個音框）的對照組 0.269 還高出 0.163（超前 60.6%），達到真實值的 75.8%。

拆成不同樂器類別看，模型在打擊樂（0.729）達到真實值（0.777）的 93.8%，貝斯（0.571／0.722）達到 79.1%，和聲樂器（0.363／0.511）只到 71.0%，而非因果對照組在打擊樂、貝斯、和聲上分別只有 0.373、0.391、0.230，全面輸給這篇論文的完整模型。在 20 秒長生成、切成前後兩個 10 秒的長時漂移測試裡，真實值本身從前半到後半就會衰退 4%，完整模型的相對衰退是 5%（前後兩段都維持在真實值的 75%），只用節拍相位（不含輔助頭）的版本衰退更小、只有 3%，而完全不加條件的基準衰退達 14–16%，顯示外部節拍訊號確實有效抑制了長時漂移。

Main results (Table I, 1,024 test samples; metrics are Beat-F, CoCoLa harmonic/percussive coherence, and FAD audio quality, with a ground-truth Beat-F of 0.570): the fully unconditioned baseline scores only 0.133; adding auxiliary heads alone (no rhythm conditioning) stays at 0.133, showing the auxiliary tasks alone do nothing; supplying only tempo and time signature without phase raises this slightly to 0.141; the full Silent Metronome conditioning (with phase) jumps to 0.380, 2.86x the baseline; adding pitch and spectrum auxiliary heads raises it to 0.411; and adding the future-token-prediction head reaches the best score of 0.432 -- 3.25x the baseline, and 0.163 points (60.6%) above the non-causal reference (which can see 50 frames ahead) at 0.269, reaching 75.8% of ground truth.

Broken down by instrument class, the model reaches 93.8% of ground truth on drums (0.729 vs. 0.777), 79.1% on bass (0.571 vs. 0.722), and only 71.0% on harmonic instruments (0.363 vs. 0.511), while the non-causal reference scores just 0.373, 0.391, and 0.230 on those same three classes -- losing across the board to this paper's full model. In a long-horizon drift test (20-second generations split into two 10-second halves), ground truth itself declines 4% from the first half to the second; the full model's relative decline is 5% (maintaining 75% of ground truth in both halves), the phase-only variant (no auxiliary heads) declines even less at 3%, while the fully unconditioned baseline declines 14-16% -- showing the external rhythm signal meaningfully suppresses long-horizon drift.

音源分離與表徵學習Separation & Representation2026-09-10

Lead Vocal Separation from Vocal Ensemble Mixtures Using Phoneme Alignment

用音素對齊做人聲重唱混音中的主唱分離

Yuma Narahata, Tomohiko Nakamura, Yuki Saito, Hiroshi Saruwatari
arXiv:2609.06488 · cs.SD, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-09-09
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文把 a cappella 重唱分離重新定義成『依音樂角色分兩軌』（主唱 vs. 伴奏）而非『依聲部種類分多軌』，在 Band-split RoPE Transformer 骨幹上用 FiLM 條件化注入逐幀音素標籤；在 jaCappella 語料庫的 10 首評測曲上，完整的音素對齊版本把主唱分離的 SI-SDRi 從純音訊基準的 9.87 dB、只用歌唱活動標籤的 13.05 dB，進一步推高到 14.85 dB，且在全部 10 首歌都贏過基準；不過依音素重疊程度細分後發現，領先只用活動標籤的幅度會隨重疊聲部增加而縮小（從 0 重疊時領先 3.49 dB 縮小到 3 個聲部重疊時只領先 0.41 dB），而且整個評測都建立在人工校正過的理想化音素對齊上，作者自己承認還沒解決真實場景缺乏對齊資訊的問題。Why this one: The paper reframes a cappella ensemble separation as a two-stem task by musical role (lead vs. accompaniment) rather than by source type, injecting frame-level phoneme labels via FiLM conditioning into a Band-Split RoPE Transformer backbone; on the jaCappella corpus's 10 evaluation songs, full phoneme-alignment conditioning raises lead-vocal SI-SDRi from an audio-only baseline's 9.87 dB and activity-only conditioning's 13.05 dB to 14.85 dB, beating the baseline on all 10 songs; however, broken down by phonetic overlap, its margin over activity-only conditioning shrinks as overlap increases (from a 3.49 dB lead at zero overlap to just 0.41 dB at three overlapping parts), and the entire evaluation rests on manually corrected, idealized phoneme alignment -- the authors themselves acknowledge the real-world lack of aligned lyrics remains unsolved.

動機Motivation

這篇論文處理的是從人聲重唱（a cappella）混音裡分離出「主唱」與「合聲伴奏」兩軌，這件事比一般的人聲/伴奏分離更難，因為目標聲部與干擾聲部全部都是人聲，音色特徵相近，時間上又經常重疊；作者指出「這個任務可用的聲學線索很有限，因為目標與干擾來源都是歌聲，聲學特性相似，時間上也經常重疊」。分離出主唱有實際用途，例如歌詞辨識與卡拉 OK 式伴奏生成。

The paper targets separating a lead vocal from accompaniment parts in a cappella mixtures, a harder problem than ordinary vocal/accompaniment separation because both target and interfering sources are singing voices with similar timbre that frequently overlap in time; the authors note "acoustic cues for this task are limited because the target and interfering sources are all singing voices with similar acoustic characteristics and often overlap in time." Extracting the lead vocal has concrete downstream uses such as lyric recognition and karaoke-style accompaniment generation.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者點名了具體的先前系統。Sarkar 等人把原本設計給語音分離的 DPTNet 拿來做四聲部人聲分離；Nakamura 等人則用 DPTNet、MRDLA、X-UMX 處理六聲部分離；Hybrid Transformer Demucs 被用來做人聲打擊樂（vocal percussion）的抽取；SepReFormer 也是從語音分離改造成六聲部人聲分離；另外有人提出用基頻軌跡（F0）條件化，但需要先做困難的聲部追蹤才能取得軌跡。這些先前系統的共同點，是把問題設定成「估計每一個獨立聲部」；這篇論文與它們的關鍵差異，在於把問題重新定義成「依音樂角色（主唱 vs. 伴奏）分離的兩軌任務」，而不是依聲部種類分離。

另一條相關的先前工作，是用輔助資訊輔助人聲分離：Jeon 等人把時間對齊的歌詞編碼成特徵；Meseguer-Brocal 與 Peeters 用對齊過的音素資訊搭配 FiLM 條件化；也有近期工作處理沒有預先對齊資訊的情況。這篇論文延續 FiLM 條件化的做法，但特別針對「主唱分離」這個任務、並系統性地分析音素重疊（phonetic overlap）程度對分離效果的影響，這是先前工作沒有做過的分析角度。

The authors name specific prior systems. Sarkar et al. adapted DPTNet, originally built for speech separation, to four-part vocal separation; Nakamura et al. addressed six-part separation using DPTNet, MRDLA, and X-UMX; Hybrid Transformer Demucs has been applied to vocal-percussion extraction; SepReFormer was likewise adapted from speech separation to six-part ensembles; other work proposed conditioning on fundamental-frequency (F0) trajectories, but obtaining those trajectories requires difficult part-tracking first. What these prior systems share is framing the task as estimating each individual vocal part; this paper's key departure is reframing it as a two-stem task that separates by musical role (lead vs. accompaniment) rather than by source type.

A related line of prior work augments singing-voice separation with auxiliary information: Jeon et al. encode time-aligned lyrics as features; Meseguer-Brocal and Peeters use aligned phoneme information with FiLM conditioning; other recent work addresses the case with no pre-aligned information available. This paper continues the FiLM-conditioning approach but applies it specifically to the lead-vocal-separation task, and adds a systematic analysis of how the degree of phonetic overlap affects separation quality -- an analysis angle prior work has not covered.

方法Method

骨幹架構是 Band-split RoPE Transformer（BS-RoFormer）：輸入是複數頻譜（2048 點窗、512 點位移），band-split 模組切成 62 個頻帶、每個用 192 維表示，接上 6 層、每層 8 個注意力頭、帶旋轉位置編碼（RoPE）的 Transformer。音素條件化則透過 FiLM：把逐幀的音素標籤（36 類，含靜音與填充）查表得到嵌入向量，在 band-split 模組之後與每一層 Transformer 之後都插入一層 FiLM，用這個嵌入向量算出的縮放與平移係數去調製特徵。

訓練用 jaCappella 語料庫，50 首日文兒歌/校歌，每首分成主唱、女高音、女低音、男高音、男低音、人聲打擊六個聲部，40 首訓練、10 首（每個曲風各取一首）評測；音素對齊標註是用 Praat 軟體人工校正過的。損失函數是絕對誤差加上多解析度 STFT 損失（窗長涵蓋 256 到 4096 點），訓練 1000 個 epoch，優化器是 Adam、學習率 5×10⁻⁵。作者比較三種設定：完全不用輔助資訊的基準、只用「是否在唱」的活動標籤、以及完整的音素對齊條件化。

The backbone is a Band-Split RoPE Transformer (BS-RoFormer): the input is a complex spectrogram (2048-sample window, 512-sample hop), split by the band-split module into 62 frequency bands each represented in 192 dimensions, followed by 6 Transformer layers with 8 attention heads and rotary positional embeddings (RoPE). Phoneme conditioning is injected via FiLM: frame-level phoneme labels (36 classes, including silence and padding) are looked up as embeddings, and a FiLM layer is inserted after the band-split module and after every Transformer layer, using that embedding to compute scale and shift coefficients that modulate the features.

Training uses the jaCappella corpus: 50 Japanese children's/school songs, each split into six parts (lead, soprano, alto, tenor, bass, vocal percussion), with 40 songs for training and 10 (one per genre subset) for evaluation; phoneme alignment annotations were manually corrected using Praat. The loss combines mean absolute error with a multi-resolution STFT loss (window lengths from 256 to 4096 samples), trained for 1000 epochs with Adam at a learning rate of 5x10^-5. The authors compare three conditions: an audio-only baseline with no auxiliary information, activity-only conditioning (singing/silent), and full phoneme-alignment conditioning.

限制Limitations

作者自陳的限制相當直接：整個評測都建立在「理想化」的假設上，用的是人工校正過的音素對齊，論文明白寫著這是為了評估這個條件化方法的潛力，而「使用沒有精準對齊的歌詞」被列為未來要處理的問題，代表這個方法目前還沒辦法直接用在真實場景（真實場景通常沒有現成的逐幀音素對齊）。其他限制還包括：只在單一語言（日文）、單一曲風（兒歌/校歌）的語料庫上驗證，沒有測試跨語言、跨曲風的泛化；也沒有做主觀聽測。

分離效果在音素高度重疊時會明顯下降——重疊聲部數從 0 個上升到 3 個時，本論文方法的 SI-SDRi 從 18.32 dB 掉到 12.15 dB，顯示這個方法對「多個聲部同時唱相同音素」的情況並沒有真正解決，只是比單純用活動標籤好。

The authors' stated limitations are direct: the entire evaluation rests on an "idealized" assumption of manually corrected phoneme alignments, which the paper explicitly frames as evaluating the conditioning method's potential; "using lyrics without accurately aligned phoneme labels" is listed as future work, meaning the method cannot yet be applied directly to real-world scenarios that lack ready-made frame-level phoneme alignment. Other limitations include validation on only a single language (Japanese) and a single genre (children's/school songs), with no test of cross-language or cross-genre generalization, and no subjective listening evaluation.

Separation quality also degrades noticeably under heavy phonetic overlap -- as the number of overlapping parts rises from 0 to 3, this method's SI-SDRi drops from 18.32 dB to 12.15 dB, showing the approach does not truly solve the case where multiple parts sing identical phonemes simultaneously, only improves on plain activity-label conditioning.

討論Discussion

主要結果（用 SI-SDRi，數字越高越好）：完全不用輔助資訊的基準版本，主唱（Vo）分離平均 9.87 dB、伴奏（Other）平均 7.15 dB；只用「是否在唱」的活動標籤，Vo 提升到 13.05 dB、Other 提升到 10.11 dB；完整的音素對齊條件化版本最高，Vo 達到 14.85 dB、Other 達到 11.76 dB。逐首歌來看，音素對齊版本在全部 10 首評測曲目上都贏過基準版本，但有 2 首歌（Dongurikorokoro 與 Kisha）反而是只用活動標籤的版本表現更好。

依音素重疊程度細分（重疊聲部數 0 到 4），音素對齊版本在每一個重疊程度上都贏過只用活動標籤的版本，但領先幅度隨重疊程度增加而縮小——例如完全不重疊時 Vo 的 SI-SDRi 是 18.32 dB（活動標籤版本 14.83 dB），重疊到 3 個聲部時掉到 12.15 dB（活動標籤版本 11.74 dB），兩者差距從 3.49 dB 縮小到 0.41 dB。作者的結論是：音素標籤提供的資訊超越單純的人聲活動偵測，等於是把音框做了類別分組，但當多個伴奏聲部與主唱唱著完全相同的音素時，這個方法的效果就會明顯打折扣。

Main results (SI-SDRi, higher is better): the audio-only baseline reaches 9.87 dB for lead vocal (Vo) and 7.15 dB for accompaniment (Other); activity-only conditioning (singing/silent) improves this to 13.05 dB (Vo) and 10.11 dB (Other); full phoneme-alignment conditioning reaches the highest scores, 14.85 dB (Vo) and 11.76 dB (Other). Song by song, phoneme-alignment conditioning beats the baseline on all 10 evaluation songs, though activity-only conditioning actually outperforms the full method on 2 of the 10 songs (Dongurikorokoro and Kisha).

Broken down by phonetic overlap (0 to 4 overlapping parts), phoneme-alignment conditioning beats activity-only conditioning at every overlap level, but the margin shrinks as overlap increases -- e.g. at zero overlap, Vo SI-SDRi is 18.32 dB (versus 14.83 dB for activity-only), while at 3 overlapping parts it drops to 12.15 dB (versus 11.74 dB for activity-only), narrowing the gap from 3.49 dB to 0.41 dB. The authors conclude that phoneme labels provide information beyond simple vocal-activity detection, functioning as a categorical grouping of frames, but the method's benefit clearly diminishes when multiple accompaniment parts sing identical phonemes to the lead vocal.

Knowing When Not to Answer: Pseudo-Ensembles for Abstention in Music Audio-Language Models

知道何時不作答：用偽整體讓音樂音訊語言模型學會拒答

Aanya Maheshwari, Vatsal Raina
arXiv:2609.04362 · cs.SD, cs.CL · 投稿submitted 2026-09-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文把音樂選擇題答案的第一個 token logit 分布，透過三種『保答案不變』的輸入擾動（選項洗牌、音訊加雜訊、標籤替換）構造出不需要重新訓練的『偽整體』，藉此讓只有 2.29 億參數的 TinyMU 學會拒答；在 MuChoMusic 基準（1,187 題）上，效果最好的選項洗牌（M=4）把準確率從單次前向傳遞的 55.7% 推高到 59.2%，用『期望熵』當不確定性指標時，錯誤保留曲線下面積（AUC-ERC）從 0.293 降到 0.261，逼近理論上限 0.098；不過音訊靜音基準本身就有 50.7% 準確率，顯示這個測驗有相當一部分可以純靠文字解出來，而且選項洗牌之所以有效，靠的正是語言模型已知會有的選項順序偏誤，如果未來模型不再受這個偏誤影響，這個方法的效果可能會跟著消失。Why this one: The paper builds a retraining-free 'pseudo-ensemble' out of the first-token logit distribution for music multiple-choice answers, using three answer-preserving input perturbations (option shuffling, audio noise, label swapping) to teach a 229M-parameter TinyMU when to abstain; on the MuChoMusic benchmark (1,187 questions), the best construction, option shuffling (M=4), raises accuracy from a single pass's 55.7% to 59.2%, and using expected entropy as the uncertainty measure drops the Area Under the Error Retention Curve (AUC-ERC) from 0.293 to 0.261, approaching the oracle's 0.098; however, the silent-audio baseline alone already scores 50.7%, showing a substantial share of the benchmark is solvable from text alone, and option shuffling works precisely because it exploits a known language-model position bias -- one that could stop working if future models become invariant to option order.

動機Motivation

音樂音訊語言模型目前主要靠選擇題準確率來評測，這種指標把「矇對」和「真的懂音樂」一視同仁，沒辦法分辨模型是不是在瞎猜。作者主張評測體系少了「模型能不能知道自己不知道、進而選擇不答」這一塊；現有單一模型的信心訊號（softmax 信心值、熵）沒辦法區分「這題本來就模稜兩可」跟「模型根本不懂」，而傳統靠訓練多個獨立模型做整體（ensemble）的不確定性估計方法，對音樂音訊語言模型來說訓練成本太高。

Music audio-language models are currently evaluated mainly on multiple-choice accuracy, a metric that treats a lucky guess identically to genuine musical understanding, with no way to tell them apart. The authors argue evaluation is missing "a way to tell when the model does not know the answer, so that it can abstain instead of guessing"; existing single-model confidence signals (softmax confidence, entropy) cannot separate a genuinely ambiguous question from one the model simply doesn't understand, while training multiple independent models for classic ensemble-based uncertainty estimation is prohibitively expensive for music audio-language systems.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者點名了具體的先前方法。傳統整體方法（Lakshminarayanan 等人、Gal 與 Ghahramani）需要獨立訓練多個模型，成本過高；文字大型語言模型常用的自陳信心（Kadavath 等人、Lin 等人、Xiong 等人）針對的是開放式生成而非音訊；語意一致性方法（Farquhar 等人、Kuhn 等人）算的是語意熵；跨模型一致性（Feng 等人）要靠多個大型語言模型協作。

測試時增強（Ayhan 與 Berens）用輸入擾動做通用的不確定性估計，虛擬整體（Malinin 等人）概念上與這篇論文的「偽整體」（pseudo-ensemble）相近。這篇論文自稱是「第一個針對音樂音訊語言模型做不確定性量化與拒答研究」的工作，關鍵差異在於：它把大型語言模型常被當成問題的「選項順序偏誤」（position bias）反過來當成資源，靠這個既有缺陷、不需要重新訓練就能便宜地構造出偽整體。

The authors name specific prior methods. Classic ensembles (Lakshminarayanan et al.; Gal and Ghahramani) require independently training multiple models, which is too costly; self-reported confidence, common for text LLMs (Kadavath et al.; Lin et al.; Xiong et al.), targets open-ended generation rather than audio; semantic-consistency methods (Farquhar et al.; Kuhn et al.) compute semantic entropy; cross-model agreement (Feng et al.) requires multiple LLMs collaborating.

Test-time augmentation (Ayhan and Berens) uses input perturbation for generic uncertainty estimation, and virtual ensembles (Malinin et al.) are conceptually close to this paper's "pseudo-ensemble." The paper calls itself "the first study of uncertainty quantification and abstention for a music audio-language model," and its key departure is treating position bias -- usually seen as a flaw in language models -- as a resource: exploiting this existing weakness to build a pseudo-ensemble cheaply, with no retraining.

方法Method

作者把音樂選擇題（MCQA）當成對選項符號（A/B/C/D）做分類，直接把生成的第一個 token 的 logit 分布當成預測分布。骨幹模型是 TinyMU（2.29 億參數），架構是把凍結的 MATPAC++ 音訊編碼器透過兩層投影層接到 SmolLM2-135M，投影層與語言模型部分曾在 MusicSkills-3.5M 上微調過；音訊統一轉成 32kHz 單聲道，只取前 10 秒。讀出機制只取生成的第一個位置、只保留 A/B/C/D 四個 token 的 logit 做 softmax 正規化，因此「只需要一次前向傳遞，而不是 K 次」。

作者用三種「保答案不變」的輸入擾動來構造偽整體，不需要重新訓練。主要方法是「選項洗牌」：用排列 πₘ 重新排列 A–D 底下的候選答案，因為正確答案的內容沒變，所以保證答案一致，計算時把每個排列的分布用 πₘ⁻¹ 映射回原始索引再平均，主要設定用 M=4 個成員（含原始順序），另外也測了 M=21；這個方法直接利用文獻中已知的語言模型選項順序偏誤。

第二種是「音訊擾動」：文字不變，只對錄音加高斯白雜訊（σ=0.01）、有損壓縮、或小幅時間平移，強度控制在不會改變答案的範圍內。第三種「標籤替換」（A/B/C/D 換成 1/2/3/4 等）是拿來做診斷用的，作者發現 TinyMU 對標籤格式的偏好很固執，這個方法反而校準得很差。不確定性指標包含單一分布的負信心與熵，以及整體層級的「期望熵的熵」（EoE）、「期望熵」（EE）、兩者相減得到的互信息（知識不確定性）。

The authors frame music MCQA as classification over the option symbols (A/B/C/D), treating the logit distribution at the first generated token as the predictive distribution. The backbone is TinyMU (229M parameters), which couples a frozen MATPAC++ audio encoder to SmolLM2-135M through a two-layer projector, with the projector and language model fine-tuned on MusicSkills-3.5M; audio is standardized to 32kHz mono, truncated to the first 10 seconds. The readout extracts logits only at the first generated position, keeps only the A/B/C/D tokens, and softmax-normalizes them, so it "costs one pass rather than K" passes.

The authors build pseudo-ensembles using three answer-preserving input perturbations, requiring no retraining. The primary method is option shuffling: a permutation reorders the candidate answers under labels A-D -- answer-preserving because the correct answer's content is unchanged -- with each permuted distribution mapped back to the original index before averaging; the main setting uses M=4 members (including the default ordering), with M=21 also tested, and it directly exploits the language-model position bias documented in prior literature.

The second is audio corruption: text is left unchanged while the recording is perturbed with additive Gaussian white noise (sigma=0.01), lossy compression, or a small time shift, kept mild enough to preserve the answer. The third, label swapping (A/B/C/D to 1/2/3/4, etc.), is diagnostic only -- the authors find TinyMU stays stubbornly tied to its label-format preference, so this method calibrates poorly. Uncertainty measures include single-distribution negative confidence and entropy, plus ensemble-level entropy-of-expected (EoE), expected entropy (EE), and mutual information (their difference, "knowledge uncertainty").

限制Limitations

作者列出的限制包括：這篇研究只用了一個模型（TinyMU）和一個基準（MuChoMusic），不確定結論能不能套用到數十億參數的大型系統或其他音樂問答資料集；方法假設模型會把答案綁定在選項符號上，如果模型根本不理會標籤，就需要不同的計分方式；選項洗牌利用的是已知的位置偏誤，如果未來的模型對選項順序變得不敏感，這個方法的效果可能會減弱，而且這個方法只適用於選擇題形式的提示；音訊擾動的強度「我們沒有做過大量調校」；標籤替換在 TinyMU 上失敗，因為模型死守 A/B/C/D 的慣例，這個發現不一定能套用到其他模型。

作者也強調「不確定性排序做得好，不等於機率有校準過」——期望熵較低只代表答案比較可能是對的，不代表 p̄ 是一個校準良好的機率值；作者也坦承沒有針對 TinyMU 是否存在曲風、文化或錄音條件上的系統性偏誤做審查，而且這篇論文本身沒有釋出新模型或新資料集。

The authors list several limitations: the study uses only one model (TinyMU) and one benchmark (MuChoMusic), leaving it unclear whether the findings generalize to multi-billion-parameter systems or other music QA datasets; the method assumes the model binds its answer to the option symbol, so a model that ignores labels entirely would need a different scoring approach; option shuffling exploits a known position bias that may weaken if future models become invariant to option order, and the method applies only to multiple-choice-style prompts; the audio-corruption severity was "not tune[d] extensively"; and label swapping failed on TinyMU because the model stays tied to A/B/C/D conventions, a finding that may not transfer to other models.

The authors also stress that "better uncertainty ranking is not the same as calibration" -- a lower expected entropy only means the answer is more likely correct, not that the probability itself is well-calibrated; they acknowledge not having audited TinyMU for systematic biases by genre, culture, or recording condition, and note the paper releases no new model or dataset.

討論Discussion

主要準確率結果（表一，涵蓋 MuChoMusic 的「知識」與「推理」兩個子集，共 1,187 題四選一問題、來自 644 首曲目）：純音訊靜音基準（等於只靠文字猜）就有 50.7%，顯示這個基準測驗有相當程度可以純靠文字解出來；單次前向傳遞（M=1）達到 55.7%；選項洗牌（M=4）達到 59.2%，比單次前向傳遞提升 3.5 個百分點，其中推理子集的提升（56.8%→61.9%）比知識子集（56.1%→58.2%）更明顯；音訊擾動（M=4）只到 57.2%；標籤替換（M=4）反而讓準確率掉到 42.7%；把選項洗牌的整體規模擴大到 M=21，準確率略升到 60.3%。

不確定性排序指標用「錯誤保留曲線下面積」（AUC-ERC，越低越好）評估：單次前向傳遞的熵是 0.293，負信心與「期望熵的熵」（EoE）都是 0.264，表現最好的是「期望熵」（EE）的 0.261，互信息反而最差（0.306），作者解釋這是因為「整體內部成員在有些整體仍然答對的題目上出現分歧」；作為對照，理論上限的 oracle 排序是 0.098，完全隨機排序是 0.407。三種偽整體構造方式裡，選項洗牌在準確率（59.2%）與不確定性排序（EE 0.261）上都全面勝出，音訊擾動次之（57.2%、EE 0.287），標籤替換兩項都表現很差（42.7%、EE 0.530）。

Main accuracy results (Table 1, spanning MuChoMusic's "knowledge" and "reasoning" subsets, 1,187 four-option questions over 644 tracks): a silent-audio baseline (equivalent to guessing from text alone) already reaches 50.7%, showing the benchmark is substantially solvable from text alone; a single forward pass (M=1) reaches 55.7%; option shuffling (M=4) reaches 59.2%, a 3.5-point gain over the single pass, with a larger gain on the reasoning subset (56.8% to 61.9%) than on knowledge (56.1% to 58.2%); audio corruption (M=4) reaches only 57.2%; label swapping (M=4) actually drops accuracy to 42.7%; and scaling option shuffling to M=21 raises accuracy slightly to 60.3%.

Uncertainty ranking is evaluated via the Area Under the Error Retention Curve (AUC-ERC, lower is better): single-pass entropy scores 0.293, negative confidence and entropy-of-expected (EoE) both score 0.264, the best is expected entropy (EE) at 0.261, and mutual information performs worst at 0.306, which the authors attribute to "members disagree[ing] on questions the ensemble still answers correctly"; for reference, an oracle ranking scores 0.098 and a fully random ranking scores 0.407. Among the three pseudo-ensemble constructions, option shuffling dominates on both accuracy (59.2%) and uncertainty ranking (EE 0.261), audio corruption trails (57.2%, EE 0.287), and label swapping performs poorly on both (42.7%, EE 0.530).
