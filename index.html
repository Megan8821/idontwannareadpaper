Data-Driven Analysis of Musical Form and Harmonic Structure in AI-Generated Popular Music: A Case Study with Suno and Udio

AI 生成流行音樂的曲式與和聲結構資料驅動分析：以 Suno 與 Udio 為例

David Dalmazzo, Laura Cros Vila, Luca Casini, Bob L. T. Sturm
DOI:10.5334/tismir.348 · cs.SD (TISMIR) · 投稿submitted 2025-10-08
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文對 20,002 首 Suno、20,002 首 Udio 與 20,010 首人類創作流行歌曲做和聲與曲式的大規模資料驅動比較，發現 Suno 對 I-V-vi-IV 這類「Axis of Awesome」和弦環的使用比率遠高於人類與 Udio——以「任意順序四和弦視窗」計算，Suno 佔 28.0%、Udio 10.5%、人類僅 8.6%；三和弦頻率與人類語料的相關係數，Udio 為 r=0.981、Suno 僅 r=0.795。作者將此解讀為生成平台的「和聲同質化」，並公開釋出 60,014 筆含逐拍和弦標註的資料集。Why this one: This paper runs a large-scale data-driven comparison of harmony and form across 20,002 Suno songs, 20,002 Udio songs, and 20,010 human-composed pop songs, finding Suno relies on the I-V-vi-IV "Axis of Awesome" chord loop far more than humans or Udio -- 28.0% of Suno songs qualify under an any-order four-chord window versus 10.5% for Udio and 8.6% for humans, and Udio's chord-trigram frequencies correlate with the human corpus at r=0.981 versus only r=0.795 for Suno. The authors read this as harmonic homogenization on generative platforms, and release a 60,014-file dataset with beat-level chord annotations.

動機Motivation

AI 音樂生成平台 Suno 與 Udio 在 2023–2024 年間迅速普及，Suno 宣稱擁有 2,500 萬使用者，Udio 使用者曾一度以每秒產生 10 首歌的速度生成音樂。但這些平台輸出的音樂在和聲與曲式上到底長什麼樣子、跟人類創作有何系統性差異，幾乎沒有大規模的實證研究。這篇論文想回答三個問題：MIR 工具套用到 AI 生成音樂上會遇到什麼特殊考量；AI 生成音樂的和聲跟人類流行音樂比起來如何；兩者在曲式結構上有什麼異同。

AI music generation platforms Suno and Udio spread rapidly in 2023-2024 -- Suno claimed 25 million users, and Udio users were at one point generating 10 songs a second -- yet almost no large-scale empirical study had examined what these platforms' outputs actually sound like harmonically and structurally, or how they systematically differ from human-composed music. The paper asks three questions: what special considerations arise when applying MIR tools to AI-generated music; how AI-generated harmony compares with human popular music; and what structural similarities and differences exist between them.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

和弦進行的統計分析在 MIR 裡並非新鮮事：De Clercq 與 Temperley 對搖滾語料庫的和弦頻率分析、Burgoyne 等人的 Billboard 和弦語料庫，都是奠基於人類創作歌曲的和聲統計研究；自動和弦辨識本身也從早期的 Chordino/NNLS chroma 這類手工特徵方法，演進到近年 BTC 一類的 Transformer 架構。這篇論文並沒有提出新的和弦辨識或結構分析模型——它直接採用既有的 consonance-ACE（Conformer 架構）與 All-In-One（聯合節拍、段落邊界與語意標籤）模型當工具，把分析對象換成 AI 生成平台的輸出。

跟這篇論文最像的其實是網路上早就流傳的「Axis of Awesome」喜劇哏——四個和弦（I-V-vi-IV）幾乎能套用在所有流行歌上——但先前從來沒有人拿這個哏去大規模量化比對 AI 生成音樂與人類音樂各自對這個和弦環的依賴程度。這篇論文的核心貢獻，是把既有的人類和聲語料統計方法，系統性地套用到一個全新的比較對象（生成式 AI 平台）上，而不是方法論本身的創新。

Statistical analysis of chord progressions is not new to MIR: De Clercq and Temperley's corpus study of rock harmony and Burgoyne et al.'s Billboard chord corpus both quantify chord statistics in human-composed songs, and automatic chord estimation itself evolved from handcrafted-feature methods like Chordino/NNLS chroma to recent Transformer architectures such as BTC. This paper proposes no new chord-recognition or structure model -- it applies existing tools (the Conformer-based consonance-ACE and the All-In-One joint beat/segment/label model) directly, changing only what gets analyzed.

The closest prior reference point is really the "Axis of Awesome" comedy bit -- the observation that four chords (I-V-vi-IV) fit almost any pop song -- but nobody had previously used it to quantify, at scale, how strongly AI-generated platforms lean on that loop compared with human music. The paper's core contribution is applying an established human-corpus statistical methodology to a genuinely new object of study (generative AI platforms), not a methodological innovation.

方法Method

作者蒐集三組各約 2 萬首的語料：Suno（每兩小時抓取平台「新歌」播放清單）、Udio（依熱門度搜尋）、以及來自 Million Song Dataset 經 YouTube 連結取得的人類流行歌（Lastfm，20,010 首）。平均長度上 Lastfm（261.3±182.1 秒）明顯長於 Suno（175.2±54.6 秒）與 Udio（143.2±98.6 秒）。和弦以 consonance-ACE 逐拍標註後正規化為羅馬數字，再做三連與四連和弦（trigram/tetragram）統計；曲式段落則用 All-In-One 模型標記並做同樣的 n-gram 分析。

針對 I-V-vi-IV 這個「Axis of Awesome」和弦環，作者定義了兩種計數方式：「同家族」（任意順序出現該四和弦視窗即算）與「嚴格環序」（必須照該固定順序出現）。在同家族、達 10% 門檻的計算下，Suno 有 28.0% 的歌曲符合、Udio 10.5%、Lastfm 僅 8.6%；嚴格環序下更懸殊，Suno 13.6% 對 Lastfm 2.8%、Udio 3.4%。三連和弦頻率跟人類語料的相關係數，Udio 高達 r=0.981，Suno 只有 r=0.795，顯示 Udio 的和聲統計特性比 Suno 更接近人類創作。

The authors assembled three corpora of roughly 20,000 songs each: Suno (scraped from the platform's "New Songs" playlist every two hours), Udio (retrieved by popularity via search), and a human reference (Lastfm, 20,010 tracks) drawn from the Million Song Dataset via YouTube links. Average duration is longest for Lastfm (261.3+/-182.1s), versus 175.2+/-54.6s for Suno and 143.2+/-98.6s for Udio. Chords are labeled per-beat with consonance-ACE, normalized to Roman numerals, and analyzed as chord trigrams/tetragrams; song sections are labeled by the All-In-One model and analyzed the same way.

For the I-V-vi-IV "Axis of Awesome" loop, the authors define two counting rules: "family" (any ordering of the four chords in a window counts) and "literal loop" (must follow the exact cyclic order). At a 10% song-level threshold, 28.0% of Suno songs qualify under the family rule versus 10.5% for Udio and 8.6% for Lastfm; under the stricter literal-loop rule the gap widens further, 13.6% for Suno versus 2.8% (Lastfm) and 3.4% (Udio). Trigram-frequency correlation with the human corpus is r=0.981 for Udio but only r=0.795 for Suno, indicating Udio's harmonic statistics track human music much more closely than Suno's do.

限制Limitations

作者自己點名了好幾個限制：consonance-ACE 對七和弦、擴展音、轉位這類複雜和弦的辨識準確率不如三和弦；和弦轉羅馬數字依賴單一的全域調性估計，遇到轉調或關係大小調模糊時容易出錯；All-In-One 模型本身在段落切分（HR.5F=0.660）與結構標籤（PWF=0.738）上的表現，都還低於已知的人類標記者一致性，代表結構分析這一層本身就帶有雜訊。

另一個作者承認但沒有完全解決的混淆變因，是歌曲長度：Suno（175 秒）與 Udio（143 秒）平均都比 Lastfm（261 秒）短，較短的歌曲本來就沒有太多篇幅發展複雜曲式，這會讓「AI 音樂曲式較單調」的結論，有一部分其實是長度差異造成，而不完全是生成模型本身的傾向。此外，平台上的風格標籤只反映使用者要求的表面美學（音色、情緒、樂器），不等於實際的和聲功能。

The authors name several limitations themselves: consonance-ACE is less accurate on complex chord types (sevenths, extensions, inversions) than on plain triads; converting chords to Roman numerals depends on a single global key estimate per track, which can fail under modulation or major/relative-minor ambiguity; and the All-In-One model's own segmentation (HR.5F=0.660) and structure-labeling (PWF=0.738) scores sit below estimated human inter-rater agreement, meaning the structural layer itself carries noise.

A confound the authors acknowledge but do not fully resolve is duration: Suno (175s) and Udio (143s) average shorter than Lastfm (261s), and shorter songs simply have less room for structural elaboration -- so part of the "AI music has simpler form" conclusion could reflect duration rather than the generative model's own tendencies. Platform genre tags also record only the surface aesthetic a user requested (timbre, mood, instrumentation), not actual harmonic function.

討論Discussion

作者將發現解讀為一種「演算法單一文化」：Suno 對西方流行和聲模板的收斂程度遠高於 Udio 與人類語料，最明顯的例子是 chorus-verse-chorus-outro 這個收尾套路，在 Suno 裡出現 4,032 次，是 Lastfm（2,061 次）的近兩倍、Udio（913 次）的三倍以上。作者認為這可能來自訓練資料本身的頻率偏誤被生成模型放大，但同時指出 Udio 表現出比「同樣訓練資料分布下預期」更高的多樣性，暗示模型架構與生成策略本身、而不只是訓練資料，也在決定輸出的和聲多樣性。

這篇論文也把討論延伸到創作能動性的問題：如果平台輸出高度收斂到少數幾種和聲與曲式套路，那麼使用者透過這些工具「創作」音樂，實際上有多少自由度，值得商榷——作者直言這類平台「可能是為了生成熟悉、具商業可行性的內容而最佳化，而非支援實驗或技能養成」。作者也公開釋出含 60,014 筆逐拍和弦與段落標註的完整資料集（CC BY 4.0）供後續研究使用。

The authors read their findings as a kind of algorithmic monoculture: Suno converges on Western pop harmonic templates far more strongly than Udio or the human corpus, most visibly in the chorus-verse-chorus-outro closing formula, which appears 4,032 times in Suno versus 2,061 in Lastfm (nearly double) and 913 in Udio (more than triple). They attribute this partly to generative models amplifying frequency biases already present in training data, but note that Udio shows more diversity than would be expected from a presumably comparable training distribution, suggesting model architecture and generation strategy -- not training data alone -- shape harmonic diversity.

The paper extends this into a question about creative agency: if a platform's output converges heavily onto a handful of harmonic and formal templates, how much freedom does a user actually exercise when "composing" with it? The authors state plainly that such platforms "may be optimized to generate familiar, commercially viable content rather than to support experimentation or skill acquisition." They also release the full dataset -- 60,014 files with beat-level chord and section annotations -- under CC BY 4.0 for follow-up work.

Cross-Cultural Music Similarity: Bridging Human Perception, Signal Processing, and Foundation Models

跨文化音樂相似度：銜接人類感知、訊號處理與基礎模型

Charilaos Papaioannou, Emmanouil Benetos, Alexandros Potamianos
DOI:10.5334/tismir.341 · cs.SD (TISMIR) · 投稿submitted 2025-09-15
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文做了第一個大規模「跨文化音樂相似度」評測，涵蓋西方、地中海/中東（佛朗明哥、阿拉伯-安達魯西亞、土耳其木卡姆）、南亞（印度斯坦、卡納提克）與京劇九種音樂傳統，請 125 位橫跨 21 國的參與者對 1,130 組音檔評分，再拿訊號處理特徵（旋律、節奏、和聲、音色）與七個基礎模型（MERT、CultureMERT、CLAP 系列、Qwen2-Audio）去對比。單一方法裡表現最好的 CLAP-Music&Speech，三元組一致性 64.9%；把訊號處理特徵與基礎模型輸出一起做線性回歸集成後，三元組一致性提升到 67.0%，平均絕對誤差從旋律特徵單獨的 29.5% 降到 19.7%（相對誤差降低約 25–30%）。但論文也點出一個 trade-off：CLAP 跟人類判斷相關性最高，但跨文化區辨力偏低（分離比 1.366）；Qwen2-Audio 區辨力最高（1.602），跟人類判斷的相關性反而較低。Why this one: This paper runs the first large-scale cross-cultural music similarity evaluation, spanning nine traditions -- Western, Mediterranean/Middle Eastern (flamenco, Arab-Andalusian, Turkish makam), South Asian (Hindustani, Carnatic), and Jingju (Beijing Opera) -- with 125 participants from 21 countries rating 1,130 audio pairs, compared against signal-processing features (melody, rhythm, harmony, timbre) and seven foundation models (MERT, CultureMERT, CLAP variants, Qwen2-Audio). The best single method, CLAP-Music&Speech, reaches 64.9% triplet agreement; a linear-regression ensemble combining signal-processing features with foundation-model outputs raises that to 67.0% and cuts mean absolute error from 29.5% (melody alone) to 19.7% (a 25-30% relative error reduction). The paper also flags a trade-off: CLAP correlates best with human judgment but discriminates poorly between cultures (separation ratio 1.366), while Qwen2-Audio discriminates cultures best (1.602) yet correlates less well with human perception.

動機Motivation

音樂相似度計算大多是西方中心的：傳統聲學特徵如色度圖（chroma）假設十二平均律，漏掉許多非西方音樂裡關鍵的微分音裝飾；深度學習模型則可能把訓練資料裡的文化偏誤原封不動繼承下來。這篇論文想知道：現有的訊號處理特徵與基礎模型，到底有多接近人類真實的跨文化音樂相似度判斷？又各自在哪些面向上失準？

Music similarity computation is mostly Western-centric: traditional acoustic features like chroma assume 12-tone equal temperament and miss the microtonal ornamentation central to many non-Western traditions, while deep learning models risk inheriting the cultural biases baked into their training data. The paper asks how closely existing signal-processing features and foundation models actually track real human cross-cultural similarity judgments, and where each falls short.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文明確比較兩類既有方法：一類是手工訊號處理特徵，包括 PYIN 基頻追蹤、動態規劃節拍偵測、CENS 色度圖與 Krumhansl-Schmuckler 調性估計、MFCC 音色特徵；另一類是自監督/對比式基礎模型，包括以 BERT 架構訓練的 MERT（及其文化調適版 CultureMERT）、對比式音訊-文字模型 CLAP（Music、Music&Speech 兩種變體）、以及多模態的 Qwen2-Audio。這些都是既有系統，論文本身不提出新模型。

這篇論文真正的差異，在於它是「第一個」把這些既有工具擺在同一套跨文化人類評分基準下直接比較，而不是像過去的研究那樣，各自在西方語料庫上做評測、宣稱通用性。論文特別指出 Krumhansl-Schmuckler 這類調性估計方法「帶有大小調西方中心假設，不適用於非三度疊置的和聲系統」，直接點名了主流和聲分析工具在跨文化情境下的失效之處。

The paper directly compares two established families of methods: hand-crafted signal-processing features -- PYIN pitch tracking, dynamic-programming beat tracking, CENS chroma with Krumhansl-Schmuckler key estimation, and MFCC timbre descriptors -- and self-supervised/contrastive foundation models, including the BERT-style MERT (and its culture-adapted variant CultureMERT), the contrastive audio-text model CLAP (Music and Music&Speech variants), and the multimodal Qwen2-Audio. All of these are existing systems; the paper proposes no new model.

What actually sets this paper apart is being the first to place these existing tools side by side under one cross-cultural human-rated benchmark, rather than each being evaluated separately on Western corpora and assumed to generalize. The paper specifically calls out that Krumhansl-Schmuckler-style key estimation carries "Western-centric assumptions (major/minor chord templates)" unsuited to non-tertian harmonic systems, directly naming where a mainstream harmonic-analysis tool breaks down cross-culturally.

方法Method

作者從九個語料庫（MagnaTagATune、FMA-medium 等西方語料，佛朗明哥的 CorpusCOFLA、阿拉伯-安達魯西亞、希臘 Lyra、土耳其木卡姆、印度斯坦、卡納提克、京劇）各取樣，共抽出 468 段 20 秒音檔，組成 1,130 組配對，請 125 位來自 21 國、涵蓋 58 種音樂文化背景的參與者，在整體相似度、文化相似度、推薦層級相似度三個維度上用 9 點量表評分。

訊號處理端計算旋律（PYIN 雙解析度 12/24 音級）、節奏（節拍追蹤、起音偵測）、和聲（CENS 色度圖、Krumhansl-Schmuckler 調性）、音色（MFCC）四類特徵；基礎模型端用 MERT、CultureMERT、CLAP、Qwen2-Audio 抽嵌入向量算餘弦相似度。作者用五項指標（Kendall τ、Spearman ρ、NDCG、三元組一致性、MAE）量化計算結果跟人類評分的吻合程度，並用線性回歸與 LightGBM 兩種方式，把訊號處理特徵跟基礎模型輸出集成起來。

The authors sample from nine corpora (Western MagnaTagATune and FMA-medium; flamenco's CorpusCOFLA; Arab-Andalusian; Greek Lyra; Turkish makam; Hindustani; Carnatic; Jingju), drawing 468 20-second clips into 1,130 pairs, rated by 125 participants from 21 countries spanning 58 musical-cultural backgrounds on a 9-point scale across three dimensions: overall similarity, cultural similarity, and recommendation-level similarity.

On the signal-processing side they compute melody (dual-resolution 12/24-bin PYIN), rhythm (beat tracking, onset detection), harmony (CENS chroma, Krumhansl-Schmuckler key), and timbre (MFCC) features; on the foundation-model side they extract embeddings from MERT, CultureMERT, CLAP, and Qwen2-Audio and compute cosine similarity. Five metrics (Kendall's tau, Spearman's rho, NDCG, triplet agreement, MAE) quantify agreement with human ratings, and linear regression plus LightGBM combine signal-processing features with foundation-model outputs into ensembles.

限制Limitations

作者自陳最大的限制是參與者組成：125 位參與者裡歐洲籍佔 84.8%（其中希臘一國就佔 62.4%），來自語料庫所涵蓋文化背景的參與者反而偏少；1,130 組配對平均每組大約只有一位參與者評分，無法做正式的評分者間一致性分析。基礎模型本身的訓練資料也偏向流行音樂，對傳統世界音樂的理解可能有限。

訊號處理端也有明確的設計侷限：PYIN 只做單旋律基頻抽取，在複音織度下不見得對應到人類實際聽到的旋律線；和聲特徵依賴的 Krumhansl-Schmuckler 調性估計，對非大小調、非三度疊置的和聲系統並不適用。整體而言，不管是訊號處理特徵還是基礎模型，跟人類評分的相關係數都偏低（Spearman ρ 多半在 0.15–0.19 之間），顯示現有方法離「真的理解」人類跨文化相似度判斷還有明顯差距。

The authors' biggest self-acknowledged limitation is participant composition: 84.8% of the 125 participants are European (62.4% from Greece alone), with relatively few from the cultures the corpora actually represent; each of the 1,130 pairs was rated by roughly one participant on average, ruling out formal inter-rater reliability analysis. The foundation models themselves are trained mostly on popular music, potentially limiting their understanding of traditional world music.

The signal-processing side has clear design limits too: PYIN extracts only monophonic pitch, which may not track the perceived melodic line in polyphonic textures, and the Krumhansl-Schmuckler key estimation behind the harmony features assumes major/minor tonality unsuited to non-tertian harmonic systems. Overall, correlation with human ratings stays modest for both signal-processing features and foundation models (Spearman's rho mostly 0.15-0.19), showing current methods remain some distance from genuinely capturing human cross-cultural similarity judgment.

討論Discussion

論文點出一個核心的 trade-off：對人類判斷相關性最好的 CLAP-Music&Speech，跨文化區辨力（分離比，衡量「文化間」與「文化內」相似度的區隔程度）只有 1.366；反而是跟人類相關性較弱的 Qwen2-Audio，區辨力達到 1.602，是所有計算方法裡最高的（人類本身的文化相似度分離比是 2.361）。這代表「優化成貼近人類整體判斷」跟「能清楚分辨文化邊界」，在目前的模型裡並不必然同時達成。

另一個有趣的發現是：人類評分裡，旋律始終是解釋力最高的因素（整體相似度貢獻 31.0%、文化相似度 34.3%、推薦層級 34.0%），但多數基礎模型反而更依賴音色特徵去判斷相似度（例如 CultureMERT-TA 音色權重高達 62.5%）。這代表基礎模型學到的「相似」，跟人類實際依賴的線索，可能是系統性不同的兩套東西——這也解釋了為什麼把兩者用回歸集成起來，能比任何單一方法都明顯進步（MAE 從 29.5% 降到 19.7%）。

The paper identifies a core trade-off: CLAP-Music&Speech, best-correlated with human judgment, has a cross-cultural separation ratio (how clearly it distinguishes between-culture from within-culture similarity) of only 1.366, while Qwen2-Audio, which correlates less well with humans, reaches the highest separation ratio of any computational method at 1.602 (humans themselves score 2.361 on cultural-similarity separation). Optimizing for alignment with overall human judgment and achieving clear cultural discrimination are not the same thing in current models.

A further finding: melody consistently explains the most variance in human ratings (31.0% for overall similarity, 34.3% cultural, 34.0% recommendation-level), while most foundation models lean more on timbre (e.g., CultureMERT-TA weights timbre at 62.5%). This suggests foundation models learn a systematically different notion of "similar" than the cues humans actually rely on -- which likely explains why combining both via regression outperforms any single method by a clear margin (MAE dropping from 29.5% to 19.7%).

A Lightweight Two-Branch Architecture for Multi-Instrument Transcription via Note-Level Contrastive Clustering

透過音符層級對比式聚類實現的輕量雙分支多樂器轉譜架構

Ruigang Li, Yongxu Zhu
DOI:10.5334/tismir.300 · cs.SD (TISMIR) · 投稿submitted 2025-06-28
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文提出一個輕量雙分支架構，把音色無關的轉譜骨幹接上一個獨立的音色編碼分支，在「音符層級」（而非逐幀）做對比式聚類（InfoNCE，溫度 τ=0.15），讓模型能在指定樂器數量 K 的情況下，對任意樂器組合同時做轉譜與動態分離。模型僅 64,905 個參數，在 BACH10 二重奏分離轉譜上達到 FS=84.6%，超越先前 RNN 式方法（Tanaka et al. 2020）的 79.6%；消融顯示換成 InfoNCE 對比損失比原本的 MSE 親和力損失「穩定且顯著」更好，但改用 Transformer 自注意力反而「顯著傷害效能」，因為自注意力會在音符邊界重疊處把原本可分的音色流錯誤地連在一起。Why this one: This paper proposes a lightweight two-branch architecture that adds a dedicated timbre-encoding branch to a timbre-agnostic transcription backbone and clusters at the note level (rather than per-frame) using InfoNCE contrastive loss (temperature 0.15), letting the model jointly transcribe and dynamically separate an arbitrary combination of instruments given a specified instrument count K. With only 64,905 parameters, it reaches FS=84.6% on BACH10 two-instrument separated transcription, beating the prior RNN-based method (Tanaka et al. 2020) at 79.6%; an ablation shows the InfoNCE contrastive loss "consistently and significantly" outperforms the original MSE-based affinity loss, but swapping in Transformer self-attention instead "significantly hurt performance" because self-attention spuriously links otherwise-separable timbre streams at overlapping note boundaries.

動機Motivation

多樂器自動轉譜過去常被當成固定類別的分類問題：模型假設樂器種類固定、需要大量訓練資料，而且一旦遇到訓練時沒看過的音色組合就失效，形同一本「音色字典」，而非真正理解音色的系統。這篇論文想做的，是讓轉譜系統能在給定樂器數量的前提下，對任意樂器組合（包括沒看過的音色）動態分離出各自的音符序列，同時維持模型輕量、可以在資源受限的裝置（例如瀏覽器）上運作。

Multi-instrument automatic transcription has often been treated as classification over a fixed set of categories: models assume a fixed instrument roster, need large amounts of training data, and fail on timbre combinations unseen during training, functioning more as a "timbre dictionary" than a system that actually understands timbre. This paper aims for a transcription system that, given only the number of instruments, can dynamically separate note sequences for arbitrary instrument combinations -- including unseen timbres -- while staying lightweight enough for resource-constrained deployment such as a web browser.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把既有工作分成三條線。第一條是通用轉譜骨幹，如 Hawthorne 等人（2018）、Bittner 等人（2022）的 BasicPitch——輕量全卷積網路，但沒有處理多樂器分離。第二條是明確做「音色分離轉譜」的方法：Gardner 等人（2022）用序列到序列模型明確指定樂器；Cwitkowitz 等人（2024）的 Timbre-Trap；Tanaka 等人（2020）則是第一個把深度聚類用在音色分離轉譜上的方法，採用 RNN 加 MSE 親和力損失做逐幀聚類。第三條是深度聚類本身的源頭：Hershey 等人（2016）最早把深度聚類用在語者分離，Luo 等人（2018）則提出可學習吸子處理來源數量可變的分離問題。

這篇論文跟 Tanaka et al.（2020）最直接可比——同樣是「轉譜骨幹+深度聚類」的框架，但差異在三處：骨幹換成輕量的雙分支 CNN（僅 64,905 參數）而非 RNN；聚類損失從 MSE 親和力換成 InfoNCE 對比損失；聚類單位從逐幀換成先聚合成完整音符事件再聚類。這些改動不是全新典範，而是針對 Tanaka et al. 框架裡三個具體環節，各自替換成論文認為更合適的既有技術組合。

The paper groups prior work into three lines. The first is general transcription backbones, such as Hawthorne et al. (2018) and Bittner et al.'s (2022) BasicPitch -- a lightweight fully-convolutional network that does not handle multi-instrument separation. The second is timbre-separated transcription proper: Gardner et al. (2022) use a sequence-to-sequence model with explicit instrument assignment; Cwitkowitz et al. (2024) propose Timbre-Trap; Tanaka et al. (2020) were first to apply deep clustering to timbre-separated transcription, using an RNN with frame-level MSE-based affinity loss. The third is deep clustering itself: Hershey et al. (2016) first applied it to speaker separation, and Luo et al. (2018) introduced learnable attractors for variable-source separation.

The most directly comparable prior work is Tanaka et al. (2020) -- the same "transcription backbone plus deep clustering" framework -- but this paper differs in three specific places: a lightweight two-branch CNN backbone (only 64,905 parameters) instead of an RNN; InfoNCE contrastive loss instead of MSE-based affinity loss; and clustering aggregated complete note events rather than individual frames. These are not a new paradigm but three targeted substitutions within Tanaka et al.'s existing framework.

方法Method

架構分兩條並行分支處理 HCQT 輸入：音色無關分支輸出逐幀與起音的活躍度後驗機率圖；音色編碼分支則產生 D 維嵌入供聚類使用。作者引入「EnergyNorm」，依 Parseval 定理把每一幀的能量變異數標準化成單位變異數（卡方分布振幅包絡），取代 BasicPitch 原本的做法；卷積核改用頻率軸上核大小 25、膨脹係數 3 的膨脹卷積，涵蓋前後兩個八度（BasicPitch 原本 39 個 bin 的核只能涵蓋半個八度）；分類損失把交叉熵換成 focal loss（γ=1，音符與起音正類權重分別 0.2、0.06）以處理類別不平衡。

聚類部分捨棄 Tanaka et al. 原本逐幀 MSE 親和力損失，改用溫度 τ=0.15 的 InfoNCE 對比損失，作者的論證是「反平行對齊比正交能拉開更大的類間距離」，而且單一音色時 InfoNCE 損失會自然消失、不會強迫模型硬分群。後處理上，不是在逐幀層級做聚類，而是先把時頻格聚合成完整音符事件再做譜聚類，大幅減少聚類樣本數也減少音符碎片化。訓練資料用 MusicNet（34 小時、330 首、11 種樂器，重新標註版 MusicNetEM）加上 24 小時、33 種 General MIDI 音色的合成資料；評測在 BACH10、PHENICX、URMP 上進行。

The architecture has two parallel branches processing HCQT input: a timbre-agnostic branch producing frame-level and onset activation posteriorgrams, and a timbre-encoding branch producing D-dimensional embeddings for clustering. The authors introduce "EnergyNorm," which standardizes each frame's energy variance to unity per Parseval's theorem (yielding a chi-squared-distributed amplitude envelope), replacing BasicPitch's original normalization; the convolution kernel becomes a dilated convolution with frequency-axis kernel size 25 and dilation factor 3, covering two octaves in each direction (versus BasicPitch's 39-bin kernel, which effectively covers only half an octave); and cross-entropy is replaced with focal loss (gamma=1, positive-class weights 0.2 for notes and 0.06 for onsets) to handle class imbalance.

For clustering, the authors replace Tanaka et al.'s frame-level MSE affinity loss with InfoNCE contrastive loss at temperature 0.15, arguing "antiparallel alignment yields larger interclass distances than orthogonality," and that with a single timbre the InfoNCE loss naturally vanishes rather than forcing an artificial split. Post-processing aggregates time-frequency bins into complete note events before spectral clustering, rather than clustering at the frame level, sharply reducing the number of clustering samples and note fragmentation. Training data is MusicNet (34 hours, 330 songs, 11 instruments, using refined MusicNetEM annotations) plus 24 hours of synthetic data across 33 General MIDI programs; evaluation runs on BACH10, PHENICX, and URMP.

限制Limitations

在 BACH10 二重奏分離轉譜上，論文方法 FS=84.6%，勝過 Tanaka et al.（2020）的 RNN 基線（79.6%）；但隨著混音樂器數增加，分離品質明顯下滑——三重奏 FS=80.1%、四重奏只剩 72.7%，分離轉譜與音色無關轉譜的比值（FS/FF）也從二重奏的 99.0% 降到四重奏的 89.9%，顯示樂器數一多，聚類就更容易出錯。系統目前仍需要在推論時手動指定樂器數量 K，也無法處理「多樂器同時彈奏同一音高」這種完全重疊的情況。

消融實驗還揭露兩個意外的負面結果：一是拿掉可學習 CQT、改回手工 CQT 參數反而更穩健——可學習版本在合成資料上「記憶而非泛化」，PHENICX 音符 F1 從手工版的 63.2% 掉到 47.9%；二是引入 Transformer 自注意力「顯著傷害效能」，因為自注意力在音符邊界重疊處，會把本該分開的音色流錯誤地連結起來，反而拉近了不同樂器的嵌入距離。合成資料訓練的模型也顯著遜於真實資料訓練的版本，作者歸因於合成音符涵蓋全部 84 個音高但真實樂器只佔一段音域，以及合成器缺乏真實演奏的動態變化。

On BACH10 two-instrument separated transcription, the method reaches FS=84.6%, beating Tanaka et al.'s (2020) RNN baseline at 79.6%; but separation quality drops clearly as the number of mixed instruments grows -- FS=80.1% for three instruments and only 72.7% for four -- and the ratio of separated to timbre-agnostic transcription (FS/FF) falls from 99.0% at two instruments to 89.9% at four, showing clustering gets harder as instrument count rises. The system still requires manually specifying the instrument count K at inference, and cannot handle instruments playing the exact same pitch simultaneously.

The ablations also surface two unexpected negative results: replacing the learnable CQT with hand-crafted CQT parameters is actually more robust -- the learnable version "led to memorization rather than generalization" on synthetic data, dropping PHENICX note F1 from 63.2% (hand-crafted) to 47.9%; and adding Transformer self-attention "significantly hurt performance," because self-attention spuriously links otherwise-separable timbre streams at overlapping note boundaries, pulling different instruments' embeddings closer together rather than apart. Models trained on synthetic data also clearly underperformed those trained on real data, which the authors attribute to synthetic notes spanning the full 84-pitch range (while real instruments occupy a narrower register) and synthesizers lacking real performance dynamics.

討論Discussion

作者做的消融很誠實地指出：不是每個看似合理的技術升級都有幫助——把 MSE 親和力損失換成 InfoNCE 對比損失，「穩定且顯著」地改善了聚類效果，是這篇論文最站得住腳的貢獻；但把 CNN 換成 Transformer 自注意力，以及把手工 CQT 換成可學習 CQT，兩個方向都反而讓效能變差。這種「負結果也如實報告」的態度，對於後續想沿用類似架構的人相當有參考價值，因為它明確劃出了哪些常見的架構升級在這個任務上不管用。

效率面向上，這個僅 64,905 參數的模型在 Intel Ultra 7 CPU 的瀏覽器環境裡，轉錄一段 301 秒的二重奏只需 17.1 秒，遠快於 BasicPitch 的 39.1 秒，顯示論文標題強調的「輕量、可部署」確實有兌現。整體而言，這篇論文的貢獻不是提出全新的轉譜典範，而是在 Tanaka et al.（2020）建立的「骨幹+深度聚類」框架裡，系統性驗證了三個具體替換（對比式損失、音符層級聚類、輕量雙分支骨幹）何者有效、何者無效。

The ablations are notably honest that not every plausible architectural upgrade helps: switching from MSE affinity loss to InfoNCE contrastive loss "consistently and significantly" improved clustering, the paper's most defensible contribution; but switching from CNN to Transformer self-attention, and from hand-crafted to learnable CQT, both made things worse. Reporting these negative results plainly is genuinely useful for anyone building on a similar architecture, since it marks out which common upgrades don't work for this task.

On efficiency, this 64,905-parameter model transcribes a 301-second duet in 17.1 seconds on an Intel Ultra 7 CPU inside a browser, far faster than BasicPitch's 39.1 seconds, so the paper's "lightweight, deployable" framing holds up in practice. Overall, the paper's contribution is not a new transcription paradigm but a systematic test, within Tanaka et al.'s (2020) existing backbone-plus-deep-clustering framework, of which of three specific substitutions (contrastive loss, note-level clustering, lightweight two-branch backbone) actually help and which don't.
