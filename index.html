Beyond Piano: Cross-Instrument MIDI Velocity Estimation via Differentiable SoundFont Proxies

不只是鋼琴：透過可微分 SoundFont 代理進行跨樂器 MIDI 力度估計

Zhanhong He, Hanyu Meng, David Defeng Huang, Roberto Togneri
arXiv:2608.08985 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：大部分公開 MIDI 檔案的力度（velocity）都是常數 64，因為只有裝了感測器的 Yamaha Disklavier 鋼琴才有真人演奏的力度標籤，吉他、小提琴等樂器完全沒有對應的標註資料。這篇論文不是硬套用鋼琴模型，而是先在鋼琴上用真實標籤訓練一個力度估計器 VeloEst，再設計了兩條跨樂器「調適」路徑：一條在波形空間比對可微分合成器（Diff-Synth），另一條是這篇論文提出的、在聲學參數空間比對的可微分 SoundFont 代理（Diff-SFProxy）。實驗直接顯示波形空間監督在跨樂器遷移上會失敗甚至比什麼都不做更差，而參數空間監督穩定勝出，這個「哪一種監督訊號在遷移時比較穩健」的對照做得乾淨，故列為深度分析。Why this one: Most public MIDI files carry a constant velocity of 64, because only sensor-equipped Yamaha Disklavier pianos produce real performance velocity labels — guitar, violin, and most other instruments have no comparable ground truth. Rather than forcing a piano-trained model onto other instruments, this paper first trains a velocity estimator (VeloEst) on piano with real labels, then designs two cross-instrument adaptation paths: one matching in waveform space via a differentiable synthesizer (Diff-Synth), and the paper's proposed alternative matching in acoustic-parameter space via a differentiable SoundFont proxy (Diff-SFProxy). The experiments cleanly show waveform-space supervision can fail on cross-instrument transfer — sometimes underperforming doing nothing at all — while parameter-space supervision holds up robustly. That clean contrast in which supervision signal survives domain transfer is why it's flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

MIDI 的力度數值控制著音符渲染出來的音量與音色細節，是表達性演奏的核心參數之一，但絕大多數公開釋出的 MIDI 檔案裡，這個欄位根本不是真人彈奏的紀錄，而是被匯出工具寫死成一個常數（通常是 64）。學習式的力度估計之所以在鋼琴上做得起來，完全是託了 Yamaha Disklavier 這類裝了力度感測鍵盤的錄音設備的福——彈奏當下就同步記錄了每個音符的真實力度，形成乾淨的監督訊號。但吉他、小提琴這些樂器沒有這種感測基礎設施，換句話說，「怎麼在完全沒有真人力度標籤的樂器上，訓練一個可信的力度估計器」本身就是一個標籤稀缺問題。作者的切入點是：與其硬造合成資料去訓練（會引入合成與真實錄音之間的落差），不如先在有標籤的鋼琴上訓練好模型，再想辦法把這個模型「調適」到目標樂器上——調適的判準是，估計出來的力度值，拿去渲染出的音訊要跟真實演奏音訊的動態聽起來一致。

MIDI velocity governs a note's rendered loudness and timbral detail, making it one of the central parameters of expressive performance — yet in most publicly released MIDI files, the field isn't a recording of a real performance at all, but a constant baked in by an export tool (typically 64). Learning-based velocity estimation works for piano only because sensor-equipped instruments like the Yamaha Disklavier capture each note's true velocity as it's played, yielding a clean supervision signal. Guitar, violin, and most other instruments have no comparable sensing infrastructure, so training a trustworthy velocity estimator for them is fundamentally a label-scarcity problem. The authors' angle: rather than manufacturing synthetic training data (which introduces a synthetic-to-real gap), first train a solid model on labeled piano data, then adapt that model to a target instrument — where "adapted correctly" is judged by whether the estimated velocities, once rendered, reproduce the dynamics of real performance audio.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把相關工作放在兩個脈絡下看。第一是既有的力度估計工作幾乎全部綁死在鋼琴上，原因正是前述的感測器標籤可得性，這使得「跨樂器」本身變成一塊沒什麼人碰過的空白。第二是可微分數位訊號處理（differentiable DSP）與可微分合成器的路線：這條路線的優勢是可以把「渲染出的音訊跟真實音訊的差距」直接寫成可以反向傳播的損失函數，讓模型透過梯度下降去調整輸入（這裡是力度）。論文選擇站在這條路線上，但同時指出一個問題：如果比對的對象是完整波形，那麼跟力度無關的因素（錄音環境、麥克風特性、合成器音色跟真實樂器的音色落差）全部會混進損失函數裡，變成雜訊甚至誤導梯度方向。这正是論文提出 Diff-SFProxy、把監督訊號從「完整波形」降到「跟力度直接相關的少數聲學參數」的動機所在。

The paper situates related work along two threads. First, prior velocity-estimation work is almost entirely piano-bound, precisely because of the sensor-label availability described above — leaving cross-instrument estimation a largely untouched gap. Second is the line of differentiable digital signal processing and differentiable synthesizers: its appeal is that the gap between rendered and real audio can be written directly as a backpropagatable loss, letting the model adjust its input (here, velocity) via gradient descent. The paper adopts this line but flags a problem: if the comparison target is the full waveform, then everything unrelated to velocity — recording environment, microphone characteristics, the timbral mismatch between a synthesizer and a real instrument — gets folded into the loss too, becoming noise or even misleading the gradient. That is exactly the motivation behind Diff-SFProxy: reducing the supervision signal from the entire waveform down to a small set of acoustic parameters directly tied to velocity.

方法Method

兩條調適路徑：第一條 Diff-Synth 用凍結權重的樂器專屬可微分合成器（鋼琴用 DDSP-Piano、吉他用 DDSP-Guitar-Synth），把音符事件（音高、起始時間、時長）跟預測出的力度一起餵進去渲染出音訊 ã，再用多解析度的 STFT 頻譜損失去比對 ã 跟真實演奏音訊 a：ℒ_audio = Σ_ω∈Ω(‖|STFT_ω(ã)|−|STFT_ω(a)|‖₁ + ‖log(|STFT_ω(ã)|+ε)−log(|STFT_ω(a)|+ε)‖₁)，其中 Ω={2048,1024,512,256,128,64} 涵蓋六種不同的視窗大小。

第二條、也是這篇論文的核心創新，是 Diff-SFProxy：不去比對整段波形，而是設計了兩個跟力度直接相關的聲學參數，從真實演奏音訊裡萃取出來當監督目標。第一個是「音高條件諧波能量」PHE：PHE_n = log(1+Σ_{h=1}^{H_n} 1/(|ℬ_{n,h}||𝒯_n|) Σ_{k∈ℬ_{n,h}}Σ_{t∈𝒯_n} X(k,t)²)，在音符起始後120毫秒的窗口𝒯_n內，沿著該音符最多5個保留諧波(H_n≤5)附近±1個頻帶ℬ_{n,h}去累加能量。第二個是「起始窗頻譜通量」OSF：OSF_n = log(1+1/|𝒪_n| Σ_{t∈𝒪_n}Σ_k[X(k,t)−X(k,t−1)]₊)，量測起始前20毫秒到起始後80毫秒這個窗口𝒪_n內的寬頻能量增幅。這兩個參數合起來組成目標向量 Z。

代理模型架構：一個6層、d=256、8個注意力頭、前饋層寬1024、dropout 0.1的Transformer編碼器，把一段5秒內最多N_max=64個音符的序列，映成預測出的(PHE_n, OSF_n)配對Z̃。每個音符token由三部分組成：可學習的音高嵌入、把起始時間/時長/力度映射成加性token方向的共享可學習基底、以及用MLP做的位置編碼；輸出頭是d→d→2維。調適損失用Huber loss（β=1.0，對離群值穩健）：ℒ_params = Huber_β(Z̃−Z)。

兩條路徑共用的正則化項：一個「反崩塌」正則化ℒ_anti = (v̄−μ)²+max(0,σ²_min−Var(v̂))（μ=0.5，σ²_min=0.01），防止模型為了討好損失函數把所有力度都預測成同一個值；另一個是「軟飽和懲罰」ℒ_satu = (1/N)Σ_{n=1}^N[max(0,v̂_n−τ)]²（τ=0.95），懲罰預測值逼近上限。總損失是ℒ=ℒ_main+λ_antiℒ_anti+λ_satuℒ_satu，三者權重比為1:0.2:0.4。

資料與實驗設定：來源域是鋼琴MAESTRO V3（198.7小時，有真實力度標籤），目標域是吉他GAPS資料集（14小時，尼龍弦古典吉他）；額外用SMD（4.7小時鋼琴）跟François Leduc FL資料集（4.0小時、混合各種吉他）做跨資料集驗證。SoundFont選用Salamander Grand Piano（鋼琴）與Spanish Classical Guitar（吉他），另外拿YDP Grand Piano做敏感度測試。

診斷實驗（力度復原）：在合成音訊上測試「代理模型的梯度方向是否真的指向正確的力度」——把MIDI用SoundFont渲染成音訊，把力度重設成常數64，再用梯度下降去優化力度，直到代理模型的預測跟目標PHE/OSF吻合。5秒切片下，鋼琴的每音符MAE從初始37.9降到12.2（域內，改善67.8%）、壓力測試下從38.5降到17.7（改善54.0%）；吉他從34.6降到9.1（域內，改善73.7%）、壓力測試下從33.8降到10.7（改善68.3%）。5秒切片被選為預設設定，10秒切片沒有帶來額外增益。

跨樂器主結果：基線包括「常數力度64」、「VeloEst零樣本套用（鋼琴預訓練、不調適）」、「VeloEst+Diff-Synth」、「VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy」。評估指標用Bark尺度特定響度相關係數r_BSSL、Bark尺度總響度相關係數r_BSTL（沒有真實標籤的資料集只能用這兩個），以及有標籤的鋼琴資料集才能算的每音符力度MAE_Velo。鋼琴（MAESTRO測試集）：常數力度MAE_Velo=14.8、r_BSSL=0.757、r_BSTL=0.622；VeloEst預訓練MAE_Velo=3.9、r_BSSL=0.878、r_BSTL=0.892；VeloEst+Diff-Synth反而惡化到MAE_Velo=19.2、r_BSSL=0.776、r_BSTL=0.752；VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy是MAE_Velo=10.5、r_BSSL=0.869、r_BSTL=0.881，比Diff-Synth全面好但仍不及鋼琴原生的預訓練模型（畢竟這裡鋼琴本身就是源域，不需要調適）。吉他（GAPS測試集）：常數力度r_BSSL=0.769、r_BSTL=0.798；VeloEst零樣本套用（不調適）分數剛好跟常數力度一樣（因為零樣本沒有用到吉他資料校準）；VeloEst+Diff-Synth大幅退步到r_BSSL=0.669、r_BSTL=0.624，比什麼都不做的常數力度還差；VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy則是這四者中最好的r_BSSL=0.794、r_BSTL=0.860。跨資料集：SMD鋼琴上Diff-SFProxy的MAE_Velo=11.3、r_BSSL=0.821、r_BSTL=0.806；FL吉他上r_BSSL=0.777、r_BSTL=0.788。

關鍵發現：在FL吉他資料集上，5秒切片的Diff-Synth（r_BSSL=0.646）甚至低於常數力度基線（r_BSSL=0.714）；而且切片長度拉長時，Diff-SFProxy幾乎不受影響（變化僅±0.006），Diff-Synth卻掉了0.083，說明波形空間監督對切片長度這種無關因素非常敏感，參數空間監督穩健得多。

消融：拿掉反崩塌與飽和正則化，MAESTRO上r_BSSL從0.869掉到0.789，輸出會塌縮成接近常數（但MAE因為塌縮點剛好接近中位數而變化不大，這是MAE這個指標的盲點）。拿掉鋼琴預訓練權重直接調適，MAESTRO上MAE_Velo惡化到20.5（相對10.5），吉他四項指標裡有三項都比常數力度基線還差，證明鋼琴預訓練提供的先驗知識是不可或缺的一環，不是可有可無的暖身。

指標選擇驗證：另外用MAESTRO的真實力度標籤，比較Pearson相關係數、餘弦相似度、MAE三種指標在「動態敏感度」（真實標籤與常數64之間的差距Δ）跟「SoundFont穩健度」（換一套SoundFont後分數的變化量）這兩個性質上的表現，結果Pearson相關係數在動態敏感度上表現最好（BSSL上+0.118、BSTL上+0.263），同時對換SoundFont最穩健（BSSL上只差0.034、BSTL上只差0.011），這是論文選用Pearson相關係數當主要評估指標的依據。

Two adaptation paths: Diff-Synth feeds note events (pitch, onset, duration) plus predicted velocities into a frozen, instrument-specific differentiable synthesizer (DDSP-Piano for piano, DDSP-Guitar-Synth for guitar) to render audio ã, then compares ã against real performance audio a with a multi-resolution STFT spectral loss: ℒ_audio = Σ_ω∈Ω(‖|STFT_ω(ã)|−|STFT_ω(a)|‖₁ + ‖log(|STFT_ω(ã)|+ε)−log(|STFT_ω(a)|+ε)‖₁), with Ω={2048,1024,512,256,128,64} spanning six window sizes.

The second path — and the paper's core contribution — is Diff-SFProxy: instead of comparing full waveforms, it extracts two velocity-related acoustic parameters from real performance audio as supervision targets. The first, Pitch-Conditioned Harmonic Energy (PHE): PHE_n = log(1+Σ_{h=1}^{H_n} 1/(|ℬ_{n,h}||𝒯_n|) Σ_{k∈ℬ_{n,h}}Σ_{t∈𝒯_n} X(k,t)²), accumulating energy within a 120ms post-onset window 𝒯_n around up to H_n≤5 retained harmonics, each within a ±1-bin band ℬ_{n,h}. The second, Onset-Window Spectral Flux (OSF): OSF_n = log(1+1/|𝒪_n| Σ_{t∈𝒪_n}Σ_k[X(k,t)−X(k,t−1)]₊), measuring broadband energy increase in a window 𝒪_n spanning 20ms before to 80ms after onset. Together these form a target vector Z.

Proxy architecture: a 6-layer Transformer encoder (d=256, 8 heads, FFN width 1024, dropout 0.1) maps a 5-second segment of up to N_max=64 notes to predicted (PHE_n, OSF_n) pairs Z̃. Each note token combines a learnable pitch embedding, a shared learnable basis mapping onset/duration/velocity to an additive token direction, and an MLP-based positional encoding; the output head goes d→d→2. The adaptation loss uses a Huber loss (β=1.0, robust to outliers): ℒ_params = Huber_β(Z̃−Z).

Shared regularizers across both paths: an anti-collapse term ℒ_anti = (v̄−μ)²+max(0,σ²_min−Var(v̂)) (μ=0.5, σ²_min=0.01) preventing the model from collapsing all predictions to one convenient value, and a soft saturation penalty ℒ_satu = (1/N)Σ_{n=1}^N[max(0,v̂_n−τ)]² (τ=0.95) discouraging near-ceiling predictions. The combined loss is ℒ=ℒ_main+λ_antiℒ_anti+λ_satuℒ_satu, weighted 1:0.2:0.4.

Data and setup: the source domain is piano MAESTRO V3 (198.7 hours, real velocity labels), the target domain is guitar GAPS (14 hours, nylon-string classical guitar), with SMD (4.7 hours piano) and the François Leduc FL dataset (4.0 hours, mixed guitar types) used for cross-dataset validation. SoundFonts: Salamander Grand Piano for piano, Spanish Classical Guitar for guitar, plus YDP Grand Piano for a sensitivity check.

Diagnostic (velocity recovery): tests on synthetic audio whether the proxy's gradient actually points toward the correct velocity — render MIDI via SoundFont, reset velocity to a constant 64, then optimize velocity via gradient descent until the proxy's prediction matches the target PHE/OSF. At 5-second segments, piano per-note MAE drops from 37.9 to 12.2 in-domain (67.8% gain) and 38.5 to 17.7 under stress testing (54.0% gain); guitar drops from 34.6 to 9.1 in-domain (73.7% gain) and 33.8 to 10.7 under stress testing (68.3% gain). 5-second segments were chosen as default since 10-second segments gave no additional gain.

Main cross-instrument results: baselines are constant velocity 64, zero-shot VeloEst (piano-pretrained, no adaptation), VeloEst+Diff-Synth, and VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy. Metrics are Pearson correlation on Bark-scale specific loudness (r_BSSL) and total loudness (r_BSTL) — the only options for datasets lacking ground truth — plus per-note velocity MAE_Velo where labels exist. Piano (MAESTRO test): constant velocity MAE_Velo=14.8, r_BSSL=0.757, r_BSTL=0.622; VeloEst pretrained MAE_Velo=3.9, r_BSSL=0.878, r_BSTL=0.892; VeloEst+Diff-Synth actually worsens to MAE_Velo=19.2, r_BSSL=0.776, r_BSTL=0.752; VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy reaches MAE_Velo=10.5, r_BSSL=0.869, r_BSTL=0.881 — beating Diff-Synth across the board though still short of the native piano-pretrained model (unsurprising, since piano is the source domain here and needs no adaptation). Guitar (GAPS test): constant velocity r_BSSL=0.769, r_BSTL=0.798; zero-shot VeloEst matches constant velocity exactly (zero-shot uses no guitar calibration); VeloEst+Diff-Synth degrades sharply to r_BSSL=0.669, r_BSTL=0.624 — worse than doing nothing; VeloEst+Diff-SFProxy is best of the four at r_BSSL=0.794, r_BSTL=0.860. Cross-dataset: on SMD piano, Diff-SFProxy gets MAE_Velo=11.3, r_BSSL=0.821, r_BSTL=0.806; on FL guitar, r_BSSL=0.777, r_BSTL=0.788.

Key finding: on FL guitar, 5-second Diff-Synth (r_BSSL=0.646) falls below the constant-velocity baseline (r_BSSL=0.714), and while Diff-SFProxy is nearly insensitive to crop length (±0.006 change), Diff-Synth drops 0.083 as crop length increases — showing waveform-space supervision is highly sensitive to factors unrelated to velocity, while parameter-space supervision is far more robust.

Ablations: removing the anti-collapse and saturation regularizers drops MAESTRO r_BSSL from 0.869 to 0.789, with outputs collapsing toward near-constant values (MAE barely moves because the collapse point happens to sit near the median — a blind spot of the MAE metric itself). Removing piano-pretrained weights and adapting from scratch worsens MAESTRO MAE_Velo to 20.5 (versus 10.5), and three of four guitar metrics fall below the constant-velocity baseline — proving piano pretraining supplies indispensable prior knowledge rather than being an optional warm start.

Metric justification: using MAESTRO's ground-truth labels, the paper compares Pearson correlation, cosine similarity, and MAE on two properties — dynamics sensitivity (the gap Δ between ground truth and constant-64) and SoundFont robustness (score change when swapping SoundFonts). Pearson correlation scores best on dynamics sensitivity (+0.118 on BSSL, +0.263 on BSTL) while remaining most robust to SoundFont swaps (only 0.034 difference on BSSL, 0.011 on BSTL) — the basis for choosing it as the primary metric.

限制Limitations

作者自己指出，復原出來的力度值本質上是「受限於所選渲染器」的：它被定義成「能讓渲染音訊最接近原始音訊感知動態的那個力度值」，而不是絕對意義上的真實演奏力度。這一點很重要，因為真實錄音裡影響響度的因素不只有力度——踏板、連續控制器、殘響等等都會混進響度變化裡，而這些因素目前完全沒有被模型獨立建模，換句話說，復原出來的「力度」有可能悄悄吸收了這些其他因素的影響，卻仍然被記錄成一個力度數值。另外，論文目前只驗證了鋼琴到吉他這一組遷移，作者自己也把「推廣到小提琴、管樂器等其他樂器」、「自動化SoundFont挑選（目前靠人工挑選音色相近的SoundFont）」、以及「復原其他表達性參數（踏板延音、運弓力度）」列為明確的未來工作，這些都還是未解決的問題，不是本文已經處理掉的範圍。

The authors themselves note that the recovered velocity is fundamentally "renderer-conditioned": it's defined as whichever velocity value makes the rendered audio best match the original audio's perceptual dynamics, not an absolute ground truth of the actual performance. This matters because real recordings' loudness is shaped by more than velocity alone — pedaling, continuous controllers, reverb all contribute — and none of these are modeled independently here, meaning the recovered "velocity" may silently absorb the influence of these other factors while still being recorded as a single velocity number. The paper also only validates one transfer pair, piano-to-guitar; the authors explicitly list generalizing to violin, wind instruments, automating SoundFont selection (currently done manually by picking a timbrally similar SoundFont), and recovering other expressive parameters (sustain pedal, bow pressure) as future work — these remain open problems, not something the paper has already resolved.

討論Discussion

把整篇論文的三組實驗放在一起看，講的其實是同一件事：監督訊號的「粒度」比監督訊號的「豐富度」更重要。波形層級的監督理論上包含比兩個聲學參數多得多的資訊，但這篇論文的結果反覆顯示，這個「多」反而是負擔——完整波形裡混雜著跟力度無關的錄音環境、合成器音色落差等雜訊，這些雜訊在跨樂器遷移時被放大，導致Diff-Synth在吉他上不但沒有改善、反而比什麼都不做（常數力度）還差。相對地，Diff-SFProxy把監督訊號主動限縮到跟力度直接掛勾的兩個聲學參數（諧波能量與起始頻譜通量），代價是理論上的資訊量變少，換來的卻是穩健的跨樂器與跨資料集遷移能力，而且對切片長度這種無關的實驗設定幾乎不敏感。消融實驗進一步補強這個結論：拿掉正則化項會讓模型走捷徑塌縮成常數輸出（而且MAE這個指標還看不太出來，凸顯了論文選用Pearson相關係數當主要指標的必要性）；拿掉鋼琴預訓練則證明了「先在有標籤的鄰近域打好基礎，再遷移」這個策略本身的價值，而不只是靠調適損失就能憑空生出正確答案。對MIR領域而言，這篇論文提供的不只是一個吉他力度估計器，更是一個可以複製到其他標籤稀缺任務上的方法論：當目標任務缺乏真實標籤、但可以定義出「哪些聲學參數跟目標變數直接相關」時，把可微分渲染的監督訊號限縮到這些參數上，可能會比端到端比對整段波形更可靠。

Taken together, the paper's three experimental threads make a single point: the granularity of a supervision signal matters more than its richness. Waveform-level supervision in principle carries far more information than two acoustic parameters, but the results repeatedly show that this abundance becomes a liability — the full waveform mixes in noise unrelated to velocity (recording environment, synthesizer-versus-real-instrument timbral mismatch), and that noise gets amplified under cross-instrument transfer, to the point where Diff-Synth on guitar doesn't merely fail to help but underperforms doing nothing at all. Diff-SFProxy, by contrast, deliberately narrows the supervision signal to two acoustic parameters directly tied to velocity (harmonic energy and onset spectral flux); the theoretical information loss buys back robust cross-instrument and cross-dataset transfer, and near-insensitivity to an unrelated experimental setting like crop length. The ablations reinforce this: removing the regularizers lets the model take the shortcut of collapsing to near-constant output (something MAE barely detects, underscoring why Pearson correlation was chosen as the primary metric), while removing piano pretraining demonstrates that starting from a solid labeled neighboring domain is itself valuable — the adaptation loss alone cannot conjure correct answers out of nothing. For MIR broadly, the paper offers not just a guitar velocity estimator but a transferable methodology: when a target task lacks ground truth but a handful of acoustic parameters can be identified as directly tied to the variable of interest, constraining differentiable-rendering supervision to those parameters can be more reliable than matching the entire waveform end to end.

為什麼挑這篇：文字轉音樂模型常常拿到很高的「準確率」——但這篇論文指出一個關鍵陷阱：如果一個模型不管有沒有被要求，本來就傾向於生成同一種常見輸出（比如四拍分組），那麼「有指定就對」的準確率其實測不出模型有沒有真的在聽指令，只是測到了它的預設偏好。論文設計了一組配對反事實實驗——同一個隨機種子下，比較「中性提示（不提屬性）」跟「明確指定某個屬性值」兩種輸出的差異，把「有出現目標」「比中性提示更好」「換一個目標值會不會真的換輸出」這三件事分開量測。最戲劇性的發現是Stable Audio 3有96.9%的中性輸出本來就是四拍分組，但明確要求四拍時反而只有56.3%命中——高準確率掩蓋了「模型根本沒在聽你要求四拍還是三拍」的事實。這種把評估方法論本身當成研究對象、專門拆穿「準確率高不代表真的可控」這個陷阱的做法很少見，故列為深度分析。Why this one: Text-to-music models often report high "accuracy" scores — but this paper flags a critical trap: if a model tends to produce the same common output (say, four-beat grouping) regardless of what it's asked for, then "correct when instructed" accuracy doesn't actually measure whether the model is listening to the instruction at all — it just measures the model's default preference. The paper designs matched counterfactual experiments — comparing, under the same random seed, a neutral prompt (no attribute stated) against explicit attribute-value treatments — and separates three distinct questions: does the target appear at all, does it improve beyond the neutral baseline, and does swapping the target value actually redirect the output. The most striking finding: Stable Audio 3 produces four-beat grouping in 96.9% of its neutral outputs, yet hits only 56.3% when four-beat is explicitly requested — high raw accuracy masking the fact that the model isn't really listening to whether you asked for three beats or four. Treating evaluation methodology itself as the object of study, specifically to expose the trap of "high accuracy doesn't mean genuinely controllable," is rare enough to warrant a deep dive.

動機Motivation

文字轉音樂系統常常會標榜自己能依照文字指令控制調性、節奏等結構性屬性，評估方式也大多是「給定一個屬性指令，檢查輸出是否符合」的單點準確率。但這個評估方式有一個結構性的漏洞：如果模型本來就傾向於輸出某種常見的預設值（比如流行音樂裡最常見的四拍分組、或是某個常見調性），那麼即使模型完全沒有理解、甚至完全忽略了文字指令，只要這個指令剛好要求的是模型的預設偏好，準確率照樣會很高。換句話說，高準確率有可能只是「模型的先驗偏好剛好跟指令對上了」，而不是「模型真的因為看懂了指令才調整輸出」。作者想解決的問題是：如何設計一套評估方法，能把「指令真的有起作用」跟「模型本來就傾向這樣輸出」這兩件事分開量測，而不是被一個容易被先驗偏好污染的單一準確率數字混為一談。

Text-to-music systems often claim to control structural attributes like key and rhythm via text instructions, and evaluation typically reduces to single-point accuracy: give an attribute instruction, check whether the output matches. But this evaluation has a structural blind spot: if a model has a strong default tendency toward some common value (say, four-beat grouping, the most common pattern in pop music, or some particular key), then even a model that completely ignores the text instruction will score high accuracy whenever the instruction happens to ask for that default. In other words, high accuracy could simply reflect "the model's prior preference happened to match the instruction," rather than "the model genuinely adjusted its output because it understood the instruction." The authors set out to design an evaluation method that separates whether an instruction is genuinely having an effect from whether the model was just going to produce that output anyway — rather than conflating the two into a single accuracy number that a strong prior can silently contaminate.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把自己定位在音樂生成評估方法論這條線上，跟既有做法最大的差異在於「反事實」設計：不是只看「指定了A、輸出是不是A」這一種條件下的結果，而是同時準備「中性提示（完全不提這個屬性）」跟「明確指定值A」「明確指定值B」三種條件，在同一個隨機種子下平行生成，再比較三者之間的差異。這樣一來就能把答案拆成三個獨立的問題：（1）出現度（Occurrence）——指定A之後，輸出裡真的有A嗎；（2）增益度（Enhancement，Δ）——指定A之後的表現，是不是真的比完全不提這個屬性時更好；（3）區辨度（Margin）——把指令從A換成B，輸出是不是真的跟著換了。論文選擇調性（key）與拍號分組（beat grouping，三拍vs四拍）作為兩個測試屬性，用S-KEY模型做調性判定、用Beat This節拍偵測器判斷每小節拍數，並且用GiantSteps、GTZAN、Ballroom、RWC Classical等既有標準資料集驗證這兩個偵測器本身的準確性，確保評估方法論不是建立在一個本身就不可靠的偵測器上。

The paper positions itself within music-generation evaluation methodology, and its key departure from prior work is the counterfactual design: rather than looking only at "asked for A, got A" under one condition, it prepares three parallel conditions — a neutral prompt (the attribute unmentioned), an explicit value-A instruction, and an explicit value-B instruction — generated under the same random seed, then compares across them. This splits the question into three independent measurements: (1) Occurrence — does the output actually contain A after A was requested; (2) Enhancement (Δ) — is performance after requesting A genuinely better than under the neutral prompt; (3) Differentiation (Margin) — does swapping the instruction from A to B actually redirect the output. The paper picks key and beat grouping (three-beat vs. four-beat) as its two test attributes, using an S-KEY model for key detection and a Beat This detector for beats-per-bar, and validates both detectors themselves against established benchmarks (GiantSteps, GTZAN, Ballroom, RWC Classical) to ensure the evaluation methodology isn't built on an unreliable detector.

方法Method

核心框架——三個互補指標：出現度（Occurrence, Acc）判斷目標是否出現在該條件的輸出裡；增益度（Enhancement, Δ）判斷指令是否讓輸出往目標方向移動、超過中性提示的表現；區辨度（Margin）判斷把目標值互換是否真的重新導向輸出。用公式表示：Δf = ½{s(y_f^a,a_f)−s(y_f^0,a_f)+s(y_f^b,b_f)−s(y_f^0,b_f)}，其中s(y,t)∈[0,1]是輸出y對目標t的評分函數，y_f^0是中性提示輸出，y_f^a、y_f^b分別是指定值a、b的輸出。

基準結構：每個系統跑256組完整「家族」（調性192組、拍號分組64組），每組家族包含中性+值A+值B三個條件，總共768個首輪輸出；三個條件在家族內共用同一組隨機種子，確保比較的公平性；另外抽出48組家族當作哨兵子集，用兩組額外的隨機種子重複測試，檢驗結果對種子的穩健度。評估視窗設定在偵測到音樂起始後的前30秒，短於15秒的片段直接判0分。

評分方法：調性用S-KEY模型判斷精確的主音-調式一致性（主要指標），並輔以MIREX式的分級給分（五度關係給0.5分、關係大小調給0.3分、平行大小調給0.2分）；拍號分組用Beat This偵測器追蹤每個小節內的拍點數，用眾數決定該片段是三拍還是四拍分組。

統計方法：主要估計量是「家族在固定基準情境下的平均反應」，用2,000次自助重抽（種子0）算百分位信賴區間；跨模型比較用20,000次配對重抽（種子20260721）；並對類型（genre）分層重抽以維持各分層樣本數。判定「有效控制」的規則是：兩個95%信賴區間都必須排除零——同時要求相對中性提示的增益度為正、以及目標值互換後的區辨度為正（拍號分組則用該目標值專屬的Δ）。

外部驗證：調性偵測器在GiantSteps EDM上精確準確率0.506、MIREX式準確率0.626；在GTZAN多曲風上精確準確率0.656、MIREX式準確率0.765。拍號偵測器在Ballroom舞曲資料集上平衡準確率達0.998、RWC Classical上0.734、GTZAN-Rhythm上0.806。另外做了60個片段、每片段3位評分者的人工聽測稽核：調性判斷跟多數決的一致率是0.796（精確）/0.850（MIREX式），拍號分組一致率0.745；並抽出28組家族（84個片段）重新讓人工標註，比較自動評分跟人工評分算出的Δ差異——調性的差異是−0.143 [−0.250,−0.036]，拍號分組的差異則剛好是零。

安慰劑對照：用跟屬性完全無關的指令去重新評分同一段音訊（比如拿「三拍」偵測器去評分「四拍指令」產生的音訊），三拍的安慰劑效應只有+0.003到+0.008（相對真實效應的+0.250到+0.469），四拍的安慰劑效應是−0.060（相對真實效應的−0.406），證明前述的效應大小不是偵測器雜訊造成的假象。

三個受測系統：ACE-Step 1.5用原生結構化欄位輸入調性/拍號；Stable Audio 3跟LeVo2用開放文字輸入，在基礎提示詞後面附加「A major」「4/4」這類文字。全部系統輸出都是純器樂、30秒片段。

主結果（調性，192組家族）：ACE-Step 1.5的Treatment agreement 0.667、Δ=0.612 [0.563,0.659]、Margin=0.628；Stable Audio 3的Treatment agreement 0.674、Δ=0.646 [0.599,0.693]、Margin=0.635；LeVo2的Treatment agreement只有0.055、Δ=0.026 [0.003,0.049]、Margin=0.021，幾乎沒有調性控制能力。

三種子哨兵驗證：調性Δ在三個種子下都維持相同的排序——Stable Audio 3 > ACE-Step > LeVo2；Stable Audio 3的拍號目標專屬Δ在每個種子下都維持相反符號（三拍約+0.417、四拍約−0.479），證明主結果不是單一種子的偶然現象。

Core framework — three complementary metrics: Occurrence (Acc) checks whether the target appears in that condition's output; Enhancement (Δ) checks whether the instruction moves the output toward the target beyond the neutral-prompt baseline; Differentiation (Margin) checks whether swapping the target value genuinely redirects the output. Formally: Δf = ½{s(y_f^a,a_f)−s(y_f^0,a_f)+s(y_f^b,b_f)−s(y_f^0,b_f)}, where s(y,t)∈[0,1] scores output y against target t, y_f^0 is the neutral-prompt output, and y_f^a, y_f^b are the outputs under explicit values a and b.

Benchmark structure: each system runs 256 complete "families" (192 for key, 64 for beat grouping), each family containing neutral + value-A + value-B conditions, for 768 first-pass outputs total; the three conditions within a family share one random seed to keep the comparison fair; a stratified 48-family sentinel subset is additionally repeated with two extra seeds to check robustness. The evaluation window is the first 30 seconds after the detected musical onset; clips shorter than 15 seconds score zero automatically.

Scoring: key uses an S-KEY model for exact tonic-mode agreement (the primary metric), supplemented by MIREX-style graded scoring (0.5 for fifth relations, 0.3 for relative major/minor, 0.2 for parallel major/minor); beat grouping uses a Beat This detector tracking beats per bar, with the modal count determining three- vs. four-beat grouping.

Statistics: the primary estimand is the family's mean response over the frozen benchmark contexts, using 2,000 bootstrap resamples (seed 0) for percentile confidence intervals; cross-model comparisons use 20,000 paired resamples (seed 20260721); genre-stratified resampling preserves stratum sizes. The rule for "effective control" requires both 95% confidence intervals to exclude zero — positive enhancement over neutral AND positive value differentiation (or the target-specific Δ, for beat grouping).

External validation: the key detector scores 0.506 exact / 0.626 MIREX-style on GiantSteps EDM, and 0.656 exact / 0.765 MIREX-style on GTZAN multi-genre. The beat detector scores 0.998 balanced accuracy on Ballroom, 0.734 on RWC Classical, and 0.806 on GTZAN-Rhythm. A human audit of 60 clips (3 raters each) found agreement with majority vote of 0.796 (exact) / 0.850 (MIREX-style) for key and 0.745 for beat grouping; a follow-up re-labeling of 28 families (84 clips) compared automatic vs. human-derived Δ — the difference was −0.143 [−0.250,−0.036] for key and exactly zero for beat grouping.

Placebo controls: rescoring the same audio with an instruction irrelevant to the attribute being measured (e.g. scoring "four-beat instruction" audio with a three-beat detector) yields placebo effects of only +0.003 to +0.008 for three-beat (versus the real +0.250 to +0.469) and −0.060 for four-beat (versus the real −0.406), confirming the reported effect sizes aren't detector-noise artifacts.

Three tested systems: ACE-Step 1.5 uses native structured fields for key/beat input; Stable Audio 3 and LeVo2 use open text, appending phrases like "A major" or "4/4" after a base prompt. All systems output instrumental-only, 30-second clips.

Main results (key, 192 families): ACE-Step 1.5 — treatment agreement 0.667, Δ=0.612 [0.563,0.659], Margin=0.628; Stable Audio 3 — treatment agreement 0.674, Δ=0.646 [0.599,0.693], Margin=0.635; LeVo2 — treatment agreement only 0.055, Δ=0.026 [0.003,0.049], Margin=0.021, essentially no key control at all.

Main results (beat grouping, 64 families): three-beat target — ACE-Step neutral 0.016 / treatment 0.266 (Δ=0.250 [0.156,0.344]); Stable Audio 3 neutral 0.016 / treatment 0.484 (Δ=0.469 [0.344,0.594]); LeVo2 neutral 0.047 / treatment 0.031 (Δ=−0.016 [−0.063,0.031], effectively no response). Four-beat target — ACE-Step neutral 0.812 / treatment 0.859 (Δ=0.047 [0.000,0.109], weakly positive); Stable Audio 3 neutral 0.969 / treatment 0.563 (Δ=−0.406 [−0.516,−0.297] — explicit instruction actively makes the output worse!); LeVo2 neutral 0.906 / treatment 0.859 (Δ=−0.047 [−0.109,0.016]).

Cross-genre results: ACE-Step's key Δ is fairly even across genres — classical 0.625, jazz 0.573, pop 0.563, rock 0.688; Stable Audio 3 shows classical 0.729, jazz 0.635, pop 0.698, rock 0.521, notably weaker on rock; LeVo2's key Δ sits between 0.010 and 0.042 across every genre, confirming its near-total loss of key control isn't genre-specific.

Three-seed sentinel: key Δ preserves the same ordering across all three seeds — Stable Audio 3 > ACE-Step > LeVo2; Stable Audio 3's target-specific beat Δ keeps opposite signs in every seed (roughly +0.417 for three-beat, −0.479 for four-beat), confirming the main results aren't an artifact of a single seed.

限制Limitations

作者自己列出六項限制。第一是因果範圍：這套方法評估的是系統釋出時的端對端使用者介面行為，並不能推論到模型內部架構層面的因果機制。第二是「載體」混淆：中性提示不是單純把屬性資訊拿掉，同時也改變了文字或欄位裡承載這個資訊的方式，這個「載體與數值之間的交互作用」目前沒有被因子化實驗設計隔離開來。第三是偵測誤差：拍號分組的效應接近零時，有可能只是偵測靈敏度的天花板，而不是模型真的完全沒反應（相對地，調性的判斷結果在多種驗證方法下都很穩健，比較不受這個問題影響）。第四是主要結果只用單一種子跑完整基準矩陣，哨兵子集提供的是「已觀測到的重複樣本」的穩健性檢驗，不是對所有可能種子的全面平均。第五是屬性範圍侷限：只評估了全域大小調跟三/四拍分組，沒有涵蓋調式轉換、局部修改、複合或不規則拍號、和弦進行等更複雜的結構屬性。第六，中性提示的建構方式對「類別型」屬性（比如大調/小調）很自然，但如果要評估連續型的控制變數，可能需要另外設計參照點（比如鄰近數值或分布配對的載體），這套方法目前沒有處理連續變數的版本。

The authors list six explicit limitations. First, causal scope: the method evaluates end-to-end released-interface behavior, not internal architectural causal mechanisms. Second, carrier confounding: the neutral prompt doesn't merely remove attribute information — it also changes how that information would have been carried in the text or field, and this carrier-value interaction hasn't been isolated via a full factorial design. Third, recognition error: near-zero beat-grouping effects could reflect the detector's sensitivity floor rather than genuine model non-response (key judgments, by contrast, stay robust across multiple validation methods and are less exposed to this issue). Fourth, the full benchmark matrix uses a single seed as its primary run; the sentinel subset provides a robustness check on observed replicates, not a comprehensive average over all possible seeds. Fifth, attribute scope: only global major/minor key and three-vs-four-beat grouping were evaluated, excluding modulation, local edits, compound or irregular meters, and chord progressions. Sixth, the neutral-prompt construction is natural for categorical attributes (e.g. major vs. minor) but evaluating continuous control variables would likely require an alternative reference point (a neighboring value or a distribution-matched carrier) — the method has no version for continuous variables yet.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有力的貢獻不是「哪個模型比較好」的排名，而是拆穿了一個評估方法論的陷阱：Stable Audio 3在四拍目標上呈現的高原始命中率（0.812中性→0.859指定，看似很正常），跟同一個模型在四拍上的真實表現（0.969中性→0.563指定，Δ=−0.406，指定反而讓命中率暴跌）完全是兩碼事。如果評估者只看「指定A之後輸出裡有沒有A」這個單一數字，Stable Audio 3看起來在四拍控制上做得不錯；但反事實設計一揭穿，事實是這個模型在完全沒被要求的情況下，96.9%的時候就已經自然生成四拍分組——所以「指定後有0.563命中」不是因為模型聽懂了指令，而是即使指令把它導向別的方向，它仍然有一半以上的機率因為強烈的預設偏好而生成四拍。這正是論文開頭想解決的問題的具體實例：高準確率可以完全是先驗偏好的產物，跟「真的有在聽指令」毫無關係。三個模型之間的對比也很有教育意義：ACE-Step跟Stable Audio 3在調性上都展現出紮實的、通過「增益度」與「區辨度」雙重門檻的控制能力，但LeVo2的調性Δ在全部曲風下都逼近零，說明它的調性控制基本上是裝飾性的、對文字指令幾乎無感。安慰劑對照、外部偵測器驗證、人工聽測稽核、跨種子哨兵這四層驗證疊在一起，讓論文能夠很有信心地說「這些效應量是真的，不是量測雜訊」——這種把評估方法論本身武裝到近乎無懈可擊的程度、再拿去戳破業界常見的「準確率高=可控」直覺的做法，對後續任何想評測生成式音樂模型可控性的工作，都提供了一套可以直接套用的檢驗清單。

The paper's strongest contribution isn't a leaderboard ranking but exposing an evaluation-methodology trap: Stable Audio 3's raw hit rate on the four-beat target (0.812 neutral → 0.859 treatment, which looks unremarkable) is a completely different story from the same model's real behavior on four-beat (0.969 neutral → 0.563 treatment, Δ=−0.406 — explicit instruction actively tanks the hit rate). If an evaluator looked only at "does A appear after A was requested," Stable Audio 3 would look like it handles four-beat control reasonably well; but the counterfactual design reveals that this model already produces four-beat grouping 96.9% of the time with zero instruction — so a 0.563 hit rate after explicit instruction isn't evidence of listening to the instruction, it's evidence that even when the instruction points elsewhere, the model's strong prior still wins the coin flip more than half the time. This is precisely the concrete instance of the problem the paper opens with: high accuracy can be purely a product of prior preference, unrelated to whether the instruction is actually being heard. The cross-model contrast is instructive too: both ACE-Step and Stable Audio 3 show genuine key control that clears both the enhancement and differentiation bars, while LeVo2's key Δ sits near zero across every genre — its key control is essentially decorative, near-insensitive to the text instruction. Stacking placebo controls, external detector validation, human listening audits, and cross-seed sentinels lets the paper state with real confidence that these effect sizes are genuine rather than measurement noise — and armoring an evaluation methodology this thoroughly, specifically to puncture the common industry intuition that "high accuracy equals controllability," hands anyone evaluating generative-music controllability a directly reusable checklist.

為什麼挑這篇：既有的音訊效果（Fx）表徵學習方法幾乎都假設可以拿到乾聲（未經處理）的參考音訊，但真實錄音裡根本不存在真正未經處理的聲音——麥克風跟錄音空間本身就會留下痕跡，這也是為什麼訓練資料只能侷限在MUSDB18這類少數的多軌資料集。RelFx換一個角度：不去學「這段音訊的效果狀態是什麼」這種絕對表徵，而是學「從一段音訊到另一段音訊之間的效果轉換距離」這種相對表徵，讓訓練不再需要乾聲參考，可以直接用任何一對「同來源、不同效果處理」的音訊片段訓練。方法本身是把既有的對比學習、雙分支CNN、交叉注意力等常見元件組合起來解決一個特定問題，沒有全新的架構突破，因此不列為深度分析；但它解除了Fx表徵學習長期依賴乾聲資料集的限制，同時額外設計了一個「反對稱」變體讓E(A,B)≈−E(B,A)成立，是紮實的表徵學習工作。Why this one: Existing audio effects (Fx) representation learning methods almost universally assume access to dry (unprocessed) reference audio, but truly unprocessed audio doesn't really exist in real recordings — the microphone and recording space leave their own trace, which is also why training data has been confined to a handful of multitrack datasets like MUSDB18. RelFx takes a different angle: rather than learning an absolute representation of "what effect state is this audio in," it learns a relative representation of "the effect-transformation distance from one audio clip to another," so training no longer needs dry references at all and can run directly on any pair of "same-source, differently-processed" clips. The method itself combines existing components — contrastive learning, a dual-branch CNN, cross-attention — to solve a specific problem, without a genuinely new architecture, so it isn't flagged as a deep dive; but it removes Fx representation learning's long-standing dependence on dry-reference datasets, and additionally designs an antisymmetric variant making E(A,B)≈−E(B,A) hold, making it solid representation-learning work.

動機Motivation

等化、破音、壓縮、殘響這些音訊效果是音樂製作的核心工具，能不能學出一個好用的Fx表徵，直接關係到Fx風格轉移、自動混音等下游應用的成效。但作者指出既有的Fx表徵學習方法有一個共同的結構性前提：需要一段乾聲（未經處理）的參考音訊當比較基準，才能定義出「這段音訊被加了什麼效果」這個絕對狀態。這個前提在實務上有三個問題：第一，真實錄音裡根本沒有真正的乾聲——麥克風特性、錄音空間的殘響，這些都已經是某種形式的「處理」；第二，同時擁有乾聲跟處理後版本的資料集非常稀少，訓練資料被侷限在MUSDB18這類有限的多軌資料集裡；第三，「絕對狀態」這個框架天生只支援單向建模（從某段音訊推斷它的效果狀態），沒辦法自然地表達「從A到B要怎麼調整」這種混音師實際思考問題的方式——混音工程師在工作時，想的通常是「這個聲音現在聽起來像這樣，我要把它調成聽起來像那樣」的相對距離，而不是抽象的絕對效果標籤。

Equalization, distortion, compression, and reverb are core tools of music production, and how good an Fx representation can be learned bears directly on downstream tasks like Fx style transfer and automatic mixing. The authors point out that existing Fx representation-learning methods share a structural assumption: they need a dry (unprocessed) reference audio as a comparison baseline to define the absolute state of "what effect has been applied to this audio." This assumption creates three practical problems. First, truly dry audio doesn't exist in real recordings — microphone characteristics and room reverb are already a form of processing. Second, datasets pairing dry and processed versions of the same audio are scarce, confining training data to a handful of multitrack sets like MUSDB18. Third, the absolute-state framing inherently supports only one-directional modeling (inferring a clip's effect state), and can't naturally express how a mixing engineer actually reasons — thinking in terms of "this sound is here, I want it to sound like that" as a relative distance, not an abstract absolute label.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把相關研究放在兩塊。第一是智慧音樂製作裡的應用：Fx風格轉移希望透過可微分訊號處理、盲參數估計、端對端神經網路等手段，捕捉一段參考訊號的音色特徵並套用到另一段訊號上；自動混音則是平衡多軌音訊的音量與音色。這兩個任務都仰賴一個好用的Fx表徵當基礎。第二是既有的Fx表徵學習方法：AFx-Rep靠效果與預設分類的方式學表徵、Fx-Encoder用對比學習學混音層級的表徵、Fx-Encoder++則進一步做到樂器層級的嵌入抽取；另外VGGish、CLAP這類通用音訊表徵雖然能用，但顆粒度不夠細，抓不住效果專屬的細節。這些方法無一例外都建立在「絕對效果狀態」的框架上，而RelFx要做的，正是把這整個框架換成「相對轉換」。

The paper situates related work in two areas. First, applications in intelligent music production: Fx style transfer aims to capture a reference signal's timbral characteristics and apply it to another signal via differentiable signal processing, blind parameter estimation, or end-to-end neural networks; automatic music mixing balances multi-track audio levels and timbre. Both tasks depend on a solid Fx representation as their foundation. Second, existing Fx representation learning: AFx-Rep learns representations via effect-and-preset classification, Fx-Encoder uses contrastive learning for mixture-level representations, and Fx-Encoder++ extends this to instrument-wise embedding extraction; general-purpose audio representations like VGGish and CLAP are usable but too coarse-grained to capture effect-specific detail. Every one of these methods is built on the absolute-effect-state framework, and RelFx's move is to replace that entire framework with relative transformation instead.

方法Method

問題設定：不去學絕對表徵E(x)→z，而是給定參考片段x、來源片段x'、效果變換θ，處理後的來源片段是x̃=Fx(x';θ)，編碼器直接吃(x, x̃)這個有序配對：z=E(x,x̃)=E(x,Fx(x';θ))。兩段輸入都不需要先做乾聲或效果標準化的前處理。對比目標鼓勵「同一種變換套用在不同內容上」得到相近的嵌入：d(z_a(θ),z_b(θ))≪d(z_a(θ),z_c(θ'))。

模型架構：頻譜前端用log-mel頻譜，STFT設定nfft=2048、hop size=512、64個mel頻帶。骨幹是CNN14風格的孿生（Siamese）CNN，六個卷積階段，通道數依序為[64,128,256,512,1024,2048]，兩個分支共享權重。在第3階段（256通道）與第5階段（1024通道）之後各插入一次雙向交叉注意力（4個頭、4×4空間池化、殘差縮放係數α=0.1）：h'_ref=h_ref+α·CrossAttn(h_ref,h_proc)。

差異門控融合（基礎模型）：全域池化後得到e_ref、e_proc∈ℝ^2048，融合方式是e_fx=g⊙(e_proc−e_ref)+(1−g)⊙e_proc，其中g=σ(W[e_ref;e_proc]+b)。差異項捕捉相對變化，絕對項保留內容特徵。

反對稱（雙向相容）變體：為了讓E(A,B)≈−E(B,A)這個反對稱性質成立，融合方式改成e_fx=g⊙(e_proc−e_ref)，g=σ(W(e_ref+e_proc)+b)——門控值本身對輸入順序不敏感，但差異項在交換輸入時會自動變號。投影頭是兩層MLP，映到128維並做L2正規化；基礎模型用ReLU，反對稱變體用tanh以保留符號翻轉的性質。

訓練資料準備：每個訓練樣本從同一首歌的同一個段落裡取兩段不重疊的片段(x_q,x'_q)，套用同一組效果設定θ；跨段落取樣（用同一結構段落內相鄰、不重疊的片段）刻意用來避免模型抄「內容捷徑」。內容相似度分析顯示，同段落相鄰片段在Fx-Encoder++嵌入空間下的平均餘弦相似度是0.938，跨曲則只有0.684，說明這個取樣策略確實把「內容相似」跟「效果相同」這兩件事分開了。

效果鏈設計：八種依序串接的效果——等化器、破音、多頻段壓縮器、增益、立體聲成像、限幅器、延遲、殘響——共72個連續參數與8個二元開關，效果順序在每個批次內隨機打亂。動態效果機率排程：每種效果被啟用的機率會根據該效果目前的訓練損失動態調整——損失較高（代表模型還沒學好）的效果，下一輪會被更頻繁地抽到，機率被限制在[0.3,0.8]之間。

損失函數：ℒ=ℒ_ctr+λ_tℒ_triplet+λ_pℒ_param。對比損失是修改版的NT-Xent（溫度τ=0.15），正樣本的logit在分母裡重複計算兩次：ℒ_ctr=−(1/2N)Σlog(e^{s_i,p(i)}/(2e^{s_i,p(i)}+Σe^{s_ik}))。三元組損失（間距m=0.3）：ℒ_triplet=(1/N)Σmax(0,d(z_a_i,z_b_i)−d(z_a_i,z_neg_i)+m)。參數回歸損失強制嵌入空間帶有連續結構：ℒ_param=MSE_masked(p̂,p)+λ_a·BCE(â,a)。超參數設定為λ_t=0.5、λ_p=0.5、λ_a=0.3、τ=0.15、m=0.3。

訓練資料與設定：6,447段混音音軌（2,681首歌、185.9小時）加上來自MoisesDB的2,585段音軌（240首歌、156.4小時）；每段10秒立體聲、44.1kHz取樣；依歌曲ID切分85%/15%訓練/驗證集；4張NVIDIA L20 GPU訓練200個epoch，每GPU批次大小48，梯度累積4步（有效批次大小768）；AdamW優化器，學習率5×10⁻⁴，權重衰減10⁻⁵，10個epoch線性暖身後餘弦退火到10⁻⁶。

Problem formulation: rather than learning an absolute representation E(x)→z, given reference clip x, source clip x', and effect transformation θ, the processed-source clip is x̃=Fx(x';θ), and the encoder takes the ordered pair (x, x̃) directly: z=E(x,x̃)=E(x,Fx(x';θ)). Neither input requires dry or effect-normalized preprocessing. The contrastive objective encourages the same transformation applied to different content to yield similar embeddings: d(z_a(θ),z_b(θ))≪d(z_a(θ),z_c(θ')).

Model architecture: the spectrogram frontend uses log-mel spectrograms, STFT with nfft=2048, hop size 512, 64 mel bins. The backbone is a CNN14-style Siamese CNN with six convolutional stages, channel dimensions [64,128,256,512,1024,2048], weights shared across branches. Bidirectional cross-attention is inserted after stages 3 (256 channels) and 5 (1024 channels), 4 heads, 4×4 spatial pooling, residual scale α=0.1: h'_ref=h_ref+α·CrossAttn(h_ref,h_proc).

Differential gating fusion (base model): after global pooling yields e_ref, e_proc∈ℝ^2048, fusion is e_fx=g⊙(e_proc−e_ref)+(1−g)⊙e_proc, where g=σ(W[e_ref;e_proc]+b). The differential term captures relative change while the absolute term retains content characteristics.

Antisymmetric (bidirectional-compatible) variant: to make E(A,B)≈−E(B,A) hold, fusion changes to e_fx=g⊙(e_proc−e_ref), g=σ(W(e_ref+e_proc)+b) — the gate itself is order-insensitive, while the differential term automatically flips sign when inputs are swapped. The projection head is a two-layer MLP to a 128-dimensional L2-normalized embedding; the base model uses ReLU, the antisymmetric variant uses tanh to preserve the sign-reversal property.

Training data preparation: each training item samples two non-overlapping clips (x_q, x'_q) from the same section of the same song, applying one shared effect configuration θ; cross-segment sampling (adjacent, non-overlapping clips within one structural section) is deliberately used to discourage content-based shortcuts. Content similarity analysis shows same-section adjacent clips have a mean cosine similarity of 0.938 in Fx-Encoder++ embedding space, versus only 0.684 across different songs — confirming this sampling strategy genuinely decouples "similar content" from "same effect."

Effects chain design: eight sequentially chained effects — equalizer, distortion, multiband compressor, gain, stereo imager, limiter, delay, reverb — with 72 continuous parameters and 8 binary switches, effect order randomized per batch. Dynamic Fx probability scheduling: each effect's activation probability is adaptively adjusted based on its running training loss — effects with higher residual loss (meaning the model hasn't learned them well yet) get sampled more frequently next epoch, clamped to [0.3, 0.8].

Loss function: ℒ=ℒ_ctr+λ_tℒ_triplet+λ_pℒ_param. The contrastive loss is a modified NT-Xent (temperature τ=0.15) with the positive logit counted twice in the denominator: ℒ_ctr=−(1/2N)Σlog(e^{s_i,p(i)}/(2e^{s_i,p(i)}+Σe^{s_ik})). The triplet loss (margin m=0.3): ℒ_triplet=(1/N)Σmax(0,d(z_a_i,z_b_i)−d(z_a_i,z_neg_i)+m). A parameter regression loss enforces continuous structure in embedding space: ℒ_param=MSE_masked(p̂,p)+λ_a·BCE(â,a). Hyperparameters: λ_t=0.5, λ_p=0.5, λ_a=0.3, τ=0.15, m=0.3.

Training data and setup: 6,447 mixed tracks (2,681 songs, 185.9 hours) plus 2,585 stems from MoisesDB (240 songs, 156.4 hours); 10-second stereo clips at 44.1kHz; split 85%/15% train/validation by song ID; 200 epochs on 4× NVIDIA L20 GPUs, batch size 48 per GPU with 4-step gradient accumulation (effective batch size 768); AdamW at learning rate 5×10⁻⁴, weight decay 10⁻⁵, 10-epoch linear warmup then cosine annealing to 10⁻⁶.

Baselines: Fx-Encoder++, AFx-Rep, VGGish, CLAP.

Evaluation task 1: Fx style transfer: embedding-guided gradient optimization adjusts a differentiable effect chain's parameters so the output audio's embedding approaches a reference embedding; 7 random restarts per sample with Adam (learning rate 10⁻², up to 200 iterations, patience 50); 100 samples per instrument drawn from MUSDB18, with median multi-resolution STFT loss L_d as the primary metric (lower is better).

Results (Table 2, median L_d): CLAP averages 1.644, VGGish 1.784, AFx-Rep 1.517, Fx-Encoder++ 1.601; RelFx trained only on MoisesDB averages 1.449; RelFx (Self-ref variant) averages 1.399; RelFx (Standard) averages 1.388 (per instrument: drums 1.403, bass 1.211, vocals 1.482, other 1.458 — the lowest average of all four); RelFx (Bi-directional) averages 1.389. The Standard configuration achieves the lowest overall average L_d. Improvements over Fx-Encoder++: drums 16.7%, bass 16.2%, vocals 12.5%, other 7.9%, averaging 13.3%. Trained only on MoisesDB (matching Fx-Encoder++'s training data), RelFx reaches 1.449 versus Fx-Encoder++'s 1.601, a 9.5% improvement.

Evaluation task 2: effects retrieval ablation (Table 3): given a processed query clip, retrieve candidates with the identical effect but different content from a pool of 500, measured via Recall@K (K=1,5,10) and L_d. Full model: R@1=67.3, R@5=79.2, R@10=83.7, L_d=1.399. Removing cross-attention: R@1=65.6, R@5=78.2, R@10=82.8, L_d=1.392 (retrieval slightly worse, L_d slightly better). Removing cross-segment sampling: R@1=69.6, R@5=80.2, R@10=83.9, L_d=1.876 (retrieval actually improves, but L_d jumps 34% — the single largest ablation effect, because the model reverts to a content shortcut that helps retrieval but is fatal for style transfer, which needs an embedding genuinely sensitive to the effect transformation rather than content). Removing parameter regression: R@1=70.4, R@5=81.7, R@10=85.5, L_d=1.437 (L_d up 2.7%). Removing dynamic Fx probability scheduling: R@1=71.7, R@5=79.8, R@10=83.3, L_d=1.465 (L_d up 4.7%). Single-branch configuration: R@1=46.1, R@5=62.6, R@10=68.8, L_d=1.457 (L_d up 4.1%, confirming the dual-branch design carries real value).

限制Limitations

作者自己承認的限制是：這套方法建模的是「整體」轉換，沒有辦法把混音裡各個獨立音軌各自被套用的效果分開處理——換句話說，如果輸入是一段完整混音，模型學到的相對距離是整體混音層級的轉換，無法回答「鼓組上加了什麼效果、人聲上又加了什麼效果」這種樂器層級的細部問題。另外，論文明確承認「未見過的效果鏈順序」跟「真實世界的混音製作鏈」都還沒有被驗證過——訓練時效果順序雖然有隨機打亂，但測試時是否能推廣到訓練時完全沒見過的效果排列組合、或是實際錄音室工作流程裡更複雜的效果串接方式，都被列為未來工作，目前的實驗結果沒有直接回答這個問題。

The authors' own acknowledged limitation is that the method models overall transformations rather than separating out effects applied to individual sources within a mixture — given a full mix as input, the learned relative distance operates at the mixture level and can't answer instrument-specific questions like "what was applied to the drums versus the vocals." The paper also explicitly notes that unseen effect-chain orderings and real-world production chains remain unvalidated — while training randomizes effect order, whether the model generalizes to effect combinations never seen during training, or to the more elaborate effect chains used in actual studio workflows, is left as future work and isn't directly answered by the current experiments.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有意思的地方，是把「跨段落取樣消融」這個看似邊緣的設計選擇，變成了整篇論文論證最有力的一組數字：拿掉跨段落取樣後，效果檢索的Recall@K指標全部小幅上升，但風格轉移的L_d卻暴增34%。這個「一好一壞」的反差正好揭穿了一個容易被忽略的陷阱——如果訓練時允許模型從同一個片段裡直接切出正樣本對，模型很容易學到「內容相似度」而不是「效果轉換」這個真正該學的訊號，而「效果檢索」這個任務剛好對這種捷徑不敏感（甚至因為內容線索而表現更好），只有像風格轉移這種真正需要用嵌入去驅動梯度優化的下游任務，才會把這個捷徑的破壞力暴露出來。這說明了論文選擇風格轉移當主要評估指標、而不是只看檢索指標，是一個經過深思熟慮的方法論決定。反對稱變體的結果也呼應了同一個主題：−0.998的近乎完美符號翻轉，證明只要在架構設計裡把「差異項」跟「內容項」明確拆開（用tanh而非ReLU、把絕對項從最終融合公式裡拿掉），相對表徵確實可以帶有代數上一致的方向性，這對後續想要做「效果空間裡的向量運算」（比如把A的殘響量減掉、加上B的殘響量）這類應用開了一個口子。整體而言，RelFx沒有提出全新的架構元件，但它精準地找到了「絕對表徵框架依賴乾聲」這個現實限制，換了一個問題定義方式把它繞開，並且用消融實驗把「為什麼要這樣設計」的每一個決定都量化驗證了一遍，是紮實但不追求架構新穎性的表徵學習工作。

The paper's most interesting move is turning what looks like a peripheral design choice — the cross-segment-sampling ablation — into its most compelling piece of evidence: removing cross-segment sampling makes every effects-retrieval Recall@K metric tick slightly up, while style-transfer L_d spikes 34%. That "one metric improves while the other collapses" contrast exposes an easy-to-miss trap: if training allows positive pairs cut directly from the same clip, the model readily learns "content similarity" instead of the effect transformation it's actually supposed to capture, and effects retrieval happens to be insensitive to this shortcut (even benefiting from the content cue) — only a downstream task like style transfer, which genuinely needs the embedding to drive gradient optimization, exposes how damaging that shortcut is. This shows the paper's choice of style transfer as its primary evaluation metric, rather than retrieval alone, was a deliberate methodological decision. The antisymmetric variant's results echo the same theme: near-perfect sign reversal at −0.998 confirms that explicitly separating the differential term from the content term in the architecture (tanh instead of ReLU, dropping the absolute term from the final fusion) does let the relative representation carry algebraically consistent directionality — opening the door to applications like vector arithmetic in effect space (subtract A's reverb amount, add B's). Overall, RelFx introduces no fundamentally new architectural component, but it precisely identifies the real-world constraint that the absolute-representation framework depends on dry references, sidesteps it with a reframed problem definition, and quantifies every design decision through ablations — solid representation-learning work that doesn't chase architectural novelty.
