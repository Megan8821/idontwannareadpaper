How Far Back Should a Transformer Look? Repetition and Copying in Music Sequence Models

Transformer 該往回看多遠?音樂序列模型中的重複與複製

Amir Fathi
arXiv:2610.02837 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：在 Nottingham 民謠上把符號音樂模型的上下文從 6 拉到 336 個 token,NLL 下降 71%,但一個只有三個擬合權重的「找前面相同片段照抄」基準就能拿回其中 94%;換到 MAESTRO 演奏資料,整體只降 9%,同一個基準只拿回 11%。長上下文的好處,在這些譜面資料上幾乎就是複製。Why this one: Extending a symbolic-music model's context from 6 to 336 tokens cuts NLL by 71% on Nottingham folk tunes, yet a copy-the-earlier-match baseline with only three fitted weights recovers 94% of that gain; on MAESTRO performances the total drop is only 9% and the same baseline recovers just 11%. On these rendered scores, the benefit of long context is almost entirely copying.

動機Motivation

符號音樂的自迴歸模型通常讓注意力覆蓋整首曲子,但全自注意力的成本隨序列長度平方成長;另一方面,也有系統以「最近的歷史就包含了預測下一個事件所需的大部分資訊」為設計前提。這兩種做法隱含的問題沒有人直接量過:預測表現到底怎麼隨可用歷史長度變化?又是音樂表示的哪些性質決定了長上下文有沒有用?

這篇還有一段自我修正的背景。作者先前的草稿得到相反結論——上下文在 16 個 token 就飽和。重新稽核後發現那條曲線是四個方法學錯誤疊出來的,所以這篇同時是一份「量測有效上下文長度時要避開哪些坑」的檢查清單。

Autoregressive models of symbolic music usually attend over the whole piece, while full self-attention costs grow quadratically with sequence length; meanwhile some systems are designed on the premise that recent history holds most of what is needed to predict the next event. Neither practice rests on a direct measurement: how does predictive performance change with the amount of available history, and which properties of the musical representation decide whether long context helps?

There is also a self-correcting backstory. The author's earlier draft reached the opposite conclusion, that context saturates at 16 tokens. A re-audit traced that curve to four methodological errors stacked together, so this paper doubles as a checklist of pitfalls for measuring effective context length.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

語言模型這邊早有同類研究。Khandelwal 等人(2018)截斷或擾動 LSTM 的歷史,發現有用上下文約到 200 個 token,遠處主要靠詞彙重複;Sun 等人(2021)發現 Transformer 超過 2K token 的長程上下文主要幫助「能從遠處照抄」的 token。這篇的核心發現,本質上就是把 Sun 等人的結論搬到符號音樂上,差別在於它對每個上下文長度各訓練一個模型,並加上因果介入(抹除、替換)而非只做相關分析。

音樂這邊,Music Transformer 用相對注意力看整段序列,Perceiver AR 把上下文推到數千 token,Anticipatory Music Transformer 則以「近期歷史最相關」為動機卻沒量化需要多長。Yi 等人報告 MAESTRO 上全長模型會利用 1024 token 以外的資訊,與本篇的 MAESTRO 結果並不矛盾,因為本篇只測到 336。

作者自己說複製基準就是 neural cache/kNN-LM 的想法:把參數化預測器和從前文檢索到的分布內插。論文沒提到的是,音樂建模早在二十年前就有這個機制:IDyOM(Pearce, 2005)結合在語料上訓練的長期模型,以及只用當前這首曲子建立的短期模型,而短期模型正是用來利用曲內重複的。這樣看,這篇的發現與其說是「Transformer 學會了複製」,不如說是「在這種表示下,小型 Transformer 的長上下文增益,大致就是 IDyOM 短期模型早已捕捉到的東西」。

Language modeling has precedents. Khandelwal et al. (2018) truncated or perturbed LSTM histories and found useful context up to about 200 tokens, with distant context mostly serving lexical repetition; Sun et al. (2021) found that Transformer context beyond 2K tokens mainly helps tokens that can be copied from far back. This paper's core finding is essentially Sun et al.'s result carried over to symbolic music; the differences are that it trains a separate model per context length and adds causal interventions (erasure, replacement) rather than correlational analysis only.

On the music side, Music Transformer applies relative attention over full sequences, Perceiver AR pushes context to thousands of tokens, and the Anticipatory Music Transformer is motivated by recent history being most relevant without quantifying how much is needed. Yi et al. report that full-horizon MAESTRO models use information beyond 1024 tokens, which does not contradict this paper's MAESTRO result because it only tests up to 336.

The copy baseline is, by the author's own description, the neural-cache / kNN-LM idea: interpolate a parametric predictor with a distribution retrieved from earlier context. What the paper does not mention is that music modeling has had this idea for two decades: IDyOM (Pearce, 2005) combines a long-term model trained on a corpus with a short-term model built from the current piece only, and that short-term model is exactly a mechanism for exploiting within-piece repetition. Read that way, the finding is less "Transformers learn to copy" than "under this representation, a small Transformer's long-context gain is largely what IDyOM's short-term model already captures".

方法Method

資料是四個語料:Nottingham(約一千首民謠)、O'Neill's 愛爾蘭曲集、MusicNet 與 MAESTRO v3。全部量化成每四分音符 4 格,每格只留最高音(skyline 規則),詞彙是休止加 47 個音高,沒有起音符號。切分以曲為單位,並先把共享一半以上 16-token 片段的曲子併成一組,使 Nottingham 測試集中與訓練集逐字相同的 48-token 區塊為 0.0%。

模型是兩層因果 Transformer(約 2.47M–2.50M 參數),對 T ∈ {6, 18, 48, 96, 192, 336} 各訓練一個,所有長度都在相同的目標位置上評分。關鍵設計是注意力分帶:第一層只看最近至多 18 個 token,第二層看剩下的歷史,使 b1 + b2 − 1 = T,並以梯度檢查確認感受野恰好是 T。作者說明先前把兩層平分時,336 模型實際上只能從最近約一半的距離照抄。另有一個二元自編碼器版本,每個潛在步只編碼互不重疊的 6 個 token。

為了歸因,每個目標都有一個「重複距離」(repeat lag):它的 16-token 歷史上一次出現在多遠之前。接著做三個檢驗。抹除檢驗把前面每次出現(連同接續的 token)換成另一首曲子的內容,對照組則抹除同樣數量、無關的片段。替換檢驗只把接續 token 換成另一個音高。最後,把訓練好的 336-token 模型在測試時截斷或替換它的歷史,不重新訓練。

The data are four corpora: Nottingham (about a thousand folk tunes), O'Neill's Irish tunes, MusicNet and MAESTRO v3. Everything is quantized to 4 steps per quarter note, keeping only the highest pitch per step (the skyline rule), with a vocabulary of rest plus 47 pitches and no onset symbol. Splits are by tune, after merging tunes that share at least half of their 16-token patterns, so 0.0% of non-constant 48-token Nottingham test blocks occur verbatim in training.

The model is a two-layer causal Transformer (about 2.47M-2.50M parameters), trained separately for each T in {6, 18, 48, 96, 192, 336} and scored on identical target positions. The key design is banded attention: the first layer sees at most the last 18 tokens and the second covers the rest, so b1 + b2 - 1 = T, with the receptive field verified by gradient inspection. The author notes that an earlier even split between layers let a 336-token model copy only from roughly the nearest half of its context. A binary-autoencoder variant encodes each latent step from a disjoint span of 6 tokens.

To attribute the gain, each target gets a repeat lag: the distance to the nearest earlier occurrence of its 16-token history. Three tests follow. The erase test overwrites every earlier occurrence (plus its continuation) with material from another tune, against a control that overwrites equally many unrelated spans. A swap test replaces only the continuation token with a different pitch. Finally, the trained 336-token model has its test-time history truncated or replaced, without retraining.

限制Limitations

最大的限制作者自己講得很清楚:結論只適用於這種「譜面渲染」表示。兩個民謠語料的反覆記號被逐音展開,表示又只留最高音、沒有起音,所以重複段落在 token 層級會完全相同。Nottingham 有 71% 的目標在 320 token 內找得到相同的 16-token 歷史,MAESTRO 則有 96.9% 找不到。民謠和 MAESTRO 的差別,有多少來自曲風、多少來自「量化譜面 vs. 表情演奏」,本篇無法分開;作者建議把反覆記號收回去重跑,但沒做。

規模也小:模型約 2.5M 參數、上下文最多 336、MusicNet 只有 43 首測試曲。96 token 處的結果高度依賴最佳化:10 個固定預算的種子裡只有 6 個在第 41–172 個 epoch 之間突然學會複製,主實驗的 seed SD 達 0.326 bits。而且 384-token 視窗排除了短曲,O'Neill's 302 首測試曲只用到 129 首,作者自己的分析顯示留下來的子集本身就更重複。

生成那一節比預測那一節弱。它的距離只用三個表面統計量,而 36 組生成結果沒有一組落在以曲子開頭建立的真實音樂區間內(0.03 到 0.17;最好的一組是 0.19)。程式碼與切分清單只「應要求提供」,對一篇核心訊息是「先前結果是隱藏資料問題造成的假象」的論文來說,這點有些尷尬。

The biggest limitation is one the author states clearly: the conclusion applies to this rendered-score representation only. In both folk corpora written repeat signs are expanded note for note, and the representation keeps only the top pitch with no onsets, so repeated sections become identical at the token level. On Nottingham, 71% of targets have an earlier matching 16-token history within 320 tokens; on MAESTRO, 96.9% have none. How much of the folk-versus-MAESTRO gap is genre and how much is quantized score versus expressive performance cannot be separated here; the author suggests rerunning with repeats collapsed but does not do it.

The scale is also small: about 2.5M parameters, at most 336 tokens of context, and only 43 MusicNet test pieces. Results at 96 tokens depend heavily on optimization: only 6 of 10 fixed-budget seeds abruptly learn to copy, between epochs 41 and 172, and the main sweep's seed SD there is 0.326 bits. The 384-token window also excludes short tunes; only 129 of 302 O'Neill's test tunes are used, and the author's own analysis shows the retained subset is intrinsically more repetitive.

The generation section is weaker than the prediction section. Its distance uses only three surface statistics, and none of the 36 generated sets falls inside the real-music band built from tune openings (0.03 to 0.17; the best set scores 0.19). Code and split manifests are available only on request, which sits awkwardly next to a paper whose central message is that earlier results were artifacts of hidden data problems.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的是因果證據鏈,而且每一環都有數字。在 Nottingham 上,336-token 模型的 NLL 從 1.391 降到 0.405 bits/token(下降 0.986,95% 區間 0.925–1.051),其中 87% 的降幅來自「336 範圍內有重複」的目標。抹除前一次出現後,這些目標的 NLL 從 0.19 升到 1.06,接近無重複目標的 0.93,而等量的對照抹除仍是 0.19;只把接續音換掉,模型給那個新音的機率從 0.3% 跳到 62%。O'Neill's 上同樣的數字是 0.22 → 1.24 與 0.4% → 71%。

另外兩個結果也值得記住。第一,6 → 48 token 時 Nottingham 的 NLL 反而變差(1.391、1.440、1.529),因為這個範圍內幾乎沒有可抄的對象(48 token 時只有 1.8% 目標的重複在視野內)。第二,模型對移調後的重複完全沒用上:0.9% 的目標只在移調下有重複,模型在這些目標上是 1.08 bits,不比無重複目標的 0.93 好。

稽核那一節對任何要報告上下文曲線的人都是實用的檢查清單。先前草稿的 Nottingham 切分讓 92.4% 的測試序列在訓練集裡有同一首曲子的另一個版本;它的每個潛在步看到約 22 個互相重疊的 token;它的平均池化預測器(v5)在 336 token 時停在 0.891 bits/bit,而因果預測器能到 0.465;它逐檔估計速度的網格把中位數曲子拉長 1.64 倍。這四項任何一項單獨存在,都可能製造出「提早飽和」的假象。對實務者的結論很具體:在重複很多的譜面資料上,便宜的顯式複製機制就能買到長注意力的大部分好處;在演奏資料上(MAESTRO 在 96 token 時已達到它那點小增益的 82%),336 以內的長注意力買不到多少。

The paper's strongest asset is its causal chain of evidence, with a number at every link. On Nottingham, the 336-token model's NLL falls from 1.391 to 0.405 bits per token (a drop of 0.986, 95% interval 0.925 to 1.051), and 87% of that drop comes from targets with a repeat in view at 336 tokens. Erasing the earlier occurrence raises those targets' NLL from 0.19 to 1.06, close to the 0.93 of targets without repeats, while an equally large control erasure stays at 0.19; swapping only the continuation lifts the probability of the injected pitch from 0.3% to 62%. On O'Neill's the figures are 0.22 to 1.24 and 0.4% to 71%.

Two further results are worth remembering. First, Nottingham NLL actually worsens from 6 to 48 tokens (1.391, 1.440, 1.529), because almost nothing is copyable in that range (only 1.8% of targets have a repeat in view at 48). Second, the model makes no use of transposed repeats: for the 0.9% of targets whose history recurs only under transposition, it scores 1.08 bits, no better than the 0.93 on targets without a repeat.

The audit section is a useful checklist for anyone reporting context curves. The earlier draft's Nottingham split placed another rendering of the same tune in training for 92.4% of test sequences, its latent steps each saw about 22 overlapping tokens, its mean-pooled predictor (v5) stays at 0.891 bits per bit at 336 tokens where the causal predictor reaches 0.465, and its per-file tempo grid stretched the median tune 1.64-fold. Each of these alone can manufacture apparent early saturation. For practitioners the takeaway is concrete: on repetition-heavy score data, a cheap explicit copy mechanism buys most of what long attention buys, while on performance data (MAESTRO reaches 82% of its small gain by 96 tokens) long attention buys little within 336 tokens.

Correlation-Based Distillation Yields More Mergeable Speech-Music Encoders

以相關性為基礎的蒸餾讓語音-音樂編碼器更容易合併

Fabian Ritter-Gutierrez
arXiv:2610.02836 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把蒸餾損失從 DistilHuBERT 換成相關性損失,其他全不動,蒸餾出來的語音學生與音樂學生在兩種機制完全不同的合併法下都更好合:任務算術下 18 次比較中 17 次更貼近較佳端點,通道排列下三層學生第一個 FFN 需要重排的通道從 98.2% 降到 78.3%。不過增益集中在語音端,音樂分數反而是舊損失較高。Why this one: Swapping the distillation loss from DistilHuBERT's to a correlation-based loss, with everything else fixed, makes distilled speech and music students easier to merge under two mechanistically unrelated algorithms: under task arithmetic they stay closer to the better endpoint in 17 of 18 comparisons, and under channel permutation the three-layer pair's first FFN needs 78.3% of channels rearranged instead of 98.2%. The gain sits on the speech side, though; the old loss keeps the higher music scores.

動機Motivation

音訊大型語言模型之類的應用需要一個同時表示語音和音樂的小型編碼器。從頭在兩個領域上預訓練很貴,比較實際的路線是:把語音自監督教師(HuBERT)和音樂教師(MERT)分別蒸餾成小學生,再事後合併成一個模型,不重新訓練。

作者先前已提出兩種合併工具:任務算術(task arithmetic, TA)在共同初始化下內插兩個學生的權重差;相關性排列(correlation-permutation, CP)先依激活相關性把通道對齊再平均。但兩份前作都固定使用同一種蒸餾損失。這篇要問的是:蒸餾損失本身會不會改變學生「好不好合」?如果會,那過去報告的合併表現有一部分其實是學生的性質,不是合併演算法的性質。

Applications such as audio large language models want one small encoder that represents both speech and music. Pre-training from scratch on both domains is expensive; a more practical route distils a speech self-supervised teacher (HuBERT) and a music teacher (MERT) into separate small students and merges them post hoc without retraining.

The author's earlier work introduced two merging tools: task arithmetic (TA) interpolates the two students' weight deltas from a shared initialization, and correlation-permutation (CP) aligns channels by activation correlation before averaging. Both earlier studies fixed the same distillation loss. This paper asks whether the distillation loss itself changes how well the students merge. If it does, part of any reported merging performance is a property of the students rather than of the merging algorithm.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

標準蒸餾目標是 DistilHuBERT(Chang 等人, 2022)的逐層預測損失。替換它的相關性損失來自作者 2024 年的抗噪蒸餾工作,由三項組成:把學生與教師特徵的交叉相關矩陣推向單位矩陣、壓低學生自身特徵的非對角相關,再保留一項餘弦項。最該點名的是,這幾乎就是 Barlow Twins(Zbontar 等人, 2021)的交叉相關目標搬到師生配對上,自相關項則類似 VICReg(Bardes 等人, 2022)的共變異數去相關項——論文有把它歸入 redundancy-reduction 家族,但沒有強調它的新意只在用途,不在形式。

合併這邊,任務算術來自 Ilharco 等人(2023)與 model soups;排列對齊則是 Git Re-Basin(Ainsworth 等人, 2023)與 Entezari 等人(2022)的排列不變性一脈。CP 與 Git Re-Basin 的 activation matching 非常接近,差別在於它涵蓋注意力頭、頭內通道與 CNN 前端。本篇本身沒有提出新方法:TA、CP、損失都是前作,新的是把損失當成唯一變數的對照實驗。

The standard distillation objective is the layer-prediction loss of DistilHuBERT (Chang et al., 2022). Its replacement comes from the author's 2024 noise-robust distillation work and has three terms: push the student-teacher cross-correlation matrix toward identity, suppress off-diagonal correlation within the student's own features, and keep a cosine term. The point most worth making is that this is nearly the Barlow Twins objective (Zbontar et al., 2021) transplanted onto teacher-student pairs, with the self-correlation term resembling VICReg's covariance decorrelation (Bardes et al., 2022); the paper places it in the redundancy-reduction family but does not stress that its novelty lies in the application, not the form.

On merging, task arithmetic comes from Ilharco et al. (2023) and model soups; permutation alignment follows Git Re-Basin (Ainsworth et al., 2023) and the permutation-invariance line of Entezari et al. (2022). CP is very close to Git Re-Basin's activation matching, differing in that it covers attention heads, within-head channels and the CNN front end. The paper proposes no new method: TA, CP and the loss are all prior work; what is new is a controlled comparison with the loss as the only variable.

方法Method

學生有三種規格:兩層 Transformer(D=768,約 23M 參數)、三層(約 31M),以及寬版兩層(D=1536、6 個注意力頭,約 62M)。前兩者從 HuBERT 初始化,寬版從同一個隨機檢查點出發,用來排除初始化的干擾。兩個學生都在 LibriSpeech 960 小時上蒸餾 200k 步,唯一差別是教師(與指定時的損失)。

評估是九項任務:SUPERB 的 ASR、關鍵詞偵測、意圖分類(IC)、情緒辨識,MARBLE 的歌手、發聲技巧、樂器、曲風辨識,加上 ESC-50。每項分數在 FBANK 下限與較佳教師之間正規化成 0–1000,平均成 Speech Score 與 Music Score。「好合」分成三種意義:內插穩健度(相對於較佳單領域端點的平均退化 Δ,單位 pp)、結構對齊(CP 要搬動多少通道、配對後相關多高),以及合併品質(Average Score)。

There are three student configurations: a two-layer Transformer (D=768, about 23M parameters), a three-layer one (about 31M), and a wide two-layer one (D=1536, 6 attention heads, about 62M). The first two start from HuBERT; the wide pair starts from a shared random checkpoint to remove the initialization confound. Both students are distilled for 200k steps on LibriSpeech 960 h, differing only in teacher (and, where stated, loss).

Evaluation covers nine tasks: ASR, keyword spotting, intent classification (IC) and emotion recognition from SUPERB; singer, vocal-technique, instrument and genre identification from MARBLE; plus ESC-50. Each task is normalized to 0-1000 between an FBANK floor and the better teacher, then averaged into a Speech Score and a Music Score. "Mergeable" is split into three senses: interpolation robustness (mean degradation Delta from the better single-domain endpoint, in pp), structural alignment (how many channels CP moves and how well they match), and merge quality (Average Score).

限制Limitations

最根本的問題在 Table 1 的一行:兩個學生的蒸餾資料都是 LibriSpeech 960 小時。也就是說,「音樂學生」是在純語音音訊上模仿 MERT。這能解釋為什麼增益集中在語音端,也讓「語音-音樂編碼器」這個標題打了折扣——音樂學生從沒看過音樂,兩個學生本來就比真實情境更相似。論文沒有討論這一點。

統計上,每個設定都是單次訓練。逐任務比較用 McNemar 與 bootstrap 檢定,但 74 個可檢定比較中只有 12 個顯著,且效果量都小於 |h| = 0.13;另有 16 個比較缺預測檔。綜合分數、Δ 平均與排列統計都沒有檢定。損失改善「好合」的機制(自相關項讓特徵去相關)作者明說只是假說,掃描 λ_sc 的直接檢驗留給未來。寬版的 KD 配對也沒跑 CP,所以只有兩層與三層有完整的跨演算法比較。

The most fundamental issue is one line of Table 1: both students are distilled on LibriSpeech 960 h. The "music student" therefore imitates MERT on speech-only audio. That plausibly explains why the gains concentrate on the speech side, and it weakens the "speech-music encoder" framing: the music student never hears music, so the two students are more alike than in a realistic setting. The paper does not discuss this.

Statistically, every configuration is a single training run. Per-task comparisons use McNemar's test and a bootstrap, but only 12 of 74 testable comparisons reach significance, all with effect size |h| < 0.13, and 16 more lacked prediction files. Composite scores, mean Delta and the permutation statistics carry no test. The proposed mechanism (the self-correlation term decorrelating features) is explicitly only a hypothesis, with the direct test of sweeping lambda_sc left to future work. The wide KD pair was never merged with CP, so only the two- and three-layer pairs have a full cross-algorithm comparison.

討論Discussion

任務算術下,相關性損失在 18 次比較(兩領域 × 三個權重 × 三種規格)中有 17 次更穩健;唯一例外是兩層、λ=0.9 的語音(−0.63 對 −0.30 pp)。差距最大在三層、λ=0.8:音樂 −0.59 對 −4.51 pp,語音 −1.01 對 −3.01 pp,同一點的 Speech Score 為 959.4 對 924.1,IC 93.57% 對 89.24%,WER 13.62% 對 17.08%。但要注意,相關性損失的學生合併前就比較強(兩層語音端點 964.9 對 944.1),所以作者才改用相對端點的 Δ。排除初始化干擾的寬版上,語音幾乎打平(−1.38 對 −1.56 pp),音樂仍有差距(−1.32 對 −2.10 pp)。

排列合併下的結構證據最有說服力,因為它不經過任何下游探針:三層學生第一個 FFN 需要重排的通道從 98.2% 降到 78.3%、第二個從 99.3% 降到 89.8%,CNN 第一層從 42.0% 降到 21.3%;配對通道的平均 |ρ| 在兩層從 60.3% 升到 68.6%、三層從 59.9% 升到 69.3%。作為對照,改成獨立初始化會讓 KD 兩層的 |ρ| 從 60.3% 掉到 47.0%,比損失造成的變化更大。

兩種演算法合起來看,相關性損失在全部 13 次比較中都拿到較高的 Speech Score,Average Score 則是 10 次,最大差距 79.5 分(三層、λ=0.5)。但音樂端方向相反:標準寬度下,KD 學生在每個權重都保有較高的 Music Score(音樂端點 935.1 對 911.0),所以誠實的總結是「語音端改善」,而不是全面更好的編碼器。直覺上說得通:去相關的特徵讓每個通道更有辨識度,線性指派問題應該更好解。但當音樂學生真的在音樂上蒸餾時這是否仍成立,才是這篇最需要的下一個實驗。

Under task arithmetic the correlation loss is more robust in 17 of 18 comparisons (two domains x three weights x three sizes); the single exception is two-layer speech at lambda=0.9 (-0.63 against -0.30 pp). The gap is widest at three layers, lambda=0.8: music -0.59 against -4.51 pp, speech -1.01 against -3.01 pp, with Speech Score 959.4 against 924.1, IC 93.57% against 89.24% and WER 13.62% against 17.08% at the same point. Note, however, that the correlation-loss students are already stronger before merging (two-layer speech endpoint 964.9 against 944.1), which is why the author uses endpoint-relative Delta. On the wide pair, which removes the initialization confound, speech is nearly tied (-1.38 against -1.56 pp) while music still differs (-1.32 against -2.10 pp).

The structural evidence under permutation merging is the most convincing because it involves no downstream probe: for the three-layer pair, the first FFN's permuted channels fall from 98.2% to 78.3%, the second from 99.3% to 89.8%, and the first CNN layer from 42.0% to 21.3%; mean matched |rho| rises from 60.3% to 68.6% at two layers and from 59.9% to 69.3% at three. For scale, switching to independent initializations drops the two-layer KD |rho| from 60.3% to 47.0%, a larger change than the loss produces.

Across both algorithms, the correlation loss wins Speech Score in all 13 comparisons and Average Score in 10, with the largest gap 79.5 points (three layers, lambda=0.5). But the music side runs the other way: at standard width the KD students keep the higher Music Score at every weight (music endpoint 935.1 against 911.0), so the honest summary is a speech-side improvement, not a uniformly better encoder. The intuition is reasonable: decorrelated features give each channel a more distinctive identity, which should make the linear-assignment problem better conditioned. Whether it holds once the music student is actually distilled on music is the experiment this paper most needs next.

End-to-End Historical Music Restoration in Latent Space

在潛在空間中端到端修復歷史音樂錄音

Steven Cho, Junghyun Koo, Raphael Lafargue, Tushar Dhyani, Eloi Moliner, Yuki Mitsufuji
arXiv:2610.00607 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-09-30
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：用一條 Wiener–Hammerstein 式的隨機退化鏈把現代管弦樂錄音「做舊」,再訓練約 40M 參數的潛在流匹配模型修復,在 1911–1925 年的真實錄音上,AA-PQ 從輸入的 4.94 升到 6.70(最佳基線 5.95),MOS-Q 3.89 對 BABE2 的 3.52。另外釋出 9.3 小時的歷史管弦樂測試集,這是這個領域少見的公開基準。Why this one: A randomized Wiener-Hammerstein-style degradation chain "ages" modern orchestral recordings to train a roughly 40M-parameter latent flow-matching restorer; on real 1911-1925 recordings, AA-PQ rises from 4.94 for the input to 6.70 (best baseline 5.95), and MOS-Q is 3.89 against BABE2's 3.52. The paper also releases a 9.3-hour historical orchestral test set, a rare public benchmark for this task.

動機Motivation

1925 年電氣錄音普及之前,大量重要演出只留在蠟筒、78 轉蟲膠唱片與留聲機錄音上,充滿嘶聲、爆音、頻寬限制與非線性失真。歷史音樂修復(HMR)的困難在於:這些錄音沒有「退化前」的原始版本,所以無法直接做監督式學習。

既有研究多半退到比較好處理的子問題:只做超解析(把理想低通濾掉的高頻補回來),或只修鋼琴、人聲等單一來源。多樂器的管弦樂錄音幾乎沒人碰。這篇的主張是:只要把退化過程模擬得夠廣、夠像,就能把這個盲目逆問題轉成可監督的問題,並為管弦樂 HMR 建立第一個端到端的基準。

Before electrical recording spread in 1925, many important performances survived only on wax cylinders, 78-rpm shellac discs and gramophone recordings, full of hiss, clicks, band limitation and nonlinear distortion. The difficulty of historical music restoration (HMR) is that these recordings have no pre-degradation original, so direct supervised learning is impossible.

Prior work mostly retreats to easier sub-problems: super-resolution alone (restoring high frequencies removed by an ideal low-pass), or single sources such as piano or vocals. Multi-instrument orchestral recordings are barely addressed. The paper's claim is that if the degradation process is simulated broadly and faithfully enough, this blind inverse problem can be turned into a supervised one, giving the first end-to-end benchmark for orchestral HMR.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

傳統做法有 Stockham 等人(1975)以頻譜平均做盲等化,以及 Godsill 與 Rayner(1998)的統計式去爆音與去嘶聲。神經網路時代,Li 等人(ISMIR 2020)和 Moliner 與 Välimäki 的兩階段 U-Net(2022)都是在「乾淨音樂 + 合成雜訊」的配對上訓練去噪;BEHM-GAN(2023)專做歷史音樂的頻寬擴展;BABE 與 BABE-2(2024)則走無配對路線,用擴散先驗同時推估退化與修復,但只在鋼琴與人聲上驗證。本篇的三個基線都來自這條線,而共同作者 Moliner 正是這些方法的作者。

方法上,修復模型明說是 LatentFlowSR(Liu 等人, 2026)拿掉頻譜與語意嵌入的版本;真正的貢獻在退化函數。這個「把退化分布刻意做寬、隨機抽參數來訓練盲修復器」的思路,最像影像領域 Real-ESRGAN(Wang 等人, 2021)的高階退化建模,語音則有 VoiceFixer(Liu 等人, 2022)的合成退化鏈——論文沒有提到這兩者,但它的退化設計本質上是把同一個策略搬到歷史錄音上。

Classical approaches include blind equalization from spectral averages (Stockham et al., 1975) and statistical click and hiss removal (Godsill and Rayner, 1998). In the neural era, Li et al. (ISMIR 2020) and the two-stage U-Net of Moliner and Valimaki (2022) trained denoisers on clean-music-plus-synthetic-noise pairs; BEHM-GAN (2023) targets bandwidth extension of historical music; BABE and BABE-2 (2024) take the unpaired route, using a diffusion prior to infer the degradation while restoring, but are validated only on piano and vocals. All three baselines here come from this line, and co-author Moliner is the author of those methods.

Methodologically, the restorer is explicitly LatentFlowSR (Liu et al., 2026) without its spectral and semantic embeddings; the real contribution is the degradation function. The idea of deliberately widening the degradation distribution and sampling its parameters to train a blind restorer most closely resembles Real-ESRGAN's high-order degradation modeling in images (Wang et al., 2021), and in speech the synthetic degradation chain of VoiceFixer (Liu et al., 2022). The paper cites neither, but its degradation design is essentially the same strategy carried over to historical recordings.

方法Method

退化函數是 H_BPF ∘ H_post ∘ Ψ ∘ H_pre,再加上留聲機雜訊。H_pre 與 H_post 是零相位 FIR 濾波器,每個八度一個衰減權重;Ψ 是帶 wet/dry 殘差的縮放 tanh 靜態非線性;H_BPF 依 BABE-2 的參數化模擬窄頻寬(上下截止頻率與兩個滾降斜率)。這就是系統識別裡經典的 Wiener–Hammerstein 結構:線性濾波夾一個靜態非線性。每個訓練樣本都重新抽一組參數,但參數分布本身只在官方 repo 裡,論文沒寫。

修復流程先用 Stable Audio 3 的 SAME-L 自編碼器把音訊編成潛在向量(在乾淨音訊上 Mel-MSE 0.11,DAC 是 0.22),再用約 40M 參數的時間條件 Transformer(8 個 AdaLN-Zero 區塊)做條件流匹配,推論時 10 步 Euler。訓練資料是 6.82 小時的公開古典錄音(5.17 小時全管弦樂、1.65 小時分部),切成 5 秒視窗,每個視窗產生 20 個退化版本,在 4 張 A100 上訓練 400k 步。

The degradation function is H_BPF ∘ H_post ∘ Psi ∘ H_pre, plus gramophone noise. H_pre and H_post are zero-phase FIR filters with one attenuation weight per octave; Psi is a scaled tanh static nonlinearity with a wet/dry residual; H_BPF models narrow bandwidth with BABE-2's parameterization (low and high cutoffs and two roll-off slopes). This is the classic Wiener-Hammerstein structure from system identification: a static nonlinearity sandwiched between linear filters. Parameters are resampled for every training example, but the parameter distribution itself appears only in the official repository, not in the paper.

The pipeline first encodes audio with Stable Audio 3's SAME-L autoencoder (Mel-MSE 0.11 on clean audio, against 0.22 for DAC), then runs conditional flow matching with a roughly 40M-parameter time-conditioned Transformer (8 AdaLN-Zero blocks), using 10 Euler steps at inference. Training data are 6.82 hours of public classical recordings (5.17 hours full orchestra, 1.65 hours sections), cut into 5-second windows with 20 degraded versions each, trained for 400k steps on four A100s.

限制Limitations

合成配對測試集有循環論證的風險。它是把 Spheres 資料集用「自己的」退化函數做舊而來,所以在這上面,用同一個函數訓練的模型(本篇與所有 -FOS 版基線)天生佔優。真正獨立的證據只有無配對的真實錄音,而那裡只能用無參考指標與聽測。

退化函數的驗證也比文字說的弱。Table 1 用 MERT、Fx-Encoder++、SAME-L 三種嵌入算 MAD,「只加留聲機雜訊」在 6 欄中有 5 欄比本篇的 WH 退化更接近真實錄音(例如 MERT 全管弦樂 3.90 對 4.23),論文以「這些編碼器本來就不是為退化設計的」帶過。而且全管弦樂與輕管弦樂兩組真實錄音彼此的 MAD 只有 2.08–3.00,所有合成退化(最低 3.86)離真實錄音都還比這個遠。

聽測規模小:38 位受試者中只有 22 位通過錨點篩選,每個方法 88 個評分、10 段 5 秒片段。AA-PQ 與 MOS-Q 的 Pearson r = 0.920 是在少數幾個系統上算的,證據力有限。訓練只有 6.82 小時,且限定無人聲的古典管弦樂。

The synthetic paired test set risks circularity. It is made by aging the Spheres dataset with the paper's own degradation function, so models trained with that same function (this model and every -FOS baseline) have a built-in advantage there. The only truly independent evidence is the unpaired real recordings, where only reference-free metrics and listening tests are possible.

The validation of the degradation function is also weaker than the text suggests. Table 1 computes MAD with MERT, Fx-Encoder++ and SAME-L embeddings, and gramophone noise alone is closer to real recordings than the WH degradation in 5 of 6 columns (for example MERT full orchestra 3.90 against 4.23); the paper explains this away as those encoders not being designed for degradations. Moreover, the two real subsets (full and light orchestra) are only 2.08-3.00 apart in MAD, while every synthetic degradation (lowest 3.86) is still farther from real recordings than that.

The listening test is small: only 22 of 38 listeners passed the anchor screening, giving 88 ratings per method over ten 5-second excerpts. The AA-PQ versus MOS-Q Pearson r = 0.920 is computed over only a handful of systems, so it carries limited weight. Training uses just 6.82 hours and is restricted to classical orchestral music without vocals.

討論Discussion

在真實歷史錄音上,結果是清楚的。AA-PQ 全管弦樂/輕管弦樂從輸入的 4.94/5.00 升到 6.70/6.86,最佳基線 Denoiser-FOS 是 5.95/6.17,未改訓練的 BABE2 是 5.88/5.87。聽測 MOS-Q 為 3.89,BABE2 為 3.52、只用留聲機雜訊訓練的消融版 SAMECFM-G 為 3.22、BABE2-FOS 為 2.72(高錨點 4.44、低錨點 1.17);MOS-P(保留原作特徵)為 4.32,SAMECFM-G 4.26、BABE2-FOS 3.90、BABE2 3.70。WH 退化相對於純雜訊的消融差距(MOS-Q 3.89 對 3.22)是支持「退化建模比模型架構重要」最直接的數字。

合成配對集上則出現品質與保真的取捨。本篇 Mel-MSE 1.90 最低(BEHM-GAN-FOS 1.97 很接近)、CLAP 0.73 最高、AA-PQ 7.21 接近真值的 7.44,但 MAD 3.70 只排第三,未改訓練的 Denoiser 是 1.21、BABE2-FOS 是 1.82。作者承認這暗示生成式方法有幻覺。奇怪的是 Denoiser 經 FOS 調適後 MAD 反而惡化到 4.19,論文沒有解釋。

這篇最持久的價值可能是那份 9.3 小時、149 首、1911–1925 年美國出版的無配對管弦樂測試集(已放上 Zenodo),以及「先把退化鏈做對」這個可重用的訓練配方。模型本身只是 LatentFlowSR 的簡化版,下一步要看的是:在退化分布公開、且有獨立於訓練退化的配對評估之後,這個優勢還剩多少。

On real historical recordings the result is clear. AA-PQ for full/light orchestra rises from 4.94/5.00 for the input to 6.70/6.86, against 5.95/6.17 for the best baseline (Denoiser-FOS) and 5.88/5.87 for the unadapted BABE2. In the listening test, MOS-Q is 3.89, against 3.52 for BABE2, 3.22 for the gramophone-noise-only ablation SAMECFM-G and 2.72 for BABE2-FOS (high anchor 4.44, low anchor 1.17); MOS-P (preservation) is 4.32, against 4.26, 3.90 and 3.70 respectively. The ablation gap between the WH degradation and noise only (MOS-Q 3.89 against 3.22) is the most direct number supporting the claim that degradation modeling matters more than the architecture.

The synthetic paired set shows a quality-preservation trade-off. This model has the lowest Mel-MSE at 1.90 (BEHM-GAN-FOS is close at 1.97), the highest CLAP at 0.73 and AA-PQ 7.21 near the ground truth's 7.44, but its MAD of 3.70 ranks only third, behind the unadapted Denoiser at 1.21 and BABE2-FOS at 1.82. The authors concede this suggests hallucination. Oddly, the Denoiser's MAD worsens to 4.19 after FOS adaptation, which the paper does not explain.

The most lasting value may be the 9.3-hour, 149-piece unpaired test set of orchestral recordings published in the US in 1911-1925 (released on Zenodo), and the reusable recipe of getting the degradation chain right first. The model itself is a simplified LatentFlowSR; the next question is how much of the advantage remains once the degradation distribution is published and paired evaluation is decoupled from the training degradation.
