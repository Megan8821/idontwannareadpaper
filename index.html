Piano2Pop: Expanding Piano Demos into Pop Productions Without Aligned Supervision

Piano2Pop：不用對齊的鋼琴–流行配對資料，把鋼琴 MIDI 小樣擴寫成完整的流行器樂編曲

Yun-Chen Cheng, Chih-Pin Tan, Tzu-Hung Huang, Yi-Hsuan Yang
arXiv:2610.31510 · cs.LG; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-22
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：台大楊奕軒團隊把 Pop2Piano／PiCoGen2 這類「流行歌縮成鋼琴」的任務反過來做：從 8,876 首流行器樂反推出鋼琴 MIDI「假小樣」，拿來微調 2.39B 參數的 ACE-Step v1.5，讓它把鋼琴 MIDI 擴寫成完整編曲。在 476 組真人鋼琴翻彈上，用自家 PiCoGen2 產生假小樣的版本拿到最好的 APA（0.964）、FAD（0.231）與頻譜形狀誤差（1.044 dB），零樣本的 ACE-Step cover 只有 APA 0.052。要保留的是：全部是客觀指標、沒有聽測；評估用的「小樣」其實是成品歌曲的翻彈，不是寫歌時真正的草稿。Why this one: Yi-Hsuan Yang's group at National Taiwan University runs the Pop2Piano/PiCoGen2 'reduce a pop song to piano' task in reverse: it derives piano MIDI 'pseudo demos' from 8,876 pop instrumentals and uses them to fine-tune the 2.39B-parameter ACE-Step v1.5 so it expands piano MIDI into a full arrangement. On 476 real piano-cover pairs, the version whose pseudo demos come from the group's own PiCoGen2 gets the best APA (0.964), FAD (0.231) and spectral-shape error (1.044 dB), while zero-shot ACE-Step cover reaches only APA 0.052. The caveats: every metric is objective, with no listening test, and the evaluation 'demos' are covers of finished songs, not the sketches a songwriter actually starts from.

動機Motivation

流行歌常常從一份鋼琴小樣開始：兩隻手就能交代旋律、和聲、聲部配置、力度與律動，但要變成成品，還得有人決定鼓、貝斯、樂器編制與混音。作者把這件事定義成 Piano2Pop：輸入鋼琴 MIDI，輸出一段連貫、像流行歌、而且忠於小樣意圖的器樂製作。

反方向的「流行歌轉鋼琴翻彈」已經有 Pop2Piano 與 PiCoGen2 等系統，作者把那類工作看成音樂縮減（reduction），Piano2Pop 則是擴寫（expansion）——不只是替不變的鋼琴聲部加伴奏，而是要把鋼琴裡的音重新分配給貝斯、鼓與主奏樂器。

難處在資料：鋼琴翻彈和原曲的長度、速度甚至段落結構都不一樣，對齊的配對資料又少又髒。作者的賭注是：多樂器轉譜與大型音樂生成模型都已經夠強，可以完全不用配對資料，從流行器樂反推鋼琴小樣來造訓練對。

A pop song often starts as a piano demo: two hands can convey melody, harmony, voicing, dynamics and groove, but turning it into a finished track still requires someone to decide drums, bass, instrumentation and mixing. The authors define this as Piano2Pop: given piano MIDI, generate an instrumental production that is coherent, idiomatic to pop, and faithful to the demo's intent.

The reverse direction, pop-to-piano cover generation, already has systems such as Pop2Piano and PiCoGen2. The authors frame those as music reduction and Piano2Pop as expansion: not just accompanying an unchanged piano part, but reassigning the piano's notes to bass, drums and lead instruments.

The obstacle is data: a piano cover and the original differ in duration, tempo and often structure, so aligned pairs are scarce and messy. The authors bet that multi-instrument transcription and large music generators are now strong enough to drop paired data entirely and build training pairs by deriving piano demos from pop instrumentals.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的對照是反方向的 Pop2Piano（Choi 與 Lee，ICASSP 2023）與 PiCoGen2（Tan 等，ISMIR 2024），後者正是這篇作者群自己的模型，在這裡被拿來當「假小樣產生器」。符號域的前身是鋼琴到管弦樂的投射式配器（Crestel 等，2018）與 Zhao 等（NeurIPS 2024）的多軌伴奏編排；它們輸出樂譜，Piano2Pop 直接輸出音訊，連音色與製作質感一起處理。

時間對齊的條件生成方面，JASCO（Tal 等，2024）與 MuseControlLite（Tsai 等，ICML 2025）用旋律、力度、節奏等可分解的控制訊號；SingSong 與 StemGen 則是給一軌、生成能配上它的另一軌。這篇的差別是直接以完整的複音鋼琴捲簾（piano roll）當條件，不先拆成旋律與和弦。

論文沒有點明、但最該寫出來的是：PiCo 路線本質上就是機器翻譯裡的反向翻譯（back-translation，Sennrich 等 2016）——用一個「目標→來源」的模型替單語資料造出假來源，再訓練「來源→目標」的模型。另一條 pair-free 路線則和 JASCO、Music ControlNet 從訓練音訊自動抽出條件訊號的做法同源，新意在強迫 MuScriptor 把整首混音轉成單一鋼琴聲部。

The most direct comparisons are the reverse-direction Pop2Piano (Choi and Lee, ICASSP 2023) and PiCoGen2 (Tan et al., ISMIR 2024); the latter is this group's own model and serves here as the pseudo-demo generator. The symbolic-domain predecessors are projective piano-to-orchestra orchestration (Crestel et al., 2018) and the multi-track accompaniment arrangement of Zhao et al. (NeurIPS 2024); those output scores, whereas Piano2Pop outputs audio and handles timbre and production texture as well.

For time-aligned conditional generation, JASCO (Tal et al., 2024) and MuseControlLite (Tsai et al., ICML 2025) use factorized controls such as melody, dynamics and rhythm, while SingSong and StemGen take one track and generate another that fits it. This paper instead conditions on a full polyphonic piano roll without first splitting it into melody and chords.

What the paper does not say, but most deserves saying: the PiCo route is essentially back-translation from machine translation (Sennrich et al., 2016), using a target-to-source model to create synthetic sources for monolingual data, then training the source-to-target model. The pair-free route shares its roots with JASCO and Music ControlNet, which extract conditions automatically from training audio; what is new is forcing MuScriptor to transcribe the entire mix as a single piano part.

方法Method

訓練資料 DB1 是 8,876 首 YouTube 流行歌（8,463 首、約 554 小時用於訓練），全都沒有真人鋼琴 MIDI，假小樣有兩種來源。pair-free 路線用 MuScriptor 轉譜，但把解碼限制在 MIDI program 0（大鋼琴），等於叫它把整首器樂「彈成」鋼琴；缺的力度則用 10 ms 能量包絡排序後對應到 25–95 的分位數。

為了讓 pair-free 小樣更像真人彈的，作者試了兩種擴增。RDA（register–density）把非貝斯聲部折回附近八度、依和聲功能刪音，因為統計顯示兩種假小樣的局部音域都比真鋼琴寬（19.77／19.88 對 16.31 半音），MuScriptor 又太密（每秒 7.94 個起音，真鋼琴 5.30）。FST（function-selective）先用 HTDemucs 分軌，組出全混、去鼓、去貝斯、只留鋼琴＋貝斯四種混音再分別轉成鋼琴，對應「鋼琴編曲是只保留部分功能的縮減」這個看法。

pair-supervised 路線則用 PiCoGen2 對每首器樂生成鋼琴翻彈；因為它一次生成一小節，作者先做節拍追蹤，再把每小節線性拉伸到對應的小節邊界。

模型是 ACE-Step v1.5 的 rectified-flow transformer（24 層、約 2.39B 參數），VAE 凍結（48 kHz 立體聲 ↔ 25 Hz、64 通道潛在）。新增的鋼琴捲簾編碼器是五層殘差 1-D 卷積（kernel 5、寬 512），每側感受野約 0.4 秒，輸出取代原本的參考音訊條件。全部設定都訓練 500,000 步，前 2,000 步只訓練編碼器。

The training set DB1 has 8,876 YouTube pop songs (8,463 for training, about 554 hours), none with real piano MIDI, and pseudo demos come from two sources. The pair-free route transcribes with MuScriptor but restricts decoding to MIDI program 0 (grand piano), effectively making it 'play' the whole instrumental as piano; missing velocities are assigned by ranking onsets in a 10 ms energy envelope and mapping quantiles into the 25–95 range.

To make pair-free demos more like human playing, the authors try two augmentations. RDA (register–density) folds non-bass voices toward the local octave and deletes notes by harmonic function, because both pseudo-demo sources have wider local pitch spreads than real piano (19.77/19.88 vs 16.31 semitones) and MuScriptor is too dense (7.94 attacks/s vs 5.30). FST (function-selective) separates stems with HTDemucs, builds four mixtures (full, no drums, no bass, piano plus bass) and transcribes each as piano, following the view that piano arrangement is a reduction keeping only some musical functions.

The pair-supervised route has PiCoGen2 generate a piano cover of each instrumental; since it generates one bar at a time, the authors beat-track the source and linearly stretch each bar to the corresponding bar boundaries.

The model is ACE-Step v1.5's rectified-flow transformer (24 layers, about 2.39B parameters) with a frozen VAE (48 kHz stereo to 25 Hz, 64-channel latents). A new piano-roll encoder, five residual 1-D conv layers (kernel 5, width 512) with about 0.4 s receptive field per side, replaces the reference-audio conditioning. Every setting trains for 500,000 steps, with only the encoder trained for the first 2,000.

限制Limitations

第一，評估的「真實輸入」不是真正的小樣。DB2 的 476 組資料是 YouTube 頻道的鋼琴伴奏翻彈，配上原曲器樂；翻彈是聽完成品之後做的縮減，資訊量和寫歌前的草稿不同，用 Kong 的轉譜模型轉成 MIDI 又多一層誤差。論文的任務定義（從小樣推出製作意圖）和評估資料其實不在同一個方向上。

第二，沒有任何聽測，只有一句「與非正式聆聽一致」。APA 的參考分布只來自 33 組人工對齊的 oracle 配對，因為樣本遠少於 CLAP 的維度，還得先 PCA 降到 32 維；FAD 用 CLAP 嵌入計算。這些指標能比相對排名，但「像不像一首好的流行編曲」沒有被直接量測。

第三，訓練分布上的指標無法預測真實表現：PiCo 在訓練分布的 APA 是四個微調系統裡最低的（0.696），換成真鋼琴卻是最高（0.964）。只看內部驗證會選錯模型。

第四，「不需要對齊監督」只對 Piano2Pop 這個方向成立。最好的 PiCo 路線依賴 PiCoGen2，而 PiCoGen2 本身就是用弱對齊的翻彈–原曲配對訓練的，而且是作者群自己的模型。最後，全部只做 30 秒片段，沒有處理整首歌的段落結構。

First, the 'real' evaluation inputs are not real demos. DB2's 476 pairs are piano backing-track covers from YouTube channels matched with the original instrumentals; a cover is a reduction made after hearing the finished song and carries different information from a pre-production sketch, and transcribing it with Kong's model adds another layer of error. The task definition (inferring production intent from a demo) and the evaluation data run in different directions.

Second, there is no listening test, only a remark that results align with informal listening. The APA reference distribution comes from just 33 manually aligned oracle pairs, and since that is far fewer samples than CLAP dimensions, it has to be PCA-reduced to 32 dimensions first; FAD uses CLAP embeddings. These metrics can rank systems relative to each other, but whether the output sounds like a good pop arrangement is never measured directly.

Third, training-distribution metrics do not predict real performance: PiCo has the lowest training-distribution APA of the four fine-tuned systems (0.696) yet the highest on real piano (0.964). Internal validation alone would pick the wrong model.

Fourth, 'without aligned supervision' holds only for the Piano2Pop direction. The best route, PiCo, depends on PiCoGen2, which was itself trained on weakly aligned cover–original pairs and is the group's own model. Finally, everything is 30-second segments; whole-song section structure is not addressed.

討論Discussion

主要結果在真鋼琴輸入上：PiCo 的 APA 0.964、FAD 0.231、頻譜形狀誤差 1.044 dB，三項都是最好；FST 次之（0.895、0.255、1.371）；只做全混轉譜的 Base 是 0.857、0.323、2.756。RDA 沒有穩定的好處，真鋼琴 FAD 甚至比 Base 差（0.396）。兩個零樣本基線都失敗：ACE-Step cover 的 APA 只有 0.052、頻譜形狀誤差 3.223；MuseControlLite 的 FAD 0.647、形狀誤差 3.984，最差。

最有說服力的是分軌能量比例。原曲裡貝斯＋鼓佔總能量 58.58%、鋼琴 12.22%。MuseControlLite 的鋼琴佔 53.01%、貝斯＋鼓只有 3.44%，幾乎就是把鋼琴原樣留著；Base 是 36.09% 與 19.69%，節奏組明顯不足；FST 拉到 51.03% 與 12.32%，鋼琴比例幾乎和原曲一樣。這支持作者的結論：保留哪些音樂功能，比讓音域與密度看起來像真鋼琴更重要。

不過 PiCo 的鋼琴只剩 5.57%，不到原曲的一半，代表它偏向「改寫過頭」。原曲本來就有鋼琴的歌，這時鋼琴聲部被吃掉；APA 也不會懲罰這件事。

整體來看，這篇的貢獻是任務定義與一套可重複的資料管線，而不是新架構。兩條路線的對照很實用：手上有反向模型就用反向翻譯，沒有的話 FST 已經能拿到大部分的好處，而且只需要現成的分軌與轉譜工具。下一步最需要的是用真正的寫歌小樣與聽測來驗證。

The headline results are on real piano input: PiCo has APA 0.964, FAD 0.231 and spectral-shape error 1.044 dB, best on all three; FST is second (0.895, 0.255, 1.371); Base, which only transcribes the full mix, gets 0.857, 0.323 and 2.756. RDA gives no consistent benefit, and its real-piano FAD is even worse than Base (0.396). Both zero-shot baselines fail: ACE-Step cover has APA of only 0.052 and shape error 3.223, while MuseControlLite is worst with FAD 0.647 and shape error 3.984.

The most convincing evidence is the stem energy shares. In the originals, bass plus drums take 58.58% of total energy and piano 12.22%. MuseControlLite has 53.01% piano and only 3.44% bass plus drums, essentially leaving the piano as is; Base has 36.09% and 19.69%, clearly short on rhythm section; FST reaches 51.03% and 12.32%, with a piano share almost identical to the originals. This supports the authors' conclusion that which musical functions are retained matters more than making register and density look like real piano.

PiCo, however, keeps only 5.57% piano, less than half of the originals, meaning it leans toward over-transformation. For songs that genuinely have piano, the piano part gets swallowed, and APA does not penalize that.

Overall, the contribution is a task definition and a reproducible data pipeline, not a new architecture. The comparison of the two routes is practical: with a reverse model at hand, use back-translation; without one, FST already captures most of the benefit using only off-the-shelf separation and transcription tools. What is needed next is validation on real songwriting demos with a listening test.

Phase-Based Onset Enhancement for Piano Transcription in Ensemble Settings

用相位強化起音：在鋼琴三重奏與協奏曲裡，讓鋼琴轉譜不被其他樂器的持續長音淹沒

Hyemi Kim, Jiyun Park, Juhan Nam
arXiv:2610.29222 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-21
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：KAIST 團隊在 Transkun（semi-CRF 鋼琴轉譜）上加一個以「相鄰幀相位差 × 幅度」為輸入的起音頭，把起音機率的對數加進每個音符區間的分數，專治管弦樂長音蓋住鋼琴弱起音的情況；他們也重錄了一份用 Disklavier 取得精確 MIDI 的協奏曲資料集 PCD-K。在同樣加了 25 小時交響樂混音訓練的條件下，起音 F1 在 MAESTRO v2 三重奏子集從 93.61 升到 94.69，在 PCD-K 從 87.93 升到 90.46，主要來自召回率。要保留的是：PCD-K 只有 70 段 12 秒、約 14 分鐘，全部是單次訓練；消融表裡完整模型的數字和主表對不起來。Why this one: A KAIST team adds an onset head to Transkun (semi-CRF piano transcription) whose input is 'frame-to-frame phase difference × magnitude', and adds the log onset probability to every note-interval score, targeting weak piano onsets buried under sustained orchestral tones; they also re-record a concerto dataset, PCD-K, with exact Disklavier MIDI. With the same 25 hours of symphonic mix-in training, onset F1 rises from 93.61 to 94.69 on the MAESTRO v2 trio subset and from 87.93 to 90.46 on PCD-K, mainly through recall. The caveats: PCD-K is only 70 twelve-second excerpts, about 14 minutes, every result is a single training run, and the full model's numbers in the ablation table do not match the main tables.

動機Motivation

鋼琴獨奏轉譜在 MAESTRO 上已經很高，但只要鋼琴和其他樂器一起演奏——伴奏、三重奏、協奏曲——表現就明顯下滑。作者指出一個具體的失敗情境：管弦樂在極強（fortissimo）持續長音時，鋼琴的較弱起音落在同一時間、甚至同一個音高或重疊的泛音上，在梅爾頻譜或 CQT 這類只看幅度的表示裡幾乎看不出來。

他們的觀察是：持續長音在那些頻帶上的相位變化相對穩定，鋼琴的新起音則會帶來任意的初始相位，造成相位突然跳動。所以即使幅度被蓋住，相位仍可能留下線索。

另一個動機是評估資料。現有精確標註的鋼琴資料集都是獨奏；有鋼琴的合奏資料集則用樂譜對齊產生標註，精度不足以評估轉譜。作者因此一邊改模型、一邊補資料。

Solo piano transcription is already very strong on MAESTRO, but once the piano plays with other instruments (accompaniment, trios, concertos) performance drops noticeably. The authors point to a specific failure: when the orchestra holds loud fortissimo sustained tones, weaker piano onsets landing at the same time, sometimes on the same pitch or overlapping harmonics, are nearly invisible in magnitude-only representations such as mel spectrograms or CQT.

Their observation is that sustained tones keep relatively stable phase behaviour in those bands, whereas a new piano onset brings an arbitrary initial phase and causes an abrupt phase jump. So even when magnitude is masked, phase may still leave a cue.

The other motivation is evaluation data. Existing precisely labelled piano datasets are solo; ensemble datasets with piano derive labels from score alignment, which is not accurate enough to evaluate transcription. The authors therefore fix the model and the data together.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

骨幹是 Transkun：Yan、Cwitkowitz 與 Duan（NeurIPS 2021）把鋼琴轉譜寫成 semi-Markov CRF，對所有可能的時間區間打分，Yan 與 Duan（ISMIR 2024）再換成縮放內積與純 encoder Transformer。對照的另一端是 Onsets and Frames 與 Kong 等的高解析度回歸模型這類以幀為單位的系統，以及 MT3／YourMT3+ 這類多樂器轉譜：後者能處理合奏，但通常不轉延音踏板，這也是作者選 Transkun 的理由。

論文稱相位是鋼琴轉譜裡「相對未被探索」的面向，就神經轉譜而言大致成立，但相位式起音偵測其實有二十年歷史。Bello 等（2005）的起音偵測教學已經整理了相位偏差（phase deviation）與複數域方法；Dixon（DAFx 2006）提出的加權相位偏差（weighted phase deviation）就是「以幅度加權的相位二階差」。這篇的式 (2) S = |Δφ| · |X|^γ 和 Dixon 的方法幾乎同構，只是改用一階差、交給 MLP 學習——兩篇都有引用，但論文沒有點出這層關係。

換句話說，新意不在相位特徵本身，而在把經典的相位起音函數做成可學習的起音頭，並以 log p_onset 的形式接進 semi-CRF 的區間分數裡。資料方面，PCD-K 是 Özer 等（TISMIR 2023）Piano Concerto Dataset 的重錄版，原版標註來自音訊–MIDI 對齊。

The backbone is Transkun: Yan, Cwitkowitz and Duan (NeurIPS 2021) cast piano transcription as a semi-Markov CRF that scores every possible time interval, and Yan and Duan (ISMIR 2024) switched to scaled inner products and an encoder-only Transformer. At the other end are frame-based systems such as Onsets and Frames and the high-resolution regression model of Kong et al., and multi-instrument transcribers such as MT3/YourMT3+, which handle ensembles but usually do not transcribe sustain pedal, which is why the authors chose Transkun.

The paper calls phase a 'relatively unexplored' aspect of piano transcription. That roughly holds for neural transcription, but phase-based onset detection is twenty years old. Bello et al.'s (2005) onset detection tutorial already covers phase deviation and complex-domain methods, and Dixon's (DAFx 2006) weighted phase deviation is a magnitude-weighted second-order phase difference. The paper's Eq. (2), S = |Δφ| · |X|^γ, is nearly isomorphic to Dixon's, except it uses the first-order difference and hands the result to an MLP; both works are cited, but the paper never draws the connection.

In other words, the novelty is not the phase feature itself but turning a classic phase onset function into a learnable onset head and wiring it into the semi-CRF interval scores as log p_onset. On the data side, PCD-K re-records the Piano Concerto Dataset of Özer et al. (TISMIR 2023), whose original labels come from audio–MIDI alignment.

方法Method

Transkun-Phase 只改一處。對輸入做 STFT（44.1 kHz、窗長 4096、hop 1024，約 23 ms），算相鄰幀的相位差並包回 [−π, π]，乘上幅度的 γ 次方（論文設 γ = 1）得到起音顯著度 S。一個 LayerNorm＋兩層線性＋GELU 的 MLP 從 S 預測每一幀、每個鋼琴鍵的起音機率。

這個機率取對數、乘上 λ_phase = 100（依驗證集調整，用來對齊區間分數的尺度），加到所有以該幀開頭的區間分數上：凡是從可能起音處開始的音符區間，分數都會被抬高。訓練目標是原本的 CRF 損失、屬性損失（力度、起訖位置微調）再加起音的二元交叉熵。

相位項只加在 88 鍵的分數圖上，不加在兩個踏板上。作者的理由是：音符起音帶來新的激發與任意初始相位，踏板只改變既有振動的衰減，相位是連續的。輸入的梅爾頻譜維持 229 bins、30–8000 Hz。

訓練時，MAESTRO v3 的鋼琴軌隨機混入作者從 YouTube 與 IMSLP 蒐集的 Symphony 資料（無鋼琴、約 25 小時、13 位作曲家），並套隨機增益。訓練 500K 次、batch 8，在單張 RTX 4090 上約 6 天。

評估資料有兩份。MAESTRO v2 三重奏子集是 v2 裡誤收、v3 又移除的六段鋼琴三重奏（Arensky、Beethoven、Mendelssohn），共約 2 小時 33 分。PCD-K 由八位研究者（兩位主修鋼琴）戴耳機跟著 PCD 的 12 秒管弦樂軌在 Disklavier 上重新演奏，得到 59 段，加上 11 段新曲目共 70 段；鋼琴與管弦樂以等響度混合，另有加殘響的版本。

Transkun-Phase changes one thing. It takes an STFT of the input (44.1 kHz, window 4096, hop 1024, about 23 ms), computes the frame-to-frame phase difference wrapped to [−π, π], and multiplies it by magnitude raised to γ (set to γ = 1) to get an onset saliency S. An MLP (LayerNorm, two linear layers, GELU) predicts per-frame, per-key onset probabilities from S.

The log of that probability, scaled by λ_phase = 100 (tuned on validation to match the interval-score scale), is added to every interval starting at that frame, raising the score of any note interval that begins at a likely onset. The training objective is the original CRF loss plus attribute losses (velocity, onset/offset refinement) plus a binary cross-entropy onset loss.

The phase term is added only to the 88 key score maps, not the two pedal maps. The authors reason that note onsets introduce new excitations with arbitrary initial phase, whereas pedals only change the decay of ongoing vibrations and keep phase continuous. The mel input stays at 229 bins, 30–8000 Hz.

During training, MAESTRO v3 piano tracks are randomly mixed with Symphony, a piano-free set the authors collected from YouTube and IMSLP (about 25 hours, 13 composers), with random gain. Training runs 500K iterations at batch size 8, about 6 days on a single RTX 4090.

There are two evaluation sets. The MAESTRO v2 trio subset is six piano-trio recordings (Arensky, Beethoven, Mendelssohn) accidentally included in v2 and removed in v3, about 2 h 33 min in total. For PCD-K, eight researchers (two piano majors) re-performed on a Disklavier while following PCD's 12-second orchestral tracks over headphones, giving 59 excerpts plus 11 new ones, 70 in all; piano and orchestra are mixed at equal level, with a reverberant version as well.

限制Limitations

第一，PCD-K 很小：70 段 × 12 秒約 14 分鐘，演奏者多是研究室成員，而且是戴耳機跟著錄好的管弦樂彈，不是真正的協奏曲現場互動；混音一律等響度，比真實錄音裡鋼琴被蓋住的程度溫和。三重奏子集也只有六段、三首曲子。所有結果都是單次訓練，沒有重複種子或信賴區間，2 個 F1 點左右的差距要在這種規模下判讀需要保留。

第二，數字對不起來。消融表中完整模型（A×|Δφ|）在三重奏的起音 F1 是 94.83、OFV 是 83.27，主表卻是 94.69 與 82.78；PCD-K 上則是 90.33 對 90.46、OFV 57.41 對 55.48。論文沒有說明消融是不是另一次訓練。

第三，對相位機制的解釋不完整。按定義，一個穩定的弦樂長音，其一階相位差是一個隨頻率決定的非零常數，而不是零；乘上它的大幅度之後，S 反而偏大。真正「持續音相位穩定、起音相位跳動」的量是二階差（Dixon 的做法），但論文的二階差變體反而略差。每幀獨立運作的 MLP 如何分辨這兩種情況，論文沒有分析。

第四，踏板與力度沒有一致改善：PCD-K 上幀層級與踏板的最佳結果都是用環境音擴增的 Transkun_Aug（踏板起音 F1 62.12 對本文 57.83）；在 PCD-K 的消融裡，只用幅度的變體 OFV（57.85）還略高於加相位的版本。

First, PCD-K is small: 70 excerpts × 12 s is about 14 minutes, performers are mostly lab members, and they played along with pre-recorded orchestra over headphones rather than interacting live as in a real concerto; mixes are always equal-level, milder than the masking in real recordings. The trio subset is only six recordings of three works. Every result is a single training run with no repeated seeds or confidence intervals, so gaps of around 2 F1 points at this scale should be read with caution.

Second, the numbers do not match. In the ablation table the full model (A×|Δφ|) has trio onset F1 94.83 and OFV 83.27, but the main table reports 94.69 and 82.78; on PCD-K it is 90.33 vs 90.46 and OFV 57.41 vs 55.48. The paper does not say whether the ablation is a separate training run.

Third, the explanation of the phase mechanism is incomplete. By definition, a stable string tone has a first-order phase difference that is a nonzero, frequency-dependent constant, not zero; multiplied by its large magnitude, S becomes large. The quantity that is truly 'stable for sustains, jumpy at onsets' is the second-order difference (Dixon's approach), yet the paper's second-order variant does slightly worse. The paper does not analyse how a per-frame MLP tells the two cases apart.

Fourth, pedal and velocity do not improve consistently: on PCD-K the best frame-level and pedal results belong to Transkun_Aug with environmental-sound augmentation (pedal onset F1 62.12 vs this paper's 57.83), and in the PCD-K ablation the magnitude-only variant's OFV (57.85) is slightly above the phase version.

討論Discussion

主結果都是對同樣用 Symphony 訓練的 Transkun 基線比。三重奏子集上，起音 F1 從 93.61 升到 94.69（召回 89.84 → 91.60），含終止點的 OF 從 82.24 升到 83.93，OFV 從 80.84 升到 82.78；踏板幀層級 92.34 → 93.00、踏板起音 66.65 → 68.97。PCD-K 上幅度更大：起音 87.93 → 90.46（召回 81.60 → 86.87，精確率反而從 96.82 降到 95.38），OF 68.14 → 70.97，OFV 52.82 → 55.48，踏板幀層級 78.95 → 84.44。

參考點很有用：只用 MAESTRO 訓練的 Transkun 在 PCD-K 混音上的起音 F1 只有 64.19（精確率 50.63），同一模型對乾淨鋼琴軌是 97.87。所以光是加入交響樂混音訓練就補上了大部分落差（64.19 → 87.93），相位再多補 2.5 點，離上限還有約 7 點。

消融最值得看的是：只用幅度、但同樣加上起音頭的變體，三重奏起音 F1 為 93.96、PCD-K 為 88.04，比基線只高 0.35 與 0.11。也就是說，多一個監督式起音頭本身幫助不大，增益主要來自相位（再多 0.87 與 2.29）。這比主表更能支持論文的主張。

踏板的結果也和作者「踏板不改變相位」的說法有張力：相位項沒有接到踏板分數圖上，PCD-K 的踏板起音 F1 卻從 51.83 升到 57.83，大概是透過共用的 Transformer 表徵間接受益。

實用上，這個改動很便宜——一個 MLP 加一個 STFT——就能讓現成的 semi-CRF 在合奏中多撈回被蓋住的音。PCD-K 有精確的鋼琴 MIDI，又公開在 Zenodo，對多樂器轉譜與鋼琴分離都是有用的小型基準，即使規模還太小，無法單獨支撐結論。

The main results all compare against a Transkun baseline trained with the same Symphony data. On the trio subset, onset F1 rises from 93.61 to 94.69 (recall 89.84 → 91.60), onset-with-offset (OF) from 82.24 to 83.93, OFV from 80.84 to 82.78; pedal frame 92.34 → 93.00 and pedal onset 66.65 → 68.97. On PCD-K the gains are larger: onset 87.93 → 90.46 (recall 81.60 → 86.87, while precision actually drops from 96.82 to 95.38), OF 68.14 → 70.97, OFV 52.82 → 55.48, pedal frame 78.95 → 84.44.

The reference points are telling: MAESTRO-only Transkun gets onset F1 of just 64.19 on PCD-K mixtures (precision 50.63), versus 97.87 for the same model on the clean piano track. So symphonic mix-in training alone closes most of the gap (64.19 → 87.93), phase adds another 2.5 points, and about 7 points remain to the upper bound.

The most informative ablation: a magnitude-only variant with the same onset head scores onset F1 93.96 on the trio subset and 88.04 on PCD-K, only 0.35 and 0.11 above the baseline. In other words, an extra supervised onset head by itself helps little; the gain comes mainly from phase (another 0.87 and 2.29). This supports the paper's claim better than the main tables do.

The pedal results sit in some tension with the authors' 'pedals preserve phase' argument: the phase term is not wired into the pedal score maps, yet PCD-K pedal onset F1 rises from 51.83 to 57.83, presumably via the shared Transformer representation.

Practically, the change is cheap (one MLP and one STFT) and lets an off-the-shelf semi-CRF recover masked notes in ensembles. With exact piano MIDI and public release on Zenodo, PCD-K is a useful small benchmark for multi-instrument transcription and piano separation, even if it is too small to carry conclusions on its own.

MQAScore: Fine-Grained Text-Music Alignment Evaluation via Large Audio Language Model

MQAScore：把文字–音樂對齊拆成曲風、樂器、情緒／主題、人聲、使用情境五個維度，逐一用大型音訊語言模型回答「是／否」來評分

Wei-Jaw Lee, Chun-Ping Wang, Xuanjun Chen, Yi-Hsuan Yang
arXiv:2610.31083 · cs.SD; cs.AI; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-22
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：台大團隊把文字生成音樂的對齊評估，從 CLAP 這種「一個總分」改成逐一檢查提示詞裡的每個概念：問大型音訊語言模型「這段音樂是不是屬於曲風 X？」，取「是」的機率。在 MusicCaps 的情緒／主題、人聲、使用情境上，Qwen3-Omni 逐概念評分的 AUC 是 0.943／0.966／0.842，同樣逐概念計算的 CLAP 只有 0.669／0.759／0.711；和 11 位受過音樂訓練的標註者相比，一致率接近人與人之間的水準。要保留的是：驗證全部用真實錄音，不是生成音樂；專家配對只有 266 組，分到各維度後每格約幾十組。Why this one: A National Taiwan University team changes text-to-music alignment evaluation from a single CLAP-style score to checking each concept in the prompt: ask a large audio language model 'Does this music belong to the genre X?' and take the probability of 'Yes'. On MusicCaps mood/theme, vocal and usage, concept-level Qwen3-Omni scoring reaches AUC 0.943/0.966/0.842, versus 0.669/0.759/0.711 for CLAP computed per concept the same way; agreement with 11 musically trained annotators approaches human–human levels. The caveats: all verification uses real recordings, not generated music, and there are only 266 expert pairs, roughly a few dozen per cell once split by dimension.

動機Motivation

文字生成音樂（TTM）的提示詞越來越長、越來越細，評估對齊卻仍主要靠 CLAP 或 MuQ-MuLan 這類文字與音訊嵌入的整體相似度。一個總分把所有屬性壓在一起，看不出到底是哪個屬性沒做到——例如提示要求人聲，模型卻輸出純器樂，總分可能幾乎不變。

往概念層級走有兩個難題。一是評估空間怎麼定義：描述從曲風、樂器到調性、和弦、速度都有。二是成本：整體的人類評分已經需要上千位標註者，概念層級的標註還要再乘上每個提示詞的屬性數。

作者的解法是只做大型音訊語言模型（LALM）目前做得到的事：不讓它開放式推理，只讓它判斷單一概念是否被音訊支持，並把概念限制在清楚可判斷存在與否的類別型標籤。

Text-to-music (TTM) prompts are getting longer and more detailed, yet alignment is still evaluated mainly by global similarity between text and audio embeddings such as CLAP or MuQ-MuLan. A single score compresses all attributes together and hides which one was missed; if a prompt asks for vocals and the model outputs an instrumental, the score may barely move.

Going to the concept level raises two problems. One is defining the evaluation space, since descriptions range from genre and instrument to key, chords and tempo. The other is cost: holistic human ratings already need thousands of annotators, and concept-level annotation multiplies that by the number of attributes per prompt.

The authors' answer is to ask large audio language models (LALMs) only for what they can currently do: no open-ended reasoning, just a judgment of whether a single concept is supported by the audio, with concepts restricted to categorical tags whose presence or absence is well defined.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

被取代的對象是全域相似度：LAION-CLAP 與 MuQ-MuLan 的餘弦相似度是目前 TTM 論文最常見的對齊指標。直接的前作是 AQAScore（Kuan、Chang 與 Lee，2026）：它已經把文字–音訊對齊寫成「這段音訊符不符合整段描述？」的是非題，取「是」的機率。MQAScore 沿用同一個計分方式，差別只在把整段描述拆成單一概念逐一發問。

論文沒有提、但最像的其實是視覺領域的兩條線。VQAScore（Lin 等，2024）在文生圖評估裡用的就是「是」的機率；TIFA（Hu 等，2023）與 Davidsonian Scene Graph 則是先用 LLM 把提示拆成原子問題，再讓 VQA 模型逐題回答。MQAScore 可以看成 TIFA 式的拆解加上 VQAScore 式的計分，搬到音樂上。

音訊端同期也有 ELSA（事件層級語意對齊）、AnyAudio-Judge（動態評分規準）與 CMI-RewardBench 等細粒度評估工作，論文有引用但沒有實驗比較。分類體系則取自 CAL500 的前五個維度，並依 MTG-Jamendo 把情緒與主題合併。

What it replaces is global similarity: LAION-CLAP and MuQ-MuLan cosine similarity are the most common alignment metrics in TTM papers. The direct predecessor is AQAScore (Kuan, Chang and Lee, 2026), which already casts text–audio alignment as a yes/no question ('does this audio match the whole description?') and takes the probability of 'Yes'. MQAScore keeps the same scoring and differs only in splitting the description into single concepts asked one at a time.

What the paper does not mention, but is closest, are two lines from vision. VQAScore (Lin et al., 2024) uses the probability of 'Yes' for text-to-image evaluation; TIFA (Hu et al., 2023) and the Davidsonian Scene Graph first use an LLM to decompose a prompt into atomic questions and then have a VQA model answer each. MQAScore can be read as TIFA-style decomposition plus VQAScore-style scoring, transferred to music.

On the audio side there is concurrent fine-grained work such as ELSA (event-level semantic alignment), AnyAudio-Judge (dynamic rubrics) and CMI-RewardBench, which the paper cites but does not compare against experimentally. The taxonomy takes the first five CAL500 dimensions, merging emotion and theme following MTG-Jamendo.

方法Method

流程分三步。先用 LLM（Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507）從 MusicCaps 的描述裡抽出原子概念並歸到五個維度之一，去掉主觀修飾語（「groovy bass」只留「bass」），合併同義詞後人工檢查，形成各維度的詞彙表。

再來是計分：每個維度有一個問句模板，例如「Does this music belong to the genre {t}? Answer Yes or No.」，一次前向計算取「Yes」與「No」的下一個 token 機率，分數為 P(Yes) / (P(Yes) + P(No))。某維度的分數是該維度所有概念分數的平均；提示裡沒有該維度就標示為不可用，不給零分。

驗證需要負例。曲風與樂器有明確的有／無標註：GTZAN 的 1,000 段裡，每段的曲風對上其他九種，形成三元組；OpenMIC-2018 用相關度大於 0.5 為有、否則為無，配出 7,037 段上的 15,241 組三元組。情緒／主題、人聲、使用情境沒有負標註，概念也不互斥，所以讓 LLM 找詞彙表裡語意最遠的概念當負例 t⁻、最近的當保留語意的對照 t⁺，分別得到 3,967、737、112 組四元組。

作者提出 Sensitivity-Invariance Ratio：R_S/I 是換成 t⁻ 與換成 t⁺ 時，z 標準化分數平均絕對變化量的比值。遠大於 1 代表評估器在乎音訊內容，接近 1 代表它分不出矛盾和近義詞。另外還有 11 位受過音樂訓練的標註者的配對判斷：147 組「哪段音樂有這個概念」與 119 組「哪個概念符合這段音樂」，Krippendorff's α 為 0.71。

The pipeline has three steps. First, an LLM (Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507) extracts atomic concepts from MusicCaps captions and assigns each to one of the five dimensions, dropping subjective modifiers ('groovy bass' becomes 'bass'); after synonym merging and manual review, this yields a vocabulary per dimension.

Next is scoring: each dimension has a question template, e.g. 'Does this music belong to the genre {t}? Answer Yes or No.', and a single forward pass gives the next-token probabilities of 'Yes' and 'No', with score P(Yes) / (P(Yes) + P(No)). A dimension's score is the mean over its concepts; a dimension absent from the prompt is reported as unavailable rather than zero.

Verification needs negatives. Genre and instrument have explicit presence/absence labels: in GTZAN's 1,000 clips, each clip's genre is paired with the other nine to form triplets; OpenMIC-2018 treats relevance above 0.5 as present and otherwise absent, giving 15,241 triplets over 7,037 clips. Mood/theme, vocal and usage have no negative labels and concepts are not mutually exclusive, so an LLM picks the semantically most distant vocabulary concept as negative t⁻ and the closest as a meaning-preserving control t⁺, giving 3,967, 737 and 112 quadruplets respectively.

The authors propose the Sensitivity-Invariance Ratio: R_S/I is the ratio of mean absolute z-normalized score change when substituting t⁻ versus t⁺. Much greater than 1 means the evaluator follows the audio; near 1 means it cannot tell a contradiction from a near-synonym. There are also pairwise judgments from 11 musically trained annotators: 147 'which clip has this concept' pairs and 119 'which concept fits this clip' pairs, with Krippendorff's α of 0.71.

限制Limitations

第一，驗證的音訊全部是真實錄音（GTZAN、OpenMIC-2018、MusicCaps），沒有任何生成音樂。評估器要用在 TTM 輸出上，而生成音樂的失敗模式（模糊的樂器音色、混在一起的風格）正是最難判斷的地方；論文只在專案網頁放了 TTM 系統的逐維度結果，沒有對應的人類標註。

第二，資料洩漏的風險沒有討論。MusicCaps 與 GTZAN 都是常見的公開資料，很可能出現在 Qwen3-Omni 或 Music Flamingo 的訓練資料中；GTZAN 本身也有已知的重複與錯標問題（Sturm，2013），單一標籤的假設論文自己也列為限制。

第三，負例偏簡單。t⁻ 是「語意最遠」的概念，等於問模型平靜和狂暴分不分得出來，AUC 自然偏高；真正有用的是分辨相近但不同的概念。R_S/I 也有分母問題：論文自己指出，整段描述層級的 AQAScore 因為換一個近義詞幾乎不動，比值被灌高（Qwen3-Omni 在使用情境上是 36.9，逐概念只有 9.09），所以這個比值不能單獨解讀。

第四，專家集很小：266 組配對分到五個維度後，每格大約 40 到 60 組，表 2 裡 86.4 對 88.6 的差距可能只差一組。最後，只檢查提示裡有的概念有沒有出現，不懲罰模型額外加入提示沒要求的東西。

First, all verification audio is real recordings (GTZAN, OpenMIC-2018, MusicCaps); there is no generated music. The evaluator is meant for TTM outputs, and generated music's failure modes (blurry instrument timbres, blended styles) are exactly where judgment is hardest; the paper only posts per-dimension TTM results on its project page, without matching human labels.

Second, data leakage is not discussed. MusicCaps and GTZAN are widely used public datasets and quite likely appear in the training data of Qwen3-Omni or Music Flamingo; GTZAN also has known repetitions and mislabels (Sturm, 2013), and the paper itself lists the single-label assumption as a limitation.

Third, the negatives are easy. t⁻ is the 'most distant' concept, effectively asking whether the model can tell calm from aggressive, which naturally inflates AUC; what matters in practice is separating close but different concepts. R_S/I also has a denominator problem: the paper itself notes that caption-level AQAScore barely moves under a near-synonym, inflating the ratio (Qwen3-Omni scores 36.9 on usage at caption level versus 9.09 per concept), so the ratio cannot be read on its own.

Fourth, the expert set is small: 266 pairs split over five dimensions leaves roughly 40 to 60 per cell, so a gap like 86.4 vs 88.6 in Table 2 may be a single pair. Finally, it only checks whether requested concepts appear and does not penalize a model for adding things the prompt never asked for.

討論Discussion

最乾淨的對照是「同一種評估器、只改粒度」。以 Qwen3-Omni 為例，整段描述層級的 AQAScore 在情緒／主題、人聲、使用情境的 AUC 是 0.776、0.870、0.718，拆成逐概念的 MQAScore 後升到 0.943、0.966、0.842。CLAP 也一樣受益：0.535、0.629、0.559 升到 0.669、0.759、0.711。拆解本身對兩種典範都有用。

在逐概念層級，LALM 和相似度方法的差距集中在抽象維度。曲風與樂器上兩者接近：曲風 AUC Qwen3-Omni 0.983、MuQ-MuLan 0.959，樂器 0.915 對 0.860。情緒／主題則拉開到 0.943 對 0.663，而 MuQ-MuLan 在人聲上的 R_S/I 只有 0.48，換成近義詞時的反應反而比換成矛盾概念大，說明它追蹤的是措辭而不是音訊。

專家一致率也是同一個圖像：人與人的一致率在五個維度上介於 82.3% 到 95.2%；Qwen3-Omni 在情緒／主題是 86.4%、使用情境 85.4%，都略高於人與人的 84.6% 與 82.5%，MuQ-MuLan 在情緒／主題只有 43.2%，低於隨機。但 LALM 之間的排名並不穩定：Music Flamingo 在樂器上最高（89.8%），在曲風卻只有 75.0%。

這篇值得留意的地方在於它把一個視覺領域已成熟的做法很誠實地搬到音樂，並提供能拿來比較評估器的工具（AUC、勝率、R_S/I）。它的實際價值要等兩件事驗證：在生成音樂上跟人類的一致率，以及能否當成 TTM 的獎勵模型而不被鑽漏洞——作者把後者列為未來工作。另外，評估器本身的「是」偏誤需要校準，不同 LALM 的分數尺度也不能直接比較。

The cleanest comparison holds the evaluator fixed and changes only granularity. For Qwen3-Omni, caption-level AQAScore has AUC 0.776, 0.870 and 0.718 on mood/theme, vocal and usage, rising to 0.943, 0.966 and 0.842 with concept-level MQAScore. CLAP benefits likewise: 0.535, 0.629 and 0.559 rise to 0.669, 0.759 and 0.711. Decomposition by itself helps both paradigms.

At the concept level, the gap between LALMs and similarity methods concentrates in abstract dimensions. On genre and instrument they are close: genre AUC 0.983 for Qwen3-Omni vs 0.959 for MuQ-MuLan, instrument 0.915 vs 0.860. Mood/theme widens to 0.943 vs 0.663, and MuQ-MuLan's vocal R_S/I is only 0.48, reacting more to near-synonyms than to contradictions, which shows it tracks wording rather than audio.

Expert agreement tells the same story: human–human agreement ranges from 82.3% to 95.2% across the five dimensions; Qwen3-Omni reaches 86.4% on mood/theme and 85.4% on usage, slightly above the human–human 84.6% and 82.5%, while MuQ-MuLan gets only 43.2% on mood/theme, below chance. But rankings among LALMs are unstable: Music Flamingo is best on instrument (89.8%) yet only 75.0% on genre.

What makes the paper worth noting is that it transfers a practice already mature in vision to music honestly and provides tools to compare evaluators (AUC, win rate, R_S/I). Its practical value awaits two checks: agreement with humans on generated music, and whether it can serve as a TTM reward model without being gamed, which the authors list as future work. The evaluator's own 'Yes' bias also needs calibration, and score scales across LALMs are not directly comparable.
