AutoSynth: Learning to Generate Editable Synthesizer Programs from Audio and Text

AutoSynth:把 MIDI、合成器參數與可變長度調變路由串成一條序列自迴歸生成,SFT 後用原生外掛渲染回饋做 GRPO,從音訊或文字產出可編輯的 Helm 音色程式

Tristan Wu, Daniel Chin, Liwei Lin, Junan Zhang, Gus Xia
arXiv:2610.10774 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：AutoSynth 把演奏事件、133 個固定參數與可變數量的調變路由排成同一條序列,讓一個音訊條件的自迴歸解碼器產出可直接在 Helm 合成器裡編輯的完整程式;文字輸入則先經 Stable Audio 3 生成參考音訊再反推。Gemini–Woosh 反推的音訊 CLAP 距離 0.37,DDSynth-RL 為 0.49;GRPO 讓 MSS 從 19.41 降到 6.94。但三個基線各用不同的合成器,RMS 包絡相似度反而輸 DDSynth-RL(0.82 對 0.87),主觀評測只有十位聽者。Why this one: AutoSynth lays out performance events, 133 fixed parameters and a variable number of modulation routes as one sequence, so a single audio-conditioned autoregressive decoder emits a complete program that can be edited directly in the Helm synthesizer; text input first goes through Stable Audio 3 to make reference audio, which is then inverted. Inversion on Gemini–Woosh reaches an audio CLAP distance of 0.37 against DDSynth-RL's 0.49, and GRPO cuts MSS from 19.41 to 6.94. But each baseline runs on a different synthesizer, RMS-envelope similarity actually trails DDSynth-RL (0.82 vs 0.87), and the listening test has only ten raters.

動機Motivation

端到端的音訊生成有兩個老問題。一是音質受限於壓縮表徵與波形重建,高壓縮率與細節保真之間始終要取捨;二是輸出是一段波形,想改一個音、一個濾波器截止頻率或一條 LFO 調變,遠不如在 DAW 裡直接動參數方便。圖學界早已在做「生成可編輯的 3D 資產」而不只是渲染結果,音訊這邊同時生成演奏、音色參數與調變結構的研究相對少。

作者認為缺口在表示法:MIDI 或 ABC 只描述音高與時值,決定聲音的大量資訊其實在合成器參數與調變關係裡。這三者合起來,對合成器能表達的聲音範圍而言,是一份精簡、可執行的聲音描述。困難在於參數空間不利於訓練:參數互相依賴也依賴 MIDI,調變路由數量可變,不同程式可能發出相似的聲音,原生外掛無法反向傳播,配對資料又少。

End-to-end audio generation has two long-standing problems. Audio quality is bounded by compressed representations and waveform reconstruction, with a persistent trade-off between compression ratio and detail fidelity; and the output is a waveform, so changing one note, a filter cutoff or an LFO modulation is far less convenient than adjusting parameters in a DAW. Graphics already generates editable 3D assets rather than only rendered results; in audio, jointly generating performance, timbral parameters and modulation structure is comparatively unexplored.

The authors locate the gap in representation: MIDI or ABC describe only pitch and timing, while much of what determines the sound lives in synthesizer parameters and modulation relationships. Together, the three form a compact, executable description of sound within a synthesizer's expressive range. The difficulty is that parameter spaces resist training: parameters depend on one another and on MIDI, the number of modulation routes varies, different programs can sound alike, native plugins cannot backpropagate, and paired data are scarce.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

合成器反推(synthesizer inversion)這條線有幾個直接可比的前作。DDSP(Engel 等 2020)與後續的可微分合成路線,把合成過程寫成可微分運算直接優化音訊損失,但代價是只能用自己重寫的簡化合成器,不能用現成外掛。InverSynth(Barkan 等 2019,本文未引)與 FlowSynth(Esling 等 2019)用神經網路從頻譜直接預測固定長度的參數向量。Synth Permutations(Hayes 等 2025)處理「多組參數發出同一聲音」的對稱性,用近似置換等變的流匹配建模多重解,並同時預測 MIDI,在 Surge XT 上實作。

跟本文最接近的是 DDSynth-RL(Wu 等 2026):同樣用 GRPO 式的後訓練,以原生合成器渲染出來的音訊算獎勵,不經合成器反向傳播。差別在於 DDSynth-RL 是遮罩離散擴散、在 Dexed(DX7 風格 FM)上預測 MIDI 與固定參數,沒有可變長度的調變路由;AutoSynth 改成自迴歸,把 MIDI、固定參數和可變長度的調變路由放進同一條序列,目標合成器是 Helm。文字驅動這一側,CTAG(Cherep 等 2024)用 CLAP 文字–音訊相似度引導演化搜尋 78 個參數,不需訓練配對;AutoSynth 則改成「文字 → Stable Audio 3 生成音訊 → 反推」的串接。

也該點明:論文的 GRPO 每組候選只用一次、沒有 PPO 剪裁也沒有參考策略 KL 正則化,實質上就是以組內標準化獎勵當基線的 REINFORCE,跟 RLOO 這類做法同一家族,「GRPO」這個名字比它實際做的事多帶了一些包袱。

Synthesizer inversion has several directly comparable predecessors. DDSP (Engel et al. 2020) and later differentiable-synthesis work write synthesis as differentiable operations and optimize audio losses directly, at the cost of using simplified, reimplemented synthesizers instead of off-the-shelf plugins. InverSynth (Barkan et al. 2019, not cited here) and FlowSynth (Esling et al. 2019) predict a fixed-length parameter vector from spectra with neural networks. Synth Permutations (Hayes et al. 2025) handles the symmetry of 'many parameter sets, one sound' with approximately permutation-equivariant flow matching that models multiple solutions while also predicting MIDI, implemented on Surge XT.

The closest work is DDSynth-RL (Wu et al. 2026): it also uses GRPO-style post-training with rewards computed on native-synthesizer renders, without backpropagating through the synthesizer. The differences are that DDSynth-RL uses masked discrete diffusion and predicts MIDI and fixed parameters on Dexed (a DX7-style FM synth), with no variable-length modulation routes, while AutoSynth is autoregressive, puts MIDI, fixed parameters and variable-length modulation routes in one sequence, and targets Helm. On the text side, CTAG (Cherep et al. 2024) uses CLAP text–audio similarity to guide evolutionary search over 78 parameters with no paired training; AutoSynth instead cascades text → Stable Audio 3 audio → inversion.

Also worth stating: the paper's GRPO uses each candidate group once, with no PPO clipping and no reference-policy KL regularization. In substance it is REINFORCE with a group-standardized reward baseline, in the same family as RLOO, and the 'GRPO' label carries more baggage than what is actually done.

方法Method

表示法。 一份程式依序是:MIDI 演奏事件(音高、力度、起始、時值,外加調變輪與觸後等控制器事件)、133 個固定參數(Helm 原生 139 個,其中六個延遲相關參數固定關閉不預測)、以及調變路由(來源、目的、量的三元組)。連續值量化成 128 格,離散參數保留原本的類別數。路由依固定的來源/目的順序正規化,以結束記號決定數量,避免同一組路由的不同排列變成不同的監督目標。固定參數內部的順序也經過設計:結構性選擇先於依賴它的數值(例如振盪器波形先於調音與音量,效果開關先於效果參數),總音量放最後。

模型。 左右聲道各算 128 帶 log-mel,經卷積前端與 12 層 Transformer 編碼器得到條件序列(編碼器約 2,800 萬參數);解碼器 12 層,每層對前文做因果自注意力、對音訊條件做交叉注意力。所有位置共用一個 128 類的數值輸出層,但當前文法位置會限制合法值。文字輸入時,凍結的 Stable Audio 3 Medium 先生成參考音訊,文字本身不進解碼器。

監督學習。 從 Helm 的 274 個出廠預設加一個初始狀態出發,依手寫規則擾動參數、路由與 MIDI,渲染出兩百萬筆五秒立體聲的音訊–程式配對;90% 來自預設擾動、10% 來自受限隨機取樣。演奏方式依音色類型調整機率,例如氛圍音色較常彈和弦、貝斯較常彈低音域單音。損失先在每個語意欄位內平均交叉熵,再對批次中出現的欄位平均,避免高頻欄位主導;有序數值欄位用高斯平滑標籤。

渲染回饋的強化學習。 後訓練混合 Stable Audio 3 生成的音訊與領域內 Helm 音訊。每個目標產生 8 個候選程式,用原生外掛渲染後計算八項加權獎勵:CLAP 距離(權重 8)、Basic Pitch 隱藏特徵距離(12)、AudioBox CE 與 CU(各 1)、wMFCC(0.07)、頻譜最佳傳輸 SOT(3.58)、多尺度頻譜 MSS(0.11)、RMS 包絡相似度(0.24)。文字只參與 CLAP 獎勵。探索用 ε-greedy 的 top-k 取樣(k=16、機率門檻 0.1),只在模型認為合理的候選內隨機。後訓練用的 10 萬條文字描述由 GPT-6 依模板生成,每條用五個種子產生 50 萬段音訊。

結果(反推)。 在跨生成器的 Gemini–Woosh 測試集(128 段)上,AutoSynth 的音訊 CLAP 距離 0.37,DDSynth-RL 0.49、Synth Permutations 0.54;未用於獎勵的 LanguageBind 距離 0.397 對 0.482 與 0.584;wMFCC 10.22 對 10.34 與 22.75;MSS 6.94 對 7.04 與 9.99;但 RMS 包絡相似度 0.82,輸給 DDSynth-RL 的 0.87。FSD50K(256 段真實錄音)上 CLAP 差距縮小(0.67 對 0.70 與 0.69),wMFCC 拉開到 14.95 對 17.93 與 23.34。

結果(文字驅動)。 Gemini 描述上,AutoSynth 的文字 CLAP 距離 0.550,勝過另兩個反推串接(0.607、0.642),但輸給直接以 CLAP 搜尋的 CTAG(0.519);LanguageBind 0.638 對 CTAG 0.645 幾乎打平,而直接生成波形的 Stable Audio 3 是 0.614。AudioBox PQ 7.433 高於 Stable Audio 3 的 7.373。FSD50K 上 FAD 7.00,遠高於 Stable Audio 3 的 2.73。

消融。 SFT 到 GRPO 在 Gemini–Woosh 反推上:CLAP 0.47→0.37、SOT 0.33→0.15、MSS 19.41→6.94、RMS 0.65→0.82;領域內 Helm 測試也全面改善(MSS 7.36→3.77),表示領域外的提升沒有犧牲領域內重建。序列順序消融只比較前 10 萬步的驗證損失,現行順序大致較低。凍結的 Stable Audio 3 SAME 編碼器(5,400 萬與 4.26 億參數)都不如聯合訓練的 2,800 萬參數編碼器,且後訓練曲線不穩、偶有崩潰。

Representation. A program is, in order: MIDI performance events (pitch, velocity, onset, duration, plus controller events such as mod wheel and aftertouch), 133 fixed parameters (Helm has 139 natively; six delay-related ones are fixed off and not predicted), and modulation routes (source, destination, amount triplets). Continuous values are quantized into 128 bins; discrete parameters keep their native categories. Routes are canonicalized by a fixed source/destination order with an end marker setting their number, so permutations of the same route set do not become distinct targets. The fixed-parameter order is designed too: structural choices precede the values that depend on them (oscillator waveform before tuning and volume, effect switches before effect controls), with output volume last.

Model. Each stereo channel gets a 128-band log-mel, passed through a convolutional front end and a 12-layer Transformer encoder (about 28M parameters) to form the conditioning sequence; the decoder has 12 layers with causal self-attention over the prefix and cross-attention to the audio. All positions share one 128-way value output layer, but the current grammatical position restricts legal values. For text input, a frozen Stable Audio 3 Medium first generates reference audio; the text itself never enters the decoder.

Supervised learning. Starting from Helm's 274 factory presets plus one initial state, hand-written rules perturb parameters, routes and MIDI, and the native plugin renders two million five-second stereo audio–program pairs; 90% come from preset perturbation and 10% from constrained random sampling. Performance probabilities depend on sound type, e.g. ambient presets more often play chords and bass presets low single notes. The loss averages cross-entropy within each semantic field, then across fields present in the batch, so frequent fields do not dominate; ordered numeric fields use Gaussian-smoothed labels.

RL from rendering feedback. Post-training mixes Stable Audio 3 audio with in-domain Helm audio. Each target gets 8 candidate programs, rendered by the native plugin and scored by an eight-term weighted reward: CLAP distance (weight 8), Basic Pitch hidden-feature distance (12), AudioBox CE and CU (1 each), wMFCC (0.07), spectral optimal transport SOT (3.58), multi-scale spectral MSS (0.11) and RMS-envelope similarity (0.24). Text enters only the CLAP reward. Exploration uses ε-greedy top-k sampling (k=16, probability threshold 0.1), randomizing only among candidates the model deems plausible. The 100,000 post-training descriptions are template-generated with GPT-6, each rendered with five seeds into 500,000 clips.

Results (inversion). On the cross-generator Gemini–Woosh set (128 clips), AutoSynth's audio CLAP distance is 0.37 versus 0.49 for DDSynth-RL and 0.54 for Synth Permutations; LanguageBind distance, not used as a reward, is 0.397 versus 0.482 and 0.584; wMFCC 10.22 versus 10.34 and 22.75; MSS 6.94 versus 7.04 and 9.99; but RMS-envelope similarity is 0.82, behind DDSynth-RL's 0.87. On FSD50K (256 real recordings) the CLAP gap narrows (0.67 vs 0.70 and 0.69), while wMFCC widens to 14.95 versus 17.93 and 23.34.

Results (text-driven). On Gemini descriptions, AutoSynth's text CLAP distance of 0.550 beats the other two inversion cascades (0.607, 0.642) but loses to CTAG (0.519), which searches directly on CLAP; LanguageBind is 0.638 versus CTAG's 0.645, nearly a tie, while Stable Audio 3, generating waveforms directly, scores 0.614. AudioBox PQ is 7.433, above Stable Audio 3's 7.373. On FSD50K, FAD is 7.00, far above Stable Audio 3's 2.73.

Ablations. SFT to GRPO on Gemini–Woosh inversion: CLAP 0.47→0.37, SOT 0.33→0.15, MSS 19.41→6.94, RMS 0.65→0.82; the in-domain Helm test also improves throughout (MSS 7.36→3.77), so out-of-domain gains did not cost in-domain reconstruction. The ordering ablation compares only validation loss over the first 100,000 steps, where the current order is generally lower. Frozen Stable Audio 3 SAME encoders (54M and 426M parameters) both underperform the jointly trained 28M encoder, with unstable post-training curves and occasional reward collapse.

限制Limitations

基線不在同一把尺上。 Synth Permutations 跑在 Surge XT(且因原作沒釋出權重,是作者自己重訓的),DDSynth-RL 跑在 Dexed,CTAG 跑在 SynthAX,AutoSynth 跑在 Helm。論文在討論節自己承認,不同合成器的表達能力差異讓「方法的貢獻」與「聲音引擎的貢獻」難以分開。所以 CLAP 0.37 對 0.49 這種差距,有多少是自迴歸加路由建模帶來的,有多少是 Helm 本身更適合這些測試聲音,目前無法判斷。最乾淨的對照——在 Helm 上只拿掉路由、或在 Dexed 上跑 AutoSynth——沒有做。

獎勵與評估指標大量重疊。 反推表的六個指標中,音訊 CLAP、wMFCC、SOT、MSS、RMS 五個都直接出現在獎勵裡,只有 LanguageBind 是獨立的。作者有意識地加了 LanguageBind 與 PQ 當未優化的檢查,這點值得肯定;但其他基線並未針對這些指標做後訓練,比較本身就偏向 AutoSynth。而且 RMS 包絡明明在獎勵裡,AutoSynth 仍輸給 DDSynth-RL(0.82 對 0.87)。

主觀評測規模小。 十位聽者、每人二十題、每個方法×資料集×維度只有 50 個評分,總共 1,800 分,報告 95% 自助法信賴區間,但沒有統計檢定。文字驅動在 FSD50K 上的對齊分數跟 CTAG 打平。

範圍受限。 訓練資料只有單音與和弦、沒有長旋律;測試只有五秒短音。FSD50K 的文字驅動 FAD 7.00 對 Stable Audio 3 的 2.73、KL 5.27 對 CTAG 的 3.94,顯示環境聲與語音超出合成器表達範圍時,這條路的天花板很低。附錄 B 的「創意超解析」也要小心解讀:加噪音輸入經 AutoSynth 重合成後 PQ 從 5.07 升到 7.40,但跟原始乾淨音訊的 CLAP 距離 0.451,比噪音輸入本身的 0.306 還遠——它是換了一個好聽的聲音,不是修復。

Baselines are not on the same ruler. Synth Permutations runs on Surge XT (and was retrained by the authors because no weights were released), DDSynth-RL on Dexed, CTAG on SynthAX, AutoSynth on Helm. The discussion itself admits that differing expressive capacity makes it hard to separate the method's contribution from the sound engine's. So for a gap such as CLAP 0.37 versus 0.49, how much comes from autoregressive route modelling and how much from Helm simply suiting these test sounds better cannot currently be told. The cleanest controls, removing routes on Helm or running AutoSynth on Dexed, are not done.

Rewards and evaluation metrics overlap heavily. Of the six inversion metrics, five (audio CLAP, wMFCC, SOT, MSS, RMS) appear directly in the reward; only LanguageBind is independent. The authors deliberately added LanguageBind and PQ as unoptimized checks, which is to their credit, but the other baselines were not post-trained on these metrics, so the comparison leans toward AutoSynth. And even with RMS envelope in the reward, AutoSynth still trails DDSynth-RL on it (0.82 vs 0.87).

The listening test is small. Ten raters, twenty questions each, 50 ratings per method × dataset × dimension, 1,800 scores in all, reported with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals but no statistical test. Text-driven alignment on FSD50K ties with CTAG.

Scope is narrow. Training data contain single notes and chords, no long melodies; tests use five-second sounds. Text-driven FSD50K results, FAD 7.00 against Stable Audio 3's 2.73 and KL 5.27 against CTAG's 3.94, show a low ceiling when environmental sounds and speech fall outside the synthesizer's range. Appendix B's 'creative super-resolution' also needs care: resynthesizing noisy input lifts PQ from 5.07 to 7.40, but its CLAP distance to the clean original is 0.451, farther than the noisy input itself at 0.306. It swaps in a nicer-sounding sound; it does not restore.

討論Discussion

這篇的核心價值在表示法,而不是某個指標贏了多少。把「演奏 → 音色 → 調變」排成一條有文法約束的序列,等於把合成器程式當成一種程式語言來生成,這跟 LLM 寫程式的思路一致,也讓「生成後再用 LLM 按指令編輯」成為可能(作者展示了 GPT-6 依使用者指令改程式再重新渲染,但只有影片示範,沒有量化)。可變長度路由與固定順序設計是先前 DDSynth-RL 與 Synth Permutations 都處理不了的部分,這點是實質進展。

「文字 → 波形生成器 → 反推」的串接也值得注意。附錄 A 顯示直接從 Stable Audio 3 的潛在空間解碼反而更差、且訓練不穩,作者的解讀是任務專用的編碼器比通用壓縮特徵更適合抽出參數資訊。這暗示短期內「可編輯音訊生成」的實際路線可能就是讓強大的波形模型當草稿,再投影到可編輯的參數空間,而不是從頭訓練文字到參數的模型——後者缺的正是配對資料。

最該追的問題是同一合成器上的公平比較,以及「獎勵設計等於把人的音色審美寫死」這件事。作者自己在討論節也說,八項獎勵的權重就是把聲音設計偏好轉成訓練目標,而合法可用的真實創作資料很少。在有共同基準之前,這篇最可信的結論是:在 Helm 上,SFT 加渲染回饋後訓練能穩定產出完整可編輯程式,而且後訓練對領域內與領域外都有幫助(MSS 分別從 7.36 降到 3.77、19.41 降到 6.94)。

The core value is the representation, not by how much a metric was won. Laying out 'performance → timbre → modulation' as one grammar-constrained sequence treats a synthesizer program as a programming language to be generated, in line with how LLMs write code, and it makes 'generate, then let an LLM edit by instruction' possible (the authors show GPT-6 editing a program per user instruction and re-rendering, but only as a demo video, without quantification). Variable-length routes and the designed ordering are exactly what DDSynth-RL and Synth Permutations could not handle, and that is real progress.

The 'text → waveform generator → inversion' cascade also deserves note. Appendix A shows that decoding directly from Stable Audio 3's latent space is worse and trains unstably; the authors read this as a task-specific encoder extracting parameter information better than general-purpose compression features. It suggests that, in the near term, the practical route to editable audio generation may be to let a strong waveform model draft and then project into an editable parameter space, rather than training text-to-parameter models from scratch, which lack precisely the paired data.

The questions most worth pursuing are a fair comparison on one synthesizer, and the fact that reward design hard-codes human timbral taste. The authors say as much: the eight reward weights translate sound-design preferences into a training objective, and legally usable real creative data are scarce. Until a shared benchmark exists, the most credible conclusion is that on Helm, SFT plus rendering-feedback post-training reliably yields complete editable programs, and post-training helps both in-domain and out-of-domain (MSS from 7.36 to 3.77 and from 19.41 to 6.94 respectively).

為什麼挑這篇：這篇把十五年來散在各篇論文裡的感知量測(SPCS 音高類別相似度、四種協和度、平衡/均勻度等節奏結構特徵)整理成 MATLAB 與 Python 雙語、MIT 授權的工具箱,並首次發表期望張量餘弦相似度的閉式內積。實例分析裡,36 個和弦探針的「脈絡契合度」與「本身協和度」兩組量測之間的 Spearman 相關都不超過 0.37。但論文說新版「快很多」卻沒有給任何計時數字,實例分析也沒有聽者資料。Why this one: This paper packages fifteen years of perception measures scattered across papers (SPCS pitch-class similarity, four consonance models, balance/evenness and other rhythm-structure features) into an MIT-licensed toolbox in MATLAB and Python, and publishes for the first time the closed-form inner product behind expectation-tensor cosine similarity. 動機Motivation

音樂認知研究常需要回答三類問題:兩組音高或節奏有多像、一個和弦有多協和、一個音階或節奏型的結構有什麼特性。作者 Milne 過去十五年圍繞「期望張量」(expectation tensor)框架做了一整套量測,被拿去預測探針音評分、微分音音階的調性穩定度、和弦情緒、節奏可辨識度與敲擊準確度,但這些程式碼從沒在同儕審查的期刊上正式記錄過,只有一包缺文件的 MATLAB 腳本。

這篇因此有兩個目的:一是把工具本身寫成一篇可引用、有一致 API 的文件(2.0 版,MATLAB 與 Python 雙語,輸出在浮點精度內一致);二是補上一個數學結果——兩個期望張量密度內積的閉式解,讓所有相似度都能精確計算,不必再把音高四捨五入到網格上。

Music cognition research keeps asking three kinds of question: how similar two pitch or rhythm collections are, how consonant a chord is, and what structural properties a scale or rhythm has. Over fifteen years the author has built a suite of measures around the 'expectation tensor' framework, used to predict probe-tone ratings, tonal stability in microtonal scales, chord affect, rhythm recognizability and tapping accuracy. Yet the code was never formally documented in a peer-reviewed venue; it was a bundle of under-documented MATLAB scripts.

The paper therefore has two aims. One is to document the tool itself as a citable artifact with a consistent API (version 2.0, MATLAB and Python, outputs identical to floating-point precision). The other is a mathematical result: a closed form for the inner product of two expectation-tensor densities, so every similarity is computed exactly instead of rounding pitches onto a grid.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文自己點名的鄰近工具有三個:MIRToolbox(Lartillot 等 2008)做音訊特徵擷取,涵蓋音色、調性與節奏;MIDI Toolbox(Eerola 與 Toiviainen 2004)處理符號 MIDI,做找調、找拍與旋律相似度;R 套件 incon 與 hrep(Harrison 與 Pearce 2020)則獨立重新實作了本工具箱的部分協和度量測,連 hEntropy 這個統計量都是他們提出的。本文的差別在於三族量測共用同一個「加權多重集」輸入,而且音高、時間都以連續實數進入,不需預先決定調音系統或節拍網格;相對地,IDyOM(Pearce 2018)這類語料庫式期待模型必須先把音高離散成符號。

另一個對照是聲部進行距離(Tymoczko 一系):它需要在兩個和弦的音之間建立一對一配對,遇到三音和弦對七音音階就得先湊齊數量;期望張量把兩邊各自先抹成密度,不需配對。節奏結構那族則直接延用既有方法:圓周自相關相位矩陣改自 Eck(2006)的 APM,DFT 量測則刻意跟 Quinn、Amiot 用 0/1 指示向量做 DFT 的數學音樂理論做法區隔,改用點位置直接計算以容納非整數位置與權重。

論文標榜「首次發表」的閉式內積,最該講清楚的是它跟什麼最像:兩個高斯混合的 L2 內積本來就有閉式解(高斯乘積仍是高斯),而以高斯核比較兩組點集合的餘弦相似度,在機器學習裡就是正規化的集合核(set kernel)或高斯核下的核均值嵌入內積。本文的貢獻在於把這個標準恆等式推到「相對」(去掉平移)與「週期」(八度或節奏循環要繞回)兩種二次形式,並用「一邊取組合、一邊取排列」省下 r! 倍的計算量,而不是發明了新的相似度概念。

The paper itself names three neighbouring tools. MIRToolbox (Lartillot et al. 2008) extracts audio features covering timbre, tonality and rhythm; the MIDI Toolbox (Eerola and Toiviainen 2004) analyses symbolic MIDI with key-finding, meter-finding and melodic similarity; and the R packages incon and hrep (Harrison and Pearce 2020) independently reimplement some of this toolbox's consonance measures, and they introduced the hEntropy statistic itself. The difference here is that all three families share one 'weighted multiset' input, with pitches and times entering as continuous reals, so no tuning system or metrical grid has to be fixed first. Corpus-based expectation models such as IDyOM (Pearce 2018), by contrast, need pitches discretized into symbols.

Another contrast is voice-leading distance (the Tymoczko line of work), which requires a one-to-one matching between the notes of two chords, so a triad compared with a seven-note scale must first be padded to equal size; expectation tensors smear each side into a density and need no matching. The rhythm-structure family reuses existing methods: the circular autocorrelation phase matrix adapts Eck's (2006) APM, and the DFT measures are deliberately distinguished from the mathematical-music-theory practice of Quinn and Amiot, who take the DFT of 0/1 indicator vectors, by computing from point positions directly so non-integer positions and weights are admitted.

For the closed-form inner product billed as published 'for the first time', the key question is what it most resembles. The L2 inner product of two Gaussian mixtures has always had a closed form (a product of Gaussians is Gaussian), and cosine similarity between two point sets under a Gaussian kernel is, in machine learning terms, a normalized set kernel, or the kernel-mean-embedding inner product under a Gaussian kernel. The contribution is extending that standard identity to the 'relative' (translation-removed) and 'periodic' (wrapping at the octave or rhythmic cycle) quadratic forms, and saving a factor of r! by taking combinations on one side and permutations on the other, not inventing a new notion of similarity.

方法Method

核心物件是期望張量:每個音(或起音時間點)換成一個高斯分布,σ 代表感知不確定度(音高約 6–12 音分);把所有有序 r 元組加起來,就得到一個密度,值代表「聽者在此處感知到幾個事件」的期望值。兩個旗標決定模式:週期與否(音高類別或節奏循環要繞回),絕對與否(相對模式投影掉全體平移的方向,只留音程)。r=1 是個別音高,r=2 相對模式是音程內容,r=3 相對模式是音程對。

相似度用兩個密度的餘弦相似度。論文舉例:把大三和弦其中一音偏 10 音分,純計數得 2/3,σ=10 音分平滑後是 0.93。加上 12–24 個泛音的「頻譜充實」並取八度週期,就是最常用的 SPCS;拿掉泛音是 PCS,拿掉週期是 SPS,兩者都拿掉是 PS。舊版只有餘弦相似度用閉式,其他都在網格上算;絕對三元張量在一個八度內約 1200³≈1.7×10⁹ 格,只實作到 r≤4。新版除了熵(高斯混合的微分熵沒有閉式)以外全部改為閉式,成本隨元素數而非值域大小成長。

協和度有五種:頻譜熵(合成頻譜越集中越協和)、模板諧和度 hMax 與 hEntropy(拿單一泛音列沿音高線滑動做互相關)、張量諧和度(和弦的有序音程向量在單一泛音列中出現的機率密度)、以及 Sethares(1993)參數化的 Plomp–Levelt 粗糙度,另加可調的 p-norm 聚合。作者用 162 個和弦(六種類型 × 三轉位 × 三間距 × 三音域)拆解各量測對什麼敏感:頻譜熵 0.70 的秩變異來自間距;hMax 0.67 來自和弦類型;粗糙度是唯一受音域影響的(0.33),間距占 0.48;張量諧和度有 0.54 不能被任何單一屬性解釋。把泛音數從 16 改成 24,hEntropy 的類型/間距占比從 0.52/0.34 翻轉成 0.31/0.57。

結構特徵針對圓上的點集:平衡度(DFT 第 0 係數,增三和弦 1.000、大三和弦 0.827、{0,1,2} 0.089)、均勻度(第 1 係數,全音音階 1.000、自然音階 0.989)、一致性、相同度、n 元組熵、邊緣偵測、投影重心、平均偏移、圓周 APM、馬可夫預測。其中相同度、n 元組熵、APM 與馬可夫預測仍需要整數位置。

實例分析:脈絡是 C 大調 I–IV–V7 四部和聲(12 音多重集),探針是四個級數(I、vi、♯IV、♭II)× 三種性質 × 三種轉位共 36 個四聲部和弦。脈絡契合度四個量測之間的 Spearman ρ 為 0.52–0.98,協和度五個之間為 0.19–0.84,跨兩族都不超過 0.37。例如主音與 ♭II 上的小三和弦(皆為第二轉位)協和度幾乎一樣(hMax 0.69 對 0.68),SPCS 卻差近四倍(0.84 對 0.22)。

The core object is the expectation tensor. Each pitch (or onset) is replaced by a Gaussian whose σ models perceptual uncertainty (about 6–12 cents for pitch); summing over all ordered r-tuples gives a density whose value is the expected number of events a listener perceives there. Two flags set the mode: periodic or not (pitch class or rhythmic cycle wraps around), and absolute or relative (the relative mode projects out the common-shift direction, keeping only intervals). r=1 is individual pitches, relative r=2 is interval content, relative r=3 is pairs of intervals.

Similarity is the cosine similarity of two densities. The paper's example: moving one note of a major triad by 10 cents gives a count-based 2/3 but a smoothed 0.93 at σ=10 cents. Adding 12–24 harmonics ('spectral enrichment') with octave periodicity gives the commonly used SPCS; dropping the harmonics gives PCS, dropping periodicity gives SPS, dropping both gives PS. Version 1 used the closed form only for cosine similarity and a grid for everything else; an absolute triad tensor over one octave has about 1200³≈1.7×10⁹ cells, and only r≤4 was implemented. Version 2 is closed-form throughout except entropy (the differential entropy of a Gaussian mixture has no closed form), with cost scaling in the number of elements, not the size of the range.

There are five consonance measures: spectral entropy (a more concentrated composite spectrum is more consonant), template harmonicity hMax and hEntropy (cross-correlating one harmonic series slid along the pitch line), tensor harmonicity (the density of the chord's ordered interval vector within a single harmonic series), and Plomp–Levelt roughness in Sethares's (1993) parameterization with a configurable p-norm aggregation. Over 162 chords (six types × three inversions × three spacings × three registers) the author decomposes what each responds to: 0.70 of spectral entropy's rank variance comes from spacing; 0.67 of hMax's from chord type; roughness alone responds to register (0.33), with spacing at 0.48; tensor harmonicity leaves 0.54 unexplained by any single property. Moving from 16 to 24 harmonics flips hEntropy's type/spacing shares from 0.52/0.34 to 0.31/0.57.

The structural features act on points on a circle: balance (zeroth DFT coefficient; augmented triad 1.000, major triad 0.827, {0,1,2} 0.089), evenness (first coefficient; whole-tone scale 1.000, diatonic 0.989), coherence, sameness, n-tuple entropy, edge detection, projected centroid, mean offset, circular APM and Markov prediction. Sameness, n-tuple entropy, the APM and Markov prediction still require integer positions.

Worked example: the context is a four-part I–IV–V7 in C major (a 12-note multiset); the probes are four degrees (I, vi, ♯IV, ♭II) × three qualities × three inversions, 36 four-voice chords. Spearman ρ among the four fit measures is 0.52–0.98, among the five consonance measures 0.19–0.84, and no cross-family value exceeds 0.37. For instance, the tonic and ♭II minor triads in second inversion are nearly equal in consonance (hMax 0.69 vs 0.68) yet differ nearly fourfold in SPCS (0.84 vs 0.22).

限制Limitations

最明顯的缺口是效能宣稱沒有數字。摘要與引言都說新版「快很多」、讓高階張量變得可行,全文卻沒有任何計時比較、沒有 r 與元素數對運算時間的曲線,只有網格大小 1.7×10⁹ 這個推論。對一篇以「工具」為主要貢獻的論文,這是讀者最想看、也最容易補上的一張表。

第二,實例分析只算了量測彼此之間的相關,沒有任何聽者資料。「兩族量測幾乎獨立,所以研究需要兩族一起用」這個結論,在這篇裡只證明了預測變數不共線,沒有證明兩者都對行為有解釋力;後者要靠引用的 Hearne 等(2026)。而且 36 個探針、單一脈絡、σ 與泛音數都固定,相關係數的穩定性沒有檢驗——方法節自己就示範了泛音數從 16 換到 24 會讓 hEntropy 的敏感度排序翻轉。

第三,第 6 節的實證回顧幾乎全是作者自己或合作者的研究,屬於自我整理,並未系統比較其他模型。其中也有結果不利的:Smit 等(2022)的跨文化研究裡,平均音高對快樂感的普遍效應證據「可忽略」。作者自列的五點限制也都成立:非週期模式在寬音域用對數頻率並非感知等距;張量諧和度與 Erlich 調和熵的關係未探討;高階與相對相似度幾乎沒有實證;DFT 只用到第 0、1 係數;位置特徵還沒有加上 σ 平滑。熟悉度這個協和度的第三因素則完全不在範圍內。

The most obvious gap is a performance claim with no numbers. The abstract and introduction say version 2 is 'substantially faster' and brings higher orders within reach, yet there is no timing comparison and no curve of run time against r or collection size, only the 1.7×10⁹ grid-size argument. For a paper whose main contribution is a tool, that is the table readers most want and the easiest to add.

Second, the worked example only correlates the measures with one another; there are no listener data. The conclusion that 'the two families are nearly independent, so studies need both' is shown here only as non-collinearity of predictors, not as both having behavioural explanatory power; the latter rests on the cited Hearne et al. (2026). With 36 probes, one context and fixed σ and harmonic counts, the stability of the correlations is not tested, and the method section itself shows that moving from 16 to 24 harmonics reverses hEntropy's sensitivity ranking.

Third, the survey of empirical applications in Section 6 is almost entirely the author's own or collaborators' work: a self-review, not a systematic comparison with other models. It includes an unfavourable result: in Smit et al. (2022), cross-cultural evidence for a universal effect of mean pitch on happiness was 'negligible'. The author's five listed limitations also stand: log frequency is not perceptually uniform across wide ranges in the non-periodic modes; tensor harmonicity's relation to Erlich's harmonic entropy is unexplored; higher-order and relative similarities have almost no empirical tests; the DFT measures use only the zeroth and first coefficients; and the positional features lack σ smoothing. Familiarity, the third factor of consonance, is out of scope entirely.

討論Discussion

對 MIR 來說,這篇的價值主要在「可以直接拿來用」:pip install musicperception-toolbox 之後,任何一組微分音、表情化時值或從錄音擷取的頻譜峰值,都能不經量化地算出跟另一組的相似度與協和度,而且不同大小的集合可以直接比。這對和弦推薦、生成模型的和聲評估、或非西方音階的分析都是方便的特徵來源,比 chroma 向量的 12 格餘弦相似度多了泛音結構與不確定度兩個旋鈕。

但要清楚它的定位:這是一組手工設計、心理聲學動機的特徵,不是學出來的表徵,也不做轉譜。輸入要嘛是符號音高,要嘛是 audioPeaks 擷取的頻譜峰值,在多樂器混音上峰值擷取的品質會直接決定結果,論文沒有評估這一段。

最值得注意的是實例分析裡跨族相關不超過 0.37 這個數字。若它在更多脈絡與參數下仍成立,代表「跟前後文合不合」與「本身好不好聽」在聲學層面上是兩個軸——這對生成音樂的評估很有參考價值,因為多數自動評分把兩者揉成單一分數。可惜這篇沒有給行為資料,這個推論目前仍是假說層級。

For MIR, the value is mainly that it is usable as-is: after pip install musicperception-toolbox, any set of microtonal pitches, expressive timings or spectral peaks taken from a recording can be compared with another for similarity and consonance without quantization, and sets of different sizes compare directly. That makes it a convenient feature source for chord recommendation, harmonic evaluation of generative models, or analysis of non-Western scales, adding two knobs, harmonic structure and uncertainty, beyond a 12-bin chroma cosine similarity.

Its position should be clear, though: these are hand-designed, psychoacoustically motivated features, not learned representations, and there is no transcription. Input is either symbolic pitch or spectral peaks from audioPeaks; on multi-instrument mixes, peak-extraction quality directly determines the result, and the paper does not evaluate that stage.

The most notable number is the cross-family correlation of at most 0.37 in the worked example. If it holds across more contexts and parameters, 'does it fit what came before' and 'does it sound good on its own' are two separate acoustic axes, which matters for evaluating generated music because most automatic scorers collapse both into one number. Without behavioural data in this paper, that inference remains at the level of a hypothesis.

動機Motivation

評估生成音樂至少要看三件事:聽起來好不好(感知品質)、訊號有沒有技術瑕疵(削波、響度異常)、以及有沒有照提示詞做(對齊)。作者指出既有方法各有盲點:偏重高階音樂語意而忽略訊號層瑕疵;迴歸模型有「趨中偏誤」,預測分數擠在平均值附近,兩首歌的差異被壓縮;通用的音訊–文字模型只抓到大致語意,漏掉「要有小提琴」這類細粒度屬性,所以 CLAP 分數高不代表真的照提示詞做。

CloudEar 的目標因此是一個成對式(pairwise)評估器:給一個提示詞和兩首生成的歌,分別判斷哪首音樂性較好、哪首較符合提示詞。成對設定直接對應人類 A/B 偏好資料,也比絕對分數更容易拉開差距。

Evaluating generated music means checking at least three things: whether it sounds good (perceptual quality), whether the signal has technical defects (clipping, abnormal loudness), and whether it follows the prompt (alignment). The authors point to blind spots in existing methods: they emphasize high-level musical semantics and overlook signal-level defects; regression models show central-tendency bias, squeezing predictions around the mean and compressing the difference between two songs; and general audio–text models capture broad semantics but miss fine-grained attributes such as 'must include violin', so a high CLAP score does not mean the prompt was actually followed.

CloudEar therefore targets a pairwise evaluator: given a prompt and two generated songs, decide which has better musicality and which better follows the prompt. The pairwise setting maps directly onto human A/B preference data and separates songs more easily than absolute scores.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接比較的基線有四個。SongBench(Wu 等 2026)是多面向的歌曲品質評分基準,CloudEar 的感知專家直接沿用它「隨機取 30 秒、抽 MuQ 特徵」的流程;HEAR(Liu 等 2026)結合 MuQ 與 MusicFM 表徵做多維音樂美學評分,在這裡被當成診斷專家的一個輸入特徵;CMI-RM(Ma 等 2026)是 CMI-RewardBench 的音樂獎勵模型,也是對齊任務上原本最強的基線;MuQ 組合則用 MuQ-Eval 評音樂性、MuQ-MuLan 的音訊–文字相似度評對齊。更早的通用做法是 Meta 的 Audiobox Aesthetics 與各種 MOS 預測器,本文沒有比較。

要點明的是,感知專家的「成對排序損失」——對兩首歌分數差取 sigmoid 再算交叉熵——就是 RankNet(Burges 等 2005)或 Bradley–Terry 模型,序數迴歸則借自單眼深度估計的 DORN(Fu 等 2018)。跨模態專家的思路(用音訊標籤器的輸出跟目標屬性比)也跟 AudioLDM、MusicGen 評估裡用 PaSST 分類器算 KL 散度相近,差別在於這裡的目標屬性由 Qwen3-30B 從提示詞抽出,再映射到 Discogs400 曲風與 MTG-Jamendo 樂器的同一套詞彙。

所以這篇的新意不在任何單一元件,而在於把三種已知訊號用一個線性層與兩層 Transformer 融合起來,並在成對偏好上訓練。這是一個系統整合的貢獻。

Four baselines are compared directly. SongBench (Wu et al. 2026) is a multi-aspect song-quality benchmark, and CloudEar's perceptual expert reuses its 'random 30-second clip, MuQ features' pipeline. HEAR (Liu et al. 2026) combines MuQ and MusicFM representations for multidimensional music aesthetics and is used here as one input to the diagnostic expert. CMI-RM (Ma et al. 2026) is the music reward model from CMI-RewardBench and the previous strongest baseline on alignment. The MuQ combination uses MuQ-Eval for musicality and MuQ-MuLan audio–text similarity for alignment. Earlier general-purpose approaches such as Meta's Audiobox Aesthetics and various MOS predictors are not compared.

Worth stating plainly: the perceptual expert's 'pairwise ranking loss', a sigmoid on the score difference followed by cross-entropy, is RankNet (Burges et al. 2005), i.e. a Bradley–Terry model, and the ordinal regression is borrowed from DORN for monocular depth estimation (Fu et al. 2018). The cross-modal expert's idea, comparing an audio tagger's output with target attributes, is also close to the PaSST-classifier KL divergence used in AudioLDM and MusicGen evaluations; the difference is that the target attributes are extracted from the prompt by Qwen3-30B and mapped onto the same Discogs400 genre and MTG-Jamendo instrument vocabularies.

The novelty is therefore not in any single component but in fusing three known signals through a linear layer and a two-layer Transformer, trained on pairwise preferences. It is a systems-integration contribution.

方法Method

感知專家:每首歌隨機取 30 秒,抽 MuQ 特徵,經 Transformer 轉接層得到感知表徵。訓練同時用三個目標:序數迴歸(把品質分成有序等級)、MLP 連續迴歸(對真值分數)、以及成對排序損失(偏好的那首分數應較高)。作者主張三者合用可以緩解迴歸預測擠在平均值附近的問題。

診斷專家:擷取四個訊號層特徵——真峰值、削波比例、波峰因數、整合響度(依 ITU-R BS.1770-5 與 EBU R128)——再加上 HEAR 分數與感知專家的分數,串接後過一個線性層得到音樂性分數,用成對偏好標籤的二元交叉熵訓練。推論時選音樂性分數較高的那首。

跨模態專家:音訊端用 Discogs400 預測 400 種曲風、用 MTG-Jamendo 樂器模型預測樂器標籤;文字端用 Qwen3-30B 從提示詞抽出結構化的曲風與樂器,映射到同一套詞彙成為標籤向量。兩層 Transformer 聯合建模音訊屬性、提示詞屬性與感知表徵,輸出對齊分數,同樣以成對 BCE 訓練。

資料:感知專家用 AIME、MusicArena 與約 7,000 小時內部人工評分音樂(主要是五個生成模型的英文歌,只保留超過 200 名聽者評分的歌)。成對訓練另加 MusicEval 與 CMI-Pref 訓練集;音樂性配對要求正規化後評分差大於 0.2,對齊配對要求共用同一提示詞。評估用 PAM-Music(SRCC、LRCC)、CMI-Test 與 MusicArena 測試集(2025 年 7–12 月生成的 1,340 對,宣稱與訓練資料無重疊),主指標是成對偏好準確率 P-ACC。

結果:CMI-Test 音樂性 P-ACC 0.8179,比 HEAR 的 0.7882 高約 3.0 個百分點;對齊 0.7635,比 CMI-RM 的 0.7389 高約 2.5 點;MusicArena 0.7657,比 CMI-RM 的 0.7194 高約 4.6 點;PAM-Music SRCC 0.7257、LRCC 0.7229,比 CMI-RM 的 0.6626、0.6379 高約 6.3 與 8.5 點。消融:只用感知專家時音樂性 0.7592,加 HEAR 到 0.7805(+2.1),加 DSP 特徵只到 0.7620(+0.3),三者全上才到 0.8179;對齊從 0.6743 升到 0.7635(+8.9),是跨模態專家帶來的最大增益。

Perceptual expert: a random 30-second clip per song, MuQ features, a Transformer adapter producing the perceptual representation. Training combines three objectives: ordinal regression (quality as ordered levels), MLP continuous regression (against the ground-truth score), and a pairwise ranking loss (the preferred song should score higher). The authors argue the combination counters the tendency of regression to squeeze predictions around the mean.

Diagnostic expert: four signal-level features, true peak, clipping ratio, crest factor and integrated loudness (per ITU-R BS.1770-5 and EBU R128), are concatenated with the HEAR score and the perceptual expert's score and passed through one linear layer to a musicality score, trained with binary cross-entropy on pairwise preference labels. At inference the song with the higher musicality score wins.

Cross-modal expert: on the audio side, Discogs400 predicts 400 genres and an MTG-Jamendo instrument model predicts instrument tags; on the text side, Qwen3-30B extracts structured genre and instrument information from the prompt, mapped onto the same vocabularies as label vectors. A two-layer Transformer jointly models audio attributes, prompt attributes and the perceptual representation to produce an alignment score, also trained with pairwise BCE.

Data: the perceptual expert trains on AIME, MusicArena and about 7,000 hours of in-house human-rated music (mainly English songs from five generators, keeping only songs rated by more than 200 listeners). Pairwise training adds MusicEval and the CMI-Pref training split; musicality pairs need a normalized rating gap above 0.2, alignment pairs share a prompt. Evaluation uses PAM-Music (SRCC, LRCC), CMI-Test and the MusicArena test set (1,340 pairs generated July–December 2025, stated to have no training overlap), with pairwise preference accuracy P-ACC as the main metric.

Results: musicality P-ACC on CMI-Test is 0.8179, about 3.0 points above HEAR's 0.7882; alignment is 0.7635, about 2.5 above CMI-RM's 0.7389; MusicArena is 0.7657, about 4.6 above CMI-RM's 0.7194; PAM-Music SRCC 0.7257 and LRCC 0.7229 are about 6.3 and 8.5 points above CMI-RM's 0.6626 and 0.6379. Ablation: the perceptual expert alone gives 0.7592 musicality, adding HEAR gives 0.7805 (+2.1), adding DSP features only 0.7620 (+0.3), and all three reach 0.8179; alignment rises from 0.6743 to 0.7635 (+8.9), the largest gain, from the cross-modal expert.

限制Limitations

最根本的問題是論文的核心主張沒有被檢驗。動機那節說既有方法的毛病是「趨中偏誤、分數被壓縮」,感知專家的三重損失就是為此設計,但消融表裡沒有「只用迴歸」對「三重損失」的比較,也沒有任何預測分數分布或變異數的圖。讀者無法判斷改善來自損失設計,還是來自多加的 7,000 小時資料。

第二,訓練與測試的分布關係偏有利。MusicArena 與 CMI-Pref 都同時出現在訓練(前者是評分資料、後者是訓練集)與測試(MusicArena 測試集、CMI-Test)中;論文只說 MusicArena 測試的 1,340 對與訓練「無重疊」,但同來源、同時期的分布優勢仍在。CMI-Test 的樣本數沒有交代。7,000 小時內部資料未公開,結果無法重現。

第三,沒有任何信賴區間或顯著性檢定。以 MusicArena 的 1,340 對、準確率約 0.76 估算,單一準確率的標準誤約 1.2 個百分點,所以消融中 DSP 的 +0.3 落在雜訊範圍內,CMI-Test 上比 HEAR 高 3.0 點與比 CMI-RM 高 2.5 點也只是勉強拉開。

第四,論文自己發現一件值得深究卻沒追下去的事:完全不看提示詞的 SongBench 與 HEAR,在 CMI-Test 對齊任務上仍有 0.6823 與 0.6970 的準確率。這代表該基準的對齊標籤跟音樂性高度相關,那麼 CloudEar 對齊的 0.7635 有多少真的來自屬性比對,需要一個「拿掉音訊品質特徵、只留屬性比對」的消融才能回答。跨模態專家也只涵蓋曲風與樂器,情緒、速度、調性、結構等提示詞屬性都不在詞彙裡。

The most basic problem is that the central claim is never tested. The motivation blames existing methods for 'central-tendency bias and compressed scores', and the perceptual expert's triple loss exists for that reason, but the ablation table has no 'regression only' versus 'triple loss' comparison and no plot of predicted-score distributions or variance. Readers cannot tell whether the gain comes from the loss design or from the extra 7,000 hours of data.

Second, the train–test relationship is favourable. MusicArena and CMI-Pref appear in both training (ratings and the training split) and testing (the MusicArena test set and CMI-Test); the paper only says the 1,340 MusicArena test pairs have 'no overlap' with training, but the advantage of a same-source, same-period distribution remains. The size of CMI-Test is not given. The 7,000 hours of in-house data are unreleased, so the results cannot be reproduced.

Third, there are no confidence intervals or significance tests. With 1,340 MusicArena pairs at roughly 0.76 accuracy, the standard error of a single accuracy is about 1.2 points, so DSP's +0.3 in the ablation sits within noise, and the 3.0-point margin over HEAR and 2.5-point margin over CMI-RM on CMI-Test are only modest separations.

Fourth, the paper notices something worth pursuing and does not: SongBench and HEAR, which never see the prompt, still reach 0.6823 and 0.6970 accuracy on CMI-Test alignment. That means the benchmark's alignment labels correlate strongly with musicality, so how much of CloudEar's 0.7635 truly comes from attribute matching would need an ablation that removes the audio-quality features and keeps only attribute matching. The cross-modal expert also covers only genre and instruments; mood, tempo, key and structure in prompts are outside its vocabulary.

討論Discussion

這篇最有用的觀察其實是負面的那一個:不看提示詞的模型也能在「提示詞對齊」上拿到近七成準確率。它提醒所有用 CMI-Test 之類偏好資料訓練音樂獎勵模型的人,人類標註者在判斷「哪首比較照提示詞」時,很大程度上被「哪首比較好聽」帶著走。若要把 CloudEar 這類評估器當成 RLHF 的獎勵訊號,這種混淆會讓生成模型學到「做得好聽」而非「照指令做」。

設計上,把 EBU R128 響度、真峰值、削波這些母帶工程師天天在看的量測直接餵給評估器,是務實的做法,但消融顯示它們單獨加上去幾乎沒有增益(+0.3),要跟 HEAR 一起才有用,這個交互作用論文沒有解釋。用 LLM 把提示詞結構化再跟標籤器比對,好處是可解釋——能說出「提示詞要鋼琴,但標籤器沒聽到」——作者在結論也把「解釋每個偏好由哪些音樂因素造成」列為未來工作,這是比再多一兩點準確率更有價值的方向。

整體而言,這是一篇簡短的系統報告,數字是正向的,但目前的證據層級只夠說「三種訊號融合後在三個基準上都贏了既有基線」,說不到「為什麼贏」。

The most useful observation is actually the negative one: models that never see the prompt still reach nearly 70% accuracy on 'prompt alignment'. It warns anyone training music reward models on preference data such as CMI-Test that human annotators judging 'which follows the prompt better' are largely swayed by 'which sounds better'. If evaluators like CloudEar are used as RLHF reward signals, that confound would teach generators to 'sound good' rather than 'follow the instruction'.

On design, feeding the evaluator EBU R128 loudness, true peak and clipping, the measures a mastering engineer checks daily, is pragmatic, but the ablation shows them adding almost nothing alone (+0.3) and only helping together with HEAR, an interaction the paper does not explain. Structuring the prompt with an LLM and matching it against taggers has the merit of interpretability, being able to say 'the prompt asked for piano but the tagger heard none', and the authors list explaining which musical factors drive each preference as future work, a more valuable direction than another point or two of accuracy.

Overall this is a short systems report with positive numbers, but the evidence currently supports 'fusing three signals beats existing baselines on three benchmarks', not 'why it wins'.
