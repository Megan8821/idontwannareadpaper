TAPE: Learning General Audio Effect Representations

TAPE：從處理後的參考音訊學出通用效果器表徵，一次前向就解碼成可編輯、可串流的可微分 DSP 效果鏈

Phillip Long, Yongyi Zang, Qihui Yang, Christodoulos Benetatos, Junfei Liu, Randal Leistikow, Julian McAuley, Perry R. Cook
arXiv:2610.40759 · cs.SD; cs.AI; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-29
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：TAPE 只看一段處理過的參考音訊，就估出背後的效果鏈，並一次前向解碼成五個可微分 DSP 模組（等化、壓縮、波形整形、小型殘差網路、殘響）的可編輯參數，套到另一段乾聲上。在 60 組跨內容測試對上，它的多解析度 STFT L1 是 1.120；對照之下，用 CMA-ES 在 ST-ITO 基本效果鏈上搜尋（每例 4,097 次渲染、每段 143 秒，約慢 275 倍）是 2.906，官方釋出的 DeepAFx-ST 音樂模型是 5.226。C++ 因果版本以 128 樣本緩衝串流時比即時快 1.76 倍。選它是因為一個乾淨的發現：把效果嵌入空間的距離壓低，並不會讓輸出更接近真正的目標。但要注意，聆聽測試（每組 6–9 人）評的是最小平方對抗變體，客觀數字來自 R3GAN 變體，兩者不是同一個模型。Why this one: TAPE looks only at a processed reference, estimates the effect chain behind it, and decodes it in one forward pass into editable parameters for five differentiable DSP modules (EQ, compression, waveshaping, a small residual network, reverb) applied to a different dry recording. On 60 cross-content test pairs its multi-resolution STFT L1 is 1.120, against 2.906 for CMA-ES search over ST-ITO's basic chain (4,097 renders per example, 143 s per clip, about 275× slower) and 5.226 for the released DeepAFx-ST music model. A causal C++ version streams 128-sample buffers 1.76× faster than real time. It was picked for one clean finding: pushing down distance in an effect-embedding space did not bring outputs closer to the actual target. One caveat: the listening tests (6–9 listeners per panel) score the least-squares adversarial variant, while the objective numbers come from the R3GAN variant, so the two are not the same model.

動機Motivation

混音師常常聽一張參考錄音就能大致拆出它的處理鏈，例如人聲上的壓縮與殘響，再把同一套處理搬到新素材上。這篇想讓模型做同一件事：只從處理後（wet）的參考音訊估出處理方式，而且輸出要是人看得懂、能調整的旋鈕，還要能在演奏者實際使用的小緩衝下即時運行。

難處在於專業效果器種類繁雜、內部多半沒有文件，我們只能透過它產生的聲音認識它。既有路線各有缺口：一次前向的回歸（DeepAFx-ST）只涵蓋少數幾種處理器；涵蓋多族效果的 ST-ITO 則要對每個例子做搜尋，原論文最多渲染 1,600 個候選。作者想要兼顧兩者：涵蓋多族、一次前向、參數可讀。

Mixing engineers can often hear a reference recording, roughly decode its processing chain (say, the compression and reverb on a vocal), and move the same treatment onto new material. This paper wants a model to do the same: estimate the treatment from the processed (wet) reference alone, output controls a person can read and adjust, and run in real time at the small buffer sizes performers actually play through.

The difficulty is that professional processors are heterogeneous, mostly undocumented, and observable only through the sound they produce. Existing routes each leave a gap. One-pass regression (DeepAFx-ST) covers only a few processors, while ST-ITO, which covers many effect families, searches per example, rendering up to 1,600 candidates in the original paper. The authors want both at once: many families, one forward pass, readable parameters.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的前身是 DeepAFx-ST（Steinmetz 等，2022）：以共用編碼器同時讀輸入與風格參考，一次前向預測可微分等化器與壓縮器的參數，訓練時不用效果標籤；後續的 DeepAFx-ST+ 又加上殘響與失真。ST-ITO（Steinmetz 等，2024）是作者自己認定最接近的工作：它的編碼器 AFx-Rep 同時看輸入與輸出的嵌入，學著辨認 63 個 VST3 插件與預設，再拿 AFx-Rep 距離當目標，用 CMA-ES 搜尋使用者提供的插件鏈，所以可以驅動不可微、甚至沒見過的插件。

TAPE 跟兩者的差別有兩點。第一，編碼器只看 wet 訊號，唯一的標籤是插件身分（68 個），不看乾聲、不預測預設。第二，解碼器直接預測參數，不做搜尋。換個角度看，TAPE 的骨架其實就是 DeepAFx-ST 那套「編碼器 → 可微分 DSP 參數」的一次前向架構，只是把自監督編碼器換成 ST-ITO 式的插件分類編碼器，再把渲染器擴充到五個模組。

模組層級也有可辨認的前例。把五種波形曲線用 softmax 混合，是以連續放鬆處理離散選擇，跟可微分架構搜尋（DARTS）同一招；可微分 DSP 本身則可追溯到 DDSP（Engel 等，2020），而 Diff-MST（Vanka 等，2024）已經從參考混音預測可微分混音台的參數。同期的 LLM2Fx-Tools、StemFX、RelFx、PEACE 都在預測整條效果鏈，作者明說沒有拿來比較。

The most direct predecessor is DeepAFx-ST (Steinmetz et al., 2022): a shared encoder reads both the input and a style reference and predicts, in one forward pass, the controls of a differentiable equalizer and compressor, trained without effect labels. DeepAFx-ST+ later added reverb and distortion. ST-ITO (Steinmetz et al., 2024) is what the authors themselves call the closest work. Its encoder, AFx-Rep, sees embeddings of both input and output and learns to identify which of 63 VST3 plugins and presets was applied. ST-ITO then uses AFx-Rep distance as the objective of a CMA-ES search over a user-supplied plugin chain, so it can drive non-differentiable and even unseen plugins.

TAPE differs from both in two ways. First, its encoder sees only the wet signal, and its only label is plugin identity (68 plugins): no dry input, no preset prediction. Second, the decoder predicts controls directly, with no search. Put another way, TAPE's skeleton is DeepAFx-ST's one-pass encoder-to-differentiable-DSP design, with the self-supervised encoder swapped for an ST-ITO-style plugin classifier and the renderer extended to five modules.

The modules also have recognisable precedents. Mixing five waveshaping curves through a softmax handles a discrete choice by continuous relaxation, the same trick as differentiable architecture search (DARTS). Differentiable DSP itself goes back to DDSP (Engel et al., 2020), and Diff-MST (Vanka et al., 2024) already predicts the controls of a differentiable mixing console from a reference mix. The concurrent systems LLM2Fx-Tools, StemFX, RelFx and PEACE all predict whole chains, and the authors say plainly that they do not compare against them.

方法Method

資料。 約 300 段來源錄音（MUSDB18 分軌、GuitarSet、Carnatic Varnam、Bach Violin、LibriSpeech 與合成測試訊號）經 68 個插件的隨機設定渲染，共近兩百萬段：19 個動態處理、14 個等化器、9 個調變、9 個前級、8 個殘響、5 個磁帶模擬、3 個飽和失真、1 個多功能。全部是 48 kHz 單聲道、處理前響度正規化到 −14 LUFS、切成五秒視窗。訓練對（A, B）是兩段內容不同、但經過同一組插件設定 θ 的錄音。

編碼器。 80 頻帶 log-mel 接 Conformer，以一個可學習的查詢 token 池化成 256 維的 z。損失是 68 類插件身分上的 additive-margin softmax，再加 VICReg 的變異數與共變異數項，防止維度塌縮。八層版本在保留集上的插件 top-1 準確率是 72.5%（六層版是 78.7%，十層與十二層反而掉到 46.6% 與 54.8%）；預設層級的準確率幾乎為零。t-SNE 上同一族的插件聚在一起，雖然沒有任何損失提到「族」。

解碼器。 編碼器凍結後，解碼器 g 是五個可微分 DSP 模組串成的鏈，每個模組由一個小 MLP 從 z 讀出參數，並各有一個門控 γ（γ = 0 即旁通）。等化：五個 Bristow-Johnson 峰值雙二階濾波器，在 FFT 格點上一次相乘，以循環卷積近似 IIR 串接，避開長遞迴的反向傳播。壓縮：硬膝在壓縮器沒作用時閾值沒有梯度，所以改用 softplus，搭配 10 毫秒因果 RMS 與一階起音／釋放，後者用前綴對數和計算以便微分。波形整形：tanh、硬削波、非對稱真空管、二極體、正弦折疊五條曲線並行，以 softmax 混合，任一條的權重上限約 0.41。

殘差色彩：一個以 FiLM 接 z 的因果 TCN（4 個膨脹區塊、32 通道、核長 13、感受野 181 樣本 = 3.8 毫秒），在 23 種殘差架構中 SI-SDR 排第一，但前幾名只差 0.1 dB。殘響：不用回授結構，而是閉式合成三秒脈衝響應，六個頻帶的濾波噪音各以預測的 RT60 衰減，加上早期反射與直達聲，再由小型 ConvNeXt 修飾 STFT。訓練先讓各模組收斂，再以多週期、多解析度 STFT、子頻帶、多尺度 CQT 判別器做對抗微調（含 feature matching），分成 R3GAN 與最小平方兩個變體。這一階段連編碼器也一起更新，學習率 5×10⁻⁶。

結果。 在 60 組跨內容保留對上，對抗微調把 STFT L1 從 2.071 降到 1.120、CLAP 距離從 0.464 降到 0.220，SI-SDR 從 −29.38 升到 −16.80 dB。聆聽測試（無錨點的 MUSHRA 式相似度評分，0–100）中，Study 1（8 人）對抗版 72.5、未處理輸入 47.6、純重建版 41.3；每位聽者都偏好對抗版，Holm 校正後 p = 0.0156。Study 3（7 人）在不重疊的另一批配對上重做，結果是 72.4 對 38.4。有一個值得記住的細節：純重建版的 STFT L1（2.071）比未處理輸入（2.335）低，聽者卻給它更低的分數。

與搜尋比較：以 AFx-Rep 為目標的 CMA-ES 是 2.906，比 TAPE 高 +1.79（95% 信賴區間 [+1.37, +2.30]），只在 60 對中的 2 對贏過 TAPE；改用 TAPE 自己的編碼器 f 當目標是 2.498，隨機搜尋是 2.936。從 TAPE 的輸出開始搜尋，誤差反而升到 2.701（AFx-Rep）與 2.454（f）。在共同的 24 kHz 頻寬並做峰值正規化後，DeepAFx-ST 音樂模型是 2.097，TAPE 是 1.310，未處理輸入是 1.724，TAPE 在 55/60 對上較低。串流：128 樣本緩衝下每次回呼平均 1.51 毫秒，112,500 次無一逾時；64 樣本時 225,000 次中有 21 次逾時。串流與離線的 STFT L1 是 1.179 對 1.177。

Data. About 300 source recordings (MUSDB18 stems, GuitarSet, Carnatic Varnam, Bach Violin, LibriSpeech and synthetic test signals) are rendered through random settings of 68 plugins, for nearly two million clips: 19 dynamics processors, 14 equalizers, 9 modulation effects, 9 preamps, 8 reverbs, 5 tape emulations, 3 saturation/distortion units and 1 multi-tool. Everything is 48 kHz mono, loudness-normalised to −14 LUFS before processing, and cut into five-second windows. A training pair (A, B) is two recordings with different content processed by the same plugin configuration θ.

Encoder. An 80-band log-mel front end feeds a Conformer, and a learned query token pools the output into a 256-dimensional z. The loss is additive-margin softmax over the 68 plugin identities, plus VICReg's variance and covariance terms to stop dimensions collapsing. The eight-layer encoder reaches 72.5% held-out plugin top-1 accuracy (six layers reach 78.7%; ten and twelve layers drop to 46.6% and 54.8%), and preset-level accuracy is near zero. In t-SNE, plugins of the same family cluster together even though no loss mentions families.

Decoder. With the encoder frozen, the decoder g is a chain of five differentiable DSP modules, each reading its parameters from z through a small MLP and each with a gate γ (γ = 0 bypasses it). Equalisation uses five Bristow-Johnson peaking biquads, applied as one multiplication on the FFT grid: a circular-convolution approximation of the IIR cascade that avoids backpropagating through a long recursion. For compression, a hard knee gives the threshold no gradient while the compressor is idle, so it is replaced by a softplus, with a 10 ms causal RMS detector and one-pole attack/release computed through prefix log-sums so they can be differentiated. Waveshaping runs five curves in parallel (tanh, hard clip, asymmetric tube, diode, sine wavefolder) and mixes them by softmax, capping any one curve at about 0.41 of the weight.

Residual colour is a causal TCN conditioned on z through FiLM (4 dilated blocks, 32 channels, kernel 13, a 181-sample = 3.8 ms receptive field). It ranked first on SI-SDR among 23 residual architectures, though the top entries were within 0.1 dB. Reverb avoids feedback structures and synthesises a three-second impulse response in closed form: noise in six bands decays at predicted per-band RT60s, early reflections and a direct impulse are added, and a small ConvNeXt refines the STFT. Training first lets the modules converge, then fine-tunes adversarially (with feature matching) against multi-period, multi-resolution STFT, sub-band and multi-scale CQT critics, in an R3GAN and a least-squares variant. This stage updates the encoder too, at a learning rate of 5×10⁻⁶.

Results. On 60 cross-content held-out pairs, adversarial fine-tuning lowers STFT L1 from 2.071 to 1.120 and CLAP distance from 0.464 to 0.220, and raises SI-SDR from −29.38 to −16.80 dB. In the listening tests (MUSHRA-style similarity ratings, 0–100, no anchors), Study 1 (8 listeners) gives 72.5 for the adversarial decoder, 47.6 for unprocessed input and 41.3 for reconstruction-only. Every listener preferred the adversarial version (p = 0.0156 after Holm). Study 3 (7 listeners) repeats this on disjoint pairs: 72.4 against 38.4. One detail worth remembering: reconstruction-only has lower STFT L1 than the unprocessed input (2.071 vs 2.335), yet listeners rated it lower.

Against search: CMA-ES on the AFx-Rep objective scores 2.906, worse than TAPE by +1.79 (95% CI [+1.37, +2.30]), and beats TAPE on only 2 of 60 pairs. With TAPE's own encoder f as the objective it scores 2.498, and random search scores 2.936. Starting the search from TAPE's output pushes error up to 2.701 (AFx-Rep) and 2.454 (f). At a common 24 kHz bandwidth with peak normalisation, the DeepAFx-ST music model scores 2.097, TAPE 1.310 and the unprocessed input 1.724, and TAPE is lower on 55 of 60 pairs. Streaming: with 128-sample buffers a callback averages 1.51 ms and none of 112,500 callbacks overrun; with 64 samples, 21 of 225,000 overrun. Streamed versus offline STFT L1 is 1.179 versus 1.177.

限制Limitations

「保留集」只在名義上成立。 資料依曲目雜湊切成 80/10/10，但插件設定在三個切分之間是共用的；MUSDB18 以分軌為單位，同一首歌的不同分軌可能落在不同切分；而且存下來的評估輸出沒有記錄曲目 ID。作者自己也寫明，評估對只是「在這個名義切分的意義上」保留。這代表跨內容泛化的數字，可能部分建立在見過同一組設定或同一首歌的基礎上。

比較對象都打了折扣。 DeepAFx-ST 用的是官方釋出模型，沒有在同一批資料上重訓，渲染器又只有等化器加壓縮器，48 kHz 下的 5.226 主要來自 12 kHz 以上缺內容。摘要稱 TAPE 的頻譜誤差低於「先前的最先進方法」DeepAFx-ST，但那是分布外的比較。ST-ITO 那邊則因為原本的五個商用 VST3 插件沒有隨程式碼釋出，改搜尋公開程式碼裡的基本鏈（多一個延遲、少一個殘差），且兩邊的峰值正規化方式不同。作者承認這組比較「不能為 TAPE 與 ST-ITO 排名」。

聆聽證據薄且不完全對應。 每組只有 6–9 位、彼此重疊的聽者，沒有標準低通錨點，刺激是 192 kb/s MP3，統計以聽者為單位，不處理題目間的變異。客觀數字來自 R3GAN 變體，聆聽分數卻來自最小平方變體。更微妙的一點是：被評估的解碼器是從「從頭聯合訓練」的檢查點出發，而這個檢查點在 Study 2 裡的分數跟乾聲差不多；聽者最喜歡的兩階段課程，作者說還沒拿來當起點測試。

涵蓋範圍。 鏈裡沒有低頻振盪器驅動的模組，3.8 毫秒的殘差感受野也做不出來，所以 9 個調變插件與磁帶的 wow/flutter 只能近似。系統只處理單聲道，也無法驅動使用者已經擁有的插件，這一點 ST-ITO 做得到。編碼器的預設準確率接近零，作者也承認重建結果無法說明解碼器從 z 拿到多少預設資訊。即時性方面，程式在回呼內配置記憶體、計時沒有與其他工作隔離，作者明言不構成硬即時保證。

The "held-out" set is held out only nominally. Tracks are split 80/10/10 by a hash of the track identifier, but plugin configurations are shared across all three splits; MUSDB18 is split by stem, so stems of one song can land in different splits; and the saved evaluation outputs do not record track IDs. The authors themselves write that the evaluation pairs are held out "only in the sense of this nominal split". Some of the cross-content generalisation may therefore rest on having seen the same settings or the same song.

Both baselines are handicapped. DeepAFx-ST is run as the released models, not retrained on these pairs, with a renderer of only an EQ and a compressor; its 5.226 at 48 kHz comes mostly from missing content above 12 kHz. The abstract says TAPE beats DeepAFx-ST, "the previous state of the art", on spectral error, but that is an out-of-distribution comparison. On the ST-ITO side, the original five commercial VST3 plugins are not distributed with the code, so the search runs over the public basic chain (one extra delay, no residual), and the two sides peak-normalise differently. The authors concede the comparison "does not rank TAPE and ST-ITO".

The listening evidence is thin and not fully matched. Each panel has only 6–9 overlapping listeners, no standard low-pass anchors, 192 kb/s MP3 stimuli, and statistics that treat listeners as the unit without modelling item variation. The objective numbers come from the R3GAN variant, the listening scores from the least-squares one. More subtly, the evaluated decoder starts from the joint-from-scratch checkpoint, which scored about the same as dry audio in Study 2. The two-stage curriculum listeners liked best has, by the authors' account, not yet been tried as a starting point.

Coverage. The chain has no LFO-driven stage, and the 3.8 ms residual cannot provide one, so the 9 modulation plugins and tape wow/flutter are only approximated. The system is mono-only and cannot drive plugins a user already owns, which ST-ITO can. Preset accuracy of the encoder is near zero, and the authors concede the reconstructions do not show how much preset information the decoder gets from z. On real-time use, the program allocates inside callbacks and its timing was not isolated from other work, and the authors state this is no hard real-time guarantee.

討論Discussion

這篇最值得帶走的不是 TAPE 本身，而是 7.2 節那個關於推論目標的發現。AFx-Rep 搜尋從縮減設定到完整設定，把它優化的距離從 0.39 壓到 0.26，STFT L1 卻沒有顯著下降（3.28 → 2.91，p = 0.08）；在 56/60 對上，它比 TAPE 更接近參考的嵌入，卻離真正的目標更遠。逐對計算的話，AFx-Rep 距離與目標誤差的 Spearman 相關中位數是 −0.17，用 TAPE 自己的編碼器 f 則是 0.65。換句話說，「在效果嵌入裡逼近參考」這個 ST-ITO 式的目標，在這批資料上並不是重建目標的好代理；拿 AFx-Rep 類嵌入當評估指標的研究，也應該把這點記在心上。

另一面是 oracle 實驗：只動 z、對真正目標做 16 步梯度，就能把誤差降 15%、改善 55/60 對，反觀用 f 當目標的潛空間 TTA 反而讓誤差升 7%。所以解碼器有能力渲染得更好，剩下的誤差有一部分出在 z 的選擇上。這也指出下一步該往哪裡走：與其加大渲染器，不如找一個與目標誤差同向的推論目標。

論文也再一次示範了客觀指標與聽感分家：從頭聯合訓練的 STFT L1 最低，聽起來卻跟乾聲差不多；殘響脈衝經修飾後時域 L1 減半、STFT L1 卻變差，聽者沒聽出差別。作者把聆聽當主要依據是對的，但樣本這麼小，結論只能停在受試者內的相對比較。如果要我補實驗，會依序做三件事：在同一批配對上重訓 DeepAFx-ST，讓訓練資料先對齊；用「同插件、不同設定」的參考，看 z 到底帶了多少設定資訊；以及在同一個凍結的 z 上接一個不受限的解碼器，量出「參數可讀」要付出多少保真度。

The most useful thing to take from this paper is not TAPE itself but the finding about inference objectives in Section 7.2. From the reduced to the full setting, the AFx-Rep search pushed the distance it optimises from 0.39 to 0.26, yet its STFT L1 did not fall significantly (3.28 → 2.91, p = 0.08). On 56 of 60 pairs it ended closer to the reference in embedding space than TAPE did, but farther from the actual target. Computed per pair, the median Spearman correlation between AFx-Rep distance and target error is −0.17, against 0.65 for TAPE's own encoder f. In other words, the ST-ITO-style objective of approaching the reference in an effect embedding is a poor proxy for the reconstruction target on these data, and work that uses AFx-Rep-like embeddings as evaluation metrics should keep that in mind.

The other side is the oracle experiment: changing only z with 16 gradient steps against the true target lowers error by 15% and improves 55 of 60 pairs, while latent TTA with f as the objective raises error by 7%. So the decoder can render better outputs, and part of the remaining error lies in the choice of z. That points to the next step: rather than a bigger renderer, find an inference objective that moves with target error.

The paper also shows again that objective metrics and listening part ways. Joint training from scratch had the lowest STFT L1 but sounded about like dry audio, and refining the reverb impulse halved its time-domain L1 while worsening STFT L1, with no difference listeners detected. Treating listening as primary is right, but with panels this small the conclusions can only be within-listener contrasts. If I were adding experiments, I would do three things in order. Retrain DeepAFx-ST on the same pairs, so the training data at least match. Use references with the same plugin but different settings, to see how much setting information z actually carries. And attach an unconstrained decoder to the same frozen z, to measure how much fidelity the readable chain costs.

動機Motivation

同一首作品可以是一段錄音、一張樂譜影像，也可以是一份符號樂譜（這裡用 Humdrum 的 kern 格式）。要讓三者落在同一個可檢索的空間，常見做法是各模態接一個大型預訓練編碼器，再學一個共享映射。問題是，每個編碼器要凍結還是微調？全微調要為每個資料集、模態、目標各存一份完整骨幹，三個模態同時維護時成本很高。

作者因此問兩個問題。第一，LoRA、QLoRA 這類參數高效微調（PEFT）能不能在三種模態上追平全微調？第二，也是比較根本的問題：單一模態上分類變好，是否就代表多模態表徵變好？微調可能把編碼器推向只在自己模態裡有用的線索，例如錄音的音色在單音主題的樂譜裡根本不存在。

One musical work can be a recording, a sheet-music image, or a symbolic score (here in Humdrum's kern format). The usual way to put all three into one retrievable space is to give each modality a large pretrained encoder and learn a shared mapping on top. The question is whether to freeze or fine-tune each encoder. Full fine-tuning stores a complete backbone per dataset, modality and target, which gets expensive when three modalities are maintained at once.

The authors therefore ask two questions. First, can parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) such as LoRA and QLoRA match full fine-tuning across all three modalities? Second, and more fundamental: does better classification within one modality mean a better multimodal representation? Fine-tuning may push an encoder toward cues useful only in its own modality. A recording's timbre, for instance, simply does not exist in the score of a monophonic theme.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

這條研究線的起點是 Dorfer 等人（2017、2018）的音訊－樂譜片段嵌入，後續工作處理速度變化、時間對齊與較不受控的演奏錄音，但大多停在兩個模態之間。CLaMP 系列把符號音樂納入對比學習，重點卻在文字－音樂的配對。最直接的前作是同一團隊的 Hidalgo-Centeno 等人（TriScore 論文）：三個模態都用凍結編碼器，再接一個輕量共享投影，結果可以做分類與作品檢索，但局部鄰域仍然按模態分群。這篇就是在那個流程前面加上「編碼器要不要適配、怎麼適配」這一步，投影架構與訓練設定直接沿用前作。

論文宣稱的核心發現是「單模態的改善不保證能轉移到多模態」，這其實跟 Kumar 等人（2022）〈Fine-Tuning can Distort Pretrained Features〉那條線很接近：微調會扭曲預訓練特徵，使得換一個下游條件時，表現不如線性探測，因而有了先探測再微調的 LP-FT。論文沒有引用這條文獻，但它的「干擾」情況正是同一現象在多模態投影上的版本。另一個值得對照的是 CLIP 式的對比對齊：這裡的共享投影是用類別交叉熵訓練的，並沒有任何損失把同一組三元組拉近，所以模態不混合幾乎是設計上可預期的。

This line of work starts with Dorfer et al. (2017, 2018), who embedded short audio excerpts and sheet-music snippets; later work handled tempo variation, temporal alignment and less controlled performance recordings, but mostly stayed with two modalities. The CLaMP family brings symbolic music into contrastive learning, with the emphasis on text–music pairs. The most direct predecessor is the same group's Hidalgo-Centeno et al. (the TriScore paper): frozen encoders for all three modalities plus a lightweight shared projection, which supported classification and piece retrieval while local neighbourhoods stayed grouped by modality. This paper adds a step in front of that pipeline, whether and how to adapt the encoders, and reuses the earlier projection architecture and training setup as is.

The headline claim, that unimodal gains need not transfer to the multimodal setting, is close to the line of Kumar et al. (2022), "Fine-Tuning can Distort Pretrained Features": fine-tuning distorts pretrained features so that under a shifted downstream condition it can underperform linear probing, which motivated probing first and then fine-tuning (LP-FT). The paper does not cite this work, but its "interfering" case is the same phenomenon applied to a multimodal projection. CLIP-style contrastive alignment is the other natural contrast. The shared projection here is trained with category cross-entropy, and no loss pulls an aligned triplet together, so unmixed modalities are close to what the design predicts.

方法Method

資料。 TriScore 有 2,287 組對齊三元組，來自 111 部作品、17 位作曲家、8 類樂器；樂譜影像是用 Verovio 從同一份 kern 渲染出來的，三個模態內容高度對應。MTD 有 2,068 個西方古典主題、30 位作曲家、10 類編制、13 類樂器，MusicXML 先轉成 kern。MTD 的樂譜與影像只描述單音主題，錄音卻可能帶著和聲、配器與上下文，模態之間的資訊量並不相等。兩者都用約 80/10/10 的分層切分。

第一階段：單模態適配。 每個編碼器各自接一個線性分類器，以交叉熵訓練：AdamW、學習率 10⁻⁵、權重衰減 0.01、批次 4（音訊因記憶體限制降為 1），依驗證集 macro F1 早停，耐心值 25 epoch。LoRA 插在自注意力的 q、k、v 投影上，搜尋 7 組設定（秩 r ∈ {2, 4, 8}，α 為 2r 或 4r，dropout 0.1 或 0）；QLoRA 用同一組搜尋空間，骨幹以 4-bit NF4 加雙重量化載入。凍結編碼器的線性探測（LP）只當參考，不參與挑選；全微調、LoRA、QLoRA 中驗證分數最高者進入下一階段，平手時選參數較少的。

第二階段：2×2 設計。 適配後的編碼器拿掉分類頭、固定成特徵抽取器；同一個骨幹的原始凍結版本當作配對的對照組。各模態嵌入先做 L2 正規化、補零到相同維度，再進一個三模態共用的淺層 MLP 投影，投影以合併三模態樣本的類別交叉熵訓練，每個資料集與目標各訓一個。兩個因子交叉後得到四種條件：凍結／適配 × 原始（投影前）／投影後。評估用 kNN（k 在驗證集上選定），gallery 包含三個模態，報告 macro F1。另外兩個量是 Δ_adapt（固定投影與否，看適配的效果）與 Δ_projection（固定編碼器狀態，看投影的效果）。

單模態結果。 TriScore 上適配對 LP 的提升大多在 20–70 pp；作品標題的 LP 幾乎是零，所以提升特別大，GraphCodeBERT 以 LoRA 達到 0.919，比 LP 高 90.8 pp。MTD 的絕對分數低很多：音訊的最高分是樂器 0.484，影像都低於 0.18，符號最高 0.205。PEFT 在 TriScore 9 個組合中選中 6 個（全是 LoRA），在 MTD 9 個中選中 8 個（6 個 QLoRA、2 個 LoRA）。換骨幹造成的差異，普遍比調整 LoRA 的秩、α、dropout 更大。

多模態結果。 作者把 2×2 結果分成三類。互補：TriScore 作曲家從 42.09（凍結原始）到 61.55（適配原始）、50.63（凍結投影）、74.99（適配投影），適配與投影互相加成。重疊：TriScore 樂器的凍結投影是 74.39，適配投影反而降到 70.03；MTD 樂器與編制也類似，組合起來沒有額外收益。干擾：MTD 作曲家的適配原始是 11.41，加上投影反而掉到 8.58。

按查詢模態拆開看，TriScore 作曲家的符號查詢是 94.70、音訊 83.04、影像只有 47.22。作品檢索上，平均 Top-5／Top-10 是 55.32／64.68，影像查詢只有 34.59／45.95。把同模態樣本從 gallery 移除後，TriScore 作曲家的影像查詢從 47.22 掉到 17.54。全 gallery 的五近鄰中，TriScore 同模態比例在作曲家與樂器空間都超過 93%，影像查詢在作曲家與標題空間是 100%。

Data. TriScore has 2,287 aligned triplets from 111 works, 17 composers and 8 instrument classes. Its score images are rendered from the same kern files with Verovio, so the three modalities correspond closely. MTD has 2,068 Western classical themes, 30 composers, 10 ensemble classes and 13 instrument classes, with MusicXML converted to kern. MTD's scores and images describe only the monophonic theme, while the recording may carry harmony, orchestration and context, so the modalities do not hold equal information. Both use stratified splits of about 80/10/10.

Stage I: unimodal adaptation. Each encoder gets a linear classifier trained with cross-entropy: AdamW, learning rate 10⁻⁵, weight decay 0.01, batch size 4 (1 for audio, due to memory), early stopping on validation macro F1 with patience 25 epochs. LoRA goes into the q, k and v projections of self-attention, with 7 searched configurations (rank r ∈ {2, 4, 8}, α of 2r or 4r, dropout 0.1 or 0). QLoRA uses the same search space with the backbone loaded in 4-bit NF4 with double quantisation. Frozen-encoder linear probing (LP) is only a reference and is not selected. Whichever of full fine-tuning, LoRA and QLoRA scores best on validation moves on, with ties going to fewer parameters.

Stage II: the 2×2 design. Adapted encoders lose their classification head and are frozen as feature extractors, and the original frozen version of the same backbone serves as the paired control. Each modality's embedding is L2-normalised, zero-padded to a common size, and passed through a shallow MLP projection shared by all three modalities, trained with category cross-entropy over pooled samples from all three, one per dataset and target. Crossing the two factors gives four conditions: frozen/adapted × raw (before projection)/projected. Evaluation is kNN (k chosen on validation) over a gallery containing all three modalities, reported as macro F1. Two contrasts follow: Δ_adapt, the effect of adaptation with projection held fixed, and Δ_projection, the effect of projection with encoder state held fixed.

Unimodal results. On TriScore, adaptation improves over LP by mostly 20–70 pp. Title LP is near zero, so gains there are especially large: GraphCodeBERT with LoRA reaches 0.919, 90.8 pp above LP. MTD scores are much lower in absolute terms: the audio maximum is 0.484 for instrument, image stays below 0.18, and symbolic peaks at 0.205. PEFT is selected in 6 of 9 TriScore combinations (all LoRA) and 8 of 9 MTD combinations (6 QLoRA, 2 LoRA). Changing the backbone generally matters more than changing LoRA's rank, α or dropout.

Multimodal results. The authors sort the 2×2 results into three patterns. Complementary: TriScore composer goes from 42.09 (frozen raw) to 61.55 (adapted raw), 50.63 (frozen projected) and 74.99 (adapted projected), so adaptation and projection reinforce each other. Redundant: TriScore instrument is 74.39 frozen-projected but falls to 70.03 adapted-projected, and MTD instrument and ensemble look similar, with no extra gain from combining. Interfering: MTD composer is 11.41 adapted-raw and drops to 8.58 once projected.

Split by query modality, TriScore composer gives 94.70 for symbolic queries, 83.04 for audio and only 47.22 for image. For piece retrieval, average Top-5/Top-10 is 55.32/64.68, and image queries reach only 34.59/45.95. Removing same-modality samples from the gallery drops TriScore composer image queries from 47.22 to 17.54. Among the five nearest neighbours in the full gallery, same-modality proportions on TriScore exceed 93% in both the composer and instrument spaces, and image queries reach 100% in the composer and title spaces.

限制Limitations

全部是單次執行。 沒有多個種子、沒有信賴區間，也沒有顯著性檢定。TriScore 作曲家那種 32.90 pp 的差距不太可能是雜訊，但 MTD 上被拿來分類成「重疊」或「干擾」的差異，例如 +0.05、−0.35、−0.68 pp，在只有幾百個測試樣本、音訊批次大小為 1 的設定下，很可能落在種子變異之內。MTD 作曲家有 30 類，隨機猜測的 macro F1 大約在 3% 上下，4.38 到 11.41 的整段「干擾」都發生在接近地板的區域。

「PEFT 選中 14/18」有選擇偏差。 LoRA 與 QLoRA 各搜尋 7 組設定，全微調只有一組，以驗證分數挑最高者時，試得多的一方本來就比較容易勝出。這不否定 PEFT 的參數效率，但「經常追平或超越全微調」這句話的強度要打折扣。第二階段又讓各模態各自選策略，一組三元組可能混用 LoRA、QLoRA 與全微調，作者也承認無法分辨三種策略各自對多模態的影響。

檢索評估比字面上寬鬆。 作品標題同時是第一階段與投影的監督標籤，所以檢索查詢的作品都在訓練時見過，本質上比較接近封閉集合的分類，而不是對新作品的檢索；判定標準又是「前 N 名中至少一個同標題」。精確的跨模態檢索（找回同一組三元組的另外兩個模態）沒有報告。

幾個沒有解釋的地方。 TriScore 的影像是從同一份 kern 確定性渲染出來的，資訊量跟符號模態相同，影像卻始終最弱；這比較像是影像編碼器（例如 ViT 的 224 解析度）的瓶頸，論文沒有討論。PaliGemma 在秩 2 與秩 8 下都是 21.9M 可訓練參數，暗示這個數字主要來自 LoRA 以外的東西，文中沒有說明。此外，內文有幾個數字與表格的捨入不一致（9.55 對 9.54、1.91 對 1.90），圖 6、7 的圖說也標錯了條件。

Everything is a single run. There are no multiple seeds, no confidence intervals and no significance tests. A 32.90 pp gap like TriScore composer is unlikely to be noise, but the MTD differences sorted into "redundant" or "interfering", such as +0.05, −0.35 and −0.68 pp, plausibly sit within seed variance given a few hundred test items and an audio batch size of 1. MTD composer has 30 classes, so chance macro F1 is around 3%, and the whole "interfering" story from 4.38 to 11.41 happens close to the floor.

"PEFT selected in 14/18" carries a selection bias. LoRA and QLoRA each search 7 configurations while full fine-tuning gets one, and when the best validation score wins, the side with more tries is more likely to win anyway. This does not undo PEFT's parameter efficiency, but it weakens the claim that PEFT "frequently matches or outperforms" full fine-tuning. Stage II also lets each modality choose its own strategy, so one triplet may mix LoRA, QLoRA and full fine-tuning, and the authors concede the separate multimodal effects of the three strategies cannot be told apart.

The retrieval evaluation is looser than it sounds. Piece title is the supervision label for both Stage I and the projection, so every queried work was seen in training. That is closer to closed-set classification than to retrieving new works, and the criterion is that at least one of the top N shares the title. Exact cross-modal retrieval (recovering the other two modalities of the same triplet) is not reported.

Several things go unexplained. TriScore images are rendered deterministically from the same kern files, so they hold the same information as the symbolic modality, yet image is always the weakest. That looks like a bottleneck in the image encoders (ViT's 224-pixel resolution, for example), which the paper does not discuss. PaliGemma has 21.9M trainable parameters at both rank 2 and rank 8, which suggests the count comes mostly from something other than LoRA, and the paper does not say what. The text also rounds a few numbers differently from the tables (9.55 vs 9.54, 1.91 vs 1.90), and the captions of Figures 6 and 7 mislabel conditions.

討論Discussion

實務上最可以帶走的是兩點。第一，參數量的節省是實在的：音訊從 300M 以上降到約 1.4M，DiT 與 ViT 從約 86–87M 降到不到 1M，符號從 110–125M 降到約 0.7–1.0M，對要同時維護三個模態、多個目標的系統很有意義。第二，挑骨幹比調 LoRA 超參數重要。程式碼預訓練的 GraphCodeBERT 在 kern 上表現亮眼，TriScore 樂器與 RoBERTa 打平（0.958），標題 0.919 對 0.901 勝出，作曲家則輸給 RoBERTa（0.823 對 0.853）。

更值得思考的是 2×2 設計本身。把「編碼器適配」與「共享投影」拆開評估，應該成為這類多模態系統的標準做法，因為最終數字漂亮時，我們通常不知道功勞在誰身上。但這篇的投影只用類別監督，所以「模態不混合」與其說是發現，不如說是損失函數的直接後果；作者在結論也提到下一步要加對比、度量學習或多正例損失。我更想看到的是在同一個 2×2 框架下換上三元組對比損失，再看適配與投影的交互作用是否改變，並補上多種子的變異。

Two practical points stand out. First, the parameter savings are real: audio drops from over 300M to about 1.4M, DiT and ViT from about 86–87M to under 1M, and symbolic from 110–125M to about 0.7–1.0M. That matters for a system maintaining three modalities and several targets at once. Second, choosing the backbone matters more than tuning LoRA hyperparameters. GraphCodeBERT, pretrained on source code, does well on kern: it ties RoBERTa on TriScore instrument (0.958) and wins on title (0.919 vs 0.901), though it loses on composer (0.823 vs 0.853).

The 2×2 design itself is worth more thought. Evaluating encoder adaptation and shared projection separately should be standard for this kind of multimodal system, because when the final number looks good we usually cannot tell which step earned it. But the projection here is supervised only by categories, so unmixed modalities are less a finding than a direct consequence of the loss. The authors' conclusion also names contrastive, metric-learning or multi-positive losses as the next step. What I would most like to see is the same 2×2 framework with a triplet contrastive loss, to check whether the interaction between adaptation and projection changes, plus variance across seeds.

動機Motivation

在點集表徵裡，一首曲子是一堆二維點：橫軸是起音時間，縱軸是音高。傳統對位手法如移調、時間平移、倒影、增值、減值、逆行，都可以寫成作用在這些點上的幾何變換。作者過去的工作（SIA、SIATEC、COSIATEC 這一系列，以及 2022–2024 年的 maximal transformable patterns 與平行比對演算法）各自用了一套相近但不統一的定義，而且早期把變換限制為雙射。

這篇想把整套詞彙一次講清楚：什麼叫「變換類」、比對與出現的精確意義、最大樣式和部分對稱的關係，以及什麼時候「互為變換」才是一個真正的等價關係。最後這點對分析很實際：如果「A 可以變成 B」不具對稱性或遞移性，那麼把一首曲子裡的出現處分群，結果就會取決於從哪一個出現處開始。

In a point-set representation a piece is a set of 2D points, onset time on the x-axis and pitch on the y-axis. Traditional contrapuntal devices (transposition, time shift, inversion, augmentation, diminution, retrograde) can all be written as geometric transformations of these points. The author's earlier work (the SIA, SIATEC and COSIATEC line, plus the 2022–2024 papers on maximal transformable patterns and parallel matching) each used related but not unified definitions, and the early ones restricted transformations to bijections.

This paper sets out to define the whole vocabulary once: what a "transformation class" is, the exact meaning of match and occurrence, how maximal patterns relate to partial symmetries, and when "is a transformation of" is a genuine equivalence relation. That last point matters in practice for analysis. If "A can become B" is not symmetric or transitive, then grouping a piece's occurrences depends on which occurrence you start from.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

幾何樣式比對在 MIR 有一條清楚的譜系。SIA／SIATEC（Meredith、Lemström 與 Wiggins，2002）只處理平移：在多聲部點集中找出所有可平移重現的最大樣式。Ukkonen、Lemström 與 Mäkinen（2003）的 P1／P2／P3 演算法同樣在平移不變的前提下做查詢比對，P3 還處理部分重疊的時長。要加進時間縮放（增減值），Romming 與 Selfridge-Field（2007）用了幾何雜湊，Lemström（2010）則提出時間縮放不變的比對演算法。這篇的框架涵蓋了這些變換，但幾乎沒有跟它們對話：11 篇參考文獻中有 9 篇是作者自己的作品，剩下兩篇是演算法教科書與 1910 年刊出海頓主題的期刊。

論文最核心的概念，是用最小的「基底點數」β 加上每對基底至多 τ 個變換，來刻畫一個變換類。這其實就是電腦視覺裡幾何雜湊（Lamdan 與 Wolfson，1988）與對齊法的基本思路：用最少的點對應唯一決定變換，再去驗證其餘的點。Romming 與 Selfridge-Field 把幾何雜湊帶進音樂檢索時，靠的正是同一個觀察。至於「變換構成群，互為變換才是等價關係」這部分，作者自己也稱定理 6.1 為眾所周知的定理；在音樂理論中，它對應的是 Lewin（1987）廣義音程系統的出發點，論文同樣沒有提及。

Geometric pattern matching has a clear lineage in MIR. SIA/SIATEC (Meredith, Lemström and Wiggins, 2002) handle translation only, finding every maximal pattern that recurs by translation in a polyphonic point set. The P1/P2/P3 algorithms of Ukkonen, Lemström and Mäkinen (2003) also do query matching under translation invariance, with P3 handling partially overlapping durations. To add time scaling (augmentation and diminution), Romming and Selfridge-Field (2007) used geometric hashing, and Lemström (2010) proposed time-scale-invariant matching algorithms. This paper's framework covers those transformations but barely engages with that work: 9 of its 11 references are the author's own, and the other two are an algorithms textbook and the 1910 journal issue that printed the Haydn theme.

The central idea, characterising a transformation class by a minimal basis size β with at most τ transformations per basis pair, is essentially the core of geometric hashing (Lamdan and Wolfson, 1988) and alignment methods in computer vision: let the fewest point correspondences determine a transformation uniquely, then verify the remaining points. That same observation is what Romming and Selfridge-Field relied on when they brought geometric hashing to music retrieval. As for "transformations form a group, so being transforms of each other is an equivalence relation", the paper itself calls Theorem 6.1 well known. In music theory it is the starting point of Lewin's (1987) generalised interval systems, which the paper also does not mention.

方法Method

變換類。 一個集合 F 要算變換類，須滿足三個條件：每個變換可由長度 λ(F) 的參數向量唯一識別；存在類別函數 φ_F(σ, p) 把參數向量與點映到像點；存在最小的基底大小 β(F) 與上限 τ(F)，使任何一對長度為 β 的相異點基底，至多只有 τ 個變換能把前者映到後者。五個具體類別的數值如下：平移 F_2T 是 λ=2、β=1、τ=1；平移加 x 軸反射（倒影）F_2TR 是 λ=3、β=1、τ=2；x 方向縮放加平移 F_2ST 是 λ=3、β=2、τ=1（負的縮放因子就是逆行）；再加反射的 F_2STR 是 λ=4、β=2、τ=2，涵蓋整套傳統對位變換；F_2STRmodμ 則讓音高軸取模 μ，μ=7 對應全音階、μ=12 對應半音階。每一類都給出基底對變換集合函數，有些附上演算法。

比對與最大樣式。 一個變換 f 在 Q 裡對 P 的最大比對，是 P 中能被 f 映進 Q 的點的像；最大樣式則是那些點本身。對雙射 f，作者證明最大樣式等於 P ∩ f⁻¹(Q)，並以定理 4.1 給出 f 與 f⁻¹ 下最大比對與最大樣式的完整對應關係。若 f 的最大樣式非空且不等於整個資料集，f 就在資料集上定義了一個部分對稱。

出現集合與壓縮率。 樣式 P 在資料集 D 中相對於 F 的出現集合，是所有落在 D 裡的 f(P)。它可以編碼成一個序對 ⟨P, Ψ⟩，Ψ 是每個出現處各取字典序最小的參數向量；需要記錄的數值是 k|P| + λ|OS| 個，而逐點列舉需要 k|OS||P| 個。壓縮率定義為 k|COV| 除以前者，COV 是所有出現處的聯集。論文的例子是一個 5 點樣式在 F_2STR 下有 4 個出現處：逐點列舉要 40 個數值，序對編碼只要 26 個，壓縮率約 1.54。為什麼 P 不直接取某個出現處、省下 λ 個數值？作者的理由是查詢樣式本身未必在 D 裡，而使用者通常要的正是「查詢 → 出現處」的變換。

異質出現集合。 允許不同出現處用不同的變換類，各自選參數向量最短的那一類，分母額外加上類別數。原例子改成異質編碼後是 40/(10+3+13)，仍然是 1.54，沒有變好；但再加入兩個單純平移的出現處後，同質 F_2STR 編碼是 60/34 ≈ 1.76，異質編碼是 60/30 = 2.0。這本質上是一種最小描述長度式的偏好：能用簡單變換解釋的，就不要用複雜的變換。

變換等價與群。 若 F 構成變換群（封閉、結合、唯一單位元、可逆），「互為變換」就是等價關係，出現集合就是一組變換等價的樣式。F_2T 與 F_2TR 是群。F_2ST、F_2STR、F_2STRmodμ 都不是，因為縮放因子為 0 的變換會把整個樣式壓到同一個起音時間，沒有反函數；若出現集合裡同時有這種出現處與正常出現處，就不構成等價集合。排除 0 之後的 F′_2ST、F′_2STR、F′_2STRmodμ 被證明是群（引理 7–9）。這也回頭說明了先前那篇拉威爾分析的做法：在 CPO 表徵中，第 3、4、8、9 號出現處相對於 F_2STR 構成一組變換等價的樣式。

Transformation classes. A set F counts as a transformation class under three conditions: each transformation is uniquely identified by a parameter vector of length λ(F); a class function φ_F(σ, p) maps a parameter vector and a point to the image point; and there is a minimal basis size β(F) with an upper bound τ(F) such that, for any pair of length-β bases of distinct points, at most τ transformations map the first onto the second. The five concrete classes are: translation F_2T with λ=2, β=1, τ=1; translation plus reflection in the x-axis (inversion) F_2TR with λ=3, β=1, τ=2; x-axis scaling plus translation F_2ST with λ=3, β=2, τ=1 (a negative scale factor is retrograde); F_2STR, adding reflection, with λ=4, β=2, τ=2, covering the full set of traditional contrapuntal transformations; and F_2STRmodμ, which takes the pitch axis modulo μ, μ=7 for diatonic and μ=12 for chromatic. Each class gets a basis-pair transformation set function, some with an algorithm.

Matches and maximal patterns. The maximal match of P in Q for a transformation f is the image of the points of P that f maps into Q, and the maximal pattern is those points themselves. For a bijection f, the paper proves the maximal pattern equals P ∩ f⁻¹(Q), and Theorem 4.1 gives the full correspondence between maximal matches and maximal patterns under f and f⁻¹. If f's maximal pattern is non-empty and not the whole dataset, f defines a partial symmetry on the dataset.

Occurrence sets and compression factor. The occurrence set of pattern P in dataset D with respect to F is every f(P) that lies inside D. It can be encoded as an ordered pair ⟨P, Ψ⟩, where Ψ holds the lexicographically least parameter vector for each occurrence. That costs k|P| + λ|OS| values, against k|OS||P| for listing every point. The compression factor is k|COV| divided by the former, where COV is the union of all occurrences. In the paper's example, a 5-point pattern has 4 occurrences under F_2STR: listing points takes 40 values, the ordered pair only 26, a compression factor of about 1.54. Why not let P be one of the occurrences and save λ values? Because the query pattern need not lie in D, and the user usually wants exactly the query-to-occurrence transformations.

Heterogeneous occurrence sets. Different occurrences may use different transformation classes, each taking the class with the shortest parameter vector, at the cost of one extra value per class in the denominator. Re-encoding the original example this way gives 40/(10+3+13), still 1.54, no better. After adding two occurrences that are plain translations, the homogeneous F_2STR encoding gives 60/34 ≈ 1.76 and the heterogeneous one 60/30 = 2.0. In essence this is a minimum-description-length preference: whatever a simple transformation can explain should not be charged to a complex one.

Transformational equivalence and groups. If F forms a transformation group (closure, associativity, a unique identity, invertibility), "being transforms of each other" is an equivalence relation, and an occurrence set is a set of transformationally equivalent patterns. F_2T and F_2TR are groups. F_2ST, F_2STR and F_2STRmodμ are not, because a scale factor of 0 collapses the whole pattern onto a single onset time and has no inverse; an occurrence set holding such an occurrence alongside ordinary ones is not an equivalence set. With 0 excluded, F′_2ST, F′_2STR and F′_2STRmodμ are proved to be groups (Lemmas 7–9). This also accounts for a step in the earlier Ravel analysis: in the CPO representation, occurrences 3, 4, 8 and 9 form a set of transformationally equivalent patterns with respect to F_2STR.

限制Limitations

沒有任何實證或複雜度分析。 全文只有定義、引理與小型手算例子，沒有在任何資料集上執行，也沒有分析比對或發現演算法的時間複雜度；基底對變換集合函數是常數時間的小程式，但真正耗時的部分是窮舉基底對與驗證其餘點，論文沒有碰。對要實作的人來說，框架告訴你「該算什麼」，卻沒有告訴你「算得動嗎」。

只有精確比對。 所有定義都要求 f(P) 完全落在 D 裡，沒有容差。真實演奏的時值不精確、轉譜帶有錯誤、變奏常有裝飾或增刪音符，這些都會讓精確比對失手。作者也明說，一個變換是點對點的函數，所以刪除點、或對不同點用不同縮放的映射，都無法在框架中直接表示。此外只有 x 方向縮放與 x 軸反射，沒有音高方向的縮放（例如音程擴張）。

定義內部有一處沒處理乾淨的地方。 定義 2 要求對所有基底對，變換數都不超過 τ。但在 F_2ST 與 F_2STR 中，如果兩個物件基底點起音相同（例如同一個和弦裡的兩個音），像點也起音相同，那麼任何縮放因子都能把前者映到後者，變換數是無限的。演算法（圖 21–23 的註解寫著「σ[0] = 0/0」）直接把縮放因子設成 1，這是一個實用的慣例，卻跟 τ(F_2ST) = 1 的定義並不相符，文中沒有討論它對比對完整性的影響：若實作恰好選了同時發聲的基底，就可能漏掉經過增減值的出現處，除非另外約定改選非同時的基底。

同樣奇怪的是，縮放因子 0 在音樂上沒有意義（整個樣式被壓成一個和弦），F_2ST 等類別卻先把它納入，到第 6 節才為了得到群而排除；一開始就定義 s ≠ 0 會簡單得多。

No empirical results and no complexity analysis. The whole paper consists of definitions, lemmas and small hand-worked examples. Nothing is run on any dataset, and the time complexity of matching or discovery algorithms is not analysed. The basis-pair transformation set functions are constant-time routines, but the expensive part, enumerating basis pairs and verifying the remaining points, is never addressed. For an implementer, the framework says what to compute but not whether it can be computed in practice.

Exact matching only. Every definition requires f(P) to lie entirely inside D, with no tolerance. Imprecise performed timing, transcription errors, and variations with ornaments or added and dropped notes all defeat exact matching. The paper also states that a transformation is a point-to-point function, so mappings that delete points or scale different points differently cannot be represented directly. And there is only x-axis scaling and x-axis reflection, with no scaling along the pitch axis (interval expansion, for example).

One part of the definitions is not cleanly handled. Definition 2 requires at most τ transformations for every basis pair. But in F_2ST and F_2STR, if the two object-basis points share an onset (two notes of one chord, say) and so do the image points, then every scale factor maps one onto the other, and the number of transformations is infinite. The algorithms (whose comments in Figures 21–23 read "σ[0] = 0/0") simply set the scale factor to 1. That is a workable convention, but it does not fit the definition τ(F_2ST) = 1, and the paper does not discuss what it means for completeness of matching. An implementation that happens to choose a simultaneous basis could miss augmented or diminished occurrences unless it is also agreed to pick non-simultaneous bases instead.

Equally odd, a scale factor of 0 means nothing musically (the whole pattern collapses into one chord), yet F_2ST and the others include it at first and exclude it only in Section 6 to get a group. Defining s ≠ 0 from the start would have been much simpler.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值在於統一詞彙，而不在新結果。對做點集樣式發現的人來說，有兩件事值得帶走。第一，「互為變換」是不是等價關係取決於變換類是否構成群，這決定了把出現處分群的結果是否與起點無關；在用增減值、逆行做分析時，要記得排除縮放因子 0。第二，異質出現集合把「用最簡單的變換解釋每個出現處」寫進了壓縮率，這跟 COSIATEC 以壓縮率挑選樣式的思路一致，也替「分析即壓縮」這條路線提供了比較細緻的計分方式。

但如果要讓這個框架在 MIR 實務上站穩，下一步得補三件事：比對與發現的複雜度分析，以及在大資料集上的實際運行；在 JKU 樣式發現資料集（JKU-PDD）這類標準評測上，跟只做平移的 SIATEC 系列正面比較；以及某種近似比對，讓框架能面對演奏 MIDI 與轉譜錯誤。另外也值得把它與 Lewin 的轉換理論和幾何雜湊文獻接上，這篇目前幾乎是一個自我封閉的體系。

The value of this paper is a unified vocabulary rather than new results. People working on point-set pattern discovery can take away two things. First, whether "being transforms of each other" is an equivalence relation depends on whether the transformation class is a group, which decides whether grouping occurrences is independent of the starting point; when analysing with augmentation, diminution or retrograde, remember to exclude a scale factor of 0. Second, heterogeneous occurrence sets write "explain each occurrence with the simplest transformation" into the compression factor. That fits COSIATEC's idea of selecting patterns by compression and gives the analysis-as-compression line a finer scoring rule.

For the framework to stand up in MIR practice, though, three things need adding: complexity analysis of matching and discovery, with actual runs on large datasets; a head-to-head comparison with the translation-only SIATEC family on a standard benchmark such as the JKU Patterns Development Dataset (JKU-PDD); and some form of approximate matching, so the framework can handle performed MIDI and transcription errors.
