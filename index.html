Adapting a Latent Audio Diffusion Model to Historical Guqin Recordings: A Listening-Driven Case Study

把潛空間音訊擴散模型改造成古琴電台:用 42.5 小時歷史錄音微調 Stable Audio 3,並由一位專家盲聽七輪來決定每個設計選擇的個案研究

Huanchen Cai
arXiv:2610.07486 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：用 412 首歷史古琴獨奏錄音(42.5 小時)在 Stable Audio 3 Medium 上訓練一個 rank-16 DoRA 轉接器,七輪盲聽都由同一位專家打分。最終版本接續沒聽過的曲子拿到 3.88/5(先前最佳轉接器 3.38),從自由撰寫的場景描述生成拿到 4.60/5;同一批潛向量上從頭訓練的自迴歸模型 11 段全部拿最低分。作者自己也說,只有一位聽者、每個條件最多十段,沒有任何成對差異達到統計顯著。Why this one: A rank-16 DoRA adapter is trained on Stable Audio 3 Medium using 412 historical solo guqin recordings (42.5 h), with every decision made through seven blind listening studies by the same expert. The final version scores 3.88/5 for continuing unseen pieces (3.38 for the best earlier adapter) and 4.60/5 for generating from free-written scene descriptions; a from-scratch autoregressive model over the same latents received the lowest score on all 11 clips. As the author states, with one listener and at most ten clips per condition, no paired difference is statistically significant.

動機Motivation

目標是一台「AI 電台」:無止盡地播放古琴風格的音樂,可以接續一段錄音的開頭,也可以照一句場景描述直接生成。古琴難做在於它的聲響習慣跟主流訓練資料不同——散音、泛音、按音三類音色,左手吟猱綽注帶出的經過半音與摩擦聲是風格的一部分而不是錯誤;曲目以五聲音階為主;最有名的演奏多半是二十世紀中期帶磁帶嘶聲、頻寬有限的錄音。

主流音樂生成模型主要用西方流行與庫存音樂訓練,對「哪裡該有半音、哪裡該有摩擦聲」沒有好的先驗。作者也發現開放授權的古琴音訊加起來不到一小時,所以問題直接變成:幾十小時、無法公開的歷史錄音,加上一位懂古琴的聽者,能把一個預訓練大模型推到多遠。

The goal is an 'AI radio' that plays guqin-style music without end, either continuing a recorded opening or generating directly from a scene description. The guqin is hard because its sonic idiom differs from mainstream training data: three timbre families (open strings, harmonics, stopped notes), passing semitones and friction noise from left-hand slides and vibrato that are part of the style rather than errors, a predominantly pentatonic repertoire, and best-known performances that survive as mid-twentieth-century recordings with tape hiss and limited bandwidth.

Mainstream music generators are trained mostly on Western popular and library music and have no good prior for where semitones and friction noise belong. The author also found that openly licensed guqin audio totals under an hour, so the question becomes how far tens of hours of non-redistributable historical recordings plus one guqin-literate listener can push a large pretrained model.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

古琴的計算研究過去集中在分析而非生成。Penttinen 等(2006)用交換式數位波導(commuted digital waveguide)做出物理模型合成器,能重現非諧性、滑音、泛音與手指摩擦聲;Huang 等(2020,ISMIR)在 GQ39 歷史錄音上聯合建模調式與演奏技法;GuqinSonGest(Han 等 2025)提供依九世紀文獻整理的技法音訊與影像;另有一條線在做減字譜的影像辨識與轉譜。作者聲稱沒有已發表的工作用現代神經生成模型生成古琴音訊。與 Penttinen 的差別是:物理模型給的是可控的單音合成,這篇要的是會自己往下彈的整段音樂。

方法本身沒有新零件。DoRA(Liu 等 2024)是 LoRA 的權重分解變體;「偏向接續的遮罩」只是把 Stable Audio 3 官方訓練碼的遮罩混合比例從 (0.1, 0.8, 0.1) 改成 (0.1, 0.3, 0.6);從頭訓練的對照組就是 MAR(Li 等 2024)那種連續潛向量自迴歸加流匹配頭,再配上 Pasini 等(2024)的上下文加噪。最接近的類比其實是影像生成裡的 DreamBooth/LoRA 個人化:用少量資料把大模型的先驗拉到一個窄領域。這篇的貢獻在於把這套做法搬到一個資料稀少、聲響慣例明確的傳統樂器上,並把每一步的聆聽結果記下來,包括失敗的。

Computational guqin research has focused on analysis rather than generation. Penttinen et al. (2006) built a commuted-digital-waveguide physical model reproducing inharmonicity, glides, harmonics and finger friction; Huang et al. (2020, ISMIR) jointly modelled mode and playing technique on the GQ39 historical recordings; GuqinSonGest (Han et al. 2025) provides technique audio and video derived from a ninth-century treatise; another strand recognises and transcribes jianzipu tablature. The author states that no published work generates guqin audio with a modern neural generator. The difference from Penttinen is that a physical model gives controllable single-note synthesis, whereas this work wants whole passages that keep playing on their own.

The method has no new components. DoRA (Liu et al. 2024) is a weight-decomposed variant of LoRA; the 'continuation-weighted mask' just changes the mask mixture in Stable Audio 3's released training code from (0.1, 0.8, 0.1) to (0.1, 0.3, 0.6); the from-scratch baseline is MAR-style (Li et al. 2024) continuous-latent autoregression with a flow head plus Pasini et al.'s (2024) context noise. The closest analogue is DreamBooth/LoRA personalisation in image generation: pulling a large model's prior into a narrow domain with little data. The contribution is carrying that recipe to a data-poor traditional instrument with clear sonic conventions, and recording every listening result, failures included.

方法Method

資料:從 638 首個人收藏的 CD 轉錄錄音中,逐首整軌聆聽,剔除有底噪、合奏、掌聲與人聲的,剩 412 首獨奏(61 位演奏者,108 個曲名)。前五名演奏者約佔 40% 時長。切分以曲目為單位(329/42/41 首),確保評估時聽到的曲子沒有任何版本出現在訓練裡;最終模型用訓練加測試共 370 首,保留驗證集做早停與聆聽。

模型:凍結的自編碼器把 59.9 秒的立體聲窗口編成 645×256 的連續潛向量(約每秒 10.8 幀),生成器是以 rectified flow 訓練的擴散 transformer,推論時用蒸餾版 8 步。DoRA 轉接器 r=α=16,可訓練參數 21.6M,在非蒸餾的 base 模型上訓練、推論時套到蒸餾模型上。每個樣本隨機保留 20 秒到整窗長度的片段,其餘補靜音潛向量。

Data: from a personal collection of 638 CD-transferred recordings, every candidate was screened by whole-track listening and those with audible noise floors, ensembles, applause or voice were excluded, leaving 412 solo recordings (61 performers, 108 titles). The top five performers account for about 40% of the duration. Splits are by composition (329/42/41 recordings), so no version of an evaluated piece is heard in training; the final model trains on training plus test (370 recordings) and keeps validation for early stopping and listening.

Model: a frozen autoencoder maps 59.9 s stereo windows to 645×256 continuous latents (about 10.8 frames per second); the generator is a diffusion transformer trained with rectified flow, sampled with the distilled model in 8 steps. The DoRA adapter uses r=α=16 (21.6M trainable parameters), is trained on the non-distilled base model and applied to the distilled model at inference. Each sample keeps a random span from 20 s to the full window, padded with the latent of silence.

限制Limitations

最大的限制作者自己寫得很清楚:全部評分來自同一位聽者,研究是邊開發邊依序進行,後面的選擇依賴前面的評分;每個條件最多十段,所有比較都不顯著(最終 3.88 對 3.38,p=0.63;pass 14 的 bootstrap 95% 區間是 [2.75, 4.75],寬約兩分)。同一位聽者在不知情下重評 15 段,平均絕對差 0.8 分,跟模型間的差距一樣大;一段展示用的作品在後來一輪被打了 1 分。R1 裡初始的 seed 差異(2.17 對 3.75,相差 1.6 分)也比任何模型差異都大。

資料面:原本要保留的一位演奏者被誤放進 412 首訓練集,汙染了 R2;曲目說明有 22 個是只從曲名推出來的;沒有檢查是否複製訓練素材(Batlle-Roca 等 2024 指出的問題),而這在一個由 61 位演奏者、前五人佔 40% 時長的小語料上不是小事。錄音與轉接器權重因版權無法公開,外人無法重現聆聽結果,只能重現程式與數字。

The largest limitation is stated plainly by the author: all ratings come from one listener, the studies ran sequentially during development so later choices depended on earlier ratings, there are at most ten clips per condition, and no comparison is significant (final 3.88 vs 3.38, p=0.63; pass 14's bootstrap 95% interval is [2.75, 4.75], about two points wide). When the same listener unknowingly re-rated 15 clips the mean absolute difference was 0.8 points, as large as the differences between models, and a showcase take was rated 1 in a later session. In R1 the seed difference alone (2.17 vs 3.75, 1.6 points) exceeded any model difference.

On the data side: a performer planned as held-out was mistakenly included in the 412-recording training set, compromising R2; 22 piece narratives are inferred from the title alone; and replication of training material (the concern raised by Batlle-Roca et al. 2024) was not tested, which is not minor for a small corpus of 61 performers where the top five hold 40% of the duration. Recordings and adapter weights cannot be released for copyright reasons, so outsiders can reproduce the code and numbers but not the listening.

討論Discussion

最有用的結果是負面的那幾個。在同一批潛向量上從頭訓練的 72M 參數自迴歸模型,11 段全部拿 1 分(「完全沒有音色」),同樣開頭下 SA3 轉接器是 2.0;驗證損失在 10k 步後反而上升 8%。作者的解讀是:幾十小時的領域裡,問題不是選哪種架構從頭訓練,而是選哪個預訓練先驗來改——音色是先驗帶進來的,聽者的抱怨都在旋律與雜音,不在樂器本身。

兩個自動指標都失敗了。針對「鋸木聲」設計的雜訊幀比例跟分數呈反方向相關(48 段上 Spearman ρ=+0.39),被標記為鋸木聲的 9 段反而數值較低;五聲音階貼合度整體追得上分數(124 段 ρ=0.52),但在同一開頭的候選之間只剩 0.31,拿它挑最佳候選只讓平均從 3.41 升到 3.52。聽者的說法是古琴的慣用半音出現在滑音中而非起音處,所以需要的是技法感知的分析,而不是幀層級統計。

文字條件的效果也很具體:對接續沒有影響(配對與刻意相反的標籤都拿 4.0),只在沒有開頭時有用——加入情緒標籤訓練後純文字生成從 2.0 升到 3.8(p=0.06,n=5)。驗證損失從未微調的 0.6020 只降到 0.5950(1.2%),晚期各輪差在 10⁻⁵ 量級,但 pass 14 是全專案評分最高的模型。

長時間播放暴露出兩個工程問題:每段生成結尾約有 0.5 秒數位靜音,接回去當上下文會讓模型在每個接縫再插兩秒靜音;每段都比上下文稍大聲,六輪後從 −10 升到 −5.5 dBFS、最多 10% 取樣削波。修剪尾巴並鎖定電平後,一段 3.5 分鐘的作品維持在 −13.8 到 −16.7 dBFS、無削波。剩下的是音樂上的問題:兩條鎖定電平的鏈中有一條在 82 秒後突然崩掉。

The most useful results are the negative ones. A 72M-parameter autoregressive model trained from scratch on the same latents received a 1 on all 11 clips ('no timbre at all'), against 2.0 for the SA3 adapter on the same openings, and its validation loss rose 8% after 10k steps. The author's reading is that in a domain of tens of hours, the question is not which architecture to train but which prior to adapt: the timbre comes from the prior, and the listener's complaints were about melody and noise, not the instrument.

Both automatic measures failed. The noisy-frame fraction aimed at 'sawing' correlated with score in the wrong direction (Spearman ρ=+0.39 over 48 clips), and the nine clips tagged as sawing had lower values. Pentatonic fit tracked scores overall (ρ=0.52 over 124 clips) but only 0.31 among candidates for the same opening, and selecting by it raised the mean only from 3.41 to 3.52. The listener's account, that idiomatic semitones occur inside slides rather than at the pluck, calls for technique-aware analysis rather than frame statistics.

Text conditioning had a specific effect: none on continuation (matching and deliberately opposite tags both scored 4.0), and a gain only when there was no opening, where training with mood tags raised text-only generation from 2.0 to 3.8 (p=0.06, n=5). Validation loss fell only from 0.6020 unadapted to 0.5950 (1.2%), with late-pass differences of order 10⁻⁵, yet pass 14 was the best-rated model of the project.

Long playback exposed two engineering problems. Every clip ends in about 0.5 s of digital silence, which fed back as context made the model insert two more seconds of silence at each seam; and each segment came out slightly louder than its context, rising from −10 to −5.5 dBFS over six rounds with up to 10% of samples clipped. Trimming the tail and locking the level kept a 3.5-minute piece between −13.8 and −16.7 dBFS without clipping. What remains is musical: one of two locked chains broke down suddenly after 82 s.

Unheard but Recognizable: Ultrasonic Signatures for Musical Instrument Recognition

聽不到但認得出:用 96 kSPS 多軌錄音與頻寬匹配的低通、純超音波對照,檢驗 20 kHz 以上的成分能不能幫樂器辨識

Izhak Kapash, Uri Rom, Ram Zamir
arXiv:2610.08850 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-03
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：MIR 資料集幾乎都是 44.1/48 kSPS,20 kHz 以上的資訊在模型看到之前就被丟了。這篇錄了一套原生 96 kSPS、15 類的多軌語料,並用同一批錄音做出 22.05 kHz 低通對照:隨機森林的 macro-F1 從低通的 82.26% 升到全頻寬的 88.38%,CNN 準確率從 87% 升到 91%;只留 20–48 kHz 的輸入在 12 類上仍有約 82% 準確率。語料只有六首曲子,而且各類別的錄音條件是否與類別混淆,論文的控制還不夠完整。Why this one: Almost every MIR dataset is 44.1/48 kSPS, so information above 20 kHz is discarded before any model sees it. This paper records a native 96 kSPS, 15-class multitrack corpus and builds a 22.05 kHz low-pass control from the same takes: random-forest macro-F1 rises from 82.26% low-passed to 88.38% full-band, CNN accuracy from 87% to 91%, and input restricted to 20–48 kHz still reaches about 82% accuracy over 12 classes. The corpus has only six pieces, and the controls do not fully rule out recording conditions being confounded with class.

動機Motivation

樂器辨識是轉譜、分離、索引的基礎任務,但頻譜相似的樂器在混音裡仍然難分。MedleyDB、MUSDB18 這類常用資料集都是 44.1 或 48 kSPS,奈奎斯特極限 22.05/24 kHz,所以 20 kHz 以上有沒有用,從來沒被測過——資料在進模型前就沒了。

物理上,樂器的超音波成分來自三個機制:泛音列延伸到 20 kHz 以上、暫態激發產生的寬頻高頻能量、金屬打擊樂器的類雜訊輻射。作者先前一篇只做吉他的初步研究發現有幫助,這篇擴大到 15 類、多個錄音室與演奏者、複音混音,並加上頻寬匹配的對照。關鍵的方法論觀察是:直接比 44.1 對 96 kSPS 無法把效果歸給超音波,因為取樣率改變的不只是頻寬,還有樣本數。

Instrument recognition underpins transcription, separation and indexing, yet spectrally similar instruments remain hard to separate in mixtures. Common datasets such as MedleyDB and MUSDB18 are 44.1 or 48 kSPS, with Nyquist limits of 22.05/24 kHz, so whether content above 20 kHz helps has never been tested: it is gone before the model sees it.

Physically, ultrasonic content in instruments comes from three mechanisms: harmonic series extending past 20 kHz, broadband high-frequency energy from transient excitation, and noise-like radiation from metallic percussion. The authors' earlier guitar-only study found a benefit; this one extends to 15 classes, several studios and performers, polyphonic mixtures, and bandwidth-matched controls. The key methodological point is that comparing 44.1 against 96 kSPS directly cannot attribute a gain to ultrasound, because the sampling rate changes sample count as well as bandwidth.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

樂器超音波的物理量測早就有:Boyk(1992)測過多種樂器在 20 kHz 以上的頻譜延伸,論文的「早期單一音源量測」指的就是這類工作;Oohashi 等(2000)的「超高頻效應」(hypersonic effect)主張聽不到的高頻會影響聆聽者的腦部反應,但那是感知爭議,本文只在結尾一筆帶過。差別在於:這些研究量的是「有沒有」,這篇問的是「對學習出來的辨識器有沒有用」。

樂器辨識這邊,IRMAS(Bosch 等 2012)與 Han 等(2017)的 CNN 主導樂器辨識、OpenMIC-2018 都在 44.1 kHz 以下工作;本文的 CNN6 取自 PANNs(Kong 等 2020),但從頭訓練、輸入改成 96 kSPS 的線性 STFT。這篇宣稱首創的東西,方法上最像的其實是頻帶遮蔽/子頻帶消融——在語音防偽領域很常見,例如 Tak 等(2020)用子頻帶分類器發現高頻帶藏著攻擊線索。這裡做的就是同一種「把某一段頻帶拿掉看掉多少」的分析,只是把頻帶延伸到奈奎斯特以外,而這需要先有原生高取樣率的錄音。

Physical measurements of instrumental ultrasound are old: Boyk (1992) measured spectral extension above 20 kHz for many instruments, the kind of 'earlier single-source measurements' the paper extends; Oohashi et al.'s (2000) 'hypersonic effect' claims inaudible high frequencies affect listeners' brain responses, a perceptual controversy the paper mentions only in passing. The difference is that those studies measure whether the content exists, whereas this one asks whether it helps a learned recogniser.

On instrument recognition, IRMAS (Bosch et al. 2012), Han et al.'s (2017) CNN for predominant-instrument recognition and OpenMIC-2018 all operate at or below 44.1 kHz; the paper's CNN6 comes from PANNs (Kong et al. 2020), trained from scratch on linear STFTs at 96 kSPS. Methodologically, what the paper presents as new is closest to band occlusion or sub-band ablation, common in speech anti-spoofing, where e.g. Tak et al. (2020) used sub-band classifiers to find attack cues in high bands. The same 'remove a band and see what drops' analysis is done here, only extended beyond Nyquist, which first requires native high-rate recordings.

方法Method

錄音:Earthworks QTC50(標稱 3 Hz–50 kHz)與 QTC30(3 Hz–30 kHz)全指向麥克風,經 Soundcraft 混音台、MOTU 2408 MK3 轉換器與 UA Apollo Twin,在 Cubase 以 96 kSPS 原生錄製,整條鏈沒有 20 kHz 截止。每個音源在隔音條件下近距離單獨收音,不加效果與動態處理。語料有六首完整多軌作品(兩首爵士、兩首藍調/搖滾、一首安達魯西亞、一首小調抒情曲),超過一小時混音、近 15 小時對齊的單軌,15 類包含卡農琴(kanun)、班蘇里笛(bansuri)、人聲等。

對照條件全部從同一批 96 kSPS 錄音數位產生:降到 44.1 kSPS、96 kSPS 但 22.05 kHz 低通、只留 20–48 kHz。切分在切窗之前、以整段錄音為單位,三種頻寬共用同一切分;固定訓練 50 epoch,不用測試集挑 checkpoint。

兩種分類器:CNN6(32 ms Hann、8 ms hop、1537 個頻率 bin 的對數功率線性 STFT,四個 5×5 卷積塊,512 維嵌入);隨機森林(200 棵樹,特徵是到 40 kHz 為止每 2 kHz 一個頻帶的相對能量,加上質心、頻寬、滾降、平坦度等統計,刻意不用會壓縮高頻的 MFCC)。另有複音混音中選定音源的 ON/OFF 多標籤偵測,以及只用 20–48 kHz 的 12 類分類。

Recording: Earthworks QTC50 (rated 3 Hz–50 kHz) and QTC30 (3 Hz–30 kHz) omnidirectional microphones, through a Soundcraft console, MOTU 2408 MK3 converter and UA Apollo Twin, recorded natively at 96 kSPS in Cubase with no 20 kHz cutoff anywhere in the chain. Each source was close-miked separately under isolated conditions without effects or dynamics. The corpus has six complete multitrack pieces (two jazz, two blues/rock-and-roll, one Andalusian, one minor-key ballad), over an hour of mixtures and nearly 15 hours of aligned stems, across 15 classes including kanun, bansuri and vocals.

All conditions are derived digitally from the same 96 kSPS takes: resampled to 44.1 kSPS, 96 kSPS low-passed at 22.05 kHz, and 20–48 kHz only. Splits are by complete take, made before windowing and shared across bandwidth conditions; training runs a fixed 50 epochs with no checkpoint selection on held-out takes.

Two classifiers: CNN6 (log-power linear STFT, 32 ms Hann, 8 ms hop, 1537 bins; four 5×5 blocks; 512-d embedding) and a 200-tree random forest on relative energy in 2 kHz bands up to 40 kHz plus centroid, bandwidth, roll-off, flatness and similar statistics, deliberately avoiding MFCCs that compress the high range. Further tasks are ON/OFF multi-label detection of selected sources in polyphonic mixtures and 12-class classification on 20–48 kHz alone.

限制Limitations

最需要追問的是類別與錄音條件的混淆。不同場次用了不同演奏者、樂器、錄音室與麥克風,而兩支麥克風的標稱上限差很多(30 kHz 對 50 kHz)。如果某些類別主要用 QTC30 錄、某些用 QTC50,超音波頻段的形狀本身就會洩漏「是哪一場、哪支麥克風」,純超音波 82% 的準確率可能有一部分在認錄音條件。論文的兩個控制不完全擋得住這點:「安靜窗口接近機率水準」只排除了穩態底噪或鏈路特徵,不排除麥克風對有聲音訊號的頻響差異;「只用 QTC50 的一整場重現了相同相對模式」是描述性的頻譜觀察,沒有拿來重做分類。

規模也小:六首曲子、每類別訓練與評估都只來自少數錄音,三個 seed 的標準差最多 1 個百分點只代表可重複,作者自己也說不是母體顯著性。複音表格裡也有取捨被一筆帶過,例如古典吉他的精確率從 68.02% 升到 92.74%,召回率卻從 96.89% 降到 84.87%。

應用面的限制論文談得少:實際流通的音樂幾乎都是 44.1/48 kHz,許多「高解析」發行是升頻或經過低通的母帶,這個結果對既有音樂庫的檢索幾乎用不上,主要意義在新錄音與典藏的錄製規格。另外,隨機森林在 44.1 kSPS 條件下 22 kHz 以上的頻帶特徵全是空的,兩個條件的特徵維度實質不同,這點沒有討論。

The question most worth pressing is confounding between class and recording conditions. Sessions used different performers, instruments, studios and microphones, and the two microphones' rated limits differ widely (30 kHz vs 50 kHz). If some classes were recorded mainly on the QTC30 and others on the QTC50, the shape of the ultrasonic band alone would leak which session and microphone was used, so part of the 82% ultrasonic-only accuracy could be recognising recording conditions. The paper's two controls do not fully close this: near-chance performance on quiet windows rules out a stationary noise floor or chain signature, not microphone response differences on active signal; and 'a complete QTC50-only session reproduced the same relative patterns' is a descriptive spectral observation that was not used to re-run classification.

Scale is also small: six pieces, with each class's training and evaluation drawn from few recordings; a three-seed SD of at most one point indicates repeatability, not population significance, as the authors say. Trade-offs in the polyphonic table pass with little comment, e.g. classical-guitar precision rises from 68.02% to 92.74% while recall falls from 96.89% to 84.87%.

The applied limits get little discussion: nearly all circulating music is 44.1/48 kHz, and many 'hi-res' releases are upsampled or low-passed masters, so the result barely applies to retrieval over existing catalogues; its main relevance is to recording specifications for new and archival material. Also, at 44.1 kSPS the random forest's band features above 22 kHz are all empty, so the two conditions effectively differ in feature dimension, which is not discussed.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的設計是那個低通對照。隨機森林 macro-F1:全頻寬 96 kSPS 88.38%、96 kSPS 低通 82.26%、44.1 kSPS 81.11%。低通與 44.1 只差 1.15 點,全頻寬比低通高 6.12 點,所以提升可以歸給保留下來的頻寬而不是取樣率本身;CNN 也一樣,低通後準確率回到 44.1 的約 87%。

各類別的差異很符合物理直覺。全頻寬相對低通,人聲 +17.1 F1、小號與薩克斯風各 +9.9、鼓 +8.7、卡農琴 +5.79;Table 1 的超音波活動機率(PUA)在鋼琴與電吉他都是 0.00,最高偵測頻率 16.2 與 17.1 kHz。有意思的是電吉他與鋼琴的 F1 也上升(電吉他 82.31%→94.44%),作者的解釋是「沒有超音波」本身也是可區分的特徵——這正是它跟木吉他、古典吉他分開的依據。

複音偵測整體準確率提升約 4 個百分點,但效果依音源而異:鼓已飽和在 99% 以上;薩克斯風準確率 +8.70 點、人聲 +4.60;木吉他召回率從 60.27% 升到 82.13%。純超音波 12 類約 82%(機率水準 8.33%),鼓、卡農琴、薩克斯風的類別 F1 超過 90%,長笛與單簧管接近 70%。

結尾的 NMF 分離案例(一段打擊樂摘錄,SDR 從 −4.07 到 2.15 dB)作者自己標為「嚴格初步」,不該當成分離能力的證據。比較紮實的建議是錄製端的:96 kSPS 儲存量是 44.1 的 2.18 倍,但可以從寬頻母帶降出一般版本,反過來被濾掉的超音波無法復原。

The paper's most valuable design is the low-pass control. Random-forest macro-F1 is 88.38% at full-band 96 kSPS, 82.26% at 96 kSPS low-passed, and 81.11% at 44.1 kSPS. Low-pass and 44.1 differ by only 1.15 points while full-band beats low-pass by 6.12, so the gain can be attributed to the retained band rather than the sampling rate; likewise the CNN returns to about 87% after low-passing, the 44.1 level.

Per-class differences fit physical intuition. Full-band over low-pass gains +17.1 F1 for vocals, +9.9 each for trumpet and saxophone, +8.7 for drums and +5.79 for kanun; in Table 1 the probability of ultrasonic activity (PUA) is 0.00 for both piano and electric guitar, with highest detected frequencies of 16.2 and 17.1 kHz. Interestingly electric guitar and piano F1 also rise (electric guitar 82.31%→94.44%); the authors' explanation is that absence of ultrasound is itself discriminative, which is exactly what separates it from acoustic and classical guitars.

Polyphonic detection improves overall accuracy by about four points, with source-dependent effects: drums are saturated above 99%; saxophone accuracy gains 8.70 points and vocals 4.60; acoustic-guitar recall rises from 60.27% to 82.13%. Ultrasonic-only 12-class accuracy is about 82% (chance 8.33%), with drums, kanun and saxophone above 90% class-wise F1 and flute and clarinet near 70%.

The closing NMF separation case (one percussion excerpt, SDR from −4.07 to 2.15 dB) is labelled 'strictly preliminary' by the authors and should not be read as evidence of separation ability. The firmer recommendation is on the recording side: 96 kSPS needs 2.18 times the storage of 44.1, but conventional copies can be derived from a wideband master, whereas filtered ultrasound cannot be restored.

Structural and Representational Agency in LLM-Driven Timbre Generation and Interpolation

LLM 驅動的音色生成與內插介面裡,使用者「能造成什麼」與「能知道什麼」是兩回事:把可達集合寫成 zonotope,並用實際渲染量測稽核介面顯示的位置

Joseph M. Cameron, Alan F. Blackwell
arXiv:2610.12532 · cs.SD; cs.HC; cs.LG; cs.MM; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-08
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：一個讓 LLM 依文字產生合成器音色、再讓使用者在畫布上內插的工具,畫面上顯示的「你在七維感知空間的位置」其實是 LLM 從提示詞猜的,不是從音訊量出來的。作者拿實際渲染去量:顯示位置的誤差約是使用者可達區域寬度的三倍(0.485–0.649 對 0.159–0.173);36 次「讓它更亮」之類的指令有 26 次沒有超過雜訊底線的變化,其中較弱模型因為解析失敗把當前位置當目標,什麼都沒做卻回報收斂。Why this one: In a tool where an LLM produces synthesiser patches from text and the user interpolates between them on a canvas, the displayed 'position in a seven-dimensional perceptual space' is the LLM's guess from the prompt, not a measurement of the audio. Measured against renders, the displayed position is off by about three times the width of the region the user can reach (0.485–0.649 vs 0.159–0.173); 26 of 36 instructions such as 'make it brighter' produced no change above the noise floor, and under the weaker model a parse failure returned the current position as the target, so the system did nothing while reporting convergence.

動機Motivation

生成式創作工具常會告訴使用者「你現在在可能性空間的哪裡」,這種顯示隱含一個保證:數值是從作品本身量出來的。但越來越多系統其實是叫語言模型描述它自己產出的東西,再把這個描述畫成看起來跟量測一模一樣的座標。這麼做很便宜(不用渲染、抽特徵、校準),又在介面上無從分辨,而大部分創作領域根本沒有公認的量測可以拿來檢查。

音訊是例外:有可以客觀量測的感知描述子。作者把自己做的工具 Timbre Canvas 當成量測這種做法的儀器,問三個問題:使用者能到達輸出空間的哪個區域;介面回報的位置對不對;一句自然語言的修正指令有沒有讓音訊產生對應的變化。

Generative creative tools often tell users where they are in a space of possibilities, and such displays carry an implicit warrant that the numbers were measured from the artefact. Increasingly, though, systems prompt a language model to describe its own output and render that description as coordinates indistinguishable from a measurement. This is cheap (no rendering, feature extraction or calibration) and invisible in the interface, and most creative domains lack an agreed measurement to check it against.

Audio is the exception, since it has objectively measurable perceptual descriptors. The authors treat their own tool, Timbre Canvas, as an instrument for measuring this practice and ask three questions: what region of the output space the user can reach; whether the interface reports the user's position correctly; and whether a natural-language refinement instruction produces a corresponding change in the audio.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

HCI 這邊,混合主動互動(Horvitz 1999)與 Amershi 等(2019)的人機互動準則把問題定為「系統該主動到什麼程度、行為要可理解」;Louie 等(2020)為生成音樂做的 AI-steering 工具給使用者明確的控制把手並問他們是否覺得有控制感。作者的區分是:這些工作把 agency 當成單一量(使用者能造成什麼),而這篇另外問使用者能不能知道自己造成了什麼。「模型回報自己的輸出」這點,最接近的是 LLM 當評審的偏誤研究(Zheng 等 2023)與思維鏈不忠實的研究(Turpin 等 2023,本文未引)——都在說模型對自己的描述不等於實際發生的事;差別是這裡的描述對象是模型根本感知不到的模態。

音色內插介面本身不新。Esling 等的 Flow Synthesizer(2019)與生成式音色空間(ISMIR 2018)從音訊學一個感知/潛空間,讓使用者在其中移動,座標是從渲染學來的;這裡的座標卻是 LLM 從文字猜的。另外,論文強調的 zonotope 刻畫,數學上就是「每條邊貢獻一段線段、全部做 Minkowski 和」的標準結果——因為權重正規化常數恰好固定為邊數,混音是 bead 位置的仿射函數,可達集合自然是 zonotope。這是一個乾淨的觀察,但不是新數學;它真正的用處是讓「目標離可達集合多遠」不用渲染就能算。

On the HCI side, mixed-initiative interaction (Horvitz 1999) and Amershi et al.'s (2019) human-AI guidelines frame the problem as calibrating initiative and making behaviour interpretable; Louie et al.'s (2020) AI-steering tools for generative music give users explicit handles and ask whether they feel in control. The authors' distinction is that this work treats agency as one quantity (what the user can cause), whereas this paper also asks whether users can know what they are causing. For 'models reporting on their own output', the nearest work is LLM-as-judge bias (Zheng et al. 2023) and unfaithful chain-of-thought (Turpin et al. 2023, not cited here): both show a model's account of itself is not what actually happened; the difference here is that the model describes a modality it cannot perceive.

Timbre interpolation interfaces are not new. Esling et al.'s Flow Synthesizer (2019) and generative timbre spaces (ISMIR 2018) learn a perceptual or latent space from audio for users to move through, with coordinates learned from renders; here the coordinates are guessed by an LLM from text. The zonotope characterisation the paper stresses is, mathematically, the standard result that each edge contributes one segment and the reachable set is their Minkowski sum: because the weight normaliser is fixed at the number of edges, the mix is affine in the bead positions. It is a clean observation rather than new mathematics; its real use is that the distance from a target to the reachable set can be computed without rendering.

方法Method

系統:使用者輸入文字,LLM 回傳商用軟體合成器 Sylenth1 的完整參數組,成為畫布上的一個頂點;使用者在頂點間畫邊,每條邊上有一顆 bead,位置 t∈[0,1] 決定兩端的混合比例;實際聽到的聲音是各頂點合成器參數的加權內插。修正指令(例如「讓它更亮」)不會生成新聲音,而是由 LLM 在七維 AudioCommons 空間(亮度、深度、硬度、粗糙度、溫暖度、尖銳度、轟鳴度)提出一個目標,再用座標下降調整現有邊上的 bead。作者把「模型只能在使用者建出的圖內部動作」稱為 enclosure。

量測設定:座標正規化到一個 10,292 個預設音色的參考語料所張成的 [0,1]⁷;語料的最近鄰距離中位數 0.0765 當解析度,兩個隨機預設平均相距 0.68。概念參考值來自 52 個單字音色概念、每個三段、由 12 位評分者 4 選 1 全部答對的 156 段範例。比較兩個模型:本地 gpt-oss:20b 與雲端 gemma4:31b-cloud。三個畫布(2、3、4 個聲音)都是第一作者在研究規劃前兩三個月日常使用時建的。

雜訊底線:同一組參數渲染六次,每維變動最多 0.074、平均 0.024;所有效果都按維度用這個底線做門檻。作者特別說明,早期版本的分析沒有這個門檻,因此高估了效果。

System: the user enters text, an LLM returns a full parameter set for the commercial software synthesiser Sylenth1, and the patch becomes a vertex on a canvas; the user draws edges between vertices, each with a bead at t∈[0,1] setting the blend of its endpoints; the audible sound is a weighted interpolation of the vertices' synthesiser parameters. A refinement instruction ('make it brighter') does not generate a new sound: the LLM proposes a target in the seven-dimensional AudioCommons space (brightness, depth, hardness, roughness, warmth, sharpness, boominess), and coordinate descent moves beads along existing edges. The authors call 'the model can only act inside the graph the user built' enclosure.

Measurement setup: coordinates are normalised to the [0,1]⁷ range spanned by a reference corpus of 10,292 presets; the corpus's median nearest-neighbour distance, 0.0765, serves as resolution, and two random presets differ by 0.68 on average. Concept references come from 156 exemplars, three for each of 52 single-word timbre concepts, each identified correctly by all 12 raters in a four-alternative forced choice. Two models are compared: local gpt-oss:20b and hosted gemma4:31b-cloud. The three canvases (2, 3 and 4 sounds) were built by the first author during ordinary use two to three months before the study was planned.

Noise floor: rendering identical parameters six times gives per-dimension variation up to 0.074, mean 0.024, and every effect is thresholded per dimension against it. The authors note that an earlier version of the analysis omitted this threshold and overstated the effect.

限制Limitations

沒有任何受試者:論文證明了顯示不忠實,沒證明使用者會被誤導,而後者才是 agency 主張真正需要的。樣本到處都小——三個畫布、九個頂點、36 次端到端試驗、52 個概念、兩個模型——而且只有一個系統、一個合成器、一個特徵空間。三個畫布與工具都出自作者本人,是自我稽核。

「參考值」是 AudioCommons 的迴歸模型輸出,不是人的感知判斷。作者的辯護是系統的最佳化器本來就以它為目標,所以在內部比較上是合適的參照;但「顯示誤差是可達範圍的三倍」這種跨系統的說法,會受到這個迴歸模型本身誤差的影響。

「36 次中 26 次沒變化」的數字要拆開看:較弱模型貢獻了其中 17 次,而原因是解析失敗後的回退路徑把當前位置當成目標——這比較像一個實作缺陷,作者把它提升為「設計模式」的說法有道理但略強。較強模型下 18 次中 9 次有可感知變化、其中 8 次方向正確,這個數字其實沒那麼糟。

There are no participants: the paper shows the display is unfaithful, not that users are misled, and the latter is what the agency claim ultimately needs. Samples are small throughout (three canvases, nine vertices, 36 end-to-end trials, 52 concepts, two models), covering one system, one synthesiser and one feature space. The canvases and the tool are the authors' own, so this is a self-audit.

The 'reference values' are AudioCommons regression outputs, not human perceptual judgements. The authors' defence is that the system's optimiser targets them, which makes them the right internal reference; but cross-system statements such as 'display error is three times the reachable extent' inherit the regression models' own error.

The '26 of 36 with no change' figure should be split: the weaker model contributes 17 of them, and the cause is a fallback path that returns the current position as the target after a parse failure, which looks more like an implementation defect; the authors' framing of it as a design pattern is reasonable but somewhat strong. Under the capable model, 9 of 18 instructions produced a perceptible change and 8 of those 9 went in the requested direction, which is less bleak.

討論Discussion

可達性:N 個聲音的可達區域仿射維度是 min(N−1, 7),每多放一個聲音剛好多一維;作者用工具本身的混音實作驗證閉式解,誤差 1.1×10⁻¹⁶。三個日常畫布分別只張成 7 維中的 1、2、3 維;抽樣 3,000 個預設,落在可達集合語料解析度以內的分別是 0、4、6 個,中位數預設離可達集合 0.51、0.40、0.37;全連通 12 個聲音的畫布也只碰到 2.3% 的預設。固定 6 個聲音、把邊從生成樹(5 條)加到完全圖(15 條),可達維度仍是 5.0,覆蓋率只在 11.9% 到 13.0% 間浮動——連線決定控制方式,只有放更多聲音才擴大範圍。

顯示誤差:三個畫布的顯示誤差 0.496、0.649、0.485,可達範圍寬度 0.159、0.173、0.163,比值 3.12×、3.75×、2.97×。作為對照,隨機預設距語料平均 0.50,所以接近 0.5 的誤差大約等於「直接報平均值」的資訊量。誤差拆成兩個獨立來源:頂點項(座標是預測而非量測)與線性項(混的是合成器參數,顯示的卻是感知向量的內插,兩組參數的平均不等於兩個聲音的平均)。

頂點項取決於模型:對 52 個概念,gpt-oss:20b 七維平均相關 r=0.357、誤差 0.515,比永遠猜語料平均(0.476)還差;gemma4:31b-cloud r=0.729、誤差 0.375。作者據此反駁「LLM 根本無法描述聲音」的說法,但強調介面在兩種設定下看起來完全一樣。線性項與模型無關:換成量測的頂點座標後,誤差仍有 0.275,約是可達範圍的 1.7 倍。

結尾的六條實務建議很具體,例如「量測,不要問模型」「解析失敗時絕不回傳輸入」「回報誤差相對可達範圍的比值,超過 1 就代表顯示已無法區分空間中的位置」。這組建議的適用範圍比音樂工具大,但作者也明說只主張機制可以推廣,不主張數字可以推廣。

Reachability: for N sounds the reachable region's affine dimension is min(N−1, 7), so each added sound contributes exactly one dimension; the closed form was verified against the tool's own mixing code to 1.1×10⁻¹⁶. The three everyday canvases span only 1, 2 and 3 of 7 dimensions; of 3,000 sampled presets, 0, 4 and 6 lie within corpus resolution of the reachable sets, and the median preset lies 0.51, 0.40 and 0.37 away; a fully connected 12-sound canvas reaches 2.3% of presets. Holding six sounds fixed and growing edges from a spanning tree (5) to the complete graph (15), reachable dimension stays at 5.0 and coverage varies only from 11.9% to 13.0%: connections determine control, and only placing more sounds extends reach.

Display error: 0.496, 0.649 and 0.485 for the three canvases, against reachable extents of 0.159, 0.173 and 0.163, giving ratios of 3.12×, 3.75× and 2.97×. For scale, a random preset lies 0.50 from the corpus mean, so an error near 0.5 conveys about as much as reporting the mean. The error has two independent sources: a vertex term (coordinates are predicted, not measured) and a linearity term (the audio blends synthesiser parameters while the display interpolates perceptual vectors, and the mean of two parameter sets is not the mean of their sounds).

The vertex term depends on the model: over 52 concepts, gpt-oss:20b reaches a mean seven-dimension correlation of r=0.357 with error 0.515, worse than always predicting the corpus mean (0.476), while gemma4:31b-cloud reaches r=0.729 with error 0.375. The authors use this to reject the view that LLMs simply cannot describe sound, while stressing that the interface looks identical under both settings. The linearity term is model-independent: with measured vertex coordinates the error is still 0.275, about 1.7 times the reachable extent.

The six closing recommendations are concrete, e.g. 'measure, do not ask', 'never return the input on a parse failure', and 'report error against extent; above 1, the display no longer distinguishes one part of the space from another'. They apply well beyond music tools, though the authors explicitly claim only that the mechanism generalises, not the magnitudes.
