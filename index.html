LightOnOCR for AMNLT: Reading Chant Notation and Lyrics in One Pass, from Single Staves to Whole Pages

用文件 OCR 模型一次讀出聖歌記譜與歌詞：全參數微調 LightOnOCR-3-1B 寫出位置式 GABC，Solesmes 的 AMLER 從已發表最佳 20.98% 降到 9.92%；但 AMNLT 基準本身是平的（驗證集音高錯誤率始終 1.68–1.89%），真正下降的是五本沒看過的抄本（24.92% → 1.31%），而且每一步都靠加資料，沒有任何消融

動機Motivation

聖歌抄本上同時寫著兩樣東西：譜表上的紐碼（neume）旋律，以及底下要唱的拉丁文歌詞。要編輯、檢索或演唱，光有其中一樣都不夠，還得知道哪幾個音屬於哪個音節。Fuentes-Martínez 等人把這個問題正式定名為 AMNLT（aligned music notation and lyrics transcription，對齊的記譜與歌詞轉寫），附上四個資料集、一系列方法與兩個把「認得對不對」和「對齊得對不對」拆開的指標（AMLER 與 AlER）。

那篇研究的結論之一是：唯一測過的視覺語言模型 PaliGemma 2（LoRA 微調）在四個資料集上 AMLER 都在 47–70%，作者寫下「目前視覺語言模型似乎無法在 AMNLT 上取得有競爭力的結果」。這篇的出發點就是質疑這句話——換一個模型、換一種訓練方式，結論還成立嗎？

作者是奧地利科學院的聖歌研究者，手上有大量奧地利哥德式與方形記譜抄本，因此第二個動機是實用的：基準上分數漂亮之外，模型能不能讀沒看過的抄本、能不能直接讀整頁而不必先偵測譜表。

A chant book carries two things at once: a melody in neumes on a staff and the Latin text sung to it. For editing, searching or performing, neither is enough alone; one also needs to know which notes belong to which syllable. Fuentes-Martínez et al. formalised this as AMNLT (aligned music notation and lyrics transcription), with four datasets, a family of methods and two metrics, AMLER and AlER, that separate recognition from alignment.

One conclusion of that study was that the only vision-language model tested, PaliGemma 2 with LoRA, stayed at an AMLER of 47–70% on all four datasets, and the authors wrote that vision-language models seem unable to achieve competitive results for AMNLT. This paper starts by questioning that sentence: does it hold for a different model trained a different way?

The author is a chant scholar at the Austrian Academy of Sciences with many Austrian Gothic and square-notation manuscripts at hand, so the second motivation is practical: beyond benchmark scores, can a model read manuscripts it has never seen, and can it read whole pages without first detecting staves?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

AMNLT 原論文的方法族。 Fuentes-Martínez 等人比較了先分開辨識再後對齊的 CRNN-CTC 基線，以及三類端到端方法：整體式 CRNN-CTC、展開（unfolding）方法，以及語言建模類（Sheet Music Transformer、DAN、TrOCR、PaliGemma 2）。這篇在方法上就屬於最後一類：影像進、序列出，不展開行。差別不在架構類型，而在起點與訓練方式。

最該點名的對照：PaliGemma 2 的設定。 原論文用 3B 的 PaliGemma 2、輸入 896 px、只在語言模型上做秩 8 的 LoRA、視覺模組與投影層凍結。這篇則用 1B 的 LightOnOCR（Pixtral 視覺編碼器＋Qwen3 解碼器），視覺編碼器全部一起訓練、輸入到 1,540 px，再把長譜表切成二到四段疊起來。作者自己在討論的 H2 也承認：寬 3,300 px 的譜表縮到 896 px，一個譜表級距（staff step）只剩約 8 px。所以「推翻 VLM 做不到」這件事，最可能的解讀是「凍結編碼器＋低解析度的 LoRA 做不到」，而不是換了一個更聰明的模型——這跟 TrOCR 以來文件辨識領域「預訓練文件模型全參數微調」的老做法並無二致。

整頁 OMR。 Sheet Music Transformer++ 與 DAN 都已經做過不需版面分析的整頁讀取，MuSViT 則用 970 萬頁樂譜做遮罩自編碼預訓練。這篇從文件 OCR 那一側走到同一個方向，新的是把它用在帶歌詞的中世紀聖歌上。

銀標資料。 「兩個不同辨識器讀出一樣的音高序列才收」這個過濾，本質上就是 co-training／一致性偽標籤（Blum & Mitchell 的雙視角協同訓練）的做法；論文沒用這個名稱，但它自己的校準也重現了這類方法的已知弱點：兩個讀者共享的系統性錯誤（把 F 譜號讀成 C、把 custos 當成音符）擋不住。

The AMNLT method families. Fuentes-Martínez et al. compared a CRNN-CTC baseline that recognises separately and aligns afterwards with three end-to-end families: holistic CRNN-CTC, unfolding methods, and language modelling (Sheet Music Transformer, DAN, TrOCR, PaliGemma 2). This paper belongs to the last family: image in, sequence out, without unfolding lines. The difference is not the architecture class but the starting point and the training regime.

The comparison that matters most: the PaliGemma 2 setup. The original study used 3B PaliGemma 2 at 896 px with rank-8 LoRA in the language model only and the vision block and projector frozen. This paper uses 1B LightOnOCR (a Pixtral vision encoder and a Qwen3 decoder), trains the vision encoder in full at up to 1,540 px, and cuts long staves into two to four stacked strips. The author's own hypothesis H2 concedes that a 3,300 px staff scaled to 896 px has about 8 px per staff step. The most likely reading of overturning the claim is therefore that a frozen encoder with low-resolution LoRA cannot do it, not that a cleverer model arrived; full fine-tuning of a pre-trained document model has been standard in document recognition since TrOCR.

Full-page OMR. Sheet Music Transformer++ and DAN already read whole pages without layout analysis, and MuSViT pre-trains on 9.7 million pages of sheet music with masked autoencoding. This paper reaches the same direction from the document-OCR side; what is new is applying it to medieval chant with lyrics.

Silver data. Keeping a staff only when two different recognisers read the same pitch sequence is in essence co-training or agreement-based pseudo-labelling in the Blum and Mitchell tradition. The paper does not use that name, but its own calibration reproduces the known weakness of such methods: errors both readers share, such as F clefs read as C or a custos taken for a note, pass the filter.

方法Method

輸出格式。 每條譜表寫成一個字串，音節與括號內的音符交替出現；多譜表影像就每條譜表一行、由上而下。關鍵的選擇是用 Kurek 的 GabcParser 所定義的「位置式」GABC：字母 a 到 m 代表譜表由下而上的線與間，要跟譜號一起讀，而不是 Einsiedeln、Salzinnes 原本那種帶八度的音名。好處是所有來源（CantusDB、Transkribus、第二讀者）都能轉進同一種編碼合併訓練；代價是譜號讀錯只扣一個 token，整行音高其實全錯，所以論文另外報告「首譜號正確率」。

資料。 預設模型 P-d 每個 epoch 29,627 個樣本：AMNLT 訓練集 4,633（F 譜號譜表算兩次）、CantusDB 對齊而來 10,463、Transkribus 611、Graz 金標 1,487＋463、銀標抄本 2,446、銀標印刷本 1,500（設上限）、多譜表影像 8,024。CantusDB 那塊來自作者先前把 Volpiano 旋律對到十本抄本影像的工作，這篇補上歌詞與音節邊界；但為了避免裁切時帶到相鄰譜表，兩個影像過濾器丟掉了 62% 的候選。

銀標過濾的校準。 在 1,474 條有人工音高標註的抄本譜表上，嚴格一致收下 59.8%，音高錯誤率從單一模型的 2.61% 降到 0.18%；但收下的譜表只有 95.8% 完全正確，原本設定的 99% 精確度沒有達到。反過來看，在 14 冊 Graz 金標上兩個讀者一致但跟人工標註不同的 103 條裡，抽查 78 條，68 條是讀者對、只有 8 條是標註對——過濾器同時是找人工標註錯誤的工具。

模型與訓練。 架構與字彙完全不改，學習率視覺端 1e-4、解碼器 5e-5，四個 epoch，單張 H100，P-d 訓練 13.4 小時。附錄裡有一個值得記下的細節：官方 checkpoint 的解碼器權重前綴跟所用的函式庫版本不合，函式庫只印一則通知就讓 28 層解碼器保持隨機初始化；作者改名後逐一比對 532 個張量才確認無誤。隨機解碼器也學得起來、只是比較差，所以不比對根本不會發現。

Output format. Each staff becomes one string in which syllables alternate with notes in parentheses; an image with several staves gets one line per staff, top to bottom. The key choice is the position-based GABC defined in Kurek's GabcParser: letters a to m name the lines and spaces of the staff from bottom to top and are read with the clef, instead of the octave-marked pitch names that Einsiedeln and Salzinnes originally use. The benefit is that every source (CantusDB, Transkribus, the second reader) can be pooled in one encoding; the cost is that a misread clef costs one token while the whole line is musically wrong, so the paper reports initial-clef accuracy separately.

Data. The default model P-d sees 29,627 examples per epoch: 4,633 AMNLT training examples (F-clef staves counted twice), 10,463 aligned through CantusDB, 611 from Transkribus, 1,487 plus 463 Graz gold, 2,446 silver manuscript staves, 1,500 capped silver print staves and 8,024 multi-staff images. The CantusDB block comes from the author's earlier work aligning Volpiano melodies to the images of ten manuscripts; this paper adds lyrics and syllable boundaries. To keep crops free of the neighbouring staff, two image filters discard 62% of the candidates.

Calibrating the silver filter. On 1,474 manuscript staves with human pitch annotation, strict agreement keeps 59.8% and lowers the pitch error from 2.61% (one model alone) to 0.18%; but only 95.8% of the kept staves are exactly right, so the intended 99% precision is not reached. Conversely, on the 14 Graz gold volumes, of 103 staves where the two readers agree but the human annotation differs, 78 were checked and 68 favour the readers against 8 for the annotation. The filter doubles as a tool for finding annotation errors.

Model and training. Architecture and vocabulary are unchanged; learning rates are 1e-4 for the vision side and 5e-5 for the decoder, four epochs on one H100, 13.4 hours for P-d. One appendix detail is worth recording: the published checkpoint stores decoder weights under a prefix the library version did not expect, and the library only printed a notice and left all 28 decoder layers randomly initialised. The author renamed them and verified all 532 tensors bit for bit. A random decoder also learns, only worse, so without that check nobody would have noticed.

限制Limitations

沒有任何一個效果被單獨驗證。 這是作者自己寫在摘要最後一句的話，也是全篇最大的限制。從 S-AMNLT 到 P-d 的九次訓練，每一步同時改了資料來源、數量、文字慣例、加權與影像型態，而且每一步只跑一個 seed。保留抄本的音高錯誤率從 24.92% 降到 1.31% 是一條漂亮的曲線，但哪一段是 CantusDB 的量、哪一段是哥德式記譜的覆蓋、哪一段是編碼統一，都分不開。討論列了五個假設（OCR 預訓練、PaliGemma 微調失敗、統一編碼、位置字母、雙模態都強），每一個都附了「該跑哪個實驗來判定」，但一個都沒跑——包括最便宜、最關鍵的「凍結視覺編碼器重跑 S-AMNLT」。

基準比較的公平性。 只有 Solesmes 能直接比：Einsiedeln 與 Salzinnes 的已發表數字是在 pseudo-gabc、多字元 token 上算的，這篇是位置編碼、單字元 token，分母比較大、譜號錯誤只扣一次。已發表方法的 bWER 是作者用兩位小數的 AlER 反推的，有幾個百分點十分之幾的誤差。再加上 AMNLT 的切分本來就不是按頁切（Solesmes 75 頁中 73 頁跨切分、Salzinnes 176 個測試頁全都出現在訓練集），而且從 S+C3 起訓練資料包含 Einsiedeln 那本抄本 CH-E 611，後續模型在 Einsiedeln 上的數字不獨立。

評估集很小。 每本保留抄本只有 40 條譜表，F 譜號全部只有 47 個；驗證頁 9 頁、測試頁 14 頁，幾乎都來自三本記譜很小的抄本；Aldersbach 檢查集 55 條，而且是從 P-d 自己的草稿修正而來，草稿裡沒被發現的錯會被算成對。

仍未解決的錯誤。 哥德式 F 譜號：保留抄本上 C 譜號 153 個讀對 152 個，F 譜號 47 個只讀對 30 個，而 F 讀成 C 時整行音高都會錯。縮寫或難辨認的歌詞會讓上方的音符跟著被省略——模型把「音節＋音符」當成一個單位寫，讀不出音節就整組跳過。另外，方形記譜抄本 A-Wn 15484 上 P-d 幾乎不寫 virga（47 處，對 P-c 的 325 處），因為自家資料的音符編碼不分形狀，virga 只存在於 AMNLT 的目標裡。

No effect is isolated. The author says so in the last sentence of the abstract, and it is the paper's main limitation. Across the nine runs from S-AMNLT to P-d, each step changes sources, amount, text convention, weighting and image forms together, and each step has one seed. The held-out pitch error falling from 24.92% to 1.31% is a clean curve, but which part is the volume of CantusDB, which the coverage of Gothic notation and which the shared encoding cannot be separated. The discussion lists five hypotheses (OCR pre-training, failed PaliGemma fine-tuning, the shared encoding, position letters, strength in both modalities), each with the experiment that would decide it, and runs none, including the cheapest and most decisive one: S-AMNLT again with the vision encoder frozen.

Fairness of the benchmark comparison. Only Solesmes is directly comparable. The published Einsiedeln and Salzinnes figures are computed on pseudo-gabc with multi-character tokens; this paper uses the position encoding with one token per character, a larger denominator and a clef error counted once. The published bWER is back-computed by the author from AlER given to two decimals, with an uncertainty of a few tenths of a point. The AMNLT splits are not page-disjoint (73 of 75 Solesmes pages span splits, and all 176 Salzinnes test folios occur in training), and from S+C3 on the training data contains CH-E 611, the Einsiedeln manuscript, so later Einsiedeln figures are not independent.

Small evaluation sets. Each held-out manuscript contributes 40 staves, and there are only 47 F clefs in all; there are 9 validation and 14 test pages, almost all from three manuscripts with small notation; the Aldersbach check set has 55 staves corrected from P-d's own draft, so an unnoticed draft error counts as correct.

Errors that remain. The Gothic F clef: on the held-out staves P-d reads 152 of 153 initial C clefs but only 30 of 47 F clefs, and an F read as C makes the whole line wrong in pitch. Abbreviated or illegible lyrics make the notes above them disappear too, because the model writes syllable and notes as one unit and skips the unit when it cannot read the syllable. And on the square-notation manuscript A-Wn 15484, P-d writes almost no virga (47 places against 325 for P-c), because the in-house note code does not distinguish shapes and the virga exists only in the AMNLT targets.

討論Discussion

最重要的結果是負面的。 九次訓練裡 AMNLT 驗證集的音高錯誤率始終在 1.68–1.89% 之間，任何資料的加入都推不動它；同一段時間保留抄本從 24.92% 降到 1.31%。一個只看 AMNLT 選模型、報結果的研究，第一輪就會以為做完了。原因在第 4.1 節：AMNLT 留出的是譜表，不是頁也不是抄本，它量的是「同一本書的其他譜表」。這個觀察比 Solesmes 上 9.92 對 20.98 的勝利更值得被引用，也提醒讀者重新看待其他 OMR 基準上的高分——昨天讀的 Cecilia 在 OpenScore 上贏、在 CVC-MUSCIMA 上輸，是同一類問題的另一個面向。

資料比架構重要，但證據是相關不是因果。 加 2,160 條三本抄本的 CantusDB 譜表就把錯誤率從 24.9% 拉到 9.6%，加 569 條四本哥德式抄本把 4.7% 拉到 2.1%，而且後者的增益集中在使用同一種德國哥德式記譜的 A-Gu 17 與 A-VOR 254。這很符合「覆蓋目標記譜比單純增加量重要」的直覺，但每一步一個 seed、多個因素同時改變，作者的措辭也很克制（“observations, not isolated effects”）。另一個參照點是前一個專案的 CRNN/Transformer 辨識器：用約 38,000 條譜表訓練，在同樣 200 條保留譜表上是 13.88%，而只用 6,083 條的 S+C3 是 9.55%——這是對「預訓練文件模型的資料效率」最直接的一點支持，但兩者解碼方式與訓練資料都不同，不是乾淨的對照。

整頁讀取的取捨很具體。 不訓練多譜表影像時，模型給整頁只讀一條就停，錯誤率超過 93%；加一個 epoch、13% 的多譜表樣本就降到約 10%。到 P-d，驗證頁與測試頁為 3.34% 與 3.82%，保留抄本頁 1.9%。

解析度決定成敗：整頁縮到 1,540 px 時譜表級距只有 7–13 px，低於 14 px 的 patch；在記譜小的頁面上，整頁 3.6%、半頁 2.3%、單條譜表 1.7%；記譜大的保留抄本則反過來，整頁 1.9% 比單條 2.5% 好。直接放大輸入沒有用（1,540／2,300／2,800 px 得 9.5／13.7／43.9%），因為模型只學過 110 個 patch 以內的位置。相鄰譜表的問題也很具體：同一批 30 條譜表，乾淨裁切 0.97%、裁到下一條一半 12.67%、整條帶進來 60.43%——這是整頁模型最有說服力的理由。

4B 模型沒有更好。 LightOnOCR-3-4B 在同樣資料上 AMNLT 驗證打平（1.80%）、保留抄本略差（2.38% 對 2.11%），記憶體 78.7 GB 對 24 GB、速度慢約 1.75 倍。對資源有限的人文研究單位，1B 的結論更實用。

值得肯定的寫法。 這篇在誠實度上明顯高於平均：自己列出五條與已發表數字比較的保留、在資料表裡標明哪些集合只用來報告、承認 P-b 在保留集上第三個 epoch 其實比選中的第四個好（1.46% 對 1.74%）卻不改選擇規則、也明白揭露用了 AI 程式助理產生程式與初稿。對想把這套方法用到自己抄本的人，最實際的建議是：先建一組真正沒看過的抄本當測試集，不要相信 AMNLT 驗證集。

The most important result is a negative one. Across nine runs the pitch error on AMNLT validation stays between 1.68% and 1.89%; no addition of data moves it, while over the same runs the held-out manuscripts fall from 24.92% to 1.31%. A study that selected and reported on AMNLT alone would have thought itself finished after the first run. Section 4.1 gives the reason: AMNLT holds out staves, not pages or manuscripts, so it measures further staves from the same books. This observation deserves citing more than the 9.92 against 20.98 win on Solesmes, and it invites a second look at high scores on other OMR benchmarks; yesterday's Cecilia, winning on OpenScore and losing on CVC-MUSCIMA, shows another face of the same problem.

Data matters more than architecture, but the evidence is correlational. Adding 2,160 CantusDB staves from three manuscripts takes the error from 24.9% to 9.6%, and adding 569 staves from four Gothic manuscripts takes 4.7% to 2.1%, with the gain concentrated in A-Gu 17 and A-VOR 254, which share that German Gothic notation. This fits the intuition that covering the target notation matters more than sheer volume, but each step has one seed and several factors change at once, and the author's wording is restrained: observations, not isolated effects. One more reference point is the predecessor project's CRNN/Transformer recogniser: trained on about 38,000 staves, it reads the same 200 held-out staves at 13.88%, against 9.55% for S+C3 with 6,083. This is the most direct support for the data efficiency of a pre-trained document model, but decoding and training data differ, so it is not a clean comparison.

The page-reading trade-off is concrete. Without multi-staff training, the model reads one staff of a page and stops, with an error above 93%; one more epoch with 13% multi-staff examples brings it to about 10%. For P-d, validation and test pages reach 3.34% and 3.82%, and held-out pages 1.9%.

Resolution decides: a whole page scaled to 1,540 px has 7–13 px per staff step, below the 14 px patch. On pages with small notation, whole page gives 3.6%, half pages 2.3%, single staves 1.7%; on held-out manuscripts with larger notation the order reverses, whole page 1.9% against 2.5% for single staves. Simply enlarging the input fails (9.5, 13.7 and 43.9% at 1,540, 2,300 and 2,800 px), because the model has learned positions only up to 110 patches. The neighbouring-staff problem is concrete too: on the same 30 staves, clean crops give 0.97%, a crop to the middle of the next staff 12.67%, and the whole next staff 60.43%. That is the strongest argument for a page model.

The 4B model is not better. LightOnOCR-3-4B on the same data ties on AMNLT validation (1.80%) and is slightly worse on held-out manuscripts (2.38% against 2.11%), with 78.7 GB of memory against 24 GB and about 1.75 times slower reading. For humanities groups with limited compute, the 1B result is the more useful one.

What the paper does well. Its candour is well above average: it lists five reservations on the comparison with published figures, marks which sets are report-only, admits that for P-b the third epoch read the held-out set better than the chosen fourth (1.46% against 1.74%) without changing the selection rule, and discloses that AI coding assistants produced code and a first draft. For anyone wanting to apply the method to their own manuscripts, the practical advice is to build a test set of truly unseen manuscripts first and not to trust AMNLT validation.

MusicListenBench: Can Audio LLMs Hear the Difference Yet?

It is marked as a deep dive, focusing on what the protocol adds over CheckList-style directional and invariance tests, and how far the conclusion that models learn to notice change faster than to ignore it depends on the frozen audio encoder.

動機Motivation

音訊大型語言模型通常用曲風、情緒、配器這類「描述整首歌」的題目來評測。作者認為底下還有一層更基本的能力：兩段短音訊是不是同一段旋律、同一個和弦、同一種樂器、哪一段比較快。這類「相同／不同」判斷是聽覺研究的標準工具，Musical Ear Test 與 PROMS 這類人類音樂能力測驗就是用它們組成的。如果模型連兩個四音動機是否相同都分不出來，它對整首歌旋律的描述就很難信任。

近來已有幾個基準發現音訊 LLM 在基本音樂感知上接近亂猜，但作者指出兩個剩下的問題。第一，一個「相同／不同」分數混了兩種能力：注意到要緊的改變，以及忽略不要緊的改變；而且這類分數會被文字先驗與答案字母偏好灌水。第二，這些基準只評測現成的模型，看不出訓練能不能修好。

所以這篇的目標有兩層：設計一個不被字母偏好污染、能把兩種失敗分開量的協定；再用它檢查後訓練到底修好了什麼、沒修好什麼。

Audio LLMs are usually evaluated on questions that describe a whole song: genre, mood, instrumentation. The authors argue there is a more basic layer underneath: are two short clips the same melody, the same chord, the same instrument, and which one is faster? Such same/different judgements are a standard tool of hearing research, and tests of human musical ability such as the Musical Ear Test and PROMS are built from them. If a model cannot tell whether two four-note motifs are the same, its description of a full song's melody is hard to trust.

Recent benchmarks already find audio LLMs near chance on basic music perception, but the authors point to two remaining problems. First, a same/different score mixes two skills: noticing a change that matters and ignoring one that does not, and such scores are inflated by text priors and answer-letter preference. Second, these benchmarks only evaluate models as they are, so they cannot tell whether training fixes the problem.

The aim therefore has two layers: a protocol that letter bias cannot contaminate and that measures the two failures separately, and then using it to check what post-training does and does not fix.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最接近的三個基準。 MUSE 測十種音樂感知技能（包括相同／不同判斷），用 200 個錄音刺激與 200 位人類受試者；Carone 等人比較 MIDI 與音訊輸入在切分音、移調、和弦性質上的表現；SonicBench 合成控制過的聲音測十二種物理屬性，並且跟這篇一樣把兩段音訊放進同一個檔案。三者都只評測、不訓練。另外 PitchBench 用 28 組控制實驗測音高聽覺，MuChoMusic、MMAU、AIR-Bench 則是題目由人或 LLM 撰寫的大型問答基準，覆蓋廣但容易被文字先驗與字母偏好污染。

這篇宣稱新的東西，其實最像 CheckList。 每題一個 flip（改了被問的屬性，答案必須變）和一個 stay（只改聲音，答案必須不變），正是 Ribeiro 等人 CheckList 裡「方向性測試」與「不變性測試」的聽覺版；作者自己也這樣寫。成對計分的想法則來自視覺的 MMVP 與 NaturalBench（一組裡全部答對才給分）。真正的新意在兩點：一是把兩種測試配成答案相反的一對，使得「不聽音訊」的模型在三個相同／不同任務上剛好 50%、在節奏任務上平均 50%，字母偏好被結構性地抵消；二是 stay 變體擋住了乾淨題的一個捷徑——乾淨的「相同」題兩段音訊完全一樣，模型只要比「聽起來像不像」就能答，不必知道旋律或和弦是什麼。

跟作者自己的前作。 同一團隊（SUTD 的 AMAAI 實驗室）先前做過 MelodySim 與 MERIT，是用專門模型學兩段音訊在旋律或其他面向上像不像；這篇改成要求音訊 LLM 用文字作答。訓練部分用的 GRPO 先前也有人用在 Qwen2-Audio 的 MMAU 問答上。

The three closest benchmarks. MUSE tests ten music perception skills, including same/different judgements, on 200 recorded stimuli with 200 human listeners; Carone et al. compare MIDI and audio input on syncopation, transposition and chord quality; SonicBench renders controlled sounds for twelve physical attributes and, like this paper, puts both clips in one file. All three only evaluate, without training. PitchBench probes pitch hearing in 28 controlled experiments, while MuChoMusic, MMAU and AIR-Bench are large QA benchmarks with human- or LLM-written questions, broad but exposed to text priors and letter bias.

What the paper presents as new is closest to CheckList. Each item has a flip variant (the queried attribute changes, so the answer must change) and a stay variant (only the sound changes, so it must not), which is the listening version of the directional and invariance tests in Ribeiro et al.'s CheckList; the authors say so themselves. Paired scoring comes from MMVP and NaturalBench in vision, which credit a group only when all its answers are right. The real novelty is twofold. Pairing the two tests with opposite answers means a model that ignores the audio scores exactly 50% on the three same/different tasks and 50% on average on rhythm, so letter preference is cancelled by construction. And the stay variant blocks a shortcut of clean pairs: a clean 'same' pair has two identical clips, so a model can answer by checking whether they sound alike without knowing what a melody or chord is.

Relation to the authors' own work. The same group (the AMAAI lab at SUTD) built MelodySim and MERIT, dedicated models that learn whether two clips are similar in melody or other aspects; here an audio LLM must answer in words. GRPO, used for training here, has previously been applied to Qwen2-Audio on MMAU question answering.

方法Method

題目格式。 每題是一個音訊檔加一個問題：檔案裡依序是片段 A、1.0 秒靜音、片段 B。四個任務：旋律（四音動機中一個音移動 100／200／400／700 音分）、和聲（和弦三音移動 100／200／300 音分，問和弦性質是否相同）、音色（鋼琴、吉他、小提琴、長笛四個樂器家族）、節奏（哪段 hi-hat 比較快，速率比 1.10–1.50）。每個任務 2,500 個訓練題、250 個乾淨測試題；問題有 8–10 種人工寫的措辭，結尾固定說明哪個字母代表哪個答案。

精確標籤與去捷徑。 所有題目先用 music21 寫成符號音樂，再用 FluidSynth 與 General MIDI 音色庫渲染，所以答案完全確定、不需人工標註。同一任務所有片段長度一樣（快的節奏是點擊比較多，不是檔案比較短），響度統一為 −23 LUFS。

flip 與 stay。 對 1,000 個乾淨測試題各做兩個變體，都只替換片段 B。flip 改被問的屬性；stay 只改聲音，四種平均分配：室內殘響（RT 0.2／0.5／1.0 秒）、±3 dB 的溫和 EQ、−50 dBFS 的底噪，以及只在旋律與和聲使用的 200 音分以內整體移調（測相對音高：整段移調的旋律仍是同一段旋律）。兩個錯誤率：漏判率 MR（flip 題答錯）與誤翻率 FFR（stay 題答錯），flip/stay 準確率 = 1 −（MR + FFR）/ 2。另外報告 FFR_c，只算乾淨題答對的那些，用來排除「本來就錯、加了雜訊還是錯」的情況。

刻意的分佈偏移。 測試集跟訓練集在三處不同：音域（旋律與音色訓練用 MIDI 60–72、測試用 55–59；和聲根音訓練 48–60、測試 62–67）、音色庫（訓練用 FluidR3_GM 或 GeneralUser GS，測試全用 MuseScore_General），以及殘響、EQ、底噪只出現在測試。另有一個真實錄音測試集（600 乾淨＋1,200 flip/stay）：旋律與和聲用 GuitarSet 並對單音或三音所在的弦做移調，音色用 Medley-solos-DB 七種樂器、兩段來自不同錄音，節奏用 MUSDB18-HQ 的鼓軌做時間伸縮。

訓練。 對四個開源模型（Qwen2-Audio、Qwen2.5-Omni、Audio Flamingo 3、Phi-4-multimodal）做 GRPO，LoRA 或全參數。獎勵是答對字母得 1，另加 0.2 的可解析輸出獎勵與最多 0.1、偏向模型目前較少輸出的那個字母的項，防止字母塌縮。群組大小 8，報告 1,000 步的 checkpoint（Audio Flamingo 3 全參數在 500 步停）。所有實驗中音訊編碼器都是凍結的——這一點對後面的解讀很關鍵。

Item format. Each item is one audio file and one question: clip A, 1.0 s of silence, clip B. Four tasks: melody (one note of a four-note motif moves by 100, 200, 400 or 700 cents), harmony (the third of a chord moves by 100, 200 or 300 cents; same chord quality?), timbre (piano, guitar, violin or flute family) and rhythm (which hi-hat track is faster, rate ratio 1.10–1.50). Each task has 2,500 training and 250 clean test items; questions use 8–10 hand-written wordings and end with a fixed instruction saying which letter means which answer.

Exact labels and no easy cues. Every item is written as symbolic music with music21 and rendered with FluidSynth and General MIDI soundfonts, so every answer is exact and no human labelling is needed. All clips in a task have the same length (a faster rhythm has more clicks, not a shorter file) and the same loudness, −23 LUFS.

flip and stay. Each of the 1,000 clean test items gets two variants, both replacing clip B. flip changes the queried attribute; stay changes only the sound, spread evenly over four changes: room echo (RT 0.2, 0.5 or 1.0 s), a mild ±3 dB EQ, hiss at −50 dBFS, and, for melody and harmony only, a transposition of up to 200 cents (testing relative pitch: a melody moved up as a whole is still the same melody). Two error rates: the miss rate MR (flip items wrong) and the false-flip rate FFR (stay items wrong), with flip/stay accuracy = 1 − (MR + FFR)/2. FFR_c counts only items whose clean version is right, to exclude errors already present on the clean pair.

Deliberate distribution shift. The test set differs from training in three ways: pitch range (melody and timbre use MIDI 60–72 in training and 55–59 at test; harmony roots 48–60 and 62–67), soundfont (FluidR3_GM or GeneralUser GS in training, MuseScore_General for all test audio), and room echo, EQ and hiss appearing only at test. A real-recording test set adds 600 clean and 1,200 flip/stay items: melody and harmony from GuitarSet with one note or the strings of the third pitch-shifted, timbre from seven instruments in Medley-solos-DB with the two clips from different recordings, rhythm from time-stretched MUSDB18-HQ drum stems.

Training. Four open models (Qwen2-Audio, Qwen2.5-Omni, Audio Flamingo 3, Phi-4-multimodal) are trained with GRPO, by LoRA or full fine-tuning. The reward is 1 for the correct letter, plus 0.2 for a parseable option and at most 0.1 favouring the letter the model currently gives less often, against letter collapse. Group size is 8, and the step-1,000 checkpoint is reported (step 500 for Audio Flamingo 3 with full fine-tuning). The audio encoder is frozen in every run, which matters for interpreting what follows.

限制Limitations

「學會注意比學會忽略快」可能是凍結編碼器的結論，而不是模型的結論。 所有 GRPO 與 SFT 執行都凍結音訊編碼器，只改語言模型怎麼讀它的輸出。作者自己在附錄 L 量到：在渲染片段上，底噪讓編碼器輸出移動的距離是真正音樂改變的 2.75 倍，而這個距離就能以 AUROC 0.92 預測訓練後模型會把哪些「相同」stay 題判成不同。換句話說，如果編碼器的表徵本身就把 −50 dBFS 的底噪放得比一個音的移動還大，只調語言端當然很難學會忽略它。論文沒有任何一個解凍編碼器的執行，所以無法區分「LLM 學不會不變性」與「這個凍結的編碼器沒給它不變的表徵」。標題式的結論應該加上這個條件。

提出的解法沒有測。 結論說要求不變性「很便宜」：獎勵只要要求同一題的乾淨版與 stay 版答案相同即可。這是一個具體、可行的一致性獎勵，但論文沒有跑它；實際跑的是把聲音變體（編解碼、換音色庫、移調）當成額外訓練題加進去，屬於資料增強。增強確實有效——殘響誤翻率 16.6% → 4.5%、底噪 52.4% → 37.7%、flip/stay 86.0% → 88.2%——但移調仍停在 45.2%，即使移調題已經在訓練裡。

人類參考很薄。 12 位受試者各答 24 題，共 288 個答案，每個任務的乾淨題只有 24 個人類答案、flip 與 stay 各 96 個；人類 87.0% 的區間是 82.3–91.1%。論文的主要對照（Gemini 2.5 Pro 低 12.8 分、訓練後模型「達到人類水準」）都是跟這個小樣本比。

範圍。 每個片段只有一個樂器或一個音軌，完整混音沒有測。節奏任務因為措辭有時問「快」有時問「慢」，250 題裡有 131 題兩個變體的正確字母相同，所以 50% 地板只在平均意義上成立（全答 A 在節奏上是 47.8%）。真實錄音集的旋律題對所有開源模型都很難（乾淨準確率最高 55.3%），讓那部分的訓練效果解讀空間有限。

'Learning to notice is faster than learning to ignore' may be a conclusion about the frozen encoder, not the model. Every GRPO and SFT run freezes the audio encoder and only changes how the language model reads its output. Appendix L measures that on rendered clips hiss moves the encoder output 2.75 times as far as a real musical change, and this distance predicts which 'same' stay pairs the trained model calls different with AUROC 0.92. If the encoder itself puts −50 dBFS hiss further away than a moved note, tuning only the language side can hardly learn to ignore it. No run unfreezes the encoder, so 'the LLM cannot learn invariance' cannot be separated from 'this frozen encoder does not give it an invariant representation'. The headline conclusion should carry that condition.

The proposed fix is not tested. The conclusion says asking for invariance is cheap: the reward only has to require the same answer for an item's clean and stay versions. That is a concrete, feasible consistency reward, but the paper does not run it; what it runs is adding sound-only versions (codec, soundfont swap, transposition) as extra training items, which is data augmentation. The augmentation does help: false flips under room echo go from 16.6% to 4.5%, under hiss from 52.4% to 37.7%, and flip/stay accuracy from 86.0% to 88.2%. But transposition stays at 45.2% even though transposed pairs are now in training.

The human reference is thin. Twelve listeners answered 24 items each, 288 answers in all: 24 clean answers per task and 96 each for flip and stay; the human 87.0% has an interval of 82.3–91.1%. The paper's main comparisons (Gemini 2.5 Pro 12.8 points below, the trained model reaching human level) are all against this small sample.

Scope. Every clip holds one instrument or one stem, so full mixes are untested. Because rhythm questions sometimes ask for the faster and sometimes the slower clip, in 131 of 250 rhythm items both variants share the correct letter, so the 50% floor holds only on average there (always 'A' gives 47.8% on rhythm). Melodies in the real-recording set are hard for every open model (at most 55.3% clean accuracy), which limits what that part says about training.

討論Discussion

現成模型的結果很明確。 乾淨題上八個開源模型有七個在 47.2–56.5%，跟「全答 A」的 52.8% 差不多；flip/stay 上五個開源模型落在 49.1–52.2% 的機率區間內，只有 Qwen2.5-Omni 明顯高（61.2%）。兩個 GPT-Audio 都在機率區間（49.6%、52.1%），Gemini 3.8 Flash 64.0%，Gemini 2.5 Pro 74.2% 是未訓練模型最好的，仍比人類 87.0% 低 12.8 分（Fisher 精確檢定 p<0.001）。字母偏好很嚴重：Qwen2-Audio 對 98.6% 的乾淨題回答 B，Phi-4-multimodal 與 Audio Flamingo 3 分別有 95.9% 與 87.7% 答 A；按答案類別平均後三者都是機率水準。

Gemini 2.5 Pro 的錯法也有意思：它只偵測到約一半的音被移動，而且移 100 音分和移 700 音分偵測率差不多；鋼琴與吉他 36 對裡有 34 對被它判為同一種樂器。

訓練修好了「注意」，沒修好「忽略」。 GRPO 讓 Qwen2.5-Omni（LoRA）乾淨題從 61.2% 到 99.0%，但 flip/stay 只到 86.0%：漏判率 36.7% → 1.9%，誤翻率 41.0% → 26.1%。七個有學到東西的執行裡有六個，漏判率下降幅度是誤翻率的 2.2–3.2 倍。拆開看：它學會忽略訓練中從沒出現的 EQ（誤翻率 33.7% → 1.4%）、部分學會忽略殘響（37.2% → 16.6%），但底噪反而略升（49.0% → 52.4%）、移調幾乎沒變（48.4% → 45.2%）。在人類幾乎不出錯的音色與節奏上，94 次誤翻裡 85 次來自底噪。旋律題它偵測單音移動的比率是 99.6%，卻有 40.3% 只是整體移調的題被判成不同——聽得到音高變了，但沒有學到相對音高。

跟 Gemini 比的細節。 訓練後的 Qwen2.5-Omni 漏判只有 Gemini 2.5 Pro 的十三分之一（1.9% 對 24.7%），誤翻率卻沒比較低（26.1% 對 26.9%）。而且 Gemini 的誤翻大多其實是「本來就沒聽出音樂改變」（旋律、和聲、音色上 209 次中 155 次），只算乾淨題答對的 FFR_c 時 Gemini 是 20.8%、訓練後模型是 25.7%。這說明兩者是相反的錯誤型態：Gemini 偏向答「相同」，訓練後的模型把任何聲音差異都當成改變。

轉移與副作用。 只用其他三個任務訓練，和聲從 59.2% 到 96.4%（Qwen2.5-Omni），幾乎等於把和聲放進訓練的 96.8%；Qwen2-Audio 的和聲 50.4% → 71.6%，甚至高於和聲在訓練裡時的 60.8%。節奏完全不轉移（只動 1.2 與 1.6 分）。作者的解讀是模型學到的是通用的「相同／不同」技能，而節奏是唯一不是相同／不同題、也唯一關於時間的任務。MusicCaps 2,735 段的描述能力幾乎不受影響（BERTScore-F1 變動 −1.28 到 +1.95）。

真實錄音。 開源模型在真實集上 48.3–55.5%；未訓練模型在兩個測試集上的排名一致（Spearman ρ=0.80）。用渲染資料訓練的 Qwen2.5-Omni 從 52.1% 升到 62.8%，加上聲音變體到 70.1%，改用 Slakh2100 訓練到 75.0%，與 Gemini 2.5 Pro（73.8%）打平。最直觀的例子是「同一個和弦彈兩次」：訓練後模型偵測到 41 個移動三音中的 38 個，但 42 對同和弦裡有 27 對被判成不同；加入聲音變體後只剩 7 對，但偵測也掉到 26 個。Gemini 2.5 Pro 則相反，42 對裡 37 對判相同、移動三音只抓到 12 個。

為什麼這篇重要。 音訊 LLM 已經被拿來標註、篩選、排序音樂，也被當成音樂生成的評審。一個加了輕微底噪就改判的評審，評的是錄音而不是音樂。這篇對 MIR 社群最實際的貢獻是一條報告規範：只報乾淨準確率不夠，至少要同時報 MR 與 FFR 以及字母分佈——它的排行榜也是這樣設計的。對 benchmark 這個子領域，它把 CheckList 的方向／不變性測試搬進聽覺，並示範了「基準＋訓練切分」比單純基準能回答更多問題。

The results for off-the-shelf models are clear. On clean items seven of eight open models score 47.2–56.5%, close to the 52.8% of always answering 'A'; on flip/stay five open models land in the 49.1–52.2% chance range, and only Qwen2.5-Omni is well above it (61.2%). Both GPT-Audio models are at chance (49.6% and 52.1%), Gemini 3.8 Flash reaches 64.0%, and Gemini 2.5 Pro, at 74.2% the best untrained model, is still 12.8 points below humans at 87.0% (Fisher exact test, p<0.001). Letter bias is severe: Qwen2-Audio answers 'B' to 98.6% of clean items, and Phi-4-multimodal and Audio Flamingo 3 answer 'A' to 95.9% and 87.7%; averaged over answer classes, all three are at chance.

Gemini 2.5 Pro errs in an interesting way: it detects only about half of the moved notes, about as often for 100 cents as for 700, and hears piano and guitar as the same instrument in 34 of 36 pairs.

Training fixes noticing, not ignoring. GRPO takes Qwen2.5-Omni (LoRA) from 61.2% to 99.0% on clean items but only to 86.0% on flip/stay: the miss rate falls from 36.7% to 1.9%, the false-flip rate from 41.0% to 26.1%. In six of the seven runs that learned, misses fall 2.2 to 3.2 times more than false flips. Broken down, it learns to ignore EQ, never seen in training (33.7% to 1.4%), partly learns to ignore room echo (37.2% to 16.6%), but hiss gets slightly worse (49.0% to 52.4%) and transposition barely moves (48.4% to 45.2%). On timbre and rhythm, where humans almost never err, hiss causes 85 of its 94 false flips. On melody it detects a single moved note in 99.6% of flip pairs, yet calls 40.3% of merely transposed pairs different: it hears that pitch changed but has not learned relative pitch.

The comparison with Gemini in detail. The trained Qwen2.5-Omni misses one thirteenth as often as Gemini 2.5 Pro (1.9% against 24.7%), yet its false-flip rate is no lower (26.1% against 26.9%). Most of Gemini's false flips actually come from not hearing the musical change in the first place (155 of 209 on melody, harmony and timbre); counted only on items answered correctly when clean (FFR_c), Gemini is at 20.8% and the trained model at 25.7%. The two make opposite errors: Gemini leans toward 'same', while the trained model treats any difference in sound as a change.

Transfer and side effects. Trained on the other three tasks only, harmony goes from 59.2% to 96.4% for Qwen2.5-Omni, nearly the 96.8% with harmony in training; Qwen2-Audio goes from 50.4% to 71.6%, higher than the 60.8% it reaches with harmony in training. Rhythm does not transfer at all (1.2 and 1.6 points). The authors read this as a general same/different skill, rhythm being the only task that is not a same/different question and the only one about timing. Captioning on 2,735 MusicCaps clips is barely affected (BERTScore-F1 changes by −1.28 to +1.95).

Real recordings. Open models score 48.3–55.5% on the real set, and untrained models rank the same way on both test sets (Spearman ρ=0.80). Trained on rendered data, Qwen2.5-Omni rises from 52.1% to 62.8%, to 70.1% with sound-only pairs, and to 75.0% when trained on Slakh2100, level with Gemini 2.5 Pro (73.8%). The most telling case is two strums of the same chord: the trained model detects 38 of 41 moved thirds but calls 27 of 42 same-chord pairs different; with sound-only pairs that drops to 7, but detection falls to 26. Gemini 2.5 Pro errs the other way, calling 37 of 42 pairs the same but detecting only 12 moved thirds.

Why this matters. Audio LLMs are already used to label, filter and rank music, and as judges of music generation. A judge that changes its verdict when quiet hiss is added scores the recording, not the music. The paper's most practical contribution to MIR is a reporting norm: clean accuracy alone is not enough, and MR, FFR and the letter distribution should be reported together, which is how its leaderboard is designed. For the benchmark subfield, it brings CheckList's directional and invariance tests into listening and shows that a benchmark with a training split can answer more questions than a benchmark alone.

動機Motivation

情境很具體：一位鋼琴家錄了一首曲子、寫了歌詞，想聽到這些詞被唱在自己的演奏上。要做到這件事得做一連串小決定：人聲唱哪些音、哪個音節落在哪個音上、每一句相對於演奏的時間在哪裡進來、每個音節怎麼發音，以及用什麼音域唱。一個聽起來很好的音訊結果，不代表這些決定容易檢查或修改。

作者先用 Suno 試過：生成的編曲把原本錄好的鋼琴換掉了，而且透過提示詞去改個別的音樂決定很困難。作者很小心地說這只是動機、不代表商用工具的普遍限制，並指出 Suno 現在已有上傳音訊、多軌編輯與音高控制。所以真正的問題被收窄成：一個以 LLM 為基礎的介面，怎麼在替錄好的演奏加上人聲時，把作曲者的決定攤開來並保留下來？

他們的答案是把這件事當成「編譯」：每個階段產出一個可讀、可修補的中間表示，LLM 負責把自然語言翻成局部修改，腳本負責驗證與套用，作曲者負責判斷音樂結果。

The setting is concrete: a pianist records a piece, writes words for it, and wants to hear them sung over their own playing. That requires a chain of small decisions: which notes the voice sings, which syllable falls on which note, where each line enters against the performance's timing, how each syllable is pronounced, and in what register. A convincing audio result does not make those decisions easy to inspect or revise.

The author first tried Suno: the generated arrangement replaced the recorded piano, and revising individual musical decisions through prompts was hard. The author is careful to call this motivation, not a general limitation of commercial tools, noting that Suno now offers uploaded audio, multitrack editing and pitch controls. The question is therefore narrowed to how an LLM-based interface can expose and preserve the composer's decisions while adding a voice to a recorded performance.

Their answer is to treat the task as compilation: each stage produces a readable, patchable intermediate representation; the LLM translates natural language into local changes, scripts validate and apply them, and the composer judges the musical result.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

可見的 LLM 工作流。 AI Chains（Wu 等人）已經示範過在多步驟 LLM 互動中暴露可編輯的中間結果能提升透明度與可控性；Anthropic 的 Agent Skills 則是把指令、腳本與資源打包成代理可載入的資料夾。ARRANGE 直接用了後者的打包方式，貢獻在於把中間表示專門化為音樂決定。

它宣稱新的東西最像什麼。 核心技術主張是「型別化編輯（typed edits）比整份重寫好」：模型輸出像 shift_entry(layer="strings", by_bars=2) 這樣的指令，由腳本檢查並套用，而不是重寫整份計畫。這本質上就是 LLM 的 function calling／工具呼叫，加上一個領域專用的編輯詞彙；論文第二個對照條件「路徑＋值修補」則幾乎就是 JSON Patch（RFC 6902）。「局部修改比整份重寫穩」在程式碼編輯代理裡早已是共識（例如各種 diff／search-replace 編輯格式），所以這篇的新意不在這個原則，而在把它落到歌詞、音節、發音這些音樂物件上。

Score Canvas 也有明確的前身：VOCALOID、Synthesizer V 這類歌聲合成編輯器的鋼琴捲軸早就把音符、歌詞與音素放在同一個可編輯視圖裡；ARRANGE 的差別是用五線譜呈現，並讓 LLM 與作曲者用同一套索引指涉同一個音。

音樂生成與後端。 YuE 做長篇歌詞到歌曲生成，ACE-Step 與 ACE-Step 1.5 支援生成與編輯，YuE2 結合符號與音訊生成——作者主動承認「合成前先做符號規劃」並非 ARRANGE 獨有。後端是現成元件：高解析度鋼琴轉譜、SoulX-Singer（以樂譜為條件的歌聲合成）、Seed-VC（歌聲轉換）。論文沒有提出新的轉譜、分離、合成或轉換模型。

Visible LLM workflows. AI Chains (Wu et al.) showed that exposing editable intermediate results in multi-step LLM interaction improves transparency and control, and Anthropic's Agent Skills package instructions, scripts and resources as folders an agent can load. ARRANGE uses the latter packaging directly; its contribution is specialising the intermediates to musical decisions.

What its new claim most resembles. The core technical claim is that typed edits beat whole rewrites: the model outputs a command such as shift_entry(layer="strings", by_bars=2), which a script checks and applies, instead of rewriting the whole plan. This is essentially LLM function calling with a domain-specific edit vocabulary, and the paper's second condition, path-and-value patches, is close to JSON Patch (RFC 6902). That local edits are more robust than full rewrites is already settled practice in code-editing agents (diff and search-replace edit formats), so the novelty is not the principle but its application to lyrics, syllables and pronunciation.

The Score Canvas also has clear predecessors: the piano-roll editors of singing synthesis tools such as VOCALOID and Synthesizer V have long put notes, lyrics and phonemes in one editable view. ARRANGE differs in rendering staff notation and letting the LLM and the composer refer to the same note through a shared index.

Music generation and backends. YuE generates long-form songs from lyrics, ACE-Step and ACE-Step 1.5 support generation and editing, and YuE2 combines symbolic and audio generation; the authors concede that symbolic planning before synthesis is not unique to ARRANGE. The backends are off the shelf: high-resolution piano transcription, SoulX-Singer for score-conditioned singing synthesis and Seed-VC for singing voice conversion. The paper proposes no new transcription, separation, synthesis or conversion model.

方法Method

六個階段。 每個技能是一個含指令、腳本與參考資料的資料夾，每個階段有明確的輸入、輸出與作曲者確認點：Listen（鋼琴錄音＋作曲者打的拍子 → 轉譜音符、調性估計、以演奏為準的拍子地圖；確認點是「小節編號跟我數的一樣嗎」）、Play the Line（作曲者邊聽錄音邊在鍵盤上彈出人聲旋律）、Fit（旋律＋歌詞 → 帶音節與逐音發音的音符）、Plan（排定層次、進入點與取用哪一次錄音）、Sing（核准的樂譜＋聲音參考 → 對齊錄音時鐘的人聲渲染）、Mix（混音、分軌與 DAW 工作階段）。論文在表 1 的註記裡特別寫明：這些確認點只是工作流程的指令，不是形式化保證，一個不受約束的代理仍可能跳過。

型別化編輯。 LLM 輸出預先定義好的操作加上帶型別的具名參數；腳本檢查語法與參數規則後套用到既有計畫上。不支援或語意模糊的請求，設計上應該產生一個提問而不是猜。作者也明白指出驗證只檢查格式，不檢查理解：一個合法的 shift_entry 仍可能改錯層或移錯小節數，所以基準要同時量「該改的有沒有改」與「不該改的有沒有被動到」。

Score Canvas。 把 Fit 的結果畫成五線譜加歌詞，每個音節有可見的索引，另有發音視圖顯示真正送進合成器的音素，並能從任一個音開始播放鋼琴與人聲。請求可以用小節與拍、音節索引、字、音高或一張樂譜截圖來指定目標；編輯詞彙包括設定欄位、移動音、插入或刪除列、改發音、提問與回答。

旋律輸入方式。 作曲者直接在鍵盤上一邊聽錄音一邊彈出人聲旋律，同時取得音高與節奏，而不是打字輸入或從自動轉譜的歌聲去修正。作者明說這是第一人稱的設計觀察，不是比較性的易用性結果。

Six stages. Each skill is a folder of instructions, scripts and references, and each stage has an explicit input, output and composer checkpoint: Listen (piano recording and tapped beats → transcribed notes, key estimate and a performance-relative beat map; checkpoint: do the bar numbers match how I count?), Play the Line (the composer plays the vocal melody on keys over the recording), Fit (melody and words → notes with syllables and per-note pronunciation), Plan (layers, entries and take selection), Sing (approved score and voice reference → a vocal render aligned to the recording's clock) and Mix (mix, stems and a DAW session). A note under Table 1 states that the checkpoints are workflow instructions, not a formal guarantee that an unconstrained agent cannot bypass them.

Typed edits. The LLM outputs a predefined operation with typed named arguments; a script checks syntax and argument rules and applies it to the existing plan. Unsupported or ambiguous requests are meant to produce a question rather than a guess. The authors note that validation checks form, not understanding: a valid shift_entry can still target the wrong layer or shift by the wrong amount, so the benchmark measures both whether intended changes happen and whether anything else is touched.

The Score Canvas. The Fit result is engraved as notation with words, each syllable has a visible index, a pronunciation view shows the sounds actually sent to the synthesiser, and playback can start from any note. A request can identify its target by bar and beat, syllable index, word, pitch or a screenshot of the score; the edit vocabulary covers setting a field, moving a note, inserting or deleting a row, changing pronunciation, and asking and answering questions.

Melody input. The composer plays the vocal line on keys while hearing the recording, capturing pitch and rhythm together instead of typing it or correcting an automatic transcription of a singer. The authors state this is a first-person design observation, not a comparative usability result.

限制Limitations

主對照的基線偏弱。 摘要與表 2 的頭條比較是型別化操作對「沒有 ARRANGE 文件的整份重寫」：Sonnet 0.98 對 0.33、Opus 0.90 對 0.69、Haiku 0.83 對 0.62、Gemma 4 31B 0.90 對 0.46、Qwen 3.5 27B 0.83 對 0.50。但整份重寫要模型把整份計畫逐字重產，本來就會動到大量無關欄位（Sonnet 平均每次改了 19.9 與 22.1 個無關欄位），這差不多是個稻草人。比較公平的對照是同樣只輸出局部變更的「路徑＋值修補」：0.81、0.73、0.75、0.67、0.69，型別化操作只領先 0.08–0.23，而論文沒有對這組差距做檢定。

樣本小、檢定是探索性的。 計畫基準只有 16 個請求（7 個真實、9 個合成）、每個 3 次抽樣；Wilcoxon 檢定沒有多重比較校正，作者自己也說是探索性的，Qwen 那格 p=0.078。樂譜修正重播只有 15 個修正，型別化與原始列的差 p=0.064。發音修正型別化 36 次成功 26 次、原始列 0 次，但原始列條件根本沒有發音資訊，這比較的是「給不給資訊」而不是「用不用型別化編輯」。

音樂評估全是作者一人。 作者同時是作曲者與聽者；聲音比較 35 次呈現、SoulX-Singer 95.9 對 Seed-VC 88.5 是單一聽者、沒有跨聽者校準；音域實驗的 185 次是同一人的「呈現」而非獨立受試者，公開的子集裡 SoulX 在 9 對中贏 7 對、p=0.098。實作例只有一段 15 小節的 The Color Purple 主題，加上一段 15 秒的 All the Things You Are。

可重現性。 許多重寫答案被截斷、有版權的編配、樂譜截圖與參考錄音沒有公開；部分實驗可以從儲存的摘要重算，但無法完整重新評分或重新生成。論文正文與公開子集的數字不同，作者有明白區分，但讀者容易混用。

沒有量到真正的主張。 論文定位是互動架構，但沒有量設定成本、費用、認知負荷或修改時間，也沒有任何其他作曲者用過；「是否提升作曲者的主導感」被明白列為待測。

The headline baseline is weak. The abstract and Table 2 compare typed operations with whole-plan rewriting without ARRANGE documentation: Sonnet 0.98 against 0.33, Opus 0.90 against 0.69, Haiku 0.83 against 0.62, Gemma 4 31B 0.90 against 0.46, Qwen 3.5 27B 0.83 against 0.50. But a rewrite asks the model to reproduce the whole plan and naturally touches many unrelated fields (Sonnet changed 19.9 and 22.1 unrelated fields per draw), which is close to a straw man. The fairer baseline is path-and-value patches, which also output only local changes: 0.81, 0.73, 0.75, 0.67 and 0.69. Typed operations lead by only 0.08–0.23 there, and the paper tests no significance for that gap.

Small samples, exploratory tests. The plan benchmark has 16 requests (7 real, 9 synthetic) with 3 draws each; the Wilcoxon tests are not corrected for multiple comparisons, the authors call them exploratory, and Qwen's cell has p=0.078. The score-correction replay has 15 corrections, with p=0.064 for typed against raw rows. Pronunciation corrections succeed in 26 of 36 typed draws and 0 of 36 raw-row draws, but the raw condition has no pronunciation information at all, so this compares giving information, not typed editing.

All musical evaluation is one person. The author is both composer and listener. The voice comparison of 35 presentations, SoulX-Singer 95.9 against Seed-VC 88.5, is one listener without cross-listener calibration; the 185 register-experiment items are presentations to one person, not independent participants, and in the released subset SoulX wins 7 of 9 pairs with p=0.098. The worked example is one 15-bar excerpt of the theme from The Color Purple, plus a 15-second excerpt of All the Things You Are.

Reproducibility. Many rewritten answers are truncated, and copyrighted fits, score screenshots and reference recordings are withheld; some experiments can be recomputed from stored summaries but not fully re-scored or regenerated. The full-study and released-subset numbers differ; the authors keep them apart, but readers may easily mix them.

The real claim is unmeasured. The paper presents an interaction architecture, but measures no setup effort, cost, cognitive load or revision time, and no other composer used it; whether it improves composer agency is explicitly left to future work.

討論Discussion

最有說服力的數字不在頭條。 比起型別化對無文件重寫，有兩個結果更值得注意。第一，有沒有文件對整份重寫幾乎沒幫助：Sonnet 有文件 0.38、沒文件 0.33，而且有文件那組 48 次裡有 11 次輸出無效。這說明問題出在「要模型重產整份狀態」這件事本身，不是缺說明。第二，前沿模型沒有讓結構變得多餘：Claude Fable 5.1 型別化 0.94 對無文件重寫 0.69（p=0.062），差距比 Sonnet 小，但仍在。有個反直覺的細節也值得記下：在型別化條件下 Sonnet（0.98）高於 Opus（0.90），但在整份重寫下 Opus（0.69）遠高於 Sonnet（0.33）——模型排名會隨介面而翻轉，這對任何「用 X 模型做音樂代理」的比較都是一個警訊。

提示詞基準不等於代理執行。 62 個請求、三首歌的策展樂譜編輯基準上，型別化提示一律高於原始列（Haiku 0.80 對 0.60、Opus 0.92 對 0.83、Sonnet 0.91 對 0.74、Gemma 0.55 對 0.49、Qwen 0.68 對 0.58）。但讓 Claude Code 代理實際載入技能、使用腳本時，Sonnet 從 0.91 掉到 0.82、Opus 從 0.92 到 0.89，只有 Haiku 升到 0.84。作者的結論很中肯：單次回應的受限基準不能取代代理執行的評估，載入指令與使用工具本身會帶來新的失敗。這是這篇對 agents 子領域最可轉移的一點。串接測試也類似：連續 8 個請求的工作階段，型別化條件下每個模型 6 次裡只有 3 次精確落在預期計畫上；排除一個計畫裡根本沒有所需時間資訊的請求後才 6 次全對。

音樂上學到的東西。 三個實際的錯誤都在音符／發音層級被找到並局部修正：把子音單獨放一個音會在 “morning” 裡多出母音、音節比音符多會讓字沒被唱、“Footsteps” 跨重複音切分時接縫處的塞音讓字聽起來斷掉。這正好說明為什麼發音視圖重要：只改文字不一定會改到送進合成器的音素。五次渲染裡各句進入點都在樂譜位置的 0.1 秒內，每次重新渲染約一分鐘。

音域規則（把旋律最高音對到參考聲音第 90 百分位的音高）與作者最後選擇的平均誤差是 1.50 個半音，對齊中位數則是 6.00；在六個聲音上 LLM 給的建議平均偏高 4.0–7.4 個半音。另一個方法學上的觀察很實用：52 個完全相同的音檔，單獨聽與按調性順序聽，緊繃度評分平均差約 7 分——聆聽條件不一致時，數字評分就難以解讀。

定位。 這篇的價值在於把「代理每一步怎麼檢查」具體化成可量的東西，並誠實地說明它量不到什麼；它不是新的歌聲模型，也沒有證明一般作曲者用得了（作者明說目標使用者是會讀譜、能在鍵盤上彈旋律的人）。對想做音樂代理的人，最可直接借用的是兩件事：把評估拆成「該改的改了沒」「不該改的動了沒」「受保護欄位被碰了沒」，以及同時報告提示詞與實際代理執行兩種條件。

The most convincing numbers are not the headline. Two results matter more than typed against undocumented rewrite. First, documentation barely helps rewriting: Sonnet scores 0.38 with documentation and 0.33 without, and the documented condition produced 11 invalid outputs out of 48. The problem lies in asking the model to regenerate the whole state, not in missing instructions. Second, a frontier model does not make the structure redundant: Claude Fable 5.1 scores 0.94 with typed operations against 0.69 for undocumented rewriting (p=0.062), a smaller gap than Sonnet's but still there. One counter-intuitive detail is worth noting: with typed operations Sonnet (0.98) beats Opus (0.90), but under rewriting Opus (0.69) is far ahead of Sonnet (0.33). Model rankings flip with the interface, a warning for any comparison of models as music agents.

A prompt benchmark is not agent execution. On the curated score-editing benchmark of 62 requests across three songs, typed prompts beat raw rows for every model (Haiku 0.80 against 0.60, Opus 0.92 against 0.83, Sonnet 0.91 against 0.74, Gemma 0.55 against 0.49, Qwen 0.68 against 0.58). But when a Claude Code agent actually loads the skill and uses scripts, Sonnet drops from 0.91 to 0.82 and Opus from 0.92 to 0.89, and only Haiku rises to 0.84. The authors' conclusion is apt: a constrained single-response benchmark cannot replace evaluating agent execution, because loading instructions and using tools add failures of their own. This is the paper's most transferable point for the agents subfield. Chained sessions tell a similar story: over eight successive requests, typed conditions ended exactly on the expected plan in only 3 of 6 sessions per model, and all 6 matched only after excluding a request whose required timing was absent from the plan.

What was learned musically. Three real faults were found and fixed locally at the note or pronunciation level: a consonant split onto its own note added a vowel-like sound in 'morning', a line with more syllables than notes left words unsung, and splitting 'Footsteps' across repeated notes put stops at the join and made the word sound broken. This is why the pronunciation view matters: changing the text does not necessarily change the sounds sent to the synthesiser. Across five renders, line entries were within 0.1 s of their score positions, with about one minute per re-render.

The register rule (matching the melody's highest note to the reference voice's 90th-percentile pitch) differs from the author's final choices by 1.50 semitones on average, against 6.00 for median matching; on six voices, LLM suggestions were on average 4.0–7.4 semitones too high. A useful methodological note: for 52 identical files heard individually and in ordered key sequences, strain ratings differed by about seven points, so numeric ratings need consistent listening conditions.

Positioning. The paper's value is in turning how each step of an agent is checked into something measurable, and in being honest about what it cannot measure. It is not a new singing model and does not show that composers in general can use it; the stated target user reads notation and can play a melody on keys. For anyone building music agents, two things can be borrowed directly: splitting evaluation into whether intended changes happened, whether anything else was touched and whether protected fields were touched; and reporting both prompt-level and actual agent-execution conditions.
