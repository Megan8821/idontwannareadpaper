DuET: Note-Frame Dual Encoding for Piano Performance-to-Score Transcription

DuET：音符與影格雙編碼器做鋼琴演奏 MIDI 轉樂譜——共用演奏時間座標的 TimeRoPE、時間×音高二維旋轉位置編碼 TPRoPE，與聯合拍點追蹤

Wei Zeng, Ye Wang
arXiv:2610.21467 · cs.SD; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-15
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：NUS 團隊把演奏 MIDI 轉樂譜（PM2S）拆成音符與 20 ms 影格兩條編碼器，用雙向交叉注意力互通，並把旋轉位置編碼的座標從序列索引換成演奏時間與音高。在自建、以流行鋼琴為主的 Gangqinpu 上 MUSTER 平均錯誤從最強基線的 6.56% 降到 3.64%；但在古典的 ASAP 上只從 11.30% 到 11.22%，漏音與多音錯誤反而較高。值得讀的是它把「拍點在影格上、樂譜屬性在音符上」這個粒度錯配講清楚並給了一個架構答案，而消融也老實顯示各元件的效果會隨資料集翻轉。Why this one: An NUS team splits performance-MIDI-to-score (PM2S) transcription into a note encoder and a 20 ms frame encoder linked by bidirectional cross-attention, and replaces sequence indices in rotary position encoding with performance time and pitch. On their own pop-focused Gangqinpu set, MUSTER mean error drops from 6.56% (strongest baseline) to 3.64%; on classical ASAP it moves only from 11.30% to 11.22%, with higher missing- and extra-note errors. It is worth reading because it states the granularity mismatch clearly (beats live on frames, score attributes on notes) and gives an architectural answer, while its ablations honestly show component effects flipping across datasets.

動機Motivation

把一段有彈性速度（rubato）的鋼琴演奏轉成可讀的樂譜，難處不在音高，而在時間：同樣 0.3 秒的間隔，在不同速度與拍號下可能是八分音符，也可能是附點十六分。要判斷這件事，模型得知道底下的拍點網格在哪裡；可是拍點常常落在沒有任何音符起音的位置，而樂譜屬性（拍內位置、時值、譜表、聲部）又是一個音符一個值。兩者的預測粒度根本不同。

作者另一個動機是曲目覆蓋。既有神經網路 PM2S 幾乎都在古典的 ASAP 上訓練與評估，但真正缺譜的是流行鋼琴。所以這篇同時做了一個以流行為主的資料集 Gangqinpu（鋼琴譜），並把整條音訊轉樂譜管線串起來：Transkun v2 做音訊轉 MIDI、DuET 做中段 PM2S、MuseScore 4 做排版。

Turning an expressive piano performance with rubato into a readable score is hard not because of pitch but because of time: the same 0.3-second gap can be an eighth note or a dotted sixteenth depending on tempo and meter. Deciding which requires knowing where the underlying beat grid lies, yet beats often fall where no note starts, while score attributes (position in bar, duration, staff, voice) are one value per note. The two prediction granularities simply differ.

The second motivation is repertoire. Neural PM2S has been trained and evaluated almost entirely on classical ASAP, but the real shortage of sheet music is in popular piano. So the paper also builds a pop-focused dataset, Gangqinpu, and assembles a full audio-to-score pipeline: Transkun v2 for audio to MIDI, DuET for the PM2S middle stage, and MuseScore 4 for engraving.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

PM2S 這條線上有三個具體前作，DuET 跟每一個的差別都很明確。Liu 等（ISMIR 2022）在音符起音上預測拍點與強拍標籤，再用動態規劃補出沒有音符的拍點——等於用後處理解決粒度錯配；DuET 改成直接在影格上監督拍點。Beyer & Dai（ISMIR 2024）是 Transformer 編碼器–解碼器，用 compound note 表示加上依序列索引的 RoPE，並以相鄰音符的起音間隔（IOI）表示時間；DuET 改以演奏時間本身當旋轉座標，任意兩音的時間距離都直接可見。Wachter 等（2025）則把外部給的拍點網格當條件，推論時需要另一個拍點追蹤器，錯誤會往下傳。

較早的 Shibata 等（2021）用 HMM 加上非局部統計，在本文的 Gangqinpu 上仍是最強基線（6.56%）——這說明舊的機率模型在流行曲上並不弱。

Three concrete predecessors in PM2S each differ clearly from DuET. Liu et al. (ISMIR 2022) predict beat and downbeat labels at note onsets, then fill in beats without notes via dynamic programming, which solves the granularity mismatch by post-processing; DuET supervises beats directly on frames. Beyer & Dai (ISMIR 2024) use a Transformer encoder-decoder with compound note representations, sequence-index RoPE, and inter-onset intervals (IOIs) for timing; DuET uses performance time itself as the rotary coordinate, so the time gap between any two notes is directly visible. Wachter et al. (2025) condition on an externally supplied beat grid, which needs a separate tracker at inference whose errors propagate.

The older HMM approach of Shibata et al. (2021), with non-local statistics, is still the strongest baseline on Gangqinpu (6.56%), showing that probabilistic models are not weak on pop material.

方法Method

輸入是兩份同源表示：音符序列（音高、起音秒數、時值、力度）與 20 ms 一格的鋼琴卷軸，每格 354 維（88 鍵的按住、踏板延長、起音、起音力度，加兩個踏板特徵），經兩層時間卷積嵌入，不做降採樣。編碼器有八層，每層兩支各自做自注意力，再做雙向交叉注意力（音符問影格、影格問音符），兩邊都只用對方自注意力後的狀態。解碼器八層，交叉注意力在音符記憶與影格記憶間交替，每個輸入音符輸出一筆樂譜紀錄：小節索引、拍內整數與分數位置、時值、譜表、小節長度、拍號分子分母、調號、聲部、倚音旗標，共 11 個預測欄位，音高直接複製。輸出長度固定等於輸入音符數，所以不需要結束符號，也不允許插入或刪音。

位置編碼是核心。TimeRoPE 把座標設成（t − 視窗起點）/ 0.02 秒，音符用連續起音時間、不取整，影格落在格點上，因此兩支之間的注意力天然對齊在同一條演奏時間軸。TPRoPE 用在音符自注意力與解碼器對音符記憶的交叉注意力：每個頭的前一半維度用時間旋轉（週期 0.1–64 秒），後一半用以中央 C 為零點的音高旋轉（週期 12–256 半音）。解碼器自注意力仍用一般的序列索引 RoPE。

訓練目標是 11 個欄位的交叉熵（調號、聲部、倚音權重 0.2）加上影格端的拍點三分類、拍序與小節相位。另有兩種遮罩：10% 機率整小節遮掉輸入音符、75% 機率遮掉解碼器歷史。推論以 32 秒視窗推進，帶前四小節的已預測樂譜當前綴。

The input comes in two forms from the same source: a note sequence (pitch, onset in seconds, duration, velocity) and a 20 ms piano roll with 354 features per frame (key-held, pedal-extended, onset, onset velocity over 88 keys, plus two pedal features), embedded by two temporal convolutions without downsampling. The encoder has eight layers; in each, both branches run self-attention, then bidirectional cross-attention (notes query frames, frames query notes), each using the other's post-self-attention state. The eight-layer decoder alternates cross-attention between note memory and frame memory and emits one score record per input note: bar index, integer and fractional beat position, duration, staff, bar length, meter numerator and denominator, key, voice, and a grace flag, eleven predicted fields with pitch copied. Output length equals input note count, so there is no end token and no insertion or deletion.

Position encoding is the core. TimeRoPE sets the coordinate to (t minus window start) / 0.02 s, using unrounded note onsets and grid-aligned frames, so attention across the two branches shares one performance-time axis. TPRoPE, used in note self-attention and decoder cross-attention to note memory, rotates the first half of each head's dimensions by time (periods 0.1 to 64 s) and the second half by pitch centered on middle C (periods 12 to 256 semitones). Decoder self-attention keeps ordinary index RoPE.

Training combines cross-entropy over the eleven fields (key, voice and grace weighted 0.2) with frame-level beat classes, beat index and bar phase. Two masks are used: whole input bars masked at 10% and decoder history masked at 75%. Inference advances in 32-second windows with a four-bar prefix of already-predicted score.

限制Limitations

ASAP 上的整體提升幾乎是零。MUSTER 平均錯誤 11.22% 對 Beyer & Dai 的 11.30%，只差 0.08 個百分點；時間相關確實較好（起音 12.34 對 15.55、時值 36.46 對 51.86），但漏音 8.78 對 7.56、多音 11.70 對 6.44 都更差。而且 ASAP 的基線數字是直接抄 Beyer & Dai 原文，不是重跑。所以「兩個資料集都最低」在 ASAP 那一側應讀成「持平，錯誤型態不同」。

Gangqinpu 上的大幅領先，資料集是作者自建、尚未公開，演奏 MIDI 由 Transkun v2 轉寫而來，評估只用 79 首「共同有效」的子集。消融又用另一組固定子集（ASAP 39 首、Gangqinpu 72 首）：完整 DuET 在 ASAP 子集上是 8.68%，在主表的 59 首上是 11.22%，代表被排除的 20 首明顯比較難，兩張表的數字不能直接互比。

元件效果也不穩定。只用時間的 TimeRoPE 在交替記憶下 ASAP 錯誤 13.68%，比序列索引版本的 9.87% 還糟；要加上音高才降到 8.68%。在 Gangqinpu 上，只用時間 RoPE 加只看音符記憶的變體（3.07%）跟完整 DuET 打平。拍點追蹤方面，Gangqinpu 上專用的影格追蹤器（拍 78.86、強拍 81.37 F1）比 DuET（77.34、79.94）還好。一對一映射也排除了裝飾音簡化與音符合併，作者自己也承認。

The overall gain on ASAP is essentially nil. MUSTER mean error is 11.22% versus 11.30% for Beyer & Dai, a 0.08-point difference; timing is indeed better (onset 12.34 vs 15.55, duration 36.46 vs 51.86), but missing notes (8.78 vs 7.56) and extra notes (11.70 vs 6.44) are worse. The ASAP baseline numbers are copied from Beyer & Dai rather than rerun. So the claim of lowest error on both datasets should read, on ASAP, as a tie with a different error profile.

The large Gangqinpu lead comes from an unreleased dataset the authors built, whose performance MIDI is Transkun v2 output, evaluated on a common-valid subset of 79 pieces. Ablations use different fixed subsets (39 ASAP, 72 Gangqinpu): full DuET scores 8.68% on the ASAP subset but 11.22% on the main 59, so the excluded 20 are clearly harder and the two tables cannot be compared directly.

Component effects are unstable. Time-only RoPE with alternating memory gives 13.68% on ASAP, worse than the index version's 9.87%; only adding pitch brings it to 8.68%. On Gangqinpu, time-only RoPE with note memory alone (3.07%) ties full DuET. For beats, the dedicated frame tracker on Gangqinpu (beat 78.86, downbeat 81.37 F1) beats DuET (77.34, 79.94). The one-to-one mapping also rules out ornament reduction and note merging, as the authors acknowledge.

討論Discussion

這篇最有用的是把問題拆對了。PM2S 長期用「音符上預測拍點、再補」或「外部給網格」來處理粒度錯配，DuET 把影格分支直接放進同一個模型、用共用時間座標讓兩邊對話，在流行鋼琴上把起音錯誤從 5.11% 壓到 2.54%、樂譜相似度的時值錯誤從 45.36% 降到 36.22%。這個方向對任何「事件序列 + 規則網格」的任務都適用，例如鼓譜或和弦標記。

論文自稱新的 TPRoPE，其實最像 Heo 等（ECCV 2024）給 ViT 用的軸向二維 RoPE：把頭的維度切兩半、各自依 x 與 y 旋轉，這裡只是把兩軸換成演奏時間與音高。雙向交叉注意力「各用對方自注意力後狀態」的寫法，也跟 ViLBERT 一類雙流共注意力層同構。新意在把這兩個既有零件放到 PM2S 的正確位置上，而不在零件本身。

下一步該看的是：Gangqinpu 公開後的獨立重現、ASAP 上漏音與多音為何變多（可能是一對一映射在古典曲密集織體下的代價），以及第一階段換成 Transkun 以外的轉寫器時，影格分支是否還有同樣的增益。

The most useful thing here is that the problem is decomposed correctly. PM2S has long handled the granularity mismatch by predicting beats on notes and filling gaps, or by taking an external grid; DuET puts a frame branch into the same model and lets the branches talk via a shared time coordinate, cutting onset error on pop piano from 5.11% to 2.54% and ScoreSimilarity duration error from 45.36% to 36.22%. The pattern suits any event-sequence-plus-regular-grid task, such as drum notation or chord labeling.

The TPRoPE the paper presents as new is closest to the axial 2D RoPE of Heo et al. (ECCV 2024) for ViTs, which splits head dimensions in half and rotates by x and y; here the axes are performance time and pitch. Bidirectional cross-attention where each side uses the other's post-self-attention state is likewise isomorphic to ViLBERT-style two-stream co-attention. The novelty is placing these existing parts in the right spot for PM2S, not the parts themselves.

What to watch next: independent reproduction once Gangqinpu is released, why missing and extra notes rise on ASAP (perhaps the cost of one-to-one mapping in dense classical textures), and whether the frame branch keeps its gain when the first stage uses a transcriber other than Transkun.

MIDI-REPA: Improving Text-to-Music Generation with Symbolic Representation Alignment

MIDI-REPA：用自動轉譜得到的鋼琴卷軸對齊 flow-matching DiT 的中間層——對齊什麼、哪一層、哪段時間步的設計研究

Song-Ze Yu, Fang-Duo Tsai, Yi-Hsuan Yang
arXiv:2610.20967 · cs.SD; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-14
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：台大與 UCSD 團隊把 REPA 的對齊目標從預訓練編碼器特徵換成 MuScriptor 轉出來的 88 鍵鋼琴卷軸，加在 122M 的 FluxAudio-S 文字轉音樂模型上。在 Song Describer 上 FAD 從 0.5275 降到 0.4947、CLAP 從 0.2220 升到 0.2686；在 ATTM 挑戰的 80 個提示上 FAD 0.497、CLAP 0.315、CCS 0.829，單張 RTX 3090 約 31 小時訓練。值得讀的是它用很小的成本測了「符號監督能不能幫音訊生成」，並把對齊的層與時間步做成可重複的設計研究；但 ATTM 表裡沒有同條件的無對齊基線，那一張表的領先不能全算在對齊頭上。Why this one: An NTU and UCSD team swaps REPA's alignment target from pretrained encoder features to an 88-key piano roll transcribed by MuScriptor, applied to the 122M FluxAudio-S text-to-music model. On Song Describer, FAD drops from 0.5275 to 0.4947 and CLAP rises from 0.2220 to 0.2686; on the ATTM challenge's 80 prompts it reaches FAD 0.497, CLAP 0.315, CCS 0.829, trained in about 31 hours on one RTX 3090. It is worth reading because it tests cheaply whether symbolic supervision helps audio generation and turns the choice of layer and timestep into a reproducible design study; but the ATTM table has no matched no-alignment baseline, so that table's lead cannot all be credited to alignment.

動機Motivation

現在的文字轉音樂模型要從密集的音訊（或其潛空間）同時學兩件事：旋律、節奏、和聲、結構這類音樂內容，以及音色與製作這類聲學實現。前者用符號表示只要稀疏的離散事件，後者卻是數百萬個連續取樣。作者的論點是：在音訊空間做去噪，會讓模型把大量容量花在聲學細節上，相對低估了稀疏的音樂結構；而要靠擴大模型、資料與算力去補，大部分研究者負擔不起。

既有的補法是把旋律或和弦當條件輸入，但沒有條件時音樂性又會掉回去。所以這篇問的是另一個方向：不改輸入、不改推論，只在訓練時把模型內部表徵往符號內容拉，能不能讓生成更好？

Current text-to-music models must learn two things at once from dense audio or its latents: musical content (melody, rhythm, harmony, structure) and acoustic realization (timbre, production). The former needs only sparse discrete events in symbolic form; the latter is millions of continuous samples. The authors argue that denoising in audio space spends much capacity on acoustic detail and underweights sparse musical structure, and that compensating by scaling models, data and compute is out of reach for most researchers.

The existing fix is to feed melody or chords as a condition, but musicality drops back when the condition is absent. So the paper asks a different question: without changing inputs or inference, does pulling the model's internal representations toward symbolic content during training alone improve generation?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接的前作是 REPA（Yu 等，ICLR 2025）：在影像 DiT 的中間層接一個投影頭，讓隱藏狀態去對齊 DINOv2 的特徵，收斂快很多。音樂端已有延伸，ACE-Step 與 Cheng 等（ICME 2026）用 MERT、CLAP、MuQ 這類預訓練音訊編碼器當對齊目標；投影頭的設計取自 iREPA（Singh 等，ICLR 2026）的單層卷積；選層方法則比較了 AG-REPA（ICML 2026）的 FoG-A 指標。條件式的路線則有 Music ControlNet 與同組的 MuseControlLite，把旋律、節奏當推論時的輸入。

MIDI-REPA 跟這些的差別在目標：不是另一個網路學出來的密集特徵，而是轉譜得到的 88 維二值鋼琴卷軸。不過要誠實說，這個做法最像的其實不是 REPA，而是多任務學習裡的「輔助轉譜頭」或深層監督：從第 L 層接一個小卷積去預測每格有哪些音在響，用負餘弦相似度當損失。叫它表徵對齊有點包裝成分；本質上是要求中間層可以線性地讀出鋼琴卷軸。

The direct predecessor is REPA (Yu et al., ICLR 2025): attach a projection head to a middle layer of an image DiT and align its hidden states with DINOv2 features, which speeds convergence considerably. Music extensions exist: ACE-Step and Cheng et al. (ICME 2026) use pretrained audio encoders such as MERT, CLAP and MuQ as alignment targets. The projector design comes from iREPA's single convolution (Singh et al., ICLR 2026), and layer selection is compared against the FoG-A score of AG-REPA (ICML 2026). The conditioning route includes Music ControlNet and the same group's MuseControlLite, which feed melody or rhythm as inference-time inputs.

MIDI-REPA differs in its target: not dense features learned by another network but an 88-dimensional binary piano roll from transcription. Honestly, the closest analogue is not REPA but an auxiliary transcription head, or deep supervision, in multi-task learning: a small convolution on layer L predicts which notes sound in each frame, trained with negative cosine similarity. Calling it representation alignment is partly packaging; in essence it requires the middle layer to linearly expose a piano roll.

方法Method

骨幹是 ATTM 挑戰官方的 FluxAudio-S：122M 參數、T5 加 CLAP 文字條件、12 個 MMDiT 區塊（前四個音訊/文字聯合、後八個融合，編號 L0–L11）。作者把原本的音訊自編碼器換成凍結的 SAME-L，10 秒 44.1 kHz 立體聲編成 108 × 256 的潛序列。符號目標離線算好：Basic Pitch 給單一卷軸；MuScriptor 給多樂器卷軸，可以保留成 7 個樂器家族的立方體，也可以合併成一張卷軸，都是 108 格 × 88 音。MuScriptor 需要樂器標籤，作者先在 MulTTiPop 上比四個樂器辨識器，取 MATPAC++ 與 Music Flamingo 的聯集以優先召回率。

資料是 ATTM 的 55,701 首去人聲 Jamendo 曲目，轉譜後丟掉 4,624 首不可用的，得到 JamendoMIDI 51,077 首；再排除 SDD 與 ATTM 參考曲，訓練用 49,034 首、約 3,161 小時。總損失是 flow-matching 損失加 λ = 0.5 乘上對齊損失，batch 128 訓練 30 萬步。

設計研究依序固定三個選擇，每組用 8 組生成種子算 95% 信賴區間。對齊什麼（在 L4）：MuScriptor 合併卷軸 FAD 0.5040 最好，立方體 CLAP 0.2665 最好，Basic Pitch 的 FAD 0.5363 比無對齊的 0.5275 還差。哪一層：L2 拿到 FAD 0.4947、CLAP 0.2686 雙佳；FoG-A 排前三的 L9、L0、L5 都不是好位置。哪段時間步：全程 [0, 1] 的 FAD 最好；只在 [0.88, 1] 對齊 CLAP 最高（0.2858）但 FAD 退到 0.5440；只在 [0, 0.2] 兩者都變差（0.5828、0.2581）。

The backbone is the ATTM challenge's official FluxAudio-S: 122M parameters, T5 plus CLAP text conditioning, and 12 MMDiT blocks (four joint audio/text then eight fused, indexed L0 to L11). The authors replace the original audio autoencoder with a frozen SAME-L that encodes 10 s of 44.1 kHz stereo into 108 by 256 latents. Symbolic targets are precomputed: Basic Pitch gives one roll; MuScriptor gives multi-instrument rolls, kept as a 7-family cube or merged into one roll, all 108 frames by 88 pitches. MuScriptor needs instrument tags, so the authors compare four recognizers on MulTTiPop and take the union of MATPAC++ and Music Flamingo to favor recall.

Data are ATTM's 55,701 vocal-removed Jamendo tracks; after discarding 4,624 unusable transcriptions, JamendoMIDI holds 51,077. Excluding SDD and ATTM reference tracks leaves 49,034 for training, about 3,161 hours. The loss is flow matching plus 0.5 times the alignment loss, trained 300k steps at batch 128.

The design study fixes three choices in turn, each with 95% intervals over 8 generation seed sets. What (at L4): the merged MuScriptor roll gives the best FAD (0.5040), the cube the best CLAP (0.2665), and Basic Pitch's FAD of 0.5363 is worse than no alignment (0.5275). Where: L2 is best on both (FAD 0.4947, CLAP 0.2686); FoG-A's top three, L9, L0 and L5, are not good spots. When: full-range [0, 1] gives the best FAD; [0.88, 1] only gives the highest CLAP (0.2858) but FAD slips to 0.5440; [0, 0.2] only worsens both (0.5828, 0.2581).

限制Limitations

最關鍵的缺口在 ATTM 那張表。表裡的 FluxAudio-S 是主辦方原版（FAD 0.757、CLAP 0.088、CCS 0.592），而作者的模型同時換了自編碼器（SAME-L）、改了訓練設定、又加了對齊。從 0.757 降到 0.497 的這一大段，有多少來自 SAME-L、多少來自對齊，表裡分不出來。能分開看的只有 SDD 設計研究：同骨幹、同資料下，對齊把 FAD 從 0.5275 降到 0.4947（約 6%），CLAP 從 0.2220 升到 0.2686（約 21%）。真正屬於對齊的效果是後面這組數字。

「Borda 排名第一」也要看清楚。在列出的六個決賽隊伍中，它是 FAD 第四、CLAP 第二、CCS 第三；排第一是三項排名加總的結果，而且這是事後把自己插進挑戰排行，不是正式參賽。資深作者楊奕軒也是 ATTM 挑戰論文的作者之一，這點不影響數字，但讀者應該知道。

論文的動機是「音樂結構」，卻沒有任何直接量測結構的指標：沒有聽測，也沒有把生成音訊再轉譜、檢查音高或節奏統計。FAD 與 CLAP 都是嵌入空間的分佈或對應指標。訓練也只跑一個初始化種子，信賴區間只涵蓋生成端的隨機性，不涵蓋訓練端。作者自己列的限制是只做了 122M、10 秒的短片段。

The key gap is the ATTM table. Its FluxAudio-S row is the organizers' original (FAD 0.757, CLAP 0.088, CCS 0.592), whereas the authors' model swaps the autoencoder (SAME-L), changes the training setup, and adds alignment. How much of the drop from 0.757 to 0.497 comes from SAME-L versus alignment cannot be separated from that table. Only the SDD design study isolates it: with the same backbone and data, alignment moves FAD from 0.5275 to 0.4947 (about 6%) and CLAP from 0.2220 to 0.2686 (about 21%). These latter numbers are the effect that truly belongs to alignment.

The Borda rank of first also needs reading carefully. Among the six finalists shown, it is fourth on FAD, second on CLAP and third on CCS; first place is the aggregate of the three ranks, and it is a post-hoc insertion into the leaderboard, not an official entry. Senior author Yi-Hsuan Yang is also an author of the ATTM challenge paper; this does not change the numbers, but readers should know it.

The motivation is musical structure, yet no metric measures structure directly: no listening test, and no re-transcription of generated audio to check pitch or rhythm statistics. FAD and CLAP are distributional or correspondence measures in embedding space. Training uses a single initialization seed, so the intervals cover generation randomness only, not training. The authors' own stated limitation is the 122M, 10-second short-clip setting.

討論Discussion

這篇最有說服力的發現是「轉譜品質決定對齊有沒有用」。同樣的設定下，Basic Pitch 卷軸讓 FAD 從 0.5275 變差到 0.5363，MuScriptor 卷軸則改善到 0.5040。也就是說，雜訊大的符號監督不只沒幫助，還會傷害。這給了一條清楚的依賴關係：MIDI-REPA 的上限綁在 AMT 系統上，轉譜每進步一次，生成這邊可能就跟著受益。

另一個值得記下的是選層：以影響速度場大小為準的 FoG-A，選出的 L9、L0、L5 都不是好的對齊位置，最好的是 L2。這跟 REPA 原文偏好早期層一致，也提醒「功能上重要的層」跟「適合放輔助監督的層」是兩件事。

後續最該補的實驗很具體：在 ATTM 上補一列「SAME-L、無對齊」的基線，以及把生成結果用 MuScriptor 再轉譜，看音符密度、音高分佈是否真的更接近真實音樂。有了這兩個數字，「符號監督改善音樂結構」才從合理推測變成被量到的事實。

The most convincing finding is that transcription quality decides whether alignment helps. Under identical settings, a Basic Pitch roll worsens FAD from 0.5275 to 0.5363 while a MuScriptor roll improves it to 0.5040. Noisy symbolic supervision does not merely fail to help; it hurts. That yields a clear dependency: MIDI-REPA's ceiling is tied to the AMT system, and each improvement in transcription may carry over to generation.

Also worth noting is layer choice: FoG-A, which ranks layers by their effect on the velocity field, picks L9, L0 and L5, none of them good alignment points; the best is L2. This matches the original REPA preference for early layers and is a reminder that functionally important layers and layers suited to auxiliary supervision are different things.

The experiments most worth adding are concrete: an ATTM row with SAME-L and no alignment, and re-transcribing generated audio with MuScriptor to see whether note density and pitch distributions move closer to real music. With those two numbers, the claim that symbolic supervision improves musical structure would turn from a plausible inference into a measured fact.

MuseBench: A Benchmark for AI-Generated Music Detection and Artifact Analysis

MuseBench：36 萬首、十個生成器、含人機共創與壓縮/後製/對抗擾動變體的 AI 生成音樂偵測基準與偽影分析

Yi Chen Liu, Syed Irfan Ali Meerza, Jiawei Yu, Xi Guo, Samuel Bright, Jian Liu
arXiv:2610.20310 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-14
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這個基準收了 361,166 首（約 5,296 小時）：19,903 首人類作品、十個生成器（含 Suno、Udio、Lyria RealTime）的 179,224 首、人機共創，以及壓縮、後製、對抗擾動變體，評了 9 個開源與 2 個商用偵測器。值得讀的不是主結果——ResNet18+Spec 與 AST 在預設設定下平衡準確率 100.0%——而是附錄的域外測試：同一個 ResNet18+Spec 在 2010 年前的 FMA 人類音樂上只有 28.4% 判對，代表它學到的很可能是資料來源而不是生成痕跡。Why this one: The benchmark holds 361,166 tracks (about 5,296 hours): 19,903 human works, 179,224 tracks from ten generators (including Suno, Udio and Lyria RealTime), human-AI co-creations, and compressed, post-processed and adversarially perturbed variants; it evaluates 9 open and 2 commercial detectors. The main result is not what makes it worth reading (ResNet18+Spec and AST reach 100.0% balanced accuracy in the default setting); the appendix out-of-domain test is: the same ResNet18+Spec labels only 28.4% of pre-2010 FMA human music correctly, suggesting it learned data provenance rather than generation traces.

動機Motivation

AI 生成的曲子正大量流入串流平台，平台需要知道哪些是生成的，這牽涉到同意、署名與分潤。但既有的偵測資料集偏窄：生成器只有兩到五個、偏重有人聲的曲子，而且幾乎都在乾淨條件下評估。真實世界的流程裡有轉檔壓縮、等化、變調變速、加噪，甚至蓄意的對抗擾動，還有人類作品被 AI 改編的中間地帶。

作者想做的是一個同時涵蓋這些維度的基準，並把「偵測器準不準」跟「AI 音樂到底有哪些可辨識的偽影」連起來看，最後給出設計偵測器的建議。

AI-generated tracks are flowing into streaming platforms, which need to know what is generated for reasons of consent, credit and compensation. Existing detection datasets are narrow: two to five generators, a bias toward vocal music, and evaluation almost only under clean conditions. Real pipelines involve transcoding, EQ, pitch and tempo changes, added noise, even deliberate adversarial perturbation, plus the middle ground of human works remixed by AI.

The authors want a benchmark covering all these dimensions at once, to connect how accurate detectors are with which artifacts of AI music are actually identifiable, and to derive recommendations for detector design.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者對照了三個前作。FakeMusicCaps（Comanducci 等，2024）在表 2 記為 33,126 首、五個生成器，全是乾淨樣本；SONICS（Rahman 等，2025）約 97,164 首，含 Suno 與 Udio，但幾乎只有人聲曲，並提出 SpecTTTra；M6（Li 等，2024）13,493 首、三個生成器。MuseBench 的增量在規模、十個生成器、人機共創與變換變體。偵測方法端最重要的參照是 Afchar 等（Deezer，ISMIR 2025 最佳論文）：證明轉置卷積上採樣會讓頻譜週期化，用這個指紋加邏輯迴歸就能偵測。

不過這個基準的設計最像的其實是語音防偽的 ASVspoof 2019/2021：留一個生成器出來當未見攻擊（LOMO 就是這個），以及 2021 年 DF 賽道把多種壓縮編碼加進評估條件。論文沒有提到 ASVspoof。另外它在第 6 節當作新診斷工具的「二階 FFT」（對幅度頻譜再做一次 FFT），本質上是倒頻譜（cepstrum）分析的變體，而用頻譜週期性抓上採樣痕跡也正是 Afchar 等已經做過的事。

The authors compare three predecessors. FakeMusicCaps (Comanducci et al., 2024) is listed in Table 2 at 33,126 tracks from five generators, all clean; SONICS (Rahman et al., 2025) has 97,164 tracks including Suno and Udio but almost only vocal music, and introduced SpecTTTra; M6 (Li et al., 2024) has 13,493 tracks from three generators. MuseBench adds scale, ten generators, co-creation and transformed variants. On the method side, the key reference is Afchar et al. (Deezer, ISMIR 2025 best paper), who proved that transposed-convolution upsampling periodizes the spectrum and detected it with that fingerprint plus logistic regression.

Yet the benchmark's design most resembles speech anti-spoofing in ASVspoof 2019/2021: holding out a generator as an unseen attack (exactly what LOMO is), and the 2021 DF track adding multiple compression codecs to evaluation conditions. The paper does not mention ASVspoof. Also, the second-order FFT (an FFT of the magnitude spectrum) presented in Section 6 as a new diagnostic is essentially a variant of cepstral analysis, and detecting upsampling via spectral periodicity is what Afchar et al. already did.

方法Method

人類端是 9,983 首 Genius-Lyrics（人聲，音訊來自 YouTube）與 9,920 首 JamendoMaxCaps（器樂），共 19,903 首。為了風格對齊，每首切成 10 秒段，用 LP-MusicCaps 產生段落描述，再用 GPT-OSS 合成整首描述，當成七個開源生成器（MusicGen、AudioLDM2、Riffusion、MAGNeT、JASCO、Stable Audio Open、Mustango）與 Lyria RealTime 的提示，每個生成 19,903 首。Suno 與 Udio 各 10,000 首則直接取自 SONICS，沒有經過這條對齊管線。AI 端合計 179,224 首。

人機共創是 31 位專業音樂人對部分人類曲目寫修改建議，用 OpenL3 找相似曲目擴充配對，GPT-OSS 改寫成提示，再交給 MusicGen-Style 生成 30 秒、32 kHz 的版本並升頻到 48 kHz，共 9,887 首。變體只套在測試集的 AI 曲目上：MP3/AAC 128 kbps、OGG q3；±6 dB 等化、±1–2 半音加 0.9 或 1.1 倍速、−14 LUFS 響度正規化；標準差 0.025 的高斯雜訊，以及以 M5 與 RawNet2 為代理模型、ε = 0.005 的 PGD 攻擊。另有 8,672 首 2010 年前的 FMA 人類音樂當域外測試。

偵測器都吃隨機 10 秒、48 kHz 單聲道片段，以複製少數類別處理不平衡，並採 70/20/10 切分。商用 API 因費用只測 1,990 首人類加 4,500 首 AI 的子集。偽影分析包括逐頻帶帶阻濾波看偽陰性率變化、Grad-CAM、EnCodec 脈衝響應、和弦進行與演奏可行性的個案。

The human side is 9,983 Genius-Lyrics tracks (vocal, audio from YouTube) and 9,920 JamendoMaxCaps tracks (instrumental), 19,903 in total. For style alignment, each track is cut into 10-second segments captioned by LP-MusicCaps, merged by GPT-OSS into a track caption, and used as the prompt for seven open generators (MusicGen, AudioLDM2, Riffusion, MAGNeT, JASCO, Stable Audio Open, Mustango) and Lyria RealTime, 19,903 tracks each. The 10,000 Suno and 10,000 Udio tracks are taken directly from SONICS without this alignment pipeline. The AI side totals 179,224.

For co-creation, 31 professional musicians write edit suggestions for some human tracks; OpenL3 finds similar tracks to extend the pairing; GPT-OSS rewrites the feedback as prompts; and MusicGen-Style generates 30-second, 32 kHz versions upsampled to 48 kHz, 9,887 in total. Variants apply only to AI test tracks: MP3/AAC at 128 kbps and OGG q3; plus or minus 6 dB EQ, 1 to 2 semitone shifts with 0.9x or 1.1x speed, and -14 LUFS normalization; Gaussian noise with std 0.025, and PGD with epsilon 0.005 using M5 and RawNet2 as surrogates. A further 8,672 pre-2010 FMA human tracks form the out-of-domain test.

Detectors take random 10-second, 48 kHz mono excerpts, with minority-class replication and a 70/20/10 split. Commercial APIs are tested on a 1,990-human plus 4,500-AI subset because of cost. Artifact analysis covers per-band band-stop filtering and its effect on false negatives, Grad-CAM, EnCodec impulse responses, and case studies of chord progressions and playability.

限制Limitations

最嚴重的問題藏在附錄表 10。預設設定下平衡準確率 100.0% 的 ResNet18+Spec，在 FMA 人類音樂上只判對 28.4%，也就是七成以上的真人作品被判成 AI；ConvNeXt 43.5%、ViT 40.7%、SpecTTTra 39.7% 也都低於擲硬幣。最穩的反而是只有約 4 千個參數的 Deezer 指紋法（94.4%）。這幾乎直接說明頂尖模型學到的是「訓練集裡人類音樂長什麼樣」——來源平台、編碼、取樣率——而不是生成痕跡。正文只用一句「強的跨生成器偵測不代表在陌生資料上可靠」帶過，沒有往捷徑學習分析。

設計上有幾個可能放大這個捷徑的地方。人聲類人類曲目的音訊來自 YouTube，本身就經過有損壓縮；而壓縮變體只套在 AI 曲目上，偵測器可能學到「有壓縮痕跡就是人」，AST 在 MP3 下偽陰性升到 24.9% 與此相符。Suno 與 Udio 取自 SONICS、沒有風格對齊。人機共創實際上是 MusicGen-Style 整段重新生成、再從 32 kHz 升頻，不是在人類音軌上疊加 AI 段落，偵測它可能只是在偵測頻寬上限。以上是推測，論文沒有做能排除它們的對照。

其他細節：正文說這個基準「比先前資料集更難」，與五個模型 99.5% 以上的結果矛盾；和弦偽影只看了 30 首 Udio/Suno 搖滾曲、沒有人類對照的量化；演奏不可能性只舉例、沒統計比例；倫理聲明提到「所有聽測都經 IRB 豁免」，但全文沒有任何聽測。

The most serious problem sits in appendix Table 10. ResNet18+Spec, at 100.0% balanced accuracy in the default setting, labels only 28.4% of FMA human music correctly, so over seventy percent of real human works are called AI; ConvNeXt (43.5%), ViT (40.7%) and SpecTTTra (39.7%) are also below chance. The most stable is the Deezer fingerprint method with about 4K parameters (94.4%). This nearly states outright that the top models learned what human music looks like in the training set (source platform, encoding, sample rate) rather than generation traces. The main text passes over it in one sentence, that strong cross-generator detection does not imply reliability on unfamiliar data, with no shortcut-learning analysis.

Several design choices may amplify this shortcut. Vocal human tracks come from YouTube and are already lossy; compression variants are applied only to AI tracks, so detectors may learn that codec traces mean human, consistent with AST's FNR rising to 24.9% under MP3. Suno and Udio come from SONICS without style alignment. Co-creation is actually full regeneration by MusicGen-Style upsampled from 32 kHz, not AI sections layered onto human stems, so detecting it may just detect a bandwidth ceiling. These are hypotheses; the paper runs no control that rules them out.

Other details: the text calls the benchmark more challenging than prior datasets, which contradicts five models above 99.5%; chord artifacts are examined on only 30 Udio/Suno rock tracks with no quantified human comparison; playability violations are shown as examples, not rates; and the ethics statement says all listening studies were IRB-exempt, though the paper contains no listening study.

討論Discussion

這份基準最有價值的結果，恰好是作者沒有放在正文強調的那一個：在域內與留一生成器設定下近乎完美的頻譜模型，換到另一個人類音樂來源就崩掉，而一個有理論依據的小型指紋偵測器反而撐住。這對平台部署的含義很直接——誤判真人作品的成本遠高於漏掉一首 AI 曲，而目前最高分的模型恰好在這個方向最脆弱。

變換實驗給了幾個可直接用的數字。Deezer 指紋法在變調變速下偽陰性率 93.6%，因為它依賴的頻譜週期性被直接打亂；以 M5 與 RawNet2 為代理的 PGD 攻擊，轉移到頻譜模型時偽陰性率只剩 9–12%。頻帶分析指出人聲曲的關鍵頻帶在 5–8 kHz（齒擦音與泛音）、器樂曲在 12–18 kHz，這些是可以拿來做針對性資料增強的具體位置。

要讓這個基準真正好用，最需要的是把人類端擴展到多個來源與編碼條件，並且讓變換同時套用在人類與 AI 兩邊。在那之前，報告這個基準上的分數時，應該把 FMA 域外準確率與預設準確率並列，否則 100% 這個數字會誤導。

The benchmark's most valuable result is the one the authors did not emphasize in the main text: spectrogram models that are near perfect in-domain and under leave-one-generator-out collapse on a different source of human music, while a small, theory-grounded fingerprint detector holds up. The implication for platform deployment is direct: misflagging a human work costs far more than missing one AI track, and today's top-scoring models are most fragile in exactly that direction.

The transformation experiments yield usable numbers. The Deezer fingerprint method has a 93.6% FNR under pitch and tempo shifts, because the spectral periodicity it relies on is directly disrupted; PGD crafted on M5 and RawNet2 transfers to spectrogram models with only 9 to 12% FNR. Band analysis locates the key band for vocal tracks at 5 to 8 kHz (sibilance and overtones) and for instrumental tracks at 12 to 18 kHz, concrete targets for focused data augmentation.

To make the benchmark truly useful, the human side most needs multiple sources and encoding conditions, with transformations applied to both human and AI tracks. Until then, anyone reporting scores on it should list FMA out-of-domain accuracy next to default accuracy, or the 100% figure will mislead.
