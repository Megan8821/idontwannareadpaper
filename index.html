Sonic Palette: Predicting Dynamic Color Themes from Music

Sonic Palette：用 MERT 特徵條件化的自迴歸 Transformer，從音樂逐格預測五色色彩主題（每個 RGB 通道切 16 格做分類），在 265 支官方 MV 上 ACBD 比非自迴歸版低 9.5%——但沒有「拿掉音樂」的對照，也沒拿 Music2Palette 來比

Wen-Li Wei, Kenneth Yang, Jen-Chun Lin
arXiv:2611.25781 · cs.MM; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-11-19
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：音樂與顏色的對應在心理學上有扎實的基礎（Palmer 等人 2013 年的 PNAS 研究指出這層關係由情緒中介），計算上則有 Music2Palette（ACM MM 2025）從一首歌生成一組靜態調色盤。本文把問題改成「隨時間變化」：用 MERT 音訊特徵條件化一個自迴歸 Transformer，逐格預測五個主色，訓練資料是 265 支官方 MV（約 18.5 小時、170 萬格）。選它是因為這週 cs.SD／eess.AS／cs.IR／cs.MM 列表裡只剩這一篇沒讀過的 MIR 論文，而且它的評估設計正好值得拆：自迴歸版的 ACBD 是 3.5161，比非自迴歸 Transformer（3.8853）低 9.5%、比整首歌一組顏色的靜態 MLP（4.6757）低 24.8%；但沒有任何對照能證明預測真的用到了音樂，而不只是學到「MV 顏色會平滑變化」這個先驗。Why this one: Music–color correspondence has a solid basis in psychology (Palmer et al.'s 2013 PNAS study showed the link is mediated by emotion), and on the computational side Music2Palette (ACM MM 2025) generates one static palette per song. This paper makes the problem time-varying: an autoregressive Transformer conditioned on MERT audio features predicts five dominant colors frame by frame, trained on 265 official music videos (about 18.5 hours, 1.7M frames). I picked it because it is the only unread MIR paper left in this week's cs.SD/eess.AS/cs.IR/cs.MM listings, and its evaluation is worth taking apart: the autoregressive model reaches an ACBD of 3.5161, 9.5% lower than a non-autoregressive Transformer (3.8853) and 24.8% lower than a static MLP that predicts one palette per song (4.6757); but no control shows the predictions actually use the music rather than just a learned prior that music-video colors change smoothly.

動機Motivation

音樂是時間性的，但它引起的感受會跨到視覺：輕柔的段落讓人聯想到柔和、偏暖的色調，激烈的段落則像是更飽和、變化更快的顏色。作者指出，現有的計算方法把「音樂配色」當成靜態問題——一段音樂輸入，輸出一組調色盤——這描述得了整首歌的整體色彩印象，卻抓不到音樂推進時的局部變化。

所以本文把任務重新定義為跨模態序列預測：給一串按時間排列的音訊特徵，輸出一串逐格的色彩主題（color theme），每格由五個主色組成。作者刻意不做精細或寫實的影像上色，只處理「調色盤層級」的視覺屬性，再用一個依調色盤重新上色的步驟把結果視覺化。宣稱的貢獻有三：一個從官方 MV 擷取的音訊—色彩序列資料集、把靜態配色延伸成逐格預測的問題設定，以及客觀指標加主觀評分的評估。目標應用是音樂驅動的視覺設計、計算藝術與創作工具。

Music is temporal, but its perceptual effect crosses into vision: gentle passages suggest muted, warmer palettes, while energetic ones evoke more saturated, faster-changing colors. The authors point out that existing computational methods treat music-to-color as a static problem (one music input, one palette out), which can describe a song's overall color impression but misses local variation as the music unfolds.

The paper therefore recasts the task as cross-modal sequence prediction: given a time-ordered sequence of audio features, output a sequence of frame-wise color themes, each made of five dominant colors. The authors deliberately avoid detailed or photorealistic colorization and work only with palette-level attributes, then visualize the result with a palette-guided recoloring step. Three contributions are claimed: a dataset of audio–color sequences extracted from official music videos, a problem formulation that extends static palette generation to frame-wise prediction, and an evaluation combining objective metrics with subjective ratings. Target applications are music-driven visual design, computational art and creative authoring tools.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

感知研究這條線是真的扎實。 Palmer、Schloss 等人（PNAS 2013）發現音樂與顏色的對應強烈受情緒中介：快板大調連到較亮、較飽和、偏黃的顏色，慢板小調連到較暗、較不飽和、偏藍的顏色；Lindborg 與 Friberg（PLoS ONE 2015）用 CIE Lab 介面讓受試者選色，加入感知情緒的模型能解釋 60–75% 的色彩反應變異；Whiteford 等人（2018）把曲風擴大到巴赫到藍調；Reymore 與 Lindsey（2025）則轉向音色，發現語意化的音色評分能預測配色的明度與彩度。值得注意的是，這些研究的單位都是「一段音樂對一個顏色」，沒有一個在研究逐格變化——本文的感知基礎其實只支撐靜態對應。

計算方面最直接的前作是 Music2Palette（Hu 等人，ACM MM 2025），它用跨模態表徵學習建立情緒對齊的音樂—調色盤配對，直接從音樂生成一組調色盤。本文跟它的差別只在時間維度：Music2Palette 一首歌一組，本文每格一組。但作者以「不能直接套用」為由沒有把 Music2Palette 放進比較，其實逐段跑它就是一個現成的強基線。

The perceptual line of work is genuinely solid. Palmer, Schloss et al. (PNAS 2013) found that music–color associations are strongly mediated by emotion: faster major-mode music maps to lighter, more saturated, yellower colors, and slower minor-mode music to darker, less saturated, bluer ones. Lindborg and Friberg (PLoS ONE 2015) had participants pick colors in a CIE Lab interface, and models including perceived emotion explained 60–75% of the variance in color responses. Whiteford et al. (2018) widened the styles from Bach to the blues, and Reymore and Lindsey (2025) turned to timbre, finding that semantic timbre ratings predict the lightness and saturation of matched colors. Note that the unit in all of these is one piece of music to one color; none studies frame-by-frame change, so the paper's perceptual grounding only supports static correspondence.

The most direct computational predecessor is Music2Palette (Hu et al., ACM MM 2025), which builds emotion-aligned music–palette pairs via cross-modal representation learning and generates one palette directly from music. This paper differs only along time: Music2Palette gives one palette per song, this one gives one per frame. Yet the authors leave Music2Palette out of the comparison as "not directly applicable", when running it segment by segment would be a ready-made strong baseline.

方法Method

資料。 265 支完整長度的官方 MV，約 18.5 小時、170 萬格影格（換算約每秒 25 格，這是我從兩個數字推的，論文沒寫影格率或音訊特徵與影格怎麼對齊）。其中 65 支來自 DEAP 資料集，另外 200 支從網路收集。切分是 225 支訓練、20 支驗證、20 支測試；驗證與測試都只取 DEAP 子集，因為 DEAP 有每支影片的效價—喚醒度（valence–arousal）標註，兩組都在四個象限各放五支。作者說論文被接受後才會釋出影片連結與逐格色彩標註。

色彩表示。 每格從量化後的 RGB 直方圖依出現頻率取前五個主色，並用歐氏距離過濾掉太相近的顏色。每個主色的 R、G、B 各切成 16 格，所以一個色彩主題是 5×3 的格標籤矩陣（等價於 5×3×16 的 one-hot 張量），預測從連續迴歸變成 15 個 16 類分類問題。

模型。 先用預訓練的 MERT 抽音訊特徵，經一個 Transformer 編碼器加上時間脈絡，再由自迴歸 Transformer 解碼器建模 p(y_t | y_1:t−1, E(X))：每格的預測同時看音訊脈絡與前面已預測的色彩主題。損失是所有主色、所有通道格標籤的交叉熵總和；推論時每個通道取機率最高的格，預測結果再自迴歸地餵回去。訓練與推論都把音訊切成不重疊的 2 秒片段，各自獨立處理。

架構最像誰。 這套做法——連續的條件序列進編碼器、離散化的目標用 GPT 式解碼器逐步吐出——跟 T2M-GPT（CVPR 2023，文字生成動作）或 Bailando（CVPR 2022，音樂生成舞蹈）是同一個樣板，只是輸出從動作 token 換成 15 個色彩格標籤；把 RGB 切格當分類，則是 Image GPT 以來處理像素顏色的標準手法。作者也引用了 T2M-GPT 與 Image GPT，所以新意在任務設定，不在架構。

重新上色。 每個像素指派到 RGB 歐氏距離最近的預測主色，直接換成那個顏色。這等於把整張圖色調分離（posterize）成五色，比 Chang 等人（SIGGRAPH 2015）的調色盤式照片重新上色粗糙得多——後者在 Lab 空間做平滑的顏色轉移，保留漸層。

Data. 265 full-length official music videos, about 18.5 hours and 1.7M frames (roughly 25 frames per second, which I derived from those two numbers; the paper states neither the frame rate nor how audio features align to frames). Of these, 65 come from the DEAP dataset and 200 were collected online. The split is 225 train, 20 validation, 20 test; validation and test come only from the DEAP subset, because DEAP has per-video valence–arousal labels, and each set has five videos per quadrant. Video links and frame-wise color annotations are promised only upon acceptance.

Color representation. For each frame, the five most frequent colors are taken from a quantized RGB histogram, with Euclidean-distance filtering to drop near-duplicates. Each dominant color's R, G and B channels are quantized into 16 bins, so a color theme is a 5×3 matrix of bin labels (equivalently a 5×3×16 one-hot tensor), turning prediction from continuous regression into 15 sixteen-way classification problems.

Model. Pre-trained MERT extracts audio features, a Transformer encoder adds temporal context, and an autoregressive Transformer decoder models p(y_t | y_1:t−1, E(X)): each frame's prediction sees both the audio context and the previously predicted color themes. The loss sums cross-entropy over the bin labels of all channels of all dominant colors; at inference the highest-probability bin is taken per channel and predictions are fed back autoregressively. Both training and inference split audio into non-overlapping 2-second segments processed independently.

What the architecture most resembles. This recipe, a continuous conditioning sequence into an encoder and a discretized target emitted step by step by a GPT-style decoder, is the same template as T2M-GPT (CVPR 2023, text to motion) or Bailando (CVPR 2022, music to dance), with motion tokens swapped for 15 color bin labels; treating binned RGB as classification is the standard pixel-color trick since Image GPT. The authors cite T2M-GPT and Image GPT themselves, so the novelty is in the task setup, not the architecture.

Recoloring. Each pixel is assigned to the predicted dominant color nearest in RGB Euclidean distance and replaced by it. That amounts to posterizing the image to five colors, far cruder than Chang et al.'s palette-based photo recoloring (SIGGRAPH 2015), which transfers color smoothly in Lab space and preserves gradients.

限制Limitations

最大的缺口：沒有證明模型用到了音樂。 自迴歸模型會把前一格的預測餵回去，光靠「上一格的顏色」就能預測出平滑、接近真值的變化。缺的對照是：拿掉音訊、只用色彩歷史的自迴歸模型，或把測試曲配上別首歌的音訊。沒有這兩個對照，9.5% 的 ACBD 改善可能主要來自色彩先驗，而不是音樂—色彩對應——這恰恰是論文的核心主張。

真值本身不是「音樂的顏色」。 MV 的配色由導演、剪接、場景與調色決定，鏡頭一切換主色就跳。ATBD 要模型追蹤的時間變化，很大一部分是剪接節奏，不是音樂內容。論文把官方 MV 的顏色當成音樂配色的真值，卻沒討論這個代理（proxy）有多可靠。

指標設計對排名很敏感。 ACBD 依頻率排名逐一比對主色：如果兩個顏色面積相近、在預測與真值裡排名對調，即使調色盤完全相同也會被重罰。指標也是在 RGB 格標籤上算絕對差，而不是 CIEDE2000 這類感知色差。比較公平的做法是用匈牙利演算法做排名無關的配對，再在 Lab 空間算距離。

規模與統計。 測試集只有 20 支 DEAP 影片，表 1 沒有變異數、沒有多個隨機種子，也沒有顯著性檢定。三個模型的學習率與訓練 epoch 各不相同（靜態 MLP 100 epoch、5×10⁻⁴；非自迴歸 50 epoch、1×10⁻⁴；本文 50 epoch、5×10⁻⁵），沒說是怎麼調的。主觀評估有 26 位評分者、五組影片、5 分量表，但分數只畫在圖 3，正文沒有任何數值或檢定——我無法從文字確認差距有多大。

片段邊界。 2 秒片段各自獨立處理，論文沒說前一片段的最後一組顏色會不會餵進下一片段；如果不會，每 2 秒就可能出現一次跳色，正好是作者批評非自迴歸版的「閃爍」問題。

The biggest gap: nothing shows the model uses the music. The autoregressive model feeds its previous prediction back in, and the previous frame's color alone can predict smooth changes close to the ground truth. The missing controls are an autoregressive model with the audio removed that uses only color history, or test songs paired with another song's audio. Without either, the 9.5% ACBD gain may come mostly from a color prior rather than music–color correspondence, which is exactly the paper's central claim.

The ground truth is not "the color of the music". A music video's palette is set by the director, editing, sets and grading, and dominant colors jump at every shot cut. Much of the temporal change ATBD asks the model to track is editing rhythm, not musical content. The paper treats official music-video colors as ground truth for music–color mapping without discussing how reliable that proxy is.

The metric is rank-sensitive. ACBD compares dominant colors rank by rank in frequency order: if two colors with similar area swap ranks between prediction and ground truth, the score is heavily penalized even when the palettes are identical. It also takes absolute differences of RGB bin labels rather than a perceptual difference such as CIEDE2000. A fairer design would use Hungarian matching for rank-free pairing and measure distance in Lab space.

Scale and statistics. The test set is just 20 DEAP videos, and Table 1 has no variance, no multiple seeds and no significance tests. The three models use different learning rates and epochs (static MLP 100 epochs at 5×10⁻⁴; non-autoregressive 50 at 1×10⁻⁴; proposed 50 at 5×10⁻⁵), with no word on how they were tuned. The subjective study has 26 raters, five video sets and a 5-point scale, but the scores appear only in Figure 3, with no numbers or tests in the text, so I cannot confirm the size of the gaps from the prose.

Segment boundaries. The 2-second segments are processed independently, and the paper does not say whether the last colors of one segment feed into the next; if not, a color jump may occur every 2 seconds, exactly the "flicker" the authors criticize in the non-autoregressive model.

討論Discussion

有數字支撐的部分。 表 1 只有三列：靜態 MLP 的 ACBD／ATBD 是 4.6757／0.9959，非自迴歸 Transformer 是 3.8853／1.0068，本文是 3.5161／0.8671。ACBD 的單位是「格」，一格等於 16 個 RGB 階，所以本文平均每個通道仍偏離約 3.5 格、約 56 個 RGB 階——離「準確預測顏色」還很遠。

ATBD 那一欄藏了一個論文沒講的事實。 靜態 MLP 在一首歌內輸出固定的顏色，預測變化為零，所以它的 ATBD 恰好等於真值的平均逐格變化量，約 0.996 格。換句話說，「完全不預測任何變化」就能拿到 0.9959；非自迴歸 Transformer 的 1.0068 比這還差；本文的 0.8671 只比「不動」好 12.9%。所謂「更能捕捉時間變化」，實際幅度就是這麼多。

相對改善的幅度。 對非自迴歸版，ACBD 低 9.5%、ATBD 低 13.9%；對靜態 MLP，ACBD 低 24.8%、ATBD 低 12.9%。這個對照確實是乾淨的單一變因：自迴歸與非自迴歸兩版的差別只在「有沒有看前面的預測」，所以「條件化在前一格上有幫助」是站得住的。站不住的是更強的那句——「音樂驅動」的部分到底貢獻多少，表 1 回答不了。

值得肯定的地方。 把問題從靜態改成時間序列是合理的方向，資料切分在四個效價—喚醒度象限之間平衡，也讓測試不會被單一情緒類型主導。DEAP 的情緒標註其實提供了一個現成的檢驗機會：Palmer 等人預測喚醒度高的曲子該更飽和、更亮，作者可以直接檢查預測顏色的明度與彩度是否隨象限移動，這比 ACBD 更能回答「模型有沒有學到音樂—色彩對應」。論文沒有做這個分析。

整體判斷。 這是一篇四頁的任務提案：問題設定新、架構是現成樣板、評估只證明了自迴歸比非自迴歸平滑。若要相信它學到了音樂，需要的是去掉音訊的對照與按情緒象限拆開的分析，這兩樣目前都不在論文裡。

What the numbers support. Table 1 has just three rows: the static MLP's ACBD/ATBD is 4.6757/0.9959, the non-autoregressive Transformer's is 3.8853/1.0068, and the paper's model's is 3.5161/0.8671. ACBD is in bins, and one bin is 16 RGB levels, so the proposed model is still off by about 3.5 bins, or about 56 RGB levels, per channel on average, which is far from accurate color prediction.

The ATBD column hides a fact the paper does not state. The static MLP outputs a constant color within a song, so its predicted change is zero and its ATBD equals the ground truth's mean frame-to-frame change, about 0.996 bins. In other words, predicting no change at all scores 0.9959; the non-autoregressive Transformer's 1.0068 is worse than that, and the proposed 0.8671 is only 12.9% better than standing still. That is the actual size of "better captures temporal variation".

Size of the relative gains. Against the non-autoregressive version, ACBD is 9.5% lower and ATBD 13.9% lower; against the static MLP, ACBD is 24.8% lower and ATBD 12.9% lower. The first comparison is a clean single-variable test: the two Transformers differ only in whether they see previous predictions, so "conditioning on the previous frame helps" holds up. What does not hold up is the stronger claim; Table 1 cannot say how much the music-driven part contributes.

What deserves credit. Moving the problem from static to temporal is a sensible direction, and balancing the splits across four valence–arousal quadrants keeps the test from being dominated by one emotion type. DEAP's emotion labels actually offer a ready-made check: Palmer et al. predict that high-arousal music should map to more saturated, lighter colors, so the authors could test whether predicted lightness and saturation shift across quadrants. That would answer "did the model learn music–color correspondence" better than ACBD. The paper does not run this analysis.

Overall. This is a four-page task proposal: a new problem setup, an off-the-shelf architectural template, and an evaluation that only shows the autoregressive model is smoother than the non-autoregressive one. Believing it learned the music would require an audio-removed control and a per-quadrant analysis, and neither is in the paper yet.
