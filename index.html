MI-MIDI 2: From Observation to Control with Sparse Autoencoders in Text-to-MIDI Generation

MI-MIDI 2：在文字轉 MIDI 生成模型上，用稀疏自編碼器從「觀察到的特徵」走到「真的能操控的特徵」

Jakub Poćwiardowski, Mateusz Modrzejewski
arXiv:2610.32007 · cs.SD; cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-10-23
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：華沙理工的這篇在 text2midi 與 MIDI-LLM 上訓練 TopK 稀疏自編碼器（SAE），問的是：從觀察證據（相關係數、共同樂器、LLM 寫的描述）挑出來的特徵，推下去之後輸出的 MIDI 真的會跟著變嗎？答案大多是否定的。把音符密度扣掉之後，10,018 個重複驗證過的相關只剩 862 個；LLM 描述整體只比打亂標籤的對照組多 0.121 次（區間跨零），唯一顯著的是樂器類（多 0.692 次）。從這一類挑出的「合成器主奏」特徵在預先登記的確認實驗裡讓 program 81 出現在 14/20 個輸出（隨機方向 1/20、基線 2/20），換一組提示也重現（6/10 對 0/10）。密度校正後挑出的 42 個特徵整體只推動目標指標 0.17 個標準差，用原始相關挑出的那組則沒有效果。Why this one: This Warsaw University of Technology paper trains TopK sparse autoencoders (SAEs) on text2midi and MIDI-LLM and asks whether features picked from observational evidence (correlations, shared instruments, LLM-written descriptions) actually change the generated MIDI when pushed. Mostly they do not. Adjusting for note density cuts 10,018 replicated correlations to 862; LLM descriptions beat shuffled-label controls by only 0.121 picks overall (interval spans zero), with instrumentation the lone exception (+0.692). A synth-lead feature from that group puts program 81 into 14/20 outputs in a prespecified confirmation (random direction 1/20, baseline 2/20) and replicates on new prompts (6/10 vs 0/10). The 42 density-adjusted features move their target metric by only 0.17 SD as a family; features picked by raw correlation show no effect.

動機Motivation

在語言模型上，SAE 特徵的「解釋」與「操控效果」之間的落差已經有不少證據：對輸入反應最強的特徵不一定是能推動輸出的那些，自動解釋分數甚至分不出訓練過和隨機初始化的 transformer。音樂生成這邊卻還沒人正面測過這件事——先前用 SAE 操控 MusicGen 的工作，效果是從生成的音訊上評的，很難量得精確。

作者的切入點是：文字轉 MIDI 模型的輸出是樂譜，音符、時值、樂器編號都能精確計數，所以「解釋能不能預測操控效果」在這裡變成一個可以量測的問題。他們要做的不是再丟一份特徵圖鑑出來，而是一套把每種觀察證據都配上對照組、再用介入實驗驗證的流程，讓研究者看得出解釋是在哪一步開始失準的。

In language models there is already plenty of evidence that SAE feature interpretations and steering effects come apart: the features that respond most strongly to inputs are not necessarily the ones that move outputs, and automated interpretability scores cannot even tell trained transformers from randomly initialized ones. For music generation nobody has tested this head-on; earlier SAE steering of MusicGen judged effects from generated audio, which is hard to measure precisely.

The authors' angle is that a text-to-MIDI model outputs a score, so notes, durations, and instrument program numbers can be counted exactly, and whether interpretations predict steering becomes a measurable question. The goal is not another feature atlas but a protocol that pairs each kind of observational evidence with a control and then tests it by intervention, so researchers can see at which step an interpretation stops predicting the music.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的前作是同一組作者的 MI-MIDI（2608.06638，本站 2026-08-11 收錄）：同樣兩個模型，用探針、logit/tuned lens、activation patching 和差均值（difference-in-means）方向做操控，屬性得事先指定。這篇改用無監督的 SAE 字典，價值在於能找到沒預先想到的屬性。Prokopiou 等人（2026）也在 Multitrack Music Transformer 上訓練了 TopK SAE，但拿來做 PID 回饋控制預先定義好的屬性；Singh 等人（ICLR 2026）在 MusicGen 上訓練 SAE、用多模態 LLM 寫描述再操控，效果從音訊判斷。本站 2026-10-15 收錄的 Aria 和聲研究則是用反事實配對加探針方向去操控小調解決，也是監督式的。

方法學上，這篇的「先篩選再確認」直接承接語言模型那邊的兩個結論：Arad 等人（EMNLP 2025）主張要按輸出分數挑特徵，AxBench（Wu 等人，ICML 2025）顯示 SAE 操控常輸給簡單基線。

論文沒有明講、但最值得點出的是：成功的那個特徵，對應的是兩種詞彙表裡都明確存在的樂器 token（REMI+ 有獨立的樂器事件，AMT 把樂器和音高編在同一個 token）。沿著它推，效果上很接近直接對 program 81 相關的少數 token 加 logit 偏置。作者自己在討論裡也承認這一點，但沒有拿「直接偏置那幾個 token」或 MI-MIDI 的差均值方向當對照，所以沒辦法說 SAE 在這裡帶來了什麼額外的東西。另外，密度校正本身就是把啟動值和指標都對 log 音符數、log token 數做殘差的偏相關，是標準的混淆控制，不是新方法。

The most direct predecessor is the same authors' MI-MIDI (2608.06638, covered here 2026-08-11): the same two models, analyzed with probes, logit and tuned lenses, activation patching, and difference-in-means steering, with attributes fixed in advance. This paper switches to unsupervised SAE dictionaries, whose value is finding attributes nobody planned for. Prokopiou et al. (2026) also trained TopK SAEs, on the Multitrack Music Transformer, but used them for PID feedback control of predefined attributes; Singh et al. (ICLR 2026) trained SAEs on MusicGen, described features with a multimodal LLM, and steered, judging effects from audio. The Aria harmony study covered here on 2026-10-15 steered minor-key resolutions with counterfactual pairs and probe directions, also supervised.

Methodologically, the screen-then-confirm design follows two conclusions from language models: Arad et al. (EMNLP 2025) argue for selecting features by an output score, and AxBench (Wu et al., ICML 2025) shows SAE steering often loses to simple baselines.

What the paper does not spell out, but most deserves pointing out: the feature that works corresponds to an instrument token that both vocabularies encode explicitly (REMI+ has a separate instrument event; AMT encodes instrument and pitch jointly in one token). Pushing along it is close in effect to adding a logit bias to the few tokens tied to program 81. The authors concede this in their discussion, but they include neither a direct token bias nor MI-MIDI's difference-in-means direction as a control, so one cannot say what the SAE adds here. The density adjustment, for its part, is a partial correlation that residualizes both activation and metric on log note and log token counts, a standard confound control rather than a new method.

方法Method

模型與字典：text2midi 是 18 層、寬 768 的解碼器，接凍結的 Flan-T5 編碼器、輸出 REMI+ token；MIDI-LLM 是 16 層、寬 2048 的 Llama 3.2 1B，用 Anticipatory Music Transformer 的詞彙。各取四層殘差流，用 1,500 個 Lakh MIDI 檔（約 1,656 萬與 2,418 萬個 token 狀態）訓練 k=32 的 TopK SAE，text2midi 字典 6,144 個特徵、MIDI-LLM 8,192 個。只保留在 1–25% 窗口裡會啟動的特徵，每本字典剩 1,746–4,875 個；用重建取代殘差後，text2midi L13 恢復 99.22% 的損失，MIDI-LLM L12 恢復 95.57%。

三條觀察路線各配一個對照。第一條是 12 個 MusPy 指標的相關，對照是音符密度校正：在發現、驗證、測試三組都達門檻才算重複驗證，八本最終字典共 10,018 個原始相關，校正後剩 862 個（每本保留 3.4–12.5%）。第二條是 LLM 描述：Claude Sonnet 5 看四個最高啟動、四個典型啟動、四個配對的未啟動片段，寫描述，然後從八個留出片段裡挑四個；隨機猜平均 2 個，對照組把啟動值打亂。第三條是共同樂器：80% 以上啟動片段共用某個 General MIDI 編號、而配對的未啟動片段完全沒有，共 27 個特徵。

介入實驗：把解碼器方向加到殘差上，把特徵啟動值補到它在發現集正啟動最大值的第 50、75 或 90 百分位，每個生成步都做；對照是一條與它正交的固定隨機方向，用同樣規則。主要比較是「特徵減隨機」在 P90。篩選階段每個候選用 10 個提示（密度校正那組用 8 個），確認階段換新提示並預先登記假設、終點與四個成功條件。

Models and dictionaries: text2midi is an 18-layer, width-768 decoder on a frozen Flan-T5 encoder that outputs REMI+ tokens; MIDI-LLM is the 16-layer, width-2048 Llama 3.2 1B with the Anticipatory Music Transformer vocabulary. From four residual layers of each, TopK SAEs with k=32 are trained on 1,500 Lakh MIDI files (about 16.56M and 24.18M token states), with 6,144 features for text2midi and 8,192 for MIDI-LLM. Keeping features that fire in 1–25% of windows leaves 1,746–4,875 per dictionary; splicing in the reconstruction recovers 99.22% of the loss at text2midi L13 and 95.57% at MIDI-LLM L12.

Each of three observational routes gets a control. The first is correlation with 12 MusPy metrics, controlled by note-density adjustment: an association counts as replicated only if it clears the threshold on discovery, validation, and test splits, and the eight final dictionaries go from 10,018 raw associations to 862 adjusted ones (3.4–12.5% kept per dictionary). The second is LLM descriptions: Claude Sonnet 5 sees four top-activating, four typical, and four matched inactive excerpts, writes a description, then picks four of eight held-out excerpts; chance is 2 on average, and the control shuffles activations. The third is shared instruments: 27 features whose General MIDI program appears in at least 80% of active excerpts and in none of the matched inactive ones.

Interventions: the decoder direction is added to the residual so that the feature's activation is raised to the 50th, 75th, or 90th percentile of its positive maxima on discovery excerpts, at every generation step; the control is a fixed random direction orthogonal to it under the same rule. The primary contrast is feature minus random at P90. Screens use 10 prompts per candidate (8 in the density-adjusted screen), and confirmation uses new prompts with a prespecified hypothesis, endpoint, and four success criteria.

限制Limitations

限制方面，最關鍵的是統計檢定力和比較設計。篩選只用 8 或 10 個提示、每個候選一條隨機方向；家族層級的效應量是看過篩選結果之後才決定要這樣算的。而且「密度校正那組有效、原始相關那組無效」這個對比，兩組的提示、字典、指標都不同，作者自己也說這只描述了兩種挑法，沒有隔離出校正本身的效果。

評審這一端也有漏洞。描述、留出辨識和生成結果的評分全由同一個 Claude Sonnet 5 完成，取樣設定沒有記錄；描述與辨識在同一次呼叫裡做，而且評審看得到樂器名稱與摘要統計，樂器類之所以唯一勝出，可能部分來自這個資訊直接寫在卡片上。沒有任何聽測，終點全是符號或視覺上的。每本最終字典只訓練一次（seed 42），SAE 訓練集與特徵刻畫集是從同一批檔案獨立抽的，沒做檔案層級的重疊檢查。合成器主奏的結果衡量的是 MIDI 裡寫了哪個樂器編號，不代表模型有「聽起來像合成器」的表徵。

On limitations, the key issues are statistical power and the comparison design. Screens use only 8 or 10 prompts and one random direction per candidate, and the family-level effect sizes were specified after seeing the screens. The contrast "density-adjusted works, raw correlation does not" compares screens that differ in prompts, dictionaries, and metrics; the authors themselves say it describes the two selections rather than isolating the effect of the adjustment.

The judging side has gaps too. Descriptions, held-out recognition, and ratings of generated output all come from the same Claude Sonnet 5, with no recorded sampling settings; description and recognition happen in one call, and the reviewer sees instrument names and summary statistics, so instrumentation being the only group to win may partly reflect that information sitting on the card. There is no listening test; all endpoints are symbolic or visual. Each final dictionary is a single run (seed 42), and the SAE training and characterization splits were drawn independently from the same files without a file-level overlap check. The synth-lead result measures which program number is written in the MIDI, not a representation of perceived synth timbre.

討論Discussion

主要數字。LLM 描述：786 張卡平均答對 2.221 次，打亂對照 2.100，差 0.121［−0.131, 0.388］；樂器類 72 張卡 2.792，比對照高 0.692［0.364, 1.033］，48 張全對的卡有 21 張屬於這類；和聲與調性類（225 張）只有 2.156。自動稽核標記了 786 張中的 504 張，48 張全對卡只有 12 張通過。合成器主奏特徵（text2midi L13 f3340）：確認實驗 lift 65 個百分點［45, 85］、p=0.000122；換新提示與新種子重現 6/10 對 0/10、p=0.0156；在另外兩本獨立訓練的 L13 字典都找得到相關 0.94 以上的對應特徵，而整本字典只有 35.2% 與 25.6% 的特徵有 r≥0.8 的對應。

家族層級：密度校正挑出的 42 個特徵在 P90 對隨機方向 +0.17 SD［0.02, 0.33］，42 個裡 27 個方向正確，P50 時只有 +0.02；原始相關挑出的 24 個是 −0.23 SD［−1.23, 0.91］，只有 8 個方向正確。樂器特徵整體沒有效果（27 個裡 10 個比隨機多、10 個比較少），只有在基線完全不出現該樂器的 10 個裡，特徵方向讓 6 個出現、隨機方向 1 個。盲評描述吻合度時，通過稽核的子集只比隨機高 0.167 分（p=0.3125），拿掉合成器主奏後變成 −0.036。

我的讀法：這篇最有用的是負面結果和那兩個對照，它把「圖鑑上的特徵看起來很有意義」和「推下去會改變音樂」之間的距離量化了。正面結果則只有一個特徵站得住，而且是最接近單一 token 的那種屬性；複音數、音高熵這類橫跨很多 token 決策的統計量，0.17 SD 的家族效應並不足以拿來控制。另外兩個細節值得記住：MIDI-LLM 密度校正組 22 個特徵裡有 18 個在有提示的生成中根本不會啟動，代表在無提示 Lakh 片段上找到的特徵不一定轉移到實際使用情境；15 個指標特徵裡有 12 個，改變最大的是別的指標而不是目標指標。作者自己提出的下一步——直接看解碼器方向投影到輸出 token 的強度——其實就是在檢驗上面那個「近似 logit 偏置」的解讀。

Main numbers. LLM descriptions: 786 cards average 2.221 correct picks against 2.100 for the shuffled control, a difference of 0.121 [−0.131, 0.388]; the 72 instrumentation cards reach 2.792, 0.692 [0.364, 1.033] above the control, and supply 21 of the 48 perfect cards, while harmony and tonality (225 cards) sits at 2.156. The automated audit flags 504 of 786 cards, and only 12 of the 48 perfect cards pass it. The synth-lead feature (text2midi L13 f3340): the confirmation lift is 65 percentage points [45, 85], p=0.000122; it replicates on new prompts and a new seed at 6/10 vs 0/10, p=0.0156; and it has a counterpart correlated above 0.94 in two other independently trained L13 dictionaries, whereas only 35.2% and 25.6% of all features have a match at r≥0.8.

Family level: the 42 density-adjusted features give +0.17 SD [0.02, 0.33] over the random direction at P90, with 27 of 42 in the predicted direction, and only +0.02 at P50; the 24 raw-correlation features give −0.23 SD [−1.23, 0.91], with 8 in the predicted direction. Program features show no overall effect (10 of 27 above random, 10 below); only among the 10 whose program never appears at baseline does the feature direction introduce it for 6, versus 1 for random. In blinded ratings of description fit, the audit-passing subset beats random by just 0.167 points (p=0.3125), and −0.036 once the synth-lead feature is removed.

My reading: the most useful part is the negative results and the two controls, which put a number on the gap between "this atlas feature looks meaningful" and "pushing it changes the music". The positive result rests on a single feature, and on the attribute closest to a single token; for statistics spread over many token decisions, such as polyphony or pitch entropy, a 0.17 SD family effect is not enough to steer with. Two further details are worth keeping: in MIDI-LLM's density-adjusted group, 18 of 22 features never fire during prompted generation, so features found on unprompted Lakh excerpts may not transfer to actual use; and for 12 of 15 metric features the largest change lands on a metric other than the target. The authors' own proposed next step, measuring how strongly decoder directions project onto output tokens, is in effect a test of the "approximate logit bias" reading above.

Melody Guidance for Audio Diffusion Models Without Retraining

免重訓的音訊擴散模型旋律引導：以可微分 SWIPE 當量測算子做擴散後驗取樣，加上三角波暖啟動

Cristobal Andrade, Sebastian J. Schlecht, Ben Maman, Meinard Müller
arXiv:2610.32211 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-23
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：FAU 與 AudioLabs Erlangen 把「讓無條件擴散模型照指定旋律生成」當成反問題：用可微分的音高估計器 dSWIPE 當量測算子做擴散後驗取樣（DPS），再從目標 F0 合成的三角波暖啟動，不必重訓任何東西。在 POP909 的 100 條旋律上，歌聲的 CREPE 原始音高準確率（RPA）0.91，條件式基線 DiffSinger 0.76、Diff-SVC 0.81；鋼琴（MR-CQT）0.96，PianoKontext 0.76。但吉他上 DDSP-Guitar 的 RPA 0.95 比它的 0.92 高；SWIPE 算出的分數與引導用的是同一家族的估計器，偏向自己；品質指標大多跨訓練資料、不能直接比；而且沒有正式聽測。Why this one: FAU and AudioLabs Erlangen treat "make an unconditional diffusion model follow a given melody" as an inverse problem: diffusion posterior sampling (DPS) with the differentiable pitch estimator dSWIPE as the measurement operator, warm-started from a triangle wave synthesized from the target F0, with no retraining. On 100 POP909 melodies, singing-voice CREPE raw pitch accuracy (RPA) is 0.91 against 0.76 for the conditional baseline DiffSinger and 0.81 for Diff-SVC; piano (MR-CQT) reaches 0.96 against 0.76 for PianoKontext. But on guitar DDSP-Guitar's RPA of 0.95 beats its 0.92; the SWIPE-based scores come from the same estimator family used for guidance and favor it; most quality metrics cross training sets and are not directly comparable; and there is no formal listening test.

動機Motivation

要控制生成音訊的旋律，現行做法幾乎都是把 F0 曲線、MIDI 或頻譜圖當條件，訓練或微調一個條件式模型。這需要成對的「音訊—旋律」資料，而且每換一種樂器、一個模型就得再訓練一次。

作者想要的是反過來：拿一個已經訓練好、從沒看過旋律輸入的無條件擴散模型，只在推論時把它拉向目標旋律，不改模型、不要成對資料。這等於把旋律控制從「訓練時學到的能力」變成「取樣時加上的約束」，任何現成的音訊擴散模型都能套用。

Controlling the melody of generated audio almost always means training or fine-tuning a conditional model on F0 curves, MIDI, or spectrograms. That needs paired audio–melody data, and has to be redone for every new instrument or model.

The authors want the reverse: take an already trained unconditional diffusion model that has never seen a melody input, and pull it toward a target melody only at inference time, without modifying the model or needing paired data. This turns melody control from a capability learned during training into a constraint applied during sampling, which any existing audio diffusion model could use.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

方法本身是三個既有零件的組合。DPS（Chung 等人，ICLR 2023）用 Tweedie 公式估出乾淨樣本，再對量測誤差取梯度來引導；CQT-Diff（Moliner 等人，ICASSP 2023）早已把這套用在音訊修復這類近似線性的反問題上。這篇的差別是把量測算子換成 dSWIPE（Strahl 與 Müller，2025）——把 SWIPE 的 argmax 換成 softmax 期望值的可微分音高估計器——而 F0 估計高度非線性、嚴重欠定，DPS 的高斯似然假設在這裡並不成立。

最該對照的有兩篇。一是本站 2026-09-08 收錄的 Pitch-Class Steering（Ye 與 Zheng，2026，即論文的參考文獻 [34]），它在 Stable Audio Open 的潛空間上訓練約 12.5 萬參數的探針，再拿探針當可微分損失引導；這篇的差別是完全不用訓練輔助模型，而且控制的是連續 F0 而不是音級。二是論文沒有引用的 DITTO（Novack 等人，ICML 2024），它同樣在推論時不微調擴散模型、透過最佳化初始雜訊對可微分特徵擷取器做旋律控制，是同一類「免重訓旋律控制」裡最直接的前例。

另外值得點出的是：「從合成的三角波、在中途時間點開始反向擴散」本質上就是 SDEdit（Meng 等人，ICLR 2022）的做法，作者引用的是自己的暖啟動論文（Andrade 與 Schlecht，DAFx 2026）。只用暖啟動、不加引導，就等於把三角波做音色轉換。

The method combines three existing parts. DPS (Chung et al., ICLR 2023) estimates the clean sample with Tweedie's formula and guides with the gradient of the measurement error; CQT-Diff (Moliner et al., ICASSP 2023) had already applied this to near-linear audio inverse problems such as restoration. The difference here is the measurement operator: dSWIPE (Strahl and Müller, 2025), a differentiable pitch estimator that replaces SWIPE's argmax with a softmax expectation. F0 estimation is highly nonlinear and badly underdetermined, so the Gaussian likelihood assumption behind DPS does not hold.

Two works matter most for comparison. One is Pitch-Class Steering (Ye and Zheng, 2026, the paper's reference [34]), covered here 2026-09-08, which trains a roughly 125K-parameter probe on Stable Audio Open's latent space and uses it as a differentiable guidance loss; this paper trains no auxiliary model and controls continuous F0 rather than pitch class. The other is DITTO (Novack et al., ICML 2024), which the paper does not cite: it also controls melody at inference time without fine-tuning the diffusion model, by optimizing the initial noise against a differentiable feature extractor, and is the most direct precedent in this class of training-free melody control.

Also worth pointing out: starting reverse diffusion midway from a synthesized triangle wave is essentially SDEdit (Meng et al., ICLR 2022); the authors cite their own warm-initialization paper (Andrade and Schlecht, DAFx 2026). Warm start alone, without guidance, amounts to timbre transfer from the triangle wave.

方法Method

流程：先從目標 F0 合成三角波（比正弦波多了諧波結構，起點更像真的樂器），潛空間模型就先編碼，波形模型直接用；從時間 t_init 開始反向擴散。每一步用 Tweedie 估出乾淨樣本、解碼成波形、丟進 dSWIPE 估 F0，和目標曲線的平方誤差對當前樣本取梯度，步長按雜訊等級與梯度範數自適應縮放，強度由 λ 控制。目標音高先做 50 cents 的高斯模糊，保留顫音這類表情。潛空間模型的梯度得穿過解碼器回傳（naive latent posterior sampling），作者也承認這可能不穩定。

超參數分析在 GOAT 吉他資料訓練的 LOUDAR 上做，100 條 POP909 旋律，λ 掃 0–5、t_init 掃 0.2–1。結果是兩個參數各管一種失敗：只加大 λ，整體準確率（OA）上升但 KAD 也變差；只靠暖啟動、λ=0 時 OA 就大幅上升，t_init 越晚越接近 1，但品質代價一樣存在。作者建議的調法是：從很晚的暖啟動開始，往前推 t_init 直到旋律開始走樣，再加大 λ 壓回來。

比較實驗涵蓋四個模型：歌聲（OpenSinger 訓練的 LOUDAR，λ=2.5、t_init=0.8）、吉他（GOAT 的 LOUDAR，λ=2、t_init=0.7）、鋼琴（MAESTRO 訓練的 CQT-Diff 與 MR-CQT-Diff，λ=0.3、t_init=0.65）。指標是 mir_eval 的 RPA 與 OA（分別用 CREPE 和 SWIPE 估音高）以及 KAD、FAD。

Pipeline: synthesize a triangle wave from the target F0 (it has harmonic structure, unlike a sine, so the starting point is closer to a real instrument), encode it for latent models or use it directly for waveform models, and start reverse diffusion at time t_init. At each step, Tweedie's formula gives a clean estimate, which is decoded to a waveform and passed through dSWIPE; the squared error against the target curve yields a gradient on the current sample, with a step size scaled by noise level and gradient norm and a strength set by λ. The target pitch is first Gaussian-blurred by 50 cents to keep expressive detail such as vibrato. For latent models the gradient has to pass back through the decoder (naive latent posterior sampling), which the authors acknowledge can be unstable.

The hyperparameter analysis uses LOUDAR trained on the GOAT guitar dataset, 100 POP909 melodies, λ from 0 to 5, and t_init from 0.2 to 1. Each parameter handles a different failure: raising λ alone improves overall accuracy (OA) but worsens KAD; warm start alone at λ=0 already raises OA sharply, approaching 1 with later t_init, but the quality cost remains. The suggested tuning is to start from a late warm start, move t_init earlier until melodic hallucinations appear, then raise λ to suppress them.

The comparison covers four models: singing voice (LOUDAR trained on OpenSinger, λ=2.5, t_init=0.8), guitar (LOUDAR on GOAT, λ=2, t_init=0.7), and piano (CQT-Diff and MR-CQT-Diff trained on MAESTRO, λ=0.3, t_init=0.65). Metrics are mir_eval RPA and OA, with pitch estimated by CREPE and by SWIPE, plus KAD and FAD.

限制Limitations

第一個問題是評估工具與引導工具重疊。引導用的是 dSWIPE，表格裡一半的音高分數卻是用 SWIPE 算的——等於拿考官自己出的題來考。比較可信的是 CREPE 那欄，而在那一欄裡，吉他的 RPA 0.92 輸給 DDSP-Guitar 的 0.95，OA 0.79 也輸給 DDSP-Guitar 的 0.84；鋼琴的 CQT-Diff 版 OA 0.88 雖高，KAD 52.4 卻遠差於 PianoKontext 的 22.2。

第二是品質比較的基礎不穩。歌聲與吉他的基線都在不同資料上訓練，作者自己也說 KAD、FAD 只能當相對參考；唯一能直接比的鋼琴組（全部用 MAESTRO），PianoKontext 的 KAD 22.2、FAD 0.25 都比這篇的兩個版本好（MR-CQT 版 29.5、0.39）。所有主觀品質判斷都是「非正式聆聽」，沒有正式聽測。

第三是控制範圍。方法只管 F0，歌聲的音素、吉他的撥弦或推弦、鋼琴的衰減全由無條件模型自己決定，作者在三種樂器上都聽到這類問題；鋼琴在 MAESTRO 複音資料上訓練，單音旋律對它來說是分布外，起音偶爾多出來或不清楚。超參數分析用的也是 POP909 加 GOAT 吉他，和後面吉他比較的設定重疊，論文沒說明兩邊的旋律是否不同。實驗只做單音旋律，沒有談複音或和聲。

The first problem is that the evaluation tool overlaps the guidance tool. Guidance uses dSWIPE, yet half the pitch scores in the table are computed with SWIPE, which amounts to grading with the examiner's own questions. The CREPE columns are more credible, and there guitar RPA of 0.92 trails DDSP-Guitar's 0.95 and OA of 0.79 trails its 0.84; the piano CQT-Diff variant has a high OA of 0.88 but a KAD of 52.4, far worse than PianoKontext's 22.2.

The second is a shaky basis for quality comparison. The singing and guitar baselines were trained on different data, and the authors themselves say KAD and FAD are only relative indicators; in the one directly comparable setting, piano (all trained on MAESTRO), PianoKontext's KAD of 22.2 and FAD of 0.25 beat both of this paper's variants (MR-CQT: 29.5 and 0.39). All subjective quality judgments rest on informal listening; there is no formal listening test.

The third is the scope of control. The method controls only F0: singing phonemes, guitar picking or bending, and piano decay are all left to the unconditional model, and the authors hear such issues on all three instruments. The piano models were trained on polyphonic MAESTRO, so a monophonic line is out of distribution and onsets are occasionally spurious or unclear. The hyperparameter analysis also used POP909 with GOAT guitar, overlapping the later guitar comparison, and the paper does not say whether the melodies differ. Experiments cover monophonic melody only, not polyphony or harmony.

討論Discussion

主要數字（CREPE 欄位）。歌聲：本方法 RPA 0.91、OA 0.86，DiffSinger 0.76、0.85，Diff-SVC 0.81、0.70，WORLD 0.59、0.66；KAD 10.0 是四者最低，DiffSinger 29.6。吉他：本方法 0.92、0.79，DDSP-Guitar 0.95、0.84，RAVE 0.92、0.75，MSD 0.85、0.68。鋼琴：MR-CQT 版 0.96、0.78，CQT 版 0.95、0.88，PianoKontext 0.76、0.63，MSD 0.81、0.67。

所以「與專門訓練的條件式模型相當」這個說法在歌聲與鋼琴的音高準確率上站得住，在吉他上則是略輸最強基線。這篇的價值不在數字打贏誰，而在它證明了一個不用任何旋律訓練資料的路線是可行的，而且同一套程式可以直接套在波形域（CQT-Diff）和潛空間（LOUDAR）兩種模型上。

超參數分析的結論比表格更有參考價值：暖啟動和引導處理的是不同的失敗，不能互相替代。這也解釋了為什麼光靠 SDEdit 式的暖啟動做不到精確旋律、光靠 DPS 又會把音訊推離真實分布。如果之後要和 DITTO 或 Pitch-Class Steering 這類方法正面比較，最需要的是一個獨立於 SWIPE 的音高評估加上正式聽測；作者提到的下一步——用學習式潛空間退化模型取代穿過解碼器的梯度，以及延伸到依音高曲線補長空缺——都還需要這兩樣東西才看得出效果。

Main numbers (CREPE columns). Singing: this method RPA 0.91, OA 0.86; DiffSinger 0.76, 0.85; Diff-SVC 0.81, 0.70; WORLD 0.59, 0.66; its KAD of 10.0 is the lowest of the four, against 29.6 for DiffSinger. Guitar: this method 0.92, 0.79; DDSP-Guitar 0.95, 0.84; RAVE 0.92, 0.75; MSD 0.85, 0.68. Piano: MR-CQT variant 0.96, 0.78; CQT variant 0.95, 0.88; PianoKontext 0.76, 0.63; MSD 0.81, 0.67.

So the claim of being comparable to dedicated conditional models holds for pitch accuracy on singing and piano, and on guitar it slightly trails the strongest baseline. The paper's value lies not in beating anyone on numbers but in showing that a route needing no melody training data works, and that the same code applies directly to both a waveform-domain model (CQT-Diff) and latent models (LOUDAR).

The hyperparameter analysis is more instructive than the table: warm start and guidance address different failures and cannot substitute for each other. That explains why SDEdit-style warm start alone cannot give an exact melody, while DPS alone pushes audio off the real distribution. A head-to-head comparison with methods such as DITTO or Pitch-Class Steering would most need a pitch evaluation independent of SWIPE plus a formal listening test; the authors' proposed next steps, replacing the through-decoder gradient with a learned latent degradation model and extending to long-gap inpainting along a pitch curve, will need both before their effect can be judged.

MusicTwins: Training Audio-Language Models to Listen like Their Persona

MusicTwins：以人設摘要、個人記憶檢索與 SFT 加 GRPO 後訓練，讓音訊語言模型像特定的人那樣聽音樂

Milan Liessens Dujardin, Kevin Miao
arXiv:2610.32495 · cs.SD; cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-10-23
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Bryel Labs 與 UC Berkeley 從 85 位 Reddit 作者的貼文建出人設（背景、觀點、人格、文風，加上對音樂的感知、聯想、偏好、情感、行為五個面向），連結到古典音樂錄音，再用 QLoRA 對 Audio Flamingo Next 做兩階段 SFT 加 GRPO。在 10 位留出人設上，音樂反應綜合分從 45.5 升到 56.7（+11.2，區間 [8.1, 15.5]），人格吻合度 +8.5，一般音樂理解只變動 +1.2（區間跨零）。要保留的是：GRPO 的獎勵評審和音樂反應的評估評審是同一個 GPT-6 Luna；沒有拿掉音訊的對照，看不出模型到底有沒有在聽；拒答題的評分規則把「我是 AI」也算對；而且只有 10 位評估人設、只有古典音樂。Why this one: Bryel Labs and UC Berkeley build personas from the posts of 85 Reddit authors (background, views, personality, and style, plus five dimensions of musical response: perception, association, preference, affect, behavior), link them to classical recordings, and post-train Audio Flamingo Next with QLoRA through two SFT stages and GRPO. On 10 held-out personas the musical-response composite rises from 45.5 to 56.7 (+11.2, interval [8.1, 15.5]), personality fidelity gains 8.5, and general music understanding moves only +1.2 (interval spans zero). The caveats: GRPO's reward judge and the musical-response evaluation judge are the same GPT-6 Luna; there is no audio-removed control, so it is unclear whether the model actually listens; the refusal rubric counts "I am an AI" as correct; and there are only 10 evaluation personas, all on classical music.

動機Motivation

用 LLM 模擬特定的人已經有不少研究：拿人口統計背景、人格特質、訪談或問卷去條件化模型。但這些做法幾乎都只處理文字刺激，沒有處理「這個人聽到一段音樂會怎麼反應」——同一首曲子，不同的人聽到的重點、聯想、喜好、情緒和後續動作（重播、跳過、分析）都不一樣。

作者想要的是一個能同時對文字與音樂刺激、以跟某個人整體身分一致的方式作答的音訊語言模型。他們列出的用途包括個人化教學與創作助理、模擬聽眾與觀眾、推薦系統與獎勵模型。這篇自我定位是框架加評估協定，而不是一個已經能上線的系統。

Simulating specific people with LLMs is a well-studied area: models are conditioned on demographics, personality traits, interviews, or surveys. But almost all of this handles textual stimuli and not how a person responds on hearing a piece of music; with the same piece, different people notice different things and differ in associations, likes, emotions, and follow-up actions (replaying, skipping, analyzing).

The authors want an audio-language model that answers both textual and musical stimuli in a way consistent with one person's overall identity. Listed uses include personalized teaching and creative assistants, simulated listeners and audiences, and recommender and reward models. The paper positions itself as a framework plus an evaluation protocol, not a deployable system.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

架構上最像的是 Generative Agents（Park 等人，2023）那一路的人物模擬：人設摘要放在系統提示、細節存在記憶裡按需檢索。LaMP（Salemi 等人，2024）也是用使用者檔案加檢索做個人化生成。這篇把同一套「摘要＋記憶＋共享模型」搬到音訊語言模型上，記憶多了 CLAP 的音訊相似度檢索，後訓練用 SFT 加 GRPO、獎勵來自 LLM 評審——訓練配方本身是標準的，新的部分主要是資料管線：從作者自己的貼文抽出問答，把提到的作品對到真實錄音，題目用「這首」「第二段錄音」指代，不直接點名。

音樂這邊，TalkPlay（Doh 等人，2025）結合音訊、歌詞、標籤與對話歷史做推薦，MusiCRS（Surana 等人，2025）用 Reddit 對話推薦音訊，兩者評的都是推薦準不準，不是像不像某個人。個人化音樂情緒辨識（Gómez-Cañón 等人，2023）只納入少數聽者特徵。VocalCritic（Li 等人，2025）根據評論者的美學偏好與文風對歌唱寫評語，最接近這篇，但不檢驗是否忠於某個人的整體身分。人格評估沿用 InCharacter（Wang 等人，2024）的訪談式量表。

Architecturally, the closest relative is the Generative Agents line of person simulation (Park et al., 2023): a persona summary in the system prompt, details stored in memory and retrieved on demand. LaMP (Salemi et al., 2024) likewise personalizes generation with user profiles plus retrieval. This paper moves the same summary-plus-memory-plus-shared-model setup onto an audio-language model, adds CLAP audio-similarity retrieval to memory, and post-trains with SFT plus GRPO rewarded by an LLM judge. The training recipe is standard; what is new is mainly the data pipeline: question–answer pairs extracted from authors' own posts, mentioned works matched to real recordings, and questions that refer to audio as "this piece" or "the second recording" without naming it.

On the music side, TalkPlay (Doh et al., 2025) combines audio, lyrics, tags, and conversation history for recommendation, and MusiCRS (Surana et al., 2025) recommends audio from Reddit conversations; both evaluate recommendation accuracy, not resemblance to a person. Personalized music emotion recognition (Gómez-Cañón et al., 2023) includes only a few listener traits. VocalCritic (Li et al., 2025), which writes singing critiques from a critic's aesthetic preferences and style, is the closest, but does not test fidelity to a person's overall identity. Personality evaluation reuses the interview-style instrument of InCharacter (Wang et al., 2024).

方法Method

人設表徵分五個面向：背景、觀點、人格（大五加同理心、懷舊傾向等）、文風、音樂檔案；音樂檔案再分兩層，第一層是音樂背景與角色，第二層是感知、聯想、偏好、情感、行為五種反應。資料來自 Reddit Language–Genre 資料集的 85 位作者，每人最多 500 則貼文，優先取音樂相關版；提到的作品對到 Wikimedia Commons、IMSLP、MusicNet、美國國會圖書館 National Jukebox 等公開來源的古典錄音（因授權限制只做古典）。

管線最後得到 1,276 組問答：外推 416、內插 381、回憶 207、回憶加內插 104、拒答 168。四個學習目標的意思是：回憶靠記憶裡的明確證據，內插靠系統提示裡的摘要，外推在沒有直接先例時靠聽和整體人設推斷，拒答則是對沒有證據支持的個人事實說不知道。為了減少洩漏，相似貼文先用 TF-IDF 加 LSA 分群，再以群為單位指派目標。切成 1,060 組訓練、216 組驗證（4 位人設），評估另用 10 位留出人設的 600 組問答—音訊題。

訓練：凍結 Audio Flamingo Next 的音訊編碼器與連接層，只用 4-bit QLoRA（rank 16）訓練語言模型主幹。第一階段 SFT 只用回憶、內插、拒答；第二階段加入外推；第三階段 GRPO，每題取樣 8 個回答，由 LLM 評審給吻合分數，吻合 4 分以上再加人格與文風獎勵各 0.15。記憶用 RAGFlow 衍生實作做 BM25 加稠密嵌入檢索，有音訊的查詢再加 CLAP 相似度，最多回傳 10 則。每段錄音最多用 30 秒。

The persona representation has five dimensions: background, views, personality (Big Five plus empathy, proneness to nostalgia, and so on), style, and a musical profile. The musical profile has two tiers: musical background and roles, then five kinds of response (perception, association, preference, affect, behavior). Data come from 85 authors in the Reddit Language–Genre dataset, up to 500 posts each, preferring music subreddits; mentioned works are matched to classical recordings from public sources such as Wikimedia Commons, IMSLP, MusicNet, and the Library of Congress National Jukebox (classical only, for licensing reasons).

The pipeline yields 1,276 question–answer pairs: 416 extrapolation, 381 interpolation, 207 recall, 104 recall-and-interpolation, and 168 anti-hallucination. Recall uses explicit evidence in memory, interpolation uses the summary in the system prompt, extrapolation answers by listening and inferring from the overall persona when there is no direct precedent, and anti-hallucination declines personal facts the evidence does not support. To limit leakage, similar posts are clustered with TF-IDF plus LSA and objectives are assigned per cluster. The split is 1,060 training and 216 validation pairs (4 personas), with evaluation on a separate set of 600 question–answer–audio items from 10 held-out personas.

Training freezes Audio Flamingo Next's audio encoder and connector and trains only the language backbone with 4-bit QLoRA (rank 16). Stage one SFT uses recall, interpolation, and anti-hallucination only; stage two adds extrapolation; stage three is GRPO with 8 sampled responses per question, scored for agreement by an LLM judge, with personality and style bonuses of 0.15 each once agreement reaches 4. Memory uses a RAGFlow-derived BM25 plus dense-embedding retrieval, with CLAP similarity added for queries with audio, returning up to 10 entries. Each recording is capped at 30 seconds.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是評審。GRPO 的獎勵評審是 GPT-6 Luna，音樂反應的評估（相似度評分與拒答判定）用的也是 GPT-6 Luna——模型被訓練去討好的對象，就是之後給它打分的對象。音樂反應這項 +11.2 分最大的貢獻來自內插、外推與拒答，而這三項在驗證集上都在 GRPO 階段上升，很難排除一部分是學到了這個評審的偏好。附錄也明說 bootstrap 區間不涵蓋評審的系統性偏差。

第二是看不出模型有沒有在「聽」。沒有拿掉音訊或換成無關音訊的對照，所以外推題的進步可能來自人設文字與題目本身，而不是音訊內容。「隨機人設加隨機記憶」的條件只拿來測一般音樂理解，沒有用在人設忠實度上。

第三是規模與指標。評估只有 10 位人設，按人設重抽的 bootstrap 區間只是近似；觀點（+6.7，區間 [−3.2, +17.8]）、回憶（+8.3）與拒答（+18.0，區間 [−4.0, +40.0]）的區間都跨零，背景甚至下降 0.8。拒答的評分規則接受「我是 AI」式的否認，所以拒答準確率本身不衡量人設忠實度。文風的作者質心是用所有保留貼文算的，沒有和人設建構分開。人格參考值本身也是從 Reddit 文字推出來的代理指標。作者自己承認的限制還有：人設被當成靜態、只測一個骨幹模型、只有古典音樂。

The biggest problem is the judge. GRPO's reward judge is GPT-6 Luna, and musical-response evaluation (similarity scoring and refusal classification) also uses GPT-6 Luna: the model is trained to please the same judge that later scores it. The largest contributors to the +11.2 musical-response gain are interpolation, extrapolation, and anti-hallucination, and all three rise during the GRPO stage on validation, so part of it may be learned preference for this judge. The appendix states outright that the bootstrap intervals do not account for systematic judge bias.

Second, it is unclear whether the model is listening. There is no control that removes the audio or swaps in unrelated audio, so extrapolation gains may come from the persona text and the question rather than the audio. The random-persona, random-memory condition is used only to test general music understanding, not persona fidelity.

Third, scale and metrics. Evaluation covers only 10 personas, and the persona-resampled bootstrap intervals are approximate; views (+6.7, interval [−3.2, +17.8]), recall (+8.3), and anti-hallucination (+18.0, interval [−4.0, +40.0]) all have intervals spanning zero, and background even drops 0.8. The refusal rubric accepts an "I am an AI" denial, so refusal accuracy by itself does not measure persona fidelity. The style centroid is computed from all retained posts and is not held out from persona construction. The personality references are themselves proxies inferred from Reddit text. Limitations the authors acknowledge include treating personas as static, testing a single backbone, and covering only classical music.

討論Discussion

主要數字（10 位留出人設，Base 指同樣給人設與記憶、但沒經過 MusicTwins 訓練的 Audio Flamingo Next）。五項平均 73.8 → 79.4（+5.7，[3.8, 8.2]）；音樂反應 45.5 → 56.7（+11.2，[8.1, 15.5]），其中內插 38.0 → 52.3（+14.3）、外推 31.5 → 40.7（+9.2，[4.9, 15.3]）、拒答 40.0 → 58.0；人格 74.0 → 82.5（+8.5，[3.6, 13.4]）；文風 77.9 → 80.5，高於同一作者隨機拆兩半的參考值 79.3；一般音樂理解（MuChoMusic、HumMusQA 與 MMAR 音樂題共 1,713 題）68.0 → 69.2，沒有災難性遺忘。

驗證集上三階段的變化很有參考價值：第一階段 SFT 讓拒答準確率從 35.7 升到 85.7，但外推從 42.2 掉到 29.2，代表學會依賴明確證據的同時，沒證據時的作答變差；第二階段把外推補回 41.1，但平均幾乎不動（60.1 → 60.0）；GRPO 才把平均推到 67.9、外推 50.0、拒答 92.9。這說明幾個目標之間確實互相拉扯，而 GRPO 在平衡它們——前提是那個評審的分數可信。

這篇最有價值的可能是評估的想法：主觀題（聯想、情感）沒有標準答案，但如果條件化在一個有文字紀錄的具體的人身上，答案範圍就縮小到可以評分。要讓這個想法站穩，下一步需要的是：換一個與獎勵不同的評審重評、加上拿掉音訊的對照、以及人類（最好是本人）判斷的吻合度，而不是只靠 LLM 的相似度分數。

Main numbers (10 held-out personas; Base is Audio Flamingo Next given the same persona and memory but without MusicTwins training). Five-component average 73.8 → 79.4 (+5.7, [3.8, 8.2]); musical response 45.5 → 56.7 (+11.2, [8.1, 15.5]), with interpolation 38.0 → 52.3 (+14.3), extrapolation 31.5 → 40.7 (+9.2, [4.9, 15.3]), and anti-hallucination 40.0 → 58.0; personality 74.0 → 82.5 (+8.5, [3.6, 13.4]); style 77.9 → 80.5, above the within-author random-split reference of 79.3; general music understanding (1,713 questions from MuChoMusic, HumMusQA, and the music items of MMAR) 68.0 → 69.2, so no catastrophic forgetting.

The stage-by-stage validation results are instructive: stage-one SFT lifts refusal accuracy from 35.7 to 85.7 but drops extrapolation from 42.2 to 29.2, so learning to lean on explicit evidence hurts answering without it; stage two restores extrapolation to 41.1 but leaves the mean almost unchanged (60.1 → 60.0); only GRPO raises the mean to 67.9, with extrapolation at 50.0 and refusal at 92.9. The objectives do pull against each other, and GRPO balances them, provided that judge's scores can be trusted.

The most valuable idea here may be the evaluation one: subjective questions (associations, affect) have no single right answer, but conditioning on a specific, documented person narrows the plausible answers enough to score. To make that idea stand up, the next steps are re-scoring with a judge different from the reward model, adding an audio-removed control, and human (ideally the persons themselves) judgments of fit, rather than relying only on LLM similarity scores.
