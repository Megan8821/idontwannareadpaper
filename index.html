CASM: Context-Aware Semi-Markov Post-Processor for Beat Tracking

CASM:依局部週期是否明確來調整約束強度的半馬可夫節拍後處理器

Zhanhong He, Hanyu Meng, Yaolong Ju
arXiv:2610.09318 · eess.AS; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：節拍追蹤的後處理器多年來被 madmom 的 DBN 壟斷,而 DBN 很吃 BPM 範圍設定。CASM 在活化峰值上做半馬可夫解碼,依「最佳週期贏過次佳週期多少」來決定節奏約束要多硬;在三個凍結骨幹上全部指標都比直接取峰值高,但 F1 的提升只有 +0.3 到 +2.1 個百分點,真正的收穫在連續性指標(TCN 的 GTZAN 下拍 CMLt +9.2)。Why this one: Beat-tracking post-processing has long been dominated by madmom's DBN, which is sensitive to its BPM range. CASM runs semi-Markov decoding over activation peaks and sets how hard the rhythmic constraint is by how much the best period beats the runner-up. On three frozen backbones it beats direct peak picking on every metric, but F1 gains are only +0.3 to +2.1 points; the real gain is in continuity (TCN GTZAN downbeat CMLt +9.2).

動機Motivation

多數節拍追蹤器分兩段:神經網路把音訊轉成逐幀的節拍活化值,再由後處理器把活化值變成離散的節拍時間點。後處理器要在「相信網路」和「相信節奏規律」之間取捨:約束太弱,漏掉的弱拍和多出來的假峰值都留著;約束太強,古典演奏常見的彈性速度(rubato)會被硬拉回網格上。

目前最常用的 DBN 把這個取捨寫死在預設的速度範圍、拍號與轉移機率裡。設定跟錄音相符時很好用,不符時就得逐曲或逐資料集重調。作者想要的是一個不用重訓骨幹、不用按資料集調 BPM 的即插即用解碼器,讓約束強度跟著每一段音樂的局部證據走。

Most beat trackers have two stages: a network turns audio into frame-wise beat activations, and a post-processor turns those into discrete beat times. The post-processor must trade off trusting the network against trusting rhythmic regularity. Too weak a constraint keeps missed weak beats and spurious peaks; too strong a constraint drags the rubato common in classical performance back onto a grid.

The dominant DBN hard-codes this trade-off in a preset tempo range, meter set and transition probabilities. It works when those settings match the recording and otherwise needs per-track or per-dataset retuning. The authors want a plug-in decoder that needs neither backbone retraining nor per-dataset BPM tuning, with constraint strength following local evidence in each passage.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

後處理器的譜系很清楚。Ellis(2007)的動態規劃節拍追蹤假設整首歌只有一個全域速度,對偏離目標間隔的拍間距(inter-beat interval, IBI)施加懲罰;Böck 與 Krebs 在 madmom 裡的 DBN 把速度與拍位置當隱藏狀態,是過去十年的事實標準;CRF 類方法則學出容許局部速度變化的序列偏好。最近的 PLPDP 改用主導局部脈動(predominant local pulse, PLP,Grosche 與 Müller 2011)估出隨時間變化的 IBI 與信心值,讓約束跟著局部證據走。

CASM 跟 PLPDP 最接近,差別在信心值的定義:PLPDP 的信心來自 PLP 峰值高度,只說明某個週期「有多強」;CASM 看的是最佳週期比次佳週期高多少(period margin),所以能分辨「強但跟兩倍速打平」的情況。

要點破的是,CASM 的時長成本 λ/2·(log2(Δ/τ)/σ)² 本質上就是 Ellis 動態規劃裡對 log 間隔比值的平方懲罰,只是把全域 τ 換成局部估計、把固定的緊度換成隨 margin 變化的權重與容忍度,並且只在活化峰值上而非每一幀上做 DP。論文自己有說 Ellis 與 PLPDP 也對拍間隔整段計分,但沒有把這層繼承關係講得這麼直白。

The lineage of post-processors is clear. Ellis's (2007) dynamic-programming beat tracker assumes one global tempo per track and penalises inter-beat intervals (IBIs) that deviate from it; the DBN of Böck and Krebs in madmom treats tempo and beat position as hidden states and has been the de facto standard for a decade; CRF-style methods learn sequence preferences that allow local tempo change. The recent PLPDP derives a time-varying IBI and confidence from the predominant local pulse (PLP, Grosche and Müller 2011), letting the constraint follow local evidence.

CASM is closest to PLPDP. The difference is the confidence: PLPDP's comes from PLP peak height and says only how strong a period is, whereas CASM measures how far the best period leads the runner-up (the period margin), so it can tell when a strong period is nearly tied with double tempo.

Worth stating plainly: CASM's duration cost λ/2·(log2(Δ/τ)/σ)² is essentially the squared log-interval-ratio penalty of Ellis's dynamic program, with the global τ replaced by a local estimate, the fixed tightness replaced by a margin-dependent weight and tolerance, and the DP run over activation peaks rather than every frame. The paper notes that Ellis and PLPDP also score whole beat intervals, but does not spell out the inheritance this directly.

方法Method

所有高於小門檻的活化峰值都是候選拍。每個候選 i 有證據分數 e_i(beat logit 經縮放與截斷,活化值超過 0.5 時為正)。兩個候選能接成相鄰拍的條件是間隔在 0.2–2 秒(30–300 BPM)之間。解碼目標是最大化 Σe_i − ΣD_ij,其中 D_ij 是相鄰兩拍間隔的時長成本。因為一次轉移直接跳到下一拍並對整段間隔計分,所以稱為半馬可夫;狀態只有「上一個被選的拍」,不需要速度狀態。

局部週期與 margin:對每個候選,在 8 秒置中視窗內計算活化值的正規化自相關(延遲 0.2–2 秒,略偏好短延遲),最佳延遲是局部週期 τ_i、分數 q*,距離最佳延遲兩幀以外的最高分是 q_alt,margin c_i = (q* − q_alt)/q*,介於 0 與 1。τ 與 c 都再以五個候選做中位數濾波。

時長成本:每段間隔用兩端點的幾何平均 τ_ij = √(τ_iτ_j)、c_ij = √(c_ic_j),所以只有兩端都明確時才算明確。成本 D_ij = c_ij·λ/(2σ(c_ij)²)·(log2(Δ_ij/τ_ij))²,σ(c) = σ0 + (1−c)σu。margin 同時控制權重(c_ij 因子)與容忍度(σ):margin 下降時權重變小、容忍度變寬,c=0 時成本完全消失,路徑退回接近直接取峰值;c 接近 1 時路徑被拉向規律網格。這個成本讓 CASM 能補拍(倍長間隔裡的弱峰值,只要拆成兩段規律間隔省下的成本大於它的低證據)也能刪拍(偏離網格的強峰值)。

解碼與保護:用 Viterbi 式 DP 求最佳路徑並回溯,允許前段不佳時重新起始。計數保護是若 CASM 路徑的拍數與直接取峰值(BeatThis 的 7 幀 max pooling、logit 門檻 0)之比落在 0.85–1.8 之外,就直接回傳後者。下拍在已選出的拍網格上用第二個 DP 解碼,允許每小節 2–7 拍並懲罰不必要的拍號變化;若與直接取峰值的下拍 F 值低於 0.6(±70 ms),改用貼齊網格的直接結果。

實驗:用 BeatThis 公開的 18 個資料集共 4,556 首、固定八折切分,GTZAN 993 首作為完全保留的測試集。三個骨幹(BeatThis、MSCNN、TCN)都在同樣資料上訓練後凍結,所以每個後處理器拿到完全相同的 50 Hz 活化值。指標是 mir_eval 的 F1(±70 ms)、CMLt 與 AMLt。CASM 沒有可學參數,只在 SMC 第 1–7 折上做一次網格搜尋後凍結,套用到所有骨幹與資料集。

Every activation peak above a small threshold is a candidate beat. Each candidate i gets an evidence score e_i (its beat logit, scaled and clipped, positive exactly when the activation exceeds 0.5). Two candidates can be consecutive beats only if they are 0.2–2 s apart (30–300 BPM). Decoding maximises Σe_i − ΣD_ij, where D_ij is the duration cost of each interval. Because a transition jumps straight to the next beat and scores the whole interval, the decoder is semi-Markov; its state is just the last selected beat, with no tempo states.

Local period and margin: for each candidate, CASM computes normalised autocorrelation of the activation in an 8 s centred window over lags of 0.2–2 s, with a mild prior toward shorter lags. The best lag is the local period τ_i with score q*; the best score more than two frames away is q_alt; the margin is c_i = (q* − q_alt)/q*, between 0 and 1. Both τ and c are median-filtered over five candidates.

Duration cost: each interval uses geometric means of its endpoints, τ_ij = √(τ_iτ_j) and c_ij = √(c_ic_j), so it counts as unambiguous only when both ends are. The cost is D_ij = c_ij·λ/(2σ(c_ij)²)·(log2(Δ_ij/τ_ij))², with σ(c) = σ0 + (1−c)σu. The margin controls both the weight (the c_ij factor) and the tolerance (σ): as it falls, the weight shrinks and the tolerance widens; at c = 0 the cost vanishes and the path moves toward direct peak picking, while near c = 1 it is pulled toward a regular grid. This one cost lets CASM add beats (a weak peak inside a doubled interval, when two regular halves save more cost than its weak evidence loses) and remove them (a strong off-grid peak).

Decoding and safeguards: a Viterbi-style DP finds and backtracks the best path, allowing a restart after a bad prefix. The count safeguard returns the direct path (BeatThis's seven-frame max pooling with a zero-logit threshold) whenever the CASM-to-direct beat-count ratio falls outside 0.85–1.8. Downbeats are decoded on the chosen beat grid by a second DP allowing 2–7 beats per bar and penalising needless meter changes; if it agrees poorly with direct downbeat peaks (F below 0.6 at ±70 ms), the snapped direct result is used.

Experiments: the public BeatThis data (4,556 tracks from 18 datasets, fixed eight-fold split), with GTZAN's 993 tracks fully held out. All three backbones (BeatThis, MSCNN, TCN) are trained on the same data and frozen, so every post-processor sees identical 50 Hz activations. Metrics are mir_eval F1 (±70 ms), CMLt and AMLt. CASM has no learned weights; one grid search on SMC folds 1–7 fixes its parameters for every backbone and dataset.

限制Limitations

提升幅度要看清楚。BeatThis 是最強骨幹,CASM 在 GTZAN 節拍 F1 只 +0.3(89.1→89.4)、SMC 節拍 F1 也只 +0.3(62.7→63.0);較大的數字都在連續性指標與下拍,例如 MSCNN 的 GTZAN 下拍 CMLt +12.5、AMLt +18.3。而在同樣的 MSCNN 與 TCN 上,把 DBN 限制在 55–215 BPM 時,GTZAN 下拍 CMLt 分別是 +15.8 與 +23.2,比 CASM 的 +12.5 與 +9.2 還高。所以「全面勝過 DBN」只在 F1 與 SMC 上成立,在 GTZAN 下拍連續性上不成立。

校準資料的切法值得追問。主表的 CASM 參數在 SMC 第 1–7 折上做網格搜尋,而主表的 SMC 數字依文中描述是各骨幹的八折交叉驗證外分數;如果確實涵蓋八折,其中七折就是 CASM 的校準資料,DBN 卻用 madmom 預設值沒有校準。乾淨的對照是圖 2:只在 SMC 第 0 折與 GTZAN 上評估,兩者都沒參與校準,而在那張圖裡 CASM 的中位數在每個校準規模都高於直接取峰值,DBN 則都低於。

其他:CASM 是離線解碼器(8 秒置中視窗用到未來上下文,回溯也需要整段序列),不能直接用在即時系統;計數保護(與直接取峰值的拍數比落在 0.85–1.8 之外就退回直接取峰值)是手寫規則,消融顯示拿掉它 SMC F1 變成 −0.3,等於 F1 的正號有一部分是靠這條規則撐住的。

The size of the gains needs reading carefully. On BeatThis, the strongest backbone, CASM adds only +0.3 GTZAN beat F1 (89.1→89.4) and +0.3 SMC beat F1 (62.7→63.0); the large numbers are in continuity metrics and downbeats, e.g. MSCNN GTZAN downbeat CMLt +12.5 and AMLt +18.3. On the same MSCNN and TCN, the DBN restricted to 55–215 BPM reaches GTZAN downbeat CMLt of +15.8 and +23.2, above CASM's +12.5 and +9.2. So "better than DBN across the board" holds for F1 and on SMC, not for GTZAN downbeat continuity.

The calibration split deserves a question. The main-table CASM parameters come from a grid search on SMC folds 1–7, while the main-table SMC numbers are described as each backbone's out-of-fold scores in eight-fold cross-validation. If they do cover all eight folds, seven of them are CASM's calibration data, whereas the DBN uses madmom defaults without calibration. The clean comparison is Fig. 2, evaluated only on SMC fold 0 and GTZAN, neither used for calibration; there CASM's median stays above direct peak picking at every calibration scale and the DBN's stays below.

Also: CASM is offline (its 8 s centred window uses future context and backtracking needs the whole sequence), so it does not transfer directly to real-time systems. The count safeguard (fall back to direct peak picking when the beat-count ratio leaves 0.85–1.8) is a hand-written rule, and the ablation shows that removing it turns SMC F1 to −0.3, so part of the positive F1 sign rests on that rule.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的不是主表,而是兩個副產品。第一是消融(表 2,BeatThis 活化值):把 margin 拿掉改成固定緊度,GTZAN 節拍 F1 變成 −0.3、SMC CMLt 從 +2.4 掉到 +0.7;margin 只控制權重或只控制容忍度,SMC F1 都變成 −0.5。也就是說,「強度」與「模糊度」分開這個想法本身是有數字撐的,而不是只是又一組調好的參數。

第二是校準穩定性(圖 2):只拿一折 SMC 做校準,CASM 的中位數就已經高於直接取峰值;DBN 則是校準資料越多 GTZAN 越好、SMC 越差,符合對校準集過度特化的解釋。對實務使用者來說,這比 +0.3 的 F1 更重要:它表示換一批新曲風時不太需要重調。

跟學習式解碼器的比較也很誠實。BeatThis-MDM 用跟骨幹一起訓練的遮罩擴散解碼器,GTZAN 每個指標都比 BeatThis+CASM 高(節拍 F1 89.7 對 89.4),SMC 節拍 F1 則較低(61.0 對 63.0)。作者的解讀是學習式解碼器偏向訓練分布,這個說法合理,但只有一組比較,證據不算多。下一步他們想把 CASM 接到 MERT 或 MusicFM 前端的系統上取代 DBN,那才是真正能看出它在最強系統上還剩多少空間的實驗。

The most valuable parts are not the main table but two by-products. The first is the ablation (Table 2, BeatThis activations): replacing the margin with fixed stiffness turns GTZAN beat F1 to −0.3 and cuts the SMC CMLt gain from +2.4 to +0.7; letting the margin control only the weight or only the tolerance turns SMC F1 to −0.5 in both cases. Separating strength from ambiguity is therefore backed by numbers rather than being one more tuned parameter set.

The second is calibration stability (Fig. 2): calibrated on a single SMC fold, CASM's median already beats direct peak picking, while for the DBN more calibration data improves GTZAN but degrades SMC, consistent with over-specialising to the calibration set. For practitioners this matters more than +0.3 F1: it suggests little retuning when moving to a new genre.

The comparison with a learned decoder is also honest. BeatThis-MDM, a masked-diffusion decoder trained jointly with the backbone, beats BeatThis+CASM on every GTZAN metric (beat F1 89.7 vs 89.4) but is lower on SMC beat F1 (61.0 vs 63.0). The authors read this as a learned decoder favouring training-like data, which is plausible but rests on a single comparison. Their next step, replacing the DBN in systems with MERT or MusicFM front-ends, is the experiment that would show how much headroom remains on the strongest systems.

MIRA: A Musical Intent Refinement Agent for Aligning Text-to-Music Generation with User Intent

MIRA:以逐項評分準則引導提示詞搜尋,讓文字生成音樂貼近使用者意圖的代理人

Zekai Liu, Zhilin Wang, Xuzheng He, Yu Cheng, Yang Yang
arXiv:2610.10355 · cs.SD; cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-10-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把「生成的歌有沒有照使用者的意思」拆成每個請求各自的可驗證評分項,建出 100 個真實請求、1,566 項評分的 MuRA-Bench,再用 MusicFlamingo 當驗證器對黑盒生成器做提示詞樹搜尋。9 次生成預算下,ACE-Step 的 Overall 從 67.6 升到 76.3;但搜尋時的驗證器與最終評分是同一個模型,盲測人評的提升只有 3.84→4.04(5 分制)。Why this one: It breaks "did the song do what the user meant" into per-request verifiable rubric items, builds MuRA-Bench (100 real requests, 1,566 items), and runs a prompt tree search over black-box generators with MusicFlamingo as verifier. With a budget of nine generations, ACE-Step's Overall rises from 67.6 to 76.3, but the search verifier and the final scorer are the same model, and the blinded human gain is only 3.84→4.04 on a 5-point scale.

動機Motivation

文字生成音樂的音質已經很好,但評估方式還停在整體分數:FAD 看分布、CLAP 看文字與音訊的大致相關。這些分數說不出「使用者要的長笛有沒有出現」「說要像某個遊戲配樂,隱含的明亮大調有沒有做到」。真實平台上的請求常常一半是明說的條件,一半藏在參考藝人、使用情境裡。

作者因此把意圖對齊定義成「滿足每個請求各自的評分準則(rubric)」:明說的條件(base)加上從參考與情境補出的特質(completion),每項都要能單獨用聽的判斷。有了逐項的訊號,它同時就能拿來修正生成:找出哪幾項沒達成,改寫提示詞再生成一次。

Text-to-music audio quality is now good, but evaluation is still holistic: FAD measures distributions and CLAP broad text–audio relevance. Such scores cannot say whether the requested flute appeared, or whether a reference to some game soundtrack implied a bright major key that was delivered. Real platform requests are often half explicit constraints and half intent hidden in referenced artists or use cases.

The authors therefore define intent alignment as satisfying a per-request rubric: explicitly stated constraints (base) plus traits completed from references and context (completion), each judgeable by listening alone. An item-level signal can also drive repair: find which items are unmet, rewrite the prompt and generate again.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

評估這一側,逐項問答式評估在文字生成影像上早就有:TIFA(Hu 等人,ICCV 2023)把提示詞拆成問題、用視覺問答模型逐題回答,GenEval 則逐物件檢查。音訊這邊,AQAScore(Kuan 等人,2026)用音訊問答的 yes/no logit 當分數,AnyAudio-Judge(Li 等人,2026)把指令拆成二元 rubric。MuRA-Bench 的差別在於 rubric 是由音樂專家從真實 Mureka 請求修訂而來,並把「明說」與「推論出來的意圖」分開計分;評分公式本身直接沿用 AQAScore。

生成這一側,DITTO(Novack 等人,2024)在推論時最佳化擴散雜訊、SMITIN(Koo 等人,2025)介入生成 Transformer 的內部表示,兩者都要碰到模型內部。MIRA 只改提示詞,所以能套在任何黑盒生成器上。

論文宣稱的新東西是「驗證器引導的提示詞搜尋」,但它最像的既有方法是文字生成影像的 Idea2Img(Yang 等人,2023):用多模態模型看生成結果、回饋給 LLM 改寫提示詞、反覆迭代。MIRA 在這個骨架上加了 LATS 式的樹搜尋與分支記憶,以及逐項 rubric 回饋。論文引用了 Self-Refine、Reflexion、LATS 與 GEPA,但沒有引用 Idea2Img 或 Promptist 這條「為生成模型自動改寫提示詞」的脈絡。

On the evaluation side, question-based item-level evaluation already exists for text-to-image: TIFA (Hu et al., ICCV 2023) turns a prompt into questions answered by a VQA model, and GenEval checks objects one by one. For audio, AQAScore (Kuan et al., 2026) scores with yes/no logits from audio question answering, and AnyAudio-Judge (Li et al., 2026) splits instructions into binary rubric items. MuRA-Bench differs in that its rubrics are revised by music experts from real Mureka requests and score explicit and inferred intent separately; the scoring formula itself is taken directly from AQAScore.

On the generation side, DITTO (Novack et al., 2024) optimises diffusion noise at inference time and SMITIN (Koo et al., 2025) intervenes on a generative Transformer's internal representations; both need access to model internals. MIRA only edits the prompt, so it works with any black-box generator.

The claimed novelty is verifier-guided prompt search, but its closest prior method is Idea2Img (Yang et al., 2023) for text-to-image: a multimodal model inspects the output, feeds back to an LLM that rewrites the prompt, and the loop repeats. MIRA adds LATS-style tree search with branch memory and item-level rubric feedback on top of that skeleton. The paper cites Self-Refine, Reflexion, LATS and GEPA, but not Idea2Img or Promptist, the line of work on automatically rewriting prompts for generative models.

方法Method

MuRA-Bench:100 個從 Mureka 匿名請求整理出的受控請求(68 個純音樂、32 個有人聲),共 1,566 項 rubric,其中 996 項 base、570 項 completion,每個請求 6–31 項(平均 15.66)。流程是 LLM 先起草,再由三位音樂專家各自修訂不重疊的子集;指名的藝人或作品會轉成一般音樂特質,而不是要求模仿。評分項分成七個面向:風格、樂器/人聲、情緒、節奏、和聲/旋律、結構/能量、製作/質感。

評分:對每一項,把音訊與 yes/no 問題交給 MusicFlamingo,取 softmax 後 yes 的機率當軟分數,先在單一音訊內平均、再跨音訊平均。主指標 Overall 是全部項目;另報 Base、Completion,以及七個面向等權平均的 D-Macro。

評估器驗證:三位專家對 25 個請求、100 個片段、1,536 項給 20 分制評分。MusicFlamingo AQA 的逐項 Spearman 是 .690、片段層級 Pearson .817、同請求內兩兩排序正確率 79.59%;Qwen3-Omni-30B 只有 .509,AnyAudio-Judge .462,CLAPScore .245。

MIRA:保留原始請求當作不可改動的意圖錨點,必要時呼叫外部工具(音樂知識檢索、參考音訊理解、情境到音樂屬性的對應),工具輸出要通過證據閘門(有可追溯來源與足夠信心)才採用,再由文字 LLM(Kimi 2.6)寫出線上 rubric,搜尋開始前就固定。搜尋樹的每個節點存放完整提示詞、生成的音訊、逐項 MusicFlamingo 分數,以及沿分支累積的記憶。

每一層從保留的父節點提出數個完整的新提示詞並各自生成、驗證;分數低於 η = 0.60 的項目會得到針對性回饋。保留前沿時留下總分最高者,以及「最低四分之一項目平均」最好的互補候選。記憶記錄每項相對父節點與同層其他候選的變化,絕對值不超過 ε = 0.03 視為穩定,據此標出要保留、尚未解決、會造成退步的方向;記憶只沿自己的分支傳遞,不跨分支共享。預算 B 計的是生成次數(含初始那次),實驗用 B = 3 與 B = 9,最後回傳總分最高的節點。

MuRA-Bench: 100 controlled requests derived from anonymised Mureka requests (68 instrumental, 32 vocal), with 1,566 rubric items, 996 base and 570 completion, 6–31 per request (mean 15.66). An LLM drafts the rubric, then three music experts each revise a disjoint subset; named artists or works are turned into general musical traits rather than imitation targets. Items fall into seven dimensions: style, instrumentation/vocal, mood, rhythm, harmony/melody, structure/energy, and production/texture.

Scoring: for each item, the audio and a yes/no question go to MusicFlamingo, and the softmax probability of yes is the soft score, averaged within each clip and then across clips. The primary metric Overall uses all items; Base, Completion and D-Macro (equal average of the seven dimensions) are also reported.

Evaluator validation: experts gave 20-point ratings on 1,536 items from 100 clips over 25 requests. MusicFlamingo AQA reaches item-level Spearman .690, clip-level Pearson .817 and 79.59% within-request pairwise accuracy; Qwen3-Omni-30B gets only .509, AnyAudio-Judge .462 and CLAPScore .245.

MIRA: the original request is kept as an immutable intent anchor. When needed, external tools are called (music-knowledge retrieval, reference-audio understanding, scenario-to-attribute mapping), and their output passes an evidence gate (traceable source, sufficient confidence) before a text LLM (Kimi 2.6) writes the online rubric, fixed before search begins. Each tree node stores the full prompt, the generated audio, item-level MusicFlamingo scores and memory accumulated along its branch.

At each layer, retained parents propose several complete new prompts, each generated and verified independently; items scoring below η = 0.60 get targeted feedback. The frontier keeps the highest aggregate score plus a complementary candidate with the best mean over its lowest-scoring quarter of items. Memory records each item's change relative to the parent and to same-layer siblings, treating changes within ε = 0.03 as stable, and marks items to preserve, items unresolved and directions that caused regressions; it propagates only along its own branch. The budget B counts generations, including the first; experiments use B = 3 and B = 9, and the highest-scoring node is returned.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是驗證器與評分器是同一個模型。MIRA 搜尋時用 MusicFlamingo 對自建的線上 rubric 打分並選最高分,最終評估再用 MusicFlamingo 對專家 rubric 打分。兩份 rubric 不同、專家 rubric 也沒有給系統看,但兩份都是在描述同一個請求,所以「找到 MusicFlamingo 會說 yes 的音訊」很可能直接轉成評估分數,而不一定是人聽得出的進步。論文沒有討論這個風險,也沒有拿別的評估器(例如表 1 的 Qwen3-Omni)交叉驗證。

人評能部分回應這點,但幅度要看清楚:只測了 ACE-Step 一個骨幹,Overall 意圖從 3.84 升到 4.04,高於 MiniMax music-3.0 的 3.99、低於 Suno v5.5 的 4.26,95% 信賴區間在圖上有畫但沒有給數值,每個片段只有一位專家評分,沒有評分者間一致性。「YuE2+MIRA 82.1 勝過 Suno v5.5 81.9」只差 0.2 分,而且只有自動評分。

比較也不對等:MIRA 有 9 次生成加上 LLM 規劃與工具呼叫,商用系統只直接生成一次,預算也不計規劃器與驗證器的呼叫。等預算的對照(Best-of-9)只在 20 個請求的子集上做。此外,專家修訂是三人各自負責不重疊子集,rubric 本身的品質沒有交叉檢查。

The biggest issue is that the verifier and the scorer are the same model. During search MIRA scores candidates with MusicFlamingo against its own online rubric and keeps the best; the final evaluation scores them with MusicFlamingo against the expert rubric. The rubrics differ and the expert one is hidden from the system, but both describe the same request, so finding audio that MusicFlamingo answers yes to may transfer straight into the evaluation score without being an audible improvement. The paper does not discuss this risk or cross-check with another judge (e.g. Qwen3-Omni from Table 1).

The human study partly answers this, but read the size: it covers only the ACE-Step backbone, and overall intent rises from 3.84 to 4.04, above MiniMax music-3.0 (3.99) and below Suno v5.5 (4.26). The 95% intervals are plotted but not reported numerically, each clip has a single expert rating, and there is no inter-rater agreement. "YuE2+MIRA at 82.1 beats Suno v5.5 at 81.9" is a 0.2-point gap on the automatic score alone.

The comparison is also unequal: MIRA gets nine generations plus LLM planning and tool calls, while commercial systems generate once, and the budget does not count planner or verifier calls. The equal-budget check (Best-of-9) uses only a 20-request subset. Finally, the three experts each revised a disjoint subset, so rubric quality was never cross-checked.

討論Discussion

主結果(表 2,全部 100 個請求,B = 9):ACE-Step 的 Overall 從 67.6 升到 76.3(+8.7)、SongGeneration2 從 60.5 升到 72.0(+11.5)、YuE2-3B 從 76.5 升到 82.1(+5.6),B = 3 時三者也都有 +4.0 到 +6.6 的提升。提升最多的面向在 SongGeneration2 的風格(+19.7)與 ACE-Step 的製作/質感(+13.7);和聲/旋律在 SongGeneration2 上即使加了 MIRA 也只有 59.8,是七個面向裡最低的。

等預算對照是最有說服力的部分,也最能看出搜尋到底貢獻多少。在 20 個請求的子集上(表 3,ACE-Step),直接生成 0.759、Best-of-9 0.800、加上工具輔助的 rubric 0.815、改成自適應搜尋 0.826、再加分支記憶 0.836。也就是說,從 0.759 到 0.836 的 7.7 分裡,有 4.1 分只要「生九次挑最好」就拿得到;工具、搜尋、記憶三者加起來是另外 3.6 分。YuE2 上是 0.816→0.848(Best-of-9)→0.872。

細看分項還有一個有趣的現象:Completion(推論出來的意圖)是搜尋真正幫上忙的地方。ACE-Step 的 Completion 從 Best-of-9 的 0.719 升到完整 MIRA 的 0.782,Base 只從 0.874 變成 0.880,中間的「工具+自適應搜尋」還一度降到 0.862。這符合直覺:明說的條件多生幾次就會中,隱含的意圖才需要先查資料、寫進提示詞。

跨版本的商用系統結果本身也有參考價值:Suno 從 v4 的 73.6 升到 v5.5 的 81.9,MiniMax 與 Mureka 也隨版本上升,表示這個基準至少能分辨出產品迭代的方向。如果作者能公開 rubric 並換一個獨立評分器重算主表,這個基準會比 MIRA 本身更有長期用處。

Main results (Table 2, all 100 requests, B = 9): Overall rises from 67.6 to 76.3 on ACE-Step (+8.7), from 60.5 to 72.0 on SongGeneration2 (+11.5) and from 76.5 to 82.1 on YuE2-3B (+5.6); at B = 3 all three gain +4.0 to +6.6. The largest dimension gains are style on SongGeneration2 (+19.7) and production/texture on ACE-Step (+13.7), while harmony/melody on SongGeneration2 reaches only 59.8 even with MIRA, the lowest of the seven dimensions.

The equal-budget comparison is the most convincing part and shows what the search really adds. On the 20-request subset (Table 3, ACE-Step), direct generation scores 0.759, Best-of-9 0.800, adding tool-grounded rubrics 0.815, switching to adaptive search 0.826, and adding branch memory 0.836. Of the 7.7 points from 0.759 to 0.836, 4.1 come from simply generating nine times and picking the best; tools, search and memory together add the other 3.6. On YuE2 it is 0.816→0.848 (Best-of-9)→0.872.

The breakdown shows where search helps: Completion (inferred intent). ACE-Step's Completion rises from 0.719 under Best-of-9 to 0.782 with full MIRA, while Base moves only from 0.874 to 0.880 and even dips to 0.862 at the tools-plus-adaptive-search step. That fits intuition: explicit constraints get hit by sampling more, while implied intent needs looking things up and writing it into the prompt.

The cross-version commercial results are useful in themselves: Suno rises from 73.6 at v4 to 81.9 at v5.5, and MiniMax and Mureka also improve across versions, so the benchmark at least tracks the direction of product iteration. If the authors release the rubrics and recompute the main table with an independent judge, the benchmark may prove more lasting than MIRA itself.

ARIA: Audio-Driven Melody–Tone Relation Modeling for Cantonese Lyric Authoring

ARIA:從歌唱錄音推估 0243 聲調旋律計畫,再據此生成粵語歌詞

Shengyu Li, Jinting Wang, Li Liu
arXiv:2610.07902 · cs.CL; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：粵語填詞要讓字的聲調貼合旋律高低,詞人慣用「0243」四級記法。ARIA 先從帶字級時間戳的歌唱錄音預測 0243 序列(TokAcc 0.644,比最強的 Qwen3.5 基線高 0.079),再用 Qwen3.5-4B 加檢索生成歌詞。但第一階段的錯誤幾乎原樣傳到歌詞:改用預測的 0243 後,相對參考計畫的 Harmony 從 0.9742 掉到 0.8482。Why this one: Cantonese lyric writing must fit each character's tone to the melody's pitch, and lyricists use a four-level "0243" shorthand for this. ARIA first predicts the 0243 sequence from singing audio with character timestamps (TokAcc 0.644, 0.079 above the strongest Qwen3.5 baseline), then generates lyrics with Qwen3.5-4B plus retrieval. But Stage 1 errors pass almost unchanged into the lyrics: with predicted 0243, Harmony against the reference plan drops from 0.9742 to 0.8482.

動機Motivation

粵語有六個聲調,唱的時候字的聲調高低必須跟旋律的走向一致,否則聽起來會變成另一個字(俗稱「倒字」)。粵語詞人因此有一套行內記法 0243:把六個聲調歸成四個相對音高等級,由低到高是 0、2、4、3。看到一段旋律,先寫出它對應的 0243 序列,再找符合這個序列的字詞。

既有的旋律條件歌詞生成大多假設旋律是乾淨的符號輸入(MIDI 音符)或者直接給定聲調序列。但實際創作常從一段清唱或哼唱錄音開始,音高藏在帶顫音、滑音的人聲裡。作者想做的是:給一段歌唱錄音加上每個字的時間邊界,直接產出可以編輯的 0243 計畫,再據此生成歌詞。

Cantonese has six lexical tones, and in singing each character's tone height must follow the melody's contour, or the word is heard as a different one. Cantonese lyricists therefore use a trade shorthand, 0243, which groups the six tones into four relative pitch levels, ordered low to high as 0, 2, 4, 3. Given a melody, the writer first writes its 0243 sequence and then looks for words that fit it.

Existing melody-conditioned lyric generators mostly assume clean symbolic melody (MIDI notes) or a given tone sequence. Real songwriting often starts from a sung or hummed recording, where pitch is buried in vibrato and glides. The authors want to take a singing recording plus per-character time boundaries, output an editable 0243 plan directly, and generate lyrics from it.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

旋律條件歌詞生成這條線,SongNet(Li 等人,ACL 2020)用格式模板控制字數與押韻,SongRewriter(Sun 等人,2023)與 REFFLY(Zhao 等人,2025)做旋律約束下的改寫;這些都在符號旋律上工作,也不處理聲調。聲調語言這邊,SmBART(Chen 與 Teufel,2024)替華語設計中介的旋律輪廓表示,ToneCraft(Cheng 等人,2025)是最直接的前作:同樣在 Fei Tsui 粵語歌詞庫上用 0243 當控制訊號做粵語歌詞生成,但 0243 序列是外部給定的。

ARIA 跟 ToneCraft 的差別在於把 0243 從輸入變成要從音訊估出來的中介表示。音訊分析這一側,Yang 等人(2021)已經用人聲分離、ASR、字級時間戳與音符擷取,在大規模粵語歌曲上量化聲調與旋律的關係;ARIA 把這種分析流程反過來當成創作工具的前端。

至於 TRATE 裡的「關係感知注意力」,在注意力 logit 上加一個由兩兩 token 關係向量投影出的偏置,本質上跟 Shaw 等人(2018)的相對位置表示、以及 Graphormer 那類把邊特徵加進注意力分數的做法是同一招;這裡的新意在於關係向量用的是音樂量(兩字中位數 log-F0 差、百分位排序差、正負號),而不只是距離。論文沒有把這個設計跟那些前作連起來。

On melody-conditioned lyric generation, SongNet (Li et al., ACL 2020) controls length and rhyme through format templates, while SongRewriter (Sun et al., 2023) and REFFLY (Zhao et al., 2025) rewrite lyrics under melody constraints; all of these work on symbolic melody and ignore tone. For tonal languages, SmBART (Chen and Teufel, 2024) designs an intermediate contour representation for Mandarin, and ToneCraft (Cheng et al., 2025) is the most direct predecessor: it also uses 0243 as the control signal for Cantonese lyrics on the Fei Tsui corpus, but the 0243 sequence is supplied externally.

ARIA differs from ToneCraft by turning 0243 from an input into an intermediate representation estimated from audio. On the audio-analysis side, Yang et al. (2021) already used vocal separation, ASR, character timestamps and note extraction to quantify tone–melody relations across a large Cantonese song corpus; ARIA turns that kind of analysis pipeline into the front end of an authoring tool.

As for TRATE's relation-aware attention, adding a bias projected from pairwise relation vectors to the attention logits is essentially the same move as Shaw et al.'s (2018) relative position representations and Graphormer-style edge features in attention scores. What is new here is that the relation vector carries musical quantities (median log-F0 gap, percentile-rank gap, sign) rather than only distance. The paper does not connect this design to that prior work.

方法Method

任務定義:輸入是一段歌唱音訊與 N 個字的時間邊界,TRATE 對每個字預測四級相對高度 y ∈ {1,2,3,4},再用固定對應 1→0、2→2、3→4、4→3 轉成 0243 碼。訓練與評估用的參考 0243 是從原歌詞的粵拼(Jyutping)確定性推出來的;推論時 TRATE 看不到歌詞或粵拼,但字的邊界在資料管線裡是靠原歌詞對齊得到的(見限制)。

三路特徵:每個字建三個特徵向量。絕對流描述字內音高水準、穩定度與時長;輪廓流保留重取樣後的字內 log-F0 軌跡與有聲遮罩;相對流記錄字在整句裡的排序、與鄰字的音高差、局部極值、位置與相對時長。三路分別編碼後做逐字的閘控融合:softmax 算出三路權重,加權和再加上三者串接的殘差投影。設計理由是持續長音可以靠輪廓,短促的字則比較依賴排序與局部統計。

關係感知序列建模:融合後的序列送進 Transformer 編碼器,每層每個注意力頭的 logit 額外加上 u·ϕ(r_ij),r_ij 是兩字之間的中位數 log-F0 差、百分位排序差、字距、正負號與相對位置。輸出頭除了主要的四級高度,還有輪廓族、音域與鄰近字兩兩高低關係等輔助頭;訓練損失包含主損失、輔助分類損失、高度一致性正則,以及移調一致性(整段升降調後預測不應改變)。TRATE 本身只有 2.37M 參數。

DRA-TCLG 分兩期訓練。第一期以 Qwen3.5-4B 加 LoRA,只學「0243 序列→一行歌詞」的對應,不加檢索。第二期才加入從 0243.hk 建的詞典:依 Fei Tsui 訓練歌詞裡的出現頻率排序,把輸入 0243 切成長度 2–4 的子碼去查候選詞,保留 2、3 字詞、少量 4 字詞、丟掉單字(多半是虛詞),序列化後放進提示詞。作者的理由是一開始就加檢索,雜訊候選會干擾聲調對齊的學習。

資料:Fei Tsui 超過 6 千首歌,第一階段過濾後超過 3.4 千份錄音、10 萬多行對齊樣本,按歌曲切分 0.8/0.1/0.1 避免洩漏;第二階段超過 17 萬組 0243–歌詞配對。比較基線有兩個四分位規則與兩個 Qwen3.5 基線(第一階段),以及 SongNet、SmBART、ToneCraft-Qwen2-7B(第二階段)。

主要數字:第一階段(表 1),TRATE 的 TokAcc 0.644、Macro-F1 0.615、Within ±1 0.885、NED 0.347;最強的 Qwen3.5 三分支基線分別是 0.565、0.537、0.844、0.398,四分位規則只有約 0.37 TokAcc。消融(圖 3)顯示三路特徵貢獻最大,比只用相對特徵的 Transformer 提升 TokAcc 0.070、降低 NED 0.059。邊界擾動 ±50 ms 時 TokAcc 只從 0.6459 降到 0.6391。第二階段的消融(表 4)很說明問題:一開始就加檢索的單階段版本 Consistency 只有 0.1579,不加檢索是 0.7619,兩階段才到 0.9421。

Task: given singing audio and time boundaries for N characters, TRATE predicts a four-level relative height y ∈ {1,2,3,4} per character and maps it to 0243 via 1→0, 2→2, 3→4, 4→3. Reference 0243 for training and evaluation is derived deterministically from the Jyutping romanisation of the original lyric; at inference TRATE sees neither lyric nor Jyutping, though in the data pipeline the character boundaries are obtained by aligning the original lyric (see Limitations).

Three streams: each character gets three feature vectors. The absolute stream describes in-character pitch level, stability and duration; the contour stream keeps the resampled in-character log-F0 trajectory and voicing mask; the relative stream records the character's rank in the line, pitch gaps to neighbours, local extrema, position and relative duration. The three are encoded separately and fused per character with a gate: a softmax yields three weights, and the weighted sum is added to a residual projection of their concatenation. The rationale is that a sustained note can rely on contour, while a short syllable depends more on rank and local statistics.

Relation-aware sequence modelling: the fused sequence goes into a Transformer encoder, and each layer and head adds u·ϕ(r_ij) to its attention logits, where r_ij holds the median log-F0 gap, percentile-rank gap, token distance, sign and relative position between two characters. Besides the main four-level height head there are auxiliary heads for contour family, register and pairwise ordering of nearby characters; the loss combines the main loss, auxiliary classification losses, a height-consistency regulariser and transposition consistency (predictions should not change under pitch shift). TRATE itself has only 2.37M parameters.

DRA-TCLG trains in two phases. Phase 1 fine-tunes LoRA on Qwen3.5-4B to map a 0243 sequence to a lyric line, with no retrieval. Phase 2 adds a dictionary built from 0243.hk, reordered by frequency in the Fei Tsui training lyrics: the input 0243 is split into sub-codes of length 2–4 to look up candidate words, keeping 2- and 3-character words, a few 4-character ones and dropping single characters (mostly function words), and the result is serialised into the prompt. The authors' reason is that retrieval from the start lets noisy candidates disrupt learning tone alignment.

Data: Fei Tsui has over 6k songs. After filtering, Stage 1 has over 100k aligned lines from over 3.4k recordings, split by song 0.8/0.1/0.1 to avoid leakage; Stage 2 has over 170k 0243–lyric pairs. Baselines are two quartile rules and two Qwen3.5 baselines for Stage 1, and SongNet, SmBART and ToneCraft-Qwen2-7B for Stage 2.

Key numbers: in Stage 1 (Table 1) TRATE reaches TokAcc 0.644, Macro-F1 0.615, Within ±1 0.885 and NED 0.347; the strongest Qwen3.5 three-branch baseline gets 0.565, 0.537, 0.844 and 0.398, and the quartile rules only about 0.37 TokAcc. The ablation (Fig. 3) shows the three-stream input contributes most, adding 0.070 TokAcc and cutting NED by 0.059 over a relative-only Transformer. Under ±50 ms boundary perturbation TokAcc drops only from 0.6459 to 0.6391. The Stage 2 ablation (Table 4) is telling: one-stage retrieval from the start gives Consistency of just 0.1579, no retrieval 0.7619, and two-stage training 0.9421.

限制Limitations

「從音訊生成歌詞」這個賣點的前提比標題聽起來強。TRATE 需要每個字的時間邊界,而資料集裡的邊界是把原歌詞對齊到錄音得到的;真實創作時手上只有一段哼唱、還沒有歌詞,邊界就得由使用者手動標或交給外部對齊器。作者在限制章節清楚承認這點,也說沒有量過準備邊界要花多少時間,所以不宣稱比直接寫符號旋律省事。

第一階段的準確度離可用還有距離。TokAcc 0.644 表示大約每三個字就錯一個;相鄰等級的「差一級」錯誤佔多數(Within ±1 為 0.885),但只要出現非相鄰錯誤,Harmony/Consistency 就掉到 0.7568/0.5592(n = 131)。這也有一部分是標註本身的上限:參考 0243 是原詞人選字的結果,同一段旋律可以填出不同 0243 的歌詞,所以就算模型完美,TokAcc 也不會到 1。

評估設計有兩個要注意的地方。表 3 的 Harmony/Consistency 是跟各系統「自己收到的」0243 比,所以完整管線的 0.9786 衡量的是第二階段有多忠實照著第一階段的預測走,而不是歌詞有多貼合真實旋律。人評(40 題、12 位粵語評分者)所有系統都用參考 0243,完全沒有測到音訊那一段。此外,除了 TRATE 主結果平均三個種子之外,其他結果都沒有信賴區間,人評也不做顯著性檢定。

The "lyrics from audio" claim rests on a stronger premise than the title suggests. TRATE needs per-character time boundaries, and in the dataset these come from aligning the original lyric to the recording; in real songwriting, with only a hummed take and no lyric yet, the boundaries must be marked by hand or by an external aligner. The authors state this plainly in their limitations, and since they did not measure boundary-preparation time, they do not claim it is cheaper than writing symbolic melody.

Stage 1 accuracy is still some way from usable. TokAcc 0.644 means roughly one character in three is wrong; most errors are one level off (Within ±1 is 0.885), but once a non-adjacent error appears, Harmony/Consistency fall to 0.7568/0.5592 (n = 131). Part of this is a ceiling in the labels: reference 0243 reflects the original lyricist's word choice, and the same melody admits lyrics with different 0243 plans, so even a perfect model would not reach TokAcc 1.

Two evaluation details matter. In Table 3, Harmony/Consistency compare each system against the 0243 it was given, so the full pipeline's 0.9786 measures how faithfully Stage 2 follows Stage 1's prediction, not how well the lyric fits the real melody. The human study (40 items, 12 Cantonese-speaking raters) gives every system the reference 0243, so it never tests the audio stage. Apart from the main TRATE result averaged over three seeds, no result has confidence intervals, and the human study reports no significance tests.

討論Discussion

這篇最值得讀的是誤差傳遞分析,而且作者沒有把它藏起來。在同樣 500 題上,把參考 0243 換成 TRATE 的預測,最終歌詞相對參考計畫的 Harmony 從 0.9742 降到 0.8482(−0.1260);兩種輸入產出的歌詞 0243 完全相同的只有 15.2%,第一階段的 NED 與最終輸出的 NED 的 Spearman 相關高達 0.8520。錯誤連續越長損失越大:單一孤立錯誤 Harmony 0.8507、連續兩個 0.7619、三個以上 0.6843。而相對於「預測計畫」的 Harmony 一直維持在 0.9716–0.9738。

結論很清楚:第二階段只會忠實實現收到的計畫,不會修正它。作者把這寫成「Stage 2 realizes rather than repairs」。這對系統設計是有用的訊號:兩階段介面的好處是可解釋、可編輯(使用者能直接改 0243 再重生),壞處是沒有任何回饋路徑。一個自然的延伸是讓第二階段在生成時考慮第一階段的機率分布,而不只吃 argmax 的硬標籤。

跟 ToneCraft 比,第二階段在參考 0243 下的提升其實很小:Harmony 0.9684→0.9740、Consistency 0.9347→0.9421;人評的差距稍大一些,聲調旋律契合度 3.185 對 2.967(5 分制)、偏好票 160 對 142。真正新的貢獻是第一階段與那份對齊好的音訊–粵拼–0243 資料(錄音因版權不釋出,只釋出標註與前處理腳本)。粵語填詞是很少被 MIR 碰到的實務問題,這篇把它變成可量化的任務,價值主要在這裡。

The most worthwhile part is the error-propagation analysis, which the authors do not hide. On the same 500 items, replacing reference 0243 with TRATE's prediction lowers final-lyric Harmony against the reference plan from 0.9742 to 0.8482 (−0.1260); the two inputs yield identical output 0243 in only 15.2% of cases, and Stage 1 NED correlates with final-output NED at Spearman 0.8520. Longer error runs cost more: Harmony is 0.8507 for isolated errors, 0.7619 for runs of two and 0.6843 for runs of three or more. Harmony against the predicted plan stays at 0.9716–0.9738 throughout.

The conclusion is clear: Stage 2 faithfully realises whatever plan it gets and does not correct it; the authors' phrase is that Stage 2 "realizes rather than repairs". That is a useful design signal. The two-stage interface is interpretable and editable (a user can fix the 0243 and regenerate), but there is no feedback path. A natural extension is to let Stage 2 condition on Stage 1's probability distribution rather than hard argmax labels.

Against ToneCraft, Stage 2's gain under reference 0243 is actually small: Harmony 0.9684→0.9740 and Consistency 0.9347→0.9421. The human gap is somewhat larger, tone–melody fit 3.185 vs 2.967 (5-point scale) and 160 vs 142 preference votes. The genuinely new contributions are Stage 1 and the aligned audio–Jyutping–0243 data (recordings withheld for copyright; annotations and preprocessing scripts released). Cantonese lyric fitting is a practical problem MIR rarely touches, and turning it into a measurable task is where this paper's value lies.
