From Discord to Harmony: Decomposed Consonance-based Training for Improved Audio Chord Estimation

從不協和到協和:分解式協和度訓練改善音訊和弦辨識

Andrea Poltronieri, Xavier Serra, Martín Rocamora (ISMIR 2025)
DOI:10.5281/zenodo.17811559 · ISMIR 2025 · 投稿submitted 2025-09-21
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：和弦辨識(Audio Chord Estimation)在 mir_eval 的二元指標上,不同架構的差距早已趨近雪線(BTC 基準 Root 81.6% vs 本文模型 84.0%,不到 3 個百分點)。這篇論文的貢獻不是又贏了一點分數,而是先證明「標註者之間的分歧不是雜訊」:在 CASD 資料集上,mir_eval Root 一致率是 0.757,但同樣結構的隨機和弦序列一致率只有 0.145,顯示真人標註者之間的分歧本身帶有和聲邏輯。基於這個發現,作者把「協和度」直接編碼進 label smoothing,再搭配把和弦拆解成根音、貝斯音、音高組三路獨立預測的解碼器,在非二元指標 Tone-by-Tone 相似度上從 77.9% 拉到 81.7%,轉位和弦的 MajMin 準確率從 BTC 的 71.5% 拉到 75.6%。Why this one: On binary mir_eval metrics, Audio Chord Estimation architectures have converged to within a few points of each other (BTC baseline Root 81.6% vs. this paper's model at 84.0%). The paper's real contribution isn't squeezing out that last bit -- it's showing that annotator disagreement isn't noise: on the CASD dataset, mir_eval Root agreement is 0.757, versus only 0.145 for a structurally matched random chord sequence, meaning real annotators disagree in harmonically coherent ways. Building on that, the authors bake consonance directly into label smoothing and pair it with a decoder that predicts root, bass, and pitch classes as three separate streams; Tone-by-Tone similarity rises from 77.9% to 81.7%, and inverted-chord MajMin accuracy climbs from BTC's 71.5% to 75.6%.

動機Motivation

音訊和弦辨識(ACE)已經發展超過二十年,近年來效能陷入停滯,被部分研究者稱為「碰到玻璃天花板」。作者鎖定兩個具體障礙:和弦類別分布極不均(在最大和弦標註庫 ChoCo 的 8064 種和弦類別中,約 74.9% 集中在大三、小三、大七、小七、屬七這五種常見類型),以及標註者之間對「什麼算一個和弦」缺乏共識。既有研究只用二元指標(答對/答錯)衡量這種分歧,卻沒有問一個更根本的問題:當兩個標註者不同意時,他們寫下的和弦彼此之間到底有多接近?

Audio Chord Estimation (ACE) has been studied for over two decades, but performance has stagnated, with some researchers calling it a glass ceiling. The authors target two concrete obstacles: severe chord-vocabulary imbalance (in ChoCo, the largest chord-annotation corpus, roughly 74.9% of its 8,064 distinct chord classes come from just five common types -- major, minor, major seventh, minor seventh, and dominant seventh) and the lack of consensus among annotators on what counts as a chord in the first place. Prior work measured this disagreement only with binary right/wrong metrics, without asking the more basic question: when two annotators disagree, how harmonically close are the two chords they actually wrote down?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

相關研究一節點名了具體系統:Fujishima 的早期 chroma 特徵法、McVicar 等人回顧的知識驅動 HMM/DBN/CRF 方法、Korzeniowski & Widmer 的全卷積模型,以及作為本文對照基準的 BTC(Bi-directional Transformer for Chord recognition,Park et al. 2019)。這篇論文跟既有系統最大的差異,不在於再提出一個新的分類器架構,而是先做了一次跨資料集的標註者一致性分析,證明「標註分歧」跟「雜訊」是兩回事——過去 Koops 等人雖然也量化過一致率(76%~92% 不等),但只用二元指標,從沒問過分歧的內容有沒有和聲邏輯。

在解碼策略上,本文延伸自 McFee & Bello(2017)提出的和弦結構分解法(拆成根音、貝斯音、音高組),但跟 McFee & Bello 不同的是,本文不把三路輸出再合併回一個固定詞彙表做分類,而是直接從三路預測重建和弦標籤,因此不需要預先設定和弦詞彙表——這解決了 Jiang et al.(2019)的大詞彙解碼方案仍然依賴固定詞彙表的限制。

The related-work section names concrete systems: Fujishima's early chroma-feature method, the knowledge-driven HMM/DBN/CRF approaches surveyed by McVicar et al., Korzeniowski and Widmer's fully convolutional model, and BTC (Bi-directional Transformer for Chord recognition, Park et al. 2019), which serves as this paper's baseline. The real difference from prior systems isn't another classifier architecture -- it's a cross-dataset annotator-agreement analysis that shows annotation disagreement is not the same thing as noise. Koops et al. had already quantified agreement rates (ranging 76%-92%) but only with binary metrics, never asking whether the disagreements themselves carried harmonic logic.

On the decoding side, this paper extends McFee and Bello's (2017) chord-structure decomposition (splitting root, bass, and pitch classes), but unlike McFee and Bello, it doesn't recombine the three streams back into a fixed-vocabulary classifier -- it reconstructs the chord label directly from the three predictions, so no predefined chord vocabulary is needed. This removes a constraint that even Jiang et al.'s (2019) large-vocabulary decoding scheme still carried.

方法Method

作者提出一個以 Conformer 為骨幹的和弦辨識模型,輸入是 22050Hz、144 維(6 個八度、每八度 24 bin)的 CQT 特徵。核心創新有兩個:第一,「分解解碼器」把根音、貝斯音、音高組(12 維 multi-hot)拆成三路獨立輸出,再直接從三路預測反解回和弦標籤,不透過固定詞彙表分類層;第二,「協和度加權的 label smoothing」——不像傳統 label smoothing 把機率平均分給所有錯誤類別,這裡依照一個從西方調性和聲實證研究得出的協和度向量([0,7,5,1,1,2,3,1,2,2,4,6],數字愈小愈協和)來分配機率,愈接近目標音高的協和音程分到的機率愈高。

在 ChoCo 語料(Isophonics + McGill Billboard 訓練/驗證,RWC Pop + USPop 測試)上,把分解解碼器加到自家 Conformer,Root 準確率從 81.4% 拉到 83.4%;再疊加協和度平滑,進一步拉到 84.0%,MIREX 指標從 79.4% 到 79.8%,Tone-by-Tone 相似度從 77.9% 到 81.7%。同一套分解解碼器套用到 BTC 基準上,Root 從 81.6% 微升到 82.9%(疊加協和度平滑後略降至 82.8%),但 Sevenths 從 65.7% 掉到 62.4%(疊加協和度平滑後回升到 64.7%),Tone-by-Tone 則從 78.4% 升到 80.4%,顯示分解解碼器的效益在不同骨幹網路、不同指標之間並不一致。

The proposed model uses a Conformer backbone over 144-bin CQT features (22,050 Hz, 6 octaves at 24 bins each). Two innovations matter: a decomposed decoder that predicts root, bass, and a 12-dim multi-hot pitch-class vector as three separate streams and reconstructs the chord label directly from them, with no fixed-vocabulary classification layer; and consonance-weighted label smoothing, which -- unlike standard label smoothing that spreads probability uniformly over wrong classes -- allocates probability mass according to a consonance vector derived from empirical studies of Western tonal harmony ([0,7,5,1,1,2,3,1,2,2,4,6], lower = more consonant), so intervals close to the target pitch get more probability mass.

On ChoCo (Isophonics + McGill Billboard for train/val, RWC Pop + USPop for test), adding the decomposed decoder to their own Conformer lifts Root accuracy from 81.4% to 83.4%; stacking consonance smoothing on top pushes it to 84.0%, MIREX from 79.4% to 79.8%, and Tone-by-Tone similarity from 77.9% to 81.7%. The same decomposed decoder applied to the BTC baseline shows a mixed pattern: Root rises slightly from 81.6% to 82.9% (then dips to 82.8% with consonance smoothing added), Sevenths drops from 65.7% to 62.4% (recovering to 64.7% with consonance smoothing), and Tone-by-Tone rises from 78.4% to 80.4% -- so the decoder's benefit is not uniformly positive across backbones or metrics.

限制Limitations

這篇論文沒有獨立的限制章節,以下三點是根據內文推敲出來的。第一,協和度向量本身取自西方調性和聲的實證研究,套用在非西方音樂或無調性音樂上是否成立完全沒有討論。第二,評估語料全部來自英語流行/搖滾音樂(Isophonics、McGill Billboard、RWC Pop、USPop),模型在其他曲風或文化脈絡下的和弦辨識表現未知。第三,論文自己承認協和度平滑帶來的是「slight improvement」(4.1 節原文用詞)——對照表 2,Root 準確率的主要漲幅(81.4%→83.4%,+2.0 個百分點)幾乎都來自分解解碼器本身,疊加協和度平滑之後只再多拉 0.6 個百分點(83.4%→84.0%),協和度這個核心賣點對最終數字的貢獻其實有限。

The paper has no standalone limitations section; the following three points are inferred from the text. First, the consonance vector itself is drawn from empirical studies of Western tonal harmony, and whether it transfers to non-Western or atonal music is never discussed. Second, all evaluation corpora are English-language pop/rock (Isophonics, McGill Billboard, RWC Pop, USPop), so performance on other genres or musical cultures is unknown. Third, the paper's own wording calls the consonance-smoothing gain a "slight improvement" (Section 4.1) -- Table 2 shows the bulk of the Root accuracy gain (81.4% to 83.4%, +2.0 points) comes from the decomposed decoder alone, with consonance smoothing adding only another 0.6 points (83.4% to 84.0%) -- so the paper's headline idea contributes relatively little to the final numbers.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有意思的地方,是把「標註者分歧」從長年被當成雜訊的東西,重新定位成可以拿來訓練模型的訊號——CASD 與隨機資料集之間的一致率差距(mir_eval Root 0.757 vs 0.145)證明了這個訊號確實存在且可測量。這個思路可以類推到其他 MIR 任務:只要一個任務的人類標註存在系統性分歧(例如音樂結構分段、調性判斷),都值得先做類似的分歧分析,而不是直接假設標註是唯一真值。另一個值得注意的設計選擇是拿掉固定和弦詞彙表——這代表模型理論上可以辨識訓練時沒看過的和弦組合,但論文並沒有做分佈外和弦的專門實驗來驗證這個理論優勢,是這篇論文留下的一個明顯後續研究方向。

The most interesting move in this paper is reframing annotator disagreement -- long treated as noise -- as a signal worth training on: the agreement-rate gap between CASD and a random dataset (mir_eval Root 0.757 vs. 0.145) proves that signal is real and measurable. That idea generalizes to other MIR tasks: wherever human annotation shows systematic disagreement (e.g., music structure segmentation, key detection), a similar disagreement analysis is worth running before assuming annotations are ground truth. Another notable design choice is dropping the fixed chord vocabulary entirely -- in principle this lets the model recognize chord combinations never seen in training, but the paper runs no dedicated out-of-vocabulary experiment to confirm that theoretical advantage, leaving it as an obvious follow-up direction.

Perceptual Errors in Music Source Separation: Looking Beyond SDR Averages

音樂音源分離中的知覺誤差:不只是看 SDR 平均值

Saurjya Sarkar, Victoria Moomjian, Basil Woods, Emmanouil Benetos, Mark Sandler (ISMIR 2025)
DOI:10.5281/zenodo.17811573 · ISMIR 2025 · 投稿submitted 2025-09-21
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：音源分離長年只看一個數字:SDR 平均值。這篇論文找了 56 位有聽力訓練的參與者(音樂人、研究者、錄音師),對 6-stem、主副唱分離、二重奏分離三種任務的分離結果打分,結果顯示 SDR 跟主觀品質評分(Quality MOS)之間的相關係數是 Spearman ρ=0.66,而且只有輸出 SDR 高於 7dB 時兩者才吻合;低於 6dB 時,同樣的 SDR 數字對應到的主觀評分可以天差地遠。最鮮明的例子是「誤分類」(把甲聲部錯放進乙聲部,但音訊本身乾淨)這種錯誤:雖然 SDR 分數不高,聽眾卻常常給出比 SDR 預期更高的品質分數。Why this one: Music source separation has long been judged by a single number: average SDR. This paper recruited 56 listeners with critical-listening experience (musicians, researchers, audio engineers) to rate separation outputs across three tasks -- 6-stem, lead/backing vocal, and duet separation -- and found the correlation between SDR and subjective Quality MOS is Spearman ρ=0.66, holding tightly only above 7 dB output SDR. Below 6 dB, the same SDR value can map to wildly different perceived quality. The clearest case is misclassification errors (a source placed in the wrong stem, but otherwise clean): despite scoring poorly on SDR, listeners often rated these examples higher in quality than SDR would predict.

動機Motivation

音源分離的標準評測流程是:算出各首歌的 SDR,再對整個測試集取平均。作者指出這個流程的問題不是 SDR 本身錯了,而是平均之後看不出模型在哪些具體情境下失敗、失敗到什麼程度——尤其當比較的對象是超出標準四軌(人聲、鼓、貝斯、其他)之外的任務時,像六軌分離、主副唱分離、重奏分離,這些任務缺乏跟四軌分離同等的資料規模與任務共識,單一平均值的比較因此可能產生誤導。

The standard evaluation pipeline for source separation is: compute per-song SDR, then average across the test set. The authors' point isn't that SDR itself is wrong -- it's that averaging hides where and how badly a model fails, especially when comparing tasks beyond the standard 4-stem setup (vocals, drums, bass, other). Tasks like 6-stem, lead/backing vocal, and duet separation lack the same data scale and task consensus as 4-stem separation, so single averaged-metric comparisons across them can be misleading.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景一節點名了具體的評測傳承:SiSEC 2008 建立的一般性評測框架、SiSEC 2015 訂出沿用至今的 4-stem(人聲/鼓/貝斯/其他)標準,以及近年的 MDX21 與 SDX23 兩個公開分離競賽——這兩個競賽都用 SDR 作為官方排名依據。作者指出,SDX23 自己也做過聽測想驗證 SDR 排名,卻沒有找到跟客觀分數的明顯相關,這跟 Cano et al. 與 Ward et al. 過去的觀察一致。既有的六軌分離工作(Watcharasupat & Lerch 2024)已經觀察到吉他、鋼琴等額外聲部的分離品質不穩定,對音色相近的聲部特別脆弱,但沒有從聽眾主觀角度深入分類這些失敗種類——這正是本文跟既有工作最大的差異:不是再訓練一個更好的分離模型,而是替失敗案例建立一套錯誤分類法(誤分類、聲部交換、洩漏/齊唱、雜訊、頻譜劣化),並實際測量聽眾對每種錯誤類型的容忍度。

The background section names concrete lineage: the SiSEC 2008 evaluation framework, the SiSEC 2015 4-stem (vocals/drums/bass/other) standard still used today, and the recent public separation challenges MDX21 and SDX23, both of which use SDR as their official ranking metric. The authors note that SDX23 itself ran a listening test to validate its SDR rankings and found no clear correlation, consistent with earlier observations by Cano et al. and Ward et al. Prior work on 6-stem separation (Watcharasupat and Lerch, 2024) had already observed unstable separation quality for extra stems like guitar and piano, especially where timbres are similar, but never broke those failures down from the listener's subjective perspective. That's the paper's real departure from prior work: rather than training a better separation model, it builds an error taxonomy for failure cases (misclassification, channel swaps, bleed/unison, noise, spectral degradation) and actually measures how tolerant listeners are of each error type.

方法Method

作者從 5 個模型(2 個 HT-Demucs 六軌分離模型、1 個主副唱分離的 HT-Demucs、2 個用於二重奏分離的 DPTNet)產生分離範例,涵蓋 6-stem、主副唱、二重奏(室內樂與合唱)三種任務。47 組混音、104 個分離輸出中,30 個是「一般」範例,64 個是刻意挑選、代表六種失敗類型之一(誤分類、交叉互換、洩漏/齊唱、雜訊、頻譜劣化)。56 位參與者(24 位完整做完、32 位部分完成)分別對每個範例的「分離難度」與「分離品質」打 0–100 分,每題平均收到 30.4 份回覆。

統計結果顯示,SDR 對 Quality MOS 的線性迴歸係數是 1.18,顯著性 F=68.31、p=2.83×10⁻¹⁶,Spearman ρ=0.66,證實 SDR 平均而言的確是有效指標。但依錯誤類型拆開看,誤分類、雜訊、交叉互換三種錯誤跟 SDR 幾乎脫鉤——尤其雜訊類型的範例不管 SDR 高低,品質分數都持續偏低且變異度極大;反觀誤分類則呈現系統性正向偏誤,聽眾給的分數持續高於 SDR 預測值。

The authors generated separation examples from 5 models (two HT-Demucs 6-stem models, one HT-Demucs for lead/backing vocals, two DPTNet models for duet separation), covering 6-stem, lead/backing vocal, and duet (chamber and choral) separation. Of 47 mixtures and 104 rated outputs, 30 were 'ordinary' examples and 64 were cherry-picked to represent one of six failure types (misclassification, cross-swap, bleed/unison, noise, spectral degradation). 56 participants (24 completing the full test, 32 partially) rated each example's separation difficulty and quality on a 0-100 scale, with an average of 30.4 responses per question.

Statistically, the linear-regression coefficient of SDR on Quality MOS is 1.18, with F=68.31 and p=2.83x10^-16, and Spearman's ρ=0.66, confirming SDR is on average a valid metric. But broken down by error type, misclassification, noise, and cross-swap decouple almost entirely from SDR -- noise examples in particular stay consistently low-rated with very high variance regardless of SDR, while misclassification shows a systematic positive bias, with listeners consistently rating it higher than SDR would predict.

限制Limitations

論文本身在 6.2 節提到一個關鍵但容易被忽略的限制:分析用的範例是刻意挑選(cherry-picked)出來代表特定失敗類型的,並非隨機抽樣,所以不同聲部之間品質差異的比較「未必能代表這些分離模型的真實表現」——這句話出自論文原文。除此之外,測試規模也偏小:47 組混音、104 個分離輸出、56 位參與者,且二重奏與六軌分離用的是不同的私有或授權資料集(URMP、BCBQ、私有流行歌翻唱資料),彼此之間的模型與資料條件並不對等,嚴格來說難以做跨任務的直接效能比較。另外,參與者的難度評分(Difficulty MOS)與輸入 SNR 之間完全沒有觀察到預期的負相關,這個違反直覺的結果論文並未進一步深究成因。

The paper itself flags a key, easily-missed limitation in Section 6.2: the examples used are deliberately cherry-picked to represent specific failure types rather than randomly sampled, so the quality comparisons across stems "may not be representative for the separation models used" -- a direct quote from the paper. Beyond that, the test scale is modest: 47 mixtures, 104 rated outputs, 56 participants, and the duet and 6-stem tasks draw on different private or licensed datasets (URMP, BCBQ, a private pop-cover dataset) with unmatched models and conditions, making strict cross-task comparison difficult. The paper also never explains why Difficulty MOS showed no expected negative correlation with input SNR at all -- a counterintuitive result left unexamined.

討論Discussion

這篇論文的核心訊息不是「SDR 沒用」,而是「SDR 只在特定區間內可信」——高於 7dB 時它跟人耳判斷高度一致,低於 6dB 時兩者可能完全脫鉤,而現有的分離競賽(MDX21、SDX23)排名幾乎全靠平均 SDR 決定名次,沒有把這個區間效應納入考量。對於任何以 SDR 為主要評測依據的下游應用(例如卡拉OK人聲分離、混音修復工具),這代表低品質輸出的自動化品質監控如果只看 SDR 門檻,很可能會誤判「誤分類但乾淨」的輸出為合格,卻放過真正影響聽感的雜訊類錯誤。論文提出的錯誤分類法(誤分類、交叉互換、洩漏/齊唱、雜訊、頻譜劣化)本身也是一個可以直接被其他分離研究採用的診斷工具,而不只是這篇論文自用的分析框架。

The core message here isn't 'SDR is useless' -- it's that SDR is only trustworthy within a specific range: it tracks human judgment closely above 7 dB but can decouple entirely below 6 dB, and current separation challenges (MDX21, SDX23) rank submissions almost entirely by average SDR without accounting for that range effect. For any downstream application that leans on SDR as its main metric (karaoke vocal removal, mix-repair tools), this means an automated quality gate based purely on an SDR threshold could pass a 'clean but misclassified' output as acceptable while letting genuinely perception-damaging noise errors slip through. The paper's error taxonomy (misclassification, cross-swap, bleed/unison, noise, spectral degradation) is itself a diagnostic tool other separation research could adopt directly, not just an analysis framework specific to this paper.

PianoBind: A Multimodal Joint Embedding Model for Pop-piano Music

PianoBind:流行鋼琴音樂的多模態聯合嵌入模型

Hayeon Bang, Eunjin Choi, Seungheon Doh, Juhan Nam (ISMIR 2025)
DOI:10.5281/zenodo.17811555 · ISMIR 2025 · 投稿submitted 2025-09-21
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：現有的音樂聯合嵌入模型(如 CLAP、TTMR++、CLaMP2/3)都是在大規模、跨曲風的通用資料上訓練,對「同質性很高」的鋼琴獨奏音樂反而不夠敏銳。PianoBind 只用了 900 小時弱標註加 17 小時精標的鋼琴資料,卻在鋼琴文字檢索任務上大幅甩開這些通用模型:域內排序中位數(Median Rank)從 CLaMP3 最好的 39 降到 10,域外(EMOPIA-Caps)的 R@10 從 CLaMP3 最好的 46.15% 提升到 51.28%。關鍵設計是「先在大量弱標註資料上預訓練、再用少量精標資料微調」,這個策略比把兩種資料混在一起訓練更有效——域內 Median Rank 從混合訓練的 17 進步到 10。Why this one: Existing music joint-embedding models like CLAP, TTMR++, and CLaMP2/3 are trained on large-scale, cross-genre general-purpose data, which makes them insensitive to the fine-grained distinctions within the highly homogeneous domain of solo piano music. Using only 900 hours of weakly-labeled plus 17 hours of expert-annotated piano data, PianoBind substantially outperforms these general-purpose models on piano text-to-music retrieval: in-domain Median Rank drops from CLaMP3's best of 39 to 10, and out-of-domain (EMOPIA-Caps) R@10 rises from CLaMP3's best of 46.15% to 51.28%. The key design choice is pre-training on the large weakly-labeled set then fine-tuning on the small expert-annotated set, which beats mixing the two sources together during training -- in-domain Median Rank improves from 17 (combined training) to 10 (pre-train + fine-tune).

動機Motivation

鋼琴雖然只是單一樂器,卻能透過複音表現出跨越風格、情緒與曲風的豐富語意。但現有的鋼琴專屬表徵模型(如 MidiBERT-Piano、PianoBART)全部只處理符號(MIDI)這一種模態,完全沒有整合音訊或文字語意;而通用的跨模態聯合嵌入模型雖然涵蓋音訊、符號、文字,卻因為訓練資料以多樂器或人聲為主,對鋼琴獨奏這種同質性高的資料域缺乏區辨力。作者鎖定的問題是:能不能在資料量遠小於通用模型、且資料本身高度同質的前提下,訓練出一個同時整合音訊、MIDI、文字三種模態的鋼琴專屬聯合嵌入模型?

Though the piano is a single instrument, its polyphony conveys rich semantics spanning genre, mood, and style. Yet existing piano-specific representation models, such as MidiBERT-Piano and PianoBART, handle only the symbolic (MIDI) modality and never integrate audio or textual semantics, while general-purpose cross-modal joint-embedding models cover audio, symbolic, and text but lack discriminative power for the homogeneous solo-piano domain, since their training data skews toward multi-instrument or vocal-centric music. The question the authors target: can a piano-specific joint-embedding model integrating audio, MIDI, and text be trained effectively with far less data than general-purpose models, on a domain that is inherently homogeneous?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景一節點名了具體的既有系統,並分成兩條路線比較。符號音樂路線包括 PianoTree VAE、CollageNet 這類專注拆解節奏/和聲/織體的可控生成模型,以及 MidiBERT-Piano 與 PianoBART 這兩個基於 Transformer 的大規模鋼琴 MIDI 預訓練模型——但它們全部停留在符號模態,不涉及音訊或文字。跨模態聯合嵌入路線則點名 MuLan(4400 萬筆音訊-文字配對)、MusCALL(音訊-文字雙編碼器)、CLAP、TTMR/TTMR++,以及符號-文字方向的 CLaMP 系列(CLaMP、CLaMP2、CLaMP3),其中 CLaMP3 首次做到音訊、符號、文字三模態對齊,是本文在表 2 中直接對照的最強基準。

PianoBind 跟這些既有系統最大的差異,不是提出一個全新的編碼器架構——它的音訊編碼器沿用 MusCALL 的 ResNet-50、符號編碼器直接用現成的 MidiBERT、文字編碼器用現成的 RoBERTa,三者都不是新設計。真正的差異在訓練策略:PianoBind 針對「資料量小又同質」這個鋼琴音樂特有的處境,系統性比較了兩種多來源訓練法(混合訓練 vs. 預訓練+微調),並且用音訊-文字、MIDI-文字兩個對比損失的平均值來做三模態對齊,而不是像 CLaMP3 那樣用 saas(符號→音訊→音訊→符號)的分階段對齊——論文自己的消融實驗(表 3)顯示,把 CLaMP3 的 saas 對齊法換成本文的平均損失,域內 R@10 從 13.56% 大幅拉到 52.76%。

The background section names concrete prior systems along two lines. The symbolic-music line includes PianoTree VAE and CollageNet, which decompose rhythm/harmony/texture for controllable generation, plus MidiBERT-Piano and PianoBART, Transformer-based large-scale piano MIDI pretraining models -- but all of these stay purely symbolic, with no audio or text. The cross-modal joint-embedding line names MuLan (44 million audio-text pairs), MusCALL (an audio-text dual encoder), CLAP, TTMR/TTMR++, and the symbolic-text CLaMP series (CLaMP, CLaMP2, CLaMP3), where CLaMP3 is the first to align audio, symbolic, and text trimodally and serves as the strongest baseline directly compared against in Table 2.

PianoBind's real departure from these systems isn't a new encoder architecture -- its audio encoder reuses MusCALL's ResNet-50, its symbolic encoder is an off-the-shelf MidiBERT, and its text encoder an off-the-shelf RoBERTa; none of the three is a new design. The actual difference is in training strategy: for piano music's specific situation of small, homogeneous data, PianoBind systematically compares two multi-source training strategies (combined training vs. pre-train-then-fine-tune), and aligns all three modalities using the average of the audio-text and MIDI-text contrastive losses, rather than CLaMP3's staged saas (symbolic-to-audio-to-audio-to-symbolic) alignment. The paper's own ablation (Table 3) shows that swapping CLaMP3's saas alignment for this paper's averaged loss lifts in-domain R@10 from 13.56% to 52.76%.

方法Method

PianoBind 的架構本身沒有新元件:音訊走 ResNet-50 處理 128 維梅爾頻譜(沿用 MusCALL),符號走 MidiBERT 處理 Compound Word 編碼的 MIDI(取最後一層隱藏狀態的平均池化),文字走 RoBERTa(同樣用平均池化取代 BERT 式的 [CLS] pooling)。三個編碼器分別投影到 512 維共享空間,用 InfoNCE 對比損失對齊音訊-文字與 MIDI-文字兩對,再取兩者損失的平均作為總損失——這一步刻意不加入音訊-MIDI 損失,因為 MIDI 本身是從音訊轉譜出來的,直接對齊不會帶來額外語意訊息。

訓練資料是 PIAST 資料集:7,367 首、約 900 小時、文字為自動蒐集(YouTube 標題/描述,經 LLM 清理)的 PIAST-YT,以及 1,986 首、約 17 小時、專家標註文字的 PIAST-AT。表 1 比較兩種多來源訓練法:混合訓練(兩來源按 7:3 比例混合)vs. 先在 PIAST-YT 預訓練、再用 PIAST-AT 微調。結果是後者全面勝出——三模態設定下,域內 Median Rank 從混合訓練的 17 降到 10,域外(EMOPIA-Caps,88 首)R@10 從混合訓練的 46.15% 升到 51.28%。跟表 2 列出的通用模型比較,PianoBind 域內 Median Rank(10)遠優於最強對手 CLaMP3(symbolic 版,Median Rank 39);域外 R@10(51.28%)也優於 CLaMP3 的 46.15%。

PianoBind's architecture has no genuinely new component: audio goes through a ResNet-50 over 128-band mel-spectrograms (reusing MusCALL), symbolic input goes through MidiBERT over Compound-Word-encoded MIDI (mean-pooled over the last hidden layer), and text goes through RoBERTa (also mean-pooled rather than using BERT-style [CLS] pooling). All three encoders project into a shared 512-dim space, aligned via InfoNCE contrastive loss on audio-text and MIDI-text pairs, with the total loss being their average -- deliberately excluding an audio-MIDI loss, since MIDI here is transcribed directly from audio and wouldn't add new semantic signal.

Training data is the PIAST dataset: PIAST-YT (7,367 tracks, ~900 hours, text auto-collected from YouTube titles/descriptions and cleaned by an LLM) and PIAST-AT (1,986 tracks, ~17 hours, expert-annotated text). Table 1 compares two multi-source training strategies: combined training (a 7:3 mix of the two sources) versus pre-training on PIAST-YT then fine-tuning on PIAST-AT. The latter wins across the board -- in the trimodal setup, in-domain Median Rank drops from 17 (combined) to 10 (pre-train+fine-tune), and out-of-domain (EMOPIA-Caps, 88 tracks) R@10 rises from 46.15% to 51.28%. Against the general-purpose models in Table 2, PianoBind's in-domain Median Rank (10) far beats the strongest competitor CLaMP3 (symbolic variant, Median Rank 39), and its out-of-domain R@10 (51.28%) also beats CLaMP3's 46.15%.

限制Limitations

論文自己在結論明確承認限制:評測資料集(域內 199 首、域外 88 首)規模都偏小,且訓練與評測資料主要集中在流行鋼琴曲風(new-age、鋼琴改編、爵士),缺乏古典或其他多元鋼琴曲風的代表性,論文原文承認這會限制研究結論的可推廣性。除此之外,MIDI 資料並非人工採譜,而是用自動轉譜工具從音訊產生,轉譜誤差會直接帶進符號模態的訓練訊號,論文沒有報告轉譜品質或誤差率。另外,論文比較的 CLaMP3 是套用作者自行實作的「多模態項目嵌入」(把音訊、符號特徵做平均融合)去跟 PianoBind 比較,而不是 CLaMP3 原始論文的設定——公平地說,這代表表 2 的部分對照組是本文作者的重新實作,而非完全復現原作者的最佳結果。

The paper explicitly acknowledges its own limitation in the conclusion: the evaluation datasets are small (199 tracks in-domain, 88 out-of-domain), and both training and evaluation data skew toward pop-piano genres (new-age, piano covers, jazz), lacking classical or other diverse piano styles -- the paper itself admits this constrains how generalizable the findings are. Beyond that, the MIDI data isn't human-transcribed but generated by automatic piano transcription from audio, so transcription errors feed directly into the symbolic modality's training signal, and the paper reports no transcription quality or error rate. Also, the CLaMP3 comparison in Table 2 uses the authors' own reimplementation of a "multimodal item embedding" (averaging audio and symbolic features) rather than CLaMP3's original setup -- to be fair, some of Table 2's baselines are this paper's own reimplementation rather than the original authors' best reported results.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最值得留意的地方,是它示範了一個資料量遠小於業界標準(17 小時精標 vs. MuLan 的 4400 萬筆配對)也能打贏通用模型的具體路徑——關鍵不在架構創新,而在「先廣後專」的訓練順序這個看似平常的策略選擇。這對其他資料稀缺的垂直 MIR 領域(例如特定樂器、特定文化圈的音樂)有直接的參考價值:與其硬湊更大的通用資料集,不如先用弱標註資料建立廣泛表徵,再用少量精標資料校準到目標領域。同時,論文也留下一個尚待驗證的問題——三模態訓練依賴的 MIDI 是從音訊自動轉譜而來,如果換成人工核對過的高品質 MIDI,三模態的優勢會不會被進一步放大,論文沒有做這個對照實驗。

The most notable takeaway here is a concrete demonstration that a dataset far smaller than industry norms (17 hours of expert annotation vs. MuLan's 44 million pairs) can beat general-purpose models -- and the key isn't architectural novelty but the seemingly mundane choice of training order: broad first, then specialized. That has direct relevance for other data-scarce vertical MIR domains (a specific instrument, a specific musical culture): rather than forcing a bigger general-purpose dataset, build broad representations from weakly-labeled data first, then calibrate to the target domain with a small expert-annotated set. The paper also leaves one question untested: since the trimodal setup relies on MIDI auto-transcribed from audio, would the trimodal advantage grow further with human-verified MIDI instead? No such comparison is run.
