Music Similarity Estimation from Jukebox Embeddings

JukeSim：把 Jukebox 第 36 層嵌入降到 150 維、每首歌擬合一個高斯、用近似 Jensen-Shannon 散度算距離，等於把 Mandel & Ellis 的「單高斯 + 散度」老配方換上生成模型特徵；dim-sim 三元組準確率 0.8146 對 DEEPSIM 0.8050、MagnaTagATune 0.9683 對 0.9665，幾乎平手，但第一次把 MIREX 時代的手工方法（RTBOF、BLS）跟深度學習放在同一把尺上；調參與比較用了同兩個類型資料集，類型檢索結果只有圖沒有數字

動機Motivation

內容式音樂相似度（content-based music similarity）只看音訊、不看任何後設資料，最直接的用途是推薦系統的冷啟動：一首新歌還沒有人聽過，協同過濾無從下手，只能靠聲音本身找出「聽起來像」的歌。

作者的假設是：生成式音樂模型既然能續寫一段音樂，內部表徵裡一定已經編碼了足夠多的音樂結構，可以直接拿來算相似度。Jukebox 的嵌入已經被證明在標記、類型、情緒、調性、旋律轉譜、節拍追蹤上都好用，但從來沒有人拿去做相似度。

第二個動機比較像補帳：MIREX 的音訊音樂相似度評測在 2014 年後停辦，當時稱霸的手工方法（Pohle 的 RTBOF、Seyerlehner 的 BLS / CMB）從來沒有跟後來的深度學習方法在同樣條件下比過，所以「手工特徵是不是早就碰到玻璃天花板」這個老問題一直懸著。

Content-based music similarity looks only at the audio, never at metadata, and its most direct use is the cold-start problem in recommendation: nobody has listened to a new track yet, so collaborative filtering has nothing to work with, and only the sound itself can say which songs it resembles.

The authors' hypothesis is that a generative music model able to continue a piece must already encode enough musical structure internally to be used for similarity directly. Jukebox embeddings have been shown to work for tagging, genre, emotion, key, melody transcription and beat tracking, but nobody had used them for similarity.

The second motivation is closer to settling an old account: MIREX's audio music similarity task stopped after 2014, and the hand-crafted methods that dominated it (Pohle's RTBOF, Seyerlehner's BLS / CMB) were never compared with later deep learning methods under the same conditions, so the old question of whether hand-crafted features had hit a glass ceiling was left open.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

手工方法這條線。 Aucouturier & Pachet（2002）把每首歌的 MFCC 擬合成高斯混合模型（GMM），用對稱 KL 散度算距離，但要靠抽樣估計；Mandel & Ellis（2005）改成單一高斯，散度變成閉式解，快很多；Pohle 等（ISMIR 2009）的 RTBOF 加上節奏相關的 Fluctuation Patterns，改用 Jensen-Shannon 散度；Seyerlehner 等（DAFx 2010）的 BLS 是一組分塊特徵、各自用 Manhattan 距離再線性加權。

深度學習這條線。 Lee 等（ICASSP / ISMIR 2020）用解耦的三元組度量學習訓練出多維相似度嵌入，並建了人工標註的 dim-sim 三元組資料；Salamon 等（ISMIR 2021）把它用在音樂段落切分，這篇沿用那邊的稱呼叫它 DEEPSIM。生成模型嵌入的探測研究則有 Castellon 等（ISMIR 2021）的 JukeMIR、Donahue 等的旋律轉譜、Tian 等的節拍追蹤，以及 Wei 等對 MusicGen 的樂理探測。

這篇其實最像誰。 JukeSim 的流程跟 Mandel & Ellis 幾乎一模一樣：每首歌一個單高斯、閉式散度、再做距離空間正規化（DSN，來自 Pohle / Seyerlehner），唯一換掉的是特徵，從 MFCC 換成 Jukebox 第 36 層。它的 FID 選項則跟 Fréchet Audio Distance（Kilgour 等，2019）是同一個公式，只是 FAD 拿來比兩個資料集、這裡拿來比兩首歌。論文沒有點出這兩層關係，也沒有跟 MERT、MusicFM 之類的音樂基礎模型比。

The hand-crafted line. Aucouturier & Pachet (2002) fit a Gaussian mixture model (GMM) to each song's MFCCs and compared songs with symmetric KL divergence, estimated by sampling; Mandel & Ellis (2005) switched to a single Gaussian, making the divergence closed-form and much faster; Pohle et al.'s RTBOF (ISMIR 2009) added rhythm-related Fluctuation Patterns and used Jensen-Shannon divergence; Seyerlehner et al.'s BLS (DAFx 2010) is a set of block-level features, each compared with Manhattan distance and then linearly weighted.

The deep learning line. Lee et al. (ICASSP / ISMIR 2020) trained a multidimensional similarity embedding with disentangled triplet metric learning and built the human-annotated dim-sim triplets; Salamon et al. (ISMIR 2021) used it for music segmentation, and this paper follows them in calling it DEEPSIM. Probing studies of generative embeddings include Castellon et al.'s JukeMIR (ISMIR 2021), Donahue et al. on melody transcription, Tian et al. on beat tracking, and Wei et al. probing MusicGen for music theory.

What this paper most resembles. JukeSim's pipeline is almost exactly Mandel & Ellis: one single Gaussian per song, a closed-form divergence, then distance space normalisation (DSN, from Pohle / Seyerlehner); the only thing replaced is the feature, MFCCs swapped for Jukebox layer 36. Its FID option is the same formula as Fréchet Audio Distance (Kilgour et al., 2019), except FAD compares two datasets and here it compares two songs. The paper names neither relationship, and does not compare against music foundation models such as MERT or MusicFM.

方法Method

抽嵌入。 用 jukemirlib 把每首歌中間約 24 秒（Jukebox 單次最多 8,192 個編碼格、每秒 344 格）餵進 Jukebox。它有 72 層、每格輸出 4,800 維。整個資料集 500 首、所有層全存會超過 5,000 GB，所以只留一層、用 16 位元、重取樣到 100 Hz，每首變成 2,378×4,800 的矩陣，再對每一維標準化後做 PCA 降到 P 維。

每首歌一個模型。 比兩種：所有格取平均變成一個向量（配餘弦、歐氏、Manhattan 距離），或擬合單一高斯（平均向量加經驗共變異數，配對稱 KL、FID、以及 AltJSD）。AltJSD 是 Schnitzer 博士論文裡的近似 Jensen-Shannon 散度：把兩個高斯的混合分布用一個高斯近似，於是有閉式解，不用抽樣。最後一律對距離矩陣做 DSN：每格減掉所在行與列的平均、除以標準差，以減少「樞紐歌」（hub，跟誰都像）與「孤兒歌」。

調參。 目標函數是類型資料集上的 P@k（k=1…20）：查詢一首歌，取最像的 k 首，算有幾首同類型；有藝人資訊時排除同藝人。調參用 Homburg（1,886 段 10 秒片段、9 類）與 Ballroom（698 段、8 種舞曲）。結論：高斯類距離全面勝過平均向量類，AltJSD 整體最好；P 在 50、100、150 中選 150；逐層掃 1–72 層，Homburg 在第 36 層最好、Ballroom 在第 42 層，作者選 36 以免偏向節奏。最後為了省計算，PCA 改成固定用 GTZAN 算一次，Homburg 幾乎不受影響、Ballroom 掉了不少。

Extracting embeddings. jukemirlib feeds about the central 24 s of each song into Jukebox (at most 8,192 codified frames per pass, 344 frames per second). It has 72 layers, each emitting 4,800 dimensions per frame. Keeping every layer for 500 songs would exceed 5,000 GB, so only one layer is kept, at 16-bit precision, resampled to 100 Hz, giving a 2,378×4,800 matrix per song; each dimension is standardised and PCA projects onto P components.

One model per song. Two options are compared: average all frames into one vector (with cosine, Euclidean or Manhattan distance), or fit a single Gaussian (mean plus empirical covariance, with symmetric KL, FID or AltJSD). AltJSD, from Schnitzer's PhD thesis, approximates the Jensen-Shannon divergence by replacing the two-Gaussian mixture with one Gaussian, which gives a closed form with no sampling. Every distance matrix then gets DSN: each entry has its row and column mean subtracted and is divided by the standard deviation, reducing hubs (songs similar to everything) and orphans.

Tuning. The objective is P@k (k=1…20) on genre datasets: for a query, take the k most similar songs and count how many share its genre, skipping same-artist songs when artist labels exist. Tuning uses Homburg (1,886 ten-second clips, 9 genres) and Ballroom (698 clips, 8 dance styles). Results: Gaussian distances beat all mean-vector distances, AltJSD is best overall; P=150 is picked from 50, 100 and 150; a sweep over layers 1–72 peaks at layer 36 on Homburg and 42 on Ballroom, and the authors keep 36 to avoid a rhythm bias. Finally, to save compute, the PCA is computed once on GTZAN; Homburg barely changes, Ballroom drops noticeably.

限制Limitations

調參集也是比較集。 層數、距離、P 與 PCA 來源都是看 Homburg 和 Ballroom 的 P@k 決定的，而第 5 節的類型檢索比較又把這兩個資料集放進去。作者自己有提到 JukeSim「部分在 Homburg 上調過」卻仍輸 DEEPSIM，這反而讓結果更可信一點，但真正乾淨的只有 1517-artists、Unique 和兩個三元組資料集。

類型檢索只有圖。 四個類型資料集的比較（Fig. 4）全文沒有任何一個數字，只有文字描述：DEEPSIM 在 Ballroom 最差、在 Homburg 最好；1517-artists 上 JukeSim 在小 k 領先、大 k 被 DEEPSIM 追過；Unique 上 JukeSim 最好、k=20 時被追平。讀者無法判斷差距是 1 分還是 10 分。

三元組差距很小、沒有信賴區間。 dim-sim 0.8146 對 0.8050，在 879 個三元組上大約是 8 個三元組的差別；MagnaTagATune 0.9683 對 0.9665，而且 RTBOF 重新實作也是 0.9683。全文沒有任何變異或顯著性檢定，所以「平手」是比「略勝」更準確的讀法，作者結論也是這樣寫的。

評估素材被替換。 dim-sim 原本是 3 秒片段，但原作者無法分享，這篇改用 7digital 的 30 秒預覽、取中間 24 秒。作者用 DEEPSIM 重現值 0.8050 對原始論文 0.8010 來說明標註仍然成立，但這只驗證了一個系統。

成本。 Jukebox 有 80 億參數，作者承認這讓 JukeSim 比 DEEPSIM 難用很多，但沒有給任何推論時間或記憶體數字。DEEPSIM 那邊則只用平均向量加餘弦，因為改用高斯會遇到奇異矩陣，所以兩者的聚合方式並不相同。

The tuning sets are also comparison sets. Layer, distance, P and PCA source were all chosen by P@k on Homburg and Ballroom, and the genre retrieval comparison in Section 5 includes both datasets again. The authors do note that JukeSim was partly tuned on Homburg and still loses there to DEEPSIM, which makes the result a little more credible, but only 1517-artists, Unique and the two triplet sets are truly clean.

Genre retrieval is shown only as a figure. The comparison on four genre datasets (Fig. 4) has no numbers anywhere in the text, only descriptions: DEEPSIM is worst on Ballroom and best on Homburg; on 1517-artists JukeSim leads for small k and DEEPSIM overtakes it for large k; on Unique JukeSim is best until DEEPSIM ties at k=20. A reader cannot tell whether the gaps are 1 point or 10.

Triplet gaps are small and have no confidence intervals. dim-sim is 0.8146 vs 0.8050, roughly 8 triplets out of 879; MagnaTagATune is 0.9683 vs 0.9665, and the RTBOF reimplementation also scores 0.9683. There is no variance or significance test anywhere, so "on par" is a more accurate reading than "slightly better", which is also how the authors' conclusion puts it.

The evaluation audio was substituted. dim-sim originally used 3 s snippets that the original authors could not share, so this paper uses 7digital's 30 s previews and takes the central 24 s. The authors cite DEEPSIM's reproduced 0.8050 against its published 0.8010 as evidence the annotations still hold, but that validates only one system.

Cost. Jukebox has 8 billion parameters, and the authors admit this makes JukeSim much harder to use than DEEPSIM, but give no inference time or memory numbers. DEEPSIM uses only a mean vector with cosine distance, because Gaussians hit singular matrices, so the two systems do not aggregate the same way.

討論Discussion

數字。 三元組評估（Table 1）：dim-sim 上 JukeSim 0.8146、DEEPSIM 0.8050、BLS 重新實作 0.7452、RTBOF 重新實作 0.7278；MagnaTagATune（533 組刪掉 97 組平票後，每組拆成兩個限制）上分別是 0.9683、0.9665、0.9349、0.9683。dim-sim 上兩個學習式方法比最好的手工方法高約 6–9 分，這是全文最有說服力的一個差距；MagnaTagATune 則幾乎飽和，分不出高下。

真正的貢獻是那條「缺失的連結」。 文中最有價值的結論不是 JukeSim 本身，而是手工方法確實被學習式方法超越，所以 2004–2005 年那批論文說的「玻璃天花板」，手工方法其實還沒碰到。作者所在的 JKU 正是 RTBOF 和 BLS 的出處（Widmer 是兩篇的共同作者），由他們用公開的距離矩陣與重新實作來做這個對照，算是有資格也有資料。

對讀者的意義。 這篇證明生成模型嵌入可以直接塞進經典的「每首歌一個高斯」框架，不需要任何相似度訓練就追平一個專門用三元組訓練的模型。但它也顯示：在比較便宜的 DEEPSIM 已經這麼接近的情況下，用 80 億參數模型換 0.01 的三元組準確率很難說划算。比較值得追的後續是作者自己提的那一句：換成更小的生成模型或音樂基礎模型，看同一套高斯加 AltJSD 的流程能不能保住分數。

Numbers. Triplet evaluation (Table 1): on dim-sim JukeSim scores 0.8146, DEEPSIM 0.8050, the BLS reimplementation 0.7452 and the RTBOF reimplementation 0.7278; on MagnaTagATune (533 triplets minus 97 ties, each split into two constraints) they score 0.9683, 0.9665, 0.9349 and 0.9683. On dim-sim the two learned methods beat the best hand-crafted one by about 6–9 points, the most convincing gap in the paper; MagnaTagATune is nearly saturated and separates nothing.

The real contribution is the missing link. The most valuable conclusion is not JukeSim itself but that learned methods do surpass hand-crafted ones, so the glass ceiling claimed in the 2004–2005 papers had not actually been reached by hand-crafted features. The authors' group at JKU is where RTBOF and BLS came from (Widmer co-authored both), so they are well placed, with published distance matrices and reimplementations, to run this comparison.

What it means for readers. The paper shows that generative embeddings can be dropped straight into the classic one-Gaussian-per-song framework and, with no similarity training at all, match a model trained specifically on triplets. It also shows that when the much cheaper DEEPSIM is this close, trading an 8-billion-parameter model for 0.01 triplet accuracy is hard to justify. The follow-up worth watching is the one the authors suggest: swap in a smaller generative model or a music foundation model and see whether the same Gaussian-plus-AltJSD pipeline keeps the score.

Who Plays What: Same-Instrument Music Separation from Video

arXiv:2611.29411 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-11-22

為什麼挑這篇：同樣是週末從本週列表挑的。選它是因為它把一個常被跳過的難題（兩位樂手拿同一種樂器時，音色和外觀都分不開）第一次放到真實合奏錄音上逐位樂手評分，而且控制實驗做得很徹底：點錯人、靜止畫面、影片錯位、只看關鍵點四種測試都指向同一個結論，就是靠動作的時間對齊在分。URMP 上中位 SDR 5.30 dB 對公開分離器最多 0.30 dB，差距大到不太可能是設定問題，作者也在基線自己的 MUSIC 基準上重現了它們發表的分數。因為架構組合新、證據鏈完整，寫成深度分析。Why this one: Also picked from this week's listings under the weekend rule. I chose it because it takes a problem usually skipped, two players of the same instrument whom neither timbre nor appearance can tell apart, and for the first time scores it player by player on real ensemble recordings, with unusually thorough controls: wrong click, still frame, time shift and keypoints-only tests all point to the same conclusion, that movement timing does the separating. On URMP its median SDR is 5.30 dB against at most 0.30 dB for released separators, a gap too large to be a setup error, and the authors also reproduce the baselines' published scores on their own MUSIC benchmark. The architecture combination is new and the chain of evidence complete, so it is written as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

想像一段弦樂四重奏的影片，你只想聽第二小提琴。音源分離器會給你一軌「小提琴」，裡面兩把都有；音訊影像（audio-visual）分離器雖然會看畫面，但大多是把樂器長什麼樣子跟聲音連起來，而兩把小提琴長得一樣。當兩位樂手拿同一種樂器時，音色和外觀都說不出哪些音是誰的。

還剩下一個線索不同：每個人在做什麼、什麼時候做。弓換方向時新音開始，左手手指在換音高時移動，長笛手在樂句前吸氣。這些動作只跟那位樂手自己的音同步。

作者把問題做成一個能直接用的形式：使用者在影片裡點一位樂手，拿回那位樂手單獨的聲音。點擊的角色像可提示分割（promptable segmentation）裡的點提示，指的是「哪一位」，不是「哪種樂器」。用途包括練習與教學（學生想聽自己那一部）、音樂會影片重混與修復，以及給以分軌訓練的音樂分析模型提供逐人分軌。

Picture a video of a string quartet in which you only want to hear the second violin. A music separator returns one "violin" track that holds both violins; an audio-visual separator looks at the video, but most of them link how an instrument looks to how it sounds, and the two violins look the same. When two players share an instrument, neither timbre nor appearance says which notes belong to whom.

One cue still differs: what each player does, and when. A bow changes direction as a note begins, left-hand fingers move when the pitch changes, a flautist breathes before a phrase. These movements are synchronised with that player's notes and nobody else's.

The authors frame the problem in a directly usable form: a user clicks one musician in a video and gets that musician's sound alone. The click works like a point prompt in promptable segmentation, saying which player, not which instrument. Uses include practice and teaching (a student wants to hear their own part), remixing and restoring concert videos, and per-player stems for music analysis models that train on separated tracks.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

純音訊分離。 BS-RoFormer 與 MVSep Mega 把錄音拆成樂器類別，四重奏裡兩把小提琴會留在同一軌。排列不變訓練（PIT）的盲分離可以拆開兩個同音色的聲部，但輸出沒有固定順序，無法回應「我要這一位」。

靠外觀的影音分離。 Sound of Pixels（Zhao 等，ECCV 2018）建立了「混兩支影片的音訊、讓模型靠畫面拆回來」的訓練法，之後的 iQuery（每種樂器一個 query）、DAVIS-Flow（生成式分離）、SAM Audio（接受視覺遮罩當提示的通用分離器）都延續這個配方。它們的訓練混音大多是不同樂器，所以看樂器外觀就夠了。

靠動作的分離。 深度學習之前，Parekh 等把弦樂手的弓速綁到矩陣分解的激活上；Li 等用弓法與顫音把影片裡的樂手對應到已知樂譜的聲部。學習式方法裡，Sound of Motions（Zhao 等，ICCV 2019）加入運動軌跡，Music Gesture（Gan 等，CVPR 2020）加入身體與手指關鍵點，並報告了鋼琴、長笛、小號的同樂器二重奏分離。

這篇最像 Music Gesture，差別在三點。 第一，Music Gesture 和 Sound of Motions 都只在「把獨奏影片混在一起」的合成資料上報 SDR，也沒有公開程式碼；這篇在真實合奏 URMP 上逐人評分。第二，這篇不從頭訓練分離器，而是借用已經會分 53 種樂器的 BS-RoFormer，影片只負責「選人」。第三，條件注入方式——每個時間層後加交叉注意力、輸出投影零初始化——本質上是 Flamingo 的門控交叉注意力或 ControlNet 的零初始化技巧，不是新發明。Slizovskaia 等雖然也在真實 URMP 上測，但把同樂器的兩人當成同一個來源。

Audio-only separation. BS-RoFormer and MVSep Mega split a recording into instrument classes, so the two violins of a quartet stay in one track. Blind separation with permutation-invariant training (PIT) can split two parts of the same timbre, but its outputs come in no fixed order and cannot answer "I want this player".

Audio-visual separation by appearance. Sound of Pixels (Zhao et al., ECCV 2018) set the recipe of mixing the audio of two videos and having the model undo the mix using the frames; iQuery (one query per instrument), DAVIS-Flow (a generative separator) and SAM Audio (a general separator that accepts a visual mask as a prompt) all follow it. Their training mixtures mostly pair different instruments, so the instrument's look is enough.

Separation by movement. Before deep learning, Parekh et al. tied string players' bow velocity to the activations of a matrix factorisation, and Li et al. matched players to parts of a known score through bowing and vibrato. Among learned models, Sound of Motions (Zhao et al., ICCV 2019) added motion trajectories, and Music Gesture (Gan et al., CVPR 2020) added body and finger keypoints and reported same-instrument duets for piano, flute and trumpet.

This paper is closest to Music Gesture, with three differences. First, Music Gesture and Sound of Motions report SDR only on synthetic mixtures of solo videos and released no code; this paper scores each player on the real ensemble recordings of URMP. Second, it does not train a separator from scratch but borrows a BS-RoFormer that already separates 53 instrument types, so the video only has to choose the player. Third, the conditioning, cross-attention after every time layer with a zero-initialised output projection, is essentially Flamingo's gated cross-attention or ControlNet's zero-init trick rather than a new mechanism. Slizovskaia et al. also tested on real URMP, but treated the two same-instrument players as one source.

方法Method

樂手表徵。 凍結的 SAM 2.1（Hiera-L）把點擊變成遮罩並在整段影片追蹤，每格取遮罩周圍的正方形裁切，再用兩個凍結模型描述這位樂手。V-JEPA 2 ViT-L 每兩格輸出 4×4 個 1,024 維 token，也就是每秒 12.5 個時間步、每步 16 個空間 token，保留樂手、樂器與可見的演奏動作。RTMW 每格偵測 48 個身體與手部關鍵點，作者算的是用框大小正規化的逐格速度，再接上 48 個信心值，得到每格 144 維、25 Hz 的動作向量。用速度而不用絕對位置，是為了強調「動」、去掉對樂手站位的依賴。

時間對齊。 影像 token、關鍵點 token 和分離器的音訊 token 取樣率都不同，所以全部打上 10 ms 網格上的時間戳，用旋轉位置編碼（RoPE）編進去，讓交叉注意力能把某個動作跟對應時間的聲音連起來。

分離器。 骨幹從 MVSep Mega 初始化，它是預訓練來分 53 種樂器分軌的 BS-RoFormer：STFT 視窗 2,048、跳距 512，頻譜切成頻帶，經過 12 個寬 256 的區塊，每塊先做時間方向 Transformer、再做頻帶方向 Transformer，共 26.3M 參數。作者在每個時間層之後插一層交叉注意力（音訊特徵當 query、樂手 token 當 key/value），輸出投影初始化為零，所以剛開始時行為跟原本的 Mega 一模一樣。53 個樂器輸出頭換成單一個預測複數遮罩的頭，整個可訓練部分 43.9M 參數。

Player representation. A frozen SAM 2.1 (Hiera-L) turns the click into a mask and tracks it through the video; each frame gets a square crop around the mask, described by two frozen models. V-JEPA 2 ViT-L outputs a 4×4 grid of 1,024-dimensional tokens every two frames, i.e. 12.5 time steps per second with 16 spatial tokens each, retaining player, instrument and visible playing. RTMW detects 48 body and hand keypoints per frame; the authors take box-normalised frame-to-frame velocities plus the 48 confidences, a 144-dimensional motion vector at 25 Hz. Velocities rather than absolute positions emphasise movement and remove dependence on where the player sits.

Temporal alignment. Video tokens, keypoint tokens and the separator's audio tokens all have different rates, so each gets a timestamp on a shared 10 ms grid, encoded with rotary position embeddings (RoPE), letting cross-attention relate a movement to the sound at the same moment.

Separator. The backbone is initialised from MVSep Mega, a BS-RoFormer pretrained to separate 53 instrument stems: STFT window 2,048, hop 512, the spectrum split into bands, then 12 blocks of width 256, each a time-direction Transformer followed by a band-direction one, 26.3M parameters. A cross-attention layer is inserted after each time layer (audio features as queries, player tokens as keys and values), with the output projection initialised to zero so the model starts out behaving exactly like Mega. The 53 instrument heads are replaced by one head predicting a complex mask; 43.9M parameters are trained in total.

限制Limitations

URMP 不是真的現場合奏。 URMP 的每個聲部是一位樂手一位樂手分開錄、事後才組合成一段錄音與一段影片。這讓每人都有乾淨分軌可以評分，但也表示樂手之間沒有真實的互相聆聽、沒有收音串音（bleed），動作和聲音的同步可能比真正同場演奏更乾淨。作者在結論裡列為限制，也把現場合奏留作未來工作。

有些設計看過測試集。 作者自己寫「部分設計選擇參考了 URMP」，例如裁切大小的影響就是在 URMP 上發現的（訓練裁切約 360 px、URMP 約 480 px，縮到 240 px 時無鏡像模型從 3.29 掉到 0.44 dB）。每個設定都只訓練一次，沒有多個 seed。

中位數蓋住了很大的分散。 58 位樂手的 95% 區間從 1.86 到 10.31 dB；有 12 位低於只把音量調小的 0.82 dB 基準。三人曲目只有 1.80 dB、五人曲目 1.14 dB、小號 1.14 dB，都只比調小音量好一點；小號跟法國號、長號、低音號一起時會失敗，兩組五重奏裡的小提琴也失敗，作者坦白說找不到原因。

基線條件不完全公平。 四個基線都用作者發布的權重，沒有在這篇的訓練資料上重訓；iQuery 和 DAVIS-Flow 輸出 11 kHz，在 48 kHz 評分時 5.5 kHz 以上全部遺失。最接近的兩個動作式方法（Sound of Motions、Music Gesture）沒有公開程式碼，無法比較。MVSep Mega 的訓練資料沒有公開，跟 URMP 重疊的可能性無法排除。

其他。 只有 26 種訓練樂器；文字提示（SAM 3）只對 33/58 位選對人，14 位（多為長笛）找不到任何人；靜止畫面測試裡模型從沒在訓練看過靜止的樂手，所以 -0.97 dB 有一部分可能只是分布外輸入的效果，作者自己也指出這點。

URMP is not a real live ensemble. Each URMP part was recorded one player at a time and then assembled into one recording and one video. That gives every player a clean stem to score against, but it also means no real mutual listening and no microphone bleed, and movement and sound may be more cleanly synchronised than in a performance in one room. The authors list this as a limitation and leave live ensembles to future work.

Some design choices saw the test set. The authors write that some design choices were informed by URMP; the crop-size effect, for instance, was found on URMP (training crops are about 360 px, URMP about 480 px, and at 240 px the no-mirror model falls from 3.29 to 0.44 dB). Each setting was trained once, with no extra seeds.

The median hides wide spread. The 95% interval over the 58 players runs from 1.86 to 10.31 dB, and 12 players fall below the 0.82 dB scaled-recording reference. Three-player pieces reach only 1.80 dB, five-player pieces 1.14 dB and trumpets 1.14 dB, each barely above turning the volume down; trumpets fail next to horn, trombone or tuba, and two violin pairs in quintets fail for reasons the authors say they could not find.

The baselines are not on fully equal terms. All four baselines run from their released weights, not retrained on this paper's data; iQuery and DAVIS-Flow output 11 kHz audio and lose everything above 5.5 kHz under 48 kHz scoring. The two closest movement-based methods (Sound of Motions, Music Gesture) have no public code and could not be compared. MVSep Mega's training data is undisclosed, so overlap with URMP cannot be ruled out.

Other points. Training covers only 26 instruments; a text prompt (SAM 3) picks the right player for only 33 of 58, and finds nobody for 14, mostly flutes. In the still-frame test the model never saw a motionless player in training, so part of the -0.97 dB may be an out-of-distribution effect, as the authors themselves note.

討論Discussion

主結果（Table 1）。 URMP 58 位同樂器樂手，中位 SDR：這篇 5.30 dB；MVSep Mega 0.30、DAVIS-Flow 0.01、iQuery -0.45、SAM Audio -3.92；原始錄音 -6.29、調小音量 x/N 0.82。純二重奏 8 位：18.73 dB 對基線最多 1.75 dB。作者自建的 500 組留出合成混音：9.56 dB 對基線最多 0.46 dB。同資料但不給影片的模型只有 0.52 dB（二重奏 2.95），所以增益來自影片而不是訓練資料。

殘留分析（Table 2）。 把輸出擬合成三個真實訊號的加權和：所有基線都讓同樂器的「搭檔」跟被點的人差不多大聲（-0.76 到 1.07 dB）；Mega 能把其他樂器壓低 18.09 dB，卻完全壓不下搭檔（0.00 dB）——它分的是樂器，不是人。這篇把搭檔壓低 23.65 dB（二重奏 39.01）、其他樂器 21.23 dB。

聽測與基線自家基準。 MUSHRA 聽測 16 人、10 個片段、響度一律正規化到 -23 LUFS：這篇 69.0（二重奏 88.0），最好的基線 SAM Audio 30.5、Mega 30.2，錨點（原始錄音）26.0；16 位聽者每一位給這篇的平均分都高於每個基線（Holm 校正後 p=1.2×10⁻⁴）。在 iQuery 與 DAVIS-Flow 自己的 MUSIC 基準上，兩者重現了發表分數（11.61 對 11.17、12.21 對 12.01），這篇 19.08 dB，比較好的那個高 6.9 dB。

動作真的是關鍵嗎（5.3–5.4 節）。 這是整篇最有價值的部分，四個測試由弱到強。點錯人：正確點擊在 58 位中有 51 位分數較高（二重奏 8/8），點錯時中位從 5.30 掉到 -2.19 dB；三個影音基線只贏 23–29 位，等於擲硬幣。靜止畫面（保留外觀、拿掉動作）：掉到 -0.97 dB，正確點擊只贏 26 位。影片錯位（外觀與動作都保留，只破壞時間對齊）：錯 0.08 s 就從 5.30 掉到 1.38 dB，0.16 s 時三個模型都低於無影片模型，錯到 4 秒也救不回來。只看關鍵點（一個像素都沒看到）：全體 4.15 dB、二重奏 12.18 dB，正確點擊同樣贏 51 位。

細節值得記下。 二重奏在任何錯位下仍保有 4.73–10.54 dB，所以在只有兩人時，模型也用了一部分跟時間無關的線索（例如這個人是誰）。動得多不代表分得好：增益跟平均關鍵點速度幾乎不相關（手臂 Spearman 0.07、手 0.03），重點是時間對不對而不是動作量。消融裡，預訓練骨幹最關鍵：不用 Mega 的權重，二重奏從 6.75 掉到 0.51 dB、留出混音從 9.54 掉到 2.08 dB，但仍然會跟著點擊（48/58）——預訓練負責「會分」，影片負責「選誰」，這個分工講得很清楚。

給讀者的判斷。 這篇最該帶走的不是 5.30 dB，而是它示範了怎麼證明一個條件式模型真的用了你以為它用的訊號：點錯人、靜止、錯位、只留關鍵點，每一個都是便宜又有說服力的控制，值得在任何影音 MIR 論文裡照做。反過來說，它的弱點也很具體：三人以上、銅管混編、以及 12/58 位比調小音量還差的那些樂手，代表離「點任何一個人都能分出來」還有一段距離。

Main results (Table 1). Median SDR over 58 same-instrument URMP players: this model 5.30 dB; MVSep Mega 0.30, DAVIS-Flow 0.01, iQuery -0.45, SAM Audio -3.92; the unchanged recording -6.29 and the scaled recording x/N 0.82. On the 8 pure-duet players: 18.73 dB against at most 1.75 dB for the baselines. On the authors' 500 held-out synthetic mixtures: 9.56 dB against at most 0.46 dB. The same model trained on the same data without video reaches only 0.52 dB (2.95 on duets), so the gain comes from the video, not the training data.

What is left in (Table 2). Fitting each output as a weighted sum of three true signals: every baseline keeps the same-instrument partner about as loud as the clicked player (-0.76 to 1.07 dB); Mega pushes other instruments 18.09 dB down but not the partner at all (0.00 dB), so it separates instruments, not players. This model keeps the partner 23.65 dB down (39.01 on duets) and other instruments 21.23 dB down.

Listening test and the baselines' own benchmark. A MUSHRA test with 16 listeners, 10 excerpts, all normalised to -23 LUFS: this model scores 69.0 (88.0 on duets), the best baselines SAM Audio 30.5 and Mega 30.2, the anchor (unchanged recording) 26.0; every one of the 16 listeners rates this model above every baseline on average (p=1.2×10⁻⁴, Holm-corrected). On the MUSIC benchmark of iQuery and DAVIS-Flow, both reproduce their published scores (11.61 vs 11.17, 12.21 vs 12.01), and this model reaches 19.08 dB, 6.9 dB above the better one.

Is it really the movement (Sections 5.3–5.4)? This is the most valuable part of the paper, four tests from weakest to strongest. Wrong click: the correct click scores higher for 51 of 58 players (8 of 8 on duets), and a wrong click drops the median from 5.30 to -2.19 dB; the three audio-visual baselines win for only 23–29 players, a coin flip. Still frame (appearance kept, movement removed): -0.97 dB, with the correct click winning for only 26. Time shift (appearance and movement kept, only their timing broken): a 0.08 s shift takes 5.30 down to 1.38 dB, at 0.16 s all three models fall below the no-video model, and shifts up to 4 s never bring the gain back. Keypoints only (not a single pixel): 4.15 dB overall and 12.18 dB on duets, with the correct click again winning for 51.

Details worth keeping. Duets keep 4.73–10.54 dB under every shift, so with only two players the model also uses some timing-independent cue, such as who the player is. Moving more does not mean separating better: the gain barely correlates with mean keypoint speed (Spearman 0.07 for arms, 0.03 for hands); timing matters, not amount. Among ablations the pretrained backbone matters most: without Mega's weights, duets fall from 6.75 to 0.51 dB and held-out mixtures from 9.54 to 2.08 dB, yet the model still follows the click (48 of 58). Pretraining supplies the ability to separate, the video supplies the choice of player, and the paper makes that division clear.

A reader's verdict. The thing to take away is not 5.30 dB but a demonstration of how to prove a conditioned model uses the signal you think it uses: wrong click, still frame, time shift and keypoints only are each cheap and convincing controls that any audio-visual MIR paper could copy. Its weaknesses are equally concrete: three or more players, mixed brass, and the 12 of 58 players who end up worse than simply turning the volume down show it is still some way from separating whoever you click.

AIRPiano: Piano Score Following with Tabletop Hand Gestures

AIRPiano：在桌面上做彈琴的手指動作就能照著已知樂譜發出聲音——樂譜提供音高，手的動作決定何時彈哪個和弦；WiLoR 手部 3D 姿態（腕相對座標加腕位移）對上樂譜和弦 token，用監督式注意力的 Transformer 預測 200 ms 之後該觸發的事件，再接 Dannenberg 式 LCS 動態規劃追蹤；只用鋼琴影片訓練，桌面 C-AR@8 67.77%（配對子集 73.08%，鋼琴 80.71%），摘要的 74% 是事後整體平移 5 格後的數字；沒有任何基線、4 位業餘參與者、沒有使用者研究

動機Motivation

沒有鋼琴的時候，鋼琴家常常還是會練：在桌上、大腿上、甚至空中動手指，邊看新譜或邊回想熟悉的曲子。如果有系統能把這些動作變成聲音，就能幫練習者聽出自己大致的手指動作跟樂譜對不對得上；也能在沒有鋼琴、也不必準確找到琴鍵位置的情況下做有表情的演奏，對大跳之類的技巧特別省力。

既有的虛擬鋼琴多半是把指尖點擊對應到虛擬或紙上的鍵盤，但那需要畫好的鍵盤、需要整張鍵盤的桌面空間，而且沒有觸覺邊界，很難點準。AIRPiano 換一個分工：音高由已知樂譜提供，手的動作只負責決定「何時」彈「哪一個」樂譜事件。

困難在於桌面上的動作不必保留鍵盤上的絕對位置——同一塊桌面可以在不同段落代表不同音域，所以手的絕對位置不可靠；手部動作跟符號樂譜的表示方式與時間結構差很多；配對好的「動作—樂譜」資料幾乎不存在，而現有鋼琴演奏影片裡的動作又跟桌面上的動作不一樣。

Without a piano, pianists often still practise: they move their fingers on a table, on their thighs or even in the air, reading a new score or recalling a familiar piece. A system that turned those movements into sound could help them hear whether their approximate finger movements line up with the score, and could allow expressive playing with no piano and no need to hit exact key positions, which helps especially with large leaps.

Existing virtual pianos mostly map fingertip taps to keys on a virtual or paper keyboard, but that needs a drawn keyboard and a full keyboard's worth of table space, and without tactile boundaries the keys are hard to hit. AIRPiano divides the work differently: the known score supplies the pitches, and hand motion only decides when to play which score event.

The difficulty is that tabletop motion need not preserve absolute keyboard positions; the same patch of table can stand for different registers in different passages, so absolute hand position is unreliable. Hand motion and symbolic scores also differ greatly in representation and temporal structure, paired motion-score data barely exist, and motion in existing piano videos differs from motion on a table.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

樂譜引導的演奏介面。 Coloring-in Piano 每按一個鍵就觸發儲存序列裡的下一個音；iFP 結合演奏者的拍點時間與力度跟一個演奏模板；Con Espressione!（Widmer 團隊）讓手部動作控制整體速度與響度，再由模型預測每個音的局部表情。虛擬指揮系統（Personal Orchestra、You're the Conductor、Home Conducting）則是用指揮棒或身體動作控制錄音或樂譜的播放速度。

視覺樂譜跟隨。 IMuSE 的鋼琴手勢追蹤把即時手部動作對上事先錄好、已對齊樂譜的排練軌跡，用來同步伴奏；Pet & Maezawa 把音訊跟鍵盤上的手與指尖位置結合，提升噪音下的追蹤，也測過只用手部關鍵點。兩者都需要每首曲子一條排練參考，而且都是在真的鍵盤上彈。

這篇最像誰、差在哪。 概念上最接近的是 Coloring-in Piano：音高全由樂譜決定、使用者只給時間。差別在 AIRPiano 不是「每個點擊觸發下一個音」，而是讓模型從手部動作與樂譜的音高與節奏模式去判斷現在該是哪一個事件，因此理論上可以容忍漏點或多點。追蹤那一層則幾乎原封不動地用了 Dannenberg（1984）的增量 LCS 比對與滑動視窗策略。可惜的是，文中沒有把 Coloring-in 式的「下一個事件」規則當成基線來比，所以讀者不知道學習式對齊到底比這個最簡單的做法好多少。

Score-guided performance interfaces. Coloring-in Piano triggers the next pitch of a stored sequence with each keypress; iFP combines the performer's beat timing and dynamics with a performance template; Con Espressione! (Widmer's group) lets hand movements control overall tempo and loudness while a model predicts each note's local expression. Virtual conducting systems (Personal Orchestra, You're the Conductor, Home Conducting) use a baton or body movement to control the playback speed of a recording or score.

Visual score following. IMuSE's piano gesture tracking matches live hand movements to prerecorded, score-aligned rehearsal trajectories to synchronise accompaniment; Pet & Maezawa combine audio with hand and fingertip positions along the keyboard to track better in noise, and also tested hand landmarks alone. Both need a rehearsal reference per piece, and both run on a real keyboard.

What it most resembles, and the difference. Conceptually closest is Coloring-in Piano: pitches come entirely from the score and the user supplies only timing. The difference is that AIRPiano does not fire the next note on each tap; the model judges from hand motion and the score's pitch and rhythm patterns which event is due, so in principle it can tolerate missed or extra taps. The tracking layer reuses Dannenberg's (1984) incremental LCS matching and sliding-window strategy almost unchanged. Unfortunately, the paper does not compare against a Coloring-in-style next-event rule, so readers cannot tell how much learned alignment gains over that simplest approach.

方法Method

動作表示。 用 WiLoR 估計雙手各 21 個關節的 3D 姿態（公制、單位分米），偵測不到的手全部設為零並給一個出現旗標。20 個非手腕關節改成相對手腕的座標，讓同樣的手指動作在桌上不同位置看起來一樣；手腕本身則改用逐格位移，保留大跳之類的整手平移。雙手攤平加旗標，每格 128 維。

樂譜表示。 同一量化起音位置的音合成一個和弦事件，用 128 維多熱向量表示音高；節奏用到下一個事件的間隔（IOI），以每拍 24 tick 量化、夾在 1–96 tick，編成 96 維 one-hot。

模型與監督。 Transformer 編碼器—解碼器：樂譜 token 走四層雙向編碼器，動作 token 走四層因果解碼器並對樂譜做交叉注意力。最後一個「監督式注意力」頭對每個動作格與每個候選事件算縮放點積，再逐元素取 sigmoid（不用 softmax，所以同一格可以有零、一或多個事件）。目標是三角形的軟標籤：峰值放在真實起音前 ε=0.1 秒、半徑 ρ=0.2 秒，所以分數在起音前 ε+ρ/2=0.2 秒越過 0.5，換來 200 ms 的預期時間。

串流推論。 每來一格就在目前的樂譜視窗與動作歷史上跑模型，分數超過 0.5 就觸發該事件、在 200 ms 後排程 MIDI 播放（固定力度與長度），降回 0.5 以下才能再觸發。接著用 Dannenberg 式 LCS 動態規劃決定要不要把這次觸發當作時間錨點：配對 +1、跳過 -1、多餘的觸發不扣分，只有創新高才接受。接受的錨點會把視窗重新置中，並用錨點內插或外插估計動作歷史的起點。

訓練資料。 沒有桌面資料，就用鋼琴影片：PianoMotion10M（116 小時，Bilibili，多為流行曲）加 PianoYT（20 多小時，古典），音訊用 TransKun 2.0 轉成 MIDI、用 Beat This! 估拍點，再量化成近似的樂譜。每次取 50 個和弦的視窗、步長 12，並對動作段的起訖隨機抖動最多 0.5 秒、把整段 IOI 乘上 0.5、1 或 2。單張 RTX 4090 訓練約 8 小時。

Motion representation. WiLoR estimates 3D poses of 21 joints per hand (metric, in decimetres); an undetected hand is zeroed and flagged. The 20 non-wrist joints become wrist-relative positions, so the same finger movement looks the same anywhere on the table; the wrist itself becomes a frame-to-frame displacement, keeping whole-hand translation such as leaps. Both hands flattened plus flags give 128 dimensions per frame.

Score representation. Notes with the same quantised onset form a chord event, with pitch as a 128-dimensional multi-hot vector; rhythm is the inter-onset interval (IOI) to the next event, quantised at 24 ticks per beat, clamped to 1–96 ticks and one-hot encoded in 96 dimensions.

Model and supervision. A Transformer encoder-decoder: score tokens pass four bidirectional encoder layers, motion tokens pass four causal decoder layers with cross-attention to the score. A final supervised-attention head computes a scaled dot product for every motion frame and candidate event, with an elementwise sigmoid instead of softmax so a frame can trigger zero, one or several events. Targets are triangular soft labels peaking ε=0.1 s before the true onset with radius ρ=0.2 s, so the score crosses 0.5 at ε+ρ/2=0.2 s before onset, buying 200 ms of anticipation.

Streaming inference. For each new frame the model runs on the current score window and motion history; an event whose score exceeds 0.5 is triggered and its MIDI scheduled 200 ms later (fixed velocity and duration), and it can retrigger only after falling below 0.5. Dannenberg-style LCS dynamic programming then decides whether the trigger becomes a timing anchor: a match scores +1, a skip -1, an extra trigger costs nothing, and only a new best is accepted. An accepted anchor recentres the window, and anchors are interpolated or extrapolated to estimate where the motion history starts.

Training data. With no tabletop data, the model trains on piano videos: PianoMotion10M (116 h from Bilibili, mostly pop) plus PianoYT (20+ h, classical), with audio transcribed to MIDI by TransKun 2.0 and beats estimated by Beat This!, then quantised into approximate scores. Windows hold 50 chords with a stride of 12; motion segment boundaries are jittered by up to 0.5 s and all IOIs scaled by 0.5, 1 or 2. Training takes about 8 hours on one RTX 4090.

限制Limitations

沒有任何基線。 作者說很難建立直接可比的基線，所以只報絕對分數。但至少有兩個便宜的基線可以做：Coloring-in 式「每偵測到一次點擊就觸發下一個事件」，以及固定速度照譜播放。沒有它們，67.77% 是好是壞無從判斷。

評估規模很小、而且任務被簡化。 VSF-Piano 只有 2 位鋼琴家、VSF-Tabletop 4 位參與者，全是業餘，共 10 首曲子、每段 30–150 秒。為了取得真值，參與者是「跟著」事先錄好的鋼琴演奏播放去敲桌子，所以評的是動作能不能對上播放的節奏，不是使用者自己決定速度與表情時能不能跟上——後者才是系統聲稱要支援的用途。

摘要的頭條數字經過事後校正。 摘要說的 74%（133 ms 容忍度、配對子集）是把參考起音整體往後平移 5 格（約 167 ms）之後的 C-AR@4；這個平移是在測試集上搜尋出來、讓 C-AR@4 最大的值。未平移時，同一指標在配對子集只有 23.20%、全桌面 26.71%。作者在正文裡有講清楚，主分析也改用未平移的 C-AR@8，但摘要只給了校正後的數字。這 5 格的系統性偏差原因不明，作者推測可能是錄影裝置的影音延遲不同。

還不是可用的即時系統。 WiLoR 每格平均 60.45 ms（P95 62.13），要達到 30 fps 得把奇偶格分給兩張 GPU；端到端延遲沒有量，使用者研究也還沒做。訓練用的樂譜是自動轉譜加拍點量化出來的，消融顯示模型可能學到這種假樂譜特有的節奏模式。另外 PianoMotion10M 的測試分割也拿去訓練了，雖然作者另外用 DTW 排除了跟評估曲目重疊的錄音。

No baselines at all. The authors say a directly comparable baseline is hard to establish, so only absolute scores are reported. Yet at least two cheap baselines were available: a Coloring-in-style rule that fires the next event on each detected tap, and fixed-tempo playback of the score. Without them, there is no way to judge whether 67.77% is good or bad.

Small evaluation, and a simplified task. VSF-Piano has 2 pianists and VSF-Tabletop 4 participants, all amateurs, over 10 pieces of 30–150 s each. To obtain ground truth, participants tapped along with playback of a prerecorded piano performance, so the evaluation measures whether motion follows the playback's timing, not whether the system keeps up when users set their own tempo and expression, which is the use the system claims to support.

The abstract's headline number is post-hoc corrected. The 74% in the abstract (133 ms tolerance, paired subset) is C-AR@4 after shifting all reference onsets 5 frames (about 167 ms) later, a shift found by searching the test set for the one that maximises C-AR@4. Unshifted, the same metric is 23.20% on the paired subset and 26.71% on the full tabletop set. The body is upfront about this and switches its main analysis to unshifted C-AR@8, but the abstract gives only the corrected number. The cause of the 5-frame systematic offset is unknown; the authors suspect differing audio-video delays across recording devices.

Not yet a usable real-time system. WiLoR takes 60.45 ms per frame on average (P95 62.13), so 30 fps needs odd and even frames split across two GPUs; end-to-end latency is not measured and the user study is not done. Training scores come from automatic transcription plus beat quantisation, and the ablations suggest the model may learn rhythm patterns peculiar to those pseudo-scores. The PianoMotion10M test split was also used for training, although the authors excluded recordings overlapping the evaluation pieces using DTW.

討論Discussion

主結果（Table 1，C-AR@8，容忍 ±8 格約 267 ms）。 同來源的 PianoMotion10M 驗證片段：30、60、120 秒分別 96.06、96.09、96.83%，長度變長幾乎不掉。自錄的鋼琴 VSF-Piano 80.71%，表示自錄資料跟訓練資料之間有領域落差。桌面 VSF-Tabletop 67.77%；只取同兩位鋼琴家的配對子集 73.08%，相對鋼琴只少 9.45%，作者據此說從鋼琴到桌面的零樣本轉移是成立的。嚴格容忍度下差很多：C-AR@2 在 PM 上約 64%，在 VSF 上只有 6–9%。

消融（Table 2，PM60 / 鋼琴 / 桌面）。 完整模型 96.09 / 80.71 / 67.77。改用絕對座標：鋼琴反而升到 82.83，桌面掉到 58.42，這是「腕相對座標幫助轉移到桌面」最直接的證據。拿掉腕位移 95.14 / 70.20 / 61.49。拿掉動作段起訖抖動 96.63 / 76.44 / 60.03。拿掉 IOI 特徵 92.79 / 80.29 / 66.17，但拿掉 IOI 縮放增強卻掉到 94.90 / 61.48 / 48.83——節奏特徵本身在 VSF 上幾乎沒用，反而是「不要太相信它的絕對尺度」最重要。

逐曲分析。 曲目間的差異比參與者間或鋼琴/桌面之間更大：Petzold 的 G 大調小步舞曲、蕭邦的華麗大圓舞曲表現較好；德布西〈月光〉的慢段落手部動作太小、一開始就抓不到起音；莫札特第十六號鋼琴奏鳴曲第一樂章的快速音階每個音分到的影格太少，追蹤會跑掉。

怎麼看這篇。 它把一個好玩又實用的互動問題形式化得很乾淨，「腕相對座標 + IOI 縮放」兩個設計各自有消融撐著，200 ms 的預期時間也用三角形標籤的幾何關係算得很清楚。但在沒有任何基線、沒有使用者研究、而且評估情境是「跟著播放敲」的情況下，現在能下的結論只有「模型能從桌面動作大致追上一段已知節奏的演奏」，還不能說它已經是能讓人自由練琴的工具。

Main results (Table 1, C-AR@8, tolerance ±8 frames, about 267 ms). On same-source PianoMotion10M validation clips of 30, 60 and 120 s: 96.06, 96.09 and 96.83%, barely changing with length. On the self-recorded piano set VSF-Piano: 80.71%, indicating a domain gap between the new recordings and the training data. On VSF-Tabletop: 67.77%; on the paired subset from the same two pianists: 73.08%, only 9.45% lower in relative terms than piano, which the authors take as support for zero-shot transfer from piano to tabletop. At strict tolerances the gap is large: C-AR@2 is about 64% on PM but only 6–9% on VSF.

Ablations (Table 2, PM60 / piano / tabletop). Full model 96.09 / 80.71 / 67.77. Absolute coordinates: piano rises to 82.83 while tabletop falls to 58.42, the most direct evidence that wrist-relative coordinates help transfer to the table. No wrist displacement: 95.14 / 70.20 / 61.49. No boundary jitter: 96.63 / 76.44 / 60.03. No IOI feature: 92.79 / 80.29 / 66.17, but no IOI scaling augmentation: 94.90 / 61.48 / 48.83. The rhythm feature itself barely matters on VSF; what matters most is not trusting its absolute scale.

Per-piece analysis. Pieces vary more than participants or piano versus table: Petzold's Minuet in G and Chopin's Grande valse brillante do well; Debussy's Clair de lune has hand movements too small in its slow passages and misses onsets from the start; the fast scales in the first movement of Mozart's Piano Sonata No. 16 give each note too few frames and tracking drifts.

How to read it. The paper formalises a fun and practical interaction problem cleanly; the two key design choices, wrist-relative coordinates and IOI scaling, each have an ablation behind them, and the 200 ms anticipation follows neatly from the geometry of the triangular labels. But with no baselines, no user study and an evaluation in which participants tap along with playback, the conclusion that can be drawn today is only that the model can roughly follow a performance of known timing from tabletop motion, not yet that it is a tool for practising freely.
