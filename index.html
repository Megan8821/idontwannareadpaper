BARS: Beat-Adaptive Rap Synthesis via Flow Matching

BARS：以伴奏音軌、歌詞與 3 秒參考人聲為條件的非自回歸 Flow Matching 饒舌人聲生成，用 ByT5 加輕量 Transformer 適配層編碼歌詞、在交叉注意力中只對歌詞端按長度比縮放 RoPE（A-RoPE）以抑制跳字與重複，並以字元／秒錯配實驗檢驗速度穩健性

Taemin Cho, Hector Martel, Enyan Koh
arXiv:2611.12061 · cs.SD; cs.LG; cs.MM; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-09
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：饒舌的難處在於音節得卡進伴奏的節拍裡，歌詞又一個字都不能漏；一般歌聲合成要給音高與時值，對饒舌不適用。這篇用非自回歸的 Flow Matching 直接從伴奏、歌詞與 3 秒參考人聲生成饒舌人聲，關鍵技巧是只在歌詞端縮放 RoPE 的位置，把歌詞「鋪」在整段伴奏的時間軸上。在原配對的伴奏與歌詞上，Whisper 轉寫的詞錯誤率是 5.1%，與真人錄音相同；把歌詞與伴奏隨機錯配時，在平均語速區間的詞錯誤率從 22.0% 降到 12.3%。選它是因為那個位置編碼技巧很小、很容易借用到任何「文字對音訊」的交叉注意力上。但要注意：沒有任何外部基準線，也沒有客觀的「是否踩在拍上」指標，節奏好壞只靠 40 位聽者的主觀評分。Why this one: Rap is hard because syllables must fit the beat's meter while not a single word of the lyrics may be lost; singing voice synthesis, which needs pitch and duration inputs, does not fit rap. This paper uses non-autoregressive Flow Matching to generate rap vocals directly from a backing track, lyrics and a 3-second reference voice; the key trick is scaling RoPE positions on the lyric side only, laying the lyrics out along the whole track's timeline. On original lyric–beat pairs, Whisper-transcribed WER is 5.1%, the same as human recordings; with lyrics and beats randomly mismatched, WER in the mean speech-rate bin falls from 22.0% to 12.3%. I picked it because the positional-encoding trick is small and easy to borrow for any text-to-audio cross-attention. But note: there is no external baseline and no objective metric of whether syllables land on the beat; rhythmic quality rests only on ratings from 40 listeners.

動機Motivation

饒舌同時受兩個限制：音節得落在特定的節拍位置，歌詞的語意又必須完整。一般的歌聲合成（SVS）需要預先給定音高與時值，但饒舌幾乎不靠音高，所謂的「flow」來自重音、切分與刻意的時間偏移，沒辦法寫成固定的音高與時值模板。

目前唯一直接從歌詞與伴奏生成饒舌人聲的系統是 Freestyler。它是自回歸的，解碼慢；更關鍵的是，它的語速與輸出長度完全被伴奏決定：伴奏太短會提早結束、太長會拖長，使用者沒辦法獨立控制語速。

作者把 BARS 定位成給饒舌創作者打樣的工具：同一段歌詞可以試不同的節奏詮釋、局部重唱某幾句而保留其他段落的風格，並用一小段參考人聲指定音色。

Rap is under two constraints at once: syllables must land in specific metrical positions, and the lyrics' meaning must stay intact. Singing voice synthesis (SVS) requires predefined pitch and duration, but rap barely relies on pitch; its 'flow' comes from stress, syncopation and deliberate timing deviations that cannot be written as fixed pitch and duration templates.

The only existing system that generates rap vocals directly from lyrics and accompaniment is Freestyler. It is autoregressive and slow to decode; more importantly, its pace and output length are fully determined by the backing track, ending early when the beat is too short and dragging when it is too long, with no independent control of speech rate.

The authors position BARS as a prototyping tool for rap artists: try different rhythmic interpretations of the same lyrics, re-generate a few lines while keeping the style of the rest, and set timbre with a short reference voice.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

架構上，BARS 幾乎是 DiTTo-TTS 搬到饒舌上：同樣用交叉注意力把文字接到音訊潛空間、不做時長預測，同樣用 70–100% 長度的片段遮罩訓練做補全。DiTTo-TTS 曾報告純文字預訓練的 ByT5 明顯不如語音預訓練的 SpeechT5；BARS 的回應是在 ByT5 後面加一個與主模型一起訓練的輕量 Transformer 適配層。另一條路是 E2 TTS：把文字補上填充符號到與音訊等長、直接串接，不用交叉注意力。

真正的新東西是 A-RoPE，而它最接近的前作是論文自己也引用的 LARoPE：查詢與鍵的位置各除以自己的序列長度。仔細算一下，A-RoPE 的相位差是 θ(i − j·N/L) = θ·N·(i/N − j/L)，也就是 LARoPE 的正規化位置再乘上音訊長度 N。兩者偏好的是同一條對角線，差別只在容許寬度：LARoPE 的寬度是片段長度的固定比例，A-RoPE 的寬度以音訊幀為單位、與片段長短無關。對饒舌來說後者其實比較合理，一個音節可以偏離的時間不該隨歌曲長度改變。

更早的同類想法是 DCTTS（Tachibana et al., 2018）的引導注意力損失：直接懲罰偏離對角線的注意力權重。A-RoPE 把同樣的對角先驗放進位置編碼，而非損失函數。歌曲生成方面，DiffRhythm 等非自回歸歌曲模型則是給每句歌詞起始時間，把對齊問題交給使用者。

Architecturally, BARS is almost DiTTo-TTS transplanted to rap: text joins the audio latent via cross-attention, there is no duration predictor, and span masks covering 70–100% of the sequence train infilling. DiTTo-TTS reported that text-only pretrained ByT5 clearly underperforms speech-pretrained SpeechT5; BARS's answer is a lightweight Transformer adapter after ByT5, trained jointly with the main model. The other route is E2 TTS: pad text with filler tokens to the audio length and concatenate, with no cross-attention.

The genuinely new piece is A-RoPE, and its closest predecessor is LARoPE, which the paper itself cites: query and key positions are each divided by their sequence length. Working it out, A-RoPE's phase difference is θ(i − j·N/L) = θ·N·(i/N − j/L), that is, LARoPE's normalized positions multiplied by the audio length N. Both prefer the same diagonal; they differ only in tolerance width: LARoPE's is a fixed fraction of the clip, A-RoPE's is measured in audio frames and independent of clip length. For rap the latter is arguably more sensible, since how far a syllable may drift should not depend on song length.

An older relative is the guided attention loss in DCTTS (Tachibana et al., 2018), which directly penalizes attention weights off the diagonal. A-RoPE puts the same diagonal prior into the positional encoding instead of the loss. On the song-generation side, non-autoregressive song models such as DiffRhythm take a start time per lyric line, handing alignment to the user.

方法Method

輸入。 伴奏、3 秒參考人聲與（訓練時的）真實饒舌人聲都經同一個仿 Stable Audio 2.0 架構的音訊 VAE 編碼成潛序列；這個 VAE 用作者的專有音樂資料訓練。歌詞經 ByT5-base 編碼，再經輕量 Transformer 適配層。

片段遮罩。 對人聲潛序列 z₁ 選一段連續遮罩：10% 機率遮住整段，否則遮住 70%–100% 長度的隨機片段。遮罩區用 Flow Matching 插值 z_t = (1−t)z₀ + t·z₁，未遮罩區保留真值當上下文，損失只在遮罩區計算。這讓模型在推論時能局部重生成某幾句。

條件組合。 伴奏潛序列投影到較低維度後，沿通道維與人聲潛序列串接，當逐幀的節奏骨架。參考人聲潛序列與時間步嵌入被前置在序列最前面。自注意力用 RoPE，但前置段與音訊段的位置索引各自從 0 開始，讓參考與時間步當全域資訊。交叉注意力前先把前置段拿掉，只讓音訊幀當查詢、歌詞當鍵與值，之後再接回去進前饋層。

A-RoPE。 音訊查詢端用標準 RoPE；歌詞鍵端的位置 j 換成 j·S，S = N/L（音訊長度除以歌詞長度）。效果是第 i 個音訊幀偏好第 i·L/N 個歌詞字元，像把歌詞表沿著伴奏時間軸攤開，但允許拉長音或停頓時偏離。

訓練與推論。 約 1,500 小時英語饒舌，依饒舌歌手不重疊切成 90/5/5。模型約 2 億參數，8 張 H100 訓練 50 萬步。推論時輸出長度設為伴奏長度，Euler 法 25 步、CFG 3.0；在 RTX 4090 上即時因子 0.027（10 秒音訊 0.27 秒生成）。

Inputs. The backing track, a 3-second reference voice and (in training) the ground-truth rap vocal are encoded into latent sequences by one audio VAE following the Stable Audio 2.0 architecture, trained on the authors' proprietary music data. Lyrics go through ByT5-base and then a lightweight Transformer adapter.

Span masking. A contiguous mask is chosen over the vocal latent z₁: the whole sequence with probability 0.1, otherwise a random span covering 70%–100% of the length. Masked positions take the Flow Matching interpolant z_t = (1−t)z₀ + t·z₁, unmasked ones keep ground truth as context, and the loss is computed only on masked positions. This enables partial re-generation of selected lines at inference.

Conditioning layout. The beat latent is projected to a lower dimension and concatenated with the vocal latent along channels as a frame-level rhythmic scaffold. The reference latent and the timestep embedding are prepended. Self-attention uses RoPE, but the prepended block and the audio block each index positions from 0, so reference and timestep act as global context. Before cross-attention the prepended tokens are removed so only audio frames are queries, with lyrics as keys and values; they are reinserted before the feed-forward layer.

A-RoPE. The audio query side uses standard RoPE; on the lyric key side, position j becomes j·S with S = N/L (audio length over lyric length). Audio frame i is thus biased toward lyric character i·L/N, like laying the lyric sheet along the track's timeline, while still allowing deviation for held syllables or pauses.

Training and inference. About 1,500 hours of English rap, split 90/5/5 with no rapper overlap. The model has about 200M parameters, trained for 500k steps on 8 H100s. At inference the output length is set to the beat length, with 25 Euler steps and CFG 3.0; the real-time factor on an RTX 4090 is 0.027 (10 s of audio in 0.27 s).

限制Limitations

沒有外部基準線。 Freestyler 沒有公開權重，所以全文只有自我消融。這不是作者的錯，但讀者無法判斷 BARS 與既有系統差多少；連一個「拿 E2 TTS 或 F5-TTS 類語音模型加伴奏條件」的簡單對照都沒有。

沒有客觀的節奏指標。 論文標題是「beat-adaptive」，但所有客觀數字都是詞錯誤率、說話人相似度與 DNSMOS，沒有任何量測音節起音是否落在拍點或小節位置上的指標（例如用拍點追蹤器算人聲起音與伴奏拍點的偏差）。節奏好壞只有 MOS-F 一項。

主觀測試有篩選。 聽測只挑兩個模型詞錯誤率都低於 5% 的樣本，作者明說是為了不讓唸錯字主導評分。這讓 MOS 能專注於 flow，但也代表聽到的是最好的那一批；錯配條件下各語速區間的詞錯誤率是 12.3%–20.5%，實際使用時多數輸出會有聽得出來的漏字或錯字。

「獨立控制語速」的介面沒講清楚。 推論時輸出長度固定等於伴奏長度，實驗中改變語速的方式是改變歌詞字數相對於伴奏長度的比例（字元／秒）。論文沒說明使用者要如何在同一段歌詞、同一段伴奏下指定不同語速。另外，A-RoPE 假設歌詞均勻鋪滿整段伴奏；若伴奏開頭有一段純器樂前奏，對角先驗會把歌詞往前奏裡推，論文沒有測這種情況。

資料與可重現性。 1,500 小時饒舌資料的來源與授權沒有交代，VAE 用專有資料訓練，論文只提到展示頁面。評估只做英語。

No external baseline. Freestyler's weights are not public, so the paper has only self-ablations. That is not the authors' fault, but readers cannot tell how far BARS is from existing systems; there is not even a simple control such as an E2 TTS- or F5-TTS-style speech model with added beat conditioning.

No objective rhythm metric. The title says 'beat-adaptive', but every objective number is WER, speaker similarity or DNSMOS; nothing measures whether syllable onsets land on beats or bar positions (for example, offsets between vocal onsets and beats from a beat tracker). Rhythm quality rests on MOS-F alone.

The listening test is filtered. Only samples where both models achieve WER below 5% are rated, which the authors say keeps mispronunciations from dominating judgments. That lets MOS focus on flow, but it also means listeners heard the best subset; WER under mismatch ranges from 12.3% to 20.5% across speed bins, so in practice most outputs would have audible missing or wrong words.

The 'independent tempo control' interface is unclear. At inference the output length equals the beat length, and the experiments vary speech rate by changing lyric length relative to beat duration (characters per second). The paper does not explain how a user would request a different pace for the same lyrics over the same beat. Also, A-RoPE assumes lyrics spread evenly across the whole track; if the beat opens with an instrumental intro, the diagonal prior would push lyrics into it, which the paper does not test.

Data and reproducibility. The source and licensing of the 1,500 hours of rap are not stated, the VAE is trained on proprietary data, and the paper mentions only a demo page. Evaluation is English-only.

討論Discussion

消融（原配對，表 1）。 完整設定（ByT5 + 適配層 + A-RoPE）詞錯誤率 5.1%，真人錄音同樣是 5.1%。拿掉 A-RoPE：6.0%；拿掉適配層：9.7%（插入率升到 3.2%，即重複）；兩者都拿掉：37.7%，替換率 25.0%，等於歌詞基本沒對上。換成 SpeechT5：兩者都有 5.6%、只有適配層 8.8%、只有 A-RoPE 7.8%、都沒有 10.0%。有個有趣細節：完整模型的替換率 2.9% 低於真人的 3.8%，生成的饒舌比真人更「咬字清楚」，與 DNSMOS 生成樣本高於真人（3.49 對 3.35）一致，都是生成過程的平滑效果。

A-RoPE 的價值主要在錯配時才顯現。 原配對下它只把詞錯誤率從 6.0% 降到 5.1%。但把歌詞與伴奏隨機錯配後（表 2，訓練分佈 μ = 17.9、σ = 2.9 字元／秒），五個速度區間全面改善：最慢的 −2σ 區間 33.6% → 20.5%、平均區間 22.0% → 12.3%、最快的 +2σ 區間 26.9% → 15.4%。錯誤方向也符合直覺：語速越快刪除越多（漏字），越慢插入越多（重複）。訓練動態也支持這點：沒有 A-RoPE 的模型前約 5 萬步幾乎不使用歌詞，之後才突然開始依賴。

但即使有 A-RoPE，錯配仍讓詞錯誤率翻倍以上：平均語速區間 12.3%，對比原配對的 5.1%。也就是說，模型很依賴「歌詞本來就是為這段伴奏寫的」這個結構；換一段伴奏，即使語速正常，也明顯變差。這正是「讓創作者在任意伴奏上試唸」這個應用情境最在意的部分。

主觀評分。 40 位聽者：MOS-F 有 A-RoPE 3.51、無 3.34（p = 0.01），真人 3.81；MOS-S 3.82 對 3.98，真人 3.95，兩個模型與真人都沒有顯著差異；MOS-Q 3.35 對 3.46（p = 0.06）。所以 A-RoPE 換到的是節奏感，音質與音色沒有顯著差別，數值上甚至略低。說話人相似度客觀上也一樣：原配對兩者都是 0.80，與真人相同。

Ablations (matched pairs, Table 1). The full setup (ByT5 + adapter + A-RoPE) reaches 5.1% WER, the same 5.1% as human recordings. Without A-RoPE: 6.0%; without the adapter: 9.7% (insertions rise to 3.2%, i.e. repetition); without both: 37.7% with 25.0% substitutions, meaning the lyrics are essentially not followed. With SpeechT5: both components 5.6%, adapter only 8.8%, A-RoPE only 7.8%, neither 10.0%. A curious detail: the full model's substitution rate of 2.9% is below the human 3.8%, so generated rap is more 'clearly articulated' than real rap, consistent with generated DNSMOS exceeding human (3.49 vs 3.35); both reflect the smoothing of generation.

A-RoPE's value shows mainly under mismatch. On matched pairs it only lowers WER from 6.0% to 5.1%. But with lyrics and beats randomly mismatched (Table 2; training distribution μ = 17.9, σ = 2.9 chars/s), all five speed bins improve: slowest −2σ 33.6% → 20.5%, mean 22.0% → 12.3%, fastest +2σ 26.9% → 15.4%. The error direction is intuitive: faster rates produce more deletions (skips), slower rates more insertions (repeats). Training dynamics agree: without A-RoPE, the model barely uses lyrics for about the first 50k steps before abruptly starting to rely on them.

But even with A-RoPE, mismatch more than doubles WER: 12.3% in the mean bin versus 5.1% on matched pairs. The model leans heavily on the structure of lyrics having been written for that beat; swap the beat and it degrades clearly even at normal speech rate. That is precisely what matters most for the stated use case of letting artists try lyrics over arbitrary beats.

Subjective scores. With 40 listeners: MOS-F is 3.51 with A-RoPE versus 3.34 without (p = 0.01), human 3.81; MOS-S is 3.82 versus 3.98, human 3.95, with neither model differing significantly from human; MOS-Q is 3.35 versus 3.46 (p = 0.06). So A-RoPE buys rhythmic feel, with no significant difference in quality or timbre, numerically even slightly lower. Objective speaker similarity agrees: 0.80 for both on matched pairs, equal to human.

AudioCodex: Streaming Autoencoders for Live Audio

AudioCodex：完全因果的連續潛空間音訊自編碼器，把立體聲對數幅度頻譜壓成約 10 Hz 的球面正規化潛序列，以 improved Mean Flow 一步解碼幅度、另以 R3GAN 訓練的因果相位估計器還原波形，搭配有界雜訊增強、USAD 表徵對齊（REPA）與 CFG 蒸餾，並用短片段潛空間生成代理任務量化重建與生成的取捨

Marco Pasini, Javier Nistal, Cyran Aouameur, Stefan Lattner, George Fazekas
arXiv:2611.12375 · cs.SD; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-09
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：現在的音樂生成模型幾乎都在自編碼器的潛空間裡運作，但通用音訊的連續潛空間自編碼器（Stable Audio Open 的 VAE、Music2Latent 等）都是非因果的，沒辦法邊收音邊編碼、邊生成邊播放。這篇是 Sony CSL 的 Music2Latent 團隊做的完全因果版本：只編碼幅度頻譜、用 Mean Flow 一步解碼、相位交給另一個 GAN。在潛空間生成代理任務上，d_z = 128 的版本生成 FAD 是 3.696，對照 Stable Audio Open VAE 的 9.518；串流模式下每個潛幀的解碼在 A100 上快於即時約 2 倍。選它是因為即時伴奏、互動合成這類應用需要的正是這種元件，而且消融做得很完整。但要注意：重建品質並沒有全面贏過非因果的 CoDiCodec（重建 FAD 0.544 對 0.304），論文也只報告運算吞吐量，沒有報告端到端延遲。Why this one: Today's music generation models almost all operate in an autoencoder's latent space, but continuous-latent autoencoders for general audio (the Stable Audio Open VAE, Music2Latent and others) are non-causal and cannot encode while recording or decode while playing. This is a fully causal version from Sony CSL's Music2Latent team: encode only the magnitude spectrogram, decode it in one step with Mean Flow, and hand phase to a separate GAN. On a latent-generation proxy task, the d_z = 128 variant reaches generative FAD 3.696 versus 9.518 for the Stable Audio Open VAE; in streaming mode, decoding each latent frame runs about 2x faster than real time on an A100. I picked it because live accompaniment and interactive synthesis need exactly this component, and the ablations are thorough. But note: reconstruction does not beat the non-causal CoDiCodec across the board (reconstruction FAD 0.544 vs 0.304), and the paper reports compute throughput only, not end-to-end latency.

動機Motivation

壓縮過的連續潛表徵是現代音訊生成的基礎：擴散或 flow 模型在潛空間裡訓練，比直接在波形上快得多。但自編碼器有個根本的拉扯：保留越多細節的潛空間，下游生成模型越難學；正則化得越強，生成越容易，重建卻越差。用生成式解碼器（讓解碼器自己「補」細節）是近年緩解這個拉扯的主流做法。

另一方面，即時應用越來越多：全雙工語音對話、即時音樂伴奏、互動合成器。這些都要求自編碼器是因果的：編碼只看過去、解碼也只看過去。語音那邊已經有因果的編解碼器（Moshi 的 Mimi 等），但它們輸出的是離散 token；通用音訊與音樂的連續潛空間自編碼器，則都是雙向的。

這篇要做的是：一個完全因果、輸出連續潛序列、能處理高取樣率立體聲通用音訊的自編碼器，而且重建與可生成性的取捨要不輸給非因果的模型。

Compressed continuous latents are the foundation of modern audio generation: diffusion or flow models trained in latent space are far faster than on waveforms. But autoencoders face a basic tension: latents that keep more detail are harder for downstream generators to learn, while stronger regularization eases generation at the cost of reconstruction. Generative decoders, which synthesize missing detail themselves, have become the main way to relax this tension.

Meanwhile, real-time applications are multiplying: full-duplex spoken dialogue, live musical accompaniment, interactive synthesizers. All require causal autoencoders, with encoding and decoding that look only at the past. Speech already has causal codecs (such as Moshi's Mimi), but they output discrete tokens; continuous-latent autoencoders for general audio and music are all bidirectional.

The goal here is a fully causal autoencoder that outputs continuous latents for high-sample-rate stereo general audio, with a reconstruction–generation trade-off no worse than non-causal models.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

AudioCodex 是同一個團隊一系列模型的延伸，最好放在這條譜系裡看。Music2Latent（ISMIR 2024） 用一致性模型當解碼器、在複數 STFT 上操作；Music2Latent2 加入「摘要嵌入」（每幀附加可學向量、經頻率方向 Transformer 後只保留這些向量當潛表徵）；CoDiCodec 統一了連續與離散表徵。AudioCodex 沿用摘要嵌入與頻率方向 Transformer，改動的是三件事：全部改成時間因果、一致性模型換成 Mean Flow、複數 STFT 換成只編碼幅度。消融第一列就是這個對照：同一因果架構下，複數 STFT 加一致性訓練的重建 FAD 2.17、生成 FAD 4.81，換成幅度加 Mean Flow 後是 0.82 與 3.61。

「幅度用生成模型、相位另外估」這個拆法，本質上就是經典的「頻譜模型 + 聲碼器」兩段式，與 HiFi-GAN、Vocos 這類從頻譜還原波形的 GAN 聲碼器同源。差別在於這裡的相位估計器吃的是全解析度的對數幅度頻譜，任務比從 Mel 頻譜還原更接近純相位重建。

論文說「目前不存在通用、可串流、輸出連續潛序列的自編碼器」。最該對照的反例是 RAVE（Caillon & Esling, 2021）：它是因果、可串流、潛空間連續的 VAE，早已被用在即時音樂表演上，這篇也在引言引用了它，卻沒有放進比較。這個宣稱成立的前提是「通用」兩字：RAVE 通常針對單一音色或資料集訓練。比較的基準線是 Music2Latent、CoDiCodec、Stable Audio Open 的 VAE，以及作為離散參考的 DAC；文中寫「五個基準」，實際列了四個。

AudioCodex extends a line of models from the same team and is best read within that lineage. Music2Latent (ISMIR 2024) used a consistency-model decoder on complex STFTs; Music2Latent2 added summary embeddings (learnable vectors appended to each frame, kept as the latent after a frequency-wise Transformer); CoDiCodec unified continuous and discrete representations. AudioCodex keeps summary embeddings and the frequency-wise Transformer and changes three things: everything becomes time-causal, the consistency model becomes Mean Flow, and complex STFT becomes magnitude-only. The first ablation row is exactly this contrast: in the same causal architecture, complex STFT with consistency training scores reconstruction FAD 2.17 and generative FAD 4.81, versus 0.82 and 3.61 with magnitude plus Mean Flow.

Splitting into a generative magnitude model plus separate phase estimation is essentially the classic spectrogram-model-plus-vocoder pipeline, related to GAN vocoders such as HiFi-GAN and Vocos that recover waveforms from spectrograms. The difference is that this phase estimator takes the full-resolution log-magnitude spectrogram, so the task is closer to pure phase retrieval than to Mel inversion.

The paper says no general-purpose streamable autoencoder with continuous latents exists. The counterexample to weigh is RAVE (Caillon & Esling, 2021): a causal, streamable VAE with continuous latents, long used in live music performance; this paper cites it in the introduction but leaves it out of the comparison. The claim holds only through the word 'general-purpose', since RAVE is usually trained per timbre or dataset. Baselines are Music2Latent, CoDiCodec and the Stable Audio Open VAE, with DAC as a discrete reference; the text says 'five' baselines but lists four.

方法Method

表徵。 立體聲各自做 STFT（窗長 2,048、跳距 1,024），取 20·log₁₀ 幅度後以 μ = −35、σ = 75 正規化，兩個聲道疊成通道維。相位完全丟掉。

編碼器。 時間因果的卷積切塊器（4 個解析度、時間方向總共下採樣 4 倍）把頻譜壓成約 10 Hz 的特徵幀；每幀附加 K = 8 個可學的摘要嵌入，經 12 層、512 維的頻率方向 Transformer（只在單一時間幀內沿頻率軸做注意力）後，只取回這 8 個嵌入，攤平後經瓶頸投影成 d_z 維潛向量，再做 RMS 正規化投到超球面上。

為什麼這樣能因果。 時間方向只有因果卷積與因果群組正規化（統計量只累積過去）；Transformer 完全不跨時間。所以串流時只要快取卷積狀態，每來一幀就處理一幀。

解碼器。 上採樣器 U 把潛序列抬回頻譜形狀，與高斯雜訊串接後餵給 U-Net 生成器 G，U 的中間特徵也注入 G 對應的解析度層。G 以 improved Mean Flow（iMF）訓練：Mean Flow 學的是兩個雜訊水準 r 與 t 之間的平均速度，因此可以一步從雜訊跳到資料。r 的取樣是 r = (1 − ξ^α)·t；α → ∞ 時退化成一般 rectified flow（穩定），α = 0 時等同一致性訓練（不穩）。作者選 α = 2.5。損失再乘上 1/(1 + F/700) 的 Mel 式頻率權重。

Representation. Each stereo channel is STFT'd (window 2,048, hop 1,024), converted to 20·log₁₀ magnitude, normalized with μ = −35 and σ = 75, and the two channels are stacked as feature maps. Phase is discarded entirely.

Encoder. A time-causal convolutional patchifier (4 resolution levels, total temporal downsampling of 4) compresses the spectrogram into feature frames at about 10 Hz; K = 8 learnable summary embeddings are appended to each frame, a 12-layer, 512-dim frequency-wise Transformer (attending along frequency within a single time frame) processes them, only those 8 embeddings are kept, flattened and projected through the bottleneck to a d_z-dim latent, then RMS-normalized onto the hypersphere.

Why this is causal. Along time there are only causal convolutions and causal group normalization (statistics accumulated from the past); the Transformer never crosses time. In streaming, cached convolution states suffice to process each new frame as it arrives.

Decoder. An upsampler U lifts the latent back to spectrogram shape; this is concatenated with Gaussian noise and fed to a U-Net generator G, with U's intermediate features injected into G at matching resolutions. G is trained with improved Mean Flow (iMF): Mean Flow learns the average velocity between two noise levels r and t, so it can jump from noise to data in one step. r is sampled as r = (1 − ξ^α)·t; α → ∞ reduces to ordinary rectified flow (stable) and α = 0 to consistency training (unstable). The authors choose α = 2.5. The loss is weighted by a Mel-like frequency factor 1/(1 + F/700).

限制Limitations

「幾乎全面勝出」的說法要打折。 表 2 中 CoDiCodec 的重建 FAD 是 0.304，AudioCodex 最好的 d_z = 512 是 0.544；以 CLAP 算的重建 FAD，DAC 0.044 也明顯最好。生成 FAD 上，d_z = 128 的 3.696 確實最好，但 d_z = 512 的 6.259 比 CoDiCodec 的 5.065 與 Music2Latent 的 5.241 都差。沒有任何一個 AudioCodex 設定能同時在重建與生成上贏過 CoDiCodec；比較公允的說法是：用一些重建品質換到更好的可生成性，而且是在因果限制下做到的。

沒有報告端到端延遲。 表 3 給的是運算時間：每個潛幀編碼 6.80 ms、解碼 41.38 ms。但依我的計算，一個潛幀對應 4 個 STFT 跳距，也就是 4,096 個樣本，在 44.1 kHz 下約 93 ms、48 kHz 下約 85 ms（論文自己也寫了這兩個數字）。模型必須先收滿一個潛幀才能輸出，所以光是緩衝就有八、九十毫秒，再加上運算，總延遲超過 100 ms。這對即時伴奏的語意層級也許夠用，但離樂手對演奏回饋常說的十幾毫秒還很遠。論文引用了 Caspe et al. 談低延遲神經合成器的工作，卻沒有正面討論這點。

生成代理任務偏短也偏小。 生成 FAD 用一個 4 層、512 維的小型 rectified-flow Transformer，訓練 4 萬步、生成 16 個潛幀（約 1.5 秒），而且只用 VGGish 算 FAD。作者自己承認這測不到長程結構。此外，代理生成器這麼小，可能天生偏好低維潛空間：d_z = 64 的生成 FAD 2.58 最好，但換成真正規模的生成模型，這條取捨曲線可能會移動。

其他。 消融只用每個領域 1k 段、單次訓練，像 CFG 權重 0.5 與 1.0（生成 FAD 同為 3.45）這類差距無法判斷是否超出雜訊。沒有任何聽測。REPA 的教師 USAD-Small 是語音與通用音訊模型，並非針對音樂。三種 d_z 一起訓練的具體機制（是截斷還是各自瓶頸）沒有交代。

'Matches or exceeds almost all' needs discounting. In Table 2, CoDiCodec's reconstruction FAD is 0.304 while AudioCodex's best, d_z = 512, is 0.544; on CLAP-based reconstruction FAD, DAC's 0.044 is clearly best. On generative FAD, d_z = 128's 3.696 is indeed best, but d_z = 512's 6.259 is worse than CoDiCodec's 5.065 and Music2Latent's 5.241. No single AudioCodex setting beats CoDiCodec on both reconstruction and generation; the fairer summary is that it trades some reconstruction for better generability, and does so under a causal constraint.

No end-to-end latency is reported. Table 3 gives compute time: 6.80 ms to encode and 41.38 ms to decode per latent frame. But by my calculation one latent frame spans 4 STFT hops, i.e. 4,096 samples, about 93 ms at 44.1 kHz and 85 ms at 48 kHz (the paper states both figures). The model must collect a full latent frame before emitting output, so buffering alone takes 85–93 ms, and with compute the total exceeds 100 ms. That may suffice for accompaniment at a semantic level, but is far from the roughly ten-odd milliseconds often cited for performer feedback. The paper cites Caspe et al. on low-latency neural synthesizers yet never addresses this directly.

The generation proxy is short and small. Generative FAD uses a 4-layer, 512-dim rectified-flow Transformer trained for 40k steps to generate 16 latent frames (about 1.5 s), scored with VGGish only. The authors admit this misses long-range structure. Moreover, so small a proxy generator may inherently favor low-dimensional latents: d_z = 64 gives the best generative FAD at 2.58, but with a full-scale generator the trade-off curve could shift.

Other points. Ablations use 1k clips per domain and single runs, so gaps such as CFG weight 0.5 versus 1.0 (both 3.45 generative FAD) cannot be judged against noise. There is no listening test. The REPA teacher, USAD-Small, is a speech and general-audio model, not music-specific. How the three d_z values are trained jointly (truncation or separate bottlenecks) is not explained.

討論Discussion

有界雜訊增強是這篇最值得帶走的小技巧。 訓練時把潛向量與球面雜訊做插值 (1 − a)·z + a·n，再投回球面；關鍵是 a 從 U(0, t) 取樣，t 是該樣本在資料端的雜訊水準。資料端雜訊小時，潛空間的擾動也小，解碼器仍拿得到細節；資料端雜訊大時，潛空間也被大幅擾動，逼解碼器只靠粗略結構。消融（表 1）：純球面瓶頸重建 FAD 0.77、生成 FAD 4.57；加有界雜訊後 0.73 與 3.95，重建甚至略好。若改成每次都從 U(0, 1) 取樣，生成 FAD 降到 3.21，但重建 FAD 升到 0.99。tanh 瓶頸的生成 FAD 是 5.14、layernorm 是 4.63，都比球面差。

REPA 與因果的代價都能量化。 加 REPA 讓重建 FAD 從 0.73 變 0.79（較差）、生成 FAD 從 3.95 變 3.45（較好），是一個明確的取捨。因果限制的代價：非因果版重建 FAD 0.65、生成 FAD 3.25，因果版 0.79 與 3.45，也就是重建差約 22%。有趣的是相位估計器不受因果影響：因果版重建 FAD 0.25，非因果版反而 0.27。相位估計用兩個判別器（幅度條件判別器 0.41、Mel 輔助判別器 0.31、兩者並用 0.25）效果最好。

在相近頻寬下比較，優勢比表面上清楚。 論文刻意不比壓縮率，但可以自己算：約 10.77 Hz × d_z = 128 大約是每秒 1,378 個浮點數，相當於立體聲 44.1 kHz 波形的 64 倍壓縮。依 Stable Audio Open 原論文，它的 VAE 是 64 通道、2,048 倍下採樣，也大約是立體聲的 64 倍壓縮。在這個近似等頻寬的對照下，AudioCodex 重建 FAD 0.773 對 1.151（以 CLAP 計 0.098 對 0.155），生成 FAD 3.696 對 9.518，兩邊都勝出，而且 AudioCodex 是因果的。d_z = 512 則是 16 倍壓縮，拿它跟其他模型比重建並不公平。

我的判斷。 這是一篇設計紮實、消融誠實（作者明寫因果有代價、α = 1.5 數字更好但會損失爆衝所以不選）的工程論文，真正的貢獻是證明「因果」與「好生成的連續潛空間」可以兼得，代價可控。但它還沒有證明自己適合「現場」：缺端到端延遲、缺在真正串流生成模型上的驗證，也缺針對音樂的評估（四個評估領域只有兩個是音樂，分領域結果放在網頁上而非論文裡）。下一步最該看的，是有人把它接到即時伴奏模型上量總延遲與聽感。

Bounded noise augmentation is the most portable trick here. During training the latent is interpolated with spherical noise, (1 − a)·z + a·n, and projected back onto the sphere; the key is that a is drawn from U(0, t), where t is that sample's data-space noise level. When data-space noise is small, latent perturbation is small and the decoder can still access detail; when it is large, the latent is heavily perturbed, forcing the decoder to rely on coarse structure. Ablations (Table 1): a plain sphere bottleneck gives reconstruction FAD 0.77 and generative FAD 4.57; adding bounded noise gives 0.73 and 3.95, with reconstruction even slightly better. Sampling from U(0, 1) every time lowers generative FAD to 3.21 but raises reconstruction FAD to 0.99. A tanh bottleneck gives generative FAD 5.14 and layernorm 4.63, both worse than the sphere.

The costs of REPA and causality are quantified. Adding REPA moves reconstruction FAD from 0.73 to 0.79 (worse) and generative FAD from 3.95 to 3.45 (better), a clear trade-off. Causality's cost: the non-causal version scores 0.65 reconstruction and 3.25 generative FAD versus 0.79 and 3.45 causal, about 22% worse reconstruction. Interestingly the phase estimator is unaffected: causal reconstruction FAD 0.25, non-causal actually 0.27. Phase estimation works best with two discriminators (magnitude-conditioned 0.41, Mel auxiliary 0.31, both 0.25).

At comparable bandwidth the advantage is clearer than it looks. The paper deliberately ignores compression ratio, but one can compute it: about 10.77 Hz × d_z = 128 is roughly 1,378 floats per second, 64x compression of a stereo 44.1 kHz waveform. Per the Stable Audio Open paper, its VAE has 64 channels at 2,048x downsampling, also about 64x for stereo. In this roughly equal-bandwidth comparison, AudioCodex scores reconstruction FAD 0.773 versus 1.151 (0.098 versus 0.155 with CLAP) and generative FAD 3.696 versus 9.518, winning on both while being causal. d_z = 512 is 16x compression, so comparing its reconstruction against other models is not fair.

My assessment. This is a well-designed engineering paper with honest ablations (the authors state that causality costs quality and that α = 1.5 scores better but is rejected for loss spikes); its real contribution is showing that causality and a generation-friendly continuous latent can coexist at a manageable cost. It has not yet shown fitness for 'live' use: there is no end-to-end latency, no validation with an actual streaming generator, and little music-specific evaluation (only two of four evaluation domains are music, and per-domain results are on the website rather than in the paper). The next thing to watch is someone wiring it into a real-time accompaniment model and measuring total latency and listening quality.

動機Motivation

拍點追蹤、和弦辨識、樂句切分、段落分析描述的是同一首歌在不同時間尺度上的組織。它們之間有明確的關係：和弦多半在強拍上換、副歌前常有導歌、一個樂句通常落在幾個完整小節裡。All-In-One、Beat-U 等聯合模型已經證明一起訓練能互相幫忙，但能這樣做的前提是：同一批錄音上要同時有這幾種標註，而且時間都對得準。

現有資源大多只涵蓋其中幾項，或有些維度是演算法估出來的。作者特別點出 SheetSage2 的做法：因為缺乏完整對齊的音訊與樂譜，只好靠自動標註的 MIDI、合成音訊與教師模型的偽標籤來補。這篇想補的是另一面：真實錄音上、逐首由專家標註並複查的多維度時間參考。

另一個動機是語言與曲庫。多數流行音樂標註集以西方英語歌為主，中文流行歌只有 POP909 這類以鋼琴編曲為目標、拍點與和弦由演算法抽取的資料。

Beat tracking, chord recognition, phrase segmentation and section analysis describe the same song's organization at different time scales. They are clearly related: chords tend to change on strong beats, a chorus is often preceded by a pre-chorus, and a phrase usually spans several whole bars. Joint models such as All-In-One and Beat-U have shown that training together helps, but that requires all these annotations on the same recordings, with accurate timing.

Existing resources mostly cover only some of these, or estimate some dimensions algorithmically. The authors single out SheetSage2: lacking fully aligned audio–score pairs, it fills the gap with automatically annotated MIDI, rendered audio and teacher pseudo-labels. This paper targets the other side: multidimensional temporal references on real recordings, annotated and reviewed song by song by experts.

Another motivation is language and repertoire. Most pop annotation sets are dominated by Western English songs; for Chinese pop there is mainly POP909, which targets piano arrangement and extracts beats and chords algorithmically.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文的表 1 把既有資料集排在一起，比較清楚：SALAMI（1,383 首）有多尺度段落，細層級常對應樂句但也包含動機等單位，沒有和弦與拍點；McGill Billboard 2.0（740 首）有和弦、樂句與段落，但 LAB 檔裡只有每個樂句的第一個和弦是人工對齊，其餘依固定速度假設線性內插；Harmonix Set（912 首）有人工拍點、下拍與功能段落，沒有和弦；Isophonics Beatles（179 首）與 RWC-Pop（100 首）四項裡有三項；POP909 的拍點與和弦是演算法抽的；SongFormer 的 SongFormDB 超過 14,000 首但混合了模型生成標籤。FaiSonic MMD 是表上唯一四項都打勾的。

跟它最像的其實是 RWC-Pop 加上 AIST 標註：同樣是一批錄音上累積拍點、段落、和弦等多層標註，也同樣包含非英語（日語）流行歌。差別在於 FaiSonic MMD 多了獨立的樂句層、規模是五倍，而且四個維度是同一位標註者在同一次作業中一起標的，而非不同時期分別補上。

論文沒提到但值得對照的是 HookTheory（SheetSage 第一版用過的資料）：它有對齊到音訊的旋律、和弦與調性，規模更大，但來自群眾標註、常常只涵蓋歌曲片段，也沒有統一的專家複查。FaiSonic MMD 走的是相反的取捨：量小但每首都經專家複查。另外，它沒有標註調性與旋律，這兩項 HookTheory 與 RWC 都有。

The paper's Table 1 lays out existing datasets clearly: SALAMI (1,383 songs) has multi-scale structure whose finer level often matches phrases but also includes motives, with no chords or beats; McGill Billboard 2.0 (740 songs) has chords, phrases and sections, but in its LAB files only the first chord of each phrase is manually aligned and the rest are linearly interpolated under a constant-tempo assumption; the Harmonix Set (912 songs) has manual beats, downbeats and functional sections but no chords; Isophonics Beatles (179) and RWC-Pop (100) cover three of the four; POP909 extracts beats and chords algorithmically; SongFormer's SongFormDB exceeds 14,000 songs but mixes in model-generated labels. FaiSonic MMD is the only row with all four checked.

The closest relative is actually RWC-Pop plus the AIST annotations: the same idea of layering beats, sections, chords and more on one set of recordings, also including non-English (Japanese) pop. The differences are that FaiSonic MMD adds an independent phrase layer, is five times larger, and has all four dimensions annotated together by one annotator in one pass rather than added separately over the years.

Not mentioned in the paper but worth comparing is HookTheory (used by the first SheetSage): it has melody, chords and key aligned to audio at larger scale, but it is crowd-annotated, often covers only excerpts of a song, and has no uniform expert review. FaiSonic MMD makes the opposite trade-off: smaller, but every song is expert-reviewed. It also lacks key and melody annotations, which both HookTheory and RWC have.

方法Method

標註流程。 兩位至少有音樂院學士學位的專家，經統一的標註準則訓練後分工固定：一位逐首聆聽、在同一次作業中標完四個維度，維度之間不規定順序、可以來回修改；另一位不參與初標，對全部 500 首做全曲複查。複查發現的問題退回，兩人一起聽、討論後修改。最後跑自動檢查（缺標籤、時間戳無效、區間重疊、超出音檔長度），異常再退回複查者。標註軟體由贊助的公司提供，支援多層標籤對照、正弦波和弦試聽與節拍器。

四個維度。 樂句：區間，與段落分開標註，段落交界處的弱起樂句可以早於下一段的第一小節開始。段落：八種功能（intro、verse、pre-chorus、chorus、post-chorus、bridge、interlude、outro）加區間。和弦：根音與性質分開記錄，共 34 種性質。節奏：人工標記的小節起點與拍號；拍點是把相鄰小節起點之間按拍號平均等分得到的，下拍就是小節起點。

統計。 速度 45.88–190 BPM，中位數 88；60–80 BPM 占 34.60%、80–100 占 26.00%。樂句 9,023 個，中位長度 12.16 秒。段落 4,625 個，副歌 29.64%、主歌 23.57%。和弦區間 50,693 個，大三 53.86%、小三 23.25%、小七 9.67%，其餘 26 種性質合計 2.69%。

Annotation process. Two experts, each with at least a bachelor's degree from a music conservatory, are trained on shared guidelines and keep fixed roles: one listens song by song and annotates all four dimensions in a single pass, in no prescribed order and free to revise; the other, not involved in initial annotation, reviews all 500 songs by full-song listening. Problems go back to the annotator; the two listen together, discuss and revise. Automatic checks (missing labels, invalid timestamps, overlapping intervals, times beyond the audio) follow, with anomalies returned to the reviewer. The annotation software, supplied by the sponsoring company, offers multilayer label views, sine-wave chord playback and a metronome.

Four dimensions. Phrases: intervals, annotated separately from sections; an anacrustic phrase may start before the first bar of the next section. Sections: eight functions (intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, post-chorus, bridge, interlude, outro) with intervals. Chords: root and quality stored separately, 34 qualities. Rhythm: manually marked bar starts with time signatures; beats are obtained by evenly subdividing the interval between adjacent bar starts per the time signature, and downbeats are the bar starts.

Statistics. Tempo spans 45.88–190 BPM, median 88; 60–80 BPM holds 34.60% and 80–100 holds 26.00%. There are 9,023 phrases with median length 12.16 s; 4,625 sections, 29.64% chorus and 23.57% verse; 50,693 chord intervals, 53.86% major, 23.25% minor, 9.67% minor seventh, with the remaining 26 qualities totalling 2.69%.

限制Limitations

單一標註者、單一複查者。 全部 500 首的四個維度都出自同一位標註者，再由同一位複查者審過，兩人有分歧時是「一起聽、討論後修改」。這能保證風格一致，但也意味著資料反映的是一個人的分析觀點。段落功能與樂句邊界本來就有詮釋空間（SALAMI 正是為此讓多位標註者標同一首），這篇沒有任何標註者間一致性的數字，也沒有保留修改前後的版本，讀者無法估計這些標籤的不確定度。

拍點不是人工標的。 拍點由小節起點等分推出，等於假設小節內速度恆定。對多數以節拍器錄製的流行歌影響不大，但抒情歌的彈性速度、漸慢收尾就會失真；拍點 F1 用 70 ms 容許窗，在 88 BPM 下一拍約 682 ms，小節內的漂移可能吃掉不少容許度。下拍是人工的，拍點不是，這兩者的可信度不同。

沒有釋出音訊。 釋出的是與 BigVGAN、DisCoder 相容的 Mel 頻譜，作者明說聲碼器重建只是近似。但基準模型吃的是 MuQ 與 MusicFM 從音訊抽的特徵；外部研究者若從重建波形抽特徵，會有分佈落差，無法完全重現表 2 的數字。

基準實驗很薄。 只有一個多任務模型、一個種子（42）、50 首測試曲，沒有單任務對照，所以無法判斷聯合訓練到底有沒有幫助；也沒有拿 All-In-One、Beat This!、BTC 等現成系統在這批測試曲上跑分。中文與英文各 25 首測試曲，卻沒有分語言報告。

One annotator, one reviewer. All four dimensions of all 500 songs come from a single annotator, checked by a single reviewer, with disagreements settled by listening together and revising. That ensures consistency, but it also means the data reflects one person's analytical view. Section functions and phrase boundaries are open to interpretation (SALAMI had multiple annotators label the same songs for this reason); the paper gives no inter-annotator agreement figure and keeps no pre-revision versions, so readers cannot estimate label uncertainty.

Beats are not hand-annotated. Beats are derived by evenly subdividing bar starts, which assumes constant tempo within a bar. That matters little for most click-tracked pop, but distorts rubato ballads and ritardando endings; beat F1 uses a 70 ms window, and at 88 BPM a beat is about 682 ms, so intra-bar drift could use up much of that tolerance. Downbeats are manual, beats are not, and the two deserve different levels of trust.

No audio released. The release contains Mel spectrograms compatible with BigVGAN and DisCoder, and the authors state that vocoder reconstruction is only approximate. Yet the baseline consumes MuQ and MusicFM features extracted from audio; outside researchers extracting features from reconstructed waveforms face a distribution shift and cannot fully reproduce Table 2.

Thin baseline experiments. One multitask model, one seed (42), 50 test songs, no single-task controls, so whether joint training helps cannot be judged; nor are off-the-shelf systems such as All-In-One, Beat This! or BTC scored on this test set. The test set has 25 Chinese and 25 English songs, yet results are not broken down by language.

討論Discussion

基準模型。 凍結 MuQ 第 6 層與 MusicFM 第 7 層（都是 25 Hz、1,024 維），以 30 秒不重疊窗抽特徵後串接成整首，2,048 維線性投影到 256 維，經兩個共享的殘差深度可分離卷積，再由四個任務閘門分流。樂句與節奏分支各用四層 TCN；和弦分支降到 6.25 Hz 後接兩層 Conformer；段落分支降到 3.125 Hz 後接四層 DiNA Transformer。解碼相當工程化：拍點用校準、閾值與非極大值抑制；邊界用邏輯迴歸校準加帶軟長度約束的動態規劃；和弦用 181 狀態的聯合 Viterbi，並以預測的拍點當換和弦的軟支持。

結果。 樂句邊界 F1 在 0.5 秒與 3 秒容許下為 62.90% 與 79.18%；段落邊界 68.83% 與 73.77%；段落功能 73.33%；和弦 Root 80.16%、Sevenths 65.66%、Tetrads 64.27%。段落邊界兩個容許度只差 4.94 點、樂句差 16.28 點，表示段落邊界一旦找到就落得很準（多半就在下拍上），樂句的位置則較模糊，這與弱起樂句的定義一致。

擴充實驗是最有資訊量的一欄。 把訓練集從 400 首擴到 2,350 首（加入作者公司更大的未公開標註庫），所有指標都上升：和弦 Majmin +5.22、MIREX +4.10、段落功能 +3.11、下拍 +3.08，但樂句 0.5 秒 F1 只 +0.42。和弦受益最多、樂句幾乎不動，暗示樂句邊界的瓶頸不在資料量，而在任務定義或模型設計。也說明 500 首對和弦辨識而言仍遠未飽和，這份公開資料比較適合當評估集與分析材料，而不是主要訓練來源。

真正有意思的研究問題在論文最後一節，作者只列了沒做：同一時間軸上可以直接問「和弦在小節裡哪個位置換」「不同段落功能的樂句長度是否不同」「樂句邊界與段落邊界差多少」。這些不需要任何模型就能用這份資料回答，可惜論文一個都沒算。

Baseline model. Frozen MuQ layer 6 and MusicFM layer 7 features (both 25 Hz, 1,024-dim) are extracted over non-overlapping 30 s windows and concatenated per song; the 2,048-dim input is projected to 256, passed through two shared residual depthwise-separable convolutions, then split by four task gates. Phrase and rhythm branches each use a four-layer TCN; the chord branch downsamples to 6.25 Hz and adds a two-layer Conformer; the section branch downsamples to 3.125 Hz and adds a four-layer DiNA Transformer. Decoding is heavily engineered: beats via calibration, thresholds and non-maximum suppression; boundaries via logistic-regression calibration plus dynamic programming with soft duration constraints; chords via a 181-state joint Viterbi using predicted beats as soft support for chord changes.

Results. Phrase-boundary F1 is 62.90% and 79.18% at 0.5 s and 3 s tolerance; section boundaries 68.83% and 73.77%; section function 73.33%; chord Root 80.16%, Sevenths 65.66%, Tetrads 64.27%. Section boundaries differ by only 4.94 points between tolerances while phrases differ by 16.28, meaning section boundaries, once found, are placed precisely (mostly on downbeats), whereas phrase positions are fuzzier, consistent with the anacrusis definition.

The expansion experiment is the most informative column. Growing training from 400 to 2,350 songs (adding the authors' company's larger unreleased annotated collection) raises every metric: chord Majmin +5.22, MIREX +4.10, section function +3.11, downbeat +3.08, but phrase F1 at 0.5 s only +0.42. Chords gain most and phrases barely move, suggesting the phrase bottleneck lies in task definition or model design rather than data volume. It also shows 500 songs are far from saturating chord recognition, so this public set is better suited as an evaluation set and analysis material than as a main training source.

The genuinely interesting research questions sit in the final section, listed but not done: on a shared timeline one can directly ask where in the bar chords change, whether phrase lengths differ by section function, and how far phrase boundaries sit from section boundaries. None of these needs a model, yet the paper computes none of them.
