SongCraft: Unified Song Generation and Editing with Reconstructive Learning

SongCraft:以重建式學習統一歌曲生成與編輯

Haohe Liu, Varun Nagaraja, Gael Le Lan, Xinhao Mei, Zhaoheng Ni, Vikas Chandra, Abdelrahman Mohamed, Yangyang Shi
arXiv:2609.16315 · cs.SD, eess.AS, eess.SP · 投稿submitted 2026-09-14
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：SongCraft 用同一套重建式預訓練同時做歌曲生成與編輯,詞級音素對齊把生成的字錯誤率(WER_s)壓到 0.133,比次佳的 LeVo(0.193)低約 31%;編輯端則靠殘差編碼器的瓶頸長度 M 拉出「能重建多少」與「還能不能被外部條件編輯」的取捨曲線,M=15 時 FAD 降到接近最佳的 0.727,同時保住 91% 的人聲 MIDI 編輯準確率。Why this one: SongCraft uses one reconstructive-pretraining model for both song generation and editing: word-level phoneme alignment drives generation WER_s down to 0.133, a 31% relative improvement over the next-best LeVo (0.193), while a tunable residual-encoder bottleneck M traces a reconstruction-vs-editability curve, hitting a near-optimal FAD of 0.727 at M=15 while keeping 91% vocal-MIDI edit accuracy.

動機Motivation

音樂製作是不斷修改的過程:聽過一次生成結果後,使用者常想「先拿掉鼓」,再「加一段貝斯」,再「把貝斯換成更暖的音色」——這些指令彼此環環相扣。但現有編輯方法各有明顯代價:以噪聲反轉(inversion)為基礎的方法(如 AudioLDM、MelodyFlow、MEDIC、DITTO)得仔細調整加噪程度,噪聲太少改不動、太多會把原始內容洗掉;以修補(inpainting)為基礎的方法(SongCreator、Seed-Music)得手動指定要改的時間區段,無法做跨全曲的整體調整,例如換聲音;配對資料式方法(AUDIT、InstructME、SongEditor)得靠昂貴的「修改前/修改後」標註,且只能處理容易模擬出來的操作。

生成端也有問題:端到端 flow matching 模型在歌詞條件下常出現漏字、疊字之類的語言錯誤;而節拍雖是音樂結構的根本,先前卻沒有工作明確把它當作生成條件用過。SongCraft 的核心洞見是把生成與編輯看成同一條「條件光譜」的兩端——生成用稀疏條件(文字、歌詞、節拍)讓模型自由決定其餘屬性,編輯用密集條件(逐字時間戳、人聲 MIDI、和弦、說話者嵌入、殘差編碼)把輸出釘死在特定目標上,兩端可以用同一個重建式預訓練模型涵蓋,不需要額外的配對編輯資料,也不需要為編輯注入噪聲。

Music production is iterative: after hearing a draft, a user often wants to "remove the drums," then "add a bass line," then "make that bass warmer" -- instructions with sequential dependencies. Existing editors each pay a cost: inversion-based methods (AudioLDM, MelodyFlow, MEDIC, DITTO) need careful noise-level tuning, since too little noise leaves the edit unchanged while too much destroys the original; inpainting methods (SongCreator, Seed-Music) require manually specifying the region to change and cannot make global edits such as swapping the singer; paired-data methods (AUDIT, InstructME, SongEditor) need expensive before/after annotations and only cover operations that are easy to simulate.

On the generation side, end-to-end flow-matching models conditioned on lyrics often skip or repeat words, and although beats are fundamental to musical structure, no prior work had used them as an explicit generation condition. SongCraft's key idea is to treat generation and editing as two ends of one "conditioning spectrum": generation uses sparse conditions (text, lyrics, beats) and lets the model fill in the rest, while editing uses dense conditions (word timestamps, vocal MIDI, chords, speaker embedding, a residual encoding) to pin the output to a specific target -- both covered by the same reconstructive-pretraining model, with no paired editing data and no noise injection required.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

歌曲生成這條線上,MusicLM、MusicGen、AudioLDM/AudioLDM 2、Stable Audio 系列走的是純器樂或文字對音樂的路線;有人聲的歌曲生成則有 Jukebox(用離散碼加自回歸 transformer,以歌手/曲風/未對齊歌詞為條件)、YuE(以 LLaMA2 架構把人聲與伴奏拆成兩條 token 串流)、DiffRhythm(用 latent flow matching,但需要強制對齊產生逐句時間戳)、SongCreator(雙序列語言模型,靠遮罩做編輯)、Seed-Music(自回歸加擴散混合,後製階段才做編輯)。SongCraft 與這些系統的差別在於:它不是先訓練生成模型、再另外加一個編輯模組或後製流程,而是讓生成與編輯共用同一組重建式訓練目標,編輯能力是訓練副產品而非事後補丁。

編輯這條線上,inversion 系(AudioLDM、MelodyFlow、MEDIC、DITTO)靠對隱空間加噪再用新條件去噪,DITTO 更進一步做推論時最佳化;inpainting 系(SongCreator、Seed-Music)靠注意力遮罩做片段級編輯;配對資料系(AUDIT、InstructME、SongEditor)直接從「指令-音訊」配對學編輯;flow-based 直接編輯系(FlowEdit、ACE-Step)則靠計算來源與目標條件之間的速度場差異,ACE-Step 用這個做歌詞編輯同時保留旋律與音色。

SongCraft 真正的技術源頭其實更接近另一支傳統——DDSP、AutoVC、SpeechSplit 這類「重建式解耦」工作,把聲音拆成音高、音量、音色,或內容、說話者、韻律等獨立因子,再靠交換某個因子做編輯。SongCraft 可以理解成把這套語音領域已經驗證過的重建式解耦手法,擴大套用到完整歌曲的歌詞、旋律、節奏、音色、和弦上——這是它跟自己在論文裡宣稱「首創」的統一框架之間,最值得說清楚的類比關係。

On generation, MusicLM, MusicGen, AudioLDM/AudioLDM 2, and the Stable Audio line target instrumental or text-to-music synthesis; vocal song generation includes Jukebox (discrete codes plus an autoregressive transformer conditioned on artist/genre/unaligned lyrics), YuE (a LLaMA2-based model splitting vocals and accompaniment into separate token streams), DiffRhythm (latent flow matching that needs forced alignment to get sentence-level timestamps), SongCreator (a dual-sequence LM edited via masking), and Seed-Music (autoregressive plus diffusion, with editing bolted on as post-production). SongCraft's difference is structural: rather than training a generator and then adding a separate editing module or post-processing stage, it shares one reconstructive training objective across both, so editing is a byproduct of training rather than a patch applied afterward.

On editing, inversion methods (AudioLDM, MelodyFlow, MEDIC, DITTO) add noise to a latent and denoise under new conditions, with DITTO adding inference-time optimization; inpainting methods (SongCreator, Seed-Music) edit segments via attention masking; paired-data methods (AUDIT, InstructME, SongEditor) learn editing directly from instruction-audio pairs; flow-based direct editors (FlowEdit, ACE-Step) compute the velocity-field difference between source and target conditions, with ACE-Step using this to rewrite lyrics while keeping melody and timbre.

SongCraft's real technical lineage sits closer to a different tradition -- reconstruction-based disentanglement work such as DDSP, AutoVC, and SpeechSplit, which splits sound into pitch/loudness/timbre or content/speaker/rhythm factors and edits by swapping one factor. SongCraft is best read as scaling that speech-domain trick up to a full song's lyrics, melody, rhythm, timbre, and chords -- the analogy most worth stating plainly against its own claim of a "unified" framework.

方法Method

SongCraft 是一個 latent flow matching 模型,訓練目標是從不同數量的可解釋條件中重建音訊。VAE 把立體聲音訊壓縮成 25Hz、512維的潛在表示(壓縮比 1920);訓練時模型以 0.7 的機率切到「生成模式」(只給稀疏條件:文字描述、歌詞、節拍),以 0.3 的機率切到「重建模式」(給滿密集條件:逐字時間戳、人聲 MIDI、和弦、說話者嵌入、殘差編碼)。骨幹是一個 36 層、36 個注意力頭、隱藏維度 2304、約 42 億參數的 Diffusion Transformer(DiT),用旋轉位置編碼與雙向自注意力,並仿 U-Net 加上跳接。

一個值得記下的技術細節是「表徵對齊」(REPA)不只用在 DiT 上,還往回用在 VAE 訓練階段——把 VAE 編碼器輸出投影後,對齊到預訓練音樂編碼器 MusicFM 的特徵(用餘弦相似度損失)。作者發現這樣做會產生「連鎖對齊效應」:VAE 潛在空間先被 REPA 對齊過之後,DiT 用同樣的 REPA 目標也能收斂到更低的最終損失(1.151 對 1.211),顯示 VAE 潛在空間的結構會直接影響下游 DiT 學表徵對齊的難度。

生成端的歌詞條件用「詞級音素對齊」:把每個詞的文字子詞嵌入與音素嵌入,在詞的邊界處先融合、再送進模型,同時附上詞級全域位置、詞內文字位置、詞內音素位置三種位置編碼;這解決了純音素條件收斂太慢、純文字條件缺乏發音資訊兩者的問題。節拍條件則是把每一幀標成「無節拍/節拍/重拍」三類,編碼後直接與潛在向量拼接輸入。編輯端則多了逐字時間戳(用 Whisper 抽時間戳、Phonemizer 轉音素、按時間戳鋪到對齊幀)、人聲 MIDI(65 音域加靜音,共 66 類,用 SWIFT-F0 抽取)、和弦(25 類,用 BTC 抽取)、說話者嵌入(3D-Speaker 抽的 512 維向量)、以及一個殘差編碼器,專門吸收前面條件都涵蓋不到的殘留聲學細節(殘響、伴奏質地、音色細節)。

殘差編碼器的瓶頸長度 M(輸出幾個固定長度的 token)是整個編輯機制的關鍵旋鈕:M 太小,模型抓不到伴奏質地與空間感,重建品質差;M 太大,殘差編碼器會把所有東西都吃進去,模型學會繞過顯式條件直接從殘差重建,編輯能力形同虛設。作者用 8 組 M∈{1,3,6,15,30,50,75,150} 的模型系統性掃過這條取捨曲線,發現 FAD 與 KL 散度在 M=15 附近取得最佳值(FAD 0.727、KL 0.121),而不是 M 越大越好——這是一個違反直覺但被實驗證實的非單調現象。

SongCraft is a latent flow-matching model trained to reconstruct audio from a variable set of interpretable conditions. A VAE compresses stereo audio into a 25 Hz, 512-dimensional latent (compression ratio 1920); during training the model switches to "generation mode" (sparse conditions only: text, lyrics, beats) with probability 0.7, and to "reconstruction mode" (full dense conditions: word timestamps, vocal MIDI, chords, speaker embedding, residual encoding) with probability 0.3. The backbone is a 36-layer, 36-head, 2304-dimensional Diffusion Transformer (~4.2B parameters) with rotary position embeddings, bidirectional self-attention, and U-Net-style skip connections.

One notable detail is that Representation Alignment (REPA) is applied not only to the DiT but pulled back into VAE training too -- the VAE encoder's output is projected and aligned, via a cosine-similarity loss, to features from the pretrained music encoder MusicFM. This produces a "cascading alignment effect": once the VAE latent space is REPA-aligned, the DiT trained with the same REPA objective converges to a lower final loss (1.151 vs. 1.211), showing that the VAE latent's structure directly shapes how hard representation alignment is for the downstream DiT.

For generation, lyrics use "word-level phoneme alignment": per-word text-subword and phoneme embeddings are fused at the word boundary before being fed to the model, together with three position embeddings (global word position, in-word text position, in-word phoneme position) -- this fixes the slow convergence of phoneme-only conditioning and the missing pronunciation cues of text-only conditioning. Beats are encoded per-frame as one of "no-beat/beat/downbeat" and concatenated directly with the latent. Editing adds word timestamps (Whisper for timing, Phonemizer for phonemes, spread across aligned frames), vocal MIDI (66 classes over a 65-note range plus silence, from SWIFT-F0), chords (25 classes, from BTC), a speaker embedding (512-d, from 3D-Speaker), and a residual encoder that absorbs whatever acoustic detail -- reverb, accompaniment texture, timbral nuance -- the explicit conditions don't cover.

The residual encoder's bottleneck length M (how many fixed-length tokens it outputs) is the key knob for the whole editing mechanism: too small and the model can't capture accompaniment texture or room acoustics, hurting reconstruction; too large and the residual encoder swallows everything, letting the model bypass explicit conditions entirely and killing editability. Sweeping M in {1,3,6,15,30,50,75,150} across eight trained models, the authors find FAD and KL divergence both bottom out around M=15 (FAD 0.727, KL 0.121) rather than at the largest M -- a counter-intuitive, non-monotonic result confirmed experimentally.

限制Limitations

作者自己列出幾點:框架其實支援「部分密集條件」的中間態(只給幾個密集屬性,其餘留給模型決定),但論文只探討生成與編輯兩個端點,中間regime留給未來工作;編輯品質會被上游特徵抽取器(和弦辨識、音高估計、說話者嵌入)的準確度卡住;瓶頸長度與重建品質之間的非單調關係也提示殘差編碼器的架構本身(例如換成分層或多尺度設計)可能還有改善空間。

更具體的弱點是聲音轉換(voice conversion)不總是可靠——殘差編碼器難免也會吃進一些和說話者相關的線索,換掉目標說話者嵌入時,這些殘留線索可能和新身分打架,尤其來源與目標的性別、音色差異很大時,容易產生雜音或轉換不完全。此外 SongCraft 用的是標準 flow matching 目標,沒有像 LeVo 那樣做偏好最佳化(DPO)後製,這也被作者點名是 SongCraft 在主觀聽感上輸給 LeVo 的可能原因;實驗也只做到 30 秒片段,更長時長的擴展留給未來工作。

The authors list several caveats themselves: the framework technically supports an intermediate regime with only some dense attributes specified and the rest left to the model, but the paper only studies the two endpoints, leaving that middle ground to future work; editing quality is bounded by the accuracy of upstream feature extractors for chord recognition, pitch estimation, and speaker embeddings; and the non-monotonic relationship between bottleneck size and reconstruction quality hints that the residual encoder's architecture itself -- e.g. a hierarchical or multi-scale design -- may still have room to improve.

More concretely, voice conversion via speaker-embedding substitution "does not always perform reliably": the residual encoder inevitably picks up some speaker-related cues, and when the target embedding is swapped in, those leftover cues can conflict with the new identity, especially when source and target differ substantially in gender or timbre, producing artifacts or incomplete conversion. SongCraft also uses plain flow-matching objectives with no preference-optimization (DPO) post-training like LeVo's, which the authors flag as a likely reason it trails LeVo on subjective ratings; experiments are also limited to 30-second segments, with longer durations left to future work.

討論Discussion

SongCraft 最值得記住的量化結果有兩組。生成端:詞級音素對齊把 WER_s 壓到 0.133,比同樣用 flow matching 骨幹的 DiffRhythm(0.588)好非常多,也比排名第二的 LeVo(0.193)低約 31% 相對值;但在人類專業評審的主觀 MOS 上,SongCraft 在六個面向中的五個都拿下最高分(整體品質 4.075 對 LeVo 的 3.150、歌詞跟隨度 4.212 對 DiffRhythm 的 2.425),只有音質(AQ)以 4.450 些微落後 LeVo 的 4.488,差距僅 0.04。這組結果的重點是:「打得贏商用等級系統」這句話背後,指的是在intelligibility與多數主觀面向領先,而不是全面碾壓——LeVo 靠額外做過 DPO 偏好最佳化,在整體感受上仍有它的位置。

編輯端的核心貢獻是把「能不能編輯」量化成三個確定性擾動實驗:人聲 MIDI 準確率隨 M 增大從 98.2%(M=1)一路掉到 27.1%(M=150);說話者相似度從 0.462 掉到 0.146;歌詞可編輯度從 0.82 掉到 0.48。三條曲線在 M=15 到 30 之間都出現明顯轉折,佐證了作者「殘差編碼器容量一大就會繞過顯式條件」的論點。消融實驗也顯示節拍條件是有代價的權衡:拿掉節拍條件反而讓 WER_s 降到全表最低的 0.105,但音樂性(Mus)從 3.62 掉到 3.44、自然度(Nat)從 3.44 掉到 3.24——語言正確與音樂性之間存在真實的拉扯,不是單純加了就都變好。

SongCraft's two most quotable results sit on opposite ends of the pipeline. On generation, word-level phoneme alignment drives WER_s to 0.133 -- far below the same flow-matching-backbone DiffRhythm at 0.588, and about 31% relatively lower than the second-place LeVo (0.193). On human-rated subjective MOS, though, SongCraft tops five of six dimensions (overall quality 4.075 vs. LeVo's 3.150; lyric-following 4.212 vs. DiffRhythm's 2.425), trailing LeVo only on audio quality (4.450 vs. 4.488, a 0.04 gap). The takeaway is that "competitive with a commercial-grade system" here means leading on intelligibility and most subjective axes, not a clean sweep -- LeVo's extra DPO preference-optimization still earns it a place on raw subjective feel.

The editing contribution is best summarized by three deterministic perturbation tests that quantify "how editable is it": vocal-MIDI accuracy falls from 98.2% (M=1) to 27.1% (M=150) as M grows; speaker similarity falls from 0.462 to 0.146; lyric editability falls from 0.82 to 0.48. All three curves bend sharply between M=15 and M=30, backing the authors' claim that a large residual-encoder capacity lets the model bypass explicit conditions. The ablations also show beat conditioning is a genuine trade-off: removing it actually lowers WER_s to the table's best 0.105, but musicality (Mus) drops from 3.62 to 3.44 and naturalness (Nat) from 3.44 to 3.24 -- linguistic accuracy and musicality pull in different directions rather than both improving together.

MUUNRiver-Bench: Diagnosing Relation-Dependent Music Retrieval with Multimodal Instructions

MUUNRiver-Bench:用多模態指令診斷「關係依賴」的音樂檢索

Zhancheng Guo, Congren Dai, Shangda Wu, Jianhuai Hu, Danni Zhao, Xiaobing Li, Maosong Sun
arXiv:2609.16090 · cs.SD, cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-09-14
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：MUUNRiver-Bench 用 Suno 可控合成打造 3,440 首曲子、七個任務,證明「音樂相似度」沒有單一答案——聲學編碼器 MERT-95M 在片段檢索(T7)拿下 0.93 mAP 遙遙領先,文字對齊編碼器 MuQ-MuLan 卻在風格/歌詞類任務(T1-T3)以 0.57、0.41、0.35 mAP 領先,兩派系統在七個任務裡各自最強的項目完全不重疊;而測試過的兩種文字融合方案都沒能穩定超越各自的骨幹模型。Why this one: MUUNRiver-Bench uses Suno's controllable synthesis to build 3,440 tracks across seven tasks and shows there is no single notion of musical similarity: the acoustic encoder MERT-95M dominates segment retrieval (T7) at 0.93 mAP, while the text-aligned MuQ-MuLan leads the style/lyric tasks (T1-T3) at 0.57/0.41/0.35 mAP -- the two encoder families top completely different tasks, and neither tested text-fusion scheme beats its own backbone consistently.

動機Motivation

給同一首參考曲,不同聽者想要的「像」可能完全不一樣:有人想要同一種風格配上新主題,有人想要保留旋律的翻唱版,有人想要音色相近但曲子不同的版本——這些意圖對應到互相矛盾的排序結果,對某個任務排第一的答案,換一個意圖可能排到最後。現有的多模態檢索系統(UniIR、GME)已經證明語言可以拿來當檢索的條件,但音樂的特殊之處在於風格、歌詞、旋律、音色、來源身分同時疊在同一段隨時間展開的訊號裡,而且彼此暗示著不相容的排序準則。

更麻煩的是,現有的音樂檢索評測本來就是分裂的:音訊對音訊系統各自處理翻唱辨識、哼唱檢索,語言對音訊模型則各自處理文字對音樂搜尋,沒有一套共同協定能拿來比較「不同指令定義的相關性關係」下,各種表徵到底表現如何。作者的解法是利用可控合成:配對的「意圖-音訊」資料本來就稀少,原因是版權限制加上真實錄音裡各種屬性天生糾纏在一起沒法拆開,而 Suno、Udio 這類系統可以指定要保留哪些屬性、改變哪些屬性,因此能人工造出「查詢與目標之間關係已知」的檢索配對。

Given the same reference track, what "similar" means can be completely different across listeners: one wants the same style with a new theme, another wants a cover that preserves the melody, another wants a different song sung in a comparable voice -- intents that imply mutually exclusive rankings, where the top answer for one purpose ranks last for another. Existing multimodal retrievers (UniIR, GME) already show language can condition retrieval, but music is distinctive in that style, lyrics, melody, timbre, and source identity all coexist in one temporally extended signal and point toward incompatible ranking criteria.

Worse, existing music-retrieval evaluation is already fragmented: audio-to-audio systems handle cover identification and query-by-humming separately from language-audio models that handle text-to-music search, with no shared protocol for comparing representations across instruction-defined relevance relations. The authors' fix is controllable synthesis: paired intent-audio data is scarce because of copyright and because real recordings entangle attributes that can't be pulled apart, whereas systems like Suno and Udio let you fix which attributes stay the same and vary the rest, making it possible to manufacture retrieval pairs with a known query-target relation.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

這篇的檢索框架延續自電腦視覺裡的「複合式影像檢索」(composed image retrieval)——把純圖對圖檢索延伸成「參考圖片加文字修飾語」的融合模態查詢,需要條件式相似度而非固定距離,以及後續的 UniIR(在 M-BEIR 上做指令引導檢索)、GME(在 UMRB 上用多模態 LLM 定義單模態/跨模態/融合模態三分法)。MUUNRiver-Bench 把這套做法套到音樂上,指令鎖定的是歌詞、人聲音色、旋律、編曲、片段身分這些音樂特有的面向,是視覺領域先前工作沒有處理過的。

音樂表徵這邊點名的系統分兩派:純聲學自監督模型 MERT、MuQ,以及聯合音樂-文字嵌入模型 LAION-CLAP、MuLan、CLaMP3;指令感知系統則有 WAVE-7B、OmniRet。評測基礎設施部分也點了具體資料集——翻唱辨識用 Da-TACOS、Cover80、SHS100K,哼唱檢索用 MTG-QBH,音訊-文字配對用 MusicCaps、Song Describer、WikiMT-X、MidiCaps,指令跟隨評測則有 CMI-Bench。作者明確指出這些既有基準要嘛做分類、要嘛評的是字幕生成與推理能力,「沒有一個評的是嵌入式的融合模態檢索」——這正是 MUUNRiver-Bench 要補的洞。

特別值得點名的是 CMI-Bench:它同樣評估音樂指令跟隨,但評的是「生成式」的字幕與推理輸出,而不是嵌入空間裡的檢索排序,兩者測的其實是不同能力。而先前用合成資料做音樂評測的工作,合成通常是拿來做字幕生成或深偽偵測的訓練/測試材料,MUUNRiver-Bench 則是反過來用合成本身的可控性——「保留哪些屬性、改變哪些屬性」——去定義查詢與目標之間該有的關係,這是它跟先前合成資料用法最核心的差異。

The retrieval framing continues from computer vision's composed image retrieval, which extends plain image-to-image search into a fused-modal query of a reference image plus a text modifier, requiring conditional similarity rather than a fixed distance -- followed by UniIR (instruction-guided retrieval on M-BEIR) and GME (a single-/cross-/fused-modal taxonomy on UMRB using multimodal LLMs). MUUNRiver-Bench adapts this to music, with instructions targeting music-specific facets -- lyrics, vocal timbre, melody, arrangement, segment identity -- that this vision-centric prior work never addressed.

Named music representation systems split into two families: acoustic self-supervised models (MERT, MuQ) and joint music-text embeddings (LAION-CLAP, MuLan, CLaMP3), plus instruction-aware systems WAVE-7B and OmniRet. Evaluation infrastructure gets named too -- Da-TACOS, Cover80, and SHS100K for cover identification, MTG-QBH for query-by-humming, MusicCaps/Song Describer/WikiMT-X/MidiCaps for audio-text pairs, and CMI-Bench for instruction following. The authors point out that these existing benchmarks either do classification or evaluate captioning and reasoning on audio-text LLMs, and that "none evaluates embedding-based fused-modal retrieval" -- the exact gap MUUNRiver-Bench targets.

CMI-Bench is worth singling out: it also evaluates music instruction-following, but through generative captioning and reasoning output rather than embedding-space retrieval ranking -- a genuinely different capability. Prior work using synthetic music data mostly used it as training or test material for captioning or deepfake detection; MUUNRiver-Bench instead exploits synthesis's own controllability -- fixing which attributes stay and which change -- to define the intended relation between query and target, which is the real difference from how synthetic data was used before.

方法Method

核心檢索目標是給定自然語言指令 q_inst 與參考音訊 q_a,在候選集合裡找出使 f_q(q_inst,q_a)ᵀf_c(c) 最大的候選 c*,查詢與候選編碼器都輸出 L2 正規化嵌入,內積等於餘弦相似度;另外定義一個不吃指令、只用參考音訊的零樣本音對音基準線,用來診斷各編碼器在沒有指令時預設偏好什麼樣的相似度。

資料生成分兩階段。第一階段建候選池:先用 Gemini 3.1 Pro 把專家給的種子曲風擴展成有配額的階層式曲風結構(反映串流資料的分佈),LLM 據此寫出 Suno 相容的風格提示詞(情緒、配器、主題、年代、速度)配上結構化標記的歌詞,再用 SunoV5 對每組風格-歌詞各合成男聲、女聲兩個版本;另外對 45、50 組取樣配對分別做歌詞重寫、跨曲風重寫,再各加 90、100 首。最終候選池是 1,720 對、共 3,440 首,涵蓋 13 大類、116 子類曲風、1,102 條風格說明,男聲女聲比例平衡。

品質控管有三層:自動一致性檢查(Qwen3-Omni-Captioner 重新分析每首曲子中段 60 秒,把每個提示面向標成吻合/部分吻合/不符/不確定,曲軌層級一致率 93.1%、面向層級 94.2%,對照組曲風刻意錯配時只有 15.0%/22.2%);兩位音樂院專家對 114 首、695 個標籤做人工複核,篩掉幻覺標籤後保留標籤的精確率從 97.6% 升到 98.7%,仍保留 91.2% 的標籤,兩位審核者一致度 Cohen's κ=0.76;另外量到 SunoV5 的音素錯誤率是 0.11。

第二階段依七個任務各自的相關性定義生查詢:T1 相似曲(風格+歌詞都要match)、T2 保留風格重寫歌詞、T3 保留歌詞重寫風格、T4 翻唱(歌詞+旋律match)、T5 人聲音色(只match音色)、T6 人聲分離後比對原曲、T7 片段比對來源曲。其中 T4 用 SunoV5 以原始人聲為條件生成男女版翻唱,人工複核篩掉不夠像的版本;T5 用 Suno V5.5 的 Persona 控制把來源歌手的聲音特徵轉移到新歌上,並用 ECAPA-TDNN 做篩選加人工標註;T6 用 openl2s 抽人聲主幹;T7 取 10 到 30 秒不重疊片段。另外針對 T2/T3 設計了一組「實例層級」變體,指令直接點名參考曲與目標主題/風格(例如「保留參考曲的音樂風格,但找一首關於在家放鬆的歌」),用來更直接地檢驗指令敏感度。

The retrieval objective, given a natural-language instruction q_inst and reference audio q_a, is to find the candidate c* in the pool maximizing f_q(q_inst,q_a)ᵀf_c(c), with both query and candidate encoders producing l2-normalized embeddings so inner product equals cosine similarity; a zero-shot audio-to-audio baseline that drops the instruction is also defined, to diagnose what similarity notion each encoder defaults to without instruction conditioning.

Data generation runs in two stages. Stage 1 builds the candidate pool: Gemini 3.1 Pro expands expert-given seed genres into a quota-bearing genre hierarchy reflecting streaming distributions, an LLM writes Suno-compatible style prompts (emotion, instrumentation, theme, era, tempo) with structured lyrics, and SunoV5 renders each style-lyric pair as male and female vocal versions; 45 and 50 sampled pairs are further rewritten (new lyrics, or cross-genre style) adding 90 and 100 more tracks. The final pool is 1,720 pairs (3,440 tracks) across 13 genres, 116 sub-genres, and 1,102 style captions, with balanced male/female vocals.

Quality control has three layers: automated consistency checking (Qwen3-Omni-Captioner re-labels each prompt aspect over the middle 60 seconds as match/partial/mismatch/uncertain, giving 93.1% track-level and 94.2% aspect-level consistency, versus only 15.0%/22.2% on a deliberately genre-mismatched control); expert human review of 114 tracks / 695 tags that raises retained-tag precision from 97.6% to 98.7% (keeping 91.2% of tags, inter-annotator Cohen's kappa=0.76); and a measured SunoV5 phoneme error rate of 0.11.

Stage 2 generates task-specific queries for seven tasks: T1 similar-music (style and lyrics both match), T2 style-preserving lyric-rewriting, T3 lyric-preserving style-rewriting, T4 cover (lyrics and melody match), T5 vocal-timbre (timbre only), T6 isolated-vocal matched back to the full mix, and T7 segment matched to its source track. T4 uses SunoV5 conditioned on the original vocals to generate male/female covers with human review discarding weak ones; T5 uses Suno V5.5's Persona control to transfer a source singer's identity onto new songs, screened with ECAPA-TDNN plus manual annotation; T6 extracts vocal stems with openl2s; T7 uses non-overlapping 10-30 second clips. An "instance-level" variant for T2/T3 names the reference and target theme/style explicitly in the instruction (e.g. "keep the musical style of the reference, but find a song about relaxed comfort at home"), giving a more direct test of instruction sensitivity.

限制Limitations

作者列出的限制包括:整個語料庫都是 Suno 合成的音樂,能不能類推到真實錄音還是問號,而且合成器本身可能帶有特定的生成器相關性,對用真實資料訓練的聲學編碼器造成系統性偏差;共享協定量到的是「表徵對特定關係的偏好」,不是指令措辭的因果效應,實例層級協定雖然揭露了敏感度但仍屬相關性證據;質控驗證的是「產生出來的屬性與挑選出的配對」,不是窮舉式的相關性或偽陰性完整度,代表基準可能沒有涵蓋所有合理的負例。

另外測試過的兩種輕量文字條件方案——線性內插與監督式 MLP 融合——都沒能穩定超越各自骨幹模型,作者承認這代表現有的條件機制與任務需求之間有根本性的落差,也點名還需要「更細粒度的指令引導」;各任務規模差異很大(從 T2 的 90 條到 T1 的 1,625 條),而且沒有回報查詢層級的信賴區間,小幅差異只能當作描述性觀察,還稱不上有統計顯著性的排名結論;現有的指令感知模型(WAVE-7B、OmniRet)本來就不是為這個任務設計的,作者表示未來會評估專門針對音樂的指令感知模型上線後的表現。

The authors' own caveats include: the entire corpus is Suno-synthesized music, so generalization to natural recordings is an open question, and the generator itself may carry model-specific correlations that systematically bias acoustic encoders trained on natural data; the shared protocol measures representation preferences for specific relations, not the causal effect of instruction wording, and even the instance-level protocol -- while it does reveal sensitivity -- remains correlational; and quality control validates "generated attributes and selected pairs," not exhaustive relevance or false-negative completeness, so the benchmark may miss valid negatives.

Both lightweight text-conditioning schemes tested -- linear interpolation and a supervised MLP fusion -- fail to consistently beat their own backbones, which the authors read as a fundamental mismatch between current conditioning mechanisms and what the task needs, calling out "the need for finer-grained instruction guidance." Task sizes vary widely (from 90 queries in T2 to 1,625 in T1) with no query-level confidence intervals reported, so small differences stay descriptive rather than statistically established rankings; and since current instruction-aware models (WAVE-7B, OmniRet) weren't designed for this task, the authors say they will evaluate music-specific instruction-aware models as they become available.

討論Discussion

全篇最核心的一句話是「沒有一種表徵能提供放諸四海皆準的音樂相似度」,七個任務裡每個任務都由不同模型稱王,而且 mAP 與 Recall@10 給出一致的名次:片段檢索(T7)MERT-95M 拿 0.93 mAP 遙遙領先,MuQ 緊追在後拿 0.91,但兩者在風格/歌詞類任務(T1-T3)最高也只到 0.38;文字對齊的 MuQ-MuLan 在 T1-T3 分別拿下 0.57、0.41、0.35 mAP,是這三個任務裡最強的,但在片段檢索上以 0.81 落後 MERT-95M 的 0.93(差 0.12);翻唱任務(T4)換成 MuQ 最強(0.70 mAP);人聲音色(T5)CLaMP3 最強但也只有 0.30;人聲分離比對(T6)則是 WAVE-7B 最強(0.55 mAP),其餘模型多半掉到 0.03-0.07。

這組數字直接證實作者說的「聲學編碼器偏向局部身分特徵,文字對齊編碼器偏向語意關係」,兩派系統的長處完全不重疊。

兩種文字融合方案的結果同樣值得記下具體數字:線性內插(α=0.2)在 T1-T3 上都輸給純 MuQ-MuLan 骨幹(T1 0.55 對 0.57、T2 0.38 對 0.41、T3 0.34 對 0.35,差距雖小但一致為負);監督式 MLP 融合在七個任務中的六個都輸給骨幹,T2 上更是輸了 0.18(0.23 對 0.41),三個隨機種子的標準差落在 0.009 到 0.035 之間,顯示訓練本身也不穩定。這說明「簡單融合機制」目前還無法在不稀釋參考音訊證據的前提下,把文字指令的資訊有效注入嵌入空間——這是這篇論文留給後續工作最明確的一道題目,而不是一個已經被解決、只等更大模型去驗證的問題。

The paper's central claim is that "no representation provides a universally suitable notion of musical similarity," with a different model topping each of the seven tasks and mAP/Recall@10 agreeing on rank order: on segment retrieval (T7), MERT-95M leads at 0.93 mAP with MuQ close behind at 0.91, yet both cap out at 0.38 on the style/lyric tasks (T1-T3); the text-aligned MuQ-MuLan tops T1-T3 at 0.57/0.41/0.35 mAP but trails MERT-95M on T7 at 0.81 vs. 0.93 (a 0.12 gap); MuQ leads the cover task (T4) at 0.70 mAP; CLaMP3 leads vocal-timbre (T5) but only at 0.30; and WAVE-7B leads isolated-vocal matching (T6) at 0.55 mAP while most other models fall to 0.03-0.07.

These numbers directly back the claim that "acoustic encoders favour local identity, whereas text-aligned encoders favour semantic relations" -- the two families' strengths simply don't overlap.

The two text-fusion schemes are worth quoting exactly too: linear interpolation (alpha=0.2) loses to the plain MuQ-MuLan backbone on all of T1-T3 (0.55 vs. 0.57, 0.38 vs. 0.41, 0.34 vs. 0.35 -- small but consistently negative gaps); the supervised MLP fusion loses to the backbone on six of seven tasks, dropping 0.18 on T2 alone (0.23 vs. 0.41), with three-seed standard deviations of 0.009-0.035 showing the training itself is unstable. In other words, "simple fusion" cannot yet inject textual instruction information into the embedding space without diluting the reference-audio evidence -- a clearly open problem the paper leaves for future work, not a solved one waiting only for a bigger model.

動機Motivation

要生成一整首連貫的長篇歌曲,需要同時處理兩種截然不同尺度的決策:一種是長程的音樂性選擇(旋律怎麼發展、段落怎麼轉換、歌詞跟音樂怎麼呼應),一種是細節的聲學實現(音色、人聲質地、瞬態)。從只生成片段走到生成完整歌曲,意味著這兩種尺度必須被同時協調好。作者的動機除了品質之外,還包括讓創作者能在合成前就檢視、修改一份「顯式的音樂規劃」,而不是把整個生成過程當黑盒;同時希望同一個系統既能吃開放域的文字描述,也能吃結構化的符號式引導。

為了同時做到這兩件事,作者主張把「音樂序列組織」與「高保真度聲學算繪」拆成兩個不同元件分別處理會更有效——這也是 StepAudio 3 Music 三段式架構(音樂 tokenizer、自回歸語言模型、flow matching 算繪器)背後的核心設計假設。

Generating a full, coherent long-form song requires coordinating two very different scales of decision at once: long-range musical choices (how a melody develops, how sections transition, how lyrics track the music) and fine-grained acoustic realization (timbre, vocal texture, transients). Moving from excerpt generation to whole-song generation means both scales have to be handled together. Beyond quality, the authors are also motivated by letting creators inspect and revise an explicit musical plan before synthesis rather than treating generation as a black box, while supporting both open-domain text control and structured symbolic guidance in the same system.

Their proposed way to do both is to split "music sequence organization" and "high-fidelity acoustic rendering" into separate components -- the core design assumption behind StepAudio 3 Music's three-part architecture (a music tokenizer, an autoregressive LLM, and a flow-matching renderer).

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

長篇音樂生成這條線上點名的系統包括:Stable Audio、DiffRhythm、ACE-Step 這類長篇 latent diffusion 方法;Seed-Music 把自回歸與擴散整合起來做有條件的歌曲生成;InspireMusic 用自回歸 token 加 flow matching 算繪器,但走多 codebook 設計;某個「全曲框架」用八個 codebook 的 RVQ 加階層式建模。StepAudio 3 Music 跟這些系統的差別很直接:同樣是自回歸加算繪器的兩段式架構,它主張單一 codebook 比多 codebook RVQ 更適合自回歸生成,即使重建保真度較低,也因為預測目標更單純而讓音樂發展更穩定——這是一個經過控制實驗(見方法段落)驗證過的具體設計選擇,不是憑空的架構偏好。

規劃這條線更能看出這篇的定位:MusiCoT 用量化過的 CLAP 表徵做規劃,StepAudio 3 Music 改用人類可讀的 ABC 記譜;Melody-CoT 只規劃人聲旋律的 token,這篇把規劃範圍擴大到完整的和聲與節奏結構;JASCO 直接拿和弦、旋律當條件,但條件本身不能被使用者檢視或編輯,ABC-CoT 則讓規劃階段的輸出是可讀、可編輯的樂譜;ChatMusician、SongComposer 已經示範過 LLM 可以處理 ABC 記譜之類的符號音樂,這篇的貢獻是把這類符號規劃跟聲學算繪串接起來,讓規劃真正能落地到最終音訊。

整體而言,StepAudio 3 Music 宣稱的「首創」與其說是全新概念,不如說是把符號音樂 LLM(ChatMusician 系)與生成式音樂算繪器(DiffRhythm/ACE-Step 系)這兩條原本分開發展的技術線,用 ABC-CoT 這一層中介表示正式接起來。

On long-form music generation, named systems include Stable Audio, DiffRhythm, and ACE-Step (long-form latent diffusion); Seed-Music (autoregressive plus diffusion for conditioned song generation); InspireMusic (autoregressive tokens with a flow-matching renderer, but a multi-codebook design); and an unnamed "full-song framework" using eight-codebook RVQ with hierarchical modeling. StepAudio 3 Music's difference from these is concrete: within the same two-stage autoregressive-plus-renderer pattern, it argues a single codebook suits autoregressive generation better than multi-codebook RVQ, even at lower reconstruction fidelity, because a simpler prediction target keeps musical development more stable -- a specific design choice backed by a controlled ablation (see Method), not an unmotivated architectural preference.

The planning side shows the paper's real positioning more clearly: MusiCoT plans with quantized CLAP representations, where this paper substitutes human-readable ABC notation; Melody-CoT plans only vocal-melody tokens, where this paper extends planning to full harmonic and rhythmic structure; JASCO conditions directly on chords and melody but that conditioning can't be inspected or edited by a user, whereas ABC-CoT's planning-stage output is a readable, editable score; and ChatMusician and SongComposer already showed LLMs can handle symbolic music like ABC notation, with this paper's contribution being to wire that symbolic planning into acoustic rendering so the plan actually reaches the final audio.

On balance, StepAudio 3 Music's claimed novelty is less a new concept than formally connecting two previously separate lines -- symbolic-music LLMs (the ChatMusician family) and generative acoustic renderers (the DiffRhythm/ACE-Step family) -- through the ABC-CoT intermediate representation.

方法Method

系統分三個元件:音樂 tokenizer(50Hz、單一 codebook、65536 詞彙量)、預測音樂 token 的 Mixture-of-Experts 自回歸語言模型、以及把離散 token 轉回連續 VAE 潛在向量再解碼成 48kHz 音訊的 flow matching DiT 算繪器。ABC-CoT 的規劃機制是兩段式分解:p(a,m|c)=p(a|c)·Π p(m_t|c,a,m_<t),模型先生成一份 ABC 記譜(涵蓋速度、拍號、調性、小節結構、和弦、旋律),再以這份規劃為條件逐步生成音樂 token。

音樂 tokenizer 本身有一個關鍵的控制實驗:作者在 25Hz 基準下比較單一 codebook VQ、語意 RVQ(多個 codebook 疊加在語意監督空間裡)、聲學 RVQ(凍結語意 token、再對殘差分開量化,但語言模型可預測性差)三種瓶頸設計,發現單一 codebook VQ 相對語意 RVQ 只犧牲「適度」的重建保真度,卻能維持良好的音樂性,因為對自回歸模型來說,單一、簡單的預測目標比多 codebook 更容易維持長程一致——這也是它捨棄多 codebook 換保真度的具體理由。定案後把幀率從 25Hz 升到 50Hz 以取得更密集的時序監督,名目位元率為 50 幀/秒 × 16 位元/幀 = 800 bit/s。

Tokenizer 訓練分三階段:第一階段仿 BEST-RQ 做自監督預訓練(把一段連續波形換成高斯噪聲再抽 Mel);第二階段加入 CTC(歌詞)、Mel(音色/瞬態)、Chroma(音高/和聲)三種監督做多工表徵學習;第三階段才插入 VQ 瓶頸,用四項損失(CTC、Mel、Chroma、VQ)加權訓練。

語言模型預訓練同樣分三階段,總計約 1.18 兆 token:第一階段 600B token(歌詞到音樂約 500B、音樂到歌詞約 100B),序列長度 16,384;第二階段 500B token 做多工中期預訓練(標準生成約 32%、ABC-CoT 生成約 16%、音樂理解約 20%、參考條件任務約 32%),序列長度拉到 32,768;第三階段 80B token 做高品質退火,核心任務是歌曲、翻唱、人聲對混音生成。

後訓練包含約 200 億 token 的監督微調(涵蓋歌詞轉音樂、純器樂、帶 ABC-CoT 的生成、翻唱、人聲對混音等子任務),以及用約 2,000 組人工排序的偏好對做 DPO,排序準則涵蓋音樂性、連貫性、條件遵從度、人聲品質。

The system has three components: a music tokenizer (50Hz, single codebook, 65536-entry vocabulary), a Mixture-of-Experts autoregressive LLM predicting music tokens, and a flow-matching DiT renderer that converts discrete tokens back into continuous VAE latents and decodes them to 48kHz audio. The ABC-CoT planning mechanism is a two-pass factorization, p(a,m|c)=p(a|c)*prod p(m_t|c,a,m_<t): the model first generates an ABC score (tempo, meter, key, bar structure, chords, melody), then generates music tokens conditioned on that plan step by step.

The tokenizer design rests on a specific controlled comparison: at a 25Hz baseline, the authors compare single-codebook VQ, semantic RVQ (multiple codebooks stacked in a semantically supervised space), and acoustic RVQ (freezing semantic tokens and separately quantizing the residual, which hurts LM predictability). Single-codebook VQ only costs a "modest" reduction in reconstruction fidelity relative to semantic RVQ while keeping good musicality, because a single, simple prediction target is easier for an autoregressive model to keep consistent over long spans than multiple codebooks -- the concrete justification for trading fidelity for a simpler codec. Having settled on single-codebook VQ, the frame rate is raised from 25Hz to 50Hz for denser temporal supervision, giving a nominal bitrate of 50 frames/s times 16 bits/frame = 800 bit/s.

Tokenizer training has three stages: self-supervised pretraining adapting BEST-RQ (replacing contiguous waveform spans with Gaussian noise before Mel extraction); multi-task representation learning adding CTC (lyrics), Mel (timbre/transients), and Chroma (pitch/harmony) supervision; and finally inserting the VQ bottleneck, trained with a weighted sum of the CTC, Mel, Chroma, and VQ losses.

LLM pretraining is also three stages, roughly 1.18 trillion tokens total: stage 1 (600B tokens: ~500B lyrics-to-music, ~100B music-to-lyrics; sequence length 16,384); stage 2 (500B tokens of multi-task mid-pretraining -- ~32% standard generation, ~16% ABC-CoT generation, ~20% music understanding, ~32% reference-conditioned tasks; sequence length 32,768); and stage 3 (80B tokens of high-quality annealing focused on song, cover-song, and vocal-to-mix generation).

Post-training adds roughly 20B tokens of supervised fine-tuning across sub-tasks (lyrics-to-music, instrumental, ABC-CoT-conditioned generation, cover-song, vocal-to-mix) plus DPO on about 2,000 expert-ranked preference pairs judged on musicality, coherence, condition adherence, and vocal quality.

限制Limitations

作者自己承認幾個評測層面的限制:SongBench 同時也被拿來篩選 SFT 資料,所以它的評分並非完全獨立於資料篩選過程;主要的客觀比較每個條件只生成一次,沒有量到不同取樣種子之間的變異;為了公平比較只取各系統都成功生成的共同子集,這排除了任何一個系統生成失敗的條件;基準系統都是透過線上服務評測的,對方完整的推論設定並不公開,無法在本地重現;論文回報的子集主要評的是有人聲、有歌詞條件的音樂生成,本身不能直接證明樂器演奏、翻唱、人聲對混音這幾類生成的相對表現。

另外兩項是方法論上的限制:字幕相似度與整體音樂品質不能證明模型在音符級或小節級上真的遵從了 ABC 記譜;規劃加編輯的比較評的是完整推論流程,要拆解出規劃、生成、編輯各自的貢獻還需要更細緻的控制實驗。作者也直接點名「複雜編曲、很長的時間依賴關係、人聲自然度、高頻重建」目前都還是待改進的地方——這幾點跟論文主打的高分並列,說明目前的領先主要體現在美學類指標,而不是每一個面向都已經解決。

The authors flag several evaluation-level limits themselves: SongBench is also used to filter the SFT data, so its scores aren't fully independent of data selection; the main objective comparison generates once per condition, so it doesn't capture variation across sampling seeds; comparisons are restricted to the common subset where every system produced valid audio, excluding any condition where a baseline failed; baselines are evaluated through hosted services whose full inference configurations aren't available for local reproduction; and the reported subsets mainly evaluate vocal, lyrics-conditioned generation, so they don't by themselves establish relative performance on instrumental generation, cover-song generation, or vocal-to-mix.

Two further limitations are methodological: caption similarity and overall quality scores don't establish note-level or bar-level adherence to the ABC notation; and the planning-plus-editing comparison evaluates the whole inference workflow, so isolating each component's individual contribution would need a more controlled comparison. The authors also directly name "complex arrangements, very long temporal dependencies, vocal naturalness, and high-frequency reconstruction" as areas still needing work -- a reminder, alongside the paper's strong headline numbers, that the current lead is mainly on aesthetics-style metrics rather than a win on every axis.

討論Discussion

在 AudioBox 美學指標與 MuQ-MuLan 描述相似度上,StepAudio 3 Music 對商用系統取得全面領先:Content Enjoyment 7.7086 對 Suno V5.5 的 7.5646、Content Usefulness 8.0052 對 7.9584、Production Quality 8.3868 對 8.2988,MuQ-MuLan 相似度 0.4465 對 0.4437,均為表中最高。

但換到人類專業評審用的 SongBench 上排名反而下滑:旋律面向 Mureka V9 以 6.8121 最強、StepAudio 3 Music 6.5394 排第三;整體音樂性(Musicality)StepAudio 3 Music 5.7465 些微超過 Suno V5 的 5.6966,但仍落後 Mureka V9 的 5.9382。到了 Artificial Analysis 音樂競技場的主觀 Elo 排名,StepAudio 3 Music 以 1105 分排第四,前面依序是 Suno V5.5(1300 以上)與 Mureka(1150 以上)。三組評測彼此不一致,說明「自動美學指標領先」跟「人類主觀偏好領先」目前是兩件不完全對應的事,不能只看其中一組就下結論說贏過商用系統。

消融實驗提供了兩個具體、可驗證的數字支撐。DPO 後製對 SongBench 均分的提升是 6.5445 到 6.6438(+0.0993),對 AudioBox Production Quality 的提升是 8.2700 到 8.3868(+0.1168),對 MuQ-MuLan 相似度的提升是 0.4157 到 0.4465(+0.0308)——這說明論文報的最終分數本身就已經內含 DPO 帶來的增益,不是單純架構的分數。

ABC-CoT 規劃加上 LLM 編輯進一步把 SongBench 均分從 6.6438 拉到 6.7220(+0.0781),其中混音維度增幅最大(+0.1199),但這個比較評的是完整推論流程,不能單獨歸功於 ABC-CoT 這一個元件。音樂 tokenizer 的算繪器規模消融也給出一個違反直覺的結果:0.9B 的 DiT 在 MCD、MS-Mel-L1、MS-STFT-L1、SI-SNR、UTMOS 五項指標上都優於 4B、8B 的更大模型,只有 SDR 由 4B 模型勝出,顯示瓶頸出在 tokenizer 本身能保留多少資訊,而不是算繪器越大越好。

On AudioBox aesthetics and MuQ-MuLan caption similarity, StepAudio 3 Music leads commercial systems across the board: Content Enjoyment 7.7086 vs. Suno V5.5's 7.5646, Content Usefulness 8.0052 vs. 7.9584, Production Quality 8.3868 vs. 8.2988, and MuQ-MuLan similarity 0.4465 vs. 0.4437 -- all table-best.

But its rank slips on SongBench, rated by human experts: Mureka V9 leads Melody at 6.8121 with StepAudio 3 Music third at 6.5394; on overall Musicality, StepAudio 3 Music's 5.7465 edges past Suno V5's 5.6966 but still trails Mureka V9's 5.9382. On the Artificial Analysis Music Arena's subjective Elo leaderboard, StepAudio 3 Music ranks fourth at 1105, behind Suno V5.5 (1300+) and Mureka (1150+). The three evaluations disagree with each other, which means "leading on automated aesthetics metrics" and "leading on human subjective preference" are not currently the same claim, and neither alone supports saying it beats commercial systems outright.

The ablations give two concrete, checkable numbers. DPO post-training raises the SongBench mean from 6.5445 to 6.6438 (+0.0993), AudioBox Production Quality from 8.2700 to 8.3868 (+0.1168), and MuQ-MuLan similarity from 0.4157 to 0.4465 (+0.0308) -- meaning the headline scores already bake in DPO's gain rather than reflecting the architecture alone.

Adding ABC-CoT planning plus LLM editing further lifts the SongBench mean from 6.6438 to 6.7220 (+0.0781), with the largest single gain on mixing (+0.1199), though this comparison covers the whole inference pipeline and can't be credited to ABC-CoT alone. A renderer-scale ablation on the music tokenizer gives a counter-intuitive result too: the 0.9B DiT beats the larger 4B and 8B models on five of six metrics (MCD, MS-Mel-L1, MS-STFT-L1, SI-SNR, UTMOS), with only SDR favoring the 4B model -- suggesting the bottleneck is how much information the tokenizer itself retains, not renderer size.
