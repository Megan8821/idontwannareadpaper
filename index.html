FLOMSS: Generative Music Source Separation by Flow Matching

FLOMSS：用 flow matching 做全頻寬立體聲的生成式音源分離——預測目標、流路徑、單/多音源與混音一致性的系統比較

Shuyang Cui, Zhi Zhong, Keisuke Toyama, Koichi Saito, Qiyu Wu, Yukara Ikemiya, Junghyun Koo, Kin Wai Cheuk, Woosung Choi, Giorgio Fabbro, Wei-Hsiang Liao, Shusuke Takahashi, Yuki Mitsufuji
arXiv:2610.17821 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-13
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Sony 團隊把條件式 flow matching 搬到 MUSDB18-HQ 四軌、44.1 kHz 立體聲分離，系統比較 x/v 預測、OT 與 Schrödinger bridge 路徑、單/多音源模型和軟/硬混音一致性。67M 的 FLOMSS-M 平均 SDR 只有 4.34 dB（SCNet 9.69、HTDemucs 8.14），但 Mel 距離（1.55 對 1.61–1.66）與各嵌入的 Fréchet 距離較好，23 人 MUSHRA 排第二（59.12，SCNet 62.59、HTDemucs 58.71）。值得讀的是它把「生成式分離在 SDR 上吃虧、在分佈指標上佔便宜」量化成一個清楚的取捨，而不是宣稱全面勝出。Why this one: A Sony team ports conditional flow matching to four-stem, 44.1 kHz stereo separation on MUSDB18-HQ and systematically compares x- vs v-prediction, OT vs Schrödinger-bridge paths, single- vs multi-source models, and soft vs hard mixture consistency. The 67M FLOMSS-M reaches only 4.34 dB average SDR (SCNet 9.69, HTDemucs 8.14), but has better Mel distance (1.55 vs 1.61–1.66) and better Fréchet distances across embeddings, and ranks second in a 23-listener MUSHRA test (59.12; SCNet 62.59, HTDemucs 58.71). It is worth reading because it quantifies a clear trade-off — generative separation loses on SDR and gains on distributional metrics — rather than claiming an across-the-board win.

動機Motivation

音源分離（music source separation, MSS）十年來幾乎都是判別式模型的天下：給混音、回歸每一軌的波形或頻譜遮罩，用 SDR 打分。這種做法在訊號層面很強，但回歸目標天生會把不確定的部分「平均掉」，聽起來常是乾淨但發悶、或殘留別的樂器。語音增強那邊已經證明擴散與 flow matching 可以用生成的方式補回細節，音樂這邊卻還沒有人系統性地試過。

作者指出既有的生成式 MSS 有兩個缺口。一是多半不是直接學「混音 → 單軌」的條件映射，而是學音源先驗再做後驗取樣或補全；二是都不輸出全頻寬立體聲，對音樂製作來說這幾乎是門檻。這篇的定位因此是一份設計空間的盤點，而不是一個新架構：在同一個骨幹上把 flow matching 的各個選項一一試過，並把評估擴充到 SDR 以外。

For a decade, music source separation (MSS) has been dominated by discriminative models: given a mixture, regress each stem's waveform or spectral mask and score it with SDR. This is strong at the signal level, but a regression target naturally averages away whatever is uncertain, so outputs often sound clean but dull, or carry bleed from other instruments. Speech enhancement has shown that diffusion and flow matching can restore detail generatively; for music, nobody had tried this systematically.

The authors point to two gaps in existing generative MSS. First, most methods do not learn the conditional mixture-to-stem mapping directly; they learn a source prior and then do posterior sampling or inpainting. Second, none output full-band stereo, which is close to a hard requirement for music production. The paper is therefore positioned as a survey of the design space rather than a new architecture: try the flow-matching options one by one on a single backbone, and extend evaluation beyond SDR.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

生成式 MSS 的前作，作者點了三個：InstGlow（Zhu 等，2022）用 Glow 學各樂器的獨立先驗，再在潛空間最佳化讓總和貼近混音；Multi-Source Diffusion（MSDM，Mariani 等，ICLR 2024）學四軌的聯合先驗，以混音為約束做後驗取樣；MGE-LDM（Chae & Lee，NeurIPS 2025）把分離寫成潛空間的條件補全。FLOMSS 跟它們的根本差別是監督式條件生成：直接學 p(單軌 | 混音)，不需要先驗加推論迴圈，代價是失去 MSDM 那種「同一個模型也能生成」的能力。

不過論文自稱的「第一個系統研究」最該拿來比的，其實是語音增強的 SGMSE（Richter 等，2023）與 FlowSE（Lee 等，ICASSP 2025）。FLOMSS 的骨幹是照 SGMSE 設定的 NCSN++ U-Net、在 SGMSE 的冪次壓縮複數 STFT 上運作、機率路徑從「混音加噪聲」流到「目標軌」——這就是把語音增強的「帶噪語音 → 乾淨語音」橋，換成「混音 → 某一軌」。硬混音一致性則直接沿用 Scheibler 等（WASPAA 2025）的 FLOSS。所以新意主要在把這套配方搬到四軌立體聲音樂並系統地做消融，而不在生成機制本身。判別式的對照組則是 SCNet、HTDemucs 與 Open-Unmix。

For generative MSS, the authors name three predecessors. InstGlow (Zhu et al., 2022) learns independent per-instrument priors with Glow and optimizes latents so their sum matches the mixture; Multi-Source Diffusion (MSDM, Mariani et al., ICLR 2024) learns a joint prior over four stems and does posterior sampling under the mixture constraint; MGE-LDM (Chae & Lee, NeurIPS 2025) casts separation as conditional inpainting in latent space. FLOMSS differs fundamentally by being supervised conditional generation: it learns p(stem | mixture) directly, with no prior-plus-inference loop, at the cost of MSDM's ability to also generate.

Yet the closest comparison for the paper's "first systematic study" claim is speech enhancement: SGMSE (Richter et al., 2023) and FlowSE (Lee et al., ICASSP 2025). FLOMSS uses an NCSN++ U-Net configured as in SGMSE, operates on SGMSE's power-compressed complex STFT, and its probability path flows from the noisy mixture to the target stem — the speech-enhancement noisy-to-clean bridge with "mixture to one stem" swapped in. Hard mixture consistency is taken directly from FLOSS (Scheibler et al., WASPAA 2025). The novelty lies in moving this recipe to four-stem stereo music with systematic ablations, not in the generative mechanism. Discriminative references are SCNet, HTDemucs and Open-Unmix.

方法Method

表示：複數 STFT（Hann 窗 1534、hop 384），幅度做冪次壓縮（α=0.5、β=0.15）。機率路徑是高斯條件路徑，均值從壓縮後的混音線性內插到目標軌，起點 N(混音, σ₀²)、終點 N(目標軌, σ₁²)。模型輸入是當下狀態與混音的串接，加上時間 t。OT-CFM 用 σ_max=0.5、σ_min=0；SB-CFM 用 Brownian bridge 噪聲尺度 1，中間時刻標準差最大為 0.5。推論各用 10 步（OT 用 Midpoint ODE、SB 用 exponential integrator）。

比較的軸有四條。預測目標：v 預測（回歸速度場）對 x 預測（直接預測目標軌）。路徑：OT-CFM 對 SB-CFM。模型：每軌一個單音源模型，對一個模型同時輸出四軌（只把輸入輸出通道擴成四倍）。混音一致性：軟約束是在 x 損失外加 0.1 倍「四軌加總 vs 混音」的 MSE；硬約束照 FLOSS 讓路徑上每個時刻的四軌加總都等於混音，因為冪次壓縮不保持可加性，只能在波形或未壓縮 STFT 域定義。

實驗設定：16M 參數的 NCSN++，另有加寬到 67M 的版本；只用 MUSDB18-HQ 官方訓練集，加上同段落 2–4 軌隨機重混的增強；每個模型訓練 93,600 步、batch 32、4 張 A100。同骨幹的判別式基線只看混音、直接回歸目標軌。評估除了 SDR 與 Mel/STFT/波形 L1，還有 VGGish、PaSST、OpenL3、CLAP、MERT 五種嵌入上的 FD 與 MMD、CLAP 上的 LPAPS，以及 23 人、十段 8 秒片段的 MUSHRA。

Representation: complex STFT (Hann window 1534, hop 384) with power-law magnitude compression (α=0.5, β=0.15). The probability path is a Gaussian conditional path whose mean interpolates linearly from the compressed mixture to the target stem, starting at N(mixture, σ₀²) and ending at N(stem, σ₁²). The network sees the current state concatenated with the mixture, plus time t. OT-CFM uses σ_max=0.5, σ_min=0; SB-CFM uses Brownian-bridge noise scale 1, peaking at a standard deviation of 0.5 mid-path. Inference uses 10 steps each (Midpoint ODE for OT, an exponential integrator for SB).

Four axes are compared. Prediction target: v-prediction (regress the velocity) vs x-prediction (predict the stem). Path: OT-CFM vs SB-CFM. Model: one single-source model per stem vs one model emitting all four (only input/output channels quadrupled). Mixture consistency: soft adds 0.1 × an MSE between the summed stems and the mixture to the x loss; hard follows FLOSS so that the stems sum to the mixture at every point on the path, which must be defined in the waveform or uncompressed STFT domain because power compression does not preserve additivity.

Setup: a 16M-parameter NCSN++, plus a widened 67M version; training only on the official MUSDB18-HQ train split with random 2–4-stem remixing from the same time region; 93,600 steps per model, batch 32, four A100s. The same-backbone discriminative baseline sees only the mixture and regresses the stem. Evaluation covers SDR and Mel/STFT/waveform L1, FD and MMD over five embeddings (VGGish, PaSST, OpenL3, CLAP, MERT), LPAPS on CLAP, and a MUSHRA test with 23 listeners on ten 8-second excerpts.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是 SDR 落差太大，大到很難只用「生成式取捨」解釋。FLOMSS-M 平均 4.34 dB，SCNet 9.69、HTDemucs 8.14；連 Open-Unmix 都有 5.19。人聲一軌 FLOMSS-M 6.21、SCNet 10.9；「其他」一軌 2.54 對 7.28。更關鍵的是同骨幹判別式基線也只有 4.95 dB，這表示 NCSN++ 這個語音增強骨幹加上只用 MUSDB 訓練 93,600 步，本身就弱；拿它跟 SCNet 比 Mel 與 FD，混進了骨幹差距。

參數與計算量也不對等。表 4 寫得很清楚：FLOMSS-M 是 67M × 4 = 268M（每軌一個模型），SCNet 41M、HTDemucs 42M；推論還要 10 步 Midpoint，也就是每段約 20 次網路前向。論文沒有報告任何推論時間。

主觀測試的排名差距很小。FLOMSS-M 59.12 對 HTDemucs 58.71，只差 0.41 分；23 人、十段片段，沒有給信賴區間或顯著性檢定，所以「排第二」不能讀成勝過 HTDemucs。另外 FD/MMD 這類分佈指標比的是整體統計像不像，對「聽起來像貝斯但其實是別的音」並不敏感，而生成式模型正是會產生這類幻覺的那一方——論文沒有任何針對幻覺或串音的分析。

比較對象的訓練資料也不一致，作者自己有標註：HTDemucs 多了 800 首、InstGlow 加了 Slakh、SAM Audio 與 MGE-LDM 是別的資料集，後兩者還分不出「其他」一軌。多音源加硬一致性全面退步（SDR 2.11–2.34）這個負結果，作者也承認可能只是路徑與訓練策略沒調對。

The biggest problem is an SDR gap too large to explain away as a generative trade-off. FLOMSS-M averages 4.34 dB against SCNet's 9.69 and HTDemucs's 8.14; even Open-Unmix reaches 5.19. On vocals it is 6.21 vs SCNet's 10.9; on "other", 2.54 vs 7.28. More telling, the same-backbone discriminative baseline only reaches 4.95 dB, meaning the speech-enhancement NCSN++ backbone trained for 93,600 steps on MUSDB alone is itself weak; comparing Mel and FD against SCNet mixes in that backbone gap.

Parameters and compute are not matched either. Table 4 states it plainly: FLOMSS-M is 67M × 4 = 268M (one model per stem), SCNet 41M, HTDemucs 42M; inference adds 10 Midpoint steps, roughly 20 network passes per segment. No inference time is reported.

The subjective ranking gaps are small. FLOMSS-M scores 59.12 vs HTDemucs's 58.71, a 0.41-point difference; with 23 listeners and ten excerpts and no confidence intervals or significance tests, "second place" cannot be read as beating HTDemucs. Distributional metrics like FD/MMD compare overall statistics and are insensitive to a stem that sounds bass-like but is actually something else — exactly the hallucination a generative model can produce — and the paper offers no analysis of hallucination or bleed.

Training data also differs across baselines, as the authors note: HTDemucs uses 800 extra songs, InstGlow adds Slakh, SAM Audio and MGE-LDM use other datasets, and the latter two cannot separate "other". The negative result that multi-source with hard consistency degrades across the board (SDR 2.11–2.34) is, by the authors' own admission, possibly a matter of untuned paths and training.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的不是排行榜，而是消融表裡那條取捨線。單音源平均：判別式 SDR 4.95、FD-VGGish 2.55；OT-CFM-x SDR 3.77、FD-VGGish 0.87；OT-CFM-v SDR 3.23、FD-VGGish 0.77；SB-CFM-x SDR 4.63、FD-VGGish 2.88。也就是說，結果越接近判別式基線，SDR 越高、分佈指標越差；反之亦然。逐步分析把這件事看得更清楚：ODE 前幾步的目標估計跟判別式基線差不多，後面幾步拿 SDR 換 Mel 與嵌入距離。這給了一個實用的旋鈕——只跑前幾步就接近判別式結果，跑完就偏向聽感。

多音源的結果也有參考價值。一個模型同時輸出四軌，FD-VGGish 從 0.87 惡化到 2.30、FD-MERT 從 5.43 到 10.6；加軟一致性把 SDR 從 3.07 拉回 3.69，但嵌入指標更差（FD-MERT 13.7）。這說明「總和等於混音」這個約束在音樂上不像在 FLOSS 的語音上那麼好用，至少在這個骨幹上是如此。

讀這篇時我會把它當成「flow matching 在音樂分離上的下限參考」。如果換成 BS-RoFormer 或 SCNet 這類為音樂設計的骨幹、加上更多訓練資料，取捨線會不會整條往上移，才是真正值得知道的事；作者承諾開源，那時就能驗證。

The paper's real value is not the leaderboard but the trade-off line in its ablation table. Single-source averages: discriminative SDR 4.95, FD-VGGish 2.55; OT-CFM-x SDR 3.77, FD-VGGish 0.87; OT-CFM-v SDR 3.23, FD-VGGish 0.77; SB-CFM-x SDR 4.63, FD-VGGish 2.88. The closer a variant's results sit to the discriminative baseline, the higher the SDR and the worse the distributional metrics, and vice versa. The stepwise analysis makes it clearer: early ODE steps give estimates close to the discriminative baseline, and later steps trade SDR for Mel and embedding distance. That is a practical knob — stop early for near-discriminative output, run to the end for perceptual quality.

The multi-source results are also informative. With one model emitting all four stems, FD-VGGish worsens from 0.87 to 2.30 and FD-MERT from 5.43 to 10.6; soft consistency pulls SDR back from 3.07 to 3.69 but makes embedding metrics worse (FD-MERT 13.7). The "stems sum to the mixture" constraint is not as useful for music as in FLOSS's speech setting, at least on this backbone.

I would read this as a lower-bound reference for flow matching in music separation. The question worth answering is whether swapping in a music-specific backbone like BS-RoFormer or SCNet, with more training data, shifts the whole trade-off line upward; the promised open-source release would make that testable.

Transfer Learning for Rhythm Game Chart Generation from Music Transcription and Across Games

節奏遊戲譜面生成的遷移學習：凍結的自動轉譜編碼器，以及跨 osu! 模式與 Clone Hero 的聯合訓練

Sojeong An, Nathan Pruyne, Chris Donahue, Dasaem Jeong
arXiv:2610.14955 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把自動轉譜（AMT）模型 MuScriptor 的第 19 層凍結起來當音訊編碼器，只訓練線性投影與 Transformer 解碼器來生成 osu! 譜面。在 standard 模式，event F1 從同架構從頭訓練的 0.323 升到 0.433；但 DDC 的 0.7M 參數 C-LSTM 也有 0.420，真正的增益比標題數字小得多。跨模式聯合訓練讓譜面最少的 mania 從 0.492 升到 0.506，改配 Clone Hero 則降到 0.482。實驗設計乾淨、控制變因做得好，但所有差距都是單次訓練、沒有變異估計。Why this one: The authors freeze layer 19 of MuScriptor, an automatic music transcription (AMT) model, as an audio encoder and train only a linear projection and a Transformer decoder to generate osu! charts. In standard mode, event F1 rises from 0.323 (same architecture trained from scratch) to 0.433; but DDC's 0.7M-parameter C-LSTM already reaches 0.420, so the real gain is much smaller than the headline. Joint training across modes lifts mania, the mode with the fewest charts, from 0.492 to 0.506, while pairing it with Clone Hero drops it to 0.482. The design controls variables well, but every gap comes from a single training run with no variance estimate.

動機Motivation

節奏遊戲的譜面（chart）是一串帶時間的動作：何時按哪個鍵、打鼓面還是鼓邊、在畫面哪裡點。每首歌、每個難度都要人手寫，所以自動生成一直有需求。譜面介於轉譜與生成之間：它的時間點大多對齊聽得到的音符起始（onset），但寫譜者會挑要標哪些聲音、強調什麼、難度怎麼推進，同一首歌可以有很多份都合理的譜面。

作者看到的缺口是：近年的譜面模型開始借用預訓練音訊編碼器（音訊壓縮模型、Whisper、MERT），但沒有人跟從頭訓練的編碼器比過，預訓練到底幫了多少不清楚；而且沒有人用過 AMT 模型，偏偏 AMT 的訓練目標就是找 onset，也就是譜面大部分時間戳的來源。第二個問題是跨遊戲遷移：每個系統都只在一款遊戲上訓練與評估，各用各的指標與容許誤差，在一款遊戲學到的東西能不能搬到另一款，從沒量過。

A rhythm-game chart is a sequence of timed actions: when to press which key, hit the drum face or rim, or click where on screen. Every song and difficulty is charted by hand, so automatic generation has long been wanted. Charts sit between transcription and generation: most timestamps align with audible note onsets, but the charter chooses which sounds to mark, what to emphasize and how difficulty develops, so one song admits many valid charts.

The authors see a gap: recent chart models borrow pre-trained audio encoders (an audio codec, Whisper, MERT), but none compare against an encoder trained from scratch, so how much pre-training helps is unknown; and none use an AMT model, even though AMT is trained to locate onsets — where most chart timestamps come from. The second question is cross-game transfer: every system is trained and evaluated on one game, each with its own metric and tolerance, so whether what is learned in one game carries to another has never been measured.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

譜面生成的起點是 Dance Dance Convolution（DDC，Donahue、Lipton、McAuley，2017；第三作者 Donahue 就是 DDC 的作者）：先用 C-LSTM 偵測 onset，再另一個模型決定每個音符的方向。後續的 TCP 改進類型階段，但類型階段仍然聽不到音訊；TaikoNation 與 AutoOsu 改為一個網路同時預測時間與類型；Yi 等與 Zhang 等把譜面寫成 token 序列。Zhang 等微調 Whisper 或 MERT 並發現 Whisper 較好，但那個比較同時換了架構與預訓練任務；audio2chart 凍結一個神經編解碼器，卻只跟「完全不聽音訊」的模型比。社群系統 Mapperatorinator 用 Whisper 架構在四個 osu! 模式上聯合訓練，但沒有發表評估。

這篇的兩個主張各有最接近的前例。「凍結預訓練表徵、上面只接淺層頭」就是 Castellon、Donahue、Liang（2021）用 Jukebox 表徵做 MIR 探測的範式，這裡把淺層頭換成一個自迴歸解碼器、把 Jukebox 換成 AMT 模型。譜面的 token 化——每段 5 秒、500 個約 10 ms 的時間 token、後接事件類型——則幾乎是 MT3（Gardner 等，2022）把轉譜寫成事件序列的格式，只是把音高與樂器換成遊戲物件。所以真正的新東西是「用 AMT 編碼器並在控制變因下跟從頭訓練比」以及「第一次量跨遊戲遷移」，不是模型結構。

Chart generation begins with Dance Dance Convolution (DDC; Donahue, Lipton and McAuley, 2017 — third author Donahue wrote DDC): a C-LSTM detects onsets, and another model assigns each note's arrow. TCP later improved the type stage, but that stage still never hears the audio; TaikoNation and AutoOsu predict times and types jointly in one network; Yi et al. and Zhang et al. write charts as token sequences. Zhang et al. fine-tune Whisper or MERT and find Whisper stronger, but that comparison changes architecture and pre-training task together; audio2chart freezes a neural codec but compares only against a model that hears no audio. The community system Mapperatorinator trains one Whisper-architecture model across all four osu! modes but has no published evaluation.

Each of the paper's two claims has a close precedent. "Freeze a pre-trained representation and train only a shallow head on top" is the probing paradigm of Castellon, Donahue and Liang (2021) on Jukebox representations, with the shallow head swapped for an autoregressive decoder and Jukebox for an AMT model. The chart tokenization — 5-second segments, 500 time tokens about 10 ms apart, followed by event types — is nearly MT3's (Gardner et al., 2022) event-sequence format for transcription, with pitch and instrument replaced by game objects. What is genuinely new is using an AMT encoder with a controlled comparison against training from scratch, and the first measurement of cross-game transfer, not the model structure.

方法Method

每首歌切成不重疊的 5 秒段（MuScriptor 的輸入長度），每段獨立生成一串 token 再接起來。時間 token 共 500 個，約 10 ms 一格。taiko 每個音符是時間加六種類型之一；mania 是時間、四欄之一、三種類型（單點、長按開始、長按結束），並在段首加一個 token 記錄哪些欄正被按住；standard 把 512×384 的遊戲區量化成 32×24 格，滑條另有曲線類型、2–4 個錨點、拍數與重複次數。詞彙量 509–577，聯合模型取聯集（三個 osu! 模式 606）。

編碼器有三個凍結的：MuScriptor 取第 19/24 層（501 幀 × 1024 維、100 Hz），YourMT3+ 的 PerceiverTF 編碼器（2.048 秒窗口滑動、取中間 55 幀，267 幀 × 3072 維、53 Hz），MERT 第 6/24 層（374 幀 × 1024 維、75 Hz）；層數都用線性 onset 探針挑。

兩個從頭訓練的對照：DDC 的 C-LSTM（0.7M），以及跟 MuScriptor 前 19 層同架構、隨機初始化的 Transformer（約 240M）。凍結編碼器只訓練線性投影加 LayerNorm（0.3M–1.6M）。解碼器是標準 Transformer 解碼器，standard 用 6 層 512 維、taiko 用 4 層 256 維、聯合與 mania 相關模型用 8 層 768 維；難度以 star rating 經零初始化 MLP 加到每個 token 嵌入。

資料是 2019 年 5 月到 2025 年 12 月的 ranked osu! 譜面：standard 2,111 首 / 8,687 份 / 117.9 小時，taiko 1,845 / 7,734 / 92.6，mania 1,955 / 6,268 / 99.5；Clone Hero 取 Expert 主吉他 2,782 首、210.6 小時，以音符密度代替難度。依歌曲 80/10/10 切分。評估在 25 ms 容許誤差下算 onset P/R/F1、要求類型也對的 event F1、配對後的類型準確率，以及參考譜面的 NLL。人類上限用同一首歌兩份人寫譜面互評（相鄰難度與任兩份）。

Each song is cut into non-overlapping 5-second segments (MuScriptor's input length); each segment is generated independently as a token sequence and the results are concatenated. There are 500 time tokens about 10 ms apart. Taiko encodes a note as a time plus one of six types; mania as a time, one of four columns and one of three types (tap, hold start, hold end), with a leading token for columns already held; standard quantizes the 512×384 playfield to a 32×24 grid, and sliders add a curve type, 2–4 anchors, a length in beats and a repeat count. Vocabularies hold 509–577 tokens; joint models take the union (606 for the three osu! modes).

Three frozen encoders: MuScriptor layer 19 of 24 (501 frames × 1024 dims, 100 Hz), YourMT3+'s PerceiverTF encoder (a 2.048 s window slid with the middle 55 frames kept, giving 267 frames × 3072 dims, 53 Hz), and MERT layer 6 of 24 (374 frames × 1024 dims, 75 Hz); layers are chosen with a linear onset probe.

Two from-scratch controls: DDC's C-LSTM (0.7M) and a randomly initialized Transformer matching MuScriptor's first 19 layers (about 240M). With frozen encoders only a linear projection plus LayerNorm is trained (0.3M–1.6M). The decoder is a standard Transformer decoder: 6 layers at 512 dims for standard, 4 at 256 for taiko, 8 at 768 for joint and mania-related models; difficulty enters as the star rating through a zero-initialized MLP added to every token embedding.

Data are ranked osu! charts from May 2019 to December 2025: standard 2,111 songs / 8,687 charts / 117.9 hours, taiko 1,845 / 7,734 / 92.6, mania 1,955 / 6,268 / 99.5; Clone Hero uses the Expert lead guitar of 2,782 songs, 210.6 hours, conditioned on note density instead of difficulty. Splits are 80/10/10 by song. Evaluation reports onset P/R/F1 at 25 ms, event F1 (type must also match), type accuracy over matched onsets, and NLL of the reference chart. A human reference scores pairs of human charts of the same song (adjacent difficulty, and any two).

限制Limitations

標題級的數字「0.323 → 0.433」比的是一個約 240M 參數、在 90–120 小時資料上從頭訓練的 Transformer，這個對照本身就很可能訓練不足或過擬合。比較公平的對照是同樣從頭訓練的 0.7M C-LSTM：standard 上 event F1 0.420 對 MuScriptor 0.433，onset F1 0.654 對 0.657；taiko 上 event F1 0.391 對 0.403，onset F1 0.743 對 0.746。相對於一個為這個任務設計的小模型，凍結 AMT 編碼器的增益大約是 event F1 +0.01、onset F1 幾乎為零。論文有寫「MuScriptor 跟 C-LSTM 在 onset F1 上相當」，但摘要與引言強調的是 0.323 那一組。

「轉譜目標讓類型更準」這個結論靠的差距更小。類型準確率 MuScriptor 對 MERT：standard 0.653 對 0.639，taiko 0.538 對 0.534；taiko 的 event F1 是 0.403 對 0.402，onset F1 MERT 反而較高（0.751 對 0.746）。跨模式的 +0.014（0.492 → 0.506）與 Clone Hero 的 −0.010（降到 0.482）也一樣。全部都是單一種子、單次生成，沒有標準差也沒有顯著性檢定，這種量級的差距在重新訓練時很可能翻轉。

評估本身也有盲點。event F1 不算 mania 的欄位、也不算 standard 的位置，所以 standard 上滑條、座標這些最難的部分，只有 NLL 間接反映。每段 5 秒獨立生成，段與段之間沒有上下文，長按跨段、樂句結構與難度推進都學不到。跨遊戲那段關於「寫譜慣例與曲風不同」的解釋，作者自己說是推測：69% 的 Clone Hero 歌曲是金屬與搖滾、70% 的 mania 歌曲是電子、動畫與遊戲音樂，但沒有任何實驗把這兩個因素拆開。

The headline "0.323 → 0.433" compares against a roughly 240M-parameter Transformer trained from scratch on 90–120 hours of data, a control that is itself likely undertrained or overfit. A fairer control is the 0.7M C-LSTM, also trained from scratch: on standard, event F1 is 0.420 vs MuScriptor's 0.433 and onset F1 0.654 vs 0.657; on taiko, event F1 0.391 vs 0.403 and onset F1 0.743 vs 0.746. Against a small task-designed model, the frozen AMT encoder gains about +0.01 event F1 and almost nothing on onset F1. The paper does say MuScriptor is "comparable on onset F1" to the C-LSTM, but the abstract and introduction lead with the 0.323 comparison.

The claim that the transcription objective improves types rests on even smaller gaps. Type accuracy, MuScriptor vs MERT: 0.653 vs 0.639 on standard, 0.538 vs 0.534 on taiko; taiko event F1 is 0.403 vs 0.402, and MERT's onset F1 is actually higher (0.751 vs 0.746). The same applies to the cross-mode +0.014 (0.492 → 0.506) and the Clone Hero −0.010 (down to 0.482). Everything is a single seed and a single generation, with no standard deviations or significance tests; gaps this size could easily flip on retraining.

The evaluation also has blind spots. Event F1 does not score mania columns or standard positions, so the hardest parts of standard — sliders and coordinates — show up only indirectly through NLL. Each 5-second segment is generated independently with no context across segments, so holds spanning segments, phrase structure and difficulty progression cannot be learned. The explanation for cross-game transfer — differing charting conventions and genres — is, by the authors' own word, speculation: 69% of Clone Hero songs are metal and rock and 70% of mania songs are electronic, anime and video-game music, but no experiment separates the two factors.

討論Discussion

撇開數字大小，這篇在實驗設計上做對了一件前人沒做的事：解碼器、資料、指標全部固定，只換編碼器，並且同時放一個「同架構從頭訓練」與一個「為任務設計的小模型」當對照。正因為兩個對照都在，讀者才看得出 0.323 那組其實是被大模型拖下來的，而真正可信的結論是「預訓練表徵至少不輸任務專用的小模型，而且可以凍結、只訓練 0.3M–1.6M 參數」——對資料少的新遊戲，這個省事程度本身就有價值。

人類參考值也值得看。standard 上兩份相鄰難度人類譜面的 event F1 是 0.549，任兩份是 0.456；模型 0.433 已經接近「任兩份人類譜面」的一致程度。不過模型是以參考譜面的 star rating 為條件生成，人類對照則是拿較簡單的那份當生成結果，兩者條件不同，不能直接說「接近人類」。

跨遊戲的負結果反而是這篇最有意思的地方：版面相似（都是分軌）的 Clone Hero 拖累了 mania，版面不同（自由座標、單軌鼓）的另兩個 osu! 模式卻有幫助，而且增益集中在三個模式共有的單點音符（0.599 → 0.612），長按反而下降。這指向「譜面跟的是什麼」比「物件長得像不像」重要。作者提的反向應用——把譜面當 onset 標註拿來訓練 AMT——也值得追：osu! 有數千份帶難度標籤的人類 onset 標註，這在轉譜資料裡是稀缺品。

Setting magnitudes aside, the design does something prior work did not: fix the decoder, data and metrics, swap only the encoder, and include both a same-architecture from-scratch control and a small task-designed model. Because both controls are present, a reader can see that the 0.323 result is dragged down by the large model, and that the trustworthy conclusion is "a pre-trained representation is at least as good as a task-specific small model, can be frozen, and needs only 0.3M–1.6M trained parameters" — for a new game with little data, that convenience is itself valuable.

The human reference is worth a look. On standard, two human charts at adjacent difficulty agree at 0.549 event F1, and any two at 0.456; the model's 0.433 is close to the any-two-charts level. But the model is conditioned on the reference chart's star rating, while the human comparison uses the easier chart as the stand-in, so the conditions differ and "close to human" cannot be read off directly.

The cross-game negative result is the most interesting part: Clone Hero, similar in layout (both lane-based), hurt mania, while the other two osu! modes, different in layout (free coordinates, single-lane drums), helped — and the gain concentrates on the tap note all three modes share (0.599 → 0.612), while holds fall. That points to what a chart follows mattering more than whether objects look alike. The reverse application the authors propose — using charts as onset labels to train AMT — is also worth pursuing: osu! offers thousands of difficulty-labelled human onset annotations, which are scarce in transcription data.

From Volpiano to Pixels: 30,000 Lines of OMR Ground Truth for Medieval Chant

從 Volpiano 到像素：把 Cantus Database 的既有轉寫對齊到中世紀聖詠手稿影像，產出三萬行光學樂譜辨識訓練資料

abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：不重新標註，而是把 Cantus Database 已有的 Volpiano 旋律轉寫，對齊到十部 12–15 世紀手稿的 IIIF 影像上，推斷譜號、把絕對音高換成譜線位置，產出 30,567 組行影像–目標序列（1,952,143 個 token）。從頭訓練的 CRNN 在 3,086 行測試集上符號錯誤率 9.6%，留一部手稿不訓練時是 21.5–31.0%。值得讀的是它對自己的誠實：各來源的抽查定義不同、測試集有一個 folio 洩漏、與舊模型的比較混入詞彙不相容，這些作者全都寫出來了。Why this one: Instead of re-annotating, the author aligns existing Volpiano melody transcriptions from the Cantus Database to IIIF images of ten 12th–15th-century manuscripts, infers clefs and converts absolute pitches into staff positions, yielding 30,567 line-image/target pairs (1,952,143 tokens). A CRNN trained from scratch reaches a 9.6% symbol error rate on 3,086 test lines, and 21.5–31.0% on a manuscript held out entirely. It is worth reading for its candour: per-source audits use different error definitions, one folio leaks between train and test, and the comparison with the older model is confounded by vocabulary mismatch — all stated by the author.

動機Motivation

中世紀聖詠的光學樂譜辨識（optical music recognition, OMR）缺的不是模型，而是把手稿影像跟音樂符號配對起來的訓練資料，而且要涵蓋不同的記譜家族——方形記譜、熙篤會記譜、德國哥德式記譜、早期法國與梅斯譜線記譜各長得很不一樣。手工標註很貴，作者之前親手為奧地利 15–16 世紀手稿做的標註，訓練出的 CRNN 在其他來源上幾乎讀不出東西。

另一方面，Cantus Database 已經累積了大量用 Volpiano 字型寫成的旋律轉寫：作者手上 2026 年 2 月 16 日的快照裡，有 57,085 首附影像連結的聖詠帶 Volpiano。這些是學者對著手稿逐音轉寫的，旋律本身大致可信，缺的只是「這串音在影像的哪一行、用的是哪個譜號」。這篇的出發點就是：與其重新標，不如把這批既有轉寫對齊回影像上。

What optical music recognition (OMR) for medieval chant lacks is not models but training data pairing manuscript images with musical symbols, across notational families — square notation, Cistercian, German Gothic, early French and Messine staff notation all look very different. Hand annotation is expensive; the CRNN trained on the author's own hand-made annotations of 15th–16th-century Austrian manuscripts reads almost nothing from other sources.

Meanwhile, the Cantus Database has accumulated many melody transcriptions in the Volpiano font: the author's snapshot of 16 February 2026 holds Volpiano for 57,085 chants with image links. Scholars transcribed these note by note from the manuscripts, so the melodies are broadly trustworthy; what is missing is which image line each string belongs to and which clef it uses. The premise is that, rather than annotating again, one can align these existing transcriptions back to the images.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最接近的既有資源是阿利坎特大學 PRAIG 團隊的 AMNLT（Fuentes-Martínez 等，2026）：三個真實手稿語料，Einsiedeln、Salzinnes、Solesmes 各 1,816、2,965、854 個譜表片段，另有 GregoSynth 以 GregorioTeX 從真實 GABC 轉寫渲染出的 126,579 條合成譜表。這篇的資料量是 AMNLT 真實部分的五倍多，而且涵蓋 AMNLT 沒有的記譜家族；代價是標註品質不是逐行人工確認。模型端用的 CRNN 加 CTC，則是 Calvo-Zaragoza 與 Rizo 在 PrIMuS（2018）提出的單聲部端到端 OMR 架構，這篇沒有改模型。

方法上真正的祖先，作者自己也點明了：Transkribus 的 Text2Image。那是手寫文字辨識（HTR）領域的做法——先用一個粗糙的 HTR 模型讀頁面，再用它當錨點把既有的全文轉寫對齊到行影像上，產生新的訓練資料。這篇就是把 Text2Image 搬到音樂：用舊的記譜模型當錨點對齊 Volpiano。差別在於音樂多了文字沒有的難題——譜號。Volpiano 存的是絕對音高，影像上看到的是相對譜線位置，兩者要靠譜號換算，而 Volpiano 根本不記錄譜號，也不記錄聖詠中途的換譜號。這篇大部分的工程都花在推斷譜號上。

The closest existing resource is AMNLT from the University of Alicante's PRAIG group (Fuentes-Martínez et al., 2026): three real-manuscript corpora — Einsiedeln, Salzinnes and Solesmes, with 1,816, 2,965 and 854 staff excerpts — plus GregoSynth, 126,579 synthetic staves rendered by GregorioTeX from real GABC transcriptions. This paper's corpus is more than five times AMNLT's real-image portion and covers notational families AMNLT lacks; the cost is that labels are not checked line by line by hand. On the model side, the CRNN with CTC is the end-to-end monophonic OMR architecture Calvo-Zaragoza and Rizo introduced with PrIMuS (2018); the paper does not change the model.

The real methodological ancestor, as the author says, is Transkribus Text2Image. In handwritten text recognition (HTR), a rough HTR model reads a page and serves as an anchor to align an existing full-text transcription to line images, producing new training data. This paper moves Text2Image to music, using an older notation model as the anchor for aligning Volpiano. The difference is a problem text does not have — clefs. Volpiano stores absolute pitch, the image shows relative staff positions, the two are related through the clef, and Volpiano records neither the clef nor clef changes inside a chant. Most of the engineering goes into inferring clefs.

方法Method

目標編碼：Volpiano 的絕對音高（c = C3、k = C4）換成從最底線算起的譜線位置 token（l1 z1 l2 … l5，l 是線、z 是線間），加上 c1…c5、f1…f4 及 f2/c4 這類多字母譜號 token、分隔符與小節線。換算用半線計數 q = index(音高) − index(譜號音高) + 2(L−1)；用七個參考譜號把快照裡全部 61,244 條非空 Volpiano 字串來回轉換一遍，確認無損。輸出時液化音（liquescence）會被省略。

流程每部手稿四步：統計 Volpiano 與換行符 7 的覆蓋率、經 IIIF 下載影像並縮放到寬 1,600 px、偵測譜表系統、輸出 gt.txt 與附信心值的 meta.tsv。偵測有六種家族：可被舊模型讀的劃線譜表用梳狀濾波與格線擬合；紅色 F 線用文字錨點與譜距模型雙重確認行號；譜線淡的以文字行為錨點；有彩色 F/C 線的改用歌詞對齊（A-KN 1018 與 1012 用 HTR 讀頁面文字再比對資料庫全文）；褐色譜線則靠行首的 F/C/D/E/G 字母決定音高。啟發式只抓到約 52% 人工標記的系統，於是用 23 頁上 235 個手繪系統訓練一個約 0.5M 參數的 U-Net，在保留資料上 124 個系統找到 118 個（誤差 0.3 個譜距內）；另一個 U-Net 用 31 頁學行首譜號字母。

對齊用動態規劃把一頁的 Volpiano 行依序對到偵測到的系統，允許一對一、兩行併一系統、一行拆兩系統與跳過；配對成本是正規化對齊成本 A，併/拆是 1.5A + 0.3，跳過 0.8。譜號推斷在 c1…c4、f2…f4 七個譜號間找總成本最低的序列：超出音域每半線 0.1、在紐姆內換譜號禁止、在紐姆分隔處換 2.0、在音節或詞分隔處 1.5、在新聖詠起點 0.3。專家先填版面問卷，再用三個瀏覽器介面畫譜線、逐頁點選審核、只看新變動的行；像「第 21 個音後換成 f3」這種文字註記會被直接解析成修正。最後在輸出的資料上從頭訓練 CRNN + CTC，並用它重新對齊被擋下的行，A ≤ 0.12 才收。

Target encoding: Volpiano's absolute pitches (c = C3, k = C4) become staff-position tokens counted from the bottom line (l1 z1 l2 … l5; l for line, z for space), plus clef tokens c1…c5, f1…f4 and multi-letter forms such as f2/c4, separators and bars. Conversion uses a half-line counter q = index(pitch) − index(clef pitch) + 2(L−1); a round-trip test with seven reference clefs converts all 61,244 non-empty Volpiano strings in the snapshot and back, confirming it is lossless. Liquescence is dropped on export.

Each source goes through four steps: count Volpiano and line-break-7 coverage, download IIIF images and resize to 1,600 px wide, detect staff systems, and export gt.txt plus a meta.tsv with confidence information. Six detector families exist: ruled staves the old model can read use a comb filter and page grid; red F lines get their line number confirmed by both a text anchor and a staff-spacing model; faint staves are anchored on the text line; coloured F/C lines switch to lyrics alignment (for A-KN 1018 and 1012, HTR reads the page text and matches it to the database full text); brown staves rely on F/C/D/E/G letters at the line start for pitch. Heuristics found only about 52% of hand-marked systems, so a U-Net of about 0.5M parameters was trained on 235 hand-drawn systems from 23 pages and located 118 of 124 held-out systems within 0.3 staff spaces; a second U-Net learned marginal clef letters from 31 pages.

Alignment uses dynamic programming to map a page's Volpiano lines in order onto detected systems, allowing match, merge two lines into one system, split one line across two, and skip; a match costs the normalized alignment cost A, merge or split 1.5A + 0.3, skip 0.8. Clef inference searches seven clefs (c1…c4, f2…f4) for the lowest-cost sequence: 0.1 per half-line beyond range, clef change inside a neume forbidden, 2.0 at a neume separator, 1.5 at a syllable or word separator, 0.3 at a new-chant marker. The expert first fills in a layout questionnaire, then uses three browser interfaces to draw staff lines, review pages by clicking systems, and review only new or changed lines; notes like "clef change to f3 after 21 notes" are parsed and applied directly. Finally a CRNN with CTC is trained from scratch on the export and used to re-align withheld lines, accepting only those with A ≤ 0.12.

限制Limitations

作者自己列了大部分限制，而且列得很具體。第一，品質抽查沒有統一定義：附加部分只算音高錯，巴黎手稿算音高加譜號書寫形式，A-KN 1010 算詩節分配，巴黎與 Klosterneuburg 的抽查還只涵蓋通過輸出規則的行。表 2 那些 0/34、1/202、3/85 的 Wilson 上界（2.6%–14.5%）因此不能拼成全語料的錯誤率。唯一比較像全域估計的是跟 AMNLT 在 CH-E 611 上的比對：1,519 個系統中 357 個不同，抽 40 個由專家判了 39 個，AMNLT 錯 26、這篇錯 10、兩者都錯 3，推得這篇約 7.8% 錯誤率（區間 4.8–11.5%）——前提是兩邊一致的就是對的，共同錯誤會被漏掉。

第二，評估有洩漏與混淆。原本要按 folio 切分，但一個解析錯誤讓 A-KN 1010 某個 folio 的片段同時出現在訓練與測試集，報告的 9.6% 用的仍是這個舊索引。跟舊模型 45.3% 的比較，作者也承認舊模型根本輸出不了全部目標 token，所以這個差距有一部分反映的是「詞彙相容性」而不只是辨識能力。重新訓練、按 folio 切乾淨之後 SER 是 9.56%，跟初始模型一樣，這個結果倒是排除了洩漏造成大幅灌水。

第三，資料有選擇偏誤：只收流程能解出來的行，淡色頁面與少見譜號形式因此偏少；好幾條規則是在同一批受審資料上調出來的。目標序列只有譜線位置的順序，沒有每個符頭的座標；Volpiano 本身不記中途換譜號，自動偵測換譜號仍未完成——第一輪 23 頁裡，18 個系統有 6 個的換譜號是對齊憑空推出來的。

另外有一個 metadata 上的怪事：arXiv 註記寫 36 頁、41 張表，但 v1 的 PDF 只有 9 頁、正文兩張表，註記很可能是指沒有上傳的附錄。

The author lists most limitations, and concretely. First, quality audits share no common definition: the addendum counts pitch errors only, the Paris manuscript counts pitches plus written clef forms, A-KN 1010 counts verse assignments, and the Paris and Klosterneuburg audits cover only lines that passed the export rules. The Wilson upper bounds in Table 2 for counts like 0/34, 1/202 and 3/85 (2.6%–14.5%) therefore cannot be combined into a corpus-wide error rate. The only quasi-global estimate is the AMNLT comparison on CH-E 611: of 1,519 systems, 357 differ; of 40 sampled, the expert judged 39 — AMNLT wrong in 26, this corpus in 10, both in 3 — giving an estimated 7.8% error (interval 4.8–11.5%), assuming agreeing systems are correct, so shared errors are missed.

Second, evaluation has leakage and confounds. The split was meant to be by folio, but a parsing error put fragments of one A-KN 1010 folio in both train and test, and the reported 9.6% still uses that index. For the comparison with the old model's 45.3%, the author concedes the old model cannot output all target tokens, so part of the gap reflects vocabulary compatibility rather than recognition alone. After retraining with a clean per-folio split, SER is 9.56%, equal to the initial model, which at least rules out large inflation from the leak.

Third, there is selection bias: only lines the pipeline could resolve are included, so faint pages and rare clef forms are under-represented, and several rules were tuned on the same material used for review. Targets give only the order of staff positions, without notehead coordinates; Volpiano does not encode mid-chant clef changes, and automatic detection of them is unfinished — in the first round of 23 pages, 6 of 18 systems had changes inferred from alignment alone with no support.

One metadata oddity: the arXiv comment says 36 pages and 41 tables, but the v1 PDF has 9 pages and two tables in the body; the comment likely refers to appendices that were not uploaded.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值在資料與流程，不在模型。最實用的數字是留一法的結果：完全沒見過的 A-KN 1012、D-KNd 1161、F-Pn 12044，SER 分別是 21.5%、31.0%、24.8%，舊模型則是 56–81%。這個量級還不能直接拿來自動轉寫，但已經夠當下一部手稿的對齊錨點——而這正是流程需要的。作者也真的這樣用了：新模型重新對齊被擋下的行，在三部手稿多收了 2,686 行，每部抽 50 行總共只找到 1 個音高錯。這是一個會自我強化的迴圈：資料越多、模型越好、能對齊的手稿越多。

「教訓」那一節對做類似工作的人很有用。區間編輯距離（interval NED）對整個譜表上下平移一線完全不敏感，因為音程不變，所以不能只用它宣稱品質；換譜號會躲過審查，因為一行一個譜號的輸出看起來完全正常。重新訓練後行首譜號 token 的正確率在 A-KN 1012 從 43% 升到 90%，但 CH-P 18 從 25% 掉到 9%，單字母譜號反而變差，說明資料擴充不是單調地讓每件事都變好。

最後一節值得一提：作者明白寫出這是由一位領域專家與 AI 程式助理（Claude Code）合作完成的，約 300 次 commit、29 個 Python 檔，助理大致自主地篩選來源、實作偵測器、訓練 U-Net 與 CRNN，專家負責古文書判讀、門檻與規則決定、以及最終是否接受標註。單一作者、大量 AI 實作、加上作者自己承認的切分錯誤，讓「獨立重現」比平常更重要；資料若照計畫釋出，最該先做的就是在乾淨切分上重跑一次所有數字。

The paper's value lies in the data and pipeline, not the model. The most useful numbers are the leave-one-out results: on the unseen A-KN 1012, D-KNd 1161 and F-Pn 12044, SER is 21.5%, 31.0% and 24.8%, versus 56–81% for the old model. That is not yet good enough for automatic transcription, but it is good enough as an alignment anchor for the next manuscript — which is what the pipeline needs. The author does exactly this: the new model re-aligns withheld lines and recovers 2,686 more across three manuscripts, with only one pitch error found in samples of 50 lines per source. It is a self-reinforcing loop: more data, a better model, more manuscripts that can be aligned.

The lessons section is useful for anyone doing similar work. Interval edit distance (interval NED) is blind to the whole staff shifting by one line, because intervals are unchanged, so it cannot support a quality claim alone; clef changes slip through review, because a one-clef-per-line export looks perfectly normal. After retraining, correct initial clef tokens rise from 43% to 90% for A-KN 1012 but fall from 25% to 9% for CH-P 18, and single-letter clefs get worse, showing that more data does not improve everything monotonically.

The final section deserves mention: the author states plainly that the work was done by a domain expert with an AI coding assistant (Claude Code), about 300 commits and 29 Python files, with the assistant largely autonomously screening sources, implementing detectors and training the U-Nets and CRNN, and the expert supplying paleographic readings, deciding thresholds and rules, and accepting the labels. A single author, extensive AI implementation and an admitted split error make independent reproduction more important than usual; if the data are released as planned, the first thing to do is rerun all the numbers on a clean split.
