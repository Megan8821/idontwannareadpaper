PianoBench: Evaluating Musical Perception and Reasoning in Language Models

PianoBench：從 2 萬小時鋼琴教學影片抽出「講解＋示範」交錯資料，分開測語言模型聽得出來與推得出來的音樂能力

Louis Bradshaw, Irmak Bukey, Daniel Chenyu Lin, John Thickstun, Chris Donahue
arXiv:2611.03839 · cs.SD; cs.AI; cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-11-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：作者從 YouTube 鋼琴教學影片做出 PianoTalk（138,212 支影片、超過 2 萬小時的講解文字與轉譜 MIDI 交錯排列），再從測試集挑出 250 題人工確認的選擇題，同一段音樂分別以音訊和 MIDI 文字提供。開源音訊語言模型比「不給音樂」的對照最多只多 6.0 個百分點；GPT 6 Astra 讀 MIDI 文字並開推理可到 93.2%，最好的音訊成績是 Gemini 3.1 Pro 的 80.4%。選它是因為「不給音樂」對照組本身就有 41.2% 到 63.6%，遠高於 25% 的亂猜，論文把這個數字擺出來，讓所有分數都改用相對增益解讀。另外，用交錯文件續訓 Qwen 3.5 9B Base，不開推理時從 42.8% 升到 56.0%。Why this one: The authors build PianoTalk from YouTube piano tutorials (138,212 videos, more than 20,000 hours of commentary text interleaved with transcribed MIDI), then draw 250 human-verified multiple-choice questions from its test split, each passage given either as audio or as MIDI text. Open-weight audio-language models gain at most 6.0 percentage points over a no-music control; GPT 6 Astra reading MIDI text with reasoning reaches 93.2%, while the best audio score is Gemini 3.1 Pro at 80.4%. I picked it because the no-music control itself scores 41.2% to 63.6%, far above 25% chance, and the paper puts that number up front so every score is read as a gain over it. Separately, continued pretraining of Qwen 3.5 9B Base on interleaved documents lifts accuracy without reasoning from 42.8% to 56.0%.

動機Motivation

音訊語言模型在 MMAU、MuChoMusic 這類綜合音樂理解基準上的分數持續上升，但這些分數說不清楚模型到底「聽到」了什麼。它們的訓練資料與評測題目多半來自整首曲子層級的標註，例如描述文字與標籤，很少要求模型指出某個動機、某個和弦進行或某段伴奏型態。作者想把兩種能力拆開：從聲音裡辨認出音樂特徵的感知能力，以及在音符已經明確給出時推論出音樂概念的推理能力。

他們找到的資料來源是鋼琴教學影片。老師先講一個概念、在琴上示範、再回頭評論剛剛彈的東西，講解和音樂在時間上緊鄰，這是一般描述文字給不了的局部對齊監督。

Audio-language models keep improving on broad music-understanding benchmarks such as MMAU and MuChoMusic, but those scores do not say what the models actually hear. Much of their training data and many evaluation questions come from track-level annotations such as captions and tags, which rarely ask a model to point to a motif, a chord progression or an accompaniment pattern. The authors want to separate two abilities: perceiving musical features from sound, and reasoning to musical concepts once the notes are given explicitly.

Their data source is piano tutorial videos. A teacher introduces an idea, demonstrates it at the keyboard and comments on what was just played, so the explanation sits right next to the music in time. That is locally aligned supervision that ordinary captions do not provide.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

評測這一側，最接近的前作是 MuChoMusic（Weck 等，2024）：從人寫的音樂描述自動產生選擇題再由人確認。RUListening（Zang 等，2025）指出 MuChoMusic 的題目與選項本身就洩漏答案，提出用「不給音訊時的作答機率」挑選干擾選項。PianoBench 的干擾選項程序直接沿用 RUListening 的想法，「不給音樂」對照組也是從那裡來的。差別在於題目來源是教學講解而不是描述文字，而且同一段音樂同時有音訊和轉譜兩種版本，可以直接比較聽與讀。MusicTheoryBench（Yuan 等，2024）用 ABC 記譜測樂理推理，但沒有對應的音訊。

訓練資料這一側，Audio Flamingo 3 與 Music Flamingo 的作法是讓語言模型把錄音、描述與擷取出的音樂屬性改寫成問答對；ChatMusician 則在 ABC 記譜與音樂相關文字上續訓。PianoTalk 不產生問答，而是把原始的講解與示範依時間順序排成一份文件，讓模型做一般的自監督語言模型訓練。

論文沒有提到的最近親是影像領域的 HowTo100M（Miech 等，2019）：同樣利用教學影片中旁白與畫面在時間上的對齊，做大規模弱監督。PianoTalk 等於把這個想法搬到音樂，再加上源分離與自動轉譜，讓「畫面」變成可以直接餵給文字模型的 MIDI 文字。

On the evaluation side, the closest prior work is MuChoMusic (Weck et al., 2024), which generates multiple-choice questions automatically from human-written captions and has humans verify them. RUListening (Zang et al., 2025) showed that MuChoMusic's questions and options leak their answers, and proposed choosing distractors by the probability a model assigns without hearing the audio. PianoBench's distractor procedure takes that idea directly from RUListening, and so does its no-music control. The difference is that questions come from teaching commentary rather than captions, and each passage exists as both audio and transcript, so listening and reading can be compared directly. MusicTheoryBench (Yuan et al., 2024) tests music-theory reasoning in ABC notation but has no matching audio.

On the training-data side, Audio Flamingo 3 and Music Flamingo have language models rewrite recordings, captions and extracted musical attributes into question-answer pairs; ChatMusician continues training on ABC notation and music-related text. PianoTalk generates no QA pairs. It lays the original commentary and demonstrations out in time order as one document for ordinary self-supervised language-model training.

The nearest relative the paper does not mention is HowTo100M (Miech et al., 2019) in vision, which used the same temporal alignment between narration and frames in instructional videos for large-scale weak supervision. PianoTalk carries that idea to music, adding source separation and automatic transcription so that the frames become MIDI text a text model can read.

方法Method

資料管線。 從少數人工挑選的影片出發，讓 Gemma 4 26B-A4B 讀標題與說明判斷是否同時有講解與獨奏鋼琴，再沿相關影片與頻道擴張。字幕少於 100 字的影片丟掉，用 Bradshaw 與 Colton（2025）的鋼琴分類器保留平均分數至少 0.3 的錄音，以 MVSEP 分出鋼琴音軌，再用同一團隊的轉譜模型轉成 MIDI，語音則用 Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 補充字幕。結果是 138,212 支影片：語音區段 17,706 小時、鋼琴區段 14,931 小時，其中 6,571 小時兩者重疊，字幕涵蓋 44 種語言，英文占 68.48%。同一頻道的影片全部放在同一個切分。

MIDI 文字。 每個音符寫成「音高、力度、起音、時值」四個數字，時間以 10 毫秒為單位，每 5 秒插一個 advance 標記並把起音重新歸零，讓數字保持在小範圍內，現成的文字分詞器就能處理。

Data pipeline. Starting from a few hand-picked videos, Gemma 4 26B-A4B reads titles and descriptions to judge whether a video contains both commentary and solo piano, and the crawl expands through related videos and channels. Videos with fewer than 100 caption words are dropped; the piano classifier of Bradshaw and Colton (2025) keeps recordings with a mean score of at least 0.3; MVSEP separates the piano stem; the same group's transcription model converts it to MIDI; and Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 supplements the captions. The result is 138,212 videos: 17,706 hours of speech intervals and 14,931 hours of piano intervals, 6,571 of them overlapping, with captions in 44 languages, 68.48% English. All videos of one channel go to the same split.

MIDI text. Each note is written as four numbers (pitch, velocity, onset, duration) in 10 ms units, with an advance marker every 5 seconds that resets onsets, so the numbers stay small and ordinary text tokenizers can handle them.

限制Limitations

樣本小，差距的不確定性沒有報。 250 題的情況下一題就是 0.4 個百分點，在 50% 附近的二項分布 95% 信賴區間大約是正負 6 個百分點。論文的關鍵比較，例如交錯續訓 56.0% 對官方指令版 50.0% 的 6.0 點差距，等於 15 題，而表上沒有任何信賴區間或配對檢定，續訓也只跑一次。開源模型「最多只多 6.0 點」這個結論，同樣落在這個雜訊範圍附近。

「推理」條件其實含有感知。 MIDI 文字是專用轉譜模型從同一段錄音轉出來的，所以「讀 MIDI 比聽音訊好」比較的是「通用音訊編碼器」與「專用轉譜器加文字推理」，不是純粹的感知與推理。論文也承認 GPT 6 Astra 答錯的 17 題中有 4 題是轉譜錯誤。

出題者與受測者重疊。 題目由 GPT 5.6 Sol 產生、干擾選項由 GPT 5.6 Terra 產生、Gemma 4 12B 打分，這三個模型都在受測名單上；人工確認只有一位具音樂訓練的作者。論文報告了它們的對照組分數落在同類範圍內，但沒有更直接的檢查。

The sample is small and gaps carry no reported uncertainty. With 250 questions, one question is 0.4 percentage points, and a binomial 95% interval near 50% is roughly plus or minus 6 points. A key comparison such as interleaved continued pretraining at 56.0% against the official instruct model at 50.0% is a 6.0-point gap, or 15 questions, and the tables give no confidence intervals or paired tests; the continued pretraining is also a single run. The conclusion that open-weight models gain "at most 6.0 points" sits near the same noise range.

The reasoning condition contains perception. MIDI text is produced from the same recording by a dedicated transcription model, so "reading MIDI beats listening" compares a general audio encoder against a specialist transcriber plus text reasoning, not pure perception against pure reasoning. The paper itself reports that 4 of GPT 6 Astra's 17 errors are transcription errors.

Question writers overlap with test-takers. GPT 5.6 Sol wrote the questions, GPT 5.6 Terra the distractors and Gemma 4 12B scored them, and all three are evaluated; human verification was a single author with musical training. The paper reports that their control scores fall within their groups' range, but offers no more direct check.

討論Discussion

音訊結果。 有「不給音樂」對照的開源音訊語言模型中，加上音訊後準確率的變化只在負 1.6 到正 6.0 個百分點之間。專有模型全部有進步，Gemini 3.1 Pro 在分離出的鋼琴音軌上拿到 80.4%，比自己的對照高 16.8 點，Qwen 3.5 Omni Plus 高 11.2 點。用 Pianoteq 重新合成的音訊與原始分離音軌的成績大致相同，表示分離殘留不是主要瓶頸。

MIDI 文字與推理。 在 11 個受益於音樂輸入的音訊語言模型中，有 8 個在 MIDI 文字條件下分數最高。GPT 5.6 Sol 不開推理是 70.4%，推理強度調到中、高、極高時分別是 85.6%、87.2%、88.0%。但對 Qwen 3 Omni 與 Gemma 4 12B 開啟推理，音訊條件並沒有變好，說明推理補不了聽不出來的部分。

交錯續訓。 作者以三種文件續訓 Qwen 3.5 9B Base：只有講解、講解與 MIDI 分開成兩份文件、兩者依時間交錯，三者都接同樣的一般指令微調（4 萬筆 Tulu 3 加 1 萬筆 MMLU 選擇題），不含任何 MIDI 問答。不開推理時，交錯版拿到 56.0%，比對照高 10.4 點；分開版 42.4%、增益 0.0，只有講解的版本增益 0.4，只做指令微調的基線是 42.8%。分開版和交錯版用的是完全相同的片段，token 數也幾乎一樣（2,988 百萬對 2,987 百萬），所以差異只來自排列順序，這是全篇設計最乾淨的一組對照。不過官方指令版開推理是 70.0%，續訓的效果仍然比不上推理。

對 MIR 來說，最實際的訊息是：最強的文字模型讀轉譜已經能回答大部分局部音樂問題，因此可以反過來用「轉譜加大模型」替錄音產生細粒度問答，作為音訊模型的訓練資料。這條路是否可行，取決於前面提到的轉譜錯誤率。

Audio results. Among open-weight audio-language models with a no-music control, adding audio changes accuracy by only -1.6 to +6.0 percentage points. Every proprietary model improves: Gemini 3.1 Pro reaches 80.4% on the separated piano stem, 16.8 points above its own control, and Qwen 3.5 Omni Plus gains 11.2 points. Audio re-rendered with Pianoteq scores about the same as the original separated stem, so separation artifacts are not the main bottleneck.

MIDI text and reasoning. Of the 11 audio-language models that benefit from musical input, 8 score highest with MIDI text. GPT 5.6 Sol gets 70.4% without reasoning and 85.6%, 87.2% and 88.0% at medium, high and extra-high effort. Turning on reasoning in Qwen 3 Omni and Gemma 4 12B does not help the audio conditions, so reasoning does not make up for what the model cannot hear.

Interleaved continued pretraining. The authors continue pretraining Qwen 3.5 9B Base on three document sets: commentary only, commentary and MIDI as separate documents, and the two interleaved in time order. All three get the same general instruction tuning (40k Tulu 3 examples plus 10k MMLU multiple-choice questions) with no MIDI QA. Without reasoning, the interleaved version reaches 56.0%, 10.4 points above its control; the separated version gets 42.4% with a gain of 0.0, commentary only gains 0.4, and the instruction-tuning-only baseline is 42.8%. Separated and interleaved use exactly the same chunks and nearly the same token count (2,988 million against 2,987 million), so the difference comes only from ordering, the cleanest contrast in the paper. Still, the official instruct model with reasoning reaches 70.0%, so continued pretraining does not match reasoning.

For MIR, the practical message is that the strongest text models reading transcripts can already answer most local musical questions, so "transcription plus a large model" could generate fine-grained QA about recordings as training data for audio models. Whether that works depends on the transcription error rate noted above.

轉譜與音高分析Transcription & Pitch2026-11-05

Deployment-constrained key estimation with a DSP-guided transformer reranker

key lime：在模板比對的 24 個調性分數上加一個 2 萬參數、115 KB 的 transformer 殘差修正，讓本機音樂軟體的調性偵測逼近 madmom

Pardis Noorzad
arXiv:2611.02408 · cs.SD; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇處理一個很實際的工程問題：本機音樂軟體匯入曲庫時要偵測調性，模型必須小、快、不帶大型執行環境。作者在既有的色度模板比對器上加一個 21,528 參數、115 KB 的 transformer，學習修正那 24 個調性分數。在 1,000 首封存的 FMAKv2 測試曲上，MIREX 分數從 61.08 升到 73.55（三個種子），只比本機跑的 madmom KeyCNN（74.16）低 0.61，而且這個差距的信賴區間跨過零。選它是因為消融把功勞歸得很清楚：只學分數投影、不看逐幀色度的版本是 60.25，比原本的模板比對還低，論文標榜的循環卷積、單位矩陣初始化、課程學習和蒸餾都沒有可分辨的效果。Why this one: This paper tackles a practical engineering problem: a local music app has to estimate keys while importing a library, so the model must be small, fast and free of a heavy runtime. The author adds a 21,528-parameter, 115 KB transformer on top of an existing chroma-template scorer to correct its 24 key scores. On a sealed holdout of 1,000 FMAKv2 tracks, MIREX rises from 61.08 to 73.55 (three seeds), only 0.61 below a locally run madmom KeyCNN (74.16), and the confidence interval of that gap crosses zero. I picked it because the ablation assigns credit cleanly: a version that learns only the score projection without frame-level chroma gets 60.25, below the template scorer itself, and the circular convolution, identity initialization, curriculum and distillation the paper describes show no distinguishable effect.

動機Motivation

作者任職的 Cue Labs 開發本機音樂應用 Cueport，匯入曲庫時要在使用者的筆電上逐首分析調性。這個場景下，分析器的體積和執行環境依賴跟準確率一樣重要：匯入幾千首歌不能太慢，也不能為了調性多裝幾百 MB。

現有的 Rust 分析器 trail mix 用色度模板比對，部署成本很低，但在 FMAKv2 測試集上 MIREX 只有 61.08，本機跑的 madmom KeyCNN 是 74.16。反過來，madmom 安裝後佔 174 MB，自監督的 S-KEY 佔 436 MB。論文要回答的是：能不能保留原本的 DSP 分析器，只補一個很小的學習元件，把這個差距補回來。

The author's company, Cue Labs, develops the local music app Cueport, which analyses each track's key on the user's laptop during library import. In that setting the analyser's size and runtime dependencies matter as much as accuracy: importing thousands of songs must not be slow, and key detection should not add hundreds of megabytes.

The existing Rust analyser, trail mix, uses chroma-template matching and is cheap to ship, but it scores only 61.08 MIREX on the FMAKv2 holdout, against 74.16 for a locally run madmom KeyCNN. In the other direction, madmom occupies 174 MB once installed and the self-supervised S-KEY 436 MB. The paper asks whether a very small learned component, added while keeping the DSP analyser, can close that gap.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

模板比對傳統。 Krumhansl-Schmuckler 方法把一首曲子的音級分布跟 24 個調性模板做相關，模板來自 Krumhansl 與 Kessler（1982）的聽感實驗，Temperley（1999）修訂了模板，Faraldo 等人（2016）則為電子舞曲另做一組。trail mix 的 DSP 部分就是把這幾組模板的 Pearson 相關取平均。

學習式方法。 madmom KeyCNN（Korzeniowski 與 Widmer，2018）以卷積網路直接從頻譜學調性分類，是這篇主要對手。STONE（2024）與 S-KEY（2025）利用「移調 n 個半音，調性表徵也該移 n 格」的自監督訊號，S-KEY 完全不需要人工標註。這篇的循環卷積把移調等變性寫進輸入層的結構，而 STONE 系列是用損失函數學出來；兩者目的相近，作法不同。

最像的既有技巧。 「固定基線的分數加上一個學出來的殘差」本身並不新，這就是 stacking 或殘差提升（boosting）的形式：把強基線的輸出當成先驗，讓第二個模型只學修正量。論文的貢獻在於把它套在部署限制下，並用消融證明修正量真的需要逐幀資訊。另外值得注意的是 libKeyFinder：同樣是純 DSP、只佔 0.8 MB，在同一份測試集上有 67.46 MIREX，比 trail mix 高 6.38 點。論文坦承這點，並把「改去修正其他 DSP 器的分數」列為未來工作。

The template tradition. The Krumhansl-Schmuckler method correlates a piece's pitch-class distribution with 24 key templates. The templates come from Krumhansl and Kessler's (1982) listening experiments, Temperley (1999) revised them, and Faraldo et al. (2016) built a set for electronic dance music. The DSP part of trail mix averages Pearson correlations over these template families.

Learned methods. madmom KeyCNN (Korzeniowski and Widmer, 2018) learns key classification from spectrograms with a convolutional network and is the main competitor here. STONE (2024) and S-KEY (2025) use the self-supervised signal that transposing audio by n semitones should shift the key representation by n, and S-KEY needs no human labels at all. This paper's circular convolution builds transposition equivariance into the input layer's structure, whereas the STONE line learns it through the loss; the goal is similar, the means differ.

The closest existing technique. Adding a learned residual to a fixed baseline's scores is not new; it is the form of stacking or residual boosting, where a strong baseline's output serves as a prior and a second model learns only the correction. The contribution is applying it under deployment constraints and showing by ablation that the correction really needs frame-level evidence. Also worth noting is libKeyFinder: pure DSP, only 0.8 MB, and 67.46 MIREX on the same holdout, 6.38 points above trail mix. The paper acknowledges this and lists reranking other DSP scorers as future work.

方法Method

DSP 部分。 以 Goertzel 濾波器組與常數 Q 轉換兩種前端算色度，平均後跟 24 個調性模板做 Pearson 相關，得到一個 24 維分數向量 s_dsp，分數最高者就是 DSP 的答案。

學習部分。 模型讀取同一串逐幀色度的前 256 幀（約 32 秒），先經過一個循環一維卷積：沿 12 個音級滑動、共用同一組核，核寬 3、輸出 3 個通道，每幀得到 36 維特徵，只有 12 個可訓練權重。原本的線性輸入投影要 468 個。接著是一層 transformer 編碼器（36 維、4 個注意力頭、72 維前饋），平均池化後得到 24 維的 z_seq。最後的 logit 是 z_seq 加上 W 乘以 s_dsp，W 是 24×24 矩陣並以單位矩陣初始化，所以訓練開始時模型輸出就等於 DSP 的排序，transformer 只需要學殘差修正。

訓練。 用 4,040 首 FMAK 曲目訓練、449 首做驗證與早停；1,000 首 FMAKv2 測試曲在模型選擇前就封存，並記下指紋。損失函數是軟目標交叉熵，把 20% 的機率分給正確調性的五度、關係調與同主音調，比例照 MIREX 計分權重分配。另有五個 epoch 的課程學習（先訓 DSP 信心高的曲子）以及 12 個半音的移調增強。

DSP part. Chroma is computed with a Goertzel filter bank and a constant-Q transform, averaged, and correlated (Pearson) against 24 key templates, giving a 24-dimensional score vector s_dsp whose maximum is the DSP answer.

Learned part. The model reads the first 256 frames (about 32 seconds) of the same frame-level chroma. It first applies a circular 1-D convolution that slides one shared kernel over the 12 pitch classes, with kernel width 3 and 3 output channels, giving 36 features per frame from just 12 trainable weights, versus 468 for the original linear input projection. One transformer encoder layer follows (36 dimensions, 4 heads, a 72-dimensional feed-forward block), and mean pooling yields a 24-dimensional z_seq. The final logits are z_seq plus W times s_dsp, where W is a 24-by-24 matrix initialized to the identity, so at the start of training the output equals the DSP ranking and the transformer only learns a residual correction.

Training. 4,040 FMAK tracks train the model and 449 handle validation and early stopping; the 1,000 FMAKv2 holdout tracks were sealed, with a recorded fingerprint, before model selection. The loss is soft-target cross-entropy that gives 20% of the mass to the true key's fifth, relative and parallel neighbours in proportion to their MIREX weights. There is also a five-epoch curriculum (high-DSP-confidence tracks first) and transposition augmentation over all 12 semitones.

限制Limitations

大部分設計選擇沒有可分辨的效果。 三個種子下，循環卷積對線性輸入是 73.55 對 72.50，隨機初始化 W 是 72.73，去掉課程學習是 73.16，蒸餾 madmom 反而是 72.50，彼此差距都落在種子變異範圍內。論文自己也這樣寫，並說保留它們只是因為成本可忽略。所以真正能確定的結論只有一句：逐幀色度序列是增益來源。

部署體積的比較有利於自己。 76.7 MB 中有 74.58 MB 是 ONNX Runtime；扣掉之後約 2.1 MB 的純 Rust 版本「尚未量測最終建置」。同時 libKeyFinder 只要 0.8 MB 就有 67.46 MIREX，KL-FMAK 多出的 6 點是用大約 96 倍的安裝體積換來的。

評測範圍窄。 訓練用 FMAK 標註、測試用 FMAKv2 標註，後者修訂了約 200 筆，兩邊標註並不一致。跨語料檢查只有 Schubert Winterreise 的 24 首，MIREX 增益 10.00 點的信賴區間是負 3.75 到正 25.00，跨過零。S-KEY 的分數是引用全 5,489 首協定的公開值，不是同一份清單上重跑的結果。此外，作者任職於使用這個系統的公司，論文有揭露這點。

Most design choices have no distinguishable effect. Over three seeds, circular against linear input is 73.55 versus 72.50, random initialization of W gives 72.73, removing the curriculum gives 73.16, and distilling madmom gives 72.50, all within seed variation. The paper says so itself, and keeps them only because they cost almost nothing. The one firm conclusion is that the frame-level chroma sequence is the source of the gain.

The footprint comparison favours the author. Of the 76.7 MB, 74.58 MB is ONNX Runtime; the roughly 2.1 MB pure-Rust version without it has not been measured as a final build. Meanwhile libKeyFinder reaches 67.46 MIREX in 0.8 MB, so KL-FMAK's extra 6 points cost about 96 times the installed size.

The evaluation is narrow. Training uses FMAK labels and testing uses FMAKv2 labels, which revise about 200 annotations, so the two label sources differ. The cross-corpus check is just 24 Schubert Winterreise tracks, and its 10.00-point MIREX gain has a 95% interval of -3.75 to +25.00, crossing zero. S-KEY's score is a published full 5,489-track number, not rerun on the same manifest. The author works for the company that ships this system, which the paper discloses.

討論Discussion

主結果。 三個種子的 MIREX 是 73.00、73.53、74.12，平均 73.55，比 DSP 高 12.47 點；精確準確率從 52.30% 升到 67.07%，高 14.77 點，甚至比本機 madmom 的 66.70% 高 0.37 點。代表種子對 DSP 的配對 bootstrap 95% 信賴區間是正 9.51 到正 15.40，McNemar 檢定 p 為 9.4×10⁻¹⁶。對 madmom 的差距是負 0.61，信賴區間負 3.27 到正 2.03，也就是說這份樣本分不出兩者高低。

修正了什麼、弄壞了什麼。 代表種子把 DSP 的 477 首錯誤中的 244 首改對，同時把 97 首原本正確的改錯，淨增 147 首。改錯的分布是五度 19、關係調 18、同主音調 22、其他 38，並沒有集中在某一種和聲關係。對不想冒險的使用情境，作者建議下一步做信心門檻，信心不足時退回 DSP 的答案，但這篇沒有做。

學到的 W。 對角線平均 0.80，五度、關係調、同主音調的位置分別是正 0.19、0.18、0.36，其他位置平均負 0.12，等於在音樂上相鄰的候選上加分。但只學 W 的版本（576 個參數）是 60.25，比 DSP 還低 0.83 點，所以這種「重新加權鄰近調」本身不夠，需要逐幀資訊才能判斷該往哪個鄰居移。延遲方面，每首只在 486 毫秒的 DSP 分析上多 0.32 毫秒。

Main result. The three seeds give 73.00, 73.53 and 74.12 MIREX, a mean of 73.55 and 12.47 points above DSP; exact accuracy rises from 52.30% to 67.07%, up 14.77 points and even 0.37 above local madmom's 66.70%. For the representative seed, the paired bootstrap 95% interval against DSP is +9.51 to +15.40, with McNemar p = 9.4×10⁻¹⁶. The gap to madmom is -0.61 with an interval of -3.27 to +2.03, so this sample cannot separate the two.

What it fixes and what it breaks. The representative seed turns 244 of DSP's 477 errors into exact matches while breaking 97 previously correct tracks, a net gain of 147. The broken ones split into 19 fifth, 18 relative, 22 parallel and 38 other errors, not concentrated in any one harmonic relation. For risk-averse uses, the author suggests a confidence gate that falls back to the DSP answer as the next step, but this paper does not test one.

The learned W. Its diagonal averages 0.80, and the fifth, relative and parallel entries average +0.19, +0.18 and +0.36, while other off-diagonal entries average -0.12, so it boosts musically adjacent candidates. Yet learning W alone (576 parameters) gives 60.25, 0.83 below DSP, so reweighting neighbouring keys is not enough on its own; frame-level evidence is needed to decide which neighbour to move to. On latency, the model adds only 0.32 ms per track on top of 486 ms of DSP analysis.

How Close to the Theme? Similarity-Label Conditioning for Game Music Generation

跟主題有多像？用「高、中、低相似度」標籤加上下文向量微調 Moonbeam，讓遊戲音樂生成能指定新段落與原段落的接近程度

Shangxuan Luo, Zixun Guo, Jinjie Shi, Joshua Reiss
arXiv:2611.01047 · cs.SD; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-31
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：作者把同一首超級瑪利歐鋼琴譜裡的兩個段落配成一對，依自訂的複合相似度（CS）分成高、中、低三級，拿來微調 839M 參數的 Moonbeam MIDI 模型，讓使用者指定續寫段落跟原段落有多像。選它是因為作者沒有把弱結果包裝成成功：高相似度標籤確實讓旋律更接近原段落（157 個情境中 62.7% 較近），但生成結果的差距只有真實段落差距的 3.2%，中與低兩級分不開，聽測也沒有測出標籤效果（高 2.9 分，信賴區間跨零）。論文的結論是標籤只決定「方向」，還控制不了「程度」。Why this one: The authors pair two sections of the same Super Mario piano score, label each pair high, mid or low by a compound similarity (CS) of their own, and fine-tune the 839M-parameter Moonbeam MIDI model so a user can request how similar a continuation should be to its source section. I picked it because the authors do not dress a weak result up as success: the high-similarity label does bring melodies closer to the context (closer in 62.7% of 157 contexts), but the generated gap is only 3.2% of the gap between real sections, mid and low cannot be told apart, and the listening test did not detect the label effect (2.9 points higher, interval crossing zero). The paper concludes that the label sets the direction of similarity, not yet its degree.

動機Motivation

作曲家在發展一個主題時，會決定每個新段落要跟原素材多像，從幾乎原樣重複到全新的想法都有。遊戲音樂特別需要這種控制：遊戲會在遊玩過程中重組循環、分支與層次，作曲家常要為同一份素材寫好幾個可以互換的段落，而重複得太精確，玩久了就會被注意到。

現有的符號音樂模型能流暢地接續一段音樂，但不讓使用者選擇「要多像」。作者想知道：如果直接從真實作品裡各段落之間的相似程度學，模型能不能學到這個控制。

When composers develop a theme, they decide how similar each new section is to the original material, anywhere from near repetition to a new idea. Game music needs this control in particular: games recombine loops, branches and layers during play, composers often write several interchangeable sections over the same material, and repetition that is too exact becomes noticeable over long sessions.

Existing symbolic music models continue a passage fluently but do not let the user choose how similar it should be. The authors ask whether a model can learn this control directly from how similar the sections of real pieces are to one another.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

控制「相近程度」的前作。 MusicVAE（Roberts 等，2018）在潛在空間內插；ImprovNet（Bhandari 等，2025）用破壞步驟的強度決定即興偏離原曲多遠；MuseMorphose（Wu 與 Yang，2023）透過小節層級屬性改寫一首曲子。這些方法都用模型機制或屬性來定義「相近」，不是從真實段落對學來的。最接近的是 Variation Transformer（Gao、Reuben 與 Collins，2024），它從真實作品的主題與變奏對學習，也包含遊戲音樂，但只用落在固定相似度範圍內的配對，不能選程度。

屬性條件。 FIGARO（Rütte 等，2023）與 Music FaderNets（Tan 與 Herremans，2020）也用離散化的屬性做條件，但那些屬性描述的是單一段落，例如音符密度或和弦。這篇的標籤描述的是兩個段落之間的關係。

最像的既有技巧。 把「生成結果相對於某個參考的分數」切成幾級、做成標籤 token 放在輸入前面，訓練時用真實配對的分數當標籤，這跟語言模型裡的控制碼（CTRL，Keskar 等，2019）以及把獎勵量化成 token 的 Quark（Lu 等，2022）、以回報為條件的 Decision Transformer 是同一個模式。論文沒有提到這條線；新意在於條件的對象是「兩段之間的相似度」，以及評測時對這個控制量做了多面向檢驗。

Prior work on controlling closeness. MusicVAE (Roberts et al., 2018) interpolates in latent space; ImprovNet (Bhandari et al., 2025) sets how far an improvisation departs from the original by the strength of a corruption step; MuseMorphose (Wu and Yang, 2023) rewrites a piece through bar-level attributes. All of these define closeness through a model mechanism or attributes rather than learning it from real section pairs. The closest is Variation Transformer (Gao, Reuben and Collins, 2024), which learns from theme-and-variation pairs in real pieces, game music included, but only uses pairs within a fixed similarity range, so the degree cannot be chosen.

Attribute conditioning. FIGARO (Rütte et al., 2023) and Music FaderNets (Tan and Herremans, 2020) also condition on discretised attributes, but those attributes describe a single passage, such as note density or chords. The label here describes a relation between two sections.

The closest existing technique. Binning a score of the output relative to a reference into a few levels, putting it in front of the input as a label token, and training with the real pair's score as the label is the same pattern as control codes in language models (CTRL, Keskar et al., 2019), as Quark (Lu et al., 2022), which quantises rewards into tokens, and as the return-conditioned Decision Transformer. The paper does not mention this line; what is new is that the condition is the similarity between two sections, and that the evaluation tests this control from several angles.

方法Method

資料。 語料是 NinSheetMusic 上 554 首超級瑪利歐鋼琴改編譜，排除雙鋼琴與混合編制後剩 515 首，其中 334 首有兩個以上主體段落，切成 236 首訓練、49 首驗證、49 首測試。段落切分與段落字母由 Gemini 3.1 Pro 讀 ABC 文字完成；兩位標註者獨立標了 47 首，AI 跟兩人的 Cohen's kappa 是 0.59 與 0.68，兩位標註者彼此是 0.58，信賴區間很寬，作者因此只宣稱「相當」而不是「更好」。

相似度標籤。 CS 是四個成分的加權平均：音級分布（取 12 種移調中最好的餘弦相似度）、時值分布、平均音域、音符密度，權重 4、3、1、1 是事先手訂、沒有調整過。3,304 個有序段落對依 0.78 與 0.60 兩個門檻切成高、中、低三級，分別有 1,090、1,172、1,042 對。CS 刻意不含旋律成分。

模型。 以凍結的 Moonbeam 839M 為骨幹，只在 query 與 value 投影上加 LoRA，加上投影層與新 token 共 4.60M 個可訓練參數。輸入依序是上下文段落的音符、分隔符、標籤 token、目標段落的音符，損失只算目標部分。為了讓模型抓住幾百個 token 之前的上下文，骨幹最後一層隱藏狀態的平均經線性投影後成為「上下文向量」，在目標段落的每個小節邊界以一個 CTX token 注入。三個對照模型分別拿掉標籤、拿掉標籤與上下文向量、以及永遠給錯誤標籤。

Data. The corpus is the 554 Super Mario piano arrangements on NinSheetMusic; removing two-piano and mixed-ensemble pieces leaves 515, of which 334 have two or more main-body sections, split into 236 training, 49 validation and 49 test pieces. Gemini 3.1 Pro segments each piece and assigns section letters by reading its ABC text; two annotators independently labelled 47 pieces, and the AI's Cohen's kappa with them is 0.59 and 0.68, while the two annotators reach 0.58 with each other. The intervals are wide, so the authors claim parity rather than superiority.

Similarity labels. CS is a weighted mean of four components: pitch-class distribution (best cosine similarity over 12 transpositions), duration distribution, mean register and note density, with weights 4, 3, 1 and 1 set by hand in advance and never tuned. The 3,304 ordered section pairs are split by thresholds of 0.78 and 0.60 into high, mid and low labels with 1,090, 1,172 and 1,042 pairs. CS deliberately has no melody component.

Model. The frozen Moonbeam 839M backbone gets LoRA on its query and value projections; with the projection layer and new tokens, 4.60M parameters train. The input is the context section's notes, a separator, a label token and then the target section's notes, with loss on the target only. To give the model a direct hold on context hundreds of tokens back, the mean of the backbone's last hidden layer passes through a linear projection to become a context vector, injected as a CTX token at every bar boundary of the target. Three controls remove the label, remove both label and context vector, or always give a wrong label.

限制Limitations

控制量很弱。 高對低的旋律差距 0.0080，只有真實段落差距 0.249 的 3.2%；連模型自己的訓練目標 CS 也只有 3.7%。真實目標段落的 CS 平均是 0.918、0.658、0.556，生成結果卻是 0.654、0.638、0.641，全擠在真實中級與低級之間。單一抽樣下旋律差距的標準差 0.044 是平均差距的五倍，所以這是平均值的移動，不是每次都靠得住的控制。

「挑最好的」就能取代標籤。 用無標籤模型生成 12 個、再以 CS 挑最接近的一個，在旋律、節奏、時值上跟一個高相似度輸出分不出差別，旋律差距只有 0.0008（負 0.0066 到正 0.0082），音級分布上甚至是挑選法更接近。對作曲家來說，這削弱了訓練一個帶標籤模型的理由。

評測設計有事後成分。 四個主要指標是在看過聽測評分與初步標籤比較之後才定的，作者因此把聽測分析標為探索性。三級門檻是用全部 3,304 對算出的三分位數，包含測試曲。語料只有單一系列的鋼琴改編，每個模型只訓練一次，也沒有作曲家參與的使用研究。

The control is weak. The high-minus-low Melody gap is 0.0080, only 3.2% of the 0.249 gap between real sections; even on CS, the model's own training target, it is 3.7%. Real target sections average CS 0.918, 0.658 and 0.556 for the three labels, while generated outputs average 0.654, 0.638 and 0.641, all squeezed between the real mid and low means. The standard deviation of single-draw Melody gaps, 0.044, is five times the mean gap, so this is a shift in the mean, not control that holds draw by draw.

Picking the best can replace the label. Generating 12 no-label outputs and keeping the one closest by CS cannot be told apart from one high-similarity output on Melody, Rhythm and Duration; the Melody gap is just 0.0008 (-0.0066 to +0.0082), and on Chroma selection is actually closer. For a composer, that weakens the case for training a labelled model.

Parts of the evaluation were fixed after the fact. The four main measures were chosen after seeing the listening ratings and the first label comparisons, so the authors mark the listening analysis as exploratory. The tertile thresholds were computed over all 3,304 pairs, test pieces included. The corpus is piano arrangements from one game series, each model is a single training run, and there is no study with composers.

討論Discussion

標籤的效果。 每個 157 個測試情境各生成 6 次，942 次中 929 次通過最少 10 個音符的門檻。四個主要指標都是高相似度標籤勝過低與中，旋律差距 0.0080（0.0045 到 0.0121），62.7% 的情境中較近，經 Holm 校正的 p 值介於 0.003 與 0.012。補充材料的 12 個指標方向全部一致。但中級沒有勝過低級：12 個指標中一個都沒有，所以實際上只有「高相似度對其他」的兩段式控制。

跟無標籤比。 只有高相似度標籤讓輸出比無標籤更接近，旋律上 66.2% 的情境較近、差距 0.0106；低標籤則沒有可量測的效果。錯誤標籤對照組的高對低在旋律上只有 36%，方向反轉，表示模型確實讀了標籤的意義。上下文向量把不穩定輸出（提早停或在幾個音上打轉）從 30.6% 降到 14.1%，並提升音級分布與時值的相似度，但不提升旋律。

它怎麼變像的。 檢查上下文專屬性：把每個輸出拿去跟三個其他曲子的段落比，高對低的旋律差距從 0.0080 變成負 0.0001，表示標籤是往「這一段」靠，而不是變得更像整個語料。機制是重述：高相似度輸出有 12.4% 會重述上下文旋律中連續 8 個以上的音，其他標籤只有 6.0% 到 7.1%，而同等 CS 的真實段落是 17.4%，所以並沒有過度抄寫。拿掉這些重述的輸出後，旋律差距降到 0.0027，信賴區間碰到零。

聽測。 26 位聽者中 6 位沒通過注意力檢查，剩下 20 位。高相似度輸出只高 2.9 分（滿分 100，信賴區間負 2.0 到正 7.7，p = 0.24），這個設計只有在效果約 7 分時才有 80% 檢定力。原因之一是每題只放一次抽樣，而那 30 對在實際播放的 10 秒裡，高相似度輸出只有 12 對共享較多旋律。聽者還有明顯的順序偏誤：緊接在上下文之後播放的 A 段，不論內容平均高 7.9 分。最有用的發現反而是指標與聽感的對應：每升一個標準差，旋律指標讓評分升 6.7 分，是唯一通過 Holm 校正的主要指標，而 CS 只有 3.4 分。這直接指向下一步：標籤本身應該包含旋律。

What the label does. Each of the 157 test contexts gets 6 draws, and 929 of 942 pass a minimum of 10 notes. On all four main measures the high label beats low and mid; the Melody gap is 0.0080 (0.0045 to 0.0121), closer in 62.7% of contexts, with Holm-corrected p-values from 0.003 to 0.012. All 12 supplementary measures agree in direction. But mid does not beat low on any of the 12, so in practice the control is two-way: high against the rest.

Against no label. Only the high label makes outputs closer than no label, closer on Melody in 66.2% of contexts by 0.0106; the low label has no measurable effect. In the wrong-label control, high beats low on Melody in only 36% of contexts, a reversal, which shows the model does read what the label means. The context vector cuts unstable outputs (stopping early or looping on a few pitches) from 30.6% to 14.1% and raises Chroma and Duration similarity, but not Melody.

How it gets closer. A specificity check scores each output against sections from three other pieces: the high-minus-low Melody gap goes from 0.0080 to -0.0001, so the label pulls toward this particular context rather than toward the corpus in general. The mechanism is restatement: 12.4% of high-label outputs restate a run of 8 or more notes of the context melody, against 6.0% to 7.1% for other labels, while real sections of equal CS do so 17.4% of the time, so there is no excess copying. Without the restating outputs, the Melody gap falls to 0.0027, with an interval reaching zero.

Listening test. Six of 26 listeners failed an attention check, leaving 20. High-label outputs were rated only 2.9 points higher (out of 100, interval -2.0 to +7.7, p = 0.24), and the design had 80% power only for an effect of about 7 points. One reason is that each trial played a single draw, and in the 10 seconds heard only 12 of the 30 high-label outputs shared more melody with the context. Listeners also showed a clear order bias: section A, played right after the context, got 7.9 points more whatever it held. The most useful finding is how measures track ratings: per standard deviation, Melody raises ratings by 6.7 points and is the only main measure to survive Holm correction, against 3.4 for CS. That points straight to the next step: the label itself should include melody.
