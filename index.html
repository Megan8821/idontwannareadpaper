A Learned Musical-Time Coordinate for Piano Audio-to-Score Transcription

學出來的音樂時間座標：讓鋼琴音訊轉譜模型自己估計每一格在小節裡的相位（downbeat phase），同時拿它引導樂譜解碼，並在小節線上自己切段、拼回整首曲子

Min-Hsing Wang, Tsung-Ren Huang
arXiv:2610.29928 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-21
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：台大的兩位作者把「這一格音訊落在小節的哪個位置」做成一個和轉譜一起學的圓周相位，用 Fourier 特徵同時餵給解碼器的音訊端與樂譜端，並拿相位歸零點當小節線，讓系統自己切段、拼回整首曲子。整首轉譜時，F_meter 在合成測試集 Syn 上從 Piano-A2S + Beat This! 的 .236 升到 .667，在真人演奏的 ASAP 上從 .143 升到 .318，而且 100% 的錄音都有輸出。要保留的是：在預先切好段的 ASAP 上，加不加這個座標的 MV2H₅ 幾乎一樣（.677 對 .678）；ASAP 整首轉譜的 OMR-NED 是 85.4，跟基線的 84.0 沒有顯著差異，符號層級的編輯距離仍然很高。Why this one: Two National Taiwan University authors turn 'where this audio frame sits within the bar' into a circular phase learned jointly with transcription, feed it as Fourier features to both the audio side and the score side of the decoder, and use its zero crossings as barlines so the system segments and stitches whole pieces itself. In whole-piece transcription, F_meter rises from .236 (Piano-A2S + Beat This!) to .667 on the synthetic Syn test set and from .143 to .318 on human-performed ASAP, with output for 100% of recordings. The caveats: on pre-segmented ASAP, MV2H₅ is essentially the same with or without the coordinate (.677 vs .678); whole-piece ASAP OMR-NED is 85.4, not significantly different from the baseline's 84.0, so symbol-level edit distance remains high.

動機Motivation

鋼琴「音訊轉樂譜」（audio-to-score, A2S）要從一段錄音直接還原出記譜：音高、音值、拍號、調號與小節線。困難在於演奏者會自己決定速度與彈性速度，同一個四分音符在音訊上可長可短，模型必須推回它在小節裡的「記譜位置」。現有的端對端系統（例如 Piano-A2S）大多只處理事先在小節線上切好的幾小節片段；整首曲子要怎麼切、切完怎麼拼回去，幾乎沒有人處理。

這篇的出發點是：如果模型內部本來就有一個「音樂時間座標」，知道每一格音訊在小節裡走到哪裡，這個座標應該能一次解決兩件事——引導解碼器把音符放到正確的拍位上，以及告訴系統小節線在哪裡，讓它自己切段。先前的爵士薩克斯風 A2S 要靠外部節拍追蹤器提供這個座標；作者要問的是，它能不能跟轉譜一起學出來。

Piano audio-to-score (A2S) transcription aims to recover a notated score directly from a recording: pitches, note values, time and key signatures, and barlines. The difficulty is that performers choose their own tempo and rubato, so the same quarter note can be long or short in the audio, and the model has to infer each note's notated position within the bar. Existing end-to-end systems (such as Piano-A2S) mostly handle excerpts of a few bars pre-segmented at barlines; how to segment a whole piece, and how to stitch the pieces back together, is largely unaddressed.

The paper's premise is that if the model internally carries a musical-time coordinate, knowing where each audio frame sits within the bar, that coordinate should solve two problems at once: guiding the decoder to place notes on the right metrical positions, and telling the system where the barlines are so it can segment itself. Earlier jazz saxophone A2S relied on an external beat tracker for this coordinate; the authors ask whether it can be learned jointly with transcription.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接的前作有三個。Piano-A2S（Zeng 等，IJCAI 2024）用階層式 GRU 解碼器，先預測小節層級、再預測音符層級的 kern 符號，是這篇的主要基線；Alfaro-Contreras 等（ICASSP 2024）用 Transformer 解碼器處理多聲部弦樂。兩者都假設輸入已經在小節線上切好。最接近這篇的是 Shanin、Riley 與 Dixon 的 Rhythm Perceiver（ICASSP 2026）：它把每一拍內的位置寫成圓周相位，以 Fourier 特徵引導爵士獨奏轉譜，但相位來自外部節拍追蹤器。這篇的差別只有一處，卻是關鍵的一處：相位由模型自己估計，而且單位從「拍」換成「小節」，所以相位歸零點直接就是小節線。

相位表示本身也不是新的。Oyama、Ishizuka 與 Yoshii（ISMIR 2021）的相位式節拍／強拍估計已經用 360 類圓周分類加平滑目標，這篇的 TCN 結構與損失函數幾乎原樣沿用，BiMamba 層則來自 BeatMamba（ICASSP 2026）。換句話說，論文宣稱首創的「學出來的座標」，最像的是把 Oyama 的強拍相位估計器當成多工學習的輔助頭，接到 Rhythm Perceiver 式的條件解碼器上。

另一條值得對照的是串接式路線：先做鋼琴轉譜（如 hFT-Transformer），再做演奏 MIDI 轉樂譜，例如 Liu 等（ISMIR 2022）以神經節拍追蹤做的 PM2S、Beyer 與 Dai（ISMIR 2024）的 Transformer，以及本站讀過的 DuET。這篇凍結的前端正是 hFT-Transformer，所以它其實介於端對端與串接之間；論文沒有跟任何串接系統比較。

There are three direct predecessors. Piano-A2S (Zeng et al., IJCAI 2024) uses a hierarchical GRU decoder that predicts bar-level and then note-level kern symbols and is this paper's main baseline; Alfaro-Contreras et al. (ICASSP 2024) use a Transformer decoder for polyphonic string music. Both assume input pre-segmented at barlines. The closest is the Rhythm Perceiver of Shanin, Riley and Dixon (ICASSP 2026): it writes position within each beat as a circular phase and uses Fourier features to guide jazz solo transcription, but the phase comes from an external beat tracker. This paper differs in one place, but a crucial one: the model estimates the phase itself, and the unit changes from beat to bar, so phase zero crossings are barlines directly.

The phase representation is not new either. The phase-aware beat/downbeat estimation of Oyama, Ishizuka and Yoshii (ISMIR 2021) already uses 360-class circular classification with smoothed targets; this paper adopts its TCN design and loss almost unchanged, and the BiMamba layers come from BeatMamba (ICASSP 2026). In other words, the 'learned coordinate' the paper presents as new most resembles attaching Oyama's downbeat-phase estimator as an auxiliary multitask head to a Rhythm-Perceiver-style conditioned decoder.

Another useful comparison is the cascade route: piano transcription (e.g., hFT-Transformer) followed by performance-MIDI-to-score conversion, such as the neural-beat-tracking PM2S of Liu et al. (ISMIR 2022), the Transformer of Beyer and Dai (ISMIR 2024), and DuET, covered earlier on this site. This paper's frozen front end is exactly hFT-Transformer, so it really sits between end-to-end and cascade; the paper compares against no cascade system.

方法Method

輸入是 16 kHz、256 個 mel 頻帶、每格 16 ms 的對數 mel 頻譜，一個視窗 1,280 格（20.48 秒）。前端是凍結的 hFT-Transformer（在 MAESTRO 上預訓練），每格對 88 個琴鍵各輸出 256 維特徵，再用 32 個可學查詢的交叉注意力壓成 88×32，攤平後投影成 256 維。之後分兩支：內容支路是兩層 Conformer（自注意力用 RoPE）；相位支路是 Oyama 式的七塊膨脹 TCN 加六層雙向 Mamba-2，最後接 360 類的逐格相位分類器。

相位 φ 每個記譜小節轉一圈、在強拍歸零。作者用 1 到 12 階諧波的 sin/cos 組成 24 維 Fourier 特徵 γ(φ)；取到 12 階是為了涵蓋 12/8 拍裡每小節 12 個八分音符。音訊端的編碼不是取最可能的類別，而是用 360 類後驗機率對 γ 加權平均，所以模型不確定時，編碼會自動變弱。樂譜端則由已解碼的前綴算出「目前寫到小節裡的哪裡」，同樣過 γ。解碼器是四層 Transformer（256 維、4 頭）：樂譜端相位投影後加到輸入，音訊端相位則在每層交叉注意力裡分別投影進 key 與 value。

目標序列沿用 Piano-A2S 由 kern 衍生的詞彙（248 個 token），但改了序列化順序：音高在記譜起點輸出、音值在記譜終點輸出——這個改動沒有做消融。損失是下一個 token 的交叉熵加 0.1 倍的逐格相位交叉熵，相位目標量化到 360 類，再以 ±3 類的三角權重平滑。總參數 23.4M，其中 17.9M 可訓練。

整首推論分三步。先用 1,280 格視窗、640 格步長掃全曲，重疊處以圓周一階矩平均，取相位歸零點當強拍，強拍間至少相隔 400 ms。接著每五小節讓解碼器為候選拍號與調號打分，用一階馬可夫模型加 Viterbi 決定全曲的拍號與調號序列，換拍號的懲罰是 8.3、換調號是 6.3，取自訓練集的變化率。最後逐段貪婪解碼，只保留能還原成合法 kern 的完整小節前綴，下一個視窗從接下來的強拍重新開始；失敗的段落以空白小節補上。

訓練資料是 HumSyn（Humdrum 的古典與拉格泰姆）和 MuseSyn（MuseScore 的流行改編），共 546 首，用 VirtuosoNet 產生不同速度（×1/√2 到 ×√2）與演奏風格，再以四種 SoundFont 與四種殘響渲染。測試是 Syn 45 首（每首四個渲染）與 ASAP 74 段真人錄音（39 首作品），已排除前端訓練用過的錄音。指標是 MV2H 的五個分量（重點是量度分量 F_meter）與 OMR-NED，顯著性以作品為單位、做 10,000 次配對 bootstrap。

The input is a 16 kHz log-mel spectrogram with 256 mel bins and 16 ms frames, 1,280 frames (20.48 s) per window. The front end is a frozen hFT-Transformer pretrained on MAESTRO, which outputs a 256-dim feature for each of the 88 keys per frame; cross-attention with 32 learned queries compresses this to 88×32, which is flattened and projected to 256 dims. Two branches follow: a content branch of two Conformer blocks (self-attention with RoPE), and a phase branch of an Oyama-style seven-block dilated TCN plus six bidirectional Mamba-2 layers, ending in a 360-class framewise phase classifier.

The phase φ completes one cycle per notated bar and resets at each downbeat. The authors build a 24-dim Fourier feature γ(φ) from sin/cos of harmonics 1 to 12; order 12 covers the twelve eighth-note units per bar in 12/8. The audio-side encoding does not take the most likely class but averages γ under the 360-class posterior, so the encoding weakens automatically when the model is uncertain. On the score side, the decoded prefix determines 'where in the bar we are now', also passed through γ. The decoder is a four-layer Transformer (256 dims, 4 heads): the score-side phase is projected and added to the input, and the audio-side phase is projected separately into keys and values in every cross-attention layer.

The target sequence reuses Piano-A2S's kern-derived vocabulary (248 tokens) but changes the serialization order: pitch is emitted at the notated onset and duration at the notated offset—a change that is not ablated. The loss is next-token cross-entropy plus 0.1 times a framewise phase cross-entropy, with phase targets quantized to 360 classes and smoothed with triangular weights over ±3 classes. The model has 23.4M parameters, 17.9M trainable.

Whole-piece inference has three steps. First, 1,280-frame windows with a 640-frame stride scan the piece, overlaps are averaged by circular first moments, and phase zero crossings become downbeats, at least 400 ms apart. Next, every five bars the decoder scores candidate time and key signatures, and first-order Markov models with Viterbi fix the piece's signature sequences, with change penalties of 8.3 (time) and 6.3 (key) taken from training-split change rates. Finally, segments are decoded greedily, keeping only the longest prefix of complete bars that reconstructs into valid kern, and the next window restarts at the following downbeat; failed segments become blank bars.

Training uses HumSyn (classical and ragtime from Humdrum) and MuseSyn (pop arrangements from MuseScore), 546 scores in all, with VirtuosoNet producing varied tempi (×1/√2 to ×√2) and performance styles, rendered with four SoundFonts and four reverbs. Testing uses 45 Syn scores (four renderings each) and 74 human ASAP recordings (39 works), excluding recordings used to train the front end. Metrics are the five MV2H components (chiefly the metrical component F_meter) and OMR-NED, with significance from 10,000 paired bootstrap draws over works.

限制Limitations

第一，座標對真人演奏的幫助沒有站穩。在預先切段的 ASAP 上，完整模型的 F_meter 是 .384，去掉座標是 .357，差 .027 且不顯著；多音高、聲部與音值反而下降（.621 對 .652、.833 對 .851、.889 對 .901），MV2H₅ 幾乎一樣（.677 對 .678）。作者自己也寫「在 ASAP 上的效益尚無定論」。而且 ASAP 預先切段的測試只有 8 首作品、633 個片段。

第二，整首轉譜的大差距，有一部分來自基線被放進它不擅長的條件。Piano-A2S 只吃 4–12 秒的輸入，用標註強拍切段時轉譜覆蓋率只有 77% 與 78%；用 Beat This! 切段時，Syn 的 F_meter 只有 .236。差距的主體可信，但不全是座標的功勞——在預先切段的 Syn 上，沒有座標的版本 MV2H₅ 已經從 Piano-A2S 的 .690 升到 .789，大部分提升來自凍結的 hFT 前端與新的解碼器。

第三，oracle 實驗顯示瓶頸在座標品質。在 ASAP 上把預測的小節線換成標註小節線，F_meter 反而從 .318 降到 .287；同時換掉座標與小節線才升到 .518。另外，整首評估用的是作者自己實作的單路徑 MV2H，它跟官方版本在 F_meter 上的平均絕對差，在 Syn 與 ASAP 分別是 0.0215 與 0.0275，跟 .318 與 .287 之間 .031 的差距是同一個量級。

第四，相位模型與樂譜模型完全在合成演奏上訓練，表情化的時間處理只來自 VirtuosoNet。ASAP 整首的 OMR-NED 是 85.4，與基線的 84.0 沒有顯著差異，符號層級的錯誤仍然很多。座標消融（圖 2）只用單一種子，而且只畫在圖上，內文沒有給數值。

First, the coordinate's benefit on human performances is not established. On pre-segmented ASAP, the full model's F_meter is .384 versus .357 without the coordinate, a .027 difference that is not significant; multi-pitch, voice and note-value scores drop instead (.621 vs .652, .833 vs .851, .889 vs .901), and MV2H₅ is essentially the same (.677 vs .678). The authors themselves call the ASAP benefit 'inconclusive'. The pre-segmented ASAP test covers only 8 works and 633 excerpts.

Second, part of the large whole-piece gap comes from placing the baseline in conditions it handles poorly. Piano-A2S accepts only 4–12 s inputs, so with annotated-downbeat segmentation its coverage is just 77% and 78%; with Beat This! segmentation its Syn F_meter is only .236. The bulk of the gap is credible, but not all of it is the coordinate's doing—on pre-segmented Syn, the variant without the coordinate already lifts MV2H₅ from Piano-A2S's .690 to .789, so most of the gain comes from the frozen hFT front end and the new decoder.

Third, the oracle experiments show the bottleneck is coordinate quality. On ASAP, replacing predicted barlines with annotated ones actually lowers F_meter from .318 to .287; only replacing both the coordinate and the barlines raises it to .518. Moreover, whole-piece evaluation uses the authors' own single-path MV2H, whose mean absolute F_meter difference from the official implementation is 0.0215 on Syn and 0.0275 on ASAP—the same order as the .031 gap between .318 and .287.

Fourth, the phase and score models are trained entirely on synthesized performances, with expressive timing coming only from VirtuosoNet. Whole-piece ASAP OMR-NED is 85.4, not significantly different from the baseline's 84.0, so symbol-level errors remain numerous. The coordinate ablation (Fig. 2) uses a single seed and appears only as a plot, with no numbers in the text.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的地方，是把「小節線在哪裡」從前處理問題變成模型的內部變數。整首轉譜時，Syn 上 F_meter 從 .236 升到 .667、MV2H₅ 從 .614 升到 .829、OMR-NED 從 75.4 降到 61.8；ASAP 上 F_meter 從 .143 升到 .318、MV2H₅ 從 .584 升到 .718，而且兩個測試集都 100% 有輸出。相較之下，基線即使拿到標註強拍，Syn 的 F_meter 也只有 .337。這說明讓切段與轉譜共用同一個座標，比「外部節拍追蹤器加片段轉譜器」的組合穩健得多。

消融的方向也有意思：只加相位監督、不把相位餵給解碼器，F_meter 不升反降，在 Syn 上還是顯著下降；音訊端或樂譜端的 Fourier 編碼單獨加都有顯著幫助，兩個一起最好。這表示幫助來自「條件化」，而不是多工學習的副作用，跟 Rhythm Perceiver 的方向一致。可惜這張圖沒有數值，讀者無法判斷幅度。

往下看，最該補的是真人演奏上的座標估計。oracle 座標能把 ASAP 的 F_meter 從 .318 拉到 .518，這個差距比任何架構改動都大。作者提到要研究表情化時間與記譜慣例對座標的影響；我認為也值得試試用 Beat This! 或 Explore This!（本站 10 月 15 日讀過的 tatum 格點模型）在真實錄音上產生的強拍做半監督訓練。另一個沒測的問題是：推論假設拍號在每五小節內固定，碰到頻繁變換拍號的近代作品會怎樣。

The paper's most valuable move is turning 'where the barlines are' from a preprocessing problem into an internal variable of the model. In whole-piece transcription on Syn, F_meter rises from .236 to .667, MV2H₅ from .614 to .829, and OMR-NED falls from 75.4 to 61.8; on ASAP, F_meter rises from .143 to .318 and MV2H₅ from .584 to .718, with output for 100% of recordings on both test sets. By comparison, the baseline given annotated downbeats reaches only .337 F_meter on Syn. Sharing one coordinate between segmentation and transcription is clearly far more robust than an external beat tracker plus an excerpt transcriber.

The direction of the ablation is also interesting: adding phase supervision alone, without feeding the phase to the decoder, does not raise F_meter and even lowers it significantly on Syn; the audio-side or score-side Fourier encoding alone each helps significantly, and both together help most. So the benefit comes from conditioning, not from a multitask side effect, consistent with the Rhythm Perceiver's direction. Unfortunately, the plot gives no numbers, so readers cannot judge the magnitudes.

Looking ahead, coordinate estimation on human performances is what most needs work. An oracle coordinate lifts ASAP F_meter from .318 to .518, a larger gap than any architectural change. The authors plan to study how expressive timing and notational conventions affect the coordinate; I think semi-supervised training on downbeats that Beat This! or Explore This! (the tatum-grid model covered here on October 15) produce for real recordings is also worth trying. Another untested question: inference assumes the time signature is fixed within each five-bar excerpt, so what happens with modern works that change meter often?

KunSet: A Corpus and Benchmark for Lyrics Alignment and Rhyme Retrieval in Kunqu Opera

KunSet：崑曲表演語料庫——48 萬個音節層級的歌詞時間標註，連到 1792 年《韻學驪珠》的音韻屬性，並以歌詞對齊與同韻檢索作為基準

Lejun Min, Siqi Chen, Delong Wang, Tong Zheng, Yunqi Lu, Zhishan Zhou, Ke Chen, Yuting Zhong, Junrong Li, Shengran Jin, Beixuan Pei, Jun Chen, Yang Li
arXiv:2610.29031 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-21

為什麼挑這篇：由北京大學藝術學院資助的團隊，從《中國崑曲藝術典藏》選出 281 段演出錄影，做出 482,164 個音節層級的時間標註（覆蓋 106.3 小時），並把每個字連到 1792 年沈乘麐《韻學驪珠》的韻部、聲調、陰陽等屬性。值得讀的是它的品質驗證：兩份從均分初始化、互不知情的盲標，與正式標註之間的起點平均絕對誤差（158.8 與 172.5 ms），跟兩份盲標彼此之間（158.1 ms）幾乎一樣，表示機器預標沒有把標註者帶偏。要保留的是：微調後的對齊模型在唱段上的誤差仍有 462.48 ms，高於人類之間 272–409 ms 的分歧；同韻檢索的 P@10 只有 17.16%，比用現代普通話韻母監督只高 0.81 個百分點。Why this one: A team funded by Peking University's School of Arts takes 281 performance videos from the Kunqu Opera Art Canon and produces 482,164 syllable-level timestamps (covering 106.3 h), linking each character to rhyme group, tone, yin–yang and other attributes from Shen Chenglin's 1792 Yunxue Lizhu. 動機Motivation

崑曲有六百年歷史，2001 年被聯合國教科文組織列為人類口述與非物質遺產代表作，但傳承幾乎完全靠口傳心授，許多劇目與技法已經失傳。分析崑曲表演的基本單位是「字」：崑曲的旋律依字聲而行，每個字又要唱出字頭、字腹、字尾。要跨演出比較這些做法，需要兩樣東西——每個字在錄音裡的起訖時間，以及每個字在傳統音韻學裡的屬性。

現有資源各缺一半。《中國崑曲藝術典藏》保存了 297 段專家選定演出的文本、曲譜與影音；KunquDB 有句子層級的時間戳記與說話者資訊，但沒有字的邊界；《韻學驪珠》已有查原書頁面的數位介面，但屬性不能直接檢索；《九宮大成》線上版（Nine Modes Manual Online）標了聲調與陰陽，卻沒有連到錄音。而在語音或現代歌唱上預訓練的強制對齊模型，碰到崑曲長時間拖腔的字與程式化的發音，表現很差。這篇要一次補上這兩個缺口。

Kunqu has a six-century history and was proclaimed a UNESCO Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity in 2001, yet it has been transmitted almost entirely through oral instruction, and many plays and techniques are already lost. The basic unit for analyzing Kunqu performance is the syllable (one Chinese character): melodies follow the tones of the lyrics, and each syllable is articulated through its head, belly and tail. Comparing these practices across performances requires two things—each syllable's start and end time in the recording, and its attributes in traditional phonology.

Existing resources each cover only half. The Kunqu Opera Art Canon preserves texts, scores and audiovisual records of 297 expert-selected performed excerpts; KunquDB has sentence-level timestamps and speaker metadata but no syllable boundaries; Yunxue Lizhu has a digital interface for source-page lookup, but its attributes are not directly searchable; Nine Modes Manual Online annotates tone and yin–yang but is not linked to recordings. And forced aligners pretrained on speech or modern singing perform poorly on Kunqu's sustained melismatic syllables and stylized pronunciation. The paper aims to close both gaps at once.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最該拿來對照的是 CompMusic 計畫的京劇研究，論文前言沒有提到這條線。Repetto 與 Serra（2014）建了京劇清唱語料，Gong Rong 等人（ISMIR 2017、Interspeech 2018）在京劇上做音節與音素層級的起點偵測與分段，處理的正是戲曲拖腔下的字邊界問題。KunSet 的規模（106.3 小時、48 萬個字）遠大於那些資料集，而且包含伴奏與念白；另一個差別是它連的是傳統韻書，而不是現代拼音。

在歌詞對齊這邊，DALI（Meseguer-Brocal 等，ISMIR 2018）提供西方流行歌的音符與詞層級對齊，Opencpop 等中文歌唱資料集有音素層級標註；論文用的 SOFA 就是在這類現代普通話歌唱上預訓練的對齊器，MFA 則針對語音。論文的「聲學錨點解碼器」是在 SOFA 原本的音素峰值打分上，加一項「每個音素區間內最強證據」的獎勵，再用 O(TS) 的動態規劃精確搜尋——本質上是多了一個峰值項的強制對齊 Viterbi，不是新的對齊框架，而且它只改推論、不改訓練。

同韻檢索則是標準的探測做法：凍結的中文 HuBERT 取均值與標準差池化，接一個小投影頭，用監督式對比學習（Khosla 等，2020）訓練。新的是標籤來源——用傳統韻部而不是現代韻母，論文也自己做了現代韻母的對照組。

The most relevant comparison is CompMusic's Jingju (Beijing opera) research, which the paper's introduction does not mention. Repetto and Serra (2014) built a Jingju a cappella singing corpus, and Rong Gong et al. (ISMIR 2017, Interspeech 2018) performed syllable- and phoneme-level onset detection and segmentation on Jingju, tackling exactly the problem of syllable boundaries under operatic melisma. KunSet is far larger than those datasets (106.3 h, 482K syllables) and includes accompaniment and stage speech; another difference is that it links to a traditional rhyme dictionary rather than modern pinyin.

On lyrics alignment, DALI (Meseguer-Brocal et al., ISMIR 2018) provides note- and word-level alignment for Western pop, and Chinese singing datasets such as Opencpop carry phoneme-level labels; the SOFA aligner used here is pretrained on such modern Mandarin singing, while MFA targets speech. The paper's 'acoustic anchor decoder' adds, on top of SOFA's own phone-peak scoring, a reward for the strongest evidence frame within each phone interval, searched exactly by O(TS) dynamic programming—in essence a forced-alignment Viterbi with an extra peak term, not a new alignment framework, and it changes inference only, not training.

Same-rhyme retrieval is a standard probing setup: frozen Chinese HuBERT with mean and standard-deviation pooling, a small projection head, and supervised contrastive learning (Khosla et al., 2020). What is new is the label source—traditional rhyme groups rather than modern finals—and the paper includes a modern-finals control itself.

方法Method

語料：281 段崑曲演出錄影（720×576、25 fps、44.1 kHz 立體聲），總長 155.19 小時，有歌詞覆蓋的 106.34 小時，其中唱 50.30、念白 55.15、標籤衝突 0.90 小時。階層是「折—句—字」：句有起訖時間、角色、唱或念與文本；字有起訖時間與音韻欄位。共 78,505 句、482,164 個字、4,357 個不同的字。唱句的中位長度 8.81 秒，念白句 2.77 秒。

預標流程：先用 VideOCR 從字幕抓出句子時間與文本，用 Mel-Band RoFormer 分離人聲。從人工標一折開始微調 SOFA 的普通話歌唱權重，模型標出的新折經過修正與審核後再加進訓練集，如此把訓練集從一折擴到七折，再用這個模型預標其餘 274 段。之後由 200 多位受訓標註者在自建的線上系統裡修正，每份再經兩輪不同審核者複查。

音韻資料庫：委託古漢語專家轉錄《韻學驪珠》原刻，轉成 JSONL 與 SQLite，提供反切、聲調、陰陽、韻部、五音、清濁六項，共 10,235 個條目、8,300 個可直接查詢的字，其中 1,664 字有多個條目。每個字保留所有候選，只有在唯一候選或所有候選一致時才填值。結果 95.6% 的字有候選，56.8% 的字六項屬性全部確定。

品質驗證有兩種。一是盲標：在 10 段測試錄音上，以不同的亂數種子把句子起訖挪動 1–3 秒，再把每句均分給各字，產生兩份與機器預標無關的起點，各自修正並經兩輪審核。二是聲學檢查：在全部 281 段的分離人聲上，比較標註字起點與同句隨機時間點前後的頻譜通量（onset strength）與 RMS 能量。

基準一是歌詞對齊：251／20／10 段的錄音層級切分，測試以盲標 A 為參考。比較預訓練 SOFA、七折微調與 251 段微調三種模型，各配原始解碼器或錨點解碼器；251 段模型微調 9,000 步、跑三個種子。指標是 11,735 個字內部邊界的平均絕對誤差（MAE）與 200 ms 內命中率（Acc@200）。

基準二是同韻檢索：只保留傳統韻部與現代韻母都確定的實例，訓練 256,333、驗證 20,674、測試 10,287 個，涵蓋 29 個韻部。以 1,536 維 HuBERT 統計特徵接 1536→256→128 的投影頭；查詢時排除同一個字與同一段錄音的候選，主要指標是依韻部巨觀平均的 P@10。

Corpus: 281 Kunqu performance videos (720×576, 25 fps, 44.1 kHz stereo), 155.19 h in total, 106.34 h covered by lyrics, of which 50.30 h sung, 55.15 h spoken and 0.90 h with conflicting labels. The hierarchy is excerpt–sentence–syllable: sentences carry start/end times, role, delivery mode (sung or spoken) and transcript; syllables carry start/end times and a phonology field. There are 78,505 sentences, 482,164 syllables and 4,357 distinct characters. Median sentence duration is 8.81 s for sung and 2.77 s for spoken lines.

Pre-annotation: VideOCR extracts sentence timestamps and transcripts from subtitles, and Mel-Band RoFormer separates vocals. Starting from one manually annotated excerpt, SOFA's Mandarin singing checkpoint is fine-tuned; newly annotated excerpts are corrected and reviewed before joining the training set, growing it from one to seven excerpts, and that model pre-annotates the remaining 274 recordings. Over 200 trained annotators then correct them in a purpose-built online system, with two further review rounds by different reviewers.

Phonology database: Classical Chinese experts transcribed the original Yunxue Lizhu print, converted to JSONL and SQLite, giving fanqie, tone, yin–yang, rhyme, wuyin and voicing—10,235 entries for 8,300 directly searchable characters, 1,664 of them with multiple entries. Every candidate is kept, and an attribute is filled only when there is a single candidate or all candidates agree. As a result, 95.6% of syllables have candidates and 56.8% have all six attributes resolved.

Quality checks come in two kinds. First, blind annotation: on the 10 test recordings, sentence boundaries are shifted by 1–3 s with different random seeds and each sentence is divided uniformly among its syllables, producing two sets of starting points unrelated to ML pre-annotation, each then corrected and reviewed twice. Second, an acoustic check: on separated vocals across all 281 recordings, onset strength (spectral flux) and RMS energy around annotated syllable starts are compared with random times in the same sentence.

Benchmark one is lyrics alignment, on a recording-level 251/20/10 split with blind annotation A as the test reference. Pretrained SOFA, seven-excerpt fine-tuning and 251-excerpt fine-tuning are compared, each with the original or the anchor decoder; the 251-excerpt model is fine-tuned for 9,000 steps with three seeds. Metrics are mean absolute error (MAE) and Acc@200 (share within 200 ms) over 11,735 internal syllable boundaries.

Benchmark two is same-rhyme retrieval: keeping instances with unambiguous traditional rhyme and modern final labels gives 256,333 train, 20,674 dev and 10,287 test instances across 29 rhyme groups. A 1,536-dim HuBERT statistics feature feeds a 1536→256→128 projection head; queries exclude candidates with the same character or recording, and the primary metric is P@10 macro-averaged over rhyme groups.

限制Limitations

第一，唱段的對齊遠未解決。最好的模型在唱句上 MAE 是 462.48 ms、Acc@200 只有 67.50%，也就是大約三分之一的唱段字邊界誤差超過 200 ms；念白的 MAE 123.77 ms 已經跟人類分歧（108–165 ms）同一個量級。而就連人類自己，唱段上的起點分歧也有 272.1–309.1 ms、終點 354.1–408.5 ms——這份語料在唱段上的「真值」本身就帶著大約三分之一秒的不確定性。論文拿人類分歧與模型誤差比較時，兩者用的邊界集合不同，作者有註明。

第二，測試集只有 10 段錄音，而且同一批錄音也是盲標驗證所用的。三個種子的整體 MAE 標準差 ≤4.5 ms，代表訓練穩定，但無法反映錄音之間的變異；劇目、行當與演員在切分之間是否重疊，論文沒有交代。

第三，音韻屬性是辭典候選，不是演出當下的實際讀音。43.2% 的字至少有一項屬性無法確定，一字多音時只保留候選、沒有依語境消歧。同韻檢索因此衡量的是「錄音能否照韻書分組」，而不是演員實際唱成什麼韻。

第四，同韻檢索的效果小。傳統韻部監督的 P@10 是 17.16%，比現代普通話韻母（16.35%）高 0.81 個百分點（95% 區間 0.47–1.18），比凍結 HuBERT（11.36%）高 5.80 個百分點。作者自己承認評估標籤就是傳統韻部，傳統對現代的優勢要打折扣。29 個韻部下隨機猜的巨觀 P@10 約 3.4%，所以 17% 確實學到了東西，但離實用還遠。

First, alignment of sung lines is far from solved. The best model's sung-line MAE is 462.48 ms with Acc@200 of only 67.50%, so roughly a third of sung syllable boundaries are off by more than 200 ms; the spoken-line MAE of 123.77 ms is already on the order of human disagreement (108–165 ms). Even humans disagree on sung lines by 272.1–309.1 ms at onsets and 354.1–408.5 ms at offsets—the corpus's sung 'ground truth' itself carries about a third of a second of uncertainty. When the paper compares human disagreement with model error, the two use different boundary sets, as the authors note.

Second, the test set is only 10 recordings, the same ones used for the blind-annotation check. The overall-MAE seed SD of ≤4.5 ms shows stable training but says nothing about variation across recordings; whether plays, role types and performers overlap across the split is not reported.

Third, the phonological attributes are dictionary candidates, not the readings actually used in performance. 43.2% of syllables have at least one unresolved attribute, and multi-reading characters keep all candidates without context-based disambiguation. Same-rhyme retrieval therefore measures whether recordings can be grouped according to the rhyme dictionary, not which rhyme the performer actually sang.

Fourth, the retrieval effect is small. Traditional rhyme supervision reaches P@10 of 17.16%, 0.81 points above modern Mandarin finals (16.35%; 95% interval 0.47–1.18) and 5.80 points above frozen HuBERT (11.36%). The authors acknowledge that evaluation uses traditional rhyme labels, so the traditional-over-modern advantage should be discounted. Random guessing gives a macro P@10 of about 3.4% with 29 groups, so 17% does reflect learning, but it is far from practical use.

討論Discussion

這篇最扎實的部分是標註品質的論證，而不是模型。正式標註與兩份盲標的起點 MAE 是 158.8 與 172.5 ms，兩份盲標彼此是 158.1 ms，MAE 之間最多只差 18.14 ms；三組配對也呈現同樣的模式——唱比念難、終點比起點難。盲標從均分初始化、完全不經過機器預標，這個設計直接回答了「ML 預標會不會讓標註者照單全收」的疑慮。聲學檢查的結果一致：標註起點後 0–200 ms 的頻譜通量比隨機時間點高 0.47 個標準差（95% 區間 0.46–0.49），排除句首字後仍有 0.43。

對齊結果的兩個改進是互補的。251 段微調把原始解碼器的 MAE 從 391.30 降到 224.04 ms；錨點解碼器在此之上再降 13.04%，到 194.83 ms，Acc@200 從 79.52% 升到 81.69%。錨點解碼器對未微調的模型也有用（391.30 降到 337.70 ms），而且它不改訓練，任何 SOFA 式模型都能直接套用。七折模型與 251 段模型的差距（同用錨點時 312.90 對 194.83 ms）說明語料規模本身就是主要貢獻。

往下看，我覺得最有意思的不是這兩個基準，而是作者在前言提到、但還沒做的事：拿實際演唱與《韻學驪珠》的規範比對，例如量化不同演員在字頭、字腹、字尾上的時間分配，或檢驗「依字行腔」的程度。這需要比字更細的音素邊界，而目前的音素邊界只是預訓練 SOFA 給的偽標籤。資料、程式與權重僅供研究用途釋出。

The strongest part of the paper is its case for annotation quality, not the models. Onset MAEs between the production annotations and the two blind annotations are 158.8 and 172.5 ms, versus 158.1 ms between the blind annotations themselves, with MAEs differing by at most 18.14 ms; all three pairs also show the same pattern—singing harder than speech, offsets harder than onsets. Because the blind annotations start from uniform division and never pass through ML pre-annotation, this design directly answers the worry that pre-annotation makes annotators accept whatever they are given. The acoustic check agrees: onset strength 0–200 ms after annotated starts exceeds random times by 0.47 standard deviations (95% interval 0.46–0.49), and still by 0.43 after excluding sentence-initial syllables.

The two alignment improvements are complementary. 251-excerpt fine-tuning cuts original-decoder MAE from 391.30 to 224.04 ms; the anchor decoder cuts a further 13.04% to 194.83 ms, raising Acc@200 from 79.52% to 81.69%. The anchor decoder also helps the un-fine-tuned model (391.30 to 337.70 ms), and since it leaves training untouched, any SOFA-style model can use it directly. The gap between the seven-excerpt and 251-excerpt models (312.90 vs 194.83 ms, both with anchors) shows that corpus scale itself is the main contribution.

Looking ahead, the most interesting thing to me is not either benchmark but what the introduction mentions and the paper has not yet done: comparing actual singing against Yunxue Lizhu's norms, for instance quantifying how performers distribute time across a syllable's head, belly and tail, or testing how closely melody follows lyric tone. That needs boundaries finer than the syllable, and the current phone boundaries are only pseudo-labels from pretrained SOFA. Data, code and weights are released for research use only.

動機Motivation

助聽器的設計與評估幾乎都圍繞語音可懂度，但聽音樂是很重要的使用情境。混音與母帶處理過的音樂和語音有兩點不同：動態範圍通常比較窄（製作鏈已經壓縮過好幾次），而且大家聽音樂的音量往往比較大。這兩點都會改變寬動態範圍壓縮（wide dynamic range compression, WDRC）應該做什麼。

商用助聽器常有「音樂模式」，通常是放慢壓縮、減少降噪，但它是否有效，證據並不一致。另一個關鍵變數是聽者的殘餘動態範圍，也就是聽閾（HTL）到不適響度（UCL）之間的距離——感音性聽損會因響度重振（recruitment）與聽覺過敏而把這段縮窄。使用者普遍偏好音樂少壓縮或不壓縮，但能不能做到又不犧牲可聽度，要看殘餘動態範圍。作者要問：需要壓縮的程度如何隨聽損型態、殘餘動態範圍與歌曲動態變化？逐首最佳化參數能不能勝過固定的臨床處方？

Hearing-aid design and evaluation revolve almost entirely around speech intelligibility, yet music listening is an important use case. Mixed and mastered music differs from speech in two ways: its dynamic range is usually narrower (the production chain has already compressed it several times), and people tend to listen to music at higher levels. Both change what wide dynamic range compression (WDRC) ought to do.

Commercial hearing aids often offer a 'music program', typically with slower compression and less noise reduction, but evidence for its effectiveness is inconclusive. Another key variable is the listener's residual dynamic range, the distance between hearing threshold (HTL) and uncomfortable loudness level (UCL)—sensorineural hearing loss narrows it through loudness recruitment and hyperacusis. Users generally prefer little or no compression for music, but whether that is possible without sacrificing audibility depends on residual dynamic range. The authors ask: how does compression demand vary with hearing-loss profile, residual dynamic range and song dynamics? And can per-song parameter optimization beat fixed clinical prescriptions?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

臨床上的基準是 NAL 系列處方：NAL-R（Byrne 與 Dillon，1986）只給線性增益，NAL-NL2（Keidser 等，2011）給非線性壓縮目標，兩者都以語音為設計對象。這篇把兩者都當基線，並為每個基線另外最佳化一個寬頻增益偏移，確保比較的是壓縮行為而不是總音量。音樂與壓縮的實證研究主要來自 Croghan、Arehart 與 Kates（2014）的偏好實驗，以及 Kirchberger 與 Russo（2016）對各曲風動態範圍與聽損者壓縮知覺的測量，這篇的出發點大多取自後者。

最接近的方法是 OPRA-RS（Gonçalves Braz 等，2022）：以自動語音辨識當目標函數，用隨機搜尋調整插入增益與壓縮閾值。這篇做的是同一件事的音樂版——把目標函數換成 HAAQI（Kates 與 Arehart，2016）加上自訂的可聽度與 UCL 遵從度，把隨機搜尋換成 CMA-ES。

論文沒有提到的一條相關線是 Cadenza 挑戰賽（包括 ICASSP 2024 的大挑戰）：它同樣以 HAAQI 為主要客觀指標，請參賽者為聽損者重混或處理音樂，並另外做主觀聽測。Cadenza 處理的是混音或分離後的音軌，這篇只動助聽器端的壓縮器，兩者互補；但 Cadenza 累積的 HAAQI 與聽測對照經驗，對解讀這篇純客觀的結果很重要。

The clinical reference points are the NAL prescriptions: NAL-R (Byrne and Dillon, 1986) gives linear gain only, and NAL-NL2 (Keidser et al., 2011) gives nonlinear compression targets, both designed around speech. This paper uses both as baselines and optimizes a broadband gain offset for each, so that comparisons reflect compression behavior rather than overall level. Empirical work on music and compression comes mainly from the preference experiments of Croghan, Arehart and Kates (2014) and from Kirchberger and Russo (2016), who measured dynamic range across genres and the perception of compression by hearing-impaired listeners; this paper draws most of its premises from the latter.

The closest method is OPRA-RS (Gonçalves Braz et al., 2022), which uses automatic speech recognition as the objective and random search to tune insertion gains and compression thresholds. This paper does the music version of the same thing—replacing the objective with HAAQI (Kates and Arehart, 2016) plus custom audibility and UCL-compliance terms, and random search with CMA-ES.

A related line the paper does not mention is the Cadenza Challenge (including the ICASSP 2024 Grand Challenge): it likewise uses HAAQI as the main objective metric, asks entrants to remix or process music for hearing-impaired listeners, and adds subjective listening tests. Cadenza works on mixed or separated tracks, whereas this paper touches only the hearing-aid compressor, so the two are complementary; but Cadenza's accumulated experience comparing HAAQI with listening tests matters for interpreting this paper's purely objective results.

方法Method

歌曲從 Free Music Archive 挑選：先依曲風過濾、按藝人熱門度排序，再人工確認是主流製作風格。每首歌算兩個動態特徵——1/3 倍頻帶之間的能量加權頻譜擴散（巨觀動態），以及寬頻的峰值對響度比（微觀動態）——兩者排名正規化後平均成「動態度」，從中選出 Tight、Normal、Dynamic 三首，各取最後 20 秒。

聽力圖來自奧登堡聽力健康紀錄（OHHR），有實測的聽閾（到 4 kHz）與 UCL，未測頻率的 UCL 用 Pascoe 模型補。每耳歸到 N1–N5、S1–S3 八種標準聽力圖之一，再依殘餘動態範圍的百分位分成 High、Moderate、Limited 三組，每組取中位點（medoid）當代表。

壓縮效能（CE）指標在 21 個 1/3 倍頻帶（100 Hz 到 10 kHz）上，分慢（400 ms 視窗）與快（20 ms 視窗）兩個時間尺度，算出耳膜處短時 RMS 位準的 0–100 百分位。UE 衡量百分位曲線超出 UCL 的部分、LE 衡量低於聽閾的部分，都以殘餘動態範圍正規化後截到 [0,1]。對頻帶平均得到 GUE（持續位準不超過 UCL）、GLE（持續位準的可聽度）與 TGUE（瞬態峰值不超過 UCL）。第一部分分析在 NAL-R 線性增益加最佳化寬頻偏移下，看哪些條件的 GUE 或 GLE 明顯低於 1。

第二部分挑出 N4 與 S2 兩張殘餘動態範圍受限的聽力圖，用 Optuna 的 CMA-ES 逐首最佳化 1、7、20 通道的 WDRC。每個通道是攻擊 15 ms、釋放 500 ms 的峰值包絡追蹤，靜態特性是兩段硬膝：T1 到 T2 之間壓縮比 R1、T2 以上 R2。插入增益與 T1 固定在 NAL-NL2 目標，攻擊與釋放時間也固定；每通道只放開 T2、R1、R2。多通道版本只在 5 個錨點通道上最佳化、其餘線性內插，加上寬頻偏移共 16 個自由參數；單通道是 4 個。單通道跑 300 次迭代，多通道各 1,200 次。

目標函數是 J = 0.4·HAAQI + 0.4·GLE + 0.2·TGUE − P，其中 GUE<1 時 P = 2(exp(5(1−GUE))−1)，是指數懲罰。權重事先決定、沒有調整。HAAQI 以 NAL-R 線性增益版本為參考，並在評分前略過插入增益，所以只反映壓縮造成的失真。每次試驗在 55 與 90 dB SPL 兩個輸入位準評估後平均。基線是同樣加了最佳化寬頻偏移的 NAL-NL2（G2）與 NAL-R（GR）。

Songs come from the Free Music Archive: filtered by genre, ranked by artist popularity, then manually checked for mainstream production style. Each track gets two dynamics features—energy-weighted spectral spread across 1/3-octave bands (macrodynamics) and broadband peak-to-loudness ratio (microdynamics)—whose rank-normalized mean defines a 'dynamism' score, from which three songs labeled Tight, Normal and Dynamic are chosen, using the final 20 s of each.

Audiograms come from the Oldenburg Hearing Health Record (OHHR), with measured thresholds (up to 4 kHz) and UCLs; UCLs at unmeasured frequencies are estimated with the Pascoe model. Each ear is assigned to one of eight standard audiograms, N1–N5 and S1–S3, then split into High, Moderate and Limited groups by residual-dynamic-range percentile, with each group's medoid as its representative.

The compression effectiveness (CE) metric computes 0–100 percentiles of short-time RMS levels at the eardrum in 21 1/3-octave bands (100 Hz to 10 kHz), on a slow (400 ms window) and a fast (20 ms window) timescale. UE measures how far the percentile curve exceeds the UCL and LE how far it falls below threshold, both normalized by residual dynamic range and clipped to [0,1]. Band averages give GUE (sustained levels within UCL), GLE (audibility of sustained levels) and TGUE (transient peaks within UCL). The first analysis applies NAL-R linear gain plus an optimized broadband offset and looks for conditions where GUE or GLE falls clearly below 1.

The second part picks two audiograms with limited residual dynamic range, N4 and S2, and uses Optuna's CMA-ES to optimize 1-, 7- and 20-channel WDRC per song. Each channel uses a peak envelope follower with 15 ms attack and 500 ms release and a two-segment hard-knee static curve: ratio R1 between T1 and T2, R2 above T2. Insertion gain and T1 are fixed to NAL-NL2 targets, as are attack and release; only T2, R1 and R2 are free per channel. Multi-channel versions optimize five anchor channels and interpolate the rest linearly, giving 16 free parameters with the broadband offset; single-channel has 4. Single-channel runs 300 iterations, multi-channel 1,200 each.

The objective is J = 0.4·HAAQI + 0.4·GLE + 0.2·TGUE − P, where P = 2(exp(5(1−GUE))−1) when GUE<1, an exponential penalty. The weights are fixed a priori, not tuned. HAAQI uses the NAL-R linear-gain version as its reference, with insertion gain bypassed before scoring, so it reflects only compression-induced distortion. Each trial is evaluated at 55 and 90 dB SPL input levels and averaged. Baselines are NAL-NL2 (G2) and NAL-R (GR), each also given an optimized broadband offset.

限制Limitations

第一，規模極小。三首 20 秒的歌、兩張代表聽力圖，就要支撐「歌曲動態是一階因素」這種一般性結論。每種動態度只有一首歌，曲風、樂器配置與動態度完全混在一起；論文說「GLE 跨訊號差距可達 0.15」，但這是三個樣本的極差。

第二，評估是循環的。HAAQI 同時是最佳化目標（權重 0.4）與報告指標，而且是在同一段 20 秒訊號上最佳化、在同一段上評分，沒有任何留出的歌曲。作者把結果定位成「這個參數空間可達到的上限」，這個說法是誠實的；但「平均提升 0.12」就應該讀成上限，而不是可部署的增益。

第三，HAAQI 的參考訊號就是 NAL-R 線性增益版本，所以 GR 的 HAAQI 在每個條件下都恰好是 1.00，任何壓縮在結構上都只會扣分。作者自己也指出這「結構上偏好單通道處理」。換句話說，HAAQI 在這裡衡量的是「跟不壓縮有多像」，而不是「聽損者聽起來多好」。

第四，摘要說的「改善 HAAQI 的同時也改善可聽度」並非每一格都成立。平均 0.12 的 HAAQI 提升只來自多頻帶壓縮器（範圍 0.03–0.22）；單通道最佳化後 HAAQI 比 G2 低最多 0.05（例如 N4 Dynamic 從 0.92 降到 0.87）。可聽度也有反例：N4 的 WDRC-20 在 Tight 上 GLE 從 0.95 降到 0.93。全篇沒有任何主觀聽測，作者在結論中明說這是必要的下一步。

First, the scale is tiny. Three 20-s songs and two representative audiograms are asked to support a general conclusion that song dynamics are a first-order factor. Each dynamism level has a single song, so genre, instrumentation and dynamism are fully confounded; the paper's 'GLE spread across signals can reach 0.15' is the range of three samples.

Second, the evaluation is circular. HAAQI is both an optimization target (weight 0.4) and the reported metric, and parameters are optimized and scored on the same 20-s signal, with no held-out songs. The authors frame the results as the achievable ceiling within this parameter space, which is honest; but the '0.12 average improvement' should then be read as a ceiling, not a deployable gain.

Third, HAAQI's reference signal is the NAL-R linear-gain version itself, so GR scores exactly 1.00 in every condition and any compression can only lose points by construction. The authors note that this structurally favors single-channel processing. In other words, HAAQI here measures how close the output is to no compression, not how good it sounds to a hearing-impaired listener.

Fourth, the abstract's claim of improving HAAQI while also improving audibility does not hold in every cell. The 0.12 average HAAQI gain comes only from multi-band compressors (range 0.03–0.22); single-channel optimization lowers HAAQI below G2 by up to 0.05 (e.g., N4 Dynamic from 0.92 to 0.87). Audibility has counterexamples too: N4 WDRC-20 on Tight drops GLE from 0.95 to 0.93. There is no subjective listening test at all, which the authors call the essential next step in their conclusion.

討論Discussion

第一部分的發現其實比最佳化更有用：在 NAL-R 線性增益下，輕度聽損且殘餘動態範圍充足時（例如 N1、N2、S1），不論哪首歌，GUE 與 GLE 都高於 0.98；也就是說，在評估過的輕中度聽損條件下，製作鏈已經壓縮過的音樂不需要助聽器再壓縮。要到 N4、N5 加上受限的動態範圍、再碰上動態豐富的歌，才惡化到 GUE 0.86、GLE 0.75。GUE 在不同歌之間只差 0.04，GLE 卻差到 0.15，作者的解釋是母帶處理把峰值壓得差不多，但安靜段落的位準因曲風而異。這個「峰值相似、谷底不同」的觀察很合理，也指出音樂模式該關心的是可聽度，而不是削峰。

第二部分的訊息是：照 NAL-NL2 語音目標設定的多頻帶壓縮器用在音樂上失真很大，HAAQI 只有 0.71–0.84，單通道版本則有 0.90–0.99。逐首最佳化能把多頻帶的 HAAQI 拉回 0.81–0.94。增益偏移與壓縮參數分工清楚：HAAQI 的改善全部來自壓縮參數，GLE 的改善大多來自增益偏移，平坦型的 N4 佔 79%、斜坡型的 S2 佔 50%。斜坡型聽損最需要細的頻率解析度：S2 上 WDRC-20 在三首歌的 GLE 是 0.92、0.94、0.84，平均比 WDRC-1 高 0.06。

這篇對 MIR 的意義在於「訊號感知」這個方向：如果助聽器能即時估計正在播放的音樂有多「動態」，就能決定要不要壓縮、壓多少。這跟音樂資訊檢索裡的響度與動態範圍估計（例如 EBU R128 的響度範圍 LRA）是同一類工具。下一步顯然是聽測，以及把攻擊與釋放時間納入最佳化——這篇刻意固定了它們，而音樂壓縮的失真有很大一部分正是來自時間常數。

The first part's finding is actually more useful than the optimization: under NAL-R linear gain, for mild loss with ample residual dynamic range (e.g., N1, N2, S1), GUE and GLE stay above 0.98 for every song; that is, within the mild-to-moderate conditions evaluated, music already compressed by the production chain needs no further compression from the hearing aid. Only N4 and N5 with limited dynamic range, combined with a dynamically rich song, degrade to GUE 0.86 and GLE 0.75. GUE varies across songs by only 0.04 while GLE varies by up to 0.15, which the authors attribute to mastering compressing peaks similarly while quiet-passage levels vary by genre. This 'similar peaks, different troughs' observation is plausible and indicates that a music program should care about audibility rather than peak limiting.

The second part's message is that multi-band compressors set to NAL-NL2 speech targets distort music heavily, with HAAQI of only 0.71–0.84 versus 0.90–0.99 for the single-channel version. Per-song optimization brings multi-band HAAQI back to 0.81–0.94. Gain offset and compression parameters play distinct roles: all HAAQI improvement comes from compression parameters, while most GLE improvement comes from the gain offset—79% for flat N4 and 50% for sloping S2. Sloping loss benefits most from fine frequency resolution: on S2, WDRC-20 reaches GLE of 0.92, 0.94 and 0.84 on the three songs, 0.06 above WDRC-1 on average.

For MIR, the paper's significance lies in the signal-aware direction: if a hearing aid could estimate in real time how dynamic the music being played is, it could decide whether and how much to compress. That is the same family of tools as loudness and dynamic-range estimation in music information retrieval (e.g., EBU R128 loudness range, LRA). The obvious next steps are listening tests and including attack and release times in the optimization—the paper deliberately fixes them, and much of the distortion in music compression comes precisely from time constants.
