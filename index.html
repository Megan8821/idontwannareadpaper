A State-Space Model of Figured-Bass Realization: Local Constraints, Coupled Voices, and Polynomial-Time Solvability

數字低音實現的狀態空間模型:局部約束、聲部耦合與多項式時間可解性

Evan Unit Lim
arXiv:2609.19397 · cs.SD, cs.DS · 投稿submitted 2026-09-16
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把數字低音四聲部實現(和聲學教材裡的規範練習)形式化成一個逐拍狀態轉移的圖搜尋問題,證明在聲部數固定、音域為有限離散音高集合、規則只看相鄰固定數量和絃的前提下,可行性判定與最低成本實現都是多項式時間可解(操作次數 O(nM^6),M 為每聲部候選音高數;聲部數與音高集合固定時降到 O(n));八拍示範例子窮舉出 14 條可行路徑、最低總成本 48,驗證動態規劃找到的解跟窮舉搜尋一致。Why this one: Formalizes four-part figured-bass realization (a standard music-theory exercise) as a graph search over beat-by-beat voicing states, proving that with a fixed number of voices, a finite discrete pitch domain per voice, and rules that only look at a fixed number of adjacent chords, both feasibility and minimum-cost realization are solvable in polynomial time (O(nM^6) operations, M being the per-voice pitch domain size; O(n) once voice count and pitch alphabet are fixed); an eight-beat worked example exhaustively finds 14 feasible complete paths with minimum total cost 48, matching what the dynamic program returns.

動機Motivation

數字低音實現是和聲學教材裡的標準練習:給定低音與數字記號,學生要填出中音、次高音與最高音三個聲部,同時遵守音域、聲部排列、平行五度/八度等規則。這是國際樂理考試的核心項目(如 ABRSM 六級、AP Music Theory、加拿大皇家音樂學院 ARCT、AMEB),長期以來卻沒有嚴謹的計算複雜度分析——多聲部之間彼此耦合(某一拍的選擇會限制下一拍能不能延續),直覺上像是組合爆炸問題。

作者的核心論點是:聲部之間有耦合,不代表這個問題本身就難解;真正決定計算複雜度的是另外兩個因素——狀態需要記住多少資訊(記憶體),以及規則往前或往後看幾個和絃(規則範圍)。只要這兩者有界,即使聲部彼此耦合,問題仍然可以在多項式時間內解決。

Figured-bass realization is a standard music-theory exercise: given a bass line with figures, students fill in three upper voices (alto, tenor, soprano) while obeying range, voice-spacing, and parallel-fifth/octave rules. It is central to major international theory exams (ABRSM Grade 6, AP Music Theory, the Royal Conservatory's ARCT, AMEB), yet has lacked a rigorous complexity analysis -- coupling between voices, where one beat's choice constrains what the next beat can do, looks combinatorially explosive.

The paper's core claim is that coupling alone does not make the problem hard; what actually decides tractability is how much information a state must remember and how many adjacent chords a rule can inspect. Bounding both keeps the problem solvable in polynomial time even with coupled voices.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

先前處理數字低音或聖詠和聲化的系統大致三種取向。CHORAL(Ebcioglu, 1986)用多層次的邏輯規則加回溯搜尋與音樂學經驗法則,生成巴赫風格聖詠;Pachet 與 Roy(2001)把和聲化問題表述成有限值域的約束滿足問題,把會互相牽動的音符打包成統一的狀態變數;DeepBach(Hadjeres 等人,2017)則是從巴赫聖詠語料學出一個模型,用偽 Gibbs 取樣在使用者給定的約束下生成聲部。

這篇論文跟三者的差別在於企圖心不同:CHORAL 與 DeepBach 都是要生成「聽起來像巴赫」的和聲化結果,追求風格逼真度;這篇論文完全不處理風格,只處理一個限縮過的教材式練習(考試規則下的四聲部實現),目標是給出可以嚴格證明的複雜度界限,而不是產生更好聽的和聲。跟 Pachet 與 Roy 的約束滿足表述法相比,這篇的貢獻是把「用完整聲部組合當狀態」這件事嚴格形式化,並第一次針對這類問題證明多項式時間可解,而不是止於「這是個約束滿足問題」的一般性描述。

Prior work on figured-bass or chorale harmonization falls into three camps. CHORAL (Ebcioglu, 1986) uses multi-level logical rules with backtracking and musicological heuristics to generate Bach-style chorales; Pachet and Roy (2001) cast harmonization as a finite-domain constraint-satisfaction problem, grouping mutually-constraining notes into a single state variable; DeepBach (Hadjeres et al., 2017) instead learns from a corpus of Bach chorales and generates voices via pseudo-Gibbs sampling under user-supplied constraints.

This paper's ambition differs from all three: CHORAL and DeepBach both aim to produce output that sounds like Bach, chasing stylistic fidelity, while this paper ignores style entirely and targets a narrower, exam-style four-part exercise, aiming for a provable complexity bound rather than better-sounding harmony. Compared with Pachet and Roy's constraint-satisfaction framing, the contribution here is formalizing "complete voicings as states" rigorously enough to prove, for the first time, that the problem is polynomial-time solvable -- going beyond the general observation that it is a constraint-satisfaction problem.

方法Method

模型把每個音高表示成拼寫三元組(音名字母、變化記號、八度),分別記錄半音位置、音階位置與拼寫類別,確保像重降或重升這類不常見的變化記號也不會被錯誤地當成別的音,這是避免同音異名混淆的關鍵。四聲部在某一拍的完整組合叫做一個「聲部狀態」,其中低音由題目給定,中音、次高音、最高音三個聲部才是要填的答案。每一拍內部要檢查音域、聲部不可交叉、聲部間距不超過八度、只能用允許的和絃音與加倍模式、導音最多出現一次等規則,只要有一項不合格,這個狀態的懲罰就設成無限大,合格的狀態才留在「合法狀態集合」裡。

相鄰兩拍之間還要再檢查一次:每個聲部只能用允許的級進或小跳,不能出現增減音程;不能有聲部交越或聲部間的音域重疊;六個聲部兩兩配對都要檢查有沒有出現平行五度或平行八度;外聲部以同向進行到完全音程時,只有在最高音是級進的情況下才允許;導音在某些和絃進行下要求必須解決到主音。這些規則同樣是不合格就罰無限大,合格才能當作一條合法的轉移邊,邊的權重則是三個上方聲部音高變動量的絕對值總和(低音是題目給的,不計入這個代價)。

作者把每一拍的合法狀態當成圖上的一層,合法狀態之間合法的轉移當成邊,邊權重是移動代價,整首曲子的可行實現就對應圖上一條從起點到終點的路徑,路徑總權重就是總移動代價,這一步證明了「可行的完整實現」和「圖上的有限路徑」是一一對應的。核心定理指出:只要聲部數固定、每個聲部的候選音高數量是有限的常數 M、規則只往前或往後看固定數量的相鄰和絃,可行性判定與最低成本實現都可以用動態規劃在多項式時間內解出,操作次數為 O(nM^6)(n 是拍數);若音高集合與規則範圍也都固定,則進一步降到 O(n)。

作者用三個手算範例驗證這套理論:四拍範例(I–IV–V–I)窮舉後找到 2 條完整可行路徑,最低總成本 12;八拍範例(I–vi–ii6–V–I6–IV–V–I)則有 14 條完整可行路徑,最低總成本 48,而且範例特別示範了「貪心法會失敗」的情況——第二拍成本較低的選擇(代價 2)其實走不到完整解,只有代價較高的選擇(代價 14)才能延伸出完整路徑,必須靠動態規劃保留多個候選前綴才找得到最優解。

Each pitch is represented as a spelled triple (letter, accidental, octave), from which the model derives semitone position, diatonic position, and a spelling class, so that unusual accidentals like double sharps are never silently reinterpreted as a different pitch, avoiding enharmonic confusion. A complete four-voice combination at one beat is a "voicing state"; the bass is supplied by the exercise, while alto, tenor, and soprano are what must be filled in. Within a single beat, the model checks each voice's range, voice-crossing, at most an octave of spacing between upper voices, membership in allowed chord tones and doubling patterns, and at most one leading tone; any violation sets that state's penalty to infinity, and only zero-penalty states survive into the "legal state" set.

Between adjacent beats, the model re-checks: each voice may only move by an allowed step or small leap, never an augmented or diminished interval; no voice-crossing or range overlap between voices; every one of the six voice pairs is checked for parallel fifths or octaves; similar motion into a perfect interval between the outer voices is allowed only when the soprano moves by step; and an active leading tone must resolve to the tonic under specified chord progressions. Any violation again incurs an infinite penalty, and a legal transition's edge weight is the summed absolute pitch movement of the three upper voices (the bass, being given, is excluded from this cost).

The legal states at each beat form one layer of a graph, legal transitions become edges weighted by movement cost, and a feasible complete realization corresponds exactly to a source-to-sink path, with total path weight equal to total movement cost, establishing a bijection between feasible realizations and finite graph paths. The main theorem shows that with a fixed number of voices, a constant per-voice pitch domain of size M, and rules that look at only a fixed number of adjacent chords, both feasibility and minimum-cost realization are solvable by dynamic programming in polynomial time, at O(nM^6) operations for n beats; fixing the pitch alphabet and rule scope as well brings this down to O(n).

Three hand-worked examples verify the theory: a four-beat I-IV-V-I example finds exactly 2 feasible complete paths with minimum total cost 12, while an eight-beat I-vi-ii6-V-I6-IV-V-I example finds 14 feasible complete paths with minimum total cost 48. The eight-beat example specifically demonstrates why a greedy strategy fails: the cheaper choice at beat 2 (cost 2) turns out to have no viable continuation to a complete solution, while the costlier choice (cost 14) is the one that extends to a full path -- only dynamic programming, which keeps multiple candidate prefixes alive, finds this optimum.

限制Limitations

作者自己列出這套模型的適用邊界。它處理的是「數字已經解讀完畢之後」的實現問題,不處理如何把任意的數字低音記號本身解讀成和絃;它只針對「考試規範式」的四聲部實現,不主張能捕捉到所有的音樂判斷,也不宣稱跟巴赫的寫作風格等價,更沒有做任何聽感偏好的驗證。不同考試機構(ABRSM、AP、ARCT、AMEB)的規則細節不同,論文並不主張自己涵蓋所有考試的規則,只主張換一套規則謂詞就能套用同一個圖結構;範例裡也刻意省略了休止符、裝飾音、掛留音,並且只用和絃音,沒有加入非和絃音或不協和音的處理,除非額外加規則。換句話說,這是一個刻意窄化過的模型,複雜度證明的適用範圍跟它處理的音樂內容一樣窄。

The author lists the model's own boundaries. It handles realization only after the figures have already been interpreted, not the interpretation of arbitrary figured-bass notation itself; it targets only "exam-style" four-part realization, without claiming to capture every musical judgment, without asserting stylistic equivalence to Bach, and without any validation against listener preference. Different exam boards (ABRSM, AP, ARCT, AMEB) use different rule details, and the paper does not claim to cover every board's rules, only that swapping in a different set of rule predicates reuses the same graph structure. The worked examples also deliberately omit rests, ornaments, and suspensions, and use chord tones only, with no non-chord-tone or dissonance handling unless explicitly added. In short, this is a deliberately narrowed model, and the complexity proof's scope is exactly as narrow as the musical content it handles.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最主要的理論結果是:在聲部數固定、每聲部候選音高數為常數 M、規則只看固定範圍的相鄰和絃這三個條件下,數字低音實現的可行性判定與最低成本實現都可以用動態規劃在多項式時間解決,操作次數為 O(nM^6);如果連音高字母集合與規則範圍都固定住,複雜度可以進一步降到 O(n)。但這個多項式結果是「對固定聲部數 k 成立」——如果聲部數本身可以變動,原本的六次方(一般寫成 M 的 2(k−1) 次方)就會隨聲部數指數成長,代表這個問題本身其實對聲部數量敏感,只是四聲部這個特例剛好落在多項式範圍內。

手算範例把理論跟實際搜尋空間的大小連在一起看:四拍範例每拍分別有 10、8、8、10 個合法的垂直狀態,但從固定起點往前展開後,真正可達的狀態數只剩 1、2、2、2 個,最終窮舉出剛好 2 條完整可行路徑,最低總成本 12;八拍範例的合法狀態數更多,展開後每層可達狀態數是 1、2、5、4、5、7、5、5,最終有 14 條完整可行路徑,最低總成本 48。八拍範例特別設計了一個「貪心法失敗」的陷阱:第二拍看起來比較便宜的選擇(代價 2)其實走到死路,真正能延伸成完整解的是代價較高的選擇(代價 14),這說明為什麼要用動態規劃保留多個候選前綴,而不能每拍只留一個看起來最好的選擇。三個範例的窮舉結果都跟動態規劃找到的解一致,間接驗證了演算法的正確性。

The paper's central theoretical result is that under three conditions -- a fixed number of voices, a constant per-voice pitch domain of size M, and rules that only look at a fixed range of adjacent chords -- feasibility and minimum-cost figured-bass realization are both solvable by dynamic programming in polynomial time, at O(nM^6) operations; fixing the pitch alphabet and rule scope too brings this down to O(n). But this polynomial result holds only for a fixed voice count k -- if the number of voices itself can vary, the sixth-power factor (generally M^(2(k-1))) grows exponentially in k, meaning the problem is genuinely sensitive to voice count, and four voices just happens to fall inside the polynomial regime.

The hand-worked examples connect the theory to actual search-space size: the four-beat example has 10, 8, 8, 10 legal vertical states per beat, but forward reachability from the fixed opening trims this to only 1, 2, 2, 2 states, yielding exactly 2 feasible complete paths at minimum cost 12; the eight-beat example has more legal states, with 1, 2, 5, 4, 5, 7, 5, 5 forward-reachable states per layer, yielding 14 feasible complete paths at minimum cost 48. The eight-beat example specifically sets a greedy trap: the seemingly cheaper choice at beat 2 (cost 2) is a dead end, while the costlier choice (cost 14) is the one that extends into a complete solution -- exactly why dynamic programming must keep multiple candidate prefixes alive rather than greedily keeping only the best-looking one per beat. All three examples' exhaustive results match what the dynamic program finds, cross-validating correctness.

Encypher: Shared Agency and Social Presence in Collaborative Music Generation for Dance Cyphers

Encypher:即興圈中協作式音樂生成的共享能動性與社會臨場感

Zhixing Chen, Cheng-Zhi Anna Huang
arXiv:2609.18062 · cs.HC, cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-17
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Encypher 用深度攝影機即時追蹤群體舞者的軀幹擺動幅度與空間隊形,把「群體能量」轉成 LyriaRT 文字轉音樂模型的提示權重與生成溫度/Top-K,讓即興饒舌圈(cypher)裡的音樂即時回應整個房間而非單一舞者;11 人的舞蹈課程研究裡,81.8% 受訪者同意這套系統提升了協作感,90.9% 同意空間覺察提升,但也有 45.5% 對「投入感」表示中立,顯示效果並非全面一致。Why this one: Encypher uses a depth camera to track a group of dancers' torso-sway amplitude and spatial formation in real time, turning "collective energy" into prompt weights and generation temperature/top-k for the LyriaRT text-to-music model, so the music in a Hip-Hop cypher responds to the whole room rather than one dancer; in an 11-person movement-class study, 81.8% agreed the system increased collaboration and 90.9% agreed it increased spatial awareness, though 45.5% were neutral on "engagement," showing the effect was not uniformly strong.

動機Motivation

生成式音樂系統在人機互動研究裡幾乎都是為單一表演者設計的——一個人做動作,系統即時生成對應的音樂。但音樂與舞蹈原本就是社會性的活動,尤其像嘻哈文化裡的即興圈(cypher)這種形式:舞者圍成一圈輪流即興,重點是能量的分享與群體回應,而不是單一舞者的個人表現。這篇論文要問的是:如果把生成式音樂系統的控制權從「個人」搬到「群體」,音樂能不能反過來變成凝聚陌生人、催生群體歸屬感的媒介?

作者的前作 Con Moto 用符號化的 MIDI 生成,讓兩位舞者精細控制音樂的個別聲部,適合排練過的雙人編舞。但這篇論文要處理的場景不一樣:cypher 裡的參與者往往互不相識,也沒有事先排練,系統必須能同時處理任意人數、任意即興動作,而且要讓每個人都感覺自己是「群體能量」的一部分,而不是被單獨挑出來控制音樂的人。這個從個人控制到群體共享能動性的轉向,才是這篇論文真正要驗證的假設。

Generative music systems in HCI research are almost always built for a solo performer -- one person moves, the system generates matching music in response. But music and dance are inherently social, especially in forms like the Hip-Hop cypher, where dancers form a circle and take turns improvising, with the point being shared energy and group response rather than one dancer's individual display. This paper asks whether shifting a generative music system's control from the individual to the group can turn music into a medium that builds connection and a sense of belonging among strangers.

The authors' earlier system, Con Moto, used symbolic MIDI generation letting two dancers finely control individual musical parts, suited to rehearsed duet choreography. This paper targets a different setting: cypher participants are often strangers with no rehearsal, so the system must handle any number of people doing unplanned improvisation, while making everyone feel part of the "group energy" rather than singled out as the one controlling the music. This shift from individual control to shared group agency is the actual hypothesis under test.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂與社會互動這條線上,前人研究(如 Tarr 等人 2015、Weinstein 等人 2016)已經證明同步跳舞會透過腦內啡機制強化群體連結,這篇論文採用一套涵蓋社會臨場感、群體凝聚力、互動風格、引導與邀請的整合框架來評估系統。技術輔助協作這條線上,reacTable(Jorda 等人,2005)讓新手可以圍著圓桌共同做音樂,是「多人共同操作單一介面」的早期代表,但 reacTable 靠的是實體轉盤這種受限的操作介面。動作聲音化這條線上,最接近的先前系統是 C-Battle(Grebel 等人,2026):用智慧型手機的手勢辨識,把嘻哈舞蹈詞彙對應到 Ableton 裡預先做好的音樂迴圈,發現舞者會把聲音回饋當成一種驗證或探索的過程。

Encypher 跟 C-Battle 的差別很具體:感測方式不同(深度攝影機 vs. 穿戴式手機)、音樂產生方式不同(即時神經網路生成 vs. 播放預先做好的迴圈)、使用情境的定位也不同(cypher 的能量分享 vs. battle 的對抗競技)。動作轉音樂的神經生成這條線上,前人系統多半用 RAVE 做低延遲音色轉換,或用擴散模型換取更好的節奏對齊但犧牲即時性;作者自己的前作 Con Moto 則是用即時 MIDI transformer,在推論階段就能調整人類意圖與 AI 生成之間的能動性分配。Encypher 跟 Con Moto 最大的差別在於:Con Moto 處理的是兩人之間的能動性分配,Encypher 要處理的是群體層級的社會動態,這是規模與問題性質上的擴展,不是單純換一個生成模型而已。

On music and social interaction, prior work (Tarr et al. 2015, Weinstein et al. 2016) has shown synchronized dancing strengthens group bonds via endorphin-driven mechanisms, and this paper adopts an integrated framework covering social presence, group cohesion, interaction style, facilitation, and invitation to evaluate the system. On technology-facilitated collaboration, the reacTable (Jorda et al., 2005) let novices make music together around a circular table, an early example of "multiple people operating one interface," but it relies on a constrained physical-disc interface. On movement sonification, the closest prior system is C-Battle (Grebel et al., 2026): smartphone gesture recognition mapping Hip-Hop movement vocabulary onto pre-made Ableton loops, finding that dancers treated the sonic feedback as validation or exploration.

Encypher's differences from C-Battle are concrete: sensing modality (depth camera vs. wearable smartphones), music production (real-time neural generation vs. playing pre-made loops), and framing (a cypher's shared energy vs. a battle's competition). On neural motion-to-music generation, prior systems mostly use RAVE for low-latency timbre transfer, or diffusion models that trade real-time responsiveness for better rhythmic alignment; the authors' own earlier system, Con Moto, used a real-time MIDI transformer that could adjust the balance of agency between human intent and AI generation at inference time. Encypher's key difference from Con Moto is scope: Con Moto handles agency-sharing between two people, while Encypher handles group-level social dynamics -- a scaling-up of the problem itself, not just a swapped generative model.

方法Method

系統由三個部分組成:處理 LyriaRT API 呼叫、音樂生成與視覺化的網頁介面;架在 2.2 公尺高、俯角 30 度的深度攝影機(ZED 2i),以每秒 30 幀跑骨架追蹤;以及作為動作資料、音樂控制與燈光中樞的 Max/MSP patch。生成模型用的是 LyriaRT——一個以兩秒為單位、參考過去十秒脈絡持續生成音訊串流的編解碼式語言模型,訓練資料規模比開放權重的 MagentaRT 更大,音質更好,但相對犧牲了精細控制,不像符號化 MIDI 生成那樣可以逐音符調整。

動作到音樂的映射有兩條主線。第一條是「能量驅動的提示詞導引」:用兩秒滾動窗口內軀幹質心在 X、Y 方向標準差的 L2 範數當作「軀幹擺動幅度」(COMAmp),數值介於 0 到 5、用 α=0.3 做指數平滑,每位舞者的 COMAmp 對應到自己被指定的提示詞權重,所有人的能量加總則塑造整體生成方向。第二條是「溫度與 Top-K 控制」:把六個固定站位區的人數分布(隊形的「圓形程度」)跟生成的多樣性參數綁在一起——舞者聚成緊密的圈,音樂就趨向連貫可預期;舞者分散開來,音樂就趨向隨機——這個設計刻意用音樂的一致性去獎勵舞者靠攏成圈,把空間隊形本身變成一種誘因。

系統經過五週跟當地 breaking 舞團(15 人)共同設計調整出幾個關鍵決定。動作詞彙上,原本用肢體末端速度做映射感覺不自然,因為 breaker 的律動主要靠身體搖擺而非銳利的肢體動作,因此改用 COMAmp;延遲上,LyriaRT 固有的兩秒生成延遲會打斷「動作馬上有回應」的預期,共同設計出「蓄能」機制——舞者累積能量到閾值才觸發音樂變化,把技術限制轉成有意義的互動節奏;風格一致性上,任意搭配的提示詞會生成不連貫的音樂,後來改成用 JSON 預設檔限定樂器組合相容的單一曲風;控制粒度上,原本用手腳速度對應溫度與 Top-K 會產生難以預期的變化,後來發現用隊形圓形程度反而更有效,顯示優先照顧空間凝聚力比照顧個人精細控制更適合這個場景。

The system has three parts: a web interface handling LyriaRT API calls, music generation, and visualization; a depth camera (ZED 2i) mounted at 2.2m and angled 30 degrees downward, running skeleton tracking at 30 fps; and a Max/MSP patch acting as the hub for motion data, music control, and lighting. The generative model is LyriaRT, a codec-based language model that continuously generates audio in two-second chunks conditioned on ten seconds of past context, trained on a larger dataset than the open-weights MagentaRT, giving better audio quality at the cost of fine-grained control compared to symbolic MIDI generation.

Motion-to-music mapping has two main lines. First, energy-driven prompt steering: torso center-of-mass amplitude (COMAmp), the L2 norm of the standard deviations of X/Y position over a rolling two-second window, scaled 0-5 and smoothed with alpha=0.3, maps each dancer's value to their own assigned prompt weight, while the group's aggregate energy shapes overall generation. Second, temperature and top-k control: how tightly dancers occupy six fixed floor zones (the formation's "circularity") is coupled to generation-diversity parameters -- a tight circle pushes the music toward coherence and predictability, while scattered dancers push it toward randomness -- deliberately using musical coherence to reward dancers for forming a circle, turning spatial formation itself into an incentive.

Five weeks of co-design with a local breaking crew (n=15) produced several key decisions. On movement vocabulary, mapping limb-tip velocity felt unnatural, since breakers groove mainly through body sway rather than sharp limb movements, so the design switched to COMAmp; on latency, LyriaRT's inherent two-second generation delay disrupted the expected immediacy of action-response, so the team co-designed a "charging" mechanic where dancers build energy to a threshold before triggering a musical change, turning a technical constraint into a meaningful interaction rhythm; on genre coherence, arbitrarily combined prompts produced incoherent music, so the design switched to JSON presets locking in one genre with compatible instruments; and on control granularity, mapping limb velocity to temperature and top-k produced unpredictable variation, while formation circularity turned out to work better, showing that prioritizing spatial cohesion over fine individual control suited this setting.

限制Limitations

作者自己列出幾個明確的限制。使用者研究只有 11 人、單一場次,資料來自自陳問卷與作者觀察,而且「有無螢幕」這個條件跟分組順序、地板膠帶標記同時變動,沒辦法乾淨地拆解出螢幕本身的效果;原本設計要跟「預先選好的播放清單」和「真人 DJ」做對照組比較,後來認為條件還不成熟而放棄,所以沒有基準線可以比較。

LyriaRT 是 closed-access 模型,生成結果沒辦法完全重現,也沒有跟開放權重的 MagentaRT 做對照;COMAmp 這套映射是針對 breaking 這個舞種特別設計的,能不能套用到其他舞種本身沒有驗證過。此外,LyriaRT 兩秒的生成延遲會打斷因果回應的預期,而且文字提示對節拍(BPM)鎖定並不可靠,某些曲風與樂器組合會直接拒絕鎖定速度,這對需要精確跟拍的 breaking 是明顯的限制。系統對超過 15 人的場景與嚴重的人身遮蔽也沒有做穩健性驗證。

The authors flag several explicit limits. The user study had only 11 participants in a single session, relying on self-report and author observation, and the "screen vs. no-screen" condition varied together with group order and floor-tape marking, so the screen's own effect cannot be cleanly isolated; an original plan to compare against a pre-selected playlist and a live DJ was abandoned as premature, leaving no baseline condition.

LyriaRT is a closed-access model, so outputs are not fully reproducible, and it was not compared against the open-weights MagentaRT; the COMAmp mapping was designed specifically for breaking, and whether it transfers to other dance forms is untested. LyriaRT's two-second generation latency also disrupts the expected immediacy of response, and text-prompt-based BPM locking is unreliable, with some genre-instrument combinations simply refusing to lock tempo -- a clear limitation for breaking, which depends on precise timing. The system's robustness was also not verified beyond about 15 people or under heavy occlusion.

討論Discussion

舞蹈課程使用者研究(11 人)裡,量化結果呈現明顯的正負分布:空間覺察同意比例最高,達 90.9%(其中 36.4% 強烈同意);協作感同意比例 81.8%;鼓勵感與享受感都是 72.7%。但投入感只有 54.5% 同意,同時有 45.5% 的受訪者選擇中立,是九個面向裡中立比例最高的一項;社會臨場感的同意比例也偏低,只有 54.6%,還有 18.2% 不同意、27.3% 中立或立場混雜。這代表系統確實讓多數人感覺協作與空間互相覺察有提升,但「投入」與「感覺彼此在場」這兩個更主觀的社交面向,效果沒有那麼一致。

質性訪談發現螢幕投影的效果是雙面的:有螢幕時,參與者常常盯著螢幕上的音樂區塊視覺化,而不是看彼此,但這反而降低了陌生人之間一開始的社交壓力,因為螢幕提供了一個「共同關注的物件」讓大家一起玩;拿掉螢幕後,雖然一開始出現尷尬,但身體上的共同在場感反而提升,而地板上貼的位置標記能部分補回螢幕拿掉後失去的空間可讀性。

在博物館活動(約 25 位輪替參觀者)與音樂科技展演(約 300 位觀眾)這兩場延伸部署裡,作者觀察到把螢幕整個拿掉、改用彩色舞台燈光對應各區能量之後,舞者彼此之間、以及舞者與地板燈光之間的注意力互動反而比有螢幕時更明顯,顯示螢幕不是必要條件,只是其中一種讓陌生人願意開始互動的媒介。不過作者也強調,音樂廳展演的觀察只來自第一作者本人,不是控制實驗,不能直接拿來跟課堂研究的量化結果比較。

In the movement-class user study (n=11), the quantitative results show a clearly uneven pattern: spatial awareness had the highest agreement at 90.9% (36.4% strongly agreeing); collaboration agreement was 81.8%; encouragement and enjoyment were both 72.7%. But engagement was agreed to by only 54.5%, with 45.5% of respondents neutral -- the largest neutral share of the nine dimensions measured; social presence agreement was also comparatively low at 54.6%, with 18.2% disagreeing and 27.3% neutral or mixed. This shows the system did raise most people's sense of collaboration and mutual spatial awareness, but the more subjective dimensions of "engagement" and "feeling each other's presence" were less consistently affected.

Qualitative interviews found the screen's effect cuts both ways: with the screen on, participants often watched the on-screen visualization of musical zones rather than each other, but this actually lowered the initial social barrier between strangers by giving the group a shared object to focus on; with the screen removed, initial awkwardness appeared but embodied co-presence increased, and floor-taped position markers partially restored the spatial legibility lost when the screen went away.

In two extended deployments -- a museum event (about 25 rotating visitors) and a music-technology showcase (about 300 audience members) -- the authors observed that removing the screen entirely and mapping each zone's energy to colored stage lighting instead produced, if anything, more visible mutual attention among dancers and between dancers and the floor lighting than the screen-based sessions. The authors stress, though, that the concert-hall observations come only from the first author and are not a controlled comparison, so they cannot be directly weighed against the classroom study's quantitative results.

Instrument Classification of Solo Sheet Music Images

獨奏樂譜影像的樂器分類

Kevin Ji, Daniel Yang, TJ Tsai
arXiv:2609.18980 · cs.CV, cs.LG, cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-17
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文把樂器辨識這個通常靠音訊做的任務,改成直接從獨奏樂譜影像判斷樂器,靠的是把樂譜轉成「bootleg score」二值矩陣、再當文字序列丟給語言模型(AWD-LSTM、GPT-2、RoBERTa)做預訓練與微調;在完整頁面分類上,RoBERTa 搭配 64-token 片段拿到巨集 F1 0.612,而移調資料增強(訓練時位移最多 3 個調、測試時最多 2 個調)把巨集 F1 從 0.611 大幅推到 0.691,是全篇單一改動效果最大的一項;最容易混淆的樂器對是中提琴與長笛,共 260 次錯誤。Why this one: This paper reframes instrument recognition -- usually done from audio -- as a task done directly from solo sheet music images, converting scores into a binary "bootleg score" matrix that is fed to language models (AWD-LSTM, GPT-2, RoBERTa) as a token sequence for pretraining and finetuning; on full-page classification, RoBERTa with 64-token fragments reaches a macro F1 of 0.612, and transposition-based data augmentation (shifting up to 3 keys in training, up to 2 at test time) pushes macro F1 from 0.611 to 0.691, the single largest gain in the paper; the most confused instrument pair is viola-flute, with 260 errors.

動機Motivation

先前的樂器辨識研究幾乎都是從音訊訊號出發——用頻譜圖搭配 CNN 或 RNN 判斷錄音裡有哪些樂器。這篇論文反過來問:能不能只看樂譜的視覺外觀就判斷這是哪個樂器的獨奏譜,完全不聽聲音?這個問題之所以不是原本音訊分類問題的翻版,是因為樂譜上能看到的線索本來就跟音色無關——包括音域高低、這個樂器習慣寫單音還是和絃、以及特定樂器慣用的音型與跳進模式,這些都可以直接從樂譜的視覺排列讀出來,不需要靠聽感去分辨。

Prior instrument-recognition research almost always starts from audio -- spectrograms fed into a CNN or RNN to identify which instruments appear in a recording. This paper instead asks whether an instrument can be identified purely from the visual appearance of its solo sheet music, without listening to any sound at all. This is not simply a visual restatement of the audio problem, because what a score reveals has nothing to do with timbre: the pitch range used, whether the instrument is written monophonically or polyphonically, and the characteristic melodic patterns and leaps idiomatic to a given instrument are all readable directly from the score's visual layout, without needing to hear anything.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音訊端的樂器辨識這條線,前人做法包括在 OpenMIC-2018 這類資料集上用 CNN、RNN 處理頻譜圖,或用預訓練 CNN 做遷移學習、以及用 ResNet 架構處理音訊分類。這篇論文真正的靈感來源是 Tsai 與 Ji(2020)的前作:他們把 NLP 裡語言模型預訓練的技巧,拿來對鋼琴樂譜做作曲家分類,利用的是鋼琴譜本身垂直方向(同時按下的和絃)的多聲部資訊。這篇論文則是把同一套「先預訓練語言模型、再微調分類器」的技巧,套用到單聲部(monophonic)樂器上,但因為單聲部樂器沒有同時按鍵的和絃結構,利用的線索改成水平方向的旋律特徵——同一套 NLP 遷移學習框架,被改造成看兩種完全不同方向的樂譜結構。

On the audio side of instrument recognition, prior work includes CNNs and RNNs applied to spectrograms on datasets like OpenMIC-2018, transfer learning from pretrained CNNs, and ResNet architectures for audio classification. This paper's real inspiration is the authors' own earlier work, Tsai and Ji (2020), which applied NLP-style language-model pretraining to composer classification of piano sheet music, exploiting the vertical, simultaneous-chord polyphony that piano scores contain. This paper adapts the same "pretrain a language model, then finetune a classifier" recipe to monophonic instruments, but since monophonic scores have no simultaneous-chord structure to exploit, the signal shifts to horizontal, melodic characteristics instead -- the same NLP transfer-learning framework repurposed to read a completely different axis of score structure.

方法Method

樂譜先轉換成「bootleg score」表示法:把每一頁樂譜編碼成一個 31×N 的二值矩陣,31 對應五線譜上下可能出現的 31 個譜位,N 對應音符事件的數量,矩陣裡標記哪個譜位被填滿音符頭。整個流程分三階段:先把 bootleg score 切成類似「單字」的 token 序列,在沒有標籤的資料上訓練三種語言模型架構——三層的 AWD-LSTM(自監督式逐詞預測)、六層的 GPT-2(transformer 解碼器)、六層的 RoBERTa(transformer 編碼器,遮罩詞預測);接著在有標籤的資料上切出固定長度片段,加上分類頭,用判別式學習率與 one-cycle 排程微調;推論時則對完整頁面或固定長度片段(重疊 50%)分別跑分類器,再把片段預測平均起來。

資料集方面,無標籤資料來自 IMSLP,共 3,760 首作品、18,915 頁;有標籤資料是 531 首經過人工標註的作品裡的 7,627 頁驗證過的獨奏頁面,依作品切分成 60% 訓練、20% 驗證、20% 測試。除了完整頁面分類,論文還設計了一個「代理任務」:對每個樂器各取 3,600 筆訓練、1,200 筆驗證、1,200 筆測試的固定長度片段(64、128、256 三種 token 長度),方便單獨評估語言模型預訓練帶來的效果差異。針對這八個以單聲部為主的樂器(小提琴、長笛、單簧管、雙簧管、小號、大提琴、中提琴、吉他),論文也另外測了資料增強:訓練時把樂譜移調最多三個調(K=3),測試時最多移調兩個調(L=2),用來模擬同一首曲子用不同調寫譜時視覺外觀的變化。

Scores are first converted to a "bootleg score" representation: each page is encoded as a 31xN binary matrix, where 31 is the number of possible staff positions and N is the number of note events, with cells marking which staff positions are filled by a notehead. The pipeline has three stages: bootleg scores are tokenized into word-like sequences and used to train three language-model architectures on unlabeled data -- a 3-layer AWD-LSTM (self-supervised next-token prediction), a 6-layer GPT-2 (transformer decoder), and a 6-layer RoBERTa (transformer encoder, masked-token prediction); labeled data is then cut into fixed-length fragments, given a classification head, and finetuned with discriminative learning rates and one-cycle scheduling; at inference, the classifier runs on either full variable-length pages or fixed-length, 50%-overlapping fragments whose predictions are then averaged.

The dataset draws unlabeled data from IMSLP, totaling 3,760 pieces and 18,915 pages; labeled data comes from 531 manually annotated pieces yielding 7,627 verified solo pages, split 60/20/20 by piece for train/validation/test. Besides full-page classification, the paper defines a proxy task: 3,600 training, 1,200 validation, and 1,200 test fixed-length fragments per instrument, at three token lengths (64, 128, 256), letting pretraining's effect be isolated more cleanly. For the eight largely-monophonic instruments studied (violin, flute, clarinet, oboe, trumpet, cello, viola, guitar), the paper also tests data augmentation: transposing scores up to three keys during training (K=3) and up to two keys at test time (L=2), simulating how the same piece's visual appearance changes when written in a different key.

限制Limitations

作者自己指出幾個限制。無標籤資料雖然比有標籤資料大 1.9 倍,但帶來的效果提升相對有限,作者認為是因為這批資料本身雜訊較多、類別也不平衡。變壓器模型(GPT-2、RoBERTa)在完整頁面分類上,片段長度拉長反而讓表現變差(RoBERTa 從 64-token 的 0.612 掉到 256-token 的 0.551),作者推測是因為訓練時對 256-token 長度的片段本身帶有偏誤;AWD-LSTM 則在不同片段長度之間表現比較穩定。單聲部樂器本身比多聲部樂器(如吉他)更容易分類錯誤,因為和絃結構原本可以拿來輔助判斷音域與可能的樂器,拿掉這條線索後可用的辨識依據變少。另外,這套方法需要人工先把樂譜裡的伴奏聲部裁掉,只留下獨奏聲部,無法直接套用在多樂器同時出現的樂譜上。

The authors point out several limits themselves. Although the unlabeled data is 1.9x larger than the labeled data, its benefit is relatively modest, which the authors attribute to noise and class imbalance in that data. For the transformer models (GPT-2, RoBERTa), longer fragment lengths actually hurt full-page classification (RoBERTa falls from 0.612 F1 at 64 tokens to 0.551 at 256 tokens), which the authors suspect reflects a training bias toward 256-token fragments; AWD-LSTM stayed more stable across fragment lengths. Monophonic instruments were more error-prone than polyphonic ones (like guitar), since chord structure that could otherwise help narrow down range and likely instrument is unavailable for a single melodic line. The method also requires manually curating scores to remove accompaniment parts first, so it cannot be applied directly to scores where multiple instruments appear together.

討論Discussion

代理任務(固定長度片段分類)的結果顯示預訓練確實有幫助:GPT-2 在 64-token 片段上,沒有預訓練時準確率是 32.1%,加上有標籤資料預訓練後提升到 42.3%,加上無標籤資料預訓練後進一步提升到 43.2%;RoBERTa 則從沒有預訓練的 34.5% 提升到無標籤預訓練後的 42.9%。完整頁面分類(巨集 F1)上,RoBERTa 用 64-token 片段拿到全表最佳的 0.612,128-token 降到 0.579,256-token 再降到 0.551,顯示片段切得越長,完整頁面預測反而越不準。

資料增強是全篇單一效果最大的改動:訓練時把樂譜移調最多三個調、測試時最多兩個調之後,代理任務準確率從 42.9% 一口氣拉到 58.8%,完整頁面巨集 F1 也從 0.611 拉到 0.691,絕對提升將近 8 個百分點,遠大於預訓練本身帶來的提升幅度。混淆矩陣分析則點出具體的錯誤模式:中提琴與長笛互相混淆最多,共 260 次錯誤;小號與雙簧管次之,259 次;雙簧管與單簧管則有 230 次。這些混淆對之間的錯誤方向並不對稱,反映的正是不同樂器音域重疊區域的差異;吉他因為本身是少數會同時彈和絃的樂器,錯誤次數在八個樂器裡最少,印證了論文一開始的假設——多聲部結構本身就是一種有用的辨識線索,拿掉它反而讓分類變難。

The proxy-task (fixed-length fragment classification) results show pretraining clearly helps: for GPT-2 on 64-token fragments, accuracy without pretraining is 32.1%, rising to 42.3% with labeled-data pretraining and further to 43.2% with unlabeled-data pretraining; RoBERTa rises from 34.5% without pretraining to 42.9% with unlabeled pretraining. On full-page classification (macro F1), RoBERTa with 64-token fragments gets the table's best score at 0.612, dropping to 0.579 at 128 tokens and 0.551 at 256 tokens -- longer fragments make full-page prediction less accurate, not more.

Data augmentation is the single largest improvement in the paper: transposing scores up to three keys in training and up to two at test time pushes proxy-task accuracy from 42.9% to 58.8%, and full-page macro F1 from 0.611 to 0.691, an absolute gain of nearly 8 points -- far larger than pretraining's own contribution. The confusion-matrix analysis points to concrete error patterns: viola and flute are confused most often, with 260 errors; trumpet and oboe next, with 259; and oboe and clarinet with 230. The asymmetry within these confusion pairs reflects differences in where instruments' pitch ranges overlap; guitar, one of the few instruments in the set that can play chords, has the fewest errors of the eight, confirming the paper's opening premise -- polyphonic structure is itself a useful cue, and removing it is what makes classification harder.
