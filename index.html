A Tutorial on Generative Models for Music Audio: From Text-to-Music to Live Performance

音樂音訊生成模型教程：用同一套符號講清楚 codec、自迴歸、擴散與 flow matching，並把 105 個系統的表示、條件、釋出與資料來源逐格列表

Mateusz Modrzejewski, Joanna Kurkowska, Dorien Herremans
arXiv:2610.38252 · eess.AS; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-28
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這是一份約百頁的教學專著，對象是會訓練神經網路但剛進音樂生成的人。它用同一套符號寫出 RVQ codec、自迴歸 token 模型、遮罩解碼、擴散、flow matching、加速取樣與偏好對齊的目標函數，再把 105 個系統依表示、生成方式、條件、應用類別與釋出條件逐格列表。選它是因為它不排名，而是教讀者怎麼讀下一篇系統報告：例如用 MusicGen 算出每秒 200 個 token、30 秒要 1,503 個解碼步，並列出一份九項的聽測報告清單。它的總結也很克制：表上所有生成範式在 2023 年就都已出現，之後的差異在表示、後訓練與是否先規劃；但因為資料、規模與評測都不同，這些表格無法判定哪一項帶來了效果提升。另外，105 列裡有 43 列的訓練資料只能標成「未揭露」。Why this one: This is a monograph of about a hundred pages for readers who can train neural networks but are new to music generation. It writes the objectives of RVQ codecs, autoregressive token models, masked decoding, diffusion, flow matching, accelerated sampling and preference alignment in one notation, then tabulates 105 systems cell by cell by representation, generative method, conditioning, application class and release conditions. I picked it because it does not rank systems; it teaches how to read the next system report, for example by computing from MusicGen's codec 200 tokens per second and 1,503 decoding steps for 30 seconds, and by listing a nine-item checklist for reporting listening tests. Its summary is also restrained: every generative paradigm in the tables was known by 2023, and later systems differ in representation, post-training and whether they plan first; but because data, scale and evaluation differ, the tables cannot establish which change produced the gains. In addition, 43 of the 105 rows can only mark their training data as undisclosed.

動機Motivation

作者認為，一個有用的音樂生成器不只要產生聽起來合理的聲音：歌詞轉歌曲的系統要讓字、歌聲與伴奏在整首歌的段落間協調；編輯要改到指定的特徵、其餘保持不變；現場使用則要反應得夠快，讓樂手來得及回應。這些需求對表示、生成與控制提出不同的要求，而相關方法散落在彼此預設讀者已熟悉前作的論文裡。

2023 年 MusicLM、MusicGen 與潛在擴散幾乎同時出現後，文獻往完整歌曲、規劃器加渲染器、少步取樣、偏好對齊、細緻控制與編輯等方向同時擴張，評測方法卻進步得比較慢，訓練資料與釋出條件也有法律爭議。這份專著想做的是：用一套共同符號教會讀者這些系統共用的基礎，讓讀者讀到下一篇系統報告時能定位它、判斷它的證據。

The authors argue that a useful music generator must do more than produce plausible sound: a lyrics-to-song system must coordinate words, singing and accompaniment across a song's sections; an edit must change the requested feature and preserve the rest; and live use must respond quickly enough for a musician to act on what they hear. These needs place different demands on representation, generation and control, and the methods are scattered across papers that assume familiarity with earlier work.

After MusicLM, MusicGen and latent diffusion arrived almost together in 2023, the literature expanded at once toward full songs, planner-renderer designs, few-step sampling, preference alignment, fine-grained control and editing, while evaluation improved more slowly and training data and release conditions became legally contested. The monograph aims to teach the shared foundations in one notation so that a reader can place the next system report and judge its evidence.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把自己放在一串綜述之間，並說清楚差別。Briot、Hadjeres 與 Pachet（2020）用「生成什麼、怎麼表示、什麼架構、什麼挑戰與策略」五個維度整理深度學習音樂生成，但寫在音訊系統還不堪用的年代、以符號音樂為主；這份專著的 D1、D2 對應它的表示與架構，D3 到 D5 則換成「能下什麼條件、用在哪、釋出了什麼」。Herremans、Chuan 與 Chew（2017）依生成的音樂維度（旋律、和聲、節奏、音色、互動等）分類，對完整混音的音訊生成器不太適用。範圍最接近的是 Li 等（2026a）依引導模態整理的綜述，涵蓋較多符號生成與多模態融合，但最新的系統只到 2025 年初。

專著的兩個主要設計各有更接近的前例，論文沒有這樣點出。第一，「同一套符號寫出擴散與 flow matching，並說明兩者是同一族、只差路徑與參數化」的寫法，在影像領域已有 Lipman 等（2024）的 Flow Matching Guide and Code 這類專文。第二，D5 那組釋出屬性（時長、輸出格式、延遲類別、權重與資料開放程度、資料來源、後訓練）以及「報告一個釋出系統」的清單，本質上是音樂版的 Model Cards（Mitchell 等，2019），只是由綜述作者回頭替 105 個系統補填。

The monograph places itself among existing surveys and states the differences. Briot, Hadjeres and Pachet (2020) organise deep-learning music generation by what is generated, how it is represented, which architecture generates it, and which challenges and strategies apply, but they wrote before audio systems were usable and focused on symbolic music; here D1 and D2 correspond to their representation and architecture dimensions, while D3 to D5 become what a system can be told, what it is for and what was released. Herremans, Chuan and Chew (2017) index systems by the musical dimension generated (melody, harmony, rhythm, timbre, interaction and so on), which fits full-mixture audio generators poorly. The closest in scope is the modality-centred survey of Li et al. (2026a), which covers symbolic generation and multimodal fusion more broadly but whose newest systems date from early 2025.

The monograph's two main design choices each have a closer precedent that it does not name. First, writing diffusion and flow matching in one notation and explaining that they are one family differing in path and parameterisation follows dedicated texts in the image domain such as the Flow Matching Guide and Code of Lipman et al. (2024). Second, the D5 release attributes (duration, output format, latency class, openness of weights and data, data provenance, post-training) and the checklist for reporting a released system are in essence a music-specific version of Model Cards (Mitchell et al., 2019), filled in retrospectively by the survey authors for 105 systems.

方法Method

四個槽位。 專著把每個系統描述成填四個槽：一種表示（codec token、連續潛在、符號或草稿，加上解碼回音訊的解碼器）、一種分布分解（逐 token、平行多 token、去噪鏈、從雜訊積分到資料的向量場、先規劃再渲染）、一條條件進入的路線（全域嵌入、與時間對齊的序列、參考音訊、經由規劃），以及一個取樣器與它的網路評估次數。之後的比較都是這四槽之間的取捨。

先講例子再講公式。 MusicGen 的 EnCodec 以 640 個樣本為步長切 32 kHz 音訊，每秒 50 幀、每幀 4 個 token、每個從 2,048 個條目選，所以每秒 200 個 token、30 秒 6,000 個 token、位元率 2,200 bps。delay pattern 讓第 q 條串流延後 q − 1 步，30 秒只要 1,503 個解碼步，不攤平的話要 6,000 步。之後才給出 RVQ、自迴歸、遮罩、擴散（含 DDPM、score、EDM 三種寫法的對照）、flow matching、consistency、DPO 與 Diffusion-DPO 的目標函數。

警告框與比較表。 文中多個警告框專門拆開容易混淆的概念：semantic 與 acoustic token 不是兩種 codec；flow matching、rectified flow、reflow、consistency model 不是同一個方法；即時率、延遲與 future visibility 量的是三件不同的事；偏好對齊與對抗式後訓練是兩種不同階段。比較表依應用類別分成 T1 器樂、T2 歌曲、T3 控制與編輯、T4 即時，每格標明出處，查不到的標「?」而不推測。

Four slots. The monograph describes every system as filling four slots: a representation (codec tokens, a continuous latent, a symbolic score or a sketch, plus a decoder back to audio), a factorisation (token by token, many tokens in parallel, a denoising chain, a vector field integrated from noise to data, or a plan followed by rendering), a route for the condition (a global embedding, a time-aligned sequence, reference audio, or through the plan), and a sampler with its number of network evaluations. Every later comparison is a trade-off between these slots.

Example before formula. MusicGen's EnCodec cuts 32 kHz audio with a stride of 640 samples, giving 50 frames per second and four tokens per frame, each from 2,048 entries, so 200 tokens per second, 6,000 tokens for 30 seconds and 2,200 bits per second. The delay pattern offsets stream q by q − 1 steps, so 30 seconds take 1,503 decoding steps instead of 6,000 when flattened. Only then come the objectives of RVQ, autoregression, masking, diffusion (with the DDPM, score and EDM conventions mapped to one another), flow matching, consistency models, DPO and Diffusion-DPO.

Warning boxes and tables. Several warning boxes separate commonly confused concepts: semantic and acoustic tokens are not two codecs; flow matching, rectified flow, reflow and consistency models are not one method; real-time factor, latency and future visibility measure three different things; preference alignment and adversarial post-training are different stages. The comparison tables split by application class into T1 instrumental, T2 song, T3 control and editing and T4 live, each cell sourced, with unsourced values marked "?" rather than inferred.

限制Limitations

表格記錄的是文件，不是品質。 作者明說表上不比較輸出品質，因為各系統用不同嵌入、不同參考集、不同聽測。這是誠實的選擇，但也代表讀者若想知道「哪個比較好」，這份專著給不出答案；它只能告訴你某個比較為什麼不能直接信。

核心結論是假說而非觀察。 「所有範式在 2023 年就已出現，之後的進步來自表示、後訓練與規劃」這句話，作者自己也承認表格無法判定是哪一項造成的。全文唯一控制資料與架構、只換生成範式的比較是 Tal 等（2025），結論是沒有明確贏家。所以這個總結比較像一個值得驗證的方向，不是從表格推得出來的結果。

收錄方式會很快過時。 系統表的截止是 2026 年 9 月底，結構化搜尋在 9 月 11 日關閉，每個查詢最多取 100 筆。10 月的第二輪複查又找到 13 篇符合條件、137 篇值得合併提及的候選，但都沒有放進分類與統計。ISMIR 2026 的 140 篇接受論文中，36 篇屬生成、只有 62 篇找得到公開版本。表格的組裝也用了 AI 助理，作者說每一格都回頭查過原始來源，但讀者無法逐格稽核。

The tables record documentation, not quality. The authors state that the tables do not compare output quality, because systems use different embeddings, reference sets and listening tests. That is an honest choice, but it means a reader who wants to know which system is better gets no answer here; the monograph can only explain why a given comparison should not be taken at face value.

The central conclusion is a hypothesis, not an observation. The authors themselves concede that the tables cannot establish which change explains the gains behind the statement that every paradigm existed by 2023 and later progress came from representation, post-training and planning. The only comparison that holds data and architecture fixed and varies the paradigm is Tal et al. (2025), which finds no clear winner. The summary is therefore a direction worth testing rather than a result derived from the tables.

The collection will age quickly. The system cutoff is the end of September 2026, the structured searches closed on 11 September, and each query returned at most 100 results. A second pass in October found 13 more eligible candidates and 137 worth a grouped mention, none of them in the classification or counts. Of ISMIR 2026's 140 accepted papers, 36 were generative and only 62 had public versions. AI assistants also helped assemble the tables; the authors say every cell was checked against its source, but readers cannot audit cell by cell.

討論Discussion

資料揭露的數字最有分量。 105 列裡有 43 列的訓練資料只能標成「未揭露、內部或網路蒐集」，20 列是授權或 Creative Commons，11 列是不新增訓練資料的推論期方法。記憶化測試或浮水印只在 10 個系統上有記錄，其餘論文作者沒有逐篇去查，所以這個欄位沒有列入表格。在歌曲類，2025 與 2026 年首次釋出的 25 個系統裡有 11 個報告了偏好或強化學習階段；器樂類表格的 22 列中，有 6 列量到快於即時，全部不是自迴歸 token 模型，作者也指出原因之一只是這些論文沒有量。

評測章節是最實用的部分。 它整理了 FAD 的已知問題（隨嵌入與參考集變動、與現代文字轉音樂的人類偏好相關性弱、小樣本估計有偏），並給出九項聽測報告清單：題目抽樣、聽者背景、隨機化與平衡、重複量測、對比的信賴區間、評分者效應、多重比較校正、播放條件，以及「若用學習式評審當訓練獎勵，就必須附上同一批題目的人類測試」。最後一項直接針對獎勵被鑽漏洞的循環問題。

法律章節區分證據的層級。 它分開記錄公司公告、訴訟主張與法院判決：例如慕尼黑地方法院 2025 年 11 月 11 日關於 OpenAI 模型重現歌詞、2026 年 7 月 31 日關於 Suno v3.5 與 v4 中六首作品的判決，兩者都尚未確定。值得知道的背景是三位作者中有兩位來自 ZAiKS Lab（波蘭的作者權集體管理組織），文中也明確表態支持創作者同意與公平報酬；這個立場是公開寫出來的，讀者可以據此判讀這一章。

The disclosure numbers carry the most weight. Of the 105 rows, 43 can only mark training data as undisclosed, in-house or web-collected, 20 are licensed or Creative Commons, and 11 are inference-time methods that add no training data. A memorisation test or watermark is documented for only 10 systems, and the authors did not search the other papers for one, so the attribute is left out of the tables. Among the 25 song systems first released in 2025 and 2026, 11 report a preference or reinforcement stage; in the 22-row instrumental table, 6 rows are measured faster than real time and none is an autoregressive token model, which the authors partly attribute to those papers simply not measuring it.

The evaluation chapter is the most practical part. It collects the known problems of FAD (dependence on embedding and reference set, weak correlation with human preference on modern text-to-music output, biased small-sample estimates) and gives a nine-item listening-test checklist: item sampling, listener background, randomisation and counterbalancing, repeated measures, intervals for the contrast, rater effects, multiple-comparison correction, playback conditions, and, if a learned judge was used as a training reward, a human test on the same items. The last item targets the circularity of reward hacking directly.

The legal chapter separates levels of evidence. It records company announcements, litigants' allegations and court judgments separately, for example the Munich Regional Court's rulings of 11 November 2025 on lyrics reproduced in OpenAI's models and of 31 July 2026 on six compositions in Suno's v3.5 and v4, neither of them final. Useful context: two of the three authors are at ZAiKS Lab (ZAiKS is a Polish authors' collective-rights society), and the text openly supports creator consent and fair compensation; the position is stated, so readers can read the chapter with it in mind.

ResMamba: A Dual-Path Convolution-Mamba Model for Music Genre Classification

ResMamba：在 ResNet 四階段骨幹裡並聯 Mamba 與卷積兩條路徑，從零訓練做 GTZAN／FMA 曲風分類

Yingyu Zhang, Pu Qu
arXiv:2611.00746 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-31
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：作者把 Mamba 的選擇性狀態空間模組包成能吃 (B, C, H, W) 特徵圖的殘差塊，和兩層 3×3 卷積並聯、以 1×1 卷積融合，再加一個彙整四個階段特徵的多尺度頭。全部從零訓練、不做資料增強，GTZAN 五折交叉驗證 88.99% ± 1.58%，比同條件的 ResNet-18（85.38%）高 3.61 個百分點；FMA-Small 十折 64.52%，FMA-Medium 官方 artist 切分 65.75%。選它是因為它把十二個常見視覺骨幹放在同一條從零訓練的流程裡比，結果很有參考價值：EfficientNet 全系列只有 76.68% 到 81.48%，Swin-T 77.48%，在這種資料量下「影像上的好架構」不一定能搬過來。但它對已發表方法的領先只有 0.35 點，而且切分方式不同，這一點要分開看。Why this one: The authors wrap Mamba's selective state-space module as a residual block that takes (B, C, H, W) feature maps, run it in parallel with two 3×3 convolutions, fuse the two with a 1×1 convolution, and add a multi-scale head that pools features from all four stages. Trained from scratch with no data augmentation, it reaches 88.99% ± 1.58% in five-fold cross-validation on GTZAN, 3.61 percentage points above ResNet-18 under the same pipeline (85.38%), plus 64.52% on FMA-Small (10-fold) and 65.75% on FMA-Medium (official artist split). I picked it because it runs twelve common vision backbones through one from-scratch pipeline, and that comparison is useful in itself: the whole EfficientNet family lands at 76.68% to 81.48% and Swin-T at 77.48%, so a strong image architecture does not automatically transfer at this data scale. Its lead over published methods, however, is only 0.35 points under a different split, and that claim has to be read separately.

動機Motivation

曲風分類是 MIR 最老的任務之一，主流做法是把 mel 頻譜當成影像丟給 CNN。作者認為卷積的感受野有限，而曲風的線索（主歌到副歌的轉換、配器變化、節奏型）常常橫跨數十秒，需要能跨長距離建立關聯的模型。

Transformer 能做到這件事，但自注意力的計算量隨序列長度平方成長；30 秒、128×1292 的 mel 頻譜攤平後在第一階段就有一萬多個位置。Mamba 這類選擇性狀態空間模型的計算量是線性的，所以作者想知道：把它嵌進一個熟悉的 ResNet 骨幹裡，在不用預訓練、不用外部資料、不做增強的條件下，能不能贏過純卷積。

Genre classification is one of the oldest MIR tasks, and the standard approach treats the mel spectrogram as an image for a CNN. The authors argue that convolutional receptive fields are limited, while genre cues such as verse-to-chorus transitions, changes in instrumentation and rhythmic patterns often span tens of seconds, which calls for a model that can relate distant positions.

A Transformer can do that, but self-attention scales quadratically with sequence length, and a flattened 30-second, 128×1292 mel spectrogram already has more than ten thousand positions at the first stage. Selective state-space models such as Mamba scale linearly, so the authors ask whether embedding one in a familiar ResNet backbone can beat pure convolution with no pretraining, no external data and no augmentation.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的前身是 CRNN（Choi 等，2017）：卷積前端抓局部頻譜圖樣，後面接循環網路處理時間脈絡。ResMamba 的 ConvMamba 塊可以看成把 GRU 換成 Mamba，而且從「先卷積再序列模型」的串聯改成「同一層兩條分支並聯」。論文有引 CRNN，但沒有點出這層關係。

並聯兩條分支、各自負責全域與局部，再串接後用線性層融合，這個設計在語音辨識裡已經有名字：Branchformer（Peng 等，2022）讓自注意力分支和卷積門控 MLP 分支並行，再合併輸出。ConvMamba 塊幾乎是同一個模板，只是全域分支從注意力換成 Mamba、輸入從一維序列換成二維特徵圖。論文比的是 MedMamba 和高光譜影像用的同名 ConvMamba，沒有提 Branchformer，這是相關研究章節最該補的一句。

音訊上的 Mamba 也已有前例，例如 Audio Mamba (AuM)（Erol 等，2024）把頻譜切成 patch 後用雙向 Mamba 做分類，是純 SSM、沒有卷積分支。另外，GTZAN 本身的問題由 Sturm（2013）系統整理過：重複曲目、標錯的曲子和同一藝人集中在同一類，都會讓 track-level 隨機切分的分數偏高。

The most direct ancestor is the CRNN (Choi et al., 2017): a convolutional front end captures local spectral patterns and a recurrent network handles temporal context. ResMamba's ConvMamba block can be read as swapping the GRU for Mamba and turning the serial "convolution then sequence model" arrangement into two parallel branches within one layer. The paper cites CRNN but does not draw this connection.

Running two branches in parallel, one global and one local, then concatenating and fusing them with a linear layer already has a name in speech recognition: Branchformer (Peng et al., 2022) runs a self-attention branch alongside a convolutional gated-MLP branch and merges their outputs. The ConvMamba block follows nearly the same template, with Mamba replacing attention in the global branch and 2D feature maps replacing 1D sequences. The paper compares itself with MedMamba and a homonymous ConvMamba for hyperspectral images but never mentions Branchformer, which is the sentence its related-work section most needs.

Mamba also has audio precedents, such as Audio Mamba (AuM) (Erol et al., 2024), which classifies spectrogram patches with a bidirectional pure-SSM model and no convolutional branch. Separately, the problems of GTZAN itself were catalogued by Sturm (2013): repeated tracks, mislabelled excerpts and artists concentrated within one genre all inflate scores under random track-level splits.

方法Method

輸入與骨幹。 22,050 Hz 的 30 秒音訊轉成 128×1292 的 dB mel 頻譜（n_fft 2048、hop 512），複製成三個通道。7×7、步長 2 的卷積加最大池化後，進入通道數 64、128、256、512 的四個階段，每階段 2 個塊（另有 [3, 4, 6, 3] 的深版本只出現在消融）。

MambaWrapper。 先用 1×1 卷積把通道投影到嵌入維度，把 H×W 攤平成序列送進單向的 MambaVision mixer（狀態維度 8、深度卷積核 3），再還原成特徵圖。第一、二階段序列太長（10,336 與 2,576 個位置），所以切成不重疊的 8×8 視窗各自掃描，每個視窗在時間軸上約 0.7 到 1.5 秒；第三、四階段只剩 640 與 160 個位置，直接整張掃。視窗只切一次、不做 Swin 式的位移。

兩種塊與多尺度頭。 AdaptMambaBlock 把 ResNet BasicBlock 的兩層卷積整個換成 MambaWrapper 加 BN，是純 SSM 的對照。ConvMambaBlock 讓同一個（必要時先 2×2 平均池化降採樣的）輸入同時走 Mamba 分支與「3×3 卷積、BN、ReLU、3×3 卷積、BN」的卷積分支，兩者在通道維度串接後用 1×1 卷積加 BN 融合，再經 DropPath 與殘差相加。跨視窗的資訊交換就靠這條卷積分支和多尺度頭補回。多尺度頭把四個階段的輸出用自適應平均池化對齊到同一大小後串接，經 1×1 卷積、dropout、全域平均池化與 128 維全連接層再分類。

訓練。 AdamW，學習率 10⁻⁴、權重衰減 10⁻²，五個 epoch 線性暖身。GTZAN 跑滿 120 個 epoch，在第 60 與 90 個 epoch 把學習率乘 0.1，不做早停，批次 16；FMA 改用平原式衰減與耐心 20 的早停，批次 32。所有比較的視覺骨幹都用 torchvision 官方實作、不載入預訓練權重，只換最後一層。

Input and backbone. Thirty seconds of 22,050 Hz audio become a 128×1292 dB mel spectrogram (n_fft 2048, hop 512), replicated across three channels. A 7×7 stride-2 convolution and max pooling feed four stages with 64, 128, 256 and 512 channels and two blocks each (a deeper [3, 4, 6, 3] variant appears only in the ablation).

MambaWrapper. A 1×1 convolution projects the channels to the embedding width, the H×W map is flattened into a sequence for a unidirectional MambaVision mixer (state size 8, depthwise kernel 3), and the result is reshaped back into a feature map. Stages 1 and 2 would give sequences of 10,336 and 2,576 positions, so they are split into non-overlapping 8×8 windows scanned independently, each covering about 0.7 to 1.5 s of audio; stages 3 and 4 have only 640 and 160 positions and are scanned whole. The windows are partitioned once, without Swin-style shifting.

Two blocks and a multi-scale head. AdaptMambaBlock replaces both convolutions of a ResNet BasicBlock with MambaWrapper plus BN, serving as the pure-SSM control. ConvMambaBlock sends the same input (average-pooled 2×2 first when downsampling) through a Mamba branch and a convolutional branch of 3×3 conv, BN, ReLU, 3×3 conv and BN; the two are concatenated along channels, fused by a 1×1 convolution with BN, and added to the residual after DropPath. Cross-window exchange is restored only through this convolutional branch and the multi-scale head. The head aligns all four stage outputs to one size with adaptive average pooling, concatenates them, and applies a 1×1 convolution, dropout, global average pooling and a 128-unit fully connected layer before the classifier.

Training. AdamW with learning rate 10⁻⁴, weight decay 10⁻² and a five-epoch linear warm-up. On GTZAN every model runs the full 120 epochs, with the learning rate multiplied by 0.1 at epochs 60 and 90, no early stopping and batch size 16; on FMA a plateau schedule and early stopping with patience 20 are used, with batch size 32. All compared vision backbones are the official torchvision implementations without pretrained weights, with only the final layer replaced.

限制Limitations

對已發表方法的領先在雜訊範圍內。 88.99% 對 Setiorini 等的 88.64%，差 0.35 點，換算成 999 首約 3.5 首；而對方是 60/20/20 的 segment-level 切分，同一首歌的片段可能同時出現在訓練與測試。論文自己在表註說明了這點，但摘要仍寫成「超越最強已發表方法」。

消融差距小於折間變異。 加上多尺度頭只多 0.60 點，而沒有多尺度頭的版本標準差是 2.55；每折約 200 首，一首就是 0.5 點。換言之，0.60 點大約是每折多對一首。真正明確的差距是「Mamba-only 對 ResNet-18」（+1.41，而且參數只有 2.2M 對 11.2M）和「加入卷積分支」（+1.60）。

沒有處理 GTZAN 的已知問題。 五折是 track-level 分層切分，沒有 artist 過濾，也沒有排除 Sturm 列出的重複曲目。論文提到 Yang 等在 artist 過濾下只有 82.4%，卻以「不可直接比較」帶過，沒有自己在那個條件下跑一次，而那正是最能說明泛化能力的實驗。

「尊重頻譜的非等向性」沒有落實在掃描方式上。 論文強調時間軸與頻率軸意義不同，但 MambaWrapper 只是把 (H, W) 依預設順序攤平後單向掃描：先沿時間掃完一條 mel 頻帶，再跳到下一條，而且前面的頻帶看不到後面的頻帶。沒有雙向掃描、先頻率後時間或多方向掃描的對照，所以無法判斷 Mamba 分支實際上利用了哪種長距離關係。

The lead over published methods is within noise. 88.99% against Setiorini et al.'s 88.64% is 0.35 points, about 3.5 of 999 tracks, and that baseline used a 60/20/20 segment-level split in which clips of one song can appear in both training and test. The paper notes this in a table footnote, yet the abstract still says it surpasses the strongest published method.

Ablation gaps are smaller than fold variance. The multi-scale head adds only 0.60 points, while the variant without it has a standard deviation of 2.55; with about 200 tracks per fold, one track is 0.5 points, so 0.60 points is roughly one extra correct track per fold. The clear gaps are Mamba-only over ResNet-18 (+1.41, with 2.2M against 11.2M parameters) and adding the convolutional branch (+1.60).

Known GTZAN problems are left untouched. The five folds are stratified at track level with no artist filtering and no removal of the repeats Sturm listed. The paper mentions that Yang et al. reach only 82.4% with artist filtering but sets it aside as not directly comparable, instead of running its own model under that condition, which is the experiment that would say most about generalisation.

"Respecting spectrogram anisotropy" is not reflected in the scan. The paper stresses that the time and frequency axes mean different things, but MambaWrapper simply flattens (H, W) in default order and scans it in one direction: it sweeps along time within one mel band, then jumps to the next, and earlier bands never see later ones. With no comparison against bidirectional, frequency-first or multi-directional scans, there is no way to tell which long-range relations the Mamba branch actually exploits.

討論Discussion

最有用的是同條件的骨幹比較。 在 128×1292 全解析度、從零訓練、不增強的條件下，ResNet-18／34／50／101 分別是 85.38%、82.38%、85.08%、82.18%，同一種塊設計裡越深反而越差；EfficientNet B0 到 V2-S 只有 76.68% 到 81.48%；Swin-T 是 77.48%；MambaVision-T 是 83.59%。ResMamba 用 15.1M 參數、15.6 GFLOPs 拿到 88.99%，比 ResNeXt-50 少 34% 參數卻高 4.11 點。這組數字支持一個比論文標題更一般的結論：每折只有 800 首訓練資料時，卷積的局部歸納偏置比容量重要。

解析度消融值得記住。 只把時間軸從 1292 壓到 320 幀，ResNet-18 就從 85.38% 掉到 78.28%；從 640 到 1292 仍多 2.20 點，代表 23 毫秒的時間解析度還沒飽和。很多曲風分類論文為了省運算會先縮圖，這個結果說明那樣做有代價。

FMA 的結果比 GTZAN 更有說服力。 FMA-Medium 用官方 artist 切分，ResMamba 65.75%，同條件 ResNet-18 是 62.99%、ResNeXt-50 是 61.00%。但 16 類的 macro F1 只有 0.4262（ResNet-18 為 0.3558），表示準確率主要由大類撐起，小類仍很弱；論文只把 F1 放在表裡，沒有討論。

The most useful result is the same-pipeline backbone comparison. At full 128×1292 resolution, from scratch and without augmentation, ResNet-18/34/50/101 score 85.38%, 82.38%, 85.08% and 82.18%, so depth hurts within each block design; EfficientNet B0 to V2-S score only 76.68% to 81.48%; Swin-T scores 77.48% and MambaVision-T 83.59%. ResMamba reaches 88.99% with 15.1M parameters and 15.6 GFLOPs, 4.11 points above ResNeXt-50 with 34% fewer parameters. These numbers support a conclusion more general than the title: with 800 training tracks per fold, the local inductive bias of convolution matters more than capacity.

The resolution ablation is worth remembering. Compressing only the time axis from 1292 to 320 frames drops ResNet-18 from 85.38% to 78.28%, and going from 640 to 1292 frames still adds 2.20 points, so 23 ms time resolution has not saturated. Many genre papers downsample to save compute, and this result shows that has a cost.

The FMA results are more convincing than GTZAN. FMA-Medium uses the official artist split, where ResMamba reaches 65.75% against 62.99% for ResNet-18 and 61.00% for ResNeXt-50 under the same pipeline. But its 16-class macro F1 is only 0.4262 (ResNet-18: 0.3558), so accuracy is carried by the large classes and the small ones remain weak; the paper lists F1 in the table without discussing it.

A Compact Statistical View of Musical-Instrument Directivity

用 von Mises-Fisher 混合分布把樂器指向性壓成「幾個瓣」：41 件管弦樂器、30 個三分之一倍頻帶的壓縮與相似度保存

Gioele Greco, Mirco Pezzoli, Raffaele Malvermi, Gian Marco Ricci, Fabio Antonacci
arXiv:2611.02927 · eess.AS; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-11-02

為什麼挑這篇：作者把每個三分之一倍頻帶的樂器指向性能量圖正規化成球面上的機率分布，用加權 EM 擬合 von Mises-Fisher（vMF）混合，取「與原圖球面 Pearson 相關達到門檻 τ」的最少成分數。在 TU Berlin 的 41 件樂器資料庫上，τ = 0.7 時平均壓縮 4.9 倍，整個 4 階球諧、30 個頻帶的語料從約 492 kB 降到約 100 kB，樂器兩兩之間 Jensen-Shannon 距離矩陣與原始矩陣的 Pearson 相關是 0.961。選它是因為「每個瓣一個方向、一個集中度、一個權重」是可以直接讀的描述子，適合做指向性的檢索與聚類；但壓縮倍數是拿「含相位的複數球諧係數」去比「只有能量的 vMF」，而且沒有任何同預算的基準，這兩點會影響對 4.9 倍的解讀。Why this one: The authors normalise each one-third-octave band of an instrument's directional energy pattern into a probability distribution on the sphere, fit a von Mises-Fisher (vMF) mixture by weighted EM, and keep the fewest components whose spherical Pearson correlation with the original reaches a threshold τ. On the TU Berlin database of 41 instruments, τ = 0.7 gives a mean compression of 4.9×, shrinking the full order-4, 30-band spherical-harmonic corpus from about 492 kB to about 100 kB, and the pairwise Jensen-Shannon distance matrix keeps a Pearson correlation of 0.961 with the original. I picked it because "one direction, one concentration and one weight per lobe" is a directly readable descriptor that suits directivity retrieval and clustering; but the compression factor compares complex SH coefficients that carry phase with an energy-only vMF model, and there is no equal-budget baseline, both of which affect how the 4.9× should be read.

動機Motivation

樂器往不同方向輻射的能量不一樣，而且隨頻率劇烈變化，這會影響聽眾位置的音色、合奏融合與廳堂聽感。近年已有整個管弦樂器家族的頻率解析指向性量測可以公開下載，問題於是從「怎麼量」轉成「怎麼表示」。

完整的球面圖在物理上最豐富，但每個頻帶要存很多係數或很多取樣方向，在整個語料上反覆比較、檢索或建模時很笨重。作者想要一種既壓縮、又保留聲學上有意義結構、還能跨樂器一致比較的描述方式，即使某個頻帶的輻射並不是單一主方向。

Instruments radiate different amounts of energy in different directions, and the pattern changes sharply with frequency, which affects timbre at the listener's position, ensemble blend and the impression in a hall. Frequency-resolved directivity measurements of whole orchestral families are now publicly available, so the question shifts from how to measure to how to represent.

Full spherical maps are physically the richest, but each band needs many coefficients or sampled directions, which makes repeated comparison, retrieval or modelling across a corpus cumbersome. The authors want a description that is compact, keeps acoustically meaningful structure and can be compared consistently across instruments, even when a band does not radiate along a single dominant direction.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

資料來自 Ackermann、Brinkmann 與 Weinzierl（2024）的指向性資料庫，更早的大規模量測是 Pätynen 與 Lokki（2010）對交響樂團樂器的整理。比較指向性的方法，論文點名 Carpentier 與 Einbond（2023）的球面相關，以及同一團隊 Pezzoli 等（ISMIR 2023）用於小提琴檢索的相似度度量；這兩者都直接在完整的球面圖或係數上算相似度，這篇則先壓成少數參數再比。

用 vMF 混合描述方向性能量，在聲學上已有 Bastine 等（2020、2022）分析房間殘響與反射方向，論文說差別在於對象換成聲源本身、目的換成跨語料壓縮與比較。擬合方法本身就是 Banerjee 等（2005）的 vMF 混合 EM，只是把資料點換成帶權重的球面格點，所以這篇的貢獻在應用、成分數選擇規則與壓縮帳目，而不是新的統計模型。

論文沒有提到的最近親是電腦圖學：Tsai 與 Shih（2006）等人把環境光照與反射瓣表示成球面高斯（spherical Gaussian）的加權和，這和未正規化的 vMF 是同一個函數形式，也已經累積了很多「用幾個瓣近似球面函數」的擬合與誤差分析經驗。另外，K = 1 時的 vMF 大致就是傳統的主軸方向加指向性指數，論文圖 1 畫的 −3 dB 範圍與質心正是這類單瓣描述子。

The data come from the directivity database of Ackermann, Brinkmann and Weinzierl (2024); an earlier large-scale survey is Pätynen and Lokki (2010) on symphony-orchestra instruments. For comparing directivities the paper names the spherical correlation of Carpentier and Einbond (2023) and the similarity measure of Pezzoli et al. (ISMIR 2023), from the same group, for violin retrieval; both compute similarity directly on full spherical maps or coefficients, whereas this paper first compresses each band into a few parameters.

vMF mixtures have already described directional energy in acoustics in Bastine et al. (2020, 2022), who analysed the directions of room reverberation and reflections; the paper says the difference is that the object is now the source itself and the goal is corpus-level compression and comparison. The fitting procedure is the vMF-mixture EM of Banerjee et al. (2005) with weighted spherical grid points as data, so the contribution lies in the application, the rule for choosing the number of components and the compression accounting, not in a new statistical model.

The closest relative the paper does not mention is computer graphics: Tsai and Shih (2006) and later work represent environment lighting and reflectance lobes as weighted sums of spherical Gaussians, which have the same functional form as unnormalised vMF densities, and that literature has accumulated a lot of experience in fitting spherical functions with a few lobes and analysing the error. Also, a single vMF with K = 1 is roughly the classic main-axis-plus-directivity-index description; the −3 dB region and centre of mass drawn in the paper's Figure 1 are exactly such single-lobe descriptors.

方法Method

能量分布。 每個頻帶的複數指向性函數以 L 階球諧係數表示，取絕對值平方得到方向能量圖，在 720 點的 Fibonacci 球面格點上取樣後正規化成總和為 1，去掉整體音量、只留角度分布。

vMF 混合。 每個成分有平均方向 μ、集中度 κ 與權重 π；κ = 0 是均勻分布，κ 越大瓣越尖。對固定的 K 用加權 EM 擬合（最多 200 次迭代、相對對數概似容差 10⁻⁷、κ 限制在 10⁻⁴ 到 120），初始方向取自彼此有最小角距的能量峰值。成分數從 1 往上加，取第一個讓重建分布與原分布的球面 Pearson 相關 ρ ≥ τ 的 K，上限 20。各頻帶獨立擬合，沒有跨頻率的連續性約束。

壓縮帳目。 原始表示每個頻帶存 (L + 1)² 個複數，也就是 2(L + 1)² 個實數；L = 4 時是 50 個。壓縮表示每個頻帶存一個有效旗標，每個成分存權重、集中度和三維直角座標的平均方向，共 5 個數。全語料 41 × 30 × 50 = 61,500 個實數，雙精度約 492 kB。

Energy distribution. Each band's complex directivity is expressed in order-L spherical-harmonic (SH) coefficients, its squared magnitude gives the directional energy pattern, and that pattern is sampled on a 720-point Fibonacci grid and normalised to sum to one, removing overall level and keeping only the angular distribution.

vMF mixture. Each component has a mean direction μ, a concentration κ and a weight π; κ = 0 is uniform and larger κ means a sharper lobe. For a fixed K the mixture is fitted by weighted EM (at most 200 iterations, relative log-likelihood tolerance 10⁻⁷, κ bounded to 10⁻⁴ to 120), with initial directions taken from energy peaks subject to a minimum angular separation. K grows from one, and the first K whose reconstruction reaches spherical Pearson correlation ρ ≥ τ with the original is kept, up to 20. Bands are fitted independently, with no continuity constraint across frequency.

Compression accounting. The raw representation stores (L + 1)² complex numbers, i.e. 2(L + 1)² reals, per band, which is 50 at L = 4. The compressed one stores a validity flag per band and, per component, a weight, a concentration and a mean direction in three Cartesian coordinates, five numbers in all. The whole corpus is 41 × 30 × 50 = 61,500 reals, about 492 kB in double precision.

限制Limitations

壓縮倍數的分子含有相位，分母沒有。 原始表示存的是複數球諧係數，裡面有相位；vMF 只模型化 |D|² 這個能量圖，相位整個丟掉。光是改存「能量圖的實數球諧係數」，每個頻帶就從 50 個數降到 25 個，已經是 2 倍。所以 4.9 倍裡有相當一部分來自「換了要表示的東西」，不是 vMF 本身的效率。公平的基準是同樣只表示能量、同樣預算的方法，例如截斷到低階的實數球諧（L = 2 只需 9 個數，跟 70% 門檻下平均每頻帶約 10 個數相當），或對整個語料做 PCA；論文一個都沒有比。

「70%」是相關係數，不是保真度。 τ = 0.7 指的是球面 Pearson 相關 0.7，相當於只解釋了約 49% 的變異。距離矩陣相關 0.961 看起來高，但沒有基準可以對照，而樂器家族之間的大差距本來就容易保留，真正難的是家族內的細微差別，這部分論文只用「鄰近關係與分枝長度局部改變」帶過。

高頻的複雜度可能部分來自 4 階截斷。 32 通道陣列限制在 L = 4，高頻時 4 階球諧本身就會產生旁瓣與振鈴。論文把 1 kHz 以上成分數上升解釋成樂器的次瓣與家族特徵，但沒有區分哪些是物理、哪些是截斷誤差。資料也是各音高平均後的指向性，Ackermann 等強調的演奏中的動態變化不在範圍內。

The numerator of the compression factor carries phase; the denominator does not. The raw representation stores complex SH coefficients, phase included, while the vMF mixture models only the energy pattern |D|² and discards phase entirely. Simply storing real SH coefficients of the energy pattern would cut each band from 50 numbers to 25, already a factor of 2. So a good part of the 4.9× comes from changing what is represented, not from the efficiency of vMF itself. A fair baseline would represent energy only under the same budget, for example real SH truncated to low order (L = 2 needs 9 numbers, close to the roughly 10 per band at the 70% target) or corpus-level PCA; the paper compares with neither.

"70%" is a correlation, not a fidelity. τ = 0.7 means a spherical Pearson correlation of 0.7, which explains only about 49% of the variance. A distance-matrix correlation of 0.961 looks high, but there is no baseline to compare it with, and the large gaps between instrument families are easy to preserve anyway; the hard part is fine distinctions within a family, which the paper covers only by saying neighbour relations and branch lengths change locally.

High-frequency complexity may partly reflect the order-4 truncation. The 32-channel array limits analysis to L = 4, and at high frequencies an order-4 expansion produces side lobes and ringing of its own. The paper attributes the rise in component count above 1 kHz to secondary lobes and family-specific detail but does not separate physics from truncation error. The data are also directivities averaged over pitches, so the variation during playing that Ackermann et al. emphasise is out of scope.

討論Discussion

壓縮與保真的取捨曲線。 τ 為 0.5、0.7、0.9、0.95 時，平均壓縮倍數分別是 6.3、4.9、2.5、1.7，對應全語料約 78、100、197、289 kB。成分數集中在高頻：幾百赫茲以下，寬鬆與中等門檻都接近 1 個成分；1 kHz 以上急升，τ = 0.95 時高頻帶要 8 到 10 個，τ = 0.7 只要 2 到 3 個。換句話說，從 0.7 往上加的預算幾乎都花在高頻的細節上，這是論文選 0.7 作為主要操作點的理由。

排列不變描述子。 EM 給成分的編號是任意的，所以作者對每個頻帶算三個與順序無關的量：有效成分數 N_eff = exp(−Σπ log π)、以 A₃(κ) = coth κ − 1/κ 加權的合成向量 r，以及二階方向矩張量 M = Σ π μμᵀ，跨頻帶串接後標準化做 PCA。前兩個主成分只解釋 10.6% 與 7.4% 的變異；銅管在第二主成分偏高、分布較散，木管集中在中央，弦樂在右下且與其他家族部分重疊。

實際意義。 這個表示最有用的地方是可讀性：每個頻帶「幾個瓣、各朝哪裡、多尖、多重」可以直接拿來描述樂器，也能當指向性檢索或學習模型的輸入特徵。但 PCA 前兩軸合計只有 18%，家族分布又互相重疊，論文沒有給任何量化的聚類或分類指標（例如以家族為標籤的準確率或輪廓係數），目前只能當作閱讀輔助，還不能證明這組描述子足以區分樂器。

The compression-fidelity curve. At τ = 0.5, 0.7, 0.9 and 0.95 the mean compression factors are 6.3, 4.9, 2.5 and 1.7, about 78, 100, 197 and 289 kB for the whole corpus. Components concentrate at high frequencies: below a few hundred hertz the relaxed and intermediate targets need close to one component, the count rises sharply above 1 kHz, and τ = 0.95 needs 8 to 10 components in the upper bands against 2 to 3 at τ = 0.7. In other words, almost all budget added beyond 0.7 goes into high-frequency detail, which is why the paper takes 0.7 as its main operating point.

Permutation-invariant descriptors. EM numbers components arbitrarily, so the authors compute three order-free quantities per band: the effective number of components N_eff = exp(−Σπ log π), a resultant vector r weighted by A₃(κ) = coth κ − 1/κ, and the second-order directional moment tensor M = Σ π μμᵀ, concatenated across bands, standardised and projected by PCA. The first two components explain only 10.6% and 7.4% of the variance; brass sits higher and more spread out on the second component, woodwinds cluster near the centre, and strings occupy a lower-right region that partly overlaps the others.

Practical meaning. The representation's main strength is readability: per band, how many lobes, where each points, how sharp and how strong can describe an instrument directly and can serve as input features for directivity retrieval or learning. But the first two PCA axes cover only 18% together and the family regions overlap, and the paper reports no quantitative clustering or classification measure (such as family-label accuracy or a silhouette score), so for now it is a reading aid and does not yet show that the descriptors are enough to tell instruments apart.
