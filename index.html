MAJEPPA: Morphing and Assessing in a Unified Piano Performance Space

MAJEPPA：在統一鋼琴演奏空間中同時做形變與評估

Jinwen Zhou, Huan Zhang, Weixi Zhai, Jinhua Liang, Aidan O. T. Hogg, Simon Dixon
arXiv:2608.11026 · eess.AS, cs.MM · 投稿submitted 2026-08-11
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：現有鋼琴演奏理解研究把「風格」「準確度」「表現力」「技巧偵測」當成互相獨立的任務分開做，資料也幾乎只收錄職業級演奏，兒童初學者的錄音幾乎不存在。這篇論文蒐集了橫跨六種程度（兒童初學到大師）與六種錄音情境（練習到音樂會）共約4,000段錄音，並把JEPA（Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture，先前只用在影像與影片上）第一次搬進符號音樂的樂譜—演奏配對，用同一個表徵空間同時支援生成與抽象評估。它老實報告了生成能力「因時間不足沒有完整評估」，這種公開留白比包裝完美的結果更值得记录。Why this one: Existing piano-performance research treats style, accuracy, expressivity, and technique detection as separate tasks, and almost all training data comes from professional recordings, leaving child-beginner practice all but absent. This paper assembles roughly 4,000 recordings spanning six expertise levels (child beginner to virtuoso) and six recording contexts (practice to concert), and is the first to bring JEPA (Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture, previously applied only to images and video) into symbolic music's score-performance pairs, using one shared representation space for both generation and abstract assessment. It's flagged as a deep dive partly because the authors candidly admit generation quality was never fully evaluated due to time constraints — an honest gap that's more useful than a polished-looking result would be.

動機Motivation

鋼琴演奏橫跨從兒童初學者到職業音樂會演奏家的完整光譜，但既有系統把「這段演奏彈得好不好」「跟哪位鋼琴家的風格接近」「用了什麼演奏技巧」「有沒有彈錯音」當成彼此獨立的任務，各自訓練各自的模型。作者認為這種切分是本末倒置：一個統一的演奏表徵才是更根本的東西，各種高層次判斷應該是從這個表徵裡「衍生」出來，而不是分別學習。要建立這樣的表徵，必須先解決兩個缺口。第一個是資料缺口：現有的符號演奏資料集幾乎全部只收錄職業演奏，初學者、練習中途、帶錯誤的演奏幾乎沒有被系統性蒐集過，但這些「不完美」的演奏正是評估系統最需要理解的對象。第二個是方法缺口：目前沒有任何框架能同時捕捉「抽象的演奏特徵」（風格、準確度、表現力）並把它跟對應的樂譜連結起來——大部分模型要嘛只做重建式的遮罩預訓練，要嘛只做生成，沒有一個框架把「評估」與「生成」放在同一個表徵裡處理。

Piano performance spans a full spectrum from child beginners to professional concert virtuosi, yet existing systems treat questions like "how good is this performance," "whose style does it resemble," "what technique was used," and "were there mistakes" as separate tasks, each trained in isolation. The authors argue this division has things backwards: a unified representation of performance is the more foundational object, and these higher-level judgments should be derived from it rather than learned independently for each task. Building such a representation requires closing two gaps. The first is a data gap: existing symbolic performance datasets focus almost exclusively on professional recordings, leaving beginner practice, mid-session mistakes, and imperfect playing — exactly what an assessment system most needs to understand — essentially uncollected. The second is a methodological gap: no existing framework simultaneously captures abstract performance characteristics (style, accuracy, expressivity) while linking them to the underlying score; most models either do reconstruction-based masked pretraining or pure generation, with none placing assessment and generation in the same representation space.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把相關研究分成兩條線。第一條是音樂表徵學習：遮罩重建式的方法如MusicBERT、MidiBERT-Piano學習局部結構；自迴歸基礎模型如Moonbeam（規模達18B參數）著重生成能力；CLaMP 3之類的跨模態對齊方法把音樂與文字或圖片連結起來。但這些方法對「演奏層級」的理解——同一份樂譜被不同程度的演奏者彈奏後產生的差異——仍然零散地分散在各自獨立的研究裡，沒有整合。第二條是JEPA（Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture）：這個架構的核心想法是不透過像素或token級的重建去學表徵，而是直接在潛在空間裡從「部分上下文」預測「目標的潛在表示」，藉此逼迫模型學到抽象、語意層級的結構而非表面細節。I-JEPA把這個想法用在影像上，V-JEPA 2延伸到影片，兩者都證明了預測潛在表示比逐像素重建更能學到有用的抽象特徵。這篇論文的關鍵洞察是：樂譜與演奏正好構成一組天然的「兩個視角」——樂譜是抽象的意圖，演奏是具體的實現——非常適合套用JEPA的預測框架，但截至目前沒有人把JEPA用在符號音樂的樂譜-演奏配對上。

The paper divides related work into two threads. The first is music representation learning: masked-reconstruction methods like MusicBERT and MidiBERT-Piano learn local structure; autoregressive foundation models such as Moonbeam (scaling to 18B parameters) emphasize generation; and cross-modal alignment approaches like CLaMP 3 connect music to text or images. But performance-level understanding — how the same score played by musicians of different skill produces different results — remains scattered across isolated studies rather than integrated into one framework. The second thread is JEPA (Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture): its core idea is to learn representations not through pixel- or token-level reconstruction but by predicting a target's latent representation directly from partial context in latent space, forcing the model toward abstract, semantically meaningful structure rather than surface detail. I-JEPA applied this to images and V-JEPA 2 extended it to video, both showing that predicting latents beats pixel-wise reconstruction for learning useful abstractions. The paper's key insight is that score and performance naturally form a two-view pair — the score is abstract intent, the performance is concrete realization — an ideal fit for JEPA's predictive framework, yet no prior work had applied JEPA to symbolic music's score-performance pairs.

方法Method

資料集構建：團隊蒐集了3,979段錄音、涵蓋942首曲目，共303.2小時，橫跨六種程度（兒童初學、兒童專業、成人初學、成人中階、鋼琴教師、大師）與六種錄音情境（練習、視奏、展演、示範、慢速示範、音樂會）。錄音來源是YouTube上的教學頻道、教程影片與比賽錄影，並由音樂院的標註者用校準文件與參照片段手動標籤。處理流程包含：長影片的斷點分段、用Transkun把音訊轉成MIDI、用子序列動態時間校正（subsequence DTW）對齊樂譜與演奏（處理彈錯、漏彈、重複段落等狀況）、最後切成固定長度的視窗。

模型架構：核心想法是把樂譜與演奏視為同一份音樂內容的兩個互補視角，模型學習在共享的潛在空間裡從樂譜表示預測演奏表示。以Aria-medium（632M參數）作為骨幹，凍結原本權重、加上LoRA微調（7500萬可訓練參數），用單一[PRED\]預測token透過綁定自注意力機制運作，並加入類別條件token（[COND_X]代表演奏者、[COND_Y]代表錄音情境），再接一個512維投影頭做對比學習。

雙通道訓練：Pass 1用因果遮罩，是標準的自迴歸生成——演奏token可以看到完整的前文，讓模型學會依樂譜生成演奏；Pass 2用區塊對角遮罩實現JEPA目標——樂譜視角與演奏視角彼此看不到對方，強迫模型只能從樂譜抽象地預測演奏,而不是靠捷徑對齊。

損失函數由四項組成：ℒ = γℒ_gen + λ(ℒ_piece + βℒ_cond + αℒ_self)，其中ℒ_gen是演奏token的下一個token交叉熵、ℒ_piece是InfoNCE損失把預測出的樂譜嵌入對齊到配對的演奏嵌入、ℒ_cond是用條件標籤間連續相似度做的軟性監督對比損失、ℒ_self是同一段演奏不同片段間的對稱InfoNCE。訓練用4張A100-80GB跑120個epoch,批次大小32(每GPU 8,梯度累積4),透過線性RoPE縮放把上下文窗口擴充到4096個token。

評估基準EVPMR涵蓋五類下游任務：演奏評分回歸(NeuroPiano、YCU-PPE-III、PISA三個資料集)、蕭邦鋼琴大賽初賽排名(130場演奏的配對排名)、七類技巧偵測、八位鋼琴家身分辨識(Pianist8)、以及無參照錯誤偵測(UMP,在三個資料集上做token級的明顯錯誤偵測)。所有任務都用凍結的表徵接線性探測器評估。

Dataset construction: The team assembled 3,979 recordings across 942 pieces (303.2 total hours), spanning six expertise levels (child beginner, child professional, adult beginner, adult intermediate, piano teacher, virtuoso) and six recording contexts (practice, sight-reading, showcase, demo, slow demo, concert). Recordings were sourced from pedagogical YouTube channels, tutorial videos, and competition footage, and manually labeled by conservatoire-trained annotators using calibration documents and reference clips. The processing pipeline includes breakpoint segmentation of long videos, audio-to-MIDI transcription via Transkun, subsequence dynamic time warping to align score and performance (handling mistakes, omissions, and repeats), and fixed-length windowing for model input.

Model architecture: The core idea treats score and performance as two complementary views of the same musical content, with the model learning to predict performance representations from score representations in a shared latent space. It builds on an Aria-medium backbone (632M parameters), frozen with LoRA adaptation (75M trainable parameters), using a single [PRED] token operating via tied self-attention, plus categorical conditioning tokens ([COND_X] for performer, [COND_Y] for recording context) and a 512-dimensional projection head for the contrastive objectives.

Two-pass training: Pass 1 uses a causal mask for standard autoregressive generation — performance tokens attend to the full preceding context, teaching the model to generate performance from score. Pass 2 uses a block-diagonal mask for the JEPA objective — score view and performance view cannot see each other, forcing the model to predict abstractly from score alone rather than taking an alignment shortcut.

The training loss combines four terms: ℒ = γℒ_gen + λ(ℒ_piece + βℒ_cond + αℒ_self), where ℒ_gen is next-token cross-entropy over performance tokens, ℒ_piece is an InfoNCE loss aligning the predicted score embedding with its paired performance embedding, ℒ_cond is a soft supervised-contrastive loss using continuous similarity between condition tags, and ℒ_self is a symmetric InfoNCE over segments of the same performance. Training runs on 4x A100-80GB GPUs for 120 epochs, batch size 32 (8 per GPU, gradient accumulation 4), with the context window extended to 4096 tokens via linear RoPE scaling.

The EVPMR benchmark covers five downstream task categories: performance-quality regression (NeuroPiano, YCU-PPE-III, PISA), Chopin Competition preliminary-round ranking (pairwise ranking over 130 performances), 7-way technique detection, 8-way pianist identification (Pianist8), and unreferenced mistake prediction (token-level detection of conspicuous mistakes across three datasets). All tasks are evaluated with linear probes on frozen representations.

限制Limitations

作者自己列出了幾個限制。第一，生成能力的評估不完整：論文承認「因時間限制,沒有對生成能力做完整評估」，只把樣本與互動式展示放到補充網站上,沒有系統性的量化結果——這代表雙通道架構裡負責生成的那一半,其實際輸出品質尚未被嚴謹檢驗過。第二,轉譜品質未經查核：Transkun雖被稱為「音符層級的最先進方法」,但作者「沒有明確檢查轉譜品質(包含踏板痕跡)」,意味著資料標籤本身可能帶有未知的轉譜誤差。第三,踏板處理的侷限：Aria的tokenizer沒有明確的踏板token(延音被折疊進音符時長裡),所以跟Aria基線的比較沒辦法把踏板這個獨立的表現線索單獨隔離出來評估。第四,資料權利的隱憂:雖然影片是公開可得的,但上傳者原本擁有的權利並未被放棄,作者承諾若被要求會移除相關內容,這代表資料集的長期可用性存在不確定性。整體而言,這篇論文的核心賣點(表徵學習)驗證得相當扎實,但配套的生成能力幾乎是空白,讀者不該把「JEPA表徵學得好」直接推論成「這個系統也能生成高品質演奏」。

The authors list several limitations themselves. First, generation is incompletely evaluated: the paper admits that "due to time constraints, we did not conduct a full-scale evaluation of the generation capabilities," deferring samples and interactive demos to a supplementary website rather than reporting systematic quantitative results — meaning the generative half of the two-pass architecture has not actually been rigorously checked for output quality. Second, transcription quality is unverified: Transkun is called "note-level state of the art," but the authors "did not explicitly check transcription quality (including pedal artefacts)," meaning the dataset's own labels may carry unknown transcription error. Third, pedal handling is limited: Aria's tokenizer has no explicit pedal tokens (sustain is folded into note durations), so the comparison against the Aria baseline cannot isolate pedalling as a discrete expressive cue. Fourth, there's a data-rights concern: although the recordings are publicly available, uploaders' underlying rights were never waived, and the authors commit to removing content on request — meaning the dataset's long-term availability is uncertain. Overall, the paper's core claim (representation learning) is validated fairly rigorously, but the paired generation capability is nearly untested — readers shouldn't infer from "the JEPA representation learns well" that "this system can also generate high-quality performances."

討論Discussion

這篇論文自稱是「第一個用JEPA做符號鋼琴演奏聯合生成與表徵學習」的工作,把樂譜-演奏配對當成自然的兩視角結構。從結果來看,MAJEPPA的各種變體在大多數任務上都超越四個基線(Aria、Moonbeam、MusicBERT、CLaMP 3):蕭邦排名從Aria的47.5%提升到59.7%,技巧偵測從66.2%提升到79.0%,PISA品質回歸的MAE從0.122降到0.090。消融實驗顯示單獨用ℒ_piece就已經能跟基線競爭,加上ℒ_cond對「標籤可分辨」型的任務有幫助,加上ℒ_self能進一步降低回歸誤差,但會稍微犧牲鋼琴家辨識的準確度——這代表「風格身分辨識」跟「演奏品質評估」在表徵空間裡可能存在某種張力,不是完全同向的目標。質性分析也頗有意思:七段YouTube上的練習歷程裡,session編號跟主成分之間的Spearman相關係數達到0.41,顯示模型在沒有時間監督訊號的情況下,學到了某種「練習進步」的方向性;UMAP投影也顯示,節奏型錯誤(搶拍、雙手脫節)跟音高型錯誤(摸錯鍵、彈錯音)在潛在空間裡形成了可分的群集。這些發現支持了論文的核心主張——一個統一的表徵空間,確實能同時承載風格、準確度與教學結構等多種訊號,而不需要為每個任務單獨訓練模型。不過如前述,生成能力那一半仍是留白,這意味著「統一表徵」目前主要體現在評估與辨識任務上,能不能真正支撐高品質的可控生成,還是一個懸而未決的問題。

The paper positions itself as "the first JEPA to model for joint symbolic piano-performance generation and representation learning," treating score-performance pairs as a natural two-view structure. On results, MAJEPPA variants outperform four baselines (Aria, Moonbeam, MusicBERT, CLaMP 3) on most tasks: Chopin ranking rises from Aria's 47.5% to 59.7%, technique detection from 66.2% to 79.0%, and PISA quality-regression MAE drops from 0.122 to 0.090. Ablations show ℒ_piece alone is already competitive with baselines, adding ℒ_cond helps tag-discriminative tasks, and adding ℒ_self further reduces regression error but slightly hurts pianist identification — suggesting some tension between "style identity" and "quality assessment" in the representation space rather than both pulling in the same direction. The qualitative analysis is also interesting: across seven YouTube practice journeys, Spearman correlation between session index and the leading principal component reaches 0.41, showing the model captures a directional sense of practice progress without any temporal supervision signal; UMAP projections likewise show timing-type mistakes (rushed entries, hands falling out of sync) and pitch-type mistakes (mistouches, wrong notes) forming separable clusters in latent space. These findings support the paper's central claim — that a unified representation space really can carry style, accuracy, and pedagogical structure simultaneously without training a separate model per task. But as noted above, the generative half remains untested, meaning the "unified representation" so far has mainly proven itself on assessment and identification tasks, and whether it can genuinely support high-quality controllable generation remains an open question.

A Dataset and Benchmark for Optical Music Recognition of String Quartet Scores

弦樂四重奏樂譜光學辨識的資料集與基準測試

Dongmin Kim, Brian Liu, Jose J. Valero-Mas, Dasaem Jeong
arXiv:2608.10978 · cs.CV, cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-11
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：光學音樂辨識（OMR）在單聲部與鋼琴譜上已經有明顯進展，但「多聲部樂譜」——像弦樂四重奏這種四個聲部同時演奏、疊在同一個系統上——一直因為缺資料集而停滯不前。這篇論文花了超過一百小時手工把OpenScore String Quartet corpus的樂譜跟IMSLP掃描本逐音符對齊，產出第一個專門給多聲部OMR用的資料集，並用兩種代表性架構、三種編碼格式（LMXE、**kern、ABC）跑出完整的基準結果。它把「編碼格式的選擇」這個常被忽略的變因量化到具體數字，是扎實但不追求架構新穎性的資料集論文，因此不列為深度分析。Why this one: Optical music recognition (OMR) has advanced substantially on monophonic and piano-form scores, but multi-part scores — like string quartets, where four independent voices stack on a single system — have stalled for lack of a suitable dataset. This paper spent over 100 hours manually re-aligning the OpenScore String Quartet corpus against IMSLP scans at the symbol level, producing the first dataset dedicated to multi-part OMR, and runs a full benchmark across two representative architectures and three encoding formats (LMXE, **kern, ABC). It quantifies the often-overlooked effect of encoding-format choice with concrete numbers — a solid dataset-and-benchmark contribution rather than a novel-architecture one, so it isn't flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

光學音樂辨識（OMR）把樂譜影像轉成結構化的數位格式，對保存音樂文化遺產、以及後續用MIR技術分析符號化樂譜都很關鍵。深度學習近年大幅推進了這個領域，但幾乎所有成果都集中在單聲部樂譜（因為歷史文獻豐富、任務形式單純）和鋼琴譜（如GrandStaff資料集）。「多聲部樂譜」——多個聲部同時由不同樂器演奏——的進展則相對有限，主因是缺乏合適的資料集。弦樂四重奏是這類樂譜的典型代表：一個系統裡四個獨立聲部並排，比鋼琴譜的複雜度更高，還帶來音域寬廣、頻繁的加線、以及樂章內外的譜號變化等額外挑戰。這篇論文正是為了填補這個資料缺口而生。

Optical music recognition (OMR) transcribes score images into structured digital formats, playing a key role in preserving musical cultural heritage and enabling downstream MIR analysis of symbolic scores. Deep learning has advanced the field substantially in recent years, but almost all progress concentrates on monophonic scores (due to abundant historical sources and a simple task formulation) and piano-form scores (as in the GrandStaff dataset). Multi-part scores — where multiple voices are performed simultaneously by different instruments — have seen comparatively limited progress, largely for lack of a suitable dataset. String quartets are a prototypical case: four independent parts sit side by side on a single system, substantially increasing complexity beyond piano-form notation and introducing further challenges such as wide pitch ranges, frequent ledger lines, and clef changes across and within movements. This paper exists to close that data gap.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者把OMR的既有研究分成三個世代。單聲部OMR是最成熟的一支，代表資料集有PrIMUS、Capitan、Seils、FMT、Guatemala，主要處理素歌等歷史文獻。鋼琴譜（grand staff）OMR近年由GrandStaff系列資料集帶動，發展出Sheet Music Transformer等端對端的編碼器—解碼器架構，把卷積視覺編碼器（如ConvNeXt）跟序列解碼器（RNN、Transformer）結合，直接輸出線性化的符號序列而不需要先做符號切割。多聲部OMR則基本上是空白，這也是這篇論文鎖定的缺口。論文選擇弦樂四重奏作為切入點，是因為它有現成、品質良好的數位樂譜語料庫可以擴充——OpenScore String Quartet corpus，收錄116首曲子、由47位作曲家橫跨1773到1994年寫成，並已提供乾淨的MuseScore編碼與渲染圖像。作者要做的，是把這份「乾淨版」樂譜跟IMSLP上「真實掃描版」的歷史印刷本或手稿逐音符對齊，讓兩者在token層級完全對應。

The authors divide prior OMR work into three generations. Monophonic OMR is the most mature strand, with representative datasets including PrIMUS, Capitan, Seils, FMT, and Guatemala, mostly targeting historical sources such as plainchant. Piano-form (grand staff) OMR has been driven in recent years by the GrandStaff dataset family, producing end-to-end encoder-decoder architectures such as Sheet Music Transformer, which pair convolutional visual encoders (e.g. ConvNeXt) with sequential decoders (RNN, Transformer) to directly output linearized symbolic sequences without upfront symbol segmentation. Multi-part OMR is essentially blank, and that is precisely the gap this paper targets. The authors chose string quartets as the entry point because a ready-made, high-quality digital score corpus already exists to extend — the OpenScore String Quartet corpus, comprising 116 pieces by 47 composers spanning 1773 to 1994, already offering clean MuseScore encodings and rendered images. Their job is to align this "clean" digital score against its "real scanned" historical print or manuscript counterpart on IMSLP, note by note, so the two match at the token level.

方法Method

掃描本蒐集與分類：團隊從IMSLP取回對應的掃描樂譜，分成三類：全譜（93首，80%，四個聲部同時印在一個系統上，絕大多數是排版印刷版）、分譜本（17首，15%，反映18、19世紀把室內樂拆成單一樂器分冊出版的習慣）、以及手稿（6首，5%，全部出自女性作曲家之手，品質參差不齊）。

視覺對齊與校正：OpenScore的編輯流程會把原始掃描本裡的印刷錯誤、過時的刻版慣例等不一致之處「撫平」成乾淨、適合演出的版本，但OMR需要的是渲染影像跟掃描本在token層級完全吻合的編碼。因此作者針對93份全譜掃描逐一手動重新編輯MuseScore檔案，讓渲染結果在視覺上對齊掃描本，這個過程耗時超過100小時，並把每一次編輯都記錄成Git版本歷史以便追溯。校正分成版面編輯（調整系統、頁面、段落斷點）與記譜編輯（修改譜號、符桿方向、圓滑線、連結線、顫音、符尾樑、演奏記號等音樂內容）。93份全譜裡有78份同時需要版面與記譜編輯，只有1份完全不用修改；最常見的編輯類別是音符（音高）修正，共7,183處。

影像切割：用三階段的YOLOv8模型管線，依序做系統邊界框偵測、五線譜高度回歸（把譜表縮放到讓符頭約佔4-5像素高，符合下游OMR模型的輸入尺度）、以及單一五線譜邊界框偵測，合成影像與掃描影像共用同一套管線。

三種符號編碼格式：LMXE（Extended Linearized MusicXML）是作者擴充既有Linearized MusicXML而來，加入明確的聲部token與譜型token（單聲部、多聲部、大譜表），並採用「小節內巢狀各聲部」的編碼順序，讓部分輸出即使解碼中斷也還能重建；kern用tab分隔的「軌道」（spine）組織聲部與譜表，結構限制較嚴格；ABC用ASCII助憶符號，結構鬆散但文字形式簡單，適合一般語言模型。所有格式都從同一條MuseScore匯出管線衍生，並用OMR-NED做往返轉換驗證，任何在往返過程中出現結構性或重大音樂錯誤（如小節插入/刪除、譜號錯誤等）的樣本會被剔除——kern與ABC轉換分別損失約5%與21%的系統級樣本。

最終規模：24,544張系統級影像、98,172張譜表級影像，來自116份合成樂譜與93份掃描樂譜。基準測試用Zeus（LSTM序列到序列模型，加大隱藏層到256維）與Sheet Music Transformer（SMT，ConvNeXt視覺編碼器+Transformer解碼器）兩個代表性架構，在四種樂譜層級的隨機切分（測試集互斥）上、對九種tokenization方案分別訓練到收斂，共136次獨立訓練跑，用OMR-NED（在music21符號空間裡計算插入與刪除數的正規化編輯距離）評分。

Scan collection and categorization: The team retrieved corresponding scanned scores from IMSLP and grouped them into three categories: full scores (93 pieces, 80%, all four parts on one system, predominantly typeset editions), part-books (17 pieces, 15%, reflecting 18th/19th-century chamber-music publishing as separate single-instrument booklets), and manuscripts (6 pieces, 5%, all by women composers, of varying quality).

Visual alignment and correction: OpenScore's editorial process smooths inconsistencies in the original scans — print errors, outdated typesetting conventions — into a clean, performance-ready edition, but OMR needs encodings whose rendered image matches the scan at the token level. The authors therefore manually re-edited the MuseScore files for all 93 full-score scans so the rendering visually aligns with the scan, a process spanning over 100 hours, with every edit recorded as an auditable Git commit history. Corrections split into layout edits (system, page, section breaks) and notation edits (clefs, stem direction, slurs, ties, tremolos, beaming, articulations). Of the 93 full scores, 78 needed both kinds of edits and only 1 needed none; the most common edit category was pitch correction, at 7,183 instances.

Image segmentation: A three-stage YOLOv8 pipeline sequentially performs system bounding-box detection, staff-height regression (resizing so noteheads are roughly 4-5 pixels tall, matching downstream OMR model input scale), and individual staff bounding-box detection — the same pipeline is shared across synthetic and scanned images.

Three symbolic encoding formats: LMXE (Extended Linearized MusicXML) extends existing Linearized MusicXML with explicit part tokens and a score-type token (single, multi, grandstaff), using a measure-wise nesting order across parts so partial decoder output remains reconstructable even if decoding breaks off early. kern organizes voices and staves into tab-separated "spines," a comparatively rigid structural format; ABC uses loosely structured ASCII mnemonics whose textual simplicity suits general-purpose language models. All formats derive from the same MuseScore export pipeline and are verified via round-trip conversion checked with OMR-NED — any sample showing structural or major musical errors on round-trip (measure insert/delete, wrong clef, etc.) is excluded from release, with kern and ABC conversion losing roughly 5% and 21% of system-level samples respectively.

Final scale: 24,544 system-level images and 98,172 staff-level images, drawn from 116 synthetic and 93 scanned scores. The benchmark trains two representative architectures — Zeus (an LSTM sequence-to-sequence model with hidden size increased to 256) and Sheet Music Transformer (SMT, a ConvNeXt visual encoder plus Transformer decoder) — across four mutually-exclusive-test-set random score-level splits and nine tokenization schemes, 136 independent training runs total, scored with OMR-NED (a normalized edit distance over insertions and deletions computed in music21's symbolic space).

限制Limitations

論文本身把限制講得相當清楚。首先，架構本身沒有創新：兩個基線Zeus與SMT都是既有的piano-form OMR架構直接拿來訓練，貢獻在於資料與基準，而不是模型設計，這也是這篇沒被列為「深度分析」的原因。其次，SMT對真實掃描本的遷移能力明顯較弱：從合成到掃描的表現下滑（domain shift）在全部34種設定裡都存在，但Zeus平均只退化39%（3.6個百分點），SMT卻退化100%（6.7個百分點）——論文坦承「這個落差的架構性根源，在這些實驗裡並未被單獨釐清」，也就是說作者知道SMT明顯較不穩健，但沒有進一步拆解原因。第三，外部基準Legato（在PDMX上預訓練的ABC模型）在這個資料集上表現遠差於論文自己訓練的基線（系統級36.1%對比自家SMT-CABC的6.0%），顯示光靠泛用的ABC預訓練不足以應付弦樂四重奏這種多聲部任務，但這也代表現有的通用OMR模型在遇到新譜面型態時遷移能力有限。第四，範圍侷限在弦樂四重奏（固定四聲部），論文自己在結論裡承認要往更大編制（如管弦樂總譜）推進，還需要更豐富的多聲部基準資料，目前的方法能否直接擴展到八件、十幾件樂器的總譜還不確定。第五，全部評估都在系統或譜表切好的影像上進行，還沒有處理「整頁」端對端辨識，論文把這列為未來方向而非本篇已解決的問題。

The paper is quite transparent about its own limitations. First, there's no architectural novelty: both baselines, Zeus and SMT, are existing piano-form OMR architectures trained as-is, with the contribution lying in the data and benchmark rather than model design — which is also why this entry isn't flagged as a deep dive. Second, SMT transfers noticeably worse to real scans: synthetic-to-scanned degradation appears across all 34 configurations, but Zeus degrades by only 39% (3.6 percentage points) on average while SMT degrades by 100% (6.7 points) — the paper candidly states "the architectural source of this gap is not isolated by these experiments," meaning the authors know SMT is markedly less robust but haven't diagnosed why. Third, the external Legato baseline (an ABC-based model pretrained on PDMX) performs far worse on this dataset than the paper's own trained baselines (36.1% system-wise vs. their own SMT-CABC at 6.0%), showing that general-purpose ABC pretraining alone is insufficient for multi-part string-quartet OMR — which also implies limited transfer for existing general OMR models when faced with an unfamiliar score layout. Fourth, the scope is fixed at string quartets (always four parts); the authors acknowledge in the conclusion that scaling to larger ensembles (e.g. orchestral scores) will require richer multi-part benchmarks, and whether the current approach extends directly to scores with eight or more instruments is unclear. Fifth, all evaluation happens on pre-segmented system- or staff-level images rather than full-page end-to-end recognition, which the paper explicitly lists as future work rather than something this study has already solved.

討論Discussion

基準測試最重要的發現是：表徵選擇比模型架構更重要。LMXE在兩個模型、兩種切割層級上都穩定拿下最低的OMR-NED，其次是字元層級的ABC（CABC）；kern系的編碼落後一截；用byte-pair encoding壓縮過的ABC反而是最差的——而且Zeus的表現隨著BPE詞彙表變大而單調惡化，顯示縮短序列長度並不能換來更好的辨識準確率。另一個發現是譜表層級的切割一律優於整個系統層級：理論上系統層級的輸入應該能利用聲部間的上下文線索（例如從其他聲部推斷拍號、從縱向對齊推斷音符時值），但作者檢查了跟這類線索直接相關的錯誤類別（音符、符頭），發現系統層級的錯誤率並沒有比譜表層級低，代表這兩個基線架構其實沒有真的利用到這些跨聲部線索——這暗示了未來要設計「真正能整合多聲部上下文」的架構，還有明顯的進步空間。最佳設定（Zeus + LMXE + 譜表層級）在合成輸入上達到3.6% OMR-NED，在掃描輸入上是5.9%，證明多聲部OMR這件事在技術上是可行的，但掃描本上仍有清楚的進步空間。整體而言，這篇論文的價值在於把「多聲部OMR」從一個沒有資料集、無從量化的空白，變成一個有明確基準數字、可以追蹤進步的研究題目，這對MIR領域的樂譜數位化與後續分析都是紮實的基礎建設。

The most important finding from the benchmark is that representation choice matters more than model architecture. LMXE consistently achieves the lowest OMR-NED across both models and both segmentation levels, followed closely by character-level ABC (CABC); kern-based encodings trail a step behind; and byte-pair-encoded ABC is the weakest of all — Zeus's performance degrades monotonically as the BPE vocabulary grows, showing that shortening sequences doesn't buy better recognition accuracy. Another finding is that staff-level segmentation beats system-level segmentation across the board: in principle, system-level input should benefit from cross-part context (inferring time signatures from concurrent voices, note durations from vertical alignment), but the authors checked error categories directly tied to such cues (note, notehead) and found system-level error rates were no lower than staff-level ones — meaning neither baseline architecture actually exploits this cross-part context, which suggests real room for future architectures explicitly designed to integrate multi-part context. The best configuration (Zeus + LMXE + staff-level) reaches 3.6% OMR-NED on synthetic input and 5.9% on scanned input, confirming multi-part OMR is technically feasible while leaving clear headroom on scanned sources. Overall, this paper's value lies in turning multi-part OMR from a blank spot with no dataset and no way to quantify progress into a research topic with concrete benchmark numbers that future work can track — solid infrastructure for score digitization and downstream analysis in MIR.

MazzikaAI: A knowledge-based performance-to-prompt compiler for real-time Arabic maqam accompaniment with a streaming text-to-music model

MazzikaAI：把演奏編譯成即時提示詞，讓串流式文字轉音樂模型伴奏阿拉伯調式音樂

Jiaxin Du, Boulbaba Abdeljaouad, Yong Zhuang, Haoyu Li
arXiv:2608.10360 · cs.HC, cs.AI, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-11
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：生成式音樂模型幾乎都用西方等律音樂訓練，遇到阿拉伯調式（maqam）這種微分音、以呼應式（call-and-response）為核心的傳統就顯得力不從心；串流式文字轉音樂模型（如Google Lyria RealTime）雖然能即時生成，卻沒有精準的控制介面。這篇論文不去微調模型，而是設計一個知識庫驅動的「演奏→提示詞」即時編譯器，把MIDI、手勢、推斷出的和聲即時編譯成不斷更新的文字提示，用純提示工程去引導一個完全沒改動過的商用生成模型演奏六種阿拉伯調式。它的評估相當紮實：量測了逐次重新提示的延遲（中位數263毫秒）、8.5分鐘實測session零掉線、還做了「有無調式知識」的消融實驗直接量化微分音的呈現率，是少見把即時互動系統的工程細節攤開來檢驗的論文，因此標記為深度分析。Why this one: Generative music models are trained almost entirely on Western equal-tempered music, leaving them ill-equipped for Arabic maqam traditions built on microtonal intervals and call-and-response phrasing; streaming text-to-music models (like Google Lyria RealTime) can generate in real time but lack precise control interfaces. Rather than fine-tuning the model, this paper builds a knowledge-based "performance-to-prompt" real-time compiler that turns live MIDI, gesture, and inferred harmony into continuously updated text prompts, steering an entirely unmodified commercial generator through six Arabic maqamat using pure prompt engineering. The evaluation is unusually rigorous: it measures re-prompting latency (263ms median), reports zero dropped connections across an 8.5-minute live session, and runs a grounding-on/off ablation that directly quantifies how much microtonal content actually appears in the output — a rare case of an interactive real-time system's engineering details being laid bare for scrutiny, hence flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

阿拉伯調式（maqam）音樂具有微分音（例如bayati調式裡「半降B」的第二音級，被作者稱為「這個調式表現力的靈魂」）、調式性、以及裝飾性的呼應式（call-and-response）結構，但生成式音樂模型的訓練框架幾乎全部建立在西方、等律的音樂傳統上，是目前最被忽視的音樂傳統之一。即時伴奏更放大了這個落差：一個AI夥伴必須即時聆聽、動態適應，同時尊重這種微分音的調式結構。串流式文字轉音樂模型（如Google Lyria RealTime）提供了強大的生成能力，卻缺乏精準的控制介面——使用者能做的往往只是換一句提示詞，無法逐拍、逐樂句地即時調整。作者選擇的路線很清楚：不對模型微調（訓練資料有限、算力成本高），而是把自然語言本身當成一個即時控制迴路的「致動器」（actuator），透過持續即時編譯的文字提示去操控一個完全沒動過的既有生成模型。

Arabic maqam music is microtonal, modal, and built on ornamented call-and-response phrasing — for instance, the half-flat second degree in bayati, which the authors call "the expressive soul" of that mode — yet generative music models' training frameworks are built almost entirely on Western, equal-tempered traditions, making maqam one of the most underserved musical traditions today. Real-time accompaniment sharpens this gap further: an AI partner must listen and adapt dynamically while still respecting these microtonal modal structures. Streaming text-to-music models (like Google Lyria RealTime) offer strong generative capability but lack a precise control interface — users can typically only swap in a new prompt, with no way to adjust phrase-by-phrase or beat-by-beat in real time. The authors' chosen path is deliberate: rather than fine-tuning the model (limited training data, high compute cost), they treat natural language itself as the actuator of a real-time control loop, steering an entirely unmodified existing generator through continuously and dynamically compiled text prompts.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把自己放在五條研究脈絡的交會點上。第一是知識型符號系統（如CHORAL）：把音樂專業知識編碼成明確規則、確定性地套用，MazzikaAI保留了這種「可檢視」的特性，但把符號式的合成換成了神經網路生成。第二是互動式與共同創作系統（Voyager、The Continuator）：透過輪流交替協商演奏的主導權，MazzikaAI用語言媒介的控制複製了這個模式。第三是神經音樂生成：涵蓋符號模型（Music Transformer）、音訊模型（MusicLM、MusicGen）、以及串流變體（Lyria RealTime）——這些串流模型雖然接受session中途更新提示，卻沒有自動的即時操控機制。第四是「提示詞作為控制」：在NLP脈絡下這通常是一次性的操作，MazzikaAI把它擴充成「連續閉迴路」的音訊生成控制，提示詞的角色從靜態查詢變成即時致動器。第五是非西方調式音樂的電腦運算研究：過去多半聚焦在分析而非生成，微分音音程與調式結構在訓練資料與控制詞彙裡都嚴重不足。這五條線索加總起來，定義了MazzikaAI試圖填補的具體缺口：一個不需要訓練資料、不需要微調、卻能精準控制非西方調式即時生成的方法。

The paper positions itself at the intersection of five research threads. First, knowledge-based symbolic systems (e.g. CHORAL) encoded musical expertise as explicit rules applied deterministically; MazzikaAI keeps this inspectability while swapping symbolic synthesis for neural generation. Second, interactive and co-creative systems (Voyager, The Continuator) negotiated performance agency through turn-taking; MazzikaAI replicates this pattern using language-mediated control. Third, neural music generation spans symbolic models (Music Transformer), audio models (MusicLM, MusicGen), and streaming variants (Lyria RealTime) — these streaming models accept mid-session prompt updates but lack any automatic steering mechanism. Fourth, prompting as control typically operates one-shot in NLP contexts; MazzikaAI extends this into continuous closed-loop audio-generation control, turning the prompt from a static query into a real-time actuator. Fifth, computational work on non-Western modal music has focused mostly on analysis rather than generation, with microtonal intervals and modal structure badly underrepresented in both training data and control vocabularies. Together these five threads define the specific gap MazzikaAI targets: a method that needs no training data and no fine-tuning, yet still precisely controls real-time generation of non-Western modal music.

方法Method

系統架構分三層：瀏覽器端（React/TypeScript）擷取MIDI鍵盤輸入、手勢（用MediaPipe偵測）、語音指令、以及樂譜（用Gemini做OMR），並在本地無延遲地渲染演奏者自己的鋼琴音；FastAPI後端維持一個128個音符事件的滾動視窗，負責編譯提示詞、協調兩個獨立的Lyria RealTime串流session；雲端生成模型端則用兩個解耦的串流引擎——一個負責旋律的伴奏（關閉鼓組、不設固定BPM），另一個是節奏固定的打擊樂層。作者的理由是「旋律伴奏跟節奏層遵循不同的時鐘」：旋律引擎需要動態地重新提示，打擊樂則要維持節拍固定的一致性，硬把兩者塞進同一個提示詞會強迫兩邊互相妥協。

狀態機與控制流程：伴奏遵循四狀態策略——Supporting（安靜地在獨奏者底下伴奏，提示詞服從度最高）、Responding（樂句結束後開啟0.3到2.5秒的回應窗口，逐字複誦獨奏者剛才彈的音高）、Sustain（單一音符持續超過2.5秒觸發最小化伴奏）、LongIdle（超過5秒靜默且有段落啟用時，樂隊自由主導）。兩個閘門管理狀態轉換：暖機閘門要求段落啟用後至少彈兩個音符才會發聲；靜默停止閘門在超過4秒安靜後停掉旋律串流。

表演狀態估計（工作記憶）：後端維護的特徵包含音域/力度（音區高低、織度提示、運動方向、演奏速度、力度弧線）、樂句結構（用靜默驅動分段、計算樂句輪次、記錄上一句音高供回應使用）、以及和聲（即時和弦模板比對、十二小節藍調段落推斷、以2秒視窗、2半音顆粒度計算「和聲指紋」）。這個和聲指紋是專門設計來穩定閘門的：它取代了原本「每個音符觸發就改變」的舊版key，那個版本在旋律快速跑動時會不斷重新提示、造成串流不穩定。

控制器與生成參數：每個伴奏狀態對應一組風格化的參數向量（溫度、top-k、guidance、密度、亮度）。以藍調Supporting狀態為例：溫度0.60、top-k 12、guidance 7.0、密度0.22；LongIdle用最高的溫度與密度（樂隊自由即興），Supporting則用最低的溫度與密度、最高的guidance（樂隊保持忠實而不搶戲）。

知識庫：整個系統把手動編寫的音樂知識完全以可讀的文字與調校過的常數呈現，編譯器本身有2,434行Python程式碼，包含15種調式描述（6種核心阿拉伯調式：bayati、rast、hijaz、nahawand、saba、kurd，加上9種西方音階，每個條目詳述音名拼寫、特徵音級、裝飾音、終止音、色彩描述、以及「避免落入西方調性」的負面引導）、38個樂隊段落（每段落3種角色，共114種角色描述）、9種曲風設定（段落順序、樂器主導偏好、打擊樂型態）、135組生成參數（9種曲風 × 3狀態 × 4個旋鈕 + 3個亮度值）、以及和聲規則（音程模板式和弦辨識、十二小節段落推斷、四層藍調和聲鎖優先序）。

提示詞合成：編譯器把提示詞組裝成有序的子句串接：[樂器規則/靜默] | [禁止打擊樂] | [特定標頭] | [場次階段] | [調式描述] | [和聲脈絡/回應] | [角色分配] | [音區] | [力度] | [空間子句]。調式描述會明確地把微分音音級用拼音方式寫出（例如「E半降」），並附上負面引導：「避免西方大小調性，維持在bayati調式世界裡」。角色分層與回應機制會把上一句的音高轉譯成具體的回應指令：「用相同的音高回應、帶藍調風格：滑進第一個音、用藍調式的推弦」。

System architecture has three tiers: a browser client (React/TypeScript) captures MIDI keyboard input, hand gestures (via MediaPipe), voice commands, and sheet music (via Gemini OMR), rendering the performer's own piano locally with no network latency; a FastAPI backend maintains a rolling 128-note-event window, compiles prompts, and orchestrates two independent Lyria RealTime sessions; and on the cloud generation side, two decoupled streaming engines run — one melodic companion (drums muted, no fixed BPM) and one tempo-locked percussion bed. The authors' rationale: "melodic accompaniment and rhythmic bed obey different clocks" — the melodic engine needs dynamic re-prompting while the percussion needs metronomic consistency, and forcing both into one prompt would force a compromise on each.

State machine and control flow: accompaniment follows a four-state policy — Supporting (quiet comping beneath the soloist at highest prompt adherence), Responding (a 0.3-2.5s answer window opens after a phrase ends, literally echoing the soloist's just-played pitches), Sustain (a single note held over 2.5s triggers minimal accompaniment), and LongIdle (after more than 5s of silence with sections active, the ensemble leads freely). Two gates manage transitions: a warmup gate requiring at least two notes after a section activates before the ensemble sounds, and a silence-stop gate that halts the melodic stream after 4s of quiet.

Performance-state estimation (working memory): the backend tracks features including register/dynamics (register, texture hint, motion direction, playing speed, dynamic arc), phrase structure (silence-driven segmentation, phrase-count tracking, last-phrase pitches for echo), and harmony (real-time chord-template matching, twelve-bar blues inference, a "harmony fingerprint" computed over a 2-second window at 2-semitone granularity). This harmony fingerprint was specifically designed to stabilize gating — it replaced an earlier key that changed on every note-on and re-prompted the model continuously during fast melodic runs, destabilizing the stream.

Controller and generation parameters: each accompaniment state maps to a genre-conditioned parameter vector (temperature, top-k, guidance, density, brightness). A representative blues Supporting-state example: temperature 0.60, top-k 12, guidance 7.0, density 0.22; LongIdle uses the highest temperature and density (the band improvises freely) while Supporting uses the lowest temperature and density with the highest guidance (the band stays literal and unobtrusive).

Knowledge base: the entire system embeds hand-authored musical knowledge as readable prose and tuned constants, with the compiler itself running 2,434 lines of Python, including 15 mode/scale descriptions (6 core Arabic maqamat — bayati, rast, hijaz, nahawand, saba, kurd — plus 9 Western scales, each specifying note spelling, characteristic degree, ornaments, resting tones, color description, and negative guidance discouraging Western tonality), 38 ensemble sections (3 role registers each, 114 role descriptions total), 9 genre configurations (section ordering, lead-instrument preference, percussion patterns), 135 generation-parameter sets (9 genres x 3 states x 4 knobs + 3 brightness values), and harmonic rules (interval-template chord recognition, twelve-bar region inference, a four-tier blues harmonic-lock priority).

Prompt synthesis: the compiler assembles prompts as an ordered concatenation of clauses: [instrument rule/silence] | [no-percussion] | [specific header] | [session stage] | [maqam description] | [harmonic context/echo] | [role assignment] | [register] | [dynamics] | [space clause]. Maqam descriptions explicitly spell out microtonal degrees phonetically (e.g. "E half-flat") with negative guidance such as "avoid western major or minor tonality, stay in bayati modal world." Role-layering and echo translate the last phrase's pitches into concrete response instructions: "answer using those same pitches with blues character: slide into the first note, bend with blues phrasing."

限制Limitations

作者列出的限制相當具體且誠實。第一，缺乏拍點層級的同步：系統沒有節拍追蹤或速度重新錨定機制，兩位測試音樂家一致的觀察是「樂隊維持自己的節拍」，而不是跟著演奏者的實際脈動呼吸——系統做到的是「語意層級的回應」（追蹤音區、密度、力度、和聲、樂句邊界），但不是「節拍層級的回應」（節拍追蹤、速度重新錨定），作者把這列為未來工作議程的首要項目。第二，樂器抑制機制不可靠：消融實驗顯示，光靠提示詞裡的負面指令（例如「以下樂器絕對不能出現」）並沒有可量測地抑制被排除的樂器，用AudioSet標籤器分析後，作者直言「提示詞層級的禁止,無法取代stem層級的API控制」——也就是說負面提示反而容易「喚起」它想避免的東西。第三，微分音的穩定性有限：提示詞式的調式描述確實能引出四分音的旋律「傾向」，但作者承認「要讓半降音級穩定地成為一個調準確的音階音級，光靠提示詞層級的控制還做不到」。第四，系統完全依賴一個沒被改動過的商用串流模型（Lyria RealTime），沒辦法在串流中途改變BPM，速度變化必須整個斷線重連。第五，和聲推斷主要只在藍調、爵士這類有明確和弦進行的曲風裡可靠，對非和聲導向的曲種適用性較低。第六，也是最根本的一點：整個評估規模很小——只有兩位專業音樂家的體驗回饋，加上一場8.5分鐘的儀器化session，作者自己承認「這只構成一次小規模的專家型感知評估，不是有較大參與者樣本的受控聆聽研究，後者仍是未來工作」。

The authors list quite specific and honest limitations. First, no beat-level synchronization: the system has no beat tracking or tempo re-anchoring, and both test musicians consistently observed that "the ensemble kept its own clock" rather than breathing with the performer's actual pulse — the system achieves semantic responsiveness (tracking register, density, dynamics, harmony, phrase boundaries) but not metric responsiveness (beat tracking, tempo re-anchoring), which the authors place at the head of their future-work agenda. Second, instrument suppression is unreliable: an ablation shows that negative prompt-level directives alone (e.g. "the following must not appear") do not measurably suppress excluded instruments — analyzed via an AudioSet tagger, the authors state plainly that "prompt-level prohibition is therefore not a substitute for stem-level API control," meaning negative prompting tends instead to evoke what it names. Third, microtonal stability is limited: prompt-level maqam grounding does elicit a quarter-tone melodic inflection, but the authors admit "rendering the half-flat second as a stably tuned scale degree remains beyond prompt-level control alone." Fourth, the system depends entirely on an unmodified commercial streaming model (Lyria RealTime), cannot change BPM mid-stream, and requires a full disconnect-and-reconnect for tempo changes. Fifth, harmonic inference is reliable mainly in blues/jazz-style genres with clear chord progressions, and less applicable to non-harmonically-driven genres. Sixth, and most fundamentally: the evaluation scale is small — just perceptual feedback from two professional musicians plus one 8.5-minute instrumented session, and the authors themselves acknowledge this "constitute[s] a small-scale perceptual evaluation by domain experts... not a controlled listening study with a larger participant pool; the latter remains future work."

討論Discussion

這篇論文的說服力主要來自它願意把即時系統的工程細節攤開來量化，而不是只給一段示範影片。在延遲與穩定性方面：知識層（狀態更新加提示詞編譯）花費「即使在p99也只要大約半毫秒」，端對端從按鍵到可聽見聲音的延遲中位數是263毫秒（p99為945毫秒），落在控制器開啟的0.3至2.5秒回應窗口的前段；在8.5分鐘的主要測試session裡，1,517次閘門觸發、166次每分鐘的API推送，零重連、零串流中斷、零因佇列滿而丟棄的資料，即使關掉閘門機制、把推送頻率拉到每分鐘202次，串流依然穩定——這說明「這套管線能承受的重新引導頻率，比設計時假設的還要高一個數量級」。在調式落地的消融實驗裡，有無調式知識造成的差異相當明確：有調式知識時，主音D在三組實驗裡有兩組拿到最高能量佔比（0.175、0.163），沒有調式知識時則從未排到第一（合併後排第四，0.094）；逐幀分析顯示，有調式知識時22.4%的有聲幀偏離十二平均律網格35音分以上（沒有時是14.9%）；當旋律線經過D上方的第二音級區域時，有調式知識時有59.4%的幀落在半降音的125-175音分帶內，沒有時只有24.1%。這些數字證明了「用自然語言描述微分音特徵」確實能統計上顯著地把生成內容推往調式方向，即使沒辦法讓每個音符都精準地調在正確頻率上。整體而言，作者認為這個架構的五個特性——模型無關、不需訓練、可檢視可編輯、確定性、效能極佳（知識層成本比生成器預算小三個數量級）——共同證明了「確定性的知識規則」可以有效地橋接專業、非西方的音樂傳統跟一個沒被微調過的基礎模型。這對資源有限、訓練資料稀缺的非西方音樂傳統來說，提供了一個不需要龐大訓練語料就能做出可用系統的可行路徑，但論文也誠實地把「節拍同步」與「大規模聆聽評估」列為尚未跨過的門檻。

This paper's persuasiveness comes largely from its willingness to lay real-time system engineering details bare and quantify them, rather than offering only a demo video. On latency and stability: the knowledge layer (state update plus prompt compilation) costs "roughly half a millisecond even at p99," with end-to-end key-press-to-audible latency at a 263ms median (945ms p99), sitting at the front of the 0.3-2.5s answer window the controller opens. Across the 8.5-minute primary test session: 1,517 gate fires, 166 API pushes per minute, zero reconnects, zero stream terminations, and zero bounded-queue drops — and even with the gate disabled and push frequency raised to 202/minute, the stream stayed stable, establishing that "the pipeline tolerates an order of magnitude more re-steering than its design assumed." In the maqam-grounding ablation, the difference with and without grounding is clear-cut: with grounding, the tonic D achieves the highest energy share in two of three arms (0.175, 0.163), while without grounding it never ranks first (pooled fourth at 0.094); frame-level analysis shows 22.4% of voiced frames land 35+ cents off the 12-TET grid with grounding versus 14.9% without; and when the melodic line visits the second-degree region above D, 59.4% of frames fall within the half-flat band (125-175 cents) with grounding versus only 24.1% without. These numbers demonstrate that describing microtonal features in natural language does statistically significantly push generated content toward the intended mode, even without pinning every note to an exactly correct frequency. Overall, the authors argue that five properties of the architecture — model-agnostic, requiring no training, inspectable and editable, deterministic, and highly performant (the knowledge layer costs three orders of magnitude less than the generator's budget) — together demonstrate that deterministic knowledge-based rules can effectively bridge expert, non-Western musical traditions and an unfine-tuned foundation model. For resource-limited, data-scarce non-Western traditions, this offers a workable path to a usable system without a massive training corpus, though the paper is also honest that beat-level synchronization and large-scale listening evaluation remain thresholds not yet crossed.
