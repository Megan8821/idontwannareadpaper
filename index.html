Local Chord Corruption Is Not Recognizer Replay: Chord-Condition Propagation in MIDI-SAG

局部和弦破壞不等於辨識器重播：MIDI-SAG 的和弦條件傳播

Weiwen Huang
arXiv:2609.03584 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-03
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文比較兩種常見的和弦穩健性測試——局部合成破壞（如替換成三全音）與完整自動和弦辨識（ACR）重播——是否可以互相替代，用固定的 MIDI 演唱伴奏生成器（MIDI-SAG）在 30 軌、三個種子共 180 次生成上做配對比較；結果顯示中央四秒三全音破壞在 STFT/CQT/CENS 三種特徵上都比 ACR 重播產生更大的效果（CENS 上 29/30 軌為正、平均差距 0.462、p=2.61×10⁻⁸），而只要同時比對重播的時間佔比與和弦關係組成，替代測試的距離就能降到最低，說明兩種測試量的是不同的機制，不能互換。Why this one: The paper asks whether a localized synthetic chord corruption (e.g. a tritone swap) is interchangeable with full automatic chord recognition (ACR) replay when stress-testing a MIDI singing-accompaniment generator (MIDI-SAG), running a paired comparison across 30 tracks and 3 seeds (180 renders total); a central four-second tritone corruption produced larger effects than ACR replay across STFT, CQT, and CENS features (CENS gap positive on 29/30 tracks, mean 0.462, p=2.61e-8), and only matching both the replay's temporal support and its chord-relation composition brought a surrogate test close to real replay, showing the two tests probe different mechanisms and are not interchangeable.

動機Motivation

生成式模型的和弦穩健性通常用兩種方式測——研究者自己動手做局部破壞（例如把某段和弦換成三全音），或是把完整的自動和弦辨識（ACR）結果整段重播進生成器。這兩種做法在文獻裡常被當成同一件事的兩種實作方式，但作者質疑：局部破壞測的是「模型對某個特定和弦錯誤有多敏感」，完整重播測的是「部署後真實會遇到的錯誤條件」，兩者的錯誤結構（哪裡變了、變成什麼關係）可能完全不同，直接把兩者的結論互換來用可能會誤導穩健性評估。

Chord-robustness for generative accompaniment models is usually tested one of two ways: researchers hand-craft a local corruption (say, swapping one chord for a tritone), or they replay a complete automatic chord recognition (ACR) output through the generator end to end. The literature often treats these as interchangeable implementations of "the same test," but the authors question that: a local corruption measures sensitivity to one specific chord error, while full replay measures the error conditions a deployed system actually faces, and the two can differ completely in error structure (where it changes, and into what relation). Swapping conclusions between them risks a misleading robustness claim.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者把先前的歌唱伴奏生成（SAG）系統分成兩類：一類是只吃音訊條件的 SingSong 與 FastSAG；另一類會額外吃旋律、和弦與 MIDI 控制訊號，包含本文測試對象 MIDI-SAG，以及同樣支援和弦條件的 AnyAccomp（用量化旋律瓶頸做控制）與 MusicGen-Chord（在 MusicGen 家族上加裝和弦進程條件）。和弦辨識端則對比 CNN-CRF 與 DeepChroma+CRF 兩種主流聲學前端。

作者指出，過去針對 SAG 穩健性的研究（如 Gao 等人的工作）多半問「模型能不能容忍不完美的控制訊號」，屬於生成器中心的問題；這篇論文反過來問「拿什麼樣的破壞測試去問這個問題」，把焦點從模型轉到評估方法本身，這是本文與既有 SAG 穩健性研究最大的差別。

The authors split prior singing-accompaniment generation (SAG) systems into two groups: audio-only conditioned systems such as SingSong and FastSAG, and systems that also take melody, chord, or MIDI control -- including MIDI-SAG, the system under test here, plus AnyAccomp (control via a quantized melodic bottleneck) and MusicGen-Chord (adding chord-progression conditioning to the MusicGen family). On the recognition side they compare two mainstream acoustic front ends, CNN-CRF and DeepChroma+CRF.

Prior SAG-robustness work, such as Gao et al., mostly asks a generator-centered question -- can the model tolerate imperfect control input -- whereas this paper flips it to ask what kind of corruption test should even be used to answer that question. Shifting the focus from the model to the evaluation method itself is the paper's main departure from existing SAG-robustness studies.

方法Method

核心做法是配對設計：固定同一首人聲軌、同一個生成種子、同樣的上下文與計分視窗，只改變「和弦條件」這一個變數，比較三種條件——(1) 中央四秒三全音置換的局部破壞；(2) CNN-CRF 或 DeepChroma+CRF 的完整 ACR 重播；(3) 保留重播的時間佔比與和弦關係組成、但不直接照抄辨識標籤的「輪廓匹配替代」。效果用三種特徵（STFT 色度、CQT、CENS）分別量三種指標：改變目標的相似度增益、輸出內部變化量、以及整個 12 秒視窗的平均變化量。

實驗從 34 軌、4,102 個候選視窗中挑出 30 組配對的 12 秒視窗，涵蓋不同音域與和聲變化特性，每組用 3 個種子（42、123、2027）各生成一次，共 180 次生成；另外抽出 18 軌的診斷子集，在符號層級的不一致量幾乎相等（0.1059 對 0.1070）的情況下，比較「換根音」與「同根音換和弦性質」兩種破壞的效果差異。

The core design is paired: the same vocal track, generation seed, context, and scoring window are held fixed while only the chord condition varies, across three conditions -- (1) a local corruption swapping in a central four-second tritone; (2) full ACR replay from either CNN-CRF or DeepChroma+CRF; and (3) a "profile-matched surrogate" that preserves replay's temporal support and chord-relation composition without directly copying its labels. Effects are measured in three feature spaces (STFT chroma, CQT, CENS) using three metrics: the changed-target similarity gain, the inside-output change, and the full-window average change over the 12-second clip.

From a pool of 34 tracks and 4,102 candidate windows, the authors select 30 paired 12-second windows spanning different registers and harmonic-change profiles, rendering each with three seeds (42, 123, 2027) for 180 renders total. An 18-track diagnostic subset further isolates root-changing substitutions from same-root quality flips at near-equal symbolic disagreement mass (0.1059 vs. 0.1070).

限制Limitations

作者自己承認的限制有三點：第一，單一的「和弦錯誤率」不足以描述 SAG 系統的穩健性，錯誤發生的位置（support）與錯誤變成什麼關係（composition）同樣重要，但這篇論文只針對這兩個座標做系統性分析，沒有窮舉所有可能影響結果的因素；第二，局部探測只能回答「哪個和聲機制重要」，要主張「部署後行為如何」還是得靠完整重播，兩者功能互補而非互相取代，這代表單靠這篇提出的替代測試仍不能完全省略真實重播實驗；第三，所有結論都建立在同一個固定的 MIDI-SAG 生成器上，換成別的生成架構是否還成立並未驗證。

The authors flag three limitations of their own. First, a single chord-error rate is not enough to characterize SAG robustness -- where an error lands (support) and what relation it becomes (composition) both matter, and this paper only systematically analyzes those two coordinates rather than every factor that could affect the outcome. Second, local probes can only answer which harmonic mechanism matters; claims about deployed behavior still require full replay, so the two are complementary rather than substitutes, meaning the proposed surrogate test cannot fully replace real replay experiments. Third, every conclusion is tied to one fixed MIDI-SAG generator, and whether it holds for a different generative architecture is left unverified.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最直接的貢獻是把「怎麼測和弦穩健性」拆成兩個可操作的座標——改變的時間佔比與改變後的和弦關係組成——並證明只要同時對齊這兩者，人工替代測試就能逼近真實 ACR 重播的效果（CENS 上 30 軌全部為正、平均差距 0.384，p=1.86×10⁻⁹；CQT 上 29/30 軌為正、平均差距 0.328）。

作者也發現完整重播的錯誤幾乎都由「換根音」主導（佔事件質量 76.12%），三全音替換只佔 1.79%，這解釋了為什麼中央三全音這種常見的局部破壞測試會系統性高估效果——它其實測的是一種在真實部署中很少見的錯誤類型。實務建議是：固定軌道、上下文與種子，同時跑基線與完整辨識序列的重播，局部破壞則專門用來驗證特定和聲機制，不要把兩者結果直接混用。

The paper's most direct contribution is decomposing "how to test chord robustness" into two operational coordinates -- changed temporal support and post-change chord-relation composition -- and showing that matching both lets a synthetic surrogate approach real ACR replay (on CENS, all 30 tracks positive, mean gap 0.384, p=1.86e-9; on CQT, 29/30 positive, mean gap 0.328).

The authors also find that full replay error is dominated by root changes (76.12% of event mass), with tritone substitutions accounting for only 1.79% -- which explains why a common local test like a central tritone systematically overstates the effect: it is actually probing an error type rarely seen in real deployment. The practical recommendation is to freeze track, context, and seed while rendering both the baseline and a full recognizer-replay sequence, reserving local corruption specifically for verifying named harmonic mechanisms rather than mixing the two results together.

How Well Do Generative Music Models Follow Emotion Conditioning?

生成式音樂模型能多忠實地遵循情緒條件？

Morteza Heydari
arXiv:2608.29987 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-30
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：作者建立一套評估流程，用 DashengLM 產生語意描述、Music2Emotion 估計 1,000 首 GTZAN 曲目的原始價／喚醒度（valence/arousal），再比較 Stable Audio Open、MusicGen 與 InspireMusic 在文字條件與音訊條件下生成的音樂是否還保留原本的情緒；結果顯示文字條件全面優於音訊條件（MusicGen 文字版 VA 距離 1.362、InspireMusic 文字版 1.367，對比 InspireMusic 音訊版 2.101），而且各系統的價（valence）保留都比喚醒度（arousal）保留穩定，類型（genre）之間的差異也很大——古典與鄉村誤差小且集中，disco、金屬與流行誤差大且分散。Why this one: The authors build an evaluation pipeline that uses DashengLM to caption audio and Music2Emotion to estimate source valence/arousal for 1,000 GTZAN tracks, then compares how well Stable Audio Open, MusicGen, and InspireMusic preserve that emotion under text versus audio conditioning; text conditioning consistently wins (VA distance 1.362 for MusicGen-text and 1.367 for InspireMusic-text, versus 2.101 for InspireMusic-audio), valence preservation is more stable than arousal preservation across all systems, and genre matters a lot -- classical and country show small, tight errors while disco, metal, and pop show large, scattered ones.

動機Motivation

近年生成式音樂模型透過文字與音訊條件提供越來越細緻的控制，但它們是否真的忠實遵循想要的情緒線索，一直沒有被直接量測過。現有評估大多用 Fréchet Audio Distance 之類的指標看整體音質，或用文字相關度看提示語意有沒有對上，卻沒有一個指標直接問「生成結果的情緒，跟指定的情緒差多遠」。這篇論文用價／喚醒度（valence/arousal）這個二維情緒模型當量尺，直接檢驗三個主流生成系統的「情緒可控性」。

Recent generative music models offer increasingly fine-grained control via text and audio conditioning, but whether they actually follow the intended emotional cue faithfully has not been directly measured. Existing evaluation mostly relies on metrics like Fréchet Audio Distance for overall audio quality, or text-relevance scores for whether the prompt semantics line up, without any metric that directly asks how far the generated emotion drifts from the specified one. This paper uses the two-dimensional valence-arousal model of emotion as its yardstick to directly test the "emotion controllability" of three mainstream generation systems.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者把生成式音樂的發展脈絡分成三段：早期統計模型、符號層級的神經方法（如 GAN、Transformer）、以及現在的音訊域系統，並點名三個具體系統作為測試對象——支援文字與旋律引導的 MusicLM、單階段 Transformer 架構的 MusicGen、以及以隱擴散模型做文字轉音訊的 Stable Audio Open，加上支援長篇生成的 InspireMusic。

情緒條件生成方面，先前的 ELMG（以正負情緒條件生成歌詞與旋律）與 EmoMusicTV（用階層式 Transformer-VAE 做情緒引導）都是這篇論文點名的具體先例，但兩者都做在符號層級，不是音訊域；這正是本文與它們最大的不同——本文檢驗的是音訊域生成系統的情緒跟隨能力，而不是符號生成。音樂情緒辨識（MER）方面則指出領域已經轉向深度多模態模型，並直接沿用其中的 Music2Emotion 當作價／喚醒度的量測工具，而非自己重新訓練一套辨識器。

The authors trace generative music's development in three stages -- early statistical models, symbolic-level neural approaches (GANs, Transformers), and today's audio-domain systems -- and name three concrete systems under test: MusicLM (text and melody guidance), MusicGen (a single-stage Transformer with text/melody conditioning), and Stable Audio Open (latent-diffusion text-to-audio), plus InspireMusic for long-form generation.

On emotion-conditioned generation specifically, the paper names two concrete precedents: ELMG (lyric-melody generation conditioned on positive/negative emotion) and EmoMusicTV (a hierarchical Transformer-VAE with emotional guidance) -- but both operate at the symbolic level, not the audio domain. That is the paper's main departure from them: it tests emotion-following in audio-domain generation systems rather than symbolic generation. On music emotion recognition (MER), the paper notes the field has shifted toward deep multimodal models, and it directly reuses one of them, Music2Emotion, as its valence-arousal measurement tool rather than training a new recognizer from scratch.

方法Method

資料是完整的 1,000 首 GTZAN 曲目（10 類曲風各 100 首，每首 30 秒）。流程分兩步：先用 DashengLM 幫每首歌產生語意描述，再用 Music2Emotion 估計每首歌的類別情緒標籤與連續的價／喚醒度分數；文字提示則是把語意描述與信心度最高的前三個情緒標籤結合，透過 GPT-4 API 重寫成通順但仍保留情緒強調的句子。

生成端測試 Stable Audio Open（1.0 版）、MusicGen（medium）、InspireMusic（1.5B-24kHz）三套系統的文字條件模式，以及 MusicGen（melody）與 InspireMusic 同一組權重的音訊條件模式；每首歌只生成一次 30 秒輸出，使用預設推論參數，不額外調參。跟隨程度用四種指標衡量：價與喚醒度各自的平均絕對誤差（MAE-V、MAE-A）、逐首歐氏距離（VA Distance）、以及皮爾森相關係數（Corr-V、Corr-A）。

The dataset is the full 1,000-track GTZAN set (10 genres, 100 tracks each, 30 seconds per track). The pipeline runs in two steps: DashengLM captions each track, then Music2Emotion estimates categorical emotion tags plus continuous valence/arousal scores for it. Text prompts combine the caption with the top-3 highest-confidence emotion tags, rewritten by the GPT-4 API into fluent sentences that keep the affective emphasis.

On the generation side, the paper tests text conditioning on Stable Audio Open (v1.0), MusicGen (medium), and InspireMusic (1.5B-24kHz), plus audio conditioning on MusicGen (melody) and InspireMusic using the same checkpoint as its text mode; each track gets a single 30-second output at default inference settings with no extra tuning. Emotion-following is scored with four metrics: mean absolute error for valence and arousal separately (MAE-V, MAE-A), per-track Euclidean distance (VA Distance), and Pearson correlation (Corr-V, Corr-A).

限制Limitations

作者列出五項限制。第一，價／喚醒度的「正確答案」跟評分工具都來自同一個 Music2Emotion 模型，量到的其實是「跟這套 MER 系統的表徵空間有多一致」，不是跟人類聽感有多一致，還需要真人聽測驗證。第二，GTZAN 本身有已知問題（可能同一位藝人重複出現、標籤有模糊地帶），需要在更大更多樣的語料上再驗證。第三，每首歌只生成一次、用預設參數，換成多次生成或調過參數的設定可能得到不同結果。

第四，MusicGen 的文字與音訊條件用的是不同權重（medium 對 melody），等於把「條件方式」跟「模型變體」的差異混在一起，只有 InspireMusic 因為文字與音訊條件共用同一組權重，比較才乾淨。第五，文字提示經過 GPT-4 重寫，結果會受這個重寫模型品質影響，換一種重寫策略可能改變結論。

The authors list five limitations. First, both the "ground truth" valence/arousal and the scoring tool come from the same Music2Emotion model, so the metric really measures consistency with that one MER system's representation space, not with human perception -- human listening validation is still needed. Second, GTZAN itself has known issues (possible repeated artists, ambiguous labels), so results need re-validation on larger, more diverse corpora. Third, each track is generated only once at default settings; multiple generations or tuned parameters might give different results.

Fourth, MusicGen's text and audio conditioning use different checkpoints (medium vs. melody), which conflates conditioning mode with model-variant differences -- only InspireMusic, which shares one checkpoint across both modes, gives a clean comparison. Fifth, text prompts are rewritten by GPT-4, so results depend on that rewriting model's quality; a different rewriting strategy could change the conclusions.

討論Discussion

最主要的量化結果是：文字條件在兩個模型家族內都顯著贏過音訊條件（InspireMusic 上文字版勝率 67.3%、p<0.001、效果量 |d|=0.502；MusicGen 上勝率 64.5%、p<0.001、|d|=0.411），最佳的是 MusicGen 文字版（VA 距離 1.362）與 InspireMusic 文字版（1.367），最差的是 InspireMusic 音訊版（2.101、相關係數趨近於零，Corr-V=0.031、Corr-A=-0.017）。

作者的解讀是：文字提示能把情緒當成明確、可直接對齊的訊號，音訊條件則要模型自己從音色、節奏等特徵裡隱含地抽出情緒，這件事本身就比較不穩定；而「價」多半靠調式與和聲傳達，文字比較容易講清楚，「喚醒度」則跟速度、力度與能量有關，文字較難精準控制，這解釋了為什麼各系統的 MAE-V 都低於 MAE-A。對想做情緒感知系統的開發者，論文的實務建議是優先用明確的情緒標籤做文字條件，而不是指望音對音的條件生成。

The main quantitative result is that text conditioning beats audio conditioning significantly within both model families (67.3% win rate for InspireMusic-text, p<0.001, |d|=0.502; 64.5% win rate for MusicGen-text, p<0.001, |d|=0.411), with MusicGen-text (VA distance 1.362) and InspireMusic-text (1.367) performing best and InspireMusic-audio performing worst (2.101, with correlation near zero: Corr-V=0.031, Corr-A=-0.017).

The authors read this as: text prompts turn emotion into an explicit, directly alignable signal, while audio conditioning forces the model to implicitly extract emotion from features like timbre and rhythm, which is inherently less stable; valence is mostly conveyed through mode and harmony, which text can state plainly, while arousal relates to tempo, dynamics, and energy, which text controls less precisely -- explaining why MAE-V is lower than MAE-A across every system. For developers building emotion-aware systems, the practical takeaway is to prioritize explicit emotion labels in text conditioning rather than relying on audio-to-audio conditioning.

Neural Music Enhancement with Dual Time-Frequency Spectral Representations for Prediction and Discrimination

雙時頻頻譜表徵的神經音樂增強（DSME）

Fei Liu, Yang Ai, Zhen-Hua Ling

為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文提出 DSME，在生成對抗架構裡讓生成器用 STFT 頻譜預測乾淨的振幅與相位、判別器改用依八度分段的 CQT 頻譜，再加上強調音高與泛音一致性的色度損失；在 Medley-solos-DB 上的去噪、去殘響、混合失真三種情境下，DSME 的 fwSSNR、MRS、L1-SD、FAD 等客觀指標都優於 MP-SENet、TFC-CPq、Mel2Mel 三個基線（例如混合失真下 fwSSNR 為 8.84，對比 8.50、5.33、5.97），主觀 ABX 偏好測試對三個基線的勝率分別是 68.79%、71.38%、63.21%（皆 p<0.01），消融實驗顯示拿掉 CQT 判別器或色度損失都會讓多數指標變差。Why this one: The paper proposes DSME, a GAN-based music enhancement model whose generator predicts clean amplitude and phase from STFT spectra while its discriminator operates on an octave-segmented CQT spectrum, plus a chroma-spectrum loss that emphasizes pitch and harmonic consistency; on Medley-solos-DB across denoising, dereverberation, and mixed-distortion settings, DSME beats three baselines (MP-SENet, TFC-CPq, Mel2Mel) on objective metrics like fwSSNR, MRS, L1-SD, and FAD (e.g. mixed-distortion fwSSNR of 8.84 vs. 8.50, 5.33, and 5.97), wins subjective ABX preference tests against the three baselines by 68.79%, 71.38%, and 63.21% respectively (all p<0.01), and ablations show removing either the CQT discriminator or the chroma loss degrades most metrics.

動機Motivation

非專業使用者上傳到網路的音樂錄音常常帶著背景噪音與殘響，影響聽感也限制了這些素材的再利用價值。音樂增強跟語音增強是不同的問題：音樂訊號是多聲部的、音色更多樣，對感知保真度的要求也更嚴格，不能直接套用語音增強的方法。過去的深度學習方法大多只對梅爾頻譜建模，沒有明確處理相位資訊，這篇論文認為相位預測與音樂特有的頻率結構（八度、泛音）都還有加強的空間。

Non-professional music recordings shared online often carry background noise and reverberation, hurting perceived quality and limiting how reusable they are. Music enhancement is a different problem from speech enhancement: music signals are polyphonic, more timbrally diverse, and held to stricter perceptual-fidelity requirements, so speech-enhancement methods cannot simply be reused as-is. Prior deep-learning approaches mostly model mel-spectrograms without explicitly handling phase information, and the authors argue there is still room to improve both phase prediction and handling of music-specific frequency structure such as octaves and harmonics.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者點名三個具體的先前方法當基線與比較對象。傳統訊號處理與早期神經網路方法在複雜失真情境下效果有限；U-Net 類的方法多半只處理加性噪音，遇到殘響或混合失真就吃力。更近期的做法裡，Kandpal 等人用 Pix2Pix 網路搭配 DiffWave 聲碼器處理音樂增強；Chae 等人的 CMGAN 則用時頻 Conformer 模組。這篇論文與這些先前方法最大的不同在於判別器的設計——不是用線性頻率的 STFT 或梅爾頻譜當判別依據，而是刻意採用對齊音樂八度結構的 CQT，並讓每個八度用不同的視窗長度，這是先前這些基線都沒有做的設計選擇。

The authors name three concrete prior methods as baselines and points of comparison. Traditional signal-processing and early neural approaches are limited in complex distortion scenarios; U-Net-style methods mostly handle additive noise only and struggle with reverberation or mixed distortion. Among more recent approaches, Kandpal et al. used a Pix2Pix network paired with a DiffWave vocoder for music enhancement, while Chae et al.'s CMGAN used time-frequency Conformer modules. This paper's main departure from all of them is its discriminator design: instead of judging quality on a linear-frequency STFT or mel-spectrogram, it deliberately uses a CQT aligned to musical octave structure, with a different window length per octave -- a design choice none of these baselines make.

方法Method

DSME 是一個生成對抗模型，由兩部分組成。生成器把失真後的波形轉成 STFT 振幅與相位頻譜（疊在一起當輸入），經過膨脹式 DenseNet 與四層時頻 Transformer 後，分別預測振幅（對失真振幅做遮罩相乘）與相位（先估計虛擬的實部／虛部，再用反正切轉回相位），最後用逆 STFT 重建波形。判別器則計算 7 個八度、每八度 12 個半音、最低頻 55 Hz 的 CQT 頻譜，每個八度用不同長度的視窗以對齊音樂結構，複數 CQT 轉成實數表示（實部虛部串接）後，把低八度的時間解析度上採樣對齊，再送進卷積區塊、自適應池化與全連接分類器。

訓練損失由五項組成：hinge 式對抗損失加特徵匹配、振幅的均方誤差損失、強調 12 個音高類別結構的色度損失、以及對相位誤差做反纏繞處理（涵蓋恆等、群延遲、瞬時角頻率三種運算）的相位損失，總損失為 L = L_G + L_FM + 0.9·L_A + 0.2·L_Chroma + 0.3·L_P。實驗在 Medley-solos-DB（5,437 訓練、2,999 驗證、11,281 測試，16 kHz）上分去噪（SNR 0–5 dB）、去殘響、混合失真三種情境，用 fwSSNR、MRS、L1-SD、FAD 等音樂專用指標，加上 LSD、ViSQOL 兩個借自語音領域的指標做評估，並用 ABX 偏好測試（20 段音訊、30 位以上聽者）做主觀驗證。

DSME is a GAN with two parts. The generator turns the distorted waveform into stacked STFT amplitude and phase spectra, passes them through a dilated DenseNet and four time-frequency Transformer layers, and then predicts amplitude (via mask multiplication on the distorted amplitude) and phase (estimating pseudo real/imaginary components and converting them back via arctangent) separately, finally reconstructing the waveform with an inverse STFT. The discriminator computes a CQT spectrum with 7 octaves, 12 bins per octave, and a 55 Hz minimum frequency, using a different window length per octave to align with musical structure; the complex CQT is converted to a real-valued representation (concatenated real and imaginary parts), its lower-octave time resolution is upsampled to match, and the result feeds into convolutional blocks, adaptive pooling, and a fully connected classifier.

The training loss has five terms: a hinge-based adversarial loss plus feature matching, an amplitude MSE loss, a chroma loss emphasizing the 12-pitch-class structure, and a phase loss that applies anti-wrapping across three operations (identity, group delay, and instantaneous angular frequency), combined as L = L_G + L_FM + 0.9·L_A + 0.2·L_Chroma + 0.3·L_P. Experiments run on Medley-solos-DB (5,437 train / 2,999 validation / 11,281 test utterances at 16 kHz) across three settings -- denoising (SNR 0-5 dB), dereverberation, and mixed distortion -- scored with music-specific metrics (fwSSNR, MRS, L1-SD, FAD) plus two metrics borrowed from speech (LSD, ViSQOL), and validated subjectively with ABX preference tests (20 clips, 30+ listeners).

限制Limitations

作者自陳的限制包括：FAD 這個指標可能偏好比較保守的輸出，這可以解釋為什麼消融實驗拿掉某些模組時 FAD 反而些微變好的反直覺結果；訓練需要成對的乾淨／失真資料，沒有這種配對就無法直接訓練；論文也把「延伸到更真實的野外錄音」列為未來工作，代表目前的驗證仍侷限在 Medley-solos-DB 這個相對乾淨的資料集上，還沒有在真正混亂的網路上傳素材上驗證過。

The authors' own limitations include that FAD "can favor more conservative outputs," which explains the counter-intuitive slight FAD improvement seen in some ablations when a module is removed; training requires paired clean/distorted data, so it cannot be trained without such pairs; and the paper lists extending to "more realistic in-the-wild recordings" as future work, meaning current validation is still confined to the comparatively clean Medley-solos-DB dataset rather than genuinely messy user-uploaded material.

討論Discussion

三個情境的客觀指標都對基線有穩定優勢：去噪情境 fwSSNR 為 14.80，對比 MP-SENet 的 14.22、TFC-CPq 的 13.35、Mel2Mel 的 6.84；去殘響情境 fwSSNR 為 12.15，對比 11.58、7.95、7.77；混合失真情境 fwSSNR 為 8.84，對比 8.50、5.33、5.97。主觀 ABX 測試上，DSME 對 Mel2Mel、TFC-CPq、MP-SENet 的勝率分別是 68.79%、71.38%、63.21%（皆 p<0.01），顯示優勢不只出現在客觀指標上。

消融實驗把混合失真情境下的 CQT 判別器或色度損失拿掉後，fwSSNR 從 8.84 分別降到 8.61 與 8.49、MRS 從 1.67 升到 1.72 與 1.69、L1-SD 從 2.28 升到 2.32 與 2.26，說明這兩個模組各自都有實質貢獻，而不是其中一個在單獨撐場面。整體而言，這篇論文的核心論點——用 STFT 的可逆性做生成、CQT 對齊八度結構做判別——在數字上站得住腳，但優勢的來源（尤其是相位損失本身的獨立貢獻）沒有被單獨消融驗證過，消融只拆了 CQT 判別器與色度損失兩塊。

Objective metrics show a consistent edge over baselines across all three settings: denoising fwSSNR of 14.80 vs. 14.22 (MP-SENet), 13.35 (TFC-CPq), and 6.84 (Mel2Mel); dereverberation fwSSNR of 12.15 vs. 11.58, 7.95, and 7.77; mixed-distortion fwSSNR of 8.84 vs. 8.50, 5.33, and 5.97. On subjective ABX tests, DSME wins against Mel2Mel, TFC-CPq, and MP-SENet by 68.79%, 71.38%, and 63.21% respectively (all p<0.01), so the advantage is not confined to objective metrics alone.

Ablating the CQT discriminator or the chroma loss in the mixed-distortion setting drops fwSSNR from 8.84 to 8.61 and 8.49 respectively, raises MRS from 1.67 to 1.72 and 1.69, and raises L1-SD from 2.28 to 2.32 and 2.26 -- showing both modules make a real, separate contribution rather than one of them carrying the result alone. Overall, the paper's core claim -- generation exploiting STFT invertibility, discrimination aligned to octave structure via CQT -- holds up numerically, but the source of the advantage (particularly the phase loss's own independent contribution) was never separately ablated; the ablation only splits out the CQT discriminator and the chroma loss.
