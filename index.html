Known Music, Unknown Response: Leakage-Audited Dynamic Arousal Forecasting With Scheduled Future Music

已知的音樂、未知的反應：把「接下來要播的音樂」當成已知的未來共變數，預測群體喚起度（arousal）的變化量——每 4 秒獨立編碼 MuQ 防止偷看邊界之後，再用同曲錯位片段當負對照

Bin Wen, Dai-Qiao Zhang, Tien-Ping Tan
arXiv:2610.23063 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-16
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇把動態音樂情緒辨識改寫成預測問題：在時間點 t 之後的情緒標註全部藏起來，只准看「已經排好、接下來要播」的音樂，預測 10 或 15 秒後群體喚起度的變化量。值得讀的是它對資料洩漏的處理：整首歌丟進 MuQ 再切片會讓切點之後的音訊滲進切點之前的狀態（相對 L2 差 0.350–0.445），所以改成每 4 秒獨立編碼；在這個規則下，未來音訊仍比校準過的非線性狀態模型高 0.150–0.249 CCC，放對位置也比同曲最相似的片段高 0.078–0.105。要保留的是：反應延遲只留 2 秒，而連續情緒標註本身的反應延遲常在數秒，最接近目標的那段「未來音訊」很可能就是標註當下在回應的刺激；最早那段 4 秒音訊比狀態模型只多 0.044–0.084，DEAM 15 秒那格的下界只有 0.005。Why this one: This paper recasts dynamic music emotion recognition as forecasting: every emotion annotation after time t is hidden, only the music that is already scheduled to play next may be inspected, and the target is the change in consensus arousal 10 or 15 s later. It is worth reading for how it handles leakage: feeding a whole song through MuQ and then slicing lets audio after the cutoff seep into states before it (relative L2 difference 0.350–0.445), so every 4-s chunk is encoded independently; under that rule, future audio still beats a calibrated nonlinear state model by 0.150–0.249 CCC, and correct placement beats the most similar same-song segment by 0.078–0.105. The caveats: the response delay is only 2 s while continuous emotion ratings typically lag the music by several seconds, so the 'future audio' nearest the target is plausibly the very stimulus the rating responds to; the earliest 4-s chunk adds only 0.044–0.084 over the state model, with a lower bound of just 0.005 in the DEAM 15-s cell.

動機Motivation

動態音樂情緒辨識（dynamic MER）的標準設定是：給一段已經聽到的音訊，估計同一時刻（或校正延遲後）的喚起度與效價。作者問的是另一個問題：如果接下來要播的音樂已經排好，但聽眾對它的反應還沒發生，能不能預測反應會怎麼變？這在播放清單規劃、配樂調整、教學媒材這類「候選刺激事先已知」的場景是實際需求，論文舉的例子是讓配樂剪輯師先用模型篩掉一批 10–15 秒的候選轉場，再交給人工審。

作者強調這個設定很容易評估錯。連續情緒標註自相關很強，直接複製當下狀態就能拿到看似合理的分數；重疊視窗讓同一首歌貢獻上萬個相關樣本，模型可以記住曲目或製作風格；雙向音訊編碼器如果先吃整首歌再切片，就會越過宣告的決策邊界；最後，即使音訊比只看狀態好，也不代表「放在正確位置的未來片段」有用，同一首歌的別段可能帶著幾乎一樣的風格與情緒。整篇論文的設計就是針對這四個漏洞。

The standard setting of dynamic music emotion recognition (dynamic MER) is: given audio that has already been heard, estimate arousal and valence at the same moment (or after lag correction). The authors ask a different question: if the upcoming music is already scheduled but the listener's response to it has not happened yet, can we predict how the response will change? This is a real need in playlist planning, soundtrack adaptation and tutoring media, where the candidate stimulus is known in advance; the paper's example is a soundtrack editor using the model to filter a set of 10–15 s candidate transitions before human review.

The authors stress that this setting is easy to evaluate wrongly. Continuous emotion labels are strongly autocorrelated, so copying the current state already earns a plausible score; overlapping windows let one song contribute tens of thousands of correlated samples, so a model can memorize the track or its production style; a bidirectional audio encoder that ingests a whole song before slicing crosses the declared decision boundary; and even if audio beats state alone, that does not show the correctly placed future segment matters, because another part of the same song may carry nearly the same style and affect. The whole design targets these four holes.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名的動態 MER 前作包括 Schmidt 與 Kim（ISMIR 2011）用條件隨機場建模情緒動態、Weninger 等（ICASSP 2014）的線上連續回歸 RNN，以及後來的多尺度注意力方法（Ma 等 2017；Zhang 等，ISMIR 2023）。這些都是「辨識」：看得到被標註那一刻附近的音訊。最接近這篇的是 ENMI（Shang 等，2024），它也用過去的情緒加上音樂特徵做多變量時間序列回歸；差別在 ENMI 沒有宣告預測起點、沒有限制哪些音訊可以被看到，也沒有同曲錯位對照。作者因此明說已發表的 dynamic MER 分數不能當直接 baseline，這個判斷是對的。

方法本身其實是時間序列文獻裡很老的東西：狀態迴歸加上一個外生變數修正項，就是 ARX（帶外生輸入的自迴歸）模型；把「已知的未來共變數」與「觀測到的過去」分開，則是 Temporal Fusion Transformer（Lim 等，2021）的分類法，論文也引用了。新的不是模型，而是評估規則。另一個值得對照的是 Mariooryad 與 Busso（2015）的標註反應延遲校正：他們把標籤往前挪以對齊刺激，這篇則反過來，用 2 秒的反應延遲決定哪些音訊「合法」。洩漏稽核的精神接近 Kapoor 與 Narayanan（2023）對機器學習科學中資料洩漏的分類，但論文沒有引用這條線。

The dynamic MER prior work the paper names includes Schmidt and Kim (ISMIR 2011), who modeled emotion dynamics with conditional random fields, the online continuous-regression RNN of Weninger et al. (ICASSP 2014), and later multi-scale attention methods (Ma et al. 2017; Zhang et al., ISMIR 2023). These are all recognition: they see the audio around the labeled moment. The closest to this paper is ENMI (Shang et al., 2024), which also regresses with past emotion plus music features as a multivariate time series; the difference is that ENMI declares no forecast origin, does not restrict which audio may be seen, and has no same-song displacement controls. The authors therefore state outright that published dynamic MER scores cannot serve as direct baselines, and that judgment is correct.

The method itself is old in the time-series literature: a state regression plus an exogenous correction term is an ARX (autoregressive with exogenous input) model, and separating known future covariates from observed past inputs is the taxonomy of the Temporal Fusion Transformer (Lim et al., 2021), which the paper cites. What is new is the evaluation rules, not the model. Another useful comparison is Mariooryad and Busso (2015) on annotator reaction-lag correction: they shift labels earlier to align them with the stimulus, whereas this paper uses a 2-s response delay to decide which audio is 'legal'. The spirit of the leakage audit is close to Kapoor and Narayanan's (2023) taxonomy of data leakage in ML-based science, though the paper does not cite that line.

方法Method

目標與合法資訊。 在起點 t 只看得到 t 以前的標註；目標是變化量 Δy = y(t+H) − y(t)，H 為 10 或 15 秒。允許看的音樂區間是 [t, t+H−δ]，δ = 2 秒。音訊用凍結的 MuQ-large 每 4 秒一段、1 秒步長獨立編碼，整段都落在合法區間內的 chunk 才能用；例如 t = 20、H = 10 時，最後一段合法 chunk 是 [24, 28]，[25, 29] 就違規。

模型。 狀態向量是當下的喚起度與效價、1/2/4 步差分、5 幀平均與已經過的秒數，先用 ridge 預測 y(t+H)。音訊端把未來軌跡的平均、標準差、終點減起點錨、未來平均減過去平均，加上 9 個距離純量串起來，做訓練折內的 PCA-256 之後，用 ridge 去擬合「狀態模型的折外殘差」，避免音訊頭只是重學一次狀態。另一組歸因實驗把狀態模型換成直接預測變化量的 histogram gradient boosting（HGB），並分出只看過去音訊、只看未來音訊兩種頭。

負對照與統計。 錯位對照保留原本的狀態與過去音訊，只換掉未來片段：一種是平移約 5 秒（15 秒預測時與正確區間仍重疊約 62%），一種是同曲中至少相隔 5 秒、MuQ 平均向量餘弦最高的片段。所有切分都以整首歌為單位（3 次重複 × 5 折外層、4 折內層），不確定性用 5,000 次以歌曲為群集的 bootstrap。資料是 PMEmo（10 秒時 611 首、19,336 個視窗）與 DEAM 中排除 305 首開發用曲後的 1,494 首（10 秒時 74,181 個視窗），只報喚起度。

Target and legal information. At origin t only labels up to t are visible; the target is the change Δy = y(t+H) − y(t), with H of 10 or 15 s. The music that may be inspected is the interval [t, t+H−δ], with δ = 2 s. Audio is encoded by a frozen MuQ-large in independent 4-s chunks at a 1-s stride, and only chunks lying entirely inside the legal interval are used; for t = 20 and H = 10, the last legal chunk is [24, 28] and [25, 29] is illegal.

Model. The state vector holds current arousal and valence, 1/2/4-step differences, a 5-frame mean and elapsed seconds, and a ridge regressor first predicts y(t+H). On the audio side, the future trajectory's mean, standard deviation, endpoint minus the anchor, and future mean minus past mean are concatenated with nine distance scalars, reduced by train-fold PCA-256, and fitted by ridge to the state model's out-of-fold residuals, so the audio head cannot simply relearn state. A separate attribution experiment swaps the state model for a histogram gradient booster (HGB) that predicts the change directly, and splits the audio head into past-only and future-only variants.

Negative controls and statistics. Displacement controls keep the original state and past audio and replace only the future segment: one shifts it by about 5 s (still overlapping the correct interval by about 62% at the 15-s horizon), the other picks the same-song segment at least 5 s away with the highest cosine similarity of mean MuQ vectors. All splits hold out whole songs (3 repeats × 5 outer folds, 4 inner folds), and uncertainty comes from 5,000 song-cluster bootstrap draws. The data are PMEmo (611 songs and 19,336 windows at 10 s) and the 1,494 DEAM songs left after excluding 305 development songs (74,181 windows at 10 s); only arousal is reported.

限制Limitations

2 秒的反應延遲可能太短。 論文自己引用的 Mariooryad 與 Busso 那條線指出，連續情緒標註相對刺激的延遲常在數秒；那麼「t+H−2 秒以前的未來音訊」裡，最靠近目標的那幾秒很可能正是標註者在 t+H 時回應的東西，這就退回到帶延遲的辨識。作者用最早那段 4 秒 chunk（離目標 5.5–11 秒）做了回應：它仍比校準 HGB 高 0.044–0.084 CCC，但 DEAM 15 秒那格的修正下界只有 0.005；最晚那段 chunk 與全區間平均比它高很多（全區間比最早 chunk 多 0.076–0.198），代表大部分增益集中在靠近目標的音訊。

基準狀態模型很弱。 狀態 ridge 的 CCC 只有 0.006–0.058，摘要裡最醒目的「+0.389–0.507」就是對它算的；換成校準 HGB（0.127–0.180）後，完整表示的增益降到 0.268–0.382。CCC 對尺度很敏感，所幸論文也報了 MAE：完整表示相對未校準 HGB 的 MAE 只降 3.7–10.0%，只看未來音訊的版本在 PMEmo 15 秒那格 MAE 還高了 1.2%。

範圍窄、流程有瑕疵紀錄。 只報喚起度，效價沒有支持結論；PMEmo 是副歌片段，標註是群體平均的感知情緒，不是個人的誘發情緒。作者坦承早期的顯式聲學因子實驗跨越了觀測邊界，已整批撤下；DEAM 的程式碼凍結 hash 與現行實作不一致，所以「事前凍結」只能當歷史紀錄；MuQ 的預訓練資料是否包含這些歌也沒排除。

A 2-s response delay may be too short. The Mariooryad and Busso line the paper itself cites shows that continuous emotion ratings often lag the stimulus by several seconds; if so, the seconds of 'future audio before t+H−2 s' nearest the target are plausibly what the annotator is responding to at t+H, which falls back to lagged recognition. The authors answer with the earliest 4-s chunk (5.5–11 s before the target): it still beats calibrated HGB by 0.044–0.084 CCC, but the corrected lower bound in the DEAM 15-s cell is only 0.005, and the latest chunk and full-support mean are much stronger (the full-support mean exceeds the earliest chunk by 0.076–0.198), so most of the gain sits in audio close to the target.

The baseline state model is weak. The state ridge reaches only 0.006–0.058 CCC, and the headline '+0.389–0.507' is measured against it; against calibrated HGB (0.127–0.180) the complete representation's gain drops to 0.268–0.382. CCC is scale-sensitive, and fortunately the paper also reports MAE: the complete representation lowers MAE relative to uncalibrated HGB by only 3.7–10.0%, and the future-only version's MAE is 1.2% higher in the PMEmo 15-s cell.

Narrow scope, with documented process problems. Only arousal is reported, and valence does not support the conclusions; PMEmo consists of chorus clips, and the labels are consensus perceived emotion, not an individual's induced emotion. The authors admit that earlier explicit acoustic-factor experiments crossed the observation boundary and withdrew them wholesale; the DEAM code-freeze hash does not match the current implementation, so the 'pre-registered freeze' is only historical provenance; and overlap between these songs and MuQ's pretraining data is not excluded.

討論Discussion

最有價值的是那個編碼器洩漏實驗。 三首 PMEmo 歌在切點之後全部補零、長度不變，再整首丟進 MuQ，切點前最後 10 秒的隱藏狀態平均餘弦只剩 0.890–0.923，最低到 0.294–0.366，相對 L2 差 0.350–0.445。這代表任何「整首編碼後再按時間切片」的 MER 或結構分析流程，宣稱只用到過去資訊都站不住。這個稽核成本極低，值得任何用 MERT、MuQ 這類雙向編碼器做時序任務的人照做一遍。

位置對照做得比一般論文認真。 拿同曲隨便一段當負例太容易，所以作者用了重疊 38–63% 的平移片段，以及 MuQ 餘弦 0.939–0.979 的最近鄰片段；正確位置分別多出 0.100–0.209 與 0.078–0.105 CCC，15 個重複×折的格子全部為正。分位數分析也很實在：變化量最小的那 25% 裡，什麼都不預測的狀態模型 MAE 最低；最大的那 25% 裡，DEAM 10 秒的 MAE 從 0.139 降到 0.104，錯位音訊只到 0.122。換句話說，音訊的價值集中在預測「大幅偏離慣性」的時刻。

和人類一致度的比較要小心讀。 DEAM 隨機切兩半標註者的 CCC 是 0.472（10 秒）與 0.529（15 秒），模型是 0.458 與 0.564；作者自己也說這不是上限，因為目標是全體平均、雜訊比半數平均少。整體來看，這是一篇模型簡單（PCA 加 ridge）、在一台 Apple M5 Pro 上跑完、但評估紀律很嚴的論文，貢獻是任務定義與稽核方法，而不是一個能直接拿去排歌的系統。

The most valuable piece is the encoder-leakage experiment. For three PMEmo songs, everything after a cutoff is zeroed with the length unchanged and the whole song is passed through MuQ; in the final 10 s before the cutoff, mean cosine similarity of hidden states falls to 0.890–0.923, the minimum to 0.294–0.366, and the relative L2 difference is 0.350–0.445. Any MER or structure pipeline that encodes a whole song and then slices by time therefore cannot claim to use only past information. The audit is very cheap and worth repeating for anyone using bidirectional encoders such as MERT or MuQ on temporal tasks.

The placement controls are more careful than usual. A random same-song segment is too easy a negative, so the authors use shifted segments overlapping 38–63% and nearest-neighbor segments with MuQ cosine 0.939–0.979; correct placement adds 0.100–0.209 and 0.078–0.105 CCC respectively, positive in all 15 repeat–fold cells. The quartile analysis is also concrete: in the 25% of windows with the smallest change, the do-nothing state model has the lowest MAE; in the largest 25%, DEAM 10-s MAE falls from 0.139 to 0.104, while displaced audio reaches only 0.122. In other words, audio's value lies in predicting large departures from inertia.

Read the comparison with human agreement carefully. Randomly splitting DEAM raters into halves gives CCC 0.472 (10 s) and 0.529 (15 s), against the model's 0.458 and 0.564; the authors themselves say this is not a ceiling, since the target averages all raters and is less noisy than a half-panel mean. Overall this is a paper with a simple model (PCA plus ridge), run on one Apple M5 Pro, but with strict evaluation discipline; its contribution is the task definition and audit method, not a system ready to schedule music.

Sound Matching with a Differentiable Karplus-Strong Algorithm

可微分 Karplus-Strong 撥弦合成的聲音比對：時域 Lagrange 分數延遲讓弦長可微、不會時間混疊；但頻譜損失對起音時間幾乎給不出方向，合成資料上的參數損失學得好卻搬不到真實吉他

Pablo Tablas de Paula, David Marttila, Rodrigo Díaz, Irán Román, Emmanouil Benetos, Joshua D. Reiss
arXiv:2610.28043 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-20
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇想讓一個以事件為單位的編碼器，從單音吉他錄音裡同時找出每一下撥弦的時間、音高與弦的物理參數，解碼器是可微分的擴充 Karplus-Strong 演算法，而且不靠外部的起音與 F0 偵測器。值得讀的是它的梯度分析：多尺度頻譜損失對起音時間的梯度方向正確率只有 51–56%，跟丟銅板差不多；五個撥弦同時存在時，所有起音一起被拉向正確位置的比例最多只有 4.1%，聯合訓練的合成資料起音 F1 因此掉到 0.01。要保留的是：最後在真實資料上表現最好的，仍是靠外部偵測器、只學音色的版本，而 Harmonics plus Noise 基線在多數重建指標上更好（多尺度頻譜損失 183.43 對 262.72）；唯一勝出的 CLAP 分布距離（163.95 對 183.11）沒有聽測佐證，「真實資料」也只是 NSynth 的木吉他單音。Why this one: This paper wants an event-based encoder to recover, from a monophonic guitar recording, the time, pitch and physical string parameters of every pluck at once, with a differentiable extended Karplus-Strong algorithm as the decoder and without external onset and F0 detectors. It is worth reading for its gradient analysis: the multi-scale spectral loss points the onset-time gradient in the right direction only 51–56% of the time, about a coin flip, and with five plucks present the share of cases where all onsets are pulled toward their targets together is at most 4.1%, which is why joint training drops onset F1 on synthetic data to 0.01. The caveats: the best version on real data still relies on external detectors and learns only timbre, and the Harmonics plus Noise baseline is better on most reconstruction metrics (multi-scale spectral loss 183.43 vs 262.72); the one metric it wins, CLAP distributional distance (163.95 vs 183.11), has no listening test behind it, and the 'real data' is only NSynth acoustic guitar single notes.

動機Motivation

Karplus-Strong 演算法（KSA）把一小段噪音丟進帶迴授的延遲線，就能便宜地模擬撥弦。DDSP 讓 DSP 參數可以端到端由神經網路學，但標準的 Harmonics plus Noise（HpN）解碼器需要大量逐幀參數；KSA 的參數少得多、天生帶有撥弦瞬態，而且參數有物理意義（衰減、阻尼、撥弦位置、力度），是很自然的替代解碼器。

作者先前的版本（DMRN+20 研討會）與 Hayes 等的線上補充材料，都靠外部的 F0 與起音偵測器。F0 偵測器在撥弦瞬態附近容易出八度錯誤，起音偵測器會漏抓、時間偏移，還會把手指碰品絲的聲音誤當撥弦。另外，在沒有顫音或推弦的前提下，一下撥弦的阻尼、衰減與 F0 本來就是常數，沒必要逐幀預測。所以目標是一個以事件為單位、自監督的模型：不用人工標註，就能說出有幾下撥弦、各在何時、什麼音高，以及弦的音色參數。

The Karplus-Strong algorithm (KSA) feeds a short noise burst through a delay line with feedback and simulates a plucked string cheaply. DDSP lets neural networks learn DSP parameters end to end, but the standard Harmonics plus Noise (HpN) decoder needs many frame-wise parameters; KSA needs far fewer, naturally carries pluck transients, and its parameters are physically meaningful (decay, damping, pluck position, dynamics), so it is a natural alternative decoder.

The authors' earlier version (DMRN+20 workshop) and the online supplement of Hayes et al. both relied on external F0 and onset detectors. F0 detectors are prone to octave errors around pluck transients, and onset detectors miss events, shift them in time, and mistake string–fret contact for plucks. Moreover, absent vibrato or bends, a pluck's damping, decay and F0 are constant over the note, so frame-wise prediction is unnecessary. The goal is therefore an event-based, self-supervised model that, without hand annotation, says how many plucks there are, when and at what pitch, together with the string's timbre parameters.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接相關的前作有幾條線。DDSP（Engel 等，ICLR 2020）開了可微分合成器當解碼器的路，DDX7（Caspe 等，ISMIR 2022）把它推到 FM 合成；這篇是把同一條路接到物理模型上。用遺傳演算法估撥弦模型參數（Riionheimo 與 Välimäki，2003）是同一個逆問題的老做法，差別在那是逐筆搜尋、這篇是訓練一個編碼器一次輸出。

頻譜損失對音高給不出好梯度，Turian 與 Henry（2020）早就指出；Hayes 等（ICASSP 2023）的正弦頻率梯度下降、Torres 等（ICASSP 2024）的頻譜最佳傳輸損失（SOT）都是為了修這件事。這篇最主要的發現「頻譜損失對起音時間也沒有方向」，本質上就是 Turian 與 Henry 的結論從頻率軸搬到時間軸：逐點比較的距離不知道能量該往哪邊移。用參數損失先在合成資料上監督、再接音訊損失，則接近 Masuda 與 Saito（TASLP 2023）的半監督聲音比對。

事件編碼器取自 Sound Event Detection Transformer（SEDT），而它的核心——固定數量的查詢向量、用匈牙利演算法配對 ground truth 做集合預測——就是 DETR（Carion 等，2020）的做法。時域可微分的部分則建立在 Yu 等（DAFx 2024）的可微分全極點濾波器上。

Several lines of prior work bear directly on this. DDSP (Engel et al., ICLR 2020) opened the path of differentiable synthesizers as decoders, and DDX7 (Caspe et al., ISMIR 2022) extended it to FM synthesis; this paper connects the same path to a physical model. Estimating plucked-string parameters with a genetic algorithm (Riionheimo and Välimäki, 2003) is the old approach to the same inverse problem; the difference is that it searches per sound, whereas this paper trains an encoder that outputs parameters in one pass.

That spectral losses give poor gradients for pitch was pointed out by Turian and Henry (2020); the sinusoidal frequency estimation by gradient descent of Hayes et al. (ICASSP 2023) and the spectral optimal transport (SOT) loss of Torres et al. (ICASSP 2024) both aim to fix it. The paper's main finding, that spectral losses also give no direction for onset time, is essentially Turian and Henry's conclusion moved from the frequency axis to the time axis: a point-wise distance does not know which way energy should move. Supervising first with a parameter loss on synthetic data and then adding audio losses is close to the semi-supervised sound matching of Masuda and Saito (TASLP 2023).

The event encoder comes from the Sound Event Detection Transformer (SEDT), whose core, a fixed set of query vectors matched to ground truth by the Hungarian algorithm as set prediction, is the DETR recipe (Carion et al., 2020). The time-domain differentiability builds on the differentiable all-pole filters of Yu et al. (DAFx 2024).

方法Method

合成器。 擴充 KSA 的迴授路徑上有一個一階全極點迴路濾波器，分開控制整體衰減 g 與頻率相關阻尼 a1；分數延遲用 5 階 Lagrange 內插（比全通濾波器在延遲改變時不容易出瞬態），並扣掉迴路濾波器的相位延遲。噪音在進延遲線前經過撥弦位置的梳狀濾波器與力度濾波器。時域版（tKSA）把這些合成一個閉迴路轉移函數，靠可微分全極點濾波器平行反傳；頻率取樣版（fKSA）把延遲換成連續相位旋轉，N_FFT = 2^14，但 KSA 共振極強（g = 0.999、a1 = 0.001 時 80 Hz 的 RT60 達 86.26 秒），長衰減一定會時間混疊。起音用直通估計器：前向取最近的整數樣本，反向走連續相位旋轉的梯度。

編碼器。 4 秒、16 kHz 的音訊同時進 log-mel 與一個可學習的 1D CNN，接 7 層膨脹 TCN，再用 10 個查詢向量經 3 層 Transformer 解碼器讀出 10 個事件槽；每槽預測存在機率、起音時間、F0（128 個對數間距 bin 上的 soft-argmax）以及衰減、阻尼、撥弦位置、力度四個音色參數。

三種訓練方式。 P-Only：只在合成資料上用參數損失，衰減與阻尼不分開監督，而是換算成前 10 個諧波的 RT60 一起監督。Audio-Only：用 CREPE 抓 F0、頻譜通量抓起音，只用音訊損失學音色。P+Audio：兩種損失一起端到端訓練，另外消融把音訊損失對起音頭、F0 頭或兩者的梯度切斷。音訊損失是 Schwär 與 Müller 版的多尺度頻譜損失（權重 0.05）加 SOT（權重 1.0）。合成資料每筆 1–10 下撥弦，真實資料是 NSynth 木吉他 D1–D6 範圍的無殘響樣本，測試集各 290 筆。

Synthesizer. The extended KSA feedback path has a first-order all-pole loop filter that separately controls overall decay g and frequency-dependent damping a1; the fractional delay uses 5th-order Lagrange interpolation (less prone than all-pass filters to transients when the delay changes) and subtracts the loop filter's phase delay. Before entering the delay line, the noise passes through a pluck-position comb filter and a dynamics filter. The time-domain version (tKSA) composes these into one closed-loop transfer function and backpropagates in parallel through differentiable all-pole filters; the frequency-sampling version (fKSA) replaces the delay with a continuous phase rotation at N_FFT = 2^14, but KSA is extremely resonant (RT60 reaches 86.26 s at 80 Hz with g = 0.999 and a1 = 0.001), so long decays inevitably time-alias. Onsets use a straight-through estimator: the forward pass snaps to the nearest integer sample and the backward pass follows the gradient of the continuous phase rotation.

Encoder. Four seconds of 16 kHz audio go in parallel through log-mel and a learnable 1D CNN, then a 7-block dilated TCN, and 10 query vectors read out 10 event slots through a 3-layer Transformer decoder; each slot predicts existence, onset time, F0 (soft-argmax over 128 log-spaced bins) and four timbre parameters: decay, damping, pluck position and dynamics.

Three training regimes. P-Only uses only the parameter loss on synthetic data, supervising decay and damping not separately but jointly as the RT60 of the first 10 harmonics. Audio-Only uses CREPE for F0 and spectral flux for onsets and learns timbre with audio losses only. P+Audio trains end to end with both losses, with ablations that cut the audio-loss gradient to the onset head, the F0 head or both. The audio loss is the Schwär and Müller multi-scale spectral loss (weight 0.05) plus SOT (weight 1.0). Synthetic clips contain 1–10 plucks; the real data are reverb-free NSynth acoustic guitar samples in the D1–D6 range, with 290 test clips each.

限制Limitations

「真實資料」其實是單音。 NSynth 每筆是一個持續音符，作者自己描述資料「主要是 0 秒的持續音，加上 3 秒處的消音」。所以多事件的起音偵測只在合成資料上驗證過，真實演奏裡的連續撥弦、和弦、碰品絲都沒有測，與動機裡「恢復一段演奏的離散結構」還有距離。

物理模型的天花板很明顯。 單一迴授環沒有琴身共鳴、手與弦的互動、弦與品絲的碰撞，作者承認這是結構性上限。真實資料上 HpN+ 的多尺度頻譜損失是 183.43、wMFCC 6.20、EnCodec 分布距離 5.20，最好的 KSA（時域 Audio-Only）分別是 262.72、12.18、40.39；KSA 只在 CLAP 分布距離上贏（163.95 對 183.11），而作者也承認提高 HpN 的幀率可能就能補上瞬態。

評估很薄。 沒有聽測，只請讀者自己去聽範例；測試集各 290 筆、沒有多個種子或誤差範圍，表 3、表 4 的排名差距有些很小。fKSA 加 SOT 在單事件起音上拿到 88.8% 的細部梯度正確率，作者推測是時間混疊造成的假象，但沒有做實驗確認。

The 'real data' are single notes. Each NSynth sample is one sustained note, and the authors themselves describe the data as 'predominantly a sustained note at 0 s with a palm mute at 3 s'. Multi-event onset detection is therefore validated only on synthetic data; consecutive plucks, chords and fret contact in real playing are untested, which is still some distance from the motivation of 'recovering the discrete structure of a performance'.

The physical model's ceiling is obvious. A single feedback loop has no guitar-body resonance, hand–string interaction or string–fret collision, which the authors acknowledge as a structural limit. On real data HpN+ reaches a multi-scale spectral loss of 183.43, wMFCC 6.20 and EnCodec distributional distance 5.20, against 262.72, 12.18 and 40.39 for the best KSA (time-domain Audio-Only); KSA wins only on CLAP distributional distance (163.95 vs 183.11), and the authors concede that raising HpN's frame rate might close the transient gap.

Evaluation is thin. There is no listening test, only an invitation to listen to the examples; each test set has 290 clips, with no multiple seeds or error ranges, and some ranking gaps in Tables 3 and 4 are small. fKSA with SOT reaches 88.8% fine gradient accuracy on single-event onsets, which the authors suspect is an artifact of time aliasing, but no experiment confirms it.

討論Discussion

梯度分析是這篇真正的貢獻。 單事件時，多尺度頻譜損失對衰減、阻尼、力度的梯度方向幾乎全對（tKSA 上衰減與阻尼的粗略與細部都是 100%，力度 99.6–100%），對 F0 只有 58–70%，對起音時間 51–56%。SOT 對 F0 反而更差（50–55%），作者推測是因為 KSA 的延遲長度也會改變衰減。多事件時更糟：K = 5 時「至少一個起音被拉對」還有 65–69%，但「全部一起被拉對」只剩 0.4–4.1%。這直接解釋了 P+Audio 的崩潰：合成資料上起音 F1 只有 0.01，而只要把音訊損失對起音頭的梯度切斷，F1 就回到 0.31–0.64，兩個頭都切斷則到 0.83–0.84。

合成到真實的落差。 P-Only 在合成資料上最好（F1 0.92、音高誤差 15.14 音分），但到 NSynth 會漏抓起音、F0 亂跳；Audio-Only 在合成資料上音高誤差高達 294 音分左右，是 CREPE 的八度錯誤造成的，卻是真實資料上最穩的 KSA 版本。也就是說，論文想擺脫的外部偵測器，目前還擺脫不了。

時域與頻率取樣的比較是實用結論。 兩者梯度正確率相近，但 fKSA 的時間混疊讓模型高估衰減、低估阻尼；真實資料上 tKSA Audio-Only 的多尺度頻譜損失 262.72，fKSA 是 299.47。對做高共振物理模型（弦、管、殘響）的人，這是選時域實作的具體理由。作者提出的下一步——同時沿時間與頻率搬運能量的最佳傳輸損失——方向合理，但目前只是主張。

The gradient analysis is the real contribution. For a single event, the multi-scale spectral loss gets the gradient direction almost always right for decay, damping and dynamics (100% coarse and fine for decay and damping on tKSA, 99.6–100% for dynamics), only 58–70% for F0, and 51–56% for onset time. SOT is even worse on F0 (50–55%), which the authors attribute to KSA's delay length also changing the decay. Multiple events are worse still: at K = 5, 'at least one onset pulled the right way' is still 65–69%, but 'all pulled the right way together' is only 0.4–4.1%. This directly explains the P+Audio collapse: onset F1 on synthetic data is only 0.01, and simply cutting the audio-loss gradient to the onset head brings F1 back to 0.31–0.64, or 0.83–0.84 when both heads are cut.

The sim-to-real gap. P-Only is best on synthetic data (F1 0.92, pitch error 15.14 cents) but misses onsets and predicts erratic F0 on NSynth; Audio-Only has a pitch error of about 294 cents on synthetic data, caused by CREPE octave errors, yet it is the most robust KSA variant on real data. In other words, the external detectors the paper set out to remove cannot be removed yet.

The time-domain versus frequency-sampling comparison is a practical result. Gradient accuracy is similar, but fKSA's time aliasing makes the model overestimate decay and underestimate damping; on real data tKSA Audio-Only reaches a multi-scale spectral loss of 262.72 against 299.47 for fKSA. For anyone building highly resonant physical models (strings, tubes, reverberation), this is a concrete reason to choose a time-domain implementation. The authors' proposed next step, an optimal-transport loss that moves energy along both time and frequency, is a sensible direction but is so far only a proposal.

MiCLA: Mixture-to-Constituent Latent Alignment for Polyphonic Audio Representation Learning

MiCLA：自監督預訓練時把兩段 log-mel 混在一起，從混合表示中「投影扣掉」其中一段的方向，再要求剩下的殘差對齊另一段的教師表示——patch 用硬扣除、CLS 用逐樣本自適應的軟扣除

Kohei Yamamoto, Kosuke Okusa
arXiv:2610.27491 · cs.SD; cs.LG; stat.ML · 投稿submitted 2026-10-20

為什麼挑這篇：MiCLA 是一般音訊（AudioSet）的自監督預訓練方法，音樂只出現在 GTZAN、NSynth、Surge 三個下游任務，放在這裡是因為它處理的「混合物裡同時保留多個成分」正是音樂表徵的核心問題。值得讀的是它取代 SSLAM「平均兩個教師目標」的做法：把混合表示中與甲平行的分量投影扣掉，剩下的要對齊乙，反過來再做一次。微調 mAP 的增益很小（AS-20K 43.2，比平均目標的對照 42.9 只多 0.3），但在 ESC-50 混合物上同時找回兩個類別的 JTA@10，波形混合從 26.1% 升到 35.3%。要保留的是：比較對象的微調數字多數是直接引用原論文；凍結探測下 MiCLA 在 GTZAN 輸給 BAT（85.6% 對 87.9%），只在 NSynth 勝出（86.4% 對 84.2%）。Why this one: MiCLA is a self-supervised pre-training method for general audio (AudioSet); music appears only in three downstream tasks (GTZAN, NSynth, Surge), and it is included here because what it targets, keeping several constituents accessible inside a mixture, is a core problem for music representations. It is worth reading for how it replaces SSLAM's 'average the two teacher targets': the component of the mixture representation parallel to clip A is projected out, the remainder must align with clip B, and then the roles swap. The fine-tuning mAP gain is small (AS-20K 43.2, only 0.3 above the averaged-target control at 42.9), but on ESC-50 mixtures, JTA@10 for retrieving both classes at once rises from 26.1% to 35.3% with waveform mixing. The caveats: most baseline fine-tuning numbers are quoted from the original papers, and under frozen probing MiCLA loses to BAT on GTZAN (85.6% vs 87.9%) and wins only on NSynth (86.4% vs 84.2%).

動機Motivation

真實錄音幾乎都是多個聲音疊在一起，多標籤分類要求表示同時保住每個成分的資訊。現有的自監督音訊預訓練——遮罩重建、師生自蒸餾、對比學習——幾乎都把一段音訊當成一個訓練單位，沒有規定「混合物的表示」應該跟「構成它的兩段音訊的表示」有什麼關係。

最接近的 SSLAM 有用到這個對應：它把兩段頻譜混在一起，遮罩預測的目標設成兩段教師目標的平均。作者認為平均會把兩個成分的監督壓成一個點，不會逼模型讓兩者在下游都讀得到。MiCLA 的出發點就是換掉這個平均目標，用方向性的殘差對齊，並且不需要類別標註或單獨分離的音軌——構成混合物的兩段本身也可以是多聲源的。

Real recordings almost always overlap several sounds, and multi-label classification needs a representation that keeps information about every constituent. Existing self-supervised audio pre-training (masked reconstruction, teacher–student self-distillation, contrastive learning) almost always treats a clip as one training unit and says nothing about how a mixture's representation should relate to the representations of the two clips that make it up.

The closest method, SSLAM, does use this correspondence: it mixes two spectrograms and sets the masked-prediction target to the average of the two teacher targets. The authors argue that averaging collapses the supervision of both constituents into one point and does not push the model to keep both readable downstream. MiCLA's starting point is to replace that averaged target with directional residual alignment, without class labels or isolated stems; the two clips forming a mixture may themselves contain multiple sources.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

骨幹與訓練流程大多是現成的：兩階段預訓練沿用 SSLAM（ICLR 2025），反向區塊遮罩、多遮罩與 CNN 預測器沿用 EAT，實例層級對比正則（ICR）沿用 AaSP，凍結評估用 BAT 提出的 Convex Gated Probing（CGP）。比較對象還有 Audio-MAE、BEATs、data2vec、AudioMosaic 等。真正新的只有第二階段的混合物對齊目標。

在「混合」這件事上，最值得對照的有三個。一是 BYOL-A（Niizumi 等，2021），它把別的片段混進來當資料增強，要求表示對背景混入不變——方向剛好和 MiCLA 相反，MiCLA 要的是兩個成分都留著。二是 MixIT（Wisdom 等，2020），用「混合物的混合物」訓練分離器，這是在波形或遮罩層級把成分拆開；MiCLA 不重建任何訊號，只在潛在空間做對應。三是影像的 Un-Mix（Shen 等，2022）與 MixMAE，它們也利用混合影像與來源影像的對應做自監督；MiCLA 的差別是全頻譜的凸組合而不是拼貼，以及「先投影扣掉一方再對齊另一方」這一步。

那個投影扣除運算子本身就是 Gram-Schmidt 正交化的一步，在表示去偏（例如 INLP 把特定屬性方向投影掉）裡很常見；把它放進自監督目標裡、讓被扣掉的那一方也接收梯度，才是這篇的設計。

The backbone and training pipeline are mostly off the shelf: two-stage pre-training follows SSLAM (ICLR 2025), inverse block masking, multi-mask training and the CNN predictor follow EAT, instance-level contrastive regularization (ICR) follows AaSP, and frozen evaluation uses the Convex Gated Probing (CGP) proposed with BAT. Other baselines include Audio-MAE, BEATs, data2vec and AudioMosaic. Only the Stage-2 mixture alignment objective is actually new.

On mixing itself, three comparisons matter most. First, BYOL-A (Niizumi et al., 2021) mixes in other clips as augmentation and asks the representation to be invariant to the added background, the exact opposite of MiCLA, which wants both constituents kept. Second, MixIT (Wisdom et al., 2020) trains a separator from mixtures of mixtures, pulling constituents apart at the waveform or mask level; MiCLA reconstructs no signal and works only in latent space. Third, Un-Mix (Shen et al., 2022) and MixMAE in vision also exploit the correspondence between mixed and source images for self-supervision; MiCLA differs in using convex interpolation of whole spectrograms rather than patch mosaics, and in the step of projecting one side out before aligning to the other.

The projection-removal operator is itself one step of Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization and is common in representation debiasing (for example, INLP projects out a protected-attribute direction); the design contribution is putting it inside a self-supervised objective and letting the removed side receive gradients too.

方法Method

第一階段。 12 層 ViT（D = 768、16×16 patch、頻率軸絕對位置編碼、時間軸 RoPE）在 AS-2M 上訓練 40 萬步，損失是遮罩自蒸餾（patch 目標取所有教師層平均、CLS 目標取最後一層 patch 平均）加上權重 0.01 的 ICR。

第二階段的混合物對齊。 同一批次內相鄰兩段標準化後的 log-mel 做凸組合，μ ~ Beta(4, 4)。定義殘差運算子 ρ_α(u, v) = u − α·(uᵀv / ‖v‖²)·v。以甲為目標、乙為對手：在每個可見 patch 位置，把混合物的 student token 對乙的 student token 完全扣除（α = 1），送進預測器後對齊甲在遮罩位置的教師 patch 目標；CLS 則用逐樣本的閉式解 α* = clip(1 − z_aᵀz_b / z_mixᵀz_b) 決定扣多少，算 α* 時停梯度，扣完後對齊甲的教師 CLS 目標。兩個方向各做一次、等權相加；μ 只用來混輸入，不加權損失。第一階段的兩個損失在第二階段保留。

幾何動機。 在理想化設定下（學生等於教師、目標單位長度、夾角 θ），單純讓混合表示同時靠近兩個目標，最佳解是兩者平均，對夾角沒有任何壓力；殘差對齊的雙向最小損失則是 2cos²θ，只有兩個方向正交時才為零。作者也說這只是傾向，不保證實際訓練會正交。下游推論時不用混合物，也沒有額外成本。

Stage 1. A 12-layer ViT (D = 768, 16×16 patches, absolute positional embeddings along frequency, RoPE along time) trains on AS-2M for 400K steps with masked self-distillation (patch targets average all teacher layers; the CLS target averages final-layer patches) plus ICR at weight 0.01.

Stage-2 mixture alignment. Adjacent standardized log-mel spectrograms in a batch are convexly mixed with μ ~ Beta(4, 4). Define the residual operator ρ_α(u, v) = u − α·(uᵀv / ‖v‖²)·v. With A as target and B as counterpart: at each visible patch position the mixture's student token has B's student token fully removed (α = 1), passes through the predictor, and is aligned with A's teacher patch targets at masked positions; for CLS, a per-sample closed-form α* = clip(1 − z_aᵀz_b / z_mixᵀz_b) decides how much to remove, computed under stop-gradient, and the residual is aligned with A's teacher CLS target. Both directions are computed and weighted equally; μ only mixes the input and does not weight the loss. Both Stage-1 losses are kept in Stage 2.

Geometric motivation. In an idealized setting (student equals teacher, unit-length targets, angle θ), simply pulling the mixture representation toward both targets has the average as its optimum and puts no pressure on the angle, whereas residual alignment has a bidirectional minimum loss of 2cos²θ, zero only when the two directions are orthogonal. The authors say this is a tendency, not a guarantee of orthogonality in practice. No mixtures are used at inference, so there is no extra cost.

限制Limitations

主要增益在雜訊範圍附近。 AS-20K 微調時，平均目標對照 42.9、完整 MiCLA 43.2，差 0.3；只對齊 patch 42.9、只對齊 CLS 43.1、CLS 改用硬扣除 43.0。這些都是同一個預訓練檢查點、3 個下游種子，標準差 0.0–0.2，預訓練本身的變異沒有估計。混合比例敏感度表是單次執行，範圍 42.9–43.3，跨度 0.4 已經比 0.3 的主要增益還大。反而拿掉第二階段的 ICR 掉 1.2，比混合物目標本身的影響大得多。

比較不完全對等。 表 III 的基線微調數字是引用各自論文，不是在同一流程下重跑；MiCLA 在 AS-20K 比 AudioMosaic 高 0.7，AS-2M 只高 0.1。CGP 雖然是統一重跑，但 MiCLA 用 608 幀輸入、其他模型用 1024 幀。換成最後一層線性探測，MiCLA 在 AS-2M、AS-20K、FSD-50K 三項都輸 BAT（34.7/29.5/52.1 對 37.4/32.5/55.8），在 AudioSet 兩項也輸 AudioMosaic。

對音樂讀者來說證據很少。 混合物是兩段 log-mel 的凸組合，作者明說不是物理混音；聯合檢索的診斷只做兩個來源、只用 ESC-50 環境音。音樂相關的只有三個單標籤任務：GTZAN（已知有重複與錯標問題）85.6% 輸 BAT 的 87.9%，Surge 49.6% 輸 BAT 的 50.7%，NSynth 86.4% 勝 BAT 的 84.2%。多樂器同時發聲的音樂標註（例如樂器辨識）完全沒有測。

The main gain sits near the noise range. In AS-20K fine-tuning, the averaged-target control reaches 42.9 and full MiCLA 43.2, a gap of 0.3; patch-only alignment gives 42.9, clip-only 43.1, and hard CLS removal 43.0. All of these use one pre-trained checkpoint with 3 downstream seeds and standard deviations of 0.0–0.2; variance from pre-training itself is not estimated. The mixing-ratio sensitivity table uses single runs ranging 42.9–43.3, a 0.4 spread already larger than the 0.3 headline gain. Removing Stage-2 ICR instead costs 1.2, far more than the mixture objective itself.

Comparisons are not fully matched. Baseline fine-tuning numbers in Table III are quoted from their papers rather than rerun under one pipeline; MiCLA beats AudioMosaic by 0.7 on AS-20K and by only 0.1 on AS-2M. CGP is rerun uniformly, but MiCLA uses 608-frame inputs and the others 1024. Under a final-layer linear probe, MiCLA loses to BAT on all of AS-2M, AS-20K and FSD-50K (34.7/29.5/52.1 vs 37.4/32.5/55.8), and to AudioMosaic on both AudioSet sets.

There is little evidence for music readers. Mixtures are convex combinations of two log-mel spectrograms, which the authors state is not physical mixing; the joint-retrieval diagnostic uses only two sources and only ESC-50 environmental sounds. The music evidence is three single-label tasks: GTZAN (known for duplicates and mislabels) at 85.6% loses to BAT's 87.9%, Surge at 49.6% loses to BAT's 50.7%, and NSynth at 86.4% beats BAT's 84.2%. Multi-instrument music tagging (for example instrument recognition) is not tested at all.

討論Discussion

聯合語意檢索是最有說服力的證據。 在 ESC-50 上，用凍結的 CLS 表示去檢索兩段混合物的類別，必須兩個類別都進前 K 名才算對（隨機猜 JTA@10 約 3.67%）。波形混合時，沒有混合物訓練的版本 10.2%/23.1%（JTA@5/JTA@10），平均目標 11.5%/26.1%，完整 MiCLA 17.2%/35.3%，也略高於 SSLAM 的 16.7%/33.2%；在預訓練用的 log-mel 混合時，平均目標 14.7%/32.6%、MiCLA 19.7%/40.0%。微調 mAP 只差 0.3 的兩個版本，在這裡差 5.0–9.2 個百分點，作者據此主張微調 mAP 看不出「成分是否還讀得到」，這個論點成立。

但要注意這個診斷跟訓練目標是同一個形狀。 兩段、凸組合、要求兩個成分都可讀——正是 MiCLA 預訓練時優化的東西。拿它當主要證據有點像在自己的考卷上考試；真正的考驗是自然錄音中三個以上、能量差很多的聲源，作者也把這列為未來工作。

對 MIR 的意義。 音樂天生就是混合物：同時有鼓、貝斯、人聲、和聲。如果「扣掉一個成分後要能對齊另一個」的目標在 MERT、MuQ 這類音樂預訓練上也有效，對樂器辨識、多標籤標記與以成分為單位的檢索會很直接；而且可以用分軌資料建構真正的物理混音，比 log-mel 凸組合更貼近現實。這篇沒有做這件事，但提供了一個便宜、推論零成本、值得在音樂上試的目標函數。

Joint semantic retrieval is the most convincing evidence. On ESC-50, the frozen CLS representation of a two-clip mixture is used to retrieve classes, and a query counts only if both classes reach the top K (random JTA@10 is about 3.67%). With waveform mixing, the no-mixture variant reaches 10.2%/23.1% (JTA@5/JTA@10), averaged targets 11.5%/26.1%, and full MiCLA 17.2%/35.3%, slightly above SSLAM's 16.7%/33.2%; with log-mel mixing, the pre-training domain, averaged targets reach 14.7%/32.6% and MiCLA 19.7%/40.0%. Two variants only 0.3 apart in fine-tuning mAP differ here by 5.0–9.2 points, and the authors argue that fine-tuning mAP cannot reveal whether constituents remain readable; that argument holds.

But note that this diagnostic has the same shape as the training objective. Two clips, convex mixing, both constituents required to be readable: exactly what MiCLA optimizes during pre-training. Using it as the main evidence is a bit like sitting an exam you wrote yourself; the real test is natural recordings with three or more sources at very different levels, which the authors list as future work.

What it means for MIR. Music is inherently a mixture: drums, bass, vocals and harmony at once. If the objective 'after removing one constituent, align with the other' also works for music pre-training such as MERT or MuQ, the payoff for instrument recognition, multi-label tagging and constituent-level retrieval would be direct, and stem datasets would allow true physical mixtures that are more realistic than log-mel convex combinations. This paper does not do that, but it offers a cheap objective with zero inference cost that is worth trying on music.
