Beyond File-Level Splits: Rethinking Symbolic Music Evaluation via Multi-View Alignment

檔案不重複不等於曲子沒看過：MVA-SWI 用五種音樂視角檢索加上保序局部對齊找出「同一首曲子的不同版本」，把它們從訓練集移走、測試集不動；在 100 對人工確認的高相似配對上，只換掉 3.29% 的訓練視窗就讓 MIDI-GPT 的 NLL 降 30.16%，GigaMIDI 官方切分偵測到的曝光（50.69%）跟隨機切分（50.71%）幾乎一樣；但 LMD-clean 仍有 17.75% 的測試曲目沒被隔開，聽感測試四組差異的信賴區間全部跨零

This paper asks whether the test set of a symbolic music generator has really gone unseen. It is about evaluation method, but by the rules evaluation of generation belongs to `generative`. I picked it for a number anyone training MIDI generators should know: at the level of works, GigaMIDI's official split leaks as much as a random split, while MAESTRO's composition-level split leaks almost nothing.

動機Motivation

符號音樂生成模型通常用「在沒看過的 MIDI 檔上的預測損失（NLL）」來報告泛化能力。問題是 MIDI 資料集裡同一首曲子常常有好幾個檔案：不同演奏者的錄音轉譜、不同編曲、不同人上傳的修改版。檔案層級的切分只保證「同一個檔案不會同時出現在訓練與測試」，不保證「同一首曲子」不會。測試檔沒被看過，但它的另一個版本可能就在訓練集裡。

作者要回答兩個問題。第一，這種作品層級的曝光到底會讓損失低估多少？第二，在多數資料集沒有可靠作品標籤的情況下，能不能只靠 MIDI 內容把同一作品的版本找出來，從訓練集移掉，同時不誤刪無關的曲子、也不改動測試檔？

第二個問題的難處在於「相似不等於同一首」。同一和弦進行、同一種 walking bass、共用的動機在不同作品之間到處都是；切太鬆會把大量合法訓練資料丟掉，切太緊又留下曝光。所以作者主張要同時報告三個量：殘留曝光、乾淨內容保留率、以及跟「用獨立作品標籤切出來的參考模型」之間的損失差距。

Symbolic music generators usually report generalisation as prediction loss (NLL) on held-out MIDI files. But MIDI collections often contain several files of the same piece: transcriptions of different performers' recordings, different arrangements, edits uploaded by different people. A file-level split only guarantees that no file is in both training and test, not that no piece is. A test file may be unseen while another version of its work sits in training.

The authors ask two questions. First, how much does this work-level exposure understate loss? Second, since most datasets lack reliable work labels, can versions of the same work be found from MIDI content alone and removed from training, without discarding unrelated pieces and without touching the test files?

The second question is hard because similarity is not identity. The same chord progression, the same walking bass, a shared motif occur across distinct works all the time; a loose criterion throws away much legitimate training data, a strict one leaves exposure. So the authors argue for reporting three quantities together: residual exposure, clean-content retention, and the loss gap to a reference model trained on a split built from independent work labels.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最接近的先前工作：Choi 等人（ISMIR 2025）的 Lakh MIDI 去重。 他們訓練了 CAugBERT，跟 CLaMP 的嵌入相似度合併，再用連通元件把重複檔分組，而且已經明確點出「同一首歌的不同版本跨切分」是評估威脅，還釋出了過濾後的 LMD 清單（本文的 LMD-clean 就是來自那裡）。所以「作品層級去重」這個問題本身不是這篇首創；它新增的是逐段的保序對齊證據、校準過的二元分類器，以及固定測試集、只清訓練集的做法。在 ATEPP 上，Choi 等人的 CAugBERT ∪ CLaMP 聯集只有 22.86% 精確率（F1 35.21%），本文是 98.08% 精確率、97.72% 召回率（F1 97.90%），差距主要就來自這一步。

它的核心其實是翻唱辨識。 Serrà 等人（2008）用 chroma 相似度加帶間隙懲罰的局部對齊做翻唱歌曲辨識，本文第 IV-B 節的作法——把兩個檔切成短片段、算片段間相似度、跑仿射間隙的 Smith-Waterman（Gotoh 1982）動態規劃找最多三條保序路徑——就是同一套技術搬到 MIDI 上，只是相似度改成旋律、低音、和聲、節奏、密度五項加權。論文自己也引了 Serrà 和 ByteCover3，但沒有直接說「這是把翻唱辨識拿來做資料切分」，這是讀者最該帶走的定位。

資料層級的先例。 MAESTRO 與 SyMuPe 的 PERiScoPe 都把同一作品的所有演奏放在同一個切分；Aria-MIDI 用作曲家與作品編號等後設資料辨識重複作品。在語言模型那邊，Lee 等人（2022）已經示範過訓練集與驗證集重疊會讓評估過度樂觀。本文的貢獻是把這個效應在 MIDI 上量化，並給出不靠後設資料的偵測器。

Closest prior work: Choi et al.'s deduplication of the Lakh MIDI dataset (ISMIR 2025). They trained CAugBERT, combined it with CLaMP embedding similarity, grouped duplicates by connected components, explicitly named cross-split versions of the same song as an evaluation threat, and released filtered LMD lists (this paper's LMD-clean comes from there). So work-level deduplication as a problem is not new here; what is added is ordered, passage-level alignment evidence, a calibrated pair classifier, and cleaning only the training pool with test files fixed. On ATEPP, Choi et al.'s CAugBERT ∪ CLaMP union reaches only 22.86% precision (F1 35.21%), against 98.08% precision and 97.72% recall (F1 97.90%) here, and that step is where the gap comes from.

At its core this is cover-song identification. Serrà et al. (2008) identified cover songs with chroma similarity and gap-penalised local alignment. Section IV-B does the same on MIDI: cut both files into short segments, score segment pairs, and run an affine-gap Smith-Waterman program (Gotoh 1982) to extract up to three order-preserving paths, with similarity now a weighted mix of melody, bass, harmony, rhythm and density. The paper cites Serrà and ByteCover3 but never says outright that it is repurposing cover-song identification for dataset splitting, which is the framing a reader should take away.

Dataset-level precedents. MAESTRO and SyMuPe's PERiScoPe keep all performances of a composition in one split; Aria-MIDI identifies repeated works from composer and opus metadata. On the language-model side, Lee et al. (2022) showed that train–validation overlap makes evaluation optimistic. This paper's contribution is to quantify the effect for MIDI and to supply a detector that does not depend on metadata.

方法Method

先證明問題存在：受控曝光實驗。 在固定的 LMD 子集中，把 38,374 個訓練視窗裡的 1,264 個（3.29%）換成三種內容之一：無關內容（Unrelated）、同等重複次數的無關內容（Repeated-unrelated）、測試曲目的另一個版本（Same）。測試是 100 組人工確認為同一作品的配對（兩位審查者，其中 6 組是全曲對片段），每個條件三個種子、各訓練 20,000 步。相對 Unrelated，Same 讓 NLL 降低：Transformer-S/M/L 為 20.30%、21.46%、27.09%，MIDI-GPT 30.16%（95% CI 27.35–33.02%），LSTM 與 TCN 只有 4.74% 與 4.04%，條件式 VAE 1.04%，潛在擴散模型的 MSE 只差 0.01%（CI 跨零）。

重複次數不是主因：MIDI-GPT 在 Repeated-unrelated 下 NLL 是 0.747，Same 是 0.526。而且效應隨訓練長度變大，MIDI-GPT 在 5,000 與 10,000 步時分別只降 4.39% 與 14.44%。要注意這 100 組是用 CAugBERT ∩ CLaMP 兩者都 ≥ 0.99 的高相似配對挑出來的，作者也明說它示範的是敏感度，不是所有作品的平均效應。

MVA-SWI 偵測器。 每個檔案有五種視角：低音音程 n-gram、經移調正規化的和聲序列、上聲部輪廓動機、音程直方圖、時值直方圖。每種視角用 Faiss 建 HNSW 索引，只有在同一視角裡互為前 50 名鄰居的配對才成為候選。每個候選配對組成 57 維特徵：29 個全域比較、8 個檢索排名統計、20 個局部特徵（18 個來自對齊路徑、2 個 chroma 特徵）。

局部對齊把檔案切成兩個四分音符長的片段（直接用 tick 除以 PPQ，不用速度表），片段相似度是旋律 0.30、低音 0.15、和聲 0.25、節奏 0.20、密度 0.10 的加權和，跑仿射間隙動態規劃（開啟懲罰 0.35、延伸 0.08）取最多三條路徑。三個直方圖梯度提升分類器取平均、再用 Platt sigmoid 校準，門檻 0.597596 是在精確率至少 0.97 的條件下最大化 F1 選出來的。最後把正邊做連通元件，訓練集中凡是跟驗證或測試檔同一元件的就移除。

First, show the problem exists: a controlled exposure study. In a fixed LMD cohort, 1,264 of 38,374 training windows (3.29%) are replaced with one of three contents: unrelated material (Unrelated), unrelated material with matched repetition (Repeated-unrelated), or another version of a test work (Same). The test side is 100 pairs confirmed as the same work by two reviewers (six are full-work-versus-excerpt pairs), three seeds per condition, 20,000 steps each. Relative to Unrelated, Same lowers NLL by 20.30%, 21.46% and 27.09% for Transformer-S/M/L, 30.16% for MIDI-GPT (95% CI 27.35–33.02%), only 4.74% and 4.04% for LSTM and TCN, 1.04% for a conditional VAE, and 0.01% in MSE for latent diffusion (CI spans zero).

Repetition is not the explanation: MIDI-GPT's NLL is 0.747 under Repeated-unrelated and 0.526 under Same. The effect also grows with training, with MIDI-GPT dropping only 4.39% and 14.44% at 5,000 and 10,000 steps. Note that the 100 pairs were nominated as high-similarity pairs scoring at least 0.99 on both CAugBERT and CLaMP, and the authors say plainly that this shows sensitivity, not the average effect over all works.

The MVA-SWI detector. Each file gets five views: bass-interval n-grams, transposition-canonicalised harmonic sequences, upper-contour motifs, an interval histogram and a duration histogram. Each view has a Faiss HNSW index, and a pair becomes a candidate only if both files are in each other's top 50 within the same view. Each candidate gets a 57-dimensional vector: 29 global comparisons, 8 retrieval-rank statistics, and 20 local features (18 from alignment paths, 2 chroma features).

Local alignment cuts each file into segments two quarter notes long (ticks divided by PPQ, ignoring the tempo map). Segment similarity is a weighted sum of melody 0.30, bass 0.15, harmony 0.25, rhythm 0.20 and density 0.10, and an affine-gap dynamic program (open 0.35, extend 0.08) extracts up to three paths. Three histogram gradient-boosting classifiers are averaged and Platt-calibrated, with the threshold 0.597596 chosen to maximise F1 subject to precision of at least 0.97. Positive edges form connected components, and any training file in a component with a validation or test file is removed.

限制Limitations

LMD-clean 是破口，而它正是最接近真實網路爬取資料的那個。 固定門檻下 MVA-SWI 在 LMD-clean 的召回率只有 67.99%，2,037 個正配對漏了 652 個；隔離後仍有 17.75% 的測試作品在訓練集裡有版本，對照 ATEPP 1.24%、ASAP 1.61%、MAESTRO 0.00%。重新校準只把 F1 從 79.67% 拉到 82.35%；SecondHandSongs 校準後 F1 只有 72.99%（CI 51.28–85.71%）。留一來源測試裡，以 1% 開發集曝光為目標，搬到 LMD-clean 實際是 12.95% 曝光、92.63% 保留率。也就是說，在最乾淨的鋼琴資料上近乎完美，在最雜的流行 MIDI 上仍漏掉相當比例。

消融沒有隔離出「保序」本身的貢獻。 拿掉 20 個局部特徵，F1 從 92.43% 降到 88.32%、召回率從 88.45% 降到 82.07%；拿掉全域或檢索特徵只降 1.92 與 0.94 點。但作者自己承認局部那一塊包含兩個與對齊無關的 chroma 特徵，所以這個消融沒有證明「時間順序」是關鍵——而那正是論文標題與方法命名的重點。

大量手調常數沒有交代來源。 五項相似度權重（0.30/0.15/0.25/0.20/0.10）、相似度平移 0.525、兩個間隙懲罰、512 片段上限，都只給數值不給選法；附錄有門檻與部分參數的敏感度，但權重本身沒有。對齊直接用 tick/PPQ、不做速度正規化，對自由速度（rubato）的演奏轉譜可能吃虧；論文報告 ASAP 上速度差 20% 以上的配對召回率仍有 98.32%，算是部分回應。

下游結果的強度要小心讀。 ATEPP 上 MVA-SWI 的絕對偏差 0.45%／0.51%（MIDI-GPT／Transformer）明顯小於隨機移除的 2.57%／5.79%，但作者明說這些比較「沒有建立條件間的顯著差異」。預算對齊的研究裡，MVA-SWI 測試損失比獨立參考高 2.15%（CI 1.85–2.48%）——曝光是零，但損失沒有完全對上，代表移除過程也改變了訓練池的組成。聽感測試 80 位聽者、4,800 筆評分，四組 MVA-SWI 減未修正的差異 +0.09、−0.05、+0.05、+0.06，信賴區間全部跨零。圖 6 與圖 7 用不同的訓練池、參考與預算協定，是不同的估計量，讀的時候很容易混在一起。

LMD-clean is the weak spot, and it is the dataset closest to real web-scraped data. At the fixed threshold MVA-SWI's recall on LMD-clean is only 67.99%, missing 652 of 2,037 positive pairs; after isolation 17.75% of test works still have a version in training, against 1.24% on ATEPP, 1.61% on ASAP and 0.00% on MAESTRO. Recalibration lifts F1 only from 79.67% to 82.35%; SecondHandSongs reaches only 72.99% calibrated F1 (CI 51.28–85.71%). In a leave-one-source test, a 1% development exposure target transfers to LMD-clean as 12.95% exposure and 92.63% retention. Near-perfect on the cleanest piano data, then, but still missing a sizeable share on the messiest pop MIDI.

The ablation does not isolate the contribution of ordering. Removing the 20 local features drops F1 from 92.43% to 88.32% and recall from 88.45% to 82.07%; removing the global or retrieval features costs only 1.92 and 0.94 points. But the authors concede that the local block includes two alignment-independent chroma features, so this ablation does not show that temporal order is what matters, which is the very point named in the title and the method.

Many hand-set constants come without a rationale. The five similarity weights (0.30/0.15/0.25/0.20/0.10), the 0.525 similarity offset, the two gap penalties and the 512-segment cap are given as values but not as choices; the appendix has sensitivity for the threshold and some parameters, but not for the weights. Alignment uses raw ticks over PPQ with no tempo normalisation, which may hurt transcriptions of rubato performances; the reported 98.32% ASAP recall for pairs differing by over 20% in beat interval partly answers this.

Read the downstream results with care. On ATEPP, MVA-SWI's absolute deviations of 0.45% and 0.51% (MIDI-GPT and Transformer) are clearly below random removal's 2.57% and 5.79%, but the authors state that these comparisons do not establish significant differences between conditions. In the budget-matched study MVA-SWI's test loss is 2.15% above the independent reference (CI 1.85–2.48%): exposure is zero, yet the loss does not fully match, so removal also changes the pool's composition. The listening test (80 listeners, 4,800 ratings) gives MVA-SWI-minus-uncorrected differences of +0.09, −0.05, +0.05 and +0.06, every interval spanning zero. Figures 6 and 7 use different pools, references and budget protocols and are separate estimands, which is easy to conflate.

討論Discussion

這篇最有用的部分不是偵測器本身，而是第 V-D 節的語料稽核：七個 MIDI 集合、共 3,685,318 個檔。在 80/10/10 的隨機檔案切分下，偵測到的測試群組曝光從 Aria-MIDI 的 23.13% 到 ASAP 的 86.77%。GigaMIDI 的官方切分是 50.69% 群組曝光、58.52% 檔案曝光，跟隨機切分的 50.71%／58.52% 幾乎相同；MAESTRO 官方切分只有 0.93%／1.13%，隨機切的話是 62.56%／67.98%。

換句話說，MAESTRO 當年「同一作品的所有演奏放同一邊」的決定，正好把這個問題擋掉了；而一個 2025 年才發表的大型資料集，官方切分在作品層級上等於沒有做。這個數字有前提：稽核用的是另一個 49 特徵的快速版偵測器，在三個有標籤的資料集上精確率 91.70–99.70%、召回率 62.89–99.66%；人工抽查 100 條接受邊確認 91–95 條，但 20 個大元件中有 9 個混了不只一首作品。所以絕對值會有誤差，但 GigaMIDI 官方切分與隨機切分幾乎一樣這件事，不太可能被誤差翻轉。

對做生成的人，實際建議很簡單：報告 NLL 或延續相似度時，至少說明切分是不是作品層級的；用 LMD 或 GigaMIDI 時，可以拿本文釋出的工具清一次訓練集再報數字。自動延續指標也印證了曝光的效果：未修正條件下長段落比對率 21.90–23.89%，MVA-SWI 2.46–4.06%，接近標籤參考的 2.41–3.94%，隨機移除則是 10.48–11.69%。

最後，受控實驗裡擴散模型幾乎不受影響（MSE 差 0.01%），自回歸模型則降 20–30%，作者提醒這兩種目標函數不能拿來比「哪一族比較會背」。但它至少說明：拿 NLL 比較自回歸模型與其他家族時，曝光帶來的偏差並不對稱，對自回歸模型的優勢可能是被高估的。

The most useful part of this paper is not the detector but the corpus audit in Section V-D: seven MIDI collections, 3,685,318 files in total. Under random 80/10/10 file splits, detected test-group exposure ranges from 23.13% for Aria-MIDI to 86.77% for ASAP. GigaMIDI's official split shows 50.69% group exposure and 58.52% file exposure, almost identical to the random-split 50.71% and 58.52%; MAESTRO's official split shows only 0.93% and 1.13%, against 62.56% and 67.98% when split at random.

In other words, MAESTRO's old decision to keep all performances of a work on one side is exactly what blocks the problem, while a large dataset published in 2025 has an official split that at the work level amounts to none. The numbers have a caveat: the audit uses a separate 49-feature fast detector, with 91.70–99.70% precision and 62.89–99.66% recall on the three labelled datasets; reviewers confirmed 91–95 of 100 sampled accepted edges, but 9 of 20 large components mixed more than one work. The absolute values carry error, but the near-identity of GigaMIDI's official and random splits is unlikely to be overturned by it.

For people working on generation the practical advice is simple: when reporting NLL or continuation similarity, at least state whether the split is at the work level, and with LMD or GigaMIDI, clean the training pool with the released tool before reporting. The automatic continuation diagnostics bear out the exposure effect: long-passage match rates are 21.90–23.89% uncorrected, 2.46–4.06% with MVA-SWI, close to the label reference's 2.41–3.94%, and 10.48–11.69% with random removal.

Finally, in the controlled study diffusion barely moves (0.01% in MSE) while autoregressive models drop 20–30%; the authors warn that these objectives cannot rank families by how much they memorise. It does at least show that when NLL is used to compare autoregressive models against other families, the bias from exposure is not symmetric, and the autoregressive advantage may be overstated.

動機Motivation

作曲很少是一次生成就結束：作者會先聽一版草稿，改一個樂器、重寫某一段的伴奏、修一個音，聽完不滿意再退回。多數符號音樂生成系統的介面卻還是「給條件、產出一份、不滿意就整份重來」，每次重新生成都可能改掉使用者原本滿意的部分。

作者認為迭代創作需要三件事同時成立：每次修改都建立在目前的專案狀態上、只改到使用者指定的範圍、不滿意可以撤銷。他們主張現有系統很少把這三件事整合成「專案層級、多輪、自然語言」的工作流程，IteraSong 就是一個把這三件事放進網頁版數位音訊工作站（DAW）的原型。

Composing is rarely a single generation: one listens to a draft, changes an instrument, rewrites the accompaniment of a section, fixes a note, and backs out if the result disappoints. Yet most symbolic generators still offer an interface of the form give conditions, get one output, regenerate everything if unhappy, and each regeneration can change parts the user already liked.

The authors argue that iterative creation needs three things at once: each edit builds on the current project state, only the requested scope changes, and unsatisfactory changes can be undone. They claim few systems combine these into a project-level, multi-round, natural-language workflow, and IteraSong is a prototype that puts all three into a web-based digital audio workstation (DAW).

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最該點名、但論文沒引用的先前工作：Loop Copilot（Zhang 等人，2023）。 它已經做過「用 LLM 當控制器、透過對話一輪輪生成與編輯音樂」，並用一張全域屬性表追蹤當前作品狀態、按請求呼叫不同的音樂模型。IteraSong 在概念上最像它；差別在於 Loop Copilot 處理音訊、呼叫的是生成模型，IteraSong 處理符號資料、呼叫的是有明確範圍的確定性與生成式編輯原語，而且加了交易式的版本控制與撤銷。論文說「很少系統整合這些能力」，最直接的反例就是這一篇。

工具編排與音樂 LLM。 MusicAgent（Yu 等人，2023）讓 LLM 把請求拆成工具呼叫、串接各種音樂模型；ChatMusician（Yuan 等人，2024）則讓 LLM 直接讀寫 ABC 記譜。IteraSong 站在前者那一邊：LLM 只負責「理解意圖、選動作」，不直接產生音符。互動式符號編輯那一側，Composer's Assistant 1 與 2（Malandro，ISMIR 2023／2024）做多軌 MIDI 填空並提供細粒度控制，但沒有自然語言對話。

昨天讀的 ARRANGE。 ARRANGE 讓 LLM 只輸出型別化的局部編輯、由腳本驗證後套用，16 個修改請求上精確修改正確率 0.83–0.98，對照無文件整份重寫的 0.33–0.69。IteraSong 的 MCP 編輯原語加上安全檢查，本質上是同一個設計原則，只是把它包成可以即時試聽的 DAW 介面。兩篇的證據都很薄：ARRANGE 是作者一人的試聽，IteraSong 則完全沒有聽覺或使用者評估。

The prior work most worth naming, which the paper does not cite: Loop Copilot (Zhang et al., 2023). It already used an LLM as a controller to generate and edit music round by round through dialogue, tracking the current piece in a global attribute table and calling different music models per request. IteraSong is conceptually closest to it; the differences are that Loop Copilot works on audio and calls generative models, while IteraSong works on symbolic data and calls scoped deterministic and generative editing primitives, with transactional versioning and undo. The paper says few systems combine these capabilities, and this is the most direct counterexample.

Tool orchestration and music LLMs. MusicAgent (Yu et al., 2023) has an LLM decompose requests into tool calls over various music models; ChatMusician (Yuan et al., 2024) has the LLM read and write ABC notation directly. IteraSong sides with the former: the LLM only interprets intent and selects actions, never writing notes itself. On the interactive symbolic side, Composer's Assistant 1 and 2 (Malandro, ISMIR 2023 and 2024) offer multi-track MIDI infilling with fine-grained control but no natural-language dialogue.

Yesterday's ARRANGE. ARRANGE has the LLM emit only typed local edits that scripts validate and apply, reaching exact-edit accuracy of 0.83–0.98 on 16 edit requests against 0.33–0.69 for undocumented whole-file rewriting. IteraSong's MCP primitives plus safety checks are essentially the same design principle, wrapped in a DAW interface with live audition. The evidence in both is thin: ARRANGE relies on one author's listening, and IteraSong has no listening or user evaluation at all.

方法Method

流程。 先由一個輕量的流行曲產生器生出一份完整標註的多軌草稿（調性、調式、速度、每軌的身分與角色、段落邊界、音符、和弦進行），正規化成一個 SongProject 物件，之後所有修改都作用在它上面。使用者每輪輸入一句自然語言，LLM（Qwen-Max）判斷目標與操作，透過 MCP（Model Context Protocol）相容的工具介面呼叫一個或多個原語；執行成功後立即可以在瀏覽器裡試聽。

四層編輯原語。 全曲層有 set_tempo、set_key_mode、song_transpose 等；音軌層有 set_instrument、add_track、regenerate_track；段落層有 set_chords、regenerate_section、harmonize_section；小節／音符層有 add_note、delete_note、transpose_note、regenerate_note。每一層都分確定性與生成式兩種，另有觀察原語（讀取專案、音軌、結構、和弦、音符）與 DAW 控制（播放、停止、循環、靜音、獨奏）。例如「把第三軌換成鋼琴」對應 set_instrument，「重新生成 Chorus 2 的貝斯」對應 regenerate_section。

安全檢查與狀態控制。 任何修改前，安全模組先檢查目標是否存在且不含糊、參數是否合法、操作是否支援，不通過就直接拒絕、專案不變。每次修改以交易方式套用到當前版本：成功就建立新版本與歷史紀錄，失敗就保留原狀態，因此可以撤銷、重做、退回任一版本。實作上後端是 Node.js，瀏覽器端用編譯成 WebAssembly 的 FluidSynth 播放 MIDI。值得注意的是：初始草稿與所有「生成式」編輯都是同一個規則式產生器，不是神經網路模型。

Pipeline. A lightweight pop generator first produces a fully labelled multi-track draft (key, mode, tempo, each track's identity and role, section boundaries, notes, chord progression), normalised into a SongProject object on which all later edits act. Each round the user types a natural-language request; the LLM (Qwen-Max) identifies the target and operation and calls one or more primitives through an MCP (Model Context Protocol) compatible tool interface; on success the result can be auditioned in the browser immediately.

Four levels of editing primitives. The global level has set_tempo, set_key_mode, song_transpose and others; the track level has set_instrument, add_track, regenerate_track; the section level has set_chords, regenerate_section, harmonize_section; the bar/note level has add_note, delete_note, transpose_note, regenerate_note. Each level has deterministic and generative variants, plus observation primitives (reading project, tracks, structure, chords, notes) and DAW controls (play, stop, loop, mute, solo). For example, change the third track to piano maps to set_instrument, and regenerate the bass in Chorus 2 maps to regenerate_section.

Safety checks and state control. Before any mutation, a safety module checks that targets exist and are unambiguous, arguments are valid and the operation is supported; failing requests are rejected with the project unchanged. Each mutation is applied transactionally to the current revision: success creates a new revision and history entry, failure leaves the state as it was, which enables undo, redo and rollback to any revision. The backend is Node.js and browser playback uses FluidSynth compiled to WebAssembly. Notably, the initial draft and every generative edit come from the same rule-based generator, not a neural model.

限制Limitations

評估只有功能測試。 5 個專案（11–12 軌、14–16 段、6,241–8,536 個音符事件）× 4 種工作流程（先改音軌、先改段落、先改音符、混合）＝ 20 個十輪對話，中英文各 10 個。每場有 1 個觀察、7 個修改（含撤銷／重做）、2 個無效請求。結果是任務完成 90.63%（145/160）、安全拒絕 100%（40/40）、狀態一致 100%（200/200）。作者自己寫明：專案共用同一個生成後端、輪次彼此相依、流程是開發時就固定的，結果只是描述性的，不代表能泛化。

兩個 100% 都很容易拿。 附錄的範例裡，無效請求是「把第 99 段升兩個半音」「把第 2 軌升 127 個半音」這種明顯超出範圍的請求，只要做存在性與範圍檢查就會擋下；狀態一致 200/200 則是交易式設計的直接結果。真正有資訊量的是 90.63% 那一項，而它的失敗高度集中：專案 2 工作流程 1 只過 1/7，專案 3 工作流程 2 與 4 分別過 2/7 與 3/7，其他 17 場全對。15 個失敗中只有 3 個是主要錯誤（都是中文的重新生成請求，LLM 給了 section_8 而不是正確的 section_9），其餘 12 個是後續依賴這一步的連鎖失敗。

論文說這 3 個錯誤「在修改前就被驗證擋下」，但 section_8 本身是存在的段落，安全模組如何判斷它「不是使用者要的那一段」並沒有說明；如果只是剛好別的參數不合，那這個保護是偶然的。另外，只測了一個 LLM，沒有任何對照組（例如不分層的原語、或讓 LLM 直接改 JSON），所以無法判斷階層式動作空間本身帶來多少好處。

音樂本身完全沒評估。 生成式編輯背後是規則式流行曲產生器，作者明說沒有評估可聽的播放、感知音樂品質，也沒有讓專業音樂人實際使用。對一個主打「創作工具」的系統，這是最大的缺口：我們只知道它能正確地執行指令，不知道執行出來的東西好不好聽、也不知道使用者是否真的想用這種方式改歌。

The evaluation is a functional test only. Five projects (11–12 tracks, 14–16 sections, 6,241–8,536 note events) times four workflows (track-first, section-first, note-first, mixed) give 20 ten-turn sessions, 10 in Chinese and 10 in English. Each session has 1 observation, 7 mutations (including undo/redo) and 2 invalid requests. Results: 90.63% task completion (145/160), 100% safe rejection (40/40), 100% state consistency (200/200). The authors state that the projects share one generation backend, turns depend on each other and workflows were fixed during development, so the results are descriptive and do not establish generalisation.

Both 100% figures come cheap. In the appendix example the invalid requests are things like raise chorus 99 by two semitones or raise track 2 by 127 semitones, obviously out of range and caught by existence and range checks; 200/200 state consistency follows directly from the transactional design. The informative number is 90.63%, and its failures are highly concentrated: project 2 workflow 1 passes only 1/7, project 3 workflows 2 and 4 pass 2/7 and 3/7, and the other 17 sessions are perfect. Of the 15 failures only 3 are primary errors (all Chinese regeneration requests where the LLM gave section_8 instead of the correct section_9); the other 12 are downstream failures of turns depending on that step.

The paper says validation rejected these three mismatches before mutation, but section_8 is an existing section, and how the safety module knew it was not the one the user meant is not explained; if some other argument just happened to be invalid, the protection was accidental. Also, only one LLM is tested and there is no control condition (flat primitives, or letting the LLM edit JSON directly), so how much the hierarchical action space itself helps cannot be judged.

The music itself is not evaluated. Generative edits come from a rule-based pop generator, and the authors state that audible playback, perceived quality and use by professional musicians were not assessed. For a system pitched as a creative tool this is the biggest gap: we know it executes instructions correctly, not whether the results sound good or whether users want to edit songs this way.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值主要在附錄的失敗結構，而不是 90.63% 這個總數。3 個主要錯誤造成 12 個連鎖失敗，代表在多輪編輯裡，一次目標綁定錯誤的代價大約是四倍。如果把連鎖排除，只看每一輪自己的目標綁定，140 個修改請求中有 137 個是對的。對多輪代理系統來說，這指出真正要補的是「綁定錯了之後怎麼讓使用者發現並修正」，而不是單輪的意圖理解。

3 個主要錯誤全部出現在中文請求、而且都是差一號的段落編號，這是一個值得追的線索：可能是中文的序數說法（例如「第二個副歌」）跟內部的 section_N 編號之間的換算出錯。論文沒有分析原因，但這種「自然語言序數對應到內部 ID」的問題，在 ARRANGE 那類系統裡也一定會出現。

跟 ARRANGE 對照，可以看出這條路線的共同主張：讓 LLM 輸出受約束的動作、由程式驗證，比讓它直接改音樂資料可靠。但兩篇都只證明了「指令有被正確執行」，都沒有證明「用這種方式做出來的音樂比較好」或「使用者比較喜歡」。下一步真正需要的是有對照組的使用者研究，而且生成後端要換成真的神經模型，否則「生成式編輯」這幾個字目前名不副實。

The value of this paper lies in the failure structure in its appendix, not in the 90.63% total. Three primary errors caused twelve downstream failures, so in multi-round editing a single target-binding error costs roughly four times its own weight. Excluding cascades and looking only at each turn's own binding, 137 of 140 mutation requests were right. For multi-round agents this says the gap to close is letting users notice and fix a wrong binding, not single-turn intent understanding.

All three primary errors occur in Chinese requests and are off-by-one section numbers, a lead worth following: perhaps the mapping from Chinese ordinal phrasing (such as the second chorus) to the internal section_N index goes wrong. The paper does not analyse the cause, but this problem of mapping natural-language ordinals to internal IDs will surely arise in ARRANGE-style systems too.

Read with ARRANGE, the shared claim of this line of work is clear: having the LLM emit constrained actions that code validates is more reliable than letting it edit music data directly. But both papers only show that instructions were executed correctly, not that the music made this way is better or that users prefer it. What is needed next is a user study with a control condition, and a real neural generation backend; until then, the words generative editing promise more than the system delivers.

動機Motivation

文字轉音樂的前提是「描述得出來就做得出來」。但這個前提很難檢驗：要知道一個模型能做出哪些音樂，得在整個提示空間上看它的輸出，而使用者每次只輸入一條提示，看不到全貌。作者擔心的是，模型可能把機率集中在一小塊「最常見的音樂」上，讓某些調性、速度、編制、動態幾乎永遠不會出現，而這種偏差在一次一條的使用中完全看不出來。

他們把這個問題定義為模型的「表現支撐」（expressive support）：在合理的提示空間上，模型實際會產出的音樂行為子集。並提出兩個可操作的問題：一、換模型和換提示寫法，哪一個對輸出分布影響比較大？二、生成的輸出能涵蓋多少真人音樂的範圍，特別是分布的尾端與罕見類別？名稱 MADlibs 取自填字遊戲 Mad Libs——隨機挑符合詞性的字填進句子裡。

The premise of text-to-music is that if you can describe it, you can make it. That premise is hard to test: knowing what music a model can make means looking at its outputs across the whole prompt space, while a user enters one prompt at a time and never sees the whole. The authors worry that a model may concentrate probability on a small region of the most common music, so that some keys, tempi, instrumentations and dynamics almost never appear, a bias invisible in one-prompt-at-a-time use.

They define this as a model's expressive support: the subset of musical behaviours the model actually produces across a reasonable prompt space. They pose two operational questions. First, does changing the model or changing the prompt wording affect the output distribution more? Second, how much of the range of human-made music do the outputs cover, especially the tails and rare categories? The name comes from the word game Mad Libs, where players fill a story with randomly chosen words of the right part of speech.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文說這是「第一個」生成式音樂模型產出分布的實證研究，這個說法太強。 這個資料庫 11-23 讀過的 TISMIR 論文就分析了 101,953 首 Suno 與 Udio 歌曲的文字條件，發現 61% 的英文歌詞在嵌入空間是離群點；09-14 讀過的另一篇則分析了同兩個系統的曲式與和聲結構。差別在於那兩篇看的是商用系統、真實使用者的提示，本文看的是開源模型、人造的隨機提示，而且直接量 MIR 特徵。說「第一個在受控提示分布下比較多個開源模型的研究」會比較準確。

多樣性量法的先例。 本文用 CLAP 嵌入的平均成對餘弦距離與有效秩（奇異值熵的指數）衡量輸出多樣性，後者跟 Vendi Score（Friedman 與 Dieng，2023，用相似度矩陣特徵值的熵定義「有效樣本數」）幾乎是同一個想法；論文沒有引用 Vendi，也沒有討論兩者的差別。生成音樂評估最常用的 Fréchet Audio Distance（Kilgour 等人，2019）衡量的是整體分布距離，本文則拆成逐特徵的 KS 距離與尾端質量比，可解讀性較高，但也只看得到它選的那幾個特徵。

模型崩塌與同質化。 Shumailov 等人（2024）的模型崩塌研究顯示，用前代模型輸出訓練會讓分布尾端退化；Jiang 等人（2026）在語言模型上區分了「單一模型內重複」與「跨模型同質」。本文的發現在方向上跟後者相反：它找到的是每個模型各有自己的預設區域，也就是跨模型之間並不同質，而是各自窄。

The paper calls itself the first empirical study of what generative music models output, which overstates it. The TISMIR paper read in this collection on 11-23 analysed text conditioning across 101,953 Suno and Udio songs and found 61% of English lyrics to be outliers in embedding space; another read on 09-14 analysed the form and harmonic structure of the same two systems. The difference is that those studied commercial systems with real users' prompts, while this one studies open models with synthetic random prompts and measures MIR features directly. The first comparison of several open models under controlled prompt distributions would be the accurate claim.

Precedents for measuring diversity. The paper measures output diversity by the mean pairwise cosine distance and the effective rank (the exponential of singular-value entropy) of CLAP embeddings; the latter is almost the same idea as the Vendi Score (Friedman and Dieng, 2023), which defines an effective sample count from the entropy of a similarity matrix's eigenvalues. The paper neither cites Vendi nor discusses the difference. Fréchet Audio Distance (Kilgour et al., 2019), the standard for generative music evaluation, measures an overall distributional distance; this paper instead uses per-feature KS distances and tail-mass ratios, more interpretable but only covering the features it chose.

Model collapse and homogenisation. Shumailov et al. (2024) showed that training on predecessor models' outputs degrades distribution tails; Jiang et al. (2026) distinguished intra-model repetition from inter-model homogeneity in language models. This paper's finding points the other way from the latter: each model has its own default region, so the models are not homogeneous with each other but each narrow in its own way.

方法Method

五種提示策略。 以 Song Describer Dataset（SDD）的說明文字為詞彙與句法來源：（1）空白提示，一萬次都是空字串；（2）關鍵字混合，「情緒＋風格＋featuring＋樂器」，4×4×4 只有 64 種組合；（3）語料誘導模板，把 SDD 說明裡的形容詞、名詞、動詞、副詞挖空成詞性欄位，保留最常見的 500 個模板；（4）淺層 PCFG，從詞性序列統計估一個機率文法；（5）依存圖提示，以第一個名詞或動詞為根，標出每個字相對根的角色，保留 600 個模板。每種策略抽一萬條（可重複抽），只有 PCFG 一萬條全不重複。

三個模型與參考資料。 MusicGen Large（自回歸，最長 30 秒）、ACE-Step 1.5 與 DiffRhythm 1.2（擴散 Transformer，生成 90 秒），全部用預設參數、只生純器樂。另外用三個模型重新生成 SDD 的 1,106 條原始說明作為對照。真人參考是從 MTG-Jamendo 隨機抽的 10,000 首（其中 208 首也在 SDD 裡）。本來想納入 Lyria，但 API 速率限制不允許大規模測試。

特徵與統計。 調性用 SKey（取所有 30 秒重疊視窗的眾數），節拍與小節線用 Beat This!（以每分鐘小節數代替 BPM，避免細分層級的雜訊），樂器標籤用 Music Flamingo（參考資料也用它重新標註，並移除「vocals」標籤），動態用 30 毫秒視窗 RMS 的標準差與最長連續靜默（RMS < 10⁻³），提示遵循度用 CLAP 餘弦相似度。連續特徵用 KS 距離、類別特徵用全變差距離，比較「同一模型換策略」與「同一策略換模型」的距離；對真人參考則看分位數寬度比與尾端質量比。

Five prompt strategies. Captions from the Song Describer Dataset (SDD) supply vocabulary and syntax: (1) blank, the empty string ten thousand times; (2) keyword mix, mood plus style plus featuring plus instrument, only 64 combinations from 4×4×4; (3) corpus-induced templates, with adjectives, nouns, verbs and adverbs in SDD captions turned into part-of-speech slots, keeping the 500 most frequent templates; (4) a shallow PCFG estimated from part-of-speech sequence counts; (5) dependency-graph prompts, rooted at the first noun or verb with each word tagged by its role relative to the root, keeping 600 templates. Each strategy draws 10,000 prompts with replacement; only PCFG yields 10,000 unique ones.

Three models and the reference. MusicGen Large (autoregressive, 30 s maximum), ACE-Step 1.5 and DiffRhythm 1.2 (diffusion transformers, 90 s), all with default parameters and instrumental-only output. All three also regenerate the 1,106 original SDD captions as a comparison. The human reference is 10,000 tracks drawn at random from MTG-Jamendo (208 of which are also in SDD). Lyria was planned but API rate limits prevented testing at scale.

Features and statistics. Key comes from SKey (mode over all overlapping 30 s windows); beats and downbeats from Beat This!, with downbeats per minute standing in for BPM to avoid subdivision noise; instrument tags from Music Flamingo, reapplied to the references with the vocals tag removed; dynamics from the standard deviation of 30 ms RMS and the longest continuous silence (RMS below 10⁻³); prompt adherence from CLAP cosine similarity. KS distance for continuous and total variation for categorical features compare same-model-different-strategy against same-strategy-different-model distances; against the human reference the paper uses quantile-width ratios and tail-mass ratios.

限制Limitations

最大的問題：提示從來沒有要求它量的那些東西。 五種策略的詞彙都來自 SDD 的說明文字，關鍵字混合只有 4 種情緒、4 種風格、4 種樂器；沒有一條提示明確指定調性、速度或動態。在這種情況下，「換提示策略，調性分布幾乎不變」幾乎是預料中的結果，不能直接推論成「模型無法被提示控制調性」。論文的「提示多樣性」是詞彙與句法的多樣性，不是音樂目標的多樣性。比較有說服力的是樂器那一項：關鍵字混合明確指定樂器，而作者說電吉他出現的比例跟其他策略「基本上一樣」——但這個比較在正文裡只有圖，沒有給出數字。

參考資料與生成條件不對等。 生成的全部是純器樂，MTG-Jamendo 卻包含大量有人聲的歌曲，作者只是把「vocals」標籤拿掉。這會直接影響樂器數量與 RMS 動態的比較：有人聲的歌曲動態起伏與編制本來就不同。MusicGen 只生 30 秒、另外兩個生 90 秒，作者也承認這會影響跨模型比較——最長靜默、30 秒視窗取眾數的調性都跟長度有關。作者主張商業音樂分布比 Jamendo 更寬、所以 Jamendo 是保守的參考，但這只是假設，沒有檢驗。

分析工具本身是在真人音樂上訓練的。 SKey、Beat This!、Music Flamingo、CLAP 都可能在生成音訊上有系統性偏差；特別是多樣性用的是 CLAP 嵌入空間，如果 CLAP 本來就把生成音訊壓得比較近，「生成多樣性較低」有一部分可能是量測工具造成的。另外全用預設取樣參數，沒有掃溫度、top-k 或擴散步數，所以結論只適用於「一般使用者的預設設定」，作者也這樣註明。

統計呈現不夠完整。 表 1 的「提示距離／模型距離」中位比在 0.24–0.95 之間，視指標（最大、平均、中位）與是否包含空白提示而定，所有 BH 校正後 p < 0.02；但最大值那一列 0.95 已經接近 1，代表在某些特徵上換提示跟換模型差不多。許多關鍵主張（某些調性完全不出現、電吉他比例不變）只有圖、沒有數字表。

The biggest problem: the prompts never ask for what is measured. All five strategies draw vocabulary from SDD captions, and keyword mix has only 4 moods, 4 styles and 4 instruments; no prompt explicitly specifies key, tempo or dynamics. Under those conditions, a key distribution that barely moves across prompt strategies is close to expected, and cannot be read as models being unable to take key from prompts. The paper's prompt diversity is lexical and syntactic, not diversity of musical targets. The instrument result is more persuasive, since keyword mix names instruments and the authors say the share of electric guitar was essentially the same as under other strategies, but the main text shows this only in a figure, without numbers.

The reference and the generations are not matched. Every generation is instrumental, while MTG-Jamendo contains many songs with vocals, and the authors only drop the vocals tag. That directly affects comparisons of instrument count and RMS dynamics, since songs with vocals differ in arrangement and dynamic contour. MusicGen generates 30 s and the other two 90 s, which the authors concede affects cross-model comparison; longest silence and key as a mode over 30 s windows both depend on length. The authors argue that commercial music is wider than Jamendo, making Jamendo a conservative reference, but this is an untested assumption.

The analysis tools were trained on human music. SKey, Beat This!, Music Flamingo and CLAP may all be systematically biased on generated audio; in particular, diversity is measured in CLAP space, and if CLAP already places generated audio closer together, part of the lower diversity could come from the instrument of measurement. All sampling parameters are defaults, with no sweep over temperature, top-k or diffusion steps, so the conclusions apply to a typical user's default settings, as the authors note.

Statistical reporting is incomplete. Table 1's median ratio of prompt distance to model distance ranges from 0.24 to 0.95 depending on the summary (max, mean, median) and on whether blank prompts are included, all with BH-corrected p < 0.02; but the max row's 0.95 is close to 1, meaning that for some features changing the prompt matters about as much as changing the model. Several key claims (some keys never appearing, unchanged electric-guitar share) are shown only in figures, without numeric tables.

討論Discussion

撇開提示設計的問題，有幾個結果本身就有價值。第一是靜默：MusicGen 每條軌出現任何靜默的機率只有 0.003、平均最長靜默 0.35 毫秒，對照 Jamendo 的 0.072 與 135.2 毫秒；DiffRhythm 則反過來，平均最長靜默 309.4 毫秒，比真人資料還長；ACE-Step 出現靜默的機率 0.091 略高於 Jamendo，但平均最長只有 36.9 毫秒（p = 0.06）。三個模型在「休止與呼吸」這件事上的行為截然不同，而這是逐條試聽時很難注意到的。

第二是提示遵循度與多樣性的取捨：ACE-Step 的 CLAP 提示遵循度最高（0.245，DiffRhythm 0.216、MusicGen 0.163），輸出多樣性卻最低（成對距離 0.33，DiffRhythm 0.52、MusicGen 0.42、SDD 0.61）。作者把這解讀為「最新的模型最貼提示、也最窄」的令人擔憂趨勢；但只有三個模型，這個「趨勢」其實只是一個資料點，而且 CLAP 遵循度的絕對值都很低（0.16–0.25）。

第三是樂器與調性的集中：三個模型的樂器數量下尾端都比 SDD 多 2.5 倍以上，鋼琴、鼓、電貝斯的比例都高於 Jamendo，C 大調與 G 大調被過度代表，ACE-Step 甚至幾乎不產生某些調性。作者提出「代理權倒置」（agency inversion）的說法：使用者以為自己透過語言在控制，但在群體層級上，模型學到的分布比使用者的提示更決定最後的音樂。

這個框架值得保留，但要真正支持「提示控制不了」，下一步應該是設計會明確要求調性、速度、靜默、編制的提示，再量這些要求被實現的比例。目前的結果比較精確的說法是：在沒有特別要求的情況下，每個模型會退回自己的預設區域，而那些預設區域比真人資料窄。這對使用者已經是有用的提醒——選哪個模型，可能比怎麼寫提示更影響你會得到什麼音樂。

Setting the prompt design aside, several results are valuable in themselves. First, silence: MusicGen produces any silence in a track with probability 0.003 and an average longest silence of 0.35 ms, against Jamendo's 0.072 and 135.2 ms; DiffRhythm goes the other way, with an average longest silence of 309.4 ms, longer than human music; ACE-Step produces silence with probability 0.091, slightly above Jamendo, but its average longest silence is only 36.9 ms (p = 0.06). The three models behave entirely differently on rests and breathing, which is hard to notice when listening one clip at a time.

Second, a trade-off between prompt adherence and diversity: ACE-Step has the highest CLAP prompt adherence (0.245, against 0.216 for DiffRhythm and 0.163 for MusicGen) and the lowest output diversity (pairwise distance 0.33, against 0.52, 0.42 and SDD's 0.61). The authors read this as a troubling trend of the newest model being the most prompt-faithful and the narrowest; but with three models this trend is a single data point, and the absolute CLAP adherence values are all low (0.16–0.25).

Third, concentration of instruments and keys: all three models over-represent the lower tail of instrument count by more than 2.5 times relative to SDD, piano, drums and bass guitar take larger shares than in Jamendo, C major and G major are over-represented, and ACE-Step almost never produces some keys. The authors propose the term agency inversion: users believe they are in control through language, but at the population level the model's learned distribution determines the music more than the user's prompting does.

The framework is worth keeping, but truly supporting prompts cannot control this would require prompts that explicitly ask for key, tempo, silence and instrumentation, and measuring how often those requests are realised. A more precise statement of the current result is that, absent specific requests, each model falls back to its own default region, and those regions are narrower than human music. That is already a useful warning for users: which model you pick may shape the music you get more than how you write the prompt.
