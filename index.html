Finite-Sample Sensitivity of Kernel Metrics for Generative Evaluation

生成式評估用核距離指標的有限樣本敏感度

Tomasz Radzikowski, Mateusz Modrzejewski
arXiv:2610.34477 · cs.LG; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-26
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：音樂生成論文愈來愈常報 KAD（Kernel Audio Distance）或 KID，但一個數字不會告訴你它在這個樣本數下「看得見」哪種差異。這篇把兩個預設核當成置換檢定，在 CLaMP 2、VGGish、PANNs 與兩組 Inception 特徵上施加已知的人造改變：每邊 780 個樣本時，兩個預設都在 20/20 次偵測到平移，但對傳輸距離至少一樣大、保留均值與共變異跡的旋轉，在音樂與音訊空間只偵測到 1–4/20（中位數頻寬）與 0–2/20（三次核）。KAD 的高斯核抓得到等距收縮（20/20），KID 的三次核大多抓不到（音樂與音訊 0–3/20）。把頻寬縮到中位數平方距離的 0.05–0.1 倍，旋轉就回到 18–20/20。值得記住的是結論本身：報核距離時要一起報樣本數與這個設定在該樣本數下偵測得到什麼。Why this one: Music generation papers increasingly report KAD (Kernel Audio Distance) or KID, but a single number does not say which differences it can see at the sample size used. This paper runs the two default kernels as permutation tests on CLaMP 2, VGGish, PANNs and two Inception feature sets, applying known synthetic changes: at 780 samples per side both defaults detect a mean shift in 20/20 runs, yet a rotation that keeps the mean and covariance trace, with at least as much transport distance, is detected in only 1–4/20 (median bandwidth) and 0–2/20 (cubic kernel) runs in the music and audio spaces. KAD's Gaussian kernel catches a matched contraction (20/20); KID's cubic kernel mostly does not (0–3/20 in music and audio). Shrinking the bandwidth to 0.05–0.1 times the median squared distance brings the rotation back to 18–20/20. Worth remembering is the conclusion itself: a reported kernel distance should state its sample size and what that configuration detects at that size.

動機Motivation

生成模型的比較幾乎都靠「把兩組樣本嵌入之後算距離」。Fréchet 家族（FID、FAD、符號音樂的 FMD）擬合高斯再比較；核距離則改用固定核的最大平均差異（MMD）：KID 用三次多項式核，KAD 用中位數啟發式（median heuristic）定頻寬的高斯核。論文裡常見的寫法是「我們的 KAD 從 X 降到 Y，所以品質或多樣性變好了」。

作者的問題很樸素：這個數字對哪種分布差異有反應？兩樣本檢定的理論早就說過答案取決於核、嵌入的幾何形狀與樣本數，但理論沒辦法告訴實務者「KAD 預設設定在 780 個樣本下看不看得見某種改變」，因為那取決於實際使用的嵌入分布，只能在那些分布上量。這篇就是去量。對 MIR 的意義特別直接：符號音樂的子集常常只有幾百首，正好落在最危險的樣本數區間。

Generative models are almost always compared by embedding two sample sets and computing a distance. The Fréchet family (FID, FAD, and FMD for symbolic music) fits Gaussians and compares them; kernel distances instead use a maximum mean discrepancy (MMD) under a fixed kernel: KID a cubic polynomial kernel, KAD a Gaussian kernel whose bandwidth is set by the median heuristic. Papers routinely write that their KAD fell from X to Y, so quality or diversity improved.

The authors ask a plain question: which distributional differences does that number respond to? Two-sample testing theory already says the answer depends on the kernel, the embedding geometry and the sample size, but theory cannot tell a practitioner whether the shipped KAD configuration sees a given change at 780 samples, because that depends on the embedding distributions actually in use and must be measured on them. This paper measures it. The relevance to MIR is direct: symbolic-music subsets often hold only a few hundred pieces, exactly the riskiest sample range.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

被檢驗的對象都是真實存在、正在被引用的指標：KID（Bińkowski 等人，ICLR 2018）、KAD（Chung 等人，2025）、FAD（Kilgour 等人，2019）與 FMD（Retkowski 等人，2024）。理論背景是 Gretton 等人（JMLR 2012）的核兩樣本檢定、Ramdas 等人（AAAI 2015）證明高斯核檢定力隨維度衰減，以及 Zhu 與 Shao（2021）證明高維下 ℓ2 距離的 MMD 主要對邊際均值與變異數有反應、ℓ1 距離的檢定才抓得到邊際分布差異。補救方法則是現成的 MMDAgg（Schrab 等人，JMLR 2023），它在一組核上聚合並保有非漸近的水準控制。

這篇最像的既有工作有兩個。O'Bray 等人（ICLR 2022）早已指出圖生成模型的 MMD 評估高度依賴核與參數——本篇自己也說是把那個模板搬到神經嵌入上。更近的是同期的 Chen（2026），也把 FID 與 KID 當置換檢定跑在 CIFAR-10 的 Inception 特徵上，同樣發現 KID 對離散度改變很弱。本篇的差別在三處：檢驗的是出廠預設（包括 KAD 的中位數頻寬）、涵蓋符號音樂與音訊、樣本數一路掃到每邊 20,000。換句話說，這不是新理論，而是把已知理論落到 MIR 實際在用的嵌入與樣本數上，量出具體數字。

Every metric under test is real and in current use: KID (Bińkowski et al., ICLR 2018), KAD (Chung et al., 2025), FAD (Kilgour et al., 2019) and FMD (Retkowski et al., 2024). The theory behind the study is the kernel two-sample test of Gretton et al. (JMLR 2012), the result of Ramdas et al. (AAAI 2015) that Gaussian-kernel power decays with dimension, and Zhu and Shao (2021), who prove that in high dimension ℓ2-distance MMD mainly responds to marginal means and variances while ℓ1-distance tests catch marginal-distribution differences. The remedy is the existing MMDAgg (Schrab et al., JMLR 2023), which aggregates over a kernel collection with non-asymptotic level control.

The closest existing work is twofold. O'Bray et al. (ICLR 2022) already showed that MMD evaluation of graph generative models hinges on the kernel and its parameters; the paper itself says it applies that template to neural embeddings. Closer still is the concurrent Chen (2026), which also runs FID and KID as permutation tests on CIFAR-10 Inception features and also finds KID weak against dispersion changes. This paper differs in three ways: it tests the shipped defaults (including KAD's median bandwidth), covers symbolic music and audio, and sweeps sample size up to 20,000 per side. In other words, this is not new theory but known theory pinned to the embeddings and sample sizes MIR actually uses, with concrete numbers.

方法Method

設計是「已知改變的檢定力實驗」。從同一個語料抽兩組不相交、各 n 個點的樣本 A 與 B，只對 B 施加一種變換 T，再用每種核設定做置換檢定（500 次標籤置換、水準 0.05），每格重複 20 次抽樣（關鍵格擴到 50 次）。五個評估空間是：folk-rnn v2 的 23,636 首曲子經 CLaMP 2 嵌入（768 維）、FMA 衍生的 1,999 段八曲風音訊經 VGGish（128 維）與 PANNs（2048 維），以及 CIFAR-10 與 STL-10 的 InceptionV3 特徵（2048 維）。預設 n=780，是符號語料最小曲風子集的大小。

變換有三個主角：平移（沿隨機單位向量移 s·r，r 為共變異跡開根號）、等向收縮（向均值縮 1−s 倍），以及旋轉——用 Cayley 變換造正交矩陣、繞均值轉，保留均值、共變異跡與特徵值。另有主成分子空間內的低秩旋轉與第一主成分放大縮小 30%，以及刪掉一個 k-means 群一半的覆蓋率損失。強度以高斯近似下的 2-Wasserstein 距離對齊：s=0.1 時旋轉的傳輸距離下界是平移的 1.07–1.37 倍，所以「旋轉至少跟平移一樣遠」。

The design is a known-change power experiment. Two disjoint samples A and B of n points are drawn from one corpus, exactly one map T is applied to B, and each kernel configuration is run as a permutation test (500 label permutations, level 0.05), with 20 independent draws per cell (extended to 50 for claim-bearing cells). The five evaluation spaces are CLaMP 2 embeddings (768-d) of the 23,636 folk-rnn v2 tunes, VGGish (128-d) and PANNs (2048-d) embeddings of 1,999 eight-genre FMA-derived clips, and InceptionV3 features (2048-d) of CIFAR-10 and STL-10. The default n=780 is the size of the smallest per-genre symbolic subset.

Three maps take the lead: a mean shift (s·r along a random unit vector, r the square root of the covariance trace), an isotropic contraction (scaling by 1−s toward the mean), and a rotation about the mean built from a Cayley-transform orthogonal matrix, keeping the mean, covariance trace and eigenvalues. Low-rank rotations inside the leading principal subspace, a ±30% rescaling of the first component, and coverage loss (deleting half of one k-means mode) are added. Strengths are matched in Gaussian-approximation 2-Wasserstein distance: at s=0.1 the rotation's transport lower bound is 1.07–1.37 times the shift's, so the rotation is at least as far away.

限制Limitations

第一，人造改變不是生成模型真實會犯的錯。旋轉這種「保留均值、跡與特徵值」的改變很乾淨，但作者沒有證據說真實生成器的失敗長這樣；他們自己在七對真實模型與參考資料上量到，共變異差的前五個特徵方向佔了 40–87% 的平方 Frobenius 範數，這比較支持低秩改變的情境，而那裡 Inception 上的秩二旋轉對所有設定都難（MMDAgg 也只有 21/50）。

第二，真實模型對照的結果其實削弱了實務緊迫性：十組模型與資料的比較（十二個配對與編碼器組合）裡，每個設定都偵測到每一對，唯一例外是窄頻寬漏掉 Aria 那一對。也就是說在這些樣本數下，「模型跟資料不同」這件事任何設定都看得出來；問題只出在小樣本、模型對模型的細比較，以及解讀到底差在哪。

第三，傳輸距離相等不代表感知上同等嚴重，作者在範圍聲明裡講得很清楚，本篇完全沒有聽測或人評。第四，控制組的經驗水準每格最多 3/20（15%），高於名目 5%，單格的結論要打折。第五，程式與逐格結果檔只「依要求提供」，沒有公開 repo，對一篇以可重現性為賣點的稽核論文是個缺口。兩個影像空間共用同一個編碼器，所以「五個空間」實際上是四個編碼器。

First, the synthetic changes are not the errors real generators make. A rotation that keeps the mean, trace and eigenvalues is a clean probe, but the authors offer no evidence that real failures look like it; their own measurement over seven model–reference pairs finds the top five eigendirections of the covariance difference holding 40–87% of its squared Frobenius norm, which points instead to low-rank changes, where the rank-two rotation on Inception features is hard for every configuration (MMDAgg reaches only 21/50).

Second, the real-model results actually weaken the practical urgency: across ten model–data comparisons (twelve pair–encoder combinations), every configuration detects every pair, the only exception being the narrow bandwidth missing the Aria pair. At these sample sizes any configuration sees that a model differs from its data; the problem is confined to small samples, fine model-versus-model comparisons, and interpreting what differs.

Third, equal transport distance does not mean equal perceptual severity, as the scope statement says plainly; there is no listening or human evaluation. Fourth, control rejection reaches up to 3/20 (15%) per cell, above the nominal 5%, so single-cell claims should be discounted. Fifth, code and per-cell result files are only available on request, with no public repository, a gap for an audit paper that sells reproducibility. The two image spaces share an encoder, so the five spaces are really four encoders.

討論Discussion

主結果（表 1，n=780）：平移在所有空間被兩個預設 20/20 偵測；等距收縮被中位數頻寬 20/20 偵測，三次核在音樂與音訊只有 0–3/20、Inception 上 4/20；旋轉被中位數頻寬 1–4/20、三次核 0–2/20 偵測，而中位數平方距離 0.05 倍的「窗口」頻寬在音樂與音訊是 20/20，MMDAgg 是 18/20 到 50/50。用實作回報的單位看，等距旋轉讓 KID 與 KAD 移動不到雜訊底一個標準差，平移則移動 9 到 29 個。換成白話：讀這個數字的人會看到平移，看不到旋轉。

樣本數是另一條軸。在 CIFAR-10 的 Inception 特徵上，中位數頻寬對旋轉從 780 時的 3/20 上升到每邊 10,000 起的全數偵測；三次核到 20,000 也只有 2/5，作者誠實地說五次抽樣不足以確立趨勢。窄頻寬與 MMDAgg 從每邊 2,500 起就全數偵測。哪個核能救回來取決於嵌入：音樂與音訊用窄高斯就夠，影像上單一高斯最好只到 26/50，ℓ1 距離的 Laplace 核卻是 20/20，同核換 ℓ2 只剩 5/20。

第 6 節的推導是這篇最有用的理論部分。三次核的 MMD² 恰好分解成 3/d·‖Δμ‖² + 3/d²·‖ΔM₂‖² + 1/d³·‖ΔM₃‖²：它確實含有共變異資訊，只是二階項被多除一個 d，在 d=768 或 2048 時被壓得極小，所以弱點是有限樣本的訊雜比，不是「看不到」。高斯核在寬頻寬下的首項是 ‖Δμ‖²/σ²，共變異差要到 σ⁻⁴ 階才進來；在中位數頻寬下，核統計量約有 0.64–0.67 落在這個均值項上。這兩條式子把經驗結果解釋得很乾淨，也說明了為什麼 KID 的「三次」不等於「看得到三階結構」。

我的判斷：對 MIR 而言最該帶走的是兩件事。一是每邊幾百個樣本時，KAD 或 KID 沒變不代表分布沒變，尤其是不動均值的結構性改變；二是作者給的報告協定（表 2）很實用——分布檢定用事先選定的 MMDAgg 並報 p 值，均值位移、共變異差、譜多樣性與抄襲檢查各自分開報。代價是 MMDAgg 的牆鐘時間約為單核的 16 倍，但在幾百個樣本時這不是問題。

Main results (Table 1, n=780): the shift is detected 20/20 by both defaults in every space; the matched contraction 20/20 by the median bandwidth, but only 0–3/20 in music and audio and 4/20 on Inception by the cubic kernel; the rotation 1–4/20 by the median bandwidth and 0–2/20 by the cubic kernel, while a window bandwidth at 0.05 times the median squared distance gets 20/20 in music and audio and MMDAgg 18/20 to 50/50. In the units the implementations report, the equal-transport rotation moves KID and KAD by less than one noise-floor standard deviation, the shift by 9 to 29. Plainly: a reader of the number would see the shift and not the rotation.

Sample size is the other axis. On CIFAR-10 Inception features, the median bandwidth goes from 3/20 against the rotation at 780 to every run from 10,000 per side; the cubic kernel reaches only 2/5 at 20,000, and the authors honestly say five draws cannot establish a trend. The narrow bandwidth and MMDAgg detect it in every run from 2,500 per side. Which kernel rescues power depends on the embedding: a narrow Gaussian suffices in music and audio, whereas on images the best single Gaussian reaches only 26/50 but an ℓ1 Laplace kernel gets 20/20, and the same kernel on ℓ2 just 5/20.

The derivation in Section 6 is the paper's most useful theory. The cubic kernel's MMD² decomposes exactly into 3/d·‖Δμ‖² + 3/d²·‖ΔM₂‖² + 1/d³·‖ΔM₃‖²: it does carry covariance information, but the second-moment term is divided by an extra d, squashing it at d=768 or 2048, so the weakness is finite-sample signal-to-noise, not blindness. For the Gaussian kernel at wide bandwidth the leading term is ‖Δμ‖²/σ², with covariance entering only at order σ⁻⁴; at the median bandwidth about 0.64–0.67 of the statistic sits on that mean term. These two formulas explain the empirical results cleanly, and show why KID's 'cubic' does not mean it sees third-order structure.

My judgement: MIR should take away two things. First, at a few hundred samples per side, an unchanged KAD or KID does not mean an unchanged distribution, especially for structural changes that leave the mean alone. Second, the reporting protocol (Table 2) is practical: a pre-chosen MMDAgg test with its p-value for distributional difference, and separate reports of mean displacement, covariance difference, spectral diversity and copying. MMDAgg costs about 16 times the wall-clock of a single kernel, which is no issue at a few hundred samples.

Automated Parameter Sensitivity Ranking in Audio Synthesis

音訊合成器參數敏感度的自動排名

Oğulcan Kibar, Barış Özcan
arXiv:2610.38740 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-28
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：合成器動輒五六十個旋鈕，哪些真的會改變聲音？這篇把合成器當黑盒子，先把每段渲染音訊正規化到 −23 LUFS（避免「只是變大聲」被誤認為音色改變），再用五種頻譜指紋做兩個測試：101 步單參數掃描（Sweep）與 128 點 Sobol 激發的逐格變異數（Sobol），最後用兩份排名的 Spearman 相關互相驗證。在五個引擎上，決定性渲染的四個得到 ρ=0.82–0.96，有渲染雜訊的 FluidSynth 只有 0.32。但兩個測試都固定其他參數在 0.5、都是一次動一個，而未打散的一維 Sobol 序列前 128 點其實就是 k/128 的均勻格點，所謂「獨立驗證」主要差在計分方式。把錨點移到 0.25 或 0.75，TorchSynth 的排名與原本只剩 ρ=0.50 與 0.58，這比主結果更值得記住。Why this one: Synthesizers expose fifty or sixty knobs; which ones actually change the sound? This paper treats the synth as a black box, normalises every render to −23 LUFS (so 'just louder' is not mistaken for timbral change), and runs two tests on five spectral fingerprints: a 101-step one-parameter sweep (Sweep) and per-cell variance over 128 Sobol excitations (Sobol), then cross-validates the two rankings with Spearman correlation. Across five engines, the four that render deterministically reach ρ=0.82–0.96 and FluidSynth, which has render noise, only 0.32. But both tests hold the other parameters at 0.5 and move one at a time, and the first 128 points of the unscrambled one-dimensional Sobol sequence are exactly the uniform grid k/128, so the 'independent validation' differs mainly in the scoring. Moving the anchor to 0.25 or 0.75 leaves TorchSynth's ranking at only ρ=0.50 and 0.58 against the original, which is more worth remembering than the headline.

動機Motivation

打開一台軟體合成器，面對的是幾十個互相耦合的控制項，其中很多在常用工作點上是多餘或聽不出差別的。作者想量化回答「哪些旋鈕真的重要」：對樂器設計者，這決定哪些控制項值得放在面板上；對聲音設計者，這決定探索的力氣花在哪；對自動搜尋（例如音色比對），這決定哪些維度可以凍結而不失去聲音範圍。

作者點出兩個音訊特有的障礙。一是有些參數只改變響度，在未正規化的音訊上算距離會被增益主導，把它誤認為音色影響；二是音訊是時間序列，微小的相位位移就會造成巨大的逐點差，所以比較必須在時頻表示上做。本篇也刻意把範圍限定在「排名與其驗證」，用排名去縮減控制空間並確認音質不受損，被列為下一步而沒有在這裡做。

A software synthesizer presents dozens of coupled controls, many of them redundant or inaudible at typical operating points. The authors want to answer quantitatively which knobs matter: for instrument designers this decides what earns front-panel space, for sound designers where to spend exploration effort, and for automated search such as sound matching which dimensions can be frozen without losing sonic territory.

They identify two audio-specific obstacles. Some parameters change only loudness, so a distance on unnormalised audio is dominated by gain and mistakes it for timbral influence; and audio is a time series, where a tiny phase shift produces a huge sample-wise difference, so comparison must happen on time–frequency representations. The paper deliberately limits its scope to the ranking and its validation; using the ranking to shrink a control space and confirming that quality survives is named as the next step rather than reported.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

敏感度分析有兩個傳統。局部的一次動一個（one-at-a-time）設計便宜好懂，但只探一個工作點、忽略交互作用；全域的變異數分解方法（Sobol 指標、Saltelli 的估計設計）把輸出變異數歸給輸入的一階與總效應，成本隨輸入數增長。本篇自己承認它不算任何一階或總階 Sobol 指標，只是把 Sobol 序列當成單參數的激發點。

所以最該點名的對照是 Morris（Technometrics 1991）的基本效應篩選法（elementary effects）：它正是為「幾十個輸入裡挑出重要的少數」而生的一次動一個設計，而且會在多個隨機起點上重複，正好補上本篇固定錨點 0.5 的盲點。本篇的 Sweep Test 本質上就是單一起點的 Morris 篩選，只是用頻譜指紋取代純量輸出。

在合成器這一側，TorchSynth 與 synth1B1（Turian 等人，2021）提供了 GPU 批次渲染的參數空間；音色比對工作如 InverSynth（Barkan 等人，2019）與 FlowSynth（Esling 等人，2020）反覆指出參數維度是瓶頸，FlowSynth 更直接學出少數幾個巨集潛在維度。本篇的差別是不需要任何學習、不碰合成器內部，只當成 [0,1]^N 到音訊的黑盒子，並用第二個測試檢查排名的一致性。

Sensitivity analysis has two traditions. Local one-at-a-time designs are cheap and interpretable but probe one operating point and ignore interactions; global variance-based methods (Sobol indices, Saltelli's estimation designs) attribute output variance to first-order and total effects at a cost that grows with the number of inputs. The paper itself concedes it computes no first- or total-order Sobol index and only uses the Sobol sequence as per-parameter excitation points.

The comparison most worth naming is therefore the elementary-effects screening of Morris (Technometrics 1991): a one-at-a-time design built precisely to pick the important few out of dozens of inputs, repeated over many random starting points, which covers exactly the blind spot of this paper's fixed 0.5 anchor. The Sweep Test is in essence single-start Morris screening with spectral fingerprints in place of a scalar output.

On the synthesizer side, TorchSynth and synth1B1 (Turian et al., 2021) provide a GPU-batched parameter space; sound-matching work such as InverSynth (Barkan et al., 2019) and FlowSynth (Esling et al., 2020) repeatedly names parameter dimensionality as the bottleneck, and FlowSynth directly learns a few macro latent dimensions. This paper differs in requiring no learning and no access to synth internals, treating the instrument as a black box from [0,1]^N to audio, and checking its ranking with a second test.

方法Method

觸發條件固定：A4（MIDI 69、440 Hz）、力度 64、1.0 秒音符放在 3.0 秒片段中，48 kHz 渲染，留 2 秒尾巴觀察釋放與衰減。所有參數正規化到 [0,1]，背景參數固定在 0.5。每段音訊先補齊到 144,000 樣本，再依 EBU R128 正規化到 −23 LUFS；低於 −70 LUFS 閘門的片段原樣通過，所以靜音仍是靜音。

五種指紋：log-STFT（1024 點 FFT、hop 256）、128 條 mel 濾波器的 log-mel、前 40 個 MFCC、RMS 包絡與頻譜重心。Sweep Test 讓單一參數走 0.00 到 1.00 共 101 步，每步對中點參考片段算五種偏差（二維圖用 Frobenius 範數、一維量用絕對差）並取平均；每種指紋在同一引擎的所有參數間做 min–max 縮放，再等權平均成 S_i。另有渲染雜訊閘門：基準渲染兩次，偏差從未超過三倍雜訊底的指紋記為零。

Sobol Test 讓單一參數取 128 個未打散的一維 Sobol 值，把 128 張指紋圖疊起來、逐格算樣本變異數再加總成 V_i，扣掉同樣以兩次渲染估的變異數底。頭條分數用 log-STFT，另有比照 S_i 做法的五指紋聚合版 V^agg。最後以 Spearman ρ 比較兩份排名。總成本是 Sweep 29,088 次加 Sobol 36,864 次，約 66,000 次渲染。

The trigger is fixed: A4 (MIDI 69, 440 Hz), velocity 64, a 1.0 s note in a 3.0 s clip rendered at 48 kHz, leaving a 2 s tail to observe release and decay. All parameters are normalised to [0,1] and background parameters sit at 0.5. Each clip is padded to 144,000 samples and normalised to −23 LUFS per EBU R128; clips below the −70 LUFS gate pass through unmodified, so silence stays silence.

Five fingerprints: log-STFT (1024-point FFT, hop 256), a 128-filter log-mel, the first 40 MFCCs, an RMS envelope and the spectral centroid. The Sweep Test moves one parameter through 101 steps from 0.00 to 1.00, computes five deviations against the midpoint reference clip at each step (Frobenius norm for maps, absolute difference for scalars) and averages them; each fingerprint is min–max scaled across the engine's parameters and the five are averaged equally into S_i. A render-noise gate renders the baseline twice and zeroes any fingerprint whose deviations never exceed three times that floor.

The Sobol Test gives one parameter 128 unscrambled one-dimensional Sobol values, stacks the 128 fingerprint maps, and sums per-cell sample variance into V_i, minus a variance floor from the same two-render baseline. The headline score uses log-STFT, with an aggregated V^agg mirroring S_i. Spearman ρ compares the two rankings. Total cost is 29,088 Sweep plus 36,864 Sobol renders, about 66,000.

限制Limitations

最根本的問題是「兩個獨立測試」沒有標題暗示的那麼獨立。兩者共用同樣的錨點 0.5、同樣一次只動一個參數、同樣的正規化與指紋。更關鍵的是，未打散的一維 Sobol 序列就是以 2 為底的 van der Corput 序列，前 128 點恰好是 {0, 1/128, …, 127/128} 這組均勻格點，只是順序不同；而 Sweep 用的是間距 0.01 的均勻格點。兩個測試探的是幾乎同一組輸入，差別只在「到中點的平均距離」與「繞平均值的變異數」這兩種計分——而變異數本身就是到平均值的均方距離。在這種情況下 ρ=0.82–0.96 更像是內部一致性，而不是外部驗證。

作者自己的錨點消融才是真正的外部檢驗，而它的結果不好：TorchSynth 背景參數改設 0.25 與 0.75 時，排名與 0.5 錨點只有 ρ=0.50 與 0.58，原因是雙極性調變路由在 0.5 正好是中性點、什麼都不做，錨點一移就衝進前十。這表示主排名描述的是一個工作區域，而不是樂器本身。

其他限制：只用單一音高、力度與音長；沒有任何聽測，所以「排名低的參數在感知上也可忽略」完全沒驗證；FluidSynth 的渲染雜訊即使把雜訊底改用三對渲染平均，驗證也只升到 ρ=0.37。

The core problem is that the 'two independent tests' are less independent than the framing suggests. Both share the 0.5 anchor, one-at-a-time movement, the same normalisation and the same fingerprints. More critically, the unscrambled one-dimensional Sobol sequence is the base-2 van der Corput sequence, whose first 128 points are exactly the uniform grid {0, 1/128, …, 127/128} in a different order, while the Sweep uses a uniform grid with spacing 0.01. The two tests probe almost the same inputs and differ only in scoring, mean distance to the midpoint versus variance around the mean, and variance is itself mean squared distance to the mean. Under these conditions ρ=0.82–0.96 reads more as internal consistency than external validation.

The authors' own anchor ablation is the real external check, and it does not go well: with TorchSynth background parameters at 0.25 and 0.75, the ranking matches the 0.5 anchor at only ρ=0.50 and 0.58, because bipolar modulation routings are neutral at 0.5 and jump into the top ten once the anchor moves. The headline ranking therefore describes one operating region, not the instrument.

Other limits: one pitch, velocity and note length; no listening test, so whether low-ranked parameters are perceptually negligible is untested; and for FluidSynth's render noise, pooling the floor over three render pairs lifts validation only to ρ=0.37.

討論Discussion

數字面：四個決定性引擎的 ρ(S,V) 是 TorchSynth 0.961、Pedalboard 0.923、Csound 0.874、Pyo 0.823，換用五指紋聚合的 V^agg 後 Csound 升到 0.959、Pedalboard 0.951；FluidSynth 是 0.319。穩健性消融做得算扎實：逐一拿掉一種指紋，Sweep 排名仍維持 ρ≥0.967、驗證 ρ≥0.919（決定性三引擎）；把掃描格點縮到 26 或 51 步，與 101 步的排名相關 0.998 與 0.9995；Sobol 預算降到 32 點，驗證相關最多只變 0.006。拿掉響度正規化後整體排名仍在 ρ=0.90–0.99，但 Pedalboard 的總音量在 Sweep 從第 44 名跳到第 11 名、在 Sobol 從第 47 名跳到第 7 名，正好實證了增益混淆。

排名內容本身是合理的：有離散濾波器模式切換的三個引擎都把它排在頂端或接近頂端；兩個尾巴由振幅包絡塑形的引擎都把釋放時間排第一，因為在 2 秒尾巴的片段上它主宰最多訊號。同一功能在不同引擎的位置差很多：振幅包絡釋放在 TorchSynth 是 0.92（第 1）、Csound 卻是 0.00（第 46）。累積曲線顯示 Pyo 與 FluidSynth 不到五個參數就佔總影響的 90%，TorchSynth 要 26/76，Pedalboard 要 34/56。

我的判斷：這是一篇工程上誠實、文件完整的測量工作，對 FluidSynth 異常的追查（128 個 Sobol 片段逐位元相同）尤其細緻。但它的結論應該讀成「在 0.5 錨點、A4 單音下的排名彼此一致」，而不是「已驗證的參數重要性」。若要真正有用，下一步應該是多錨點的 Morris 設計或真正的 Sobol 總效應指標，加上聽測。

On the numbers: ρ(S,V) on the four deterministic engines is 0.961 for TorchSynth, 0.923 Pedalboard, 0.874 Csound and 0.823 Pyo; with the five-fingerprint V^agg, Csound rises to 0.959 and Pedalboard to 0.951; FluidSynth is 0.319. The robustness ablations are solid: dropping each fingerprint in turn keeps the Sweep ranking at ρ≥0.967 and validation at ρ≥0.919 on the deterministic trio; sweep grids of 26 and 51 steps match the 101-step ranking at 0.998 and 0.9995; a Sobol budget of 32 points moves validation by at most 0.006. Removing loudness normalisation keeps overall orderings at ρ=0.90–0.99 but lifts Pedalboard's master volume from rank 44 to 11 in the Sweep and from 47 to 7 in the Sobol Test, demonstrating the gain confound.

The rankings themselves are sensible: the three engines with a discrete filter-mode switch put it at or near the top, and the two whose tail is shaped by an amplitude envelope rank its release first, since on a clip with a 2 s tail it governs the most signal. The same function lands very differently across engines: amp-envelope release is 0.92 (rank 1) on TorchSynth but 0.00 (rank 46) on Csound. Cumulative curves show fewer than five parameters carrying 90% of total influence on Pyo and FluidSynth, versus 26 of 76 on TorchSynth and 34 of 56 on Pedalboard.

My judgement: this is honest, well-documented measurement work, and the tracing of the FluidSynth anomaly (all 128 Sobol clips bit-identical) is especially careful. But its conclusion should read as 'rankings at a 0.5 anchor on a single A4 note agree with each other', not 'validated parameter importance'. To be truly useful, the next step is a multi-start Morris design or genuine Sobol total-effect indices, plus listening tests.

A Learning-Based Method of Automatic Music Transcription

一種基於學習的自動音樂轉譜方法

為什麼挑這篇：一篇鋼琴逐幀轉譜論文：CQT（每八度 36 格、共 252 維）接 BiLSTM，前面加一個「改良式多頭注意力」，在七種組態的逐步消融裡把逐幀 F1 從單向 LSTM 的 71.20% 推到 88.20%（4.43M 參數）。選它的理由主要是反面教材：評估協定與領域慣例脫節——MAESTRO 只拿來訓練、沒有在官方測試集上評估，MAPS 用的是 23,928／2,991／2,991 的檔案隨機切分而不是常見的實體鋼琴測試設定，只報逐幀指標、沒有音符層級的 onset F1，也沒有跟 Onsets and Frames 這類系統在同一把尺上比較。為了處理 41:1 的類別不平衡而設計的加權 BCE，權重只有 0.65:0.35，最後最佳結果還是用普通 BCE 拿到的。值得記住的是怎麼讀一篇轉譜論文的評估章節。Why this one: A frame-level piano transcription paper: a CQT (36 bins per octave, 252 dimensions) feeds a BiLSTM preceded by a 'modified multihead attention', and a seven-configuration stepwise ablation lifts frame F1 from 71.20% for a unidirectional LSTM to 88.20% (4.43M parameters). It was picked mainly as a cautionary example: the evaluation protocol is detached from field convention. MAESTRO is used only for training and never evaluated on its official test split, MAPS uses a random 23,928/2,991/2,991 file split rather than the usual real-piano test setting, only frame-level metrics are reported with no note-level onset F1, and there is no same-yardstick comparison with systems like Onsets and Frames. The weighted BCE designed for the 41:1 class imbalance uses weights of just 0.65:0.35, and the best result was obtained with plain BCE anyway. Worth remembering is how to read the evaluation section of a transcription paper.

動機Motivation

自動音樂轉譜（AMT）要把錄音轉成 MIDI 或樂譜，是 MIR 的基礎工具，而複音鋼琴仍是難題。作者列出四個成因：多個音同時發聲造成頻譜混疊；協和音程（八度、五度、四度）的泛音大量重疊，遮住各音的基頻；擊槌、延音踏板、弦共鳴與殘響讓前一個音的衰減拖進後面的幀；以及極端的類別不平衡——88 鍵中同一時間通常只有少數幾鍵在響，作者在訓練語料裡量到非作用與作用標籤的比例約為 41:1（53.6 億對 1.29 億）。

本篇的目標是在小參數量下把逐幀轉譜做好：把問題寫成 88 個音高的逐幀多標籤分類，用循環網路建模時間，並設計注意力模組與加權損失來處理上面這些困難。

Automatic music transcription (AMT) converts recordings into MIDI or scores and is a basic MIR tool, yet polyphonic piano remains hard. The authors list four causes: simultaneous notes mix their spectra; consonant intervals (octaves, fifths, fourths) overlap heavily in overtones, masking individual fundamentals; hammer strikes, sustain pedal, string coupling and reverberation drag earlier notes' decay into later frames; and extreme class imbalance, since only a few of 88 keys sound at once, measured in the training corpus at about 41:1 inactive to active labels (5.36 billion versus 0.129 billion).

The goal is good frame-level transcription at a small parameter count: the task is framed as frame-wise multi-label classification over 88 pitches, time is modelled with recurrent networks, and an attention module and a weighted loss are designed to address the difficulties above.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文的相關研究章節從 Moorer 的早期工作、NMF、Poliner 與 Ellis 的 SVM 加 HMM，一路寫到 Sigtia 等人（2016）的 CNN 加 RNN 語言模型與 Kelz 等人（ISMIR 2016）的逐幀 CNN，然後直接把自己接在 Morán（2017）的 GitHub 專案與一份 Stanford CS229 課程報告後面。這個定位本身就是問題：它跳過了這個領域過去八年的主線。

最該點名的是三個它沒有正面比較的系統。第一，Onsets and Frames（Hawthorne 等人，ICASSP 2018）把 onset 偵測與逐幀預測分開並互相條件化，正是 MAESTRO 資料集論文（Hawthorne 等人，ICLR 2019，本篇自己引用了）的基準模型；第二，Kong 等人（TASLP 2021）的高解析度鋼琴轉譜以回歸 onset／offset 時間把 MAESTRO 的音符 F1 推到約 0.97；第三，序列到序列的 Transformer 轉譜（Hawthorne 等人，ISMIR 2021；MT3，ICLR 2022）。這些系統在 MAESTRO 官方測試集上的逐幀 F1 大約九成，本篇 88.20% 的數字看起來接近，但不是同一個測試集，不能並列。

至於「新穎」的部分：CQT 加 BiLSTM 的逐幀轉譜在 Böck 與 Schedl（ICASSP 2012）就做過了；所謂改良式注意力是對隱藏狀態套 tanh 後做 softmax 再逐元素相乘，從公式看比較接近特徵閘控（類似 squeeze-and-excitation 的乘法重新加權），而不是 Bahdanau 意義下的對齊注意力。

The related-work section runs from Moorer's early work, NMF and the SVM-plus-HMM of Poliner and Ellis, through the CNN-plus-RNN language model of Sigtia et al. (2016) and the frame-wise CNN of Kelz et al. (ISMIR 2016), then attaches itself directly to Morán's (2017) GitHub project and a Stanford CS229 course report. That positioning is itself the problem: it skips the main line of the field over the past eight years.

Three systems it never compares against deserve naming. First, Onsets and Frames (Hawthorne et al., ICASSP 2018), which separates onset detection from frame prediction and conditions one on the other, is the baseline of the MAESTRO dataset paper (Hawthorne et al., ICLR 2019) that this paper itself cites. Second, the high-resolution piano transcription of Kong et al. (TASLP 2021) regresses onset and offset times and pushes MAESTRO note F1 to about 0.97. Third, sequence-to-sequence Transformer transcription (Hawthorne et al., ISMIR 2021; MT3, ICLR 2022). These systems reach roughly 90% frame F1 on the official MAESTRO test split; this paper's 88.20% looks close but is on a different test set and cannot be set beside them.

As for the novel parts: CQT-plus-BiLSTM frame-wise transcription was already done by Böck and Schedl (ICASSP 2012), and the modified attention applies tanh to hidden states, a softmax, and an element-wise product, which from the equations is closer to feature gating (a multiplicative reweighting akin to squeeze-and-excitation) than to alignment attention in Bahdanau's sense.

方法Method

前處理：音訊統一為 44.1 kHz、降為單聲道，計算 7 個八度、每八度 36 格的 CQT（每個半音 3 格），hop 512 樣本、約 11.6 毫秒一幀，得到 252 維特徵；訓練時切成每段 40,000 幀。標籤是 88 維二元向量（MIDI 21–108）。

七種組態依序是：三層單向 LSTM（各 256 單元、dropout 0.2，1.59M 參數）；三層 BiLSTM（各向 256，4.24M）；LSTM—注意力—兩層 LSTM（1.66M）；同樣結構全改 BiLSTM（4.35M）；注意力放在最前面接三層 LSTM（1.66M）；注意力在前接三層 BiLSTM（4.35M）；最後把單頭換成 K=3 的改良式多頭注意力（4.43M）。改良式注意力是 e = tanh(W·h + b)，softmax 後與隱藏狀態相乘；多頭版本把三個頭的輸出相加而不是串接，再經 W^O、b^O 與 tanh。

輸出層是 88 個獨立 sigmoid。損失比較了 BCE 與加權 BCE，加權版設 w₁=0.65、w₀=0.35。優化器 Adam、學習率 10⁻³，驗證損失提前停止（patience 10）。推論時以 0.5 二值化，再做兩步形態學後處理：刪掉短於 3 幀（約 35 毫秒）的片段，並補上同音高 1–2 幀的空隙。評估只有逐幀的精確率、召回率、準確率（TP/(TP+FP+FN)）與 F1。

Preprocessing: audio is resampled to 44.1 kHz and downmixed to mono, then a CQT over 7 octaves with 36 bins per octave (3 per semitone) is computed with a 512-sample hop, about 11.6 ms per frame, giving 252-dimensional features; training uses chunks of 40,000 frames. Labels are 88-dimensional binary vectors (MIDI 21–108).

The seven configurations are: three unidirectional LSTM layers (256 units each, dropout 0.2, 1.59M parameters); three BiLSTM layers (256 per direction, 4.24M); LSTM, attention, then two LSTMs (1.66M); the same with all BiLSTMs (4.35M); attention first followed by three LSTMs (1.66M); attention first followed by three BiLSTMs (4.35M); and finally the single head replaced by a K=3 modified multihead attention (4.43M). The modified attention computes e = tanh(W·h + b), applies a softmax, and multiplies by the hidden state; the multihead version sums the three heads instead of concatenating them, then applies W^O, b^O and tanh.

The output layer is 88 independent sigmoids. Losses compared are BCE and weighted BCE with w₁=0.65 and w₀=0.35. Training uses Adam at learning rate 10⁻³ with early stopping on validation loss (patience 10). At inference outputs are thresholded at 0.5, then two morphological steps remove segments shorter than 3 frames (about 35 ms) and fill 1–2-frame gaps in the same pitch. Evaluation reports only frame-level precision, recall, accuracy (TP/(TP+FP+FN)) and F1.

限制Limitations

評估協定是最大的問題。MAPS 的切分是 23,928／2,991／2,991 個檔案，合計 29,910，看起來是對整個 MAPS 做 8:1:1 的隨機檔案切分；MAPS 大部分檔案是單音（ISOL）與隨機和弦（RAND、UCHO），同一首曲子也會以不同鋼琴重複出現，隨機切分會讓測試集被簡單樣本主導，並讓同曲或同鋼琴洩漏進訓練。領域常用的做法（例如 Sigtia 等人的設定）是用合成鋼琴訓練、拿兩台實體 Disklavier（ENSTDkAm／ENSTDkCl）的曲目測試。MAESTRO 則只有訓練與驗證，表 1 的測試欄是空的，所以「MAPS+MAESTRO 比只用 MAPS 好」（85.40% 對 88.20%）也是在 MAPS 的隨機測試集上量的。

其次，只報逐幀指標。轉譜領域的主要指標是 mir_eval 的音符層級 F1（onset 容差 50 毫秒，有無 offset 兩種），本篇自己的限制章節也承認快速重複音會糊成一條長音，而逐幀指標正好看不出這種錯。第三，所有數字都是單次執行，沒有變異範圍，而好幾個組態之間只差 1 個百分點（加權 BCE 87.20% 對 BCE 88.20%）。

第四，消融有混淆。「注意力放前面比放中間好 7.40 個百分點」比較的是 Attention–LSTM（82.20%）與 LSTM–Attention（74.80%），兩者層的排列與是否有最後的線性層都不同，不只是注意力位置。第五，加權 BCE 的權重 0.65:0.35 只等於約 1.86:1，與它要對付的 41:1 不平衡差了一個數量級以上，而最佳結果最後是用普通 BCE 拿到的，這等於自己的實驗否定了這部分動機。

The evaluation protocol is the biggest problem. The MAPS split is 23,928/2,991/2,991 files, 29,910 in total, which looks like a random 8:1:1 file split over all of MAPS; most MAPS files are isolated notes (ISOL) and random chords (RAND, UCHO), and the same piece recurs on different pianos, so a random split lets easy samples dominate the test set and leaks pieces or pianos into training. The common protocol (for example Sigtia et al.'s) trains on synthesised pianos and tests on pieces from the two real Disklaviers (ENSTDkAm/ENSTDkCl). MAESTRO has only training and validation, with the test column of Table 1 empty, so even 'MAPS+MAESTRO beats MAPS only' (88.20% versus 85.40%) is measured on the random MAPS test set.

Second, only frame-level metrics are reported. The field's main metric is note-level F1 from mir_eval (50 ms onset tolerance, with and without offsets), and the paper's own limitations admit that fast repeated notes blur into one long note, exactly the error frame metrics hide. Third, every number is a single run with no spread, while several configurations differ by about one point (weighted BCE 87.20% versus BCE 88.20%).

Fourth, the ablation is confounded. 'Attention first beats attention in the middle by 7.40 points' compares Attention–LSTM (82.20%) with LSTM–Attention (74.80%), which differ in layer arrangement and in having a final linear layer, not only in attention position. Fifth, weights of 0.65:0.35 amount to only about 1.86:1, more than an order of magnitude short of the 41:1 imbalance they target, and the best result came from plain BCE, so the paper's own experiment undercuts that motivation.

討論Discussion

表 3 的數字依序是：單向 LSTM 71.20%（精確率 0.76、召回率 0.66）、BiLSTM 75.98%、LSTM–Attention 74.80%、BiLSTM–Attention 82.00%、Attention–LSTM 82.20%、Attention–BiLSTM 86.10%、多頭注意力加 BiLSTM 用加權 BCE 87.20%（召回率 0.90）、同架構改用 BCE 88.20%（精確率 0.89、召回率 0.86、準確率 78.90%），只用 MAPS 訓練則是 85.40%。雙向化帶來 4.78 個百分點，注意力放前面相對放中間多 7.40 個百分點，加權 BCE 換成 BCE 多 1 個百分點。

在它自己的協定內，趨勢是一致的：雙向優於單向、輸入端加權有幫助、多頭比單頭略好。作者也指出 3 個頭分別關注低音區基頻、中音區和聲與高音區泛音，但沒有給出量化證據，只有定性描述。逐幀 F1 從 71% 到 88% 的進步，有一部分很可能只是把 Morán 原始基準的 MSE 加 ReLU 換成 sigmoid 加交叉熵就能拿到的。

我的判斷：這篇作為「BiLSTM 加注意力在小參數下能做到多好」的教學示範是清楚的，七組架構圖與公式完整，適合入門。但作為研究貢獻，它的主張（「建立了有效的輕量方案」）沒有被支撐：沒有官方測試集、沒有音符指標、沒有同尺比較、沒有變異範圍。若要讓這組數字有意義，最低限度是在 MAESTRO 官方測試集上用 mir_eval 報音符 F1，並放上 Onsets and Frames 作為對照。

Table 3 reads: unidirectional LSTM 71.20% (precision 0.76, recall 0.66), BiLSTM 75.98%, LSTM–Attention 74.80%, BiLSTM–Attention 82.00%, Attention–LSTM 82.20%, Attention–BiLSTM 86.10%, multihead attention with BiLSTM under weighted BCE 87.20% (recall 0.90), the same with BCE 88.20% (precision 0.89, recall 0.86, accuracy 78.90%), and MAPS-only training 85.40%. Bidirectionality adds 4.78 points, front attention adds 7.40 points over middle attention, and switching weighted BCE to BCE adds 1 point.

Within its own protocol the trends are consistent: bidirectional beats unidirectional, input-side reweighting helps, and multihead is slightly better than single-head. The authors say the 3 heads attend to low-register fundamentals, mid-range harmony and upper partials, but give only qualitative description, no quantitative evidence. Part of the 71% to 88% gain may simply come from replacing the MSE-plus-ReLU of Morán's original baseline with sigmoid plus cross-entropy.

My judgement: as a tutorial on how far a BiLSTM with attention can go at a small parameter count, the paper is clear, with complete diagrams and equations for all seven architectures, and suits beginners. As a research contribution its claim of an effective lightweight solution is unsupported: no official test split, no note metrics, no same-yardstick comparison, no spread. To make these numbers meaningful, the minimum is note F1 via mir_eval on the official MAESTRO test split, with Onsets and Frames as a reference.
