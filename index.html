Rethinking Music Tokenization: A Semantic Codec toward High-Fidelity LLM Music Generation

重新思考音樂符元化：邁向高保真 LLM 音樂生成的語意編解碼器

Huakang Chen, Guobin Ma, Yuepeng Jiang, Dake Guo, Jingbin Hu, Hanke Xie, Wenhao Li, Lingxin Xiong, Jian Zhao, Zhonglin Jiang, Yong Chen, Lei Xie, Pengcheng Zhu
arXiv:2609.21240 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-21
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：沒有繞過語音式定義去猜「音樂語意是什麼」，而是先用探測實驗量出答案，再照這個答案去設計編解碼器，讓重建品質與語言模型可預測性同時進步而非互相犧牲。Why this one: Instead of assuming music semantics mirror speech, it measures what "semantic" means for music via probing experiments first, then builds a codec around that answer -- improving reconstruction and LM-predictability together rather than trading one for the other.

動機Motivation

神經音訊編解碼器把原始波形轉成離散符元，供語言模型式生成使用，但兩個目標互相拉扯：高保真重建希望符元保留每個細微的聲學特徵，語言模型卻希望符元容易預測。SoundStream、EnCodec、DAC 這類以重建為目標的編解碼器會產生高熵的符元序列，對語言模型來說很難建模。

語意引導式編解碼器在語音領域解決了這個問題——把音素或 ASR 特徵蒸餾進部分符元流——但語音的「語意」定義套用到音樂上並不成立：音樂是複音的、節奏更密集、音色更多樣，而且人聲通常和伴奏糾纏在一起，不像語音那樣是單一說話者獨立存在。這篇論文的核心作法，是不再借用語音對「語意內容」的定義，改為針對音樂做實證定義，再據此打造編解碼器。

Neural audio codecs turn raw waveforms into discrete tokens for language-model-based generation, but two goals fight each other: high-fidelity reconstruction wants tokens that capture every acoustic nuance, while a language model wants tokens that are easy to predict. Reconstruction-optimized codecs such as SoundStream, EnCodec and DAC produce high-entropy sequences that are hard for an LM to model.

Semantic-guided codecs solve this for speech, distilling phonetic or ASR-derived features into part of the token stream, but speech's notion of semantics doesn't transfer: music is polyphonic, rhythmically dense, timbrally diverse, and its vocals are tangled with accompaniment rather than sitting alone as a single speaker's voice. The paper's core move is to stop borrowing speech's definition of "semantic content" and instead define it empirically for music, then build a codec around that definition.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

以重建為導向的神經編解碼器（SoundStream、EnCodec、DAC，以及 WavTokenizer、BigCodec 這類單一碼本版本）透過殘差向量量化(RVQ)優化感知保真度，但沒有一個直接以「語言模型好不好預測」為目標。語音端的語意引導式編解碼器補上了這一塊：SpeechTokenizer 把 HuBERT 特徵蒸餾進第一層 RVQ，Mimi 把 WavLM 蒸餾進專屬的語意量化器，CosyVoice 則從 ASR 編碼器取得監督式符元。這些方法都假設「語意」大致等同於音素內容，論文認為這套假設套用到音樂上並不成立。

針對音樂，論文點出三種既有作法，並各自指出具體缺陷。第一種是把語音編解碼器直接拿到音樂上重訓（YuE），這樣會繼承原本針對音素設計的語意限制，作者認為這「可能無法捕捉複雜混音中的音樂結構」。第二種是「先量化、後生成」路線（LeVo、HeartMuLa），把音樂自監督模型（MERT 或 MuQ）搭配擴散模型解碼器，這把編解碼器的任務從「重建」轉向「生成」，犧牲了作者想保留的重建導向目標。

第三種是逐音軌（stem-wise）符元化（DUO-TOK），把人聲音軌當作「語意」、伴奏當作「聲學」，靠音源分離把混音拆開——但音源分離本身會有損失，論文指出這會讓重建變差，符合把緊密耦合的音源硬拆開會造成不可逆資訊損失的預期。MuSeC 對這三種做法的回應，是直接從混音訊號中拆解出語意與聲學內容，不經過任何音源分離步驟，同時保留以重建為導向的訓練目標。

Reconstruction-focused neural codecs (SoundStream, EnCodec, DAC, and single-codebook variants like WavTokenizer and BigCodec) optimize perceptual fidelity through residual vector quantization, but none of them target LM-friendliness directly. On the speech side, semantic-guided codecs close that gap: SpeechTokenizer distills HuBERT features into the first RVQ level, Mimi distills WavLM into a dedicated semantic quantizer, and CosyVoice derives supervised tokens from an ASR encoder. These all assume that "semantics" means something like phonetic content, which the paper argues doesn't fit music.

For music specifically, the paper identifies three existing strategies, each with a concrete flaw. First, retraining a speech codec directly on music (YuE) inherits a semantic constraint built for phonemes, which "may not capture musical structure in complex mixtures." Second, a "quantize-then-generate" route (LeVo, HeartMuLa) pairs a music self-supervised model (MERT or MuQ) with a diffusion decoder, shifting the codec's job from reconstruction toward generation and sacrificing the reconstruction-oriented objective the authors want to keep.

Third, stem-wise tokenization (DUO-TOK) treats the vocal stem as "semantic" and the accompaniment as "acoustic," splitting the mixture via source separation -- but separation is lossy, and the paper reports this "degrades reconstruction, consistent with irreversible information loss incurred when separating tightly coupled sources." MuSeC's answer to all three is to factorize semantic and acoustic content directly from the mixed signal, without any separation step, while keeping a reconstruction-oriented training objective.

方法Method

MuSeC 第一步是用實驗量出「音樂語意內容」，而不是假設它是什麼。作者對三個音樂自監督骨幹模型（MERT、MuQ、LeVo 的 BEST-RQ）加上語音基準模型 WavLM 做逐層探測，在 MARBLE 基準的 MIR 任務上測每一層特徵：分類標籤、曲風、情緒、調性、節拍、和弦、歌詞辨識。結果發現調性、節拍、和弦這類「樂譜層級」屬性在較淺層就達到高峰，而分類標籤、曲風、情緒、歌詞這類「高層描述」則在中後段層才達到高峰；WavLM 在所有任務上都落後三個音樂專用模型。

這個分層結果就是操作型定義：淺層達峰的算「聲學內容」，深層達峰的算「語意內容」。語意骨幹選用 LeVo 的 BEST-RQ，因為它在分類標籤與歌詞辨識兩項任務都領先，而生成任務正好最在意這兩項能力。

架構上，語意編碼器是凍結的 SSL 模型（第 6 層）接 k-means 量化器，碼簿大小 2048，每 25Hz 一幀輸出一個語意符元。聲學端是因果卷積前端（960 倍降採樣至 25Hz）加滑動窗 Transformer，解碼器則對稱做因果卷積上採樣；RVQ 用 32 層殘差碼本（碼簿大小 1024，維度 1024），訓練時隨機截斷層數（9 到 32 層之間），推論時取前 16 層。每一幀最終輸出 1 個語意符元加 16 個聲學符元。

訓練分三階段：第一階段先在 5000 小時音樂上離線做 k-means，固定語意詞彙表；第二階段凍結語意符元，在 12 萬小時、24kHz 音訊上訓練聲學編碼器、RVQ 與解碼器——凍結語意這步很關鍵，作者說這會「迫使聲學 RVQ 只能編碼語意流沒有涵蓋到的資訊，在沒有明確解耦目標的情況下驅動語意與聲學內容自然分離」；第三階段凍結編碼器與碼本，只用 2 萬小時經篩選的高品質 48kHz 音訊重訓解碼器做超解析度，把固定的 25Hz 符元渲染到 48kHz。生成器損失結合多解析度 STFT、立體聲和差 STFT、RVQ 承諾損失、對抗損失與特徵匹配損失，所有權重都設為 1、沒有額外調參。

主結果（MTT 標籤 AP、和弦 AUC、歌詞辨識準確率、語言模型 top-1/5/10 準確率與困惑度、PESQ、STOI、梅爾頻譜 L1 距離）顯示：MuSeC（16 聲學層）在 PESQ 拿到 2.201，對照組 XCodec-YuE 只有 1.642；STOI 是 0.683 對 0.617；梅爾 L1 是 0.883 對 1.905，重建品質全面領先。語言模型可預測性方面 top-1 準確率 0.490（XCodec-YuE 是 0.498，接近打平但符元幀率只有對方一半），top-5 是 0.651（對方 0.783）。消融實驗顯示拿掉語意符元後困惑度從 1.694 飆升到 5.771、PESQ 從 2.201 掉到 1.540；拿掉聲學分支則和弦準確率從完整模型的 0.365 掉到只剩 k-means 單獨語意符元時的 0.171，證實兩條分支確實各自扛住了不同資訊。

MuSeC starts by empirically measuring "music semantic content" rather than assuming a definition. The authors run layer-wise probing on three music self-supervised backbones (MERT, MuQ, and LeVo's BEST-RQ) plus WavLM as a speech baseline, testing each layer on MARBLE MIR tasks: tagging, genre, emotion, key, beat, chord estimation, and lyrics transcription. Score-level attributes -- key, beat, chord -- peak in shallow layers, while high-level descriptors -- tagging, genre, emotion, lyrics -- peak in middle-to-deep layers, and WavLM trails all three music-specific models on every task.

That stratification becomes the operational definition: shallow-peaking attributes are "acoustic," deep-peaking ones are "semantic." LeVo's BEST-RQ is chosen as the semantic backbone because it leads on both tagging and lyrics transcription, the two capabilities generation cares about most.

Architecturally, the semantic encoder is a frozen SSL model (layer 6) followed by a k-means quantizer with 2048 clusters, emitting one semantic token per 25 Hz frame. The acoustic side is a causal convolutional front-end (960x downsampling to 25 Hz) plus a sliding-window Transformer, mirrored by a causal convolutional decoder. A 32-level residual codebook (size 1024, dimension 1024) is trained with random truncation depth between 9 and 32 levels, and inference keeps the first 16, giving one semantic plus 16 acoustic tokens per frame.

Training runs in three stages. Stage one fits k-means offline on 5,000 hours of music to fix the semantic vocabulary. Stage two freezes the semantic tokens and trains the acoustic encoder, RVQ and decoder on 120,000 hours of 24 kHz audio -- freezing semantics is the key trick, since it "forces the acoustic RVQ to encode only the information absent from the semantic stream, which drives the factorization of semantic and acoustic content without an explicit disentanglement objective." Stage three freezes everything upstream and retrains only the decoder on 20,000 hours of filtered high-quality 48 kHz audio for super-resolution, rendering the fixed 25 Hz tokens at 48 kHz. The generator loss combines multi-resolution STFT, stereo sum-difference STFT, RVQ commitment, adversarial and feature-matching terms, all weighted equally with no tuning.

On the main results table (MTT tagging AP, chord AUC, lyrics accuracy, LM top-1/5/10 accuracy and perplexity, PESQ, STOI, mel-L1), MuSeC with 16 acoustic levels reaches PESQ 2.201 versus 1.642 for the XCodec-YuE baseline, STOI 0.683 versus 0.617, and mel-L1 0.883 versus 1.905 -- reconstruction wins across the board. On LM-friendliness, top-1 accuracy is 0.490 against XCodec-YuE's 0.498 (nearly matched at half the token rate), while top-5 is 0.651 versus 0.783. Ablations show that removing the semantic tokens pushes perplexity from 1.694 to 5.771 and PESQ from 2.201 down to 1.540, while removing the acoustic branch drops chord accuracy from the full model's 0.365 down to 0.171 (the k-means-only semantic score), confirming each branch carries genuinely separate information.

限制Limitations

論文沒有寫獨立的限制章節，但幾個侷限藏在數字與敘述裡。首先，聲學分支雖然把和弦準確率從 0.171 拉高到 0.365，作者自己承認這「仍落後於連續表徵的頂線 0.542」，代表 k-means 離散化這一步本身就會丟資訊。其次，25Hz 的符元幀率是 XCodec-YuE（50Hz）的一半，序列長度減半有利語言模型，但 top-5 準確率也跟著從 0.783 掉到 0.651，這是明確的取捨而非單方面勝出。

評估範圍也有限：MARBLE 裡的演奏層級與聲學層級任務並未納入評估，只挑了樂譜層級與高層描述任務；歌詞測試更是因為 MARBLE 中英文涵蓋不足，作者自建了一個小型雙語測試集，樣本規模不明。整套方法也完全針對音樂設計，論文沒有測試過在語音資料上的表現，跨領域泛化能力未知。

The paper has no standalone limitations section, but several constraints surface in the numbers. First, while the acoustic branch lifts chord accuracy from 0.171 to 0.365, the authors admit it "still trails the continuous topline (0.542)," meaning the k-means discretization step itself loses information. Second, the 25 Hz token rate is half of XCodec-YuE's 50 Hz -- shorter sequences help the LM, but top-5 accuracy also drops from 0.783 to 0.651, a genuine trade-off rather than a one-sided win.

Evaluation scope is also limited: MARBLE's performance-level and acoustic-level tasks are excluded, leaving only score-level and high-level description tasks. The lyrics evaluation relies on a small, self-constructed bilingual test set built because MARBLE's Chinese/English coverage was insufficient, and its exact size isn't given. The whole method is music-specific, with no reported test on speech data, so cross-domain generalization is untested.

討論Discussion

論文的主張是：把「音樂語意」從一個假設變成一個可測量的定義，再用這個定義去設計編解碼器，能讓重建保真度與語言模型可預測性同時提升，而不是像過去那樣二選一。從消融實驗看，語意與聲學兩條分支確實各司其職——拿掉任何一邊都會讓對應的能力大幅下降，而完整模型在兩邊都逼近各自的單獨最佳值，作者稱之為「乾淨的雙重解離」。

比較有意思的判斷題在於：這篇論文的「首創」到底新在哪。從方法論看，逐層探測音樂 SSL 模型再依探測結果分派任務，其實是把語音端「用探測找語意層」的做法（如 SUPERB 系列的分析套路）搬到音樂上，並不是全新發明；真正的貢獻是把這套探測結果轉成編解碼器設計的具體依據，並且證明對混音直接做語意／聲學分流，效果優於既有的音軌分離式作法。換句話說，這篇論文的創新比較像是「把既有分析工具用在對的問題上」，而不是提出一個全新演算法。

The paper's claim is that turning "music semantics" from an assumption into a measured definition, then designing the codec around that definition, lets reconstruction fidelity and LM-predictability improve together instead of trading off, as prior designs forced. The ablations back this up: removing either branch tanks the matching capability, while the full model approaches each branch's standalone best on both axes -- what the authors call a "clean double dissociation."

The more interesting judgment call is what's actually novel here. Methodologically, layer-wise probing of music SSL models to locate where semantics live is really an import of a technique already common in speech representation analysis (in the spirit of SUPERB-style probing studies), not a new idea by itself. The real contribution is turning that probing result into a concrete codec design choice, and showing that splitting semantic from acoustic content directly on the mixed signal beats the existing stem-separation approach. Put differently, the innovation here reads more like "applying an existing analysis tool to the right problem" than inventing a new algorithm from scratch.

Confidence-Guided Markov Weighting for Semi-Supervised Tabla Stroke Transcription

信心引導馬可夫加權：半監督塔布拉鼓點轉譜

Rahul Bapusaheb Kodag, Vipul Arora
arXiv:2609.21610 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-09-21
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把 CTC 對偽標籤「照單全收」的假設換掉：對每個不確定的鼓點用學到的鼓點轉移機率去加權猜測範圍，而不是像前作 ATC-R 那樣把不確定位置一視同仁地當成萬用字元。Why this one: Replaces CTC's take-the-pseudo-label-at-face-value assumption: it weights guesses at uncertain strokes using a learned stroke-transition model, instead of treating every uncertain position as an equally-likely wildcard the way prior work (ATC-R) does.

動機Motivation

塔布拉鼓點轉譜（TST）要把塔布拉鼓的錄音轉成鼓點記號序列，但標註好的錄音很稀少，全監督訓練很難做。半監督學習的做法是讓老師模型幫沒標註的錄音生成偽標籤，但標準 CTC 損失函數假設「拿到的目標序列是對的」——當老師模型的偽標籤本身有錯，這個錯誤就會直接傳給學生模型。

前作 ATC-R 已經知道在不確定的位置放寬限制、允許多種候選鼓點，但論文指出它「沒有利用鼓點標籤之間的序列關聯性」——也就是說，猜某個不確定鼓點時，完全沒參考前後鼓點通常會怎麼接續。這篇論文要補的正是這一塊：用學到的鼓點轉移機率，把「猜測」變成有依據的加權猜測。

Tabla Stroke Transcription (TST) converts tabla recordings into symbolic stroke sequences, but annotated recordings are scarce, making fully supervised training hard. The semi-supervised fix has a teacher model generate pseudo-labels for unlabelled recordings -- but standard CTC loss assumes "the supplied target sequence is correct," so any error in the teacher's pseudo-label propagates straight into the student.

Prior work (ATC-R) already relaxes uncertain positions by allowing multiple candidate strokes, but the paper points out it "does not exploit the sequential relationships among stroke labels" -- guessing an uncertain stroke ignores what strokes typically come before and after it. This paper's contribution is exactly that gap: turning the guess into a weighted guess grounded in a learned stroke-transition model.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

塔布拉轉譜本身的既有系統包括：早期以起始點分割再分類的 Segment-and-Classify (S&C)；OTM，借用西方鼓組的轉移學習，但需要逐一標出鼓點起始時間；一套後續的元學習方法改善了資料效率，但仍需要時間軸標註；ADRM 首次做到不需要起始時間標註的序列級轉譜，但仍要序列級的鼓點標註；另有一套以音色做無監督分群的方法，橫跨塔布拉與姆里單伽鼓，但分群結果事後還要對回實際標籤。這篇論文的位置是：不需要起始時間標註，而且首次把半監督學習（利用未標註錄音）帶進這個任務。

在更廣的「圖式 CTC 擴展」脈絡下，W-CTC 引入萬用字元轉移處理部分標籤，STC 允許任意位置缺標籤，BTC 用旁通轉移處理不完美轉錄，OTC 把萬用字元和旁通機制合而為一，GTC 把 CTC 廣義化成接受加權圖形式的多重候選監督。ATC／ATC-R 用偽標籤的信心值找出不確定的符元，ATC-R 會把低信心的符元換成「接受任何非空白標籤」的萬用字元。這篇論文的 CMW-ATC 在此基礎上，把「信心引導」與「馬可夫橋接」結合，用學到的鼓點轉移機率與兩側可靠鄰居的資訊去加權候選序列，這正是 ATC-R 缺少的序列關聯性。

信心估計方面，既有的 CEM 框架用模型中間表徵訓練輔助信心模型；TeLeS 結合時間與詞彙相似度但需要時間對齊；TruCLeS 用真實類別機率加詞彙相似度建構連續目標，省去了時間對齊需求。但鼓點是原子類別，詞彙相似度在這裡沒有意義，所以這篇論文改為在序列級監督下，用二元的「對／錯」目標訓練專屬的鼓點級信心估計模型（S-CEM）。

Prior tabla-specific systems include Segment-and-Classify (S&C), an early onset-segment-then-classify pipeline; OTM, which transfers learning from Western drum kits but still needs onset-level annotation; a later meta-learning approach that improves data efficiency but still needs temporal annotation; ADRM, the first to reach sequence-level TST without onset timings, though it still needs sequence-level stroke labels; and a timbre-based unsupervised clustering method spanning tabla and mridangam, which still needs its clusters mapped back to labels afterward. This paper's position is: no onset annotation needed, and it is the first to bring semi-supervised learning (using unlabelled recordings) into this task.

In the broader "graph-based CTC extension" lineage, W-CTC introduces wildcard transitions for partial labels, STC allows missing labels at arbitrary positions, BTC adds bypass transitions for imperfect transcripts, OTC merges the wildcard and bypass mechanisms, and GTC generalizes CTC to accept weighted-graph supervision with multiple candidate sequences. ATC/ATC-R uses pseudo-label confidence to flag uncertain tokens, with ATC-R replacing low-confidence tokens with a wildcard that accepts any non-blank label. This paper's CMW-ATC builds on that by combining confidence guidance with Markov-bridge conditioning -- weighting candidate sequences using learned stroke transitions and reliable neighbouring strokes on both sides, exactly the sequential relationship ATC-R lacks.

On confidence estimation, prior CEM frameworks train an auxiliary confidence model from a model's internal representations; TeLeS combines temporal and lexical similarity but needs temporal alignment; TruCLeS builds continuous targets from true-class probability plus lexical similarity, dropping the alignment requirement. But tabla strokes are atomic classes where lexical similarity is meaningless, so this paper instead trains a dedicated Stroke-Level Confidence Estimation Model (S-CEM) with binary correct/incorrect targets under sequence-level supervision.

方法Method

整體架構有三塊：在標註資料上訓練好、之後凍結的老師模型；鼓點級信心估計模型 S-CEM；學生模型（標註資料用一般 CTC 訓練，未標註資料用新提出的 CMW-ATC 訓練）。特徵是 128 維對數梅爾頻譜（44.1kHz，2048 點窗，10ms 位移），模型骨幹是 12 層帶瓶頸投影、時間卷積、殘差連接的 C-TDNN-F。

S-CEM 的訓練方式：把凍結的老師模型套用到標註錄音上得到預測序列，用鼓點層級的 Levenshtein 對齊比對預測與參考答案，每個預測鼓點得到二元正確與否的標籤（對到同一個參考鼓點記 1，否則記 0），再對每個鼓點取「CTC 層前的表徵」與「CTC 後驗機率向量」的時間平均值拼接成特徵向量，餵給三層全連接網路（512、256、128 個神經元，ReLU，最後 sigmoid），用二元交叉熵訓練，輸出連續的信心分數。

CMW-ATC 的核心是：先用標註序列估計一階馬可夫鼓點轉移機率（加一平滑），再用信心門檻 τ 把偽標籤序列切成「可靠」與「不可靠」兩類位置，連續的不可靠位置合併成「不確定範圍」。針對每個不確定範圍，依照左右是否有可靠邊界分成四種情況，各自用馬可夫橋接分布計算候選序列的機率權重——有左右邊界時用完整的橋接式分布，只有一側邊界或完全沒有邊界時用對應簡化式。不確定範圍內的每個候選序列都算出一個標準化權重，最終損失是這些加權候選序列與學生模型 CTC 機率的乘積後取負對數期望；標註資料則照樣用一般 CTC 訓練，兩者加權相加成總損失。

訓練用 Adam，學習率 1e-3，老師、學生與 S-CEM 都跑 100 個 epoch；信心門檻 τ 與損失權重 λ 在驗證集上網格搜尋（τ∈{0.4,...,0.9}，λ∈{0.1,...,1.0}），TID+TSD 與 TID-S 選到 (τ,λ)=(0.6,0.5)，HMR 選到 (0.5,0.4)；解碼用集束搜尋，集束寬 120。未標註資料來自 YouTube，包含 676 分鐘塔布拉獨奏與 602 分鐘印度斯坦音樂會錄音。

主要結果（鼓點錯誤率 SER，數字越低越好）：CMW-ATC 在 TID+TSD 拿到 18.3%，相較純 CTC 基準的 25.1% 降低 27.1%，相較 ATC-R 的 22.9% 再降低 20.1%；在 HMR（45 位演奏者、含人聲與伴奏、跨四種塔拉節奏循環，難度最高）拿到 33.3%，相較 CTC 的 43.8% 降低 24.0%，相較 ATC-R 的 39.6% 降低 15.9%；在 TID-S 拿到 12.7%，相較 CTC 的 17.8% 降低 28.7%，相較 ATC-R 的 16.0% 降低 20.6%。

消融實驗把「用 S-CEM 還是 CTC 信心值」與「用馬可夫加權還是均勻/萬用字元加權」拆開測試：光是把 ATC-R 的 CTC 信心換成 S-CEM（仍用萬用字元加權）就能讓三個資料集的 SER 各降 4.5% 到 7.5%；只做雙向馬可夫加權（不換信心來源）比只做單向前向馬可夫再降 7.2% 到 9.0%；完整版 CMW-ATC 同時換信心來源與加權方式，相較「只換信心來源、加權方式不變」的版本再降 9.8% 到 11.6%。錯誤類型分解顯示，以 TID+TSD 為例，CMW-ATC 把插入錯誤從 CTC 基準的 5.4 個降到 3.3 個（降低 38.9%），但替代錯誤仍是最大宗，占整體錯誤的六成以上。

The pipeline has three parts: a teacher model trained on labelled data then frozen, a Stroke-Level Confidence Estimation Model (S-CEM), and a student model trained with standard CTC on labelled data and the new CMW-ATC loss on unlabelled data. Features are 128-dim log-mel spectrograms (44.1 kHz, 2048-sample window, 10 ms shift); the backbone is a 12-layer C-TDNN-F with bottleneck projections, temporal convolution and residual connections.

S-CEM is trained by running the frozen teacher on labelled recordings, aligning predictions to references with stroke-level Levenshtein alignment to get a binary correct/incorrect label per predicted stroke, then averaging the pre-CTC representation and CTC posterior over each stroke's frames into a feature vector fed to a three-layer fully-connected network (512, 256, 128 units, ReLU, sigmoid output), trained with binary cross-entropy to output a continuous confidence score.

CMW-ATC's core idea: estimate a first-order Markov stroke-transition model from labelled sequences (with add-one smoothing), then use a confidence threshold tau to split each pseudo-label sequence into reliable and unreliable positions, merging consecutive unreliable positions into "uncertain spans." Each span gets one of four treatments depending on whether it has a reliable boundary on the left, right, both, or neither, each computing candidate-sequence weights from a Markov-bridge distribution (the full bridge form when both boundaries exist, simplified forms otherwise). Every candidate sequence within a span gets a normalized weight, and the final loss is the negative log of the expectation of the student's CTC probability under these weighted candidates; labelled data still uses plain CTC, and the two losses are summed.

Training uses Adam at learning rate 1e-3 for 100 epochs each for teacher, student and S-CEM; the confidence threshold tau and loss weight lambda are grid-searched on validation data (tau in {0.4,...,0.9}, lambda in {0.1,...,1.0}), settling on (tau, lambda) = (0.6, 0.5) for TID+TSD and TID-S, (0.5, 0.4) for HMR; decoding uses beam search with beam width 120. Unlabelled data comes from YouTube: 676 minutes of tabla solos plus 602 minutes of Hindustani concert recordings.

Main results (Stroke Error Rate, lower is better): CMW-ATC reaches 18.3% on TID+TSD, a 27.1% reduction from the plain-CTC baseline's 25.1% and a further 20.1% reduction from ATC-R's 22.9%. On HMR (45 performers, vocal and instrumental accompaniment across four talas, the hardest setting) it reaches 33.3%, down 24.0% from CTC's 43.8% and 15.9% from ATC-R's 39.6%. On TID-S it reaches 12.7%, down 28.7% from CTC's 17.8% and 20.6% from ATC-R's 16.0%. Ablations isolate the two ingredients: swapping ATC-R's CTC confidence for S-CEM alone (keeping wildcard weighting) already cuts SER by 4.5-7.5% across the three datasets; switching from forward-only to bidirectional Markov weighting (confidence source unchanged) cuts a further 7.2-9.0%; the full CMW-ATC, changing both confidence source and weighting together, beats the confidence-only change by 9.8-11.6%. Error-type breakdown on TID+TSD shows CMW-ATC cutting insertion errors from CTC's 5.4 down to 3.3 (a 38.9% reduction), though substitution errors remain the largest error category at over 60% of the total.

限制Limitations

論文自己承認：替代（substitution）錯誤在所有資料集上都仍是殘餘錯誤的主要來源，方法對這類錯誤的改善相對有限。候選序列建構本身「保留偽標籤位置的數量，不會主動新增或刪除鼓點位置」，換句話說插入、刪除錯誤的減少是學生模型訓練的間接效果，而不是這個方法針對性解決的問題——如果偽標籤的長度本身就對不上，CMW-ATC 無法修正。

難度最高的 HMR 資料集（45 位演奏者、含人聲與伴奏、跨四種塔拉）錯誤率仍明顯高於其他兩個資料集，作者把這歸因於更大的聲學與演奏者變異性，但沒有進一步拆解是哪一種變異性影響最大。整套方法也只在塔布拉這一種樂器上驗證，儘管相關研究一節提到姆里單伽鼓的分群方法，論文並未把 CMW-ATC 實際套用到塔布拉以外的打擊樂器上。

The authors admit substitution errors remain the dominant residual error category across all datasets, and the method's improvement there is comparatively limited. Candidate construction itself "preserves the number of pseudo-label positions and does not explicitly add or remove stroke positions" -- meaning any reduction in insertion/deletion errors is an indirect side effect of student training rather than something CMW-ATC directly targets, and it cannot fix cases where the pseudo-label's length is simply wrong.

The hardest dataset, HMR (45 performers, vocal and instrumental accompaniment, four talas), still shows a clearly higher error rate than the other two, which the authors attribute to greater acoustic and performer variability without further breaking down which factor dominates. The whole method is validated only on tabla; although the related-work section mentions a mridangam clustering method, CMW-ATC itself is never actually applied to a percussion instrument beyond tabla.

討論Discussion

論文的結論句直接把三個資料集的數字擺出來：CMW-ATC 在 TID+TSD、HMR、TID-S 分別拿到 18.3%、33.3%、12.7% 的鼓點錯誤率，相較純 CTC 基準降低 24.0% 到 28.7%，相較 ATC-R 降低 15.9% 到 20.6%——這是這篇論文最直接可驗證的主張，而且有明確數字支撐，不是空泛的「效果顯著」。

值得注意的是，這篇論文宣稱的「首創」其實是組合式的：馬可夫橋接分布本身不是新東西，論文自己也承認「這個原理過去沒有被用在語音或音樂轉譜的不確定性感知偽標籤監督上」——也就是說，新意主要在於「把馬可夫橋接接到 ATC 這個既有框架裡」這個組合動作，而不是發明了全新的機率模型。同一批作者也把信心來源從既有的 CTC 後驗換成專門訓練的 S-CEM，這一步的貢獻同樣是「換掉一個既有子模組」而非架構突破。整體來說，這是一篇把兩個獨立技巧（信心估計、序列先驗）紮實組合進既有框架、並用完整消融證明兩者都有貢獻的工程型論文。

The paper's closing line puts the numbers directly on the table: CMW-ATC reaches stroke error rates of 18.3%, 33.3% and 12.7% on TID+TSD, HMR and TID-S respectively, a 24.0-28.7% reduction from the plain-CTC baseline and a 15.9-20.6% reduction from ATC-R -- a directly checkable, numerically-backed claim rather than a vague "significant improvement."

It's worth noting that what this paper calls new is really a combination: the Markov-bridge distribution itself isn't a new object, and the authors themselves admit "this principle has not been used for uncertainty-aware pseudo-label supervision in speech or music transcription" before -- meaning the novelty is mainly in wiring a Markov bridge into the existing ATC framework, not in inventing a new probabilistic model from scratch. The same authors also swap the confidence source from off-the-shelf CTC posteriors to a purpose-trained S-CEM, which is likewise a swapped sub-module rather than an architectural breakthrough. Overall, this reads as an engineering-style paper that solidly combines two separable techniques -- confidence estimation and a sequence prior -- into an existing framework, and backs both contributions with a full ablation.

Training Music Sample Identification Models on Real Sample Pairs

以真實取樣配對訓練音樂取樣識別模型

R. Oguz Araz, Joan Serrà, Xavier Lizarraga-Seijas, Emilio Molina, Xavier Serra, Yuki Mitsufuji, Dmitry Bogdanov
arXiv:2609.21911 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-21
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：有了 WhoSampled130K 這個真實取樣配對資料集之後，同一套訓練食譜換成用真實配對訓練，效果比原本靠合成配對訓練的最強系統多出一大截，而且架構與訓練法本身的貢獻比資料來源更大。Why this one: With the WhoSampled130K real-pair dataset now available, training the same recipe on real pairs instead of synthetic ones beats the previous strongest synthetic-pair system by a wide margin -- and the architecture/recipe itself contributes more than the data source alone.

動機Motivation

取樣識別（sample identification）要判斷兩首曲子中，其中一首是不是把另一首的某個片段拿來做音樂性轉換後製作出來的（例如挖出一段鼓點或和弦重新編排）。這個領域長期缺乏大規模標註資料，研究者只能人工合成取樣配對來訓練模型。

問題是合成配對「無法完整模擬音樂製作人實際做取樣時使用的複雜轉換手法，例如刮盤（scratching）和切片（chopping）」。這篇論文能做起來，關鍵是有了 WhoSampled130K 這個新資料集，裡面有 114,721 首曲目之間的 79,111 組真實標註取樣配對。論文的核心主張很直接：就算資料量有限，用真實配對訓練也大幅勝過用近乎無限的合成配對訓練。

Sample identification (SI) is the task of telling whether one track was created by musically transforming a segment of another -- pulling out a drum break or chord progression and reworking it. The field has long lacked large-scale annotated data, forcing researchers to synthesize sample pairs artificially instead.

The problem is that artificial pairs "cannot fully imitate the complex transformations music producers use in sampling, e.g., scratching and chopping." What makes this paper possible is the new WhoSampled130K dataset, containing 79,111 annotated real sample pairs across 114,721 tracks. The paper's central thesis is direct: even with limited data, training on real pairs substantially outperforms training on effectively unlimited synthetic pairs.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

合成配對生成路線的代表作包括：Van Balen 等人（2013）用規則式的頻譜峰值指紋比對，在 Sample100 上拿到 mAP 0.390；Cheston 等人（2025）改用音源分離產生人工配對，配上 ResNet50-IBN 加 GeM 池化，mAP 提升到 0.441；SampleID（Riou 等人，2026）是目前的最強基準，用天生多音軌的資料集即時生成配對，搭配 NT-Xent 損失，在 Sample100 拿到 mAP 0.603、在 SamplePairs 拿到 0.450。

相關任務的系統也提供了對照：NMFP 是用音訊劣化鏈訓練的曲目識別模型；CLEWS 是版本識別系統，靠訊號操弄達成對速度、調性的不變性；Fish 則同時處理曲目識別與版本識別，對複合式的操弄與劣化都有一定強健性——這三個系統在後面的大規模測試裡都被直接拿來對比。

這篇論文的關鍵差異在於：它是「第一個針對真實世界取樣配對、完全監督式的訓練食譜」。過去的做法都仰賴合成資料做增強，這篇論文改用真正的商業歌曲取樣配對；同時它也是第一篇直接比較 triplet 損失與 NT-Xent 損失在取樣識別任務上表現的論文。

The artificial-pair lineage includes Van Balen et al. (2013), a rule-based spectral-peak fingerprinting approach reaching mAP 0.390 on Sample100; Cheston et al. (2025), which uses source separation to generate artificial pairs with a ResNet50-IBN plus GeM-pooling backbone, reaching mAP 0.441; and SampleID (Riou et al., 2026), the current strongest baseline, which generates pairs on-the-fly from a natively multi-stem dataset with NT-Xent loss, reaching mAP 0.603 on Sample100 and 0.450 on SamplePairs.

Related-task systems provide additional reference points: NMFP is a track-identification model trained with audio degradation chains; CLEWS is a version-identification system using signal manipulation for tempo/key invariance; Fish handles both track and version identification with some robustness to combined manipulation and degradation -- all three are directly compared against in the paper's large-scale benchmark.

This paper's key difference is being "the first fully supervised training recipe for real-world sample identification." Prior work relied on synthetic augmentation; this one trains on actual commercial-track sample pairs instead. It is also the first paper to directly compare triplet versus NT-Xent losses for the sample-identification task specifically.

方法Method

訓練資料處理流程：從 WhoSampled130K 取出真實取樣配對（來源與目的曲目各自標有精確到 1 秒的時間戳），先把每個時間戳獨立加上 [-2.5, 2.5] 秒範圍內均勻抽樣的隨機偏移，再各取 10 秒音訊片段，音量正規化到 [-30, 15] dBFS 範圍內均勻抽樣的目標峰值（正值會刻意造成削波）。

特徵是 258 個頻帶的變 Q 轉換（起始頻率 27.5Hz，每八度 36 個頻帶，頻寬參數 γ=5），對應 6 秒音訊的 230 個時間幀（幀率 40Hz），再做動態範圍壓縮、加性雜訊與逐項最小-最大縮放前處理。前端沿用 SampleID 針對變 Q 轉換設計的架構，後端是 ResNet50-IBN 加 GeM 池化，接投影頭（批次正規化、線性層、L2 正規化），輸出 1024 維嵌入向量。

訓練時對變 Q 轉換做「訊號操弄鏈」：裁切 258 個連續頻帶做音高移調、以 [0.6, 1.67] 倍率做時間伸縮後裁回 230 幀、遮蔽最多涵蓋 15% 頻帶與 15% 時間幀的十字型區域，每個操弄各自以 0.25 的機率獨立套用；另外對波形本身做「音訊劣化鏈」：混入 [0, 12] dB 訊噪比的背景噪音、加房間殘響、模擬麥克風響應，中間穿插 [-9, 0] dB 的隨機增益。損失函數是歐氏距離下的 triplet 損失，margin 設 0.3，用批次內最難負例；優化器是不加權重衰減的 Adam，學習率從 3e-4 以餘弦排程退火到 1e-6，共 2 萬次迭代，394 對真實取樣配對成一批，在一張 NVIDIA L40S GPU 上以 FP16 混合精度訓練 25 小時。

論文另外訓練了一個對照組 SampleID-R：直接複製 SampleID 的架構與訓練流程，但改用真實配對（而非合成配對）訓練，且因為真實配對本身已經含有這些變化，訊號操弄機率同樣調整為每項各 0.25，訓練到收斂用了 3.8 萬次迭代（SIE 只需要 2 萬次）。

小型基準測試（Sample100、SamplePairs）結果：SIE 在 Sample100 拿到 mAP 0.758，相較 SampleID-R 的 0.651 與 SampleID 的 0.603 都有明顯提升；在 SamplePairs 拿到 mAP 0.701，相較 SampleID-R 的 0.511 與 SampleID 的 0.450 更是大幅拉開差距，相當於比 SampleID 分別高出 26% 與 56% 的相對 mAP。

在上萬筆查詢的 WhoSampled130K 大型測試集（10,444 筆查詢、8,463 組取樣配對）上，SIE 拿到 mAP 0.389±0.009，SampleID-R 是 0.299±0.008，SampleID 是 0.226±0.007；相較之下，改做曲目識別的 NMFP 只有 0.101±0.005，版本識別的 CLEWS 是 0.181±0.007，同時處理兩種任務的 Fish 是 0.207±0.007——確認取樣識別與這兩個相鄰任務並不能直接互通。

消融實驗顯示：只用「訊號操弄鏈」不用「音訊劣化鏈」時 mAP 是 0.365±0.009，反過來只用劣化鏈是 0.334±0.008，兩者都用時最高、達 0.389±0.009，顯示操弄鏈（音高與時間的不變性）比劣化鏈（訊噪比與殘響的不變性）貢獻更大。損失函數比較上，triplet 損失（0.389±0.009）比表現最好的 NT-Xent（溫度 0.01 時 0.332±0.008）高出 0.057 mAP，遠超過兩者信賴區間的寬度——這和曲目／版本識別文獻裡「NT-Xent 通常更好」的既有結論相反，是這篇論文第一次針對取樣識別任務做的直接對照。片段長度測試（5、6、8 秒）則顯示三者差異都在信賴區間寬度以內，沒有實質差別。

Training data pipeline: real sample pairs from WhoSampled130K (source and destination tracks each carry timestamps accurate to 1 second) get each timestamp independently perturbed by an offset drawn uniformly from [-2.5, 2.5] s, then 10 s of audio is extracted around each and peak-normalized to a target drawn uniformly from [-30, 15] dBFS (positive targets deliberately induce clipping).

Features are a 258-bin variable-Q transform (starting at 27.5 Hz, 36 bins per octave, bandwidth parameter gamma=5), giving 230 frames at 40 Hz for a 6 s segment, followed by dynamic-range compression, additive noise, and per-item min-max scaling. The front-end reuses SampleID's variable-Q-tailored design; the back-end is a ResNet50-IBN with GeM pooling followed by a projection head (batch norm, linear layer, L2 normalization) producing 1024-dimensional embeddings.

Training applies a "signal manipulation" chain to the variable-Q transform -- pitch transposition by cropping to 258 consecutive bins, time-stretching by a factor in [0.6, 1.67] then cropping back to 230 frames, and spectral masking of a cross-shaped region covering up to 15% of bins and 15% of frames, each applied independently with probability 0.25 -- plus an "audio degradation" chain on the waveform itself: background noise at [0, 12] dB SNR, room reverberation, and simulated microphone response, interleaved with random gains in [-9, 0] dB. The loss is triplet loss over Euclidean distances with margin 0.3, using the hardest in-batch negative; the optimizer is Adam without weight decay, learning rate annealed from 3e-4 to 1e-6 by cosine schedule over 20k iterations, batches of 394 real pairs, trained for 25 hours on one NVIDIA L40S GPU with FP16 mixed precision.

The paper also trains a controlled baseline, SampleID-R: SampleID's exact architecture and recipe, but trained on real pairs instead of synthetic ones, with manipulation probabilities kept at 0.25 each (since real pairs already contain this variation), trained to convergence at 38k iterations versus SIE's 20k.

On the small benchmarks (Sample100, SamplePairs): SIE reaches mAP 0.758 on Sample100, clearly ahead of SampleID-R's 0.651 and SampleID's 0.603; on SamplePairs it reaches mAP 0.701, pulling even further ahead of SampleID-R's 0.511 and SampleID's 0.450 -- a 26% and 56% relative mAP gain over SampleID respectively. On the large WhoSampled130K test set (10,444 queries, 8,463 sample pairs), SIE reaches mAP 0.389+/-0.009, SampleID-R 0.299+/-0.008, and SampleID 0.226+/-0.007. By contrast, the track-identification system NMFP scores only 0.101+/-0.005, the version-identification system CLEWS scores 0.181+/-0.007, and Fish (both tasks) scores 0.207+/-0.007 -- confirming sample identification doesn't transfer cleanly from either neighboring task.

Ablations show: signal manipulation alone (no degradation chain) gives mAP 0.365+/-0.009, degradation alone gives 0.334+/-0.008, and combining both gives the best 0.389+/-0.009, indicating manipulation invariance (pitch, time) contributes more than degradation invariance (SNR, reverb). On loss functions, triplet loss (0.389+/-0.009) beats the best NT-Xent setting (temperature 0.01, 0.332+/-0.008) by 0.057 mAP, well beyond either confidence interval's half-width -- contradicting the track/version-identification literature's usual finding that NT-Xent wins, and this is the first direct comparison of the two for sample identification specifically. Segment-duration tests (5, 6, 8 s) show no difference beyond the confidence-interval widths.

限制Limitations

論文自己指出，訓練資料量增加時效果提升會遞減但尚未飽和，代表模型理論上還能吃下比現有資料集更多的真實配對資料，只是目前拿不到更多。

小型基準（Sample100 只有 75 筆查詢，SamplePairs 只有 100 筆）樣本數太少，論文只能報告點估計而非信賴區間，數字的統計穩定性不如大型測試集。評估範圍也排除了 Bhattacharjee 等人的兩階段系統，以及 Van Balen、Cheston 等早期方法——後兩者是因為沒有可用的實作，並非效果不如人。

論文也留了一個開放問題：既然只用 5% 的真實資料就能追上用近乎無限合成資料訓練的 SampleID，那麼如果未來的合成配對生成流程做得更好，這個差距會不會繼續存在，論文承認「這些配對訓練出的模型能否在真實配對上大規模遷移，仍是一個開放問題」。

The authors note that performance gains diminish as training-data volume increases but do not saturate, meaning the model could in principle use more real training data than the current dataset provides.

The small benchmarks (Sample100 has 75 queries, SamplePairs 100) are too small for confidence intervals, so the paper reports point estimates there, which are statistically less stable than the large-scale test set's numbers. The evaluation also excludes Bhattacharjee et al.'s two-stage system and the earlier Van Balen and Cheston methods -- the latter two because no usable implementation exists, not because they underperform.

The paper leaves one question explicitly open: since just 5% of the real data already matches SampleID trained on effectively unlimited synthetic pairs, it's unclear whether that gap would persist if future synthetic-pair pipelines improve. The authors acknowledge "whether models trained on such pairs would transfer to real pairs at scale remains an open question."

討論Discussion

論文把貢獻拆成兩層來看：第一層是資料來源的差異——SampleID-R（用真實配對）比 SampleID（用合成配對）在 Sample100 高出 0.048 mAP、在 SamplePairs 高出 0.061 mAP，證實真實配對確實比合成配對更有效；第二層是架構與訓練食譜本身的差異——SIE 與 SampleID-R 之間的差距（Sample100 上 0.107 mAP）反而大於 SampleID-R 與 SampleID之間的差距（0.048 mAP）。換句話說，這篇論文真正想強調的是：資料來源只是故事的一半，怎麼訓練（架構、損失函數、資料增強策略）貢獻的效果比換資料來源還大。

這篇論文宣稱的「首創」主要有兩點：一是第一個針對真實取樣配對的完全監督訓練食譜，二是第一次直接比較 triplet 與 NT-Xent 損失在這個任務上的表現，並得出和曲目／版本識別文獻相反的結論（triplet 更好）。兩者都不是全新的演算法或架構，而是「拿既有的做法（SampleID 的架構、triplet 損失）套用到一個以前資料不足以支撐的設定」，新意在於資料與嚴謹的對照實驗設計，而不是模型本身。

The paper splits its contribution into two layers: the data-source difference -- SampleID-R (real pairs) beats SampleID (synthetic pairs) by 0.048 mAP on Sample100 and 0.061 on SamplePairs, confirming real pairs are genuinely more effective -- and the architecture/recipe difference, where the gap between SIE and SampleID-R (0.107 mAP on Sample100) is actually larger than the gap between SampleID-R and SampleID (0.048 mAP). In other words, the paper's real point is that data source is only half the story: how you train (architecture, loss function, augmentation strategy) contributes more than switching the data source alone.

What this paper calls new comes down to two things: the first fully supervised training recipe for real sample pairs, and the first direct comparison of triplet versus NT-Xent loss for this task, landing on the opposite conclusion from the track/version-identification literature (triplet wins). Neither is a new algorithm or architecture -- both are existing techniques (SampleID's architecture, triplet loss) applied to a setting that previously lacked enough data to support them. The novelty is in the data and the rigor of the controlled comparison, not in the model itself.
