Beyond Frequency: Dissonance Spectrum for Perceptually Motivated Music Understanding

超越頻率:為感知動機的音樂理解而生的不協和頻譜

Tianle Wang, Xinyi Tong, Liangke Zhao, Jishang Chen, Sirui Zhang, Haoxin Zhang, Xin Jin, Duo Xu, Xiaobing Li, Song-Chun Zhu
arXiv:2608.25621 · cs.SD, cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-08-26
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文提出「不協和頻譜」(Dissonance Spectrum, DS):把 Stolzenburg 的容忍度有理數近似與 Tenney 的對數諧波距離組合成一個核函數,套用在 CQT 上,把每一對頻率的不協和關係加權後歸屬回原本的時頻位置,而不是像傳統做法只給一個整體分數。作為即插即用模組接進 MU-LLaMA 做音樂問答、接進 MERT 做情緒辨識,六個種子平均下來,DS 分支在 MusicQA 的 BERTScore-R 上比原始基線高 0.0072,在情緒辨識的平均 R²(價性╱激發度)上高 0.0116,兩邊都贏過同參數量的高斯雜訊對照組與架構相同但沒有不協和加權的純 CQT 對照組。但作者自己也承認,這不是完整的協和感知模型,忽略了聽覺遮蔽、音色與文化學習偏好等因素。Why this one: This paper introduces the Dissonance Spectrum (DS): combining Stolzenburg's tolerance-based rational approximation with Tenney's logarithmic harmonic distance into a kernel applied over a CQT, attributing each pair of frequencies' dissonance relation back to its original time-frequency location rather than collapsing everything into one scalar score. Plugged in as a module into MU-LLaMA for music QA and into MERT for emotion recognition, averaged across six seeds, the DS branch beats the baseline by 0.0072 BERTScore-R on MusicQA and by 0.0116 mean R2 (valence/arousal) on emotion regression, outperforming both a parameter-matched Gaussian-noise control and an architecture-matched plain-CQT branch. The authors are explicit that this is not a complete consonance model -- it ignores auditory masking, timbre, and culturally learned preference.

動機Motivation

傳統的音樂特徵表示——頻譜圖、CQT、學到的嵌入——描述的是「時間與頻率上有多少能量」,卻沒有直接表示音符與音符之間「協和或不協和」的關係。但聽眾與音樂家對音樂張力、和聲穩定度的感受,恰恰建立在這種頻率之間的相對關係上,而不是單一頻率本身的能量高低。這篇論文要解決的問題,就是現有的音樂表示法完全沒有把這層關係顯式地編碼進去,下游任務(問答、情緒辨識)只能靠模型自己隱式地學,學不學得到、學到什麼都無從檢視。

Standard music representations -- spectrograms, CQTs, learned embeddings -- describe how much energy sits at each time-frequency location, but say nothing directly about the consonance or dissonance relationship between simultaneous pitches. Yet listeners' and musicians' sense of harmonic tension and stability is built on exactly those pairwise relationships between frequencies, not on the energy of any single frequency in isolation. The problem this paper targets is that no existing representation encodes that relational layer explicitly, leaving downstream tasks like music QA or emotion recognition to learn it implicitly, if at all, with no way to inspect what was learned.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂表示法這邊,論文點名 CQT、色度特徵(chroma)與調性空間描述子雖然常用,但都「沒辦法穩定、可直接檢視地表達局部的協和或不協和」;神經模型如 MERT、MusicFM、MuLan、CLAP 這類音樂基礎模型雖然在下游任務上有效,同樣是把協和關係隱藏在學到的嵌入裡,無法拆解檢視。協和感知理論這邊,論文站在兩條既有路線上:一是 Helmholtz 與 Plomp & Levelt 的「拍音與臨界頻帶干涉」說,二是作者實際採用的兩個具體方法——Stolzenburg(2015)的容忍度有理數近似,以及 Tenney(1984)的對數諧波距離。DS 的做法是把這兩個各自獨立的理論工具第一次組合成一個核函數,再套用到完整的 CQT 上做逐頻段歸因。

最接近的既有系統是 Schwär 等人(2025)對多軌錄音做逐時間點的感官不協和分析,以及 Poltronieri 等人(2025)拿協和感知做和弦估計的標籤平滑。差別在於:前者需要分軌的多軌錄音,後者需要和弦標籤當監督訊號,DS 則不需要 stem 或任何額外標註,直接對混音後的 CQT 做確定性運算就能得到逐頻段的不協和分佈。

On the representation side, the paper names CQT, chroma features, and tonal-space descriptors as common but unable to give "a stable, directly inspectable account of local consonance or dissonance"; neural foundation models such as MERT, MusicFM, MuLan, and CLAP are effective downstream but bury any consonance relationship inside a learned embedding that cannot be inspected. On the perception-theory side, the paper builds on two prior threads: Helmholtz's and Plomp & Levelt's beating/critical-band-interference account, and, more directly, the two techniques it actually reuses -- Stolzenburg's (2015) tolerance-based rational approximation and Tenney's (1984) logarithmic harmonic distance. DS's contribution is combining those two previously separate tools into a single kernel and applying it across the full CQT for per-bin attribution.

The closest existing systems are Schwar et al. (2025), which computes time-varying sensory dissonance on multitrack recordings, and Poltronieri et al. (2025), which uses consonance-aware label smoothing for chord estimation. The difference: the former needs separated multitrack stems, the latter needs chord labels as supervision, while DS needs neither -- it runs a deterministic computation directly on the mixed-down CQT to get a per-bin dissonance distribution.

方法Method

DS 的核心是兩層計算。第一層是頻率對關係核函數:先建一組簡化分數集合(1≤p≤q≤60、互質),用 Tenney 的 C(p/q)=log₂(pq) 當複雜度,再用 Stolzenburg 的容忍度近似(誤差在 1% 以內就套用該分數的複雜度值),經過八度摺疊後得到一個只跟「音高差」有關、範圍在 [0,1] 的不協和函數。第二層是把這個核函數套到 CQT(72 bins/octave、8 個八度、共 576 個頻段)上:對每個時間幀 t、每個頻段 k,把它跟其他所有頻段 l 的振幅乘積,乘上兩者音高差對應的不協和值,加總平均,得到一個跟原始 CQT 同樣大小的「不協和頻譜」矩陣,而不是單一分數。直接兩兩計算的複雜度是 O(K²T),論文改用頻率軸上的相關運算把複雜度降到 O(KT log K)。

要把 DS 接進既有模型,論文設計了一個閘控殘差轉接器:把快取好的 DS 編碼成 token 序列,用注意力機制跟原本模型的隱藏層做融合,輸出投影層初始化為零、閘控偏置初始化為負值,保證訓練一開始跟沒加 DS 的原始模型完全一樣。新增的參數量很小,MU-LLaMA 上約加 1.36M,情緒辨識模型上只加 0.21M。

DS is computed in two layers. The first is a pairwise pitch-relation kernel: build a set of reduced fractions (1<=p<=q<=60, coprime), score complexity with Tenney's C(p/q)=log2(pq), apply Stolzenburg's tolerance rule (snap to a rational's complexity when the observed ratio is within 1% of it), then fold across the octave to get a dissonance function of pitch difference alone, bounded in [0,1]. The second layer applies that kernel to a CQT (72 bins/octave, 8 octaves, 576 bins total): for every time frame t and bin k, it multiplies k's magnitude with every other bin l's magnitude, weights the product by the dissonance value for their pitch distance, and averages -- producing a full time-frequency matrix rather than a single score. Naive pairwise computation is O(K^2 T); the paper instead implements it as a frequency-axis correlation, bringing it down to O(KT log K).

To plug DS into an existing model, the paper uses a gated residual adapter: the cached DS is encoded into a token sequence and fused into the host model's hidden states via attention, with the output projection zero-initialized and the gate bias initialized negative so the model matches its no-DS baseline exactly at the start of training. The added parameter count is small -- about 1.36M on top of MU-LLaMA, and only 0.21M on the MERT-based emotion model.

限制Limitations

論文自己講得很清楚,DS 不是完整的協和感知模型:核函數只編碼了「週期性╱諧波距離」這一種先驗,刻意做了八度摺疊,完全沒有處理聽覺濾波器頻寬、遮蔽效應、調性語法、節奏張力,以及文化或個人學習到的偏好——這些全部被列為暫不處理的範圍。音色也是明講的弱點:音色會改變泛音的位置與振幅,進而改變協和感受,但 DS 沒有對此建模。

驗證方式上也有限制:音樂理論驗證(音程、和弦、調式排序)是靠理論推導出的「應該排序」去對照,並沒有另外找聽眾做真人聽覺測試;MusicQA 用的是自動化文字相似度指標(BLEU、METEOR、BERTScore),量的是跟參考答案的字面相似度,不是真正的樂理正確性。統計上,MusicQA 的 BERTScore-R 提升在 Holm 校正後的配對 t 檢定 p 值是 0.0017(顯著),但校正後的精確符號檢定 p 值只有 0.09375(邊緣顯著);情緒辨識那邊的符號檢定校正後 p 值同樣是 0.09375。作者也坦言,評估只涵蓋兩種下游任務家族,沒有完全把 DS 這個轉換本身的效果,跟分支輸入的壓縮與正規化方式分開驗證。

The authors are explicit that DS is not a complete consonance model: the kernel encodes only one prior (periodicity/harmonic-distance) with deliberate octave folding, and it does not model auditory-filter bandwidth, masking, tonal syntax, rhythmic tension, or culturally/individually learned preference -- all listed as out of scope. Timbre is called out too: it reshapes where partials sit and how loud they are, which in turn reshapes perceived consonance, and DS does not account for that.

The validation itself has limits: the music-theory checks (interval, chord, mode ordering) compare against theory-derived expected rankings rather than a real listening study with human participants, and MusicQA is scored with automatic text-similarity metrics (BLEU, METEOR, BERTScore) that measure surface overlap with a reference answer rather than actual harmonic correctness. Statistically, the Holm-adjusted paired-t p-value for the MusicQA BERTScore-R gain is 0.0017 (significant), but the Holm-adjusted exact sign-test p-value is only 0.09375 (marginal); the emotion-recognition sign test is likewise 0.09375 after correction. The authors also admit the evaluation spans only two downstream task families and does not fully isolate DS's own effect from the input compression and normalization used in its branch.

討論Discussion

整體來看,DS 在音樂理論驗證上排序準確度很高:13 個音程的斯皮爾曼相關係數 ρ=0.951,7 個教會調式排序 ρ=0.893,顯示這個核函數確實抓到了理論上該有的協和╱不協和順序。下游任務上,DS 分支在 MusicQA 與情緒辨識的指標上全部贏過原始基線、同參數量的高斯雜訊對照組,以及架構相同但沒有不協和加權的純 CQT 對照組——例如情緒辨識的平均 R²,DS 是 0.6589,基線是 0.6473,純 CQT 對照組是 0.6542,顯示光是多一條 CQT 路徑或多一點參數量都不足以解釋這個進步,不協和加權本身確實有貢獻,只是相對於純 CQT 的邊際貢獻不算大。

論文把這個結果定位成「音樂特定的歸納偏置可以補強端到端學習」,並主張 DS 保留的時頻矩陣格式讓研究者可以檢視「模型認為哪裡不協和」,而不只是拿到一個分數——這是它跟其他神經表示法比起來獨有的可解釋性優勢。未來工作方向包括把萃取與視覺化流程包裝成可重複使用的函式庫、用真人聽覺資料校正核函數,以及把它遷移到生成、風格分析等其他音樂任務上。

Overall, DS's ordinal accuracy on the music-theory checks is high: Spearman rho=0.951 across 13 intervals and rho=0.893 across the seven church modes, showing the kernel does recover the theoretically expected consonance ordering. On the downstream tasks, the DS branch beats the baseline, a parameter-matched Gaussian-noise control, and an architecture-matched plain-CQT branch across every reported MusicQA and emotion-recognition metric -- for instance mean R2 is 0.6589 for DS versus 0.6473 for the baseline and 0.6542 for the plain-CQT branch, indicating that neither an extra CQT pathway nor extra parameters alone explains the gain, though the marginal benefit of dissonance-weighting itself, relative to plain CQT, is modest.

The paper frames this as evidence that "music-specific inductive biases can complement end-to-end learning," and argues DS's retained time-frequency layout gives researchers something to inspect -- where a modeled dissonance relation concentrates -- rather than only a scalar score, which is its distinguishing interpretability advantage over other neural representations. Stated future directions include packaging the extraction and visualization pipeline as a reusable library, calibrating the kernel against real listener data, and testing transfer to other music tasks such as generation and style analysis.

AllMusicCaps: Album Reviews as Complementary Supervision for Music CLAP

AllMusicCaps:用專輯樂評當作音樂 CLAP 的補充監督訊號

Pablo Alonso-Jiménez, Xavier Lizarraga-Seijas, Xavier Serra, Dmitry Bogdanov
arXiv:2608.25244 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-26
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：AllMusicCaps 用 AllMusic 網站上 94,041 篇專業樂評人寫的樂評,當作訓練音樂 CLAP 模型的補充文字來源,跟 LP-MusicCaps、M4-RAG 這類「拿標籤或網路搜尋結果丟給 LLM 生成描述」的既有資料集不同,直接從樂評裡萃取具體引句再改寫成 245,346 筆字幕。在以自然語言、非標籤式描述為主的 Song Describer 檢索基準上,加入樂評衍生字幕後 MRR 從 15.1 提升到 18.8(+3.7 分),尤其在 LLM 判定為「敘事╱評價性」的第四級複雜度查詢上,基線準確率只有約 60%,加入樂評字幕後提升到約 72%。但作者自己也承認樂評沒辦法涵蓋既有語料庫那麼廣的音樂與音效多樣性,而且樂評衍生字幕對「樂器編制」這類製作層面描述的幫助不如對「情緒、場景」這類描述明顯。Why this one: AllMusicCaps uses 94,041 professional album reviews from AllMusic as a complementary text source for training music CLAP models, in contrast to prior datasets like LP-MusicCaps and M4-RAG that generate captions by feeding tags or web-search results to an LLM; it instead extracts verbatim quotes from reviews and rewrites them into 245,346 captions. On the Song Describer benchmark, whose captions favor natural, non-tag-style description, adding review-derived captions lifts MRR from 15.1 to 18.8 (+3.7 points), with the biggest gains on the LLM-judged, most narrative/evaluative complexity tier: baseline accuracy there is only about 60%, rising to about 72% with review captions added. The authors concede that reviews alone cannot match the diversity of existing corpora, and that review-derived captions help scene/mood description more than instrumentation-level production detail.

動機Motivation

現有的音樂 CLAP 模型讓文字查詢跟音樂嵌入對齊,支援用文字檢索音樂,大多靠 LLM 把標籤或網路搜尋結果轉寫成訓練用字幕,這些字幕「準確但表達範圍狹窄」——描述的是客觀的曲風、樂器、情緒標籤,卻缺少「敘事框架」(像是「聽起來就像……」)與「評價性形容詞」(像是「沙啞的人聲」)這類複雜查詢真正會用到的語言。這篇論文要解決的問題,是既有訓練資料的文字風格太單一,導致模型面對表達比較口語、比較主觀的查詢時檢索效果變差。

Existing music CLAP models, which align text queries with music embeddings to support text-based retrieval, mostly rely on an LLM rewriting tags or web-search results into training captions. Such captions are "accurate but expressively narrow": they capture objective genre, instrument, and mood tags but miss the narrative framing (e.g. "feels like...") and evaluative adjectives (e.g. "raspy vocals") that more complex, conversational queries actually use. The problem this paper targets is that the text register of existing training data is too uniform, which hurts retrieval when a real query is phrased more subjectively or narratively.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名幾個具體的既有音樂 CLAP:MusCALL 用私有目錄裡的專業字幕、MuLan 用 YouTube 描述╱標籤╱播放清單、TTMR 用音樂標記資料集、CLaMP3 則是拿歌名與歌手名去網路搜尋再用 LLM 後製。這些系統的共通點是,「大規模使用專輯樂評文字」這件事目前沒有人做過。字幕生成這邊,主流做法是 LP-MusicCaps 開創的「用 LLM 把標籤集合轉寫成自然語言字幕」,後續工作多半是改進提示詞或加上網路檢索增強生成——這篇論文的差異在於把底層文字來源換成專業樂評人寫的長文,而不是標籤或開放網路檢索結果。

訓練目標上,論文把 LeJEPA 的 SigReg 正規化項加進標準的 InfoNCE 對比損失,聲稱這樣可以在不犧牲檢索表現的前提下,拿回等向高斯嵌入原本才有的探測優勢——這是把兩個各自獨立的既有技術組合起來,而不是提出全新的訓練目標。

The paper names specific prior music CLAPs: MusCALL uses professional captions from private catalogs, MuLan uses YouTube descriptions/tags/playlists, TTMR uses music-tagging datasets, and CLaMP3 web-searches song title and artist name and LLM-post-processes the results. Their shared gap, per the paper, is that "album-review text has not been explicitly used at scale" by any of them. On caption generation specifically, the dominant approach is LP-MusicCaps, which prompts an LLM to turn tag sets into natural-language captions; follow-ups mostly refine the prompting or add retrieval-augmented generation from web search. This paper's difference is the underlying text source itself: professional, long-form album reviews rather than tags or open-web retrieval.

On the training objective, the paper adds LeJEPA's SigReg regularizer on top of the standard InfoNCE contrastive loss, claiming this recovers the probing advantage of isotropic Gaussian embeddings without costing retrieval performance -- combining two existing techniques rather than proposing a new objective.

方法Method

資料上,論文先把 94,041 篇 AllMusic 專輯樂評,依作者╱專輯名稱比對到 Discogs 的發行後設資料,再擴充到 245,346 首曲目(含 YouTube 曲目層級後設資料)。字幕生成有兩條管線:AMCQuotes 先用 Qwen 2.5-32B 從樂評裡萃取「事實性描述」的原文引句(限定曲風、製作風格、樂器編制、情緒、節奏,排除主觀評論與傳記資訊),再用第二個 LLM 把引句、Discogs 標籤與 YouTube 後設資料合併改寫成短句字幕;AMCStruct 則是單次呼叫 Llama 3-70B,依固定欄位(曲風、情緒、能量、速度、樂器編制、製作風格)產生結構化字幕,覆蓋率較高但幻覺風險也較高。

模型架構是 OMAR-RQ small(78M 參數的音訊編碼器)搭配 all-MPNet-base-v2(110M 參數的文字編碼器),投影到 512 維共享空間。訓練分兩階段:先在 650 萬筆只有原始 YouTube╱Discogs 後設資料(沒有 LLM 字幕)的音軌上,用 8 張 H100 跑 40 萬步預訓練;再用 AMC、LP-MusicCaps(47.6 萬筆)、M4-RAG(24.3 萬筆)、Freesound(32.7 萬筆)、Pro Sound Effects(102 萬筆)依 0.40╱0.15╱0.25╱0.14╱0.06 的比例加權取樣,跑 15 萬步微調,單次訓練約需 36 小時。

On data, the paper first matches 94,041 AllMusic album reviews to Discogs release metadata by artist/album name, then expands to 245,346 tracks including YouTube track-level metadata. Two caption pipelines are compared: AMCQuotes first has Qwen 2.5-32B extract verbatim "factual" quotes from reviews (restricted to genre, production style, instrumentation, mood, rhythm; excluding subjective opinion and biography), then a second LLM merges those quotes with Discogs tags and YouTube metadata into short rewritten captions; AMCStruct instead makes a single Llama 3-70B call to fill fixed schema fields (style, mood, energy, tempo, instrumentation, production), trading higher coverage for a higher hallucination risk.

The architecture is OMAR-RQ small (a 78M-parameter audio encoder) paired with all-MPNet-base-v2 (a 110M-parameter text encoder), projected into a shared 512-dimensional space. Training has two stages: a 400k-step pretraining run on 8 H100 GPUs over 6.5M tracks carrying only raw YouTube/Discogs metadata, followed by a 150k-step, roughly 36-hour fine-tuning run that weight-samples AMC, LP-MusicCaps (476k tracks), M4-RAG (243k tracks), Freesound (327k), and Pro Sound Effects (1.02M) at ratios of 0.40/0.15/0.25/0.14/0.06.

限制Limitations

論文自己列出的限制包括:樂評本身涵蓋不到既有語料庫那麼廣的音樂與音效多樣性;AMCStruct 因為單次生成、涵蓋範圍廣,人工檢查認為幻覺風險「偏低」但沒有量化;模型在曲風、場景、情緒這類描述上表現較好,樂器編制這類製作層面的描述進步比較有限;而且 AMCQuotes 跟 AMCStruct 用了不同的提示詞策略也用了不同的 LLM,論文自己承認「兩個變因無法完全拆開」,所以看不出提升到底來自資料來源本身還是生成方式。

數字上也能看出侷限:在標準 MusicCaps 檢索上,只用 AMCQuotes 或只用 AMCStruct 訓練(沒有混合既有資料)的 MRR 分別只有 5.6 與 4.6,遠低於基線的 7.2——樂評字幕必須跟既有資料混合使用才有幫助,單獨使用反而讓標準基準的表現變差;而且在 MTG-Jamendo 樂器分類這一項任務上,論文自己承認所有版本的模型都沒有贏過外部基準線。

The authors' own stated limitations: reviews alone cannot match the diversity of existing music/sound corpora; AMCStruct's single-pass generation trades for higher coverage at a hallucination risk manual inspection judged "low" but did not quantify; the model helps genre/scene/mood description more than production-level detail like instrumentation; and because AMCQuotes and AMCStruct differ in both prompting strategy and the LLM used, the authors admit "the two factors cannot be fully separated," so it is unclear how much of any gain comes from the data source itself versus the generation method.

The numbers themselves show real limits too: training on AMCQuotes or AMCStruct alone, without mixing in existing corpora, gives MusicCaps retrieval MRR of only 5.6 and 4.6 respectively -- both well below the 7.2 baseline -- so review-derived captions only help when blended with existing data, and hurt standard-benchmark performance when used alone. On MTG-Jamendo instrument classification specifically, the authors concede that none of their model variants beat the strongest external baseline.

討論Discussion

整體來看,樂評衍生字幕確實是既有語料庫的補強訊號,但不是全面替代:混入 AMCQuotes 後,Song Describer MRR 從 15.1 提升到 18.8,GTZAN 分類準確率從 86.4 微升到 87.1,而且複雜度第四級(敘事╱評價性)查詢的準確率從基線約 60% 提升到約 72%,提升幅度明顯大於第一級(標籤可直接表達的查詢,兩者都在 87% 左右)——這說明效益集中在原本表現最差的那類複雜查詢上,而不是全面拉高所有查詢。

訓練細節上也有值得注意的發現:把音訊編碼器所有層的特徵都拿來用(而非只取單一層),比只用第 12 層再提升 MusicCaps 檢索 0.5 分、Song Describer 0.5 分,並讓 DimSim(維度相似度)指標從 74.3 大幅提升到 83.0;而且大多數模型在訓練 15 萬步的過程裡,下游表現其實在 2 萬到 6 萬步就已經到頂,即使訓練損失還在持續下降。論文把資料集、模型權重與程式碼都公開釋出,但限定僅供非商業科學研究使用,未來工作聚焦在把這套資料應用到音樂字幕生成任務上,以及更深入研究 SigReg 這類幾何正規化方法。

Overall, review-derived captions act as a complementary signal on top of existing corpora rather than a wholesale replacement: adding AMCQuotes lifts Song Describer MRR from 15.1 to 18.8 and GTZAN accuracy slightly from 86.4 to 87.1, and the biggest jump is on the most narrative/evaluative complexity tier -- from roughly 60% to roughly 72% -- versus almost no change on the tag-expressible tier (both around 87%). So the benefit concentrates on the hardest, most narrative queries rather than lifting everything uniformly.

A separate training-detail finding stands out: combining features from all audio-encoder layers, instead of using only layer 12, adds 0.5 MRR points on both MusicCaps and Song Describer and raises the DimSim similarity metric sharply from 74.3 to 83.0; and most model variants peak in downstream performance within 20k-60k of the 150k training steps even as training loss keeps falling. The dataset, weights, and code are released for non-commercial research use; stated future work is applying the dataset to music captioning itself and studying SigReg-style geometric regularization further.

Drive-to-Music: Context-Aware Generative Audio for In-Vehicle Experiences

Drive-to-Music:面向車內體驗的情境感知生成式音訊

Cosmin Dragoiu, Nooshin Nabizadeh
arXiv:2608.12615 · cs.SD, cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-08-12
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Drive-to-Music 是一套把行車紀錄器影像與車輛遙測資料(車速、車外溫度)轉換成即時生成音樂的系統管線:先用視覺語言模型描述行車場景,再用 LLM 把場景描述與車輛資料轉成音樂描述詞,最後餵給生成式音訊模型(選定 Stable Audio 2.5)合成配樂,同時生成專輯封面與車內氣氛燈光。跟既有車內音訊系統相比——SoundsRide(2021)是即時混音既有音效、非 AI 生成也非情境化音樂,既有的情緒感知車內音樂推薦系統則是「挑選」既有曲庫而非生成新曲——這篇論文的差異在於直接生成前所未有的原創配樂,而不是挑選或混音。但論文本身的驗證相當薄弱:模型選型每個候選只測約十個樣本,「有限的使用者研究」也沒有公布參與人數、方法或任何量化數字。Why this one: Drive-to-Music is a system pipeline that turns dashcam imagery and vehicle telemetry (speed, exterior temperature) into real-time generated music: a vision-language model describes the driving scene, an LLM converts that description plus telemetry into musical descriptors, and a generative audio model (Stable Audio 2.5, as selected) synthesizes the soundtrack, alongside generated cover art and cabin ambient lighting. Compared with prior in-vehicle audio systems -- SoundsRide (2021) remixes existing sound effects in real time rather than generating AI music, and prior mood-aware in-vehicle recommenders select from an existing music library rather than generate new tracks -- this paper's difference is generating genuinely novel soundtracks rather than selecting or remixing. But the paper's own validation is thin: model selection is based on roughly ten samples per candidate, and the "limited user studies" report no participant count, methodology, or quantitative figures at all.

動機Motivation

車內音樂長期以來只是「被動的旅伴」——播放清單或電台,跟駕駛當下的路況、天氣、車速沒有任何連動。論文主張,音樂其實可以做成一種情境感知的介面,依照即時的行車狀況調整,藉此提升駕駛體驗、專注力與身心舒適度。這篇論文要解決的問題是:現有的 AI 音樂生成技術已經廣泛用在內容創作、素材庫音樂等領域,唯獨車用場景還沒有人做過——既有的車內音訊系統要嘛是挑選或混音既有曲目,要嘛評估只做在模擬器裡,沒有真正部署到實車上。

In-vehicle music has long been a passive travel companion -- a playlist or radio station with no connection to the current road, weather, or speed. The paper argues music could instead be an adaptive, context-aware interface that responds to real-time driving conditions to improve driver experience, attention, and well-being. The gap it targets: AI music generation is already widely used in content creation and stock-music libraries, but the automotive domain remains untouched -- existing in-vehicle audio systems either select or remix existing tracks, and where AI generation has been tested at all, evaluation stayed in a simulator rather than an actual vehicle.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名兩個具體的既有車內音訊系統。SoundsRide(Koutsogeorgis 等人,2021,曾獲 UIST 2021 最佳論文獎)是一套車內音訊擴增實境系統,把環境線索連結到音樂事件,靠即時預測與混音把既有音效素材接起來播放——重點是混音既有素材,不是生成新內容。另一個是 Çelik 等人(2020)的情緒感知車內音樂推薦系統,靠推斷駕駛情緒去挑選預期能改善舒適度與駕駛行為的既有曲目。

這篇論文最像的既有工作其實就是這兩個系統目標的組合:同樣是想讓車內音訊回應駕駛情境,但做法從「即時混音既有素材」或「挑選既有曲庫」換成「即時生成全新的原創配樂」。論文也引用了一般性的 AI 音樂生成文獻,但坦承這些系統過去從未被應用在車用場景,也沒有實車部署跟評估的先例。

The paper names two specific prior in-vehicle audio systems. SoundsRide (Koutsogeorgis et al., 2021, winner of a UIST 2021 Best Paper Award) is an in-vehicle audio augmented-reality system that links environmental cues to musical events, using continuous prediction and real-time mixing to stitch together existing sound material -- the operative word being mixing existing material, not generating new content. The other is Celik et al.'s (2020) mood-aware in-vehicle music recommender, which infers driver mood and selects existing tracks expected to improve comfort and driving behavior.

The closest prior work is effectively the combination of these two systems' goals -- making in-vehicle audio responsive to driving context -- but with the mechanism swapped from real-time remixing of existing material, or selecting from an existing library, to real-time generation of an entirely new soundtrack. The paper also cites general AI-music-generation literature but concedes none of it had previously been applied to the automotive setting, nor tested in an actual deployed vehicle.

方法Method

系統管線分六個階段。第一階段用視覺語言模型(選定 LiquidAI LFM 2.5 VL 1.6B)描述行車鏡頭畫面(周遭環境、地標、植被、天空與天氣);第二階段用 LLM(選定 LiquidAI LFM 2.5 1.2B Instruct)把場景描述跟車速、車外溫度合併成音樂描述詞(情緒、氛圍、風格);第三階段平行生成專輯封面(Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large Turbo)與曲名;第四階段用直方圖分析遮蔽天空╱道路╱建築等低資訊區域後,萃取主色調來調整車內氣氛燈光;第五階段對所有輸出做安全檢查(音樂品質評分、響度、真峰值、突然音量跳動、噪音底床,以及封面與曲名的內容審查),沒過的部分會重新生成。所有生成模型都跑在雲端,車機只負責蒐集資料上傳、接收生成結果播放,只需要間歇性網路連線。

模型選型是拿約十個樣本比較延遲與品質。生成音樂這一步比較了 ElevenLabs(15.7 秒延遲、MuQ-MuLan 語意對齊分數 0.09)、Mubert(7.4 秒、0.29)與 Stable Audio 2.5(6.2 秒、0.36),最後選 Stable Audio 2.5,因為它延遲最低且語意對齊分數最高;要求輸出是不帶人聲的純演奏音樂,長度至少 90 秒。

The system pipeline has six stages. Stage one uses a vision-language model (LiquidAI LFM 2.5 VL 1.6B, as selected) to describe the dashcam scene (surroundings, landmarks, vegetation, sky, weather); stage two uses an LLM (LiquidAI LFM 2.5 1.2B Instruct, as selected) to merge that description with speed and exterior temperature into musical descriptors (mood, atmosphere, style); stage three generates cover art (Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large Turbo) and a song title in parallel; stage four extracts a dominant color from the scene, after histogram-masking low-information regions like sky/road/buildings, to drive cabin ambient lighting; stage five runs safety checks on every output (music-quality score, loudness, true-peak, sudden loudness jumps, noise floor, plus content screening on the cover art and title), regenerating anything that fails. All generative models run in the cloud; the in-vehicle unit only collects data, uploads it, and plays back the result, needing only intermittent connectivity.

Model selection compared roughly ten samples per candidate for latency and quality. For music generation specifically: ElevenLabs (15.7s latency, 0.09 MuQ-MuLan semantic-alignment score), Mubert (7.4s, 0.29), and Stable Audio 2.5 (6.2s, 0.36) -- Stable Audio 2.5 was chosen for combining the lowest latency with the highest semantic alignment, with the requirement that output be instrumental (no vocals) and at least 90 seconds long.

限制Limitations

這篇論文沒有獨立的限制小節,弱點要從內文推敲。最明顯的是評估規模:模型選型每個候選只測約十個樣本,論文自己說的「有限的使用者研究」完全沒有公布參與人數、實驗方法或任何量化指標,只寫參與者對情境相關性與個人化給予正面評價這種定性描述。系統宣稱支援「隨行車狀況演變而平滑過渡」,但論文沒有提供任何量化數據驗證這個過渡真的平滑。

另外,系統完全仰賴雲端運算,需要至少間歇性的網路連線才能運作;雖然要求生成音樂不帶人聲(理論上降低分心風險),但論文並未評估這套系統對駕駛分心或行車安全的實際影響;個人化目前僅限於環境情境,還沒有納入使用者自己的音樂偏好。論文自己在未來工作裡承認,系統目前只用了行車鏡頭與車速╱溫度兩類訊號,尚未整合路況、導航等其他資訊。

The paper has no standalone limitations section, so the weaknesses have to be read out of the text. The clearest one is evaluation scale: model selection tested only about ten samples per candidate, and the paper's own "limited user studies" disclose no participant count, methodology, or quantitative metric at all -- only the qualitative statement that participants appreciated the contextual relevance and personalization. The system claims to support smooth transitions as driving conditions evolve, but no quantitative data backs that claim.

Beyond that, the system depends entirely on cloud compute and needs at least intermittent connectivity to function; while output is required to be instrumental (nominally reducing distraction risk), the paper never evaluates the system's actual effect on driver distraction or safety; and personalization currently uses only environmental context, not the user's own musical preferences. The authors themselves note, as future work, that the system currently uses only dashcam imagery plus speed/temperature, with traffic and navigation data not yet integrated.

討論Discussion

整體來看,這篇論文的核心貢獻是工程整合,不是新的模型架構:三個生成元件(場景理解、音樂生成、封面生成)全部是拿現成的商用或開源模型,依延遲與品質做系統性的取捨分析組裝起來——例如封面生成選了延遲僅 1.4 秒但 GenEval 分數只有 0.66 的 Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large Turbo,而不是延遲 40.4 秒、分數 0.91 的 Qwen Image,清楚展示這是拿延遲換品質的工程決策,而非追求指標最佳化。

論文自己也承認,目前呈現的證據偏向概念可行性展示而非嚴謹的系統性驗證,結論也停留在「展示了車用情境下即時生成音樂的可行性」這種較保守的表述。未來工作方向明確寫出要加入路況、導航與使用者偏好等訊號,以強化個人化與情境相關性,但這些都還沒有實作或驗證。

Overall, this paper's core contribution is engineering integration rather than a new model architecture: all three generative components (scene understanding, music generation, cover-art generation) are off-the-shelf commercial or open models, assembled through a systematic latency-versus-quality tradeoff analysis -- for instance the cover-art model chosen has only a 1.4-second latency but a GenEval score of just 0.66 (Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large Turbo), passed over in favor of the 40.4-second, 0.91-score Qwen Image, a clear latency-for-quality engineering call rather than metric optimization.

The authors themselves frame the evidence as a feasibility demonstration rather than rigorous systematic validation, and their stated conclusion stays correspondingly modest: showing the feasibility of real-time, context-aware music generation in automotive settings. Stated future work explicitly adds traffic, navigation, and user-preference signals to strengthen personalization and contextual relevance, but none of that is yet implemented or evaluated.
