SPDMX: Large-Scale Open Stem Audio From Symbolic Music

SPDMX：把公有領域 PDMX 樂譜以開源 FluidSynth 音色庫與 MIDI-DDSP 依樂器類別渲染成 25 萬首、49 萬條單聲道分軌的 CC BY 4.0 語料，其中 8.8 萬首多軌共 2,734 小時，並以 8 人聽測決定每類樂器的渲染配方

Phillip Long, Eduardo Escoto, Julian McAuley, Zachary Novack
arXiv:2611.09140 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-11-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：音源分離、伴奏生成、MIDI 轉音訊都需要分軌，但能合法再散布的分軌資料不是太小（MUSDB18 150 首），就是靠商用音色庫（Slakh2100 的音訊雖是 CC BY，渲染用的 VST 卻不能散布）。這篇把公有領域的 PDMX 樂譜全部用開源合成器渲染出來：253,982 首、490,159 條分軌、5,469 小時，其中 88,403 首是多軌（2,734 小時），整條渲染管線也一起釋出。選它是因為開放授權、可重製的大規模分軌資料是分離與表徵學習最缺的基礎建設。但要先講清楚：論文本文裡沒有任何下游實驗數字，分離與轉譜的「可用性檢查」只放在專案頁；65.2% 的歌只有一條分軌；四種常見樂器（貝斯、鼓、吉他、鋼琴）都在場的歌只有 632 首。Why this one: Source separation, accompaniment generation and MIDI-to-audio all need stems, but redistributable stem data is either small (MUSDB18 has 150 songs) or depends on commercial sample libraries (Slakh2100's audio is CC BY, but the VSTs that rendered it cannot be redistributed). This paper renders the whole public-domain PDMX score collection with open synthesizers: 253,982 songs, 490,159 stems, 5,469 hours, of which 88,403 songs (2,734 hours) are multitrack, and releases the render pipeline too. I picked it because openly licensed, reproducible stem data at scale is the infrastructure separation and representation learning most lack. But to be clear up front: the paper body contains no downstream experiment numbers, and the separation and transcription usability checks live only on the project page; 65.2% of songs have a single stem; only 632 songs contain all four of bass, drums, guitar and piano.

動機Motivation

文字、影像、語音都有大型公開資料集，音樂沒有，原因是錄音的著作權擋住了再散布。而且現代任務要的不只是混音，而是個別的分軌（stem）：音源分離要分軌當監督訊號，伴奏生成要以某幾軌為條件生成其他軌，MIDI 轉音訊需要對齊的樂譜與音訊。

論文把既有資源的取捨歸成三個維度：真實度、規模、授權。錄音型（MUSDB18、MedleyDB、MoisesDB）真實但小，最大的 MoisesDB 也只有 240 首、14.4 小時；合成型裡規模最大的 Slakh2100 有 2,100 首、145 小時，但依賴不能散布的商用音色庫，所以別人無法用同樣的方法再做一份。PDMX 本身有符號層級的規模，可是直接用 General MIDI 音色渲染聽起來很乾、很假。作者要做的是同時拿到樂譜資料集的規模和一條完全開源的渲染管線。

Text, images and speech all have large public datasets; music does not, because copyright blocks redistribution of recordings. And modern tasks need more than mixtures: they need individual stems. Source separation uses stems as supervision, accompaniment generation conditions on some stems to produce others, and MIDI-to-audio needs aligned scores and audio.

The paper frames existing resources along three axes: realism, scale and licensing. Recorded sets (MUSDB18, MedleyDB, MoisesDB) are realistic but small; even the largest, MoisesDB, has 240 songs and 14.4 hours. The largest synthetic set, Slakh2100, has 2,100 songs and 145 hours but relies on non-redistributable commercial sample libraries, so nobody else can rebuild it with the same method. PDMX has symbolic scale, but naive General MIDI rendering sounds dry and fake. The authors want score-collection scale and a fully open render pipeline at the same time.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

方法上最接近的先前工作是 Slakh2100（Manilow 等，2019）：把 Lakh MIDI 用取樣音色渲染成多軌，並讓混音等於分軌線性相加。SPDMX 幾乎照抄了這個做法，連「混音 = 分軌和、整首只套一個防削波增益」這條都明說是沿用 Slakh2100。差別在兩處：符號來源從流行歌 MIDI 換成公有領域樂譜，渲染器從商用 VST 換成開源音色庫。

渲染堆疊最接近的是 CocoChorales（Wu 等，2022）：同樣用 MIDI-DDSP 合成、同樣全開放。它有 240,000 首、1,411 小時，曲數其實和 SPDMX 同一量級，但內容是 Coconet 生成的四聲部聖詠，不是真實曲目。SPDMX 說自己是「最大的開放授權合成多軌資料」，靠的是 2,734 對 1,411 小時這個比較，而且是按混音時長算一次、不乘分軌數。

EnsembleSet（Sarkar 等，2022）與 SynthSOD 則用商用音色庫渲染室內樂與管弦樂分軌，品質較高但規模小（EnsembleSet 80 首、6.15 小時）且不能重製。SPDMX 在真實度上沒有宣稱贏過這些，它換到的是規模與可重製性。

The closest prior work in method is Slakh2100 (Manilow et al., 2019): render Lakh MIDI into multitrack audio with sample libraries, and make the mix equal the linear sum of stems. SPDMX nearly copies this recipe; even the rule that the mix is the stem sum with a single anti-clip gain is explicitly credited to Slakh2100. The differences are two: the symbolic source changes from pop MIDI to public-domain scores, and the renderer changes from commercial VSTs to open soundfonts.

The closest render stack is CocoChorales (Wu et al., 2022): also MIDI-DDSP synthesis, also fully open. It has 240,000 songs and 1,411 hours, so its song count is in the same range as SPDMX, but its content is four-part chorales generated by Coconet rather than real repertoire. SPDMX's claim to be the largest openly licensed synthetic multitrack set rests on 2,734 versus 1,411 hours, counted once per mixture rather than multiplied by stem count.

EnsembleSet (Sarkar et al., 2022) and SynthSOD render chamber and orchestral stems with commercial libraries, giving higher quality but small scale (EnsembleSet: 80 songs, 6.15 hours) and no reproducibility. SPDMX does not claim to beat them on realism; what it buys is scale and reproducibility.

方法Method

管線分三步。一、清理 MIDI：PDMX 的 GM 音色編號常跟音軌名稱不符（例如標成小提琴的軌被編碼成鋼琴），作者用音軌名稱的別名表修正音色，並丟掉沒有音符的軌。統計顯示鋼琴佔分軌數的 57%，但只佔非靜音時數的 41%，代表很多鋼琴軌又短又稀疏。非靜音時數是以 0.25 秒窗、RMS ≥ 0.01（約 −40 dBFS）計算。

二、用聽測選渲染配方：比較四種家族：單一 GM 音色庫（SGM-V2.01，稱 basic）、從 FluidR3_GM、GeneralUser GS、Arachno、Airfont 等可散布音色庫中抽樣並加輕度殘響（varied）、以及兩種 MIDI-DDSP 混合（單聲部弦樂、銅管、木管用 MIDI-DDSP，其他退回 basic 或 varied）。十個樂器類別各取兩條 10 秒片段，8 位聽者給 0–100 的真實度分數。規則是取平均最高者，但 basic 與 varied 接近時保留 varied 以增加音色多樣性；作者明說這不是顯著性規則。結果：鼓、風琴、吉他用 basic；鋼琴、人聲、敲擊、其他用 varied；弦樂與銅管用 MIDI-DDSP 加 basic，木管用 MIDI-DDSP 加 varied。

三、組裝：每條分軌先做響度正規化到 −23 LUFS 並限峰，再乘上「該軌最大 note-on 力度 / 全曲最大力度」來恢復軌間平衡，最後整首混音套一個統一的防削波增益，讓混音仍等於分軌線性和。MIDI-DDSP 只能合成 16 kHz，所以這些分軌是上採樣到 44.1 kHz 的，頻寬仍然受限。人聲軌用合唱音色，不做有歌詞的歌聲合成。

The pipeline has three steps. One, clean the MIDI: PDMX GM program numbers often disagree with track names (a track named violin encoded as piano), so the authors correct programs from track-name aliases and drop tracks with no notes. Piano accounts for 57% of stems but only 41% of non-silent hours, so many piano tracks are short and sparse. Non-silent hours are counted over 0.25 s windows with RMS ≥ 0.01 (about −40 dBFS).

Two, pick render recipes by listening: four families are compared: one GM bank (SGM-V2.01, called basic); sampling among redistributable banks such as FluidR3_GM, GeneralUser GS, Arachno and Airfont plus light reverb (varied); and two MIDI-DDSP hybrids, where monophonic strings, brass and winds use MIDI-DDSP and everything else falls back to basic or varied. Two 10-second excerpts per each of ten categories were rated 0–100 for realism by 8 listeners. The rule is highest mean, but keep varied when basic and varied are close, for timbral diversity; the authors state this is not a significance rule. Result: basic for drums, organ, guitar; varied for piano, voice, mallet, misc; MIDI-DDSP over basic for strings and brass, over varied for wind.

Three, assemble: each stem is loudness-normalised to −23 LUFS with peak limiting, then scaled by the ratio of its maximum note-on velocity to the song maximum to restore inter-track balance; a single anti-clip gain on the sum keeps the mix equal to the linear stem sum. MIDI-DDSP synthesises at 16 kHz, so those stems are upsampled to 44.1 kHz and remain band-limited. Voice tracks use choir soundfonts; lyric singing synthesis is out of scope.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是本文沒有任何下游實驗數字。摘要與結論都說「配對預算的分離與轉譜檢查確認了資料可用」，但那些檢查只在專案頁上，論文裡一個 SDR 或 F1 都沒有。所以讀這篇論文本身，無法判斷用 SPDMX 訓練的分離模型在真實錄音上表現如何，也無法判斷它跟 Slakh2100 比是好是壞，雖然作者特地切出了一個 632 首的 BDGP 子集就是為了做這個比較。

聽測規模很小：8 位聽者、每類樂器 2 條片段，而且數值只畫在圖上，本文沒有寫出任何平均分數。作者自己也說它只是「設計參考」，不能證明整個語料的真實度。

規模數字要打折看：65.2%（165,579 首）只有一條分軌，平均每首 1.93 條分軌；能直接用於標準四軌分離設定的只有 632 首，遠少於 Slakh2100 的 2,100 首。「多軌時數超過 Slakh2100 全部」是真的，但其中大量是兩三軌的鋼琴與人聲組合。

其他限制作者自己列了：MIDI 來自打譜軟體，節奏與力度都是量化的、沒有演奏表情；全部分軌都是單聲道；MIDI-DDSP 分軌頻寬上限 8 kHz。我另外注意到，響度先正規化到 −23 LUFS 再按最大力度比例縮放，代表軌間平衡只由力度決定，跟樂器本身的響度無關，短笛和低音號會被放到差不多大聲。

The biggest problem is that the paper body contains no downstream experiment numbers. The abstract and conclusion say matched-budget separation and transcription checks confirm usable data, but those checks live only on the project page; the paper has not a single SDR or F1. Reading the paper alone, one cannot tell how a separator trained on SPDMX does on real recordings, or whether it beats Slakh2100, even though the authors carved out a 632-song BDGP subset precisely for that comparison.

The listening study is tiny: 8 listeners, 2 excerpts per category, and the values appear only in a figure; the text reports no mean scores. The authors themselves call it design guidance that does not establish corpus-wide realism.

The scale figures need discounting: 65.2% of songs (165,579) have a single stem, the mean is 1.93 stems per song, and only 632 songs fit the standard four-stem separation setup, far fewer than Slakh2100's 2,100. That the multitrack hours exceed all of Slakh2100 is true, but much of it is two- or three-stem piano and voice combinations.

Other limits the authors list: MIDI comes from engraved scores with quantised timing and dynamics and no performance expression; all stems are mono; MIDI-DDSP stems top out at 8 kHz bandwidth. I would add that normalising to −23 LUFS and then scaling by the max-velocity ratio means inter-track balance is set only by velocity, not by the instrument's natural loudness, so a piccolo and a tuba end up roughly equally loud.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值在基礎建設，不在方法。真正讓人想用的是「開源渲染管線 + 公有領域樂譜」這個組合：任何人都可以換一個音色庫、換一批樂譜重做一份，這是 Slakh2100 做不到的。對分離研究者來說，它最可能的用途是預訓練或資料擴增，而不是取代 MUSDB18 這類真實錄音測試集。

我最想看到的實驗是：在 632 首 BDGP 子集與 Slakh2100 上用同樣預算訓練同一個分離模型，都拿 MUSDB18 或 MoisesDB 的真實錄音測試。只有這個數字能回答「開源音色庫渲染出來的分軌到底夠不夠真」。專案頁上也許有，但論文該把它放進來。

另一個值得注意的是曲目分布：PDMX 來自 MuseScore 社群，古典與教學曲目偏多，鋼琴佔 57% 分軌。用它訓練的模型對流行樂的鼓與貝斯會學到多少，很難說。它可能最適合的反而是古典、室內樂這類現有分軌資料最少的領域，剛好跟 EnsembleSet、SynthSOD 的目標重疊，而且規模大兩個數量級。

The value here is infrastructure, not method. What makes it attractive is the combination of an open render pipeline and public-domain scores: anyone can swap in another soundfont set or another score collection and rebuild it, which Slakh2100 cannot offer. For separation researchers its likely use is pre-training or augmentation, not replacing real-recording test sets like MUSDB18.

The experiment I most want is: train the same separator with the same budget on the 632-song BDGP subset and on Slakh2100, and test both on real recordings from MUSDB18 or MoisesDB. Only that number answers whether open-soundfont stems are real enough. The project page may have it, but the paper should include it.

The repertoire distribution also matters: PDMX comes from the MuseScore community, skews toward classical and teaching pieces, and piano is 57% of stems. How much a model trained on it learns about pop drums and bass is unclear. Its best fit may instead be classical and chamber music, where existing stem data is scarcest; that overlaps exactly with EnsembleSet and SynthSOD's targets, at two orders of magnitude more scale.

檢索、推薦與情緒Retrieval, RecSys & Emotion2026-11-10

Concordance-Optimal Continuous Music Emotion Estimation: Variance Decomposition, Dual-Branch Temporal Fusion, and Conformal Prediction Intervals

以歌曲間／歌曲內變異分解說明 MSE 與視窗 CCC 各自優化什麼，並用凍結 MERT 第 7 層加 Basic Pitch 轉譜符號特徵的雙分支 TCN 交叉注意力模型，在 DEAM 與 PMEmo 上聯合訓練連續喚醒度／正負向度預測，附 λ 掃描、歌曲層級 Wilcoxon 檢定與 split-conformal 區間（MMAS）

Mohammad Faraz Akhtar, Aditya Parashar, Ayush Jain, Madhur Gupta, Ritesh Kumar, Soubhik Chakraborty
arXiv:2611.08966 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-11-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：連續音樂情緒辨識的慣用指標是把所有歌所有影格混在一起算的相關係數，但這個數字主要反映「歌與歌之間平均情緒的差異」，一個只會猜每首歌平均值、完全不追蹤變化的模型也能拿高分。這篇把這件事講得很清楚，並用一個掃描證明：損失函數裡 CCC 項的權重 λ 從 0 調到 1，混合相關（pooled r）從 0.843 降到 0.810，歌曲內相關（per-song r）卻從 0.204 升到 0.368。選它是因為這個量測問題值得每個做連續情緒的人知道。但它自己的消融表也顯示，以作者自訂的主要指標（per-song r）來看，符號分支和交叉注意力對喚醒度都沒有幫助：完整模型 0.363，拿掉注意力是 0.382、拿掉符號分支是 0.371，反而略高。Why this one: The standard metric in continuous music emotion recognition is a correlation pooled over all frames of all songs, but that number mostly reflects differences in average emotion between songs, so a model that only guesses each song's mean and tracks no movement can still score well. This paper lays that out clearly and shows it with a sweep: raising the weight λ on the CCC term from 0 to 1 lowers pooled r from 0.843 to 0.810 while raising per-song r from 0.204 to 0.368. I picked it because this measurement problem is worth knowing for anyone doing continuous emotion. But its own ablation table also shows that on the authors' declared primary metric, per-song r, the symbolic branch and the cross-attention do not help arousal: the full model gets 0.363, removing attention gives 0.382 and removing the symbolic branch gives 0.371, both slightly higher.

動機Motivation

連續（動態）音樂情緒辨識要預測一首歌裡喚醒度（Arousal）與正負向度（Valence）隨時間的變化，標註是每 0.5 秒一個值。標註資料是巢狀的：每首歌貢獻很多影格，所以總變異可以拆成「歌曲之間」（每首歌整體情緒高低不同）與「歌曲之內」（情緒隨時間移動）兩部分。

文獻幾乎都只報告混合所有影格的 pooled 相關係數，而這個數字由比較大的那一部分主導，實際上就是歌曲間變異。所以一個只輸出每首歌平均值的模型，pooled r 可以很漂亮，但歌曲內相關是零。既然連續 MER 的前提就是要追蹤動態，這是個實質的量測問題，不是技術細節。

作者的第二個動機在建模：多數系統只用手工聲學特徵，沒用到和聲、節奏、旋律輪廓這些已知能預測正負向度的符號特徵。他們想做一個只吃原始音訊、能在不同資料集間共用的模型。

Continuous (dynamic) music emotion recognition predicts how Arousal and Valence move within a song, with one annotation every 0.5 s. The data are nested: each song contributes many frames, so total variance splits into a between-song part (songs differ in overall emotional level) and a within-song part (emotion moves over time).

The literature almost always reports only a pooled correlation over all frames, and that number is dominated by the larger component, which in practice is between-song variance. So a model that outputs each song's mean can post a fine pooled r with zero within-song correlation. Since the whole premise of continuous MER is tracking dynamics, this is a substantive measurement problem, not a technicality.

The second motivation is modelling: most systems use hand-crafted acoustic features and ignore symbolic properties such as harmony, rhythm and melodic contour that are known to predict valence. The authors want a model that takes only raw audio and can be shared across datasets.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

先講論文宣稱首創的部分最像什麼。「歌曲間／歌曲內」分解就是單因子變異數分析（ANOVA）的組間／組內平方和，而 per-song r 本來就是 MediaEval Emotion in Music / DEAM（Aljanaki 等，2017）官方基準採用的指標，論文自己也拿官方 OpenSMILE 基準的 per-song 喚醒度 r ≈ 0.36 來比。用 CCC 當訓練損失來對抗 MSE 造成的「預測軌跡被壓扁」，在連續情感辨識也早已常見，例如 Trigeorgis 等（2016） 在 AVEC 語音情緒上就直接優化 CCC，之後 AVEC 系列挑戰也以 CCC 為主指標。所以這篇的貢獻比較像「把散落的常識寫成一套明確論證並用掃描驗證」，而不是新發現；作者的首創宣稱也收得很窄，只說 CCC 因式分解當設計理由與 conformal 校準「在連續 MER」沒人做過。

建模上最接近的是 Music2Emo：同樣用 MERT 嵌入加和弦與調性特徵，以知識蒸餾跨資料集。這篇的差別是符號分支換成十個隨時間變化的轉譜特徵、融合從 MLP 換成視窗內交叉注意力、跨資料集從蒸餾換成直接聯合訓練。另一個點名的對照是 MCAN，在 DEAM 子集上報告 CCC ≈ 0.50，但用隨機切分，協定不同。

First, what the paper's claimed novelties most resemble. The between/within decomposition is the between-group and within-group sum of squares from one-way ANOVA, and per-song r is already the official metric of the MediaEval Emotion in Music / DEAM benchmark (Aljanaki et al., 2017); the paper itself compares against the official OpenSMILE baseline's per-song arousal r ≈ 0.36. Using CCC as a training loss to counter the trajectory flattening that MSE induces is also long established in continuous affect recognition: Trigeorgis et al. (2016) optimised CCC directly for AVEC speech emotion, and later AVEC challenges used CCC as the main metric. So the contribution reads more as turning scattered common knowledge into an explicit argument verified by a sweep than as a discovery; the novelty claim itself is narrow, only that the CCC factorisation as design rationale and conformal calibration are new in continuous MER.

On modelling, the closest system is Music2Emo: MERT embeddings plus chord and key features, with knowledge distillation across datasets. The differences here are ten time-varying transcription features in the symbolic branch, windowed cross-attention instead of MLP fusion, and plain joint training instead of distillation. Another named comparison is MCAN, which reports CCC ≈ 0.50 on a DEAM subset under a random split, a different protocol.

方法Method

理論部分。MSE 的最佳解是條件期望；當特徵對歌曲整體水準的資訊遠多於對瞬間變化的資訊時，條件期望會接近每首歌的平均，於是 MSE 會把預測軌跡往常數壓。Lin 的 CCC 可以因式分解成 CCC = r · C_b，r 獎勵形狀一致，C_b 懲罰平均偏移與變異被壓縮。在 16 秒視窗內沿時間軸計算的 (1 − CCC_w) 項，正好懲罰 MSE 鼓勵的東西。損失是 L = MSE + λ(1 − CCC_w)，λ 就是兩個準則之間的匯率。

模型（MMAS）。分支 A：音訊重採樣到 24 kHz 送進凍結的 MERT-v1-95M，取第 7 層 768 維隱藏狀態平均池化到 0.5 秒網格；第 7 層是用訓練歌曲上的線性探測選的，正負向度 pooled r 從第 0 層 0.576 升到第 7 層 0.687，再降到第 12 層 0.541。分支 B：Basic Pitch 轉成 MIDI，在 2 秒上下文內算十個特徵，包括音符密度、大小調比例、不協和度、拍子熵、速度變異、平均音高、輪廓斜率、音域，以及 Krumhansl–Schmuckler 調性剖面的大調權重與調性清晰度。

兩分支各自經過三層殘差擴張卷積 TCN（64 通道，擴張 1/2/4，感受野 15 影格 = 7.5 秒），再以聲學當 query、符號當 key/value 做 4 頭交叉注意力，把聲學編碼、注意力輸出、符號編碼串接後經小 MLP 回歸。輸入是 32 影格（16 秒）視窗、步長 4 影格，重疊預測取平均。總參數 233,027，λ = 0.5，AdamW，每折在 RTX 3050 上約 2 分鐘。

評估與不確定性。DEAM 1,764 首加 PMEmo 767 首，按歌曲分組做 5 折；per-song 統計只納入至少 20 個可評估影格且標註標準差大於 10⁻⁶ 的歌，這條規則是事先固定的。顯著性檢定在歌曲層級做 Wilcoxon 符號秩檢定。另外用 split conformal（α = 0.05）在驗證集影格上取絕對殘差分位數，產生固定寬度的預測區間，取代早期版本錯標成「95% 區間」的視窗間標準差。

Theory. MSE is minimised by the conditional mean; when features say much more about a song's overall level than about its moment-to-moment movement, that mean sits near the song average, so MSE pushes the predicted trajectory toward a constant. Lin's CCC factorises as CCC = r · C_b: r rewards shape agreement and C_b penalises mean offset and variance compression. A (1 − CCC_w) term computed along time inside a 16-second window penalises exactly what MSE rewards. The loss is L = MSE + λ(1 − CCC_w), with λ as the exchange rate between the two criteria.

Model (MMAS). Branch A: audio resampled to 24 kHz goes through frozen MERT-v1-95M; the 768-d layer-7 hidden state is mean-pooled onto a 0.5 s grid. Layer 7 was chosen by a linear probe on training songs; pooled valence r rises from 0.576 at layer 0 to 0.687 at layer 7, then falls to 0.541 at layer 12. Branch B: Basic Pitch transcribes to MIDI and ten features are computed in a 2 s context: note density, mode ratio, dissonance, beat entropy, tempo variance, pitch mean, contour slope, pitch range, and Krumhansl–Schmuckler major weight and key clarity.

Each branch passes through a three-block residual dilated TCN (64 channels, dilations 1/2/4, receptive field 15 frames = 7.5 s), then 4-head cross-attention with acoustic queries and symbolic keys/values; acoustic encoding, attention output and symbolic encoding are concatenated and regressed by a small MLP. Inputs are 32-frame (16 s) windows with a 4-frame step, with overlapping predictions averaged. 233,027 parameters, λ = 0.5, AdamW, about 2 minutes per fold on an RTX 3050.

Evaluation and uncertainty. 1,764 DEAM plus 767 PMEmo songs, song-grouped 5-fold CV; per-song statistics include only songs with at least 20 evaluable frames and annotation SD above 10⁻⁶, a rule fixed in advance. Significance uses a song-level Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Split conformal (α = 0.05) takes an absolute-residual quantile over validation frames to give fixed-width intervals, replacing an earlier version's window-spread band mislabelled as a 95% interval.

限制Limitations

以作者自訂的主要指標來看，多模態與注意力都沒有幫上忙。 論文明說 per-song r 是主要評估準則，但 DEAM 消融表裡，完整 MMAS 的 per-song 喚醒度 r 是 0.363；拿掉交叉注意力改成串接是 0.382，拿掉整個符號分支是 0.371，兩者都比完整模型高。符號分支帶來的 +0.05 只出現在 pooled r（0.767 → 0.819）。轉譜破壞實驗也印證這點：把符號特徵全換成訓練平均值，per-song 喚醒度 r 從 0.314 到 0.314，完全不變。作者對注意力有坦白說「買到的是 pooled 準確度，不是動態」，但對符號分支仍寫「多模態主張成立」，這跟他們自己的論證互相矛盾。

「架構讓歌曲內追蹤變三倍」混了兩件事。 Ridge 基準 per-song 喚醒度 r 0.126，MMAS 0.363；但 Ridge 是以平方誤差擬合的，而 λ = 0 的深度模型（同架構、只用 MSE）是 0.204。所以 0.237 的差距裡，架構只解釋約 0.078，其餘約 0.159 來自 CCC 損失。另外 Ridge 的 pooled r 0.829 其實比 MMAS 的 0.819 高。

Conformal 區間只在邊際上接近有效：目標 95%，實得喚醒度 93.7%、正負向度 90.0%；第 10 百分位的歌只有 78.3% 與 68.1%。區間寬度約 0.43，佔 [0,1] 標註範圍的四成多，實用性有限。作者有誠實列出，也指出以歌曲為單位的 group conformal 是正解。

其他：正負向度在 DEAM 上明顯較弱（pooled 0.685、per-song 0.220）；語料以西方流行樂為主；Wilcoxon 檢定用的是另一次 70/10/20 切分的 353 首測試歌，per-song 值（0.344）與 5 折結果（0.363）不是同一批。

On the authors' own primary metric, neither multimodality nor attention helps. The paper says per-song r is the primary criterion, yet in the DEAM ablation full MMAS has per-song arousal r 0.363, replacing cross-attention with concatenation gives 0.382, and removing the whole symbolic branch gives 0.371; both are higher than the full model. The symbolic branch's +0.05 shows up only in pooled r (0.767 → 0.819). The corruption study agrees: replacing all symbolic features with their training mean leaves per-song arousal r at 0.314, unchanged from 0.314. The authors do admit attention buys pooled accuracy rather than dynamics, but still write that the multimodal claim holds, which contradicts their own argument.

The claim that the architecture triples within-song tracking mixes two things. The ridge baseline has per-song arousal r 0.126 and MMAS 0.363; but ridge is fitted by squared error, and the λ = 0 deep model (same architecture, MSE only) gets 0.204. So of the 0.237 gap, architecture explains about 0.078 and the CCC loss about 0.159. Ridge's pooled r of 0.829 is also higher than MMAS's 0.819.

Conformal intervals are only approximately valid marginally: against a 95% target, coverage is 93.7% for arousal and 90.0% for valence; the 10th-percentile song gets only 78.3% and 68.1%. Interval width is about 0.43, over four tenths of the [0,1] annotation range, which limits practical use. The authors report this honestly and point to song-level group conformal as the fix.

Others: valence is clearly weaker on DEAM (pooled 0.685, per-song 0.220); the corpora are mainly Western pop; the Wilcoxon test uses 353 test songs from a separate 70/10/20 split, so its per-song value (0.344) and the 5-fold result (0.363) come from different songs.

討論Discussion

這篇最值得帶走的是那張 λ 掃描表，不是 MMAS 這個系統。同一組特徵、同一組折，只改 λ，per-song 喚醒度 r 就在 0.204 和 0.368 之間移動，差 1.8 倍，而 pooled r 只從 0.843 動到 0.810。從 λ = 0 到 0.1 這一步，pooled 只掉 0.008，per-song 卻升 0.150，作者算成將近 19 比 1 的報酬；0.25 之後曲線就平了。這說明任何只報 pooled r 的連續 MER 論文，讀者都無法判斷它有沒有在追蹤動態。

也正因為這個論證很有說服力，它反過來削弱了論文的系統貢獻：照作者自己的標準，MMAS 裡真正讓 per-song r 上升的是損失函數，不是 MERT 加符號特徵的雙分支設計。一個「MERT 單分支 + TCN + CCC 損失」的版本，在 per-song 上大概就夠了（消融表裡是 0.371）。符號特徵也許在別的地方有用，例如可解釋性，但論文沒有展示。

工程面倒是做得很誠實：修掉 DEAM 與 PMEmo 有 572 個歌曲 ID 撞號的問題、發現早期 PMEmo 根本沒跑轉譜（十個特徵有八個是 0）、拿掉沒有驗證依據的「緊張度 = A − V」，這些都寫進論文裡。修完之後 PMEmo 的 pooled r 從約 0.32／0.16 升到 0.742／0.746，這本身就是另一個「先檢查資料再談模型」的例子。

The thing to take away is the λ sweep, not MMAS as a system. Same features, same folds, only λ changes, and per-song arousal r moves between 0.204 and 0.368, a factor of 1.8, while pooled r only moves from 0.843 to 0.810. The step from λ = 0 to 0.1 costs 0.008 pooled and gains 0.150 per-song, which the authors compute as a return of nearly nineteen to one; past 0.25 the curve is flat. It means no reader can tell whether a continuous-MER paper reporting only pooled r tracks dynamics at all.

Precisely because that argument is persuasive, it undercuts the system contribution: by the authors' own standard, what raises per-song r in MMAS is the loss, not the dual-branch MERT-plus-symbolic design. A version with a single MERT branch, a TCN and the CCC loss is probably enough on per-song r (0.371 in the ablation table). Symbolic features may help elsewhere, for example interpretability, but the paper does not show that.

The engineering is reported honestly: fixing 572 song-ID collisions between DEAM and PMEmo, discovering that transcription had never run on early PMEmo audio (eight of ten features were zero), and dropping an unvalidated tension index defined as A − V all go into the paper. After the fixes PMEmo pooled r rose from about 0.32 / 0.16 to 0.742 / 0.746, another example of checking the data before discussing the model.

Count-Density Networks for Modal Plate Parameter Estimation

用「計數密度」網路從合成板式殘響脈衝響應估計數量未知的模態頻率、衰減與增益：以 1/20 八度格點上的模態密度加總數預測、和保持的整數分配解碼，比較實值 U-Net 與複數值 Transformer 兩種骨幹（DAFx 參數估計挑戰 Task B）

Rodrigo Diaz, Pablo Tablas De Paula, David Marttila, Ilias Ibnyahya, Chin-Yun Yu
arXiv:2611.02083 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-01
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：要從一段板式殘響的脈衝響應反推裡面所有共振模態，困難在於模態數量事先不知道、可能成千上萬，而一般神經網路只會輸出固定長度的向量。這篇的解法是不直接輸出模態清單，而是預測「每個頻率格裡有幾個模態」的密度圖加一個總數，再用和保持的四捨五入轉成整數，有點像人群計數用密度圖代替逐一偵測。選它是因為這是把集合預測問題轉成密度回歸的乾淨例子，對樂器與效果器的系統識別都適用。在 100 條測試 IR 上，總誤差 0.318，最強的傳統方法（Fast Burg）是 1.098；但增益誤差 0.85 跟傳統方法（最低 0.907）差不多，而且佔了總誤差約九成。Why this one: Recovering every resonant mode from a plate-reverb impulse response is hard because the number of modes is unknown in advance and may run into the thousands, while ordinary networks output fixed-length vectors. This paper does not output a mode list directly; it predicts a density map of how many modes fall in each frequency bin plus a total count, then converts that to integers by sum-preserving rounding, somewhat like crowd counting replacing per-person detection with density maps. I picked it because it is a clean example of turning a set-prediction problem into density regression, applicable to system identification of instruments and effects. On 100 test IRs the overall error is 0.318 versus 1.098 for the strongest classical method (Fast Burg); but the gain error of 0.85 is close to the classical methods (best 0.907) and makes up about ninety percent of the total error.

動機Motivation

第一屆 DAFx 參數估計挑戰的 Task B，要從參考板式殘響模擬器產生的脈衝響應（IR）中，找回一組無序、數量未知的模態三元組：頻率 ω、衰減率 σ、增益 b。這是典型的系統識別問題：拿到 IR 之後，如果能得到模態參數，就能用模態合成重現、修改這個殘響，或者跟物理模型對照。

難處有三個。一是模態數量 M 不知道，而且板子在高頻的模態密度會變得很高，要同時決定「有幾個」和「每個是什麼」。二是模態密度跨越好幾個數量級，用均勻的損失會讓密集頻段主導訓練。三是增益要恢復絕對尺度，但挑戰提供的 .wav 檔是峰值正規化過的，尺度資訊只存在另外的 normalization_factor 欄位。作者的動機是：傳統的子空間法與自迴歸法在這麼多模態時又慢又不準，能不能用一個網路直接從 IR 一次推出全部。

Task B of the first DAFx Parameter Estimation Challenge asks for an unordered set of unknown size of modal triples (frequency ω, decay rate σ, gain b) from impulse responses (IRs) produced by a reference plate-reverb simulator. It is classic system identification: given an IR, modal parameters let you resynthesise and modify the reverb with modal synthesis, or compare it against a physical model.

There are three difficulties. First, the mode count M is unknown, and plate modal density gets very high at high frequencies, so the model must decide both how many and what each one is. Second, modal density spans orders of magnitude, so a uniform loss would let dense bands dominate training. Third, gains must be recovered on an absolute scale, but the challenge's .wav files are peak-normalised and the scale lives only in a separate normalization_factor field. The authors' motivation: classical subspace and autoregressive methods are slow and inaccurate with this many modes, so can a network infer them all from the IR in one pass?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

傳統基準是兩類高解析度頻譜估計法。ESPRIT、Matrix Pencil、Root-MUSIC 屬子空間法，把訊號分頻帶後估計極點；Burg、Yule–Walker 屬自迴歸法，配合 CLEAN 迭代扣除已找到的模態。這些方法在模態少、分離得開時很準，但要事先決定模型階數或每頻帶的模態上限（論文設 32 頻帶、每帶最多 100 個模態、AR 階數 200），模態密集時就會漏抓或抓錯。

論文沒有點名，但這個「計數密度」的想法最像電腦視覺裡的人群計數密度圖（Lempitsky 與 Zisserman，2010）：不偵測每個人，而是回歸一張積分等於人數的密度圖。這裡把「人」換成「模態」、把影像平面換成對數頻率軸。另一條可比較的路線是 DETR 式的集合預測：固定數量的查詢槽加匈牙利匹配損失，但當模態可能上千個時，匹配成本與槽位數都會變得很大，密度法避開了這個問題。

第一作者 Diaz 先前的 可微分模態共振器（Diaz 等，ICASSP 2023）是用神經網路預測剛體的模態參數再合成聲音，屬於「模態數量固定」的設定；這篇把它推到數量未知、密度極高的情況。B2 的複數值 Transformer 則直接沿用 Eilers 與 Jiang（2023） 的複數注意力與複數層正規化構件。

The classical baselines are two families of high-resolution spectral estimators. ESPRIT, Matrix Pencil and Root-MUSIC are subspace methods that split the signal into bands and estimate poles; Burg and Yule–Walker are autoregressive methods combined with CLEAN, iteratively subtracting found modes. They are accurate when modes are few and well separated, but require a model order or per-band mode cap chosen in advance (here 32 bands, at most 100 modes per band, AR order 200), and they miss or misplace modes when density is high.

The paper does not name it, but the count-density idea is closest to crowd-counting density maps in computer vision (Lempitsky and Zisserman, 2010): instead of detecting each person, regress a density map whose integral equals the count. Here persons become modes and the image plane becomes a log-frequency axis. Another comparable route is DETR-style set prediction with a fixed number of query slots and a Hungarian matching loss, but with possibly thousands of modes the matching cost and slot count grow large; the density approach sidesteps that.

First author Diaz's earlier differentiable modal resonators (Diaz et al., ICASSP 2023) predicted rigid-body modal parameters with a network and then synthesised sound, in a fixed-mode-count setting; this paper pushes that to an unknown count at very high density. B2's complex-valued Transformer directly reuses the complex attention and complex layer-norm building blocks of Eilers and Jiang (2023).

方法Method

訓練資料。用 GPU 版的參考板模擬器產生 20 個 HDF5 分片，每片 16,384 條 5 秒、44.1 kHz 的 IR，共 327,680 條。每條附六個正規化物理參數與三個阻尼參數，真實模態由模擬器的解析公式即時算出，只用來當標籤；推論時網路不使用板子的解析模型。

共同前處理。兩個方法都吃峰值正規化的波形 x̃ = x / g_x，g_x 是原始 IR 的峰值。因為合成 IR 在 10 kHz 以上沒有模態，先降採樣到 22.05 kHz，只保留 10 kHz 以下的 FFT 頻格。增益目標以 b_m / g_x 表示，輸出時再乘回 g_x。

目標與損失。把模態累加到 20 Hz–10 kHz 的 1/20 八度對數頻格上，得到每格的模態數；每格內模態的衰減與增益放進固定數量的有序槽。計數損失全部在 log(1 + ·) 空間計算，避免密集頻格主導；包含逐格、總數、累積計數與多解析度（2、5、10 倍聚合）四種 Huber 損失，再加一個交叉熵讓預測密度分布對齊目標分布。累積與多解析度項讓計數在多個頻率尺度上都一致，而不只是逐格對。衰減與增益用只在有模態的槽上計算的 Huber 損失。

和保持解碼（兩方法共用）。把預測的總數 M̄ 四捨五入得 M̃，把每格密度分數縮放到總和為 M̃ 後取下整，剩下的名額分給小數部分最大的格（最大餘數法）。這保證每格是非負整數且總和正好是 M̃。格內模態的頻率以決定性方式擺放，衰減與增益取該格前 N_k 個槽。

B1：U-Net（約 310 萬參數）。輸入三通道：列正規化的對數幅度、sin 相位、cos 相位，在 1/120 八度細格上平均池化。另有一個跨步 CNN 編碼 IR 前 0.75 秒成 128 維向量，與 log g_x 一起注入瓶頸。編碼器 32/64/128/256 通道、瓶頸 512，瓶頸對頻率平均後接兩個全域頭預測總數與增益 RMS 尺度 b_RMS；解碼器輸出降到 1/20 八度，每格 768 個槽。增益拆成幅度、符號（二元交叉熵）與 b_RMS。AdamW、學習率 10⁻³、26 個 epoch，單張 A100 約 24 小時。

B2：複數值 Transformer（約 9,960 萬實值參數）。對全解析度複數頻譜做保留相位的對數壓縮與每 IR 的 RMS 正規化，五層複數 CNN 切成 64 個複數 token，接六層複數 Transformer（維度 320、8 頭），再經 2,048 單元的稠密瓶頸接各個頭。參數大多在瓶頸（8,390 萬），CNN 與 Transformer 合計只有 940 萬。增益直接回歸帶號值，不做 B1 的拆解。7 個 epoch，單張 A100 約 27 小時。

Training data. A GPU implementation of the reference plate simulator produces 20 HDF5 shards of 16,384 five-second 44.1 kHz IRs each, 327,680 in total. Each comes with six normalised physical parameters and three damping parameters; ground-truth modes are computed on the fly from the simulator's analytical formula and used only as labels. At inference the networks do not use the plate's analytical model.

Shared preprocessing. Both methods take the peak-normalised waveform x̃ = x / g_x, where g_x is the raw IR's peak. Since the synthetic IRs have no modes above 10 kHz, they are downsampled to 22.05 kHz and only FFT bins below 10 kHz are kept. Gain targets are expressed as b_m / g_x and multiplied back by g_x on output.

Targets and loss. Modes are accumulated onto a 1/20-octave log-frequency grid from 20 Hz to 10 kHz, giving a per-bin count; decay and gain of the modes in each bin go into a fixed set of ordered slots. All count terms are computed in log(1 + ·) space so dense bins do not dominate: Huber losses on per-bin, total, cumulative and multi-resolution counts (aggregation factors 2, 5, 10), plus cross-entropy aligning the predicted and target density distributions. Cumulative and multi-resolution terms enforce consistency across frequency scales, not just bin by bin. Decay and gain use slot-wise Huber losses masked to occupied slots.

Sum-preserving decoding (shared). The predicted total M̄ is rounded to M̃; per-bin density scores are rescaled to sum to M̃ and floored, and the leftover modes go to the bins with the largest fractional remainders (largest-remainder method). This guarantees non-negative integer counts summing exactly to M̃. Frequencies are placed deterministically inside each bin, and decay and gain are taken from the first N_k slots of that bin.

B1: U-Net (about 3.1 M parameters). Three input channels (row-normalised log magnitude, sin phase, cos phase) are mean-pooled into 1/120-octave bins. A strided CNN encodes the first 0.75 s of the IR into a 128-d vector, injected with log g_x into the bottleneck. Encoder widths 32/64/128/256, bottleneck 512; the bottleneck is averaged over frequency into two global heads for the total count and gain RMS scale b_RMS; the decoder outputs at 1/20 octave with 768 slots per bin. Gain is factorised into magnitude, sign (binary cross-entropy) and b_RMS. AdamW, learning rate 10⁻³, 26 epochs, about 24 hours on one A100.

B2: complex-valued Transformer (about 99.6 M real parameters). Phase-preserving log compression and per-IR RMS normalisation are applied to the full-resolution complex spectrum; a five-layer complex CNN tokenises it into 64 complex tokens, followed by a six-layer complex Transformer (dimension 320, 8 heads) and a 2,048-unit dense bottleneck feeding the heads. Most parameters sit in the bottleneck (83.9 M); the CNN and Transformer together have only 9.4 M. Gain is regressed directly as a signed value, without B1's factorisation. 7 epochs, about 27 hours on one A100.

限制Limitations

增益幾乎沒有解出來。 總誤差 RE = (RE_Ω + RE_σ + RE_b)/3 + min(1, ΔM/M)。B1 的增益誤差 RE_b 是 0.8525，除以 3 後約 0.284，佔總誤差 0.318 的將近九成；B2 的 0.9207 也佔了 0.330 的約九成三。傳統方法裡最好的 Yule–Walker 增益誤差是 0.9071，所以神經方法在增益上只好了大約 0.05。真正大幅領先的是頻率（B1 0.0077 對 Yule–Walker 0.1816）、衰減（B2 0.0159 對 Fast Burg 0.9038）與模態數（B1 誤差 1.43% 對 Fast Burg 42.87%）。作者在結論有承認「增益仍是主要誤差來源」，但標題與摘要強調的「總誤差比傳統方法低」主要是靠另外三項撐起來的。

測試集與訓練集同分布。 100 條比較用 IR 雖然是另外產生的，但用的是同一個參考模擬器、同樣的預設參數範圍，而網路正是在 32 萬條同模擬器的 IR 上訓練的。傳統方法則完全沒有針對這個模擬器調整，只用通用設定（32 頻帶、每帶 100 模態上限）。所以這個比較說明的是「在模擬器分布內，學習法大勝」，對真實板式殘響（例如實體 EMT 140 錄下的 IR）會怎樣，論文沒有測。

沒有任何消融。 時域 0.75 秒編碼、log g_x 條件、累積與多解析度計數損失、增益的符號／幅度拆解，這些設計各貢獻多少都沒有實驗。B1 與 B2 的差異也混了很多變因：骨幹不同、參數量差 30 倍、增益表示不同、訓練 epoch 數不同（26 對 7），所以「B2 衰減準三倍、增益差一點」無法歸因到複數值架構。

其他：延遲比較是 A100（0.001 秒）對 24 核 CPU（Matrix Pencil 17.4 秒），作者自己也說不可直接比；ESPRIT 的時間因為中途恢復執行只是近似值；論文沒有給 M 的典型大小，讀者不知道 1.43% 的計數誤差相當於差幾個模態。

Gain is barely solved. Overall error is RE = (RE_Ω + RE_σ + RE_b)/3 + min(1, ΔM/M). B1's gain error RE_b is 0.8525; divided by 3 that is about 0.284, nearly ninety percent of its total 0.318. B2's 0.9207 likewise accounts for about 93% of 0.330. The best classical gain error, Yule–Walker's, is 0.9071, so the neural methods improve gain by only about 0.05. The large leads are in frequency (B1 0.0077 versus Yule–Walker 0.1816), decay (B2 0.0159 versus Fast Burg 0.9038) and mode count (B1 1.43% error versus Fast Burg 42.87%). The authors admit in the conclusion that gain remains the dominant error, but the headline lower-overall-error claim rests mainly on the other three terms.

Test and training sets share a distribution. The 100 comparison IRs were generated separately, but by the same reference simulator with the same default parameter ranges, and the networks were trained on 327,680 IRs from that simulator. The classical methods were not tuned to the simulator at all and use generic settings (32 bands, cap of 100 modes per band). So the comparison shows that learning wins inside the simulator's distribution; how it fares on real plate reverbs, such as IRs recorded from a physical EMT 140, is untested.

There are no ablations. The contributions of the 0.75 s time-domain encoder, log g_x conditioning, cumulative and multi-resolution count losses, and the gain sign/magnitude factorisation are not measured. The B1-versus-B2 difference also mixes many factors: different backbones, a 30× parameter gap, different gain representations, and different epoch counts (26 versus 7), so B2's three-times-better decay and slightly worse gain cannot be attributed to the complex-valued architecture.

Others: latency compares an A100 (0.001 s) against a 24-core CPU (Matrix Pencil 17.4 s), which the authors say is not directly comparable; ESPRIT's timing is approximate because its run was resumed; the paper never gives a typical M, so readers cannot tell how many modes a 1.43% count error amounts to.

討論Discussion

這篇值得讀的是解碼方式，不是兩個骨幹。「預測密度 + 預測總數 + 最大餘數法取整」把數量未知的集合預測，變成固定形狀的回歸加一個保證總和正確的後處理，完全不需要匈牙利匹配。這招在 MIR 裡有很多可以搬過去的地方：多音高轉譜裡同一時間有幾個音、鼓譜裡一小段有幾下擊打、或樂器模態分析（鐘、鋼片琴、鑼）這種同樣要找出大量共振的問題。

它的弱點也很具體：頻率在格內是「決定性擺放」的，所以我推測頻率精度上限受 1/20 八度格寬限制，誤差低可能是因為模態夠密、平均下來誤差小。如果拿去處理模態稀疏、每格只有零或一個模態的樂器，格內位置就會成為主要誤差，那時可能還是得回到子空間法做局部精修。一個自然的混合做法是用計數密度網路決定「每個頻段有幾個模態」，再把這個數字交給 ESPRIT 當模型階數，正好補上傳統方法最弱的那一步。

增益的失敗也值得思考。頻率與衰減可以從頻譜峰的位置與寬度讀出，增益卻牽涉到同一格內上百個模態彼此干涉後的幅度與正負號，逐槽 Huber 損失很難學到。也許需要像作者先前可微分模態共振器那樣，把預測的模態合成回 IR 再用重建損失訓練，讓增益由整體波形來約束。

What is worth reading here is the decoding, not the two backbones. Predicting a density plus a total, then rounding with the largest-remainder method, turns unknown-size set prediction into fixed-shape regression plus a post-process that guarantees the right total, with no Hungarian matching. The trick transfers to plenty of MIR problems: how many notes sound at once in multi-pitch transcription, how many hits fall in a short drum segment, or modal analysis of instruments such as bells, glockenspiels and gongs, which likewise needs many resonances recovered.

Its weakness is concrete too: frequencies are placed deterministically inside each bin, so I suspect frequency precision is bounded by the 1/20-octave bin width and the error stays low on average possibly because modes are dense. On instruments with sparse modes, where each bin holds zero or one mode, the within-bin position would become the main error, and a subspace method might still be needed for local refinement. A natural hybrid is to let the count-density network decide how many modes each band holds and hand that number to ESPRIT as the model order, filling exactly the step where classical methods are weakest.

The gain failure is also instructive. Frequency and decay can be read from the position and width of spectral peaks, but gain involves the amplitudes and signs of hundreds of modes interfering within one bin, which slot-wise Huber losses struggle to learn. It may need what the authors' earlier differentiable modal resonators did: synthesise the predicted modes back into an IR and train with a reconstruction loss, so gain is constrained by the whole waveform.
