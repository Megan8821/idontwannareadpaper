Randomized Scores and Diverse Timbres: Augmenting Automatic Music Transcription with Online-Generated Data

把合成採譜資料拆成「樂譜結構」與「音色覆蓋」兩個旋鈕:部分打亂音符無妨,音色多樣才是遷移關鍵

Haiwen Xia, Chao Zhang, Qiuqiang Kong
arXiv:2610.11197 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-08
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇把合成採譜資料拆成兩個獨立旋鈕,發現部分打亂 MIDI 片段裡的音符代價很小(MAPS 上甚至從 64.99 升到 68.28 onset F1),而把鋼琴預設音色從 11 個擴到 285 個,MAPS 從 59.40 升到 75.70。但在強離線資料混合下,線上資料最多只多約 1.4 分;音符完全隨機時,MulTTiPop 仍掉了 10 分。Why this one: Splitting synthetic AMT data into two independent knobs, this paper finds that partly scrambling the notes of a MIDI clip costs little (on MAPS it even helps, 64.99 to 68.28 onset F1), while widening the piano presets from 11 to 285 lifts MAPS from 59.40 to 75.70. But on top of a strong offline mixture, online data adds at most about 1.4 points, and fully random notes still lose 10 points on MulTTiPop.

動機Motivation

自動採譜(automatic music transcription, AMT)的瓶頸一直是資料:要有精確到每個音符起訖的標註,而人工標註多聲部音樂非常昂貴,所以現有資料集在樂器、錄音條件與曲風上的覆蓋都很窄。用 MIDI 合成音訊是最常見的解法,因為標籤是免費的。

但合成資料到底靠什麼讓模型在真實錄音上變好,一直沒有被拆開來看:是因為合成的樂譜像真的音樂(和聲、節奏、聲部之間的相依),還是因為渲染時用了夠多種音色?既有做法通常讓兩者一起變——換一份 MIDI 也換了一組音色,混音增強同時改變複音數與音色。作者想把這兩個因素分開控制,回答「設計合成採譜資料時,預算該花在哪裡」。

The bottleneck of automatic music transcription (AMT) has always been data: it needs note-accurate onset and offset labels, annotating polyphonic music by hand is very expensive, and existing datasets therefore cover a narrow range of instruments, recording conditions and genres. Rendering audio from MIDI is the usual remedy, because the labels come for free.

What has not been separated is why synthetic data helps on real recordings. Is it because the rendered scores resemble real music (harmony, rhythm, dependence between voices), or because rendering used enough different timbres? Existing recipes usually change both at once: a new MIDI file brings a new set of timbres, and mixing augmentation changes polyphony and timbre together. The authors want to control the two factors separately and answer where the budget for synthetic AMT data should go.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

渲染路線的代表是 Slakh(Manilow 等 2019),用 Lakh MIDI 配取樣音源渲染出兩千多首多軌資料,保留完整樂譜;SynthTab(Zang 等 2024)把同樣的做法用到吉他六線譜。增強路線則重組既有的音訊與標籤:Simon 等(2022)把單音軌轉錄混成多聲部訓練資料,YourMT3+(Chang 等 2024)在不同資料集之間做跨曲目的音軌混合。後者已經暗示「編曲不必真實」,但每條音軌內部的旋律結構仍是原樣。

跟本文最接近的是 Sato 與 Akama(2024):同樣用 Lakh 的樂譜配 NSynth 的單音取樣來渲染,論文自己也承認。差別在於本文把「樂譜要多真」做成連續可調的變數(破壞比例 ρ 與內插強度 s),而且資料在訓練時線上即時生成、每筆只用一次。

值得點明的是,「用隨機音符訓練或評估多音高模型」並不新:MAPS 資料集本身就收錄了隨機組合音高的和弦(RAND)子集。更廣義地看,本文的做法就是機器人領域的領域隨機化(domain randomization):刻意打亂與任務無關的因素,逼模型只學該學的東西。本文的貢獻是在現代序列到序列採譜器上,把這個直覺量化成可對照的消融。

Slakh (Manilow et al. 2019) represents the rendering route: Lakh MIDI rendered with sampled instruments into some two thousand multitrack songs, with complete scores retained; SynthTab (Zang et al. 2024) applies the same idea to guitar tablature. The augmentation route recombines existing audio and labels: Simon et al. (2022) mix monophonic transcriptions into polyphonic training data, and YourMT3+ (Chang et al. 2024) mixes stems across songs and datasets. The latter already hints that arrangements need not be realistic, but the melodic structure within each stem stays intact.

The closest work is Sato and Akama (2024), which also renders Lakh scores with isolated NSynth notes, as the paper acknowledges. The difference is that here 'how realistic the score is' becomes a continuous knob (corruption rate ρ and interpolation strength s), and data are generated online during training, each pair used once.

Worth stating plainly: training or evaluating multi-pitch models on random notes is not new; the MAPS dataset itself contains a subset of randomly composed chords (RAND). More broadly, the approach is domain randomization from robotics: deliberately scramble factors irrelevant to the task so the model learns only what it should. The contribution is to quantify that intuition as controlled ablations on a modern sequence-to-sequence transcriber.

方法Method

取樣器:從 MIDI 片段取出音符,每個音符有樂器、音高、起點、終點、力度五個屬性。先定義「因子化」分布 r(e):保留樂器,音高與時長從來源語料 2.048 秒片段的經驗邊際分布抽,起點與力度在可行範圍內均勻抽(時長不均勻抽,因為太多長音會讓問題病態)。每個片段抽一個破壞比例 ρ,每個音符以機率 ρ 換成 r(e) 的一個樣本 ẽ,最後輸出 e' = (1−s)e + s·ẽ,音高再四捨五入到合法 MIDI 值。ρ=0 是原樣(Source),ρ~U[0,1]、s>0 是部分破壞(Corrupted),ρ=s=1 是每個音符獨立重抽(Note-factorized)。

渲染器有兩種。音符級渲染把每個音符對應到相容的 NSynth 錄音,偵測發聲區段並對齊到目標起點,縮短時加平滑衰減,可平行處理。音軌級渲染把整條 MIDI 音軌送進 Pianoteq 免費版並隨機抽一個預設音色;免費版有 797 個預設、25 個樂器群,但缺 8 個固定音高,落在這些音高上的音符改標成同音級最近可彈的八度。每個 Pianoteq 預設同時改變弦齊奏寬度、共鳴、琴槌硬度等物理模型參數,所以抽一個預設等於一次改動多個音色面向。

模型:卷積前端只在頻率軸池化,接時間軸 Transformer 編碼器,線性投影後切成 C=13 條樂器通道(沿用 YourMT3+ 的分類),每條通道由權重共享的自回歸解碼器輸出事件 token;給定音訊時通道之間條件獨立、平行解碼。與 YourMT3+ 的差別是拿掉 Perceiver-TF 編碼器,換成標準 Transformer(RoPE、RMSNorm)。

實驗:輸入 2.048 秒、16 kHz、128 mel、8 ms hop。實驗 1–3 用約 60M 參數的 Base 模型訓練 200k 步,實驗 4 用約 190M 的 Large 訓練 320k 步。指標為 50 ms 容忍度的 onset F1,分不看樂器(Agn)與看樂器(Prog)兩種,鼓不計(兩個渲染器都不產生鼓聲)。實驗 1 固定 NSynth 渲染器比較取樣器;實驗 2 固定 MAESTRO 片段比較 Pianoteq 預設覆蓋(11、57、285 個平台鋼琴預設,再擴到 program 0–7);實驗 3 比較只用 Slakh、只用線上、兩者每批各半、先線上後 Slakh 四種策略;實驗 4 在八個資料集組成的強離線混合上加線上資料,並與 YourMT3+、MuScriptor-Large 比較。

Sampler: notes from a MIDI clip each carry program, pitch, onset, offset and velocity. A factorized distribution r(e) keeps the program, draws pitch and duration from empirical marginals over 2.048 s segments of the source corpus, and draws onset and velocity uniformly over their feasible ranges (duration is not uniform, since too many long notes would make the task ill-posed). Each clip gets a corruption rate ρ; each note is replaced by a draw ẽ from r(e) with probability ρ, and the output is e' = (1−s)e + s·ẽ, with pitch rounded to a valid MIDI value. ρ=0 is Source, ρ~U[0,1] with s>0 is Corrupted, and ρ=s=1 is Note-factorized.

Two renderers. The note renderer maps each note to a compatible NSynth recording, detects its sounding region, aligns it to the target onset and applies a smooth decay when shortening, all in parallel. The stem renderer sends a whole MIDI stem to the free Pianoteq and samples one preset; the free version has 797 presets in 25 instrument groups but lacks 8 fixed pitches, so affected notes are relabelled to the nearest playable octave of the same pitch class. Each preset sets coupled physical-model parameters such as unison width, sympathetic resonance and hammer hardness, so sampling a preset changes several aspects of timbre at once.

Model: a convolutional front end that pools only along frequency feeds a temporal Transformer encoder; a linear projection splits the result into C=13 instrument channels (YourMT3+'s classes), each decoded into event tokens by an autoregressive decoder with shared weights. Given the audio, channels are conditionally independent and decoded in parallel. The change from YourMT3+ is replacing its Perceiver-TF encoder with a standard Transformer (RoPE, RMSNorm).

Experiments: 2.048 s, 16 kHz inputs, 128 mel bins, 8 ms hop. Experiments 1–3 train the ~60M Base model for 200k steps; Experiment 4 trains the ~190M Large for 320k. The metric is onset F1 at 50 ms, instrument-agnostic (Agn) or program-aware (Prog), drums excluded since neither renderer produces them. Experiment 1 fixes the NSynth renderer and compares samplers; Experiment 2 fixes MAESTRO clips and varies Pianoteq coverage (11, 57 and 285 grand-piano presets, then programs 0–7); Experiment 3 compares Slakh only, online only, half-and-half batches, and online-then-Slakh; Experiment 4 adds online data to a strong eight-dataset offline mixture and compares with YourMT3+ and MuScriptor-Large.

限制Limitations

「樂譜結構大致不必要」這句話比表格說得重。部分破壞確實沒傷到多樂器集(Corrupted s=0.5 在 RWC 36.70、Slakh 50.30,與 Source 的 36.41、49.55 相當),MAPS 還從 64.99 升到 68.28;但 MulTTiPop 上 Source 仍最好(22.92 對 21.53),而完全因子化讓 MulTTiPop 掉 10.36(到 12.56)、RWC 掉 8.20、Slakh 掉 12.72。比較準確的說法是「部分破壞無妨,全部打散代價很大」:需要的不是完整的樂譜結構,而是保留一部分真實的音符共現。

強離線混合下的收益很小且方向不一。+NSynth 只在 MulTTiPop 上 +0.76(Agn)/+1.35(Prog),在 MAPS 與 RWC 小幅退步;+Mixed 在 MAPS +0.41、RWC +1.32/+0.96,卻讓 MulTTiPop Agn 降 0.43。所有表格都是單次訓練,沒有種子變異或信賴區間,零點幾分的差距無法和隨機波動分開;作者在第 4.6 節也承認收益「modest and domain dependent」。

音色覆蓋實驗只有單一樂器(鋼琴)、batch 8、每個覆蓋等級只抽一組固定種子的預設子集,所以「單調上升」是四個點的趨勢;而且 4% 只有 11 個預設,從 59.40 跳到 75.70 的大半可能反映的是「太少」而非「越多越好」。另外,2.048 秒的輸入窗本來就裝不下多少樂句或和聲進行,短視窗下樂譜結構不重要的結論,未必能推到長上下文的採譜器上。論文也沒說明 MAPS 用的是哪個子集。

'Realistic score structure is largely unnecessary' says more than the table does. Partial corruption indeed leaves the multi-instrument sets intact (Corrupted s=0.5: RWC 36.70, Slakh 50.30, against Source's 36.41 and 49.55) and lifts MAPS from 64.99 to 68.28. But Source is still best on MulTTiPop (22.92 vs 21.53), and full factorization costs 10.36 on MulTTiPop (down to 12.56), 8.20 on RWC and 12.72 on Slakh. A more accurate reading is 'partial corruption is harmless, full scrambling is costly': what is needed is not complete score structure but some real note co-occurrence.

On a strong offline mixture the gains are small and point in different directions. +NSynth helps only MulTTiPop, +0.76 (Agn) and +1.35 (Prog), and slips slightly on MAPS and RWC; +Mixed adds +0.41 on MAPS and +1.32/+0.96 on RWC but loses 0.43 MulTTiPop Agn. Every table reports a single training run with no seed variance or confidence intervals, so differences of a few tenths cannot be told apart from noise; Section 4.6 itself calls the gains 'modest and domain dependent'.

The timbre experiment covers one instrument (piano), uses batch size 8, and draws one fixed-seed preset subset per coverage level, so 'monotonic' is a four-point trend. With only 11 presets at 4%, much of the jump from 59.40 to 75.70 may reflect 'too few' rather than 'more is better'. Also, a 2.048 s input window holds little phrasing or harmonic progression to begin with; that score structure matters little under short windows need not carry over to long-context transcribers. The paper does not say which MAPS subset it uses.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值在於把常被一起改動的兩個變數拆開,而結論很有實用性:做合成採譜資料時,比起找更「像音樂」的 MIDI,更值得投資的是音色覆蓋。表 2 最醒目的一點是,只用 Pianoteq 渲染、覆蓋 program 0–7 的模型在 MAPS 上得到 78.13,略高於用真實 MAESTRO 錄音訓練的 77.14(差 0.99),而後者在 MAESTRO 自己的測試集上是 94.87。換言之,真實資料的優勢主要在同分布,跨鋼琴的遷移靠音色多樣性就能追上。

實驗 3 也有一個值得記住的落差:只用線上資料的模型在 Slakh 上比只用 Slakh 低 26.06(49.78 對 75.84),但在三個外部集上的差距都在 1 分以內。這說明同分布測試集會嚴重高估真實資料的價值,也提醒讀者:拿 Slakh 測試分數比較合成資料方案,幾乎說明不了遷移能力。

架構部分是附帶結果:離線版本在 MAPS(86.77 對 85.83)和 MulTTiPop(23.28 對 19.15)上高於 YourMT3+,但 RWC 低了 3.42(Agn)與 4.58(Prog),所以「Perceiver-TF 不是必要的」只成立了一半。和 MuScriptor-Large 比,MulTTiPop 上最好的 24.04 仍遠落後 39.06,作者歸因於後者約 11,000 小時的訓練量。下一個合理的問題是:把線上渲染接到這種規模的模型上,音色覆蓋的收益會不會被規模吃掉。

The value of the paper is in separating two variables that are usually changed together, and the conclusion is practical: for synthetic AMT data, timbral coverage is a better investment than more 'musical' MIDI. The most striking point in Table 2 is that a model trained only on Pianoteq renderings covering programs 0–7 reaches 78.13 on MAPS, slightly above the 77.14 of training on real MAESTRO recordings (a 0.99 gap), even though the latter scores 94.87 on MAESTRO's own test set. In other words, real data's advantage is mostly in-distribution, and cross-piano transfer can be matched with timbral diversity.

Experiment 3 contains a gap worth remembering: the online-only model is 26.06 below Slakh-only training on Slakh (49.78 vs 75.84), yet within one point on all three external sets. In-distribution test sets therefore badly overstate the value of real data, and Slakh test scores say almost nothing about how well a synthetic-data recipe transfers.

The architecture result is a side note. The offline model beats YourMT3+ on MAPS (86.77 vs 85.83) and MulTTiPop (23.28 vs 19.15) but trails on RWC by 3.42 (Agn) and 4.58 (Prog), so 'Perceiver-TF is not essential' holds only halfway. Against MuScriptor-Large, the best MulTTiPop score of 24.04 is far below 39.06, which the authors attribute to the latter's roughly 11,000 hours of training audio. The natural next question is whether timbral-coverage gains survive when online rendering is attached to a model at that scale.

STEMMA: Song-to-Stem Multi-Audio Reasoning for Large Audio Language Models

STEMMA:先指定「歌曲—段落—分軌」的出處關係再抽音訊,訓練音訊語言模型做多段音樂比較推理

Hoyeol Sohn, Wonil Kim, Keunhyoung Kim, Sangeun Kum, Taehyoung Kim, Dongjoo Moon, Theerasak Charoenchob, Teeratep Weerapang, Jongpil Lee, Juhan Nam
arXiv:2610.11884 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-08
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：STEMMA 不比較互不相干的曲目,而是用已知的製作出處(是否同一首歌、是否相鄰段落、哪條分軌屬於哪個混音)建構多音訊音樂問答,並以「關係先行」的方式查詢目錄。微調後 Music Flamingo 的結構關係題從 0.2205 升到 0.8189,單音訊分數沒有下降;但屬性關係題(調性、速度、標籤)最高只有 0.3526,機率值是 0.25,而且結構題沒有報告單純音訊嵌入的基線。Why this one: Instead of comparing unrelated tracks, STEMMA builds multi-audio music QA from known production provenance (same song, adjacent section, which stem belongs to which mix), querying a catalog relation-first. Fine-tuning lifts Music Flamingo's structural-relation accuracy from 0.2205 to 0.8189 with no drop in single-audio scores; but attribute relations (key, tempo, tags) top out at 0.3526 against 0.25 chance, and no plain audio-embedding baseline is reported for the structural tasks.

動機Motivation

音樂專用的音訊語言模型(large audio-language model, LALM)如 LLark、Music Flamingo,幾乎都是一次只聽一段錄音。但取樣搜尋、編曲、從混音裡找素材這類應用,問的是錄音之間的關係:這兩段是不是同一首歌?這段後面接的是哪一段?這條鼓組是不是從這個混音裡分出來的?回答這些問題需要「跨音訊對位」:先在每段輸入裡找到證據,才能推論它們之間的關係。

既有的多音訊資料集大多比較兩首獨立抽出的曲目,彼此沒有任何製作上的關聯,所以只能問屬性比較(誰比較快、誰的樂器比較多),問不了結構關係。作者的出發點是:音樂製作資料本來就帶著「歌曲—段落—分軌」的階層出處,這些關係的答案是確定的,不需要語言模型去生成標籤。

Music-specialised large audio-language models (LALMs) such as LLark and Music Flamingo nearly all listen to one recording at a time. Yet applications like sample search, arrangement and finding material inside a mix ask about relations between recordings: are these two excerpts from the same song, which section follows this one, was this drum stem separated from this mix? Answering requires cross-audio grounding: evidence must be located in each input before a relation across inputs can be inferred.

Existing multi-audio datasets mostly compare two independently sampled tracks with no production relationship, so they can only ask attribute comparisons (which is faster, which has more instruments), not structural ones. The authors' starting point is that music production data already carry a song–section–stem hierarchy of provenance, and the answers to these relations are determined by it, with no need for a language model to generate labels.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

單音訊方面,LLark(Gardner 等 2024)把開源標註統一成指令格式;Music Flamingo(Ghosh 等 2026)用大規模合成描述擴展到和聲、結構與音色;MOSS-Music 是類似流程的開源模型。這些都只在單段錄音上訓練與評估。多音訊方面,Beyond Single-Audio(Chen 等 2024)證明針對雙音訊微調不會明顯犧牲單音訊能力,PolyAudio 擴展到交錯的多音訊脈絡,MUGEN 則發現候選音訊越多表現越差、瓶頸在聲學比較。

最直接的對照是 Jamendo-MT-QA(Koh 等 2026):抽兩首完整曲目,用 LLM 根據各自的描述與後設資料生成比較題。因為兩首歌沒有出處關係,題目只能比屬性;STEMMA 改從同一首歌抽段落與分軌,才問得出結構題。

要點破的是,「同一首歌裡取出的兩段當正例、別首歌當負例」正是音樂自監督對比學習最常見的配對方式(例如 CLMR,Spijkervet 與 Burgoyne 2021);「哪條分軌屬於這個混音」則正是 Riou 等人多軌對比式取樣辨識(2026)與 JEPA 分軌檢索(2025)處理的問題,而這兩篇本文都有引用。STEMMA 的新意在於把這套正例與困難負例的構造搬進自然語言問答格式,當作 LALM 的指令資料,而不是新的關係定義。

On the single-audio side, LLark (Gardner et al. 2024) unifies open-source annotations into an instruction format; Music Flamingo (Ghosh et al. 2026) scales it with synthetic captions covering harmony, structure and timbre; MOSS-Music is an open model trained with a similar pipeline. All are trained and evaluated one recording at a time. On the multi-audio side, Beyond Single-Audio (Chen et al. 2024) shows two-audio fine-tuning need not sacrifice single-audio ability, PolyAudio extends to interleaved multi-audio contexts, and MUGEN finds performance falls as candidates increase, with acoustic comparison the bottleneck.

The most direct comparison is Jamendo-MT-QA (Koh et al. 2026), which samples two full tracks and has an LLM write comparative questions from their captions and metadata. With no shared provenance, the questions can only compare attributes; STEMMA draws sections and stems from the same song, which makes structural questions possible.

Worth stating plainly: 'two excerpts of one song are a positive, another song is a negative' is the most common pairing in contrastive self-supervised music learning (e.g. CLMR, Spijkervet and Burgoyne 2021), and 'which stem belongs to this mix' is exactly the problem of Riou et al.'s multi-track contrastive sample identification (2026) and JEPA stem retrieval (2025), both cited here. STEMMA's novelty is moving that positive and hard-negative construction into natural-language QA as instruction data for LALMs, not a new definition of the relations.

方法Method

目錄:每首歌切成有標籤的段落,每段再分離成各個樂器角色。每一列是一個片段(整段混音稱為 section,單一分軌稱為 section stem),存四個出處欄位(曲目、段落序號、段落類別、分軌角色)與速度、調性、樂器、曲風等屬性。內部的 15,856 首授權曲目帶有製作端標註;兩個公開語料(JamendoMaxCaps 69,297 首、FMA 可商用子集 12,119 首)則全部用工具估計:SongFormer 切段、SCNet 分離、Essentia 估調性與速度、Discogs-EffNet 打曲風與樂器標籤。

關係先行:隨機抽兩段,同曲目的機率只有 O(1/N),同段落更低,抽樣會浪費絕大多數樣本。所以先寫下關係,再用一條 SQL 查詢同時撈出符合的正例與「保留部分脈絡但違反關係」的困難負例。關係由八種運算子(same/different、close/far、overlap/no_overlap、greater/less)組成,共九種:結構類四種(同曲目、相鄰段落、同段落不同分軌、母子——哪條分軌包含在這段混音裡),屬性類五種(同調、速度相近(容忍 3 BPM)、速度較快、樂器標籤至少重疊兩個、曲風標籤至少重疊兩個)。題型分成雙音訊是非或短答,以及一個錨點加四個候選的選擇題。

訓練資料 STEMMA-Instruct 共 194,544 筆,一半是上述關係題(97,272 筆),另一半是指代(coreference)題:把兩到四首完整歌曲各自標成 Audio A、B 等,目標答案把每首歌的單音訊描述放到正確標籤下,教模型把語言裡的代號對到正確的輸入。兩個骨幹(Music Flamingo、MOSS-Music)在兩張 B200 上全參數微調一個 epoch。

評估用 STEMMA-Bench:同樣流程,但改用 MUSDB18、MedleyDB、MoisesDB(分軌是錄製時的原始分軌而非分離結果,語料與訓練不重疊),共 300 題(每個語料 100 題、481 首歌),其中結構題 127、屬性題 173,全部人工檢查,不合理的題目在同一分層下替換。另外報告 MUGEN 音樂分析、Jamendo-MT-QA 的 2,010 題短答,以及四個單音訊基準(MMAU-Pro、MMAU、MMAR 的音樂子集與 RUL-MuChoMusic)。對照組包括直接問答系統,以及「各段先各自描述、再交給 Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 推理」的串接系統。

Catalog: each song is cut into labelled sections, and each section is separated into instrument roles. Each row is one excerpt (a full-section mix is a section, a single separated role a section stem) storing four provenance fields (track, section index, section category, stem role) plus tempo, key, instruments and genres. An in-house set of 15,856 licensed songs carries production metadata; the two public corpora (69,297 JamendoMaxCaps songs and 12,119 from a commercially reusable FMA subset) have every field estimated: SongFormer for sections, SCNet for stems, Essentia for key and tempo, Discogs-EffNet for genre and instrument tags.

Relation first: two random excerpts share a track with probability O(1/N), and a section even more rarely, so sampling wastes most draws. Instead a relation is written first, and one SQL query retrieves matching positives and hard negatives that keep some context but violate the relation. Relations combine eight operators (same/different, close/far, overlap/no_overlap, greater/less) into nine: four structural (same track, adjacent section, same section with a different stem, and parent–child, i.e. which stem is contained in this mix) and five attribute (same key, close tempo within 3 BPM, faster tempo, at least two shared instrument tags, at least two shared genre tags). Formats are two-audio yes/no or short answer, and an anchor with four audio candidates as multiple choice.

The training set, STEMMA-Instruct, has 194,544 instances: half are relation questions (97,272), half are coreference questions, where two to four full songs are labelled Audio A, B and so on and the target places each song's single-audio description under the right label, teaching the model to map in-text references to the correct input. Both backbones (Music Flamingo and MOSS-Music) are fully fine-tuned for one epoch on two B200 GPUs.

Evaluation uses STEMMA-Bench, built the same way from MUSDB18, MedleyDB and MoisesDB (authored rather than separated stems, no corpus overlap with training): 300 questions (100 per corpus, 481 songs), 127 structural and 173 attribute, all checked by hand, with implausible items replaced within the same stratum. Also reported are MUGEN Music Analysis, 2,010 short-answer Jamendo-MT-QA items, and four single-audio benchmarks (the music parts of MMAU-Pro, MMAU and MMAR, plus RUL-MuChoMusic). Baselines include direct QA systems and cascades in which each input is captioned separately and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B reasons over the captions.

限制Limitations

起點效應:Music Flamingo 的大幅進步,有一部分可能只是學會答題格式。它微調前在結構題 0.2205、屬性題 0.1734,都低於四選一的機率值 0.25;單音訊的 RUL-MuChoMusic 也只有 0.2207。論文規定無法解析的回答算錯,但沒有報告各系統的解析失敗率。作者說「起點越低的骨幹進步越多」,這個現象跟「越不守格式的骨幹,從同格式的訓練資料得到越多」分不開。MOSS-Music 起點較高,結構題進步 23.6 分、屬性題只有 0.6 分,可能才比較接近真實的能力增量。

缺少非語言模型的基線:同曲目、同段落、母子這幾種結構關係本質上是檢索問題,用音訊嵌入的餘弦相似度,甚至波形互相關(母子關係中分軌是混音的加法成分),都可能解得很好。論文引用了 Riou 等人的取樣辨識與分軌檢索,卻沒有拿這類系統當基線,所以讀者無法判斷 0.8189 是「推理」,還是 LALM 學會了一個檢索器本來就能做的事。訓練題與測試題由同一套模板生成,格式與題型也完全同分布。

屬性題的天花板不明:屬性題最高只有 0.3526,多數系統在機率值附近或以下。訓練用的屬性全部由 Essentia 與 Discogs-EffNet 估計,相差 3 BPM、單一調性標籤這種細節,正是這些工具常出錯的地方(例如速度的倍頻錯誤);論文沒說明測試集屬性標籤的來源與誤差,無法分辨是模型聽不出來,還是標籤本身有雜訊。此外結構題只有 127 題,每題約佔 0.79 分;內部的 15,856 首曲目也不公開。

Starting-point effects: part of Music Flamingo's large gain may simply be learning the answer format. Before tuning it scores 0.2205 on structural and 0.1734 on attribute relations, both below the four-way chance of 0.25, and only 0.2207 on single-audio RUL-MuChoMusic. Unparseable answers count as wrong, but parse-failure rates are not reported. The authors note that weaker backbones gain more; that cannot be separated from 'backbones that follow the format less gain more from same-format training data'. MOSS-Music starts higher and gains 23.6 on structural but only 0.6 on attribute relations, which may be closer to the real capability gain.

No non-LLM baseline: same track, same section and parent–child are at heart retrieval problems that cosine similarity between audio embeddings, or even waveform cross-correlation (in parent–child the stem is an additive component of the mix), might solve well. The paper cites Riou et al.'s sample identification and stem retrieval but does not use such systems as baselines, so readers cannot tell whether 0.8189 is 'reasoning' or an LALM learning what a retriever already does. Training and test questions also come from the same templates, so format and question type are fully in-distribution.

The ceiling on attribute relations is unclear: the best is 0.3526 and most systems sit near or below chance. Training attributes are all estimated by Essentia and Discogs-EffNet, and a 3 BPM difference or a single key label is exactly where such tools err (tempo octave errors, for instance); the paper does not state the source or error of the test-set attribute labels, so model deafness cannot be told apart from label noise. Structural questions number only 127, about 0.79 points each, and the 15,856 in-house songs are not released.

討論Discussion

這篇最有說服力的證據來自串接系統的對照。在 Jamendo-MT-QA 上,最好的系統是串接(Music Flamingo 描述 → Qwen3.6,0.8716),高於最好的微調模型 0.8622;但在 STEMMA-Bench 結構題上順序反轉,最好的微調模型 0.8189 對最好的串接 0.5039,微調後的 Music Flamingo 比它自己的串接高 33.9 分。這把一件事講得很清楚:比較兩首無關的曲目,大多可以靠各自的文字描述回答;同源片段之間的關係則不行,文字描述丟掉了需要的聲學細節。

消融也有一個有趣的結果:兩種資料加在一起的效果大於分開效果的總和。Music Flamingo 的多音訊平均 +28.24,而單用指代 +5.55、單用關係 +18.29,合計 23.84;MOSS-Music 是 +6.88,對 3.50 加 0.84 的 4.34。作者的解讀是兩者教的東西不同:指代教模型把多個輸入分開,關係題教它們之間的關聯。單音訊方面,所有條件下的平均都高於預訓練模型,沒有看到遺忘。

另一個值得注意的是,預訓練系統中結構題最強的是不專攻音樂的 Qwen2.5-Omni(0.5354),高於 MOSS-Music 的 0.3543 和 Music Flamingo 的 0.2205,暗示原生支援多段音訊輸入比音樂專門預訓練更重要。對做取樣辨識或分軌檢索的人來說,STEMMA 的關係先行目錄是可以直接拿來產生評測資料的工具;但要宣稱 LALM 在這類任務上有價值,下一步必須跟專門的嵌入檢索器正面比較。

The most convincing evidence comes from the cascade comparison. On Jamendo-MT-QA the best system is a cascade (Music Flamingo captions into Qwen3.6, 0.8716), above the best tuned model's 0.8622; on STEMMA-Bench's structural relations the order flips, with the best tuned model at 0.8189 against the best cascade's 0.5039, and tuned Music Flamingo 33.9 points above its own cascade. This makes one thing clear: comparisons between unrelated tracks can mostly be answered from per-track text descriptions; relations between excerpts of the same source cannot, because the captions drop the acoustic detail needed.

The ablation has an interesting result: the two data roles together beat the sum of their separate effects. Music Flamingo's multi-audio average rises +28.24, against +5.55 for coreference alone and +18.29 for relations alone, 23.84 combined; MOSS-Music gains +6.88 against 3.50 plus 0.84, or 4.34. The authors read this as the two teaching different things: coreference teaches keeping inputs apart, relation questions teach what links them. Single-audio averages stay above the pretrained models in every condition, so no forgetting is visible.

Also notable: the strongest pretrained system on structural relations is Qwen2.5-Omni (0.5354), which is not music-specialised, ahead of MOSS-Music (0.3543) and Music Flamingo (0.2205), suggesting native multi-audio input matters more than music-specific pretraining. For people working on sample identification or stem retrieval, STEMMA's relation-first catalog is a ready tool for generating evaluation data; but claiming LALMs add value on these tasks requires, as a next step, a head-to-head comparison with dedicated embedding retrievers.

Reference-Free Singing Pitch Correction via Music-Constrained Sequence Editing

免參考旋律的歌聲音準修正:把改音寫成「要不要改、改幾個半音」的序列編輯,再用調性先驗重排

Biao Dong, Jiajun Li, Binzhen Zhu, Mingwei Yi, Tong Liu, Yuanhao Zhang, Jiqing Han, Yongjun He
arXiv:2610.11524 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-08

為什麼挑這篇：只靠清唱本身、沒有目標旋律也沒有伴奏,這個系統用凍結的 MusicBERT 加 adapter 逐音決定要不要改、改幾個半音,再以軟性的調性先驗重排候選。在 80 首真實業餘/專業成對歌曲上,音符準確率從 73.25% 升到 83.43%(BERT-APC 為 79.44%);但它改壞 3.19% 的正確音,BERT-APC 只有 1.11%,而且評估資料不公開。Why this one: From a cappella singing alone, with no target melody or accompaniment, this system uses frozen MusicBERT with adapters to decide per note whether to correct and by how many semitones, then reranks candidates with a soft key prior. On 80 real amateur/professional song pairs it lifts note accuracy from 73.25% to 83.43% (BERT-APC: 79.44%); but it damages 3.19% of correct notes against BERT-APC's 1.11%, and the evaluation data are private.

動機Motivation

歌聲音準修正在音樂製作、K 歌平台和 AI 輔助創作裡都很常見,但現有方法幾乎都需要「參考」:目標旋律、伴奏、專業歌手的模板音高曲線,或至少要人手設定調性與音階。而很多實際情境只有人聲本身——清唱、隨口哼的旋律草稿、沒有工程檔的舊錄音。

免參考的修正跟音高估計不一樣:不只要知道唱了什麼音,還要判斷每個音該不該改。大部分的音其實是對的,錯誤很稀疏,所以整條旋律重新預測會不必要地改動正確的音。作者因此把問題寫成音符層級的序列編輯:對每個音決定「保留」或「改幾個半音」,依據只有輸入演唱本身的旋律脈絡與調性。

Singing pitch correction is common in music production, karaoke platforms and AI-assisted creation, but existing methods nearly all need a reference: a target melody, the accompaniment, a professional singer's template pitch curve, or at least a key and scale set by hand. Many real situations offer only the voice: a cappella takes, hummed melody sketches, old recordings without project files.

Reference-free correction differs from pitch estimation: besides knowing which note was sung, it must decide whether each note should change. Most notes are in fact right and errors are sparse, so re-predicting the whole melody would needlessly alter correct notes. The authors therefore cast the problem as note-level sequence editing: for each note, decide 'keep' or 'shift by so many semitones', using only the melodic context and tonality of the input performance.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

商用工具 Auto-Tune 與 Melodyne 靠使用者指定的音階或音符做吸附。學術系統也都依賴外部資訊:Deep Autotuner(Wager 等 2020)從人聲與伴奏的頻譜預測每個音的移調量;KaraTuner 以樂譜音符為條件;Neural Singing Voice Beautifier(NSVB)把業餘演唱對齊到專業模板;Diff-Pitcher 等擴散與 CVAE 模型則以樂譜或 MIDI 為條件。本站 9 月 25 日讀過的 MIDIBack 也屬於這一類,把伴奏的符號音符一起編碼。

最接近的是 BERT-APC(Kim 等 2026):同樣免參考,從演唱音符的脈絡推論正確音高。本文的差別在輸出形式:BERT-APC 直接預測目標音高,本文拆成「要不要改」與「改多少」兩個頭,再加一層調性重排。作者群與本站 9 月 24 日讀過的 SRF-SVB 是同一個團隊。

把機制拆開看,兩個主要元件都有很明確的前身。雙頭的因子化分布 P(Δ=0)=1−a、P(Δ=d)=a·b(d),就是文字編輯模型 LaserTagger 與 GECToR「先標 KEEP/EDIT、再決定怎麼改」的做法(論文有引用),在統計上等同零膨脹(zero-inflated)的類別分布。調性重排則是 ASR 裡的淺層融合(shallow fusion):在模型的對數機率上加一個外部先驗,只是這個「語言模型」是 Krumhansl–Schmuckler 的調性輪廓——也就是把 Auto-Tune 的音階吸附變成可加權的軟約束。

Commercial tools Auto-Tune and Melodyne snap pitch to a user-specified scale or notes. Academic systems also rely on outside information: Deep Autotuner (Wager et al. 2020) predicts per-note shifts from vocal and accompaniment spectrograms; KaraTuner conditions on score notes; the Neural Singing Voice Beautifier (NSVB) aligns an amateur take to a professional template; Diff-Pitcher and other diffusion and CVAE models condition on scores or MIDI. MIDIBack, read here on 25 September, belongs to this group too, encoding the accompaniment's symbolic notes alongside the voice.

The closest work is BERT-APC (Kim et al. 2026), also reference-free, inferring correct pitches from the context of the sung notes. The difference is the output form: BERT-APC predicts target pitches directly, while this paper splits the decision into 'whether to change' and 'by how much' heads, then adds tonal reranking. The authors are the team behind SRF-SVB, read here on 24 September.

Taken apart, both main components have clear ancestors. The factorized two-head distribution P(Δ=0)=1−a, P(Δ=d)=a·b(d) is the 'tag KEEP/EDIT first, then decide the edit' scheme of the text-editing models LaserTagger and GECToR (both cited), statistically a zero-inflated categorical distribution. Tonal reranking is shallow fusion from ASR: an external prior added to the model's log-probabilities, where the 'language model' is a Krumhansl–Schmuckler key profile. In effect, Auto-Tune's scale snapping becomes a weighted soft constraint.

方法Method

流程:先用 SOME 從清唱錄音抽出音符層級的音高、起點與時長,用 RMVPE 抽逐幀 F0 供重建。音高量化到半音,樂器設為固定的人聲代碼,小節位置、拍號與速度從起點序列估計,湊成 MusicBERT 的 octuple 表示。只修改音高、時間結構不動。修正後把新音高以半音為單位移調回 F0 曲線(穩定段平移、音符邊界線性內插),再用 NSF-HiFi-GAN 重新合成。

編碼器:MusicBERT 凍結,在 12 個 Transformer 區塊後各插一個門控 adapter:LayerNorm → 768 降到 64 → GELU → dropout 0.1 → 回到 768,再乘上可訓練的純量門 σ(g)。這是 Houlsby 式 adapter,用來補 MusicBERT(在一般多聲部 MIDI 上預訓練)和單音人聲 MIDI 之間的落差。

雙頭修正:必要性頭輸出 a_i = P(該音需要修正);修改頭在六個非零偏移 {−3,−2,−1,+1,+2,+3} 半音上輸出分布 b_i(d)。合起來 P(Δ=0)=1−a_i、P(Δ=d)=a_i·b_i(d)。必要性頭用全部音符的二元交叉熵訓練,修改頭只在被擾動的音符上算交叉熵,兩者等權相加。

調性重排:對輸入以時長加權的音級直方圖跑 Krumhansl–Schmuckler 演算法,和 24 個大小調模板的相關係數經溫度 τ=0.15 的 softmax 成為調性後驗;每個調的音階內 7 個音級權重為 1、其餘 5 個為 η=0.15,依後驗加權得到音級先驗 r。再用後驗熵與音符數算出整首歌的信心 c∈[0,1](音符少於 8 個時 c=0)。每個音在 {0,±1,±2,±3} 七個候選中取 log P(Δ=d) + λ·c·log r((p+d) mod 12) 最大者;λ 在真實驗證集上以 2.5 最佳。

資料與訓練:從 16,000 首專業錄音(約 1,060 小時中英流行歌)抽出人聲 MIDI,每條序列擾動 20% 的音(70% 獨立、30% 為二到四個音的連續段),合成資料只用於訓練。評估用另一份私有的業餘/專業成對演唱(由聲樂學生錄製),以 DTW 對齊到音符層級,依歌曲切成 20 首驗證(8,299 個音)與 80 首測試(30,682 個音)。訓練分三階段:先只訓練 adapter 8k 步(用 MusicBERT 原本的遮罩預測頭),再只訓練兩個頭 12k 步,最後聯合訓練 25k 步。

Pipeline: SOME extracts note-level pitch, onset and duration from the a cappella recording, and RMVPE extracts frame-level F0 for reconstruction. Pitches are quantised to semitones, the instrument is set to a fixed vocal code, and bar positions, time signature and tempo are estimated from the onsets to complete MusicBERT's octuple representation. Only pitch is edited; timing is untouched. Corrected pitches are mapped back onto the F0 contour by semitone shifts (shifted stable regions, linear interpolation at note boundaries) and resynthesised with NSF-HiFi-GAN.

Encoder: MusicBERT stays frozen, with a gated adapter after each of its 12 Transformer blocks: LayerNorm, 768 down to 64, GELU, dropout 0.1, back to 768, multiplied by a trainable scalar gate σ(g). These Houlsby-style adapters bridge the gap between MusicBERT's general polyphonic MIDI pretraining and monophonic vocal MIDI.

Two-head correction: a necessity head outputs a_i = P(note needs correction); a modification head outputs a distribution b_i(d) over six nonzero offsets {−3,−2,−1,+1,+2,+3} semitones. Together P(Δ=0)=1−a_i and P(Δ=d)=a_i·b_i(d). The necessity head is trained with binary cross-entropy over all notes, the modification head with cross-entropy only on perturbed notes, with equal weights.

Tonal reranking: the Krumhansl–Schmuckler algorithm runs on the input's duration-weighted pitch-class histogram; correlations with 24 major and minor templates pass through a softmax at temperature τ=0.15 to give a key posterior. Each key's 7 scale pitch classes get weight 1 and the other 5 get η=0.15, and posterior weighting yields a pitch-class prior r. A song-level confidence c in [0,1] comes from posterior entropy and note count (c=0 below 8 notes). Each note picks, among seven candidates {0,±1,±2,±3}, the one maximising log P(Δ=d) + λ·c·log r((p+d) mod 12); λ=2.5 is best on the real validation set.

Data and training: vocal MIDI from 16,000 professional recordings (about 1,060 hours of Chinese and English pop) with 20% of notes perturbed per sequence (70% independent, 30% in spans of two to four notes); synthetic data are used only for training. Evaluation uses a separate private set of paired amateur and professional takes recorded by vocal students, DTW-aligned to note level and split by song into 20 validation songs (8,299 notes) and 80 test songs (30,682 notes). Training has three stages: adapters only for 8k updates (with MusicBERT's original masked-prediction head), heads only for 12k, then jointly for 25k.

限制Limitations

修得多,也傷得多。本文修好 46.81% 的錯音,BERT-APC 只修好 26.18%;但本文改壞 3.19% 的正確音,BERT-APC 只有 1.11%,將近三倍。換算成全部音符:本文修好約 12.52 個百分點、改壞約 2.34 個百分點,淨增 10.18;BERT-APC 是 7.00 減 0.81,淨增 6.19。消融也顯示兩個賣點都在拿傷害換修復:雙頭比單一七類分類器多修 7.49 個百分點(39.32%→46.81%),但傷害從 1.93% 升到 3.19%;拿掉調性重排時傷害只有 1.77%。在製作情境裡,改壞原本唱對的音往往比沒修好更刺耳,這個取捨論文沒有討論。

評估的「正確答案」本身有雜訊。參考是同一首歌的專業演唱,作者也承認它不是絕對真值;而依流程推斷,準確率是在 SOME 轉錄並量化到半音之後計算的,所以「錯音」混合了真正唱錯的音、偏離超過半個半音的走音、SOME 自己的轉錄錯誤,以及專業歌手不同的旋律處理。偏移只考慮 ±3 個半音,八度錯誤(轉錄常見)與半音以內的音準偏差都不在處理範圍。

可重現性與主觀測試:評估資料私有,BERT-APC 是作者自行重新實作、在同一份語料上訓練,沒有和原作者報告的數字對照。主觀測試是 30 位受過聲樂訓練的聽者各聽 5 段,沒有報告信賴區間或顯著性;而且準確率低於原始輸入的 Key Snapping 在「整體品質」拿到 3.67,反而高於 BERT-APC 的 3.47,顯示聽者的整體評分和音符準確率並不一致。

It fixes more and damages more. The method repairs 46.81% of wrong notes against BERT-APC's 26.18%, but damages 3.19% of correct notes against BERT-APC's 1.11%, nearly three times as many. Over all notes, it repairs about 12.52 points and damages about 2.34, a net +10.18; BERT-APC is 7.00 minus 0.81, net +6.19. The ablations show both selling points trade harm for repair: the two heads repair 7.49 points more than a single seven-way classifier (39.32% to 46.81%) while harm rises from 1.93% to 3.19%, and without tonal reranking harm is only 1.77%. In production, breaking a note that was sung right is often more jarring than leaving an error unfixed, and the paper does not discuss this trade-off.

The evaluation's 'correct answers' are themselves noisy. References are professional takes of the same song, which the authors admit are not absolute ground truth; and, as inferred from the pipeline, accuracy is computed after SOME transcription and semitone quantisation, so 'wrong notes' mix genuine wrong notes, drift of more than half a semitone, SOME's own transcription errors, and professional singers' different melodic choices. Only ±3 semitone offsets are considered, so octave errors (common in transcription) and sub-semitone intonation are out of scope.

Reproducibility and the listening test: the evaluation data are private, and BERT-APC is the authors' own reimplementation trained on the same corpus, with no check against its original reported numbers. The listening test has 30 vocally trained listeners rating 5 excerpts each, with no confidence intervals or significance; and Key Snapping, whose accuracy is below the raw input, gets 3.67 for overall quality, above BERT-APC's 3.47, showing listeners' overall ratings do not track note accuracy.

討論Discussion

這篇最實在的貢獻,是把三個熟悉的零件組成一條可用的免參考修正流程,而且每個零件都有消融撐著:拿掉 adapter,準確率從 83.43% 掉到 76.87%(−6.56,傷害率升到 7.52%,顯示凍結的 MusicBERT 直接用在人聲 MIDI 上會亂改);拿掉調性重排掉到 81.52%(−1.91);雙頭換成單一分類器掉到 82.35%(−1.08)。相對於輸入的 73.25%,完整系統 +10.18,比 BERT-APC 多 3.99 個百分點,且相對兩個修正基線的優勢都在逐首 Wilcoxon 檢定下顯著(Holm 校正後 p<0.001)。

調性重排的結果最值得玩味。單獨的 Key Snapping(把音吸到估計調性的最近音級)讓準確率從 73.25% 降到 71.76%,改壞 9.62% 的正確音;但同樣的調性資訊,當成以模型機率為主的軟先驗時就有幫助。作者還觀察到調性先驗在合成擾動上幾乎沒用、在真實演唱上才有用,意思是真實的唱錯不是隨機的,而是傾向落在調外的音。這也暗示訓練時隨機的 ±3 半音擾動和真實錯誤分布不一樣,是一個可以改進的地方。

主觀測試中本文在音準(3.59,輸入 3.10)與整體品質(3.94,輸入 3.25)最高,自然度 3.93 幾乎和輸入的 3.97 持平,說明符號層級的修正配上同一個聲碼器不會明顯傷音質。作者打算下一步一併處理節奏與唱法;更直接的改進或許是讓 λ 不只依整首歌的調性信心調整,而是依局部段落(例如轉調處)調整,並把傷害率做成可調的操作點,讓使用者自己決定要激進還是保守。

The most concrete contribution is assembling three familiar parts into a working reference-free correction pipeline, with an ablation behind each: without adapters accuracy falls from 83.43% to 76.87% (−6.56, with harm rising to 7.52%, so frozen MusicBERT applied directly to vocal MIDI over-edits); without tonal reranking it falls to 81.52% (−1.91); with a single classifier instead of two heads, to 82.35% (−1.08). Against the 73.25% input the full system adds +10.18, 3.99 points more than BERT-APC, and both gains over the correction baselines are significant under song-level Wilcoxon tests (Holm-adjusted p<0.001).

The tonal reranking result is the most interesting. Key Snapping on its own (moving notes to the nearest pitch class of the estimated key) lowers accuracy from 73.25% to 71.76% and damages 9.62% of correct notes; the same tonal information helps once it is a soft prior subordinate to the model's probabilities. The authors also observe that the tonal prior barely helps on synthetic perturbations but helps on real singing, meaning real errors are not random but tend to land on out-of-key notes. That also suggests the random ±3 semitone training perturbations differ from the real error distribution, which is room for improvement.

In the listening test the method scores highest on pitch correctness (3.59, input 3.10) and overall quality (3.94, input 3.25), with naturalness at 3.93, nearly level with the input's 3.97, so symbolic correction through the same vocoder does not visibly hurt sound quality. The authors plan to add rhythm and singing technique next; a more direct improvement might be to adapt λ to local passages (for instance at modulations) rather than song-level key confidence alone, and to expose the harm rate as a tunable operating point so users choose between aggressive and conservative correction.
