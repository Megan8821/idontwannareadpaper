What Do Audio-Language Judges of Generated Music Listen For? Four Probes with Controls from Expert Ratings

音訊–語言評審到底在聽什麼:用拿掉音訊、同提示詞換片段、對調順序、改寫指令四種探測,各配一個由專家評分算出的對照,稽核五個開源評審在 MusicEval 與 SongEval 上的表現

Mateusz Modrzejewski, Tomasz Radzikowski
arXiv:2610.13247 · cs.SD; cs.CL; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-09
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：用開源音訊–語言模型給生成音樂打分,通常只回報它跟專家評分的相關係數。這篇設計四種探測去拆那個相關係數:拿掉音訊後五個評審的品質相關掉到 0.01–0.05,沒有超過只靠提示詞就能達到的 0.08;換成同提示詞的別人片段時,五個都跟著聽到的片段走對齊分數,但品質分數只有兩個跟著走;成對比較時四個較小的評審在 69–99% 的配對裡不論順序都挑同一個位置;單是改寫指令,Music Flamingo 的品質相關就在 0.00 到 0.25 之間擺盪。Why this one: Open audio-language models that score generated music are usually reported only by their correlation with expert ratings. This paper designs four probes to take that correlation apart: without audio, the five judges' quality correlation drops to 0.01–0.05, no higher than the 0.08 the prompt alone reaches; when another model's clip for the same prompt is played, all five follow the clip they hear on alignment, but only two do on quality; in pairwise judging, the four smaller judges pick the same slot regardless of order in 69–99% of pairs; and rewording the instruction alone swings Music Flamingo's quality correlation between 0.00 and 0.25.

動機Motivation

文字生成音樂(text-to-music)的模型越來越多,自動評審開始被拿來排名:把一段生成片段和它的提示詞丟給一個音訊–語言模型,請它從 1 到 5 打分,再看它跟專家評分的 Spearman 相關。MusicEval 上報告的數字從接近零到中等都有。

問題是相關係數只說「有多一致」,不說「一致從哪來」。它可能來自評審真的聽了音樂,也可能來自提示詞本身(某些提示詞天生比較容易生成好聽的東西),或者來自提問方式(位置、措辭)。語音評審那邊已經有人揭露過這類捷徑,但音樂評審還沒有被這樣稽核過。作者想補的就是這個缺口,而且他們注意到音樂資料有語音沒有的兩個條件:專家同時評了「音樂品質」和「提示詞對齊」兩個目標,以及許多模型渲染了同一個提示詞,可以在其他條件不變下換掉片段。

Text-to-music models are multiplying, and automatic judges are now used to rank them: a generated clip and its prompt are given to an audio-language model, which rates it from 1 to 5, and the judge is validated by its Spearman correlation with expert ratings. Reported numbers on MusicEval range from near zero to moderate.

The problem is that a correlation says how much a judge agrees, not where the agreement comes from. It could come from actually listening to the music, from the prompt itself (some prompts tend to yield better music), or from the protocol (position, wording). Such shortcuts have been exposed for speech judges, but music judges have not been audited this way. The authors fill that gap, noting two conditions music data offers that speech did not: experts rated both musical quality and prompt alignment, and many models rendered the same prompt, so one clip can be swapped for another with everything else fixed.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的前作在語音與通用音訊。AudioJudge(Manakul 等 2025)發現語音評審有位置偏誤,而且品質差距越小偏誤越大;Park 等(2026)稽核語音評審的協定層捷徑,發現有評審不論順序都選同一個位置,並建議每個模型、每個協定都配一個對應的探測;Foo 等(2026)顯示音訊–語言模型在問答基準上拿掉音訊仍保有 60–72% 的準確率;Zang 等(2025)也指出純文字模型在音樂問答上分數不低。音樂評審這邊,CMI-RewardBench(Ma 等 2026)與 JASTIN(Zhang 等 2026a)在 MusicEval 上報告零樣本評審的一致性,但都沒有做探測。

要點明的是,四個探測裡有兩個是直接搬過來的老方法。對調順序就是 MT-Bench(Zheng 等 2023)與 Wang 等(2023)檢查 LLM 評審位置偏誤的做法;「只看提示詞的下限」在精神上等同自然語言推論(NLI)裡的「只看假設句基線」(hypothesis-only baseline,Gururangan 等 2018、Poliak 等 2018,本文未引),都是先算出「不看真正輸入能拿到多少」再拿評審去比。

The closest prior work is on speech and general audio. AudioJudge (Manakul et al. 2025) found position bias in speech judges that grows as the quality gap narrows; Park et al. (2026) audited protocol-level shortcuts in speech judges, found a judge that picks the same slot regardless of order, and recommended a matched probe per model and protocol; Foo et al. (2026) showed that audio-language models keep 60–72% of their question-answering accuracy without audio; and Zang et al. (2025) noted that text-only models score well on music QA. On the music side, CMI-RewardBench (Ma et al. 2026) and JASTIN (Zhang et al. 2026a) report zero-shot judge agreement on MusicEval, without probes.

Worth stating: two of the four probes are established methods carried over. The order swap is how MT-Bench (Zheng et al. 2023) and Wang et al. (2023) check LLM judges for position bias, and the 'prompt-only floor' is in spirit the hypothesis-only baseline from natural language inference (Gururangan et al. 2018; Poliak et al. 2018, not cited here): first compute how much can be reached without the real input, then compare the judge against it.

方法Method

真正新的部分是兩件事。第一,下限不是用另一個模型去猜,而是從人類評分算出來:用其他模型對同一提示詞的片段,估計「提示詞本身」對專家評分的影響能帶來多少相關。第二,同提示詞互換:讓評審聽另一個模型為同一提示詞生成的片段(長度差 2 秒內),再把評審分數同時迴歸到「播放片段」與「被替換片段」的專家評分上,真的在聽的評審應該只跟著前者走。這是利用 MusicEval 多模型共用提示詞的結構才做得到的反事實測試。

資料是 MusicEval 全部 2,748 個片段(31 個模型、14 位專家、260 個不同提示詞,其中 25 個模型共用同一組 100 個提示詞),外加 SongEval 的 2,399 首歌(沒有提示詞與模型標記,所以只能做順序與改寫兩種探測)。單一專家對其餘專家平均的相關是 0.605,在同一模型內只有 0.252,這是評審的參考上限。

Two things are actually new. First, the floor is not guessed by another model but computed from human ratings: using other models' clips for the same prompt, it estimates how much correlation the prompt's effect on expert ratings can produce. Second, the same-prompt swap: the judge hears another model's clip for the same prompt (duration within 2 s), and its score is regressed on the expert ratings of both the clip played and the clip replaced; a judge that listens should follow only the former. This counterfactual test is possible only because many MusicEval models share prompts.

The data are all 2,748 MusicEval clips (31 models, 14 experts, 260 distinct prompts, with 25 models sharing one set of 100 prompts), plus 2,399 SongEval songs (no prompts or model identifiers, so only the order and wording probes apply). One expert agrees with the mean of the others at 0.605 overall and only 0.252 within a model, the reference ceiling for a judge.

限制Limitations

作者自己列的限制很完整:只測能放進一台工作站的開源評審,最大的 Qwen3-Omni 用社群做的 FP8 量化;MusicEval 片段最長 30 秒,提示詞可能出現在評審的訓練資料裡;專家的品質與對齊評分在模型內相關達 0.74,所以「品質分數其實在跟對齊走」這個分解的解析度有限;改寫指令、回答格式與兩輪對話的探測只在 300 個片段上做;模型層級的相關只有 31 個點,區間很寬。

我會再補三點。第一,順序探測把兩段 12 秒片段串成一條、中間加一聲提示音,而且只問整體品質,這跟實際使用評審的方式有距離;雖然作者對三個評審補做了分開輸入,Qwen2.5-Omni 的同位置率反而從 0.69 升到 0.77,說明偏誤不是串接造成的,但其餘兩個評審沒測。第二,「同位置」指的是取最大值後的判決,但 Music Flamingo 在 99% 的配對裡都選第二段,順序平均後的偏好卻仍有 0.66 與專家一致。這暗示機率差裡還有訊號,偏誤更像一個固定偏移,可以用兩種順序平均來校正;論文沒有把這點當成可用的補救寫出來。

第三,四個探測都在診斷,沒有一個在修。論文沒有測試「順序平均」「用 token 機率而非生成的數字」這些簡單補救能把相關拉回多少,只在兩輪對話探測裡順帶指出:評審寫出來的機率反而比 yes 的 token 機率跟專家更不一致(差 0.14–0.20)。另外,整體相關本來就低(多數在 0.1–0.3),在這麼小的訊號上做差值檢定,不顯著的結果很多是檢定力不足,而不是「確定沒有聽」。

The authors' own limitations are thorough: only open judges that fit one workstation are tested, the largest (Qwen3-Omni) runs on a community FP8 quantization; MusicEval clips are at most 30 s and their prompts may be in the judges' training data; expert quality and alignment ratings correlate at 0.74 within a model, which limits the resolution of the 'quality score actually tracks alignment' decomposition; the wording, answer-format and two-turn probes use 300 clips; and model-level correlations rest on 31 points with wide intervals.

I would add three points. First, the order probe concatenates two 12-second crops with a beep and asks only for overall quality, which is some distance from how judges are used; the authors ran separate inputs for three judges, and Qwen2.5-Omni's same-slot rate actually rose from 0.69 to 0.77, so concatenation is not the cause, but the other two judges were not tested. Second, 'same slot' refers to the argmax verdict, yet Music Flamingo picks the second clip in 99% of pairs while its order-averaged preference still agrees with the experts in 0.66. That suggests the probability margin still carries signal and the bias behaves like a fixed offset that order averaging can correct; the paper does not spell this out as a usable remedy.

Third, all four probes diagnose and none repairs. The paper does not test how much simple remedies such as order averaging or reading token probabilities instead of generated digits recover, noting only in the two-turn probe that the judges' written probabilities agree with the experts less than the token probability of 'yes' (by 0.14–0.20). Also, overall correlations are low to begin with (mostly 0.1–0.3), so with differences tested on such a small signal, many non-significant results reflect limited power rather than 'definitely not listening'.

討論Discussion

主要結果幾條。拿掉音訊後,品質相關掉到 0.01–0.05(換成 30 秒靜音則是 0.00–0.04),都沒有超過提示詞下限 0.08;對齊目標更明顯,五個評審都低於下限(0.02–0.07 對 0.19)。有音訊時,全部片段上的品質相關是 0.11(Qwen2.5-Omni)到 0.31(Qwen3-Omni),遠低於單一專家的 0.61。同提示詞互換在 2,327 次互換中顯示:對齊目標上五個都跟著播放的片段走;品質目標上只有 Audio Flamingo 3(播放 0.17 對被替換 0.07,差 0.10)與 Qwen3-Omni(0.24 對 0.07,差 0.17)顯著,Qwen2.5-Omni 反而是 0.02 對 0.08。分解後,Qwen3-Omni 的品質分數載在專家對齊評分上 0.37、品質上只有 0.07。

成對比較的 600 對裡,四個較小評審不論順序都選同一位置的比例是 0.69–0.99,Music Flamingo 有 99% 選第二段;Qwen3-Omni 只有 0.25,而且隨專家品質差距變大從 0.30 降到 0.19,跟語音評審的模式一樣。改寫指令的影響跟換評審差不多:在全部片段上 Music Flamingo 的品質相關從 0.00 到 0.25(配對差 0.24,區間 0.21–0.27),Qwen3-Omni 從 0.13 到 0.31;但 Qwen3-Omni 的對齊相關幾乎不受措辭影響(0.34–0.35)。評審排名也不能跨資料集轉移:Qwen2.5-Omni 在 MusicEval 品質上是小評審裡最弱的,到 SongEval 卻是最強的(0.57–0.60)。

這篇最有用的地方,是它跟另一個方向的觀察互相印證。CloudEar(2026)發現完全不看提示詞的品質模型在 CMI-Test 對齊任務上也有 0.68–0.70 的準確率,也就是人類的對齊標籤被品質帶著走;這篇則發現最強的評審 Qwen3-Omni 的「品質」分數其實主要在追專家的「對齊」評分。兩件事合起來說,目前的音樂評審和音樂偏好資料都還分不太開這兩個目標。如果要把這類評審拿去當強化學習的獎勵,作者建議每次都附上這四個探測(他們的表 11 是範本),這是成本很低、應該變成慣例的做法。

The main results are as follows. Without audio, the quality correlation drops to 0.01–0.05 (0.00–0.04 with 30 s of silence instead), none above the 0.08 prompt-only floor; on alignment every judge falls below the floor (0.02–0.07 against 0.19). With audio, the quality correlation over all clips ranges from 0.11 (Qwen2.5-Omni) to 0.31 (Qwen3-Omni), far below a single expert's 0.61. Over 2,327 swaps, the same-prompt swap shows all five following the clip played on alignment; on quality only Audio Flamingo 3 (0.17 played vs 0.07 replaced, a difference of 0.10) and Qwen3-Omni (0.24 vs 0.07, a difference of 0.17) are significant, while Qwen2.5-Omni is 0.02 vs 0.08. Decomposed, Qwen3-Omni's quality score loads 0.37 on the expert alignment rating and only 0.07 on expert quality.

Across 600 pairs, the four smaller judges pick the same slot regardless of order in 0.69–0.99 of pairs, with Music Flamingo choosing the second clip 99% of the time; Qwen3-Omni does so in only 0.25, falling from 0.30 to 0.19 as the expert quality gap widens, the same pattern seen in speech judges. Rewording the instruction matters about as much as changing the judge: over all clips, Music Flamingo's quality correlation ranges from 0.00 to 0.25 (paired difference 0.24, interval 0.21–0.27) and Qwen3-Omni's from 0.13 to 0.31, while Qwen3-Omni's alignment correlation barely moves with wording (0.34–0.35). Judge rankings also do not transfer: Qwen2.5-Omni, the weakest small judge on MusicEval quality, is the strongest on SongEval (0.57–0.60).

The most useful aspect is how it corroborates an observation from the other direction. CloudEar (2026) found that quality models that never see the prompt still reach 0.68–0.70 accuracy on CMI-Test alignment, meaning human alignment labels are pulled along by quality; this paper finds that the strongest judge's 'quality' score mainly tracks the experts' 'alignment' rating. Together they say current music judges and music preference data cannot yet separate the two targets well. Before using such judges as reinforcement-learning rewards, the authors recommend reporting all four probes every time (their Table 11 is a template), a cheap practice that should become routine.

LiveSynth: A Streaming Neural Synthesizer for Instrument Cloning and Text-to-Instrument

LiveSynth:不回饋自身輸出的因果 Transformer,以逐幀 MIDI 與 CLAP 音色嵌入產生 100 Hz 連續潛變量,再由因果 VAE 即時解碼,做成可演奏的零樣本樂器複製與文字生成樂器

Kyungsu Kim, Yejin Kim, Kyogu Lee
arXiv:2610.13054 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-09
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：LiveSynth 把「給一段參考錄音或一句文字就能換音色」的神經合成器做成串流版本:因果 VAE 把 48 kHz 音訊壓成每 10 毫秒一幀的連續潛變量,一個不把自己輸出餵回去的因果 Transformer 依 MIDI 與 CLAP 嵌入逐幀生成。在 M3 MacBook Air 上每 10 毫秒音訊算 8.78 毫秒(RTF 0.88)。NSynth 上 MERT 相似度 0.540、MSS 13.1,都優於 TokenSynth 與 CTD;但音符 F1 只有 0.210(CTD 0.361),而且光是因果 VAE 重建就把 F1 從 0.476 拉到 0.288,沒有任何聽測。Why this one: LiveSynth turns the 'swap timbre from a reference recording or a sentence' neural synthesizer into a streaming system: a causal VAE compresses 48 kHz audio into continuous latents at one frame per 10 ms, and a causal Transformer that never feeds its own outputs back generates them frame by frame from MIDI and a CLAP embedding. On an M3 MacBook Air it takes 8.78 ms per 10 ms of audio (RTF 0.88). On NSynth its MERT similarity of 0.540 and MSS of 13.1 beat TokenSynth and CTD; but note F1 is only 0.210 (CTD 0.361), the causal VAE reconstruction alone drops F1 from 0.476 to 0.288, and there is no listening test.

動機Motivation

近一兩年的「生成式神經合成器」已經可以拿一段參考錄音或一句文字當音色條件,零樣本地把一段 MIDI 演奏渲染成那個樂器的聲音。但這些系統都是離線的:一次生成完整的音訊段落,演奏者按下琴鍵時它不會即時回應,所以不能當樂器用。

另一條路是 DDSP 這類可微分訊號處理合成器,效率高、能即時跑,也延伸到複音;但換一個沒見過的音色通常要針對該樂器重新訓練或最佳化。作者想同時拿到兩邊的好處:即時、低延遲,又能零樣本換音色。順帶加上兩個互動功能:音色在演奏中途連續漸變,以及演奏者停下後由模型接著「自己彈」,做成一問一答的即興。

Over the past year or two, 'generative neural synthesizers' have learned to take a reference recording or a sentence as a timbre condition and render a MIDI performance in that instrument's sound zero-shot. But these systems are offline: they generate a complete audio segment at once and do not respond when the performer presses a key, so they cannot serve as instruments.

The other route is DDSP-style differentiable signal-processing synthesis, which is efficient, runs in real time and extends to polyphony, but adapting to an unseen timbre usually requires instrument-specific training or optimization. The authors want both: real-time, low-latency operation and zero-shot timbre control. They also add two interactive features: continuous timbre morphing mid-performance, and the model 'playing on its own' after the performer stops, for call-and-response jamming.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接比較的離線基線有兩個。TokenSynth(Kim 等 2025,同一實驗室)用編解碼器 token 的語言模型,以 CLAP 嵌入當音色條件,同樣支援參考音訊與文字;CTD(Demerlé 等 2024)是潛在擴散模型,結合音訊控制與風格轉移。AnySynth(Jing 等 2026)用上下文學習做零樣本樂器複製,但數字是自報的、資料不同,論文只列作參考。DDSP(Engel 等 2020)與 DDSP-Piano 代表可即時、但要逐樂器訓練的一側。

架構上最接近、也幾乎是直接沿用的是 LiveBand(Pasini 等 2026):同樣的「不回饋」連續序列生成器與條件式對抗訓練,只是 LiveBand 以即時音訊為條件做伴奏,LiveSynth 改成以 MIDI 與音色嵌入為條件做合成。即時音樂生成的其他例子還有 Magenta 的 Live Music Models(Lyria 團隊 2025)與 ReaLJam。

本文沒有提到、但最該拿來對照的是 RAVE(Caillon 與 Esling 2021):它也是一個可即時串流的變分自編碼器,在樂手社群裡已被廣泛當成即時音色轉換外掛使用。差別在於 RAVE 一個模型只學一種音色、要逐樂器訓練,輸入是音訊而非 MIDI;LiveSynth 則是一個模型、以 CLAP 嵌入零樣本換音色。另外,文字到音訊嵌入的 Procrustes 對齊是 Choi 等(2023)的既有做法,「不回饋」生成器與對抗訓練來自 LiveBand,所以這篇的新意主要在把這些部件組成一個以 MIDI 驅動、可零樣本換音色的串流合成器,以及 MIDI 條件的設計細節。

Two offline baselines are compared directly. TokenSynth (Kim et al. 2025, from the same lab) is a language model over codec tokens, conditioned on CLAP embeddings, that also supports reference audio and text; CTD (Demerlé et al. 2024) is a latent diffusion model combining audio control and style transfer. AnySynth (Jing et al. 2026) does zero-shot instrument cloning through in-context learning, but its numbers are self-reported on different data and are listed only for context. DDSP (Engel et al. 2020) and DDSP-Piano represent the real-time side that needs per-instrument training.

The closest architecture, adopted almost directly, is LiveBand (Pasini et al. 2026): the same feedback-free continuous sequence generator and conditional adversarial training, except LiveBand conditions on live audio for accompaniment while LiveSynth conditions on MIDI and a timbre embedding for synthesis. Other real-time music generation examples include Magenta's Live Music Models (Lyria Team 2025) and ReaLJam.

The comparison the paper does not mention but most needs is RAVE (Caillon and Esling 2021): also a real-time streaming variational autoencoder, already widely used by musicians as a live timbre-transfer plugin. The difference is that RAVE learns one timbre per model and needs per-instrument training, with audio rather than MIDI as input, while LiveSynth swaps timbre zero-shot from a CLAP embedding in a single model. Also, the text-to-audio Procrustes alignment is an existing technique from Choi et al. (2023), and the feedback-free generator with adversarial training comes from LiveBand, so the novelty lies mainly in assembling these parts into a MIDI-driven streaming synthesizer with zero-shot timbre control, plus the details of the MIDI conditioning.

方法Method

第一層是因果音訊自編碼器。作者先試過在量化的編解碼器 token 上做語言模型,但要低延遲就得用很短的幀、零前瞻,重建品質明顯下降,量化又讓它更差,所以改用不量化的高斯 VAE。編碼器改自 DAC、解碼器改自 Vocos,所有感受野只看當下與過去;48 kHz 單聲道音訊以 480 點跳距壓成每 10 毫秒一幀、128 維的連續潛變量。解碼器 796 萬參數,一個因果 ConvNeXt 堆疊接一次反 STFT,每幀吐出 480 個取樣點。VAE 在 11 個音樂、語音與一般音訊資料集上以 1 秒片段訓練 40 萬步,之後凍結。

第二層是條件化。MIDI 對齊到 100 Hz,每個音高每幀是 12 種狀態之一(不發聲、放開、或起音/持續各乘以 5 個力度等級),把每個發聲音高的狀態嵌入加總、加一個基準嵌入、再做 RMS 正規化,讓同時發聲音數多寡不影響尺度。持續中的音會在嵌入前 64 維套一個 RoPE 式的旋轉,角度由距離起音的時間決定,這樣起音離開注意力窗口後,模型仍知道這個音已經響了多久。

MIDI 條件在訓練時做結構化丟棄:整段丟棄機率 0.15,丟一段連續區間機率 0.35(長度為片段的四分之一到四分之三),被丟的幀換成同一個學來的「缺席」嵌入,跟代表「明確全部靜音」的基準嵌入不同。推論時在一段演奏之後送入缺席條件,模型就會接著演奏。音色條件是凍結的 LAION-CLAP 512 維嵌入,經共用線性投影後以 adaLN-Zero 注入每個 Transformer 區塊。訓練時一段片段只有一個音色嵌入,推論時介面允許每幀換一個,所以用球面線性插值(slerp)在兩個嵌入間移動就能做音色漸變。

CLAP 的文字與音訊嵌入之間有已知的模態落差,而 LiveSynth 只用音訊嵌入訓練。作者因此用 NSynth 訓練集的「來源加樂器家族」樣板提示詞,和對應樂器的音訊嵌入,擬合一個不置中的正交 Procrustes 映射,推論時只把它套在文字條件上。

第三層是不回饋的生成器。第 t 幀輸出只取決於到 t 為止的雜訊、MIDI 與音色條件,不吃自己先前產生的潛變量,時間依賴完全靠 Transformer 的因果隱藏狀態承載,所以誤差不會沿著輸入累積。注意力用 500 幀(5 秒)的滑動窗口;訓練時用帶狀因果遮罩平行處理所有幀,推論時用 500 幀的 KV 快取一次產生一幀,兩者看到的上下文相同。生成器 1.9 億參數。

訓練用 R3GAN 的成對相對式對抗目標加自適應梯度懲罰。判別器(1.54 億參數)另有自己的 MIDI 編碼器,把 MIDI 表徵與潛變量沿通道串接,經非因果 ConvNeXt 堆疊逐步降採樣,每個區塊以 FiLM 注入音色,最後時間平均池化輸出一個整段的真假分數。L1 潛變量重建損失(權重 3)只算在條件完整、沒被丟棄的樣本上,因為條件不完整時有多種合理輸出,逐點重建只會把預測拉向平均。訓練資料是用 Lakh MIDI 渲染的 NSynth 樂器(85%,先以 LavaSR 從 16 kHz 升到 48 kHz)加 Slakh2100 音軌(15%),10 秒片段,20 萬次更新。

零樣本樂器複製的評估用 53 個未見過的 NSynth 樂器共 530 段、12 個 Slakh 測試類別共 480 段,各 5 秒,LiveSynth 用 10 秒參考。NSynth 上 LiveSynth 的 MERT 0.540(TokenSynth 0.465、CTD 0.438)、MSS 13.1(26.0、19.5)、PANNs 0.819(0.794、0.755);Slakh 上 MERT 0.506 只比 TokenSynth 的 0.498 高一點,MSS 12.4 對 28.7 與 16.1。但音符 F1 在 NSynth 是 0.210,低於 TokenSynth 0.317 與 CTD 0.361;PQ 5.95 也是三者最低(6.88、6.78)。

文字生成樂器在 16 個 NSynth 類別、每類 32 段 MIDI 上評估。Procrustes 對齊讓 LiveSynth 的 MusicCoCa 相似度從 0.149 升到 0.165、F1 從 0.181 升到 0.279、CE 從 4.27 升到 4.64;對齊後的 TokenSynth 在 MusicCoCa(0.177)與 PQ(7.00 對 6.60)上仍較強。音色漸變在 64 組樂器配對上做 10 秒 slerp,PANNs 相似度對兩端單調變化,但交叉點落在 6.7 秒而不是 5 秒,作者推測是 KV 快取還留著先前的音色條件。即時性在無風扇的 M3 MacBook Air 上以 MLX int8 測 6,000 幀:平均每幀 8.78 毫秒,第 99 百分位 9.77 毫秒,仍在 10 毫秒預算內。

The first layer is a causal audio autoencoder. The authors first tried language modeling over quantized codec tokens, but low latency forces short frames and zero lookahead, which clearly hurt reconstruction, and quantization made it worse, so they use an unquantized Gaussian VAE instead. The encoder is adapted from DAC and the decoder from Vocos, with every receptive field restricted to current and past inputs; 48 kHz mono audio is compressed with a hop of 480 samples into 128-dimensional continuous latents at one frame per 10 ms. The 7.96M-parameter decoder is a causal ConvNeXt stack followed by a single inverse STFT that emits 480 samples per frame. The VAE is trained for 400k steps on 1-second clips from 11 music, speech and general-audio datasets, then frozen.

The second layer is conditioning. MIDI is aligned to 100 Hz, and each pitch in each frame takes one of 12 states (inactive, offset, or onset/sustain at each of five velocity levels); the state embeddings of active pitches are summed, a baseline embedding is added, and RMS normalization keeps the scale independent of how many notes sound. For sustained notes, a RoPE-style rotation keyed to time since onset is applied to the first 64 embedding dimensions, so the model still knows how long a note has been sounding after its onset leaves the attention window.

MIDI conditioning gets structured dropout during training: the whole clip is dropped with probability 0.15 and a single contiguous span with probability 0.35 (one quarter to three quarters of the clip), with dropped frames replaced by one learned 'absent' embedding, distinct from the baseline embedding that means 'explicitly all inactive'. At inference, supplying the absent condition after a performed prefix makes the model continue playing. The timbre condition is a frozen 512-dimensional LAION-CLAP embedding, projected by a shared linear layer and injected into every Transformer block through adaLN-Zero. Training uses one timbre embedding per clip, but the interface accepts a new one every frame, so moving between two embeddings by spherical linear interpolation (slerp) produces a timbre morph.

CLAP has a known modality gap between text and audio embeddings, and LiveSynth trains only on audio embeddings. The authors therefore fit an uncentered orthogonal Procrustes map from template prompts built from NSynth training 'source and family' labels to the matching instruments' audio embeddings, and apply it only to text conditions at inference.

The third layer is the feedback-free generator. The output at frame t depends only on noise, MIDI and timbre conditions up to t, never on its own previously generated latents; temporal dependence is carried entirely by the Transformer's causal hidden states, so errors do not accumulate through the inputs. Attention uses a 500-frame (5-second) sliding window; training processes all frames in parallel under a band-causal mask, and inference generates one frame at a time with a 500-frame KV cache, giving the same context. The generator has 190M parameters.

Training uses R3GAN's paired relativistic adversarial objective with adaptive gradient penalties. The 154M-parameter discriminator has its own MIDI encoder, concatenates the MIDI representation with the latents along channels, downsamples through a non-causal ConvNeXt stack with timbre FiLM in each block, and outputs one sequence-level real/fake logit after temporal average pooling. An L1 latent reconstruction loss (weight 3) applies only to fully conditioned samples, because incomplete conditioning admits many valid outputs and pointwise reconstruction would pull predictions toward the average. Training data are NSynth instruments rendered with Lakh MIDI (85%, first upsampled from 16 to 48 kHz with LavaSR) plus Slakh2100 stems (15%), in 10-second clips, for 200k updates.

Zero-shot cloning is evaluated on 530 clips from 53 unseen NSynth instruments and 480 clips from 12 Slakh test classes, 5 seconds each, with a 10-second reference for LiveSynth. On NSynth, LiveSynth reaches MERT 0.540 (TokenSynth 0.465, CTD 0.438), MSS 13.1 (26.0, 19.5) and PANNs 0.819 (0.794, 0.755); on Slakh its MERT of 0.506 is only slightly above TokenSynth's 0.498, with MSS 12.4 against 28.7 and 16.1. But note F1 on NSynth is 0.210, below TokenSynth's 0.317 and CTD's 0.361, and PQ of 5.95 is the lowest of the three (6.88, 6.78).

Text-to-instrument is evaluated on 16 NSynth classes with 32 MIDI excerpts each. Procrustes alignment raises LiveSynth's MusicCoCa similarity from 0.149 to 0.165, F1 from 0.181 to 0.279 and CE from 4.27 to 4.64; aligned TokenSynth remains stronger on MusicCoCa (0.177) and PQ (7.00 vs 6.60). Timbre morphing uses a 10-second slerp over 64 instrument pairs: PANNs similarity to both endpoints changes monotonically, but the crossover falls at 6.7 s rather than 5 s, which the authors attribute to earlier timbre conditions lingering in the KV cache. Real-time performance is measured on a fanless M3 MacBook Air with MLX int8 over 6,000 frames: 8.78 ms per frame on average and 9.77 ms at the 99th percentile, within the 10 ms budget.

限制Limitations

作者在結論裡承認的限制:音符準確度與音質較低、接續演奏的節奏依賴很弱、沒有聽測也沒有演奏者研究。這幾點都有數字支撐,但嚴重程度值得說清楚。

音符 F1 的落差主要來自因果 VAE,而不是生成器。在 NSynth 上,真實音訊經 VAE 重建後 F1 就從 0.476 掉到 0.288、Slakh 上從 0.785 掉到 0.564;LiveSynth 的 0.210 與 0.305 已經在這個天花板附近。也就是說,要追上 CTD 的 0.361,得先換一個更好的因果表徵,調生成器沒有用。PQ 也一樣:NSynth 上 VAE 重建 6.43、LiveSynth 5.95,而 TokenSynth 是 6.88。

客觀指標本身也有可疑之處。文字生成樂器那張表裡,「真實樂器」的 F1 只有 0.238、CE 只有 3.78,反而低於對齊後 LiveSynth 的 0.279 與 4.64。真實錄音在轉譜 F1 與內容享受度上輸給生成結果,說明 YourMT3+ 在 NSynth 單音上的轉譜與 Audiobox Aesthetics 在這種素材上的評分都不太可靠;NSynth 真值本身的 F1 也只有 0.476。在這樣的尺上,0.03 到 0.07 的 MERT 或 F1 差距很難解讀,沒有聽測就更難判斷。

接續演奏的結果比摘要語氣弱。音高類別分布的 JS 距離 0.229 接近真實接續的 0.251、遠低於打亂配對的 0.655,但起音間隔分布的 JS 距離 0.529 幾乎等於打亂配對的 0.561、遠高於真實的 0.204;也就是模型記得用哪些音,卻不記得怎麼打拍子。PQ 也從真實的 6.60 掉到 5.68。

其他幾點:即時性只測了模型的每幀計算,沒有含 MIDI 輸入與音訊緩衝的端到端延遲;RTF 0.88 只留 12% 餘裕,第 99 百分位 9.77 毫秒已貼近 10 毫秒上限,換到較慢的機器或同時跑其他外掛就可能掉幀。訓練時一段只有一個音色嵌入,推論時的逐幀漸變其實是分布外的用法,交叉點延後到 6.7 秒就是症狀之一。文字評估的提示詞是由 NSynth 類別樣板生成,與擬合 Procrustes 的樣板同一風格,對齊的增益可能高估了對自由文字的效果。基線 TokenSynth 出自同一實驗室,CTD 照原始流程跑,參考長度等條件不完全一致。

Limitations the authors acknowledge in the conclusion: lower note accuracy and audio quality, weak rhythmic dependence in continuation, and no listening tests or performer studies. Each is backed by numbers, but the severity is worth spelling out.

The note-F1 gap comes mainly from the causal VAE, not the generator. On NSynth, VAE reconstruction of real audio already drops F1 from 0.476 to 0.288, and on Slakh from 0.785 to 0.564; LiveSynth's 0.210 and 0.305 already sit near that ceiling. So matching CTD's 0.361 requires a better causal representation first; tuning the generator will not do it. PQ is similar: on NSynth the VAE reconstruction scores 6.43 and LiveSynth 5.95, against TokenSynth's 6.88.

The objective metrics themselves are questionable. In the text-to-instrument table, the 'real instrument' row has F1 of only 0.238 and CE of 3.78, below aligned LiveSynth's 0.279 and 4.64. Real recordings losing to generated output on transcription F1 and content enjoyment suggests that YourMT3+ transcription and Audiobox Aesthetics scoring are unreliable on NSynth material; NSynth ground truth itself has F1 of just 0.476. On such a ruler, MERT or F1 gaps of 0.03 to 0.07 are hard to interpret, and without a listening test even harder.

The continuation result is weaker than the abstract's tone. The pitch-class JS distance of 0.229 is close to the true continuation's 0.251 and far below the shuffled control's 0.655, but the inter-onset-interval JS distance of 0.529 nearly equals the shuffled control's 0.561 and is far above the true 0.204; the model remembers which notes to use but not how to keep time. PQ also drops from 6.60 for real continuations to 5.68.

A few more points: real-time performance is measured only as per-frame model computation, without end-to-end latency including MIDI input and audio buffering; an RTF of 0.88 leaves 12% headroom and the 9.77 ms 99th percentile is close to the 10 ms limit, so a slower machine or other plugins running alongside could cause dropouts. Training uses one timbre embedding per clip, so per-frame morphing at inference is out of distribution, and the crossover delayed to 6.7 s is one symptom. Text prompts are generated from NSynth class templates in the same style as those used to fit Procrustes, so the alignment gain may overstate the effect on free-form text. The TokenSynth baseline comes from the same lab, CTD runs its original pipeline, and conditions such as reference length are not fully matched.

討論Discussion

這篇的定位很清楚:不是在品質上贏,而是把零樣本音色條件做成真的可以接在鍵盤後面彈的東西。10 毫秒的演算法延遲加上 RTF 0.88,在筆電上不需要 GPU 就能跑,這比再多 0.02 的 MERT 更決定它會不會被樂手用。

最值得借鑑的設計是兩個小技巧。一是「缺席」嵌入與「全部靜音」嵌入分開:讓模型分得出「這一幀沒有人彈」與「這一幀不知道有沒有人彈」,後者才觸發自己接著彈;這等於把無分類器引導(classifier-free guidance)的條件丟棄,改造成一個互動功能。二是只對完整條件的樣本算 L1 重建、其餘交給對抗損失,避免在多解的情況下把輸出拉向平均。

要注意的是,目前的證據只支撐「在客觀指標上與離線基線同級、某些指標較好」,支撐不了「可以當樂器用」:樂器好不好用要看演奏者的手感、延遲是否被察覺、音色是否穩定,這些都需要演奏者研究。音符準確度的瓶頸已經被作者自己定位在因果 VAE(重建就把 NSynth F1 砍掉約四成),所以下一步的關鍵不是更大的生成器,而是更好的低延遲連續表徵。如果這點解決,這套 MIDI 條件設計加上不回饋生成器,很可能成為即時神經合成器的通用骨架。

The paper's positioning is clear: it does not win on quality, but it makes zero-shot timbre conditioning something you can actually play from a keyboard. A 10 ms algorithmic latency and an RTF of 0.88 on a laptop without a GPU matter more for whether musicians use it than another 0.02 of MERT.

Two small design ideas are most worth borrowing. One is separating the 'absent' embedding from the 'all inactive' embedding, so the model can tell 'nobody is playing in this frame' from 'it is unknown whether anyone is playing', and only the latter triggers autonomous continuation; this turns classifier-free-guidance-style condition dropout into an interactive feature. The other is applying L1 reconstruction only to fully conditioned samples and leaving the rest to the adversarial loss, which avoids pulling outputs toward the average when many outputs are valid.

Note that the current evidence supports only 'on par with offline baselines on objective metrics, better on some', not 'usable as an instrument': whether an instrument works depends on how it feels to play, whether latency is noticed and whether timbre stays stable, all of which need performer studies. The authors have themselves located the note-accuracy bottleneck in the causal VAE (reconstruction alone cuts NSynth F1 by about 40%), so the key next step is a better low-latency continuous representation rather than a bigger generator. If that is solved, this MIDI conditioning design with a feedback-free generator could well become a general backbone for real-time neural synthesizers.

動機Motivation

預訓練音訊編碼器現在被當成通用介面,下游任務往往在訓練編碼器時還不存在。一個嵌入對未知任務有沒有用,部分取決於哪些訊號性質在預訓練目標下存活了下來;而這些存活下來的資訊,在嵌入被拿來取代原始音訊分享時(版權或隱私敏感的場景)又變成風險。作者問的就是:除了訓練目標要求的資訊之外,嵌入裡還留著多少原始音訊?

他們選音樂而不是語音,理由有兩個:重建一段錄音需要保住音高、音色、節奏與時間結構,這是比語音更嚴格的考驗;而音樂資料常因授權限制,只公開衍生特徵而不公開音訊,正好是實務上會發生的場景。

Pretrained audio encoders now serve as general interfaces, and downstream tasks often do not exist when the encoder is trained. Whether an embedding helps an unknown task depends partly on which signal properties survive the pretraining objective, and the same surviving information becomes a risk when embeddings are shared in place of audio for copyright or privacy reasons. The authors ask: beyond what the training objective required, how much of the source audio remains in the embedding?

They choose music over speech for two reasons: reconstructing a recording requires preserving pitch, timbre, rhythm and temporal structure, a stricter test than speech; and music data, because of licensing, is often released as derived features rather than audio, which is exactly the practical scenario.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

表徵反演在視覺領域由來已久。Mahendran 與 Vedaldi(2015)最佳化輸入去對齊目標表徵;Dosovitskiy 與 Brox(2016)訓練一個解碼器從高階特徵重建影像;Fredrikson 等(2015)則把反演當成隱私攻擊。音訊方面,Comanducci 等(2021)從 CNN 中間特徵重建語音,Polyak 等(2021)從離散自監督單元重新合成語音並檢查內容、韻律與說話者是否保留。McFee 實驗室自己的前作(Deng 等 2025)與 Kim 等(2019)則從另一個方向測量嵌入對已知音訊變換的敏感度。

這篇跟它們的差別在於橫向比較:同一個預訓練解碼器架構、同一套訓練流程,只換條件介面,去比四種訓練目標不同的編碼器。但要點明的是,「拿凍結的自監督表徵去條件化一個擴散模型,看它生成出什麼來判斷表徵保留了什麼」在視覺上已經有 RCDM(Bordes 等 2022,本文未引)做過,方法幾乎同構;AudioLDM 以 CLAP 音訊嵌入為條件訓練,實質上也已是一個 CLAP 反演解碼器。這篇的新意主要在把這個探測搬到音樂、並把重建是否「找得回原曲」量化成檢索指標。

Representation inversion has a long history in vision. Mahendran and Vedaldi (2015) optimize inputs to match target representations; Dosovitskiy and Brox (2016) train a decoder to reconstruct images from high-level features; Fredrikson et al. (2015) frame inversion as a privacy attack. In audio, Comanducci et al. (2021) reconstruct speech from intermediate CNN features, and Polyak et al. (2021) resynthesize speech from discrete self-supervised units and check whether content, prosody and speaker are preserved. The McFee lab's own earlier work (Deng et al. 2025) and Kim et al. (2019) measure, from another direction, how embeddings respond to known audio transformations.

The difference here is the cross-encoder comparison: the same pretrained decoder architecture and training protocol, with only the conditioning interface changed, applied to four encoders with different training objectives. Worth stating, though: conditioning a diffusion model on frozen self-supervised representations and reading what it generates to judge what the representation retains was already done in vision by RCDM (Bordes et al. 2022, not cited here), with a nearly isomorphic method; and AudioLDM, trained conditioned on CLAP audio embeddings, is effectively already a CLAP inversion decoder. The novelty lies mainly in bringing this probe to music and quantifying whether a reconstruction 'finds its way back' to the source as a retrieval metric.

方法Method

四個編碼器、六種介面。VGGish 每 0.96 秒一個 128 維嵌入(5 秒約 6–7 個,用官方 PCA 白化後的 8 位元輸出);CLAP 有兩種,整段 5 秒一個 512 維嵌入,或每 1 秒各一個;AudioSet 訓練的 ConvNeXt Tiny 每約 0.32 秒一幀,一種把 7 個頻率位置平均成 768 維,一種攤平保留成 5,376 維;EnCodec 則用量化後的 zq,每秒 150 幀、128 維,5 秒 750 個 token,當作重建導向的正向對照。CLAP 與 ConvNeXt 的雙介面設計是用來在編碼器不變的前提下,單獨看時間與頻率壓縮的影響。

解碼器從 Stable Audio Open(以創用 CC 資料訓練)初始化:固定的 VAE 把 5 秒、44.1 kHz 立體聲壓成 64 通道潛變量(降採樣 2048 倍),24 層、寬 1536 的擴散 Transformer 以交叉注意力接 768 維條件。原本的 T5 文字條件換成各編碼器的嵌入,經一個學習的線性層投影,且投影權重以 N(0, 10⁻³) 初始化、偏差為零,減少換介面初期的擾動。

另一個改動是讓交叉注意力偏向時間上對應的區段:潛變量與條件 token 各自標上 [0, 1] 的正規化時間,注意力分數加上一個依時間差分桶、每個頭各自學的偏差(初始為零),並以硬遮罩只允許時間差 0.2 以內的配對。這表達一個弱先驗:重建 5.0–5.2 秒的那段,主要該看同一段的條件。訓練用 Stable Audio Open 原本的 v-prediction 目標,不做條件丟棄、引導權重 1,只保留條件預測;推論用 150 步確定性 DDIM(η=0),隨機性只來自初始雜訊。

資料是百萬歌曲資料集(MSD)配 Tagtraum CD2 曲風標記,依藝人切分成 219,810 訓練、26,190 驗證、26,367 測試曲目,共 15 個曲風。因為 Stable Audio Open 只學過器樂與音效,先用 Demucs 去掉人聲,編碼與重建都用器樂混音。評估有四項:多解析度 STFT 距離、Stable Audio Open VAE 空間的餘弦相似度(以 20 萬組隨機不同片段對作為參考分布)、在全部 26,367 段測試片段中以 VAE 空間找回原曲的 R@1 與 R@10,以及 MusicNN 前十標籤的重疊比例。每個介面在 4 張 GPU 上微調約 36 小時。

Four encoders, six interfaces. VGGish gives one 128-dimensional embedding per 0.96 s (about 6–7 per 5 s, using the released PCA-whitened 8-bit output); CLAP comes in two forms, one 512-dimensional embedding per 5-second clip or one per second; AudioSet-trained ConvNeXt Tiny gives a frame about every 0.32 s, either averaged over its 7 frequency positions to 768 dimensions or flattened to 5,376; EnCodec uses the quantized zq at 150 frames per second and 128 dimensions, 750 tokens per 5 s, as a reconstruction-trained positive control. The paired CLAP and ConvNeXt interfaces isolate the effect of temporal and spectral compression while the encoder stays fixed.

The decoder is initialized from Stable Audio Open (trained on Creative Commons data): a fixed VAE compresses 5-second, 44.1 kHz stereo into 64-channel latents (2048× downsampling), and a 24-layer, width-1536 diffusion Transformer takes 768-dimensional conditions through cross-attention. The original T5 text conditioning is replaced by each encoder's embeddings via a learned linear projection, initialized from N(0, 10⁻³) with zero bias to reduce the initial disturbance of the new interface.

Another change biases cross-attention toward temporally corresponding regions: latent and condition tokens get normalized times in [0, 1], the attention score gets a head-specific learned bias bucketed by time difference (initialized to zero), and a hard mask allows only pairs within 0.2 of each other. This expresses a weak prior that reconstructing 5.0–5.2 s should mainly look at conditions from the same region. Training uses Stable Audio Open's original v-prediction objective with no conditioning dropout and guidance weight 1, keeping only the conditional prediction; inference uses 150-step deterministic DDIM (η=0), so randomness comes only from the initial noise.

The data are the Million Song Dataset (MSD) with Tagtraum CD2 genre labels, split by artist into 219,810 training, 26,190 validation and 26,367 test tracks across 15 genres. Because Stable Audio Open learned only instrumental audio and sound effects, vocals are first removed with Demucs, and the instrumental mixes are used for both encoding and reconstruction. Evaluation has four parts: multi-resolution STFT distance, cosine similarity in the Stable Audio Open VAE space (calibrated against 200k random distinct crop pairs), R@1 and R@10 for retrieving the source among all 26,367 test crops in VAE space, and MusicNN top-10 tag overlap. Each interface is fine-tuned for about 36 hours on 4 GPUs.

限制Limitations

作者自己最重要的一句聲明是:失敗的重建可能反映的是解碼器的限制,所以這是一個標準化的稽核,不是「最多能還原多少」的上限。這意味著所有結果都只是下限,隱私結論只能說「至少洩漏這麼多」。

我會補幾點。第一,跨編碼器的比較混了很多變因,作者有承認架構與訓練資料不同,但沒提到輸入格式:VGGish 吃 16 kHz 單聲道、CLAP 48 kHz 單聲道、ConvNeXt 32 kHz 單聲道,只有 EnCodec 吃 48 kHz 立體聲,而重建目標是 44.1 kHz 立體聲。三個單聲道編碼器根本沒有立體聲像與高頻的資訊,MR-STFT 上吃虧有一部分是輸入端就注定的。VGGish 還經過 8 位元量化。

第二,檢索與相似度都在 Stable Audio Open 的 VAE 空間裡算,而解碼器正是在這個空間裡訓練的。這讓 VAE 指標天生偏向解碼器的訓練目標,R@1 也是在這把尺上量出來的;如果改用獨立的嵌入(例如 MERT)做檢索,數字可能不同。作者沒做這個對照。MusicNN 又是在 MSD 上訓練的,測試資料也來自 MSD,標籤重疊在分布內偏樂觀。

第三,證據的厚度有限。論文是四頁短文,初始化與局部注意力的消融只放在專案網頁上,而且作者自己說它們的效益在驗證損失上比在最終指標上明顯;每個條件只用一次初始雜訊,沒有報告不同雜訊下的變異。去人聲後只剩器樂,人聲(最具識別性、隱私上也最敏感的部分)完全不在稽核範圍內。隱私結論也停在「R@1 有多高」,沒有做更直接的洩漏測量,例如在更大曲庫裡指認曲目或藝人。

The authors' own most important caveat is that a failed reconstruction may reflect the decoder's limitations, so this is a standardized audit, not a bound on maximal recoverability. All results are therefore lower bounds, and privacy conclusions can only say 'at least this much leaks'.

I would add a few points. First, the cross-encoder comparison mixes many factors; the authors acknowledge architecture and training data differ but not input format: VGGish takes 16 kHz mono, CLAP 48 kHz mono, ConvNeXt 32 kHz mono, and only EnCodec takes 48 kHz stereo, while the target is 44.1 kHz stereo. The three mono encoders carry no stereo image or high-frequency information at all, so part of their MR-STFT deficit is fixed at the input. VGGish is also 8-bit quantized.

Second, both retrieval and similarity are computed in the Stable Audio Open VAE space, the very space the decoder is trained in. That biases the VAE metric toward the decoder's training objective, and R@1 is measured on the same ruler; retrieval with an independent embedding (MERT, for example) might give different numbers. The authors do not run that check. MusicNN is also trained on MSD while the test data come from MSD, so tag overlap is optimistic in-distribution.

Third, the evidence is thin. The paper is a four-page short paper, the initialization and local-attention ablations live only on the project page, and the authors themselves say their benefit shows more in validation loss than in final metrics; each condition uses a single initial noise draw, with no variance across noise reported. With vocals removed only instrumentals remain, so the voice, the most identifying and privacy-sensitive part, is entirely outside the audit. The privacy conclusion also stops at 'how high R@1 is', without more direct leakage measures such as identifying tracks or artists in a larger catalog.

討論Discussion

結果表(5 秒 MSD 測試片段):MR-STFT 距離從 VGGish 的 0.21、CLAP 5 秒 0.20、CLAP 1 秒 0.19、ConvNeXt 兩種 0.18,到 EnCodec 0.13;VAE 空間餘弦相似度 0.15、0.16、0.18、0.19、0.24、0.47;R@1 是 0.004、0.006、0.024、0.094、0.352、0.894;MusicNN 前十標籤重疊 0.733、0.751、0.771、0.799、0.812、0.847。EnCodec 用自己原生解碼器的上限是 MR-STFT 0.10、VAE 0.89、R@1 1.000、標籤 0.909。兩個重建指標之間幾乎不相關(|r| 0.02–0.17)。

讀這張表最重要的一點:MR-STFT 在六種介面間只差 0.21 到 0.13,幾乎分不出來,R@1 卻跨了兩個數量級(0.004 到 0.894)。所以如果只看頻譜距離,會以為各種嵌入都差不多;真正有鑑別力的是「能不能在兩萬六千段裡指認出原曲」。這是一個值得被其他嵌入研究沿用的評估習慣。

編碼器內部的對照最乾淨,也最有實用意義。ConvNeXt 從平均頻率軸改成保留頻率軸,R@1 從 0.094 跳到 0.352(約 3.7 倍);CLAP 從整段一個嵌入改成每秒一個,R@1 從 0.006 升到 0.024(4 倍)。換句話說,決定洩漏多少的,除了編碼器的訓練目標,還有你決定公開哪一層、多細的時間解析度。如果要以嵌入代替音訊公開,先在時間與頻率上池化是一個便宜的保護手段。

另一面是「風格」與「錄音本身」的分離。VGGish 與 CLAP 的重建抓不準音高與時值,R@1 幾乎是零,但標籤重疊仍有 0.73 到 0.75;論文的頻譜圖範例裡,只有一個嵌入的 CLAP 5 秒重建仍跟著原曲在低頻開開關關,VGGish 也保住了粗略的節奏。對版權討論來說,這表示壓縮過的嵌入大概不足以重建可辨識的錄音,但足以重建「聽起來像那種音樂」的東西。這正好是生成模型訓練資料爭議的核心,這篇提供的是一個量化這條界線的起點,而不是答案。

The results table (5-second MSD test clips): MR-STFT distance goes from 0.21 for VGGish, 0.20 for CLAP 5 s, 0.19 for CLAP 1 s and 0.18 for both ConvNeXt interfaces down to 0.13 for EnCodec; VAE-space cosine similarity is 0.15, 0.16, 0.18, 0.19, 0.24 and 0.47; R@1 is 0.004, 0.006, 0.024, 0.094, 0.352 and 0.894; MusicNN top-10 tag overlap is 0.733, 0.751, 0.771, 0.799, 0.812 and 0.847. EnCodec through its own native decoder, the topline, reaches MR-STFT 0.10, VAE 0.89, R@1 1.000 and tags 0.909. The two reconstruction metrics are barely correlated (|r| 0.02–0.17).

The key reading of this table: MR-STFT spans only 0.21 to 0.13 across the six interfaces, hardly distinguishable, while R@1 spans two orders of magnitude (0.004 to 0.894). Looking only at spectral distance, one would conclude the embeddings are all similar; what actually discriminates is whether the source can be identified among twenty-six thousand clips. That is an evaluation habit other embedding studies should adopt.

The within-encoder comparisons are the cleanest and most practically meaningful. Switching ConvNeXt from averaging the frequency axis to keeping it lifts R@1 from 0.094 to 0.352 (about 3.7×); switching CLAP from one embedding per clip to one per second lifts R@1 from 0.006 to 0.024 (4×). In other words, how much leaks depends not only on the encoder's training objective but on which layer you publish and at what temporal resolution. If embeddings are to be released in place of audio, pooling over time and frequency first is a cheap protection.

The other side is the separation of 'style' from 'the recording itself'. VGGish and CLAP reconstructions miss exact pitch and timing and their R@1 is near zero, yet tag overlap is still 0.73 to 0.75; in the paper's spectrogram example, the single-embedding CLAP 5 s reconstruction still switches low frequencies on and off with the source, and VGGish keeps the coarse rhythm. For copyright debates, this means compressed embeddings probably cannot rebuild a recognizable recording but can rebuild something that 'sounds like that kind of music'. That is exactly the core of disputes over generative-model training data; this paper offers a starting point for quantifying that line, not an answer.
