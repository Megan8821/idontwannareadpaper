GlobalTap: Globally Diverse Beat Tracking Benchmark via Crowdsourced Tapping

GlobalTap：以 REPP 錄音式線上打拍收集十國 746 位非專業聽者的拍點，經 KDE 匯總與近等時格線擬合，做成涵蓋 59 國 200 段流行樂的節拍追蹤基準，並以 130 人聽測驗證群眾標註與專家參考標註無整體差異

William T. Botticelli-Wells, Manuel Anglada-Tort, Elif Celen, Rena Far, Harin Lee, Peter M. C. Harrison, Nori Jacoby
arXiv:2611.11137 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-08
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：節拍追蹤的訓練與評測資料幾乎都是西方流行與舞曲，而且靠專家逐拍校正，擴不了規模。這篇改用群眾打拍：746 位來自十個國家的受試者替 59 國的 200 段流行樂打拍，再把所有拍點匯總成共識拍點。在另一組 240 段驗證集上，130 位獨立聽者對群眾版與專家參考版的評分差距是 Δ = −0.01（95% CI [−0.12, +0.10]），偵測不到整體差異。選它是因為它同時處理了資料的文化偏誤與標註成本，而且把「拍點本來就有多種合理解讀」量化成可以釋出的資料。但要注意：這是 200 段的評測集，不夠拿來訓練；格線擬合是簡單啟發式，遇到自由速度（rubato）就會失準。Why this one: Beat-tracking training and evaluation data are almost all Western pop and dance music, and they rely on experts correcting beats one by one, which does not scale. This paper uses crowd tapping instead: 746 participants from ten countries tapped along to 200 popular-music excerpts from 59 countries, and the pooled taps are turned into consensus beats. On a separate 240-excerpt validation set, 130 independent listeners rated crowd and expert-reference beat tracks at Δ = −0.01 (95% CI [−0.12, +0.10]), with no overall difference detected. I picked it because it addresses both the cultural bias and the annotation cost of the data, and it turns the fact that beats admit several reasonable readings into releasable data. But note: this is a 200-excerpt evaluation set, too small for training, and the grid fitting is a simple heuristic that breaks down on free tempo (rubato).

動機Motivation

節拍追蹤是 MIR 的基礎任務，速度、拍號、段落分析都依賴它。現有系統（例如 Beat This!）表現已經很強，但它們的統一訓練集是 15 個語料庫、4,556 首曲子，內容大多是 Ballroom、RWC、Beatles、Harmonix 這類西方流行與舞曲。要擴充曲風很難，因為傳統標註流程需要熟悉該曲風的音樂家，用專業軟體逐拍校正，費時又無法放大。

另一個較少被說出來的問題是：標註者幾乎都是一兩位受過訓練的專家。專家標註可以合理代表某種「結構上正確」的讀法，但不見得反映一般聽眾實際在哪裡感覺到拍子。同一段音樂可以用不同的節拍層級（tactus）或相位去打，這種歧義本身就是知覺事實，而單一標註把它抹平了。

作者的目標因此有兩層：用可放大的線上打拍流程產生跨文化的評測資料，並且保留多位聽者之間的分歧，讓它成為不需要參考答案的品質指標。

Beat tracking is a core MIR task that tempo, meter and structure analysis all build on. Current systems such as Beat This! are already strong, but their unified training set has 4,556 tracks from 15 corpora, mostly Western pop and dance such as Ballroom, RWC, Beatles and Harmonix. Widening genre coverage is hard because traditional annotation needs musicians familiar with the style correcting beats one by one in specialist software, which is slow and does not scale.

A less often stated problem is that annotators are almost always one or two trained experts. Expert annotations can validly represent a structurally informed reading, but they do not necessarily show where ordinary listeners actually feel the beat. The same passage can be tapped at different metrical levels (tactus) or phases; that ambiguity is itself a perceptual fact, and a single annotation flattens it.

The authors' goal therefore has two layers: produce cross-cultural evaluation data with a scalable online tapping pipeline, and keep the disagreement among listeners so it becomes a reference-free quality indicator.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把既有標註分成四類：演算法或節拍器初稿加專家校正（Beatles、GTZAN、RWC、Ballroom）、專家手動標註（Candombe、Harmonix）、打拍或拍手衍生（Hainsworth、卡納提克與土耳其 makam 資料、MIREX 評測集），以及從樂譜或錄音後設資料推導（ASAP、GuitarSet、Groove MIDI）。這個分類本身沒問題，但要看清楚這篇跟其中幾個最接近的工作差在哪。

MIREX 節拍評測集（McKinney et al., 2007） 是最直接的前身：它已經找了 40 位聽者打拍，但把每個人的拍點當成獨立的一份標註，評測時取平均。GlobalTap 的差別是把所有人的拍點匯總成一條共識格線，並改用線上群眾，人數從 40 擴到每段 114–120 人。TapCorrect（Driedger et al., 2019） 則是把打拍結果吸附到附近的音訊線索上；GlobalTap 刻意完全不用音訊特徵，只靠拍點分佈，作者也承認之後可以把兩者結合。

我認為論文沒點名、但最該對照的是 Holzapfel et al.（2012，IEEE TASLP）的 selective sampling：它用多個節拍追蹤器彼此的一致度（mean mutual agreement）在沒有參考答案的情況下找出困難曲目。GlobalTap 的 ISTC 本質上是同一個想法，只是把「多個演算法」換成「多個人」。另外，Dannenberg 與 Wasserman（2009）已經示範過不用標準答案、從多條打拍序列估計拍點與誤差分佈，KDE 匯總也是這條路線的延伸，而不是全新的估計方法。

The paper sorts existing annotations into four kinds: algorithm or metronome drafts corrected by experts (Beatles, GTZAN, RWC, Ballroom), manual expert annotation (Candombe, Harmonix), tapping- or clapping-derived (Hainsworth, Carnatic and Turkish makam data, the MIREX evaluation set), and derivation from scores or recording metadata (ASAP, GuitarSet, Groove MIDI). The taxonomy is fine, but it matters how this work differs from the closest of these.

The MIREX beat evaluation set (McKinney et al., 2007) is the most direct predecessor: it already had 40 listeners tap, but treated each person's taps as a separate annotation and averaged scores at evaluation time. GlobalTap instead pools everyone's taps into one consensus grid, and moves to online crowds, from 40 people to 114–120 per excerpt. TapCorrect (Driedger et al., 2019) snaps tapped beats to nearby audio cues; GlobalTap deliberately uses no audio features at all, only the tap distribution, and the authors acknowledge the two could be combined.

The comparison I think the paper should have named, and does not, is selective sampling by Holzapfel et al. (2012, IEEE TASLP): it used the agreement among several beat trackers (mean mutual agreement) to find difficult excerpts without a reference. GlobalTap's ISTC is essentially the same idea with several people in place of several algorithms. Also, Dannenberg and Wasserman (2009) already showed how to estimate beat times and error distributions from multiple tap sequences without ground truth, so the KDE pooling extends that line rather than being a new estimator.

方法Method

資料收集：用 PsyNet 搭配 Prolific（部分用 Cint）招募，以 REPP 收集打拍。REPP 把刺激和受試者的敲擊錄在同一條音訊裡，再對齊已知刺激來還原敲擊時間，避開瀏覽器計時抖動，一般筆電也有高時間精度。受試者先過音量校正、標記錄音檢查與練習，沒過就不能進正式任務。指示是「打得簡單、穩定、間隔均勻；速度變了就跟著調整」，刻意不指定節拍層級。總計 1,479 人、36,625 個試次、2,185,514 個拍點；基準集 746 人來自十個國家（美、韓、法、墨、埃、澳、巴西、日、波蘭、南非），專業程度偏低（正式音樂訓練中位數 1 年，Gold-MSI 平均 4.10/7）。

匯總：先把每個試次中相鄰間隔超過中位數 3.5 個 MAD 的拍點剔除，再把所有人的拍點合併，以 σ = 80 ms 的高斯核在 5 ms 格點上做 KDE，取顯著度 ≥ 最大值 0.10、間距 ≥ 150 ms 的峰值。σ 在 70–100 ms 之間結果都穩定。

格線擬合：把峰值擬合成一條近等時格線 {φ + nT}，分四步。一，在 48–214 BPM 的週期與相位上做窮舉搜尋，最大化 S(T, φ) = D̄ + 0.5·C，D̄ 獎勵拍點落在峰值上，C 是有被格線拍點對到的峰值比例。二，用附近峰值做最小平方修正，並把靠近峰值的拍點往峰值拉 45%。三，若多數拍點中點也有峰值就把拍速加倍，並補上空隙。四，若峰值分成兩群不同相位，就各擬合一條，再用另一個也獎勵均勻間距的分數三選一。

樣本數與品質指標：每段的兩組獨立 50 人，KDE 相關 r = .814、峰值 F = .880、最終格線 F = .918；20 人時格線仍有 F = .893。正式基準每段 114–120 人（中位數 117）。另提供兩個不需參考答案的指標：拍點間隔的變異係數 CV，以及 ISTC（受試者間打拍一致度：每 100 ms 格子裡有幾位不同受試者打拍，取每秒最大值再平均）。

驗證與結果：240 段驗證集（80 段 Ballroom、20 段 MIREX、100 段來自 13 個語料、40 段 GlobalMood）把格線渲染成 1,100 Hz 點擊聲疊在音樂上，130 人以 7 點量表評分，共 6,487 筆。整體 200 段配對差 Δ = −0.01（p = .231，d_z = −0.02）；Ballroom 參考版顯著較好但效果小（5.14 對 5.01，p = .007，d_z = −0.22）；混合語料與 MIREX 無顯著差異。CV 與群眾版評分的相關 ρ = −.43，ISTC 在混合語料為 ρ = .68。

在 GlobalTap 上，群眾共識與 madmom 的 F = .897、與 Beat This! 的 F = .841，兩個追蹤器彼此 F = .875，群眾與追蹤器的一致度和追蹤器之間相當。各國面板的節拍層級選擇有顯著差異（p < .001）：日本最少加倍、最多減半（7.5% / 20.1%），埃及、墨西哥、南非最常打成兩倍速（28.1%、28.0%、26.0%）；相位沒有一致差異（p = .152）。

Collection: participants were recruited via PsyNet with Prolific (and Cint for some), and taps were recorded with REPP. REPP records the stimulus and the participant's taps in one audio stream and recovers tap times by aligning to the known stimulus, avoiding browser timer jitter and giving high timing precision on ordinary laptops. Participants passed volume calibration, a marker-recording check and practice first, or could not proceed. The instruction was to keep taps simple, steady and evenly spaced and to adjust if tempo changes, with no metrical level assigned. In total 1,479 people, 36,625 trials and 2,185,514 taps; the benchmark's 746 tappers came from ten countries (US, KR, FR, MX, EG, AU, BR, JP, PL, ZA), with low expertise (median 1 year of formal training, Gold-MSI mean 4.10/7).

Pooling: within each trial, taps whose neighboring interval exceeded the median by more than 3.5 MADs were removed; all taps were then pooled and smoothed by a Gaussian KDE with σ = 80 ms on a 5 ms grid, keeping peaks with prominence ≥ 0.10 of the maximum and spacing ≥ 150 ms. Values of σ between 70 and 100 ms gave stable results.

Grid fitting: the peaks are fit with one near-isochronous grid {φ + nT} in four steps. One, exhaustive search over period (48–214 BPM) and phase maximizing S(T, φ) = D̄ + 0.5·C, where D̄ rewards beats landing on peaks and C is the share of peaks matched by a grid beat. Two, least-squares refinement on nearby peaks, with each beat near a peak moved 45% of the way toward it. Three, the beat rate is doubled when most midpoints between beats also have peaks, and gaps are filled. Four, when peaks split into two phase groups, a grid is fit to each and the three are compared with a second score that also rewards even spacing.

Sample size and quality measures: two independent groups of 50 per excerpt reached KDE correlation r = .814, peak F = .880 and final-grid F = .918; with 20 people the grids still agreed at F = .893. The final benchmark has 114–120 tappers per excerpt (median 117). Two reference-free indicators are provided: the coefficient of variation CV of beat intervals, and ISTC (inter-subject tapping coherence: count distinct participants tapping in each 100 ms bin, take the maximum per second and average).

Validation and results: on the 240-excerpt validation set (80 Ballroom, 20 MIREX, 100 from 13 corpora, 40 GlobalMood), grids were rendered as 1,100 Hz clicks over the music and 130 listeners gave 6,487 ratings on a 7-point scale. Over 200 paired excerpts Δ = −0.01 (p = .231, d_z = −0.02); the Ballroom reference was significantly better but with a small effect (5.14 vs 5.01, p = .007, d_z = −0.22); mixed corpora and MIREX showed no significant difference. CV correlated with crowd ratings at ρ = −.43, and ISTC at ρ = .68 on the mixed corpora.

On GlobalTap, crowd consensus agreed with madmom at F = .897 and Beat This! at F = .841, with tracker–tracker F = .875, so crowd–tracker agreement matches tracker–tracker agreement. Metrical-level choice differed significantly across panels (p < .001): Japan tapped least at double rate and most at half rate (7.5% / 20.1%), while Egypt, Mexico and South Africa tapped double rate most often (28.1%, 28.0%, 26.0%); phase showed no consistent difference (p = .152).

限制Limitations

規模與用途：200 段、每段約 30 秒，作者自己也說比較適合評測而不是訓練深度模型。原始音訊不重新散布，要從來源語料庫與 YouTube 自行取得，YouTube 連結會隨時間失效，可重現性有風險。

格線擬合是啟發式：只能擬合單一近等時格線，處理不了自由速度或複雜節奏。驗證集裡 ASAP（rubato 為主）群眾版只拿到 3.35/7，Hainsworth 參考版明顯較好（4.72 對 4.09，d = −1.31）。KDE 峰值落在參考拍點 ±70 ms 內的比例，Ballroom 是 90.3%，混合語料只有 56.2%。

參數在驗證集上調：網格擬合的設定是在 Ballroom、混合語料與 MIREX 上開發出來的，然後又在同一批語料上做聽測驗證，這是同一份資料既調參又驗證。作者做了單一參數擾動 18–50% 的敏感度分析（輸出 F = .947–1.000），緩解但沒有消除這個問題。

「無差異」不等於「等價」：Wilcoxon 檢定 p = .231 只代表沒偵測到差異，論文沒有做等價檢定；不過 95% CI [−0.12, +0.10] 在 7 點量表上算窄。聽者只給一個整體分數，設計上分不出分數反映的是層級、相位還是局部時間。

國家面板不等於文化背景：受試者是依招募國分組，不代表他們從小聽的音樂；招募平台以西方受試者為主，面板人數也不均（49–111）。

Scale and use: 200 excerpts of about 30 s each; the authors themselves say it suits benchmarking rather than training deep models. Source audio is not redistributed and must be fetched from source corpora and YouTube, and YouTube links decay over time, which puts reproducibility at risk.

Grid fitting is heuristic: it fits only a single near-isochronous grid and cannot handle free tempo or complex rhythm. In the validation set, the crowd version scored only 3.35/7 on ASAP (mostly rubato), and the reference was clearly better on Hainsworth (4.72 vs 4.09, d = −1.31). The share of KDE peaks within ±70 ms of a reference beat was 90.3% on Ballroom but only 56.2% on the mixed corpora.

Parameters were tuned on the validation set: the grid settings were developed on Ballroom, the mixed corpora and MIREX, and the listening validation then ran on those same corpora, so one dataset served for both tuning and validation. The authors ran a sensitivity analysis perturbing each setting by 18–50% (outputs matched at F = .947–1.000), which mitigates but does not remove the issue.

No difference is not equivalence: the Wilcoxon test at p = .231 only means no difference was detected; the paper runs no equivalence test, although the 95% CI [−0.12, +0.10] is narrow on a 7-point scale. Listeners gave one overall score, so by design the rating cannot separate metrical level, phase and local timing.

Country panel is not cultural background: participants are grouped by recruitment country, which need not reflect the music they grew up with; the platforms draw mostly on Western participants, and panel sizes are uneven (49–111).

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的地方，不是「群眾可以取代專家」，而是它把專家標註當成多種合理讀法之一。半速打拍那 9 段很能說明：參考拍點很快（間隔 0.28–0.37 秒），群眾打在偏好速度附近（0.57–0.75 秒），結果 9 段全都是群眾版評分較高（中位數 Δ = +0.75）。也就是說，有些「錯誤」其實是評測標準選了一個多數人不會打的層級。這對節拍追蹤的評測方式是直接的提醒：CMLt 會懲罰這種差異，AMLt 不會，兩者在 GlobalTap 上的落差（madmom .807 對 .956）大部分就是層級選擇造成的。

釋出的資料不只有共識拍點，還有去識別化的原始拍點與 KDE，這讓後續研究可以用自己的匯總方法，或直接把拍點分佈當成軟標籤來評測，不必接受作者的格線。

我會想看到的下一步有三個：一，把 TapCorrect 式的音訊對齊加到格線之後，看時間精度能進步多少，同時讓層級與相位仍由聽者決定；二，在 GlobalTap 上評測更多追蹤器，尤其是有 tempo 先驗的模型，檢查 madmom 的優勢是否來自它的 DBN 速度先驗剛好接近一般聽眾的偏好速度（這是我的推測，論文沒有測）；三，把同一流程用在下拍與段落邊界上，作者也提到了這個方向。

The most valuable part of this paper is not that crowds can replace experts, but that it treats expert annotation as one of several valid readings. The nine half-rate excerpts show this well: the reference beats were fast (intervals 0.28–0.37 s), the crowd tapped near preferred rates (0.57–0.75 s), and all nine got higher crowd ratings (median Δ = +0.75). Some errors, in other words, are the reference choosing a level most people would not tap. That is a direct reminder for how beat tracking is scored: CMLt penalizes such differences and AMLt does not, and the gap between them on GlobalTap (madmom .807 vs .956) is mostly metrical-level choice.

The release includes not just consensus beats but de-identified raw taps and KDEs, so later work can apply its own aggregation, or use the tap distribution directly as a soft label for evaluation, without accepting the authors' grid.

Three next steps I would want: one, add TapCorrect-style audio alignment after the grid to see how much timing precision improves while leaving level and phase to listeners; two, evaluate more trackers on GlobalTap, especially ones with tempo priors, to check whether madmom's advantage comes from its DBN tempo prior happening to sit near ordinary listeners' preferred rates (my speculation; the paper does not test it); three, apply the same pipeline to downbeats and section boundaries, which the authors also mention.

Music Inpainting With Similarity-Guided Latent Diffusion

SimDPS：從同一首歌其餘部分以前後文相似度檢索候選片段，當成缺口區域的帶雜訊輔助觀測加入擴散後驗取樣的似然項，免訓練地套在 ACE-Step 1.5 潛空間與 MAESTRO 波形擴散模型上修補 5 秒與 2 秒長缺口，並以多候選低頻頻譜損失做早期引導

Sean Turland, Eloi Moliner, Vesa Välimäki
arXiv:2611.11522 · cs.SD; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-08
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：長缺口（數秒）音訊修補有兩條路：自相似檢索直接複製歌曲別處的片段，或用擴散模型生成。前者找不到好片段就沒救，後者容易越生越偏離原曲。這篇把檢索到的片段當成缺口區域的「帶雜訊觀測」，加進擴散後驗取樣（DPS）的似然項，不用訓練、可以套在任何預訓練擴散模型上。在 MUSDB18-HQ 500 段、5 秒缺口上，多候選版本六個客觀指標都最好，例如 MFCC 距離 97.8（DPS 105.6、直接貼上檢索片段 112.1）。選它是因為它的想法簡單、可以直接拿現成音樂基礎模型來用。但要注意：客觀指標的差距大多落在一個標準差以內，聽測只有 6 段、23 人，而且效果高度依賴檢索片段本身的品質。Why this one: Long-gap (multi-second) audio inpainting has two routes: self-similarity retrieval copies a segment from elsewhere in the song, or a diffusion model generates one. The first fails when no good segment exists; the second tends to drift away from the original track as it goes. This paper treats the retrieved segment as a noisy observation of the gap region and adds it to the likelihood term of diffusion posterior sampling (DPS), with no training, usable on any pretrained diffusion model. On 500 MUSDB18-HQ excerpts with 5 s gaps, the multi-candidate variant is best on all six objective metrics, for example MFCC distance 97.8 (DPS 105.6, pasting the retrieved segment 112.1). I picked it because the idea is simple and works directly with off-the-shelf music foundation models. But note: most objective gaps fall within one standard deviation, the listening test has only 6 excerpts and 23 listeners, and results depend heavily on the quality of the retrieved segment itself.

動機Motivation

音訊修補（inpainting）要把缺掉的片段補成聽起來合理的內容，用途從封包遺失補償（毫秒級）到老錄音修復與後製編輯錯誤（數秒級）。這篇鎖定長缺口：缺掉的可能是一整個樂句、副歌或動機，模型必須維持長距離的音樂一致性，而不只是局部平滑。

傳統的自迴歸模型或時頻稀疏方法依賴訊號穩態，處理不了數秒的缺口。目前有兩種主要做法：一是自相似檢索，利用音樂的重複結構，從同一首歌的其他地方找片段貼上，對重複性強的音樂很有效，但它是確定性的，找不到夠像的片段時無法依局部細節調整；二是生成式模型，從學到的先驗中取樣，可以生出與前後文融合的新內容，但缺口越長越容易出現「身分漂移」：局部聽起來很順，卻保不住旋律或節奏位置這些屬於這首歌的特徵。

用鋼琴捲軸或文字描述來約束生成可以減少漂移，但實務上很少有這種輔助資訊。作者想要的是只用歌曲本身就能取得的約束。

Audio inpainting fills a missing segment with plausible content, with uses ranging from packet-loss concealment (milliseconds) to restoring old recordings and fixing post-production edits (seconds). This paper targets long gaps: the missing part may be a whole phrase, chorus or motif, and the model must keep long-range musical coherence, not just local smoothness.

Classical autoregressive or time-frequency sparsity methods rely on stationarity and cannot handle multi-second gaps. Two main approaches exist today. One is self-similarity retrieval, which exploits musical repetition by pasting a segment from elsewhere in the same song; it works well on repetitive music but is deterministic and cannot adapt to local detail when no close match exists. The other is generative modelling, sampling from a learned prior to produce new content that blends with the context, but the longer the gap the more it suffers identity drift: locally smooth, yet losing track-specific traits such as melody or rhythmic placement.

Conditioning on piano rolls or text descriptions can reduce drift, but such side information is rarely available in practice. The authors want a constraint obtainable from the song itself.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

兩條路線各有直接的前身。檢索那一側是 Perraudin et al.（2018，IEEE/ACM TASLP）的相似度圖修補：在歌曲其他部分搜尋前後文最像的片段直接替換。這篇的搜尋程序就是從它來的，差別是改成固定長度候選，並把結果當成擴散的引導訊號而非最終輸出。生成那一側是作者群自己的 Moliner & Välimäki（2023）擴散式音訊修補，以及其底層的 DPS（Chung et al., 2023）：用觀測到的部分算似然梯度，經由分數網路間接影響缺口。對照組裡的 RePaint（Lugmayr et al., 2022）則是每步把已知區域重新覆蓋的做法。

論文宣稱的新東西是「把檢索片段當成缺口區域的帶雜訊觀測」。這在數學上就是在 DPS 的觀測運算子裡多加一個噪聲較大的偽觀測，跟 SDEdit（Meng et al., 2022） 用參考訊號加噪當起點的精神相近，只是 SimDPS 把參考放進每一步的似然項而非初始值。多候選版本更接近 ILVR（Choi et al., 2021）：ILVR 在取樣早期用參考圖的低通成分引導，後期放手讓先驗補細節；SimDPS-Multi 也是只比對低頻頻譜，而且只在 σ ≥ 0.5 的早期步驟施加。

這些對照論文都沒有點名，也沒有拿 SDEdit 式「從加噪檢索片段起步」這個最自然的基準來比。我認為那是讀者最想知道的一組對照：如果簡單起點替換就能拿到大部分好處，這篇的似然形式就不是關鍵。

Each route has a direct predecessor. On the retrieval side is similarity-graph inpainting by Perraudin et al. (2018, IEEE/ACM TASLP): search the rest of the song for the segment whose context matches best and substitute it. This paper's search procedure comes from there, with fixed-length candidates and the result used as a diffusion guide rather than the final output. On the generative side are the authors' own diffusion-based audio inpainting (Moliner & Välimäki, 2023) and the underlying DPS (Chung et al., 2023): a likelihood gradient on the observed part reaches the gap indirectly through the score network. The RePaint baseline (Lugmayr et al., 2022) instead re-imposes the known region at every step.

The claimed novelty is treating the retrieved segment as a noisy observation of the gap. Mathematically that is adding a noisier pseudo-observation to the DPS measurement operator, close in spirit to SDEdit (Meng et al., 2022), which starts from a noised reference, except SimDPS puts the reference in the likelihood at every step rather than in the initial value. The multi-candidate variant is closer still to ILVR (Choi et al., 2021): ILVR guides early sampling with a reference's low-pass content and lets the prior fill detail later; SimDPS-Multi likewise matches only low-frequency spectra and only in early steps with σ ≥ 0.5.

The paper names none of these comparisons, and does not test the most natural baseline, an SDEdit-style start from the noised retrieved segment. I think that is the comparison readers most want: if a simple initialization swap captures most of the gain, this likelihood form is not what matters.

方法Method

問題設定：修補寫成線性逆問題 Y = M(X + ε)，M 是對角二元遮罩，缺口區間 [t_s, t_e] 落在 M 的零空間。DPS 的似然梯度只定義在觀測到的列上，缺口只能透過分數網路學到的相關性間接受約束，作者認為這就是長缺口（> 300 ms）漂移的原因。

相似度引導：引入輔助表示 X̃，假設它在缺口上是真值加上變異數 σ²_X̃ 的高斯雜訊。合成觀測 Ỹ = MY + (I − M)X̃，等效雜訊是非等向的：觀測列用 σ²_Y，缺口列用 σ²_X̃。因為兩部分作用在互補子空間，對數似然可拆成兩項相加，梯度變成 −∇(L_Y/σ²_Y + L_X̃/σ²_X̃)。兩個變異數依既有做法以梯度範數正規化，權重 ω_Y 與 ω_X̃ 控制兩種約束的平衡，設成零就關掉其中一項。

多候選：取相似度最高的 K = 15 個候選，以相似度分數 softmax 加權。因為多個候選的 Frobenius 損失會互相拉扯梯度，改用頻譜損失：沿時間軸做傅立葉轉換，只比較截止頻率 f_c = 1.6 Hz 以下的幅度，強調大結構、忽略高頻細節。這個引導只在 σ(τ) ≥ 0.5 的早期步驟施加，之後交給預訓練先驗補細節，稱為 SimDPS-Multi；單一候選版本稱 SimDPS-Solo。

相似度搜尋：在缺口前後各取一段前後文，以線性權重（越靠近缺口越重）計算候選與觀測的加權距離。潛空間版本約 25 Hz，可以窮舉搜尋，前後文約 1.0 秒；波形版本先降到 12 kHz，用 STFT（0.75 秒前後文）與色度圖（5.0 秒前後文）兩種特徵在低解析度上找初始候選，再在小範圍內以邊界連續誤差做樣本級微調。

實驗設定：潛空間實驗用 ACE-Step 1.5 的 VAE（25 Hz、64 通道）與 base Diffusion Transformer，它以 flow matching 訓練，速度場換算成分數後以無條件模式使用。因為模型傾向把結尾生成靜音或收尾段落，作者多生成 5 秒再丟掉。取樣 100 步，ω_Y = 0.5，Solo 的 ω_X̃ = 0.3、Multi 的 ω_X̃ = 0.1。客觀評測用 MUSDB18-HQ 測試集 500 段 15 秒片段，中間隨機挖 5 秒，檢索來源是同一首歌缺口以外的部分，比較六種方法：潛空間線性內插、直接貼上檢索片段、ACE-Step 內建 RePaint、DPS、Solo、Multi。

波形實驗用在 MAESTRO 上訓練的 MR-CQTdiff（44.1 kHz、6 秒片段），取樣 50 步，ω_Y = 0.3，SimDPS-Strict 的 ω_X̃ = 0.15、Relaxed 為 0.04；聽測片段 6 秒、中間缺 2 秒，以 LPC 當低錨點。

Setup: inpainting is a linear inverse problem Y = M(X + ε), where M is a diagonal binary mask and the gap [t_s, t_e] lies in the null space of M. The DPS likelihood gradient is defined only on observed rows, so the gap is constrained only indirectly through correlations the score network learned, which the authors see as the cause of drift on long gaps (> 300 ms).

Similarity guidance: an auxiliary representation X̃ is introduced, modelled as the ground truth on the gap plus Gaussian noise of variance σ²_X̃. The synthetic observation Ỹ = MY + (I − M)X̃ has non-isotropic effective noise: σ²_Y on observed rows and σ²_X̃ on gap rows. Because the two parts act on complementary subspaces, the log-likelihood splits into two additive terms, and the gradient becomes −∇(L_Y/σ²_Y + L_X̃/σ²_X̃). Both variances are normalized by gradient norms following prior work, and weights ω_Y and ω_X̃ balance the two constraints; setting one to zero disables it.

Multiple candidates: the top K = 15 candidates are weighted by a softmax over similarity scores. Because Frobenius losses across several candidates produce conflicting gradients, a spectral loss is used instead: Fourier-transform along time and compare only magnitudes below a cutoff f_c = 1.6 Hz, emphasizing coarse structure and ignoring high-frequency detail. This guidance applies only in early steps with σ(τ) ≥ 0.5, after which the pretrained prior refines detail; this is SimDPS-Multi, and the single-candidate version is SimDPS-Solo.

Similarity search: context on both sides of the gap is compared with a linear weight (heavier nearer the gap) to compute a weighted distance between each candidate and the observation. The latent version runs at about 25 Hz, so exhaustive search is cheap, with about 1.0 s of context; the waveform version resamples to 12 kHz, finds an initial candidate at low resolution using two features, STFT (0.75 s context) and chromagram (5.0 s context), then refines at sample level within a small window by boundary continuity error.

Experimental setup: the latent experiment uses ACE-Step 1.5's VAE (25 Hz, 64 channels) and base Diffusion Transformer, trained with flow matching, whose velocity is converted to a score and used unconditionally. Because the model tends to end generations with silence or closing material, the authors generate 5 extra seconds and discard them. Sampling uses 100 steps, ω_Y = 0.5, ω_X̃ = 0.3 for Solo and 0.1 for Multi. The objective evaluation uses 500 15 s excerpts from the MUSDB18-HQ test split with a random interior 5 s gap, retrieves from the rest of the same song, and compares six methods: latent linear interpolation, pasting the retrieved segment, ACE-Step's built-in RePaint, DPS, Solo and Multi.

The waveform experiment uses MR-CQTdiff trained on MAESTRO (44.1 kHz, 6 s segments), with 50 steps, ω_Y = 0.3, and ω_X̃ = 0.15 for SimDPS-Strict and 0.04 for Relaxed; listening excerpts are 6 s with a 2 s central gap, and LPC is the low anchor.

限制Limitations

統計支撐偏弱：客觀評測沒有顯著性檢定，而多數差距小於標準差。MFCC 距離 97.8 ± 50.9 對 DPS 的 105.6 ± 41.7、Boundary error 1.70 ± 0.56 對 1.98 ± 0.75，都在一個標準差內；CLAP 0.843 對直接貼上的 0.842 幾乎相同，「所有指標都最好」這句話在這一項上沒有實質意義。

聽測規模小、比較未校正：潛空間聽測 6 段、23 人，鋼琴 16 人（17 人去掉 1 人）。結果是逐段做 Wilcoxon 檢定，報告的 p 值（如 Reggae p < 0.01、Pop p = 0.02、Piano-2/3 p = 0.02）沒有做多重比較校正。而且結論本身是「看情況」：檢索片段好的時候直接貼上就很好，多候選版甚至會變差。

基準組設定不利於對手：RePaint 在大多數指標最差，作者自己歸因於 15 秒前後文對它是分布外輸入。摘要卻把「優於廣泛使用的 RePaint」當成主要結果之一，這個比較不公平。

檢索範圍只有同一首歌：重複性低的音樂、缺口落在獨一無二的段落（例如橋段或結尾）時，就沒有好候選可找。論文也沒報告運算成本：DPS 每步都要對分數網路反向傳播，100 步的代價不小。

超參數敏感：波形實驗中作者明說 ω_X̃ 是關鍵，Strict 與 Relaxed 的表現差很多，但沒說各個 ω 值是怎麼選的、是否在測試曲目上調過；多候選版本在波形域乾脆失效而被拿掉。

Weak statistical support: the objective evaluation has no significance tests, and most gaps are smaller than the standard deviation. MFCC distance 97.8 ± 50.9 vs DPS 105.6 ± 41.7, and boundary error 1.70 ± 0.56 vs 1.98 ± 0.75, both fall within one standard deviation; CLAP 0.843 vs 0.842 for direct pasting is essentially identical, so best on all metrics carries no real meaning for that one.

Small listening tests, uncorrected comparisons: the latent-domain test has 6 excerpts and 23 listeners, the piano test 16 (17 minus 1 excluded). Results are per-excerpt Wilcoxon tests, and the reported p-values (e.g. Reggae p < 0.01, Pop p = 0.02, Piano-2/3 p = 0.02) are not corrected for multiple comparisons. And the conclusion itself is it depends: when the retrieved segment is good, pasting it is already very good, and the multi-candidate variant can even make things worse.

A baseline set up against the rival: RePaint is worst on most metrics, which the authors themselves attribute to the 15 s context being out of distribution for it. Yet the abstract presents outperforming widely adopted RePaint as a headline result, which is not a fair comparison.

Retrieval only within the same song: for less repetitive music, or a gap on a unique section such as a bridge or ending, there is no good candidate. The paper also reports no compute cost: DPS backpropagates through the score network at every step, and 100 steps is not cheap.

Hyperparameter sensitivity: in the waveform experiment the authors say ω_X̃ is critical, and Strict and Relaxed differ a lot, but they do not say how each ω was chosen or whether it was tuned on test tracks; the multi-candidate variant simply failed in the waveform domain and was dropped.

討論Discussion

這篇的貢獻比較像工程上的整合，而不是新的取樣理論：它把 Perraudin 式檢索與 DPS 接起來，並示範可以直接拿 ACE-Step 這種公開音樂基礎模型、不微調地做修補。這一點對修復與編輯的實務很有用，因為多數人手上不會有專門訓練的修補模型。

從結果看，最誠實的總結是：SimDPS 主要在「檢索片段普通」的情況下有幫助。檢索片段很好時（Electro-Rock、Metal、Piano-1），直接貼上就夠了；檢索片段很差時，SimDPS-Strict 也沒有一致優勢（Piano-6 甚至是無引導的 DPS 與 Relaxed 分數較高，雖然所有方法都偏低）。真正受益的是中間地帶，例如 Reggae、Pop、Piano-2/3。這意味著實務上要先有一個檢索品質的判斷，才知道要不要啟動擴散，而相似度分數 s 本身就是一個現成的候選指標，論文沒有往這個方向分析。

我會想看到三個補充：一，加上 SDEdit 或 ILVR 式的基準，把「似然形式」與「有一個好參考」這兩個因素分開；二，把檢索語料擴大到同一歌手、同一專輯或整個資料庫，看在非重複段落是否還有效；三，報告推論時間與 ω 的選法，讓別人能判斷這套方法在實際修復流程中的成本。

This paper's contribution is more an engineering integration than new sampling theory: it connects Perraudin-style retrieval with DPS and shows inpainting is possible directly with a public music foundation model like ACE-Step, without fine-tuning. That matters for restoration and editing in practice, since most people do not have a purpose-trained inpainting model.

The most honest summary of the results is that SimDPS helps mainly when the retrieved segment is mediocre. When it is very good (Electro-Rock, Metal, Piano-1), pasting is enough; when it is poor, SimDPS-Strict also shows no consistent advantage (on Piano-6, unguided DPS and Relaxed even scored higher, though all methods were rated low). The middle ground benefits, such as Reggae, Pop and Piano-2/3. In practice this means deciding on retrieval quality first, to know whether to run diffusion at all, and the similarity score s is a ready-made candidate signal for that, a direction the paper does not analyze.

Three additions I would want: one, SDEdit- or ILVR-style baselines to separate the likelihood form from simply having a good reference; two, a retrieval corpus widened to the same artist, album or a whole database, to see whether it still works on non-repeating sections; three, inference time and how ω was chosen, so others can judge the cost of this method in a real restoration workflow.

動機Motivation

計算調性分析很依賴時間跨度：短窗看得到局部和聲細節，長窗看得到大範圍的調性區域。這讓「離調（tonicization）」與「轉調（modulation）」很難區分，因為樂理上兩者的差別就在持續時間與結構重要性。傳統單鏈隱藏馬可夫模型（HMM）調性追蹤器主要靠轉移機率控制調性的持續，並不會分別在不同時間跨度上推論調性後驗。

另一個問題出在把音高證據變成機率的那一步。調性輪廓方法衡量一段音高類別分佈有多接近大小調樣板，但要從這些擬合度算出後驗機率，必須選一個證據尺度。專家的調性標註只是單一標籤，沒有說聽者在 24 個候選調上的機率分佈是什麼，所以無法用來校準。樂曲中刻意製造的調性模糊（例如貝多芬第四號交響曲發展部延長 24 小節的減七和弦、雙焦點終止）也說明了：單一標籤之外，確實存在需要量化的不確定性。

作者因此想找一個不依賴標註答案、而是依賴樂理預期的約束：相近的調之間轉調，應該保留較多的調性連續性。

Computational tonal analysis depends heavily on temporal span: short windows show local harmonic detail, long windows show broad tonal regions. That makes tonicization and modulation hard to tell apart, since in music theory the difference lies precisely in duration and structural weight. Conventional single-chain hidden Markov model (HMM) key trackers regulate persistence mainly through transition probabilities and do not infer key posteriors separately over different spans.

Another problem lies in turning pitch evidence into probabilities. Key-profile methods measure how close a passage's pitch-class distribution is to major and minor templates, but deriving posterior probabilities from these fits requires choosing an evidence scale. An expert key annotation is a single label and does not say what a listener's probability distribution over 24 candidate keys is, so it cannot calibrate the scale. Deliberate tonal ambiguity in the repertoire (such as the diminished-seventh harmony prolonged for 24 measures in the development of Beethoven's Fourth Symphony, or the bifocal close) also shows that real uncertainty beyond a single label needs quantifying.

The author therefore seeks a constraint that relies not on annotated answers but on a music-theoretical expectation: changes between closely related keys should retain more tonal continuity.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

調性輪廓這條線從 Krumhansl–Schmuckler 演算法開始，這篇用的是 Temperley（2007）的 TKP 輪廓，以 KL 散度衡量擬合度。轉調追蹤這邊，作者點名了 Noland & Sandler（2006） 與 Nápoles López et al.（2019） 的 HMM 追蹤器，它們用轉移矩陣（有時依調性距離設定）讓調性不會頻繁跳動。這篇的差別是不用轉移矩陣，而是在每個窗獨立推論後驗，再看相鄰窗後驗的重疊，結果在圖 1 裡自己浮現出「IV、V、vi 比 i、ii、iii 連續性高」的近親順序。

但要看清楚核心技巧到底是什麼。式（1）L_i = exp[−N·D_KL(x‖f_i)] 加上歸一化，就是對負 KL 做 softmax，N 是逆溫度；作者引用的 Bissiri et al.（2016）一般化貝氏更新，也正是「調溫後驗（tempered posterior）」。所以這篇在做的事情，本質上是在沒有標籤的情況下選 softmax 溫度，跟機器學習裡的溫度縮放校準（Guo et al., 2017）是同一個問題，只是後者用驗證集的對數似然來選，這裡改用一個樂理推導的連續性準則。

多尺度那一部分則可以對照 Sapp（2005）的 keyscape：它早就把每個時間點、每種窗長的調性結果畫成三角形階層圖，讓局部離調與全域調性同時可見。這篇的 λ_cross 是在 keyscape 的兩層之間給出一個量化的相容度，而不只是看圖。

The key-profile line starts with the Krumhansl–Schmuckler algorithm; this paper uses Temperley's (2007) TKP profiles with KL divergence as the fit. For tracking key changes, the author names the HMM trackers of Noland & Sandler (2006) and Nápoles López et al. (2019), which use transition matrices (sometimes set from key distance) to keep the key from jumping too often. The difference here is no transition matrix: posteriors are inferred independently per window and the overlap of adjacent posteriors is examined, and the proximity ordering IV, V, vi more continuous than i, ii, iii emerges on its own in Figure 1.

But it is worth being clear about what the core technique is. Equation (1), L_i = exp[−N·D_KL(x‖f_i)], plus normalization, is a softmax over negative KL with N as inverse temperature; the generalized Bayesian updating of Bissiri et al. (2016) the author cites is exactly a tempered posterior. So what this paper does is, in essence, choose a softmax temperature without labels, the same problem as temperature-scaling calibration in machine learning (Guo et al., 2017), except that the latter picks it by validation log-likelihood while this paper uses a continuity criterion derived from music theory.

The multiscale part can be compared with Sapp's (2005) keyscapes, which long ago plotted key results for every time point and window length as a triangular hierarchy, making local tonicization and global key visible together. This paper's λ_cross gives a quantitative compatibility between two layers of such a keyscape, rather than just a picture.

方法Method

後驗與證據強度：x 是一個窗的正規化、依時值加權的音高類別分佈，f_i 是候選調 i 的 TKP 輪廓。似然類支持 L_i = exp[−N·D_KL(x‖f_i)]，在 24 個大小調上取均勻先驗後歸一化成後驗。B 小節窗的有效證據強度設為 N = k(h_b·B)^α，h_b 是每個樂章的和聲密度（非空和聲標註數 / 小節數），k 是整體增益，α 是縮放指數（主網格 0 ≤ α ≤ 1）。N 是有效強度，不是獨立觀測次數；α > 0 時窗越長後驗越集中。

連續性指標 λ：相鄰兩個不重疊窗的後驗 p、q，重疊 ⟨p, q⟩ = Σ p_i q_i，λ = J⟨p, q⟩ / (1 + J⟨p, q⟩)，J = 24。在 λ = 0.50 時，「延續前一窗的調性信念」與「改用均勻、不偏任何調的先驗」在等先驗權重下對下一窗證據的預測支持相等。作者也給出恆等式：記 S(r) = Σ r_i²、S_Σ = S(p) + S(q)、D² = ‖q − p‖²，則 ⟨p, q⟩ = (S_Σ − D²)/2，所以後驗的集中度與位移共同決定 λ。

語料與事件：使用 Distant Listening Corpus 3.2 版（含 DCML 標註的貝多芬、莫札特等語料）。先篩選 TKP 輪廓適用的子語料：在穩定單一調的 4 小節窗中，標註的調要在 KL 損失最低的前三名裡出現超過 70%，且至少有 100 個這種窗。40 個子語料中，7 個沒過前三名門檻、4 個窗數不足、3 個因風格（Peri、Monteverdi、Frescobaldi）排除，剩 26 個。

診斷事件選 I→i、I→ii、I→iii，理由是它們與原調共用主音或只差一個升降記號，樂理上應該保有可觀的連續性。它們與 C 大調的對稱 KL 散度分別是 0.88、0.81、0.61 nats，最近的 V、IV、vi 則是 0.45、0.45、0.37。轉調前後兩個區域都要至少 B 小節，而且每個窗的調外音時值不得超過 20%（約為穩定窗的第 90 百分位）。

事件比例 G：對每個事件，在 N = 0.01 到 1,000 的 1,200 個對數間隔點上算 λ_e(N)，記下最大重疊所在 N_peak,e 與之後第一次降到 λ* 的 N_cross,e。G(k, α) 是有效證據強度 k(h_b·B)^α 落在 (N_peak,e, N_cross,e) 之間的事件比例，也就是「後驗已過了重疊最大值、但 λ 仍高於門檻」的比例。主分析在 1 ≤ k ≤ 8、0 ≤ α ≤ 1、4 小節窗、λ* = 0.50 上計算，另做 λ* = 0.45 與 8 小節窗兩個穩健性分析。

結果：α = 0.30 時，I→i、ii、iii 的中位 λ 分別在 k = 3.95、4.66、5.21 跌破 0.50（兩兩差異的 bootstrap 區間都含零）。325 個事件（121 / 104 / 100，來自 253 個樂章）中 310 個最大 λ 超過 0.50。G 在 (α, k) 平面形成一條斜脊，最高 0.489 在 (0.25, 3.65)；G* = 0.43 時 α = 0.30 的高 G 區約為 2.95 ≤ k ≤ 3.55。λ* = 0.45 時 G 最高 0.585、脊位置從 k = 3.25 只移到 3.20；8 小節窗（191 個事件）最高 0.550，脊在 k = 3.05。

Posterior and evidence strength: x is a window's normalized, duration-weighted pitch-class distribution and f_i the TKP profile of candidate key i. Likelihood-like support L_i = exp[−N·D_KL(x‖f_i)] is normalized into a posterior under a uniform prior over 24 major and minor keys. Effective evidence strength for a B-bar window is N = k(h_b·B)^α, where h_b is each movement's harmonic density (non-empty harmony annotations / bars), k the overall gain and α the scaling exponent (0 ≤ α ≤ 1 in the main grid). N is an effective strength, not a count of independent observations; for α > 0, longer windows give more concentrated posteriors.

Continuity measure λ: for posteriors p, q of two adjacent non-overlapping windows, the overlap is ⟨p, q⟩ = Σ p_i q_i and λ = J⟨p, q⟩ / (1 + J⟨p, q⟩), with J = 24. At λ = 0.50, carrying forward the previous window's key belief and switching to a uniform, key-neutral prior give equal predictive support for the next window's evidence under equal prior weights. The author also gives an identity: with S(r) = Σ r_i², S_Σ = S(p) + S(q) and D² = ‖q − p‖², ⟨p, q⟩ = (S_Σ − D²)/2, so posterior concentration and displacement jointly determine λ.

Corpus and events: the study uses version 3.2 of the Distant Listening Corpus (with DCML-annotated Beethoven, Mozart and other corpora). Subcorpora are first screened for TKP applicability: in stable single-key 4-bar windows, the annotated key must rank in the top three lowest KL losses in more than 70% of cases, with at least 100 such windows. Of 40 subcorpora, 7 fail the top-three criterion, 4 have too few windows, and 3 are excluded on stylistic grounds (Peri, Monteverdi, Frescobaldi), leaving 26.

The diagnostic events are I→i, I→ii and I→iii, because they share the departed key's tonic or differ by one accidental and so should retain substantial continuity in music-theoretical terms. Their symmetrized KL divergences from C major are 0.88, 0.81 and 0.61 nats, versus 0.45, 0.45 and 0.37 for the closest keys V, IV and vi. Both regions around a change must last at least B bars, and no window may have more than 20% of its duration on out-of-key pitch classes (about the 90th percentile in stable windows).

Event share G: for each event, λ_e(N) is computed at 1,200 log-spaced points from N = 0.01 to 1,000, recording N_peak,e at maximum overlap and N_cross,e at the first later descent to λ*. G(k, α) is the share of events whose effective strength k(h_b·B)^α lies between N_peak,e and N_cross,e, that is, past the overlap maximum but with λ still above threshold. The main analysis covers 1 ≤ k ≤ 8, 0 ≤ α ≤ 1, 4-bar windows and λ* = 0.50, with robustness analyses at λ* = 0.45 and with 8-bar windows.

Results: at α = 0.30, the median λ of I→i, ii and iii falls below 0.50 at k = 3.95, 4.66 and 5.21 (bootstrap intervals of pairwise differences all contain zero). Of 325 events (121 / 104 / 100, from 253 movements), 310 reach a maximum λ above 0.50. G forms an oblique ridge in the (α, k) plane, peaking at 0.489 at (0.25, 3.65); at G* = 0.43 the high-G region at α = 0.30 spans about 2.95 ≤ k ≤ 3.55. With λ* = 0.45, G peaks at 0.585 and the ridge moves only from k = 3.25 to 3.20; with 8-bar windows (191 events), G peaks at 0.550 with the ridge at k = 3.05.

限制Limitations

約束有一部分是預設的：方法的核心假設是「中等距離轉調的中位 λ 應該略高於 0.50」，並據此挑出 I→i、ii、iii 當診斷事件。換句話說，結果所找到的區域，很大程度是由作者先設定的樂理預期決定的；如果換一組預期（例如認為 I→iii 應該明顯低於 0.50），高 G 區就會移動。論文把它稱為描述性約束，這個定位是誠實的，但讀者要知道這不是從資料中獨立學到的值。

G 的峰值不到一半：最高 G 只有 0.489，代表即使在最佳設定下，也有超過一半的診斷事件不在「已變尖但仍連續」的區間。高 G 區的邊界還取決於可調的門檻 G*（0.40–0.48），門檻一變，區域大小從 1,745 個網格點縮到 24 個。

沒有外部驗證：沒有拿這組參數去做局部調性估計，看準確度是否比別的設定好；也沒有任何聽測檢查 λ = 0.50 在知覺上是否有意義，作者自己也把這列為未來工作。AUROC 0.97 是跟作者先前預印本（Tsai, 2026，只用兩個 Mozart 樂章）的區域比對，屬於自我一致性，不是獨立驗證。

α 無法辨識：α = 0 時 G 已經接近最大值，連續性準則只約束了 N，沒辦法分開決定 k 與 α。

案例研究只有一段：Mozart K. 310 終樂章第 29–32 小節，作者也說只是示範。輪廓固定用 TKP，不同的輪廓（例如 Krumhansl–Kessler）會不會給出同一個脊，論文沒有測。

Part of the constraint is assumed: the method's core assumption is that the median λ of intermediate-distance key changes should sit slightly above 0.50, and I→i, ii and iii are chosen as diagnostic events on that basis. In other words, the region found is largely determined by the music-theoretical expectations the author sets first; with different expectations (say, that I→iii should fall clearly below 0.50), the high-G region would move. The paper calls it a descriptive constraint, which is an honest framing, but readers should know this is not a value learned independently from data.

Peak G is under half: the maximum G is only 0.489, so even at the best setting more than half of the diagnostic events are not in the sharpened-but-still-continuous interval. The region's boundary also depends on a tunable threshold G* (0.40–0.48); changing it shrinks the region from 1,745 grid settings to 24.

No external validation: the parameters are not used for local key estimation to see whether accuracy beats other settings, and no listening test checks whether λ = 0.50 is perceptually meaningful, which the author also lists as future work. The AUROC of 0.97 compares against the region from the author's earlier preprint (Tsai, 2026, using only two Mozart movements), which is self-consistency, not independent validation.

α is not identifiable: G is already near its maximum at α = 0, so the continuity criterion constrains only N and cannot set k and α separately.

One case study: mm. 29–32 of the K. 310 finale, which the author calls illustrative. Profiles are fixed to TKP; whether other profiles (such as Krumhansl–Kessler) give the same ridge is not tested.

討論Discussion

如果把這篇放回 MIR 的脈絡，它其實回答了一個實務上會碰到、但很少被明說的問題：用調性輪廓做機率式的調性估計時，溫度要設多少。大部分系統只取最大後驗的調，所以溫度不影響輸出；但只要後驗要拿去做別的事，例如當 HMM 的觀測機率、做不確定性呈現、或與其他模型融合，溫度就直接決定結果。這篇提供了一個不需要「機率標註」就能縮小選擇範圍的方法，這是它對 MIR 最實際的貢獻。

K. 310 案例最有意思的地方，是它示範了多尺度後驗可以同時保留兩種解讀：4 小節窗偏 C 小調（後驗 0.53–0.65），16 小節窗偏 C 大調，而在高 G 區內 λ_cross 維持在 0.47–0.58，接近等支持點。這比單一路徑的 HMM 更貼近樂理上「同主音小調借用（mode mixture）發生在大調脈絡內」的描述。作者用階層式預測處理（predictive processing）來詮釋，是合理的連結，但目前只是詮釋，沒有資料支撐。

我認為最值得做的後續是：一，用這個 N 去跑局部調性估計，與 HMM 或深度模型比較，在 DCML 標註上量化準確度與校準誤差（例如 ECE），這樣才知道這個約束在數字上值多少；二，把 λ_cross 擴展成 keyscape 式的整首樂曲圖，讓離調與轉調的區分可以用多尺度相容度來操作；三，換不同調性輪廓與更多非古典語料，看斜向脊是否穩定。

Placed back in an MIR context, this paper answers a question that comes up in practice but is rarely stated: what temperature to use for probabilistic key estimation with key profiles. Most systems take only the MAP key, so the temperature does not affect output; but as soon as the posterior is used for something else, such as HMM emission probabilities, uncertainty display or fusion with other models, the temperature directly shapes the result. The paper offers a way to narrow the choice without probabilistic annotations, and that is its most practical contribution to MIR.

The most interesting part of the K. 310 case is that it shows multiscale posteriors can hold two readings at once: the 4-bar window favors C minor (posterior 0.53–0.65), the 16-bar window favors C major, and within the high-G region λ_cross stays at 0.47–0.58, near the equal-support point. That fits the music-theoretical description of mode mixture occurring inside a major-key context better than a single-path HMM. The author's interpretation via hierarchical predictive processing is a reasonable link, but for now it is interpretation without supporting data.

The follow-ups I think most worth doing: one, run local key estimation with this N and compare with HMM or deep models on DCML annotations, quantifying accuracy and calibration error (such as ECE), to learn what the constraint is worth in numbers; two, extend λ_cross into a keyscape-style whole-piece plot, so the tonicization/modulation distinction can be operationalized through multiscale compatibility; three, try other key profiles and more non-classical corpora to see whether the oblique ridge holds.
