Revisiting Input Time-frequency Representations in Multi-pitch Estimation for Vocal Ensembles

重新檢視合唱多音高估計的時頻輸入表示

Junyoung Koh, Hao-Wen Dong
arXiv:2610.03656 · cs.SD, cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-10-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把合唱多音高估計慣用的 HCQT 換成一張 2048 點 STFT 的前 360 個線性頻率格,同一個 ConvNeXt 編碼器在五個人聲合唱測試集上的精確格準確率全部上升(jaCappella 從 0.648 到 0.683),段落層級 F1 合併提升 0.020(95% 信賴區間 0.018–0.022),特徵計算也更省。拿掉 7.7 kHz 以內、預測音域之上的泛音內容,優勢就縮小——贏的是泛音覆蓋,不是頻率解析度。Why this one: Replacing the HCQT customary in vocal-ensemble multi-pitch estimation with the first 360 linear bins of a 2048-sample STFT raises exact-bin accuracy on all five a cappella and choir test sets with the same ConvNeXt encoder (jaCappella 0.648 to 0.683), lifts pooled segment-level F1 by 0.020 (95% CI 0.018-0.022), and is cheaper to compute. Removing the upper-harmonic content between the top of the pitch range and 7.7 kHz shrinks the advantage: what wins is harmonic coverage, not frequency resolution.

動機Motivation

合唱與無伴奏人聲的多音高估計(multi-pitch estimation, MPE)特別難:各聲部音域重疊,常常唱在只差一兩個半音的基頻上,泛音在時頻圖上疊在一起。過去幾年的標準做法是用諧波常數 Q 轉換(harmonic CQT, HCQT)當輸入,理由是它的頻率解析度隨音高等比例變化,而且把各次泛音對齊到同一個音高格上,方便卷積核彙整。

代價是特徵計算很貴。人聲合唱資料少,訓練時通常從分軌即時混音(on the fly)來擴充資料,每一批都得重新算 HCQT。這篇問的是一個很基本、但沒人在這個任務上認真回頭驗證過的問題:那些看起來為音樂量身打造的性質,在合唱 MPE 上到底有沒有必要?

Multi-pitch estimation (MPE) for choirs and a cappella groups is especially hard: voice ranges overlap, singers often sit one or two semitones apart, and their harmonics pile up in the time-frequency plane. For several years the standard input has been the harmonic constant-Q transform (HCQT), on the grounds that its frequency resolution scales with pitch and that it aligns each harmonic onto the same pitch grid, which helps convolutional filters aggregate them.

The cost is feature extraction. Vocal-ensemble data are scarce, so training mixtures are usually built on the fly from isolated stems, and the HCQT must be recomputed for every batch. The paper asks a basic question nobody had carefully re-checked for this task: are those apparently music-tailored properties actually necessary for choral MPE?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

HCQT 是 Bittner 等人在 Deep Salience(2017)提出的,把多個以諧波倍率為基準的 CQT 疊成通道。Cuesta 等人的 Late/Deep CNN(2020)把它搬到人聲四重唱,之後的 Voas 系列模型再把估出的音高分配給 SATB 聲部。Basic Pitch(2022)則用同一張 CQT 平移出各諧波通道,降低成本但沒有放棄對數頻率軸。這篇跟它們的差別是整個拿掉對數頻率與音高對齊,直接餵線性 STFT,讓卷積前端自己學。

論文沒提到的是,「CQT 不一定比較好」在轉譜領域並不是新發現。Kelz 等人(2016)在逐框鋼琴轉譜上比較過多種輸入表示,發現 CQT 並沒有贏過較簡單的、以 STFT 為基礎的對數濾波頻譜;Onsets and Frames 與 MT3 這類主流轉譜模型也用 mel 頻譜而不是 CQT。所以這篇比較準確的定位是:把這個在鋼琴轉譜上已有跡象的結論,在合唱 MPE、而且在完全不做頻率軸重映射的極端設定下重新驗證,並且找出優勢來自哪裡。

The HCQT was introduced by Bittner et al. in Deep Salience (2017), stacking CQTs computed at harmonic ratios as channels. Cuesta et al.'s Late/Deep CNN (2020) carried it to vocal quartets, and the later Voas models assign the estimated pitches to SATB parts. Basic Pitch (2022) derives the harmonic channels by shifting a single CQT, which is cheaper but keeps the log-frequency axis. This paper differs from all of them by dropping log frequency and pitch alignment entirely: a linear STFT goes straight in and the convolutional front end has to learn the rest.

What the paper does not mention is that "CQT is not necessarily better" is not new in transcription. Kelz et al. (2016) compared input representations for frame-wise piano transcription and found CQT did not beat simpler STFT-based log-filtered spectrograms, and mainstream transcription models such as Onsets and Frames and MT3 use mel spectrograms rather than CQT. A more accurate framing is that this paper re-tests a conclusion already hinted at for piano, in choral MPE and in the extreme setting of no frequency-axis remapping at all, and locates where the advantage comes from.

方法Method

輸入是 44.1 kHz、窗長 2048、跳距 256 的 STFT,只取前 360 個頻率格(0–7.7 kHz,每格約 21.5 Hz),取 log(1+|X|)。輸出是 C1 到 C7、每格 20 音分的 360 個音高格,沿用 CREPE 的量化;每個發聲音高只標一個正格,用二元交叉熵訓練,解碼時直接對活化值設門檻,不做峰值挑選。

模型是兩層卷積前端加一個沿時間軸運算的編碼器。作者換了五種編碼器——ConvNeXt、TCN、Conformer、ConvNeXt V2、InceptionNeXt——隱藏維度都是 256,參數量在 1850 萬到 1940 萬之間,用來確認結論不是某個架構特有的。HCQT 的對照組用同一個 ConvNeXt 編碼器,另外也比較 Basic Pitch 式的諧波堆疊。

訓練資料是七個資料集的分軌,共 175 首、133.3 小時,每個樣本混 1 到 5 軌,參考音高大多由 pYIN 從單軌自動抽出。擴充包含隨機增益、合成房間脈衝響應與移調。評估有兩種:整首歌層級的多音高準確率(各模型各自挑最佳門檻),以及 2,894 個 30 秒驗證片段的 F1 消融(門檻固定 0.5,配對 bootstrap 信賴區間)。

The input is an STFT at 44.1 kHz with a 2048-sample window and 256-sample hop, keeping only the first 360 bins (0-7.7 kHz, about 21.5 Hz per bin), compressed as log(1+|X|). The output is 360 pitch bins at 20 cents from C1 to C7, following CREPE's quantization; each active pitch gets one positive bin, training uses binary cross-entropy, and decoding thresholds the activations directly instead of peak-picking.

The model is a two-layer convolutional stem plus an encoder that runs along time. The authors swap in five encoders (ConvNeXt, TCN, Conformer, ConvNeXt V2, InceptionNeXt), all with hidden size 256 and 18.5M to 19.4M parameters, to check that the conclusion is not architecture-specific. The HCQT control uses the same ConvNeXt encoder, with Basic Pitch-style harmonic stacking as a further comparison.

Training data are stems from seven datasets, 175 songs and 133.3 hours in total, mixing one to five stems per example, with reference pitch mostly extracted automatically from each stem by pYIN. Augmentation includes random gain, synthetic room impulse responses and pitch shifting. Evaluation has two layers: song-level multipitch accuracy (each model gets its own best threshold), and an F1 ablation on 2,894 30-second validation segments (threshold fixed at 0.5, paired bootstrap intervals).

限制Limitations

最乾淨的證據是同一個 ConvNeXt 換輸入:jaCappella 0.648 到 0.683、ACappellaSet 0.492 到 0.533、Korean 0.584 到 0.614、ChoralSynth 0.638 到 0.657,但 Cantoría 只從 0.561 到 0.563,幾乎沒差。表格裡跟 Late/Deep CNN 與 Voas 的比較則同時換了訓練資料與架構,那些差距(例如 jaCappella 0.387 對 0.706)不能歸功於輸入表示。

更值得注意的是兩個真正一起錄音的合唱集。在 ESMUC 上,舊的 Late/Deep CNN 以 0.564 贏過所有線性 STFT 模型(最高 0.499);Dagstuhl 上 Conformer 只有 0.576,低於 HCQT 版 ConvNeXt 的 0.617。作者自己也承認,訓練與多數評估混音是把分開錄的單軌疊起來,缺少真實合唱裡的聲學交互與相互調音。換句話說,優勢在最接近真實合唱的條件下最不穩。

另外三點:門檻是在評估曲目上替每個模型各自最佳化的,所有數字都偏樂觀;參考音高主要來自 pYIN,誤差會進到評估;計算成本只畫在圖裡,正文只說「大幅降低」,沒有給倍數。

The cleanest evidence is the same ConvNeXt with the input swapped: jaCappella 0.648 to 0.683, ACappellaSet 0.492 to 0.533, Korean 0.584 to 0.614, ChoralSynth 0.638 to 0.657, but Cantoría only 0.561 to 0.563, essentially no change. The table's comparisons with Late/Deep CNN and Voas change training data and architecture at the same time, so those gaps (for example 0.387 versus 0.706 on jaCappella) cannot be credited to the input representation.

More telling are the two choirs actually recorded together. On ESMUC the old Late/Deep CNN, at 0.564, beats every linear-STFT model (best 0.499); on Dagstuhl the Conformer reaches only 0.576, below the HCQT ConvNeXt's 0.617. The authors acknowledge that training and most evaluation mixtures stack separately recorded stems, missing the acoustic and tuning interactions of a real choir. In other words, the advantage is least stable under the conditions closest to real choral singing.

Three more points: thresholds are optimized per model on the evaluation songs, so all numbers lean optimistic; reference pitch comes mostly from pYIN, whose errors enter the evaluation; and computational cost appears only in a figure, with the text saying "substantially" reduced but giving no ratio.

討論Discussion

消融把「為什麼贏」拆得相當清楚。窗長從短到長掃過去,46 毫秒(也就是 2048 點)在 10、20、50 音分三種容差下都最好,更短或更長都變差,所以不是「頻率解析度越細越好」。頻率範圍延伸到 7.7 kHz 以上沒有幫助,但砍到 C7(預測音域上限)優勢就縮小。HCQT 的音高定位誤差跟最佳 STFT 設定差不多,F1 卻每個容差都比較低,表示差距也不在定位精度。

剩下的解釋是:預測音域之上的泛音帶有分辨重疊聲部的資訊,而線性 STFT 原封不動地保留了它們。這個解釋合理,但還不完整——HCQT 透過諧波堆疊理論上也看得到高次泛音,為什麼利用得比較差,論文沒有正面回答。

實務上,對需要即時混音擴充資料的小資料任務,這是一個便宜又不吃虧的預設值。作者說下一步要看樂器是否適用;鋼琴這類泛音結構穩定的樂器,答案可能跟人聲不同,值得直接拿 Kelz 等人的設定對照。

The ablations take apart why it wins fairly cleanly. Sweeping the window length, 46 ms (that is, 2048 samples) is best at 10-, 20- and 50-cent tolerance, with both shorter and longer windows worse, so it is not "finer frequency resolution is better". Extending the range beyond 7.7 kHz does not help, but cutting it at C7, the top of the predicted range, shrinks the advantage. HCQT reaches pitch error comparable to the best STFT setting yet lower F1 at every tolerance, so the gap is not about localization precision either.

The remaining explanation is that harmonics above the predicted pitch range carry information for separating overlapping voices, and the linear STFT keeps them intact. That is plausible but incomplete: through harmonic stacking the HCQT can in principle see upper harmonics too, and the paper does not directly answer why it uses them less well.

Practically, for small-data tasks that rely on on-the-fly mixing, this is a cheap default that costs nothing in accuracy. The authors name instruments as the next step; for instruments with stable harmonic structure such as piano the answer may differ from voice, and comparing directly against Kelz et al.'s setting would be worthwhile.

Rubric-Based Optimization for Text-to-Music Generation

以評分量表最佳化文字生成音樂

Ping Wang, Guang Yang, Shao-Rong Su, Junkai Wu, Pang Wei Koh, Noah A. Smith
arXiv:2610.03589 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：讓現成的音訊語言模型照一份七個面向的評分量表打分,當成文字生成音樂的獎勵:在 MusicGen-small 上,用 CLAP、SongEval、Audiobox 任何一個單一指標建 DPO 偏好,都會讓另一個指標退步,三個量表評分者則讓三者同時進步;在 ACE-Step 上用 DiffusionNFT 的最佳一組,聽者在 52 次比較中有 36 次偏好訓練後的模型。但遇到能精確量測的屬性,量表只追回約四分之一的速度增益、約六分之一的樂器增益,調性則完全沒有。Why this one: An off-the-shelf audio-language model scoring a seven-dimension rubric serves as the reward for text-to-music post-training: on MusicGen-small, building DPO preferences from any single metric among CLAP, SongEval and Audiobox degrades at least one of the others, while all three rubric raters improve all three together; with DiffusionNFT on ACE-Step, listeners preferred the best run in 36 of 52 comparisons. But on attributes that can be measured exactly, the rubric recovers only about a quarter of the tempo gain, about a sixth of the instrument gain, and none of the key gain.

動機Motivation

文字生成音樂的品質是多面向的:是否貼合提示、編曲、旋律、律動、結構、製作品質。後訓練(post-training)需要獎勵訊號,現有做法有兩條路。一條是最佳化單一自動指標,例如 CLAP 文字音訊相似度、SongEval 歌曲美感預測器、Audiobox-Aesthetics 音質預測器,便宜但各自只看一小塊,直接最佳化容易鑽漏洞。另一條是像 MusicRL 那樣收集人類偏好、訓練專屬獎勵模型,涵蓋面廣,但每換一個情境就得重新標註與訓練。

這篇測第三條路:拿現成、開放權重的音訊語言模型(audio-language model, ALM),給它一份寫明要聽什麼、怎麼給分的量表(rubric),直接當評審。要回答兩件事:量表獎勵能不能不收偏好、不訓練評審,就同時改善好幾個面向?當某個屬性能被精確量測時,量表還是對的獎勵嗎?

Text-to-music quality is multidimensional: prompt adherence, arrangement, melody, groove, structure, production quality. Post-training needs a reward, and existing work takes one of two routes. One optimizes a single automatic metric, such as CLAP text-audio similarity, the SongEval song-aesthetics predictor or the Audiobox-Aesthetics quality predictor; these are cheap but each covers a narrow slice, and optimizing one directly invites exploitation. The other, as in MusicRL, collects human preferences and trains a dedicated reward model, which is broad but must be re-annotated and retrained for every new setting.

This paper tests a third route: an off-the-shelf open-weight audio-language model (ALM), handed a rubric that spells out what to listen for and how to score, used directly as the judge. It asks two things. Can rubric rewards improve several aspects at once without preference collection or judge training? And when an attribute can be measured precisely, is a rubric still the right reward?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂生成的偏好最佳化已有不少前例。MusicRL 用大量人類偏好訓練獎勵模型,再以強化學習微調 MusicLM;LeVo 對歌曲生成器做多偏好 DPO;MR-FlowDPO 把文字對齊、製作品質、語意一致性幾個現成獎勵組合起來;DRAGON 則在擴散生成器上最佳化一整族實例與分布層級的獎勵。評審這邊,PAM 用提示 ALM 做無參考品質評估,AnyAudio-Judge 把音訊指令拆成量表條目並訓練專屬評審。這篇跟它們的差別是評審完全不訓練,品質面向以文字寫進量表,改一個面向只要改量表。

作者宣稱自己是第一個直接用現成 ALM 量表分數後訓練文字生成音樂模型的。就音樂而言或許如此,但「拿現成的多模態語言模型當生成模型的獎勵」在影像領域早已存在:DDPO(Black 等人,2023)就用 LLaVA 描述生成圖片、再與提示比對,作為擴散模型的提示對齊獎勵;文字領域的 RLAIF 與「量表即獎勵」也是同一個想法。這篇真正新的,是在音樂上把量表獎勵和精確量測的客觀獎勵正面對比,並把評審失效的方式記錄下來。

Preference optimization for music generation has many precedents. MusicRL trains a reward model on large-scale human preferences and fine-tunes MusicLM with reinforcement learning; LeVo applies multi-preference DPO to a song generator; MR-FlowDPO combines off-the-shelf rewards for text alignment, production quality and semantic consistency; DRAGON optimizes diffusion generators under a family of instance- and distribution-level rewards. On the judging side, PAM uses prompted ALMs for reference-free quality assessment, and AnyAudio-Judge decomposes audio instructions into rubric items and trains a dedicated judge. This paper differs in that the judge is never trained: the quality dimensions are written into the rubric as text, so changing a dimension means editing the rubric.

The authors claim to be the first to post-train text-to-music models directly from off-the-shelf ALM rubric scores. That may hold for music, but using an off-the-shelf multimodal language model as the reward for a generator is established in images: DDPO (Black et al., 2023) used LLaVA to describe generated images and compared the description with the prompt as a prompt-alignment reward for diffusion, and RLAIF and rubrics-as-rewards in text follow the same idea. What is genuinely new here is the head-to-head comparison in music between rubric rewards and exactly measured objective rewards, and the documentation of how judges fail.

方法Method

一般任務的量表有七個面向,權重人工設定:提示對齊 0.25、風格與配器 0.15、旋律與動機 0.10、節奏與律動 0.10、結構與結尾 0.15、製作品質 0.15、整體音樂性 0.10。每個面向列出要聽的證據,在 0、2、5、8、10 分各有錨點描述。ALM 回傳一個 JSON,解析器嚴格拒收格式錯誤(重試一次就放棄,不補零)。提示方式有兩種:一次問完全部面向(joint),或每個面向分開問、順序打亂(dimension-wise,成本七倍)。

評審有三個:專攻音樂的 Music Flamingo、7B 的 Qwen2-Audio、30B 的 Qwen3-Omni。生成器有兩個:3 億參數的自迴歸 MusicGen-small,與 35 億參數的流匹配擴散模型 ACE-Step v1,都只訓練 LoRA。獎勵用兩種方式消化:DPO 對每個提示抽 6 個候選,把前 2 名和後 2 名兩兩配對,偏好集固定;DiffusionNFT 則在每次更新時從當前策略重新抽樣,直接用純量獎勵,每次 8 個提示乘 4 個候選,跑 125 次更新。

提示來自 MusicCaps(訓練 4,416、驗證 552、測試 553)。另外從 MTG-Jamendo 建速度、調性、配器三組提示,用 Beat This 節拍追蹤(±5 BPM 內滿分)、S-KEY 調性偵測(24 調精確命中)、Essentia 樂器標記器(指定樂器相對最強非指定樂器的對比分數)當客觀獎勵,與量表獎勵在同一套 DiffusionNFT 設定下對照。

The general rubric has seven hand-weighted dimensions: prompt alignment 0.25, style and instrumentation 0.15, melody and motif 0.10, rhythm and groove 0.10, structure and ending 0.15, production quality 0.15, overall musicality 0.10. Each lists audible evidence to listen for, with anchor descriptions at 0, 2, 5, 8 and 10. The ALM returns a JSON object and a strict parser rejects malformed output (one retry, then failure, never zero-filled). There are two prompting strategies: all dimensions in one request (joint), or each dimension in a separate request in shuffled order (dimension-wise, seven times the cost).

There are three raters: the music-specialized Music Flamingo, the 7B Qwen2-Audio and the 30B Qwen3-Omni. There are two generators: the 300M-parameter autoregressive MusicGen-small and the 3.5B-parameter flow-matching diffusion model ACE-Step v1, both trained through LoRA only. Rewards are consumed two ways. DPO samples 6 candidates per prompt and pairs each of the top 2 with each of the bottom 2, with the preference set fixed; DiffusionNFT resamples from the current policy at every update and uses the scalar reward directly, with 8 prompts times 4 candidates per update over 125 updates.

Prompts come from MusicCaps (4,416 train, 552 validation, 553 test). Separate tempo, key and instrumentation prompt sets are built from MTG-Jamendo, with Beat This beat tracking (full reward within ±5 BPM), S-KEY key detection (exact match among 24 keys) and the Essentia instrument tagger (requested instruments relative to the strongest non-requested one) as objective rewards, compared with rubric rewards under the same DiffusionNFT setup.

限制Limitations

「量表沒有取捨」這個結論要配著幅度一起看。MusicGen 上單一指標的 DPO 確實有明顯取捨:以 Audiobox 排序的那次讓 Audiobox 提升 0.584,但 CLAP 掉 0.019、SongEval 掉 0.053;以 SongEval 排序的讓 SongEval 提升 0.195,Audiobox 卻掉 0.345。量表的三次則全為正,但幅度小得多,例如 Qwen3-Omni 只有 CLAP +0.0013、SongEval +0.0031、Audiobox +0.0311。動得少本來就比較不容易傷到別的指標,所以「沒有取捨」有一部分可能只是「沒怎麼動」。

聽測規模很小:7 位聽者、40 個提示、52 次判斷,36 次偏好訓練後模型(69%)。符號檢定 p = 0.008,但作者自己報的 bootstrap 95% 區間是 48–89%,下界跨過 50%,兩種不確定性的說法並不一致。而且只比了訓練前後,沒有拿單一指標訓練出來的模型做對照,無法說明聽者偏好的是量表帶來的、而不是任何後訓練都會帶來的改變。

其他限制作者大多有寫:只用開放權重 ALM;只測兩個生成器;片段很短,評不到長程結構;和聲、聲部進行、樂句等面向本來就缺少可信的自動評估。DPO 與 DiffusionNFT 的比較是兩邊各自調參後的結果,不是同一組超參數。

The "no trade-off" conclusion needs to be read alongside magnitudes. Single-metric DPO on MusicGen does show clear trade-offs: the Audiobox-ranked run raises Audiobox by 0.584 but lowers CLAP by 0.019 and SongEval by 0.053; the SongEval-ranked run raises SongEval by 0.195 while Audiobox drops 0.345. The three rubric runs are all positive but much smaller, for example Qwen3-Omni at only CLAP +0.0013, SongEval +0.0031, Audiobox +0.0311. Moving little naturally makes it harder to hurt other metrics, so part of "no trade-off" may simply be "not much movement".

The listening test is small: 7 listeners, 40 prompts, 52 judgments, with 36 preferring the trained model (69%). The sign test gives p = 0.008, but the authors' own bootstrap 95% interval is 48-89%, with the lower bound crossing 50%, so the two statements of uncertainty do not agree. It also compares only before and after training, with no model trained on a single metric as a control, so it cannot show listeners prefer something the rubric brought rather than something any post-training would bring.

The authors note most other limits: open-weight ALMs only; two generators; short clips that cannot test long-range form; and dimensions such as harmony, voice leading and phrasing lack trustworthy automatic evaluators in the first place. The DPO versus DiffusionNFT comparison is between separately tuned configurations, not a single hyperparameter setting.

討論Discussion

這篇最有用的部分其實是評審失效的紀錄。Music Flamingo 用一次問完的方式,在超過 98% 的片段上給七個面向同一個分數,獎勵幾乎沒有面向資訊;訓練後 Audiobox 掉 0.921(約為基準的 15%),但同一個評審自己打分時,七個面向看起來全都進步,信賴區間都不含零。Qwen2-Audio 讓 SongEval 最多升 1.198,卻讓 CLAP 掉 0.058 到 0.075,約基準 0.22 的四分之一到三分之一——偏好泛泛的「好聽」,犧牲貼合提示。只有 Qwen3-Omni 兩種問法都讓三個指標一起進步,最佳一組是 dimension-wise:CLAP +0.018、SongEval +0.106、Audiobox +0.339。

最佳化器的影響也很大。同樣用 Qwen3-Omni 一次問完,ACE-Step 上 DiffusionNFT 的 Audiobox 提升是 0.418,DPO 只有 0.014,約三十倍。

客觀屬性的實驗給出清楚的分工。速度:客觀獎勵在第 40 次更新從 0.268 升到 0.555,量表在第 60 次從 0.277 只到 0.353。調性:客觀獎勵從 0.043 到 0.126,量表一直停在 0.045 左右——事前篩檢就顯示 Qwen3-Omni 分不出調對不對,策略學會的是取悅評審。配器:客觀獎勵在第 20 次從 0.414 到 0.592,量表只到 0.443,第 30 次又跌回 0.402。所以結論是能量的就直接量,量表留給難以形式化的感知品質;作者附的評審篩檢流程(格式、分數塌縮、正誤分離、反事實替換)值得任何想用 ALM 當獎勵的人照抄。

The most useful part of the paper is the record of judge failures. With joint prompting, Music Flamingo gives all seven dimensions the same score on more than 98% of clips, so the reward carries almost no dimensional information; after training Audiobox drops 0.921 (about 15% of baseline), yet scored by the same judge all seven dimensions appear to improve, with confidence intervals excluding zero. Qwen2-Audio raises SongEval by up to 1.198 but lowers CLAP by 0.058 to 0.075, a quarter to a third of the 0.22 baseline: it favors generic polish at the expense of prompt adherence. Only Qwen3-Omni improves all three metrics under both strategies, with the best run dimension-wise at CLAP +0.018, SongEval +0.106, Audiobox +0.339.

The optimizer matters a lot as well. With the same Qwen3-Omni joint prompting on ACE-Step, DiffusionNFT raises Audiobox by 0.418 against 0.014 for DPO, about thirty times.

The objective-attribute experiments give a clear division of labor. Tempo: the objective reward goes from 0.268 to 0.555 by update 40, while the rubric goes from 0.277 to only 0.353 by update 60. Key: the objective reward goes from 0.043 to 0.126, and the rubric stays near 0.045 throughout; the pre-training screen already showed Qwen3-Omni cannot tell correct keys from wrong ones, so the policy learned to please the judge. Instrumentation: the objective reward goes from 0.414 to 0.592 by update 20, the rubric reaches only 0.443 and falls back to 0.402 by update 30. The conclusion is to measure what can be measured and keep rubrics for perceptual qualities that resist formalization; the paper's rater screening procedure (parse validity, score collapse, correct-versus-incorrect separation, counterfactual swaps) is worth copying for anyone using an ALM as a reward.

PEACE: Joint Embeddings of DSP Effects Code and Audio

PEACE:DSP 效果器程式碼與音訊的聯合嵌入

David Braun, Adam Finkelstein
arXiv:2610.03405 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：第一個把 Faust 效果器程式碼和它處理過的音訊放進同一個嵌入空間的系統,程式碼端用一個在 Faust 編譯器方塊圖上跑訊息傳遞的圖神經網路。把所有參數遮掉之後,光聽音訊就能從 420 種有序雙效果鏈中找回正確的那一條:R@1 17.4%、R@10 61.4%(隨機為 0.24% 與 2.4%);在訓練時沒見過的商用殘響外掛上,R@10 達 53.5%,預訓練的 AFx-Rep 只有 28.3%,LAION-CLAP 10.7%。Why this one: The first system to place Faust effect code and the audio it processed in one embedding space, with a graph neural network that passes messages over the Faust compiler's block diagram on the code side. With every parameter masked, audio alone retrieves the correct chain among 420 ordered two-effect chains at 17.4% R@1 and 61.4% R@10 (chance 0.24% and 2.4%); on commercial reverb plugins unseen in training it reaches 53.5% R@10, against 28.3% for pretrained AFx-Rep and 10.7% for LAION-CLAP.

動機Motivation

效果器外掛是音樂製作裡無所不在的東西,但沒有任何多模態嵌入能把「處理過的音訊」連回「產生它的程式碼」。CLIP 連起了圖與文,CLAP 連起了音訊與文字,作者問的是:如果把音訊和 DSP 程式碼連起來,能做什麼?

現有路線各缺一塊。用文字描述效果太粗,「冷冽洞穴裡閃爍的殘響」分不出兩組相近設定,而且人工標註昂貴。直接回歸參數是病態問題:許多不同設定聽起來一樣,例如兩個線性非時變效果交換順序,輸出完全相同。分類或圖生成方法避開了病態問題,卻只產出固定標籤或一張圖,不是可以互相比較的嵌入。只吃音訊的效果編碼器(如 AFx-Rep)能從濕訊號學到效果表示,但沒辦法比較兩段程式,也做不到從程式碼找音訊。

Effect plugins are everywhere in music production, yet no multimodal embedding links processed audio back to the code that produced it. CLIP linked images and text, CLAP linked audio and text; the authors ask what a link between audio and DSP code would enable.

Existing routes each miss a piece. Text descriptions are too coarse: "shimmery reverb in a cold cavern" cannot tell two nearby configurations apart, and human labels are costly. Direct parameter regression is ill-posed because many settings sound identical; for instance, swapping the order of two linear time-invariant effects leaves the output unchanged. Classification or graph-generation methods avoid ill-posedness but yield fixed labels or a single graph, not embeddings in which programs can be compared. Audio-only effect encoders such as AFx-Rep learn effect representations from wet signals but cannot compare two programs or retrieve audio from code.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的前身有三個。ST-ITO(Steinmetz 等人)以分類「套了哪個效果、哪組參數」的監督任務訓練出 AFx-Rep 效果編碼器;Fx-Encoder++ 能依 CLAP 查詢從整首混音中抽出特定樂器的效果表示;SLAP 把 BYOL 式的非對比目標用在語言與音訊上,不需要批次內負樣本,模態間隙也比 CLAP 小。PEACE 本質上就是 SLAP 的架構,把文字端換成程式碼端、音訊端換成 AFx-Rep。

效果鏈估計方面,Lee 等人的盲估音訊處理圖以自迴歸方式生成圖,後續有圖剪枝;Okita 與 Katayose 先用網路預測效果種類與乾訊號,再以演化策略搜尋順序與參數。這些都輸出標籤或圖,而 PEACE 輸出嵌入,能對任意長度的效果鏈做檢索。最接近的先例是 Kouteili 等人的 Sonic Pi 程式碼—音訊聯合嵌入,但那是「產生聲音」的程式,而且他們的文字編碼器在參數大幅改變時餘弦相似度仍高於 0.995,因為分詞器把數字切得支離破碎。

論文沒有點明的是,BoxGraph 的設計思路在程式碼表示學習裡早有先例:Allamanis 等人(2018)就用門控圖神經網路在語法樹加資料流邊組成的程式圖上學表示。BoxGraph 新在把這個想法用在 Faust 的方塊圖代數上,並為參數值設計可遮罩的嵌入。

There are three direct predecessors. ST-ITO (Steinmetz et al.) trains the AFx-Rep effect encoder on a supervised task of classifying which effect and parameter configuration was applied; Fx-Encoder++ extracts instrument-specific effect representations from full mixes given CLAP queries; SLAP applies a BYOL-style non-contrastive objective to language and audio, needing no in-batch negatives and leaving a smaller modality gap than CLAP. PEACE is essentially the SLAP architecture with the text side swapped for code and the audio side swapped for AFx-Rep.

For effect-chain estimation, Lee et al.'s blind estimation of audio processing graphs generates graphs autoregressively, with graph pruning in later work; Okita and Katayose first predict effect types and the dry signal with a network, then search orderings and parameters with an evolution strategy. These all output labels or graphs, whereas PEACE outputs embeddings that support retrieval over chains of any length. The closest precedent is Kouteili et al.'s joint Sonic Pi code-audio embedding, but those programs generate sound, and their text encoder kept cosine similarity above 0.995 even when parameters changed substantially, because tokenizers fragment numbers.

What the paper does not point out is that BoxGraph's design has precedent in code representation learning: Allamanis et al. (2018) learned representations with gated graph neural networks over program graphs built from syntax trees plus data-flow edges. BoxGraph is new in applying that idea to Faust's block diagram algebra and in giving parameter values maskable embeddings.

方法Method

訓練目標沿用 SLAP。音訊與程式碼各有一個線上分支(編碼器、投影器、預測器)和一個以指數移動平均(τ = 0.98)更新的目標分支;損失是四項餘弦距離之和,兩項跨模態、兩項同模態,權重 λ = 0.5。檢索時用預測器輸出 q 而非投影 z。

音訊端直接採用 AFx-Rep:7,760 萬參數的 CNN14,中/側聲道共享權重,各接一個 MLP,輸出串成 1024 維。作者比較凍結、微調、從頭訓練三種初始化。

程式碼端有兩個選擇。其一是 T5.1.1 最小版的 3,500 萬參數編碼器,直接讀 Faust 原始碼,浮點數四捨五入到小數兩位、保留參數註解、對 token 取平均。其二是 1,550 萬參數的 BoxGraph:把 Faust 編譯器 Box API 產生的方塊當節點,par 與 seq 兩種組合運算子當內部節點,參數、導線、匯流排與 DSP 函式(如 reverb)當葉節點。

BoxGraph 每個節點的初始特徵由型別嵌入、DSP 詞彙嵌入、參數值嵌入組合而成;連續參數線性投影,離散參數各有一張專屬嵌入表,並多留一列給 [MASK]。訓練時每個參數值有 10% 機率被遮罩,推論時可以全部遮掉,讓嵌入只反映「用了哪些效果、什麼順序」。訊息傳遞是 4 層共享權重,邊帶有子節點位置嵌入,以逐元素最大值彙整,每層重新注入原始特徵而不沿用上一層狀態;遇到比訓練時更大的圖,就把層數加到圖的直徑。最後只對 seq 節點與 DSP 節點取平均作為圖嵌入,這比取全部節點或根節點都好。

資料方面,21 個 Faust 效果器、各 1 到 18 個參數,隨機抽參數並以響度變化 ±35 LU 篩掉極端設定;每段 10 秒、48 kHz,串 1 到 3 個效果,共 20 萬筆訓練、8,192 驗證、4,096 測試,濕訊號統一正規化到 −18 LUFS,避免編碼器靠音量作弊。

The training objective follows SLAP. Audio and code each have an online branch (encoder, projector, predictor) and a target branch updated by exponential moving average (τ = 0.98); the loss sums four cosine distances, two cross-modal and two intramodal, weighted with λ = 0.5. Retrieval uses the predictor output q rather than the projection z.

The audio side adopts AFx-Rep unchanged: a 77.6M-parameter CNN14 with weights shared over mid and side channels, each followed by an MLP, concatenated into 1024 dimensions. The authors compare frozen, fine-tuned and from-scratch initializations.

The code side has two options. One is the 35M-parameter encoder of the smallest T5.1.1, reading Faust source directly, with floats rounded to two decimals, parameter comments kept and tokens mean-pooled. The other is the 15.5M-parameter BoxGraph: boxes from the Faust compiler's Box API become nodes, the par and seq composition operators become internal nodes, and parameters, wires, buses and DSP functions (such as reverb) become leaves.

Each BoxGraph node's initial feature combines a type embedding, a DSP vocabulary embedding and a parameter-value embedding; continuous parameters are projected linearly, and each discrete parameter has its own embedding table with an extra [MASK] row. During training each parameter value is masked with 10% probability, and at inference all can be masked so the embedding reflects only which effects are used and in what order. Message passing runs 4 weight-shared layers, edges carry a child-position embedding, aggregation is element-wise max, and each layer re-injects the original features instead of carrying the previous state; for graphs larger than in training, the layer count grows to the graph's diameter. The graph embedding is the mean over seq and DSP nodes only, which beat pooling all nodes or taking the root.

For data, 21 Faust effects with 1 to 18 parameters each are sampled randomly, with extreme settings filtered by a ±35 LU loudness change; 10-second 48 kHz clips pass through 1 to 3 effects, giving 200K training, 8,192 validation and 4,096 test pairs, with wet audio normalized to -18 LUFS so the encoder cannot cheat on loudness.

限制Limitations

兩個程式碼編碼器在混合長度的跨模態檢索上幾乎打平:AFx-Rep 從頭訓練時,微調 T5 在音訊找程式碼的 R@1 是 50.6%、BoxGraph 48.5%;反方向 BoxGraph 50.8%、T5 48.4%。這個「打平」的意思是,專門為 Faust 設計的圖結構,並沒有比一個只讀原始碼的通用 Transformer 多帶來多少。

單一效果內的檢索差異極大(圖庫 1024,隨機 R@10 約 1%):多數殘響與延遲類 R@10 在 70% 到 88% 之間(ReverbVital 較低,53% 到 64%),但 Gate 只有 2.7%、Limiter 2.8%、StereoWidth 0.9%、Panner 7.9%。作者的解釋都合理:對數 mel 頻譜看不清動態與非線性,中/側表示分不出往左還是往右偏,Limiter 遇到小聲輸入、Gate 遇到大聲輸入時根本不改變音訊,單聲道輸入也沒有寬度可調。但這也代表「聯合嵌入」的品質大半取決於音訊前端,程式碼端再好也救不回來。

其他問題:參數隨機抽樣、與音源隨機配對、音源本身不一定是乾的,都拉大模擬與真實的差距;分布外測試只有殘響,沒有使用者研究。BoxGraph 的 DSP 節點是查詞彙表的原子單位,它其實不「讀」效果器內部的程式,遇到詞彙表外的新效果就沒有嵌入可用——這點 T5 反而有優勢。監督式基準取三個種子平均,種子間 R@1 最多差 4.9 個百分點;檢索的各列看起來是單次訓練,沒有報告變異。

The two code encoders nearly tie on mixed-length cross-modal retrieval: with AFx-Rep trained from scratch, fine-tuned T5 reaches 50.6% R@1 for audio-to-code against 48.5% for BoxGraph, and in the other direction BoxGraph reaches 50.8% against 48.4% for T5. That tie means a graph structure designed specifically for Faust adds little over a general Transformer reading the source.

Within-effect retrieval varies enormously (gallery 1024, chance R@10 about 1%): most reverb and delay types reach 70% to 88% R@10 (ReverbVital is lower, at 53% to 64%), but Gate only 2.7%, Limiter 2.8%, StereoWidth 0.9% and Panner 7.9%. The authors' explanations are all reasonable: log-mel spectrograms see dynamics and nonlinearity poorly, the mid/side representation cannot distinguish left from right panning, Limiter leaves quiet input unchanged and Gate leaves loud input unchanged, and mono input has no width to adjust. But it also means the quality of the joint embedding depends largely on the audio front end, which no code encoder can rescue.

Other issues: randomly sampled parameters, random pairing with sources, and sources that are not always fully dry all widen the sim-to-real gap; the out-of-distribution test covers only reverb, and there is no user study. BoxGraph's DSP nodes are atomic vocabulary lookups, so it does not actually read an effect's internal code and has no embedding for a new effect outside its vocabulary; here T5 has the advantage. The supervised baselines average three seeds, which differ by up to 4.9 points in R@1, while the retrieval rows appear to be single runs with no variance reported.

討論Discussion

鏈長泛化顯示兩個編碼器在訓練範圍(N ≤ 3)內幾乎重合,N = 3 時表現最好;超出之後 T5 慢慢退化,R@10 在每個 N > 3 都維持在 85% 以上,BoxGraph 卻從 N = 3 的九成多掉到 N = 8 的六成出頭。把層數加到圖直徑這個技巧保證資訊能傳遍整張圖,但顯然不保證表示能外推。

參數全遮的拓樸檢索則是 BoxGraph 贏:R@1 17.4%、R@10 61.4%,T5 是 12.4% 與 43.2%;若不計順序、只要效果集合對就算對,BoxGraph 升到 27.2% 與 67.4%,部分原因正是線性非時變效果交換順序後輸出不變。跟監督式分類器比,檢索還落後:音訊端從頭訓練的分類器 R@1 21.4%,但凍結 AFx-Rep 時兩者打平(12.7% 對 12.6%)。作者的論點是分類器只能輸出訓練時見過的固定標籤,檢索則能擴展到更長的鏈。

最出乎意料的結果在殘響檢索:圖庫是 1,831 個來自四款商用殘響外掛的房間脈衝響應,跟 Faust 殘響無關。從頭訓練的 PEACE 加 BoxGraph 的 q 達到 R@10 53.5%,AFx-Rep 28.3%、Fx-Encoder++ 11.6%、LAION-CLAP 10.7%。更便宜的版本是凍結 AFx-Rep、只訓練 SLAP 的頭,R@10 也從 28.7% 升到 38.6%。這表示跨模態對齊本身能把現成的效果編碼器調得更好,不一定要重訓骨幹。

應用面,作者提議把鏈檢索接到 Okita 與 Katayose 的流程裡,取代效果分類網路:先檢索前 K 個拓樸,再交給 CMA-ES 搜參數。這是很具體、可驗證的下一步。他們也坦白引了「苦澀的教訓」,承認長期來看通用的多模態語言模型編碼器可能會勝過 BoxGraph 這種手工圖結構——鏈長泛化的結果已經有一點這個味道。

Chain-length generalization shows the two encoders nearly overlapping inside the training range (N ≤ 3), peaking at N = 3; beyond it T5 degrades gradually, holding R@10 above 85% for every N > 3, while BoxGraph falls from the mid-90s at N = 3 to the low 60s at N = 8. Growing the layer count to the graph diameter guarantees information reaches every node, but clearly does not guarantee the representation extrapolates.

On topology retrieval with all parameters masked, BoxGraph wins: 17.4% R@1 and 61.4% R@10, against 12.4% and 43.2% for T5; counting a hit whenever the effect set is right regardless of order raises BoxGraph to 27.2% and 67.4%, partly because linear time-invariant effects commute. Retrieval still trails a supervised classifier: with the audio arm trained from scratch the classifier reaches 21.4% R@1, though with frozen AFx-Rep the two tie (12.7% versus 12.6%). The authors' argument is that a classifier can only output the fixed labels seen in training, whereas retrieval extends to longer chains.

The most surprising result is reverb retrieval: the gallery is 1,831 room impulse responses from four commercial reverb plugins unrelated to the Faust reverbs. From-scratch PEACE with BoxGraph reaches 53.5% R@10 on q, against 28.3% for AFx-Rep, 11.6% for Fx-Encoder++ and 10.7% for LAION-CLAP. A cheaper variant freezes AFx-Rep and trains only the SLAP heads, and R@10 still rises from 28.7% to 38.6%. Cross-modal alignment by itself can improve an off-the-shelf effects encoder without retraining the backbone.

For applications, the authors propose plugging chain retrieval into Okita and Katayose's pipeline in place of the effect classifier: retrieve the top-K topologies, then hand them to CMA-ES parameter search. That is a concrete, testable next step.
