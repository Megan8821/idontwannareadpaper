Harmonica: Accurate and Lightweight Instrument-Agnostic Music Transcription

Harmonica：兼顧精準與輕量的樂器無關自動音樂轉譜

Longshen Ou, et al.
arXiv:2609.04640 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：樂器無關（instrument-agnostic）自動轉譜長期被單一樂器或固定樂器庫的模型主導，Harmonica 用多層次諧波卷積（multi-depth harmonic convolution）搭配「位移後聚合」（shift-and-aggregate）的參數化方式，在八個涵蓋鋼琴、吉他、混合管弦樂的資料集上，以僅 26.3K 參數的 nano 版本達到 frame F1 0.796，比同量級的 Basic Pitch 高 14.6 個百分點，推論速度達 1,622.5 倍即時；x-large 版本（15.1M 參數）則在多數資料集的 frame F1 排名第一，顯示同一套架構可以同時做到跨樂器泛化與極端輕量化。Why this one: Instrument-agnostic automatic music transcription has long been dominated by single-instrument or fixed-instrument-set models. Harmonica combines multi-depth harmonic convolution with a parameter-efficient "shift-and-aggregate" formulation, and across eight datasets spanning piano, guitar, and mixed orchestral instruments its 26.3K-parameter nano variant reaches a frame F1 of 0.796 -- 14.6 points above Basic Pitch at a comparable size -- while running at 1,622.5x real time; the 15.1M-parameter x-large variant ranks first in frame F1 on most in-domain test sets, showing the same architecture scales from extreme efficiency to state-of-the-art accuracy.

動機Motivation

現有的自動音樂轉譜（AMT）系統多半針對特定樂器最佳化，像是鋼琴專用的 Onsets and Frames、hFT-Transformer，或是像 MT3、YourMT3+ 這類序列到序列模型雖然宣稱多樂器通用，但鮮少有系統性驗證「同一個模型能不能穩定轉譜差異很大的樂器」。作者認為，樂器無關的穩健表現其實是多樂器混音轉譜的前提，如果單一樂器都轉不好，把多種樂器混在一起只會更糟。與此同時，近年 AMT 系統的參數量持續膨脹（MuScriptor 超過 3 億參數），使得在資源受限裝置上部署變得困難。Harmonica 因此把目標設定成同時解決「跨樂器泛化」與「運算效率」兩個問題，而不是只在某個資料集上刷新分數。

Most existing automatic music transcription (AMT) systems are tuned for a specific instrument -- piano-specific Onsets and Frames and hFT-Transformer, or sequence-to-sequence models like MT3 and YourMT3+ that claim multi-instrument coverage but are rarely tested for whether one model transcribes genuinely different instruments reliably. The authors argue that robust instrument-agnostic performance is actually a prerequisite for multi-instrument mixture transcription: if a model cannot transcribe a single instrument well, mixing several together will not help. Meanwhile recent AMT systems keep growing in size (MuScriptor exceeds 300M parameters), making deployment on resource-constrained devices difficult. Harmonica therefore targets both cross-instrument generalization and computational efficiency at once, rather than chasing a state-of-the-art number on one dataset.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

相關研究可分三條路線，而 Harmonica 跟其中已有的「諧波聚合」路線最接近，這也是它真正該被拿來比較的對象，不是自稱前所未有的創新。Basic Pitch 用輕量 CNN 處理各種樂器但準確度有限；MT3、YourMT3+ 這類序列到序列模型把轉譜完全交給資料驅動的自迴歸解碼器學習，不需要人工設計的樂理先驗，但也因此需要巨大模型與資料量，而且 offset（音符結束時間）預測普遍偏弱。

真正跟 Harmonica 同一條路線的是 HPPNet 的 Harmonic Dilated Convolution（HD-Conv）、TriAD 的三維諧波層，以及 SFT-CRNN 的注意力式諧波聚合——這些方法都已經在用諧波結構幫助頻率到音高的對應，但都只在單一深度做一次聚合。Harmonica 的核心差異，是把這個聚合動作重複做在網路的多個深度，並用「位移後串接、再以一個 1x1 卷積聚合」取代 HD-Conv 對中心頻率 bin 的重複參數化；消融實驗顯示這個位移聚合法每層少用 39.9% 參數，卻能達到跟 HD-Conv 相當的效果。換句話說，Harmonica 不是發明了諧波聚合，而是把既有技巧做得更深、更省參數。

Related work here splits into three lines, and Harmonica sits closest to the "harmonic aggregation" line already in the literature -- that is its real point of comparison, not a claim to a wholly new idea. Basic Pitch uses lightweight CNNs across many instruments but with limited accuracy; sequence-to-sequence systems like MT3 and YourMT3+ hand transcription entirely to a data-driven autoregressive decoder with no explicit music-theoretic prior, at the cost of huge model and data budgets and generally weak offset (note-end) prediction.

The systems actually on Harmonica's own line are HPPNet's Harmonic Dilated Convolution (HD-Conv), TriAD's 3-D harmonic layers, and SFT-CRNN's attention-based harmonic aggregation -- all of these already exploit harmonic structure to help frequency-to-pitch mapping, but each aggregates only once, at a single depth. Harmonica's actual difference is repeating that aggregation at multiple depths in the network, and replacing HD-Conv's redundant parameterization of the center frequency bin with a "shift, concatenate, then aggregate via a single 1x1 convolution" formulation. The ablation shows this shift-and-aggregate design uses 39.9% fewer parameters per layer than HD-Conv while matching its accuracy. In other words, Harmonica did not invent harmonic aggregation; it made an existing technique deeper and cheaper.

方法Method

系統輸入用 Constant-Q Transform（CQT，每半音三個頻率 bin）取代 STFT，因為 STFT 在低音域頻率解析度太差（16kHz 取樣下每個 bin 只有 7.8Hz 間距），而 CQT 的對數頻率間距讓輸入的頻率軸直接對齊輸出的音高軸，把問題簡化成「原地判斷該頻率 bin 是不是基頻」，而不是學習頻率到音高的映射，這也是模型能做到極輕量的關鍵之一。

網路架構是兩個 ResNet 區塊做前端，接一個沿頻率軸步長為 3 的卷積做降採樣，主幹則是 l 個「諧波卷積層＋ResNet 區塊」交替堆疊，最後用三個獨立的頻率分組 LSTM（FGLSTM）分別輸出 onset、sustain、offset 三張 [0,1] 機率圖，經確定性解碼器（閾值 peak detection 加 sustain 追蹤）還原成音符事件。模型從 nano 到 x-large 共五種規模，經由（通道數 c₂、層數 l）兩個參數縮放，參數量從 26.3K 一路到 15.1M。

諧波卷積本身檢查一個頻率 bin 是否為基頻的方法，是去看它在諧波與次諧波位置上的能量；Harmonica 的「位移後聚合」做法是把特徵圖沿頻率軸位移 M 個諧波偏移量、逐通道串接，再用一個 1x1 卷積把 c₂M 個通道聚合回 c₂ 個通道，取代 HD-Conv 對中心 bin 的重複參數化。

The frontend uses a Constant-Q Transform (CQT, three bins per semitone) instead of an STFT, because STFT gives poor low-register frequency resolution (7.8 Hz per bin at 16 kHz), while CQT's logarithmic spacing aligns the input frequency axis directly with the output pitch axis. This turns the problem into deciding, in place, whether a given bin is a fundamental, rather than learning a frequency-to-pitch mapping -- one of the reasons the model can be made this small.

The architecture starts with two ResNet blocks as a frontend, then a stride-3 convolution along the frequency axis for downsampling; the trunk alternates l blocks of harmonic-convolution layers and ResNet blocks, and three separate frequency-grouped LSTMs (FGLSTM) output onset, sustain, and offset probability maps, which a deterministic decoder (threshold-based peak detection plus sustain tracking) turns into note events. Five model sizes -- nano through x-large -- are set by scaling two parameters, channel width c2 and depth l, ranging from 26.3K to 15.1M parameters.

Harmonic convolution itself checks whether a bin is a fundamental by reading energy at harmonic and subharmonic positions; Harmonica's "shift-and-aggregate" shifts the feature map by M harmonic offsets along frequency, concatenates the shifted copies channel-wise, and aggregates the resulting c2*M channels back to c2 with a single 1x1 convolution, replacing HD-Conv's redundant parameterization of the center bin.

限制Limitations

論文自己也承認幾個侷限。第一，即使是 x-large 版本，onset+pitch（OnP F1）跟加上 offset 要求的 OnPOff F1 之間仍有明顯落差，顯示音符「何時結束」始終比「何時開始」難判斷。第二，跨樂器族群的表現並不均勻——比較數字撐得住這個說法：MT3 在破音電吉他資料集 GOAT 上的 OnP F1 只剩 0.291，而 Harmonica 系列在同一批資料上被形容得更穩定，但論文並未給出它在 GOAT 上的精確數字，只呈現「整體較一致」的定性結論。第三，用合成資料訓練的 Slakh-stem 表現低於真實錄音，反映合成到真實的泛化落差依然存在。整體而言，論文驗證的是「同一組參數化在八個資料集上的相對排名」，而非某個絕對分數放到任意真實錄音環境都成立。

The paper acknowledges several limits itself. First, even the x-large variant shows a clear gap between onset+pitch F1 (OnP) and the offset-inclusive OnPOff F1, meaning when a note ends remains harder to call than when it starts. Second, performance is uneven across instrument families -- concretely, MT3's OnP F1 collapses to 0.291 on the distorted-guitar GOAT dataset, and Harmonica is described as more consistent on the same data, though the paper does not report its own exact GOAT number, only a qualitative "more consistent" claim. Third, Slakh-stem (synthetic multi-instrument audio) scores lower than the real-recording datasets, showing the synthetic-to-real generalization gap has not closed. Overall, the paper validates the relative ranking of one parameterization across eight datasets, not an absolute score guaranteed to hold on arbitrary real-world recordings.

討論Discussion

這篇論文的說服力主要來自規模掃描而非單點比較：同一套架構在 26.3K 到 15.1M 參數之間畫出一條效率前緣，nano 版本比同量級的 Basic Pitch（16.8K 參數）在 frame F1 上高 14.6 個百分點，x-large 版本則在多數同域測試集的 frame F1 拿下第一，而消融實驗（位移聚合法用少 39.9% 的每層參數打平 HD-Conv）把「為什麼有效」的機制交代得比多數轉譜論文清楚。

比較弱的地方在於評語與證據的比例——論文用「maintains competitive accuracy」形容 medium 版本，但表 2 給出的具體數字（MAESTRO OnP F1 0.951、MAPS 0.866、GuitarSet 0.896）本身已足夠支撐這句話，不需要額外的定性形容詞來加強，這反而讓人想追問 x-large 相對於 medium 版本，那些多出來的參數換來的精確度提升是否值得。對於想部署轉譜模型到裝置端的讀者，nano / small 兩個版本提供的精確度與速度取捨，會是這篇論文最直接可用的部分。

The paper's persuasiveness comes mainly from the scale sweep rather than a single comparison point: one architecture traces an efficiency frontier from 26.3K to 15.1M parameters, with the nano variant beating the similarly-sized Basic Pitch (16.8K parameters) by 14.6 frame-F1 points and the x-large variant ranking first in frame F1 on most in-domain test sets; the ablation (shift-and-aggregate matching HD-Conv with 39.9% fewer parameters per layer) also explains the mechanism more clearly than most transcription papers do.

The weaker spot is the ratio of adjectives to evidence -- the paper describes the medium variant as "maintains competitive accuracy," but Table 2's actual numbers (MAESTRO OnP F1 0.951, MAPS 0.866, GuitarSet 0.896) already carry that claim on their own, making the added qualitative language feel unnecessary and prompting the question of whether x-large's extra parameters actually buy a proportionate accuracy gain over medium. For readers wanting to deploy transcription on-device, the accuracy/speed trade-off offered by the nano and small variants is the most directly usable part of this paper.

Beyond SDR: How Music Source Separation Reshapes Rhythm-Relevant Signal Properties

超越 SDR：音樂音源分離如何重塑節奏相關的訊號特性

Chuxin Ding
arXiv:2609.04224 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：音樂學者常用分離出來的鼓聲部去量測微時間差與力度變化，但這篇論文指出，大家一直用來挑選分離模型的 signal-to-distortion ratio（SDR）其實測不到這個用途真正在乎的東西。在 MUSDB18-HQ 上比較 Spleeter、HT-Demucs、BS-Roformer、SCNet-XL 四個模型後發現，onset 時間點的準確度確實跟 SDR 相關（ρ=0.62），但代表起音形狀與力度動態的指標跟 SDR 幾乎無關（|ρ|≤0.29）；更關鍵的是，只要換成用起音保真度排名，SDR 排名最高的 BS-Roformer（11.68dB）反而是四個模型中扭曲起音斜率最嚴重的，排名還輸給分數較低的 SCNet-XL（11.63dB）。Why this one: Musicologists routinely use separated drum stems to measure microtiming and dynamics, but this paper shows that signal-to-distortion ratio (SDR) -- the metric everyone uses to pick a separator -- does not actually track what that use case cares about. Comparing Spleeter, HT-Demucs, BS-Roformer, and SCNet-XL on MUSDB18-HQ, onset timing accuracy does correlate with SDR (rho=0.62), but the measures of attack shape and dynamic-envelope distortion barely do (|rho|<=0.29); more strikingly, ranking by attack fidelity instead of SDR flips the leaderboard -- BS-Roformer, the SDR leader at 11.68 dB, distorts attack slope the most of the four models, worse than the lower-scoring SCNet-XL (11.63 dB).

動機Motivation

音樂資訊檢索裡有一群研究會拿分離出來的鼓聲部去做節奏分析——量測演奏者的微時間差（microtiming）、力度輪廓——這個做法背後有個沒被驗證過的假設：分離模型會保留跟節奏測量相關的訊號特性。過去關於 p-centre（感知節拍中心）的研究已經證明，起音的銳利程度可以讓感知到的拍點偏移幾十毫秒，跟研究者正在測量的微時間差是同一個量級。換句話說，如果分離過程本身就會扭曲起音形狀，那用分離訊號量出來的「演奏者的節奏個性」有可能其實是「分離模型的個性」。這篇論文要驗證的，正是業界唯一的篩選標準 SDR，能不能保證這件事不會發生。

A line of MIR research uses separated drum stems for rhythm analysis -- measuring a performer's microtiming and dynamic profile -- resting on an untested assumption: that separation preserves the signal properties rhythm measurement actually reads. Prior work on the p-centre (perceived beat location) has shown that attack sharpness alone can shift perceived timing by tens of milliseconds, the same order of magnitude as the microtiming deviations being studied. In other words, if separation itself reshapes attack shape, then a "performer's rhythmic personality" measured from separated stems could really be the separator's personality instead. This paper tests whether SDR, the field's sole de facto selection criterion, actually guarantees that this does not happen.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文比較的四個模型，剛好對應音樂源分離十年來的世代演進，也是這篇論文的實際比較對象，而不是抽象地談「分離技術」。Spleeter（2019）是最早被廣泛部署的開源基準，用頻域 U-Net；Demucs 系列從純波形 U-Net 演化到 HT-Demucs 這種靠跨域 transformer 融合時域與頻域的混合架構；BS-Roformer 用帶旋轉位置編碼的 transformer 處理頻帶分割後的子頻帶；SCNet 則是不用注意力機制、靠稀疏壓縮 CNN 達到相近品質。

論文自己指出，SDR「跟感知品質對不齊」這件事其實已經是文獻裡的常識，先前的 p-centre 研究也早就確立了起音、時長、包絡形狀共同決定感知時間點。這篇論文真正新增的，不是「SDR 有問題」這個觀察本身，而是把發展者慣用的 BSS-Eval（SDR/SIR/SAR）跟分析者實際會用的 onset F-measure、八維力度描述子放在同一組資料上直接對照，量出兩者不對齊的具體幅度，並找出 SDR 排名跟起音保真度排名互相顛倒的具體案例。

The four models compared happen to trace source separation's decade of generational change, and they are this paper's actual point of comparison rather than an abstract discussion of "separation technology." Spleeter (2019) was the first widely deployed open baseline, built on a frequency-domain U-Net; the Demucs line evolved from a pure waveform U-Net into HT-Demucs, a hybrid that fuses time and frequency domains via a cross-domain transformer; BS-Roformer applies a rotary-position transformer over band-split sub-bands; and SCNet reaches comparable quality with a sparse-compression CNN and no attention at all.

The paper itself notes that "SDR misaligns with perceptual quality" is already common knowledge in the literature, and prior p-centre work had already established that attack, duration, and envelope shape jointly determine perceived timing. What this paper actually adds is not the observation that SDR is flawed, but a direct, matched comparison -- on the same data -- between the developer-facing BSS-Eval family (SDR/SIR/SAR) and the analyst-facing measures (onset F-measure, an eight-descriptor dynamic profile), quantifying exactly how much the two disagree and surfacing concrete cases where the SDR ranking and the attack-fidelity ranking invert.

方法Method

資料是 MUSDB18-HQ 測試集 50 首曲子，因為原始音軌是真正獨立錄製的乾淨聲部，才能拿來當作沒有爭議的比較基準。四個模型各自對 50 首曲子的 4 個聲部分離後，共 200 筆鼓聲部觀測值。開發者視角的指標用 BSS-Eval v4 的 SDR/SIR/SAR/ISR 加上 scale-invariant SDR；分析者視角則另外設計了 onset F-measure（±50 毫秒容忍窗）、從 RMS 包絡取出的八維力度描述子（強度、變異度、峰度、衝擊性、偏態、通量、起音斜率、爆發率），以及用最小平方分解還原原始聲部比例的「標記精確度」。

除了主要的四模型基準比較，論文還做了一個輸入長度掃描：固定同一段 5 秒鼓聲片段，分別放進 15 秒、30 秒、整首歌四種不同長度的上下文裡分離，在 20 首曲子上跑三個 GPU 模型，用成對 Wilcoxon 符號等級檢定比較 BS-Roformer 跟 SCNet-XL 的起音漂移是否有顯著差異，藉此檢驗「用注意力機制的模型對上下文比較敏感」這個假說。

The data is the MUSDB18-HQ test set of 50 tracks, chosen because its original stems were recorded as genuinely isolated sources, giving an unambiguous ground truth for comparison. Each of the four models separates all four stems on all 50 tracks, yielding 200 drum-stem observations. The developer-facing metrics are BSS-Eval v4's SDR/SIR/SAR/ISR plus scale-invariant SDR; the analyst-facing metrics are a purpose-built onset F-measure (+/-50 ms tolerance), an eight-descriptor dynamic profile drawn from the RMS envelope (level, variation, crest, impulsiveness, skewness, flux, attack slope, burst rate), and a "labeling precision" score from least-squares decomposition back onto the true sources.

Beyond the main four-model benchmark, the paper runs an input-length sweep: the same fixed 5-second drum region is separated inside four different contexts -- the region alone, 15 seconds, 30 seconds, and the full track -- across 20 tracks on the three GPU-based models, using a paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test to check whether BS-Roformer's attack drift significantly exceeds SCNet-XL's, testing the hypothesis that attention-based models are more context-sensitive.

限制Limitations

論文列出的侷限相當具體。第一，只量了「起音扭曲的程度」，沒有真的找聽眾驗證這個扭曲量究竟造成多少可感知的節拍偏移，p-centre 跟量測結果之間仍缺一道實驗橋樑。第二，MUSDB18-HQ 以西方流行╱搖滾為主，而且多數受測模型的微調資料集裡都用了同樣的 100 首訓練曲目，泛化到其他音樂文化的結論未經檢驗。第三，每個架構只測了一個 checkpoint，沒有排除同一架構換一次訓練種子分數就會跑掉的可能性。第四，輸入長度實驗只針對鼓聲部做，其餘聲部只當作對照組，而且固定取歌曲 30 到 35 秒處的片段，並未考慮音樂本身的樂句結構，其中一首曲子剛好落在近乎無聲的段落，成了離群值。

The paper's own limitations are quite specific. First, it only quantifies the degree of attack distortion, without a listener study confirming how much perceptible beat-timing shift that distortion actually causes -- the bridge from p-centre theory to these measurements remains untested. Second, MUSDB18-HQ skews Western pop/rock, and the same 100 training tracks underlie most of the tested models' fine-tuning, so generalization to other musical cultures is unverified. Third, only one checkpoint per architecture was tested, leaving open whether scores would shift under a different training seed for the same architecture. Fourth, the input-length experiment covers only the drum stem, with other stems serving as controls only, and it fixes the sampled region at 30-35 seconds into each track regardless of musical phrase structure, which happened to land one track in a near-silent passage that became an outlier.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最站得住腳的地方，是它把「SDR 可能不夠」這句常見警語，換成了可以直接拿來做決策的具體數字：onset F-measure 跟 SI-SDR 的相關係數高達 0.62，幾乎是 SDR 唯一能真正保證的東西；而起音斜率的相關係數只有 -0.17，力度變異度是 -0.16，即使是四個係數裡最強的起音峰度（crest）也只有 -0.29，代表 SDR 分數對「聲音形狀有沒有被扭曲」幾乎沒有預測力。真正有殺傷力的是表 3 那組排名反轉：BS-Roformer 的 SDR 比 SCNet-XL 高出僅 0.05dB，但起音斜率誤差卻是 SCNet-XL 的兩倍以上，意味著單看 SDR 挑模型，對於要研究節奏微時間差的人反而可能挑到最不適合的那一個。比較可惜的是，論文的核心證據集中在鼓聲部，對其餘聲部只當背景對照，如果同一套方法也套用在貝斯、人聲上，能不能得到一樣的排名反轉，目前還不知道。

This paper's strongest contribution is turning the familiar warning "SDR might not be enough" into numbers a practitioner can actually act on: onset F-measure correlates with SI-SDR at rho=0.62, roughly the only thing SDR reliably guarantees, while attack-slope correlation is only -0.17, dynamic-variation correlation is -0.16, and even the strongest of the four, crest correlation, is just -0.29 -- meaning an SDR score carries almost no predictive power over whether a sound's shape has been distorted. The sharpest result is the ranking inversion in Table 3: BS-Roformer beats SCNet-XL in SDR by only 0.05 dB, yet its attack-slope error is more than double SCNet-XL's, meaning choosing a separator by SDR alone could hand a rhythm researcher the worst available option for that purpose. The one gap is that the core evidence is drum-centric, with other stems serving only as controls, so whether the same ranking inversion holds for bass or vocals remains untested.

Pitch-Class Steering for Diffusion-Based Music Generation via Latent-Space Probes

透過潛在空間探針對擴散式音樂生成進行音高類別導引

Yushi Ye, et al.
arXiv:2609.04516 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：像 MusicGen、AudioLDM 這類生成模型，能給的控制大多只有粗略的文字描述，沒辦法指定精確的旋律。這篇論文訓練一個只有約 12.5 萬參數的輕量卷積探針，從 Stable Audio Open（一個潛在擴散模型）的 VAE 潛在空間裡解碼出逐幀的音高類別，推論時把這個凍結探針當成可微分的損失函數，用梯度把生成過程導向使用者指定的音高序列，不需要重新訓練底層模型。結果顯示，在 guidance scale λ=0.05 時旋律一致度從基準的 0.112 提升到 0.274，達到 2.4 倍（p<10⁻⁵），而且 CLAP 文字相似度（0.274→0.279）與 Fréchet Audio Distance 幾乎沒有變化，代表這個導引方式沒有明顯犧牲音質或文字對齊。Why this one: Generation systems like MusicGen and AudioLDM mostly offer coarse text-based control, with no way to specify a precise melody. This paper trains a lightweight ~125K-parameter convolutional probe to decode frame-level pitch-class information from the VAE latent space of Stable Audio Open, a latent diffusion model, then uses that frozen probe as a differentiable loss at inference time, steering generation toward a user-specified pitch sequence via gradients -- with no retraining of the base model. At guidance scale lambda=0.05, melodic coherence rises from a baseline of 0.112 to 0.274, a 2.4x improvement (p<10^-5), while CLAP text-audio similarity (0.274 to 0.279) and Frechet Audio Distance stay essentially unchanged, meaning the steering costs little in audio quality or text adherence.

動機Motivation

可控音樂生成目前多半停留在「用文字描述風格」這個層次，像 MusicGen、AudioLDM 都做得到「聽起來像爵士鋼琴」，但做不到「照著這串音符彈」。過去要做更精細的控制，對自迴歸模型已經有辦法（像是激活值修補、線性探針當導引向量），但擴散模型的潛在空間裡，音高這類細粒度資訊到底能不能被解碼出來、解碼出來之後能不能反過來用來控制生成，一直沒有被系統性驗證過。這篇論文要回答的問題，就是 VAE 潛在空間裡的音高類別資訊，是否同時「可被讀出」與「可被導引」——如果兩者都成立，就代表不用改動或重新訓練底層擴散模型，也能做到細粒度的內容控制。

Controllable music generation still mostly lives at the level of text describing style -- MusicGen and AudioLDM can produce something that "sounds like jazz piano" but cannot be told to "play this exact note sequence." Finer-grained control already has a toolkit for autoregressive models (activation patching, linear probes used as steering vectors), but whether fine-grained information like pitch can even be decoded from a diffusion model's latent space, and whether that decoded information can then be used to steer generation, had not been systematically tested. This paper asks whether pitch-class information in a VAE latent space is both readable and steerable at once -- if both hold, fine-grained content control becomes possible without modifying or retraining the base diffusion model at all.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把先前做法分成兩條線，而它自己刻意站在兩者中間。第一條是「改模型」路線：Music ControlNet 加一個額外的條件網路去接旋律或力度輸入，但需要改架構、重新訓練；Mustango 則是把樂理屬性轉成文字增強去間接控制。第二條是「推論期介入」路線：DITTO 與 DITTO-2 在去噪過程中加入額外的損失函數做優化，但那些損失函數要靠人工設計，也沒有利用潛在空間本身的幾何結構；自迴歸模型上則已經有用線性探針權重當導引向量、或直接注入平均激活向量的做法，但那些技巧是為離散 token 序列設計的，搬不到連續的擴散潛在空間。

這篇論文的位置，是把 DITTO 那種推論期介入，跟自迴歸模型上「用探針做導引」的做法接在一起——換句話說，它並不是發明了機率導引或探針分析本身的任何一項技術，而是把兩者接起來、套用在擴散模型的連續潛在空間上，並且證明不需要修改取樣器或重新訓練底層模型。探針本身的角色也做了轉向：先前的探針工作（像 Wei 等人分析 MusicGen 的樂理知識、Koo 等人分析注意力頭）都只把探針當診斷工具用來「看模型知不知道」，這篇論文則是第一次把同一種探針直接當成推論時的控制訊號來源。

The paper splits prior work into two lines and places itself deliberately between them. One line modifies the model: Music ControlNet adds a separate conditioning network for melody or dynamics input, requiring architecture changes and retraining; Mustango instead converts musical attributes into text augmentations for indirect control. The other line intervenes at inference time: DITTO and DITTO-2 optimize an auxiliary loss during denoising, but that loss function has to be hand-designed and does not exploit the latent space's own geometry; on autoregressive models there is already a toolkit of linear-probe weights used as steering vectors or mean-activation-vector injection, but those tricks are built for discrete token sequences and do not transfer to a continuous diffusion latent space.

This paper's actual position is connecting DITTO-style inference-time intervention with the "probe as steering signal" idea from autoregressive models -- in other words, it does not invent classifier guidance or probing as techniques, it connects the two and applies them to a diffusion model's continuous latent space, while showing neither the sampler nor the base model needs to change. The probe's own role also shifts: prior probing work (e.g., Wei et al. on what MusicGen encodes about music theory, Koo et al. on attention heads) only used probes diagnostically, to check what the model knows; this paper is the first to use the same kind of probe directly as an inference-time control signal.

方法Method

探針是一個兩層一維卷積網路，吃進 VAE 潛在張量（64 個聲道×T 幀），輸出 T 幀×12 個音高類別的多標籤機率。兩層卷積的 kernel size 都是 5，搭配 group normalization（8 組）與 ReLU，最後用一個逐點卷積壓到 12 個輸出聲道再接 sigmoid，隱藏維度 128，總參數量約 12.5 萬。訓練資料用 MAESTRO 的鋼琴音訊-MIDI 配對，2,637 個訓練片段、417 個驗證片段，並且對潛在向量加不同強度（σ∈{0, 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7}）的高斯雜訊做資料增強（加完雜訊後重新做 RMS 正規化），用 binary cross-entropy 訓練 50 個 epoch（AdamW，學習率 10⁻³，weight decay 10⁻⁵，梯度裁剪 norm 1.0）。

使用者輸入 MIDI 音符與速度，轉成逐幀的音高類別目標（刻意去掉八度資訊，只留音高類別）。導引從去噪第 40% 步（⌊0.4N⌋）開始，每兩步介入一次：把當下潛在向量複製一份、轉成 float32 丟進探針，算出 predicted 跟目標之間的 binary cross-entropy 損失 L，再取梯度 g=∇L。更新規則是 zₜ ← zₜ − clamp(λ·rms(zₜ)/rms(g)·g, ±α·rms(zₜ))，其中 λ 是導引強度，α=0.05 是每步最大更新比例上限。

這條更新規則的理論意義，是把 BCE 的梯度當成 ∇log p(y*|zₜ) 在特定去噪步驟上的近似——這跟 classifier guidance 的數學形式是同一件事，差別只在於「分類器」換成了探針、目標從類別換成音高序列。RMS 正規化讓更新量自動跟著 zₜ 的尺度縮放（rms(δ)=λ·rms(zₜ)），而 α=0.05 這個逐元素上限保證單步更新絕對不超過潛在向量 RMS 的 5%，論文認為這是防止導引把去噪過程整個帶偏、產生崩壞或雜訊假影的關鍵設計，而不是隨便設一個學習率。

The probe is a two-layer 1D convolutional network taking a VAE latent tensor (64 channels x T frames) and outputting a T-frame x 12-class multi-label probability map. Both convolutions use kernel size 5 with group normalization (8 groups) and ReLU, followed by a pointwise convolution down to 12 channels and a sigmoid; hidden dimension is 128, for roughly 125K total parameters. Training uses MAESTRO's piano audio-MIDI pairs -- 2,637 training clips, 417 validation clips -- augmented with Gaussian noise at several strengths (sigma in {0, 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7}) added to the latents (with RMS renormalization afterward), trained for 50 epochs with binary cross-entropy (AdamW, lr=10^-3, weight decay=10^-5, gradient clipping at norm 1.0).

A user supplies MIDI note numbers and a tempo, converted into frame-level pitch-class targets (octave information is deliberately dropped, keeping only pitch class). Guidance starts at 40% of the way through denoising (floor(0.4N)) and intervenes every two steps: the current latent is cloned, cast to float32, passed through the probe, and a binary cross-entropy loss L is computed against the target, then differentiated to get a gradient g. The update rule is z_t <- z_t - clamp(lambda*rms(z_t)/rms(g)*g, +/-alpha*rms(z_t)), where lambda is the guidance scale and alpha=0.05 caps the per-step update ratio.

The theoretical point of this update rule is treating the BCE gradient as a step-wise approximation to grad log p(y*|z_t) -- mathematically the same form as classifier guidance, just with the probe standing in for a classifier and the target being a pitch sequence instead of a class label. RMS normalization makes the update scale automatically with z_t (rms(delta)=lambda*rms(z_t)), and the alpha=0.05 per-element cap guarantees no single step moves more than 5% of the latent's RMS -- a deliberate design the paper argues prevents guidance from derailing denoising into collapse or artifacts, rather than an arbitrarily chosen learning rate.

限制Limitations

探針本身的解碼準確度並不高——驗證集上 micro-F1 只有 0.663、macro-F1 0.657，每個音高類別的 F1 介於 0.620 到 0.683 之間，加雜訊到 σ=1.0 時還會再掉到 0.579。論文自己也承認，導引只在鋼琴音色、且訓練資料同樣是鋼琴的 MAESTRO 上驗證過，換到跨樂器混音、音色互相重疊的場景會如何，完全未知。測試旋律也刻意設計得單純：全部取自 F 自然小調、120 BPM、一拍一音的音階或琶音，沒有測過更複雜的節奏或跨八度的旋律線。導引目前也只做音高類別控制，拿不到節奏、音域（八度）這些其他面向；而且整個方法只在 Stable Audio Open 這一個底層模型上驗證過，能不能套用到其他潛在擴散音樂模型還沒有答案。

The probe's own decoding accuracy is modest -- validation micro-F1 is only 0.663, macro-F1 0.657, per-class F1 ranges from 0.620 to 0.683, and it drops further to 0.579 at noise level sigma=1.0. The paper acknowledges guidance is validated only on piano timbre with training data (MAESTRO) that is itself piano-only; how it behaves in mixed-instrument textures with overlapping timbres is completely untested. The test melodies are also deliberately simple -- all drawn from an F natural minor scale at 120 BPM, one note per beat, scales or arpeggios only, with no test of more complex rhythms or melodic lines crossing octaves. Guidance currently controls pitch class alone, with no access to rhythm or register (octave), and the whole method is validated on a single base model, Stable Audio Open, leaving open whether it transfers to other latent-diffusion music models.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有意思的地方，是探針準確度不高（F1 約 0.66）卻能帶來很大的下游改善這件事本身——論文的解釋是，導引不需要探針每一幀都判斷正確，因為梯度是同時把所有音高類別、所有幀的資訊聚合起來，就算個別判斷有雜訊，累積方向依然有用，這也是為什麼 λ 從 0.03 一路加到 0.20，一致度都維持在 0.27 上下、不再明顯提升（推測是導引主要在壓低表現最差的少數嘗試，而不是持續拉高平均值）。

跟基準 0.112 比起來 2.4 倍的提升（λ=0.05 時 0.274，p=5.7×10⁻⁶）確實顯著，但要注意這個一致度指標本身的天花板不高：洗牌對照組落在 0.066 到 0.081 之間，比隨機猜的 1/12≈0.083 還低，原因是鋼琴複音音樂裡單音高的 pYIN 追蹤本來就有限制，所以 0.274 這個數字要放在「滿分遠低於 1.0」的脈絡下理解，不是「四分之一時間精確命中」這麼直觀的意思。音質沒有明顯犧牲（CLAP 0.274→0.279、p=0.75；FAD 267.7→260.4）這點則撐得住論文「導引幾乎零成本」的說法。

The most interesting part of this paper is that a probe with only modest accuracy (F1 around 0.66) still produces a large downstream improvement -- the paper's explanation is that guidance does not need the probe to be correct on every frame, since the gradient aggregates information across all pitch classes and all frames at once, so noisy per-frame judgments still add up to a useful direction. That is also likely why coherence plateaus around 0.27 as lambda rises from 0.03 to 0.20 without further gains, suggesting guidance mainly suppresses the worst outlier trials rather than continuing to raise the average.

The 2.4x improvement over the 0.112 baseline (0.274 at lambda=0.05, p=5.7x10^-6) is genuinely significant, but the coherence metric's own ceiling is not high: the shuffled control sits at 0.066-0.081, below the naive 1/12=0.083 chance level, because monophonic pYIN pitch tracking is inherently limited on polyphonic piano audio -- so 0.274 should be read against a ceiling well under 1.0, not as "correct a quarter of the time" in the intuitive sense. The absence of a quality penalty (CLAP 0.274 to 0.279, p=0.75; FAD 267.7 to 260.4) does support the paper's claim that the steering is nearly free.
