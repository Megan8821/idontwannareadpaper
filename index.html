SheetSage2: Coherent Lead-Sheet Transcription with Synthetic Supervision

SheetSage2:以合成監督做出前後一致的主旋律譜轉譜

Junyan Jiang, Ruibin Yuan, Jiahao Pan, Wei Xue, Yike Guo, Gus Xia, Yann LeCun
arXiv:2610.05336 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-04
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把節拍、調性、和弦、段落與旋律併成一條事件序列,單一個自迴歸模型在八個資料集的 15 組基準–指標中有 12 組超過列出的先前系統。做法是先訓練一個吃約 39 萬首(多數由 MIDI 渲染)的逐框老師,用結構化解碼產生一致的完整序列,再蒸餾給學生。全域速度錨點把 osu2017 的速度層級混用率從 Beat This! 的 20.74% 壓到 1.48%。Why this one: Folding beats, key, chords, sections and melody into one event sequence, a single autoregressive model beats the listed prior systems on 12 of 15 benchmark-metric pairs across eight datasets. The recipe is to train a frame-wise teacher on about 394,000 songs (mostly rendered from MIDI), use structured decoding to turn its outputs into consistent complete sequences, and distill those into a student. A global tempo anchor cuts the share of osu2017 recordings that mix tempo levels from 20.74% with Beat This! to 1.48%.

動機Motivation

Lead sheet(主旋律譜)要同時寫出拍點與小節線、拍號、速度、和弦記號、調號、旋律與段落。MIR 傳統上把這些拆成獨立任務,各有成熟模型,但拼起來不保證彼此相容:音名拼法要看局部調性,音符位置與時值必須落在同一張拍點網格上。一首歌第一段沒有鼓、第二段有鼓,節拍追蹤器可能前段判 80 BPM、後段判 160 BPM,記譜出來同一段旋律就變成兩種時值。

第二個問題是資料。端到端的序列模型(TUTTI、MuScriptor)需要大量對齊好的「音訊–完整樂譜」配對,但現有資料集多半只標了節拍、和弦或旋律其中一部分,時間也常不準。SheetSage2 想同時處理「局部預測準但整體不一致」與「沒有完整標註」這兩件事。

A lead sheet has to state beats and bar lines, meter, tempo, chord symbols, key signature, melody and sections all at once. MIR has traditionally split these into separate tasks, each with mature models, but stitching them together does not guarantee compatibility: pitch spelling depends on the local key, and note positions and durations must sit on one shared beat grid. If a song's first verse has no drums and the second does, a beat tracker may call the first 80 BPM and the second 160 BPM, and the same melody gets notated with two different sets of durations.

The second problem is data. End-to-end sequence models (TUTTI, MuScriptor) need large numbers of aligned audio-to-full-score pairs, but existing datasets mostly annotate only beats, chords or melody, often with imprecise timing. SheetSage2 tries to address both problems: accurate local predictions that are globally inconsistent, and the absence of complete annotations.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的前身是 SheetSage1(Donahue 等人,2022):用 Jukebox 表徵加 Hooktheory 標註做旋律轉譜,節拍沿用 madmom,和弦與調性另外估,最後拼成樂譜。SheetSage2 換成 MERT2-FS 編碼器,並把所有屬性放進同一條事件序列。另一端是 MT3(Gardner 等人,2022)與 MuScriptor 這類多樂器轉譜,輸出的是音符事件,不是帶拍號與和弦記號的可讀樂譜。

節拍部分,madmom 的 DBN(Böck 等人,2016)只在局部建模速度,Beat This!(2024)乾脆拿掉 DBN。SheetSage2「先選一個全域速度錨點、再允許局部相對變化」的做法,其實很像 Davies 與 Plumbley(2007)的情境相依節拍追蹤:先做速度歸納,再在固定速度假設下追拍。論文沒有引這條線。

三段式流程的核心——用帶結構化解碼的老師產生完整序列,再讓自迴歸學生去學——作者自己承認就是 Kim 與 Rush(2016)的序列層級知識蒸餾;用 MIDI 合成音訊當監督則延續 Slakh(2019)。旋律「依前一個音的音程類別打分」與 TONet(2022)把音高拆成音級與八度,同屬對付八度錯誤的路線。真正新的地方是把這些現成元件串成一條能吃部分標註的管線,並把結果收斂到單一模型。

The direct predecessor is SheetSage1 (Donahue et al., 2022): melody transcription from Jukebox representations with Hooktheory labels, beats taken from madmom, chords and key estimated separately, and everything stitched into a score. SheetSage2 switches to a MERT2-FS encoder and puts every attribute into one event sequence. At the other end, multi-instrument transcribers such as MT3 (Gardner et al., 2022) and MuScriptor output note events, not a readable score with meter and chord symbols.

For rhythm, madmom's DBN (Böck et al., 2016) models tempo only locally, and Beat This! (2024) drops the DBN entirely. SheetSage2's choice of one global tempo anchor with local relative variation closely resembles Davies and Plumbley's (2007) context-dependent beat tracking, which induces tempo first and then tracks beats under a fixed-tempo assumption. The paper does not cite that line of work.

The core of the three-stage pipeline, a teacher with structured decoding producing complete sequences for an autoregressive student, is by the authors' own account sequence-level knowledge distillation (Kim and Rush, 2016); using MIDI-rendered audio as supervision follows Slakh (2019). Scoring melody notes by interval class relative to the previous note belongs to the same family of octave-error fixes as TONet (2022), which splits pitch into chroma and octave. What is new is chaining these existing pieces into a pipeline that tolerates partial labels and collapsing the result into one model.

方法Method

第一步是合成資料。Los Angeles MIDI Dataset 有 404,714 個去重檔案,但其中的調性、和弦、旋律軌資訊常不可靠(Beatles MIDI 的調性 MIREX 分數只有 0.400)。作者先用專家規則或軌道名稱建立高精確度的種子集,再微調預訓練的符號模型當自動標註器。以旋律為例,軌名含「melody」的 17,134 個檔案擴充到 185,887 首,約 10.8 倍。和弦與調性則是把明確透露和弦的那一軌拿掉,讓模型從其餘聲部推回來。全部用 FluidSynth 與 FluidR3 GM 音色庫渲染成 24 kHz 音訊。

第二步是 Prober,兩個各自微調 MERT2-FS 的逐框模型。非旋律模型以 25 Hz 輸出 160 個二元特徵:拍點、拍號分母、中位速度、相對速度、和弦根音與成分、調性、段落邊界與 23 類段落標籤。旋律模型在預測出的拍點網格的十六分音符細分上運作,輸出「音高 × 音程類別」的分類分佈。逐框輸出對標註裡數十毫秒的時間誤差較不敏感,缺的標籤直接遮掉。訓練資料共 393,980 首、22,016.7 小時,其中真實錄音只有 32,755 首。

第三步是六階段結構化解碼,每一階段固定的決定約束下一階段:(1) 對整首的中位速度啟動值取平均,在 60–240 BPM 選一個錨點;(2) 在錨點條件下用動態規劃聯合解出八分音符脈衝與局部速度,相鄰間隔的 log 比值有懲罰;(3) 在脈衝上解小節位置與拍號變化,允許不完整小節;(4) 調性與段落只能在強拍改變;(5) 和弦邊界對齊拍點,拼法由局部調性決定;(6) 旋律用七個音程桶(以前一個音為基準)打分再做動態規劃,避免把 C4–E4–G4 轉成 C5–E4–G4 這種局部八度跳動。

最後是蒸餾。SheetSage2-AR 由 MERT2-FS 編碼器加自迴歸解碼器組成,只用 Prober 在 441,094 段錄音(28,434.3 小時,主要是 CC0 與授權的合成音樂)上產生的偽標註訓練。事件語言混合 10 ms 量化的絕對拍點時間與十六分音符網格上的相對位置;推論時以文法限制做貪婪解碼,輸出可轉成 ABC 或 MIDI。

Step one is synthetic data. The Los Angeles MIDI Dataset has 404,714 deduplicated files, but their key, chord and melody-track information is often unreliable (Beatles MIDI files score only 0.400 on the MIREX key metric). The authors build high-precision seed sets from expert rules or track names, then fine-tune a pretrained symbolic model as an auto-labeler. For melody, 17,134 files with tracks named "melody" expand to 185,887 songs, about 10.8x. For chords and key, the track that states the harmony is removed and the model learns to recover it from the remaining parts. Everything is rendered to 24 kHz audio with FluidSynth and the FluidR3 GM SoundFont.

Step two is the Prober: two frame-wise models, each with its own fine-tuned MERT2-FS. The non-melody model outputs 160 binary features at 25 Hz: beat events, meter denominator, median tempo, relative tempo, chord root and components, key, section boundaries and 23 section labels. The melody model runs on sixteenth-note subdivisions of the predicted beat grid and outputs a categorical distribution over pitch-by-interval-class. Frame-wise outputs are less sensitive to annotation timing errors of tens of milliseconds, and missing labels are masked. Training covers 393,980 songs and 22,016.7 hours, of which only 32,755 songs are real recordings.

Step three is a six-stage structured decoder in which each fixed decision constrains the next: (1) pool median-tempo activations over the recording and pick one anchor in 60-240 BPM; (2) jointly decode eighth-note pulses and local tempo by dynamic programming given the anchor, penalizing log-ratio jumps between adjacent intervals; (3) decode bar positions and meter changes on the pulses, allowing incomplete bars; (4) let key and section change only at downbeats; (5) align chord boundaries to beats and spell them from the local key; (6) score melody onsets with seven interval buckets relative to the previous note and run dynamic programming, so C4-E4-G4 is not transcribed as C5-E4-G4.

The last step is distillation. SheetSage2-AR is a MERT2-FS encoder with an autoregressive decoder, trained only on Prober pseudo-labels for 441,094 recordings (28,434.3 hours, mostly CC0 and licensed synthetic music). The event language mixes absolute beat times quantized to 10 ms with relative positions on a sixteenth-note grid; inference is grammar-constrained greedy decoding, and the output converts to ABC or MIDI.

限制Limitations

作者的限制章節寫得清楚:十六分音符網格表示不了三連音;全曲單一速度錨點不適合大幅變速的作品;和弦詞彙只到七和弦,九和弦等延伸和弦無法表示;訓練與評估以穩定節拍的流行音樂為主,不適用古典或自由節奏音樂。

論文沒強調的有幾點。第一,一致性實驗中 Hooktheory 的部分與訓練資料重疊,附錄自己註明不是 held-out;真正獨立的只有 osu2017 的 135 首與 RWC 的 333 段,而 osu2017 是第一作者(Jiang, 2026)釋出的資料集。第二,「先前專家系統」那一欄的設定各不相同:ChordFormer 在 osu2017 與 JAAH 用五模型集成、在 Chords1217 用逐曲留出模型,madmom 的和弦模型與 Chords1217 有訓練重疊。作者都有標註,但讀表時要記得那不是同一把尺。

第三,AR 沒贏的三組:GTZAN 節拍 F1 86.27 對 Beat This! 89.01,HarmonixSet 0.5 秒邊界 F1 67.96 對 SongFormer 70.63,Chords1217 的 83.81 略低於 ChordFormer 的 83.94。第四,合成資料的增益並不平均:節拍 +2.17、強拍 +3.71、和弦 +1.57、段落 +1.58,但旋律只有 +0.01、調性 −0.24。最後,部分訓練資料是私有或授權的,權重雖然公開,訓練流程無法完整重現。

The authors' limitations section is clear: a sixteenth-note grid cannot represent triplets; one tempo anchor per recording does not suit pieces with large tempo changes; the chord vocabulary stops at sevenths, so ninths and other extended chords cannot be represented; and training and evaluation center on popular music with a steady beat, not classical or free-rhythm music.

A few points get less emphasis. First, the Hooktheory portion of the consistency experiments overlaps the training data, as the appendix itself notes; the only independent sets are 135 osu2017 recordings and 333 RWC passages, and osu2017 was released by the first author (Jiang, 2026). Second, the prior-specialist column mixes settings: ChordFormer is a five-model ensemble on osu2017 and JAAH and a per-track held-out model on Chords1217, and madmom's chord model overlaps Chords1217 in training. The authors flag all of this, but the column is not one common yardstick.

Third, the three pairs AR does not win: GTZAN beat F1 86.27 against 89.01 for Beat This!, HarmonixSet 0.5-second boundary F1 67.96 against 70.63 for SongFormer, and Chords1217 at 83.81, just below ChordFormer's 83.94. Fourth, the gain from synthetic data is uneven: beat +2.17, downbeat +3.71, chord +1.57, structure +1.58, but melody only +0.01 and key -0.24. Finally, part of the training data is private or licensed, so although the weights are public, training cannot be fully reproduced.

討論Discussion

最值得看的是一致性數字,全域速度錨點的效果比 F1 明顯得多。osu2017 的速度層級混用率:Beat This! 20.74%,Prober 2.96%,同一組 Prober 啟動值改接 madmom DBN 是 3.70%,AR 1.48%;Hooktheory 上分別是 17.30%、0.34%、1.36%、0.51%。旋律的八度混用率在 RWC 的 333 段上,SheetSage1 是 34.83%,完整解碼 9.31%,拿掉前一音依賴的版本 12.91%,AR 11.71%。換句話說,音程依賴打分本身貢獻約 3.6 個百分點,大部分改進來自整個系統的換代。

蒸餾的結果比預期好:學生在 15 組中有 10 組超過老師,包括所有節拍與強拍項目(例如 GTZAN 強拍 F1 從 78.74 到 80.45)。這與 Kim 與 Rush 在機器翻譯看到的現象一致——結構化解碼產生的乾淨目標本身就有正則化效果。旋律上的跳躍最大:RWC-Pop 人聲 F1 從 SheetSage1 的 62.71 到 82.51,Rock Corpus 從 49.19 到 67.08。附錄還顯示 MuScriptor、YourMT3+、Demucs+ROSVOT 的人聲旋律分數都遠低於 SheetSage1,最接近的 YourMT3+ 也差 13.59 分。

一個有意思的旁證是:只用合成資料訓練的 Prober,在 RWC-Pop 人聲旋律仍有 57.76% F1,即使合成音訊裡完全沒有人聲。這暗示預訓練編碼器已經把「主旋律」抽象到足以跨音色轉移。作者在結論也坦白,資料更多時一些明確的音樂約束可能就不必要了。對使用者來說,這篇的實際價值是一個公開權重、直接輸出 ABC 的流行音樂 lead sheet 轉譜器;對研究者來說,是「部分標註 → 結構化老師 → 統一學生」這套配方。

The consistency numbers are the part to read, and the global tempo anchor shows up far more clearly there than in F1. Tempo-level mixing on osu2017: Beat This! 20.74%, Prober 2.96%, the same Prober activations decoded by madmom's DBN 3.70%, AR 1.48%; on Hooktheory, 17.30%, 0.34%, 1.36% and 0.51%. Octave mixing on 333 RWC passages: SheetSage1 34.83%, full decoding 9.31%, the variant without previous-pitch dependence 12.91%, AR 11.71%. In other words, interval-dependent scoring itself contributes about 3.6 percentage points; most of the improvement comes from the system as a whole.

Distillation works better than one might expect: the student beats the teacher on 10 of 15 pairs, including every beat and downbeat entry (GTZAN downbeat F1 from 78.74 to 80.45, for example). This matches what Kim and Rush saw in machine translation: clean targets from structured decoding act as a regularizer. The biggest jump is in melody: RWC-Pop vocal F1 goes from 62.71 for SheetSage1 to 82.51, and Rock Corpus from 49.19 to 67.08. The appendix also shows MuScriptor, YourMT3+ and Demucs+ROSVOT all scoring far below SheetSage1 on vocal melody; the closest, YourMT3+, trails by 13.59 points.

One interesting side result: a Prober trained only on synthetic data still reaches 57.76% vocal-melody F1 on RWC-Pop, although the synthetic audio contains no singing at all. That suggests the pretrained encoder abstracts the main melody well enough to transfer across timbres. The authors' conclusion admits that with more data some explicit musical constraints may become unnecessary. For users, the practical value is an open-weight pop lead-sheet transcriber that emits ABC directly; for researchers, it is the recipe of partial labels, a structured teacher and a unified student.

A Multidimensional Model for Quantifying Tonal Strength: A Continuous Framework of Analyzing Tonal Evolution Beyond Tonal-Atonal Binary Classification

量化調性強度的多維模型:超越調性/無調性二分、分析調性演變的連續框架

Yuliang Li, Nan Nan, Meilian Gu, Xiaohong Guan
arXiv:2610.04871 · cs.SD, cs.MM · 投稿submitted 2026-10-04
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：用三個可解釋的特徵——音級分佈均勻度、相鄰視窗調性中心的穩定度、局部調性清晰度——經因素分析合成一個連續的「調性程度」分數,在 196 首調性/無調性二分資料上 F1 0.985,並在 21 位作曲家、1,561 首作品上畫出從巴洛克到魏本的連續下滑。分類幾乎已經飽和,真正值得看的是連續分數本身和它的混淆因素。Why this one: Three interpretable features, pitch-class distribution uniformity, stability of tonal centers between adjacent windows, and local tonal clarity, are combined by factor analysis into one continuous tonal-degree score. It reaches F1 0.985 on a 196-piece tonal/atonal set and traces a continuous decline from the Baroque to Webern across 1,561 works by 21 composers. The classification task is close to saturated; what deserves attention is the continuous score itself and its confounds.

動機Motivation

「調性 vs. 無調性」的二分在 19 世紀末到 20 世紀初最不管用:華格納、馬勒、史特勞斯、德布西、史克里亞賓都落在中間地帶,延伸調性、調式寫法、印象派和聲與自由無調性之間沒有一條明確的界線。既有的計算方法大多在做調性辨識(輸出一個調名)或二元分類,沒有給出「調性有多強」的連續量。

作者想要的是一個可解釋、可跨作品與作曲家比較的連續尺度,用來描述音樂史上調性的漸進消解,也作為未來行為與神經實驗挑選刺激時的控制變數。他們順帶測試這個分數和情緒正負向(valence)有沒有關係。

The tonal-versus-atonal binary breaks down most visibly in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries: Wagner, Mahler, Strauss, Debussy and Scriabin sit in between, and there is no clear line separating extended tonality, modal writing, impressionist harmony and free atonality. Existing computational methods mostly identify keys (outputting a key name) or classify tonal versus atonal; none gives a continuous measure of how strong the tonality is.

The authors want an interpretable continuous scale that can be compared across works and composers, to describe the gradual dissolution of tonality in music history and to serve as a control variable when choosing stimuli for future behavioral and neural experiments. Along the way they test whether the score relates to emotional valence.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

三個特徵都建立在 Krumhansl 與 Kessler(1982)的 24 個調性模板與 Krumhansl–Schmuckler 尋調演算法上。其中「局部調性清晰度」——每個視窗與最佳調性模板的最大相關——基本上就是 MIRtoolbox(Lartillot 與 Toiviainen,2007)早已提供的 key clarity,這篇的改變只是改成四音視窗並對全曲取平均。論文沒有提到這個對應。

最接近的先前工作是 Weiß 與 Müller 一系列的「調性複雜度」量測:以 chroma 的平坦度、熵與五度圈上的分散程度,描述一段音樂離單一調性中心多遠,Weiß 等人(2019)並用 2,000 份錄音畫出跨三百年的演化曲線。本文的「音級分佈均勻度」(與均勻分佈的餘弦相似度)在概念上就是一種平坦度指標。差別在於本文用符號 MIDI 而不是音訊,並把三個指標壓成單一分數。

二元分類這條線,Honingh 與 Bod(2010、2011)用音級集合類別與音程分佈區分調性與無調性,本文拿後者的「視窗內不屬於最佳自然音階的音數」當基準。Moss 等人(2024)則用調性空間中的音程分佈推論風格變遷。相對於這些工作,本文的賣點是可解釋的多維組合,而不是新的訊號。

All three features rest on the 24 key profiles of Krumhansl and Kessler (1982) and the Krumhansl-Schmuckler key-finding algorithm. Local tonal clarity, each window's maximum correlation with the best-matching key profile, is essentially the key clarity measure that MIRtoolbox (Lartillot and Toiviainen, 2007) has long provided; the change here is only the four-note window and averaging over the piece. The paper does not mention this correspondence.

The closest prior work is Weiß and Müller's series of tonal complexity measures, which use chroma flatness, entropy and spread on the circle of fifths to describe how far a passage is from a single tonal center; Weiß et al. (2019) used 2,000 recordings to draw evolution curves spanning three centuries. This paper's pitch-class uniformity (cosine similarity to a uniform distribution) is conceptually a flatness measure. The differences are that this paper works on symbolic MIDI rather than audio and collapses three indicators into one score.

On the classification side, Honingh and Bod (2010, 2011) separated tonal from atonal music with pitch-class set categories and interval distributions; the paper uses the latter's count of notes outside the best-fitting diatonic scale in each window as its baseline. Moss et al. (2024) inferred stylistic change from interval distributions in tonal space. Against these, the paper's selling point is an interpretable multidimensional combination, not a new signal.

方法Method

每首作品先算全曲的音級持續時間分佈 p。特徵 U 是 p 與均勻分佈的餘弦相似度,值越高越接近均勻、調性越弱,之後取 U′ = 1 − U 讓方向一致。接著以四個音為一窗、每次前進兩個音切出重疊視窗,每窗與 24 個模板算 Pearson 相關,取最大者的調為局部中心。S 是相鄰視窗所選調性模板之間相關的平均,反映調性中心是否穩定;C 是各窗最大相關的平均,反映局部像不像某個調。

窗長選四音,依據是 Krumhansl(1990)指出 K–S 演算法只用前四個音就能判對 Bach 48 首前奏曲中 44 首的調。作者把窗長從 4 改到 20 音,C 的 AUC 都在 0.99 以上,S 介於 0.947 到 0.965。

整合方式是單因素最大概似因素分析:三個標準化特徵 z = λT + ε,T 用迴歸因素分數估計。整合前的診斷:KMO 0.756,Bartlett 檢定 χ² = 475.77、p < 0.001,第一主成分特徵值 2.644、解釋 87.67% 變異。分類門檻 0.06 是在同一份 196 首資料上取 F1 最佳值。資料方面,二元集合是 100 首 EMOPIA 流行鋼琴片段加 96 首第二維也納樂派作品;歷史語料的 1,561 首來自 GitHub、MAESTRO、kunstderfuge 與 MuseScore。

For each piece the method computes the pitch-class duration distribution p over the whole work. Feature U is the cosine similarity between p and a uniform distribution; higher means closer to uniform and weaker tonality, so U' = 1 - U is used to align directions. The piece is then cut into overlapping windows of four notes with a hop of two notes. Each window is correlated (Pearson) with the 24 profiles, and the best-matching key is the local center. S is the mean correlation between the profiles chosen for adjacent windows, measuring whether the tonal center is stable; C is the mean of each window's maximum correlation, measuring how key-like each local window is.

The four-note window follows Krumhansl (1990), who showed the K-S algorithm identifies the keys of 44 of Bach's 48 preludes from their first four notes. Varying the window from 4 to 20 notes, C keeps AUC above 0.99 and S stays between 0.947 and 0.965.

Integration is a one-factor maximum-likelihood factor analysis: the three standardized features follow z = λT + ε, and T is estimated with regression factor scores. Diagnostics before integration: KMO 0.756, Bartlett's test χ² = 475.77, p < 0.001, first principal component eigenvalue 2.644, explaining 87.67% of variance. The classification threshold of 0.06 is the F1-optimal cutoff on the same 196 pieces. The binary set consists of 100 EMOPIA pop piano excerpts and 96 works by Second Viennese School composers; the 1,561-work historical corpus comes from GitHub, MAESTRO, kunstderfuge and MuseScore.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是二元資料集的混淆。調性那一半全是 EMOPIA 的當代流行鋼琴片段,無調性那一半全是荀白克、貝爾格、魏本——兩邊差的不只是調性,還有年代、曲風、織度與來源。在這種設定下,單一特徵就已經接近天花板:局部清晰度 F1 0.9756、AUC 0.9977,Honingh 與 Bod 的基準 F1 0.9749、AUC 0.9966,完整模型 F1 0.9851、AUC 0.9985。以準確率換算,模型與基準在 196 首中只差 2 首,而且門檻還是在同一份資料上調出來的。

第二,三個指標的單因素模型剛好是恰好識別(三個負荷加三個獨特變異,對上三個變異數與三個共變數),無法檢定模型適配。KMO 與第一主成分解釋率只說明三者高度相關,不說明它們量的是同一件事。

第三,歷史語料的來源混雜:多數古典與浪漫派作曲家來自 MAESTRO 的演奏 MIDI,華格納、馬勒、史特勞斯與第二維也納樂派來自 MuseScore 的樂譜 MIDI,柴可夫斯基的 274 首來自 kunstderfuge。演奏 MIDI 與總譜 MIDI 的音符密度和聲部數不同,會直接影響四音視窗裡看到什麼,所以作曲家之間的差異有一部分可能是來源差異。作者自己列的限制只有「只限符號 MIDI 與西方藝術音樂」以及「線性整合」兩項。

The biggest problem is the confound in the binary dataset. The tonal half is entirely contemporary EMOPIA pop piano excerpts and the atonal half is entirely Schoenberg, Berg and Webern, so the two sides differ not only in tonality but in era, genre, texture and source. In that setting single features are already near the ceiling: local clarity reaches F1 0.9756 and AUC 0.9977, the Honingh and Bod baseline F1 0.9749 and AUC 0.9966, and the full model F1 0.9851 and AUC 0.9985. In accuracy terms the model and the baseline differ by 2 of 196 pieces, and the threshold was tuned on that same data.

Second, a one-factor model on three indicators is exactly identified (three loadings plus three unique variances against three variances and three covariances), so model fit cannot be tested. KMO and the first component's share of variance show only that the three features are strongly correlated, not that they measure the same thing.

Third, the historical corpus mixes sources: most Classical and Romantic composers come from MAESTRO performance MIDI, Wagner, Mahler, Strauss and the Second Viennese School from MuseScore score MIDI, and Tchaikovsky's 274 pieces from kunstderfuge. Performance MIDI and full-score MIDI differ in note density and number of voices, which directly changes what a four-note window sees, so part of the difference between composers may be a difference between sources. The authors' own limitations list only two items: symbolic MIDI and Western art music only, and a linear integration.

討論Discussion

歷史曲線本身符合樂史敘事:巴洛克與古典作曲家平均都為正,且平均減一個標準差仍大於零;蕭邦、舒曼、布拉姆斯平均為正但下界跌破零;李斯特是第一個平均為負的浪漫派(−0.09);德布西、史克里亞賓、荀白克平均為負但上界為正;貝爾格與魏本整個區間為負,魏本平均 −0.99 最低。作曲家內部的時間趨勢,馬勒(斜率 −0.0225/年,p = 0.001)與荀白克(−0.0232/年,p = 0.012)顯著,史特勞斯(p = 0.070)與華格納(p = 0.373)不顯著。

不過這是用一把專為區分調性與無調性設計的尺,去重述大家已經知道的歷史,吻合是預期中的結果,不是獨立證據。比較有資訊量的會是例外:哪些作品離作曲家平均很遠、三個子特徵不同步的作品長什麼樣。作者在討論裡說「局部清楚但中心常換」這種混合狀態正是多維整合的理由,但沒有實際展示任何一首這樣的作品。

情緒分析的效果非常小:EMOPIA 正向 valence 片段平均 1.033(n = 555),負向 0.965(n = 516),Mann–Whitney 單尾 p = 0.00768,效果量 r = 0.074。EMOPIA 全是調性音樂,而且分析沒有控制大小調;大小調本身就與 valence 強相關,所以分不出分數反映的是調性強度還是調式。作者也說這個分數不該單獨當情緒預測因子。這份工作的實際用途比較像一個可重現的特徵抽取器,程式碼與語料放在匿名儲存庫公開。

The historical curve itself matches the music-historical narrative: Baroque and Classical composers all have positive means, with mean minus one standard deviation still above zero; Chopin, Schumann and Brahms have positive means but lower bounds below zero; Liszt is the first Romantic with a negative mean (-0.09); Debussy, Scriabin and Schoenberg have negative means but positive upper bounds; Berg and Webern are negative across the whole interval, with Webern lowest at -0.99. Within composers, the trend over composition year is significant for Mahler (slope -0.0225 per year, p = 0.001) and Schoenberg (-0.0232 per year, p = 0.012), and not for Strauss (p = 0.070) or Wagner (p = 0.373).

Still, this is a scale designed to separate tonal from atonal music being used to restate history everyone already knows; agreement is the expected outcome, not independent evidence. The informative cases would be the exceptions: works far from their composer's mean, or works where the three sub-features diverge. The discussion names the mixed state of clear local keys with frequently shifting centers as the reason for multidimensional integration, but never shows a single such work.

The emotion effect is very small: positive-valence EMOPIA excerpts average 1.033 (n = 555) and negative ones 0.965 (n = 516), one-sided Mann-Whitney p = 0.00768, effect size r = 0.074. EMOPIA is entirely tonal, and the analysis does not control for mode; since major versus minor is itself strongly tied to valence, it cannot tell whether the score reflects tonal strength or mode. The authors also say the score should not be used alone as an emotion predictor. Its practical use is closer to a reproducible feature extractor, with code and corpora released in an anonymous repository.

From Isolated Feature to Orbits: Discovering Music Concepts via Multi-SAE Alignment

從孤立特徵到軌道:以多個對齊的稀疏自編碼器發現音樂概念

Liwei Lin, Gus Xia
arXiv:2610.01864 · cs.SD, cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-10-01
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：讓多個 SAE 分別處理相鄰半音移調的同一段音訊,並強迫它們共用 Top-K 支撐,訓練後就能從解碼器字典讀出「上移一個半音」的後繼關係,串成 12 個特徵一圈的環:大三和弦環、小三和弦環,以及只在 MuQ 出現、對應下屬和弦根音的環。只靠少數錨點,MuQ 的和弦環在給定和弦邊界時 RWC 根音加權召回 86.21,與全監督的 LVCR(87.96)相近。Why this one: Several SAEs each see the same audio at adjacent semitone transpositions and are forced to share their Top-K support; after training, a move-up-one-semitone successor relation can be read straight from the decoder dictionaries and chained into 12-feature rings: a major-chord ring, a minor-chord ring, and a ring tied to the subdominant chord root that appears only in MuQ. With a few anchors, MuQ's chord rings reach 86.21 root weighted recall on RWC given chord boundaries, close to the fully supervised LVCR (87.96).

動機Motivation

探測(probing)與稀疏自編碼器(SAE)是目前理解音樂基礎模型的兩個主要工具,但兩者都停在「一個特徵對應一個概念」:探測需要預先定義好的標籤,SAE 則是事後拿外部標籤一個個去對特徵。音樂概念卻常是有結構的——C、C♯、D……的大三和弦在移調下構成 12 個元素的循環,它們共同的不變量才是「大三和弦」這個抽象概念。

作者的想法是:如果能把特徵之間的關係一起找出來,只要標出一圈中的一兩個特徵,其餘就能沿著順序推得;一整圈共享的不變量則揭露更高層的概念,即使那個概念本身沒有成為單一 SAE 特徵。他們選音高移調當結構來源,因為和弦、調性、旋律會隨移調等變,而配器與節奏不會。

Probing and sparse autoencoders (SAEs) are the two main tools for understanding music foundation models, but both stop at one feature per concept: probing needs predefined labels, and SAE analyses match features to external labels one at a time after the fact. Musical concepts are often structured, though. Major chords on C, C#, D and so on form a 12-element cycle under transposition, and the invariant they share is the abstract concept of a major chord.

The authors' idea is that if the relations between features can be recovered too, labeling one or two features in a cycle is enough to infer the rest along the order, and the invariant shared by the whole cycle reveals a higher-level concept even when that concept never becomes a single SAE feature. They use pitch transposition as the source of structure because chords, keys and melodies change equivariantly under transposition while instrumentation and rhythm do not.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂上的 SAE 工作,Singh 等人(2025)在 MusicGen 找出與音色、織度、風格相關的特徵,AudioSAE(2026)則擴到一般音訊模型,兩者都分析個別特徵。探測方面,Castellon 等人(2021)對 Jukebox、Wei 等人(2024)對生成模型是否編碼樂理概念的研究,都是全曲池化後訓練監督分類器。

方法上最像的有兩條線。其一是 Universal SAE(Thasarathan 等人,2025),在多個模型上共同訓練字典以找出共享概念;本文是把「多個模型」換成「同一個模型看多個移調版本」。其二是論文沒點名的 STONE 與 S-KEY(Kong 等人,2024、2025):它們用移調等變性,在一個 12 維循環輸出上自監督學習調性。S-KEY 在這篇裡只被當成調性偵測的比較基準,但「用移調在 12 元循環上誘導結構」的核心想法同源。差別在於 S-KEY 是訓練一個新模型去學調性,本文是用同一個歸納偏置去解讀凍結模型的內部表示。

LLM 可解釋性裡也有類似觀察:Engels 等人(2024)發現語言模型用圓形結構表示星期與月份。本文的「環」可以看成音樂版本,但多了一個明確的生成元(移調)用來找出順序。

Among SAE work on music, Singh et al. (2025) found features tied to timbre, texture and style in MusicGen, and AudioSAE (2026) extended SAEs to general audio models; both analyze individual features. On the probing side, Castellon et al. (2021) on Jukebox and Wei et al. (2024) on whether generative models encode music theory both pool over whole clips and train supervised classifiers.

Two lines of work are methodologically closest. One is Universal SAEs (Thasarathan et al., 2025), which train dictionaries jointly across several models to find shared concepts; this paper replaces several models with one model seeing several transpositions. The other, which the paper does not name, is STONE and S-KEY (Kong et al., 2024, 2025), which use transposition equivariance to learn keys self-supervised on a 12-dimensional cyclic output. S-KEY appears here only as a key-detection baseline, yet the core idea of inducing 12-cycle structure through transposition is shared. The difference is that S-KEY trains a new model to learn keys, while this paper uses the same inductive bias to read the internals of a frozen model.

LLM interpretability has a similar observation: Engels et al. (2024) found that language models represent days of the week and months on circles. The rings here can be read as a music counterpart, with the addition of an explicit generator, transposition, that recovers the order.

方法Method

資料是 Slakh2100 訓練集中依調性平衡挑出的 326 首當訓練、164 首當驗證,每段以相位聲碼器產生 −2 到 +2 半音的五個版本。凍結的 MuQ 與 MusicFM(都是 12 層、25 fps)抽出逐框表示;每個相鄰移調版本各配一個 Top-K SAE(正文用三個),共用一個可學習的前置偏置。

關鍵是 Top-K 的選擇聯合進行:每個 SAE 的啟動值先以 RMS 正規化,再逐索引對各版本取最大值,取前 K 大形成共同遮罩,套回各自未正規化的啟動值做重建。由於同一個索引在所有 SAE 上總是一起被選、一起在時間對齊的移調樣本上更新,同索引的特徵會傾向編碼「同一概念的相鄰移調」。作者也明說這只是歸納偏置,不是保證。

訓練後只看解碼器字典,不用任何輸入或標籤。以中間的 SAE 為參考空間,特徵 i 的後繼是:先跳到上方 SAE 的同索引特徵(代表上移半音),再用解碼器向量的餘弦相似度在參考 SAE 中找最接近者;同時從下方 SAE 反向驗證。兩個方向的相似度取平均來選目標、取較大者當信心,低於門檻 τ 就不接受;多個來源指向同一目標時只留信心最高的。

反覆套用後繼映射會得到三種結構:回到起點的環、自己指向自己的不動點(移調不變的概念),以及中途斷掉的開放序列。標出環上一兩個特徵後,其餘的語意沿順序傳遞。正文表格用 K = 48,其餘視覺化多用 K = 32、τ = 0.5。

Training data are 326 key-balanced songs from the Slakh2100 training split, with 164 for validation; each segment is pitch-shifted by a phase vocoder into five versions from -2 to +2 semitones. Frozen MuQ and MusicFM (both 12 layers, 25 fps) provide frame-level representations, and each adjacent transposition gets its own Top-K SAE (three in the main setup), all sharing one learnable pre-encoder bias.

The key step is joint Top-K selection. Each SAE's activations are RMS-normalized, the maximum is taken per index across views, and the top K indices form one shared mask that is applied to each SAE's unnormalized activations for reconstruction. Because an index is always selected in every SAE together and updated together on time-aligned transposed examples, same-index features tend to encode adjacent transpositions of one concept. The authors say plainly that this is an inductive bias, not a guarantee.

After training only the decoder dictionaries are used, with no inputs or labels. With the middle SAE as reference space, the successor of feature i is found by jumping to the same index in the upper SAE (one semitone up) and then taking the reference feature with the most similar decoder vector by cosine; the lower SAE checks the reverse direction. The mean of the two directional similarities selects the target and the larger one is the confidence; transitions below threshold τ are rejected, and when several sources point to one target only the most confident survives.

Iterating the successor map yields three structures: rings that return to their start, fixed points that map to themselves (transposition-invariant concepts), and open sequences that break off. Labeling one or two features on a ring propagates meaning along the order. The main tables use K = 48; most visualizations use K = 32 and τ = 0.5.

限制Limitations

作者寫的限制有三:只能處理隨移調變化的概念,節奏這類不變概念無法組織;只看逐框層級;12 元素的環可能與移調步長和西方音樂資料有關。

更關鍵的是評估設計。第一,「下屬環」的調性評估忽略關係大小調的差異(表 2 標題即如此),附錄也把它當成調號偵測,而 MuQ 上的環在小調曲子裡跟隨的是關係大調。這讓人懷疑這圈特徵編碼的或許是自然音階集合(調號),只是在 IV 級和弦出現時最活躍;「下屬」是一種解讀,不是唯一可能。第二,即使用這種寬鬆標準,o-SAE 的歌曲層級準確率在 GiantSteps 只有 50.50、FMAKv2 56.92;改用和弦推調性的表 3,MuQ 的 MIREX 分數 70.17 也仍低於 S-KEY 的 73.24。

第三,s-SAE 對照組的特徵是用監督探測器的權重去找的,所以「標準 SAE 找不到主音與屬音特徵」這個結論依賴探測器本身的品質。第四,錨點和環的認定是人工看啟動圖決定的;附錄 G.1 顯示 MuQ 第 6 層在任何門檻下都找不到小三和弦環,不同隨機種子得到的圖也不完全相同,作者只說核心語意在特徵置換下可重現。最後,SAE 只在無人聲的合成資料上訓練,真實錄音上的結果是轉移測試。

The authors list three limitations: only concepts that change under transposition can be organized, so invariant ones such as rhythm cannot; the analysis is frame-level only; and the 12-element rings may be tied to the transposition step and to Western music data.

The evaluation design matters more. First, key evaluation of the subdominant ring ignores the relative major/minor distinction (as Table 2's title says), the appendix treats it as key-signature detection, and in minor-key songs MuQ's ring follows the relative major. That raises the possibility that the ring encodes the diatonic collection (the key signature) and is simply most active when the IV chord sounds; subdominant is one interpretation, not the only one. Second, even under this lenient criterion, o-SAE's song-level accuracy is only 50.50 on GiantSteps and 56.92 on FMAKv2; inferring keys from chords in Table 3, MuQ's MIREX score of 70.17 still trails S-KEY's 73.24.

Third, the s-SAE features in the ablation are retrieved using supervised probe weights, so the conclusion that a standard SAE lacks tonic and dominant features depends on the probes' quality. Fourth, anchors and rings are identified by inspecting activation plots; Appendix G.1 shows no minor-chord ring in MuQ layer 6 at any threshold, and different random seeds give different graphs, with the authors claiming only that core semantics are reproducible up to feature permutation. Finally, the SAEs are trained only on synthetic audio without vocals, so results on real recordings are transfer tests.

討論Discussion

和弦環是這篇最紮實的部分。在給定參考和弦邊界時,MuQ 第 2 層 o-SAE 的根音加權召回在 POP909、RWC、Slakh 分別是 85.29、86.21、84.67;同層的全監督線性探測是 84.17、86.01、86.05,LVCR 是 84.25、87.96、85.65。改由 SAE 特徵自己估邊界,則降到 80.03、79.79、78.45。也就是說,幾個錨點加上環的順序,就能得到與監督探測接近的和弦辨識,差距主要出在邊界偵測。

o-SAE 相對 s-SAE 的增益並不一致:估邊界時,MusicFM 的根音召回(POP909)從 71.08 升到 74.01,MuQ 從 77.42 升到 80.03;但在給定邊界的 MajMin 指標上,MuQ 的 o-SAE 在三個資料集都略低於 s-SAE(例如 POP909 78.96 對 79.45)。所以對齊訓練的主要價值是讓結構變得可讀,而不是讓特徵更好用。

論文最有意思的論點是「可線性還原,不等於模型裡有明確的概念」:探測器在主音與屬音上也有訊號,但 SAE 只找到下屬;而用和弦啟動值加人工權重就能把調性推回來(MuQ 的 MIREX 70.17),顯示調性資訊可能分散在和弦表示裡。這個論點有道理,但還沒排除另一個解釋:SAE 的稀疏目標偏好高變異、常出現的方向,未必等同模型「明確擁有」的概念。MuQ 有這個環而 MusicFM 沒有,本身就是一個有用的模型比較訊號。

The chord rings are the most solid part of the paper. Given reference chord boundaries, MuQ layer-2 o-SAE reaches root weighted recall of 85.29, 86.21 and 84.67 on POP909, RWC and Slakh; a fully supervised linear probe on the same layer gets 84.17, 86.01 and 86.05, and LVCR 84.25, 87.96 and 85.65. With boundaries estimated from SAE features instead, the numbers drop to 80.03, 79.79 and 78.45. So a few anchors plus the ring order give chord recognition close to supervised probing, and the gap lies mostly in boundary detection.

The gain of o-SAE over s-SAE is inconsistent. With estimated boundaries, root recall on POP909 rises from 71.08 to 74.01 for MusicFM and from 77.42 to 80.03 for MuQ; but on MajMin with given boundaries, MuQ's o-SAE is slightly below s-SAE on all three datasets (POP909 78.96 versus 79.45, for example). The main value of alignment training is making structure readable, not making features more useful.

The paper's most interesting argument is that linear recoverability does not mean the model holds an explicit concept: probes find tonic and dominant signals too, but the SAE recovers only the subdominant, and keys can be rebuilt from chord activations with hand-set weights (MIREX 70.17 for MuQ), suggesting key information is spread across chord representations. The argument is reasonable but does not rule out an alternative: the SAE's sparsity objective favors high-variance, frequent directions, which need not coincide with concepts the model explicitly holds. That MuQ has this ring and MusicFM does not is itself a useful signal for comparing models.
