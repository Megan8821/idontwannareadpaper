Assessing Alignment in Automatic Piano Transcription Datasets: A Benchmark for Scoring Methods

以受控的音高互換、起音位移與結構增刪三種 MIDI 破壞，用單調性、穩定性、靈敏度、一致性四個指標評比 14 種音訊–MIDI 對齊評分法，提出以轉譜模型輸出做軟式匈牙利配對、加入同音高 IOI 的 STI 分數，並拿來替 MAESTRO、SMD、MAPS、MusicNet 等六個鋼琴轉譜資料集的對齊品質排名

Lucas de Souza Brandão, Lucas Gris, Joaquim Cavalcante, Chukwuemeka Nkama, Andrea Poltronieri
arXiv:2611.14443 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-10

為什麼挑這篇：鋼琴轉譜模型的訓練與評測都假設音訊與 MIDI 完全對齊，但 MAPS、MusicNet 早就被指出有對齊誤差，而且過去多半是用「轉譜分數變低」間接推論出來的。這篇先建一個評比框架：對原始 MIDI 做受控破壞，看哪種評分法的分數會隨破壞程度乖乖下降。它提出的 STI 分數在細微起音位移上的靈敏度是 0.95（傳統 MIREX 轉譜 F 值只有 0.80、CENS 特徵餘弦相似度只有 0.23）。選它是因為資料集本身的對齊品質是轉譜領域很少被量化、卻會影響所有排行榜的問題。但要注意：這是 4 頁的工作坊論文，STI 依賴的轉譜模型多半在 MAESTRO 上訓練，可能偏袒 MAESTRO；而且各方法對真實資料集的排名並不一致。Why this one: Piano transcription models are trained and evaluated on the assumption that audio and MIDI are perfectly aligned, yet MAPS and MusicNet have long been reported to contain alignment errors, mostly inferred indirectly from lower transcription scores. This paper first builds a benchmark: apply controlled corruptions to the original MIDI and check which scoring methods drop consistently as the corruption grows. Its proposed STI score reaches 0.95 sensitivity on fine onset shifts (the standard MIREX transcription F-measure gets 0.80, CENS cosine similarity only 0.23). I picked it because dataset alignment quality is rarely quantified in transcription, yet it affects every leaderboard. But note: this is a 4-page workshop paper, the transcription models STI relies on are mostly trained on MAESTRO and may favour it, and the methods do not agree on how real datasets rank.

動機Motivation

自動音樂轉譜（AMT）的監督訊號是成對的音訊與 MIDI。訓練時，模型學的是「這個時間點這個音高開始發聲」；評測時，MIREX 標準要求起音落在參考值 50 ms 以內才算對。兩者都默認標註的起音、音高、結束點跟錄音完全吻合。

這個假設已經被多次打破。MAPS 的錄音部分、MusicNet 都被發現有明顯的對齊誤差；SMD 被認為比 MAPS 原始標註好，但也不是完美。問題在於這些證據大多是間接的：拿某個轉譜模型在不同容忍窗下跑分，分數異常才回頭懷疑標註。轉譜分數低的原因很多（音色、錄音環境、曲風），不一定是對齊造成的。

所以作者想要的是一個直接量測對齊品質的工具，而且要先證明這個工具本身可靠，再拿去替資料集打分。

The supervision signal for automatic music transcription (AMT) is paired audio and MIDI. In training, the model learns that a given pitch starts at a given time; in evaluation, the MIREX standard counts an onset correct only within 50 ms of the reference. Both assume the annotated onsets, pitches and offsets match the recording exactly.

That assumption has been broken repeatedly. The recorded part of MAPS and MusicNet were both found to have noticeable alignment errors; SMD is thought to be better than the original MAPS annotations but not perfect either. The trouble is that this evidence is mostly indirect: run a transcription model under different tolerance windows and, if scores look odd, suspect the labels. Transcription scores can be low for many reasons (timbre, recording conditions, style), not only misalignment.

So the authors want a tool that measures alignment quality directly, and they want to show the tool itself is reliable before using it to grade datasets.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

對齊誤差的先前研究有幾個具體的點。Gong et al.（DAFx 2019）分析並修正了 MAPS，但只處理單一子集、靠人工驗證。Yan & Duan（ISMIR 2024）的 Transkun 順帶釋出了 MAPS 的替代標註，並觀察到用新標註時轉譜分數上升。MT3（Gardner et al., 2021） 則在不同起音容忍窗下發現 MusicNet 的對齊問題。這些都是「用轉譜結果反推」的路線。另一條與 MusicNet 直接相關、論文沒有引用的是 Maman & Bermano（ICML 2022），他們用 EM 式的反覆對齊重新產生 MusicNet 標註，等於已經把「MusicNet 對齊很差」當成要修的事實。

評分方法這側，候選的工具都是現成的：MrMsDTW 與 Sync Toolbox（Müller 等人） 的 CENS、DLNCO 特徵對齊；Madmom 起音偵測加 mir_eval F 值；CLaMP 3、CLAP 嵌入相似度。這篇的貢獻不是新工具，而是用同一組受控破壞把它們放在同一把尺上比較。

論文提出的 STI 最接近的是 mir_eval 本身的音符配對：mir_eval 也是在預測與參考音符間做二部圖最大配對，只是用硬門檻（50 ms 內算對）。STI 把硬門檻換成 exp(−c/τ) 的軟權重、改用匈牙利演算法求全域最佳配對，並在成本裡加入「同音高前一個音到這個音的間隔」（pitch-constrained IOI）。真正新的是後面兩點；軟化的 F 值本身是既有評測的平滑版本。

Prior work on alignment errors has a few concrete points. Gong et al. (DAFx 2019) analysed and corrected MAPS, but only one subset and with manual validation. Transkun by Yan & Duan (ISMIR 2024) released alternative MAPS annotations and observed higher transcription scores with them. MT3 (Gardner et al., 2021) found MusicNet's alignment problems by varying onset tolerance windows. All of these infer misalignment from transcription results. Another line directly about MusicNet, which the paper does not cite, is Maman & Bermano (ICML 2022), who regenerated MusicNet labels by EM-style iterative alignment, effectively treating poor MusicNet alignment as a fact to fix.

On the scoring side, the candidate tools are all off the shelf: CENS and DLNCO feature alignment via MrMsDTW and the Sync Toolbox (Müller et al.); Madmom onset detection plus mir_eval F-measure; CLaMP 3 and CLAP embedding similarity. The contribution is not a new tool but putting them on one scale with the same controlled corruptions.

The proposed STI is closest to mir_eval's own note matching: mir_eval also computes a maximum bipartite matching between predicted and reference notes, but with a hard threshold (correct within 50 ms). STI replaces the hard threshold with a soft weight exp(−c/τ), uses the Hungarian algorithm for a globally optimal assignment, and adds to the cost the interval since the previous note of the same pitch (pitch-constrained IOI). The last two are what is new; the softened F-measure itself is a smoothed version of existing evaluation.

方法Method

破壞方式。 三種破壞各有 11 個嚴重度 S ∈ {0, 0.1, …, 1}。音高互換：隨機挑 ⌊S·N⌋ 個音，換成 21–108 範圍內的其他音高。結構破壞：刪掉一些音、再插入同樣數量的隨機音，總共改動 ⌊2·S·N⌋ 個。起音位移：每個音隨機提前或延後 Δt = 500 ms·S²；平方映射讓 4 個嚴重度落在 50 ms 以下（0、5、20、45 ms），專門測細微誤差。力度不破壞，因為力度沒有明確的 MIDI 到音訊對應。

評分法（14 種，四大類）。 嵌入式：CLaMP 3 直接嵌入音訊與 MIDI；CLAP 先把 MIDI 用 FluidSynth 合成成音訊再比。特徵對齊式：頻譜、CENS、DLNCO 三種特徵，配兩種比法——MrMsDTW 對齊後沿路徑平均餘弦相似度（DTW-C、DTW-D），或不對齊、直接逐格餘弦相似度（CS-S、CS-D、CS-C）。起音式：Madmom 偵測起音後算 mir_eval 起音 F 值。轉譜式：用 Basic Pitch、Transkun、ByteDance 三個模型轉譜，再以 MIREX 標準算音符 F 值（AMT-BP/TK/BD），或以 STI 計分（STI-BP/TK/BD）。

STI 細節。 每個音表示為（音高、起音、同音高 IOI）。同音高的預測–參考配對成本 c = |Δ起音| + |ΔIOI|，不同音高給 10⁶ 懲罰，匈牙利演算法求一對一配對，每對權重 w = exp(−c/0.05)。τ = 0.05 秒對應 MIREX 的 50 ms。權重總和當成軟性配對數，算出連續的精確率、召回率與 F 值。

四個後設指標。 單調性：每個樣本的嚴重度與分數做 Spearman 相關，取負號平均。穩定性：同一嚴重度下各樣本分數的變異係數，換算成 1/(1+σ/μ)。靈敏度：S=0 到 S=1 的相對降幅。一致性：分數對 S（或起音的 S²）做線性擬合的 R²。資料取自 MAESTRO 驗證集、SMD、以及 MAPS 中兩個軟體合成子集（MAPS-S，視為完美對齊）。

Corruptions. Each of three corruption types has 11 severity levels S ∈ {0, 0.1, …, 1}. Pitch swap: ⌊S·N⌋ random notes get a different pitch in 21–108. Structural: delete notes and insert the same number of random ones, ⌊2·S·N⌋ changes in total. Onset shift: each note moves earlier or later by Δt = 500 ms·S²; the quadratic map puts four levels under 50 ms (0, 5, 20, 45 ms) to probe fine errors. Velocity is not corrupted because it has no well-defined MIDI-to-audio mapping.

Scoring methods (14, four families). Embedding: CLaMP 3 embeds audio and MIDI directly; CLAP compares audio after synthesising the MIDI with FluidSynth. Feature alignment: spectrogram, CENS and DLNCO features with two comparisons, either cosine similarity along an MrMsDTW path (DTW-C, DTW-D) or unaligned frame-wise cosine similarity (CS-S, CS-D, CS-C). Onset: Madmom onsets scored with mir_eval onset F1. Transcription: Basic Pitch, Transkun and ByteDance transcribe the audio, scored either with the MIREX note F-measure (AMT-BP/TK/BD) or with STI (STI-BP/TK/BD).

STI details. Each note is (pitch, onset, same-pitch IOI). A same-pitch predicted–reference pair costs c = |Δonset| + |ΔIOI|, different pitches get a 10⁶ penalty, the Hungarian algorithm finds a one-to-one assignment, and each pair is weighted w = exp(−c/0.05). τ = 0.05 s matches the MIREX 50 ms. The weight sum is a soft match count from which continuous precision, recall and F-measure follow.

Four meta-metrics. Monotonicity: negated Spearman correlation between severity and score per sample, averaged. Stability: 1/(1+σ/μ) of scores across samples at a fixed severity. Sensitivity: relative drop from S=0 to S=1. Consistency: R² of a linear fit of score on S (S² for onsets). Data come from the MAESTRO validation split, SMD, and two software-synthesised MAPS subsets (MAPS-S, treated as perfectly aligned).

限制Limitations

評分器跟受評對象不獨立。 STI-TK 與 STI-BD 用的 Transkun 和 ByteDance 都是在 MAESTRO 上訓練的。表 3 裡 STI-TK 給 MAESTRO 87、MusicNet 45，這個差距有多少是對齊、多少是「模型在訓練分佈上轉得比較好」，論文沒有拆開。同一張表裡不依賴 MAESTRO 訓練的 STI-BP 給 MAESTRO 34、SMD 31，幾乎分不出差異，這反而提示排名可能受訓練域影響。MAPS-S 排第一也有同樣問題：它是軟體合成的鋼琴，不只對齊完美，音色也最乾淨、最好轉。

破壞方式不像真實誤差。 每個音獨立隨機前後移動，是白噪聲式的抖動。真實資料集的對齊問題常是整首的固定延遲、隨時間漂移、或某一段整體錯位。DTW 類方法對固定延遲本來就不敏感，對這種情境的評比結論可能完全不同。

真實資料集的結論很弱。 作者自己承認各方法對錄音資料集的排名不一致，只剩「MusicNet 最差、MAPS-S 最好」這種大方向。SMD 的分數變異極大（STI-TK 61 ± 32），代表個別曲目差很多，但論文沒有列出是哪些曲目、也沒有人工抽查驗證。STI 犧牲了穩定性：SMD 上細微起音的穩定性只有 0.63–0.67，CENS 類方法是 0.93–0.99。

The scorer is not independent of what it scores. Transkun and ByteDance, used by STI-TK and STI-BD, are both trained on MAESTRO. In Table 3 STI-TK gives MAESTRO 87 and MusicNet 45; how much of that gap is alignment and how much is the model transcribing its training distribution better is not separated. In the same table STI-BP, which does not depend on MAESTRO training, gives MAESTRO 34 and SMD 31, barely distinguishable, which hints that the ranking may be domain-driven. MAPS-S ranking first has the same issue: it is a software-synthesised piano, not only perfectly aligned but also the cleanest and easiest to transcribe.

The corruptions do not resemble real errors. Each note moves independently at random, which is white-noise jitter. Real dataset misalignment is often a constant offset for a whole piece, drift over time, or a section that is shifted as a block. DTW-style methods are insensitive to constant offsets by design, so conclusions for that scenario could be completely different.

The conclusions on real datasets are weak. The authors admit the methods disagree on recorded datasets, leaving only broad trends such as MusicNet worst and MAPS-S best. SMD scores vary enormously (STI-TK 61 ± 32), meaning individual pieces differ a lot, but the paper does not list which pieces or check any of them by hand. STI also trades away stability: on fine onset shifts in SMD it reaches only 0.63–0.67, against 0.93–0.99 for the CENS-based methods.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的不是 STI，而是「先用受控破壞驗證評分器，再拿評分器去評資料」這個兩段式設計。表 1 的結果本身就很有參考性：嵌入式方法幾乎沒用，CLAP 在音高互換上的單調性只有 0.22、在起音位移上只有 0.25，CLaMP 3 在起音位移上也只有 0.55；換句話說，拿通用音樂嵌入當對齊品質指標，基本上看不到起音誤差。Onset-F1 對音高互換完全沒反應（單調性 0.00），這是預期中的，但也提醒了只看起音的檢查會漏掉什麼。

STI 相對 MIREX F 值的增益集中在起音位移：單調性 0.95 對 0.61–0.64、一致性 0.63–0.65 對 0.45–0.47。結構破壞與音高互換上兩者差不多。在只看 0–45 ms 的表 2，STI 的靈敏度約 0.34–0.52，CENS/頻譜類只有 0.01–0.04——後者在細微誤差區間幾乎是平的，這也解釋了表 3 裡 CS-S、DTW-C 分數全擠在 71–93 之間、拉不開。

對使用者的實際建議是：要篩資料集裡對齊可疑的曲目，用 STI 這類轉譜式分數排序，再人工抽查最低的那幾首；但不要拿它的絕對值跨資料集比較，因為轉譜模型本身的域偏差混在裡面。下一步最自然的是加入整段延遲與漂移兩種破壞，以及用一個不在任何受評資料集上訓練的轉譜模型重跑表 3。

The most valuable part is not STI but the two-stage design: validate scorers with controlled corruptions first, then use them to grade data. Table 1 is useful on its own: embedding methods are nearly useless, with CLAP reaching only 0.22 monotonicity on pitch swaps and 0.25 on onset shifts, and CLaMP 3 only 0.55 on onset shifts. In other words, a general music embedding used as an alignment metric basically cannot see onset errors. Onset-F1 does not react to pitch swaps at all (monotonicity 0.00), which is expected but is a reminder of what onset-only checks miss.

STI's gain over the MIREX F-measure is concentrated on onset shift: monotonicity 0.95 versus 0.61–0.64, consistency 0.63–0.65 versus 0.45–0.47. On structural and pitch corruptions the two are similar. In Table 2, restricted to 0–45 ms, STI sensitivity is about 0.34–0.52 against 0.01–0.04 for CENS and spectrogram methods, which are nearly flat in the fine-error range. That also explains why CS-S and DTW-C scores in Table 3 are all squeezed into 71–93 and cannot separate datasets.

The practical advice for users: to flag suspect pieces within a dataset, rank by a transcription-based score such as STI and hand-check the lowest few; but do not compare its absolute values across datasets, because the transcription model's domain bias is mixed in. The natural next steps are adding constant-offset and drift corruptions, and rerunning Table 3 with a transcription model not trained on any of the graded datasets.

音樂聚類與相似度Music Clustering & Similarity2026-11-12

Exploring Post-Hoc Disentanglement of Audio Foundation Models for Music Similarity

把條件相似度網路（CSN）的屬性遮罩套在凍結的 CLAP、MuQ、MERT 嵌入上，系統比較時間池化（平均／注意力）、適配模組（恆等／單層／MLP）與遮罩方式（固定／可學）共 30 種組合，以三元組準確率與特異度評估曲風、樂器、情緒三種條件相似度在 MTG-Jamendo 與 MagnaTagATune 上的事後解糾纏

Lucas de Souza Brandão, Gabriel da Costa Merlin, Diego Furtado Silva
arXiv:2611.14415 · cs.SD; cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-11-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：CLAP、MERT 這類音樂基礎模型的嵌入很會判斷「整體像不像」，但沒辦法指定「只看樂器像不像」。過去做多面向相似度的方法都從頭訓練骨幹網路。這篇改成凍結骨幹，只在上面加一個小適配層與屬性遮罩，結果在 MTG-Jamendo 上 MERT 的三元組準確率從 58.1% 升到 68.6%、CLAP 從 69.3% 升到 72.7%。選它是因為它問的是很實際的問題：已經有基礎模型，要多少額外參數才能拿到可控的相似度。但要注意：提升大多來自訓練過的適配層本身，遮罩對自身屬性的增益只有約 1 個百分點，「解糾纏」主要是壓低其他屬性的表現。Why this one: Embeddings from music foundation models such as CLAP and MERT are good at judging overall similarity but cannot be told to compare only instrumentation. Previous multi-facet similarity methods trained their backbones from scratch. This paper freezes the backbone and adds only a small adaptation layer and attribute masks on top; on MTG-Jamendo, MERT triplet accuracy rises from 58.1% to 68.6% and CLAP from 69.3% to 72.7%. I picked it because it asks a practical question: given a foundation model, how many extra parameters does controllable similarity take. But note: most of the gain comes from the trained adaptation layer itself, the masks add only about 1 percentage point on their own attribute, and the disentanglement mostly works by lowering performance on the other attributes.

動機Motivation

兩首歌「像」可以有很多理由：同曲風、同樂器編制、或同樣的情緒。推薦與檢索系統常常需要只沿著其中一個面向找相似，例如「樂器組合一樣、但曲風不同」的曲子；要解釋為什麼推薦了某首歌，也需要知道是哪個面向讓兩者相近。

CLAP、MuQ、MERT 等音樂基礎模型經過大規模預訓練，整體相似度判斷很強，但所有面向都混在同一個嵌入空間裡，沒辦法單獨取出其中一個。既有的多面向相似度方法（條件相似度網路 CSN 及其延伸）都是把骨幹從頭訓練，等於丟掉基礎模型已經學到的知識，而且需要的計算資源並不是每個團隊都有。

這篇問的是：能不能不動骨幹，只在凍結的嵌入上加一點東西，就把它拆成屬性專屬的子空間？

Two tracks can be similar for many reasons: same genre, same instrumentation, or the same mood. Recommendation and retrieval often need similarity along just one facet, such as tracks with the same instruments but a different genre; explaining why a track was recommended also requires knowing which facet made them close.

Music foundation models such as CLAP, MuQ and MERT are strong at overall similarity after large-scale pretraining, but every facet is mixed into one embedding space and none can be pulled out alone. Existing multi-facet similarity methods (Conditional Similarity Networks, CSN, and extensions) train the backbone from scratch, discarding what foundation models already know and requiring compute not every team has.

The question here is whether, without touching the backbone, adding a little on top of frozen embeddings can split them into attribute-specific subspaces.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接的方法來源是 Veit et al. 的條件相似度網路（CSN, CVPR 2017）：在共享嵌入上為每個條件學一個遮罩，逐元素相乘後在子空間裡算三元組損失。音樂上的先行者有三個：Lee et al.（ICASSP 2020，Adobe）的解糾纏多維度量學習，對曲風、情緒、樂器等條件切出子空間；Ribecky et al.（2021，Fraunhofer IDMT） 的多輸入架構，處理調性、速度、年代等條件；Hashizume et al.（2025） 把條件相似度延伸到混音裡的個別樂器聲部。這些都是從音訊特徵端到端訓練骨幹，這篇的差別只在於骨幹凍結。

值得說清楚的是，這篇的最佳設定其實跟一個更普通的東西很像。「MLP 適配層 + 固定遮罩」等於：共享一個小 MLP，再把輸出向量平均切成三段，每段各自負責一個屬性的度量學習。這就是 凍結嵌入上的多任務投影頭——MARBLE 等基準評估基礎模型時常用的探測（probing）做法加上三元組損失。論文把它框成 CSN 的事後版本，但沒有拿「三個獨立投影頭」當對照，所以無法判斷遮罩這個結構本身是否必要。

另一個相關但沒被引用的方向是 條件式檢索的文字查詢：CLAP 類模型本來就能用「樂器相近」之類的文字當查詢；跟那種免訓練做法比，學到的遮罩有多少額外好處，也是讀者會想知道的。

The direct source is Conditional Similarity Networks by Veit et al. (CSN, CVPR 2017): learn a mask per condition over a shared embedding, multiply element-wise, and apply a triplet loss in the subspace. Music precedents include three works: disentangled multidimensional metric learning by Lee et al. (ICASSP 2020, Adobe), carving subspaces for genre, mood, instrumentation and so on; the multi-input architecture of Ribecky et al. (2021, Fraunhofer IDMT) for key, tempo and era; and Hashizume et al. (2025), extending conditional similarity to individual instrument parts in a mix. All train the backbone end-to-end from audio features; this paper differs only in freezing it.

It is worth saying plainly that the best configuration resembles something more ordinary. An MLP adapter plus fixed masks amounts to a small shared MLP whose output vector is cut into three equal slices, each doing metric learning for one attribute. That is multi-task projection heads on frozen embeddings, the probing setup benchmarks like MARBLE use to evaluate foundation models, plus a triplet loss. The paper frames it as post-hoc CSN but does not compare against three independent projection heads, so whether the mask structure itself is needed cannot be judged.

Another related direction, not cited, is conditional retrieval via text queries: CLAP-style models can already take a query like 'similar instrumentation'. How much a learned mask adds over that training-free approach is something readers would also want to know.

方法Method

骨幹與池化。 從凍結的 CLAP、MuQ 或 MERT 抽出逐格特徵；長曲目切成不重疊的 10 秒區塊後串接，再用自適應平均池化壓成固定的 128 段。CLAP 的雙流輸出先在第一維取平均。128 段再聚合成單一向量，有兩種選擇：直接平均，或用一層線性層對 128 段打注意力分數、softmax 後加權相加。

適配模組。 三選一：恆等（不變換）、單層（LayerNorm → 線性 → GELU，加殘差）、MLP（LayerNorm → 線性 → GELU → dropout → 線性，加殘差）。輸出記為 x̂。

遮罩。 每個條件 c ∈ {曲風、樂器、情緒} 有一個與嵌入同維的遮罩 m_c，屬性嵌入 e_c = m_c ⊙ x̂。固定遮罩：把維度平均切成三段不重疊的連續區塊，二值且不訓練。可學遮罩：從 N(0.9, 0.7²) 初始化，經 ReLU 後使用，以梯度更新。

損失。 每個條件各算一個三元組損失（歐氏距離、邊界 Δ = 0.1），再加三個正則項：遮罩 L1（λ = 10⁻⁵，讓遮罩稀疏）、遮罩兩兩之間的餘弦平方和（λ = 10⁻³，鼓勵子空間正交）、x̂ 的 L2（λ = 10⁻⁵，避免靠放大範數縮小相對距離）。固定遮罩下前兩項沒有梯度。

資料與評估。 MTG-Jamendo（raw_30s，55,701 首，依超過 3,500 位藝人分組切分以防洩漏）用於訓練與測試；MagnaTagATune（25,863 段，標籤經正規化並人工歸入三類）只當外部測試集。三元組條件：錨點與正例至少共享一個該類標籤，負例在該類有標籤但與錨點零交集、且藝人不同。訓練時用半困難挖掘，測試三元組離線固定。所有組合跑 5 個種子，Adam、學習率 10⁻⁴、批次 128、早停耐心 20。

評估指標有兩個。三元組準確率 A 分條件計算。效果 E = 用遮罩後的準確率 − 用未遮罩 x̂ 的準確率；特異度 S = 對自身條件的效果 − 對其他兩個條件效果的總和。理想的遮罩應讓自身條件上升、其他條件下降。

Backbone and pooling. Frame-level features come from frozen CLAP, MuQ or MERT; long tracks are cut into non-overlapping 10 s chunks and concatenated, then adaptive average pooling reduces them to a fixed 128 segments. CLAP's dual-stream output is first averaged over its first dimension. The 128 segments are aggregated into one vector either by plain averaging or by a linear layer that scores them, softmax-normalises over time and takes a weighted sum.

Adapter. One of three: Identity (no change), One Layer (LayerNorm → linear → GELU, with residual), MLP (LayerNorm → linear → GELU → dropout → linear, with residual). The output is x̂.

Masks. Each condition c ∈ {genre, instrument, mood} has a mask m_c of embedding dimension, and e_c = m_c ⊙ x̂. Fixed masks split the dimensions into three equal non-overlapping contiguous blocks, binary and untrained. Learned masks are initialised from N(0.9, 0.7²), passed through ReLU and trained by gradient descent.

Loss. A triplet loss per condition (Euclidean distance, margin Δ = 0.1), plus three regularisers: mask L1 (λ = 10⁻⁵, sparsity), the sum of squared cosine similarities between mask pairs (λ = 10⁻³, orthogonality), and L2 on x̂ (λ = 10⁻⁵, to stop inflated norms shrinking relative distances). Under fixed masks the first two have no gradient.

Data and evaluation. MTG-Jamendo (raw_30s, 55,701 tracks, split by more than 3,500 artists to prevent leakage) is used for training and testing; MagnaTagATune (25,863 clips, tags normalised and manually grouped into the three categories) serves only as an external test set. Triplets: anchor and positive share at least one tag of that category; the negative has tags in the category but none shared with the anchor, and a different artist. Training uses semi-hard mining; test triplets are fixed offline. Every configuration runs 5 seeds with Adam, learning rate 10⁻⁴, batch 128 and early-stopping patience 20.

There are two evaluation measures. Triplet accuracy A is computed per condition. Effect E = accuracy with the mask − accuracy with the unmasked x̂; Specificity S = effect on the mask's own condition − the sum of effects on the other two. An ideal mask raises its own condition and lowers the others.

限制Limitations

基準線比較不對等。 表 1 的基準線是「未訓練、平均池化的原始嵌入」，而所有其他設定都在 MTG-Jamendo 上用曲風、樂器、情緒標籤做過度量學習。用表 3 的數字拆開看：MERT 在 MTG-Jamendo 上，MLP 適配後未遮罩的 x̂ 三類平均已經是 68.4%，套上遮罩後是 68.6%，所以 10.5 個百分點的提升裡約 10.3 點來自訓練過的適配層，遮罩只多 0.2 點。CLAP 也是如此：基準 69.3%、x̂ 71.7%、遮罩後 72.7%，遮罩只貢獻約 1 點。

解糾纏主要靠壓低其他屬性。 作者自己也指出這點。CLAP 的遮罩對自身屬性的增益只有 +0.8 到 +1.3 點（樂器 73.2→74.1、曲風 75.5→76.8、情緒 66.4→67.2），但情緒遮罩讓樂器準確率掉 7.6 點（73.2→65.6）。更有問題的是 MuQ 與 MERT 的情緒遮罩：在 MTG-Jamendo 上連自身的情緒準確率都下降（MuQ 66.8→65.3、MERT 64.2→64.0）。特異度的定義把「其他屬性下降」算成加分，所以特異度數字高不代表遮罩真的抓到了該屬性。

其他沒交代的設計。 沒說明 MERT 用哪一層：MERT 最後一層在多數下游任務都不如中間層，58.1% 的低基準線可能部分來自這個選擇。標籤式三元組只要共享一個標籤就算正例，與人類對「樂器相近」的判斷未必一致，也沒有任何聽測或人工判斷驗證。這是 4 頁工作坊論文，作者也承認架構變化「結果不明確」。

The baseline comparison is uneven. Table 1's baseline is the untrained, mean-pooled raw embedding, while every other configuration has been metric-trained on MTG-Jamendo with genre, instrument and mood tags. Table 3 lets us split it: for MERT on MTG-Jamendo, the unmasked x̂ after the MLP adapter already averages 68.4% over the three types and the masked version 68.6%, so about 10.3 of the 10.5-point gain comes from the trained adapter and the masks add only 0.2. CLAP is the same: baseline 69.3%, x̂ 71.7%, masked 72.7%, the masks contributing about 1 point.

Disentanglement mostly works by lowering other attributes. The authors note this themselves. CLAP masks gain only +0.8 to +1.3 points on their own attribute (instrument 73.2→74.1, genre 75.5→76.8, mood 66.4→67.2), yet the mood mask drops instrument accuracy by 7.6 points (73.2→65.6). More troubling are the MuQ and MERT mood masks: on MTG-Jamendo they lower even their own mood accuracy (MuQ 66.8→65.3, MERT 64.2→64.0). The Specificity definition counts drops on other attributes as credit, so a high Specificity does not mean the mask actually captured its attribute.

Other unexplained choices. The MERT layer used is not stated; MERT's last layer underperforms middle layers on most downstream tasks, and the low 58.1% baseline may partly reflect that. Tag-based triplets count any shared tag as a positive, which may not match human judgements of similar instrumentation, and there is no listening test or human validation. This is a 4-page workshop paper, and the authors concede the architectural variations give inconclusive results.

討論Discussion

這篇最有用的發現其實是一個負面結果：固定遮罩只保留三分之一的維度，三類準確率卻幾乎不掉（表 3 裡在 MTG-Jamendo 上，各遮罩對自身屬性的準確率與未遮罩版相差都在 3 個百分點以內）。作者據此推論預訓練空間「高度糾纏、資訊冗餘地分佈在各維度」。這對想做可解釋推薦的人是重要提醒：直接在 CLAP 或 MERT 嵌入上挑維度，挑不出屬性專屬的方向，必須先經過一個訓練過的變換。

第二個發現是「需要可訓練參數、但不需要很多」。恆等適配（只訓練遮罩）在 MERT 上甚至略差於基準（57.8% 對 58.1%），而單層與 MLP 適配之間、平均與注意力池化之間、固定與可學遮罩之間的差距多半在 1–2 個百分點、方向隨骨幹與資料集而變。最佳特異度出現在不同設定：CLAP 在 MTG-Jamendo 是單層 + 注意力 + 固定遮罩的 7.41，MuQ 是單層 + 平均 + 固定遮罩的 7.69。換句話說，選最簡單的單層加固定遮罩就夠了。

跨資料集的泛化有正面訊號：只在 MTG-Jamendo 訓練，MagnaTagATune 上 MERT 仍從 60.3% 升到 70.3%。如果要把這篇往前推，我會先補三組對照：三個獨立投影頭、只用三元組損失訓練但不加遮罩的適配層、以及 CLAP 文字查詢式的條件檢索。這三組能回答遮罩這個結構到底有沒有在做事。

The most useful finding is actually a negative one: fixed masks keep only a third of the dimensions, yet accuracy on all three types barely drops (in Table 3, on MTG-Jamendo, each mask's accuracy on its own attribute is within 3 percentage points of the unmasked version). The authors conclude the pretrained space is highly entangled, with information spread redundantly across dimensions. That is an important warning for anyone building explainable recommendation: picking dimensions directly from CLAP or MERT embeddings will not yield attribute-specific directions; a trained transformation is needed first.

The second finding is that trainable parameters are needed, but not many. The Identity adapter (masks only) is even slightly below baseline on MERT (57.8% versus 58.1%), while the gaps between One Layer and MLP, mean and attention pooling, or fixed and learned masks are mostly 1–2 points, with direction depending on backbone and dataset. The best Specificity comes from different settings: 7.41 for CLAP on MTG-Jamendo with One Layer + attention + fixed masks, 7.69 for MuQ with One Layer + mean + fixed masks. In other words, the simplest One Layer with fixed masks is enough.

Cross-dataset generalisation is a positive sign: trained only on MTG-Jamendo, MERT still rises from 60.3% to 70.3% on MagnaTagATune. To push this further I would add three controls first: three independent projection heads, an adapter trained with triplet loss but no masks, and CLAP text-query conditional retrieval. Those would answer whether the mask structure is doing any work.

MusicFlux: Flexible Conditioning for Text-Guided Music Generation and Editing

MusicFlux：把 TangoFlux 流匹配 DiT 微調成同時做文字生成音樂、修補／延伸與音效退化還原的單一模型，比較 FLUX.1 Kontext 式的來源–目標序列串接，與首次用於音訊的 CAR-Flow 條件感知重參數化（以輕量平移網路把來源音訊、文字與時間遮罩編成流端點的加性位移，維持原序列長度）

Teysir Baoueb, Leonardo Boulitreau de M. M. Marques, Paraskevas Stamatiadis, Gaël Richard
arXiv:2611.13834 · cs.SD; cs.AI; cs.LG; eess.SP · 投稿submitted 2026-11-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：影像界的 FLUX.1 Kontext 把原圖跟待生成的目標串成一條序列，就讓同一個模型兼做生成與編輯；但音樂序列很長，序列加倍代價太高。這篇在 TangoFlux 上同時試了串接版與一個不加長序列的 CAR-Flow 版：後者用約 460 萬參數的平移網路，把來源音訊、文字與遮罩編成流的起點與終點的加性位移。結果是取捨分明——串接版修補 KAD 0.0099、CAR 版 0.2496；CAR 版推論的動態記憶體 75.7 MiB 對 150.1 MiB、即時率 0.119 對 0.220。選它是因為它把一個影像界的新條件化技巧第一次搬到音訊，而且誠實報出它輸在哪。但要注意：CAR 版在修補與還原上明顯落後，作者宣稱的「效率提升超過 50%」照表上數字算其實是 46–50%，而且整篇沒有聽測。Why this one: FLUX.1 Kontext in image editing concatenates the source image and the target into one sequence so a single model does both generation and editing; but music sequences are long and doubling them is costly. On top of TangoFlux this paper tries both a concatenation variant and a CAR-Flow variant that keeps the sequence length: the latter uses about 4.6M parameters of shift networks to encode source audio, text and mask as additive shifts of the flow's start and end points. The result is a clear trade-off: inpainting KAD 0.0099 for Concat versus 0.2496 for CAR; inference dynamic memory 75.7 MiB versus 150.1 MiB and real-time factor 0.119 versus 0.220 for CAR versus Concat. I picked it because it brings a new conditioning trick from image generation to audio for the first time and reports honestly where it loses. But note: CAR lags clearly on inpainting and restoration, the claimed 'over 50%' efficiency gain works out to 46–50% from the table's own numbers, and there is no listening test.

動機Motivation

音樂編輯要同時做到兩件事：滿足新的條件（換樂器、補一段、修掉音效失真），又保留原曲沒被要求改動的部分。訓練音樂生成模型很貴，所以編輯系統通常拿預訓練的文字生成音樂模型，加上「來源音訊」這個條件來改裝。

既有的改裝方式多半要額外的架構：外掛 adapter、ControlNet 分支、或額外的注意力運算。影像界最近出現一個更簡單的做法——FLUX.1 Kontext 直接把來源圖跟帶雜訊的目標串成一條序列，讓預訓練的自注意力自己學哪些要保留、哪些要生成，不用任何專門模組，生成和編輯用同一套權重。

問題是音訊很長。30 秒的潛在序列串接後長度加倍，自注意力成本約變成四倍，批次大小或可處理的音訊長度都要砍，收斂也受影響。作者想知道：能不能保留 Kontext 這種「統一生成與編輯」的好處，但不付序列加倍的代價？

Music editing has to do two things at once: satisfy a new condition (swap instruments, fill a gap, undo effect degradation) and preserve whatever was not asked to change. Training music generators is expensive, so editing systems usually adapt a pretrained text-to-music model by adding the source audio as a condition.

Existing adaptations mostly need extra architecture: adapters, a ControlNet branch, or additional attention. Image editing recently produced something simpler: FLUX.1 Kontext concatenates the source image with the noisy target into one sequence and lets the pretrained self-attention learn what to keep and what to generate, with no dedicated module and one set of weights for both generation and editing.

The catch is that audio is long. Concatenating a 30-second latent sequence doubles its length and roughly quadruples self-attention cost, forcing smaller batches or shorter audio and hurting convergence. The authors ask whether the unify-generation-and-editing benefit of Kontext can be kept without paying for the doubled sequence.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂編輯這側的前身很明確。AIRGen（Lin et al., 2024） 在 MusicGen 上掛異質 adapter 加遮罩訓練，做編曲、修補與精修；Instruct-MusicGen（Zhang et al., ISMIR 2025） 加音訊與文字融合模組，做指令式的聲部增刪與抽取；Hou et al.（ICASSP 2025） 給 Stable Audio Open 加 ControlNet 分支，用旋律與文字控制。作為比較對象的 Stable Audio 3 則是把來源音訊線性投影後加到每個 Transformer 區塊的隱藏狀態，SonicMaster 是把退化音訊直接當成整流流的起點，往乾淨音訊搬運。

論文的串接版就是 FLUX.1 Kontext（Black Forest Labs, 2025） 的直接移植，連位置編號的配置方式都照抄：目標佔 0…L−1、來源佔 L…2L−1。CAR 版的來源是 CAR-Flow（Chen et al., NeurIPS 2025）：用條件相關的加性位移 μ₀(y)、μ₁(y) 移動流的起點與終點，讓速度網路少學一部分搬運。

這篇宣稱首次把 CAR-Flow 用在音訊。值得指出的是，CAR 版在數學上跟兩個既有做法非常接近。把位移代進內插式，z_t = x_t + (1−σ)μ_tgt + σμ_src，也就是在網路輸入端加上一個隨時間內插的條件項——這跟 Stable Audio 3 的「投影後相加」 本質上是同一類加性條件化，差別只在於 SA3 每個區塊都加、CAR 版只在輸入端加，且同時改了回歸目標。另一方面，μ_src 把起點從純雜訊挪向來源，概念上也跟 SonicMaster 以退化音訊當起點 相近。論文沒有把 CAR 跟最常見的「把遮罩後的來源沿通道維度串接」做比較——那是 Stable Diffusion 修補模型的標準做法，同樣不加長序列，是最該拿來對照的基準。

The music-editing predecessors are clear. AIRGen (Lin et al., 2024) adds heterogeneous adapters with masked training to MusicGen for arrangement, inpainting and refinement; Instruct-MusicGen (Zhang et al., ISMIR 2025) adds audio and text fusion modules for instruction-driven stem addition, removal and extraction; Hou et al. (ICASSP 2025) give Stable Audio Open a ControlNet branch driven by melody and text. Among the baselines, Stable Audio 3 linearly projects the source audio and adds it to the hidden states of every Transformer block, and SonicMaster uses the degraded audio directly as the start of a rectified flow toward clean audio.

The Concat variant is a direct port of FLUX.1 Kontext (Black Forest Labs, 2025), down to the position-ID layout: target at 0…L−1, source at L…2L−1. The CAR variant comes from CAR-Flow (Chen et al., NeurIPS 2025): condition-dependent additive shifts μ₀(y), μ₁(y) move the flow's start and end points so the velocity network has less transport to learn.

The paper claims the first use of CAR-Flow for audio. Worth pointing out is how close the CAR variant is mathematically to two existing approaches. Substituting the shifts into the interpolant gives z_t = x_t + (1−σ)μ_tgt + σμ_src, an additive, time-interpolated conditioning term at the network input. That is the same family as Stable Audio 3's project-and-add, differing in that SA3 adds at every block while CAR adds only at the input and also changes the regression target. Meanwhile μ_src moves the start point from pure noise toward the source, conceptually close to SonicMaster starting from the degraded audio. The paper does not compare CAR with the most common alternative, concatenating the masked source along the channel dimension, the standard Stable Diffusion inpainting approach, which also keeps sequence length and is the baseline that most needs to be there.

方法Method

基底模型。 TangoFlux 是流匹配的混合 DiT：FLAN-T5 文字編碼接上學習的長度嵌入，與音訊潛在序列在 MMDiT 雙流區塊裡做聯合注意力，之後進單流區塊；時間步與池化後的文字向量經自適應正規化調變網路。最多 30 秒、44.1 kHz。作者先用 JamendoMaxCaps 把整個骨幹微調成文字生成音樂模型，再訓練編輯版本。網路是 6 個雙流 + 18 個單流區塊、8 個頭、每頭 128 維。

統一的遮罩表述。 三個任務共用同一組輸入：來源潛在 s、時間遮罩 m（1 = 要生成、0 = 已知）、文字。文字生成音樂時 s 是全零、m 全為 1；修補與延伸時 m 只在要補的區段為 1；音效還原時 s 是退化音訊、m 全為 0。

MusicFlux-Concat。 把 [x_t; s] 沿序列維度串接，遮罩 [0; m] 沿通道維度附加，得到 2L × (C+1) 的輸入；唯一新增的參數是從 C 到 C+1 通道的投影。DiT 對 2L 長度做完整自注意力，損失只算目標那一半。總參數約 860M。

MusicFlux-CAR。 兩個平移網路 μ_src、μ_tgt 都吃（s、池化文字向量 e、遮罩 m），產生加性位移：z₀ = ε + μ_src、z₁ = x₁ + μ_tgt。DiT 在長度 L 的 z_t = (1−σ_t)z₁ + σ_t z₀ 上預測 z₀ − z₁，推論後以 x̂₁ = z_final − μ_tgt 還原。每個平移網路有兩個分支。全域分支先對已知區段的 s 取平均，再跟 e 串接，經兩層 MLP 得到每通道的偏移，廣播到整條序列。空間分支對 s ⊙ (1−m) 做核大小 3 的一維深度可分離卷積，並加上可學的位置偏置。兩分支輸出串接後經零初始化的線性層，所以訓練一開始等於沒有位移。兩個網路合計約 460 萬參數，不到骨幹的 0.6%。

損失與訓練。 生成與還原用全序列 MSE；修補／延伸用遮罩區 MSE 加 0.1 倍的已知區 MSE。三個任務等機率抽樣。用 JamendoMaxCaps（約 36.2 萬首、5.8 萬小時，每 30 秒由 Qwen2-Audio 自動產生描述，共約 181 萬組）訓練 4 個 epoch，3 張 L40S，每卡批次 CAR 32、Concat 16。潛在空間來自 Stable Audio Open 1.0 的自編碼器，30 秒對應 645 個潛在格。推論 50 步、CFG 4.5，依驗證集 CAF-Score 挑檢查點。

評估。 生成：CAF-Score（CLAP 加大型音訊語言模型評分）與 KAD。修補／延伸：自訂的 FLEUR*，請 Qwen3-Omni 分別對（只聽編輯結果）、（配遮罩來源）、（配原始音訊）三種情境，以 0–1 評分邊界銜接、音色、響度、空間感、節奏與瑕疵，三者平均；外加 KAD。還原：沿用 SonicMaster 的 KAD、KL、梅爾頻譜 SSIM 與 Audiobox Aesthetics 的製作品質 PQ。

Base model. TangoFlux is a flow-matching hybrid DiT: FLAN-T5 text encoding plus a learned duration embedding attends jointly with audio latents in MMDiT dual-stream blocks before single-stream blocks; the timestep and pooled text vector modulate the network through adaptive normalisation. Up to 30 s at 44.1 kHz. The authors first fine-tune the whole backbone on JamendoMaxCaps for text-to-music, then train the editing variants. The network has 6 dual-stream + 18 single-stream blocks, 8 heads of 128 dimensions.

One masked formulation. All three tasks share one input set: source latent s, temporal mask m (1 = generate, 0 = known), and text. For text-to-music s is zeros and m all ones; for inpainting and outpainting m is 1 only on the region to fill; for effect restoration s is the degraded audio and m all zeros.

MusicFlux-Concat. [x_t; s] is concatenated along the sequence and the mask [0; m] appended as a channel, giving a 2L × (C+1) input; the only new parameters are a projection from C to C+1 channels. The DiT runs full self-attention over length 2L and the loss is computed only on the target half. About 860M parameters in total.

MusicFlux-CAR. Two shift networks μ_src and μ_tgt take (s, pooled text vector e, mask m) and produce additive shifts: z₀ = ε + μ_src, z₁ = x₁ + μ_tgt. The DiT predicts z₀ − z₁ on z_t = (1−σ_t)z₁ + σ_t z₀ of length L, and after inference x̂₁ = z_final − μ_tgt. Each shift network has two branches. The global branch averages s over known frames, concatenates e and runs a two-layer MLP for a per-channel offset broadcast along the sequence. The spatial branch applies a kernel-3 depthwise-separable 1-D convolution to s ⊙ (1−m) plus a learnable positional bias. The branches are concatenated and passed through a zero-initialised linear layer, so training starts with no shift. Together the two networks add about 4.6M parameters, under 0.6% of the backbone.

Loss and training. Generation and restoration use full-sequence MSE; inpainting/outpainting uses masked-region MSE plus 0.1 × known-region MSE. The three tasks are sampled with equal probability. Training uses JamendoMaxCaps (about 362K tracks, 58K hours, each 30 s auto-captioned by Qwen2-Audio, about 1.81M pairs) for 4 epochs on 3 L40S GPUs, per-device batch 32 for CAR and 16 for Concat. Latents come from the Stable Audio Open 1.0 autoencoder, 645 frames per 30 s. Inference uses 50 steps and CFG 4.5, with checkpoints chosen by validation CAF-Score.

Evaluation. Generation: CAF-Score (CLAP plus large audio-language model rating) and KAD. Inpainting/outpainting: a custom FLEUR* in which Qwen3-Omni rates, from 0 to 1, boundary continuity, timbre, loudness, ambience, rhythm and artefacts in three settings (edited audio alone, paired with the masked source, paired with the original), averaged; plus KAD. Restoration: SonicMaster's KAD, KL, mel-spectrogram SSIM and Audiobox Aesthetics production quality (PQ).

限制Limitations

CAR 版在需要細節的任務上明顯落後。 修補／延伸的 KAD：JMC 測試集串接版 0.0099、CAR 版 0.2496（約 25 倍）；SDD 上 0.0380 對 0.4744。音效還原的 KAD：串接版 0.0489、CAR 版 0.2680，CAR 版還比專門模型 SonicMaster 的 0.1137 差，KL、SSIM、PQ 四項也全輸 SonicMaster。所以摘要說「兩者都能跟強基準競爭」，對 CAR 版的還原結果並不成立。作者的解釋（加性位移成為資訊瓶頸）很合理：空間分支只有核大小 3 的卷積，全域分支把整段來源平均成一個向量，局部細節能傳進去的管道很窄。

效率數字被說得比表上大。 結論寫推論的即時率與動態記憶體「減少超過 50%」。照表 2 計算：即時率 0.2203→0.1188 是減少 46%，動態記憶體 150.11→75.71 MiB 是減少 49.6%，兩者都沒超過 50%。訓練時的動態記憶體減少 28%（2748→1988 MiB）與表上一致。另外，量測是批次 1、645 格的 30 秒序列；序列加倍的代價主要在更長的音訊上才會放大，論文沒有量測更長的長度。

評估設計的問題。 沒有任何人類聽測，修補品質完全靠大型音訊語言模型評分（FLEUR*）。生成任務上 CAR 版的 CAF-Score 0.5196 甚至高於真實音訊的 0.5102，提示這個指標在這個區間可能已經飽和、分不出好壞。訓練資料的描述是 Qwen2-Audio 自動產生的，跨域到人寫描述的 SDD 時，兩個版本的生成都輸 SA3（CAF 0.4950–0.5213 對 0.5593）。基準只在部分欄位有數字：SA3 沒有 JMC 的結果。也沒有平移網路的消融（只用全域分支、只用空間分支、只移起點或終點）。

CAR lags clearly on detail-sensitive tasks. Inpainting/outpainting KAD on the JMC test set is 0.0099 for Concat and 0.2496 for CAR (about 25×); on SDD 0.0380 versus 0.4744. Effect-restoration KAD is 0.0489 for Concat and 0.2680 for CAR, which is worse than the specialised SonicMaster's 0.1137, and CAR also loses to SonicMaster on all four of KAD, KL, SSIM and PQ. So the abstract's claim that both are competitive with strong baselines does not hold for CAR on restoration. The authors' explanation, that additive shifts act as an information bottleneck, is plausible: the spatial branch is just a kernel-3 convolution and the global branch averages the whole source into one vector, so the channel for local detail is narrow.

The efficiency figure is stated larger than the table. The conclusion says inference real-time factor and dynamic memory drop 'by over 50%'. From Table 2, real-time factor 0.2203→0.1188 is a 46% reduction and dynamic memory 150.11→75.71 MiB is 49.6%, neither over 50%. The 28% training dynamic-memory reduction (2748→1988 MiB) matches the table. Measurements also use batch 1 and a 645-frame 30 s sequence; the cost of doubling grows mainly on longer audio, which is not measured.

Evaluation design. There is no human listening test; inpainting quality rests entirely on a large audio-language model's ratings (FLEUR*). On generation, CAR's CAF-Score of 0.5196 is even above real audio's 0.5102, suggesting the metric may be saturated in this range and unable to separate quality. Training captions are auto-generated by Qwen2-Audio, and out of domain on the human-captioned SDD both variants lose to SA3 on generation (CAF 0.4950–0.5213 versus 0.5593). Baselines have numbers only in some columns: SA3 has no JMC results. There is no ablation of the shift networks (global branch only, spatial branch only, shifting only the start or only the end).

討論Discussion

這篇最清楚的貢獻是把「統一生成與編輯」的兩種條件化路線放在同一個基底、同一份資料上做了公平的對照，而且結果是一個乾淨的取捨。串接版讓來源直接進注意力，所有修補與還原的分佈指標都最好：SDD 修補 KAD 0.0380，比 SA3 的 1.4794 低了一個數量級以上，FLEUR* 0.8428 也高於 SA3 的 0.8248。CAR 版犧牲了這些，換到序列長度不變、推論成本約減半、訓練時每卡能放兩倍的批次。

值得注意的是，CAR 版在 SDD 修補上仍贏 SA3（KAD 0.4744 對 1.4794、FLEUR* 0.8390 對 0.8248），而 SA3 也是一種加性條件化，而且每個區塊都加。如果這個比較成立，說明在流的端點上做位移，比在網路內部相加更有效率。但兩者的骨幹、訓練資料（開放的 JamendoMaxCaps 對私有的 AudioSparx + Freesound）與參數量（約 859M 對 459M）都不同，這個結論目前只能當假說。

我認為最值得追的方向是兩者的混合：用 CAR 位移傳遞全域資訊（文字、整體音色），再只把遮罩邊界附近的少量來源潛在格串接進序列，給注意力看局部細節。另一條是把空間分支換成更大感受野的網路，看修補 KAD 的差距能補回多少。作為一篇 4 頁、沒有聽測的會議投稿，它的實驗結論可信度有限，但「CAR-Flow 能用在音訊編輯、代價在局部細節」這件事已經被清楚示範。

The clearest contribution is a fair comparison of two conditioning routes for unifying generation and editing, on the same base model and data, with a clean trade-off as the result. Concat feeds the source straight into attention and wins every distribution metric for inpainting and restoration: SDD inpainting KAD 0.0380, more than an order of magnitude below SA3's 1.4794, and FLEUR* 0.8428 above SA3's 0.8248. CAR gives these up in exchange for unchanged sequence length, roughly half the inference cost, and twice the per-GPU batch in training.

Notably, CAR still beats SA3 on SDD inpainting (KAD 0.4744 versus 1.4794, FLEUR* 0.8390 versus 0.8248), and SA3 is also an additive conditioning, applied at every block. If the comparison holds, shifting the flow endpoints is more efficient than adding inside the network. But backbone, training data (open JamendoMaxCaps versus private AudioSparx + Freesound) and size (about 859M versus 459M) all differ, so for now this is a hypothesis.

The direction I find most worth pursuing is a hybrid: carry global information (text, overall timbre) with CAR shifts, and concatenate only a few source latent frames near the mask boundary so attention can see local detail. Another is replacing the spatial branch with a network of larger receptive field to see how much of the inpainting KAD gap closes. As a 4-page submission without a listening test its experimental conclusions carry limited weight, but it clearly demonstrates that CAR-Flow works for audio editing and that the cost lands on local detail.
