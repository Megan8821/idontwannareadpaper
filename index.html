Understanding Automatic Mixing: A Subtask-Oriented Analysis of Two-Stage Mixing System

理解自動混音：兩階段混音系統的子任務式分析

Jinjie Shi, Wei Hua, Kunzhu Xie, Make Li, Yuchen Liu, Joshua Reiss
arXiv:2609.02835 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：用受控聽測比較兩階段混音系統與單階段基線，證明明確的子任務拆解本身能帶來增益：MEGAMI 家族兩階段版本以 61.57 分打贏單階段的 54.65 分（q=0.0426），Diff-MST 家族兩階段版本以 28.69 分打贏單階段的 19.44 分（q=7.25×10⁻⁴），同時發現分組錯誤比響度錯誤傷害更大。Why this one: Uses controlled listening tests to compare two-stage mixing systems against single-stage baselines, showing that explicit subtask decomposition itself helps: the two-stage MEGAMI variant scores 61.57 versus 54.65 for its single-stage counterpart (q=0.0426), and the two-stage Diff-MST variant scores 28.69 versus 19.44 (q=7.25×10⁻⁴), while grouping errors turn out to hurt far more than loudness errors.

動機Motivation

自動混音要同時處理多軌錄音裡的局部平衡、頻譜與動態互動、空間配置與整體風格，軌數一多、樂器種類一雜，問題就變得棘手。近來的兩階段系統把「組內處理」跟「組間協調」分開訓練，看起來效果不錯，但沒人真的驗證過：這些系統贏，到底是因為子模型本身比較強，還是因為拆解任務這件事本身就有幫助？這篇論文不提新的混音系統，而是把既有的兩階段框架當成分析工具，用三組受控聽測直接回答這個問題。

Automatic mixing has to juggle local balance, spectral and dynamic interactions between tracks, spatial placement, and overall style all at once, and this only gets harder as track counts grow and instrumentation diversifies. Recent two-stage systems, which train intra-group processing separately from inter-group coordination, look promising, but nobody had actually verified whether their gains come from stronger component models or from the decomposition itself. Rather than proposing a new mixing system, this paper uses an existing two-stage framework purely as an analysis scaffold, running three controlled listening experiments to answer that question directly.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名了四個具體系統當比較對象。ELL（Equal Local Loudness）是不需訓練、純規則式的組內平衡方法；Diff-MST 用可微分混音台預測增益、等化、壓縮與聲像等參數；MEGAMI 用條件式生成模型產生協調過的軌道層級效果表徵，但公開釋出的權重只在 14 軌以內的資料上訓練過，拿來做全混音推論時得先靠中繼資料把軌道分組、湊進它習慣的軌數；NoMix 則是只做共用前處理、完全不混音的對照組。

先前也有工作證明分組能改善主觀的清晰度與品質評分，以及有些系統本身就用分開訓練的子群組模型，但論文指出這些工作都沒有把「拆解本身的貢獻」跟「子模型較強」這兩件事分開驗證過——這正是這篇論文要補的洞。

The paper names four concrete comparison systems. ELL (Equal Local Loudness) is a training-free, rule-based method for intra-group balancing; Diff-MST predicts gain, EQ, compression, and panning through a differentiable mixing console; MEGAMI uses conditional generative modeling to produce coordinated track-level effect representations, but its released checkpoint was only trained on 14 tracks or fewer, so full-mix inference needs metadata-driven grouping to fit tracks into that budget; NoMix is an unprocessed control that applies only the shared preprocessing.

Prior work has shown subgrouping can improve perceived clarity and quality ratings, and some systems already train separate subgroup models, but none of it separated the contribution of decomposition itself from simply having stronger component models -- that is the gap this paper fills.

方法Method

框架把多軌輸入 X 透過分組函式 g 切成 G 組，先各自跑組內處理 F_intra，再由 F_inter 做組間協調產出最終混音 y。論文設計了三種分組方式：先前資料集慣用的「貝斯/鼓/人聲/其他」四分組；論文新設計、更貼近混音實務的七分組（貝斯獨立為低頻，鼓拆成中低打擊與高頻打擊，人聲與其他都再拆出主奏/伴奏/鋪底）；以及直接依軌道 metadata 標籤分組、通常會超過七組的「依樂器分組」。

實驗把 ELL 當組內處理，搭配 MEGAMI 或 Diff-MST 當組間協調，組成兩階段版本（2S-MEGAMI、2S-Diff-MST），跟原本直接吃全部軌道的單階段版本對照。三組聽測分別回答：全混音模型能不能直接搬到組內混音任務上（RQ1）、下游模型能不能補償分組或響度上的錯誤（RQ2）、以及兩階段拆解是否真的改善全混音品質（RQ3）。26 位受試者中留下 18 位通過 Pearson r>0.75 的一致性篩選，對 3 段各 23–27 軌的流行/搖滾片段打 1–100 分，共蒐集 1170 筆評分。

The framework splits multitrack input X into G groups via a grouping function g, runs each group through intra-group processing F_intra, then coordinates them with F_inter to produce the final mix y. Three grouping schemes are tested: the four-group bass/drums/vocal/other convention from prior datasets; a new seven-group scheme designed for mixing practice (splitting bass into low-frequency, drums into low-mid versus high percussion, and both vocal and other into lead/accompaniment/pad); and an instrument-based grouping driven directly by track metadata, which typically produces more than seven groups.

ELL serves as the intra-group processor, paired with either MEGAMI or Diff-MST for inter-group coordination to form two-stage variants (2S-MEGAMI, 2S-Diff-MST), compared against single-stage versions that process all tracks directly. Three listening experiments answer in turn whether full-mix models transfer to intra-group mixing (RQ1), whether downstream models compensate for grouping or loudness errors (RQ2), and whether two-stage decomposition genuinely improves full-mix quality (RQ3). Of 26 initial participants, 18 passed a Pearson r>0.75 reliability filter and rated three 23-27-track pop/rock excerpts on a 1-100 scale, yielding 1,170 ratings in total.

限制Limitations

只測了三段編制稠密的流行/搖滾片段，音樂風格與可推廣性有限；Diff-MST 在多個比較裡分數普遍偏低，可能已經碰到評分下限，導致「下游模型真的有沒有補償錯誤」跟「分數本來就壓不下去」很難分開判讀。

分組跟各階段模型都是照預先定好的規則手動執行，雖然有利於重現，但要走向全自動的功能分組跟端到端整合仍是未來工作。留下的 18 位受試者以有混音或音樂製作經驗者為主，適合做細緻的技術比較，但不一定代表一般聽眾的偏好。

Only three densely arranged pop/rock excerpts were tested, limiting musical diversity and how far the findings generalize. Diff-MST scored uniformly low across several comparisons, which may reflect a floor effect that makes it hard to tell genuine downstream compensation apart from ratings that simply can't drop further.

Grouping and per-stage models were executed manually according to predefined rules, which helps reproducibility but leaves fully automatic functional grouping and end-to-end integration as future work. The 18 retained listeners were mostly experienced in mixing or music production, suitable for fine-grained technical comparisons but not necessarily representative of general-audience preferences.

討論Discussion

三組聽測合起來指出一個具體的設計原則。RQ1 顯示全混音模型轉移到組內任務時表現差異很大——ELL 與 MEGAMI 的組內評分（61.08、55.47）明顯高於 Diff-MST（21.36，q 值最低到 2.68×10⁻⁸），說明轉移能力跟模型架構與訓練方式有關，不是「全混音模型天生都能處理簡單任務」。RQ2 顯示分組錯誤傷害明確：MEGAMI 從四分組換成七分組，評分從 50.59 顯著拉高到 65.39（q=6.44×10⁻⁶）；但響度錯誤的影響普遍不顯著（MEGAMI 的 with-balance 63.39 vs no-balance 57.13，q=0.151）。

RQ3 是最直接的證據：兩階段版本在 MEGAMI 家族（61.57 vs 54.65，q=0.0426）與 Diff-MST 家族（28.69 vs 19.44，q=7.25×10⁻⁴）都顯著贏過對應的單階段版本，證明拆解本身、而不只是子模型強弱，是兩階段系統勝出的原因。論文因此主張：未來系統可以把「有明確目標的局部任務」交給高效率的專用處理，再用學習模型做全域協調，而不是硬把整個混音問題塞進一個模型裡。

The three listening experiments together point to a concrete design principle. RQ1 shows transfer varies sharply by model: ELL and MEGAMI score much higher on intra-group mixing (61.08 and 55.47) than Diff-MST (21.36, with q as low as 2.68x10^-8), meaning transfer ability tracks model architecture and training, not just whether a model was built for full mixes. RQ2 shows grouping errors matter concretely: switching MEGAMI from four groups to seven raises its score significantly from 50.59 to 65.39 (q=6.44x10^-6), while loudness perturbations mostly don't reach significance (MEGAMI's with-balance 63.39 vs. no-balance 57.13, q=0.151).

RQ3 is the most direct evidence: two-stage variants significantly beat their single-stage counterparts for both the MEGAMI family (61.57 vs. 54.65, q=0.0426) and the Diff-MST family (28.69 vs. 19.44, q=7.25x10^-4), showing decomposition itself -- not just component strength -- drives the two-stage advantage. The paper's takeaway is that future systems should hand well-defined local objectives to efficient task-specific processing and reserve learned models for global coordination, rather than forcing the whole mixing problem into one monolithic model.

Vocal Music under Phoneme-Conditional Analysis

音素條件分析下的聲樂音樂

Hayoon Kim, Kyogu Lee
arXiv:2608.30823 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-31
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：用同一首歌裡「有標記音素」對「配對控制音素」的內部比較，拆解出音素本身如何影響演唱的聲學特徵，涵蓋九種語言、超過五千首歌；結果顯示只靠 35 維歌曲層級特徵向量就能做九語言分類，達到 85.5% 平衡準確率（機率基準僅 11.1%），但語言層級的「音素效應會不會累積成語言指紋」（H2）在九個語言的樣本數下仍未獲得統計支持。Why this one: Compares marker phonemes against matched control phonemes within the same song to isolate how phoneme identity itself shapes the acoustics of singing, across nine languages and over five thousand songs; a 35-dimensional song-level feature vector alone reaches 85.5% balanced accuracy on nine-way language classification (chance is 11.1%), though whether phoneme-level effects actually accumulate into a language fingerprint (H2) is not statistically supported at this nine-language sample size.

動機Motivation

不同語言的聲樂音樂，就算拿掉伴奏，聽起來也帶著自己的聲音身分——阿拉伯流行樂偏好密集的裝飾音與彈性的音節時長，日本流行樂則整齊劃一、幾乎不用花腔。論文要問的是：這種差異能不能被量化，而且能不能追溯回具體的音素？

先前研究已經證明語言結構會形塑音樂型態（節奏變化反映母語的語調節奏、聲調語言的詞彙音高會限制旋律走向），但留下三個缺口：節奏研究大多用樂譜級的符號時值，量不到音節以下的精細時長；子音庫存（如咽音、擠喉音、捲舌音）對演唱的影響幾乎沒人研究過；節奏、聲調、音色三者過去分開研究、樣本量小，沒有統一整合過。論文提出三層假說：H1 是音素本身的構音/聲學特性會直接形塑所在音節的演唱實現；H2 是這些 H1 效應反覆出現、累積成語言特有的演唱型態；H3 是這種型態會固化成一個音樂傳統的身分特徵。

Vocal music from different languages carries a distinctive sonic identity even with the accompaniment stripped away -- Arabic pop favors dense micro-ornamentation and flexible syllable timing, while Japanese pop is metronomically uniform and largely avoids melisma. The paper asks whether these differences are measurable and traceable to specific phonemes.

Prior work established that linguistic structure shapes musical patterns -- rhythmic variability mirrors native-language prosody, and lexical tone constrains melodic contour -- but left three gaps: rhythm studies relied on notation-level durations that miss sub-syllabic timing; the effect of consonant inventories (pharyngeals, ejectives, retroflexes) on singing was virtually unexamined; and rhythm, tone, and timbre had only been studied separately on small samples, never integrated. The paper proposes a three-level hypothesis: H1, that a phoneme's articulatory/acoustic properties directly shape how its host syllable is sung; H2, that recurring H1 effects accumulate into language-specific vocal patterns; and H3, that such patterns harden into a musical tradition's identity.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

節奏方面的標準工具是正規化成對變異指數（nPVI），先前研究在語言與音樂類型間重複驗證出「重音計時/音節計時」的鏡像關係，但論文指出在聲樂音樂裡這個鏡像「會減弱甚至反轉，而且在真正演唱該語言時最弱」——作者把這歸咎於符號記譜分析的解析度不足，而不是語言與音樂的連結本身失效。

聲調與旋律對應方面，已有研究指出粵語流行曲有超過九成的聲調-旋律走向一致，但別的傳統就鬆得多；處理歌詞重音與音節感知中心的既有工作，始終沒碰觸「次要構音特徵（咽音、擠喉音、捲舌音）對聲學的實際影響」這塊。音素標記的聲學特徵則借用既有的語音學基準：英語 /ɹ/ 的 F3 下降、阿拉伯語強調音的 F2 下降、韓語喉音對比的 VOT 與音高起始差異、法語鼻化元音的延長、日語促音的閉塞時長比。跨文化研究已經證實歌唱整體上比說話慢、音高高且穩定，但「哪些音韻特性造成這些差異、音素層級的效應如何累加成一個傳統的特有聲音」始終沒有答案——這正是這篇論文要填的空。

The standard rhythm tool is the normalized Pairwise Variability Index (nPVI); prior work repeatedly confirmed a stress-timed/syllable-timed mirroring between language and music, but the paper notes that in vocal music this mirroring "diminishes or reverses, weakest where the language is actually sung" -- the authors attribute this to the limited resolution of notation-based analysis rather than a failure of the speech-music link itself.

On tone-melody correspondence, prior work found over 90% parallel tone-melody motion in Cantonese pop, though other traditions are far looser; text-setting research on lyric stress and syllable perceptual centers never addressed how secondary articulatory features (pharyngeals, ejectives, retroflexes) actually affect the acoustics. Marker phonemes borrow acoustic signatures from established phonetics: F3 lowering for English /r/, F2 lowering for Arabic emphatics, VOT and pitch-onset separation for Korean laryngeal contrasts, French nasal-vowel lengthening, Japanese geminate closure-duration ratios. Cross-cultural studies already show singing is globally slower, higher, and more pitch-stable than speech, but which phonological properties produce these differences, and how phoneme-local effects aggregate into a tradition's characteristic sound, remained open -- that is the gap this paper fills.

方法Method

核心方法是「音素條件分析」：在同一首歌裡，把某個有標記音素配對到聲學上相似的非標記控制音素，同時固定歌手、旋律與曲風。九種語言橫跨六個語系、三種構音標記類型（如阿拉伯語的咽音/擠喉音、英語的央元音/近音 /ɹ/、法語的鼻化元音/小舌音、印地語的捲舌音/氣聲濁音、日語的促音/鼻音/雙唇擦音、韓語的三向喉部對比、土耳其語的高後不圓唇元音，以及國語與瑞典語額外疊加聲調/音高重音）。

語料來自 Spotify 各國每日排行榜、對應到 YouTube 的 48kHz/24bit FLAC 音檔，語言判定要求四個訊號中至少兩個一致；歷經人聲分離、歌詞轉寫、逐音素對齊後，9331 首歌有音素層級對齊，通過「對齊一致性」門檻的剩 5024 首（各語言 370–751 首）。人聲分離用 Mel-Band RoFormer（SDR > 3 dB）；對齊同時跑兩套聲學模型（MMS 強制對齊、在 5062 段演唱語料上微調過的 wav2vec2 CTC）取平均，在 NUS-48E 標竿上驗證，74.6% 的音素起點誤差在 50 毫秒內，優於任一套單獨模型；每個音素中央 70% 區段抽取五類聲學特徵（共振峰與頻譜傾角、旋律偏移、時長、花腔音符數、音色）。

統計上，先用配對 Cohen's d 搭配 5000 次置換檢定與 Bonferroni 校正（50 格，α=0.05/50）抓效應量，再用線性混合模型控制節拍位置；最後用 35 維歌曲層級特徵（20 個標記-控制差異加 15 個原始音素分布統計）餵給 200 棵樹的隨機森林，以歌手分組做五折交叉驗證預測語言。

The core method is phoneme-conditional analysis: within a single song, each marker phoneme is paired with an acoustically matched non-marker control phoneme, holding singer, melody, and genre constant. Nine languages span six language families and three marker types (Arabic pharyngeals/emphatics, English schwa/approximant /r/, French nasal vowels/uvular, Hindi retroflexes/breathy stops, Japanese geminates/nasal/bilabial fricative, Korean's three-way laryngeal contrast, Turkish high back unrounded vowel, plus Mandarin and Swedish which layer tone/pitch-accent on top of articulatory markers).

The corpus comes from Spotify national daily charts matched to 48kHz/24-bit FLAC audio on YouTube, with language identification requiring agreement from at least two of four signals; after vocal separation, lyric transcription, and phoneme alignment, 9,331 tracks got phoneme-level alignment, and 5,024 passed an alignment-agreement gate (370-751 per language). Vocal separation uses Mel-Band RoFormer (SDR > 3 dB); alignment averages two acoustic models (MMS forced alignment and a wav2vec2 CTC model fine-tuned on 5,062 sung utterances), validated on NUS-48E where 74.6% of onsets land within 50ms, beating either model alone; five acoustic dimensions (formants and spectral tilt, melodic deviation, duration, melisma note count, timbre) are extracted from the central 70% of each phoneme.

Statistically, paired Cohen's d with 5,000-iteration permutation tests and Bonferroni correction (50 cells, alpha=0.05/50) captures effect sizes, linear mixed models control for metrical position, and a 35-dimensional song-level vector (20 marker-control differences plus 15 raw phoneme-distribution statistics) feeds a 200-tree random forest predicting language under artist-grouped 5-fold cross-validation.

限制Limitations

九種語言排除了搭嘴音語言、擠喉音豐富的語系與更複雜的聲調系統；排行榜語料集中在 2018–2024 年、每種語言只綁定一到兩個國家，地理代表性有限。音素邊界的對齊誤差會傳播到所有特徵上，設下可測效應量的下限——只有在起點誤差遠小於音素本身時長時，單一音素的效應才抓得到。

拿來對照的「說話基準」來自不同語料庫、不同說話者、不同蒐集流程，論文自己承認「保存比例只是量級上的比較，不是真正配對過的對照」。46% 的曲目沒通過對齊一致性門檻，集中在阿拉伯語與瑞典語，論文認為問題出在「歌詞品質不夠好，不是對齊器不夠好」。人聲分離後殘留的伴奏會滲入音色與頻譜特徵——恰好是效應量最大的那些維度。論文測的是聲學特性，不是可聽性，認為「下一步該做的是感知測試」；而 H3（語言型態是否固化成傳統身分）完全沒有測試，作者建議未來要用翻唱版本或多語言歌手來拆解這個問題。

The nine languages exclude click languages, ejective-rich inventories, and richer tone systems; the chart-based corpus is concentrated in 2018-2024 and ties each language to one or two countries, limiting geographic representativeness. Phoneme boundary errors propagate into every feature, setting a floor on measurable effect sizes -- single-phoneme effects are only recoverable when onset error is well below the phoneme's own duration.

The speech baselines used for comparison come from different corpora, speakers, and protocols, and the authors themselves call the preservation ratios "order-of-magnitude comparisons rather than matched contrasts." 46% of tracks failed the alignment-agreement gate, concentrated in Arabic and Swedish, which the paper attributes to needing "better lyrics, not a better aligner." Residual accompaniment surviving vocal separation leaks into timbral and spectral features -- exactly where the largest effects sit. The paper measures acoustics, not audibility, and states that "a perceptual test is the probe" for the next step; H3, whether these patterns harden into tradition-level identity, is left entirely untested, with cover songs or multilingual singers proposed as a way to disentangle it in future work.

討論Discussion

結果整體支持 H1：九組標記音素中有九組（除了印地語）在至少三個聲學維度上達到顯著效應，時長效應被壓縮得最明顯——國語聲調延長只達到已發表口語基準的約 30%（+26 毫秒 vs 口語的 50–120 毫秒），韓語氣聲/緊喉對比甚至反轉方向（演唱中變成 −10 毫秒，口語裡是 +25–40 毫秒）；共振峰轉變只有在捲舌這種「歌手本來就得做的舌體動作」上完整保留（國語捲舌 F2 位移 +543 Hz，幾乎等於口語基準的 +500 Hz），次要構音特徵如阿拉伯語強調音的 F2 位移只剩口語基準的約 4%。論文據此推論：歌唱裡承載音素身分的主要是音源頻譜特性（音色、發聲品質），而不是共振峰轉變。

H2 在語言層級得到的支持很薄弱：35 維歌曲層級向量做九語言分類拿到 85.5% 平衡準確率（機率基準 11.1%），遠遠超過隨機，證明語言之間確實可分；但用「音素標記出現頻率」或「效應量大小」去預測各語言的分類召回率，Spearman 相關係數只有 0.27 與 0.50、p 值 0.49 與 0.17（n=9），論文自己的結論是「在這個樣本數下不支持，但也沒被推翻」。論文最後把這個發現接回 MIR：語言其實是聲樂音軌的一個可聽屬性，而且主要棲身在發聲與頻譜形狀，而不是聲樂模型慣常評估的音高與時序上。

The results broadly support H1: nine of ten marker sets (all but Hindi) reach significant effects across at least three acoustic dimensions, with duration effects compressed the most clearly -- Mandarin tonal lengthening reaches only about 30% of published speech baselines (+26ms vs. 50-120ms in speech), and Korean's aspirated/tense contrast even reverses direction (-10ms when sung vs. +25-40ms in speech). Formant transitions are only fully preserved where the gesture is one "the singer must perform anyway" -- Mandarin retroflex F2 shift (+543Hz, almost matching the +500Hz speech baseline) -- while secondary articulatory features like Arabic emphatic F2 shift retain only about 4% of the speech baseline. The paper concludes that phoneme identity in singing is carried mainly by source-spectrum properties like timbre and voice quality, not by formant transitions.

Support for H2 at the language level is thin: the 35-dimensional song-level vector reaches 85.5% balanced accuracy on nine-way language classification (chance is 11.1%), far above chance and establishing that languages are indeed separable, but using marker frequency or effect magnitude to predict each language's classification recall yields Spearman correlations of only 0.27 and 0.50 with p=0.49 and 0.17 (n=9) -- the authors' own conclusion is that this is "unsupported rather than refuted" at this sample size. The paper ties this back to MIR: language turns out to be an audible property of a vocal track, one that lives mainly in phonation and spectral shape rather than the pitch and timing that singing-voice models are usually evaluated on.

What Are You Listening to? Temporal Music Grounding for Audio-to-Text Large Language Models

你在聽什麼？音頻轉文字大型語言模型的音樂時序定位

Kun Fang, Ziyu Wang, Ichiro Fujinaga
arXiv:2608.29480 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-31
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：提出「音樂時序定位」任務與 MusicGroundingBench 基準，用可控生成的鋼琴 MIDI 音訊測試模型能不能把答案釘在真正的時間區段上，而不是只看最後文字答對沒；結果顯示 Music Flamingo 與 Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct 這兩個現成音頻語言模型在零樣本下幾乎定位失敗（音符級 IoU≥0.5 僅 0.0842 與 0.0006），但針對任務微調過的自製模型能拉到 0.57–0.81，顯示問題出在訓練資料而非任務本身做不到。Why this one: Introduces temporal music grounding and the MusicGroundingBench benchmark, using controlled synthetic piano MIDI audio to test whether models can localize their answers to real time spans rather than just getting the final text answer right; results show two off-the-shelf audio-language models, Music Flamingo and Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct, largely fail to ground in zero-shot settings (note-level IoU>=0.5 of only 0.0842 and 0.0006), while task-specifically fine-tuned custom models reach 0.57-0.81, indicating the gap is about training data rather than the task being inherently unsolvable.

動機Motivation

大型音頻語言模型能生出聽起來很像那麼回事、音樂上也說得通的回答，但論文指出這不代表回答真的「紮根」在音訊輸入上——現有的音樂評測大多只看最終文字輸出對不對，不會去檢查模型的答案是不是真的對應到一段具體的時間區間。這件事在音樂上特別重要，因為要判斷的目標常常是關係性的、重複出現的，或由結構定義出來的，而不是單一、可以一眼抓到的聲音事件，模型很容易講出聽起來合理、其實沒有真正「聽」過的答案。

視覺與影片領域的定位任務已經研究很久，但現有的音訊定位工作幾乎都只做環境音、語音事件，或很粗略的時間對齊——音樂需要的精細時序定位是一塊沒人碰過的空白，這篇論文要填的正是這個洞。

Large audio-language models can produce responses that sound fluent and musically plausible, but the paper argues this doesn't mean the responses are actually grounded in the audio input -- existing music evaluations mostly check whether the final text answer is correct, without testing whether that answer corresponds to a specific, real time span. This matters especially for music, where the relevant target is often relational, repeated, or structurally defined rather than a single, easily-spotted acoustic event, making it easy for a model to produce a plausible-sounding answer without genuinely perceiving it.

Grounding has been studied extensively in vision and video, but existing audio grounding work focuses almost entirely on environmental sounds, speech events, or coarse temporal alignment -- the fine-grained temporal localization music requires is an unaddressed gap, and this paper sets out to fill it.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

先前的定位系統多半來自視覺與影片領域，論文點名了 TALL、Proposal-Free Video Grounding、UniVTG、SlowFocus 等具體方法，以及 BLIP-2 這類具備定位能力的多模態 LLM。音訊定位方面，論文點名 AudioTime 跟時序音訊問答的既有工作，但指出它們鎖定的是環境音事件或粗粒度對齊，不是音樂。

音樂專用的音頻語言模型方面，論文點名 Audio Flamingo 系列（1–3 代）、MusiLingo、Music Understanding LLaMA、MuMu-LLaMA、LLARK，以及專門評測音樂理解的基準 MuChoMusic——MuChoMusic 已經指出「現有音樂導向的音頻語言模型在感知與概念層級的理解上仍然有限」，但它評的一樣是最終答案對不對，不是答案有沒有對應到正確的時間區段。這篇論文跟以上所有工作的差異在於：明確要求模型輸出時間區間、而不只是答對最終問題，並藉此打造出第一個針對音樂的受控定位基準。

Prior grounding systems mostly come from vision and video; the paper names concrete methods such as TALL, Proposal-Free Video Grounding, UniVTG, and SlowFocus, plus grounding-capable multimodal LLMs like BLIP-2. On audio grounding it names AudioTime and prior temporal audio QA work, but notes these target environmental sound events or coarse alignment, not music.

On music-specific audio-language models it names the Audio Flamingo series (1-3), MusiLingo, Music Understanding LLaMA, MuMu-LLaMA, and LLARK, plus the MuChoMusic benchmark for evaluating music understanding -- MuChoMusic already showed that "current music-oriented audio-language models remain limited in perceptual and concept-level understanding," but it still only evaluates whether the final answer is correct, not whether that answer maps to the right time span. This paper's difference from all of the above is requiring models to output actual time spans rather than just a correct final answer, building the first controlled grounding benchmark specifically for music.

方法Method

任務定義是：給模型一段音訊跟一個自然語言查詢，模型要回傳對應到查詢音樂事件或型態的時間區間。MusicGroundingBench 分成兩個子集。MGBench-3N 是音符級定位：用 PianoTeq 9 Stage 把 MIDI 合成成最多三個連續音符的單聲部鋼琴片段，涵蓋全部 88 個琴鍵，訓練/驗證/測試各 4200/900/900 段，查詢分成絕對音高、相對比較、序數指涉、型態關係四類，共 102900 組問答對，並刻意維持 15% 的空答案比例。

MGBench-2B 是結構化定位與理解：用演算法生成、具備精確符號-音訊對齊的單聲部兩小節片段（訓練/驗證/測試 9007/1128/1124 段），控制拍號、速度、調性、音域、和聲內容、重複型態等變數，定位問答對 63240 組（涵蓋節奏、調性、音程、小節比較、和弦、重複、和聲型態七類），另有 82204 組理解問答對。比較對象包含用 MERT 音訊特徵搭配 FiLM/XAttn 的傳統條件機制、外部音頻 LLM 基線 Music Flamingo 與 Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct，以及論文自製、用 LoRA 微調且同樣接上 MERT 特徵的 LLaMA-3-8B 與 Qwen3.5-9B-Base。

The task is defined as: given an audio clip and a natural-language query, the model returns a time span corresponding to the queried musical event or pattern. MusicGroundingBench has two subsets. MGBench-3N is note-level grounding: monophonic piano clips of up to three sequential notes, synthesized from MIDI via PianoTeq 9 Stage across all 88 keys, split 4,200/900/900 for train/val/test, with queries in four families (absolute pitch, relative comparison, ordinal reference, pattern relation) totaling 102,900 QA pairs, deliberately keeping a 15% empty-answer ratio.

MGBench-2B is structured grounding and understanding: algorithmically generated monophonic two-bar excerpts with exact symbolic-to-audio alignment (9,007/1,128/1,124 for train/val/test), controlling meter, tempo, tonality, register, harmonic content, and repetition type, with 63,240 grounding QA pairs across seven categories (rhythm, tonality, interval, bar comparison, chord, repetition, harmonic pattern) plus 82,204 understanding QA pairs. Comparisons include classic conditioning mechanisms (FiLM, XAttn) on MERT audio features, external audio-LLM baselines Music Flamingo and Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct, and the paper's own LoRA-tuned LLaMA-3-8B and Qwen3.5-9B-Base, both also built on MERT features.

限制Limitations

基準完全來自演算法生成的合成 MIDI 音訊，不是真實錄音，論文自己承認「這不是要解決音樂定位問題的完整方案，而是一個受控的第一步」。MGBench-3N 的音樂內容刻意稀疏、幾乎沒有樂句結構；所有音訊都是單聲部鋼琴，不涵蓋複音音樂、管弦編制或不同樂器；就連 MGBench-2B 也只涵蓋兩小節的片段，不是完整曲式或長篇結構。

理解類問題的正確性評分是二元的（對或錯），可能掩蓋部分理解的情況；而且理解評測完全依賴單一個 LLM（Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct）當裁判，可能帶入系統性偏誤。

The benchmark relies entirely on algorithmically generated synthetic MIDI audio rather than real recordings, and the authors acknowledge "this design is not intended as a comprehensive solution to music grounding, but as a carefully controlled first step." MGBench-3N's musical content is sparse and tightly controlled, with minimal phrase structure; all audio is strictly monophonic piano, not representative of polyphonic music, orchestration, or diverse instruments; and even MGBench-2B covers only two-bar excerpts, not full compositions or longer-form structure.

Correctness scoring for understanding questions is binary (right or wrong), which can mask partial understanding, and the understanding evaluation relies entirely on a single LLM judge (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), which may introduce systematic bias.

討論Discussion

零樣本下的兩個現成模型幾乎定位失敗：Music Flamingo 在 MGBench-3N 上音符級 IoU≥0.5 只有 0.0842、F1 僅 0.0342，Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct 更是幾乎掛零（IoU≥0.5 = 0.0006，F1 = 0.0000）；在 MGBench-2B 上兩者也只有 0.1118 與 0.0647。

相對地，經過任務專屬微調的自製模型表現明顯不同：LLaMA-3-8B 在 MGBench-3N 拿到 IoU≥0.5 = 0.5796、F1 = 0.7569，起訖點的平均絕對誤差只有 17.93/51.87 毫秒；Qwen3.5-9B-Base 在結構更複雜的 MGBench-2B 上更進一步衝到 IoU≥0.5 = 0.8107、F1 = 0.9221，起訖誤差壓到 9.34/13.28 毫秒。這說明現成音頻語言模型的失敗主要是訓練資料沒教過這個任務，不是任務本身做不到。

定位訓練跟理解表現之間的關係則因模型而異、沒有一致方向：對 LLaMA-3-8B，加入定位資料並不會提升整體理解準確率（反而從 0.6726 微降到 0.6646）；但對 Qwen3.5-9B-Base，混合定位資料反而讓整體準確率從 0.6512 進步到 0.6868。論文因此把這個基準定位成「用來檢驗音頻語言模型是否真的把回答釘在時間證據上的受控測試場」，並承諾釋出資料集、程式碼與模型。

The two off-the-shelf models nearly fail at zero-shot grounding: Music Flamingo reaches only IoU>=0.5 = 0.0842 and F1 = 0.0342 on MGBench-3N, while Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct is essentially at zero (IoU>=0.5 = 0.0006, F1 = 0.0000); on MGBench-2B both remain low at 0.1118 and 0.0647. In contrast, task-specifically fine-tuned custom models perform very differently: LLaMA-3-8B reaches IoU>=0.5 = 0.5796 and F1 = 0.7569 on MGBench-3N, with onset/offset mean absolute error of just 17.93/51.87 ms; Qwen3.5-9B-Base goes further on the more complex MGBench-2B, reaching IoU>=0.5 = 0.8107 and F1 = 0.9221, with onset/offset error down to 9.34/13.28 ms. This suggests the off-the-shelf models' failure is mainly about training data never covering this task, not the task being unsolvable.

The relationship between grounding training and understanding performance is model-dependent with no consistent direction: for LLaMA-3-8B, adding grounding data doesn't improve overall understanding accuracy (it slightly drops from 0.6726 to 0.6646), but for Qwen3.5-9B-Base, mixing in grounding data raises overall accuracy from 0.6512 to 0.6868. The paper positions this benchmark as "a controlled testbed for assessing whether audio-language models ground their responses in temporally localized musical evidence," and commits to releasing the datasets, code, and models.
