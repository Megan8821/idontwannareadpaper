On a Separate Note: Robust Score-Informed Note Separation with a Two-Stream TFC-TDF U-Net and Adaptive Set Ownership

On a Separate Note:以雙串流 TFC-TDF U-Net 與 Adaptive Set Ownership 做穩健的樂譜引導音符分離

Benjamin Shiue-Hal Chou, Purvish Jajal, Nicholas John Eliopoulos, James C. Davis, George K. Thiruvathukal, Kristen Yeon-Ji Yun, Hao-Wen Dong, Yung-Hsiang Lu
arXiv:2609.29071 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-24
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：NoteSep 是(就作者所知)第一個以深度學習做「逐音符」而非「逐樂器聲部」分離的 score-informed 系統,在 SCNS-Eval 上達到 7.39 dB 的中位數 SI-SDR,相較於 score-informed NMF 的 2.49 dB 與 Melodyne(僅比對得上的音符)的 -3.88 dB;光是 Adaptive Set Ownership 這個聯合分離階段,就把 SI-SDR 從 5.09 dB(NoteGrab Selective)推進到 7.39 dB,同時把八度混淆率從 5.19% 壓到 2.96%。Why this one: NoteSep is, to the authors' knowledge, the first deep-learning system for score-informed note-level (rather than instrument-stem) separation, reaching a median SI-SDR of 7.39 dB on SCNS-Eval versus 2.49 dB for score-informed NMF and -3.88 dB for Melodyne on matched notes only; the Adaptive Set Ownership joint-separation stage alone lifts SI-SDR from 5.09 dB (NoteGrab Selective) to 7.39 dB while cutting octave confusion from 5.19% to 2.96%.

動機Motivation

Score-informed 音符分離的目標,是從複音混音中抽出「某一個被彈奏出的音符」本身的波形,這對修正一次演出中彈錯的音、把某條旋律線抽給學生單獨聽,或凸顯某個聲部方便分析都很有用。現有的音樂音源分離系統,包括建立在 CLAP 之類音訊—文字嵌入上的通用分離器,運作的顆粒度都停在「樂器」或「文字提示」層級──能抽出「那把小提琴」,卻抽不出「小提琴在第 12 小節彈的第三個音」,因為同一樂器的不同音符音色相近,語意嵌入幾乎無法區分它們。八度關係與完全五度的音符又共享大部分泛音列,使問題更嚴重;非諧波成分、揉音、空間殘響與打擊類起音更進一步增加分離難度。

既有那些真正用上對齊樂譜的 score-informed 系統,分離的顆粒度也只到「樂器」或「合唱聲部」(例如男高音與男低音)為止,從未到單一被記譜的音符。作者提出的問題是:一個直接以音高、起音與結束時間為條件的網路,能不能單獨分離出那一個音符;再進一步,一個同時看整段時間窗內所有現存音符的第二階段,能不能修正單音符抽取器各自留下的錯誤。

Score-informed note separation aims to extract the waveform of one specific performed note out of a polyphonic mixture, useful for fixing a wrong take, isolating a line for a student, or highlighting a voice for analysis. Existing music source separation systems, including semantic-embedding-driven universal separators built on CLAP, work at the instrument or text-prompt level: they can pull out "the violin" but not "the third note the violin plays in bar 12," because notes from the same instrument share timbre and semantic embeddings carry little information to tell them apart. Octave-related notes and perfect fifths compound the problem by sharing most of their harmonic partials, while inharmonicity, vibrato, room acoustics, and percussive attacks make separation harder still.

Prior score-informed systems that do use aligned notation stop at the level of an instrument or a choir section (tenor vs. bass), never an individual scored note. The authors ask whether a network conditioned directly on a note's pitch, onset, and offset can separate that note on its own, and whether a second stage that looks at every note active in a time window at once can correct the errors any single-note extractor leaves behind.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

過去的音符分離工作主要靠諧波梳狀濾波、非負矩陣分解(NMF)或 score-informed 模板分解;其中最強的基線是 score-informed NMF,它在樂譜引導下把混音拆成音符級的頻譜模板,但受限於「諧波梳」這個假設,在八度、非諧波泛音與寬頻起音上表現會大幅下滑。商用工具 Melodyne 走的是另一條路:它完全不使用樂譜,只在混音裡直接聚類偵測到的成分,結果只有 54.42% 的記譜音符能以正確音高在 150 毫秒內對應上,而就算是對應上的那些音符,中位數 SI-SDR 也只有 -3.88 dB。

在樂器層級,Miron 等人的 score-informed CNN 與 score-informed 版本的 X-UMX 都用了對齊樂譜來分離管弦樂各聲部,但輸出永遠是一個樂器或聲部一條波形,從不到單一音符;而且 Miron 的 CNN 是在其測試樂譜的合成演出版本上訓練的,等於考的就是練過的題目。NoteSep 真正的創新之處,其實是把兩個各自已經存在的想法組合起來:像上述樂器層級 score-informed 系統那樣、以樂譜為條件逐一抽取單一目標,再加上把所有同時重疊的估計值放在一起比較、彼此協調。這種「讓多個同時發生的抽取結果互相比對以解決能量歸屬爭議」的做法,概念上很接近一般聯合分離頭在調解同一份能量的競爭宣稱時所做的事,只是這裡套用在遠比一個樂器聲部更稀疏、也更高解析度的單一音符目標上。

Prior note-separation work relied mainly on harmonic comb filtering, non-negative matrix factorization (NMF), or score-informed template decomposition; the strongest of these baselines is score-informed NMF, which decomposes a mixture into note-level spectral templates under score guidance, but its harmonic-comb assumption degrades sharply on octaves, inharmonic partials, and broadband attacks. The commercial tool Melodyne takes a different route: it clusters components detected directly in the mixture with no access to the score at all, matching only 54.42% of scored notes to the correct pitch within 150 ms, and scoring a median SI-SDR of only -3.88 dB even on the notes it does match.

At the instrument level, Miron et al.'s score-informed CNN and a score-informed variant of X-UMX use aligned notation to separate orchestral parts, but they output one waveform per instrument or voice, never per note, and Miron's CNN was trained on synthetic renditions of the very scores it is tested on. NoteSep's real claim to novelty is combining two ideas that already exist separately: score-conditioned extraction of one target at a time, as in the instrument-level systems above, plus a set-level reconciliation across concurrently overlapping estimates. Reconciling multiple simultaneous extractions by comparing them against each other is conceptually close to how a general joint-separation head resolves competing claims on shared energy, just applied to the sparser and far higher-resolution target of a single note rather than an instrument stem.

方法Method

逐音符抽取器 NoteGrab 取一段以被查詢音符起音與結束時間為中心的混音片段,先用諧波—打擊分離(HPSS)拆成諧波與打擊兩個分量,再分別餵進各自的 27.4M 參數 TFC-TDF v3 U-Net(該模型曾在 2023 Sound Demixing Challenge 的 Leaderboard B 拿下第一)。三組雙向交叉注意力模組讓兩條串流在最深的編碼器層、瓶頸層與最早的解碼器層互相交換資訊。一個選擇性閘門只有在樂譜顯示低一或兩個八度處有重疊音符時,才會把諧波分量裡低於目標基頻的部分歸零,打擊分量與未觸發閘門的情況則維持原樣;這個「選擇性閘控」版本比「無閘控」版本 SI-SDR 更高、八度混淆也更低,證實動機那節提到的八度碰撞問題確實存在,而且是可以被處理的。

逐一抽取音符仍然讓每個估計值互相看不到彼此,因此第二階段 Adaptive Set Ownership(ASO)會一次處理 6 秒音訊,以及該時間窗內所有同時存在的音符。它為每個音符建立九張特徵圖(混音幅度、該估計值佔全體估計總和的比例、相位相似度、與最近諧波的距離、起音/結束時間差,以及其中一張學習出來的能量閘門),把每個音符的脈絡拿去跟同時發生的其他音符的平均值和最大值比較,再預測一個對數增益,用來在所有重疊音符之間重新分配共享的混音能量;只有在所有音符的權重都塌陷成零時才退回使用原始幅度。因為兩條串流是共享權重且可批次運算的,分離一個含 K 個音符的時間窗只需要 K 次 NoteGrab 加上固定成本的一次 ASO,所以這個額外的聯合階段不會隨 K 線性增加成本。

NoteGrab, the per-note extractor, takes a segment centered on the queried note's onset and offset, splits it via harmonic-percussive source separation (HPSS) into harmonic and percussive components, and feeds each into its own 27.4M-parameter TFC-TDF v3 U-Net, the model that placed first on Leaderboard B of the 2023 Sound Demixing Challenge. Three bidirectional cross-attention modules let the two streams exchange information at the deepest encoder stage, the bottleneck, and the first decoder stage. A selective gate zeroes out harmonic content below the target's fundamental only when the score shows an overlapping note one or two octaves lower, leaving the percussive stream and the ungated case untouched; this selective-gating variant raises SI-SDR over the ungated version and lowers octave confusion, confirming that the octave-collision problem raised in the motivation is real and addressable.

Extracting notes one at a time still leaves each estimate blind to the others, so the second stage, Adaptive Set Ownership (ASO), processes six seconds of audio and every note active in that window together. For each note it builds nine feature maps -- mixture magnitude, the estimate's share of the summed estimates, phase similarity, distance to the nearest harmonic, onset/offset distance, and a learned energy gate among them -- compares each note's context against the mean and max of its concurrent neighbors, and predicts a log-gain that reallocates the shared mixture energy among all overlapping notes, falling back to raw magnitudes only when every note's weight collapses to zero. Because the streams are shared and batchable, separating a K-note window costs K NoteGrab passes plus one constant-cost ASO pass, so the added joint stage does not scale with K.

限制Limitations

NoteSep 需要每個音符都有正確音高、起音與結束時間的對齊樂譜;上游對齊步驟只要出錯,就會直接決定分離出來的是哪一個音符,而論文並未評估對齊本身出錯時系統會怎麼樣。訓練與評估都使用由符號資料集與獨立音符樣本庫(NSynth、TinySOL)合成出來的混音,雖然 NoteSep 確實有轉移到真實合奏錄音(PHENICX-Anechoic 與 Bach10)上,但在 SI-SDRi 上仍略遜於針對該任務專門訓練的 Miron score-informed CNN(PHENICX 上 8.45 dB 對 10.73 dB;論文只主張贏過 NoteGrab,並未主張贏過這個任務專屬模型)。

此外,ASO 只在固定 6 秒的時間窗內比對同時發生的音符,對於起音或結束時間落在窗外的競爭音符造成的干擾就無法修正;而且額外跑一次 ASO,只有在整段樂句的音符都要一次全部抽取時才划算,單獨抽取一個音符時並不值得。

NoteSep requires an aligned score with correct pitch, onset, and offset for every note, so any error in the upstream alignment step propagates directly into which note gets extracted, and the paper does not evaluate what happens when the aligned score itself is wrong. Training and evaluation both use synthetic mixtures rendered from symbolic datasets and isolated-note libraries (NSynth, TinySOL); NoteSep does transfer to real ensemble recordings on PHENICX-Anechoic and Bach10, but there it remains behind the task-specific Miron score-informed CNN in SI-SDRi (8.45 dB vs. 10.73 dB on PHENICX -- the paper only claims to beat NoteGrab there, not this task-specific model). Because ASO only reconciles notes concurrent within a fixed six-second window, it cannot correct interference from a competing note whose onset or offset falls outside that window, and the extra ASO pass is only worthwhile when every note in a passage is being extracted at once, not when a single note is pulled out in isolation.

討論Discussion

中位數 SI-SDR 從 score-informed NMF 的 2.49 dB 升到 NoteSep 的 7.39 dB,是論文回報的最大差距,消融實驗也支持這個說法:把 ASO 從完整流程中拿掉(只剩 NoteGrab Selective)會讓 SI-SDR 從 7.39 dB 掉到 5.09 dB,八度混淆率也大致翻倍,從 2.96% 升到 5.19%,說明真正扛起大部分效果的是「聯合協調同時發生的估計值」,而不是單音符抽取器本身。在編輯任務上,讓 NoteSep 對音符做變調、變時或平衡調整並重新混音後,2f 感知品質分數為 71.08,相較於沒有 ASO 的 NoteGrab 只有 32.38,再一次證實提升主要來自聯合階段,而不是抽取器架構上的小修小補。

NoteSep 唯一沒有領先的場合,是對上專門在同一封閉資料集上訓練與評估的任務專屬基線(Bach10 上的 Miron CNN);這是一個只在合成資料上訓練一次、就直接套用到未見過的真實錄音上的系統可預期的代價,而不是「逐音符」這個問題設定本身的弱點。

The headline result -- median SI-SDR rising from 2.49 dB (score-informed NMF) to 7.39 dB (NoteSep) -- is the largest reported gap in the paper, and the ablations back it: removing ASO from the full pipeline (NoteGrab Selective alone) drops SI-SDR from 7.39 dB to 5.09 dB and roughly doubles octave confusion from 2.96% to 5.19%, so joint reconciliation of concurrent estimates does most of the work, not the per-note extractor alone.

On the editing task, letting NoteSep transform pitch, timing, or balance and remix the result scores a 2f perceptual-quality value of 71.08 versus 32.38 for NoteGrab without ASO, again isolating the joint stage as the source of most of the improvement rather than incremental tweaks to the extractor. The one place NoteSep does not lead is against task-specific baselines trained and evaluated on the same closed dataset (Miron's CNN on Bach10), which is an expected trade-off for a system trained once on synthetic data and applied unseen to real recordings, not a weakness specific to the note-level formulation itself.

Statistical Models for Automatic Fingering-Annotated Piano Sheet Music Transcription

以統計模型做自動指法標註的鋼琴樂譜轉譜

Daniel Penner, Abram Hindle
arXiv:2609.28787 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-23
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：作者提出的 8 個手部與指法標註模型中,表現最好的 Synthesis 模型其實是把既有的雙向 Kalman 濾波分手法與 Nakamura 的一階 HMM 指法模型直接組合起來,在 PIG 資料集上達到 90.8% 分手準確率與 56.6% 手指+分手聯合準確率,勝過論文自己提出、真正新穎的 3-gram-R 語言模型方法(90.4% / 50.7%);換言之,論文標題強調的「新方法」裡跑最快也最準的那一個,骨子裡是既有兩個系統的重組。Why this one: Of the paper's 8 new hand-and-fingering models, the best performer, Synthesis, is a direct combination of an existing bidirectional Kalman-filter hand tracker and Nakamura's first-order HMM fingering model, reaching 90.8% hand-separation and 56.6% joint hand+finger accuracy on the PIG dataset -- beating the paper's own genuinely novel 3-gram-R language-model approach (90.4% / 50.7%); the fastest and most accurate "new method" in the title is, underneath, a recombination of two prior systems.

動機Motivation

自動音樂轉譜的目的是讓沒有樂譜的樂曲也能被彈奏、讓沒受過樂理訓練的作曲者能從錄音生出樂譜。但光有音符的音高與時值還不夠:要印出真正可用的鋼琴樂譜,音符必須被分派到左右手兩個譜表,而好的鋼琴樂譜通常還帶有指法標註,讓演奏者在視奏或練習高難度樂段時知道該用哪根手指。要從音訊完全自動推導出音符、手與指法這三件事,需要三個步驟:先辨識每個時刻在彈的所有音符(音高、起音、時值、結束時間),再把每個音符分派到最合適的手,最後在考量手部從前一個音符移動到下一個音符的前提下,把每個音符分派到某隻手上的某根手指。

現有的自動轉譜方法能高準確度辨識彈奏的音符,但只轉出單一 MIDI 檔或單一譜表,不會自動產生可彈奏的指法;雖然轉譜、分手、指法各自都有人研究,卻沒有既有工作把三者整合成一個端到端的工具。作者的目標正是自動產生可彈奏的手部與指法標註,並把這套方法整合進一個從鋼琴音訊到指法標註樂譜的完整流程。

Automatic music transcription aims to make music accessible where no sheet music exists and to let composers without formal training generate sheet music from a recording alone. But pitch and timing are not enough: to print usable piano sheet music, notes must be split across the two staves for each hand, and good sheet music often carries fingering annotations that guide a player through sight-reading or fast, complex passages. Deriving note, hand, and fingering information from audio end to end needs three steps: identify every note being played (pitch, onset, duration, offset), assign each note to the optimal hand, and finally assign each note to a specific finger on that hand while accounting for the hand's transitions between consecutive notes.

Existing automatic transcription methods identify played notes with high accuracy, but they transcribe only to a single MIDI file or a single stave and do not attempt to generate playable fingerings; although note transcription, hand separation, and fingering are each individually studied, no existing work combines all three into an end-to-end tool. The authors' goal is to automatically generate playable hand and fingering annotations and integrate the approach into a complete pipeline from piano audio to fingering-annotated sheet music.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

手部分派方面,Hadjakos 等人先提出以 Kalman 濾波器即時追蹤演奏者手部位置的方法:把每個音符分給某隻手,再把該手的預期位置拉向那個音或和弦,音符間隔越久則不確定性越大;後續版本把這個 Kalman 濾波器改成雙向版本,準確率因此提升。指法方面,Nakamura 等人提出 PIG 資料集(150 首古典鋼琴曲、309 次演出的指法標註),並在其上比較了四種 HMM 變體與兩種深度神經網路(前饋網路與 LSTM),結果標準 HMM 就贏過兩個深度神經網路,高階 HMM 表現又更好;而唯一同時處理手與指法的既有方法,是 Nakamura 等人提出的 merged-output HMM,用兩個各代表一隻手的子 HMM 合併輸出。

本文最好的模型 Synthesis,做法就是把上述兩個各自獨立的既有系統直接拼在一起:先用雙向 Kalman 濾波器分手,再把兩手各自的音符序列丟進 Nakamura 那款一階 HMM 指法模型。換句話說,論文標題裡「新的統計方法」中最強的那一個,骨子裡不是新架構,而是把既有的分手技巧與既有的指法技巧組合成階層式管線;真正算是新架構的,反而是論文另外提出的三個 N-gram 語言模型方法(3-gram-M、3-gram-T、3-gram-R),把手指與指法序列當成語言模型的 token 來預測。

On hand assignment, Hadjakos et al. first proposed a Kalman-filter approach that tracks a pianist's hand position in real time from note events alone: each note is assigned to a hand, and that hand's expected position is then pulled toward the note or chord, with uncertainty growing over longer gaps between transitions; a later version made this filter bidirectional and improved accuracy further. On fingering, Nakamura et al. introduced the PIG dataset (150 classical pieces, 309 performances with ground-truth fingering) and compared four HMM variants against two deep neural networks (a feed-forward network and an LSTM) on it, finding that a standard HMM already beat both neural networks and that higher-order HMMs did better still; the only prior method that handles hand and fingering jointly is Nakamura et al.'s merged-output HMM, which combines two per-hand sub-HMMs.

This paper's best-performing model, Synthesis, is exactly a direct combination of those two pre-existing systems: a bidirectional Kalman filter for hand separation feeding each hand's note stream into Nakamura's first-order HMM fingering model. In other words, the strongest of the paper's "new statistical approaches" is not a new architecture at all, but a hierarchical pipeline stitched from an existing hand-tracking technique and an existing fingering technique; the genuinely new architectures are instead the paper's three N-gram language-model approaches (3-gram-M, 3-gram-T, 3-gram-R), which treat hand-and-finger sequences as tokens for a language model to predict.

方法Method

論文提出 8 個手部與指法標註模型:兩個以一階 HMM 為基礎的基線(Baseline-M 用單一 HMM 同時決定手與指的組合狀態;Baseline-S 先用二元 HMM 分手,再各自用一個 HMM 決定指法)、前述的 Synthesis 組合模型、兩個新增代價函數的規則式擴充(Novel-V 用 Viterbi 解碼,考慮手指交叉、和弦內指距等規則;Novel-B 改用 beam search,能追蹤兩隻手個別的最後狀態,代價矩陣改用理想距離「區間」而非單一值),以及三個把指法標註視為語言模型問題的 N-gram 方法(3-gram-M 只用單一 trigram;3-gram-T 把手部序列、左右手各自的指法序列共四個 trigram 模型在對數空間相加;3-gram-R 在 3-gram-T 上再疊加手指不可交叉、同時音高由高指演奏兩條規則)。

為了完整評估從音訊到指法標註樂譜的端到端流程,論文另外組了一條轉譜管線:先用 Kong 等人的高解析度鋼琴轉譜模型把音訊轉成 MIDI,交給挑選的標註模型分派手與指法,再用 PM2S 把演奏 MIDI 轉成節奏量化過的樂譜 MIDI(PM2S 在 MV2H 各項子指標上都優於 MuseScore 的內建量化),接著用自己的手部估計覆蓋 PM2S 的分手結果、把速度統一為單一數值,最後用 MuseScore 轉成 MusicXML 並把指法標註對應回樂譜音符上。

The paper proposes 8 hand-and-fingering models: two first-order-HMM baselines (Baseline-M uses a single HMM over the combined hand-finger state; Baseline-S first separates hands with a binary HMM, then runs a separate HMM per hand for fingering), the Synthesis combination described above, two rule-based extensions with added cost parameters (Novel-V decodes with Viterbi and penalizes finger crossings and chord-span violations; Novel-B decodes with beam search instead, tracking each hand's own last state, with ideal-distance matrices expressed as ranges rather than single values), and three N-gram approaches that treat annotation as a language-modeling problem (3-gram-M uses a single trigram; 3-gram-T sums four trigram models -- for the hand sequence and for each hand's fingering separately -- in log space; 3-gram-R adds two hand-crafted rules against finger crossing and pitch-finger ordering on top of 3-gram-T).

To evaluate a complete audio-to-annotated-score pipeline, the paper assembles: Kong et al.'s high-resolution piano transcription model to turn audio into MIDI, the chosen annotation model to assign hands and fingers, PM2S to convert the performance MIDI into a rhythm-quantized score MIDI (PM2S outperforms MuseScore's built-in quantization on every MV2H sub-metric), the paper's own hand estimates overwriting PM2S's hand separation and the tempo normalized to a single value, and finally MuseScore to render MusicXML with the fingering annotations mapped back onto score notes.

限制Limitations

評估靠的是跟真實演出的指法標註比對,但同一段音樂往往不只一種可彈的指法,這代表評估分數可能誤判某些其實可彈的估計為錯誤。每個模型都只在古典鋼琴演出上訓練,而且純粹是統計方法,能否推廣到其他曲風仍是未知數。端到端評估用的是合成鋼琴音訊(因為 PianoVAM 資料集截至 2026 年 8 月尚未公開指法標籤,只能改用 MuseScore 4 內建 WAV MIDI 播放器合成 10 首評估曲),但轉譜模型本身是在真實演奏錄音上訓練的,這個落差可能會壓低送進標註模型的轉譜品質,也不反映管線在真實情境下的實際表現。

Evaluation relies on matching against real-performance fingering labels, but a given passage often has more than one playable fingering, so the metric can penalize an estimate that is actually playable but differs from the recorded one. Every model is trained exclusively on classical piano performances and is purely statistical, so generalization to other genres or styles remains untested. The end-to-end evaluation uses synthesized piano audio -- the PianoVAM dataset's fingering labels were not yet public as of August 2026, so the authors instead synthesized 10 PIG evaluation pieces with MuseScore 4's built-in WAV MIDI player -- while the transcription model itself was trained on real performance recordings, a mismatch that could lower the transcription quality fed to the annotation models and does not reflect the pipeline's behavior under real conditions.

討論Discussion

在只評估標註模型本身時,Synthesis 拿下最高分(手 90.8%、聯合 56.6%),而且執行時間最短;論文自己新提出、真正屬於新架構的 3-gram-R 緊追在後(90.4% / 50.7%),兩者的手部準確率差距經自助法(bootstrap)檢定只有 0.4 個百分點且不顯著。作為對照,唯一既有的聯合方法 Nakamura-Merged 分手準確率只有 67.4%,是所有模型裡最差,執行時間卻是 Synthesis 的 179 倍(498.02 秒 對 2.78 秒 的幾何平均)。

而在端到端管線的可讀性測試裡,作者比較 Synthesis 與 3-gram-R 對蕭邦 A 小調圓舞曲 B.150 這段訓練集之外樂句的標註,Synthesis 標出 9 處「同指連續彈奏」「手指不可交叉的交叉」或「手部誤判」等問題,3-gram-R 則完全沒有這三類錯誤,顯示準確率數字上的些微差距,在實際可彈奏性的質性檢查上其實有明顯落差。

把整條管線拿去跟唯一可比較的既有音訊到樂譜系統 Audio-to-Score 比,對方在轉譜準確率(90.6% 對 89.3%)與分手準確率(90.3% 對 87.7%/88.8%)上都略勝一籌,但因為對方完整程式碼未公開,比較範圍侷限在 MAPS 資料集裡少數已釋出結果的曲目,而且 Audio-to-Score 完全不做指法標註、也非完全開源。因此論文的貢獻更準確地說,是把「分手、指法、轉譜」三者第一次串成一條可公開重現的完整管線,而不是在單一子任務上刷新最佳準確率。

When evaluating annotation models alone, Synthesis scores highest (90.8% hand, 56.6% joint) and has the lowest runtime; the paper's own genuinely new architecture, 3-gram-R, follows close behind (90.4% / 50.7%), with the hand-accuracy gap between them only 0.4 percentage points and not statistically significant under the paired bootstrap test. For contrast, the only prior joint method, Nakamura-Merged, has the worst hand-separation accuracy of any model (67.4%) yet runs 179 times slower than Synthesis by geometric-mean runtime (498.02 s vs. 2.78 s); in a qualitative end-to-end test on the first line of Chopin's Waltz in A Minor B.150 (outside the training set), Synthesis's output shows 9 instances of same-finger repetition, unplayable finger crossings, or wrong hand labels, while 3-gram-R's output for the same passage has none of these three error types -- so a small gap in the accuracy numbers hides a visible gap in actual playability under closer inspection.

Against the only comparable prior audio-to-score system, Audio-to-Score, that system edges out this pipeline on transcription accuracy (90.6% vs. 89.3%) and hand-assignment accuracy (90.3% vs. 87.7%/88.8%), but because its full code was never released, the comparison is limited to the small subset of MAPS pieces it published outputs for, and Audio-to-Score neither produces fingering annotations nor is fully open-source. The paper's contribution is therefore more accurately described as the first publicly reproducible pipeline chaining hand separation, fingering, and transcription together end to end, rather than a new state of the art on any single sub-task.

動機Motivation

修復老唱片(例如蟲膠唱片)這類歷史錄音,正好落在訊號處理與典藏保存的交界上:這些錄音同時帶有錄音設備造成的頻寬限制、寬頻底噪、脈衝式雜訊,以及製造與播放媒介、歲月耗損留下的其他劣化。神經網路式的增強模型解決能力很強,但通常的做法是把它識別出來的雜訊全部移除,而不是讓使用者自己決定要介入到什麼程度。問題是「什麼才算修復好的音訊」本身就是主觀判斷──完全去除所有噪聲聽起來反而可能不真實、不自然,不如保留一部分。

為了填補這個落差,近年的研究開始探索生成式增強與盲頻寬延展模型,但針對歷史音樂的既有框架(U-Net、GAN、擴散模型)大多採用「先分離、再去噪、再延展」的串接式管線。這種串接設計容易讓早期分離階段的錯誤一路傳遞下去,累積成幻覺人聲之類的問題,擴散模型的高運算成本也讓即時使用變得不可行,而且在獨唱與鋼琴錄音上訓練的模型往往難以推廣到複雜的多聲部混音。作者想做的是把「高品質處理」與「即時可調控」這兩件事合在一起。

Restoring historic audio such as shellac discs sits at the intersection of signal processing and preservation: these recordings carry heavy band-limiting, broadband hiss, impulsive transients, and other degradations imparted by the recording equipment, the manufacturing process, the playback medium, and age-related wear. Neural enhancement models are powerful, but they typically remove all the noise they can identify rather than letting a practitioner decide how much intervention to apply. The trouble is that what counts as a "restored" signal is itself subjective -- complete noise removal can sound inauthentic and unnatural compared to a less complete one.

To close this gap, recent research explores generative enhancement and blind bandwidth extension, but existing frameworks targeting historical music (U-Nets, GANs, diffusion models) mostly rely on cascaded pipelines that separate, denoise, and extend in sequence. These cascaded designs let early separation mistakes propagate forward, compounding into problems like hallucinated vocal content; diffusion's high computational cost also rules out real-time use, and models trained on solo singing and piano recordings struggle to generalize to complex multi-voice mixtures. The authors set out to combine high-quality processing with real-time, steerable control.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的既有比較對象是 BABE-2,一個以擴散模型做生成式等化的音樂修復方法;但 BABE-2 本身只負責延展頻寬與音色,不處理去噪,作者只好在它前面接上一個兩階段 U-Net 去噪器,對人聲錄音再多接一層 HT-Demucs 做人聲分離,才能組成完整的修復管線——這正是本文想避免的「串接式」設計。另一個對照組是商用工具 iZotope RX 12,代表音訊工程師常用的多階段 DSP 流程(去爆音、去劈啪聲、去嗡聲、頻譜去噪、頻譜復原五道手續依序執行)。BEHM-GAN 則是另一支專門做歷史音樂頻寬延展的 GAN 方法,同樣是獨立於分離之外的另一個階段。

RESTORE 的核心做法,是直接擴充一個既有的音樂音源分離骨幹網路 Hybrid Transformer Demucs(HTDemucs)──把它原本用來分離鼓、貝斯、人聲、其他四個乾淨分軌的輸出頭,換成六個語意分軌(人聲、音樂、寬頻噪聲、瞬態噪聲、殘差,加上一個獨立生成的高頻延展分軌),讓分離與生成在同一次前向傳遞裡完成,藉此避免串接管線裡錯誤一路向下傳遞的問題。這個「把分離骨幹直接改造成語意分解器」的做法,概念上與既有串接管線裡『先分離、再處理』的想法一致,差別在於把所有步驟壓進單一網路,並且用一個混音一致性懲罰項強迫五個乾淨分軌的和必須等於輸入混音,讓劣化只能被算進雜訊分軌而不是被悄悄丟掉。

The most direct prior comparison is BABE-2, a diffusion-based generative equalizer for music restoration; but BABE-2 only handles bandwidth and coloration, not denoising, so its authors deploy it behind a two-stage U-Net denoiser and, for vocal recordings, an additional HT-Demucs separation step -- exactly the cascaded design this paper sets out to avoid. Another baseline is the commercial tool iZotope RX 12, representing the multi-pass DSP workflow audio engineers commonly use (de-click, de-crackle, de-hum, spectral de-noise, and spectral recovery run in sequence). BEHM-GAN is a separate GAN-based approach specifically for historical-music bandwidth extension, again a stage kept independent of separation.

RESTORE's core move is to directly repurpose an existing music source separation backbone, Hybrid Transformer Demucs (HTDemucs): its original four clean output heads (drums, bass, vocals, other) are replaced with six semantic stems (vocals, music, broadband hiss, transients, residual, plus an independently synthesized high-frequency-extension stem), so separation and generation happen in one forward pass instead of a cascade. Turning a separation backbone directly into a semantic decomposer is conceptually the same idea as the "separate, then process" logic behind the cascaded pipelines above, just collapsed into a single network, with a mixture-consistency penalty forcing the five clean stems to sum back to the input mixture so degradation is accounted for in the noise stems rather than quietly discarded.

方法Method

RESTORE 沿用 HTDemucs 的雙域(時域+頻域)雙 U-Net 架構,瓶頸處是一個跨域 Transformer 編碼器,同時在各自域內做自注意力、在兩域之間做交叉注意力。作者把輸出頭從原本的四個換成六個,新分軌的初始化直接沿用預訓練權重裡最相近的既有分軌(人聲繼承人聲、音樂繼承 other、寬頻噪聲與瞬態噪聲都繼承鼓,因為爆音與寬頻底噪跟打擊類起音、噪聲類特徵最像;殘差繼承貝斯),並把瞬態與延展兩個頭的初始輸出刻意縮小(分別乘 0.075 與 0.05),避免沿用權重在初始化階段就輸出過大或比例錯誤的訊號。

訓練資料是把乾淨全頻音訊模擬出歷史錄音的劣化:用分段線性頻域濾波器模擬頻寬限制、注入取樣自真實 78 轉唱片雜訊分佈(15.2 至 52.3 dB SNR)的底噪、再加上對數均勻分佈(中位數每秒 1.58 次)的爆音。損失函數把 HTDemucs 原本的 L1 波形損失擴充成:五個乾淨分軌各自的 L1 加多解析度 STFT 損失、一個混音一致性懲罰(強迫五個非延展分軌加總等於輸入混音)、一個讓殘差分軌預設趨近靜音的能量懲罰,以及只作用在高頻延展分軌上的對抗式損失與特徵匹配損失。訓練分兩階段:第一階段凍結 Transformer 瓶頸、不啟用判別器,先建立基本的語意分離;第二階段解凍瓶頸並啟動 Multi-Period 與 Multi-Resolution 判別器,只對延展分軌施加對抗訓練,避免判別器訊號污染到其他確定性分軌。

RESTORE keeps HTDemucs's dual-domain (time + spectral) bi-U-Net structure, with a cross-domain Transformer bottleneck that interleaves self-attention within each domain and cross-attention between them. The authors expand the output from four heads to six, initializing each new stem from the closest pretrained donor head (vocals from vocals, music from other, both broadband and transient noise from drums since clicks and hiss resemble drum-like impulsive and noise-like features, and residual from bass), while deliberately shrinking the transient and extension heads' initial outputs (scaled by 0.075 and 0.05 respectively) so the copied weights do not emit large or wrongly-scaled signals before the network adapts.

Training data simulates historical degradation on clean full-band audio: a piecewise-linear frequency-domain filter for band-limiting, hiss injected from a distribution measured on real 78 RPM discs (15.2-52.3 dB SNR), and clicks at a log-uniform rate with a median of 1.58 per second. The loss extends HTDemucs's L1 waveform loss with per-stem L1 plus multi-resolution STFT loss on the five clean stems, a mixture-consistency penalty forcing the five non-extension stems to sum back to the input, a light energy penalty pushing the residual stem toward silence by default, and an adversarial plus feature-matching loss applied only to the high-frequency-extension stem. Training runs in two stages: phase one freezes the Transformer bottleneck with no discriminator to establish basic semantic separation, and phase two unfreezes it and activates Multi-Period and Multi-Resolution discriminators applied exclusively to the extension stem, so adversarial signal cannot contaminate the deterministic stems.

限制Limitations

論文的定量評估分成兩種:一種是在合成劣化的資料上跟乾淨參考比對(SI-SDR、ViSQOL),另一種是在真實歷史錄音上使用無需參考答案的 Fréchet Audio Distance(FAD)。但真實歷史錄音本來就沒有乾淨的參考音訊,所以在真實資料上唯一能報告的只有 FAD 這種分佈層級的相似度指標,沒辦法像合成資料一樣直接算出每一段錄音修復對錯的逐段準確率,可調控性(steerability)與音樂洩漏(music bleed)這兩項指標同樣只在合成測試集上驗證過。論文結尾也自己指出,目前高頻延展只有單一分軌同時服務人聲與音樂,尚未針對兩者的頻譜特性分別建立獨立的延展頭,而且僅有客觀指標,還沒有做真人聆聽實驗來驗證典藏保存場景下的主觀感受、真實性與可調控性。

The paper's quantitative evaluation splits into two kinds: comparison against clean references on synthetically degraded data (SI-SDR, ViSQOL), and reference-free Fréchet Audio Distance (FAD) on real historical recordings. But real historical recordings have no clean reference by definition, so the only thing reportable on them is a distribution-level similarity metric like FAD, not a per-clip accuracy the way synthetic data allows; steerability and music-bleed are likewise validated only on the synthetic test set, not confirmed on real historical audio. The paper itself notes that the current high-frequency-extension stem is shared between vocals and music rather than having independent heads tuned to each source's spectral characteristics, and that only objective metrics are reported so far, with no listening study yet to validate subjective quality, authenticity, or steerability in an actual archival setting.

討論Discussion

在合成測試集的全頻修復上,RESTORE 在每一項指標都勝過所有基線:SI-SDR 達 0.76 dB、ViSQOL 達 2.86,FAD-CLAP 只有最強基線(U-Net 級聯,0.81)的一半左右(0.40)。特別值得注意的是,共用同一個 HTDemucs 骨幹、但沒有經過本文訓練策略的原版 HT-Demucs,FAD-CLAP 只從 0.86 微幅改善到 0.83,說明效果主要來自任務設計與訓練策略,而不是模型容量本身。在人聲分軌的頻寬匹配比較上,RESTORE 的 SI-SDR 達 7.65 dB,優於 HT-Demucs 的 4.21 dB,殘留噪聲也壓到 -8.36 dB(HT-Demucs 為 -5.38 dB,BABE-2 為 -2.86 dB)。

在真實歷史錄音上,即使關掉生成式高頻延展,RESTORE 的 FAD 仍全面優於所有基線,確認效果的主要來源是分離與去噪的重新設計,而高頻延展只是額外可選的加分項。速度上,單一前向傳遞就能一次產生六個分軌,即時因子(RTF)僅 0.0195,比 U-Net 級聯快約 50 倍,比擴散模型基線 BABE-2 快超過 1300 倍,參數量也只有 BABE-2 的 1/3.7;每個分軌的增益調整都與其實際音量呈近乎線性關係(Spearman 0.91 至 0.97),而瞬態與寬頻噪聲分軌裡混進的音樂能量都低於 0.2%(0.03% 與 0.17%),代表把這兩軌靜音幾乎不會誤刪音樂內容,「可調控卻不誤傷」的核心宣稱有具體數字支撐,而不只是定性描述。

On full-band synthetic restoration, RESTORE beats every baseline on every metric: SI-SDR of 0.76 dB, ViSQOL of 2.86, and FAD-CLAP at roughly half the strongest baseline's value (0.40 vs. 0.81 for the cascaded U-Net). Notably, plain HT-Demucs -- sharing the same backbone and size but not this paper's training strategy -- only improves FAD-CLAP marginally, from 0.86 to 0.83, indicating the gains come from the task formulation and training strategy rather than model capacity. On the bandwidth-matched vocal stem, RESTORE reaches 7.65 dB SI-SDR versus HT-Demucs's 4.21 dB, and pushes residual noise down to -8.36 dB versus -5.38 dB for HT-Demucs and -2.86 dB for BABE-2.

On real historical recordings, RESTORE has the lowest FAD across categories even with the generative high-frequency extension disabled, confirming that the redesigned separation and denoising drive most of the gain, with extension as an optional add-on. On speed, a single forward pass produces all six stems at a real-time factor of 0.0195, about 50x faster than the cascaded U-Net and over 1,300x faster than the diffusion-based BABE-2, with 3.7x fewer parameters. Each stem's gain tracks its realized loudness in a near-linear way (Spearman 0.91-0.97), and the music energy leaking into the transient and broadband stems stays under 0.2% (0.03% and 0.17% respectively), giving the paper's "steerable without collateral damage" claim concrete numbers rather than a purely qualitative description.
