Beat This! Live – Online Beat and Downbeat Tracking by Predicting the Future

Beat This! Live：把離線節拍追蹤模型改成因果版本，訓練時把標籤往前移幾格讓模型「先猜」，再用移位因果局部最大值（SCLM）挑峰；GTZAN 上線上 beat F1 從 BeatNet+ 的 80.6 拉到 86.7、downbeat 從 56.5 拉到 74.1，零延遲以下（-17 ms）的版本也有 85.9 / 72.2；但還不是真正的即時實作，測試只有 GTZAN，比較對象用的是不同訓練資料的公開權重

動機Motivation

節拍（beat）與強拍（downbeat）追蹤近年進步很多，但最準的系統幾乎都是離線的：整首歌先讀完，再用雙向序列模型或大範圍時間上下文做判斷。這對分析資料庫沒問題，對現場伴奏、燈光同步、跟拍的機器人或 DJ 工具就完全不能用，因為未來的音訊還沒來。

光是因果（causal）也不夠。人大約能察覺 50 ms 的音訊延遲，所以線上系統還要把延遲壓低。作者的出發點是：音樂有時間規律，人跟著節拍器拍手時其實是在預期下一拍，那模型也可以學著「提早幾格」說出節拍會在哪裡，用預測換來的時間去做更好的峰值挑選，甚至做到零延遲或負延遲。

作者明說這篇的目標是做出有競爭力的線上模型，而不是已經上線的即時實作。

Beat and downbeat tracking have improved a lot in recent years, but the most accurate systems are almost all offline: they read the whole song first and then decide with bidirectional sequence models or wide temporal context. That is fine for analysing a collection and useless for live accompaniment, lighting sync, tapping robots or DJ tools, where the future audio has not arrived yet.

Causality alone is not enough either. People can notice roughly 50 ms of audio latency, so an online system also has to keep latency low. The authors start from the observation that music is temporally regular and that people tapping to a metronome anticipate the next beat; a model can likewise learn to say a few frames early where a beat will fall, and spend the time it gains on better peak picking, even reaching zero or negative latency.

The authors state plainly that the goal is a competitive online model, not a deployed real-time implementation.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

基礎模型：Beat This!（Foscarin、Schlüter、Widmer，ISMIR 2024）。 它的賣點是不用 madmom 的動態貝氏網路（DBN）後處理，只用局部最大值挑峰就接近最佳水準，而且全部開源。這篇的架構、訓練資料（15 個資料集共 4,556 首）、切分與超參數完全沿用它，所以跟 Beat This! 之間的差距可以乾淨地歸因到「改成因果」與「後處理」。資料庫裡之前讀過的 Explore This!（tatum 格點）和 CASM（半馬可夫後處理）也都建在 Beat This! 上，但都是離線的。

線上方法的前例。 BeatNet（Heydari 等，ISMIR 2021）用 CRNN 加粒子濾波同時追 beat、downbeat 和拍號；BeatNet+（TISMIR 2024）把 LSTM 加到四層並加一條擾動輸入的訓練分支，是這篇比較的現有最好線上系統。BEAST（Chang & Su，ICASSP 2024）用分塊的串流 Transformer 並在塊之間繼承上下文。

The base model: Beat This! (Foscarin, Schlüter, Widmer, ISMIR 2024). Its selling point is near state-of-the-art accuracy without madmom's dynamic Bayesian network (DBN) postprocessing, using only local-maximum peak picking, and it is fully open source. This paper reuses its architecture, training data (4,556 tracks from 15 datasets), splits and hyperparameters unchanged, so the gap to Beat This! can be cleanly attributed to causality and postprocessing. Explore This! (tatum grid) and CASM (semi-Markov postprocessing), both read earlier in this database, are also built on Beat This!, but both are offline.

Online precedents. BeatNet (Heydari et al., ISMIR 2021) tracks beats, downbeats and meter with a CRNN plus particle filtering; BeatNet+ (TISMIR 2024) deepens the LSTM to four layers and adds a training branch with perturbed input, and is the best existing online system compared here. BEAST (Chang & Su, ICASSP 2024) uses a block-wise streaming Transformer that carries context between blocks.

方法Method

因果化。 所有沿時間軸運作的層都要改：頻率×時間的 2D 卷積換成因果卷積（靠 padding 與裁切），時間方向的部分 Transformer 與六個 Transformer 區塊的雙向注意力換成因果自注意力；頻率方向的注意力不違反因果所以保留。作者用梯度檢查驗證：對 1,500 格輸入的第 750 格輸出求梯度，只改注意力時最後一個非零梯度落在第 754 格（卷積的感受野偷看了 4 格），兩者都改才真正停在 750。

串流。 原本的 30 秒不重疊區塊在線上會讓每塊開頭沒有過去上下文；全歷史因果注意力的成本又會隨時間線性成長。所以改用因果滑動視窗注意力（視窗 W 格），每格計算量固定。

標籤移位與 SCLM。 訓練時把真實 beat/downbeat 標籤往前移 s 格，逼模型提早預測。推論時用「移位因果局部最大值」（Shifted Causal Local Max, SCLM）：在 t 格往前後各看 s 格的視窗裡，中心值超過門檻且是最大值才發出 beat；downbeat 只在已判定為 beat 時、視窗內任一 downbeat 值超過門檻才發出，等同離線版的「downbeat 對齊到 beat」。若視窗比 2s+1 小，就用部分預測時間換更低延遲，甚至負延遲。

Making it causal. Every layer that operates over time has to change: the frequency×time 2D convolutions become causal convolutions (via padding and cropping), and the bidirectional attention in the time-directed partial Transformers and all six Transformer blocks becomes causal self-attention; frequency-directed attention does not break causality and is kept. A gradient check confirms it: for output frame 750 of a 1,500-frame input, making only attention causal leaves the last non-zero gradient at frame 754 (the convolutions' receptive field peeks 4 frames ahead), and only changing both stops it at 750.

Streaming. The original non-overlapping 30 s blocks would leave each block without past context online, and causal attention over the full history grows linearly in cost. So the model uses causal sliding-window attention over W frames, at constant cost per frame.

Label shift and SCLM. During training the true beat/downbeat labels are moved s frames earlier, forcing the model to predict ahead. At inference, Shifted Causal Local Max (SCLM) emits a beat at frame t if the centre value of a window of s frames on each side exceeds the threshold and is the window maximum; a downbeat is emitted only on a beat frame when any downbeat value in the window exceeds the threshold, the causal analogue of offline downbeat-to-beat snapping. A window smaller than 2s+1 trades some foresight for lower, even negative, latency.

限制Limitations

還不是即時系統。 作者自己寫明真正的即時實作不在範圍內：要逐格處理就得快取卷積與 Transformer 的中間表示，最好用編譯語言重寫。文中的 RTF（0.05–0.08，單執行緒 CPU）是在整段音訊上算的，延遲數字（23 ms、-17 ms）是從頻譜窗與移位格數推出來的理論值，不含逐格推論的實際計算時間。另外 23 ms 那部分來自置中 STFT 的隱性前看，因為作者直接用 Beat This! 公開的頻譜，不是所有訓練資料都有音訊可以重算。

測試只有 GTZAN。 GTZAN 每首 30 秒、速度大多穩定。古典類 beat F1 只有 56.3、downbeat 40.2，變速、自由速度或拍號變化的音樂完全沒有評估；GTZAN 以外的測試集也沒有。

跟 BeatNet+ 的比較不是同一條件。 BeatNet+ 用的是公開權重，訓練資料跟這篇的 4,556 首不同，所以 80.6→86.7 有多少來自架構、多少來自資料，分不出來。BEAST、BeatNet 的 RTF 是從論文抄來的，硬體不同。

暖身問題被評估設定部分遮住。 Table 2 顯示因果模型在每首前 10 秒明顯較差（SW-256 第一段 beat F1 82.7，離線 91.8），但 Table 4 的標準評估照慣例忽略每首前 5 秒。三個 seed 只報平均，沒有變異；也沒有拍號追蹤，而 BeatNet+ 有。

Not yet a real-time system. The authors state that a real real-time implementation is out of scope: frame-by-frame processing needs cached intermediate states of the convolutions and Transformers, ideally in a compiled language. The RTFs reported (0.05–0.08 on one CPU thread) are computed over whole recordings, and the latencies (23 ms, -17 ms) are theoretical values derived from the spectrogram window and the number of shifted frames, excluding actual per-frame compute. The 23 ms itself comes from the centred STFT's implicit lookahead, because the authors reuse the spectrograms published with Beat This!; not every training dataset has audio to recompute them.

GTZAN is the only test set. GTZAN pieces are 30 s long and mostly steady in tempo. Classical reaches only 56.3 beat F1 and 40.2 downbeat F1, and music with tempo changes, free tempo or meter changes is not evaluated at all; there is no test set beyond GTZAN.

The BeatNet+ comparison is not like for like. BeatNet+ runs from its public weights, trained on different data from this paper's 4,556 tracks, so how much of 80.6→86.7 comes from architecture and how much from data cannot be separated. The RTFs of BEAST and BeatNet are copied from their papers, on different hardware.

The warm-up problem is partly hidden by the protocol. Table 2 shows causal models are clearly worse in the first 10 s (SW-256 first-segment beat F1 82.7 vs 91.8 offline), but the standard evaluation in Table 4 ignores the first 5 s of each piece by convention. Three seeds are averaged with no variance reported, and there is no meter tracking, which BeatNet+ has.

討論Discussion

數字。 GTZAN 上三個版本：Live Large（512 維、視窗 512）beat 86.7 / downbeat 74.1、延遲 23 ms；Live（128 維）86.1 / 72.8；Live Zero（-17 ms）85.9 / 72.2。對照 BeatNet+ 80.6 / 56.5、BEAST 80.0 / 46.8、Meier 等的 RNN-PLP-On 74.7（只有 beat）。拿來對照的離線 Beat This! small 在 GTZAN 是 88.8 / 77.2，所以最大的線上版只比小的離線版低約 2–3 分。分類別看，十類中 beat 只有雷鬼輸 BeatNet+（88.7 對 91.8），downbeat 十類全贏，爵士 63.4 對 38.5、古典 40.2 對 19.2。

消融。 只改卷積幾乎不傷（beat 88.8→88.9），改注意力才傷（downbeat 77.2→73.6）。在驗證集上，不移位的因果模型只能用門檻法，beat F1 83.1；移 1 格就到 84.5，移 5 格反而掉到 82.4，表示「提早預測」變難的代價很快就蓋過多看幾格的好處。

這個想法最像什麼。 標籤移位加 SCLM 本質上是固定延遲平滑（fixed-lag smoothing）：用 s 格的決策延遲換更好的峰值判斷，只是把那 s 格搬到訓練目標裡，讓決策延遲不再等於輸出延遲。作者自己也說靈感來自 Meier 等（TISMIR 2024）的 beat lookahead；差別在 Meier 是在後處理端用 PLP 外推，這篇是讓網路直接學。值得注意的是 madmom 的 DBN 本身就有線上前向模式，這篇沒有拿它當基線。

Numbers. Three variants on GTZAN: Live Large (512-dim, 512-frame window) reaches 86.7 beat / 74.1 downbeat F1 at 23 ms latency; Live (128-dim) 86.1 / 72.8; Live Zero (-17 ms) 85.9 / 72.2. Against BeatNet+ 80.6 / 56.5, BEAST 80.0 / 46.8, and Meier et al.'s RNN-PLP-On 74.7 (beats only). The offline Beat This! small used as reference scores 88.8 / 77.2 on GTZAN, so the largest online variant is only about 2–3 points below the small offline one. By genre, BeatNet+ wins only reggae beats out of ten genres (91.8 vs 88.7), and loses every downbeat genre, e.g. jazz 63.4 vs 38.5 and classical 40.2 vs 19.2.

Ablations. Causal convolutions barely hurt (beat 88.8→88.9); causal attention does (downbeat 77.2→73.6). On the validation set an unshifted causal model, limited to thresholding, gets 83.1 beat F1; a 1-frame shift reaches 84.5, while a 5-frame shift drops to 82.4, so the cost of predicting further ahead quickly outweighs the benefit of a longer look.

What the idea most resembles. Label shift plus SCLM is essentially fixed-lag smoothing: spend s frames of decision delay on a better peak decision, except that the s frames are moved into the training target so decision delay no longer equals output delay. The authors credit Meier et al.'s (TISMIR 2024) beat lookahead; the difference is that Meier extrapolates with PLP in postprocessing, while here the network learns it directly. Notably, madmom's DBN already has an online forward mode, and the paper does not use it as a baseline.

Line-Level Singer Diarization in Multi-Member Pop Songs: A Benchmark, Lead-Vocal Isolation, and Song-Level Decoding

多人偶像團體歌曲的逐句歌手分群：ColorCoded-SD 基準（43 首 K-pop、1,740 句、每首 3–8 人，人與人一致率 89.1%）；karaoke 分離出主唱、歌唱微調的說話人嵌入、加上兩個免訓練的整首解碼器，B3-F1 從標準流程的 0.641 提到 0.699（IdolSongsJp 0.800→0.867）；主唱分離是最大單一因素，乾聲下兩個編碼器分不出高下，商用語音分群只找到平均 1.35 人；單一作者、資料來自自家公司的網站，基準 12-24 才公開

為什麼挑這篇：這篇的核心是說話人嵌入加分群，資料端靠音源分離把主唱抽出來，所以歸 `representation`；它附帶一個基準，但不是樂譜理解的基準，不歸 `benchmark`。選它是因為它把錯誤拆成「輸入、編碼器、解碼」三塊，每一塊都有配對 bootstrap 檢定，而且把不成功的嘗試也列出來；資料庫 08-18 讀過的歌手引導分離（Singer-Informed Vocal Source Separation）只處理兩人對唱，這篇把問題推到四到八人的團體。Why this one: The core of this paper is speaker embeddings plus clustering, with source separation pulling out the lead vocal on the input side, so it goes under `representation`; it ships a benchmark, but not a score-understanding one, so not `benchmark`. I picked it because it splits the error into input, encoder and decoding, backs each part with paired bootstrap tests, and lists what did not work. The singer-informed separation paper read on 08-18 handles only two-singer duets; this one pushes the problem to groups of four to eight.

動機Motivation

K-pop 與 J-pop 偶像團體有四到十多名成員，一首歌裡輪流唱。粉絲很流行做「歌詞分配（line distribution）」或彩色歌詞影片，每一句標上唱的那位成員的顏色；作者公司經營的標註工具上就有 6,241 個藝人的 19,042 個這種專案。判斷每句是誰唱的是最花時間的一步，看起來正是說話人分群（speaker diarization）的用途。

但語音分群直接搬過來不行：商用服務在平均 5.3 人的歌上只找到平均 1.35 個說話人，43 首裡有 33 首只找到一個。作者指出這個任務跟語音分群有三點不同：句子時間從歌詞或強制對齊就已知，所以問題是分群而不是切段；團體成員刻意被挑來聲音融合，唱法、製作和音域相近，還疊著和聲與即興；人數通常從成員名單就知道（6,915 個專案裡 95.7% 是全員都有唱）。

K-pop and J-pop idol groups have four to more than ten members who trade lines within a song. A popular fan format, the line-distribution or colour-coded lyrics video, marks each lyric line with the colour of the member who sings it; the annotation tool run by the author's company holds 19,042 such projects for 6,241 artists. Deciding who sings each line is the slowest step and looks like a job for speaker diarization.

But speech diarization does not transfer: a commercial service finds on average 1.35 speakers in songs with 5.3 singers, and a single speaker in 33 of 43 songs. The author names three differences from speech diarization: line timings are known from lyrics or forced alignment, so the problem is grouping rather than segmentation; members are chosen to blend, sharing vocal style, production and pitch range, layered with harmonies and ad-libs; and the singer count is usually known from the member list (in 95.7% of 6,915 projects every member sings).

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最接近的先前工作：FruitsMusic（Suda 等，ISMIR 2024）。 它首先在日本偶像團體的音樂影片上做歌手分群，並指出 ECAPA-TDNN 嵌入在混音上會退化。差別是 FruitsMusic 跨同團體多首歌分群、以幀計分，這篇在單首歌內分群、以句計分，所以數字不能直接比——這篇也沒有在 FruitsMusic 上報任何結果。IdolSongsJp（Suda 等，ISMIR 2025）是委託製作的偶像風格歌曲，每位歌手都有整首錄音，這篇拿它當未見過歌手的測試。

解碼器其實是既有技巧搬到「句」上。 黏著（sticky）Viterbi 就是 VBx（Landini 等，2022）那種對時間的 HMM 轉移先驗，作者也這樣說，只是單位從幀換成句；這跟 sticky HDP-HMM 給「留在同一狀態」加獎勵是同一個想法。「跨專輯連結」把一個團體各首歌的群集用匈牙利演算法對到固定的成員身份，對應的是廣播新聞裡的跨節目說話人分群（cross-show diarization）：同一批人會在不同錄音裡重複出現。

分離與歌手嵌入。 前端用的 HT-Demucs、BS-RoFormer、Mel-RoFormer 都是現成模型，社群的 karaoke 模型再把主唱和和聲分開。資料庫 08-18 讀過的歌手引導分離（Xu & Kim，2026）用乾淨的報名片段條件化分離器，但只處理兩人對唱；這篇試過類似的「目標歌手抽取」迴圈，結果無效，列在負面結果裡。

Closest prior work: FruitsMusic (Suda et al., ISMIR 2024). It introduced singer diarization on Japanese idol-group music videos and showed that ECAPA-TDNN embeddings degrade on mixtures. The difference is that FruitsMusic clusters a group's lines across several songs and scores frames, while this paper clusters within one song and scores lines, so the numbers are not directly comparable, and the paper reports no result on FruitsMusic. IdolSongsJp (Suda et al., ISMIR 2025) consists of commissioned idol-style songs with a full recording by every singer, and serves here as the unseen-singer test.

The decoders are existing techniques moved to lines. The sticky Viterbi pass is a VBx-style (Landini et al., 2022) HMM transition prior over time, as the author says, with lines instead of frames as units; it is the same idea as the sticky HDP-HMM's bonus for staying in a state. Discography linking maps the clusters of a group's songs onto fixed member identities with the Hungarian method, which corresponds to cross-show diarization in broadcast news: the same people recur across recordings.

Separation and singer embeddings. The front ends, HT-Demucs, BS-RoFormer and Mel-RoFormer, are off-the-shelf, and a community karaoke model further splits lead from backing vocals. Singer-informed separation (Xu & Kim, 2026), read here on 08-18, conditions a separator on a clean enrollment clip but handles only two-singer duets; this paper tried a similar target-singer extraction loop and lists it among the negative results.

方法Method

資料。 從標註網站挑五個團體（BLACKPINK 4 人、ILLIT 5、KATSEYE 6、BABYMONSTER 7、Stray Kids 8），只保留至少兩位不同使用者的專案在 0.25 秒格點上有 75% 一致的歌，剩 50 首；音訊對得上官方錄音的 43 首成為 ColorCoded-SD。被另一個專案判給不同人的 30 句刪掉，被確認的 1,470 句、只有一人標註的 270 句。因為不附音訊，釋出內容包含官方錄音編號、10 Hz chroma 指紋與 DTW 腳本，讓使用者把時間對到自己的音檔（平移裁切測試誤差 0.03 秒內）。IdolSongsJp 的句標籤則從「每位歌手的獨唱版本」反推：某 100 ms 幀的母帶跟某人獨唱版的殘差夠小，就判為那人唱，得到 14 首 309 句。

流程。 音訊分離後切成 3 秒視窗（1.5 秒跳）、去掉太安靜的視窗，用說話人編碼器取嵌入；每首歌先減掉全曲平均嵌入（去掉所有歌手共享的製作成分）再正規化，句向量取視窗平均，用平均連結階層式分群（AHC）分成 k 群。短於 1.2 秒或分離後無聲的句子（佔 11%）沒有聲紋，借用時間上最近一句的群。

歌唱微調。 用監督式對比學習在 2,277 首、1,765 位歌手（約 125 小時，排除所有評估藝人）上微調 ECAPA2 和 SpeechBrain ResNet：同一首歌裡的不同歌手當負例權重加倍，因為那正是分群要解決的混淆；再加一項讓嵌入不要離原始語音嵌入太遠的錨定項（λ=0.2）。架構與推論成本都不變。

整首解碼。 黏著解碼利用「連續兩句同一人唱」的機率 57.2%（遠高於隨機的約 20%），在群中心估計與帶黏著獎勵 β 的 Viterbi 之間交替；跨專輯連結把同團體各首歌的群中心對到 R 個成員原型，再用混合後的參考重新解碼五輪（a=0.75、β=0.2，用留一團體選出）。

Data. Five groups are taken from the annotation site (BLACKPINK 4 members, ILLIT 5, KATSEYE 6, BABYMONSTER 7, Stray Kids 8), keeping a song only if projects by at least two different users agree at 75% on a 0.25 s grid, leaving 50 songs; the 43 whose audio matches the official recording form ColorCoded-SD. 30 lines another project assigns to a different singer are dropped, leaving 1,470 confirmed and 270 single-annotator lines. No audio is shipped, so the release includes official recording identifiers, 10 Hz chroma fingerprints and a DTW script that maps timings onto a user's own copy (within 0.03 s in a shift-and-trim test). IdolSongsJp line labels are derived from each singer's solo version: if a 100 ms frame of the master has a small enough residual against someone's solo version, that singer is labelled, yielding 309 lines in 14 songs.

Pipeline. After separation, audio is cut into 3 s windows with a 1.5 s hop, quiet windows are dropped, and a speaker encoder embeds each window. Each song's mean embedding is subtracted (removing the production component all singers share) before normalising; a line vector is the mean of its windows, and lines are grouped into k clusters by average-linkage agglomerative clustering (AHC). Lines under 1.2 s or silent after separation (11%) have no voiceprint and take the cluster of the nearest line in time.

Singing adaptation. ECAPA2 and a SpeechBrain ResNet are fine-tuned with supervised contrastive learning on 2,277 songs and 1,765 singers (about 125 h, all evaluation artists excluded): negatives from a different singer of the same song get double weight, since that is exactly the confusion grouping must resolve, plus an anchor term (λ=0.2) keeping embeddings close to the original speech embeddings. Architecture and inference cost are unchanged.

Song-level decoding. Sticky decoding exploits that consecutive lines share a singer 57.2% of the time (against about 20% by chance), alternating centroid estimation with a Viterbi pass that rewards staying with bonus β. Discography linking maps each song's centroids onto R member prototypes of the group and re-decodes five rounds against blended references (a=0.75, β=0.2, chosen leave-one-group-out).

限制Limitations

利益關係與可取得性。 單一作者，資料來自作者自己公司經營的網站；服務條款修訂後才允許研究使用，而那份條款 2026-12-24 才生效，所以基準在這篇論文出現時還不能下載，結果暫時無法被外部重現。

在基準上調參。 所有元件都在 ColorCoded 歌曲上開發和調整，作者承認這部分數字略為樂觀。唯一完全沒碰過的 IdolSongsJp 只有 14 首，信賴區間很寬（完整系統 0.867 [0.78, 0.94]）；ColorCoded-SD 的主結果區間也有 0.699 [0.63, 0.76]，而 Stray Kids 只有 3 首歌、ILLIT 6 首。

任務被簡化了三處。 多人合唱的句子（佔標註句的 16.4%）不在任務內；人數 k 直接給定，不給的話自動估人數只有 23–28% 的歌猜對；句子時間來自人工標註，不是強制對齊，實際應用還得先解決多語歌詞的對齊。

比較對象偏弱。 商用語音分群（ElevenLabs Scribe v2）是在完整混音上跑、而且本來就為語音調校，B3-F1 0.386 被大幅超越並不意外；跟領域內最接近的 FruitsMusic 則因為協定不同完全沒有比較。範圍也只有 K-pop 和 J-pop 風格。

Conflict of interest and availability. The paper has a single author, and the data come from a website run by the author's own company; research use is permitted only under revised terms of service that take effect on 2026-12-24, so the benchmark cannot be downloaded when the paper appears and the results cannot yet be reproduced externally.

Tuning on the benchmark. All components were developed and tuned on the ColorCoded songs, and the author concedes those numbers are mildly optimistic. The only untouched set, IdolSongsJp, has 14 songs and wide intervals (full system 0.867 [0.78, 0.94]); the main ColorCoded-SD result is 0.699 [0.63, 0.76], and Stray Kids contributes only 3 songs, ILLIT 6.

The task is simplified in three places. Lines sung by several members (16.4% of annotated lines) are excluded; the singer count k is given, and without it automatic counting gets the exact number in only 23–28% of songs; line timings come from human annotations, not forced alignment, so a real application still has to solve multilingual lyric alignment first.

Weak comparisons. The commercial speech diarizer (ElevenLabs Scribe v2) runs on full mixes and is tuned for speech, so beating its 0.386 B3-F1 by a wide margin is unsurprising; the closest in-domain work, FruitsMusic, is not compared at all because the protocols differ. Coverage is limited to K-pop and J-pop style.

討論Discussion

主結果。 ColorCoded-SD 上，標準流程（Demucs 人聲＋ECAPA2＋單首分群）B3-F1 0.641、逐句正確率 66.3%；完整系統 0.699、72.4%（+0.058，p=0.001）。只看被確認的句子是 +0.045（p=0.012）。IdolSongsJp 從 0.800 到 0.867（+0.066，p=0.003），而主唱輸入＋ECAPA2-ft＋黏著解碼這個組合最高 0.875。拿人與人一致率 89.1% 當參考，正確率的差距（66.3→89.1）只補上大約四分之一。

三塊各佔多少。 主唱分離在「分離後仍留有主唱」的 1,308 句上貢獻 +0.039（ECAPA2）到 +0.059（ECAPA2-ft），比換編碼器還大；在 IdolSongsJp 上換成歌手乾聲（等於完美分離）時兩個編碼器都是 0.871，混音上兩者差 0.07——輸入夠乾淨，編碼器選哪個就不重要了。歌唱微調讓 ResNet 的 B3-F1 +0.060、EER 從 30.7% 降到 26.5%，跟 ECAPA2 打平但 CPU 時間只要八分之一（0.13 對 1.1 秒）。兩個解碼器單獨用都不顯著（+0.005、+0.007），合起來才 +0.028；增益隨團體歌曲數成長（BLACKPINK +4.5 分），ILLIT 反而 -1.8，IdolSongsJp 上跨專輯連結沒用（-0.003），因為那 18 位歌手不是固定團體。

錯誤落在哪。 短於 1.5 秒的句子只有 0.57 正確，沒有聲紋、借鄰句的 11% 只有 0.47；團體之間從 BLACKPINK 0.91 到 ILLIT 0.48，跟成員聲音多像有關。負面結果很有參考價值：訓練資料加四倍（577→2,277 首）沒有增益，用主唱分離過的資料訓練也沒有轉移得比較好，說明編碼器很早就飽和，剩下的空間在短句與被分離器吃掉的句子。

Main results. On ColorCoded-SD the standard pipeline (Demucs vocals + ECAPA2 + per-song clustering) reaches 0.641 B3-F1 and 66.3% line accuracy; the full system 0.699 and 72.4% (+0.058, p=0.001). On confirmed lines alone the gain is +0.045 (p=0.012). IdolSongsJp goes from 0.800 to 0.867 (+0.066, p=0.003), and lead input + ECAPA2-ft + sticky decoding reaches the highest 0.875. Taking human agreement of 89.1% as reference, the accuracy gap (66.3→89.1) is only about a quarter closed.

How the three parts split. On the 1,308 lines where a lead survives separation, lead input adds +0.039 (ECAPA2) to +0.059 (ECAPA2-ft), more than any encoder change; on IdolSongsJp with the singers' dry takes (an oracle separator) both encoders score 0.871, against a 0.07 gap on the mix, so with clean enough input the encoder choice stops mattering. Singing adaptation lifts the ResNet by +0.060 B3-F1 and cuts EER from 30.7% to 26.5%, tying ECAPA2 at one eighth of the CPU time (0.13 vs 1.1 s). Neither decoder is significant alone (+0.005, +0.007); together they add +0.028. The gain grows with how many songs a group contributes (BLACKPINK +4.5 points), ILLIT drops -1.8, and on IdolSongsJp linking does not help (-0.003) because its 18 singers do not form a fixed group.

Where the errors are. Lines under 1.5 s are 0.57 correct, and the 11% without a voiceprint, which borrow a neighbour's cluster, only 0.47; across groups accuracy runs from 0.91 (BLACKPINK) to 0.48 (ILLIT), tracking how alike the members sound. The negative results are useful: four times the training data (577→2,277 songs) brings no gain, and training on lead-separated data transfers no better, so the encoder saturates early and the remaining headroom lies in short lines and lines the separator silences.

Music Circuit: Trace and Control Style Dynamics in Large Music Generation Model

為什麼挑這篇：資料庫 11-20 讀過 Harmonic Circuit，把電路發現搬到符號音樂模型上解釋和弦進行；這篇把同一套思路推到大型歌曲生成模型 YuE2，而且不只解釋，還拿電路來控制曲風，跟提示詞和 CAA 正面比較，所以排在今天第三篇。技術上「KL 加權動態方向」是新東西，因此標成深度分析；也正因為它宣稱「勝過提示詞」，值得把評審設計和聽感測試的結果攤開來看。Why this one: The database read Harmonic Circuit on 11-20, which brought circuit discovery to symbolic music models to explain chord progressions; this paper pushes the same line of work to a large song generator, YuE2, and does not stop at explanation: it uses the circuit to control style and compares it head to head with the prompt and CAA, which is why it is today's third paper. The KL-weighted dynamic direction is technically new, so it is marked as a deep dive; and precisely because it claims to beat the prompt, the judging setup and the listening results deserve to be laid out.

動機Motivation

曲風是使用者在大型音樂生成模型裡最常拿來控制的屬性之一，但它在模型內部怎麼被表示、由哪些元件推動，幾乎沒有人研究過。語言模型那邊，電路分析（circuit analysis）已經能把一個表徵追溯到少數幾個注意力頭和 MLP 神經元組成的稀疏子網路，也成功用來解釋和控制情緒這類貫穿整段輸出的「全域屬性」。

作者把問題拆成三個：曲風這種動態的全域概念在表徵裡以什麼形式存在？哪些內部元件實質上推動它？能不能直接用這些元件控制曲風？他們的直覺是，音樂的資訊是分層組織的（調性、節奏、配器），所以曲風很可能不是固定在某一個位置的一個向量。

Style is one of the attributes users most often control in large music generation models, yet how it is represented inside the model and which components drive it has barely been studied. In language models, circuit analysis can trace a representation to a sparse sub-network of a few attention heads and MLP neurons, and has been used to explain and control global attributes such as emotion that run through a whole output.

The authors split the question in three: in what form does style, a dynamic global concept, exist in the representations? Which internal components substantially drive it? Can those components be used to control style directly? Their intuition is that musical information is organised in levels (mode, rhythm, instrumentation), so style is unlikely to be one vector fixed at one position.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最接近的先前工作：Emotion Circuits（Wang 等，2025a）。 它先抽出情緒方向，再找出沿這個方向寫入的元件並介入，讓同一情境在沒有情緒提示詞時產生不同情緒的回應。這篇的整體流程——抽方向、按元件對方向的貢獻排名、對選中元件注入差值——幾乎是把它搬到音樂上，作者也明說是類比。新增的部分是處理「方向隨位置變」：用每一步的 KL 散度加權合成一個方向。

更早、而且作者沒點名的近親：推論時介入（Inference-Time Intervention, ITI；Li 等，2023）。 ITI 挑出少數注意力頭，在生成的每一步沿著兩種條件的平均差方向推一把。這篇的 steering（式 8：在選中元件上加 α 倍的平均差）在形式上就是 ITI 加上 MLP 神經元。比較組裡的 CAA（Rimsky 等，2024）則是在殘差流的單一層加平均差，不挑元件。

音樂這邊。 先前的作曲家向量（Jiang 等，2026）與用稀疏自編碼器找音樂概念（Singh 等，2026）都停在殘差流方向，不追是哪些元件寫出來的。資料庫 11-20 讀過的 Harmonic Circuit 在符號音樂模型上用 Information Flow Routes 追和弦進行的資訊流，但只做解釋、不做生成控制，也不是歌曲級的音訊模型。

Closest prior work: Emotion Circuits (Wang et al., 2025a). It extracts emotion directions, finds the components that write along them, and intervenes to make the same scenario yield replies with different emotions without any emotional prompt. This paper's pipeline (extract a direction, rank components by contribution to it, inject differences into the selected ones) is close to a port of that work to music, and the authors say so by analogy. The new part is dealing with directions that change with position, by merging them into one direction weighted by per-step KL divergence.

An older relative the authors do not name: Inference-Time Intervention (ITI; Li et al., 2023). ITI selects a few attention heads and pushes them along the mean difference between two conditions at every generation step. This paper's steering (Eq. 8: add α times the mean difference to the selected components) is formally ITI extended to MLP neurons. The CAA baseline (Rimsky et al., 2024) adds the mean difference to the residual stream at a single layer, without selecting components.

On the music side. Earlier composer vectors (Jiang et al., 2026) and sparse-autoencoder musical concepts (Singh et al., 2026) stay at residual-stream directions and do not trace which components write them. Harmonic Circuit, read here on 11-20, used Information Flow Routes to trace chord-progression information in symbolic music models, but only explained, did not control generation, and was not a song-level audio model.

方法Method

模型與資料。 YuE2 先由自迴歸分支寫出 ABC 樂譜，再產生音樂 token，最後由非自迴歸分支用 flow matching 生成聲學潛變量、VAE 解碼成音訊；三個階段都讀得到曲風標籤。作者用 GPT-6 Astra 寫短歌詞，每首配五種曲風（流行、金屬搖滾、經典 funk、電藍調、鄉村），保留五個版本都通過 CLAP 與 Gemini 3.6 Flash 檢查的歌詞，共 69 組：54 組拿來抽表徵、15 組只用於測試。

第一步：階段定位。 三個階段的標籤各自設成曲風或空標籤，共八種組合，再用 Shapley 值把完整標籤相對空標籤的 CLAP margin 增益（+0.262）分給三個階段：樂譜 76%、音樂 token 23%、聲學渲染 0%。只把空標籤的樂譜換成曲風標籤寫出的樂譜，非流行曲風的命中就從 216 首中的 6 首升到 115 首。

第二步：靜態觀察。 在每個位置，把同一歌詞五個曲風版本的隱藏狀態減掉五者平均（去掉共享的歌詞內容），加總正規化得到曲風方向；元件貢獻是它的激活乘上寫入方向與曲風方向的內積。結果是每個位置都分得開曲風，但樂譜開頭的方向跟後面幾乎正交，選出的 MLP 神經元幾乎不重疊，注意力頭只有機率水準的重疊（Jaccard 機率值 0.005 與 0.27）。

第三步：KL 加權動態方向。 對每一步計算曲風標籤與空標籤兩個下一 token 分佈的 KL 散度；由連鎖律，整段生成的 KL 等於逐步 KL 的期望和，所以逐步 KL 可視為該步在整體曲風差異中的份額。用它加權平均兩種標籤下的元件激活差，得到每個曲風一個固定方向 δ_s。元件按「注入 δ 後在自己那層把殘差往曲風方向推多少」打分，只留正分的，每個曲風每個階段 560 個元件（392 個 MLP 神經元、168 個注意力頭）。生成時在樂譜階段每一步對這些元件加上 α·δ_s（α=0.5），音樂 token 階段則注入前 128 步的平均差，所有階段都沒有曲風文字。

Model and data. YuE2's autoregressive branch first writes an ABC score, then generates music tokens, and a non-autoregressive branch generates acoustic latents with flow matching that a VAE decodes to audio; all three stages can read the style tag. The authors write short lyrics with GPT-6 Astra, pair each with five styles (pop, metal rock, classic funk, electric blues, country), and keep lyric groups whose five versions all pass CLAP and Gemini 3.6 Flash checks: 69 groups, 54 for extracting representations and 15 for testing only.

Step 1: stage localization. Each of the three stages gets either the style tag or the empty tag, eight combinations in all, and Shapley values split the CLAP-margin gain of the full tag over the empty tag (+0.262) among the stages: score 76%, music tokens 23%, acoustic rendering 0%. Replacing only the empty-tag score with the score written under the style tag raises non-pop hits from 6 to 115 of 216.

Step 2: static observation. At each position, the hidden states of a lyric's five style versions are centred on their mean (removing shared lyric content) and summed and normalised into a style direction; a component's contribution is its activation times the inner product of its write direction with the style direction. Styles are separable at every position, but the direction at the start of the score is nearly orthogonal to later ones, the selected MLP neurons barely overlap, and attention heads overlap only at chance level (chance Jaccard 0.005 and 0.27).

Step 3: KL-weighted dynamic direction. At each step, the KL divergence between the next-token distributions under the style tag and the empty tag is computed; by the chain rule the KL over the whole generation equals the expected sum of per-step KLs, so each step's KL is read as its share of the overall style difference. Weighting the component activation differences between the two tags by it yields one fixed direction δ_s per style. Components are scored by how far injecting δ pushes the residual along the style direction in their own layer, and only positive ones are kept: 560 per style and stage (392 MLP neurons, 168 attention heads). During generation, α·δ_s (α=0.5) is added to these components at every step of the score stage, the music-token stage injects the mean difference over its first 128 steps, and no stage receives style text.

限制Limitations

「勝過提示詞」只在自動評審下成立。 11 位聽者、54 題完成（43 題非流行）的初步聽感測試裡，電路和提示詞都遠高於基線（Wilcoxon p<0.001），但兩者之間提示詞評分較高的有 19 題、電路 13 題、平手 11 題（p=0.64），被選為「最接近目標曲風」的次數提示詞 27、電路 14（p=0.06）。作者在正文寫「兩者都沒有顯著差異」，這沒錯，但人耳的趨勢跟自動評審是反向的。

評審不夠獨立。 兩個自動評審是 CLAP 和 Gemini 3.6 Flash，而資料集本身就是用這兩者篩出來的；作者在附錄承認 Gemini 不是完全獨立的評審。電路對 CAA 的優勢在 CLAP 下顯著（McNemar 28 對 13，p=0.03），在 Gemini 下不顯著（25 對 19，p=0.45）。

比較基準本身偏弱。 提示詞在金屬只有 17/30、funk 13/30 命中，電路的領先幾乎都來自這兩類；這比較像 YuE2 對某些曲風標籤反應不佳，提示詞措辭沒有探索。

範圍窄。 只有一個模型（YuE2）、五種曲風、15 首測試歌詞各兩組 seed；「稀疏」是相對的，每個曲風每階段仍有 560 個元件。電路並不完全複製提示詞的音樂決定：一半金屬樂譜落在 C♯m 而不是典型的 D♯m，四成 funk 和藍調保留空標籤的調性，藍調的 6/8 拍電路從來沒寫出來。

"Beating the prompt" holds only under the automatic judges. In a preliminary listening test with eleven listeners and 54 completed questions (43 non-pop), circuit and prompt are both rated far above the baseline (Wilcoxon p<0.001), but between the two the prompt is rated higher in 19 questions, the circuit in 13, with 11 ties (p=0.64), and the prompt is picked as closest to the target style 27 times against 14 (p=0.06). The paper's statement that neither difference is significant is correct, but the human trend runs opposite to the automatic judges.

The judges are not fully independent. The two automatic judges are CLAP and Gemini 3.6 Flash, and the dataset itself was filtered with both; the appendix concedes Gemini is not a fully independent judge. The circuit's edge over CAA is significant under CLAP (McNemar 28 vs 13, p=0.03) but not under Gemini (25 vs 19, p=0.45).

The baseline is weak where the gain is. The prompt hits only 17/30 for metal and 13/30 for funk, and nearly all of the circuit's lead comes from these two styles; this looks more like YuE2 responding poorly to certain style tags, and prompt wording is not explored.

Narrow scope. One model (YuE2), five styles, 15 test lyrics with two seed pairs each; "sparse" is relative, with still 560 components per style and stage. The circuit does not fully reproduce the prompt's musical decisions: half of the metal scores land in C♯m rather than the typical D♯m, four in ten funk and blues scores keep the empty tag's key, and the circuit never writes the blues prompt's 6/8 meter.

討論Discussion

主表。 測試集每個條件 150 首（15 歌詞×5 曲風×2 seed），截斷算未命中。CLAP 命中：空標籤基線 30、曲風提示詞 113、隨機電路 34、CAA 118、動態電路 133；Gemini：30、111、33、120、126。去掉本來就預設的流行，非流行四類 CLAP 下電路 103/120、提示詞 84/120；Gemini 下 101/120 對 87/120。同歌詞同 seed 配對，電路勝提示詞在 CLAP（McNemar 32 對 12，p=0.004）和 Gemini（31 對 16，p=0.04）下都顯著。隨機電路——同樣元件數、同樣注入範數——跟基線差不多，說明效果來自選中的元件，而不是注入本身；用單一位置方向或等權平均方向建的電路命中低很多，所以 KL 加權是關鍵。

對 CAA 的優勢主要在穩定。 在每一步加同一個殘差向量常讓生成崩掉：CAA 150 首截斷 23 首、電路 4 首，CAA 生成時間 177 秒、電路 84 秒；只看完整生成，CAA 命中率不比電路低。換句話說，這個方法的實際價值可能更在於「把介入限制在少數元件上比較不會弄壞生成」，而不是找到了更好的曲風方向。

因果驗證。 只把 0.23% 的 MLP 神經元歸零，輸出離曲風標籤輸出的距離就達到拿掉標籤的大約 80%；隨機元件即使範數相同也幾乎沒影響，但注意力頭歸零會不分對象地破壞輸出，所以頭的消融不能當乾淨的反向介入。配器上，電路與提示詞在 13 種樂器 CLAP 標籤上的變化相關係數 0.92–0.98，隨機電路只有 0.46–0.76。作者把方法延伸到人聲性別也有效果，但那部分只用 CLAP、一個 seed，而且方向強烈依賴「male vocal / female vocal」的措辭。

這篇真正的發現。 比起「勝過提示詞」，更站得住的是兩點：曲風主要在符號樂譜階段決定（Shapley 76%），以及曲風在樂譜裡是隨位置變化的表徵。這對 YuE2 這種「先寫譜再出聲」的混合架構特別有意義——想控制曲風，介入點應該放在譜，而不是聲學端。

Main table. Each condition has 150 test generations (15 lyrics × 5 styles × 2 seeds), with truncations counted as misses. CLAP hits: empty-tag baseline 30, style prompt 113, random circuit 34, CAA 118, dynamic circuit 133; Gemini: 30, 111, 33, 120, 126. Excluding pop, the default style, the circuit gets 103/120 on the four non-pop styles under CLAP against the prompt's 84/120, and 101/120 against 87/120 under Gemini. Pairing the same lyric and seed, the circuit beats the prompt significantly under both CLAP (McNemar 32 vs 12, p=0.004) and Gemini (31 vs 16, p=0.04). A random circuit with the same component count and injection norm stays near the baseline, so the effect comes from the selected components, not the injection itself; circuits built from single-position or equally averaged directions hit far less, so the KL weighting is key.

The edge over CAA is mainly stability. Adding the same residual vector at every step often derails generation: CAA truncates 23 of 150 against 4 for the circuit, and takes 177 s against 84 s; among complete generations CAA's hit rate is no lower. In other words, the method's practical value may lie more in "confining the intervention to a few components breaks generation less" than in having found a better style direction.

Causal checks. Zeroing only 0.23% of the MLP neurons moves the output about 80% as far from the style-tag output as removing the tag; random components barely matter even at matched norm, but zeroing attention heads damages the output indiscriminately, so head ablation is not a clean inverse intervention. For instrumentation, the circuit's and the prompt's changes across 13 CLAP instrument tags correlate at 0.92–0.98, against 0.46–0.76 for the random circuit. An extension to vocal gender also works, but it uses only CLAP and one seed, and the direction depends strongly on the "male vocal / female vocal" wording.

What the paper really shows. More solid than "beating the prompt" are two findings: style is decided mainly in the symbolic score stage (Shapley 76%), and style in the score is a representation that changes with position. This matters especially for hybrid score-then-audio architectures such as YuE2: to control style, the intervention belongs in the score, not at the acoustic end.
