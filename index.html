MuLaCover: Flexible Cover Song Generation via Symbolic Tonal Control

MuLaCover：把來源曲的主旋律＋和弦「導奏譜」正規化到 120 BPM，用 tanh 閘控交叉注意力注入 3B 的 HeartMuLa——不必逐格對齊，可以改速度、改結構、只給一段副歌也能翻唱整首

Lynn Yi, Zihan Xiong, Junjie Cao, Xiaolong Weng, Yingzhe Ma, Gus Xia, Jinliang Liu, Yuxin Xie, Xianwei Zhuang, Yuguo Yin, Qiao Jin, Gongxi Zhu, Jiayu Wang, Junjie Liang, Qin Yue, Ziyu Wang, Dading Chong, Meng Cao, Jiahuan Zhou, Dongchao Yang
arXiv:2610.26684 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-19
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：MuLaCover 把翻唱定義成「保留主旋律與和弦、其他都重做」，用轉譜出來、時間軸統一拉到 120 BPM 的導奏譜（lead sheet）去控制 3B 參數的 HeartMuLa，因此不必跟來源逐格對齊，也能只給第一段副歌就生成整首。值得讀的是它在只給片段的任務上，翻唱辨識 top-1 從開源對手的 0.52 拉到 0.70；要保留的是：測試的 32 首來源曲全是 Suno-v5.5 生成的，翻唱辨識只在 32 首裡挑、接近天花板（0.97），聽測裡「保留原曲辨識度」顯著輸 Suno（3.45 對 3.81），而且導奏譜只要 5% 音符音高被打亂，top-1 就從 0.97 掉到 0.63。Why this one: MuLaCover defines a cover as 'keep the melody and chords, redo everything else', and controls the 3B-parameter HeartMuLa with a transcribed lead sheet whose timeline is normalized to 120 BPM, so it needs no frame-level alignment with the source and can generate a whole song from just the first chorus. It is worth reading because on that partial-reference task it lifts cover-identification top-1 from the open-source rivals' 0.52 to 0.70; the caveats are that all 32 test source songs were generated by Suno-v5.5, cover identification ranks among only those 32 songs and sits near ceiling (0.97), the listening study rates identity preservation significantly below Suno (3.45 vs 3.81), and shifting the pitch of just 5% of lead-sheet notes drops top-1 from 0.97 to 0.63.

動機Motivation

翻唱辨識（cover song identification, CSI）研究了二十年，結論很一致：不同版本之間調性、速度、配器、音色、結構甚至歌詞都可能變，真正穩定的是調性內容——主旋律和和弦進行。作者把這個「不變／可變」的分法反過來用在生成上：既然 CSI 把調性內容當作版本的身分，翻唱生成就應該明確控制調性內容，其餘交給文字提示（歌詞、風格標籤）。

問題出在現有的音訊翻唱系統怎麼表示調性內容。SongEcho 用來源人聲的逐格 F0 曲線，ACE-Step 1.5 用量化後的音訊潛在表示，兩者都跟目標音訊同取樣率。這等於強制翻唱和原曲逐格對齊：不能換速度、不能重排段落，也不能只拿一段主歌或副歌當參考。MuLaCover 想解掉的就是這個綁定。

Two decades of cover song identification (CSI) research agree on one point: across versions, key, tempo, instrumentation, timbre, structure and even lyrics can change, and what stays stable is the tonal content, meaning the melody and the chord progression. The authors turn this invariant/variant split around for generation: if CSI treats tonal content as a version's identity, cover generation should control tonal content explicitly and leave the rest to text prompts (lyrics and style tags).

The problem lies in how existing audio cover systems represent tonal content. SongEcho uses the frame-level F0 contour of the source vocal, and ACE-Step 1.5 uses quantized audio latents, both sampled at the target audio's rate. That forces the cover into frame-by-frame alignment with the original: no tempo change, no rearranged sections, and no way to use only a verse or chorus as reference. MuLaCover aims to remove that binding.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文直接比較的是三個系統。SongEcho（Li 等，ICLR 2026）用逐元素線性調變把來源 F0 注入生成模型，是逐格對齊的代表；ACE-Step 1.5（Gong 等，2026）是大型開源音樂基礎模型，翻唱時以來源的音訊潛在表示當條件；Suno-v5.5 是閉源商用系統。在注入方式上，作者對照的是 Coco-Mulla（Lin 等，2023，論文引用為 content-based controls）和 MusiConGen（Lan 等，2024）用的前綴微調（prefix tuning）。符號式翻唱方面，論文點了 Wang 等（2022）與 BOSSA 這類鋼琴翻唱，但範圍窄。

論文沒有引用、但最該拿來比的有兩個。一是 Meta 的 JASCO（Tal 等，2024）：它也以和弦符號與旋律音高為條件生成音樂，只是這些條件跟輸出逐格對齊；MuLaCover 真正新的那一步，是先把導奏譜正規化到固定 BPM 再用交叉注意力讀，讓對齊交給注意力去學。二是 GACA 本身：tanh 閘控、輸出投影零初始化、讓模型在訓練開始時完全等於原本骨幹的交叉注意力，幾乎就是 DeepMind Flamingo（Alayrac 等，2022）的 gated cross-attention，也跟 ControlNet（Zhang 等，2023）的零初始化同一個思路；差別只在閘門是逐位置、逐維度由另一個交叉注意力算出，而不是 Flamingo 的單一可學純量。

The paper compares directly against three systems. SongEcho (Li et al., ICLR 2026) injects source F0 into a generator through element-wise linear modulation and represents frame-aligned conditioning; ACE-Step 1.5 (Gong et al., 2026) is a large open-source music foundation model that conditions covers on the source's audio latents; Suno-v5.5 is a closed commercial system. For the injection mechanism, the authors contrast their design with the prefix tuning used by Coco-Mulla (Lin et al., 2023, cited as content-based controls) and MusiConGen (Lan et al., 2024). On the symbolic side they mention piano-cover systems such as Wang et al. (2022) and BOSSA, which are narrow in scope.

Two things the paper does not cite are the most useful comparisons. One is Meta's JASCO (Tal et al., 2024), which also conditions music generation on chord symbols and melody pitch, but with those conditions frame-aligned to the output; MuLaCover's genuinely new step is normalizing the lead sheet to a fixed BPM and reading it with cross-attention, so alignment is left to attention to learn. The other is GACA itself: tanh gating, a zero-initialized output projection, and a model that equals the original backbone at the start of training is almost exactly the gated cross-attention of DeepMind's Flamingo (Alayrac et al., 2022), and shares ControlNet's (Zhang et al., 2023) zero-initialization idea. The difference is only that the gate is computed per position and per dimension by another cross-attention rather than being Flamingo's single learned scalar.

方法Method

條件訊號。 來源曲先用 YourMT3+ 轉出人聲旋律、用 Jiang 等（2019）的大詞彙和弦轉譜器轉出和弦，估出 BPM 後把整份導奏譜時間伸縮到 120 BPM，再量化到十六分音符。旋律編成 T×256 的二值鋼琴捲簾（128 個 MIDI 音高 × 起音／延續），和弦編成 T×12 的音級捲簾，各自線性投影到 D/2 維後串接成條件序列 c。

注入方式（GACA）。 骨幹是 3B 的 HeartMuLa（28 層 decoder-only Transformer，本身就吃歌詞與風格兩個提示）。只在最後 8 層插入模組：每層先用一個自注意力把 c 精煉成 c_l（並把上一層的 c_{l-1} 加進來），套上 RoPE 後當交叉注意力的鍵與值，以骨幹的隱藏狀態為查詢；更新量乘上一個由另一個交叉注意力算出、經 tanh 限制在 (−1, 1) 的逐位置逐維度閘門，再加回殘差。閘門的輸出投影零初始化，所以訓練開始時模型就是原本的 HeartMuLa。只要在 c 上做注意力遮罩，就能只保留某段（outpainting）或挖掉某段（inpainting）。

三階段課程。 第一階段用 30 秒片段暖身，第二階段用整首歌，兩者的條件、歌詞、風格與目標音訊都來自同一版本，骨幹凍結、只訓練約 3 億參數的新模組。第三階段「跨版本微調」：同一首歌取兩個不同版本，c 來自版本 i，歌詞、風格與目標音訊來自版本 j，並在最後 8 層加 LoRA（可訓練約 4 億參數）。

Conditioning signal. The vocal melody of the source is transcribed with YourMT3+ and the chords with the large-vocabulary chord transcriber of Jiang et al. (2019); after estimating BPM, the whole lead sheet is time-stretched to 120 BPM and quantized to sixteenth notes. The melody becomes a T×256 binary piano roll (128 MIDI pitches × onset/sustain) and the chords a T×12 pitch-class roll; each is linearly projected to D/2 dimensions and concatenated into the condition sequence c.

Injection (GACA). The backbone is the 3B HeartMuLa (a 28-layer decoder-only Transformer that already takes lyrics and style prompts). Modules go into the last 8 layers only: each layer refines c into c_l with a self-attention (adding the previous layer's c_{l-1}), applies RoPE, and uses it as keys and values for a cross-attention queried by the backbone's hidden states. The update is multiplied by a per-position, per-dimension gate computed by another cross-attention and bounded to (−1, 1) by tanh, then added back residually. The gate's output projection is zero-initialized, so at the start of training the model is exactly the original HeartMuLa. Attention masks on c keep only one span (outpainting) or remove one span (inpainting).

Three-stage curriculum. Stage 1 warms up on 30-second clips and Stage 2 trains on full songs; in both, the condition, lyrics, style and target audio come from the same rendition, the backbone is frozen, and only about 300M new parameters are trained. Stage 3 is cross-cover fine-tuning: for two different renditions of one song, c comes from rendition i while lyrics, style and target audio come from rendition j, with LoRA added to the last 8 layers (about 400M trainable parameters).

限制Limitations

資料與測試幾乎全是合成的。 約 20 萬首訓練曲「來自音樂生成服務商」，第三階段的翻唱組也是讓服務商把 1,250 首來源曲各生成 7 個風格版本（每次回兩首，共 2 萬首，篩掉調性不一致的配對後約 13 萬對）。主測試的 32 首來源曲則是 Suno-v5.5 生成的，而 Suno-v5.5 本身就是對照組之一。真人錄音只在附錄 G 測了 8 首（4 中 4 英），作者自己也說規模小。

翻唱辨識指標接近天花板。 CoverHunter 只在 32 首來源曲裡排序（隨機猜是 1/32），全曲任務 MuLaCover 0.97、Suno 0.98、ACE-Step 0.95，差距小到看不出排序；附錄 G 的 8 首真人錄音更是三個系統都在 0.97–0.98。

對轉譜錯誤很敏感。 附錄 I 隨機讓 5% 音符音高偏移 ±3 半音，top-1 就從 0.97 掉到 0.63；10% 掉到 0.42，刪除與偏移各 10% 時只剩 0.28。可是條件本來就靠 YourMT3+ 自動轉譜，真實歌曲的轉譜錯誤率很可能高於 5%。附錄 A 也提到模型對整體調性敏感，換調的配對只能丟掉，所以它其實不會做「換調翻唱」。

主觀評估單薄。 聽測收到 34 份、保留 22 人、95 個完整區塊，只用 8 首歌；專家訪談只有一位專家、一種曲風（R&B）、一首歌，結論還交給語言模型摘要，結果是 Suno 第一、MuLaCover 第二。消融全在 7 萬首的縮小版上做，單次訓練、沒有變異數。

Data and test are almost all synthetic. About 200,000 training songs come 'from music generation providers', and the Stage 3 cover groups were made by asking providers to render 7 stylistic versions of each of 1,250 source songs (two songs per request, 20,000 in total, about 130,000 pairs after dropping key-mismatched pairs). The 32 main test sources were generated by Suno-v5.5, which is itself one of the baselines. Human recordings appear only in Appendix G, with 8 songs (4 Chinese, 4 English), which the authors themselves call small.

The cover-identification metric is near ceiling. CoverHunter ranks among only the 32 source songs (chance is 1/32); on the full-song task MuLaCover scores 0.97, Suno 0.98 and ACE-Step 0.95, too close to order them. On the 8 human recordings in Appendix G all three systems sit at 0.97–0.98.

Sensitive to transcription errors. In Appendix I, shifting the pitch of a random 5% of notes by up to ±3 semitones drops top-1 from 0.97 to 0.63; at 10% it falls to 0.42, and with 10% deletions plus 10% shifts it is 0.28. Yet the condition relies on automatic YourMT3+ transcription, whose error rate on real songs is plausibly above 5%. Appendix A also notes the model is sensitive to global key and key-changed pairs had to be discarded, so it does not really do key-changing covers.

Thin subjective evaluation. The listening study received 34 submissions and kept 22 participants and 95 complete blocks over just 8 songs; the expert interview is one expert, one genre (R&B) and one song, summarized by a language model, and it ranks Suno first and MuLaCover second. All ablations are on a 70k-song reduced model, single runs, with no variance.

討論Discussion

全曲任務：跟 Suno 互有勝負，不是全面打平。 客觀指標上 MuLaCover 的音質分數跟 Suno 差不多（AudioBox PQ 8.34 對 8.30），歌詞音素錯誤率 0.13 對 0.11，略輸；但風格貼合度 MuQ-Sim 是 0.41 對 0.50，明顯輸。論文強調 MuLaCover 的 MuQ-Dist（跟原曲的風格距離）最高（0.48 對 Suno 0.33），可是這個值在導奏譜被加雜訊時也會從 0.48 升到 0.58，SongEcho 在片段任務上幾乎失去辨識度時也有 0.63——作者自己在 SongEcho 那裡承認這代表「漂走」，所以它不能單獨當成風格控制好的證據。

聽測上，風格控制 3.71 對 3.72、創意 3.65 對 3.51，打平或略勝；保留原曲辨識度 3.45 對 3.81，顯著輸；整體音樂性 3.75 對 3.88。摘要說「與 Suno-v5.5 具競爭力」是合理的措辭，「全面相當」就說過頭了。

片段任務才是它的主場。 只給第一段副歌、前奏到副歌前，或挖掉副歌時，MuLaCover 的平均 top-1 是 0.70，ACE-Step 0.52、SongEcho 0.41；歌詞錯誤率 0.12 對 0.36 與 0.55。這是逐格對齊系統在結構上做不到的事，Suno 也因為不支援而缺席，所以這裡的優勢比全曲任務紮實。

消融有幾個值得記的數字（縮小版，完整模型 top-1 0.98）：拿掉第一階段暖身掉到 0.11，換成前綴微調掉到 0.09、歌詞錯誤率從 0.14 升到 0.43；拿掉和弦只剩 0.46，換成 SongEcho 式的 F0 只剩 0.33；拿掉 BPM 正規化剩 0.73；拿掉閘門剩 0.86。反倒是第三階段跨版本配對的貢獻比預期小：改回同版本配對後 top-1 一樣是 0.98，只有 MuQ-Sim 從 0.38 掉到 0.35。換句話說，「調性內容要用符號＋和弦、而且要鬆開時間對齊」這個主張證據最強；「跨版本資料是缺少的監督訊號」的證據其實只有 0.03 的風格分數差。

Full-song task: wins and losses against Suno, not across-the-board parity. On objective metrics MuLaCover's audio quality is close to Suno's (AudioBox PQ 8.34 vs 8.30) and its phoneme error rate is slightly worse (0.13 vs 0.11), but its style fit MuQ-Sim is clearly lower (0.41 vs 0.50). The paper stresses that MuLaCover has the highest MuQ-Dist (style distance from the source, 0.48 vs Suno's 0.33), yet this value also rises from 0.48 to 0.58 when the lead sheet is corrupted, and SongEcho reaches 0.63 on the partial task while nearly losing identifiability, which the authors themselves read as drift there, so it cannot stand alone as evidence of good style control.

In the listening study, style control is 3.71 vs 3.72 and creativeness 3.65 vs 3.51, a tie or slight edge; identity preservation is 3.45 vs 3.81, significantly worse; overall musicality is 3.75 vs 3.88. The abstract's 'competitive with Suno-v5.5' is fair wording; 'on par across the board' would overstate it.

The partial-reference task is where it shines. Given only the first chorus, the intro up to the chorus, or everything but the chorus, MuLaCover's mean top-1 is 0.70 against 0.52 for ACE-Step and 0.41 for SongEcho, with phoneme error rates of 0.12 vs 0.36 and 0.55. Frame-aligned systems structurally cannot do this, and Suno is absent because it lacks the feature, so this advantage is more solid than the full-song one.

A few ablation numbers worth remembering (reduced model, full top-1 0.98): removing the Stage 1 warmup drops it to 0.11, and prefix tuning drops it to 0.09 while the phoneme error rate rises from 0.14 to 0.43; dropping chords leaves 0.46 and SongEcho-style F0 leaves 0.33; removing BPM normalization leaves 0.73 and removing the gate 0.86. The Stage 3 cross-cover pairing contributes less than expected: reverting to same-rendition pairs keeps top-1 at 0.98 and only lowers MuQ-Sim from 0.38 to 0.35. In other words, the claim that tonal content should be symbolic, include chords and loosen temporal alignment is the best supported; the claim that cross-version data is the missing supervision rests on a 0.03 style-score gap.

Structural Context from Audio and Lyric Evidence for Music Structure Analysis Using the New SheetSage-MSA Dataset

SCALE 與 SheetSage-MSA：從 HookTheory 使用者標註整理出 9,010 首、82.5 小時的部分標註段落資料集，邊界偵測與段落功能標註各走一條 MuQ 音訊路徑，再用自動轉寫歌詞的重複程度修正副歌判斷

Hongbing Li, Yaolong Ju
arXiv:2610.26253 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-19
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Li 與 Ju 做了兩件事：把 SheetSage／HookTheory 的使用者段落標註整理成 9,010 首歌、13,880 段的部分標註資料集，並提出 SCALE——邊界偵測與功能標註各用一條 MuQ 路徑，另以自動轉寫歌詞的重複度只修正副歌分數。值得讀的是在相同訓練資料下，它在 SongFormBench-HX 的 0.5 秒邊界 F 值比 SongFormer 高 2.7 點（0.730 對 0.703）；要保留的是摘要的 3.5 點是拿 SongFormer 自己論文的數字比，新資料集對邊界幾乎沒幫助（+0.1 點），而消融裡「拿掉整首歌的全域脈絡」邊界分數完全沒變。Why this one: Li and Ju do two things: they turn the SheetSage/HookTheory user section annotations into a partially labeled dataset of 13,880 sections from 9,010 songs, and they propose SCALE, which gives boundary detection and functional labeling separate MuQ pathways and uses the recurrence of automatically transcribed lyrics to adjust only the chorus score. It is worth reading because with the same training data its 0.5-second boundary F-measure on SongFormBench-HX beats SongFormer by 2.7 points (0.730 vs 0.703); the caveats are that the abstract's 3.5 points compares against numbers from SongFormer's own paper, the new dataset barely helps boundaries (+0.1 point), and in the ablation removing the full-song global context leaves boundary scores unchanged.

動機Motivation

音樂結構分析（music structure analysis, MSA）要把一首歌切成不重疊的段落（邊界偵測），再替每段標上前奏、主歌、副歌、橋段、尾奏等功能（功能標註）。作者點出三個卡點。第一，細緻標註太少：常用的 HarmonixSet 只有 912 首，扣掉驗證與測試後能拿來訓練的更少。第二，近年的系統（All-In-One、LinkSeg、SongFormer）多半讓兩個子任務共用一個編碼器，但邊界要的是精確時間與局部變化，功能標註要的是長距離脈絡，兩者需求不同。第三，端到端系統幾乎只聽音訊，忽略歌詞這種很直接的結構線索：主歌通常帶新詞，副歌常重複同樣的句子。

Music structure analysis (MSA) splits a song into non-overlapping sections (boundary detection) and labels each with a function such as intro, verse, chorus, bridge or outro (functional labeling). The authors name three sticking points. First, fine-grained annotations are scarce: the widely used HarmonixSet has only 912 songs, leaving even fewer for training after validation and test splits. Second, recent systems (All-In-One, LinkSeg, SongFormer) mostly share one encoder between the two subtasks, although boundaries need precise timing and local change while labels need long-range context. Third, end-to-end systems listen almost only to audio and ignore lyrics, a direct structural cue: verses usually bring new text and choruses often repeat the same lines.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名的主要比較對象是 SongFormer（Hao 等，2025），它融合短窗與長窗的自監督表徵，是目前 SongFormBench 上的最佳系統；另外兩個基線是 All-In-One（Kim 與 Nam，2023，在分離後的音軌上用鄰域注意力同時做節拍與結構）和 LinkSeg（Buisson、McFee 與 Essid，2024，用成對連結預測加圖注意力網路）。功能類別沿用 Wang、Hung 與 Smith（2022）的七類分法。歌詞方面，作者引了 ALMA-Chor（Bao 等，ICASSP 2026），它用音訊與歌詞對齊加 Mamba 做副歌偵測，但只偵測副歌、不做完整結構。

論文沒有提、但其實最相近的有兩條線。一是「用歌詞重複找結構」本身：Watanabe 等（2016）和 Fell 等（COLING 2018）都在歌詞行之間算自相似矩陣來切分歌詞段落，SCALE 的「跟非相鄰段落比相似度」就是同一個想法，只是改用 Longformer 嵌入並把結果加到副歌 logit 上。二是「邊界與標註分開做」：MSAF（Nieto 與 Bello，2016）時代的經典流程本來就是先用一套演算法（例如 Foote 的 novelty 曲線）找邊界、再用另一套做標註，SCALE 的雙路徑其實是把那個兩段式拆分搬回深度學習架構裡。

The main comparison is SongFormer (Hao et al., 2025), which fuses short- and long-window self-supervised representations and is the current best system on SongFormBench; the other baselines are All-In-One (Kim and Nam, 2023, joint beat and structure analysis with neighborhood attention on demixed stems) and LinkSeg (Buisson, McFee and Essid, 2024, pairwise link prediction with a graph attention network). The function classes follow the seven-class taxonomy of Wang, Hung and Smith (2022). For lyrics, the authors cite ALMA-Chor (Bao et al., ICASSP 2026), which detects choruses with audio–lyric alignment and Mamba, but only choruses, not full structure.

Two lines the paper does not mention are actually closest. One is finding structure from lyric repetition itself: Watanabe et al. (2016) and Fell et al. (COLING 2018) both compute self-similarity matrices between lyric lines to segment lyrics, and SCALE's 'compare with non-adjacent segments' is the same idea, swapped to Longformer embeddings and added to the chorus logit. The other is doing boundaries and labels separately: the classic pipelines of the MSAF era (Nieto and Bello, 2016) already found boundaries with one algorithm (such as Foote's novelty curve) and labeled with another, so SCALE's two pathways effectively bring that two-stage split back into a deep architecture.

方法Method

資料集。 SheetSage 釋出的 HookTheory 資料裡，每筆是一段使用者標註的樂段，附 YouTube 連結與用 madmom 修正過的節拍對齊。26,175 段中有 20,072 段抓得到音訊，其餘 6,103 段因影片下架或版權限制而缺。作者把同一首歌的多個片段合併、以第一與最後一個節拍當片段起訖、把功能名稱對應到七類（前奏、主歌、導歌、副歌、橋段、器樂、尾奏），丟掉缺漏、複合或不支援的名稱，最後得到 9,010 首、13,880 段、約 82.5 小時。標註是「部分的」：沒標到的區域在損失裡直接遮掉，授權為 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0。

模型。 音訊特徵來自凍結的 MuQ，包含 30 秒窗的局部表徵與最長 420 秒的整首表徵。邊界路徑先用可學閘門融合兩種表徵，降到約 8.33 Hz 後過四層 BiMamba（雙向比較候選邊界前後的內容，並有輔助邊界頭），再平均池化、過四層 Transformer，最後用兩個交叉注意力讀整首表徵。功能路徑完全獨立，用四層帶 RoPE 的 Transformer 輸出逐格類別。

歌詞路徑用 SoulX-Singer 的前處理流程偵測人聲並轉寫歌詞，SaT 分行，Longformer（只微調最後兩層）編碼，再算跟非相鄰段落的相似度與相對位置特徵，經兩層 Transformer 給每段一個副歌 logit，校準後只加到副歌類別上，不影響其他類別與邊界。解碼時對邊界機率做峰值挑選（門檻 0.35、間隔至少 2 秒），段內平均功能 logit 取最高類。

Dataset. In the HookTheory data released with SheetSage, each entry is a user-annotated excerpt with a YouTube link and a madmom-refined beat alignment. Audio was available for 20,072 of 26,175 excerpts; the other 6,103 were missing because videos were removed or copyright-restricted. The authors merge excerpts of the same song, use the first and last beats as excerpt bounds, map function names to seven classes (intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, instrumental, outro), and drop missing, composite or unsupported names, ending with 13,880 sections from 9,010 songs, about 82.5 hours. Annotations are partial: unlabeled regions are masked out of the losses. The license is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Model. Audio features come from a frozen MuQ, both local 30-second-window representations and full-song ones of up to 420 seconds. The boundary path fuses the two with a learned gate, downsamples to about 8.33 Hz, runs four BiMamba blocks (comparing content before and after a candidate boundary, with an auxiliary boundary head), then average-pools, applies a four-layer Transformer and reads the full-song representation through two cross-attention blocks. The function path is fully separate, a four-layer RoPE Transformer producing frame-level classes.

The lyrics path detects vocals and transcribes lyrics with the SoulX-Singer preprocessing pipeline, splits lines with SaT, encodes with Longformer (last two layers fine-tuned), computes similarity to non-adjacent segments plus relative-position features, and passes them through a two-layer Transformer to get one chorus logit per segment; after calibration it is added only to the chorus class and touches neither other classes nor boundaries. Decoding peak-picks boundary probabilities (threshold 0.35, at least 2 seconds apart) and takes the top class of the averaged function logits within each segment.

限制Limitations

摘要的數字混了資料與模型兩個因素。 摘要說 SCALE 比 SongFormer 高 3.5（HR.5F）與 4.2（HR3F）點、ACC 高 0.9 點，這是把「用 HX＋SheetSage-MSA 訓練的 SCALE」跟「SongFormer 論文自己報告的數字」比；兩者訓練資料不同。真正同資料的比較是表 2 上半：HR.5F 0.730 對 0.703（+2.7）、HR3F 0.821 對 0.784（+3.7）、ACC 0.798 對 0.795（+0.3）。ACC 的 0.3 點在 200 首測試歌、單次訓練下很難說有差。另外正文說「Table 3 reports results under two experimental settings」，其實應是表 2。

新資料集只幫了功能標註。 加入 SheetSage-MSA 後 ACC 從 0.798 升到 0.816（+1.8），但 HR.5F 只從 0.730 到 0.731、HR3F 從 0.821 到 0.822。這跟資料的性質一致：HookTheory 片段是使用者挑的局部段落，起訖靠節拍對齊推回，邊界本來就不精確，作者在結論也承認不同資料集對邊界放在人聲起點還是小節第一拍的慣例不一致。

其他。 測試時的歌詞來自自動轉寫，論文沒有報告轉寫品質；評估時導歌被併進主歌，所以資料集裡辛苦保留的導歌類別在評分上看不到。資料集靠 YouTube 連結取得音訊，已經有 23% 的片段抓不到，未來只會更多。全文只有一個測試集、沒有多次訓練的變異數。

The abstract's numbers mix data and model. The abstract says SCALE beats SongFormer by 3.5 (HR.5F) and 4.2 (HR3F) points and by 0.9 points in ACC, but that compares SCALE trained on HX plus SheetSage-MSA against the numbers reported in SongFormer's own paper, with different training data. The true same-data comparison is the upper half of Table 2: HR.5F 0.730 vs 0.703 (+2.7), HR3F 0.821 vs 0.784 (+3.7), ACC 0.798 vs 0.795 (+0.3). A 0.3-point ACC difference on 200 test songs with a single run is hard to call real. The text also says 'Table 3 reports results under two experimental settings' when it means Table 2.

The new dataset only helps labeling. Adding SheetSage-MSA raises ACC from 0.798 to 0.816 (+1.8), but HR.5F only goes from 0.730 to 0.731 and HR3F from 0.821 to 0.822. That fits the data: HookTheory excerpts are partial regions chosen by users, with bounds recovered from beat alignment, so boundaries are imprecise to begin with, and the authors concede in the conclusion that datasets disagree on whether a boundary sits at the vocal onset or the first beat of the bar.

Other points. Test-time lyrics come from automatic transcription, and the paper reports no transcription quality; pre-chorus is mapped to verse at evaluation, so the pre-chorus class the dataset keeps is invisible in scoring. The dataset relies on YouTube links for audio, 23% of excerpts are already unretrievable, and that share will only grow. There is one test set and no variance across training runs.

討論Discussion

消融（表 3，都以完整的 HX＋SheetSage-MSA 模型為基準：ACC 0.816、HR.5F 0.731、HR3F 0.822）把各元件的份量講得很清楚。拿掉 BiMamba 邊界分數掉最多：HR.5F 0.671（−6.0）、HR3F 0.753（−6.9）。拿掉功能路徑的 RoPE，ACC 掉到 0.781（−3.5）。改成共用功能編碼器，三個指標都掉：ACC −1.1、HR.5F −2.5、HR3F −3.2，這是「兩個任務要分開」這個主張最直接的證據。

但有兩個結果跟論文的敘事不太合。第一，「Local Boundary Only」拿掉邊界路徑裡的整首脈絡與交叉注意力，HR.5F 0.730、HR3F 0.822，跟完整模型幾乎一樣；ACC 掉到 0.807 只是因為解碼時功能是在預測段落內平均，邊界小變動會連帶影響。也就是說，論文花了不少篇幅描述的全域脈絡，對邊界本身沒有可量測的貢獻。第二，拿掉歌詞路徑只讓 ACC 從 0.816 掉到 0.808（−0.8），邊界不動，這在設計上是預期的（殘差只加在副歌上），但也說明歌詞目前只是小幅修正。

整體看，這篇 4 頁短文最站得住的貢獻是資料集與「邊界路徑用 BiMamba、任務分開」這兩點；歌詞與全域脈絡的部分還需要更強的證據，例如報告副歌類別的單獨準確率，或在歌詞轉寫錯誤較多的歌上做分析。

The ablation (Table 3, all against the full HX plus SheetSage-MSA model: ACC 0.816, HR.5F 0.731, HR3F 0.822) makes each component's weight clear. Removing BiMamba hurts boundaries most: HR.5F 0.671 (−6.0) and HR3F 0.753 (−6.9). Removing RoPE from the function path drops ACC to 0.781 (−3.5). Switching to a shared function encoder lowers all three metrics, ACC by 1.1, HR.5F by 2.5 and HR3F by 3.2, the most direct evidence for the claim that the two tasks should be separated.

Two results fit the narrative less well. First, 'Local Boundary Only', which removes the full-song context and cross-attention from the boundary path, scores HR.5F 0.730 and HR3F 0.822, essentially the full model; ACC falls to 0.807 only because labels are averaged within predicted segments at decoding, so small boundary changes spill over. In other words, the global context the paper spends considerable space describing makes no measurable contribution to boundaries themselves. Second, removing the lyrics path lowers ACC only from 0.816 to 0.808 (−0.8) and leaves boundaries untouched. That is expected by design (the residual touches only the chorus), but it also shows lyrics are currently a small correction.

Overall, the best-supported contributions of this 4-page short paper are the dataset and the 'BiMamba boundary path with separated tasks' design; the lyrics and global-context parts need stronger evidence, such as chorus-class accuracy reported separately, or an analysis on songs with more lyric transcription errors.

Whistle and Wrong Lyrics: Synthetic Queries Are Not Enough for Real-World Query-by-Melody

吹口哨與唱錯詞：三代合成查詢（物理渲染、ElevenLabs 條件生成、開源歌聲合成加真人誤差模型）都讓合成測試變好，卻沒有一代讓真人口哨的哼唱檢索進步——一篇把負面結果與選模陷阱完整攤開的單人研究

為什麼挑這篇：獨立研究者 Rivolta 用一個 520 萬參數、只看去調性音高曲線的旋律編碼器做「吹口哨或唱錯詞也能找歌」，並造了三代合成查詢去訓練。值得讀的是它誠實的負面結果：合成測試的 top-1 從 0.180 升到 0.251（口哨），但真人口哨上反而 210 對 214（共 339 筆）；另外還記錄了兩個很實用的陷阱——拿測試曲目的真人口哨訓練會讓沒見過的 2 首從 53 掉到 41（共 90 筆），以及已飽和的選模指標會默默選回起始權重。要保留的是真人評估只有 8 首歌、比的是別人的哼唱而非原曲錄音，「唱錯詞」完全沒有真人測試。Why this one: Independent researcher Rivolta builds whistle and wrong-lyrics song search on a 5.2M-parameter melody encoder that sees only a key-normalized pitch contour, and trains it with three generations of synthetic queries. It is worth reading for its honest negative result: synthetic-test top-1 rises from 0.180 to 0.251 (whistles), yet on real whistles it is 210 vs 214 of 339; it also documents two practical pitfalls, namely that training on real whistles of the test tunes drops the 2 unseen tunes from 53 to 41 of 90, and that a saturated selection metric silently picks the starting weights. The caveats are that the real evaluation covers only 8 songs, scores against other people's hums rather than the song recordings, and has no real wrong-lyrics test at all.

動機Motivation

歌卡在腦子裡、卻想不起歌名，是很常見的經驗。Shazam 這類辨識要原曲正在播放才有用，哼唱搜尋（query by humming, QbH）才是對的工具。但人找歌不只會哼：也會吹口哨，或隨便編詞唱。Google 的 Hum to Search 部落格就提過，主要用唱歌片段訓練的模型「對唱歌夠好，對哼與口哨不行」；公開的 QbH 研究（CHAD、ByteHum）也只評估哼與唱。作者找不到任何把口哨或唱錯詞的查詢跟原曲錄音配對的公開資料集。

口哨難在它是不同的聲源：近乎純音，比哼唱高約三個八度，人聲音高模型常把它報低一個八度，而且人吹口哨時會把旋律音域壓縮。唱錯詞的問題則是：頻譜模型可能學會用歌詞與音色，而錯詞查詢恰好不共享這些；只看旋律的模型天生不受影響，但編出來的詞會改變有聲／無聲的節奏，讓音高追蹤出現斷點與毛刺。

Having a song stuck in your head without remembering its name is a common experience. Recognition apps like Shazam need the recording to be playing, so query by humming (QbH) is the right tool. But people do not only hum: they also whistle, or sing with made-up words. Google's Hum to Search blog noted that a model trained mostly on sung clips 'worked well enough for people singing, but not for people humming or whistling', and the open QbH literature (CHAD, ByteHum) evaluates only hums and sung queries. The author found no public dataset pairing whistled or wrong-lyrics queries with song recordings.

Whistles are hard because they are a different source: a near-pure tone about three octaves above a hum, which vocal pitch models often report an octave low, and people compress the tune's range when whistling. The wrong-lyrics problem is that a spectrogram model may learn to use lyrics and timbre, which a wrong-lyrics query does not share; a melody-only model is immune by design, but made-up words change the voiced/unvoiced pattern and create gaps and glitches in pitch tracking.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文直接比較或引用的先前工作有：CHAD（Amatov 等，2023），用半監督流程收集翻唱片段與群眾外包哼唱，以 CREPE 音高活化訓練 ResNet18，在 MIR-QBSH 上 top-10 0.921，並明說「純哼唱搜尋需要加入一部分真人哼唱資料」——這篇的結論就是它在口哨上的翻版；ByteHum（Du 等，ICASSP 2024），用分離後的翻唱曲弱監督訓練 CNN 並做無監督領域適應（作者坦承沒讀到全文，不引用其數字）；Google Hum to Search，用 SPICE 音高生成合成哼唱；以及 Shen 與 Lee 的「Whistle for Music」，把口哨轉成 MIDI 再做近似字串比對。另外也用 MERT-v1-95M 當頻譜式基線。

論文沒有談到、但最該放在一起看的有兩個。一是 Salamon、Serrà 與 Gómez（2013）的旋律輪廓檢索：從混音中抽人聲主旋律、去調性後跟查詢比對，正是這篇「htdemucs 分離人聲＋RMVPE 抽 F0＋去調性編碼」流程的方法祖先，但論文只把它引為 MTG-QBH 資料集的出處。二是機器人領域的 domain randomization（Tobin 等，2017）：用參數隨機化的模擬器生成訓練資料、期待轉移到真實世界。這篇第一代「物理渲染＋人為誤差隨機化」就是這個做法，而它失敗的方式——合成上大進步、真實上不動——正是 sim-to-real 文獻裡最典型的失敗模式。

The prior work compared or cited includes CHAD (Amatov et al., 2023), which collects cover fragments with a semi-supervised pipeline plus crowdsourced hums, trains ResNet18 on CREPE pitch activations, reaches top-10 0.921 on MIR-QBSH, and states that 'the pure search-by-humming setup requires adding a portion of real humming data', of which this paper's conclusion is the whistle analogue; ByteHum (Du et al., ICASSP 2024), which trains a CNN with weak supervision from separated covers plus unsupervised domain adaptation (the author admits not having read the full text and reports none of its numbers); Google Hum to Search, which generated synthetic hums from SPICE pitch; and Shen and Lee's 'Whistle for Music', which transcribes whistles to MIDI and uses approximate string matching. MERT-v1-95M serves as a spectrogram baseline.

Two things the paper does not discuss belong right alongside it. One is the melody-contour retrieval of Salamon, Serrà and Gómez (2013): extract the predominant vocal melody from the mixture, key-normalize it and match it to the query, which is the methodological ancestor of this paper's 'htdemucs vocal stem plus RMVPE F0 plus key-normalized encoder' pipeline, yet the paper cites it only as the source of the MTG-QBH dataset. The other is domain randomization from robotics (Tobin et al., 2017): generate training data from a simulator with randomized parameters and hope it transfers to the real world. The first generation here, physical rendering plus randomized human errors, is exactly that, and the way it fails, large synthetic gains with no real movement, is the textbook sim-to-real failure mode.

方法Method

檢索系統。 哼與唱用 RMVPE 每 10 ms 抽音高並清理；口哨改用自寫的頻譜峰值追蹤器（200–6,000 Hz 取最強峰、6 dB 音調比當有聲門檻）。在 1,702 段 MLEnd 口哨上，它跟「把音訊放慢一半再跑 RMVPE」的結果在共同有聲格上中位數一致 99.7%；直接跑 RMVPE 則中位數 55.4% 的格被報低一個八度。歌曲端用 htdemucs 分出人聲再跑 RMVPE。編碼器吃減去中位數音高後的音高曲線與有聲旗標（20 ms），過卷積前端、6 層 Transformer（d=256）與遮罩注意力池化，得到 256 維向量，約 520 萬參數，查詢與歌曲共用。基線 E2b 先用 HumTrans 哼唱對 MIDI 做 InfoNCE，再用 CHAD 翻唱片段以 CLEWS 損失微調。搜尋時每首歌最多取 24 個 8 秒窗，取最高分。

第一代合成查詢。 從 11,083 首歌的人聲 F0 出發，先加入人為誤差（音符偏移 ±1–2 半音、漏音、速度伸縮 0.5–1.9、音程縮放、漂移等），再實際渲染成音訊：口哨是正弦波加 −50 到 −40 dB 的二次諧波，放進 500–4,000 Hz；唱錯詞是 16 諧波聲源加固定共振峰濾波，音節從 la、na、ma 等 8 個裡隨機抽。渲染後再用真實查詢會用的追蹤器重新抽音高，得到 86,110 條查詢曲線。先前直接改曲線、不經追蹤器的版本（D-031）沒有增益，還讓 MTG-QBH 掉 4.2 點。

第二、三代。 第二代把分離出的人聲上傳到 ElevenLabs music_v2_5 當條件，生成口哨與 la-la 唱。第三代改用開源的 YingMusic-Singer-Plus 唱部分錯詞、含糊詞或無意義音節，用在 87 段 ElevenLabs 口哨上訓練的 DDSP 合成口哨，並從 MLEnd 訓練者的 718 段錄音擬合真人旋律誤差模型（漂移、音程誤差、抖動、速度變化等）。

Retrieval system. Hums and singing get RMVPE pitch every 10 ms plus cleaning; whistles get a custom spectral-peak tracker (strongest peak in 200–6,000 Hz with a 6 dB tonal-ratio voicing gate). On 1,702 MLEnd whistles it agrees with RMVPE run on half-speed audio on a median 99.7% of jointly voiced frames, whereas plain RMVPE reports a lower octave on a median 55.4% of frames. Songs go through an htdemucs vocal stem and RMVPE. The encoder takes the median-subtracted pitch contour and a voicing flag (20 ms) through a convolutional front end, a 6-layer Transformer (d=256) and masked attention pooling into a 256-d vector, about 5.2M parameters shared by queries and songs. The baseline E2b is trained with InfoNCE on HumTrans hum–MIDI pairs and then fine-tuned with the CLEWS loss on CHAD cover windows. Search scores up to 24 windows of 8 seconds per song and keeps the best.

First-generation synthetic queries. Starting from the vocal F0 of 11,083 songs, human errors are added first (note shifts of ±1–2 semitones, dropped notes, tempo stretch 0.5–1.9, interval scaling, drift and more), then audio is actually rendered: whistles are a sinusoid with a second harmonic at −50 to −40 dB placed in 500–4,000 Hz; wrong-lyrics singing is a 16-harmonic source with fixed formant filters, with syllables drawn at random from 8 such as la, na and ma. Each rendering is re-tracked with the tracker a real query would get, giving 86,110 query contours. An earlier version that edited contours directly without a tracker (D-031) gave no gain and cost 4.2 points on MTG-QBH.

Second and third generations. The second uploads separated vocals as conditioning to ElevenLabs music_v2_5 to produce whistles and la-la singing. The third uses the open-source YingMusic-Singer-Plus to sing partially wrong, mumbled or nonsense lyrics, a DDSP whistle synthesizer trained on 87 ElevenLabs whistles, and a human melody-error model (drift, interval error, jitter, tempo variation and more) fitted on 718 recordings of MLEnd training performers.

限制Limitations

作者自己列了 8 條限制，算是寫得最完整的一節，重點如下。

真人評估跟真正的任務有距離。 MLEnd Hums and Whistles 只有哼與口哨、沒有原曲錄音，所以「真人口哨」的分數是拿口哨去比其他人哼同一首歌的平均嵌入，是 8 選 1 的封閉集合（隨機猜 0.125），339 段口哨、26 位測試者。完全保留沒訓練過的只有 2 首歌、90 段；每首歌的個別效應很大。而「唱錯詞」從頭到尾只有合成測試，沒有任何真人資料。

統計力有限。 每一輪都只有一個隨機種子，作者明說一兩點的差異分不出來。以表演者為單位重抽的信賴區間很寬：E2b 是 [0.500, 0.753]。

合成與真實的差距沒有被量測。 三代產生器失敗，但沒有分析真人查詢到底哪個性質沒被模擬到；誤差模型也沒有實際加入漏音。歌曲端只用人聲旋律，器樂勾子（例如前奏的吉他句）找不到；高於約 2 kHz 或很吵的口哨追蹤不準。最後，沒有跟 Google（無公開數字）或 ByteHum（沒讀到全文）直接比較，而模型權重與資料在撰文時仍是私有、「將會釋出」。

The author lists 8 limitations, the most thorough section of the paper; the key points follow.

The real evaluation is some distance from the actual task. MLEnd Hums and Whistles has hums and whistles but no song recordings, so the 'real whistle' score compares a whistle against the mean embedding of other people's hums of the same song, an 8-way closed set (chance 0.125) over 339 whistles from 26 test performers. Only 2 songs and 90 whistles are fully held out from training, and per-song effects are large. Wrong-lyrics singing has synthetic tests only and no real data at all.

Limited statistical power. Every round uses a single seed, and the author states that differences of one or two points are not resolved. Performer-bootstrap confidence intervals are wide: [0.500, 0.753] for E2b.

The synthetic-to-real gap is not measured. Three generators fail, but there is no analysis of which property of real queries they miss, and the error model does not actually apply note deletions. The song side uses only the vocal melody, so instrumental hooks (such as a guitar riff in an intro) are unreachable, and whistles above about 2 kHz or in heavy noise are tracked poorly. Finally, there is no direct comparison with Google (no published numbers) or ByteHum (full text not read), and the model weights and data were still private at the time of writing, 'to be released'.

討論Discussion

合成上很乾淨的進步，真實上完全不動。 在 11,050 首歌的合成測試集上，第三輪（D-042）把口哨 top-1 從 0.180 拉到 0.251、唱錯詞從 0.193 拉到 0.264，各約 +0.07；每種 4,263 筆查詢下二項標準誤約 0.006，所以這是真的增益。代價很小：HumTrans 哼唱 766 筆對降到 763 筆（共 769），MIR-QBSH 4,232 降到 4,209（共 4,431）。可是換到真人口哨，D-042 對 210 段、E2b 對 214 段（共 339），沒有轉移。作者自己的結論寫得很準：合成測試量的是「對產生器的擬合」，不是「對人的穩健性」。

兩個比結果本身更有用的教訓。 第一，D-041 加入 8 首中 6 首的真人口哨訓練，那 6 首 top-1 +0.060，沒見過的 2 首卻從 53 掉到 41（共 90）；拿掉真人配對（D-042）又回到 56。在這麼小的封閉集合裡，真人資料教會的是「這幾首歌長怎樣」，不是「口哨怎麼對應到哼唱」，混在一起算的全體分數 0.640 不能當成口哨能力進步。第二，D-040 用 HumTrans 驗證 top-1 選模，它在起點就是 0.9948、之後只會往下飄，結果「最佳檢查點」默默選回了起始權重，結果檔裡每個數字都跟 E2b 一模一樣。作者的規則是：選模指標必須能隨訓練移動，而且要追蹤目標領域。

真正影響大的是前端。 同一組 E2b 權重，口哨 top-1 用峰值追蹤器是 0.631、用放慢版 RMVPE 是 0.584、直接用 RMVPE 只有 0.159。MERT 微調後在 HumTrans 有 0.947，到 MIR-QBSH 只剩 0.169，而音高編碼器是 0.949。第三代開源查詢全部打不過 E2b：改動最少的 B 組最佳檢查點差 −0.012（信賴區間 [−0.038, +0.015]），完整訓練的 A、C 組掉約 9 點。這篇的價值不在方法，而在把「合成資料不夠」這件事用三種產生器、成對信賴區間與決策紀錄講得讓人無從質疑。

Clean synthetic progress, no real movement. On the synthetic test set with an 11,050-song gallery, round three (D-042) lifts whistle top-1 from 0.180 to 0.251 and wrong-lyrics from 0.193 to 0.264, about +0.07 each; with 4,263 queries per style the binomial standard error is about 0.006, so the gain is real. The cost is small: HumTrans hums go from 766 to 763 correct of 769, MIR-QBSH from 4,232 to 4,209 of 4,431. But on real whistles D-042 gets 210 right against 214 for E2b (of 339): no transfer. The author's own phrasing is precise: the synthetic test measures fit to the generator, not robustness to people.

Two lessons more useful than the result itself. First, D-041 adds real whistles of 6 of the 8 songs to training; those 6 gain 0.060 top-1, while the 2 unseen songs fall from 53 to 41 of 90, and removing the real pairs (D-042) brings it back to 56. In a closed set this small, real data teaches what these particular songs sound like, not how whistles map to hums, so the pooled 0.640 cannot be read as a whistling gain. Second, D-040 selected checkpoints on HumTrans validation top-1, which was 0.9948 at the start and only drifted down, so the 'best checkpoint' silently returned the starting weights, and every number in its result file equals E2b's exactly. The author's rule: a selection metric must be able to move with training and must track the target domain.

The front end is what matters most. With the same E2b weights, whistle top-1 is 0.631 with the peak tracker, 0.584 with half-speed RMVPE and only 0.159 with plain RMVPE. A fine-tuned MERT reaches 0.947 on HumTrans but only 0.169 on MIR-QBSH, against 0.949 for the pitch encoder. None of the third-generation open-source runs beats E2b: arm B, which changes the model least, has a best-checkpoint difference of −0.012 (CI [−0.038, +0.015]), and fully trained arms A and C lose about 9 points. The paper's value lies not in the method but in making 'synthetic data is not enough' hard to dispute, with three generators, paired confidence intervals and a decision log.
