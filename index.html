D-Composer: Language Modeling for Simultaneous Drum Transcription and Sound Event Decomposition

D-Composer：用語言模型把鼓組錄音拆成「鼓類＋起始時間＋單擊音色 token」的事件序列——每次擊打各配 32 個 0.1 kbps 自製編解碼器 token，同時完成轉譜與單擊音色抽取

Patrick O'Reilly, Saumya Pailwan, Annie Chu, Jason Smith, Bryan Pardo
arXiv:2610.22535 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-15
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：O'Reilly、Pardo 等人把鼓的轉譜、分離與單擊音色抽取併成一個序列生成問題：9,000 萬參數的編碼器－解碼器 Transformer 讀入鼓組錄音，對每一次擊打依序輸出鼓類、以 10 ms 量化的起始時間，以及 32 個能解碼回該擊打聲音的 token。值得讀的是同一類鼓在一段裡可以有不同音色，而且整套只用兩張 RTX 4090 訓練；要保留的是在兩個真實資料集上，五類平均 F1 仍落後專用轉譜模型 ADTOF（E-GMD 0.38 對 0.45、MDB 0.46 對 0.57，平均是我從表 3 自行算的），它勝出的合成測試集與訓練資料同源，而且全篇沒有聽測。Why this one: O'Reilly, Pardo and colleagues fold drum transcription, separation and one-shot extraction into one sequence-generation problem: a 90M-parameter encoder–decoder Transformer reads a drum recording and, for each hit, emits a drum class, an onset quantized to 10 ms, and 32 tokens that decode back to that hit's sound. It is worth reading because one class can carry several timbres within a clip, and the whole system trains on two RTX 4090s; the caveats are that on the two real datasets its five-class mean F1 still trails the dedicated ADTOF transcriber (E-GMD 0.38 vs 0.45, MDB 0.46 vs 0.57, means I computed from Table 3), the synthetic test set it wins on comes from the same pipeline as its training data, and there is no listening test.

動機Motivation

鼓組在流行音樂裡通常被理解成「重複的單擊音色（one-shot）照譜排出來」：底鼓、小鼓、腳踏鈸各有一個取樣，在時間軸上反覆觸發。但錄音與混音把這些事件疊成一條波形之後，個別的擊打就再也拿不出來。MIR 有三個各自獨立的任務在挖這個潛在結構：轉譜給出何時、哪一類；分離給出每一類一軌；單擊抽取給出每一類一個代表聲音。三者都只還原一部分，沒有一個能直接給出可以編輯的鼓機譜。

最接近的嘗試是分析合成（Analysis-by-Synthesis, AbS）路線，作者點名它的三個弱點：對電子鼓音色泛化差、依賴高品質的成對音訊與轉譜資料、每一類只能有一個音色，所以同一段裡兩種不同的底鼓會被壓成一個。D-Composer 的目標是讓模型直接把錄音「拼寫」成一連串離散事件，每個事件帶著自己的聲音，加總回去就是原錄音。

In popular music, drums are usually thought of as repeated one-shots arranged by a score: a kick, a snare and a hi-hat sample each fired many times along the timeline. Once recording and mixing sum these events into one waveform, individual hits can no longer be pulled out. MIR has three separate tasks digging at this hidden structure: transcription gives when and which class, separation gives one track per class, and one-shot extraction gives one representative sound per class. Each recovers only part of it, and none directly yields an editable drum-machine pattern.

The closest attempt is the Analysis-by-Synthesis (AbS) line, for which the authors name three weaknesses: poor generalization to electronic kit timbres, reliance on high-quality paired audio and transcripts, and only one timbre per class, so two different kicks in one clip collapse into one. D-Composer aims to have a model 'spell out' a recording directly as a sequence of discrete events, each carrying its own sound, which sum back to the original.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名的主要對手是 Torres、Peeters 與 Richard 的 Inverse Drum Machine（IDM, 2026）：只有 50 萬參數，用重建損失加轉譜監督同時做轉譜、分離與單擊抽取。它的取捨是輸出樣本級的稠密轉譜、需要後處理稀疏化，每類只有一個單擊，而且只在聲學鼓音色的模擬資料上訓練。另外兩個專用基線是 ADTOF（Zehren 等，2023，以群眾外包的節奏遊戲譜訓練的鼓轉譜）與 DOSE（Hwang 等，2025，從混音抽出單擊）。概念上的近親有 Manilow 等的 Cerberus（2020，同時分離與轉譜，但以遮罩分出整個樂器類別）、Kumar 等的 TAC（2026，把音訊讀成帶時間戳的事件與描述文字；D-Composer 把描述文字換成聲學 token），以及 Ellis 等的 Recomposer（2025）。

論文沒有引用、但其實最像的是 Google 的 MT3（Gardner 等，2022）與 Hawthorne 等（2021）的序列到序列轉譜：頻譜編碼器加自迴歸解碼器，輸出時間、樂器、音高的事件 token。D-Composer 的「轉譜那一半」幾乎就是把 MT3 的配方縮到 12 個鼓類，真正新的是在每個事件後面接上 32 個能還原聲音的 token。更早的祖先是 NMF 鼓轉譜，例如論文有引的 Wu 與 Lerch（2015），以及 Dittmar 與 Gärtner（2014）的即時轉譜加分離：範本矩陣就是單擊音色，活化矩陣就是轉譜。D-Composer 可以看成用生成模型取代線性分解，換來每次擊打各自的音色。

The main rival is the Inverse Drum Machine (IDM; Torres, Peeters and Richard, 2026): only 500K parameters, using reconstruction loss plus transcript supervision to do transcription, separation and one-shot extraction together. Its trade-offs are dense sample-level transcripts that need post-hoc sparsification, one one-shot per class, and training only on simulated acoustic-kit audio. Two task-specific baselines are ADTOF (Zehren et al., 2023, a drum transcriber trained on crowdsourced rhythm-game charts) and DOSE (Hwang et al., 2025, one-shot extraction from mixtures). Conceptual relatives are Cerberus by Manilow et al. (2020, joint separation and transcription, but masking out whole instrument classes), TAC by Kumar et al. (2026, reading audio as timestamped events with captions; D-Composer swaps the captions for acoustic tokens), and Recomposer by Ellis et al. (2025).

What the paper does not cite, but is actually closest, is Google's MT3 (Gardner et al., 2022) and the sequence-to-sequence transcription of Hawthorne et al. (2021): a spectrogram encoder plus an autoregressive decoder emitting time, instrument and pitch event tokens. D-Composer's 'transcription half' is nearly the MT3 recipe narrowed to 12 drum classes; what is genuinely new is appending 32 sound-restoring tokens to each event. An older ancestor is NMF drum transcription, such as Wu and Lerch (2015), which the paper cites, and the real-time transcription plus separation of Dittmar and Gärtner (2014): the template matrix is the one-shots and the activation matrix is the transcript. D-Composer can be read as replacing linear factorization with a generative model, gaining a separate timbre for every hit.

方法Method

一、鼓單擊編解碼器 DOC。 現成的高保真編解碼器表示一個 3 秒單擊要幾百個 token：DAC 是 2,322 個、CoDiCodec 是 512 個，一段 50 次擊打的鼓就要上萬 token。作者因此自己訓練 DOC：先用凍結的 MelodyFlow VAE 取得 48 kHz 立體聲潛在序列，再用可學習的 register 把整段池化成單一個 128 維摘要向量，每一維做 5 階的有限純量量化（FSQ），每 4 維組成一個乘積碼，得到 32 個 token、字彙 5⁴＝625。解碼端是流匹配，推論只走 5 步 Euler。DOC 有 1.4 億參數，位元率 0.1 kbps，比 CoDiCodec 的 2.3 kbps 低二十多倍。

二、交錯的符號－聲學 token。 輸出序列以 BOS 開頭，每個事件依序是一個鼓類 token（12 類，對應簡化的 General MIDI 打擊樂編號）、一個起始時間 token（0 到 9,990 ms，每 10 ms 一格，共 1,000 個）和 32 個 DOC token，最後以 EOS 結束。事件按起始時間排序，同時發生時再按類別排。這種表示天然稀疏，靜音不花 token，事件數也可以變動；一個事件佔 34 個 token。推論用 nucleus 0.95 取樣，並以文法約束保證鼓類之後只能接時間、時間之後只能接聲學 token。

三、合成訓練資料。 譜來自 Lakh MIDI 與 Groove MIDI，單擊取自 Logic Pro 內建音色庫、Native Instruments Battery 4 與 Drum and Percussion Kits 資料集，共 44,858 個。每次取一段鼓譜，每個出現的鼓類最多抽 4 個不同單擊，演奏時隨機輪替，這就是「同類多音色」的來源；其中 40% 的樣本強制從同一套鼓抽，以保證音色一致。單擊與譜都做變速、失真、延遲、殘響、時間抖動等增強，混好後再加殘響、等化與動態壓縮。目標聲學 token 是把單擊經過同樣的處理後再編碼，等於假設整條處理鏈是線性的。

四、模型與成本。 編碼器讀 MelodyFlow 潛在序列，解碼器以交叉注意力接收，兩邊各 12 層、寬 512，共 9,000 萬參數，只用 teacher forcing 的交叉熵訓練。訓練片段 4 秒、最多 50 個事件，10 萬步、批次 16，兩張 RTX 4090 跑 46 小時；DOC 另花 14 小時。作者估算全部實驗耗電 396 kWh，約為 2017–2023 年 ISMIR 論文平均訓練成本的 175%。

1. Drum One-shot Codec (DOC). Off-the-shelf high-fidelity codecs need hundreds of tokens for a 3-second one-shot (2,322 for DAC, 512 for CoDiCodec), so a 50-hit drum clip runs to tens of thousands of tokens. The authors therefore train DOC: a frozen MelodyFlow VAE gives a 48 kHz stereo latent sequence, learnable registers pool it into a single 128-dimensional summary vector, each dimension is quantized to 5 levels with Finite Scalar Quantization (FSQ), and every 4 dimensions form a product code, giving 32 tokens with a vocabulary of 5⁴ = 625. The decoder is flow matching, using only 5 Euler steps at inference. DOC has 140M parameters and runs at 0.1 kbps, over twenty times lower than CoDiCodec's 2.3 kbps.

2. Interleaved symbolic–acoustic tokens. The output starts with BOS; each event is a class token (12 classes, following a simplified General MIDI percussion map), an onset token (0 to 9,990 ms in 10 ms steps, 1,000 values) and 32 DOC tokens, and the sequence ends with EOS. Events are sorted by onset, then by class when simultaneous. The representation is naturally sparse, spending no tokens on silence and allowing any number of events; each event costs 34 tokens. Inference uses nucleus sampling at 0.95 with a grammar constraint so that a class is followed only by an onset and an onset only by acoustic tokens.

3. Synthetic training data. Scores come from Lakh MIDI and Groove MIDI; one-shots come from the Logic Pro stock library, Native Instruments Battery 4 and the Drum and Percussion Kits dataset, 44,858 in total. For each score excerpt, up to 4 different one-shots are drawn per class and rotated randomly at each hit, which is where intra-class timbre variation comes from; 40% of examples are forced to draw from one kit for timbral coherence. One-shots and scores are augmented with speed change, distortion, delay, reverb and timing jitter, and the mix gets further reverb, EQ and compression. Target acoustic tokens are made by passing each one-shot through the same processing before encoding, which assumes the whole chain is linear.

4. Model and cost. The encoder reads MelodyFlow latents and the decoder attends to it by cross-attention; each side has 12 layers of width 512, 90M parameters in total, trained only with teacher-forced cross-entropy. Training clips are 4 seconds with up to 50 events, for 100K steps at batch size 16, taking 46 hours on two RTX 4090s; DOC takes another 14 hours. The authors estimate total experiment energy at 396 kWh, about 175% of the mean training cost of ISMIR papers from 2017–2023.

限制Limitations

轉譜在真實資料上沒有贏專用模型。 表 3 按五類（底鼓、小鼓、通鼓、腳踏鈸、銅鈸）列 F1。我把它平均起來：E-GMD 上 ADTOF 0.45、IDM 0.40、D-Composer 0.38；MDB-Drums 上分別是 0.57、0.44、0.46。D-Composer 只在合成測試集領先（0.39 對 ADTOF 0.35、IDM 0.21），但那批資料是用它自己的生成流程，以未見過的 Groove MIDI 譜與同一批音色庫的未見單擊合成的，屬於同分佈。作者自己也寫明這些結果「不表示它是更好的轉譜器」，摘要裡的「comparably or better」要配著這句話讀。通鼓在 MDB 上只有 0.02，低於兩個對手的 0.10。

評估只有頻譜距離。 單擊抽取與重建都用多尺度頻譜損失（MSL），作者承認它和人耳判斷對應得不好，聽測留待未來。單擊抽取的比較也不完全對等：IDM 與 DOSE 每類只輸出一個單擊，D-Composer 則從它對每次擊打的輸出裡隨機挑一個當代表；它的單擊訓練資料也和 DOSE 的訓練資料透過 Drum and Percussion Kits 有重疊。底鼓上它輸給 DOSE（5.68 對 5.19）。DOC 本身的重建 MSL 是 3.71，比 DAC 的 1.71、CoDiCodec 的 2.25 差不少，上限是 MelodyFlow VAE 的 1.29。

範圍與可用性。 所有評估都切成 4 秒片段，起始時間 token 也只到 9.99 秒，長錄音要怎麼分段再接起來沒有討論。沒有力度（velocity），鼓類只有 12 個。由於商用音色庫的授權禁止以「重新包裝」的形式散布，作者暫不釋出模型權重，這對一篇定位為工具的論文是實際的限制。

Transcription does not beat the dedicated model on real data. Table 3 gives F1 for five classes (kick, snare, tom, hi-hat, cymbal). Averaging them myself: on E-GMD, ADTOF 0.45, IDM 0.40, D-Composer 0.38; on MDB-Drums, 0.57, 0.44 and 0.46. D-Composer leads only on the synthetic test set (0.39 vs ADTOF 0.35 and IDM 0.21), but that set is synthesized with its own pipeline from unseen Groove MIDI scores and unseen one-shots from the same libraries, so it is in-distribution. The authors state outright that these results do not demonstrate its superiority as a transcriber, and the abstract's 'comparably or better' should be read with that. Toms on MDB score only 0.02, below both rivals' 0.10.

Evaluation is spectral distance only. One-shot extraction and reconstruction both use multi-scale spectral loss (MSL), which the authors acknowledge corresponds poorly to human judgement; listening tests are left to future work. The one-shot comparison is not fully like-for-like: IDM and DOSE output one one-shot per class, while D-Composer's representative is a random pick among its per-hit outputs, and its one-shot training data overlaps DOSE's through the Drum and Percussion Kits dataset. On kick it loses to DOSE (5.68 vs 5.19). DOC's own reconstruction MSL is 3.71, well behind DAC's 1.71 and CoDiCodec's 2.25, with the MelodyFlow VAE's 1.29 as the ceiling.

Scope and availability. All evaluation uses 4-second excerpts and the onset vocabulary stops at 9.99 seconds; how to window and stitch longer recordings is not discussed. There is no velocity, and only 12 classes. Because commercial sample-library licences forbid redistribution in 'repackaged' form, the authors are withholding model weights for now, a real limitation for a paper positioned as a tool.

討論Discussion

這篇最有說服力的數字其實是重建，不是轉譜。把預測的單擊照預測的時間疊回去，和原錄音比 MSL，D-Composer 在四個資料集上都比 IDM 低約 26% 到 30%：E-GMD 7.87 對 10.63、MDB 12.27 對 17.32、合成 15.83 對 22.65、Freesound 鼓循環 20.39 對 27.83。最後一組是網路上各式電子鼓循環，最直接考驗 IDM「只見過聲學鼓」的弱點，差距在那裡也維持住。換句話說，它的轉譜不一定更準，但拆出來的東西拼回去更像原聲，而這正是「可編輯鼓譜」這個用途需要的性質。

設計上值得記住的是 DOC 的取捨：把一個單擊壓到 32 個 token，換來能在消費級 GPU 上訓練的序列長度，代價是音質明顯掉一截。作者自己點出更好的低位元率編解碼器是下一步。我會補一句：既然聲學 token 在序列裡本質上是被「抄寫」出來的，也可以讓解碼器改輸出連續潛在，或只輸出單擊庫的檢索索引；後者在授權受限的情況下可能更實用。

最後，這套方法的上限被合成資料框住。訓練目標建立在「混音是單擊線性相加」的假設上，真實錄音的串音、房間聲與壓縮都會打破它。作者提議未來用強化學習把無標註的真實錄音拉進端到端訓練，這是縮小 MDB 上那約 0.11 差距的合理路徑，但目前還只是提案。

The paper's most convincing number is reconstruction, not transcription. Summing the predicted one-shots at the predicted times and comparing with the original by MSL, D-Composer is about 26% to 30% lower than IDM on all four sets: E-GMD 7.87 vs 10.63, MDB 12.27 vs 17.32, synthetic 15.83 vs 22.65, and Freesound drum loops 20.39 vs 27.83. The last set is assorted electronic loops from the web, the most direct test of IDM's 'acoustic kits only' weakness, and the gap holds there too. In other words, its transcription is not necessarily more accurate, but what it takes apart sums back closer to the source, which is exactly the property an editable drum pattern needs.

The design choice worth remembering is DOC's trade-off: squeezing a one-shot into 32 tokens buys sequence lengths trainable on consumer GPUs, at a clear cost in audio quality. The authors themselves name a better low-bitrate codec as the next step. I would add that since the acoustic tokens are essentially 'transcribed' into the sequence, the decoder could instead emit continuous latents, or just retrieval indices into a one-shot library; the latter may be more practical given the licensing constraints.

Finally, the method's ceiling is set by synthetic data. The training targets assume a mix is a linear sum of one-shots, which bleed, room sound and compression in real recordings all break. The authors propose bringing unlabelled real recordings into end-to-end training via reinforcement learning, a sensible path to closing the roughly 0.11 gap on MDB, but for now it is only a proposal.

Rethinking Magnitude Compression in Spectral Losses for DDSP Parameter Estimation

重新檢討 DDSP 參數估計中頻譜損失的振幅壓縮——用不必訓練的「一致度」預測該用線性還是對數頻譜損失，而常見的線性＋對數混合在三個系統裡都不是最好

Byunghoo Park, Jayeon Yi, Minje Kim
arXiv:2610.22962 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-16
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：UIUC 的 Minje Kim 團隊追問一個常被當成預設的選擇：DDSP 的頻譜損失要不要取對數。他們提出不必訓練的「一致度」（concordance），量測一個損失能多常把候選參數按真實距離排對，用它預測每個系統該用線性還是對數，再以三個系統驗證。在平板殘響與減法合成器上，線性損失的參數誤差分別約為對數版的六分之一與一半，七位專家聽測裡六位偏好線性；為了測反方向而挑的第三個系統則換成對數較好。值得讀的是它給了「先算再選」的程序；要保留的是只有三個小系統、只比兩個極端，而前兩個系統的線性與對數一致度差距只有 0.017 與 0.010。Why this one: Minje Kim's group at UIUC questions a choice usually taken as default: whether a DDSP spectral loss should be log-compressed. They propose a training-free measure, concordance, which checks how often a loss ranks candidate parameter settings in the order of their true distance, use it to predict whether each system wants a linear or log loss, and verify on three systems. On a plate reverb and a subtractive synthesizer, the linear loss gives about one sixth and one half of the log version's parameter error, and six of seven experts prefer it in listening tests; a third system chosen to test the reverse direction favours log instead. It is worth reading for its 'measure first, then choose' procedure; the caveats are only three small systems, only the two extremes compared, and linear-versus-log concordance gaps of just 0.017 and 0.010 in the first two systems.

動機Motivation

用可微分數位訊號處理（DDSP）去擬合一個已知的訊號模型，例如替合成器找出能重現某個聲音的旋鈕值，或替物理模型找參數，關鍵在損失函數。這類系統的可調參數很少，但每動一個參數都可能讓頻譜峰值移位、共振改變，所以損失給出的梯度方向好不好，直接決定能不能擬合到目標。

音訊振幅的動態範圍很大，而人耳對響度的感受是次線性的，所以從 μ-law 到 Engel 等的多尺度頻譜（MSS）損失，都習慣用對數或冪次把振幅壓縮後再比較。作者的疑問是：這個「像人耳」的偏好，不一定等於「對擬合參數最有資訊」。如果參數變動主要表現在少數幾個強的頻譜分量上，壓縮反而會把最有用的梯度壓小。目前選損失只能各訓練一輪來比，沒有事前判斷的準則，這篇想補上這個缺口。

Fitting a known signal model with differentiable digital signal processing (DDSP), such as finding synthesizer knob settings that reproduce a sound or fitting a physical model, hinges on the loss. Such systems have few parameters, yet moving one can shift spectral peaks or change resonances, so whether the loss gives a good gradient direction directly decides whether the target can be reached.

Audio magnitudes span a wide dynamic range and loudness perception is sublinear, so from μ-law to the multi-scale spectral (MSS) loss of Engel et al., magnitudes are habitually log- or power-compressed before comparison. The authors' question is that this 'ear-like' preference need not be what is most informative for fitting parameters. If parameter changes show up mainly in a few strong spectral components, compression shrinks exactly the most useful gradients. Today the loss is chosen only by training each candidate and comparing, with no criterion for deciding in advance; this paper tries to fill that gap.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接相關的先前工作有三條。Schwär 與 Müller（2023）重新檢視 MSS 損失，發現壓縮會加劇頻譜洩漏，建議用較輕的壓縮；Braun 與 Tashev（2021）在語音增強裡發現冪次壓縮與未壓縮的 L1 表現相當，兩者都勝過對數；Li 等（2021）在語音去殘響裡發現中間的冪次指數最好，理由是過度壓縮傷到承載共振峰的高能量區。作者的批評是這些解釋各自綁在特定系統上。Salimi 等（2025）系統性比較了各種聲音相似度指標在可微分迭代聲音匹配上的表現，發現最佳損失因合成器而異；這篇則追問「為什麼因系統而異」。Hayes 等（2023）也早已指出頻譜損失對正弦頻率估計的梯度很差。

論文沒有提到、但概念上最近的是 Turian 與 Henry（2020）的〈I'm Sorry for Your Loss〉：他們讓正弦波的音高逐步偏離，檢查各種頻譜距離是否隨之單調增加，結論是大多數距離對音高的表現很差。這篇的「一致度」本質上是同一個想法推廣到多維參數：在目標附近隨機擾動參數，看損失的排序與參數距離的排序有多一致。數學上它就是換到 0 到 1 尺度的 Kendall τ（沒有平手時 C＝(τ+1)/2），作者沒有點出這層關係。新意在於把它對線性項與對數項分開計算，並沿動態範圍逐層加入頻率格，用來定位「資訊區」。

Three strands of prior work bear directly on this. Schwär and Müller (2023) revisit the MSS loss and find that compression worsens spectral leakage, recommending milder compression; Braun and Tashev (2021) find in speech enhancement that power-law compression performs on par with uncompressed L1 and both beat log; Li et al. (2021) find in speech dereverberation that an intermediate power exponent works best, blaming over-compression of the high-energy regions that carry formants. The authors' criticism is that each explanation is tied to its own system. Salimi et al. (2025) systematically compare sound-similarity metrics for differentiable iterative sound matching and find that the best loss varies by synthesizer; this paper asks why it varies. Hayes et al. (2023) had already shown that spectral losses give poor gradients for sinusoidal frequency estimation.

The conceptually closest work, which the paper does not mention, is Turian and Henry's (2020) 'I'm Sorry for Your Loss': they shift a sinusoid's pitch step by step and check whether each spectral distance rises monotonically, concluding that most perform poorly on pitch. Concordance is essentially that idea generalized to many parameters: perturb parameters randomly around a target and see how well the loss ranking agrees with the parameter-distance ranking. Mathematically it is Kendall's τ rescaled to 0–1 (C = (τ+1)/2 without ties), a link the authors do not state. The novelty is computing it separately for the linear and log terms and adding frequency bins layer by layer down the dynamic range to locate the 'informative region'.

方法Method

問題設定。 網路讀入目標聲音、輸出參數，訊號模型依參數重新渲染，再在頻譜上與目標比較。損失統一寫成線性項加對數項的加權和：線性項比的是振幅的絕對差，權重自然集中在高能量的頻率格；對數項比的是相對差，把權重往低能量移。每個系統保留原本的基準損失，把其中一項的權重設成零，得到線性、對數、混合三種變體，其他一切相同。對數項的 ε 沿用各自的原實作：平板是 10⁻⁷，合成器是 10⁻²。

三個系統。 第一個是第一屆 DAFx 參數估計挑戰的模態平板殘響模擬器，估 6 個物理參數（厚度、密度、高度、張力、直流衰減時間、10 kHz 與直流的衰減比）。第二個是 Masuda 與 Saito（2021）的雙振盪器減法合成器，估 7 個參數，基頻直接給定。第三個是單振盪器合成器後接指數衰減殘響，估 6 個參數；它是在一致度分析顯示資訊區偏低能量之後才被挑進來，專門用來測反方向。

一致度。 每個系統隨機取 100 個目標，每個目標在正規化參數空間裡往隨機方向偏移最多 0.15，取 32 個候選並渲染，計算「損失大小順序與參數距離順序一致」的成對比例。對線性項與對數項分別計算，兩者的差就單獨反映了壓縮的效果。第二種用法是只保留距目標峰值 F dB 以內的頻率格，逐步放寬 F，觀察對數項的一致度如何變化。

訓練與評估。 三個系統都分三段訓練：先只用參數損失，接著線性過渡到頻譜損失，最後只用頻譜損失；三種變體從同一個參數損失檢查點分岔。評估刻意避開所有被比較的損失，改用 MFCC 距離，加上有真值時的參數正規化均方誤差（NMSE）。真實錄音方面，平板用 12 條實測的 EMT 140 脈衝響應，合成器用 Moog Minitaur 兩個音色的單音；另做 MUSHRA 式聽測，7 位音訊專家、每個系統 12 題，響度對齊到 −23 LUFS。

Setup. A network reads the target sound and outputs parameters, the signal model re-renders from them, and the result is compared with the target on a spectrogram. The loss is written as a weighted sum of a linear term and a log term: the linear term compares absolute magnitude differences, so its weight concentrates on high-energy bins; the log term compares relative differences, shifting weight toward low energies. Each system keeps its original baseline loss and zeroes one term's weight to get linear, log and hybrid variants, with everything else identical. The log term's ε follows each reference implementation: 10⁻⁷ for the plate and 10⁻² for the synthesizer.

Three systems. The first is the modal plate reverb simulator from the 1st DAFx parameter estimation challenge, estimating 6 physical parameters (thickness, density, height, tension, DC decay time, and the ratio of decay at 10 kHz to DC). The second is the two-oscillator subtractive synthesizer of Masuda and Saito (2021), estimating 7 parameters with the fundamental supplied. The third is a one-oscillator synthesizer followed by an exponentially decaying reverb, estimating 6 parameters; it was chosen only after concordance showed a low-energy informative region, specifically to test the reverse direction.

Concordance. For each system, 100 random targets are each displaced in a random direction by up to 0.15 in normalized parameter space to get 32 rendered candidates, and the measure is the fraction of pairs whose loss ordering matches their parameter-distance ordering. Computed separately for the linear and log terms, their difference isolates the effect of compression. A second use keeps only bins within F dB of the target's peak and widens F step by step to see how log concordance changes.

Training and evaluation. All systems train in three phases: parameter loss only, a linear crossfade to the spectral loss, then spectral loss only; the three variants branch from one shared parameter-loss checkpoint. Evaluation deliberately avoids every compared loss, using MFCC distance plus parameter normalized mean squared error (NMSE) where ground truth exists. For real recordings, the plate uses 12 measured EMT 140 impulse responses and the synthesizer uses single notes from two Moog Minitaur patches; a MUSHRA-style test has 7 audio experts and 12 trials per system, loudness-matched to −23 LUFS.

限制Limitations

只有三個系統，而且都很小。 參數只有 6 到 7 個，結論能否延伸到參數上百的 DDSP 合成器（例如 Engel 等的諧波加噪聲模型）或神經音訊編解碼器，沒有證據。前兩個系統的線性減對數一致度只有 +0.017 與 +0.010（t 值 2.5 與 3.5），方向一致但幅度很小；第三個系統的 −0.037（t＝−7.8）反而最明顯。而第三個系統是看過一致度之後才挑的，它是一次事前預測的確認，不是隨機抽樣的檢驗。

只比了兩個端點。 冪次壓縮（例如 0.3 次方）正是 Braun 與 Tashev 認為不輸未壓縮、Li 等認為最好的區間，這篇沒有測，作者在結論裡也承認。另一個沒有消融的旋鈕是對數項的 ε：平板用 10⁻⁷、合成器用 10⁻²，差了五個數量級，而 ε 本身就決定了對數項在多低的能量之下不再敏感，可能和「資訊區」的結論糾纏在一起。混合變體也只用基準的權重比例，沒有掃描。

頻譜損失只負責微調。 每個系統都先用參數損失訓練，再從同一個檢查點換成頻譜損失，所以一致度只在目標附近 0.15 的鄰域量測，剛好對應這種局部微調的情境；從頭只用頻譜損失訓練時結論是否成立，並不知道。聽測只有 7 人，線性與混合在兩個實驗裡都沒有分開，真實錄音也只用在兩個系統上。

Only three systems, all small. They have 6 to 7 parameters, and there is no evidence the conclusion extends to DDSP synthesizers with hundreds of parameters (such as the harmonic-plus-noise model of Engel et al.) or to neural audio codecs. The linear-minus-log concordance gap in the first two systems is only +0.017 and +0.010 (t of 2.5 and 3.5), consistent in sign but small; the third system's −0.037 (t = −7.8) is the clearest. And the third system was picked after seeing its concordance, so it confirms an advance prediction rather than testing a random sample.

Only the two endpoints. Power-law compression (for example, an exponent of 0.3) is exactly the range Braun and Tashev found on par with uncompressed and Li et al. found best, and it is untested here, as the authors acknowledge in the conclusion. Another unablated knob is the log term's ε: 10⁻⁷ for the plate versus 10⁻² for the synthesizer, five orders of magnitude apart. ε itself sets the energy below which the log term stops being sensitive, so it may be entangled with the informative-region conclusion. The hybrid variant also uses only the baseline weight ratio, with no sweep.

The spectral loss only fine-tunes. Every system first trains on the parameter loss and switches to the spectral loss from a shared checkpoint, so concordance is measured within a 0.15 neighbourhood of the target, matching this local fine-tuning setting; whether the conclusion holds when training from scratch on the spectral loss alone is unknown. The listening test has only 7 listeners, linear and hybrid are not separated in either experiment, and real recordings are used for only two systems.

討論Discussion

最有實用價值的發現是混合變體。線性加對數是 DDSP 文獻最常見的預設，看起來像兩全其美的保險，但它在三個系統裡沒有一個是最好的。平板上它的參數誤差 0.221 和對數版的 0.218 差不多，是線性版 0.036 的六倍；合成器上是 0.0161，線性是 0.0072；殘響系統上是 0.0238，比線性的 0.0108 和對數的 0.0049 都差。也就是說兩項相加的結果不一定落在兩者之間，可能比兩者都糟。作者的建議很具體：之後的論文至少把只用線性的變體和 MSS 基準一起報告，成本只是多訓練一輪。

客觀與主觀指標指向同一邊。在模型自己渲染的目標上，平板的 MFCC 距離是線性 7.88、對數 8.76、混合 8.35，合成器是 6.91、11.80、12.02；換成真實錄音，平板是 13.92、19.01、15.98，合成器是 11.45、14.01、12.76，線性仍然最好。聽測裡線性減對數的差距，平板是 +15.7 分（95% 信賴區間 4.1 到 27.3，p＝0.016），合成器是 +6.9 分（0.8 到 13.1，p＝0.033），兩個實驗都是 7 人中 6 人偏好線性。

一致度的逐層分析也支持「資訊區」的解釋：對數項的一致度在平板上於 90 dB 達到高峰，再把更低能量的格加進來後掉了 0.026；合成器在 60 dB 達到高峰後掉 0.012；殘響系統只掉 0.003。這個工具的價值在於訓練前就能算，而這篇唯一一次真正的事前預測（殘響系統）也確實應驗。下一步最該做的是把同樣的檢查跑在冪次壓縮與更大的合成器上，看一致度能不能挑出中間的最佳指數。

The most practically useful finding is about the hybrid variant. Linear plus log is the most common default in the DDSP literature and looks like a safe compromise, yet it is best in none of the three systems. On the plate its parameter error of 0.221 is close to the log version's 0.218 and six times the linear version's 0.036; on the synthesizer it is 0.0161 against linear's 0.0072; on the reverb system it is 0.0238, worse than both linear's 0.0108 and log's 0.0049. So the sum of the two terms need not fall between them and can be worse than both. The authors' advice is concrete: future papers should at least report a linear-only variant alongside the MSS baseline, at the cost of one extra training run.

Objective and subjective metrics point the same way. On targets the model itself rendered, plate MFCC distance is 7.88 for linear, 8.76 for log and 8.35 for hybrid, and the synthesizer's is 6.91, 11.80 and 12.02; on real recordings the plate gives 13.92, 19.01 and 15.98 and the synthesizer 11.45, 14.01 and 12.76, with linear still best. In the listening test, the linear-minus-log gap is +15.7 points on the plate (95% CI 4.1 to 27.3, p = 0.016) and +6.9 on the synthesizer (0.8 to 13.1, p = 0.033), with 6 of 7 listeners preferring linear in both.

The layer-by-layer concordance analysis supports the 'informative region' account: log concordance peaks at 90 dB on the plate and drops 0.026 once lower-energy bins are added; the synthesizer peaks at 60 dB and drops 0.012; the reverb system drops only 0.003. The tool's value is that it can be computed before training, and the paper's one genuine advance prediction (the reverb system) did come true. The obvious next step is to run the same check on power-law compression and larger synthesizers, to see whether concordance can pick an intermediate best exponent.

動機Motivation

一段旋律可以有很多種合理的配和聲，所以「和人類標註的和弦一不一致」不等於「好不好聽」，也不等於「有沒有還原作曲者的決定」。常見的評估要不是挑幾首歌做個案分析，要不是在大語料上算一個總準確率，後者把所有不一致都當成同一種錯誤。

作者先前的研究用六首有敘事背景的歌比較電腦配和聲與原曲，屬於前一種。這篇想往語料庫走一步，同時保留可解釋性：把模型凍結、規則一條不改，丟到獨立蒐集的人類標註上，再把每一個不一致拆開來看，是模型「表示不到」那個和弦，還是「有得選卻選了別的」。他們提了三個研究問題：凍結模型能重現多少人類和弦決定（RQ1）、不一致中有多少是候選不足、多少是選擇差異（RQ2）、這種決策層級的診斷比總準確率多提供了什麼（RQ3）。

A melody admits many reasonable harmonizations, so agreeing with an annotated chord is not the same as sounding good, nor as recovering the composer's decision. Common evaluations either analyse a few songs case by case or compute one overall accuracy on a large corpus, and the latter treats every disagreement as the same kind of error.

The authors' earlier study compared computer harmonizations with six narrative-associated songs, the first kind. This paper moves toward a corpus while keeping explainability: freeze the model, change no rule, run it on independently collected human annotations, and take each disagreement apart to see whether the model 'cannot represent' the chord or 'had it available but chose another'. They pose three research questions: how much of the human chord decisions a frozen model reproduces (RQ1), how much disagreement comes from missing candidates versus different choices (RQ2), and what a decision-level diagnostic adds beyond overall accuracy (RQ3).

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文的相關研究章節以和聲理論與可解釋 AI 的綜述為主，例如 Lerdahl 與 Jackendoff 的生成式調性音樂理論、Krumhansl 的調性階層、Temperley 的音樂結構認知模型、Moss 等對貝多芬弦樂四重奏的和聲語料統計；它沒有和任何具體的自動配和聲系統做數字比較。

EMH 的核心是「在每個和聲段替候選和弦打分數，再用維特比式動態規劃找全域最佳的和弦路徑」，這並不新。最像的是 Simon、Morris 與 Basu 的 MySong（CHI 2008）：同樣只看旋律，用隱藏馬可夫模型加維特比解碼配和弦，差別是 MySong 的轉移與發射機率從語料學來，EMH 則用手訂的四個權重。另一個應該對照的是 Yeh 等（2021，JNMR）的〈Automatic melody harmonization with triad chords〉，他們就在 HookTheory 的 lead sheet 資料上比較了模板比對加 HMM、遺傳演算法、BiLSTM 等方法，規模比這篇的 19 段大得多，也提供了現成的基線。SheetSage（Donahue 等，2022）則是這篇所用 HookTheory 資料的發布來源。

至於「把錯誤拆成字彙外與選擇差異」，和弦辨識領域已有類似的討論：Humphrey 與 Bello（2015）的〈Four timely insights on automatic chord estimation〉指出字彙大小與標註主觀性會讓單一準確率失真，Koops 等（2019）則量化了流行音樂和聲標註者之間的分歧。這篇的貢獻是把這個視角搬到配和聲的評估上，並附上可重現的程式碼標籤。

The paper's related-work section mostly surveys harmony theory and explainable AI, such as Lerdahl and Jackendoff's generative theory of tonal music, Krumhansl's tonal hierarchies, Temperley's models of musical structure cognition and Moss et al.'s corpus statistics of Beethoven string quartets; it makes no numerical comparison with any specific automatic harmonization system.

EMH's core, scoring candidate chords in each harmonic segment and finding the globally best chord path by Viterbi-like dynamic programming, is not new. The closest is MySong by Simon, Morris and Basu (CHI 2008): it likewise reads only the melody and assigns chords with a hidden Markov model and Viterbi decoding, the difference being that MySong learns transition and emission probabilities from a corpus while EMH uses four hand-set weights. Another natural comparison is Yeh et al. (2021, JNMR), 'Automatic melody harmonization with triad chords', which compared template matching with an HMM, a genetic algorithm, a BiLSTM and other methods on HookTheory lead sheets, at far larger scale than these 19 excerpts and with ready-made baselines. SheetSage (Donahue et al., 2022) is the release source of the HookTheory data used here.

As for splitting errors into out-of-vocabulary and selection differences, chord recognition has had similar discussions: Humphrey and Bello (2015), 'Four timely insights on automatic chord estimation', argued that vocabulary size and annotation subjectivity distort a single accuracy figure, and Koops et al. (2019) quantified disagreement among harmony annotators of popular music. This paper's contribution is bringing that perspective to harmonization evaluation, backed by a reproducible code tag.

方法Method

模型（EMH v0.12）。 輸入是單聲部旋律事件。系統只從旋律推出調性，依拍號切出和聲決策段，候選字彙固定為 10 個以調為準的功能和弦：七個自然音三和弦（I、ii、iii、IV、V、vi、vii°）加上 ii7、V7、viiø7。副屬和弦、九和弦以上的延伸和弦等都刻意排除。每個候選依四項打分：旋律與和弦相容性（權重 0.50）、功能轉移（0.25）、和聲持續（0.10）、終止式偏好（0.15），再用維特比式動態規劃找整條最佳路徑，所以局部得分最高的和弦不一定出現在最終結果裡。

資料與防火牆。 從 HookTheory 公開 JSON 檔（經 SheetSage 發布）的 TEST 分割中，依旋律與拍號條件決定性地挑出 20 段，實際處理 19 段；剩下一段是複拍子，轉接程式的時間對應還沒驗證，因此排除。模型只拿到旋律與必要的時間資訊，人類和弦由評估程式在生成完成後另外讀取。

對齊與指標。 人類和弦以「時間重疊最大者」對應到 EMH 的每個決策段；439 段中有 40 段內含多個人類和弦、4 段的人類標註覆蓋不完整，兩者都保留。指標包括和弦根音一致、正規化後的完整和弦一致、以 EMH 推出的調為準的和聲功能一致、候選覆蓋率（人類和弦是否在 10 個候選內）、根音候選覆蓋率，以及候選有覆蓋時的條件一致率。每一段最後被歸入三個互斥類別：完全一致、人類和弦不在候選內、在候選內但沒被選。程式碼標籤為 emh-v0.12.1-hooktheory。

Model (EMH v0.12). The input is monophonic melodic events. The system infers the key from the melody alone, cuts meter-aware harmonic decision segments, and uses a fixed vocabulary of 10 key-relative functional chords: the seven diatonic triads (I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii°) plus ii7, V7 and viiø7. Secondary dominants and extended chords from the ninth upward are deliberately excluded. Each candidate is scored on four terms: melody–chord compatibility (weight 0.50), functional transition (0.25), harmonic persistence (0.10) and cadential preference (0.15), then Viterbi-like dynamic programming finds the best full path, so the locally best chord need not appear in the result.

Data and firewall. From the TEST partition of the public HookTheory JSON archive (released via SheetSage), 20 excerpts are deterministically selected by melodic and metrical criteria and 19 are processed; the remaining one is in compound meter, whose time mapping in the adapter had not been verified, so it is excluded. The model receives only the melody and necessary timing, and the evaluator reads the human chords separately after generation.

Alignment and metrics. Each EMH decision segment is assigned the human chord with maximum temporal overlap; of 439 segments, 40 contain more than one human chord and 4 have incomplete reference coverage, and both are kept. Metrics are chord-root agreement, normalized full-chord agreement, harmonic-function agreement relative to the EMH-inferred key, candidate coverage (whether the human chord is among the 10 candidates), root candidate coverage, and agreement conditional on coverage. Each segment is finally placed in one of three mutually exclusive categories: exact match, human chord absent from the candidates, or present but not selected. The code tag is emh-v0.12.1-hooktheory.

限制Limitations

沒有基線，20.5% 無從判讀。 全篇沒有任何對照：沒有「永遠給 I」、沒有在候選中隨機選，也沒有一個從 HookTheory 學出來的 HMM。我自己粗估：候選覆蓋率 53.8%、候選 10 個，在候選中隨機選的完全一致率約 5.4%，EMH 的 20.5% 大約是它的四倍；但這遠不是有意義的下限，流行歌裡 I、IV、V、vi 佔了大宗，一個只會選最常見和弦的模型可能就有相當的分數。沒有這類參照，讀者無法知道 EMH 的「選擇」到底帶來多少資訊。

功能一致率建立在不可靠的調上。 47.6% 的功能一致，是把人類和弦放到 EMH 自己推出的調裡解讀，而 EMH 推出的調只有 8/19（42.1%）與 HookTheory 標註的主音與大小調都相符。作者在討論裡有誠實交代，但摘要仍把這個數字和其他指標並列。

規模與完成度。 19 段、439 個和聲段，選取條件非隨機，作者自己稱之為概念驗證。沒有聽測、沒有獨立的功能分析，也沒有對四個權重做消融，所以「有得選卻沒選」的 146 段到底是哪一項分數造成的，無法回答。PDF 的頁首還留著「Author Name et al.」與「Example for Preparation of Your Own Paper」等模板字樣，DOI 也是佔位的 ahfeXXXX，看得出是為 AHFE 2026 準備、尚未定稿的版本。

No baseline, so 20.5% cannot be interpreted. There is no comparison of any kind: no 'always I', no random choice among candidates, no HMM learned from HookTheory. My own rough estimate: with 53.8% candidate coverage and 10 candidates, a random pick among candidates would match exactly about 5.4% of the time, so EMH's 20.5% is about four times that; but this is far from a meaningful floor, since I, IV, V and vi dominate pop songs, and a model that always picks the most common chord might score comparably. Without such a reference, readers cannot tell how much information EMH's 'choices' actually carry.

Function agreement rests on an unreliable key. The 47.6% function agreement interprets human chords within EMH's own inferred key, and that key matches HookTheory's annotated tonic and mode in only 8 of 19 excerpts (42.1%). The authors disclose this honestly in the discussion, but the abstract still lists the figure alongside the other metrics.

Scale and polish. There are 19 excerpts and 439 harmonic segments, chosen by non-random criteria, which the authors themselves call a proof of concept. There is no listening test, no independent functional analysis and no ablation of the four weights, so which score term causes the 146 'available but not selected' segments cannot be answered. The PDF's running headers still read 'Author Name et al.' and 'Example for Preparation of Your Own Paper', and the DOI is the placeholder ahfeXXXX, marking it as an unfinalized version prepared for AHFE 2026.

討論Discussion

這篇真正有用的是表 2 的三分法。439 段中 90 段（20.5%）完全一致、203 段（46.2%）人類和弦不在候選內、146 段（33.3%）在候選內卻沒被選。再對照兩個覆蓋率：根音覆蓋 95.7%（420/439），完整和弦覆蓋卻只有 53.8%（236/439），表示缺的幾乎都不是根音，而是和弦性質與延伸音，例如其他七和弦、掛留和弦、副屬和弦。翻成行動就很清楚：下一版擴大字彙，最多能讓那 46.2% 變成可選；但即使在候選有覆蓋的 236 段裡，EMH 也只選對 38.1%（90/236），所以光擴字彙不夠，選擇本身也是大問題。

作者的態度值得肯定：不把人類標註當唯一正解，也不宣稱電腦配得比較好或比較差，只說這是一個可以拿來比較後續版本的凍結基線。對教學用途，這種把每一個和弦決定攤開、告訴你「人類用的和弦你根本沒有」的輸出，比一個總分有用。但它距離一個 MIR 評估方法還差兩步：一是加上至少一個學習式基線與一個頻率先驗，讓 20.5% 有參照；二是把樣本擴到幾百段並報告信賴區間。下一版若做到這兩點，這個三分法就能直接套用到任何配和聲模型上，包括神經網路，價值才會超出 EMH 本身。

What is genuinely useful here is Table 2's three-way split. Of 439 segments, 90 (20.5%) match exactly, 203 (46.2%) have a human chord outside the candidates, and 146 (33.3%) have it among the candidates but not chosen. Set against the two coverage rates, root coverage of 95.7% (420/439) versus full-chord coverage of only 53.8% (236/439), what is missing is almost never the root but the chord quality and extensions, such as other sevenths, suspensions and secondary dominants. The action items follow directly: widening the vocabulary in the next version can at most make that 46.2% selectable; but even in the 236 covered segments EMH picks the human chord only 38.1% of the time (90/236), so widening the vocabulary is not enough, and selection itself is a big problem.

The authors' stance deserves credit: they do not treat the human annotation as the only correct answer, nor claim the computer harmonizes better or worse, saying only that this is a frozen baseline for comparing later versions. For teaching, output that lays out each chord decision and says 'the human used a chord you do not have' is more useful than one score. But it is still two steps short of an MIR evaluation method: first, adding at least one learned baseline and a frequency prior so that 20.5% has a reference; second, scaling to hundreds of excerpts with confidence intervals. If the next version does both, the three-way split can be applied directly to any harmonization model, neural ones included, and its value will reach beyond EMH itself.
