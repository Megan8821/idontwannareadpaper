Retrieving Individual Stems from Music Mixtures with Slot Embeddings

以 Slot Embedding 從音樂混音中檢索個別 Stem

David Braun, Junyi Fan, Pranay Manocha, Donald S. Williamson, Adam Finkelstein
arXiv:2609.35672 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-28
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇把 DETR 式的 slot decoder 搬到 stem 檢索上:一段混音輸出多個 slot embedding,在不指定樂器家族的情況下,MoisesDB 上 All R@1 為 56.8%,而 CIR–MuQ 基線只有 23.6%;即使要模型自己預測 stem 數量,R@1 仍有 56.0%,高於基線在指定家族後的 52.4%。Why this one: It transplants a DETR-style slot decoder into stem retrieval: one mixture yields several slot embeddings, and without any instrument-family filter it reaches 56.8% All R@1 on MoisesDB versus 23.6% for the CIR–MuQ baseline; even when the model must predict the stem count itself, R@1 is 56.0%, above the baseline's family-filtered 52.4%.

動機Motivation

音樂製作人常拿一首現成歌曲當參考,想在自己的 stem 素材庫(單一樂器的獨立錄音)裡找到聽起來像其中某個聲部的聲音。現行做法把整段混音壓成單一 embedding 再去比對素材庫,但這個向量混雜了所有樂器,只能代表「整體聽感」。

結果是使用者得先指定目標樂器家族(例如「吉他」)縮小搜尋範圍,才有像樣的召回率。這一步需要有經驗的人介入,也讓流程難以自動化。作者想把「先講出樂器家族」這個生產任務,改成「看幾組檢索結果再挑一組」的辨識任務。

Music producers often use an existing song as a reference and want to find, in their library of stems (isolated single-instrument recordings), a sound resembling one of its parts. Current systems squeeze the whole mixture into one embedding and match that against the library, but the vector blends every instrument and represents only the overall sound.

As a result the user must first name the target instrument family (say, "guitar") to narrow the search before recall becomes decent. That step needs skilled human input and blocks automation. The authors want to turn "naming the family up front" into a recognition task: look at a few retrieval lists and pick one.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名的先前工作有四條線。Contrastive Instrument Retrieval(CIR,Le Vaillant 與 Molle,ICASSP 2026)微調 AST,讓混音 embedding 靠近其 stem 的 embedding,但混音仍是單一向量,所以要靠家族篩選。Show Me the Instruments(SMTI,Kim 等人,ICASSP 2023)先訓練樂器分類 encoder,再訓練混音 encoder 去預測一組凍結的 solo embedding,用 permutation-invariant training 配對。

第三條線是先用 HT-Demucs 做音源分離再編碼,但分離瑕疵會污染 embedding,而且輸出類別固定。第四條是 slot 模型:AudioSlots 處理雙人語音、MusicSlots 處理合成和弦、CARL 從凍結特徵學 slot。這些都不是拿多軌真實錄音做 stem 級 contrastive 訓練。

跟既有方法最像的其實是 SMTI:兩者都用最小成本配對把輸出 slot 對到 solo embedding。差別在 SMTI 第二階段只是模仿凍結的老師,而 Stembed 讓 solo 與混音共用同一個可微調的 encoder,並用 supervised contrastive loss 一起訓練。slot decoder 本身則直接沿用 DETR 的設計。

The paper names four lines of prior work. Contrastive Instrument Retrieval (CIR; Le Vaillant and Molle, ICASSP 2026) fine-tunes an AST so a mixture embedding lands near its stems' embeddings, but the mixture is still one vector, hence the family filter. Show Me the Instruments (SMTI; Kim et al., ICASSP 2023) first trains an instrument classification encoder, then trains a mixture encoder to predict a set of frozen solo embeddings via permutation-invariant training.

The third line is to separate first with HT-Demucs and then encode, but separation artifacts contaminate the embeddings and the output categories are fixed. The fourth is slot models: AudioSlots (two-speaker mixtures), MusicSlots (synthesized chords), and CARL (slots from frozen features). None does stem-level contrastive training on real multitrack recordings.

The closest existing method is SMTI: both use minimum-cost matching to align output slots with solo embeddings. The difference is that SMTI's second stage merely imitates a frozen teacher, whereas Stembed shares one fine-tunable encoder between solos and mixtures and trains them jointly with a supervised contrastive loss. The slot decoder itself is taken straight from DETR.

方法Method

每個訓練 batch 有 B=32 組、每組 n=4 個同一首歌的 stem,並由此組出 10 個音訊節點:4 個 solo、2 組不相交的雙 stem 混音、4 組三 stem 混音。每個節點各自取樣 5 秒視窗並正規化到 −18 LUFS,由同一個 encoder 處理,所以 solo 與混音在同一個 embedding 空間。

backbone 是 MuQ(只取前 4 層 Conformer,微調),後面接 4 層 transformer slot decoder:K=4 個可學習 query 對 frame token 做 cross-attention,每個 slot 輸出一個 256 維 embedding 和一個 presence logit。整體 191M 參數,其中 123M 在 MuQ。

訓練時,把 m 個 solo embedding 用最小平方歐氏距離配對到 m 個不同 slot(K 與 m 很小,直接窮舉,不用 Hungarian)。被配到的 slot 繼承該 stem 的身分,同一身分的 embedding 互為正樣本,其餘為負樣本,套用溫度 τ=0.1 的 supervised contrastive loss。presence head 用二元交叉熵預測「這個 slot 有沒有被配到」,總損失為 L=1.0·Lc+0.1·Lp。

推論時沒有 solo 可配對,就取 presence 最高的 m 個 slot 當查詢;不知道 m 時,取 presence 機率超過 50% 的 slot。評估用 oracle 依最小成本把每個目標 stem 指派給一個被選中的 slot,模擬使用者的選擇。

Each training batch holds B=32 groups of n=4 stems from one song, from which ten audio nodes are built: 4 solos, 2 disjoint pairs, and 4 triples. Each node samples its own 5-second window, is normalized to −18 LUFS, and goes through the same encoder, so solos and mixtures live in one embedding space.

The backbone is MuQ (first 4 Conformer layers, fine-tuned), followed by a 4-block transformer slot decoder: K=4 learned queries cross-attend to frame tokens, and each slot emits a 256-dimensional embedding and a presence logit. The model has 191M parameters, 123M of them in MuQ.

During training, the m solo embeddings are matched to m distinct slots by minimum squared Euclidean distance (K and m are tiny, so exact enumeration replaces the Hungarian algorithm). Matched slots inherit that stem's identity; same-identity embeddings are positives, the rest negatives, under a supervised contrastive loss with τ=0.1. A presence head trained with binary cross-entropy predicts whether a slot was matched; the total loss is L=1.0·Lc+0.1·Lp.

At inference no solos exist to match against, so the m slots with the highest presence become queries; when m is unknown, slots with presence probability above 50% are kept. Evaluation uses an oracle that assigns each target stem to one selected slot by minimum cost, emulating the user's choice.

限制Limitations

最明顯的限制是評估規模與設定。切分只有 166/37/35 首歌(9 個 artist group 在測試集),表中的標準差只反映訓練 seed,不含測試歌曲太少造成的不確定性。評估時資料庫裡放的是查詢用的原始 stem,而實際目標是找「相似但不同」的 stem;論文自己把這列為未來工作。

第二,slot 選擇靠 oracle。真實使用者要看每個 slot 的檢索結果再挑,這個人機環節沒有做使用者研究,只有網站 demo。第三,數量預測偏向 3:預測 stem 數量的 exact-count 準確率是兩 stem 61.9%、三 stem 70.8%、四 stem 43.3%,對兩 stem 混音多估 30.8%,對四 stem 混音少估 56.7%;訓練只有兩到三 stem 混音,沒有四 stem。

第四,優勢並非處處成立。加上家族篩選後,MoisesDB 上 CIR–MuQ 的 R@5 是 81.7%,略高於 Stembed 的 81.2%;Slakh2100 上 Family R@5 是 70.0% 對 68.0%,CIR–MuQ 也較高。Stembed 在 Slakh 的 All R@1 只有 38.1%,遠低於 MoisesDB 的 56.8%。

第五,基線並非完全同條件:CIR–MuQ 用 8 層 MuQ(220M)且是 triplet + 歐氏距離,Stembed 用 4 層,作者自己承認 slot 版 triplet 對照組與 CIR–MuQ 也在距離函數、margin 與深度上有差異。

The most visible limit is evaluation scale and setup. The split is 166/37/35 songs (9 artist groups in test), and the reported standard deviations reflect training seeds only, not the uncertainty from having so few test songs. The gallery contains the original query stems, whereas the real goal is finding similar-but-different stems; the paper itself lists this as future work.

Second, slot choice relies on an oracle. Real users would inspect each slot's results and pick one, but there is no user study, only a web demo. Third, count prediction is biased toward three: exact-count accuracy is 61.9% for two-stem, 70.8% for three-stem, and 43.3% for four-stem mixtures; the detector overcounts 30.8% of two-stem mixtures and undercounts 56.7% of four-stem ones. Training used two- and three-stem mixtures but no four-stem ones.

Fourth, the advantage is not universal. With the family filter, CIR–MuQ's R@5 on MoisesDB is 81.7% versus Stembed's 81.2%; on Slakh2100 Family R@5 is 70.0% versus 68.0%, again favoring CIR–MuQ. Stembed's All R@1 on Slakh is only 38.1%, well below its 56.8% on MoisesDB.

Fifth, the baselines are not perfectly matched: CIR–MuQ uses 8 MuQ layers (220M) with a triplet loss and Euclidean distance while Stembed uses 4 layers, and the authors concede that their slot-based triplet control also differs from CIR–MuQ in distance function, margin, and depth.

討論Discussion

數字拆開來看,這篇的主張有相當清楚的證據支撐。All R@1 的比較有一個結構性上限:池化式 encoder 每段混音只回傳一份排名,三個目標 stem 至多一個排第一,所以 All R@1 天花板是 33.3%,CIR–MuQ 的 23.6% 本來就受限。Stembed 的 56.8% 超過這個天花板,證明多個 slot 確實各自去找不同的 stem。

跟「先分離再檢索」的對照也有說服力:HT-Demucs + Stembed 為 53.2%,比直接檢索的 56.8% 低,而且還讓 oracle 從六個分離輸出中挑。凍結 encoder 最多只到 10.7%。用真實 stem 查詢時 Stembed 為 75.1%,代表從混音抽出目標表徵仍損失約 18 個百分點。

消融最值得記住的是負樣本:只留同曲負樣本時 R@1 掉到 7.9%,只留跨曲負樣本是 45.3%,兩者都要;把同曲與跨曲混合成隨機跨曲混音幾乎不影響(57.5% 對 56.8%),暗示時間對齊並非必要。另外 cosine-triplet 版本達到 58.0%,高於 InfoNCE 的 56.8%,所以增益主要來自 slot 結構而非損失函數。

延伸的方向是作者提到的:用 slot 當生成模型的條件,或以檢索加重合成重建混音。對做 MIR 檢索的人,這篇最可重用的是那套訓練圖(solo、pair、triple 共用 stem)與 presence 預測,可以搬到其他多來源檢索任務。

Broken down, the claim is well supported by the numbers. The All R@1 comparison has a structural ceiling: a pooled encoder returns one ranking per mixture, so at most one of three target stems can rank first, capping All R@1 at 33.3%; CIR–MuQ's 23.6% is bounded by that. Stembed's 56.8% exceeds the ceiling, showing the slots really do chase different stems.

The comparison with separate-then-retrieve is also convincing: HT-Demucs + Stembed reaches 53.2%, below direct retrieval's 56.8%, even though the oracle picks among six separator outputs. The best frozen encoder reaches only 10.7%. With true stems as queries Stembed gets 75.1%, so extracting a target representation from a mixture costs about 18 points.

The most instructive ablation concerns negatives: same-song negatives alone give 7.9% R@1, cross-song negatives alone 45.3%, and both are needed. Replacing temporally aligned same-song mixtures with random cross-song mixtures barely matters (57.5% versus 56.8%), suggesting alignment is not essential. The cosine-triplet variant reaches 58.0%, above InfoNCE's 56.8%, so the gain comes mainly from the slot structure rather than the loss.

The authors point to slots as conditioning for generative models, or retrieval-plus-resynthesis to rebuild a mixture. For MIR retrieval work, the most reusable pieces are the training graph (solos, pairs, and triples sharing stems) and the presence prediction, both of which could transfer to other multi-source retrieval tasks.

JazzSAMBA: A Synchronous and Asynchronous Multi-take Band Audio Dataset of Jazz Standards for Live Music Models

JazzSAMBA:供即時音樂模型使用的爵士標準曲同步/非同步多 take 樂團音訊資料集

Phillip Long, Jacob Nguyen, Jace Hosto, Gage Hosto, Jett Takazawa, Fares Nofal, Sebastian Stade, Nithya Shikarpur, Julian McAuley, Cheng-Zhi Anna Huang, Stephen Brade, Aleksandra Teng Ma
arXiv:2609.34931 · cs.SD, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.MM, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-09-28
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：資料集論文:76 首爵士標準曲、8 位樂手、原創錄製的逐樂器 stem,分非同步(疊錄)50 首與同步(現場合奏)26 首,偏好 take 約 8.1 小時、兩個層級共約 16.1 小時;示範的兩個基線任務中,音源分離對鋼琴與管樂有明確提升,但譜面條件的伴奏效果差異很小。Why this one: A dataset paper: 76 jazz standards by 8 musicians, originally recorded per-instrument stems in 50 asynchronous (overdubbed) and 26 synchronous (live ensemble) songs, about 8.1 hours of preferred takes and 16.1 hours across both tiers; of the two demo tasks, source separation improves clearly for piano and horns, while the effect of chart conditioning on accompaniment is small.

動機Motivation

多數多軌資料集(MUSDB18、MedleyDB、MoisesDB)偏重流行與搖滾,任務被限制在人聲、鼓、貝斯這類標準樂器組合,曲子也是事先編排好的。爵士把預先寫好的譜(lead sheet)與即興結合,即興的空間受和聲與曲式約束,而不是完全不受限,這使它成為互動伴奏與即時生成的合適測試場。

作者認為缺口在於:沒有一個「原創錄製、逐樂器乾淨 stem、附時間對齊的和弦與曲式標註」的爵士標準曲資料集。互動伴奏、即時生成和音源分離都需要乾淨的合奏 stem;用譜面做條件的生成與具曲式意識的分析,也需要能對齊的結構標註。

Most multitrack datasets (MUSDB18, MedleyDB, MoisesDB) emphasize pop and rock, restrict tasks to standard instrument sets like vocals, drums, and bass, and involve pre-arranged pieces. Jazz combines pre-composed lead sheets with improvisation, and the improvisation space is bounded by harmony and form rather than left unconstrained, which makes it a suitable testbed for interactive accompaniment and live generation.

The authors see the gap as the absence of an originally recorded jazz-standards dataset with clean per-instrument stems and time-aligned chord and form annotations. Interactive accompaniment, live generation, and source separation all need clean ensemble stems, and chart-conditioned generation and form-aware analysis need structural labels that can be aligned to them.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文用一張比較表(Table 1)把相關資料集按 stem、take、雙協定、和弦、曲式、獨奏者、MIDI 逐項對照。MUSDB18、MoisesDB 有 stem 但不是爵士;Slakh2100 是渲染的 MIDI 合成。爵士方面,Weimar Jazz Database 與 Jazz Structure Dataset 提供和聲、曲式與獨奏轉譜標註,Jazz Harmony 資料集提供對齊音訊的和弦,JAZZVAR 是獨奏鋼琴變奏。

最接近的是 Jazz Trio Database(Cheston 等人,TISMIR 2024):它有鋼琴三重奏標註,但 stem 是靠音源分離產生,不是原始錄音;另一個是同一組作者的 H2H Music Improv,提供逐樂器、多 take 的自由即興,但不限於特定風格、也不是爵士標準曲。JazzSAMBA 的差別是把近距離收音的合奏 stem、雙錄音協定與標準曲上的定時譜面標註放在同一份資料裡。

互動伴奏一側,論文列舉 Anticipatory Music Transformer(離線)以及 RL-Duet、ReaLchords、StreamGen、RealJam、LiveBand 等線上系統,多半是符號流行/古典或非爵士音訊。這些系統正是這份資料集想服務的對象,實驗裡也直接拿 StreamGen 來當伴奏基線。

The paper compares related corpora feature by feature in Table 1: stems, takes, dual protocols, chords, sections, soloists, and MIDI. MUSDB18 and MoisesDB have stems but are not jazz; Slakh2100 is rendered MIDI. On the jazz side, the Weimar Jazz Database and Jazz Structure Dataset offer harmony, form, and solo transcription annotations, the Jazz Harmony dataset provides audio-aligned chords, and JAZZVAR covers solo-piano variations.

The closest is the Jazz Trio Database (Cheston et al., TISMIR 2024): it annotates piano-trio recordings, but its stems come from source separation rather than original tracking. Another is H2H Music Improv from the same group, which provides per-instrument multi-take free improvisation but is not tied to any style or to jazz standards. JazzSAMBA's difference is combining close-mic ensemble stems, two recording protocols, and timed chart annotations on standards in one corpus.

On the interactive-accompaniment side, the paper lists the Anticipatory Music Transformer (offline) and the online systems RL-Duet, ReaLchords, StreamGen, RealJam, and LiveBand, mostly on symbolic pop/classical or non-jazz audio. These are the systems the dataset aims to serve, and the experiments use StreamGen directly as the accompaniment baseline.

方法Method

資料以兩種協定錄製。非同步協定下樂手分別錄音:先錄鼓建立節奏基礎,其餘樂器依序疊錄在先前的偏好 take 上,每種樂器錄兩個乾淨 take,偏好 take 組成偏好混音,其餘為備選層。速度固定,所以偏好混音標註一次即可複製到備選層。同步協定則是整個樂團一起錄兩個 take,樂手選一個偏好,近距離收音會有串音(bleed),這是較難的情境,每個 take 獨立標註。

標註共用同一個小節格:小節起點與序號;和弦、曲式與獨奏者用「小節加上小節內比例」的偏移表示。和弦標籤來自書面譜(用 MusPyExpress 解析內部譜庫,再由樂手對時),保留原始符號(如 E-7b5),不是從音訊估計。曲式標籤用爵士慣用詞(intro、head-in、head-out、solos、outro),另附各段樂手與獨奏順序。

規模上共 76 首標準曲、8 位樂手,樂器是鼓、貝斯、鋼琴、小號與薩克斯風(每曲只用中音或次中音其一);50 首非同步、26 首同步。偏好混音約 8.1 小時(非同步 5.5、同步 2.5),兩層合計約 16.1 小時,速度 75–270 BPM,音訊為 48 kHz。鼓與鋼琴在非同步協定下有真值 MIDI,其餘 MIDI 由 MuScriptor 自動轉譜;有串音的 stem 另附 Wiener 式去串音版本。

兩個示範任務:一是音源分離,微調 HT-Demucs 6s、Open-Unmix 與 BS-RoFormer SW,在非同步 stem 加總混音上分離鼓、貝斯、鋼琴與管樂;二是譜面條件伴奏,微調 StreamGen 的因果解碼器,比較僅聆聽、加曲式、加和弦、兩者皆加四種條件。

The data is recorded under two protocols. In the asynchronous protocol musicians record separately: drums first to set the rhythmic foundation, then the other instruments overdub on earlier preferred takes. Each instrument records two clean takes; preferred takes form the preferred mixture and the rest an alternate tier. Because tempo is fixed, the preferred mixture is annotated once and the labels are copied to the alternate tier. In the synchronous protocol the whole band records two ensemble takes together and the musicians pick a preferred one; close-mic bleed makes this the harder condition, and takes are annotated independently.

Annotations share one bar grid: bar onsets and indices, with chords, sections, and soloists expressed as a bar plus a fractional offset. Chord labels come from written lead sheets (an internal chart collection parsed with MusPyExpress, then time-aligned by the musicians), keeping the written symbol (e.g. E-7b5) rather than an audio estimate. Section labels use jazz form tags (intro, head-in, head-out, solos, outro), with a per-section musician table and an ordered solo sequence.

In scale: 76 standards by 8 musicians on drums, bass, piano, trumpet, and saxophone (alto or tenor, exclusive per song), 50 asynchronous and 26 synchronous. Preferred mixtures total about 8.1 hours (5.5 asynchronous, 2.5 synchronous) and both tiers about 16.1 hours, at 75–270 BPM and 48 kHz. Drums and piano have ground-truth MIDI in the asynchronous protocol, the rest from MuScriptor automatic transcription; stems with bleed also ship in a Wiener-style de-bleeded version.

The two demo tasks are: source separation, fine-tuning HT-Demucs 6s, Open-Unmix, and BS-RoFormer SW to separate drums, bass, piano, and horns from summed asynchronous stems; and chart-conditioned accompaniment, fine-tuning StreamGen's causal decoder under four conditions: listen-only, +sections, +chords, and +both.

限制Limitations

最大的限制是測試集極小:分離評估的非同步測試只有 5 首,伴奏評估是非同步 5 首、同步 3 首,而且全文沒有信賴區間或顯著性檢定。同步協定(26 首)沒有拿來評估分離,只出現在伴奏實驗裡。8 位樂手、5 種樂器,風格與音色多樣性有限。

分離結果並不支持「微調帶來全面提升」。零樣本的 BS-RoFormer 已是最強:鼓 19.12、貝斯 20.83、鋼琴 18.31、管樂 21.57 dB,微調後為 18.97、20.62、17.54、21.15,四項都略低,作者自己也說零樣本仍是 BS 的主要結果,微調後落在約 0.8 dB 內。有明顯好處的是原本較弱的模型:HT-Demucs 的鋼琴從 4.92 升到 9.42 dB,管樂從無法報告變成 14.62 dB;Open-Unmix 的鋼琴與管樂也從沒有分數變成 6.07 與 9.38 dB。

伴奏實驗的差異更小、也更不穩定。非同步鼓的 COCOLA 是僅聆聽 52.2、加和弦 52.5、加曲式 50.7、兩者皆加 51.4,而真值為 55.6;管樂的 Beat F1 只有 0.02 到 0.06。條件加入後有些指標反而下降(例如非同步貝斯 Beat F1 從 0.16 降到 0.09–0.10)。作者也只說這些標註「能區分條件策略」,並稱這是「一條基線」。

此外,「首個」的宣稱是相對於作者列出的資料集而言;和弦與曲式標註在偏好混音上是人工對時,但同步 take 的標註品質沒有量化,MIDI 有一部分是自動轉譜,也就不是真值。

The biggest limitation is tiny test sets: separation is evaluated on only 5 asynchronous test songs, accompaniment on 5 asynchronous and 3 synchronous, with no confidence intervals or significance tests anywhere. The synchronous protocol (26 songs) is not used for separation at all and appears only in the accompaniment experiment. Eight musicians and five instruments limit stylistic and timbral diversity.

The separation results do not support "fine-tuning helps across the board." Zero-shot BS-RoFormer is already strongest: drums 19.12, bass 20.83, piano 18.31, horns 21.57 dB; after fine-tuning they are 18.97, 20.62, 17.54, 21.15, slightly lower on all four, and the authors themselves say zero-shot remains BS's headline result, with fine-tuning within about 0.8 dB. The clear gains are for weaker models: HT-Demucs piano rises from 4.92 to 9.42 dB and horns go from unreportable to 14.62 dB; Open-Unmix piano and horns go from no score to 6.07 and 9.38 dB.

The accompaniment differences are smaller and less stable. For asynchronous drums COCOLA is 52.2 listen-only, 52.5 +chords, 50.7 +sections, 51.4 +both, versus 55.6 for ground truth; horn Beat F1 is only 0.02 to 0.06. Some metrics get worse with conditioning (asynchronous bass Beat F1 falls from 0.16 to 0.09–0.10). The authors only say the annotations "can discriminate conditioning strategies" and call this "one baseline."

In addition, "first" is relative to the corpora the authors list; chord and section labels on preferred mixtures are hand-aligned, but the annotation quality of synchronous takes is not quantified, and part of the MIDI comes from automatic transcription and so is not ground truth.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值主要在資料本身,而不是實驗結論。資料的設計有兩個實用的巧思:非同步協定固定速度,讓一份標註可以套到所有備選 take;同步協定保留真實串音,使「乾淨疊錄」與「現場合奏」可以直接對照。兩層 take 加上樂手自己選的偏好版本,也提供了「同一首曲子的表演差異」這種其他資料集少見的變因。

對 MIR 使用者最直接的用途是分離:如果要在爵士合奏上分離鋼琴或管樂,4.92 → 9.42 dB(HT-Demucs 鋼琴)這種幅度的提升是實際的,但對 BS-RoFormer 這類強模型則沒有必要微調。ChoraleBricks 的管樂資料反而把 BS 的管樂從 21.57 降到 2.00 dB,說明合唱式管樂並不能遷移到爵士合奏。

伴奏實驗比較像是「這份標註能做什麼」的 demo,而不是可用來下結論的基準;要當作基準,至少需要更多測試曲目與變異數估計。如果之後有人拿它訓練即時伴奏模型,同步層的串音與 26 首的規模會是第一個瓶頸。

The value here is mainly the data, not the experimental conclusions. Two design choices are practical: the asynchronous protocol fixes tempo so one annotation covers all alternate takes, and the synchronous protocol keeps genuine bleed, so clean overdubs and live ensemble playing can be compared directly. Two take tiers plus musician-chosen preferred versions also provide performance variation on the same piece, which few other datasets offer.

For MIR users the most direct use is separation: to separate piano or horns from a jazz combo, an improvement on the scale of 4.92 → 9.42 dB (HT-Demucs piano) is real, but for a strong model like BS-RoFormer fine-tuning is unnecessary. Fine-tuning on ChoraleBricks wind data actually drops BS horns from 21.57 to 2.00 dB, showing chorale wind parts do not transfer to jazz combos.

The accompaniment experiment is better read as a demo of what the annotations can do than as a benchmark to draw conclusions from; as a benchmark it would need more test songs and variance estimates. If someone trains a live accompaniment model on it, the synchronous tier's bleed and its 26-song size will be the first bottlenecks.

YuE2: Unifying Symbolic and Audio Music Generation at Frontier Quality

YuE2:以符號規劃統一符號與音訊音樂生成,達到前沿品質

Ruibin Yuan, Jiahao Pan, Junyan Jiang, et al. (HKUST, M-A-P, ACE Studio 等/et al.)
arXiv:2609.33757 · eess.AS, cs.LG, cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-27
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：單一 3.58B 的 AR–NAR Mixture-of-Transformers 先寫出可讀的 ABC 樂譜,再產生語義 token 與聲學潛在向量;同一 checkpoint 下,專家偏好有規劃 49.3% 對無規劃 34.6%,在 WildSongBench 上 SongBench Global Avg 為 6.73(best-of-8 為 6.96),與 Suno v5 的整體偏好幾乎持平(40.4% 對 39.9%)。Why this one: A single 3.58B AR–NAR Mixture-of-Transformers first writes a readable ABC score, then semantic tokens and acoustic latents; with the same checkpoint, experts prefer planning 49.3% to 34.6% for no planning, and on WildSongBench it reaches a SongBench Global Avg of 6.73 (6.96 for best-of-8), with overall expert preference against Suno v5 nearly even (40.4% versus 39.9%).

動機Motivation

作者把「生成一首歌」看成逐層承諾(progressive musical commitment):文字與歌詞表達意圖;樂譜決定旋律、和聲、節奏與曲式;演奏與音訊層再決定時值、音色、表情與混音。符號模型(如 Music Transformer)能把作曲攤開來,但停在譜面;音訊模型(MusicLM、MusicGen、YuE)產出完整錄音,卻讓作曲隱含在音訊裡,使用者無法檢查或修改。

問題是:單一模型能否走完整條階層,讓作曲既可讀、可編輯,又提升成品的品質?難處在於一般音訊語料沒有對齊的音符、和弦、拍點與段落標註,無法直接學這個階層,所以作者還得自己造出符號與語義兩種監督訊號。

The authors view generating a song as progressive musical commitment: text and lyrics state intent; a score settles melody, harmony, rhythm, and form; performance and audio levels then resolve timing, timbre, expression, and mixing. Symbolic models (such as Music Transformer) expose composition but stop at the score; audio models (MusicLM, MusicGen, YuE) produce complete recordings but leave composition implicit, so users cannot inspect or revise it.

The question is whether a single model can traverse the whole hierarchy, keeping composition readable and editable while improving the finished song. The obstacle is that ordinary audio corpora lack aligned notes, chords, beats, and sections, so the hierarchy cannot be learned directly, and the authors must build both symbolic and semantic supervision themselves.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文的相關研究點名很具體。音訊端:MusicLM、MusicGen、MusicFlow、YuE、SongBloom、LeVo 2、DiffRhythm 2、HeartMuLa、Muse,這些都不輸出可讀樂譜。符號端:Music Transformer、SymPAC 只生成符號,MIDI-DDSP 把樂譜接到器樂演奏,BACH 把符號擴到歌曲但要外部工具合成人聲與伴奏。

和 YuE2 最像的是 Seed-Music 的 lead-sheet 轉歌曲流程(專有、細節有限、沒有公開實作),以及規劃類方法:MusiCoT 先生成量化的音訊-文字 embedding、ACE-Step 1.5 先規劃速度、調性、長度與結構。差別在 YuE2 規劃的是可讀的 ABC 樂譜(旋律加和弦),而不是抽象 embedding 或元資料。

架構上,「AR 語言模型加獨立 DiT」是常見設計(Qwen-Music、HeartMuLa、LeVo 2 等),YuE2 改用 Mixture-of-Transformers,把兩者放進同一個 28 層 backbone、共享一次注意力計算。表徵端則承接 MERT、MusicFM、MuQ 這條線,並用 SheetSage、MT3、Beat This!、ChordFormer、SongFormer 的功能取代多個專用分析器。從技術上看,「先寫譜再生成」是 chain-of-thought 式規劃在音樂上的變體;新意在譜面是給人讀與改的。

The related-work coverage is specific. Audio side: MusicLM, MusicGen, MusicFlow, YuE, SongBloom, LeVo 2, DiffRhythm 2, HeartMuLa, and Muse, none of which outputs a readable score. Symbolic side: Music Transformer and SymPAC generate only symbols, MIDI-DDSP connects scores to instrumental performance, and BACH extends symbolic generation to songs but needs external tools for vocals and accompaniment.

Closest to YuE2 are Seed-Music's lead-sheet-to-song pipeline (proprietary, limited details, no public implementation) and planning methods: MusiCoT generates quantized audio-text embeddings first, and ACE-Step 1.5 plans tempo, key, duration, and structure. The difference is that YuE2 plans a readable ABC score (melody plus chords) rather than abstract embeddings or metadata.

Architecturally, an AR language model plus a separate DiT is the common design (Qwen-Music, HeartMuLa, LeVo 2, and others); YuE2 instead uses a Mixture-of-Transformers, putting both in one 28-layer backbone with a shared attention computation. On representations it continues the MERT, MusicFM, and MuQ line, and replaces several specialized analyzers (SheetSage, MT3, Beat This!, ChordFormer, SongFormer) with one transcriber. Technically, "write the score first" is a chain-of-thought-style planning variant for music; the novelty is that the score is meant to be read and edited by people.

方法Method

生成分兩段:p(s,c,z|y)=p_AR(s,c|y)·p_NAR(z|y,s,c)。s 是 ABC 樂譜(用 BPE 序列化),c 是 25 Hz 的語義 token,z 是 25 Hz、64 維的連續聲學潛在向量,最後由另訓的 VAE 解碼成 48 kHz 立體聲。AR 部分因果地預測 s 與 c;NAR 部分以雙向 flow matching 預測 z,並以完整的 s 與 c 為條件。

backbone 是 28 層、約 3.58B 參數的 Mixture-of-Transformers:每層的 AR 與 NAR 專家各有自己的正規化、QKV 投影與 MLP,但共享同一次注意力,注意力遮罩是離散位置因果、聲學位置雙向、離散位置不可看聲學位置。訓練目標為 L=0.25·L_AR+L_FM,資料約 34.6 萬小時,整首歌塞進 24,576 個位置的 context。訓練時混合四種任務(有無譜、有無語義 token),所以同一 checkpoint 能做有規劃與無規劃的生成。

缺少對齊標註的問題靠兩個新模型解:MERT2 用凍結的 MuQ 與 Qwen2-Audio 兩個編碼器的特徵,經共享瓶頸與 4 級殘差 VQ 產生「雙視角」目標,再經四階段課程訓練;語義 tokenizer 是加了因果注意力、再用歌詞 CTC 與 mel/chroma 重建做監督、最後量化成 32,768 個碼的版本(375 bit/s,碼本使用率超過 99%)。SheetSage2 則是 MERT2-FS 加 LoRA 與 6 層 RoFormer 自迴歸解碼器,一次輸出旋律、和弦、調性、拍點與曲式,標籤先由非自迴歸的 Prober 產生。

同一 checkpoint 支援三種用法:創作(自己生成 s)、編輯(把修改後的 s 當固定前綴,重生成下游 token 與潛在向量)、翻唱(SheetSage2 從參考錄音抽出 s,配新風格描述)。三者共用同一個樂譜介面,不需要翻唱配對資料。

Generation has two stages: p(s,c,z|y)=p_AR(s,c|y)·p_NAR(z|y,s,c). Here s is an ABC score (BPE-serialized), c is a 25 Hz semantic token sequence, and z is a 25 Hz, 64-dimensional continuous acoustic latent, decoded to 48 kHz stereo by a separately trained VAE. The AR part predicts s and c causally; the NAR part predicts z by bidirectional flow matching conditioned on the full s and c.

The backbone is a 28-layer, roughly 3.58B-parameter Mixture-of-Transformers: in each layer the AR and NAR experts have their own normalization, QKV projections, and MLPs but share one attention computation, with a mask that is causal among discrete positions, bidirectional among acoustic ones, and blocks discrete positions from seeing acoustic ones. The objective is L=0.25·L_AR+L_FM over about 346,000 hours of music, with whole songs packed into a 24,576-position context. Four task variants (with or without score, with or without semantic tokens) are mixed in training, so one checkpoint can generate with and without planning.

Two new models supply the missing aligned labels. MERT2 builds "two-view" targets from frozen MuQ and Qwen2-Audio features through a shared bottleneck and a 4-level residual VQ, then trains through a four-stage curriculum; the semantic tokenizer adds causal attention, supervises with lyric CTC and mel/chroma reconstruction, and quantizes to a 32,768-entry codebook (375 bit/s, over 99% codebook utilization). SheetSage2 is MERT2-FS with LoRA and a 6-layer autoregressive RoFormer decoder emitting melody, chords, key, beats, and form in one sequence, with labels first produced by a non-autoregressive Prober.

One checkpoint supports three uses: creation (it generates s itself), editing (a revised s is fixed as a prefix and downstream tokens and latents are regenerated), and covers (SheetSage2 extracts s from a reference recording, paired with a new style description). All three share the score interface and need no cover-pair training data.

限制Limitations

這份技術報告沒有獨立的限制章節,以下是從內文讀出的問題。第一,「與商用系統競爭」的證據強度不一:自動指標上 best-of-8 的 SongBench Global Avg 為 6.9632,是所有系統中最高;但 best-of-8 的挑選標準正是 SongBench Musicality(其次 Q3O、PER),等於用評估指標來挑輸出,不是同一把尺。專家聆聽則對 Suno v6 明顯落後,整體偏好 31.6% 對 59.3%,作者也坦承這與較高的 SongBench Avg 不一致。

第二,其他指標並非全面領先:SongEval 上 best-of-8 的 Avg 為 4.2960,低於公開系統 HeartMuLa 的 4.5519(但 HeartMuLa 曾用 SongEval 做後訓練篩選);歌詞 PER 為 0.0844(best-of-8 為 0.0979),公開系統中最低是 MiniMax Music 3 的 0.0627。文字對齊指標中 Suno v5 在 MuLan 與 AllMusicCaps 都領先。

第三,規劃效果雖顯著,樣本卻不大:有無規劃的直接比較只有 212 筆有效專家評價,整體品質 p=0.0070、音樂性 p=0.0141;旋律與和弦進行各只由 4 位聽者做 100 組配對。「專家」一詞涵蓋 289 位線上招募的參與者加 62 位付費標註者。第四,翻唱實驗顯示取捨:完整樂譜讓作品辨識(CLEWS mAP 0.647)最好,但 SongBench Musicality 是 5.104,低於無和弦的 5.490 與無樂譜的 5.691。

第五,監督來源有循環風險:YuE2 學的是 SheetSage2 的轉譜,而 SheetSage2-AR 又是只用 Prober 標籤訓練的,因此譜面錯誤會傳進生成模型。訓練資料的來源與授權在正文中沒有討論。

The report has no dedicated limitations section; the following are issues read from the body. First, the strength of evidence for "competitive with proprietary systems" varies: on automatic metrics best-of-8 has the highest SongBench Global Avg of any system at 6.9632, but best-of-8 selects by SongBench Musicality (then Q3O, then PER), so the output is picked with the evaluation metric itself, which is not the same yardstick. In expert listening it clearly trails Suno v6, 31.6% versus 59.3% overall preference, and the authors admit this conflicts with its higher SongBench Avg.

Second, it does not lead on every metric: on SongEval best-of-8's Avg is 4.2960, below the public system HeartMuLa's 4.5519 (though HeartMuLa used SongEval in post-training filtering); lyric PER is 0.0844 (0.0979 for best-of-8), and the lowest among public systems is MiniMax Music 3 at 0.0627. On text alignment Suno v5 leads on both MuLan and AllMusicCaps.

Third, the planning effect is significant but the sample is modest: the direct planning comparison has only 212 retained expert evaluations, p=0.0070 for overall quality and p=0.0141 for musicality; melody and chord progression are each judged by just 4 listeners over 100 pairs. "Expert" here covers 289 online-recruited participants plus 62 paid annotators. Fourth, the cover experiment shows a trade-off: the full score gives the best work identity (CLEWS mAP 0.647) but SongBench Musicality is 5.104, below 5.490 without chords and 5.691 without a score.

Fifth, the supervision has a circularity risk: YuE2 learns from SheetSage2 transcriptions, and SheetSage2-AR is trained only on Prober labels, so score errors propagate into the generator. The origin and licensing of the training data are not discussed in the body.

討論Discussion

最有說服力的證據是同 checkpoint 的對照。同一組 192 個 prompt、同一個解碼器與同樣的候選預算下,有規劃對無規劃的整體偏好是 49.3% 對 34.6%,音樂性 45.0% 對 29.4%,旋律 44.0% 對 29.5%,和弦進行 39.0% 對 21.0%。統一的 MoT 對分開的 LM+DiT(1.7B 加 1.7B)也有優勢:整體 53.4% 對 35.6%(p=0.0084),音質 48.5% 對 29.6%(p=3.06×10⁻⁴),但文字對齊幾乎持平(34.0% 對 33.5%)。

樂譜介面的檢驗也帶了數字:生成的譜與自己的錄音,旋律相似度 0.9464、和弦 0.9246,對不對應的錄音只有 0.2160 與 0.2473;調性一致 0.9307,人聲 onset F1 0.9396。局部編輯下,旋律音高準確度 84.17%、和聲 79.54%、節奏 73.43%,未編輯部分與原曲的一致性維持在 90.16% 至 94.34%,SongBench 品質 6.64–6.73,對照 6.70(未編輯)。零樣本翻唱在 948 首未見作品上勝過 SongEcho(CLEWS mAP 0.647 對 0.419,Discogs-VINet 0.288 對 0.122),移除和弦降到 0.598 與 0.179,移除整份樂譜降到 0.006 與 0.004。

對 MIR 社群,這篇的可攜資產也許不是生成模型,而是 MERT2 與 SheetSage2:MERT2 在 MARBLE 的 15 項指標上拿下 14 項,SheetSage2-AR 在 15 組基準—指標對中領先 12 組。真正需要保留疑問的是「與 Suno 競爭」這句話:在同一評估指標上做挑選、音質上贏而整體偏好對 Suno v6 輸,說得準確一點是「在自己挑的維度上接近」,而不是全面持平。

The most convincing evidence is the same-checkpoint comparison. On the same 192 prompts, decoder, and candidate budget, planning beats no planning 49.3% to 34.6% overall, 45.0% to 29.4% on musicality, 44.0% to 29.5% on melody, and 39.0% to 21.0% on chord progression. The unified MoT also beats a separate LM+DiT (1.7B plus 1.7B): 53.4% to 35.6% overall (p=0.0084) and 48.5% to 29.6% on audio quality (p=3.06×10⁻⁴), though text alignment is nearly even (34.0% versus 33.5%).

The score interface is tested with numbers too: generated scores versus their own recordings give melody similarity 0.9464 and chord 0.9246, against only 0.2160 and 0.2473 for mismatched recordings; key agreement is 0.9307 and vocal onset F1 0.9396. Under local edits, melody pitch accuracy is 84.17%, harmony 79.54%, and rhythm 73.43%, agreement with unedited content stays between 90.16% and 94.34%, and SongBench quality is 6.64–6.73 against 6.70 unedited. Zero-shot covers on 948 unseen works beat SongEcho (CLEWS mAP 0.647 versus 0.419, Discogs-VINet 0.288 versus 0.122); removing chords drops these to 0.598 and 0.179, and removing the whole score drops them to 0.006 and 0.004.

For the MIR community, the portable assets may not be the generator but MERT2 and SheetSage2: MERT2 leads 14 of 15 MARBLE metrics, and SheetSage2-AR leads 12 of 15 benchmark–metric pairs. The claim that deserves some doubt is "competitive with Suno": with selection on the evaluation metric and a loss to Suno v6 in overall preference despite winning on audio quality, it is more accurate to say it is close on dimensions the authors chose, not level across the board.
