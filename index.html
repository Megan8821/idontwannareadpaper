Music Hallucination in Audio-Language Models: A Hierarchical Formulation and Empirical Study

音訊語言模型的音樂幻覺:階層式建模與實證研究

Yu Liu, Jiahui Liu, Zhilin Liu, Cong Cao, Fangfang Yuan, Yuling Yang, Pin Xu, Yanbing Liu
arXiv:2609.20195 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-16
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：用「幻覺分五層」的方式(音源事件、時間屬性、調性、風格、情緒)診斷九個音訊語言模型(4個開源、5個閉源)在音樂描述任務上的幻覺,發現人聲誤判在九個模型上都存在(自由生成人聲幻覺率45.9%–61.3%),調性感知則是九個模型差異最大的一層(結構化問答準確率從 MiMo-V2-Omni 的 0.0% 到 Qwen2.5-Omni 的 68.6%);兩種免訓練緩解法在探測式評測裡有效,但換到自由生成格式常常失效甚至惡化。Why this one: The paper diagnoses hallucination in nine audio-language models (4 open-source, 5 closed-source) across a five-layer taxonomy (sound events, temporal properties, tonal attributes, style, emotion), finding vocal misperception universal across all nine models (free-form vocal hallucination rate 45.9%-61.3%) while tonal perception shows the largest cross-model spread (structured key accuracy from 0.0% for MiMo-V2-Omni to 68.6% for Qwen2.5-Omni); two training-free mitigation methods help under probing but often fail or worsen results under free-form generation.

動機Motivation

音訊語言模型(如 Qwen2-Audio、SALMONN、Audio-Flamingo、GPT-4o、Gemini)已經能做音樂描述與問答,但常常對輸入音訊生成聽起來很有把握、卻沒有證據支持的說法。跟「有沒有這個物件」這種單一命題的幻覺不同,音樂幻覺橫跨樂器、人聲、速度、調性、風格與情緒六種完全不同性質的宣稱,每一種需要不同的證據才能驗證,把它們混在一起打一個總分沒有意義。

音樂理解本身有階層性:聲音事件(有沒有這個樂器/人聲)、時間屬性(速度、節奏)、調性屬性(調性、和聲)、風格(曲風、年代)、情緒(情緒、氛圍),這五層在「能不能被客觀驗證」上差異很大。而且音樂描述天生是選擇性的——一段有效描述可能沒提到某個真實存在的屬性,如果把「沒提到」直接當成「幻覺」,判定標準本身就是錯的。

Audio-language models such as Qwen2-Audio, SALMONN, Audio-Flamingo, GPT-4o, and Gemini now produce music captions and answer questions about audio, but they often generate confident claims unsupported by the input. Unlike single-claim object-existence hallucination, music hallucination spans six qualitatively different claim types -- instruments, vocals, tempo, key, style, and emotion -- each verifiable by different evidence, so collapsing them into one score is not meaningful.

Music understanding is itself hierarchical: sound events (is this instrument/voice present), temporal properties (tempo, rhythm), tonal attributes (key, harmony), style (genre, era), and emotion (mood, affect) -- these five layers differ sharply in how objectively they can be verified. Music descriptions are also inherently selective: a valid property may simply go unmentioned in a reference caption, so treating "unmentioned" as "hallucinated" is the wrong standard to begin with.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

先前的音訊幻覺研究大多把音樂當成廣泛音訊領域裡的一種,沒有針對音樂本身的階層做細分。AHa-Bench 與 HalluAudio 是廣泛的音訊幻覺評測集,涵蓋各種聲音場景;Nishimura 等人(2024)與 AVHBench 專注在「音訊跟畫面對不上」這種音畫不一致的幻覺,不是音訊本身內部的宣稱錯誤。這篇論文跟它們的差別在於:第一次針對音樂做「分層」的幻覺診斷,並且用音樂資訊檢索(MIR)工具(節拍偵測、調性偵測)當客觀證據來源,而不是只靠語言模型自己判斷。

另一條相關的線是音樂理解能力評測,如 MusicCaps、Song Describer 這類字幕資源,MuChoMusic 評測多模態音樂理解,CMI-Bench 測指令遵循,MQAD 是音樂問答資料集。這些資源測的是模型「答得對不對」,用嚴格比對答案或固定選項,沒有處理「模型講了一個評測集裡沒問、但也沒被證據推翻的內容」該怎麼判定的問題——這正是幻覺判定跟能力評測的根本差異。

幻覺緩解這條線上,視覺語言模型有 VCD、M3ID、OPERA 這類對比式或信心感知的解碼方法,音訊視覺領域有 Audio-Aware Decoding、AVCD;這篇論文提出的 ADD-M 是同類對比式解碼在音樂領域的延伸,但作者自己發現:在探測式問答裡有效的緩解,換到自由生成格式常常失效甚至讓幻覺率變高,說明既有緩解方法的評測方式本身可能高估了效果。

Prior audio-hallucination research mostly treats music as one broad domain within general audio, without a music-specific layer taxonomy. AHa-Bench and HalluAudio are broad hallucination benchmarks covering assorted sound scenes; Nishimura et al. (2024) and AVHBench focus on audio-video mismatch rather than errors internal to the audio claim itself. This paper's difference is offering the first layer-wise hallucination diagnosis specific to music, and routing hard-layer claims through MIR tools (beat tracking, key detection) as objective evidence rather than relying on an LLM judge alone.

A second related line is music-understanding benchmarks: MusicCaps and Song Describer provide caption resources, MuChoMusic evaluates multimodal music understanding, CMI-Bench tests instruction-following, and MQAD is a music QA dataset. These measure whether a model's answer matches a reference via strict matching or fixed answer sets, without addressing what to do when a model states something the reference never asked about yet the audio never contradicts -- exactly the gap between capability evaluation and hallucination diagnosis.

On mitigation, vision-language work offers contrastive or confidence-aware decoding (VCD, M3ID, OPERA), and audio-visual work extends this with Audio-Aware Decoding and AVCD; this paper's ADD-M is the same contrastive-decoding idea extended to music. But the authors themselves find that gains under discriminative probing often fail to transfer to free-form generation -- sometimes even raising the hallucination rate -- suggesting that existing mitigation evaluation protocols may overstate real-world benefit.

方法Method

論文把音樂幻覺定義成「模型講出跟輸入音訊沒有根據的音樂宣稱」,只處理知覺層面的幻覺(有沒有正確聽出音樂內容),不處理事實記憶類的幻覺(比如亂編作曲家或發行年份)。核心分類法把音樂理解切成五層:硬層(L1 聲音事件如樂器與人聲、L2 時間屬性如速度節奏、L3 調性屬性如調性和聲)可以用訊號處理工具客觀驗證;軟層(L4 風格如曲風年代、L5 情緒如情緒氛圍)則要靠語言模型做語意矛盾判斷。

判定每個宣稱時用三元判決:「1=被證據推翻」「0=被證據支持或相容」「⊥=證據不足、無法判斷」,特別保留「無法判斷」這個選項,避免把單純沒提到的合法屬性錯判成幻覺。硬層宣稱優先用訊號處理工具驗證(人聲用語音活動偵測算「有聲比例」、速度用節拍追蹤估 BPM 並分成慢/中/快三檔、調性用調性輪廓比對演算法估計並設信心門檻 0.8,信心不足或速度落在模糊區間就直接判「無法判斷」),軟層則交給大型語言模型(DeepSeek V3)做語意矛盾檢查。

論文設計了三種互補的評測方式當「診斷儀表板」,不是合成單一分數:區分式探測(問是非題,比如「這段有沒有鋼琴」,用來測「無論有沒有,模型都愛回答有」的肯定偏誤)、自由形式描述(給統一提示詞讓模型自己生成完整描述,再自動抽取宣稱去驗證,最貼近真實使用情境)、結構化多維度提問(針對樂器、人聲、速度、曲風、情緒、調性各問一題,方便逐屬性比較)。三種評測互相之間的相關係數本身就是一個發現:區分式探測跟自由形式描述的排名相關只有 ρ=0.17(p=0.67),幾乎沒有關聯。

兩個免訓練緩解法:ADD-M(音訊依賴感知解碼)量測模型預測對「拿掉音訊」這個操作有多敏感,用 KL 散度算「音訊依賴分數」,再用這個分數修正下一個字的機率分佈,分數越低代表模型越不依賴音訊、修正力道就越大;v2 版本進一步加上「只在模型高熵、低音訊依賴時才修正」的閘門機制。TPA(分類導引知覺錨定)則是在生成前,先用五層分類法各問一題(比如「這段有什麼樂器」「速度慢中快」)取得回答,把這些回答串起來當額外上下文再讓模型生成完整描述。

資料方面,無標籤的探測與生成資料來自 MusicCaps,抽樣 500 段、實際可用 440 段;樂器驗證額外用 IRMAS 測試集(2,874 段、11 個樂器類別)當客觀標籤來源。九個受測模型分開源(Qwen2-Audio-7B、SALMONN-7B、Audio-Flamingo-3、Qwen2.5-Omni-7B)與閉源(Gemini 2.5 Flash、Gemini 2.5 Pro、Gemini 3 Pro、GPT-4o Audio、MiMo-V2-Omni)兩組,區分式探測大約問了 6,500 題是非題。

The paper defines music hallucination as a model generating music-related claims unsupported by the input audio, restricted to perceptual hallucination (whether the model correctly perceived the content) rather than factual-memory hallucination (e.g. inventing a composer or release year). The core taxonomy splits music understanding into five layers: hard layers (L1 sound events -- instruments/vocals, L2 temporal properties -- tempo/rhythm, L3 tonal attributes -- key/harmony) that can be objectively verified with signal-processing tools, and soft layers (L4 style -- genre/era, L5 emotion -- mood/affect) that require an LLM judge for semantic contradiction checking.

Each claim gets a ternary verdict: 1 = contradicted by evidence, 0 = supported or compatible, undetermined = insufficient evidence to judge -- explicitly keeping an undetermined option so a legitimately omitted attribute is never wrongly scored as a hallucination. Hard-layer claims are preferentially verified with signal-processing tools (a voice-activity detector's voiced-frame ratio for vocals; beat tracking mapped to slow/medium/fast tempo bands; a key-estimation algorithm gated at confidence 0.8, with ambiguous cases routed to undetermined), while soft-layer claims go to an LLM judge (DeepSeek V3) for contradiction checking.

Three complementary evaluation paradigms form a diagnostic dashboard rather than a single collapsed score: discriminative probing (yes/no questions, e.g. "does this contain piano?", isolating the affirmative bias -- the tendency to say yes regardless of ground truth); free-form description (one unified prompt, with claims auto-extracted from the open-ended output, closest to real deployment); and multi-dimensional structured querying (one question per attribute -- instruments, vocals, tempo, genre, mood, key). A finding in itself: cross-paradigm rank correlation between discriminative probing and free-form is only rho=0.17 (p=0.67), essentially no relationship.

Two training-free mitigation probes were tested. ADD-M (audio-dependency-aware decoding) measures how sensitive a token's predicted distribution is to ablating the audio, via KL divergence (an audio dependency score), then corrects the next-token distribution more strongly when that score is low; a v2 variant adds a gate that only corrects when the model is both high-entropy and weakly audio-dependent. TPA (taxonomy-guided perceptual anchoring) instead queries the model once per layer before generation (e.g. "what instruments are present," "is the tempo slow/medium/fast"), then feeds those answers back in as extra context for the final description.

Data for probing and free-form generation comes from MusicCaps, sampling 500 clips down to 440 usable after filtering; instrument verification additionally uses the IRMAS test split (2,874 clips, 11 instrument categories) as an objective label source. Nine models were tested, split into open-source (Qwen2-Audio-7B, SALMONN-7B, Audio-Flamingo-3, Qwen2.5-Omni-7B) and closed-source (Gemini 2.5 Flash, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Gemini 3 Pro, GPT-4o Audio, MiMo-V2-Omni), with roughly 6,500 yes/no questions asked under discriminative probing.

限制Limitations

作者自己強調 MuseDiag 目標是「可稽核的逐宣稱診斷」,不是要抓出音樂裡每一個錯誤;矛盾判定的規則本身讓幻覺率是保守的下界——語意不夠明確的宣稱一律歸類到「無法判斷」,所以要跟涵蓋率(coverage)一起看才有意義。目前的客觀證據閘門只鎖定「短音訊片段」跟「可規模化驗證」的屬性(人聲、樂器、速度、調性、曲風、情緒),像是密集複音、空間音場、長篇結構或表演細節這類更豐富的音樂現象,需要新的可稽核證據跟更大規模的人類研究才能處理。作者也明確表示 ADD-M 與 TPA 是「訓練無關的診斷式介入,不是普遍適用的緩解演算法」——效果會不會轉移,要看部署的實際生成格式而定。

The authors themselves stress that MuseDiag targets "auditable claim-level diagnosis," not catching every musical error. The contradiction-based rule makes hallucination rate a conservative lower bound, since underspecified claims are always routed to undetermined -- so the rate must be read alongside coverage, not on its own. Current objective-evidence gates only cover short clips and attributes with scalable verification (vocals, instruments, tempo, key, genre, mood); richer phenomena such as dense polyphony, spatial sound fields, long-form structure, or expressive performance nuance would need new auditable evidence and larger human studies. The authors also explicitly call ADD-M and TPA "training-free diagnostic interventions, not universal mitigation algorithms" -- whether gains transfer depends on the actual deployment generation format.

討論Discussion

三個研究問題的結果收斂成三個重點。第一,人聲誤判在九個模型上全部都有——自由形式生成下的人聲幻覺率介於 45.9% 到 61.3% 之間,但同一批模型在結構化問答裡的人聲準確率卻有 75.7% 到 89.5%,顯示模型「被直接問」答得出來,但「自由發揮」時反而更容易講錯,問題出在生成格式而非感知能力本身;控制實驗把提示詞裡的屬性提示拿掉後,人聲幻覺率只從 50.2% 微幅變成 52.3%,說明這不是提示詞誘導出來的。

第二,調性感知是模型間差異最大的一層:結構化問答的調性準確率,MiMo-V2-Omni 是 0.0%(334 題全部答不出來)、SALMONN 是 5.1%、GPT-4o 是 8.1%,但 Qwen2.5-Omni 有 68.6%、Audio-Flamingo-3 有 65.3%,顯示這是架構本身的能力邊界,不是單純的肯定偏誤——GPT-4o 甚至呈現另一種矛盾:調性是非題的肯定偏誤極低(1.9%)、機率準確率接近隨機(48.9%),但自由生成時調性幻覺率卻高達 87.6%。

第三,三種評測範式之間的模型排名並不一致。整體而言 Audio-Flamingo-3 在三種範式的總分上都排第一,但其餘模型的排名會隨評測方式大幅重排:SALMONN 在樂器是非題準確率高達 95.3%,但肯定偏誤同樣高達 98.5%(幾乎有問必答「有」);GPT-4o 在自由描述裡幾乎不主動列樂器(精確率只有 8.4%),但自由生成調性幻覺率卻是九個模型裡最高的 87.6%。緩解實驗顯示 ADD-M v1 讓四個開源模型的探測準確率都提升(+1.0 到 +4.7 個百分點),對肯定偏誤最嚴重的 SALMONN 降幅最大(-16.4 個百分點);但換到自由形式生成,ADD-M 反而讓所有四個模型的幻覺率都上升,TPA 只在 Audio-Flamingo-3 一個模型上讓自由生成幻覺率下降——這證明探測式評測測出來的進步,不能直接當成「幻覺被解決了」的證據。

The three research questions converge on three points. First, vocal misperception appears in all nine models -- free-form vocal hallucination rates run 45.9% to 61.3%, yet the same models score 75.7% to 89.5% accuracy on structured vocal questions, showing that models answer correctly when asked directly but err more under open-ended generation; a controlled ablation removing attribute hints from the prompt moved the vocal hallucination rate only from 50.2% to 52.3%, showing this isn't a prompt-induced artifact. Second, tonal perception shows the largest cross-model spread: structured key accuracy is 0.0% for MiMo-V2-Omni (all 334 queries unanswered), 5.1% for SALMONN, and 8.1% for GPT-4o, versus 68.6% for Qwen2.5-Omni and 65.3% for Audio-Flamingo-3 -- an architectural capability boundary, not simple affirmative bias. GPT-4o shows a distinct failure mode: near-chance probing accuracy (48.9%) with an extremely low yes-bias (1.9%), yet an 87.6% free-form key hallucination rate.

Third, model rankings are not consistent across the three evaluation paradigms. Audio-Flamingo-3 ranks first on all three aggregate scores, but the rest reorder substantially: SALMONN reaches 95.3% instrument-probing accuracy yet has a 98.5% yes-bias (answering "yes" almost regardless of ground truth); GPT-4o rarely lists instruments in free-form output (8.4% precision) yet has the highest free-form key hallucination rate of all nine models, at 87.6%. In the mitigation experiments, ADD-M v1 raises probing accuracy for all four open-source models (+1.0 to +4.7 points), cutting yes-bias most for the highest-bias model, SALMONN (-16.4 points) -- but under free-form generation, ADD-M instead raises the hallucination rate for all four models, and TPA lowers it for only one model, Audio-Flamingo-3. This is direct evidence that a probing-measured gain cannot be taken as proof that hallucination itself has been fixed.

Timbre Analysis of the Hulusi, a Southwestern Chinese Free-Reed Instrument, using Machine Learning

葫蘆絲(中國西南自由簧樂器)的音色分析:機器學習取徑

Yang Xia, Rolf Bader
arXiv:2609.17612 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-15
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：用 COMSAR 框架抽取九個心理聲學特徵,對九把不同調性(C、B♭、A、G、F)的葫蘆絲共 81 個平均音高做自組織映射(SOM)分群,發現只有頻譜重心、銳度與碎形關聯維度三個特徵能形成有效的音高分群,其中碎形關聯維度(量測起音瞬態的混沌程度)效果最好——只有高音區的 fa、sol、la 三個音清楚成群,頻譜擴散、粗糙度等其餘特徵幾乎看不出分群效果。Why this one: Using the COMSAR framework's nine psychoacoustic features on 81 averaged pitches from nine hulusi instruments in five keys (C, B-flat, A, G, F), Kohonen self-organizing maps find that only spectral centroid, sharpness, and fractal correlation dimension form usable pitch clusters, with the fractal correlation dimension (a measure of initial-transient chaoticity) working best -- only the three highest notes (fa, sol, la) cluster cleanly, while spectral spread, roughness, and most other features show no discernible clustering.

動機Motivation

葫蘆絲是雲南的自由簧吹奏樂器,由吹嘴、葫蘆與三根裝有銅簧片的竹管組成;跟西方手風琴不同,它的音高是由管長決定,而不是簧片本身的振動頻率決定。這個樂器在中國國內外都愈來愈受歡迎,雲南省小學音樂課已經把它列為必修,教學也擴散到北京等其他城市,但作者指出「葫蘆絲的發聲機制有很多面向至今仍不清楚」。

先前研究多半集中在跟它類似的樂器上,比如巴烏、笙、寮國/泰國的 khaen,葫蘆絲本身的相關研究相對稀少。簧片與管身之間的非線性耦合讓分析變得複雜,標準的音樂資訊檢索(MIR)特徵不一定夠用,這篇論文要問的是:能不能用心理聲學特徵搭配機器學習,把不同音高、不同樂器的葫蘆絲音色區分開來、聚類出有意義的結構?

The hulusi is a free-reed wind instrument from Yunnan, made of a mouthpiece, a gourd, and three bamboo pipes each fitted with a copper reed; unlike a Western accordion, its pitch is set by pipe length rather than the reed's own vibration frequency. The instrument has grown popular both in China and abroad -- it is now mandatory in Yunnan primary-school music education and has spread to cities like Beijing -- yet the authors note that "many aspects of its sound production remain unknown."

Prior work has mostly studied similar instruments -- the bawu, the sheng, and the Lao/Thai khaen -- leaving comparatively little research on the hulusi itself. The nonlinear coupling between reed and tube complicates analysis, and standard MIR features may not suffice, so the paper asks whether psychoacoustic features combined with machine learning can separate hulusi timbre by pitch and by instrument into meaningfully clustered structure.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

樂器音色研究的主流工具是像 librosa 這樣的標準 MIR 框架,但作者認為這類框架的特徵設計「不對應到人耳實際感知的特徵」,因此改用 COMSAR 這套心理聲學框架。分群方法上,作者刻意不用深度學習,理由是神經網路是「黑盒子」;改用 Kohonen 自組織映射(SOM),因為 SOM 的分群結果可以直接畫成二維地圖,「分群成功的原因看得到」。

跟同類自由簧樂器研究相比,巴烏研究處理的是有指孔的類似樂器,但作者的葫蘆絲有七個指孔、管與管之間的耦合更複雜;笙跟葫蘆笙(hulusheng)這類樂器則幾乎沒有指孔或指孔控制很少,跟葫蘆絲七孔的設計方式不一樣。跟早期用多維度量表(MDS)做音色區辨的研究相比,那些研究通常只用三個以內的音色特徵,這篇論文一次分析七個心理聲學參數,並且把碎形關聯維度這個抓非線性簧管耦合行為的特徵加進來——這是標準 MIR 方法裡沒有的。

The dominant tool for instrument-timbre research is a standard MIR framework like librosa, but the authors argue such frameworks' features "do not correspond to perceptual features," so they use the COMSAR psychoacoustic framework instead. For clustering, they deliberately avoid deep learning, calling neural networks "black boxes," and use Kohonen self-organizing maps (SOMs) instead, since a SOM's clustering can be drawn directly as a 2D map where "reasons for successful clustering are displayed."

Compared with bawu research on a similar reed instrument with tone holes, the hulusi studied here has seven finger holes and more complex tube-to-tube coupling; compared with the sheng and hulusheng, which have minimal or no finger-hole control, the hulusi's seven-hole design differs structurally. Compared with earlier multidimensional-scaling (MDS) studies of listener timbre discrimination, which rarely used more than three timbre features, this paper analyzes seven psychoacoustic parameters at once and adds the fractal correlation dimension -- a feature capturing nonlinear reed-tube coupling that is absent from standard MIR approaches.

方法Method

資料蒐集用三位雲南製琴師提供的九把葫蘆絲,調性分別是 F、G、A、B♭、C;每把琴的九個音高各吹十次、每次錄 3 秒、取樣率 44.1kHz,在無響室裡錄製,總共 810 段錄音,平均成 81 個「平均音高」樣本;每個平均音高再切成 50 個重疊的時間窗、每個 2^13 個樣本點,做傅立葉分析看頻譜隨時間的變化。

七個心理聲學特徵分別是:頻譜重心(振幅加權平均頻率,代表「亮度」)、頻譜擴散(頻率分布偏離重心的程度)、頻譜通量(相鄰頻譜幀之間的變化率)、粗糙度(用 Helmholtz-Bader 演算法,兩個頻譜峰相差約 33Hz 時粗糙度最大)、銳度(用高頻加權積分,單位是 acum)、聲壓級(標準聲學強度量測)、以及碎形關聯維度(量測起音瞬態的非線性混沌程度,能抓到簧片與管身耦合的非線性行為,並統計非諧波泛音結構)。

分群模型用 18×18 神經元的 Kohonen 自組織映射:先隨機初始化,對 81 筆訓練向量逐一找出相關係數最高的最佳匹配神經元,用墨西哥帽函數更新該神經元與鄰近神經元,跑 500 次疊代收斂,輸出「統一距離矩陣」(U-matrix)——顏色深代表鄰近神經元之間歐氏距離小、聚類一致性高,顏色淺代表分群邊界。另外定義一個分群品質指標 C = S/O,其中 S 是同一音高內部的平均距離、O 是該音高跟其他音高之間的平均距離;C 小於 1 代表分群成功,C 越小分群品質越好。

Data collection used nine hulusi instruments in keys F, G, A, B-flat, and C, provided by three Yunnan instrument makers; each instrument's nine pitches were played ten times, each recorded for 3 seconds at 44.1kHz in an anechoic chamber, for 810 recordings total, averaged into 81 "mean pitch" samples. Each averaged note was then split into 50 overlapping time windows of 2^13 samples each and Fourier-analyzed to track spectral change over time.

The seven psychoacoustic features are: spectral centroid (amplitude-weighted average frequency, i.e. brightness), spectral spread (deviation of the frequency distribution from the centroid), spectral flux (rate of change between adjacent spectral frames), roughness (via the Helmholtz-Bader algorithm, maximal at roughly a 33Hz gap between spectral peaks), sharpness (a high-frequency-weighted integral in acum units), sound pressure level (standard acoustic intensity), and the fractal correlation dimension (measuring nonlinear chaoticity in the initial transient, capturing reed-tube coupling nonlinearity and counting non-harmonic overtone structure).

Clustering used an 18x18-neuron Kohonen self-organizing map: random initialization, then for each of the 81 training vectors, the best-matching neuron (highest correlation) and its neighbors are updated via a Mexican-hat function, over 500 iterations to convergence, producing a U-matrix where dark regions mean small Euclidean distance between neighboring neurons (i.e. consistent clustering) and light regions mark cluster boundaries. A cluster-quality index C = S/O is also defined, where S is the mean distance within the same pitch and O is the mean distance to other pitches; C below 1 indicates successful clustering, and smaller is better.

限制Limitations

作者自己列出幾點限制。粗糙度演算法「對非常低頻的聲音可能太簡化,但對葫蘆絲的頻率範圍應該還算適用」——換句話說這個假設沒有另外驗證過。為什麼高音 fa、sol、la 分群特別清楚、為什麼碎形維度是最佳特徵,作者直接說「這超出本論文的範圍」,也沒有做簧片振動或吹奏氣壓的量測去解釋背後的物理機制,留給後續論文處理。低音音高在所有特徵組合下都持續分不出群、始終「分散在各處」。訓練 SOM 用的錄音資料並未公開,只能向作者本人索取。

The authors list several explicit limits themselves. The roughness algorithm "might be too simple for very low frequencies, but for the frequency range of the hulusi, it is assumed to be appropriate" -- an assumption that is not independently validated. Why fa, sol, and la cluster especially cleanly, and why the fractal dimension is the best-fit feature, is stated as "beyond the scope of the paper," with no reed-vibration or blowing-pressure measurements taken to explain the underlying physical mechanism, left for a future paper. Bass pitches fail to cluster under every feature combination tested, remaining scattered throughout. The recordings used to train the SOMs are not publicly available and can only be requested from the author directly.

討論Discussion

單一特徵的 SOM 結果顯示:銳度這個特徵能把高音區與中音區聚出相對清楚的群,但中音區的銳度反而比高音區高(泛音較多所致),這跟直覺相反;頻譜重心也出現類似反轉——中音區比高音區更「亮」,因為最高音的泛音反而較少。頻譜擴散、聲壓級、粗糙度三個特徵則完全看不出跟音高相關的分群。多特徵組合(頻譜重心+銳度+碎形維度)比單一特徵稍有改善,但真正決定性的結果來自分群品質指標:九個音高裡,只有 fa、sol、la 三個高音的 C 值遠小於 1(幾乎完美分群),usi、do、re 三個音的 C 值偏大(等於沒有分群),其餘音落在中間。

作者提出的物理解釋是:fa、sol、la 這三個音需要按住最上面那個指孔,吹奏時需要的氣壓比其他音低,而氣壓降低正好對應到起音瞬態的混沌程度降低(碎形維度變小)——換句話說,分群效果最好的不是「音色最特別」的音,而是「吹奏方式改變(氣壓降低)最明確」的音,這把一個機器學習分群結果跟一個具體的演奏動作連結起來,而不是停在「碎形維度是最佳特徵」這個描述性結論上。

The single-feature SOM results show sharpness clustering treble and mid-range fairly cleanly, but counterintuitively, mid-range sharpness is higher than treble (due to more overtones); spectral centroid shows a similar reversal -- mid-range sounds "brighter" than treble, since the highest notes actually carry fewer overtones. Spectral spread, SPL, and roughness show no pitch-related clustering at all. Multi-feature combinations (centroid + sharpness + fractal dimension) improve slightly over single features, but the decisive result comes from the cluster-quality index: of the nine pitches, only the three highest -- fa, sol, and la -- have C values far below 1 (near-perfect clustering); usi, do, and re have large C values (no clustering); the rest fall in between.

The physical explanation the authors offer is that fa, sol, and la require the uppermost finger hole to stay open, which needs lower blowing pressure than the other pitches -- and lower pressure corresponds to lower initial-transient chaoticity, i.e. a smaller fractal dimension. In other words, the pitches that cluster best are not the ones with the most distinctive timbre, but the ones with the clearest change in playing technique (reduced pressure), tying the machine-learning clustering result to a specific performance action rather than stopping at the descriptive claim that fractal dimension is the best feature.

Sectional Structure and Emotional Dynamics in Chinese Pop Songs: An Empirical Analysis of Valence-Arousal Trajectories across 100 Songs

中文流行歌曲的段落結構與情緒動態:100 首歌的價性—喚起軌跡實證分析

Jingyi Lyu
arXiv:2609.15675 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-13
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把 100 首中文流行歌總共 1,046 個人工標註的段落(Intro、Verse、Pre-chorus、Chorus、Bridge、Interlude、Outro)對齊到用凍結教師模型逐 0.5 秒估計出的價性—喚起(Valence-Arousal)軌跡,發現段落之間的情緒差異主要反映在喚起(Arousal)而非價性(Valence)上:主歌平均喚起 −0.073,副歌 0.206、橋段 0.275;副歌相對主歌喚起平均高出 0.279(99% 的歌是正的),但價性只高出 0.119(89% 的歌是正的)。最高喚起的段落有 79 首歌落在後三分之一,但只有 31 首歌是最後一次副歌,顯示「情緒高點」跟「最後副歌」並不能畫上等號。Why this one: The paper aligns 1,046 manually annotated sections (Intro, Verse, Pre-chorus, Chorus, Bridge, Interlude, Outro) across 100 Chinese pop songs with continuous Valence-Arousal trajectories estimated every 0.5s by frozen teacher models, finding that sectional emotional differences show up mainly in Arousal rather than Valence: mean Arousal is -0.073 for Verse, 0.206 for Chorus, and 0.275 for Bridge; Chorus exceeds Verse by 0.279 in Arousal (positive in 99% of songs) but only 0.119 in Valence (positive in 89%). The highest-Arousal section falls in the final third of the song in 79 of 100 songs, but is the final Chorus specifically in only 31, showing that "emotional peak" and "final chorus" are not the same thing.

動機Motivation

動態音樂情緒辨識(MER)可以用連續的價性—喚起(VA)軌跡描述一首歌情緒何時、如何變化,但光靠時間位置沒辦法解釋這些變化在歌曲結構裡代表什麼功能意義。音樂情緒不是貼在整首歌上的單一標籤,而是隨時間發展、分歧、回返、收束的動態過程;VA 模型用價性(Valence)描述情緒正負向、用喚起(Arousal)描述激動程度,是動態 MER 常用的連續表示方式。

問題是,同樣的情緒變化如果出現在主歌、預副歌或副歌,可能對應到完全不同的形式脈絡與組織功能。流行音樂形式研究(如 Summach 2011)指出主歌通常建立敘事與相對低強度的基準,預副歌則透過織度、節奏、音域、和聲節奏的變化來累積動能、強化抵達副歌的感覺;但這條研究路線只講段落的功能,沒有用連續 VA 軌跡去量化這些功能對應到多少具體的情緒變化量。這篇論文要補的正是「動態 MER 講得出情緒何時變、卻講不出形式脈絡」跟「形式研究講得出段落功能、卻沒有量化情緒動態」中間的空隙。

Dynamic music emotion recognition (MER) can describe when and how emotion changes within a song via continuous Valence-Arousal (VA) trajectories, but temporal position alone cannot explain what those changes mean within the song's structure. Musical emotion is not a single label attached to a whole song but a dynamic process that develops, diverges, returns, and resolves over time; the VA model, using Valence for emotional polarity and Arousal for activation level, is the standard continuous representation in dynamic MER.

The problem is that the same emotional change occurring in a Verse, Pre-chorus, or Chorus can correspond to entirely different formal contexts and organizational functions. Research on pop-song form (e.g. Summach 2011) argues Verse typically establishes a low-intensity narrative baseline while Pre-chorus builds momentum via changes in texture, rhythm, register, and harmonic rhythm to strengthen the arrival at Chorus -- but that line of work describes sectional function without quantifying how much emotional change corresponds to it via continuous VA trajectories. This paper targets exactly that gap between dynamic MER (which tracks when emotion changes but not its formal context) and form research (which explains sectional function but not quantified emotional dynamics).

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

連續 VA 建模這條線上,Russell(1980)的環狀情緒模型把情緒放進價性與喚起兩維空間;Schubert(1999, 2004)把這套二維情緒空間用在連續量測音樂反應上;1000 Songs 與 DEAM 這類資料集提供逐時間點的價性喚起標註。表徵學習方面,MERT(Li 等人,2024)透過大規模自監督預訓練學音高、音色與更高階的音樂語意,可以遷移到很多音樂理解任務;PMEmo(Zhang 等人,2018)則提供流行歌與副歌片段的動態情緒標註。這篇論文用的凍結教師模型就是奠基在 MERT 表徵與動態 OpenSMILE 特徵上,在 PMEmo 上訓練跟挑選,價性的片段層級 PCC/CCC 是 0.8029/0.8002、喚起是 0.8574/0.8548,本論文分析的 100 首歌都不在教師模型的訓練、驗證或測試集裡。

段落功能研究這條線上,Summach(2011)主張預副歌的功能是為副歌的抵達鋪墊動能;Nobile(2022)進一步把主歌—預副歌—副歌這條序列講成一個有方向性的形式過程,預副歌推動結構運動、副歌是抵達點;de Clercq(2017)則強調形式標籤本身是知覺性的、天生模糊,不該脫離具體音樂脈絡機械式解讀。這篇論文跟這三者的差別在於:它們談的是形式功能本身,沒有用連續 VA 軌跡去量化這些功能對應多少實際情緒變化量。

最直接的先行研究是 Raboy 與 Taparugssanagorn(2024),他們從主歌與副歌段落抽取音訊與歌詞特徵,結合不同段落的資訊做「歌曲整體」情緒分類。這篇論文跟它的差別很具體:對方鎖定的是預先定義好的「主歌—副歌—主歌」這一種結構,輸出是整首歌一個情緒類別;這篇論文則涵蓋七種功能段落(含間奏、橋段、尾奏),分析的是段落層級連續的 VA 變化,而且進一步檢視同一種段落類型「重複出現時」的情緒狀態會不會改變——這是先前研究完全沒碰過的問題。

On continuous VA modeling, Russell's (1980) circumplex model places emotion in a two-dimensional Valence-Arousal space; Schubert (1999, 2004) applied this space to continuous measurement of musical responses; datasets like 1000 Songs and DEAM provide time-continuous VA annotations. On representation learning, MERT (Li et al., 2024) learns pitch, timbre, and higher-level musical semantics via large-scale self-supervised pretraining, transferring across many music-understanding tasks; PMEmo (Zhang et al., 2018) provides dynamic emotion annotations for pop songs and chorus excerpts. This paper's frozen teacher models build on MERT representations plus dynamic OpenSMILE features, trained and selected on PMEmo, reaching segment-level PCC/CCC of 0.8029/0.8002 for Valence and 0.8574/0.8548 for Arousal; none of the 100 songs analyzed here were in the teacher models' train/val/test split.

On sectional-function research, Summach (2011) argues Pre-chorus builds momentum toward the Chorus's arrival; Nobile (2022) frames Verse-Pre-chorus-Chorus as a directional formal process where Pre-chorus propels motion and Chorus is the arrival point; de Clercq (2017) stresses that formal labels are themselves perceptual and inherently ambiguous, not to be read mechanically outside musical context. This paper's difference from all three is that they discuss formal function itself without using continuous VA trajectories to quantify how much actual emotional change corresponds to each function.

The closest direct predecessor is Raboy and Taparugssanagorn (2024), who extract audio and lyric features from Verse and Chorus sections and combine them for whole-song emotion classification. The concrete difference: their work targets one predefined Verse-Chorus-Verse structure and outputs a single song-level emotion category, while this paper covers seven functional section types (including Interlude, Bridge, and Outro), analyzes continuous section-level VA change, and additionally examines whether the same section type's emotional state shifts across its repeated occurrences within a song -- a question prior work never addressed.

方法Method

樣本從 QQ 音樂歌手榜前 60 名歌手裡用配額抽樣選出:排名 1–10 的歌手各選 3 首、11–30 各選 2 首、31–60 各選 1 首,總共湊出 100 首歌(60 位配額歌手、含合作曲共 68 個演出者掛名),平均長度 257.48 秒、平均 10.46 個段落。所有 100 首歌都由研究者本人親自聽譜標註成七類段落(Intro、Verse、Pre-chorus、Chorus、Bridge、Interlude、Outro),總共標出 1,046 個段落;其中 10 首歌另外請一位受過正式音樂訓練的人覆核,覆核結果沒有更動任何段落類型或邊界。

價性與喚起用兩個架構相同但各自獨立訓練、凍結參數的音訊教師模型估計:每個時間點把 MERT-v1-95M 第六層隱藏表徵(768 維)跟動態 OpenSMILE ComParE_2016 特徵(260 維)串接,投影到 112 維後餵進一個全域—局部 BiGRU,輸出範圍 −1 到 1 的價性與喚起值。全曲推論用 30 秒滑動視窗、15 秒跳躍步長,重疊視窗用 Hann 加權平均融合,產生 0.5 秒解析度的連續 VA 軌跡,100 首歌總共產生 51,448 個預測影格,其中 51,444 個成功對齊回 1,046 個標註段落(4 個超出邊界的尾端影格被捨棄)。每個段落的 VA 值定義成該段落所有影格預測值的算術平均。

統計上以「歌曲」而不是「段落」當推論單位:對同一首歌裡都出現的兩種段落類型做配對比較,用雙尾 Wilcoxon 符號等級檢定,搭配 5,000 次無母數自助法重抽估 95% 信賴區間,並在同一組比較家族裡做 Holm 校正;同一首歌裡如果同種轉換出現多次,會先在歌曲內部取平均再進入跨歌分析,避免重複轉換次數不均造成的權重偏差。因為部分歌手在樣本裡貢獻不只一首歌,另外加做以歌手為聚類單位的自助法重抽,檢查主要結論會不會被少數高產歌手拉著走。全曲情緒路徑則另外定義路徑長度、首末段落位移量、以及兩者的比值(路徑直線程度)三個指標。

The sample was drawn via quota sampling from the top 60 artists on the QQ Music Singer Chart: 3 songs per artist ranked 1-10, 2 per artist ranked 11-30, 1 per artist ranked 31-60, yielding 100 songs (60 quota artists, 68 distinct performer credits including collaborations), with mean duration 257.48s and 10.46 sections per song on average. All 100 songs were manually annotated by the researcher into seven section categories (Intro, Verse, Pre-chorus, Chorus, Bridge, Interlude, Outro), identifying 1,046 sections total; 10 songs were additionally reviewed by a musically trained reviewer, whose review produced no changes to any section type or boundary.

Valence and Arousal were estimated with two identically-architected but independently trained, frozen audio teacher models: at each time step, a 768-dim representation from MERT-v1-95M's sixth hidden layer is concatenated with 260-dim dynamic OpenSMILE ComParE_2016 features, projected to 112 dimensions, and fed to a global-local BiGRU outputting Valence and Arousal in [-1, 1]. Full-song inference uses a 30-second sliding window with a 15-second hop, fused across overlapping windows via Hann-weighted averaging into a continuous VA trajectory at 0.5-second resolution -- yielding 51,448 prediction frames across the 100 songs, of which 51,444 were successfully mapped onto the 1,046 annotated sections. Each section's VA value is the arithmetic mean of its constituent frame-level predictions.

Statistically, songs (not sections) are the unit of inference: paired comparisons between two section types occurring in the same song use two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with 5,000-resample nonparametric bootstrap 95% confidence intervals and Holm correction within each comparison family; when the same transition recurs multiple times in one song, values are first averaged within that song before cross-song analysis, to avoid weighting bias from unequal repeat counts. Since some singers contribute more than one song, a singer-clustered bootstrap resampling is run as a sensitivity check, to see whether the main findings are driven by a few prolific artists. Whole-song emotional paths are additionally summarized by three measures: path length, first-to-last-section displacement, and their ratio (path directness).

限制Limitations

作者自陳的限制包括:段落標註主要由研究者一人完成,只有 10 首歌另外找人覆核,並沒有針對全部語料做系統性的標註者間一致性檢驗,橋段、間奏跟區分度不高的主歌/副歌邊界仍可能保留一定的詮釋彈性。VA 數值是凍結教師模型的估計值,不是人工標註的真實情緒,結論只能當作這批樣本裡的相對趨勢,不能直接當成聽眾的真實感受。首末段落之間的差異只描述「歌曲較早位置」跟「較晚位置」的差別,不能拿來當作「重複本身會讓情緒增強」的證據。樣本限定在 QQ 音樂榜前 60 名歌手的 100 首中文流行歌,涵蓋的年代、風格、平台與歌手範圍有限,結論能否推廣到其他語種或曲風的流行歌,論文並未驗證。

The authors' stated limits include: section annotation was done primarily by the researcher alone, with only 10 songs additionally reviewed, and no systematic inter-rater agreement check across the full corpus, so Bridge, Interlude, and weakly differentiated Verse/Chorus boundaries may retain some interpretive flexibility. The VA values are frozen-teacher-model estimates, not human-annotated ground-truth emotion, so conclusions should be read as relative trends within this sample rather than direct claims about listener experience. Differences between first and last occurrences of a section only describe earlier-vs-later position in the song and are not evidence that repetition itself intensifies emotion. The sample is limited to 100 Chinese pop songs from the top 60 artists on one chart, covering a limited range of eras, styles, platforms, and artists, and the paper does not test whether its conclusions generalize to other languages or genres.

討論Discussion

主要發現的統計證據相當扎實。以歌曲加權的段落平均喚起看,主歌 −0.073、副歌 0.206、橋段 0.275;同曲配對比較進一步顯示,副歌相對主歌的價性平均高 0.119(89% 的歌是正的、Holm 校正後 p<.001),喚起平均高 0.279(99% 的歌是正的、p<.001)——喚起的效果量跟一致性都明顯大於價性,這正是論文主張「段落差異主要反映在喚起上」的數字依據。預副歌雖然只出現在 38 首歌裡,但相對主歌喚起高 0.135(84.2% 為正)、副歌又比預副歌高 0.200(100% 為正,全部 38 首都成立),呈現相當一致的「逐步累積」型態。

局部轉換分析顯示,946 次相鄰段落轉換裡,出現頻率最高的八種轉換(如主歌→副歌 116 次、副歌→間奏 111 次、副歌→尾奏 91 次)加起來就佔了 76.5%,但把連續重複的同類段落收斂之後,仍有 49 種不同的全曲段落序列、其中 36 種只出現一次——換句話說,全曲的段落編排非常多樣,但「局部怎麼轉換」相對固定。轉進副歌的路徑也不是單一模式:362 次進入副歌的轉換裡,價性喚起同時明顯上升的佔 38.1%(138 次)、只有喚起明顯上升的佔 19.6%(71 次)、兩者大致持平的佔 17.7%(64 次)。副歌→尾奏這個轉換則相對一致地同時下降(價性 −0.132、喚起 −0.188),對應到論文講的「收束」功能。

全曲軌跡層級,喚起最高的段落有 79 首歌落在後三分之一,平均與中位數的正規化位置分別是 0.733 與 0.755,支持「情緒高點偏向後段」;但把「哪一種段落」最喚起攤開來看,53 首是副歌、19 首是間奏、13 首是橋段、10 首是尾奏——最後一次副歌恰好是全曲最高喚起段落的只有 31 首,不到三分之一。同一段落類型重複出現時,末次也普遍比初次的 VA 更高(副歌喚起平均漲 0.225、90% 的歌是正的;間奏漲 0.260、93.9% 為正),但把副歌的漲幅拿去跟主歌、預副歌、間奏的漲幅比較,三個 Holm 校正後的 p 值分別是 0.142、0.214、0.751,都沒有顯著差異——也就是說,「後段情緒比較高」是整首歌的共同趨勢,不是副歌獨有的現象,論文因此把這個現象命名為「局部循環—全域累積」,而不是簡單的單調上升。

The evidence behind the main finding is solid. Song-weighted mean Arousal is -0.073 for Verse, 0.206 for Chorus, and 0.275 for Bridge; within-song paired comparisons further show Chorus exceeds Verse by 0.119 in Valence (positive in 89% of songs, Holm p<.001) and by 0.279 in Arousal (positive in 99% of songs, p<.001) -- the Arousal effect is both larger and more consistent than the Valence effect, which is exactly the numeric basis for the paper's claim that sectional differences show up mainly in Arousal. Pre-chorus, though present in only 38 songs, shows a fairly consistent buildup: 0.135 higher Arousal than Verse (positive in 84.2%), and Chorus 0.200 higher than Pre-chorus (positive in all 38 songs).

The local-transition analysis finds that among 946 adjacent-section transitions, the eight most frequent types (Verse-to-Chorus 116 times, Chorus-to-Interlude 111, Chorus-to-Outro 91, etc.) together account for 76.5%, yet after collapsing consecutive repeats of the same section, 49 distinct whole-song sequences remain, 36 occurring only once -- whole-song arrangement is highly diverse even though local transitions are relatively fixed. Entries into Chorus follow no single pattern: of 362 such transitions, both Valence and Arousal rise notably in 38.1% (138 cases), Arousal alone rises in 19.6% (71), and both stay roughly flat in 17.7% (64). Chorus-to-Outro, by contrast, shows a fairly consistent joint decrease (Valence -0.132, Arousal -0.188), matching the paper's "closure" function.

At the whole-song trajectory level, the highest-Arousal section falls in the final third in 79 of 100 songs, with mean/median normalized position 0.733/0.755, supporting a later-stage emotional peak; but broken down by section type, that peak section is Chorus in 53 songs, Interlude in 19, Bridge in 13, and Outro in 10 -- the final Chorus specifically is the peak in only 31 songs, under a third. Repeated section types also tend to reach higher VA at their last occurrence than their first (Chorus Arousal up 0.225 on average, positive in 90% of songs; Interlude up 0.260, positive in 93.9%), but comparing Chorus's gain against Verse's, Pre-chorus's, and Interlude's gives Holm-adjusted p-values of 0.142, 0.214, and 0.751 -- none significant. So the later-is-higher pattern is a whole-song tendency, not something unique to Chorus, which is why the paper names it "local cycles-global accumulation" rather than simple monotonic rise.
