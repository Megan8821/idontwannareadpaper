From Classification to Recommendation: Empirical Analysis of Audio Embedding Models Application for Content-Based Music Recommendation

從分類到推薦：音訊嵌入模型應用於內容基礎音樂推薦的實證分析

Qingrui Li, Haowei Lou, Chengkai Huang, Quan Z. Sheng, Lina Yao
arXiv:2608.06928 · cs.IR · 投稿submitted 2026-08-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇不提新模型，而是把六個常見的預訓練音訊編碼器（Wav2Vec 2.0、HuBERT、Music2Vec、MERT、CLAP-General、CLAP-Music）系統性地放進三種推薦介面（KNN、SASRec、TIGER）交叉測試，直接回答一個業界常問卻少人認真量過的問題：「分類模型的嵌入拿來做推薦到底行不行、怎麼挑」。結論之一——生成式推薦系統的 Semantic-ID 加深反而經常變差——跟直覺相反，對正在導入 TIGER 類架構的團隊有立即參考價值。Why this one: Rather than proposing a new model, this paper cross-tests six common pretrained audio encoders (Wav2Vec 2.0, HuBERT, Music2Vec, MERT, CLAP-General, CLAP-Music) against three recommendation interfaces (KNN, SASRec, TIGER), answering a question practitioners ask constantly but rarely measure rigorously: whether classification-trained embeddings actually transfer to recommendation, and which one to pick. Its counter-intuitive finding — that deeper Semantic-ID quantization in generative recommenders often hurts rather than helps — has immediate relevance for teams adopting TIGER-style architectures.

動機Motivation

音樂串流平台長期面對「冷啟動」問題：一首新上架的歌沒有任何互動紀錄，純靠協同過濾完全無法推薦，這時音訊本身的內容資訊就是唯一可用的信號。近年出現大量以分類或對比學習訓練的預訓練音訊模型（語音領域的 Wav2Vec 2.0、HuBERT，音樂領域的 Music2Vec、MERT，語言-音訊對齊的 CLAP），它們在分類、音樂標記等任務上表現很好，但作者指出一個常被忽略的落差：這些模型的訓練目標（遮蔽預測、對比學習、音訊-文字對齊）本質上是為了讓「同類的聲音靠近」，而推薦系統需要的是「使用者會喜歡的東西靠近」——這是由主觀、行為驅動的偏好決定的相似性，兩者不必然一致。更進一步，近期生成式推薦系統（把嵌入量化成離散的 Semantic ID，再用自迴歸方式生成推薦序列，例如 TIGER 架構）興起後，音訊嵌入在這個新範式下到底有沒有用、該怎麼設計量化方式，幾乎完全沒有系統性研究。這篇論文就是要把這塊空白填上。

Music streaming platforms face a persistent cold-start problem: a newly uploaded track has no interaction history, so collaborative filtering has nothing to work with, and audio content becomes the only usable signal. A wave of pretrained audio models trained via classification or contrastive objectives has emerged — speech-domain Wav2Vec 2.0 and HuBERT, music-domain Music2Vec and MERT, and language-audio-aligned CLAP — all strong on classification and tagging benchmarks. The authors flag an overlooked gap: these training objectives pull acoustically similar sounds together, but recommendation needs items a given listener would enjoy pulled together — a subjective, behavior-shaped notion of similarity that doesn't automatically follow from acoustic similarity. Compounding this, the recent rise of generative recommender systems — which quantize embeddings into discrete Semantic IDs and generate recommendation sequences autoregressively, as in the TIGER architecture — raises an almost entirely unstudied question: do audio embeddings even help in this new paradigm, and how should the quantization be designed? This paper sets out to close that gap systematically.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景橫跨三條線：(1) 預訓練音訊模型——語音導向的 Wav2Vec 2.0、HuBERT 用遮蔽預測，音樂領域的 Music2Vec、MERT 針對音樂結構調整目標，CLAP 則用對比學習把音訊與文字描述對齊；(2) 音訊感知推薦系統——過去已有內容基礎與神經網路方法把音訊特徵餵進推薦模型，但多半針對單一任務手工設計編碼器，鮮少系統比較多個現成編碼器；(3) 生成式推薦——以 TIGER 為代表，用殘差量化自編碼器（RVQ）把連續嵌入轉成一串離散的 Semantic ID token，再用類似語言模型的方式自迴歸生成推薦。這篇論文的定位很清楚：不做新編碼器、不做新推薦演算法，而是把「現有的六個編碼器 × 現有的三種推薦介面」全部交叉組合起來測，並且進一步拆解 RVQ 的寬度（codebook width）、深度（quantization depth）、保留前綴長度這三個設計選項對推薦效果的影響。

The related work spans three threads: (1) pretrained audio models — speech-oriented Wav2Vec 2.0 and HuBERT trained via masked prediction, music-domain Music2Vec and MERT with objectives adapted to musical structure, and CLAP, which uses contrastive learning to align audio with text descriptions; (2) audio-aware recommendation — prior content-based and neural approaches have fed audio features into recommenders, but typically with encoders hand-designed for one task, rarely comparing several off-the-shelf encoders systematically; and (3) generative recommendation — exemplified by TIGER, which uses a residual-quantization autoencoder (RVQ) to convert continuous embeddings into a sequence of discrete Semantic ID tokens, then generates recommendations autoregressively in a language-model-like fashion. The paper's positioning is deliberate: it proposes no new encoder and no new recommendation algorithm, but instead cross-tests six existing encoders against three existing recommendation interfaces, and further dissects how RVQ's codebook width, quantization depth, and retained prefix length each shape recommendation quality.

方法Method

實驗設計成三層。第一層是「推薦介面」：KNN 直接用嵌入的餘弦相似度做檢索，等於直接檢驗原始嵌入幾何空間的品質；SASRec 是序列推薦器，用音訊向量初始化可訓練的物品嵌入，再靠使用者行為序列微調，等於檢驗「嵌入被下游任務適應後」的效果；TIGER 則是生成式推薦，先用 RVQ 把嵌入量化成離散 Semantic ID，再自迴歸生成序列，檢驗嵌入在離散化與生成式框架下還剩多少資訊。第二層是「六個編碼器」：Wav2Vec 2.0、HuBERT（語音導向）、Music2Vec、MERT（音樂領域）、CLAP-General、CLAP-Music（語言-音訊對齊，一般版與音樂特化版）。第三層是針對 TIGER 的消融：改變 RVQ 的 codebook 寬度（64 到 4096）、量化深度（1 到 12 層），以及推薦時只保留前幾層 Semantic ID 前綴的效果。資料集用 LFM1b 與 Music4All-Onion 各抽約 5000 首歌的子集，分別對應約 8000 位使用者、190 萬與 200 萬筆互動紀錄，評估指標是 Recall@50、NDCG@50、MRR@50，總共形成六個問題（RQ1 到 RQ6）逐一回答編碼器選擇、介面交互、預訓練目標、預訓練資料領域、量化寬度/深度、RVQ 各層貢獻度的影響。

The experiment is structured in three layers. The first is the recommendation interface: KNN retrieves directly via cosine similarity on raw embeddings, testing the quality of the pretrained geometry itself; SASRec is a sequential recommender that initializes trainable item embeddings from audio vectors and fine-tunes them against user behavior sequences, testing how much adaptation can compensate for the starting embedding; TIGER is the generative recommender, quantizing embeddings into discrete Semantic IDs via RVQ before autoregressive generation, testing how much information survives discretization and the generative framing. The second layer is the six encoders: Wav2Vec 2.0 and HuBERT (speech-oriented), Music2Vec and MERT (music-domain), and CLAP-General and CLAP-Music (audio-text alignment, general and music-specialized). The third layer is a TIGER-specific ablation varying RVQ codebook width (64 to 4096), quantization depth (1 to 12 levels), and how many leading Semantic-ID prefix levels are retained at recommendation time. Two datasets — roughly 5,000-track subsets of LFM1b and Music4All-Onion, with about 8,000 users and 1.9M/2.0M interactions respectively — are evaluated with Recall@50, NDCG@50, and MRR@50, organized around six research questions (RQ1–RQ6) covering encoder choice, interface interaction, pretraining objective, pretraining domain, quantization width/depth, and per-RVQ-layer contribution.

限制Limitations

作者自己點出的限制很直接：兩個資料集都只抽了約五千首歌的子集，且評估只用離線指標（Recall、NDCG、MRR），沒有真人聽感或滿意度資料，離線指標高不代表使用者真的會喜歡；音訊只是偏好訊號的一種，藝人、曲風標籤、流行度等其他常見推薦特徵完全排除在分析之外，所以「音訊嵌入的貢獻」實際上是在一個被簡化的情境裡測出來的，換到含有更多特徵的正式系統，結論未必等比例成立。另外，六個編碼器都是直接拿現成的、未針對推薦任務微調的版本，等於只回答了「哪個現成模型的嵌入幾何比較適合推薦」，而不是「音訊模型的推薦上限在哪」；如果之後有人針對推薦目標微調這些編碼器，排名很可能改變。

The authors' own limitations are stated plainly: both datasets are ~5,000-track subsets evaluated purely offline (Recall, NDCG, MRR), with no listener-satisfaction data, so strong offline numbers don't guarantee real listener preference. Audio is treated as the only signal, excluding artist identity, genre tags, and popularity — features that typically matter in production recommenders — so the measured "contribution of audio embeddings" is bounded by this simplified setting and may not scale proportionally to a full-featured system. All six encoders are used off-the-shelf, without any fine-tuning toward the recommendation objective, meaning the study answers "whose pretrained geometry happens to suit recommendation better" rather than "what is the ceiling for audio-based recommendation" — fine-tuning any of these encoders toward the task could plausibly reorder the rankings.

討論Discussion

整體結論可以濃縮成三句可操作的建議。第一，如果要直接用嵌入做檢索（KNN），選音樂領域、語言對齊的 CLAP-Music 通常最好；但如果推薦器本身有訓練（SASRec），下游監督訊號可以大幅拉平不同編碼器的差距，代表「起點嵌入的品質」在有充分行為資料微調的情境下沒那麼關鍵。第二，音樂領域預訓練（Music2Vec、MERT、CLAP-Music）系統性優於語音或通用音訊預訓練，這點在 KNN 與 TIGER 下特別明顯，替「該不該花資源訓練音樂專用編碼器」這個常見工程決策提供了實證支持。第三，也是最違反直覺的一點：在 TIGER 這類生成式推薦裡，把 RVQ 加深、Semantic ID 容量拉大，並不會穩定提升效果，反而經常在深度超過三層之後開始不穩定或退化，第三層殘差幾乎沒有額外的推薦相關資訊。這對正在設計 Semantic-ID 系統的工程團隊是直接可用的指引：資源該分配到 codebook 寬度而不是深度。整篇論文的價值不在提出新方法，而在把業界普遍靠直覺做的選型決策，換成了有對照組的系統性證據。

The findings distill into three actionable takeaways. First, for direct retrieval on raw embeddings (KNN), the music-domain, text-aligned CLAP-Music generally wins; but once the recommender itself is trained (SASRec), downstream supervision closes much of the gap between encoders, meaning the quality of the starting embedding matters less once there's enough behavioral data to adapt on. Second, music-domain pretraining (Music2Vec, MERT, CLAP-Music) systematically beats speech or general-audio pretraining, especially under KNN and TIGER — empirical support for a common but often intuition-driven engineering decision about whether to invest in music-specific encoders. Third, and most counter-intuitively, deepening RVQ quantization and expanding Semantic-ID capacity in TIGER-style generative recommenders does not reliably help, and often becomes unstable or degrades performance beyond three levels, with the third residual layer carrying almost no additional recommendation-relevant information. That's directly actionable guidance for teams designing Semantic-ID systems: spend capacity on codebook width, not depth. The paper's value isn't a new method — it's replacing intuition-driven encoder and quantization choices with controlled, systematic evidence.

Audio-to-Score Transcription with the SheetSage-A2S Dataset

以 SheetSage-A2S 資料集進行音訊轉樂譜轉錄

Eoin Cummins, Zhongyi Huang, Alexandre D'Hooge, Zhuoru Mo, Yaolong Ju
arXiv:2608.06165 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：自動音樂轉錄（AMT）研究長期集中在把音訊變成 MIDI 或 piano-roll，這篇論文把目標拉高一層，直接轉成人類可讀的樂譜（**kern 格式的主旋律加和弦），而且是流行音樂而不是古典音樂——這正是過去資料稀缺、少有人碰的方向。作者建了第一個針對流行音樂的音訊-樂譜配對資料集（61 小時、9468 個片段），並用一套很扎實的工程手段（八度推斷、預訓練特徵、資料增強）把符號錯誤率從 66.85% 壓到 20.92%，同時在古典音樂基準上把先前最佳的 15.3% SER 打到 4.98%，兩邊都刷新了紀錄。Why this one: Automatic music transcription research has long focused on converting audio to MIDI or piano-roll; this paper raises the target a level higher, transcribing directly to human-readable score notation (**kern-format melody plus chords) — and for popular music rather than classical, a direction long starved of data. The authors built the first audio-score paired dataset for popular music (61 hours, 9,468 clips) and, through a solid engineering pipeline (octave inference, pretrained features, data augmentation), drove the symbol error rate from 66.85% down to 20.92%, while also pushing the classical-music benchmark's prior best of 15.3% SER down to 4.98% — a new state of the art on both fronts.

動機Motivation

把一段錄音變成音樂家能直接看懂、演奏的樂譜（Audio-to-Score, A2S），跟一般說的自動音樂轉錄（AMT）不完全一樣：AMT 通常只求輸出 MIDI 或 piano-roll 這種中介表示，A2S 則要求輸出符合記譜規範的樂譜，需要處理調號、和弦標記、節奏量化等更貼近人類閱讀習慣的資訊。作者指出這個領域被兩個問題卡住：一是配對的音訊-樂譜資料稀缺，過去幾乎所有 A2S 資料集都侷限在古典音樂，而且音訊多半是合成音（MIDI 渲染出來的），不是真實錄音；二是儘管預訓練模型與資料增強已經在 AMT 領域證明有效，A2S 研究卻很少系統性採用這些技巧。流行音樂本身結構更多樣（主旋律加和弦的 lead sheet 形式很常見，來源多是社群手抄的樂譜網站），如果能把 A2S 擴展到流行音樂並用上真實錄音，會是這個領域第一次真正貼近實際使用場景（例如自動生成一首歌的主旋律和弦譜）。

Converting a recording into a score a musician can read and play (Audio-to-Score, A2S) is not quite the same task as standard Automatic Music Transcription (AMT): AMT typically targets an intermediate representation like MIDI or piano-roll, while A2S must produce notation-compliant output — key signatures, chord labels, rhythmic quantization — matching how humans actually read scores. The authors identify two problems holding the field back. First, paired audio-score data is scarce, and nearly all prior A2S datasets are confined to classical music with synthetic (MIDI-rendered) audio rather than real recordings. Second, even though pretrained models and data augmentation have proven effective in AMT, A2S research has rarely adopted them systematically. Popular music has more structural variety — lead-sheet-style melody-plus-chords notation is common, often sourced from community-transcribed sheet-music sites — so extending A2S to popular music with real audio would bring the field, for the first time, close to an actual use case: automatically generating a lead sheet for a song.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

過去的 A2S 系統分兩派：模組化方法先做 AMT 得到 MIDI，再用規則或另一個模型把 MIDI 轉成樂譜；端到端方法直接用 CNN 加 RNN 把音訊映射到符號序列。但現有資料集幾乎全部侷限古典音樂並使用合成音訊，作者認為這既限制了任務難度的代表性（合成音訊沒有真實錄音的噪音、混音、人聲等複雜度），也限制了模型的泛化能力。同時，AMT 領域近年已經證明「用預訓練音訊特徵取代從頭訓練的 CNN 編碼器」與「資料增強（變調、變速）」都能明顯提升效果，但 A2S 的既有工作大多還停留在從零訓練的小模型。這篇論文就是把這兩條線接起來：一方面提供真實錄音、跨曲風的新資料集，另一方面把 AMT 領域已驗證有效的技巧系統性搬進 A2S。

Prior A2S systems split into two camps: modular pipelines that first run AMT to obtain MIDI, then convert MIDI to score via rules or a second model; and end-to-end approaches that map audio directly to symbol sequences via CNN-RNN architectures. Existing datasets, however, are almost entirely confined to classical music with synthetic audio, which the authors argue both understates task difficulty (synthetic audio lacks the noise, mixing, and vocal complexity of real recordings) and limits model generalization. Meanwhile, AMT research has already shown that replacing from-scratch CNN encoders with pretrained audio features, and adding data augmentation (pitch/tempo shifting), both meaningfully improve results — yet prior A2S work has largely stuck with small, from-scratch-trained models. This paper connects the two threads: providing a new dataset of real, cross-genre recordings, and systematically porting AMT's proven techniques into A2S.

方法Method

資料集建構分三階段。第一階段抓取：從 26175 次嘗試中成功下載 20072 段 YouTube 音訊，配上原始 SheetSage 標註提供的節拍-時間對齊資訊。第二階段是最棘手的「八度推斷」：原始標註只有相對音高、沒有絕對八度，作者用 Mel-band Roformer 抽出人聲幹軌，再用 RMVPE 做音高估計得到 72 個半音間距的機率箱，接著窮舉 12 種假設（6 種八度位移 × 2 種起始時間偏移），挑信心最高且超過 0.2 門檻的組合，最終得到 9468 個八度可信的片段（98.66% 落在第 3、4 八度，符合流行音樂主旋律的常見音域）。第三階段是把 JSON 標註轉成 kern：包含依調號推斷正確的音名拼法、用 Harte 語法標和弦、時間對齊、節奏量化，再合併主旋律譜線與和弦譜線。模型架構方面，基線是 CNN 編碼器加 Transformer 解碼器，作者做了三處改動：把解碼器前饋層維度從 256 擴到 1024 並改用 pre-norm（訓練更穩定）；把 CNN 換成凍結的 MuQ（自監督音樂特徵抽取器，每秒輸出 25 個特徵向量，比原本的 5.38 個密得多，再用線性投影加 LayerNorm 把維度從 1024 壓到 256，並改用一維位置編碼）；以及做離線資料增強（變調 ±1~3 半音、變速 0.9~1.1 倍，每個訓練樣本產生 6 個增強版本，資料量擴大 7 倍）。評估指標用基於 Levenshtein 距離的符號錯誤率（SER），推論階段用貪婪解碼。

Dataset construction has three stages. Acquisition: 20,072 YouTube audio clips were successfully downloaded out of 26,175 attempts, paired with beat-to-time alignment from the original SheetSage annotations. The hardest stage, octave inference, addresses that the original annotations carry relative pitch only, with no absolute octave: the authors extract a vocal stem via Mel-band Roformer, run RMVPE pitch estimation to get 72 semitone-spaced probability bins, then exhaustively test 12 hypotheses (6 octave shifts × 2 start-time offsets), keeping the highest-confidence combination above a 0.2 threshold — yielding 9,468 clips with confident octave assignment (98.66% falling in octaves 3–4, consistent with typical pop-melody range). The final stage converts JSON annotations to kern: inferring correct note spelling from the key signature, labeling chords with Harte syntax, aligning timing, quantizing rhythm, and merging melody and chord spines. On the model side, the baseline is a CNN encoder with a Transformer decoder; the authors make three changes: expanding the decoder feedforward dimension from 256 to 1024 with pre-norm (more stable training); replacing the CNN with a frozen MuQ self-supervised music feature extractor (25 feature vectors/second versus the baseline's 5.38, projected down from 1024 to 256 dimensions via a linear layer plus LayerNorm, with 1D rather than 2D positional encoding); and offline data augmentation (pitch shifts of ±1–3 semitones, tempo scaling of 0.9–1.1×, generating 6 augmented variants per example for a 7× data expansion). Evaluation uses Levenshtein-distance-based Symbol Error Rate (SER) with greedy decoding at inference.

限制Limitations

作者誠實列出了資料本身的雜訊來源：SheetSage-A2S 的原始標註來自社群手抄樂譜，和弦標記的細緻程度不一致（有人每次和聲變化才標一次，有人每個節奏重音或每個小節都標一次），主旋律的音節、滑音、裝飾音處理也不統一，還有部分片段標註提早結束、或人聲靜默時卻標了伴奏音，這些都會人為拉高在這個資料集上量出來的錯誤率，代表 20.92% SER 這個數字本身帶有資料品質的噪音，不能直接等同於模型能力的天花板。模型本身則在複雜和弦（七和弦、擴展和聲）上表現差、容易混淆和弦轉位，最大弱點是音符時長判斷：容易把連續音符合併或把單一音符切開，尤其在饒舌等重複音高密集的段落特別明顯，另外偶有不超過八分音符的細微時間偏差。整體而言，這篇論文承認自己是在一個標註品質參差不齊的新資料集上打地基，後續需要專門處理標註不一致與建立更細緻的評估指標。

The authors are candid about noise inherent to the data itself: SheetSage-A2S annotations come from community-transcribed sheet music, and chord-labeling granularity is inconsistent (some annotators mark chords only at harmony changes, others at every rhythmic strum or every measure); melodic treatment of syllables, pitch bends, and grace notes is likewise inconsistent; and some clips have annotations that end early or label accompaniment during vocal silence. All of this artificially inflates the measured error rate on this dataset, meaning the 20.92% SER figure carries data-quality noise and shouldn't be read as a hard ceiling on model capability. The model itself struggles with complex chords (7ths, extended harmonies), confuses chord inversions, and its biggest weakness is note-duration prediction — merging consecutive notes or splitting a single note, especially in pitch-repetitive passages like rap verses — plus occasional minor timing deviations rarely exceeding an eighth note. Overall, the paper is upfront that it's building on a new dataset with uneven annotation quality, and follow-up work needs to tackle annotation inconsistency and finer-grained evaluation metrics directly.

討論Discussion

三項貢獻疊加出的結果相當扎實：第一個真實錄音的流行音樂 A2S 資料集、驗證了 MuQ 這類自監督音樂特徵在轉錄任務上遠優於從零訓練的 CNN（在新資料集上帶來最大單項提升，相對誤差降低 53%）、以及資料增強帶來的最後一段提升，三者疊加把古典音樂基準的 SER 從既有最佳的 15.3% 打到 4.98%（相對誤差降低 67.5%），流行音樂基準則建立在 20.92% SER。值得注意的是消融實驗顯示的優先順序：架構調整（pre-norm、擴大前饋層）本身就貢獻了古典音樂上 44% 的誤差降低，MuQ 帶來最大的單項跳躍，資料增強是錦上添花——這個順序對之後想在類似任務上省算力的研究者是有用的參考。作者也誠實地把「哪些錯誤是模型的鍋、哪些是資料標註不一致造成的」分開講清楚，沒有把數字灌水成模型能力的全部功勞，這種對資料侷限的坦白在轉錄類論文裡不算常見，也是這篇值得肯定的地方。後續方向包括擴展到樂器演奏的旋律（不只是人聲主旋律）、修正標註不一致、以及設計比 SER 更貼近音樂可讀性的評估指標。

The three stacked contributions add up to something solid: the first real-recording popular-music A2S dataset, evidence that self-supervised music features like MuQ substantially outperform a from-scratch-trained CNN for transcription (the single largest gain on the new dataset, a 53% relative error reduction), and a final increment from data augmentation — together driving the classical-music benchmark from the prior best of 15.3% SER down to 4.98% (a 67.5% relative reduction), while establishing a 20.92% SER benchmark for popular music. The ablation ordering is worth noting: architectural changes alone (pre-norm, wider feedforward) already account for a 44% error reduction on classical music, MuQ delivers the single biggest jump, and augmentation is the final polish — a useful prioritization for anyone trying to conserve compute on a similar task. The authors are also unusually candid about separating which errors are the model's fault versus artifacts of inconsistent annotation, rather than inflating the numbers as pure model capability — a transparency about data limitations that's not that common in transcription papers, and worth crediting. Future directions include extending to instrument-performed melodies (not just vocal lines), resolving annotation inconsistencies, and designing evaluation metrics closer to musical readability than raw SER.

MI-MIDI: Mechanistic Interpretability of Text-to-MIDI Generation Models via Probing, Lenses and Steering

MI-MIDI：透過探針、透鏡與操控技術對文字轉 MIDI 生成模型做機制可解釋性分析

Jakub Poćwiardowski, Mateusz Modrzejewski
arXiv:2608.06638 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：機制可解釋性（mechanistic interpretability）這套從語言模型研究發展出來的工具——線性探針、logit/tuned lens、activation patching、方向性操控——過去幾乎只用在音訊音樂模型（例如 MusicGen），這篇是少數把整套工具搬到符號音樂（text-to-MIDI）模型上的研究，而且刻意選了架構迥異的兩個模型（cross-attention 的 text2midi 與把 MIDI token 塞進 Llama 的 MIDI-LLM）對照分析，發現兩者形成音樂預測的方式截然不同——這個架構決定可操控性的發現，對想要控制生成模型輸出（例如調音域、調複音密度）的人有直接的工程意義，所以標記為深度分析。Why this one: Mechanistic interpretability tooling developed for language models — linear probes, logit/tuned lenses, activation patching, directional steering — has almost exclusively been applied to audio music models (e.g. MusicGen). This paper is one of the first to bring the full toolkit to symbolic, text-to-MIDI models, and deliberately contrasts two architecturally different systems (the cross-attention-based text2midi versus MIDI-LLM, which grafts MIDI tokens onto a Llama backbone) to find that they form musical predictions in fundamentally different ways. That architecture-determines-controllability finding has direct engineering relevance for anyone trying to steer generation output (register, polyphony density, etc.), which is why it's flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

近年語言模型的機制可解釋性研究發展出一整套成熟工具：用線性探針檢驗某個概念是否可以從內部激活值線性解碼出來；用 logit lens／tuned lens 觀察模型在每一層「如果現在就輸出，會輸出什麼」；用 activation patching 找出資訊在哪一層被因果性地綁定到輸出；用方向性操控（steering）驗證某個方向的激活加法能不能可控地改變輸出。這些工具在音樂生成模型上的應用幾乎全部集中在音訊模型（如 MusicGen），符號音樂生成——尤其是最近因為 MIDICaps 這類資料集興起的文字轉 MIDI 模型——完全沒人碰過。作者提出三個核心問題：這些模型內部到底編碼了哪些音樂概念（音高、樂器、和聲）？這些概念在網路的哪一層、什麼時候形成最終預測？以及這些被解碼出來的資訊是不是「因果上真的有用」，能不能拿來操控生成結果？這篇論文的目標就是把語言模型可解釋性的方法論第一次系統性地搬到符號音樂生成模型上，並且刻意選兩個架構差異很大的模型做對照，檢驗「架構設計」本身如何決定音樂概念的表徵方式與可操控性。

Mechanistic interpretability research on language models has matured into a full toolkit: linear probes test whether a concept is linearly decodable from internal activations; logit/tuned lenses reveal what a layer would output if generation stopped there; activation patching locates where information becomes causally bound to the output; and directional steering tests whether adding an activation vector can controllably shift the output. Applied to music generation, this toolkit has almost exclusively targeted audio models like MusicGen — symbolic music generation, especially the recent wave of text-to-MIDI models enabled by datasets like MIDICaps, has gone entirely unexamined. The authors pose three core questions: what musical concepts (pitch, instrument, harmony) are actually encoded internally; at which layer and when do these concepts crystallize into a final prediction; and is the decoded information causally useful — can it actually steer generation? The paper's goal is to bring language-model interpretability methodology to symbolic music generation systematically for the first time, deliberately contrasting two architecturally distinct models to examine how architecture itself determines how musical concepts are represented and how controllable they are.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景涵蓋三條線：文字條件式音樂生成（特別是 MIDICaps 這類資料集，讓端到端的文字轉 MIDI 模型變得可行）；機制可解釋性技術本身（線性探針、logit/tuned lens、activation steering，均源自語言模型研究）；以及既有的音樂模型可解釋性工作（過去幾乎只做音訊模型如 MusicGen，符號系統幾乎是空白）。研究對象選了兩個代表性但架構迥異的系統：text2midi 是編碼器-解碼器架構，用凍結的 Flan-T5 當文字編碼器，透過 cross-attention 把文字資訊持續餵給 18 層、768 維隱藏層的解碼器，用 REMI+ 做符號音樂的 tokenization；MIDI-LLM 則是把 Llama 3.2 1B（16 層、2048 維隱藏層）這個純解碼器語言模型的詞表直接擴充，塞進 MIDI token，用 context 方式（而不是 cross-attention）做文字條件，tokenization 用 AMT 格式。這個選擇本身就是研究設計的核心：一個是「音樂 token 和文字 token 從一開始就在共享的殘差流裡競爭」的架構，一個是「文字資訊透過獨立通道持續注入」的架構,這兩種條件方式理論上應該導致完全不同的資訊處理模式。

The related work spans three threads: text-conditioned music generation, particularly datasets like MIDICaps that made end-to-end text-to-MIDI models feasible; the mechanistic interpretability techniques themselves (linear probes, logit/tuned lenses, activation steering), all originating in language-model research; and prior music-model interpretability work, which has almost exclusively targeted audio models like MusicGen, leaving symbolic systems essentially unexamined. The two studied systems are chosen to be architecturally distinct: text2midi is an encoder-decoder model with a frozen Flan-T5 text encoder feeding a REMI+-tokenized, 18-layer, 768-hidden-dimension decoder via cross-attention conditioning; MIDI-LLM instead extends a Llama 3.2 1B decoder-only language model (16 layers, 2048 hidden dimensions) with an expanded vocabulary that includes MIDI tokens, conditioning on text via context rather than cross-attention, and using AMT-style tokenization. This choice is itself the core of the research design: one architecture has music and text tokens competing in a shared residual stream from the start, while the other injects text information through a persistently separate channel — two conditioning strategies that should, in principle, produce very different information-processing patterns.

方法Method

方法分四層。線性探針：在每一層的激活值上訓練 L2 正則化邏輯回歸探針，檢驗音高類別、樂器家族、和弦性質、和聲功能、推估調性等概念能不能線性解碼，同時對真實生成內容與來自 SynTheory 資料集的受控合成範例都做探測，並用打亂標籤的對照探針確認結果反映的是真實表徵而非探針本身的容量。Logit lens 與 tuned lens：經典 logit lens 直接看每一層「現在停下來會輸出什麼」；對於詞表混雜文字與音樂 token 的 MIDI-LLM，額外分析限制在音樂詞表內的分佈；tuned lens 額外訓練一個小型仿射轉換器，把中間層表徵映射到最終層的座標系，用來區分「預測在後段真的發生劇烈變化」還是「只是座標系旋轉」這兩種可能。Activation patching：把某一層的激活值替換成對比提示詞（例如鋼琴 vs. 小提琴）產生的激活值，量測樂器資訊在哪一層被因果性地「綁定」進生成內容，藉此定位資訊整合發生的深度。方向性操控：用對比提示詞的激活值算出「均值差」方向，加回殘差流裡，並設計了一個新穎的雙向協議——分別檢驗「往正向加」與「往負向加」是否會產生方向相反的效果（真正的方向性控制）還是只是對稱的漂移，追蹤的指標包括音密度（代表節奏/能量）、平均 MIDI 音高（代表音域）、同時發聲的音符數（代表複音密度）。實驗規模包括 1000 個隨機生成提示用於完整生成探測、200 個序列子樣本用於逐 token 探測、5 折分組交叉驗證、tuned lens 用 6 萬個 token 位置訓練、5 萬個位置留作驗證，操控實驗涵蓋 10680 個序列，並用 2000 次自助抽樣算信賴區間。

The methodology has four layers. Linear probing: L2-regularized logistic regression probes are fit on activations at each layer to test whether pitch class, instrument family, chord quality, harmonic function, and estimated key are linearly decodable, run on both realistic full generations and controlled synthetic examples from the SynTheory dataset, with shuffled-label control probes confirming results reflect genuine representational content rather than probe capacity. Logit and tuned lens: the classic logit lens shows what each layer would predict if generation stopped there; for MIDI-LLM, whose vocabulary mixes text and music tokens, the analysis is additionally restricted to the musical-vocabulary distribution; the tuned lens trains a small affine translator mapping intermediate representations into the final layer's basis, distinguishing genuine late-stage prediction refinement from mere basis rotation. Activation patching: activations at a given layer are swapped with those from a contrasting prompt (e.g. piano vs. violin) to measure at which layer instrument information becomes causally bound into generation, localizing the depth of information integration. Directional steering: difference-in-means vectors computed from contrastive prompts are additively injected into the residual stream, using a novel bidirectional protocol that separately tests whether adding the vector in the positive versus negative direction produces genuinely opposite effects (true directional control) versus merely symmetric drift, tracking note density (tempo/energy proxy), mean MIDI pitch (register), and simultaneous note count (polyphony). Experimental scale includes 1,000 randomly generated prompts for full-generation probing, 200-sequence subsamples for token-level probing, 5-fold grouped cross-validation, a tuned lens trained on 60,000 token positions with 50,000 held out for evaluation, steering experiments spanning 10,680 sequences, and 2,000 bootstrap resamples for confidence intervals.

限制Limitations

作者列出五點限制。均值差方向的品質問題：對比提示詞在詞彙和音樂內容上同時有差異，方向的「乾淨程度」直接取決於對比組設計得好不好，混雜的對比可能讓操控方向不夠純粹。指標間的相依性：音符密度（用來代表節奏/能量）本身跟複音密度高度相關，兩者不是完全獨立的測量，操控其中一個難免影響另一個，讓因果解讀打了折扣。標籤品質：序列層級的調性估計用 Krumhansl-Schmuckler 演算法，本身就有誤差，一些較弱的高層概念表徵結果可能只是反映標籤噪音而非模型真的沒學到。抽象概念難以量化：更抽象的音樂性質（例如整體樂句結構、情緒）在多軌 MIDI 裡難以明確定義與測量，完全排除在量化分析之外。模型範圍：只研究了兩個模型，無法把「條件機制的差異」和「其他架構差異（模型大小、tokenization、訓練資料）」完全切開，觀察到的差異有可能部分來自條件機制之外的因素。

The authors list five limitations. Difference-in-means direction quality: contrastive prompts vary both lexically and musically, so the cleanliness of a steering direction depends heavily on how well-matched the contrast set is, and mixed contrasts can leave the direction impure. Metric interdependence: note density (used as a tempo/energy proxy) correlates strongly with polyphony, so the two are not fully independent measurements, and steering one inevitably affects the other, weakening causal interpretation. Label quality: sequence-level key estimates rely on the Krumhansl-Schmuckler algorithm, which itself carries error, so some weak higher-level concept representations may reflect label noise rather than a genuine gap in what the model learned. Hard-to-quantify abstraction: more abstract musical properties (overall phrase structure, mood) are difficult to define and measure unambiguously in multi-track MIDI and are excluded from the quantitative analysis entirely. Model scope: only two models are studied, so differences attributable to the conditioning mechanism cannot be fully separated from other architectural differences (model size, tokenization, training data) — the observed contrasts may partly reflect factors beyond conditioning alone.

討論Discussion

最核心的發現是兩個模型形成音樂預測的方式截然不同，而這個差異可以直接追溯到條件機制的設計。text2midi 用 cross-attention 讓解碼器在每一層都能重新查閱編碼過的文字提示，結果是預測在各層逐步、漸進地精煉，沒有單一層出現劇烈跳躍，樂器資訊的整合也是分散在各層，沒有集中的深度峰值——這種「隨時可查閱提示」的設計讓操控也變得穩定：在所有層同時注入操控向量依然穩定，且方向性最乾淨。MIDI-LLM 則呈現截然不同的兩階段模式：前 12 層幾乎完全停留在繼承自文字模型的表徵基底裡，音樂詞表的預測準確率接近零，詞表機率質量維持在跟詞表大小成比例的約 30%；到第 13 層開始劇烈轉向，第 15 層音樂詞表機率質量衝到約 95%，這個轉向還有明確的順序：先是特殊符號、再是音符、最後才是時間/音長token。用 tuned lens 檢驗發現這個劇烈跳躍有一部分只是座標系旋轉（不是真正新資訊的產生），但排除座標系效應後仍有實質的預測精煉在發生。這個「資訊晚期才突然綁定」的模式直接反映在操控實驗上：MIDI-LLM 只有單層注入才穩定，多層同時注入操控向量會累積干擾，操控強度被限制在 α=0.75 這個上限；而 text2midi 全層注入依然穩定。這給出一個可以直接落地的工程結論：用 cross-attention 條件的模型，操控時可以放心用全層注入；用 LLM 改造、共享殘差流條件的模型，操控必須挑單一層小心注入，貪心地全層加會讓效果崩掉。這種「架構設計決定表徵形成路徑，進而決定可操控性上限」的因果鏈，是這篇論文對可解釋性研究和實務操控應用都有貢獻的地方。

The central finding is that the two models form musical predictions in fundamentally different ways, traceable directly to their conditioning mechanism. text2midi's cross-attention lets the decoder re-consult the encoded text prompt at every layer, producing gradual, layer-by-layer prediction refinement with no single sharp jump, and instrument-information integration is distributed across depth with no concentrated peak — this "always-available prompt" design also makes steering stable: injecting a steering vector at every layer simultaneously remains stable and yields the cleanest directional control. MIDI-LLM shows a starkly different two-stage pattern: the first 12 layers remain almost entirely within a basis inherited from the underlying text model, with near-zero musical-vocabulary prediction accuracy and vocabulary probability mass sitting at roughly 30% — proportional to vocabulary size — before rotating sharply starting at layer 13, reaching roughly 95% musical-vocabulary mass by layer 15, in an ordered sequence: special tokens first, then notes, then timing/duration tokens. The tuned lens shows part of this sharp jump is merely basis rotation rather than genuinely new information, though substantial real prediction refinement remains even after accounting for that. This late, sudden-binding pattern shows up directly in the steering results: MIDI-LLM is stable only with single-layer injection, while injecting the steering vector across all layers accumulates disruptive interference, capping usable strength at α=0.75; text2midi, in contrast, remains stable with all-layer injection. This yields directly usable engineering guidance: for cross-attention-conditioned models, all-layer steering injection is safe; for LLM-adapted models sharing a residual stream between prompt and generation, steering must target a single carefully chosen layer, since greedy all-layer injection collapses performance. This causal chain — architecture determines representation-formation pathway, which in turn determines the ceiling on controllability — is the paper's core contribution to both interpretability research and practical steering applications.
