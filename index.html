SubRoformer: Subband Time-Domain Representation for Music Source Separation

SubRoformer：先用固定的非均勻濾波器組把波形切成解調、降頻的複數子頻帶，再在 100 Hz 細網格估遮罩、25 Hz 粗網格跑軸向 RoFormer 的音源分離

Chunyat Wu, Chuang Ding, Paris Smaragdis, Qiuqiang Kong
arXiv:2611.08130 · eess.AS; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-11-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：BS-RoFormer 與 Mel-RoFormer 都是先對整段波形做一次 STFT，再把頻率格點分組；這篇改成在波形上先用 128 個重疊的三角形帶通濾波器切出子頻帶，每帶轉成解析訊號、解調到零頻、降頻 60 倍成 800 Hz 的複數基頻序列，再對每帶做 32 點局部 STFT。選它是因為它把「表示層要花多少參數」這件事量出來了：在分離器完全相同的對照下，表示與遮罩模組只佔 2.76M（11.45%），BS-RoFormer 則是 50.87M（70.47%）。只用 MUSDB18-HQ 訓練時，四個單聲部 SubRoFormer-B 的四聲部平均 SDR 是 10.63 dB，比 BS-RoFormer 自報的 10.02 dB 高 0.61 dB，但 bass 低 1.38 dB。代價也寫在表上：這個固定前端本身的重建 SDR 只有約 28 dB，STFT 則是 138 dB。Why this one: BS-RoFormer and Mel-RoFormer take one STFT of the whole waveform and then group its frequency bins; this paper instead splits the waveform with 128 overlapping triangular band-pass filters, turns each band into an analytic signal, demodulates it to zero frequency, decimates it by 60 into an 800 Hz complex baseband sequence, and then takes a 32-point local STFT per band. I picked it because it measures how many parameters the representation layer costs: with an identical separator, the representation and mask modules take 2.76M (11.45%), against 50.87M (70.47%) for BS-RoFormer. Trained on MUSDB18-HQ only, four single-stem SubRoFormer-B models reach a four-stem mean SDR of 10.63 dB, 0.61 dB above the 10.02 dB BS-RoFormer reports, but 1.38 dB lower on bass. The cost is also in the tables: the fixed frontend alone reconstructs at only about 28 dB SDR, against 138 dB for the STFT.

動機Motivation

音源分離要同時抓住長時間的音樂脈絡，又不能丟掉音高、暫態與相位這些細節。純波形模型一秒就要處理數萬個樣本，只好靠學出來的編碼器壓縮長度；全頻帶 STFT 模型則讓所有頻率共用同一組時頻解析度，低頻想要窄頻寬、高頻想要短時窗的需求只能折衷。

作者想把兩種「縮短序列」分開處理。第一種在訊號層：固定的子頻帶分析把每個頻帶降到很低的取樣率，而且保留一條明確的合成路徑。第二種在特徵層：分離器在 100 Hz 的細網格上估遮罩，但把相鄰四幀合成一個 patch，讓最主要的 Transformer 只在 25 Hz 上建模長距離依賴，再用跳接把細網格特徵接回來。

Source separation must capture long-range musical context without losing pitch, transients and phase. Waveform models face tens of thousands of samples per second and shorten the sequence with learned encoders; full-band STFT models give every frequency the same time–frequency resolution, so the need for narrow bandwidth at low frequencies and short windows at high frequencies can only be compromised.

The authors want to separate two kinds of sequence reduction. The first is at the signal level: a fixed subband analysis lowers each band to a very low sampling rate while keeping an explicit synthesis path. The second is at the feature level: the separator estimates masks on a 100 Hz fine grid, but groups four neighbouring frames into a patch so that its main Transformer models long-range dependencies at 25 Hz, and a skip path brings the fine-grid features back.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的比較對象是 BS-RoFormer（Lu 等，2024）與 Mel-RoFormer（Wang 等，2023）：前者把 STFT 頻率格點切成不等寬、互不重疊的頻帶，後者改成重疊的 mel 頻帶，兩者都在時間軸與頻帶軸交替做注意力。SubRoformer 的分離器主體直接沿用 BS-RoFormer 的軸向區塊，差別在前端：分頻發生在波形上、STFT 之前，而且每帶先降頻。再往前是 BSRNN（Luo 與 Yu，2023），它用 RNN 做同樣的時間／頻帶交替；以及 Demucs 系列，用學出來的編碼器縮短波形序列。

論文宣稱的「子頻帶時域表示」最像的其實是作者群自己的前作 CWS-PResUNet（Liu、Kong 等，2021）：同樣先用 PQMF 把波形濾波降頻成子頻帶，再對每帶做 STFT 後分離。差別在於 PQMF 是等寬、實數的子頻帶，這裡是 mel 加線性間距、重疊的三角形頻帶，而且解調成複數基頻。從訊號處理看，「帶通、解調、降頻」本身就是教科書上的複數調變濾波器組（Vaidyanathan 1990 的多速率框架，論文有引用），新的是把它和 mel 分頻接在一起。

The most direct comparisons are BS-RoFormer (Lu et al., 2024) and Mel-RoFormer (Wang et al., 2023): the former splits STFT bins into unequal, non-overlapping bands, the latter uses overlapping mel bands, and both alternate attention along time and band axes. SubRoformer's separator body directly reuses BS-RoFormer's axial blocks; the difference is the frontend, where band splitting happens on the waveform, before the STFT, and each band is decimated first. Further back are BSRNN (Luo and Yu, 2023), which performs the same time/band alternation with RNNs, and the Demucs line, which shortens waveform sequences with learned encoders.

The closest precedent to the claimed subband time-domain representation is in fact the authors' own earlier CWS-PResUNet (Liu, Kong et al., 2021), which also filters and decimates the waveform into PQMF subbands and then separates on per-band STFTs. The difference is that PQMF gives equal-width real subbands, whereas here the bands are overlapping triangles with mel plus linear spacing, demodulated to complex baseband. In signal-processing terms, band-pass, demodulate and decimate is the textbook complex modulated filter bank (the multirate framework of Vaidyanathan 1990, which the paper cites); what is new is coupling it with mel band splitting.

方法Method

固定前端。 128 個三角形目標響應的濾波器在 0 到 Fs/2 間加總為一，低頻用 mel 間距、高頻用 390 Hz 的線性間距，以 SciPy firwin2 與 Hamming 窗做成 FIR，係數固定不學。每帶經 Hilbert 轉換成解析訊號、乘上 e^{−j2πf_k n/Fs} 移到零頻，再每 60 個樣本取一個：48 kHz 變成 800 Hz 的複數序列。合成路徑反過來做內插、重新調變、取實部、跨帶加總。論文另外提出多相（polyphase）實作，讓濾波和降頻一起做，不去算之後會被丟掉的樣本，複雜度從 O(KNL) 降到 O(K·L/D·log(L/D))。

重建代價。 表 I 在 50 首測試曲上量前端本身：mel–linear 版本的混音重建 SDR 是 28.04 dB（vocals 26.80、bass 47.78、drums 26.58、other 29.03），係數率 4.267，與 STFT 的 4.271 幾乎一樣；但 STFT、MDCT、Haar 小波都在 131 到 139 dB。也就是說，這個前端在分離之前就先放進了約 28 dB 的失真。

Fixed frontend. 128 filters with triangular target responses that sum to one between 0 and Fs/2, mel-spaced at low frequencies and linearly spaced at 390 Hz above, are built as FIR filters with SciPy firwin2 and a Hamming window; the coefficients are fixed, not learned. Each band is made analytic with a Hilbert transform, shifted to zero frequency by multiplying with e^{−j2πf_k n/Fs}, and kept every 60th sample: 48 kHz becomes an 800 Hz complex sequence. Synthesis reverses this with interpolation, remodulation, taking the real part and summing over bands. The paper also gives a polyphase implementation that filters and decimates jointly without computing samples to be discarded, reducing complexity from O(KNL) to O(K·L/D·log(L/D)).

Reconstruction cost. Table I measures the frontend alone on the 50 test songs: the mel–linear version reconstructs the mixture at 28.04 dB SDR (vocals 26.80, bass 47.78, drums 26.58, other 29.03) with a coefficient rate of 4.267, almost equal to the STFT's 4.271; but STFT, MDCT and the Haar wavelet packet all reach 131 to 139 dB. In other words, the frontend inserts about 28 dB of distortion before any separation happens.

限制Limitations

作者自己在結論裡把 10.63 dB 稱為「初步」結果，並列出要變成穩固期刊主張所缺的東西：版本化的評測產物、重複種子、效率量測、參數對齊的四聲部消融。消融全部只做 vocals、每個設定一個種子，而且用的是 400k 步的檢查點；10.88 dB 的參照分數來自另一次初步訓練，跟主結果不是同一批。所以「局部子頻帶 STFT 比全域 STFT 分頻好 1.45 dB」這類數字只能當方向。

比較表混了三種不同的東西：基線分數取自各自原論文，訓練步數、推論片段長度都不一樣；多聲部與單聲部的模型數量不同；加資料的實驗用了 1.4M 步與不同 batch size，所以從 10.19 升到 11.59 dB 的 1.40 dB 不能全算給那 500 首內部歌曲，而且那批歌曲沒有公開。與 ACE Studio 的比較用的是有損 Opus 匯出、模型版本與訓練資料都未知。前端約 28 dB 的重建上限對目前 10 dB 左右的分離分數影響不大，但如果分離品質繼續往上推，它會變成天花板，論文沒有討論這一點。

The authors themselves call 10.63 dB a preliminary result in the conclusion and list what is missing to make it a robust journal claim: versioned evaluation artifacts, repeated seeds, efficiency measurements and parameter-matched four-stem ablations. All ablations are vocals-only with one seed per setting and use 400k-update checkpoints; the 10.88 dB reference score comes from a separate preliminary run, not the same batch as the main results. Numbers such as local subband STFT beating global-STFT band splitting by 1.45 dB are therefore directional only.

The comparison tables mix three different things: baseline scores are taken from their original papers with different update counts and inference segment lengths; multi-stem and single-stem entries use different numbers of models; and the extra-data run uses 1.4M updates and a different batch size, so the 1.40 dB rise from 10.19 to 11.59 dB cannot all be credited to the 500 internal songs, which are not released. The ACE Studio comparison uses lossy Opus exports with unknown model version and training data. The frontend's roughly 28 dB reconstruction ceiling matters little at current separation scores around 10 dB, but it becomes a ceiling if separation quality keeps rising, and the paper does not discuss this.

討論Discussion

結果。 不加資料時，單聲部 SubRoFormer-B（四個網路共 388.68M）四聲部平均 10.63 dB：vocals 12.63、bass 10.20、drums 10.40、other 9.27；BS-RoFormer 是 11.02／11.58／9.66／7.80，平均 10.02。多聲部版本裡，22.59M 的 SubRoFormer-S 拿到 10.29 dB，比 41.2M 的 SCNet-large 高 0.60 dB。

有一點值得注意：放大到 B 之後，多聲部版本反而從 10.29 掉到 10.19 dB，其中 bass 掉了 0.78 dB；作者說這只能說明聯合預測的效益依模型大小與聲部而定，不能直接歸因為過擬合。加 500 首歌後，多聲部 SubRoFormer-B 到 11.59 dB，仍低於用三個 93.4M 網路的 BS-RoFormer L12（11.99 dB）。

消融。 把前端換成全域 STFT 分頻或 mel 分頻，vocals SDR 從 10.88 掉到 9.43 與 8.88 dB；從 128 帶減到 64 帶掉 1.31 dB（但頻寬與取樣率也一起變了）。拿掉跳接融合掉 0.43 dB，GEGLU 比加法融合高 0.11 dB。反而把 patch 因子從 4 降到 1 會提高 0.14 dB，代表 25 Hz 的粗網格是用一點品質換序列長度，只是論文沒有量執行時間，無法知道換到多少。

我覺得這篇最有用的是表 III 的參數拆帳：同一個分離器，BS-RoFormer 七成參數花在頻帶投影與遮罩頭上，而固定濾波器組把這部分壓到 2.76M。這說明 band-split 家族的大部分參數可能不在「建模」上，值得後續用參數對齊的四聲部實驗確認。

Results. Without extra data, single-stem SubRoFormer-B (388.68M across four networks) reaches a four-stem mean of 10.63 dB: vocals 12.63, bass 10.20, drums 10.40, other 9.27; BS-RoFormer reports 11.02/11.58/9.66/7.80 with a mean of 10.02. Among multi-stem models, the 22.59M SubRoFormer-S reaches 10.29 dB, 0.60 dB above the 41.2M SCNet-large.

One point stands out: scaling up to B lowers the multi-stem version from 10.29 to 10.19 dB, with bass down 0.78 dB; the authors say this only shows that the benefit of joint prediction depends on model size and stem, and cannot be attributed to overfitting. With 500 extra songs, multi-stem SubRoFormer-B reaches 11.59 dB, still below BS-RoFormer L12 with three 93.4M networks (11.99 dB).

Ablations. Replacing the frontend with global-STFT band splitting or mel band splitting lowers vocals SDR from 10.88 to 9.43 and 8.88 dB; going from 128 to 64 bands costs 1.31 dB (though bandwidth and sampling rate change with it). Removing skip fusion costs 0.43 dB, and GEGLU beats additive fusion by 0.11 dB. By contrast, lowering the patch factor from 4 to 1 gains 0.14 dB, meaning the 25 Hz coarse grid trades a little quality for sequence length; the paper measures no runtime, so the size of that saving is unknown.

I find Table III the most useful part, the parameter accounting: with the same separator, BS-RoFormer spends about 70% of its parameters on band projections and mask heads, while the fixed filter bank cuts this part to 2.76M. This suggests that most parameters in the band-split family may not go to modelling, which deserves confirmation with parameter-matched four-stem experiments.

ProCraft: Instruction-Driven Music Production from Free-Form Text

ProCraft：0.5B 的 codec 語言模型照自由文字指令一次完成七種音樂編輯（含複合指令），用持續編輯頭的門控交叉注意力、中間層對齊 MERT／音高／樂器，以及由工具呼叫 LLM 即時合成的編輯三元組訓練

Yuan-Chiao Cheng, Yi-Hsuan Yang, Vincent K.M. Cheung
arXiv:2611.07423 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：製作人常一次提好幾個要求，例如「拿掉 bass、讓鋼琴亮一點、空間感多一點」，但現有的文字引導編輯器多半一次只做一種操作、只吃幾秒的片段。ProCraft 從 InspireMusic 0.5B 微調，對 30 秒片段支援移除、抽取、伴奏生成、單樂器 cover、樂器轉換、去噪與效果器七種操作。選它是因為它把自己輸在哪裡講得很清楚：Slakh 移除任務上 SI-SDR 是 −2.96 dB，HTDemucs 是 +8.31 dB，而 11.3 dB 的差距裡有約 7.3 dB 來自 0.9 kbps 的單碼本 codec。它也說明 SI-SDR 會誤導：在 MoisesDB 抽取任務上，AudioSep 的 SI-SDR 較高（−3.69 對 −15.14 dB），卻只有 7.3% 的項目真的抽出了指定聲部，ProCraft 是 82.0%。16 人聽測中，它在移除與去噪上平均分數最高，在抽取與樂器轉換上跟大 16 倍的 Audio-Omni 沒有顯著差異。Why this one: Producers often make several requests at once, such as "remove the bass, make the piano brighter and more spacious", yet most text-guided editors perform one operation per instruction on clips of a few seconds. ProCraft, fine-tuned from InspireMusic 0.5B, supports seven operations on 30 s excerpts: removal, extraction, accompaniment generation, single-instrument cover, instrument transfer, denoising and audio effects. I picked it because it is clear about where it loses: on Slakh removal its SI-SDR is −2.96 dB against +8.31 dB for HTDemucs, and about 7.3 dB of that 11.3 dB gap comes from the 0.9 kbps single-codebook codec. It also shows how SI-SDR misleads: on MoisesDB extraction AudioSep scores higher SI-SDR (−3.69 vs −15.14 dB) but isolates the requested stem in only 7.3% of items, against 82.0% for ProCraft. In a 16-listener test it has the highest mean ratings on removal and denoising, and no significant difference from the 16-times larger Audio-Omni on extraction and instrument transfer.

動機Motivation

分離、伴奏生成、樂器轉換、效果器各自都有專門模型，但每個只做一種編輯；製作流程裡的要求常常是好幾項一起來。自由文字編輯器能直接收這種要求，但作者整理的表 1 顯示，現有系統大多只支援少數操作或樂器、只處理幾秒片段，或一次只做一件事。例如 Audio-Omni 能用自由文字改整個混音，卻做不好「只拿掉一個樂器」這類聲部層級操作；LLM2Fx-Tools 能處理多步驟指令，但輸出的是效果器參數，不是編輯後的音訊。

要在單一生成模型裡做到這件事有兩個難點：模型得從措辭鬆散的指令推出要動哪些聲部、做哪些操作；而且指令沒提到的部分都要保留。後者在伴奏生成這類創作型操作上尤其難，因為模型要寫新東西，同時不能動到原本的混音。

Separation, accompaniment generation, instrument transfer and audio effects each have dedicated models, but each performs one kind of edit, whereas production requests often arrive together. Free-text editors can take such requests directly, but the authors' Table 1 shows that most existing systems support few operations or instruments, handle clips of a few seconds, or do one thing per instruction. Audio-Omni, for example, edits whole mixtures from free text but struggles with stem-level operations such as removing a single instrument; LLM2Fx-Tools handles multi-step instructions but outputs effect parameters rather than edited audio.

Doing this in one generative model raises two difficulties: the model must infer which stems and operations a loosely worded instruction targets, and it must preserve everything the instruction does not mention. The latter is especially hard for creative operations such as accompaniment generation, where the model writes new material without touching the existing mix.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

架構上最接近的前例是語音領域的 SpeechX（Wang 等，2024）：把文字指令與音訊 codec token 接成一條序列，讓一個 codec 語言模型做去噪、目標說話者抽取等多種任務。ProCraft 等於把這個做法搬到多聲部音樂上。音樂這邊最直接的對手是 Instruct-MusicGen（Zhang 等，2025），它在 MusicGen 上另開一條路徑接收來源音訊，指令與音訊分開進模型；ProCraft 則讓兩者在同一條因果序列裡，作者也用同樣資料重訓 Instruct-MusicGen 來比較這個差別。

兩個新增機制都直接借自影像與視覺語言模型：持續編輯頭是 BLIP-2 的 Q-Former 式可學查詢，加上 Flamingo 的 tanh 門控交叉注意力（初始化為零）；中間層對齊則是 REPA（Yu 等，2025）的做法，從擴散 Transformer 移到 codec 語言模型。至於「On-the-fly Editing-pair Synthesis」，最像的是 InstructPix2Pix（Brooks 等，2023）用 LLM 寫編輯指令、再自動生成配對影像的資料流程；差別在這裡的目標音訊是由多軌素材實際算出來的，效果器也真的被執行，所以指令與目標按構造一致。

The closest architectural precedent is SpeechX (Wang et al., 2024) in speech, which concatenates a text instruction and audio codec tokens into one sequence so that a single codec language model performs denoising, target-speaker extraction and other tasks. ProCraft essentially carries this over to multi-stem music. The most direct music competitor is Instruct-MusicGen (Zhang et al., 2025), which adds a separate path to MusicGen for the source audio so that instruction and audio enter the model separately; ProCraft puts both in one causal sequence, and the authors retrain Instruct-MusicGen on the same data to compare this difference.

Both added mechanisms are borrowed directly from image and vision-language models: the persistent edit head is a BLIP-2 Q-Former-style set of learnable queries combined with Flamingo's tanh-gated cross-attention initialised at zero; intermediate-layer alignment is the REPA (Yu et al., 2025) recipe moved from diffusion Transformers to a codec language model. As for On-the-fly Editing-pair Synthesis, the closest precedent is the data pipeline of InstructPix2Pix (Brooks et al., 2023), in which an LLM writes edit instructions and paired images are generated automatically; the difference here is that the target audio is computed from multitrack material and the effects are actually executed, so instruction and target match by construction.

方法Method

序列與 codec。 凍結的 WavTokenizer 把 24 kHz 單聲道混音編成每秒 75 個、詞彙 4,096 的單一碼流，30 秒就是 2,250 個 token。輸入序列是 [指令 token；來源 codec；32 個可學查詢；目標 codec]，模型以 teacher forcing 預測目標 codec，解碼後再用 InspireMusic 凍結的 flow matching 超解析與 HifiCodec 升到 48 kHz 單聲道。

編輯頭。 32 個查詢在因果注意力下已看過指令與來源，它們最後一層的隱藏狀態就是編輯頭 h_edit；24 層每一層都加一條指向 h_edit 的交叉注意力，輸出乘上 tanh(α)、α 從零開始學。因為 h_edit 來自最後一層卻要給每一層用，訓練改成兩次前向：先編碼前綴拿到 h_edit 與 KV cache，再對目標做 teacher forcing。

對齊與損失。 第 4 層（共 24 層）的隱藏狀態接四個 MLP 投影器，分別對齊 MERT-330M 嵌入（餘弦距離）、88 個音高加 47 個鼓音的逐幀活動、8 類樂器活動（二元交叉熵），以及 StemFX 效果器嵌入。總損失是下一個 token 交叉熵加上 z-loss 與這四項，權重分別是 10⁻⁴、0.5、1.0、0.5、0.5。

資料。 訓練只用 Slakh2100（約 145 小時）：40% 項目是聲部操作加效果器、40% 只有聲部操作、20% 只有效果器。Qwen3-30B-A3B 依任務、現有樂器與 30 種「編輯動機」之一寫出指令，並以工具呼叫指定效果器鏈，再用 MultiAFX 在各聲部上實際渲染。

Sequence and codec. A frozen WavTokenizer encodes a 24 kHz mono mix into one stream of 75 tokens per second from a 4,096-entry vocabulary, so 30 s is 2,250 tokens. The input sequence is [instruction tokens; source codes; 32 learnable queries; target codes]; the model predicts the target codes with teacher forcing, and the decoded audio is lifted to 48 kHz mono by InspireMusic's frozen flow-matching super-resolution and HifiCodec.

Edit head. Under causal attention the 32 queries have already seen the instruction and source, and their final-layer hidden states form the edit head h_edit; each of the 24 layers gains a cross-attention path to h_edit whose output is scaled by tanh(α), with α learned from zero. Because h_edit comes from the last layer but is used in every layer, training takes two forward passes: first the prefix is encoded to obtain h_edit and the KV cache, then the target is processed with teacher forcing.

Alignment and loss. Hidden states at layer 4 (of 24) feed four MLP projectors aligned respectively to MERT-330M embeddings (cosine distance), frame-level activity of 88 pitches plus 47 drum notes, activity of 8 instrument classes (binary cross-entropy), and StemFX effect embeddings. The total loss is next-token cross-entropy plus a z-loss and these four terms, weighted 10⁻⁴, 0.5, 1.0, 0.5 and 0.5.

Data. Training uses only Slakh2100 (about 145 hours): 40% of items combine a stem operation with effects, 40% are a stem operation alone, and 20% are effects alone. Qwen3-30B-A3B writes each instruction from the task, the instruments present and one of 30 editing motivations, specifies the effect chain as tool calls, and MultiAFX renders it on the stems.

限制Limitations

整個系統是 24 kHz 單聲道 codec 加上 48 kHz 單聲道的升頻，評測也全部在單聲道上做，立體聲與空間效果在資料生成時就被排除；對「音樂製作」這個定位來說是很大的缺口。codec 本身就讓有標準答案的任務吃了約 7.3 dB 的 SI-SDR，所以在移除、抽取、去噪上，它與 HTDemucs 這類專門分離器仍有明顯差距，作者在結論也承認。

訓練資料只有合成的 Slakh，MoisesDB 是唯一的真實錄音測試集，也就是分布外測試；指令全由同一個 Qwen 模型寫成，指令分布與效果器詞彙都繼承它的偏好。聽測規模小：16 位評分者，每項任務只準備 3 組來源與指令，每人每項任務只聽其中一組。在伴奏與 cover 上，Audio-Omni 與 SteerMusic 的保留分數較高（cover 上顯著）；作者認為那是因為它們常直接回傳原混音，這個解釋有道理，但同時也代表 ProCraft 在創作型任務上的音質仍落後。消融也不是全面有利：在 MoisesDB 樂器轉換上，拿掉兩個機制的 ProCraft-plain 在 CLAP-T（0.163 對 0.136）與 CLAP-A 上顯著較高。

The whole system is a 24 kHz mono codec followed by 48 kHz mono upsampling, all evaluation is mono, and stereo and spatial effects are excluded from data generation; for a system positioned as music production this is a large gap. The codec alone costs about 7.3 dB of SI-SDR on tasks with a ground truth, so on removal, extraction and denoising it still trails dedicated separators such as HTDemucs by a clear margin, as the authors acknowledge in the conclusion.

Training data is only synthetic Slakh, and MoisesDB is the only real-recording test set, i.e. an out-of-domain test; all instructions are written by the same Qwen model, so the instruction distribution and effect vocabulary inherit its preferences. The listening test is small: 16 raters, only 3 source-and-instruction pairs prepared per task, and each rater hears one of them per task. On accompaniment and cover, Audio-Omni and SteerMusic receive higher Preservation ratings (significantly on cover); the authors attribute this to their often returning the original mix, which is plausible, but it also means ProCraft's audio quality still lags on creative tasks. The ablation is not uniformly favourable either: on MoisesDB instrument transfer, ProCraft-plain without the two mechanisms scores significantly higher on CLAP-T (0.163 vs 0.136) and CLAP-A.

討論Discussion

單一操作。 在 Slakh 樂器轉換上，TokenSynth 的音高時序保留最好（frame-F1 0.641）但樂器辨識率只有 0.420，DDPM 反演編輯器正好相反（0.326 與 0.653）；ProCraft 是唯一在樂器辨識、frame-F1 與旋律 chroma 三項都高於 0.55 的系統。效果器任務上 InstructFX2FX 最接近目標，但 ProCraft 在 MoisesDB 的 AFx-Rep 餘弦（0.195）高於同樣走 DSP 的 FxSearcher 與 Text2FX（0.157、0.092），作者據此認為從指令推出正確效果與訊號處理本身一樣重要。伴奏上 MusicGen-Stem 的樂器辨識（0.947 對 0.840）與 APA（0.524 對 0.366）都較高。

機制消融。 拿掉交叉注意力與對齊後，創作型任務差不多，但需要「刪掉一部分、留下其餘」的任務明顯變差：MoisesDB 移除的編輯成功率從 38.0% 掉到 26.0%、編輯強度從 7.4 掉到 4.9 dB，抽取的 SI-SDR 從 −15.1 掉到 −17.4 dB，而且在所有門檻下都是 ProCraft 領先。用同一份資料重訓的 Instruct-MusicGen 在每一項操作上都落後，支持「指令與音訊放在同一序列」這個設計，但這裡骨幹也不同，兩者無法完全分開。

複合指令與聽測。 在 Slakh 複合指令上，ProCraft 的移除 SI-SDR 是 −7.39 dB，次佳的 Audio-Omni 是 −24.88 dB；三項有標準答案的任務都領先至少 8.25 dB（MoisesDB 上至少 6.33 dB）。聽測中移除任務的保留分數 4.06 對 Audio-Omni 1.38，但移除的「編輯」分數 2.50 對 1.69 經 Holm 校正後並不顯著（p = 0.341）。Audio-Omni 的參數是 7.9B、訓練音訊約 44,100 小時，ProCraft 是 0.5B、145 小時，這個對比讓「抽取與轉換打平」顯得有意義。

我覺得最值得帶走的是評測上的提醒：只看 SI-SDR 或 CLAP-A，「什麼都不改、直接回傳原音」或「改錯聲部」都可能拿到好分數，所以論文把編輯成功率與保留度分開量，這個做法值得其他編輯論文照做。

Single operations. On Slakh instrument transfer, TokenSynth preserves pitch and timing best (frame-F1 0.641) but recognises the requested instrument only 0.420 of the time, while the DDPM-inversion editor shows the opposite (0.326 and 0.653); ProCraft is the only system above 0.55 on instrument match, frame-F1 and melody chroma. On audio effects InstructFX2FX is closest to the target, but on MoisesDB ProCraft's AFx-Rep cosine (0.195) exceeds the DSP-based FxSearcher and Text2FX (0.157, 0.092), from which the authors argue that inferring the right effect from the instruction matters as much as the signal processing. On accompaniment, MusicGen-Stem leads on instrument match (0.947 vs 0.840) and APA (0.524 vs 0.366).

Mechanism ablation. Removing cross-attention and alignment leaves creative tasks about the same but clearly hurts tasks that must delete part of the mix and keep the rest: MoisesDB removal edit rate falls from 38.0% to 26.0% and edit strength from 7.4 to 4.9 dB, extraction SI-SDR falls from −15.1 to −17.4 dB, and ProCraft leads at every threshold. Instruct-MusicGen retrained on the same data trails on every operation, supporting the single-sequence design for instruction and audio, though the backbones also differ, so the two cannot be fully separated.

Compound instructions and listening test. On Slakh compound instructions ProCraft reaches −7.39 dB SI-SDR on removal against −24.88 dB for the next best, Audio-Omni; it leads by at least 8.25 dB on all three tasks with a ground truth (at least 6.33 dB on MoisesDB). In the listening test, removal Preservation is 4.06 against 1.38 for Audio-Omni, but removal Editing at 2.50 vs 1.69 is not significant after Holm correction (p = 0.341). Audio-Omni has 7.9B parameters and about 44,100 hours of training audio against ProCraft's 0.5B and 145 hours, which makes the tie on extraction and transfer meaningful.

The point I would take away is about evaluation: with SI-SDR or CLAP-A alone, returning the source unchanged or editing the wrong stem can still score well, so the paper measures edit success and preservation separately, a practice other editing papers should copy.

A Unified Multi-Encoder Framework for Music Lyric Intelligibility Assessment: Bridging Invasive and Non-Invasive Paradigms

歌詞可懂度評估的多編碼器框架：WavLM 逐幀對齊與 MuQ 全域音樂脈絡各自與 T5 歌詞嵌入做交叉注意力、逐耳打分再融合，並以 Whisper WER（有參考歌詞）或 Whisper 轉寫（無參考歌詞）補語意（ICASSP 2026 Cadenza 挑戰）

Jing Yang, Shenghao Liao, Shuqing Zhang, Yongyi Deng, Pan Li, Zerui Han, Gongping Huang

為什麼挑這篇：這是 ICASSP 2026 Cadenza 挑戰（預測聽損模擬下的歌詞可懂度）的兩頁參賽論文。凍結的 WavLM、MuQ、Whisper 與 T5 共約 3.3B 參數，只訓練約 6M 的融合模組。選它是因為它的消融表本身說了一件論文沒強調的事：在有參考歌詞的設定下，完整模型的評測集 RMSE 是 26.54，只用 Whisper WER 的官方基線是 29.08；但拿掉 Whisper 之後，WavLM 加 MuQ 的 RMSE 變成 30.68，比單用 WER 的基線還差。換句話說，可懂度的主要訊號仍來自 ASR 的錯誤率，那 3.3B 的聲學編碼器是在 WER 之上再改善約 2.5 RMSE。無參考歌詞版本把評測集 RMSE 從 STOI 基線的 34.89 降到 29.68，相關係數從 0.21 升到 0.57。Why this one: This is a two-page entry to the ICASSP 2026 Cadenza Challenge, which asks for predictions of lyric intelligibility under simulated hearing loss. Frozen WavLM, MuQ, Whisper and T5 total about 3.3B parameters, and only a fusion module of about 6M is trained. I picked it because its ablation table shows something the paper does not stress: in the setting with reference lyrics the full model reaches an evaluation RMSE of 26.54 against 29.08 for the official baseline that uses Whisper WER alone; but without Whisper, WavLM plus MuQ give an RMSE of 30.68, worse than the WER-only baseline. In other words, the main intelligibility signal still comes from the ASR error rate, and the 3.3B acoustic encoders improve on WER by about 2.5 RMSE. The version without reference lyrics lowers the evaluation RMSE from 34.89 for the STOI baseline to 29.68 and raises correlation from 0.21 to 0.57.

動機Motivation

歌詞聽不清楚會直接影響聽眾的理解與投入，對聽損者尤其如此。STOI、PESQ 這類語音指標是為說話聲設計的，遇到唱歌時的音高變化、拉長的母音與伴奏遮蔽就不太準。ICASSP 2026 Cadenza 挑戰提供了一個基準：不同曲風的歌曲片段，模擬無、輕度、中度聽損，由聽者標出聽懂的比例，參賽系統要預測這個分數。

作者想要一個框架同時處理兩種情況：有參考歌詞時（論文稱 invasive，即一般說的 intrusive）可以用 ASR 錯誤率當線索；沒有歌詞時（non-invasive）也能預測。

Unclear lyrics directly affect listeners' comprehension and engagement, especially for people with hearing loss. Speech metrics such as STOI and PESQ were designed for spoken voice and are inaccurate for singing, with its pitch variation, stretched vowels and masking by accompaniment. The ICASSP 2026 Cadenza Challenge provides a benchmark: song excerpts from various genres, simulated with no, mild and moderate hearing loss, labelled by listeners with the proportion understood, and entrants must predict this score.

The authors want one framework for two cases: with reference lyrics (which the paper calls invasive, usually termed intrusive) the ASR error rate can serve as a cue; without lyrics (non-invasive) the model should still predict.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文的相關研究只有一句話提到 STOI 與 PESQ，沒有點名任何學習式可懂度預測器。實際上最接近的是語音端的 Clarity Prediction Challenge（為聽損者預測助聽器輸出的可懂度）系列：第二屆（CPC2，2023）有好幾個表現好的非侵入式系統直接利用 Whisper 等 ASR 模型的中間表示或輸出，這篇「Whisper 提供主要訊號、其他編碼器補充」的做法與它們同一路線。逐耳分開打分、再用可學權重合併左右耳，也與 MBI-Net（Zezario 等，2022）為助聽器設計的雙耳分支結構非常相似。

官方基線本身就是兩種最簡單的做法：Whisper 基線用 ASR 轉寫與參考歌詞的 WER 預測可懂度，STOI 基線則用經典的短時客觀可懂度。這篇的差別在於加入音樂表示模型 MuQ（Zhu 等，2025）作為全域音樂脈絡，並用 T5 嵌入歌詞或轉寫文字，與音訊做交叉注意力。

The paper's related work mentions only STOI and PESQ in one sentence and names no learned intelligibility predictor. The closest line is in fact the speech-side Clarity Prediction Challenge series, which predicts the intelligibility of hearing-aid output for listeners with hearing loss: several strong non-intrusive systems in the second round (CPC2, 2023) used intermediate representations or outputs of ASR models such as Whisper, and this paper's approach of Whisper as the main signal plus other encoders follows the same path. Scoring each ear separately and combining left and right with learnable weights is also very similar to the binaural branch structure of MBI-Net (Zezario et al., 2022), designed for hearing aids.

The official baselines are themselves the two simplest approaches: the Whisper baseline predicts intelligibility from the WER between an ASR transcript and the reference lyrics, and the STOI baseline uses classic short-time objective intelligibility. This paper differs by adding the music representation model MuQ (Zhu et al., 2025) for global musical context and by embedding lyrics or transcripts with T5 for cross-attention with the audio.

方法Method

編碼器。 左右耳分開抽特徵。WavLM 取最後一層的逐幀特徵，與 T5-base 的文字嵌入做雙向交叉注意力，再經 MLP 輸出該耳分數。MuQ 取第 1–3 層與第 10–12 層串接，經注意力池化得到全域嵌入，與 T5-large 嵌入做同樣的交叉注意力與打分。兩種 T5 只是為了讓維度對上 WavLM 與 MuQ。

有參考歌詞。 Whisper small、medium、large 各轉寫一次，取三者 WER 的平均。每個編碼器先把左右耳分數各自與該耳 WER 串接、經線性層，再用可學權重 β 加權合併；最後 WavLM 與 MuQ 兩個分數再經一個線性層得到最終分數。

無參考歌詞。 改用 Whisper Large 轉寫「未處理」的音訊，轉寫結果經 T5 嵌入後取代參考歌詞，並拿掉 WER 模組；每耳把 WavLM 與 MuQ 分數串接、經線性層，再用可學權重 α 合併。訓練用 SGD（動量 0.9、學習率 10⁻⁴），單張 A800。

Encoders. Features are extracted separately for left and right ears. WavLM's last-layer frame features undergo bidirectional cross-attention with T5-base text embeddings, then an MLP outputs that ear's score. MuQ concatenates layers 1–3 and 10–12, applies attention pooling to obtain a global embedding, and goes through the same cross-attention and scoring with T5-large embeddings. The two T5 sizes only serve to match the dimensions of WavLM and MuQ.

With reference lyrics. Whisper small, medium and large each transcribe once and their WERs are averaged. For each encoder, each ear's score is concatenated with that ear's WER and passed through a linear layer, then the two ears are combined with learnable weights β; finally the WavLM and MuQ scores pass through one more linear layer to give the final score.

Without reference lyrics. Whisper Large transcribes the unprocessed audio, the transcript embedded by T5 replaces the reference lyrics, and the WER module is dropped; for each ear the WavLM and MuQ scores are concatenated and passed through a linear layer, then combined with learnable weights α. Training uses SGD (momentum 0.9, learning rate 10⁻⁴) on a single A800.

限制Limitations

兩頁的篇幅只夠放一張結果表：沒有重複種子或信賴區間、沒有和其他參賽隊伍比較，也沒有依聽損程度或曲風拆開的結果，所以看不出模型在哪類歌曲上有效。消融只做在有參考歌詞的版本上。

所謂「無參考」版本其實仍用到未經聽損處理的原始音訊來產生轉寫，也就是有一個乾淨的參考訊號，只是不需要人工歌詞；這和不需任何參考的真正非侵入式評估不同，論文沒有區分這一點。另外，無參考版本的評測集 RMSE（29.68）仍比有參考的 Whisper 基線（29.08）差，所以它的進步是相對 STOI 而言。最後，摘要說「顯著優於基線」，但正文沒有任何統計檢定。

Two pages leave room for one results table: no repeated seeds or confidence intervals, no comparison with other challenge entries, and no breakdown by hearing-loss level or genre, so one cannot see on which songs the model works. The ablation covers only the version with reference lyrics.

The so-called non-invasive version still uses the original audio without hearing-loss processing to produce its transcript, i.e. it has a clean reference signal and only dispenses with human-written lyrics; this differs from truly non-intrusive assessment with no reference at all, and the paper does not draw the distinction. Moreover, the non-invasive version's evaluation RMSE (29.68) is still worse than the Whisper baseline with reference lyrics (29.08), so its gain is relative to STOI. Finally, the abstract says it significantly outperforms the baselines, but the text contains no statistical test.

討論Discussion

主要結果。 有參考歌詞的 T013a 在驗證集 RMSE 26.61、相關 0.69，評測集 26.54、0.67；Whisper 基線是 29.32／0.59 與 29.08／0.58。摘要的「9.2%」是驗證集的 RMSE 改善，評測集上是約 8.7%。無參考的 T013b 在評測集 RMSE 29.68、相關 0.57，STOI 基線是 34.89、0.21。

消融。 評測集上拿掉 MuQ 是 27.14、拿掉 WavLM 是 27.76、拿掉 Whisper 是 30.68。前兩者只差 0.6 與 1.2 RMSE，拿掉 Whisper 差 4.1；而且「沒有 Whisper」的版本比單用 WER 的官方基線還差 1.6。這說明在這份資料上，聲學表示模型本身還無法取代 ASR 錯誤率；它們的價值是在 WER 之上提供補充。

對 MIR 來說，這篇提醒的是任務定義：歌詞可懂度目前最好的代理指標仍是「ASR 能不能聽懂」，所以 ASR 在歌聲上的偏誤（例如對某些曲風或語言特別差）會直接進入可懂度預測。若要讓這類模型用在助聽器或混音建議上，需要按聽損程度與曲風拆開的誤差分析，這是後續版本最該補的。

Main results. T013a, with reference lyrics, reaches RMSE 26.61 and correlation 0.69 on validation and 26.54 and 0.67 on evaluation; the Whisper baseline scores 29.32/0.59 and 29.08/0.58. The abstract's 9.2% is the RMSE improvement on validation; on evaluation it is about 8.7%. T013b, without reference lyrics, reaches evaluation RMSE 29.68 and correlation 0.57, against 34.89 and 0.21 for the STOI baseline.

Ablation. On evaluation, removing MuQ gives 27.14, removing WavLM 27.76 and removing Whisper 30.68. The first two cost only 0.6 and 1.2 RMSE, removing Whisper costs 4.1, and the no-Whisper version is 1.6 worse than the official WER-only baseline. On this data, then, acoustic representation models cannot yet replace the ASR error rate; their value is as a complement on top of WER.

For MIR, the paper is a reminder about task definition: the best current proxy for lyric intelligibility is still whether an ASR system understands the lyrics, so ASR biases on singing (for example, poor performance on certain genres or languages) flow directly into intelligibility predictions. To use such models for hearing aids or mixing advice, error analysis broken down by hearing-loss level and genre is needed, and that is what a later version should add most.
