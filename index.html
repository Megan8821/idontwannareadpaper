Automatic Lyric Transcription for Greek Songs: Scaling and Task Composition Effects in Whisper Adaptation

希臘歌曲自動歌詞轉譯：Whisper 微調中的規模與任務組成效應

Maria Frangiadaki, Dimitrios Damianos, Kosmas Kritsis, Vassilis Katsouros
arXiv:2609.11302 · cs.CL, cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文針對「希臘語歌詞自動轉譯」做了系統性研究：以三種規模的 Whisper（Small/Medium/Large-v3）分別測試純轉譯、轉譯與英譯多任務（2:1、4:1），以及先在希臘一般語音上微調再轉入歌唱的兩階段適應，並自建了 17,458 段、19.65 小時的 GAD-ALT 語料。結果顯示 Whisper Large-v3 零樣本 WER 為 53.6%，兩階段微調後降到 27.2%；多任務學習只在 Whisper Small 上有正則化效果（33.6% 優於純轉譯的 36.7%），到了 Large-v3 反而是純轉譯與兩階段表現最好。作者也做了一個容易被忽略的消融：拿掉人聲分離、直接用未分離的混音訓練與測試，WER 從 27.2% 回升到 33.4%，顯示這套結果高度依賴外部人聲分離工具的品質。Why this one: This paper runs the first systematic study of Whisper adaptation for Greek automatic lyric transcription (ALT), testing three model scales (Small/Medium/Large-v3) against transcription-only training, multitask training with English translation (2:1 and 4:1 ratios), and a two-stage speech-to-singing adaptation, on a newly curated 17,458-segment (19.65-hour) GAD-ALT corpus. Whisper Large-v3's zero-shot WER of 53.6% drops to 27.2% after two-stage fine-tuning; multitask learning only helps as a regularizer for Whisper Small (33.6% vs. 36.7% for transcription-only), while Large-v3 does best with transcription-only or two-stage training instead. A frequently-skipped ablation shows the result depends heavily on an external vocal-separation step: removing it and training/testing on raw mixed audio pushes WER back up from 27.2% to 33.4%.

動機Motivation

自動歌詞轉譯（ALT）比一般語音辨識困難，因為要面對旋律變化、節奏彈性與伴奏的干擾；而希臘語目前完全沒有公開的 ALT 基準，儘管希臘有豐富的歌唱傳統。這篇論文把 Whisper 這個多語言預訓練模型，拿來系統性測試在「規模」「任務組成」「適應策略」三個維度上，對希臘歌唱轉譯效果的影響，同時自己建立一份對齊過的希臘歌唱語料庫（GAD-ALT）作為訓練與評估的基礎。

Automatic lyric transcription (ALT) is harder than ordinary speech recognition because it must cope with melodic variability, rhythmic freedom, and instrumental interference, and Greek currently has no public ALT benchmark despite a rich singing tradition. The paper systematically tests how a multilingual pretrained model, Whisper, responds along three axes -- model scale, task composition, and adaptation strategy -- for Greek singing transcription, while curating an aligned Greek singing corpus (GAD-ALT) to serve as the training and evaluation basis.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

ALT 領域早期以 HMM 搭配 GMM 或 DNN 為主流做法，後續出現 DALI、DAMP-Sing 這類基準資料集，把評測標準化；近年則轉向以 CTC 或注意力機制的 encoder-decoder（AED）模型為主。多語言自監督模型如 wav2vec 2.0、XLS-R，以及 OpenAI 的 Whisper，是目前最常被拿來當基礎模型微調的選項——但論文也指出 Whisper 在乾淨人聲上表現不錯，一遇到有伴奏的複音音訊，零樣本準確率就明顯下滑。

希臘語音辨識本身已有 M2DS2、MSDA 這類用自監督預訓練加上師生偽標籤訓練的框架，但論文明確指出「希臘語音辨識與歌唱聲音分析的交集，在文獻裡幾乎是空白」。換句話說，這篇論文本身並沒有提出新的模型架構——它做的事情是把「多任務學習」「兩階段適應」這兩個在一般 ASR 領域已經行之有年的技巧，系統性地套用到一個全新的組合（希臘語加歌唱）上，最大貢獻是產出第一份希臘 ALT 基準與語料庫，而不是方法本身的創新。

Early ALT work relied on HMMs combined with GMMs or DNNs, later standardized by benchmark datasets such as DALI and DAMP-Sing, and more recently shifted toward CTC- or attention-based encoder-decoder (AED) models. Multilingual self-supervised models like wav2vec 2.0 and XLS-R, plus OpenAI's Whisper, are now the most common base models for fine-tuning -- though the paper notes Whisper's zero-shot accuracy, strong on clean vocals, degrades sharply once accompaniment is present.

Greek ASR already has self-supervised frameworks such as M2DS2 and MSDA that use teacher-student pseudo-labeling, but the paper states plainly that "the intersection of Greek ASR and singing voice analysis is virtually non-existent in the literature." In other words, the paper introduces no new model architecture: it systematically applies two techniques long established in general ASR -- multitask learning and two-stage domain adaptation -- to a genuinely new combination (Greek plus singing), with its main contribution being the first Greek ALT benchmark and corpus rather than methodological novelty.

方法Method

作者以 Hybrid Transformer Demucs 分離人聲，再用 CTC 強制對齊（結合對齊比例、CTC log 機率、時長懲罰三種訊號做信心分數），並用 GPT-4o-mini 產生逐段英譯，建出 17,458 段、共 19.65 小時的 GAD-ALT 語料（訓練 78.8%、驗證 10.8%、測試 10.4%，平均每段 4 秒）。在此之上測試三種 Whisper 規模（Small 244M、Medium 769M、Large-v3 1.55B）與四種訓練方式：純轉譯、2:1 與 4:1 的轉譯/翻譯多任務、以及先在 Mozilla Common Voice 希臘語一般語音（37.5 小時，凍結編碼器）微調、再解凍在歌唱資料上訓練的兩階段適應。

零樣本 WER 隨規模明顯下降（Small 92.3%、Medium 65.1%、Large-v3 53.6%），但論文強調「光靠加大規模，填不平語音到歌唱的領域落差」。微調後最佳結果來自 Large-v3 的兩階段適應，WER 降到 27.2%；多任務學習只在 Small 上是有效的正則化（2:1 比例達 33.6%，優於純轉譯的 36.7%），但 Medium 與 Large-v3 都是純轉譯或兩階段表現最好（分別為 30.3%、28.4%，兩階段後降到 30.1%、27.2%）。

The authors use Hybrid Transformer Demucs for vocal separation, CTC forced alignment with a confidence score combining alignment ratio, CTC log-probability, and duration penalty, and GPT-4o-mini for segment-level English translation, producing a 17,458-segment, 19.65-hour GAD-ALT corpus (78.8% train / 10.8% validation / 10.4% test, ~4 seconds per segment). On top of this they test three Whisper scales (Small 244M, Medium 769M, Large-v3 1.55B) against four training regimes: transcription-only; 2:1 and 4:1 transcribe-translate multitask; and a two-stage adaptation that first fine-tunes on Mozilla Common Voice Greek speech (37.5 hours, frozen encoder) before unfreezing on singing data.

Zero-shot WER falls sharply with scale (92.3% for Small, 65.1% for Medium, 53.6% for Large-v3), but the paper stresses that "scale alone cannot bridge the speech-to-singing domain gap." The best fine-tuned result is Large-v3's two-stage adaptation at 27.2% WER; multitask learning is a useful regularizer only for Small (33.6% at a 2:1 ratio, versus 36.7% for transcription-only), while both Medium and Large-v3 do best with transcription-only or two-stage training (30.3% and 28.4% respectively, improving to 30.1% and 27.2% with two-stage).

限制Limitations

論文自己點出兩個限制。第一，兩階段適應的第一階段用的是 Common Voice 這種「乾淨、朗讀式」的一般語音，可能反而讓模型學到跟歌唱聲學特性有落差的習慣，作者建議未來改用更接近自然口語（如對話或 podcast）的語料。第二，也是這篇 review 認為更值得注意的一點：論文做了把人聲分離拿掉的消融——直接在原始混音（含伴奏）上訓練與測試，WER 從最佳的 27.2% 回升到 33.4%，顯示這整套結果高度依賴一個外部、非本文貢獻的人聲分離模型（Demucs）；同時論文也試過用 SNR 控制的重混、加混響等資料增強手法，結果全部是「一致地讓表現變差」，作者只報告了現象，沒有進一步分析原因。

The paper names two limitations itself. First, stage-one adaptation uses Common Voice, a clean, read-speech corpus, which may reinforce speech habits that remain acoustically distant from singing; the authors suggest future work should use more naturalistic corpora (conversational or podcast-style speech). Second -- and the more notable point for this review -- an ablation removing vocal separation, training and testing directly on the raw mixed audio, pushes WER from the best 27.2% back up to 33.4%, showing the whole pipeline leans heavily on an external vocal-separation model (Demucs) that is not itself a contribution of this paper. The authors also tried several data-augmentation tricks (SNR-controlled remixing, added reverb) and report that all of them "consistently degraded performance," without further analysis of why.

討論Discussion

作者對 200 個錯誤樣本做了人工分類，發現最大宗是「語意替換」（25.5%，聲音相近但意思不同的詞）與「邊界飄移」（24.0%，拖腔造成的斷詞錯誤），其次是「幻覺/不合規則音節」（19.5%）、「拼寫歧義」（17.0%，希臘語同音異形字）、「虛詞增刪」（12.0%）與「型態變化飄移」（2.0%）。更關鍵的觀察是：模型規模變大，主要是降低錯誤的嚴重程度，而不是改變錯誤類型的分布——這暗示歌唱轉譯的困難，有一部分是希臘語聲學與構詞本身的特性造成，不是單純堆模型容量就能解決的問題。

整體而言，這篇論文的核心貢獻是「第一個希臘語 ALT 基準與語料庫」，方法本身是既有技巧（多任務、兩階段微調）的組合套用，誠實地說沒有方法論創新，但產出的資料與系統性比較（尤其是人聲分離消融）對後續想做低資源語言歌唱轉譯的人相當有參考價值。

Manual classification of 200 errors from the best model finds the largest categories are semantic substitution (25.5%, phonetically similar but semantically different words) and boundary drift (24.0%, melisma-induced word-segmentation errors), followed by hallucinated/phonotactically implausible content (19.5%), orthographic ambiguity (17.0%, Greek homophones with multiple spellings), function-word insertion/deletion (12.0%), and morphological drift (2.0%). The more important observation is that increasing model scale mainly reduces error severity rather than shifting the error-type distribution -- suggesting some of the difficulty is intrinsic to Greek singing's acoustics and morphology rather than something more model capacity alone can fix.

Overall, the paper's core contribution is the first Greek ALT benchmark and corpus; the method itself is a combination of established techniques (multitask learning, two-stage fine-tuning) with, honestly, no methodological novelty, but the resulting data and systematic comparison -- especially the vocal-separation ablation -- are genuinely useful for anyone tackling low-resource singing transcription next.

A General Approach to Enharmonicism

一個廣義的等音理論

Caleb Scott Alons
arXiv:2609.10603 · math.HO, cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文是純數學/音樂理論的形式化工作，不涉及音訊或資料集：作者把 Hook（2007）原本只處理「7 個音級」的等音理論，推廣到任意數量的音級，並提出兩個判準——「鏡射對稱性」與「最大均勻性」（後者借用 Clough 與 Douthett 既有的音階理論概念）——來說明為什麼標準十二平均律等音系統會被普遍採用。論文完全沒有實驗、資料或評測指標，是它自己最大的限制：兩個判準本身也只是作者提出並用範例佐證，沒有語料庫分析或聆聽實驗去驗證它們是否真的對應音樂實務。Why this one: This is a pure mathematics/music-theory formalization paper with no audio or dataset involved: the author generalizes Hook's (2007) enharmonic theory, originally limited to 7 diatonic tones, to arbitrary cardinality, and proposes two criteria -- a "reflection property" and "maximal evenness" (the latter borrowed from Clough and Douthett's existing scale theory) -- to explain why the standard 12-tone enharmonic system is favored. The paper's biggest limitation is built into its method: it runs no experiments, no dataset, and no evaluation metric, so the two criteria are asserted and illustrated by worked examples rather than validated against any corpus analysis or listening study of actual musical practice.

動機Motivation

音樂裡「C# 等於 Db」這種等音關係，傳統上是從調律與聲學角度解釋。這篇論文反其道而行，主張要區分「語法」（抽象的形式結構）與「聲學」（實際的物理聲音特性），嘗試完全不談調律，純粹用數學結構去解釋為什麼標準十二音等音系統會是「特別」的，以及是否存在其他同樣合理但非標準的等音系統。

The equivalence "C# equals Db" is traditionally explained through tuning and acoustics. This paper goes the other way, arguing for a distinction between "syntax" (abstract formal structure) and "acoustics" (physical sound properties), and tries to explain, without invoking tuning at all, why the standard 12-tone enharmonic system is special using pure mathematical structure alone -- and whether other, non-standard enharmonic systems could be equally well justified.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文直接建立在 Hook（2007）《Enharmonic Systems: A Theory of Key Signatures, Enharmonic Equivalence and Diatonicism》之上，把 Hook 原本固定「7 個音級」的框架，推廣成任意數量音級 n 都能套用的一般理論。另一個借用的既有概念是 Clough 與 Douthett 提出的「最大均勻性」（音級在半音圈上盡量平均分布）——這原本是音階理論裡常見的判準，論文把它挪來當作判斷「一個等音系統是否合乎音樂實務」的兩個標準之一。

論文也引用真實存在過的非標準等音系統當佐證，包括 Vicentino 的 31 音大鍵琴（Archicembalo）、Costeley 的十九音作品、Salinas 的中庸全音律，以及現代作曲家 Partch 與 Johnston 的微分音探索。換句話說，這篇論文沒有提出全新的音樂理論概念——它做的是把 Hook 既有的等音理論做數學上的推廣，再借用另一個既有的音階判準（最大均勻性），組合起來解釋標準系統的特殊性，屬於既有理論的形式化整合，而非原創的新理論。

The paper builds directly on Hook's (2007) "Enharmonic Systems: A Theory of Key Signatures, Enharmonic Equivalence and Diatonicism," generalizing Hook's framework -- originally fixed at 7 diatonic tones -- into a theory that applies to any cardinality n. The other borrowed concept is Clough and Douthett's "maximal evenness" (tones spread as evenly as possible around the chromatic circle), an existing scale-theory criterion the paper repurposes as one of two tests for whether an enharmonic system is "musically practical."

The paper also cites real historical non-standard enharmonic systems as evidence, including Vicentino's 31-tone Archicembalo, Costeley's 19-tone compositions, Salinas's meantone tunings, and the microtonal explorations of modern composers Partch and Johnston. In other words, the paper introduces no genuinely new musical-theoretic concept: it mathematically generalizes Hook's existing enharmonic theory and combines it with another existing scale-theory criterion (maximal evenness) to explain why the standard system is special -- a formal synthesis of established theories rather than an original one.

方法Method

論文分三層建構：「音級理論」定義有限音級集合 D 與其移調操作，以及由某個生成音程 p 決定的「音級排序」；「半音理論」把每個音級配上修飾符（♭、♮、♯等，構成無限集合）組成半音集合 D*，並定義由半音空間生成的線性排序（廣義的「五度圈排序」）；「等音理論」則透過一個把音級指標映射到累加和的指定函數 A_e，定義等音等價關係（例如讓 C# 與 Db 落在同一個等價類裡）。在此之上，論文提出兩個判準：「鏡射對稱性」（每個旋轉類都有鏡射對應）與「最大均勻性」。

論文用具體例子驗證框架：標準十二音系統由 E=(2,2,1,2,2,2,1) 生成，同時滿足鏡射對稱與最大均勻性；一個十九音系統（E'=(3,3,2,3,3,3,2)，引用自 Mandelbaum 1961）只在完全音程（純四、純五度）上保有鏡射對稱，整體上則沒有；論文另外構造一個十一音的反例，顯示隨便挑一組 E 元組，產生的系統可能兩個判準都不滿足。

The paper builds three nested layers: "diatonic theory" defines a finite diatonic set D with a transposition operation and "prime diatonic orderings" generated by an interval p; "chromatic theory" pairs each diatone with a modifier (flat, natural, sharp, etc., forming an infinite set) into a chromatic set D*, with a linear ordering generated from that chromatic space (a generalized "line of fifths"); "enharmonic theory" then defines an enharmonic equivalence relation via an assignment function A_e that maps diatonic indices to cumulative sums (so that, e.g., C# and Db fall into the same equivalence class). On top of this the paper proposes two criteria: the "reflection property" (every rotation class has a mirror counterpart) and "maximal evenness."

The framework is verified with worked examples: the standard 12-tone system, generated by E=(2,2,1,2,2,2,1), satisfies both the reflection property and maximal evenness; a 19-tone system (E'=(3,3,2,3,3,3,2), cited from Mandelbaum 1961) keeps the reflection property only for perfect fourths and fifths, not globally; and the paper constructs an 11-tone counterexample showing that an arbitrarily chosen E-tuple can fail both criteria at once.

限制Limitations

這篇論文最大的限制，是它本身的性質：從頭到尾沒有音訊、沒有資料集、沒有任何評測指標或使用者研究——「鏡射對稱性」與「最大均勻性」這兩個判準，完全是作者主張並用手算範例佐證，並沒有拿真實樂譜語料去驗證這兩個判準是否真的對應作曲家或聽者實際偏好的系統。論文也明確承認框架不處理「同一個音該記成 C# 還是 Db」這種調性記譜慣例的問題，而且預設音級數量是有限的，「完美音程」的定義也只在特定條件下成立。

The paper's biggest limitation is structural: there is no audio, no dataset, no evaluation metric, and no user study anywhere in it -- the "reflection property" and "maximal evenness" criteria are simply asserted by the author and illustrated with hand-worked examples, with no corpus study of real scores checking whether these criteria actually track what composers or listeners prefer in practice. The paper also explicitly does not address which specific spelling (C# vs. Db) to use in a given key-signature context, assumes a finite diatonic cardinality throughout, and its definition of "perfect interval" only holds under specific conditions.

討論Discussion

論文的結論是：標準十二音系統之所以特別，可以完全不靠聲學或調律，只靠「鏡射對稱」加「最大均勻」這兩個抽象數學判準來說明，把過去「從聲學推出十二音系統」與「純結構上偏好十二音系統」這兩種歷史上分開的說法統一了起來。作者也主張這套廣義記法能讓作曲家有系統地在非標準等音系統裡工作，呼應 Vicentino、Costeley、Partch 等人的微分音實踐。但這個「統一」與「有用」的說法本身也還停留在理論層次——論文沒有回頭去檢驗這套判準能不能真的解釋歷史上出現過的哪些非標準系統被採用、哪些沒有，這是它留給後續研究的問題。

The paper concludes that the standard 12-tone system's special status can be explained entirely without acoustics or tuning, using just two abstract mathematical criteria -- reflection property and maximal evenness -- unifying two historically separate accounts (deriving the 12-tone system from acoustics, versus preferring it on purely structural grounds). The author also argues the generalized notation lets composers work systematically in non-standard enharmonic systems, echoing the microtonal practice of Vicentino, Costeley, and Partch. But this claim of "unification" and "usefulness" itself remains at the theoretical level -- the paper never checks whether its criteria actually explain which historical non-standard systems were adopted and which were not, leaving that question open for future work.

MVWeaver: A Hierarchical Music Video Generation Agent with a Learned Song-to-Visual Bridge

MVWeaver：具備可學習「歌曲到視覺橋接」的階層式音樂錄影帶生成代理

Sifei Li, Minyan Luo, Xu Li, Guodong Qi, Xincan Wang, Hanwen Wang, Chen Zhang, Pengfei Wan, Oliver Deussen, Weiming Dong

為什麼挑這篇：MVWeaver 是一個把「歌曲理解」轉成「音樂錄影帶（MV）分鏡計畫」的階層式生成代理，核心賣點是額外訓練一個「歌曲到視覺橋接」模型（用 LoRA 微調 Qwen 3.6-27B，基於 1,861 組真實歌曲與 MV 配對訓練）。在 20 首測試歌曲上，對比同樣走「直接生成 MV」路線的 AutoMV，四項 1–5 分制指標全面勝出（概念一致性 4.30 對 3.95、鏡頭銜接 4.10 對 3.10）；但把「橋接」模組拿掉的消融實驗顯示，橋接本身帶來的差距其實不大（鏡頭銜接 4.10 對 3.90、概念一致性 4.30 對 4.25），意味著論文對 AutoMV 的優勢，有很大一部分來自整條階層式管線本身，而不只是橋接模型。Why this one: MVWeaver is a hierarchical generation agent that turns "song understanding" into a "music video (MV) shot plan," with its headline component being a separately trained "song-to-visual bridge" (a LoRA fine-tune of Qwen 3.6-27B on 1,861 real song-MV pairs). Across 20 test songs it beats the comparable direct-MV-generation baseline AutoMV on all four 1-5 scored metrics (e.g. conceptual coherence 4.30 vs. 3.95, shot-to-shot continuity 4.10 vs. 3.10); but an ablation that removes only the bridge shows a much smaller gap (continuity 4.10 vs. 3.90, coherence 4.30 vs. 4.25), suggesting a large share of the advantage over AutoMV comes from the hierarchical pipeline itself rather than the bridge model specifically.

動機Motivation

音樂錄影帶不只是把歌詞畫面化，專業 MV 通常會從歌曲裡提取視覺聯想，再發展成一套連貫的視覺敘事。但現有自動 MV 生成系統常常做不到「長篇幅的連貫性」與「真正扎根於歌曲意義的視覺發展」——容易流於通用的畫面套路，或角色/場景設定前後不一致。這篇論文要解決的正是這兩個問題：如何讓生成的 MV 既緊扣歌曲內容、又能在數分鐘長度裡保持視覺一致性。

Music videos do more than illustrate lyrics -- professional MVs typically extract visual associations from a song and develop them into a coherent visual narrative. Existing automatic MV generation systems, however, often fail at long-form coherence and at grounding visual development genuinely in the song's meaning, tending to fall back on generic visual situations or inconsistent character/location setups. This paper targets exactly those two problems: keeping a generated MV tightly tied to the song while holding visual consistency across several minutes of runtime.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把既有系統分成三類。第一類是 AniME、AniMaker、VISTA、STAGE 這種階層式長片段生成代理，擅長處理多鏡頭工作流程與連貫性，但驅動來源是故事或文字指令，不是歌曲。第二類是 GLANCE、BEAT 這種「依音樂結構剪輯既有畫面」的系統——它們對齊、編排的是已經存在的素材，不會生成一個全新的視覺世界。第三類則是 YingVideo-MV、AutoMV、AllocMV 這種直接生成 MV 的系統，但主要靠通用大型語言模型做推理，缺乏從真實 MV 裡萃取出來的「歌曲對應視覺」領域知識。

MVWeaver 跟 AutoMV 這類最接近的系統相比，差別不在於整體階層式規劃架構本身（AniME、STAGE 等系統早就有類似的多層次規劃），而在於額外訓練了一個專門的「橋接」模型，把 1,861 組真實歌曲與 MV 配對、附上人工/教師標註的「五階段聯想鏈」（從歌曲證據到視覺概念的推理過程）拿來微調，取代 AutoMV 那種讓通用 LLM 直接零樣本推理的做法。用 ML 的角度看，這其實就是幫既有的階層式規劃架構，加裝一個用 LoRA 微調過、針對這個特定結構化預測任務的「翻譯層」，而不是提出一種全新的生成架構典範。

The paper groups prior systems into three types. The first is agentic long-form video systems like AniME, AniMaker, VISTA, and STAGE, which handle multi-shot workflows and continuity well but are driven by stories or text prompts rather than songs. The second is music-guided editing systems like GLANCE and BEAT, which align and arrange existing footage to musical structure rather than generating a new visual world. The third is direct MV generation systems -- YingVideo-MV, AutoMV, AllocMV -- which rely mainly on general-purpose LLM reasoning and lack domain knowledge distilled from real song-to-visual associations.

Compared to its closest relatives like AutoMV, MVWeaver's difference is not the overall hierarchical planning architecture itself -- systems like AniME and STAGE already use similar multi-level planning -- but the addition of a dedicated "bridge" model fine-tuned on 1,861 real song-MV pairs annotated with human/teacher-labeled "five-stage association chains" (a reasoning trace from song evidence to visual concept), replacing AutoMV's reliance on zero-shot general-LLM reasoning. In ML terms, this amounts to bolting a LoRA-fine-tuned "translation layer" for this specific structured-prediction task onto an existing hierarchical planning architecture, rather than proposing a genuinely new generation paradigm.

方法Method

歌曲分析分兩層：歌曲整體層由 Gemini 3.1 Pro 產生「八維度」的音樂與語意側寫；細粒度層則做人聲分離（HT-Demucs）、帶時間戳的歌詞轉錄與對齊、用 SongFormer 做段落切分、以及用 Librosa 做能量分析，另外用 madmom 估拍點/重拍、Librosa 抓 onset，合併成節奏候選點來決定鏡頭時長。規劃分四層：先形成 MV 整體概念（MV-brief），再結合橋接模型的判斷展開成一連串「視覺發展」並定義可重複使用的角色/服裝/場景資產，接著展開成場景（含資產指派、轉場），最後拆成可直接生成的鏡頭。

橋接模型是用 LoRA（rank 8、α=16）微調 Qwen 3.6-27B，只訓練 58.36M 參數、跑 2 個 epoch，訓練資料是 1,841 組（另外 20 組留作測試，共 1,861 組）歌曲與 MV 配對。生成端則是 GPT Image 2 產生資產與參考幀、Kling v3 生成非演唱鏡頭動畫、Kling Avatar 依人聲片段生成演唱鏡頭。

Song analysis has two levels: a song-level "eight-dimensional" musical-and-semantic profile from Gemini 3.1 Pro, and fine-grained analysis combining vocal separation (HT-Demucs), timestamped lyric transcription and alignment, section segmentation via SongFormer, and energy analysis via Librosa, plus beat/downbeat estimation from madmom merged with Librosa onset detection into rhythmic candidates for shot timing. Planning proceeds through four levels: an overall MV-brief, then, combined with the bridge model's local decisions, a sequence of "visual developments" defining reusable character/costume/location assets, then scenes (with asset assignment and transitions), and finally individual renderable shots.

The bridge model is a LoRA fine-tune (rank 8, alpha 16) of Qwen 3.6-27B, training only 58.36M parameters for 2 epochs on 1,841 song-MV pairs (with 20 more held out for test, 1,861 total). Generation uses GPT Image 2 for assets and reference frames, Kling v3 to animate non-singing shots, and Kling Avatar to generate singing shots from vocal segments.

限制Limitations

論文表 1 在 20 首測試歌曲、四項 1–5 分制指標（ISA、GDE、CC、SSC）上，MVWeaver 對 AutoMV 全面勝出（如 SSC 鏡頭銜接 4.10 對 3.10、CC 概念一致性 4.30 對 3.95）；但把橋接模組拿掉的消融實驗，差距明顯縮小（SSC 4.10 對 3.90、CC 4.30 對 4.25，約僅 0.05–0.20 分之差）。這代表論文標題主打的「可學習橋接」，實際上只能解釋對 AutoMV 總優勢裡的一小部分，大部分差距其實來自整條階層式規劃管線與後端生成模型（GPT Image 2、Kling v3/Avatar）本身，論文並沒有把這兩個因素的貢獻拆開來講。

另外，整套系統高度依賴多個商用閉源模型（Gemini 3.1 Pro、GPT Image 2、Kling 系列），重現與計算成本的問題，論文完全沒有討論。作者自己承認的限制，只提到未來要處理跨鏡頭運鏡的連續性。

In Table 1, across 20 test songs and four 1-5 scored metrics (ISA, GDE, CC, SSC), MVWeaver beats AutoMV across the board (e.g., SSC continuity 4.10 vs. 3.10, CC coherence 4.30 vs. 3.95); but the ablation that removes only the bridge shrinks that gap sharply (SSC 4.10 vs. 3.90, CC 4.30 vs. 4.25 -- roughly a 0.05-0.20 point difference). That means the paper's headline "learned bridge" explains only a small slice of the overall advantage over AutoMV, with most of the gap coming from the hierarchical planning pipeline and the backend generation models (GPT Image 2, Kling v3/Avatar) themselves -- a split the paper never separates out explicitly.

The whole system also leans heavily on several closed, commercial models (Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT Image 2, the Kling family), and reproducibility and compute cost go undiscussed. The only limitation the authors acknowledge themselves is future work on cross-shot camera-motion continuity.

討論Discussion

除了表 1 的自動指標，論文另外做了 34 人參與、10 組 MV 對比、共 30 題的盲測偏好研究：在「歌曲詮釋」偏好上，MVWeaver 得票 64.71%、AutoMV 為 37.65%；「長程連貫性」偏好 70.00% 對 23.53%；整體偏好 67.06% 對 30.59%（注意這兩邊加總並非剛好 100%，顯示題目設計裡可能還有「不分軒輊」之類的第三選項）。作者將 AutoMV 的問題歸因於「容易落入通用畫面套路」與「缺乏可重複使用的場景資產，導致片段之間場景漂移」，而 MVWeaver 靠可重複使用的資產與蒙太奇轉場維持長篇連貫性。

整體而言，這篇論文示範了「幫既有階層式 MV 生成管線，加裝一個從真實 MV 蒸餾出的領域知識橋接模型」確實能提升表現，但消融結果也誠實地顯示，橋接模型不是效果的主要來源，論文標題與敘事對這個模組的著重程度，略高於它實際貢獻的比重。

Beyond the Table 1 automatic metrics, the paper ran a blind preference study with 34 participants over 10 MV pairs (30 questions total): MVWeaver was preferred 64.71% to AutoMV's 37.65% on song interpretation, 70.00% to 23.53% on long-range coherence, and 67.06% to 30.59% overall (note the two sides don't sum to exactly 100%, suggesting the questions may have allowed a third "no preference" option). The authors attribute AutoMV's weaknesses to falling back on generic visual situations and lacking reusable location assets, causing scenes to drift across segments, while MVWeaver holds long-form coherence through reusable assets and planned montage transitions.

Overall, the paper demonstrates that bolting a domain-knowledge bridge distilled from real MVs onto an existing hierarchical MV pipeline does improve performance, but the ablation honestly shows the bridge is not the main source of that gain -- the paper's title and framing emphasize this module somewhat more than its actual share of the improvement warrants.
