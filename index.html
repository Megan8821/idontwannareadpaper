DiffSynth-Music: Audio-Conditioned KV-Cache Adapters for Controllable Music Generation

DiffSynth-Music：音訊條件式 KV 快取轉接器實現可控音樂生成

Zhongjie Duan, Shengchuan Gao, Hong Zhang, Yingda Chen
arXiv:2609.12774 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-11
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：DiffSynth-Music 在凍結的 ACE-Step-1.5-XL-SFT 音樂生成骨幹上加裝三個音訊條件模板，用層級 KV 注入支援節拍、人聲、伴奏、韻律、參考音色五種控制。單一控制的效果全面超越五個對照模型：節拍一致性 Beat-F1 從 0.3031 提升到 0.8496，人聲重建誤差 V-MSE 從 0.0158 降到 0.0006，伴奏誤差 A-MSE 從 0.0221 降到 0.0023，韻律音高命中率 Pitch50 從 0.0366 提升到 0.4671（超過最強對照 MiniMax-Music3 的 0.0402），而生成品質與文字對齊分數只有小幅下滑。Why this one: DiffSynth-Music adds three audio-conditioning template modules onto the frozen ACE-Step-1.5-XL-SFT music generator, using layer-wise KV injection to support beat, vocal, accompaniment, prosody, and reference-audio control. Single-control results beat all five baselines outright: Beat-F1 rises from 0.3031 to 0.8496, vocal reconstruction error (V-MSE) falls from 0.0158 to 0.0006, accompaniment error (A-MSE) falls from 0.0221 to 0.0023, and prosody pitch-hit rate (Pitch50) rises from 0.0366 to 0.4671 (beating the strongest baseline, MiniMax-Music3, at 0.0402), while general quality and text-alignment scores dip only slightly.

動機Motivation

文字提示與歌詞能指定音樂的風格、樂器編制與演唱內容，卻難以精確控制節奏、旋律走向與特定演奏細節。當使用者想要「照著某段既有音樂的節奏、人聲或伴奏走」時，純文字條件完全無能為力。

這篇論文要解決的問題是：如何在不重新訓練整個音樂生成骨幹的前提下，加入以音訊為條件的細粒度控制——包含節拍、人聲旋律、伴奏、韻律（音高與時長）與參考音色五種控制型態，並讓它們可以彼此組合使用，例如同時用伴奏與韻律條件生成「換人聲但保留編曲與旋律」的翻唱版本。

Text prompts and lyrics can specify a song's genre, instrumentation, and sung content, but they cannot pin down beat timing, melodic contour, or performance-level detail. When a user wants to follow an existing piece's rhythm, vocal line, or accompaniment, text conditioning alone offers no lever.

This paper asks how to add fine-grained, audio-derived control -- beats, vocal melody, accompaniment, prosody (pitch and timing), and a reference-audio style cue -- onto a pretrained music generator without retraining its backbone, and how to let these five control types be combined, e.g. accompaniment plus prosody together to produce a cover with a new singer but the original arrangement and melody.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

文字到音樂生成這條主線上已有 MusicGen、Stable-Audio-Open、ACE-Step 系列，以及處理長篇歌曲的 MiniMax-Music3、DiffRhythm-2、HeartMuLa、SongBloom、LeVo-2，這些系統把生成品質做得很好，但條件主要是文字與歌詞，缺乏對節奏、旋律等演奏細節的直接控制。

在「怎麼把額外控制訊號塞進一個已凍結的生成骨幹」這個問題上，圖像生成領域已有 ControlNet（零初始化卷積接可訓練分支）、T2I-Adapter（把控制輸入轉成特徵餵給凍結模型）、LoRA（低秩權重更新）與 IP-Adapter（用獨立交叉注意力路徑處理圖片條件）。DiffSynth-Music 最像 IP-Adapter——都是從條件輸入衍生額外的注意力上下文——但做法不同：IP-Adapter 用另一條交叉注意力路徑處理圖片，DiffSynth-Music 則把音訊條件轉成跟生成分支同維度的 key/value，直接與生成分支的 key/value 拼接在同一個聯合注意力裡，而非另開一條路徑。

在音樂控制這一支，Music-ControlNet 把節奏、力度等時變條件塞進頻譜圖擴散模型，JASCO 用 flow matching 結合文字與和弦、旋律、鼓點等符號或音訊條件。DiffSynth-Music 與它們的差異在於：用同一套共享 VAE 把五種控制訊號都轉成統一的音訊條件表示以便組合；韻律條件是用資料驅動方式重建（對人聲做包絡與基頻的正弦波再合成），而非像 JASCO 靠資訊瓶頸與時間模糊去除干擾線索。

The text-to-music line already includes MusicGen, Stable-Audio-Open, and the ACE-Step family, plus long-form systems such as MiniMax-Music3, DiffRhythm-2, HeartMuLa, SongBloom, and LeVo-2 -- strong on quality but conditioned mainly on text and lyrics, without direct control over performance-level details like beat timing or melodic contour.

On injecting extra control into an already-frozen generator, image generation already has ControlNet (a trainable branch via zero-initialized convolutions), T2I-Adapter (translating control inputs into features for a frozen model), LoRA (low-rank weight updates), and IP-Adapter (a separate cross-attention path for image conditioning). DiffSynth-Music is closest to IP-Adapter in spirit -- both derive extra attention context from a conditioning input -- but differs in mechanism: IP-Adapter routes conditions through a decoupled cross-attention path, while DiffSynth-Music projects audio conditions into keys and values at the same width as the generation branch and concatenates them directly into one joint attention operation.

Within music-specific control, Music-ControlNet injects time-varying rhythm and dynamics into spectrogram diffusion, and JASCO combines text with chord/melody/drum conditions via flow matching. DiffSynth-Music differs by routing all five control types through one shared VAE into a common representation for composability, and by reconstructing prosody through pitch- and envelope-based resynthesis of the vocal, rather than JASCO's information-bottleneck and temporal-blurring approach to suppressing unwanted cues.

方法Method

骨幹是凍結的 ACE-Step-1.5-XL-SFT 擴散變換器（DiT），另外訓練三個從骨幹初始化、共用 VAE 的「模板模組」：Control（處理節拍、人聲、伴奏）、Prosody（處理韻律）、Reference（處理參考音色）。每個模板模組把對應的控制音訊轉成跟生成分支同維度的 key 與 value，在每一層注意力裡跟生成分支自己的 key/value 拼接後再做 softmax 注意力，等於是把控制訊號寫進生成分支的「記憶」裡，而不是修改文字條件或動生成骨幹本身的權重。

一個關鍵的效率設計是：控制音訊本身不含噪聲，所以模板的時間步固定在「乾淨資料端點」（t=1），輸出的 key/value 快取在整個取樣過程中只算一次、重複使用，不必像生成分支一樣在 50 步取樣裡每步都重新算控制訊號，大幅降低了音訊條件化帶來的額外計算量。

訓練資料來自約 6 萬首音樂：節拍用 Essentia 的 Degara 節拍追蹤器取出時間點、合成成指數衰減脈衝；人聲與伴奏用 Demucs 分離；韻律是把人聲做整流與低通濾波取出包絡，再用 pYIN 估計基頻合成正弦波，藉此保留音高與時長但削弱歌詞與音色線索；參考音訊則取整首歌裡音量最大的 10 秒片段。訓練用 conditional flow matching，只更新模板參數，生成骨幹全程凍結。

The backbone is a frozen ACE-Step-1.5-XL-SFT diffusion transformer (DiT). Three template modules are initialized from the backbone and share its VAE: Control (beats, vocals, accompaniment), Prosody, and Reference. Each template converts its control audio into keys and values at the same width as the generation branch; at every attention layer these are concatenated with the generation branch's own keys/values before softmax attention, writing the control signal into the generation branch's attention memory rather than altering the text condition or the backbone's weights.

A key efficiency trick: since control audio is noise-free, each template's timestep is fixed at the clean-data endpoint (t=1), so its key/value cache is computed once and reused across all sampling steps, instead of being recomputed at every one of the 50 diffusion steps -- substantially cutting the overhead of audio conditioning.

Training pairs come from about 60k recordings. Beats are timestamps from Essentia's Degara beat tracker, rendered as decaying pulses; vocals and accompaniment come from Demucs separation; prosody resynthesizes the vocal's envelope (rectified, low-pass filtered) and pYIN-estimated pitch as a sine tone, keeping pitch and timing while suppressing lyrics and timbre; the reference is the loudest 10-second window of the track. Training uses conditional flow matching, updating only the template parameters while the generation backbone stays frozen.

限制Limitations

論文自己承認控制有得有失：加入五種控制能力後，部分 AudioBox-Aesthetics 美學分數與全部 MuLan-T 文字對齊分數都比未加控制的骨幹低，作者的說法是「大致相當」，但數字上其實是全面下滑，只是幅度不大（PQ 從 8.1016 降到 8.0555–8.2418 之間，MuLan-T 從 0.4024 降到 0.3452–0.3900）。

評估只做了「單一控制」情境，論文自己在結論裡承認還沒有用真人聽測評估「同時啟用多種控制」（例如伴奏+韻律同時開啟）時的音樂品質，只做了自動指標；節拍、人聲、伴奏、韻律訊號全部靠 Essentia、Demucs、pYIN 等既有工具自動抽取，這些工具本身會有誤差，論文也承認抽取錯誤會直接反映到訓練資料的條件訊號裡。

100 首測試樣本規模不大（50 首英文、50 首中文），且用來訓練與評估的私有中文資料集細節（規模、版權、如何取得授權）論文完全沒有交代，讀者無法判斷結果對其他語言、其他錄音品質的可推廣性。

The paper's own numbers show a broad, if modest, quality cost: adding the five control types lowers some AudioBox-Aesthetics scores and every MuLan-T text-alignment score relative to the uncontrolled backbone (PQ falls from 8.1016 to a 8.0555-8.2418 range; MuLan-T falls from 0.4024 to 0.3452-0.3900). The paper calls this "broadly comparable," but the direction is consistently downward, just by a small margin.

Evaluation only covers single-control settings; the conclusion itself admits joint conditioning (e.g. accompaniment plus prosody together) has not been assessed with human listening, only automatic metrics. Beats, vocals, accompaniment and prosody are all extracted with off-the-shelf tools (Essentia, Demucs, pYIN) whose own errors, the paper acknowledges, propagate directly into the training conditions.

The 100-sample test set is small (50 English, 50 Mandarin), and the paper gives no detail on the private Mandarin training corpus -- its size, licensing, or how consent was obtained -- leaving generalization to other languages or recording qualities untested.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最實用的貢獻其實是工程層面的：把「控制音訊沒有噪聲」這件事直接轉成一個快取機制，讓控制條件只算一次、整個取樣過程重複使用，這個設計思路可以直接套用到其他「凍結骨幹+外掛條件」的擴散生成任務，不限於音樂。

論文把「首創」定位在整合五種控制型態的統一介面，但仔細對照相關研究會發現，其實每一種控制型態單獨拿出來看都能在既有文獻裡找到對應（節奏控制像 Music-ControlNet、參考音色像 IP-Adapter 式的圖片提示、符號加音訊混合條件像 JASCO），真正的貢獻是把它們放進同一個共享 VAE 潛空間、用同一套 KV 注入機制統一起來，讓五種控制可以任意組合疊加，這點在論文的貢獻列點裡其實講得比較隱晦。

由於韻律訊號的重建方式（正弦波 carrier 加包絡）並不保證完全去除演唱者音色與可懂度，用它訓練出來的模型在「換聲但保留旋律」這類應用上，實際隱私或版權風險（是否仍能被辨識出原唱者）值得後續研究者用真人測試而非純自動指標去驗證。

The paper's most practically useful contribution is arguably engineering rather than architectural: turning the fact that control audio carries no noise into a caching trick, computing each control's contribution once and reusing it across the whole sampling trajectory. That idea generalizes directly to other frozen-backbone-plus-adapter diffusion tasks beyond music.

The paper frames its novelty as a unified interface for five control types, but each type individually maps onto prior work (rhythm control resembles Music-ControlNet, reference-style conditioning resembles IP-Adapter's image prompting, mixed symbolic/audio conditioning resembles JASCO). The real contribution -- folding all five into one shared VAE latent space with one KV-injection mechanism so they compose freely -- is understated in the paper's own list of contributions.

Because the prosody resynthesis (sine carrier plus envelope) does not guarantee full removal of singer timbre or intelligibility, applications like "swap the singer, keep the melody" carry a real question -- whether the original singer remains recognizable -- that deserves human evaluation rather than automatic metrics alone in follow-up work.

Real-Time Music Source Separation on a Low-Power Audio DSP

低功耗音訊 DSP 上的即時音樂音源分離

Jianan Li, Li Liu, Ken Malsky, Gabby Yi
arXiv:2609.12201 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文問一個很直接的問題：現有「即時」音樂音源分離系統真的能跑在對應的嵌入式音訊硬體上嗎？在一顆商用音訊 DSP（2MB 晶片內 SRAM、實測 2.07 GMAC/s）上檢驗五個已發表系統，沒有一個同時滿足記憶體與算力限制——記憶體刷掉 16–51M 參數的 TasNet/X-UMX 家族（超出 8.0–25.5 倍），算力刷掉只有 0.4M 參數的 RT-STT（需要 5.5 倍於裝置算力）。作者接著自己設計一個真正裝得上的模型，發現訓練方式（用連續而非分塊補零的卷積上下文）比架構本身更關鍵：分塊訓練的模型分塊評測拿 3.93dB，逐幀連續推論卻在 2 秒內崩潰成靜音；改用連續訓練後在 MUSDB18-HQ 拿到 4.70dB cSDR，在裝置上 11.6ms 的每幀時限內用掉 10.43ms，比裝不上的系統低 0.5–0.7dB。Why this one: This paper asks a blunt question: do published "real-time" music source separators actually run on the embedded audio hardware they target? Tested against a commercial audio DSP (2 MB on-chip SRAM, 2.07 GMAC/s measured), none of five published systems satisfies both memory and compute limits: memory rules out the 16-51M-parameter TasNet/X-UMX family (by 8.0-25.5x), while compute rules out the 0.4M-parameter RT-STT (needing 5.5x the device's rate). The authors then build one that fits, finding that training regime -- continuous rather than block-padded convolution context -- matters more than architecture: a block-trained model scores 3.93 dB under block-wise evaluation but collapses to silence within 2 seconds under real frame-by-frame inference; switching to continuous-context training reaches 4.70 dB cSDR on MUSDB18-HQ, running in 10.43 ms of an 11.6 ms per-frame deadline on the actual device, 0.5-0.7 dB behind the systems that don't fit.

動機Motivation

音樂音源分離的即時系統（如 HS-TasNet、RT-STT）通常都是在 i7 等級 CPU 或 RTX 等級 GPU 上量測延遲與速度，但助聽器、入耳監聽、現場擴音處理器這類產品用的是低功耗音訊 DSP：固定的乘加運算（MAC）速率、每幀硬性時限、只有幾 MB 晶片內 SRAM。作者指出「RTX 3080Ti 上 3.9ms」這種數字，對這類硬體完全沒有參考意義。

論文要回答的問題很直接：有沒有任何一個已發表的即時音樂分離系統，真的能跑在這類商用嵌入式音訊晶片上？如果沒有，卡在記憶體還是算力，還是兩者都卡？如果都不行，該怎麼設計一個真正裝得上、品質又儘量不掉太多的系統？

Real-time separators such as HS-TasNet and RT-STT are typically timed on an i7-class CPU or an RTX-class GPU, but products like hearing aids, in-ear monitors, and live-sound processors run on low-power audio DSPs: a fixed MAC rate, a hard per-frame deadline, and only a few megabytes of on-chip SRAM. The authors point out that a number like "3.9 ms on an RTX 3080Ti" simply does not transfer to this class of hardware.

The question is direct: does any published real-time music separator actually run on this class of commercial embedded audio chip? If not, is it memory, compute, or both that rules it out? And if none fits, how do you design one that does, without giving up too much quality?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

即時音源分離的既有系統包括 HS-TasNet（23ms 延遲）、RT-STT（同樣 23ms 延遲、約 0.4M 參數）、伴奏分離與人聲消除相關工作，這些系統都強調「延遲不是唯一限制」，會分開報告演算法延遲與每幀計算時間，RT-STT 論文甚至指出「只有 9M 參數」的 Conv-TasNet 實際跑起來仍然很慢。骨幹架構沿用 TFC-TDF U-Net 系（Choi 等人的頻譜圖 U-Net），band-split 系列（Band-Split RNN、Band-Split RoPE Transformer）與 SCNet 則是在桌面級硬體上做效率最佳化，並非針對嵌入式預算。

這篇論文與上述工作最大的差異在於：不是比較「哪個模型參數少」，而是直接把「記憶體」與「每幀算力」列成兩條獨立、硬體無關的判準，套用已發表系統的公開參數量與架構重新驗證。結果顯示參數量完全無法預測是否裝得上——HS-TasNet-S（1600 萬參數）滿足算力但超記憶體 8 倍，RT-STT（38 萬參數）滿足記憶體但超算力 5.5 倍，兩個限制幾乎是反相關的。這種「權重重用係數」（卷積核在頻率軸上被重複使用的倍數）分析是本篇區別於過去比較方法的核心工具。

深度濾波（deep filtering）技術本身也不是新發明：Mack 與 Habets 提出複數 FIR 逐頻段濾波，DeepFilterNet 用它做語音增強，先前也有人拿它當 Hybrid Demucs 的離線後濾波器。這篇論文的差異是把它改成因果、逐音源可關閉（gated）的版本，並把「未來幾幀的濾波器階數」直接當成一個可調的延遲旋鈕。

Existing real-time separators include HS-TasNet (23 ms latency), RT-STT (same 23 ms latency, ~0.4M parameters), plus work on accompaniment separation and vocal cancellation; all of them stress that latency alone isn't the whole story, reporting algorithmic latency and per-frame compute time separately, and RT-STT's own paper notes that Conv-TasNet, despite "only 9M parameters," is slow in practice. The backbone lineage is the TFC-TDF U-Net (Choi et al.'s spectrogram U-Net); band-split models (Band-Split RNN, Band-Split RoPE Transformer) and SCNet optimize efficiency at desktop scale, not against an embedded budget.

This paper's key departure is not comparing "which model has fewer parameters" but formalizing memory and per-frame compute as two independent, hardware-agnostic constraints, then re-checking published systems' own reported architectures against them. Parameter count turns out to predict neither: HS-TasNet-S (16M params) meets the compute deadline but blows the memory budget 8x, while RT-STT (383K params) fits memory easily but needs 5.5x the available compute -- the two constraints are nearly anti-correlated. The "weight reuse factor" (how many times a convolution kernel is reused across frequency bins) is the analytical tool that sets this paper's method apart from prior size-based comparisons.

Deep filtering itself is not new: Mack and Habets introduced per-bin complex FIR filtering, DeepFilterNet applies it to speech enhancement, and others have used it as an offline post-filter on Hybrid Demucs. This paper's variant is causal and per-source gated, and turns the filter's future-tap count into an explicit, tunable latency knob.

方法Method

骨幹是因果版的 TFC-TDF U-Net，處理混音的實數 STFT（1024 點、hop 512、無置中補零，讓每一幀只依賴過去樣本）。兩個關鍵選擇不是為了準確度、而是為了串流穩定：一是用 GRU 而非 LSTM 做遞迴——GRU 狀態是有界凸組合，實測連續跑 120 秒後仍恰好停在 1.00，而 LSTM 狀態是無界累加，同樣測試下從 399 一路長到 962 還在漲；二是輸出複數比率遮罩（作用在輸入頻譜上），讓靜音天生對應到靜音，而不是靠訓練學出來。

深度濾波模組從潛在特徵預測每個時頻格點的複數 FIR 濾波器（階數 N=5）與一個閘控值 G，濾波結果與遮罩估計以 G 加權混合；P 個過去分接頭與 Q 個未來分接頭的分配，直接決定額外延遲（Q×11.6ms）。

論文發現真正關鍵的是訓練方式而非架構：因果卷積傳統上用分塊、每塊左補零的方式訓練，但推論時吃的是真實的連續過去幀，堆疊 8 層卷積後，5.8 秒訓練片段裡約 16/65（近四分之一）幀其實落在「部署時根本不會出現」的補零情境。改成單一連續片段（不做塊內補零）訓練後，逐幀連續推論的品質才回到跟分塊評測差不多的水準（差 0.1dB 以內）。

The backbone is a causal TFC-TDF U-Net operating on the mixture's real STFT (1024-point, hop 512, no center padding, so each frame depends only on past samples). Two choices are dictated by streaming stability rather than accuracy: a GRU instead of an LSTM for the recurrence -- the GRU's state is a bounded convex combination and empirically sits at exactly 1.00 after 120 s of streaming, while a matched LSTM's cell state is an unbounded running sum that grows from 399 to 962 and keeps rising; and a complex ratio mask applied to the input spectrum, so silence maps to silence by construction rather than by training.

A deep-filtering head predicts, per time-frequency bin, a complex FIR filter (order N=5) and a gate G from the latent features; the filtered estimate and the mask estimate are blended by G. The split between P past taps and Q future taps sets the added latency directly, at Q x 11.6 ms.

The paper's central finding is about training, not architecture: causal convolutions are conventionally trained on zero-padded chunks, but inference sees a real, continuous past. With 8 stacked conv layers, about 16 of 65 frames in a 5.8 s training chunk -- nearly a quarter -- sit in a zero-padding regime that never occurs in deployment. Training on a single continuous segment instead (no internal chunk padding) restores frame-by-frame streaming quality to within 0.1 dB of block-wise evaluation.

限制Limitations

作者自己承認品質有落差：部署模型的 4.70dB cSDR 比 RT-STT 低 0.47dB（用 BSSEval-v4 換算則差 0.69dB），跟 HS-TasNet 幾乎打平；他們不主張贏過 RT-STT，只主張 RT-STT 這類系統根本裝不上這顆晶片。若不受算力限制，同架構可以做到 5.49dB，但需要 6.4 倍算力。

論文自己也點名 MAC 數字不是萬能：MAC 相同的兩個模型，若一個做頻率軸遞迴、一個做時間軸遞迴，向量化效率可以差很多，所以真正要看的是實測時間而非單純 MAC 計數。整篇評估只用 cSDR/uSDR 兩個客觀指標，沒有人耳聽測，也完全沒有測功耗（作者自己承認只報告算力、不報告能耗），MUSDB18-HQ 這個資料集本身也沒有測試跨領域（不同錄音風格、不同語言歌曲）的穩健性。

RT-STT 的算力數字並非直接取自對方論文，而是作者自己重現後量到 4.93dB 的版本反推的，屬於下界估計而非精確覆現；記憶體限制的推導假設所有對照系統都用 int8（1 byte/權重），這是對它們最寬鬆的假設，而它們自己都沒有宣稱做到 int8，所以真實差距只會更大。

The authors' own numbers show a real quality gap: the deployed model's 4.70 dB cSDR sits 0.47 dB below RT-STT on this paper's metric (0.69 dB under BSSEval-v4), and is indistinguishable from HS-TasNet. They do not claim to beat RT-STT, only that RT-STT cannot run on this device class; the same architecture without the compute constraint reaches 5.49 dB but needs 6.4x the available compute.

The paper itself flags that MAC count alone is not sufficient: at equal MAC, a frequency-axis recurrence vectorizes far worse than a time-axis one, so measured time (not raw MAC) is what should be trusted. Evaluation uses only the objective cSDR/uSDR metrics, with no listening test and no energy measurement (the paper reports compute, not power), and MUSDB18-HQ alone leaves cross-domain robustness -- other recording styles or languages -- untested.

RT-STT's compute figure is not taken directly from its own paper but backed out from the authors' own reproduction, which scored 4.93 dB -- a lower bound, not an exact reproduction. The memory analysis grants every baseline int8 weights (1 byte per parameter), the most generous assumption possible; none of those systems actually claims int8, so the real gap is larger still.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有意思的地方，是把「即時音源分離裝不上嵌入式硬體」這件事，從一句模糊的抱怨變成兩條可以獨立驗證的不等式（記憶體、每幀算力），並且證明參數量這個常見的簡便指標在這裡完全失靈——這個分析框架本身可以直接套用在其他嵌入式音訊模型的可行性評估上，不限於音源分離。

更值得注意的發現是訓練與推論之間的「串流失配」：一個在標準分塊評測下拿 3.93dB、看起來完全正常的模型，換成真實逐幀連續推論卻在 2 秒內崩潰成靜音，而這個問題不會在常見的評測協定裡被發現，因為分塊評測本身會在每個區塊重設狀態，剛好把這個失配蓋掉。這意味著業界可能有其他「論文上分數正常、實際部署會啞掉」的系統從未被抓到。

由於這是分析單一商用 DSP（ADSP-21835）的結果，作者也承認這個結論只在 Cortex-M 這類受限微控制器的等級上成立、換到有向量處理單元的應用處理器上就不適用，讀者在套用這套「兩條件判準」時要注意硬體等級的邊界。

The most interesting contribution is turning "real-time separation doesn't fit on embedded hardware" from a vague complaint into two independently checkable inequalities (memory, per-frame compute), and showing that parameter count -- the usual quick proxy -- fails completely here. The analytical framework itself transfers directly to feasibility checks for other embedded audio models, not just source separation.

The more striking finding is the training/inference streaming mismatch: a model that scores a perfectly normal 3.93 dB under the standard block-wise protocol collapses to silence within 2 seconds under real frame-by-frame inference, and the standard protocol never catches this because it resets state at every block boundary, papering over exactly the mismatch that matters. This raises the possibility that other systems with normal-looking published scores silently fail the same way in real deployment, undetected.

Since this is an analysis of one commercial DSP (ADSP-21835), the authors acknowledge the verdict holds only at the Cortex-M-class microcontroller tier and would not hold on an application processor with a vector backend -- a boundary readers should keep in mind before applying the two-constraint framework elsewhere.

CMA-OT: Hierarchical Expert Supervision for Dance-to-Music Generation

CMA-OT：面向舞蹈生成音樂的階層式專家監督框架

Jinting Wang, Chenxing Li, Dong Yu, Li Liu
arXiv:2609.13118 · cs.AI, cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-11
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：舞蹈生成音樂（D2M）的難題在於舞蹈只提供節奏、風格等稀疏線索，音樂創作卻需要結構、配器、表現力等密集資訊。CMA-OT 引入預訓練的 Jukebox VQ-VAE 當「音樂專家」，用課程學習由粗到細（top→middle→bottom）依序對齊 DiT 生成器的深、中、淺層表徵，並用融合 Gromov-Wasserstein 最優傳輸取代單純餘弦相似度做對齊。在 AIST++ 資料集上，節拍覆蓋 F1 從最強對照 MotionComposer 的 96.45 提升到 99.12，FAD（PANNs 版）從沒有對齊的基準 21.12 大幅降到 12.50；消融顯示課程學習本身是關鍵——同時對齊三個尺度但不用課程（Base+MA-OT）的 FAD 只有 20.64，反而是加上課程之後（Base+CMA）才把 FAD 大幅降到 13.36。Why this one: Dance-to-music (D2M) generation is hard because dance offers only sparse cues (rhythm, style) while music needs dense structure, instrumentation, and expressive dynamics. CMA-OT brings in a pretrained Jukebox VQ-VAE as a "music expert," using curriculum learning to align coarse-to-fine (top, then middle, then bottom) with the DiT generator's deep, middle, and shallow layers, and replacing plain cosine similarity with Fused Gromov-Wasserstein optimal transport for the alignment itself. On AIST++, beat-coverage F1 rises from the strongest baseline's (MotionComposer) 96.45 to 99.12, and FAD (PANNs-based) drops sharply from the no-alignment baseline's 21.12 to 12.50; ablations show curriculum learning itself is the key factor -- aligning all three scales at once without a curriculum (Base+MA-OT) reaches only 20.64, while adding the curriculum (Base+CMA) is what brings FAD down sharply to 13.36.

動機Motivation

舞蹈影片能提供的線索其實很稀疏——大致就是節奏與風格，但要作曲，需要的是密集資訊：巨觀的整體風格、中觀的旋律與和聲、微觀的節奏與音色細節。過去的舞蹈生成音樂方法幾乎都只靠這些稀疏線索、只在最終音訊輸出上做重建監督，等於要模型同時學會「對齊節奏」和「把音樂寫好」兩件事，在配對資料本來就稀缺的情況下，這種雙重負擔讓生成音樂普遍缺乏音樂性與結構連貫性。

這篇論文想問的是：能不能引入一個外部、預訓練好的「音樂專家」，直接在生成器的中間層表徵上做監督（而不是只看最終輸出），把作曲知識分層注入生成過程，藉此把「跟上節奏」與「寫出好音樂」這兩件事分開處理？

A dance video's cues are genuinely sparse -- essentially rhythm and style -- but composing music needs dense information: macro-level overall style, meso-level melody and harmony, and micro-level rhythm and timbral detail. Prior dance-to-music methods rely almost entirely on these sparse cues and supervise only the final audio output, forcing the model to learn rhythmic alignment and music composition simultaneously; combined with scarce paired data, that dual burden leaves generated music short on musicality and structural coherence.

This paper asks whether bringing in an external, pretrained "music expert" to supervise the generator's intermediate-layer representations directly -- not just the final output -- can inject compositional knowledge in a layered way, separating "keep time with the dance" from "write coherent music" as two problems rather than one.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

既有的舞蹈生成音樂方法，如 D2M-GAN、LORIS、MotionComposer 等，多半用 STGCN 之類的預訓練編碼器抽取動作特徵，或是直接用姿態節奏線索，再用重建損失優化生成器；這類設計能抓住基本的動作語意與節奏對齊，但缺乏對音樂結構的顯式建模。CMA-OT 與它們的差異在於：不再只靠稀疏的舞蹈線索與最終輸出監督，而是額外引入一個跟舞蹈完全無關的預訓練音樂模型（Jukebox 的 VQ-VAE 編碼器）當專家，在生成器的中間層表徵上做監督。

這個「拿預訓練模型的中間層表徵去監督另一個生成模型」的做法，其實是 REPA（representation alignment）這個既有技術路線的延伸——REPA 本身是拿預訓練視覺編碼器去對齊擴散模型內部表徵，後續的 VideoREPA 把它用在影片生成上做物理規律對齊，TARO 與 HunyuanVideo-Foley 則用在音訊生成上。CMA-OT 與這些既有 REPA 類方法最大的不同，在於後者大多做「全域」對齊（單一尺度、逐點餘弦相似度），沒有處理好專家表徵與生成器表徵在時間解析度上本來就對不齊的問題；CMA-OT 則是把 Jukebox 三個階層（top/middle/bottom）分別對到 DiT 的深、中、淺層，並用課程學習依序啟動，這一點在既有 REPA 系工作裡沒有人做過。

在對齊的計算方式上，這篇論文也不是用最直觀的餘弦相似度，而是採用 Gromov-Wasserstein 最優傳輸的擴充版本 FGW（融合了特徵相似度與序列內部結構關係），FGW 過去用在圖匹配、動作切割等任務，論文自己也承認「FGW 用在生成模型的分層監督上」這件事此前少有人做，是把既有工具搬到新場景，而非發明新的傳輸理論。

Existing D2M methods such as D2M-GAN, LORIS, and MotionComposer typically extract motion features with pretrained encoders like STGCN, or use pose-based rhythmic cues directly, then optimize the generator with reconstruction losses -- capturing basic motion semantics and rhythmic alignment but without explicit modeling of musical structure. CMA-OT's departure is to stop relying solely on sparse dance cues and final-output supervision, instead bringing in a pretrained music model with no connection to dance at all -- Jukebox's VQ-VAE encoder -- as an expert supervising the generator's intermediate-layer representations.

This "supervise a generator's intermediate layers with a pretrained model's representations" idea extends the existing REPA (representation alignment) line: REPA itself aligns diffusion-model internals with a pretrained vision encoder, VideoREPA applies the idea to video generation for physics alignment, and TARO and HunyuanVideo-Foley apply it to audio generation. CMA-OT's departure from these prior REPA-style methods is that they mostly perform global alignment (a single scale, point-wise cosine similarity) without addressing the temporal-resolution mismatch between expert and generator representations; CMA-OT instead maps Jukebox's three hierarchy levels (top, middle, bottom) onto the DiT's deep, middle, and shallow layers respectively, activated in sequence via curriculum learning -- something no prior REPA-style work has done.

For the alignment computation itself, rather than the obvious cosine similarity, the paper uses Fused Gromov-Wasserstein (FGW) optimal transport, which combines feature similarity with intra-sequence structural relations. FGW has previously been used for graph matching and action segmentation; the paper itself acknowledges that applying FGW to hierarchical supervision in generative models is largely unexplored, making this an adaptation of an existing tool to a new setting rather than a new transport theory.

方法Method

生成骨幹是條件流匹配（flow matching）的 DiT（12 層 LLaMA 解碼器層），輸入是舞蹈影片的 I3D 視覺風格特徵與姿態節奏特徵；音樂潛在表示來自 DiffRhythm 的預訓練 VAE（壓縮倍率 2048，得到 21.5Hz、64 維的潛在表示）。監督訊號來自 Jukebox 1B Lyrics 模型的 VQ-VAE 編碼器，取其 top/middle/bottom 三個離散潛在層級，分別代表全域風格、旋律和聲結構、局部節奏音色。透過對 1000 個乾淨音樂樣本做表徵相似度分析（CKNNA），作者發現 Jukebox 的 bottom/middle/top 層恰好分別跟 DiT 的第 2、6、10 層最相似，因此把三個尺度的對齊點對應到這三層。

對齊訓練分三階段用餘弦退火課程調整權重：前 30 個 epoch 只對齊頂層（全域風格）、中間逐步加入中層對齊（旋律與和聲）並讓頂層權重平滑下降、100 epoch 後底層對齊（節奏細節）主導。每個尺度的對齊損失用 FGW 距離計算，並用一個依據語意成本與結構成本比例自動算出的係數 α_k，動態決定每個尺度該偏重語意相似還是結構一致，而不是固定權重。

整體訓練目標是流匹配生成損失加上這個階層對齊損失的和；推論階段完全不需要 Jukebox 專家，生成器只靠舞蹈特徵做條件生成，因此推論階段的計算量跟沒有專家監督的基礎模型完全一樣，額外開銷只發生在訓練時期。

The generation backbone is a conditional flow-matching DiT (12 LLaMA decoder layers), conditioned on I3D visual style features and pose-based rhythm features from the dance video; music latents come from DiffRhythm's pretrained VAE (compression factor 2048, giving 21.5 Hz, 64-dimensional latents). Supervision comes from the VQ-VAE encoder of a pretrained Jukebox 1B Lyrics model, whose top/middle/bottom discrete levels represent global style, melodic/harmonic structure, and local rhythm/timbre respectively. A representation-similarity analysis (CKNNA) over 1,000 clean music samples found that Jukebox's bottom/middle/top levels are respectively most similar to DiT layers 2, 6, and 10, fixing the three alignment points accordingly.

Alignment training follows a three-phase cosine-annealed curriculum: the first 30 epochs align only the top level (global style); middle-level alignment (melody and harmony) is introduced gradually as the top-level weight smoothly decays; after epoch 100, bottom-level alignment (rhythmic detail) dominates. Each scale's alignment loss is computed via FGW distance, with a coefficient alpha_k derived automatically from the ratio of semantic to structural cost, dynamically deciding per scale whether to weight semantic similarity or structural consistency rather than using a fixed weight.

The overall training objective sums the flow-matching generation loss and this hierarchical alignment loss; at inference, the Jukebox expert is not needed at all -- the generator conditions only on the dance features, so inference compute matches the no-expert base model exactly, with all extra cost confined to training.

限制Limitations

課程學習的三個階段邊界（30、100 epoch，每次轉換 25 epoch）是手動設定的超參數，論文沒有討論這些邊界對其他資料集或更長/更短訓練排程的敏感度，也沒有做「邊界怎麼選」的消融——換一組資料，這幾個數字要不要重新調，並不清楚。

評估的兩個資料集規模都不大：AIST++ 只有 1,020 支影片、TikTok 只有 445 支涵蓋 85 首歌，且評測都是「訓練分佈內」的舞蹈與音樂配對；論文完全沒有測試訓練資料以外類型的舞蹈風格或音樂類型的泛化能力，也沒有討論若換一個規模更大、風格更雜的資料集，三段式課程是否仍然穩定。

使用者研究只找了 40 位志願者對 30 個 AIST++ 樣本評分，樣本數偏小；論文也沒有報告訓練與推論的實際運算時間或算力開銷（只提到推論階段跟基礎模型算力相同，但沒有給出具體數字），無法評估這套流程在實務部署上的成本。

論文正文宣稱課程學習（Base+CMA）讓 FAD_p 比 Base 下降 40.81%，但對照表 II 的實際數字（21.12 降到 13.36），算出來的降幅其實是 36.74%，兩者對不太起來，可能是筆誤或用了未列出的其他基準；引用這個百分比時建議以表格原始數字為準。

The curriculum's three phase boundaries (epochs 30 and 100, with 25-epoch transitions) are manually set hyperparameters; the paper never discusses how sensitive these boundaries are to other datasets or to longer/shorter training schedules, and runs no ablation on how to choose them -- whether they'd need retuning for a different dataset is left open.

Both evaluation datasets are small: AIST++ has only 1,020 videos and TikTok only 445 videos across 85 songs, and all evaluation is on in-distribution dance-music pairs. The paper tests no generalization to dance styles or music genres outside the training distribution, and does not discuss whether the three-phase curriculum stays stable on a larger, more heterogeneous dataset.

The user study recruited only 40 volunteers to rate 30 AIST++ samples, a small sample; the paper reports no actual training or inference compute cost (it only states inference compute matches the base model, without giving numbers), leaving the practical deployment cost of the pipeline unassessed.

The main text claims curriculum learning (Base+CMA) cuts FAD_p by 40.81% relative to Base, but the actual Table II figures (21.12 down to 13.36) work out to a 36.74% reduction -- the two numbers don't reconcile, possibly a typo or an unstated different baseline; when citing this result, the raw table figures are the safer reference.

討論Discussion

消融實驗把「課程學習」跟「多尺度監督」的個別貢獻拆得很乾淨：同時對齊三個尺度但不用課程（Base+MA-OT）的 FAD 只有 20.64，比完全不做對齊的 Base（21.12）好一點點，甚至在部分指標上比單一尺度對齊還差；反而是用了課程、只加餘弦相似度的 Base+CMA 就把 FAD 大幅降到 13.36。這說明真正吃重的是「怎麼安排監督時序」而不是「監督訊號本身有多豐富」——同時把三個不同尺度的梯度硬塞進去，反而會互相打架讓訓練變得不穩定。

把 FGW 換成更簡單的餘弦相似度或標準最優傳輸做對照（Table IV），FGW 在四個指標上都是最好的，但差距其實不大（例如 F1 從標準 OT 的 98.97 提升到 99.12），真正的大跳躍來自「有沒有用課程學習」而不是「用哪種對齊距離」，這點跟論文摘要強調 FGW 重要性的敘事順序略有落差，讀者要注意消融數字本身透露的貢獻權重排序。

論文選定 Jukebox 三層分別對應 DiT 第 2/6/10 層，是靠 1000 個樣本的表徵相似度分析（CKNNA）得出，這個對應關係是否會隨著 DiT 層數、隱藏維度或訓練資料改變而跟著變，論文沒有討論；如果之後有人想把這套方法搬到不同架構的生成器上，這一步很可能需要重新做一次類似的分析，而不是直接套用論文給的層數。

The ablations cleanly separate the contributions of curriculum scheduling from multi-scale supervision itself: aligning all three scales at once without a curriculum (Base+MA-OT) reaches FAD only 20.64 -- barely better than no alignment at all (Base, 21.12), and worse than several single-scale variants on some metrics. Adding the curriculum with plain cosine similarity (Base+CMA) instead drops FAD sharply to 13.36. The heavy lifting comes from how supervision is sequenced, not how rich the supervision signal is -- forcing three different scales' gradients in simultaneously makes training fight itself.

Swapping FGW for plain cosine similarity or standard OT (Table IV) leaves FGW ahead on all four metrics, but by a modest margin (e.g. F1 rises from standard OT's 98.97 to 99.12 with FGW); the larger jump comes from whether a curriculum is used at all, not from which alignment distance is chosen -- a nuance the abstract's emphasis on FGW slightly understates, and worth weighing against the ablation numbers directly.

The mapping of Jukebox's three levels onto DiT layers 2, 6, and 10 comes from a representation-similarity analysis (CKNNA) over 1,000 samples; the paper does not discuss whether this mapping would shift with a different DiT depth, hidden size, or training corpus. Porting the method to a differently-shaped generator would likely require redoing a similar analysis rather than reusing the paper's specific layer numbers.
