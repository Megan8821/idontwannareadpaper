MuSP-Bench: Advanced Multimodal Benchmarking of Music Understanding across Score and Performance

MuSP-Bench：跨樂譜與演奏的音樂理解多模態基準測試

Milan Liessens Dujardin, Song-Ze Yu, Kevin Miao
arXiv:2608.28212 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-31
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇引入的 MuSP-Bench 是第一個要求模型同時讀樂譜與聽演奏的音樂理解基準：490 題橫跨 18 首完整鋼琴獨奏曲與 6 段管絃樂節錄。結果說明現有前沿模型在跨模態整合上仍薄弱——最強的 GPT-5.6 用 ABC 記譜作答樂譜題只有 51.1%，聽音訊回答演奏題最好的 Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus 也只有 14.2%（僅語氣風格題例外衝到 79.2%）；而把樂譜圖片與音訊一起餵給模型時，Muse Spark 的分數從單獨符號輸入的 30.6% 崩到 6.9%，顯示「多模態」本身不會帶來理解力，反而暴露了整合上的失敗。Why this one: MuSP-Bench is the first music-understanding benchmark that requires a model to read the score and listen to the performance at the same time: 490 questions spanning 18 complete piano pieces and 6 orchestral excerpts. The results show current frontier models still integrate poorly across modalities -- the best score-only score, GPT-5.6 on ABC notation, is only 51.1%, and the best audio-only performance score, Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus, is just 14.2% (except on tonality/style questions, where it jumps to 79.2%). Feeding the model both a score image and audio actually collapses Muse Spark's accuracy from 30.6% (symbolic-only) to 6.9%, showing that adding modalities does not add understanding -- it exposes an integration failure.

動機Motivation

音樂交流從來是樂譜(記錄意圖)與演奏(實現於聲音)的雙軌:一份總譜可以被演奏出速度、觸鍵、分句完全不同的兩個版本,而聽眾光聽演奏也推不出作曲家原本寫在紙上的確切音高與節拍。作者認為,現有評測把這兩個模態切開來考,測不出模型是否真的懂音樂——一個模型可能把樂譜讀得頭頭是道,但完全聽不出演奏者做了什麼詮釋上的選擇,反之亦然。MuSP-Bench 的動機就是強迫模型同時面對兩者:490 題裡有的題目只給樂譜、有的只給演奏音訊、有的兩者都給,藉此觀察模型是否具備跨模態的音樂理解,而不只是單一模態的表面辨識。

Musical communication runs on two tracks: the score encodes intent, while the performance realizes it in sound with choices of tempo, touch, and phrasing that the score alone does not fix. A listener cannot recover the exact pitches and rhythms on the page from audio alone, and a reader of the score cannot know what interpretive choices a performer made. The authors argue that evaluating these modalities separately, as prior benchmarks do, cannot tell whether a model understands music -- it might parse a score fluently while missing every performance nuance, or vice versa. MuSP-Bench's motivation is to force models to face both at once: some of its 490 questions give only the score, some only performance audio, and some both, to probe integrated rather than single-modality understanding.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

樂譜端的先行基準包括 MusicTheoryBench、WildScore、ABC-Eval、MSU-Bench 等,但它們幾乎只吃 ABC 記譜或單張樂譜圖片,不涉及聲音;音訊端則有 HumMusQA、MUSE、CMI-Bench、MusTBench,考的是音高、曲風、樂器辨識等聽覺任務,但不要求對照樂譜。唯一嘗試跨模態的 MuseBench 同時給樂譜與音訊,但作者指出它的音訊題目只問「調性是否正確」「音符完整度」「速度穩定性」這類粗粒度指標,不觸及運音(voicing)、分句或演奏技法。MuSP-Bench 跟這些最大的差別,是它要求模型針對同一份作品同時處理符號記譜(ABC/MIDI 轉文字)、樂譜圖片與演奏音訊三種輸入,並且題目本身分成識別、定位、量化、比較、排序、推論、抄寫、解釋、總結九種動作類型,比 MuseBench 的粗粒度分類細得多。

Score-side predecessors such as MusicTheoryBench, WildScore, ABC-Eval, and MSU-Bench mostly consume only ABC notation or a single score image, with no audio involved. Audio-side benchmarks like HumMusQA, MUSE, CMI-Bench, and MusTBench test listening tasks such as pitch, genre, and instrument recognition, but without requiring the score. The only genuinely cross-modal predecessor, MuseBench, provides both score and audio, but the authors note its audio questions only ask about coarse properties -- key correctness, note completeness, tempo stability -- and never touch voicing, phrasing, or technique. MuSP-Bench's biggest departure from all of these is requiring a model to handle three input types for the same piece -- symbolic notation (ABC/MIDI-as-text), score images, and performance audio -- and its questions are tagged with nine action types (identification, localization, quantification, comparison, ordering, inference, transcription, explanation, summarization), a far finer taxonomy than MuseBench's coarse categories.

方法Method

資料來源是鋼琴的 (n)ASAP 資料集配合 PianoCoRe 做 MIDI 對齊,管絃樂則取自 BSED;涵蓋 18 首完整鋼琴獨奏/樂章與 6 段管絃樂節錄,總共 490 題,其中 460 題是開放式作答(答案格式固定為音高、小節號、和弦進行、時間點或樂句)、30 題為選擇題。每題還標了四個維度:模態(僅樂譜/僅演奏/兩者/任一)、時間跨度(短 19.2%、中 25.1%、長 36.1%、任意 19.6%)、內容層級(音高→時間組織→配器→演奏實現→旋律和聲曲式詮釋理解)與動作類型。

受測模型包含 GPT-5.6、Qwen3.6-Plus、Audio Flamingo Next(僅音訊)、Muse Spark 1.2 與 Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus(這兩者可吃三種模態全部),另外設了一個只給作曲家與曲名、不給任何樂譜或音訊的「元資料基線」,用來檢驗題目是否真的需要分析內容才能答對。

整體加權平均分是 GPT-5.6 48.1%、Muse Spark 1.2 25.9%、Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus 16.5%、Qwen3.6-Plus 14.1%。拆開來看,樂譜題(180 題)最高是 GPT 讀 ABC 的 51.1%,讀樂譜圖片掉到 27.2%;演奏題(106 題)最高是 GPT 讀 MIDI-as-text 的 59.4%,音訊表現最好的 Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus 僅 14.2%,Audio Flamingo Next 更只有 0.9%。把 ABC 的音名換成純 MIDI 數值後,GPT 的準確率掉了 26.5 個百分點,顯示模型很依賴符號記譜裡的文字化音名,而不是真的在做音高推理。

The dataset draws on the (n)ASAP piano dataset with PianoCoRe for MIDI alignment, and BSED for orchestral material -- 18 complete piano pieces/movements plus 6 orchestral excerpts, 490 questions total, with 460 open-ended (answer formats fixed to pitch, bar number, chord progression, timestamp, or phrase) and 30 multiple-choice. Each question is tagged along four dimensions: modality (score-only/performance-only/both/either), time horizon (short 19.2%, medium 25.1%, long 36.1%, any 19.6%), content hierarchy (pitch to temporal organization to orchestration to performance realization to melodic/harmonic/formal/interpretive understanding), and action type.

Models tested include GPT-5.6, Qwen3.6-Plus, Audio Flamingo Next (audio-only), Muse Spark 1.2, and Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus (the latter two can take all three modalities), plus a metadata-only baseline given nothing but the composer and title, used to check whether the questions truly require analyzing the content.

The weighted overall means are GPT-5.6 48.1%, Muse Spark 1.2 25.9%, Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus 16.5%, and Qwen3.6-Plus 14.1%. Breaking it down: on score-only questions (180 of them) the best is GPT reading ABC at 51.1%, dropping to 27.2% when given score images instead; on performance-only questions (106 of them) the best is GPT reading MIDI-as-text at 59.4%, while the best audio-only score, Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus, reaches only 14.2%, and Audio Flamingo Next just 0.9%. Replacing ABC pitch names with raw MIDI note numbers costs GPT 26.5 percentage points, showing the model leans on the textual pitch names in symbolic notation rather than genuine pitch reasoning.

限制Limitations

基準本身也有明顯限制:曲目全部來自古典鋼琴獨奏與管絃樂節錄,泛用性不明,換成流行音樂或非西方傳統音樂可能得到完全不同的結果。490 題中有 30 題是選擇題,對強模型而言可能存在猜對的機率地板;而僅給作曲家與曲名的「元資料基線」仍拿到 2–7% 的分數,不算零,顯示部分題目可能被模型從訓練資料裡記住的曲目背景「蒙到」,而不是即時分析。此外,論文自己也承認,把多模態輸入疊加後性能反而下降(而非提升)這件事目前只描述現象,沒有進一步拆解是輸入衝突、注意力分散還是模型架構本身的瓶頸造成的。

The benchmark has its own limits. All repertoire is classical piano solo and orchestral excerpts, so generalization to popular or non-Western musical traditions is untested and could look very different. 30 of the 490 questions are multiple-choice, giving strong models a nonzero chance of guessing correctly; and the metadata-only baseline, given nothing but composer and title, still scores 2-7% -- not zero, suggesting some questions may be answered from memorized facts about famous pieces rather than live analysis. The paper also only describes, without further diagnosing, the fact that stacking modalities hurts rather than helps performance -- it does not distinguish whether this comes from conflicting inputs, diluted attention, or an architectural bottleneck.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有意思的發現,是「多模態」在這裡不是加分項而是減分項:當同時給樂譜圖片與音訊時,Muse Spark 的分數從只給符號記譜的 30.6% 掉到 6.9%,Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus 更是全程低於 6%。這與一般直覺——更多資訊應該帶來更好表現——正好相反,說明現有多模態音樂模型的融合機制還沒學會「聽跟看要互相校準」,而是彼此干擾。另一個反直覺結果,是符號記譜(ABC、MIDI-as-text)在分析型題目上全面壓過樂譜圖片與音訊,但在「作曲家/曲名辨識」與「調性/風格判斷」這類全局性、風格性題目上,圖片與音訊反而佔優(例如 Muse Spark 用圖片辨識作曲家達 79.2%),顯示符號輸入擅長局部精確分析,感官輸入擅長全局風格感知,兩者目前無法互補,只能二選一。

The most interesting finding is that 'multimodal' is a penalty here, not a bonus: giving both a score image and audio drops Muse Spark's accuracy from 30.6% (symbolic-only) to 6.9%, and Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus stays below 6% throughout. This runs against the usual intuition that more information should help, and points to current multimodal music models not yet learning to calibrate what they hear against what they see -- the two streams interfere rather than reinforce. A second counter-intuitive result is that symbolic notation (ABC, MIDI-as-text) dominates analytical questions, but score images and audio pull ahead on global, style-level questions like composer/title identification and tonality/style judgment (Muse Spark hits 79.2% identifying composers from images) -- suggesting symbolic input is good at precise local analysis while sensory input is good at global style perception, with no way yet to combine the two.

Exploring the Design Space of Representation Learning for Audio Transformations

探索音訊變換表徵學習的設計空間

Sungho Lee, Marco Martínez-Ramírez, Junghyun Koo, Wei-Hsiang Liao, Kyogu Lee, Yuki Mitsufuji
arXiv:2608.28127 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-31
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文把「處理感知的音訊表徵該學什麼」拆成三個目標分開比較,結論是兩種嵌入要分開留著才有用:轉換嵌入(zT)負責距離型任務,處理後音訊嵌入(zy)負責探針型任務。數字上,跨樂器檢索準確率從 AFx-Rep 的 20.5%、Fx-Encoder++ 的 13.2% 拉到本文組合的 48.7–49.8%;效果鏈估計的巨集 F1 從 Fx-Encoder++ 的 48.1% 拉到 60.9%;跨樂器風格轉換的頻譜距離也從 Fx-Encoder++ 的 1.144 降到 0.647,數字都在同一批對照實驗下量出來,不是分開宣稱。Why this one: The paper splits 'what should a processing-aware audio representation capture' into three objectives and compares them head to head, concluding that two embeddings should be kept separate: the transformation embedding (zT) serves distance-based tasks, the processed-audio embedding (zy) serves probe-based tasks. In numbers, cross-instrument retrieval accuracy rises from AFx-Rep's 20.5% and Fx-Encoder++'s 13.2% to 48.7-49.8% for this paper's combinations; macro F1 on effect-chain estimation rises from Fx-Encoder++'s 48.1% to 60.9%; and the spectral distance for cross-instrument style transfer drops from Fx-Encoder++'s 1.144 to 0.647 -- all measured under the same controlled comparison, not claimed separately.

動機Motivation

神經音訊表徵學習已經在「內容導向」任務上有不少成果(辨識樂器、分類曲風等),但一旦任務換成「這段音訊被怎麼處理過」——例如判斷用了什麼壓縮比、EQ 曲線或殘響長度——現有表徵的表現就明顯掉隊。更根本的問題是,連「處理感知的表徵該捕捉什麼」都沒有共識:該記錄處理本身(例如「這是一個失真效果」),還是記錄處理完的聲音(例如「這是一把加了失真的吉他」)?這兩者是完全不同的設計選擇,先前研究各自暗中選了一邊,卻沒有人把它們放在同一套模型、同一批資料、同一組評測下直接比較,導致讀者無法判斷某個方法表現好,到底是目標選對了,還是架構或資料湊巧佔了便宜。

Neural audio representation learning has made progress on content-oriented tasks -- instrument recognition, genre classification -- but drops off sharply on tasks about how audio was processed: what compression ratio, EQ curve, or reverb length was applied. The deeper problem is that there is no consensus on what a processing-aware representation should even capture: the processing itself (e.g., 'this is a distortion effect') or the processed sound (e.g., 'this is a distorted guitar')? These are fundamentally different design choices, and prior work has each implicitly committed to one side without ever comparing them under the same model, data, and evaluation -- so a reader cannot tell whether a method's good score comes from the right objective or just a lucky architecture/data draw.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

先前具體做法可以分三類,而且各有明確的捷徑漏洞。Fx-Encoder 與強化版 Fx-Encoder++ 走對比學習,用「同一段音軌」或「同一種樂器」的不同處理版本互為正例,但這代表模型可能光靠「認出來源音色」就能把正例配對出來,不需要真的理解處理本身;AFx-Rep 走監督分類,直接預測離散的效果器預設(preset)類別,但這種做法無法泛化到訓練時沒見過的連續參數組合。CLAP、MERT、VGGish、PANN 這類通用音訊表徵模型則完全是內容導向,論文的實驗顯示它們在處理相關任務上表現明顯落後專門設計的方法。本文與這些方法最大的差別,是刻意在建構訓練批次(batch)時阻斷兩種捷徑:正例一定換源(不同樂器/音軌但處理相同),困難負例則用同一個來源但換不同處理,逼模型不能只靠辨認音色去投機。

Prior concrete approaches fall into three camps, each with an identifiable shortcut. Fx-Encoder and its stronger successor Fx-Encoder++ use contrastive learning with same-stem or same-instrument positives, which lets the model match positives just by recognizing source timbre, without understanding the processing itself. AFx-Rep uses supervised classification over discrete preset labels, which cannot generalize to continuous parameter combinations unseen during training. General-purpose audio models such as CLAP, MERT, VGGish, and PANN are purely content-oriented, and the paper's own experiments show them lagging well behind purpose-built methods on processing-related tasks. This paper's biggest departure is deliberately blocking both shortcuts in batch construction: positives always swap the source (different instrument/stem, same processing), and hard negatives keep the same source but swap the processing, so the model cannot cheat by recognizing timbre alone.

方法Method

作者把「處理感知表徵該學什麼」統一成三個目標的組合:處理一致性(ℒT,多視角對比學習,拿相同處理鏈但不同來源的音訊互為正例)、描述對齊(ℒP,把效果器參數結構化成文字描述,用對比學習讓轉換嵌入貼近描述嵌入)、以及透過前向預測的等變性(ℒy,用一個前向預測器從輸入嵌入加上處理條件去預測處理後音訊的嵌入)。三個目標的權重各自是 0 或 1 的二元開關,方便逐一比較每種組合。

架構上,音訊編碼器是凍結的 Stable Audio Open 編碼器外加一個可訓練的 4 層、1024 維 transformer(帶旋轉位置編碼);描述編碼器則把效果類型與正規化後的參數各自過一層線性層,再送進 6 層、512 維的 transformer。訓練用 FlashAdamW,峰值學習率 1e-4,cosine 退火,單張 H100 跑 10 萬步。處理器庫涵蓋等化器、濾波器、動態(壓縮/限幅)、失真(含神經放大器模擬)、殘響(數位與脈衝響應)、延遲、調變效果,鏈長從 1 到 8 層階層式取樣。

檢索任務上(20-way,1000 輪),ℒT+ℒP 組合的平均準確率是 61.1%,勝過 AFx-Rep 的 45.2% 與 Fx-Encoder++ 的 36.0%,差距在「跨樂器」與「跨音軌」這兩種較難的條件下最明顯(例如跨樂器 49.8% 對 20.5% 對 13.2%)。探針式評測(在凍結嵌入上加 MLP)上,ℒT+ℒP+ℒy 組合的效果鏈估計巨集 F1 是 60.9%,勝過 AFx-Rep 的 56.1% 與 Fx-Encoder++ 的 48.1%,但個別效果類別差異很大——濾波器與延遲的 F1 有 74.8–77.2%,失真只有 32.8%。風格轉換(推論時優化嵌入距離)在跨樂器條件下,轉換嵌入 zT 的頻譜距離是 0.647,優於 AFx-Rep 的 0.832 與 Fx-Encoder++ 的 1.144(p<0.001)。

The paper unifies 'what a processing-aware representation should learn' into a combination of three objectives: processing consistency (ℒT, multi-view contrastive learning where audio with the same processing chain but different sources are positives), description alignment (ℒP, structuring effect parameters into text descriptions and contrastively aligning the transformation embedding to them), and equivariance via forward prediction (ℒy, a forward predictor that estimates the processed-audio embedding from the input embedding plus processing conditioning). Each objective's weight is a binary 0/1 switch, making every combination directly comparable.

The audio encoder is a frozen Stable Audio Open encoder plus a trainable 4-layer, 1024-dim transformer with rotary positional embeddings; the description encoder passes effect type and normalized parameters through type-specific linear layers into a 6-layer, 512-dim transformer. Training uses FlashAdamW at a peak learning rate of 1e-4 with cosine annealing, 100k steps on a single H100. The processor library spans equalizer, filter, dynamics (compression/limiting), distortion (including neural amp models), reverb (digital and impulse-response), delay, and modulation effects, with chains of length 1-8 sampled hierarchically.

On retrieval (20-way, 1000 episodes), the ℒT+ℒP combination averages 61.1% accuracy, ahead of AFx-Rep's 45.2% and Fx-Encoder++'s 36.0%, with the gap widest under the harder cross-instrument and cross-stem conditions (e.g. cross-instrument 49.8% vs. 20.5% vs. 13.2%). On probe-based evaluation (an MLP on frozen embeddings), the ℒT+ℒP+ℒy combination reaches 60.9% macro F1 on effect-chain estimation, ahead of AFx-Rep's 56.1% and Fx-Encoder++'s 48.1% -- though per-category scores vary widely: filter and delay reach 74.8-77.2% F1, distortion only 32.8%. On style transfer (optimizing toward the embedding at inference time), under cross-instrument conditions the transformation embedding zT reaches a spectral distance of 0.647, better than AFx-Rep's 0.832 and Fx-Encoder++'s 1.144 (p<0.001).

限制Limitations

作者自己列了幾個侵蝕實用性的限制:泛化的主要瓶頸還是訓練來源的多樣性——同樣是 ℒT+ℒP 組合,面對訓練時沒見過的 MUSDB 來源準確率是 61.1%,換成沒見過的 Linux 效果器插件但來源熟悉時反而有 83.2%,顯示「換聲音來源」比「換效果器實作」更傷表現。方法還依賴一種叫 Fx-normalization 的前處理,需要事先知道音訊的來源類別,限制了可擴展性。描述對齊(ℒP)這個目標又依賴結構化的效果器參數描述,很多真實世界的商用外掛根本沒有這種說明文件,等於這個目標在實務上用不上。最後,由於非線性效果器的處理效果本身是訊號相關的,論文承認「完全把內容和處理分開」在數學上就是不可能的任務,目前的方法只是把殘留的內容洩漏降到比基線更低,而不是消除。

The authors list several limitations that erode practicality: generalization is mainly bottlenecked by training source diversity -- the same ℒT+ℒP combination scores 61.1% on unseen MUSDB sources, but 83.2% on unseen Linux plugin implementations when the source is familiar, showing that a novel source hurts more than a novel effect implementation. The method also depends on a preprocessing step called Fx-normalization that requires knowing the source category in advance, limiting scalability. The description-alignment objective (ℒP) needs structured effect-parameter descriptions that most real-world commercial plugins simply don't ship with, making that objective unusable in practice for them. Finally, because nonlinear effects are inherently signal-dependent, the authors acknowledge that fully separating content from processing is mathematically impossible -- the method only lowers residual content leakage below the baselines, not eliminates it.

討論Discussion

最值得討論的地方,是這篇論文證明「加更多目標未必更好」這件事是有方向性的:把等變性目標(ℒy)加到處理一致性(ℒT)上會提升表現,但加到描述對齊(ℒP)上卻讓表現略降,兩種疊加方式效果不對稱。這暗示 ℒy 提供的訊號跟 ℒT 是互補的(都在講「同一種處理」),但跟 ℒP 提供的訊號有一定衝突(ℒP 是靠文字描述硬性對齊,ℒy 是靠預測任務隱性學習)。另一個值得注意的結果是「來源分類洩漏」實驗:轉換嵌入 zT 拿去做 4 類樂器來源分類時準確率只有 67.2%到 89.4%(依組合而異),遠低於處理後音訊嵌入 zy 的 93.2%到 95.1%,證實 zT 確實比較「去內容化」——這正是論文的核心主張:兩種嵌入該分開留著,而不是硬要學出一個萬用嵌入。

The most discussion-worthy point is that the paper shows 'adding more objectives' is not uniformly better -- it has a direction. Adding the equivariance objective (ℒy) on top of processing consistency (ℒT) improves results, but adding it on top of description alignment (ℒP) slightly hurts them; the two additions are asymmetric. This suggests the signal ℒy provides is complementary to ℒT (both are about 'the same processing'), but somewhat conflicts with the signal from ℒP (which hard-aligns via text description, versus ℒy's implicit learning through a prediction task). Another notable result is the source-leakage probe: the transformation embedding zT, used for 4-way instrument-source classification, only reaches 67.2-89.4% accuracy depending on the combination, well below the processed-audio embedding zy's 93.2-95.1% -- confirming zT is indeed more content-free, which is exactly the paper's core claim: keep the two embeddings separate rather than forcing a single universal one.

Klangfarbenakkord and Klangfarbenharmonien: Metric Space Models for Music on Informational Geometry

音色和弦與音色和聲學:音樂於資訊幾何上的度量空間模型

Yusei Tamura, Shigekazu Ishihara, Ken Ito
arXiv:2608.28026 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-31
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文把音色差異換算成一個具體的頻率數字:用 Wasserstein 距離量約德爾唱法(假聲)跟胸聲唱同一個音高的頻譜差異,得到 442 Hz,扣掉單純的音高質心位移(353 Hz)後,純粹由頻譜「形狀」改變造成的音色變化是 89 Hz。作者用同一套量尺分析單簧管「破裂音 G」跟長笛、雙簧管、低音管同音齊奏的相容性,並跟雙簧管演奏者合作設計出 13 種新的替代指法,實際找出跟單簧管「破裂音 G」音色距離最小的組合——這是一套從物理量直接導出樂器實務建議的具體案例,不是純理論宣稱。Why this one: The paper converts timbre difference into a concrete frequency number: using the Wasserstein distance to measure the spectral gap between yodel (falsetto) and chest-voice singing at the same pitch gives 442 Hz; subtracting the pure pitch centroid shift (353 Hz) leaves 89 Hz of timbre change attributable purely to spectral shape. The authors apply the same measure to the clarinet's 'throat G' and its unison compatibility with flute, oboe, and bassoon, and working with an oboist they design 13 new alternate fingerings, actually locating the combination with the smallest timbre distance to the clarinet's throat G -- a concrete case of deriving a practical instrument-technique recommendation directly from a physical quantity, not a purely theoretical claim.

動機Motivation

荀白克在 1911 年的《和聲學》裡提出「音色旋律」(Klangfarbenmelodie)的構想,質疑西方音樂理論兩百年來只用「音高」(基頻,如 440 Hz)描述聲音,卻完全略過音色。他自己提出的十二音技法其實仍然只是音高序列,沒有真正回答「音色能不能像音高一樣被系統化」這個問題;二戰後的整體序列主義嘗試把音色、語音、空間性都納入可控參數,但受限於當時的類比訊號處理技術,加上 1980 年代數位化之後大家的注意力轉向 IRCAM 之類的實務演奏系統,這個理論問題被擱置了將近一個世紀。本文的動機就是回頭把荀白克這個問題,用 2020 年代的資訊幾何工具重新解決一次。

In his 1911 Harmonielehre, Schoenberg proposed the idea of Klangfarbenmelodie (melody of timbres), questioning why two centuries of Western music theory described sound only by pitch (fundamental frequency, e.g. 440 Hz) while ignoring timbre entirely. His own twelve-tone technique still only sequenced pitch and never actually answered whether timbre could be systematized the way pitch was; post-war total serialism tried to bring timbre, speech, and spatiality under control as parameters, but was limited by the analog signal processing of the time, and once digitization in the 1980s shifted attention toward practical performance systems like IRCAM's, the theoretical question was set aside for nearly a century. This paper's motivation is to return to Schoenberg's question and answer it with the tools of information geometry available in the 2020s.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音色空間量化不是新題目。Grey 1977 年用多維標度(MDS)把樂器音色依相似度映成心理空間,Krumhansl 1989 年接續做了類似的音色地圖研究,但這批研究測的是「聽感相似度」,依賴問卷收集的主觀評分,而且在 1990 年代數位合成器音質穩定後,這條研究線就因為需求下降而停滯。2010 年代機器學習興起後,Esling 與 Agon 用變分自編碼器(VAE)建構「生成式音色空間」,把既有樂器音色當成可以連續內插的「格點」,拿心理聲學上的音色相似度去正規化潛在空間——這條路線做得相當實用,可以連續變形出介於兩個樂器之間的新音色。

本文跟 Esling/Agon 最大的不同,是完全不需要先驗定義任何樂器的「代表音色格點」,而是直接拿頻譜當機率密度函數,用最優傳輸(Wasserstein 距離)算物理層的頻譜差異,不涉及任何心理量表或神經網路訓練——這犧牲了生成能力,換來的是每個數字都能回推到具體的頻率單位 [Hz],可以直接拿來給演奏者當指法選擇的參考。

Quantifying timbre space is not new. Grey (1977) used multidimensional scaling (MDS) to map instrumental timbres into a perceptual space by similarity, and Krumhansl (1989) continued that line with a similar timbre-mapping study, but both relied on subjective similarity ratings from listening surveys, and that research line stalled once digital synthesizer quality stabilized in the 1990s and reduced demand for it. With the rise of machine learning in the 2010s, Esling and Agon built a 'Generative Timbre Space' with a variational autoencoder (VAE), treating existing instrumental timbres as continuously interpolatable 'lattice points' and regularizing the latent space using psychoacoustic timbre similarity -- a quite practical line of work that can continuously morph a new timbre between two instruments.

This paper's biggest departure from Esling/Agon is that it never needs to predefine any instrument's 'representative lattice-point timbre'; instead it treats the spectrum directly as a probability density function and computes the physical spectral difference via optimal transport (Wasserstein distance), with no psychological rating scale or neural-network training involved -- trading away generative capability for the property that every number reduces back to a concrete frequency unit [Hz] that a performer can use directly as a fingering reference.

方法Method

作者把樂器音色的短時傅立葉頻譜正規化成機率密度函數,兩個頻譜之間定義一維 Wasserstein 距離 W1(公式為累積分佈函數差的積分),單位是 [Hz]。用約德爾(假聲)跟胸聲唱同一個音高做示範:兩者的頻譜質心分別是 2066 Hz 與 1713 Hz,質心位移 353 Hz;而 W1 距離算出來是 442 Hz。因為 W1 恆大於等於質心位移(論文附了完整證明),兩者的差 442 − 353 ≈ 89 Hz 就被定義成扣除單純音高變化後、純粹來自頻譜「形狀」改變的音色差異。

把單一樂器從最低音到最高音的每個音,兩兩算 Wasserstein 距離,就得到一個自距離矩陣;論文用持續同調(persistent homology,材料科學裡常見的拓樸分析工具)把這個矩陣視覺化成三維點雲,結果單簧管的「破裂音 G」清楚地跟其他音域的音色群聚分開來,呈現獨立一叢。三音和弦(如英國管 C、低音單簧管 E、低音管 G 疊在一起)則用三邊的 Wasserstein 距離,套海龍公式算出「Wasserstein 三角形」面積,當成三個聲部整體音色分離程度的指標;論文另外定義了一個「音色離散度」σ(三邊距離平方的均方根),兩種指標對同一批和弦排出的順序略有差異,可以互補使用。

實務應用上,作者跟雙簧管演奏者冨永誠司合作,為單簧管「破裂音 G」對應的 F 音設計了 13 種新指法,逐一算出跟單簧管音色的 Wasserstein 距離,找出相容性最高的組合;低音管部分則比較了維也納愛樂低音管首席 Stefan Turnovsky 傳統上使用的替代指法,確認其音色距離確實小於標準指法,驗證了這種口耳相傳的演奏技巧背後有客觀的物理依據。

The authors normalize each instrument's short-time Fourier spectrum into a probability density function and define a one-dimensional Wasserstein distance W1 between two spectra (an integral of the cumulative-distribution difference), with units of [Hz]. Demonstrating with yodel (falsetto) versus chest voice at the same pitch: the two spectral centroids are 2066 Hz and 1713 Hz, a centroid shift of 353 Hz, while W1 comes out to 442 Hz. Since W1 is always greater than or equal to the centroid shift (the paper includes a full proof), the difference, 442 − 353 ≈ 89 Hz, is defined as the timbre difference attributable purely to spectral 'shape' change, after subtracting plain pitch change.

Computing the pairwise Wasserstein distance between every note across an instrument's full range gives a self-distance matrix; the paper visualizes this matrix with persistent homology (a topological analysis tool common in materials science) as a 3D point cloud, and the clarinet's 'throat G' clearly separates into its own cluster apart from the other-register timbre groups. For a three-note chord (e.g. English horn C, bass clarinet E, and bassoon G stacked together), the three pairwise Wasserstein distances feed into Heron's formula to give the area of a 'Wasserstein triangle,' used as an index of how separated the three voices' timbres are overall; the paper also defines a 'timbre deviation' σ (the root-mean-square of the three squared distances), and the two indices rank the same set of chords slightly differently, so they're meant to be used as complements.

On the practical side, working with oboist Seiji Tominaga, the authors design 13 new fingerings for the F note that corresponds to the clarinet's 'throat G,' compute the Wasserstein distance to the clarinet timbre for each, and identify the most compatible combination; for bassoon, they compare the alternate fingering traditionally used by Vienna Philharmonic principal bassoonist Stefan Turnovsky and confirm its timbre distance is indeed smaller than the standard fingering, validating that this orally-transmitted performance technique has an objective physical basis.

限制Limitations

作者自己承認的限制不少。三聲部的「Wasserstein 三角形」用海龍公式算面積,是因為三角形三邊長一定能構成一個三角形(三角不等式保證);但一旦擴展到四聲部以上,四段任意長度的 Wasserstein 距離未必能構成一個合法的四面體(需要滿足格拉姆矩陣的條件),所以這套面積/體積指標本質上卡在三聲部,無法直接推廣到四聲部或更多聲部的和弦——論文說明這是數學上的限制而非工程限制。方法論上,這篇也完全沒有做任何聽覺實驗或問卷,所有「音色相容性」的判斷都是從頻譜距離直接推論,沒有驗證這套物理量跟人耳實際聽感的相關程度有多高;論文引用的先前研究(如 Grey、Krumhansl)恰好是走問卷評分這條路,兩者目前並未互相校準。此外,雙簧管的 13 個新指法與低音管的替代指法只在單一位演奏者、單一支樂器上測試,沒有跨樂器廠牌或跨演奏者重複驗證。

The authors acknowledge several limitations themselves. The three-voice 'Wasserstein triangle' area works because any three side lengths satisfy the triangle inequality and thus form a valid triangle; but extending to four or more voices runs into the fact that four arbitrary Wasserstein distances need not form a valid tetrahedron (they must satisfy Gram-matrix conditions), so this area/volume index is mathematically stuck at three voices and cannot be directly generalized -- the paper is explicit that this is a mathematical limit, not an engineering one.

Methodologically, the paper runs no listening experiment or survey at all; every 'timbre compatibility' judgment is inferred directly from spectral distance, with no check on how well this physical quantity correlates with actual human perception -- and the prior work it cites (Grey, Krumhansl) took exactly the survey-based route the paper does not attempt to calibrate against. In addition, the oboe's 13 new fingerings and the bassoon's alternate fingerings were tested on a single player and a single instrument each, with no cross-instrument or cross-player replication.

討論Discussion

最值得注意的討論,是論文結尾提出的「艾倫費斯特定理」類比:當 Wasserstein 指標都趨近於零(音色差異消失)時,這套「音色和聲學」的公式會退化回傳統古典和聲理論——就像量子力學在宏觀極限下退化回牛頓力學一樣。這個極限性質保證了這套框架不會跟古典理論互相矛盾,而只是在音色維度上把它擴展出去,這也是作者拿來論證這套方法可以套用到流行音樂、傳統音樂等各種曲風,而不只是西方古典管弦樂的理由。

另外值得留意的是論文明確定位自己跟 Esling/Agon 那條 VAE 路線是互補而非取代關係——後者面向生成與內插(適合作曲、音色設計),本文面向既有錄音的度量與診斷(適合合奏訓練、指法選擇),兩條路線分別回應荀白克三個問題裡不同的部分,論文最後也提到未來想用資訊幾何把「音序」「音色序」「語音作為音色動態變化」三個主題統一成一個系統,但這部分在本文只是展望,沒有具體推導。

The most notable point in the discussion is the 'Ehrenfest theorem' analogy the authors close with: as the Wasserstein indices all approach zero (timbre difference vanishes), this 'timbre harmony' framework degenerates back to classical harmony theory -- the same way quantum mechanics reduces to Newtonian mechanics in the macroscopic limit. This limiting property guarantees the framework never contradicts classical theory, only extends it along the timbre dimension, which is also the authors' argument for why the method should apply to pop and traditional music genres, not just Western classical orchestral writing.

Also worth noting is that the paper explicitly positions itself as complementary to, not a replacement for, the Esling/Agon VAE line -- that line targets generation and interpolation (useful for composing and sound design), while this paper targets measuring and diagnosing existing recordings (useful for ensemble training and fingering choice); the two lines answer different parts of Schoenberg's three questions. The paper closes by looking ahead to unifying 'pitch sequence,' 'timbre sequence,' and 'speech as dynamic timbre variation' into one information-geometric system, but that remains a stated aspiration here, without concrete derivation.
