Expressive Guitar Synthesis via Controllable Latent Flow Matching

以可控潛在流匹配實現的表情化吉他合成

Hsueh-Wei Fu, Yen-Tung Yeh, Yi-Hsuan Yang
arXiv:2610.36786 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-27
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇把吉他合成拆成兩段：先從逐弦 MIDI 預測每條弦的 f0 與音量包絡曲線，再用潛在流匹配（latent flow matching）在 Stable Audio Open 的潛在空間裡逐弦生成音訊，中間的曲線可以讓使用者直接改。在 GuitarSet 上，f0 平均誤差 26.52 cents（同架構但不經中間曲線的單段版 33.53、DDSP-Guitar 50.35），包絡相關係數 0.88（單段版 0.63）；32 人聽測四項都是合成系統中最高（整體偏好 3.04，DDSP-Guitar 2.62，真實錄音 3.91）。最值得記住的是一個副產品：零初始化的跨弦注意力閘門在第一段幾乎沒長大（[−0.04, 0.05]），只在合成段變大，跟 DDSP-Guitar 把跨弦互動放在預測段的設計正好相反。Why this one: This paper splits guitar synthesis into two stages: it first predicts per-string f0 and amplitude-envelope curves from string-wise MIDI, then renders each string in the Stable Audio Open latent space with latent flow matching, and the intermediate curves stay user-editable. On GuitarSet it reaches an f0 mean error of 26.52 cents (33.53 for a matched single-stage variant without the curves, 50.35 for DDSP-Guitar) and an envelope correlation of 0.88 (0.63 single-stage); in a 32-listener test it scores highest among synthesized systems on all four criteria (overall preference 3.04, DDSP-Guitar 2.62, real recordings 3.91). The finding most worth remembering is a by-product: the zero-initialized cross-string attention gates barely grow in the first stage ([−0.04, 0.05]) and only grow in the synthesizer, the opposite of DDSP-Guitar's choice to put string interaction in its predictor.

動機Motivation

MIDI 只說了「彈什麼」，沒說「怎麼彈」。對吉他來說，一個音的大部分表情在音符內部：推弦、滑音、顫音造成的連續音高偏移，以及每條弦各自的起音與衰減。直接把 MIDI 映射到音訊的模型只能把這些決定藏在網路裡，使用者想改一個音的推弦幅度也無從下手。

另一條路是像 VIOLET 那樣把演奏技巧標籤直接餵給模型，但推論時要使用者懂樂器，訓練時也需要真實錄音裡極少有的技巧標註。這篇要的是兩者之間：讓模型自己預測出一層可讀、可改的連續控制曲線，再用比 DDSP 更寫實的生成器把曲線變成聲音。

MIDI says what is played, not how. For guitar, much of a note's expression lives inside the note: the continuous pitch deviations of bends, slides and vibrato, and each string's own attack and decay. A model that maps MIDI straight to audio has to hide these decisions inside the network, and a user who wants a smaller bend on one note has nothing to adjust.

The other route, taken by VIOLET, feeds playing-technique labels to the model directly, but that asks the user to know the instrument at inference and needs technique annotations that real recordings rarely have. This paper wants something in between: let the model predict a readable, editable layer of continuous control curves, then turn those curves into sound with a generator more realistic than DDSP.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

「音符 → 連續表情曲線 → 音訊」的分層做法並不新。最直接的前身是 MIDI-DDSP（Wu 等人，ICLR 2022）：它從音符預測 f0 與響度曲線，再交給 DDSP 合成器，使用者可以在中間層改表情。DDSP-Guitar（Jonason 等人，DAFx 2024）把同樣的想法搬到逐弦吉他，並在預測段加了跨弦注意力；Wiggins 與 Kim（WASPAA 2023）則做了可微分的逐弦吉他模型。小提琴這邊，ViolinDiff（Kim 等人，ICASSP 2025）先預測逐音 pitch bend，再用擴散模型合成。

所以這篇最準確的描述是：MIDI-DDSP／DDSP-Guitar 的兩段式架構，把 DDSP 合成器換成在 Stable Audio Open 潛在空間上跑的 axial DiT 流匹配生成器，再加上兩段都有的閘控跨弦注意力和一個偽力度預測器。跟 DDSP-Guitar 的具體差別有兩點：合成段也讓各弦互相看見，而 DDSP-Guitar 的合成器是各弦獨立處理的；輸入不需要從錄音推得的力度。用時變曲線控制擴散模型這件事，也跟 Music ControlNet（Wu 等人，2024）以旋律、力度曲線控制文字生成音樂是同一類技巧，論文沒有提到這條線。

The layered recipe of notes to continuous expression curves to audio is not new. The most direct ancestor is MIDI-DDSP (Wu et al., ICLR 2022), which predicts f0 and loudness curves from notes and hands them to a DDSP synthesizer, letting users edit expression at the middle layer. DDSP-Guitar (Jonason et al., DAFx 2024) moved the same idea to string-wise guitar and added cross-string attention in its predictor; Wiggins and Kim (WASPAA 2023) built a differentiable string-wise guitar model. For violin, ViolinDiff (Kim et al., ICASSP 2025) predicts note-wise pitch bend and then synthesizes with diffusion.

So the most accurate description of this paper is: the MIDI-DDSP / DDSP-Guitar two-stage architecture with the DDSP synthesizer swapped for an axial-DiT flow-matching generator in the Stable Audio Open latent space, plus gated cross-string attention in both stages and a pseudo-velocity predictor. It differs from DDSP-Guitar in two concrete ways: strings also see each other in the synthesis stage, whereas DDSP-Guitar's synthesizer processes strings independently; and the input needs no velocity derived from the recording. Steering a diffusion model with time-varying curves is also the same family of technique as Music ControlNet (Wu et al., 2024), which controls text-to-music with melody and dynamics curves; the paper does not mention that line.

方法Method

輸入是逐弦（6 條弦）的影格層級符號表示，四個通道：音高、是否發聲、音符內相對位置、起音，外加每個音的力度。時間格與 Stable Audio Open（SAO）潛在表示對齊，hop 2048 樣本，約 21.53 Hz。

第一段是表演預測器：一個 axial transformer（256 維、6 層、8 頭），沿時間軸做自注意力，沿弦軸做跨弦注意力，每個跨弦區塊乘上一個零初始化的閘門，所以訓練從「各弦獨立」出發，只有真的有用才會打開。輸出是每條弦的 f0（以 CREPE 在 50–1100 Hz 抽取）與 log-RMS 包絡。損失是影格 L1、一階差分 L1，加上權重 1.3 的音符層級 L1（先在音符內平均，避免長音主導）。

第二段是合成器：一個 3 層的條件編碼器吃預測出的曲線，並且重新注入 MIDI 的發聲與起音資訊，讓模型能在第一段預測不可靠的地方自行修正；編碼結果透過 AdaLN 調變一個 8 層、384 維的 axial DiT，以 rectified flow 目標訓練，推論用 CFG 2.0、50 步 Euler。DiT 輸出每條弦的 SAO 潛在表示，經凍結的 SAO 解碼器變成逐弦音訊，再各自通過一個學出來的 0.25 秒 FIR 濾波器後相加，模擬麥克風收到的混音。

偽力度預測器是一個 2 層、64 維的小 transformer，只看音高、log 時值、前後 IOI 四個音符特徵，讓使用者不必逐音填力度。訓練資料是 GuitarSet，沿用 DDSP-Guitar 的錄音層級切分（306／18／36 段錄音）。

The input is a frame-level symbolic representation for each of the six strings with four channels (pitch, voiced state, relative position within the note, onset) plus a per-note velocity. The time grid matches the Stable Audio Open (SAO) latent rate, a hop of 2048 samples or about 21.53 Hz.

Stage one is a performance predictor: an axial transformer (256 dimensions, 6 layers, 8 heads) with self-attention along time and cross-string attention along the string axis. Each cross-string block is scaled by a zero-initialized gate, so training starts from independent strings and opens the gate only if it helps. It outputs per-string f0 (extracted with CREPE over 50–1100 Hz) and a log-RMS envelope. The loss is frame-wise L1, first-difference L1, and a note-level L1 weighted 1.3 that averages within each note first so long notes do not dominate.

Stage two is the synthesizer: a 3-layer conditioning encoder reads the predicted curves and re-injects the MIDI voiced and onset flags so the model can correct regions where stage one is unreliable. Its output modulates an 8-layer, 384-dimensional axial DiT through AdaLN, trained on a rectified-flow objective and sampled with CFG 2.0 and 50 Euler steps. The DiT produces per-string SAO latents, the frozen SAO decoder turns them into per-string audio, and each string passes through a learned 0.25 s FIR filter before summation to imitate the microphone mix.

The pseudo-velocity predictor is a small 2-layer, 64-dimensional transformer that reads only pitch, log duration and preceding and succeeding IOI, so users need not fill in velocity note by note. Training uses GuitarSet with DDSP-Guitar's recording-level split (306/18/36 recordings).

限制Limitations

主要比較用的是「oracle 力度」：這個力度是從真實錄音每個音的包絡峰值反推的，等於把一部分正確的動態答案交給了模型。換成只看 MIDI 的偽力度後，音高幾乎不變（f0 中位誤差 10.45 對 10.46 cents），但包絡 MAE 從 0.30 退到 0.61、包絡相關從 0.88 掉到 0.63，跟單段版一樣；而且這個版本沒有進聽測。所以「只給 MIDI 就能合成」有成立，但「合成得比基線好」主要是在 oracle 力度下量到的。

「MIDI-only」也要打折扣：輸入是逐弦 MIDI，也就是使用者必須先決定每個音在哪條弦上，一般的 MIDI 檔沒有這個資訊。訓練需要六弦分軌（hexaphonic）錄音，GuitarSet 只有木吉他、六位演奏者、約三小時，作者自己也把弦分離列為未來工作。

論文的賣點是「控制曲線可編輯」，但沒有任何實驗真的去改曲線（例如加一段顫音）再量輸出是否照做；即使給真實曲線，oracle 合成的 f0 MAE 仍有 15.32 cents。聽測是 18 段 8 秒片段分三組，每位受試者只聽 6 段，沒有報信賴區間，前三名系統的差距（整體偏好 3.04、2.62、2.54）無法判斷是否顯著。DDSP-Guitar 用的是公開預訓練模型而非重訓，它在 MSSL 上反而較好（8.27 對 8.79）。

The main comparison uses oracle velocity: velocity derived from the peak of each note's envelope in the real recording, which hands the model part of the correct dynamics. With MIDI-only pseudo-velocity, pitch is almost unchanged (f0 median error 10.45 vs 10.46 cents), but envelope MAE degrades from 0.30 to 0.61 and envelope correlation drops from 0.88 to 0.63, the single-stage level, and this variant was not in the listening test. So synthesis from MIDI alone works, but being better than the baselines was mostly measured with oracle velocity.

MIDI-only also needs a discount: the input is string-wise MIDI, so the user must first decide which string each note is on, information ordinary MIDI files lack. Training needs hexaphonic recordings, and GuitarSet is acoustic guitar only, six players, about three hours; the authors themselves list string separation as future work.

The selling point is editable control curves, yet no experiment actually edits a curve (say, adds vibrato) and measures whether the output follows; even with ground-truth curves, oracle synthesis still has an f0 MAE of 15.32 cents. The listening test uses 18 eight-second excerpts in three sets, each listener hears only 6, and no confidence intervals are reported, so whether the gaps among the top three systems (overall preference 3.04, 2.62, 2.54) are significant cannot be judged. DDSP-Guitar is the public pretrained model rather than a retrained one, and it is actually better on MSSL (8.27 vs 8.79).

討論Discussion

最乾淨的一組對照是單段版：同一個合成器，只是條件換成名目音高與力度。中間曲線讓 f0 MAE 從 33.53 降到 26.52 cents，包絡相關從 0.63 升到 0.88，聽測內容正確度 3.46 對 2.85。這是全文最能說明「先預測表演、再合成」本身有用、而不只是換了更強生成器的證據。

指標之間的矛盾值得注意。單段版控制指標全面贏過 DDSP-Guitar，聽測內容正確度卻比較低（2.85 對 3.09）；DDSP-Guitar MSSL 較好，聽感品質卻輸（2.92 對 3.10）。作者的解釋是影格層級指標抓不到漏音、節奏錯誤與局部雜音，而 MSSL 獎勵的是錄音特有的混音特性，這兩點都合理，但目前只是推測。

閘門的數值是全文最有意思的觀察：第一段的閘門停在 [−0.04, 0.05]，合成段的條件編碼器與 DiT 分別長到 [−0.17, 0.37] 與 [−0.40, 0.37]。消融也一致：拿掉 DiT 的跨弦注意力 MSSL 從 8.213 升到 8.363，全拿掉升到 8.525，而且多弦片段的退步（+0.255、+0.614）是單弦片段（+0.055、+0.158）的四倍左右。換句話說，弦與弦的耦合是聲學層面的事（共鳴、琴身），不是演奏決策層面的事。這跟 DDSP-Guitar 的設計正好相反，對之後做多聲部樂器合成的人是一條實用的線索。

The cleanest comparison is the single-stage variant: the same synthesizer conditioned on nominal pitch and velocity instead. The intermediate curves lower f0 MAE from 33.53 to 26.52 cents, raise envelope correlation from 0.63 to 0.88, and lift listener content correctness from 2.85 to 3.46. This is the paper's best evidence that predicting the performance first helps in itself, rather than just using a stronger generator.

The disagreements among metrics are worth noting. The single-stage variant beats DDSP-Guitar on every control metric yet gets lower content correctness (2.85 vs 3.09); DDSP-Guitar has the better MSSL yet loses on perceived audio quality (2.92 vs 3.10). The authors suggest that frame-level metrics miss dropped notes, rhythmic errors and local artifacts, and that MSSL rewards recording-specific mixing traits; both are plausible, but for now they are conjectures.

The gate values are the most interesting observation in the paper: stage-one gates stay within [−0.04, 0.05], while the synthesizer's conditioning encoder and DiT grow to [−0.17, 0.37] and [−0.40, 0.37]. The ablation agrees: removing cross-string attention from the DiT raises MSSL from 8.213 to 8.363, removing it everywhere raises it to 8.525, and the degradation in multi-string windows (+0.255, +0.614) is about four times that in single-string windows (+0.055, +0.158). In other words, string-to-string coupling is an acoustic matter (sympathetic resonance, the body), not a performance-decision matter. That is the opposite of DDSP-Guitar's design and a practical lead for anyone synthesizing other polyphonic instruments.

SMEBench: Benchmarking Neural Music Editing with Synthesized MIDI Edits

SMEBench：以合成 MIDI 編輯為基準評測神經音樂編輯

Matthieu Cervera, Francesco Paissan, Micael Antunes, Mirco Ravanelli, Ricard Marxer, Cem Subakan
arXiv:2610.36715 · cs.SD; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-27
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：音樂編輯的評測一直缺「正確答案長什麼樣」。這篇在 MIDICaps 的 MIDI 上做 12 種已知編輯（移除／替換／抽出／疊加樂器、斷奏、圓滑奏、漸強漸弱、修音準），用同一個 SoundFont 渲染出成對的來源與目標，得到 11,160 組配對、3,600 組平衡測試集，外加 416 組由 MoisesDB 真實分軌做成的 SME-Real。用它檢驗六個系統，文字—音訊分數挑出的冠軍和對照目標挑出的冠軍不同：以指令為條件時，CLAP-Text 最高的 VCI（0.266）在目標 CLAP-Audio 上是六個系統裡最低的（0.363，ZETA 0.633）。更刺眼的一點是附錄數字顯示的：原封不動回傳來源，跟目標的 CLAP-Audio 相似度是 0.873，比所有編輯系統都高（最好的 SteerMusic 0.691）。Why this one: Music-editing evaluation has always lacked a picture of the correct answer. This paper applies 12 known edits (remove, replace, extract or double an instrument, staccato, legato, crescendo and decrescendo, pitch correction) to MIDICaps MIDI and renders source and target with the same SoundFont, giving 11,160 pairs, a balanced 3,600-pair test set, and SME-Real, 416 pairs built from real MoisesDB stems. Testing six systems with it, the winner picked by text–audio score differs from the winner picked against the target: under instruction conditioning, VCI has the highest CLAP-Text (0.266) but the lowest target CLAP-Audio of all six systems (0.363, ZETA 0.633). A starker point shows in the appendix numbers: returning the source untouched scores 0.873 CLAP-Audio similarity to the target, higher than every editing system (best, SteerMusic, 0.691).

動機Motivation

評一個音樂編輯系統，要同時問三件事：要求的改變有沒有發生、不該動的東西有沒有保住、有沒有多出不該有的改變。現行做法通常只有來源音訊和一句文字，沒有「編輯後應該長怎樣」的參考。這會造成兩種盲點：跟來源比相似度，什麼都不改的系統會拿滿分；跟文字比 CLAP 分數，只看大方向語意，對某一軌的斷奏或漸強幾乎不敏感。

電腦視覺早就有附參考答案的編輯基準（EditVal、UltraEdit），音樂這邊還沒有。這篇要補的就是那個缺少的物件：一個跟來源只差一個已知操作、其餘音樂內容完全不變的配對目標。

Evaluating a music editor means asking three things at once: did the requested change happen, was what should stay untouched preserved, and were no unwanted changes added. Current practice usually has only the source audio and a sentence of text, with no reference for what the edited result should sound like. That leaves two blind spots: similarity to the source gives a perfect score to a system that changes nothing, and CLAP scores against the text capture broad semantics while barely reacting to staccato or a crescendo on one track.

Computer vision already has editing benchmarks with reference answers (EditVal, UltraEdit); music does not. This paper supplies the missing object: a paired target that differs from the source by one known operation while all other musical content stays fixed.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把既有資源分成兩類。有配對的：AUDIT（Wang 等人，2023）與 InstructME（Han 等人，2023）的目標是靠混音、分離、遮罩或分軌操作得到的，碰不到演奏本身。沒配對的：MedleyMDPrompts（ZETA 一文，Manor 與 Michaeli，2024）和 ZoME-Bench（MEDIC 一文）用真實錄音配上人寫的來源／目標描述，真實但沒有目標音訊。

跟 SMEBench 最像的其實是論文沒提到的一條線：Slakh2100（Manilow 等人，2019）用取樣音源渲染 MIDI 得到成對分軌，Instruct-MusicGen（Zhang 等人，2024）就是用 Slakh 分軌組出加入／移除／抽出樂器的訓練三元組。SMEBench 的樂器類編輯本質上是同一套「渲染 MIDI 得到配對」的技巧；真正新的部分是斷奏、圓滑奏、力度與音準這些作用在演奏層級的編輯、四種文字描述，以及把配對目標拿來診斷指標本身。

受測系統方面，SDEdit、DDIM inversion、ZETA、VCI（Cervera 等人，2025，同一作者群）與 SteerMusic 都是在 AudioLDM2-music 上做零樣本編輯，MusicGen 當作純文字生成基線；較新的 MusicMagus 之類零樣本方法沒有納入。

The paper sorts existing resources into two kinds. Paired: the targets of AUDIT (Wang et al., 2023) and InstructME (Han et al., 2023) come from mixing, separation, masking or stem manipulation and never touch the performance itself. Unpaired: MedleyMDPrompts (from the ZETA paper, Manor and Michaeli, 2024) and ZoME-Bench (from the MEDIC paper) pair real recordings with hand-written source and target descriptions, realistic but with no target audio.

The closest relative of SMEBench is actually a line the paper does not mention: Slakh2100 (Manilow et al., 2019) renders MIDI with sample libraries to get paired stems, and Instruct-MusicGen (Zhang et al., 2024) used Slakh stems to build add, remove and extract training triplets. SMEBench's instrumentation edits are essentially the same render-MIDI-to-get-pairs technique; what is genuinely new is the performance-level edits (staccato, legato, dynamics, tuning), the four text views, and using paired targets to diagnose the metrics themselves.

Among the tested systems, SDEdit, DDIM inversion, ZETA, VCI (Cervera et al., 2025, the same group) and SteerMusic all edit zero-shot on AudioLDM2-music, with MusicGen as a text-only generation baseline; newer zero-shot methods such as MusicMagus are not included.

方法Method

來源是 MIDICaps 的 168,385 個 MIDI。每個樣本隨機切 10 秒，先檢查哪些編輯適用：不存在的樂器不能移除，抽出後幾乎沒聲音的就丟掉。12 種操作分四族：樂器類（移除、替換、抽出、疊加樂器；移除、替換、抽出鼓）、奏法（斷奏把時值設為 IOI 的 40% 並限制在 40–120 ms；圓滑奏延長 15% IOI 並加 CC68 等控制訊息）、力度（漸強、漸弱）、音準（來源每個音隨機偏移 ±50 cents，目標回到 A4=440 的十二平均律）。有意思的是力度：直接改 MIDI 力度聽起來不好，所以改在渲染後的音訊上乘增益包絡。

要編輯哪一軌不是均勻抽，而是依「感知使用量」加權（活躍時間乘上力度平方，依 Dannenberg 2006 的力度—RMS 關係），讓顯著的聲部較常被選中。來源與目標用同一個 SoundFont（五套中隨機一套）以 FluidSynth 渲染成 22.05 kHz 單聲道，再用 LPAPS 與 CLAP 相似度的上下界（取第 5 百分位）去掉沒變化和變太多的配對，並設 Audiobox Aesthetics 下限。

文字端有四種：來源描述、編輯後描述（只改受影響的部分）、由範本產生的指令、列出樂器的標準化描述。描述由 Qwen3-30B-A3B 根據從 MIDI 與音訊抽出的結構化特徵生成，再由 LLM 評審過濾。結果共 11,160 組，測試集每種操作 300 組共 3,600 組；SME-Real 從 107 首 MoisesDB 歌曲以分軌組合、音高與節奏相近的分軌互換、增益包絡做出 416 組。

The source is MIDICaps' 168,385 MIDI files. Each sample is a random 10-second crop, checked first for which edits apply: an absent instrument cannot be removed, and an extraction that leaves almost nothing is discarded. The 12 operations fall into four families: instrumentation (remove, replace, extract or double an instrument; remove, replace or extract drums), articulation (staccato sets durations to 40% of the IOI clipped to 40–120 ms; legato extends notes by 15% of the IOI and adds CC68 and related messages), dynamics (crescendo, decrescendo) and pitch (every source note is detuned uniformly within ±50 cents, the target returns to equal temperament at A4 = 440). Dynamics is telling: editing MIDI velocity sounded poor, so a gain envelope is applied to the rendered audio instead.

The edited track is not drawn uniformly but weighted by perceived usage (active time times squared velocity, following Dannenberg's 2006 velocity–RMS relation), so prominent parts are chosen more often. Source and target are rendered with the same SoundFont (one of five, chosen at random) through FluidSynth at 22.05 kHz mono, then pairs that barely change or change too much are removed with lower and upper LPAPS and CLAP bounds set at the 5th percentile, plus a minimum Audiobox Aesthetics score.

On the text side there are four views: a source caption, an edited caption that changes only the affected content, a template-generated instruction, and a normalized caption listing the instruments. Captions are generated by Qwen3-30B-A3B from structured features extracted from MIDI and audio and filtered by an LLM judge. The result is 11,160 pairs, a test set of 300 per operation for 3,600 total, and SME-Real, 416 pairs from 107 MoisesDB songs built from stem subsets, swaps between stems with similar pitch and rhythm, and gain envelopes.

限制Limitations

最根本的問題，作者自己也承認：編輯不是一對一的。「把弦樂換成銅管」有無數種正確做法，配對目標只是其中一種，所以「離目標近」量的是跟這一個實現的一致，不是編輯是否正確。這在樂器替換、疊加這類開放操作上影響最大。

人工驗證規模小：17 位受試者（只有 9 位做完 20 題），共 236 次評分。指令符合度平均 3.99（95% CI [3.72, 4.23]），13.2% 的評分是差或很差；圓滑奏（3.33）、移除樂器（3.27）、替換鼓（3.00）三種操作沒有穩定超過量表中點。移除樂器是最基本的編輯卻排在後面，可能代表即使依使用量加權選軌，被移除的聲部仍常常聽不太出來。這三種被排除在主要感知平均之外，但仍留在基準裡，用總分比較系統時要注意。

描述的生成與過濾用同一家族的 Qwen 模型。附錄拿 Mistral 當獨立評審，8,461 則描述上兩者一致率 69.8%、Cohen's κ 0.41，Qwen 接受 44.0%、Mistral 接受 62.6%，可見過濾結果相當依賴選哪個評審。還有一個隱性偏差：資料建構時就用 CLAP 與 LPAPS 的界限過濾，又用同樣的 CLAP 與 LPAPS 來評系統。最後，FluidSynth 渲染的 22.05 kHz 單聲道和真實錄音差距不小，416 組的 SME-Real 只涵蓋樂器與力度類編輯。

The most fundamental issue, which the authors concede: editing is not one-to-one. Replacing strings with brass has countless valid realizations and the paired target is one of them, so closeness to the target measures agreement with this one realization, not whether the edit is correct. This matters most for open-ended operations such as replacement and doubling.

Human validation is small: 17 participants (only 9 finished all 20 trials), 236 ratings in all. Mean instruction alignment is 3.99 (95% CI [3.72, 4.23]) and 13.2% of ratings are bad or very bad; legato (3.33), remove instrument (3.27) and replace drums (3.00) do not reliably clear the scale midpoint. Instrument removal is the most basic edit yet sits near the bottom, which suggests that even with usage-weighted track selection the removed part is often hard to hear. These three are excluded from the headline perceptual average but stay in the benchmark, which matters when systems are compared on aggregate scores.

Captions are generated and filtered by the same Qwen model family. The appendix uses Mistral as an independent judge: on 8,461 captions the two agree 69.8% of the time with Cohen's κ of 0.41, Qwen accepting 44.0% and Mistral 62.6%, so the filter's output depends heavily on which judge is used. There is also a quieter bias: construction filters on CLAP and LPAPS bounds, and the same CLAP and LPAPS then score the systems. Finally, FluidSynth renders at 22.05 kHz mono are far from real recordings, and the 416-pair SME-Real covers only instrumentation and dynamics edits.

討論Discussion

排名翻轉是主要結果，數字也撐得住。以編輯後描述為條件時，CLAP-Text 冠軍是 MusicGen（0.350），但它在目標 CLAP-Audio（0.492）與目標 LPAPS（5.926）上都是最差；兩個目標指標都選 SteerMusic（0.691、4.482）。以指令為條件時，CLAP-Text 冠軍 VCI（0.266）的目標 CLAP-Audio 只有 0.363，是六個系統裡最低，目標指標選的是 ZETA（0.633、4.954）。而且 MusicGen 的 CLAP-Text 0.350 比真正的目標音訊本身（0.278）還高：一個完全不看來源的生成器，在文字對齊上贏過正確答案。

我覺得附錄表 5 有一個比排名翻轉更重要、論文卻沒正面講的數字：Identity（什麼都不做）跟目標的 CLAP-Audio 是 0.873、LPAPS 是 1.513，所有編輯系統都在 0.36–0.69 與 4.48–6.10 之間。也就是說，在這兩個指標下，沒有任何系統比「不編輯」更接近正確答案。這暗示 AudioLDM2 反演重建本身的損失遠大於編輯帶來的差異，系統之間的目標指標差距，可能主要是在比誰的重建比較不糟。要區分這兩者，需要一個「反演後不編輯」的對照組，論文沒有報。

另一個實用發現是 Audiobox Aesthetics：正確的目標音訊（6.734）分數比來源（6.903）還低，真實資料上也一樣（6.952 對 7.247），所以用美學分數當品質指標，會系統性地懲罰真的有做編輯的輸出。整體來說，這份基準最大的價值不在排出系統名次，而在提供一把能檢驗指標本身的尺。

The ranking reversal is the main result, and the numbers back it. Under edited-caption conditioning, the CLAP-Text winner is MusicGen (0.350), yet it is worst on target CLAP-Audio (0.492) and target LPAPS (5.926); both target metrics pick SteerMusic (0.691, 4.482). Under instruction conditioning, the CLAP-Text winner VCI (0.266) has a target CLAP-Audio of only 0.363, the lowest of all six systems, and the target metrics pick ZETA (0.633, 4.954). Moreover, MusicGen's CLAP-Text of 0.350 is higher than that of the true target audio itself (0.278): a generator that never looks at the source beats the correct answer on text alignment.

I think appendix Table 5 holds a number more important than the reversals that the paper never states head-on: Identity (doing nothing) scores 0.873 CLAP-Audio and 1.513 LPAPS against the target, while every editing system lies between 0.36–0.69 and 4.48–6.10. Under these two metrics, no system gets closer to the correct answer than not editing. This suggests that the reconstruction loss of AudioLDM2 inversion far outweighs the difference the edit makes, and the target-metric gaps between systems may mostly compare whose reconstruction is less bad. Separating the two would need an invert-without-editing control, which the paper does not report.

Another practical finding concerns Audiobox Aesthetics: the correct target audio (6.734) scores lower than the source (6.903), and the same holds on real data (6.952 vs 7.247), so using the aesthetics score as a quality metric systematically penalizes outputs that actually perform the edit. Overall, the benchmark's main value is not ranking systems but providing a ruler for testing the metrics themselves.

Gangqinpu: A Multi-Genre, Multi-Task Piano Dataset with Aligned Audio, MIDI, and Musical Scores

Gangqinpu（鋼琴譜）：對齊音訊、MIDI 與樂譜的多曲風、多任務鋼琴資料集

Wei Zeng, Anvay Mathur, Ye Wang
arXiv:2610.34694 · cs.SD; cs.AI; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-26
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：同時對齊音訊、演奏 MIDI 與樂譜的鋼琴資料一直以古典為主，最常用的 ASAP 只有 1,067 段演奏。這篇從網路收集鋼琴錄音與對應樂譜，用 Transkun v2 自動轉譜、DualDTWNoteMatcher 與 RAScoP 做音符層級對齊，做出 18,786 組配對、1,007.1 小時、涵蓋流行、古典、爵士等曲風的資料集，並用一個八特徵的分類器濾掉照譜直接合成的「死板」演奏（專家標註保留集準確率 98.61%）。加入訓練後，Beat This! 在 ASAP 測試集的拍點 F1 也從 57.69 升到 61.98；但轉譜與表情演奏生成的進步主要只在 Gangqinpu 自己的測試集上，ASAP 上的表情演奏生成三項誤差反而都變大。Why this one: Piano datasets aligning audio, performance MIDI and scores at once have been mostly classical, and the most used, ASAP, has only 1,067 performances. This paper collects piano recordings and matching scores from the web, transcribes them with Transkun v2, aligns notes with DualDTWNoteMatcher and RAScoP, and produces 18,786 pairs and 1,007.1 hours spanning pop, classical, jazz and more, filtering out deadpan renderings synthesized straight from the score with an eight-feature classifier (98.61% accuracy on an expert-labeled held-out set). Adding it to training lifts Beat This! beat F1 on the ASAP test set from 57.69 to 61.98 as well; but gains in transcription and expressive rendering are mainly on Gangqinpu's own test set, and on ASAP all three rendering errors actually grow.

動機Motivation

一首鋼琴曲有三種互補的表示：音訊是聲音的實現，演奏 MIDI 記錄每個音的時間與力度，樂譜記錄作曲結構。把三者對齊，可以同時支撐轉譜、演奏 MIDI 轉樂譜、表情演奏生成與拍點追蹤等任務。問題是現有資料集大多只有其中兩種；ASAP 三種都有，但 1,067 段演奏裡只有 519 段有音訊，而且全是西方古典。

古典以外的需求其實更大：流行與爵士常常只有簡譜、即興或非正式編曲，所以從錄音轉譜特別有用；表情演奏生成若能用在流行樂，也能把 DAW 裡量化過的 MIDI 變得像真人彈的。這些應用都需要更大、曲風更廣的對齊資料。

A piano piece has three complementary representations: audio is the acoustic realization, performance MIDI records each note's timing and velocity, and the score records the compositional structure. Aligning all three supports transcription, performance-MIDI-to-score conversion, expressive rendering and beat tracking at once. The trouble is that most datasets have only two of the three; ASAP has all three, but only 519 of its 1,067 performances come with audio, and all are Western classical.

The need beyond classical is arguably larger: pop and jazz often have only lead sheets, improvisation or informal arrangements, so transcribing from recordings is especially useful, and expressive rendering applied to pop could make quantized MIDI in a DAW sound human-played. All of these need larger aligned data covering more genres.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

演奏 MIDI 的取得有兩條路。直接錄製：MAESTRO（Hawthorne 等人，2019）用 Disklavier 錄了 198.7 小時，但需要專門設備。自動轉譜：ATEPP（Zhang 等人，2022）轉了職業鋼琴家的錄音，Aria-MIDI 擴展到上百萬個網路錄音，論文沒提到的 GiantMIDI-Piano（Kong 等人，2022）也是同一做法。有樂譜對齊的：ASAP 與 (n)ASAP 提供拍點與音符層級對齊，ACPAS 混合真實與合成音訊，PianoCoRe 把既有語料整合成大型的音符對齊集合；POP909 有流行曲的拍點與和弦，但沒有對應獨立樂譜的鋼琴錄音。

從技術上看，Gangqinpu 最接近的是 ATEPP 的配方（網路錄音 + 自動轉譜 + 部分樂譜），再加上 PianoCoRe 的 RAScoP 對齊精修，套用到一個非古典為主的網路來源。真正新的是規模與曲風：15,645 個樂譜群組、1,007 小時，以及表情性過濾和跨資料集的防洩漏切分。爵士方向有一個論文沒比較的前例 PiJAMA（Edwards 等人，2023），它提供兩百多小時的爵士鋼琴轉譜，但沒有樂譜；這正好凸顯 Gangqinpu 的差別在於有樂譜對齊。

Performance MIDI comes two ways. Direct capture: MAESTRO (Hawthorne et al., 2019) recorded 198.7 hours on Disklaviers but needs specialized equipment. Automatic transcription: ATEPP (Zhang et al., 2022) transcribed professional pianists' recordings, Aria-MIDI scaled to over a million web recordings, and GiantMIDI-Piano (Kong et al., 2022), which the paper does not mention, did the same. With score alignment: ASAP and (n)ASAP provide beat- and note-level alignment, ACPAS mixes real and synthesized audio, and PianoCoRe consolidates existing corpora into a large note-aligned collection; POP909 has beats and chords for pop songs but no piano recordings paired with independent scores.

Technically, Gangqinpu is closest to the ATEPP recipe (web recordings plus AMT plus partial scores) with PianoCoRe's RAScoP alignment refinement, applied to a web source that is not mainly classical. What is genuinely new is scale and genre: 15,645 score groups and 1,007 hours, plus the expressiveness filter and the cross-dataset leakage-aware split. On the jazz side there is a precedent the paper does not compare against, PiJAMA (Edwards et al., 2023), with over two hundred hours of transcribed jazz piano but no scores; that contrast highlights that Gangqinpu's difference is the score alignment.

方法Method

流程有四道過濾。收集網路上的鋼琴錄音與樂譜後，先保留至少 60 秒的錄音（過濾 1）與至少 32 小節、純鋼琴的樂譜（過濾 2）。錄音以 Transkun v2 轉成演奏 MIDI，樂譜轉成 MusicXML 後用 SyMuPe 與 Partitura 展開反覆記號、產生樂譜 MIDI。選 Transkun v2 是因為它能分開表示音符結束與延音踏板，不會把踏板延長算進音長。

對齊先用 Parangonar 的 DualDTWNoteMatcher（作者引用的比較是音符匹配 F1 99.0%，Nakamura 法 98.5%），再以 RAScoP 精修，精修時補出的音符會另外標記。原始對齊的插入率或刪除率任一超過 10% 就丟掉（過濾 3）。拍點從樂譜的節拍格經對應音符轉到演奏時間，取鄰近音符演奏起音的中位數；每個拍點帶一個啟發式信心值（有觀測音符 0.95、全是補出的 0.40、內插再乘 0.75、外插 0.25），低於 0.50 標為低信心、低於 0.35 標為模糊，供下游遮罩。

表情性過濾（過濾 4）是一個 MLP（32 與 16 個隱藏單元），輸入八個從樂譜—演奏對應算出的特徵：局部起音殘差的標準差、一階自相關、熵，和弦內起音差，力度標準差與種類數，上下譜表力度差，和弦最高音與其他音的力度差。標註來自兩位專家反覆聆聽達成共識的 779 段表情與 370 段非表情演奏，再加上 982 段 ASAP 當正例、加噪的樂譜渲染當負例。曲風先由 Qwen-Flash 看標題與上傳者資訊判斷，判不出來再用 LAION-CLAP 聽音訊。最後以移調不變的音程模式找出相似樂譜，跟 ASAP 一起分群後切分，訓練／驗證／測試為 17,773／499／514。

The pipeline has four filters. After collecting web piano recordings and scores, it keeps recordings of at least 60 seconds (filter 1) and piano-only scores of at least 32 measures (filter 2). Recordings are transcribed to performance MIDI with Transkun v2, and scores are converted to MusicXML and processed with SyMuPe and Partitura to unfold repeats and produce a score MIDI. Transkun v2 is chosen because it represents note offsets separately from the sustain pedal instead of extending notes by pedal.

Alignment starts with Parangonar's DualDTWNoteMatcher (the cited comparison gives 99.0% note-matching F1 versus 98.5% for Nakamura's method) and is refined with RAScoP, with notes interpolated during refinement flagged. A pair is dropped if raw insertion or deletion rate exceeds 10% (filter 3). Beats are mapped from the score's metrical grid to performance time through the note correspondences, taking the median performed onset of nearby notes; each beat carries a heuristic confidence (0.95 with an observed note, 0.40 if all are interpolated, times 0.75 when interpolated, 0.25 when extrapolated), with below 0.50 marked low-confidence and below 0.35 ambiguous, for masking downstream.

The expressiveness filter (filter 4) is an MLP with hidden layers of 32 and 16 units over eight features computed from score–performance correspondences: standard deviation, lag-one autocorrelation and entropy of local onset residuals, within-chord onset spread, velocity standard deviation and number of distinct velocities, upper- versus lower-staff velocity contrast, and the velocity gap between a chord's top note and the rest. Labels come from 779 expressive and 370 non-expressive performances agreed on by two experts through repeated listening, plus 982 ASAP performances as positives and noise-perturbed score renderings as negatives. Genre is first judged by Qwen-Flash from title and uploader metadata, then by LAION-CLAP on the audio if undetermined. Finally, similar scores are found with transposition-invariant interval patterns and grouped jointly with ASAP before splitting, giving 17,773/499/514 train/validation/test.

限制Limitations

所有標註都是自動產生的，沒有人工稽核（作者把它列為未來工作）。這帶來一個評測上的循環：Gangqinpu 測試集的演奏 MIDI 與拍點，本身就是 Transkun 加上這條對齊流程的產物。所以「在 Gangqinpu 上進步很多」有一部分可能是模型學會了這條流程的慣例與錯誤型態，而不只是學會了音樂。真正的外部驗證只有 ASAP，而 ASAP 上的結果是混合的。

ASAP 測試集很小：Beat This! 只有 26 段，PM2S 與 S2A 是 59–61 段，全部沒有信賴區間，4 個百分點的 F1 提升在這個樣本數下是否穩定無法判斷。基線是只用 ASAP 從頭訓練的模型，拍點 F1 只有 57.69，遠低於公開版 Beat This! 的水準，所以這裡證明的是「有比沒有好」，不是能推進目前最好的系統。

資料來源也交代得不清楚：論文只說是「網路上公開的鋼琴錄音與樂譜」，沒有說是哪個網站，也沒有任何授權或著作權的討論，音訊只以網址加下載腳本提供，日後可能失效。「表情演奏」的定義刻意不管來源，經人工編輯的 MIDI 渲染也算，所以這不等於真人演奏。分類器也有明顯弱點：80 ms 隨機抖動的合成渲染只有 30.8% 被判為非表情，代表它主要在量「有多不規則」，而不是「像不像音樂上的處理」。曲風標籤由 LLM 看標題判斷，也沒有驗證。

All annotations are automatic with no manual audit (the authors list it as future work). That creates an evaluation loop: the performance MIDI and beats in Gangqinpu's test set are themselves products of Transkun plus this alignment pipeline. So part of the large in-domain gain may be models learning the pipeline's conventions and error patterns rather than music. The only truly external check is ASAP, and results there are mixed.

The ASAP test sets are small: 26 pieces for Beat This!, 59–61 for PM2S and S2A, none with confidence intervals, so whether a 4-point F1 gain is stable at this size cannot be judged. The baseline is a model trained from scratch on ASAP alone with a beat F1 of only 57.69, far below the published Beat This!, so what is shown is that having the data beats not having it, not that it advances the best current system.

The data source is also poorly specified: the paper says only publicly accessible piano recordings and sheet music online, without naming the site, and there is no discussion of licensing or copyright; audio is provided only as URLs with a download script, which may rot. The definition of expressive deliberately ignores provenance and admits hand-edited MIDI renderings, so it is not the same as human performance. The classifier has a clear weakness too: synthetic renderings with 80 ms random jitter are flagged non-expressive only 30.8% of the time, meaning it mostly measures irregularity rather than musically shaped deviation. Genre labels come from an LLM reading titles and are not validated.

討論Discussion

拍點追蹤是唯一跨資料集都成立的進步。在 ASAP 測試集，拍點 F1 從 57.69 升到 61.98、強拍 F1 從 43.23 升到 47.55，CMLt 從 21.66 到 28.57；在 Gangqinpu 測試集，拍點 F1 從 60.18 到 78.16、強拍從 39.60 到 63.66。這合理：拍點標註來自樂譜節拍格，比音符層級的表情資訊更不受轉譜誤差影響。

另外兩個任務要分開看。PM2S 在 Gangqinpu 上大多進步（拍點標籤 +14.02、強拍 +20.95、量化起音 +18.87、音值 +19.32），但拍號 macro-F1 反而從 51.00 降到 45.43；在 ASAP 上，拍點、強拍、音值變好，左右手分配（86.65→84.81）、調號（36.26→30.87）、拍號、量化起音（53.59→49.71）都變差。S2A 表情演奏生成在 Gangqinpu 上三項誤差都下降（力度 MAE 17.50→13.05、IOI 78.51→54.66 ms），在 ASAP 上三項都上升（音長 MAE 999.66→1,058.28 ms）。作者對此寫得很節制，把結論定位成「主要是領域內的效益」，這是對的。

表情性分類器的對照實驗值得一提：SyMuPe 品質分類器把 93 段非表情演奏中的 76 段判成高品質，GigaMIDI 的 NOMML 則把每一段都判成表情，合併準確率 80.71% 與 82.58% 只是反映類別比例，macro-F1 只有 56.88% 與 45.23%（本文 98.70%）。這說明「照譜機械渲染」在網路樂譜資料裡是真實存在、又容易被既有工具漏掉的污染。對想用網路 MIDI 或轉譜資料訓練表情模型的人，這個過濾器本身可能比資料集更有用。

Beat tracking is the only gain that holds across datasets. On the ASAP test set, beat F1 rises from 57.69 to 61.98, downbeat F1 from 43.23 to 47.55 and CMLt from 21.66 to 28.57; on Gangqinpu's test set, beat F1 goes from 60.18 to 78.16 and downbeat from 39.60 to 63.66. That makes sense: beat labels come from the score's metrical grid and are less exposed to transcription error than note-level expressive detail.

The other two tasks need to be read separately. PM2S improves on most Gangqinpu targets (beat labels +14.02, downbeats +20.95, quantized onsets +18.87, note values +19.32), but time-signature macro-F1 drops from 51.00 to 45.43; on ASAP, beats, downbeats and note values improve while hand assignment (86.65 to 84.81), key signature (36.26 to 30.87), time signature and quantized onsets (53.59 to 49.71) all get worse. S2A expressive rendering lowers all three errors on Gangqinpu (velocity MAE 17.50 to 13.05, IOI 78.51 to 54.66 ms) and raises all three on ASAP (duration MAE 999.66 to 1,058.28 ms). The authors are restrained here, framing the result as primarily in-domain benefit, which is right.

The classifier comparison is worth noting: the SyMuPe quality classifier labels 76 of 93 non-expressive performances as high quality, and GigaMIDI's NOMML labels every sample expressive, so their pooled accuracies of 80.71% and 82.58% merely reflect class balance, with macro-F1 of only 56.88% and 45.23% (98.70% here). This shows that mechanical score renderings are a real contaminant in web score data and one that existing tools miss. For anyone training expressive models on web MIDI or transcribed data, the filter itself may be more useful than the dataset.
