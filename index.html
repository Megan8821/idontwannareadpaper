Do Music Generative Models Understand Musical Qualities? Automatic Music Evaluation with Model-Intrinsic Signals

音樂生成模型懂得音樂品質嗎?以模型內在訊號做自動音樂評估

Xiaosha Li, Chun Liu, Ziyu Wang
arXiv:2609.37710 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-29
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇不看音訊、只讀 MusicGen 自己的逐 token loss、預測熵與 SAE 潛變數,就預測人類評分:五個基準合併後 Pearson r 為 0.81(large 模型 0.82),而 Meta Audiobox Aesthetics 最好的一軸只有 0.20,平均 loss 為 −0.08。Why this one: It ignores the audio and reads only MusicGen's own per-token loss, predictive entropy and SAE latents to predict human ratings: pooled over five benchmarks Pearson r is 0.81 (0.82 with the large model), versus 0.20 for the best Meta Audiobox Aesthetics axis and −0.08 for mean loss.

動機Motivation

生成式音樂模型進步很快,但沒有人聽的時候怎麼判斷輸出好不好,仍是開放問題。現有的自動評估(音訊美學評分器、音樂指令基準的獎勵模型)都只看輸出的波形,把音樂模型當黑盒,只能自動化「打分」這一步,說不出人為什麼給高分或低分。

作者借用音樂認知的兩個說法:重複接觸塑造偏好(mere exposure effect),以及聆聽時不斷形成預期、被驚訝所影響(Huron 的音樂期待理論)。自回歸音樂模型剛好有對應物:訓練見過的資料,和下一個 token 的機率分佈。於是問題變成:模型能不能只靠自己的內部訊號,判斷自己的輸出人類會打幾分?

Generative music models have advanced fast, yet judging their output without human listeners remains open. Existing automatic evaluators (audio aesthetics predictors, reward models built on music-instruction benchmarks) look only at the output waveform and treat the music model as a black box. They automate the rating step but cannot say why a listener rates one clip high and another low.

The authors borrow two ideas from music cognition: repeated exposure shapes preference (the mere exposure effect), and listening continually forms predictions whose violations carry affect (Huron's theory of musical expectation). An autoregressive music model has counterparts of both: the data it was trained on, and its next-token distribution. The question becomes whether a model can judge how humans would rate its own output using only its internal signals.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名的基準有兩類。評分預測側有 Meta Audiobox Aesthetics(從波形預測製作與內容四軸)、CMI-Bench 及其獎勵模型版 CMI-RewardBench。人評資料側有 MusicEval、SongEval、MusicPref、AIME 和 Music Arena(後者類似 Chatbot Arena 的成對對戰)。這些都只從音訊判斷,作者的差異在於輸入換成生成器自己的內部訊號。

最接近的是同一組作者先前的工作(Li 等人,arXiv 2602.02738):在音樂 LLM 上發現平均 loss 和人評的音樂性不對齊,有訊號的是 loss 隨時間的形狀。本篇把它擴成 loss 加熵加 SAE 三種訊號。SAE 的部分則完全沿用 Singh、Cherep 與 Maes(ICLR 2026)在 MusicGen 上訓練的音樂 SAE,不重新訓練,只是把他們用來「轉向生成」的潛變數改拿去做評估。

方法層面其實是在凍結模型的特徵上訓練一個小型迴歸頭,屬於 probing 的做法;新意主要在訊號選擇與五個基準統一的評估協定,而不是新的架構。

The paper names two families of prior work. On the predictor side: Meta Audiobox Aesthetics (four production and content axes from the waveform) and CMI-Bench with its reward-model variant CMI-RewardBench. On the human-data side: MusicEval, SongEval, MusicPref, AIME and Music Arena (head-to-head battles in the style of Chatbot Arena). All judge from audio alone; the difference here is that the input is the generator's own internal signals.

The closest is the same authors' earlier work (Li et al., arXiv 2602.02738), which found that mean loss does not align with human-rated musicality in music LLMs and that the shape of the loss curve over time carries the signal. This paper extends that to loss, entropy and SAE latents. The SAE is the music SAE of Singh, Cherep and Maes (ICLR 2026) trained on MusicGen, reused without retraining; their latents were used to steer generation, here they are repurposed for evaluation.

Mechanically this is a small regression head trained on frozen-model features, i.e. probing. The novelty lies in the choice of signals and the unified protocol over five benchmarks, not in a new architecture.

方法Method

對一段音訊,在 teacher forcing 下用 MusicGen 取三種逐步訊號:真實 token 的負對數似然(loss)、下一步分佈的 Shannon 熵、以及第 12 層隱藏狀態經 SAE 得到的 4096 維稀疏潛變數。loss 和熵各有四個 codebook 加一個有效遮罩,時間軸統一池化到 1500 格。

預測模型是三條分支:每個訊號一個 1D-CNN 編碼器,輸出各 128 維,後端串接,再接兩層 MLP 輸出 1 到 5 的評分,全模型約 124 萬參數(約 84% 在 SAE 壓縮層)。訓練用單一 MSE 目標,Adam、學習率 1e-4。

五個基準的監督格式不同。MusicEval 與 SongEval 直接取專家平均分;MusicPref、AIME、Music Arena 只有成對勝負,作者用 Elo 轉成每個片段的連續分數,AIME 每片段有 12 場對戰可直接估計,另兩者每片段只有一場,所以先估系統層級的 Elo,再加上單場調整。

For each clip, three per-step signals are extracted from MusicGen under teacher forcing: the negative log-likelihood of the true token (loss), the Shannon entropy of the next-step distribution, and 4096-dimensional sparse SAE latents from layer-12 hidden states. Loss and entropy each have four codebook channels plus a validity mask, and the time axis is pooled to 1500 frames.

The predictor has three branches: one 1D-CNN encoder per signal (128 dimensions each), late concatenation, and a two-layer MLP that outputs a 1-to-5 rating. The full model has about 1.24M parameters, roughly 84% of them in the SAE compression layer. Training uses a single MSE objective with Adam at a learning rate of 1e-4.

The five benchmarks supervise differently. MusicEval and SongEval give mean expert ratings directly. MusicPref, AIME and Music Arena give only pairwise outcomes, which the authors convert to per-clip scores with Elo. AIME has 12 battles per clip and is fitted per clip; the other two have one battle per clip, so a system-level Elo is fitted first and a single-match adjustment added.

限制Limitations

首先是範圍:全部訊號來自 MusicGen(small 與 large),SAE 也只有 Singh 等人訓練的那一個,沒有檢驗其他生成器或非自回歸模型。評估的音訊也是別的系統產生的,模型是在 teacher forcing 下「評」它們,和評自己的輸出不是同一件事,雖然標題問的是後者。

其次是標籤。MusicPref 和 Music Arena 每片段只有一場對戰,片段分數其實是系統 Elo 加小幅調整,所以預測器很可能是在認出「哪個生成系統」。這解釋了 MusicPref 上 Single-Loss 的 r 高達 0.95,但那是我的推測,論文沒有做對應的檢查。

再來是分析的規模:loss 與熵的組合、頻譜和 SAE 概念的分析都只用 MusicEval 乾淨測試集的 270 個片段,SAE 概念的人工驗證只有一位有六年經驗的製作人加 Gemini 標註。所有結果是相關,不是因果。

First, scope: every signal comes from MusicGen (small and large), and the SAE is the single one trained by Singh et al.; no other generator or non-autoregressive model is tested. The audio being rated was produced by other systems and the model scores it under teacher forcing, which is not the same as judging its own output, though that is what the title asks.

Second, labels. MusicPref and Music Arena have one battle per clip, so a clip's score is essentially its system's Elo plus a small adjustment, and the predictor may simply be recognising which generator made the clip. This could explain Single-Loss reaching r = 0.95 on MusicPref, but that is my inference; the paper runs no check for it.

Third, scale of the analyses: the loss–entropy, spectral and SAE-concept analyses all use only the 270 clips of the MusicEval clean test split, and the SAE concepts are validated by one producer with six years' experience plus Gemini captions. All results are correlational, not causal.

討論Discussion

數字上最有說服力的是整體比較。五個基準合併的測試集上,Single-SAE 的 Pearson r 在 small/large 分別為 0.73/0.81,Loss+Entropy+SAE 為 0.81/0.82,Single-Loss 為 0.64/0.67,Single-Entropy 為 0.70/0.69;Audiobox 四軸只有 0.07 到 0.20(注意它是 zero-shot,訓練過的預測器不是同一把尺),平均 loss 為 −0.08。MusicEval 上 Single-SAE 為 0.79/0.77,Audiobox 最好是 0.62。但組合不一定更好:每個基準都有一群統計上打平的頂尖模型,單一最佳只在 24 欄裡的 4 欄勝過第二名。

最有趣的是 loss–entropy 的錯配:把每個 token 依 loss 與熵的四分位分成四類後,「過度自信卻錯」(CW)的比例與評分 r = −0.18,「不確定且錯」(UW)為 +0.29,UW 減 CW 為 +0.25,ℓ−H(超額驚訝)為 +0.30(Spearman 0.35)。也就是評分高的片段比模型自己預期的更令人意外。Gemini 盲審的佐證很薄:CW 視窗 6/10 有聽感干擾、UW 為 3/10,樣本只有各 10 個。

The pooled comparison is the most convincing number. On the union of the five held-out splits, Single-SAE reaches Pearson r of 0.73/0.81 (small/large), Loss+Entropy+SAE 0.81/0.82, Single-Loss 0.64/0.67 and Single-Entropy 0.70/0.69; the four Audiobox axes score only 0.07 to 0.20 (they are zero-shot, so trained predictors are not the same yardstick) and mean loss −0.08. On MusicEval, Single-SAE gets 0.79/0.77 against 0.62 for the best Audiobox axis. Combining signals is not always better: every benchmark has a group of statistically tied top models, and the single best beats the runner-up in only 4 of 24 columns.

The most interesting finding is the loss–entropy mismatch. Splitting tokens by loss and entropy quartiles, the share of overconfident-wrong tokens (CW) correlates with rating at r = −0.18, uncertain-wrong (UW) at +0.29, UW minus CW at +0.25, and excess surprise ℓ−H at +0.30 (Spearman 0.35). Higher-rated clips are more surprising than the model expects. The Gemini blind audit is thin support: 6/10 CW windows were audibly disruptive versus 3/10 UW, from only 10 windows each.

Emergent Tonal Structure in Learned Chord Embeddings and Its Relation to Tonal Tension

學得的和弦嵌入中浮現的調性結構及其與調性張力的關係

Maral Ebrahimzadeh, Gilberto Bernardes, Sebastian Stober
arXiv:2609.36460 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-29
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：只用 64 維 skip-gram 和弦嵌入,加上十二調移調擴增,在 BPS-FH 上的移調等變 Procrustes 誤差是 0.074(不擴增為 0.464),與人類張力評分的相關為 r = 0.473(留一交叉驗證 0.406),屬於中等程度。Why this one: Plain 64-dimensional skip-gram chord embeddings with 12-key transposition augmentation reach a transposition-equivariance Procrustes error of 0.074 on BPS-FH (0.464 without augmentation), and a correlation with human tension ratings of r = 0.473 (0.406 under leave-one-out), a moderate level.

動機Motivation

音樂理論與認知研究長期用「調性空間」(tonal pitch space)描述音符、和弦與調的關係,並以空間中的距離與移動來建模張力。這些空間大多是手工設計、帶理論先驗的幾何。

另一方面,skip-gram 這類方法可以只靠共現統計學出和弦嵌入。作者想知道:這樣簡單的嵌入是只捕捉局部和聲相似,還是也能撐起調性、移調與張力相關的結構?他們特別關心移調擴增(把語料轉到十二個調)對這些性質有什麼影響。

Music theory and cognition have long described relations among notes, chords and keys with tonal pitch spaces, and model tension through distances and movements in those spaces. Most such spaces are hand-designed geometries with theoretical priors.

Separately, methods like skip-gram can learn chord embeddings from co-occurrence statistics alone. The authors ask whether such a simple embedding captures only local harmonic similarity, or can also support tonal, transpositional and tension-related structure. They focus on what transposition augmentation (moving the corpus into all twelve keys) does to these properties.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

理論端,論文引用 Longuet-Higgins、Chew 的螺旋陣列(spiral array)、Lerdahl 的 Tonal Pitch Space 與調性張力模型、MorpheuS(在螺旋陣列上加入聲部進行),以及 Bernardes 等人的 Tonal Interval Space(TIV)與以它為基礎的張力模型(Navarro-Cáceres 等人 2020)。共同點是手工設計的幾何。

資料端,最近的先前工作是 Chuan、Agres 與 Herremans(2020)用 word2vec 分析複音切片(polyphonic slice)嵌入,已經看到功能和聲與隱含的五度圈。本篇與它的差別是改用和弦符號 token,並進一步測試移調等變、關鍵中心(key centroid)、轉調方向向量與張力量測。其他相關還有 Chord2vec(Madjiheurem 等人)、Lahnala 等人(ISMIR 2021)對和弦嵌入的分析、以及用自編碼器在潛空間中發現五度圈的工作。

換句話說,「skip-gram 嵌入裡會浮現五度圈」本身不是新發現,Chuan 等人已經顯示過;新的是移調擴增的對照與張力的探測。

On the theory side the paper cites Longuet-Higgins, Chew's spiral array, Lerdahl's Tonal Pitch Space and tension model, MorpheuS (voice-leading added on spiral-array geometry), and Bernardes et al.'s Tonal Interval Space (TIV) with the tension model built on it (Navarro-Cáceres et al., 2020). What they share is hand-designed geometry.

On the data side, the closest prior work is Chuan, Agres and Herremans (2020), who analysed word2vec embeddings of polyphonic slices and already saw functional harmony and an implicit circle of fifths. This paper differs by using chord-symbol tokens and by testing transposition equivariance, key centroids, key-steering vectors and tension measures. Other related work includes Chord2vec (Madjiheurem et al.), Lahnala et al.'s analysis of chord embeddings (ISMIR 2021), and autoencoder work finding the circle of fifths in latent spaces.

In other words, the circle of fifths emerging from skip-gram embeddings is not itself new, since Chuan et al. showed it; the new parts are the augmentation contrast and the tension probes.

方法Method

資料是兩個標註語料:BPS-FH(貝多芬 32 首鋼琴奏鳴曲第一樂章,604 個序列,9,542 個大小三和弦事件)與 Isophonics(披頭四,515 個序列,13,470 個事件)。和弦一律簡化成 24 個大小三和弦 token,七和弦與轉位資訊都丟掉。

模型是 gensim 的 skip-gram:視窗 5、負採樣 5、64 維、兩個隨機種子。AUGMENTED 設定用十二調全部移調的語料訓練 50 個 epoch;ORIGINAL 設定用原語料訓練 600 個 epoch,讓總訓練對數大致相當。

分析分四塊:五度圈結構(循環排序誤差、與理想五度圈距離模板的 Spearman 相關、Fifth R@2);移調等變(Procrustes 誤差與前 8 近鄰 Jaccard);調的中心向量(以該調音階內和弦的平均向量為中心,比較調內與調外距離);以及從調中心差向量做轉調「導向」。

The data are two annotated corpora: BPS-FH (first movements of Beethoven's 32 piano sonatas; 604 sequences, 9,542 major/minor triad events) and Isophonics (the Beatles; 515 sequences, 13,470 events). Chords are reduced to a 24-token vocabulary of major and minor triads; sevenths and inversions are discarded.

The model is gensim skip-gram: window 5, 5 negatives, 64 dimensions, two random seeds. The AUGMENTED setting trains on the corpus transposed through all 12 keys for 50 epochs; the ORIGINAL setting trains on the raw corpus for 600 epochs so that total training pairs roughly match.

Four analyses follow: circle-of-fifths structure (cyclic ordering error, Spearman correlation with an ideal circle-of-fifths distance template, Fifth R@2); transposition equivariance (Procrustes error and top-8 neighbour Jaccard); key centroids (the mean vector of a key's chords, comparing in-key and out-of-key distances); and key steering using differences between key centroids.

限制Limitations

作者自己承認的限制:輸入只有大小三和弦標籤,沒有轉位、實際低音、聲部配置、節奏重音與和弦外音,所以張力實驗只是診斷性的,不是完整的預測器。語料也只有兩個,BPS-FH 是古典奏鳴曲、Isophonics 是流行,兩者量級差異也可能反映標註差異而非曲風。

還有幾點論文沒有太強調。移調等變的優勢有一部分是設計出來的:十二調擴增讓訓練統計在構造上就具移調對稱,作者在文中自己也這樣說。人類張力實驗只有 12 條短和弦進行,其中進行 C 完全沒有重現人評的明顯變化;而且那個動態量測還包含兩個手工設計的項(重複與局部解決),解決項的權重 0.343 僅次於調相對項 0.491,權重又是在同樣的 12 條上擬合,所以不能算純粹由嵌入得出。論文的圖 2 也只和 TIV、Lerdahl、MorpheuS 做曲線比較,沒有給出數字。

The authors acknowledge that the input is only major/minor triad labels, with no inversion, realised bass, voicing, rhythmic salience or non-chord tones, so the tension experiment is diagnostic, not a full predictor. There are also only two corpora, a classical sonata set and a pop set, and differences in magnitude may reflect annotation rather than genre.

Several points are less emphasised. Part of the equivariance advantage is by construction: 12-fold augmentation makes training statistics transposition-symmetric, as the paper itself says. The human-tension experiment uses only 12 short progressions, and on Progression C the measure misses a pronounced change in the human ratings. The dynamic measure also includes two hand-designed terms (repetition and local resolution); the resolution weight 0.343 is second only to the key-relative term at 0.491, and the weights are fitted on the same 12 progressions, so the measure is not purely embedding-derived. Figure 2 compares curves with TIV, Lerdahl and MorpheuS but gives no numbers.

討論Discussion

擴增的效果在每個指標上方向一致。BPS-FH 上移調等變 Procrustes 誤差 AUG 為 0.074、ORIG 為 0.464,Jaccard 為 0.875 對 0.609;Isophonics 為 0.180 對 0.622 與 0.792 對 0.480。五度圈模板相關(大調/小調)在 BPS-FH 為 0.868/0.958 對 0.736/0.793,AUG 的循環排序誤差為 0,Fifth R@2 為 1.000/1.000(ORIG 為 0.979/0.771)。調內外分離的 AUC 為 0.931/0.891 對 0.891/0.849。轉調導向在 α = 2.0 時,目標命中率 0.630 對 0.377。

對照組讓結論比較可信:隨機與標籤置換的嵌入,調辨別 AUC 接近或低於 0.5;全域洗牌掉到 0.46/0.58;序列內洗牌 AUC 仍約 0.92,但 Procrustes 誤差從 0.07 升到 0.17,表示粗略的調性結構主要來自序列層級共現,而非和弦順序。

張力端則較溫和:嵌入量與 TIV 對應量的相關,脈絡距離 Pearson 0.733(ORIG 0.707)、調相對距離 0.624(ORIG 0.513);與人評的相關 r = 0.473,留一為 0.406、7/12 條進行 r ≥ 0.6。這支持「有一些張力相關的結構」,不支持「可取代手工模型」,作者自己也說目標不是勝過先前的張力模型。

The effect of augmentation points the same way on every metric. On BPS-FH, transposition-equivariance Procrustes error is 0.074 for AUG versus 0.464 for ORIG, with Jaccard 0.875 versus 0.609; on Isophonics 0.180 versus 0.622 and 0.792 versus 0.480. Circle-of-fifths template correlation (major/minor) on BPS-FH is 0.868/0.958 versus 0.736/0.793, AUG has zero cyclic error and Fifth R@2 of 1.000/1.000 (ORIG 0.979/0.771). In-key versus out-of-key AUC is 0.931/0.891 versus 0.891/0.849. In key steering at α = 2.0, target-hit rate is 0.630 versus 0.377.

The controls make the conclusion more credible: random and label-permuted embeddings give key-discrimination AUC near or below 0.5; global shuffling drops it to 0.46/0.58; within-sequence shuffling keeps AUC around 0.92 but raises Procrustes error from 0.07 to 0.17, suggesting broad tonal structure comes from sequence-level co-occurrence rather than chord order.

The tension side is more modest. Correlation of embedding quantities with matched TIV quantities is Pearson 0.733 for contextual distance (ORIG 0.707) and 0.624 for key-relative distance (ORIG 0.513); against human ratings r = 0.473, 0.406 under leave-one-out, with r ≥ 0.6 on 7 of 12 progressions. This supports some tension-related structure, not a replacement for hand-built models, and the authors say outperforming earlier tension models is not their goal.

Perceptually Motivated Alignment and Interpolation of Pitch-Aligned Time-Frequency Representations

以感知為導向的音高對齊時頻表示之對齊與內插

Shahan Nercessian, Jeff Sontag, Alejandro Koretzky

為什麼挑這篇：把 Tonal Interval Vector 改寫成可逆算子,用它做音高排列對齊(1024 組隨機色度對的平均 TIV 距離從 7.308 降到 2.276),並以五度圈上的環狀最佳傳輸讓大三和弦到小三和弦經過 Fadd9、Gm9/C、Gm/C 等有和聲意義的中間態;但評估只有一個手作例子與合成資料。Why this one: It rewrites the Tonal Interval Vector as an invertible operator and uses it for pitch-permutation alignment (mean TIV distance over 1024 random chroma pairs falls from 7.308 to 2.276) and for circular optimal-transport interpolation on the circle of fifths, taking a C major triad to C minor through Fadd9, Gm9/C and Gm/C; but the evaluation is one hand-built example plus synthetic data.

動機Motivation

色度、pitch class profile 與轉譜得到的 piano roll 都屬於「音高對齊的時頻表示」(PTFR),是和弦辨識與調性估計的基礎。作者想要的是對這些表示做操作:重新配和聲、轉調、把獨立生成的兩層音樂對齊。

問題是常見做法用歐氏距離,數學上一致,卻不符合感知上的協和,結果在音樂上不合理。生成模型(MusicGen、Diff-A-Riff 之類)也缺少調整和聲結構的工具。作者希望有一套「感知上合理」的工具箱,既可單獨用來分析與創作,也可作為生成模型的條件介面。

Chroma, pitch class profiles and transcription-derived piano rolls are all pitch-aligned time-frequency representations (PTFRs), the basis of chord recognition and key estimation. The authors want to operate on them: re-harmonise, modulate, or align two independently generated musical layers.

The problem is that common practice uses Euclidean distance, which is mathematically consistent but does not match perceived consonance and yields musically implausible results. Generative models (MusicGen, Diff-A-Riff and similar) also lack tools to adjust harmonic structure. The authors want a perceptually grounded toolkit, usable on its own for analysis and composition and as a conditioning interface for generative models.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

核心前身是 Bernardes 等人(2016)的 Tonal Interval Vector(TIV):對色度做加權 DFT 取 1 到 6 階係數,權重由雙音協和度的主觀評分得來,象徵式輸入的權重為 [2,11,17,16,19,7]。後續的 TIV.lib(Ramires 等人 2020)提供實作,但主要用於分析。本文的第一個貢獻其實是把這個既有算子擴充成可逆、可擴到 12·o 維(多個八度)的版本。

對齊部分,最像的既有做法是 Ellis 與 Poliner(2007)翻唱辨識中用色度互相關求環狀移位;本文的 cross-correlation 基線就是這招,只是改在感知平滑後的向量上做。內插部分最接近 Henderson 與 Solomon(DAFx 2019)的 Audio Transport,用最佳傳輸在線性頻率軸上做連音;本文差別是改到對數音高軸並提出五度圈上的環狀版本。

作者另外指出,TIV.lib 的 Temperley 剖面在反推後 C 大調出現不尋常的 D 強調,可能是實作問題。

The core predecessor is Bernardes et al.'s Tonal Interval Vector (TIV, 2016): a weighted DFT of a chroma vector keeping coefficients 1 to 6, with weights derived from subjective dyad-consonance ratings (for symbolic input [2, 11, 17, 16, 19, 7]). TIV.lib (Ramires et al., 2020) implements it but is used mainly for analysis. The paper's first contribution is really an extension of this existing operator to an invertible one that scales to 12·o dimensions (multiple octaves).

For alignment, the closest existing technique is the circular chroma cross-correlation of Ellis and Poliner (2007) for cover-song identification; the paper's cross-correlation baseline is exactly that, applied to perceptually smoothed vectors. For interpolation, the closest is Henderson and Solomon's Audio Transport (DAFx 2019), which uses optimal transport on a linear frequency axis to build a portamento; the difference here is a log-pitch axis and a circular variant on the circle of fifths.

The authors also note that the Temperley profile in TIV.lib, once recovered by inversion, shows an unexpected emphasis on D for C major, possibly an implementation problem.

方法Method

第一部分是 TIV 的擴充。把 DFT 寫成實數矩陣 F,去掉兩個零成分、把直流分量移到最後一列,使 F 可逆;加權矩陣 W 的直流項設為自由參數 κ,κ = 0 時完全回到原本的 TIV,κ = 1 時整個轉換可逆。再加上正規化,以及用線性內插把權重延伸到多個八度。最後定義「感知平滑」特徵抽取 A(c) = Fᵀ W F c,相當於在 TIV 域套用固定的對角濾波。

第二部分是對齊:找排列矩陣 P 使 TIV 距離最小,這是 NP-hard 的二次指派問題(QAP)。作者先近似成線性指派(LAP),向量情形用 argsort(依重排不等式),矩陣情形用匈牙利演算法,再用 2-opt 貪婪交換改善。

第三部分是內插:把色度視為機率質量,在五度圈順序上做最佳傳輸,距離取環狀,質量可往兩個方向移動。最後是一個 3 維圓柱座標的自編碼器:r 為 Hoyer 稀疏度(音階密度),θ 為根音在五度圈上的角度,z 由網路學成,代表音階的明暗色彩;編碼器與解碼器各為三層、隱藏維度 2 的線性層。

Part one extends the TIV. The DFT is written as a real matrix F; two null components are removed and the DC component moved to the last row, making F invertible. The DC entry of the weight matrix W becomes a free parameter κ: κ = 0 recovers the original TIV exactly, while κ = 1 makes the whole transform invertible. Normalisation steps and linear interpolation of weights to multiple octaves follow. The perceptually smoothed feature extractor is A(c) = Fᵀ W F c, a fixed diagonal filter in the TIV domain.

Part two is alignment: find a permutation matrix P minimising TIV distance, which is an NP-hard quadratic assignment problem (QAP). The authors first approximate it with a linear assignment problem (LAP): argsort for vectors (via the rearrangement inequality) and the Hungarian algorithm for time-varying matrices, then refine with greedy 2-opt swaps.

Part three is interpolation: chroma is treated as probability mass and moved by optimal transport in circle-of-fifths order with a circular cost, so mass can travel either way around. Finally, a 3-D cylindrical autoencoder: r is Hoyer sparsity (scale density), θ the root's angle on the circle of fifths, and z is learned (scale colour); encoder and decoder are each three linear layers with hidden dimension 2.

限制Limitations

評估很薄。對齊只用一個手作的例子(D 大調 I-IV-V-I 對 C 小調 cantus firmus)加 1024 組隨機生成的色度對,後者不是真實音樂;沒有真實錄音、沒有和弦辨識或調性估計之類的下游任務,也沒有聽測。內插只展示一組 C 大調到 C 小調的三和弦。幾何音階表示只用 106 條內部自建的音階剖面訓練,沒有保留測試集,「有效且有音樂性」主要靠示範網站。

方法上也有疑點:argsort 求出的是全域最佳排列,但任意排列不保證保留五度等音程關係,能把三個音映射到任何三個音,作為「音樂上合理的對齊」是否站得住,論文只用距離指標回答。環狀最佳傳輸需要非負輸入,所以目前無法和可能產生負值的感知平滑特徵結合,作者在結論中承認這點。

The evaluation is thin. Alignment uses one hand-built example (an I-IV-V-I in D major against a C-minor cantus firmus) plus 1024 randomly generated chroma pairs, which are not real music; there are no real recordings, no downstream task such as chord recognition or key estimation, and no listening test. Interpolation shows a single C major to C minor triad pair. The geometric scale representation is trained on 106 internally constructed scale profiles with no held-out set, and its effectiveness and musicality rest mainly on the demo site.

There are methodological questions too. Argsort gives the globally optimal permutation, but an arbitrary permutation need not preserve fifths and other intervals and can map three notes to any three; whether this is musically plausible alignment is answered only with a distance metric. Circular optimal transport requires non-negative input, so it cannot currently be combined with the perceptually smoothed features, which may go negative; the authors acknowledge this in the conclusions.

討論Discussion

表 1 以原始 TIV 距離報告(避免偏向自己的新特徵)。向量情形,音樂例子的距離:無對齊 23.354、argmax 與 cross-correlation 都是 13.206、argsort 5.361、argsort+2-opt 4.350、Hungarian 7.622、Hungarian+2-opt 7.891。隨機資料的平均:7.308、6.534、5.055、3.086、2.276、3.183、2.559。矩陣情形的隨機資料:26.344 降到最好的 13.012(Hungarian+2-opt)。

要小心的是結果並非單調:2-opt 在音樂例子的向量情形對 Hungarian 反而變差(7.622 到 7.891),在矩陣情形對 argsort 也變差(100.266 到 107.568)。內插的表 2 則較有說服力:線性淡入淡出在 t = 1/3、1/2、2/3 都停在 Cadd♯9,環狀 OT 依序經過 Fadd9、Gm9/C、Gm/C,t = 1/2 是既非大調也非小調的六音音集。

整體是一篇有想法的信號處理論文,貢獻是工具而非證據;要看出它對 MIR 任務或生成模型條件控制有沒有幫助,還需要真實資料與下游實驗。

Table 1 reports the original TIV distance, to avoid favouring the authors' own feature. Vector case, musical example: no alignment 23.354, argmax and cross-correlation both 13.206, argsort 5.361, argsort+2-opt 4.350, Hungarian 7.622, Hungarian+2-opt 7.891. Random-pair averages: 7.308, 6.534, 5.055, 3.086, 2.276, 3.183, 2.559. Matrix case on random data: from 26.344 down to 13.012 at best (Hungarian+2-opt).

The results are not monotonic, though: 2-opt makes the vector-case musical example worse for Hungarian (7.622 to 7.891) and the matrix-case musical result worse for argsort (100.266 to 107.568). Table 2 for interpolation is more persuasive: linear fading sits at Cadd♯9 for t = 1/3, 1/2 and 2/3, while circular OT passes through Fadd9, Gm9/C and Gm/C, with t = 1/2 a six-note set that is neither major nor minor.

Overall this is an idea-rich signal-processing paper whose contribution is tools rather than evidence; whether it helps MIR tasks or conditioning of generative models needs real data and downstream experiments.
