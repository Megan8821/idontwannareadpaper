Restore, Separate, Restore: A Modular Framework for Music Source Restoration

還原、分離、再還原:音源還原的模組化框架

Tobias Morocutti, Emmanouil Karystinaios, Gerhard Widmer
arXiv:2610.08284 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把音源還原(MSR)拆成「混音還原 → 單一模型八軌分離 → 逐軌還原專家」三段,在 MSR Challenge 測試集上平均 MMSNR 從只做分離的 3.980 dB 升到 4.050 dB,但仍落後挑戰賽冠軍 xlancelab 的 4.459 dB;第三段在驗證集挑出的五軌,到測試集只剩三軌有進步,這個落差本身就是這篇最有用的發現。Why this one: It splits music source restoration (MSR) into three stages, mixture restoration, then a single eight-stem separator, then per-stem restoration experts, and raises average MMSNR on the MSR Challenge test set from 3.980 dB (separation only) to 4.050 dB, still behind the challenge winner xlancelab at 4.459 dB. Of the five stems where the third stage was kept based on validation, only three still improve on test, and that gap is the paper's most useful finding.

動機Motivation

傳統音源分離假設混音就是乾淨音軌的線性相加。真實的發行錄音不是這樣:每一軌先被 EQ、壓縮、殘響處理過,整首再經過母帶處理,最後可能還被編碼器或類比傳輸弄壞。音源還原(Music Source Restoration, MSR)要的是把錄進去之前的原始音軌找回來,等於要同時反轉非線性處理與傳輸劣化。

作者的出發點是一個角色區分:遮罩式分離模型(如 BS-RoFormer)擅長把能量分配給對的音源,但遮罩只能重新加權混音裡已有的內容,補不回被處理掉的音色細節或暫態。所以他們讓分離與還原由不同類型的模型負責,而不是像多數挑戰賽系統那樣全部交給 BS-RoFormer。

Conventional source separation assumes a mixture is a linear sum of clean stems. Released recordings are not: each stem has been equalized, compressed and reverberated, the mix has been mastered, and the result may then be damaged by codecs or analog transmission. Music Source Restoration (MSR) asks for the stems as they were before any of that, which means inverting nonlinear processing and transmission degradation at the same time.

The authors start from a division of roles. Masking-based separators such as BS-RoFormer are good at assigning energy to the right source, but a mask can only reweight what is already in the mixture; it cannot put back timbral detail or transients that processing removed. So they give separation and restoration to different model types, rather than handing everything to BS-RoFormer as most challenge systems did.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

MSR 由 Zang 等人(2025)正式定義並釋出 RawStems 未處理音軌資料集,隨後的 MSRBench(2026)改用專業混音而非合成相加的音軌–混音對,並催生 ICASSP 2026 MSR Challenge。冠軍 xlancelab 用一串 BS-RoFormer(Lu 等人,2024)分別做去噪、分軌、人聲去殘響,而且按音源群組各自微調;DTT-BSR+ 則是生成加迴歸的兩段式串接。這篇的還原模型直接沿用 Apollo(Li 與 Luo,2025),一個頻帶切分加序列建模、用 GAN 目標訓練的 44.1 kHz 音訊還原網路。

與 xlancelab 的差別有兩點:分離只用一個共享的 248M 參數模型輸出八軌,而不是每組一個模型;還原被放在分離前後各一次。

第三段「在分離器實際輸出上訓練後處理還原網路」最像的既有方法,是 Schaffer 等人在 ISMIR 2022 的 Make it Sound Good(MSG):拿 Demucs 的分離結果訓練一個 GAN 後處理器去修分離瑕疵。兩者思路幾乎相同,差別只在 MSG 的目標是混音內的音軌、這裡的目標是未處理音軌,但論文沒有引用 MSG。分離段的 random mixing 則來自 Jeon 等人(2024)把不同歌曲音軌亂配成「噪音式」混音的資料增強。

MSR was formalized by Zang et al. (2025), who released the RawStems dataset of unprocessed stems; MSRBench (2026) then switched to professionally mixed rather than synthetically summed stem-mixture pairs and led to the ICASSP 2026 MSR Challenge. The winner, xlancelab, chains BS-RoFormer models (Lu et al., 2024) for denoising, stem separation and vocal dereverberation, fine-tuned per source group; DTT-BSR+ is a two-stage generative-regression cascade. This paper reuses Apollo (Li and Luo, 2025) for restoration, a band-split sequence model trained with a GAN objective for 44.1 kHz audio.

It differs from xlancelab in two ways: one shared 248M-parameter separator outputs all eight stems instead of one model per group, and restoration happens both before and after separation.

The closest existing idea to Stage 3, training a post-processing restorer on the separator's actual outputs, is Make it Sound Good (MSG) by Schaffer et al. at ISMIR 2022, which trained a GAN post-processor on Demucs outputs to repair separation artifacts. The idea is nearly identical; the difference is that MSG targets the stem as mixed while this paper targets the unprocessed stem. The paper does not cite MSG. The random-mixing augmentation in the separator follows Jeon et al. (2024), who pair stems from unrelated songs into cacophonous mixtures.

方法Method

論文先把問題寫成 y = d(m(Σ f_i(s_i))):s_i 是乾淨音軌,f_i 是逐軌處理,m 是母帶,d 是傳輸劣化。三段各自負責一層。

第一段混音還原:Apollo 從官方預訓練權重出發,凍結前三個 BSNet 區塊,微調其餘部分 20 個 epoch;一半機率對輸入與目標都套上模擬母帶,再對輸入加類比/聲學效果或編碼壓縮,目標是反轉 d。

第二段分離:拿六軌的 BS-RoFormer 權重擴成九個輸出(八個目標軌加一個不回傳梯度的輔助 other 軌),新增的打擊、合成器、管弦三個遮罩頭隨機初始化,約 17,000 步微調。分離器是在乾淨、未做母帶的混音上訓練,跟推論時吃到的第一段輸出(近似已做母帶的混音)不一致,作者承認並留待未來處理。random mixing 的機率從 0.9 線性退火到 0,使測試 MMSNR 從 3.81 升到 3.94 dB(三個種子平均)。

第三段逐軌還原:八軌各一個 Apollo,從第一段權重初始化,訓練對是「分離器在已還原混音上的實際輸出」對「RawStems 的乾淨音軌」,不用合成劣化。只有在驗證集 MMSNR 有進步的音軌才保留這一段。還原模型的損失是多解析度 mel 損失、直接最佳化評估指標的 multi-mel-SNR 損失,加上對抗損失。

The paper writes the problem as y = d(m(Σ f_i(s_i))): s_i are clean stems, f_i per-stem processing, m mastering and d transmission degradation. Each stage handles one layer.

Stage 1, mixture restoration: Apollo starts from the official checkpoint with the first three BSNet blocks frozen and is fine-tuned for 20 epochs. With probability 0.5 both input and target get simulated mastering, and the input then receives analog/acoustic effects or codec compression; the goal is to invert d.

Stage 2, separation: a six-stem BS-RoFormer checkpoint is extended to nine outputs (eight targets plus an auxiliary other stem with no back-propagated gradient). The new percussion, synthesizer and orchestral mask heads start from random weights; fine-tuning runs about 17,000 steps. The separator is trained on clean, unmastered mixtures, which mismatches the Stage 1 output it sees at inference (an approximation of the mastered mix); the authors acknowledge this and defer it. Annealing the random-mixing probability linearly from 0.9 to 0 raises test MMSNR from 3.81 to 3.94 dB, averaged over three seeds.

Stage 3, stem restoration: one Apollo per stem, initialized from Stage 1, trained on pairs of the separator's real outputs on restored mixtures and the clean RawStems stems, with no synthetic degradation. A stem keeps this stage only if it improves validation MMSNR. Restoration losses combine a multi-resolution mel loss, a multi-mel-SNR loss that directly optimizes the evaluation metric, and an adversarial loss.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是第三段幾乎沒有淨效益。驗證集上有五軌(人聲、吉他、合成器、打擊、管弦)保留第三段,到測試集只有人聲(1.486→1.535)、吉他(3.045→3.070)、合成器(3.102→3.131)三軌進步,打擊(0.823→0.783)與管弦(4.698→4.662)反而退步,整體 MMSNR 只從 4.047 變成 4.050 dB。若八軌全套第三段,平均會掉到 3.972 dB,貝斯一軌就掉了 0.526 dB。

文字與表格有對不上的地方。正文說保留第三段後「FAD-CLAP 變差(0.274→0.279)」,但 0.274 是八軌全套的數字;跟真正的比較基準(只做一、二段的 0.293)相比,最終系統的 0.279 其實是變好。讀者需要自己對表才看得出來。

其他:沒有任何主觀聽測,只引用挑戰賽客觀指標與 MOS 的 Spearman ρ=0.9 當間接支持;「單一分離器參數量與訓練成本低很多」的說法沒有列出 xlancelab 的參數量對照;第二段的母帶處理訓練/推論不一致被擱置。

The main problem is that Stage 3 yields almost no net benefit. On validation it is kept for five stems (vocals, guitars, synthesizers, percussion, orchestral); on test only vocals (1.486→1.535), guitars (3.045→3.070) and synthesizers (3.102→3.131) improve, while percussion (0.823→0.783) and orchestral (4.698→4.662) get worse, and overall MMSNR moves only from 4.047 to 4.050 dB. Applying Stage 3 to all eight stems would lower the average to 3.972 dB, with bass alone dropping 0.526 dB.

The text and tables do not line up in one place. The prose says that after keeping Stage 3, FAD-CLAP degrades (0.274→0.279), but 0.274 is the all-eight-stems figure; against the real baseline, Stages 1+2 at 0.293, the final system's 0.279 is an improvement. Readers have to cross-check the tables to see this.

Other gaps: there is no listening test, only the challenge's Spearman ρ=0.9 between objective metrics and MOS as indirect support; the claim of a much lower parameter count and training cost than per-stem systems gives no parameter figures for xlancelab; and the mastering mismatch between Stage 1 and Stage 2 is left open.

討論Discussion

這篇真正站得住的結論是第一段:分離器從沒看過劣化混音,但前面加一個還原模型,三個指標都變好(MMSNR 3.980→4.047 dB、Zimtohrli 0.015→0.014、FAD-CLAP 0.300→0.293)。幅度不大,但方向一致,也支持「先還原再分離」的分工。

跟冠軍比,平均 MMSNR 4.050 對 4.459 dB,單一模型在八軌中只有合成器勝出(3.131 對 2.244 dB),人聲(1.535 對 1.596)與鼓(5.604 對 5.806)接近,貝斯(6.956 對 8.145)與管弦(4.662 對 6.071)差距明顯。所以比較準確的讀法是:用一個模型換掉一串專門模型,代價是平均約 0.41 dB。

作者在結論把第三段的不穩定歸因於 MSRBench 與挑戰賽測試集是兩次獨立蒐集、分布不同,並呼籲未來的基準要明確定義劣化範圍、區分同分布評估與跨條件魯棒性。這個建議比框架本身更值得帶走:在這類小資料集上用單一指標做「留不留」的選擇,很容易選到驗證集的雜訊。

The conclusion that holds up is Stage 1: although the separator never saw degraded mixtures, putting a restorer in front improves all three metrics (MMSNR 3.980→4.047 dB, Zimtohrli 0.015→0.014, FAD-CLAP 0.300→0.293). The gains are small but consistent, and they support restoring before separating.

Against the winner, average MMSNR is 4.050 vs 4.459 dB. The single model wins only on synthesizers (3.131 vs 2.244 dB), is close on vocals (1.535 vs 1.596) and drums (5.604 vs 5.806), and is clearly behind on bass (6.956 vs 8.145) and orchestral (4.662 vs 6.071). The accurate reading is that replacing a chain of specialist models with one model costs about 0.41 dB on average.

The authors attribute Stage 3's instability to MSRBench and the challenge test set being two independent collections with different distributions, and call for future benchmarks to define the degradation regime explicitly and separate matched-distribution evaluation from cross-condition robustness. That advice is worth more than the framework itself: on datasets this small, keep-or-drop decisions made on one validation metric easily select validation noise.

Extending Music Annotation Schemas: Zero-Shot Prediction or Few-Shot Adaptation?

擴充音樂標註架構:零樣本預測還是少樣本調適?

Christos Plachouras, Emmanouil Benetos, Johan Pauwels
arXiv:2610.06920 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：在 MGPHot 的專家評分上模擬「曲庫要新增七個屬性」的情境,結果連只用 8 首標註曲平均值當預測的基線都贏過 CLAP 與 Music Flamingo 的零樣本預測;在凍結特徵上接嶺迴歸頭最划算,128 首時已逼近全監督 CNN,更深的微調最多只再改善 0.0046 RMSE。Why this one: Simulating a catalogue that must add seven new attributes on MGPHot's expert ratings, it finds that even predicting the mean of 8 annotated tracks beats zero-shot prediction from CLAP and Music Flamingo. Ridge heads on frozen features are the best value and approach a fully supervised CNN at 128 tracks, while deeper fine-tuning improves RMSE by at most 0.0046 more.

動機Motivation

音樂標籤研究通常假設標註架構(有哪些屬性、怎麼定義、取值範圍)是固定的。商業曲庫不是這樣:新曲風、新聲音、新服務需求出現時,架構就要加欄位,而專家標一首歌要好幾分鐘。問題於是變成:要為整個曲庫補上新屬性,該靠音訊–語言模型零樣本預測,還是花錢標少量歌再做監督式調適?標到多少首,監督式才划算?

作者指出這個情境在 MIR 裡幾乎沒有被直接比較過,於是用 Pandora 音樂基因體計畫衍生的 MGPHot 專家評分建了一個可重用的基準。

Music tagging research usually assumes a fixed annotation schema: which attributes exist, how they are defined and what values they take. Commercial catalogues do not work that way. New styles, sounds and service needs mean adding fields, and an expert takes several minutes per track. The question becomes: to backfill a new attribute across a catalogue, should one rely on zero-shot prediction from audio-language models, or pay for a few annotations and adapt a supervised model? At what number of annotated tracks does supervision pay off?

The authors note that MIR has barely compared these options directly, so they build a reusable benchmark on MGPHot's expert ratings, which derive from Pandora's Music Genome Project.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

資料基礎是 MGPHot(Oramas 等人,2025,TISMIR):21,320 首流行歌、58 個屬性、0 到 1 的連續專家評分。Ramoneda 等人(2026)已在上面比過多個凍結表徵,但架構是固定的。連續評分的迴歸可追溯到 Aljanaki 與 Soleymani(2018)的中層感知特徵。

零樣本路線包括 Choi 等人(2019)的詞嵌入零樣本標籤、MuLan(2022)與 CLAP(Wu 等人,2023)的音訊–文字對比嵌入,以及生成式的 Music Flamingo(2026)。CMI-Bench(2025)已顯示這類模型在多數 MIR 任務上表現平平。持續學習與少樣本路線則有 González-Barrachina 等人(2024)的音樂分類持續學習、Wang 等人(2021)的少樣本持續音訊分類,以及 LC-Protonets(2025)的多標籤少樣本標籤,但都是分類,沒有延伸到迴歸。

方法本身大多是移植:CLAP 的「多錨點文字 + softmax 加權」評分就是影像品質評估的 CLIP-IQA(Wang 等人,2023),Music Flamingo 讀選項字母 logit 的做法來自 Q-Align(2024)。「凍結特徵接線性頭在小資料下贏過深度微調」也呼應 Kumar 等人(ICLR 2022)關於微調會扭曲預訓練特徵的結論。這篇的貢獻在於把它們放進同一個預算與評估條件下比較,而不是新方法。

The data is MGPHot (Oramas et al., 2025, TISMIR): 21,320 popular-music tracks, 58 attributes, continuous expert ratings from 0 to 1. Ramoneda et al. (2026) already compared several frozen representations on it, but with a fixed schema. Regressing continuous ratings goes back to Aljanaki and Soleymani's (2018) mid-level perceptual features.

The zero-shot line includes word-embedding zero-shot tagging by Choi et al. (2019), the audio-text contrastive embeddings of MuLan (2022) and CLAP (Wu et al., 2023), and the generative Music Flamingo (2026). CMI-Bench (2025) already showed such models are mediocre on most MIR tasks. The continual and few-shot line includes continual music classification by González-Barrachina et al. (2024), few-shot continual audio classification by Wang et al. (2021), and multi-label few-shot tagging with LC-Protonets (2025), all of them classification rather than regression.

Most of the methods are transplants. Scoring with multiple text anchors and a softmax over CLAP similarities is CLIP-IQA (Wang et al., 2023) from image quality assessment, and reading option-letter logits from Music Flamingo comes from Q-Align (2024). That linear heads on frozen features beat deeper fine-tuning with little data also echoes Kumar et al. (ICLR 2022) on fine-tuning distorting pretrained features. The contribution is comparing these under one budget and evaluation protocol, not a new method.

方法Method

兩種擴充情境:E1 把七個人聲屬性整類拿掉當新屬性(跟既有類別語意關聯低),E2 從七個類別各抽一個(關聯高)。兩者都是 51 個既有屬性加 7 個新屬性。支援集大小 K 為 8、32、128 首,每種從 12,800 首訓練曲隨機抽 8 次,在 4,286 首演出者不重疊的測試曲上算七個新屬性的平均 RMSE。超參數用兩個擴充共用的 45 個既有屬性挑,不動用目標屬性。

比較的方法分四類。零樣本:CLAP 用五個描述評分等級的文字錨點,溫度 T=0.07 的 softmax 加權;Music Flamingo 用五個選項字母的 logit 加權。半監督:把 CLAP 的「高端減低端」相似度差當輸入做線性迴歸。凍結表徵:在 CLAP 的 512 維音訊嵌入或在既有 51 屬性上預訓練的 ShortChunkCNN 的 512 維特徵上,每個屬性接一個嶺迴歸頭。深度調適:解凍 CNN 最後一個卷積區塊加共享層,並用 256 首舊曲做經驗回放以防遺忘。

基線是「預測支援集平均值」(完全不用音訊),上限參考是用全部訓練資料學 58 個屬性的 CNN。另外還做了資料污染檢查:剔除 Music Flamingo 與 CLAP 訓練來源中影片 ID 或歌手加歌名完全相符的曲目後重算。

There are two extension scenarios. E1 removes all seven vocal attributes as the new ones (low semantic relatedness to existing categories); E2 takes one from each of seven categories (high relatedness). Both add 7 attributes to 51 existing ones. Support sets of K = 8, 32 and 128 tracks are drawn 8 times each from 12,800 training tracks, and macro RMSE over the seven new attributes is computed on 4,286 artist-disjoint test tracks. Hyperparameters are chosen on the 45 existing attributes shared by both extensions, never on the targets.

Four method families are compared. Zero-shot: CLAP with five text anchors describing rating levels, weighted by a softmax at T=0.07, and Music Flamingo with logits over five option letters. Semi-supervised: linear regression on CLAP's high-minus-low anchor similarity difference. Frozen representations: one ridge head per attribute on CLAP's 512-dimensional audio embedding or on 512-dimensional features of a ShortChunkCNN pretrained on the 51 existing attributes. Deep adaptation: unfreeze the CNN's last convolutional block and shared layer, with experience replay of 256 old tracks against forgetting.

The baseline predicts the support-set mean and uses no audio; the reference is a CNN trained on all training data for all 58 attributes. A contamination check also removes tracks whose video ID or artist plus title exactly match Music Flamingo or CLAP training sources, then recomputes the error.

限制Limitations

主要結果只以圖表呈現,正文沒有列出各方法的 RMSE 數值。以下數字是從圖 2 目測讀出的近似值,不是論文報告的精確數字:E1 中 CLAP 零樣本約 0.220、Music Flamingo 約 0.209,而 K=8 的支援集平均基線約 0.197;E2 中兩者約 0.261 與 0.252,基線約 0.239。正文給出精確數值的只有深度調適(最多改善 0.0046)、遺忘量(−0.000186 到 +0.001028)與污染檢查。

零樣本那邊的對照有點吃虧:CLAP 用的是通用 checkpoint,作者也承認 CLAP 處理否定句有困難,錨點文字還得改寫。Music Flamingo 被要求輸出評分這種非對話任務,本來就不是它的強項,結論更像是「目前這兩個模型做不好連續評分」,不一定能推廣到所有音訊–語言模型。

E1/E2 用「是否同類別」當語意關聯的代理,作者自己也說 CLAP 與 CNN 在兩情境互有勝負的原因需要更嚴格的對照才能確定。另外只有一個資料集、只有流行音樂。

The main results appear only as a figure; the text gives no RMSE values per method. The following are approximate values read off Figure 2, not exact numbers reported by the paper: in E1, CLAP zero-shot is about 0.220 and Music Flamingo about 0.209, while the support-mean baseline at K=8 is about 0.197; in E2 the two are about 0.261 and 0.252, with the baseline about 0.239. The only exact numbers in the text are for deep adaptation (at most 0.0046 improvement), forgetting (−0.000186 to +0.001028) and the contamination check.

The zero-shot side is somewhat handicapped. CLAP is a general checkpoint, the authors note it struggles with negation, and anchor wording had to be rewritten. Music Flamingo is asked to output a rating, a non-conversational task it was not built for. The conclusion reads more as these two models are poor at continuous ratings today than as a statement about all audio-language models.

E1 and E2 use same-category membership as a proxy for semantic relatedness, and the authors themselves say that explaining why CLAP and the CNN trade wins across the two scenarios needs more controlled comparisons. There is also only one dataset, covering only popular music.

討論Discussion

最反直覺的結果是:預測新屬性時,知道「這個屬性大概落在多少」比零樣本模型「聽了歌再判斷」更準。作者的解讀是標註帶來的好處有一部分只是估出了評分分布,而零樣本模型在刻度上偏得厲害。

凍結特徵接嶺迴歸頭是實務上的甜蜜點。依圖 2 目測,E1 在 K=128 時 CLAP 頭約 0.175,已接近全監督 CNN 的約 0.173;E2 在 K=128 時 CNN 頭約 0.171,接近參考線約 0.167。兩種特徵各有勝場:新類別(E1)時 CLAP 較好,K=8 時優勢最大;跨類別(E2)時在舊屬性上預訓練的 CNN 較好。

深度調適的效益很小:K=8 時兩個情境都變差,最好的情況是 E2、K=32 改善 0.0046 RMSE;舊屬性的遺忘量在 −0.000186 到 +0.001028 之間,幾乎沒影響。污染檢查剔除到剩 2,480 首後,Music Flamingo 的目標 RMSE 在 E1 只變 +0.0004、E2 變 −0.0008,與 CNN 的差異 95% 區間包含零,沒有證據顯示污染灌水。對曲庫營運者的建議很直接:先標幾十首、在現有模型特徵上接線性頭,不用急著微調,也別指望零樣本。

The most counter-intuitive result is that, for a new attribute, knowing roughly where its values fall beats a zero-shot model that listens to the track. The authors read this as part of the benefit of annotation being simply an estimate of the rating distribution, with zero-shot models badly miscalibrated on the scale.

Ridge heads on frozen features are the practical sweet spot. Reading Figure 2 approximately, in E1 at K=128 the CLAP head is about 0.175, close to the fully supervised CNN at about 0.173; in E2 at K=128 the CNN head is about 0.171, near the reference at about 0.167. Each feature set has its niche: CLAP is better for the new category (E1), with its largest advantage at K=8, while the CNN pretrained on existing attributes is better across categories (E2).

Deep adaptation adds little: it worsens both scenarios at K=8, and its best case is a 0.0046 RMSE gain in E2 at K=32; forgetting on existing attributes ranges from −0.000186 to +0.001028, essentially none. The contamination check, which leaves 2,480 tracks, changes Music Flamingo's target RMSE by only +0.0004 in E1 and −0.0008 in E2, and the 95% interval for its difference from the CNN includes zero, so there is no evidence of inflated scores. The advice for catalogue operators is plain: annotate a few dozen tracks, fit linear heads on existing model features, skip fine-tuning for now, and do not count on zero-shot.

Geometric Representations for Transformed Pattern Matching in Music

音樂中變形樣式比對的幾何表徵

David Meredith
arXiv:2610.08236 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：以拉威爾《海頓名小步舞曲》裡 31 處 HAYDN 主題(Soucy 找到 28 處,作者另找出 3 處)為唯一案例,示範八種點集音高–時間表徵各自能把哪些移調、倒影、逆行、八度錯置與和弦化關係變成精確的幾何變換;在四種非模數表徵中,有 12 處出現無法用平移、時間縮放與反射對應到任何其他出現處。Why this one: Using the 31 occurrences of the HAYDN theme in Ravel's Menuet sur le nom d'Haydn (28 found by Soucy plus 3 found by the author) as its only case, it shows which transpositions, inversions, retrogrades, octave displacements and chordal collapses each of eight point-set pitch-time representations turns into exact geometric transformations; in the four non-modular representations, 12 occurrences cannot be mapped onto any other by translation, time scaling and reflection.

動機Motivation

符號音樂的樣式比對大多把音樂當成序列,通常一個聲部一條。鍵盤音樂讓這個假設破功:聲部結構只是樂譜上的提示(符桿方向、連線),編輯也不一定前後一致,甚至有跨聲部的連結線。作者指出,即使用相當嚴格的定義,可能的聲部切法數量也會隨音符數呈指數成長,逐一搜尋不可行。

實例是拉威爾 1909 年的《海頓名小步舞曲》。作者找到一處先前文獻似乎漏掉的 HAYDN 主題(B–A–D–D–G,第 52–54 小節),它橫跨樂譜上不同的「聲部」,而且音符在時間上重疊。若比對演算法要求每個匹配都落在單一聲部內,就會漏掉它。所以作者主張改用不含聲部資訊的點集表徵,把音樂變形當作幾何變換來找。

Most symbolic pattern matching treats music as sequences, typically one per voice. Keyboard music breaks this assumption: voice structure is only hinted at by the score (stem directions, beaming), editors are not always consistent, and there are even ties between notes in different voices. The author notes that even under a fairly strict definition, the number of possible voice segmentations grows exponentially with the number of notes, so searching them all is infeasible.

The example is Ravel's 1909 Menuet sur le nom d'Haydn. The author finds an occurrence of the HAYDN theme (B–A–D–D–G, bars 52–54) that earlier literature seems to have missed; it spans different notated voices and its notes overlap in time. A matcher that requires each match to lie within one voice would miss it. Hence the argument for point-set representations without voice information, with musical transformations treated as geometric ones.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

點集表徵這條線的起點是作者自己與 Lemström、Wiggins 在 2002 年提出的 SIA/SIATEC:把音符當成音高–時間平面上的點,找出在平移下等價的樣式類(translational equivalence class)。Ukkonen、Lemström 與 Mäkinen(2003)的 P1–P3 演算法把它推到部分匹配,P3 還把音符當成水平線段、比對重疊長度;Lemström(2010)之後再加入時間縮放不變性。序列路線的代表則是 Mongeau 與 Sankoff(1990)的編輯距離,天生假設單聲部。

這篇是回顧加延伸,而非新演算法(搜尋演算法在作者 2024 年 MML 工作坊與 2026 年 CCIS 版本)。新東西有三:用「中點時間」(midtime)取代起音時間,讓逆行精確對應到時間軸反射;用形態音高(morphetic pitch)、morph(mod 7)與 chroma(mod 12)處理調內移位、八度錯置;以及把一組樣式在某變換類下的關係畫成「樣式間變換圖」(IPTG)。

中點時間最接近的既有做法其實就是 P3 的線段表示:線段在時間上反射後,不變的正是它的中點。IPTG 則幾乎就是 Lewin(1987)變換理論裡的變換網路,也是 SIATEC 平移等價類推廣到更大變換群的版本。論文的參考文獻只有 10 筆,SIA、P1–P3、Lemström 與 Lewin 都沒有引用。

The point-set line starts with SIA/SIATEC by the author, Lemström and Wiggins (2002): notes become points in a pitch-time plane, and the algorithm finds classes of patterns equivalent under translation (translational equivalence classes). Ukkonen, Lemström and Mäkinen (2003) extended it to partial matching with P1–P3, where P3 treats notes as horizontal line segments and measures overlap; Lemström (2010) later added time-scale invariance. The sequential line is represented by Mongeau and Sankoff's (1990) edit distance, which inherently assumes monophony.

This paper is a review plus extensions, not a new algorithm (the search algorithm is in the author's 2024 MML workshop paper and its 2026 CCIS version). Three things are new: using midtime instead of onset so that retrograde corresponds exactly to reflection in time; using morphetic pitch, morph (mod 7) and chroma (mod 12) to handle diatonic transposition and octave displacement; and drawing the relations among a set of patterns under a transformation class as an inter-pattern transformation graph (IPTG).

The closest existing idea to midtime is P3's line-segment representation: when a segment is reflected in time, what stays fixed is its midpoint. The IPTG is almost exactly a transformation network from Lewin's (1987) transformational theory, and a generalization of SIATEC's translational equivalence classes to a larger transformation group. The paper cites only 10 references, and none of SIA, P1–P3, Lemström or Lewin is among them.

方法Method

八種表徵由兩個時間軸(起音、中點時間)乘上四個音高軸組成:半音音高(chromatic pitch,A0=0,中央 C=39)、形態音高(五線譜上的位置,A0=0,中央 C=23,不受升降記號影響)、chroma(半音音高 mod 12)、morph(形態音高 mod 7)。縮寫分別是 CPO、CPM、MPO、MPM、CO、CM、MO、MM。

變換類有三個。F_2STR:沿時間軸縮放(係數可為負,即逆行)、平移、可選的音高軸反射(倒影)。F_2STRMod12 與 F_2STRMod7 是同樣的組合,但音高的平移與反射在模 12 或模 7 下進行。時間縮放係數為 0 時,一段旋律會被壓成和弦,這讓「把主題音符同時奏出」也成為一種變換;但這種變換不可逆,所以關係圖不再對稱。

作者把 Soucy(2009)整理的 28 處加上自己找到的 A、B、C 三處共 31 個樣式,在每種表徵下算出 IPTG(以鄰接表格呈現),並在 OMNISIA 開源專案中附上可重現的 Java 程式。例如在 CPM 中,第 1 處經時間反射再平移 ⟨364,−12⟩ 就精確對到第 18 處;在 MPM 中,第 1 處可對應到 14、16、18、19 與 B。

The eight representations cross two time axes (onset, midtime) with four pitch axes: chromatic pitch (A0=0, middle C=39), morphetic pitch (staff position, A0=0, middle C=23, independent of accidentals), chroma (chromatic pitch mod 12) and morph (morphetic pitch mod 7). Their abbreviations are CPO, CPM, MPO, MPM, CO, CM, MO and MM.

There are three transformation classes. F_2STR is scaling along the time axis (the factor may be negative, i.e. retrograde), translation, and optional reflection in the pitch axis (inversion). F_2STRMod12 and F_2STRMod7 are the same combination with pitch translation and reflection taken mod 12 or mod 7. A time-scale factor of 0 collapses a melody into a chord, so playing the theme's notes simultaneously also becomes a transformation; such a transformation is not invertible, so the graph is no longer symmetric.

The author takes the 28 occurrences catalogued by Soucy (2009) plus three the author found, A, B and C, for 31 patterns, computes an IPTG for each representation (shown as adjacency tables), and provides reproducible Java code in the open-source OMNISIA project. For example, in CPM occurrence 1 maps exactly onto occurrence 18 by time reflection followed by translation by ⟨364,−12⟩; in MPM occurrence 1 maps onto 14, 16, 18, 19 and B.

限制Limitations

全篇只有一首曲子、一個主題,沒有任何量化評估:沒有召回率或精確率,沒有搜尋時間,也沒有在其他曲目上試。表徵的好處都是事後展示:已知的 31 處出現在哪種表徵下彼此相連,而不是用演算法在整首曲子裡搜尋、再看找到了什麼。

寬鬆的變換類會帶來大量誤報,但論文沒有衡量。在 MO 表徵下,除了不完整的第 23 處,每一處出現都能對應到「和弦化」的出現處 2、7、17、25、26、27。作者自己在第 2 節也指出,加九度與加四度和弦在拉威爾的作品中到處都是,未必是在引用 HAYDN 主題。也就是說,最寬鬆的表徵在這首曲子裡會把很多普通和弦也當成匹配,而論文沒有計算這有多少。

還有一些小問題:MO 的定義段落說 t 是「中點時間」,但 MO 照命名與圖說應該是起音時間;部分出現處(13、24、A)本身就是不完整的,只能靠部分匹配處理,但本文的 IPTG 只考慮完整、精確的匹配。在四種非模數表徵中,有 12 處出現(11、13、15、17、21–25、28、A、C)與任何其他出現處都沒有 F_2STR 關係。

The whole paper rests on one piece and one theme, with no quantitative evaluation: no recall or precision, no search times, no other repertoire. The benefits of each representation are shown after the fact, as which of the 31 known occurrences connect to each other, not by running a search over the piece and seeing what it finds.

Permissive transformation classes should produce many false positives, and the paper does not measure them. In the MO representation, every occurrence except the incomplete occurrence 23 maps onto the chordal occurrences 2, 7, 17, 25, 26 and 27. The author also notes in Section 2 that added-ninth and added-fourth chords are everywhere in Ravel and may not be references to the HAYDN theme. So the most permissive representations would count many ordinary chords in this piece as matches, and the paper does not count how many.

There are smaller issues. The paragraph defining MO says t is the midtime, though by its name and caption MO uses onset time. Some occurrences (13, 24, A) are incomplete and need partial matching, but the IPTGs here consider only complete, exact matches. In the four non-modular representations, 12 occurrences (11, 13, 15, 17, 21–25, 28, A and C) have no F_2STR relation to any other occurrence.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值在於把一個常被模糊帶過的問題講清楚:「這段算不算主題的出現」取決於你選的表徵和變換類,而不是演算法本身。用同一組 31 個樣式在八種表徵下對照,讀者能直接看到每種表徵換來了什麼:CO 是唯一能把八度錯置的第 1、11、22 處連起來的;CM 能找到第 18 處到第 22 處這種「逆行加八度錯置」;MO 能把第 B 處對到第 22 處。

音樂學上的副產品也有意思。31 處裡沒有任何一處在音高與節奏上跟 Écorcheville 公布的主題完全相同;第 23 處少了 G,作者懷疑是 Soucy 把低音譜號讀成高音譜號;作者另外找到的 B 處是一個完整的倒影,剛好把第 19、20 處串在一起。

對 MIR 實務來說,這篇更像一份設計手冊而非成果:要做多聲部符號音樂的變形比對,先決定要容許哪些音樂變形,再選對應的音高軸與時間軸。下一步需要的是在多首曲子上用完整搜尋去衡量每種表徵的召回與誤報,否則最寬鬆的 mod 7 加和弦化組合在實際曲庫裡可能什麼都配得上。

The paper's value is in stating clearly a question usually glossed over: whether a passage counts as an occurrence of a theme depends on the representation and transformation class chosen, not on the algorithm. Comparing the same 31 patterns across eight representations shows directly what each one buys. CO is the only one that links the octave-displaced occurrences 1, 11 and 22; CM finds retrograde-plus-octave-displacement relations like 18 to 22; MO maps occurrence B onto occurrence 22.

The musicological by-products are interesting too. None of the 31 occurrences matches the theme published by Écorcheville exactly in pitch and rhythm. Occurrence 23 lacks a G, and the author suspects Soucy read a bass clef as treble. The newly found occurrence B is a complete inversion that happens to bind occurrences 19 and 20 together.

For MIR practice this reads more like a design manual than a result: to do transformed matching in polyphonic symbolic music, first decide which musical transformations to allow, then pick the matching pitch and time axes. The next step is to measure recall and false positives of each representation with full search over many pieces; otherwise the most permissive mod 7 plus chordal-collapse combination may match almost anything in a real corpus.
