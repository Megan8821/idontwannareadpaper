Towards AI-Generated Music Plagiarism Detection as a Version Identification Problem

把 AI 生成音樂的抄襲偵測當成版本辨識問題

Fotis Koutsikos, Ioannis Prokopiou, Spyridon Kantarelis, Vassilis Lyberatos, Pantelis Vikatos, Athanasios Aidinis, Themos Stafylakis, Athanasios Voulodimos, Giorgos Stamou
arXiv:2610.09075 · cs.SD; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把「AI 生成的歌有沒有抄」改寫成音樂版本辨識(music version identification, MVI)問題,用 70 起真實抄襲案建出 350,654 對的 COPYCAT 基準。凍結的 CLEWS 嵌入只用單一距離門檻時,整體 F0.5 為 0.612;把逐維絕對差 |Δz| 丟進 XGBoost 後升到 0.803,但最難的「AI 重生成 + DSP」類別召回率仍只有 50.0%。Why this one: It recasts "did this AI-generated track copy something" as a music version identification (MVI) problem and builds COPYCAT, 350,654 pairs grown from 70 real plagiarism cases. A single distance threshold on frozen CLEWS embeddings reaches an overall F0.5 of 0.612; feeding the coordinate-wise absolute difference |Δz| to XGBoost raises it to 0.803, yet recall in the hardest category, AI re-synthesis plus DSP, is still only 50.0%.

動機Motivation

Suno、Udio 這類文字生成音樂系統普及之後,問題從「這首是不是 AI 做的」變成「這首 AI 做的歌是不是太像某首既有作品」。前者是合成音訊偵測,後者才是抄襲,兩者要抓的訊號完全不同:生成模型可以把音色、製作,甚至速度與音高全部換掉,只留下旋律或和聲的輪廓。法律上的「實質相似」(substantial similarity)沒有統一標準,能拿來訓練與評估的已判決案例又少,所以這個任務一直缺像樣的基準。

作者的賭注是:翻唱/版本辨識模型本來就被訓練成忽略演奏、編制、速度與製作的差異,只認作品本身,這正是抄襲偵測需要的不變性。真正的問題是,這種不變性能不能從「人翻唱人」延伸到「AI 重生成人的作品」。

Once text-to-music systems such as Suno and Udio became common, the question shifted from "is this track AI-made" to "is this AI-made track too close to an existing work". The first is synthetic-audio detection; only the second is plagiarism, and the signals differ: a generator can replace timbre, production and even tempo and pitch while keeping only the melodic or harmonic outline. Substantial similarity has no agreed legal standard, and adjudicated cases usable for training or evaluation are scarce, so the task has lacked a proper benchmark.

The authors' bet is that version identification models are already trained to ignore differences in performance, instrumentation, tempo and production and recognise only the work, which is exactly the invariance plagiarism detection needs. The real question is whether that invariance carries over from humans covering humans to AI re-synthesis of human works.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

過去的音樂抄襲偵測多走符號路線,例如 Liu 等人(ACM MM 2021)用二分圖匹配比對旋律、Park 等人(2022)用 Siamese CNN;音訊路線則有 Go(2025)的 Music Segment Transcription System 與 Lu 等人(2025)的 MelodySim。這些方法都是直接算兩段音樂的相似度。這篇改用現成的 MVI 模型:CLEWS(Serrà 等人,ICML 2025)是在 Discogs-VI 上以弱標註片段做監督式對比學習的 CQT 嵌入,WEALY(Mancini 等人,ICASSP 2026)則用凍結的 Whisper 做歌詞比對。

「拿版本辨識模型去查生成音樂有沒有複製」本身不是首創:YuE(Yuan 等人,2026)的記憶化稽核就用了 ByteCover2,Batlle-Roca 等人(ISMIR 2024)也用多種音訊相似度去量生成音樂對訓練資料的複製。這篇真正新的是 COPYCAT 這個按混淆程度分層的基準。

方法上最該點破的是:把兩個嵌入的逐維絕對差 |u−v| 交給分類器,是 Siamese 網路的標準做法。Koch 等人(2015)的一次學習 Siamese 網路用的就是加權的逐維 L1 距離,Sentence-BERT(Reimers 與 Gurevych,2019)的分類頭也串接 |u−v|。換句話說,論文的「coordinate-wise shift」等於在凍結嵌入上學一個加權距離,而論文沒有引用這條脈絡。

Earlier plagiarism detection was mostly symbolic, e.g. bipartite graph matching of melodies by Liu et al. (ACM MM 2021) and a Siamese CNN by Park et al. (2022); audio-based work includes Go's (2025) Music Segment Transcription System and MelodySim by Lu et al. (2025). All of these compute a direct similarity between two excerpts. This paper instead reuses off-the-shelf MVI models: CLEWS (Serrà et al., ICML 2025), CQT embeddings trained with supervised contrastive learning on weakly labelled Discogs-VI segments, and WEALY (Mancini et al., ICASSP 2026), which matches lyrics through a frozen Whisper encoder.

Using a version identification model to check generated music for copying is not new in itself: YuE (Yuan et al., 2026) used ByteCover2 in its memorization audit, and Batlle-Roca et al. (ISMIR 2024) measured replication of training data in generated music with several audio similarity metrics. What is genuinely new here is COPYCAT, a benchmark stratified by level of concealment.

On the method, the point worth making is that feeding the coordinate-wise absolute difference |u−v| of two embeddings to a classifier is the standard Siamese recipe. The one-shot Siamese network of Koch et al. (2015) uses a weighted component-wise L1 distance, and the Sentence-BERT (Reimers and Gurevych, 2019) classification head concatenates |u−v|. The paper's "coordinate-wise shift" is in effect a learned weighted distance on frozen embeddings, and the paper does not cite this line of work.

方法Method

COPYCAT 從 SMP 資料集出發:70 起真實抄襲指控,依時間標註切出 1,219 段 20 秒、22,050 Hz 的單聲道錨點,再用 HT Demucs v4 拆成人聲、貝斯、鼓與其他四軌。AI 衍生品來自三個能吃音訊條件的生成器:MusicGen-melody 以錨點的 chromagram 為條件整段重生成;AudioLDM 2 以錨點為聲學參考做擴散生成;MGE-LDM 保留一軌(貝斯、鼓或旋律和聲樂器)並補畫其餘伴奏。文字提示刻意隨機、與原曲風格無關,共得 5,472 對 AI 衍生品。再疊上十種 DSP 混淆(±2、±4 半音移調,0.90–1.10 倍速),擴成 73,590 個音訊變體。

負樣本在 CLEWS 嵌入空間分三種難度挖掘:隨機、同類別最近鄰、全域最近鄰,各約 79,000 對。最終共 350,654 對,正樣本 113,690、負樣本 236,964;正樣本裡「AI + DSP」佔 48.1%,「人類抄襲 + DSP」佔 33.0%。

模型端是凍結的 CLEWS(1024 維)與 WEALY(512 維),組合方式有三層:(1)每一支各自挑距離函數與門檻,目標是加重精確率的 F0.5;(2)依人聲活動偵測做分數融合,但網格搜尋選出 α=1.0,等於把 WEALY 完全丟掉;(3)在 |Δz| 上訓練 XGBoost,以原曲分組做 5 折交叉驗證,跑 10 個種子。

消融結果:五個「人聲有效」旗標單獨就有 F0.5 0.836,接近加進工程特徵後的 0.853,作者判定這是捷徑(旗標洩漏了類別)而移除。剩下 24 個工程特徵只有 0.617,幾乎等於 CLEWS 門檻的 0.612;完整 1024 維 |Δz| 到 0.811;只取最易變的 K=512 維是 0.792(每折 54 秒,1024 維要 119 秒),K=256 掉到 0.749。最終的 Hybrid Top-512(24 個特徵 + 512 維)整體 F0.5 為 0.803。

COPYCAT starts from the SMP dataset: 70 real plagiarism allegations, cut by their timestamps into 1,219 mono 20-second anchors at 22,050 Hz, each split into vocals, bass, drums and other with HT Demucs v4. AI derivatives come from three generators with audio conditioning: MusicGen-melody re-synthesises the whole excerpt from its chromagram; AudioLDM 2 runs diffusion with the anchor as acoustic reference; MGE-LDM keeps one stem (bass, drums or melodic/harmonic instruments) and inpaints the rest. Text prompts are randomised and deliberately unrelated to the source style, giving 5,472 AI-derivative pairs. Ten DSP obfuscations (±2 and ±4 semitones, 0.90–1.10× tempo) expand this to 73,590 audio variants.

Negatives are mined in CLEWS embedding space at three hardness levels, random, nearest within category and nearest globally, about 79,000 pairs each. The final set has 350,654 pairs, 113,690 positive and 236,964 negative; among positives, AI + DSP is 48.1% and human plagiarism + DSP 33.0%.

The models are frozen CLEWS (1024-d) and WEALY (512-d), combined at three levels: (1) each branch picks its own distance function and threshold for precision-weighted F0.5; (2) vocal-activity-gated score fusion, where grid search picks α = 1.0 and so discards WEALY entirely; (3) XGBoost on |Δz|, with 5-fold cross-validation grouped by original track and 10 seeds.

The ablation: the five vocal-validity flags alone reach F0.5 0.836, close to the 0.853 obtained with the engineered features, so the authors treat them as a shortcut (they leak the category) and drop them. The remaining 24 engineered features give 0.617, barely above the CLEWS threshold at 0.612. The full 1024-d |Δz| reaches 0.811; keeping only the K = 512 most volatile dimensions gives 0.792 (54 s per fold against 119 s for 1024), and K = 256 falls to 0.749. The final Hybrid Top-512 (24 features plus 512 dimensions) scores an overall F0.5 of 0.803.

限制Limitations

第一個問題在資料構造。每個 AI 衍生品都會把錨點用 Demucs 分出的原唱人聲(加上移調與變速)再混回去,所以「AI 生成」類別的正樣本其實都帶著原曲的人聲。這比較像「原唱 + AI 伴奏」,而不是一首被生成模型整首重寫的歌;實際上 Suno 這類系統不會把原唱貼回去。論文這樣做是為了讓人聲有效旗標不受樂器滲漏影響,但它也讓 WEALY 這一支的表現更難解讀。

第二,負樣本是在 CLEWS 空間挖出的最近鄰,也就是刻意挑 CLEWS 距離分不開的配對,所以 CLEWS 門檻吃虧幾乎是設計出來的;XGBoost 又在同一個分布上訓練與測試。交叉驗證只按原曲分組,三個生成器與十種 DSP 在訓練和測試裡都出現過,沒有任何「沒見過的生成器」測試;而論文最在意的 Suno、Udio,恰好因為不開放音訊條件而沒被納入。

第三是數字本身:最終的 Hybrid Top-512(0.803)其實比單用完整 1024 維 |Δz|(0.811)還低,選它是為了省訓練時間;在「原曲 + DSP」類別,精確率從門檻法的 96.9% 掉到 87.2%。最難的 AI + DSP 類別召回率 50.0%,也就是一半的抄襲還是會漏掉。作者在結論把系統定位成「交給專家複審前的初篩」,這個定位是誠實的。

The first problem is in the data construction. Every AI derivative has the anchor's Demucs-separated original vocals (pitch- and tempo-augmented) mixed back in, so positives in the AI categories all carry the source's vocals. That is closer to "original singer plus AI backing" than to a song wholly rewritten by a generator; in practice a system like Suno does not paste the original vocals back. The paper does this so the vocal-validity flags are not affected by instrumental bleed, but it also makes the WEALY branch harder to interpret.

Second, negatives are nearest neighbours mined in CLEWS space, i.e. chosen precisely because CLEWS distance cannot separate them, so the CLEWS threshold losing is close to built in, and XGBoost is trained and tested on that same distribution. Cross-validation groups only by original track; all three generators and all ten DSP transforms appear in both training and test, with no unseen-generator test, and Suno and Udio, the systems the paper cares most about, are excluded because they offer no audio conditioning.

Third, the numbers themselves: the final Hybrid Top-512 (0.803) is actually below the full 1024-d |Δz| alone (0.811) and is chosen to save training time, and on Original + DSP its precision drops from the threshold's 96.9% to 87.2%. Recall in the hardest AI + DSP category is 50.0%, so half the plagiarism is still missed. The authors' conclusion positions the system as triage before expert review, which is an honest framing.

討論Discussion

這篇最值得記下的不是 0.612 到 0.803 這個數字,而是距離門檻為什麼會失敗。作者畫了 CLEWS 距離對 DSP 強度的趨勢線:移調後的原曲始終遠在門檻之內;人類翻唱與 MGE-LDM(保留一軌原聲)落在門檻附近;MusicGen 與 AudioLDM 2 的衍生品一開始就被推到接近最遠的距離,DSP 再怎麼加,趨勢線都是平的。也就是說,對整段重生成而言,「偏了多遠」已經不帶資訊,資訊只剩在「往哪些維度偏」。

逐類別看更清楚:CLEWS 門檻在 AI + DSP 的召回率只有 10.3%,XGBoost 拉到 50.0%,將近五倍;人類抄襲則從 30.6% 到 86.5%。WEALY 在分數融合裡被直接歸零,但在 XGBoost 的特徵重要度裡,WEALY 距離反而是排名最高的工程特徵族群。標量融合會把一個「精確率低、但帶互補資訊」的分支整個丟掉,這對任何多分支相似度系統都是有用的提醒。

我對這篇的保留是:它證明的是「在同一組生成器與混淆分布之內,學出來的加權距離勝過固定距離」,這比較接近度量學習的常識,而不是證明 MVI 表徵能遷移到 AI 抄襲。要回答標題的問題,下一步應該是留出生成器做測試,並做一版不把原唱人聲混回去的資料。

The finding worth keeping is not the 0.612-to-0.803 number but why distance thresholds fail. The authors plot CLEWS distance against DSP intensity: pitch-shifted originals stay well inside the threshold; human covers and MGE-LDM (which keeps one original stem) sit near it; MusicGen and AudioLDM 2 derivatives start out near maximal distance, and their trendlines stay flat however much DSP is added. For whole-excerpt re-synthesis, "how far it drifted" carries no information any more; what remains is "along which dimensions".

Per category it is clearer still: the CLEWS threshold's recall on AI + DSP is 10.3% and XGBoost lifts it to 50.0%, nearly five-fold; human plagiarism goes from 30.6% to 86.5%. WEALY gets zero weight in score fusion, yet in XGBoost's feature importance the WEALY distances are the highest-ranked engineered feature family. Scalar fusion can throw away a branch that is low in precision but carries complementary information, a useful reminder for any multi-branch similarity system.

My reservation is that the paper shows "within one set of generators and obfuscations, a learned weighted distance beats a fixed one", which is closer to standard metric learning than to evidence that MVI representations transfer to AI plagiarism. Answering the title's question would need held-out generators and a version of the data without the original vocals mixed back in.

Toward Part-Aware Choral Transcription with singing voice assignment

邁向分聲部的合唱轉譜:結合歌聲聲部指派

Hanyu Meng, Zhanhong He, Zixun Guo, Yaolong Ju
arXiv:2610.09861 · eess.AS; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：PawCT 號稱第一個從合唱混音直接輸出 SATB 四條獨立音符軌的端到端模型:在 YouChorale 上,分聲部音符 F1(50 ms)從改裝基線的 0.165 升到 0.225,不分聲部的 PagCT 也把先前最佳的 0.237 推到 0.382。但絕對值仍低,而且主打的音域與連續性先驗,比不加先驗的 PawCT(0.217)只多 0.008。Why this one: PawCT is presented as the first end-to-end model that outputs four separate SATB note tracks directly from a choral mixture. On YouChorale, part-aware note F1 (50 ms) rises from 0.165 for an adapted baseline to 0.225, and the part-agnostic PagCT lifts the previous best from 0.237 to 0.382. Absolute values remain low, and the headline range and continuity priors add only 0.008 over plain PawCT (0.217).

動機Motivation

鋼琴轉譜已經相當成熟,但合唱轉譜的實際用途——排練、教學、還原樂譜——需要的是女高音、女低音、男高音、男低音(SATB)各自一條譜,而不是把四個聲部攪在一起的一條音符軌。最近的音符層級合唱轉譜(Yu 等人)輸出的正是這種合併軌。

合唱比多樂器轉譜難在兩點。第一,人聲起音柔和、音高會滑動,音符邊界難切;第二,四個聲部是同一種「樂器」,音色線索很弱,音域又互相重疊,光看音高分不出是誰唱的。多樂器轉譜可以靠樂器身分把音符分到不同軌,合唱沒有這條路可走。

Piano transcription is fairly mature, but the practical uses of choral transcription, rehearsal, teaching and score reconstruction, need separate parts for soprano, alto, tenor and bass (SATB), not one note track with all four voices merged. Recent note-level choral transcription (Yu et al.) outputs exactly such a merged track.

Choirs are harder than multi-instrument ensembles in two ways. First, sung onsets are soft and pitch glides, so note boundaries are hard to segment. Second, the four parts are the same "instrument", so timbral cues are weak while ranges overlap, and pitch alone cannot say who sang a note. Multi-instrument transcription can route notes to tracks by instrument identity; choral transcription has no such route.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

早期的合唱轉譜多停在音高層級:McLeod 等人(2017)結合聲學模型與符號音樂模型處理聲部指派,Cuesta 與 Gómez 的工作以 pitch salience 加深度網路估計四部的音高軌跡並分配聲部。這些方法輸出的是 F0 曲線,不是有起訖點的音符。Yu 等人的音符層級合唱模型補上了音符,卻丟掉了聲部。PawCT 的骨幹則幾乎原封不動地來自 Kong 等人(2021)的高解析度鋼琴轉譜:CRNN 編碼器加上起音、收音、frame 三種輸出頭。

論文自稱「第一個」端到端分聲部合唱轉譜,在合唱這個子領域大致成立。但它的核心結構——共用編碼器、每個聲源一組 piano-roll 輸出頭、再用 presence 頭關掉不存在的聲源——最像的是 Manilow 等人(2020)的 Cerberus:同一個網路對每個樂器各輸出一組遮罩與 piano roll。差別在於 Cerberus 的各個頭靠樂器音色區分,PawCT 的四個頭面對的是同一種音色,所以要另外靠音域與旋律連續性的先驗。論文沒有引用 Cerberus。

OC 先驗(依起音分組、按音高由高到低排序、懲罰聲部內大跳與重疊)是符號音樂聲部分離的經典啟發式,屬於 Chew 與 Wu(2004)contig 映射、Temperley 串流規則那一路「音高鄰近 + 時間連續」的原則。論文引用了這類原則,但把它用在改寫訓練標籤,而不是推論。

Earlier choral transcription mostly stayed at pitch level: McLeod et al. (2017) handled voice assignment by combining acoustic and symbolic music models, and Cuesta and Gómez estimated four-part F0 trajectories with pitch salience and deep networks and assigned them to voices. Those outputs are F0 contours, not notes with onsets and offsets. The note-level choral model of Yu et al. added notes but dropped the parts. PawCT's backbone comes almost unchanged from the high-resolution piano transcription model of Kong et al. (2021): a CRNN encoder with onset, offset and frame heads.

The paper's "first" end-to-end part-aware choral transcription claim largely holds within the choral subfield. But its core structure, a shared encoder, one set of piano-roll heads per source, and a presence head that silences absent sources, is closest to Cerberus (Manilow et al., 2020), one network that outputs a mask and a piano roll per instrument. The difference is that Cerberus's heads are separated by instrument timbre, while PawCT's four heads all face the same timbre and therefore need range and melodic-continuity priors. The paper does not cite Cerberus.

The OC prior, grouping by onset, ordering by descending pitch, penalising within-part leaps and overlaps, is a classic symbolic voice-separation heuristic in the pitch-proximity plus temporal-continuity tradition of Chew and Wu's (2004) contig mapping and Temperley's streaming rules. The paper cites such principles but uses them to rewrite training labels rather than at inference.

方法Method

PawCT 的輸入是 16 kHz、229 bin 的 log-Mel 頻譜(hop 160 樣本)。編碼器是四個卷積區塊加兩層雙向 GRU(每方向 256),每個 frame 得到 512 維的表示。S、A、T、B 各有一組 sigmoid 頭,輸出 88 個 MIDI 音高的起音、收音與初步 frame 機率;四個聲部的初步 frame 再和切斷梯度的起音、收音串在一起,經一層雙向 GRU 精修。另有一個 presence 頭,把表示對時間取平均後過兩層 MLP,判斷哪幾個聲部有出現,並用它的機率閘控各聲部輸出,沒唱的聲部就輸出空軌。

訓練目標有三項:各聲部的 BCE、聲部存在的 BCE(權重 0.2),以及「聯集損失」——把四個聲部的預測取最大值合併,再對合併後的真值算 BCE。聯集損失的用意是:就算聲部判錯,音符本身也要留住。

RP 與 OC 不是額外的損失,而是改寫訓練用的聲部標籤。RP 對每個音符算一個成本:超出該聲部音域(S 60–88、A 55–79、T 48–72、B 40–67)的半音數,加上偏離音域中心的小懲罰,再加上與原始標籤不同時的懲罰 λ_part=2,取成本最低的聲部。OC 在此之上加入聲部內音高跳躍(λ_cont=0.35)與時間重疊(λ_ov=4)的懲罰,並對同一起音的至多四個音,窮舉所有保持音高順序的一對一指派。

對照組 PagCT 是同一架構、只留一組全域輸出頭(14.84M 參數);兩段式基線則先用 PagCT 轉譜,再用 BiLSTM 符號模型 Post-VA 把每個音符分到 SATB。資料是 YouChorale:452 首錄音、261 首曲子、共 22 小時 25 分,其中 418 首是完整四部;訓練時做 ±1–3 半音移調擴增,資料量擴成約七倍。

PawCT takes a 16 kHz, 229-bin log-Mel spectrogram (160-sample hop). The encoder is four convolutional blocks and a two-layer BiGRU (256 per direction), giving a 512-d representation per frame. Each of S, A, T and B has its own sigmoid heads for onset, offset and preliminary frame probabilities over 88 MIDI pitches; the four parts' preliminary frames are concatenated with the gradient-detached onsets and offsets and refined by a one-layer BiGRU. A presence head mean-pools the representation over time and passes it through a two-layer MLP to decide which parts are active, and its probabilities gate each part's outputs so an absent part yields an empty track.

The objective has three terms: per-part BCE, part-presence BCE (weight 0.2), and a "union loss" that max-pools the four parts' predictions and applies BCE against the merged ground truth. The union loss is meant to keep a note even when its part is wrong.

RP and OC are not extra losses; they rewrite the part labels used as training targets. RP gives each note a cost: semitones outside the part's range (S 60–88, A 55–79, T 48–72, B 40–67), a small penalty for distance from the range centre, and λ_part = 2 when the part disagrees with the original label; the lowest-cost part wins. OC adds penalties for within-part pitch leaps (λ_cont = 0.35) and temporal overlap (λ_ov = 4), and for each onset group of up to four notes it searches all one-to-one assignments that preserve pitch order.

The control, PagCT, is the same architecture with a single global set of heads (14.84M parameters). The two-stage baseline transcribes with PagCT and then assigns each note to SATB with a BiLSTM symbolic model, Post-VA. The data is YouChorale: 452 recordings of 261 pieces, 22 h 25 min in total, 418 of them full four-part; training uses ±1–3 semitone transposition, enlarging the data about sevenfold.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是差距小到需要變異數,論文卻沒有給。PawCT-RP-OC 的 0.225 對上不加先驗的 PawCT 的 0.217,只差 0.008;只加 RP 反而掉到 0.209。這些都是單次訓練、沒有信賴區間,作者主打的兩個先驗到底是穩定的增益還是種子雜訊,從論文看不出來。相比之下,聯集損失的效果(0.190 → 0.217,四個聲部都進步)才是比較站得住的發現。

第二,兩段式基線不太公平。Post-VA 是在真值音符上訓練、卻在 PagCT 解碼出來(有漏、有碎片)的音符上測試,訓練與測試分布不一致,0.175 有一部分來自這個落差。作者把它解釋成「事後指派救不回漏掉的音符」,這沒錯,但沒有試過拿 PagCT 的輸出去訓練 Post-VA。

第三,不分聲部從 0.237 到 0.382 的進步,有一部分是模型大小:PagCT 有 14.84M 參數,Yu 等人的模型是 4.23M;不做擴增時 PagCT 是 0.333。分聲部模型的推論門檻設成 frame 0.03、onset 0.01、offset 0.005,低到暗示輸出機率校準得很差。最後,0.225 這個絕對水準離排練或還原樂譜能直接使用還很遠。

The biggest problem is that the margins are small enough to need variance estimates, and none are given. PawCT-RP-OC at 0.225 against plain PawCT at 0.217 is a gap of 0.008, and RP alone drops to 0.209. All are single training runs without confidence intervals, so whether the two headline priors give a stable gain or seed noise cannot be read from the paper. By contrast, the union loss (0.190 to 0.217, improving all four parts) is the more defensible finding.

Second, the two-stage baseline is not quite fair. Post-VA is trained on ground-truth notes but tested on PagCT's decoded notes, which are missing and fragmented, so part of its 0.175 comes from that train-test mismatch. The authors explain it as post-hoc assignment being unable to recover missed notes, which is true, but they do not try training Post-VA on PagCT outputs.

Third, part of the part-agnostic jump from 0.237 to 0.382 is model size: PagCT has 14.84M parameters against 4.23M for Yu et al., and without augmentation PagCT scores 0.333. The part-aware models' inference thresholds of 0.03 (frame), 0.01 (onset) and 0.005 (offset) are low enough to suggest badly calibrated probabilities. Finally, an absolute level of 0.225 is still far from directly usable for rehearsal or score reconstruction.

討論Discussion

這篇最有用的觀察在表 1:MuScriptor 的 frame F1 是 0.730,和 PagCT 的 0.738 幾乎一樣,但 50 ms 音符 F1 只有 0.084(PagCT 為 0.382);MT3 與 YourMT3+ 都只有 0.127;Onsets and Frames 精確率 0.450、召回率 0.178。也就是說,大型通用轉譜模型追得到合唱的音高,卻切不出柔和連音的起點——「音高追得準」不等於「音符切得對」。對任何想直接拿通用 AMT 模型處理人聲合奏的人,這是很實際的提醒。

跨資料集的結果比主表更有說服力。只在 YouChorale 上訓練,PawCT-RP-OC 在 CSD 上平均 0.174、兩段式為 0.118;在 Cantoría 上是 0.313 對 0.115。端到端相對兩段式的優勢在兩個沒見過的資料集上都存在;而在 Cantoría 上,先驗比不加先驗的 PawCT 多了 0.042(0.313 對 0.271),比在 YouChorale 上的 0.008 明顯得多。這暗示先驗在分布外可能更有價值,但同樣沒有變異數可以確認。

VA Rate(分聲部 F1 除以不分聲部 F1)要小心讀:改裝版 YourMT3+ 的 VA Rate 是 62.20%,看起來不差,但它的絕對 F1 只有 0.079。作者自己也提醒要和絕對值一起看,這點處理得很誠實。

The most useful observation is in Table 1: MuScriptor's frame F1 is 0.730, almost the same as PagCT's 0.738, but its 50-ms note F1 is 0.084 (PagCT 0.382); MT3 and YourMT3+ both get 0.127; Onsets and Frames has precision 0.450 against recall 0.178. Large general-purpose transcription models can track choral pitch but cannot segment the onsets of soft legato notes: accurate pitch tracking is not accurate note segmentation. That is a practical warning for anyone planning to apply a general AMT model to vocal ensembles.

The cross-dataset results are more convincing than the main table. Trained only on YouChorale, PawCT-RP-OC averages 0.174 on CSD against 0.118 for the two-stage pipeline, and 0.313 against 0.115 on Cantoría. The end-to-end advantage holds on both unseen datasets, and on Cantoría the priors add 0.042 over plain PawCT (0.313 vs 0.271), much more than the 0.008 on YouChorale. This hints that the priors may matter more out of distribution, though again there is no variance to confirm it.

VA Rate (part-aware F1 divided by part-agnostic F1) needs careful reading: the adapted YourMT3+ has a VA Rate of 62.20%, which looks decent, but its absolute F1 is 0.079. The authors themselves warn that it must be read alongside absolute values, which is handled honestly.

Tracing Inputs, Verifying Outputs: Validating Attribution in Music Generation

追蹤輸入、驗證輸出:音樂生成中的歸屬驗證

Taejun Kim, Wonil Kim, Jongmin Jung, Hyeongseok Wi, Sangeun Kum, Keunhyoung Luke Kim, Taehyoung Kim, Dongjoo Moon, Seungsoon Park, Taewan Kim, Virginie Berger, Juhan Nam, Jongpil Lee
arXiv:2610.09637 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Neutune 用只吃音訊、不吃文字的分軌生成器 MixAudio 實作「以輸入紀錄做歸屬」,再用換條件實驗證明提示決定音色、脈絡決定和聲(換掉脈絡後,和舊脈絡的 chroma 相關從 0.21 掉到 0.02),並以自家版本辨識模型 musicDNA 稽核記憶化:994 個查詢裡只找到 1 例未列名的錄音重現。但 musicDNA 的細節沒有公開,標註者是作者本人,32 例確認記憶化裡有 31 例是資料重複登記。Why this one: Neutune implements "attribution by input records" with MixAudio, a stem generator conditioned on audio only, no text. Conditioning swaps show the prompt sets timbre and the context sets harmony (after a context swap, chroma correlation with the old context falls from 0.21 to 0.02), and an audit with their own version identification model, musicDNA, finds a single unnamed reproduction of a recording among 994 queries. But musicDNA is not described, the annotators are the authors, and 31 of the 32 confirmed memorizations are duplicate registrations in the catalog.

動機Motivation

GEMA 對 Suno(2026)一案的一審認定:模型記住受保護作品並在輸出中重現,構成侵權。這把一個問題推上檯面——一首生成的歌,到底該算是哪些既有作品的貢獻?作者把歸屬拆成兩個必須分開舉證的主張:某首曲子確實被用來產生這次的輸出,而且它的音樂特性確實影響了輸出。光看相似度不夠,因為節奏、和聲或演奏風格的相似,可能來自訓練集裡的其他歌;反過來,確定有用到,也不代表它真的起了作用。

他們的做法是讓生成只吃有出處的音訊並記錄下來,第一個主張就從推論變成紀錄。剩下要證明的是兩件事:這些輸入真的有影響輸出,以及輸出沒有偷偷重現紀錄以外的訓練曲。

In GEMA v. Suno (2026), the court of first instance held that protected works memorized by a model and reproduced in its outputs infringed copyright. That brings a question to the surface: which existing works should a generated song be credited to? The authors split attribution into two claims that need separate evidence: a given track was used to produce this output, and its musical characteristics actually shaped the output. Similarity alone is not enough, since rhythmic, harmonic or stylistic resemblance can come from other training tracks; conversely, confirmed use does not show the track had any effect.

Their approach is to condition generation only on audio with known provenance and record it, which turns the first claim from inference into a record. Two things remain to be shown: that those inputs really shape the output, and that outputs do not quietly reproduce training tracks outside the record.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者把既有的歸屬方法分成三類。訓練資料歸屬(training data attribution, TDA)用影響函數(Koh 與 Liang,2017)或 TRAK(Park 等人,2023)估計移除某首訓練曲會改變多少;Choi 等人(2025)把它用在音樂生成,Deng 等人(2025)提議據此分潤。問題是這個量只能靠重訓驗證,Han 等人(2026)還發現同一批訓練曲會在不同輸出裡都排到前面。輸出端歸屬是在訓練集裡找相像的,例如 Barnett 等人(2024)以音訊嵌入檢索、Batlle-Roca 等人(2024)用多種相似度量測複製片段。第三類是輸入端歸屬:Morreale 等人(2025)提出 attribution-by-design,作者團隊自己的 Kim 等人(2025)做了記錄元件層級輸入的音樂 AI agent,這篇補上那篇沒做的驗證。

生成器本身的設計並不新。以一小段參考音訊決定風格、以既有分軌為脈絡生成新分軌,最接近的是 Sony CSL 的 Diff-A-Riff(Nistal 等人,2024):同樣吃一段音訊參考加上伴奏脈絡,輸出單一樂器軌;StemGen(Parker 等人,2024)也是以既有分軌為條件生成新分軌。MixAudio 的差別是一次可以生成任意多軌、每軌有自己的提示,而且完全不吃文字。論文在相關研究引用了 Nistal 等人(2024),但附錄的候選基線表沒有列入 Diff-A-Riff,也沒說明原因。

用版本辨識模型做記憶化稽核也不是首創:YuE(Yuan 等人,2026)就用過 ByteCover2,只是只看分數分布、沒有人工聆聽。這篇的新東西是讓人聽過每一個被標記的分軌,留下一組可以拿來評估偵測器的確認標籤。

The authors sort prior attribution methods into three kinds. Training data attribution (TDA) estimates how much removing a training track would change an output, via influence functions (Koh and Liang, 2017) or TRAK (Park et al., 2023); Choi et al. (2025) applied it to music generation and Deng et al. (2025) proposed royalties based on it. The catch is that this quantity can only be verified by retraining, and Han et al. (2026) find the same training tracks rank at the top across different outputs. Output-based attribution searches the training set for resemblance, e.g. embedding retrieval by Barnett et al. (2024) and multiple similarity measures for replicated fragments by Batlle-Roca et al. (2024). The third kind is input-based: Morreale et al. (2025) proposed attribution-by-design, and the authors' own Kim et al. (2025) built a music AI agent that records component-level input use; this paper adds the validation that work left open.

The generator design itself is not new. Using a short audio reference to set style and existing stems as context for a new stem is closest to Sony CSL's Diff-A-Riff (Nistal et al., 2024), which also takes an audio reference plus an accompaniment context and outputs a single instrument track; StemGen (Parker et al., 2024) likewise generates new stems conditioned on existing ones. MixAudio differs by generating any number of stems in one pass, each with its own prompt, and taking no text at all. The paper cites Nistal et al. (2024) in its related work, but Diff-A-Riff is not in the appendix's table of candidate baselines, and no reason is given.

Using version identification for memorization audits is not a first either: YuE (Yuan et al., 2026) used ByteCover2, though only looking at score distributions without listening. What is new is that every flagged stem was listened to, leaving a set of confirmed labels on which detectors can be evaluated.

方法Method

MixAudio 是 20 億參數、在離散 token 上運作的生成器:神經音訊編解碼器以殘差向量量化,把每個提示、脈絡與目標分軌轉成 token 序列。生成單位是曲子的一個段落;一條輸入序列裡放提示與要生成的目標軌,多生成一軌就多接一組「提示–目標」。有脈絡時把脈絡接在最前面,並與目標軌共用位置編碼以保持時間對齊。段落的調性與拍點位置另外輸入,分軌層級的位置編碼把第 i 個提示綁到第 i 個目標。在紀錄上,提示對應錄音端的貢獻,脈絡對應作曲端(和聲與速度)的貢獻;作者強調這是根據模型的實測行為,不是著作權法上的界定。

評估集有 6,810 個實例、15,034 條目標分軌,來自內部資料庫的訓練與驗證切分,以及外部的 MoisesDB 與 MUSDB18。每條目標軌的提示,是同一段落裡該軌自己錄音的 1–2 秒片段。對照組是 ACE-Step v1.5 的 Lego 模式(2B 版本,與 MixAudio 同大小)。

忠實度用兩個測試。提示遵循:生成軌與自己提示的相似度(Matched)減去與別首提示的相似度(Random),風格用去中心化的 MERT 餘弦,音色用 MFCC 餘弦。換條件:只換提示或只換脈絡、另一個固定,看音色(MFCC)與和聲(在拍點網格上逐小節平均的 chroma 相關)往哪邊移動。

記憶化稽核只查有旋律的樂器軌(不查貝斯與鼓),驗證與訓練兩組各 497 個查詢;訓練組沿用訓練時完全相同的條件,最容易誘發記憶化。四個檢索器——musicDNA、CLEWS、CLAP、MERT——各取驗證組第 25 高的分數為門檻(標記前 5%)。三位作者盲聽每個被標記的分軌,分為 Type I(錄音重現)、Type II(換音色重現作曲)、A(重複登記)、B(無關)、C(只共享常見元素)、D(次要作曲元素)。

MixAudio is a 2-billion-parameter generator working on discrete tokens: a neural audio codec with residual vector quantization turns every prompt, context and target stem into token sequences. The unit of generation is one section of a track. A single input sequence holds the prompts and target stems, and one more stem means one more prompt–target pair. A context, when present, is prepended and shares positional encoding with the targets to keep them time-aligned. The section's key and beat positions are extra inputs, and a stem-level positional encoding binds the i-th prompt to the i-th target. In the record, the prompt maps to the recording-side contribution and the context to the composition-side one (harmony and tempo); the authors stress this rests on measured model behaviour, not copyright law.

The evaluation set has 6,810 instances and 15,034 target stems from the in-house training and validation splits and the external MoisesDB and MUSDB18. Each target's prompt is a 1–2 s excerpt of that stem's own recording in the same section. The baseline is ACE-Step v1.5 in Lego mode (the 2B checkpoint, the same size as MixAudio).

Faithfulness gets two tests. Prompt adherence takes a generated stem's similarity to its own prompt (Matched) minus its similarity to another track's prompt (Random), with style as centred MERT cosine and timbre as MFCC cosine. Conditioning swaps replace only the prompt or only the context, holding the other fixed, and check where timbre (MFCC) and harmony (bar-averaged chroma correlation on the beat grid) move.

The memorization audit covers only melody-carrying stems (not bass or drums), with 497 queries each in a validation and a training split; training queries reuse the exact conditioning seen in training, the setting most likely to draw out memorization. Four retrievers, musicDNA, CLEWS, CLAP and MERT, each take the 25th-highest validation score as threshold (flagging the top 5%). Three authors blind-listen to every flagged stem and label it Type I (recording reproduced), Type II (composition reproduced in another timbre), A (duplicate registration), B (unrelated), C (commonplace elements only) or D (incidental composition).

限制Limitations

最核心的偵測器 musicDNA 是作者公司的自家模型,論文對它的架構、訓練資料、訓練目標都沒有交代,只知道它用 20 秒視窗、取平方歐氏距離。所以「musicDNA 精確率與召回率最高」這個結論無法重現。評估本身也有循環:被評估的 171 個分軌,是由包含 musicDNA 在內的四個檢索器標記出來的,召回率只算池子裡的確認案例,任何檢索器都沒標到的記憶化根本不會進來;標註者又是三位作者。論文有註明召回率只限池內、計數是下限,也交代了標註者身分,算是坦白。

第二,32 例確認記憶化裡有 31 例是「重複登記」:同一份錄音在資料庫裡被登記了兩次,生成軌其實是在像自己提示的來源。這種近乎一模一樣的配對,是任何版本辨識模型最容易抓到的情況。所以 musicDNA 召回 29/32(0.91)對 CLEWS 22/32(0.69)這組比較,測的主要是「認出同一份錄音」,而不是論文真正關心的 Type II(換音色重現作曲)——後者一例都沒出現。CLEWS 用的是 SHS100K 的公開權重,不是 Discogs-VI 版本,也可能因此吃虧。

第三,提示是目標軌自己同一段落錄音的 1–2 秒,所以 Matched 分數有一部分是在量「重建原軌」,而不是遵循任意一段參考音訊。整個系統只在 Neutune 自己授權的資料庫上評估;完全不吃文字,也讓它無法和主流文字生成音樂系統直接比較,作者承認沒測過只用音訊條件是否犧牲品質。生成品質在聽測裡仍明顯低於真實分軌:音質 MOS 為 2.80,ACE 2.28,真實分軌 3.68。

The central detector, musicDNA, is the authors' company's in-house model, and the paper gives no architecture, training data or objective, only that it uses 20 s windows and squared Euclidean distance. The claim that musicDNA has the best precision and recall therefore cannot be reproduced. The evaluation is also circular: the 171 judged stems were flagged by four retrievers including musicDNA, recall counts only confirmed cases in that pool, so a memorization no retriever flagged never enters; and the annotators are three of the authors. The paper does note that recall is limited to the pool and the counts are lower bounds, and states who the annotators are, which is candid.

Second, 31 of the 32 confirmed memorizations are "duplicates": the same recording registered twice in the catalog, so the generated stem resembles its own prompt's source. Such near-identical pairs are the easiest case for any version identification model. The comparison of musicDNA recalling 29/32 (0.91) against CLEWS 22/32 (0.69) thus mostly tests "recognise the same recording", not the paper's real concern, Type II (composition reproduced in a different timbre), of which there was not a single case. CLEWS also runs on its public SHS100K checkpoint rather than the Discogs-VI one, which may handicap it.

Third, the prompt is a 1–2 s excerpt of the target stem's own recording in the same section, so the Matched score partly measures reconstruction of the original stem rather than following an arbitrary reference. Everything is evaluated on Neutune's own licensed catalog; taking no text also rules out direct comparison with mainstream text-to-music systems, and the authors admit they did not test whether audio-only conditioning costs quality. Listening tests still put generation quality well below real stems: quality MOS is 2.80, against 2.28 for ACE and 3.68 for real stems.

討論Discussion

這篇最有說服力的是換條件實驗,它把「輸入有沒有用」從相似度變成可檢驗的因果陳述。換掉脈絡後,MixAudio 生成軌與新脈絡的 chroma 相關從 0.00 升到 0.21(真實分軌對自己脈絡是 0.20),與舊脈絡的相關從 0.21 掉到 0.02(無關配對的水準就是 0.02),音色則留在提示上(0.92 → 0.89)。ACE 不論怎麼換,與脈絡的 chroma 相關都在 0.00 到 0.10 之間——它根本沒在跟脈絡的和聲,所以換了也沒東西可動。附錄的移調測試結論一致:脈絡移 ±2 半音後,MixAudio 有 76% 跟著移(上限 86%),ACE 只有 33%(下限 22%)。

提示遵循也示範了一個值得學的評估細節。只報 Matched 時,ACE 在多數來源的 MERT 風格相似度看起來更好,但 ACE 的 Random 分數也高(例如 MoisesDB 上 0.264,MixAudio 為 0.124),表示它的輸出彼此很像。改看 Matched − Random,MixAudio 在每個來源、兩種指標都領先,例如訓練來源的 MERT 是 0.535 對 0.428。很多生成論文只報 Matched,這種扣掉基線的做法應該成為慣例。

那一例 Type I 的追蹤也很有意思:用原始訓練條件重生成 8 次,8 次都重現同一段錄音;拿掉脈絡後是 0 次。這表示訓練時見過的脈絡本身就足以把記住的分軌「拉出來」,和 Roh 等人(2025)發現歌詞提示能讓上線模型吐出訓練曲是同一種現象。對一個以「我們有紀錄」為賣點的系統,這反而是最該寫進使用規範的一點。

The most convincing part is the swap experiment, which turns "did the input matter" from a similarity claim into a testable causal one. After a context swap, MixAudio's chroma correlation with the new context rises from 0.00 to 0.21 (real stems score 0.20 against their own context), its correlation with the old context falls from 0.21 to 0.02 (the unrelated-pair level is 0.02), and timbre stays with the prompt (0.92 to 0.89). ACE's chroma correlation with any context stays between 0.00 and 0.10 in every condition: it does not follow the context's harmony at all, so a swap has nothing to move. The transposition test in the appendix agrees: with the context shifted ±2 semitones, MixAudio follows 76% of the time (ceiling 86%) and ACE 33% (floor 22%).

Prompt adherence also shows an evaluation detail worth copying. Reporting Matched alone, ACE looks better on MERT style similarity for most sources, but its Random scores are high too (0.264 on MoisesDB against MixAudio's 0.124), meaning its outputs resemble each other. On Matched − Random, MixAudio leads on every source and both metrics, e.g. 0.535 against 0.428 for MERT on the training source. Many generation papers report Matched only; subtracting the baseline should become standard practice.

The follow-up on the single Type I case is telling: regenerated eight times with its original training conditioning, it reproduces the same recording in all 8; with the context removed, in 0 of 8. A context seen in training is enough to draw a memorized stem out, the same phenomenon as Roh et al. (2025) finding that lyric prompts make deployed models emit training songs. For a system whose selling point is "we keep records", this is the point most worth writing into its usage rules.
