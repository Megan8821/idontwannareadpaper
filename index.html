Revisiting Expressive Timing in Piano Performance at Scale: A Distant Listening, Multi-Corpus Study

大規模重探鋼琴演奏的表情時值：以「遠距聆聽」橫跨六個 MIDI 語料庫檢驗速度不變性、圓滑／斷奏與旋律領先

Patricia Hu, Huan Zhang, Silvan David Peter, Carlos Eduardo Cancino-Chacón, Simon Dixon, Gerhard Widmer
DOI:10.5334/tismir.317 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-07-17
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：演奏研究的經典假說（Repp 的速度關係不變性、Bresin 與 Battel 的圓滑／斷奏、Goebl 的旋律領先）大多建立在兩三位鋼琴家、一兩首曲子的實驗室錄音上。這篇把六個與樂譜逐音對齊的 MIDI 語料庫（780 首、6,330 場演奏、約 636 小時、1,400 萬個音）拿來重驗：記號對 KOR 的影響在三個資料集都顯著，旋律領先約 17.39 毫秒、領先對落後約 70:30，與力度差呈正相關；音程協和度與重疊的相關不顯著（r = −0.50，P = 0.21）。選它是因為它示範了大資料能怎麼補實驗室研究，但也要注意：摘要說時值模式「不」隨速度等比縮放，結論卻說「大致」等比縮放，兩處講法互相矛盾。Why this one: The classic hypotheses of performance research (Repp's relational invariance with tempo, Bresin and Battel on legato/staccato, Goebl on melody lead) mostly rest on lab recordings of two or three pianists playing one or two pieces. This paper retests them on six score-aligned MIDI corpora (780 pieces, 6,330 performances, about 636 hours, 14M notes): score markings affect KOR significantly in all three datasets tested, melody lead averages about 17.39 ms with a roughly 70:30 lead-to-lag ratio and correlates positively with velocity difference, and interval consonance shows no significant link to overlap (r = −0.50, P = 0.21). It was picked as a demonstration of how large-scale data can complement lab studies, with one caveat: the abstract says timing patterns do not scale proportionally with tempo, while the conclusion says they generally do. The two statements contradict each other.

動機Motivation

表情演奏研究的奠基論文幾乎都是小樣本、受控設計：Repp（1994）讓兩位鋼琴家以三種速度各彈三次舒曼〈夢幻曲〉，Desain 與 Honing（1994）讓兩位鋼琴家以三種速度彈貝多芬 WoO 70 的主題與第一變奏，兩篇對同一個問題得出相反結論。這種設計能嚴格控制變因，但結論能不能推廣到別的曲目、別的演奏者、真實音樂會情境，一直沒有辦法回答。

作者借用文學研究裡 Moretti 的「遠距閱讀」（distant reading），提出「遠距聆聽」（distant listening）：不細看單一演奏，而是在大量真實演奏上檢驗既有假說。推動這件事的是兩個條件的成熟：高品質的大型對齊資料集（(n)ASAP、Batik-plays-Mozart），以及鋼琴轉譜品質已高到可以從唱片產生 ATEPP 這種 500 多小時的轉譜語料。

The foundational papers of expressive performance research are almost all small, controlled designs. Repp (1994) had two pianists play Schumann's 'Träumerei' three times at each of three tempi; Desain and Honing (1994) had two pianists play the theme and first variation of Beethoven's WoO 70 at three tempi. The two papers reached opposite conclusions on the same question. Such designs control variables tightly, but whether their conclusions carry over to other repertoire, other performers or real concert settings has never been answerable.

Borrowing Moretti's 'distant reading' from literary studies, the authors propose 'distant listening': rather than scrutinising single performances, test existing hypotheses across many real-world ones. Two developments make this possible: large, high-quality aligned datasets ((n)ASAP, Batik-plays-Mozart), and piano transcription good enough to turn commercial recordings into a 500-plus-hour transcribed corpus such as ATEPP.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

三個研究問題各對應一條文獻線。速度關係不變性：Repp（1994）發現 IOI（音間起音間隔）大致隨整體速度等比縮放，支持不變性；Desain 與 Honing（1994）則找到速度與 IOI 的顯著交互作用，並指出倚音與和弦的 IOI 量法本身就會偏向某一邊。圓滑與斷奏：Bresin 與 Battel（2000）在莫札特 KV 545 第二樂章上用 KOR（鍵重疊比）量化記號效果；Repp（1995）提出高音區衰減快、演奏者會多重疊；Repp（1996b）提出不協和音程重疊較少，但只在聽覺實驗中支持過。和弦不同步：Palmer（1989、1996）與 Goebl（2001）記錄了 20–30 毫秒的旋律領先，Goebl 進一步發現若改量手指觸鍵時間，旋律領先幾乎消失，暗示它是力度造成的機械結果（音量大的音槌較快擊弦）。

論文自己沒點到、但最該對照的先例，是 CHARM 計畫的 Mazurka 研究（Sapp 2008）：同樣是「一首曲子很多版本」的大規模演奏時值分析，只是從音訊擷取拍點。本篇的差別在於用逐音對齊的 MIDI，所以能量到 KOR 與和弦內的毫秒級不同步，這是只有拍點的資料做不到的。

Each research question maps to a line of literature. Relational invariance: Repp (1994) found IOIs (inter-onset intervals) roughly scale with global tempo, supporting invariance; Desain and Honing (1994) found a significant tempo-by-IOI interaction and pointed out that how IOIs are measured around grace notes and chords can itself bias the result. Articulation: Bresin and Battel (2000) quantified marking effects with KOR (key overlap ratio) on Mozart's KV 545, second movement; Repp (1995) proposed more overlap in the faster-decaying high register; Repp (1996b) proposed less overlap for dissonant intervals, supported only in listening experiments. Chord asynchrony: Palmer (1989, 1996) and Goebl (2001) documented 20–30 ms melody lead, and Goebl showed it nearly vanishes when finger-key contact times are measured, implying a mechanical effect of dynamics (louder hammers reach the string sooner).

The closest precedent, which the paper does not name, is the CHARM Mazurka project (Sapp 2008): likewise a large-scale, many-versions-per-piece timing study, but with beats extracted from audio. The difference here is note-level aligned MIDI, which allows KOR and millisecond within-chord asynchrony to be measured; beat-only data cannot do that.

方法Method

資料是六個與 MusicXML 逐音對齊的鋼琴語料：Magaloff 演奏的蕭邦全集（155 場）、Zeilinger 的貝多芬（30）、Batik 的莫札特（36）、Vienna 4×22（88）、(n)ASAP（1,067，來自 Yamaha 電子鋼琴大賽）與 ATEPP 對齊子集（5,094，唱片轉譜）。前四個是錄製 MIDI，ATEPP 是轉譜，總計 780 首、6,330 場、約 636 小時。

實驗一（速度不變性）：IOI 去掉倚音、和弦取旋律音或最早的起音，再除以樂譜上的時值距離並取對數。每首曲子至少要有三場演奏，以估計速度的三分位數分成慢、中、快三組。第一個檢定比較組內與組間的演奏兩兩相關（Fisher Z，α = 0.05）；第二個檢定是逐曲 ANOVA，因子為速度組與節拍強度（弱拍、拍點、強拍），關鍵在交互作用項：不變性只預測主效應，交互作用顯著代表時值模式隨速度改變形狀。

The data are six piano corpora note-aligned to MusicXML: Magaloff's complete Chopin (155 performances), Zeilinger's Beethoven (30), Batik's Mozart (36), Vienna 4×22 (88), (n)ASAP (1,067, from the Yamaha e-Competition) and the aligned subset of ATEPP (5,094, transcribed from recordings). The first four are recorded MIDI, ATEPP is transcribed; in total 780 pieces, 6,330 performances, about 636 hours.

Experiment 1 (relational invariance): IOIs exclude grace notes and take the melody note, or the earliest onset, for chords; each is divided by its notated duration and log-transformed. Pieces need at least three performances, which are split into slow, medium and fast groups by tertiles of estimated tempo. The first test compares within-group and between-group pairwise correlations between performances (Fisher Z, α = 0.05). The second is a per-piece ANOVA with tempo group and metrical strength (offbeat, beat, downbeat) as factors. The interaction term is the key: invariance predicts main effects only, and a significant interaction means the timing pattern changes shape with tempo.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是結論自相矛盾。摘要寫「時值模式不會隨速度等比縮放」，結論第一段卻寫「時值模式大致會等比縮放……大致支持 Repp 的關係不變性」。看數字，後者比較貼近：ANOVA 交互作用只在 0%（Vienna 4×22，0/2 首）到 17.5%（ATEPP 蕭邦，14/80 首）的曲子顯著，其中兩格（8.00%、7.69%）接近 5% 的偽陽性率。表 2 也有標示錯誤：表註說組內是對角線、組間是下三角，內文卻反過來寫。

其次，交互作用檢定的檢定力被設計壓低了。Desain 與 Honing 用的是 113 與 223 個 IOI 層級，這裡把所有位置壓成三種節拍強度，形狀改變大多會被平均掉，等於偏向不變性；ANOVA 把每個音當獨立觀測，也沒處理同一場演奏內的相依性，用混合效應模型會更恰當。再者，慢、中、快三組是不同演奏者，速度與演奏者風格混在一起，而不變性原本是「同一個人換速度」的假說。作者有承認這點，也指出 ATEPP 有 79.19% 的曲子在同一速度組裡含同一演奏者的多場演奏。

The biggest problem is that the paper contradicts itself. The abstract says 'timing patterns do not scale proportionally with tempo', while the first paragraph of the conclusion says 'timing patterns generally do scale proportionally… largely support the relational invariance hypothesis proposed by Repp'. The numbers favour the latter: the ANOVA interaction is significant in 0% (Vienna 4×22, 0/2 pieces) to 17.5% (ATEPP Chopin, 14/80) of pieces, and two cells (8.00%, 7.69%) sit close to the 5% false-positive rate. Table 2 is also mislabelled: the caption says within-group is the diagonal and between-group the lower triangle, while the text says the reverse.

Second, the design reduces the power of the interaction test. Desain and Honing used 113 and 223 IOI levels; here all positions are collapsed into three metrical strengths, which averages away most shape changes and so favours invariance. The ANOVA also treats each note as independent and ignores dependence within a performance, where a mixed-effects model would fit better. Moreover, the slow/medium/fast groups contain different performers, so tempo is confounded with performer style, whereas invariance was originally a hypothesis about one person changing tempo. The authors acknowledge this and note that 79.19% of ATEPP pieces contain several performances by the same performer within one tempo group.

討論Discussion

實驗二用 KOR = 重疊時間 ÷ IOI 量旋律聲部的圓滑程度，並用 CC64 踏板值的直方圖找出各資料集的制音點（Vienna 76、Batik 75、Magaloff 60，而不是慣用的 64），把踏板延長的音長算進「聲音結束」。記號效果三個資料集都顯著（Batik F(1,11957) = 537.3 最清楚）。音區效果只在 Magaloff 顯著（r(5) = 0.96），Batik 反而是顯著負相關（r(4) = −0.90，P = 0.02）；這些相關各只用 4–7 個八度平均值算，自由度很小。

協和度的否定結果要打折扣。論文把同音反覆列為最協和（分數 8），但同一個鍵必須先放開才能再按，同音的鍵重疊在機械上幾乎只能是負的，這會把「越協和、重疊越多」的相關往負方向拉；r 本身是 −0.50，方向與假說相反，很可能跟這個人為因素有關。比較穩的發現是上行音程普遍比下行重疊多，例如完全四度上行 KOR 0.72±1.35、下行 0.46±1.26。

實驗三最有資訊量。三音以上的和弦裡，低音落後（59.81%）多於領先（39.29%），領先在強拍最大（28.8 毫秒）；旋律整體約 70:30 領先（右手內約 80:20），平均 17.39 毫秒（SD 18.13）。旋律領先隨力度差增大，符合 Goebl 的力度假說。速度與織體的影響可忽略：速度迴歸只有 76/248 顯著，織體只有 11/248（4.4%），後者等於偶然水準。

要提醒的是，ATEPP 是轉譜資料，17 毫秒等級的不同步已接近轉譜模型起音估計的精度範圍；論文第 8 節只比較 ATEPP 與 (n)ASAP 在 125 首共同曲目上的分布形狀，沒有同一場演奏的成對誤差，所以「轉譜可靠」只能算初步證據。整體而言，這篇最有價值的不是哪個假說被推翻，而是一套可重跑的流程（程式碼公開，私有資料也釋出量測值）；讀的時候以表格數字為準，不要只看摘要。

Experiment 2 measures melody-voice articulation as KOR = overlap time ÷ IOI, and locates each dataset's damper point from a histogram of CC64 pedal values (Vienna 76, Batik 75, Magaloff 60, rather than the conventional 64), extending pedalled notes to their sound-off point. The marking effect is significant in all three datasets (clearest in Batik, F(1,11957) = 537.3). The register effect is significant only for Magaloff (r(5) = 0.96), and Batik shows a significant negative correlation instead (r(4) = −0.90, P = 0.02); each of these correlations uses just 4–7 octave means, so degrees of freedom are tiny.

The negative consonance result should be discounted. The paper scores the unison as most consonant (score 8), but a repeated key must be released before it can be struck again, so unison key overlap is mechanically almost always negative. That drags the consonance–overlap correlation downward; r itself is −0.50, opposite to the hypothesis, quite possibly because of this artefact. The sturdier finding is that upward intervals overlap more than downward ones in general, e.g. perfect fourth upward KOR 0.72±1.35 versus 0.46±1.26 downward.

Experiment 3 is the most informative. On chords of three or more notes, bass lags (59.81%) more often than it leads (39.29%), with bass lead largest on downbeats (28.8 ms); melody leads at roughly 70:30 overall (80:20 within the right hand), averaging 17.39 ms (SD 18.13). Melody lead grows with velocity difference, consistent with Goebl's velocity hypothesis. Tempo and texture effects are negligible: only 76/248 tempo regressions and 11/248 (4.4%) texture regressions are significant, the latter at chance level.

One caution: ATEPP is transcribed, and asynchronies around 17 ms approach the onset precision of transcription models. Section 8 only compares distribution shapes between ATEPP and (n)ASAP over 125 shared pieces, with no paired error on the same performance, so 'transcription is reliable' is preliminary evidence at best. Overall, the paper's main value is not any single overturned hypothesis but a rerunnable pipeline (code is public, and measurements are released even for the proprietary corpora). Read it by the numbers in its tables rather than by its abstract.

Distorted Realities: Classifying Extreme Vocals Between Harmony and Noise – A Machine–Human Evaluation of Vocal Confusion Patterns

失真的現實：極端金屬人聲技巧分類的人機比較——158 位聆聽者對上 ComParE + SVM，比較 Shriek、Growl、Scream 的混淆模式

Xuhong Qiu, Emilia Parada-Cabaleiro
DOI:10.5334/tismir.310 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-07-16
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：極端金屬的人聲技巧（Black Shriek、Death Growl、Hardcore Scream）在音樂資料庫裡幾乎沒有細分標籤。這篇在 EMVD 資料集上讓 158 位熟悉此類音樂的聆聽者與一個 ComParE 特徵加線性 SVM 的分類器分辨同一批 71 段乾聲：人類 UAR 76.2%，機器 90.0%（95% 自助法信賴區間 82.9–96.3%）。人類最常把 Scream 聽成 Shriek（23.5%），機器在這個方向是 0%。選它是因為「人機混淆模式並排比較」的做法值得借鏡，但「機器勝過人類」要打折扣：機器在同一資料集的數百個樣本上學過標註慣例，人類只聽過 4 段示範。Why this one: Extreme metal vocal techniques (Black Shriek, Death Growl, Hardcore Scream) have almost no fine-grained labels in music libraries. On the EMVD dataset, this paper has 158 listeners familiar with the genre and a linear SVM on ComParE features classify the same 71 dry vocal excerpts: humans reach 76.2% UAR, the machine 90.0% (95% bootstrap CI 82.9–96.3%). Humans most often hear Scream as Shriek (23.5%), a confusion the machine makes 0% of the time. It was picked because the side-by-side comparison of human and machine confusion patterns is worth borrowing, but 'machine beats humans' needs discounting: the machine learned the labelling conventions from hundreds of examples in the same dataset, while humans heard only 4 demonstration clips.

動機Motivation

極端人聲技巧在金屬、硬核、龐克裡很常見，但分類體系多半來自圈內口耳相傳：同一個詞（Fry Scream、Tunnel Growl）在不同社群的意思不同，學術上也有好幾套互不相容的分類（Nieto、Sadolin、Tailleur 等人）。結果是串流平台的標籤停在「metal」這種粗粒度，推薦常常從老派金屬跳到 metalcore；醫療上，某些技巧與病理嗓音聲學上相似，也有誤診的顧慮。

作者想回答兩件事：從業者實際上怎麼分類這些技巧；以及在同一批錄音上，人耳與機器分類各會在哪裡混淆、混淆是否一致。後者的動機是，如果人機犯同樣的錯，代表類別邊界本身模糊；如果只有人會錯，代表聲學上可分、只是人沒有用到那些線索。

Extreme vocal techniques are common in metal, hardcore and punk, but their taxonomies mostly come from scene lore: the same term (Fry Scream, Tunnel Growl) means different things in different communities, and academia has several incompatible schemes (Nieto, Sadolin, Tailleur et al.). As a result, streaming tags stop at coarse labels like 'metal', and recommendation often jumps from old-school metal to metalcore. Medically, some techniques resemble pathological voices acoustically, raising misdiagnosis concerns.

The authors ask two things: how practitioners actually categorise these techniques, and, on the same recordings, where human ears and a machine classifier each get confused and whether the confusions agree. The reasoning is that if humans and machines make the same errors, the category boundary itself is blurry; if only humans err, the classes are acoustically separable and people simply do not use those cues.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接的前作有三個。Kalbag 與 Lerch（2022）的 MVD 收錄 57 首歌，依 Nieto 的 Fry Scream 分類把尖叫分成高、中、低三種。Tailleur 等人（2024）的 EMVD 由 27 位歌手錄製 8 種技巧、共 1,068 段，並用 EfficientNet 吃 log-mel 頻譜：乾淨與失真二分類 93%，四分類微平均 75%、巨集平均 70%。這篇沿用 EMVD 的四類，但分類器換成 openSMILE 的 ComParE 特徵（6,373 維）加線性 SVM。

這個分類器本身並不新：ComParE 加 SVM 是 INTERSPEECH 副語言挑戰賽多年的標準基線，作者自己先前的歌唱技巧研究（Parada-Cabaleiro 等人 2023）也用同一套。所以本篇的貢獻不在模型，而在人機比較的設計；90% 對 Tailleur 等人的 70% 巨集平均，作者也坦承設定不同（測試集只取品質 2 分的歌詞段落），不能當成同一把尺的勝負。

There are three direct predecessors. Kalbag and Lerch (2022) built MVD from 57 songs, splitting screams into high, mid and low following Nieto's Fry Scream typology. Tailleur et al. (2024) built EMVD, 1,068 clips of 8 techniques from 27 singers, and trained EfficientNet on log-mel spectrograms: 93% for clean versus distorted, 75% micro and 70% macro for four classes. This paper keeps EMVD's four classes but swaps the classifier for openSMILE's ComParE features (6,373 dimensions) with a linear SVM.

That classifier is not new: ComParE plus SVM has been the standard baseline of the INTERSPEECH paralinguistics challenges for years, and the second author's earlier singing-technique work (Parada-Cabaleiro et al. 2023) used the same setup. So the contribution lies in the human–machine comparison design, not the model. The authors also admit that 90% versus Tailleur et al.'s 70% macro is not a same-yardstick win, since the test set here contains only quality-2 lyric excerpts.

方法Method

研究分兩階段。第一階段是質性訪談：以文字訊息非同步訪談極端人聲從業者，多數人支持依頻率分成高、中、低三類，也有人主張依風格分類。作者用 117 段課程提供的商業錄音片段檢驗這個分法：以最大振幅頻率分箱成三組，再做 Welch ANOVA（ω² = 0.47），改用頻譜重心時 ω² = 0.75。

第二階段是人機比較。資料取 EMVD 裡每類至少 100 段的四類（Clear Voice、Black Shriek、Death Growl、Hardcore Scream，共 946 段）。測試集是 71 段品質 2 分、約 15 秒的歌詞演唱，來自 23 位歌手（11 段乾淨、20 Shriek、21 Growl、19 Scream）。聆聽實驗在線上進行，158 位受試者先聽 4 段示範，再各自隨機標 20 段，每段得到 49–59 個標註。

機器端擷取 ComParE 特徵，用 ANOVA F 值選前 k 個（k 從 100 到 1,000），接線性 SVM。評估採歌手獨立的三折，三個測試折合起來正好是聆聽實驗那 71 段，超參數在訓練歌手內做巢狀網格搜尋。另有三個輔助分析：以 SVM 權重絕對值平均衡量特徵群重要性；訓練一個 SVM 去預測聆聽者的多數決標籤；以及只用 jitter、shimmer、HNR 的模型與拿掉這三類的模型做消融。

The study has two phases. Phase one is qualitative: extreme vocalists were interviewed asynchronously by text message. Most favoured a high/mid/low frequency grouping, while some argued for style-based grouping. The authors check this split on 117 commercial excerpts from course materials: binned into three groups by maximum-amplitude frequency, then tested with Welch's ANOVA (ω² = 0.47), and ω² = 0.75 when spectral centroid is used instead.

Phase two is the human–machine comparison. It uses EMVD's four classes with at least 100 clips each (Clear Voice, Black Shriek, Death Growl, Hardcore Scream; 946 clips). The test set is 71 quality-2 lyric excerpts of about 15 seconds from 23 singers (11 clean, 20 Shriek, 21 Growl, 19 Scream). The listening test ran online: 158 participants heard 4 demonstration clips, then each labelled 20 randomly assigned excerpts, giving 49–59 labels per excerpt.

The machine side extracts ComParE features, selects the top k by ANOVA F (k from 100 to 1,000) and trains a linear SVM. Evaluation is singer-independent 3-fold, with the three test folds together making up exactly the 71 listening-test excerpts, and hyperparameters chosen by nested grid search on training singers. Three auxiliary analyses follow: feature-group importance from mean absolute SVM weights, an SVM trained to predict listeners' majority labels, and an ablation comparing a model using only jitter, shimmer and HNR with one using all other features.

限制Limitations

人機比較並不對等。機器在同一個資料集、同一種錄音條件下，用訓練歌手的數百段學 EMVD 的標註慣例；人類只聽過 4 段示範，而且要用的是資料集作者定義的類別名，不見得是他們圈內熟悉的那套。所以「90% 對 76.2%」量到的，有一部分是「誰學過這個資料集的定義」，而不只是聲學辨識能力。作者在討論承認乾聲、無伴奏的條件對人不利，但沒有點出訓練量的不對稱。

測試集小。71 段裡每類只有 11–21 段，機器「Scream 誤判成 Shriek 0%」是 19 段裡 0 段；信賴區間 82.9–96.3% 的寬度也說明了這點。超參數網格是「依先前手動調整預先定義」的，若先前調整用的是同一批資料，會有輕微洩漏。特徵群重要性用 SelectKBest 之後的線性 SVM 權重絕對值平均來算，被選進來的特徵數與特徵間相關都會影響結果，排名的解釋力有限。

還有幾處內部不一致。摘要與 3.2 節說訪談了 11 位從業者，引言卻寫七位。117 段的驗證中，Levene 檢定報 F(2,92)，等於只有 95 個樣本，頻譜重心那次卻是 F(2,114)。這個驗證本身也是循環論證：組別是用最大振幅頻率分箱切出來的，再拿同一個變數檢定組間差異當然顯著，無法說明分類法的效度。預測人類多數決的 SVM，文中寫的是「在各折的測試項目上擬合」，字面上像是在測試資料上訓練，可能只是措辭問題，但沒有釐清。此外，倫理審查是研究完成後才回溯諮詢的。

The human–machine comparison is not balanced. The machine learns EMVD's labelling conventions from hundreds of clips by training singers, in the same dataset and recording conditions; humans heard only 4 demonstrations and had to use the dataset authors' category names, not necessarily the ones from their own scene. So '90% versus 76.2%' partly measures who has learned this dataset's definitions, not just acoustic discrimination. The discussion admits that dry, unaccompanied excerpts disadvantage humans, but it does not mention the asymmetry in training exposure.

The test set is small. Each class has only 11–21 of the 71 clips, so the machine's '0% Scream misread as Shriek' is 0 out of 19, and the 82.9–96.3% confidence interval shows the same thing. The hyperparameter grid was 'defined a priori based on preliminary manual tuning'; if that tuning used the same data, there is mild leakage. Feature-group importance is the mean absolute linear-SVM weight after SelectKBest, so the number of features selected per group and correlations among features both shape the result, which limits how much the ranking explains.

There are also several internal inconsistencies. The abstract and Section 3.2 say 11 practitioners were interviewed, but the introduction says seven. The 117-excerpt validation reports Levene's test with F(2,92), which implies 95 samples, while the centroid analysis uses F(2,114). The validation itself is circular: groups are formed by binning maximum-amplitude frequency, so finding that they differ significantly on that same variable says nothing about the taxonomy. The human-prediction SVM is described as 'fitted on the fold-specific test items', which reads as training on the test data; it may just be a wording problem, but it is not clarified. Finally, the ethics committee was consulted only retrospectively, after the study was completed.

討論Discussion

最有意思的結果是混淆模式的對照。人類 Clear Voice 召回 97%、Growl 約 80%，Shriek 68.6%、Scream 60.9%，Shriek 的精確率最低（63%），像是拿不定主意時的預設答案。機器重現了兩個人類的錯誤方向：Growl 被當成 Shriek（機器 14.3%、人類 15.2%），Scream 被當成 Growl（10.5% 對 11.5%），這兩組大概是真正模糊的邊界。但人類最大的錯誤（Scream 聽成 Shriek 23.5%）機器完全沒犯，代表這兩類在 ComParE 空間裡可分，只是人沒有用到那些線索。

輔助分析支持「線索權重不同」的說法：預測 EMVD 標籤的模型以 shimmer 權重最高、MFCC 最低；預測聆聽者多數決的模型（UAR 78.3%）則反過來，MFCC 排第一、頻譜類排最後。消融顯示只用 jitter、shimmer、HNR 時 UAR 降到 76.7%，拿掉它們則降到 82.3%，兩種情況 Shriek 與 Scream 的互相混淆都增加，表示這兩類靠的是跨特徵群的細部組合。

對 MIR 有用的做法，是把人類混淆矩陣當作類別邊界的資訊，而不是要打敗的對象。但要讓人聲技巧成為可用的標籤，下一步應該是作者自己提的因子設計（有無伴奏 × 人聲類別），以及在商業錄音上做外部驗證；附錄裡九類設定的結果（SVM 巨集平均 52%、CNN 32%）也提醒我們，四類只是簡單版本。

The most interesting result is the comparison of confusion patterns. Human recall is 97% for Clear Voice and about 80% for Growl, but 68.6% for Shriek and 60.9% for Scream, and Shriek has the lowest precision (63%), as if it served as the default answer under uncertainty. The machine reproduces two human error directions: Growl as Shriek (machine 14.3%, humans 15.2%) and Scream as Growl (10.5% versus 11.5%). These are probably genuinely blurry boundaries. The largest human error, however (Scream heard as Shriek, 23.5%), never occurs for the machine, which means the two classes are separable in ComParE space and people simply do not use those cues.

The auxiliary analyses support the 'different cue weighting' reading. The model predicting EMVD labels weights shimmer highest and MFCCs lowest; the model predicting listener majority labels (78.3% UAR) flips this, with MFCCs first and spectral features last. In the ablation, UAR drops to 76.7% with only jitter, shimmer and HNR, and to 82.3% without them; Shriek–Scream confusion rises in both cases, indicating that these two classes rely on fine-grained combinations across feature groups.

For MIR, the useful idea is to treat human confusion matrices as information about category boundaries, not as a target to beat. Before vocal technique can become a usable tag, though, the next step is the factorial test the authors propose themselves (accompanied versus unaccompanied × vocal class), plus external validation on commercial recordings. The 9-class results in the appendix (SVM macro 52%, CNN macro 32%) are a reminder that the four-class setting is the easy version.

EEG-Based Decoding of Musical Mode and Melodic Direction Using Empirical Mode Decomposition

以經驗模態分解從腦電解碼調式與旋律方向：六位鋼琴家彈音階時的 EEG，IMF 能量特徵加寬淺神經網路分類

Sergio Guillén, Lorenzo J. Tardón, Ana M. Barbancho, Isabel Barbancho
arXiv:2610.37148 · cs.SD; eess.SP · 投稿submitted 2026-10-27
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：六位受過音樂院訓練的鋼琴家一邊彈 C 大調或 A 自然小調音階、一邊錄 64 通道 EEG。作者用經驗模態分解（EMD）取前四個本質模態函數（IMF），算標準差、RMS、Welch 功率，餵給單一隱藏層的「寬」神經網路：受試者內調式 83.78%、方向 84.57%，混合受試者的設定裡調式 73.17%、方向 87.28%。選它是因為「從大腦解碼音樂結構」是 MIR 少見的方向，但它也是一個很好的評估反例：大調與小調是分兩大段依序錄的，又用隨機的逐試次五折交叉驗證，調式與錄音時段完全混在一起，數字很可能高估了。Why this one: Six conservatory-trained pianists played C major or A natural minor scales while 64-channel EEG was recorded. The authors apply empirical mode decomposition (EMD), take the first four intrinsic mode functions (IMFs), compute standard deviation, RMS and Welch power, and feed them to a single-hidden-layer 'wide' neural network: within-subject accuracy is 83.78% for mode and 84.57% for direction, and in a pooled-subject setting 73.17% for mode and 87.28% for direction. It was picked because decoding musical structure from the brain is a rare direction in MIR, but it is also a good counterexample in evaluation: major and minor were recorded in two consecutive blocks, yet trials were split by randomised five-fold cross-validation, so mode is fully confounded with recording time and the numbers are likely inflated.

動機Motivation

調式（大調／小調）與旋律方向（上行／下行）是影響音樂情緒最基本的兩個參數。過去的 EEG 研究幾乎都在被動聆聽的情境下，看各頻帶的頭皮功率分布或功能連結；旋律輪廓則多半用 MEG 或 EEG 的 oddball 範式研究。作者認為被動聆聽忽略了演奏時聽覺與動作的緊密耦合，所以改成讓受試者實際彈琴、同時錄 EEG。

方法上的動機是，EEG 是非線性、非平穩的訊號，EMD 不需要預先定義基底函數，能自適應地拆出振盪成分，已在癲癇偵測與想像語音辨識上用過。作者想知道：EMD 拆出的成分能不能區分調式與方向，而且不受慣用手影響。

Mode (major/minor) and melodic direction (ascending/descending) are two of the most basic parameters shaping musical emotion. Prior EEG work almost always used passive listening, looking at scalp power distribution or functional connectivity across bands; melodic contour has mostly been studied with MEG or EEG oddball paradigms. The authors argue that passive listening ignores the tight auditory–motor coupling of performance, so participants here actually play the piano while EEG is recorded.

The methodological motivation is that EEG is nonlinear and non-stationary, and EMD separates oscillatory components adaptively without predefined basis functions; it has been used for epilepsy detection and imagined-speech recognition. The authors want to know whether EMD components can discriminate mode and direction, and whether this holds regardless of handedness.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

神經科學這條線上，Schmidt 與 Trainor（2001）發現額葉 EEG 不對稱能區分音樂情緒的效價，Sammler 等人（2007）發現愉悅的音樂伴隨額中線 theta 增強，Fujioka 等人（2004）用 MEG 的不匹配負波證明音樂訓練強化旋律輪廓的自動編碼。這些都是被動聆聽、看群體平均，不做單一試次分類；本篇的差別是主動演奏加上單一試次分類。

MIR 這條線最該對照的是 OpenMIIR（Stober 等人 2015），它公開了聆聽與想像音樂時的 EEG，並做跨受試者的辨識。本篇的資料不公開（需來信索取），也沒有做留一受試者（LOSO）評估，可比性有限。至於論文宣稱首創的「EMD 加神經網路」，其實最像 Djemili 等人（2016）的癲癇分類流程：同樣是 EMD 取 IMF、算統計與能量特徵、接多層感知器，本篇主要是把標籤換成調式與方向。「寬神經網路」（Wide Neural Network）是 MATLAB Classification Learner 裡的預設模型名稱，架構上就是單一隱藏層的 MLP。

On the neuroscience side, Schmidt and Trainor (2001) found that frontal EEG asymmetry distinguishes the valence of musical emotion, Sammler et al. (2007) found increased frontal midline theta for pleasant music, and Fujioka et al. (2004) used the MEG mismatch negativity to show that musical training strengthens automatic encoding of melodic contour. All of these use passive listening and group averages, not single-trial classification; this paper differs by using active performance and single-trial classification.

On the MIR side, the closest comparison is OpenMIIR (Stober et al. 2015), which released EEG recorded during music perception and imagination and evaluated across subjects. This paper's data are not public (available on request), and there is no leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) evaluation, so comparability is limited. As for the claimed novelty of 'EMD plus neural network', it most resembles the epilepsy pipeline of Djemili et al. (2016): EMD into IMFs, statistical and energy features, then a multilayer perceptron. The main change here is swapping the labels for mode and direction. 'Wide Neural Network' is a preset name in MATLAB's Classification Learner; architecturally it is a single-hidden-layer MLP.

方法Method

受試者是六位至少十年音樂院鋼琴經驗的成員（兩位左撇子）。每份樂譜含 30 條音階，變因是調式（C 大調、A 自然小調）、方向、速度（120 或 240 bpm）與演奏手；雙手同時彈的段落排除，每人留下 16 個子實驗，總計 2,880 個試次，每人約剔除 20 個沒彈完的試次。設計是分區塊的：先彈完 C 大調的所有子實驗，休息一分鐘，再彈 A 小調。

錄音用 BrainVision actiChamp-Plus 64 通道（61 個 EEG 電極），取樣 2,500 Hz。前處理是 0.1–40 Hz FIR 帶通、ICA 移除與 EOG 相關係數超過 0.35 的成分，再降到 100 Hz，並用 MIDI 時間戳切出每條音階。每個電極做 EMD，固定取四個 IMF；每個 IMF、每個電極算標準差、RMS 與 Welch 累積功率，得到 3 × 61 = 183 維特徵，每個 IMF 分開分類。

分類器是一個 75 單元的 tanh 隱藏層加 softmax 輸出，正則化 λ = 1.5 × 10⁻³，最多訓練 2,000 個 epoch；超參數在一個資料子集上調，取各受試者最佳值的眾數固定下來。評估採隨機五折交叉驗證：受試者內是每人單獨做；「受試者間」則是把六人的試次全部混在一起再切。對照組有 LDA、線性 SVM、二次 SVM、1-NN 與 5-NN。

Participants are six members of the university with at least ten years of conservatory piano training (two left-handed). Each score contains 30 scales, varied by mode (C major, A natural minor), direction, tempo (120 or 240 bpm) and playing hand. Two-handed passages are excluded, leaving 16 sub-experiments per person and 2,880 trials in total, with about 20 incomplete trials removed per person. The design is blocked: all C major sub-experiments come first, then a one-minute break, then A minor.

Recording uses a 64-channel BrainVision actiChamp-Plus (61 EEG electrodes) at 2,500 Hz. Preprocessing is a 0.1–40 Hz FIR band-pass, ICA removal of components whose correlation with EOG exceeds 0.35, downsampling to 100 Hz, and epoching each scale with MIDI timestamps. EMD is run per electrode with exactly four IMFs; for each IMF and electrode, standard deviation, RMS and Welch cumulative power give 3 × 61 = 183 features, and each IMF is classified separately.

The classifier has one 75-unit tanh hidden layer and a softmax output, regularisation λ = 1.5 × 10⁻³ and at most 2,000 epochs; hyperparameters were tuned on a data subset and fixed by taking the mode across participants. Evaluation is randomised 5-fold cross-validation: within-subject uses one person's data at a time, while the 'inter-subject' setting pools all six people's trials before splitting. Baselines are LDA, linear SVM, quadratic SVM, 1-NN and 5-NN.

限制Limitations

最嚴重的是調式與錄音時段完全混淆。大調的試次全部在前半段、小調全部在後半段，中間只隔一分鐘；隨機的逐試次五折切分又讓同一區塊相鄰的試次同時出現在訓練與測試集。電極阻抗與導電膠的變化、疲勞、注意力隨時間的漂移，都會讓前半段與後半段的 EEG 統計量不同，分類器只要學會「這是前半段還是後半段」就能拿到高分。要排除這點，至少要以區塊為單位切分，或者把大小調的順序在受試者間對調。

方向的分類也有動作混淆。上行與下行音階的手部移動方向相反，指法（拇指穿越或手指跨過）也不同，眼睛還要跟著樂譜移動。ICA 只移除了眼動成分，肌電只靠 40 Hz 低通處理；而分類最準的偏偏是頻率最高的 IMF1，正是肌電與動作偽訊最容易殘留的頻段。論文把這解讀為高頻神經活動很重要，卻沒有做任何偽訊對照（例如只用周邊電極或只用 EOG 通道分類）。

「受試者間」的設定也不是跨受試者：它把六人的試次混在一起隨機切，每個測試折都含有訓練時見過的人。徵兆是混合設定下的方向準確率（87.28%）反而高於受試者內（84.57%），作者自己也說 LOSO「常常失敗」，留待未來。此外沒有對機率水準做置換檢定，也沒有報各折變異；WNN 對線性 SVM 在調式只多 1.25 個百分點（83.78% 對 82.53%），方向多 3.21（84.57% 對 81.36%），「一致較佳」的結論站不住。六個人也不足以支撐關於慣用手的結論（右撇子 AUC 0.93、左撇子 0.89）。

The most serious issue is that mode is fully confounded with recording time. All major trials come in the first half and all minor trials in the second, separated by one minute, and the randomised trial-level 5-fold split puts neighbouring trials from the same block into both training and test sets. Changes in electrode impedance and gel, fatigue, and attentional drift over time all make first-half and second-half EEG statistics differ, so a classifier that merely learns 'first half or second half' scores well. Ruling this out requires at least block-wise splits, or counterbalancing the order of major and minor across participants.

Direction classification has a motor confound. Ascending and descending scales move the hand in opposite directions with different fingering (thumb-under versus finger-over), and the eyes follow the score. ICA removed only ocular components, and EMG was handled only by the 40 Hz low-pass; yet the best-performing component is IMF1, the highest-frequency one, exactly where EMG and movement artefacts most easily survive. The paper reads this as evidence that high-frequency neural activity matters, without any artefact control (for example, classifying from peripheral electrodes or EOG channels alone).

The 'inter-subject' setting is not cross-subject either: it pools all six people's trials and splits randomly, so every test fold contains trials from people seen in training. Telling signs are that pooled direction accuracy (87.28%) is actually higher than within-subject (84.57%), and that the authors themselves say LOSO 'often fails' and leave it to future work. There is also no permutation test against chance and no fold variance; the WNN beats the linear SVM by only 1.25 points for mode (83.78% vs 82.53%) and 3.21 for direction (84.57% vs 81.36%), so 'consistently superior' cannot be established. Six participants is too few for the handedness claim (AUC 0.93 for right-handers vs 0.89 for left-handers).

討論Discussion

數字面上，受試者內 IMF1 的調式 83.78%、方向 84.57%，平均 AUC 0.91 與 0.92；IMF2 到 IMF4 掉到 63.54–68.79%。混合受試者的 IMF1 調式 73.17%、方向 87.28%，AUC 0.81 與 0.94。頻譜分析顯示 IMF 大致對應傳統 EEG 頻帶，但 alpha 與 beta 之間有約 5 Hz 的缺口，gamma 上段約有 12 Hz 沒被涵蓋；頂枕區的 alpha 差異在 IMF 裡因此看不到。這點作者說得很坦白，也說明固定取四個 IMF 的 EMD 並不是嚴格優於固定頻帶。

地形圖的解讀（大調左顳葉較強、額中線 theta 增強）與文獻一致，但都是六人平均、沒有統計檢定的描述，加上上述的時段混淆，只能當成假說。另外，這篇其實已在 2026 年 7 月刊登於 IEEE Access，arXiv 版是 10 月才上傳的轉貼，所以「新」是指 arXiv 上新。對 MIR 讀者，這篇最有用的是當作 EEG 解碼的評估檢查表：區塊設計就要用區塊切分，宣稱跨受試者就要做 LOSO，最好的頻段剛好是偽訊頻段時，就要做偽訊對照。

In numbers, within-subject IMF1 reaches 83.78% for mode and 84.57% for direction, with mean AUC 0.91 and 0.92; IMF2 to IMF4 fall to 63.54–68.79%. Pooled-subject IMF1 reaches 73.17% for mode and 87.28% for direction, AUC 0.81 and 0.94. Spectral analysis shows the IMFs roughly match the conventional EEG bands, but there is a gap of about 5 Hz between alpha and beta and about 12 Hz of upper gamma left uncovered, so the parieto-occipital alpha difference is invisible in the IMFs. The authors state this candidly, and it shows that EMD with a fixed four IMFs is not strictly better than fixed bands.

The topographic readings (stronger left temporal activity for major, enhanced frontal midline theta) agree with the literature, but they are six-person averages described without statistical tests, and with the time confound above they can only be hypotheses. Note also that the paper already appeared in IEEE Access in July 2026; the arXiv version is a repost uploaded in October, so it is 'new' only on arXiv. For MIR readers, its best use is as an evaluation checklist for EEG decoding: a blocked design needs block-wise splits, a cross-subject claim needs LOSO, and when the best band is also the artefact band, an artefact control is required.
