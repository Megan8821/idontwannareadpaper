A Dataset and Analysis Framework for Large-Scale Synthesizer Preset Generation

大規模合成器音色預設的資料集與分析框架：Sylenth1-10KPresets——292 個原廠 preset 加 10,000 個「以原廠為種子」突變出來的 preset，每個附 179 個參數真值、A4 渲染音訊與七個 AudioCommons 音色描述子，並給出參數→描述子回歸與描述子→參數反推三個基準（最近鄰檢索 macro MAE 2.94，CVAE 13.85）

Joseph M. Cameron, Alan F. Blackwell
DOI:10.5334/tismir.367 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-11-16
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：合成器自動編程（synthesizer programming）一直缺一份「參數真值＋人看得懂的音色標籤」的資料。本文用 Spotify 的 Pedalboard 驅動商用軟體合成器 Sylenth1，從 292 個非琶音器原廠 preset 出發，隨機挑一部分「非預設」參數、用原廠值的 KDE 重抽，產生 10,000 個新 preset；每個在 A4 渲染 0.5 秒、正規化到 −23 LUFS 後算七個 AudioCommons 描述子。選它是因為它的基準結果很誠實：最近鄰檢索的反推誤差 2.94 已貼近合成器本身的重渲染雜訊（約 1.5），CVAE 卻是 13.85，作者直接承認這個指標已經飽和。要注意：描述子是 AudioCommons 模型的輸出，不是人的評分；整份資料只有一台合成器、一個音高。Why this one: Synthesizer programming research has long lacked a dataset pairing ground-truth parameters with human-readable timbre labels. This paper drives the commercial soft synth Sylenth1 through Spotify's Pedalboard, starts from 292 non-arpeggiator factory presets, picks a random subset of each preset's non-default parameters and resamples them from KDEs of factory values, producing 10,000 new presets; each is rendered as a 0.5 s A4 note, normalised to −23 LUFS, and scored with seven AudioCommons descriptors. I picked it because its baselines are honest: nearest-neighbour inversion reaches a round-trip error of 2.94, close to the synth's own re-render noise (about 1.5), while a CVAE gets 13.85, and the authors say outright that this metric is saturated. Caveat: the descriptors are outputs of AudioCommons models, not human ratings, and the whole dataset covers one synthesizer at one pitch.

動機Motivation

preset 是大多數人用合成器的方式，卻很少被當成研究語料。 商用軟體合成器出廠就附幾百到上千個專家設計的 preset（Sylenth1 有 512 個，Diva 超過 1,200 個），但 MIR 對音色的研究多半從音訊出發——NSynth、DDSP、AudioCommons 都把音色當成波形的性質，而不是「哪組旋鈕造成這個聲音」。作者引用 Seago 等人的觀察：10 台 Yamaha DX7 裡有 9 台只存著原廠 preset，說明一般音樂人很少自己調參數。

想做的事是讓「亮一點、暖一點」直接對應到參數。 如果有一份資料同時有完整參數、渲染音訊和語意描述子，就能訓練「參數→描述子」的預測模型，也能訓練反方向「我要這種音色→給我一組參數」的生成模型。作者刻意把範圍縮到單一減法合成器、單一音高（A4），換來參數層級的精確真值與一致的渲染流程，並在開頭就聲明這個取捨。

Presets are how most people use synthesizers, yet they are rarely treated as a research corpus. Commercial soft synths ship with hundreds to over a thousand expert-designed presets (512 for Sylenth1, over 1,200 for Diva), but MIR work on timbre mostly starts from audio: NSynth, DDSP and AudioCommons treat timbre as a property of the waveform, not of which knobs produced it. The authors cite Seago et al.'s observation that 9 out of 10 Yamaha DX7 units held only factory presets, a sign that ordinary musicians rarely program sounds themselves.

The goal is to map "a bit brighter, a bit warmer" directly onto parameters. A dataset with full parameters, rendered audio and semantic descriptors supports parameter→descriptor prediction and the reverse, generative direction ("I want this timbre, give me parameters"). The authors deliberately narrow the scope to one subtractive synth at one pitch (A4), buying exact parameter-level ground truth and a uniform rendering chain, and state this trade-off up front.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

「商用合成器 preset＋參數真值」並非首見。 Esling 等人的 Flow Synthesizer（2020）本身就是在 Diva 的 preset 資料上訓練 VAE 加正規化流，學音訊與 preset 之間的可逆映射；Le Vaillant 等人（2021）把類似做法推到有 144 個參數的 Yamaha DX7。從音訊反推參數的 sound matching 也有長歷史，例如 Barkan 等人的 InverSynth（2019）用 CNN 直接回歸合成器參數。本文跟它們的差別不在模型，而在多了七個語意描述子，以及一套固定、防洩漏的切分。

跟 synth1B1 的對比是作者自己挑的。 Turian 等人（2021）用 torchsynth 均勻亂數生成十億個聲音，參數完整但很多聽起來沒有音樂意義；本文改用「以原廠 preset 為種子」的抽樣換取可聽性與音樂合理性。最接近的語意控制前例是 Yutani 等人（2024）的 wavetable CVAE（三個標籤），HCI 端則有 SynthScribe（Brade 等 2024）的文字查 preset。

「factory-conditioned randomization」其實最像演化式合成器編程裡的突變算子。 從一個母體 preset 出發、只改一部分參數，這正是 Yee-King 的 SynthBot 這類基因演算法的 mutation 步驟，只是這裡的新值從原廠值的分佈抽、而且不做選擇。論文沒有把它放進這條脈絡。

"Commercial synth presets plus parameter ground truth" is not new. Esling et al.'s Flow Synthesizer (2020) was itself trained on Diva preset data, using a VAE with normalising flows to learn an invertible map between audio and presets; Le Vaillant et al. (2021) pushed a similar approach to the 144-parameter Yamaha DX7. Sound matching from audio to parameters also has a long history, e.g. Barkan et al.'s InverSynth (2019), a CNN that regresses synth parameters directly. What this paper adds is not a model but seven semantic descriptors and a fixed, leakage-free split.

The contrast with synth1B1 is the authors' own choice. Turian et al. (2021) generated a billion sounds with uniformly random torchsynth parameters, complete but often musically meaningless; this paper trades scale for audibility and plausibility by seeding from factory presets. The closest semantic-control precedent is Yutani et al.'s (2024) wavetable CVAE with three labels; on the HCI side, SynthScribe (Brade et al. 2024) offers text search over presets.

"Factory-conditioned randomization" is closest to the mutation operator of evolutionary synth programming. Starting from a parent preset and changing only some parameters is exactly the mutation step of genetic-algorithm systems such as Yee-King's SynthBot, except that new values are drawn from the factory distribution and no selection is applied. The paper does not place it in that lineage.

方法Method

參數清理。 Sylenth1 的 VST3 介面暴露 246 個參數；去掉琶音器、音高與滑音、總音量與 bypass 等 67 個與音色無關的控制，剩下 179 個（106 個連續、55 個列舉、18 個布林）。512 個原廠 preset 裡只留 292 個不靠琶音器的；所有 LFO 統一改成與速度同步，避免 Hz 與音符時值混用。

生成流程。 每個隨機 preset 先隨機挑一個原廠母體，找出它與 Init 預設值不同的參數集合 A，從 1 到 |A| 均勻抽 k，再均勻挑 k 個參數重抽：連續參數從原廠值擬合的 KDE 抽，類別參數依原廠頻率抽。渲染後 RMS 低於 10⁻³ 視為無聲，最多重試三次；三次都無聲就套用修正啟發式（強制濾波器輸入、截止頻率拉到 40%–60%、混音推到中段，最多動 7 個控制）。作者承認這個啟發式被觸發幾次沒有記錄。

標註。 每個 preset 在 A4（MIDI 69）、力度 100 渲染 0.5 秒單聲道、44.1 kHz，用 pyloudnorm 正規化到 −23 LUFS，再算七個 AudioCommons 描述子（0–100）。資料以 JSON 發布，附全部 10,292 個 WAV。

Parameter cleanup. Sylenth1's VST3 interface exposes 246 parameters; removing 67 timbre-irrelevant controls (arpeggiator, pitch and portamento, master volume, bypass and so on) leaves 179 (106 continuous, 55 enum, 18 boolean). Of the 512 factory presets only the 292 that do not rely on the arpeggiator are kept, and all LFOs are forced to tempo sync to avoid mixing Hz and note-value rates.

Generation. For each random preset a factory parent is picked at random, the set A of its parameters that differ from the Init defaults is found, k is drawn uniformly from 1 to |A|, and k of those parameters are resampled: continuous ones from KDEs fitted on factory values, categorical ones by factory frequency. A render with RMS below 10⁻³ counts as silent and triggers up to three retries; after three silent draws a corrective heuristic forces filter inputs, sets cutoffs to 40%–60% and raises mixer levels, touching at most 7 controls. The authors admit they did not log how often this heuristic fired.

Annotation. Each preset is rendered as a 0.5 s mono A4 note (MIDI 69) at velocity 100 and 44.1 kHz, normalised to −23 LUFS with pyloudnorm, and scored with the seven AudioCommons descriptors (0–100). The data ship as JSON with all 10,292 WAVs.

限制Limitations

標籤是模型輸出，驗證有循環的味道。 七個描述子來自 AudioCommons 的回歸模型，這些模型本身就是吃 MFCC、頻譜統計等聲學特徵；作者拿 librosa 的頻譜重心、頻帶能量比去做「收斂效度」（亮度對頻譜重心 r = 0.87，深度與 boominess 對低頻比 0.88，溫暖對高頻比 −0.78，sharpness 對頻譜重心 0.77，hardness 對過零率 0.69），證明的比較是「兩組頻譜特徵彼此一致」，而不是「人聽起來也是這樣」。roughness 最弱：它最相關的是頻寬（0.72），作者自己也說它可能只在追蹤整體的刺耳程度。真正的聽測沒有做，只提供了 44 個 WAV 的稽核集。

音高與單一合成器。 200 個 preset 在 A2–A6 重渲染後，深度與 boominess 在兩端平均偏移約 16 分（滿分 100），sharpness 最多 14，最穩的 hardness 也有約 8，個別 preset 可偏到約 50。也就是說 A4 的標籤不能拿來代表這個 preset 的整體音色。全部資料只來自 Sylenth1，重渲染需要這套商用外掛的授權。

生成的偏誤。 KDE 是對每個參數各自擬合邊際分佈、各自抽，參數之間的聯合結構只靠「沒被改到的那些參數」保留；隨機 preset 因此擴大了覆蓋，但仍被 292 個母體的偏好綁住。另外文中一處說收斂效度用了 10,000 個渲染，圖說卻寫 9,998 個，差的兩個沒有解釋。

The labels are model outputs, and their validation is somewhat circular. The seven descriptors come from AudioCommons regression models that themselves consume acoustic features such as MFCCs and spectral statistics; the authors' convergent-validity check against librosa's spectral centroid and band-energy ratios (brightness vs centroid r = 0.87, depth and boominess vs low-band ratio 0.88, warmth vs high-band ratio −0.78, sharpness vs centroid 0.77, hardness vs zero-crossing rate 0.69) mostly shows that two sets of spectral features agree, not that listeners hear it that way. Roughness is weakest: its top correlate is bandwidth (0.72), and the authors themselves suggest it may just track overall harshness. No listening test was run; only a 44-WAV audit set is provided.

Pitch and a single synth. Re-rendering 200 presets at A2–A6 shifts depth and boominess by about 16 points (out of 100) at the extremes, sharpness by up to 14, and even the most stable descriptor, hardness, by about 8; individual presets move by up to about 50. The A4 labels therefore cannot stand in for a preset's overall timbre. Everything comes from Sylenth1, and re-rendering requires a licence for that commercial plugin.

Generation bias. The KDEs are fitted and sampled per parameter on marginals, so joint structure between parameters survives only through the parameters left untouched; random presets widen coverage but stay tied to the 292 parents' preferences. Also, one passage says the validity check used 10,000 renders while the figure caption says 9,998, and the missing two are not explained.

討論Discussion

最有價值的其實是三個基準的數字。 切分方式先用最近的原廠 preset 反推每個隨機 preset 的母體，再依 292 個母體做 70/15/15 分組，得到 6,711／2,100／1,481 筆，874 筆因母體不明確被標記。參數→描述子回歸的 macro MAE：平均值預測 15.78、ridge 12.10、MLP 10.78 ± 0.12（四個種子）；最好預測的是 warmth（8.91），最難的是 roughness（12.05）。值得注意的是，描述子幾乎是參數的確定函數（重渲染雜訊約 1.5），MLP 卻只把誤差從 15.78 降到 10.78，約三成——參數到音色的非線性映射還有很大的空間。

反推方向的結論更尖銳。 最近鄰檢索在 7 維描述子空間找最近的訓練 preset、重新渲染，round-trip macro MAE 是 2.94；CVAE 從先驗抽樣是 13.85 ± 0.32，取 z = 0 是 16.94 ± 2.33，而且可聽率平均只有 90.6%（四個種子介於 67.1%–100%）。作者的解讀是指標已經飽和，未來要比多樣性或稀疏區域的泛化。我會補一句：目標只有 7 個數字，訓練集卻有 6,711 個點密密地鋪在這個空間裡，「找到描述子相近的任一 preset」本來就容易；這個任務被描述子的低維度定義得太寬，與其說資料集「本身就飽和」，不如說是評估目標選得太鬆。

對 MIR 的實際用途。 它最適合當「語意描述子→合成器參數」的可重現入門基準，以及研究 preset 空間結構的素材（PCA 第一主成分不到 4% 變異、前 20 個加起來約三分之一，沒有明顯的低維骨架）。要拿來訓練真正能用的語意合成介面，還得先補上聽測與多音高標註。

The most valuable part is the three baselines. Splits are built by recovering each random preset's parent as its nearest factory preset and then splitting the 292 parents 70/15/15, giving 6,711 / 2,100 / 1,481 items, with 874 flagged as having an ambiguous parent. For parameter→descriptor regression the macro MAE is 15.78 for the mean predictor, 12.10 for ridge and 10.78 ± 0.12 for an MLP (four seeds); warmth is easiest (8.91), roughness hardest (12.05). Notably, the descriptors are nearly a deterministic function of the parameters (re-render noise about 1.5), yet the MLP only cuts error from 15.78 to 10.78, roughly 30%, so the nonlinear parameter-to-timbre map leaves plenty of headroom.

The inversion result is sharper. Nearest-neighbour retrieval in the 7-D descriptor space, re-rendered, gives a round-trip macro MAE of 2.94; a CVAE sampled from the prior gives 13.85 ± 0.32, with z = 0 it gives 16.94 ± 2.33, and its audibility averages only 90.6% (67.1%–100% across four seeds). The authors read this as metric saturation and call for diversity or sparse-region generalisation metrics. I would add that the target is just 7 numbers while 6,711 training points densely tile that space, so finding any preset with similar descriptors is easy by construction; the task is under-constrained by the descriptors' low dimension, which is less a property of the dataset than of a loose evaluation target.

Practical use for MIR. It works best as a reproducible entry benchmark for descriptor→synth-parameter mapping and as material for studying preset-space structure (PC1 explains under 4% of variance and the first 20 PCs about a third, so there is no obvious low-dimensional skeleton). Training a genuinely usable semantic synth interface would first need listening tests and multi-pitch labels.

Navigating relational agency in AI feedback musicianship: a diffractive dialogue

在 AI 回授樂器演奏中周旋於關係性的能動性：一場繞射式對話——兩位作者各自設計、各自演奏的 AI 回授樂器（RAVE 潛在空間回授的「溯」與 MDRNN 手勢回授的 Agentier）合奏五場、共 87 分鐘，以書面反思加五次錄音討論（總計 23,206 字）提煉出 funnelling、injection 等五個概念

Yichen Wang, Charles Patrick Martin, David Gunawan, David Cooper
arXiv:2611.13555 · cs.SD; cs.HC · 投稿submitted 2026-11-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：兩位作者各自做了一把 AI 回授樂器：「溯」把 RAVE 聲音模型的輸出接回輸入，並讓每個潛在維度帶著上一個時間窗的值；Agentier 用自回歸 MDRNN 生成八路 MIDI 控制訊號，與演奏者的手勢互相接續。他們合奏五場（兩場現場、三段錄音，共 87 分鐘），各寫反思再做五次錄音對談，用 Barad 的能動實在論與「繞射式對話」整理出五個主題。選它是因為今天 arXiv 列表上的 MIR 論文幾乎都讀過了，而它是少數未讀、又跟神經音訊模型實際演奏有關的一篇；它也點出一件工程論文很少談的事：有狀態的 AI 樂器需要「暖機」才進得了好用的狀態。要注意：研究對象、樂器設計者、演奏者與分析者都是同兩個人，沒有外部觀察者，也沒有任何量化資料。Why this one: The two authors each built an AI feedback instrument: Sù routes the output of a RAVE audio model back into its input and lets every latent dimension carry the previous window's value; Agentier uses an autoregressive MDRNN to generate eight MIDI control streams that interleave with the performer's gestures. They played five duets (two live, three recorded, 87 minutes in total), each wrote reflections, then held five recorded conversations, and used Barad's agential realism and a "diffractive dialogue" to derive five themes. I picked it because nearly every MIR paper on today's arXiv lists had already been read, and this is one of the few unread ones that concerns actually performing with neural audio models; it also names something engineering papers rarely discuss: stateful AI instruments need a warm-up before they reach a usable state. Caveat: the subjects, instrument designers, performers and analysts are the same two people, there is no outside observer, and there is no quantitative data.

動機Motivation

「控制器」的框架裝不下會自己動的樂器。 數位樂器（DMI）設計長期把樂器當成把演奏者意圖傳出去的工具，合奏研究則習慣用「誰帶、誰跟」來描述互動。作者認為 AI 樂器帶有循環的內部狀態——RAVE 的輸出被送回去當輸入，RNN 的輸出變成它自己的歷史——所以演奏時常出現兩個人聽到同一段聲音、卻對「誰在控制」讀出相反結論的時刻。

論文從一個具體場景開始。 一場吵雜的即興中段突然出現一段稀疏的空檔，兩位演奏者都認為那是轉折點：一位讀成「信任系統、控制得很緊」，另一位讀成「把能動性交給樂器、之後要再搶回來」。研究問題是：用 Barad 的能動實在論來看，演奏者與 AI 樂器在回授音樂中的關係是什麼？

The controller framing cannot hold instruments that move on their own. Digital musical instrument (DMI) design has long treated instruments as tools that transmit the performer's intention, and ensemble research tends to describe interaction as who leads and who follows. The authors argue that AI instruments carry recurrent internal state (RAVE's output is fed back as input; an RNN's output becomes its own history), so performances often contain moments where two players hear the same sound yet read opposite conclusions about who is in control.

The paper opens with a concrete scene. Midway through a noisy improvisation a sparse gap appears, and both performers mark it as a turning point: one reads it as trusting the system under tight control, the other as handing agency to the instrument, to be reclaimed later. The research question is what Barad's agential realism reveals about the relationship between performers and AI instruments in feedback music.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

回授音樂的論述是直接前身。 Mudd（2023）訪談 no-input mixer 演奏者，提出回授演奏需要「放下意圖」、能動性是糾纏的；Magnusson 等人（2022）觀察到回授系統的演奏者會「跟它玩、跟它對抗、被它玩」；Kiefer 等人（2025）把「回授音樂家身分」（feedback musicianship）定義為以訊號循環為核心的音樂實踐。本文的反思提問直接改寫自 Mudd 的訪談題目。

AI 回授樂器已有前例。 Visi 的 Sophtar（2024）把 RAVE 嵌進回授吉他式的弦樂器；Living Looper（Shepardson 等 2025）用 RAVE 做即時循環；Stefánsdóttir 與 Magnusson（2025）寫過自己用 Living Looper 加小提琴演出的實踐紀錄。作者指出這些紀錄把「音訊 AI」與「手勢 AI」分開談，本文是第一次在同一個合奏裡並排兩種。方法上的「繞射式對話」則直接借自 Morrison 與 McPherson（2024），差別是他們用寫的，這裡用講的。

被提問框架本身最該對照的是 George Lewis 的 Voyager。 「機器是在帶還是在跟」這個問題，Lewis 在 1980 年代的即興系統 Voyager 就已經處理過，他的立場也是拒絕把電腦當成伴奏者。本文有引 Lewis（2000），但沒有把自己最後「放棄帶／跟二分」的結論跟這條脈絡對齊；另外「溯」的潛在回授——把當前時間窗的潛在值跟上一窗混合——在訊號處理上就是對潛在軌跡做一階平滑，作者把它當成新機制介紹。

Writing on feedback music is the direct predecessor. Mudd (2023) interviewed no-input mixer players and argued that feedback performance involves "letting go of intention" and entangled agency; Magnusson et al. (2022) observed performers of feedback systems playing with, against, or being played by them; Kiefer et al. (2025) defined feedback musicianship as music-making centred on the recurrent circulation of signals. This paper's reflection prompts are adapted directly from Mudd's interview questions.

AI feedback instruments already exist. Visi's Sophtar (2024) embeds RAVE in a feedback-guitar-style string instrument; the Living Looper (Shepardson et al. 2025) uses RAVE for live looping; Stefánsdóttir and Magnusson (2025) documented their own practice with the Living Looper and violin. The authors note that these accounts treat audio AI and gestural AI separately, and that this is the first to set both side by side in one ensemble. The diffractive dialogue method is borrowed from Morrison and McPherson (2024); the difference is that theirs was written and this one is spoken.

The framing most worth comparing against is George Lewis's Voyager. Whether the machine is leading or following was already addressed by Lewis's improvising system Voyager in the 1980s, and his position was likewise to refuse to treat the computer as an accompanist. The paper cites Lewis (2000) but does not align its final rejection of the lead/follow binary with that lineage. Also, Sù's latent feedback, which mixes the current window's latent values with the previous window's, is in signal-processing terms first-order smoothing of the latent trajectory, yet the authors present it as a new mechanism.

方法Method

兩把樂器。 「溯」（Sù）在 Max/MSP 裡跑一個預訓練的 RAVE 人聲模型（第一場用打擊樂模型），有三層機制：可調增益的音訊回授（可混入 Make Noise 0-COAST 硬體合成器與雜訊）、模型內部的潛在回授、以及用 MIDI 直接改個別潛在維度。Agentier 在 Raspberry Pi 上跑 IMPSY 的混合密度 RNN（MDRNN），用第二作者錄的八路連續控制器資料訓練，與演奏者以 0.1 秒的切換時間輪流產生控制訊號，輸出接到 Roland S-1 合成器的音符與七個音色參數，以及觸控板的八條 LED。

五場演出。 P1（2025/11/03，音樂廳，23 分鐘）、P2（2025/11/22，後院 DIY 演出，29 分鐘）、P3–P5（2025/12/19 同一天錄的三段，11、12、12 分鐘），共 87 分鐘。中間樂器有改：P1 之後「溯」從打擊樂模型換成人聲模型，Agentier 從旋鈕控制器換成觸控板。

三階段分析。 第一階段兩人各自看錄影、依四個提問寫反思（2,906 字與 3,577 字），其中一題是「什麼時候覺得樂器在帶而不是在回應」；第二階段讀彼此的反思後錄五次對談（約 19 到 37 分鐘，合計兩小時多）；第三階段用本地的轉錄與語者分離模型轉成逐字稿，一起找出「單獨反思時不會出現」的交會點。總語料 23,206 字。

Two instruments. Sù runs a pretrained RAVE voice model (a percussion model in the first show) in Max/MSP with three layers: audio feedback with adjustable gain (optionally mixed with a Make Noise 0-COAST hardware synth and noise), latent feedback inside the model, and direct MIDI manipulation of individual latent dimensions. Agentier runs IMPSY's mixture density RNN (MDRNN) on a Raspberry Pi, trained on eight continuous controller streams recorded by the second author; it trades control with the performer with a 0.1 s switchover, and its outputs drive notes and seven timbre parameters on a Roland S-1 synth plus eight LED sliders on a touch pad.

Five performances. P1 (2025/11/03, concert hall, 23 min), P2 (2025/11/22, DIY backyard show, 29 min), and P3–P5 (three takes recorded on 2025/12/19; 11, 12 and 12 min), 87 minutes in total. The instruments changed along the way: after P1, Sù switched from the percussion model to the voice model, and Agentier from a knob controller to a touch pad.

Three-phase analysis. In phase one each author reviewed the recordings and wrote reflections against four prompts (2,906 and 3,577 words), one being "when did you feel the instrument was leading rather than responding?" In phase two they read each other's reflections and recorded five conversations (about 19 to 37 minutes each, a little over two hours in all). In phase three they transcribed these with local transcription and diarisation models and jointly looked for points of interference that would not have emerged from the reflections alone. The full corpus is 23,206 words.

限制Limitations

研究者就是研究對象。 兩位演奏者同時是樂器設計者、受訪者與分析者，第一作者的樂器還有 Dolby 的專利申請；作者在限制一節只提到樣本是「我們兩人的合奏實踐」、時間只有兩個月，以及沒有深入談樂器設計與模型訓練。沒有第三方觀察者、沒有聽眾回饋，也沒有對照條件，五個主題是否只反映這兩人的語彙無法判斷。

「立場反轉」有更平凡的解釋。 論文最醒目的發現是：早期一人把控制等同於穩定、另一人等同於發散，到 P5 兩人互換，而「樂器本身沒有改變」。但 P1 到 P2 之間兩把樂器都換過設定，P3–P5 又是同一天連錄三段，熟練度與疲勞都會影響感受；文中沒有試著排除這些因素。

聲音與說法沒有接起來。 「稠密織體讓人願意放手、稀疏織體被兩人讀成相反意思」是可以用音訊驗證的命題——例如算頻譜通量或事件密度，對照反思裡標註的時間點——但全文沒有任何一個來自錄音的量測。錄音與逐字稿雖然公開在 Zenodo，讀者要自己去對。

The researchers are the subjects. The two performers are also the instrument designers, interviewees and analysts, and the first author's instrument is subject to a Dolby patent application; the authors' limitations section mentions only that the data come from "the duet practice of the first two authors" over two months, and that instrument design and model training were not examined in depth. There is no third-party observer, no audience feedback and no control condition, so one cannot tell whether the five themes reflect anything beyond these two people's vocabulary.

The "reversal" has a more mundane explanation. The paper's headline finding is that early on one performer equated control with stability and the other with divergence, and by P5 they had swapped, "even though the instruments themselves didn't change their behaviour." Yet both instruments changed configuration between P1 and P2, and P3–P5 were recorded back to back on one day, so growing fluency and fatigue could both shape perception; the paper makes no attempt to rule these out.

Sound and talk are never connected. "Dense textures invite letting go, while one sparse passage was read in opposite ways" is a claim that audio could test, for instance by computing spectral flux or event density against time-stamped moments in the reflections, but the paper reports no measurement from the recordings at all. The recordings and transcripts are public on Zenodo, so readers would have to do that matching themselves.

討論Discussion

五個概念。 一、控制是關係性的，而且會隨演出改變；二、織體與演奏者一起決定控制的樣態，作者把這些時刻叫「回授時刻」（feedback moment）；三、兩把樂器都有狀態、每場都得從零建起，進入可用的「回授環境」（feedback environment）本身就是共同探索；四、維持樂器能動性的兩種手法——第一作者在系統「卡住」時注入一下雜訊（injection），第二作者用持續的觸控輸入塞滿 RNN 的歷史、讓它只能短暫插話（funnelling）；五、「周旋於能動性」是一種在五場中逐漸練成的技能，取代了「帶／跟」的二分。

對做 MIR 的人有用的是「狀態」這一點。 injection 與 funnelling 翻成工程語言，其實是「用外部激發把自回歸模型推離不動點」與「用使用者輸入佔滿條件脈絡，壓低模型自由生成的比例」。這提醒做即時生成模型的人：模型在演出開頭的冷啟動行為、以及對輸入歷史的依賴，是使用者體驗的一部分，卻很少出現在評估裡。作者甚至主張回授環境「本來就該難進入」，這跟 Wessel 與 Wright「容易上手」的 NIME 傳統正好相反。

方法上的意義有限。 繞射式對話讓「立場反轉」這類單獨反思看不到的現象浮上來，這是它的價值；但它產出的是詞彙，不是可檢驗的主張。這篇最適合當成「AI 樂器使用者在乎什麼」的質性線索，再由後續研究去量。附帶一提，作者在致謝裡說明用了 Claude Code 協助資料分析與編修。

Five concepts. First, control is relational and shifts across performances. Second, textures and performers jointly shape what control feels like; the authors call these "feedback moments." Third, both instruments are stateful and must be built up from scratch each time, so reaching a productive "feedback environment" is itself a shared exploration. Fourth, two techniques sustain the instruments' agency: the first author injects a burst of noise when the system stalls (injection), and the second floods the RNN's history with constant touch input so it can only interrupt briefly (funnelling). Fifth, "navigating agency" is a skill built up over the five performances that replaces the lead/follow binary.

The useful point for MIR is state. In engineering terms, injection means kicking an autoregressive model off a fixed point with external excitation, and funnelling means saturating the conditioning context with user input to cut the share of free generation. This reminds builders of real-time generative models that cold-start behaviour at the start of a performance and dependence on input history are part of the user experience, yet rarely appear in evaluations. The authors even argue that the feedback environment should be hard to enter, the opposite of Wessel and Wright's NIME tradition of instruments that are easy to start with.

Methodological value is limited. The diffractive dialogue surfaced phenomena such as the reversal that individual reflections did not show, which is its merit; but what it produces is vocabulary, not testable claims. The paper is best read as a qualitative lead on what users of AI instruments care about, for later studies to measure. Incidentally, the acknowledgements state that Claude Code assisted with data analysis and editing.

Diplomatic Transcriptions Between Document and Text: Encoding Beethoven's Sketchbooks

介於文件與文本之間的外交式轉寫：為貝多芬草稿本做編碼——以 1823 年的 Notirungsbuch K（現散落三館七個殘片、約 480 則草稿）為例，在 MEI 上並存「只描述看得到什麼」的外交式轉寫與「補上譜號、時值的」註解式轉寫，用逐筆描下的 SVG 筆畫把兩者連起來，並介紹 Facsimile Explorer 工具與尚在開發的「流動轉寫」

為什麼挑這篇：草稿本是作曲家寫給自己看的：可能沒有譜號、沒有拍號，一個實心符頭加符桿未必代表四分音符。本文是「Beethovens Werkstatt」這個 16 年期研究計畫的資料模型說明：每一筆貝多芬的筆畫都被描成 SVG 路徑（一頁約 8–10 小時），分組成「書寫區」，再各自有一份只記位置與外形的外交式轉寫（DT）和一份補上音樂詮釋的註解式轉寫（AT），兩者以屬性互相指回原始筆畫。選它是因為 arXiv 今天幾乎沒有未讀的 MIR 論文，而它處理的「視覺層與語意層分開存」正是光學樂譜辨識（OMR）的核心問題，它產出的資料也可以當 OMR 的訓練真值。要注意：這是方法與工具的描述，沒有任何量化評估；號稱最創新的「流動轉寫」只有概念驗證。Why this one: Sketchbooks are written by composers for themselves: there may be no clef or time signature, and a filled notehead with a stem need not mean a quarter note. This paper documents the data model of Beethovens Werkstatt, a 16-year research project: every Beethoven pen stroke is traced as an SVG path (about 8–10 hours per page) and grouped into writing zones, each of which gets a diplomatic transcription (DT) that records only position and shape and an annotated transcription (AT) that adds musical interpretation, with both pointing back to the original strokes through attributes. I picked it because today's arXiv lists had almost no unread MIR papers, and its separation of a visual layer from a semantic layer is the core issue of optical music recognition (OMR); the data it produces could also serve as OMR ground truth. Caveat: this is a description of methods and tools with no quantitative evaluation, and the "fluid transcription" it calls most innovative exists only as a proof of concept.

動機Motivation

草稿不遵守記譜規則，用標準編碼描述它就是「標籤濫用」。 一份給雕版師的抄本必須讓別人看得懂；草稿本只是作曲家的備忘，可能只想記下一串音高而不管時值。這時如果用 MEI 的常規寫法把它編成「G4 四分音符」，就是把編者的詮釋硬塞進文件，借用 TEI 的說法叫 tag abuse。比較穩妥的做法是退回去描述「看到什麼」——一個實心符頭加一根符桿，而不是一個四分音符。

兩種既有的外交式版本彼此不能取代。 Weise（1957）編 Grasnick 3 草稿本時盡量保留原頁版面，容易對照原件，但密的地方一樣密，也不幫讀者分開不同草稿；Brandenburg（1978）編 Keßler 草稿本時依音樂邏輯重排、把修改放在上方的 ossia 譜表，好讀但很難對回原頁。Feder（1987）把這兩種都稱為「外交式版本」。本文想在數位版本裡同時保有兩者，並讓讀者在兩端之間切換。

Sketches ignore notation rules, so describing them with standard encoding is "tag abuse." A copy prepared for an engraver must be legible to others; a sketchbook is the composer's aide-mémoire and may record only a sequence of pitches without durations. Encoding such a sketch with MEI's usual patterns as a "G4 quarter note" forces the editor's interpretation into the document, which TEI calls tag abuse. The safer course is to fall back to describing what is seen: a filled notehead with a stem, not a quarter note.

The two existing kinds of diplomatic edition cannot replace each other. Weise (1957), editing the Grasnick 3 sketchbook, kept the original page layout, which makes comparison with the source easy but leaves dense passages just as dense and does not help readers separate sketches; Brandenburg (1978), editing the Keßler sketchbook, rearranged by musical logic and placed revisions in ossia staves above, which reads well but is hard to map back to the page. Feder (1987) calls both "diplomatic editions." This paper wants a digital edition that keeps both and lets readers move between the two ends.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

DT／AT 的切分，在 OMR 裡叫「agnostic／semantic」。 作者自己點名了 Iñesta（2019）與 Ríos-Vila 等人的 agnostic transcription：只記符號外形與在譜線上的位置、不管音高時值。Calvo-Zaragoza 與 Rizo（2018）的 PrIMuS 資料集就為每段譜例同時提供 agnostic 與 semantic 兩種編碼。作者強調的差別是：OMR 把 agnostic 當成要「翻譯」掉的中間格式，本文則把 DT 和 AT 當成同等重要、永久並存的兩個視角，而且認為從 DT 到 AT 這一步是編者的詮釋，現在還沒有足夠的真值可以自動化。

文字學界的對應物是 TEI 的「文件／文本」雙軌轉寫。 例如歌德《浮士德》的歷史考證數位版就把「文件式轉寫」與「文本式轉寫」分開做、互相連結。本文其實是把這套文字學的成熟做法搬到音樂記譜上，論文以 TEI 的 tag abuse 定義開場，但沒有引用這類文字版本的前例。

同類的音樂專案。 Mazzagufo 等人（2024）關注的是把文字與音樂文本結合、追蹤跨來源的創作過程；本文聚焦在 MEI 對視覺資訊的處理，以及讓編輯詮釋透明化。工具面則與 Cartographer App 共用大部分架構，用 Verovio 渲染、MEI-Friend 校對。

The DT/AT split is what OMR calls agnostic versus semantic. The author names Iñesta (2019) and Ríos-Vila et al.'s agnostic transcription, which records only symbol shape and staff position without pitch or duration. Calvo-Zaragoza and Rizo's (2018) PrIMuS dataset already provides both agnostic and semantic encodings for every incipit. The difference the author stresses is that OMR treats the agnostic form as an intermediate to be translated away, whereas here DT and AT are equally important views that persist side by side, and the DT→AT step is considered editorial interpretation for which there is not yet enough ground truth to automate.

The textual-scholarship counterpart is TEI's document/text dual transcription. The historical-critical digital edition of Goethe's Faust, for example, keeps a documentary transcription and a textual transcription separate and linked. This paper essentially carries that mature practice from literary editing over to music notation; it opens with TEI's definition of tag abuse but does not cite such textual-edition precedents.

Comparable music projects. Mazzagufo et al. (2024) combine verbal and musical texts to trace the compositional process across sources; this paper concentrates on MEI's handling of visual information and on making editorial interpretation transparent. On the tooling side, it shares most of its architecture with the Cartographer App, renders with Verovio, and proofreads with MEI-Friend.

方法Method

重建手稿。 Notirungsbuch K 寫於 1823 年初，主要是第九號交響曲第一樂章與《狄亞貝里變奏曲》op.120 的草稿，約 480 則中約五分之一還對不到任何完成作品。它現存七個殘片、分藏波昂貝多芬故居、柏林國家圖書館與巴黎國家圖書館，合計原本 88 頁中的 84 頁。作者用 MEI 的 <facsimile>／<surface> 描述每份現存文件的掃描序列，再用 <foliaDesc> 的 <folium>、<bifolium> 指向這些頁面，在另一個檔案裡重建原始裝訂順序；這樣可以並存多種互相競爭的重建方案。

座標以毫米為準。 不同館的掃描解析度不同，而且掃描圖有未知寬度的邊框，所以作者用 W3C Media Fragments 標出紙頁在圖中的區域，再加一個自訂的 rotate 參數（這批掃描約在 −1° 到 +1° 之間）。沒有完全用 IIIF Image API 的理由是：IIIF 先裁切再旋轉會產生空角，而且有些館的伺服器只支援 90° 倍數旋轉。換算後大部分資料都以毫米儲存。

三層資料。 最底層是逐筆描下的 SVG 筆畫，依書寫區（WZ，暫可視為一則草稿，不能跨頁）分組，WZ 內再分書寫層記錄修改。DT 用 MEI 的視覺屬性記錄「第二線上的實心符頭、符桿朝上」，並以 @facs 指向筆畫；為此新增了 <draft>、<system>、<rastrum> 與 @bw:stem.flags 等自訂元素與屬性，連音線與圓滑線因為不知道音高而統一記成 <curve>。AT 是接近 MEI Basic 的一般樂譜，用打譜軟體產生，補上譜號、時值、修正疑似寫錯一度的音、把「allo」正規化成 allegro，可以跨多個 WZ，並以 @corresp 指回 DT。

工具與流程。 Facsimile Explorer（FX）是瀏覽器端的 JavaScript 應用，直接透過 GitHub REST API 讀寫資料庫：衝突時自動推到新分支並嘗試合併，失敗才保留分支給人處理。實際流程是先匯入 AT，再在 FX 裡點一個 SVG 筆畫、再點 AT 中對應的元素，FX 自動把語意資訊剝掉、算出相對於該五線譜的位置，生成 DT 的預設值。Liquifier 在每次提交時自動輸出補上 <supplied> 的 AT、MEI Basic 版本、MIDI 與預先渲染的 SVG。

Reconstructing the manuscript. Notirungsbuch K was used in early 1823, mostly for sketches of the first movement of the Ninth Symphony and the Diabelli Variations op.120; about a fifth of its roughly 480 sketches still match no finished work. It survives as seven fragments held by the Beethoven-Haus Bonn, the Berlin State Library and the Bibliothèque nationale in Paris, together 84 of a presumed 88 pages. The author describes each surviving document's scan sequence with MEI <facsimile>/<surface>, then uses <foliaDesc> with <folium> and <bifolium> pointing at those pages to rebuild the original binding order in a separate file, so that competing reconstructions can coexist.

Coordinates in millimetres. Scans from different libraries differ in resolution and include margins of unknown size, so the author marks the page region in each image with W3C Media Fragments plus a custom rotate parameter (roughly −1° to +1° for these scans). IIIF's Image API alone was rejected because it crops before rotating, leaving empty corners, and some library servers only rotate in 90° steps. After conversion most data are stored in millimetres.

Three layers of data. At the bottom are SVG pen strokes traced one by one and grouped into writing zones (WZs, roughly one sketch each and never spanning pages), with writing layers inside a WZ for revisions. A DT uses MEI's visual attributes to record "filled notehead on the second line, stem up" and points to the strokes via @facs; this required custom elements and attributes such as <draft>, <system>, <rastrum> and @bw:stem.flags, and ties and slurs are both encoded as <curve> because pitch is unknown. An AT is an ordinary score close to MEI Basic, produced in notation software, which supplies clefs and durations, corrects notes that seem misplaced by a second, normalises "allo" to allegro, may span several WZs, and points back to the DT via @corresp.

Tools and workflow. Facsimile Explorer (FX) is a browser-side JavaScript app that reads and writes the data repository directly through the GitHub REST API; on conflicts it pushes to a new branch and tries to merge, keeping the branch for manual resolution only if that fails. In practice the AT is imported first; the editor then clicks an SVG stroke and the matching AT element, and FX strips the semantic information and computes the position relative to that staff to produce default DT values. On every commit, the Liquifier exports ATs with <supplied> elements, MEI Basic versions, MIDI and pre-rendered SVGs.

限制Limitations

沒有任何量化評估。 全文唯一跟成本有關的數字是描 SVG 一頁約 8–10 小時；以 84 頁粗估就是約 670–840 小時（這是我的換算，論文沒有給總工時），而建 DT／AT 要多少時間、FX 比手工快多少、兩位編者對同一個 WZ 的 DT 一致度多高，都沒有報告。對一篇提出新資料模型的期刊論文，至少應該有一則示範性的時間或一致性量測。

最核心的賣點還沒做完。 作者稱「流動轉寫」（在 DT 與 AT 之間用滑桿漸變、逐步拆開密集的草稿）是高度創新的概念，但中間步驟的順序「仍在考慮」，Liquifier 對它的支援是「進行中」，目前只有概念驗證；遇到同一處有多個創作階段時，也只能讓使用者先選一個版本再漸變。

通用性與可攜性。 模型只在一本貝多芬草稿本上試過，作者自己也說其他作曲家的草稿很可能需要修改；<draft>、<rastrum> 等自訂元素尚未進入 MEI 標準；FX 綁死 GitHub API、只開放給計畫成員寫入，作者也承認這些架構決定「事後看不理想」，並把工具定位為概念驗證而非正式產品。

There is no quantitative evaluation. The only cost figure is that tracing SVGs takes about 8–10 hours per page; across 84 pages that is roughly 670–840 hours (my own arithmetic; the paper gives no total), and nothing is reported on how long DTs and ATs take, how much faster FX is than manual work, or how well two editors' DTs of the same WZ agree. For a journal paper proposing a new data model, at least one illustrative time or agreement measurement would be expected.

The central selling point is unfinished. The author calls "fluid transcription" (morphing between DT and AT with a slider to untangle dense sketches step by step) a highly innovative concept, but the sequence of intermediate steps is "still under consideration," Liquifier support is "work in progress," and only proofs of concept exist; where one spot has several genetic stages, the user must pick one version before morphing.

Generality and portability. The model has been tried on a single Beethoven sketchbook, and the author says other composers' sketches will probably need changes; custom elements such as <draft> and <rastrum> are not yet in the MEI standard; FX is tied to the GitHub API and open for writing only to project members, and the author concedes these architectural decisions "seem less than ideal in hindsight," positioning the tools as proofs of concept rather than production software.

討論Discussion

真正的主張是「文件與文本是光譜的兩端，不是二選一」。 DT 接近 Weise，AT 接近 Brandenburg，但兩者都含有詮釋（DT 也要決定一筆是符桿還是小節線、屬於哪條五線譜），所以作者把它們看成 MEI「視覺域／邏輯域」光譜上的兩個位置，並靠 @facs 與 @corresp 的雙向連結讓讀者在任一個筆畫上同時看到兩種讀法。DT 畫完之後，AT 中沒被對到筆畫的部分就自動成為「編者補上的內容」——這讓編者介入在資料裡變得可以查詢，是這套模型最實用的一點。

對 MIR 的意義在資料，而不在模型。 每一個 AT 元素都能追到 DT、再追到一筆一筆的筆跡，這正是手寫樂譜 OMR 最缺的、帶有逐符號定位的真值；Liquifier 輸出的 MIDI 與 MEI Basic 也可以拿來比對草稿與完成作品。作者也預期等資料夠多，AI 可以接手描筆畫這一步。但目前只有一本、約 480 則草稿，而且資料是專為人文詮釋設計的（例如刻意不分連音線與圓滑線），直接拿去訓練前要先釐清標註規範。

我的整體判斷。 這是一篇紮實的數位音樂學資料模型說明，把手稿重建、跨館座標、筆跡層與兩種轉寫之間的連結都講得很具體；但「重大典範轉移、將對領域產生顯著影響」這類結論，在沒有任何使用者研究或成本量測、而流動轉寫也還沒做完的情況下，目前只能看成願景。

The real claim is that document and text are two ends of a spectrum, not a choice. DTs resemble Weise and ATs resemble Brandenburg, but both contain interpretation (a DT must still decide whether a stroke is a stem or a barline and which staff it belongs to), so the author treats them as two positions on MEI's visual-to-logical spectrum and uses two-way @facs and @corresp links so that readers can see both readings from any pen stroke. Once a DT is complete, whatever in the AT has no matching stroke is automatically an editorial supplement, which makes editorial intervention queryable in the data; that is the model's most practical feature.

For MIR the value lies in the data, not the model. Every AT element can be traced to a DT and then to individual pen strokes, which is exactly the symbol-level, located ground truth that handwritten-score OMR most lacks; the MIDI and MEI Basic exports from Liquifier also allow sketches to be compared with finished works. The author expects that, once there is enough data, AI could take over stroke tracing. For now, though, there is one sketchbook of about 480 sketches, and the data are designed for humanistic interpretation (ties and slurs are deliberately not distinguished, for example), so annotation conventions must be clarified before using it for training.

My overall judgement. This is a solid account of a digital-musicology data model, concrete about manuscript reconstruction, cross-library coordinates, the stroke layer and the links between the two transcriptions. But conclusions such as "a major paradigm shift" that "will have significant impact in the field" can only be read as a vision for now, given that there is no user study or cost measurement and fluid transcription is unfinished.
