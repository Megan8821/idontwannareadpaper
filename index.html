Evaluating the Evaluation of Music Generation Models with Generative Psychometrics

評估「音樂生成模型的評估」：用生成式心理計量學（AI-GENIE）把 2020–2025 年 150 篇 ISMIR 生成論文的 284 道聽測題目壓成 57 題、六個維度（節奏、旋律、音訊、作曲、結構、歌詞）——但「穩定」量的是題目文字嵌入的語意穩定，不是聽眾作答

João Carlos Santana Silveira, Caio Von Rondow Morais, Frederico Pedrosa, Lucas N. Ferreira
arXiv:2611.21809 · cs.SD; cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-11-16
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：音樂生成論文的主觀聽測各問各的，本文想從過去六年的 ISMIR 論文裡歸納出一套共同題庫。作者從 648 篇 ISMIR 2020–2025 論文中篩出 150 篇生成論文，抽出 284 道標準化題目，再用 AI-GENIE（文字嵌入＋UVA 去冗餘＋EGA／Walktrap 分群＋bootEGA 穩定性）收斂成 57 題、六個維度。選它是因為它順手量出一個很刺耳的數字：150 篇裡只有 5 篇附上可用的問卷連結。要注意的是，整個分析沒有任何聽眾作答資料，「84%–100% 穩定」指的是題目文字嵌入在重抽樣下的分群穩定，不是心理計量意義上被驗證過的量表。Why this one: Subjective listening tests in music generation papers each ask different questions, and this paper tries to distil a shared item bank from six years of ISMIR papers. The authors filter 648 ISMIR 2020–2025 papers down to 150 generation papers, extract 284 standardized items, and run AI-GENIE (text embeddings + UVA redundancy pruning + EGA/Walktrap community detection + bootEGA stability) to arrive at 57 items in six dimensions. I picked it because it measures one uncomfortable number along the way: only 5 of the 150 papers link to a working questionnaire. Caveat: the analysis contains no listener responses at all; the "84%–100% stability" refers to how stably the item texts' embeddings cluster under resampling, not to a psychometrically validated scale.

動機Motivation

客觀指標（FAD、CLAP 分數、音高類別熵等）在不同論文間大致一致，但主觀聽測的協定差異很大：題目、量表、受試人數各自為政，而且常常寫得不夠細，別人無法重現。作者的目標是從既有論文中找出聽測題目的共同維度，以及每個維度裡最具代表性的題目，作為未來統一協定的基礎。

這個問題對 MIR 很實際：兩篇生成論文都說「MOS 4.1」，若一篇問的是「音質」、另一篇問的是「你喜不喜歡」，數字根本不可比。本文是 LAMIR（第二屆拉丁美洲 MIR 工作坊）接受的短文，篇幅與實驗規模也相應較小。

Objective metrics (FAD, CLAP score, pitch-class entropy and so on) are fairly consistent across papers, but subjective listening-test protocols vary widely: items, scales and participant counts differ, and they are often described too vaguely to reproduce. The authors aim to find the common dimensions of listening-test items in existing papers, and the most representative item within each, as a basis for a unified protocol.

The problem is practical for MIR: if two generation papers both report "MOS 4.1" but one asked about audio quality and the other asked whether the listener liked it, the numbers are not comparable. The paper is a short paper accepted at LAMIR (the 2nd Latin American MIR workshop), and its scope and experiments are correspondingly small.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂生成評估的回顧不是新題目。 Yang 與 Lerch（2020，Neural Computing and Applications）就整理過生成音樂的客觀與主觀評估並提出一組客觀指標；Agres、Forth 與 Wiggins（2016）從計算創造力角度檢討過聽測設計。語音領域更直接的先例是 Kirkland 等人（SSW 2023）的「Stuck in the MOS pit」，系統性地盤點 TTS 論文怎麼報告 MOS 聽測，結論同樣是協定細節大量缺漏。本文的差別在於範圍限定在 ISMIR，而且不只是計數，還試圖推出題目的潛在維度。

方法上，最像的既有做法是「句子嵌入＋分群」的主題建模。 EGA（Golino 與 Epskamp 2017 提出的探索性圖分析）原本是用受試者作答的偏相關網路估計維度數；AI-GENIE（Russell-Lasalandra 等人 2026）把作答換成題目文字的 LLM 嵌入，理由是嵌入空間的相似度與人類作答的共變相近。拿掉這層心理計量語彙，本文做的事跟 BERTopic（Grootendorst 2022）把句子嵌入後分群、再由人命名主題非常接近；差別在於用 UVA 去冗餘與 bootEGA 檢查分群穩定度。

作者在音樂領域引用的唯一先例是 Ribeiro 等人（2026）用同法預測出兒童音樂發展的三維結構，並與 Buren 等人（2025）實際作答資料的因素分析一致——但那是另一個問題的旁證，不是本文六個維度的驗證。

Reviewing music generation evaluation is not a new topic. Yang and Lerch (2020, Neural Computing and Applications) surveyed objective and subjective evaluation of generated music and proposed a set of objective metrics; Agres, Forth and Wiggins (2016) critiqued listening-test design from a computational-creativity angle. A more direct precedent from speech is Kirkland et al.'s "Stuck in the MOS pit" (SSW 2023), which systematically audited how TTS papers report MOS tests and likewise found protocol details widely missing. This paper differs by restricting itself to ISMIR and by trying to infer latent dimensions of the items rather than only counting practices.

Methodologically, the closest existing technique is topic modelling by sentence embedding plus clustering. EGA (Exploratory Graph Analysis, Golino and Epskamp 2017) originally estimates dimensionality from a partial-correlation network of respondents' answers; AI-GENIE (Russell-Lasalandra et al. 2026) replaces the answers with LLM embeddings of the item texts, on the grounds that embedding similarity approximates human response covariation. Stripped of the psychometric vocabulary, what this paper does is very close to BERTopic (Grootendorst 2022), which embeds sentences, clusters them and has a human name the topics; the differences are UVA redundancy pruning and a bootEGA stability check.

The only music-domain precedent the authors cite is Ribeiro et al. (2026), who used the same method to predict a three-dimensional structure of children's musical development that matched Buren et al.'s (2025) factor analysis of real responses—but that is indirect support from a different problem, not validation of this paper's six dimensions.

方法Method

題目收集。 以 ISMIR25Meta 資料集為底，保留 2020 年以後的論文並手動補上 2025 年，共 648 篇。用 Gemini 依摘要判斷是否屬於生成任務，得到 150 篇；平行的關鍵字篩選得到 152 篇，作為交叉驗證（論文沒報告兩份名單的重疊有多少）。作者手動抽出聽測段落，再請 LLM 依國際測驗委員會的題目改編指引改寫成標準化題目，例如把「creativity (C)」改成「rate the generation quality in terms of creativity」，並附上量表與受試人數；接著人工比對原文、刪掉 LLM 編造的題目，最後得到 284 題。

題目分析。 每題用 OpenAI text-embedding-3-small 轉成 1536 維向量，送進 AI-GENIE：UVA 反覆剔除語意冗餘的題目，從 284 題降到 57 題；EGA 建出稀疏網路後用 Walktrap 隨機遊走分群；bootEGA 重抽樣檢查每題是否穩定落在同一群。六個群由一位具音樂博士學位的作者命名，再以網路負荷量（network loading, NL，題目在自己群內的標準化節點強度）挑出各維度最具代表性的題目。

Item collection. Starting from the ISMIR25Meta dataset, the authors keep papers from 2020 onward and add 2025 manually, for 648 papers. Gemini classifies each abstract as generation-related or not, yielding 150 papers; a parallel keyword filter yields 152 as a cross-check (the overlap between the two lists is not reported). The authors manually extract the listening-test passages, and an LLM rewrites them into standardized items following International Test Commission adaptation guidelines—for example turning "creativity (C)" into "rate the generation quality in terms of creativity", with scale and sample size attached. A manual pass compares each item with the source text and removes hallucinated ones, leaving 284 items.

Item analysis. Each item is embedded with OpenAI text-embedding-3-small (1536 dimensions) and passed to AI-GENIE: UVA iteratively removes semantically redundant items, reducing 284 to 57; EGA builds a sparse network and partitions it with the Walktrap random-walk algorithm; bootEGA resamples to check that each item stays in the same community. The six communities are named by an author with a PhD in music, and network loading (NL, an item's standardized node strength within its community) picks the most representative item per dimension.

限制Limitations

最根本的問題：沒有任何聽眾作答。 傳統 EGA 量的是「受試者在兩題上的分數是否一起變動」；這裡量的是「兩道題目的文字是否相近」。所以這六個維度描述的是 ISMIR 論文用什麼字眼提問，而不是聽眾實際上分辨得出哪些面向。bootEGA 的 84%–100% 穩定度也只表示嵌入分群在重抽樣下不會亂跳；含有「rhythm」的題目聚在一起並不令人意外。作者在結論把「用新的聽測做實證驗證」列為未來工作，等於承認這一步還沒做。

報告不完整。 284 題刪到 57 題，被刪掉的 227 題去了哪、六個維度各有幾題、各維度在 150 篇論文中出現的頻率，論文都沒有給；作者說收集了受試人數與量表，結果裡卻沒有任何統計。最常見的泛用題目——「自然度」「音樂性」「創造力」——歸到哪一群也沒交代。

其他限制。 只看單一會議、單一嵌入模型（作者自己承認）；維度名稱由一位作者主觀命名；「作曲」維度最強的題目是「我喜歡這首曲子」，混進了偏好而非屬性，作者也指出了這點；和聲沒有成為獨立維度，被吸收或刪除，這對音樂評估是個值得追問的結果，但論文只用一句話帶過。

The fundamental issue: there are no listener responses. Classical EGA measures whether respondents' scores on two items move together; here it measures whether the two items' wording is similar. The six dimensions therefore describe the vocabulary ISMIR papers use when asking questions, not the aspects listeners can actually tell apart. The 84%–100% bootEGA stability only shows that the embedding clusters do not jump around under resampling; it is unsurprising that items containing "rhythm" cluster together. The authors list empirical validation with new listening tests as future work, which concedes that this step has not been done.

Incomplete reporting. The paper does not say where the 227 items removed between 284 and 57 went, how many items each dimension holds, or how often each dimension appears across the 150 papers. The authors say they collected sample sizes and scales, yet report no statistics on them. Nor do they say which community absorbed the most common generic items—"naturalness", "musicality", "creativity".

Other limits. A single venue and a single embedding model (acknowledged by the authors); dimension names assigned subjectively by one author; the strongest "Composition" item is "I like this composition", which mixes preference with an attribute, as the authors themselves note; and harmony did not emerge as its own dimension, being absorbed or pruned—a result worth probing for music evaluation, which the paper dispatches in one sentence.

討論Discussion

最有用的是附帶的盤點數字，而不是那六個維度。 「150 篇生成論文只有 5 篇附上可用問卷」這個比例（約 3%）本身就足以支持作者的動機，也比任何嵌入分群更有說服力。若作者把收集到的受試人數、量表類型（5 點 Likert、MUSHRA 等）做成年度統計，這篇的參考價值會高很多。

六個維度可以當成題庫起點，但不是協定。 表 1 列的代表題目——「生成鼓組的節奏穩定度」（NL 0.59）、「旋律整體品質如何」（0.55）、「音質的主觀評估」（0.53）、「我喜歡這首曲子」（0.52）、「長期結構」（0.61）、「輸入文字與生成歌詞的印象是否相近」（0.50）——有些綁定特定任務（鼓組、歌詞），未必適合當通用題目。真正要成為協定，需要像 Kirkland 等人對 TTS 那樣給出具體報告清單，或用實際作答做驗證性因素分析。

值得做的後續： 拿這 57 題去跑一次真實聽測，看作答資料的 EGA 是否重現六群，特別是「旋律」與「作曲」能否分開、和聲是否自成一維。那才會回答標題那個問題。

The most useful output is the incidental audit number, not the six dimensions. "Only 5 of 150 generation papers provide a working questionnaire link" (about 3%) supports the motivation on its own and is more persuasive than any embedding clustering. Had the authors tabulated the sample sizes and scale types (5-point Likert, MUSHRA, etc.) they collected by year, the paper would be far more useful as a reference.

The six dimensions are a starting item bank, not a protocol. The representative items in Table 1—"rhythmic stability of the generated drum" (NL 0.59), "what is the overall quality of the melody?" (0.55), "a subjective assessment of the audio quality" (0.53), "I like this composition" (0.52), "long-term structure" (0.61), and "are the impressions of the input text and the generated lyrics similar?" (0.50)—are partly tied to specific tasks (drums, lyrics) and may not suit a general questionnaire. To become a protocol it would need a concrete reporting checklist, as Kirkland et al. provided for TTS, or a confirmatory factor analysis on real responses.

The follow-up worth doing: run these 57 items in an actual listening test and check whether EGA on the responses reproduces the six communities—in particular whether Melody and Composition separate and whether harmony forms its own dimension. That would answer the question in the title.

Music Similarity Learning in Sparse Latin Scenarios

稀疏拉丁音樂情境下的音樂相似度學習：以 Amazon 評論資料的「正則化餘弦」共現相似度為目標，用 SBERT 嵌入＋後設資料特徵訓練 XGBoost 迴歸，從全域商品零樣本轉移到拉丁音樂（NDCG@10 0.477 對同域 0.480，但 Spearman 0.400 對 0.521），全文沒有任何基線比較

Gabrielly Xavier dos Santos, Lucas Dias Cler, Olga Goussevskaia
arXiv:2611.22846 · cs.IR · 投稿submitted 2026-11-17
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇想證明：不用音訊、不用深度模型，只靠公開的商品後設資料與評論文字，也能估出音樂之間的相似度，而且能轉移到資料稀少的拉丁音樂。做法是在 Amazon CDs_and_Vinyl＋Digital_Music 上，把使用者共現算成「正則化餘弦」當目標，再用 all-MiniLM-L6-v2 嵌入、Jaccard 重疊、同藝人旗標等特徵訓練 XGBoost。選它是因為「非西方曲目的冷啟動」是 MIR 推薦裡實在的問題，而 SHAP 顯示的特徵依賴轉移（全域模型約 55% 靠其他後設資料，拉丁模型約 60% 靠評論資料）有參考價值。但要注意：全文沒有任何基線——沒有純協同過濾、沒有熱門度、沒有深度模型——所以「足以與深度方法競爭」這個說法沒有被測過。Why this one: This paper sets out to show that, without audio or deep models, publicly available product metadata and review text suffice to estimate item-item music similarity, and that this transfers to sparsely documented Latin music. On Amazon CDs_and_Vinyl + Digital_Music, user co-occurrence is turned into a "regularized cosine" target, and XGBoost is trained on all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings, Jaccard overlaps, an exact-artist flag and similar features. I picked it because cold start for non-Western catalogues is a real MIR recommendation problem, and the SHAP shift in feature reliance (the Global model relies about 55% on other metadata, the Latin model about 60% on review data) is informative. Caveat: the paper contains no baseline at all—no plain collaborative filtering, no popularity, no deep model—so its claim of competing with deep approaches is untested.

動機Motivation

作者觀察到兩道門檻：深度音訊模型與 LLM 推薦系統需要大量算力，且仰賴授權音訊或廠商專有特徵；公開音樂語料又偏重西方、英語曲目，拉丁美洲等非西方曲風在 MIR 資料中占比很低，基於相似度或嵌入的推薦也被證明會讓弱勢文化群體的藝人吃虧。

因此他們主張：具競爭力、穩健的音樂推薦不一定需要深度音訊模型或專有特徵。研究問題是，一個可解釋的輕量模型，能不能只靠公開後設資料與評論學會逼近協同過濾的相似度，並在沒看過拉丁音樂的情況下零樣本轉移過去。

The authors identify two barriers: deep audio models and LLM-based recommenders need heavy compute and depend on licensed audio or vendor-specific features, and public music corpora are skewed toward Western, English-language catalogues, with Latin American and other non-Western genres a small share of MIR data; similarity- and embedding-based recommenders have also been shown to disadvantage artists from underrepresented cultural groups.

They therefore argue that competitive, robust music recommendation need not require deep audio models or proprietary features. The research question is whether a lightweight, interpretable model can learn to approximate collaborative-filtering similarity from public metadata and reviews alone, and transfer zero-shot to Latin music it has never seen.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

本質上這是「從內容特徵預測協同過濾訊號」的冷啟動做法。 最接近的既有工作是 van den Oord、Dieleman 與 Schrauwen（NeurIPS 2013）用卷積網路從音訊預測 CF 隱因子，以及 Gantner 等人（ICDM 2010）學習「屬性→隱因子」映射來處理冷啟動商品。本文把預測對象從隱因子換成成對的物品相似度，把輸入從音訊換成後設資料與評論嵌入，模型換成 XGBoost——方向相同，差在不走嵌入、改走成對特徵。論文本身引用了 van den Oord 那篇與 Oramas 等人（2017）的深度多模態冷啟動推薦，卻沒有把它們當成實驗對照。

目標函數的「正則化餘弦」也有前身。 在分母加一個懲罰項來壓低少量共現造成的虛高相似度，是物品式 CF 裡常見的收縮（shrinkage）技巧，例如 Koren 等人在 Netflix Prize 時期用的 n/(n+λ) 收縮。本文的差別是懲罰項不固定，而是隨共現比例變動，上限為 2。物品式 CF 本身則可追溯到 Sarwar 等人（WWW 2001）。

評論挖掘與地區落差。 用評論補充稀疏評分矩陣來自 McAuley 與 Leskovec（RecSys 2013）的 HFT，資料來源同為 Amazon。拉丁音樂在 MIR 裡已有 Latin Music Database 的曲風分類基準等專門資源；作者主張把這條線延伸到推薦，是他們所知第一個明確以此為目標的混合 CF 工作。

At its core this is a cold-start approach that predicts a collaborative-filtering signal from content features. The closest prior work is van den Oord, Dieleman and Schrauwen (NeurIPS 2013), who predicted CF latent factors from audio with a convolutional network, and Gantner et al. (ICDM 2010), who learned attribute-to-latent-factor mappings for cold-start items. This paper replaces the target (latent factors → pairwise item similarity), the input (audio → metadata and review embeddings) and the model (→ XGBoost)—the same direction, but through pairwise features rather than an embedding. The paper itself cites van den Oord et al. and Oramas et al.'s (2017) deep multimodal cold-start recommender, yet uses neither as an experimental comparison.

The "regularized cosine" target also has precedents. Adding a penalty to the denominator to deflate similarities inflated by a handful of co-occurrences is the standard shrinkage trick of item-based CF, such as the n/(n+λ) shrinkage Koren and colleagues used during the Netflix Prize. The difference here is that the penalty is not fixed but varies with the co-occurrence ratio, capped at 2. Item-based CF itself goes back to Sarwar et al. (WWW 2001).

Review mining and the regional gap. Using reviews to complement sparse rating matrices comes from McAuley and Leskovec's HFT (RecSys 2013), also on Amazon data. Latin music already has dedicated MIR resources such as the Latin Music Database genre-classification benchmark; the authors claim to extend this line to recommendation, which to their knowledge no prior hybrid CF work has explicitly targeted.

方法Method

資料。 合併 Amazon 評論資料集的 CDs_and_Vinyl 與 Digital_Music，16 個後設欄位保留 6 個，缺值用二元旗標標記（描述缺 37.54%、details 缺 12.40%）。只要 categories 裡有「latin」就歸為拉丁域，共 13,109 件、42,465 則評論，其中 55.66% 的商品只有一則評論；全域則排除拉丁商品並只留至少 10 則評論的商品，從 747,880 件降到 84,544 件、3,239,399 則評論。

目標。 以使用者二元互動向量計算 S(a,b) = 共現數 /（√(N_a·N_b) + α），其中 α = 共現數/N_a + 共現數/N_b，最大為 2。用我自己的算例說明效果：兩件各只有 1 則評論且共現 1 次，原始餘弦是 1.0，正則化後是 1/3；兩件各 100 則、共現 1 次，α 只有 0.02，幾乎不受影響。成對抽樣方面，全域保留所有共現 ≥3 的 1,006,988 對，再從共現 0、1、2 各抽 335,662 對，總計 2,013,974 對；拉丁域保留所有共現 ≥1 的 102,635 對，加上等量零共現對，共 205,270 對。

特徵與模型。 每對商品計算屬性集合的 Jaccard、兩者有評論的年份重疊、描述與「最佳一則評論」的 all-MiniLM-L6-v2 嵌入之餘弦、歐氏距離與內積、同藝人旗標與各自的缺值旗標，送進 XGBoost 迴歸。評估三種設定：全域→全域、拉丁→拉丁（各先做 80/20 商品層級切分防洩漏）、全域→拉丁零樣本；指標為 RMSE、Spearman、NDCG@5/10，四個隨機種子，另做 SHAP 分析。

Data. CDs_and_Vinyl and Digital_Music from the Amazon reviews dataset are merged; 6 of 16 metadata fields are kept, with binary flags for missing values (description missing 37.54%, details 12.40%). Any item whose categories contain "latin" goes to the Latin domain: 13,109 items and 42,465 reviews, with 55.66% of items having a single review. The Global domain excludes Latin items and keeps only items with at least 10 reviews, going from 747,880 to 84,544 items and 3,239,399 reviews.

Target. On binary user-interaction vectors, S(a,b) = co-occurrences / (√(N_a·N_b) + α), with α = co-occurrences/N_a + co-occurrences/N_b, at most 2. My own worked example of the effect: two items with one review each that co-occur once have a raw cosine of 1.0 and a regularized score of 1/3; two items with 100 reviews each that co-occur once get α of only 0.02, almost unchanged. For pair sampling, the Global domain keeps all 1,006,988 pairs with at least 3 co-occurrences and samples 335,662 pairs from each of the 0-, 1- and 2-co-occurrence buckets, for 2,013,974 pairs; the Latin domain keeps all 102,635 pairs with at least 1 co-occurrence plus an equal number of zero pairs, for 205,270.

Features and model. For each pair: Jaccard over attribute sets, overlap of the years in which each item was reviewed, cosine, Euclidean distance and dot product of all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings of the description and the "top" review, an exact-artist flag and each item's missing-value flags, all fed to an XGBoost regressor. Three setups are evaluated: Global→Global and Latin→Latin (each with an item-level 80/20 split before pair generation to prevent leakage) and Global→Latin zero-shot; metrics are RMSE, Spearman and NDCG@5/10 over four seeds, plus a SHAP analysis.

限制Limitations

沒有基線，是這篇最大的缺口。 表 2 只有本方法自己的三組數字：NDCG@10 全域 0.731、拉丁同域 0.480、零樣本 0.477；Spearman 0.484、0.521、0.400；RMSE 0.031、0.118、0.153。沒有「只用同藝人旗標」「只用描述嵌入」「熱門度」這類簡單對照，也沒有任何深度模型。結論說 XGBoost 能以「一小部分的訓練成本」達到「具競爭力的排序品質」，但論文既沒有比較對象，也沒有報告任何訓練時間或成本。

零樣本的說法挑了最有利的指標。 NDCG@10 0.477 對 0.480 確實接近，作者稱之為「統計上具競爭力」，但沒有做任何檢定；同一張表的 Spearman（0.400 對 0.521）與 RMSE（0.153 對 0.118）都明顯較差。NDCG 的查詢與候選清單怎麼構成也沒有說明，而拉丁評估集有一半是目標為 0 的零共現對，這種組成本身可能就讓排序變簡單。

特徵與目標來自同一批評論。 評論年份重疊與評論嵌入都取自產生共現目標的那批評論，在拉丁模型裡這類特徵占 SHAP 約 60%。這不一定是洩漏，但削弱了「只靠後設資料」的說法。作者自己承認的限制還有：「latin」標籤混雜拉丁爵士、拉丁流行、墨西哥地區音樂等子曲風；全域有 ≥10 評論過濾而拉丁域沒有，所以兩域的絕對數值不可直接比較；Amazon 評論與串流平台的播放、跳過行為可能不同。

The absence of baselines is the paper's largest gap. Table 2 contains only the method's own numbers in three setups: NDCG@10 of 0.731 Global, 0.480 Latin in-domain and 0.477 zero-shot; Spearman of 0.484, 0.521 and 0.400; RMSE of 0.031, 0.118 and 0.153. There is no simple comparison such as exact-artist flag only, description embedding only or popularity, and no deep model. The conclusion says XGBoost delivers "competitive ranking quality at a fraction of the training cost", but the paper has nothing to compete against and reports no training time or cost.

The zero-shot claim picks the most favourable metric. NDCG@10 of 0.477 vs 0.480 is indeed close, and the authors call it "statistically competitive", but no test is run; in the same table Spearman (0.400 vs 0.521) and RMSE (0.153 vs 0.118) are clearly worse. How NDCG queries and candidate lists are formed is not explained, and half of the Latin evaluation pairs are zero-co-occurrence pairs with target 0, a composition that may itself make ranking easier.

Features and target come from the same reviews. Review-year overlap and review embeddings are drawn from the reviews that generate the co-occurrence target, and in the Latin model such features account for about 60% of SHAP attribution. This is not necessarily leakage, but it weakens the "metadata only" framing. Limitations the authors acknowledge: the "latin" tag lumps together Latin jazz, Latin pop, Regional Mexican and other subgenres; the Global domain has a ≥10-review filter while the Latin domain does not, so absolute values are not directly comparable across domains; and Amazon reviews may differ from streaming behaviour such as plays and skips.

討論Discussion

SHAP 的轉移是這篇最有意思的觀察。 全域模型約 55% 靠「其他後設資料」（同藝人、類別與商店的 Jaccard、缺值旗標）；拉丁同域模型約 60% 靠「評論資料」；描述嵌入在三種設定下都穩定在約 20%；零樣本模型介於兩者之間，約 43% 後設資料、35% 評論。這說明在稀疏小眾曲目上，結構化標籤反而不可靠，少數評論的語意成了主要訊號——這對做非西方曲目推薦的人是有用的提示。

拉丁同域 Spearman 反而較高（0.521 對 0.484），作者解釋為稀疏小眾的目標值分布不連續，相對排序比絕對數值容易抓。這個解釋合理，但也再次說明三組數字之間不能直接比較，正如作者在限制裡所說。

要讓結論站得住，最少需要補三件事： 一組簡單基線（至少同藝人規則與純描述嵌入）、說清楚 NDCG 的查詢與候選定義、以及作者已經列為未來工作的 Last.fm-360k 與 Million Song Dataset 上的對照實驗。在那之前，這篇比較像一個可重現的特徵工程起點，而不是「不需要深度模型」的證據。

The SHAP shift is the paper's most interesting observation. The Global model relies about 55% on "Other Metadata" (exact artist, Jaccard over categories and store, missing-value flags); the Latin in-domain model relies about 60% on "Review Data"; description embeddings stay at about 20% in all setups; the zero-shot model sits in between at about 43% metadata and 35% reviews. This suggests that in sparse niche catalogues structured tags become unreliable and the semantics of a few reviews become the main signal—a useful hint for anyone building recommenders for non-Western catalogues.

Spearman is actually higher in Latin in-domain (0.521 vs 0.484), which the authors attribute to a less continuous target distribution in the sparse niche, making relative order easier to capture than absolute value. The explanation is plausible, but it again shows that the three sets of numbers cannot be compared directly, as the authors note in their limitations.

At minimum, three things are needed to make the conclusion stand: a few simple baselines (at least an exact-artist rule and description embeddings alone), a clear definition of NDCG queries and candidates, and the matched experiments on Last.fm-360k and the Million Song Dataset that the authors already list as future work. Until then, this is more a reproducible feature-engineering starting point than evidence that deep models are unnecessary.

Convolutional VocalTrax: Replacing Backpropagation Through Time with Convolution in Differentiable Articulatory Synthesis

卷積版 VocalTrax：把可微分 Pink Trombone 聲道波導的逐樣本遞迴，換成精確頻率響應＋5.8 ms 凍結區塊卷積，聲道前後向傳遞在 A100 上快 47–63 倍，AudioMNIST 重建的辨識率 14.5% 對原版 10.0%，但 log-mel 誤差高 11%

Yongyi Zang, Debasish Ray Mohapatra, Randal Leistikow, Perry R. Cook
arXiv:2611.17181 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-11-12

為什麼挑這篇：VocalTrax 用梯度下降穿過可微分的 Pink Trombone 聲道合成器，從錄音反推可解釋的發音控制（舌位、收縮、聲門緊張度），但它的 Kelly-Lochbaum 波導每個音訊樣本更新兩次，反向傳播要穿過每秒約 88,000 步的遞迴，在 GPU 上很慢。本文推導這個波導的精確頻率響應，假設聲道在 256 樣本（5.8 ms）內不動、脈衝響應在 4096 樣本（93 ms）後可截斷，改成區塊卷積。選它是因為這是物理建模「聲音比對」（sound matching）的乾淨範例，和合成器參數反推、歌聲物理模型同屬一條線；它與 MIR 的連結是方法而非資料——實驗只用了英語數字語音。Why this one: VocalTrax recovers interpretable articulatory controls (tongue position, constrictions, glottal tenseness) from a recording by gradient descent through a differentiable Pink Trombone vocal-tract synthesizer, but its Kelly-Lochbaum waveguide is updated twice per audio sample, so backpropagation runs through roughly 88,000 recurrent steps per second of audio and is slow on GPUs. This paper derives the waveguide's exact frequency response and, assuming the tract is static within 256 samples (5.8 ms) and its impulse response can be truncated after 4096 samples (93 ms), replaces the recursion with block convolution. I picked it as a clean example of physical-model sound matching, in the same line as synthesizer parameter inversion and physical singing-voice models; its link to MIR is methodological rather than data-driven—the experiments use only spoken English digits.

動機Motivation

發音合成（articulatory synthesis）模擬聲道本身，控制參數有物理意義、容易編輯；但要讓它重現一段錄音，就得做聲學到發音的反推（AAI）。資料驅動的 AAI 需要昂貴的發音與聲學平行資料；分析合成（analysis-by-synthesis）則直接搜尋讓合成器重現目標的參數，可微分實作讓這個搜尋能用梯度下降。

VocalTrax 已經做到這點，但很慢：在 A100 上光是聲道的反向傳遞就要每秒音訊 0.74 秒，一段一秒長的片段跑 1000 步要約 24 分鐘。作者的目標是把時間遞迴從聲道與其梯度中拿掉，而不改動音源、損失與最佳化器。

Articulatory synthesis simulates the vocal tract itself, so its controls are physically meaningful and easy to edit; but making it reproduce a recording requires acoustic-to-articulatory inversion (AAI). Data-driven AAI needs expensive parallel articulatory and acoustic data; analysis-by-synthesis instead searches directly for the controls that make the synthesizer match the target, and a differentiable implementation lets that search use gradient descent.

VocalTrax already does this, but slowly: on an A100 the backward pass through the tract alone takes 0.74 s per second of audio, and a 1000-step fit of a one-second clip takes about 24 minutes. The authors aim to remove the time recursion from the tract and its gradient without changing the source, the loss or the optimizer.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最像的既有技巧，是 DDSP 的逐幀 FFT 卷積加上聲道的解析轉移函數。 Engel 等人（DDSP，ICLR 2020）早就把時變濾波器切成逐幀的 FIR，用 FFT 卷積與重疊相加實作；Smyth 與 Zurale（2021）推導過 Pink Trombone 聲道的解析轉移函數，Südholt 等人（2023）據此逐幀擬合母音的面積函數，但只比對幅度、合成要等擬合完才做。本文的貢獻是把兩者接起來：保留相位，推導的是 VocalTrax 實際執行的波導（含半樣本更新），並把它放進端到端的時域擬合中，讓幾何形狀逐幀變動。

被加速的對象。 VocalTrax（Mo 等人 2024）以 JAX 實作 Pink Trombone，用 CREPE 估 F0，以 log-mel 損失端到端擬合逐幀控制。Cámara 等人（2026）把它延伸到雙音（biphonic）歌唱的複製合成，仍是穿過逐樣本遞迴求導——這正是本文方法可以直接受惠的音樂用途。

放在音樂科技的脈絡裡， 共同作者 Perry Cook 在 1990 年代的 SPASM 就是以波導聲道模型做即時歌聲合成；Kelly-Lochbaum（1962）的分段圓管模型則是這整條線的源頭。可微分遞迴濾波器的其他做法，如 Kuznetsov 等人（2020）在時域或透過頻率響應訓練 IIR，也是本文明說的參照。

The closest existing technique is DDSP's frame-wise FFT convolution combined with the tract's analytical transfer function. Engel et al. (DDSP, ICLR 2020) already split time-varying filters into frame-wise FIRs implemented with FFT convolution and overlap-add; Smyth and Zurale (2021) derived the analytical transfer function of the Pink Trombone tract, and Südholt et al. (2023) used it to fit vowel area functions frame by frame, matching magnitude only and synthesizing audio only after the fit. This paper's contribution is to connect the two: it keeps the phase, derives the waveguide exactly as VocalTrax executes it (including half-sample updates), and places it inside the end-to-end time-domain fit, with geometry moving frame to frame.

What is being accelerated. VocalTrax (Mo et al. 2024) implements the Pink Trombone in JAX, estimates F0 with CREPE and fits frame-wise controls end-to-end with a log-mel loss. Cámara et al. (2026) extended it to copy-synthesis of biphonic singing, still differentiating through the sample-by-sample recursion—precisely the musical use that this method could benefit directly.

In a music-technology context, co-author Perry Cook's SPASM in the 1990s already used a waveguide vocal-tract model for real-time singing synthesis, and Kelly and Lochbaum's (1962) segmented-tube model is the origin of the whole line. Other approaches to differentiable recursive filters, such as Kuznetsov et al. (2020) training IIRs in the time domain or through their frequency response, are cited explicitly as reference points.

方法Method

精確的靜態響應。 聲道有 44 段，反射係數 r_i =（A_i − A_{i+1}）/（A_i + A_{i+1}），每次傳播乘上損耗 d = 0.999，聲門與嘴唇反射係數為 0.75 與 −0.85。固定幾何時，作者在頻域從嘴唇往聲門遞推 q_i 與 t_i（沿聲道 43 步，對所有頻率向量化），得到半樣本時脈下的響應 H_h，再合成音訊率響應 H(ω)。因為段數 44 是偶數，第二項相消，H(ω) 化簡為 H_h(ω/2)/4——也就是從聲門到嘴唇的每條路徑都走偶數個半步。在雙精度下，這個響應與遞迴波導直接算出的脈衝響應相對誤差為 6×10⁻¹⁰。

凍結區塊卷積。 幾何會動時做兩個假設：每 256 樣本（44.1 kHz 下 5.8 ms）的區塊內幾何不變，取區塊中點內插的反射係數；脈衝響應只保留前 4096 樣本（93 ms），由 8192 點逆 FFT 求得。輸出是各區塊音源乘上該區塊脈衝響應的線性卷積總和，用補零 FFT 與重疊相加實作。梯度只穿過 FFT 與 43 步的沿聲道遞迴，長度與片段長短無關。

近似誤差。 固定幾何時唯一誤差是截斷：遞迴模型中，輸出對 100 ms 以前音源的敏感度能量最多只占 1.2×10⁻¹¹，單精度下波形差 8.1×10⁻⁶，即捨入誤差層級。幾何移動時，區塊尾巴沿用激發它的那個區塊的濾波器，而真實波導中的波會穿過當下的幾何；直徑以平均值 5% 與 20% 正弦擺動時，256 樣本區塊的波形誤差為 1.04% 與 4.00%（64 樣本為 0.79% 與 2.95%，但成本加倍）。

Exact stationary response. The tract has 44 segments with reflection coefficients r_i = (A_i − A_{i+1})/(A_i + A_{i+1}); each propagation step scales waves by d = 0.999, and the glottis and lips reflect with 0.75 and −0.85. For fixed geometry the authors recurse q_i and t_i in the frequency domain from lips to glottis (43 steps along the tract, vectorized over all frequencies) to obtain the half-sample-clock response H_h, then combine it into the audio-rate response H(ω). Because 44 segments is even, the second term cancels and H(ω) reduces to H_h(ω/2)/4—every path from glottis to lips takes an even number of half steps. In double precision this matches the impulse response computed directly from the recurrent waveguide to a relative error of 6×10⁻¹⁰.

Frozen-block convolution. For moving geometry, two assumptions: geometry is constant within 256-sample blocks (5.8 ms at 44.1 kHz), using reflection coefficients interpolated at the block midpoint; and the impulse response is kept for its first 4096 samples (93 ms), obtained by an inverse FFT over 8192 frequencies. The output is the sum of linear convolutions of each block's source with that block's impulse response, computed with zero-padded FFTs and overlap-add. Gradients pass only through the FFTs and the 43-step recursion along the tract, so their path length does not depend on clip duration.

Approximation error. For fixed geometry the only error is truncation: in the recurrent model, at most 1.2×10⁻¹¹ of the energy of an output sample's sensitivity to the source lies more than 100 ms in the past, and single-precision waveforms differ by 8.1×10⁻⁶, round-off level. With moving geometry, a block's tail keeps the filter of the block that excited it, whereas in the waveguide the waves propagate through the current geometry; with diameters oscillating sinusoidally by 5% and 20% of their mean, 256-sample blocks give waveform errors of 1.04% and 4.00% (64-sample blocks give 0.79% and 2.95%, at twice the cost).

限制Limitations

絕對辨識率接近隨機。 評估用 AudioMNIST 中 12 位保留說話者的 600 段數字錄音；只在 36 位其他說話者真實語音上訓練的 wav2vec 2.0 分類器，對真實錄音的準確率為 86.7%，對原版 VocalTrax 重建是 10.0%（10 類，等於隨機），本文 14.5%，提升 4.5 個百分點（95% 區間 2.2–6.6）。作者明說這只反映相對而非實用的可懂度，而且三種合成條件的預測都集中在少數幾個數字。原論文報告的 20.0%（真實 73.5%），作者試了不同超參數仍無法重現。

在原本的目標上反而變差。 log-mel 誤差 0.278 對 0.250，高 11.3%（區間 10.1%–12.4%）；表 5 的頻譜形狀類描述子（log-STFT、MFCC、頻譜重心、對比）也是原版較接近參考。

加速有條件。 47–63 倍只是聲道部分；完整一步只快約 3 倍，因為沒改動的 LF 聲門音源用逐樣本迴圈算相位。在 CPU 上原版一秒片段每步只要 0.15 秒；在 GPU 大批次下原版每秒可做 514 次片段更新，本文只有 73 次，記憶體也較多（379 對 145 MiB）。其他：只有一個資料集、一個最佳化種子、沒有聽測；無聲段用 1 Hz 佔位，造成約 45% 的輸出能量落在 60 Hz 以下（真實錄音只有 3.8%）。對 MIR 讀者而言，沒有任何歌聲或音樂實驗。

Absolute recognition is near chance. Evaluation uses 600 digit recordings from 12 held-out AudioMNIST speakers; a wav2vec 2.0 classifier trained only on real speech from 36 other speakers scores 86.7% on the real recordings, 10.0% on original VocalTrax reconstructions (ten classes, i.e. chance), and 14.5% on this model's, a gain of 4.5 points (95% interval 2.2–6.6). The authors state that this reflects relative rather than practical intelligibility, and that predictions for all three synthetic conditions concentrate on a few digits. The original paper's reported 20.0% (73.5% on real) could not be reproduced despite trying different hyperparameters.

It is worse on the original objective. Log-mel error is 0.278 vs 0.250, 11.3% higher (interval 10.1%–12.4%); in Table 5 the spectral-shape descriptors (log-STFT, MFCC, centroid, contrast) are also closer to the reference for the original.

The speed-up is conditional. The 47–63× applies to the tract only; a full step is about 3× faster, because the unmodified LF glottal source computes its phase with a per-sample loop. On a CPU the original needs only 0.15 s per step for a one-second clip; on a GPU with large batches the original reaches 514 clip updates per second against 73 for this model, which also uses more memory (379 vs 145 MiB). Also: one dataset, one optimization seed, no listening test; unvoiced frames use a 1 Hz placeholder that puts about 45% of output energy below 60 Hz (3.8% in the recordings). For MIR readers, there is no singing or music experiment at all.

討論Discussion

為什麼 log-mel 較差、辨識率卻較高？ 作者的解釋有數據支持：本文擬合出的四個聲道控制，相鄰幀之間的變化只有原版的 0.46–0.52 倍（緊張度為 0.80 倍）；兩種渲染器的差異與反射係數變化速度相關（Spearman ρ = 0.45）。所以凍結區塊等於一個時間平滑先驗：不鼓勵快速抖動的軌跡，犧牲一些頻譜細節。逐數字看，「six」與「eight」的運動量掉最多（0.38、0.39 倍），損失差距也最大；全濁音的「one」「nine」幾乎沒差。這暗示原版可能靠抖動的發音軌跡壓低損失，卻沒有讓數字更好認。

參數可以搬回原版用。 把本文擬合的參數直接交給原版遞迴 VocalTrax 渲染，波形中位數只變 5.7%，辨識率仍有 13.8%，比原版高 3.8 點（區間 2.5–5.1），log-mel 升到 0.310。這表示快模型可以當擬合器、慢模型當渲染器。作者也指出梯度消失本來就不是原版的問題（聲道有損耗、記憶很短），好處在計算的成本與深度，而非讓損失面更平滑。

對音樂的意義。 這個「已知 LTI 結構→精確頻率響應→區塊卷積」的轉換，原則上適用於任何有損耗的數位波導，例如弦樂或管樂的物理模型、Cámara 等人的雙音歌唱模型。若作者在未來工作中把 LF 音源的相位改成每段預先計算，估計完整一步能快 17–25 倍；加上歌聲實驗，才會真正碰到 MIR 的問題。

Why is log-mel worse but recognition better? The authors' explanation is backed by data: between consecutive frames, the four tract controls fitted by this model change only 0.46–0.52 times as much as the original's (0.80 for tenseness), and the gap between the two renderers correlates with how fast reflection coefficients change (Spearman ρ = 0.45). The frozen blocks thus act as a temporal smoothness prior, discouraging jittery trajectories at the cost of some spectral detail. Per digit, "six" and "eight" lose the most motion (0.38 and 0.39 times) and have the largest loss gaps, while fully voiced "one" and "nine" show almost none. This suggests the original may lower its loss with jittery articulatory trajectories that do not make digits easier to recognize.

The parameters transfer back. Rendering this model's fitted parameters with the original recurrent VocalTrax changes the waveform by a median of 5.7%, keeps recognition at 13.8%, 3.8 points above the original (interval 2.5–5.1), and raises log-mel error to 0.310. The fast model can serve as the fitter and the slow one as the renderer. The authors also note that vanishing gradients were never a problem for the original (the tract is lossy with short memory); the benefit is in the cost and depth of the gradient computation, not a smoother loss.

What it means for music. The conversion "known LTI structure → exact frequency response → block convolution" applies in principle to any lossy digital waveguide, such as physical models of strings or winds, or Cámara et al.'s biphonic singing model. If, as planned in future work, the LF source phase is precomputed once per clip, the authors estimate a full step could be 17–25× faster; a singing experiment would be needed before it truly addresses an MIR problem.
