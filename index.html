Ghost in the Encoder: Decodable Artist Identity Representations in Lyrics-to-Song Generation

編碼器裡的幽靈:歌詞轉歌曲生成模型中可解碼的歌手身分表徵

Arhan Vohra, Choenden Kyirong, Laura Ibáñez-Martínez, Martín Rocamora
arXiv:2609.39552 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-30
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：只給歌詞、完全不給歌手名,ACE-Step 1.5 的歌詞編碼器仍能被線性探針辨識出 100 位歌手中的哪一位:準確率 0.344,約為詞袋基準 0.085 的 4 倍,而生成音訊上仍有 0.109(隨機為 0.01)。Why this one: Given only lyrics and no artist name, a linear probe on ACE-Step 1.5's lyric encoder identifies which of 100 artists wrote them: 0.344 accuracy, about 4× the bag-of-words baseline of 0.085, and the signal is still at 0.109 on generated audio (chance 0.01).

動機Motivation

文字轉歌曲模型可以被提示去模仿特定歌手,甚至整首吐出訓練資料裡的歌。過去對這件事的記錄全是行為層面:拿歌詞去提示,看輸出像不像,而且樣本都很小(少於 50 首)。模型內部到底有沒有把「歌手」這件事編碼進去、在哪一層編碼,沒有人看過。

這很重要,因為現行的防護多半擋在提示端(例如過濾歌手名)。如果歌詞本身就是一條帶有歌手資訊的條件通道,那麼不寫歌手名也擋不住模仿。作者因此想問:不給任何歌手標籤,模型能不能只從歌詞就在內部表徵出歌手身分?

Text-to-song models can be prompted to imitate particular artists, or even to regurgitate whole training songs. Prior documentation of this is purely behavioral: prompt with lyrics, listen to the result, on small samples (fewer than 50 songs). Nobody has examined whether, or where, the model internally encodes artist identity.

That matters because current safeguards mostly sit at the prompt side, for example by filtering artist names. If the lyrics themselves are a conditioning channel that carries artist information, omitting the name does not prevent imitation. The authors therefore ask whether, with no artist label supplied, a model internally represents artist identity from lyrics alone.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

行為層面的前作有三個。Roh 等人(Bob's confetti, arXiv 2507.17937)用整首歌的歌詞提示 YuE,並用「同音替換」(把 mom's spaghetti 換成 Bob's confetti)繞過 Suno 這類閉源系統的歌詞過濾,仍能引出整曲複製。Coelho 以公開中繼資料標籤組出類似歌手描述的提示,只有軼事證據。Nagarajan 與 Dong 用 LLM 產生描述詞,再以 CLAP 相似度在僅兩位歌手的 MusicGen 實驗上測試。三者都把生成當黑盒。

機制層面的前作則是探針與稀疏自編碼器:Wei 等人探測 MusicGen 是否編碼樂理概念,SMITIN(Koo 等人)用探針找出控制樂器出現的注意力頭,Singh 等人在 MusicGen 殘差流上訓練 SAE,TADA 把概念定位到擴散模型的特定層。這些找的都是類型、拍號這類音樂語意概念,本篇是第一個把同一套工具轉去找「歌手」的。技術上它仍是 Alain 與 Bengio(2017)的線性探針,新意在對象和跨整條條件化管線的量測,不在新方法。

Three behavioral precursors exist. Roh et al. (Bob's confetti, arXiv 2507.17937) prompted YuE with full lyrics and used homophonic substitution ("mom's spaghetti" to "Bob's confetti") to bypass lyric filters of closed systems such as Suno while still eliciting whole-song replication. Coelho assembled artist-like prompts from public metadata tags, with anecdotal evidence only. Nagarajan and Dong generated descriptors with an LLM and scored CLAP similarity on a two-artist MusicGen test. All three treat generation as a black box.

The mechanistic precursors are probes and sparse autoencoders: Wei et al. probed MusicGen for music-theory concepts, SMITIN (Koo et al.) used probes to find attention heads controlling instrument presence, Singh et al. trained SAEs on MusicGen's residual stream, and TADA localized concepts to layers of diffusion models. These look for genre or time signature; this paper is the first to aim the same tools at artists. Technically it is still the linear probe of Alain and Bengio (2017); the novelty lies in the target and in measuring across the whole conditioning pipeline, not in a new method.

方法Method

研究對象是開放權重的 ACE-Step 1.5(acestep-v15-turbo):一個 Qwen 語言模型產生思維鏈(CoT)中繼資料,再由擴散 Transformer(DiT)合成音訊;DiT 透過交叉注意力同時接收說明文字編碼器(Qwen3-0.6B)、音色編碼器與一個 8 層的歌詞編碼器。

資料是 Last.fm 上 5 個類型(pop、rock、hip-hop、R&B、country)各 20 位、共 100 位歌手,每位取 Genius 上最受歡迎的 20 首 2024 年前的英文歌,共 2,000 首;歌詞中的歌手名以同音替換匿名化。訓練語料不公開,所以只能靠「選熱門歌」提高它被訓練過的機率。

作者用 PyTorch hook 擷取歌詞編碼器各層、合併後條件序列、10 個 DiT 層的交叉注意力 K/V,以及 8 個去噪時間步的音訊潛變數;每個張量對非特徵軸取平均,訓練多類別邏輯迴歸,報告 5 折交叉驗證準確率與 1000 次置換檢定。輸出端則對生成音訊取 MuQ-MuLan 與 LAION-CLAP 嵌入再做同樣探測,並用版本比對模型 CLEWS 看生成歌是否像原曲的翻唱。基準是 Qwen3 詞嵌入與 TF-IDF 詞袋。

The subject is the open-weight ACE-Step 1.5 (acestep-v15-turbo): a Qwen language model writes chain-of-thought (CoT) metadata, and a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) synthesizes audio, receiving a caption encoder (Qwen3-0.6B), a timbre encoder and an 8-layer lyric encoder through cross-attention.

The data are 100 artists, 20 from each of five genres (pop, rock, hip-hop, R&B, country) on Last.fm, each with their 20 most popular pre-2024 English songs on Genius: 2,000 songs. Artist names inside lyrics are anonymized by phonetic replacement. Because the training corpus is undocumented, the authors can only choose popular songs to raise the odds that they were seen.

PyTorch hooks capture each lyric-encoder layer, the merged conditioning sequence, cross-attention K/V at 10 DiT layers, and audio latents at 8 denoising steps. Each tensor is averaged over non-feature axes and fed to a multiclass logistic regression, reporting 5-fold cross-validated accuracy and 1,000 permutation tests. On the output side, MuQ-MuLan and LAION-CLAP embeddings of generated audio get the same probe, and the version-matching model CLEWS checks whether generated songs resemble the original as covers. Baselines are Qwen3 token embeddings and TF-IDF bag-of-words.

限制Limitations

作者的結論相當節制,但有幾個缺口值得看清楚。第一,探針是相關性證據:論文自己說明沒有做因果介入(例如沿該方向轉向或抹除),所以「這條通道造成模仿」只是假說。第二,只研究一個模型,結論能否推到 YuE 或 Suno 一類自回歸或閉源系統沒有驗證。

第三,「歌手身分」被定義成歌詞所屬的歌手標籤,是功能性定義;編碼器吃進的可能只是這位歌手的用詞、主題與類型習慣。論文用類型內探測控制類型(hip-hop 0.585、country 0.338),但 hip-hop 的 TF-IDF 也高達 0.403,詞彙特徵本身就能解釋大部分訊號。第四,訓練集成員資格無法驗證,所以不能說這是記憶,還是泛化出的風格。

第五,到了音訊端訊號弱很多:MuQ-MuLan 在生成音訊上 0.109,在原曲上 0.449;而 CoT 那段的分析(合併點 0.085 可能只是平均池化稀釋)、CLEWS 的差異(R@400 為 0.449 對 0.333)和「歌曲辨識觸發器」的說法,作者自己標明是非正式聆聽加推測,留待未來。

The authors' conclusions are measured, but several gaps deserve naming. First, probes give correlational evidence: the paper says plainly that no causal intervention (steering or erasing along the direction) was run, so "this channel causes imitation" remains a hypothesis. Second, only one model is studied; whether this carries to autoregressive or closed systems such as YuE or Suno is untested.

Third, "artist identity" is defined functionally as the artist label of the lyrics, so the encoder may be picking up that artist's vocabulary, themes and genre habits. Within-genre probing controls genre (hip-hop 0.585, country 0.338), yet the hip-hop TF-IDF baseline reaches 0.403, so lexical features explain much of the signal there. Fourth, training-set membership cannot be verified, so this cannot be called memorization rather than generalized style.

Fifth, the audio-side signal is far weaker: 0.109 for MuQ-MuLan on generated audio versus 0.449 on the originals. The CoT analysis (the 0.085 at the merge point may only be mean-pooling dilution), the CLEWS gap (R@400 of 0.449 versus 0.333) and the "song-recognition trigger" idea are labelled by the authors themselves as informal listening plus conjecture for future work.

討論Discussion

這篇最紮實的部分是梯度式的證據鏈:歌詞編碼器輸出 0.344,比輸入端的 Qwen3 詞嵌入(0.153)高一倍多,比 TF-IDF(0.085)高 4 倍,峰值在第 5 層(0.361)。這說明歌手資訊至少有一部分是預訓練時在編碼器裡學到的,不只是輸入詞彙自帶。經過交叉注意力後降到 0.292(−15%),且各歌手的可解碼度與編碼器端高度相關(ρ≈0.90);加噪的初始潛變數正好是隨機的 0.01,進入 DiT 前幾層後明顯上升,最後幾層只剩 0.10–0.14。

最有意思的是 CoT 的分離現象:開啟 CoT 後,音訊的歌手探針略降(MuQ-MuLan 0.109 到 0.101,CLAP 0.043 到 0.042),但 CLEWS 的翻唱檢索卻更高(R@10 為 0.055 對 0.022,隨機是 0.005)。作者推測 LM 產生的說明可能是「歌曲辨識觸發器」,這是值得追的線索,但目前只靠非正式聆聽。

對 MIR 讀者的實用價值在於:可以用這套探針審計其他生成器,但現在還不能拿它當防護機制,因為因果與跨模型的驗證都還沒做。

The strongest part is the graded chain of evidence. The lyric-encoder output reaches 0.344, more than double the Qwen3 input embedding (0.153) and 4× TF-IDF (0.085), peaking at layer 5 (0.361). So at least part of the artist information is learned in the encoder during pretraining rather than carried by the input vocabulary. Cross-attention lowers it to 0.292 (−15%), and per-artist decodability is highly correlated with the encoder side (ρ≈0.90). The noisy initial latent sits at exactly the 0.01 chance level, rises in the first DiT layers, and falls to 0.10–0.14 in the last ones.

The most interesting result is the CoT dissociation. Turning CoT on slightly lowers the artist probe on audio (MuQ-MuLan 0.109 to 0.101, CLAP 0.043 to 0.042), yet raises CLEWS cover retrieval (R@10 of 0.055 versus 0.022, random 0.005). The authors speculate that the LM caption acts as a song-recognition trigger. That is a lead worth chasing, but it currently rests on informal listening.

For MIR readers the practical value is a probe recipe for auditing other generators. It cannot yet serve as a safeguard, because the causal and cross-model checks are still missing.

When the Label Ignores the Request: Auditing Policy-Selected Targets in Synthetic Conversational Music Recommendation

當標籤無視使用者的要求:稽核合成式對話音樂推薦中由策略選出的目標

Sanjeev Suresh
arXiv:2609.39696 · cs.IR · 投稿submitted 2026-09-30
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：在 RecSys Challenge 2026 的 TalkPlay 基準上,使用者明確點名一首歌的 82 個開發集回合中,有 41 個官方標籤指向另一首歌;只改 1.5% 的訓練回合,衝突切片的 nDCG@20 就從 0.523 升到 0.802(+53.3%),官方指標不降。Why this one: In the RecSys Challenge 2026 TalkPlay benchmark, the official label contradicts the user's named song in 41 of 82 audited development turns; changing just 1.5% of training turns lifts nDCG@20 on the conflict slice from 0.523 to 0.802 (+53.3%) without hurting the official metric.

動機Motivation

對話式音樂推薦很缺大規模資料,所以 RecSys Challenge 2026 用 TalkPlayData 2 管線合成:一個 LLM 扮聽眾,另一個扮推薦者,每個使用者回合之後被記錄下來的下一首歌,就成為該回合的官方標籤,系統要在整個曲庫中排名,以 nDCG@20 計分。

問題是這個標籤記錄的是「生成策略接著播了什麼」,不一定是使用者剛才要求的。多數回合(例如「適合深夜開車的憂鬱歌」)沒辦法分辨兩者,但有一類回合可以直接比對:使用者說出一首歌的歌名。作者主張這類需求在真實音樂搜尋中是主流,所以如果訓練目標教系統無視它,就是學到使用者最不能容忍的錯。

Conversational music recommendation lacks large-scale data, so RecSys Challenge 2026 uses the TalkPlayData 2 pipeline to synthesize it: one LLM plays the listener, another the recommender, and the track logged after each user turn becomes that turn's official label. Systems rank the whole catalog and are scored by nDCG@20.

The catch is that the label records what the generation policy played next, not necessarily what the user just asked for. Most turns ("something moody for late-night driving") cannot tell the two apart, but one family can be compared directly: the user names an exact song. The author argues this is the dominant intent in real music search, so a training target that teaches systems to ignore it teaches the failure users forgive least.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者沒有像 TalkPlay 自己的意圖分類那樣把「指名曲目」和類型、心情篩選一視同仁;該分類沒有「確切項目」這一類,本文把它獨立出來。與評測資料品質有關的前作:Northcutt 一脈的測試集標籤錯誤研究、MultiWOZ 2.1/2.2/2.4 對狀態標籤的逐版重標註,但這裡的標籤並沒有記錄錯,只是忠實記錄了策略的選擇,所以重標註不是解法,本文只「補」目標,不刪官方標籤。

最接近的是 Fashion-AlterEval(只在評估端擴充相關集)和 Xu 等人用 LLM 提議相關項目補訓練標籤。差別是它們依賴人工或模型的相關性判斷,本文依據的是曲庫 metadata 能解析出的具體曲目。另外,生成階段的一致性守衛(往返一致性過濾等)在生成器裡執行,本文則是出貨後只用參與者看得到的對話與 metadata 事後量測。方法上其實是 CheckList/RecList 式的行為測試加上標籤增補與匹配對照,不是新模型。

The paper does not follow the benchmark's own intent catalog, which treats a named track like any genre or mood filter and has no exact-item category; here that family is made first-class. On evaluation-data quality, the relevant precedents are test-set label-error audits and the successive MultiWOZ 2.1, 2.2 and 2.4 state re-annotations. But these labels do not misrecord anything; they faithfully log the policy's choice, so re-annotation is not the remedy, and the paper only supplements targets and never removes the official label.

The nearest neighbours are Fashion-AlterEval, which widens relevance sets at evaluation time only, and Xu et al., who add LLM-proposed relevant items to training labels. Those rely on human or model relevance judgments; this paper relies on a concrete track that catalog metadata resolves. Generation-time guards such as round-trip consistency filtering run inside the generator, while this is a post hoc measurement from participant-visible evidence. Methodologically it is CheckList/RecList-style behavioral testing plus label supplementation and a matched control, not a new model.

方法Method

稽核部分完全用可見資訊。偵測器是確定性的:用規則運算式從最新一則使用者訊息抽出被引號框住的歌名與「play…」「find…」這類祈使片段,再用曲庫的歌名與歌手欄位解析成具體曲目;找不到對應、同分或只是泛泛偏好時就棄權。作者用 GPT-5.5 在訓練集 120 回合的分層樣本上盲測:偵測器 40 個陽性中有 37 個是真正的點歌要求;漏報則是 80 個未標記回合中有 3 個。

介入方式是:偵測器解析出官方標籤沒滿足的目標時,該回合保留官方標籤、加上要求滿足的目標集,整回合權重降為 0.1。完整訓練集 121,592 個回合中有 1,873 個(約 1.5%)被改動,新增 1,878 個正例。基礎系統是多路召回(三路 BM25、三路稠密、四路偽相關回饋、一路微調雙編碼器,每回合約 1,550 個候選)加上 49 個特徵的 XGBoost LambdaRank 重排序器。

對照組是「匹配控制」:特徵多一個二元的「有明確要求」旗標,但只用官方標籤訓練,以排除專家模型只是學到「這回合有點歌要求」。兩者都只在偵測器觸發時才取代基礎分數,且所有設定在評估前凍結。

The audit uses only visible information. The detector is deterministic: regular expressions extract quoted titles and imperative spans ("play…", "find…") from the latest user message, and catalog title and artist fields resolve them to concrete tracks. It abstains on no match, on ties, or on broad preference language. The author validated it blind with GPT-5.5 on a stratified 120-turn training sample: 37 of 40 detector positives were genuine track requests, and the annotator found 3 misses among 80 unflagged turns.

The intervention: when the detector resolves a target the official label does not satisfy, the turn keeps its official label, adds the request-satisfying target set, and the whole turn's weight drops to 0.1. In the full training set, 1,873 of 121,592 turns (about 1.5%) are changed, adding 1,878 positive rows. The base system is multi-arm retrieval (three BM25 arms, three dense arms, four pseudo-relevance-feedback arms and one fine-tuned bi-encoder, about 1,550 candidates per turn) with a 49-feature XGBoost LambdaRank reranker.

The matched control has the same features plus a binary exact-request flag but trains on official labels only, ruling out that the specialist merely learned that a turn contains a request. Both replace base scores only when the detector fires, and all settings were frozen before evaluation.

限制Limitations

最大的限制是規模:偵測器在 8,000 個評估回合中只找到 82 個指名曲目的回合(1.0%),而結論依賴只有 43 個案例的衝突切片(來自 29 個 session),且偵測器是 TalkPlay 專用的規則運算式。作者自己也強調 41/82 描述的是被稽核的回合,不是整個基準。

第二,主要讀數有部分循環:切片和訓練補充用的是同一個偵測器解析出的目標,所以專家模型在該切片上變好,有一部分是「學會偵測器認定的答案」。匹配控制排除了「只是看到旗標」,但不能排除這一點。論文自己也承認覆寫策略的切片分數「按構造」接近 1,所以選擇訓練目標而不是覆寫。

第三,整個評估只在開發集上做,補充訓練並未放進最終排行榜提交(綜合分 0.528、nDCG@20 為 0.440,第 12 名/44 隊),所以沒有隱藏測試集的證據。第四,依賴 LLM 標註驗證偵測器,樣本只有 120 回合。第五,作者也說標籤衝突不一定是錯誤:生成器的 16–32 首候選池與「不可重複」限制可能強迫它選替代曲目。

The largest limit is scale: the detector finds only 82 named-track turns among 8,000 evaluated turns (1.0%), the headline conclusion rests on a 43-case conflict slice from 29 sessions, and the detector is a TalkPlay-specific regex system. The author stresses that 41/82 describes the audited turns, not the benchmark at large.

Second, the main readout is partly circular: the slice and the training supplement use targets resolved by the same detector, so some of the gain is learning the detector's notion of the answer. The matched control rules out merely seeing the flag but not this. The paper itself notes that an override's slice score is near 1 by construction, which is why it studies training targets instead.

Third, everything is evaluated on the development set only, and the supplement was not part of the leaderboard submission (composite 0.528, nDCG@20 of 0.440, rank 12 of 44), so there is no hidden-test evidence. Fourth, detector validation rests on an LLM annotator over just 120 turns. Fifth, the author concedes that the conflicts are not necessarily errors: the generator's 16–32 track pool and no-repeat rule can force a substitute.

討論Discussion

論文最好的地方是把主張寫得很窄,並且每一步都有數字。官方 nDCG@20 為 0.1908 對 0.1914(+0.31%),95% 信賴區間 [+0.0002, +0.0012],落在預先宣告的保留邊界(不低於 −0.001)內;「滿足要求」的整體 nDCG@20 為 0.1922 對 0.1943(+1.09%);衝突切片從 0.523 到 0.802(+53.3%),session 層級 bootstrap 差值 +0.277,CI [+0.163, +0.400],10,000 次重抽全為正。逐項來看,82 個指名回合中專家把要求的曲目排第一的有 66 個,控制組是 46 個;在標籤衝突的那一半,完全不在前 20 的從 18 個降到 1 個。

作者還誠實列出被拒絕的五類要求:硬性歌手限制偵測精準度只有 74%;「換別的」類回合抑制被拒曲目反而讓官方指標掉 0.0009;專輯與年份的 metadata 不可靠;泛語意要求(125 個樣本)沒有一個能在曲庫中驗證目標。這個「證據門檻」比結果本身更可移植。

對評測設計的啟示是:整體平均會藏住小而重要的行為切片,合成基準應該並列報告這類切片。

The paper's best quality is a deliberately narrow claim with numbers at every step. Official nDCG@20 moves from 0.1908 to 0.1914 (+0.31%), 95% CI [+0.0002, +0.0012], inside the predeclared preservation margin (no worse than −0.001). Request-satisfying nDCG@20 over the whole split goes from 0.1922 to 0.1943 (+1.09%). The conflict slice goes from 0.523 to 0.802 (+53.3%), with a session-level bootstrap delta of +0.277, CI [+0.163, +0.400], positive in all 10,000 resamples. Of the 82 named-track turns, the specialist ranks the requested track first on 66 versus 46 for the control, and among label-conflict turns the number absent from the top 20 falls from 18 to 1.

The author also honestly lists five request families that were declined: hard artist constraints (74% detector precision), rejection turns (suppressing the rejected track cost 0.0009 of the official metric), album and year constraints (unreliable metadata), and broad semantic requests (none of 125 screened produced a verifiable target set). That evidence bar is more portable than the result itself.

The lesson for benchmark design is that whole-split averages hide small but important behaviors, so synthetic benchmarks should report such slices alongside the official metric.

Don't CLAP: Are Music-Text Models Bag-of-Words?

別 CLAP:音樂—文字模型是詞袋嗎?

Yuan-Chiao Cheng, Alexander Lerch
arXiv:2609.30540 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-24
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把說明文字中兩種樂器的屬性對調(同樣的字、不同的意思)之後,四個對比式音樂—文字模型的平均準確率只有 0.431–0.519(隨機為 0.5);CLAP 分數看不出「失真吉他配乾淨鋼琴」與「乾淨吉他配失真鋼琴」的差別。Why this one: When one property is swapped between two instruments in a caption (same words, different meaning), four contrastive music-text models score a mean accuracy of only 0.431–0.519 (chance 0.5): the CLAP score cannot tell "distorted guitar over clean piano" from "clean guitar over distorted piano".

動機Motivation

文字生成音樂的系統通常以兩件事評估:音質與提示忠實度。音質有 FAD、KAD 這類代理指標;提示忠實度除了聽測,最常見的客觀指標就是 CLAP 分數,也就是對比式音樂—文字模型的音訊嵌入與文字嵌入的餘弦相似度。

問題是這個分數到底有沒有讀懂文字。當屬性綁在特定樂器上(例如「失真的吉他」),文字嵌入有沒有保留這個綁定?如果沒有,用 CLAP 分數宣稱模型忠實於提示,就是在量一個對屬性綁定不敏感的東西。而大型音訊語言模型(LALM)雖然能寫流暢的描述,但被認為多半靠文字先驗回答音樂問題,所以 CLAP 分數仍是主流。

Text-to-music systems are usually judged on audio quality and prompt faithfulness. Quality has proxies such as FAD and KAD. For faithfulness, besides listening studies, the most common objective measure is the CLAP score: the cosine similarity between a contrastive music-text model's audio and text embeddings.

The question is whether that score actually reads the text. When an attribute is bound to a particular instrument (a distorted guitar), does the text embedding keep the binding? If not, claiming prompt faithfulness from CLAP scores means measuring something insensitive to attribute binding. Large audio-language models (LALMs) can write fluent descriptions but are thought to answer music questions largely from text priors, which is one reason the CLAP score remains the default.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文的出發點是視覺領域已知的事:Yuksekgonul 等人(ICLR 2023,ARO)證明 CLIP 像詞袋,認得圖說裡提到的概念卻忽略屬性、關係與順序;Winoground 是同類的組合性測試。Koishigarina 等人(ICLR 2026)則指出 CLIP 的單模態文字與影像嵌入各自其實還保有綁定資訊,是跨模態比較才把它弄丟。音訊領域有 CompA(Ghosh 等人),對只在順序或屬性上不同的環境音事件配對,對比式音訊—語言模型僅略高於隨機;Zang 等人(ISMIR 2025)顯示 LALM 沒有音訊也能在音樂問答基準上拿高分。

本篇的差別是兩點:對象是音樂,而且擾動限定為「恰好交換一個屬性」,因為單純洗牌字詞常得到樂理上等價的句子(「lo-fi C minor beat」與「C minor lo-fi beat」)。它的盲測基準也沿用 SugarCrepe 的「只看文字」基線想法。這是把 ARO 式檢驗搬到音樂,而不是新的概念。

The starting point is a known result in vision: Yuksekgonul et al. (ICLR 2023, ARO) showed CLIP behaves like a bag-of-words, matching the concepts a caption names but ignoring attributes, relations and order, and Winoground is a sibling compositionality test. Koishigarina et al. (ICLR 2026) found that CLIP's unimodal text and image embeddings each still keep the binding, and it is lost in the cross-modal comparison. In audio, CompA (Ghosh et al.) found contrastive audio-language models barely above chance on environmental-sound pairs differing only in order or attributes, and Zang et al. (ISMIR 2025) showed LALMs score well on music QA even without audio.

This paper differs in two ways: the domain is music, and the perturbation exchanges exactly one property, because plain word shuffling often yields musically equivalent text ("lo-fi C minor beat" versus "C minor lo-fi beat"). Its blind baseline follows SugarCrepe's text-only idea. In short it carries an ARO-style test to music rather than introducing a new concept.

方法Method

作者建立 MASB(Music Attribute-Swap Benchmark):400 段 10 秒的創用 CC 錄音,取自 Song Describer Dataset 與 MTG-Jamendo,由單一標註者剪輯與標註,每段只標一種屬性——timbre 140 段、領奏與伴奏(lead/accompaniment)123 段、首次出場順序(onset order)137 段。每段有嚴格模板和自然改寫兩種說明格式,共 800 組說明配對。擾動是把模板中兩件樂器的位置對調,所以原句 c+ 與擾動句 c− 的字完全相同。

受測者是四個對比式模型(LAION-CLAP、MS-CLAP 2023、MuQ-MuLan、CLaMP 3)與一個 LALM(Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct)。對比式模型在音訊對 c+ 的餘弦相似度高於對 c− 時算對,隨機為 0.5;LALM 被問「這段描述符合音訊嗎」,比較 Yes 的機率。

四個實驗:Exp.1 是上述交換準確率;Exp.2 讓 LALM 不看音訊、只在兩句中選一句,測文字先驗,並用 Cohen's κ 與「先驗平衡準確率」把先驗和音訊證據分開;Exp.3 只比較文字嵌入,看交換句、同義改寫句與其他錄音的說明之間的距離;Exp.4 用 MoisesDB 分軌做出 300 組對稱混音,只差哪件樂器先進場,使文字先驗被抵消。

The authors build MASB (Music Attribute-Swap Benchmark): 400 ten-second Creative Commons excerpts from the Song Describer Dataset and MTG-Jamendo, cut and annotated by a single annotator, each with one property: timbre (140 clips), lead versus accompaniment (123) or order of first appearance (137). Each clip has a strict-template caption and a natural rewrite, giving 800 caption pairs. The perturbation exchanges the two instruments' slots in the template, so the original c+ and perturbed c− contain exactly the same words.

The subjects are four contrastive models (LAION-CLAP, MS-CLAP 2023, MuQ-MuLan, CLaMP 3) and one LALM (Qwen2-Audio-7B-Instruct). A contrastive model is correct when the audio is closer to c+ than to c− (chance 0.5); the LALM is asked whether a description matches the audio and its Yes probabilities are compared.

Four experiments follow. Exp. 1 is the swap accuracy above. Exp. 2 has the LALM choose between the two captions without audio to measure the text prior, using Cohen's κ and prior-balanced accuracy to separate prior from audio evidence. Exp. 3 uses text embeddings only, comparing the swapped caption, a synonym paraphrase and other clips' captions. Exp. 4 builds 300 symmetric MoisesDB mixes that differ only in which instrument enters first, cancelling the prior.

限制Limitations

資料規模與設計都偏小:400 段錄音、單一標註者、兩件樂器、10 秒片段、模板式說明,只涵蓋三種屬性,沒有真正的長句、複雜編曲或多樂器場景。LALM 只測了一個(Qwen2-Audio),所以「LALM 靠文字先驗」不能推到所有 LALM。

第二,「詞袋」這個結論比證據強。Exp.1 和 Exp.4 發現近似隨機,但這也可能是音訊編碼器根本沒聽出屬性(Exp.4 顯示沒有任何模型讀得出哪件樂器先進場),不一定是文字編碼器的問題。能直接支持文字端失敗的只有 Exp.3(交換句中位距離 0.007–0.030,小於同義改寫的 0.062–0.122),而餘弦距離很小並不等於資訊真的不見了,Koishigarina 等人就發現綁定資訊可能仍在嵌入裡、只是比較方式讀不出來。

第三,作者自己說明對比式訓練為何會忽略詞序的兩個解釋(目標不需要解析細節;訓練資料多是標籤與短語)都沒有被測試。第四,Exp.4 的 Qwen2-Audio 先驗平衡優勢無法延續到 MASB-Order,作者歸因於分佈差異,但同樣沒有驗證。

Scale and design are small: 400 clips, one annotator, two instruments, 10-second excerpts and template captions covering only three properties, with no long sentences, complex arrangements or many-instrument scenes. Only one LALM (Qwen2-Audio) is tested, so "LALMs lean on text priors" cannot be generalized to all of them.

Second, the "bag-of-words" conclusion is stronger than the evidence. Near-chance results in Exp. 1 and Exp. 4 could also mean the audio encoder never hears the attribute (Exp. 4 shows no model can read which instrument enters first), not that the text encoder is at fault. The only direct support for a text-side failure is Exp. 3 (median swap distance of 0.007–0.030, below the paraphrase's 0.062–0.122), and a small cosine distance does not prove the information is gone: Koishigarina et al. found binding information may remain in the embedding but be unreadable by the comparison.

Third, the authors' two explanations for why contrastive training ignores word order (the objective rarely needs detail parsing, and training data are mostly tags and short phrases) are explicitly untested. Fourth, Qwen2-Audio's prior-balanced onset margin in Exp. 2 does not carry over to MASB-Order; the authors blame distribution shift but do not verify it.

討論Discussion

論文的數字撐得住主要論點。Exp.1 中四個對比式模型沒有一個在三種屬性上都高於隨機,平均 0.431–0.519;兩個通過 Holm 校正的結果是領奏角色上的 MuQ-MuLan(0.642)與 MS-CLAP(0.630),但同樣的模型在其他屬性仍近隨機,CLaMP 3 在領奏上甚至只有 0.337、MuQ-MuLan 在出場順序上 0.383,低於隨機。Qwen2-Audio 平均 0.681(timbre 0.639、lead 0.720、onset 0.690)好得多,但不看音訊時已有 0.644:timbre 0.682、lead 0.679、onset 0.573,加上音訊只把整體拉到 0.681,timbre 反而從 0.682 降到 0.639。

先驗平衡後三種屬性是 0.63–0.65;對稱音訊集 Exp.4 上五個模型全落在 0.497–0.538。所以較公允的讀法是:CLAP 分數不能當成對屬性綁定忠實的證據,但「音訊編碼器聽不到」與「文字編碼器沒編進去」尚未被拆開。

實務上,評測文字轉音樂時,應該搭配聽測或使用扣掉文字先驗的對照,而不是只報 CLAP 分數。

The numbers support the main point. In Exp. 1 no contrastive model beats chance on all three properties, with mean accuracy of 0.431–0.519. The two results surviving Holm correction are MuQ-MuLan (0.642) and MS-CLAP (0.630) on lead role, yet the same models are near chance elsewhere, and CLaMP 3 scores only 0.337 on lead role and MuQ-MuLan 0.383 on onset, below chance. Qwen2-Audio is much better at a mean of 0.681 (timbre 0.639, lead 0.720, onset 0.690), but without audio it already reaches 0.644 overall (timbre 0.682, lead 0.679, onset 0.573); adding audio lifts it only to 0.681, and on timbre lowers it from 0.682 to 0.639.

Prior-balanced accuracy is 0.63–0.65 across the three properties, and on the symmetric audio set of Exp. 4 all five models fall within 0.497–0.538. A fair reading is that the CLAP score cannot be taken as evidence of attribute-faithful prompts, but "the audio encoder cannot hear it" and "the text encoder did not encode it" have not yet been separated.

Practically, evaluating text-to-music should pair listening tests or prior-controlled baselines with the CLAP score rather than reporting it alone.
