Interpretable Singing Emotion Recognition Using Acoustic and Vocal Technique Features

以聲學與歌唱技巧特徵實現可解釋的歌唱情緒辨識

Fotini Kiriakopoulou, Vassilis Lyberatos, Spyridon Kantarelis, Athanasios Voulodimos, Giorgos Stamou
arXiv:2610.32629 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-23
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇用一組小而可讀的歌唱專用特徵（韻律、頻譜、音素時長，加上由 CNN 估出的六種歌唱技巧機率）做歌唱情緒辨識，再用 SHAP 解釋。在 RAVDESS 唱歌子集上，歌唱最佳化特徵集的 UAR 是 79.6%，只從 eGeMAPS 選出的語音最佳化特徵集是 76.2%，兩者信賴區間重疊；比較站得住的是跨歌手標準差比值 0.68（95% CI 0.45–0.93）。值得記住的是一個誠實的負面數字：拿掉技巧特徵，UAR 只從 79.6% 掉到 79.4%，所以技巧特徵的價值在解釋，不在準確率。Why this one: This paper recognizes emotion in singing with a small, readable set of singing-specific features (prosody, spectrum, phoneme durations, plus CNN-estimated probabilities for six vocal techniques) and explains the classifier with SHAP. On the RAVDESS singing subset the singing-optimized set reaches 79.6% UAR against 76.2% for a speech-optimized set chosen from eGeMAPS alone, with overlapping confidence intervals; the firmer result is a cross-singer SD ratio of 0.68 (95% CI 0.45–0.93). The number worth remembering is an honest negative one: removing the technique features moves UAR only from 79.6% to 79.4%, so their value lies in explanation, not accuracy.

動機Motivation

歌唱情緒辨識（singing emotion recognition, SiER）近年多半用深度模型直接從音訊學表徵，準確率不錯，但說不出是哪些聲音特性讓模型判成「悲傷」。對音樂推薦、演唱分析這類應用來說，知道依據往往跟知道答案一樣重要。

作者的出發點是：唱歌不是拉長的說話。唱歌的音域更寬、諧波更持續、共鳴更有特色，而情緒表達又跟歌唱技巧綁在一起，例如抖音（vibrato）、咽音（pharyngeal）與氣聲（breathy）。通用的語音情緒特徵沒有這些維度，所以作者想問：把歌唱專用的描述子明確放進來，能不能得到一個既準又能解釋的模型？

Singing emotion recognition (SiER) now mostly relies on deep models that learn representations straight from audio. They score well but cannot say which vocal properties made the model answer "sad". For music recommendation or performance analysis, knowing the basis of a prediction often matters as much as the prediction.

The authors start from the premise that singing is not stretched speech. It has a wider pitch range, more sustained harmonics and distinctive resonance, and emotional expression is tied to vocal technique such as vibrato, pharyngeal resonance and breathiness. Generic speech-emotion features lack these dimensions, so the question is whether explicitly adding singing-specific descriptors yields a model that is both accurate and explainable.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

這篇最直接的前身是 Eyben 等人（EURASIP 2015）：他們在受控的歌劇錄音上抽 openSMILE 特徵（響度、頻譜通量、alpha ratio、Hammarberg index 等），排序重要性，再用 SVM 在留一歌手（leave-one-singer-out, LOSO）協定下評估。這篇的評估協定與「特徵排序 + 淺層分類器」的骨架幾乎一樣。另一個前例是 Heng（OSF 2026），在 RAVDESS 唱歌樣本上用 XGBoost 加 SHAP 找頻譜特徵貢獻，這也正是本篇在 RAVDESS 上的分類器與解釋工具組合。

所以真正新的只有一件事：把歌唱技巧偵測器的逐幀輸出（六種技巧的平均、標準差、最大值、峰值因子與技巧熵）當成情緒特徵。那個偵測器本身來自 PrimaDNN（Yamamoto 等人，EUSIPCO 2023）的雙輸入（Mel 頻譜 + 音高曲線）設計。音樂情緒辨識這邊，Chowdhury 等人（ISMIR 2019）走 mid-level 感知特徵的可解釋路線，跟本篇是同一種思路，只是對象是整首音樂而非人聲。

The most direct ancestor is Eyben et al. (EURASIP 2015), who extracted openSMILE features (loudness, spectral flux, alpha ratio, Hammarberg index and others) from controlled opera recordings, ranked them, and evaluated an SVM under a leave-one-singer-out (LOSO) protocol. This paper keeps almost the same skeleton of feature ranking, a shallow classifier and LOSO. A second precedent is Heng (OSF 2026), which used XGBoost with SHAP on RAVDESS singing to find spectral feature contributions, which is exactly this paper's classifier and explainer pairing on RAVDESS.

So the one genuinely new ingredient is using the frame-level output of a vocal-technique detector (mean, SD, maximum and peak factor for six techniques, plus a technique entropy) as emotion features. The detector itself follows the dual-input (mel spectrogram plus pitch contour) design of PrimaDNN (Yamamoto et al., EUSIPCO 2023). On the music side, Chowdhury et al. (ISMIR 2019) took the same interpretable route with mid-level perceptual features, but for whole pieces rather than the voice.

方法Method

特徵抽取：音訊重取樣到 22.05 kHz，抽 130 個純量特徵，分四類。韻律類用 eGeMAPS、librosa 與 CREPE 算 f0 統計、音高變化率、jitter、shimmer；頻譜類有 alpha ratio、共振峰、頻譜通量；音素類用 Montreal Forced Aligner 對齊後算音素與停頓時長、構音速率；技巧類則來自一個雙輸入 CNN 對 vibrato、glissando、mix、pharyngeal、falsetto、breathy 六種技巧的逐幀機率。所有特徵都按歌手做 z-score 正規化。

技巧 CNN 在 GTSinger 的 11 萬個 1.5 秒片段上訓練，用 focal loss（α=0.25、γ=2）處理類別不平衡，留下義大利語子集測未見過的語言。

特徵選擇用巢狀 LOSO：內層以樹模型算重要性，統計每個特徵在各內層切分中進入前 N 名的次數（各類別內 N=10、全集 N=40），留下跨歌手穩定的那些，再以 Pearson 相關 0.9 為門檻去掉冗餘。最後 RAVDESS 用 XGBoost、GTSinger 用隨機森林；GTSinger 的片段預測以多數決合成整段演唱的預測。評估指標是 UAR 與 Macro-F1，信賴區間用歌手層級 bootstrap（10,000 次）。解釋層面，SHAP 看特徵貢獻，Grad-CAM 檢查技巧 CNN 是否看在合理的時頻區域。

Feature extraction: audio is resampled to 22.05 kHz and 130 scalar features are computed in four groups. Prosody uses eGeMAPS, librosa and CREPE for f0 statistics, pitch-change rate, jitter and shimmer; spectral features include alpha ratio, formants and spectral flux; phonetic features come from Montreal Forced Aligner boundaries (phoneme and pause durations, articulation rate); technique features are frame-level probabilities from a dual-input CNN for vibrato, glissando, mix, pharyngeal, falsetto and breathy. All features are z-scored per singer.

The technique CNN is trained on 110,000 1.5 s segments of GTSinger with focal loss (α=0.25, γ=2) for class imbalance, holding out the Italian subset to test an unseen language.

Feature selection is nested LOSO: inner-loop tree models compute importances, a counter tracks how often each feature enters the top N across inner splits (N=10 within each group, N=40 over the full set), the stable ones are kept, and features with Pearson correlation above 0.9 are pruned. The final classifiers are XGBoost on RAVDESS and a random forest on GTSinger, where clip predictions are merged by majority vote into one prediction per performance. Metrics are UAR and Macro-F1 with singer-level bootstrap intervals (10,000 iterations). For explanation, SHAP gives feature contributions and Grad-CAM checks that the technique CNN attends to plausible time-frequency regions.

限制Limitations

準確率的提升比摘要給人的印象小。RAVDESS 上 79.6% 對 76.2% 的 95% CI 是 76.9–82.2% 對 72.2–80.0%，彼此重疊；GTSinger 上是 61.0% 對 60.2%，而那是快樂／悲傷二分類，只有 7 位歌手。另外，歌唱最佳化集是從包含 eGeMAPS 在內的 130 維全集裡挑的，語音最佳化集只從 eGeMAPS 挑，候選池大小不同，本身就可能解釋一部分差距。

VocalSet 上的分析有循環的味道。歌唱最佳化模型的輸入本來就有技巧機率（RAVDESS 集裡有 breathy、falsetto/glissando 與技巧熵），所以它的預測跟真實技巧標籤的互資訊較高（0.474 對 0.352），幾乎是設計上的必然，不能當成「特徵抓到真實技巧資訊」的獨立證據。技巧 CNN 本身在未見語言上的 Macro-F1 只有 0.42，作者也承認會帶入標籤雜訊。

還有一個協定上的疑慮：按歌手做 z-score 需要測試歌手自己的多段錄音來算平均與標準差，而 RAVDESS 每位演員的錄音涵蓋所有情緒，正規化等於拿到一個情緒平衡的參考點。這對兩個特徵集一視同仁，不影響兩者的比較，但會讓絕對數字比單段、未知歌手的真實情境樂觀。RAVDESS 是演員演出的情緒、GTSinger 只有兩類，這兩點作者在限制裡有寫。

The accuracy gain is smaller than the abstract suggests. On RAVDESS, the 95% CIs for 79.6% versus 76.2% are 76.9–82.2% and 72.2–80.0%, which overlap; on GTSinger it is 61.0% versus 60.2%, for a binary happy/sad task with only seven singers. Moreover, the singing-optimized set is selected from the full 130-feature pool that already contains eGeMAPS, while the speech-optimized set is selected from eGeMAPS alone, so the difference in candidate pool could explain part of the gap.

The VocalSet analysis is partly circular. The singing-optimized model takes technique probabilities as input (its RAVDESS set includes breathy, falsetto/glissando and technique entropy), so higher mutual information between its predictions and true technique labels (0.474 vs 0.352) is close to guaranteed by design and is not independent evidence that the features capture real technique information. The technique CNN itself reaches only 0.42 Macro-F1 on the unseen language, and the authors acknowledge the label noise this introduces.

There is also a protocol concern: per-singer z-scoring needs several recordings of the test singer to compute a mean and SD, and since each RAVDESS actor covers all emotions, normalization effectively provides an emotion-balanced reference point. This applies equally to both feature sets and does not bias their comparison, but it makes the absolute numbers more optimistic than a single-clip, unknown-singer setting. The acted emotions of RAVDESS and the two-class labels of GTSinger are limitations the authors do state.

討論Discussion

消融表比主表更有資訊。只用韻律特徵就有 66.4% UAR，拿掉韻律掉到 62.5%，是最大的一塊；只用技巧特徵是 35.0%（六類的機率水準約 16.7%），拿掉技巧只從 79.6% 掉到 79.4%，標準差從 7.0 降到 6.5。換句話說，技巧特徵帶有情緒資訊，但大多和韻律重疊，它們的貢獻在讓 SHAP 的解釋能用歌手聽得懂的詞彙說出來。

最有意思的發現是交互作用：SHAP 相依圖顯示抖音變異度的作用取決於構音速率，慢速時偏向「悲傷」，快速時偏向「快樂」。這跟 Dromey 等人（2015）抖音參數隨情緒變化的觀察一致，又多了一層條件。風格穩定性也有數字：語音最佳化集在美聲與流行之間差 63.0% 對 58.2%，最難的流行歌手掉到 52.6%；歌唱最佳化集是 60.9% 對 61.2%，只是兩組各只有 3 和 4 位歌手。

The ablation table is more informative than the headline table. Prosody alone reaches 66.4% UAR and removing it drops the full set to 62.5%, the largest single contribution; techniques alone reach 35.0% (chance for six classes is about 16.7%), and removing them moves UAR only from 79.6% to 79.4% while SD falls from 7.0 to 6.5. In other words, technique features carry emotion information but mostly overlap with prosody; their contribution is letting SHAP explanations speak in a vocabulary singers understand.

The most interesting finding is an interaction: the SHAP dependence plot shows that vibrato variability pushes toward "sad" at slow articulation rates and toward "happy" at fast ones. This agrees with Dromey et al. (2015) on vibrato changing with emotion and adds a condition to it. Style stability also has numbers: the speech-optimized set scores 63.0% on bel canto versus 58.2% on pop, falling to 52.6% for the hardest pop singer, while the singing-optimized set holds at 60.9% versus 61.2%, though the groups have only three and four singers.

MuseFlow-TSE: Music-Aware Target Speech Extraction via Cross-Source Band-Splitting and Data-to-Data Flow Matching

MuseFlow-TSE：以跨音源頻帶切分與資料到資料流匹配實現的音樂感知目標語者擷取

Lu Ma, Yongliang Wang, Zhewen Su, Xin Tian
arXiv:2610.29119 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-21
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：目標語者擷取（target speech extraction, TSE）幾乎都在沒有音樂的混音上驗證，這篇專門處理有背景音樂與歌聲的場景：第一段用 BS-RoFormer 式頻帶切分同時估出目標、干擾語者、音樂與雜訊，第二段從第一段的估計出發做 1–4 步流匹配修補，並把估出的干擾當負向條件。在自建的合成 Complex 場景上，只用第一段就有 SI-SDRi 13.0 dB、WER 12.0%、音樂殘留 2.9%（TF-GridNet 11.9 dB、7.2%）；加上第二段 SI-SDRi 只多 0.2 dB，PESQ 從 2.71 升到 2.88。值得記住的是：大部分好處來自明確建模音樂的判別式第一段，生成式修補是小幅的品質換延遲。Why this one: Target speech extraction (TSE) is almost always validated on music-free mixtures; this paper targets scenes with background music and singing. Stage one uses BS-RoFormer-style band-splitting to jointly estimate target, interfering speakers, music and noise; stage two runs 1–4 flow-matching steps starting from that estimate, conditioned negatively on the estimated interference. On the authors' synthetic Complex scenario, stage one alone gives 13.0 dB SI-SDRi, 12.0% WER and 2.9% music leakage (TF-GridNet: 11.9 dB, 7.2%); adding stage two adds only 0.2 dB SI-SDRi while PESQ rises from 2.71 to 2.88. The point worth remembering is that most of the gain comes from the discriminative stage that models music explicitly, and the generative refiner is a small quality-for-latency trade.

動機Motivation

TSE 給一段目標語者的註冊語音（enrollment），從混音裡把那個人的聲音抽出來。真實場景常有背景音樂、歌聲、雜訊與殘響，但既有 TSE 幾乎只在無音樂的混音上驗證，零樣本遇到音樂時明顯退化。音樂是寬頻、強諧波、跟人聲頻帶高度重疊的干擾，比雜訊難得多。

作者把兩條路線的缺點並列：判別式方法（遮罩）忠於內容但只能刪、不能補；生成式方法能補細節，但從高斯雜訊出發既慢又可能幻聽或讓語者身分漂移。音樂又多了兩個麻煩：重疊多，需要補的東西多；被刪掉的伴奏或歌聲可能被生成模型「補」回來。這篇想用「判別式負責刪、生成式負責補」的分工來同時解決。

TSE takes an enrollment utterance of the target speaker and extracts that voice from a mixture. Real scenes often contain background music, singing, noise and reverberation, yet existing TSE is validated almost only on music-free mixtures and degrades clearly in zero-shot music tests. Music is wideband, strongly harmonic and overlaps the voice band far more than noise does.

The authors contrast the two families: discriminative masking is faithful to content but can only delete, not restore; generative methods restore detail but, starting from Gaussian noise, are slow and may hallucinate or drift in speaker identity. Music adds two problems: heavy overlap means more must be restored, and deleted accompaniment or singing may be generated back in. The paper aims to solve both with a split of "discriminative deletion, generative insertion".

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

「先判別式估計、再生成式修補」本身不是新點子。語音增強裡 StoRM（Lemercier 等人，IEEE/ACM TASLP 2023）就是先用預測模型給初估，再從初估出發跑擴散；TSE 裡最直接的前身是 Mask2Flow-TSE（Moon 等人，2026），同樣先遮罩、再從遮罩結果做流匹配，並提出了刪除／插入（D/I）分析，本篇的 D/I 比例與「從遮罩起點把 NFE 從 16 降到 1」都沿用它。SoloSpeech（Wang 等人，2025）則是串接式生成管線，但要 50 步乘 8 個候選。

第一段的骨架來自音樂分離：BS-RoFormer（Lu 等人，ICASSP 2024）的非均勻頻帶切分加 RoPE Transformer，再加上 SepReformer（Shin 等人，NeurIPS 2024）的早分支與跨音源互動。各分支相加等於混音的守恆約束，就是 Wisdom 等人（ICASSP 2019）的混音一致性（mixture consistency）。所以這篇最準確的描述是：Mask2Flow-TSE 的兩段式，把第一段換成會明確估音樂的 BS-RoFormer 多分支分離器，再加上把干擾估計餵給第二段的負向條件。真正新的是後者與 MusicLeak 損失。

"Discriminative estimate first, generative refinement second" is not new. In speech enhancement, StoRM (Lemercier et al., IEEE/ACM TASLP 2023) runs diffusion starting from a predictive model's initial estimate. In TSE the closest ancestor is Mask2Flow-TSE (Moon et al., 2026), which also masks first and then runs flow matching from the masked result, and which introduced the deletion/insertion (D/I) analysis; this paper reuses both its D/I ratios and the finding that a masked start cuts NFE from 16 to about 1. SoloSpeech (Wang et al., 2025) is a cascaded generative pipeline but needs 50 steps times 8 candidates.

Stage one borrows from music separation: the non-uniform band-split RoPE Transformer of BS-RoFormer (Lu et al., ICASSP 2024), plus the early split and cross-source interaction of SepReformer (Shin et al., NeurIPS 2024). Requiring the stems to sum to the mixture is the mixture consistency of Wisdom et al. (ICASSP 2019). So the most accurate description is Mask2Flow-TSE's two-stage scheme with stage one replaced by a BS-RoFormer multi-stem separator that explicitly estimates music, plus a negative condition feeding the interference estimates into stage two. The genuinely new parts are that condition and the MusicLeak loss.

方法Method

輸入是 16 kHz 單聲道混音，STFT 用 512 點、hop 128，保留 256 個頻率 bin。第一段把頻率切成 37 個非均勻子頻帶（1 kHz 以下每帶 2 bin，往上逐漸變寬到 24 bin），經 6 個時間／頻帶雙路徑區塊後，用 FiLM 閘控分出 6 個音源槽：1 個目標、3 個干擾語者、1 個音樂（含歌聲）、1 個雜訊。目標槽的 query 加上凍結的 ReDimNet 語者向量，其他槽與身分無關。再經 4 個時間、頻帶、跨音源三路徑區塊，每槽輸出一個複數遮罩。

第一段的損失很多：分組 PIT 的負 SI-SNR、音樂與雜訊各自的 SI-SNR、守恆項、多解析度 STFT、語者相似度與把歌聲當困難負例的推離項，以及 MusicLeak：目標估計投影到真實音樂上的能量占比。

第二段是資料到資料的流匹配：起點不是高斯雜訊而是第一段的目標估計，終點是乾淨目標，路徑取直線插值。骨幹是 8 個 AdaLN-Zero DiT 雙路徑區塊（384 維）加 U-ViT 跳接，條件包括流時間、語者向量，以及新的負向條件：把第一段估出的音樂、干擾語者、雜訊幅度做時間平均後投影成一個向量，告訴模型哪些東西已經刪掉、不准補回來。一個小的難度頭預測目標能量占比 ρ，換算成 1 到 4 步的 Euler 步數；訓練時直接在可回傳梯度的少步輸出上算 −SI-SNR，並隨機遮掉 30% 的初估，逼模型從混音裡找內容。另有一個 MeanFlow／α-Flow 的單步版本。

The input is a 16 kHz mono mixture; the STFT uses 512 points with a hop of 128 and keeps 256 frequency bins. Stage one splits frequency into 37 non-uniform sub-bands (2 bins per band below 1 kHz, widening to 24 bins higher up), runs 6 time/band dual-path blocks, then uses FiLM gating to open 6 source slots: one target, three interfering speakers, one music (including singing) and one noise. Only the target slot's query receives the frozen ReDimNet speaker embedding. Four time/band/cross-source triple-path blocks follow, and each slot outputs a complex mask.

Stage one has many losses: grouped-PIT negative SI-SNR, SI-SNR for music and noise, a conservation term, multi-resolution STFT, speaker similarity plus a push-away term that treats singing as a hard negative, and MusicLeak, the share of the target estimate's energy that projects onto the true music.

Stage two is data-to-data flow matching: the path starts from the stage-one target estimate rather than Gaussian noise and ends at the clean target, with straight-line interpolation. The backbone is 8 AdaLN-Zero DiT dual-path blocks (384 dimensions) with U-ViT skips, conditioned on flow time, the speaker embedding and the new negative condition: the stage-one music, interfering-speaker and noise magnitudes, time-averaged and projected into one vector that tells the model what has been deleted and must not return. A small difficulty head predicts the target energy ratio ρ and maps it to 1–4 Euler steps. Training applies −SI-SNR directly to the gradient-carrying few-step output and masks 30% of the initial estimate so the model must recover content from the mixture. A one-step MeanFlow/α-Flow variant is also trained.

限制Limitations

所有證據都來自一個合成場景。訓練與測試都是從 LibriSpeech、VoxCeleb2、WSJ0、MUSDB18-HQ、MoisesDB 線上混出的 3 秒片段，作者自己在實驗設定與結論裡都寫明「不能證明對真實錄音的泛化」，也沒有按有無音樂、有無歌聲分層報告。也就是說，標題裡的「音樂感知」只在聚合後的 Complex 場景上被量過一次。

基線怎麼得到的沒有交代清楚。TF-GridNet 與 SepReformer 標明是「adapted」，其他系統是否在同一份資料上重新訓練、用了多少計算量，論文都沒說；表裡也沒有任何信賴區間或多次種子。九個消融每個只給一組「前 → 後」數字。摘要本身完全沒有結果數字，正文只有兩張表，這篇讀起來更像一份架構說明。

All evidence comes from one synthetic scenario. Training and test are 3 s clips mixed online from LibriSpeech, VoxCeleb2, WSJ0, MUSDB18-HQ and MoisesDB, and the authors state in both the setup and the conclusion that this does not establish generalization to natural recordings; nor are results stratified by whether music or singing is present. The "music-aware" claim in the title is therefore measured only once, on the aggregated Complex scenario.

How the baselines were obtained is unclear. TF-GridNet and SepReformer are marked "adapted", but whether the other systems were retrained on the same data, and with what compute, is not stated; there are no confidence intervals or multiple seeds. Each of the nine ablations reports a single before-to-after pair. The abstract contains no results at all and the body has two tables, so the paper reads more like an architecture description.

討論Discussion

主表最清楚的訊息是：判別式第一段（Config-D）就贏了所有基線的 SI-SDRi（13.0 dB，次高的 TF-GridNet 11.9）、WER（12.0%，TF-GridNet 14.6%）與音樂殘留（2.9%，最好的基線 Mask2Flow-TSE 6.5%）。直接把 BS-RoFormer 分離器串上 SepReformer 只有 10.5 dB、殘留 7.8%，支持「聯合估計勝過串接」。

但生成式那段的帳要算清楚。從 D 到 DG，SI-SDRi 多 0.2 dB、PESQ 多 0.17、SIM 從 0.89 到 0.90，平均 NFE 從 1 變 1.6；音樂殘留反而從 2.9% 升到 3.4%，DNSMOS 是 3.06，低於 SoloSpeech 的 3.28 和 Mask2Flow-TSE 的 3.21。換句話說，生成式修補確實把一點音樂帶回來了，負向條件是在壓這個副作用：拿掉它，殘留從 3.4% 變 6.9%、歌聲殘留從 2.1% 變 5.4%。作者自己也把第二段定位成可選的品質換延遲，這個定位比摘要的框架誠實。

消融裡最具體的音樂證據是頻帶切分：拿掉它，音樂殘留從 2.9% 升到 6.2%，音樂分支本身的 SDR 從 9.4 掉到 6.1 dB，跟 BS-RoFormer 在音樂分離上的優勢一致。起點的消融也很清楚：從高斯雜訊改成第一段估計，NFE 從 16 降到 1.6、WER 從 16.8% 降到 12.2%。對 MIR 讀者來說，這篇的參考價值在於把音樂分離的工具（頻帶切分、多分支守恆）搬進語音任務，並示範了「被分離掉的歌聲會被生成模型補回來」這個失效模式，以及一個可量的指標 MusicLeak 去追它。

The clearest message of the main table is that the discriminative stage alone (Config-D) beats every baseline on SI-SDRi (13.0 dB vs 11.9 for TF-GridNet, the next best), WER (12.0% vs 14.6%) and music leakage (2.9% vs 6.5% for Mask2Flow-TSE, the best baseline). Simply cascading a BS-RoFormer separator into SepReformer gives 10.5 dB and 7.8% leakage, supporting joint estimation over cascading.

The generative stage needs careful accounting. From D to DG, SI-SDRi gains 0.2 dB, PESQ 0.17 and SIM goes from 0.89 to 0.90, while average NFE rises from 1 to 1.6; music leakage actually increases from 2.9% to 3.4%, and DNSMOS is 3.06, below SoloSpeech (3.28) and Mask2Flow-TSE (3.21). In other words, the refiner does bring some music back, and the negative condition is what contains that side effect: removing it raises leakage from 3.4% to 6.9% and singing leakage from 2.1% to 5.4%. The authors themselves position stage two as an optional quality-for-latency trade, which is more honest than the abstract's framing.

The most concrete music evidence in the ablations is band-splitting: removing it raises music leakage from 2.9% to 6.2% and drops the music stem's own SDR from 9.4 to 6.1 dB, consistent with BS-RoFormer's strength in music separation. The start-point ablation is also clear: replacing Gaussian noise with the stage-one estimate cuts NFE from 16 to 1.6 and WER from 16.8% to 12.2%. For MIR readers, the value lies in carrying music-separation tools (band-splitting, multi-stem conservation) into a speech task, in documenting the failure mode where separated singing is generated back in, and in MusicLeak as a measurable metric for tracking it.

ChoreoStudio: Planning What, When, and Who in Music-Driven Group Choreography

ChoreoStudio：在音樂驅動的群舞編排中規劃「什麼隊形、何時變換、由誰移動」

Jing Xu, Cunjian Chen, Qiuhong Ke
arXiv:2610.32325 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-23
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇在音樂和群舞動作之間插入一份明確、可編輯的編舞計畫：一個 Director 從段落層級的音樂結構與 Qwen3-Omni 寫的文字描述，預測相對隊形點集、隊形變換的時間閘門和每位舞者的移動分配，再交給擴散模型生成全身動作，生成後由確定性的 Verifier 檢查並回饋重新規劃。它先用頻率分解指出既有方法的毛病：舞者間距離的低頻變化（隊形演變）只有真實舞蹈的六成（IDDV 0.181 對 0.302）。在 AIOZ-GDance 三人設定上 GMR 10.42（TCDiff++ 11.57）、IDDV 0.267。最值得記住的是附錄裡的一個數字：單一循環、不經 Verifier 重選時 GMR 是 15.43，比 TCDiff++ 和 ST-GDance（14.76）都差，主表的領先主要來自驗證回饋的多輪重規劃。Why this one: This paper inserts an explicit, editable choreography plan between music and group dance motion: a Director predicts a relative formation point set, a transition gate for when the formation changes, and a per-dancer mobility allocation from segment-level musical structure and a text description written by Qwen3-Omni; a diffusion model then generates full-body motion, and a deterministic Verifier checks the result and feeds back for replanning. It first uses a frequency decomposition to diagnose existing methods: the low-frequency variation of inter-dancer distances (formation evolution) is only about 60% of real dance (IDDV 0.181 vs 0.302). In the three-dancer AIOZ-GDance setting it reaches GMR 10.42 (TCDiff++ 11.57) and IDDV 0.267. The number most worth remembering is in the appendix: a single cycle without Verifier reselection gives GMR 15.43, worse than both TCDiff++ and ST-GDance (14.76), so the headline lead mostly comes from verification-guided multi-round replanning.

動機Motivation

單人舞生成只需要決定「怎麼動」，群舞還要決定隊形長什麼樣、什麼時候換、由誰負責移動。真人編舞的隊形變換通常對齊高層的音樂結構，例如從前奏進副歌、從副歌進高潮。

作者指出既有群舞模型有兩個沒解決的問題。第一，生成前所有舞者是可互換的，對稱的跨舞者注意力無法決定誰當領舞、誰在側翼，獨立初始化的雜訊只是任意地打破對稱。第二，隊形不是一個明確的物件，而是藏在潛在互動裡，或被外部指定的軌跡取代。

他們用一個頻率分析把問題具體化：把每對舞者的地面距離用 0.5 Hz 低通濾波拆成慢速隊形變化與快速殘差，發現既有生成器保留了不少高頻活動，卻明顯缺少真實舞蹈裡的低頻隊形演變。也就是舞者一直在動、局部也協調，但整體隊形很少真正改變。

Single-dancer generation only decides how to move; group dance must also decide what the formation looks like, when it changes and who moves to change it. In human choreography, formation changes usually line up with high-level musical structure, such as moving from the intro into the chorus or from the chorus into the climax.

The authors name two unsolved problems in existing group models. First, dancers are exchangeable before generation, so symmetric cross-dancer attention cannot decide who leads and who flanks, and independent initial noise breaks the symmetry arbitrarily. Second, the formation is not an explicit object; it is hidden in latent interactions or replaced by externally given trajectories.

They make this concrete with a frequency analysis: splitting each pair's ground-plane distance with a 0.5 Hz low-pass filter into slow formation change and a fast residual shows that existing generators keep plenty of high-frequency activity but clearly lack the low-frequency formation evolution of real dance. The dancers keep moving and stay locally coordinated, but the group rarely truly reorganizes.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

單人音樂驅動舞蹈的代表是 FACT（Li 等人，2021）的跨模態 Transformer、Bailando（Siyao 等人，2022）的離散動作碼本，以及 EDGE（Tseng 等人，2023）的可編輯擴散；本篇的運動損失權重直接沿用 EDGE。群舞這邊有 GDanceR／GCD（Le 等人，2023）的聯合生成與對比式訓練、CoDancers（Yang 等人，2024）的一致性目標、TCDiff 與 TCDiff++（Dai 等人，2025、2026）的軌跡與步法控制，以及 ST-GDance（Xu 等人，2025）的長序列時空互動。

跟 ChoreoStudio 最像的其實是 TCDiff 這條線：TCDiff 也把群舞拆成「先決定位置，再生成動作」兩步，先產生舞者軌跡再以軌跡為條件生成身體動作。差別在於規劃的對象：TCDiff 預測絕對軌跡，ChoreoStudio 預測去掉質心與尺度的相對隊形、段落層級的變換閘門和移動分配，並讓計畫成為生成器唯一的群體資訊入口。單人舞裡 Lodge（Li 等人，CVPR 2024）的粗到細兩段式（先生成全域編舞基元，再補局部動作）也是同類想法。

Representative single-dancer music-to-dance systems are FACT (Li et al., 2021) with a cross-modal Transformer, Bailando (Siyao et al., 2022) with a discrete motion codebook, and EDGE (Tseng et al., 2023) with editable diffusion; this paper inherits EDGE's motion loss weights directly. For groups there are GDanceR/GCD (Le et al., 2023) with joint and contrastive generation, CoDancers (Yang et al., 2024) with coherence objectives, TCDiff and TCDiff++ (Dai et al., 2025, 2026) with trajectory and footwork control, and ST-GDance (Xu et al., 2025) with long-sequence spatio-temporal interaction.

The closest relative is actually the TCDiff line: TCDiff also splits group dance into deciding positions first and generating motion second, producing dancer trajectories and then conditioning body motion on them. The difference is what gets planned: TCDiff predicts absolute trajectories, whereas ChoreoStudio predicts a centroid- and scale-normalized relative formation, segment-level transition gates and mobility allocations, and makes the plan the generator's only channel for group information. For single dancers, the coarse-to-fine design of Lodge (Li et al., CVPR 2024), which first generates global choreographic primitives and then fills in local motion, is the same kind of idea.

方法Method

音樂端：librosa 抽的 438 維聲學特徵經音樂編碼器後，由一個可訓練的拆解器分成兩路。幀層級的節奏特徵只給動作生成器用；段落層級的結構特徵則依音樂自相似度學出的分段做池化，只給 Director 用。另外，凍結的 Qwen3-Omni 聽音樂並讀取稀疏標籤（音樂風格、舞蹈風格、人數），寫一段關於情緒、能量走向、齊舞程度與隊形意圖的描述，再用凍結的 Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B 轉成語意 token。這段描述每首只生成一次並預先快取。

Director 分四步：感知、規劃、排程、編碼。為了在還沒有動作時區分可互換的舞者，每位舞者抽一個去中心化的隨機「演員種子」，整首固定，不對應任何預設角色。規劃步驟對每段建立多頭有向關係圖，經圖訊息傳遞產生每位舞者的 2D 位置，去掉質心、除以 RMS 半徑後乘上預測的尺度。因為距離矩陣是從同一組點算出來的，幾何上一定可實現。關係圖的入度給出一個軟性角色分數，草擬的距離也會回灌到下一輪圖更新。

排程步驟是這篇最核心的耦合設計。變換閘門 g 的輸入包含相鄰兩段計畫隊形的差距、目前生成動作實際觀測到的變化，以及已知動作的比例；排程後的隊形是上一段與本段的凸組合，形式上就是 GRU 式的閘控插值。閘門開啟後，移動分配 μ 用 softmax 在舞者間分配，輸入包含每位舞者被排程的位移量，所以「隊形要變多少」決定「要不要變」，「誰要走多遠」又決定「誰來動」。

編碼步驟把計畫轉成兩種生成器條件：每位舞者逐幀的指令 token（經 token-wise AdaLN 調變每個 Transformer 區塊），以及從關係圖取 log 得到的跨舞者注意力偏置。作者刻意不讓 Director 的隱藏狀態繞過計畫直接進生成器；附錄的「latent bypass」消融把介面換成不受限的 Director 特徵，GMR 從 10.42 變差到 14.52。

生成器是聯合處理所有舞者的條件擴散模型，直接預測乾淨動作，1,000 個訓練雜訊級、50 步取樣、classifier-free guidance 權重 2.0；訓練時每位舞者各自抽雜訊級，讓長序列生成時重疊段可以保持已知。生成完一整條軌跡後，凍結的 Verifier 做確定性檢查：計畫實現誤差、膠囊碰撞（半徑 0.09 公尺）、間距下限 0.6 公尺、分散上限 2.0 公尺、腳滑、靜止與拍點對齊，彙總成獎勵與硬性合格旗標，違規事件（最多 8 個，含時間與涉及的舞者）編碼成回饋 token 給下一輪 Director。

訓練時所有計畫目標都從真實動作自動抽取，不需要人工編舞標註：以序列層級的匈牙利匹配把演員種子對到真實舞者，生成器學著實現從真實動作抽出的教師計畫，Director 學著從音樂、描述與狀態還原那份計畫。整個模型在兩張 RTX 4090 上訓練 55 個 epoch，同一個檢查點涵蓋 2 到 7 人。

On the music side, 438-dimensional librosa features pass through a music encoder and a trainable disentangler that splits them into two streams. Frame-level rhythmic features go only to the motion generator; segment-level structural features, pooled over segments learned from music self-similarity, go only to the Director. In addition, a frozen Qwen3-Omni listens to the music and reads sparse labels (music style, dance style, group size), writes a description of mood, energy progression, unison and formation intent, and a frozen Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B turns it into semantic tokens. The description is generated once per piece and cached.

The Director has four steps: Perceive, Plan, Schedule and Encode. To tell exchangeable dancers apart before any motion exists, each dancer draws a centered random "actor seed" fixed for the whole piece and tied to no predefined role. Planning builds a multi-head directed relation graph per segment, and graph message passing yields a 2D position per dancer, which is centered, divided by its RMS radius and multiplied by a predicted scale. Because the distance matrix comes from one point set, it is geometrically realizable by construction. In-degree in the graph gives a soft role score, and the drafted distances are fed back into the next graph update.

Scheduling is the core coupling. The transition gate g takes the gap between consecutive planned formations, the change actually observed in the generated motion and the share of known motion; the scheduled formation is a convex combination of the previous and current segment, which is in form a GRU-style gated interpolation. Once the gate opens, a softmax allocates mobility μ across dancers using each dancer's scheduled displacement, so how much the formation should change decides whether it changes, and how far each dancer must travel decides who moves.

Encoding turns the plan into two generator conditions: per-dancer, frame-aligned instruction tokens that modulate every Transformer block through token-wise AdaLN, and a cross-dancer attention bias taken as the log of the relation graph. The authors deliberately prevent Director hidden states from bypassing the plan into the generator; the appendix "latent bypass" ablation, which swaps the interface for unrestricted Director features, worsens GMR from 10.42 to 14.52.

The generator is a conditional diffusion model over all dancers jointly that predicts clean motion directly, with 1,000 training noise levels, 50 sampling steps and a classifier-free guidance weight of 2.0; noise levels are sampled per dancer during training so overlapping frames can stay fixed in long-sequence generation. After each complete trajectory, a frozen Verifier runs deterministic checks: plan realization error, capsule collision (radius 0.09 m), a 0.6 m minimum spacing, a 2.0 m dispersion limit, foot skating, freezing and beat alignment. These are combined into a reward and a hard pass flag, and violation events (up to 8, with timing and affected dancers) are encoded as feedback tokens for the next Director round.

All plan targets are extracted automatically from ground-truth motion, with no manual choreography labels: sequence-level Hungarian matching assigns actor seeds to real dancers, the generator learns to realize a teacher plan extracted from real motion, and the Director learns to recover that plan from music, description and state. The model trains for 55 epochs on two RTX 4090 GPUs, and one checkpoint covers groups of 2 to 7.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是比較的公平性。主表的 ChoreoStudio 用的是多輪 Verifier 回饋並挑最高分候選的協定，基線則各自單次取樣。附錄 C.4 給了關鍵對照：三人設定下單一循環、50 步的 ChoreoStudio 是 GMR 15.43、GMC 79.32，比 TCDiff++（11.57、82.31）和 ST-GDance（14.76、81.24）都差。同表也顯示，在相同的 100 步預算下，兩輪回饋重規劃（GMR 10.36）明顯好於從兩個獨立樣本挑一個（13.62），所以回饋本身是有用的；但沒有人給基線同樣的「生成多個、用驗證器挑」的待遇。

Verifier 本身也和評估指標部分重疊。它檢查碰撞、間距、拍點對齊，然後評估又用 TIF（碰撞）與 BAS（拍點對齊）打分，等於在推論時針對評估指標做了選擇。BAS 0.351 接近真實的 0.363，有一部分可能來自這個選擇，而不只是模型本身。

論文的動機是「隊形變換對齊音樂結構」，但這一點從沒被直接量過。變換 F1 0.80 與平均偏移 0.32 秒，是拿預測變換對比從真實動作抽出的偽標籤，不是對比音樂段落邊界；沒有任何一個指標檢查生成的隊形變換是否落在前奏到副歌這類邊界上。最接近的證據是「拿掉音樂結構」的消融：GMR 從 10.42 變 14.33、FMR 從 0.848 變 0.798，只說明段落特徵有用，沒說明它對齊了什麼。

其他幾點：主表雖宣稱以 bootstrap 估不確定性，卻沒有任何信賴區間；多樣性（Div 14.35）低於 TCDiff++ 的 17.43，摘要說的 state-of-the-art 不含這項；兩人設定的 TIF 是 0.10，CoDancers 是 0.08；模型訓練涵蓋 2 到 7 人，表只報到 4 人；使用者研究（30 人）只有長條圖，沒有數字。表 2 的「最佳基線」那一列數值（IDDV 0.181）跟附錄裡 TCDiff 的數值一樣，但正文說最強基線是 TCDiff++，兩處對不上。

The biggest issue is comparison fairness. ChoreoStudio's main-table numbers use multi-round Verifier feedback with best-candidate selection, while each baseline is sampled once. Appendix C.4 provides the key control: in the three-dancer setting, a single 50-step cycle of ChoreoStudio gives GMR 15.43 and GMC 79.32, worse than both TCDiff++ (11.57, 82.31) and ST-GDance (14.76, 81.24). The same table shows that at a matched budget of 100 steps, two rounds of feedback replanning (GMR 10.36) clearly beat picking the better of two independent samples (13.62), so the feedback itself helps; but no baseline receives the same treatment of generating several samples and selecting with a verifier.

The Verifier also partly overlaps with the evaluation metrics. It checks collisions, spacing and beat alignment, and evaluation then scores TIF (collisions) and BAS (beat alignment), which amounts to selecting for evaluation metrics at inference time. A BAS of 0.351, close to the real 0.363, may owe part of its value to this selection rather than to the model alone.

The paper's motivation is that formation changes line up with musical structure, yet this is never measured directly. Transition F1 of 0.80 and a mean offset of 0.32 s compare predicted transitions with pseudo-labels extracted from real motion, not with musical segment boundaries; no metric checks whether generated formation changes fall on intro-to-chorus boundaries. The nearest evidence is the "without music structure" ablation, where GMR goes from 10.42 to 14.33 and FMR from 0.848 to 0.798, showing only that segment features help, not what they align with.

Other points: the protocol mentions bootstrap uncertainty, but no confidence intervals appear in any table; diversity (Div 14.35) is below TCDiff++'s 17.43, so the abstract's state-of-the-art claim does not cover it; in the two-dancer setting TIF is 0.10 against CoDancers' 0.08; the model is trained on 2 to 7 dancers but tables stop at 4; the 30-person user study is shown only as a bar chart without numbers. The "best baseline" row in Table 2 (IDDV 0.181) matches the appendix value for TCDiff, while the text calls TCDiff++ the strongest baseline, so the two do not agree.

討論Discussion

這篇最可重用的貢獻是那組頻率式隊形指標。IDDV 量低頻的舞者間距離變化，Jitter 量高頻殘差，FMR 是低頻占總變化的比例；三者都對照真實資料解讀，而不是越大越好。數字很有說服力：TCDiff 與 ST-GDance 的 IDDV 是 0.181 與 0.179，真實舞蹈 0.302，ChoreoStudio 0.267。傳統的碰撞率與相關係數看不出這個差距，這組指標可以直接拿去評任何群舞生成器。

計畫可控性的實驗做得乾淨。固定音樂、演員種子、擴散雜訊與其他計畫變數，只改一項：改隊形讓實現誤差降 40%、78.54% 的樣本往要求的方向變；改變換時間降 37%、74.85%；交換移動分配降 29%、68.68%；TIF 與 Jitter 的變化都在 0.02 以內。語意描述也可控：把「緊密」改成「開展」，隊形擴大 0.34 公尺，82.6% 的配對方向正確。這比主表更能說明「計畫真的是生成器的控制通道」。

消融裡有一個值得追問的不一致。拿掉整個 Director，GMR 是 15.23；只拿掉語意描述，GMR 反而更差，是 17.68。描述是 Director 的輸入之一，拿掉一個輸入卻比拿掉整個模組傷得更重，論文沒有解釋。可能的原因是無 Director 版本另外換了較好的條件方式，但這需要作者說明。另外 w/o Verifier 的 GMR 16.10，跟 C.4 的單循環 15.43 大致是同一件事的兩種量法，也呼應了前面「領先大多來自重規劃」的判斷。

對 MIR 來說，這篇提供的是一個方向而不是結論：把音樂結構分析（段落、能量走向）接到群體空間決策上，是音樂理解的一個新出口。若要真正支持「隊形跟著音樂結構走」，下一步應該是量生成變換點和音樂段落邊界的對齊，例如用 MSAF 之類的結構分析工具給出邊界，再算命中率。

The most reusable contribution is the frequency-based formation metrics. IDDV measures low-frequency variation of inter-dancer distances, Jitter the high-frequency residual, and FMR the low-frequency share of total variation; all are read against real data rather than maximized. The numbers are persuasive: TCDiff and ST-GDance reach IDDV 0.181 and 0.179, real dance 0.302, ChoreoStudio 0.267. Conventional collision and correlation scores cannot see this gap, and these metrics can be applied directly to any group dance generator.

The plan-controllability experiments are clean. With music, actor seeds, diffusion noise and the other plan variables fixed, changing one component at a time gives: formation edits cut realization error by 40% with 78.54% of samples moving in the requested direction; timing edits 37% and 74.85%; mobility swaps 29% and 68.68%; changes in TIF and Jitter all stay within 0.02. The semantic description is controllable too: changing "compact" to "expansive" widens the group by 0.34 m with 82.6% of pairs moving the right way. This shows better than the main table that the plan really is the generator's control channel.

One ablation inconsistency deserves a question. Removing the whole Director gives GMR 15.23, while removing only the semantic description is worse, at 17.68. The description is one of the Director's inputs, so dropping one input hurting more than dropping the whole module is unexplained in the paper. Perhaps the no-Director variant uses a different and better conditioning path, but the authors would need to say so. Also, the w/o-Verifier GMR of 16.10 and the single-cycle 15.43 in C.4 measure roughly the same thing in two ways, echoing the earlier judgment that the lead mostly comes from replanning.

For MIR, the paper offers a direction rather than a conclusion: connecting music structure analysis (segments, energy progression) to collective spatial decisions is a new outlet for music understanding. To actually support the claim that formations follow musical structure, the next step would be to measure alignment between generated transition points and musical segment boundaries, for example by obtaining boundaries from a structure analysis tool such as MSAF and computing a hit rate.
