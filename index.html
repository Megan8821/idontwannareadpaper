Humanoid Musical Robots as Experimental Interfaces for Music-Evoked Emotion

人形音樂機器人作為音樂誘發情緒研究的實驗介面

Vincent K.M. Cheung, Jia-Yeu Lin
arXiv:2608.20433 · cs.RO, cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-24
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這是一篇立場論文（position paper），主張人形音樂機器人可以當作研究「音樂如何誘發情緒」的實驗工具：透過機器人固定的聲學輸出但可獨立操控的肢體動作、呼吸節奏與擬人化線索，把過去混在一起的聽覺與具身因素拆開來做因果實驗。論文用薩克斯風機器人 WAS-5 做技術可行性案例，展示它能把新式嘴唇機構的聲壓範圍拉高 33.8%，也能即時依參與者打拍子的速度調整或引導節奏，但完全沒有做真正的情緒量測——這篇沒有宣稱任何情緒學上的實證發現，只證明「這套機器人做得到」。Why this one: This is a position paper arguing that humanoid musical robots can serve as an experimental tool for studying how music evokes emotion: because a robot's acoustic output is reproducible while its body movements, breathing rhythm, and anthropomorphic cues can be manipulated independently, it lets researchers causally separate auditory and embodied factors that live performers confound together. The paper uses the WAS-5 saxophone robot as a technical feasibility case study, showing its new lip mechanism raises achievable sound pressure range by 33.8% and that it can follow or lead a participant's tapping tempo in real time -- but it runs no actual emotion measurement. It makes no empirical claim about music-evoked emotion, only that the platform is technically capable of supporting such experiments.

動機Motivation

音樂認知研究長期用預錄音樂刺激透過喇叭或耳機播放給受試者聽，這個做法隱含一個假設：純聽覺資訊就足以捕捉音樂表演的表達內容。但真實世界的音樂經驗是具身、多模態、互動的——視覺、動作與社交線索都會影響聆聽者「聽到」與「感受到」什麼。問題是，過去的實驗設計沒辦法在維持嚴謹對照的前提下，系統性操弄這些具身與互動因素：真人演奏者沒辦法逐次重複到毫秒不差，喇叭播放又完全抽掉了視覺與互動成分。這篇論文提出的解法是把人形音樂機器人當成實驗介面——機器人的聲學輸出可以精準重現，肢體動作、表情、呼吸節奏卻可以獨立於聲音之外被參數化操控，讓「音樂誘發情緒」的因果機制第一次有機會被逐項拆解測試。

Music cognition research has long relied on pre-recorded musical stimuli played through speakers or headphones, a design that implicitly assumes auditory information alone is sufficient to capture the expressive content of a musical performance. But real-world musical experience is embodied, multimodal, and interactive -- visual, motor, and social cues all shape what a listener hears and feels. The obstacle is that no prior setup could manipulate these embodied and interactive factors systematically while preserving experimental control: a live performer cannot repeat a performance identically across trials, while speaker playback strips out the visual and interactive components entirely. This paper's proposed solution is to use humanoid musical robots as experimental interfaces -- a robot's acoustic output can be precisely reproduced while its movement, expression, and breathing rhythm can be parametrically controlled independently of the sound, giving researchers a first real opportunity to dissect the causal mechanisms of music-evoked emotion piece by piece.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

機器人演奏系統本身不是新東西：打擊樂機器人如 Cog 與 Shimon、絃樂機器人如 Violin MUBOT 與 Toyota 小提琴機器人、管樂機器人如早稻田長笛與薩克斯風機器人系列，這些系統過去的開發目標主要是「表演」本身或研究人機互動，而不是拿來當認知科學的測量工具——這正是這篇論文跟它們最大的差異：不是造一台會演奏的機器人，而是主張把既有的機器人平台重新定位成可操控實驗變項的科學儀器。

論文的理論基礎來自音樂情緒研究裡的 BRECVEMA 框架，涵蓋腦幹反射、預期、同步等機制，也涵蓋涉及視覺想像與情緒感染的具身、社會性歷程；並引用既有發現支撐「視覺與互動確實會改變音樂體驗」的前提，例如視聽並呈的音樂比純聽覺版本被評為美感體驗更高、聽眾評價現場演奏時主要依賴的其實是視覺而非聽覺資訊、以及現場音樂比預錄音樂更能活化大腦的中腦邊緣獎賞網絡。

Robotic music-performance systems are not new: percussion robots such as Cog and Shimon, string robots such as the Violin MUBOT and the Toyota Violin Robot, and wind robots such as the Waseda flute and saxophone robot series. These systems were previously built mainly to perform or to study human-robot interaction, not to serve as cognitive-science measurement instruments -- and that is exactly this paper's departure from them: rather than building another robot that can play, it repositions existing robot platforms as scientific instruments whose experimental variables can be independently controlled.

The paper's theoretical grounding comes from the BRECVEMA framework for music-evoked emotion, spanning mechanisms from brain-stem reflex, expectancy, and entrainment through embodied, socially grounded processes such as visual imagery and emotional contagion. It also cites prior findings supporting the premise that vision and interaction genuinely change musical experience -- audio-visual music is rated more aesthetically engaging than audio alone, listeners evaluating live performance rely primarily on visual rather than auditory information, and live music activates the brain's mesolimbic reward network more than pre-recorded music.

方法Method

論文把「實驗介面」定義成一套能在嚴謹操弄實驗變項的同時觀察受試者行為變化的裝置，並指出人形機器人具備三個關鍵性質：參數化控制（動作動態、手勢時機、表達線索可獨立於聲音調整）、可重複性（同一段表演能在不同受試者之間以極低變異度重演）、以及模態解耦（多模態與互動變項可以互不干擾地個別操弄）。案例研究用的是 31 個自由度的薩克斯風機器人 WAS-5，具備模擬人類呼吸氣流的機制、手指/眼睛/眼皮/眉毛動作，新的嘴唇機構採 8 方向致動結構逼近人類嘴唇對簧片的環向接觸與徑向施力。

技術可行性實驗讓受試者對 WAS-5 演奏的六音旋律打拍子，分成三種條件：固定 60/120 bpm 的對照組、機器人依受試者拍子速度即時調整的跟隨組、以及機器人主動引導受試者拍子朝目標值靠近的引導組，節奏估計是即時用視訊追蹤受試者動作完成的。論文另外提出三套理論導向的未來實驗範式，分別測試「現場表演 vs. 喇叭播放」對投入感與信任感的差異、操弄機器人擬人動作（身體搖擺、眉眼動作、呼吸動作）對情緒感染強度的影響、以及多台機器人之間動作同步/不同步對社會共調節效果的作用。

The paper defines an "experimental interface" as a setup that lets researchers rigorously manipulate experimental variables while observing changes in participant behavior, and argues humanoid robots offer three key properties: parametric control (movement dynamics, gesture timing, and expressive cues tunable independently of sound), reproducibility (a performance repeated across trials with minimal variability), and modality decoupling (multimodal and interaction variables manipulated independently). The case study uses WAS-5, a 31-degree-of-freedom saxophone robot mimicking human respiratory airflow with finger, eye, eyelid, and eyebrow movement; its new lip mechanism uses an 8-directional actuation structure approximating the circumferential contact and radial force human lips apply to a reed.

The technical-feasibility experiment had participants tap their feet to a six-note melody performed by WAS-5 under three conditions: a control condition at fixed 60/120 bpm, a follower condition where the robot adjusted tempo to match participants' tapping rate in real time, and a leader condition where the robot guided participants' tapping toward a target tempo, with tempo estimated via real-time video tracking. The paper further outlines three theory-grounded future paradigms: comparing live robotic performance against speaker playback for engagement and trust; parametrically varying anthropomorphic cues to test emotional-contagion intensity; and varying synchrony across multiple audience robots to test social co-regulation effects.

限制Limitations

論文自己列了六項限制，其中最關鍵的一項是誠實承認整篇論文沒有任何情緒學上的實證資料：互動實驗只驗證了「機器人能不能跟隨或引導打拍子行為」，並非「機器人是否真的改變了音樂誘發的情緒」，作者明白寫出後續要有效度驗證過的主觀情緒量表搭配生理與行為量測才算數。其他限制包括機器人的材料、感測器與致動器會限制可操弄的實驗空間、機器人本身的建造與校準門檻高、受試者對機器人的接受度受個人與文化差異影響（結果可能無法跨地區跨時代推論）、以及必須留意類人機器人外型引發的恐怖谷效應。作者也提到虛擬機器人角色雖然失去物理臨場感，卻能提供更大的實驗設計彈性，算是一個可能的替代路徑。

The authors list six limitations, and the most consequential is their own admission that the paper contains no empirical emotion data at all: the interaction experiment only verifies that the robot can follow or lead tapping behavior, not that it actually changes music-evoked emotion; the authors state explicitly that establishing that will require validated subjective-emotion questionnaires paired with physiological and behavioral measures. Other limitations include the robot's materials, sensors, and actuators constraining the viable experimental state-space; the construction and calibration burden; individual and cultural variability in receptivity to robots, meaning results may not generalize across regions or over time; and the need to design around the uncanny-valley effect. The authors also note virtual musician robots, despite lacking physical grounding, could offer additional design flexibility as an alternative path.

討論Discussion

論文的結論明確把自己定位成一篇立場論文而非實證研究：三個貢獻分別是提出「人形音樂機器人作為實驗介面」這個框架、用 WAS-5 案例證明可重複的聲學控制與閉環互動確實技術上可行、以及提出三套理論導向的實驗範式供後續研究採用。作者特別澄清「不主張所有音樂經驗都涉及情緒，也不主張機器人能完整複製人類演奏的豐富性」，機器人被定位成補充而非取代真人演奏者的研究工具。整體而言，這篇論文的價值在於搭好一個可操作的實驗框架與平台，真正回答「機器人表演會不會改變聽眾的情緒」這個問題，還要等後續實驗把六項限制裡提到的量表與生理量測補上。

The conclusion explicitly frames the paper as a position piece rather than an empirical study: its three contributions are introducing the framework of humanoid musical robots as experimental interfaces, demonstrating via the WAS-5 case study that reproducible acoustic control and closed-loop interaction are technically feasible, and proposing three theory-grounded experimental paradigms for future work. The authors are careful to clarify that they "do not claim that all musical experiences are emotional, nor that humanoid robots can fully replicate the richness of human performance" -- the robot is positioned as complementary to, not a replacement for, studies using human musicians. On the whole, the paper's contribution is a workable experimental framework and platform; actually answering whether robotic performance changes listeners' emotions awaits future experiments filling in the questionnaires and physiological measures flagged in its own limitations.

Generalized Audio-Driven Synthesis of Precise Drummer Motion

廣義音訊驅動的精準鼓手動作生成

Álvaro G. Iñesta, Mattia Ryffel, Amit H. Bermano, Robert W. Sumner, Martin Guay
arXiv:2608.19055 · cs.CV, cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-20
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇解決的是一個先前兩個系統都沒處理好的問題：如何只靠音訊（不需要 MIDI）生成精準到看得出打在哪個鼓上的擬真鼓手動作。既有的兩個資料驅動方法——Shahid et al. 用強化學習訓練機器人打鼓、Kyriakou et al. 的 DRUMS 用 LSTM 加動作比對的混合系統——都吃 MIDI 輸入，前者動作缺乏人類的表現力，後者受限於資料庫比對與只有約 35 分鐘、雜訊高的訓練資料。這篇改用擴散模型直接吃音訊，並提出一個把「骨架姿態」與「鼓棒尖端座標」分開監督的雙目標損失函式，把鼓棒擊點誤差從純旋轉監督版本的平均 8.5 公分（鼓）、8.3 公分（鈸）壓到 2.0 公分與 1.8 公分，使用者研究裡生成動作與真人動作的偏好率只差 41.3% vs 58.7%，統計上不顯著，代表生成結果已經接近人類難以分辨真假的程度。Why this one: This paper tackles a problem neither of the two prior systems fully solved: synthesizing drummer motion precise enough to see which drum is being hit, driven by raw audio alone rather than MIDI. The two existing data-driven methods -- Shahid et al.'s reinforcement-learning-trained drumming robot and Kyriakou et al.'s DRUMS, an LSTM-plus-motion-matching hybrid -- both require MIDI input; the former lacks human-like expressivity due to mechanical constraints, and the latter is limited by database search and roughly 35 minutes of noisy training data. This paper instead conditions a diffusion model directly on audio and introduces a dual-objective loss separating skeletal-pose supervision from stick-tip Cartesian supervision, cutting mean impact-point error from 8.5 cm (drums) / 8.3 cm (cymbals) under rotation-only supervision down to 2.0 cm / 1.8 cm. In a user study, participants preferred generated motion over ground truth only 41.3% of the time versus 58.7% for ground truth -- a gap that is not statistically significant, indicating the generated motion is close to indistinguishable from real performance.

動機Motivation

音樂驅動的角色動作生成在舞蹈與旋律樂器演奏上已經有不錯的成果，但打鼓有幾個舞蹈生成沒有的根本困難。第一，鼓的聲音本質上是嘈雜的瞬態爆發，不同鼓件的聲音彼此相似，很難從音訊裡萃取出有鑑別力的特徵，也難以跨鼓組、跨錄音環境泛化。第二，鼓棒速度常常超過每秒 10 公尺，動作生成需要做到公分等級的精準度，同時又要避免肢體末端（鼓棒）出現抖動發散。第三，同一個聲音事件可能是左手或右手打出來的，這是一個「一對多」問題，系統不只要生成精準動作，還要做出高層的左右手協調決策。作者指出既有的生成式框架「還沒準備好」橋接嘈雜打擊訊號與高速物理執行之間的落差——它們擅長處理旋律樂器那種平滑、低頻的軌跡，但沒有能力解決鼓點起始的模糊性，也無法在極端加速度下維持穩定。

Music-driven character animation has achieved solid results for dance and melodic-instrument performance, but drumming introduces obstacles that dance generation does not face. First, drum audio consists of noisy transient bursts whose timbres are similar across different drum components, making discriminative feature extraction difficult and hindering generalization across kits and environments. Second, stick velocities often exceed 10 m/s, requiring centimeter-level motion precision while avoiding jitter at the limb extremities holding the sticks. Third, the same acoustic event could have been struck by either hand, a one-to-many problem requiring not just precise motion but a high-level coordination decision. The authors argue existing generative frameworks "remain ill-equipped" to bridge noisy percussive signals and high-velocity physical execution -- they excel at melodic instruments' smooth, low-frequency trajectories but cannot resolve drum-onset ambiguity or stay stable under extreme accelerations.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

動作生成技術本身經歷了從姿態混合、狀態機、動作圖到現在業界標準的動作比對（motion matching），再到近年的資料驅動生成模型（擴散模型因為能捕捉動作分布的多樣性，被認為優於 GAN 與 VAE）。音訊驅動動作生成這條線上，Tseng et al. 的 EDGE 用擴散模型做舞蹈生成，是這篇論文直接沿用並改造的架構基礎——但作者強調 EDGE 的設計「無法直接套用」到打擊樂，因為舞蹈動作平滑連續，鼓點動作卻是離散的瞬間爆發。

真正跟這篇做同一件事的先前系統只有兩個：Shahid et al.（2025）用強化學習訓練人形機器人以 MIDI 輸入打鼓，結果因為機械限制而缺乏人類的表現力；Kyriakou et al.（2025）的 DRUMS 系統用 LSTM 驅動手臂、動作比對處理軀幹與臉部、決定性函式處理腿部，同樣吃 MIDI 輸入，但受限於資料庫搜尋與啟發式規則，動作多樣性與物理合理性都有限，訓練資料也只有約 35 分鐘，雜訊高且常有腳滑問題。這篇論文跟這兩者的差異很明確：改用原始音訊而非 MIDI 輸入，讓系統能泛化到沒有對齊 MIDI 的音樂；設計新的雙目標損失函式同時兼顧骨架合理性與鼓棒末端精準度；並提出兩個新的評測指標（擊點偏差 IPD 與打擊對齊分數 PAS），讓這個子領域第一次有辦法系統性比較不同方法。

Motion-synthesis technology evolved from pose blending and state machines through motion graphs to today's industry-standard motion matching, and more recently to data-driven generative models, with diffusion favored over GANs and VAEs for capturing the full diversity of plausible motion. Within audio-conditioned motion synthesis, Tseng et al.'s EDGE uses diffusion for dance generation and is the architecture this paper directly adapts -- but the authors stress EDGE's design "does not translate" to percussion, since dance motion is smooth and continuous while drum motion is discrete and bursty.

Only two prior systems actually attempt the same task: Shahid et al. (2025) trained a humanoid robot via reinforcement learning to play drums from MIDI, but results "lack human-like expressivity due to mechanical constraints"; and Kyriakou et al.'s (2025) DRUMS drives arms with an LSTM, torso and face with motion matching, and legs with deterministic functions, also from MIDI, but is limited by database search and heuristics constraining diversity and plausibility, with training data of only about 35 minutes, noisy and prone to foot sliding. This paper's departure from both is explicit: raw audio instead of MIDI so it generalizes to unaligned music; a new dual-objective loss balancing skeletal plausibility against stick-tip precision; and two new metrics (Impact Point Deviation, Percussive Alignment Score) enabling systematic comparison for the first time.

方法Method

動作以每秒 120 幀表示（對應鼓棒可能超過每秒 10 公尺的速度）：身體用 27 個關節的連續 6D 旋轉表示，鼓棒方向另用 6D 旋轉表示，鼓棒尖端則額外用明確的三維卡氏座標表示。訓練資料是一位專業爵士鼓手在 Roland TD-25KV 電子鼓組上錄製的 3 小時 30 分 53 秒動作捕捉（120 Hz，約 151.8 萬幀），涵蓋基本功、17 種常見節奏型態（搖滾、迪斯可、放克、爵士、雷鬼、金屬等，三種速度）與即興演奏，刻意排除跨手打法。資料再用兩階段方式擴增：先用不同鼓組音色把動作對應的 MIDI 重新合成成音訊，再疊加 13 種隨機音訊效果（殘響、雜訊、等化、壓縮、變調等，強度從 [0,1] 均勻取樣），每個動作片段擴增出 50 種音訊版本。

音訊特徵是手工設計的 44 維向量，包含鼓件起始偵測、節拍追蹤、振幅包絡、頻譜重心與 40 維 MFCC，作者認為這比套用 Jukebox 之類的預訓練音訊編碼器更可解釋、更好維護。模型主幹沿用 EDGE 的音訊條件化 Transformer 擴散架構，重點創新在損失函式：標準做法用正向運動學把旋轉誤差轉成關節位置來監督，但旋轉誤差會沿著運動鏈累積，導致鼓棒末端精度不夠——這篇改用 ℒ = 0.5‖r-r̂‖² + 1‖p_sticks-p̂_sticks‖²，把身體姿態用旋轉監督、鼓棒尖端額外用卡氏座標直接監督，理由是「幾公分的誤差就決定打中還是打空」。訓練用 3×10⁻⁴ 學習率、batch size 128，在單張 RTX 3090 上跑 6000 個 epoch、約 48 小時。

Motion is represented at 120 Hz to capture stick velocities exceeding 10 m/s: the body uses continuous 6D rotations across 27 joints, stick orientation uses 6D rotation, and stick tips additionally carry explicit 3D Cartesian coordinates. Training data is 3h30m53s of 120 Hz motion capture (about 1.52 million frames) from a professional jazz drummer on a Roland TD-25KV kit, covering fundamentals, 17 common grooves at three tempos, and improvised songs, deliberately excluding cross-armed playing. Data is augmented in two stages: audio is re-synthesized from paired MIDI using randomized drum-kit timbres, then 13 randomized effects (reverb, noise, EQ, compression, pitch shift, strength sampled from [0,1]) are layered on, producing 50 audio variants per motion clip.

Audio features are a hand-designed 44-dimensional vector -- onset detection, beat tracking, amplitude envelope, spectral centroid, and 40-dimensional MFCCs -- which the authors argue is more explainable and maintainable than pretrained encoders such as Jukebox. The backbone reuses EDGE's audio-conditioned Transformer diffusion architecture; the key innovation is the loss: standard practice supervises joint position via forward kinematics from rotation, but rotation error accumulates along the kinematic chain, leaving the stick tip imprecise. This paper instead uses ℒ = 0.5‖r-r̂‖² + 1‖p_sticks-p̂_sticks‖², supervising body pose by rotation while supervising stick tips directly by Cartesian coordinates, reasoning that "a few centimeters of error determines hitting the drum versus missing." Training uses a 3×10⁻⁴ learning rate, batch size 128, for 6000 epochs (~48 hours) on one RTX 3090.

限制Limitations

論文承認系統只吃單軌鼓聲，遇到混音完成的音樂必須先用 Demucs 或 Spleeter 之類的音源分離工具抽出鼓軌，生成品質會受限於分離品質（作者的實驗顯示現有分離器「已經夠穩健」，但仍是外部依賴）；模型假設固定的鼓組空間配置，換一組配置不同的鼓需要額外資料或幾何條件化，留給未來工作；120 Hz 的高幀率是精準度的前提，降低取樣率會讓時間量化誤差變大；訓練資料只來自一位專業爵士鼓手，論文沒有明確討論換成不同鼓手風格時的泛化情形。

The authors acknowledge the system only takes isolated drum audio; polyphonic mixes must first go through source-separation tools such as Demucs or Spleeter to extract the drum stem, and output quality depends on separation quality (their experiments suggest current separators are "sufficiently robust," but it remains an external dependency). The model assumes a fixed drum-kit spatial configuration, and generalizing to a different kit layout is left as future work requiring additional capture or geometric conditioning. The 120 Hz frame rate is a precondition for accuracy; a lower sampling rate would increase temporal-quantization error. Training data comes from a single professional jazz drummer, and the paper does not explicitly address generalization to other drummers' styles.

討論Discussion

作者坦承跟既有方法做直接的量化比較「並不容易」，因為資料集、輸入模態（音訊 vs. MIDI）與評測方式都不一樣，而且發表當時沒有公開的程式碼或標準化精度指標可以對照——這也是為什麼這篇特別強調自己提出的 PAS 與 IPD 指標的價值：讓這個子領域第一次有辦法系統性比較。論文也指出 PAS 背後「離散音訊事件與加速度峰值之間做軟性對應」的核心概念可以推廣到打字、拍手、節奏性踏步等其他需要緊密音訊-動作同步的任務。

未來方向包括加入手指與臉部動作以提升視覺真實感、透過風格遷移處理龐克 vs. 慵懶爵士這類風格差異、開發手動調整左右手指派與時間偏移的創作工具，以及參考近期大提琴合成與人物-物件互動研究，探索兩階段模型架構。整體而言，這篇論文用一個相對成熟的擴散骨幹加上針對性的損失設計，把「音訊驅動鼓手動作」這個長期被 MIDI 綁住的子問題，第一次做到接近人類難以分辨真假的精度。

The authors admit direct quantitative comparison to prior work "remains non-trivial" because datasets, input modality (audio vs. MIDI), and evaluation methods all differ, and no public codebase or standardized precision metric existed at the time of writing -- which is why the paper leans on its own PAS and IPD metrics as a contribution: they let this sub-area be systematically benchmarked for the first time. The authors note that PAS's underlying idea, a soft correspondence between discrete audio events and acceleration peaks, generalizes to other tasks needing tight audio-motion synchrony, such as typing, clapping, or rhythmic footstepping.

Future directions include finger- and facial-level motion for visual realism, style transfer to capture differences such as punk energy versus laid-back jazz, manual authoring tools for overriding hand assignment and timing offsets, and exploring two-stage architectures following recent cello-synthesis and human-object-interaction work. Overall, by pairing a fairly established diffusion backbone with a targeted loss redesign, the paper pushes audio-driven drummer motion synthesis -- long tied to MIDI input -- to a precision level close to indistinguishable from real performance for the first time.

SingDance: Compositional Zero-Shot Singing-and-Dancing Video Generation with Role-Aware Audio Conditioning

SingDance：具角色感知音訊調控的可組合零樣本演唱與舞蹈影片生成

Tao Feng, Xu Li, Xiangyang Luo, Ming Wen, Huadai Liu, Chen Zhang, Wei Xue
arXiv:2608.16220 · cs.SD, cs.CV, cs.MM · 投稿submitted 2026-08-18
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇的核心手法不是新的擴散或影片生成骨幹，而是把「演唱時的嘴型同步」重新定義成一個可以獨立操控的語意角色欄位（畫面中人是聲音的來源，還是純粹伴舞的聆聽者），藉此把「純舞蹈」「純說話」「聆聽反應」跟從未在訓練資料裡出現過的「邊唱邊跳」四種行為，用同一套共享的語音表徵與注入路徑串起來。跟先前的音樂驅動舞蹈系統（如 EDGE、X-Dancer、OmniDance）或語音驅動人物動畫系統（如 Wan-S2V、InfiniteTalk）不同的地方在於：這篇從頭到尾沒看過任何一段「邊唱邊跳」的真實影片，純靠角色欄位切換就在推論時零樣本合成出來。用 InfiniteTalk 三分之一左右的參數量（5.63B vs 18.88B），SingDance 在嘴型同步準確度上仍拿到有競爭力的數字（LSE-C 7.995 vs InfiniteTalk 8.535），而在美感與畫質指標上反而是五個基準裡最高的（Aesthetic 0.593、Imaging 0.722），使用者偏好調查裡動作節奏對齊的偏好率是 61-68% 對 32-39%，大幅贏過同樣做語音驅動影片的 Wan-S2V。Why this one: SingDance's core move isn't a new diffusion or video backbone -- it's reframing lip-sync articulation as an explicit, independently controllable semantic role (whether the on-screen subject is the vocal source or merely a listener dancing along), letting a single shared speech representation and injection pathway cover speaking, listening/reacting, dancing-only, and the never-jointly-observed singing-and-dancing behavior. Unlike prior music-driven dance systems (EDGE, X-Dancer, OmniDance) or speech-driven avatar systems (Wan-S2V, InfiniteTalk), SingDance never sees a single real singing-and-dancing video during training -- it composes that behavior zero-shot at inference purely by flipping the role field. Using roughly a third of InfiniteTalk's parameters (5.63B vs 18.88B), SingDance still achieves competitive lip-sync accuracy (LSE-C 7.995 vs. InfiniteTalk's 8.535) while topping all five baselines on aesthetic and imaging quality (0.593 and 0.722). In human preference studies for motion-beat alignment, it wins 61-68% of votes against Wan-S2V's 32-39%.

動機Motivation

個人化舞蹈影片生成其實藏著兩種不同的行為，過去的研究把它們當成兩個獨立問題處理：音樂驅動的舞蹈生成方法只管編舞，完全不建模嘴型與發聲；語音驅動的人物動畫方法則預設畫面裡的人就是聲音的來源。但同一位使用者、同一首歌，創作者有時想要「邊唱邊跳」的影片，有時只想要「純伴舞」的影片——如果一個框架能同時支援兩種形式，就能大幅簡化個人化影片創作的流程。這篇論文的關鍵洞見是把「畫面中人是否在發聲」重新定義成一個明確的語意角色：畫面中人要嘛是聲音的來源（source），要嘛是接收畫面外聲音的聆聽者（listener）。

Personalized dance-video generation actually conflates two distinct behaviors that prior work always treated as separate problems: music-conditioned dance methods handle choreography only and never model vocal articulation, while speech-driven avatar methods assume the on-screen person is the one producing the voice. But given the same subject and the same song, a creator might sometimes want a video where the subject sings and dances, and other times just a dancing-along video with no lip-sync -- a single framework supporting both would substantially simplify personalized video creation. This paper's key insight is reframing whether the on-screen subject is vocalizing as an explicit semantic role: the visible subject is either the source producing vocals, or a listener receiving vocals from off-screen.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂驅動的舞蹈生成這條線上，從早期的 FACT、Bailando 到用擴散模型的 EDGE、精細編舞資料集 FineDance，再到近期把音樂對齊到 2D 姿態 token 的 X-Dancer、拆分動作與外觀專家的 MACE-Dance，以及把文字生成模型改造成音樂條件化的 MusicInfuser、結合圖像/文字/MERT 特徵的 5B 骨幹 OmniDance、階層式全域到局部音樂條件化的 Wan-Dancer——這些系統全都只處理編舞，完全不涉及畫面中人是否在唱歌。

另一條線是語音驅動的人物影片生成，例如用「幀對齊語音注入」做時間條件化的 Wan-S2V、多角色動畫的 HY-Avatar、稀疏幀配音的 InfiniteTalk，以及專門處理說話者/聆聽者互動的 DualTalk、UniLS、LPM 1.0 與 StreamAvatar——但這些系統要嘛預設畫面中人就是說話者，要嘛用完全獨立的路徑分別處理說話與聆聽兩種行為。SingDance 跟這些系統最大的不同，是刻意讓來源角色與聆聽角色共用同一套語音表徵與注入路徑，只透過角色條件的切換去改變行為，而不是像 DualTalk 等系統那樣為兩種角色設計不同的處理管線；跟音樂驅動舞蹈系統的差異則是額外引入了角色感知的語音控制，把「會不會對嘴」變成一個可獨立操縱的軸。

Within music-conditioned dance generation, the lineage runs from early FACT and Bailando through the diffusion-based EDGE and the fine-grained FineDance dataset, to systems that align music to 2D pose tokens (X-Dancer), split motion and appearance experts (MACE-Dance), adapt text-to-video backbones with music conditioning (MusicInfuser), combine image/text/MERT features in a 5B backbone (OmniDance), and use hierarchical global-to-local music conditioning (Wan-Dancer) -- all handling choreography only, never whether the on-screen subject is singing.

A separate lineage is speech-driven human video generation: Wan-S2V's frame-aligned speech injection, HY-Avatar for multi-character animation, InfiniteTalk for sparse-frame dubbing, and systems modeling speaker/listener interaction such as DualTalk, UniLS, LPM 1.0, and StreamAvatar. But these systems either assume the on-screen subject is always the speaker, or use entirely separate pathways for speaking versus listening, as DualTalk does. SingDance's central departure is deliberately sharing one speech representation and injection pathway across source and listener roles, changing only the role condition to switch behavior, rather than building distinct pipelines per role; its departure from the dance lineage is adding role-aware speech control, turning "whether lips are synced" into an independently controllable axis.

方法Method

語音走 Wav2Vec 2.0 做逐層加權聚合後映射成全域語音特徵與逐幀對齊的局部語音 token；音樂則走 MuQ（音樂導向的聲學/結構特徵）映射成逐幀對齊的局部音樂 token。角色控制透過五欄位提示結構（語音角色、風格、動作、首幀、鏡位）實現，標準化的「語音角色」欄位決定訊號要指派給畫面中人（來源）還是畫面外（聆聽者），文字提示裡的肢體動作描述跟發聲行為描述分開寫，並搭配一組全域角色嵌入與局部角色 token 貫穿訓練與推論。

針對五種任務（說話、聆聽反應、純伴舞-器樂、純伴舞-歌曲、邊唱邊跳）設計了「硬性精簡路由」，依任務決定要把哪些 token 送進交叉注意力——關鍵是「歌曲+來源角色」這個組合（對應邊唱邊跳）在訓練時完全沒有出現過，是推論時才第一次被組合出來的零樣本配置。

訓練分兩階段：語音階段用約 30 萬段五秒影片（HuMoSet 加自建網路影片，約 1.2 萬段是真正的畫面外聆聽反應，其餘是畫面內說話），此階段不涉及音樂路徑；舞蹈階段用約 4.8 萬段五秒影片（清洗過的 MA-Data 加自建舞蹈資料，1.8 萬段器樂、3 萬段有歌曲），器樂範例只有音樂做時變音訊輸入，歌曲範例保留語音加音樂（即使沒有對嘴，歌聲的分句與樂句邊界仍能影響編舞），並保留 20% 的說話範例避免遺忘，訓練目標是標準的流匹配損失，沒有額外的對嘴或節拍對齊損失。

文字-音訊分類器自由引導採三段式，分別用文字引導強度與音訊引導強度組合；骨幹初始化自 Wan2.2-TI2V-5B，語音階段在 480p 訓練 8 個 epoch，舞蹈階段先在 480p 訓練 8 個 epoch、再於 704×1280 訓練約 3000 步，用 64 張 A100、batch size 64、AdamW 學習率 1×10⁻⁵。

Speech uses Wav2Vec 2.0 with layer-wise weighted aggregation, mapped into global speech features and frame-aligned local speech tokens; music uses MuQ (music-oriented acoustic/structural features) mapped into frame-aligned local music tokens. Role control uses a five-field prompt structure (vocal role, style, action, first frame, camera); the standardized vocal-role field assigns the signal to the on-screen subject (source) or off-screen (listener), body-motion text is kept separate from vocal-behavior text, and a paired global role embedding plus local role token run through training and inference. A hard-compact routing scheme picks which tokens enter cross-attention for each of five tasks (speaking, listening/reacting, dancing-only instrumental, dancing-only song, singing-and-dancing) -- critically, the song+source combination for singing-and-dancing is never observed during training and is composed zero-shot only at inference.

Training runs in two stages: a speech stage on roughly 300,000 five-second clips (HuMoSet plus proprietary web video, about 12,000 genuine off-screen listening/reacting, the rest on-screen speaking) with no music pathway; and a dance stage on roughly 48,000 clips (cleaned MA-Data plus proprietary dance data, 18,000 instrumental and 30,000 with songs), where instrumental examples use music as the only time-varying audio, song examples retain both speech and music since vocal phrasing still informs choreography without lip-sync, and speech examples make up 20% of dance-stage data to prevent forgetting; training uses a standard flow-matching loss with no dedicated lip-sync or beat-alignment loss. Text-audio classifier-free guidance uses a three-pass scheme with separate text and audio guidance scales; the backbone initializes from Wan2.2-TI2V-5B, training 8 epochs at 480p in the speech stage, then 8 epochs at 480p followed by ~3000 steps at 704×1280 in the dance stage, on 64 A100 GPUs, batch size 64, AdamW at 1×10⁻⁵.

限制Limitations

論文自己承認 SingDance 目前只處理短片段、單人物、且角色是整段影片二元固定的：它還無法處理片段內的角色轉換（例如一開始聆聽、後來自己開口唱）、多人重唱、多重語音角色，也沒有自動判斷角色歸屬的機制。要把這種角色感知的組合能力延伸到更長、多人的表演場景，論文明白說是留給未來工作。

The authors acknowledge SingDance currently targets short, single-person clips with a binary, clip-level-fixed vocal role: it does not yet model within-clip role transitions (e.g., starting as a listener and later becoming the singer), duets, multiple simultaneous vocal roles, or automatic role attribution. Extending this role-aware compositional approach to longer, multi-person performances is explicitly left as future work.

討論Discussion

論文把貢獻歸納成三點：提出以顯式聲音角色為條件的影片生成問題定義，把說話、聆聽、純伴舞、邊唱邊跳統整成四種相關的生成行為；設計來源與聆聽角色共用語音表徵與注入路徑、再透過任務路由的語音/音樂/角色 token 做逐幀聯合音訊注入的架構；並且證明只用不對稱的監督資料（從未看過邊唱邊跳的配對影片），就能組合泛化到訓練時沒出現過的「歌曲+來源角色」配置。

數字上的佐證相當扎實：在邊唱邊跳與純伴舞兩個測試集上，SingDance 的節奏對齊指標 BeatAlign（0.27 與 0.30）與 MBCR（0.26）都高於 Wan-S2V（0.26/0.25、0.20/0.22），節奏偏好調查分別拿到 61.33% 與 68.00%，遠高於 Wan-S2V 的 38.67% 與 32.00%；消融實驗顯示拿掉音樂 token 後 BeatAlign 從 0.2723 掉到 0.2462、MBCR 從 0.2558 掉到 0.2386，證明音樂條件確實對節奏對齊有貢獻而非裝飾性設計。整體而言，這篇論文的價值不在於推出更強的影片生成骨幹，而在於證明「把發聲角色顯式標註成可組合的條件變數」這個做法，能讓一個相對輕量（5.63B 參數，約為 InfiniteTalk 的三分之一）的系統零樣本泛化到訓練時看不到的行為組合，同時嘴型同步與畫質都不必犧牲。

The paper distills its contribution into three points: formulating video generation conditioned on an explicit vocal role, unifying speaking, listening, dancing-only, and singing-and-dancing as four related behaviors; a role-aware architecture where source and listener share one speech representation and injection pathway while task-routed tokens are composed via frame-wise joint audio injection; and demonstrating that asymmetric supervision alone -- never observing a paired singing-and-dancing video -- is enough to compositionally generalize to the untrained song+source configuration.

The numeric backing is solid: on both test sets, SingDance's BeatAlign (0.27 and 0.30) and MBCR (0.26) both exceed Wan-S2V's (0.26/0.25 and 0.20/0.22), and human preference for rhythm alignment comes in at 61.33% and 68.00% versus Wan-S2V's 38.67% and 32.00%; an ablation removing the music token drops BeatAlign from 0.2723 to 0.2462 and MBCR from 0.2558 to 0.2386, showing the music condition genuinely contributes to beat alignment rather than being decorative. Overall, the paper's value isn't a stronger video backbone -- it's showing that explicitly labeling vocal role as a composable condition lets a comparatively lightweight system (5.63B parameters, about a third of InfiniteTalk's) generalize zero-shot to behavior combinations never seen in training, without sacrificing lip-sync accuracy or image quality.
