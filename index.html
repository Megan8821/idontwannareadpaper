FlowSep 2: Self-Supervised Flow Matching for Language-Queried Audio Source Separation

FlowSep 2：用於語言查詢音訊音源分離的自監督流匹配模型

Yi Yuan, Xubo Liu, Haohe Liu, Xiyuan Kang, Mark D. Plumbley, Wenwu Wang
arXiv:2608.22111 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-22
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：FlowSep2 把語言查詢音源分離（LASS）從『預測遮罩』改造成『生成』：用整流流匹配搭配 DiT 骨幹，在混音與文字查詢的條件下直接從雜訊生成目標音源的潛在表徵，並加入師生自監督目標 Self-Flow 讓表徵先對齊再訓練流匹配。在 AudioCaps 上，FAD 從自家前代 FlowSep 的 2.86 降到 0.88，CLAP 分數從 41.9 提升到 44.9，也贏過 SAM-Audio 的 FAD 1.58。但它只用通用音效資料訓練，遇到音樂混音（MUSDB18）時輸給 SAM-Audio，CLAPA 是 58.5 比 62.8——生成式分離的進步是真的，但還不是音樂專用分離的贏家。Why this one: FlowSep2 reframes language-queried audio source separation (LASS) as generation rather than mask prediction: rectified flow matching with a DiT backbone synthesizes the target source's latent from noise, conditioned on the mixture and a text query, plus a teacher-student self-supervised objective (Self-Flow) that aligns latents before flow training. On AudioCaps it cuts FAD from its own predecessor FlowSep's 2.86 to 0.88 and lifts CLAP score from 41.9 to 44.9, beating SAM-Audio's FAD of 1.58. The catch: trained only on general sound data, so on music mixtures (MUSDB18) it trails SAM-Audio, 58.5 vs. 62.8 CLAPA -- the generative-separation gain is real but not yet a win for music-specific separation.

動機Motivation

語言查詢音源分離（Language-Queried Audio Source Separation, LASS）讓使用者用一句文字描述，就能從混音裡把對應的聲音抽出來，應用範圍涵蓋自動音訊剪輯、多媒體檢索、輔助聽覺裝置，以及嘈雜環境下的語音增強。但主流做法幾乎都是判別式（discriminative）的遮罩預測——模型從頻譜圖直接算出一張遮罩，乘上混音頻譜就得到目標音源。這類方法在聲音強烈重疊的複雜場景裡容易失敗：不是把目標聲音過度壓抑，就是留下明顯的頻譜破洞（spectral holes），分離結果聽起來破碎不完整。

Language-Queried Audio Source Separation (LASS) lets a user pull one sound out of a mixture using a text description, with applications in audio editing, multimedia retrieval, assistive listening, and speech enhancement in noisy environments. Most existing systems are discriminative: they predict a mask from the mixture's spectrogram and multiply it back in. This breaks down when sound events overlap heavily, either over-suppressing the target or leaving spectral holes -- audible gaps where separation failed. FlowSep2 targets exactly this failure mode by replacing mask prediction with generation.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

判別式遮罩法的代表是 LASS-Net 與 AudioSep，兩者都是直接從頻譜圖回歸一張遮罩再乘回混音；FlowSep2 論文本身量到它們在 AudioCaps 上的 FAD 分別是 5.09 與 4.38，明顯差於生成式做法。真正的生成式先例是作者自己前一代的 FlowSep：用整流流匹配（rectified flow matching, RFM）搭配 UNet，把分離當成從雜訊生成目標潛在表徵的任務，FAD 已經降到 2.86。

FlowSep2 對 FlowSep 的改動很具體：骨幹從 UNet 換成 DiT（Diffusion Transformer），並加入師生自監督目標 Self-Flow，先讓潛在表徵對齊、再進流匹配訓練，訓練資料也擴大到超過 5,650 小時。消融實驗量到單獨換骨幹的效果：DiT-RFM 在 AudioCaps 的 FAD 是 1.38，同條件的 UNet-RFM 則是 1.68。跟工業級的 SAM-Audio（同樣走流匹配路線的通用音源分離系統）相比，FlowSep2 在通用音效資料集上更好（AudioCaps FAD 0.88 對 1.58），但兩者最像的地方在於都把分離視為條件式生成而非遮罩預測——FlowSep2 的差異化賣點是額外疊加的自監督表徵對齊。

The discriminative baselines are LASS-Net and AudioSep, both of which regress a mask straight from the mixture's spectrogram and multiply it back in; the paper measures their FAD on AudioCaps at 5.09 and 4.38 respectively, clearly worse than the generative approaches. The real generative precedent is the authors' own prior system, FlowSep: rectified flow matching (RFM) with a UNet backbone, treating separation as generating the target's latent representation from noise, already reaching FAD 2.86.

FlowSep2's changes on top of FlowSep are concrete: the backbone moves from UNet to a DiT (Diffusion Transformer), and a teacher-student self-supervised objective, Self-Flow, aligns latents before flow training; training data also scales to over 5,650 hours. An ablation isolates the backbone swap alone: DiT-RFM reaches FAD 1.38 on AudioCaps versus 1.68 for UNet-RFM under the same setup. Against the industrial SAM-Audio -- another flow-matching universal separator -- FlowSep2 does better on general-audio benchmarks (AudioCaps FAD 0.88 vs. 1.58), and conceptually the two are closest to each other: both treat separation as conditional generation rather than masking. FlowSep2's differentiator is the added self-supervised representation alignment on top of that shared idea.

方法Method

FlowSep2 由四個模組組成：文字端用 FLAN-T5 文字編碼器，輸出固定長度 50、維度 1024 的特徵；音訊端用預訓練的 Stable-Audio VAE 把波形壓縮成潛在表徵，壓縮比約 320 倍，時間維度 512、頻率維度 128。核心是整流流匹配（RFM）：從高斯雜訊 z₀ 沿線性路徑插值到目標潛在表徵 z₁，模型學的是這條路徑上的速度場 v = z₁ − (1−σ)z₀，並以混音潛在表徵與文字特徵作為條件。

Self-Flow 是額外加上去的自監督目標：同一筆資料造出「學生」與「老師」兩種視角，學生用伯努利抽樣混合兩個不同流時間點的雜訊程度，老師則取兩者中較乾淨的一個，老師的參數只用指數移動平均（EMA）更新、不做反向傳播；兩邊表徵用餘弦相似度算對齊損失，跟 RFM 損失加權相加成總損失。骨幹換成 DiT 後有 S/M/L 三種深度（12/16/24 層），消融顯示深度從 12 加到 24，AudioCaps 上的 FAD 從 1.09 降到 0.84。

FlowSep2 has four components: a FLAN-T5 text encoder producing fixed-length-50, 1024-dimensional text features; a pretrained Stable-Audio VAE that compresses the waveform into a latent (roughly 320x compression, 512 time steps by 128 frequency bins); and a core rectified flow matching (RFM) objective that linearly interpolates from Gaussian noise z0 to the target latent z1 and learns the velocity field v = z1 - (1-sigma)z0 along that path, conditioned on the mixture latent and the text feature.

Self-Flow is the added self-supervised objective: two views, student and teacher, are built from the same clip -- the student mixes two different flow-time noise levels via Bernoulli sampling, the teacher takes the cleaner of the two, and the teacher's parameters are updated only by EMA, never by backprop. The two views' representations are aligned with a cosine-similarity loss, added to the RFM loss as the total training objective. With the backbone swapped to DiT, three depths were tested (12/16/24 layers); the ablation shows FAD on AudioCaps dropping from 1.09 at depth 12 to 0.84 at depth 24.

限制Limitations

最大的限制是音樂資料：FlowSep2 完全用通用音效資料集訓練（VGGSound、AudioCaps、AudioSetCaps、WavCaps 共 5,650 小時），論文自己說是因為「缺乏授權清楚的大規模音樂資料集」。結果反映在零樣本測試的 MUSDB18 上——FlowSep2-M 的 CLAPA 是 58.5，輸給同樣走流匹配路線但涵蓋更廣的 SAM-Audio（62.8）。換句話說，FlowSep2 在通用音效分離上贏過 SAM-Audio，但音樂音源分離不是它目前的強項。訓練成本也不低：單一 NVIDIA A100-80GB 要跑 400 萬步。論文自己列出的下一步是補上音樂訓練資料，以及加入空間線索、視覺資訊等額外條件模態。

The biggest limitation is music data: FlowSep2 is trained purely on general-sound datasets (VGGSound, AudioCaps, AudioSetCaps, WavCaps, 5,650 hours total), which the paper attributes to a lack of large, cleanly licensed music datasets. This shows up on the zero-shot MUSDB18 evaluation: FlowSep2-M scores 58.5 CLAPA, behind SAM-Audio's 62.8 -- another flow-matching separator but with broader coverage. So FlowSep2 beats SAM-Audio on general sound-effect separation but is not yet ahead on music-specific separation. Training cost is also substantial: a single NVIDIA A100-80GB GPU for 4 million steps. The authors' own stated next steps are adding music training data and richer conditioning modalities such as spatial cues and visual information.

討論Discussion

整體來看，FlowSep2 把 LASS 從遮罩預測改成條件生成後，在重疊聲音多的場景確實比判別式方法更穩，AudioCaps 上的 CLAP 分數從 LASS-Net 的 33.9 一路推到 44.9，FAD 從 5.09 壓到 0.88。Self-Flow 的價值主要在訓練效率：論文說加了它之後收斂只要 100 萬到 150 萬步，比單純 RFM 快。

人類評分（10 位評分者，6 位有音訊研究背景、4 位無關背景，5 分李克特量表）在 AudioCaps 上整體印象（OVL）給到 4.09、音訊與文字的相關性（REL）給到 4.13，顯示生成品質也有主觀評分支持，不是只有客觀指標好看。但這些主觀分數缺乏跟判別式基準的直接對照，只能算是這個模型自身的參考值。

Overall, turning LASS from mask prediction into conditional generation does make FlowSep2 more robust under heavy overlap: CLAP score on AudioCaps climbs from LASS-Net's 33.9 to 44.9, and FAD drops from 5.09 to 0.88. Self-Flow's main payoff is training efficiency -- the paper reports convergence in about 1-1.5M steps with it, faster than plain RFM.

Human ratings (10 raters, 6 with audio-research backgrounds and 4 without, 5-point Likert scales) give AudioCaps an Overall Impression (OVL) of 4.09 and Audio-Text Relation (REL) of 4.13, so the generation quality has subjective backing too, not just favorable objective metrics. That said, these subjective scores are not directly compared against the discriminative baselines, so they read as a reference point for the model itself rather than a head-to-head win.

Vibrato Matching for Modulation Control and Blending in Sound Mixtures

顫音匹配：混音中的調變控制與音色融合

Jeremy Hyrkas
arXiv:2608.22057 · cs.SD, eess.SP · 投稿submitted 2026-08-22
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇不是要蓋新的分離模型，而是反過來研究顫音（vibrato）如何影響人耳與演算法判斷「有幾個音源」。作者把顫音抑制（先拔除一個聲音原有的顫音）與顫音轉移（把另一個聲音的顫音貼上去）合成一套逐諧波的「顫音匹配」演算法，分別處理調頻與振幅調變。用既有的同度音源分離演算法 Common Fate Transform（CFT）驗證：匹配前 CFT 還能大致分開兩個音源，匹配後「完全失去分離這兩個音源的能力」——但全程沒有做聽覺測試，也沒有任何分離效果的量化數字，只靠頻譜圖與這句定性描述。Why this one: This paper does not build a new separation model; instead it studies how vibrato shapes whether ears and algorithms perceive one source or several. The author combines vibrato suppression (stripping a signal's existing vibrato) with vibrato transfer (applying another signal's vibrato pattern) into a single per-harmonic 'vibrato matching' algorithm, handling frequency modulation and amplitude modulation separately. It is validated against an existing unison source-separation algorithm, the Common Fate Transform (CFT): before matching, CFT still roughly separates two sources; after matching, it 'completely degrades the CFT's ability to separate these sources' -- but no listening test and no quantitative separation metric back this up, only spectrograms and that qualitative claim.

動機Motivation

混音裡如果兩個樂器同時演奏同一個音高（同度，unison），聽者要靠什麼線索才聽得出「這其實是兩個聲音」？這篇論文的答案是顫音模式的差異——顫音是持續音高度上的週期性調變，通常伴隨音量的調變。過去已經有顫音抑制與顫音轉移兩種技術，但都是各自獨立處理：抑制只管拔掉顫音，轉移多半把整段訊號套一個簡單的振幅包絡，沒有拆到逐諧波的層級，也沒人把兩者合起來當一套工具用在「混音融合」這個方向上。

When two instruments play the same pitch at once (unison), what cue lets a listener tell there are actually two sources? This paper's answer is differing vibrato patterns -- vibrato being a periodic pitch modulation on a sustained note, usually paired with a loudness modulation. Vibrato suppression and vibrato transfer already existed as separate techniques: suppression only strips vibrato out, and prior transfer work mostly applied one simple amplitude envelope to the whole signal rather than working per-harmonic, and nobody had combined the two into one tool aimed at blending sources in a mix.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

顫音抑制的既有方法直接砍掉訊號裡的顫音（論文引用 [2]）；顫音轉移的既有做法（引用 [1, 3]）是把來源訊號的整段振幅包絡套用到目標訊號上，屬於整段訊號層級的操作，沒有拆解到個別諧波。另一條相關路線是正弦模型（sinusoidal modeling），操縱諧波成分各自的調頻與調幅參數再重新合成聲音。

同度音源分離這邊，Common Fate Transform（CFT）與相關研究是用調頻／調幅模式本身當線索去拆開同度訊號。這篇論文最像的既有方法其實是正弦模型加顫音轉移的組合，但做得更細：不是套一個全域振幅包絡，而是逐諧波分別算調頻與調幅，還額外處理了非諧波殘響部分的振幅調變——這是先前顫音轉移方法沒碰過的部分。

Prior vibrato suppression work simply strips vibrato from a signal (the paper cites ref [2]); prior vibrato transfer work (refs [1, 3]) applies the source signal's whole-signal amplitude envelope to the target, operating at the level of the entire signal rather than per harmonic. A related line is sinusoidal modeling, which manipulates each harmonic partial's own AM and FM parameters before resynthesis.

On the unison-separation side, the Common Fate Transform (CFT) and related work use the FM/AM modulation pattern itself as the cue for splitting unison signals apart. The closest existing approach to this paper is really sinusoidal modeling combined with vibrato transfer, but done at finer granularity: instead of one global amplitude envelope, it computes FM and AM separately per harmonic, and it additionally handles amplitude modulation of the non-harmonic residual -- something prior vibrato-transfer work did not touch.

方法Method

演算法分兩階段。第一階段「顫音抑制」：先用截止頻率低於顫音速率（約 2 Hz）的高階 FIR 低通濾波器抓出基頻的「顫音濾波」基線，再用實際瞬時基頻與這條基線的比值建構一個去調變延遲，把訊號沿時間軸重新取樣，去掉調頻；接著在頻譜圖上，對每個頻率沿時間軸做同樣的低通濾波，抓出振幅的基線，用比值去掉調幅。

第二階段「顫音轉移」：先用巴特沃斯帶通濾波器逐一取出來源訊號各諧波，算出每個諧波的均方根振幅包絡，濾波後得到基線，兩者比值就是逐諧波的調幅型態，套到目標訊號對應的諧波上；調頻部分則用來源基頻與其基線的比值建構「調變延遲」，套用到目標的諧波上。非諧波殘響則是先用帶阻濾波器濾除各諧波頻率，把剩下的殘響頻譜切成 d 個等寬頻段，逐段找最大振幅頻率、算出對數振幅的頻譜包絡，再用同一套顫音濾波器抓出基線比值，內插回全頻段後套用到目標的殘響上。

The algorithm has two stages. Stage one, vibrato suppression: a high-order FIR lowpass filter with a cutoff below typical vibrato rates (about 2 Hz) extracts a smoothed 'vibrato-filtered' baseline of the fundamental frequency; the ratio between the actual instantaneous fundamental and that baseline builds a demodulating delay used to resample the signal along time and remove FM. The same lowpass filtering is then applied per frequency bin on the spectrogram magnitude to extract an amplitude baseline, whose ratio removes AM.

Stage two, vibrato transfer: a Butterworth bandpass filter isolates each harmonic of the source signal, its RMS envelope is computed and lowpass-filtered to a baseline, and the ratio gives a per-harmonic AM pattern applied to the corresponding target harmonic; FM is transferred by building a modulating delay from the ratio of the source fundamental to its own baseline and applying it to the target's harmonics. The non-harmonic residual is handled by bandstop-filtering out the harmonic frequencies, splitting the remaining spectrum into d equal-width frequency bands, finding the peak-magnitude frequency per band per frame to build a log-amplitude spectral envelope, filtering that the same way to get a baseline ratio, then interpolating back across frequency and applying it to the target's residual.

限制Limitations

論文自己承認幾個限制：一是沒有做聽覺測試，「尚未做聽覺測試以確認顫音匹配確實會降低聽者辨識多個音源的能力」，目前「降低分離效果」的證據全部來自演算法（CFT）表現而非人耳；二是完全沒有量化指標，分離效果好壞只靠頻譜圖與文字描述，沒有 SDR、SIR 這類數字。

三是論文自己指出「調變以外的頻譜特徵也在同度音源分離裡扮演角色」，暗示顫音匹配不保證讓分離演算法徹底失效。此外方法本身假設訊號是單音、有清楚諧波結構、且能準確估計基頻，對噪音成分重的樂器可能不適用；論文也沒有討論運算複雜度或即時可行性。

The authors acknowledge several limitations themselves: first, no listening test was run -- 'listening tests have not yet been conducted to confirm that using vibrato matching on unison signals reduces a listener's ability to perceive multiple sources' -- so the current evidence for degraded separability comes entirely from an algorithm's (CFT's) behavior, not from human ears. Second, there is no quantitative metric at all; separation quality is judged only from spectrograms and prose, with no SDR, SIR, or similar numbers reported.

Third, the paper itself notes that 'spectral features other than modulation play a role in unison source separation,' implying vibrato matching alone does not guarantee that a separation algorithm will fully fail. The method also assumes a monophonic, clearly harmonic signal with accurately estimable fundamental frequency, which may not hold for noisier instruments; the paper does not discuss computational complexity or real-time feasibility either.

討論Discussion

作者把顫音匹配定位成三種用途：訊號層面可以用範例改變顫音、跨樂器移植顫音來做出新音色，或幫物理建模合成加料；錄音工程上可以用來讓雙軌人聲的顫音對齊；創作與混音上則可以把多個樂器的聲音融合成單一聽感來源。最具體的證據是兩組源分離測試：人聲同度混音在顫音匹配後，CFT「完全失去分離這兩個音源的能力」；低音管與低音雙簧管的混音匹配後，第二個音源「被拆分到兩個分離出來的成分裡」，即使個別音源都先餵給演算法當提示，CFT 仍「無法把音源拆開」。論文把原始碼與音訊範例都公開在網路上，但沒有列出下一步的具體研究方向。

The authors frame vibrato matching around three uses: on the signal side, changing vibrato by example, transplanting it across instrument classes to create new timbres, or augmenting physical-modeling synthesis; in audio engineering, aligning vibrato across double-tracked vocal takes; and creatively, fusing multiple instruments into a single perceived sound source. The most concrete evidence is two separation tests: after matching, CFT 'completely degrades' on a unison vocal mixture, and on a bassoon/bass-oboe mixture the second source 'splits between the two separated components,' with CFT unable to unmix the sources even when primed with each source in isolation. Source code and audio examples are published online, but the paper lists no concrete next research direction.

MusPyExpress: Extending MusPy with Enhanced Expression Text Support

MusPyExpress：為 MusPy 擴充表情文字支援

Phillip Long, Hao-Wen Dong, Julian McAuley, Zachary Novack

為什麼挑這篇：MIDI 是符號音樂研究最常用的表示法，卻幾乎不記錄樂譜上的表情文字（tempo、力度、圓滑線、重音等演奏指示）。MusPyExpress 在既有工具庫 MusPy 上加了 28 種新的表情文字標註類別，並拿目前最大的 MusicXML 資料集 PDMX（222,820 首）驗證：95.33%（212,406 首）帶有表情文字，總計超過 350 萬筆標註。論文用這批資料訓練約 2000 萬參數的小型 transformer 做三種生成任務，結果顯示「前綴＋即時制」的表情標註模型明顯優於「預期式」模型，但作者自己也承認各指標互有領先、沒有一個設定全面最好，可能只是模型規模太小的產物。Why this one: MIDI is the dominant symbolic-music representation, yet it captures almost none of the expression text on sheet music -- tempo, dynamics, slurs, accents, and other performance instructions. MusPyExpress adds 28 new expression-text annotation classes to the existing MusPy library and validates them on PDMX, the largest MusicXML dataset (222,820 scores): 95.33% (212,406 scores) contain expression text, over 3.5 million markings in total. The authors train a small (~20M-parameter) transformer on this data for three generation tasks, and find that 'prefix + real-time' expression-tagging models clearly beat 'anticipatory' ones -- though they concede no single setup dominates every metric, which may just be an artifact of the small model size.

動機Motivation

符號音樂建模長期以 MIDI 類表示法為主，但 MIDI 排除了西方樂譜裡常見的表情文字標註——tempo、力度記號、圓滑線、演奏技法等。這些標記是樂譜溝通的重要部分，扮演「這段音符該怎麼演奏」的路標角色，但目前 AI 建模方法幾乎沒碰過這一層資訊。MusicXML 格式雖然存了大量這類標記，可是既有的符號音樂處理工具大多是為 MIDI 格式最佳化，沒有準備好去利用 MusicXML 裡這批豐富的表情文字。

Symbolic music modeling has long relied on MIDI-like representations, which omit the expression-text annotations common in Western sheet music -- tempo, dynamics markings, slurs, articulations. These markings are a key part of how music is communicated on the page, acting as a roadmap for how a passage should be played, yet current AI approaches largely ignore this layer. MusicXML files store plenty of this information, but existing symbolic-music processing libraries are generally optimized for MIDI and are not equipped to harness the expression text available in MusicXML.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

這篇論文直接點名了四個既有工具，並指出各自的缺口。MusPy（這篇論文本身要擴充的基礎庫）雖然能處理通用的符號音樂，但「幾乎無法解析大部分表情文字，例如力度、圓滑線與演奏技法」。music21 是最知名的工具箱之一，也支援 MusicXML，但這些標註「常被埋在物件階層很深的地方，下游建模要抽取出來很麻煩」。symusic 同樣支援 MusicXML，但它的設計目標是「有效率地解析 MIDI，而非廣義的符號音樂表示」，開發時就沒有著重在擷取表情文字。jSymbolic 則專注於從符號音樂抽統計特徵，適合分類任務，但不適合彈性的輸入輸出處理。

換句話說，MusPyExpress 最像的既有方法就是 music21——兩者都想從 MusicXML 抽出豐富標註——差別在於 MusPyExpress 刻意把這些標註攤平成容易在 Python 裡直接取用的物件，而不是像 music21 那樣深埋在物件階層裡，目的就是讓它能直接餵進下游的生成式模型訓練管線。

The paper names four existing tools and their specific gaps. MusPy, the library this work extends, handles general symbolic music but 'fails to parse most expression text, like dynamics, slurs, and articulations.' music21, one of the best-known toolkits, also supports MusicXML, but its annotations 'are often embedded deep within its object hierarchy, making them cumbersome to extract for downstream modeling tasks.' symusic supports MusicXML too, but its 'primary design goal is efficient MIDI parsing rather than broad symbolic music representation,' so it was not built with expression-text extraction in mind. jSymbolic focuses on extracting statistical features for classification, not flexible input-output handling.

The closest existing tool to MusPyExpress is really music21 -- both aim to pull rich annotations out of MusicXML -- but MusPyExpress's difference is deliberately flattening those annotations into objects that are easy to access directly in Python, instead of burying them in an object hierarchy the way music21 does, specifically so they can feed straight into a downstream generative-modeling pipeline.

方法Method

MusPyExpress 擴充的是 MusPy 既有的通用 Music 物件，底層格式不變，但標註系統大幅擴增，新增了 28 種表情文字類別，分成文字類（如 ChordSymbol、RehearsalMark）、結構控制類（如 Dynamic、HairPinSpanner、TempoSpanner）、音符層級標註（如 Articulation、SlurSpanner、VibratoSpanner）與通用類別。標註分兩個層級存放：跟時間相關的整體記號與排練記號放在樂曲層級，力度與個別文字指示則放在聲部層級。

一個關鍵設計是「spanner」概念，用來表示「跨越一段時間長度」的表情文字，例如漸慢（accelerando）記成 TempoSpanner，跟單點的速度記號（如 vivace）用的 Tempo 物件區分開來。工具還提供雙重計時系統，可以在「拍號時間單位（tempo-agnostic 的時間步）」與「以秒為單位、會反映速度變化的即時制時間」之間互轉，並在輸出 MIDI 或音訊時真正把這些標記體現出來——例如圓滑線段落的音符時值會延伸到下一個音符、重音音符的音量會明顯調高，論文用可調常數控制這些效果（如漸慢延長 3 倍、重音音量提高 1.5 倍）。

MusPyExpress extends MusPy's existing general-purpose Music object; the underlying format is unchanged, but the annotation system is substantially expanded with 28 new expression-text classes, grouped into text-based (e.g. ChordSymbol, RehearsalMark), structural-control (e.g. Dynamic, HairPinSpanner, TempoSpanner), note-level (e.g. Articulation, SlurSpanner, VibratoSpanner), and general-purpose categories. Annotations are stored at two levels: temporally-related marks and rehearsal marks at the score level, dynamics and individual text instructions at the track level.

A key design is the 'spanner' concept, for expression text that spans an explicit duration -- an accelerando is a TempoSpanner, distinct from the Tempo object used for a single-point marking like vivace. The library also implements dual timing: converting between a tempo-agnostic metrical time-step unit and a seconds-based real-time unit that reflects tempo changes, and it actually renders these markings in MIDI/audio output -- for example, notes in a slurred passage have their duration extended to the next note, and accented notes get a marked loudness boost, controlled by configurable constants (e.g. slowing tempo 3x for fermatas, raising velocity 1.5x for accents).

限制Limitations

論文自己列出三項限制。一是範圍：目前只針對個別聲部（track）建模，還沒處理整份總譜（full score），留給未來工作。二是模型規模：各項客觀指標（音高類別熵、音階一致性、律動一致性、困惑度）在不同設定下互有領先，沒有哪一種設定全面最好，論文自己承認這可能是「模型規模太小」造成的假象，而不是設計本身的優劣。三是涵蓋範圍：目前只實作了「最常見」的表情文字類型，MusicXML 本身並不限制創作者能標註什麼，所以一定還有沒收錄到的標記。

另外幾個沒明講但看得出來的限制：表情文字的實際演奏效果（如漸慢多少、重音多大聲）用的是人工訂的常數，帶有主觀判斷；訓練資料只用了 PDMX 裡有標註的 357,749 條聲部；序列長度上限 1024，長曲子可能被截斷；即時制的表示法把歌曲截到 60 秒，超過的部分表情文字就沒了。

The authors list three explicit limitations. First, scope: the current work models individual tracks rather than full scores, leaving that for future work. Second, model scale: across the objective metrics (pitch class entropy, scale consistency, groove consistency, perplexity), no single configuration dominates every metric, which the authors themselves attribute to 'an artifact of our small model size' rather than a design flaw. Third, coverage: only 'the most common types' of expression text are implemented, and MusicXML does not constrain what creators can annotate, so gaps remain.

Several further limitations are implicit rather than stated outright: the actual rendered effect of expression text (how much a fermata slows a tempo, how loud an accent gets) relies on manually curated constants, which is a subjective choice; training used only the 357,749 annotated PDMX tracks; the maximum sequence length of 1024 tokens can truncate longer pieces; and the real-time representation truncates songs at 60 seconds, dropping any expression text beyond that point.

討論Discussion

PDMX 的統計本身就是一項發現：22.28 萬份 MusicXML 裡有 95.33%（21.24 萬份）帶表情文字，總數超過 350 萬筆，平均每首 20.1 筆，其中 tempo（108.3 萬筆）與 dynamic（107.0 萬筆）最多。論文也發現浪漫樂派作曲家標註表情文字的頻率高於巴洛克與古典樂派，並指出這符合音樂史：浪漫樂派作曲家常明確標出表情記號以強調個人風格，巴洛克與古典樂派則更依賴演奏慣例。

三個生成任務裡，最實用的是表情標註（依音符生成表情文字），論文把它定位成「MIDI 轉演奏渲染」的中介步驟：給一份沒有表情記號的純 MIDI，模型可以建議該加上 legato、crescendo、dolce 之類的標記，用來自動幫大量沒有表情資訊的 MIDI 資料庫加註。前綴式模型在這個任務上明顯優於預期式模型——以即時制表示為例，前綴模型的整體標註準確率是 33.90%，預期式模型是 27.75%——論文認為原因是表情標記本身是稀疏序列，前綴設定的預測問題比預期式簡單。

The PDMX statistics are a finding in their own right: of 222,820 MusicXML files, 95.33% (212,406) contain expression text, over 3.5 million markings in total, averaging 20.1 per piece, with tempo (1,082,640) and dynamics (1,069,492) the most frequent types. The authors also find that Romantic-era composers annotate expression text more often than Baroque and Classical composers, which they note matches music history: Romantic composers often encoded expression markings explicitly to emphasize individual expressivity, while Baroque and Classical composers relied more on performance convention.

Of the three generation tasks, the most practically useful is expression tagging (adding expression text given notes), which the authors frame as an intermediate step in MIDI-to-performance rendering: given a plain MIDI file with no expression markings, the model can suggest labels like legato, crescendo, or dolce, enabling automatic annotation of large unexpressive MIDI collections. Prefix-conditioned models clearly beat anticipatory ones on this task -- in the real-time representation, the prefix model's overall tagging accuracy is 33.90% versus 27.75% for the anticipatory model -- which the authors attribute to expression tokens forming a sparse sequence that is an easier prediction target under the prefix setup.
