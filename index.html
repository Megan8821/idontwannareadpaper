Can Music Generation Models Tap Along? Evaluating Generative Approaches to Beat Tracking

音樂生成模型能跟著打拍子嗎？在凍結的 MusicGen 上只訓練 10.4M 參數的音訊條件轉接器，生成與歌曲同步的打擊樂軌（click／合成 groove／取樣 groove）再用輕量 TCN 讀出拍點，GTZAN F70 0.851，接近 Beat This! 的 0.888，並以去鼓測試與按 onset 強度分組的漏拍／誤報分析檢驗是否只是在追 onset

Kun Fang, Ziyu Wang, Ichiro Fujinaga

為什麼挑這篇：拍點追蹤一向是從音訊直接預測時間戳。這篇在凍結的 MusicGen 上只訓練 10.4M 參數的轉接器，讓模型「跟著歌演奏」一條打擊樂軌，再從裡面讀出拍點：GTZAN F70 0.851，低於 Beat This! 的 0.888，但 AMLt 0.907 高於它的 0.895；把鼓拿掉後 F70 只掉 0.08，Beat This! 掉 0.17、madmom 掉 0.25。選它是因為它換了問題的形狀，而且附了一套按 onset 強度分組的漏拍／誤報分析，可以拿來檢查任何追拍器是不是只在追 onset。要注意推論時要跑 MusicGen-large 加自迴歸生成，成本遠高於傳統模型，論文沒有報告。Why this one: Beat tracking has always meant predicting timestamps directly from audio. This paper trains only a 10.4M-parameter adapter on a frozen MusicGen so the model ‘plays along’ with a percussion track, then reads beats from it: GTZAN F70 0.851, below Beat This!'s 0.888, but AMLt 0.907, above its 0.895; with drums removed, F70 drops only 0.08, versus 0.17 for Beat This! and 0.25 for madmom. I picked it because it changes the shape of the problem, and it comes with a miss/false-positive analysis grouped by onset strength that can check whether any beat tracker is just following onsets. Note that inference runs MusicGen-large plus autoregressive generation, far costlier than conventional models, and the paper does not report this.

動機Motivation

傳統拍點追蹤把音訊直接對應到拍點時間戳：從 RNN、TCN 到 Transformer，骨幹一路換，輸出始終是一串時間。作者的出發點是人怎麼表達拍子——點頭、拍手，或樂手即興加一條節奏聲部——拍子是透過動作或演奏表現出來的，不是寫下時間戳。直接預測的模型可能學到統計上有效、但與人的節奏知覺無關的特徵，最典型的就是追 onset：哪裡最響就把拍點放哪裡。

另一方面，近年的伴奏生成模型（JukeDrummer、MusicGen-Stem、STAGE）已經能為一首歌生成同步的鼓軌。要生成對得上的鼓，模型必須抓到歌的節奏結構，即使它從沒被要求輸出拍點。於是問題變成：能不能把拍點追蹤改寫成生成任務——先讓模型「跟著演奏」一條打擊樂軌，再從這條軌裡讀出拍點？附帶的好處是輸出不只是時間戳，而是一條聽得見的打擊樂軌。

Conventional beat tracking maps audio directly to beat timestamps: the backbone has moved from RNNs to TCNs to Transformers, but the output is always a list of times. The authors start from how people express the beat—nodding, clapping, or, for musicians, improvising a rhythmic part—where the beat is shown through movement or performance, not written down as timestamps. Direct-prediction models may learn statistically useful features unrelated to human rhythm perception, the classic case being onset-following: put the beat wherever it is loudest.

Meanwhile, recent accompaniment generators (JukeDrummer, MusicGen-Stem, STAGE) can already generate a drum track synchronized with a song. To generate drums that fit, a model must capture the song's rhythmic structure, even though it is never asked to output beats. So the question becomes: can beat tracking be recast as a generative task—first have the model ‘play along’ with a percussion track, then read the beats from that track? A side benefit is that the output is not just timestamps but an audible percussion track.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接預測派。 madmom 的 RNN（Böck 等 2016）、Böck & Davies 2020 的 TCN、Beat Transformer（Zhao 等 2022）、Beat This!（Foscarin 等 2024）都是從頻譜直接輸出逐幀拍點機率。Foscarin 等 2026 把 masked diffusion 用在拍點追蹤，雖然是生成模型，生成的仍是拍點標籤而非音訊。本文的差別在於中間多了一條真的音訊。

伴奏生成派。 JukeDrummer（Wu 等 2022）是「beat-aware」鼓伴奏生成，但它把拍點資訊當作條件餵進去；STAGE（Strano 等 2025）以 MusicGen-small 全參數微調、把參考音訊的 token 前置當條件；MusicGen-Stem 做多聲部生成與編輯。這些工作關心生成的鼓好不好聽、跟原曲合不合，評估拍點對齊時是拿生成軌和原曲各自抽出的拍點互比，而不是對人工標註。本文反過來：訓練目標是從人工拍點標註合成的打擊樂，評估對的也是人工標註。

探測派。 Wei 等 2024 與 Kanatas 等 2026 證明 MusicGen／Jukebox 的隱藏層讀得出速度、拍號甚至拍點，但那只是探測，沒回答這些資訊能否用來生成同步的音訊。

Direct prediction. madmom's RNN (Böck et al. 2016), the TCN of Böck & Davies 2020, Beat Transformer (Zhao et al. 2022) and Beat This! (Foscarin et al. 2024) all output frame-wise beat probabilities directly from spectrograms. Foscarin et al. 2026 apply masked diffusion to beat tracking; it is generative, but it still generates beat labels, not audio. The difference here is that real audio sits in the middle.

Accompaniment generation. JukeDrummer (Wu et al. 2022) is ‘beat-aware’ drum accompaniment generation, but it feeds beat information in as a condition; STAGE (Strano et al. 2025) fully fine-tunes MusicGen-small and prepends the reference audio's tokens as the condition; MusicGen-Stem does multi-stem generation and editing. These works care whether the drums sound good and fit the song, and evaluate beat alignment by comparing beats extracted from the generated track and from the original, not against human annotations. This paper does the reverse: the training target is percussion synthesized from human beat annotations, and evaluation is against those annotations.

Probing. Wei et al. 2024 and Kanatas et al. 2026 show that tempo, meter and even beats can be read from MusicGen/Jukebox hidden states, but that is probing; it does not answer whether this information can drive synchronized audio generation.

方法Method

最該點出的相似對象。 我認為是 Beat Transformer 的「先分離再追拍」：它先用 Spleeter 把鼓、貝斯等聲部拆開，讓模型看到更乾淨的節奏線索。本文其實是同一思路的生成版本——不是把鼓從混音裡拆出來，而是重新「畫」一條只表達拍子的鼓，再交給 TCN 讀。從這個角度看，生成模型扮演的是一個學出來的前端。

打擊樂目標。 從同一份拍點標註合成三種目標，各訓練一個模型：Click——下拍 kick、其餘拍 hi-hat，像節拍器；Groove (synth)——14 種依曲風固定的規則節奏型，用合成的 kick、snare、hi-hat、cowbell、conga；Groove (sampled)——從 Groove MIDI Dataset 依曲風與速度挑真人演奏的鼓 MIDI，逐小節對齊到標註拍點，再用 AVL Drumkits 取樣渲染。曲風標籤只用來造訓練目標，推論時模型只看音訊。

生成模型。 兩個凍結的 MusicGen 中間夾一個可訓練轉接器。輸入歌曲轉成 EnCodec token 後送進 MusicGen-large，取每一層的幀級特徵做可學權重加總，再過一疊擴張卷積（約 5 秒上下文）得到條件序列 C。生成端是 MusicGen-small，C 以兩種方式注入：逐幀加到解碼器輸入，以及每層一個 Flamingo 式的 gated cross-attention。只訓練轉接器，共 10.4M 參數。註腳的消融：編碼端改用 MusicGen-small，驗證集 F 降 0.013、GTZAN 降 0.003；改用從頭訓練的編碼器則分別降 0.052 與 0.064。

兩個訓練技巧。 一、以 0.8 的機率把自迴歸上下文換成靜音 token，逼模型去看歌而不是重複自己剛生成的節奏；註腳說沒有這招，驗證集 F 掉到 0.31。二、以 0.5 的機率把輸入換成用 HT Demucs 去鼓的版本，讓模型學會從其他樂器推拍子。每個模型以 10 秒片段訓練 30 個 epoch，對目標 EnCodec token 做交叉熵。推論時一次生成 30 秒、貪婪解碼，並用係數 1.5 的 classifier-free guidance 加強條件，防止節奏漂走。

讀出拍點與比較對象。 生成軌裡不是每個鼓擊都在拍上，也可能有雜訊，所以為每種目標各訓練一個約 143K 參數的 TCN（Böck & Davies 2020 架構，16 kHz log-mel 輸入），後接 DBN，用 300 首歌的生成軌訓練。比較對象：Stable Audio ControlNet（凍結 Stable Audio Open 1.0、複製前 12 層做條件分支，561M 可訓練參數，目標同 Groove sampled）；STAGE（作者釋出的權重，每次接續 5 秒地生成到 30 秒）；Beat This! 與 madmom。所有生成模型都配同樣訓練方式的 TCN。訓練用 Beat This! 整理的 Ballroom、Hainsworth、RWC、ASAP；評估在 124 首驗證子集與完全沒見過的 GTZAN 999 首，只用每首前 30 秒。

主結果（GTZAN）。 F70：Click 0.851、Groove synth 0.850、Groove sampled 0.849，三種目標幾乎沒差；Beat This! 0.888、madmom 0.870；Stable Audio ControlNet 0.691、STAGE 0.335。但在允許倍速、半速與反拍的 AMLt 上，本文 0.907–0.912 高於 Beat This! 的 0.895（madmom 0.923 仍最高），AMLc 0.874–0.881 也高於 Beat This! 的 0.834：模型大多跟得上歌，錯誤主要在拍子層級或相位。同樣的 TCN+DBN 套在另外兩個生成模型上 AMLt 只有 0.753 與 0.482，所以不是 DBN 撐起來的。

TCN 與音質。 不用 TCN、直接在 Click 生成軌上挑 onset 峰值，F70 仍有 0.747。音質（Audiobox Aesthetics PQ）：Groove sampled 5.93 最低，Stable Audio ControlNet 7.54、STAGE 7.09——後兩者更像真人演奏，但常對不上拍或亂加過門。

去鼓測試。 推論時把輸入換成去鼓版（ASAP 是獨奏鋼琴不處理）。因為本文模型訓練時見過去鼓音訊，另訓練一個不用去鼓增強的版本（w/o DR aug），和同樣沒見過的 Beat This!、madmom 公平比較。依圖 3 標示的數字，GTZAN 上 F70 的下降：Beat This! −0.17、madmom −0.25、本文 −0.08、w/o DR aug −0.14、Stable Audio ControlNet −0.25。即使沒用增強，本文模型也掉得比兩個傳統模型少；而同樣用了增強的擴散模型掉得和 madmom 一樣多，作者推測是自迴歸「延續已建立的節奏」帶來的好處。

onset 依賴分析。 以 log-mel 的 spectral flux 為 onset 強度並在每首歌內標準化。把標註拍點按該處 onset 強度分五組算漏拍率；把離最近拍點至少 140 ms 且 0.2 拍的強峰值視為「反拍 onset」，同樣分五組，算被誤當拍點的比例。為避免倍速／半速干擾，只保留四個方法估的速度都在標註 ±10% 內的歌（458 首）。在去鼓後的 metal 與 disco 上，本文模型每一組的漏拍率都最低，反拍 onset 變強時誤報率也維持低，Beat This! 與 madmom 則陡升。

The closest relative. I think it is Beat Transformer's ‘demix, then track’: it first splits drums, bass and other parts with Spleeter so the model sees cleaner rhythmic cues. This paper is a generative version of the same idea—instead of extracting the drums from the mix, it redraws a drum part that expresses only the beat and hands it to a TCN. Seen this way, the generative model acts as a learned front end.

Percussion targets. Three targets are synthesized from the same beat annotations, one model each: Click—kick on downbeats, hi-hat elsewhere, like a metronome; Groove (synth)—14 fixed rule-based genre patterns with synthesized kick, snare, hi-hat, cowbell and conga; Groove (sampled)—human-performed drum MIDI from the Groove MIDI Dataset chosen by genre and tempo, aligned bar by bar to annotated beats and rendered with AVL Drumkits samples. Genre labels are used only to build training targets; at inference the model sees only audio.

Generative model. A trainable adapter sits between two frozen MusicGen models. The input song is encoded to EnCodec tokens and passed through MusicGen-large; frame-level features from every layer are combined by a learnable weighted sum, then a stack of dilated convolutions (about 5 s of context) yields a condition sequence C. The generator is MusicGen-small, and C enters in two ways: frame-wise addition to the decoder input, and a Flamingo-style gated cross-attention in each layer. Only the adapter is trained, 10.4M parameters. Footnote ablations: using MusicGen-small as the encoder lowers F by 0.013 on validation and 0.003 on GTZAN; a from-scratch encoder lowers them by 0.052 and 0.064.

Two training tricks. First, with probability 0.8 the autoregressive context is replaced by a silence token, forcing the model to attend to the song rather than repeat its own rhythm; a footnote says without it validation F drops to 0.31. Second, with probability 0.5 the input is replaced by an HT Demucs drum-removed version, so the model learns to infer the beat from other instruments. Each model trains for 30 epochs on 10 s excerpts with cross-entropy on target EnCodec tokens. At inference it generates 30 s in one pass with greedy decoding and classifier-free guidance at 1.5 to strengthen the condition and prevent rhythmic drift.

Beat readout and baselines. Not every generated hit is on a beat and artifacts occur, so each target gets its own ~143K-parameter TCN (Böck & Davies 2020 architecture, 16 kHz log-mel input) plus DBN, trained on generated tracks for 300 songs. Baselines: Stable Audio ControlNet (frozen Stable Audio Open 1.0 with a conditioning branch copied from its first 12 layers, 561M trainable, same target as Groove sampled); STAGE (released weights, generated to 30 s by repeated 5 s continuation); Beat This! and madmom. All generative models get an identically trained TCN. Training uses Beat This!'s compilation of Ballroom, Hainsworth, RWC and ASAP; evaluation uses a 124-song validation subset and the unseen 999 GTZAN songs, first 30 s only.

Main results (GTZAN). F70: Click 0.851, Groove synth 0.850, Groove sampled 0.849—targets barely matter; Beat This! 0.888, madmom 0.870; Stable Audio ControlNet 0.691, STAGE 0.335. On AMLt, which allows double/half tempo and off-beat, these models score 0.907–0.912, above Beat This!'s 0.895 (madmom's 0.923 is still highest), and AMLc 0.874–0.881 beats Beat This!'s 0.834: the models mostly keep up, with errors mainly in metrical level or phase. The same TCN+DBN on the other two generators gives AMLt of only 0.753 and 0.482, so the DBN does not explain it.

TCN and audio quality. Plain onset peak-picking on the Click track, with no TCN, still reaches F70 0.747. Audio quality (Audiobox Aesthetics PQ): Groove sampled is lowest at 5.93, Stable Audio ControlNet 7.54, STAGE 7.09—the latter two sound more like real playing but often miss the beat or add arbitrary fills.

Drum removal. At inference the input is replaced by its drum-removed version (ASAP, solo piano, is left as is). Since these models saw drum-removed audio in training, a version without that augmentation (w/o DR aug) is also trained for a fair comparison with Beat This! and madmom, which never saw it. From the numbers printed in Figure 3, F70 drops on GTZAN are: Beat This! −0.17, madmom −0.25, this model −0.08, w/o DR aug −0.14, Stable Audio ControlNet −0.25. Even without augmentation it drops less than both conventional models, while the diffusion model, despite the augmentation, drops as much as madmom; the authors credit autoregressive ‘continuation of an established rhythm’.

Onset dependence. Onset strength is log-mel spectral flux, standardized within each song. Annotated beats are split into five groups by the onset strength at that position to compute miss rates; strong peaks at least 140 ms and 0.2 beats from the nearest beat count as ‘off-beat onsets’, also split into five groups, to compute how often they are taken for beats. To avoid octave-error interference, only songs where all four methods' tempo is within ±10% of the annotation are kept (458). On drum-removed metal and disco, this model has the lowest miss rate in every group and its false-positive rate stays low as off-beat onsets grow stronger, while those of Beat This! and madmom rise steeply.

限制Limitations

比的是現成系統，不是架構。 作者承認各生成模型的訓練資料不完全相同：STAGE 只用 214 首（13 小時）且目標是真人鼓 stem，本文用 1,319 首（49 小時），Beat This! 則有 4,556 首（167 小時）。STAGE 的 F70 只有 0.335，很大一部分可能因為它從來就不是為了對準人工拍點而訓練，又被逐段接續生成；放進主表容易讓人誤讀成「自迴歸伴奏模型做不到」。

推論成本沒有報。 可訓練參數 10.4M 看起來很小，但推論要跑凍結的 MusicGen-large（約 3.3B 參數）當編碼器，再由 MusicGen-small 逐幀自迴歸生成 30 秒，加上 CFG 每步要兩次前向。和 madmom 的 0.73M、Beat This! 的 20.3M 比，計算量差了幾個數量級，表 1 只列可訓練參數，讀者看不出這個差距。而且只處理每首前 30 秒，長曲裡速度變化、段落轉換時的穩定度沒有測。

TCN 的對照不完全乾淨。 用來證明「拍點主要來自生成軌」的對照是讓同一個 TCN 直接讀原曲，F70 只有 0.373。但這個 TCN 只在生成的打擊樂上訓練過，讀混音本來就是跨域，0.373 主要反映域差。onset 峰值挑選的 0.747 才是比較有說服力的數字——它說明生成軌本身確實把拍點「寫」得很清楚。

去鼓與 onset 分析的範圍。 去鼓音訊來自 Demucs 分離，殘留與失真和「本來就沒有鼓的音樂」不同，作者也承認這點。onset 分析只在 458 首四個方法速度都對的歌上做，等於先排除了最難的一批；圖 4 只有曲線沒有數值表，metal 與 disco 以外的曲風差距不大。音質只用 PQ 一個自動指標，沒有聽測。

It compares existing systems, not architectures. The authors admit the generative models' training data differ: STAGE used only 214 songs (13 h) with human drum stems as targets, this model 1,319 songs (49 h), and Beat This! 4,556 songs (167 h). STAGE's F70 of 0.335 may largely reflect that it was never trained to align with annotated beats and was generated by segment-wise continuation; putting it in the main table invites the misreading that autoregressive accompaniment models cannot do this.

Inference cost is not reported. 10.4M trainable parameters looks small, but inference runs a frozen MusicGen-large (about 3.3B parameters) as encoder, then MusicGen-small generates 30 s frame by frame, with CFG needing two forward passes per step. Against madmom's 0.73M and Beat This!'s 20.3M, compute differs by orders of magnitude; Table 1 lists only trainable parameters, hiding the gap. And only the first 30 s of each song is processed, so stability through tempo changes and section transitions in long pieces is untested.

The TCN control is not fully clean. The control meant to show that beats come mainly from the generated track has the same TCN read the original song directly, giving F70 of only 0.373. But that TCN was trained only on generated percussion, so reading a full mix is out of domain, and 0.373 mostly reflects the domain gap. The 0.747 from onset peak-picking is the more convincing number—it shows the generated track itself ‘writes’ the beats clearly.

Scope of the drum-removal and onset analyses. Drum-removed audio comes from Demucs separation, whose residue and artifacts differ from music that never had drums, as the authors acknowledge. The onset analysis covers only the 458 songs where all four methods got the tempo right, which excludes the hardest cases up front; Figure 4 gives curves with no numeric table, and genres other than metal and disco show small differences. Audio quality is judged by a single automatic metric, PQ, with no listening test.

討論Discussion

這篇最有意思的不是 F70 0.851——那仍低於 Beat This! 3.7 個百分點，也低於 madmom——而是它換了問題的形狀。把「拍點在哪」改成「跟著演奏一條鼓」之後，錯誤型態變了：AMLt 與 AMLc 比 Beat This! 高、去鼓後掉得少、比較不會被反拍上的強 onset 拉走。這和 2026-11-11 讀過的 GlobalTap 群眾打拍基準呼應：人在沒有明顯鼓聲時仍能穩定打拍，而直接預測的模型常在這裡垮掉。

但要把這個好處歸因給「生成」，證據還不夠。自迴歸延續既是優點也是缺點：作者自己說模型可能只是延續自己的節奏而不聽歌，所以才需要 0.8 機率的靜音 token 與 CFG 1.5 把它拉回條件。去鼓後掉得少，可能是因為穩定，也可能只是比較「固執」；對速度真的會變的音樂（rubato 鋼琴、自由節奏的段落），固執會變成缺點。論文的評估以流行與舞曲為主、只看前 30 秒，這一面還沒被測到。

最該補的對照。 一是 Beat Transformer 這類「先分離再追拍」的方法；二是用同一套去鼓增強重訓的直接預測模型（例如 Beat This!）。目前 w/o DR aug 的比較是把本文的增強拿掉，但沒有反過來給傳統模型同樣的增強；如果 Beat This! 加上去鼓增強就追平，那「生成」本身的貢獻就小得多。

實用面。 輸出一條聽得見的打擊樂軌，對節拍器練習、DJ 對拍、或給人工標註者當參考都有直接用處，這是直接預測模型給不了的。但以現在的計算成本，它更像分析工具而非生產系統。作者提出的按 onset 強度分組的漏拍率與反拍誤報率，本身就值得被其他追拍論文採用，因為 F-measure、CMLt、AMLt 都看不出一個模型是不是只在追 onset。

The most interesting part is not F70 0.851—still 3.7 points below Beat This! and below madmom—but that it changes the shape of the problem. Recasting ‘where are the beats’ as ‘play a drum part along’ changes the error profile: higher AMLt and AMLc than Beat This!, smaller drops without drums, and less pull from strong off-beat onsets. This echoes the GlobalTap crowd-tapping benchmark read on 2026-11-11: people keep a steady beat without obvious drums, which is exactly where direct-prediction models tend to break.

But the evidence is not yet enough to credit this to ‘generation’. Autoregressive continuation cuts both ways: the authors themselves say the model may simply continue its own rhythm without listening, which is why it needs the 0.8 silence-token dropout and CFG at 1.5 to pull it back to the condition. Dropping less without drums could mean stability, or merely stubbornness; on music whose tempo really moves (rubato piano, free-time passages), stubbornness becomes a flaw. The evaluation is mostly pop and dance and uses only the first 30 s, so this side is untested.

Most needed controls. One is a ‘demix, then track’ method like Beat Transformer; the other is a direct-prediction model retrained with the same drum-removal augmentation (e.g. Beat This!). The w/o DR aug comparison removes this paper's augmentation, but never gives the conventional models the same augmentation; if Beat This! with drum-removal augmentation catches up, the contribution of generation itself is much smaller.

Practical side. An audible percussion track is directly useful for metronome practice, DJ beat-matching, or as a reference for human annotators, which direct prediction cannot offer. At current compute cost, though, it is more an analysis tool than a production system. The miss rate and off-beat false-positive rate grouped by onset strength deserve adoption by other beat-tracking papers in their own right, since F-measure, CMLt and AMLt cannot reveal whether a model is just following onsets.

動機Motivation

歌單辨識（setlist identification）要回答的是：一段完整的演唱會錄音裡依序唱了哪些歌、每首從幾分幾秒到幾分幾秒。它是版本辨識（version identification，VI，即翻唱辨識）的一個應用，但條件比標準基準嚴苛得多。作者來自 Nowon，一家做版本辨識與 AI 生成音樂侵權偵測的瑞士公司，列出生產環境的四個難處：音質從廣播級到手機現場錄音都有，夾雜混響、觀眾喧嘩、削波與低頻轟鳴；現場編曲常常移調、變速、加長獨奏、做串燒或刪改段落；一場演唱會 1–3 小時，參考資料庫可能有上百萬首；而且不是丟一段乾淨查詢、回答一首歌，而是要沿著整條時間軸持續產生偵測。

這份報告的目的很單純：在公開的 ASID 基準上用同一套協定比較三個 VI 系統，看近年的片段級嵌入方法在這種條件下到底進步了多少。

Setlist identification answers: in a full concert recording, which songs were performed, in what order, and from when to when. It is an application of version identification (VI, i.e. cover-song identification), under much harsher conditions than standard benchmarks. The authors are from Nowon, a Swiss company working on version identification and copyright-infringement detection for AI-generated music, and list four production challenges: quality ranges from broadcast to phone captures, with reverberation, crowd noise, clipping and low-frequency rumble; live arrangements often change key and tempo, extend solos, form medleys or cut sections; a concert lasts 1–3 hours while the reference database may hold millions of songs; and instead of one clean query answered with one song, detections must be produced continuously along the whole timeline.

The report's aim is simple: compare three VI systems under one protocol on the public ASID benchmark, and see how much recent segment-level embedding methods have improved under these conditions.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

版本辨識的演進。 早期用手工 chroma 加序列對齊：Ellis & Poliner 2007 的拍同步 chroma 互相關、Serrà 等 2008 的 Qmax 局部對齊、Bertin-Mahieux & Ellis 2012 的 2D 傅立葉幅度（2DFTM）。之後轉向深度嵌入加最近鄰檢索：MOVE 與它蒸餾縮小的 Re-MOVE 從 cremaPCP chroma 學固定維度向量；CQTNet、ByteCover 系列、CoverHunter 改架構與損失；CLEWS（Serrà 等，ICML 2025）以弱標註片段做對比學習產生片段級嵌入，再用 bpwr 這類距離縮減處理長度不一的匹配。

歌單辨識本身。 Wang 等 2014（ISMIR）最早把它定義成「整場演唱會影片的歌單辨識與歌曲切分」，在 20 場演唱會、1,152 首錄音室歌曲上評估。Yesiler 等 2021（ICASSP）建立 ASID 資料集與協定：滑動視窗對整個參考庫比對、合併同一首的連續偵測、重疊時保留信心最高者，可選再用 SVM 過濾誤報；當時比較了 Qmax、2DFTM 與 Re-MOVE，Re-MOVE 最好。

這篇跟它們的差別。 方法上沒有新東西——協定完全照 ASID，比較的兩個新系統一個是公開的 CLEWS，一個是不公開的 NAVE。報告的貢獻是資料集重建後的第一份更新數字。值得點出的是：NAVE 的 recognition 打分是 L2 距離加對角帶動態規劃，本質上就是 Serrà 2008 年 Qmax 那類局部對齊，只是把 chroma 換成學出來的片段嵌入。

報告也沒提到另一條路線：用音訊指紋辨識 DJ 混音（例如 Sonnleitner & Widmer 對速度與音高變化穩健的 quad 指紋）。指紋法對同一錄音的重播很有效，但對真正重新演奏的現場版會失效，這正是歌單辨識要用 VI 而不是指紋的理由，報告沒有把這層取捨講清楚。

How VI evolved. Early work used hand-crafted chroma with sequence alignment: Ellis & Poliner 2007's beat-synchronous chroma cross-correlation, Serrà et al. 2008's Qmax local alignment, Bertin-Mahieux & Ellis 2012's 2D Fourier transform magnitude (2DFTM). Then came deep embeddings with nearest-neighbor retrieval: MOVE and its distilled, smaller Re-MOVE learn fixed-size vectors from cremaPCP chroma; CQTNet, the ByteCover series and CoverHunter vary architectures and losses; CLEWS (Serrà et al., ICML 2025) uses contrastive learning on weakly labeled segments to produce segment-level embeddings, then reduces distances with schemes like bpwr for variable-length matching.

Setlist identification itself. Wang et al. 2014 (ISMIR) first framed it as setlist identification and song segmentation for full concert videos, evaluated on 20 concerts and 1,152 studio tracks. Yesiler et al. 2021 (ICASSP) built the ASID dataset and protocol: sliding windows matched against the whole reference set, merging consecutive detections of the same song, keeping the most confident on overlap, with an optional SVM to filter false positives; among Qmax, 2DFTM and Re-MOVE, Re-MOVE was best.

How this report differs. There is no new method—the protocol follows ASID exactly, and the two newer systems are the public CLEWS and the undisclosed NAVE. The contribution is the first updated numbers after rebuilding the dataset. Worth pointing out: NAVE's recognition scoring is L2 distance with diagonal-band dynamic programming, essentially the local alignment of Serrà's 2008 Qmax with chroma replaced by learned segment embeddings.

The report also omits another line of work: audio fingerprinting for DJ-mix identification (e.g. Sonnleitner & Widmer's quad fingerprints, robust to tempo and pitch changes). Fingerprinting works well for replays of the same recording but fails on genuinely re-performed live versions, which is exactly why setlist identification needs VI rather than fingerprints; the report does not spell out this trade-off.

方法Method

資料。 ASID 原始資料當時無法取得，作者重新蒐集音訊並重抽特徵。原本 75 場（10 場開發、65 場評估），有 3 場評估演唱會已找不到，實際評估 62 場。總計約 100 小時、五種曲風（流行 14、搖滾／金屬 21、獨立／另類 15、嘻哈／饒舌 8、電子 7 場），參考庫 1,298 首與演出者相關的錄音室版本，音質三級：AQ-A 廣播／官方 32 場、AQ-B 專業小場地錄音 18 場、AQ-C 手機／相機 15 場。

協定與指標。 120 秒視窗、30 秒跳距掃過整場，每個視窗對所有參考曲打分取最高者，接著合併連續相同的偵測、解決重疊、再合併。DAP 是被偵測到（至少有一段重疊的正確匹配）的標註比例，等於標註層級的召回率，誤報不影響它；DLP 是正確偵測與標註在時間上重疊的長度占總標註長度的比例。另報告後處理後的 TP、FP、FN 數。

三個系統。 Re-MOVE：12 維 cremaPCP 輸入、256 維向量、餘弦距離，用原作者的程式與權重。CLEWS：Zenodo 上的公開權重與官方推論程式，吃原始音訊，每首歌產生多個 1024 維片段嵌入，以正規化平方歐氏距離加 bpwr-5 或 bpwr-10 縮減。NAVE（Neural Audio Version Embeddings）：Nowon 內部系統，256 維 L2 正規化片段嵌入，兩種打分——recognition 是生產用的 L2 距離加對角帶動態規劃；pooled 是把片段嵌入平均成一個向量，類似 Re-MOVE 的消融，用來單看嵌入品質。

附加實驗。 一、ASID 原有的 SVM 二階段過濾只套在 Re-MOVE 上，因為它的特徵與分數分佈是為 Re-MOVE 設計的。二、用 TVSM-pseudo CRNN（逐幀語音／音樂活動偵測）在 NAVE recognition 前做音樂閘控，比較四種策略：overlap_mean（視窗至少 50% 落在音樂遮罩內）、music_beats_speech（同上但要求音樂壓過語音）、conservative_gate（遲滯平滑加最短音樂長度、要求 80% 重疊）、music_island_loose（只保留連續「音樂島」內的視窗）。

主要結果（62 場）。 DAP／DLP／FP：Re-MOVE 重現值 0.779／0.573／1,053（原論文 0.803／0.605）；Re-MOVE 加 SVM 0.601／0.507／115；CLEWS 0.903／0.689／272；NAVE recognition 0.903／0.697／280；NAVE pooled 0.910／0.705／346。片段級嵌入比 Re-MOVE 高約 12 個百分點；pooled 比 recognition 多抓 0.7 個百分點，但 FP 多 24%，作者解讀為動態規劃對齊在扮演隱性的誤報過濾器。

分層結果。 NAVE recognition 依音質：AQ-B 0.971 最高、AQ-A 0.897、AQ-C 手機 0.862。依曲風：嘻哈／饒舌最差（DAP 0.80–0.85、DLP 只有約 0.27）；電子樂上 pooled 0.982 明顯高於 recognition 的 0.873，作者推測對角帶對齊不適合高度重複的結構。閘控：conservative_gate 的 DLP 0.707 最高、FP 287；music_island_loose 太激進，DAP 降到 0.839。儲存估算：5 分鐘參考曲在 20 秒視窗、5 秒跳距下有 57 個片段，百萬首歌 CLEWS 約 233 GB、NAVE 約 58 GB、Re-MOVE 約 1 GB。

Data. The original ASID data was unavailable, so the authors re-collected audio and re-extracted features. Of the original 75 concerts (10 development, 65 evaluation), 3 evaluation concerts could no longer be found, leaving 62. In total about 100 hours across five genres (pop 14, rock/metal 21, indie/alternative 15, hip-hop/rap 8, electronic 7 concerts), 1,298 studio reference songs associated with the performers, and three quality tiers: AQ-A broadcast/official, 32 concerts; AQ-B professional small-venue, 18; AQ-C phone/camera, 15.

Protocol and metrics. A 120 s window with 30 s hop sweeps each concert; each window is scored against all references and the top one kept; consecutive identical detections are merged, overlaps resolved, then merged again. DAP is the fraction of annotations detected (at least one overlapping correct match), i.e. annotation-level recall, unaffected by false positives; DLP is the fraction of total annotated duration overlapped by correct detections. Post-processed TP, FP and FN counts are also reported.

Three systems. Re-MOVE: 12-dim cremaPCP input, 256-dim vector, cosine distance, using the original code and weights. CLEWS: public Zenodo weights and official inference code, raw audio input, several 1024-dim segment embeddings per song, normalized squared Euclidean distance reduced with bpwr-5 or bpwr-10. NAVE (Neural Audio Version Embeddings): Nowon's internal system with 256-dim L2-normalized segment embeddings and two scorers—recognition, the production L2 distance with diagonal-band dynamic programming; and pooled, which averages segment embeddings into one vector, a Re-MOVE-like ablation to test embedding quality alone.

Extra experiments. First, ASID's original SVM second stage is applied only to Re-MOVE, since its features and score distributions were designed for Re-MOVE. Second, a TVSM-pseudo CRNN (frame-level speech/music activity detection) gates NAVE recognition windows, with four strategies: overlap_mean (at least 50% of the window in the music mask), music_beats_speech (same, but music must dominate speech), conservative_gate (hysteresis smoothing, minimum music duration, 80% overlap required), and music_island_loose (keep only windows inside contiguous ‘music islands’).

Main results (62 concerts). DAP/DLP/FP: Re-MOVE reproduced 0.779/0.573/1,053 (original paper 0.803/0.605); Re-MOVE with SVM 0.601/0.507/115; CLEWS 0.903/0.689/272; NAVE recognition 0.903/0.697/280; NAVE pooled 0.910/0.705/346. Segment-level embeddings beat Re-MOVE by about 12 points; pooled catches 0.7 points more than recognition but with 24% more FPs, which the authors read as the DP alignment acting as an implicit false-positive filter.

Breakdowns. NAVE recognition by quality: AQ-B highest at 0.971, AQ-A 0.897, AQ-C phone 0.862. By genre: hip-hop/rap is worst (DAP 0.80–0.85, DLP only about 0.27); on electronic, pooled's 0.982 clearly exceeds recognition's 0.873, which the authors attribute to diagonal-band alignment suiting highly repetitive structure poorly. Gating: conservative_gate has the highest DLP, 0.707, with 287 FPs; music_island_loose is too aggressive, dropping DAP to 0.839. Storage: a 5-minute reference gives 57 segments with 20 s windows and 5 s hop; a million songs take about 233 GB for CLEWS, 58 GB for NAVE, and 1 GB for Re-MOVE.

限制Limitations

NAVE 是黑箱。 報告沒有說明 NAVE 的架構、訓練資料、損失函數或訓練規模，也沒有釋出權重。讀者無法判斷它比 CLEWS 好的那一點點來自模型、訓練資料（例如是否見過現場錄音、是否和 ASID 參考庫有重疊），還是打分方式。在這種情況下，「NAVE 勝過 CLEWS」這個結論無法被獨立檢驗。

差距落在雜訊內。 NAVE recognition 與 CLEWS 的 DAP 同為 0.903，DLP 0.697 對 0.689，FN 105 對 109，FP 還多 8 個。結論說 NAVE「在偵測準確度上改進了 CLEWS」，但 DAP 完全相同，差別只在 DLP 的 0.8 個百分點，且只有單次執行、沒有信賴區間或顯著性檢定。作者自己在限制裡也承認 DAP 已接近飽和，小差距難以解讀。真正站得住的是 4 倍的儲存差距，那是由維度算出來的，不需要實驗。

數字有對不上的地方。 依 DAP =（標註數 − FN）／標註數反推，Re-MOVE 與 NAVE 各列推得的標註總數都在約 1,080–1,090，但 CLEWS 那列 DAP 0.903、FN 109 推出來約 1,120。表 3 依音質分層的 TP 加總是 958，不是表 2 的 973；演唱會數加總 61，不是 62。可能是計數口徑不同或有一場未分類，但報告沒有說明。

閘控沒有達到宣稱的目的。 閘控的目標是壓低非音樂片段（掌聲、說話、靜音）的誤報，但除了把 DAP 拉低 6.4 個百分點的 music_island_loose，其他三種策略的 FP 都比不過濾還多（287–331 對 280）。報告把閘控的價值轉到「邊界更準」，但證據只有一場 Adele 演唱會的兩張圖，沒有量化。

NAVE is a black box. The report gives no architecture, training data, loss or training scale for NAVE, and releases no weights. Readers cannot tell whether its slight edge over CLEWS comes from the model, the training data (e.g. whether it saw live recordings, or overlaps with ASID's references), or the scoring. Under these conditions, ‘NAVE beats CLEWS’ cannot be independently verified.

The gap is within noise. NAVE recognition and CLEWS both have DAP 0.903, DLP 0.697 vs 0.689, FN 105 vs 109, and NAVE has 8 more FPs. The conclusion says NAVE ‘improves the public CLEWS checkpoint in detection accuracy’, but DAP is identical; the only difference is 0.8 points of DLP, from a single run with no confidence interval or significance test. The authors themselves concede in their limitations that DAP is near saturation and small differences are hard to interpret. What does hold up is the 4× storage gap, which follows from the dimensions and needs no experiment.

Some numbers do not reconcile. Back-solving DAP = (annotations − FN)/annotations, the Re-MOVE and NAVE rows each imply about 1,080–1,090 annotations, but the CLEWS row (DAP 0.903, FN 109) implies about 1,120. Table 3's per-quality TPs sum to 958, not Table 2's 973, and its concert counts sum to 61, not 62. This may be a counting convention or an unclassified concert, but the report does not say.

Gating does not achieve its stated goal. Gating is meant to cut false positives in non-music segments (applause, speech, silence), yet apart from music_island_loose, which lowers DAP by 6.4 points, all three other strategies produce more FPs than no filtering (287–331 vs 280). The report shifts gating's value to ‘more accurate boundaries’, but the evidence is two figures from one Adele concert, with no quantification.

討論Discussion

這份報告的價值在於它是少數把翻唱辨識放回「一整場、很吵、邊界不明」條件下的測量。最有資訊量的其實不是排名而是分層數字：嘻哈的 DLP 只有約 0.27，表示就算認出是哪首歌，時間範圍也大多不對——以旋律與和聲為主的嵌入，對以人聲節奏為主的饒舌現場本來就吃力。手機錄音（AQ-C 0.862）比專業錄音（AQ-B 0.971）低約 11 個百分點，而廣播錄音反而不是最容易的，作者猜是廣播混音有更多觀眾聲與口白，但沒有驗證。

報告提出的評估問題值得認真看：DAP 只要求「有一段重疊」，DLP 也只看覆蓋長度，兩者都不直接懲罰邊界偏移，或把相鄰兩首歌接成一首。當 DAP 已經到 0.90，下一步需要的是邊界層級的指標，例如以容許窗計算的切分邊界 F 值（音樂結構分析早就這樣做），再搭配每小時誤報數這類部署導向的量。

我會想看的補充。 一、把 CLEWS 的 1024 維嵌入用 PCA 或乘積量化壓到 256 維再比一次——如果壓縮後 DAP 也不掉，NAVE 的儲存優勢就不是模型本身的功勞，而是任何片段級嵌入都拿得到的工程收益。二、補一個真正大規模的干擾庫：報告的主要動機之一是百萬級規模，但實驗的參考庫只有 1,298 首、且都是同一批演出者的歌，檢索難度比生產環境低很多。三、把 NAVE 公開到可重現的程度，否則這類報告比較像產品技術說明，而非可被引用的比較。

總的來說，它給實務者的結論是：在現場音訊上，從 chroma 向量換到片段級學習嵌入，召回率大約能多 12 個百分點；而內部系統與最新公開模型之間的差距已經很小。

The report's value is that it is one of the few measurements of version identification under ‘whole concert, noisy, unclear boundaries’ conditions. The most informative part is not the ranking but the breakdowns: hip-hop's DLP of about 0.27 means that even when the song is recognized, its time span is mostly wrong—melody- and harmony-oriented embeddings naturally struggle with rap performances driven by vocal rhythm. Phone recordings (AQ-C 0.862) trail professional ones (AQ-B 0.971) by about 11 points, and broadcasts are not the easiest; the authors guess broadcast mixes carry more crowd noise and commentary, but do not verify it.

The evaluation issue the report raises deserves attention: DAP only requires ‘some overlap’, and DLP only measures coverage, so neither directly penalizes boundary shifts or two adjacent songs merged into one. With DAP already at 0.90, the next step needs boundary-level metrics, such as segmentation boundary F-measure within a tolerance window (long standard in music structure analysis), plus deployment-oriented quantities such as false positives per hour.

What I would want added. First, compress CLEWS's 1024-dim embeddings to 256 dims with PCA or product quantization and compare again—if DAP holds, NAVE's storage advantage is not the model's doing but an engineering gain any segment-level embedding can get. Second, a genuinely large distractor set: million-scale catalogs are a main motivation, yet the reference set has only 1,298 songs, all by the same performers, which makes retrieval much easier than in production. Third, open NAVE up enough to be reproducible; otherwise reports like this read more as product documentation than as citable comparisons.

Overall, its message for practitioners is: on live audio, moving from chroma vectors to segment-level learned embeddings buys roughly 12 points of recall, while the gap between internal systems and the latest public model is already small.

I picked it because whether a non-Transformer backbone can serve as a music foundation model is worth following, but these experiments cannot answer it: all eight downstream tasks use roughly 20M-parameter models trained from scratch without the pretrained weights, the vocal-technique and singer tasks even take CREPE and WavLM embeddings as input, and they sit in the same table as frozen-probe scores of MERT and MuQ. Soft ROSA raises pretraining validation accuracy from 93.3% to 94.8%, at the cost of time per sample going from 3.32 s to 9.49 s.

動機Motivation

MERT、MusicFM、MuQ 這幾個近年的音樂基礎模型都建立在 Transformer 上。作者指出兩個成本問題：自注意力的計算量隨序列長度平方成長，推論時的 KV 快取記憶體也隨長度線性成長。對幾分鐘長的整首歌、又用高時間解析度的音訊 tokenizer 時，序列會非常長，這兩項就成了瓶頸。

RWKV 是兼具 Transformer 式平行訓練與 RNN 式線性時間推論的架構，在語言模型上已有和 Transformer 相近的表現。音樂上，MIDI-RWKV 已經把 RWKV-7 用在符號音樂補全，但那是生成離散事件，不是學可遷移的音訊表徵。作者想回答的是：RWKV 能不能當自監督音樂表徵學習的骨幹？

第二個動機是多模態。音樂資訊散在波形、頻譜與詮釋資料（曲風、藝人、歌詞）裡，Transformer 模型常用 cross-attention 融合，而 RWKV 沒有現成的 cross-attention，怎麼融合是個開放問題。

Recent music foundation models such as MERT, MusicFM and MuQ are all built on Transformers. The author points to two costs: self-attention compute grows quadratically with sequence length, and the inference-time KV cache grows linearly with it. For full songs several minutes long, tokenized at high temporal resolution, sequences become very long and both become bottlenecks.

RWKV combines Transformer-style parallel training with RNN-style linear-time inference, and has performed close to Transformers in language modeling. In music, MIDI-RWKV has applied RWKV-7 to symbolic music infilling, but that generates discrete events rather than learning transferable audio representations. The question here is whether RWKV can serve as the backbone for self-supervised music representation learning.

The second motivation is multimodality. Musical information is spread across waveforms, spectrograms and metadata (genre, artist, lyrics); Transformer models often fuse these with cross-attention, but RWKV has no built-in cross-attention, so how to fuse them is an open question.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂表徵學習。 早期以對比學習為主：CLMR（Spijkervet & Burgoyne 2021）、MULE（McCallum 等 2022）。之後是大規模遮罩預測：MERT 結合聲學與音樂教師；MusicFM 探索統一的音樂資訊基礎模型；MuQ 以 Mel 殘差向量量化（Mel-RVQ）為自監督目標；MT2 用多個 class token 做多任務自監督。這些全是 Transformer。

線性時間的音訊骨幹其實不是空白。 論文把相關研究寫成「非 Transformer 架構在音樂表徵上幾乎沒人探索」，但在一般音訊上，用狀態空間模型做表徵早有人做：SSAMBA（Shams 等 2024）是以 Mamba 為骨幹的自監督音訊表徵模型，Audio Mamba（Erol 等 2024）用雙向 Mamba 取代 AST 的注意力做音訊分類，兩者都以「避開平方複雜度」為動機，也都和 Transformer 做了同設定對照。論文只泛稱「state-space 與 recurrent 架構」，沒有點名任何音訊上的例子。MusicRWKV 的「首創」較準確的說法是：第一個以 RWKV（而非 Mamba）做音樂音訊表徵。

最像的先前工作。 我認為是 Castellon 等 2021 的 CALM 研究：它證明 Jukebox 這類在音訊 codec token 上做語言模型的生成模型，中間層探測出的表徵在標記、曲風、調性、情緒上都很強——而 MusicRWKV 評估的正好就是 MTT、GTZAN、GiantSteps、EMO 這一組。MusicRWKV 的預訓練同樣是在 Jukebox token 上做逐 token 交叉熵預測，本質是一個多了頻譜 token 與詮釋資料串流、把 Transformer 換成 RWKV 的 codec 語言模型。差別是 CALM 真的拿預訓練表徵去探測，MusicRWKV 下游卻沒有用預訓練權重。

Soft ROSA 來自一個 GitHub 實作（wjie98/rosa_soft），論文沒有引用任何描述它的論文，只說它對「歷史候選表示」做可微檢索與聚合，可看成免路由器的專家選擇。從它吃 Q、K、V 並帶有 sink 機率的介面看，功能上很接近在 RWKV 層裡插回一個帶 sink 的注意力——也就是把作者想避開的機制換個名字加回來。

Music representation learning. Early work was contrastive: CLMR (Spijkervet & Burgoyne 2021), MULE (McCallum et al. 2022). Then came large-scale masked prediction: MERT combines acoustic and musical teachers; MusicFM explores a unified foundation model for music informatics; MuQ uses Mel residual vector quantization (Mel-RVQ) as its self-supervised target; MT2 uses multiple class tokens for multitask self-supervision. All are Transformers.

Linear-time audio backbones are not uncharted. The paper presents non-Transformer architectures as almost unexplored for music representation, but for general audio, state-space representation models already exist: SSAMBA (Shams et al. 2024) is a self-supervised audio representation model with a Mamba backbone, and Audio Mamba (Erol et al. 2024) replaces AST's attention with bidirectional Mamba for audio classification. Both are motivated by avoiding quadratic complexity, and both compare against Transformers under matched settings. The paper only mentions ‘state-space and recurrent architectures’ generically, naming no audio example. A more accurate version of the ‘first’ claim is: the first to use RWKV (rather than Mamba) for music audio representation.

The closest prior work. I think it is Castellon et al. 2021 (CALM): it showed that probing intermediate layers of Jukebox, a language model over audio codec tokens, yields strong representations for tagging, genre, key and emotion—exactly the MTT, GTZAN, GiantSteps and EMO set MusicRWKV evaluates. MusicRWKV's pretraining is likewise token-wise cross-entropy over Jukebox tokens; in essence a codec language model with extra spectrogram-token and metadata streams and the Transformer swapped for RWKV. The difference is that CALM actually probes the pretrained representations, while MusicRWKV's downstream models do not use the pretrained weights.

Soft ROSA comes from a GitHub implementation (wjie98/rosa_soft); the paper cites no paper describing it, saying only that it performs differentiable retrieval and aggregation over ‘historical candidate representations’ and can be viewed as router-free expert selection. Given an interface that takes Q, K, V and has a sink probability, it functionally resembles inserting an attention with a sink back into the RWKV layers—re-adding, under another name, the mechanism the author set out to avoid.

方法Method

輸入。 三種模態轉成 token：波形用 Jukebox tokenizer 轉成離散 codec token；頻譜用 MuQ 的 Mel-RVQ tokenizer；詮釋資料（曲風、藝人、有的話加歌詞）轉成文字 token。最大長度分別為 20,000、10,000、500。

架構。 波形與頻譜 token 先過一個「可逆壓縮模組」：把相鄰 token 分成固定長度的組、投影到潛在表示，訓練時另有反投影把原序列重建回來；壓縮步幅 10。詮釋資料不壓縮。三個模態各進一個 6 層 RWKV 編碼器，接著用 FiLM（γ⊙H+β，γ、β 由「對應的模態」決定）做調變，再把三路串接後送進 4 層共享 RWKV 融合。隱藏維度 1024，共 883M 可訓練參數，論文說其中相當一部分來自壓縮模組。

目標。 主損失是三個模態各自的交叉熵預測損失相加，再加上 λ 權重的重建正則項（波形與頻譜嵌入與其重建的 L2 距離）。論文沒有說明預測的是下一個 token 還是被遮罩的 token，也沒給 λ 的值。

Soft ROSA 變體。 在各模態的 RWKV 編碼器裡插入 Soft ROSA，對 Q、K、V 做「結構化候選匹配與聚合」，輸出再傳給後面的 RWKV 層。除此之外兩個模型設定完全相同。

預訓練。 約 25,000 首從網路蒐集的公開完整錄音，盡量平衡曲風，以曲風分層 80／20 切訓練與驗證，並排除與下游資料重疊的錄音。Adam、初始學習率 1e-3、批次 4、400 個 epoch、ReduceLROnPlateau，以最低驗證損失選檢查點。

下游——這是最關鍵的一點。 論文說直接把 883M 的預訓練模型遷移到下游會嚴重過擬合，所以每個任務改訓練一個隱藏維度 512、2–6 層、約 19.7M–21.5M 參數的小模型，從頭訓練、不載入預訓練權重。輸入也依任務換：MTT、GTZAN、EMO 用波形加頻譜；GiantSteps 調性用諧波 Mel 頻譜；NSynth 樂器用頻譜、音高用諧波 CQT；VocalSet 唱法用 CREPE 音高嵌入、歌手辨識用 WavLM 說話人嵌入。

下游結果。 與 MARBLE 或原論文報告的數字並列：MTT ROC-AUC 88.6（MERT 90.6、MuQ 90.5、MERT2-30s 91.9）、AP 46.1（表上最高，次高 MERT2-30s 41.3）；GiantSteps 調性 70.3（MERT2-FS 67.1）；GTZAN 86.6（MERT2-30s 91.7、MusicFM 84.1）；EMO valence R² 62.1、arousal 75.1（MERT2-FS 63.5、MERT2-30s 80.0）；NSynth 樂器 73.3、音高 87.4（MERT 72.6、94.4）；VocalSet 唱法 89.1、歌手 83.0（MERT 76.9、87.1）。

Soft ROSA 消融與遙測。 參數 883M→952.66M、峰值記憶體 64.90→69.80 GiB、每樣本時間 3.32→9.49 秒，驗證準確率 93.30%→94.82%。逐層遙測（第 0–5 層）：最大路由機率從 0.31 升到 0.84、正規化熵從 0.73 降到 0.18、候選質量（1 − sink 機率）從 0.86 降到 0.58，作者解讀為從分散檢索轉向選擇性檢索。

Inputs. Three modalities become tokens: waveforms via the Jukebox tokenizer into discrete codec tokens; spectrograms via MuQ's Mel-RVQ tokenizer; metadata (genre, artist, lyrics when available) as text tokens. Maximum lengths are 20,000, 10,000 and 500.

Architecture. Waveform and spectrogram tokens first pass an ‘invertible compression module’ that groups adjacent tokens into fixed-length segments and projects them to a latent, with an inverse projection reconstructing the original sequence during training; the stride is 10. Metadata is not compressed. Each modality goes through its own 6-layer RWKV encoder, then FiLM modulation (γ⊙H+β, with γ and β determined by ‘the corresponding modality’), then the three streams are concatenated into a 4-layer shared RWKV for fusion. Hidden size is 1024, with 883M trainable parameters, a substantial share of which, the paper says, sits in the compression modules.

Objective. The main loss sums per-modality cross-entropy prediction losses, plus a λ-weighted reconstruction regularizer (L2 distance between waveform/spectrogram embeddings and their reconstructions). The paper does not say whether it predicts next tokens or masked tokens, nor give λ.

Soft ROSA variant. Soft ROSA is inserted into each modality's RWKV encoder, doing ‘structured candidate matching and aggregation’ over Q, K, V, and its output feeds the remaining RWKV layers. Otherwise the two models are configured identically.

Pretraining. About 25,000 publicly accessible full-length recordings collected online, genre-balanced where possible, split 80/20 with genre stratification, excluding recordings overlapping downstream data. Adam, initial learning rate 1e-3, batch size 4, 400 epochs, ReduceLROnPlateau, checkpoint chosen by lowest validation loss.

Downstream—the crucial point. The paper says transferring the 883M pretrained model directly caused severe overfitting, so each task instead trains a small model with hidden size 512, 2–6 layers and about 19.7M–21.5M parameters, from scratch, without loading pretrained weights. Inputs also change per task: waveform plus spectrogram for MTT, GTZAN and EMO; harmonic Mel spectrograms for GiantSteps key; spectrograms for NSynth instrument and harmonic CQT for pitch; CREPE pitch embeddings for VocalSet technique and WavLM speaker embeddings for singer ID.

Downstream results. Alongside numbers from MARBLE or original papers: MTT ROC-AUC 88.6 (MERT 90.6, MuQ 90.5, MERT2-30s 91.9), AP 46.1 (highest in the table; next is MERT2-30s at 41.3); GiantSteps key 70.3 (MERT2-FS 67.1); GTZAN 86.6 (MERT2-30s 91.7, MusicFM 84.1); EMO valence R² 62.1, arousal 75.1 (MERT2-FS 63.5, MERT2-30s 80.0); NSynth instrument 73.3, pitch 87.4 (MERT 72.6, 94.4); VocalSet technique 89.1, singer 83.0 (MERT 76.9, 87.1).

Soft ROSA ablation and telemetry. Parameters 883M→952.66M, peak memory 64.90→69.80 GiB, time per sample 3.32→9.49 s, validation accuracy 93.30%→94.82%. Layer-wise telemetry (layers 0–5): top routing probability rises from 0.31 to 0.84, normalized entropy falls from 0.73 to 0.18, and candidate mass (1 − sink probability) falls from 0.86 to 0.58, which the author reads as a shift from distributed to selective retrieval.

限制Limitations

下游實驗測的不是預訓練表徵。 這是整篇最根本的問題。貢獻第 2 條說證明了 RWKV 可以作為自監督音樂表徵學習的有效骨幹，但八個下游任務全都沒有用預訓練權重——它們是各自從頭訓練的監督式小模型。所以表 3、表 4 測的是「一個 RWKV 分類器在這些資料集上從頭訓練能到多少」，而不是「MusicRWKV 學到的表徵有多好」。預訓練模型唯一的評估是它自己的驗證準確率 93.3%，而這是對什麼的準確率，論文沒有定義清楚。

比較不是同一把尺。 對照組的數字取自 MARBLE 或原論文，大多是凍結表徵加淺層探測；MusicRWKV 則是對每個任務端到端監督訓練、還選了專屬輸入特徵。作者在表註中也說這只是「參考比較」。更嚴重的是部分輸入本身就是其他預訓練模型的產物：唱法分類吃 CREPE 嵌入、歌手辨識吃 WavLM 嵌入。唱法 89.1 對 MERT 76.9 的 12 個百分點，至少有一部分應該記在 CREPE 頭上。

數字內部有疑點。 MTT 上 MusicRWKV 的 ROC-AUC 88.6 是表上倒數第二，AP 46.1 卻高出所有對照 4.8 個百分點以上。如果標籤集合相同，ROC-AUC 偏低而 AP 大幅偏高很不尋常，常見原因是標籤集合、切分或 AP 的平均方式（micro 或 macro）不同，論文都沒有說明。

效率主張沒有被量測。 動機整個建立在「比 Transformer 省」，但沒有任何和同規模 Transformer 的速度或記憶體對照。表 5 反而顯示預訓練峰值記憶體 64.9 GiB、批次只有 4、每個樣本 3.32 秒；加 Soft ROSA 後慢到 2.86 倍。Soft ROSA 的 +1.5 個百分點只有單一種子，沒有變異數。

可重現性。 預訓練資料是「網路上公開可得」的 25,000 首，沒有清單；FiLM 的 γ、β 由誰產生、壓縮模組如何做到「可逆」、λ 是多少都沒寫。程式碼倒是有釋出。

The downstream experiments do not test the pretrained representations. This is the most fundamental problem. Contribution 2 claims to show RWKV is an effective backbone for self-supervised music representation learning, yet none of the eight downstream tasks uses the pretrained weights—each is a small supervised model trained from scratch. So Tables 3 and 4 measure how far an RWKV classifier trained from scratch gets on these datasets, not how good MusicRWKV's learned representations are. The pretrained model's only evaluation is its own validation accuracy of 93.3%, and the paper never clearly defines what that is the accuracy of.

The comparison is not on the same scale. Baseline numbers come from MARBLE or original papers, mostly frozen representations with shallow probes; MusicRWKV is trained end to end per task with task-specific input features. The table note itself calls these ‘reference comparisons’. Worse, some inputs are themselves outputs of other pretrained models: vocal technique takes CREPE embeddings and singer ID takes WavLM embeddings. At least part of the 12-point lead in technique, 89.1 vs MERT's 76.9, belongs to CREPE.

Internal oddities in the numbers. On MTT, MusicRWKV's ROC-AUC of 88.6 is second-lowest in the table, yet its AP of 46.1 exceeds every baseline by at least 4.8 points. If the tag set is the same, low ROC-AUC with much higher AP is unusual; common causes are a different tag set, split, or AP averaging (micro vs macro), none of which the paper states.

The efficiency claim is never measured. The motivation rests entirely on being cheaper than Transformers, but there is no speed or memory comparison against a Transformer of similar size. Table 5 instead shows pretraining peak memory of 64.9 GiB at batch size 4 and 3.32 s per sample; with Soft ROSA it is 2.86 times slower. Soft ROSA's +1.5 points comes from a single seed with no variance.

Reproducibility. The pretraining data is 25,000 ‘publicly accessible’ songs from the internet, with no list; who produces FiLM's γ and β, how the compression module is ‘invertible’, and the value of λ are all unstated. The code, at least, is released.

討論Discussion

「RWKV 能不能當音樂基礎模型的骨幹」是個好問題，時機也合理：音樂錄音動輒幾分鐘，現有模型多半只吃 5–30 秒片段，線性時間的骨幹有機會讓整首歌一次進模型。但這篇提出了問題，卻沒有設計出能回答它的實驗。最小的可行版本其實很直接：把預訓練好的 883M 模型凍結、取各層表徵，照 MARBLE 協定做線性探測，和同資料量、同 token 輸入的 Transformer 基線比；再報告整首歌長度下兩者的記憶體與吞吐量。這兩張表任何一張都比目前的表 3 更能支持結論。

作者提到大模型遷移時嚴重過擬合，這本身是個有意思的負面結果，但更常見的處理是凍結骨幹只訓練探測頭，而不是放棄預訓練。論文沒說有沒有試過凍結探測，這個空白讓讀者無法分辨是表徵本身不好，還是遷移方式的問題。

Soft ROSA 的遙測。 越深層路由越集中（熵 0.73→0.18）在許多架構裡都會出現，單憑這組數字很難推到「神經符號互動」或「免路由器專家選擇」這樣的解讀。它也沒有和控制組比——例如在同位置插一層普通注意力，看是否得到同樣的 +1.5 個百分點。考慮到 Soft ROSA 讓每個樣本變慢 2.86 倍，正是作者想用 RWKV 避掉的成本，這個模組在效率導向的框架裡是否值得，需要更多證據。

可以帶走的。 任務專屬輸入的觀察是務實的：調性用諧波頻譜、唱法用音高嵌入，就能把約 20M 的從頭訓練小模型推到和大型基礎模型探測相近、甚至更高的分數。這反過來提醒我們：MARBLE 上的基礎模型比較也應該附一個「任務專屬特徵＋小模型」的基線，才知道預訓練到底多給了多少。

‘Can RWKV be the backbone of a music foundation model?’ is a good question, and well timed: recordings run several minutes while current models mostly take 5–30 s excerpts, and a linear-time backbone could let a whole song in at once. But this paper raises the question without designing experiments that answer it. The minimal version is straightforward: freeze the pretrained 883M model, take layer-wise representations, run linear probes under the MARBLE protocol, and compare with a Transformer baseline on the same data and token inputs; then report memory and throughput for both at full-song length. Either table would support the conclusion better than the current Table 3.

The author notes that transferring the large model overfit badly. That is an interesting negative result in itself, but the usual remedy is to freeze the backbone and train only a probe, not to abandon pretraining. The paper does not say whether frozen probing was tried, a gap that leaves readers unable to tell whether the representations are poor or the transfer method is.

Soft ROSA telemetry. Routing that concentrates with depth (entropy 0.73→0.18) appears in many architectures, and these numbers alone hardly support readings like ‘neural-symbolic interaction’ or ‘router-free expert selection’. There is also no control—say, inserting a plain attention layer at the same position to see whether it yields the same +1.5 points. Given that Soft ROSA makes each sample 2.86 times slower, precisely the cost RWKV was chosen to avoid, whether the module is worth it in an efficiency-driven framework needs more evidence.

What to take away. The observation about task-specific inputs is practical: harmonic spectrograms for key and pitch embeddings for vocal technique push a ~20M model trained from scratch to scores close to, or even above, probes of large foundation models. In turn, this suggests foundation-model comparisons on MARBLE should include a ‘task-specific features plus small model’ baseline, to show how much pretraining actually adds.
