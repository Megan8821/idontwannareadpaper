Does Notation Matter? MusicXML Tokenisation for Symbolic Music Generation and Understanding

記譜重要嗎？為符號音樂生成與理解設計的 MusicXML 斷詞方法

Andrea Poltronieri, Matteo Spanio, Simone Chieppa, Xavier Serra, Martín Rocamora
arXiv:2610.34247 · cs.SD; cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-10-25
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇提出 MxlTok，一套保留同音異名拼寫、力度、奏法記號、和弦標記與聲部結構的 MusicXML 斷詞（tokenisation）框架，並在 13K 份 PDMX 樂譜上訓練 26 個約 20M 參數的 GPT-2／BERT，對照 REMI+、Octuple 與當純文字處理的 ABC。線性探針下，measure-first 版的作曲家辨識準確率 0.781（REMI+ 最佳 0.748）、伴奏建議 MAP 0.815（REMI+ 最佳 0.726）。最有說服力的是一組消融：先把樂譜轉成 MIDI 再轉回來，measure-first 的作曲家優勢就掉到 REMI+ 的水準（0.735 對 0.734–0.743）。生成端 JS 分數 89.2 對 81.5、16 人聽測也排第一，但前幾名的偏好差距大約只在一個標準誤（約 0.34）之內。Why this one: This paper introduces MxlTok, a MusicXML tokeniser that keeps enharmonic spelling, dynamics, articulations, chord symbols and part structure, and trains 26 GPT-2/BERT models of about 20M parameters on 13K PDMX scores against REMI+, Octuple and ABC treated as plain text. Under linear probing, the measure-first variant reaches 0.781 composer accuracy (best REMI+ 0.748) and 0.815 accompaniment-suggestion MAP (best REMI+ 0.726). The most convincing piece is an ablation: once scores are round-tripped through MIDI, the measure-first composer advantage falls to REMI+ level (0.735 vs 0.734–0.743). On generation it leads the JS score (89.2 vs 81.5) and ranks first in a 16-listener study, but the top preference gaps sit within roughly one standard error (about 0.34).

動機Motivation

符號音樂的深度學習幾乎都建立在 MIDI 上，而 MIDI 是「演奏了什麼」的描述式格式：升 C 與降 D 會被壓成同一個音高、聲部與譜表的階層被攤平成事件串、奏法與力度只能從力度值和時值反推。樂譜格式則是「應該怎麼演奏」的規範式格式，這些資訊都是明寫的。

作者要問的是：這些額外的記譜資訊，對模型到底有沒有可量測的好處？過去 MIDI 的斷詞方式已經被系統比較過好幾輪，樂譜格式卻幾乎沒有，MusicXML 雖然是樂譜交換的事實標準，在 MIR 裡卻很少被直接拿來訓練模型。作者指出，在他們用的 PDMX 子集裡，53% 的樂譜帶有力度記號、50% 帶有奏法記號、61% 至少有一個音的拼寫無法從音高類別還原，所以這不是邊角資訊。

Deep learning on symbolic music is built almost entirely on MIDI, a descriptive format recording what was played: C-sharp and D-flat collapse into one pitch, the hierarchy of parts and staves is flattened into event streams, and articulations and dynamics can only be inferred back from velocity and duration. Score formats are prescriptive and state all of this explicitly.

The authors ask whether this extra notational information gives models a measurable benefit. MIDI tokenisation has been compared systematically several times, score formats hardly at all, and MusicXML, the de facto standard for score interchange, is rarely used directly for training in MIR. In the PDMX subset they use, 53% of scores carry dynamics, 50% carry articulations and 61% have at least one note whose spelling cannot be recovered from pitch class, so this is not marginal information.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

MIDI 斷詞這條線有清楚的系譜：REMI（Huang 與 Yang，2020）加入小節與時值 token，REMI+（FIGARO，von Rütte 等人）擴充到多軌，Octuple（MusicBERT，Zeng 等人，2021）把一個音的多個屬性併成一個複合嵌入，MidiTok（Fradet 等人）把這些統一成一個函式庫，Fradet 等人後來又顯示在 MIDI token 上做 BPE 對生成與理解都有幫助。這篇的框架設計明白地照搬 MidiTok 的介面，差別在於底層文法來自 MusicXML 的樹，而不是 MIDI 事件。

樂譜這邊，NotaGen 與 MuPT 都用 ABC 記譜，但把它當純文字丟進通用的子詞斷詞器，「c''」這樣的字元組合才隱含一個音高。最接近 MxlTok 的是 Mayer 等人的 LMX：它為光學樂譜辨識（OMR）把 MusicXML 樹深度優先線性化，但丟掉力度、踏板，不支援多聲部總譜與反覆記號。MxlTok 本質上就是把 LMX 擴充到完整記譜元素，再加上 BPE 與兩種序列化順序；論文自己也這樣描述。另一個論文沒提的近親是端到端 OMR 常用的 Humdrum kern 系編碼（例如 Ríos-Vila 等人的 Sheet Music Transformer），同樣是保留記譜資訊的 token 文法。

MIDI tokenisation has a clear lineage: REMI (Huang and Yang, 2020) added bar and duration tokens, REMI+ (FIGARO, von Rütte et al.) extended it to multitrack, Octuple (MusicBERT, Zeng et al., 2021) pooled a note's attributes into one compound embedding, MidiTok (Fradet et al.) unified these in one library, and Fradet et al. later showed that BPE on MIDI tokens helps both generation and understanding. This framework copies MidiTok's interface openly; the difference is that the underlying grammar comes from the MusicXML tree rather than MIDI events.

On the score side, NotaGen and MuPT both use ABC notation but feed it to generic subword tokenisers as plain text, so a pitch is only implied by a character sequence such as c''. The closest relative of MxlTok is LMX by Mayer et al., a depth-first linearisation of the MusicXML tree for optical music recognition (OMR) that drops dynamics and pedalling and supports neither multi-part scores nor repeats. MxlTok is essentially LMX extended to the full set of notational elements, plus BPE and two serialisation orders; the paper describes it that way itself. A relative the paper does not mention is the Humdrum kern family of encodings common in end-to-end OMR (for example Ríos-Vila et al.'s Sheet Music Transformer), another token grammar that keeps notation.

方法Method

每個音寫成一組有序 token：前綴（裝飾音、和弦內音等情境）、根 token（音符類型與時值，例如四分音符），以及後綴（附點、連結線、力度、奏法）。聲部、符桿方向與譜表只在數值改變時才輸出（delta 編碼），也支援微分音。基本詞彙約 750 個 token，MIDI 斷詞器通常是 200–400 個。序列化有兩種：part-first（PF，對應 score-partwise，一個樂器的所有小節排完再換下一個）與 measure-first（MF，對應 score-timewise，同一小節裡所有聲部排在一起）。兩者都無損，只是給模型的歸納偏置不同。BPE 試了 5k 與 10k 兩種詞彙量。

實驗設計是 26 個從頭訓練的模型：GPT-2 負責生成、BERT 負責理解，都是 6 層、8 個注意力頭、512 維、約 20M 參數，訓練資料是 PDMX 的 Rated and Deduplicated 子集（13K 份）。對照組是 REMI+、Octuple（MIDI 版與 MusicXML 版）和以 HuggingFace BPE 處理的 ABC。

理解任務用凍結的 BERT 嵌入加兩層線性探針：KernScores 的 1,968 份樂譜做 20 位作曲家分類（5-fold），CIPI 的 652 首鋼琴曲做 9 級難度估計，Wikifonia 的 5,000 份旋律譜做伴奏建議檢索（把旋律與伴奏拆開，配對正確者為正例）。生成任務用複音率、音階一致性、律動一致性三個分布的 Jensen–Shannon 距離合成 JS 分數，每個模型 20 個提示各生 10 首；另做成對偏好聽測，16 人、960 個判斷，用 Bradley–Terry 模型轉成分數。

Each note is an ordered group of tokens: prefixes for context (grace note, chord member), a root token for note type and duration (for example quarter), and suffixes for notational modifiers (dot, tie, dynamics, articulation). Voice, stem and staff are emitted only when they change (delta encoding), and microtonal alterations are supported. The base vocabulary is about 750 tokens, against 200–400 for typical MIDI tokenisers. There are two serialisation orders: part-first (PF, mirroring score-partwise, all measures of one instrument before the next) and measure-first (MF, mirroring score-timewise, all parts of a measure together). Both are lossless and differ only in inductive bias. BPE is tried at 5k and 10k.

The study trains 26 models from scratch: GPT-2 for generation and BERT for understanding, both 6 layers, 8 heads, 512 dimensions, about 20M parameters, on the Rated and Deduplicated subset of PDMX (13K scores). Baselines are REMI+, Octuple (MIDI and MusicXML versions) and ABC tokenised with HuggingFace BPE.

Understanding uses frozen BERT embeddings with a two-layer linear probe: 20-composer classification on 1,968 KernScores scores (5-fold), 9-level difficulty estimation on the 652 piano pieces of CIPI, and accompaniment-suggestion retrieval on 5,000 Wikifonia lead sheets (melody and accompaniment split, matching pairs as positives). Generation is scored by a JS score combining Jensen–Shannon distances of polyphony rate, scale consistency and groove consistency, with 10 outputs for each of 20 prompts per model, plus a pairwise preference study with 16 listeners and 960 judgments converted to Bradley–Terry scores.

限制Limitations

第一，表 3 的說明寫「所有標準差都小於 0.05」所以省略，但很多關鍵差距本身就在這個範圍內：作曲家分類 MF 0.781 對 REMI+ 0.748 差 0.033，難度的 ±1 準確率 MF 10k 0.695 對 REMI+ 無 BPE 0.667 差 0.028。上界 0.05 不等於實際標準差，但讀者無法判斷哪些差距穩定。而且優勢並非每個 BPE 設定都成立：在 ±1 準確率上，REMI+ 無 BPE（0.667）高於 MF 無 BPE（0.619）與 MF 5k（0.650）。

第二，消融只做在作曲家分類上，而且結論比數字寬鬆。論文說 MIDI 來源的 MxlTok「追平」MIDI 模型，但這只對 MF 成立（0.735 對 0.734–0.743）；PF 掉到 0.672–0.678，比 REMI+ 低約 6 個百分點。

第三，「音樂文法感知的斷詞 vs 當純文字」與「MusicXML vs ABC」這兩個問題被同一組對照混在一起：ABC 只試了通用 BPE，沒有任何依照 ABC 文法切的版本，所以 ABC 墊底（伴奏 MAP 最高 0.602）無法歸因到格式還是斷詞方式。

第四，聽測規模小：16 人、每個模型約 21 個片段，Bradley–Terry 分數的標準誤約 0.34。PF 無 BPE 的整體偏好 0.81、MF 無 BPE 0.60、REMI+ 無 BPE 0.51，彼此都在誤差範圍內。聽測是合成音訊，論文沒說力度與奏法記號有沒有被渲染出來，而這正是 MusicXML 多出來的資訊。

First, Table 3 omits standard deviations because all are below 0.05, yet many key gaps fall inside that range: MF beats REMI+ on composer accuracy by 0.033 (0.781 vs 0.748), and MF 10k beats no-BPE REMI+ on ±1 difficulty accuracy by 0.028 (0.695 vs 0.667). An upper bound of 0.05 is not the actual deviation, but readers cannot tell which gaps are stable. Nor does the advantage hold at every BPE setting: on ±1 accuracy, no-BPE REMI+ (0.667) is above no-BPE MF (0.619) and MF 5k (0.650).

Second, the ablation covers composer classification only, and its conclusion is looser than its numbers. The paper says MIDI-derived MxlTok models match their MIDI counterparts, but that holds only for MF (0.735 vs 0.734–0.743); PF drops to 0.672–0.678, about 6 points below REMI+.

Third, two questions are tested by one contrast: music-grammar-aware versus plain-text tokenisation, and MusicXML versus ABC. ABC is only tried with generic BPE, never with a grammar-aware split, so its last place (best accompaniment MAP 0.602) cannot be attributed to the format or to the tokenisation.

Fourth, the listening study is small: 16 listeners, about 21 clips per model, and Bradley–Terry standard errors around 0.34. Overall preference for no-BPE PF (0.81), no-BPE MF (0.60) and no-BPE REMI+ (0.51) all overlap within error. Stimuli are synthesised, and the paper does not say whether dynamics and articulations were rendered, which is exactly the information MusicXML adds.

討論Discussion

最乾淨的結論有兩個。一是 ABC 加通用 BPE 確實明顯落後：伴奏檢索 MAP 0.556–0.602 對 MxlTok 的 0.777–0.815，JS 分數 50.6–56.0 對 78.0–89.2，偏好分數也墊底（無 BPE −1.40）。二是作曲家分類的消融：MF 的優勢在樂譜先經過 MIDI 之後消失，這比「MusicXML 模型分數比較高」更接近因果證據，說明差異來自 MIDI 丟掉的那些記譜內容。

伴奏建議的差距最大（MAP 0.815 對 0.726、H@1 0.702 對 0.579），但我會想先問一件論文沒交代的事：Wikifonia 是帶和弦標記的旋律譜，拆成旋律與伴奏之後，和弦標記留在哪一邊？如果留在旋律那邊，MusicXML 模型等於直接看到了和聲，而 MIDI 那邊沒有。這仍然算是「記譜有用」，但是很窄的一種。

生成端的兩把尺彼此不一致：聽眾最偏好的 PF 無 BPE，在 MusicXML 設定裡 JS 分數最低（78.0），還低於 REMI+ 10k（81.5）。BPE 也一樣：5k 在 JS 上普遍最好，聽測裡三種格式卻都是無 BPE 勝出。作者把 BPE 失效歸因於整段序列做 BPE、在提示邊界切壞合併，但沒有實驗驗證。這和本站 2026-08-20 收錄的那篇（2608.18025）結論同方向：BPE 縮短序列不等於降低預測難度。

本站 2026-08-26 收錄的 MusPyExpress（2608.21678）走的是另一條路：在 MIDI 式事件上加入表情文字 token，同樣用約 20M 參數的模型與 PDMX。把它或 Zhang 等人「REMI 加調號與聲部 token」的做法拿來當對照，才能把「資訊量」和「文法」兩件事分開，這是這篇最缺的一組實驗。實用面上，公開的 7 個 MusicXML 預訓練模型與函式庫本身就有價值。

Two conclusions are clean. One is that ABC with generic BPE clearly lags: accompaniment MAP 0.556–0.602 against 0.777–0.815 for MxlTok, JS scores of 50.6–56.0 against 78.0–89.2, and the lowest preference scores (−1.40 without BPE). The other is the composer ablation: the MF advantage disappears once scores pass through MIDI, which is closer to causal evidence than higher MusicXML scores alone, and shows the gap comes from the notational content MIDI discards.

Accompaniment suggestion shows the largest gap (MAP 0.815 vs 0.726, H@1 0.702 vs 0.579), but I would first ask something the paper does not state: Wikifonia consists of lead sheets with chord symbols, so after splitting melody from accompaniment, which side keeps the chord symbols? If they stay with the melody, the MusicXML model sees the harmony outright while the MIDI side does not. That still counts as notation helping, but in a narrow sense.

The two generative yardsticks disagree. The listeners' favourite, no-BPE PF, has the lowest JS score among MusicXML settings (78.0), below REMI+ 10k (81.5). BPE behaves the same way: 5k is generally best on JS, yet listeners prefer no BPE for all three formats. The authors attribute this to applying BPE over whole sequences and breaking merges at the prompt boundary, without testing it. This points the same way as the paper featured here on 2026-08-20 (2608.18025): a shorter sequence after BPE is not an easier prediction problem.

MusPyExpress (2608.21678, featured 2026-08-26) took another route, adding expression-text tokens to MIDI-style events, also with a roughly 20M-parameter model on PDMX. Using it, or Zhang et al.'s REMI with key-signature and voice tokens, as a baseline would separate information content from grammar, and that is the experiment this paper most lacks. Practically, the library and the seven released MusicXML-pretrained models are useful in their own right.

JIT-MPE: Just-in-Time Multi-Pitch Estimation under Output Deadlines

JIT-MPE：在輸出期限內交卷的即時多音高估計

Yoshiyuki Kobayashi
arXiv:2610.33113 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-23
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇單一作者的論文做了一個 86k 參數的因果式多音高估計（MPE）模型，適用於混合編制合奏：每個 20 ms 的影格先立即給一個估計，再隨著多到 20、40、80、160 ms 的音訊重新估計同一格。更重要的是評估方式：每個輸出只在「當時的音訊確實算得出來」的期限上計分。在 50 ms 期限下，它在 45 組配對比較中有 38 組顯著高於六個已發表離線 MPE 系統中的五個（只有兩組較低，都在 BSED-real）；在四個保留測試集上，與自己 160 ms 前瞻的最終輸出相差 −1.27 到 +0.60 個 F1 點；AAM 上音符起始後頭 20 ms 的召回率從 9.8% 提高到 38.4%。單一 CPU 執行緒上最慢的一格只要 4.15 ms。Why this one: This single-author paper builds an 86k-parameter causal multi-pitch estimation (MPE) model for mixed ensembles that answers each 20 ms frame immediately and re-estimates the same frame as 20, 40, 80 and 160 ms of extra audio arrive. The evaluation matters more: each output is scored only at a deadline by which the audio needed to compute it has actually arrived. At a 50 ms deadline it is significantly better than five of six published offline MPE systems in 38 of 45 paired comparisons (lower in only two, both on BSED-real); on four held-out sets it stays within −1.27 to +0.60 F1 points of its own 160 ms lookahead output; and on AAM, recall in the first 20 ms after an onset rises from 9.8% to 38.4%. The slowest measured frame takes 4.15 ms on one CPU thread.

動機Motivation

即時音高顯示、樂譜跟隨與自動伴奏，都得在幾十毫秒內知道「現在哪些音在響」。但不限樂器的多音高估計，幾乎全都是為離線轉譜設計：模型讀完整段錄音再回答，影格分數也不在乎答案是什麼時候算得出來的。另一方面，低延遲轉譜的研究幾乎都只做獨奏，特別是鋼琴。所以「混合編制合奏的 MPE 如果必須在期限內交卷，會損失多少準確度」這個問題，作者認為還沒人量過。

作者也點出另一個張力：顯示器想在音一響就畫出來，但音符起始的頭幾毫秒通常不足以判斷音高。等越久越準、越早越快，這兩個需求在單一固定前瞻（lookahead）的模型裡只能取一。作者把需要 10 ms 等級反應的情境（例如樂器本身的發聲回應）明確排除在外。

Live pitch displays, score followers and accompaniment systems need to know which pitches are sounding within tens of milliseconds. Yet instrument-agnostic multi-pitch estimation has been designed almost entirely for offline transcription: the model reads the whole recording, and frame-wise scores ignore when each answer could have been computed. Low-latency transcription, meanwhile, has targeted solo instruments, mostly piano. How much accuracy MPE for mixed ensembles loses when it must answer by a deadline has, the author argues, never been measured.

The author also points to a tension: a display wants to draw a pitch as soon as it sounds, but the first milliseconds of a note rarely suffice to resolve its pitch. Waiting longer is more accurate and answering sooner is faster, and a model with a single fixed lookahead can serve only one of these. Uses needing about 10 ms, such as an instrument's own response, are explicitly out of scope.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

離線 MPE 的參照系統包括：deep salience（Bittner 等人，2017）用諧波 CQT（HCQT）估音高顯著度；Basic Pitch（Bittner 等人，2022）是輕量、不限樂器的轉譜模型；Weiß 與 Peeters 比較多種深度 MPE 架構；Timbre-Trap 與 SS-MPE（Cwitkowitz 等人）分別處理少標註與無標註的情境。HPPNet 用諧波擴張卷積把泛音結構寫進鋼琴轉譜模型，JIT-MPE 的「諧波主幹＋諧波收集」就是這條思路的延伸。低延遲這邊，最接近的是 Hu 等人把鋼琴轉譜改成最低延遲的研究，JIT-MPE 的非對稱視窗也直接沿用他們的做法，差別在於 JIT-MPE 處理的是合奏、而且不限樂器。

JIT-MPE 宣稱的兩個新東西，其實都在語音領域有明確的前例。「依期限計分」直接來自 Baumann 等人的增量式語音辨識評估、串流感知（streaming perception）與同步翻譯的 SimulEval，作者也都引用了。「一個模型多個延遲輸出頭」最像串流語音辨識裡一個模型服務多種右側上下文的做法，例如 Dual-mode ASR（Yu 等人，2021）。論文的貢獻是把這兩件事搬進 MPE 並做紮實的量測，而不是發明它們。

本站 2026-09-08 收錄的 Harmonica（2609.04640）也是不限樂器的輕量模型，nano 版只有 26.3K 參數、影格 F1 0.796，但它不在這篇的比較裡；本站 2026-10-06 的 2610.03656 則顯示，MPE 的關鍵是頻譜要涵蓋足夠的泛音。JIT-MPE 的主幹在頻率上能看到 ±384 格（±128 個半音，超過整個音域），跟那個結論一致。

The offline MPE references include deep salience (Bittner et al., 2017), which estimates pitch salience from a harmonic CQT (HCQT); Basic Pitch (Bittner et al., 2022), a lightweight instrument-agnostic transcriber; Weiß and Peeters' comparison of deep MPE architectures; and Timbre-Trap and SS-MPE (Cwitkowitz et al.) for low-label and label-free settings. HPPNet builds overtone structure into piano transcription with harmonically dilated convolutions, and JIT-MPE's harmonic stem and gather extend that idea. On the latency side, the closest work is Hu et al.'s minimum-latency piano transcription, whose asymmetric windows JIT-MPE adopts directly; the difference is that JIT-MPE handles instrument-agnostic ensembles.

Both things JIT-MPE presents as new have clear precedents in speech. Deadline-based scoring comes straight from Baumann et al.'s incremental speech recognition evaluation, streaming perception and SimulEval for simultaneous translation, all of which the author cites. One model with several delayed output heads is closest to streaming ASR models that serve several right-context sizes, such as Dual-mode ASR (Yu et al., 2021). The contribution is bringing both into MPE and measuring them carefully, not inventing them.

Harmonica (2609.04640, featured here on 2026-09-08) is also a light instrument-agnostic model, 26.3K parameters with frame F1 0.796 in its nano variant, but it is not in this comparison. And 2610.03656 (featured 2026-10-06) showed that what matters for MPE is spectral coverage of the overtones; JIT-MPE's stem reaches ±384 bins (±128 semitones), more than the whole pitch range, which fits that finding.

方法Method

前端：每個 20 ms 影格用兩個結束在目標時間的分析視窗（85 ms 與 341 ms），視窗是非對稱的，長上升、短下降（10 ms 與 20 ms），用來縮短前端延遲。頻譜投影到每半音 3 格的對數頻率軸（300 格）。接著是 8 層的諧波卷積主幹（24 通道、頻率擴張 1 到 64），時間上只因果地看三格，累積感受野 17 格。

「諧波收集」對 88 個音各自取主幹在 18 個泛音與次泛音位置的特徵，組成每音 64 維；「音程聚合」再透過秩 16 的殘差，把每個音和同度、二度、四五度、八度、十二度、雙八度位置上的音結合。之後是所有音共用的 64 單元 GRU，沿時間跑。

延遲頭是核心：第 n 列的頭 d 讀第 n+d 列的 GRU 狀態，d ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4, 8}，也就是等 0、20、40、80、160 ms，五個頭一起訓練。部署時，顯示器先畫 d=0 的立即估計，再隨各頭就緒改寫最近的歷史，像一條在播放頭後面慢慢定型的鋼琴捲軸。

評估用「stale-hold 時鐘」：在期限 D 下，查詢時間 q 只能用 q+D 之前的音訊，回答最近一個目標時間不晚於 q 的列，並從「來得及」的頭裡選一個（在獨立的 187 個片段上選定，不碰評估集）。D=50 ms 時讀的是 d=2 頭，也就是 40 ms 前瞻，留下至少 10 ms 給計算。

訓練資料是 767 個公開片段（GuitarSet、Slakh2100-redux、MusicNet、Winterreise、Cantoria、Dagstuhl ChoirSet、vocadito，以及只有鼓、當負例用的 Groove），兩階段共 13,400 步，第二階段混入鼓與人聲、補低音、加壓縮與起始輔助損失。所有設計凍結後才評三個保留資料集（AAM 100 首、ChoraleBricks 10 首、BSED 20 段×4 個真實演出＋1 個合成版）。每個模型三個種子，配對 bootstrap 以作品為群組重抽 10,000 次，不做多重比較校正。

Front end: each 20 ms frame is described by two analysis windows (85 ms and 341 ms) that end at the target time, asymmetric with a long rise and a short fall (10 ms and 20 ms) to cut front-end delay. The spectrum is projected onto a log-frequency axis with three bins per semitone (300 bins). An 8-layer harmonic convolutional stem follows (24 channels, frequency dilations 1 to 64), causal over three frames, with a 17-frame receptive field.

A harmonic gather reads, for each of the 88 notes, stem features at 18 harmonic and subharmonic positions, giving 64 features per note; an interval aggregation then combines each note with notes at unison, seconds, fourths and fifths, octaves, twelfths and double octaves through a rank-16 residual. A 64-unit GRU shared across notes runs over time.

The delay heads are the core: head d for row n reads the GRU state at row n+d, with d ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4, 8}, that is, waits of 0, 20, 40, 80 and 160 ms, all trained jointly. In deployment, a display draws the immediate d=0 estimate and rewrites recent history as each head becomes ready, like a piano roll that settles just behind the play head.

Evaluation uses a stale-hold clock: under deadline D, a query at time q may use only audio up to q+D, and is answered by the latest row whose target time is not after q, read through a head that is ready in time (chosen on a separate set of 187 clips, never on evaluation data). At D = 50 ms this is head d=2, i.e. 40 ms of lookahead, leaving at least 10 ms for computation.

Training uses 767 public clips (GuitarSet, Slakh2100-redux, MusicNet, Winterreise, Cantoria, Dagstuhl ChoirSet, vocadito, plus drum-only Groove as negatives) over two stages totalling 13,400 steps, the second adding drum and vocal mixing, low-register tones, compression and an auxiliary onset loss. Three held-out sets were scored only after every design choice was frozen (AAM, 100 songs; ChoraleBricks, 10 chorales; BSED, 20 excerpts in four real performances plus one synthetic rendering). Each model has three seeds, and a paired bootstrap resamples work clusters 10,000 times, with no multiplicity correction.

限制Limitations

作者自己列的限制很誠實：只評影格層級的音高存在，不做音符層級轉譜，沒有歌聲與真實流行樂錄音，也沒測聽者怎麼看待一個會改寫最近歷史的顯示器。最關鍵的一條是：沒有訓練一個固定 40 ms 前瞻的專用模型，所以無法說延遲頭比這樣的模型準。換句話說，「一個模型服務所有期限」的代價還沒量出來。

「38/45」這個數字需要拆開看。38 組勝出中有 18 組是對 SS-MPE v0.1.0 與 Timbre-Trap 這兩個最弱的系統，另有 10 組來自只有 5 首的 TRIOS 和 4 首的 PHENICX。比較的是「完整流程」：JIT-MPE 用在訓練資料保留片段上校準過的偏置，其他系統用作者預設閾值，這對 JIT-MPE 略有利，論文也明說了。

兩個保留集上的反常沒有解釋。BSED-real 是最大的例外：JIT-MPE 只有 64.5%，比 WP-DRCNN 低 7.9 點（CI [−9.9, −6.0]），比 MuScriptor 低 6.8 點；同樣的 20 段換成合成版就沒有差距，所以問題出在真實錄音，不是曲目重疊。ChoraleBricks 上延遲頭完全沒幫助（+0.02），50 ms 甚至比全長輸出高 0.60，作者說原因不明。

開頭後的表現在期限下仍明顯落後：50 ms 時起始後頭 20 ms 的召回率是 38.4%，而離線的 Basic Pitch 是 63.6%、WP-DRCNN 是 70.7%。另外，期限只限制可用的音訊，不含音訊介面延遲；AAM 的起始時間取自 MIDI，第一格也包含發音瞬態，論文也指出這張圖量的是召回、不是偵測延遲。

The author's own limitations are candid: only frame-level pitch presence is evaluated, not note-level transcription; singing and real pop recordings are absent; and how listeners perceive a display that rewrites its recent history is untested. The most important one: no model with a fixed 40 ms lookahead was trained, so there is no claim that the delay heads beat such a model. In other words, the cost of serving every deadline with one model has not been measured.

The 38-of-45 figure needs unpacking. Eighteen of the 38 wins are against SS-MPE v0.1.0 and Timbre-Trap, the two weakest systems, and 10 come from TRIOS (5 pieces) and PHENICX (4 pieces). The comparison is of complete pipelines: JIT-MPE uses biases calibrated on clips held out from its training data, while the others use author-default thresholds, a slight advantage the paper acknowledges.

Two held-out anomalies remain unexplained. BSED-real is the main exception: JIT-MPE reaches only 64.5%, 7.9 points below WP-DRCNN (CI [−9.9, −6.0]) and 6.8 below MuScriptor; on the synthetic renderings of the same 20 excerpts that gap disappears, so the problem lies in real recordings, not repertoire overlap. On ChoraleBricks the delay heads do not help at all (+0.02), and the 50 ms read is even 0.60 above full length, for reasons the author has not identified.

Onset performance under a deadline still lags clearly: at 50 ms, recall in the first 20 ms after an onset is 38.4%, against 63.6% for offline Basic Pitch and 70.7% for WP-DRCNN. The deadline also bounds only the audio used, not audio-interface latency; AAM onsets come from MIDI, so the first bin includes the attack, and the paper notes the figure measures recall, not detection delay.

討論Discussion

這篇最有價值的不是模型，而是把「什麼時候算得出來」變成 MPE 評估的第一級座標。曲線很清楚地說明了前瞻的邊際效益：開發集上 0 ms 是 77.24%、20 ms 79.73%、40–60 ms 80.83%，80 ms 以後只到 81.03–81.13%，也就是超過 40 ms 再等最多只多 0.3 點。AAM 上則是 80.41% → 82.58% → 83.85%（全長）。對要做即時系統的人來說，「40 ms 前瞻拿到大部分準確度」是一個可以直接用的設計數字。

消融把增益拆得很乾淨。在 50 ms 讀延遲頭而不是立即頭，開發集多 3.59 點，AAM、BSED-real、BSED-synthetic 各多 2.17、1.70、2.69 點；非對稱視窗加多頭訓練在開發集多 1.55 點；諧波前端多 0.55 點，但在 BSED-real 上是三步裡最大的（+2.20）。附錄有一個值得注意的細節：如果用最近列而不是 stale-hold 時鐘讀，延遲頭在開發集的增益會從 +3.59 變成 +2.61，可見時鐘定義本身會影響約 1 點，這種評估細節很少有論文交代。

失誤的分布也很有說服力：AAM 上 50 ms 比全長多漏的 13.6k 個影格，有 79% 落在起始後 60 ms 內；而全長 JIT-MPE 與雙向模型之間的差距，20.3k 個多漏影格裡有 16.9k 個在起始後 500 ms 以上。也就是說，期限的代價集中在起音，雙向模型的優勢則在持續音。

我想看到的下一步有兩個。一是 Baumann 的增量評估框架除了準確度還有「編輯開銷」，也就是輸出被改寫了多少次；對一個會改寫歷史的顯示器，這是直接相關的指標，卻沒有報告。二是補上固定 40 ms 前瞻的專用模型與 Harmonica 這類輕量模型的比較。計算成本本身已經不是問題：每秒音訊 0.68 GMAC，每 20 ms 中位數 2.42 ms。

The most valuable part is not the model but making when-it-can-be-computed a first-class axis of MPE evaluation. The curves show the marginal value of lookahead plainly: on development data, 77.24% at 0 ms, 79.73% at 20 ms, 80.83% at 40–60 ms, and only 81.03–81.13% from 80 ms on, so waiting beyond 40 ms adds at most 0.3 points. On AAM it is 80.41% → 82.58% → 83.85% at full length. For anyone building a live system, 40 ms of lookahead capturing most of the accuracy is a design number they can use directly.

The ablation separates the gains cleanly. Reading the delay heads at 50 ms instead of the immediate head adds 3.59 points on development data and 2.17, 1.70 and 2.69 on AAM, BSED-real and BSED-synthetic; asymmetric windows with multi-head training add 1.55 on development data; the harmonic front end adds 0.55, but is the largest of the three steps on BSED-real (+2.20). An appendix detail is worth noting: reading the nearest row instead of the stale-hold clock changes the delay-head gain on development data from +3.59 to +2.61, so the clock definition alone moves about one point, an evaluation detail few papers report.

The error breakdown is convincing too: of the 13.6k frames JIT-MPE additionally misses at 50 ms versus full length on AAM, 79% lie within 60 ms of an onset, whereas 16.9k of the 20.3k frames separating full-length JIT-MPE from the bidirectional model lie 500 ms or more after an onset. The deadline's cost sits in attacks; the bidirectional model's edge sits in sustained notes.

Two next steps I would want. Baumann's incremental framework measures edit overhead, how often outputs get rewritten, alongside accuracy; for a display that rewrites its history this is directly relevant, yet it is not reported. And a dedicated fixed-40 ms model plus light models such as Harmonica should be added to the comparison. Compute is no longer the issue: 0.68 GMAC per second of audio and a median of 2.42 ms per 20 ms hop.

動機Motivation

自動 DJ 系統大多針對電子舞曲（EDM），因為它樂句規整、有純器樂的前奏尾奏、拍點穩定，固定規則就能處理得不錯。人聲密集的流行歌難得多：段落結構多變、人聲突出、風格分散，轉場好不好不只取決於怎麼混，還取決於選哪首、從哪裡切、用什麼手法。這三個決定彼此牽動：換了下一首，可行的切點和手法就跟著變。

作者認為規則系統的兩個問題是：各個決定被獨立、確定性地最佳化，抓不到彼此的耦合；而且只給一個答案，排除了其他同樣可行的選擇。LLM 能同時讀兩首歌的結構、人聲活動與候選切點，取樣多次還能得到多個方案；但純文字 LLM 聽不到自己規劃的混音，沒有外部回饋的自我檢查又不可靠，所以需要用渲染出來的音訊做驗證。

Most automatic DJ systems target electronic dance music (EDM), whose regular phrasing, instrumental intros and outros and steady beat grid let fixed rules work well. Vocal-rich pop is much harder: structure varies, vocals are prominent and styles are diverse, so a transition's quality depends not only on how two tracks are mixed but on which track comes next, where it is cued and which technique is used. These decisions are coupled: change the next track and the feasible cues and techniques change with it.

The authors see two problems with rule-based systems: each decision is optimised independently and deterministically, missing the coupling, and they return a single answer, excluding other valid ones. An LLM can read both tracks' structure, vocal activity and candidate cues together, and repeated sampling yields several plans. But a text-only LLM cannot hear the mix it plans, and self-checks without external feedback are unreliable, so verification has to happen on rendered audio.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

自動 DJ 的直接前作有三個。Bittner 等人（ISMIR 2017，Spotify）最佳化歌單順序與交叉淡化點；Vande Veire 與 De Bie（2018）為鼓打貝斯（drum and bass）挑結構切點與轉場類型，並偵測人聲衝突；Mosaikbox 用音源分離壓制轉場時的衝突。學習式的方法如 DJtransGAN（Chen 等人，ICASSP 2022，與本篇同有 Wei-Han Hsu 與 Yi-Hsuan Yang 兩位作者）用對抗訓練學推桿與 EQ 曲線。DJustify 和它們的差別是：目標曲風改成流行歌，處理人聲的方式是「讓切點避開唱到一半的樂句」而不是分離或偵測，並且把選歌、切點、合成三件事放進同一個迴圈。

「LLM 提案、外部檢查器回報、LLM 修補」這個結構本身不新，最接近的是 CRITIC（Gou 等人，ICLR 2024）這類用外部工具驗證 LLM 輸出再修正的做法；論文引用的「沒有外部回饋時自我修正會失效」（Huang 等人）正是這條線的出發點。音樂領域裡，本站 2026-10-26 收錄的 MixAgent（2610.29093）也是「LLM 規劃＋評論者修正」，但它的評論者是用 DPO 訓練、直接輸出數值參數的模型；DJustify 的檢查器則是手寫的確定性規則。

依整體走向排歌也有前例：論文引用的 Dave Cliff「Hang the DJ」（HP Labs，2000）就已經依速度與能量排序曲目。DJustify 的新意是讓 LLM 依歌詞、效價／喚醒度、速度與調性挑歌來實現一段故事，而不只是沿著速度或能量的數值變化。

There are three direct predecessors. Bittner et al. (ISMIR 2017, Spotify) optimise playlist order and crossfade points; Vande Veire and De Bie (2018) pick structural cue points and transition types for drum and bass, with vocal-clash detection; Mosaikbox uses source separation to suppress clashes during the blend. Among learning-based methods, DJtransGAN (Chen et al., ICASSP 2022, sharing two authors, Wei-Han Hsu and Yi-Hsuan Yang, with this paper) learns fader and EQ curves adversarially. DJustify differs in targeting pop, in handling vocals by cueing so that a sung phrase is not cut rather than by separating or detecting them, and in placing track selection, cueing and synthesis in one loop.

The propose–check–repair structure itself is not new. The closest pattern is CRITIC (Gou et al., ICLR 2024) and similar work that verifies LLM output with external tools before revising; the finding the paper cites, that self-correction fails without external feedback (Huang et al.), is that line's starting point. In music, MixAgent (2610.29093, featured here on 2026-10-26) is also an LLM planner with a correcting critic, but its critic is a DPO-trained model that outputs numerical parameters, whereas DJustify's checker is a set of hand-written deterministic rules.

Ordering a set by its overall shape also has precedent: Dave Cliff's “Hang the DJ” (HP Labs, 2000), which the paper cites, already sequenced tracks by tempo and energy. DJustify's novelty is having an LLM pick tracks by lyrics, valence/arousal, tempo and key to realise a story, rather than only following numeric changes in tempo or energy.

方法Method

前處理用現成工具取得拍點／強拍、段落結構與和弦。另外兩個關鍵特徵：一是自己在 Jamendo 上訓練的 CRNN 歌聲偵測，逐格判斷有沒有人聲；二是用 MuQ-MuLan 嵌入做效價／喚醒度回歸，再讓音訊語言模型讀音訊與轉錄歌詞做修正。切點錨點限定在樂句結尾、段落邊界、和聲重新開始處與低頻起始點，確保切點都在拍格上、音樂上站得住。

規劃器拿到上一首 A 和檢索模組給的候選池，一次決定下一首 B、A 的切出點與 B 的切入點（都從預先算好的候選清單「cue card」裡挑）、重疊長度與轉場手法。手法從長重疊的「blend」（例如 B 的副歌疊在 A 的尾奏上）到「cut」（搭配 echo、brake、backspin、filter 等效果的硬切）。推理指的是開啟模型的思考模式。

檢查器有四類確定性關卡：人聲（唱到一半被切 → 把切點移到樂句結尾；人聲在重疊區相撞 → 縮短重疊或改硬切）、能量（靜音或能量空洞 → 調長度或手法）、結構（A 切太早、B 進太晚、B 停在前奏裡 → 移切點）、配對（效價／喚醒度不合 → 重選 B）。失敗會回傳具體修正指示，最多修四輪，仍失敗就加標記回傳。全部在一張 48 GB RTX 6000 Ada 上用 4-bit 量化模型本地執行。

故事模式用同一個 LLM，從固定的開場曲出發，依「開場、推進、收尾」三幕各挑一首，受速度與調性相容性約束，再對每個接點跑同一個轉場迴圈。

Preprocessing uses off-the-shelf tools for beats and downbeats, structural segmentation and chords. Two features are key: a CRNN singing-voice detector the authors train on Jamendo for frame-wise vocal activity, and a valence/arousal regression over MuQ-MuLan embeddings, refined by an audio-language model reading the audio and transcribed lyrics. Cue anchors are restricted to phrase ends, section boundaries, harmonic restarts and low-frequency onsets, so every cue is on-grid and musically sound.

Given outgoing track A and a candidate pool from a retrieval module, the planner chooses incoming track B, a cue-out on A and a cue-in on B (each from a precomputed shortlist, the cue card), an overlap length and a technique. Techniques range from blend moves with long overlap (for example B's chorus riding over A's outro) to cut moves, abrupt handovers with echo, brake, backspin or filter. Reasoning means switching on the model's thinking mode.

The checker has four families of deterministic gates: vocal (cut mid-phrase → move the cue to the phrase end; vocals collide in the overlap → shorten it or cut), energy (silence or energy hole → adjust length or technique), structure (A cut too early, B entering too late or staying in its intro → move the cue) and pairing (valence/arousal mismatch → re-select B). A failure returns a concrete repair instruction, for up to four rounds; a plan still failing is returned flagged. Everything runs locally on one 48 GB RTX 6000 Ada with a 4-bit quantised model.

Story mode uses the same LLM: starting from a fixed opening track, it picks one track per act (opening, build, close) under tempo and key compatibility gates, then runs the same transition loop at each junction.

限制Limitations

最大的缺口是少了一組對照：用同樣的人聲感知切點錨點，配上簡單的規則（例如挑第一個合格錨點、固定手法），不經過 LLM。消融顯示 Ours−C−R（不推理、不檢查）平均 5.7，反而比完整系統的 5.6 略高；換掉切點卻掉到 4.8。所以在目前的證據下，「LLM 規劃」對平均品質的貢獻還沒被證明，論文自己也說檢查器「增加的是可靠性，不是平均品質」。

轉場聽測只有 12 組歌曲配對，每位受試者評一個隨機子集。檢查器修好人聲被切斷的例子只有兩個種子（DJ 經驗聽者的評分從 2.0、1.8 升到 5.0、4.8），作者明說這不是人聲錯誤率的量測。

「多樣性」的算法偏寬：只要手法、切出點或切入點任一不同就算不同的轉場，而且有檢查器的條件把沒通過所有關卡的輸出也算進去。完整系統每對中位數 6.5 個不同轉場（IQR 5–8），但有推理時加上修補從 5 到 6.5 並不顯著（p = .054）。

故事模式的比較混了好幾件事。Story 與 No-story 選的歌每一組都不同，開場曲政策也不同（No-story 隨機挑開場），作者自己也承認增益來自選歌加排序加開場。和人類 DJ 的比較裡，人類是自由選歌，而故事模式每一幕挑一首、三分鐘的歌單大約只有三首；p = .95 與 p = .42 是「沒偵測到差距」，不是「沒有差距」，作者也是這樣寫的。Story 勝過交叉淡化的 p = .043 未經校正。基線方面，Mosaikbox 是 EDM 工具，交叉淡化是重新實作、不是 Bittner 完整的歌單最佳化器。

The biggest gap is a missing control: the same vocal-aware cue anchors with a simple rule (for example, take the first valid anchor with a fixed technique), no LLM. The ablation shows Ours−C−R (no reasoning, no checker) averaging 5.7, slightly above the full system's 5.6, while swapping the cues drops the score to 4.8. On current evidence, then, the LLM planner's contribution to average quality is unproven, and the paper itself says the checker adds reliability, not higher average quality.

The transition test uses only 12 track pairs, with each participant rating a random subset. The checker's rescue of cut vocals rests on two seeds (DJ-experienced ratings rising from 2.0 and 1.8 to 5.0 and 4.8), which the authors state is not a measured vocal-error rate.

The diversity count is generous: transitions count as distinct if they differ in technique, cue-out or cue-in at all, and checker conditions include outputs that did not pass every gate. The full system yields a median of 6.5 distinct transitions per pair (IQR 5–8), but with reasoning on, repair's increase from 5 to 6.5 is not significant (p = .054).

The story comparisons mix several things. Story and No-story chose different songs in every set and used different opener policies (No-story draws its opener at random), and the authors concede the gain comes from selection, ordering and opener together. Against the human DJ, the human chose tracks freely, and with one track per act, a three-minute set holds about three tracks; p = .95 and p = .42 mean no detected gap, not no gap, as the authors also write. Story's win over the crossfade (p = .043) is uncorrected. As for baselines, Mosaikbox is an EDM tool and the crossfade is a reimplementation, not Bittner's full playlist optimiser.

討論Discussion

撇開包裝，這篇最紮實的發現其實是一個 MIR 問題：在人聲密集的流行歌上，轉場品質主要由切點候選決定。同一條管線、同一個修補迴圈，只把切點來源從自家的人聲感知錨點換成 Bittner 的偵測器，分數就從 5.6 掉到 4.8（Δ = +0.75，p = .013），而兩者在 12 組裡只有 1 組選到相同切點。這比「LLM 能當 DJ」更值得被引用。

與既有系統的差距是實在的：對 Mosaikbox Δ = +1.83（p < 10⁻⁴），對交叉淡化 Δ = +0.89（p = .001），對人類 DJ Δ = −1.02（p = .0003），四組比較在 Holm 校正後都仍顯著。28 位有 DJ 經驗的聽者把人類 DJ 拉得更遠（Δ = −1.9），代表專業耳朵聽得出的差距比一般聽眾大。可執行率從 91% 升到 98% 再到 99%，主要靠推理模式，這是 LLM 迴圈最明確的貢獻：讓方案不出錯，而不是讓它更好聽。

故事模式在故事連貫性上比 No-story 高約 1.9 分（p = .002），兩者轉場品質無法區分（p = .85），這組對照設計得算乾淨，至少說明故事規劃改變了聽者感受到的整體走向。

我會想看的是：一個不用 LLM、只用他們錨點加規則的基線；更長的歌單（三首很難稱為「DJ set」）；以及檢查器關卡的觸發率統計。作者把未來工作放在用音訊語言模型當驗證器，但他們自己引用的文獻正說明這類模型「能評分卻不一定真的聽到」；在那之前，可以參考 MixAgent 用渲染結果訓練評論者的做法。

Stripped of its framing, the paper's most solid finding is an MIR one: on vocal-rich pop, transition quality is driven mainly by the cue candidates. Same pipeline, same repair loop, only the cue source swapped from the authors' vocal-aware anchors to Bittner's detector, and the score falls from 5.6 to 4.8 (Δ = +0.75, p = .013), with the two choosing the same cues on only 1 of 12 pairs. That deserves citing more than the claim that an LLM can DJ.

The margins over existing systems are real: Δ = +1.83 over Mosaikbox (p < 10⁻⁴), +0.89 over the crossfade (p = .001) and −1.02 against the human DJ (p = .0003), all four comparisons significant after Holm correction. The 28 DJ-experienced listeners put the human DJ further ahead (Δ = −1.9), so trained ears hear a larger gap than general listeners. Renderability rises from 91% to 98% to 99%, mostly from the reasoning mode, and this is the LLM loop's clearest contribution: making plans that do not break, rather than mixes that sound better.

Story mode beats No-story on story coherence by about 1.9 points (p = .002) while the two are indistinguishable on transition quality (p = .85). That contrast is reasonably clean and shows that story planning changes the overall shape listeners perceive.

What I would want: a non-LLM baseline using their anchors plus rules; longer sets (three tracks hardly make a DJ set); and statistics on how often each gate fires. The authors name audio-language-model verifiers as future work, but the literature they cite says such models can rate audio without reliably perceiving it; until that changes, MixAgent's approach of training a critic on rendered outcomes is a useful reference.
