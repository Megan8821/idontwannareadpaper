ProSing: Zero-Shot Fine-Grained Singing Technique Conversion

ProSing：零樣本、細粒度的歌唱技巧轉換——在預訓練的 Vevo1.5 流匹配聲學模型上加入逐幀技巧條件，用「成對訓練＋音素對齊」切斷條件捷徑，並以連續音高軌跡控制顫音與滑音（單技巧 F1 0.143→0.439，N-MOS 3.36 對 Vevo1.5 2.41）

Xinhao Guan, Jingyue Huang, Kangrui Chen, Julian McAuley, Zhong-Qiu Wang
arXiv:2611.19223 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-11-14
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：給一段沒有刻意用技巧的清唱錄音，指定「這幾個字改成氣聲、那一段加顫音」，輸出要保留歌詞、旋律、時值與歌手音色——這就是本文定義的細粒度歌唱技巧轉換（STC）。做法是拿 Vevo1.5（約 101k 小時語音＋7k 小時歌聲預訓練）來微調，加上逐幀技巧標籤與連續的顫音／滑音音高軌跡。選它是因為它的消融把一個常被忽略的問題講得很具體：單純把技巧標籤加進去，模型會從內容表徵與音色提示偷看技巧線索，單技巧 F1 只有 0.143；改成成對訓練與音素對齊後升到 0.389。要注意：所有 F1 都是 STARS 這個自動標註器給的，它在真實錄音上的平均 F1 也只有 0.726。Why this one: Given a plain sung recording with no deliberate technique, the task is to request "make these words breathy, add vibrato to that phrase" while preserving lyrics, melody, timing and singer timbre—what this paper defines as fine-grained singing technique conversion (STC). The approach fine-tunes Vevo1.5 (pretrained on about 101k hours of speech plus 7k hours of singing) with frame-level technique labels and continuous vibrato/glissando pitch trajectories. I picked it because its ablation makes a frequently overlooked problem concrete: simply adding technique labels lets the model peek at technique cues in the content representation and timbre prompt, giving a single-technique F1 of only 0.143; paired training plus phoneme alignment raises it to 0.389. Caveat: every F1 comes from the STARS automatic annotator, whose own average F1 on real recordings is only 0.726.

動機Motivation

歌唱技巧——氣聲、假聲、混聲、咽音、顫音、滑音——是歌聲表現力的主要來源，但現有兩條路線各缺一半。歌唱風格轉換（SSC）改的是既有錄音，能保住原唱的音色與演出細節，但目標風格是從參考音訊抽出來的整包表徵，改技巧時節奏、情緒也可能一起被帶走。可控技巧的歌聲合成（SVS）能明確指定技巧，卻是從歌詞與樂譜重新合成，原錄音的演出特徵保不住。

作者因此把問題定為「音訊到音訊、可以明確指定技巧與區段」的轉換，並加兩個限制：零樣本（歌手訓練時沒見過）與細粒度（局部時段、可多技巧並存）。他們點出兩個障礙：一是有技巧標註的歌聲資料稀少（標註需要專業知識），從頭訓練的音質與泛化都成問題；二是既有方法要嘛只做整段轉換，要嘛只支援單一技巧或已見過的歌手。

Singing techniques—breathy voice, falsetto, mixed voice, pharyngeal voice, vibrato, glissando—are a main source of vocal expressiveness, but the two existing routes each cover only half. Singing style conversion (SSC) edits an existing recording and so preserves the original timbre and performance detail, but the target style is a whole reference-derived representation, so changing a technique can drag rhythm and emotion along with it. Technique-controllable singing voice synthesis (SVS) lets you name techniques explicitly, but it re-synthesizes from lyrics and a score, losing the original performance.

The authors therefore frame the problem as audio-to-audio conversion with explicit technique and region control, plus two constraints: zero-shot (singers unseen in training) and fine-grained (localized in time, multiple techniques at once). They name two obstacles: technique-annotated singing data are scarce (annotation needs expertise), so training from scratch hurts quality and generalization; and existing methods either convert globally or support only one technique or only seen singers.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的比較對象是 TCSinger（Zhang 等，EMNLP 2024）與 TechSinger（Guo 等，AAAI 2025）。 兩者都能在合成時指定技巧，但輸入是樂譜與歌詞；本文把 TCSinger 當基準時，得先用 STARS 從原錄音反推樂譜再餵進去，這一步本身就讓它很難保住原演出。另一端是 Vevo（Zhang 等，ICLR 2025）及其 Amphion 中的 Vevo1.5：本文的骨幹就是它，Vevo1.5 原本能做整段風格轉換，但沒有可編輯的技巧變數。

在 STC 這個題目上，O'Connor、Dixon 與 Fazekas（CMMR 2021）早就做過「零樣本歌唱技巧轉換」，但只能整段轉換；Chen 與 Soo（ICASSP 2023）用 CycleGAN 做少樣本版本。最接近本文連續音高控制的是 VibE-SVC（Choi 等，Interspeech 2025）：它同樣從高頻 F0 輪廓抽出顫音成分來控制，但只處理顫音一種技巧。本文的差別在於把四種音色類技巧與兩種音高類技巧放進同一個逐幀條件，而且做的是轉換而非合成。

「成對訓練」這個本文最強調的貢獻，本質上是資訊洩漏問題的老解法換了形式。 AutoVC、SpeechSplit 這類語音轉換方法用資訊瓶頸防止內容特徵夾帶說話人或韻律資訊；本文不靠瓶頸，而是利用 GTSinger 本來就有的「同一歌手、同一段落，一次正常唱、一次用技巧唱」平行錄音，讓技巧分支的內容條件改從正常版抽取。這比較像傳統平行語料語音轉換的訓練方式，配上從另一段錄音隨機裁切的音色提示——後者在零樣本語音合成裡也是常見做法。

The most direct comparisons are TCSinger (Zhang et al., EMNLP 2024) and TechSinger (Guo et al., AAAI 2025). Both let you specify techniques at synthesis time, but their inputs are a score and lyrics; to use TCSinger as a baseline here, the authors first have to recover a score from the source recording with STARS and feed that in, a step that by itself makes preserving the original performance hard. At the other end is Vevo (Zhang et al., ICLR 2025) and its Amphion release Vevo1.5: this paper's backbone, which can already do whole-clip style conversion but has no editable technique variables.

On STC itself, O'Connor, Dixon and Fazekas (CMMR 2021) already did "zero-shot singing technique conversion", but only globally; Chen and Soo (ICASSP 2023) did a few-shot version with CycleGAN. The closest precedent for the continuous pitch control is VibE-SVC (Choi et al., Interspeech 2025), which likewise extracts a vibrato component from a high-frequency F0 contour for control, but handles vibrato only. What differs here is putting four timbral and two pitch techniques into one frame-level condition, and doing conversion rather than synthesis.

"Paired training", the contribution the paper stresses most, is essentially an old fix for information leakage in a new form. Voice-conversion methods such as AutoVC and SpeechSplit use information bottlenecks to stop content features from carrying speaker or prosody information; this paper uses no bottleneck, but instead exploits GTSinger's existing parallel recordings—same singer, same passage, sung once plainly and once with the technique—and derives the technique branch's content condition from the plain version. That is closer to classic parallel-corpus voice conversion training, combined with a timbre prompt randomly cropped from a different recording, which is also common practice in zero-shot TTS.

方法Method

骨幹。 Vevo1.5 的 tokenizer 把 Whisper 特徵與色度圖編成 12.5 Hz 的離散 token（保留歌詞、旋律與演唱風格，壓低音色資訊），再上採樣到 50 Hz；流匹配 Transformer（FMT）以這些內容風格表徵與一段音色提示的梅爾頻譜為條件，生成目標梅爾頻譜，最後用 Vocos 聲碼器轉波形。ProSing 凍結 Whisper／色度抽取器、tokenizer 與聲碼器，只微調 FMT 與新加的技巧條件模組。

成對訓練與音素對齊。 原始訓練是「用目標音訊自己的內容表徵＋自己的音色提示重建自己」，技巧線索可能早就藏在條件裡，模型不必看技巧標籤也能重建，這就是作者說的條件捷徑。ProSing 改成：正常版與技巧版兩個分支的內容條件都從正常版抽取。因為兩版的音素時長不同，作者把兩邊標註的音素邊界換成 50 Hz 幀位置，用 Levenshtein 對齊音素序列，建立從技巧版時間軸到正常版時間軸的分段線性映射，再對正常版表徵做線性內插。兩個分支每個 epoch 共用一段從同一歌手另一段正常錄音隨機裁切的音色提示；另外把兩段不同技巧群組的片段串接，造出「段內切換技巧」的合成資料。

Backbone. Vevo1.5's tokenizer encodes Whisper features and a chromagram into 12.5 Hz discrete tokens (keeping lyrics, melody and performance style while suppressing timbre), upsampled to 50 Hz; a flow-matching Transformer (FMT) generates the target mel spectrogram conditioned on these content-style representations and a timbre-prompt mel, and a Vocos vocoder produces the waveform. ProSing freezes the Whisper/chroma extractors, tokenizer and vocoder and fine-tunes only the FMT plus the new technique-conditioning modules.

Paired training and phoneme alignment. The original scheme reconstructs a target from its own content representation and its own timbre prompt, so technique cues may already sit in the conditions and the model can reconstruct without looking at technique labels—the conditioning shortcut. ProSing instead derives the content condition for both the plain and technique branches from the plain rendition. Because phoneme durations differ between the two, the authors convert annotated phoneme boundaries to 50 Hz frame positions, align the phoneme sequences with Levenshtein alignment, build a piecewise-linear map from the technique timeline to the plain timeline, and linearly interpolate the plain representation. Both branches share, per epoch, a timbre prompt randomly cropped from another plain recording by the same singer; composite examples concatenating segments from two technique groups expose the model to within-utterance technique switches.

限制Limitations

評分尺的天花板很低。 消融裡所有技巧 F1 都由 STARS 預測，而 STARS 在真實 GTSinger 錄音上的六技巧平均 F1 只有 0.726，顫音／滑音更只有 0.380／0.534。所以最終音高類 F1 0.195 到底是模型差還是量尺差，無法分辨；作者有列出這個參考值，算是誠實，但沒有把它推到結論。

基準不太對等。 TCSinger 是合成系統，被迫先經 STARS 轉成樂譜再合成，結果 CER 64.1%、F0-RMSE 413.4 音分，N-MOS 1.48——這更像是在量「轉譜再合成」這條流水線的損失，而不是技巧控制的能力。Vevo1.5 只能整段轉換，所以只比了單技巧情況。真正局部、多技巧的對手不存在，多技巧欄只有 ProSing 一個系統，而且信賴區間有 ±0.53／±0.51。

原樣保留其實不算好。 原錄音的 CER 是 23.5%，ProSing 是 27.6%，多 4.1 個百分點；F0-RMSE 166.7 音分，比一個半音還大，而這是在音色類技巧（理論上不該動音高）的 24 個案例上量的。另外，聽測沒有附原錄音或重建版當錨點，3.36 分的 N-MOS 很難換算成「離原唱多遠」。

The measuring stick has a low ceiling. Every technique F1 in the ablation comes from STARS predictions, and STARS itself averages only 0.726 F1 across the six techniques on real GTSinger recordings, with just 0.380/0.534 on vibrato/glissando. So whether the final pitch-technique F1 of 0.195 reflects the model or the ruler cannot be told apart; the authors do report this reference value, which is honest, but they don't carry it into their conclusions.

The baselines are not on equal footing. TCSinger is a synthesis system forced to go through STARS transcription to a score first; it ends up with 64.1% CER, 413.4-cent F0-RMSE and an N-MOS of 1.48—which measures the loss of a transcribe-then-synthesize pipeline more than technique control. Vevo1.5 converts only globally, so it is compared on single-technique cases only. No real local, multi-technique competitor exists: the multi-technique columns contain ProSing alone, with confidence intervals of ±0.53/±0.51.

Source preservation is not actually good. The source recordings have 23.5% CER and ProSing has 27.6%, 4.1 points more; F0-RMSE is 166.7 cents, more than a semitone and a half, measured on 24 timbral-technique cases where pitch in principle should not move. The listening test also includes no source or reconstruction anchor, so an N-MOS of 3.36 is hard to translate into "how far from the original".

討論Discussion

消融裡最值得看的是一個作者沒有多談的數字：歌手相似度。 只加標籤的版本 SpkSim 是 0.753，換成成對訓練後掉到 0.538，之後一路維持在 0.52–0.53；直到加入 OpenSinger 與 M4Singer 共 53.06 小時、58 位歌手的額外資料才回到 0.631，但單技巧 F1 也從 0.439 退到 0.374。換句話說，切斷條件捷徑的代價是音色保真度，而補回音色的代價又是技巧遵循度。作者把這寫成「互補效果」，但對使用者來說這是一條實實在在的取捨曲線，最終模型停在中間某一點。

連續軌跡這一步的貢獻比較乾淨。 音高類 F1 從 0.097 升到 0.162，ΔF0-CCC 達 0.756，代表指定的顫音軌跡確實傳到輸出音高上。顫音軌跡的做法很樸素：pYIN 抽 F0、轉音分、減去 260 ms 移動平均、再過 3–9 Hz 帶通（涵蓋受訓歌手典型 5–7 Hz 的顫音速率）；滑音則是技巧版與對齊後正常版去顫音軌跡的差。這種「把可量化的技巧交給連續訊號、把難量化的交給類別標籤」的切分，比一律用類別標籤更合理。

真正要用起來還差一步： 作者自己說目前只能從「沒有技巧」轉到「有技巧」，不能把一種技巧換成另一種；而實際歌唱錄音很少是完全沒技巧的。訓練用的 GTSinger 有 80.59 小時、20 位專業歌手、9 種語言，但 CER 只在 36 個中文單技巧案例上量，跨語言的表現沒有拆開報告。

The most telling number in the ablation is one the authors say little about: singer similarity. The label-only variant has SpkSim 0.753; paired training drops it to 0.538, and it stays around 0.52–0.53 thereafter; only adding 53.06 hours from 58 singers in OpenSinger and M4Singer brings it back to 0.631, while single-technique F1 falls from 0.439 to 0.374. In other words, cutting the conditioning shortcut costs timbre fidelity, and restoring timbre costs technique following. The authors call these "complementary effects", but for a user it is a real trade-off curve, and the final model sits somewhere in the middle.

The continuous-trajectory step is the cleaner contribution. Pitch-technique F1 rises from 0.097 to 0.162 and ΔF0-CCC reaches 0.756, meaning the specified vibrato trajectory really does reach the output pitch. The vibrato trajectory is simple: pYIN F0, converted to cents, minus a 260 ms moving average, then a 3–9 Hz band-pass (covering the typical 5–7 Hz vibrato rate of trained singers); glissando is the difference between the de-vibratoed technique trajectory and the aligned plain one. Splitting techniques so that measurable ones get a continuous signal and hard-to-measure ones get a categorical label is more sensible than labels for everything.

One step short of practical use: the authors say it can only convert from "no technique" to "technique", not swap one technique for another, and real sung recordings are rarely technique-free. GTSinger provides 80.59 hours from 20 professional singers in 9 languages, but CER is measured only on 36 Chinese single-technique cases, and cross-language performance is not broken out.

EE-Motion: An EEG-based Web Architecture for Live Affective Music Recommendation

EE-Motion：用 EEG 即時調整音樂推薦的網頁架構——瀏覽器透過 Web Bluetooth 接 Muse 2 頭帶，以額葉 α 不對稱與 β/α 喚醒指數映射到 Russell 環狀模型的效價–喚醒座標，再由「情感轉接器」讓 ACE（圖神經網路）與 SASRec 不必重訓就能服務匿名訪客（延遲約 80–90 ms，低於 125 ms 期限；live 與離線 nDCG@10 無顯著差異）

Sabino Roccotelli, Potito Aghilar, Vito Walter Anelli, Alejandro Bellogín, Tommaso Colafiglio, Michelantonio Trizio, Tommaso Di Noia
arXiv:2611.21032 · cs.IR · 投稿submitted 2026-11-16
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這是一篇投 ACM Web Conference 2027 的系統展示論文：瀏覽器直接用 Web Bluetooth 配對消費級 EEG 頭帶 Muse 2，後端算出效價（額葉 α 不對稱）與喚醒（β/α 比），以固定係數的 sigmoid 壓到 [0,1]×[0,1]，再交給一個「情感轉接器」對曲目重新評分；匿名訪客只要挑最多十首喜歡的歌，用它們嵌入的平均當作使用者向量。選它是因為情緒推薦在 MIR 常停在離線資料集，這篇把整條即時管線接起來並量了延遲。但要看清楚它證明了什麼：只證明了系統跑得夠快（兩個模型約 80.0 與 90.3 ms），以及轉接器不會讓離線準確度變差（nDCG@10 0.0231 對 0.0236）；腦波訊號是否真的反映情緒、是否讓推薦更好，完全沒有測。Why this one: This is a system demo paper for the ACM Web Conference 2027: the browser pairs a consumer Muse 2 EEG headband directly over Web Bluetooth, the backend computes valence (frontal alpha asymmetry) and arousal (a beta/alpha ratio), squashes them into [0,1]×[0,1] with a fixed-coefficient sigmoid, and hands them to an "affective adapter" that re-scores tracks; an anonymous visitor just picks up to ten favourite tracks, whose averaged embeddings serve as the user vector. I picked it because affective recommendation in MIR usually stops at offline datasets, and this paper wires up the whole live pipeline and measures its latency. But be clear about what it shows: only that the system is fast enough (about 80.0 and 90.3 ms for the two models) and that the adapter does not hurt offline accuracy (nDCG@10 0.0231 vs 0.0236); whether the EEG signal actually reflects emotion, or improves recommendations, is not tested at all.

動機Motivation

聽音樂時的情緒不是固定的，在一次聆聽過程中會持續漂移；情緒誘發研究顯示，聽一分鐘音樂就能引起可量測的效價–喚醒變化。但推薦系統把情緒納入時，通常是從過去的收聽紀錄、靜態情境屬性或使用者自填來推，例如同一團隊的 ACE 把每位使用者錨定在他過去聽過曲目的效價–喚醒重心，當下心情偏離平均時就跟不上。

作者把「讓推薦跟上當下情緒」拆成三個卡點。一是服務端：推薦系統的基礎設施是為點擊、播放這類離散事件設計的，沒有地方接每秒數百個取樣、充滿雜訊的生理訊號。二是模型端：眼前的使用者多半是匿名新訪客，協同過濾需要使用者嵌入表、要重訓或 fold-in；SASRec 這類序列模型避開了使用者表，但只吃離散 item token，不知道怎麼融入連續的情緒訊號。三是網頁端：可穿戴腦機介面過去要靠第三方驅動程式，W3C 的 Web Bluetooth 讓瀏覽器能直接配對低功耗藍牙裝置，但還沒有一條開放的端到端架構把它接到推薦上。

Emotion while listening to music is not fixed; it drifts continuously within a session, and emotion-elicitation studies show that even one minute of music induces measurable valence–arousal shifts. Yet when recommenders include emotion, they usually infer it from past listening logs, static context attributes or self-reports; the same group's ACE, for example, anchors each user at the valence–arousal centroid of previously consumed items, which cannot follow a mood that departs from that average during a session.

The authors split "make recommendations follow the current emotion" into three bottlenecks. First, serving: recommendation infrastructure is built for discrete events like clicks and plays, with no place to plug in noisy physiological signals sampled hundreds of times per second. Second, modelling: the visitor is usually an anonymous newcomer, and collaborative filtering needs a user embedding table, retraining or fold-in; sequential models like SASRec avoid user tables but consume only discrete item tokens and have no way to fuse a continuous affective signal. Third, the web: wearable BCIs historically required third-party drivers; W3C Web Bluetooth lets browsers pair directly with Bluetooth Low Energy devices, but no open end-to-end architecture yet connects that to recommendation.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

用生理訊號做情緒音樂推薦並不新。 Ayata、Yaslan 與 Kamasak（IEEE Trans. Consumer Electronics 2018）就用穿戴式感測器（光體積描記與皮膚電反應）辨識情緒再推薦音樂；EEG 與音樂情緒的關聯也有 DEAP 資料集（Koelstra 等 2012）這類以音樂影片誘發、標註效價與喚醒的標準資源。以額葉 α 不對稱當效價指標，則源自 Davidson 一系列的情緒偏側化研究，是 EEG 情緒研究裡最常用、也最常被質疑的指標之一。本文沒有在這些方法上前進——它直接拿固定公式，明說只是「未校準的生理代理指標」。

本文真正的貢獻在系統與介面層。 它跟 ACE（Aghilar 等，SIGIR 2026，同一團隊）的關係是：ACE 是把效價–喚醒融入表徵學習的圖模型，本文把 ACE 的「情感接地」機制抽象成一個轉接器，套到結構不同的模型（包括 SASRec，Kang 與 McAuley 2018）上，並接上同團隊的 WarpRec 服務框架。

「用使用者挑的幾首歌的嵌入平均當使用者向量」其實就是以 item 表示使用者的老做法。 這跟 FISM（Kabbur 等 2013）那類 item-based 因子模型、以及推薦系統裡的 fold-in 思路是同一件事；作者自己引了 Deep Sets（Zaheer 等 2017）當集合池化的依據。它的好處是免重訓，但也意味著冷啟動的品質完全取決於 item 嵌入本身。

Physiology-driven emotional music recommendation is not new. Ayata, Yaslan and Kamasak (IEEE Trans. Consumer Electronics 2018) already used wearable sensors (photoplethysmography and galvanic skin response) to recognise emotion and recommend music; for EEG and musical emotion there are standard resources such as the DEAP dataset (Koelstra et al. 2012), with music-video stimuli annotated for valence and arousal. Using frontal alpha asymmetry as a valence index goes back to Davidson's work on emotional lateralisation and is among the most used—and most questioned—indices in EEG emotion research. This paper does not advance those methods; it takes a fixed formula and says plainly that these are "uncalibrated physiological proxy indices".

The real contribution is at the system and interface level. Relative to ACE (Aghilar et al., SIGIR 2026, same group), a graph model that injects valence–arousal into representation learning, this paper abstracts ACE's "affective grounding" into an adapter, applies it to structurally different models (including SASRec, Kang and McAuley 2018), and connects it to the group's WarpRec serving framework.

"Average the embeddings of a few chosen tracks to get a user vector" is the old idea of representing users by items. It is the same move as item-based factor models like FISM (Kabbur et al. 2013) and the fold-in idea in recommender systems; the authors themselves cite Deep Sets (Zaheer et al. 2017) for set pooling. It avoids retraining, but it also means cold-start quality depends entirely on the item embeddings.

方法Method

架構。 四個微服務：前端、曲目目錄、bci-processor（EEG 前處理與特徵）、recommender（用 WarpRec 對候選評分），之間以 HTTP 與 WebSocket 溝通，可以全部跑在單機，也能分散部署。推薦端只需要 [0,1]×[0,1] 的效價–喚醒座標，作者稱之為「輸入可替換性」：任何能輸出同格式座標的來源都能接上，包括展示裡的手動模式。

EEG 管線。 Muse 2 的四個通道（TP9、AF7、AF8、TP10）以 256 Hz 取樣，透過 Muse-js 在瀏覽器裡讀取。後端用環形緩衝存 1024 個取樣（4 秒），轉成 MNE 的 Raw 結構，以 MNE 的 LOF 函式偵測異常通道，並輔以人工目視檢查訊號、調整頭帶位置。之後做 1–40 Hz 因果帶通濾波，用 NeuroKit2 估 θ（4–8 Hz）、α（8–13 Hz）、β（13–30 Hz）功率；第二個環形緩衝存 20 筆連續觀測，對每個頻帶、每個通道做時間平均。效價代理是 log(α_AF8/α_AF7)，喚醒代理是 (β_AF7+β_AF8)/(α_AF7+α_AF8)，再以固定係數的 sigmoid 映到 [0,1]。座標在 Russell 環狀平面上以 8 Hz 更新。

情感轉接器。 它住在推薦服務裡，輸入是使用者挑選的曲目、即時的效價–喚醒座標，以及預先算好、各自附有 item 層級效價–喚醒輪廓的 item 嵌入，輸出 top-k。匿名訪客的表示就是所選曲目嵌入的平均。論文沒有給出轉接器如何把座標與嵌入分數合併的公式，只說它把 ACE 的機制推廣到不同模型家族。

Architecture. Four microservices—front-end, track catalogue, bci-processor (EEG preprocessing and features) and recommender (scoring candidates with WarpRec)—talk over HTTP and WebSocket and can run on one machine or distributed. The recommender needs only valence–arousal coordinates in [0,1]×[0,1]; the authors call this "input swappability": any source emitting coordinates in that format plugs in, including the demo's manual mode.

EEG pipeline. The Muse 2's four channels (TP9, AF7, AF8, TP10) are sampled at 256 Hz and read in the browser via Muse-js. The backend keeps a circular buffer of 1024 samples (4 s), converts it to an MNE Raw structure, flags anomalous channels with MNE's LOF function, and adds manual visual inspection and headset repositioning. It then applies a causal 1–40 Hz band-pass and estimates theta (4–8 Hz), alpha (8–13 Hz) and beta (13–30 Hz) power with NeuroKit2; a second circular buffer holds 20 successive observations and averages each band per channel. The valence proxy is log(α_AF8/α_AF7), the arousal proxy (β_AF7+β_AF8)/(α_AF7+α_AF8), each mapped to [0,1] by a fixed-coefficient sigmoid. Coordinates update on Russell's circumplex plane at 8 Hz.

Affective adapter. It lives inside the recommender and takes the visitor's chosen tracks, the live valence–arousal coordinates, and precomputed item embeddings each tagged with an item-level valence–arousal profile, returning the top-k. An anonymous visitor is represented by the mean of the chosen tracks' embeddings. The paper gives no formula for how the adapter combines the coordinates with embedding scores, only that it generalises ACE's mechanism to other model families.

限制Limitations

沒有任何證據顯示 EEG 讓推薦變得更符合情緒。 唯一的品質評估是 live 版與離線版的 nDCG@10 比較：ACE 0.0231 對 0.0236、SASRec 0.0164 對 0.0148，Wilcoxon 檢定 p>0.05。這只說明轉接器沒有把離線準確度弄壞，而且它是在 Lastfm-VADS 的離線資料上算的，跟頭帶量到的腦波無關；測了多少使用者、座標怎麼設定，論文都沒交代。沒有使用者研究，沒有把 EEG 座標跟自評情緒對照，也沒有比較「用 EEG」與「隨機座標」或「手動拖曳」的推薦差異。

訊號本身相當脆弱。 AF7、AF8 位在前額，正是眨眼與眼動、額肌電最容易污染的位置，而 1–40 Hz 帶通濾不掉眨眼的低頻成分，論文也沒有去除眼動偽跡的步驟。額葉 α 不對稱在個體之間的基線差異很大，用固定係數的 sigmoid、沒有個人化基線校正，座標很可能主要反映「這個人的頭型與頭帶戴法」而非當下情緒。作者在倫理聲明裡自己承認這是控制訊號，不是經驗證的情緒辨識。

延遲數字的解讀也要小心。 兩個模型的端到端延遲約 80.0±7.4 與 90.3±7.0 ms，都低於 8 Hz 串流的 125 ms 期限；但 4 秒的分析窗加上 20 筆觀測的平滑，代表座標對情緒變化的實際反應是秒級，8 Hz 只是更新頻率。而且推薦清單是按需重排，不是每次座標更新都重算。基準機器是 MacBook Pro M4 單機，沒有網路延遲。

There is no evidence that EEG makes recommendations more emotionally fitting. The only quality evaluation compares live and offline nDCG@10: 0.0231 vs 0.0236 for ACE and 0.0164 vs 0.0148 for SASRec, Wilcoxon p>0.05. That shows only that the adapter does not break offline accuracy, and it is computed on Lastfm-VADS offline data with no connection to headset EEG; the number of users and how coordinates were set are not reported. There is no user study, no comparison of EEG coordinates against self-reported emotion, and no comparison of EEG against random coordinates or manual dragging.

The signal itself is fragile. AF7 and AF8 sit on the forehead, exactly where blinks, eye movements and frontalis EMG contaminate most, and a 1–40 Hz band-pass does not remove the low-frequency blink component; no ocular-artifact removal is described. Frontal alpha asymmetry has large inter-individual baseline differences, and with a fixed-coefficient sigmoid and no personal baseline, the coordinates may mainly reflect head shape and headset fit rather than current emotion. The authors' own ethics statement concedes this is a control signal, not validated emotion recognition.

Read the latency numbers carefully too. End-to-end latency for the two models is about 80.0±7.4 and 90.3±7.0 ms, both under the 125 ms deadline of the 8 Hz stream; but a 4 s analysis window plus 20-observation smoothing means the coordinates respond to emotional change on a timescale of seconds—8 Hz is only the update rate. The list is also re-ranked on demand, not on every coordinate update. The benchmark ran locally on a MacBook Pro M4, with no network delay.

討論Discussion

這篇的價值在於把「接得起來」這件事做完，並把介面做對。 輸入可替換性是個好設計：手動拖曳模式讓沒有頭帶的人也能操作，同時也讓這個架構可以拿來接任何別的情緒來源（例如臉部表情或心率）。對 MIR 研究者來說，一個能在瀏覽器裡跑、不必裝驅動、session 結束就清掉所有資料的實驗平台，是做情緒推薦使用者研究時的實用基礎設施。

但手動模式也恰好暴露了這篇最該補的實驗。 既然系統對座標來源不敏感，最直接的對照就是：同一批受試者，一組用 EEG 座標，一組用自己拖曳的座標，一組用隨機座標，比較他們對推薦的評分。展示裡已經內建「喜歡／無感／不喜歡」三級評分，資料收集的管道其實都在了。沒有這個對照，讀者無法知道 EEG 帶來的是情緒資訊還是雜訊。

另一個值得追問的是 nDCG 的絕對值。 0.02 左右的 nDCG@10 在 Last.fm 這類稀疏資料上並不罕見，但它也表示兩個骨幹在離線任務上本來就很弱；在這個基礎上談「情緒導向的重排」，重排的效果很可能被基礎排序的雜訊淹沒。

The paper's value is in finishing the wiring and getting the interface right. Input swappability is good design: the manual drag mode lets people without a headset use it, and it also lets the architecture accept any other affect source (facial expression, heart rate). For MIR researchers, a browser-based platform that needs no drivers and wipes all data at the end of a session is useful infrastructure for user studies on affective recommendation.

But the manual mode also exposes exactly the experiment this paper most needs. Since the system is agnostic to where coordinates come from, the obvious control is: same participants, one condition with EEG coordinates, one with coordinates they drag themselves, one with random coordinates, and compare their ratings of the recommendations. The demo already has a three-tier liked/indifferent/disliked rating, so the data-collection path exists. Without that control, readers cannot tell whether EEG contributes emotional information or noise.

Another question worth pressing is the absolute nDCG. An nDCG@10 around 0.02 is not unusual on sparse Last.fm-style data, but it also means both backbones are weak on the offline task to begin with; on that base, the effect of "emotion-guided re-ranking" may well be drowned out by noise in the underlying ranking.

I picked it for two reasons: today's arXiv listings had few MIR papers, and this one has checkable musical-design content (for example, when flight levels map to semitones, 25–27% of note pairs land on a minor second, tritone or major seventh); and it is unusually candid about what it did not originate. Caveat: there is no listening test or user study at all, the only listening judgements are the author's own, and the code and draft were written by Claude Code, which the author fully discloses.

動機Motivation

ADS-B 是飛機自己廣播的位置與高度訊息，一台小型地面接收器就能收到。作者的 Pitchaxis 計畫想讓這些資料取得音樂形式：高度給音高、方位給在小節中的位置、訊號強度給響度。Patchbay 是這個計畫的編輯器，名字取自錄音室裡用跳線在設備之間路由訊號的面板。

作者的出發點是：資料不是一條訊號。在動手設計積木之前，他先問接收器回報的東西長什麼樣子——形狀（連續、階梯、會繞回的角度、類別）、解析度、誰產生、能維持多久不變——因為這些性質決定了該提供什麼積木。最關鍵的一個觀察是：如果讓資料抵達觸發音符，音符就會照接收器的時鐘每 5 秒重複一次，不管飛機做了什麼；如果讓數值改變觸發音符，慢變的訊號又幾乎不會響。所以「什麼時候發聲」必須跟「資料說了什麼」分開處理。

ADS-B is the position and altitude data that aircraft broadcast about themselves, which a small ground receiver can collect. The author's Pitchaxis project aims to give these data musical form: pitch from altitude, place in the bar from bearing, loudness from signal strength. Patchbay is the project's editor, named after the studio panel of jacks and cables that routes signals between devices.

The starting point is that the data are not one signal. Before designing blocks, the author asks what the receiver's output looks like—shape (continuous, stepped, wrapping angle, category), resolution, who produces it, how long it stays put—because those properties decide which blocks to offer. The key observation: if data arrival triggers notes, they repeat on the receiver's clock every 5 s regardless of what the aircraft does; if a change in value triggers notes, slow signals almost never sound. So "when a note starts" has to be handled separately from "what the data say".

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者自己把「不是首創」寫得很清楚，這點值得先講。 與節拍同步、掃過被追蹤物體就觸發音符的旋轉線，scoreTable*（2006）已用在攝影機追蹤的實體圓盤上，ProjectRadarSequencer（2013）也做過；在 ADS-B 飛機上用擴張距離環觸發音符的，是 SYNTHRADAR（2026 年 9 月 30 日發布），而作者是看過它之後才在 10 月 16 日實作距離掃描。把掃描放進可接線的環境也不新：Max 的 PingSeq（2014）與 Voltage Modular 的 Sonar Sequencer 都有。直接把 ADS-B 變聲音的還有 adsbTheremin（2015，可重播並輸出 MIDI）與給航管員用、每 5 秒一筆資料就觸發的 SonAir（2023）。

真正剩下的新東西很窄：「飛機集合」這種連線型別。 一條線帶著所有在視野內的飛機與它們的全部欄位，Read 把它按欄位打開、Select 按飛機篩選，衍生出的數值都以飛機為鍵，最後在每架飛機一個聲部的 Voice 積木重新會合。這跟 VCV Rack 等模組合成環境的多聲道（polyphonic）線最像——一條線同時帶多個聲道——作者也承認兩者還沒比較過。型別化連線本身則有 libmapper 與 Max 的先例。

放在聲音化（sonification）的脈絡裡， 這屬於 Hermann、Hunt 與 Neuhoff 在《The Sonification Handbook》（2011）所說的參數映射聲音化；高度對音高的線性映射再四捨五入到最近 MIDI 音，Brown 等人在 ICAD 2003 的指引就這樣建議。本文補上的是常數與錨點的選擇：從 FL180 起，每 1,000 呎一個半音。

The author is explicit about what is not original, and that is worth stating first. A rotating line locked to a tempo that triggers notes as it sweeps tracked objects was published by scoreTable* (2006) for camera-tracked pucks and again by ProjectRadarSequencer (2013); an expanding range ring over ADS-B aircraft that triggers notes was published by SYNTHRADAR (30 September 2026), and the author implemented the range scan on 16 October after seeing it. Scans inside patching environments are not new either: PingSeq for Max (2014) and Sonar Sequencer for Voltage Modular. Direct ADS-B-to-sound systems include adsbTheremin (2015, with replay and MIDI output) and SonAir (2023), for air traffic controllers, which triggers on each data arrival every fifth second.

What remains new is narrow: the "aircraft set" as a connection kind. One cable carries every aircraft in view with all its fields; Read opens it by field, Select filters it by aircraft, and derived numbers stay keyed by aircraft so they meet again at a Voice with one voice per aircraft. The closest analogue is the polyphonic cable of modular environments like VCV Rack—one cable carrying several channels—and the author admits the two have not been compared. Typed connections per se have precedents in libmapper and Max.

In sonification terms, this is parameter-mapping sonification as described in Hermann, Hunt and Neuhoff's *The Sonification Handbook* (2011); a linear altitude-to-pitch map rounded to the nearest MIDI note is what Brown et al.'s ICAD 2003 guidelines recommend. What this paper adds is the choice of constant and anchor: one semitone per 1,000 ft starting at FL180.

方法Method

訊號普查。 用同一台跑 readsb 的接收器錄了 356 幀、1,797 秒，共 363 架不同飛機。readsb 在飛機最後一次被聽到後還會繼續列一段時間（數值凍結），所以只算最近 5 秒內被聽到的：73% 的飛機幀符合，平均每幀列出 127.5 架中有 92.9 架。各訊號維持在容差內的典型時間差很多：訊息速率與距離約 10 秒、方位 16 秒、訊號強度 25 秒、高度等 15–96 秒，駕駛設定值 152–344 秒。20 個數值通道以秩相關做平均連結聚類，在 0.5 的門檻下只剩 10 群——距離、氣壓高度、GPS 高度、選定高度、地速與氣壓設定落在同一群，所以「高度給音高、地速給音色」其實是用了同一個變異來源兩次。

四種連線與 27 個積木。 四種連線回答四個問題：哪些飛機（飛機集合）、每個聲音用什麼數字（數值）、何時發聲（事件）、聽起來如何（音訊）。27 個積木分成 Sky、Rhythm、Shape、Sound、Effects 五組。規則包括：飛機集合整包傳遞、數值以飛機為鍵；接錯型別時編輯器拒絕並用文字解釋原因，12 種錯誤配對中有 3 種會建議插入橋接積木；拒絕形成迴路；每個積木都有即時讀數，不會無聲失敗。

掃描積木。 依方位掃描時是一條旋轉的線，依距離掃描時是一個擴張或收縮的環，週期以節拍時鐘的小節計（預設 96 BPM、每小節 4 拍、一圈 2 小節＝5 秒，重播速度 4 倍時一圈涵蓋 20 秒的錄製資料）。實作上每 25 ms 評估一次、往前看 120 ms，檢查這一步掃過了哪些飛機，再以線性內插把事件時間放進步內。作者明列三個限制：用的是評估當下的位置、掃描前緣沒有寬度、掃描只是眾多事件來源之一。

Signal census. Using the same readsb receiver, the author recorded 356 frames over 1,797 s covering 363 distinct aircraft. readsb keeps listing an aircraft for a while after it was last heard (with frozen values), so only aircraft heard in the last 5 s count: 73% of aircraft-frames, or 92.9 of the 127.5 aircraft listed per frame on average. Typical stability times differ widely: about 10 s for message rate and range, 16 s for bearing, 25 s for signal strength, 15–96 s for altitude and the like, and 152–344 s for pilot-selected values. Average-linkage clustering of 20 numeric channels by rank correlation leaves only 10 groups at a 0.5 cut—range, pressure altitude, GPS altitude, selected altitude, ground speed and pressure setting fall into one—so "altitude to pitch, ground speed to timbre" draws on the same source of variation twice.

Four kinds and 27 blocks. The four kinds answer four questions: which aircraft (aircraft set), what number goes into each sound (value), when it starts (event), what it sounds like (audio). The 27 blocks fall into five groups: Sky, Rhythm, Shape, Sound, Effects. Rules include: the aircraft set travels whole and values are keyed by aircraft; a mistyped join is refused with an explanation in words, and for 3 of the 12 mismatched pairs the editor offers a bridging block; loops are refused; every block shows a live readout, so nothing fails silently.

The scan block. Scanning by bearing it is a rotating line; by range, a ring that grows or shrinks; its period is set in bars of the beat clock (defaults 96 BPM, 4 beats per bar, one turn of 2 bars = 5 s, covering 20 s of capture at the default 4× replay). In practice the patch is evaluated every 25 ms with a 120 ms look-ahead, checks which aircraft the scan passed during the step, and places each event inside the step by linear interpolation. The author lists three limits: positions are those at evaluation time, the scan front has no width, and the scan is only one of many event sources.

限制Limitations

沒有聽眾，也沒有使用者。 作者在「沒有建的東西」那節自己寫得很直白：這個工具只被作者本人使用和測試過，沒有觀察過任何使用者；Ground 範例「聽起來有點音樂性」是唯一的聆聽判斷，而且出自建造者本人。他也坦承 77 種機型聽不出來、3 種引擎類別聽得出來，只是設計判斷而非實驗結果。所以這篇關於「好不好聽」「有沒有用」的問題一律沒有答案，論文也明說不主張掃描能幫任何人完成任務或降低工作負荷。

型別系統只做到「種類」為止，已知會出錯。 數值線不帶單位、範圍或形狀：Glide 把方位從 359 度移到 1 度時會繞遠路，Summary 對 359 和 1 取平均得到 180——作者列為已知錯誤。Glide 用真實時間、Enter/exit 與 Level-off 用資料時間，重播速度不是 1 倍時兩者會不一致。Closest pair 用直線外推預測最接近點，在轉彎與改平時會錯，位置也可能晚到 6 秒。

資料與可重現性。 編輯器只能重播兩段 2026 年 9 月 9 日在新澤西中部錄的、各 355 幀約 30 分鐘的資料，不能接使用者自己的接收器或即時資料；打包時約三分之一的列被丟掉（例如 A 段 54,525 列只留 36,688）。普查統計來自單一接收器、單一錄音，相關性結構反映的是那片天空的交通，不是飛機的普遍性質。掃描積木本身沒有提交的測試。

No listeners and no users. The author is blunt in the "what is not built" section: the tool has been used and tested only by the author, no user has been observed, and "the Ground example sounds somewhat musical" is the only listening judgement, made by its builder. The author also concedes that "77 aircraft types can't be told apart by ear but 3 engine classes can" is a design judgement, not an experimental result. So every question about whether it sounds good or is useful goes unanswered, and the paper explicitly does not claim that a scan helps anyone do a task or reduces workload.

The type system stops at the kind and is known to misbehave. Value cables carry no unit, range or shape: Glide moves a bearing from 359 to 1 degrees the long way round, and Summary averages 359 and 1 to 180—listed as known faults. Glide counts real time while Enter/exit and Level-off count data time, so they disagree at any replay speed other than 1×. Closest pair predicts closest approach by straight-line extrapolation, which is wrong in turns and level-offs, with positions up to 6 s old.

Data and reproducibility. The editor can only replay two captures recorded on 9 September 2026 in central New Jersey, each 355 frames of about 30 minutes; it cannot read the user's own receiver or a live feed, and packing drops about a third of rows (e.g. capture A keeps 36,688 of 54,525). The census statistics come from one receiver and one recording, so the correlation structure reflects that sky's traffic, not aircraft in general. The scan block itself has no committed test.

討論Discussion

這篇最有 MIR 味道的部分是第五節的和聲檢查，而且有數字。 「飛行高度層」範例故意用每 1,000 呎一個半音，代價是：A、B 兩段錄音分別有 94% 與 99% 的幀裡至少有兩個音站在相鄰高度層，所有音對中有 27% 與 25% 落在小二度、三全音或大七度。改成每層 2 個半音降到 16%，3 個半音是 25–26%，4 個半音只剩 0.2–0.3%——但那時只剩三個音級，FL180 到 FL450 會跨九個八度。這組數字把「映射常數」從美學偏好變成可以權衡的設計參數，附帶的 check_intervals.py 也能重算。

它驗證前提的方式也值得學。 這個映射成立的前提是巡航飛機真的停在高度層上。作者檢查後發現 99.8% 與 99.6% 的平飛列落在高度層 ±100 呎內，而均勻分布的機率是 22.5%——但他主動說明這只是檢查不是發現：規則本來就要求如此、高度本來就以 25 呎為格（Mode S 編碼）、爬升率以 64 呎/分為階。把「看起來漂亮的統計」主動降級，在設計類論文裡不常見。

Ground 積木把資料與和聲的分工講清楚了。 和弦進行（例如 A 小調 i VII VI V）是預先寫好的，資料只決定何時換和弦、哪些飛機發聲、各自用哪個和弦音；平飛時取最近的和弦音，爬升或下降超過每分 300 呎時改用經過音。作者還指出「最近和弦音」會讓 5,750 呎的飛機與低音形成平行五度，於是做了總移動量最小、把平行五／八度計為 6 個半音代價的聲部進行選項。最後要提：作者揭露程式與初稿由 Claude Code 與多個 Claude 模型完成，作者互動約 2.3 小時、代理工作約 15.6 小時——這篇本身就是一個「人指揮、AI 寫」的研究產出樣本。

The most MIR-flavoured part is Section 5's harmonic check, and it comes with numbers. The Flight levels example deliberately uses one semitone per 1,000 ft, at a cost: in 94% and 99% of frames of captures A and B, at least two tones sit on adjacent levels, and 27% and 25% of all pairs fall in the minor second, tritone or major seventh classes. At two semitones per level that drops to 16%, at three it is 25–26%, at four only 0.2–0.3%—but then only three pitch classes remain and FL180 to FL450 would span nine octaves. These numbers turn the mapping constant from an aesthetic preference into a tradeable design parameter, and the accompanying check_intervals.py reproduces them.

Its way of checking a premise is also worth copying. The mapping assumes cruising aircraft really sit on flight levels. The author finds 99.8% and 99.6% of level rows within ±100 ft of a level, against 22.5% under a uniform spread—but volunteers that this is a check, not a finding: the rules require it, altitudes come on a 25 ft grid (Mode S encoding), and climb rate comes in 64 ft/min steps. Proactively downgrading a pretty statistic is rare in design papers.

The Ground block makes the division between data and harmony explicit. The chord cycle (e.g. i VII VI V in A minor) is composed; the data decide only when the chord changes, which aircraft sound and on which chord tones; level aircraft take the nearest chord tone, and those climbing or descending faster than 300 ft/min take a passing note. The author notes that "nearest tone" makes an aircraft at 5,750 ft move in parallel fifths with the bass, so adds a voice-led option minimising total motion and costing each parallel fifth or octave as 6 semitones. Finally: the author discloses that the code and draft were produced by Claude Code and several Claude models, with about 2.3 hours of author interaction against about 15.6 hours of agent work—the paper is itself a sample of human-directed, AI-written research output.
