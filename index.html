Weakly Supervised Tabla Stroke Transcription via an Adaptive Dynamic Rhythm Language Model (ADRM)

透過自適應動態節奏語言模型（ADRM）進行弱監督塔布拉鼓點轉譜

Rahul Bapusaheb Kodag, Vipul Arora
arXiv:2608.30314 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-31
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把塔布拉鼓點轉譜重新表述成不需要逐音時間標註的弱監督問題，用結合塔拉節奏先驗與局部自適應馬可夫模型的 ADRM 對 CTC 解碼網格重新評分；四個資料集上鼓點錯誤率相對純聲學解碼降低 18.9–35.4%，甚至在兩個資料集上打贏完全監督式的 S&C 基線（29.9–31.3% 對 40.2–67.4%）。Why this one: Reframes tabla stroke transcription as weakly supervised, needing no onset-level timing labels, by rescoring a CTC decoding lattice with ADRM, which combines a tala-conditioned rhythmic prior with a local adaptive Markov model; stroke error rate drops 18.9-35.4% relative to acoustic-only decoding across four datasets, and even beats the fully supervised S&C baseline on two of them (29.9-31.3% vs. 40.2-67.4%).

動機Motivation

塔布拉鼓是印度斯坦音樂裡最常用的節奏樂器之一，但比起有音高的樂器，鼓點轉譜研究一直偏少。現有的自動轉譜大多需要逐個鼓點標出精確起始時間的強監督標註，成本很高；但塔布拉的節拍循環結構（塔拉）讓「序列層級」的標註——只寫出鼓點順序、不標時間——變得既有音樂意義又可行。這篇論文把塔布拉鼓點轉譜（TST）重新表述成一個弱監督的序列建模問題，不需要逐音精細對齊，並主張塔拉提供的節奏框架本身就能拿來當額外訊號，在沒有時間標註的情況下幫忙修正聲學辨識的錯誤。

Tabla is one of the most widely used rhythmic instruments in Hindustani music, yet stroke transcription has received far less attention than pitched-instrument transcription. Existing automatic transcription mostly needs costly onset-level annotation marking the exact timing of every stroke, but tabla's cyclic metrical structure (tala) makes sequence-level annotation -- writing down only the stroke order, without timing -- both musically meaningful and practically feasible. The paper reframes tabla stroke transcription (TST) as a weakly supervised sequence modeling problem needing no fine-grained temporal alignment, and argues that tala's rhythmic framework can itself serve as an extra signal to correct acoustic-recognition errors even without timing labels.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文比較的先前系統包括：S&C（segment-and-classify）分段分類法，先偵測起始點再逐段分類鼓點，是完全監督式的做法；OTM，一種跨鼓點類別的遷移學習方法；PTM2，用元學習處理少樣本鼓點辨識——但這兩者都仍然需要逐音起始時間標註；以及 Gowriprasad 等人 2025 年提出的無監督鼓點聚類法，完全不用文字標籤。

這篇論文把 CTC（連結時序分類）拿來做聲學建模——CTC 本身借自語音辨識，先前已用於免對齊的音樂轉譜（如鼓組轉譜）——但差別在於，論文額外設計了自適應動態節奏語言模型（ADRM），把塔拉的節奏先驗跟局部節奏適應結合起來對 CTC 產生的解碼網格重新評分，這是先前純聲學或純規則式方法都沒有做過的。

The paper compares against several concrete prior systems: S&C (segment-and-classify), which first detects onsets and then classifies each segment and is fully supervised; OTM, a transfer-learning approach across stroke categories; and PTM2, a meta-learning method for few-shot stroke recognition -- both OTM and PTM2 still require onset-level annotation. It also compares against an unsupervised stroke-clustering method (Gowriprasad et al., 2025) that uses no textual labels at all.

The paper borrows CTC (Connectionist Temporal Classification) from speech recognition for acoustic modeling, a technique already used in alignment-free music transcription such as drum transcription, but its distinguishing move is the Adaptive Dynamic Rhythm Language Model (ADRM), which combines a tala-conditioned global rhythmic prior with local adaptive rhythm modeling to rescore the CTC decoding lattice -- something no prior acoustic-only or purely rule-based method had done.

方法Method

系統先用 12 層的 C-TDNN-F 聲學模型把梅爾頻譜特徵轉成鼓點的 CTC 解碼網格，再用 ADRM 重新評分。ADRM 由兩部分組成：全域節奏先驗（一個以塔拉為條件的貝氏 n-gram 模型，或一個雙向 GRU 語言模型），以及局部自適應節奏模型（用狄利克雷參數化的一階馬可夫模型，搭配指數遺忘、記憶大約 33 個鼓點，遺忘率 ρ=0.03）。兩者以權重 λ=0.4 內插合併，並依聲學信心（用解碼熵計算）動態調整節奏訊號的權重（β=0.6），信心低時更依賴節奏、信心高時更依賴聲學。

重新評分要解決一個技術難題：標準 CTC 網格會把不同鼓點歷史的路徑合併到共同節點，沒辦法區分節奏脈絡。論文用「狀態展開」把每個節點擴充成（節點, 節奏參數）的元組來保留脈絡，再用帶剪枝的優先佇列搜尋（beam=150）找出最佳路徑。論文也釋出了兩個新資料集：133 分鐘、6 種塔拉、30 種鼓點的塔布拉即興演奏資料集（TID），以及對應的合成版本 TID-S。

The system first turns log-Mel features into a CTC decoding lattice of strokes using a 12-layer C-TDNN-F acoustic model, then rescores that lattice with ADRM. ADRM has two parts: a global rhythmic prior (either a tala-conditioned Bayesian n-gram model or a bidirectional GRU language model), and a local adaptive rhythm model (a Dirichlet-parameterized first-order Markov model with exponential forgetting, retaining roughly 33 strokes of memory at forgetting rate rho=0.03). The two are interpolated with weight lambda=0.4, and the rhythm signal's influence is dynamically scaled by acoustic confidence (computed from decoding entropy) with weight beta=0.6 -- leaning more on rhythm when the acoustic model is unsure, and more on acoustics when it is confident.

Rescoring solves a specific technical problem: a standard CTC lattice merges paths with different stroke histories at shared nodes, losing rhythmic context. The paper uses "state expansion," representing each node as a (node, rhythm-parameter) tuple to preserve that context, then searches with a pruned priority queue (beam=150) to find the best path. The paper also releases two new datasets: a 133-minute Tabla Improvisation Dataset (TID) spanning 6 talas and 30 strokes, and a synthetic counterpart, TID-S.

限制Limitations

論文沒有獨立的限制章節，但在誤差分析裡承認：刪除型錯誤（漏掉的鼓點）改善最少，因為一旦某個鼓點在解碼網格裡就完全消失，重新評分也救不回來；聲學上容易混淆的鼓點（如 Dhin 與 Dha、Tin 與 Na）光靠節奏模型也分不開，這類替換型錯誤仍是最大的誤差來源。起訖點的精細定位也還做得不好——在 50 毫秒的嚴格容忍窗下，F1 只有 6–9%，要放寬到 500 毫秒才能到 32–36%。

The paper has no dedicated limitations section, but its error analysis admits that deletion errors -- strokes that vanish entirely -- improve the least, since a stroke missing from the decoding lattice cannot be recovered by rescoring alone; acoustically confusable strokes (Dhin vs. Dha, Tin vs. Na) remain hard to separate by rhythm alone, and these substitution errors stay the largest error source. Fine-grained onset localization is also still weak -- F1 is only 6-9% under a strict 50ms tolerance window, rising to 32-36% only once the window widens to 500ms.

討論Discussion

ADRM 帶來一致且顯著的改善：在四個資料集上，相對於純聲學解碼，鼓點錯誤率（SER）分別降低 18.9%（TSD）、31.7–32.4%（HMR）、29.7–34.1%（TID）與 34.3–35.4%（TID-S）。更值得注意的是，論文的弱監督系統在 TSD 與 HMR 上都打贏了完全監督式的 S&C 基線（S&C 的 SER 分別是 40.2% 與 67.4%，遠高於論文方法的 29.9–31.3%），代表塔拉節奏先驗提供的訊號，補足了不需要逐音標註所損失的資訊。消融實驗顯示 λ=0.4（全域與局部節奏各半左右）、ρ=0.03、β=0.6 都是各自維度上的最佳設定，任何一端（純全域或純局部）都會讓 SER 變差。

論文的立場很克制：只主張自己降低了轉譜錯誤率、而且贏過某個特定的監督基線，沒有宣稱解決了塔布拉轉譜或超越所有既有方法。未來工作明確列出線上即時解碼，以及把 ADRM 搬到卡納提克音樂的姆里單伽鼓上。

ADRM delivers consistent, sizeable gains: relative to acoustic-only decoding, stroke error rate (SER) drops by 18.9% on TSD, 31.7-32.4% on HMR, 29.7-34.1% on TID, and 34.3-35.4% on TID-S. More notably, the paper's weakly supervised system beats the fully supervised S&C baseline on both TSD and HMR (S&C's SER is 40.2% and 67.4%, far above the paper's 29.9-31.3%), showing that the tala rhythmic prior's signal makes up for the information lost by skipping onset-level labels. Ablations show lambda=0.4 (roughly balancing global and local rhythm), rho=0.03, and beta=0.6 are each near-optimal along their own axis, with either extreme (pure global or pure local) making SER worse.

The paper's claim is measured: it argues only that it lowers transcription error and beats one specific supervised baseline, not that it solves tabla transcription or surpasses every existing method. Future work explicitly names online real-time decoding and porting ADRM to Carnatic mridangam.

MusGU+: Toward a Musician-Centered Evaluation Framework and Discovery Tool for Generative Music AI

MusGU+：邁向以音樂人為中心的生成式音樂 AI 評估框架與探索工具

Laura Ibáñez-Martínez, Roser Batlle-Roca, Xavier Serra, Martín Rocamora
arXiv:2608.30940 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-31
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：提出 MusGU+ 評估框架，用適應性、可用性、可控性三個面向、共 15 項準則（三級量表：完全／部分／不支援）評估 10 個生成式音樂系統，並打造互動探索工具；發現只有 DDSP-VST、Neutone Morpho、RAVE 三個系統真正照顧到個別音樂人的限制，但論文全篇沒有公布任何系統的總分或逐項得分，只用敘事分群呈現結果。Why this one: Introduces MusGU+, a framework evaluating 10 generative music systems across three dimensions -- Adaptability, Usability, Controllability -- with 15 criteria on a three-level scale (fully/partially/not supported), plus an interactive discovery tool; finds only DDSP-VST, Neutone Morpho, and RAVE genuinely accommodate individual musicians' constraints, though the paper never publishes any system's total or per-criterion score, presenting results only as narrative clusters.

動機Motivation

生成式音樂系統常被行銷成「讓所有人都能做音樂」的民主化工具，但這篇論文指出，現有評測要嘛只看生成輸出的音質等技術指標，要嘛只看模型開放程度與可重現性，沒有人有系統地比較「這些系統對真正的音樂人來說到底好不好用」。作者引用 Suno 執行長把生成系統定位成「用來優化音樂創作，而不是培養創意與藝術探索」的說法，指出這跟許多音樂人對創作實踐的理解正好相反，也是這篇論文動機的具體例子。

Generative music systems are often marketed as tools that "democratize" music-making, but the paper argues that existing evaluations either focus on output-level technical metrics or on a model's openness and reproducibility, with no systematic comparison of whether these systems are actually practical for working musicians. As a concrete example of the gap, the authors cite Suno's CEO framing generative systems as technology "for optimizing music creation rather than fostering creativity and artistic exploration," which the paper argues runs directly counter to how many musicians understand their own creative practice.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名的先前工作分成三群。第一群是輸出層級評測，針對 MusicLM、MusicGen、Stable Audio Open 這類系統做音質或風格指標評分，但完全不管這些系統能不能被音樂人拿來用；第二群是 MusGO（Music-Generative Open AI），專門評估生成系統的透明度、可重現性，以及是否只是「假開源」（open-washing），但不涉及實際創作流程中的可用性；第三群是零星的音樂人使用經驗研究，例如 Ronchini 等人 2025 年發現音樂人很難把創作意圖轉成文字提示、Pons 等人 2025 年的觀察研究顯示把生成系統整合進實際工作流程在技術上仍然很吃力，但這些研究規模小、彼此沒有整合成一個可比較的框架。

MusGU+ 跟 MusGO 最接近，也最容易被誤認為同一件事，論文特別把兩者分開：MusGO 問「這個系統的研究做得負不負責任、開不開放」，MusGU+ 問「這個系統音樂人拿來用好不好用」，是互補而非取代的關係。

The prior work the paper names falls into three groups. The first is output-level evaluation of systems like MusicLM, MusicGen, and Stable Audio Open on audio-quality or style metrics, without regard to whether musicians can actually use them. The second is MusGO (Music-Generative Open AI), which assesses a generative system's transparency, reproducibility, and whether it amounts to "open-washing," but does not touch usability in an actual creative workflow. The third is a scattering of musician-experience studies -- for instance, Ronchini et al. (2025) found musicians struggle to translate creative intent into text prompts, and an observational study by Pons et al. (2025) found integrating generative systems into real workflows still technically demanding -- but these are small-scale and never unified into one comparable framework.

MusGU+ sits closest to MusGO and risks being conflated with it, so the paper explicitly separates the two: MusGO asks whether a system's research practice is responsible and open, while MusGU+ asks whether the system is actually usable by musicians -- a complementary question, not a substitute.

方法Method

框架分成三個評估面向：適應性（Adaptability，5 項準則，例如硬體需求、資料集大小、能不能微調）、可用性（Usability，6 項準則，例如介面完整度、即時性、是否能整合進既有工作流程）、可控性（Controllability，4 項準則，例如條件輸入的多樣性、能不能做時變控制）。每項準則用三級量表評分：完全支援、部分支援、不支援，分別對應 1、0.5、0 分。評分由一位作者查閱官方論文、文件與程式碼後初評，再由第二位作者獨立覆核，有分歧就一起檢視證據討論到共識為止。

論文評估了 10 個具代表性的生成式音樂系統，涵蓋學術（如 RAVE、AFTER、JAM）、產業研究（如 MusicGen、DDSP-VST）與商業封閉系統（Suno、Udio）三種類型，並打造了一個互動式探索工具，讓使用者可以依單一準則排序、依最低分數門檻過濾，或依「音訊輸入」「即時」等標籤篩選，而不是只給一張靜態排行榜。

The framework has three evaluation dimensions: Adaptability (5 criteria, e.g. hardware requirements, dataset size, whether the model can be fine-tuned), Usability (6 criteria, e.g. interface completeness, real-time capability, workflow integration), and Controllability (4 criteria, e.g. diversity of conditioning inputs, time-varying control). Each criterion uses a three-level scale -- fully, partially, or not supported -- mapped to scores of 1, 0.5, and 0. One author scores each system from official papers, documentation, and code, a second author independently reviews it, and disagreements are resolved by jointly re-examining the evidence until consensus is reached.

The paper evaluates 10 representative generative music systems spanning academic (RAVE, AFTER, JAM), industrial-research (MusicGen, DDSP-VST), and closed commercial (Suno, Udio) categories, and builds an interactive discovery tool that lets users sort by a single axis, filter by a minimum score threshold, or filter by tags like "audio input" or "real-time," instead of offering only a static leaderboard.

限制Limitations

論文自陳四個限制。第一，完全沒有做正式的使用者研究——所有評分都來自文獻回顧與作者自身經驗，還沒有真的請音樂人做過以工作流程為主的評測。第二，範圍只涵蓋音訊領域、面向通用音樂生成的系統，不涵蓋符號或 MIDI 生成、特定樂器模型，或產出中介音樂表徵的工具。第三，框架不評估藝術或美學價值、創作支援程度、協作潛力這類體驗層面的東西，這些需要另外做以使用者為中心的評測才補得上。第四，框架不直接評估著作權、文化偏誤、勞動影響等倫理與法律面向，作者強調 MusGU+「補充而非取代」倫理評估。

The paper states four limitations of its own. First, there is no formal user study at all -- every score comes from literature review and the authors' own experience, not from musicians actually doing workflow-based evaluation. Second, the scope covers only audio-domain, general-purpose music generation systems, excluding symbolic or MIDI-based generation, instrument-specific models, and tools that produce intermediate musical representations. Third, the framework does not evaluate experiential dimensions such as artistic or aesthetic value, creative support, or collaboration potential, which would require separate user-centered evaluation. Fourth, it does not directly evaluate ethical or legal dimensions like authorship, cultural bias, or labor impact; the authors stress that MusGU+ is meant to "complement rather than replace" ethical assessment.

討論Discussion

三個面向的發現都清楚分群，但論文刻意不用單一總分排名，而是用敘事分組呈現。適應性上，只有 DDSP-VST、Neutone Morpho、RAVE 三個系統真正照顧到個別音樂人會遇到的限制（如硬體與資料量）；AFTER、YuE、JAM、Stable Audio Open Small、MusicGen 這五個雖然提供正式的微調管道，但技術、運算與資料門檻都偏高；Suno 與 Udio 則完全沒有使用者可自行調整模型的管道。可控性上同樣是 DDSP-VST、Neutone Morpho、RAVE、AFTER 這四個系統對可解釋的音樂特徵提供最細緻的時變控制，而 Suno 與 Udio 敬陪末座，只能做全域式、特徵高度糾纏在一起的條件控制。

這裡有個值得指出的方法論落差：論文在 15 項準則上都給出 0、0.5、1 三級分數，理論上完全可以彙整成一張定量表格，但論文最終選擇只用互動視覺化跟文字敘述呈現分群，沒有公布任何一個系統的總分或逐項得分。這讓讀者能看出「誰屬於哪一群」，卻拿不到可以拿來複驗或比較細節差異的具體數字——這是這篇論文在方法嚴謹度上留下的一個具體缺口，而不是無法避免的限制。

All three dimensions produce a clear grouping, but the paper deliberately avoids a single overall ranking, presenting narrative clusters instead. On adaptability, only DDSP-VST, Neutone Morpho, and RAVE genuinely accommodate the constraints an individual musician faces (hardware, data volume); a middle group of five (AFTER, YuE, JAM, Stable Audio Open Small, MusicGen) offer formal fine-tuning pathways but with high technical, computational, and data barriers; and Suno and Udio offer no user-driven adaptation pathway at all. Controllability shows the same four systems (DDSP-VST, Neutone Morpho, RAVE, AFTER) providing the most fine-grained, time-varying control over interpretable musical features, while Suno and Udio rank lowest, offering only global, heavily entangled conditioning.

One methodological gap is worth flagging: the paper scores all 15 criteria on a 0/0.5/1 scale, which could in principle be compiled into a quantitative table, but the paper ultimately presents only interactive visualization and narrative grouping, without publishing any system's total or per-criterion score. This lets a reader see which cluster a system falls into but leaves no concrete numbers to re-derive or compare in detail -- a specific rigor gap in the paper, not an unavoidable limitation.

Artificial Rosetta Stone: Constrained Maximum A Posteriori (MAP) Reconstruction of Symbolic Raga Sequences via Order-k Markov Models

人工羅塞塔石碑：透過 k 階馬可夫模型對拉格符號序列進行受限最大後驗（MAP）重建

Saanvi Raghavendran, Abhishek Bhattacharjee

為什麼挑這篇：把拉格符號序列的缺失片段重建，形式化成有理論保證的受限最大後驗問題，用 k 階狄利克雷－馬可夫模型與動態規劃求解（時間複雜度 O(T·N^(k+1))）；合成實驗最佳準確率 0.539（k=2，缺失 10%），真實 Yaman 錄音實驗（30 段序列）在缺失 30% 時達到 0.414 準確率，是均勻基準 1/7 的 2.9 倍，但論文明確聲明只驗證了可行性，不代表歷史真確性。Why this one: Formalizes reconstructing missing passages in raga symbolic sequences as a theoretically guaranteed constrained MAP problem, solved with order-k Dirichlet-Markov models and dynamic programming (O(T*N^(k+1)) time); best synthetic accuracy is 0.539 (k=2, 10% missing), and a real Yaman-recording pilot (30 sequences) reaches 0.414 accuracy at 30% missingness, 2.9x the uniform baseline of 1/7, though the paper explicitly claims only feasibility, not historical authenticity.

動機Motivation

印度斯坦古典音樂的身分認同來自音高素材、特徵性的音型、樂句手法、重音、裝飾音、音域與演出慣例的組合，不只是一個音階；但實際的錄音檔案常常不完整、有噪音、切分不佳、轉寫不確定，或乾脆缺了一段演出。論文把「補回缺失片段」正式定義成一個逆問題：觀測到的序列只帶有部分資訊，而一個拉格（raga）的語法帶有限制允許的補全方式。作者刻意選擇一條理論優先的路線——先寫下精確的機率模型、推導估計式、界定容許補全的集合、證明解碼器最優、量化統計不確定性——而不是先拼湊多個機器學習模組、事後再補一個收斂定理。

Hindustani classical music's identity comes from a combination of pitch material, characteristic movements, phraseology, emphasis, ornamentation, register, and performance convention -- not just a scale; but real archival recordings are often incomplete, noisy, poorly segmented, transcribed with uncertainty, or missing entire passages. The paper formally casts filling in a missing passage as an inverse problem: the observed sequence carries only partial information, while a raga's grammar encodes constraints on what completions are admissible. The authors deliberately choose a theory-first route -- writing down a precise probability model, deriving the estimator, defining the admissible completion set, proving the decoder's optimality, and quantifying statistical uncertainty -- rather than assembling several machine-learning modules and attaching a convergence theorem afterward.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名了幾種具體的先前方法並說明差異。統計上，馬可夫與機率自動機模型早就被用來對旋律結構做統計建模，把「上下文」轉成條件機率，但表達力受限於固定的階數；變階馬可夫模型與 N-gram 模型則常用在演算作曲上，捕捉局部依賴關係。這篇論文承認自己延續了這條「透明機率核心」的路線，但把目標從自由生成改成有條件的重建。隱馬可夫模型能表示不可直接觀測的潛在音樂狀態，論文把它當成可選的延伸，而不是拿它來背書某個沒被證明過的語法保證。

論文特別點名神經方法（VAE、階層式模型）能捕捉長距離結構，但指出一個具體缺陷：「統計上看起來合理」不等於「滿足硬性語法限制」——一個解碼器完全可能對違反明確編碼規則的序列給出非零機率。這正是這篇論文的核心主張所在：先前工作幾乎都把機率模型、容許語法、觀測機制、最佳化演算法、統計假設這五件事混在一起講，論文主張一篇重建論文應該明確講清楚自己用的是哪一種保證，這是它跟以上所有先前工作最大的不同。

The paper names several concrete prior approaches and states how it differs. Statistically, Markov and probabilistic-automaton models have long been used for melodic structure, turning "context" into conditional probability, but their expressiveness is limited by a fixed order; variable-order Markov and N-gram models are commonly used in algorithmic composition to capture local dependencies. The paper acknowledges it continues this "transparent probabilistic core" tradition but changes the goal from free generation to conditional reconstruction. Hidden Markov Models can represent latent musical states that aren't directly observed, and the paper treats HMMs as an optional extension rather than a source of unproven grammatical guarantees.

The paper specifically names neural methods (VAEs, hierarchical models) as capable of capturing long-range structure, but points out a concrete flaw: statistical plausibility does not imply satisfying hard constraints -- a decoder can assign nonzero probability to a sequence that violates an explicitly encoded rule. This is the paper's central claim: prior work almost always conflates five distinct things -- the probability model, the admissible grammar, the observation mechanism, the optimization algorithm, and the statistical assumptions -- and the paper argues a reconstruction paper should state clearly which guarantee it is using. That is its biggest difference from all of the above.

方法Method

一個拉格被定義成一對 (𝒜_R, 𝒞_R)：𝒜_R 是有限符號字母表（例如帶音域標籤的七個 svara），𝒞_R 是明確編碼的限制條件集合（禁止的轉移、樂句模板等）。給定觀測到的片段跟缺失位置，模型用 k 階馬可夫模型（張量 P，自由參數個數為 N^k(N-1)，例如 N=7 時 k=1 到 4 分別是 42、294、2058、14406 個參數）配合對稱狄利克雷先驗做貝氏估計，得到的後驗均值可以寫成經驗頻率跟均勻分布之間的收縮插值，樣本少的上下文會被拉向均勻分布，樣本多的上下文保留原本的經驗頻率。

硬性限制透過「支持集合投影」處理：給定參考機率列跟允許的後繼集合，用 KL 散度最小化重新正規化該列，證明這是唯一符合限制的投影方式。重建問題被寫成受限的最大後驗估計，論文用動態規劃求解，證明只要序列長度有限、轉移分數只依賴前 k 個符號跟下一個符號、限制條件可以用有限的擴增狀態表示，動態規劃保證找到全域最優解，時間複雜度為 O(T·N^(k+1))，空間複雜度 O(N^k)。

A raga is defined as a pair (𝒜_R, 𝒞_R): 𝒜_R is a finite symbolic alphabet (e.g. seven register-labeled svaras), and 𝒞_R is an explicit set of encoded constraints (forbidden transitions, phrase templates, etc.). Given an observed fragment and its missing positions, the model uses an order-k Markov tensor P (with N^k(N-1) free parameters -- for N=7, orders k=1 through 4 give 42, 294, 2,058, and 14,406 parameters) with a symmetric Dirichlet prior for Bayesian estimation; the resulting posterior mean is a shrinkage interpolation between empirical frequency and the uniform distribution, pulling sparsely observed contexts toward uniformity while leaving well-supported contexts close to their empirical frequencies.

Hard constraints are handled through "support projection": given a reference probability row and an allowed set of successors, renormalizing that row by KL-divergence minimization, which the paper proves is the unique projection satisfying the constraint. Reconstruction is posed as constrained MAP estimation, solved by dynamic programming; the paper proves that as long as the sequence length is finite, the transition score depends only on the previous k symbols and the next symbol, and constraints are representable via a finite augmented state, the dynamic program is guaranteed to find the global optimum, in O(T*N^(k+1)) time and O(N^k) space.

限制Limitations

論文有一整節專門講文化與科學上的限制，態度非常克制。作者明白寫出：七個音的字母表不足以定義拉格的身分；符號量化拿掉了絕對頻率、相對音準、微分音、音高身分之間的互動，也抹去了裝飾音（gamaka）的細節；模型完全忽略節奏、拍子層級結構跟散板；重建結果不代表跟任何有文獻記載的歷史演出吻合，模型「不宣稱電腦已經還原出客觀唯一的歷史真相」。真實錄音的來源資料集也沒有完整的錄音日期、演出者身分、音高基準標註或授權資訊，作者因此把這個實驗定位成「可行性試驗，不是檔案級的重建」。

論文也主動提出倫理提醒：音樂傳統不是密碼，拉格語法也不是翻譯金鑰，計算建模這類文化遺產時應該諮詢真正的從業者與相關社群。

The paper has an entire section devoted to cultural and scientific limitations, and its tone is notably restrained. The authors state plainly that a seven-note alphabet is not sufficient to define raga identity; symbolic quantization discards the interaction between absolute frequency, relative intonation, microtonal inflection, and pitch-class identity, and erases ornamental (gamaka) detail; the model ignores rhythm, metrical structure, and rubato entirely; and reconstructions make no claim of matching any documented historical performance -- the model does "not claim the computer has recovered an objectively unique historical truth." The real-audio source dataset also lacks complete documentation of recording dates, performer identity, tonic annotation, or licensing, so the authors frame the experiment as "a feasibility pilot, not an archival reconstruction."

The paper also raises an ethical caution of its own: musical traditions are not ciphers, and a raga's grammar is not a translation key, so computationally modeling this kind of cultural heritage should involve consultation with actual practitioners and communities.

討論Discussion

合成實驗涵蓋六種拉格風格的字母表、k∈{1,2,3}、缺失率 10–50%，最佳結果出現在 k=2、缺失 10% 時，準確率 0.539，相對於均勻猜測基準的 1/7≈0.143 有明顯提升，但論文也誠實指出「準確率沒有隨階數單調上升」——k=3 在合成資料上反而不是最好的。真實錄音實驗用 42 段 Yaman 曲風錄音，經自動音高擷取、切分、量化後篩出 30 段可用序列（24 段訓練、6 段測試，約 1700 次轉移），在同樣的 k=2、缺失 10% 條件下準確率達到 0.470，缺失 30% 時是 0.414，缺失 50% 時是 0.371，全部都優於 1/7 的隨機基準，作者特別強調 30% 缺失下的 0.414「是均勻基準的 2.9 倍」。

論文的結論用詞很小心：只主張這是一個有嚴謹統計保證的重建方法，在受控條件下有效，並且合成結果驗證了實作正確；真實錄音的結果只證明「可行性」，明確表示還低於專家驗證過的水準，也不能拿來當作歷史真確性的證據。作者把「補回缺失片段的統計方法」跟「這個補全在文化上或歷史上算不算數」嚴格分開，並列出連續音高貝氏模型、隱藏樂句狀態、學習式先驗、檔案驗證等作為明確的未來工作方向，而非宣稱已經解決。

Synthetic experiments span six raga-inspired alphabets, orders k in {1,2,3}, and masking rates of 10-50%; the best result is k=2 at 10% missingness, with accuracy 0.539, clearly above the uniform-guess baseline of 1/7≈0.143, though the paper is candid that "accuracy does not improve monotonically with order" -- k=3 is not the best on synthetic data. The real-audio experiment uses 42 Yaman-style recordings, which automated pitch extraction, segmentation, and quantization narrow down to 30 usable sequences (24 for training, 6 for testing, about 1,700 transitions); under the same k=2 setting, accuracy reaches 0.470 at 10% missingness, 0.414 at 30%, and 0.371 at 50%, all above the 1/7 random baseline, with the authors specifically noting that 0.414 at 30% missingness "exceeds the uniform baseline by a factor of 2.9."

The paper's conclusion is carefully worded: it claims only a reconstruction method with rigorous statistical guarantees that works under controlled conditions, with the synthetic results validating the implementation; the real-audio results establish feasibility alone, explicitly below expert-validated performance, and are not offered as evidence of historical authenticity. The authors keep the statistical method for filling gaps strictly separate from whether that fill counts as culturally or historically valid, and list continuous-pitch Bayesian models, hidden phrase states, learned priors, and archival validation as explicit future work rather than claiming these are already solved.
