GlobalMood: A Cross-Cultural Benchmark for Music Emotion Recognition

GlobalMood：音樂情緒辨識的跨文化基準資料集

Harin Lee, Elif Çelen, Peter Harrison, Manuel Anglada-Tort, Pol van Rijn, Minsu Park, Marc Schönwiesner, Nori Jacoby (ISMIR 2025)
DOI:10.5281/zenodo.17811304 · ISMIR 2025 · 投稿submitted 2025-09-21
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：現有音樂情緒辨識（MER）資料集幾乎都建立在西方聽眾與英文詞彙上（MIREX、CAL500、DEAM、MTG-Jamendo皆如此），GlobalMood改用由下而上、參與者母語自建詞彙的方式，蒐集59國1,180首歌、5種語言2,519名參與者共988,925筆情緒評分；結果顯示Gemini 2.5 Pro的zero-shot相關已達r=0.50、逼近人類評分者間的一致度上限，而CLAP微調後從zero-shot的r=0.08提升到r=0.31，但若把非英語詞先機器翻譯成英文再微調則只到r=0.13，證明跨文化情緒理解的關鍵在母語詞彙本身，而非單純的資料量。Why this one: Existing music emotion recognition (MER) datasets -- MIREX, CAL500, DEAM, MTG-Jamendo among them -- are built almost entirely on Western listeners and English vocabulary; GlobalMood instead uses bottom-up, participant-coined native-language terms to collect 988,925 emotion ratings from 2,519 participants across 5 languages on 1,180 songs from 59 countries. Gemini 2.5 Pro's zero-shot correlation reaches r=0.50, approaching the ceiling set by human inter-rater agreement, and fine-tuning CLAP on this data lifts it from zero-shot r=0.08 to r=0.31 -- but fine-tuning on the same data machine-translated into English only reaches r=0.13, showing that native-language vocabulary itself, not mere data volume, is what drives cross-cultural emotion understanding.

動機Motivation

音樂情緒辨識（MER）的訓練資料幾乎都來自西方聽眾與英語詞彙（如MIREX 2007用AllMusic英文標籤分成的5個心情群集、CAL500用美國大學生標註的18個英文心情詞、DEAM用連續valence/arousal標註、MTG-Jamendo用56個群眾外包但幾乎全英文的心情標籤），這些資料集有志一同地把「情緒詞彙該有哪些」這件事，交給研究者事先訂好的封閉清單決定，聽者只能在清單裡打勾。作者質疑：如果某個文化裡表達音樂情緒的詞根本沒有英文對應詞（例如南韓語裡的「懇求/appeal」），用固定清單蒐集資料從一開始就注定漏掉這些詞，不管後續模型訓練得多好都補不回來。

Music emotion recognition (MER) datasets are trained almost entirely on Western listeners using English vocabulary -- MIREX 2007's five mood clusters drawn from AllMusic English tags, CAL500's 18 English mood terms rated by US undergraduates, DEAM's continuous valence/arousal ratings, and MTG-Jamendo's 56 crowdsourced but still nearly all-English mood tags. These datasets share one assumption: what emotion vocabulary even exists is decided upfront by a closed list the researchers wrote, and listeners can only check boxes from that list. The authors ask: if a term used to describe musical emotion in some culture has no English equivalent at all (e.g. Korean's "appeal/plead"), a fixed-list collection method guarantees that term is missing from the start, and no amount of downstream model training can recover it.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景一節點名的既有系統分兩條路線。第一條是純西方的心情資料集：MIREX 2007（240–250首西方歌曲，5個心情群集，取自AllMusic英文標籤）、CAL500（500首美國流行/搖滾，18個英文詞，由美國大學生標註）、DEAM（2,058首，連續valence/arousal標註）、MTG-Jamendo（18,486首，56個自由眾包但仍以英文為主的標籤），以及用歐洲聽眾建立、共45個描述詞的GEMS分類法（用來標註Emotify資料集）。第二條是已經注意到跨文化落差的先驅研究：Hu等人（2014）比較K-pop的南韓與美國聽眾心情標註，但做法是把MIREX的心情類別直接翻譯成韓文，南韓標註者仍被限制在西方定義的類別裡作答。

跟GlobalMood關係最近的前身研究，是作者群自己先前發表的Lee et al.（2021）——比較美國、巴西、南韓三國聽眾對同一批歌曲的心情標註，發現「energetic」「sad」等詞跨文化一致度高，但「love」「dreamy」等抽象詞分歧很大。GlobalMood跟這個前身研究的差別在於規模與方法：從3國、9個研究者預先定義的固定類別，擴大到59國、5語言、988,925筆評分，而且情緒詞本身不是研究者訂好再翻譯，是參與者用母語自己迭代生成（bottom-up elicitation）。這個「先蒐集全球歌曲池、再讓當地參與者自己造詞、最後才統一評分」的三段式流程，才是本文相對於自己前作與Hu等人翻譯法的核心方法論差異。

The background section names two lines of prior work. The first is purely Western mood datasets: MIREX 2007 (240-250 Western songs, 5 mood clusters from AllMusic English tags), CAL500 (500 US pop/rock songs, 18 English terms rated by US undergraduates), DEAM (2,058 excerpts with continuous valence/arousal ratings), MTG-Jamendo (18,486 songs, 56 freely crowdsourced but still mostly English tags), and the 45-descriptor GEMS taxonomy built from European listeners (used to annotate Emotify). The second line already noticed the cross-cultural gap: Hu et al. (2014) compared Korean and American listeners' mood ratings of K-pop, but did so by translating MIREX's mood categories into Korean, still confining Korean annotators to Western-defined categories.

The closest predecessor to GlobalMood is the authors' own earlier study, Lee et al. (2021), comparing American, Brazilian, and Korean listeners rating the same songs, finding terms like "energetic" and "sad" cross-culturally consistent while abstract terms like "love" and "dreamy" diverged sharply. GlobalMood differs from that predecessor in both scale and method: from 3 countries and 9 researcher-predefined fixed categories to 59 countries, 5 languages, and 988,925 ratings, with emotion terms elicited bottom-up in participants' native languages rather than predefined then translated. This three-stage pipeline -- assemble a global song pool, let local participants coin their own terms, then collect ratings uniformly -- is the paper's core methodological departure from both its own predecessor and Hu et al.'s translation approach.

方法Method

方法分三段。第一段是全球代表性歌曲池：從59個國家2017–2023年YouTube週榜前100名中，排除同時登上一國以上排行榜的歌曲（確保曲風具國家獨特性），每國抽20首，共1,180首，每首取15秒片段並正規化至-5dB。第二段是情緒詞的由下而上蒐集：200首歌的子集，由每國參與者以10人一組的鏈式標註（第一人下標籤、後面每人評分並可再加新標籤），兩輪蒐集後得到的獨特詞彙數是英語644、法語528、西班牙語870、韓語629、阿拉伯語283個，取分數最高的前20個詞作為該語言的正式詞表。

第三段是大規模評分：1,741名新參與者對全部1,180首歌評分，每首歌每種語言平均獲得8.38次評分，總計988,925筆。用六個在至少四種語言都有直譯詞的詞（fun、happy、rhythmic、love、sad、calm）算跨國一致度（rbetween）對比國內一致度（rwithin，當作測量誤差基準）：calm一致度最高（rbetween=0.52 vs rwithin=0.49），happy雖是公認的基本情緒，一致度卻明顯偏低（rbetween=0.37 vs rwithin=0.45），顯示直譯詞不保證感受一致。

接著用GlobalMood評測Gemini與CLAP兩種模型。Gemini的zero-shot表現隨版本持續進步：1.5 Flash平均相關r=0.34（南韓最弱，r=0.27），2.0 Flash補上南韓差距達到r=0.42，2.5 Pro達到r=0.50，已經逼近人類評分者之間的一致度上限；但用10個人工評分範例做few-shot反而讓2.5 Pro降到r=0.47。

CLAP的zero-shot表現很差（平均r=0.08），用GlobalMood微調後大幅提升到r=0.31；作者另外做了一個對照組——把非英語詞先用LLM翻成英文再微調，結果只到r=0.13，證明微調效果來自母語使用者自己造出的文化特定詞，而非單純資料量增加。分語言看，阿拉伯語從r=0.11升到0.46，提升幅度最大，法語只從0.07到0.17，提升最小。

The method has three stages. First, a globally representative song pool: from 59 countries' weekly YouTube top-100 charts (2017-2023), excluding any track appearing on more than one country's chart (to keep each country's selection genre-distinctive), 20 songs per country were sampled for 1,180 songs total, each trimmed to a 15-second excerpt and loudness-normalized to -5dB. Second, bottom-up elicitation of emotion terms on a 200-song subset: ten-participant chains per country (the first tags, each subsequent participant rates and can add new tags), yielding unique-term counts of 644 (English), 528 (French), 870 (Spanish), 629 (Korean), and 283 (Arabic) after two rounds, with the top 20 highest-scoring terms per language kept as that language's official vocabulary.

Third, large-scale rating collection: 1,741 new participants rated the full 1,180-song set, yielding an average of 8.38 ratings per song per language and 988,925 ratings total. For six terms with direct translations in at least four languages (fun, happy, rhythmic, love, sad, calm), between-country agreement (rbetween) was compared against within-country agreement (rwithin, serving as a measurement-error baseline): calm showed the highest agreement (rbetween=0.52 vs rwithin=0.49), while happy -- despite being considered a basic universal emotion -- showed a notably larger gap (rbetween=0.37 vs rwithin=0.45), indicating that direct-translation equivalents don't guarantee matching perception.

GlobalMood was then used to evaluate Gemini and CLAP. Gemini's zero-shot performance improved steadily across versions: 1.5 Flash averaged r=0.34 (weakest on Korean, r=0.27), 2.0 Flash closed the Korean gap to reach r=0.42 overall, and 2.5 Pro reached r=0.50, approaching the ceiling set by inter-rater human agreement; but few-shot prompting with 10 human-rated examples actually dropped 2.5 Pro to r=0.47.

Zero-shot CLAP performed poorly (mean r=0.08); fine-tuning on GlobalMood substantially improved it to r=0.31. A control condition -- fine-tuning on the same data but with non-English terms first machine-translated into English -- only reached r=0.13, showing the gains come from native speakers' own culturally-specific terms rather than mere data volume. By language, Arabic showed the largest gain (r=0.11 to 0.46) and French the smallest (r=0.07 to 0.17).

限制Limitations

論文自己在討論裡承認的限制包括：目前只涵蓋5個語言/地區（美、法、墨、韓、埃及），雖然未來計畫擴到20種以上語言，但現階段的「跨文化」代表性仍有限；由下而上蒐集情緒詞的方法雖然能減少研究者偏誤，卻也帶來反向的偏誤——鏈式標註中越早出現的標籤越容易主導後續詞表，而且沒有理論保證所有蒐集到的詞都真的是情緒詞（論文舉了法文「étranger/外國的」這種混入的例子）。此外，音訊只取15秒片段，是否能代表整首歌的情緒表達也未經驗證。論文也坦言，Gemini與CLAP的評測用的都是商用API或既有預訓練模型，無法排除這些模型的訓練資料本身可能已經包含跨文化語料，讓評測結果偏向樂觀。

Limitations the paper itself acknowledges: coverage is currently limited to five languages/regions (US, France, Mexico, Korea, Egypt), and while the authors plan to expand to over 20 languages, current cross-cultural representativeness remains limited. The bottom-up term-elicitation method reduces researcher bias but introduces a different one -- tags that appear early in a chain can dominate the resulting vocabulary, and there's no theoretical guarantee every collected term is actually about emotion (the paper cites French "étranger"/foreign as an example that slipped in). Audio excerpts are also only 15 seconds, and whether that represents a full song's emotional expression is untested. The paper also concedes that evaluating Gemini and CLAP relies on commercial APIs and existing pretrained models, so it can't rule out that those models' training data may already include cross-cultural material, which could bias the evaluation results optimistically.

討論Discussion

這篇論文的貢獻不在於提出新模型，而在於指出一個現有MER基準都繞不開的方法論問題：用直譯詞彙做跨文化評測，測到的可能是翻譯品質而不是情緒理解。CLAP微調實驗最能說明這點——同樣的資料量，「翻譯後微調」只把zero-shot的r=0.08拉到0.13，但「用母語使用者自己造的詞微調」卻拉到0.31，兩者相差超過兩倍，直接反駁了「多語言資料只是換個語言複述英文標籤」的隱含假設。對後續研究的實際啟示是：如果要做跨文化或多語言的音樂（或更廣泛的多模態）理解評測，直譯詞表可能從一開始就低估了模型的真實跨文化落差，也低估了微調帶來的潛在改善空間。

This paper's contribution isn't a new model but identifying a methodological problem that existing MER benchmarks can't sidestep: cross-cultural evaluation using direct-translation vocabulary may be measuring translation quality rather than emotional understanding. The CLAP fine-tuning experiment makes this concrete -- with the same amount of data, fine-tuning on machine-translated terms only lifts zero-shot r=0.08 to 0.13, while fine-tuning on terms coined by native speakers themselves lifts it to 0.31, more than double the gap, directly undercutting the implicit assumption that multilingual data is just English labels restated in another language. The practical implication for future work: cross-cultural or multilingual music (or broader multimodal) understanding benchmarks built on direct-translation vocabularies may understate both the real cross-cultural gap in models and the potential gains available from fine-tuning.

Fx-Encoder++: Extracting Instrument-Wise Audio Effects Representations from Mixtures

Fx-Encoder++：從混音中萃取逐樂器音訊效果表徵

Yen-Tung Yeh, Junghyun Koo, Marco A. Martínez-Ramírez, Wei-Hsiang Liao, Yi-Hsuan Yang, Yuki Mitsufuji (ISMIR 2025)
DOI:10.5281/zenodo.17811567 · ISMIR 2025 · 投稿submitted 2025-09-21
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：既有音訊效果表徵模型只能二選一——要嘛分析整首混音（FX-Encoder、AFx-Rep），要嘛只處理已分軌的單一樂器如吉他（Tone Embedding、OpenAMP），沒有模型能直接從完整混音中拆解出某個樂器單獨被套用的效果。Fx-Encoder++加入一個以CLAP查詢向量為條件的extractor機制解決這個問題，在MUSDB18混音層級效果檢索上R@1達19.4%（優於AFx-Rep的16.7%、FX-Encoder的12.3%），逐樂器檢索在金標準分軌輸入下mAP@10達25.6%；但論文自己的數字也顯示，不做分離直接從混音萃取的準確度（R@10僅11.7–12.2%）仍不如先用Hybrid Demucs分離再編碼的管線（R@10=20.5%），顯示「免分離」目前仍是拿準確度換來的取捨。Why this one: Existing audio-effects representation models force a binary choice -- analyze the whole mixture (FX-Encoder, AFx-Rep) or handle only an already-isolated single instrument like guitar (Tone Embedding, OpenAMP); none could pull out the effects applied to one specific instrument directly from a complete mixture. Fx-Encoder++ adds a CLAP-query-conditioned extractor mechanism to solve this, reaching R@1=19.4% on MUSDB18 mixture-level effects retrieval (vs AFx-Rep's 16.7% and FX-Encoder's 12.3%) and mAP@10=25.6% on instrument-wise retrieval with ground-truth isolated stems as input; but the paper's own numbers also show that extracting directly from a mixture without separation (R@10 only 11.7-12.2%) still trails a pipeline that separates with Hybrid Demucs first then encodes (R@10=20.5%), meaning the "no separation needed" advantage currently comes at a real accuracy cost.

動機Motivation

通用音訊表徵模型（如VGGish、PANN、CLAP）在音樂資訊檢索的許多任務上都很好用，但它們的訓練目標是辨識語意內容（這是什麼樂器、這是什麼歌），對音訊效果（equalizer、reverb、compression等）這種細微的音色調整並不敏感。這個落差在智慧音樂製作（自動混音、混音風格轉換）這類應用裡特別致命，因為這些任務需要模型分別理解「整首混音被加了什麼效果」以及「混音裡的某一個樂器單獨被加了什麼效果」——後者正是既有音訊效果表徵模型完全做不到的能力缺口。

General-purpose audio representations (VGGish, PANN, CLAP) work well across many MIR tasks, but their training objectives target semantic content recognition -- what instrument, what song -- and are insensitive to the subtle timbral shifts introduced by audio effects (equalizers, reverb, compression). This gap is especially damaging for intelligent music production applications (automatic mixing, mixing style transfer), which need models to understand both how effects shape an entire mixture and how effects were applied to one specific instrument within it -- and the latter is a capability gap that no existing audio-effects representation model closes.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把既有音訊效果表徵方法分成兩類，並用表1直接列出對照。第一類是混音層級的對比式方法FX-Encoder，以及分類式方法AFx-Rep（訓練目標是判斷兩段音訊之間套用了哪一種效果、以及該效果屬於哪個預設集），兩者都只能處理整首混音，無法拆解到個別樂器。第二類是Tone Embedding與OpenAMP，兩者都鎖定吉他這單一樂器的音色表徵，但僅限於已經分軌的吉他錄音，無法處理完整混音。作者指出，到目前為止唯一嘗試從個別樂器音軌編碼效果資訊的是FX-Encoder，但它做的仍是「彙總混音層級的結果」，不是「辨識效果如何分別套用在每個樂器上」——這正是Fx-Encoder++要補的缺口。

要從混音中直接抽出逐樂器效果表徵，論文指出兩條直覺路徑都行不通。第一條是先做音源分離再分析各軌效果，但分離演算法本身會產生高頻遺失、瞬態模糊等分離瑕疵，這些瑕疵會混進效果特徵裡，讓「先分離再分析」這條路徑不可靠。第二條是效果參數還原（reverse engineering）常見的做法，需要同時取得處理後（wet）與處理前（dry）的音訊做比對，但真實混音裡幾乎不可能拿到未處理過的原始音軌。Fx-Encoder++的核心貢獻，就是用一個以CLAP文字或音訊查詢為條件的「extractor」機制，直接從完整混音的表徵中萃取出特定樂器的效果表徵，不需要分離、也不需要dry版本。

The paper splits prior audio-effects representation work into two lines, laid out directly in Table 1. The first is mixture-level: FX-Encoder (contrastive) and AFx-Rep (classification-based, trained to identify which effect was applied between two clips and which preset it belongs to), both limited to whole mixtures with no per-instrument breakdown. The second is Tone Embedding and OpenAMP, both targeting guitar-only timbre representations, but restricted to already-isolated guitar recordings rather than full mixtures. The authors note that FX-Encoder is the only prior work encoding effects from individual instrument stems at all, but it still models the aggregate mixture result rather than identifying how effects were applied to each instrument separately -- exactly the gap Fx-Encoder++ targets.

Two intuitive approaches to extracting per-instrument effects directly from a mixture both fail, the paper argues. First, separate-then-analyze: source separation introduces artifacts (missing high frequencies, transient smearing) that distort effect characteristics, making the pipeline unreliable. Second, effect-parameter reverse engineering typically needs both the processed (wet) and unprocessed (dry) versions of a track for comparison, but the unprocessed original is essentially never available in real mixtures. Fx-Encoder++'s core contribution is an "extractor" mechanism, conditioned on a CLAP-derived text or audio query, that pulls an instrument-specific effects representation directly out of a complete mixture's embedding -- without separation and without a dry reference.

方法Method

模型建立在FX-Encoder既有的SimCLR對比學習框架上，沿用其三個關鍵設計：Fx-Normalization（先中和音訊原本就帶有的效果，確保套用相同效果後才是真正可比的正樣本對）、批次內樂器組成一致（避免模型抄捷徑，靠辨認「鼓+貝斯」和「吉他+人聲」這種樂器組合差異，而不是真的學到效果特徵）、以及手工構造的難負樣本（同樣的音樂內容套用不同效果鏈，強迫模型專注在效果本身而非內容）。編碼器主幹沿用PANN架構處理梅爾頻譜。

本文真正新增的部分是「extractor」機制：一個3層MLP，輸入是混音層級的效果嵌入，加上CLAP編碼出的樂器查詢向量（訓練時用音訊查詢、推論時可換成文字查詢，例如"this is the sound of {instrument}"），輸出是該樂器的專屬效果嵌入。訓練目標結合混音層級對比損失與樂器層級對比損失，總損失為兩者加權和（λmix=0.8、λinst=0.2），並採用課程學習：一開始λmix=1.0，訓練過程中才逐步加入樂器層級目標——原因是模型要先建立起可靠的混音層級表徵，樂器層級的梯度訊號才有意義。訓練資料是MoisesDB（240首、11個音軌類型、12種曲風、14小時以上）。

在MUSDB18的混音層級效果檢索任務（500候選中找出效果配置正確的目標）上，Fx-Encoder++全面超越既有方法：整首混音R@1從FX-Encoder的12.3%、AFx-Rep的16.7%，提升到19.4%；鼓聲部分R@1從FX-Encoder的12.1%提升到26.1%，貝斯從7.3%（FX-Encoder）提升到22.1%。在跨資料集的MedleyDB上全面表現下滑（混音R@1僅5.6%），顯示效果是否分辨得出來，很大程度取決於訓練時看過多少該樂器類型的資料。

逐樂器檢索是本文的核心宣稱。用金標準的分軌音訊當輸入時（Oracle情境），Fx-Encoder++的mAP@10達25.6%，優於AFx-Rep的19.3%。但論文自己攤開的數字顯示一個微妙之處：不做分離、直接用extractor從完整混音抽取的做法（R@10僅11.7–12.2%），雖然勝過通用型音源分離管線USS(m)（R@10=9.9%），卻不如用Hybrid Demucs先分離出四個標準音軌再編碼的MSS(m)（R@10=20.5%）——換句話說，「不需要分離模型」這個賣點目前是用檢索準確度換來的，MSS(m)僅限於MUSDB18的四個固定音軌類型，而extractor可以處理任意樂器，這是兩者真正的取捨，而非extractor全面勝出。

The model builds on FX-Encoder's existing SimCLR contrastive framework, reusing three of its key design elements: Fx-Normalization (neutralizing whatever effects a clip already carries, so that applying the same effect afterward produces genuinely comparable positive pairs), consistent instrument composition within each batch (preventing the model from taking the shortcut of distinguishing instrument combinations like "drums+bass" vs "guitar+vocals" instead of actually learning effect features), and hand-crafted hard negatives (identical content processed with different effect chains, forcing focus on effects rather than content). The encoder backbone is PANN, processing mel-spectrograms.

The genuinely new piece is the "extractor" mechanism: a 3-layer MLP that takes the mixture-level effects embedding plus a CLAP-derived instrument query vector (audio query at training time, swappable for a text query like "this is the sound of {instrument}" at inference) and outputs that instrument's effects embedding. The training objective combines mixture-level and instrument-level contrastive losses as a weighted sum (λmix=0.8, λinst=0.2), using curriculum learning that starts at λmix=1.0 and gradually phases in the instrument-level objective -- because the model needs a reliable mixture-level representation before instrument-level gradients carry any useful signal. Training data is MoisesDB (240 tracks, 11 stem types, 12 genres, 14+ hours).

On MUSDB18's mixture-level effects-retrieval task (identifying the correctly effects-matched target among 500 candidates), Fx-Encoder++ beats all prior methods: full-mixture R@1 rises to 19.4% from FX-Encoder's 12.3% and AFx-Rep's 16.7%; drums R@1 rises to 26.1% from FX-Encoder's 12.1%; bass rises to 22.1% from FX-Encoder's 7.3%. Performance drops across the board on the cross-dataset MedleyDB (mixture R@1 only 5.6%), showing that how well effects can be told apart depends heavily on how much of that instrument type appeared during training.

Instrument-wise retrieval is the paper's central claim. With ground-truth isolated stems as input (the Oracle setting), Fx-Encoder++ reaches mAP@10=25.6%, beating AFx-Rep's 19.3%. But the paper's own numbers reveal a subtlety: extracting directly from the full mixture without separation (R@10 only 11.7-12.2%) beats the universal-source-separation pipeline USS(m) (R@10=9.9%), but falls short of MSS(m) -- Hybrid Demucs separating into four standard stems before encoding (R@10=20.5%). In other words, the "no separation model needed" selling point currently trades away some retrieval accuracy; MSS(m) is restricted to MUSDB18's four fixed stem types while the extractor handles arbitrary instruments, which is the real trade-off rather than a clean win for the extractor.

限制Limitations

論文自己在結論列出的限制包括：效果參數配對任務（Ld/matching）表現不如檢索任務穩定，沒有任何一個模型在所有樂器、所有資料集上都最好（例如MUSDB18的鼓聲部分反而是通用模型VGGish的參數還原誤差最低）；而且論文坦承對「單一效果」的理解能力仍然不足，複合效果鏈（多個效果疊加）比單一效果更容易被模型辨識出來，這點在圖2的效果數量實驗中很明顯——效果越多、Fx-Encoder++領先幅度越大，反過來說單一效果的辨識力是相對弱項。另外，如前述，直接從混音抽取（不分離）的檢索準確度仍低於「先做高品質分離再編碼」的管線，顯示extractor機制本身還沒能完全補上分離造成的資訊落差，只是避開了對外部分離模型的依賴。

Limitations the paper lists in its conclusion: performance on the effect-parameter-matching task (Ld) is less consistently strong than on retrieval, with no single model winning across all instruments and datasets (e.g. general-purpose VGGish actually gets the lowest parameter-matching error on MUSDB18 drums). The paper also admits weaker understanding of single effects specifically -- complex effect chains are easier to identify than a single effect, visible in Figure 2's effect-count experiment where Fx-Encoder++'s lead widens as more effects are stacked, implying single-effect recognition is comparatively weak. And as noted above, direct mixture-level extraction without separation still trails a pipeline that separates first with a high-quality model then encodes, showing the extractor mechanism hasn't fully closed the information gap that separation provides -- it has only avoided depending on an external separation model.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最值得留意的地方，不是extractor機制本身的架構（3層MLP並不複雜），而是它把「效果表徵」這個原本只能在混音或單一樂器兩個極端之間二選一的問題，重新定義成一個可以用查詢向量控制粒度的檢索介面——這個設計選擇比模型容量或訓練資料規模更關鍵，因為它讓同一套編碼器同時服務兩種不同粒度的下游應用（整體母帶處理 vs. 逐軌混音校正），不需要為每個粒度各訓練一個模型。對後續研究更實際的啟示在於：MSS(m)用完整分離模型仍勝過extractor直接萃取，顯示「用查詢向量從混合表徵中萃取子成分」這個想法本身是可行的，但要真正取代分離管線，extractor機制在資訊保真度上還有明顯的進步空間，這是後續工作最值得優先補強的方向。

What's most notable here isn't the extractor mechanism's architecture itself (a 3-layer MLP is not complex), but that it reframes effects representation -- previously a binary choice between mixture-level or single-isolated-instrument -- as a retrieval interface whose granularity is controlled by a query vector. That design choice matters more than model capacity or training data scale, since it lets one encoder serve two different downstream granularities (whole-mix mastering vs. per-track mix correction) without training a separate model for each. The more practical takeaway for follow-up work: MSS(m) with a full separation model still beats direct extractor extraction, showing that "pull a sub-component out of a mixed representation via a query vector" is workable in principle, but the extractor still has a clear fidelity gap to close before it can actually replace a separation pipeline -- the most promising direction for future work to prioritize.

Towards Robust Music Transcription by Measuring Cross-Version Consistency in Western Classical Music

邁向穩健的音樂轉譜：用跨版本一致性衡量西方古典音樂轉譜模型

Yannik Venohr, Yiwei Ding, Christof Weiß (ISMIR 2025)
DOI:10.5281/zenodo.17811552 · ISMIR 2025 · 投稿submitted 2025-09-21
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：自動音樂轉譜在超出鋼琴獨奏的多樂器場景一直難以量測進步，因為標註資料稀缺，而過往用多版本資料訓練的嘗試（如Krause等人2023的對比學習）發現模型學到的其實是樂器音色而非音高。本文提出跨版本一致性（CVC）——用DTW對齊同一作品的不同錄音版本、比較模型預測的餘弦相似度，完全不需要標註；實驗顯示CVC在固定訓練域內與AP高度相關（SWD ρ=0.97、BSQD ρ=0.93），而在CVC與AP看似脫鉤的少數案例中（BPSD域內僅ρ=0.38），CVC反而準確預警了模型在域外資料（RWC+TRIOS）上的過擬合（相關性回升到ρ=0.70）。Why this one: Progress in automatic music transcription beyond solo piano has long been hard to measure because annotated data is scarce, and prior attempts to train on multi-version data (e.g. Krause et al. 2023's contrastive learning) found models learned instrument timbre rather than pitch. This paper proposes Cross-Version Consistency (CVC) -- aligning different recordings of the same work via DTW and comparing the model's predictions by cosine similarity, requiring no annotation at all; experiments show CVC correlates strongly with AP within a fixed training domain (ρ=0.97 for SWD, 0.93 for BSQD), and in the rare cases where CVC seems decoupled from AP (ρ only 0.38 within BPSD), CVC actually gave early warning of the model's overfitting, confirmed by out-of-domain AP on RWC+TRIOS recovering a ρ=0.70 correlation.

動機Motivation

自動音樂轉譜（AMT）在鋼琴獨奏這種單一樂器場景已經有不錯的成果，但一旦超出鋼琴、進入管弦樂或多樂器的場景，標註品質好、規模夠大的資料集非常稀缺，這讓「模型到底進步了沒有」變得很難量測——論文形容這是AMT研究撞上了一塊「玻璃天花板」。更根本的問題是，深度學習模型很容易過擬合到特定資料集的偏誤（哪種錄音室、哪個年代的錄音、哪種樂器組合），在同一個資料集內表現很好，換一個沒看過的資料集就大幅下滑，但因為標註稀缺，這種過擬合往往要等到真的拿到域外標註資料才能被抓到。

Automatic music transcription (AMT) works reasonably well in solo-piano settings, but once it moves beyond piano into orchestral or multi-instrument scenarios, large, well-annotated datasets become scarce, making it hard to even measure whether models are actually improving -- the paper describes AMT research as having hit a "glass ceiling." The deeper problem is that deep learning models readily overfit to a specific dataset's biases (which studio, which era of recording, which instrument combination), performing well within that dataset but dropping sharply on an unseen one -- and because annotations are scarce, this overfitting typically isn't caught until out-of-domain annotated data happens to become available.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名了三條具體的既有做法。第一條是Weiß等人自己先前的研究（2020、2022），已經用多版本資料集來做評測——研究跨版本的泛化能力、比較不同資料切分策略的影響，發現不同架構之間的差異常常比同一架構換一次訓練種子的差異還小，換資料集評測時這些架構差異幾乎完全消失，說明深度學習模型對資料集偏誤的過擬合是長期存在的老問題。

第二條是Krause等人（2023）嘗試把多版本資料用在訓練而非評測上：一種做法是對比學習，把同一版本裡時間相近的音框當正樣本、時間遠的當負樣本，但作者發現這樣學到的表徵其實在區分樂器音色而非音高與和聲；另一種做法直接縮小不同版本在時頻表徵上的距離，MPE結果較有希望。第三條、也是跟本文關係最近的前身研究，是Liu與Weiß（2024）在ICLR Tiny Papers提出的師生式領域調適法：他們也用了「跨版本一致性（CVC）」這個詞，但做法是把CVC當成一個二元過濾器——比較教師模型在不同版本上的標註，只留下兩邊都一致的標籤給學生模型訓練；本文最大的方法論差異，是把CVC從一個訓練用的二元過濾規則，重新定義成一個連續的機率相似度度量，並且系統性地探討它跟標準評測指標的關係，單獨拿來當評測工具使用，而不是訓練流程的一部分。

The paper names three concrete prior lines. First, Weiß et al.'s own earlier work (2020, 2022) already used multi-version datasets for evaluation -- studying cross-version generalization and comparing the effect of different data-splitting strategies, finding that differences between architectures are often smaller than differences from just changing the training seed, and that these architecture differences nearly vanish under cross-dataset evaluation -- evidence that deep models overfitting to dataset biases is a long-standing problem.

Second, Krause et al. (2023) used multi-version data for training rather than evaluation: one approach treated temporally close frames within a version as contrastive positive pairs and distant ones as negatives, but the authors found the resulting representations captured instrument timbre rather than pitch and harmony; another approach directly minimized the distance between different versions' time-frequency representations, with more promising MPE results. Third, and the closest predecessor here, is Liu and Weiß (2024, ICLR Tiny Papers), whose teacher-student domain adaptation method also uses "cross-version consistency (CVC)" but as a binary filter -- comparing a teacher's annotations across versions and keeping only agreeing labels for student training. This paper's departure: redefining CVC as a continuous probability-based similarity measure and studying it as a standalone evaluation tool, not a training-pipeline filter.

方法Method

CVC的核心想法很直接：給模型同一首作品的兩個不同錄音版本，先用動態時間規整（DTW，以chroma與onset特徵在SyncToolbox的MrMsDTW實作下計算）把兩個版本在時間上對齊，再用餘弦相似度比較兩個版本在每一個對齊音框上的音高機率預測，對所有對齊音框取平均，就是這一對版本的CVC分數。這個量測完全不需要任何人工標註——它問的問題不是「模型對不對」，而是「模型在換一個演奏者、換一個錄音環境時，預測是否維持一致」。論文也坦白承認CVC有一個已知陷阱：一個對每個音框都做出恆定但錯誤預測的模型（例如每一格都固定猜同一組音高），也會得到很高的CVC分數，所以CVC只能當作輔助指標，不能單獨拿來判斷模型好壞。

實驗用三個結構化的多版本資料集：舒伯特《冬之旅》資料集（SWD，24首、每首9個版本，鋼琴+人聲）、貝多芬鋼琴奏鳴曲資料集（BPSD，32首第一樂章、每首11個版本，鋼琴）、貝多芬弦樂四重奏資料集（BSQD，70首、每首9個版本，弦樂，即將發表）。域外測試用RWC古典分集（35首、混合編制）與TRIOS（5首、混合編制），兩者都嚴格排除了跟三個訓練資料集樂器編制重疊的曲目。比較的模型架構從1.3萬參數的Basic Pitch，到40萬參數的Deep Salience，再到39萬到456萬參數的三種尺寸ResNet（S/M/L），涵蓋一個數量級以上的模型容量差異。

第一組實驗問：CVC能不能當作同一個資料域裡模型好壞的替代指標？固定ResNet-M架構、只調整訓練資料量，CVC與平均精度（AP）的相關係數在三個資料集上都很高（SWD ρ=0.97、BSQD ρ=0.93、BPSD ρ=0.77）。但換成調整訓練資料的「來源域」（在SWD、BPSD、BSQD各種組合上訓練再各自測試）時，BPSD這個測試域上的相關性大幅減弱（ρ僅0.38）——具體來看，只用BSQD訓練的模型在BPSD上AP偏低，卻仍然維持著跟其他模型相當的CVC分數；檢查預測內容後發現，這些模型系統性地漏掉高音域的音高，亦即它們是「同樣錯得很一致」，恰好對應到CVC設計本身就承認的那個陷阱。

第二組實驗把這個看似矛盾的案例拿去對照域外資料：把「BPSD上的CVC」拿去跟「RWC+TRIOS這兩個域外資料集上的AP」對照，相關性從域內的0.38一口氣回升到0.70——換句話說，那些在BPSD上CVC偏低的模型，雖然在BPSD域內的AP沒有明顯偏低，但換到真正沒看過的資料上，AP確實比較差，證明CVC在域內看似「失準」的那些案例，其實是提前預警了模型對BPSD資料集偏誤的過擬合。比較五種架構時也觀察到類似效果：Deep Salience與ResNet-M在訓練域內的AP幾乎相同，但CVC不同，而換到域外測試，ResNet-M（帶殘差連接）表現確實略勝一籌，呼應了此前Weiß等人（2020）「殘差連接有助於泛化」的發現。

CVC's core idea is direct: given two different recordings of the same work, first align them in time via Dynamic Time Warping (DTW, computed on chroma and onset features using SyncToolbox's MrMsDTW implementation), then compare the model's pitch-probability predictions at each aligned frame pair using cosine similarity, and average across all aligned frames to get that version-pair's CVC score. This measure needs no annotation at all -- it doesn't ask "is the model correct" but "does the model's prediction stay consistent when the performer or recording changes." The paper also candidly admits a known trap: a model that makes a constant but wrong prediction at every frame (e.g. always guessing the same fixed set of pitches) also gets a high CVC score, so CVC can only serve as a supplementary metric, never a standalone judge of quality.

Experiments use three structured multi-version datasets: the Schubert Winterreise Dataset (SWD, 24 works x 9 versions, piano+voice), the Beethoven Piano Sonata Dataset (BPSD, 32 first movements x 11 versions, piano), and the Beethoven String Quartet Dataset (BSQD, 70 works x 9 versions, strings, forthcoming). Out-of-domain test sets are the classical subset of RWC (35 works, mixed instrumentation) and TRIOS (5 works, mixed instrumentation), both strictly excluding pieces whose instrumentation overlaps the three training datasets. Compared architectures range from Basic Pitch's 13K parameters to Deep Salience's 406K, up to three ResNet sizes (S/M/L) spanning 393K to 4.56M parameters -- over an order of magnitude of model-capacity difference.

The first experiment set asks: can CVC substitute for a quality metric within one domain? Fixing the ResNet-M architecture and varying only training-data amount, CVC correlates strongly with Average Precision (AP) on all three datasets (ρ=0.97 for SWD, 0.93 for BSQD, 0.77 for BPSD). But varying the training data's source domain instead (training on combinations of SWD/BPSD/BSQD, testing on each) sharply weakens the correlation on the BPSD test set (ρ only 0.38): models trained only on BSQD show low AP on BPSD yet retain CVC scores comparable to other models -- inspecting the predictions reveals these models systematically miss high-register pitches, i.e. they're "consistently wrong the same way," exactly the trap the CVC design itself already flags.

The second experiment set takes this apparent contradiction and checks it against out-of-domain data: comparing "CVC on BPSD" against "AP on the out-of-domain RWC+TRIOS," the correlation jumps from the weak within-domain 0.38 back up to 0.70 -- meaning the models with lower CVC on BPSD, while not showing obviously lower AP within BPSD itself, really do perform worse once tested on genuinely unseen data, showing that CVC's apparent "failure" within-domain was actually an early warning of overfitting to BPSD's dataset biases. A similar effect appears comparing five architectures: Deep Salience and ResNet-M have nearly identical within-domain AP but different CVC, and on out-of-domain testing ResNet-M (with residual connections) does perform slightly better, echoing Weiß et al.'s (2020) earlier finding that residual connections aid generalization.

限制Limitations

論文自己承認的限制包括：CVC存在「一致地錯」這個已知陷阱（前述BSQD訓練模型的例子），必須搭配標準指標一起看，不能單獨當作模型品質的唯一依據；三個多版本資料集全部侷限在西方古典音樂（藝術歌曲、鋼琴奏鳴曲、弦樂四重奏），論文完全沒有測試流行樂、爵士或非西方音樂，而這些曲風往往沒有DTW可以依賴的「同一份樂譜、不同演奏」關係，CVC的適用性在這類曲風上完全未知。此外，本文的實驗只涵蓋MPE（逐音框多音高估計）這個轉譜子任務，論文自己也在結論承認尚未把CVC延伸到音符層級或利用Transformer的stream-level轉譜任務上做驗證。

Limitations the paper itself acknowledges: CVC has the known "consistently wrong" trap (the BSQD-trained model example above), so it must be read alongside standard metrics rather than serving as a sole quality judge; all three multi-version datasets are confined to Western classical music (art song, piano sonatas, string quartets), with no testing at all on pop, jazz, or non-Western music, genres that often lack the "same score, different performance" relationship DTW depends on -- CVC's applicability there is entirely untested. The experiments also cover only the MPE (frame-level multi-pitch estimation) transcription subtask; the paper's own conclusion admits CVC hasn't yet been validated on note-level or Transformer-based stream-level transcription tasks.

討論Discussion

這篇論文的價值不在於提出更強的轉譜模型，而在於指出一個評測方法論上的空缺：當標註資料稀缺時，「模型在同一份樂譜的不同演奏版本上預測是否一致」本身就能提供訊號，而且這個訊號跟標準指標不是完全重疊的兩件事——CVC看似跟AP脫鉤的那些案例，反而是抓出過擬合的機會窗口。這對管弦樂、合唱這類annotated資料長期稀缺的領域特別有實際意義，因為多版本錄音（同一首交響曲被錄過幾十次）遠比逐音符標註容易取得。論文結尾也提出一個尚待驗證但值得關注的延伸方向：既然CVC能當評測訊號，是否也能直接當成對比學習的距離度量用於訓練（而不只是像Liu與Weiß那樣拿來過濾標籤），這條路目前只有初步論證，還沒有實驗支持。

This paper's value isn't a stronger transcription model but pointing to a gap in evaluation methodology: when annotated data is scarce, whether a model's predictions stay consistent across different performances of the same score is itself informative, and that signal doesn't fully overlap with standard metrics -- the cases where CVC seems decoupled from AP turn out to be exactly where overfitting gets caught. This has real practical relevance for domains like orchestral or choral music where annotated data has long been scarce, since multi-version recordings (a symphony recorded dozens of times) are far easier to obtain than note-level annotation. The paper closes with an unvalidated but notable extension: since CVC works as an evaluation signal, could it also serve directly as a distance measure for contrastive training (rather than only filtering labels, as in Liu and Weiß) -- currently only argued for, with no supporting experiments yet.
