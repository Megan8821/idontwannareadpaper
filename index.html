Investigating the Performance and Energy Costs of Replicating Band-Split RNN for Music Source Separation

重現 Band-Split RNN 音樂音源分離模型的效能與能耗成本

Paul Magron, Romain Serizel, Constance Douwes
arXiv:2609.21918 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-18
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：作者重現出來的最佳版本 BSRNN 平均 cSDR 達 7.87 dB(直接照抄重現只有 7.42 dB),仍比原論文的 8.24 dB 少 0.37–0.82 dB;但真正醒目的是代價——整個計畫燒掉 24.2 MWh 電力,是訓練出最佳模型所需 230–354 kWh 的約 53 倍,相當於 15 個歐洲人一年的用電量,替「重現一篇沒放出完整訓練管線的論文」標出了具體價碼。Why this one: The authors' best BSRNN reimplementation reaches 7.87 dB average cSDR (7.42 dB for the direct replication), still 0.37-0.82 dB behind the original paper's 8.24 dB, but the real headline is cost: the full project burned 24.2 MWh, about 53x the 230-354 kWh needed to train just the best model and roughly the yearly electricity use of 15 Europeans -- a concrete price tag on reproducing a paper that never released its full training pipeline.

動機Motivation

音樂音源分離的架構愈來愈常被當成組件重複使用,但像 Band-Split RNN(BSRNN)這樣強大的系統,官方釋出的只有模型架構本身,沒有把重新訓練所需的資料前處理、訓練流程與評估程式碼一併放出來。作者指出同樣的問題也出現在 BSRNN 的競爭對手身上:Hybrid Demucs 是一堆模型組成的「模型包」,重新訓練很繁瑣;HT Demucs 需要在私有資料集上微調,使得重現「根本不可能」;BS-RoFormer 則需要龐大算力,從頭訓練曠日費時到不切實際。

正因為 BSRNN 影響力夠大——後續已被改造用於電影對白分離與語音增強——作者決定從頭完整重現整條訓練管線,系統性地測試原論文語焉不詳的設計選擇(損失函式組成、提前停止的判準、資料增強方式),而且不同於一般 MSS 論文,他們把每一次訓練所耗的電力與碳排也記錄下來並公開報告,把「可重現性」本身當成一項可以量化代價的東西,而不只是口惠。

Music source separation architectures increasingly get reused as building blocks, but a strong one like Band-Split RNN (BSRNN) ships without a full reproducible pipeline -- the original authors released the model architecture but not the data preprocessing, training protocol, or evaluation code needed to retrain it from scratch. The authors document the same problem across BSRNN's rivals: Hybrid Demucs is a "bag of models" that is tedious to retrain, HT Demucs needs fine-tuning on a private dataset that makes replication "plain impossible," and BS-RoFormer demands so much compute that retraining is prohibitively long.

Motivated by BSRNN's influence -- it has since been adapted for cinematic dialogue separation and speech enhancement -- the authors reimplement the full pipeline from scratch, systematically vary design choices the original paper leaves underspecified (loss formulation, early-stopping criterion, data augmentation), and, unusually for an MSS paper, measure and report the electricity and carbon cost of every training run. The goal is to treat reproducibility itself as something with a quantifiable price, not just a courtesy.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者在他們的表一裡把 BSRNN 放進一整排對照組:原論文的 BSRNN 平均 cSDR 是 8.24 dB,排在中段——KUIELab-MDX-Net(7.47 dB)與 Hybrid Demucs(7.64 dB)比它差,TFC-TDF UNet v3(8.34 dB)與 SCNet(9.00 dB)比它好,BS-RoFormer(9.80 dB)與 SCNet-large(9.69 dB)則大幅領先。BSRNN 並不是排行榜上最強的模型——作者選它的理由是它在「效能夠好、算力需求適中」這個甜蜜點上,而這正是為什麼它缺乏可重現管線這件事,比起「差一點點打不過 SOTA」更值得在意。

這篇論文真正新的地方不是分離方法本身,而是一套查帳式的做法:作者把 MIR 領域外「Green AI」路線的能耗方法論——像是 codecarbon 套件與 Green Algorithms 計算器,原本是拿來稽核大型 NLP 與視覺模型訓練碳排的工具——搬進來,用在 MSS 重現這件事上,並主張這是首次這樣做。過去那些非官方的 BSRNN 重現版本,不是效能明顯低於原論文,就是悄悄把管線改去配合別的資料集;這篇論文反而選擇盡可能貼齊原始設定,把原論文語焉不詳之處逐一記錄下來,並把自己的數字跟已發表數字之間的落差,直接當成研究對象攤開來看,而不是想辦法藏起來。

The related-work comparison (their Table 1) places BSRNN, at 8.24 dB average cSDR in its original paper, in the middle of a competitive field: KUIELab-MDX-Net (7.47 dB) and Hybrid Demucs (7.64 dB) trail it, TFC-TDF UNet v3 (8.34 dB) and SCNet (9.00 dB) beat it, and BS-RoFormer (9.80 dB) and SCNet-large (9.69 dB) beat it by a wide margin. BSRNN is not the strongest model on the leaderboard -- the authors pick it because it sits in a sweet spot of good performance for moderate compute, which is exactly why its lack of a reproducible pipeline matters more than a marginal SOTA gain would.

What is actually new here is not a separation method but an accounting exercise: the paper borrows its energy methodology from the Green AI line of work outside MIR -- tools like the codecarbon library and the Green Algorithms calculator, previously used to audit the carbon cost of training large NLP and vision models -- and applies it, for what the authors present as the first time, to MSS reproduction specifically. Where unofficial BSRNN reimplementations before this one either under-perform the original or quietly adapt the pipeline to a different dataset, this paper instead commits to matching the original setup as closely as possible, documents every point where the original paper is ambiguous, and turns the gap between its own numbers and the published ones into the object of study rather than something to hide.

方法Method

BSRNN 分三個階段。band-split 模組先把 STFT 依非等寬的頻帶切開(呼應人耳音高感知本來就在不同頻率上解析度不同),把每個頻帶投影進維度 N 的潛在空間;接著兩組堆疊的殘差 RNN 區塊,輪流沿著時間軸與頻帶軸建模(group norm、雙向 LSTM、全連接層,重複 R 次),最後每個頻帶各自接一個隱藏維度 4N 的 MLP,產生複數遮罩,跟輸入頻譜逐元素相乘。作者訓練兩種規模:小模型(N=64、R=8、3230 萬參數)用 4 張 RTX 2080 Ti 做快速迭代,大模型(N=128、R=12、1.467 億參數)用 2 張 Tesla L40S;損失函式是時域 L1 加上 STFT 實部與虛部各自的 L1,用 Adam、初始學習率 1e-3、每兩個 epoch 衰減 0.98。

原論文用的是 8 張 GPU、batch size 16 的設定,作者手上沒有那樣的硬體,只能用 batch size 4 到 8,並按比例放大學習率、讓有效步長(學習率/批次大小)維持不變——他們拿這個替代方案跟梯度累積比較過,發現效果相當。接著逐一(不是一起)掃過七項設計選擇:損失函式的組成(只用時域、只用 STFT、或兩者合併)、提前停止的判準(驗證損失 vs. 驗證 uSDR)、STFT 窗長(2048 vs. 4096 個取樣點)、遮罩估計器的寬度 μ(4、2、1 三種)、靜音丟棄要套用在全部音源還是只套用在目標音源,以及要不要保留原論文的 Source Activity Detector 前處理,或改用類似 Open-Unmix 的增強方式(聲道互換、增益縮放)。

BSRNN has three stages. A band-split module divides the STFT into subbands of unequal bandwidth (following how human pitch perception is non-uniform across frequency) and projects each into a latent space of size N. Two stacked residual RNN blocks then alternate between modeling the sequence across time and across bands (group norm, bidirectional LSTM, dense layer, repeated R times), and a per-band MLP with hidden size 4N produces a complex mask that is multiplied elementwise with the input spectrogram. The authors train two sizes: a small model (N=64, R=8, 32.3M parameters) for fast iteration on 4x RTX 2080 Ti GPUs, and a large model (N=128, R=12, 146.7M parameters) on 2x Tesla L40S GPUs, optimized with a combined loss of time-domain L1 plus real- and imaginary-STFT L1 terms, Adam at learning rate 1e-3 with 0.98 decay every two epochs.

Because the original paper used a batch size of 16 across 8 GPUs -- hardware the authors do not have -- they instead train with batch size 4-8 and scale the learning rate proportionally to keep the effective step size (lr/batch) constant, a substitution they validate against gradient accumulation and find equivalent. They then sweep seven design choices one at a time: loss decomposition (time-only vs. STFT-only vs. combined), the early-stopping criterion (validation loss vs. validation uSDR), STFT window size (2048 vs. 4096 samples), the mask-estimator width mu (4 vs. 2 vs. 1), whether silence-dropping is applied to all sources or only the target, and whether to keep the original Source Activity Detector preprocessing or replace it with Open-Unmix-style augmentation (channel swap, gain rescaling) instead.

限制Limitations

重現出來的模型並沒有完全補上跟原論文的差距:作者直接照著重現的版本,在測試集上平均 cSDR 只有 7.42 dB,比原論文的 8.24 dB 少了 0.82 dB;就算是表現最好的變體——拿掉原論文的 Source Activity Detector 前處理、改用類似 Open-Unmix 的增強方式——也只追回到 7.87 dB,大概補上一半的落差。作者認為部分原因是真的硬體不夠(湊不出原論文 8 張 GPU、batch size 16 的規模),部分則是原論文本身寫得不清楚——它從沒說清楚提前停止到底該看驗證損失還是驗證 uSDR;作者預設用 uSDR,但沒辦法確認這跟原作者當初的做法是否一致。

能耗的記帳本身也有一個誠實的缺口:作者承認他們報出來的 24.2 MWh 總計畫用電量裡,有一部分不是正經的實驗,而是「自己實作上的失誤,導致訓練跑到一半中斷或重複跑」,而且他們用的計算平台沒辦法把這部分浪費跟正當的探索性實驗乾淨地分開。用三個不同隨機種子跑同一個設定,每個音源的結果會有大約 0.3 dB 的變異,所以全文各處的單次跑法比較(包括 7.42 dB 對 8.24 dB 這個標題級的落差)都疊加了種子雜訊,不只是架構與流程上的差異。作者也提醒,他們的 SAD 前處理有可能單純做得比原版弱,因為原論文從沒有單獨拿掉 SAD 來看它自己的貢獻有多大。

The replicated model does not fully close the gap to the original: the authors' direct reimplementation reaches 7.42 dB average cSDR on the test set versus the original paper's 8.24 dB, a 0.82 dB shortfall, and even their best variant -- dropping the original Source Activity Detector preprocessing and switching to Open-Unmix-style augmentation -- only recovers to 7.87 dB, closing roughly half the gap. The authors attribute part of this to genuine hardware limits (they cannot match the original's batch size of 16 across 8 GPUs) and part to an ambiguity in the original paper itself, which never states whether early stopping should track validation loss or validation uSDR; they default to uSDR and cannot verify this matches what the original authors did.

The energy accounting has its own honesty gap: the authors admit that part of their reported 24.2 MWh total project cost is not principled experimentation but their "own implementation errors, which resulted in, e.g., interrupted or redundant training runs," and that their computing platform does not let them separate that waste from legitimate exploration cleanly. Across three random seeds, results vary by about 0.3 dB per source, so single-run comparisons throughout the paper (including the 7.42 vs. 8.24 dB headline gap) carry uncontrolled seed noise on top of the architectural and protocol differences. They also flag that their SAD preprocessing may simply be a weaker implementation than the original's, since the original paper never isolates SAD's contribution on its own.

討論Discussion

這篇論文把自己定位成對「可重現研究」的貢獻,而不是提出新的分離方法,所以主要的實證主張都在講:哪些設計選擇真的有差。把時域損失跟 STFT 損失合併使用,效果跟只用其中一種差不多(都在 7.1–7.2 dB 之間),這推翻了先前「時域損失項是必要的」這種假設;把 STFT 窗長從 2048 放大到 4096 會讓效果掉大約 0.5 dB,因為鼓聲需要比較小的窗長才保得住的時間解析度;而遮罩估計器的寬度 μ 會決定哪個樂器受惠——μ=2 讓貝斯變好,代價是鼓與其他樂器變差。作者把每種設定的 SDR 對訓練耗電量(kWh)畫成圖,找出柏拉圖前緣,並指出好幾種設定(包括原論文那套 SAD 前處理)其實是被嚴格支配的——存在別的設定,SDR 更高、耗電更少,兩頭都贏。

在永續性這件事上,最醒目的數字是:整個計畫——包含跑失敗的、拿來做原型的、以及系統性掃過的消融實驗——總共耗掉 24.2 MWh,大約是訓練出最佳模型所需 230–354 kWh 的 53 倍;作者指出這個比例,已經比他們引用的另一篇研究(總計畫能耗大約是最佳模型訓練成本的 2000 倍)好上不少。他們把這個結果解讀成「程式碼公開」的直接回報:既然這次把 BSRNN 完整管線都放出來了,之後想重現的人(包括接著這篇論文做下去的人)就不必從頭重新摸索同一批設計選擇,整個領域累積下來的能耗也會跟著降低。

Framed as a contribution to reproducible research rather than a new separation method, the paper's main empirical claims are about which design choices actually matter. A combined time-domain-plus-STFT loss performs about the same as either component alone (7.1-7.2 dB), contradicting prior assumptions that time-domain loss terms are essential; a larger STFT window (4096 vs. 2048 samples) costs about 0.5 dB, because drums need the finer time resolution the smaller window preserves; and the mask-estimator width mu trades off which instrument benefits, with mu=2 helping bass at the expense of drums and other. Plotting every configuration's SDR against its training energy in kWh, they identify a Pareto frontier and show that several configurations, including the original SAD preprocessing, are strictly dominated -- there exist other points with both better SDR and lower energy.

On sustainability, the headline number is that the entire project -- including failed runs, prototyping, and the systematic ablation sweep -- consumed 24.2 MWh, about 53 times the 230-354 kWh needed to train just the best model, which the authors note is a far better ratio than a prior study they cite where total project energy ran to roughly 2000 times the best model's training cost. They frame this as the direct payoff of code availability: with the original BSRNN pipeline released in full, later replication attempts (including future ones building on this work) would not need to re-derive the same design choices from scratch, and the field's aggregate energy footprint would shrink accordingly.

ArtifactBench: Lineage-Aware Evaluation of AI-Generated Music Detectors under Distribution Shift

ArtifactBench:在分布偏移下對 AI 生成音樂偵測器做系譜感知評測

Heewon Oh
arXiv:2609.23550 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-20
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：在 ArtifactBench 的 562 首「共同成功」測試交集上,表現最好的 AI 音樂偵測器(ArtifactNet v9.4)AUROC 達 0.982,但兩個常被引用的基準系統 SpecTTTra 與 CLAM,一旦資料集刻意偏離它們各自的訓練分布,AUROC 就掉到 0.299 與 0.284——幾乎跟亂猜沒兩樣;Deezer 自己的 ISMIR 2025 偵測器介於中間,AUROC 0.761,它在真音樂上的假陽性率從 FMA 資料庫的 0.020 一路飆到網路蒐集的困難負樣本的 0.211,說明單一總分會把「哪個偵測器真的能類推」這件事藏起來。Why this one: On ArtifactBench's 562-track common-success test intersection, the strongest AI-music detector (ArtifactNet v9.4) reaches 0.982 AUROC while two widely-cited baselines, SpecTTTra and CLAM, fall to 0.299 and 0.284 -- barely above chance -- once the cohort is deliberately shifted away from each detector's original training distribution; Deezer's own ISMIR-2025 detector sits in between at 0.761 AUROC, and its false-positive rate on real music swings from 0.020 on the FMA archive to 0.211 on web-sourced hard negatives, showing that a single aggregate score hides which detector actually generalizes.

動機Motivation

AI 生成音樂偵測器通常只用一個總體正確率數字來評比,但這個數字背後藏著論文很少講清楚的選擇:測試集裡包含哪些生成器版本、這些曲目有沒有已經滲進某個偵測器自己的訓練資料、每個偵測器是不是真的把每個檔案都打過分數,以及一旦測試集納入偵測器不熟悉的音訊,它在真實音樂上的假陽性率會飄移多少。作者把這件事定調成一個「評測治理」問題,而不是偵測問題:「一個基準測試的名字可能對應到好幾份清單,清單裡引用的音訊可能只剩一部分還找得到,而一個模型公開的訓練切分,也可能跟名義上的測試集重疊」,於是不同的偵測器實作可能悄悄跳過不同的檔案,產出的分數其實從一開始就無法互相比較。

AI-generated music detectors are typically benchmarked against a single aggregate accuracy number, but that number depends on choices the paper rarely makes explicit: which generator versions are in the test set, whether any of those tracks leaked into a detector's own training data, whether every detector actually scored every file, and how much a detector's real-music false-positive rate shifts once the test set includes audio outside its comfort zone. The author frames this as an evaluation-governance problem, not a detection problem: "a benchmark name may refer to several manifests, only a subset of referenced audio may still be available, and a model's public training split may overlap the nominal test set," so different detector implementations can silently skip different files and produce scores that were never actually comparable.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

這個領域主要有四個偵測器家族撐著:伴隨大型 SONICS 語料庫一起推出的 SpecTTTra 頻譜圖 Transformer 模型;Deezer 那個訓練在人工重建音訊上、後來被連結到傅立葉域證據的邏輯回歸偵測器;把音樂導向與語音導向表徵放在對比式訓練底下結合起來的 CLAM;以及不直接吃原始音訊、而是在估計出的編碼殘差與諧波/打擊結構上運作的 ArtifactNet。先前的基準測試各自只補了評測問題的一角,沒有把整體補齊:Echoes 控制真實參考音訊與生成音訊之間的語意對齊,阻止偵測器抄捷徑;HAIM 把標籤空間從單純的真/假二分,擴展成人類與 AI 協作的不同階段;BAMM 則拿真實電視廣播音訊去壓力測試偵測器,揭露乾淨錄音室測試集看不出來的領域落差。

ArtifactBench 真正不一樣的地方,是把「量測這件事本身」——而不只是音訊內容——當成受測對象:它同時追蹤三種狀態,而不是全部塞進單一個「沒看過」標籤——內容完全重複、屬於某個偵測器自己公開訓練切分的一部分,以及光靠公開證據沒辦法判定曝光與否的情況。作者拿這套流程去對 SpecTTTra 公開的 SONICS 系譜、以及另一個對照系統的 MoM 系譜做外洩稽核,分別發現 241 筆與 200 筆資料其實落在那些系統自己的訓練或驗證切分裡——這種重疊,單純比較正確率是完全看不出來的。

Four detector families anchor the field: the SpecTTTra spectrogram-transformer models introduced alongside the large SONICS corpus; a logistic detector from Deezer trained on artificial reconstructions and later connected to Fourier-domain evidence; CLAM, which combines music- and speech-oriented representations under contrastive training; and ArtifactNet, which operates on estimated codec residuals and harmonic/percussive structure rather than raw audio. Prior benchmarks each patch one piece of the evaluation problem without covering the rest: Echoes controls semantic alignment between real references and generated tracks to block shortcut learning, HAIM expands the label space beyond a binary real/fake split into stages of human-AI collaboration, and BAMM stress-tests detectors on real broadcast-television audio to expose a domain gap invisible in clean studio test sets.

ArtifactBench's distinguishing move is to treat the measurement layer itself -- not just the audio -- as the object under test: it tracks exact content overlap, membership in a detector's own published training partitions, and cases where exposure cannot be resolved from public evidence, reporting these as three separate states rather than collapsing everything into a single 'unseen' label. Running a leakage audit against SpecTTTra's public SONICS lineage and against a comparison system's MoM lineage, the author finds 241 and 200 rows respectively map into those systems' own training or validation splits -- overlap that a naive accuracy comparison would never surface.

方法Method

核心資料集有 828 首曲目:605 首合成(310 首來自 AIME 釋出版本、180 首來自 Suno 的 CDN/補充資料來源、115 首來自 Udio),以及 223 首真人音樂(150 首來自 FMA 資料庫、73 首是從公開網路上找來的「困難負樣本」)。每首曲目都帶有穩定 ID、被評測音訊位元組的 SHA-256 雜湊值,以及系譜 ID,確保出自同一個原始來源的錄音不會被分到不同切分裡;凡是能對到 ArtifactNet 自己訓練資料的位元組完全相符項目,以及靠聲學指紋找到的兩個真人音訊重複版本,都直接被移除。接著在各個來源分層內部,用公開發布的 salt 值與固定的 20/10/70 目標比例做決定論式切分,分成 166 首的校準集、83 首的驗證集,以及 579 首的封存測試集,而且這個切分在跑最後的四模型比較之前就先寫進 manifest 凍結。

四個版本鎖定的偵測器,在各自所需的輸入格式之前都接受相同的前處理:ArtifactNet v9.4(每首曲目取七段等間隔的四秒片段打分,七段裡至少四段數值有效時取中位數)、SpecTTTra-α(120 秒置中裁切)、Deezer ISMIR 2025 邏輯回歸偵測器,以及 CLAM(決定論式 90 秒裁切,checkpoint 與編碼器版本都用雜湊值鎖定)。每個偵測器的操作門檻只在校準切分上決定一次——在假陽性率不超過 5% 的門檻裡,挑真陽性率最高的那個——決定後就凍結,不再碰驗證或測試資料。解碼失敗、模型輸出非有限值、機率超出合理範圍,都各自記成獨立的失敗事件,不會被悄悄丟掉或硬轉成一個分類結果;所有偵測器之間的主要比較,都只算在四個偵測器全部成功打分的曲目交集上。

The core cohort has 828 tracks: 605 synthetic (310 from the AIME release, 180 from Suno's CDN/supplementary sources, 115 from Udio) and 223 real (150 from the FMA archive, 73 'hard negative' recordings pulled from the open web). Every track carries a stable ID, a SHA-256 digest of the evaluated audio bytes, and a lineage ID that keeps recordings from the same underlying source from crossing between splits; exact byte matches recovered from ArtifactNet's own training data, and two real-audio duplicates found by acoustic fingerprinting, were removed outright. Tracks are then split deterministically within each source stratum, using a published salt and a fixed 20/10/70 target ratio, into a 166-track calibration set, an 83-track validation set, and a 579-track sealed test set, with the split frozen to a manifest before the final four-model comparison ran.

Four version-pinned detectors are evaluated under identical preprocessing up to each model's own required input format: ArtifactNet v9.4 (which scores seven evenly-spaced four-second chunks per track and takes the median when at least four of seven are numerically valid), SpecTTTra-alpha (120-second center crop), the Deezer ISMIR-2025 logistic detector, and CLAM (deterministic 90-second crop, with checkpoint and encoder revisions pinned by hash). The operating threshold for each detector is selected once, on the calibration split only, by maximizing true-positive rate among thresholds that keep false-positive rate under 5%, then frozen before touching validation or test data. Decode failures, non-finite model outputs, and out-of-range probabilities are logged as distinct failure events rather than silently dropped or converted into a class prediction, and every headline comparison across detectors is computed only on the intersection of tracks all four detectors successfully scored.

限制Limitations

作者自己講得很直接:資料集本身就是一項限制——它「規模不大、類別不平衡,而且集中在少數幾個生成器家族上」,也就是說,像是關於 Udio 生成曲目的結論,其實只建立在 828 首總數裡相對薄的一小片切片上;部分補充曲目也缺少平台版本標籤,讓那幾筆的系譜判定精確度打了折扣。外洩稽核本身也天生不完整:光靠公開系譜證據,沒辦法重建 CLAM 宣稱的每一筆訓練資料身分,留下 316 筆真人音樂的曝光狀態無法判定——既不能確認乾淨,也不能確認有汙染;內容雜湊比對抓得到完全相同的位元組重複,但抓不到同一份錄音經過感知上無異的重新編碼或重新母帶處理的版本。

範疇也刻意設得很窄,這會限制讀者該怎麼解讀這個基準測試的結果:它只區分「完全生成」跟「完全真人」的音訊,不處理部分 AI 協助或人類參與製作程度的情況;而這裡評測的 Deezer 偵測器,是作者自己重現的 ISMIR 2025 方法公開版本,不是 Deezer 實際上線的產品系統,所以這些數字不該被當成在評論一個真正部署的商用產品。作者也特別聲明,這個基準測試沒辦法判定著作權歸屬或創作意圖——片段層級的定位、混合製作過程的追蹤,以及任何商業或法律上的裁決,都明確排除在範疇之外。

The author is explicit that the cohort itself is a limitation: it is "modest, class-imbalanced, and concentrated in several generator families," meaning conclusions about, say, Udio-generated tracks rest on a comparatively thin slice of the 828-track total, and platform version labels are missing for some of the supplementary tracks, weakening the precision of the lineage claims for those rows. The leakage audit is also incomplete by construction: public lineage evidence cannot reconstruct every training identity claimed for CLAM, leaving 316 real-music rows with unresolved exposure status rather than a confirmed clean or dirty label, and content-hash matching can catch exact byte duplicates but not every perceptually-identical re-encoding or remaster of the same recording.

Scope is deliberately narrow in ways that limit how the benchmark's results should be read: it only distinguishes fully-generated audio from fully-real audio, not partial AI assistance or degree of human involvement in production, and the Deezer detector evaluated here is the authors' own public reimplementation of the ISMIR-2025 method, not Deezer's actual production system, so its numbers should not be read as a statement about a deployed commercial product. The author is also careful to state the benchmark cannot establish authorship, copyright status, or intent -- segment-level localization, hybrid-production tracking, and any commercial or legal adjudication are explicitly out of scope.

討論Discussion

為什麼總分會誤導人,表四的逐生成器拆解是最清楚的例子:ArtifactNet 在 AIME 生成的曲目上真陽性率有 0.926,Suno 上是 0.897,但在 Udio 上掉到 0.650;Deezer 偵測器則幾乎呈相反的樣貌——在 AIME 上只有 0.332,但在 Suno 上 0.968、Udio 上 0.875;而 Deezer 對真人音樂的假陽性率,更從乾淨的 FMA 資料庫的 0.020,一路飆到網路蒐集的困難負樣本的 0.211。如果全部平均成單一數字,這兩個偵測器看起來差不多強(整體 AUROC 0.982 對 0.761);但按生成器與領域拆開來看,它們失敗的地方幾乎是互補的——這正是單一排行榜分數最容易藏起來的那種樣態。

這篇論文還量化了一個大多數偵測論文完全不會報告的取捨:覆蓋率跟嚴格程度之間的權衡。用寬鬆規則——七段四秒片段裡只要至少四段給出有限數值就採納 ArtifactNet 對該曲目的分數——它能對 579 首測試曲目裡的 562 首打分,AUROC 0.982;換成嚴格規則——只要有一段片段是非有限值就整首拒收——只剩 467 首能打分,AUROC 微幅上升到 0.985,但一旦把每首沒打到分的曲目都算成失敗,平衡準確率就會崩到 0.710。作者的結論不是主張哪個偵測器絕對比較強,而是「分母」——一個排行榜數字究竟是在哪些曲目上算出來的——必須講清楚,不能被預設。

The clearest illustration of why aggregate scores mislead is the per-generator breakdown in Table 4: ArtifactNet holds a 0.926 true-positive rate on AIME-generated tracks and 0.897 on Suno, but drops to 0.650 on Udio, while the Deezer detector shows almost the opposite profile -- a weak 0.332 on AIME but 0.968 on Suno and 0.875 on Udio -- and Deezer's false-positive rate on real music itself swings from 0.020 on the clean FMA archive to 0.211 on web-sourced hard negatives. Averaged into a single number, these two detectors would look roughly comparable (0.982 vs. 0.761 AUROC overall); broken out by generator and domain, they turn out to fail in almost complementary places, which is exactly the kind of pattern a single leaderboard score is built to hide.

The paper also quantifies a coverage-versus-strictness tradeoff that most detection papers do not report at all: under a lenient rule that accepts ArtifactNet's per-track score whenever at least four of seven four-second chunks produce a finite value, it scores 562 of 579 test tracks at 0.982 AUROC; under a strict rule that rejects any track with even one non-finite chunk, it only scores 467 tracks, AUROC rises slightly to 0.985, but balanced accuracy collapses to 0.710 once every unscored track is counted as a failure. The author's conclusion is not a claim that any detector is definitively better, but that 'the denominator' -- which tracks a leaderboard number is actually computed over -- needs to be stated, not assumed.

Hi-Singers: A Comprehensive High-Quality Dataset for Expressive Audio-Driven Singing Head Synthesis

Hi-Singers:用於富表現力音訊驅動演唱頭部合成的高品質綜合資料集

Yichi Zhang, Hui Zhang, Guanjun Liu, Yuefeng Zou, Fengzhao Sun, Jun Yu
arXiv:2609.22264 · cs.MM · 投稿submitted 2026-09-07
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把以擴散模型為基礎的 Hallo,在 Hi-Singers 的 29,608 個演唱片段(約 170 小時)上微調後,Beat Alignment Score 從 0.139 提升到 0.168,對嘴同步指標 Sync-C 也從 7.58 提升到 7.72;而只在語音資料上訓練過的通用基準模型 SadTalker 與 V-Express,在同一個演唱測試集上 BAS 只有 0.052 與 0.076——差距大約三倍,作者把這個結果解讀成:只用語音訓練出來的說話頭模型,學不到演唱需要的誇張下顎動作與貼著節拍走的律動。Why this one: Fine-tuning the diffusion-based Hallo model on Hi-Singers' 29,608 singing clips (about 170 hours) lifts its Beat Alignment Score from 0.139 to 0.168 and its lip-sync Sync-C from 7.58 to 7.72, while generic speech-trained baselines SadTalker and V-Express manage only 0.052 and 0.076 BAS on the same singing test set -- a roughly 3x gap that the authors read as evidence that talking-head models trained on speech alone do not transfer to the exaggerated jaw motion and beat-locked rhythm singing requires.

動機Motivation

音訊驅動的說話頭合成,在一般語音上已經能做到接近真人的畫質,但演唱是不同的問題:演唱音訊裡有旋律跟伴奏,會干擾原本就不容易做好的音畫對齊,而演唱表演所需要的臉部表情與下顎張合,也遠比語音相對靜態的動作誇張得多。只在語音資料上訓練出來的模型,套用到演唱上常常會出現「節奏漂移」,因為它們從沒學過要把臉部動作鎖定在音樂節拍上,而不是鎖定在音素的時序上。作者認為問題根源在資料而不是模型:現有的說話頭合成資料集嚴重偏向語音,少數幾個專門收集演唱的資源,不是規模太小,就是只提供 3D 動作參數而不是照片級真實的 2D 影片,或者是在受控的攝影棚環境裡蒐集的,套用到真實世界的影片時類推能力不足。

Audio-driven talking-head synthesis has reached photorealistic results for ordinary speech, but singing is a different regime: singing audio carries melody and instrumental accompaniment that interfere with straightforward audio-visual alignment, and singing performance demands far more exaggerated facial expressions and jaw openings than the comparatively static motion of speech. Models trained only on speech data tend to exhibit 'rhythmic drift' when applied to singing, because they have never learned to lock facial motion to a musical beat rather than to phoneme timing. The authors trace this to a data problem rather than a modeling one: datasets for talking-head synthesis are heavily biased toward speech, and the handful of existing singing-specific resources are either too small, limited to 3D motion parameters rather than photorealistic 2D video, or collected in controlled studio settings that do not generalize to in-the-wild footage.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

說話頭合成這條研究線,從早期的 3D 可變形臉部模型(VOCA、FLAME),經過 NeRF 系方法,一路走到近期的擴散模型(Wav2Lip、EMO、Hallo、V-Express),但這些模型幾乎都是拿語音資料集(像是收錄真實世界說話片段的 HDTF,或用來做說話者身分辨識、不是演唱的 VoxCeleb)訓練出來的。

專門針對演唱的資料集雖然有,但很有限:MusicFace 與 Song2Face 分別只有大約 40 小時與 2 小時的攝影棚 3D 臉部動畫,SingingHead 提供約 27 小時、解析度 1024p 的實驗室 4D 掃描,ChorusHead 與 RapVerse 雖然擴展到真實世界影片,但仍停在 3D 頭部動畫或身體動作的表示法上。這些資源沒有一個同時滿足「真實世界蒐集、照片級 2D 影片、大規模演唱內容」三個條件,而這正是 Hi-Singers 想填補的空缺——它提供 29,608 個片段(約 170 小時),都是從 1080p 以上的來源影片裁出的 512×512 臉部畫面。

The talking-head literature runs from early 3D morphable face models (VOCA, FLAME) through NeRF-based methods to recent diffusion systems -- Wav2Lip, EMO, Hallo, V-Express -- all trained predominantly on speech datasets such as HDTF (in-the-wild talking-head clips) and VoxCeleb (speaker identity, not singing).

A handful of datasets do target singing specifically: MusicFace and Song2Face provide roughly 40 and 2 hours of studio 3D facial animation respectively, SingingHead offers about 27 hours of lab-recorded 4D scans at 1024p, and ChorusHead and RapVerse extend to in-the-wild footage but stay in 3D head-animation or body-motion representations. None of these combine in-the-wild collection, photorealistic 2D video, and singing-specific content at scale, which is the gap Hi-Singers is built to fill with 29,608 clips (~170 hours) of 512x512 face crops from 1080p+ source footage.

方法Method

資料建置的起點,是從 YouTube、TikTok、Bilibili 上蒐集來的大約 1,200 GB(4,500 小時)正面演唱影片,涵蓋中英文與多種曲風,再依照四項原則篩選——真的是在唱歌(不是背景放著音樂、人在講話)、臉部解析度夠高且穩定、五官完整不被遮擋或變形、鏡頭連續不會晃動過度——並用自動化流程強制執行:先用 face-alignment 網路把畫面裁到偵測出的臉周圍 512×512,再用 SyncNet 濾掉音畫不同步的片段;接著用臉部中心位移的滑動視窗分析,加上 solvePnP 估出的歐拉角,剔除動作過大或角度偏離正面的片段;最後用 DSL-FIQA 以 5 fps 打影像品質分數,同時用硬門檻與分數序列的四分位距做篩選。

留下來的片段還要再經過人工評分:十位工程師針對五項標準打分——演唱內容相關性、美學品質、動作穩定度、發音清晰度,以及 DSL-FIQA 分數——再用邏輯回歸學出權重,把這五項合併成每個片段一個接受分數。最後得到 29,608 個片段,每段 20 到 25 秒,總共約 170 小時,中英文比例接近各半,曲風以流行為主,也涵蓋古典、搖滾、嘻哈。為了驗證資料集的價值,作者拿三個現有架構做基準測試——Hallo(階層式擴散模型)、SadTalker(基於 3D 係數)、V-Express(潛在空間擴散模型)——把 Hallo 在 Hi-Singers 上微調,拿去跟它原本用語音訓練出的權重比較,也跟改用既有的 CelebV-HQ 資料集訓練的版本比較,評估指標用 FID/FVD 看畫質、Sync-C/Sync-D 看對嘴同步,再加上自訂的 Beat Alignment Score 看節奏準確度。

Construction starts from about 1,200 GB (4,500 hours) of front-facing singing footage scraped from YouTube, TikTok, and Bilibili across English and Chinese content and multiple genres, then narrows it through four stated principles -- centered on actual singing (not talking over background music), sufficiently high and stable face resolution, fully visible undistorted facial features, and continuous framing without excessive shake -- enforced by an automated pipeline: a face-alignment network crops to 512x512 around the detected face and SyncNet filters out audio-visual misalignment, a sliding-window analysis of facial-center displacement plus solvePnP-estimated Euler angles rejects excessive motion or off-axis framing, and DSL-FIQA scores image quality at 5 fps to filter on both a hard threshold and the interquartile range of the score sequence.

Surviving clips then go through manual scoring: ten engineers rate five criteria -- singing-content relevance, aesthetic quality, movement stability, articulation clarity, and the DSL-FIQA score -- and a logistic regression learns weights that combine these into one acceptance score per clip. The result is 29,608 clips of 20-25 seconds each, roughly 170 hours total, split close to evenly between English and Chinese and spanning pop (dominant), classical, rock, and hip-hop. To validate the dataset, the authors benchmark three existing architectures -- Hallo (hierarchical diffusion), SadTalker (3D-coefficient-based), and V-Express (latent diffusion) -- fine-tuning Hallo on Hi-Singers and comparing against its original speech-trained weights and against training on the existing CelebV-HQ dataset instead, using FID/FVD for visual quality, Sync-C/Sync-D for lip-sync, and a purpose-built Beat Alignment Score for rhythmic accuracy.

限制Limitations

Hi-Singers 這篇論文本身沒有獨立的限制章節,所以要從資料集與評測設計本身反推侷限。語料只涵蓋中英文,對其他語言的類推能力完全沒測過;曲風分布以流行為主(圖四),這可能讓「好的」節奏與表情動作被定義得偏向這一種音樂風格。用來算出表三、表四那些數字的評測基準集只有 200 個片段,拿來主張類推能力其實偏薄;主觀 MOS 評分也只找了 10 位研究人員,不是更大範圍的評審團,這讓 3.85 到 3.92 這個 MOS 提升能撐起的分量打了折扣。

架構比較的範圍也不寬:只在三個既有系統(Hallo、SadTalker、V-Express)上做了微調或評測,所以 Hi-Singers 的優勢能不能類推到這個「擴散模型加 3D 係數」家族以外的自迴歸或 GAN 系演唱合成方法,完全沒有驗證過。表四按曲風拆開來看,資料集帶來的進步也不平均——嘻哈這個類別表現落後其他曲風,甚至在 Sync-C 上還輸給原始的 Hallo 權重(7.66 對 7.85)——論文報告了這個落差,但沒有進一步分析;論文也完全沒有討論失敗案例、對抗性穩健度,或釋出模型與資料的部署風險。

Hi-Singers has no dedicated limitations section in the paper itself, so the constraints have to be read off the dataset and evaluation design. The corpus covers only English and Chinese content, leaving generalization to other languages untested, and pop dominates the genre mix (Figure 4), which risks skewing what 'good' rhythmic and expressive motion means toward one musical style. The evaluation benchmark used to report Table 3 and Table 4 numbers holds only 200 clips, a modest slice for claims about generalization, and subjective MOS scores come from just 10 researchers rather than a broader panel, which narrows how much weight the 3.85-to-3.92 MOS improvement can bear.

The architecture comparison is also narrow: only three existing systems (Hallo, SadTalker, V-Express) were fine-tuned or evaluated on the dataset, so it remains untested whether Hi-Singers' advantage generalizes to autoregressive or GAN-based singing-synthesis approaches outside this diffusion-and-3D-coefficient family. Genre-level results in Table 4 also show the dataset's gains are uneven -- hip-hop performance trails the other genres and even loses to the original Hallo weights on Sync-C (7.66 vs. 7.85) -- a gap the paper reports without further analysis, and there is no discussion of failure cases, adversarial robustness, or deployment risk for the released models and data.

討論Discussion

這個資料集有用最有力的證據,來自「在 Hi-Singers 上微調」跟「在既有的 CelebV-HQ 資料集上用同一個 Hallo 架構訓練」兩者的對比:用 CelebV-HQ 訓練,結果反而比原本用語音訓練出的權重更差(FID 從 23.21 惡化到 42.23,FVD 從 188.34 惡化到 385.67);而用 Hi-Singers 訓練,則每一項指標都變好(FID 23.15、FVD 181.42、Sync-C 7.72、BAS 0.168、MOS 3.92)。這個對比比單看數字本身更重要,因為它把「資料集的貢獻」跟「架構的貢獻」分開來看:同一個模型換一個既有資料集,結果往錯的方向走;換成專門收集的真實世界演唱片段,結果才往對的方向走。

節奏這個指標把領域落差講得最清楚:只用語音訓練出的通用基準模型 SadTalker 與 V-Express,在演唱測試集上 BAS 分別只有 0.052 與 0.076,對比在 Hi-Singers 上微調過的 Hallo 有 0.168,差距大約兩到三倍——這不是單靠語音資料訓練能補起來的落差。再往下拆,用 Hi-Singers 訓練後,中文演唱受益最多(Sync-C 7.82、BAS 0.170),優於英文(7.62、0.166);而搖滾在所有曲風裡 BAS 單項最高(0.172),儘管流行曲風的 MOS 最高(3.95)。作者把這個公開在 Hugging Face 上的資料集與 200 片段評測基準,定位成給演唱合成研究重複使用的基礎建設,而不是綁死在自家模型上的一次性結果。

The strongest evidence for the dataset's value is the contrast between fine-tuning on Hi-Singers versus on the existing CelebV-HQ dataset with the same Hallo architecture: CelebV-HQ training actually makes results worse than the original speech-trained weights (FID rises from 23.21 to 42.23, FVD from 188.34 to 385.67), while Hi-Singers training improves every metric (FID 23.15, FVD 181.42, Sync-C 7.72, BAS 0.168, MOS 3.92). That contrast matters more than the raw numbers alone, because it isolates the dataset's contribution from the architecture's: the same model, on a different existing dataset, moves in the wrong direction, while training on singing-specific in-the-wild footage moves it in the right one.

The rhythm metric tells the clearest domain-gap story: generic speech-trained baselines SadTalker and V-Express score BAS of just 0.052 and 0.076 on the singing test set, versus 0.168 for Hallo fine-tuned on Hi-Singers, a roughly 2-3x gap that speech-only training simply cannot close. Breaking results down further, Chinese-language singing benefits most from Hi-Singers training (Sync-C 7.82, BAS 0.170) versus English (7.62, 0.166), and rock tracks post the single highest BAS (0.172) among genres despite pop leading on MOS (3.95). The authors frame the released dataset and 200-clip benchmark, both public on Hugging Face, as reusable infrastructure for singing-synthesis research rather than a one-off result tied to their own model.
