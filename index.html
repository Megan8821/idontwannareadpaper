MusicLayout: Explicit Structural Planning for Controllable Text-to-Music Generation

MusicLayout：為可控文字轉音樂生成加入顯式結構規劃層

Shuyu Li, Kejun Zhang, Jiahe Lei, Shulei Ji, Zihao Wang, Jiaxing Yu, Wanying Wu, Lei Wang
arXiv:2608.09035 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：現在的文字轉音樂系統幾乎都只靠一句全域文字提示決定整首歌，段落怎麼排、素材何時重複或變化、樂器何時進出，全部隱藏在模型內部，使用者生成前完全看不到也改不了。這篇論文把「段落配置」明確變成一層可檢視、可編輯的中介表示（不是額外輸入，而是模型自己先生成、使用者可以再改），架構建立在 ACE-Step 1.5 上，用單一自迴歸序列先生成 layout 再生成音訊 token。難得的是它老實報告了三個資料集（合成 MIDI、跨域 MIDI、真實人聲伴奏）上結果不一致：在訓練分佈內全面勝出，換到真實錄音音色時反而全面輸給沒有 layout 的基線，這個誠實的失敗案例本身就很有參考價值，因此標記為深度分析。Why this one: Today's text-to-music systems decide an entire song from one global text prompt, leaving section order, when material repeats or varies, and when instruments enter or exit, entirely implicit and impossible to inspect or edit before generation. This paper turns that structural plan into an explicit, inspectable, editable intermediate representation — not a separate input, but something the model generates first and a user can then revise — built on ACE-Step 1.5 with a single autoregressive sequence that emits a layout before audio tokens. It's flagged as a deep dive because it candidly reports inconsistent results across three evaluation sets (synthesized MIDI, out-of-domain MIDI, and real vocal-plus-accompaniment audio): dominant in-distribution, but clearly worse than the no-layout baseline once real recorded timbre enters the picture — an honest failure case that's informative in its own right.

動機Motivation

文字轉音樂生成近年進步很快，但主流系統（MusicGen、AudioLDM 2、MeLoDy 等）幾乎都只靠一句全域文字提示決定整首曲子，提示可以描述風格、情緒、樂器編制或速度，卻無法指定「段落怎麼組織、素材什麼時候重複或變化、織度怎麼演變、樂器何時加入或退出」這類長程結構安排。既有的可控生成方法通常是額外加一個屬性通道（旋律參考、鼓點、和弦、力度），這些控制仍然是局部的、隱式的，或需要額外的符號輸入，沒有一個統一、隨時間對齊的表示可以同時描述整首曲子的段落組織與編制安排。作者借用影像生成領域的想法：像 PlanGen 這類方法透過在生成內容前先暴露一層顯式的高階規劃，明顯提升了可控性；這篇論文把同樣的原則搬到音樂上,只是把「沿著空間規劃」換成「沿著時間規劃」。

Text-to-music generation has advanced quickly, but mainstream systems (MusicGen, AudioLDM 2, MeLoDy, and similar) rely almost entirely on a single global text prompt to determine an entire piece. A prompt can describe genre, mood, instrumentation, or tempo, but it cannot specify how sections are organized, when material recurs or varies, how texture evolves, or when instruments enter and exit — the long-range structural decisions that shape a song. Existing controllable-generation methods typically bolt on a single attribute channel (a melody reference, drum pattern, chords, dynamics), and these controls remain local, implicit, or dependent on extra symbolic input; no unified, time-aligned representation exists that describes both the sectional organization and the instrumental arrangement of an entire piece at once. The authors borrow an idea from image generation, where methods like PlanGen expose an explicit high-level plan before content generation and measurably improve controllability; this paper adapts the same principle to music, simply replacing planning-over-space with planning-over-time.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景涵蓋兩條線。文字轉音樂生成方法上：自迴歸系統（Jukebox、MusicLM、MusicGen）直接預測離散音訊 token；擴散式系統（Noise2Music、Moûsai）用逐步去噪合成；混合式系統（AudioLDM 2、MeLoDy）把語言模型式的語意建模與擴散合成結合；較新的 Stable Audio 3 支援變長生成，ACE-Step 1.5 則結合語言模型規劃與 DiT 合成。可控生成方面：多數工作是在全域提示外再補一個額外控制通道，例如旋律、節奏、力度、和弦、鼓點；Seed-Music 用符號化的 lead-sheet 管線；ACE-Step 1.5 本身已經納入 metadata 與隱式的「歌曲藍圖」規劃。作者指出這些既有做法的共同侷限：控制是逐項的、外加的、任務特定的，或者只到粗粒度的 metadata 層級，沒有一個表示能同時、顯式地暴露段落、織度、重複變化關係與樂器編制,並且讓使用者在生成音訊前就能檢視與修改。這正是 MusicLayout 想填的空白。

The related work spans two threads. On text-to-music generation architectures: autoregressive systems (Jukebox, MusicLM, MusicGen) predict discrete audio tokens directly; diffusion-based systems (Noise2Music, Moûsai) synthesize via iterative denoising; hybrid systems (AudioLDM 2, MeLoDy) combine language-model-style semantic modeling with diffusion synthesis; and more recent systems like Stable Audio 3 support variable-length generation while ACE-Step 1.5 combines LM-based planning with DiT synthesis. On controllable generation: most work supplements the global prompt with a single extra control channel — melody, rhythm, dynamics, chords, or drums; Seed-Music uses a symbolic lead-sheet pipeline; ACE-Step 1.5 itself already incorporates metadata and an implicit song-blueprint plan. The authors point to a shared limitation across this prior work: control is piecemeal, bolted-on, task-specific, or limited to coarse metadata, with no single representation that explicitly exposes sections, texture, repetition/variation relationships, and instrumental arrangement together, in a form a user can inspect and edit before audio is generated. That is the gap MusicLayout is built to fill.

方法Method

MusicLayout 本身是一套結構化表示：沿著共用時間軸,同時描述「整首曲子層級」與「片段層級」的資訊。整首曲子層級用「家族」（family）把曲子內部素材相近的片段分組（fam_0、fam_1...），並記錄片段之間的重複／變化關係。片段層級每個片段對應一段時間區間，帶有：段落標籤（11 類，如 intro、verse、prechorus、chorus、bridge、breakdown、outro、transition、hook、solo、build）、織度（10 類，如 layered、rhythm_driven、melodic_front、percussive、build_up、sparse_pulse 等）、變化強度（4 級：unique、same、light_var、strong_var，用該片段與所屬家族原型的 Jaccard 距離與能量/密度/角色數差異加權計算後分級）、樂器角色（25 類，如 acoustic_piano、electric_guitar、bass、strings、synth_pad、drums 等）以及每個角色的音域、密度、能量屬性。整份 layout 序列化成一段類 XML 的純文字格式，方便人類閱讀與編輯。生成架構建立在 ACE-Step 1.5（17 億參數語言模型）之上，音訊 tokenizer（5Hz）與 Diffusion Transformer 保持凍結不動，模型被擴充成先在同一個自迴歸序列裡生成 layout token,再以 layout 為條件生成音訊 token,形式化為聯合分布 p(l,a|c)=p(l|c)p(a|c,l)。訓練分兩階段：第一階段只更新新增詞彙對應的 embedding 與輸出層權重（凍結其餘部分），跑 10 個 epoch；第二階段完整微調整個語言模型,同時最佳化「layout 規劃」與「layout 條件下的音訊生成」兩個損失,跑 100 個 epoch。訓練用 FreeMIDI（24474 筆訓練樣本，MIDI 經 FluidSynth 合成音訊，提示語由另一個語言模型生成）,並在域外資料 MidiCaps（1040 筆）與完全域外的真實中文人聲伴奏資料 MuChin（1000 筆，人聲已用 BS-RoFormer 分離掉）上做評估,藉此檢驗跨資料集甚至跨音色型態的泛化能力。

MusicLayout is itself a structured representation describing both piece-level and segment-level information along a shared timeline. At the piece level, "families" group segments that share similar musical material within a piece (fam_0, fam_1, ...) and record repetition/variation relationships across segments. At the segment level, each segment spans a time interval and carries: a section label (11 categories — intro, verse, prechorus, chorus, bridge, breakdown, outro, transition, hook, solo, build), a texture label (10 categories — layered, rhythm_driven, melodic_front, percussive, build_up, sparse_pulse, and others), a variation strength (4 levels — unique, same, light_var, strong_var, computed from a weighted combination of Jaccard distance and differences in energy/density/role count relative to the segment's family prototype), instrument roles (25 categories — acoustic_piano, electric_guitar, bass, strings, synth_pad, drums, and others), and register/density/energy attributes per role. The whole layout is serialized into a human-readable, XML-like plain-text format for inspection and editing. The generation architecture builds on ACE-Step 1.5 (a 1.7B-parameter language model), keeping its 5Hz audio tokenizer and Diffusion Transformer frozen, and extends it to generate layout tokens first within the same autoregressive sequence, then condition audio-token generation on that layout — formalized as the joint distribution p(l,a|c)=p(l|c)p(a|c,l). Training runs in two stages: stage one updates only the embedding and output-layer rows for newly introduced vocabulary (everything else frozen) for 10 epochs; stage two fully fine-tunes the language model, jointly optimizing a layout-planning loss and a layout-conditioned audio-generation loss for 100 epochs. Training uses FreeMIDI (24,474 examples, MIDI synthesized to audio via FluidSynth, prompts generated by another language model), with evaluation on out-of-domain MidiCaps (1,040 examples) and a fully out-of-domain real-recording set, MuChin (1,000 examples of Chinese vocal-plus-accompaniment audio with vocals separated out via BS-RoFormer) — testing generalization across both dataset and, critically, timbral realism.

限制Limitations

作者自陳的限制相當明確。方法只能在生成前規劃,無法對已存在的音訊做局部編輯或重新生成某一段同時保留其餘部分不變；樂器表示停在「類別」層級,不描述精確音色、演奏細節或後製效果；訓練資料完全來自 MIDI 合成音訊,聲學多樣性與真實感有限,論文也直接承認 layout 「只保證是一個結構完整的請求,不保證每個指定屬性都會被完美實現」。最值得注意的是實測到的失敗模式：在域內 FreeMIDI 上,加了 layout 的模型幾乎全指標勝出;換到域外 MidiCaps 時,生成式 layout 的結構複雜度指標（SCM）從參考 layout 的 0.087 惡化到 0.804,顯示模型自己生成的 layout 已經偏離真實結構分佈；換到真實錄音的 MuChin 時情況更明顯,FAD 從基線的 2.480 惡化到 3.456、SCM 從 0.186 惡化到 0.655,作者將此歸咎於訓練（MIDI 合成音訊）與評估（真實人聲伴奏錄音）之間的分佈落差——這代表這套方法目前驗證的可控性,主要成立在「音色本身也是合成出來」的封閉世界裡,離真實錄音場景還有明顯距離。

The authors state their limitations plainly: the method only plans before generation and cannot edit existing audio locally or regenerate one region while preserving the rest; instrument representation stops at the categorical level, not exact timbre, performance nuance, or production effects; training data is entirely MIDI-synthesized audio with limited acoustic diversity and realism, and the paper is explicit that a valid layout "is a well-formed structural request, not a guarantee that every specified attribute will be realized perfectly." The most notable limitation is an observed failure mode rather than a stated caveat: on in-domain FreeMIDI, the layout-conditioned model wins on nearly every metric; but moving to out-of-domain MidiCaps, the generated-layout condition's structural-complexity metric (SCM) degrades from the reference layout's 0.087 to 0.804, indicating the model's own generated layout has drifted from the true structural distribution; moving further to the real-recording MuChin set, this worsens sharply — FAD degrades from the baseline's 2.480 to 3.456, SCM from 0.186 to 0.655 — which the authors attribute to a distribution gap between MIDI-synthesized training audio and real vocal-plus-accompaniment evaluation recordings. This means the controllability validated here mainly holds within a closed world where the timbre itself is also synthetic, and there remains a clear gap before it transfers to real recorded audio.

討論Discussion

整篇論文最有意思的地方不是「加了 layout 就變好」這個直覺結論,而是它系統性地拆解了這個結論在哪裡成立、在哪裡崩潰。在域內合成資料上,不論用參考 layout 還是模型自己生成的 layout,幾乎所有聲學與結構指標都優於沒有 layout 的匹配資料對照組（同樣資料訓練但拿掉 layout）,證明結構規劃本身確實提供了額外訊號,而不只是資料量增加的效果——這一點透過刻意設計的「打亂 layout」消融組得到驗證：把 layout 換成不相關的另一份 layout,效果介於有 layout 與無 layout 之間,說明「layout 形狀本身」與「layout 跟內容是否對應」都各自有貢獻。更值得工程實務參考的是細粒度編輯實驗：直接在 240 秒的曲子中段替換樂器角色、調降某段的密度與能量、或重寫段落順序,都能在生成音訊中量到對應的、方向正確的變化（例如密度調降段落的均方根音量確實下降約 15%）,證明這層表示不是裝飾性的,而是真的能被人類編輯並傳導到最終音訊。但作者對域外與真實錄音結果的坦白同樣重要：一旦音色從合成 MIDI 換成真實錄音,模型生成的 layout 品質與最終音訊品質同時崩壞,這說明目前的收穫主要來自「在訓練分佈內把隱式規劃變顯式」,還沒有解決「顯式規劃遇到分佈外音色時該如何維持有效性」這個更難的問題,也是這條研究路線接下來最值得攻克的地方。

The most interesting part of this paper isn't the intuitive headline that adding a layout helps — it's the systematic dissection of exactly where that claim holds and where it breaks. On in-domain synthetic data, both the reference layout and the model's own generated layout beat a matched-data control (same training data, layout removed) on nearly every acoustic and structural metric, showing that structural planning contributes real signal rather than just benefiting from more effective use of the same data — confirmed by a deliberately designed shuffled-layout ablation: pairing training examples with an unrelated layout lands performance between the with-layout and without-layout conditions, indicating both the shape of a layout and its correspondence to content each contribute independently. More practically relevant is the fine-grained editing experiment: directly swapping instrument roles mid-piece, lowering density and energy in a section, or rewriting section order in a 240-second track each produces measurable, correctly-directed changes in the generated audio (e.g. RMS level in a density-reduced section drops by roughly 15%) — evidence that the representation is not merely decorative but genuinely editable by a human and does propagate through to the final audio. Equally important is the authors' candor about the out-of-domain and real-recording results: once timbre shifts from synthesized MIDI to real recordings, both the quality of the model's generated layout and the quality of the final audio degrade together, indicating that the current gains mainly come from making an implicit plan explicit within the training distribution, and have not yet solved the harder problem of keeping explicit planning effective under out-of-distribution timbre — arguably the most important direction for this line of research to tackle next.

A Dual Evaluation for Music Transcription

音樂轉譜的雙軸評估：記譜相似度與播放相似度

Ping Wang, Guang Yang, Nazif Can Tamer, Victoria Ebert, Noah A. Smith
arXiv:2608.04511 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：自動音樂轉譜系統輸出的樂譜同時要服務兩個目的：給音樂家讀的記譜、以及可以重新演奏播放的內容，但過去的評估幾乎都把這兩者混為一談。這篇論文第一次系統性地把「記譜相似度」（用光學樂譜辨識文獻裡的編輯距離）與「播放相似度」（用 14 種音訊特徵、DTW／TWED、CLaMP 3、CLEWS、甚至讓 Gemini 當裁判）分開驗證，並找 106 位真人做 3180 次成對比較當黃金標準，結果發現兩軸經常給出完全不同的系統排名——這代表過去只看單一指標的轉譜論文，排名本身可能就是誤導的。因為方法論本身的嚴謹度與對整個領域評估慣例的挑戰性，標記為深度分析。Why this one: Automatic music transcription systems output sheet music that must serve two purposes at once — readable notation for musicians, and content that can be re-played as audio — yet prior evaluation has almost always conflated the two. This paper is the first to systematically separate notation similarity (an edit-distance metric borrowed from optical music recognition) from playback similarity (validated across 14 audio features, DTW/TWED, CLaMP 3, CLEWS, and even Gemini as an audio judge), anchored by 106 human listeners making 3,180 pairwise judgments as ground truth. It finds the two axes frequently rank systems completely differently — meaning transcription papers that report only one metric may be reporting a misleading ranking. Flagged as a deep dive for its methodological rigor and its direct challenge to a field-wide evaluation convention.

動機Motivation

自動音樂轉譜（AMT）系統的輸出——可讀寫的樂譜——天生要同時服務兩種完全不同的使用情境：音樂家要拿它來讀、理解、甚至列印演奏；另一方面它也可以被重新播放，變成一段可聽的音訊。作者主張這兩個目的分別對應「記譜與參考樂譜的相似度」和「播放與原始演奏的相似度」兩種本質不同的評估,理由是：記譜精確不代表播放起來像原曲（例如演奏者的表情性節奏與原始拍點不同步）；反過來,播放聽起來很像,也可能是犧牲了記譜的可讀性或符號結構換來的。但目前 AMT 評估幾乎都是逐幀的音高-起始-結束指標,這套指標本來是為 MIDI 系統設計的,並不適合評估完整的「音訊轉樂譜」管線；既有的記譜導向指標（Cogliati、MV2H、MUSTER）要嘛沒有互斥的懲罰項、要嘛偏重樂曲結構而非視覺工程排版,而且作者發現 MUSTER 的各項子分數彼此之間的排名並不一致——有的子分數跟記譜相似度高度相關,有的卻跟播放偏好高度相關,混在一個平均分數裡反而模糊了兩者的差異。

The output of an Automatic Music Transcription (AMT) system — readable, editable sheet music — inherently serves two distinct use cases: musicians read, understand, and perform from it, and it can also be re-rendered into audio for playback. The authors argue these two purposes call for two fundamentally different evaluations: notation similarity to a reference score, and playback similarity to the original performance — because notation accuracy doesn't guarantee playback resembles the original (expressive performance timing may not align with the notated beat), and conversely, playback that sounds close to the original may have sacrificed notational readability or symbolic structure to get there. Yet current AMT evaluation is dominated by frame-level pitch-onset-offset metrics designed for MIDI-output systems, which are ill-suited to evaluating a complete audio-to-score pipeline; existing notation-focused metrics (Cogliati, MV2H, MUSTER) either lack disjoint penalty terms, emphasize musical structure over visual engraving quality, or — as the authors demonstrate for MUSTER — produce subscores that disagree with each other, with some tracking notation similarity and others tracking playback preference, a distinction a single averaged score obscures entirely.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

記譜側既有工作：Cogliati 等人用跨多個記譜維度（音符、時值、休止符、小節線、聲部分配）的編輯距離,再迴歸對齊人類評分,但沒有互斥懲罰；MV2H 對多音高、聲部分離、拍子、音值、和聲設計了互斥懲罰,但偏重樂曲結構甚於視覺可讀性,而且要先轉成 MIDI 才能比較,等於繞過了記譜本身；MUSTER 提供多個獨立錯誤率（音高、漏音、多音、起始、結束、聲部、手部分配、音階錯誤）,但官方彙總分數 MeanER 忽略了樂譜組織相關的屬性,作者實測顯示 MUSTER 子分數之間的相關性並不一致（VoiceER 跟光學樂譜辨識編輯距離的相關係數高達 0.94,但 OnsetER 跟人類播放偏好的相關係數只有 0.84,兩者衡量的其實是不同的東西）。播放側既有工作：Ycart 等人用成對聆聽測試驗證鋼琴轉譜指標,並從人類判斷訓練學習型指標,但比較的是合成 MIDI 對合成 MIDI,不是把樂譜重新演算成音訊去對照真實錄音；Simonetta 等人證明標準 MIDI 層級指標跟聽眾對「演奏詮釋是否保留」的判斷相關性很弱,這正是這篇論文動機的一部分來源。這篇論文的獨特之處在於：第一次把「用光學樂譜辨識編輯距離比較 MusicXML 樂譜」與「把樂譜重新演算成音訊再跟原始錄音比較播放相似度」完全分開驗證,並用 106 位真人、3180 次成對判斷做為黃金標準校準。

On the notation side, prior work includes Cogliati et al., who compare edit distance across multiple notation dimensions (notes, durations, rests, barlines, staff assignment) regression-mapped to human ratings but without disjoint penalties; MV2H, which enforces disjoint penalties across multi-pitch, voice separation, meter, note value, and harmony but emphasizes musical structure over visual readability and converts scores to MIDI for comparison, effectively bypassing notation itself; and MUSTER, which provides separate error rates (pitch, miss, extra, onset, offset, voice, hand, scale) but whose official aggregated MeanER omits score-organization properties — the authors show MUSTER's own subscores disagree with each other, with VoiceER correlating strongly with OMR edit distance (ρ=0.94) while OnsetER correlates with human playback preference (ρ=0.84), meaning the subscores are tracking genuinely different things. On the playback side, Ycart et al. validate piano-transcription metrics through pairwise listening tests and train learned metrics from human judgments, but compare synthesized MIDI against synthesized MIDI rather than audio rendered from a score against an actual recording; Simonetta et al. show standard MIDI-level metrics correlate weakly with listener judgments of preserved interpretation, part of the motivation for this work. This paper's distinguishing move is fully separating OMR-edit-distance comparison of MusicXML scores from audio-rendered-score-versus-original-recording playback comparison, calibrated against 106 human listeners and 3,180 pairwise judgments as ground truth.

方法Method

記譜相似度用 OMR-NED（光學樂譜辨識正規化編輯距離）：把樂譜拆成音高、升降記號、符頭、符桿、圓滑線、連結線、演奏記號、譜號、調號、拍號、力度、歌詞等符號,計算插入與刪除操作數,除以兩份樂譜符號總數正規化（取代視為一次刪除加一次插入）,數值越低代表需要人工修改的量越少。播放相似度則是一整套多方法評估：先用固定管線把候選 MusicXML 經 MuseScore 轉 MIDI、再經 FluidSynth 配 SGM 音色庫合成 44.1kHz 音訊並做峰值正規化,確保渲染步驟本身不含可學習成分、不會偷偷影響評估。接著在 14 種音框級音訊特徵（Chroma、HPCP、Chroma CENS、Tonnetz、CQT、STFT 半音、音高顯著度、Mel 頻譜、MFCC、PCEN-Mel、Log-STFT、頻譜通量、起始強度、節奏圖）上,分別測試 DTW（用餘弦距離逐幀比對,路徑長度正規化）與 TWED（額外用 ν 控制時間彈性懲罰、λ 控制編輯懲罰,測了四組參數）；同時測了三種模型／嵌入式評估：讓 Gemini（2.0 到 3.1 版）以 ABX 格式直接聽兩個候選片段判斷哪個更像參考錄音（並用雙向評估校正約 59% 的「偏好選第一個」位置偏誤）、CLaMP 3 多模態嵌入的音訊-音訊／音訊-symbolic 跨模態相似度、以及 CLEWS 學習型音訊嵌入評估器（測了距離、相似度、全域餘弦、片段中位數等聚合方式,最後選片段平均相似度）。人類黃金標準則用參考錨定的二選一強迫選擇（ABX）,106 位參與者各做 30 次試驗,共 3180 次有效判斷,用 Bradley–Terry 模型從成對勝負估計每個系統的潛在偏好分數。評測資料是從 ATEPP 抽出的鋼琴演奏錄音,230 段錄音涵蓋 23 首作品、30 位演奏者、6 位作曲家（貝多芬、莫札特、蕭邦、巴哈、布拉姆斯、李斯特）,每段截到三分鐘。系統組合方面,把 8 個音訊轉 MIDI 模型（MT3、ISMIR 2021、ISMIR 2022 small/base、Basic Pitch、位元組跳動鋼琴模型、Transkun、Aria-AMT）分別接上 3 個 MIDI 轉樂譜轉換器（music21、MuseScore、MIDI2ScoreTransformer）,組成 24 套模組化管線,並額外納入論文完成後才發布的端到端系統 Rubato 作個案研究。

Notation similarity is measured with OMR-NED (OMR Normalized Edit Distance): a score is decomposed into pitch, accidentals, note heads, beams, ties, slurs, articulations, clefs, key/time signatures, dynamics, and lyrics as discrete symbols; insertions and deletions between candidate and reference are counted and normalized by total symbol count (a substitution counts as one deletion plus one insertion), with lower values meaning less manual correction needed. Playback similarity is a full multi-method evaluation: candidate MusicXML is rendered through a fixed pipeline — MuseScore to MIDI, then FluidSynth with an SGM soundfont to 44.1kHz audio, peak-normalized — ensuring the rendering step itself contains no learned component that could quietly bias evaluation. Fourteen frame-level audio features (Chroma, HPCP, Chroma CENS, Tonnetz, CQT, semitone-binned STFT, pitch salience, mel spectrogram, MFCC, PCEN-Mel, log-STFT, spectral flux, onset strength, tempogram) are each tested with DTW (frame-wise cosine-distance alignment, path-length normalized) and TWED (adding a ν parameter for temporal-gap penalty and λ for edit penalty, four configurations tested); three model/embedding-based evaluators are also tested: Gemini (versions 2.0 through 3.1) judging pairwise ABX comparisons by ear to pick which candidate better matches a reference recording (bidirectionally evaluated to correct a roughly 59% first-candidate position bias), CLaMP 3's multimodal audio-audio and audio-symbolic cross-modal similarity, and CLEWS, a learned audio-embedding evaluator (distance, similarity, global-cosine, and segment-median aggregations were tested, with segment-mean similarity selected as best). The human gold standard uses reference-anchored two-alternative forced choice (ABX): 106 participants each completed 30 trials, yielding 3,180 valid judgments, aggregated via a Bradley–Terry model that estimates each system's latent preference score from pairwise wins. The evaluation set draws piano performance recordings from ATEPP — 230 recordings spanning 23 works, 30 performers, and 6 composers (Beethoven, Mozart, Chopin, Bach, Brahms, Liszt), each capped at three minutes. On the system side, 8 audio-to-MIDI models (MT3, ISMIR 2021, ISMIR 2022 small/base, Basic Pitch, ByteDance's piano model, Transkun, Aria-AMT) are each paired with 3 MIDI-to-score converters (music21, MuseScore, MIDI2ScoreTransformer) to form 24 modular pipelines, with the end-to-end system Rubato — released after this study — added as a separate case study.

限制Limitations

作者明確列出範圍限制：整套研究只涵蓋西方古典鋼琴演奏、且只評估每段錄音的前三分鐘，結論不一定能推廣到其他樂器、合奏、人聲、非古典曲風或更長的樂曲結構。播放側結果依賴固定的渲染管線,雖然所有系統都用同一套渲染流程確保公平,但渲染器、音色庫與正規化方式本身可能同時影響自動指標與真人聽感的判斷方向。OMR-NED 只衡量符號層級的相似度,並沒有驗證「記譜品質」本身是否符合人類讀譜與編輯的實際感受——換句話說,兩份符號距離相近的樂譜,人類讀起來的難易度不一定相同。個案研究的端到端系統 Rubato 是在人類聆聽實驗結束後才發布的,所以它的播放品質完全沒有真人判斷可以佐證,目前只能用自動指標推測。作者也坦言未來需要加入樂譜可讀性的真人視覺評估,並測試這套指標選擇方法在不同曲目、不同參與者、不同系統組合下是否依然穩定。

The authors explicitly scope their limitations: the study covers only Western classical piano performance and evaluates only the first three minutes of each recording, so conclusions may not transfer to other instruments, ensembles, voice, non-classical genres, or longer musical structures. Playback-side results depend on a fixed rendering pipeline; although every system is rendered identically for fairness, the renderer, soundfont, and normalization choices could themselves influence both automatic metrics and listener judgments in the same direction. OMR-NED measures only symbol-level similarity and does not validate whether "notation quality" as measured actually matches human readability or editing effort — two scores with similar symbol-level distance to a reference need not be equally easy for a human to read. The end-to-end case-study system, Rubato, was released after the human listening study concluded, so its playback quality has no human judgment to corroborate it and can currently only be inferred from automatic metrics. The authors acknowledge that future work should add human visual evaluation of notation readability and test whether this metric-selection methodology remains stable across held-out pieces, participants, and system combinations.

討論Discussion

核心發現用一句話講完就是：記譜側與播放側常常青睞不同的轉換器家族,系統排名不是單一維度的事。具體來說,MIDI2ScoreTransformer 這個轉換器在記譜相似度上系統性最強（OMR-NED 落在 86 到 90 之間）,MuseScore 在播放相似度上最強（CLEWS 超過 0.82）,而 music21 在兩個維度都表現平庸；更有意思的是,當轉換器本身較弱（music21）時,上游用哪個音訊轉 MIDI 模型幾乎不影響結果（八個模型全部擠在 OMR-NED 96.1 到 96.9 的窄範圍內）,但換成強的轉換器（MuseScore 或 MIDI2ScoreTransformer）,上游模型的選擇會讓分數相差超過 5 分——這代表「評估轉譜系統」如果只看整條管線的單一分數,很可能把差異歸因錯了環節。人類驗證的結果同樣值得注意：在測試的所有自動指標裡,CLEWS 片段平均相似度（ρ=0.971）與 Gemini 3.1 Pro 當裁判（ρ=0.970）跟人類判斷相關性最高,幾乎打平,但 CLEWS 的每千次評估成本只要 0.37 美元,Gemini 裁判要 5.10 美元,人類本身則要 333.33 美元——這給出一個非常直接的實務建議：播放相似度評估首選 CLEWS,不建議用昂貴又跟模型版本綁定的語言模型當裁判。端到端系統 Rubato 的個案研究進一步印證了論文的核心論點：它在記譜相似度上是全部 25 套系統裡最好的（OMR-NED=72.30）,播放相似度也有競爭力但非最佳（CLEWS=0.749,排名第 6）,同時贏過 24 套模組化管線中的 19 套（兩個維度合計）,顯示端到端設計確實有機會繞開模組化管線「記譜好就播放差、播放好就記譜差」的結構性折衷——但它的播放品質目前完全沒有真人聽感驗證,這個開放問題也呼應了論文一開始就強調的立場：轉譜評估不能只看一個數字,而且新系統發布得比評估基礎設施還快的落差,本身就是這個領域需要正視的問題。

The core finding in one sentence: notation-side and playback-side evaluation frequently favor different converter families, so system ranking is not a single-dimensional question. Concretely, MIDI2ScoreTransformer is systematically strongest on notation similarity (OMR-NED in the 86–90 range), MuseScore is strongest on playback similarity (CLEWS above 0.82), and music21 is mediocre on both; more interesting still, when the downstream converter is weak (music21), the choice of upstream audio-to-MIDI model barely matters — all eight models cluster in a narrow OMR-NED range of 96.1–96.9 — but with a strong converter (MuseScore or MIDI2ScoreTransformer), the upstream model choice spreads scores by more than 5 points, meaning that evaluating a transcription system by a single end-to-end score risks attributing differences to the wrong stage of the pipeline. The human-validation results are equally noteworthy: among all automatic metrics tested, CLEWS segment-mean similarity (ρ=0.971) and Gemini 3.1 Pro as judge (ρ=0.970) correlate best with human judgment, essentially tied — but CLEWS costs $0.37 per 1,000 evaluations versus $5.10 for the Gemini judge and $333.33 for humans, giving a direct practical recommendation: default to CLEWS for playback evaluation rather than an expensive, model-version-dependent LLM judge. The end-to-end system Rubato's case study further supports the paper's central argument: it is the best of all 25 systems on notation similarity (OMR-NED=72.30), competitive but not best on playback similarity (CLEWS=0.749, ranked 6th), and beats 19 of 24 modular pipelines combined across both dimensions — evidence that end-to-end design can genuinely sidestep the structural notation-versus-playback tradeoff inherent to modular pipelines. But its playback quality currently has zero human-listening validation, an open question that echoes the paper's opening argument: transcription evaluation cannot rest on a single number, and the fact that new systems are now being released faster than the evaluation infrastructure to properly assess them is itself a problem the field needs to reckon with.

Steering Dense Music Retrieval with Open-Vocabulary Concept Discovery

以開放詞彙概念發現操控稠密音樂檢索

Julien Guinot, Alain Riou, Elio Quinton, György Fazekas
arXiv:2608.08757 · cs.SD, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-09

為什麼挑這篇：使用者常常想對檢索結果做細緻調整——「再環境一點、少一點失真、拿掉吉他」——但現有的稠密音樂檢索系統缺乏這種細粒度控制。這篇論文的關鍵洞察是：過去用「哪個神經元的解碼方向跟文字概念的嵌入最像」來挑要操控的稀疏特徵，這套文字側的啟發式方法在多模態情境下會系統性失準，因為文字空間裡跟某個概念最對齊的神經元，不見得是真正在處理該概念的音訊範例裡被啟動的神經元。作者把選特徵這件事重新定義成一個純幾何的「稀疏反演」問題，完全不需要成對的音訊-文字監督或重新訓練稀疏自編碼器，方法簡潔但背後的診斷（模態落差、特徵分裂與吸收）具有超越這篇論文本身的參考價值，因此標記為深度分析。Why this one: Users often want fine-grained control over retrieval results — more ambient, less distorted, no guitar — but existing dense music retrieval systems lack this granularity. This paper's key insight is that the standard approach to picking which sparse feature to edit — ranking neurons by how closely their decoder direction aligns with a concept's text embedding — fails systematically in multimodal settings, because the neurons most aligned with a concept on the text side are not necessarily the neurons that actually activate for audio examples expressing that concept. The authors reframe feature selection as a purely geometric "sparse inversion" problem, requiring no paired audio-text supervision and no sparse-autoencoder retraining. The method itself is simple, but the underlying diagnosis — modality gap, feature splitting, and feature absorption — has reference value beyond this one paper, which is why it's flagged as a deep dive.

動機Motivation

使用者在做音樂檢索時常常想做細緻調整——「再環境一點」「少一點失真」「拿掉吉他但保留其他特質」——這需要對嵌入空間裡的個別語意概念做精準的加強或抑制，但現有的稠密檢索系統缺乏這種細粒度控制介面。作者指出問題的根源在於「概念歸因」（concept attribution）：要操控一個概念，得先找到嵌入空間裡對應這個概念的方向或神經元，而既有方法幾乎都靠文字側的對齊啟發式——用一個概念的文字嵌入去跟稀疏自編碼器（SAE）裡每個解碼器原子算餘弦相似度，挑最像的那個。但這套方法在多模態情境下有一個根本性的缺陷：文字空間裡跟某個概念最對齊的神經元，不保證是音訊範例裡真正被啟動、真正在編碼這個概念的神經元——這就是常說的「模態落差」（modality gap），加上概念在音訊端經常分散在多個神經元上（而非集中在單一神經元），也加上文字對齊的原子容易吸收多個語意因子（feature splitting/absorption），三個因素疊加讓文字側啟發式方法在音訊端變得不可靠。論文用實驗證實了這個診斷：用餘弦相似度選出的「支持集」（support）不論在結構上還是功能上都表現不佳,跟真正的正例音訊支持集重疊度很低。

Users doing music retrieval often want fine-grained adjustments — more ambient, less distorted, no guitar but otherwise similar — which requires precisely amplifying or suppressing individual semantic concepts within an embedding space, an interface existing dense retrieval systems lack. The authors trace the root cause to concept attribution: to steer a concept, you first need to locate the direction or neurons in embedding space that correspond to it, and existing methods almost universally rely on a text-side alignment heuristic — computing cosine similarity between a concept's text embedding and every decoder atom in a sparse autoencoder (SAE), then picking the closest match. This approach has a fundamental flaw in multimodal settings: the neurons most aligned with a concept on the text side are not guaranteed to be the neurons actually activated by, and genuinely encoding, that concept in audio examples — the well-known "modality gap" — compounded by concepts often being distributed across multiple neurons on the audio side rather than localized to one, and by text-aligned atoms tending to absorb multiple semantic factors at once (feature splitting/absorption). Together these make text-side heuristics unreliable on the audio side. The paper backs this diagnosis experimentally: cosine-similarity-selected "supports" perform poorly both structurally and functionally, overlapping only weakly with the true positive audio support set.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

背景涵蓋三條線。稀疏自編碼器（SAE）本身：近年在語言模型、多模態系統與生成模型的機制可解釋性研究裡，SAE 被廣泛用作把稠密表徵拆成可解讀的稀疏特徵、進而做表徵操控的介面。概念歸因方法：手動或範例式方法（人工檢視神經元、挖掘範例、事後命名）成本高；幾何式探測方法如「Discover-Then-Name」（DTN）把概念的文字嵌入跟 SAE 解碼器原子做對齊，選出最相關的神經元，是目前最接近本篇方法的既有做法。多模態嵌入模型：CLAP 與 MuQ 被列為目前最先進的音樂-文字聯合編碼器，也是這篇論文實驗中拿來抽取音訊嵌入的骨幹。這篇論文與 DTN 這類既有方法的關鍵差異在於問題的定義本身：不把概念歸因當成「獨立地依文字相似度替每個神經元打分排序」，而是重新定義成「稀疏反演問題」——找一組稀疏碼，使其解碼後的表徵既能重建這個概念、又能維持在音訊嵌入空間的幾何結構裡；這個定義本身不需要成對的音訊-文字監督，也不需要重新訓練 SAE，是純粹的推論時（inference-time）方法。

The related work spans three threads. Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) themselves: recent mechanistic-interpretability research on language models, multimodal systems, and generative models has widely used SAEs as an interface for decomposing dense representations into interpretable sparse features and then manipulating them. Concept attribution methods: manual or example-based approaches (inspecting neurons by hand, mining exemplars, post-hoc naming) are costly; geometric probing methods such as Discover-Then-Name (DTN), which align a concept's text embedding to SAE decoder atoms and select the most-aligned neurons, are the closest prior approach to this paper's method. Multimodal embedding models: CLAP and MuQ are cited as state-of-the-art joint music-text encoders and serve as the backbones from which this paper extracts audio embeddings for its experiments. The key difference from DTN-style prior work lies in how the problem itself is framed: rather than independently ranking neurons by text-side similarity, this paper reframes concept attribution as a sparse inversion problem — finding a sparse code whose decoded representation both reconstructs the concept and remains geometrically consistent with the audio embedding space. This framing requires neither paired audio-text supervision nor SAE retraining; it is a purely inference-time method.

方法Method

SAE 訓練設定：音訊嵌入取自 JamendoMaxCaps 資料集，分別用 CLAP 與 MuQ 的音訊塔（皆輸出 512 維嵌入）抽取；SAE 採用 BatchTopK 變體，字典大小 m=4096（擴張倍率 8），測試了五種稀疏度 L0∈{5,10,20,50,100}；訓練用 AdamW（學習率 1e-4）在單張 L40 GPU 上跑 5 萬步，解碼器是綁定編碼器權重的線性層。既有基線 Discover-Then-Name（DTN）的做法是：對每個概念的文字嵌入 z_c，計算它跟每個解碼器原子 d_i 的餘弦相似度，直接選相似度最高的那個原子；作者實測顯示這樣選出來的「支持集」在不同稀疏度下基數變化很大，本質上是挑出一整批神經元的「集合」（bundle）而非單一神經元。核心方法「稀疏模態反演」把問題定義成一個最佳化：在連續空間裡最佳化一個潛在的前激活向量 u，讓它經過訓練時同樣的稀疏化算子 Π 、再經解碼器 D 得到的表徵，跟概念的文字嵌入 z_c 之間的餘弦距離最小。但無約束的反演容易漂移到不像音訊的方向，於是加入一個「流形正則化」項：用馬氏距離衡量反演結果跟從 1 萬筆 MaxCaps 測試集嵌入估計出來的音訊經驗分佈之間的距離，正則化係數固定為 γ=1e-4，確保反演出的概念仍落在合理的音訊空間內。最佳化用兩種策略：一是 Adam 梯度下降（從語意最接近 z_c 的音訊鄰居的稀疏碼開始初始化，避免收斂到爛的局部解）；二是利用解碼器是線性的這個性質，用 FISTA（快速迭代收縮閾值演算法），在 CPU 上單次反演只要約 20 毫秒（沒有正則化項時要約 20 秒）。最後再做一次 IDF 加權後處理，用 log(N/(該神經元在多少樣本中被啟動+ε)) 當權重，抑制過於通用、頻繁啟動的神經元，取加權後啟動值最高的幾個座標當作最終的概念支持集。

The SAE training setup: audio embeddings are extracted from the JamendoMaxCaps dataset using both CLAP and MuQ audio towers (each producing 512-dimensional embeddings); the SAE uses a BatchTopK variant with a dictionary size of m=4096 (expansion factor 8), tested across five sparsity levels L0∈{5,10,20,50,100}; training uses AdamW (learning rate 1e-4) for 50k steps on a single L40 GPU, with the decoder instantiated as a linear layer tied to the encoder weights. The existing Discover-Then-Name (DTN) baseline works by computing cosine similarity between a concept's text embedding z_c and every decoder atom d_i, then selecting the single highest-similarity atom; the authors show empirically that the resulting "support" varies substantially in cardinality across sparsity levels — it is really selecting a bundle of neurons rather than one clean unit. The core method, sparse modality inversion, frames the problem as an optimization: optimize a latent pre-activation vector u in continuous space so that, after passing through the same sparsifying operator Π used during SAE training and then the decoder D, the resulting representation has minimum cosine distance to the concept's text embedding z_c. Unconstrained inversion drifts toward non-audio-like directions, so a manifold regularization term is added: the Mahalanobis distance between the inversion result and an empirical audio distribution estimated from 10k MaxCaps test-set embeddings, with a fixed regularization coefficient γ=1e-4, keeping the recovered concept within plausible audio space. Two optimization strategies are tested: Adam gradient descent (initialized from the sparse code of the nearest audio neighbor to z_c, to avoid converging to a bad local solution), and FISTA (fast iterative shrinkage-thresholding algorithm), which exploits the decoder's linearity to run a single inversion in roughly 20ms on CPU (versus roughly 20 seconds without the regularization term). A final IDF-weighting post-processing step down-weights overly generic, frequently active neurons using log(N/(number of samples in which that neuron activates + ε)) as a weight, with the top weighted-activation coordinates forming the final concept support.

限制Limitations

作者自陳的限制集中在方法對上游元件的依賴：整套方法的效果被底層的文字-音樂嵌入空間品質與 SAE 重建保真度上限卡住，如果一個概念在嵌入空間裡本身就對齊得很弱、很罕見、很抽象，或者在兩個模態間的表徵很分散，反演就可能失敗；SAE 本身固有的重建誤差、特徵分裂、特徵吸收問題,也會一路傳導、疊加到概念歸因的結果上；方法對稀疏度與流形正則化的超參數選擇有一定敏感度,雖然論文說實務上相對穩定；更根本的是,直接套用在稠密嵌入上的 SAE 本質上還是一種「準線性」的操控介面,能做的編輯精細度有其上限。論文自己也承認,在某些概念、支持集大小與編輯強度的組合下仍然存在效果不好的區域，可能來自概念本身的歧義、嵌入空間品質不足，或 SAE 重建保真度不夠這三個原因中的一個或多個，但論文並未進一步拆解是哪一個在主導。此外，評估侷限在 20 個高頻標籤,對稀有或全新概念的表現如何仍是未知；也沒有跟其他近期多模態概念歸因方法做比較；流形正則化的具體設計選擇看起來偏經驗性,對這個選擇的敏感度分析也不完整。

The authors' own stated limitations center on dependency on upstream components: the method's effectiveness is bounded by the quality of the underlying text-music embedding space and the fidelity of the SAE reconstruction — if a concept is weakly grounded, rare, abstract, or diffusely represented across modalities, inversion may fail; inherent SAE issues (reconstruction error, feature splitting, feature absorption) propagate directly into concept-recovery failures; the method shows some sensitivity to sparsity and manifold-regularization hyperparameters, though the paper reports it is reasonably stable in practice; and more fundamentally, SAEs applied directly to dense embeddings remain a quasi-linear control interface, which caps how fine-grained an edit can be. The paper also acknowledges that poor-performing regions still exist for some combinations of concept, support size, and edit strength, attributing this to some mix of concept ambiguity, embedding-space quality, or SAE reconstruction fidelity, without fully disentangling which factor dominates. Beyond that, evaluation is restricted to 20 high-frequency tags, leaving behavior on rare or novel concepts unclear; there is no comparison against other recent multimodal concept-attribution methods; and the specific design choice behind the manifold regularization appears empirically motivated, without a full sensitivity analysis of that choice.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最重要的貢獻與其說是一個新方法,不如說是一個重新框架問題的視角：概念控制的可靠性,關鍵不在於找到「個別文字對齊的神經元」，而在於恢復「一個概念在目標模態裡真正對應的稀疏支持集」——這句話直接點出過去做多模態可解釋性時常見的一個盲點：把文字空間的幾何結構當成音訊空間的代理，而兩者未必一致。實驗結果支持這個框架的價值：在概念歸因分析中，反演方法在不同稀疏度下的支持集召回率比餘弦探測基線高出 10% 到 70%，用限制在選中神經元上的分類器做 AUROC 評估時，反演方法達到 65% 到 80%，而基線只有大約 50%（等同隨機）；在可操控檢索實驗中，反演方法在「編輯強度」與「保留度」之間取得的權衡曲線系統性地優於 DTN 基線——在相同的偏離程度下能取得更大的目標概念變化，而且反演方法產生的編輯結果在馬氏距離意義上明顯更貼近真實音訊流形，DTN 基線則會把嵌入拉向文字流形而非音訊流形，這正好印證了論文開頭診斷的問題。更值得注意的是這套方法完全是推論時（inference-time）操作，不需要成對音訊-文字監督也不需要重新訓練 SAE，讓它可以直接接上既有系統，實現「使用者即時做概念級編輯來精修檢索結果，不用重新訓練嵌入模型或提供新範例」這種互動式音樂檢索場景。作者也誠實地把這個方法定位成一個起點而非終點：未來想做更平滑、更像滑桿一樣精細的編輯，需要把稀疏分解跟更具表現力或個體自適應的檢索機制結合起來——這代表目前的操控粒度仍然停留在「支持集開關」層級，還沒到真正連續可調的程度。

This paper's most important contribution is less a new method than a reframing of the problem: reliable concept control depends less on finding individually text-aligned neurons than on recovering the sparse support that actually instantiates a concept in the target modality — directly naming a common blind spot in multimodal interpretability work, where text-space geometry gets treated as a proxy for audio-space geometry when the two need not agree. The results back up this framing: in the concept-attribution analysis, inversion achieves 10–70% better bundle recall than the cosine-probing baseline across sparsity levels, and a classifier restricted to the selected neurons reaches 65–80% AUROC under inversion versus roughly 50% (chance level) under the baseline; in the steerable-retrieval experiments, inversion achieves a systematically better edit-strength-versus-preservation tradeoff curve than DTN — larger target-concept gains at matched levels of off-target drift — and inversion-edited embeddings stay measurably closer to the true audio manifold in Mahalanobis-distance terms, while the DTN baseline pulls embeddings toward the text manifold instead, directly confirming the problem diagnosed at the outset. Equally notable is that the whole method is inference-time only, requiring neither paired audio-text supervision nor SAE retraining, meaning it can be dropped into existing systems to enable interactive music retrieval where users make real-time, concept-level edits to refine results without retraining the embedding model or supplying new examples. The authors are candid that this is a starting point rather than an endpoint: achieving smoother, slider-like fine-grained edits will require combining sparse factorization with more expressive or instance-adaptive retrieval mechanisms — meaning the current granularity of control is still closer to a support-set on/off switch than a truly continuous dial.
