Equivariant Music Transformer

等變音樂 Transformer

Zixun Guo, Simon Dixon
arXiv:2608.03920 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-05
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇提出「等變性不會隨著模型變大或訓練變久而自動出現」的實證證據，並且用一個乾淨的自蒸餾式輔助損失函式把等變性直接塞進訓練目標裡，而不是像過去那樣靠資料增強或架構技巧繞路。研究設計成三組對照——不加輔助損失、資料增強、模型層級正則化——讓因果關係看得很清楚。Why this one: It gives concrete evidence that equivariance does not emerge from scale or longer training, then folds it directly into the training objective via a self-distillation auxiliary loss instead of leaning on data augmentation or architectural tricks. The three-way comparison (no auxiliary loss vs. augmentation vs. model-level regularization) isolates the causal claim cleanly.

動機Motivation

人類聽音樂時，一段旋律不管移調多少半音、往前或往後平移多少拍，聽起來都還是「同一個東西」——這是一種等變性（equivariance）：輸入做了某種變換，輸出應該跟著做出可預期的對應變換。symbolic music transformer 理論上該有這個特性，但作者觀察到一個不尋常的現象：目前主流的自迴歸 transformer，只用 next-token cross-entropy 訓練，並不會自動學到這個特性，而且模型越大、訓練越久，反而越不等變——模型把容量拿去記憶絕對位置模式，而不是共享的音樂結構。這篇論文的出發點就是要質疑「等變性會隨著規模湧現」這個常見假設，並且直接在訓練目標裡加入監督訊號，強迫模型學到它。

When a listener hears a melody transposed up a few semitones or shifted a few beats in time, it still registers as "the same" musical idea — a property called equivariance: a transformation of the input should produce a predictable, corresponding transformation of the output. Symbolic music transformers ought to have this property, but the authors report something counter-intuitive: today's standard autoregressive transformers, trained purely with next-token cross-entropy, do not acquire it automatically — and larger models trained longer become *less* equivariant, apparently spending their extra capacity memorizing absolute patterns rather than shared musical structure. The paper's starting point is to question the common assumption that equivariance emerges from scale, and to instead inject it directly into the training objective as an explicit supervisory signal.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

過去讓模型「感知」平移不變性的做法大致三類：一是輸入端做特徵工程（例如用相對音高、相對時值編碼取代絕對值）；二是架構上動手腳，像相對位置編碼（relative position embedding）；三是資料增強，把同一段音樂做隨機移調、平移後都丟進去訓練。這三種做法在音高估計、速度估計、表徵學習等 MIR 任務上都證實有效，但作者指出一個共同盲點：不管輸入端或架構端做了什麼調整，幾乎所有生成模型的訓練目標最後都還是標準的 next-token cross-entropy，從來沒有人在「模型層級」明確要求等變性作為訓練目標的一部分。這篇論文接的正是這個空白：把等變性從「盼望架構設計帶來的副產物」提升成「顯式的訓練目標」。

Prior attempts to give models translation-invariant perception of music fall into three camps: input-level feature engineering (relative rather than absolute pitch/duration encodings), architectural modifications such as relative position embeddings, and data augmentation via random transposition and time-shifting during training. Each has shown benefits for MIR tasks like pitch estimation, tempo estimation, and representation learning. But the authors point to a shared blind spot: regardless of what happens at the input or architecture level, nearly every generative model's training objective still defaults to plain next-token cross-entropy — no one has explicitly supervised equivariance as a model-level training objective. This paper fills exactly that gap, promoting equivariance from a hoped-for side effect of architecture choices to an explicit training target.

方法Method

EMT 用雙分支架構同時優化兩個目標。主分支就是標準自迴歸 next-token 預測，用 cross-entropy loss。輔助分支做的是「自蒸餾式」等變性正則化：對輸入序列 x 套用一個隨機的音樂變換 T（音高平移、時間平移），丟進模型得到變換後的預測分佈 p^T，再把預測分佈套用反變換 T^-1，拿去跟原始輸入（不做變換）得到的預測分佈 p 做 KL 散度比較——理論上，如果模型真的等變，「先變換輸入再反變換輸出」應該要等於「完全不變換」。這裡的關鍵設計是對原始預測 p 做 stop-gradient，避免模型學到讓兩邊都塌縮成同一個平凡解來騙過損失函式。總損失是兩者加權和，權重 λ 設得很小（0.001），等變損失是輔助角色而非主導。

架構本身建立在 Moonbeam 之上，做了幾個 MIR 特化的修改：改用絕對 onset time 而不是相對 delta，方便變換能均勻套用到輸入輸出兩端；把八度與音高類別合併成一本統一的 pitch 字典，簡化變換操作；用 token delay pattern 取代原本的 GRU 解碼器來捕捉 onset、pitch、duration、instrument、velocity 五個屬性之間的相依關係；embedding 用直接相加而非串接，增加每個屬性的表達力。此外還疊加了兩個既有的等變性技巧作為對照與互補：Fundamental Music Embedding（讓輸入層本身帶有音樂距離感）和 Multi-Dimensional Relative Attention（用相對位置而非絕對位置編碼）。

訓練資料是 LakhMIDI：消融實驗用 LMD-clean 的 7.9 萬條序列（約 8000 萬 token），主觀聆聽評測用完整 LMD。模型不大，12 層、hidden size 768，約 9600 萬參數，兩張 A100/H100 分散式訓練。變換範圍是 ±12 半音、0 到 5 秒（0.25 秒為單位），測試時故意用更大範圍（±24 半音、0 到 10 秒）做分佈外評估。

EMT jointly optimizes two branches. The main branch is a standard autoregressive next-token predictor trained with cross-entropy loss. The auxiliary branch implements equivariance as a self-distillation regularizer: a random musical transformation T (pitch shift, time shift) is applied to the input sequence x, the model's prediction on the transformed input p^T is computed, an inverse transform T^-1 is applied to that prediction, and the result is compared via KL divergence against the model's prediction on the untransformed input p. In a perfectly equivariant model, "transform the input then invert the output" should match "never transforming it at all." A stop-gradient on the untransformed anchor prediction prevents the trivial collapse where both branches converge to the same degenerate output just to cheaply minimize the loss. The total loss is a weighted sum with a small λ=0.001, keeping the equivariance term auxiliary rather than dominant.

The backbone builds on Moonbeam with several MIR-specific modifications: absolute onset times instead of relative deltas so transformations apply uniformly across input and output; a unified pitch dictionary merging octave and pitch-class tokens to simplify transformation; a token delay pattern replacing the original GRU decoder to capture dependencies among onset, pitch, duration, instrument, and velocity; and additive rather than concatenative embeddings for greater per-attribute expressiveness. Two existing equivariance techniques are layered on as complementary feature-level engineering: Fundamental Music Embedding (a music-distance-aware input layer) and Multi-Dimensional Relative Attention (relative rather than absolute positional encoding).

Training uses the LakhMIDI dataset: ablations use LMD-clean (79k sequences, ~80M tokens), and the subjective listening study uses the full LMD. The model is modest — 12 layers, hidden size 768, ~96M parameters, trained on 2 A100/H100 GPUs. Transformations span ±12 semitones and 0-5 seconds (0.25s steps) during training, with deliberately wider out-of-distribution ranges (±24 semitones, 0-10s) at test time.

限制Limitations

論文本身很誠實地把「等變性不會隨規模湧現」講成一個負面結果，但這個結論的證據鏈其實依賴特定的架構家族（Anticipatory Music Transformer、MIDI-LLM）和特定資料集（LakhMIDI），換一種完全不同的 tokenization 或訓練語料，趨勢是否一樣穩健並未驗證。其次，λ=0.001 是憑經驗選出來的單一數值，論文沒有做完整的敏感度分析說明這個超參數在不同資料規模或模型大小下要怎麼調，若 λ 選得不好，等變損失可能反過來傷害生成品質。第三，主觀聆聽測試只找了 12 位受試者評估，樣本數偏小，雖然用 Wilcoxon 檢定確認顯著性，但外部效度有限。最後，變換只涵蓋音高平移與時間平移這兩種最基本的音樂對稱性，速度伸縮（tempo rubato）、調式轉換等其他音樂上有意義的變換完全沒有討論，等變性框架能否推廣到這些變換也是未知數。

The paper is admirably honest in framing "equivariance does not emerge from scale" as a negative result, but the evidence chain rests on a specific architecture family (Anticipatory Music Transformer, MIDI-LLM) and a specific corpus (LakhMIDI); whether the trend holds under a different tokenization scheme or training corpus is untested. Second, λ=0.001 is a single empirically chosen value without a full sensitivity analysis across model or dataset scale — a poorly chosen λ could let the equivariance term actively hurt generation quality. Third, the subjective listening study used only 12 participants; while a Wilcoxon test confirms statistical significance, the external validity of such a small panel is limited. Finally, the transformations studied are limited to the two most basic musical symmetries — pitch shift and time shift — leaving tempo rubato, mode changes, and other musically meaningful transformations entirely unaddressed, so it remains open whether the equivariance framework generalizes to them.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有意思的地方不是「加了一個新 loss 讓模型變好」這種常見套路，而是它先做了一個扎實的診斷實驗，證明業界預設「規模夠大自然會學到好的歸納偏誤」這個信念在等變性這件事上是錯的——甚至是反過來的。這對整個 MIR 領域的啟示可能比 EMT 本身的架構更重要：如果連「移調不變性」這種音樂裡最基本、最容易驗證的對稱性都不會隨規模自然浮現，那麼其他更複雜的音樂結構性質（比如曲式的層級性、和聲功能的等價關係）恐怕更不會白白出現，勢必要靠明確的訓練目標或架構偏誤去誘導。另外，EMT 用比 Anticipatory 模型小得多的參數量就達到相近的生成品質，同時等變性大幅提升，這說明「顯式正則化」有時候比「單純擴大模型規模」更划算——對計算資源有限的研究者是個實用的訊息。比較可惜的是論文沒有討論等變損失對訓練時間、記憶體開銷的實際代價，這在評估「值不值得採用」時是缺一角的資訊。

The paper's most interesting contribution isn't "add a new loss and things get better" — it's the diagnostic work up front, which shows that the field's implicit assumption, that sufficient scale naturally yields good inductive biases, is wrong, even inverted, for equivariance. That may matter more for MIR broadly than EMT's architecture itself: if even the most basic, easily verifiable musical symmetry (transposition invariance) doesn't emerge from scale alone, more complex structural properties of music (hierarchical form, functional harmonic equivalence) are unlikely to emerge for free either, and will likely need explicit training objectives or architectural biases to induce. It's also notable that EMT matches Anticipatory-scale generation quality with far fewer parameters while gaining substantially in equivariance — suggesting explicit regularization can sometimes be a better bargain than simply scaling up, a practically useful signal for compute-constrained researchers. One gap: the paper doesn't discuss the actual training-time or memory overhead of the auxiliary equivariance branch, which matters for judging whether the tradeoff is worth adopting.

How Much AI Is in This Track? Quantifying the Proportion of AI-Generated Stems in Hybrid Music Mixtures

這首歌裡有多少 AI 成分？量化混音中 AI 生成音軌的比例

Fernando Garcia de la Cruz, David López-Ayala, Pablo Zinemanas, Emilio Molina, Martín Rocamora
arXiv:2608.07285 · eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：過去 AI 音樂偵測幾乎都假設「這首歌不是純 AI 就是純人類」的二分法，但實務上混音才是常態——鼓是 AI 生成、人聲是真人錄的。這篇把偵測問題重新定義成「AI 能量佔比是多少」的迴歸問題，而不是分類問題，並且順手發現原本的二元分類器拿來當佔比估計器用其實嚴重失準，是少見把 evaluation methodology 本身講清楚、講誠實的論文。Why this one: Prior AI-music detectors almost universally assume a track is either fully AI or fully human, but real-world production is hybrid — AI drums under a human vocal is the norm, not the exception. This paper reframes detection as a regression problem (what fraction of the acoustic energy is AI-generated) rather than binary classification, and shows along the way that reusing an existing binary classifier as an implicit ratio estimator is badly miscalibrated — an unusually candid piece of evaluation methodology work.

動機Motivation

業界報告指出，平台上每天上傳的音樂有超過一半含有 AI 生成成分，但同一份調查也顯示 97% 的聽眾根本聽不出來哪些是 AI 生成的。歐盟 AI 法案第 50 條這類法規開始要求 AI 生成內容要能被標示，這把「偵測」從學術問題變成合規需求。問題是，目前的偵測系統幾乎都是在「純 AI vs. 純人類」這種乾淨的二分類情境下訓練跟評估，現實中的混音場景——AI 鼓組搭配真人主唱、AI 貝斯配上真人吉他——完全沒被觸及過。作者的核心觀察是：如果一首歌 49% 的能量來自 AI 生成的鼓組，把它硬塞進「AI/人類」二元標籤本身就是概念上的錯誤，真正該問的問題是「這首歌裡有多少比例是 AI 做的」，這需要一個連續的迴歸目標，而不是離散分類。

Industry reports suggest more than half of daily music uploads on some platforms already contain AI-generated content, while the same reports find that 97% of listeners cannot tell which tracks these are. Regulation such as Article 50 of the EU AI Act is starting to require AI-generated content to be disclosed, turning detection from an academic curiosity into a compliance requirement. The trouble is that virtually all existing detectors are trained and evaluated on a clean binary setting — fully AI versus fully human — and never touch the realistic hybrid case where AI-generated drums sit under a human vocal, or an AI bassline under a human guitar part. The authors' core observation is that forcing a track whose energy is 49% AI-generated drums into an "AI or human" binary label is a category error to begin with; the right question is what fraction of the track is AI-generated, which calls for a continuous regression target rather than a discrete class.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

現有的二元 AI 音樂偵測器靠的是類神經音訊 codec 解碼時，transposed convolution 層會留下特定的頻譜偽影（spectral artifact），偵測器就是在抓這個偽影的痕跡，在純 AI 或純人類的音軌上可以做到超過 99% 準確率。但這類偵測器已知有兩個弱點：一是有很強的平台偏誤（不同 generator 留下的偽影不一樣，換一個生成器準確率就崩掉），二是遇到後製處理（壓縮、broadcast 級的音質轉換）準確率會大幅下降。作者也指出 deepfake 偵測這個鄰近領域近年已經從「這張圖是不是假的」二分類，轉向「圖裡哪個區域是被竄改的」像素級定位，但音樂偵測領域完全沒有類似的演進——沒有任何先前工作研究過 AI 生成內容以不同比例混入人類創作內容時要怎麼偵測。這篇論文要補的正是這塊空白，某種意義上是把 deepfake 領域「從偵測到定位」的思路搬到音樂上，只是定位的維度從空間換成了「能量佔比」。

Existing binary AI-music detectors exploit spectral artifacts left by transposed-convolution layers during neural audio codec decoding, achieving over 99% accuracy on purely-AI or purely-human tracks. But such detectors have two known weaknesses: strong platform-specific bias (artifacts differ across generators, and accuracy collapses when the generator changes), and steep degradation under post-processing such as compression or broadcast-quality transcoding. The authors also note that the adjacent field of deepfake detection has already moved from binary "is this image fake" classification toward pixel-level localization of which regions were manipulated — but music detection has seen no equivalent evolution. No prior work has studied detection when AI-generated content is mixed with human-created content at varying proportions. This paper fills exactly that gap, in effect porting the deepfake field's "from detection to localization" shift into music, except the localization axis becomes an energy proportion rather than a spatial region.

方法Method

核心度量是「AI 能量佔比」α，定義成所有 AI 生成音軌（stem）的均方根能量平方和，除以整首混音裡所有音軌的均方根能量平方和——用能量而不是單純算「有幾軌是 AI 生成的」，是因為一個很輕的 AI 合成弦樂鋪底，跟一個很吵的 AI 鼓組，對整首歌可辨識度的貢獻天差地遠，用能量加權比較符合實際聽感上「AI 成分佔多少」的直覺。

資料建構分三步：先用 MoisesDB 這個含 2 到 10 軌的 240 首多軌資料集，取出每一軌原始的人聲音軌；每一軌都額外經過 EnCodec（3kbps）編碼再解碼一次，刻意把 codec 偽影「烙印」進去，模擬 AI 生成的頻譜特徵，同時保留原本的音樂內容——這一步的用意是把偵測任務限定在「架構偽影能不能被抓到」，排除掉「AI 生成的音樂內容本身聽起來就比較假」這個干擾因子。接著對每首歌的 n 軌音源，窮舉所有 2^n 種「這一軌是真的還是編碼過的」組合，依照牽涉到幾軌分層，確保 α 在整個 [0,1] 區間都有密集的取樣點，總共產生 21,212 個混音樣本。

比較的模型有兩個：一個是複現既有論文的二元 CNN 分類器（在純真/純 AI 音軌上有 99.97% 準確率），另一個是用完全相同的 CNN 骨幹、但改成直接對 α 做迴歸的 regressor。兩者都在 5 秒窗口上評估，也測試了 1 秒窗口的效果差異。

The core metric is the AI energy ratio α: the sum of squared RMS energy across all AI-processed stems, divided by the summed squared RMS energy of every stem in the mix. Using energy rather than a simple stem count matters because a quiet AI-synthesized string pad and a loud AI drum kit contribute very differently to how "AI" a track sounds, and energy-weighting matches that intuition better than treating every stem as equally important.

Data construction proceeds in three steps. Starting from MoisesDB (240 tracks, 2-10 stems each), every stem is additionally round-tripped through EnCodec (3 kbps) encode-decode to imprint codec artifacts that mimic AI-generation signatures while preserving the underlying musical content — this isolates the detection task to "can the architectural artifact be found," removing the confound of "AI-generated content simply sounding different." For each track's n stems, all 2^n real/AI combinations are enumerated and organized by how many stems are affected, ensuring dense coverage of α across the full [0,1] range — 21,212 mixtures in total. Two models are compared: a binary CNN classifier reproduced from prior work (99.97% accuracy on pure tracks) and a regressor with an identical backbone trained directly to predict α, both evaluated at 5-second windows, with 1-second windows tested as a contrast.

限制Limitations

論文自己講得很清楚：整個實驗只用了 EnCodec 一種 codec、固定在 3kbps 這個位元率，現實中的商業生成器（Suno、Udio）用的架構、位元率完全不同，偽影特徵不一定相同，結論能不能直接搬過去用是未知數。混音樣本也完全沒有做專業後製（等化、壓縮），跟真實發行的音樂在音質上有落差，作者自己承認這是「方法學上的可控基礎，而不是可以直接部署的偵測器」。另外，α 的定義需要拿到每一軌的原始 stem 才算得出來，但實務上拿到的往往只是已經混好的成品，沒有 stem-level 的存取權限，這代表這個方法在真實場景下的可操作性其實有限，需要額外的音源分離步驟才能用，而分離本身也會引入誤差。最後，不同樂器的可偵測性差異很大（鼓、吉他容易分辨，人聲、貝斯幾乎分不出來），這代表 α 的估計精確度其實會隨著混音的樂器組成系統性地偏移，論文雖然指出這點但沒有進一步提出校正方案。

The paper is upfront about its limits: the entire study uses only EnCodec at a fixed 3 kbps, while commercial generators (Suno, Udio) use different architectures and bitrates with potentially different artifact signatures, so it's unclear how well the findings transfer. The mixtures also have no professional post-processing (EQ, compression), leaving a gap versus real released music — the authors themselves call this "a controlled methodological foundation rather than a deployment-ready detector." Computing α also requires access to individual original stems, but real-world deployment usually only has the finished mix, meaning the method needs an additional source-separation step to be usable in practice, which would itself introduce error. Finally, detectability varies sharply by instrument (drums and guitar separate cleanly; vocals and bass barely separate at all), meaning α estimates will be systematically biased depending on a mix's instrumentation — a point the paper flags but does not propose a correction for.

討論Discussion

這篇論文的價值不完全在偵測準確率本身，而在於它把一個常被簡化成「二選一」的問題重新框架成連續量測，這個框架轉換其實對整個 AI 內容治理的討論有啟發性——不只是音樂，任何「混合創作」的媒介（共同寫作的文字、人機協作的圖像）都可能面臨同樣的問題：與其問「這是不是 AI 做的」，更誠實的問法或許是「這裡面有多少是 AI 做的」。論文裡最讓人意外的發現是，原本號稱 99.97% 準確率的二元分類器，拿來當作 α 的隱式估計器時表現比瞎猜（用平均值當預測）還差（R²=-0.85）——這給整個領域一個警訊：一個在自己訓練分佈（純 AI/純人類）上表現極好的分類器，套用到分佈外的中間地帶時，校準品質可能完全不能信任，論文用具體數字把這個常被忽略的問題攤開來看，是很紮實的貢獻。比較意外的是作者對「樂器可分性差異」只當成限制帶過，其實這個發現本身滿有意思的——它暗示未來的偵測器設計應該要針對不同樂器分開建模，而不是用單一模型硬套所有音軌，這方向論文自己點出來了卻沒有再往下推。

This paper's value isn't primarily about detection accuracy — it's the reframing of a problem usually flattened into a binary choice as a continuous measurement, and that reframing has implications beyond music: any hybrid-creation medium (human-AI co-written text, human-AI collaborative images) faces the same question, where "how much of this is AI" may be the more honest question than "is this AI." The most striking finding is that the celebrated 99.97%-accurate binary classifier, when repurposed as an implicit α estimator, performs worse than simply predicting the mean (R²=-0.85) — a cautionary result for the field: a classifier that excels on its own training distribution (pure AI/pure human) may be completely uncalibrated once pushed into the out-of-distribution middle ground, and this paper puts concrete numbers behind a problem that's often glossed over. It's a bit surprising the authors treat the instrument-dependent separability gap as merely a limitation rather than pursuing it further — the finding itself suggests future detectors should be modeled per-instrument rather than applying one model uniformly across all stems, a direction the paper identifies but doesn't chase.

Rhythm Guitar Tablature Continuation Through Picking Pattern Generation

透過彈奏型態生成延續節奏吉他指法譜

Alexandre D'Hooge, Ken Déguernel, Louis Bigo
DOI:10.5334/tismir.368 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-08-10
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：大多數符號音樂生成論文一股腦把「和弦圖」跟「彈奏節奏」綁在同一個生成步驟裡，這篇反其道而行，先把兩者拆開——和弦位置的選擇跟彈奏型態（picking pattern）的生成分開處理——讓每個子問題都可以獨立進步，而且用 54 位吉他手做的使用者研究老實揭露了一個尷尬的事實：量化指標判定比較好的模型，人類反而覺得比較不好用。Why this one: Most symbolic-guitar generation work bundles chord-diagram choice and rhythmic playing pattern into a single generative step; this paper deliberately separates them — chord-position selection from picking-pattern generation — so each subproblem can improve independently, and a 54-guitarist user study honestly surfaces an uncomfortable finding: the model that scores better on quantitative metrics is the one guitarists find less usable.

動機Motivation

節奏吉他的譜寫，不只是決定「彈哪個和弦」，還要決定「怎麼彈」——用什麼樣的節奏型態去撥弦、哪幾條弦要一起響、哪幾條弦要跳過。市面上的 Guitar Pro 之類的譜曲軟體要靠人工一小節一小節慢慢複製貼上調整，效率很低。過去嘗試用 AI 輔助生成完整 tablature 的作法，通常是把「彈哪個和弦」跟「怎麼彈」這兩件事綁在同一個生成步驟裡直接輸出 fret/string 的組合，但這樣做等於把兩個性質完全不同的子問題疊在一起硬 train 一個模型——選和弦位置比較像離散的樂理決策，彈奏型態則更接近節奏與觸弦質地的風格選擇。這篇論文的出發點就是把兩者拆開：先確定和弦的把位圖，再獨立生成「picking pattern」——一種只描述「哪幾條弦在什麼時間點被撥動」、完全不涉及品格資訊的中介表示法，讓兩個子任務都可以各自被改進、被評估。

Composing rhythm guitar parts isn't just choosing which chord to play — it's also deciding how to play it: what rhythmic pattern to strum with, which strings sound together, which get skipped. Notation software like Guitar Pro currently makes composers copy-paste and adjust bar by bar, which is slow. Prior AI-assisted tablature generation typically bundles "which chord" and "how to play it" into one generative step that outputs fret/string combinations directly — forcing a single model to jointly learn two qualitatively different subproblems, since chord-position selection is closer to a discrete harmonic decision while picking pattern is more a stylistic choice about rhythm and texture. This paper's starting point is to split the two apart: fix the chord diagram first, then independently generate the "picking pattern" — an intermediate representation describing only which strings are struck at which moments, entirely decoupled from fret information — so each subtask can be improved and evaluated on its own terms.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

過去的符號吉他生成研究大多直接對完整的 tablature（同時包含弦、品、時間資訊）做序列生成，這篇論文引用的相關工作顯示，這類端對端做法在生成多樣性與可彈奏性之間常常顧此失彼。作者選擇的切入點，是先定義一套「texture feature」來描述彈奏型態的統計特性：橫向特徵包括每拍平均幾個 onset、onset 間隔的變異程度；縱向特徵包括每次撥弦同時響幾條弦（thickness）、涉及弦號的變異程度、弦的重心位置。這套特徵不只用來當生成時的條件輸入，也直接拿來當評估指標——這種「用同一套特徵語言同時做 conditioning 跟 evaluation」的設計在符號音樂生成論文裡不算常見，好處是評估的維度直接對應到模型被要求優化的維度，不會有評估標準跟訓練目標脫節的問題。訓練資料用 DadaGP，這是一個含超過 2.6 萬個 Guitar Pro 檔案的資料庫，涵蓋各種流行音樂的吉他編曲，是目前吉他 tablature 生成領域少數規模夠大的公開資料集。

Prior work on symbolic guitar generation mostly generates complete tablature (string, fret, and timing jointly) as a single sequence. The related work cited here suggests such end-to-end approaches often trade off diversity against playability. This paper's angle is to first define a set of "texture features" describing the statistical properties of a picking pattern: horizontal features (average onsets per beat, variation in inter-onset interval) and vertical features (thickness — how many strings sound per onset — string-number variation, and string centroid position). These features serve double duty as both generation conditioning and evaluation metrics — a design choice not common in symbolic music generation, whose benefit is that the evaluation axes directly match what the model is asked to optimize, avoiding a mismatch between training objective and evaluation criteria. Training data comes from DadaGP, a corpus of over 26,000 Guitar Pro files spanning popular-music guitar arrangements, one of the few sufficiently large public datasets for guitar tablature generation.

方法Method

系統分成兩個互補的模型。第一個是規則式（rule-based）模型，單純模仿吉他手在編曲軟體裡複製貼上的工作流程：根據和弦圖做弦的對應規則，優先保留貝斯音（低音弦），並且複製既有的琶音型態；當前後兩個和弦圖用到的弦數不同時，設計了專門的映射函式來處理弦數不匹配的狀況。這個模型完全確定性，沒有隨機性，輸出穩定但缺乏變化。

第二個是類 GPT-2 架構的解碼器，參數量只有 84 萬（比一般語言模型小非常多），使用 155 個 token 的詞彙表涵蓋弦、onset、時值與 metadata。條件化的方式是在 attention 之前把 texture control、和弦圖資訊、結構訊號一起餵進去（pre-attention conditioning）。訓練分兩階段：第一階段用全部的 picking pattern（不論有沒有和弦圖標註）做基礎訓練；第二階段只用有明確和弦圖標註的 3 萬 1596 個樣本做精修。這個模型還設計了一個專門的「吉他和弦圖損失函式（guitar chord diagram loss）」，對彈到和弦圖之外、不被允許的弦（out-of-diagram note）的 token 額外懲罰。

資料前處理從 DadaGP 的 290 萬個小節裡篩出 230 萬個判定為節奏吉他的小節，組成 260 萬個四小節序列，去重後剩下 100 萬個獨特 pattern。選四小節為單位，是因為過去研究顯示這個時間尺度剛好能同時捕捉到一致的音樂特徵跟對比性的段落變化。

The system pairs two complementary models. The first is a rule-based model that mimics a guitarist's copy-paste workflow in notation software: it maps strings from chord-diagram rules, prioritizes retaining bass notes on low strings, and replicates existing arpeggio patterns, with dedicated mapping functions handling the case where consecutive chord diagrams use different numbers of strings. This model is fully deterministic — stable output, but no variation.

The second is a GPT-2-style decoder with only 0.84M parameters (far smaller than typical language models), using a 155-token vocabulary covering strings, onsets, durations, and metadata. Conditioning is applied pre-attention, combining texture controls, chord-diagram information, and structural signals. Training happens in two phases: a base phase on all picking patterns regardless of chord-diagram annotation, followed by a refinement phase on only the 31,596 samples with explicit chord-diagram annotations. A custom "guitar chord diagram loss" additionally penalizes tokens that strike strings not permitted by the diagram (out-of-diagram notes).

Preprocessing filters DadaGP's 2.9M bars down to 2.3M bars judged to be rhythm guitar, forming 2.6M four-bar sequences that deduplicate to 1M unique patterns. Four bars was chosen because prior work shows this window captures both consistent musical characteristics and contrastive section changes.

限制Limitations

使用者研究只用了 5 個測試提示（prompt）去產生延續段落讓 54 位吉他手評分，測試場景數量偏少，能不能涵蓋不同曲風、不同難度的節奏型態是存疑的。而且這是非互動式的評測設計——受試者只是聽/看已經生成好的段落打分，跟真實使用情境（邊生成邊調整、反覆嘗試）有落差，論文自己也承認這點。另一個問題出在評估基準本身：參考資料集（DadaGP）裡本來就有 4.1% 的「out-of-diagram note」，包括裝飾音、悶音（dead note）跟單純的轉譜錯誤，這代表用「跟參考資料的差距」當作品質指標時，參考答案本身就不乾淨，量化指標的可信度打了折扣。最後，整個方法目前只在 4/4 拍上驗證過，論文自己列為未來工作要擴展到其他拍號，代表目前的技術泛化性有限。

The user study used only 5 test prompts to generate continuations rated by 54 guitarists — a small number of scenarios, leaving open whether the findings cover the range of genres and pattern difficulties in practice. It's also a non-interactive evaluation design: participants rated pre-generated segments rather than interacting with the system in a real compose-and-adjust loop, a gap the authors acknowledge themselves. A second issue lies in the evaluation baseline: the reference dataset (DadaGP) itself contains 4.1% out-of-diagram notes — ornaments, dead notes for muting, and outright transcription errors — meaning the "ground truth" used to compute distance-based metrics is itself noisy, undercutting the reliability of the quantitative scores. Finally, the whole method has only been validated in 4/4 time; the authors list extending to other time signatures as future work, meaning current generalizability is limited.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最值得注意的地方，不是提出了哪個新穎的模型架構（用的其實是很小、很傳統的 GPT-2 解碼器），而是評估設計本身帶出的張力：量化指標判定 Transformer 模型比規則式模型更好（edit distance 更低、texture distance 更貼近參考資料），但使用者研究裡，吉他手反而覺得規則式模型的輸出更可彈、更一致、不需要修改就能直接上場演奏，Transformer 只在「有趣程度」這個主觀維度上勝出。論文自己點出癥結：時值 token 的些微差異就能大幅改變彈奏的律動感，而弦的選擇差異一旦造成「彈到和弦圖外的弦」，會直接衝擊可彈奏性——但這些對彈奏體驗至關重要的細節，edit distance 這種逐 token 比對的指標根本測不出來。這對整個符號音樂生成領域是個提醒：光靠自動化指標去斷定「哪個模型比較好」可能得到跟實際使用體驗完全相反的結論，尤其是牽涉到「這東西人類要不要拿去用」的創作輔助工具，使用者研究不是錦上添花，而是必要的驗證手段。可惜的是論文沒有進一步嘗試「規則式生成 + Transformer 局部修飾」這種混合式方案，兩個模型目前是平行對照，而不是互補使用，這其實是很自然的下一步。

This paper's most notable contribution isn't a novel model architecture — the generative model is in fact a small, conventional GPT-2 decoder — but the tension its evaluation design surfaces: quantitative metrics judge the Transformer better (lower edit distance, closer texture-distance alignment to reference data), yet in the user study, guitarists found the rule-based model's output more playable and consistent, usable for performance without editing, while the Transformer won only on the subjective "interest" dimension. The paper pinpoints why: small differences in duration tokens can substantially change a pattern's groove, and string-choice differences that produce out-of-diagram notes directly hurt playability — details that token-level edit distance simply cannot capture. That's a broader caution for symbolic music generation: automated metrics alone can rank models in the opposite order from how they'll actually be experienced, and for creative tools whose whole point is whether a human will actually use the output, user studies aren't a nice-to-have but a necessary check. It's a shame the paper doesn't explore a hybrid — rule-based generation refined locally by the Transformer — since the two models are currently treated as parallel alternatives rather than complementary; that seems like a natural next step.
