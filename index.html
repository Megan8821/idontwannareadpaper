From Local Kernels to Global Form: Modeling the Emergence of Musical Content

從局部核到整體形式：為音樂內容的浮現建模

Francesco Vitucci, Michele Lorusso, Francesco Scagliola
arXiv:2608.24660 · cs.SD, cs.CL · 投稿submitted 2026-08-25
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文放寬馬可夫鏈的時間齊性假設，用重疊滑動視窗直接從單一符號序列估計局部轉移核，藉此偵測樂曲形式邊界，而不引入外部劃分或提高模型階數；在 Debussy《Syrinx》（273 個音符事件）的個案研究裡，視窗長度 L=6 時，音高與時值兩個維度都在兩個已知的結構邊界（第 9、26 小節）達到理論最大 Jensen-Shannon 距離 0.447，但音高維度有 78.7%（210/267）的比較落在這個高原，時值只有 24.0%（64/267），作者自己也強調這個重合只是「與邊界敏感度一致」而非「強力驗證」，因為理論最大值本身就是視窗幾何決定的，不構成自動分段演算法。Why this one: The paper relaxes the time-homogeneity assumption of Markov chains, estimating local transition kernels directly from a single symbolic sequence via overlapping sliding windows to detect formal boundaries in a piece, without exogenous partitions or higher-order models; in a case study on Debussy's Syrinx (273 note events), at window length L=6 both pitch and duration dimensions reach the theoretical maximum Jensen-Shannon distance of 0.447 at the two known structural boundaries (measures 9 and 26), though pitch plateaus at that maximum for 78.7% (210/267) of comparisons versus only 24.0% (64/267) for duration; the authors themselves caution that this coincidence is "consistent with boundary sensitivity" rather than "strong validation," since the theoretical maximum is itself set by window geometry and does not constitute an automatic segmentation algorithm.

動機Motivation

標準馬可夫模型假設轉移機率在整首曲子裡固定不變（時間齊性），但作者認為這個假設「恰好丟掉了讓一段序列讀起來像形式的時間脈絡」——音樂事件不是孤立符號，它們透過在時間結構裡的位置、重複、期待與解決才產生意義。與其像高階馬可夫模型那樣增加記憶深度，作者選擇保留一階馬可夫性質、但放寬時間齊性假設，直接從資料本身（而非外部強加的劃分）推導出隨時間變化的局部轉移核。

Standard Markov models assume transition probabilities stay fixed throughout a piece (time-homogeneity), but the authors argue this assumption "discards precisely the temporal context that makes a sequence intelligible as form" -- musical events are not isolated symbols, they acquire meaning through their position in a temporal structure, through recurrence, expectation, and resolution. Rather than adding memory depth the way higher-order Markov models do, the authors keep the first-order Markov property but relax time-homogeneity, deriving time-varying local transition kernels directly from the data itself rather than from an externally imposed partition.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者點名幾個具體的先前系統。古典的馬可夫作曲傳統（可追溯到 Ames 在 1950–60 年代的工作）建立了這個領域，但一直假設時間齊性；Pearce 與 Wiggins 的 IDyOM 框架用可變階數模型加深預測能力，骨子裡依然是時間齊性的。另一批「非齊性」模型——Buenger 的樂句劃分法與 Roig 等人的節拍格模型——雖然也放寬了齊性假設，卻是用手動指定的外部劃分（樂句、拍子）來定義非齊性，而不是像這篇論文一樣從資料裡觀察推導出來。

Chawin 與 Rom 的滑動視窗音高類別直方圖也用了視窗化，但那是邊際分布（哪些音高出現），這篇論文用的是有方向性的條件轉移（哪個音高接著哪個音高），資訊量更細；深度學習式的 Transformer 生成模型雖然生成品質更好，卻缺乏這種可解讀、隨時間變化的轉移矩陣。這篇論文與這些方法最大的不同，在於「觀察驅動」而非「外部指定」的非齊性來源。

The authors name several concrete prior systems. Classical Markov-chain composition (tracing back to Ames in the 1950s-60s) established the tradition but always assumed time-homogeneity; Pearce and Wiggins's IDyOM framework adds predictive depth via variable-order models but remains time-homogeneous under the hood. Another line of "non-homogeneous" models -- Buenger's phrase-based segmentation and Roig et al.'s beat-grid model -- also relax homogeneity, but define the non-homogeneity exogenously via hand-specified partitions (phrases, beats), rather than deriving it observationally from the data as this paper does.

Chawin and Rom's sliding-window pitch-class histograms also use windowing, but over marginal distributions (which pitches occur), whereas this paper uses directed conditional transitions (which pitch follows which), a finer-grained signal; Transformer-based deep generative models produce better output quality but lack this kind of interpretable, time-resolved transition matrix. The paper's central departure from all of these is that its source of non-homogeneity is observation-driven rather than externally imposed.

方法Method

方法核心是用重疊滑動視窗估計一系列局部轉移矩陣。給定長度 T 的事件序列與視窗長度 L，可以產生 W = T − L + 1 個重疊視窗，每個視窗涵蓋 L−1 次轉移；視窗內的計數矩陣正規化後就得到該視窗的局部核。若某一列在視窗內完全沒有觀察到轉移，就退回（fallback）到全域核，避免出現未定義的機率。生成用的「時間不齊性鏈」則透過一個排程函式 g(r)，把每個轉移時間點對應到某個局部核估計，也可以選擇性地加入收縮（shrinkage）項，讓局部核往全域核的方向平滑，強度用 λ 控制。

比較連續視窗之間的差異時，作者測試了 Jensen-Shannon、Hellinger、Frobenius 三種距離，並發現前向 KL 散度在音高比較裡有約 84% 的情況會因支撐集改變而變成無限大，因此不適用。由於每次只有一個轉移進、一個轉移出視窗，連續視窗距離有理論上限 1/√(L−1)（L=6 時上限為 0.447）。個案研究選用 Debussy 的獨奏長笛曲《Syrinx》(1913)，常被引用為 A–B–A' 三段式結構的範例；移除 14 個裝飾音、合併 21 個連結音之後保留 273 個音符事件，分別對音高（28 個狀態）與時值（23 個狀態）建模，並用第 9、26 小節作為文獻引用的參考邊界（不是絕對真值）。

The core method estimates a sequence of local transition matrices using overlapping sliding windows. Given a sequence of T events and window length L, this produces W = T - L + 1 overlapping windows, each spanning L-1 transitions; the count matrix within each window is normalized into that window's local kernel. If a row has no observed transitions within a given window, the model falls back to the global kernel to avoid undefined probabilities. The generative "time-inhomogeneous chain" then uses a schedule function g(r) mapping each transition time to a particular local kernel estimate, with an optional shrinkage term that smooths local kernels toward the global kernel at a strength controlled by lambda.

To compare consecutive windows the authors tested Jensen-Shannon, Hellinger, and Frobenius distances, and found forward KL divergence unusable because roughly 84% of pitch comparisons produced infinite values from support changes. Since only one transition enters and one exits per step, the distance between consecutive windows has a theoretical upper bound of 1/sqrt(L-1) (0.447 at L=6). The case study uses Debussy's solo flute piece Syrinx (1913), often cited as an example of tripartite A-B-A' form; after removing 14 grace notes and merging 21 tied notes, 273 note events remain, modeled separately for pitch (28 states) and duration (23 states), with measures 9 and 26 used as reference boundaries drawn from the published literature (not ground truth).

限制Limitations

作者自己列出幾項限制。首先，音高與時值曲線各自的「高原」都很寬，單一維度都無法拿來當成可靠的自動分段器，論文明白寫著「描述性的驗證不等於一套現成的分段演算法」。第二，連續視窗距離的理論上限本來就是視窗幾何決定的，L=6 時兩個參考邊界都恰好落在這個上限上，這件事「本身不構成強力驗證」，只能說與邊界敏感度一致。

第三，個案研究只分析單一樂曲，缺乏跨曲目的通用性，而且文獻上對《Syrinx》三段式的巨觀結構雖有共識，對內部的細部劃分點卻仍有分歧。第四，距離量測比較的是高度重疊的相鄰視窗，這限制了它能不能被解讀成「真正的邊界偵測」，作者建議未來應該改成比較邊界前後的視窗，而且最好不重疊。第五，音高與時值目前是各自獨立取樣、獨立分析，論文假設兩者在給定形式位置下條件獨立，並沒有真的檢驗兩者的聯合一致性（joint pitch-rhythm coherence）。

The authors list several limitations themselves. First, the "plateau" in both the pitch and duration curves is broad, so neither dimension alone can serve as a reliable automatic segmenter; the paper states plainly that "descriptive validation is not equivalent to a turnkey segmentation algorithm." Second, the theoretical upper bound on consecutive-window distance is itself set by window geometry, so both reference boundaries landing exactly on that bound at L=6 "is not, by itself, strong evidence," only consistency with boundary sensitivity.

Third, the case study analyzes only a single piece, lacking cross-corpus generality, and while the literature agrees on Syrinx's tripartite macroform it disagrees on the finer internal divisions. Fourth, the distance measure compares strongly overlapping adjacent windows, which limits whether it can be read as genuine boundary detection; the authors suggest future work should instead compare boundary-preceding and boundary-following windows, preferably non-overlapping ones. Fifth, pitch and duration are currently sampled and analyzed independently, with the paper assuming conditional independence given formal position rather than actually testing their joint pitch-rhythm coherence.

討論Discussion

論文用「五百次重新取樣」的複製實驗，量化局部核學到的分布跟原始樂曲差多少。當 L=2 時，重新取樣結果與原曲幾乎完全相同（狀態不匹配率為 0），這是視窗太短導致的退化現象；L=6 時，音高的狀態不匹配率上升到 0.678、時值為 0.344，全域 JS 散度分別是 0.167 與 0.129；L=20 時不匹配率進一步上升到音高 0.839、時值 0.588，全域 JS 散度分別是 0.303 與 0.222。加入收縮項（λ=1）可以消除 L=2 的退化複製現象，但代價是讓分段 JS 散度大幅上升——音高從 0.196 升到 0.618、時值從 0.177 升到 0.476。

作者的結論相當克制：他們強調的是「可解讀性優於自動化」，局部核可以被作曲家檢視、編輯、重新排程，但「形式控制沒辦法只從單一鋼琴捲軸或全域分布相似度推斷出來」。整體而言，這篇論文的貢獻是方法論式的——觀察驅動的視窗化、軌跡視覺化、多尺度分析能讓「被單一全域估計器掩蓋的形式變化」浮現出來，但作者自己反覆強調這不等於自動分段，也沒有展示跨語料庫的通用性。

The paper uses a resampling experiment (500 draws) to quantify how far the distribution learned by the local kernels departs from the original piece. At L=2 the resampled output is nearly identical to the original (state mismatch rate 0), a degenerate copying effect caused by the window being too short; at L=6 the pitch state-mismatch rate rises to 0.678 and duration to 0.344, with global JS divergence of 0.167 and 0.129 respectively; at L=20 the mismatch rate rises further to 0.839 for pitch and 0.588 for duration, with global JS divergence of 0.303 and 0.222. Adding a shrinkage term (lambda=1) removes the degenerate copying at L=2, but at the cost of a large jump in sectional JS divergence -- pitch rises from 0.196 to 0.618, duration from 0.177 to 0.476.

The authors' conclusion is notably restrained: they emphasize "interpretability over automation" -- local kernels can be inspected, edited, and rescheduled by composers, but "formal control cannot be inferred from a single piano roll or from global distributional similarity." Overall, the paper's contribution is methodological -- observation-driven windowing, trajectory visualization, and multiscale analysis surface "formal change obscured by a single global estimator" -- but the authors repeatedly stress this does not amount to automatic segmentation, and no cross-corpus generality is demonstrated.

Low-Latency Spell Correction for Japanese Music Search Queries

面向日文音樂搜尋查詢的低延遲拼寫校正

Anshul Garg, Pavni Tandon, Karan Bhukar, Tanmay Khandelwal, Ujjal Kumar Dutta
arXiv:2609.04262 · cs.SD, cs.LG, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-09-02
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文為日文音樂搜尋設計了一個只有 6 層（3 層編碼器＋3 層解碼器）的緊湊 BART 序列到序列模型，靠「腳本感知」的合成錯字生成管線（鍵盤鄰接、假名輸入相鄰鍵、濁音半濁音交替、大小假名交替、從真實查詢記錄挖出的語音混淆規則）加上把混合腳本標題正規化成單一腳本這一步，一次處理平假名、片假名、漢字、羅馬字四種文字系統；在 1,618 筆真實查詢的評測集上，字元錯誤率（CER）11.62% 是所有對照系統裡最低的，比 SymSpell 的 25.95%、Kato 等人的 Lattice Path Edit Distance 的 31.65% 都低很多，推論延遲小於 4 毫秒，比零樣本 LLM（Claude Haiku，約 800 毫秒）快超過 200 倍；不過完全匹配率（41.09%）反而輸給 LLM 的 48.27%，消融實驗顯示只用隨機雜訊或只用 QWERTY 鍵盤模型都會讓 F1 大幅下降到 9.61% 與 4.48%。Why this one: The paper builds a compact 6-layer (3 encoder + 3 decoder) BART sequence-to-sequence model for Japanese music search, driven by a "script-aware" synthetic misspelling pipeline (keyboard adjacency, kana flick-input adjacency, dakuten/handakuten alternation, small/large kana alternation, and phonetic-confusion rules mined from real query logs) plus a step that normalizes mixed-script titles to a single canonical script, handling hiragana, katakana, kanji, and romaji in one unified model; on a 1,618-query real-world evaluation set it achieves the lowest character error rate (CER) among all systems tested at 11.62%, well below SymSpell's 25.95% and Kato et al.'s Lattice Path Edit Distance at 31.65%, at sub-4ms inference latency -- over 200x faster than a zero-shot LLM (Claude Haiku, ~800ms) -- though its exact-match rate (41.09%) actually trails the LLM's 48.27%, and ablations show that random-noise-only or QWERTY-keyboard-only training data collapses F1 to 9.61% and 4.48% respectively.

動機Motivation

日文查詢的拼寫校正比英文困難，因為使用者可能用平假名、片假名、漢字、羅馬字四種文字系統中的任何一種或混合輸入，而且每種文字系統會產生自己特有的錯誤型態；音樂目錄裡的實體名稱又經常本身就是混合腳本（例如日文歌名夾雜英文團名），使用者搜尋時用的腳本還可能跟目錄裡的正式寫法不一致。作者指出傳統的編輯距離方法沒辦法妥善處理日文特有的語音與腳本轉換錯誤，而生產環境又要求次毫秒等級的延遲，所以需要一個單一模型同時處理四種腳本、又要夠快能上線。

Spell correction for Japanese queries is harder than for English because users may type in hiragana, katakana, kanji, or romaji individually or mixed, and each script produces its own characteristic error patterns; music catalog entity names are themselves frequently mixed-script (e.g. a Japanese song title alongside a Latin-script artist name), and users may search using a different script than the catalog's canonical entry. The authors note that traditional edit-distance methods poorly capture Japanese-specific phonetic and script-conversion errors, while production requirements demand sub-millisecond-scale latency -- motivating a single model that handles all four scripts while staying fast enough to serve online.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者把 SymSpell 這種靠預先計算刪除鄰域的字典式方法當成標準基線，並指出這類方法在日文這種語音複雜的語言上效果會變差。Kato 等人（EMNLP 2023）提出的 Lattice Path Edit Distance 是專門為日文設計的、考慮羅馬化的編輯距離指標，用來從查詢記錄裡挖掘錯字配對，證明了標準編輯距離對日文並不夠用；BART 這種去噪自編碼器則是先前已被用在英文與中文文法錯誤校正上的做法。

這篇論文與這些先前系統最大的差異，在於同時做到「腳本感知的合成資料生成」與「混合腳本正規化」——先前系統要嘛只處理單一腳本，要嘛需要為每種腳本訓練分開的模型；這篇論文則用一個共用的位元組級 BPE 分詞器與正規化步驟，讓同一個模型能學到「純腳本對純腳本」的一致映射，藉此把「同時校正錯字又要轉換腳本」這個更難的問題拆開來處理。

The authors treat SymSpell -- a dictionary-based method relying on precomputed delete-neighborhoods -- as the standard baseline, noting its effectiveness degrades for phonetically complex languages like Japanese. Kato et al. (EMNLP 2023) proposed Lattice Path Edit Distance, a romanization-aware edit-distance metric designed specifically for Japanese to mine misspelling pairs from query logs, demonstrating that standard edit distance is insufficient for Japanese; BART, a denoising-autoencoder architecture, has previously been applied to grammatical error correction in English and Chinese.

This paper's main departure from these prior systems is combining script-aware synthetic data generation with script normalization -- prior systems either handled a single script or needed separate models per script, whereas this paper uses a shared byte-level BPE tokenizer and a normalization step so one model learns a consistent pure-script-to-pure-script mapping, splitting apart the harder problem of simultaneously correcting spelling and converting scripts.

方法Method

模型是一個 6 層（3 編碼器＋3 解碼器）的緊湊 BART，隱藏維度 768、12 個注意力頭、前饋層維度 3072，推論用 beam size 4 的束搜尋；訓練 30,000 步，有效批次大小 32,768，學習率 5×10⁻⁵，BF16 精度。分詞器是在音樂目錄標題上訓練的位元組級 BPE，詞彙量 5 萬，可以不做腳本特定前處理就同時涵蓋四種日文腳本、羅馬字、數字與特殊字元，最長序列 20 個 token 就能涵蓋 99.9% 的音樂查詢。

合成錯字管線結合五種技巧：鍵盤鄰接（羅馬字用 QWERTY 鄰接、假名用手機 flick 輸入鄰接）、從查詢記錄與日文維基百科錯字資料集（JWTD）挖出的語音混淆規則（每個腳本類別保留前 1000 條規則，依經驗頻率加權）、濁音／半濁音交替、大小假名交替、以及一般性的字元刪除／重複／換位／去空格。每個標題套用 1 到 min(5, ⌈0.3×查詢長度⌉) 個錯誤。混合腳本標題會先依「片假名 >> 平假名 >> 羅馬字 >> 漢字」的優先順序正規化成單一腳本再生成錯字，訓練資料共 280 萬則標題、加上約 300 萬則從記錄篩選出的真實查詢—實體配對，並以 1:4 的比例混入「本來就拼對」的恆等配對，教模型學會保留已經正確的查詢。

The model is a compact 6-layer (3 encoder + 3 decoder) BART with 768 hidden dimensions, 12 attention heads, and a 3072-dimensional feedforward network, using beam search of size 4 at inference; training runs 30,000 steps with an effective batch size of 32,768, a learning rate of 5e-5, and BF16 precision. The tokenizer is a byte-level BPE trained on music catalog titles with a 50,000 vocabulary, covering all four Japanese scripts plus romaji, digits, and special characters without script-specific preprocessing; a 20-token max sequence length covers 99.9% of music queries.

The synthetic misspelling pipeline combines five techniques: keyboard-layout errors (QWERTY adjacency for romaji, flick-input adjacency for kana), phonetic-confusion rules mined from query logs and the Japanese Wikipedia Typo Dataset (JWTD, top 1000 rules per script category weighted by empirical frequency), voiced/unvoiced (dakuten/handakuten) alternation, small/large kana alternation, and generic perturbations (deletion, repetition, transposition, space removal). Each title receives 1 to min(5, ceil(0.3 x query length)) errors. Mixed-script titles are first normalized to a single script by priority order (katakana >> hiragana >> romaji >> kanji) before misspellings are generated; training data totals 2.8M titles plus roughly 3M real query-to-entity pairs filtered from logs, with a 1:4 ratio of already-correct identity pairs mixed in so the model learns to preserve correct queries.

限制Limitations

作者列出的限制包括：這是查詢層級的校正，沒有利用更大範圍的搜尋情境；漢字輸入法轉換造成的錯誤依然難以處理；合成資料假設目錄標題本身拼寫正確，這個假設對使用者自建內容（例如播放清單名稱）就不成立；混合腳本查詢的表現明顯落後單一腳本查詢，例如混合腳本的完全匹配率只有 29.34%、保留正確查詢的比例（PT）只有 69.01%，遠低於整體平均的 88.36%。

The authors' stated limitations include: correction happens at the query level without leveraging broader search context; kanji IME-conversion errors remain difficult to handle; the synthetic data assumes catalog titles are correctly spelled, an assumption that breaks down for user-generated content such as playlist names; and mixed-script queries perform noticeably worse than single-script ones -- e.g. mixed-script exact match is only 29.34% and pass-through accuracy (PT) only 69.01%, well below the 88.36% overall average.

討論Discussion

整體結果（表 2）顯示：SymSpell 完全匹配 22.19%、CER 25.95%、F1 8.69%；Lattice Path Edit Distance 完全匹配 20.46%、CER 31.65%、F1 6.54%；零樣本 LLM（Claude Haiku）完全匹配最高（48.27%）、F1 42.46%，但 CER 27.24%、延遲約 800 毫秒；這篇論文的完整管線完全匹配 41.09%、CER 最低（11.62%）、F1 31.68%、保留正確查詢比例（PT）88.36%，延遲小於 4 毫秒。作者特別指出 LLM 雖然完全匹配率較高，卻會出現把短查詢過度擴寫的失控情況（例如「ミスチル」被校正成「Mr.Children」，CER 高達 275%），顯示完全匹配率不能單獨當作品質指標。

消融實驗（表 2 的變體）顯示：只用隨機雜訊訓練，F1 只有 9.61%；只用 QWERTY 鍵盤模型，F1 掉到最低的 4.48%；拿掉腳本正規化這一步，F1 從完整管線的 31.68% 降到 23.78%。這三個數字合在一起，撐住了論文的核心論點——「資料品質彌補模型規模」：一個只有 6 層的小模型，靠精心設計的合成錯字生成與腳本正規化，就能在延遲遠低於 LLM 的情況下逼近甚至部分超越更大模型的品質；不過完全匹配率仍輸給 LLM 提醒讀者，這個優勢主要體現在速度與 CER，而不是每一項指標。

Overall results (Table 2): SymSpell reaches 22.19% exact match, 25.95% CER, 8.69% F1; Lattice Path Edit Distance reaches 20.46% exact match, 31.65% CER, 6.54% F1; the zero-shot LLM (Claude Haiku) has the highest exact match (48.27%) and 42.46% F1, but 27.24% CER and roughly 800ms latency; this paper's full pipeline reaches 41.09% exact match, the lowest CER (11.62%), 31.68% F1, 88.36% pass-through accuracy, and sub-4ms latency. The authors specifically note the LLM, despite its higher exact-match rate, exhibits runaway over-expansion on short queries (e.g. "Mistiru" corrected to "Mr.Children" at 275% CER), showing exact match alone is not a sufficient quality signal.

Ablations (Table 2 variants) show: training on random noise alone yields only 9.61% F1; QWERTY-keyboard-only training drops F1 to the lowest value, 4.48%; removing script normalization drops F1 from the full pipeline's 31.68% to 23.78%. Together these numbers support the paper's central claim -- "data quality compensates for model size": a 6-layer model, through carefully designed synthetic misspelling generation and script normalization, approaches or partially exceeds larger-model quality at far lower latency than the LLM; still, its lagging exact-match rate is a reminder that the advantage shows up mainly in speed and CER, not uniformly across every metric.

VocalCoachBench: Benchmarking Audio-Language Models on Expert Feedback for Singing

VocalCoachBench：以專家回饋評測音訊語言模型的歌唱教練能力

Hayeon Bang, Hounsu Kim, Wonil Kim, Juhan Nam
arXiv:2609.04241 · cs.SD, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-08-06
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：VocalCoachBench 用 515 段錄音、18 位專業聲樂教師寫出的 1,056 份回饋、拆解成 12,051 條「原子級」教練主張（診斷、矯正、優點各佔一部分），把「歌唱教練回饋」拆成結構化任務（三選一排序、七類問題 Top-3 預測、片段級問題分類）與開放式任務（診斷覆蓋率、矯正合適度）分開評測；結果顯示 12 個受測的音訊語言模型在三選一排序上最高只到 74.5%（Qwen3.5-Omni Plus，隨機基準線 50%），在七類問題 Top-3 預測上沒有任何模型贏過「純標籤先驗」基準線的 62.4%，而在最嚴格的「精準命中」診斷指標上，表現最好的模型（Qwen3.5-Omni Plus）也只有 6.9% 的命中率，所有模型都低於 7%，顯示現有模型能做粗略的表現比較與大類問題辨識，但幾乎做不到專家等級的精準問題定位。Why this one: VocalCoachBench uses 515 recordings, 1,056 feedback submissions written by 18 professional vocal trainers, decomposed into 12,051 atomic coaching claims (split across diagnosis, correction, and strength), and separates "singing coaching feedback" into structured tasks (triplet ranking, seven-category Top-3 issue prediction, segment-level issue classification) and open-ended tasks (diagnosis coverage, correction appropriateness); across 12 tested audio-language models, the best triplet-ranking pairwise accuracy reaches only 74.5% (Qwen3.5-Omni Plus, versus a 50% random baseline), no model beats the 62.4% label-prior baseline on seven-category Top-3 issue prediction, and on the strictest "strict hit" diagnosis metric even the best model (Qwen3.5-Omni Plus) reaches only a 6.9% hit rate, with every model staying below 7% -- showing current models can do rough performance comparison and broad issue identification, but almost none can pinpoint problems at expert-level precision.

動機Motivation

現有的音訊語言模型評測大多聚焦在「辨識、描述、推理」聲音內容，但作者認為面向專家使用場景（例如歌唱教學）需要不一樣的能力——聲樂教練同時評估音準、節奏、氣息支撐、發聲、表現力等多個面向，指出主要問題，用有教學意義的語言解釋問題，並給出具體可執行的矯正方向。作者主張評測這種專業能力，需要跳脫「描述音訊內容」，改成評測「診斷式回饋」本身，而目前沒有一個公開的基準能做到這件事。

Existing audio-language model benchmarks mostly focus on recognizing, describing, and reasoning about audio content, but the authors argue expert-facing applications like vocal coaching demand a different kind of capability -- a vocal coach simultaneously evaluates pitch, rhythm, breath support, vocalization, and expression, identifies the primary issue, explains it in pedagogically meaningful terms, and gives a concrete, actionable corrective direction. Evaluating this kind of expertise requires moving beyond describing audio content toward evaluating diagnostic feedback itself, which no existing public benchmark does.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者點名幾類具體的先前基準與系統，說明它們為何不夠。通用音訊語言模型基準如 AIR-Bench、MMAU、AudioBench 只評測辨識、描述或字幕生成，沒有評測模型能不能指出領域特定問題或給出矯正建議；音樂描述資料集如 LP-MusicCaps、SongDescriber 同樣偏重字幕式的描述而非診斷；SALMONN、Qwen2-Audio、Audio Flamingo 這些具代表性的音訊語言模型雖然通用理解能力強，卻從未被拿來評測「產生專家等級教練回饋」這件事。歌唱評估文獻多半是聲學特徵加自動評分（音準準確度、顫音、音量）或無參考品質估計，關注的是打分數而非指出具體問題與矯正策略。

最接近的先前資源，如 NeuroPiano、CROCUS、Expert-Novice 這些收集鋼琴演奏專家評語與評分的資料集，以及 Ego-Exo4D、VidDiffBench 這類具身技能基準，都沒有把「診斷準確度」與「矯正適切度」明確當成評測目標，也沒有系統性地處理歌唱的片段級（segment-grounded）回饋。VocalCoachBench 與這些先前工作最大的不同，在於它是第一個公開釋出、同時包含專家自由文字回饋、結構化問題標籤、受控排序、以及片段級問題標註的歌唱基準。

The authors name several concrete prior benchmarks and systems and explain why they fall short. General audio-language-model benchmarks such as AIR-Bench, MMAU, and AudioBench evaluate only recognition, description, or captioning, without testing whether a model can identify domain-specific problems or give corrective guidance; music-description datasets like LP-MusicCaps and SongDescriber similarly emphasize captioning over diagnosis; representative audio-language models such as SALMONN, Qwen2-Audio, and Audio Flamingo have strong general audio understanding but have never been evaluated on generating expert-level coaching feedback. The singing-assessment literature mostly relies on acoustic features plus automatic scoring (pitch accuracy, vibrato, volume) or reference-free quality estimation, which prioritizes scoring rather than pinpointing specific problems and corrective strategies.

The closest prior resources -- NeuroPiano, CROCUS, and Expert-Novice, which collect expert ratings and critiques for piano performance, and embodied-skill benchmarks like Ego-Exo4D and VidDiffBench -- do not explicitly treat diagnosis accuracy and correction appropriateness as evaluation targets, nor do they systematically address segment-grounded feedback for singing. VocalCoachBench's main departure from all of this prior work is being the first publicly released singing benchmark that combines expert free-text coaching feedback, structured issue labels, controlled rankings, and segment-grounded issue annotations.

方法Method

資料集由兩個互補子集組成：同曲子集（207 段錄音，全部演唱同一首「Amazing Grace」，取自 DAMP-S-AG，用音準準確度當代理指標，按 1:2:1 的比例分層抽樣不同程度的演唱者，用來做受控比較）；多樣曲目子集（308 段錄音，取材自 PopBuTFy、N20EMv2、LM-SSD、NUS-48E、MedleyDB、MedleyVox 等九個公開資料集，每位歌手最多收錄四段，涵蓋多種教練情境與錄音條件）。總計 515 段錄音、18 位至少三年教學經驗的專業聲樂教師、1,056 份回饋、4,443 個回饋區塊、拆解出 12,051 條原子級教練主張（6,087 條診斷、4,527 條矯正、1,437 條優點）。問題分類法分七個細類、歸在三個母類別下：發聲技術（氣息支撐、發聲、技巧）、音樂準確度（音準、節奏）、演繹表現（咬字、表現力）。

前導研究發現量表式評分的評分者間一致性很低（Krippendorff's α 約 0.323，各面向 α 介於 0.171 到 0.393），教練們解釋「量表在實際教學裡本來就不是統一使用的」，因此正式標註改成以具體教練行動為中心，而非量表分數。同曲子集額外收集三選一排序附文字理由；多樣曲目子集額外收集帶時間區間的片段級問題標註。評測分兩大類：結構化任務（三選一排序、七類問題 Top-3 預測、片段級問題分類，用固定目標打分）與開放式任務（用原子主張做診斷覆蓋率與矯正合適度評分，由 LLM 裁判打分，裁判與專家標註的一致性分別是 83.4%／κ=0.779（診斷覆蓋率）與 79.2%／κ=0.687（矯正合適度））。

The dataset has two complementary subsets: a same-song subset (207 recordings, all singing "Amazing Grace" from DAMP-S-AG, stratified-sampled across proficiency levels at a 1:2:1 ratio using a pitch-accuracy proxy, enabling controlled comparison) and a diverse-song subset (308 recordings drawn from nine public datasets including PopBuTFy, N20EMv2, LM-SSD, NUS-48E, MedleyDB, and MedleyVox, capped at four recordings per singer, capturing varied coaching scenarios and recording conditions). In total: 515 recordings, 18 professional vocal trainers with at least three years of coaching experience, 1,056 feedback submissions, 4,443 feedback blocks, decomposed into 12,051 atomic coaching claims (6,087 diagnosis, 4,527 correction, 1,437 strength). The issue taxonomy has seven fine categories under three parents: technical production (breath support, vocalization, technique), musical accuracy (pitch, rhythm), and delivery (diction, expression).

Pilot studies found low inter-rater agreement on scalar ratings (Krippendorff's alpha around 0.323, aspect-level alpha ranging 0.171-0.393); trainers explained that "scoring scales are not used uniformly in actual teaching practice," so formal annotation was redesigned around concrete coaching actions rather than scalar scores. The same-song subset additionally collects triplet rankings with written rationales; the diverse-song subset additionally collects time-span segment-level issue annotations. Evaluation splits into structured tasks (triplet ranking, seven-category Top-3 issue prediction, segment-level issue classification, scored against fixed targets) and open-ended tasks (diagnosis coverage and correction appropriateness scored via atomic claims by an LLM judge, whose agreement with expert labels is 83.4%/kappa=0.779 for diagnosis coverage and 79.2%/kappa=0.687 for correction appropriateness).

限制Limitations

作者自陳的限制包括：只涵蓋英語獨唱，同曲子集只用單一首歌；評測是純音訊、單次評測，真實教學會用到視覺與肢體線索，也會根據多次嘗試調整回饋；片段級評測假設模型拿到的是專家已經標好的片段，沒有測試模型自己找出問題片段的能力；開放式評測是用「有沒有覆蓋到專家的原子主張」來打分，可能低估了模型給出的、專家沒寫進參考答案裡但依然合理的回饋；作者也提醒模型輸出不能取代專業教學，因為錯誤的發聲指導可能造成傷害。

The authors' stated limitations include: coverage limited to English solo singing, with the same-song subset restricted to a single song; evaluation is audio-only and one-shot, whereas real coaching uses visual and bodily cues and adapts feedback across repeated attempts; segment-level evaluation assumes expert-provided segments rather than testing whether a model can locate the problematic segment itself; open-ended evaluation scores feedback by coverage of expert atomic claims, which may under-credit valid feedback a model gives that falls outside the available reference claims; and the authors caution that model outputs should not substitute for professional instruction, since incorrect vocal guidance can cause harm.

討論Discussion

結構化任務結果（表 3）：三選一排序的成對準確率，開源主協定最佳為 Qwen2.5-Omni 的 68.7%，閉源最佳為 Qwen3.5-Omni Plus 的 74.5%（隨機基準線 50%），非傳遞性三元組（cycle rate）介於 11.9% 到 43.4%；七類問題 Top-3 預測（F1@3）開源主協定最佳為 Fun-Audio-Chat 的 44.6%，閉源最佳為 Gemini 3 Flash Preview 的 50.1%，但兩者都低於「純標籤先驗」基準線的 62.4%，等於沒有一個模型贏過不看音訊的統計基準；片段級問題分類（任一命中準確率）最佳為 MiMo-Audio 與 Kimi-Audio 並列的 40.5%，但論文指出 MiMo-Audio 是靠對每個片段都預測同一個類別（vocalization）才打平多數基準線的 40.5%，而不是真正的辨識能力。

開放式任務結果（表 4）：最嚴格的「精準命中」診斷率，最佳模型 Qwen3.5-Omni Plus 也只有 6.9%，最差的 Audio Flamingo 3 只有 1.7%，所有模型都低於 7%；較寬鬆的「粗略命中」率則從 2.6%（Music Flamingo）到 63.2%（Qwen2.5-Omni）不等，落差很大。矯正合適度上，Qwen3.5-Omni Plus 達到 98.4% 的有效矯正率，但同樣使用「簡易協定」評測的 Audio Flamingo 3 只有 9.0%、Music Flamingo 只有 20.3%；作者特別提醒，高矯正有效率不能單獨解讀為模型懂得診斷——「模型即使診斷本身鬆散或籠統，仍然可能生成看起來合理的矯正建議」，這正是這篇論文把診斷與矯正拆開評分的原因。

Structured-task results (Table 3): on triplet ranking, the best open-weight main-protocol pairwise accuracy is Qwen2.5-Omni at 68.7%, the best closed-source is Qwen3.5-Omni Plus at 74.5% (random baseline 50%), with non-transitive triplet (cycle) rates ranging 11.9%-43.4%; on seven-category Top-3 issue prediction (F1@3), the best open-weight main-protocol model is Fun-Audio-Chat at 44.6% and the best closed-source is Gemini 3 Flash Preview at 50.1%, but both stay below the 62.4% label-prior baseline -- meaning no model beats a statistical baseline that never sees the audio; on segment-level issue classification (any-match accuracy) the best is a tie between MiMo-Audio and Kimi-Audio at 40.5%, though the paper notes MiMo-Audio only matches that majority-baseline score by predicting the same category ("vocalization") for every segment, not through genuine recognition.

Open-ended results (Table 4): on the strictest "strict hit" diagnosis metric, even the best model (Qwen3.5-Omni Plus) reaches only 6.9%, and the worst (Audio Flamingo 3) only 1.7%, with every model below 7%; the looser "coarse hit" rate spans a much wider range, from 2.6% (Music Flamingo) to 63.2% (Qwen2.5-Omni). On correction appropriateness, Qwen3.5-Omni Plus reaches a 98.4% valid-correction rate, but Audio Flamingo 3 and Music Flamingo -- both evaluated under the "simple protocol" -- reach only 9.0% and 20.3% respectively; the authors specifically caution that a high valid-correction rate should not alone be read as evidence of accurate diagnosis, since "models can generate plausible corrections even when their diagnoses remain loose or generic" -- precisely why the paper scores diagnosis and correction separately.
