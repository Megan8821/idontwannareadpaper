Agentic Music Post-Production

代理式音樂後製：以參考曲引導、可編輯效果鏈規劃加上學習式偵錯與數值修正的多軌混音代理（MixAgent）

Jaywon Koo, Junghyun Koo, Marco A. Martínez-Ramírez, Wei-Hsiang Liao, Seungheon Doh, Qiyu Wu, Vicente Ordonez, Yuki Mitsufuji
arXiv:2610.29093 · cs.SD; cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-10-21
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Sony 與 Rice 大學的 MixAgent 把多軌混音拆成三個決定：用哪些效果器（凍結的 Qwen3-Omni 參考檢索到的範例來規劃軌、子群組、總線三層的效果鏈）、哪裡要修（3,490 萬參數的偵錯器）、怎麼修（Qwen3.5-4B 評論器直接輸出數值參數，先 SFT 再用渲染後的音訊結果做 DPO）。在 MedleyDB 留出的 20 段上，學習式修正把 MRS 誤差從 2.17 降到 1.75（19%），DPO 比只做 SFT 再降 8.4%（1.91→1.75）；MSS 2.83，三個多模態 LLM 基線最好的是 3.67。要保留的是：主要評估的風格參考是同一首歌的另一段，幾乎等於看過答案；AlbumDB 上結果互有勝負；而且拿掉檢索與學習式修正之後（MSS 4.52），它比單次生成的基線還差。Why this one: MixAgent, from Sony and Rice University, splits multitrack mixing into three decisions: which effects to use (a frozen Qwen3-Omni plans track, subgroup, and full-mix effect chains guided by retrieved examples), what to fix (a 34.9M-parameter detector), and how to fix it (a Qwen3.5-4B critic that outputs numerical parameters directly, trained with SFT and then DPO on rendered audio outcomes). On 20 held-out MedleyDB clips, learned refinement cuts MRS error from 2.17 to 1.75 (19%), and DPO adds 8.4% over SFT alone (1.91→1.75); MSS is 2.83 against 3.67 for the best of three multimodal-LLM baselines. The caveats: the main evaluation's style reference is another excerpt of the same song, which is nearly seeing the answer; AlbumDB results are mixed; and without retrieval and learned refinement (MSS 4.52) it is worse than the single-pass baselines.

動機Motivation

語言模型代理已經會寫程式、修圖，但在創作軟體裡最難的是把高階目標變成精確設定：聽出「人聲太小」不等於知道該動哪個旋鈕、動多少。混音又特別糾纏：效果器在單軌、子群組（例如整組鼓）、總線三層都有，彼此互相影響，鼓大聲了人聲就被蓋掉。想要的聲音也很難用文字描述，所以作者選擇參考曲引導的設定：給一首混好的歌當風格範例。

既有系統各管一段。神經網路混音（Koo 等人 2023 的 MST、Moliner 等人 2026）輸出好聽的音訊但不暴露參數；Diff-MST 能預測可編輯參數，卻只能用固定的效果器序列；LLM2Fx-Tools 只處理單軌效果鏈；RIME 是依指令修改已經混好的成品。作者也發現，直接讓多模態模型「聽了再改」常常越改越差。這篇想做的是從原始分軌出發、全程可檢查可修改的完整混音代理，並回答一個更一般的問題：對於透過數值工具行動的代理，什麼樣的回饋才有用。

Language-model agents already write code and edit images, but in creative software the hard part is turning a high-level goal into precise settings: hearing that 'the vocal is too quiet' does not say which control to move or by how much. Mixing is especially entangled: effects apply at three levels, single tracks, subgroups (e.g. all drums), and the full mix, and they interact, so louder drums mask the vocal. The desired sound is also hard to describe in words, so the authors use a reference-guided setting in which a finished song serves as the style example.

Existing systems each cover part of the problem. Neural mixing (MST from Koo et al. 2023, Moliner et al. 2026) produces good audio without exposing parameters; Diff-MST predicts editable parameters but only for a fixed effect sequence; LLM2Fx-Tools handles single-track effect chains; RIME edits an already finished mix according to instructions. The authors also find that simply prompting multimodal models to listen and revise often makes the mix worse. The goal here is a complete mixing agent that starts from raw tracks and stays inspectable and editable throughout, and that answers a more general question: what kind of feedback helps agents that act through numerical tools.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最該對照的先前系統有四個。Diff-MST（Vanka 等人，2024）從原始分軌預測一個固定可微分混音台的參數（每軌增益、EQ、壓縮、聲像，加上總線 EQ 與壓縮），不能選效果器也不能分群組；附錄裡它在 MedleyDB 上的 MSS 是 4.31，MixAgent 是 2.83，但作者也承認 Diff-MST 的訓練曲目可能和評估片段重疊。MST（Koo 等人，2023）是端到端風格轉換，直接輸出音訊、沒有參數。LLM2Fx-Tools（Doh 等人，2026，部分作者重疊）用工具呼叫還原單軌效果鏈，MixAgent 把它延伸到多軌與三層結構，評論器的訓練方式也沿用它。GRAFX（Lee 等人，2024）是可微分音訊處理圖庫，這篇的範例計畫、訓練資料與 oracle 全部來自它。

也值得對照昨天（2026-10-25）收錄的 MixJudge：兩篇都在 MedleyDB 上評估、都在處理「評論混音」，但 MixJudge 學的是可用文字查詢的評分（這段人聲是不是太悶），MixAgent 的評論器則直接吐出參數數值。

論文沒有講、但值得點出的一句：評論器的 A3 數值修正，本質上是把 GRAFX 的梯度擬合「攤銷」成一次前向預測，訓練目標正是同一語料上 GRAFX 擬合出的參數。這和 DeepAFx-ST（Steinmetz、Bryan 與 Reiss，2022）用網路從參考音訊直接預測效果器參數是同一類做法，差別在於這裡用 LLM 加 DPO、而且一次只修一個元素。

Four prior systems matter most. Diff-MST (Vanka et al., 2024) predicts the parameters of a fixed differentiable console from raw tracks (per-track gain, EQ, compression, and panning, plus full-mix EQ and compression) and cannot choose effects or group tracks; in the appendix its MedleyDB MSS is 4.31 against MixAgent's 2.83, though the authors concede Diff-MST's training songs may overlap the evaluation clips. MST (Koo et al., 2023) is end-to-end style transfer that outputs audio with no parameters. LLM2Fx-Tools (Doh et al., 2026, with overlapping authors) recovers single-track effect chains through tool calls; MixAgent extends it to multitrack and three levels, and reuses its critic training recipe. GRAFX (Lee et al., 2024) is a differentiable audio-processing graph library, and this paper's example plans, training data, and oracles all come from it.

It is also worth comparing with MixJudge, covered yesterday (2026-10-25): both evaluate on MedleyDB and both critique mixes, but MixJudge learns a text-queryable score (is this vocal too muddy), while MixAgent's critic outputs parameter values directly.

What the paper does not say, but deserves pointing out: the critic's A3 numerical correction essentially amortizes GRAFX's gradient fitting into a single forward prediction, since its training targets are exactly GRAFX-fitted parameters on the same corpus. That is the same family as DeepAFx-ST (Steinmetz, Bryan, and Reiss, 2022), which predicts effect parameters from a reference with a network; the difference is an LLM with DPO, correcting one element at a time.

方法Method

規劃：分群代理先聽各軌並讀樂器標籤，把軌分到子群組（例如大鼓、小鼓、鈸成一組鼓）。凍結的 Qwen3-Omni 規劃器再依序選出單軌、子群組、總線的效果鏈，每一層參考前一層的決定。為了避免它只挑很短的鏈，系統從資料庫檢索範例：資料庫存的是 GRAFX 擬合專業混音後再修剪掉作用很小的效果器所得的「擬真值」計畫，用三個文字檢索（比對分軌描述）加一個音訊檢索（比對參考曲）的範例，只給效果器選擇與順序、不給參數值。效果鏈之後固定不變。

階層式修正：規劃器先根據參考曲給初始參數，之後由下而上修：先單軌、再子群組、最後總線。偵錯器（四層雙向 Transformer，3,490 萬參數）預測每個元素需要修正的機率，超過 0.15 才交給評論器，總線一律檢查。評論器是 Qwen3.5-4B 接凍結的 RLAT 效果器編碼器，中間用可訓練的音訊轉接器（8,900 萬參數，LoRA 9,900 萬），聽該元素、目前混音與參考曲，輸出修正。每層只走一次，修完重新渲染，下一層聽到的是修過的音訊。

Planning: a grouping agent listens to the tracks and reads their instrument labels, assigning them to subgroups (kick, snare, and cymbals form a drum group). A frozen Qwen3-Omni planner then selects effect chains for tracks, subgroups, and the full mix in order, each level guided by the earlier choices. To keep it from choosing very short chains, the system retrieves examples from a database of pseudo-ground-truth plans obtained by fitting GRAFX to professional mixes and pruning effects that barely change the audio; three text-retrieved examples (matching track captions) plus one audio-retrieved example (matching the reference) show effect choice and order, not parameter values. The chains then stay fixed.

Hierarchical refinement: the planner sets initial parameters from the reference, and revision proceeds bottom-up, tracks, then subgroups, then the full mix. A detector (a four-layer bidirectional Transformer, 34.9M parameters) predicts the probability that each element needs correction, and only those above 0.15 go to the critic; the full mix is always inspected. The critic is Qwen3.5-4B connected to a frozen RLAT effects encoder through a trainable audio adapter (89M parameters, plus 99M of LoRA); it hears the element, the current mix, and the reference, and outputs a correction. Each stage gets one pass and is re-rendered, so the next stage hears the corrected audio.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是參考曲的來源。主要評估（表 2 到表 5）的風格參考是同一首歌、同一段落裡另一個不重疊的 15 秒窗，用同一套專業混音做成。這等於讓系統看到幾乎同一組效果設定的另一段樣本，對「重建誤差」極為有利，跟真實情境（拿別人的歌當參考）差很多。真正用其他歌當參考的只有聽測，而聽測只有 10 段、11 位受試者各評 10 題；MixAgent 顯著勝過三個 LLM 基線（Holm 校正後 p<0.001），但和原始人工混音仍有明顯差距。

評估規模小：MedleyDB 只有 20 段（17 首歌），AlbumDB 14 段。AlbumDB 上結果互有勝負：MSS 3.81 對 GPT-4o-audio 的 3.77，TuneJury 與單次 Gemini 打平，FxEnc++ 和 AF 都輸給單次 GPT-4o-audio。指標本身也不可靠：附錄寫 TuneJury 把專業混音排在「直接加總」之上只有 48%，排在隨機增益之上只有 41%，作者自己也說它只能抓嚴重劣化，但主表仍把 TuneJury 0.21 當成優勢之一。

還有幾點作者自己有寫：監督訊號來自 GRAFX 擬合參數，不是工程師真正的設定；用擬真值擬合同一份計畫可達 0.90 MRS，MixAgent 是 1.75，參數估計還差很遠；效果器庫只是專業工具的一小部分，計畫定了之後評論器不能增刪效果器；也沒有做使用者實際怎麼看、怎麼改計畫的研究。

The biggest issue is where the reference comes from. In the main evaluation (Tables 2–5) the style reference is another non-overlapping 15-second window from the same section of the same song, made with the same professional mix. That lets the system see another sample of nearly the same effect settings, which strongly favors reconstruction error and is far from the real use case (a reference from someone else's song). Only the listening test uses references from other songs, and it has 10 clips and 11 participants rating 10 trials each; MixAgent beats the three LLM baselines significantly (p<0.001, Holm-corrected) but remains clearly below the original human mixes.

The evaluation is small: 20 MedleyDB clips (17 songs) and 14 AlbumDB clips. AlbumDB results are mixed: MSS 3.81 against 3.77 for GPT-4o-audio, TuneJury tied with single-pass Gemini, and FxEnc++ and AF both lost to single-pass GPT-4o-audio. The metrics are shaky too: the appendix says TuneJury ranks the professional mix above the plain sum only 48% of the time and above random track gains only 41%; the authors themselves say it only catches severe degradation, yet the main table still presents TuneJury 0.21 as a strength.

The authors state several more: supervision comes from GRAFX-fitted parameters, not the engineer's real settings; fitting the same plans to ground truth reaches 0.90 MRS against MixAgent's 1.75, so parameter estimation is far from solved; the effects library is a small subset of professional tools, and the critic cannot add or remove effects once the plan is fixed; and there is no study of how users actually read or edit the plans.

討論Discussion

消融表（表 5）比主表更能說明這個系統的價值在哪。檢索讓 MRS 降 0.64（有無修正都一樣：2.81→2.17、2.39→1.75），學習式修正降 0.42，兩者可加總。但兩者都拿掉時 MSS 是 4.52，比單次生成的 Qwen3-Omni（3.73）、Gemini（3.77）、GPT-4o-audio（3.67）都差。換句話說，階層式代理架構本身沒有帶來好處，進步全來自 GRAFX 範例檢索與用 MixCritic 訓練的修正器。檢索的效果也有一部分只是讓規劃器選更多效果器（每軌 1.55 個變 3.77 個）；用擬真值擬合時，有範例的計畫 MRS 1.08，GRAFX 自己的計畫 0.89。

回饋形式的比較（表 3）是這篇對「代理怎麼用數值工具」最有一般性的發現。未經訓練的 Qwen3-Omni 當評論器時，只有 42–52% 的修正讓混音變好，最後 MRS 2.18–2.44，比完全不修（2.17）還差，呼應了 LLM 自我修正在沒有外部訊號時常常失敗的文獻（Huang 等人，2024）。訓練之後回饋越具體越好：知覺描述 2.09、指名工具 1.98、直接給數值 1.75；而且訓練過的數值評論器還贏過拿到正確答案的知覺與工具描述 oracle（1.87、1.78）。這表示瓶頸在「把文字建議翻成數字」這一步，跟今天另一篇推導深度論文的結論方向一致。

實務上，可檢查、可回退的參數紀錄（評估集上 241 次修正、每段約 12 次，三分之二讓總混音誤差下降）是相對於端到端神經混音最實在的賣點。但要變成工程師真的會用的工具，還需要在跨歌曲參考下的大規模評估，以及實際使用者研究。

The ablation table (Table 5) says more about where the value lies than the main table does. Retrieval lowers MRS by 0.64 with or without refinement (2.81→2.17, 2.39→1.75), learned refinement by 0.42, and the gains add. But with both removed, MSS is 4.52, worse than single-pass Qwen3-Omni (3.73), Gemini (3.77), and GPT-4o-audio (3.67). In other words, the hierarchical agent structure itself brings no benefit; the gains come entirely from GRAFX example retrieval and the corrector trained on MixCritic. Part of retrieval's effect is simply that the planner picks more effects (1.55 per track becomes 3.77); when fitted to ground truth, plans with examples reach 1.08 MRS and GRAFX's own plans 0.89.

The feedback-form comparison (Table 3) is the paper's most general finding about agents using numerical tools. With untrained Qwen3-Omni as critic, only 42–52% of corrections improve the mix and final MRS is 2.18–2.44, worse than no revision (2.17), echoing the literature showing LLM self-correction often fails without an external signal (Huang et al., 2024). After training, more specific feedback is better: perceptual advice 2.09, tool-specific 1.98, numerical 1.75; and the trained numerical critic even beats oracle perceptual and tool advice written with ground-truth access (1.87, 1.78). The bottleneck is turning verbal advice into numbers, which points the same way as today's derivation-depth paper.

In practice, the inspectable, revertible parameter log (241 corrections on the evaluation set, about 12 per clip, two thirds of which lower full-mix error) is the most concrete advantage over end-to-end neural mixing. Turning it into a tool engineers would use still needs large-scale evaluation with cross-song references and a real user study.

Derivation Depth Exposes Reasoning Failures in Tool-Augmented Music Analysis

推導深度揭露工具輔助音樂分析的推理失敗：音訊語言模型讀得到 MIR 工具輸出，卻推不出結論

Rupali Rajesh, Sethuraman T V, Savya Khosla, Srinidhi Sunkara, Aditya Shanmugham, Rohan Maheshwari, Derek Hoiem
arXiv:2610.29060 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-21
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇不問音訊語言模型聽不聽得出調性，而是問：MIR 工具已經把調性、和弦交給它了，它推得出答案嗎？作者固定音樂事實、只改變工具輸出和答案之間隔了幾步推理（推導深度）。在轉調題上，報告直接寫「新調是大調」時三個開源模型都是 100%；改成「新調：D 大調」只多一步，就掉到 57–81%，儘管單獨問「D 大調是大調還是小調」它們全答對；給軌外屬和弦掉到 0–26%，只給和弦組成音則是 0%。把結論故意寫反，五個模型（包括兩個 Gemini）配對正確率都是 0%。要保留的是：題目全是 FluidSynth 合成的鋼琴短曲，每個任務只有 6 首基礎作品，機制分析只做了 Qwen2.5-Omni。Why this one: This paper does not ask whether audio-language models can hear the key; it asks whether they can derive the answer once an MIR tool has already handed them the key and the chords. The authors hold the musical fact fixed and vary only how many reasoning steps separate the tool output from the answer (derivation depth). On modulation, all three open models score 100% when the report says 'the new key is major'; replacing that with 'new key: D major', one step away, drops them to 57–81%, even though all three answer 'is D major major or minor' correctly when asked alone; giving the secondary dominant drops them to 0–26%, and giving only the chord's pitches to 0%. When the conclusion is deliberately inverted, all five models, both Geminis included, score 0% pair accuracy. The caveats: every item is a short FluidSynth piano render, each task has only 6 base compositions, and the mechanistic analysis covers only Qwen2.5-Omni.

動機Motivation

音訊語言模型直接從音訊辨認調性、終止式、曲式這類基本音樂屬性，表現接近隨機（作者引用 MMAU 與 MUSE benchmark）。很自然的補救是在音訊和模型中間塞一個 MIR 工具：讓 librosa 之類的分析器報出調性、和弦序列、自相似結構，模型只負責根據這些量測回答高階問題。近期的工具增強音訊系統（EChO-Agent、多來源證據融合）也確實報告了可觀的提升。

問題在於這些系統只量最終正確率，把「工具量錯」和「模型讀錯工具輸出」混在一起。而 MIR 工具給的通常是中間量測，不是答案本身：使用者問「轉到大調還是小調」，工具回「新調：D 大調」，模型至少還要知道 D 大調是大調。這篇要把第二種失敗單獨拆出來：在音樂資訊正確且完整的前提下，模型能不能從工具輸出推出結論。

Audio-language models perform near chance when recovering basic musical properties such as key, cadence, or form directly from audio (the authors cite MMAU and the MUSE benchmark). The natural fix is to put an MIR tool between the audio and the model: an analyzer such as librosa reports the key, chord sequence, or self-similarity structure, and the model only has to answer higher-level questions from those measurements. Recent tool-augmented audio systems (EChO-Agent, multi-source evidence fusion) do report substantial gains.

The problem is that these systems measure only final accuracy, which conflates tool errors with failures to read the tool's output. MIR tools usually return intermediate measurements rather than the answer: the user asks whether a modulation goes to a major or minor key, the tool says 'new key: D major', and the model still has to know that D major is a major key. This paper isolates the second failure: given correct and complete musical information, can the model derive the conclusion from the tool output.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接相關的先前工作有兩條。一條是 benchmark：MMAU（Sakshi 等人，2024）與 MUSE（Carone 等人，2025）顯示音訊語言模型從音訊抽取音樂屬性接近隨機，這是這篇的出發點，但它們不提供工具輸出。另一條是工具增強系統：EChO-Agent（Zhang 等人，2026）與 Olev 和 Alumäe（2026）的多來源證據融合把外部證據接進音訊問答，報告的是端到端正確率，不區分工具錯和推理錯。這篇的差別在於把工具輸出當成受控變因，固定內容、只改格式與推導距離。

論文沒有講、但最值得講的一句：「每個事實單獨問都會、合起來就不會」這個現象，在純文字 LLM 上早就有名字，叫組合性落差（compositionality gap，Press 等人，Findings of EMNLP 2023），之後 Balesni 等人（2024）稱為 two-hop curse，也都是用類似的邏輯：先確認模型知道 A→B 與 B→C，再看它能不能直接答 A→C。這篇的知識探針（單獨問「D 大調是大調嗎」）就是這個實驗設計搬到音樂理論上。而「把結論寫反、模型照單全收」也接近 RAG 文獻裡的知識衝突實驗（Xie 等人，ICLR 2024），只是這裡模型本身幾乎沒有從音訊得到的「內部知識」可以對抗。所以新意不在現象本身，而在於把它定位成工具增強 MIR 的設計問題，並給出一把可以直接拿來測系統的推導深度尺。

There are two direct lines of prior work. One is benchmarks: MMAU (Sakshi et al., 2024) and MUSE (Carone et al., 2025) show that audio-language models extract musical properties from audio near chance, which is this paper's starting point, but they supply no tool output. The other is tool-augmented systems: EChO-Agent (Zhang et al., 2026) and the multi-source evidence fusion of Olev and Alumäe (2026) feed external evidence into audio question answering and report end-to-end accuracy, without separating tool errors from reasoning errors. This paper differs by treating the tool output as a controlled variable: content fixed, only format and derivation distance changed.

What the paper does not say, but most deserves saying: 'knows each fact alone, fails when combining them' already has a name in text-only LLMs, the compositionality gap (Press et al., Findings of EMNLP 2023), later called the two-hop curse by Balesni et al. (2024), both using the same logic of first confirming the model knows A→B and B→C, then asking A→C directly. This paper's knowledge probes (asking 'is D major a major key' alone) are that design moved into music theory. And 'invert the conclusion, the model follows it' is close to the knowledge-conflict experiments in the RAG literature (Xie et al., ICLR 2024), except that here the model has almost no audio-derived internal knowledge to push back with. The novelty is therefore not the phenomenon but framing it as a design problem for tool-augmented MIR and providing a derivation-depth ruler that can be applied to real systems.

方法Method

題目：五個任務都是最小配對（minimal pair），兩個版本只差一個音樂屬性：終止式（正格或阻礙）、轉調（新調是大調或小調）、音階（利地安或伊奧尼安，差一個音）、動機（移調再現或新動機）、曲式（ABA 或通篇創作）。全部以程式產生 MIDI，用 FluidSynth 和 TimGM6mb 音色庫渲染成鋼琴音訊，工具報告由已知樂譜填入，所以標準答案是精確的。每個任務 6 首基礎作品，各在 -6 到 +6 半音之間做最多五個移調，最多 30 對（音階 23 對）。

格式與推導階梯：同一個資訊寫成六種格式，F1 判定句、F3 散文直接寫結論，F2 只給結論依據的單一值（鍵值對），F4–F6 給完整工具輸出，F5 加入無關雜訊（BPM、RMS），F6 是 JSON；另有 F1 反轉版，寫出錯的結論。為了排除 F2 資訊不完整的混淆，另外每個任務產生 72 題推導階梯：d0 寫結論，d1 給前一步事實（轉調題給原調與新調），d2 給那個事實的證據（樞紐的軌外屬和弦），d3 只給和弦組成音。

Items: each of the five tasks is a minimal pair whose two versions differ in exactly one musical property: cadence (authentic or deceptive), modulation (new key major or minor), scale (Lydian or Ionian, one note apart), motif (transposed return or new motif), and structure (ABA or through-composed). All are generated programmatically as MIDI and rendered to piano audio with FluidSynth and the TimGM6mb soundfont; the tool report is filled from the known score, so ground truth is exact. Each task has 6 base compositions, each rendered at up to five transpositions between -6 and +6 semitones, for up to 30 pairs (23 for scale).

Formats and the derivation ladder: the same information is written in six formats. F1 (verdict) and F3 (prose) state the conclusion, F2 gives only the single value it follows from (a key–value pair), and F4–F6 give the full tool output, with F5 adding irrelevant clutter (BPM, RMS) and F6 in JSON; a flipped F1 states the wrong conclusion. To remove a confound in F2's incomplete information, the authors also build a 72-item derivation ladder per task: d0 states the conclusion, d1 gives the fact one step away (for modulation, home and new key), d2 gives the evidence for that fact (the pivot secondary dominant), and d3 gives only the chord's pitches.

限制Limitations

樣本小而且人工。格式實驗（表 2）結構與轉調合起來是 60 對、12 首作品，信賴區間寬，例如 Music Flamingo 的落差是 65 [48,83]；同一首作品的移調彼此不獨立（作者也因此以作品為單位做 bootstrap），所以實際的獨立單位是每任務 6 首。所有電池題目都是 FluidSynth 合成鋼琴，真實錄音只有 60 段 GuitarSet，而且只測調性一題。

「照著錯誤結論走」的解讀要小心。沒有音訊時，模型手上只有那份報告，跟著報告走是合理行為，不能直接算成「不會驗證」。即使給了音訊，開源模型在合成題上只靠音訊的配對正確率是 0–21%（隨機 25%），也就是說它們本來就聽不出答案，拿什麼去驗證？比較有說服力的是 GuitarSet：開源模型單靠音訊能認出 50–62% 的調，卻仍有 33–100% 會跟著錯誤標註走（AF3 是 33%，Qwen 63%，MF 100%）。

其他：機制探針（logit lens、activation steering）只做在 Qwen2.5-Omni 上；論文說 Gemini 3.6 Flash 會先內部推理再作答，開源模型則沒有對應的推理設定，所以「前沿模型退化較少」混了模型規模與推理模式兩個因素，論文沒有拆開；倫理聲明提到收集了一位音樂家的人類聆聽基線，正文卻沒有報告任何結果。

The sample is small and artificial. The format experiment (Table 2) pools structure and modulation into 60 pairs from 12 compositions, and the intervals are wide, e.g. 65 [48,83] for Music Flamingo's gap; transpositions of the same composition are not independent (which is why the authors bootstrap over compositions), so the real independent unit is 6 compositions per task. Every battery item is a FluidSynth piano render; the only real recordings are 60 GuitarSet clips, tested on key alone.

The 'follows the wrong conclusion' result needs care. Without audio, the report is all the model has, and following it is rational, not a failure to verify. Even with audio, the open models' audio-only pair accuracy on the synthetic items is 0–21% (chance 25%): they cannot hear the answer, so there is nothing to verify against. GuitarSet is more convincing: the open models identify the key from audio alone in 50–62% of recordings yet still follow a wrong annotation 33–100% of the time (AF3 33%, Qwen 63%, MF 100%).

Other points: the mechanistic probes (logit lens, activation steering) cover only Qwen2.5-Omni; the paper notes that Gemini 3.6 Flash reasons internally before answering, and no comparable reasoning setting is reported for the open models, so 'frontier models degrade less' mixes model scale with reasoning mode, which the paper does not separate; and the ethics statement mentions a human listening baseline from one musician, but the results never report it.

討論Discussion

最刺眼的數字是 d1：「新調：D 大調」問「新調是大調還是小調」，這幾乎是字串比對，三個開源模型只拿到 57–81%，而且同一個事實單獨問都是 100%。這讓人懷疑問題有一部分在提示格式與作答習慣，不完全是「推理」。不過結構題的 logit lens 給了一個有意思的對照：正確結論在 93% 的題目裡會在中間層變得可解碼（第 25 層邊際 +0.9），然後在輸出前消失，作者稱為「形成了但遺失」；轉調題則從頭到尾邊際都是零，是「根本沒形成」。activation steering 把 Qwen 從 25% 最多推到 29%，在提示裡直接寫出規則也沒用（Qwen 26→12），兩個 Gemini 卻因此升到 92% 與 99%。

兩次呼叫的緩解法（第一次只讓模型說工具輸出代表什麼、第二次只根據自己的摘要作答）結果很混雜：終止式 d2 從 17% 升到 42%，結構 d1 卻從 100% 掉到 62%、d2 從 90% 掉到 38%，因為摘要漏掉了第二次需要的資訊。外部化中間步驟救不了深度。

對系統設計的啟示其實有張力。作者建議工具設計要考慮留給模型多少推導；但讓工具直接給結論，模型就變成傳聲筒，結論錯它也照抄，系統上限就是工具本身。比較務實的做法可能是把可規則化的推導（大小調判斷、和弦命名）寫成程式放在工具那一側，同時保留原始量測讓模型或人能檢查。作者提的訓練方向（獎勵「讀對工具輸出」，並用反轉結論當驗證訊號）也合理，但前提是模型能從音訊得到獨立證據，而目前開源模型在合成題上連這一步都做不到。

The most striking number is d1: given 'new key: D major' and asked whether the new key is major or minor, which is close to string matching, the three open models score only 57–81%, while answering the same fact alone at 100%. That suggests part of the problem lies in prompt format and answering habits, not purely 'reasoning'. The structure logit lens offers an interesting contrast, though: the correct conclusion becomes decodable at an intermediate layer on 93% of items (margin +0.9 at layer 25) and then fades before the output, which the authors call 'formed but lost'; for modulation the margin stays at zero throughout, 'never formed'. Activation steering moves Qwen from 25% to at most 29%, and stating the rule in the prompt does not help (Qwen 26→12), while the same rule lifts the two Geminis to 92% and 99%.

The two-call mitigation (first call: say what the tool output indicates; second call: answer from that summary alone) is mixed: cadence d2 rises from 17% to 42%, but structure d1 falls from 100% to 62% and d2 from 90% to 38%, because the summary drops what the second call needs. Externalizing the intermediate step does not buy depth.

The design implication carries a tension. The authors say tool design must account for how much derivation it leaves to the model; but if the tool states conclusions, the model becomes a pass-through that copies wrong conclusions too, and the system's ceiling is the tool itself. A more practical route may be to implement rule-like derivations (major/minor, chord naming) as code on the tool side while keeping the raw measurements for the model or a person to check. The proposed training direction (rewarding correct tool reading, with flipped conclusions as a verification signal) is reasonable, but it assumes the model has independent evidence from the audio, which the open models currently lack even on the synthetic items.

MuVGM: The Multimodal Video Game Music Dataset

MuVGM：結合 MIDI、原始音訊、遊玩影片、封面圖與文字描述的多模態電玩音樂資料集

Tyler H. McIntosh, Simon Dixon
DOI:10.5334/tismir.381 · TISMIR · 投稿submitted 2026-10-23
sourcecited by
為什麼挑這篇：倫敦瑪麗皇后大學的 MuVGM 主張電玩音樂是少數「為特定畫面與情境而寫」的音樂，所以適合研究音樂與脈絡的關係。資料集有 7,129 首曲子、1,484 款遊戲、39 個主機、橫跨 37 年，每首都配粉絲轉譜 MIDI、YouTube 原聲、20 秒遊玩影片、封面圖、LLM 生成的遊戲描述與音樂描述，以及由詞彙表查出的情緒值。用 ImageBind 嵌入做跨模態檢索，在 200 首候選中 R@1 零樣本是 0.1125，訓練一個小型雙編碼器後到 0.3162（隨機 0.005）。要保留的是：檢索查詢裡的音樂描述與標籤是 Qwen3-Omni 聽目標音訊生成的，等於把答案的一部分放進題目；MIDI 與音訊的相似度驗證也沒有隨機配對基線。Why this one: MuVGM, from Queen Mary University of London, argues that video game music is one of the few musics written for specific, observable scenes and contexts, which makes it well suited to studying music–context relationships. The dataset has 7,129 tracks from 1,484 games on 39 consoles across 37 years, each paired with a fan-transcribed MIDI, YouTube reference audio, a 20-second gameplay clip, box art, LLM-generated game descriptions and music captions, and affect values looked up from a lexicon. In cross-modal retrieval with ImageBind embeddings, R@1 among 200 candidates is 0.1125 zero-shot and 0.3162 after training a small bi-encoder (chance 0.005). The caveats: the captions and tags in the retrieval query were generated by Qwen3-Omni listening to the target audio, which puts part of the answer into the query; and the MIDI–audio similarity check has no random-pairing baseline.

動機Motivation

多模態音樂資料集多半只建立「機械性」或「描述性」的對應：演奏對樂譜、音訊對文字描述、影片對音訊。圖像和音樂的配對常靠嵌入相似度，例如 Art2Mus 用 ImageBind 空間的餘弦距離把畫作配上音樂，結果學到的是巧合的相似或知覺隱喻（視覺上的暖對應音色上的暖），而不是作曲意圖：音樂聽起來像那個場景，但不是為它而寫。

作者認為電玩音樂是例外：它是為特定的畫面、敘事與遊戲機制而作，這些環境可以直接觀察，作曲決策（引發情緒、標記角色與地點、維持主題連貫）都有明確的功能框架（Collins，2008）。但現有電玩音樂資料集都很窄，通常只有影片—MIDI 或音訊—MIDI 這類雙模態配對，沒有一個把所有模態整合起來。MuVGM 要補的就是這個缺口。

Most multimodal music datasets establish only 'mechanical' or 'descriptive' correspondences: performance with notation, audio with captions, video with audio. Image–music pairs are often made by embedding similarity; Art2Mus, for example, pairs artworks with music by cosine distance in ImageBind space, so what gets learned is coincidental resemblance or perceptual metaphor (visual warmth to timbral warmth), not compositional intent: the music sounds like the scene but was not written for it.

The authors argue video game music is the exception: it is composed for specific visuals, narratives, and game mechanics that can be directly observed, and compositional decisions (eliciting emotion, marking characters and places, keeping thematic coherence) sit inside explicit functional frameworks (Collins, 2008). Yet existing game-music datasets are narrow, usually bimodal pairs such as video–MIDI or audio–MIDI, and none brings all the modalities together. MuVGM is meant to fill that gap.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

電玩音樂資料集的直接前例：VGMIDI（Ferreira 與 Whitehead，2019）是附效價—喚醒度標註的鋼琴改編；NES-MDB（Donahue 等人，2018）有 5,200 首 NES 原曲與表現屬性；YM2413-MDB（Choi 等人，2022）是 669 首 FM 合成並附多標籤情緒。這些都只有音樂本身。最接近的是 NES-VMDB（Cardoso 等人，2024），把 NES-MDB 配上短遊玩片段做影片條件生成；GameVibe（Barthet 等人，2024）則把遊玩影片配上觀眾投入度曲線。作者的論點是它們只有「局部脈絡」（當下畫面），缺「全域脈絡」（整體敘事、封面這類副文本），而且 NES-VMDB 只有一個主機，MuVGM 有 39 個。

論文沒有特別強調、但最值得連起來看的是：MuVGM 的 MIDI 對音訊配對流程，跟 Raffel（2016）建 Lakh MIDI 的 LMD-aligned 是同一個問題，都是把網路上的使用者 MIDI 對到真實錄音、用比對分數篩掉錯的。Raffel 用的是 DTW 對齊的信心分數，MuVGM 改用 Essentia 的翻唱偵測（Serrà 等人的方法），並用 8bit 與 GeneralUser GS 兩套音色合成來吸收主機音色差異。音樂描述與標籤則是 LP-MusicCaps（Doh 等人，2023）那條偽標註路線，用 Qwen3-Omni 逐 30 秒段生成再由 Gemma 4 彙整。所以這篇的新意主要在整合與規模，而不在個別技術。

The direct game-music precedents: VGMIDI (Ferreira and Whitehead, 2019) provides piano arrangements with valence–arousal annotations; NES-MDB (Donahue et al., 2018) has 5,200 NES compositions with expressive attributes; YM2413-MDB (Choi et al., 2022) has 669 FM-synthesis pieces with multi-label emotion tags. All cover only the music itself. The closest is NES-VMDB (Cardoso et al., 2024), which pairs NES-MDB with short gameplay clips for video-conditioned generation; GameVibe (Barthet et al., 2024) links gameplay footage to viewer-engagement traces. The authors' argument is that these capture only local context (the on-screen moment), not global context (overall narrative, paratexts such as box art), and NES-VMDB covers one console where MuVGM covers 39.

What the paper does not emphasize, but is most worth connecting: MuVGM's MIDI-to-audio pairing is the same problem Raffel (2016) solved for LMD-aligned in the Lakh MIDI dataset, matching user-made MIDI from the web to real recordings and discarding wrong matches by a score. Raffel used a DTW alignment confidence; MuVGM uses Essentia's cover song detection (the method of Serrà et al.) and renders each MIDI with two soundfonts, 8bit and GeneralUser GS, to absorb console timbre differences. The captions and tags follow the LP-MusicCaps (Doh et al., 2023) pseudo-labeling route, with Qwen3-Omni captioning each 30-second chunk and Gemma 4 merging them. The novelty is therefore mainly integration and scale, not any single technique.

方法Method

六階段流程與每階段保留率：一、從 VGMusic.com 抓粉絲轉譜 MIDI，去掉重混、組曲、重編曲與 10 秒以下的音效，得到 3,074 款遊戲的 16,844 首。二、用 MediaWiki 搜尋 API 對每款遊戲找維基百科頁面，以 difflib 字串相似度大於 0.6 為準，取得類型、模式、年份與封面，剩 13,557 首（80.5%）。

三、在 YouTube 搜尋原聲，每首最多評估 10 個候選，用 Essentia 翻唱偵測比對 MIDI 合成音，第一個信心超過 0.90 的就採用，剩 11,480 首（84.7%）。四、找整部遊戲的實況錄影（longplay），用 audfprint 指紋以原聲當查詢定位曲子出現的時間點，至少 20 個共同雜湊才算數，切出 20 秒 480p 片段；超過 13.5 小時的影片要切成 12 小時段，避免時間戳溢位，剩 7,129 首（62.1%）。

五、用 Gemma 4 26B 加檢索增強生成，為每款遊戲寫敘事、機制、玩家體驗三段描述，沒有附來源的回應一律拒收。六、原聲切成 30 秒段由 Qwen3-Omni 30B 描述，再由 Gemma 4 彙整成一段描述加五個音樂標籤與五個情緒標籤；情緒詞查 NRC-VAD 詞彙表 v2.1 取效價、喚醒、支配度的平均與標準差。

The six stages and their retention: (1) fan-transcribed MIDI from VGMusic.com, removing remixes, medleys, re-orchestrations, and sound effects under 10 seconds, gives 16,844 tracks from 3,074 games. (2) Each game is matched to a Wikipedia page through the MediaWiki Search API with a difflib string similarity above 0.6, collecting genre, modes, year, and box art; 13,557 tracks remain (80.5%).

(3) Reference audio is searched on YouTube, evaluating up to 10 candidates per track with Essentia cover song detection against MIDI renders and taking the first one above 0.90 confidence; 11,480 remain (84.7%). (4) Longplay recordings are searched and audfprint fingerprinting, with the reference audio as query, locates when each track plays, requiring at least 20 common hashes, before a 20-second 480p clip is cut; videos over 13.5 hours are split into 12-hour chunks to avoid timestamp wraparound; 7,129 remain (62.1%).

(5) Gemma 4 26B with retrieval-augmented generation writes three paragraphs per game, on narrative, mechanics, and player experience, rejecting any response without sources. (6) The audio is split into 30-second chunks captioned by Qwen3-Omni 30B, then merged by Gemma 4 into one caption plus five musical and five emotion tags; the emotion words are looked up in the NRC-VAD Lexicon v2.1 to give the mean and standard deviation of valence, arousal, and dominance.

限制Limitations

跨模態檢索實驗有一個沒被處理的洩漏。查詢用的「曲目層級脈絡」包含音樂描述與標籤，而這些是 Qwen3-Omni 聽目標音訊本身生成的。所以「從遊戲脈絡找回音樂」有一部分其實是「從音訊的描述找回音訊」。CKA 也顯示音訊和描述（0.50）、標籤（0.48）的對齊比和影片（0.39）高。論文的權重消融只比了「只用曲目」（R@1 0.0941）和「只用遊戲」（0.0801），沒有單獨測「只用影片」或「拿掉描述」，而那才是能回答「脈絡本身有沒有訊號」的條件。同樣地，類型分類（micro F1 0.66、macro 0.49）與多人模式分類（正確率 0.76）用的遊戲嵌入含有 LLM 寫的機制描述，很可能直接寫了類型與多人模式，論文沒有做不含文字的探針。

MIDI 與音訊的驗證缺基線。表 2 的餘弦相似度 MFCC 0.8954、chroma 0.7790、CLAP 0.7019，作者說「相似度高、對應良好」，但沒有報告隨機配對（例如同主機不同曲）的相似度；chroma 與 MFCC 平均向量在調性音樂之間本來就容易很像，沒有對照就無法判斷 0.78 算高還是低。

篩選偏誤：最後只保留了第一階段的 42%（7,129／16,844），最大一刀在影片階段（62.1%），等於偏向有完整實況錄影的熱門遊戲。粉絲轉譜品質不一，作者自己舉了 Kid Icarus 標題曲被轉成比 NES 硬體能發出的更密的編曲。情緒值是「模型挑的詞、人類評的尺」，作者明確說這不是人類資料，也沒有任何人工驗證。版權上採學術申請制，不是開放授權。另外，第二作者 Simon Dixon 是 TISMIR 前任共同主編，論文已在利益衝突聲明中揭露。

The cross-modal retrieval experiment has an unaddressed leak. The 'track-level context' in the query includes the caption and tags, which Qwen3-Omni generated by listening to the target audio itself. So 'retrieving music from game context' is partly 'retrieving audio from a description of that audio'. CKA agrees: audio aligns more with captions (0.50) and tags (0.48) than with video (0.39). The weight ablation compares only track-only (R@1 0.0941) and game-only (0.0801) queries, with no video-only or no-caption condition, which is what would show whether the context itself carries signal. Likewise, genre classification (micro F1 0.66, macro 0.49) and multiplayer classification (accuracy 0.76) use game embeddings that include LLM-written mechanics descriptions, which likely state the genre and multiplayer modes outright; there is no text-free probe.

The MIDI–audio validation lacks a baseline. Table 2's cosine similarities are MFCC 0.8954, chroma 0.7790, and CLAP 0.7019, and the authors call this good correspondence, but they report no random-pairing similarity (e.g. different tracks from the same console); averaged chroma and MFCC vectors tend to look alike across tonal music anyway, so without a control there is no telling whether 0.78 is high.

Selection bias: only 42% of the stage-one tracks survive (7,129 of 16,844), with the biggest cut at the video stage (62.1%), favoring popular games with full longplays. Fan transcription quality varies; the authors' own example is the Kid Icarus title theme transcribed denser than the NES hardware could play. The affect values are 'model-chosen words on a human-rated scale'; the authors say plainly this is not human data, and there is no human validation. Access is by academic request, not an open license. Also, second author Simon Dixon is a former co-editor-in-chief of TISMIR, which the paper discloses as a competing interest.

討論Discussion

探索性分析裡最有用的發現是：主機世代是所有模態裡最主要的解釋因素，發行年份幾乎沒有獨立貢獻。控制主機世代之後，最強特徵與年份的相關掉了超過 90%，所以作者建議把主機而不是年份當作時間描述變數。這對想用這個資料集做「電玩音樂演變」研究的人是實用的提醒；相對地，類型主要反映在音色與封面視覺上，而不是音符層級的結構，多人模式則幾乎沒有影響。資料也相當偏向早期：1995 年以前的遊戲佔 50.7%，最常見的音樂標籤是 arpeggiated（3,677）與 chiptune（3,600），情緒標籤是 triumphant（3,390）與 playful（3,388）。

檢索數字本身值得一看：200 首候選中，只用封面與遊戲描述的嵌入 R@1 就有 0.0801，約隨機的 16 倍；訓練雙編碼器（遊戲層級五折切分）後 R@1 0.3162±0.0235，1,000 首候選時 0.1521（隨機 0.001）。但如前述，在拆掉自生成描述之前，這些數字不能直接解讀成「遊戲脈絡能預測音樂」。主機分類只有 0.53 正確率（39 類，macro F1 0.32），作者也注意到這和探索性分析裡主機效應最大互相矛盾，推測是 ImageBind 嵌入沒有好好編碼主機身分；論文沒有報告多數類基線，0.53 的意義也難以判斷。

整體來說，MuVGM 的價值在於規模與模態廣度，以及把每個階段的錯誤模式寫得很誠實（例如 375 個封面比例異常中 144 個是真的配錯、已手動修正）。如果要拿來訓練「依畫面生成配樂」的模型，最可靠的配對是影片片段與音訊，因為那是由指紋直接對時間；LLM 生成的描述與情緒值則應該照作者建議，只當條件或啟發，不當預測目標。

The most useful exploratory finding is that console generation is the dominant explanatory factor in every modality, and release year contributes almost nothing on its own. After controlling for console generation, the strongest features' correlations with year fall by over 90%, so the authors recommend console rather than year as the temporal descriptor. That is a practical warning for anyone studying the evolution of game music with this dataset; genre, by contrast, shows up mainly in timbre and box-art visuals rather than note-level structure, and multiplayer has almost no effect. The data also skews early: pre-1995 games are 50.7%, the commonest musical tags are arpeggiated (3,677) and chiptune (3,600), and the commonest emotion tags triumphant (3,390) and playful (3,388).

The retrieval numbers are worth a look: among 200 candidates, embeddings from box art and game descriptions alone reach R@1 0.0801, about 16 times chance; a trained bi-encoder (five-fold game-level split) reaches R@1 0.3162±0.0235, and 0.1521 among 1,000 candidates (chance 0.001). But as noted above, until the self-generated captions are removed, these numbers cannot be read as 'game context predicts the music'. Console classification reaches only 0.53 accuracy (39 classes, macro F1 0.32); the authors notice the contradiction with console having the largest exploratory effect and suspect ImageBind does not encode console identity well, but there is no majority-class baseline, so 0.53 is hard to interpret.

Overall, MuVGM's value is scale and modality breadth, plus an honest record of each stage's error modes (e.g. of 375 box-art aspect-ratio outliers, 144 were genuine mismatches and were fixed by hand). For training models that generate music from visuals, the most reliable pairing is the video clip with the audio, since fingerprinting aligns them in time directly; the LLM-generated descriptions and affect values should, as the authors recommend, serve as conditioning or heuristics, not prediction targets.
