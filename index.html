Point-and-Play: Instrument Timbre Cloning from Music Mixtures

Point-and-Play：直接從多樂器混音中複製指定樂器的音色——以提示門控統計池化（CGSP）從 LoRA 微調的 CLAP 嵌入抽出音色碼，再驅動流匹配單音合成器演奏新音高

Baotong Tian, Zhiyao Duan
arXiv:2610.19445 · cs.SD; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-14
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：Rochester 的 Duan 團隊把「從一段完整混音裡指定一把樂器、把它變成可彈奏的音源」定成一個新任務，跳過先分離再複製的兩段式流程，直接從混音的 CLAP 表徵抽出音色碼。在 27 個未見過的 NSynth 樂器上，文字提示版的 CLAP 分數是 0.706，高於 CLAPSep 分離後再複製的 0.560 與 Demucs 的 0.541，離「用乾淨錄音的另一個音」這個上限 0.762 還差 0.056；但仍低於用真實分軌算理想遮罩的 0.726。值得讀的是任務定義清楚、四個損失各自的作用在消融裡一目了然；要保留的是評估全靠 CLAP 家族的嵌入、沒有聽測，而且混音是人工拼的四個 NSynth 單音。Why this one: Duan's group at Rochester defines a new task, turning one cued instrument inside a full mixture into a playable instrument, and skips the separate-then-clone pipeline by extracting a timbre code directly from the mixture's CLAP representation. On 27 unseen NSynth instruments the text-cued version scores CLAP 0.706, above separate-then-clone with CLAPSep (0.560) and Demucs (0.541), and 0.056 short of the 'clean recording of another note' upper bound of 0.762; it remains below an oracle ratio mask built from the true stem (0.726). It is worth reading for a clear task definition and an ablation that shows what each of the four losses does; the caveats are that evaluation relies entirely on CLAP-family embeddings, there is no listening test, and the mixtures are synthetic sets of four NSynth notes.

動機Motivation

神經樂器合成器已經能從一段獨奏錄音學會某把樂器的音色，再用新的音高與力度去演奏它。但製作人真正想要的音色，常常只出現在一首混好的多樂器歌裡：一段弦樂、一把特定音色的電吉他，旁邊永遠有鼓、貝斯和人聲。現有方法碰不到這種素材。

直覺的做法是先做音源分離，再把分出來的軌當成獨奏參考去複製。作者指出這條路有三個問題：多數分離器只會分固定的幾軌（人聲、鼓、貝斯、其他），對任意樂器不適用；分離的殘留雜訊會一路傳到合成器；而且重建波形本身不是目標，使用者要的是音色，不是那條分軌。因此本文主張直接從混音推出音色碼，中間不經過重建。

Neural instrument synthesizers can already learn an instrument's timbre from a solo recording and play it at new pitches and velocities. But the timbre a producer actually wants often appears only inside a fully mixed multi-instrument song: a string pad or a particular electric guitar, always surrounded by drums, bass and vocals. Existing methods cannot reach that material.

The obvious route is to separate first and then clone from the separated stem. The authors name three problems with it: most separators recover only a fixed set of stems (vocals, drums, bass, other) and do not handle arbitrary instruments; separation artifacts propagate into the synthesizer; and reconstructing a waveform is not the goal anyway, since the user wants the timbre, not the stem. The paper therefore infers a timbre code directly from the mixture, with no intermediate reconstruction.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

合成端的前作很明確。TokenSynth（Kim 等，ICASSP 2025）用 CLAP 音色嵌入加 MIDI 生成神經編碼器 token，能從獨奏參考複製樂器；FlowSynth（Yang 等，ICASSP 2026）以分佈式流匹配加上測試時搜尋提升單音品質，本文的合成器就是照它的架構做的。這兩者的參考音訊都必須是乾淨的獨奏。分離端則有 CLAPSep（Ma 等，2024），把文字或音訊提示放進 CLAP 空間去條件化一個遮罩網路，本文拿它當主要的兩段式對照。

真正最像的前作有兩個，論文都有引用但沒正面比較。DisMix（Luo 等，2024）已經能用提示從混音中拆出每個音源的音高與音色潛在表徵並重新組合；差別在 DisMix 是在原混音裡換音色，本文是把音色帶出混音、彈出混音裡沒有的新音。另一個是 Kim 等（ICASSP 2023）的混音樂器檢索，它訓練的正是「從混音嵌入找出裡面有哪些樂器」的編碼器，本文還沿用了它的 NLakh 資料。

至於新提出的 CGSP 模組，最接近的既有技巧是語者辨識裡的注意力統計池化（attentive statistics pooling，Okabe 等，2018）：同樣是加權平均加加權標準差。本文的改動是權重由外部提示決定、用各自獨立的 sigmoid 而非 softmax，並把結果當殘差加回提示嵌入上。

The synthesis-side predecessors are clear. TokenSynth (Kim et al., ICASSP 2025) generates neural codec tokens from MIDI and a CLAP timbre embedding and can clone an instrument from a solo reference; FlowSynth (Yang et al., ICASSP 2026) improves single-note quality with distributional flow matching and test-time search, and this paper's synthesizer follows its architecture. Both require a clean solo reference. On the separation side, CLAPSep (Ma et al., 2024) places a text or audio cue in CLAP space to condition a masking network, and serves here as the main two-stage comparison.

The two closest predecessors are cited but not compared head-to-head. DisMix (Luo et al., 2024) already uses a query to disentangle per-source pitch and timbre latents from a mixture and recombine them; the difference is that DisMix swaps timbre within the original mixture, while this paper carries the timbre out of the mixture to play new notes. The other is mixture-based instrument retrieval (Kim et al., ICASSP 2023), which trains exactly an encoder that identifies which instruments a mixture contains; this paper even reuses its NLakh data.

The proposed CGSP module is closest to attentive statistics pooling from speaker verification (Okabe et al., 2018): a weighted mean plus a weighted standard deviation. The changes here are that the weights come from an external cue, use independent sigmoids rather than a softmax, and the result is added as a residual to the cue embedding.

方法Method

系統分兩段，分開訓練。第一段是音色抽取器：混音分支用音樂領域的 LAION-CLAP（HTSAT-base）音訊塔，以 rank-8 LoRA 微調，輸出一個全域碼 g 和一串 token；提示分支是凍結的 CLAP 音訊或文字編碼器，把提示（一段同類樂器的錄音，或像「guitar」這樣的類別名）映到共用空間。CGSP 先算每個 token 與提示的內積，經 sigmoid 得到門控值；再用門控值對 token 做加權平均與加權標準差；最後由 MLP 把兩者轉成殘差，加到正規化後的提示嵌入上。MLP 最後一層初始化為零，所以訓練一開始輸出就等於提示本身，再慢慢學會從混音裡補上這把樂器特有的資訊。

訓練用四個損失。L_inv 讓同樂器組合、不同演奏內容的兩段混音的全域碼靠近，目的是讓表徵不受音高與旋律影響；L_algn^a 與 L_algn^t 分別把音訊提示與文字提示得到的音色碼，對齊到該樂器單獨錄音的 CLAP 嵌入（InfoNCE，溫度 0.1）。音訊提示刻意取自同類別的另一把樂器，逼模型從混音裡找，而不是照抄提示。L_sel^a 用錯誤類別的音訊提示去抽，若結果太像目標就以 hinge 懲罰。權重是 1、1、1、0.25，單張 A100 訓練 18k 步約三小時。

第二段是合成器：凍結抽取器後，訓練一個 24 層、寬 1,024 的 DiT，以 AdaLN 接收「音色碼投影 + 音高嵌入 + 力度嵌入」，在 DiffRhythm 的 VAE 潛在空間做條件流匹配，訓練 150k 步。推論時跑 50 步 Euler，每個條件生成 8 個候選，保留音樂 CLAP 嵌入與音色碼最相似的那個。

The system has two stages trained separately. Stage one is the timbre extractor. The mixture branch is the HTSAT-base audio tower of a music-domain LAION-CLAP checkpoint, fine-tuned with rank-8 LoRA, producing a global code g and a token sequence. The cue branch is a frozen CLAP audio or text encoder that maps the cue (a recording of another instrument in the same category, or a category name such as 'guitar') into the shared space. CGSP computes a sigmoid gate from each token's dot product with the cue, takes the gate-weighted mean and standard deviation of the tokens, and an MLP turns those into a residual added to the normalized cue embedding. The MLP's last layer is zero-initialized, so the output starts as the cue itself and gradually learns the mixture evidence specific to this instrument.

Four losses train it. L_inv pulls together the global codes of two mixtures with the same instruments but different content, to make the representation invariant to pitch and melody. L_algn^a and L_algn^t align the audio-cued and text-cued codes with the CLAP embedding of the isolated instrument (InfoNCE, temperature 0.1). The audio cue is deliberately a different instrument from the same category, forcing the model to look in the mixture rather than copy the cue. L_sel^a extracts with a wrong-category audio cue and applies a hinge penalty if the result is too close to the target. Weights are 1, 1, 1 and 0.25; training takes 18k steps, about three hours on one A100.

Stage two is the synthesizer. With the extractor frozen, a 24-layer, 1,024-wide DiT receives 'projected timbre code + pitch embedding + velocity embedding' via AdaLN and is trained with conditional flow matching in the DiffRhythm VAE latent space for 150k steps. Inference runs 50 Euler steps, generates 8 candidates per condition and keeps the one whose music-CLAP embedding is closest to the timbre code.

限制Limitations

評估的尺幾乎全來自同一個家族。主指標是生成音與目標音的 CLAP 餘弦相似度；作者有刻意改用通用音訊的 HTSAT-tiny 檢查點來量，避開訓練用的那個，但推論時的 8 選 1 是用音樂 CLAP 挑的，抽取器的訓練目標也是對齊 CLAP 嵌入，這種「用 CLAP 選、用 CLAP 量」的設計會讓 CLAP 分數偏好本方法。沒有任何聽測，作者把它列為未來工作。

比較的條件也不完全對等。分離再複製與提示基線用的是「以乾淨分軌 CLAP 嵌入訓練」的解碼器，Point-and-Play 用的是同架構、但以自家音色碼訓練的解碼器，兩邊的生成器不是同一個。FAD 的說法也要打折：音訊提示版的 FAD 是 1.92，作者稱它接近上限的 1.85；但完全不看混音、只用同類別另一把樂器當提示的基線 FAD 是 1.82，反而更好。換句話說，FAD 主要反映「生成的是不是像樣的樂器音」，分不出音色有沒有抓對。

資料面的限制更大。主結果只用 27 個保留的 NSynth 樂器，混音是隨機挑四把樂器的單音疊在一起，離真實製作的歌很遠；MoisesDB 只出現在混音長度的檢索實驗裡。文字提示是粗類別名，混音裡若有兩把同類樂器就無法指定，作者在結論中承認。音高也沒有完全去掉：用嶺迴歸從音色碼預測音高，R² 從乾淨嵌入的 0.783 降到 0.263，但不是零。

The measuring sticks nearly all come from one family. The primary metric is CLAP cosine similarity between generated and target notes. The authors deliberately measure with a general-audio HTSAT-tiny checkpoint rather than the training one, but the 8-way candidate selection at inference uses music CLAP, and the extractor itself is trained to align with CLAP embeddings, so 'select by CLAP, score by CLAP' favors this method on CLAP score. There is no listening test; the authors list it as future work.

The comparison conditions are also not fully matched. The separate-then-clone and cue baselines use a decoder trained on clean-stem CLAP embeddings, while Point-and-Play uses the same architecture trained on its own codes, so the generators differ. The FAD claim needs discounting too: the audio-cued version has FAD 1.92, which the authors call close to the 1.85 upper bound, but the baseline that ignores the mixture entirely and just uses another same-category instrument as the cue scores 1.82, which is better. FAD mainly reflects whether the output sounds like a plausible instrument note, not whether the timbre is the right one.

The data limits are larger. The main results use only 27 held-out NSynth instruments, with mixtures made by stacking single notes from four random instruments, far from a produced song; MoisesDB appears only in the mixture-duration retrieval study. Text cues are coarse category names, so two same-category instruments in one mixture cannot be told apart, as the conclusion admits. Pitch is not fully removed: a ridge regressor predicting pitch from the code drops from R² 0.783 on clean embeddings to 0.263, but not to zero.

討論Discussion

主表的數字支持「直接抽取勝過兩段式」這個核心主張，至少在這個設定下。CLAP 分數上，文字提示版 0.706、同類音訊提示 0.678、目標樂器音訊提示 0.696，三者都高於 CLAPSep（0.560）與 Demucs 挑最接近的那軌（0.541）；MSS 也從兩段式的 3.55 降到 2.64–2.75。只用提示、不看混音的基線最高只有 0.634（類別平均嵌入），代表抽取器確實從混音裡拿到了這把樂器特有的資訊，而不只是猜類別。

消融把四個損失的分工講得很清楚。拿掉 L_algn^a，音訊提示的分數從 0.708 掉到 0.417，比錯誤提示的 0.430 還低；拿掉 L_algn^t，文字提示從 0.699 掉到 0.578，但另一種提示幾乎不受影響，說明兩個對齊損失各管一種模態。拿掉 L_sel^a，錯誤音訊提示的分數從 0.430 升到 0.469，選擇性變差，同時正確提示也降到 0.664。拿掉 L_inv 則兩種提示都下降（0.654、0.640）。

有兩個細節值得注意。第一，用目標樂器本身當音訊提示（0.696）並沒有比文字提示（0.706）好，可見提示主要在「指出是哪一類」，音色細節是從混音補上的，這正是 CGSP 零初始化殘差設計想要的行為。第二，理想遮罩的 0.726 仍高於本方法，表示如果分離器夠好，兩段式並非沒有空間；本文贏的是現有、實際可用的分離器。

The main table supports the core claim that direct extraction beats the two-stage pipeline, at least in this setting. On CLAP score the text-cued version reaches 0.706, the same-category audio cue 0.678 and the target-instrument audio cue 0.696, all above CLAPSep (0.560) and Demucs-closest-stem (0.541); MSS drops from 3.55 for the two-stage systems to 2.64–2.75. Cue-only baselines that ignore the mixture top out at 0.634 (the category-centroid embedding), so the extractor does recover instrument-specific information from the mixture rather than just guessing the category.

The ablation spells out how the four losses divide the work. Without L_algn^a, the audio-cued score falls from 0.708 to 0.417, below the wrong-cue score of 0.430; without L_algn^t, the text-cued score falls from 0.699 to 0.578 while the other modality is barely affected, showing each alignment loss serves one modality. Without L_sel^a, the wrong-audio-cue score rises from 0.430 to 0.469, so selectivity worsens, and the matched cue also drops to 0.664. Removing L_inv lowers both cues (0.654 and 0.640).

Two details stand out. First, using the exact target instrument as the audio cue (0.696) is no better than the text cue (0.706), so the cue mainly says which category to look for and timbre detail comes from the mixture, which is what the zero-initialized residual in CGSP is designed to do. Second, the oracle ratio mask at 0.726 still beats this method, so a good enough separator would leave room for the two-stage approach; what this paper beats is the separators actually available today.

Learning Where to Split: Dynamic Band Splitting for Audio Source Separation

學會在哪裡切：動態頻帶切分（DBS）——固定 token 預算下以 Hard Top-K 與直通估計逐段預測頻帶邊界，套在 BSRNN、TIGER、BS-RoFormer、Mel-RoFormer 上

Bingzhe Zhang, Kai Li, Xiaolin Hu
arXiv:2610.14993 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：清華團隊讓頻帶切分式分離器（BSRNN 一系）的頻帶邊界隨每段混音改變，同時固定 token 數。同架構、同訓練下對上均勻切分的對照，DnR v2 平均 SI-SDR 多 1.07 dB；把訓練好的模型換回 BSRNN 原始切法，分數從 9.07 掉到 8.62 dB——這兩組數字是「邊界位置本身有用」最乾淨的證據。摘要裡的 29.0% 則是對論文表中的 BSRNN 原始數字（7.03 dB）算的，對自己重訓的 128 頻帶 BSRNN 是 7.69→9.07（約 18%）。要保留的是：token 數固定不等於計算量固定，DnR v2 上 DBS-BSRNN 的 MACs 從 76.6 升到 124.0 G/s；MUSDB18-HQ 的靜態基線分數也明顯偏低。Why this one: A Tsinghua team lets the band boundaries of band-split separators (the BSRNN family) change with each mixture while keeping the token count fixed. Against a uniform-split control with the same architecture and training, average SI-SDR on DnR v2 rises by 1.07 dB; swapping the trained model's boundaries back to the original BSRNN layout drops it from 9.07 to 8.62 dB. Those two numbers are the cleanest evidence that boundary placement itself matters. The 29.0% in the abstract is computed against the BSRNN figure listed in the paper's table (7.03 dB); against the authors' own retrained 128-band BSRNN it is 7.69 to 9.07 (about 18%). The caveats: a fixed token count is not a fixed compute budget, as DBS-BSRNN's MACs on DnR v2 rise from 76.6 to 124.0 G/s, and the static baselines on MUSDB18-HQ score noticeably low.

動機Motivation

頻帶切分（band split）是近年音樂分離最主流的前端：BSRNN 把 STFT 的頻率軸依人工設計的非均勻切法分成幾十個頻帶，每個頻帶壓成一個固定維度的 token，後面的網路只需在頻帶序列上建模，比在上千個頻率 bin 上直接處理便宜得多。BS-RoFormer、Mel-RoFormer、TIGER 都沿用這個思路。

問題在於切法是寫死的，每段混音都用同一組邊界。但有意義的頻譜結構會隨內容移動：一段以人聲為主的片段，和一段充滿打擊樂與音效的片段，需要細切的位置不同。固定邊界可能把兩個該分開的諧波塞進同一個寬頻帶，卻在沒什麼內容的地方浪費細切的 token。直接增加頻帶數可以提高解析度，但分離器的成本也跟著漲。本文要回答的是：在 token 數不變的前提下，能不能讓模型自己決定每段混音該在哪裡切。

Band splitting has become the dominant front end in music separation. BSRNN divides the STFT frequency axis into a few dozen bands using a hand-designed non-uniform layout, compresses each band into a fixed-dimensional token, and the downstream network only models the band sequence, which is far cheaper than working on a thousand frequency bins. BS-RoFormer, Mel-RoFormer and TIGER all follow this idea.

The problem is that the layout is fixed: every mixture uses the same boundaries. Informative spectral structure moves with content, so a vocal-dominated passage and a passage full of percussion and sound effects need fine resolution in different places. A fixed layout can merge two harmonics that should be separate into one wide band while spending fine-grained tokens where little is happening. Adding more bands raises resolution but also raises the separator's cost. The question here is whether, with the token count held constant, the model can decide for itself where to split each mixture.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接前作是頻帶切分這一系。BSRNN（Luo & Yu，2023）提出人工非均勻切法；BS-RoFormer（Lu 等，2024）把 RNN 換成 RoPE Transformer；Mel-RoFormer（Wang 等，2023）改用彼此重疊的 Mel 頻帶；TIGER（Xu 等，2025）是輕量版。SCNet（Tong 等，2024）則把頻帶壓縮做進階層式編碼器–解碼器。這些方法的切法都是設計時決定、所有輸入共用；DBS 的差別就是讓切法隨輸入改變，並保留原始 STFT 解析度去估遮罩。

另一條相關線是可學習前端。LEAF（Zeghidour 等，2021）與本文比較的可學習濾波器組（Dai 等，2023）會在訓練中學出濾波器，但學完後對所有輸入仍是同一組；Saijo & Bando（2026）的頻譜特徵壓縮（SFC）用交叉注意力或 Mamba 取代頻帶編碼器，不再有明確的頻帶。DBS 介於兩者之間：保留連續、互不重疊的頻帶，只讓邊界動。

本文沒有引用、但概念上最接近的是語言模型裡的動態 token 池化（Nawrot 等，2023）：同樣是用一個邊界預測器在序列上決定切點，再把每段池化成一個 token，並以直通估計傳梯度。DBS 等於把這套做法搬到頻率軸，並加上「每個頻率區塊固定配額」的 Hard Top-K，確保 token 數不變。

The direct predecessors are the band-split family. BSRNN (Luo & Yu, 2023) introduced the hand-designed non-uniform layout; BS-RoFormer (Lu et al., 2024) replaced the RNN with RoPE Transformers; Mel-RoFormer (Wang et al., 2023) uses overlapping Mel bands; TIGER (Xu et al., 2025) is a lightweight variant. SCNet (Tong et al., 2024) builds band compression into a hierarchical encoder-decoder. In all of them the layout is fixed at design time and shared across inputs; DBS differs by making the layout depend on the input while keeping full STFT resolution for mask estimation.

A second related line is learnable front ends. LEAF (Zeghidour et al., 2021) and the learnable filterbank compared here (Dai et al., 2023) learn filters during training, but once trained they apply the same filters to every input. Spectral Feature Compression (SFC) by Saijo & Bando (2026) replaces the band encoder with cross-attention or Mamba and no longer has explicit bands. DBS sits between the two: it keeps contiguous, non-overlapping bands and only moves the boundaries.

Not cited, but conceptually closest, is dynamic token pooling in language models (Nawrot et al., 2023): a boundary predictor chooses split points along a sequence, each segment is pooled into one token, and gradients pass through a straight-through estimator. DBS essentially moves that recipe onto the frequency axis and adds a Hard Top-K with a fixed quota per frequency block so the token count never changes.

方法Method

切分。 頻率軸先被分成四個固定區塊（B1–B4），每個區塊有固定的頻帶配額，例如 DnR v2 與 MUSDB18-HQ 是 1025 個 bin 配 128 個頻帶。區塊內的邊界由預測器決定：它對實部與虛部做時間卷積，再算每個 bin 的三個能量統計（時間平均、峰均比、標準差均值比），經 BiLSTM 得到每個 bin 的上下文向量。相鄰兩個 bin 之間的邊界分數由兩項組成：學到的特徵差異，加上 0.15 倍的能量統計變化（論文稱為聲學轉換先驗）；再經可學習的尺度與位置偏置轉成機率。Hard Top-K 從每個區塊挑出配額減一個最高分的位置，加上區塊起點，就得到剛好該數量的連續頻帶。

編碼與分離。 每個 bin 先編碼成 D 維特徵，同一頻帶內的 bin 以學到的權重加權池化成一個 token，權重在時間上共用。這樣不論頻帶多寬，每個頻帶在每個影格都只產生一個 D 維 token，可以直接餵進原本的 BSRNN、TIGER、BS-RoFormer 或 Mel-RoFormer。四個區塊的 token 放在同一條序列裡，可以跨區塊互動。

還原。 分離後的 token 先做「機率引導平滑」：依該頻帶的平均邊界機率，和前一個頻帶的 token 內插，邊界越不確定就越往前一個靠，避免展開後出現階梯狀跳變。接著用同一組切分把 token 複製回每個 bin，再經一個沿頻率方向的殘差卷積區塊（深度可分離卷積，核大小 3）讓相鄰 bin 互相混合，最後由跨頻率共用的解碼頭輸出每個 bin 的複數遮罩。

訓練。 Hard Top-K 不可微，所以前向用硬切分，反向則對一個局部的軟指派求導（直通估計），另有一條不改變前向值的「信心路徑」補梯度給邊界機率。再加一個比例損失，讓每個區塊的平均邊界機率接近目標壓縮比（權重 0.01）。

Splitting. The frequency axis is first divided into four fixed blocks (B1–B4), each with a fixed band quota; on DnR v2 and MUSDB18-HQ, 1025 bins map to 128 bands. Boundaries within a block come from a predictor that applies temporal convolutions to the real and imaginary parts, computes three energy statistics per bin (temporal mean, peak-to-mean and std-to-mean ratios), and runs a BiLSTM to get a context vector per bin. The score between two adjacent bins is a learned feature difference plus 0.15 times the change in energy statistics (the 'acoustic transition prior'), turned into a probability by a learned scale and position bias. Hard Top-K picks the quota-minus-one highest positions in each block, plus the block start, yielding exactly the required number of contiguous bands.

Encoding and separation. Each bin is encoded into a D-dimensional feature, and the bins in a band are pooled with learned weights, shared across time, into one token. However wide a band is, it yields one D-dimensional token per frame, which feeds unchanged BSRNN, TIGER, BS-RoFormer or Mel-RoFormer backbones. Tokens from all four blocks share one sequence, so they can interact across blocks.

Reconstruction. Separated tokens first get 'probability-guided smoothing': each is interpolated with the previous band's token according to the band's mean boundary probability, leaning toward the previous token when the boundary is uncertain, to avoid step-like jumps after expansion. The same partition then copies each token back to its bins, a residual frequency-direction block (depthwise convolution, kernel 3) mixes neighboring bins, and a frequency-shared head outputs a complex mask per bin.

Training. Hard Top-K is not differentiable, so the forward pass uses the hard partition and the backward pass differentiates a local soft assignment (straight-through estimation); a value-preserving 'confidence path' adds gradient to the boundary probabilities without changing forward values. A ratio loss (weight 0.01) keeps each block's mean boundary probability near a target compression ratio.

限制Limitations

token 固定不等於計算量固定。 論文的賣點是「固定頻帶 token 預算」，但還原端的頻率細化是在完整 1025 個 bin 上做的，計算量因此上升。DnR v2 上 DBS-BSRNN 是 124.0 G MACs/s，靜態 128 頻帶 BSRNN 是 76.6（多 62%）；DBS-TIGER 從 20.4 升到 31.6；MUSDB18-HQ 上 DBS-MelRoFormer 從 171.7 升到 252.9。另一方面參數量大幅下降（DnR v2 上 BSRNN 從 35.5M 降到 4.7M），因為逐頻帶的專屬 MLP 換成了跨頻率共用的解碼頭。參數與計算一起變，很難說增益只來自邊界。

基線偏弱。 MUSDB18-HQ 上所有模型都是作者以四軌聯合、不用外部資料、學習率 1e-5、每張卡 batch 1 重新訓練的。靜態 BS-RoFormer 只有 4.50 dB，比參數少得多的靜態 BSRNN（5.09）還低，也遠低於原論文報告的單軌模型成績，看起來是訓練不足。在這種基線上的 1.84 dB 增益，不能直接外推到調好的系統。EchoSet 則是拿自己的 DBS 模型對照文獻上的 BSRNN 與 TIGER 數字，不是同條件重訓。

系統很脆弱。 拿掉頻率細化，平均 SI-SDR 從 9.07 掉到 3.92；拿掉聲學轉換先驗掉到 4.09，都遠低於靜態 BSRNN 的 7.69。這代表 DBS 不是一個隨插即用的小改動，元件之間高度相依；聲學先驗的係數 0.15 也是手設的。全文沒有報告多個種子或變異數。作者自列的限制是頻帶數固定、每個推論窗只有一組切法；試過讓頻帶數也可學，結果模型總是選最大配額。

Fixed tokens are not fixed compute. The selling point is a fixed band-token budget, but reconstruction refines features over all 1025 bins, which raises compute. On DnR v2, DBS-BSRNN costs 124.0 G MACs/s versus 76.6 for the static 128-band BSRNN (62% more); DBS-TIGER goes from 20.4 to 31.6; on MUSDB18-HQ DBS-MelRoFormer goes from 171.7 to 252.9. Parameter counts, meanwhile, drop sharply (BSRNN on DnR v2 from 35.5M to 4.7M) because per-band MLPs are replaced by a frequency-shared head. With both parameters and compute changing, it is hard to attribute the gain to boundaries alone.

Weak baselines. On MUSDB18-HQ every model was retrained by the authors as a joint four-stem model with no external data, learning rate 1e-5 and batch size one per GPU. Static BS-RoFormer reaches only 4.50 dB, below the much smaller static BSRNN (5.09) and far below the target-specific results in the original paper, which suggests undertraining. A 1.84 dB gain over such a baseline does not transfer directly to a tuned system. On EchoSet the DBS models are compared with published BSRNN and TIGER numbers, not matched retrains.

Fragility. Removing frequency refinement drops average SI-SDR from 9.07 to 3.92, and removing the acoustic transition prior drops it to 4.09, both far below the static BSRNN's 7.69. DBS is not a drop-in tweak; its components depend heavily on each other, and the 0.15 prior coefficient is hand-set. No multiple seeds or variances are reported. The authors' own limitations are a fixed band count and one partition per inference window; letting the band count be learned made the model always pick the largest quota.

討論Discussion

這篇最有說服力的不是主表，而是兩組控制實驗。第一組是重新訓練的固定切法對照：同架構、同 128 頻帶，均勻切分是 8.00 dB，Mel 頻帶 5.87，可學習濾波器組甚至崩到 −11.72（作者在附錄追到分析–合成轉換與頻帶架構不相容），DBS 是 9.07，比最好的固定切法多 1.07 dB，三個音源都有提升。第二組是推論時換邊界：權重完全不動，只把學到的邊界換成均勻或 BSRNN 原始切法，平均分數分別少 1.27 與 0.45 dB。這組最乾淨，因為參數與計算量都相同，差異只來自邊界位置。

有個值得注意的旁證：均勻切分的對照（8.00）本身就比 BSRNN 人工設計的切法（7.69）好，代表在 128 頻帶這個解析度下，人工切法不一定是好的起點。DBS 的優勢有一部分可能是「擺脫一個不太好的固定切法」，而不全是「每段混音都切得不一樣」。

區塊消融說明固定配額是必要的：只用一個區塊時，邊界全擠到低頻，平均分數掉到 6.30；兩個區塊 8.32；四個區塊 9.07。這其實回答了一個隱含問題：完全自由的邊界會被低頻的大能量吸走，所以 DBS 的「動態」是在人為劃定的區塊裡動。在 64 段 DnR v2 測試片段上，每段的切法都不同，相鄰三秒窗之間也會變，同一輸入重跑則完全一致。

對 MIR 讀者來說，最實用的訊號是 Mel-RoFormer 那一列：它是三個骨幹裡基線最強的（7.25 dB），DBS 仍帶來 0.82 dB，而且四個音軌都有進步。但在把這套方法用到調好的大型分離器之前，還需要一組計算量對齊的比較。

The most convincing part of this paper is not the main table but two control experiments. The first is retrained fixed layouts with the same architecture and 128 bands: uniform splitting gives 8.00 dB, Mel bands 5.87, and the learnable filterbank collapses to -11.72 (the appendix traces this to an analysis-synthesis mismatch with the band architecture), while DBS reaches 9.07, 1.07 dB above the best fixed layout, with gains on all three sources. The second is inference-time boundary substitution: with weights untouched, replacing learned boundaries with uniform or original BSRNN boundaries lowers the average by 1.27 and 0.45 dB respectively. This is the cleanest test, because parameters and compute are identical and only boundary placement differs.

One side result is worth noting: the uniform control (8.00) already beats BSRNN's hand-designed layout (7.69), so at 128 bands the hand-designed layout is not necessarily a good starting point. Part of DBS's advantage may come from escaping a mediocre fixed layout, not entirely from splitting each mixture differently.

The block ablation shows the fixed quotas are necessary: with one block, boundaries crowd into low frequencies and the average falls to 6.30; two blocks give 8.32; four give 9.07. This answers an implicit question: fully free boundaries get pulled toward high-energy low frequencies, so DBS's 'dynamic' splitting moves within hand-set blocks. Across 64 DnR v2 test excerpts every partition differed, partitions changed between adjacent three-second windows, and repeated inference on the same input was identical.

For MIR readers the most useful signal is the Mel-RoFormer row: it has the strongest baseline of the three backbones (7.25 dB), DBS still adds 0.82 dB, and all four stems improve. Before applying this to tuned large-scale separators, though, a compute-matched comparison is still needed.

動機Motivation

在許多聲樂傳統裡，音與音之間連續的音高移動不是裝飾，而是旋律本身的一部分。韓國傳統音樂把這件事直接命名為「시김새」（sigimsae）：主音上的音高彎曲、附著在主音上的裝飾音、以及音與音之間的過渡動作，都算在內。它和長短（jangdan，節奏週期）、旋律線（garak）並列為韓國音樂的三大要素。兩首用同一音階的曲子，可能因為 sigimsae 不同而屬於不同的「토리」（tori，地域性旋律體系）。

這種連續的音高現象一寫成五線譜，就只能用倚音或裝飾記號近似，形式還取決於記譜者的詮釋，所以嚴謹的分析必須直接從音訊出發。但作者指出兩個長期障礙：可供研究的公開音訊幾乎不存在（高品質錄音多是商業發行、有版權，公共檔案則多是田野錄音或有伴奏的演出）；而 sigimsae 從來沒有在音訊層級被系統化標註過，各曲種、各流派的術語與分類也不一致。

In many vocal traditions, continuous pitch movement within and between notes is not ornament but part of the melodic line itself. Korean traditional music names this directly as sigimsae: pitch inflections on a main note, dependent ornamental notes attached to it, and transitional gestures between notes. It is counted with jangdan (rhythmic cycle) and garak (melodic line) as one of the three fundamental elements of Korean music. Two pieces on the same scale may belong to different tori (regional melodic systems) because their sigimsae differ.

Written in staff notation, these continuous pitch phenomena can only be approximated with grace notes or ornament symbols whose form depends on the transcriber, so rigorous analysis has to start from audio. The authors name two long-standing obstacles: public research audio is virtually nonexistent (high-quality recordings are commercial and copyrighted, while public archives hold field recordings or accompanied performances), and sigimsae has never been systematically annotated at the audio level, with terminology and categories varying across genres and lineages.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

非西方音樂資料集的直接前例是 CompMusic 計畫，以及由它延伸出的 Saraga（印度藝術音樂）；聲樂方面有 SANIDHA（錄音室品質的卡納提克聲樂）與喬治亞複音歌唱的 Erkomaishvili 資料集。Gugak-VocalSet 在形式上最像 SANIDHA：同樣強調錄音室、無伴奏、專業歌手，差別在它附了逐事件的裝飾標註。

就標註內容而言，最接近的前作是 ROD 資料集：它為六種印度斯坦音樂的 gamaka（同樣是連續音高裝飾）做了專家事件級標註，本文偵測實驗用的 don't-care 遮罩也是從那裡借來的。差別在規模與範圍：本文是 17 類、跨韓國正樂與民俗樂多個曲種。至於歌唱技巧資料集，VocalSet 與 GTSinger 雖然類別很廣，但標籤是整個檔案或片段層級，沒有時間位置。

韓國本身其實已有兩個語料：一個國家資助的國樂資料集只能在韓國境內使用，sigimsae 標籤只有六類；Finding Tori 只涵蓋單一曲種（土俗民謠），且不直接散布音訊。本文的新意因此主要在三件事：公開、跨曲種、以及一套為跨曲種一致性重新設計的本體。

The direct precedents for non-Western music datasets are the CompMusic project and its descendant Saraga (Indian art music); on the vocal side there are SANIDHA (studio-quality Carnatic vocals) and the Erkomaishvili dataset of Georgian polyphonic singing. Gugak-VocalSet is formally closest to SANIDHA, with the same emphasis on studio, unaccompanied, professional singers; the difference is that it adds per-event ornament annotations.

In annotation content, the closest predecessor is the ROD dataset, which provides expert event-level annotations for six Hindustani gamaka types (also continuous pitch ornaments); the don't-care masking used in this paper's detection experiments comes from there. The differences are scale and scope: 17 types across several genres of Korean court and folk music. Singing-technique datasets such as VocalSet and GTSinger cover many categories, but their labels are file- or clip-level with no time positions.

Korea already has two corpora: a nationally funded gugak dataset is accessible only within Korea and has just six sigimsae categories, and Finding Tori covers a single genre (tosogminyo) and does not distribute audio directly. The novelty is therefore mainly threefold: public availability, multiple genres, and an ontology redesigned for cross-genre consistency.

方法Method

錄音。 曲目由國立國樂院樂器研究科挑選，20 位專業歌手各錄 6–27 首，平均每首 286.7 秒。為了能做語料層級的比較，每首都以固定的基準音（청，cheong）與節拍器錄製；每首錄兩次，由有 20 年以上國樂錄音經驗的錄音師選較穩的一次，再做混音、音準修正、速度修正與兩階段品質檢查。原始與修整後的音訊都有釋出（48 kHz、24-bit）。性別分布不均：242 首女聲（79.3%）、63 首男聲，而且男聲全部是正樂。

本體。 作者先調查正歌與盤索里樂譜集裡依音高輪廓可區分的裝飾，再做一輪試標，補上漏掉的曲種特有類型，最後定為 17 類。最上層依「在旋律中的功能位置」分成起音、顫音（요성，yoseong）、音中、收尾、強調、過渡六組，例如起音處由下往上滑到主音、收尾處的推聲（추성）與退聲（퇴성）。刻意排除了曲種特有的發聲與音色區別，只看音高變化與音與音的銜接。

標註。 14 位國樂聲樂專長的標註者依專長曲種分配曲目，每首只由一人標；設計本體的成員負責校正。共 68,272 個片段、71,742 筆標註，其中 5.1% 有多重標籤，片段平均 0.47 秒。另有歌詞的樂句級對齊（7–20 秒）、80 類情緒與 38 類音色標籤。

基線。 F0 用 RMVPE 以 10 ms 抽取，表示成三通道（音分正規化輪廓、有聲旗標、ΔF0）。分類任務比較 F0CNN、MelCNN 與凍結的 MERT-v1-95M、CultureMERT、CultureMERT-TA（各層加 2 層 MLP 探針），以歌曲層級 5 折交叉驗證。事件偵測用約 0.8M 參數的 EDTCN，逐影格預測 18 類（含「無裝飾」），以 focal loss 訓練。

Recording. Repertoire was chosen by the National Gugak Center's Instrument Research Division; 20 professional singers recorded 6–27 tracks each, averaging 286.7 seconds per track. To allow corpus-level comparison, every piece was recorded at a fixed reference pitch (cheong) with a metronome. Each piece was recorded twice; an engineer with over 20 years of gugak recording experience chose the steadier take, which then went through mixing, pitch tuning, tempo correction and a two-stage quality review. Both original and refined audio are released (48 kHz, 24-bit). The gender split is uneven: 242 female tracks (79.3%) and 63 male, all of them jeongak.

Ontology. The authors surveyed ornaments distinguishable by pitch contour in jeongga and pansori score collections, ran a prototype annotation round to add missed genre-specific types, and settled on 17. The top level groups them by functional position in the melody into onset, vibrato (yoseong), mid-note, offset, accent and transition, for example an upward slide into the main note at onset, or chuseong and toeseong at the offset. Genre-specific vocal production and timbre distinctions were deliberately excluded in favor of pitch movement and note transitions.

Annotation. Fourteen annotators specializing in Korean traditional singing were assigned tracks by genre, one annotator per track; the member who designed the ontology did the correction pass. The result is 68,272 segments and 71,742 labels, 5.1% multi-label, with a mean segment length of 0.47 seconds. There is also phrase-level lyric alignment (7–20 s) plus 80 mood and 38 timbre tags.

Baselines. F0 is extracted with RMVPE at 10 ms and represented as three channels (cents-normalized contour, voicing flag, ΔF0). Classification compares F0CNN, MelCNN, and frozen MERT-v1-95M, CultureMERT and CultureMERT-TA with a 2-layer MLP probe per layer, using song-level 5-fold cross-validation. Event detection uses an EDTCN of about 0.8M parameters predicting 18 per-frame classes (including 'no ornament'), trained with focal loss.

限制Limitations

標註一致性是最該先看的數字。 因為每首只有一位標註者，作者另找兩位成員各自重標 10 首的前 60 秒（共 10 分鐘）。兩人在「哪裡有裝飾」上的事件 F1（±200 ms）是 0.597，起音 F1 0.720；但連 17 類都要相同時只剩 0.357。把最常混淆的類別併成 10 類，同標籤比例從 56% 升到 78%。也就是說，17 類的細分本身就有相當的主觀性，而這個一致性檢查只涵蓋全庫 24.5 小時中的 10 分鐘。

作者用另一個觀察支持「混淆來自聲學上的模糊而非標註雜訊」：從 F0CNN 混淆矩陣推出的 10 類分組，和從標註者分歧推出的分組，在那 10 首上得到幾乎相同的同標籤比例（78.4% 對 78.0%）。但模型是用這些標註訓練出來的，它的混淆很可能本來就繼承了標註習慣，這個論證有循環的成分。

錄音條件改變了被研究的對象。 固定基準音與速度是為了可比性，但作者自己說有歌手覺得不自然。更關鍵的是後製做了音準修正，而 sigimsae 正是音高現象；論文沒有說明標註與基線是用原始還是修整後的音訊。另外，男聲全是正樂，性別與曲種完全混淆，作者在跨曲種實驗中有提到這點。

Annotation agreement is the number to read first. Because each track has a single annotator, two project members independently re-annotated the first 60 seconds of 10 tracks (10 minutes in total). On where ornaments occur they reach event F1 (±200 ms) of 0.597 and onset F1 of 0.720, but only 0.357 when the 17-class label must also match. Merging the most-confused classes into 10 groups raises the same-label rate from 56% to 78%. The 17-way distinctions are therefore substantially subjective, and this check covers only 10 minutes of a 24.5-hour corpus.

The authors argue that confusion reflects acoustic ambiguity rather than annotation noise, because a 10-class grouping derived from the F0CNN confusion matrix and one derived from annotator disagreement give nearly the same same-label rate on those 10 tracks (78.4% vs 78.0%). But the model was trained on these annotations, so its confusions may simply inherit annotation habits; the argument is partly circular.

Recording conditions change the object of study. A fixed reference pitch and tempo serve comparability, but the authors note that some singers found this unnatural. More importantly, post-production included pitch tuning, and sigimsae is a pitch phenomenon; the paper does not say whether annotations and baselines use the original or refined audio. Male voices are all jeongak, so gender and genre are fully confounded, which the authors acknowledge in the cross-genre experiment.

討論Discussion

分類結果支持「領域知識的表示仍然有用」這個主張，但範圍要說清楚。F0CNN 的 Macro F1 是 0.441（加權 F1 0.530），MelCNN 0.400，凍結 MERT 最好的一層（L7）是 0.358；只是 MERT 這邊只做凍結探針、沒有微調，也沒有試更大的 330M 版本，所以結論是「凍結表徵裡的現成資訊不夠」，不是「基礎模型打不過小 CNN」。所有 MERT 系模型都在中間層（L5–L7）最好、最後一層最差，和先前發現的「高層偏語意、中層偏音高與聲學」一致。

CultureMERT 的結果是本文最有意思的一點。它在土耳其、印度、希臘音樂共 650 小時上做過持續預訓練，作者原本假設微分音與裝飾旋律的共通性會遷移過來，結果反而在每一層都比原版 MERT 低約 0.02–0.04。換句話說，「非西方」不是一個可以共用的類別，預訓練資料若沒有涵蓋目標傳統，持續預訓練可能幫倒忙。

事件偵測呈現一個分工：F0 在影格級 Macro F1 領先（0.324，mel 0.269），mel 則在事件級 F1（0.763 對 0.675）與 IoU F1 上領先。作者的解讀是類別靠音高、定位靠能量包絡與音色轉換。這個解讀合理，但要注意事件級 F1 遠高於影格級 Macro F1，而論文沒有明說表 4 的事件 F1 是否要求類別正確；若主要衡量的是定位，F0 在這一欄吃虧也就不意外。0.8M 參數的 EDTCN(mel) 與 95M 的 MERT 不相上下（0.763 對 0.756），對想在這個資料上起步的人是個實用的參考點。

跨曲種實驗中，正樂訓練、民俗樂測試的 Macro F1 下降 34.0%，反方向下降 19.2%，兩者仍遠高於隨機。對 MIR 而言，這份資料最直接的價值是提供一個 17 類、時間對齊的音高裝飾基準，可以拿來檢驗轉譜或歌聲合成系統是否保留了這些細節。

The classification results support the claim that domain-informed representations still matter, but the scope needs stating. F0CNN reaches Macro F1 0.441 (weighted F1 0.530), MelCNN 0.400, and frozen MERT's best layer (L7) 0.358. MERT is only probed frozen, without fine-tuning, and the larger 330M version is not tested, so the conclusion is that ready-made information in frozen representations is insufficient, not that foundation models lose to a small CNN. All MERT-family models peak at intermediate layers (L5–L7) and are worst at the last layer, consistent with prior findings that upper layers lean semantic while middle layers carry pitch and acoustic detail.

The CultureMERT result is the most interesting point here. It was continually pre-trained on 650 hours of Turkish, Indian and Greek music, and the authors hypothesized that shared microtonal and ornamental traits would transfer; instead it scores about 0.02–0.04 below the original MERT at every layer. 'Non-Western' is not a shareable category, and continual pre-training that does not cover the target tradition can hurt.

Event detection shows a division of labor: F0 leads on frame-level Macro F1 (0.324 vs 0.269 for mel), while mel leads on event-level F1 (0.763 vs 0.675) and IoU F1. The authors read this as class identity coming from pitch and localization from energy envelope and timbral transitions. That is plausible, but event-level F1 is far above frame-level Macro F1, and the paper does not state whether Table 4's event F1 requires the class to match; if it mostly measures localization, F0 losing that column is unsurprising. The 0.8M-parameter EDTCN(mel) matches 95M MERT (0.763 vs 0.756), a practical reference point for anyone starting on this data.

In the cross-genre experiment, training on jeongak and testing on minsogak lowers Macro F1 by 34.0%, and the reverse by 19.2%, both still well above chance. For MIR, the dataset's most direct value is a 17-class, time-aligned benchmark of pitch ornaments that can test whether transcription or singing synthesis systems preserve these details.
