Unifying Score and Performance for Fine-Grained Music Understanding in Audio-Language Models

統一樂譜與演奏表示：讓音訊語言模型讀懂音樂的細節

Milan Liessens Dujardin, Song-Ze Yu, Kevin Miao
arXiv:2609.10351 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-09
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文主張音樂理解需要同時保留樂譜記譜與演奏時間/力度資訊的表示法，而不是二選一，因此提出 MuNo-SP，並用一套自動生成流程做出 MAESTROCaps 資料集（148 組對齊的樂譜—演奏長篇分析、31,080 組問答）；在自建的 MuSP-Bench 上，GPT-5.6 Sol 在 442 題上的平均準確率從基準的 44.8%（最佳基準 55.0%）大幅提升到用 MuNo-SP 的 80.4%（最佳 80.8%），盲測人類評審在 9 段摘錄中有 8 段偏好 MuNo-SP 分析勝過純 MIDI 分析（58.1% 對 39.5%）；不過語料集中在西方古典鋼琴曲（僅 148 首、13 位作曲家），能描述的內容仍受限於 MIDI 能捕捉到的範圍（時間、力度、時長、踏板，捕捉不到音色或聲學特性），而且自動生成的長篇分析仍可能反映 GPT-5.6 Sol 對知名作品的先備記憶，而非真的從樂譜現讀分析出來。Why this one: The paper argues music understanding needs a representation that unifies score notation with performance timing/dynamics rather than choosing one or the other, introducing MuNo-SP together with an automatic pipeline that produced MAESTROCaps (148 aligned score-performance analyses, 31,080 QA pairs); on their MuSP-Bench, GPT-5.6 Sol's aggregate accuracy across 442 questions jumps from a 44.8% baseline mean (55.0% best baseline) to 80.4% mean (80.8% best) with MuNo-SP, and blind human judges preferred MuNo-SP-based analyses over MIDI-only ones on 8 of 9 excerpts (58.1% vs 39.5%); however, the corpus is concentrated on Western classical piano (only 148 pieces, 13 composers), can only describe what MIDI captures (timing, velocity, duration, pedal -- not timbre or acoustics), and the automated captions still may reflect GPT-5.6 Sol's prior exposure to well-known works rather than a fresh reading of the score.

動機Motivation

大型音訊語言模型（LALM）在標籤、檢索、字幕生成等粗粒度音樂理解任務上已有進展，但對於演奏中的力度變化、句法、發音、時間掌握等細膩詮釋，理解仍處於早期階段。作者指出，現有的訓練管線多半仰賴「粗糙、時間對齊薄弱」的字幕資料，沒辦法教會模型辨認具體的演奏決策，限制了這類模型在音樂教育或藝術實踐上的實際應用。作者提出的研究問題是：能不能找到一種表示法，同時精確編碼樂譜內容與演奏實現方式，讓模型學到的不只是「這首曲子叫什麼」，而是「這段演奏做了什麼」？

Large audio-language models (LALMs) have progressed on coarse music-understanding tasks like tagging, retrieval, and captioning, but understanding of fine interpretive nuance -- dynamics, phrasing, articulation, timing -- remains at an early stage. The authors note existing training pipelines rely on "coarse, weakly time-grounded" captions that cannot teach a model to recognize concrete performance decisions, limiting real-world use in education or artistic practice. Their research question: can a single representation precisely encode both score content and how it was actually performed, so a model learns not just what a piece is called but what a specific performance does?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者點名了具體的先前系統。在符號樂譜理解方面，MusicTheoryBench、ZIQI-Eval、SSMR-Bench、ABC-Eval、MSU-Bench 等基準都測試樂譜閱讀與樂理推理，但作者指出「現有系統在樂譜理解上仍不可靠」；在音訊端，Audio Flamingo Next、GaMMA、Gemini 3.1 Pro、Qwen3.5-Omni、GPT-4o Audio 等多模態模型直接處理音訊，MMAR、MMAU-Pro、MusTBench 等基準測它們的時間理解與音樂知識，但同樣「在從音訊做細粒度音樂理解上仍不可靠」。

在表示法選擇上，MusicXML 是事實標準但「冗長」，不利於有限的語言模型上下文窗口；ABC 記譜精簡卻「難以被模型解讀」；MIDI「富含演奏資訊卻天生模糊」，缺少明確的記譜結構（樂句邊界、聲部區分）；REMI、TSD、Structured、CPWord、Octuple 等機器導向的符號化方式則「繼承了 MIDI 在語意上的侷限」。在自動字幕生成方面，LP-MusicCaps（220 萬筆偽字幕，靠 GPT-3.5 配合片段級標籤生成）「跟具體音樂事件沒有綁定」；MIDICaps（16.8 萬筆，Claude 3 Opus 把 MIDI 特徵轉成文字）「字幕沒有保留序列、時間或局部演奏的細節」；MusicBench、FUTGA、SonicVerse 等資料集多半以 10 秒片段為單位，時間定位只到「粗略的視窗層級」。

這篇論文與它們的關鍵差異，在於同時解決「表示法要精簡到能放進語言模型上下文」與「要保留演奏時間/力度細節」這兩個過去互斥的目標——MuNo-SP 只用完整帶時間標記 MusicXML 的 15.6% token 數，就同時保留樂譜結構與演奏對齊資訊，不像先前工作那樣只能二選一。

The authors name specific prior systems. On symbolic score understanding, benchmarks including MusicTheoryBench, ZIQI-Eval, SSMR-Bench, ABC-Eval, and MSU-Bench test score reading and music-theoretic reasoning, but the authors find "current systems remain unreliable in score understanding"; on the audio side, multimodal models such as Audio Flamingo Next, GaMMA, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Qwen3.5-Omni, and GPT-4o Audio work directly from audio, evaluated by benchmarks like MMAR, MMAU-Pro, and MusTBench, yet remain "unreliable for fine-grained musical understanding from audio."

On representation choice, MusicXML is the de facto standard but "verbose" and impractical for limited LLM context windows; ABC notation is compact but "hard to interpret" for models; MIDI is "performance-rich but inherently ambiguous," lacking explicit notational structure such as phrase boundaries and voice distinction; machine-oriented tokenizations like REMI, TSD, Structured, CPWord, and Octuple "inherit the semantic limitations of MIDI." On automated captioning, LP-MusicCaps (2.2M pseudo-captions via GPT-3.5 with clip-level tags) is "not tied to concrete musical events"; MIDICaps (168k files, Claude 3 Opus verbalizing MIDI features) has captions that "do not preserve the sequence, timing, or local realization"; datasets like MusicBench, FUTGA, and SonicVerse mostly work in 10-second segments, with temporal grounding only at a "coarse window level."

This paper's key departure is resolving a tension prior work treated as either-or: a representation compact enough for an LLM context window, versus one detailed enough to preserve performance timing and dynamics. MuNo-SP uses only 15.6% of the tokens of a fully timed MusicXML while retaining both notational structure and performance alignment -- rather than trading one for the other as prior representations did.

方法Method

MuNo-SP 是一套文字表示法，用階層式結構表示樂曲：樂器宣告 → 小節（附上實際演奏起始時間 MM:SS.cc、時長、平均力度）→ 拍 → 樂器 → 聲部。獨奏音樂省略樂器層，重疊聲部則保留聲部包裹標籤。單一音符的格式是「音高 記譜時值 MIDI力度 實際起始時間+實際時長」（例如「C#4 四分音符 v64 00:01.25+00.48」），和絃用逗號分隔音高並依序給力度，休止符、連音、圓滑線、裝飾音、琶音、倚音等記譜細節都附掛在對應事件上；力度、速度、表情記號、踏板事件則巢狀掛在拍層級，MIDI 延音踏板轉成帶時間戳的「踏板開始／踏板結束」。

資料生成管線分五個階段。第一階段整合鋼琴資料（MAESTRO 的音訊/MIDI 同步資料、ASAP 的 222 份樂譜與 1,068 場演奏、(n)ASAP 對齊標註、PianoCoRe）與管絃樂資料（Beethoven Symphony Expression Dataset，20 段管絃樂節錄、各 4 個演奏版本），最終整理出 179 首/樂章的三重對應版本（記譜、MIDI、音訊）。第二階段是樂譜校正演算法：先從 (n)ASAP 標記的缺漏音與外部樂譜差異偵測「該刪除」的候選，再從演奏時間視窗偵測「該插入」的候選，接著用 (n)ASAP 與 PianoCoRe 的對齊結果做門檻共識驗證（PianoCoRe 需要至少 10 次有效對齊、涵蓋至少 50% 可用錄音才算數），最後人工覆核；148 個項目總共修正了超過 2,000 個錯誤，所有決策都留有紀錄。

第三階段做樂譜—演奏對齊，MAESTRO 系列沿用原始未修改的演奏 MIDI，靠 PianoCoRe 對應關係換算成修正後樂譜的音符級對照；BSED 則直接用資料集自帶的對齊標註。第四階段把對齊後的演奏屬性併入 MusicXML 再轉成 MuNo-SP。第五階段用 GPT-5.6 Sol 分兩步生成字幕：先把樂譜切成大段落、主題呈現/再現、樂句（通常 1–4 小節），每段都附上起訖小節、音樂功能與證據；再依旋律、和聲張力、織度、力度與句法、結構功能、節奏時間、演奏決策七個面向生成帶時間戳的長篇分析，最後用 Gemini 3.7 Flash 生成獨立、可從聆聽驗證、不含糊的問答對，MAESTROCaps 最終含 148 篇長篇分析、31,080 組問答，音訊總長 10.38 小時，涵蓋 13 位作曲家。

MuNo-SP is a text representation organized hierarchically: instrument declaration -> bar (carrying the performed onset in MM:SS.cc, duration, mean velocity) -> beat -> instrument -> voice. Solo music omits the instrument layer, while overlapping voices keep voice-wrapper tags. A single note is written as "pitch written-duration MIDI-velocity performed-onset+sounding-duration" (e.g. "C#4 qtr v64 00:01.25+00.48"); chords list comma-separated pitches with ordered velocities; rests, tuplets, ties, slurs, ornaments, arpeggios, and grace notes attach to their events. Dynamics, tempo, expression markings, and pedal events nest at the beat level, with MIDI sustain converted into timestamped pedal-start/pedal-stop lines.

The data pipeline has five stages. Stage 1 integrates piano data (MAESTRO's synchronized audio/MIDI, ASAP's 222 scores and 1,068 performances, (n)ASAP alignments, PianoCoRe) with orchestral data (the Beethoven Symphony Expression Dataset: 20 excerpts x 4 performances each), yielding 179 pieces/movements with three aligned views (notation, MIDI, audio). Stage 2 is a score-correction algorithm: it detects deletion candidates from (n)ASAP-marked missing notes and external score differences, detects insertion candidates from performance-time windows, validates both against (n)ASAP and PianoCoRe alignments with threshold-based consensus (PianoCoRe requires at least 10 valid alignments covering at least 50% of available recordings), then applies manual verification; across 148 entries this corrected over 2,000 errors, each decision logged.

Stage 3 aligns score to performance -- MAESTRO-linked items keep the original unmodified performance MIDI and propagate PianoCoRe correspondences to the corrected score, while BSED uses its own supplied alignments. Stage 4 folds aligned performance attributes into MusicXML and converts to MuNo-SP. Stage 5 uses GPT-5.6 Sol to first segment the score into sections, thematic returns, and 1-4 bar phrases with supporting evidence, then generate timestamped long-form analyses across seven dimensions (melody, harmony/tension, texture, dynamics/phrasing, structural function, rhythm, performance decisions); Gemini 3.7 Flash then generates standalone, listening-verifiable question-answer pairs. The result, MAESTROCaps, holds 148 long-form analyses and 31,080 QA pairs over 10.38 hours of audio across 13 composers.

限制Limitations

作者列出的限制包括：目前語料集中在西方古典鋼琴曲，管絃樂只有概念驗證性質的一小部分，還沒有全面涵蓋；生成的分析「繼承了來源表示法的侷限——因為只根據樂譜與對齊過的 MIDI，能描述時間、力度、時長、踏板，但沒辦法描述音色、聲學特性等 MIDI 裡沒有的東西」；樂譜或對齊本身如果有誤、又沒被驗證機制抓到，錯誤就會傳到訓練資料裡；要擴大規模，需要像 MuScriptor 這樣的自動記譜系統，目前只能仰賴既有的對齊資料集。

模型生成的回應「可能部分反映了模型對知名作品的先備記憶」，這種先備知識對字幕內容的影響程度目前未知；作者也承認「聆聽終究是一種被聽者的文化、歷史與訓練形塑的內在經驗」，這些分析雖然扎根於具體音樂事件，但終究只代表一種聆聽方式。

The authors list several limitations: the current corpus is concentrated on Western classical piano music, with orchestral coverage limited to a small proof-of-concept rather than comprehensive; generated analyses "inherit the limitations of their source representations" -- grounded only in scores and aligned MIDI, they can describe timing, velocity, duration, and pedal use, but not timbre, acoustics, or other properties absent from MIDI; errors in scores or alignments that escape the verification mechanism can propagate into the training supervision; and scaling further would require an automatic transcription system like MuScriptor, since the pipeline currently depends on existing aligned datasets.

Model responses "may partly reflect prior exposure to well-known works," and how much this prior exposure shapes the generated captions is unknown; the authors also acknowledge that "listening remains an internal experience shaped by the listener's culture, history, and training" -- the analyses, though grounded in concrete events, still represent just one way of listening.

討論Discussion

在自建的 MuSP-Bench（442 題，排除作曲家/作品辨識題後）上，GPT-5.6 Sol 用 MuNo-SP 整體平均準確率達到 80.4%，最佳配置 80.8%，對比基準平均 44.8%、最佳基準 55.0%，等於平均提升 35.6 個百分點、最佳提升 25.8 個百分點；細看子任務，只需樂譜的題目上，MuNo-S 的 83.3% 大幅超過 ABC 記譜的 51.1%（+32.2）與樂譜圖片的 27.2%（+56.1）；只需演奏資訊的題目上，MuNo-SP 的 76.4% 超過 MIDI-as-text（整數音高）的 32.1%（+44.3）與 MIDI-as-text（ABC 音高）的 59.4%（+17.0）；同時需要樂譜與演奏的題目上，MuNo-SP 的 80.6% 超過 ABC+MIDI 的 45.8%（+34.8）。

換到另外兩個模型也一致：Meta Muse Spark 1.2 最佳準確率從 36.0% 提升到 62.0%（+26.0），Alibaba Qwen3.6-Plus 從 15.8% 提升到 25.3%（+9.5），提升幅度隨模型能力遞減但方向一致。token 效率上，MuNo-S 平均只需 33,973 個 token（完整帶時間標記 MusicXML 的 7.0%），MuNo-SP 平均 75,779 個（15.6%），而 ABC+MIDI 需要 63,316 個（13.1%），MIDI-as-text 需要 57,692 個（11.9%）——換句話說 MuNo-SP 用比 ABC+MIDI 更少的 token 拿到明顯更高的準確率。

人類盲測（9 段摘錄）在整體偏好上，MuNo-SP 以 58.14% 對 MIDI 的 39.53%（其餘為平局）贏得 8/9 段的多數票；用 Gemini 3.7 Flash 當裁判、擴大到 27 對比較（81 次判定）時，整體偏好是 MuNo-SP 83.95% 對 MIDI 16.05%，多數結果是 23/27，且與人類多數判斷的一致率達到 100%（9/9，取兩者共用的子集）。程式化驗證 148 篇完整分析（56,600 次時間戳出現），嚴格比對下有 63 次（0.111%）時間戳無法對應到實際素材、放寬到 ±100 毫秒容差後降到 12 次（0.021%），顯示生成內容的事實錯誤率相當低。

On the authors' own MuSP-Bench (442 questions, after excluding composer/work-identification items), GPT-5.6 Sol with MuNo-SP reaches an overall mean accuracy of 80.4% (best configuration 80.8%) versus a 44.8% baseline mean (55.0% best baseline) -- a 35.6-point mean gain and 25.8-point best-case gain. Broken down: on score-only questions, MuNo-S's 83.3% far exceeds ABC notation's 51.1% (+32.2) and score images' 27.2% (+56.1); on performance-only questions, MuNo-SP's 76.4% exceeds MIDI-as-text with integer pitch's 32.1% (+44.3) and MIDI-as-text with ABC pitch's 59.4% (+17.0); on joint score-and-performance questions, MuNo-SP's 80.6% exceeds ABC+MIDI's 45.8% (+34.8). The pattern holds across other models: Meta Muse Spark 1.2's best accuracy rises from 36.0% to 62.0% (+26.0), and Alibaba Qwen3.6-Plus's from 15.8% to 25.3% (+9.5) -- smaller gains on the weaker model, but the same direction.

On token efficiency, MuNo-S averages just 33,973 tokens (7.0% of a fully timed MusicXML) and MuNo-SP averages 75,779 tokens (15.6%), while ABC+MIDI needs 63,316 tokens (13.1%) and MIDI-as-text needs 57,692 (11.9%) -- MuNo-SP reaches clearly higher accuracy than ABC+MIDI while using fewer tokens. In a blind human evaluation over 9 excerpts, overall preference favored MuNo-SP 58.14% to MIDI's 39.53% (the remainder ties), a majority in 8 of 9 excerpts; scaling to 27 pairwise comparisons (81 judgments) with Gemini 3.7 Flash as judge gives an overall preference of 83.95% for MuNo-SP versus 16.05% for MIDI, a majority in 23 of 27 pairs, with 100% agreement (9/9) against the human majority on their shared subset. Programmatic validation across 148 full analyses (56,600 timestamp occurrences) found 63 unsupported timestamps under strict matching (0.111%), dropping to 12 (0.021%) with a +-100ms tolerance -- a low factual-error rate for generated content.

Population Ecology of Tunes

曲調的族群生態學：愛爾蘭傳統音樂裡的選擇與多樣性

John M. McBride, Armand M. Leroi
arXiv:2609.09501 · q-bio.PE, cs.SD, physics.soc-ph · 投稿submitted 2026-09-08
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文用愛爾蘭傳統音樂社群網站 TheSession.org 十三年、約 2 萬首曲調的每週新增紀錄，套用生態學裡的族群出生過程模型，比較中性演化、頻率相關選擇、逐曲選擇三種假說；結果顯示逐曲選擇模型比中性模型的 AIC 低 57,700（壓倒性支持），曲調間天生的「適應度」差異中有 29% 可以用社會聲望與旋律特徵（如音級熵、音符密度）解釋，而且有些曲調靠傳統「組曲」演奏習慣被連帶帶紅，類似遺傳學裡的選擇性清除；儘管存在方向性選擇，整體曲庫多樣性仍持續上升（熵增加 0.37 位元），靠的是新創作曲子不斷加入；不過這份資料只來自單一線上平台的愛好者社群，「存進曲本」只是學會演奏的替代指標而非直接測量，而且能解釋的變異只有 29%，作者自己承認熵、音符密度這類旋律指標「很可能只跟曲調好不好記、好不好學部分相關」。Why this one: The paper applies ecological birth-process models to thirteen years of weekly tunebook-add records for roughly 20,000 Irish traditional tunes on TheSession.org, comparing neutral, frequency-dependent, and per-tune selection hypotheses; the per-tune selection model beats the neutral model by a Delta-AIC of -57,700 (overwhelming support), 29% of the variance in tunes' intrinsic fitness is explained by social prestige and melodic features (e.g. pitch-class entropy, note density), and some tunes are carried along by the tradition of playing tunes in sets -- an effect the authors liken to a genetic selective sweep; despite this directional selection, overall repertoire diversity keeps rising (entropy up 0.37 bits) as new compositions keep arriving; however, the data comes from a single online platform's enthusiast community, a 'tunebook add' is only a proxy for actually learning a tune rather than a direct measure, only 29% of the fitness variance is explained, and the authors themselves acknowledge that melodic measures like entropy and note density 'are likely only partially correlated with what matters most: memorability and learnability.'

動機Motivation

文化演化研究長期以來想回答一個根本問題：文化的變化究竟是隨機漂移、還是真的有選擇作用在挑選特定的變體？先前研究多半用嬰兒名字、陶器紋樣、狗種、流行歌排行榜等頻率時間序列，但這些資料通常「被截斷到只看熱門變體、聚合區間太長、母體定義不清楚」，導致選擇與漂移難以區分。作者主張愛爾蘭傳統音樂剛好能繞開這些限制：它有清楚的口傳學習機制、離散的單位（一首一首的曲子）、可觀察的母體（固定聚會的樂手），而且 TheSession.org 這個社群網站累積了十三年、每週更新的「使用者把哪些曲子加進自己曲本」紀錄，涵蓋約兩萬首曲調，是少見能長期、細緻追蹤文化演化的真實資料集。

Cultural-evolution research has long asked a basic question: is cultural change essentially random drift, or does genuine selection favor particular variants? Prior studies mostly used frequency time series -- baby names, pottery motifs, dog breeds, pop charts -- but these were typically "truncated to popular variants, aggregated over long intervals, with undefined populations," making selection hard to distinguish from drift. The authors argue Irish traditional music sidesteps these constraints: it has a well-defined oral-transmission mechanism, discrete units (individual tunes), an observable population (session musicians), and, crucially, TheSession.org has accumulated thirteen years of weekly records of which tunes users add to their personal tunebooks, covering roughly 20,000 tunes -- a rare dataset able to track cultural evolution longitudinally at fine resolution.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者點名了具體的先前研究。理論框架方面，Boyd 與 Richerson（1988）、Cavalli-Sforza 與 Feldman（1981）、Henrich 與 Boyd（1998）借用族群遺傳學，建立了偏向式傳遞、漂移與選擇的模型；實證研究方面，Hahn 與 Bentley（2003）、Bentley 等人（2004、2007）、Shennan 與 Wilkinson（2001）、Herzog 等人（2004）、Newberry 等人（2017）分析過嬰兒名字、陶器紋樣、狗種、排行榜、詞彙、音樂取樣等資料；音樂領域裡，Youngblood（2019）在音樂取樣傳統中偵測到從眾偏誤，Mauch 等人（2015）研究了 1960 至 2010 年美國流行音樂的演化，Street 等人（2022）同樣用 TheSession 的資料研究母體規模對民謠曲調複雜度的影響。

這些先前工作的共同限制，是資料多半被截斷到熱門變體、聚合時間跨度太長、母體邊界不清楚，選擇與漂移因此糾纏在一起難以分開。這篇論文與它們的關鍵差異，在於用「每週」解析度、完整涵蓋約兩萬首曲調（不只是熱門的那些）、橫跨十三年，母體（固定去愛爾蘭傳統音樂聚會的樂手）也界定得相對清楚；另外，作者把族群遺傳學裡「選擇性清除」（selective sweep，指一個有利變異透過與其連鎖的其他變異一起被快速固定）的概念，直接套用在「曲調透過組曲搭配被連帶帶紅」的現象上，並精確指認出一支 YouTube 影片如何讓一首沉寂已久的曲子重新翻紅（每 500 次觀看對應約 1 次新增曲本紀錄），這是先前研究沒有做到的因果機制追蹤。

The authors name specific prior work. On theoretical frameworks, Boyd and Richerson (1988), Cavalli-Sforza and Feldman (1981), and Henrich and Boyd (1998) built models of biased transmission, drift, and selection by analogy with population genetics; empirically, Hahn and Bentley (2003), Bentley et al. (2004, 2007), Shennan and Wilkinson (2001), Herzog et al. (2004), and Newberry et al. (2017) analyzed baby names, pottery motifs, dog breeds, pop charts, words, and music sampling; within music specifically, Youngblood (2019) detected conformity bias in music-sampling traditions, Mauch et al. (2015) studied the evolution of US popular music from 1960-2010, and Street et al. (2022) used the same TheSession data to study how population size affects folk-tune complexity.

What these prior studies share is a limitation: data truncated to popular variants, long aggregation windows, and poorly defined populations, entangling selection with drift. This paper's key departure is weekly-resolution data covering roughly all 20,000 tunes (not just the popular ones) over thirteen years, with a comparatively well-defined population (musicians who attend Irish traditional sessions); it also applies the population-genetics concept of a "selective sweep" (a favored variant dragging linked variants to fixation) directly to how tunes get carried along through set-pairing traditions, and precisely traces the causal mechanism by which one YouTube video revived a long-dormant tune (roughly one new tunebook add per 500 views) -- a level of mechanistic tracing prior cultural-evolution studies had not achieved.

方法Method

作者建了離散時間的族群出生過程模型：每個時間點 t，曲調 i 的曲本累積數 F_{t,i} 換算成相對頻率 f_{t,i}，再依「權重」w 算出被選擇（新增）的機率 p_{t,i}。中性模型設 w=f_{t,i}，等同族群遺傳學的 Wright-Fisher 模型；頻率相關選擇模型設 w=f_{t,i}^(1+β)，用單一全域參數 β 描述從眾（β>0）或喜新（β<0）；逐曲選擇模型設 w=f_{t,i}·exp(β_i)，讓每首曲子各自帶一個獨立的適應度係數 β_i；還有一個加了飽和項的版本，考慮「能新增這首曲子的使用者母體有限」。

統計推論上用 Ridge 正規化（高斯先驗）搭配 L-BFGS 優化，不確定性用對角 Hessian 的 Laplace 近似估計，模型選擇用 AIC。資料來自 TheSession.org（10.6 萬會員）：主資料集 DS1 涵蓋 2012 年 12 月到 2026 年 3 月、82 萬 752 筆新增事件、2 萬 2216 首不同曲調，聚合成 695 週、694 次週轉換；主分析用其中「累積新增數至少 10 次」的子集，共 1 萬 1791 首曲子（另外 47%、即 1 萬 425 首曲子從未達到這個門檻，被排除在主分析外）；另有 DS2 提供每週會員總數用於回推歷史頻率。

為了預測「哪些曲子天生適應度高」，作者用 XGBoost 迴歸樹（最大深度 3），以 9 個標準化特徵（TheSession 錄音次數、Discogs 擁有數、Spotify 最高人氣三個聲望特徵；音級熵、音符密度、LZ76 複雜度、平均音程、輪廓複雜度五個旋律特徵；以及曲子平均頻率的對數）去預測每首曲子的選擇係數 β_i，樣本數 8,051 首、五折交叉驗證。另外，作者也分析了 4 萬 1743 組「兩首曲子搭配演奏」的紀錄，比對隨機組合的虛無模型，檢驗是否有曲子靠著跟熱門曲子搭配而「被帶紅」（類似遺傳學裡的連鎖／選擇性清除）。

The authors build a discrete-time ecological birth-process model: at each timestep t, tune i's cumulative tunebook count F_{t,i} converts to relative frequency f_{t,i}, and a weight w determines the selection (add) probability p_{t,i}. The neutral model sets w=f_{t,i}, equivalent to a Wright-Fisher model; the frequency-dependent selection model sets w=f_{t,i}^(1+beta), with a single global parameter beta capturing conformity (beta>0) or novelty bias (beta<0); the per-tune selection model sets w=f_{t,i}*exp(beta_i), giving each tune its own fitness offset beta_i; a further variant adds a saturation term accounting for the limited pool of users who could plausibly add a given tune.

Inference uses ridge regularization (a Gaussian prior) with L-BFGS optimization, uncertainty via a Laplace approximation over the diagonal Hessian, and model comparison via AIC. Data comes from TheSession.org (106,000 members): the main dataset (DS1) spans December 2012 to March 2026, with 820,752 tunebook-add events across 22,216 distinct tunes, aggregated into 695 weekly bins (694 week-to-week transitions); the main analysis restricts to tunes with at least 10 cumulative adds, leaving 11,791 tunes (the other 47%, or 10,425 tunes, never reached this threshold and were excluded). A second dataset (DS2) supplies weekly total membership counts per tune, used to back-calculate historical frequencies.

To predict which tunes are intrinsically fitter, the authors fit an XGBoost regressor (max depth 3) on 9 standardized features -- three prestige features (TheSession recording count, Discogs have-count, Spotify max popularity), five melodic features (pitch-class entropy, note density, LZ76 complexity, mean absolute interval, contour complexity), and log mean popularity -- to predict each tune's selection coefficient beta_i, over 8,051 tunes with 5-fold cross-validation. Separately, the authors analyze 41,743 two-tune "set" recordings against a null model of random pairings, testing whether tunes get carried to popularity by association with already-popular tunes -- an effect analogous to genetic linkage/selective sweeps.

限制Limitations

作者列出的限制包括：資料只來自單一線上平台，其使用者是自我篩選過的愛好者社群，「在 TheSession.org 上熱門的曲子未必完全反映聚會現場真正熱門的曲子」；「曲本新增」這個觀測單位只是學會、正在學、或只是先加入書籤存查的替代指標，不是直接測量學會演奏；Spotify 人氣反映的是廣泛聽眾而非真正驅動口傳的樂手，熵、音符密度這類旋律指標「很可能只跟曲調好不好記、好不好學部分相關」，這正是預測模型只能解釋 29% 變異的原因。

中性模型假設曲子完全透過同儕接觸傳播，但現實中樂手也可能透過錄音、書籍、網路資源接觸到曲子，不只是聚會現場；觀察窗口只有約十三年，而愛爾蘭傳統音樂早已是成熟的傳統，如果真的存在某種「數位化轉型」，有可能發生在資料開始之前，或者變化太慢、十三年偵測不到；作者也承認沒有真正的傳播樹（誰跟誰學、何時學）可用，只能靠曲本紀錄間接推論。

The authors list several limitations: the data come from a single online platform whose users are a self-selected community of enthusiasts, so "the tunes that are popular on TheSession.org may not perfectly reflect the tunes that are popular in sessions"; the unit of observation, a tunebook add, is a proxy for learning and playing a tune rather than a direct measure -- a user may have just learned it, intend to learn it, or simply be bookmarking it; Spotify popularity reflects listeners broadly rather than the musicians who actually drive oral transmission, and melodic measures like entropy and note density "are likely only partially correlated with what matters most: memorability and learnability," which the authors say explains why their predictive model captures only 29% of the variance.

The neutral model assumes tunes spread purely through peer exposure at sessions, whereas real musicians also encounter tunes via recordings, books, and online resources; the roughly thirteen-year observation window sits within an already well-established tradition, so any digital-era transition could predate the data or be too slow to detect within thirteen years; and the authors acknowledge they lack a true transmission tree (who learned what from whom, and when), relying instead on indirect inference from tunebook records.

討論Discussion

逐曲選擇模型比中性模型的 AIC 低 57,700（壓倒性支持），逐曲適應度係數 β_i 的範圍是 -0.84 到 1.60；相較之下，頻率相關選擇模型只比中性模型好 1,000（加上飽和項後是 1,900），只佔逐曲模型改善量的約 3%，顯示「每首曲子各自的特性」遠比「整體從眾傾向」更能解釋誰紅誰不紅；頻率相關模型估計的從眾偏誤本身也很微弱（β_f=0.024，加飽和項後 0.048）。

舉例來說，曲子 Maids of Selma（編號 2766）從 37 次曲本累積成長到 260 次，滑動窗分析顯示它在 2020 年之前的適應度略偏負，但一支同年 10 月上線、累積超過 8 萬次觀看的 YouTube 影片上線後，適應度躍升到 β_i≥0.7，換算下來大約每 500 次觀看對應 1 次新增曲本紀錄；相對地，The Bag of Potatoes（編號 391）排名從第 321 名跌到第 501 名，β=-0.32。

用 XGBoost 預測逐曲適應度係數，樣本外（held-out）R² 為 0.29、樣本內 R² 為 0.42、預測值與觀測值的皮爾森相關係數 r=0.56；拆解變異來源，社會聲望類特徵（錄音數、Discogs 擁有數、Spotify 人氣）獨立貢獻約 16%，旋律類特徵只貢獻約 3%，曲調平均頻率（從眾基準）貢獻約 1%，三者加總約佔可解釋變異的三分之二。

組曲共現分析上，4 萬 1743 組兩首曲子的搭配紀錄中，觀察到的最熱門組合被錄製了 134 次，而在依熱門度加權隨機重組的虛無模型模擬中最熱門組合最多只被錄了 8 次，在虛無模型產生的 4 萬 788 種不同組合裡排不進前段——顯示曲子搭配紀錄確實有超乎隨機的聚集，例如 Maids of Selma 與 Lisnagun 這組先前罕見的搭配，在 YouTube 影片之後出現了 27 次共現（18 次是精確配對，排名第 134），Lisnagun 本身的獨立適應度也達到 β_i=0.89。

多樣性方面，儘管存在方向性選擇，整個資料集的資訊熵在觀察期間仍上升了 0.37 位元，活躍曲目數從約 9,000 首倍增到超過 2 萬 2000 首，顯示多樣性上升主要是由新創作曲子持續加入所驅動；但如果只看「觀察開始時就存在」的曲子子集，熵反而是逐漸下降的——也就是說，選擇確實會侵蝕既有曲庫的多樣性，只是被源源不絕的新作品抵銷掉了。

The per-tune selection model beats the neutral model by a Delta-AIC of -57,700 (overwhelming support), with per-tune fitness coefficients beta_i ranging from -0.84 to 1.60; by contrast, frequency-dependent selection improves only 1,000 over neutral (1,900 with a saturation term) -- about 3% of the per-tune model's improvement -- showing that a tune's own individual traits explain far more of who succeeds than any overall conformity tendency, and the estimated conformity bias itself is weak (beta_f=0.024, or 0.048 with saturation). For example, the tune "Maids of Selma" (tune 2766) grew from 37 to 260 cumulative tunebook adds: a sliding-window analysis shows fitness was slightly negative before 2020, then jumped to beta_i>=0.7 after a YouTube video posted that October accumulated over 80,000 views -- roughly one new tunebook add per 500 views. By contrast, "The Bag of Potatoes" (tune 391) fell from 321st to 501st in ranking, with beta=-0.32.

Predicting per-tune fitness coefficients with XGBoost yields a held-out R-squared of 0.29, an in-sample R-squared of 0.42, and a Pearson correlation of r=0.56 between predicted and observed values; decomposing the variance, social-prestige features (recording count, Discogs have-count, Spotify popularity) contribute about 16% uniquely, melodic features contribute only about 3%, and mean tune frequency (the conformity baseline) contributes about 1% -- together roughly two-thirds of the explained variance. In the set-cooccurrence analysis of 41,743 two-tune pairings, the most-recorded observed pair appears 134 times, whereas in a null model of popularity-weighted random pairings the most-recorded simulated pair never exceeds 8 occurrences among 40,788 distinct simulated combinations -- evidence of far-greater-than-random clustering. One example, "Maids of Selma" paired with "Lisnagun" (previously a rare pairing), co-occurred 27 times after the YouTube video (18 as an exact pair, ranked 134th), with Lisnagun's own independent fitness reaching beta_i=0.89.

On diversity, despite directional selection the dataset's overall entropy rose by 0.37 bits over the observation period, and the number of active tunes roughly doubled from about 9,000 to over 22,000 -- showing that rising diversity is driven mainly by the continual arrival of new compositions. Restricted to only the tunes present at the start of observation, entropy instead gradually declines -- meaning selection does erode the diversity of an existing repertoire, but this erosion is offset by a steady stream of new compositions.

TimeCues Studio：團隊導向的音樂標註與演算法原型工作站

Sapir Caduri, Yoav Goldberg
arXiv:2609.10338 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-09

為什麼挑這篇：這篇論文指出現有的音樂標註工具（Sonic Visualiser、Praat 等）都是「一次處理一軌」，完全沒有語料庫、團隊或演算法迭代的概念，因此打造 TimeCues Studio：在同一條以節拍鎖定的時間軸上同時支援多種標記型態（含允許「多個候選時間點皆合法」的模糊標註）、疊加多種音樂特徵視覺化與 Demucs 分離的音軌，並直接接上演算法比較引擎與瀏覽器內 Python 沙盒；論文用一個 109 首 EDM 歌曲的實際個案研究展示工具的使用方式（每首中位數約 10 個邊界、39% 是 drop、23% 是 buildup）。不過整篇論文沒有正式的「限制」章節，也沒有拿它跟最接近的競品 LabelBuddy 做任何量化比較，唯一的量化結果就是這個個案研究的標註統計，而非工具本身在標註效率或演算法評比準確度上的實測數據。Why this one: The paper argues existing music-annotation tools (Sonic Visualiser, Praat, etc.) all work "one track at a time" with no notion of a corpus, a team, or an algorithm in the loop, and builds TimeCues Studio to fill that gap: a single beat-locked timeline supporting several marker types (including ambiguity-aware annotation where multiple candidate timestamps are all valid), overlaid with multiple music-feature visualizations and Demucs-separated stems, wired directly into an algorithm-comparison engine and a browser-based Python sandbox; the paper demonstrates the tool through a case study of 109 EDM tracks (a median of about 10 boundaries per track, 39% drops, 23% buildups). However, the paper has no formal limitations section and offers no quantitative comparison against its closest competitor, LabelBuddy -- the only quantitative results reported are this case study's annotation statistics, not measurements of the tool's own annotation efficiency or algorithm-ranking accuracy.

動機Motivation

作者主張，自動音樂分析是許多多媒體應用的基礎，但現有的標註工具都只能「一次處理一軌」，完全沒有語料庫、團隊、或是把演算法納入迭代迴圈的概念。像燈光提示、影片轉場這類下游應用「對轉場時機的要求極為精確——只要提示卡在邊界上，觀眾馬上就會聽見、看見」，但既有工具評分時只認一個「參考時間戳」加一個固定容差窗，沒辦法處理「一個段落邊界其實有好幾個合法的起始點，中間的點才是真正不合法」這種標註本質上的模糊性。每一次演算法評估都得串接一整套各自獨立的工具，也沒有半自動化的支援——沒辦法用演算法先猜、人再輕微修正，也沒辦法直接把自己寫的偵測腳本插進標註流程。

The authors argue automated music analysis underlies many multimedia applications, yet existing annotation tools all work "one track at a time," with no notion of a corpus, a team, or an algorithm in the loop. Downstream uses like lighting cues or video transitions "demand very precise transitions: a lighting cue or video transition that misses the boundary is immediately visible and audible to the audience," but existing tools score against a single reference timestamp within a fixed tolerance window, unable to represent that a section boundary can have several valid starting points while everything in between is invalid. Every algorithmic evaluation requires stitching together a pipeline of separate tools, with no built-in support for semi-automation -- no way to let an algorithm guess first and a human lightly correct it, or to plug in one's own detection script directly into the annotation workflow.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

作者點名了具體的先前系統。標註工具方面，Sonic Visualiser 與 Praat 都只能處理單軌，缺少語料庫/團隊/演算法整合；Songle 是網頁工具但「針對主動聆聽與音高」而非結構；Tony 專注在音高而非結構標註；audino、BAT 都「針對標籤與逐字稿」；GECKO 是為對話標註設計的；最接近的競品是 LabelBuddy（Prokopiou 等人，2026），它「把標註設計成對容器化後端結果的驗證」，而 TimeCues 更進一步，加上瀏覽器內自寫偵測器與具備結構感知的標註格式。評分方法方面，標準評估函式庫 mir_eval「只記錄單一參考時間戳」，SALAMI 資料集（Smith 等人，2011）早就指出這種單一時間戳做法「是武斷的」，共識分群方法（Ren 等人，2017）與 IRT 加權（Nakano 與 Goto，2024）則是處理多標註者分歧的其他做法。

論文列出的音樂結構分析演算法基準涵蓋 Foote 的新穎度曲線分割（2000）、MSAF 系列管線（Nieto 與 Bello 2016 等）、Marmoret 等人的張量分解（2020）、Truong 等人的變點偵測（2018、2020）、以及 Kim 與 Nam（2023）、Hao 等人（2026）的深度模型。這篇論文與這些既有系統的關鍵差異，在於把「多候選、允許模糊」的標註格式與評分方式，和「團隊協作的語料庫標註」、「瀏覽器內可直接寫偵測腳本」整合進同一個共用的節拍鎖定時間軸，而不是像先前工具那樣把標註、比較、原型開發拆成互相獨立的環節。

The authors name specific prior systems. Among annotation tools, Sonic Visualiser and Praat both work on a single track, lacking corpus/team/algorithm integration; Songle is web-based but "target[s] active listening and pitch" rather than structure; Tony focuses on pitch rather than structural annotation; audino and BAT both "target tags and transcripts"; GECKO is designed for conversation annotation. The closest competitor is LabelBuddy (Prokopiou et al., 2026), which "frames annotation as verification over containerized back-ends"; TimeCues goes further with browser-authored custom detectors and a structure-aware scheme. On scoring, the standard evaluation library mir_eval "records a single timestamp per reference," and the SALAMI dataset (Smith et al., 2011) had already shown this single-timestamp approach is "arbitrary"; consensus-clustering methods (Ren et al., 2017) and IRT weighting (Nakano and Goto, 2024) are alternative ways of handling multi-annotator disagreement.

The music-structure-analysis baselines the paper bundles span Foote's novelty-curve segmentation (2000), the MSAF pipeline family (Nieto and Bello 2016, among others), Marmoret et al.'s tensor decomposition (2020), Truong et al.'s change-point detection (2018, 2020), and deep models from Kim and Nam (2023) and Hao et al. (2026). This paper's key departure from these prior systems is integrating a multi-candidate, ambiguity-aware annotation scheme and scoring method with team-based corpus annotation and browser-authored detector scripts, all on one shared beat-locked timeline -- rather than splitting annotation, comparison, and prototyping across separate, disconnected tools as before.

方法Method

標註生命週期分兩塊。資料集準備階段，管理者上傳整批音訊，建立節拍格有三種模式：靜態 BPM（由 librosa、madmom 等五種估計器建議數值）、動態（可編輯的速度曲線，處理速度會漂移的曲子）、手動（適用現場演出或剪接過的錄音）。標註工具本身提供四種標記型態（單一時間點的 Cue、不重疊區段的 Boundary、可重疊區段的 Span、DJ 式重複段落的 Loop/Pattern），疊加在共用的視覺化堆疊上（預設是三頻段染色波形，可選頻譜圖、MFCC、色度圖、節奏圖、自相似矩陣、新穎度曲線、起始點、頻譜通量），也能疊加 Demucs 分離出的人聲/鼓/貝斯/其他音軌。標記可以標成「多候選」（同一個事件記錄好幾個都合法的時間點）並標示「關鍵／非關鍵」，決定評分時的容忍程度。

模型開發這一側，「演算法檢視儀表板」把多種既有的音樂結構分析基準（涵蓋新穎度、特徵法、張量分解、變點偵測、深度模型等家族）跑在同一首歌上，疊在共用波形下方逐列呈現。評分機制的核心參數是容差窗 τ 與非關鍵權重 w_o（預設 0.5）：只要預測落在任一個合法候選的 τ 範圍內就算命中，關鍵標記權重為 1、非關鍵為 w_o，除了標準 mir_eval 指標，還額外算「平均最近邊界距離」與「關鍵段落召回率」。使用者也能在瀏覽器內的 Python 沙盒（Playground）寫自己的偵測腳本，腳本在隔離的子行程裡執行、有記憶體與時間限制，同一支腳本既能拿來測單曲、也能跑整個語料庫的批次評估，還能反過來當標註輔助：AutoGuess 功能把多個演算法的預測結果做視窗內聚類，只留下同意程度夠高的聚類，做成待審核卡片。

The annotation lifecycle has two parts. In dataset preparation, an admin uploads a batch of audio and sets up a beat grid in one of three modes: static BPM (suggested by five estimators including librosa and madmom), dynamic (an editable tempo curve for drifting tempos), or manual (for live or edited recordings). The annotation tool itself provides four marker types -- single-point Cues, non-overlapping Boundaries, overlapping Spans, and DJ-style repeated Loops/Patterns -- layered over a shared visualization stack (a default 3-band color-coded waveform, with optional spectrogram, MFCC, chromagram, tempogram, self-similarity matrix, novelty curve, onsets, and spectral flux), which can also overlay Demucs-separated vocal/drum/bass/other stems.

Markers can be flagged "multi-candidate" (recording several equally valid timestamps for one event) and "critical" versus "optional," which controls scoring tolerance.

On the model-development side, an "Algorithm Inspect" dashboard runs bundled music-structure-analysis baselines (spanning novelty, feature-based, tensor-decomposition, change-point, and deep-model families) on the same song, each rendered as a row beneath the shared waveform. The core scoring parameters are a tolerance window tau and an optional-marker weight w_o (default 0.5): a prediction counts as a hit if it falls within tau of any valid candidate, critical markers get weight 1 and optional markers w_o, and beyond standard mir_eval metrics the tool also computes a mean nearest-boundary distance and a critical-section recall.

Users can also write their own detection scripts in an in-browser Python sandbox ("Playground"), executed in an isolated child process under memory and time limits; the same script tests a single song or runs batch evaluation across the whole corpus, and can double as an annotation aid -- an "AutoGuess" feature clusters predictions from several algorithms within a time window, keeping only clusters with high enough agreement, and surfaces them as review cards.

限制Limitations

論文本身「沒有正式的限制章節」，不過附錄裡的個案研究透露了幾個隱含的侷限：展示用的語料庫是 EDM（電子舞曲），工具能不能同樣適用於其他曲風並未展示；標記側重「頻段層級」的變化（例如三頻段波形凸顯的低頻/中頻/高頻切換），對廣頻段的整體事件是否一樣好用並不清楚；動態或手動模式是給速度不穩定的音樂用的，暗示靜態 BPM 模式對這類音樂不夠用；音軌分離的視覺化品質完全取決於 Demucs 分離結果的好壞，工具本身假設 Demucs 的分離「夠好」；另外，雖然三頻段波形讓標註可以主要靠「看」就完成，但有些事件終究還是需要「聽」才能判斷。論文也沒有提供任何量化的準確度數字、失敗案例分析，或是跟最接近的競品 LabelBuddy、或 Sonic Visualiser 等既有工具的直接比較。

The paper has "no explicit limitations section," though the case study in the appendix reveals several implicit constraints: the demonstration corpus is EDM (electronic dance music), so it is not shown whether the tool works equally well for other genres; the tool emphasizes band-level changes (the 3-band waveform highlighting bass/mid/treble transitions), leaving unclear whether it serves broadband events equally well; the dynamic and manual beat-grid modes exist specifically for tempo-unstable music, implying the static-BPM mode is inadequate there; the quality of stem visualization depends entirely on Demucs separation quality, which the tool simply assumes is adequate; and while the 3-band waveform lets much annotation happen by sight, some events still require listening to judge correctly. The paper also provides no quantitative accuracy figures, no failure-case analysis, and no direct comparison against its closest competitor LabelBuddy or established tools like Sonic Visualiser.

討論Discussion

論文唯一的量化結果，來自附錄裡一個 109 首歌曲的 EDM 個案研究（涵蓋 Afro/Organic House、Psybass、Glitch Hop、Melodic Techno 等子曲風）。每首歌中位數約有 10 個邊界標記，依類型拆分：drop（咚段）佔 39%、buildup（蓄力段）佔 23%、breakdown（過渡段）佔 11%、intro 與 outro 各佔 10%、bridge 佔 5%、silence（靜音）佔 3%；大約 20% 的邊界被標成「非關鍵」，其餘標成「關鍵」。

整個 109 首歌曲庫（含音軌分離結果、分析資料、MSAF 輸出、BPM、演算法分群結果）總共佔用 1.6 GB 儲存空間。除了這組個案研究的標註統計以外，論文沒有提供任何工具本身的效能指標——沒有跟 LabelBuddy 或其他既有工具的並排比較，也沒有量測標註速度提升、跨標註者一致性改善幅度，或是演算法排名系統本身的準確度，所以「這個工作站到底比既有工具好多少」目前仍缺乏數字佐證。

The paper's only quantitative results come from a 109-track EDM case study in the appendix (spanning Afro/Organic House, Psybass, Glitch Hop, and Melodic Techno subgenres). Each track has a median of about 10 boundary markers, broken down by type: drops account for 39%, buildups 23%, breakdowns 11%, intros and outros 10% each, bridges 5%, and silences 3%; about 20% of boundaries were flagged "optional," the rest "critical." The full 109-track corpus (stems, analysis data, MSAF output, BPM, and algorithm clustering results) occupies 1.6 GB of storage. Beyond this case study's annotation statistics, the paper reports no performance figures for the tool itself -- no side-by-side comparison against LabelBuddy or other existing tools, no measurement of annotation-speed gains or inter-annotator-agreement improvement, and no accuracy figures for the algorithm-ranking system itself, so how much better this workstation is than existing tools remains unquantified.
