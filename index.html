MIDIBack: Harmony-Aware Singing Pitch Correction via Joint Vocal-Accompaniment Symbolic Modeling

MIDIBack:透過人聲與伴奏聯合符號建模做和聲感知式歌聲音高校正

Joaquim Cavalcante, Yicheng Gu, Adriel Trajano, Yuri de Malheiros, Thais Gaudencio
arXiv:2609.28008 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-23
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：單一個以伴奏和聲為條件、聯合人聲與伴奏事件的 OctupleMIDI Transformer,在六種音高毀損情境下整體達到 78.6% 原始音高準確率(RPA),在整體移調加上習得走音的組合情境下達到 81.5%;拿掉伴奏條件後,移調情境準確率從 81.5% 崩跌到 35.8%,這是論文最核心的消融實驗證據,說明校正靠的是和聲脈絡而非單純記憶人聲音高。Why this one: A single OctupleMIDI Transformer that conditions vocal pitch correction on the (uncorrupted) backing track reaches 78.6% overall raw pitch accuracy across six corruption regimes and 81.5% under combined key-outshift plus learned detuning; stripping out the accompaniment collapses outshift-condition accuracy from 81.5% to 35.8%, the paper's central ablation evidence that harmonic context -- not just vocal-note memorization -- drives the correction.

動機Motivation

自動音高校正(APC)必須把「非刻意的走音」和「刻意的表現性音高變化」分開,這件事很難做,因為人聲的音高感知和音色緊密綁在一起:受過訓練的歌手就算明顯偏離目標音,聽起來也不會被認為「走音」,因此聽眾對人聲音高的容忍範圍遠大於音高固定的樂器。傳統 DSP 工具如 Auto-Tune 與 Melodyne 靠靜態、規則式的音階量化來解決這個問題,但在快速轉調或非順階張力音時就很脆弱,通常還需要人工微調。

深度學習式的 APC 系統補上了一部分這個缺口,但仍然浪費了一半可用的訊號。DDPC 只用原始 CQT 頻譜當條件,完全沒有伴奏音軌的音符級複音脈絡;BERT-APC 則以人聲音符加上粗略的節拍與時間中繼資料為條件,卻從沒看過伴奏實際的和弦或調性。MIDIBack 想問的問題是:如果把人聲與伴奏的音符聯合建模進同一個符號序列,模型能不能從當下的和聲脈絡去推斷「應該唱的音高」,而不是只靠人聲本身的先驗習慣?

Automatic pitch correction (APC) must separate genuine intonation errors from expressive pitch variation, which is hard because vocal pitch perception is tightly coupled with timbre: trained singers can land noticeably off a target note without sounding "out of tune," so listeners tolerate far more deviation in voice than in fixed-pitch instruments. Classic DSP tools such as Auto-Tune and Melodyne solve this with static, rule-based scale quantization, which is brittle under rapid harmonic modulation or non-diatonic tension notes and typically needs manual adjustment.

Deep-learning APC systems close part of that gap but still throw away half the available signal. DDPC conditions only on raw CQT spectra, with no note-level polyphonic context from the backing track. BERT-APC conditions on vocal notes plus coarse metrical and temporal metadata, but never looks at the actual chord or key context. MIDIBack's motivating question is whether jointly modeling vocal and backing-track notes in one symbolic sequence lets a model infer the intended pitch from the momentary harmonic context, instead of vocal priors alone.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

DSP 這條線從 TD-PSOLA 與 WORLD 這類訊號處理式移調演算法,一路到商用外掛 Auto-Tune 與 Melodyne,全部都靠死板、依音樂理論規則的音階量化,而且都需要人工先設定調性與音階。可調音高的神經聲碼器與編碼器(如 Source-Filter HiFi-GAN、PeriodGrad、PitchFlower)改善了音質,但它們不是決策者——仍然需要人工指定目標音高曲線,「這一音到底該是哪個音」的決定還是丟回給使用者。

最接近的兩個「會做決策」的學習式系統是 DDPC 與 BERT-APC,MIDIBack 明確是拿這兩個當對照。DDPC 是參考式模型,只用原始 CQT 頻譜當條件,看得到聲學音高但看不到伴奏音軌符號化、音符級的和聲。BERT-APC 是無參考式,靠人聲音符加上粗略的節拍/時間中繼資料預測應唱音高——這是個進步,但依然沒有編碼伴奏實際的和弦音。MIDIBack 的差異是結構性的,而不是提出一個新任務:它把人聲和伴奏音符序列化進同一個 OctupleMIDI 序列,再微調一個預訓練的雙向 Transformer(Adversarial-MidiBERT),讓伴奏和聲跟要校正的人聲音符共用同一個注意力脈絡,而不是把伴奏當成另一條獨立的聲學特徵流。

The DSP lineage runs from TD-PSOLA and WORLD -- signal-processing algorithms for pitch-shifting -- through commercial plugins Auto-Tune and Melodyne, all of which rely on rigid, music-theory-based scale quantization and need a human to set the key and scale by hand. Pitch-controllable neural vocoders and codecs (e.g., Source-Filter HiFi-GAN, PeriodGrad, PitchFlower) improved audio quality but are not decision-makers: they still need a manually specified target pitch curve, pushing the actual "what note should this be" decision back onto the user.

The two closest learned decision-makers are DDPC and BERT-APC, and MIDIBack is explicitly positioned against both. DDPC is a reference-based model conditioned on raw CQT spectra, so it sees acoustic pitch but no symbolic, note-level harmony from the backing track. BERT-APC is reference-free and predicts in-tune targets from the vocal notes plus coarse metrical/temporal metadata -- an improvement, but it still never encodes the backing track's actual chord tones. MIDIBack's difference is structural rather than a new task: it serializes vocal and backing notes into one OctupleMIDI sequence and fine-tunes a pretrained bidirectional Transformer (Adversarial-MidiBERT) on it, so accompaniment harmony becomes part of the same attention context as the vocal notes being corrected, instead of a separate acoustic feature stream.

方法Method

每首歌先被轉成一條符號化時間軸:人聲音符由 GAME 轉譜(輸出的是連續音高中心,不是量化到半音),伴奏由 MuScriptor 轉譜,再由 Madmom 把兩條聲部的起始時間、時值、拍號與速度合併成同一個時鐘。每個音符變成一個 8-tuple(小節、小節內位置、樂器編號、音高、時值、力度、拍號、速度),編號 0 代表人聲,非 0 代表伴奏樂器——這就是骨幹模型要吃的 OctupleMIDI 格式。因為人聲音高是連續值而非量化到半音,MIDIBack 在最接近的兩個半音音高嵌入之間做內插,權重是小數偏移量,讓微分音的細節不必擴充音高詞彙就能保留。

訓練時只毀損人聲音符的音高,同時用三種方式疊加:從 ±0.25 到 ±1.0 半音均勻抽樣的樂句級整體移調、一個另外預訓練、凍結參數的 GRU 走音器產生的逐音自迴歸走音,以及獨立的 ±1.0 半音均勻雜訊。骨幹模型是一個 12 層、隱藏維度 768、12 個注意力頭的 Transformer 編碼器(Adversarial-MidiBERT),序列長度最長到 1024,訓練目標是只在人聲位置還原四捨五入後的轉譜音高,用交叉熵損失計算,而伴奏音軌所有位置的梯度都被擋掉,所以伴奏只能當條件脈絡,永遠不會變成訓練目標。毀損是漸進導入的:前 20 個 epoch 只用乾淨輸入訓練,之後毀損機率在 11,000 步內從 0 線性退火到 0.4。

Each song is first transcribed into a single symbolic timeline: vocal notes come from GAME (yielding continuous pitch centers, not quantized semitones), the backing track is transcribed by MuScriptor, and Madmom merges both streams' onset, duration, time-signature and tempo into one clock. Every note becomes an 8-tuple (bar, position, instrument program, pitch, duration, velocity, time signature, tempo), with program=0 marking vocal notes and non-zero programs marking backing instruments -- this is the OctupleMIDI format the backbone expects. Because vocal pitch is continuous rather than semitone-quantized, MIDIBack interpolates between the two nearest semitone pitch embeddings, weighted by the fractional offset, so microtonal nuance survives without expanding the pitch vocabulary.

Training corrupts only the vocal notes' pitch, three ways at once: a phrase-level key outshift drawn uniformly from ±0.25 to ±1.0 semitones, an autoregressive note-to-note drift from a separately pretrained, frozen GRU detuner, and independent uniform noise in ±1.0 semitones. The backbone -- a 12-layer, 768-dimension, 12-head Transformer encoder (Adversarial-MidiBERT), sequence length up to 1024 -- is trained to recover the rounded transcription pitch at vocal positions only, via cross-entropy loss with gradients blocked at all backing-track positions, so the accompaniment can only act as conditioning context, never as a training target. Corruption is introduced progressively: the first 20 epochs train on clean input, after which the corruption probability is annealed from 0 to 0.4 over 11,000 steps.

限制Limitations

作者自己講得很清楚評估範圍有限:因為真實標籤是模型自己四捨五入後的轉譜輸出,而不是經樂手核對過的樂譜,而且毀損(整體移調、習得走音、均勻雜訊)是合成出來的,不是真實錄音裡自然發生的走音,所以 78.6% 的整體 RPA 量的是「在轉譜衍生的目標上恢復毀損」的能力,不是真實走音錄音上的感知調音品質。論文明確標出這一點,並把「用專業標註資料集、對重新合成的音訊做主觀聽測」列為未來工作,而不是把目前的數字當作端到端音訊品質的替代指標。

論文沒有多談的另一個限制是:整條管線依賴兩個上游轉譜工具——人聲用 GAME、伴奏用 MuScriptor——它們的錯誤會直接傳進 MIDIBack 的和聲條件與它自己的偽真實標籤裡。因為這兩者在這篇論文裡都是凍結、現成的元件,沒有被聯合訓練或單獨評估,轉譜失敗的模式(例如調號判斷錯誤、漏抓和弦音)是這六種毀損情境評估沒有量到的誤差來源。

The authors are explicit about the evaluation's scope: because ground-truth is the model's own rounded transcription output rather than a musician-verified score, and the corruptions (outshift, learned detuning, uniform noise) are synthetic rather than naturally occurring mistakes, the 78.6% overall RPA measures corruption recovery on transcription-derived targets, not perceptual tuning quality on real out-of-tune recordings. The paper explicitly flags this and calls for professionally annotated datasets and subjective listening tests on resynthesized audio as future work, rather than presenting the current numbers as a proxy for end-to-end audio quality.

A further limitation the paper doesn't dwell on: the whole pipeline depends on two upstream transcribers, GAME for vocals and MuScriptor for the backing track, whose errors would propagate directly into MIDIBack's harmonic conditioning and its own pseudo-ground-truth targets. Since both are frozen, off-the-shelf components rather than jointly trained or evaluated here, transcription failure modes (e.g., wrong key signature, missed chord tones) are an unmeasured source of error that the six-corruption-regime evaluation does not capture.

討論Discussion

跟 DDPC 對比時(DDPC 只在它原生的均勻雜訊情境下訓練與評估),MIDIBack 是打平而非壓倒性勝出:Uniform 情境 73.1% 對 74.6%,Detune 情境 80.8%,但在乾淨輸入上明顯領先(95.2% 對 86.1%,把「乾淨輸入被誤改的比例」從 13.9% 壓到 4.8%)。

跟 BERT-APC 對比時(BERT-APC 只在它原生的純走音情境訓練),MIDIBack 在 BERT-APC 的本命情境上打平(走音情境 92.3% 對 95.3%,乾淨情境 97.1% 對 98.6%),但在整體移調情境(36.2% 對 33.7%)以及兩個複合情境(39.4% 對 33.9%;40.0% 對 38.1%)上明顯領先——剛好是伴奏和聲脈絡最該發揮作用的地方。論文最核心的數字是伴奏消融實驗:拿掉伴奏後,移調情境的 RPA 從 81.5% 崩到 35.8%,掉了 45.7 個百分點,這是支撐論文核心論點最有力的證據。

案例研究則從因果面而非單純統計面補強了這個結論:固定人聲音符為真實值,只把伴奏音符在 4 小節或 8 小節的區間內移調 ±0.5 或 ±1.0 半音,模型預測的音高殘差會朝同方向移動,而且在較長的 8 小節區間裡效果更明顯。這是一個動態推理的結果,不只是一個消融數字——它顯示模型是在主動追蹤一個會移動的和聲目標,而不是死記一個固定的旋律先驗,這正是論文自己用來主張 MIDIBack 是「和聲感知」而非只是「和聲制約」的判準。

Against DDPC (trained and evaluated only on uniform-noise corruption, its native regime), MIDIBack is competitive rather than dominant: 73.1% vs. 74.6% on Uniform, 80.8% on Detune, but clearly ahead on clean input (95.2% vs. 86.1%, cutting the corruption-rate-on-clean-input metric from 13.9% to 4.8%).

Against BERT-APC (trained on its native detune-only regime), MIDIBack is comparable on the conditions BERT-APC was built for (92.3% vs. 95.3% detune RPA, 97.1% vs. 98.6% clean) but pulls ahead specifically on outshift (36.2% vs. 33.7%) and the two compound conditions (39.4% vs. 33.9%; 40.0% vs. 38.1%) -- exactly where accompaniment harmony should matter most. The paper's central number is the accompaniment-ablation result: removing it collapses outshift RPA from 81.5% to 35.8%, a 45.7-point drop that is the strongest evidence for the paper's stated thesis.

The case study reinforces this causally rather than just statistically: with vocal notes held fixed at ground truth, shifting only the backing track's notes by ±0.5 or ±1.0 semitones over 4- or 8-bar windows moves the model's predicted pitch residuals in the same direction, with a larger effect over the longer 8-bar window. That is a dynamic-reasoning result, not just an ablation number -- it shows the model is actively tracking a moving harmonic target rather than having memorized a static melodic prior, which is the paper's own criterion for calling MIDIBack "harmony-aware" rather than merely "harmony-conditioned."

Pose-Aware Multimodal Automatic Tagging for Greek Traditional Music

姿態感知的多模態希臘傳統音樂自動標記

Alexandros Alexiou, Charilaos Papaioannou, Alexandros Potamianos
arXiv:2609.27094 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-22
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：在 Lyra 資料集 28 個標籤的舞蹈子集上,單獨用音訊可達 macro ROC-AUC 0.821,但加入影像與舞者骨架序列、用 Gated 融合後可達 0.864(+4.3 個百分點);骨架單獨使用只有 0.586,卻能讓音訊+影像的組合從 0.852 再拉到 0.864,而且相對增益最大的類別正是最難的地理來源標籤。Why this one: Audio alone reaches 0.821 macro ROC-AUC on Lyra's 28-label dance subset, but a Gated-fusion model that adds video and dancer-pose skeleton streams reaches 0.864 (+4.3 points), and skeleton alone -- despite scoring only 0.586 on its own -- still lifts the audio+video pair from 0.852 to 0.864 when added, with the biggest relative gain on the hardest category, geographic-origin tags.

動機Motivation

大多數自動音樂標記系統只用音訊,但現場表演本質上是多模態的:樂器、地區風格、舞蹈形式這些標籤,同時被編碼在聲音、表演者的外觀、以及他們的動作裡。這件事對希臘傳統音樂這類具文化特定性的曲目更重要而非更不重要——一個地區性的舞步或特定服裝,可能跟一個從音訊推出的曲風標籤帶有一樣的語意資訊,而這類曲目在 MIR 基準測試裡本來就偏少。論文問的問題比「影片有沒有幫助標記」更具體:它問的是舞者的姿態——一種完全捨棄外觀、輕量且保護隱私的骨架序列——能不能在音訊之外提供互補訊號,用的是希臘傳統音樂電視節目錄影構成的 Lyra 資料集。

Most automatic music tagging systems work from audio alone, even though live performance is inherently multimodal: instrument, regional-style and dance-form labels are simultaneously encoded in sound, in what performers look like, and in how they move. This matters more, not less, for culturally specific repertoires like Greek traditional music, where a regional dance style or a specific costume can carry the same semantic information as an audio-derived genre tag, and where MIR benchmarks remain thin. The paper's concrete question is narrower than "does video help tagging": it specifically asks whether dancer pose -- a lightweight, privacy-preserving skeleton stream that discards appearance entirely -- carries complementary signal beyond audio, using the Lyra dataset of Greek traditional music broadcasts.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

Lyra 本身就是既有工作,不是論文自己造的對照組:它是一個 2022 年 ISMIR 的資料集,收錄 1,570 支希臘電視音樂表演影片,2023 年的後續論文已經用 Audio Spectrogram Transformer(AST)在上面建立了一個很強的純音訊標記基準,並做了跨文化遷移的實驗。這篇論文明確是延伸那條純音訊的研究線,而不是提出新資料集或新任務——它的貢獻是加入 Lyra 先前工作完全沒碰過的兩個模態:對齊過的影片與姿態衍生的骨架序列。

另外兩條研究線也有助於定位這個方法。第一,像 Oramas 等人的多模態曲風分類、Li 與 Kumar 的跨模態影片對音樂檢索,已經證明視覺資訊能在表演理解任務裡補強音訊,但都沒有針對具文化特定性館藏做多標籤自動標記。第二,姿態建模在音樂與舞蹈領域已經存在——ST-GCN 及其延伸用於骨架動作辨識、Bailando 用於舞蹈生成、Pedersoli 與 Goto 只用影片做舞蹈節拍追蹤——但論文指出的空白是:骨架資料從沒被系統性測試過用於多標籤自動標記,尤其不是在不受控的廣播錄影上,而是在乾淨的動作捕捉資料上。「不受控的廣播錄影」這個限定詞,才是這篇論文真正的切入點:既有的姿態應用於音樂的工作都假設乾淨、單一表演者的畫面,但 Lyra 的影片裡觀眾、樂手、歌手常常同時入鏡。

Lyra itself is prior work, not a baseline the paper invents: it's a 2022 ISMIR dataset of 1,570 Greek television music-performance videos, and a 2023 follow-up paper established Audio Spectrogram Transformer (AST) as a strong audio-only tagging baseline on it, plus cross-cultural transfer results. This paper is explicitly an extension of that audio-only line, not a new dataset or a new tagging task -- its contribution is adding two modalities Lyra's prior work never touched: aligned video and pose-derived skeleton streams.

Two other lines matter for placing the method. First, multimodal MIR work such as Oramas et al.'s multimodal genre classification and Li & Kumar's cross-modal video-to-music retrieval established that visual information complements audio for performance understanding, but none of it targets multilabel autotagging of a culturally specific collection. Second, pose-based modeling for music and dance already exists -- ST-GCN and its extensions for skeleton-based action recognition, Bailando for dance generation, and Pedersoli & Goto for dance-beat-tracking from video alone -- but the paper's stated gap is that skeleton data has not been systematically tested for multilabel autotagging, especially not from unconstrained broadcast footage rather than curated motion-capture data. That "unconstrained broadcast" qualifier is the paper's real point of departure: existing pose-in-music work assumes clean, single-performer footage, while Lyra's videos contain audience members, musicians and singers on screen at once.

方法Method

Lyra 的 1,570 支影片中有 767 支含有舞蹈內容,姿態擷取管線能從中產生有效骨架的有 749 支(分成 533/62/154 的訓練/驗證/測試集),評估用的是資料集前 30 個標籤中的 28 個(有兩個標籤在舞蹈子集裡完全沒有正例)。從原始廣播畫面走到這條骨架序列,本身就是一個五階段的管線:先用一個在 Kinetics-400 上預訓練、只用 300 個人工標註的一秒片段微調過的 MViT 模型偵測舞蹈場景,片段層級準確率達 98%;

接著用 ByteTrack 搭配 YOLO 偵測器追蹤畫面裡的每一個人;每一幀選出主要舞者的方式是取「邊界框面積乘上偵測信心分數」最大者(這樣選是因為畫面裡常同時出現觀眾、樂手與歌手);再用 AlphaPose 從裁切出的主舞者身上擷取 17 個 COCO 關鍵點;最後有一個品質過濾階段,用「跟片段中位姿態的自相似度」濾掉離群姿態,並依關節信心、骨長一致性、左右對稱性與抖動程度給剩下的姿態打分,每個片段最多保留 32 個姿態,少於 8 個有效姿態的片段直接捨棄。

三個模態都被切成對齊的 8 秒片段。音訊用 AST(論文證實是最強的單一模態);影片測試了五種骨幹網路,SlowFast-50 勝出,並在所有融合實驗裡固定當作影片編碼器;骨架用一個輕量的 6 層區塊 ST-GCN 變體,鄰接矩陣是固定而非自適應的,刻意保持簡單以避免在較小、較吵的舞蹈子集上過擬合——而且根據一項明確的消融實驗,骨架編碼器是用較短的 3.69 秒窗訓練,而非對齊用的 8.00 秒窗,因為較長的窗會讓純骨架準確率明顯變差(macro ROC-AUC 0.596 對 0.555)。

論文比較了四種融合策略——晚期融合(機率平均)、早期融合(token 層級的 Transformer 自注意力)、閘控融合(GMU 式、每個模態學一個閘)、跨注意力(MulT 式的成對注意力)——沒有有效舞者姿態的片段,用一個學習出來的「缺失骨架 token」處理,而不是補零,論文顯示這樣做確實比較好(在最佳三模態設定下 macro ROC-AUC 0.864 對 0.860)。

Of Lyra's 1,570 tracks, 767 contain dance content, and the pose pipeline yields valid skeletons for 749 of those (a 533/62/154 train/val/test split), evaluated on 28 of the dataset's top-30 labels (two had zero positive instances in the dance subset). Getting from raw broadcast footage to that skeleton stream is itself a five-stage pipeline: a Kinetics-400-pretrained MViT model, fine-tuned on just 300 manually labeled one-second clips, detects dance scenes at 98% segment-level accuracy;

ByteTrack with a YOLO detector then tracks every person in frame; a primary dancer is picked per frame by maximizing bounding-box area times detection confidence (chosen because the footage often has audience members, musicians and singers on screen simultaneously); AlphaPose extracts 17 COCO keypoints from the cropped primary dancer; and a quality-filtering stage removes outlier poses via a self-similarity-to-medoid filter and scores the rest on joint confidence, bone-length consistency, symmetry and jitter, keeping up to 32 poses per clip and discarding clips with fewer than 8 valid poses.

All three modalities are segmented into aligned 8-second clips. Audio uses AST (the paper's confirmed strongest single modality); video is tested across five backbones, with SlowFast-50 winning and used as the fixed video encoder in every fusion experiment; skeletons use a lightweight 6-block ST-GCN variant with fixed (not adaptive) adjacency, deliberately kept simple to avoid overfitting the noisier, smaller dance subset -- and, per an explicit ablation, trained on shorter 3.69-second windows rather than the aligned 8.00-second ones, because longer windows measurably hurt skeleton-only accuracy (0.596 vs. 0.555 macro ROC-AUC).

Four fusion strategies are compared -- late (probability averaging), early (token-level Transformer self-attention), gated (a GMU-style learned gate per modality), and cross-attention (MulT-style pairwise attention) -- and clips with no valid dancer pose are handled with a learned missing-skeleton token rather than zero-filling, which the paper shows is measurably better (0.864 vs. 0.860 macro ROC-AUC in the best trimodal setting).

限制Limitations

作者直接點出規模與涵蓋率的限制:舞蹈子集只有 749 支影片,而骨架管線只能在大約 50% 的片段裡取得有效姿態,原因是遮蔽、多人混淆與場景轉換——也就是說有一半的舞蹈畫面完全沒有貢獻骨架訊號,這就限制了這個模態能發揮的上限。只選單一主舞者是刻意的取捨,不是疏漏:論文選擇這樣做,是因為在不受控的廣播畫面(觀眾、樂手、歌手同時入鏡)上做完整的多人姿態建模被判斷為「明顯更容易出錯」,但這也代表對於群舞或合舞,除了那位最顯眼的舞者以外,其他所有人帶的資訊在設計上就被丟棄了。

論文沒有特別強調的一個限制是:表 2 裡各融合策略之間 ROC-AUC 的差異,並沒有報告統計顯著性檢定,即使跨隨機種子的標準差(例如三模態 Gated 融合是 0.864±0.003,跨注意力是 0.853±0.009)跟部分被拿來比較的差距是同一個數量級。類別層級分析用的也是 Lyra 既有、為先前純音訊工作設計的前 30 個標籤詞彙;如果換一套對動作更敏感的標籤集,骨架看起來最有幫助的類別可能會改變。

The authors flag scale and coverage directly: the dance subset is 749 videos, and the skeleton pipeline recovers valid poses for only about 50% of clips because of occlusion, multi-person ambiguity and scene transitions -- meaning half the dance footage contributes no skeleton signal at all, which caps how much the modality can help. The single-primary-dancer restriction is a deliberate trade-off, not an oversight: the paper chose it because full multi-person pose modeling on unconstrained broadcast footage (with audience, musicians and singers all in frame) was judged "substantially more error-prone," but it means that for ensemble or group dances, the information carried by everyone except the single most prominent dancer is discarded by construction.

A limitation the paper does not foreground: no statistical significance testing is reported for the ROC-AUC differences between fusion strategies, even though the seed-to-seed standard deviations in Table 2 (e.g., Gated A+V+S at 0.864±0.003 vs. Cross-Attention at 0.853±0.009) are on the same order as some of the gaps being compared. The category-level analysis is also based on Lyra's existing top-30 label vocabulary, defined for the prior audio-only work; a label set designed for movement-sensitive tags might change which categories skeleton appears to help most.

討論Discussion

最主要的數字是:最佳三模態(音訊+影片+骨架,閘控融合)模型 macro ROC-AUC 達 0.864,對比最強的單一模態基準(音訊/AST)0.821,提升 4.3 個百分點;把骨架加進最佳的音訊+影片組合,能再從 0.852 拉到 0.864,又多 1.2 個百分點,即使骨架單獨使用只有 0.586。這種「單獨弱、融合時卻有用」的組合,正是論文提出的假設,而且閘控融合在所有模態組合下的 ROC-AUC 與 PR-AUC 都持續是最強的融合策略,跨注意力則常在 F1 上勝出。

類別層級的細分結果帶出一個值得認真看待的但書:樂器標籤仍然由音訊/影片主導(A+V 的平均 F1 最高,0.508),但論文自己的數字顯示,骨架真正帶來最大幫助的地方集中在曲風、尤其是地理來源標籤——V+S 在地理來源上達到平均 F1 0.324,對照論文自己說的「單一模態都低於 0.086」,這是所有模態各自最難的類別上一個很大的相對躍進。這個型態符合論文自己的論述(動作編碼了音訊與外觀單獨捕捉不到的地區性、身體化認同),而不是「骨架什麼都有幫助」這種齊頭式的結論,比摘要裡那單一個數字所暗示的更精確。

The headline number is a macro ROC-AUC of 0.864 for the best trimodal (audio+video+skeleton, gated fusion) model, versus 0.821 for the strongest unimodal baseline (audio/AST) -- a 4.3-point gain -- and adding skeleton to the best audio+video pair moves it from 0.852 to 0.864, a further 1.2 points, even though skeleton alone scores only 0.586. That combination -- weak in isolation, still useful in fusion -- is exactly the paper's stated hypothesis, and gated fusion is consistently the strongest fusion strategy on ROC-AUC and PR-AUC across every modality combination, while cross-attention tends to win on F1 instead.

The category-level breakdown adds a caveat worth taking seriously: instrument tags stay audio/video-led (best mean F1 0.508 for A+V), but the paper's own numbers show skeleton's biggest wins concentrated in genre and especially geographic-origin tags, where V+S reaches 0.324 mean F1 against unimodal scores the paper itself reports as "below 0.086" -- a large relative jump on the category that was hardest for every single modality alone. That pattern is consistent with the paper's framing (movement encodes regional/embodied identity that audio and appearance alone miss) rather than a uniform "skeleton helps everything" result, and it is a more precise claim than the abstract's single number suggests.

A Stem-Agnostic Approach to Hybrid AI Music Detection

音軌無關的混合式 AI 音樂偵測方法

Richa Namballa, François Rigaud, Romain Hennequin
arXiv:2609.26956 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-22
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把一種新提出的逐頻段「inspectrogram」(局部合成內容機率圖)與目標音軌的維納濾波能量遮罩結合,單一個音軌無關的 CNN 在 MUSDB 的 oracle 分離音軌上做到偽陽性率低於 10%,還能推廣到訓練時沒看過的樂器(吉他、鋼琴,於 MoisesDB 測試);但一旦換成真實的音源分離模型(HTDemucs),貝斯偵測準確率就掉到接近隨機猜測,原因是分離過程把偵測所需的高頻雜訊特徵一併濾掉了。Why this one: By pairing a novel per-band "inspectrogram" of localized synthetic-content probability with a Wiener-filter energy mask of the target stem, a single stem-agnostic CNN reaches under 10% false-positive rate on oracle-separated MUSDB stems and generalizes to instrument types (guitar, piano) never seen in training on MoisesDB -- but bass detection collapses to chance once real source-separation (HTDemucs) is used, because separation discards the high-frequency artifacts the detector needs.

動機Motivation

既有的 AI 音樂偵測器擅長回答一個二元問題——這整首歌是不是完全由 AI 生成?——準確率常常超過 99%,但當生成工具被融入一般的製作流程、而不是用來生出整首歌時,這個問法就站不住腳了。一首「混合」曲目可能是 AI 生成的人聲配上真實樂團伴奏,或反過來,而平台已經回報 AI 生成音訊佔每日新上傳音樂的多數,這讓「該不該標示、要不要排除進推薦、算不算詐欺性串流而要扣款」這類決策的重要性升高——這些決策需要知道的是哪一軌是合成的,而不只是整首歌算不算合成。二元偵測器在這種情境下也表現得很差:它們倚賴的頻譜偽跡,一旦合成音軌跟真實樂器人聲混在一起,顯著度就大幅下降;即使一首混合曲目被正確標記成「含有部分 AI 內容」,一個全域二元分數也完全看不出哪個樂器才是合成的。

Existing AI-music detectors are excellent at a binary question -- is this whole track fully AI-generated? -- often exceeding 99% accuracy, but that framing breaks down as generative tools get folded into ordinary production workflows rather than used to make entire songs. A "hybrid" track might have an AI-generated vocal over a real band, or vice versa, and platforms already report that AI-generated audio makes up the majority of daily new uploads, which raises the stakes for labeling, recommendation-exclusion and demonetization decisions that need to know which stems are synthetic, not just whether the track as a whole is. Binary detectors also degrade badly here: the spectral artifacts they key on become far less salient once a synthetic stem is blended with real instruments and vocals, and even when a hybrid track is correctly flagged as "some AI content present," a global binary score gives no indication of which instrument was the synthetic one.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

目前的二元偵測器(Afchar 等人提出)倚賴生成模型解碼器的轉置卷積層留下的週期性「棋盤格」偽跡,但設計上就假設一首歌不是完全真實就是完全生成,而且因為要針對每個生成平台與編碼器分別重新訓練,泛化能力很差。真正處理混合問題的前兩個嘗試都被明確點名,而且這篇論文是在它們的基礎上做延伸。Rigaud 等人處理兩軌的情況(人聲對伴奏),用的是編碼過的 MUSDB18-HQ 音軌,他們證明最直覺的做法——先分離混音、再對每一軌跑二元偵測器——會失敗,因為音源分離沒辦法乾淨地把偽跡跟生成內容一起分離出來;他們的解法是把局部二元偵測器的輸出,跟目標音軌在各頻帶上的相對訊噪比結合起來。

de la Cruz 等人則改估計一首歌裡有多少比例的能量是生成的,發現偵測效果同時跟音軌種類與頻率有關:AI 生成的鼓與吉他比人聲與貝斯容易抓到,因為它們的生成偽跡落在編碼器保留得比較完整的頻率範圍。這篇論文明確指出自己跟 Rigaud 等人的差異在於精細度:不是每個音軌只算一個相對訊噪比數值,而是建構 inspectrogram——一張完整的二維時頻機率圖——再跟一張對應的時頻能量遮罩做關聯,把 Rigaud 等人「按頻帶算訊噪比」的想法同時延伸到時間軸上,並且從固定的兩軌設定推廣到任何能用能量遮罩定義出來的音軌。

Current binary detectors, developed by Afchar et al., key on periodic "checkerboard" artifacts left by a generative decoder's transposed-convolution layers, but by design they assume a track is either fully authentic or fully generated, and they generalize poorly because they must be retrained per generation platform and codec. The two prior attempts at the actual hybrid problem are both explicitly named and built on. Rigaud et al. tackle the two-stem case (vocals vs. accompaniment) on encoded MUSDB18-HQ stems and show that the obvious pipeline -- separate the mixture, then run a binary detector on each stem -- fails, because source separation doesn't cleanly isolate the artifacts alongside the generated content; their fix combines local binary-detector outputs with the target stem's relative signal-to-noise ratio across frequency bands.

de la Cruz et al. instead estimate what fraction of a track's energy is generated and find detection is both stem- and frequency-dependent: AI-generated drums and guitar are easier to catch than vocals and bass, because their generation artifacts sit at frequencies the codec preserves more strongly. This paper's stated departure from Rigaud et al. specifically is granularity: rather than a single relative-SNR number per stem, it builds the inspectrogram, a full two-dimensional time-frequency probability map, and correlates it with a matching time-frequency energy mask -- extending Rigaud et al.'s frequency-band SNR idea into time as well as frequency, and generalizing from a fixed two-stem setup to any stem definable by an energy mask.

方法Method

整個管線先從一個純二元的基礎模組開始:一個 Band-Split Regression(BS-Reg)集成模型,訓練資料是把 Free Music Archive 的曲目用 EnCodec(24 kbps、48 kHz)自動編碼,產生成對的真實/合成「fakeprint」。每個 5 秒的音訊片段(跳躍間隔 0.25 秒)被切成 32 個涵蓋 0 到 16 kHz、每個 500 Hz 寬的均勻頻帶,每個頻帶各自訓練一個獨立的邏輯迴歸分類器,輸出該頻帶區域性的合成內容機率。論文指出 BS-Reg「從 3 kHz 以上」表現穩定,但在密集的低頻區間比較吵,偽陽性變多、真陽性變少。把這個逐頻帶偵測器沿著一首歌的完整長度滑動,產生的就是 inspectrogram:一張連續的二維時頻機率圖,而不是先前工作那種單一的全域數值或逐頻帶訊噪比數值。

光有偵測信心分數看不出被標記的是哪個樂器,所以 inspectrogram 會跟目標音軌的維納濾波能量遮罩配對:用同樣的 5 秒/0.25 秒跳躍間隔做 STFT,聚合進同樣的 32 個頻帶,得出跟 inspectrogram 維度相同的「音源對混音能量比」。這個遮罩用兩種方式計算——用真實音軌算出的「oracle」版本,以及用 4 軌或 6 軌版 HTDemucs 分離出的音軌算出的實務版本——這樣同一個偵測器就能在有無真實分離模型介入的情況下分別評估。這兩張 32 頻帶的圖疊成一個雙通道、64×32 的輸入窗(大約 20 秒音訊、64 個時間區塊),送進一個 5 層卷積編碼器,接自適應平均池化與一個全連接分類器,用二元交叉熵訓練;某音軌的真實標籤是「1」代表是生成的,「0」代表是真實的,推論時把各窗口的預測平均成單一個曲目層級的機率。

訓練資料是 MUSDB18-HQ 150 首曲目的混合版本,四軌格式(人聲、鼓、貝斯、其他):每首曲目 2⁴=16 種真實/合成音軌組合全部用上,以曲目為單位切成 80/10/10,batch size 256,初始學習率 10⁻⁷,最多訓練 100 個 epoch,連續 10 個 epoch 沒進步就提前停止。泛化能力則用 MoisesDB 的 240 首歌測試,下混成六個音軌(人聲、鼓、貝斯、其他、吉他、鋼琴),其中吉他與鋼琴訓練時完全沒看過。

論文最明確的一個實證發現來自除錯貝斯:用 oracle 音軌訓練的模型整體偽陽性率低於 10%,但一換成真實的 HTDemucs 分離,貝斯偵測就整個崩潰,原因是 HTDemucs 把(有限的)重建保真度都集中在貝斯的 0 到 500 Hz 頻段,把偵測器真正需要的高頻泛音與音頭資訊都丟掉了——而且直接在 MSS 版的貝斯遮罩上重新訓練也沒能解決這個問題,所以作者把貝斯從最終的目標清單中排除,只在人聲、鼓、「其他」,以及訓練時沒看過的吉他和鋼琴上做評估。

The pipeline starts with a purely binary building block: a Band-Split Regression (BS-Reg) ensemble, trained on Free Music Archive tracks auto-encoded through EnCodec (24 kbps, 48 kHz) to create paired real/synthetic "fakeprints." Each 5-second audio chunk (0.25-second hop) is split into 32 uniform 500 Hz bands spanning 0-16 kHz, and an independent logistic regression classifier is trained per band to output that band's local probability of synthetic content. The paper reports BS-Reg is reliable "from 3 kHz upward" but noisier below that, with more false positives and fewer true positives in the dense low-frequency region. Sliding this band-wise detector across a track's full duration produces the inspectrogram: a continuous two-dimensional time-frequency probability map, rather than the single global or per-band-SNR number prior work produced.

Detection confidence alone doesn't say which instrument is being flagged, so the inspectrogram is paired with a Wiener-filter energy mask for one target stem: the same 5-second/0.25-second-hop STFT, aggregated into the same 32 frequency bands, giving a source-to-mixture energy ratio with identical dimensions to the inspectrogram. This mask is computed two ways -- an "oracle" version from ground-truth stems, and a practical version from stems separated by 4- or 6-stem HTDemucs -- so the same detector can be evaluated with and without a real separation model in the loop. The two 32-band maps are stacked into a 2-channel, 64x32 input window (roughly 20 seconds of audio, 64 temporal chunks), fed to a 5-layer convolutional encoder with adaptive average pooling and a dense classifier trained with binary cross-entropy; a stem's ground-truth label is 1 if that stem is generated and 0 if real, and windows are combined by averaging into one track-level probability at inference.

Training data is a hybrid variant of MUSDB18-HQ's 150 tracks, four-stem format (vocals, drums, bass, "other"): every one of the 2^4=16 real/synthetic stem combinations per track is used, split 80/10/10 at the track level with a batch size of 256, an initial learning rate of 10^-7, up to 100 epochs, and early stopping after 10 epochs without improvement. Generalization is tested on MoisesDB's 240 songs, down-mixed to six stems (vocals, drums, bass, other, guitar, piano) not all seen during training.

The paper's clearest empirical finding came from debugging bass: an oracle-trained model reached under 10% false-positive rate overall, but bass detection collapsed once real HTDemucs separation was used, because HTDemucs concentrates its (limited) reconstruction fidelity on bass's 0-500 Hz band and discards the higher-frequency harmonics and transients the detector actually needs -- and retraining directly on MSS-based bass masks did not fix this, so the authors excluded bass from the final target list and evaluate only on vocals, drums, "other," plus the unseen guitar and piano.

限制Limitations

這篇論文對「自己的核心結果被別人的模型卡住」這件事講得異常坦白:「系統效能與實用性從根本上受限於音源分離的品質。」這不是客套話——貝斯偵測一換成 HTDemucs 就崩到接近隨機猜測,以及鋼琴在 MoisesDB 裡數量偏少、常常在曲目裡稀疏或靜音,在測試的兩種 MSS 設定下偽陽性率都明顯偏高,這兩件事直接證明了這個瓶頸。作者自己提的解法——用同一個能量遮罩額外做後處理,先確認某個音軌到底存不存在——是未來工作,不是目前系統已經在做的事。

另外兩個限制是論文自己明講的,不需要讀者自己推論。第一,標籤的設計假設目標音軌不是完全真實就是完全合成;如果一個音軌本身就是混合的(例如一段真實人聲被加上一層 AI 生成的和聲),就落在這種二元逐軌標籤能表示的範圍之外,論文自己說這「仍是一個限制」。第二,所有實驗都只用一種編碼器——24 kbps 的 EnCodec——當作「生成」的唯一代理。論文主張換成不同編碼器或商用生成服務時,只有輕量的 BS-Reg 階段需要重新訓練、下游 CNN 不用動,但這只是作者自己提出、尚未驗證過的假設,不是實驗結果。

The paper is unusually candid that its own headline result is bottlenecked by someone else's model: "system performance and practicality remain inherently bounded by source separation quality." That's not a hedge -- it's demonstrated directly by the bass failure (collapse to near-chance once HTDemucs is in the loop) and by piano, which is underrepresented in MoisesDB, often sparse or silent within a track, and shows a noticeably higher false-positive rate under both MSS settings tested. The authors' own proposed fix -- post-processing that checks whether a stem is even present, using the same energy mask -- is future work, not something the current system does.

Two other limits are explicit in the paper's own framing rather than something a reader has to infer. First, the label formulation assumes a target stem is entirely authentic or entirely synthetic; a stem that is itself a hybrid (say, a real vocal edited with an AI-generated harmony layer) falls outside what the binary per-stem label can represent, and the paper states this "remains a limitation." Second, every experiment uses one codec, EnCodec at 24 kbps, as the sole proxy for "generation." The claim that only the lightweight BS-Reg stage -- not the downstream CNN -- would need retraining for a different codec or a commercial generative service is stated as a hypothesis the authors have not tested, not a result.

討論Discussion

最明確的正面結果是定性但具體的:同一組 CNN 權重,不分音軌種類,在 oracle 與 HTDemucs 分離兩種情境下,都能成功分辨人聲、鼓與「其他」的真實與生成內容,而且從 oracle 訓練換成 MSS 訓練,鼓與「其他」的效能下降被明確形容為「只有輕微」。用 oracle 訓練出來的模型,拿去在 MSS 分離出的遮罩上評估時,表現出乎意料地好;論文把這歸因於 HTDemucs 本身就是在 MUSDB 上訓練的,這個解釋合理,但也提醒讀者,這個「oracle 訓練遷移到 MSS」的結果,對於沒有在同一個語料庫上訓練過的分離系統來說,可能偏樂觀。

論文第二個值得注意的主張,是對訓練時沒看過的樂器種類的泛化能力:吉他與鋼琴在 MUSDB 訓練時被歸進通用的「其他」類別,但用 oracle 訓練出的模型拿去測試 MoisesDB 裡分別標記出來的吉他與鋼琴音軌時,能「相對準確地」辨識出來——這是偵測器學到的是合成音訊的一般性偽跡、而非特定樂器樣式的證據。不過論文自己也很小心地為這個主張加上但書:鋼琴在 MSS 設定下偽陽性率偏高,作者明確指出是兩個因素疊加造成的——HTDemucs 本身已知在分離鋼琴上表現較弱,加上鋼琴在 MoisesDB 曲目裡能量偏低、常常靜音——所以「能泛化到沒看過的樂器」這個結果,更準確的講法應該是「能泛化到能被乾淨分離出來的沒看過的樂器」,這比標題給人的印象要窄一些,但也更誠實。

The clearest positive result is qualitative but specific: a single stem-agnostic model -- the same CNN weights regardless of stem type -- successfully distinguishes real from generated content for vocals, drums and "other" in both the oracle and HTDemucs-separated settings, and the drop in performance from oracle-trained to MSS-trained is described as "only minor" specifically for drums and "other." The oracle-trained model, evaluated with MSS-based masks, was found to do surprisingly well; the paper attributes this to HTDemucs itself having been trained on MUSDB, which is a plausible explanation but also a reminder that the oracle-to-MSS transfer result may be somewhat optimistic for separation systems not trained on the same corpus.

Generalization to instrument types absent from training is the paper's second notable claim: guitar and piano, folded into the generic "other" category during MUSDB training, are picked up "relatively accurately" by the oracle-trained model when tested on MoisesDB's separately labeled guitar and piano stems -- evidence the detector is learning something about synthetic-audio artifacts in general rather than instrument-specific patterns. That claim comes with an asterisk the paper is careful to attach itself: piano's elevated false-positive rate under MSS settings is explained by two compounding factors the authors name explicitly -- HTDemucs' own documented weakness at separating piano, and piano's low, often-silent energy presence in MoisesDB tracks -- so the "generalizes to unseen instruments" result should be read as "generalizes to unseen instruments that separate cleanly," which is a narrower and more honest claim than the headline suggests.
