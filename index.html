MSVAE: Rethinking Musical Semantics in Continuous Latent Spaces

MSVAE：把音樂語意直接寫進連續潛在空間——以 MuQ 為底、聲學只做有界閘控修正的 VAE，並把 64 維潛在切成「旋律和聲」與「歌詞語音」兩個 32 維子空間分別對齊 SheetSage 與 FireRedASR

Guobin Ma, Yuepeng Jiang, Dake Guo, Huakang Chen, Yuan Zhou, Lingxin Xiong, Wenkai Cheng, Jingbin Hu, Hanke Xie, Chunbo Hao, Wenhao Li, Ziqian Ning, Jixun Yao, Jian Zhao, Zhonglin Jiang, Yong Chen, Lei Xie, Pengcheng Zhu
arXiv:2610.17187 · eess.AS; cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-12
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：西北工業大學 ASLP 團隊主張：歌詞對不齊的問題不該全丟給生成器解，而該在 VAE 的潛在空間裡就先把語意排好。他們把 64 維、25 Hz 的潛在切成兩半，一半對齊 SheetSage 的旋律與和弦隱藏層，另一半對齊 FireRedASR 的語音編碼；在同一套 1B 歌詞生成歌曲的流程下，只給未對時的音素前綴，PER 是 0.136，其他表徵在同樣條件下是 0.851 到 1.579。值得讀的是消融做得完整、而且直接比較「在表徵端」與「在生成器端（REPA）」加語意的差別；要保留的是「PER 最低」只比需要句級時間戳的 ACE-Step 1.5（0.141）低 0.005，全篇沒有聽測。Why this one: NPU's ASLP group argues that lyric misalignment should not be left entirely to the generator but handled by organizing semantics inside the VAE latent first. They split a 64-dimensional, 25 Hz latent in half, aligning one half with SheetSage's melody and chord hidden states and the other with FireRedASR's speech encoder; under a shared 1B lyrics-to-song pipeline given only an untimed phoneme prefix, PER is 0.136, while other representations under the same condition score 0.851 to 1.579. It is worth reading for a thorough ablation and a direct comparison of adding semantics on the representation side versus the generator side (REPA); the caveats are that 'lowest PER' is only 0.005 below ACE-Step 1.5 with sentence-level timestamps (0.141), and there is no listening test.

動機Motivation

目前主流的歌詞生成歌曲系統（DiffRhythm、ACE-Step 等）都是在一個連續潛在空間上做擴散或流匹配，而那個潛在空間來自只為波形重建而訓練的 VAE。重建目標保證聲學細節留得住，但沒有任何東西逼旋律、和聲、歌詞這些內容因素在潛在空間裡排得整齊又可預測。結果是生成器除了學分佈，還得自己摸索內容藏在哪裡，歌詞與人聲的對應尤其難學。

現有解法全部在生成器端：句級時間戳、表徵對齊損失、強化學習獎勵、半自回歸解碼。作者的問題是反過來的：能不能做一個 VAE，潛在空間本身就把「作品層級的旋律和聲」與「歌詞層級的語言人聲」兩條語意線分開放好，同時重建品質不掉太多？

Mainstream lyrics-to-song systems (DiffRhythm, ACE-Step and others) run diffusion or flow matching in a continuous latent space produced by a VAE trained only for waveform reconstruction. The reconstruction objective keeps acoustic detail, but nothing forces content factors such as melody, harmony and lyrics to be organized neatly and predictably in the latent. The generator therefore has to learn not only the distribution but also where the content lives, and lyric–vocal correspondence is especially hard to learn.

Existing fixes all sit on the generator side: sentence-level timestamps, representation-alignment losses, reinforcement-learning rewards, semi-autoregressive decoding. The authors ask the reverse question: can a VAE be built whose latent already separates two semantic streams, work-level melody and harmony and lyric-level linguistic-vocal content, without losing much reconstruction quality?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

生成器端的前作論文點得很清楚。DiffRhythm（Ning 等，2025）訓練與推論都要句級時間戳；ACE-Step（Gong 等，2025）用 REPA 把 DiT 中間層同時對齊 MERT 與 mHuBERT，但微調時 mHuBERT 的權重被降到 0.01，以免壓掉器樂聲音——作者拿這點當「異質語意目標放在同一個表徵上會互相干擾」的證據；ACE-Step 1.5 再加上以交叉注意力一致性為獎勵的強化學習；DiffRhythm 2 改用半自回歸的區塊流匹配，代價是放棄整段並行。表徵端最接近的是 SAME（Parker 等，2026），它在 Transformer 自編碼器裡加了語意正則化，但訓練資料沒有人聲歌曲，也不區分旋律和聲與歌詞語音。

論文沒有正面討論、但其實最像的有兩個。第一是視覺生成的 VA-VAE（Yao 等，2025），把 VAE 潛在空間對齊 DINOv2 特徵以緩解「重建好、生成難」的兩難，MSVAE 的對齊損失本質上是同一招搬到音樂。第二是語音編碼器 SpeechTokenizer（Zhang 等，2024），把 HuBERT 蒸餾進第一層 RVQ、其餘層放聲學細節——「指定一塊潛在給語意」這個想法在那裡已經存在。MSVAE 的差別在於指定兩塊、各對一種語意，而且是連續潛在而非離散碼。另外「以 SSL 特徵為底、再補聲學」的後驗設計，也和 X-Codec（Ye 等，2025）把語意特徵與聲學特徵合併後再量化的做法同一系譜。

The paper is clear about generator-side prior work. DiffRhythm (Ning et al., 2025) needs sentence-level timestamps at training and inference; ACE-Step (Gong et al., 2025) uses REPA to align intermediate DiT features with both MERT and mHuBERT, but the mHuBERT weight is cut to 0.01 during fine-tuning to avoid suppressing instruments, which the authors cite as evidence that heterogeneous semantic targets on one representation interfere; ACE-Step 1.5 adds reinforcement learning with a cross-attention consistency reward; DiffRhythm 2 switches to semi-autoregressive block flow matching, giving up full-sequence parallelism. On the representation side the closest is SAME (Parker et al., 2026), which adds semantic regularization to a Transformer autoencoder but trains on no vocal songs and does not separate melody–harmony from lyric content.

Two works the paper does not discuss head-on are in fact the closest. One is VA-VAE from image generation (Yao et al., 2025), which aligns VAE latents with DINOv2 features to ease the reconstruction-versus-generation dilemma; MSVAE's alignment loss is essentially that trick moved to music. The other is SpeechTokenizer (Zhang et al., 2024), which distills HuBERT into the first RVQ layer and leaves acoustic detail to the rest, so 'reserve part of the latent for semantics' already exists there. MSVAE differs by reserving two parts, one per semantic type, in a continuous rather than discrete latent. Its 'SSL base plus acoustic correction' posterior also belongs to the same lineage as X-Codec (Ye et al., 2025), which merges semantic and acoustic features before quantization.

方法Method

架構。 輸入立體聲波形，輸出 64 維、25 Hz 的高斯潛在。後驗的「底」來自凍結的 MuQ 第 6 層特徵（左右聲道各自處理），經投影得到均值與對數變異數；另有一個可訓練的 DAC 式卷積聲學編碼器，只能預測有上界的修正量，而且每一維的修正要乘上一個由 MuQ 投影特徵算出的 sigmoid 閘。作者稱之為語意優先的閘控殘差後驗：聲學分支沒有一條可以完全取代 MuQ 的通道。解碼器是 BigVGAN 式。

分區雙流對齊。 取樣出的潛在沿通道切成兩個 32 維分區。前半經投影頭對齊 SheetSage 旋律轉譜與和弦辨識 Transformer 的隱藏層（以拍為單位，依時長重複展開到 25 Hz），後半對齊 FireRedASR-AED-L 編碼器最後一層（原生約 25 Hz，不需插值）；損失是沿特徵軸的餘弦相似度，兩個參考編碼器都只在離線抽特徵時使用。KL 也分兩區各自計算。對照組是讓兩個參考都對齊全部 64 維。

訓練。 約 60 萬首、4 萬小時的人聲與器樂曲，24 kHz 立體聲、4 秒片段，訓練 50 萬步；之後凍結編碼器，只用 30 萬首、2 萬小時把解碼器再精修 50 萬步到 48 kHz 輸出。損失包括左右聲道多解析度 STFT、和差 STFT、MPD/MRD/MSS-CQT 判別器、權重 0.01 的 KL，以及權重 50 的對齊損失。

Architecture. A stereo waveform goes in and a 64-dimensional, 25 Hz Gaussian latent comes out. The posterior's base comes from frozen MuQ layer-6 features (each stereo channel processed separately), projected into a mean and log-variance; a trainable DAC-style convolutional acoustic encoder can only predict bounded corrections, and each dimension's correction is multiplied by a sigmoid gate computed from the projected MuQ feature. The authors call this a semantic-first gated residual posterior: the acoustic branch has no path that can fully replace MuQ. The decoder is BigVGAN-style.

Partitioned dual-stream alignment. The sampled latent is split along channels into two 32-dimensional partitions. The first is projected and aligned with hidden states from SheetSage's melody-transcription and chord-recognition Transformers (beat-indexed, repeated by duration onto the 25 Hz grid); the second is aligned with the final FireRedASR-AED-L encoder layer (natively about 25 Hz, no interpolation). The loss is cosine similarity along the feature axis, and both reference encoders are used only for offline feature extraction. KL is also computed per partition. The control aligns both references with all 64 dimensions.

Training. About 600k vocal and instrumental tracks (40k hours) at 24 kHz stereo, 4-second crops, 500k steps; then the encoder is frozen and only the decoder is refined for another 500k steps on 300k tracks (20k hours) to output 48 kHz. Losses include per-channel multi-resolution STFT, sum-and-difference STFT, MPD/MRD/MSS-CQT discriminators, KL weighted 0.01 and alignment weighted 50.

限制Limitations

「PER 最低」的幅度很小。 未對時的 MSVAE 是 0.136，需要句級時間戳的 ACE-Step 1.5 是 0.141，差 0.005。真正大的差距是在同樣不給時間戳的條件下：其他表徵 0.851 到 1.579，等於根本沒照歌詞唱。所以這篇的貢獻是「去掉時間戳仍可用」，而不是「比時間戳方法更準」。給 MSVAE 時間戳則 PER 降到 0.069，但 SongEval 五項全部下降，作者把這解讀成取捨。

重建品質的跳躍沒有被解釋。 消融表裡，閘控殘差後驗在不對齊、全維對齊、只對 SheetSage、只對 ASR 四種設定下 PESQ 都卡在 1.45–1.47，幾乎就是只用 MuQ 的 1.478；只有分區對齊（2.253）與 Chroma+ASR 分區（2.206）才「解鎖」重建。這表示閘門在多數設定下近乎關閉，而什麼條件讓它打開，論文只描述現象沒有分析，這對想沿用這個後驗的人是風險。

評估全是自動指標。 附錄的限制章節自己承認沒有受控聽測；PER 是先用 YingMusic-Singer 分離人聲、再用 Qwen3-ASR 轉寫，200 首評估歌詞也是 AI 生成的。下游只試了非自回歸流匹配生成器。48 kHz 輸出是解碼器端的頻寬補足，編碼器仍只看 24 kHz。和 SAME 的比較也不是同一預算：SAME 是 256 維，重建分數（SAME-L PESQ 2.886）明顯較高。

The 'lowest PER' margin is small. Untimed MSVAE scores 0.136 and ACE-Step 1.5 with sentence-level timestamps scores 0.141, a gap of 0.005. The large gap appears under the same no-timestamp condition: other representations score 0.851 to 1.579, meaning they effectively ignore the lyrics. The contribution is thus 'still usable without timestamps', not 'more accurate than timestamp methods'. Giving MSVAE timestamps lowers PER to 0.069 but lowers all five SongEval scores, which the authors read as a trade-off.

The jump in reconstruction quality is not explained. In the ablation, the gated residual posterior stays at PESQ 1.45–1.47 with no alignment, full-dimensional alignment, SheetSage only, or ASR only, almost exactly the MuQ-only 1.478; only partitioned alignment (2.253) and partitioned Chroma+ASR (2.206) 'unlock' reconstruction. The gate is apparently near-closed in most settings, and the paper describes rather than analyzes what opens it, which is a risk for anyone reusing the posterior.

Evaluation is entirely automatic. The appendix limitations admit there is no controlled listening test; PER is computed by separating vocals with YingMusic-Singer and transcribing with Qwen3-ASR, and the 200 evaluation lyrics are AI-generated. Downstream use is tested only with non-autoregressive flow-matching generators. The 48 kHz output is decoder-side bandwidth extension; the encoder still sees 24 kHz. The comparison with SAME is not budget-matched either: SAME is 256-dimensional and reconstructs clearly better (SAME-L PESQ 2.886).

討論Discussion

數字。 在 MUSDB18-HQ 重建上，同為 64 維的對手中 MSVAE PESQ 2.253，高於 ACE-Step 1.5 的 1.912 與 Stable Audio Open 的 1.784，STOI 0.740 則略低於 ACE-Step 的 0.756。MARBLE 線性探測五項都贏其他自編碼器：情緒效價 R² 0.469 對 0.267、喚醒度 0.672 對 0.578、和弦 0.634 對 0.548、調性 0.634 對 0.609、拍點 F1 0.576 對 0.506。文字生成音樂上 FAD 從 0.660 降到 0.547、CCS 從 0.698 升到 0.735，CLAP 0.174 略低於 SAME 的 0.179–0.180。

最有說服力的對照。 固定同一個未對齊的聲學 VAE、只在生成器端加 REPA：對齊 MERT+mHuBERT 時 PER 1.394 降到 0.694；對齊同樣的 SheetSage+ASR 時 PER 降到 0.198，但 SongEval 音樂性從 3.09 掉到 2.76。把同一組對齊改放在 VAE 訓練裡，PER 0.182、音樂性 3.28。同樣的語意參考、放的位置不同，結果不同，這是全篇最乾淨的一組證據。另一個有趣的結果是器樂：只對 SheetSage 的 FAD 是 0.662，加上 ASR 分區後是 0.547，作者的解釋是兩半都有指定目標時，旋律和聲資訊比較不會外溢到另一半。

子空間真的分工了嗎。 GiantSteps 調性的 5-NN 純度在分區設定下前半 0.555、後半 0.076（隨機基準 0.046），全維對齊則兩半差不多（0.436 對 0.379）。把一半歸零再解碼的聽感描述也一致：只留前半得到伴奏與哼唱式旋律、沒有歌詞；只留後半得到能聽懂的歌聲與稀疏節奏。後者只是質性描述，沒有量化。

對領域的意義。 如果結果成立，歌曲生成的對齊工程可以部分前移到表徵設計，生成器就不必依賴時間戳；90 秒與 150 秒從頭訓練的版本 PER 分別是 0.175 與 0.236，代表長度一拉長仍會退化。程式碼與權重已公開，下一步最需要的是聽測與自回歸生成器上的驗證。

Numbers. On MUSDB18-HQ reconstruction, among 64-dimensional rivals MSVAE reaches PESQ 2.253, above ACE-Step 1.5 (1.912) and Stable Audio Open (1.784), while its STOI of 0.740 is slightly below ACE-Step's 0.756. It beats the other autoencoders on all five MARBLE linear probes: valence R² 0.469 vs 0.267, arousal 0.672 vs 0.578, chords 0.634 vs 0.548, key 0.634 vs 0.609, beat F1 0.576 vs 0.506. In text-to-music, FAD drops from 0.660 to 0.547 and CCS rises from 0.698 to 0.735, while CLAP at 0.174 is slightly below SAME's 0.179–0.180.

The most convincing control. Fix one unaligned acoustic VAE and add REPA only on the generator side: aligning with MERT+mHuBERT lowers PER from 1.394 to 0.694; aligning with the same SheetSage+ASR lowers it to 0.198 but drops SongEval musicality from 3.09 to 2.76. Putting the same alignment into VAE training instead gives PER 0.182 and musicality 3.28. Same semantic references, different placement, different result: this is the cleanest evidence in the paper. Another interesting result is instrumental music: SheetSage-only alignment gives FAD 0.662 and adding the ASR partition gives 0.547; the authors suggest that when both halves have assigned targets, melody–harmony information leaks less into the other half.

Do the subspaces really specialize? GiantSteps key 5-NN purity under partitioning is 0.555 for the first half and 0.076 for the second (chance 0.046), while full-dimensional alignment leaves both halves similar (0.436 vs 0.379). Listening descriptions from zeroing one half agree: keeping only the first half yields accompaniment and hummed melody with no lyrics; keeping only the second yields intelligible singing over sparse rhythm. The latter is qualitative only, not quantified.

What it means for the field. If the results hold, part of the alignment engineering in song generation can move into representation design, freeing the generator from timestamps; models trained from scratch at 90 and 150 seconds reach PER 0.175 and 0.236, so it still degrades with length. Code and weights are released; what is most needed next is a listening test and validation with autoregressive generators.

Sori: Streamable and Controllable Model for Non-musical Audio to Symbolic Music Transformation

Sori：把咳嗽、雨聲這類非音樂聲音即時轉成 MIDI——以領域對抗訓練把非音樂音訊拉近音樂特徵空間，再用無分類器引導的連續參數 γ 調整「貼近輸入」與「好聽」之間的取捨

Kyungsu Kim, Yejin Kim, Kyogu Lee
arXiv:2610.15474 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-10-11
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：首爾大學 Kyogu Lee 團隊定義了一個新任務：把非音樂聲音（咳嗽、腳步、雨聲）轉成符號音樂，並明說它是一對多、沒有唯一正解。做法是用轉譜模型的架構，只在音樂資料上學轉譜，再以領域對抗訓練讓編碼器分不出音樂與非音樂，最後用 CFG 的 γ 在推論時調整。在 AudioSet 評估集上，γ=1.0 時 onset F1 0.327、MSS 18.3，三個對照的轉譜模型最好分別是 0.300 與 21.7，主觀音樂性 3.27 對最高 2.37。值得讀的是任務定義與兩軸評估很清楚，模型也真能在 M3 CPU 上即時跑；要保留的是聽測只有 21 人，AudioSet 評估集沒有濾掉音樂片段，而 onset F1 似乎會隨音符密度上升。Why this one: Kyogu Lee's group at Seoul National University defines a new task, turning non-musical sounds (coughs, footsteps, rain) into symbolic music, and states plainly that it is one-to-many with no unique answer. The approach uses a transcription architecture trained for transcription on musical data only, applies domain adversarial training so the encoder cannot tell music from non-music, and exposes a CFG scale γ at inference. On the AudioSet evaluation set, γ=1.0 gives onset F1 0.327 and MSS 18.3, against the best of three reference transcription models at 0.300 and 21.7, with subjective musicality 3.27 versus at most 2.37. It is worth reading for a clear task definition and two-axis evaluation, and the model really runs in real time on an M3 CPU; the caveats are only 21 listeners, an AudioSet evaluation set not filtered for music clips, and an onset F1 that appears to rise with note density.

動機Motivation

作者從具體音樂（musique concrète）以來的傳統出發：聲音是否有音樂性，取決於聽者怎麼詮釋，而不是聲音本身。他們想把這件事做成一個可計算的任務——非音樂音訊轉符號音樂（NASMT），輸入是講話、環境噪音或日常聲響，輸出是一段 MIDI。

這和自動轉譜（AMT）的根本差別是：AMT 假設音訊與樂譜一對一，NASMT 則是一對多，一段雨聲可以有很多種合理的音樂詮釋。所以評估要分兩軸：輸入對應度（輸出多貼近輸入的時間與頻譜結構）與音樂性（輸出本身是否連貫）。兩者天生互相拉扯，越貼近輸入越不像音樂。作者希望這個取捨在推論時連續可調，而且模型夠輕、能在現場演出或聲音裝置裡即時跑。

The authors start from the tradition running back to musique concrète: whether a sound is musical depends on how the listener interprets it, not on the sound itself. They want to turn this into a computable task, Non-musical Audio to Symbolic Music Transformation (NASMT), whose input is speech, environmental noise or everyday sound and whose output is a MIDI sequence.

The fundamental difference from automatic music transcription (AMT) is that AMT assumes a one-to-one mapping between audio and score, whereas NASMT is one-to-many: a recording of rain admits many valid musical interpretations. Evaluation therefore has two axes: input correspondence (how closely the output follows the input's temporal and spectral structure) and musicality (whether the output is coherent on its own). The two pull against each other; the closer to the input, the less musical. The authors want this trade-off continuously adjustable at inference, and the model light enough to run in real time in live performance or sound installations.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

方法上幾乎每一塊都有直接的前作。領域對抗訓練（DAT，Ganin 等，2016）的用法照搬 Sato 與 Akama（ICME 2024）：他們用合成資料訓練轉譜、再以對抗式領域混淆讓模型適應真實錄音；Sori 把「真實錄音」換成「非音樂聲音」，這是全篇最關鍵、也最像既有技巧的一步。逐幀稀疏事件表示來自 Wei 等（ISMIR 2024）的串流鋼琴轉譜；每個時間步內的巢狀自回歸解碼來自 Nested Music Transformer（Ryu 等，ISMIR 2024）；自回歸模型上的 CFG 則沿用 Sanchez 等（ICML 2024）。

對照組選的是三個不分樂器的單軌轉譜模型：Basic Pitch（Bittner 等，2022）、Deep Salience（Bittner 等，2017）與 Timbre-Trap（Cwitkowitz 等，2024）。作者自己也說它們是為音樂輸入設計的，只能當參考點。論文沒提到但概念上相近的，是 DDSP 的 Tone Transfer（Engel 等，2020），它把任意單音輸入轉成樂器聲音；差別是 Tone Transfer 是音訊到音訊、追蹤 F0 與響度，Sori 輸出的是離散 MIDI 事件，而且刻意不追求忠實。商用 DAW 裡「把旋律或鼓聲轉成 MIDI」的功能本質上是 AMT，也就是 Sori 的 γ 拉到很高那一端。

Almost every component has a direct predecessor. Domain adversarial training (DAT, Ganin et al., 2016) is applied following Sato and Akama (ICME 2024), who trained transcription on synthetic data and adapted it to real recordings through adversarial domain confusion; Sori replaces 'real recordings' with 'non-musical sound', the step that matters most and is closest to an existing technique. The frame-aligned sparse event representation comes from Wei et al.'s streaming piano transcription (ISMIR 2024); nested autoregressive decoding within each timestep comes from the Nested Music Transformer (Ryu et al., ISMIR 2024); CFG for autoregressive models follows Sanchez et al. (ICML 2024).

The references are three instrument-agnostic single-track transcription models: Basic Pitch (Bittner et al., 2022), Deep Salience (Bittner et al., 2017) and Timbre-Trap (Cwitkowitz et al., 2024). The authors themselves note these were designed for musical input and serve only as reference points. Conceptually close but not mentioned is DDSP's Tone Transfer (Engel et al., 2020), which turns arbitrary monophonic input into instrument sound; the difference is that Tone Transfer is audio-to-audio and tracks F0 and loudness, while Sori outputs discrete MIDI events and deliberately does not aim for fidelity. The 'convert melody or drums to MIDI' features in commercial DAWs are essentially AMT, i.e. the high-γ end of Sori's range.

方法Method

架構。 16 kHz 輸入轉成 512 頻帶的 log-Mel（hop 800 樣本，即 50 ms），進入 8 層、寬 768 的 Transformer 編碼器。時間解碼器（8 層）以因果交叉注意力為每個時間步產生一個情境向量；事件解碼器（4 層）在每個時間步內自回歸地生成數量不定的音符事件。詞彙共 530 個：88 個 note-off、88 音高乘 5 個力度級距的 440 個 note-on、EOS 與 PAD。每步解出的事件轉成 4 通道鋼琴捲簾（onset、offset、力度、持續中），餵回下一步的時間解碼器。為了限制每幀計算量，每個時間步最多解 4 個事件，多的以溢出旗標延到下一幀；訓練資料中 99.52% 的時間步不需要溢出。配合 ALiBi 位置編碼與 attention-sink KV 快取，可以串流超過訓練長度。推論時共 153M 參數。

訓練。 解碼器只看過音樂：仿 Nlakh 流程，用 NSynth 衍生的取樣樂器渲染 Lakh MIDI，得到 953 種樂器、953 萬個 5 秒訓練片段。DAT 用 AudioSet 非平衡訓練集可下載的 165 萬個片段；判別器從 CLS 表徵判斷特徵來自音樂或非音樂。前 5 萬步只訓練轉譜，之後每 5 次判別器更新配 1 次編碼器與解碼器更新，對抗損失權重 0.01，共 30 萬步。訓練時以 0.2 機率把編碼器輸出換成可學的空嵌入，供 CFG 的無條件分支使用。

控制。 推論時 logits 依 γ 在條件與無條件之間做仿射組合：γ=0 完全不看輸入，γ=1 是一般條件生成，γ>1 放大輸入影響。巢狀解碼讓 CFG 作用在每個時間步內的聯合事件分佈上。

Architecture. 16 kHz input becomes a 512-bin log-Mel spectrogram (hop 800 samples, i.e. 50 ms) fed to an 8-layer, width-768 Transformer encoder. A Temporal Decoder (8 layers) uses causal cross-attention to produce one context vector per timestep; an Event Decoder (4 layers) autoregressively generates a variable number of note events within each timestep. The vocabulary has 530 tokens: 88 note-offs, 440 note-ons (88 pitches times 5 velocity bins), EOS and PAD. Decoded events become a 4-channel piano roll (onset, offset, velocity, active) fed back to the Temporal Decoder at the next step. To bound per-frame cost, at most 4 events are decoded per timestep and surplus events spill to the next frame via a flag; 99.52% of training timesteps need no spillover. With ALiBi and an attention-sink KV cache it can stream beyond the training length. The model has 153M parameters at inference.

Training. The decoder only ever sees music: following the Nlakh pipeline, Lakh MIDI is rendered with NSynth-derived sample instruments, giving 953 instruments and 9.53M five-second training segments. DAT uses 1.65M downloadable clips from AudioSet's unbalanced training split; the discriminator judges from a CLS representation whether features come from music or non-music. The first 50k steps train transcription only; after that, every 5 discriminator updates are paired with 1 encoder–decoder update, adversarial weight 0.01, for 300k steps total. During training the encoder output is replaced by a learned null embedding with probability 0.2 to train the unconditional branch for CFG.

Control. At inference, logits are combined affinely between conditional and unconditional according to γ: γ=0 ignores the input, γ=1 is standard conditional generation, γ>1 amplifies the input's influence. Nested decoding lets CFG act on the joint event distribution within each timestep.

限制Limitations

作者自己列的三條。 AudioSet 評估集沒有濾掉含音樂的片段，所以評估集並非全是非音樂；巢狀自回歸設計的貢獻沒有單獨消融；FMD 的參考分佈是 Nlakh 測試集，它和訓練資料同樣出自 Lakh MIDI，因此 FMD 可能高估「像訓練資料」而不是一般意義的音樂性。

onset F1 似乎被音符密度左右。 γ=0 時兩個模型都完全不看輸入，理論上對應度應該接近隨機。但 Sori 在 γ=0 的 onset F1 是 0.087，沒有 DAT 的版本卻是 0.282，MSS 也從 57.2 降到 25.4。兩者唯一差別是訓練方式，這最可能的解釋是無條件輸出的音符密度不同，而密集的音符本來就更容易碰到偵測出的 onset。這表示表中「拿掉 DAT 對應度更好」的結論，至少有一部分可能只是音符較多。論文沒有報告音符密度，無法排除。

主觀評估規模小。 剔除 12 位注意力檢查不過的人之後只剩 21 位，每人評 20 組、13 種條件。Friedman 檢定顯示 γ 的效果顯著（p<0.001），但各條件之間兩兩差距是否顯著沒有報告，例如 γ=1.0 的對應度 2.61 對 Deep Salience 的 2.63。另外，即時性的餘裕不大：M3 CPU 上平均 RTF 0.82、最大 0.90。

The three the authors list. The AudioSet evaluation set is not filtered for music-containing clips, so it is not entirely non-musical; the contribution of the nested autoregressive design is not ablated separately; and FMD's reference distribution is the Nlakh test set, which comes from the same Lakh MIDI corpus as training, so FMD may reward 'resembles the training data' rather than musicality in general.

Onset F1 appears driven by note density. At γ=0 both models ignore the input, so correspondence should be near chance. Yet Sori scores onset F1 0.087 at γ=0 while the no-DAT version scores 0.282, and MSS drops from 57.2 to 25.4. The only difference is training, and the likeliest explanation is that unconditional outputs differ in note density, with denser notes simply hitting detected onsets more often. This means the table's conclusion that removing DAT improves correspondence may be at least partly just more notes. The paper does not report note density, so this cannot be ruled out.

Subjective evaluation is small. After excluding 12 participants who failed the attention check, 21 remain, each rating 20 sets of 13 conditions. A Friedman test shows γ has a significant effect (p<0.001), but pairwise significance is not reported, e.g. γ=1.0's correspondence of 2.61 versus Deep Salience's 2.63. The real-time margin is also modest: mean RTF 0.82 and maximum 0.90 on an M3 CPU.

討論Discussion

γ 的掃描結果。 對 Sori，γ 從 0、0.5、1、2、3 一路增加時，onset F1 是 0.087、0.236、0.327、0.363、0.368，MSS 從 57.2 降到 11.8，客觀對應度單調變好但在 γ=2 之後趨緩；主觀對應度在 γ=2 達到 2.97 後於 γ=3 降回 2.52。音樂性則是倒 U 形：主觀 3.34（γ=0.5）、3.27（γ=1）、2.59（γ=2）、2.20（γ=3），FMD 在 γ=0.5 最好（33.6）、γ=3 時升到 248.0。所以 γ 確實是一個可用的旋鈕，最實用的區間是 0.5 到 1。

對照轉譜模型。 γ=1.0 時 Sori 的 onset F1（0.327）與 MSS（18.3）都勝過三個參考模型（Deep Salience 0.300 與 21.7 是其中最好），FMD 51.3 對 181.8–472.7，主觀音樂性 3.27 對 1.51–2.37，主觀對應度 2.61 則與 Deep Salience 的 2.63 相當。這個比較對參考模型不太公平，它們本來就不是為非音樂輸入設計，作者也這麼說。

DAT 到底做了什麼。 拿掉 DAT 後，在 γ≥1 時音樂性下降最明顯：γ=1 主觀 3.27 降到 2.72、γ=2 從 2.59 降到 2.07，FMD 也全面變差。作者因此說 DAT 是把取捨往音樂性那邊推，而不是全面提升。但 γ=0 的主觀音樂性反而是沒有 DAT 的較高（2.78 對 2.19），這是個沒被討論的現象：無條件分支理應與 DAT 無關，對抗訓練卻似乎讓它變差了。

意義。 這篇的價值主要在把一個藝術實踐問題定義成可評估的任務，並給出第一個可串流的基線；方法本身是幾個既有零件的組合。後續若要建立這個任務，最需要的是乾淨的非音樂評估集，以及不受音符密度影響的對應度指標。

The γ sweep. For Sori, as γ goes 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, onset F1 is 0.087, 0.236, 0.327, 0.363, 0.368 and MSS falls from 57.2 to 11.8, so objective correspondence improves monotonically but flattens after γ=2; subjective correspondence peaks at 2.97 at γ=2 and falls back to 2.52 at γ=3. Musicality is an inverted U: subjective 3.34 (γ=0.5), 3.27 (γ=1), 2.59 (γ=2), 2.20 (γ=3), and FMD is best at γ=0.5 (33.6) and rises to 248.0 at γ=3. So γ is a usable knob, with 0.5 to 1 the most practical range.

Against transcription models. At γ=1.0, Sori's onset F1 (0.327) and MSS (18.3) beat all three references (the best being Deep Salience at 0.300 and 21.7), FMD is 51.3 versus 181.8–472.7, subjective musicality 3.27 versus 1.51–2.37, and subjective correspondence 2.61 is on par with Deep Salience's 2.63. The comparison is not quite fair to the references, which were not designed for non-musical input, as the authors acknowledge.

What DAT actually does. Without DAT, musicality drops most at γ≥1: subjective 3.27 to 2.72 at γ=1 and 2.59 to 2.07 at γ=2, and FMD worsens throughout. The authors therefore say DAT shifts the trade-off toward musicality rather than improving everything. But at γ=0 the no-DAT model actually has higher subjective musicality (2.78 vs 2.19), an undiscussed result: the unconditional branch should be unrelated to DAT, yet adversarial training seems to have made it worse.

Significance. The paper's value lies mainly in turning an artistic practice into an evaluable task and offering the first streamable baseline; the method itself combines existing parts. To establish the task, what is most needed next is a clean non-musical evaluation set and a correspondence metric unaffected by note density.

The MPB Corpus: A Dataset of Melody, Rhythm, Harmony, and Melody–Harmony Relationships in Brazilian Popular Music

MPB 語料庫：巴西流行音樂十位作曲家、500 首作品的旋律輪廓、旋律節奏、和弦類型系譜與旋律—和聲關係標註，以及作曲家節奏風格的置換檢定

Carlos de L. Almada, Hugo T. de Carvalho, Felipe D. Martins

為什麼挑這篇：里約聯邦大學團隊花了約五年，由同一位樂理專家從出版歌本手工分析 Jobim、Chico Buarque、Caetano Veloso 等十位 MPB 作曲家各 50 首作品，釋出 8,426 個旋律片段的輪廓與節奏編碼、17,053 個和弦的功能分析，以及 23,447 個音符的旋律—和聲關係，CC-BY-4.0。因為來源歌本有版權，資料只有衍生的符號編碼，沒有音訊、MIDI 或 MusicXML。值得讀的是它補上英語流行樂以外極少見的和聲與旋律同時標註，而且分析模型（161 種和弦類型的系譜、26 個節奏字母）第一次以英文完整發表；要保留的是只有一位標註者、沒有一致性檢驗，而用來示範的節奏風格檢定 R=1.072 雖然顯著（p<10⁻⁴），效果量其實不大。Why this one: A team at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro spent about five years having one music-theory expert hand-analyze 50 works each by ten MPB composers, including Jobim, Chico Buarque and Caetano Veloso, from published songbooks, releasing contour and rhythm encodings for 8,426 melodic segments, functional analysis for 17,053 chords and melody–harmony relations for 23,447 notes under CC-BY-4.0. Because the source songbooks are copyrighted, the data contain only derived symbolic encodings, with no audio, MIDI or MusicXML. It is worth reading because joint harmony and melody annotation is rare outside anglophone pop, and the analytical models (a genealogy of 161 chord types, a 26-letter rhythm alphabet) appear in full in English for the first time; the caveats are a single annotator with no agreement check, and the demonstration test of rhythmic style, R=1.072, is significant (p<10⁻⁴) but small in effect size.

動機Motivation

MPB（música popular brasileira）不是「所有巴西流行音樂」，而是 1955 年到 1990 年代、以 Tom Jobim、Chico Buarque、Caetano Veloso、Milton Nascimento 等人為核心的一個特定美學圈：和聲高度半音化與密集，節奏承襲森巴、蕭樂、巴伊昂等強烈切分的巴西類型，旋律常把和聲張力音放在結構位置上。它在 20 世紀巴西文化史上地位核心，卻幾乎沒有被做過結構化與量化研究。

作者的研究問題是：在旋律輪廓、旋律節奏、和聲以及旋律—和聲關係這四個面向上，哪些特徵是這個美學共享的常規（他們仿 Tymoczko 的「延伸共同實踐」稱為 MPB Common Practice），哪些偏離常規、構成個別作曲家的風格？要回答它，先得有資料。而在 MIR 這一側，巴西流行音樂的結構資料極少，版權又讓樂譜與錄音無法公開，所以他們選擇釋出衍生的分析編碼，而不是原始素材。

MPB (música popular brasileira) does not mean 'all Brazilian popular music' but a specific aesthetic circle from 1955 to the 1990s centred on Tom Jobim, Chico Buarque, Caetano Veloso, Milton Nascimento and others: highly chromatic and dense harmony, rhythm drawn from strongly syncopated Brazilian genres such as samba, choro and baião, and melodies that often place harmonic tensions at structural points. It is central to 20th-century Brazilian cultural history yet has received almost no structural or quantitative study.

The authors' research question is which features across melodic contour, melodic rhythm, harmony and the melody–harmony relationship are shared norms of this aesthetic (an 'MPB Common Practice', after Tymoczko's 'extended common practice') and which deviate from them to form individual composers' styles. Answering it requires data first. On the MIR side, structural data for Brazilian popular music are scarce and copyright prevents releasing scores or recordings, so they release derived analytical encodings rather than the source material.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文回顧的流行音樂語料庫研究多半以和聲為中心、又以英語搖滾與流行樂為主，例如 deClercq 與 Temperley（2011）的搖滾和聲語料庫、Mauch 等（2015）的美國流行樂演化研究。拉丁美洲方面有哥倫比亞 bambuco（Cano 等，2021）、烏拉圭 candombe（Rocamora 等，2015）與 salsa（Gómez-Marín 等，2024）；巴西方面，Moss 等（2020）的蕭樂研究只看和聲，Sambaset（Maia 等，2019）與馬拉卡圖的研究（Fonseca 等，2021）只看節奏，而且多以錄音為主。MPB 語料庫的差別是同時涵蓋旋律、節奏、和聲與兩者關係，而且是以樂譜為基礎的專家分析。

論文沒有比較、但功能上最接近的其實是 Hooktheory 的 TheoryTab 資料：它同樣提供旋律與以級數標記的功能和聲對齊，規模大得多，但由群眾標註、以英語流行樂為主（SheetSage 就是用它訓練的）。爵士方面的 Weimar Jazz Database（Pfleiderer 等，2017）也有旋律加和弦的符號標註，但對象是即興獨奏而非歌曲本身。MPB 語料庫的定位因此是：規模小、單一專家、曲目集中，但分析深度（每個音的和聲功能）更高。

分析模型本身也有明確的前身。和弦類型模型 GCT 與 Cambouropoulos 等（2014）的 General Chord Type 撞名，作者在註腳說明兩者不同並附轉換程式；旋律輪廓的 c-letter 是 Parsons code 的細分版；旋律—和聲關係依據的是爵士教學常用的和弦—音階理論（Gonda，1972）。

The popular-music corpus studies reviewed are mostly harmony-centred and mostly anglophone rock and pop, such as deClercq and Temperley's (2011) rock harmony corpus and Mauch et al.'s (2015) study of US pop evolution. In Latin America there is work on Colombian bambuco (Cano et al., 2021), Uruguayan candombe (Rocamora et al., 2015) and salsa (Gómez-Marín et al., 2024); for Brazil, Moss et al.'s (2020) choro study covers harmony only, while Sambaset (Maia et al., 2019) and the maracatu work (Fonseca et al., 2021) cover rhythm only and are mostly recording-based. The MPB Corpus differs by covering melody, rhythm, harmony and their relationship together, through expert score-based analysis.

Not compared in the paper but functionally closest is Hooktheory's TheoryTab data, which likewise aligns melody with Roman-numeral functional harmony at much larger scale, but is crowd-annotated and mostly anglophone pop (SheetSage was trained on it). For jazz, the Weimar Jazz Database (Pfleiderer et al., 2017) also has symbolic melody-plus-chord annotation, but of improvised solos rather than the songs themselves. The MPB Corpus is thus positioned as small, single-expert and repertoire-focused, but deeper in analysis (the harmonic function of every note).

The analytical models have clear antecedents too. The chord-type model GCT shares its acronym with Cambouropoulos et al.'s (2014) General Chord Type; a footnote says the two differ and provides conversion code. The c-letters for contour are a finer version of Parsons code, and the melody–harmony relation rests on the chord–scale theory common in jazz pedagogy (Gonda, 1972).

方法Method

和聲：和弦類型屬（GCT）。 以 10 個原型和弦（大小三和弦與各種七和弦）為「屬」，透過三種轉換遞迴衍生變體：ADD（加音，如加九度）、SUB（替換，如以四度取代三度得到掛四屬七）、ALT（半音變化，如降十三）。為避免組合爆炸，作者訂了一組規則（例如 SUB 只允許三種情況、十一度只能加在 z、y、x、w 四屬），最後限定在 161 種和弦類型，最多到第 6 代；屬七所在的 Y 屬最龐大，有 59 個成員。每個和弦另記根音、低音、功能級數、調性、調式與小節位置。

旋律：旋律過濾模型（MFM）。 先把旋律切成相對獨立的片段（稱為 word），再分別編碼。音高以 7 個 c-letter 表示相鄰音的動向：同音、級進（1–2 半音）、琶音（3–5 半音）、跳進（6 半音以上），各分上下。節奏把一拍切成 12 等分，只看起音間隔、不看時值，以 26 個 r-letter 表示一拍內的起音配置（a 到 v 已涵蓋幾乎全部曲目，w 到 z 是預留的萬用字母）。衍生指標有 CIEI（輪廓的上下平衡度，0–1）與 CMI（反節拍性，r-letter 各有 0.1 到 1 的權重，以拍首結尾的字會打 8 折）。

旋律—和聲關係。 依和弦—音階理論判定每個旋律音是結構音還是經過性的「inflection」，結構音再分根音、三音、五音、六或七音、單純張力音、變化張力音。MAI（旋律錨定指數）把這六類以 9、7、8、5、3、1 的權重加權平均後正規化到 0–1。視覺化工具 NF web 以 12 條半音射線乘 5 圈同心圓呈現全域（調內級數）與局部（和弦功能）的組合。

流程。 歌本先以記譜軟體轉成 MIDI，在 E6 插入標記音標示片段邊界；c-word 由腳本自動產生，r-word、和聲分析與音符功能則全部手工完成，由第一作者一人標註，其他兩位作者寫程式檢查起音數不一致、數值越界與缺值。旋律—和聲關係只分析每首曲子的一段連續摘錄。

Harmony: Genera of Chord Types (GCT). Ten archetypal chords (major and minor triads and various sevenths) define 'genera', and variants are derived by recursively applying three transformations: ADD (adding a note, e.g. a ninth), SUB (substitution, e.g. a fourth replacing the third to give a suspended dominant) and ALT (chromatic alteration, e.g. a flat thirteenth). To avoid combinatorial explosion the authors impose rules (e.g. SUB is allowed in only three cases, elevenths may be added only to genera z, y, x and w), limiting the system to 161 chord types up to the sixth generation; the dominant genus Y is the largest, with 59 members. Each chord also records root, bass, functional degree, key, mode and bar position.

Melody: Melodic Filtering Model (MFM). The melody is first cut into relatively autonomous segments ('words'), which are then encoded. Pitch uses 7 c-letters for the motion between adjacent notes: repetition, step (1–2 semitones), arpeggio (3–5 semitones) and leap (6 or more), each up or down. Rhythm divides a beat into 12 units and, considering only inter-onset intervals and not durations, encodes onset configurations within a beat with 26 r-letters ('a' to 'v' cover nearly the whole repertoire; 'w' to 'z' are reserved wildcards). Derived indices are CIEI (up–down balance of contour, 0–1) and CMI (countermetricity; each r-letter has a weight from 0.1 to 1, and words ending on the beat are discounted by 20%).

Melody–harmony relation. Chord–scale theory decides whether each melody note is structural or a passing 'inflection'; structural notes are further labelled root, third, fifth, sixth/seventh, simple tension or altered tension. MAI (melodic anchoring index) is a weighted average of these six classes with weights 9, 7, 8, 5, 3, 1, normalized to 0–1. The NF web visualization shows combinations of global (scale degree) and local (chord function) context on 12 chromatic rays times 5 concentric circles.

Pipeline. Songbooks are entered into notation software and exported as MIDI, with a marker note at E6 indicating segment boundaries; c-words are generated by script, while r-words, harmonic analysis and note functions are entirely manual. The first author annotated everything alone; the other two wrote code to check onset-count mismatches, out-of-range values and missing values. The melody–harmony relation is analyzed on one contiguous excerpt per piece.

限制Limitations

單一標註者、沒有一致性檢驗。 作者說這是為了和聲分析的一致性，但和聲功能分析本身就有詮釋空間（論文也承認它「目前無法可靠自動化」），只有一人標註代表資料反映的是一位專家的讀法，沒有任何量化的可信度指標。程式檢查只能抓格式錯誤，抓不到詮釋差異。旋律切段也是手工的，作者自己說這是流程中標準化程度最低的一步，正式形式化還在進行中。

資料型態限制了可做的事。 因為版權，釋出的只有 c-word、r-word、和弦表與音符功能表，沒有實際音高與時值序列；c-letter 只記方向與粗略距離（3–5 半音都算琶音），所以無法從資料重建旋律，也無法拿來訓練一般的符號音樂模型。和音訊的連結只有自動抓取的 Spotify、ISRC 與 MusicBrainz 識別碼，而分析依據的是歌本的「名義形式」（去掉前奏、間奏、尾奏與重複），與錄音版本不一定對得上。節奏量化到每拍 12 格，也不包含任何微時序。

選樣與權重是人為決定。 十位作曲家裡只有 Rita Lee 一位女性，作者解釋是受限於有可靠出版樂譜者；每人固定 50 首，但沒有說明如何從作品中選出。MAI 的權重（根音 9、五音 8、三音 7……）與 CMI 的字母權重都是手訂的，沒有敏感度分析；把五音的錨定程度排在三音之上這類選擇會直接影響作曲家之間的比較。

One annotator, no agreement check. The authors say this ensures consistency in harmonic analysis, but functional harmonic analysis is inherently interpretive (the paper itself says it 'cannot currently be reliably automated'); a single annotator means the data reflect one expert's reading, with no quantitative reliability measure. Code checks catch formatting errors, not interpretive differences. Melodic segmentation is also manual, and the authors say it is the least standardized step, with formalization still in progress.

The data format limits what can be done. Because of copyright, only c-words, r-words, a chord table and a note-function table are released, with no actual pitch or duration sequences; c-letters record only direction and coarse distance (3–5 semitones all count as arpeggio), so melodies cannot be reconstructed and the data cannot train ordinary symbolic music models. The link to audio consists only of automatically retrieved Spotify, ISRC and MusicBrainz identifiers, and the analysis follows the songbook's 'nominal form' (no intros, interludes, codas or repeats), which may not match the recording. Rhythm is quantized to 12 units per beat, with no microtiming.

Selection and weights are human choices. Only one of the ten composers, Rita Lee, is a woman, which the authors attribute to the availability of reliable published scores; each composer has exactly 50 pieces, but how they were chosen is not stated. The MAI weights (root 9, fifth 8, third 7, and so on) and the CMI letter weights are hand-set with no sensitivity analysis; choices such as ranking the fifth as more anchored than the third directly affect comparisons between composers.

討論Discussion

探索性分析的幾個數字。 節奏上，多數作曲家最常見的起音位置是拍首（位置 1），Rita Lee 是例外：半拍位置（7）佔 48.2%。反節拍字母總比例從 Edu Lobo 的 22.3% 到 Djavan 的 40.9%；森巴色彩的字母 n 在 Chico Buarque（16.3%）、João Bosco（14.8%）、Jobim（13.4%）最多，Rita Lee（26.9%）與 Milton Nascimento（19.0%）則以 e 為主。CMI 從 Milton 的 0.388 到 Chico 的 0.564。

輪廓的 CIEI 都落在 0.856–0.898，作者解讀為旋律有一種上下相抵的「補償律」。MAI 從 Jobim 的 0.582 到 Rita Lee 的 0.741，單曲最極端的例子是 Rita Lee 的 Saúde（0.817）對 Jobim 的 Angela（0.416）。

統計檢定的意義。 唯一的推論統計是節奏：以每首曲子的 r-letter 分佈計算 Jensen–Shannon 距離，R 是「不同作曲家之間平均距離」除以「同一作曲家內平均距離」。觀測值 R=1.072，1 萬次置換中沒有一次超過它，p<10⁻⁴。結論「r-letter 分佈帶有作曲家風格資訊」是對的，但 1.072 代表跨作曲家的距離只比作曲家內部大約 7%，以 500 首的樣本量，很小的差異也會顯著。這比較像證明資料「有訊號」，而不是證明風格可以被清楚區分。其他關於爵士、搖滾影響的解讀都是描述性的，沒有檢定。

對 MIR 的用處。 最直接的用途是符號層級的風格分析、作曲家相似度與分群（作者已在試 LDA），以及以 Markov 鏈做風格化生成；有 Spotify 與 MusicBrainz 連結，也可以當作音訊和弦辨識在巴西曲目上的弱參考。作者預告的 150 首蕭樂、森巴、爵士對照語料庫與擴充到約 50 位作曲家，會讓「MPB 共同實踐」這個問題真正可以被檢驗；在那之前，這份資料最大的價值是把一套成熟的分析框架與它的第一批產出公開出來。

Some numbers from the exploratory analysis. In rhythm, most composers' most frequent onset position is the downbeat (position 1); Rita Lee is the exception, with the half-beat position (7) at 48.2%. The total share of countermetric letters ranges from 22.3% for Edu Lobo to 40.9% for Djavan; the samba-coloured letter 'n' is most common in Chico Buarque (16.3%), João Bosco (14.8%) and Jobim (13.4%), whereas Rita Lee (26.9%) and Milton Nascimento (19.0%) favour 'e'. CMI runs from 0.388 for Milton to 0.564 for Chico.

Contour CIEI lies within 0.856–0.898 for all, which the authors read as a 'compensatory law' of upward and downward motion. MAI runs from 0.582 for Jobim to 0.741 for Rita Lee, and the extreme single-song example is Rita Lee's Saúde (0.817) versus Jobim's Angela (0.416).

What the statistical test means. The only inferential statistic concerns rhythm: Jensen–Shannon distance between per-piece r-letter distributions, with R defined as mean between-composer distance divided by mean within-composer distance. The observed R=1.072, and none of 10,000 permutations exceeded it, p<10⁻⁴. The conclusion that r-letter distributions carry composer-style information is correct, but 1.072 means between-composer distance is only about 7% larger than within-composer distance, and with 500 pieces even small differences become significant. This shows the data 'contain signal' more than it shows styles are clearly separable. Other readings about jazz or rock influence are descriptive and untested.

Use for MIR. The most direct uses are symbolic-level style analysis, composer similarity and clustering (the authors are already trying LDA), and stylistic generation with Markov chains; with Spotify and MusicBrainz links it can also serve as a weak reference for audio chord recognition on Brazilian repertoire. The announced 150-song choro, samba and jazz control corpus and expansion to about 50 composers would make the 'MPB Common Practice' question genuinely testable; until then, the dataset's main value is making a mature analytical framework and its first output public.
