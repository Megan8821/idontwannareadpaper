CoJEPA: Combining Contrastive Learning and JEPA for Global-Local Music Representations

CoJEPA：結合對比學習與 JEPA 的全域-局部音樂表徵學習

Gabriel Meseguer-Brocal, Yuexuan Kong, Romain Hennequin
arXiv:2608.30974 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-08-31
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把 JEPA 跟對比學習兩個目標放進同一個共享骨幹訓練，拿掉 JEPA 慣用的 EMA teacher，在和弦估計上比 46 倍大的 MusicFM 高出一倍以上（重疊分數 0.399 vs 0.193），但拍點追蹤仍輸給 MusicFM（F-measure 0.789 vs 0.866）。Why this one: Trains JEPA and contrastive learning jointly on one shared backbone, dropping JEPA's usual EMA teacher; beats a 46x-larger MusicFM on chord estimation more than twofold (0.399 vs 0.193 overlap) but still trails it on beat tracking (F-measure 0.789 vs 0.866).

動機Motivation

音樂自監督學習裡有兩派主流方法：JEPA 系在潛空間預測被遮蔽的區域，能捕捉細粒度時序資訊，但訓練不穩定，需要 EMA（指數移動平均）teacher 才能避免表徵崩塌；對比學習系把不同視角的表徵拉近，訓練穩定，卻只對整段序列的全域表徵下損失，在節拍、和弦這類需要逐幀判斷的局部任務上偏弱。

論文的立場是：與其擴大任一種方法的規模，不如把兩者的歸納偏誤（inductive bias）合在同一個骨幹網路裡訓練，同時吃到局部與全域的好處，並藉此拿掉 JEPA 賴以維生的 EMA teacher。論文原文的說法是「結合具有互補歸納偏誤的目標，可以取代規模」。

Music self-supervised learning has two dominant families: JEPA-style methods, which predict masked regions in latent space, and contrastive methods, which pull different views' representations together. JEPA captures fine-grained temporal information but is unstable to train, needing an EMA (exponential moving average) teacher to avoid representational collapse; contrastive learning trains stably but applies its loss only to whole-sequence global representations, making it weaker on frame-level tasks like beat tracking or chord estimation.

Rather than scaling either method further, the paper's premise is that combining their complementary inductive biases in one shared backbone can capture both local and global structure at once, letting JEPA drop the EMA teacher it normally depends on -- in the authors' words, "combining objectives with complementary inductive biases can substitute for scale."

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文把先前的音樂自監督方法分成四條線，並逐一指出差異。第一是聯合嵌入架構（對比學習系），擅長全域語意表徵，但論文指出這類方法「在需要細粒度時序資訊的任務上經常表現不佳」。第二是遮蔽建模，論文點名 MusicFM 與 MERT 兩個具體系統：兩者都在輸入空間（頻譜或波形）重建被遮蔽的部分，論文認為這會「鼓勵模型專注在低層次細節」，而非抽象語意。

第三是 JEPA 本身，在潛空間而非輸入空間做預測，理論上避開低層細節干擾，但「訓練不穩定、對超參數敏感」的問題仍未解決。第四是先前已有人把 JEPA 跟 VICReg（另一種對比式方法）結合，但論文指出那個組合「EMA 仍然是穩定訓練所必需的」——先前的混合方案沒有真正解決 EMA 依賴。CoJEPA 跟它的差異在於：直接用對比損失取代 EMA 的穩定化功能，而不是把兩者疊加。

The paper sorts prior music SSL work into four lines and states its difference from each. The first is joint-embedding architectures (the contrastive family), strong on global semantics but, the paper notes, they "often underperform on tasks requiring fine-grained temporal information." The second is masking-based modeling, naming two concrete systems, MusicFM and MERT: both reconstruct masked content in input space (spectrogram or waveform), which the paper argues "may encourage the model to focus on low-level details" rather than abstract semantics.

The third is JEPA itself, predicting in latent space to avoid that low-level bias, but it "remains difficult to optimize in practice due to training instability and sensitivity to hyperparameters." The fourth is a prior attempt combining JEPA with VICReg (another contrastive-style method) -- but in that combination "EMA is still necessary to stabilize the training," so the EMA dependency was never actually removed. CoJEPA's difference is replacing EMA's stabilizing role with the contrastive loss itself, rather than stacking the two together.

方法Method

CoJEPA 用單一共享骨幹（ViT-1D Tiny，12 層、192 維、3 個注意力頭，共 7.1M 參數）同時扮演 JEPA 的 teacher 與 student，不再用 EMA 建立獨立的 teacher 權重。輸入是 16 kHz 音訊轉成的 mel 頻譜（128 個頻率 bin），切成 patch token 後餵進骨幹；masking 策略是遮住連續 50–75% 長度的時間區塊。

前向傳播時，同一個骨幹先以「teacher 模式」處理兩個完整視角，產生用於對比損失的 class token，以及作為 JEPA 預測目標的序列 token；再以「student 模式」處理被遮蔽的版本，讓一個較小的 predictor（6 層、3.5M 參數）從可見 token 還原被遮蔽區域的表徵。梯度分成兩條路徑：NT-Xent 對比損失走 teacher 路徑，負責把全域嵌入空間排列整齊；JEPA 損失走 predictor 與 student 路徑，總損失是兩者相加。對比損失撐起的「結構化全域嵌入空間」，取代了 EMA 原本用來防止表徵崩塌的功能。

CoJEPA uses a single shared backbone (a ViT-1D Tiny: 12 layers, 192-dim, 3 attention heads, 7.1M parameters total) to play both JEPA's teacher and student roles, replacing the separate EMA-updated teacher entirely. Input is a 16 kHz audio waveform converted to a 128-bin mel spectrogram, patchified into tokens; masking blocks out contiguous temporal spans covering 50-75% of the sequence.

In the forward pass, the same backbone first runs in "teacher mode" on two full views, producing class tokens for the contrastive loss and sequence tokens as JEPA's prediction targets; it then runs in "student mode" on the masked view, and a smaller predictor (6 layers, 3.5M parameters) reconstructs the masked tokens' representations from the visible ones. Gradients split into two paths: the NT-Xent contrastive loss flows through the teacher path to structure the global embedding space, while the JEPA loss flows through the predictor and student path; the total loss sums both. The contrastive loss's "well-structured global embedding space" takes over the collapse-prevention role EMA normally plays.

限制Limitations

論文自己的結果顯示 CoJEPA 並非全面最優。拍點追蹤（beat tracking）上，CoJEPA 的 F-measure 是 0.789，輸給 MusicFM 的 0.866，也輸給單獨的 JEPA（0.806）——顯示對比損失的加入在這個局部任務上反而拖累了純 JEPA 的表現。BPM 分類（ACC@2）上，CoJEPA 的 0.861 也不如純對比學習的 0.910，同樣是「合併目標」在特定任務上輸給單一目標的例子。

另外，論文的所有實驗都在單一 GPU、7.1M 參數的小模型規模上完成，跟作為主要對照組的 MusicFM（330M 參數、更大的 Conformer 骨幹）差了 46 倍，論文雖然主張「更聰明的設計可以取代規模」，但沒有驗證 CoJEPA 這套組合在更大規模下是否還能維持同樣的相對優勢，也沒有測試遮蔽比例、預測器大小等超參數的敏感度。

The paper's own results show CoJEPA is not uniformly best. On beat tracking, CoJEPA's F-measure is 0.789, trailing both MusicFM (0.866) and plain JEPA alone (0.806) -- meaning adding the contrastive loss actually hurts performance relative to pure JEPA on this local task. On BPM classification (ACC@2), CoJEPA's 0.861 also falls short of contrastive-only training's 0.910, another case where the combined objective loses to a single objective on a specific task.

All experiments also run at a small scale -- a single GPU and a 7.1M-parameter model, 46x smaller than the main baseline MusicFM (330M parameters, a larger Conformer backbone). While the paper argues "smarter designs can substitute for scale," it does not test whether CoJEPA's relative advantage holds at larger scale, nor does it report sensitivity to hyperparameters like masking ratio or predictor size.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最值得注意的地方，不是「JEPA 加對比學習」這個組合本身早有人嘗試過（論文自己點名了 JEPA+VICReg 的先例），而是它精確定位了先前混合方案沒解決的問題——EMA 依賴——並且證明對比損失可以直接頂替 EMA 的穩定化角色，而不需要額外機制。這個設計選擇的效果在和弦估計上特別明顯：CoJEPA 在第 4 層就拿到 0.399 的重疊分數，是 MusicFM（0.193）的兩倍以上，也遠勝單獨的對比學習（0.258）與單獨的 JEPA（0.211）。

但論文對「為什麼混合目標在和弦這種調性任務上特別有效、卻在拍點追蹤上不進反退」沒有給出機制性的解釋，只停留在觀察層級的敘述（例如「JEPA 在淺層產生最可遷移的特徵，但對深層缺乏足夠指引」）。對於一篇主打「用設計取代規模」的論文，這個解釋落差是可以再深挖的空間。

The most notable contribution here is not the JEPA-plus-contrastive combination itself -- the paper names a prior JEPA+VICReg precedent -- but pinpointing exactly what that prior hybrid failed to solve: EMA dependency. It shows the contrastive loss can directly substitute for EMA's stabilizing role without any extra mechanism. This design choice pays off clearest on chord estimation: at layer 4, CoJEPA reaches a 0.399 overlap score, more than double MusicFM's 0.193 and well above standalone contrastive learning (0.258) and standalone JEPA (0.211).

What the paper doesn't offer is a mechanistic explanation for why the combined objective helps so much on a tonal task like chords yet regresses on beat tracking -- it stays at the level of observation (e.g., "JEPA produces the most transferable features early but lacks sufficient guidance for deeper layers"). For a paper whose selling point is substituting design for scale, that explanatory gap is worth digging into further.

TUTTI: Toward Generalizable Audio-to-Score Transcription via Fully Synthesized Data

TUTTI：用全合成資料打造可泛化的音訊轉樂譜系統

Jianhuai Hu, Yashan Wang, Shangda Wu, Zhancheng Guo, Shijie Liang, Wuna Meng, Chuanqi Yang, Xiaobing Li, Feng Yu, Maosong Sun
arXiv:2609.00640 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-01
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：用 NotaGen 生成 363,610 筆多樂器 ABC 譜的合成資料集 TuttiCorpus，訓練一個不含 CNN 前端的純 Transformer 直接把頻譜轉成 ABC 記譜；在薩克斯風（訓練資料完全沒出現過的樂器）上把 MV2H 總分從基線 60.3 拉到 85.3，聲部辨識更是達到 100.0 的理論上限。Why this one: Generates a 363,610-pair synthetic multi-instrument corpus (TuttiCorpus) via NotaGen and trains a pure Transformer, with no CNN front-end, to decode spectrograms directly into ABC notation; on tenor saxophone -- an instrument absent from pretraining -- it lifts MV2H total score from a 60.3 baseline to 85.3, with voice detection hitting the theoretical ceiling of 100.0.

動機Motivation

音訊轉樂譜（Audio-to-Score, A2S）長期卡在資料稀缺：人類作曲的存量有限，而「聲學演奏」跟「對應樂譜」精準對齊的真實錄音更是稀少。過去的系統因此走「先做多音高估計，再做節奏量化」的分階段管線，或是用 CNN 抽取局部聲學特徵、搭配 RNN／注意力機制解碼——這些設計某種程度上是在資料不足時，用架構工程去彌補資料的缺口。

第二個問題是缺乏泛化性：既有模型幾乎都只處理單一樂器情境（獨奏鋼琴、弦樂四重奏、薩克斯風各自訓練各自的模型），論文指出「目前還沒有一個能穩健處理各種樂器編制組合的統一、可泛化 A2S 模型」。論文的解法不是設計更精巧的架構，而是反過來問：如果訓練資料的規模與多樣性足夠大，能不能讓一個不含客製化 CNN 前端的純 Transformer 直接學會這件事？

Audio-to-Score (A2S) transcription has long been bottlenecked by data scarcity: the total corpus of human-composed music is finite, and real recordings with acoustic performances precisely aligned to their scores are exceedingly rare. Prior systems compensated with architecture -- either a pipeline of separate multi-pitch estimation and rhythm quantization stages, or a CNN front-end extracting local acoustic features feeding an RNN or attention-based decoder -- engineering choices that partly exist to make up for insufficient data.

The second problem is generalizability: existing models are almost all trained for a single instrumentation setting (solo piano, string quartet, saxophone each getting its own model), and the paper notes "a unified, generalizable A2S model capable of robustly transcribing musical pieces across a wide and unconstrained variety of instrumentations has not yet been proposed." Rather than engineering a cleverer architecture, the paper asks the reverse question: if training data were large and diverse enough, could a pure Transformer with no customized CNN front-end simply learn this directly?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名了兩個具體的先前系統並說明差異。Zeng et al.（2024）提出一個具階層式解碼器的 Seq2Seq 模型，能同時捕捉小節層級的中繼資料與音符層級序列，並用一套「表現力演奏渲染」（EPR）系統把量化的 MIDI 人性化；這篇論文是 TUTTI 在 ASAP 資料集上的主要比較對象。Alfaro-Contreras et al.（2024）則是把 Transformer 解碼器整合進 A2S 管線，並設計了專門的二維位置編碼來保留 CNN 特徵圖裡的頻率-時間關係——這是 TUTTI 在弦樂四重奏資料集上的比較對象。

這兩篇的共通點，也是論文點出的關鍵限制，是它們都還是「小規模資料 + 客製化架構」的路線：CNN 前端加 RNN 或帶特殊位置編碼的 Transformer，本質上是在資料不足時用架構設計去彌補。TUTTI 的差異不是提出更精巧的解碼器，而是反過來問「資料量夠不夠大，就可以讓純 Transformer 不需要客製化 CNN 前端」——把過去被當成架構問題的東西，重新定義成資料規模問題。

The paper names two concrete prior systems and states its difference from each. Zeng et al. (2024) proposed a Seq2Seq model with a hierarchical decoder that captures both bar-level metadata and note-level sequences, using an Expressive Performance Rendering (EPR) system to humanize quantized MIDI -- this is TUTTI's main comparison point on the ASAP dataset. Alfaro-Contreras et al. (2024) integrated a Transformer decoder into the A2S pipeline with a specialized two-dimensional positional encoding to preserve frequency-time relationships in CNN feature maps -- this is TUTTI's comparison point on the quartets dataset.

What both share, and what the paper identifies as the real limitation, is that they still follow a "small data plus customized architecture" recipe: a CNN front-end paired with an RNN or a specially-positioned Transformer, essentially using architectural design to compensate for insufficient data. TUTTI's difference isn't a cleverer decoder -- it's asking whether enough data volume lets a pure Transformer skip the customized CNN front-end altogether, reframing what used to be treated as an architecture problem as a data-scale problem instead.

方法Method

TUTTI 是標準的 Transformer 編碼器-解碼器架構：頻譜（1025 維）先經線性投影層映成 512 維的連續嵌入，由 9 層的「聲學層級編碼器」抽取聲學與和聲脈絡；解碼端分兩階段——先用 9 層「跨模態 patch 層級解碼器」以階層式策略對齊小節層級的結構（拍子、複音）與對應的聲學片段，再用 3 層「字元層級解碼器」以串接方式逐字元解出 ABC 記譜的具體符號。模型總參數量 88.9M，單次處理長度約 14.8 秒。

訓練資料 TuttiCorpus 靠 NotaGen（一個符號音樂生成模型）依樂派、作曲家、樂器編制等提示生成 363,610 份不重複 ABC 樂譜，再用 VirtuosoNet（鋼琴）或規則式方法（力度 ±15、時間抖動 ±10 毫秒、彈性速度 ±3%）把量化樂譜渲染成有表現力的 MIDI，改用 SFZ 音色庫合成音訊（而非缺乏聲學細節的通用 MIDI 合成器），再用 Dual Dynamic Time Warping 把演奏對齊回量化樂譜，取得小節層級的精確時間錨點。編制涵蓋獨奏（64.6%）到六人以上大合奏（1.2%）。

TUTTI is a standard Transformer encoder-decoder: a 1025-dim spectrogram is linearly projected into a 512-dim embedding, and a 9-layer "acoustic-level encoder" extracts acoustic and harmonic context. Decoding happens in two stages -- a 9-layer "cross-modal patch-level decoder" hierarchically aligns bar-level structure (meter, polyphony) with the corresponding acoustic segment, then a 3-layer "character-level decoder" cascades through it to decode the specific ABC-notation characters. The model totals 88.9M parameters and processes segments up to about 14.8 seconds.

The TuttiCorpus training data comes from NotaGen, a symbolic music generation model, prompted by period, composer, and instrumentation to produce 363,610 unique ABC scores. These are rendered into expressive MIDI via VirtuosoNet (piano) or rule-based humanization (velocity +/-15, timing jitter +/-10ms, tempo rubato +/-3%), synthesized to audio with SFZ sample libraries (rather than acoustically flat General MIDI), and aligned back to the quantized score via Dual Dynamic Time Warping for precise bar-level timing anchors. Instrumentation ranges from solo (64.6%) to ensembles of six or more players (1.2%).

限制Limitations

消融實驗顯示預訓練是效能的主要來源：在 ASAP 資料集上，沒有預訓練時 MV2H 總分只有 53.4（多音高偵測甚至只有 13.2），加上多樂器預訓練後總分拉到 76.1，多音高偵測衝到 70.7。這代表論文的核心宣稱——「資料規模是效能的主要驅動力」——有明確的消融數字撐著，不是空話。但也意味著沒有這批 363,610 筆合成資料，TUTTI 的架構本身並不特別佔優勢。

論文自己承認目前系統只能處理不超過 14.8 秒的音訊片段，把這個機制延伸到不受長度限制、完整曲目的轉譜，仍是「未來值得努力的方向」。另外，訓練資料完全是合成的（NotaGen 生成樂譜、SFZ 音色庫合成音訊），論文在薩克斯風上驗證了跨樂器泛化，但沒有在人類演奏的真實錄音上做同等規模的跨資料集驗證，真實世界演奏中的雜訊、麥克風特性、演奏瑕疵是否會讓這套「純合成資料訓練」的優勢打折，論文沒有測試。

The ablation shows pretraining is the main source of performance: on ASAP, MV2H total score without pretraining is just 53.4 (multi-pitch detection only 13.2), rising to 76.1 with full multi-instrumentation pretraining (multi-pitch jumps to 70.7). This gives real ablation numbers behind the paper's central claim that "data scale is the primary driver of performance" -- but it also means the architecture itself isn't especially advantaged without this 363,610-sample synthetic corpus.

The authors acknowledge the current system only handles audio segments up to 14.8 seconds, and extending it to unconstrained full-piece transcription remains "a promising avenue for future work." The training data is also entirely synthetic (NotaGen-generated scores, SFZ-synthesized audio); while the paper validates cross-instrument generalization on saxophone, it does not run an equivalent cross-dataset validation on real human recordings, leaving untested whether real-world noise, microphone characteristics, and performance imperfections would erode the advantage of training purely on synthetic data.

討論Discussion

這篇論文的核心主張很乾脆：只要資料量夠大夠多樣，純 Transformer 不需要客製化 CNN 前端也能做好 A2S。最有說服力的證據是薩克斯風的跨樂器遷移結果——這個樂器完全沒出現在預訓練資料裡，全樂器預訓練版本卻把 MV2H 總分從基線（Martínez-Sevilla et al. 的 60.3）拉到 85.3，聲部辨識更是打到 100.0 的理論上限，比沒預訓練版本的 52.5–55.8 高出一大截。

但這個「資料規模取代架構工程」的敘事，建立在一個沒有被論文自己檢驗的前提上：合成資料的音樂多樣性跟合成音訊的擬真度，是否真的能覆蓋真實錄音的分布。論文用 SFZ 音色庫取代通用 MIDI 合成器、用 DualDTW 做精確對齊，這些工程細節確實比過去粗糙，但終究是合成管線，跟「用人類真實演奏錄音訓練」在分布上仍有本質差異——這正是資料稀缺問題原本存在的理由，論文繞過了它，而不是解決了它。

This paper's core claim is blunt: given enough data volume and diversity, a pure Transformer doesn't need a customized CNN front-end to do A2S well. The most persuasive evidence is the saxophone cross-instrument result -- an instrument entirely absent from pretraining data -- where the full-instrumentation model lifts MV2H total from a 60.3 baseline (Martínez-Sevilla et al.) to 85.3, with voice detection hitting the theoretical ceiling of 100.0, far above the 52.5-55.8 range without pretraining.

But the "data scale replaces architecture engineering" narrative rests on a premise the paper never directly tests: whether synthetic musical diversity and synthesized audio realism actually cover the distribution of real recordings. Using SFZ sample libraries instead of General MIDI and DualDTW for precise alignment are genuine engineering improvements over cruder pipelines, but it remains a synthetic pipeline -- distributionally distinct from training on real human performances, which is precisely the scarcity problem the paper works around rather than solves.

On the Human and Computer Alignment of Attribute-Based Music Matches

屬性層級的音樂相似度判斷：人類與電腦指標的對齊程度

Roser Batlle-Roca, Woosung Choi, Joan Serrà, Fabio Morreale, Wei-Hsiang Liao, Xavier Serra, Emilia Gómez, Yuki Mitsufuji
arXiv:2609.00987 · cs.SD · 投稿submitted 2026-09-01
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：建立 MATCHA 資料集，讓 83 位專業音樂人針對「旋律、和聲、節奏、聲部、音色」五個屬性做三選一的音樂相似度判斷；人類對人類作曲的翻唱／抄襲案例判斷一致度高（如旋律 Fleiss' κ≈0.60），但對 AI 生成音樂（Stable Audio Open）的判斷幾乎降到機會水準（κ≤0.19），而四個常見計算相似度指標沒有一個能同時對齊全部五個屬性。Why this one: Introduces MATCHA, having 83 expert musicians make three-way forced-choice similarity judgments across five attributes (melody, harmony, rhythm, voice, timbre); human agreement on human-composed cover/plagiarism cases is high (e.g. melody Fleiss' kappa ~=0.60) but collapses to near-chance on AI-generated (Stable Audio Open) cases (kappa <=0.19), and none of four common computational similarity metrics aligns with all five attributes.

動機Motivation

生成式 AI 音樂系統越來越容易做出跟人類作品高度相似的曲子，這牽動著訓練資料是否被複製、創作者的著作權與生計等爭議。過去評估「這首生成音樂是不是抄了某首訓練資料」的做法多半用單一整體相似度分數，但論文指出「音樂相似度本質上是主觀的，而且取決於多個音樂維度」——把旋律、和聲這些不同屬性混在一個分數裡，會掩蓋掉評審者之間真正的分歧點在哪裡。

論文的核心假設是：如果把「整體像不像」拆成「旋律像不像」「節奏像不像」等屬性層級的判斷，人類評審之間的一致性應該會提高，因為每次只需要針對單一維度做判斷。論文因此設計了一個三選一的三元組比較實驗，同時想知道：人類在屬性層級上的判斷一致嗎？現有的計算相似度指標，能不能對齊人類在各個屬性上的判斷？

Generative AI music systems increasingly produce tracks highly similar to human-composed music, raising disputes over training-data replication, creator copyright, and livelihoods. Prior work assessing whether a generated track replicates a specific training example has typically used a single overall similarity score, but the paper argues "musical similarity is inherently subjective, and depends on multiple music dimensions" -- collapsing melody, harmony, and other distinct attributes into one number obscures where evaluators actually disagree.

The paper's core hypothesis is that breaking "how similar overall" down into attribute-level judgments -- how similar is the melody, the rhythm, and so on -- should raise agreement among human raters, since each judgment only concerns one dimension. It therefore designs a three-way forced-choice triplet comparison experiment to ask two questions: do humans agree at the attribute level, and do existing computational similarity metrics align with human judgments on each attribute?

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文採用既有的「音樂複製評估」（MiRA）框架作為對照的計算指標，框架本身包含四個具體方法：CoverID（翻唱曲辨識，鎖定旋律與和聲層面的對應）、以 PaSST 音訊分類器為基礎的 KL 散度、CLAP（對比式語言-音訊預訓練嵌入）、以及 Discogs-EffNet（捕捉更廣的語意、風格與音色關係）。這四個指標各自針對複製偵測設計，但論文指出它們幾乎都沒有拿真人評審的屬性層級判斷驗證過。

論文也點名了翻唱辨識（version identification）文獻——即便樂器編制、速度、音色、錄音條件不同，也要偵測出是同一首作品——以及用 CLMR（音樂對比式表徵學習）與 CLAP 這類嵌入方法做相似度比對的既有做法。這篇論文跟它們的差異，不是提出新的相似度指標，而是先建立一個「人類在屬性層級上到底同不同意」的行為學基準，再回頭檢驗 MiRA 這四個既有指標跟這個基準對不對得上——換句話說，論文的定位是評測既有指標，而不是取代它們。

The paper adopts an existing computational framework, Music Replication Assessment (MiRA), as its comparison point; it comprises four concrete metrics: CoverID (cover-song identification, focused on melodic and harmonic correspondence), KL divergence based on the PaSST audio classifier, CLAP (Contrastive Language-Audio Pretraining embeddings), and Discogs-EffNet (capturing broader semantic, stylistic, and timbral relationships). Each was designed for replication detection, but the paper notes none had been validated against human attribute-level judgments before.

It also cites version-identification literature -- detecting the same underlying work despite differing instrumentation, tempo, timbre, or recording conditions -- and embedding-based similarity approaches using CLMR (Contrastive Learning of Musical Representations) and CLAP. This paper's difference from all of them is not proposing a new similarity metric, but first establishing a behavioral baseline of whether humans even agree at the attribute level, then checking whether MiRA's four existing metrics track that baseline -- its role is evaluating existing metrics, not replacing them.

方法Method

MATCHA 資料集由 300 組三元組構成，每組由 83 位受試者中的部分人評分，共收集 1105 筆評分（每組平均 3.7 筆）。受試者以專業或半專業音樂人為主（63.8% 是有經驗的專業音樂人），並用 30 組（10%）品質控制題篩掉隨意作答者（得分 ≤0.25 者被排除）。三元組分四類：人類抄襲案例（HP，75 組，來自 SMP 資料集）、人類翻唱版本（HV，75 組，來自 Discogs-VI）、AI 生成案例（AS，120 組，來自 Stable Audio Open）、以及媒體報導過的 AI 抄襲爭議案例（AM，30 組）。

AI 生成案例的產生分兩種：一種是「較不相似」樣本，純粹用文字描述（先用 Music Flamingo 抽取描述、再用 QWEN 3.5 摘要）從隨機噪聲生成；另一種是「較相似」樣本，用 DDIM 反演（把參考音訊反推回噪聲、再重新取樣生成，中間加入受控隨機性）來刻意製造跟參考曲目更接近的版本，取樣時的無分類器引導強度為 -6.0（反演）與 6.0（生成），共跑 100 步擴散。受試者針對每個三元組，在旋律、和聲、節奏、聲部、音色五個屬性上分別做三選一判斷（比較樣本 A、比較樣本 B，或都不像）。

The MATCHA dataset comprises 300 triplets, rated by a subset of 83 participants, collecting 1105 total ratings (3.7 per triplet on average). Participants are mostly professional or semi-professional musicians (63.8% experienced professionals), with 30 triplets (10%) used as attention checks to filter careless raters (those scoring <=0.25 were excluded). The triplets split into four categories: Human-Plagiarism (HP, 75 cases, from the SMP dataset), Human-Versions (HV, 75 cases, from Discogs-VI), AI-generated (AS, 120 cases, from Stable Audio Open), and AI cases documented in media reports (AM, 30 cases).

AI-generated cases come in two kinds: "less similar" samples generated from random noise conditioned purely on text descriptions (extracted via Music Flamingo, summarized by QWEN 3.5), and "more similar" samples produced via DDIM inversion -- inverting the reference audio back to noise and resampling with controlled stochasticity to deliberately produce a closer match, using classifier-free guidance scales of -6.0 (inversion) and 6.0 (generation) over 100 diffusion steps. For each triplet, participants made a three-way forced choice (excerpt A, excerpt B, or neither) separately for melody, harmony, rhythm, voice, and timbre.

限制Limitations

人類一致度的數字本身就分裂成兩個世界：在人類抄襲案例（HP）上，旋律屬性的一致度約 88%、Fleiss' κ≈0.60，屬於中等偏高的一致；但在 AI 生成案例（AS）上，所有屬性的 κ 值都 ≤0.19，而且有 52–64% 的評分選了「都不像」（相較其他子集只有 17–34%），論文形容這個結果「近乎不可解讀」。這代表整份資料集裡，人類創作內容跟 AI 生成內容的相似度判斷，其實是兩種不同的評測情境，不能一概而論。

在人類-計算對齊的部分，論文自己承認「沒有任何一個指標能對齊全部屬性，不同指標捕捉的是互補的音樂維度」——CoverID 對旋律與和聲對齊較好，DEfNet 在節奏結構上稍微一致，CLAP 擅長聲部（人聲）相似度，KL 散度則完全抓不到旋律或和聲層面的相似性。論文也承認研究範圍侷限在西方音樂傳統、僅涵蓋五個屬性（不含歌詞、結構、力度動態、情緒等），且刺激樣本是從「已知高度相似」的來源挑出來的，不能代表隨機配對曲目時的一致度。

Human agreement itself splits into two different worlds. On human-plagiarism cases (HP), melody agreement is around 88% with Fleiss' kappa ~=0.60, a moderate-to-high level. On AI-generated cases (AS), every attribute's kappa is <=0.19, and 52-64% of ratings chose "neither" (versus 17-34% in other subsets) -- the paper describes this as "near-uninterpretable." In other words, whatever consistency exists for human-composed similarity judgments does not carry over to AI-generated content; the two cannot be treated as the same evaluation task.

On human-computational alignment, the paper concedes "no single metric aligns with all attributes, and different metrics capture complementary musical dimensions" -- CoverID aligns better with melody and harmony, DEfNet is somewhat more consistent for rhythmic structure, CLAP excels at vocal/voice similarity, and KL divergence fails to capture melodic or harmonic similarity at all. The authors also acknowledge the study is limited to Western musical traditions, covers only five attributes (excluding lyrics, structure, dynamics, or emotional content), and drew stimuli from sources with pre-established strong similarity, so results may not generalize to randomly paired tracks.

討論Discussion

這篇論文最有價值的發現，不是「屬性層級判斷比整體判斷一致度高」這個直覺上的假設被證實（雖然確實如此），而是它意外揭露了一個評測方法論的斷層：目前用來偵測 AI 音樂複製的計算指標（CoverID、CLAP、KL 散度、DEfNet），全部都是拿人類創作內容的相似度直覺校準出來的，但人類評審面對 AI 生成內容時，判斷本身就近乎隨機（κ≤0.19），這代表現在拿這些指標去審查 AI 生成內容是否抄襲，其實缺乏一個站得住腳的人類判斷基準可以對照。

換句話說，論文標題問的是「人類跟電腦對不對得齊」，但更根本的問題其實是「人類自己對 AI 生成內容的相似度判斷，能不能形成一個可靠的基準」——如果連人類評審對著 AI 生成的三元組都經常選「都不像」而不是明確判斷，那麼任何跟這類人類評分做比對的計算指標，都繼承了同一個不穩定的地基。論文對這一層自我指涉的困境著墨不多，只在討論裡簡短帶過「相似性本身不等於抄襲或侵權」，值得更進一步追問。

The most valuable finding here isn't the confirmed hypothesis that attribute-level judgments raise agreement over holistic ones -- it's an unintended methodological gap this exposes: the computational metrics currently used to detect AI music replication (CoverID, CLAP, KL divergence, DEfNet) are all calibrated against human intuitions about human-composed similarity, yet when the same human raters judge AI-generated content, their agreement is itself near chance (kappa <=0.19). That means there's no solid human-judgment baseline to check these metrics against when the content in question is AI-generated in the first place.

The paper's title asks whether humans and computers align, but the more fundamental question is whether human judgments of AI-generated similarity form a reliable baseline at all -- if human raters facing AI-generated triplets frequently pick "neither" rather than a clear match, any computational metric benchmarked against those ratings inherits the same instability. The paper touches this self-referential problem only briefly, noting in passing that "similarity alone is not directly equivalent to plagiarism or copyright infringement," a point that deserves more direct follow-up.
