Predicting Music Valence and Arousal via Textual and Multimodal In-Context Learning in Large Language Models

用文字與多模態 in-context learning 讓大型語言模型修正小模型的音樂效價與喚醒度預測

Chi-En Dai, Chih-Pin Tan, Yi-Hsuan Yang
arXiv:2610.31202 · cs.SD; cs.AI; cs.CL · 投稿submitted 2026-10-22
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：台大楊奕軒團隊不讓 LLM 從零猜情緒分數，而是先用一個 91M 的小模型（BEATs＋MLP，只在 CH818 華語流行上訓練）給出初值，再塞 CH818 的示範給 GPT-5 或 Qwen3-Omni，請它只預測「該往哪裡修正多少」。在完全沒碰過的 MER60（60 首西洋流行）上，GPT-5 用 80 個示範把效價 R² 從小模型的 0.110 拉到 0.427，喚醒度從 0.783 到 0.874；Qwen3-Omni 直接聽音訊，10 個示範就到 0.346。要保留的是：測試集只有 60 個片段，±0.054 只反映示範抽樣的變異、沒有反映測試集的抽樣誤差，而且論文沒有和「CH818 上做 kNN 或線性校正」這類不需要 LLM 的基線比。Why this one: Yi-Hsuan Yang's group at National Taiwan University does not ask an LLM to guess emotion scores from scratch: a 91M-parameter base model (BEATs plus an MLP, trained only on the Chinese-pop CH818) gives an initial estimate, and GPT-5 or Qwen3-Omni, shown CH818 demonstrations, predicts only the correction. On the fully held-out MER60 (60 Western pop clips), GPT-5 with 80 demonstrations lifts valence R² from the base model's 0.110 to 0.427 and arousal from 0.783 to 0.874; Qwen3-Omni, listening to the audio directly, reaches 0.346 with 10 demonstrations. The caveats: the test set is 60 clips, the ±0.054 reflects only demonstration sampling, not test-set sampling error, and there is no comparison against LLM-free baselines such as kNN or linear recalibration on CH818.

動機Motivation

音樂情緒辨識（music emotion recognition, MER）裡，喚醒度（arousal，能量高低）從音訊預測得還不錯，效價（valence，正負向）一直很難，因為它更抽象、更依賴脈絡。作者先做了一個前導測試：在 MER60 上零樣本詢問，GPT-5 的喚醒度 R² 有 0.699，效價卻是 −0.801；Qwen3-Omni 30B 分別是 0.764 與 0.037。對照 2017 年 Hu 與 Yang 用 SVR 做跨資料集時的效價 0.270，大模型的零樣本效價明顯更差。

作者還提了一個論點：MER60 的歌都發行超過 15 年，很可能已經出現在預訓練語料裡，零樣本效價仍然很差，代表「聽過這首歌」或記住相關文字並不足以推出效價。所以這篇的問題是：不重新訓練大模型，只靠推論時的示範（in-context learning, ICL），能不能把效價預測救回來？

In music emotion recognition (MER), arousal (energy) is predicted reasonably well from audio, but valence (positive vs. negative) stays hard because it is more abstract and context-dependent. The authors' pilot test asks models zero-shot on MER60: GPT-5 gets arousal R² 0.699 but valence −0.801, and Qwen3-Omni 30B gets 0.764 and 0.037. Against the 0.270 cross-dataset valence of Hu and Yang's 2017 SVR, zero-shot valence from large models is clearly worse.

The authors add an argument: MER60's songs were released more than 15 years ago and are probably in the pre-training corpora, yet zero-shot valence is still poor, which suggests that having 'heard the song' or memorized related text is not enough to infer valence. The question is therefore whether demonstrations at inference time (in-context learning, ICL), with no retraining of the large model, can rescue valence prediction.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的比較對象是作者實驗室自己的前作：Hu 與 Yang（2017）在 CH818 與 MER60 之間做跨文化情緒預測，用手工特徵加 SVR，在 MER60 上拿到效價 0.270、喚醒度 0.800，這組數字在這篇的表 1 裡仍然是很強的基線。Music2Emo（Kang 與 Herremans）把 MERT 特徵接上回歸頭，本篇的小模型就是它的音訊分支、把 MERT 換成 BEATs。LLM 用在音樂情緒的前例是 Yang 等人（ISMIR 2025），但那是用 LLM 為 MIDI 標類別情緒，不是從音訊回歸連續值。

論文沒有講、但最值得講的一句：「小模型先預測、LLM 再修正殘差」這個架構，本質上就是 ASR 的生成式錯誤修正（generative error correction，如 HyPoradise，NeurIPS 2023）——第一階段模型給候選、LLM 負責改錯，只是這裡改的是連續數值而不是文字。而「LLM 靠示範做數值回歸」本身也已經有 Vacareanu 等人（COLM 2024）〈From Words to Numbers〉系統性地測過，他們發現 LLM 在 ICL 回歸上常常接近 kNN 一類的傳統方法。這篇的新意在於把兩者接起來、用在跨文化的音樂情緒上，而不是 ICL 回歸本身。

The most direct comparison is the lab's own earlier work: Hu and Yang (2017) did cross-cultural emotion prediction between CH818 and MER60 with handcrafted features and an SVR, reaching valence 0.270 and arousal 0.800 on MER60, which remains a strong baseline in this paper's Table 1. Music2Emo (Kang and Herremans) puts a regression head on MERT features; the base model here is its audio branch with MERT swapped for BEATs. The LLM precedent for music emotion is Yang et al. (ISMIR 2025), but that uses LLMs to assign categorical emotions to MIDI, not to regress continuous values from audio.

What the paper does not say, but most deserves saying: the 'small model predicts, LLM corrects the residual' design is essentially generative error correction from ASR (e.g., HyPoradise, NeurIPS 2023), where a first-pass model supplies a hypothesis and an LLM fixes it, except that here the correction is a number rather than text. LLM numeric regression from demonstrations has also been tested systematically by Vacareanu et al. (COLM 2024, 'From Words to Numbers'), who found ICL regression often lands near classical methods such as kNN. The novelty is joining the two for cross-cultural music emotion, not ICL regression itself.

方法Method

第一階段的小模型：每 5 秒取一段 BEATs 特徵，平均成 768 維，再接一個帶 batch normalization、ReLU、dropout 的 MLP，用 MSE 訓練 150 個 epoch，只用 CH818（818 個 30 秒華語流行片段，3 位專家標註）。它在 MER60 上的效價 R² 是 0.110、喚醒度 0.783；同樣在 DEAM 上訓練的版本，到了 MER60 效價是 −0.127，Music2Emo 是 −0.438，可見跨資料集落差之大。

第二階段有兩個變體。文字 ICL 用 GPT-5：每個示範包含小模型的預測、Qwen2-Audio 產生的聽感描述、Whisper-large-v3 在 HTDemucs 分離出來的人聲上轉出的歌詞、真值，以及兩者的差值 Δ；查詢只給前三項，請模型輸出 Δ，最後加回小模型的預測並裁到 1–9 範圍。多模態 ICL（MICL）用 Qwen3-Omni 30B，把聽感描述換成原始音訊，直接讓音訊 token 跟文字交錯放進上下文。示範隨機抽自 CH818，溫度 0，每組跑 5 次取平均；MER60 全部 60 首只當測試集。

Stage one: BEATs features are taken every 5 seconds, averaged into a 768-dimensional vector, and fed to an MLP with batch normalization, ReLU and dropout, trained with MSE for 150 epochs on CH818 only (818 thirty-second Chinese-pop clips, annotated by three experts). On MER60 it reaches valence R² 0.110 and arousal 0.783; the same model trained on DEAM falls to valence −0.127 on MER60, and Music2Emo to −0.438, which shows how large the cross-dataset gap is.

Stage two has two variants. Text ICL uses GPT-5: each demonstration holds the base prediction, a listener-feeling description generated by Qwen2-Audio, lyrics transcribed by Whisper-large-v3 from HTDemucs-separated vocals, the ground truth, and the offset Δ between them; the query gives only the first three, the model outputs Δ, which is added back to the base prediction and clipped to 1–9. Multimodal ICL (MICL) uses Qwen3-Omni 30B and replaces the description with raw audio, interleaving audio tokens with text in the context. Demonstrations are drawn at random from CH818, temperature is 0, each setting is averaged over 5 runs, and all 60 MER60 clips serve only as the test set.

限制Limitations

最大的問題是測試集太小。MER60 只有 60 個片段，R² 在 60 個點上的抽樣誤差本來就大；表裡的 ±0.054 是 5 次隨機抽示範之間的標準差，並不包含測試集本身的不確定性。80-shot 的 0.427 和 40-shot 的 0.354 之間差 0.073，在 60 個點上未必站得住。

第二是缺少不用 LLM 的對照。這個框架等於讓 LLM 看 CH818 的「預測—真值」配對再去修正，那最該比的是：在 CH818 上對小模型輸出做線性校正、或用文字嵌入做 kNN 回歸，看能拿到多少。論文自己的消融其實透露了一點：只給小模型預測、不給描述與歌詞時（ID 1），GPT-5 的效價只有 0.002、喚醒度 0.535，比小模型自己的 0.110 與 0.783 還差，代表 LLM 單看數字時是在破壞初值，不是在校正。

第三是聽感描述的角色。作者請 Qwen2-Audio 寫「聽者的感受」，包括整體情緒、能量與旋律和聲的情緒色彩，這段文字本身就是另一個零樣本情緒預測器的輸出。消融裡描述是最有用的單一輸入（單獨 0.209），所以增益有一部分可能來自「疊加了第二個情緒模型」，而不是 GPT-5 的推理。

The biggest problem is the size of the test set. MER60 has only 60 clips, and R² on 60 points carries large sampling error; the ±0.054 in the tables is the standard deviation across 5 random demonstration draws and does not include uncertainty from the test set itself. The 0.073 gap between 80-shot (0.427) and 40-shot (0.354) may not hold up on 60 points.

Second, there is no LLM-free control. The framework lets the LLM see CH818 prediction–truth pairs and then correct, so the obvious comparison is a linear recalibration of base outputs on CH818, or kNN regression on text embeddings. The paper's own ablation hints at this: given only the base prediction, with no description or lyrics (ID 1), GPT-5 reaches valence 0.002 and arousal 0.535, worse than the base model's own 0.110 and 0.783, so with numbers alone the LLM damages the anchor rather than calibrating it.

Third is the role of the description. Qwen2-Audio is asked to write 'the listener's feelings', covering overall mood, energy and the emotional color of melody and harmony, so that text is itself the output of another zero-shot emotion predictor. The description is the most useful single input in the ablation (0.209 alone), so part of the gain may come from stacking a second emotion model rather than from GPT-5's reasoning.

討論Discussion

兩個變體的最佳設定很不一樣，這是全篇最有意思的觀察。文字 ICL 的效價隨示範數一路往上：5-shot 0.188、10-shot 0.274、40-shot 0.354、80-shot 0.427；MICL 在 10-shot 達到 0.346 後就往下掉，80-shot 剩 0.238，作者推測是音訊 token 讓上下文太長。輸入組合也相反：文字 ICL 三樣全給最好（0.427），只要拿掉任何一樣就掉到 0.306 以下；MICL 則是「小模型預測＋歌詞」最好（0.346），再加上聽感描述反而降到 0.263，單給描述更是 −0.487。

模型規模的趨勢也清楚：80-shot 文字 ICL 換成 Qwen2-7B 掉到 0.164、Qwen3.5-122B 是 0.285、GPT-5-mini 是 0.317；10-shot MICL 換成 Qwen2.5-Omni-3B 是 0.123、7B 是 0.308。不過喚醒度的改善幅度小得多（0.783 到 0.874），而且 MICL 的喚醒度（0.768）甚至略低於小模型本身，所以標題說的「預測效價與喚醒度」，真正的貢獻幾乎都在效價。

實務上值得注意的是成本：每個查詢要塞 80 個示範給 GPT-5，或 10 段 30 秒音訊給 Qwen3-Omni，拿來標一首歌的情緒並不便宜。如果之後的 kNN 或線性校正基線能拿到接近的數字，這套方法的價值就會從「效果」轉到「可解釋性」——作者在 GitHub 上放了模型給出的推理範例，這可能才是 LLM 真正多出來的東西。

The two variants peak in very different places, which is the most interesting observation in the paper. Text-ICL valence keeps rising with demonstrations: 0.188 at 5 shots, 0.274 at 10, 0.354 at 40, 0.427 at 80. MICL peaks at 0.346 with 10 shots and then falls to 0.238 at 80, which the authors attribute to audio tokens making the context too long. Input combinations also diverge: text ICL is best with all three inputs (0.427) and drops to 0.306 or below when any one is removed, while MICL is best with base prediction plus lyrics (0.346), drops to 0.263 when the description is added, and reaches −0.487 with the description alone.

The scale trend is clear too: for 80-shot text ICL, Qwen2-7B drops to 0.164, Qwen3.5-122B gets 0.285 and GPT-5-mini 0.317; for 10-shot MICL, Qwen2.5-Omni-3B gets 0.123 and 7B 0.308. Arousal gains are much smaller, though (0.783 to 0.874), and MICL arousal (0.768) is even slightly below the base model, so although the title says valence and arousal, nearly all of the contribution is in valence.

Cost matters in practice: each query feeds GPT-5 80 demonstrations, or Qwen3-Omni ten 30-second audio clips, which is not cheap for labeling one song. If a later kNN or linear-recalibration baseline gets close, the method's value shifts from accuracy to interpretability; the authors post the models' reasoning examples on GitHub, and that may be what the LLM really adds.

Heard But Not Said: Prompting Understates Temporal Information of Musical Moments in Frozen Large Audio-Language Models

聽到了卻說不出：直接問時間戳記會低估凍結的大型音訊語言模型對音樂片段的時間資訊

Eunsik Shin
arXiv:2610.29256 · cs.SD; cs.CL · 投稿submitted 2026-10-21
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：首爾大學的單一作者研究，用 Slakh2100 的分軌啟動資訊做出 570 組成對查詢：同一段音訊配一句「狀態」描述（只有鋼琴和貝斯在彈）和一句「變化」描述（吉他退出，剩鋼琴和貝斯），在七個凍結的大型音訊語言模型上比較三種取出時間範圍的方法。直接要時間戳記時所有模型都在機率水準或以下（R@1@0.5 最高 0.072，基線 0.098）；改成逐窗讀 yes/no logit 後，狀態查詢全部高於基線，Qwen3-Omni 的變化查詢在給 8 秒前文後從 0.118 升到 0.268，追平狀態查詢。結論是「會驗證、不會找」。要保留的是：只測樂器有無、資料是 MIDI 合成音，而且最好的分數也只有約 0.3，沒有和 CLAP 一類的對比式模型做同樣的滑窗比較。Why this one: A single-author study from Seoul National University builds 570 paired queries from Slakh2100 stem activity: the same audio span gets a 'state' description ('only piano and bass are playing') and a 'change' description ('the guitar drops out, leaving piano and bass'), and three ways of extracting the time span are compared on seven frozen large audio-language models. Asked directly for timestamps, every model is at or below chance (best R@1@0.5 0.072, baseline 0.098); reading yes/no logits window by window instead, every model beats the baseline on state queries, and Qwen3-Omni's change queries rise from 0.118 to 0.268 with 8 seconds of preceding context, matching its state queries. The conclusion is 'can verify, cannot locate'. The caveats: only instrument presence is tested, the audio is MIDI-rendered, the best scores are still around 0.3, and no contrastive model such as CLAP is run through the same sliding window.

動機Motivation

大型音訊語言模型（large audio-language model, LALM）看起來懂音樂，但只要請它指出「某段描述發生在第幾秒」，表現通常很差。這篇要分辨兩種解釋：是模型內部根本沒有時間資訊，還是資訊在、只是「直接生成時間戳記」這個取出方式卡住了。

音樂的時間定位（temporal grounding）比環境音難，因為一首歌的各個段落常常共用樂器、速度與音色，要找出「某個時刻」往往得拿它跟前後文比。作者因此特別區分兩種查詢：只看當下就能判斷的「狀態」，和必須跟前面比才能判斷的「變化」，並且操控模型能聽到多少前文。

Large audio-language models (LALMs) seem to understand music, yet when asked at which second a described moment happens they usually do poorly. This paper separates two explanations: the model holds no temporal information internally, or the information is there and the extraction method, generating a timestamp, is the bottleneck.

Temporal grounding is harder in music than in environmental sound because sections of a song often share instrumentation, tempo and timbre, so locating a moment usually means comparing it with its surroundings. The author therefore distinguishes two query types, a 'state' that can be judged from the moment alone and a 'change' that requires comparison with what came before, and manipulates how much preceding audio the model hears.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最接近的前作是 Lin 與 Lee（2026）的 activation steering：他們發現一般音訊 LALM 內部有提示問不出來的時間訊號，但設定只有三個事件（機率 27.7%），也沒有操控前文或比較編碼器。音樂端的 MusTBench（Kwon 等人，2026）用提示做基準測試、再靠訓練改善；An 等人（2026）在 LALM 內部表徵上接一個訓練過的頭來定位，但作者指出那分不清資訊是原本就在、還是頭自己學出來的。音訊時刻檢索的專用系統則有 AM-DETR（Munakata 等人，ICASSP 2025）與逐幀對齊的 FLAM（Wu 等人，ICML 2025）。

論文沒有講、但最值得講的一句：Exp. 2 的「逐窗問 yes/no、讀 logit 當分數」，跟 VQAScore（Lin 等人，ECCV 2024）以及音訊版的 AQAScore 是同一招，只是拿來當定位分數而不是生成評估；而「先列出候選片段、再逐一打分」的骨架，就是 TALL 與 MCN（2017）那一代 proposal-based 影片時刻檢索的做法。所以「模型會驗證卻不會找」這個結論，換個角度看就是：把搜尋交給外部的滑窗，模型只負責當分類器，表現就會好很多——這正是兩階段方法本來的分工。理論上的根據來自 Hu 與 Levy（EMNLP 2023）「提示不能取代機率測量」。

The closest prior work is Lin and Lee's (2026) activation steering, which found temporal signal inside general-audio LALMs that prompting does not surface, but in a three-event setting (chance 27.7%) with no context manipulation or encoder comparison. On the music side, MusTBench (Kwon et al., 2026) benchmarks via prompting and then improves via training; An et al. (2026) attach a trained head to LALM internals for localization, which the author notes cannot separate information already present from information the head learns. Dedicated audio moment-retrieval systems include AM-DETR (Munakata et al., ICASSP 2025) and the frame-wise FLAM (Wu et al., ICML 2025).

What the paper does not say, but most deserves saying: Exp. 2's 'ask yes/no per window and read the logit as a score' is the same move as VQAScore (Lin et al., ECCV 2024) and its audio version AQAScore, used here as a localization score rather than for generation evaluation; and the skeleton of 'enumerate candidate segments, then score each' is how the proposal-based video moment retrieval of TALL and MCN (2017) worked. Seen that way, 'the model can verify but not locate' means that when an external sliding window does the search and the model only acts as a classifier, results improve a lot, which is exactly the two-stage division of labor. The theoretical grounding is Hu and Levy (EMNLP 2023), 'prompting is not a substitute for probability measurements'.

方法Method

探針的建法：Slakh2100 把 MIDI 渲染成分軌音訊，所以每一刻哪些樂器在響是確知的。作者找「稀疏織體」（至少 3 秒、最多四軌、且該組合佔全曲不到五分之一）與「樂器退出」（一軌連續響至少 6 秒後靜音至少 3 秒）。當稀疏織體緊接在退出之後，同一段就能寫成狀態查詢和變化查詢；變化查詢包含完整的狀態描述再加上退出資訊。兩句指向同一段音訊、同樣位置與長度，所以機率水準在構造上相同。再加上可聽度過濾（−6 dB 頻帶 SNR 等），去掉 221 首重複曲目後得到 503 首、713 組，其中 143 組當開發集，570 組評估。

三種取出方法：Exp. 1 給整首歌請模型生成起訖秒數；Exp. 2 不問時間，以 1 秒步長滑窗，每個窗結尾是被判斷的 2 秒、往前延伸 C 秒（C 為 0、2、4、8），讀「是否符合」的 yes/no logit 差，並對每組（曲目、查詢）做 z 正規化；Exp. 3 讀最後一個提示 token 對音訊 token 的注意力，取後三分之一層、所有 head 的平均。分數曲線用開發集調好的容忍度展開成區間，另外報一個把長度固定為真值長度的 oracle 診斷。

七個模型依編碼器分兩群：四個是短窗編碼器（Qwen2.5-Omni 2 秒、Qwen3-Omni 1 秒、兩個 MOSS 4 秒），三個是把輸入補到 30 秒的 Whisper 式編碼器（Qwen2-Audio、Audio Flamingo 3、Music Flamingo）。控制組有查詢打亂、跨曲目查詢、以 2 秒為單位打亂音訊；統計用 McNemar 檢定、以曲目為單位的 bootstrap，並做 Holm 校正。

Probe construction: Slakh2100 renders MIDI into per-instrument stems, so which instruments sound at every moment is known exactly. The author finds 'sparse textures' (at least 3 s, at most four stems, in a combination covering under a fifth of the track) and 'departures' (a stem playing at least 6 s then silent at least 3 s). When a sparse texture begins right after a departure, the same span can be written as a state query and a change query; the change query contains the full state description plus the departure. Both point to the same audio, position and duration, so chance is identical by construction. After audibility filtering (−6 dB best-band SNR and so on) and removing 221 duplicated tracks, there are 713 pairs on 503 tracks: 143 for dev, 570 for evaluation.

Three extraction methods: Exp. 1 gives the full track and asks for start and end seconds. Exp. 2 never asks for a time: it slides a window with a 1 s hop, each window ending with the judged 2 s and extending C seconds earlier (C = 0, 2, 4, 8), reads the yes–no logit margin for 'does this match', and z-normalizes per (track, query). Exp. 3 reads attention from the final prompt token to audio tokens, averaged over the last third of layers and all heads. Score curves are expanded into spans with a dev-tuned tolerance, plus an oracle diagnostic that fixes the duration to the ground truth.

The seven models split by encoder: four use short-segment encoders (Qwen2.5-Omni 2 s, Qwen3-Omni 1 s, both MOSS models 4 s), three use Whisper-style encoders that pad input to 30 s (Qwen2-Audio, Audio Flamingo 3, Music Flamingo). Controls are query shuffle, cross-track queries and 2 s audio shuffling; statistics use McNemar tests, track-level bootstrap and Holm correction.

限制Limitations

作者自己承認的範圍限制很清楚：Slakh2100 是 MIDI 合成音，查詢只涵蓋「哪些樂器在響或退出」，和聲、力度、節奏、人聲結構都沒測，也沒有真實錄音（作者建議下一步用 MUSDB18）。另一個坦白寫出來的是絕對分數偏低：Exp. 2 最好的是 Qwen3-Omni 在 C=8 時變化查詢的標準 R@1@0.3 0.312，基線是 0.147，大約兩倍，離可用還很遠。

論文沒有寫、但影響解讀的一點是缺少對比式模型的同條件基線。Exp. 2 的設定其實就是「外部滑窗＋二元分類器」，那最自然的參照是把 CLAP 或 FLAM 放進同一個滑窗、用文字—音訊相似度打分。沒有這一列，就無法判斷 LALM 的「驗證能力」比一個專門做音訊—文字對齊的小模型強還是弱。

另外，編碼器與規模、訓練資料是糾纏在一起的：短窗群裡表現最好的 Qwen3-Omni 也是最大的模型，作者自己也說規模與編碼器設計混在一起。30 秒補齊群的退化可能來自補齊，也可能只是那三個模型共用 Whisper 系的訓練背景，證據是相關性的。整篇是單一作者，探針、提示與程式碼要到發表後才釋出。

The scope limits the author acknowledges are clear: Slakh2100 is MIDI-rendered, queries cover only which instruments are present or leave, harmony, dynamics, rhythm and vocal structure are untested, and there are no real recordings (MUSDB18 is suggested as a next step). Also stated openly: absolute scores are low. The best Exp. 2 result is Qwen3-Omni's change query at C=8, standard R@1@0.3 of 0.312 against a 0.147 baseline, roughly double and still far from usable.

Not written in the paper but important for interpretation is the missing same-condition contrastive baseline. Exp. 2 is in effect 'external sliding window plus binary classifier', so the natural reference is CLAP or FLAM in the same window scoring by text–audio similarity. Without that row one cannot tell whether an LALM's 'verification ability' is stronger or weaker than a small model built for audio–text alignment.

Encoder, scale and training data are also entangled: Qwen3-Omni, the best short-segment model, is also the largest, and the author notes that scale is confounded with encoder design. The degradation in the 30 s padded group could come from padding or simply from the three models sharing a Whisper-family background; the evidence is correlational. The work has a single author, and the probe, prompts and code are to be released only upon publication.

討論Discussion

核心結果分三層。第一，給模型那一段（C=0）時，七個模型的狀態查詢都高於基線，oracle R@1@0.5 從 Qwen2-Audio 的 0.165 到 Qwen2.5-Omni 的 0.279（基線 0.100），而且每個模型的狀態都贏過同一段的變化查詢。第二，前文的效果跟著編碼器走：短窗群裡 Qwen3-Omni 的變化查詢從 0.118 升到 0.268（p=2×10⁻¹⁹），與狀態查詢的差距不再顯著（Holm p=0.92），MOSS-Audio 從 0.160 到 0.211；30 秒補齊群則反過來，前文越長狀態越差，Audio Flamingo 3 從 0.226 掉到 0.107，剛好一半。第三，Exp. 1 直接要時間戳記時沒有任何一格高於基線，多數模型的答案只取決於查詢的句型（狀態給開頭、變化給中段），打亂音訊也不變。

兩個細節很值得注意。MOSS-Music 和 MOSS-Audio 共用同一個編碼器，前者的變化查詢卻幾乎沒有回升（p=0.09），作者據此說「訓練可以壓掉編碼器原本允許的能力」，音樂專用微調不一定幫上時間推理。另外，把每個窗的 yes/no 判斷改成實際生成文字再計分，狀態分數會減半、變化掉到基線以下——有用的資訊在 logit 的連續差值裡，一旦二值化就丟了。

對做音樂時刻檢索的人來說，這篇的實用結論是：別期待 LALM 一次就吐出正確時間，比較可行的是外部提候選、模型逐一驗證。對做 LALM 的人來說，30 秒補齊的 Whisper 式編碼器在需要「比前後」的任務上可能是結構性的弱點，這比單純加音樂資料更值得檢查。

The core results come in three layers. First, handed the segment alone (C=0), all seven models beat the baseline on state queries, with oracle R@1@0.5 ranging from Qwen2-Audio's 0.165 to Qwen2.5-Omni's 0.279 (baseline 0.100), and every model's state query beats the change query on the same spans. Second, the effect of context follows the encoder: among short-segment models, Qwen3-Omni's change queries rise from 0.118 to 0.268 (p=2×10⁻¹⁹) and the gap to state queries is no longer significant (Holm p=0.92), while MOSS-Audio goes from 0.160 to 0.211; in the 30 s padded group it reverses, with state scores falling as context grows, and Audio Flamingo 3 drops from 0.226 to 0.107, exactly half. Third, no Exp. 1 cell beats the baseline when timestamps are requested directly; most models' answers depend only on the query's form (track opening for state, mid-track for change) and survive audio shuffling unchanged.

Two details stand out. MOSS-Music shares its encoder with MOSS-Audio, yet its change queries barely recover (p=0.09), from which the author concludes that training can suppress a capability the encoder permits, so music-specific fine-tuning does not necessarily help temporal reasoning. And when each window's yes/no judgment is generated as text and then scored, state scores halve and change falls below baseline: the useful information sits in the continuous logit margin and is lost once binarized.

For people working on music moment retrieval, the practical conclusion is not to expect an LALM to emit the right timestamp in one go; proposing candidates externally and having the model verify each is more workable. For people building LALMs, Whisper-style encoders padded to 30 s may be a structural weakness on tasks that require comparing with context, which is worth checking before simply adding more music data.

MixJudge: Learning a Language-Queryable Critic for Vocal Mixing

MixJudge：用合成的人聲混音問題訓練一個可以用語言提問的混音評審

Kuan-Hsun Ho, Yuan-Chiao Cheng, Yen-Tung Yeh, Yi-Hsuan Yang
arXiv:2610.30783 · cs.SD; cs.CL · 投稿submitted 2026-10-22
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇不教模型怎麼混音，而是教它「評」混音：作者在人聲＋伴奏上合成 11 種混音問題（太大聲、悶、刺耳、太濕、壓縮過度、被伴奏蓋掉等）、每種分三個嚴重度，共 78,740 組音訊—文字配對，用 CLAP 加一個關係融合網路訓練出 MixJudge，可以用一句話問「這段人聲是不是太悶」。在 MedleyDB 的 88 組真實人聲上，它從 31 種處理候選裡挑出的版本有 72.7% 比不處理更接近專業混音（隨機 38.0%）。要保留的是：比較對象只拿到混好的音訊、MixJudge 卻拿到分軌，資訊不對等；「接近專業版」只用音量和八個八度頻帶能量來量，量不到殘響與壓縮；而且沒有任何聽測。Why this one: This paper does not teach a model to mix but to judge a mix: the authors synthesize 11 vocal-mix issues on vocal-plus-backing clips (too loud, muddy, harsh, too wet, over-compressed, masked by the backing and so on) at three severity levels, 78,740 audio–text pairs in all, and train MixJudge, CLAP plus a relational fusion network, so one can ask in a sentence whether a vocal sounds muddy. On 88 real vocals from MedleyDB, the version it picks from 31 processing candidates is closer to the professional mix than no edit 72.7% of the time (chance 38.0%). The caveats: the compared systems receive only the mixed audio while MixJudge receives the stems, an unequal input; 'closer to professional' is measured only with level and eight octave-band energies, which cannot capture reverb or compression; and there is no listening test.

動機Motivation

混音除了動手處理，還要能判斷結果好不好。現有的自動混音研究幾乎都在「產出」那一邊：從分軌直接推處理參數，或拿一首參考曲做混音風格轉換。音訊語言模型雖然能給對話式的混音建議，但目前的評估把建議當成整體來看，沒有分開測「它能不能判斷某一個具體問題，而且判斷會隨嚴重度變化」。

作者想要的是一個明確的評審：給它一個混音狀態和一句描述問題的話，它回答這段混音有沒有這個問題、有多嚴重。同一把尺套到不同版本上，就能拿來比較處理方案。難處在於資料：公開資料集很少同時有未處理的分軌和專業混音，同一份素材有多個專業版本的就更少，所以作者選擇自己合成問題。

Mixing involves not only applying processing but also judging the result. Existing automatic-mixing research sits almost entirely on the production side: inferring processing parameters from stems, or transferring mixing style from a reference track. Audio-language models can offer conversational mixing advice, but current evaluations treat that advice as a whole and do not separately test whether a model can judge one specific issue in a way that tracks its severity.

The authors want an explicit critic: given a mix state and a sentence naming an issue, it says whether the mix has that issue and how badly. Applying the same yardstick to different versions then allows processing options to be compared. The difficulty is data: public datasets rarely contain both raw stems and professional mixes, and multiple professional versions of the same material are rarer still, so the authors synthesize the issues themselves.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文點名的最近鄰是 MixAssist（Clemens 與 Marasović，COLM 2025）：它微調音訊語言模型產生對話式混音建議，評估的是建議整體有沒有用；MixJudge 把同一件事拆成「針對單一問題的判斷」。產出端的前作包括 Steinmetz 等人（ICASSP 2021）的可微分混音台、Martínez-Ramírez 等人（ISMIR 2022）用域外資料訓練的自動混音、Koo 等人（ICASSP 2023）以對比學習分離音效的混音風格轉換，這些都在做處理而不是評判。文字端則有 Text2FX（Chu 等人，ICASSP 2025）用 CLAP 嵌入引導音效，以及 LLM2Fx-Tools 讓 LLM 呼叫效果器。

論文沒有講、但最值得講的一句：「人為合成已知嚴重度的劣化、再用排序損失讓模型學會分辨輕重」，在影像品質評估裡就是 RankIQA（Liu 等人，ICCV 2017）的做法——用合成失真的排序來預訓練一個無參考品質評估器。MixJudge 把這個想法搬到混音、再加上文字查詢，讓「品質分數」變成「針對某個問題的分數」。另一個值得對照的是 Meta 的 Audiobox Aesthetics（2025），它給的是整體美感分數，正是作者說的那種「不透明的單一分數」。

The nearest neighbor the paper names is MixAssist (Clemens and Marasović, COLM 2025), which fine-tunes audio-language models to give conversational mixing advice and evaluates whether the advice as a whole is useful; MixJudge splits the same task into issue-specific judgments. Production-side predecessors include the differentiable mixing console of Steinmetz et al. (ICASSP 2021), automatic mixing trained on out-of-domain data by Martínez-Ramírez et al. (ISMIR 2022) and the contrastive effect-disentangling style transfer of Koo et al. (ICASSP 2023); all of them process rather than judge. On the text side, Text2FX (Chu et al., ICASSP 2025) steers effects with CLAP embeddings and LLM2Fx-Tools has an LLM call effect plugins.

What the paper does not say, but most deserves saying: 'synthesize degradations with known severity, then use a ranking loss to teach the model to tell degrees apart' is exactly RankIQA (Liu et al., ICCV 2017) in image quality assessment, which pre-trains a no-reference quality model on rankings of synthetic distortions. MixJudge carries that idea to mixing and adds text queries, turning a quality score into a score for one named issue. Another useful contrast is Meta's Audiobox Aesthetics (2025), which outputs overall aesthetic scores, precisely the kind of opaque single score the authors argue against.

方法Method

課程（curriculum）：一個 clip 是 10 秒的人聲＋伴奏（伴奏是所有非人聲分軌的總和），單聲道、44.1 kHz。11 個問題是太大聲／太小聲、悶／薄、刺耳／暗、太濕／太乾、壓縮過度／壓縮不足，以及遮蔽（伴奏蓋過人聲）；前十個兩兩相反，遮蔽沒有對應的反義。共有 18 個啟發式「配方」，每個配方先檢查這個 clip 有沒有能表現該問題的素材，再以輕、中、重三級施加劣化，多數非音量類配方會把受影響聲部的響度拉回原值，避免問題跟整體音量混在一起，最後用聲學檢查篩掉不合格的。每個狀態配一句模板生成的描述，例如「The voice sounds completely muddy in the low mids」。訓練資料來自 236 首 MoisesDB 與 134 首 MUSDB18-HQ。

模型：用 CLAP 的音訊編碼器分別編碼伴奏、人聲與混合，加上角色嵌入與差值特徵，送進一個 2,560→2,048→1,024→512 的 GELU 融合網路，跟文字嵌入算餘弦相似度。每個訓練批次裡的樣本都是同一個 clip 的不同狀態，避免模型靠歌曲或歌手身分走捷徑：「嚴重度組」包含乾淨版和某配方的所有嚴重度，「比較組」包含乾淨版和最多七個同嚴重度的配方。

損失有四項：仿 SigLIP 的成對 sigmoid 匹配（同配方同嚴重度的跨 clip 配對都算正例）、問題選擇性邊界損失、反義問題邊界損失、以及嚴重度組內所有有序對的排序損失。報兩個版本：凍結 CLAP 只訓練融合網路，以及額外解凍 HTSAT 最後一個 stage 和 RoBERTa 最後兩層的 adapted 版。

Curriculum: a clip is 10 seconds of vocal plus backing (the sum of all non-vocal stems), mono at 44.1 kHz. The 11 issues are too loud/too quiet, muddy/thin, harsh/dull, too wet/too dry, over-compressed/under-compressed, and masking (the backing obscures the vocal); the first ten form opposite pairs and masking has no opposite. There are 18 heuristic recipes. Each first checks whether the clip has material that can express the issue, then applies a mild, medium or severe degradation; most non-level recipes restore the affected source's loudness so the issue is not confounded with overall level; acoustic checks then reject unsuitable states. Each state gets a template caption such as 'The voice sounds completely muddy in the low mids'. Training data come from 236 MoisesDB and 134 MUSDB18-HQ songs.

Model: CLAP's audio encoder separately encodes backing, vocal and blend; role embeddings and difference features are added and passed through a 2,560→2,048→1,024→512 GELU fusion network, whose output is compared with the text embedding by cosine similarity. Every training group holds different states of the same clip so the model cannot shortcut via song or singer identity: a 'severity group' contains the clean state and all severities of one recipe, and a 'comparison group' contains the clean state and up to seven recipes at the same severity.

There are four losses: SigLIP-style pairwise sigmoid matching (cross-clip pairs with the same recipe and severity count as positives), an issue-selectivity margin loss, an opposite-issue margin loss, and a ranking loss over all ordered pairs in a severity group. Two versions are reported: frozen CLAP with only the fusion network trained, and an adapted version that also unfreezes the last HTSAT stage and the last two RoBERTa layers.

限制Limitations

第一是比較不對等。三個生成式音訊語言模型（Music Flamingo、Audio Flamingo Next、Qwen2.5-Omni）和凍結 CLAP 只拿到混合後的音訊，MixJudge 卻同時拿到人聲、伴奏與混合。判斷「人聲是不是被蓋掉」「人聲是不是太大聲」時，有沒有分軌差很多，所以基線的低分有一部分來自輸入，不能全歸給 MixJudge 的訓練。

第二是評審考試的分布內問題。4,557 個合成測試樣本是用同一套 18 個配方產生的，嚴重度相關 0.971、完整排序正確率 0.922 衡量的是「學會了自己的配方」，而不是「懂真實混音裡的問題」。真正測外推的只有選單選擇那 88 組。

第三是選單選擇的距離量法。「接近專業版」只用 RMS 音量差加上 62 Hz 到 16 kHz 八個八度頻帶的對數能量差，這剛好對應增益與 EQ 類處理，但對殘響與壓縮幾乎量不到；而選單裡本來就有大量增益與 EQ 候選。所以 72.7% 的接近率主要證明它在音量與音色平衡上挑得對，殘響和動態有沒有判對，這個指標說不出來。作者也承認專業版只是一種實現，不是唯一最佳解。整篇沒有聽測，也只處理單聲道，立體聲寬度與相位問題都排除了。

First, the comparison is unequal. The three generative audio-language models (Music Flamingo, Audio Flamingo Next, Qwen2.5-Omni) and frozen CLAP receive only the blend, while MixJudge receives vocal, backing and blend. Judging whether the vocal is masked or too loud is far easier with stems, so part of the baselines' low scores comes from their input and cannot all be credited to MixJudge's training.

Second, the critic's exam is in-distribution. The 4,557 synthetic test examples come from the same 18 recipes, so a severity correlation of 0.971 and full-order accuracy of 0.922 measure 'learned its own recipes', not 'understands issues in real mixes'. Only the 88 menu-selection cases test transfer.

Third is the distance used in menu selection. 'Closer to professional' uses only the RMS level difference plus log-energy differences across eight octave bands from 62 Hz to 16 kHz, which matches gain and EQ processing but barely registers reverb or compression, and the menu is full of gain and EQ candidates. So the 72.7% closer rate mainly shows that it picks well on level and tonal balance; the metric cannot say whether reverb and dynamics were judged correctly. The authors also concede the professional version is one realization, not a unique optimum. There is no listening test, and only mono is handled, so stereo width and phase are excluded.

討論Discussion

評審考試的數字差距很大。adapted 版的反義問題雙向正確率 0.839（隨機 0.250）、嚴重度相關 0.971、完整排序正確率 0.922（隨機 0.048）、選擇性 0.838（隨機 0.091）；最好的基線 Qwen2.5-Omni 在這四項分別只有 0.374、0.309、0.239 與 0.197。但問題檢索這一項差距小得多：文字找音訊的 R@1 是 0.164，Qwen2.5-Omni 是 0.142，隨機 0.064——在「從同一 clip 的多個版本裡挑出有這個問題的那個」上，MixJudge 的領先其實不大，絕對值也低。

選單選擇上，平均證據策略的接近率 0.727、整份選單的 Spearman 相關 0.730、中位回收率 0.665（隨機 0.380、0、0.291）。在 88 份選單中有 83 份存在比不處理更好的候選，條件在這 83 份上，MixJudge 挑到改善版本的比例是 77.1%（平均）與 75.9%（最大值），挑過的候選橫跨 31 個中的 22 個，表示不是固定挑同一種處理。有個值得追問的細節：凍結 CLAP 的接近率只有 0.330，中位回收率卻有 0.552／0.636，跟 fusion-only 版的 0.567／0.500 差不多，論文沒有解釋這個落差。

消融顯示評審能力與下游效用不是同一件事：單用 SigLIP 損失時接近率是 0.739／0.773，最大值策略下甚至比完整模型的 0.716 還高；加上排序損失讓嚴重度相關從 0.578 跳到 0.916，選擇性卻只有 0.384；最後加上兩個邊界損失才把選擇性拉到 0.838。作者選完整版的理由是「分數比較符合問題的語意」，這是合理的取捨，但讀者應該知道它不是在每個下游指標都最好。

The critic's-exam gaps are large. The adapted version gets both-poles opposite accuracy 0.839 (chance 0.250), severity correlation 0.971, full-order accuracy 0.922 (chance 0.048) and selectivity 0.838 (chance 0.091); the best baseline, Qwen2.5-Omni, gets only 0.374, 0.309, 0.239 and 0.197 on those four. Issue retrieval is much closer, though: text-to-audio R@1 is 0.164 against Qwen2.5-Omni's 0.142 and chance 0.064, so on picking the version of a clip that has the queried issue, MixJudge's lead is small and the absolute value low.

In menu selection, the mean-evidence policy gets a closer rate of 0.727, whole-menu Spearman correlation 0.730 and median recovery 0.665 (chance 0.380, 0 and 0.291). An improving candidate exists in 83 of 88 menus; conditioned on those, MixJudge picks an improving one 77.1% (mean) and 75.9% (max) of the time, and its choices span 22 of the 31 candidates, so it is not always picking the same treatment. One detail deserves a question: frozen CLAP's closer rate is only 0.330, yet its median recovery is 0.552/0.636, close to the fusion-only version's 0.567/0.500, and the paper does not explain the discrepancy.

The ablation shows that critic ability and downstream utility are not the same thing. With SigLIP loss alone the closer rate is 0.739/0.773, higher under the max policy than the full model's 0.716; adding the ranking loss lifts severity correlation from 0.578 to 0.916 but leaves selectivity at 0.384; only the two margin losses raise selectivity to 0.838. The authors choose the full model because its scores better match issue semantics, a reasonable trade-off, but readers should know it is not best on every downstream metric.
