Harmonic Representations in Symbolic Music Transformers are Localized and Controllable

符號音樂 Transformer 的和聲表徵是局部且可操控的：用巴赫聖詠終止式的單音反事實對，在 Aria 上做激活修補、線性探針與轉向

Adi Silberschein, Megan Wei, Tamar Rott Shaham
arXiv:2610.15957 · cs.SD; cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-10-11
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：把巴赫聖詠終止式裡 V 和弦的三音降半音，做成只差一個音的反事實對，拿來對 Aria（以 LLaMA 為骨架的符號鋼琴模型）做激活修補、線性探針與轉向。和聲資訊的因果效應從第 6 層開始出現；調式（大/小調）的探針準確率在第 8–9 層達到 82.1%，調性要到第 15 層才到 65.5%。沿著探針導出的「同主音小調」方向推，解決到小調主和弦 i 的比例變成 57%（未轉向時 81% 解決到大調主和弦 I），FMD 從 80.27 升到 106.65。全部結果來自一個模型、一個語料，而且每個條件的 α 都是挑效果最強的那個。Why this one: Lowering the third of the V chord at Bach chorale cadences by a semitone gives counterfactual pairs that differ by one note; the authors use them to patch, probe and steer Aria, a LLaMA-based symbolic piano model. The causal effect of harmonic information appears from layer 6 onward; mode (major/minor) peaks at 82.1% probe accuracy around layers 8–9, while key only reaches 65.5% by layer 15. Pushing along a probe-derived parallel-minor direction makes 57% of continuations resolve to the minor tonic i (unsteered, 81% resolve to the major tonic I), with FMD rising from 80.27 to 106.65. All results come from one model on one corpus, and α is chosen per condition for the strongest effect.

動機Motivation

符號音樂生成模型已經能寫出像樣的四部和聲，但沒人說得清它們是靠結構化的和聲概念，還是靠分散、不可解讀的統計。這個問題不只是學術好奇：現有的控制手段不是微調（NotaGen、MIDI-LLM），就是在輸入或解碼端動手腳（DeepBach 的約束取樣、Anticipatory Music Transformer）。如果模型內部真的有「調性」「調式」這類方向，就可以在推論時直接推它，不用重新訓練。

作者選終止式當切入點，理由是它是和聲上的「動態決策」：屬和弦之後要解決到哪裡，是模型在單一時間點必須做出的選擇，而不是整段樂曲的靜態屬性。巴赫聖詠規則清楚、可以精準做反事實，是合適的試驗場。

Symbolic music generators can now write passable four-part harmony, but nobody can say whether they rely on structured harmonic concepts or on distributed, opaque statistics. This is not only academic: current control methods either fine-tune (NotaGen, MIDI-LLM) or intervene at the input or decoding level (DeepBach's constrained sampling, the Anticipatory Music Transformer). If the model internally has directions for key or mode, they could be pushed at inference time without retraining.

The authors choose cadences because they are a dynamic harmonic decision: where the dominant resolves is a choice the model makes at one moment, not a static property of the whole piece. Bach chorales follow clear conventions and allow precise counterfactuals, which makes them a suitable testbed.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

最直接的前作是第二作者自己的 SynTheory（Wei 等，ISMIR 2024），用合成刺激去探 Jukebox 與 MusicGen 是否編碼和弦、調性等樂理概念，結論是在較深層；Koo 等（2024）在 MusicGen 的個別注意力頭上找到調性偵測。這篇跟它們的差別是改用真實樂曲的最小編輯，而且問的是「解決到哪裡」這種當下決策，不是靜態標籤。

方法上最像的其實是 Ma 與 Xia（2024，ICML 機制可解釋性研討會）：他們也是先用探針找和弦根音與性質，再把探針導出的向量加進殘差流去干預。這篇的轉向向量是兩個類別的探針權重相減、正規化後乘上該層平均範數，這跟語言模型裡的 Inference-Time Intervention（Li 等 2023）用探針方向做干預是同一招；作者引了 Turner 等與 Rimsky 等的激活加法，但沒有跟 ITI 定位。其他音樂轉向工作如 SMITIN（Koo 等 2025）、Facchiano 等（2025）控制速度與音色、Singh 等用稀疏自編碼器，都在音訊模型上。

所以真正新的是刺激設計與分析對象（符號模型、終止式），不是干預工具本身。另外值得記住：Aria（Bradshaw 等，ISMIR 2025）是在鋼琴演奏資料上訓練的，巴赫四部聖詠對它而言並非主場。

The most direct predecessor is the second author's own SynTheory (Wei et al., ISMIR 2024), which used synthetic stimuli to test whether Jukebox and MusicGen encode chords, keys and other theory concepts, finding them in deeper layers; Koo et al. (2024) located key detection in individual MusicGen attention heads. This paper differs by using minimal edits of real pieces and by asking about an in-the-moment decision, where to resolve, rather than a static label.

Methodologically the closest work is Ma and Xia (2024, ICML mechanistic interpretability workshop), who also probed chord root and quality and then added probe-derived vectors to the residual stream. Here the steering vector is the difference of two class probe weights, normalised and scaled by the layer's mean norm, which is the same move as Inference-Time Intervention (Li et al. 2023) in language models; the authors cite Turner et al. and Rimsky et al. on activation addition but do not position against ITI. Other music steering work, such as SMITIN (Koo et al. 2025), Facchiano et al. (2025) for tempo and timbre, and Singh et al. with sparse autoencoders, targets audio models.

So what is new is the stimulus design and the object of study (a symbolic model, cadences), not the intervention tools. Also worth remembering: Aria (Bradshaw et al., ISMIR 2025) was trained on piano performance data, so four-part Bach chorales are not its home ground.

方法Method

資料：用 music21 在聖詠中找出 V→I（大調）與 V→i（小調）終止式，在 V 和弦結束處截斷，把 V 的三音降半音變成 v，得到 184 對（大調來源）與 91 對（小調來源），各取三個取樣種子，共 552 與 273 組生成。光是這一個音，大調來源解決到 I 的比例就從 87.5% 掉到 51.4%。

激活修補：逐層把反事實序列最後一個 token 的殘差流貼到原序列，用 IIA 計分；只看第一個生成音的音級，落在反事實解決和弦就算成功、落在原和弦算失敗，兩者皆是或皆非則不計。第 0–5 層幾乎沒效果，第 6 層開始上升並一路增加到後段。

探針：在每小節最後一個 token 上抽殘差流（3,176 個樣本），逐層訓練調式（2 類）與調性（18 類，機率基準 5.6%）的線性探針。調式在第 8–9 層達 82.1% 後略降；調性要到第 10 層後才急升，第 15 層 65.5%。

轉向：v = 平均範數 ×（w₁ − w₂）/‖w₁ − w₂‖，乘上 α 加在探針表現最好的層（調性 11–15、調式 5–10），作用在所有位置。調式轉向（α = 0.25）把 I 從 110 例降到 58 例，但流向分散；關係小調方向（α = 0.15）讓 vi 佔 32%；同主音小調方向（α = 0.1）讓 i 佔 57%，只動第 12 層也有 118 例中 76 例轉向。

Data: music21 finds V→I (major) and V→i (minor) cadences; each chorale is cut at the end of V and V's third is lowered a semitone to make v, yielding 184 pairs from major sources and 91 from minor, each continued with three seeds (552 and 273 generations). This single note alone drops resolutions to I in major sources from 87.5% to 51.4%.

Patching: layer by layer, the residual stream of the last token is copied from the counterfactual into the original and scored with IIA, using only the first generated note's pitch class: success if it belongs only to the counterfactual resolution chord, failure if only to the original, and not counted if it belongs to both or neither. Layers 0–5 have almost no effect; the effect rises from layer 6 and keeps growing.

Probes: residual activations at the last token of each bar (3,176 samples) train per-layer linear probes for mode (2 classes) and key (18 classes, chance 5.6%). Mode peaks at 82.1% around layers 8–9 then dips; key rises sharply after layer 10 to 65.5% at layer 15.

Steering: v = mean norm × (w₁ − w₂)/‖w₁ − w₂‖, scaled by α and added at the best probe layers (11–15 for key, 5–10 for mode) at all positions. Mode steering (α = 0.25) cuts I from 110 to 58 cases but spreads diffusely; the relative-minor direction (α = 0.15) gives vi 32%; the parallel-minor direction (α = 0.1) gives i 57%, and steering layer 12 alone shifts 76 of 118 cases.

限制Limitations

α 是每個條件掃一遍、挑「和聲效果最強」的那個值，而且看起來是在同一批評估資料上挑的，沒有獨立的驗證集。57% 與 32% 因此比較接近上限，而不是一個使用者照設定就能拿到的數字。

品質宣稱的尺很寬。同主音小調轉向的 FMD 從 80.27 升到 106.65，相對漲了三成；作者說這「接近基線」，參照物是巴赫聖詠對巴赫賦格的 FMD 195.97。關係小調轉向的結構錯誤率 2.9%（未轉向 0.0%）。和聲熵從 1.26 升到 1.50 被解讀為「更多樣」，但也可能只是更不像巴赫。

IIA 只看第一個生成音，而且把同時屬於兩個和弦、或兩者皆非的音排除，被排除的比例沒有報告；圖 3 的具體數值也沒寫進正文。探針沒有控制任務（Hewitt 與 Liang 2019 那種），關係調較難分的現象也可能只是反映小節內的音級統計——關係大小調本來就共用同一組音。

最後，只有一個模型、一個語料、沒有跟其他轉向法（例如類別平均差向量）或單純改輸入的做法比較。作者自己承認聖詠和聲受限，結論能否推到半音化較重的曲目尚未驗證。

α is scanned per condition and the value with the strongest harmonic effect is reported, apparently on the same evaluation data, with no separate validation set. The 57% and 32% figures are therefore closer to ceilings than to numbers a user would get from a fixed setting.

The quality claim uses a loose ruler. Parallel-minor steering raises FMD from 80.27 to 106.65, a 33% relative increase; the authors call this close to baseline by comparison with the 195.97 FMD between Bach chorales and Bach fugues. Relative-minor steering has a 2.9% structural error rate (0.0% unsteered). Roman-numeral entropy rising from 1.26 to 1.50 is read as more variety, but it could equally mean less Bach-like.

IIA looks only at the first generated note and drops notes that belong to both chords or to neither, and the dropped share is not reported; the Fig. 3 values are not given in the text. The probes have no control task (in the sense of Hewitt and Liang 2019), so the difficulty separating relative keys might just reflect pitch-class statistics within the bar, since relative major and minor share the same notes.

Finally, there is one model, one corpus, and no comparison with other steering methods (such as class mean-difference vectors) or with simply editing the input. The authors acknowledge chorales are harmonically constrained and that generalisation to more chromatic repertoire is untested.

討論Discussion

最有意思的是不對稱性：同主音小調方向只要 α = 0.1、甚至只動一層就能讓 i 佔 57%，關係小調方向要 α = 0.15、整段層數才讓 vi 佔 32%。作者用探針的混淆矩陣解釋：把關係大小調合成一類後準確率從 65.5% 升到 83.4%，按同主音合併只有 65.3%，表示模型把關係調放得很近，推起來自然不乾脆。這跟 Krumhansl 與 Kessler（1982）的調距離研究一致，關係大小調在知覺上本來就是近鄰。

不過「幾何對應樂理」這個結論要打折。關係調共用音級集合，任何只看音級分布的特徵都會把它們放在一起，未必需要模型學到抽象的調性概念。要分辨兩者，得拿第 0 層嵌入或純音級直方圖當基線探針，看 83.4% 有多少是白送的。

對做控制的人來說，訊息是正面的：在 Aria 這類模型上，調性方向是個便宜、免訓練的推論時旋鈕，品質下降有限。但要變成工具，還需要固定 α 的規則（例如按範數比例或在驗證集上選）、跟改提示詞這種最簡單做法的對照，以及在非聖詠語料上的複製。

The most interesting finding is the asymmetry: the parallel-minor direction needs only α = 0.1, or even a single layer, to make i 57% of resolutions, while the relative-minor direction needs α = 0.15 across the full layer range to make vi 32%. The authors explain it with probe confusions: merging relative keys into one class raises accuracy from 65.5% to 83.4%, merging parallel keys gives only 65.3%, so the model places relative keys close together and pushing between them is less decisive. This matches Krumhansl and Kessler (1982), where relative major and minor are perceptual neighbours.

The claim that internal geometry mirrors music theory deserves a discount, though. Relative keys share a pitch collection, so any feature that only sees pitch-class distributions would group them, without the model needing an abstract notion of key. Separating the two would need a baseline probe on layer-0 embeddings or plain pitch-class histograms, to see how much of the 83.4% comes for free.

For people working on control, the message is positive: on Aria-like models a key direction is a cheap, training-free inference-time knob with limited quality loss. Turning it into a tool still needs a fixed rule for α (for example a norm ratio, or selection on a validation set), a comparison with the simplest alternative of editing the prompt, and replication on corpora other than chorales.

Explore This! Beat and Downbeat Tracking From a Learned Tatum Grid

Explore This!：先學一條 tatum 脈動網格，再在每個網格點上預測 280 種拍子/小節狀態的聯合分布，來做拍點與重拍追蹤

Robert Kihlborg, André Holzapfel, Jan Schlüter
arXiv:2610.14817 · cs.SD; cs.LG; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-10-10
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：不再輸出彼此獨立的逐幀拍點/重拍機率，而是先預測一條 tatum 網格（每 1 秒視窗 12 個候選速度帶，用可微的「soft recall」訓練），再在每個 tatum 點上預測 280 種拍子與小節聯合狀態的分布。以 Beat This 為骨架、資料與訓練設定完全相同，GTZAN 重拍 CMLt 從 67.3 升到 72.7、重拍 AMLt 從 79.1 升到 86.2，但拍點 F1 從 89.1 降到 88.0，整體仍輸給最好的 DBN 系統（重拍 CMLt 74.7）。作者自己定位為概念驗證。Why this one: Instead of independent framewise beat/downbeat probabilities, the model first predicts a tatum grid (12 candidate tempo bands per 1-second window, trained with a differentiable 'soft recall'), then predicts at each tatum a distribution over 280 joint beat/bar states. Built on Beat This with identical data and training, it raises GTZAN downbeat CMLt from 67.3 to 72.7 and downbeat AMLt from 79.1 to 86.2, but beat F1 drops from 89.1 to 88.0 and it still trails the best DBN system overall (downbeat CMLt 74.7). The authors present it as a proof of concept.

動機Motivation

主流拍點追蹤流程是：神經網路輸出逐幀的拍點與重拍機率，再用 DBN 或找局部極大值把它們變成時間點。作者指出這個表示法有結構性缺陷：每一幀的機率彼此獨立，所以無法表達節拍歧義——一段在 A、B 兩種解讀間搖擺的節奏，跟一段在 C、D、E、F 四種解讀間搖擺的節奏，可能產生一模一樣的逐幀機率（論文圖 1）。內部一致性也只能靠後處理補，DBN 又通常假設拍號固定，不用 DBN 則會出現重拍重複或漏掉。

資料和指標讓這個問題看不見：Beat This 的訓練曲目 97.7% 每分鐘拍號變化不到一次，85% 是 4/4、9.6% 是 3/4；F1 把拍點當獨立事件，AMLt 只認二或四的分組，12/8 被聽成 4/4 會被判全錯。

理想做法是對整段標註建立完整分布，但 30 秒 1500 幀、每幀三種狀態就是 3¹⁵⁰⁰ 種可能。作者要找的是介於「全聯合」和「全獨立」之間的分解。

The dominant pipeline has a network output framewise beat and downbeat probabilities, then turns them into time points with a DBN or peak picking. The authors argue this representation is structurally flawed: framewise probabilities are independent, so they cannot express metrical ambiguity. A rhythm torn between readings A and B can produce exactly the same framewise probabilities as one torn between C, D, E and F (the paper's Fig. 1). Internal consistency has to be patched in afterwards; DBNs usually assume a fixed time signature, and DBN-free systems produce duplicated or missing downbeats.

Data and metrics hide the problem: 97.7% of Beat This's training pieces have fewer than one time-signature change per minute, 85% are in 4/4 and 9.6% in 3/4; F1 treats beats as independent events, and AMLt only accepts groupings of two or four, so hearing 12/8 as 4/4 counts as completely wrong.

The ideal would be a full distribution over annotations, but 1500 frames over 30 seconds with three states each gives 3¹⁵⁰⁰ possibilities. The authors look for a factorisation between fully joint and fully independent.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

直接的比較對象是 Beat This（Foscarin 等，ISMIR 2024）：Transformer 加上容許位移的 BCE 損失，不用 DBN 就拿到當時最好的 F1。這篇保留它的前端與六個 Transformer 區塊，只在第 3 與第 6 區塊後插入網格與子網格模組，所以兩者的差距可以歸因到輸出表示。另一條線是 Böck 等（2016）的逐幀活化加 DBN，後續 TCN、SpecTNT（Hung 等）、Zhao 等的擴張注意力都沿用同一策略。

「先找脈動、再往上疊拍子與小節」並不新：Klapuri 等（2006）就同時估計 tatum、tactus 與小節層級，Grosche 與 Müller 的 PLP（主導局部脈動）是最常見的脈動估計。最接近的是 Gagneré 等的多假設模型，也在網格上產生多個節拍解讀，但網格來自 PLP，遇到速度或拍號突變就失效（這類曲目甚至被排除在訓練外），而且多假設只用於自監督預訓練，推論時仍回到逐幀活化。

這篇子網格狀態（每拍幾個 tatum、拍內相位、每小節幾拍、小節內相位）本質上就是 Whiteley 等（2006）與 Krebs 等（2015）的 bar-pointer 狀態空間，只是離散在學到的 tatum 上，而且轉移不是手寫的機率模型，而是由網路在每個 tatum 點直接預測。

The direct comparison is Beat This (Foscarin et al., ISMIR 2024): a Transformer with a shift-tolerant BCE loss that reached the best F1 at the time without a DBN. This work keeps its frontend and six Transformer blocks and only inserts grid and subgrid modules after blocks 3 and 6, so the gap between the two can be attributed to the output representation. The other line is Böck et al. (2016), framewise activations plus a DBN, followed by TCNs, SpecTNT (Hung et al.) and the dilated attention of Zhao et al., all with the same strategy.

Finding a pulse first and stacking beats and bars on top is not new: Klapuri et al. (2006) jointly estimated tatum, tactus and measure, and Grosche and Müller's PLP (predominant local pulse) is the most common pulse estimator. The closest work is Gagneré et al.'s multiple-hypothesis model, which also derives several metrical readings from a grid, but its grid comes from PLP and fails on abrupt tempo or meter changes (such pieces are excluded from training), and the hypotheses are only used in self-supervised pretraining while inference falls back to framewise activations.

The subgrid state here (tatums per beat, phase within the beat, beats per bar, phase within the bar) is essentially the bar-pointer state space of Whiteley et al. (2006) and Krebs et al. (2015), discretised on a learned tatum, with transitions predicted by the network at each tatum instead of written as a hand-specified probability model.

方法Method

前端：30 秒、128 個 mel 頻帶、每秒 50 幀的頻譜，經 Beat This 的前端轉成 1024 維，再投影到 K = 256（介於 Beat This 小型 128 與大型 512 之間），通過前三個 Transformer 區塊。

網格模組：把 1500 幀切成 30 個 1 秒視窗。每個視窗預測 12 個候選，各自佔三分之一個八度，合計涵蓋每分鐘 30–480 個脈動的四個八度；每個候選有一個速度參數（用 sigmoid 夾在自己的區間內）和一個相位參數，外加 12 類 softmax 權重。每個候選畫成一條值域 [0, 1] 的正弦波，soft recall 定義為正弦波在真實拍點上的平均值，訓練目標是加權 soft recall 最大化。單一候選的 soft recall 不是凹函數，會卡在局部極值，所以用混合專家的形式並在反傳時把權重混入一點均勻分布，讓每個候選都拿到梯度。

相鄰視窗的一致性：用邊界處的相位跳動與速度差定義兩候選間的不一致（大到一定程度就軟夾住），再用 Sinkhorn 演算法算兩組 12 候選分布之間的 EMD 當正則。推論時每個視窗取權重最大的候選，串接後以 3 幀標準差的高斯平滑，峰值即 tatum 點；正弦、餘弦與 tatum 遮罩投影到 256 維加回特徵，再過剩下三個 Transformer 區塊。

Frontend: a 30-second spectrogram with 128 mel bands at 50 frames per second goes through Beat This's frontend to 1024 dimensions, is projected to K = 256 (between Beat This's small 128 and large 512), and passes the first three Transformer blocks.

Grid module: the 1500 frames are split into 30 one-second windows. Each window predicts 12 candidates, each spanning a third of an octave, together covering four octaves from 30 to 480 pulses per minute; each candidate has a tempo parameter (sigmoid-clamped within its band) and a phase parameter, plus a 12-way softmax weight. Each candidate is rendered as a sine wave in [0, 1], soft recall is the mean of that wave at the true beats, and training maximises weighted soft recall. Soft recall for one candidate is not concave and has local maxima, hence the mixture-of-experts form, with some uniformity mixed into the weights on the backward pass so every candidate gets gradient.

Consistency across windows: the inconsistency between two candidates is defined by the phase jump at the border and the tempo difference (soft-clamped once large), and an EMD between the two 12-candidate distributions, computed with the Sinkhorn algorithm, is used as a regulariser. At inference the top candidate per window is kept, windows are concatenated and smoothed with a 3-frame Gaussian, and the peaks are the tatum points; sine, cosine and tatum mask are projected to 256 dimensions, added back, and pass the remaining three Transformer blocks.

限制Limitations

最關鍵的一點是作者自己點出的：Viterbi 推論並沒有贏過簡單的 0.5 閾值。交叉驗證中拍點 CMLt 是 79.2（閾值）對 78.5（Viterbi），重拍 CMLt 是 78.4 對 76.9；GTZAN 上 Viterbi 只多 0.1–1.1 分。整篇的論點是「聯合分布能表達歧義」，但真正利用聯合結構的解碼方式沒有帶來好處，代表要嘛資料裡歧義樣本太少，要嘛模型沒學出那份表達力。論文也沒有任何直接評估歧義輸出的實驗。

網格是天花板。交叉驗證網格召回率 91.1%、GTZAN 90.5%，漏掉的拍點後面無法補回；ASAP（古典鋼琴）召回 80.9% 但 AMLt 只有 56.3%，SMC 召回 69.1%。1 秒一個速度的設計跟不上古典音樂的彈性速度，作者也承認縮短視窗會破壞損失的凸性。

其他：子網格損失因 BCE 數值不穩改用 MSE；對網格與子網格的超參數很敏感；「拍號變化」子集只佔交叉驗證資料 1.6%，曲目數沒有列出，36.4 → 46.9 的提升樣本可能不大；GTZAN 上其他系統的數字直接取自原論文，不是重跑。

The most important point is one the authors raise themselves: Viterbi inference does not beat a simple 0.5 threshold. In cross-validation, beat CMLt is 79.2 (threshold) versus 78.5 (Viterbi) and downbeat CMLt 78.4 versus 76.9; on GTZAN Viterbi adds only 0.1–1.1 points. The paper's argument is that a joint distribution can express ambiguity, yet the decoder that actually uses the joint structure brings no benefit, which means either the data has too few ambiguous examples or the model has not learned that expressiveness. There is also no experiment that evaluates the ambiguity output directly.

The grid is a ceiling. Grid recall is 91.1% in cross-validation and 90.5% on GTZAN, and beats missed there cannot be recovered; on ASAP (classical piano) recall is 80.9% but AMLt only 56.3%, and SMC recall is 69.1%. One tempo per second cannot follow classical rubato, and the authors admit shorter windows break loss convexity.

Other issues: the subgrid loss uses MSE because BCE was numerically unstable; the model is sensitive to grid and subgrid hyperparameters; the changing-meter subset is only 1.6% of the cross-validation data with the piece count not stated, so the 36.4 → 46.9 gain may rest on a small sample; GTZAN numbers for other systems are copied from their papers rather than rerun.

討論Discussion

數字上的故事很清楚：拍點層級跟 Beat This 持平或略輸，重拍連續性明顯較好。交叉驗證重拍 CMLt 78.4 對 73.5；按拍號切開，純 4/4 是 84.0 對 78.9，純非 4/4 是 56.0 對 54.4（幾乎沒差），拍號變化的曲目是 46.9 對 36.4。GTZAN 上 72.7 的重拍 CMLt 也接近 Beat This 加 DBN 的 73.3。換句話說，這個架構不用 DBN 就拿回了大部分 DBN 帶來的重拍一致性。

但這份提升比較像來自「結構約束」而不是「歧義建模」。子網格狀態本身就保證每小節拍數一致，KL 一致性損失又懲罰前後矛盾，這兩者都直接對應 CMLt 這種連續性指標；而真正處理歧義的 Viterbi 沒加分。作者在討論裡也承認，閾值推論能贏 Beat This 正說明理論引導設計的正則化力量。

這篇值得讀的地方在於它誠實又可拆解：網格與子網格可以分開改進，學到的 tatum 也可能取代 PLP 用在 Gagneré 那類方法。要證明歧義表達真的有用，下一步需要有多位標註者或刻意挑 12/8 與 4/4 可互換的曲目，並設計能為多種合理解讀給分的指標。程式碼已公開。

The numerical story is clear: beat-level performance matches or slightly trails Beat This, while downbeat continuity is clearly better. Cross-validation downbeat CMLt is 78.4 versus 73.5; split by meter, pure 4/4 is 84.0 versus 78.9, pure non-4/4 is 56.0 versus 54.4 (almost no difference), and pieces with changing meter are 46.9 versus 36.4. On GTZAN, the 72.7 downbeat CMLt is close to Beat This plus DBN at 73.3. In other words, the architecture recovers most of the downbeat consistency a DBN provides, without a DBN.

But the gain looks more like the effect of structural constraints than of ambiguity modelling. The subgrid state itself guarantees a consistent number of beats per bar, and the KL consistency loss penalises contradictions over time, both of which map directly onto a continuity metric like CMLt; the Viterbi decoder that actually handles ambiguity adds nothing. The authors concede in their discussion that threshold inference beating Beat This shows the regularising power of theory-informed design.

The paper is worth reading because it is honest and separable: grid and subgrid can be improved independently, and the learned tatum could replace PLP in methods like Gagneré's. To show that expressing ambiguity really helps, the next step needs multi-annotator data or deliberately chosen pieces where 12/8 and 4/4 are interchangeable, plus a metric that credits several plausible readings. The code is released.

Predicting Audio Popularity: A Comparative Study of Metadata and Acoustic Features Across Music and Sound Effects

預測音訊熱門度：在 Freesound 的音樂與音效上比較元資料、平台聲學描述子、eGeMAPS 與 MERT 嵌入

Ikram El Hadi, Nihad Elbari, Kawtar Tlemçani, Théo Mariotte, Meysam Shamsi
arXiv:2610.16809 · cs.SD; cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-10-12

為什麼挑這篇：用約 3.5 萬個 Freesound 音檔，分成音樂與音效兩組，把下載數（依分位數切 3 類）與平均評分（含「沒有評分」共 4 類）當分類目標，比較 22 個元資料特徵、47 個平台聲學描述子、88 維 eGeMAPS 與 1024 維 MERT 嵌入，模型是隨機森林、XGBoost 與 MLP。元資料最能預測下載數（F1：音樂 0.735、音效 0.846），平台描述子最能預測評分（0.640、0.572）。但音效組的 eGeMAPS 與 MERT 在三個模型上都是同一個分數、信賴區間 ±0.000，看起來像模型只輸出常數，而且論文正文有一處跟自己的表格矛盾。Why this one: Using about 35,000 Freesound clips split into music and sound effects, the authors classify download count (3 quantile classes) and average rating (4 classes including 'no rating') from 22 metadata features, 47 platform acoustic descriptors, 88-dimensional eGeMAPS or 1024-dimensional MERT embeddings, with Random Forest, XGBoost and an MLP. Metadata best predict downloads (F1: music 0.735, sound effects 0.846); platform descriptors best predict rating (0.640, 0.572). But eGeMAPS and MERT on sound effects give the same score across all three models with ±0.000 intervals, which looks like constant predictions, and one passage of the text contradicts the paper's own table.

動機Motivation

音訊分享平台上的內容越來越多，能不能事先預測一個檔案會不會被下載、被給高分，對推薦與搜尋排序都有用。作者認為過去的熱門度預測大多用封閉平台資料，拿不到原始音訊，因此「元資料」與「純聲學特徵」各自能解釋多少，很少被系統性比較。

Freesound 同時提供原始音訊、描述性元資料（類別、標籤、授權、上傳者）與使用者互動指標（下載數、評分），適合拿來做這種比較。作者另外把音樂（10–60 秒，多集中在 10–20 秒）和音效（0.5–3 秒）分開處理，理由是兩者在長度、結構與使用情境上差很多，適用的聲學描述子也可能不同。

Audio sharing platforms hold ever more content, and predicting in advance whether a file will be downloaded or rated highly is useful for recommendation and search ranking. The authors argue that most popularity prediction work uses closed-platform data without raw audio, so how much metadata versus purely acoustic features can explain has rarely been compared systematically.

Freesound offers raw audio, descriptive metadata (category, tags, licence, uploader) and user interaction indicators (downloads, ratings) at once, which suits such a comparison. The authors also treat music (10–60 seconds, mostly 10–20) and sound effects (0.5–3 seconds) separately, because they differ greatly in length, structure and use, and different acoustic descriptors may suit each.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

論文引用的前作包括 Lee 與 Lee（2018，IEEE TMM）用速度、能量、調性等描述子做音訊熱門度預測，Martin-Gutierrez 等（2020）結合聲學、元資料與社群文字的多模態深度模型，以及 Interiano 等（2018）分析排行榜歌曲的可預測性。沒有提到的是 Pachet 與 Roy（2008）那篇著名的〈Hit Song Science Is Not Yet a Science〉，他們發現聲學特徵預測熱門歌曲幾乎不比隨機好；這篇宣稱聲學特徵「顯著高於機率水準」，但目標換成 Freesound 下載數、族群也完全不同，兩者不能直接對照。

「很少有開放平台同時提供音訊、元資料與熱門度」這個前提也不完全成立：FMA 資料集（Defferrard 等，ISMIR 2017）就附帶完整音訊、元資料與播放數、收藏數。這篇真正的特點是把音效納入、並同時比較四種特徵族群，方法本身是標準的表格分類流程，沒有新模型。

Cited prior work includes Lee and Lee (2018, IEEE TMM), who predicted audio popularity from descriptors such as tempo, energy and key; Martin-Gutierrez et al. (2020), a multimodal deep model combining acoustics, metadata and social-media text; and Interiano et al. (2018) on the predictability of chart songs. Not mentioned is Pachet and Roy's (2008) well-known 'Hit Song Science Is Not Yet a Science', which found acoustic features barely beat chance for predicting hits; this paper claims acoustic features are significantly above chance, but the target is Freesound downloads and the population is entirely different, so the two cannot be compared directly.

The premise that few open platforms provide audio, metadata and popularity together does not fully hold either: the FMA dataset (Defferrard et al., ISMIR 2017) ships full audio, metadata, and listen and favourite counts. What is actually distinctive here is including sound effects and comparing four feature families side by side; the method is a standard tabular classification pipeline with no new model.

方法Method

目標：下載數依分位數切成低、中、高三類（類別大致平衡）；平均評分切成四類，0 類是「沒有任何評分」，其餘三類為低、中、高。兩組資料各約 1.75 萬與 1.79 萬筆，80/20 切分（訓練 13,963 / 測試 3,491，以及 14,340 / 3,586），表上沒標哪組是音樂。

特徵：元資料 22 維，偏態數值（論文舉例「ratings、comments、duration」）取對數，上傳時間轉成「年齡」，類別變數 one-hot，上傳者以投稿頻率表示，標籤做多標籤指標，檔名做雜湊，描述文字取 GloVe 平均。平台描述子 47 維（多由 Essentia 計算，缺值補 0）。eGeMAPS 88 維泛函。MERT-v1-330M 取最後一層、時間平均成 1024 維，輸入轉單聲道 24 kHz 並截斷到固定長度。

模型與評估：隨機森林、XGBoost、三層 MLP（128-64-32、ReLU、最多 300 個 epoch 加早停）。訓練集用 SMOTE 過採樣，評估用 F1。另外對音效組的 XGBoost 做 SHAP 分析：元資料中最重要的是類別、子類別、時長或檔案大小、授權與位元深度；音效的聲學描述子以頻譜平坦度、亮度、時間展布最重要，音樂則是「溫暖度」進前五。

Targets: downloads are cut into low, medium and high by quantiles (roughly balanced classes); average rating is cut into four classes, where class 0 means no rating at all and the other three are low, medium and high. The two sets have roughly 17,500 and 17,900 items each with an 80/20 split (13,963 train / 3,491 test, and 14,340 / 3,586); the table does not say which set is music.

Features: 22 metadata features, with skewed numbers (the paper gives 'ratings, comments, duration' as examples) log-transformed, upload time turned into an age feature, categoricals one-hot encoded, the uploader represented by contribution frequency, tags as multi-label indicators, filenames hashed, and descriptions as averaged GloVe vectors. 47 platform descriptors (mostly computed with Essentia, missing values set to 0). 88 eGeMAPS functionals. MERT-v1-330M last-layer output averaged over time to 1024 dimensions, with input converted to mono at 24 kHz and truncated to a fixed length.

Models and evaluation: Random Forest, XGBoost, and a three-layer MLP (128-64-32, ReLU, up to 300 epochs with early stopping). SMOTE oversamples the training set and F1 is the metric. A SHAP analysis on the sound-effect XGBoost models finds category, subcategory, duration or file size, licence and bit depth most important among metadata; for sound-effect acoustics, spectral flatness, brightness and temporal spread lead, while for music 'warmth' is in the top five.

限制Limitations

最可疑的是音效組的 eGeMAPS 與 MERT：評分任務在三個模型上全是 0.223 ± 0.000，下載任務全是 0.203 ± 0.000。三種完全不同的模型拿到一模一樣、零變異的分數，最可能的解釋是輸入特徵是常數或缺值、模型只輸出單一類別，而不是作者說的「短訊號擷取不到有意義的特徵」。同一套 eGeMAPS 在音樂下載任務上拿到 0.57–0.60，泛函統計本來也不需要長訊號。

可能的標籤洩漏。元資料把「ratings、comments」列為取對數的數值特徵，如果那是評分次數，評分目標的 0 類（沒有評分）就直接可讀；評論數與上傳年齡也跟下載數高度相關。論文沒有說明是否排除了這些欄位。

報告本身也有矛盾。音效評分那段寫「元資料再次給出最好結果 0.41」，緊接著又說聲學描述子 0.57「勝過元資料」。摘要說用交叉驗證，方法節卻是單一 80/20 切分，±95% 信賴區間的來源沒交代；F1 是 macro 還是 weighted 也沒寫。MERT 只取最後一層（MERT 原論文顯示中間層通常較好），而且沒有把元資料與聲學特徵合併的實驗，「互補」的說法因此沒有直接證據。

Most suspicious are eGeMAPS and MERT on sound effects: the rating task gives 0.223 ± 0.000 for all three models and the download task 0.203 ± 0.000. Three very different models producing identical, zero-variance scores most likely means the input features are constant or missing and the models output a single class, not, as the authors say, that short signals yield no meaningful features. The same eGeMAPS set scores 0.57–0.60 on music downloads, and functionals do not need long signals in the first place.

Possible label leakage. The metadata list 'ratings, comments' among log-transformed numeric features; if that is the number of ratings, class 0 of the rating target (no rating) is directly readable, and comment count and upload age are also strongly tied to downloads. The paper does not say whether such fields were excluded.

The report also contradicts itself. The sound-effect rating passage says metadata again give the best result at 0.41, then immediately says acoustic descriptors at 0.57 outperform metadata. The abstract claims cross-validation while the method section uses a single 80/20 split, and the source of the ±95% intervals is not given; whether F1 is macro or weighted is not stated either. MERT is taken from the last layer only (the MERT paper shows middle layers are usually better), and there is no experiment combining metadata with acoustic features, so the 'complementary' claim has no direct evidence.

討論Discussion

把可信的部分留下來，結論大概是：在 Freesound 上，一個檔案會不會被下載，主要取決於它被歸在哪個類別、用什麼授權、多長、品質規格如何，也就是「找不找得到、能不能用」。這跟作者的解釋一致：使用者靠元資料挑內容，評分才反映聽感。音樂下載 F1 0.735 對聲學描述子 0.657，音效 0.846 對 0.720，差距都在 8–13 分之間。

評分方向的結果比較有意思，但也最需要查證。音樂評分上平台描述子 0.640 高過元資料 0.510，表面上支持「聽感決定評分」；可是評分目標有一個「沒有評分」類，而描述子缺值補 0，如果描述子缺值跟檔案年代或上傳管道有關，它也可能在間接預測「有沒有人評過」。拆掉 0 類、只在有評分的檔案上重做，才能回答聲學特徵到底在預測什麼。

對 MIR 讀者，這篇的價值主要是提醒：熱門度預測很容易被平台結構與洩漏特徵主導，任何「聲學特徵有效」的宣稱都要先通過洩漏檢查與常數預測檢查。資料集若公開會有用，但論文沒有說明是否釋出。

Keeping only the credible parts, the conclusion is roughly this: on Freesound, whether a file gets downloaded depends mainly on its category, licence, length and technical specs, that is, on whether it can be found and used. This matches the authors' reading that users pick content by metadata while ratings reflect listening. Music downloads score 0.735 F1 with metadata versus 0.657 with acoustic descriptors, sound effects 0.846 versus 0.720, a gap of 8–13 points in both.

The rating results are more interesting but also need the most checking. For music rating, platform descriptors at 0.640 beat metadata at 0.510, which on the surface supports 'listening decides rating'; but the rating target has a no-rating class and descriptors fill missing values with 0, so if missing descriptors correlate with file age or upload route, they may be indirectly predicting whether anyone rated the file. Dropping class 0 and redoing the task on rated files only would show what the acoustic features actually predict.

For MIR readers, the paper's main value is as a reminder: popularity prediction is easily dominated by platform structure and leaky features, and any claim that acoustic features work should first pass a leakage check and a constant-prediction check. The dataset would be useful if released, but the paper does not say whether it is.
