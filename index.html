VioTech: Frame-Level Violin Playing Technique Detection with Multi-Scale Mixture-of-Experts

VioTech：不切音符、直接逐幀偵測小提琴演奏技巧，用八個時間尺度的專家混合處理撥弦、跳弓、泛音與換弓

Ting-Kang Wang, Vincent K.M. Cheung, Nan-Tien Lai, Li Su
arXiv:2611.02680 · cs.SD; cs.AI; cs.LG; cs.MM; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-02

為什麼挑這篇：VioTech 不先切音符，直接以 10 毫秒解析度逐幀判斷小提琴技巧：凍結的 MERT 編碼器接上 8 個時間視窗從 20 毫秒到 2560 毫秒的卷積專家，再由音色、運弓、換弓三個任務各自的閘門加權。跨資料集測在 RWC 上，五類共同技巧的宏平均準確率是 90.49%，前作 VioPTT 是 80.04%、MERTech 是 52.27%；拿掉專家混合後，撥弦（pizzicato）F1 從 94.00% 掉到 57.46%。選它是因為消融裡有一個沒被藏起來的反例：換弓偵測在沒有專家的版本是 64.09%，加上專家反而降到 40.02%。另外附上 120 首人工標註的真實獨奏資料集 VioTech120 與標註介面。Why this one: VioTech skips note segmentation and labels violin techniques frame by frame at 10 ms: a frozen MERT encoder feeds eight convolutional experts with windows from 20 ms to 2,560 ms, and separate gates for the tonal, articulation and legato tasks weight them. Tested cross-dataset on RWC, its macro accuracy over five shared technique classes is 90.49%, against 80.04% for the earlier VioPTT and 52.27% for MERTech; removing the mixture of experts drops pizzicato F1 from 94.00% to 57.46%. I picked it because the ablation contains a counterexample the authors did not hide: bow-change detection is 64.09% without experts and falls to 40.02% with them. It also ships VioTech120, 120 manually annotated real solo recordings, plus the annotation interface.

動機Motivation

小提琴的演奏技巧，例如撥弦（pizzicato）、跳弓（spiccato）、泛音（flageolet）、連弓（legato），是表情的核心，但能自動辨識它們的模型很少。既有做法大多走「音符層級」流程：先估出每個音符的起訖，再對每個音符分類技巧。這對鋼琴還算可行，對弓弦樂器卻很脆弱：弓擦弦的起音很軟，連弓時弓一直貼著弦，音符邊界本身就估不準，後面的分類跟著出錯。

逐幀預測可以繞開切分，卻會碰上另一個問題：不同技巧的時間尺度差很多。撥弦只是一個短暫的起音瞬態，跳弓取決於運弓的包絡，換弓則要跨音符的長脈絡才看得出來。單一時間視窗顧不到全部，這是作者引入多時間尺度專家的理由。

Violin techniques such as pizzicato, spiccato, flageolet (harmonics) and legato are central to expressive playing, yet few models detect them automatically. Most existing systems use a note-level pipeline: estimate each note's onset and offset, then classify a technique per note. That is workable for piano but fragile for bowed strings, where onsets are soft and the bow stays on the string through legato, so the note boundaries are themselves unreliable and the classifier inherits the errors.

Frame-level prediction sidesteps segmentation but runs into a different problem: techniques live on very different time scales. Pizzicato is a short attack transient, spiccato depends on the bowing envelope, and a bow change only shows up with cross-note context. No single window covers all of them, which is why the authors introduce experts at multiple time scales.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

小提琴技巧辨識的早期工作把問題當成孤立音符或固定長度片段的分類。Su、Lin 與 Yang（2014）用幅度與相位衍生頻譜的稀疏編碼分類技巧，Kruger 與 Jacobs（2020）把傳統音訊特徵與 Hartley 轉換特徵交給 SVM。這些都假設輸入已經切好。最直接的前作是同一團隊的 VioPTT（ICASSP 2026）：在 Kong 等人的高解析度鋼琴轉譜架構上做技巧感知的音符轉譜，靠合成 MIDI 做資料增強，但仍依賴音符邊界。VioTech 沿用 VioPTT 的轉譜骨幹，差別在於技巧判斷改成逐幀，不再經過音符切分。

另一條線是 MERTech（Li 等，ICASSP 2024）：微調整個 MERT 來偵測演奏技巧，並以音高與起音偵測當輔助任務。VioTech 則把 MERT 凍結，只讓每個專家學自己的一組層權重。論文表上 MERTech 在 RWC 的宏平均準確率只有 52.27%，但沒有交代它是在同一份訓練資料上重訓，還是直接套用原本釋出的模型，這個差距因此不好解讀。

論文說專家混合在細粒度音樂分析中「用得很少」，引的是 Shazeer 的稀疏 MoE、Switch Transformer 與 GShard。但這裡的設計是一組共用的專家、每個任務一個 softmax 閘門、所有專家都參與加權，跟那幾個稀疏路由的大模型不太像，反而幾乎就是推薦系統裡的 MMoE（Multi-gate Mixture-of-Experts，Ma 等，KDD 2018），論文沒有提到它。專家本身是不同膨脹率的深度可分離卷積，概念上接近 WaveNet／TCN 的多尺度感受野。新的地方是把這兩個既有元件組合到小提琴技巧上，並讓每個專家的時間視窗固定、可以解讀。

Early violin-technique work treated the problem as classifying isolated notes or fixed-length clips. Su, Lin and Yang (2014) used sparse coding of magnitude and phase-derived spectra, and Kruger and Jacobs (2020) fed classic audio features plus Hartley-transform features to an SVM. All of these assume pre-segmented input. The most direct predecessor is the same group's VioPTT (ICASSP 2026), technique-aware note transcription built on Kong et al.'s high-resolution piano transcription architecture with synthetic MIDI augmentation, which still depends on note boundaries. VioTech keeps VioPTT's transcription backbone; the difference is that technique decisions are made per frame without going through note segmentation.

The other line is MERTech (Li et al., ICASSP 2024), which fine-tunes all of MERT for playing-technique detection with pitch and onset detection as auxiliary tasks. VioTech instead freezes MERT and lets each expert learn only its own set of layer weights. The paper's table gives MERTech a macro accuracy of just 52.27% on RWC, but it does not say whether MERTech was retrained on the same training data or used as originally released, so that gap is hard to interpret.

The paper says mixtures of experts have been "limited" in fine-grained music analysis, citing Shazeer's sparse MoE, Switch Transformer and GShard. But the design here, a shared pool of experts with one softmax gate per task and every expert contributing to the weighted sum, looks little like those sparsely routed large models and is almost exactly MMoE (Multi-gate Mixture-of-Experts, Ma et al., KDD 2018) from recommender systems, which the paper does not mention. The experts themselves are depthwise separable convolutions at different dilations, conceptually close to WaveNet/TCN multi-scale receptive fields. What is new is combining these two existing components for violin technique and fixing each expert's time window so it can be interpreted.

方法Method

系統分兩部分。轉譜模組沿用 Kong 等人的高解析度鋼琴轉譜架構（VioPTT 已改給小提琴用）：輸入 16 kHz 單聲道，轉成每秒 100 幀的 log-mel，三個平行分支分別輸出逐幀的音符活動、起音與收音，每支涵蓋 88 個 MIDI 音高。這三組輸出不是最終目標，而是當作「音符感知線索」交給後面的專家混合。

多尺度專家建在凍結的 MERT-v1-330M 上。每個專家先學一組 25 維的層權重，對 MERT 各層隱藏狀態做 softmax 加權和，投影到 128 維並對齊到 10 毫秒的幀；接著是核大小 k、膨脹 d 的深度可分離卷積，時間視窗為 k·d·10 毫秒。預設 8 個專家，核大小從 2 到 256 指數間隔，對應 20 毫秒到 2560 毫秒的視窗，最後各接一個雙向 GRU。

聚合階段，轉譜的三組輸出各投影成 16 維並拼成線索向量；每個專家的特徵與線索拼接後過兩層 MLP。三個任務各有一個閘門網路，依所有專家的閘門摘要與線索算出 softmax 權重，對專家特徵加權。輸出頭有三個：音色（正常／撥弦／泛音）、運弓（正常／跳弓）、換弓。換弓不當分類，而是回歸以換弓點為中心的高斯峰。

資料方面，VioTech120 收錄 120 首獨奏練習曲：帕格尼尼 24 首隨想曲、Wohlfahrt 60 首與 Kayser 36 首練習曲，音訊與對齊的 MIDI 取自 ViolinMIDIDataset，由兩位學琴十年以上的半職業小提琴手逐音符標註、互相審閱。訓練時以 8:2 混入合成資料集 MOSA-VPT（約 76 小時，只有檔案層級標籤）。跨資料集測試用 RWC 的小提琴錄音，依前作先把音階切成單音再評估。

結果：在 RWC 的五類共同技巧上，VioTech 宏平均準確率 90.49%，VioPTT 80.04%、MERTech 52.27%，跳弓準確率 97.16%。納入換弓的 F1 評估中，完整模型宏平均 76.90%；拿掉 MERT 層權重 76.86%、拿掉轉譜線索 76.19%、拿掉專家混合 70.60%。專家混合讓撥弦 F1 從 57.46% 升到 94.00%、跳弓從 59.78% 升到 90.50%，但換弓從 64.09% 降到 40.02%。

音符轉譜不是主要目標，但也有提升：Bach10 的 F1 是 62.8（VioPTT 50.7、無專家版本 56.1），URMP 是 51.9（VioPTT 44.6、無專家版本 46.3）。閘門分析顯示音色類技巧偏好 160 毫秒以下的專家，運弓與換弓偏好長視窗；換弓約 70% 的權重落在 320 毫秒以上的專家。

The system has two parts. The transcription module reuses Kong et al.'s high-resolution piano transcription architecture (already adapted to violin in VioPTT): 16 kHz mono input becomes a log-mel spectrogram at 100 frames per second, and three parallel branches output frame-wise note activity, onsets and offsets, each over 88 MIDI pitches. These outputs are not the end goal; they are passed on as "note-aware cues" to the mixture of experts.

The multi-scale experts sit on a frozen MERT-v1-330M. Each expert first learns a 25-dimensional set of layer weights, takes a softmax-weighted sum of MERT's hidden states, projects it to 128 dimensions and aligns it to 10 ms frames; then comes a depthwise separable convolution with kernel size k and dilation d, giving a window of k·d·10 ms. The default is eight experts with kernel sizes spaced exponentially from 2 to 256, i.e. windows from 20 ms to 2,560 ms, each followed by a bidirectional GRU.

For aggregation, the three transcription outputs are each projected to 16 dimensions and concatenated into a cue vector; each expert's features are concatenated with the cues and passed through a two-layer MLP. Each of the three tasks has its own gating network that computes softmax weights from all experts' gate summaries plus the cues and mixes the expert features. There are three heads: tonal (normal / pizzicato / flageolet), articulation (normal / spiccato) and legato. Legato is not a classification but a regression onto Gaussian peaks centred on bow-change points.

For data, VioTech120 holds 120 solo études: Paganini's 24 Caprices plus 60 Wohlfahrt and 36 Kayser studies, with audio and aligned MIDI taken from the ViolinMIDIDataset, labelled note by note by two semi-professional violinists with over ten years of training and peer-reviewed between them. Training mixes in the synthetic MOSA-VPT dataset (about 76 hours, file-level labels only) at 8:2. The cross-dataset test uses RWC violin recordings, with scales first cut into single notes as in prior work.

Results: on RWC's five shared technique classes VioTech reaches 90.49% macro accuracy, against 80.04% for VioPTT and 52.27% for MERTech, with 97.16% on spiccato. In the F1 evaluation that includes legato, the full model's macro F1 is 76.90%; without MERT layer weights it is 76.86%, without transcription cues 76.19%, without the mixture of experts 70.60%. The experts raise pizzicato F1 from 57.46% to 94.00% and spiccato from 59.78% to 90.50%, but bow change drops from 64.09% to 40.02%.

Note transcription is not the main goal but also improves: F1 is 62.8 on Bach10 (VioPTT 50.7, no-expert variant 56.1) and 51.9 on URMP (VioPTT 44.6, no-expert variant 46.3). Gate analysis shows tonal techniques favouring experts under 160 ms while articulation and legato favour longer windows; about 70% of legato's weight goes to experts above 320 ms.

限制Limitations

最明顯的問題是換弓。拿掉專家混合的版本在換弓（LB）上是 64.09%，完整版只有 40.02%，少了 24 個百分點。作者的解釋是路由偏向短視窗、壓低了稀疏的長程事件；可是閘門分析又說換弓約 70% 權重落在 320 毫秒以上的專家，兩個說法之間有張力，論文沒有處理。宏平均 F1 的領先（76.90 對 70.60）主要由撥弦與跳弓撐起，換弓這一項是退步的。

其他兩個元件的貢獻幾乎看不出來。拿掉 MERT 層權重後宏平均 F1 是 76.86，與完整版的 76.90 只差 0.04；在準確率表上，這個版本的正常音色（86.11 對 79.07）與撥弦（93.68 對 89.60）甚至更高。拿掉轉譜線索後 F1 是 76.19，只少 0.71。主表沒有標準差，只有專家數量實驗做了三次重複，所以這些差距是否超出雜訊無從判斷。

評估設定也削弱了主張。RWC 測試把音階預先切成單音，因此這個「跨資料集逐幀」結果其實是在孤立音上測的，恰好避開了論文動機裡最難的連續運弓情境。泛音的 F1 只有 30.10%。表 1 中 VioPTT 與 MERTech 的 TN 與 AN 兩欄數字完全相同（77.22／77.22、53.36／53.36），看起來像是抄錯欄位。

VioTech120 只收練習曲，只有兩位標註者，沒有報告標註者間一致性；它的測試集結果只畫在圖上，沒有表格數字可以核對。

The clearest problem is bow change. The variant without the mixture of experts scores 64.09% on bow change (LB), the full model only 40.02%, a loss of 24 points. The authors attribute it to routing favouring short windows and under-weighting sparse long-range events; yet their gate analysis says about 70% of legato's weight goes to experts above 320 ms. The two statements sit in tension and the paper does not address it. The macro-F1 lead (76.90 vs 70.60) is carried mainly by pizzicato and spiccato, while bow change regresses.

The other two components barely register. Without MERT layer weights the macro F1 is 76.86, just 0.04 below the full model's 76.90; in the accuracy table that variant is even higher on tonal normal (86.11 vs 79.07) and pizzicato (93.68 vs 89.60). Without transcription cues F1 is 76.19, only 0.71 lower. The main tables have no standard deviations, and only the expert-count experiment was repeated three times, so there is no way to tell whether these gaps exceed noise.

The evaluation setup also weakens the claim. The RWC test cuts scales into single notes beforehand, so this "cross-dataset frame-level" result is actually measured on isolated notes, avoiding exactly the continuous-bowing case that motivates the paper. Flageolet F1 is only 30.10%. In Table 1, VioPTT and MERTech have identical numbers in the TN and AN columns (77.22/77.22 and 53.36/53.36), which looks like a column copied by mistake.

VioTech120 contains only études and two annotators, with no inter-annotator agreement reported; its test-set results appear only in figures, with no table values to check.

討論Discussion

這篇最有用的發現是專家混合的效益集中在稀有、瞬態的技巧：撥弦與跳弓的 F1 各提升 30 多個百分點，而正常、持續等常見類別本來就接近天花板，幾乎沒有變化。作者的專家數量實驗也顯示最佳專家數因技巧與資料集而異：泛音在 VioTech120 上是 2 或 4 個專家最好，在 RWC 上卻是 8 個。所以「8 個專家」是一個沒有普適性的超參數，換到別的樂器需要重新調。

閘門分佈給了一個可讀的圖像：音色類技巧偏好 160 毫秒以下的短視窗，運弓與換弓偏好長視窗，撥弦在 320 毫秒處有額外權重；層權重則顯示音色類偏好 MERT 的中低層。這比一般黑盒分類器多給了一點可解讀性。但要注意，拿掉層權重幾乎不影響分數，所以「不同技巧用不同層」這個解讀未必對預測有實質作用。

對想延伸的人，最直接的方向是補上長程換弓：例如保留一條不參與閘門競爭的共享長脈絡路徑，因為無專家版本在換弓上反而最好。公開的資料集與標註介面，可能比模型本身更容易被沿用，尤其是它能同時標指法與把位，替以後的小提琴轉譜研究留了擴充空間。

The most useful finding is that the benefit of the experts concentrates on rare, transient techniques: pizzicato and spiccato F1 each rise by more than 30 points, while common classes such as normal and sustained are near ceiling already and barely move. The authors' expert-count experiment also shows the best number of experts varies by technique and dataset: flageolet peaks with two or four experts on VioTech120 but with eight on RWC. So "eight experts" is not a universal setting and would need retuning for another instrument.

The gate distributions give a readable picture: tonal techniques favour windows under 160 ms, articulation and legato favour long windows, and pizzicato picks up extra weight at 320 ms; the layer weights show tonal classes favouring lower-to-middle MERT layers. That is a bit more interpretability than a typical black-box classifier. But since removing the layer weights barely changes the scores, the reading that "different techniques use different layers" may have little practical effect on predictions.

For anyone extending this, the obvious next step is fixing long-range bow change, for instance by keeping a shared long-context path that does not compete in the gating, since the no-expert variant is actually best at bow change. The released dataset and annotation interface may get more reuse than the model itself, especially because the interface can also record fingering and position, which leaves room for future violin transcription work.

MEX: The Musical Expectation Benchmark Suite

MEX：音樂預期模型的基準套件——用重複、段落邊界、與 IDyOM 的一致性、腦電編碼四項任務，檢驗從音訊估出的驚奇度與不確定性

Mathias Rose Bjare, Augustin Bouquillard, Giorgia Cantisani, Seung-Goo Kim, Stefan Lattner, Gerhard Widmer
arXiv:2611.01843 · cs.SD; cs.AI; eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-11-01

為什麼挑這篇：MEX 把音樂預期模型的感知驗證收成四項標準任務：重複出現時驚奇度是否下降、峰值是否對齊段落邊界（兩項都用 SALAMI）、從音訊估出的音高驚奇度是否與符號模型 IDyOM 一致（200 首愛爾蘭民謠 × 5 種音色，共 2000 例）、以及能否預測聽 18 首人聲旋律時的腦電。基線是作者自己的兩個音訊模型 GIVT 與 DIF：與 IDyOM 的 Spearman 相關落在 0.37 到 0.62；腦電任務中表現最好的 DIF（雜訊層級 0.5）比 GIVT 多出的預測增益 Δz 只有 0.0025。選它是因為它把 MIR 與認知科學各自為政的評估方式放到同一把尺上；但目前只有作者自家兩個模型的結果，也沒有不靠預測模型的聲學基線可以對照。Why this one: MEX packages perceptual validation of musical-expectation models into four standard tasks: whether surprisal drops on repetition and whether its peaks align with segment boundaries (both on SALAMI), whether pitch surprisal estimated from audio agrees with the symbolic model IDyOM (200 Irish folk tunes × 5 timbres, 2,000 examples), and whether it predicts EEG recorded while listening to 18 vocal melodies. The baselines are the authors' own two audio models, GIVT and DIF: Spearman correlations with IDyOM range from 0.37 to 0.62, and on the EEG task the best model, DIF at noise level 0.5, gains only Δz = 0.0025 over GIVT. I picked it because it puts the separate evaluation habits of MIR and cognitive science on one yardstick; but so far it reports only the authors' two models, with no acoustic baseline that bypasses a predictive model.

動機Motivation

音樂預期被認為是聆聽經驗的核心：緊張與解決、記憶、情緒與報償性的愉悅都跟它有關。資訊理論的取向把預期形式化為「音樂資訊動態」（music information dynamics, MID）：從統計模型對後續的分佈算出驚奇度（surprisal，也就是資訊量 IC，負對數似然）與熵（不確定性）。

問題在於兩個社群各自評估。MIR 端的序列模型通常只看保留資料上的預測表現，這說明不了模型的預期是否像人；認知科學端則常常只用某一個模型、在某一份資料上算出驚奇度，就拿去解釋腦與行為，若那個模型校準不佳，推論可能出錯。作者想要的是一個像 MIREX 那樣的共同基準，讓不同模型能在同一組人類資料上比較。

Musical expectation is thought to be central to listening: tension and release, memory, emotion and reward-related pleasure are all tied to it. The information-theoretic approach formalises expectation as music information dynamics (MID): from a statistical model's distribution over continuations one computes surprisal (information content, IC, the negative log-likelihood) and entropy (uncertainty).

The trouble is that the two communities evaluate separately. On the MIR side, sequence models are usually judged only on held-out predictive performance, which says nothing about whether their expectations resemble a listener's. On the cognitive side, studies often compute surprisal from a single model on a single dataset and use it to explain brain and behaviour; if that model is poorly calibrated, the inferences may be wrong. The authors want a shared benchmark in the spirit of MIREX, so different models can be compared on the same human data.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

符號領域的標準是 IDyOM（Pearce，2005，承接 Conklin 與 Witten 1995 的多重視角系統）：用變長 n-gram 學語料統計，只能處理單聲部序列。Di Liberto 等（eLife，2020）證明 IDyOM 的旋律驚奇度在腦電與皮層電圖中有編碼；Kern 等（eLife，2022）改用 Music Transformer 的驚奇度預測 MEG；PolyRNN（Robert 等，2024）把模型推到多聲部。音訊領域則是作者團隊自己的三篇：GIVT（ICASSP 2025）、在擴散模型雜訊空間估驚奇度的 DIF（ISMIR 2025），以及雜訊增強自編碼器（NeurIPS 2025 研討會）。

MEX 的四項任務幾乎都直接來自這三篇：RE 與 NE 出自 GIVT 那篇，SB 與 SY 出自 ISMIR 2025 那篇。所以 MEX 的實質貢獻是把作者既有的評估流程標準化、公開資料與程式，而不是新的評估概念。

最接近的類比其實不在 MIR，而是視覺神經科學的 Brain-Score（Schrimpf 等，2018）：同樣用神經資料替模型打分、同樣主張基準要隨對腦的理解演進。在音訊表徵上，HEAR（2021）與 MARBLE（2023）採取「模型交出嵌入、基準負責下游任務」的介面；MEX 的介面更簡單，只要求一條固定幀率的一維時間序列。這個設計其實讓非模型的聲學特徵（例如頻譜通量）也能直接跑同一套任務，但論文沒有拿來當對照。SB 任務本身則是 Foote 式的新穎度分段，只是把新穎度曲線換成驚奇度。

The symbolic standard is IDyOM (Pearce, 2005, building on Conklin and Witten's 1995 multiple-viewpoint systems), which learns corpus statistics with variable-order n-grams and handles only monophonic sequences. Di Liberto et al. (eLife, 2020) showed IDyOM's melodic surprisal is encoded in EEG and electrocorticography; Kern et al. (eLife, 2022) used Music Transformer surprisal to predict MEG; PolyRNN (Robert et al., 2024) extended modelling to polyphony. In audio, the work is the authors' own three papers: GIVT (ICASSP 2025), DIF, which estimates surprisal in a diffusion model's noise space (ISMIR 2025), and noise-augmented autoencoders (NeurIPS 2025 workshop).

Nearly all four MEX tasks come straight from those papers: RE and NE from the GIVT paper, SB and SY from the ISMIR 2025 paper. So MEX's real contribution is standardising the authors' existing evaluation protocols and releasing data and code, not new evaluation concepts.

The closest analogue is not in MIR but in visual neuroscience: Brain-Score (Schrimpf et al., 2018), which likewise scores models against neural data and likewise argues benchmarks should evolve with our understanding of the brain. For audio representations, HEAR (2021) and MARBLE (2023) use a "model hands over embeddings, benchmark runs downstream tasks" interface; MEX's interface is simpler still, asking only for a one-dimensional time series at a constant frame rate. That design would let non-model acoustic features such as spectral flux run the same tasks directly, but the paper does not use them as controls. The SB task itself is Foote-style novelty segmentation with surprisal in place of the novelty curve.

方法Method

每個基準由一項任務與一份資料集組成，輸入是一條從音樂算出的固定幀率實數時間序列，任務負責把它與標註或腦電配對、彙整成一個分數。所有任務都以零樣本方式執行，不做任務特定的微調。

基線模型是 GIVT 與 DIF，兩者都是自迴歸 Transformer，在 Music2Latent 自編碼器的潛在表徵上（每秒 11 幀）估計下一幀的密度。IC 是負對數似然；熵用「路徑條件熵」（pathwise conditional entropy），以 32 個蒙地卡羅樣本估計，避開多步取樣的誤差累積。DIF 可以在不同雜訊層級 l 下估計，l=0 保留完整聲學細節，l 越大越偏向抽象特徵，論文報告 l=0、0.3、0.5。

RE（重複）：在 SALAMI 的 1,310 首錄音上，把「某段與它之前最近一個同標籤段」定義為一次重複，計算兩段標準化後的平均 MID 差，用單尾 t 檢定看平均是否為負；ALL 納入所有重複，FI 只看第一次重複。

SB（段落邊界）：把 MID 曲線當新穎度函數，用 σ 為 1 到 7 幀的高斯核平滑後取差分，再以 Röder 峰值挑選，在 ±1 秒容忍度內預測 SALAMI 三個層級（Lowercase、Uppercase、Function）的邊界；各層級的峰值參數以五折交叉驗證選 F1 最佳值。

SY（與 IDyOM 一致性）：從 The Session 取 200 首愛爾蘭民謠，以五種 SoundFont 音色（Pad、合成人聲、尼龍吉他、平台鋼琴、小號）固定力度合成，共 2,000 例；把音符起音前後兩幀的 MID 平均，與 IDyOM 的 IC／熵算 Spearman 相關。

NE（腦電編碼）：用聽 18 首 NHSS 人聲旋律的腦電資料，以多變量時間響應函數（mTRF）的脊迴歸、−100 到 700 毫秒的延遲，比較「IC＋熵＋聲學包絡」的完整模型與「只有包絡」的縮減模型，以 Fisher z 轉換後的相關差 Δz 衡量增益，逐受試者留一試次交叉驗證，並以 FDR 校正多重比較。

結果：RE 的所有差值皆為負且顯著，IC 從 −0.004（DIF l=0.5、Function、ALL）到 −0.111（DIF l=0.5、Lowercase、FI），熵最大降幅是 −0.134（同一設定）；FI 的降幅普遍大於 ALL。SB 以 Lowercase 對齊最好、GIVT 最好，DIF 雜訊越高對齊越差，精確率普遍偏低。SY 的相關介於 0.373 與 0.617，DIF 高於 GIVT（合併音色的 IC：DIF l=0.3 為 0.523，GIVT 為 0.390），鋼琴音色最不一致。

NE 中，GIVT 的 IC 增益 p=0.0159、熵 p=0.4347；DIF l=0.3 的 IC p=0.0006、熵 p=0.0057；DIF l=0.5 的 IC p<0.0001、熵 p=0.0168。經 Bonferroni 校正（α=0.00625）後，只剩 DIF l=0.3 的 IC 與熵、以及 DIF l=0.5 的 IC 仍顯著。模型間比較，DIF l=0.5 的 IC 比 GIVT 多 Δz=0.0025（p<0.001）。

Each benchmark is a task plus a dataset. The input is a real-valued time series at a constant frame rate computed from the music, and the task pairs it with annotations or EEG and aggregates a score. Every task runs zero-shot, with no task-specific fine-tuning.

The baselines are GIVT and DIF, both autoregressive Transformers that estimate next-frame densities over Music2Latent autoencoder latents at 11 frames per second. IC is the negative log-likelihood; entropy is the pathwise conditional entropy, estimated from 32 Monte Carlo samples to avoid error accumulating over multi-step sampling. DIF can estimate at different noise levels l, where l=0 keeps full acoustic detail and larger l favours more abstract features; the paper reports l=0, 0.3 and 0.5.

RE (repetition): on SALAMI's 1,310 recordings, a repetition is a segment paired with the most recent earlier segment carrying the same label. The task computes the difference in standardised mean MID between the two and uses a one-tailed t-test on whether the mean is negative; ALL includes every repetition, FI only the first.

SB (segment boundaries): the MID curve is treated as a novelty function, smoothed with Gaussian kernels of σ = 1 to 7 frames and differenced, then Röder peak picking predicts SALAMI boundaries at three levels (Lowercase, Uppercase, Function) within ±1 s; peak-picking parameters per level are chosen for best F1 by five-fold cross-validation.

SY (IDyOM consistency): 200 Irish folk tunes from The Session are synthesised at constant velocity with five SoundFont timbres (pad, synth voice, nylon guitar, grand piano, trumpet), giving 2,000 examples; MID averaged over the two frames around each note onset is correlated with IDyOM's IC and entropy using Spearman's rank correlation.

NE (neural encoding): using EEG recorded while listening to 18 NHSS vocal melodies, multivariate temporal response function (mTRF) ridge regression with lags from −100 to 700 ms compares a full model (IC + entropy + acoustic envelope) with a reduced envelope-only model. The gain is Δz, the difference in Fisher-z-transformed correlations, with leave-one-trial-out cross-validation per participant and FDR correction for multiple comparisons.

Results: every RE difference is negative and significant, IC ranging from −0.004 (DIF l=0.5, Function, ALL) to −0.111 (DIF l=0.5, Lowercase, FI), with the largest entropy drop −0.134 in the same setting; FI drops are generally larger than ALL. SB aligns best at the Lowercase level and best for GIVT, with DIF alignment worsening as noise rises and precision generally low. SY correlations lie between 0.373 and 0.617, DIF above GIVT (combined-timbre IC: 0.523 for DIF l=0.3, 0.390 for GIVT), with piano the least consistent timbre.

In NE, GIVT's IC gain has p=0.0159 and entropy p=0.4347; DIF l=0.3 has IC p=0.0006 and entropy p=0.0057; DIF l=0.5 has IC p<0.0001 and entropy p=0.0168. After Bonferroni correction (α=0.00625), only DIF l=0.3 IC and entropy and DIF l=0.5 IC remain significant. Between models, DIF l=0.5 IC beats GIVT by Δz=0.0025 (p<0.001).

限制Limitations

作為基準，最大的缺口是比較對象。所有基線都是作者自己的模型，而且 GIVT 與 DIF 共用同一個 Music2Latent 表徵、同樣每秒 11 幀；任務也大多出自他們先前的論文。目前的結果等於「作者的模型在作者的任務上」，還看不出基準能不能區分本質不同的方法。

更關鍵的是缺少非預測性的聲學對照。既然介面只要一條時間序列，頻譜通量、RMS 包絡或自相似矩陣的新穎度曲線都能直接跑 RE 與 SB；沒有這些對照，就無法判斷「重複時驚奇度下降」與「峰值對齊邊界」到底是預期模型的貢獻，還是任何會隨聲學變化起伏的特徵都做得到。NE 有放包絡當控制，RE 與 SB 卻沒有。

效應量普遍很小。RE 的最大降幅是 0.134 個標準差；NE 中模型之間的差異是 Δz=0.0025 這種量級，八項增益檢定在 Bonferroni 校正後只剩三項顯著，作者自己也說模型比較中「只有少數」通過校正。SB 只以圖呈現，沒有可以核對的精確率與召回率數字。

資料面也有限制。SY 用的是固定力度合成的單聲部民謠，這是音訊與符號最容易對上的情境，而且它驗證的是與 IDyOM 一致，不是與人一致。NE 只有 18 首人聲旋律，論文沒有寫出受試者人數。SALAMI 的音訊不是開放取得，作者承認這是可及性障礙。

As a benchmark, the biggest gap is what gets compared. Every baseline is the authors' own model, and GIVT and DIF share the same Music2Latent representation at the same 11 frames per second; most of the tasks also come from their earlier papers. The current results amount to "the authors' models on the authors' tasks" and do not yet show whether the benchmark separates fundamentally different methods.

More important is the absence of non-predictive acoustic controls. Since the interface takes any time series, spectral flux, an RMS envelope or a self-similarity novelty curve could run RE and SB directly. Without them there is no telling whether "surprisal drops on repetition" and "peaks align with boundaries" reflect the expectation model or would be achieved by any feature that tracks acoustic change. NE includes the envelope as a control; RE and SB do not.

Effect sizes are generally small. The largest RE drop is 0.134 standard deviations; in NE, differences between models are on the order of Δz=0.0025, and only three of the eight gain tests survive Bonferroni correction, with the authors themselves noting that "only a few" model comparisons survive. SB is shown only as a figure, with no precision or recall values to check.

The data are limited too. SY uses constant-velocity synthesised monophonic folk tunes, the easiest case for matching audio to symbols, and it validates agreement with IDyOM, not with listeners. NE has only 18 vocal melodies, and the paper does not state the number of participants. SALAMI audio is not openly available, which the authors acknowledge as an accessibility obstacle.

討論Discussion

即使只有作者自己的模型，基線結果已經透露一個有意思的取捨：把 DIF 的雜訊層級從 0 調高，與 IDyOM 的 IC 一致性在 l=0.3 最高（合併音色依序為 0.489、0.523、0.490），腦電 IC 增益在 l=0.5 最強，但段落邊界對齊卻隨雜訊升高而變差。換句話說，適度抹掉聲學細節、偏向抽象表徵，讓模型更像符號模型與大腦，卻讓它對表面聲學變化造成的邊界變鈍。這指出「好的預期模型」可能不是單一軸，四項任務各自在量不同的東西，作者在結語提到的任務本體論確實有必要。

RE 中 FI 降幅普遍大於 ALL，表示驚奇度的下降集中在第一次重複，後續重複不再單調遞減。這跟人類對重複的適應與「熟悉化後再度變得可預期」的直覺一致，也是比單看平均似然更細緻的觀察。

要讓 MEX 成為真正的共同基準，最值得補的是三種對照列：純聲學新穎度特徵、經自動轉譜後的 IDyOM、以及符號領域的 Music Transformer。作者規劃加入旋律填空（cloze）作業與連續的緊張度評分，並納入 MTG-Jamendo 等開放資料；若能做到，它會是 MIR 少數直接以人類感知資料為評分依據的基準。

Even with only the authors' models, the baselines reveal an interesting trade-off: raising DIF's noise level from 0, IC agreement with IDyOM peaks at l=0.3 (combined timbres 0.489, 0.523, 0.490 in turn) and the EEG IC gain is strongest at l=0.5, yet segment-boundary alignment worsens as noise rises. Moderately wiping out acoustic detail in favour of abstract representations makes the model more like the symbolic model and the brain, but blunts its response to boundaries caused by surface acoustic change. That suggests a "good expectation model" may not lie on a single axis, since the four tasks measure different things, and the task ontology the authors mention in closing is genuinely needed.

In RE, FI drops are generally larger than ALL, meaning the decrease in surprisal concentrates on the first repetition and does not keep falling monotonically afterwards. That fits intuitions about how listeners adapt to repetition and is a finer-grained observation than average likelihood alone.

To make MEX a genuine shared benchmark, the most valuable additions would be three kinds of control rows: purely acoustic novelty features, IDyOM run on automatic transcriptions, and the symbolic-domain Music Transformer. The authors plan to add melodic cloze tasks and continuous tension ratings and to include open datasets such as MTG-Jamendo; if they do, it would be one of the few MIR benchmarks scored directly against human perceptual data.

動機Motivation

文字生成音樂（TTM）的研究大多集中在模型架構、音訊表徵、訓練規模與取樣演算法，訓練配對中的文字本身反而少有人系統研究。作者列出一串沒被分開回答過的問題：訓練描述該含多少音樂資訊（全部還是部分）、該用什麼格式（自由文字還是明確結構）、該是全曲一份還是隨時間變化、推論時要不要改寫提示，以及要怎麼評估文字對齊。

影像生成已經給了線索：DALL·E 3 用詳細的合成描述重新標註訓練資料而提升品質，PixArt-α 強調高資訊密度描述的好處。另一方面，目前常用的 CLAP 分數只給一個整體相似度，看不出是哪個屬性沒對上，於是作者同時想要一個能逐屬性診斷的評估方式。

Text-to-music (TTM) research has mostly focused on architecture, audio representation, training scale and sampling, while the text in the training pairs has seen little systematic study. The authors list questions that have not been answered separately: how much musical information training captions should contain (all or partial), what format they should take (free-form or explicit structure), whether they should be one per track or vary over time, whether prompts should be rewritten at inference, and how to evaluate text alignment.

Image generation already offers hints: DALL·E 3 improved quality by recaptioning training data with detailed synthetic descriptions, and PixArt-α stresses the value of dense, high-information captions. Meanwhile the commonly used CLAP score gives only an overall similarity and cannot show which attribute failed, so the authors also want an evaluation that diagnoses attribute by attribute.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

在 TTM 裡豐富文字條件並不是新想法。Mustango（2024）把和弦、拍點與調性加進音樂描述；FUTGA（2024）替整首歌合成分段、帶時間結構的描述；Music Metadata LLMs（論文引用 [5]）把音樂表示成結構化 metadata、轉成自由描述並補全缺漏欄位，這是與 SCAP 最接近的前作，SCAP 的欄位本質上就是 metadata。這篇的差別在於不提新格式，而是把結構、欄位覆蓋率與時間粒度拆成三個獨立變因做受控比較。時間變化的條件也讓人想到同為 Adobe Research 的 Music ControlNet（Wu、Donahue、Watanabe、Bryan，2024），它以旋律、力度、節奏曲線提供隨時間變化的控制；TSCAP 等於是文字版的同一件事，但論文沒有引用它。

推論端的提示改寫也有前例：ACE-Step 1.5 把短查詢擴寫成含 metadata、歌詞與長描述的歌曲藍圖，Qwen-Music 把自由提示改寫成結構化標籤與歌詞。這篇的差別是把「忠實抽取使用者明說的內容」與「補上沒說的欄位」拆成兩步，避免補全時蓋掉使用者的意圖。

評估端，Music DSG 是把影像領域的 TIFA（Hu 等，2023）與 DSG（Davidsonian Scene Graph，Cho 等，2024）的問答式評估移植到音樂。音訊上已有 AQAScore 與 AnyAudio-Judge，後者同樣把指令拆成可獨立驗證的二元評分項，而且涵蓋音樂。Music DSG 與 AnyAudio-Judge 的實際差別主要是三點：沿用 DSG 的題目依賴關係、調性／BPM／拍號改用專門偵測器、以及以信心分數加權彙整。

Enriching text conditioning in TTM is not a new idea. Mustango (2024) adds chords, beats and key to music descriptions; FUTGA (2024) synthesises segment-level, temporally structured descriptions for full songs; Music Metadata LLMs (the paper's reference [5]) represent music as structured metadata, turn it into free-form captions and impute missing fields. That is the closest prior work to SCAPs, whose fields are essentially metadata. This paper's difference is that it proposes no new format but separates structure, field coverage and temporal granularity into three independent variables for a controlled comparison. Time-varying conditioning also recalls Music ControlNet (Wu, Donahue, Watanabe and Bryan, 2024), also from Adobe Research, which provides time-varying control through melody, dynamics and rhythm curves; TSCAPs are the text version of the same idea, but the paper does not cite it.

Inference-time prompt rewriting has precedents too: ACE-Step 1.5 expands short queries into song blueprints with metadata, lyrics and long captions, and Qwen-Music rewrites free-form prompts into structured tags and lyrics. The difference here is splitting "faithfully extract what the user stated" from "fill in the unstated fields" into two steps, so completion does not overwrite the user's intent.

On evaluation, Music DSG ports the question-answering evaluation of image-domain TIFA (Hu et al., 2023) and DSG (Davidsonian Scene Graph, Cho et al., 2024) to music. Audio already has AQAScore and AnyAudio-Judge, the latter likewise splitting instructions into independently verifiable binary rubric items and covering music. The practical differences between Music DSG and AnyAudio-Judge come down to three things: DSG's dependencies between questions, dedicated detectors for key, BPM and time signature, and confidence-weighted aggregation.

方法Method

SCAP 有一組固定欄位：類型、情緒、能量、調性、BPM、拍號、樂器、人聲與關鍵詞，放不進其他欄位的資訊（演奏技巧、效果、時間順序、力度）都收進關鍵詞。全曲 SCAP（GSCAP）由內部音樂庫的曲目層級 metadata 建成，同一首歌的所有訓練片段共用；逐段 SCAP（TSCAP）則用在內部目標上微調過的 Qwen2.5-Omni，對每 30 秒、間隔 10 秒的重疊視窗產生偽標籤，這些目標結合了人工 metadata 與 MIR 自動推論的標籤。

生成器是一個預訓練的流匹配 DiT（Base），原本以 32 秒片段配自由描述訓練。作者保留架構、潛在表徵與目標函數，只把條件換成 SCAP。兩個軸交叉成四組：來源是全曲（G）或逐段（T），覆蓋率是全欄位（F）或訓練時隨機丟掉部分欄位（P），得到 FT、PT、FG、PG。每組都從 Base 起始，在 32 張 A100 上微調 15 萬步；TSCAP 版本把每個 32 秒片段配上時間重疊最多的視窗。

推論時的改寫器分兩步：Transcribe 只把提示中明說的資訊放進對應欄位，產生部分 SCAP；Infer 再把空著的欄位補上與使用者意圖相容的值。Transcribe 預設用 Qwen3.5-35B；Infer 比較了 Claude Opus 4.8、GPT-5.6 Sol、Qwen3.5 的 35B／112B／397B、以文字模式使用的 Qwen3-Omni-30B 與 FireRed Audio，另有從訓練資料隨機抽值的 Random，以及不補全的 Null。

Music DSG 分三步：由 Qwen3.5-35B-A3B 把描述拆成兩個層級、十一種類型的是非題（第一層是存在、類型、情緒、能量、BPM、調性、拍號、排除；第二層是屬性、數量、關係）；由 Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B 回答，調性改用 S-KEY 式偵測器，BPM 與拍號用 TCN 拍點追蹤加動態貝氏網路；最後依題型加權平均（存在、類型、情緒、BPM、排除為 1.5，調性、拍號、關係為 0.5）。父題答「否」時子題直接判否，信心分數也乘上父題平均信心。

驗證 Music DSG 用兩份資料：MusicEval（129 個提示、413 段生成音樂、專家對齊評分），以及內部的 200 個提示 × 3 個 TTM 模型共 600 段，由 128 位一般聽眾（每對約 5 票）與 13 位職業音樂人（400 對、每對約 3 票）做成對偏好。生成評估用 Song Describer 的純器樂子集（586 段、每個提示生成 4 次）與內部的 genre9k（18 種類型各 500 段）。

評估指標結果：Music DSG 信心版本與人類偏好的 Spearman 相關，在內部一般聽眾、內部職業音樂人與 MusicEval 上分別是 0.421、0.414、0.248；二元版本是 0.351、0.334、0.193；AnyAudio-Judge 是 0.414、0.385、0.225；CLAP 是 0.165、0.211、0.235，CLaMP 3 與 MuQ-MuLan 更低。

生成結果（Song Describer，Infer 用 Qwen3.5-35B）：Base 的 CLAP／DSG／FAD／MuQ 是 0.320／0.689／7.63／3.95。FG 為 0.344／0.738／6.68／3.99，平均相對提升 +7.0%；FT 的對齊最好（0.351／0.756），但 FAD 是 7.12；PG 的 FAD 最低（6.59）。不補全的 Null 只有 +2.0% 到 +3.1%，MuQ 還降到 3.71 至 3.82，低於 Base。

比較 Infer 模型（FG）：Qwen3.5-112B 與 GPT-5.6 Sol 都是 +7.5%，Qwen3.5-35B +7.0%，Claude Opus 4.8 +6.8%，Qwen3.5-397B +6.6%，兩個多模態模型 +6.4% 與 +5.8%。Random 的 CLAP 掉到 0.298、DSG 掉到 0.657，平均 −0.2%。

A SCAP has fixed fields: genre, mood, energy, key, BPM, time signature, instruments, vocals and keywords, with anything that fits nowhere else (playing techniques, effects, temporal order, dynamics) kept in keywords. The global SCAP (GSCAP) is built from track-level metadata in an internal music corpus and shared by all training segments of a track; the time-varying SCAP (TSCAP) comes from a Qwen2.5-Omni fine-tuned on in-house targets, applied to overlapping 30-second windows with a 10-second hop to produce pseudo-labels. Those targets combine human metadata with MIR-inferred labels.

The generator is a pretrained flow-matching DiT (Base), originally trained on 32-second segments with free-form captions. The authors keep its architecture, latent representation and objective and only swap the conditioning for SCAPs. Two axes cross into four variants: source is global (G) or temporal (T), coverage is full (F) or partial (P, randomly dropping fields during training), giving FT, PT, FG and PG. Each starts from Base and is fine-tuned for 150k steps on 32 A100s; TSCAP variants pair each 32-second crop with the window of greatest overlap.

The inference rewriter has two steps: Transcribe puts only what the prompt states explicitly into the matching fields, producing a partial SCAP; Infer then fills the empty fields with values compatible with the user's intent. Transcribe defaults to Qwen3.5-35B; Infer compares Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.6 Sol, Qwen3.5 at 35B/112B/397B, and Qwen3-Omni-30B and FireRed Audio in text-only mode, plus Random, which samples values from the training data, and Null, which leaves the SCAP partial.

Music DSG has three steps. Qwen3.5-35B-A3B decomposes a caption into yes/no questions of eleven types on two levels (level one: presence, genre, mood, energy, BPM, key, time signature, exclusion; level two: attribute, count, relation). Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B answers them, except that key uses an S-KEY-style detector and BPM and time signature use a TCN beat tracker with a dynamic Bayesian network. Scores are weighted by question type (1.5 for presence, genre, mood, BPM and exclusion; 0.5 for key, time signature and relation). A child question is set to "no" when a parent is "no", and its confidence is multiplied by the parents' mean confidence.

Music DSG is validated on two sets: MusicEval (129 prompts, 413 generated clips, expert alignment ratings), and an internal set of 200 prompts × 3 TTM models, 600 clips, with pairwise preferences from 128 general listeners (about 5 votes per pair) and 13 professional musicians (400 pairs, about 3 votes per pair). Generation is evaluated on Song Describer's instrumental subset (586 clips, 4 generations per prompt) and the internal genre9k (18 genres × 500 clips).

Metric results: the confidence version of Music DSG reaches Spearman correlations with human preference of 0.421, 0.414 and 0.248 on internal general listeners, internal professionals and MusicEval; the binary version 0.351, 0.334 and 0.193; AnyAudio-Judge 0.414, 0.385 and 0.225; CLAP 0.165, 0.211 and 0.235, with CLaMP 3 and MuQ-MuLan lower still.

Generation results (Song Describer, Infer with Qwen3.5-35B): Base scores 0.320/0.689/7.63/3.95 on CLAP/DSG/FAD/MuQ. FG reaches 0.344/0.738/6.68/3.99, an average relative gain of +7.0%; FT has the best alignment (0.351/0.756) but FAD 7.12; PG has the lowest FAD (6.59). Without completion (Null) gains are only +2.0% to +3.1%, and MuQ falls to 3.71–3.82, below Base.

Comparing Infer models (FG): Qwen3.5-112B and GPT-5.6 Sol both reach +7.5%, Qwen3.5-35B +7.0%, Claude Opus 4.8 +6.8%, Qwen3.5-397B +6.6%, and the two multimodal models +6.4% and +5.8%. Random drops CLAP to 0.298 and DSG to 0.657, an average of −0.2%.

限制Limitations

對照實驗少了最關鍵的一組。四個 SCAP 版本都從 Base 再微調 15 萬步，但沒有「自由文字條件同樣再訓練 15 萬步」的版本，所以結構化描述的提升有多少其實來自額外的訓練量，無從分離。另外 GSCAP 來自曲目 metadata，而 Base 的自由描述從哪裡來、含多少資訊，論文沒有說明；論文宣稱「差異主要可歸因於文字條件」，但訓練量與描述的資訊來源其實同時改變了。

Null 的結果其實透露了提升來自哪裡。只做 Transcribe、不補全時，Song Describer 上只有 +2.0% 到 +3.1%，MuQ 甚至低於 Base；大部分提升要靠 LLM 把空欄位補滿。這表示系統的進步有相當部分來自「LLM 擴寫提示」，而公平的比較應該也讓 Base 吃 LLM 擴寫過的自由提示，就像 DALL·E 3 的提示擴寫那樣，這組也沒做。

評估有循環之虞。Music DSG 的調性與 BPM 題用專門偵測器打分，而 SCAP 正好有明確的調性與 BPM 欄位，TSCAP 的訓練目標又部分來自 MIR 自動推論的標籤，模型等於被訓練去命中評估器量的東西。Qwen 家族同時擔任 TSCAP 標註者、改寫器、題目拆解者與問答者。

Music DSG 的優勢有一半很薄。對 AnyAudio-Judge 的領先只有 0.007、0.029 與 0.023，沒有信賴區間或顯著性檢定；在 MusicEval 上對 CLAP 也只領先 0.013（0.248 對 0.235）。「比 CLAP 更貼近人類」這個結論只在內部資料上明確成立，而且絕對相關仍然中等。

最後，生成結果沒有任何人類聽測，只靠自動指標；Infer 模型之間 5.8% 到 7.5% 的差距是單次結果、沒有變異數。內部音樂庫、genre9k 與 Base 模型都沒有公開，外部無法重現主要表格。

The controlled comparison is missing its most important arm. All four SCAP variants are fine-tuned from Base for another 150k steps, but there is no variant that keeps free-form conditioning for the same 150k steps, so how much of the gain comes from extra training cannot be separated out. Also, GSCAPs come from track metadata, while the paper never says where Base's free-form captions came from or how much information they carry; it claims the differences are "primarily" attributable to the text conditioning, yet training compute and the caption information source both change.

The Null results show where the gain comes from. With Transcribe only and no completion, Song Describer gains are just +2.0% to +3.1%, and MuQ is even below Base; most of the gain depends on the LLM filling the empty fields. So a good part of the improvement comes from LLM prompt expansion, and a fair comparison would also feed Base LLM-expanded free-form prompts, as DALL·E 3's prompt upsampling does. That arm is missing too.

The evaluation risks circularity. Music DSG scores key and BPM questions with dedicated detectors, SCAPs carry explicit key and BPM fields, and TSCAP training targets partly come from MIR-inferred labels, so the model is effectively trained to hit what the evaluator measures. The Qwen family serves at once as TSCAP labeller, rewriter, question decomposer and answerer.

Half of Music DSG's advantage is thin. Its lead over AnyAudio-Judge is only 0.007, 0.029 and 0.023, with no confidence intervals or significance tests; on MusicEval its lead over CLAP is just 0.013 (0.248 vs 0.235). The conclusion that it tracks humans better than CLAP holds clearly only on the internal data, and the absolute correlations remain moderate.

Finally, generation is judged without any human listening test, by automatic metrics alone; the 5.8% to 7.5% spread between Infer models is a single run with no variance. The internal corpus, genre9k and the Base model are not released, so the main tables cannot be reproduced externally.

討論Discussion

撇開缺少的對照組，幾個方向性結果仍有參考價值。全欄位穩定優於部分欄位：在 Song Describer 上 FT 對 PT 是 +6.8% 對 +5.5%、FG 對 PG 是 +7.0% 對 +6.2%。逐段與全曲之間則出現取捨：TSCAP 的對齊最好（FT 的 DSG 0.756），GSCAP 的 FAD 較好（PG 6.59、FG 6.68，對 FT／PT 的 7.12）。作者的解釋是改寫器產生的是單份、像全曲的 SCAP，比較接近 GSCAP 的訓練分佈，這是假說而非驗證過的結論。

Random 的結果很有說服力：隨機補滿欄位讓 CLAP 掉到 0.298、低於 Base 的 0.320，表示有用的是補進「音樂上相容」的值，而不只是讓格式變完整。LLM 規模也不是越大越好：112B 優於 397B，開源與閉源模型差距在一個百分點內。對實務者來說，最便宜的收益很可能就是推論端的 Infer 這一步。

Music DSG 真正的賣點是可解讀，能指出哪個屬性沒對上，但論文沒有展示逐題型的分析，例如 BPM 題與樂器題各自的正確率，或哪些屬性最常失敗。補上這張表，比再多一個整體相關係數更能說明它與 CLAP 的差別。

Setting the missing controls aside, several directional results are still useful. Full coverage consistently beats partial: on Song Describer, FT vs PT is +6.8% vs +5.5% and FG vs PG is +7.0% vs +6.2%. Temporal versus global shows a trade-off: TSCAPs give the best alignment (FT's DSG 0.756), while GSCAPs give better FAD (PG 6.59, FG 6.68, vs 7.12 for FT/PT). The authors explain that the rewriter outputs a single, track-like SCAP closer to the GSCAP training distribution; that is a hypothesis, not a verified conclusion.

The Random result is persuasive: filling fields at random drops CLAP to 0.298, below Base's 0.320, showing that what helps is filling in musically compatible values, not merely making the format complete. Bigger LLMs are not always better either: 112B beats 397B, and open and closed models fall within one point of each other. For practitioners, the cheapest gain is probably the Infer step at inference time.

Music DSG's real selling point is interpretability, the ability to say which attribute failed, yet the paper shows no per-question-type analysis, such as accuracy on BPM versus instrument questions or which attributes fail most often. Adding that table would show the difference from CLAP better than another overall correlation coefficient.
