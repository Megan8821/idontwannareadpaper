Harmonic Circuit: Chord Progression Lies in a Sparse Principal Information Flow in Symbolic Music Models

和聲電路：在 ChatMusician 與 MuPT 裡，用 Information Flow Routes＋Auto-Emergence 找出預測 I…V–I 終止主和弦根音的稀疏資訊流子網路（只佔 1.7%／5.3% 的跨 token 邊），切掉後 83.2%／88.3% 原本答對的預測翻盤——但對照組是「同數量的隨機邊」，不是「同權重的邊」

Ruiji Yu, Gus Xia
arXiv:2611.24453 · cs.SD; cs.AI; cs.LG · 投稿submitted 2026-11-18
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：機制可解釋性（mechanistic interpretability）裡的「電路發現」在 NLP 已經有 IOI、Greater-Than 這些經典案例，音樂模型這邊目前多停在探針（probing）與稀疏自編碼器（SAE）。本文把 Information Flow Routes 與 Auto-Emergence 兩個現成工具搬到兩個讀寫 ABC 記譜的符號音樂模型（ChatMusician-7B、MuPT-190M），任務是在 Nottingham 民謠的 963 段 I…V–I 進行裡預測最後主和弦的根音。選它是因為任務設計得乾淨、數字也給得具體：抽出的電路只佔 1.7%（ChatMusician）與 5.3%（MuPT）的跨 token 邊，切掉後原本答對的預測分別有 83.2% 與 88.3% 翻盤。要注意的是，對照組是隨機抽「同數量」的邊，而被選進電路的本來就是貢獻權重最高的邊，所以「比隨機重要」幾乎是建構上就會成立的結論。Why this one: Circuit discovery in mechanistic interpretability has classic NLP cases such as IOI and Greater-Than, while work on music models has mostly stopped at probing and sparse autoencoders (SAEs). This paper ports two existing tools, Information Flow Routes and Auto-Emergence, to two symbolic music models that read and write ABC notation (ChatMusician-7B and MuPT-190M), with the task of predicting the root of the final tonic in 963 I…V–I progressions from the Nottingham folk corpus. I picked it because the task is cleanly designed and the numbers are concrete: the extracted circuit holds only 1.7% (ChatMusician) and 5.3% (MuPT) of cross-token edges, and ablating it flips 83.2% and 88.3% of originally correct predictions. Caveat: the control ablates the same number of random edges, while the circuit is by construction made of the highest-contribution edges, so "more important than random" is close to guaranteed by design.

動機Motivation

符號音樂語言模型已經能續寫出像樣的和聲進行，但沒人說得清楚模型內部是怎麼「知道」該回到主和弦的。作者認為和聲進行是做電路發現的好題目：它有明確的句法結構，類似 NLP 裡的間接受詞辨識（Indirect Object Identification, IOI）——模型要把開頭建立的主音資訊一路帶到結尾，才能在屬和弦之後正確解決。

所以本文的問題很窄：給模型一段以 I 開頭、中間任意和弦、最後是 V 的 ABC 樂譜，停在下一個和弦符號的左引號後面，看模型如何預測那個主和弦根音字母；然後找出負責這個預測的子網路，並用介入實驗確認它是必要的。作者明說這是「第一個在音樂領域做的系統性電路發現研究」，也明說只研究這一個行為。

Symbolic music language models can already continue plausible chord progressions, but nobody can say how the model internally "knows" to return to the tonic. The authors argue that harmonic progression is a good target for circuit discovery: it has an explicit syntactic structure, analogous to Indirect Object Identification (IOI) in NLP, where the model must carry the tonal centre established at the start all the way to the end to resolve correctly after the dominant.

The question is therefore narrow: give the model an ABC score that starts on I, passes through arbitrary chords and ends on V, stop right after the opening quote of the next chord symbol, and see how it predicts the root letter of that tonic chord; then find the sub-network responsible for this prediction and confirm by intervention that it is necessary. The authors explicitly claim this is "the first systematic circuit-discovery study in the music domain", and explicitly say they study only this one behaviour.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

音樂模型的可解釋性先前工作是有的，本文點名了三個，差別在「層次」。 Ma、Li 與 Xia（NeurIPS 2024 Audio Imagination workshop）對音樂 LLM 做探針加介入，問的是「模型有沒有學到符號概念」；Singh、Cherep 與 Maes（ICLR 2026）用 SAE 在大型生成模型裡找可解釋概念並拿來操控；Facchiano 等人（2025）在 MusicGen 上用活化修補（activation patching）做可解釋的操控。這些都停在「某個表徵裡有什麼」，本文要回答的是「資訊從哪些 token、在哪幾層流向預測位置」。

方法上沒有新東西，兩個核心工具都是直接沿用。 Information Flow Routes（Ferrando 與 Voita，EMNLP 2024）把每層每個位置當節點、注意力頭輸出當邊，用「拿掉這條邊後接收端範數下降多少」當權重；Auto-Emergence（Xu 等人，ICML 2026）則從預測位置往回貪婪地挑最強邊、遞迴展開。後者本質上是在加權 DAG 上做貪婪的最大權重回溯，跟 Information Flow Routes 原論文自己用門檻值修剪出子圖、以及 ACDC（Conmy 等人 2023）用自動修剪找電路的思路最接近；差別在它不設門檻，而是給每個 token 一個「進場順序」。驗證則是 Wang 等人（ICLR 2023）在 IOI 研究裡用的注意力邊消融。

Interpretability work on music models does exist; the paper names three, and the difference is the level of explanation. Ma, Li and Xia (NeurIPS 2024 Audio Imagination workshop) applied probing plus intervention to a music LLM to ask whether it learns symbolic concepts; Singh, Cherep and Maes (ICLR 2026) used SAEs to find and steer interpretable concepts in large generative models; Facchiano et al. (2025) used activation patching for interpretable steering of MusicGen. All of these stay at "what is in a representation"; this paper asks "from which tokens, at which layers, does information flow to the prediction position".

Methodologically nothing is new; both core tools are reused as-is. Information Flow Routes (Ferrando and Voita, EMNLP 2024) treats every position at every layer as a node and attention-head outputs as edges, weighted by how much the receiver's norm drops when the edge is removed; Auto-Emergence (Xu et al., ICML 2026) greedily picks the strongest edge backwards from the prediction position and expands recursively. The latter is essentially greedy maximum-weight back-tracing on a weighted DAG, closest to Information Flow Routes' own threshold pruning and to ACDC (Conmy et al. 2023)'s automated pruning; the difference is that it uses no threshold and instead gives each token an "emergence order". Validation uses the attention-edge ablation from Wang et al.'s IOI study (ICLR 2023).

方法Method

資料與任務。 從 Nottingham ABC 民謠庫 1,034 個曲塊中，每個調性段落取最後一個相鄰的 V→I 根音對，往回找到最近的 I 和弦，剩下 963 筆（882 大調、81 小調）。輸入截斷在終止主和弦根音所在的 token 之前。另外從巴赫聖詠旋律—和聲語料（BCMH）用同樣規則做出 98 筆 SATB 四聲部樣本（55 大調、43 小調），只拿 MuPT 測，因為它的預訓練明確包含複音音樂。

建圖與抽電路。 對每筆樣本，Information Flow Routes 產生每層的下三角貢獻矩陣；Auto-Emergence 以最後位置所有層的殘差表示為起點，把進入它們的跨 token 邊放進候選池，每次選最強的一條加入電路，並把該邊來源 token 在更低層的入邊再加入候選池，直到所有 token 都進場。所以每筆樣本有自己的電路，不存在一個跨樣本共享的固定電路。

Data and task. From the 1,034 tune blocks of the Nottingham ABC folk corpus, the last adjacent V→I root pair in each tonal segment is taken and traced back to the nearest preceding I chord, leaving 963 samples (882 major, 81 minor). Input is truncated before the token containing the root of the terminal tonic. The same rule yields 98 four-part SATB samples (55 major, 43 minor) from the Bach Chorales Melody–Harmony corpus (BCMH), tested only on MuPT because its pretraining explicitly includes polyphonic music.

Graph and circuit extraction. For each sample, Information Flow Routes produces a lower-triangular contribution matrix per layer; Auto-Emergence starts from the residual states of the last position at all layers, puts their incoming cross-token edges into a pool, repeatedly adds the strongest edge to the circuit and then adds the lower-layer incoming edges of that edge's source token to the pool, until every token has entered. Each sample therefore has its own circuit; there is no single fixed circuit shared across samples.

限制Limitations

對照組太弱，是最大的問題。 電路由貢獻權重最高的邊組成，而隨機對照抽的是同數量、但平均權重低得多的邊。附錄 E.2 自己就寫了：電路只佔 1.7%／5.3% 的邊，卻握有約 25%／30% 的貢獻權重。在這種條件下「切電路比切隨機邊傷得重」幾乎是必然的；比較有說服力的對照應該是權重相當的非電路邊，或拿 Information Flow Routes 的簡單門檻子圖來比。介入方式是把注意力機率設成零且不重新正規化，也會讓殘差流偏離分布，這點文中沒有討論。

任務可能可以靠複製解掉。 I…V–I 的終止主和弦根音，就是這段開頭的那個 I 和弦根音，多數時候也就是 K: 標頭裡的調名字母。模型可能只是在做「找到開頭和弦、複製字母」的歸納頭（induction head）式行為，而不是用到 V→I 解決的和聲知識。論文沒有報告標頭 token 是否進入電路，也沒有拿「第一個和弦換掉」之類的反事實去區分這兩種解釋。

主要結果只畫在圖上。 消融前後的 top-1 準確率、目標 rank、KL 散度只呈現在圖 4 的長條圖裡，正文沒有給數值，讀者只能從附錄表 1 的翻盤率與淨 margin 下降間接判斷；random control 是否真的「幾乎不變」也沒有數字。作者自陳的限制有兩項：每筆樣本各有電路、找不到共享的注意力頭清單；任務只有一個西洋調性行為。BCMH 只有 98 筆，規模偏小。

The control is too weak, and this is the biggest issue. The circuit consists of the highest-contribution edges, while the random control draws the same number of edges with much lower average weight. Appendix E.2 says so itself: the circuit holds only 1.7%/5.3% of edges yet about 25%/30% of contribution weight. Under these conditions "ablating the circuit hurts more than ablating random edges" is nearly inevitable; a more convincing control would be weight-matched non-circuit edges, or a comparison with Information Flow Routes' simple threshold subgraph. The intervention also sets attention probabilities to zero without renormalizing, which pushes the residual stream off-distribution; the paper does not discuss this.

The task may be solvable by copying. The terminal tonic root in I…V–I is the root of the opening I chord, and usually also the key letter in the K: header. The model may simply be doing induction-head-style "find the opening chord, copy its letter" rather than using harmonic knowledge of V→I resolution. The paper does not report whether header tokens enter the circuit, nor use counterfactuals such as swapping the first chord to separate the two explanations.

The main results are only in a figure. Top-1 accuracy, target rank and KL divergence before and after ablation appear only as bars in Figure 4, with no values in the text; readers must infer from the flip rates and net margin drops in Appendix Table 1, and there is no number showing the random control is "almost unchanged". The authors list two limitations: each sample has its own circuit, with no shared list of attention heads; and the task is a single Western tonal behaviour. BCMH has only 98 samples.

討論Discussion

有數字支撐的部分。 附錄表 1 的「淨 margin 下降」（切電路造成的根音字母 logit 領先幅度下降，減去切五組同數量隨機邊的平均下降）：ChatMusician 為 2.52（95% CI 2.42–2.62），樣本一致率 95.6%，翻盤率 83.2%；MuPT 為 1.07（0.98–1.17），一致率 75.4%，翻盤率 88.3%。小調子集效果更強（ChatMusician 3.49、MuPT 1.97），但只有 81 筆。MuPT 一致率只有四分之三，代表約四分之一樣本的電路並不比隨機邊重要，這比正文「幾乎每個樣本」的語氣要弱。

最有意思的是邊的分類。 在同樣的邊數預算下（平均每類約 47.6／42.5 條），「同一和弦符號內部」的邊淨 margin 下降最大：ChatMusician 1.36、MuPT 0.44，而它們只佔電路的 10.7%／4.5%；「和弦符號之外」的邊佔了 58.0%／67.8%，效果卻是 −0.05 與 0.02。作者據此說模型先在每個和弦符號內組出和弦表徵再拿來預測。這跟上面的複製假說也相容：和弦符號內部的邊本來就是把根音字母和後續字元綁在一起的地方。

BCMH 的低音聲部結果值得記下來。 按來源音符的聲部分組消融，中音聲部（alto）淨下降 0.16，次中音（tenor）0.01（CI 跨零），低音（bass）是 −0.24，也就是切掉比切隨機邊還不傷。作者說這表示模型不像人類分析者那樣倚重低音；但他們也承認輸入裡每個和弦都已用和弦符號寫出根音，四個聲部的音符本來就是冗餘資訊，這個結果比較像是在說「有捷徑可走時模型不看音符」。

整體來說，這是一篇把 NLP 工具鏈完整搬過來、設計乾淨的工作坊論文，價值在於示範與基線，而不是發現了什麼新的音樂機制。下一步最值得做的是用拿掉和弦符號、只留音符的輸入重做，並加上權重相當的對照組。

What the numbers support. Appendix Table 1 reports the "net margin drop" (the drop in the root letter's logit lead caused by ablating the circuit, minus the mean drop from five same-size random edge sets): ChatMusician 2.52 (95% CI 2.42–2.62), consistency rate 95.6%, flip rate 83.2%; MuPT 1.07 (0.98–1.17), consistency 75.4%, flip rate 88.3%. The minor-key subset shows stronger effects (ChatMusician 3.49, MuPT 1.97) but has only 81 samples. MuPT's consistency of only three quarters means that for about a quarter of samples the circuit is no more important than random edges, which is weaker than the main text's "almost every sample" tone.

The edge-category analysis is the most interesting part. At an equal edge budget (about 47.6/42.5 edges per category on average), edges within one chord symbol give the largest net margin drop: 1.36 for ChatMusician and 0.44 for MuPT, while making up only 10.7%/4.5% of the circuit; edges outside chord symbols make up 58.0%/67.8% yet score −0.05 and 0.02. The authors conclude that the models first assemble a chord representation inside each chord symbol and then use it. This is also compatible with the copying hypothesis above: within-symbol edges are exactly where the root letter is bound to the following characters.

The BCMH bass result is worth noting. Ablating edges grouped by the voice of their source note gives alto a net drop of 0.16, tenor 0.01 (CI spans zero), and bass −0.24, i.e. less damaging than random edges. The authors read this as the model not relying on the bass as a human analyst would; but they also concede that every chord's root is already spelled out in a chord symbol, so the four voices' notes are redundant, and the result reads more like "the model ignores the notes when a shortcut exists".

Overall, this is a cleanly designed workshop paper that ports the NLP toolchain wholesale; its value is as a demonstration and baseline rather than as a discovery of a new musical mechanism. The most useful next step would be to redo it with chord symbols removed and only notes kept, plus a weight-matched control.
