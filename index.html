Variable-Rate Harmonic-Percussive Time-Scale Modification with Real-Time Playback in Python

可變速率諧波-打擊樂時間伸縮:Python 即時播放實作

Sayema Lubis, Clark Peng, Jared Carreño, TJ Tsai
arXiv:2609.18999 · cs.SD, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-07-20
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：這篇把 MIR 社群最常用的 Driedger & Mueller 諧波-打擊樂分解式時間伸縮演算法,從離線批次處理搬進即時的「可變速率播放」場景(伴奏系統要即時跟著演奏者調整速度),並用預先算好的查找表取代線上的 FFT 與瞬時頻率運算,在 β≤0.25 時主觀聽測勝率落到約 52%(等同雜訊水準,判定為不可分辨),同時把總執行時間砍掉約 45–50%,達到比即時快 150 倍的處理速度。Why this one: This paper moves the MIR community's most widely used harmonic-percussive time-scale modification algorithm (Driedger and Mueller's) from offline batch processing into real-time variable-rate playback for automatic accompaniment, replacing the online FFT and instantaneous-frequency computation with precomputed lookup tables; at beta<=0.25 the approximation reaches a subjective listening-test win rate of about 52% (statistical noise, i.e. perceptually indistinguishable), while cutting total runtime by roughly 45-50% and achieving 150x faster than real time.

動機Motivation

自動伴奏這類應用需要一種和一般時間伸縮(TSM)不同的使用情境:要伸縮的錄音內容早就知道,但實際播放速度卻無法預先決定,而是要跟著現場演奏者即時、連續地變動。現有的開源 TSM 實作幾乎都是為離線批次處理設計——固定伸縮倍率、算完再整段輸出——不適用這種「內容已知、速率未知」的即時場景。作者指出,目前唯二支援即時處理的開源實作是 Soundtouch 與 Rubber Band,兩者都是 C++ 撰寫,一來難以整合進以 Python 為主的研究工具生態,二來所用演算法要嘛不是最先進做法(Soundtouch),要嘛沒有被研究社群充分驗證過(Rubber Band)。

因此這篇論文要做的,其實是把研究社群已經驗證過、廣泛使用的離線演算法搬進即時場景,而不是發明新的訊號處理技巧。作者設定的三個目標很具體:(a) 支援伸縮倍率隨時間動態變化的即時處理,(b) 實作或近似一個公認先進、且被研究社群廣泛採用的離線方法,(c) 用 Python 撰寫,方便融入既有的研究軟體生態。

Automatic musical accompaniment needs a setting different from ordinary time-scale modification (TSM): the recording to be stretched is known in advance, but the actual playback rate cannot be fixed ahead of time -- it must change continuously in response to a live performer. Existing open-source TSM implementations are almost all built for offline batch processing, with a fixed stretch factor computed once and the output written out afterward, which does not fit this "known content, unknown rate" real-time setting. The authors note that only two open-source implementations support real-time use, Soundtouch and Rubber Band, both written in C++, which makes them hard to integrate into a mostly Python-based research ecosystem; their underlying algorithms are also either not state of the art (Soundtouch) or unvetted by the research community (Rubber Band).

So what this paper actually does is bring an offline algorithm that the research community has already validated and widely adopted into a real-time setting, rather than invent a new signal-processing technique. The authors set three concrete goals: (a) real-time processing that supports a TSM factor changing dynamically over time, (b) implementing or approximating an offline method that is state of the art and widely used in the research community, and (c) writing it in Python so it integrates easily into the existing research-software ecosystem.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

時間伸縮的做法大致分四類。時域的疊加平均法(OLA)把窗好的分析幀用不同的合成跳躍長度重新排列,對打擊樂聲音處理得不錯,但沒辦法維持局部週期結構,容易出現相位跳躍雜訊;WSOLA 等變體對此做了改善。頻域的相位聲碼器(phase vocoder)則是對 STFT 係數的相位做修正以避免相位跳躍,但對瞬態(打擊樂)類聲音處理效果較差。神經合成類方法作者也提到,但受限於硬體資源沒有採用。

真正被這篇拿來當基礎的是第三類——訊號分解法,其中最關鍵的先前工作是 Driedger 與 Mueller 提出的方法:把訊號拆成打擊樂與諧波兩個成分,打擊樂用 OLA 處理、諧波用相位聲碼器處理,再把結果混合回去。論文原文直接點名這是「MIR 社群裡最廣泛使用的方法之一」。這篇論文的定位因此不是提出新演算法,而是把 Driedger & Mueller 這套已經驗證過的離線方法,改造成可以即時運作、伸縮倍率能逐幀動態變化的版本——真正新的地方是即時化與後面提到的查找表近似,不是分解式 TSM 本身。既有的離線 Python 生態(TSM Toolbox、libtsm、librosa、PyTSMod、AudioStretchy)也全部只支援離線,這篇填補的正是這個沒人做過的「即時版本」空缺。

TSM methods split into roughly four families. Time-domain overlap-add (OLA) rearranges windowed analysis frames with a different synthesis hop size; it handles percussive sound well but cannot preserve local periodic structure, producing phase-jump artifacts, which variants like WSOLA try to fix. The frequency-domain phase vocoder instead corrects the phase of STFT coefficients to avoid phase jumps, but is weaker on transient, percussive sound. Neural synthesis methods are mentioned too but ruled out here for hardware reasons.

The real foundation for this paper is the third family, decomposition-based TSM, and specifically Driedger and Mueller's method: split the signal into percussive and harmonic components, process the percussive part with OLA and the harmonic part with a phase vocoder, then remix them. The paper itself calls this "one of the most widely used methods in the MIR community." This paper's real position, then, is not a new algorithm but a real-time, per-frame-dynamic adaptation of that already-validated offline method -- real-time-ization plus the lookup-table approximation described below is the actual novelty, not the decomposition-based TSM itself. Existing offline Python tooling (TSM Toolbox, libtsm, librosa, PyTSMod, AudioStretchy) is also offline-only, which is exactly the real-time gap this paper fills.

方法Method

整個系統(HPS-TSM-Realtime)分三步:先離線用中值濾波把輸入訊號拆成諧波成分與打擊樂成分;接著諧波部分用相位聲碼器(PV)處理、打擊樂部分用疊加平均法(OLA)處理;最後把兩者輸出混合送到喇叭。跟先前工作最大的不同在於,這裡的伸縮倍率 α_t 可以逐幀動態改變——因為諧波與打擊樂成分是對完整錄音提前算好的,所以「分解」本身是離線前處理,論文所講的「即時」指的是後面的合成與播放階段。

即時相位聲碼器分五步:先算目前幀與前一幀之間的分析跳躍長度 H_a=H_s/α_t(H_s 是固定的合成跳躍長度,設為 N/4,N=2048、取樣率 22050Hz);對目前幀做漢寧窗與 STFT;用瞬時頻率估計修正相位,做法是把每個頻率倉的名目頻率加上一個根據觀測相位差算出的修正項,再逐步累積成修正後的相位;用修正相位重建合成幀,並除以漢寧窗平方和做正規化;最後把合成幀疊加進輸出緩衝區。

打擊樂端則用疊加平均法(OLA)處理:視窗長度固定為 L=256,合成跳躍長度固定為 H̃_s=128,正好是 PV 合成跳躍長度的四分之一,因此每算一個 PV 幀,對應要算四個 OLA 幀,分析跳躍長度同樣隨 α_t 動態調整。輸出緩衝區大小等於 PV 視窗大小(2048):每一輪先把 PV 與 OLA 的合成輸出疊加進緩衝區,再把緩衝區最前面 512 個樣本(已經不會再被更動的部分)送出去播放,其餘樣本往前搬移、後面補零,準備下一輪。

作者分析執行時間後發現,相位聲碼器就佔掉整個系統執行時間的 93%,因此把加速重點放在 PV 上,提出 HPS-TSM-Approx:離線階段先用固定的分析跳躍長度 H_{a,pre} 對諧波訊號算好整段的 STFT 幅度與瞬時頻率,存成查找表;線上階段不再即時算 FFT 與瞬時頻率,而是用最近鄰或下界的幀索引直接查表取值,再照常修改相位並反轉回時域。這個做法唯一的可調參數就是 H_{a,pre}——數值越小,近似越接近原始的即時 PV(H_{a,pre}=1 時完全精確,但需要的記憶體與預先運算量高到不切實際),數值越大則越省資源但音質可能變差。論文用 H_{a,pre}∈{2048,1024,512,256,128} 建了五種近似版本,分別標記成 β=1.0、0.5、0.25、0.125、0.0625。

The whole system (HPS-TSM-Realtime) has three steps: median filtering offline splits the input into a harmonic component and a percussive component; the harmonic part is processed with a phase vocoder (PV) and the percussive part with overlap-add (OLA); the two outputs are then mixed and sent to the speaker. The key difference from prior work is that the stretch factor alpha_t can change dynamically every frame -- because the harmonic and percussive components are precomputed for the whole recording ahead of time, the decomposition itself is offline preprocessing, and "real-time" in this paper refers only to the later synthesis-and-playback stage.

The real-time phase vocoder has five steps: compute the analysis hop size between the current and previous frame as H_a = H_s / alpha_t (H_s is a fixed synthesis hop size set to N/4, with N=2048 and a 22050Hz sample rate); apply a Hann window and STFT to the current frame; correct the phase using an instantaneous-frequency estimate, adding to each bin's nominal frequency a correction term computed from the observed phase difference and accumulating it into a corrected phase; reconstruct the synthesis frame from the corrected phase, normalized by the sum of squared Hann windows; and overlap-add the synthesis frame into the output buffer.

The percussive part is processed with overlap-add: a fixed window length L=256 and a fixed synthesis hop size of 128, exactly one quarter of the PV synthesis hop, so four OLA frames are computed for every one PV frame, with the analysis hop likewise scaled dynamically by alpha_t. The output buffer is the same size as the PV window (2048): each round adds the PV and OLA synthesis outputs into the buffer, then flushes the first 512 samples (the portion that will no longer change) to the speaker, shifting the rest forward and padding the end with zeros for the next round.

Profiling showed the phase vocoder alone accounts for 93% of the system's total runtime, so the authors focus their speedup there with HPS-TSM-Approx: offline, they compute the full STFT magnitude and instantaneous frequency of the harmonic signal at a fixed analysis hop H_a,pre and store them as lookup tables; online, instead of computing an FFT and instantaneous frequency in real time, they look up the nearest (or lower-bound) frame index directly, then modify the phase and invert as usual. The only tunable knob is H_a,pre -- smaller values approximate the exact real-time PV more closely (H_a,pre=1 is exact but needs impractical memory and precomputation), larger values save resources at a perceptual-quality cost. The paper builds five approximations with H_a,pre in {2048,1024,512,256,128}, labeled beta=1.0, 0.5, 0.25, 0.125, 0.0625.

限制Limitations

作者自己點出幾個限制:執行時間變短不等於延遲變短——串流系統的延遲是由區塊大小與演算法前瞻量決定,只要系統跑得比即時快,縮短執行時間只是增加「比即時快幾倍」的餘裕,不會讓延遲跟著變短。其次,所有效能數字都是在 22050Hz 單聲道訊號上量測,立體聲或更高取樣率的情況會更慢,而且這些量測是在記憶體存取不成問題的前提下做的,量測範圍有限。主觀聽測顯示,近似程度太粗(β=1.0)時音質明顯變差(勝率只有 11.8%),換言之查找表近似並非在所有設定下都「免費」,需要挑對 β 值才能兼顧效能與音質。

The authors flag several limits themselves. Faster runtime does not mean lower latency: in a streaming system, latency is set by block size and algorithmic look-ahead, so as long as the system already runs faster than real time, cutting runtime only buys more real-time-factor headroom rather than shortening latency. Second, all performance numbers are measured on 22050Hz mono audio; stereo or higher sample rates would be slower, and the profiling assumes memory access is not a bottleneck, so its scope is limited. The listening tests also show that a too-coarse approximation (beta=1.0) clearly hurts quality (an 11.8% win rate), meaning the lookup-table approximation is not "free" under every setting -- picking the right beta matters for balancing speed against quality.

討論Discussion

主觀聽測(24 位受試者、1114 次成對評比)顯示:β=1.0 時勝率只有 11.8%(p=0.000,顯著變差,音質明顯不如原始版本);β=0.5 勝率回升到 45.1%(p=0.057,在 0.05 顯著水準下不算顯著差異);β=0.25 以下勝率落在 50.9%–52.6% 之間,作者認為已經到達「統計雜訊」的範圍,也就是聽不出差異。執行時間分析顯示,相位聲碼器占掉基準系統 93% 的執行時間,而查找表近似能把 PV 的執行時間砍半,總執行時間因此減少 45–50%,在一般消費級硬體上可以做到比即時快 150 倍。

代價也很具體:β 越小,近似越準確,但預先運算與記憶體需求會等比例上升——從 β=1.0 的每秒 10.8 次 FFT、0.168MB 記憶體,一路長到 β=0.0625 的每秒 172 次 FFT、2.69MB 記憶體,但線上執行時間在各個 β 值之間幾乎沒有差別(約 3.97–4.03 毫秒/秒音訊)。這代表「要多好的音質」與「願意花多少記憶體做預先運算」才是真正的取捨,而不是執行時間本身;作者給的實務建議是,硬體撐得住原始版本就直接用,撐不住的話用 β=0.5 或 β=0.25,音質要求高就選 β=0.25。

The listening tests (24 participants, 1114 pairwise ratings) show a clear pattern: at beta=1.0 the win rate is only 11.8% (p=0.000, a significant quality drop from the original); at beta=0.5 it recovers to 45.1% (p=0.057, not significant at the 0.05 level); at beta<=0.25 the win rate sits between 50.9% and 52.6%, which the authors read as having reached statistical noise -- i.e., perceptually indistinguishable. The runtime analysis shows the phase vocoder alone eats 93% of the baseline system's runtime, and the lookup-table approximation cuts the PV's runtime roughly in half, for a 45-50% reduction in total runtime, reaching about 150x faster than real time on consumer-grade hardware.

The cost is just as concrete: a smaller beta gives a more accurate approximation but scales up precomputation and memory proportionally -- from 10.8 FFTs/sec and 0.168MB at beta=1.0 up to 172 FFTs/sec and 2.69MB at beta=0.0625 -- while online runtime barely changes across beta values (about 3.97-4.03 ms per second of audio). So the real trade-off is between desired audio quality and how much memory you're willing to spend on precomputation, not runtime itself; the authors' practical recommendation is to use the exact baseline if the hardware can sustain it, fall back to beta=0.5 or beta=0.25 if it cannot, and pick beta=0.25 specifically when audio quality matters most.

TTM-Bench: A Framework for Text-to-Music System Performance Benchmarking

TTM-Bench:文字轉音樂系統效能評測框架

Giorgia Adorni, Michela Papandrea, Battista Rimoldi, Tiziano Leidi
arXiv:2609.18585 · cs.SD, cs.LG, cs.MM, eess.AS · 投稿submitted 2026-09-16
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：TTM-Bench 用 100 首商業歌曲、13 個文字轉音樂系統(9 個開源、4 個商用)建立跨系統評測協定,發現商用系統在音樂內容對齊分數(MCAS)上全面領先開源系統,但排名前三的商用系統——Lyria 3(0.634)、Stable Audio 2.5(0.634)、Stable Audio 3(0.631)——用 bootstrap 重新取樣後彼此差距其實統計上不顯著;效率面更看得出「品質好不代表快或便宜」:YuE 生成一次要 439.5 秒、耗能 40.9 Wh,MCAS 卻只有 0.378,墊底。Why this one: TTM-Bench builds a cross-system evaluation protocol from 100 commercial tracks and 13 text-to-music systems (9 open-source, 4 commercial), finding commercial systems lead open ones across the board on musical-content alignment (MCAS), yet the top three commercial systems -- Lyria 3 (0.634), Stable Audio 2.5 (0.634), and Stable Audio 3 (0.631) -- are not statistically distinguishable after bootstrap resampling; on efficiency, quality and speed/cost clearly diverge -- YuE takes 439.5 seconds and 40.9 Wh per generation yet scores lowest on MCAS at 0.378.

動機Motivation

文字轉音樂(TTM)系統之間的差異很大——條件輸入格式不同、架構不同、取用方式也不同(有些開源可本地跑,有些只透過商用 API 存取)。同一句文字提示,在不同系統上不一定能表達出相同的音樂內容,但硬是把提示改寫成某個系統偏好的格式,又可能悄悄改變了原本想表達的音樂規格本身。現有的自動指標——CLAP 相似度、Fréchet Audio Distance(FAD)——各自量測不同面向,選哪個指標取決於評測目的,沒有一套協定可以在「輸入格式、架構、取用方式都不一樣」的前提下,對現有系統做系統性比較。

換句話說,問題不是「指標不夠好」,而是沒有一層轉譯機制能把同一份音樂規格,公平地轉成每個系統各自需要的輸入格式,再回頭比較輸出;而且現有評測大多只看生成內容的品質,很少同時把「用哪種方式取用系統」(本地跑模型 vs 打 API)所帶來的效率與成本差異也放進同一張比較表裡。

Text-to-music (TTM) systems differ widely -- in conditioning-input format, architecture, and access mode (some open-source ones run locally, others are only reachable through a commercial API). The same text prompt may not express the same musical content across systems, yet forcibly rewriting the prompt into whatever format a given system prefers risks quietly changing the underlying musical specification itself. Existing automatic metrics -- CLAP-based similarity, Fréchet Audio Distance (FAD) -- each measure a different dimension, and which one to use depends on the evaluation goal; there is no protocol for systematically comparing existing systems when their input formats, architectures, and access modes all differ.

In other words, the gap is not "the metrics aren't good enough" but the lack of a translation layer that turns one canonical musical specification fairly into whatever input format each system needs, so outputs can then be compared on equal footing; existing evaluations also mostly look only at generated-content quality, rarely putting the efficiency and cost differences that come from access mode (running weights locally vs. calling an API) on the same comparison table.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

自動指標這條線上,CLAP 對比相似度量的是文字與音訊的語意對應程度,FAD 則是比較生成音訊與參考音訊的分布距離,兩者的數值都會因為所用的嵌入模型、參考資料、前處理方式、樣本數與聚合方法不同而改變,本身就難以跨論文比較。學習型評分器如 MusicEval、MuQ-Eval、SongBench、AudioEval、TuneJury,則是用人工標註資料去估計文字與音訊的對齊程度,但可靠度取決於人工標註本身的品質、涵蓋範圍與潛在偏誤。

跨系統比較這條線,作者點名 Grötschla 等人的工作、Music Arena、ATTM Grand Challenge——這些工作都在處理「評測結果因人而異、因設定而異」的問題,但用的是偏好式或受控條件下的比較,沒有處理「系統本來就用不同輸入格式、不同取用方式」這件事。TTM-Bench 跟這些工作最大的不同在於:它不是提出新的偏好評分機制,而是先做一層規格轉譯——把同一份音樂規格轉成每個系統各自需要的輸入格式——再用同一套內容對齊與效率量表比較 13 個「現成、已經公開或商用」的系統,把取用方式(本地跑 vs 打 API)本身也當成要量測的變數之一。

On automatic metrics, CLAP-based contrastive similarity measures text-audio semantic correspondence, and FAD compares the distributions of generated and reference audio; both numbers shift with the embedding model, reference data, preprocessing, sample size, and aggregation used, making them hard to compare across papers on their own. Learned evaluators such as MusicEval, MuQ-Eval, SongBench, AudioEval, and TuneJury instead use human-annotated data to estimate text-audio alignment, but their reliability depends on the quality, coverage, and potential bias of that human annotation.

On cross-system comparison, the paper names Grötschla et al., Music Arena, and the ATTM Grand Challenge -- all address result variability across raters and settings, but through preference-based or controlled-condition comparisons, without handling the fact that systems already differ in input format and access mode. TTM-Bench's real difference from this work is that it doesn't propose a new preference-scoring scheme; instead it builds a specification-translation layer first -- turning one musical specification into whatever input format each system needs -- then compares 13 already-available open and commercial systems on the same content-alignment and efficiency scales, treating access mode itself (running locally vs. calling an API) as a variable to measure.

方法Method

音樂內容對齊分數(MCAS)由三個子分數加權組成:MCAS = 0.40×語意對齊(SA)+ 0.35×曲風對齊(GA)+ 0.25×描述詞對齊(DA)。SA 是用 LAION-CLAP 算文字提示與生成音訊的餘弦相似度;GA 是曲風集合的 Jaccard 重疊度(占 20%)加上分類器分數相似度(占 80%,再由 Discogs519、Discogs400、MTG-Jamendo 三個分類器以 50%、25%、25% 加權合成);DA 則是音色類別相似度(50%)、BPM 相對誤差(40%)、速度標籤的序數距離(10%)加權而成。

權重本身是憑經驗設定的:SA 權重最高,因為它反映整體條件對應程度,GA、DA 量的是更細的音樂屬性。為了檢查這組權重是否穩健,作者另外測了等權重方案、對權重做局部擾動、以及逐一拿掉某個子分數,並用 Spearman ρ 與 Kendall τ 檢驗排名是否一致。

效率面分兩種取用方式分別量測。本地執行的系統量「延遲」(從模型端條件輸入到產出可用波形為止,用 CUDA 同步計時)、「即時因子」(RTF,量到的延遲除以輸出音訊長度)、峰值記憶體、峰值顯存、GPU 使用率、GPU 耗能;透過 API 取用的商用系統則只量客戶端能看到的延遲、RTF 與供應商公告的每次呼叫費用,不去猜測看不到的伺服端資源使用。當系統原生不支援輸出目標長度(30 秒)時,效率量的是產出這個標準化 30 秒輸出所需要的運算量。

實驗設定包含 100 首合法取得的商業發行歌曲,涵蓋多個年代、曲風與速度範圍;評測系統共 13 個,9 個開源(ACE-Step v1 3.5B、AudioLDM2 Music、DiffRhythm、HeartMuLa OSS 3B、InspireMusic 1.5B Long、MusicGen Large、MusicLDM、Stable Audio Open 1.0、YuE)加 4 個商用 API(Lyria 2、Lyria 3、Stable Audio 2.5、Stable Audio 3)。為了排除人聲/歌詞生成能力的干擾,參考音訊與生成音訊都先用 HTDemucs 抽出純器樂音軌,只比較器樂生成的部分。

每首參考曲會先用既有 MIR 工具與音訊分析抽出 46 個描述屬性,再用 Llama 3.1 8B 依這些屬性生成 10 種提示詞變體(從逗號分隔的屬性清單到自然語言描述都有),並驗證這些變體有沒有保留原本選定的屬性;每首曲子留下與器樂參考音訊 CLAP 相似度最高的 5 種提示詞變體,得到 100×13×5=6,500 組提示詞,每組再各生成 4 首 30 秒曲子,總共 26,000 首生成曲目。

穩健性分析則用配對式的參考層級 bootstrap 重取樣,重複 10,000 次,每次用相同的參考曲索引為所有系統重新取樣 100 首參考曲、重算系統分數與兩兩比較結果;三個子分數之間的關聯性則分別在單次生成層級與系統平均層級,用 Pearson 與 Spearman 相關係數檢查。

The Musical-Content Alignment Score (MCAS) is a weighted sum of three sub-scores: MCAS = 0.40 x Semantic Alignment (SA) + 0.35 x Genre Alignment (GA) + 0.25 x Descriptor Alignment (DA). SA is LAION-CLAP cosine similarity between the text prompt and generated audio; GA combines genre-set Jaccard overlap (20%) with classifier-score similarity (80%, itself a 50/25/25 weighted mix of the Discogs519, Discogs400, and MTG-Jamendo classifiers); DA combines timbre-category similarity (50%), relative BPM error (40%), and ordinal tempo-label distance (10%).

The weights themselves are set empirically: SA gets the highest weight because it reflects overall conditioning correspondence, while GA and DA capture narrower musical properties. To check whether this weighting is robust, the authors also test equal weighting, local perturbations of the weights, and leaving out one sub-score at a time, checking rank agreement with Spearman's rho and Kendall's tau.

Efficiency is measured separately by access mode. For locally run systems, they measure latency (from model-side conditioning to a usable waveform, timed with CUDA synchronization), real-time factor (RTF, latency divided by output duration), peak RAM, peak VRAM, GPU utilization, and GPU energy; for commercial systems accessed via API, they measure only client-observed latency, RTF, and the provider's listed per-call cost, without guessing at server-side resource use they cannot see. When a system cannot natively produce the target 30-second duration, efficiency is measured for whatever computation is needed to reach that standardized 30-second output.

The setup uses 100 legally obtained, commercially released tracks spanning multiple decades, genres, and tempo ranges, evaluated across 13 systems: 9 open-source (ACE-Step v1 3.5B, AudioLDM2 Music, DiffRhythm, HeartMuLa OSS 3B, InspireMusic 1.5B Long, MusicGen Large, MusicLDM, Stable Audio Open 1.0, YuE) plus 4 commercial APIs (Lyria 2, Lyria 3, Stable Audio 2.5, Stable Audio 3). To remove any influence from vocal or lyric-generation ability, both reference and generated audio are first passed through HTDemucs to extract instrumental stems, so only instrumental generation is compared.

Each reference track first has 46 descriptor attributes extracted using existing MIR tools and audio analysis; Llama 3.1 8B then generates 10 prompt variants from those attributes (ranging from comma-separated attribute lists to natural-language descriptions), validated to preserve the selected attributes. The 5 variants with the highest CLAP similarity to the instrumental reference are kept per track, giving 100x13x5=6,500 prompts, each rendered into 4 30-second tracks, for 26,000 generated tracks total.

Robustness is assessed with paired, reference-level bootstrap resampling over 10,000 iterations, resampling 100 references with the same indices across all systems each time and recomputing system scores and pairwise contrasts; correlation among the three sub-scores is checked separately at the per-generation level and the system-mean level using Pearson and Spearman correlations.

限制Limitations

作者自己承認的限制包括:100 首參考曲雖然橫跨多個年代、曲風與速度,但這是「個案研究」式的取樣,不是有統計代表性的樣本;MCAS 的加權方式目前是實驗性設定,還需要拿真人判斷去進一步驗證;評測範圍限定在「純器樂、文字條件」生成,不代表帶人聲或歌詞的系統表現也一樣;bootstrap 分析顯示,MCAS 上的小幅差距對參考曲組成的變動並不穩健——例如 Stable Audio 2.5 對 Lyria 3 的 95% 信賴區間是 [-0.012, 0.013],跨過零,代表兩者差距統計上站不住腳;耗能量測也只算 GPU 部分,沒有涵蓋 CPU 或整台機器的耗能,效率圖像並不完整。

The authors' own caveats: the 100 reference tracks span multiple decades, genres, and tempos, but form a case-study sample rather than a statistically representative one; MCAS's weighting is currently an experimentally chosen setting still needing validation against human judgments; the evaluation is restricted to text-conditioned, instrumental-only generation, so it may not generalize to vocal or lyric-inclusive systems; bootstrap analysis shows small MCAS gaps are not robust to changes in the reference set -- e.g. the 95% interval for Stable Audio 2.5 vs. Lyria 3 is [-0.012, 0.013], crossing zero, meaning that gap is not statistically established; and energy measurement covers only the GPU, leaving out CPU and whole-system energy, so the efficiency picture is incomplete.

討論Discussion

MCAS 結果顯示商用系統全面壓過開源系統:開源最高的是 MusicGen Large(0.541)與 Stable Audio Open 1.0(0.539),商用最高的是 Lyria 3(0.634)、Stable Audio 2.5(0.634 打平)、Stable Audio 3(0.631),最低的開源系統 YuE 只有 0.378。但 bootstrap 檢定顯示,商用系統彼此之間的差距其實站不住腳:Stable Audio 2.5 對 Lyria 3 的 95% 信賴區間是 [-0.012, 0.013]、Lyria 3 對 Stable Audio 3 是 [-0.008, 0.014],都跨過零。

三個子分數(SA、GA、DA)在單次生成層級的相關性偏弱(Pearson r=0.217–0.281),代表三者各自量到不完全重疊的資訊;但在系統平均層級相關性明顯變強(r=0.767–0.879),顯示這三個面向雖然生成當下各自獨立,系統整體強弱卻是一致的。

效率面的數字更能說明「好不代表快或便宜」:開源系統裡 ACE-Step v1 3.5B 延遲最短(2.3 秒)、耗能最低(0.2 Wh),但它的 MCAS(0.517)並不是開源裡最高的;反觀 YuE 生成一次要 439.5 秒、RTF 高達 14.7、耗能 40.9 Wh(是 ACE-Step 的 200 倍以上),MCAS 卻是全表最低的 0.378——換句話說,YuE 同時是最慢、最耗能,也是內容對齊最差的系統。商用系統裡,Stable Audio 2.5 延遲最短(5.6 秒)、RTF 最低(0.2),但 Lyria 3 每次呼叫最便宜(0.04 美元),兩者都不是同一套系統同時做到,呼應了論文的結論:生成速度快不代表成本低,內容對齊好也不代表運算需求低。

MCAS results show commercial systems beating open ones across the board: the best open systems are MusicGen Large (0.541) and Stable Audio Open 1.0 (0.539), the best commercial ones are Lyria 3 (0.634), Stable Audio 2.5 (0.634, tied), and Stable Audio 3 (0.631), while the weakest open system, YuE, scores only 0.378. But bootstrap testing shows the gaps among commercial systems don't actually hold up: the 95% interval for Stable Audio 2.5 vs. Lyria 3 is [-0.012, 0.013], and for Lyria 3 vs. Stable Audio 3 it's [-0.008, 0.014] -- both crossing zero. The three sub-scores (SA, GA, DA) correlate weakly at the per-generation level (Pearson r=0.217-0.281), meaning each captures partly non-overlapping information, but correlate much more strongly at the system-mean level (r=0.767-0.879) -- so while the three axes are fairly independent generation by generation, systems that are strong on one tend to be strong overall.

Efficiency numbers make "better doesn't mean faster or cheaper" concrete: among open systems, ACE-Step v1 3.5B has the shortest latency (2.3s) and lowest energy (0.2 Wh), yet its MCAS (0.517) isn't the highest among open systems; YuE, in contrast, takes 439.5 seconds per generation, an RTF of 14.7, and 40.9 Wh of energy (over 200x ACE-Step's), while scoring the table's lowest MCAS at 0.378 -- meaning YuE is simultaneously the slowest, most energy-hungry, and worst-aligned system. Among commercial systems, Stable Audio 2.5 has the shortest latency (5.6s) and lowest RTF (0.2), while Lyria 3 is the cheapest per call ($0.04) -- no single system does both, echoing the paper's conclusion that faster generation doesn't mean lower cost, and better content alignment doesn't mean lower compute demand.

CPR: Combining Global Composing, Local Performing and Full-Sequence Refining in Piano Rendering with Continuous Autoregressive Modelling

CPR:結合全局作曲、局部演奏與全序列精煉的連續自回歸鋼琴演奏渲染

Chong Jing, Junan Zhang, Zhizheng Wu
arXiv:2609.18216 · cs.SD, cs.AI · 投稿submitted 2026-09-16
abspdfhtmlcited by
為什麼挑這篇：CPR 把語音領域 DiTAR 的「全局自回歸+局部生成」架構搬進鋼琴 MIDI 演奏渲染,並把 MiniMax Music 3 裡容易誤差累積的 Local-LLM 換成局部 flow matching;結果只用 4 步函數評估(NFE)就把 FAD_clap 壓到 0.129(P-MUSE 為 0.161,NFE=25)、音符起始 F1 拉到 78.4(P-MUSE 75.7),代價是音色相似度 89.3 略低於 P-MUSE 的 90.6;拿掉論文提出的 MT-RoPE、改用一般 RoPE 是消融實驗裡最傷的一項,F1 直接掉到 64.4。Why this one: CPR ports DiTAR's global-autoregressive-plus-local-generation recipe from speech synthesis into piano MIDI-to-music rendering, replacing MiniMax Music 3's error-prone Local-LLM with local flow matching; with only 4 function evaluations (NFE) it drives FAD_clap down to 0.129 (vs. P-MUSE's 0.161 at NFE=25) and onset F1 up to 78.4 (vs. P-MUSE's 75.7), at the cost of a slightly lower timbre similarity, 89.3 vs. P-MUSE's 90.6; the biggest single ablation hit comes from swapping its proposed MT-RoPE for standard RoPE, which drops onset F1 to 64.4.

動機Motivation

鋼琴 MIDI 轉音樂的渲染任務,要同時做到兩件事:忠實彈出目標音符,又要重現參考錄音的音色。現有做法各自卡在不同瓶頸:離散編碼式自回歸模型(如 MIDI-VALLE)能做因果式的時序建模,方便逐步生成、也方便條件跟隨,但量化過程本身會丟掉聲學細節;flow matching 類方法(如 P-MUSE)不需要量化,能保留較好的聲學結構,但代價是要對整個序列做全域注意力,運算成本高,而且因為不是逐步生成,語意結構(音符跟隨度)相對較弱。

這篇論文的想法是:能不能用連續的自回歸建模,同時拿到兩邊的好處——維持自回歸架構的因果時序與條件跟隨能力,又靠不量化的連續表示避免離散化造成的聲學細節損失?具體做法是把逐 token 的分類頭換成一個以小區塊(patch)為單位的局部 flow matching 生成頭,讓自回歸模型改成在連續的聲學潛在空間裡逐塊生成,而不是逐個離散 token 生成。

Piano MIDI-to-music rendering has to do two things at once: faithfully play the target notes and reproduce the reference recording's timbre. Existing approaches each hit a different bottleneck: discrete-codec autoregressive models (like MIDI-VALLE) give causal, step-by-step temporal modeling that is good for condition-following, but quantization itself discards acoustic detail; flow-matching methods (like P-MUSE) need no quantization and preserve acoustic structure better, but at the cost of full-sequence attention over the whole clip, which is expensive, and because generation isn't step-by-step, semantic structure -- how well it follows the notes -- is comparatively weaker.

This paper's idea is to ask whether continuous autoregressive modeling can get both benefits at once: keep the causal, step-by-step, condition-following structure of an autoregressive model, while using an unquantized continuous representation to avoid the acoustic-detail loss that discretization causes. Concretely, they replace the usual per-token classification head with a local flow-matching generation head operating on patches, so the autoregressive model generates patch by patch in a continuous acoustic latent space instead of token by token in a discrete one.

背景與相關研究Introduction & Prior Work

先前系統裡,MIDI-VALLE 是離散編碼式自回歸模型,論文指出它的問題是訓練時用教師強制(teacher forcing),測試時卻要自己餵自己前一步的輸出,這種訓練/測試不一致會讓誤差一路累積;P-MUSE 是全序列 flow matching transformer,音色相似度表現好,但論文形容它「musicality 上的自然度不足」;MiniMax Music 3 則是串接式系統,先用一個 Hybrid-LLM 加全序列 flow matching,其中負責細節的 Local-LLM 元件本身也是逐 token 自回歸,論文直接點名這個設計「可能讓誤差累積惡化」。

但這篇論文架構上真正最像的先例,其實不是上面這幾個音樂生成系統,而是語音領域的 DiTAR——一個把「全局自回歸」與「局部擴散模型」結合、用聚合過的區塊(patch)token 銜接兩者的連續自回歸 TTS 系統。CPR 可以理解成把 DiTAR 這套「自回歸決定大方向、局部生成模型補聲學細節」的配方搬到鋼琴演奏渲染上,並且直接把 MiniMax Music 3 裡那個容易累積誤差的 Local-LLM,換成 DiTAR 式的局部 flow matching,這是它跟自己所稱「首創」框架之間最值得說清楚的技術系譜。FireRedTTS-3 強調潛在表示的語意結構、點出「還原精細度」與「生成穩定性」之間的取捨,則是 CPR 提出 BREPA 瓶頸設計時參考的另一條思路。

Among prior systems, MIDI-VALLE is a discrete-codec autoregressive model that the paper says suffers from train-test mismatch: trained with teacher forcing but at test time fed its own previous outputs, letting errors accumulate. P-MUSE is a full-sequence flow-matching transformer with strong timbre similarity but, in the paper's words, "lacking in naturalness in musicality." MiniMax Music 3 is a cascaded system pairing a Hybrid-LLM with full-sequence flow matching, where the detail-handling Local-LLM component is itself token-by-token autoregressive -- something the paper explicitly says "can worsen error accumulation."

But the closest architectural precedent for this paper isn't actually any of these music-generation systems -- it's DiTAR from speech synthesis, a continuous autoregressive TTS system that combines a global autoregressive model with a local diffusion model, bridged by aggregated patch tokens. CPR is best read as porting DiTAR's recipe -- autoregression for the big picture, a local generative model for acoustic detail -- into piano performance rendering, and specifically replacing MiniMax Music 3's error-prone Local-LLM with DiTAR-style local flow matching; that is the technical lineage most worth stating plainly against its own claim of a novel framework. FireRedTTS-3, which emphasizes the latent's semantic structure and the trade-off between reconstruction fidelity and generation stability, is the other idea CPR draws on for its BREPA bottleneck design.

方法Method

整個系統分三段:Composer 與 Performer 一起在 24kHz 上聯合訓練,Refiner 則另外訓練負責把 24kHz 升到 48kHz。做法上不是逐 token 回歸,而是把相鄰的 P 個 token 聚合成一個區塊(patch)嵌入,並把原本基於離散 token 的分類頭,換成基於連續潛在向量的局部 flow matching 生成頭;實作上是把每 5 個 20 毫秒的 Mel 幀聚合成一個 100 毫秒的區塊。

Composer 是自回歸 transformer,權重從 Qwen3-0.6B 初始化(約 5 億參數)。MIDI 輸入以 25Hz 光柵化成音符起始與力度兩個通道,分別編碼後用 FiLM(特徵線性調製)融合;區塊嵌入經過一個 transformer 編碼器聚合成 [CLS] token,再與提示音訊、MIDI、區塊嵌入各自的 CLAP 表示串接起來。這裡的關鍵設計是 Modality-Time RoPE(MT-RoPE):把旋轉位置編碼的維度切成兩半,一半編碼「屬於哪個模態」,一半編碼「在時間軸上的位置」;MIDI 以 25Hz、音訊區塊以 10Hz 各自獨立索引,但透過縮放係數(MIDI 用 10/25、音訊用 1)把兩者對齊回同一條實際時間軸上。

Performer 是局部 flow matching 模組,用線性路徑 x_t = t·x_1 + (1−t)·x_0(x_0 為高斯雜訊)生成下一個聲學區塊,條件包括前兩個乾淨的歷史區塊、Composer 的隱狀態,以及代表音色先驗的 CLAP 嵌入。為了做分類器無關引導(CFG),論文設計了一套階層式的條件丟棄規則:丟掉 Composer 隱狀態時一定連 CLAP 也丟掉,否則 CLAP 以 0.2 的機率單獨丟掉——這樣做是為了逼模型多靠「歷史區塊」本身去做音色轉移,而不是只依賴一個全域的 CLAP 先驗。推論時用一階 Euler ODE 求解器,每個區塊只跑 4 步。

Refiner 是仿 Vocos 架構的 24→48kHz 升頻器,約 1400 萬參數,獨立訓練,專門補回因為低取樣率生成而流失的高頻諧波成分。BREPA(瓶頸表示對齊)則只在訓練時作用在一條額外的對齊分支上:先用轉置卷積把 Composer 的隱狀態從 10Hz 升到 25Hz,再用一個 1024→768 的瓶頸做強制壓縮,逼模型把大部分維度拿去表示語意結構、剩下維度留給音色等殘餘聲學細節,並用餘弦相似度損失把這個表示對齊到預訓練的 MuQ 自監督特徵;訓練損失是 flow matching 損失加上 0.5 倍的 BREPA 損失。

Composer-Performer 用 AdamW(β1=0.9、β2=0.999、權重衰減 0.01),8 張 RTX 5090、訓練 200,000 步;從 Qwen3 初始化的自回歸部分學習率設為 2×10⁻⁵,其餘模組用 1×10⁻⁴。Refiner 先預訓練 700,000 步、再微調 90,000 步,損失函數是多解析度 STFT 加上 15 倍的多解析度 Mel 損失,再加對抗損失與特徵匹配損失。訓練資料涵蓋約 5,000 小時,來自 MAESTRO 真實鋼琴演奏、Slakh 裡的鋼琴音軌,以及用 NSynth 算繪出來的 Lakh 單軌 MIDI,共八個 MIDI 鋼琴子類別。

The system has three stages: the Composer and Performer are jointly trained at 24kHz, while the Refiner is trained separately to upsample 24kHz to 48kHz. Rather than regressing token by token, adjacent groups of P tokens are aggregated into one patch embedding, and the usual classification head over discrete tokens is replaced with a local flow-matching generation head over continuous latents; concretely, five 20ms Mel frames are aggregated into one 100ms patch.

The Composer is an autoregressive transformer initialized from Qwen3-0.6B (about 500M parameters). MIDI input is rasterized at 25Hz into onset and velocity channels, encoded separately and fused via FiLM (feature-wise linear modulation); patch embeddings are aggregated into a [CLS] token through a transformer encoder, then concatenated with CLAP embeddings of the prompt audio, the MIDI, and the patch embeddings. The key design here is Modality-Time RoPE (MT-RoPE): rotary position embedding dimensions are split in half, one half encoding which modality a token belongs to and the other its position on the time axis; MIDI (at 25Hz) and audio patches (at 10Hz) are indexed independently but aligned back onto one shared physical time axis via scale factors (10/25 for MIDI, 1 for audio).

The Performer is a local flow-matching module generating the next acoustic patch along a linear path x_t = t*x1 + (1-t)*x0 (x0 Gaussian noise), conditioned on the two previous clean history patches, the Composer's hidden state, and a CLAP embedding as a timbre prior. For classifier-free guidance, the paper uses a hierarchical dropout rule: dropping the Composer's hidden state always drops CLAP too, otherwise CLAP alone is dropped with probability 0.2 -- forcing the model to rely more on the history patches themselves for timbre transfer rather than just a global CLAP prior. Inference uses a first-order Euler ODE solver with only 4 steps per patch.

The Refiner is a Vocos-style 24-to-48kHz upsampler with about 14M parameters, trained separately to restore the high-frequency harmonic content lost to low-sample-rate generation. BREPA (Bottlenecked Representation Alignment) acts only on an extra alignment branch during training: a transposed convolution upsamples the Composer's hidden states from 10Hz to 25Hz, then a 1024-to-768 bottleneck forces irreversible compression, pushing most dimensions to capture semantic structure while leaving the rest for residual acoustic detail like timbre, aligned via a cosine-similarity loss to pretrained MuQ self-supervised features; the training loss is the flow-matching loss plus 0.5 times the BREPA loss.

The Composer-Performer is trained with AdamW (beta1=0.9, beta2=0.999, weight decay 0.01) on 8 RTX 5090 GPUs for 200,000 steps; the Qwen3-initialized autoregressive part uses a learning rate of 2e-5, other modules 1e-4. The Refiner is pretrained for 700,000 steps and fine-tuned for 90,000 more, with a loss combining multi-resolution STFT, 15x multi-resolution Mel loss, an adversarial loss, and a feature-matching loss. Training data totals about 5,000 hours, drawn from real MAESTRO piano performances, piano tracks from Slakh, and single-track Lakh MIDI rendered with NSynth, across eight MIDI piano subcategories.

限制Limitations

作者自己點出一個具體的取捨:加入 Refiner 之後,MIDI 準確度與自然度的主觀評分都提升了,但音色相似度反而下降(主觀音色相似度從 C-P 的 4.214 掉到 CPR 的 4.000),顯示純粹靠映射式的超解析度設計,沒辦法把低解析度生成階段就已經流失的細節找回來,作者也把改良 Refiner 設計留給未來工作。另外,CPR 的音色相似度客觀分數(89.3)也略低於全序列 flow matching 的 P-MUSE(90.6),作者認為這是因為 P-MUSE 用全域雙向注意力,能捕捉到更豐富的音色細節,這是連續自回歸架構本身要付出的代價。

The authors flag one concrete trade-off themselves: adding the Refiner raises subjective MIDI accuracy and naturalness scores but lowers timbre similarity (subjective timbre similarity drops from C-P's 4.214 to CPR's 4.000), showing that a purely mapping-based super-resolution design cannot recover detail already lost at the low-resolution generation stage; improving the Refiner's design is left to future work. CPR's objective timbre-similarity score (89.3) is also slightly below full-sequence flow-matching P-MUSE's 90.6, which the authors attribute to P-MUSE's full bidirectional attention capturing richer timbral detail -- a cost that comes with the continuous autoregressive architecture itself.

討論Discussion

客觀指標上,CPR 在 FAD_clap 拿到全表最佳的 0.129(P-MUSE 0.161、MIDI-VALLE 0.390),音符起始 F1 是 78.4,比 P-MUSE 的 75.7 高 2.7 個百分點(相對提升約 3.6%),而且只需要 4 步函數評估(NFE),比 P-MUSE 的 25 步少了超過六倍;代價是音色相似度 89.3,略低於 P-MUSE 的 90.6。主觀評測(14 個樣本、13 位聽者)也呈現一致的取捨:CPR(48kHz)在 MIDI 準確度(4.714)與自然度(4.071)上都贏過 P-MUSE(4.571、3.857),分別高出 0.143 與 0.214 分,但音色相似度(4.000)比 P-MUSE 的 4.071 略低 0.071 分。

消融實驗給出三個具體數字。拿掉 BREPA:音符起始 F1 從 78.4 掉到 73.2(掉 5.2 個百分點,相對約 6.6%),FAD_clap 從 0.129 惡化到 0.145;只拿掉 BREPA 裡的瓶頸(表示對齊還在但不強制壓縮):F1 只掉到 78.1,幾乎沒差,顯示瓶頸本身帶來的額外約束才是關鍵,不是對齊這件事本身;把 MT-RoPE 換成一般 RoPE 則是影響最大的一項改動:F1 直接崩到 64.4(掉 14 個百分點,相對約 18%),FAD_clap 惡化到 0.251(相對變差近一倍)。作者的結論是,語意層面的表示約束(BREPA)沒辦法取代顯式的跨模態時間對齊(MT-RoPE)——兩者各自處理不同的問題,拿掉任何一個都會讓條件跟隨能力明顯變差。

On objective metrics, CPR posts the table's best FAD_clap at 0.129 (vs. P-MUSE's 0.161 and MIDI-VALLE's 0.390) and an onset F1 of 78.4, 2.7 points above P-MUSE's 75.7 (about 3.6% relative), using only 4 function evaluations (NFE) versus P-MUSE's 25 -- more than 6x fewer -- at the cost of a timbre similarity of 89.3, slightly below P-MUSE's 90.6. Subjective ratings (14 samples, 13 listeners) show the same trade-off: CPR (48kHz) beats P-MUSE on MIDI accuracy (4.714 vs. 4.571, +0.143) and naturalness (4.071 vs. 3.857, +0.214), but trails slightly on timbre similarity (4.000 vs. 4.071, -0.071).

The ablations give three concrete numbers. Removing BREPA drops onset F1 from 78.4 to 73.2 (-5.2 points, about 6.6% relative) and worsens FAD_clap from 0.129 to 0.145; removing just BREPA's bottleneck (keeping the alignment but not the forced compression) only drops F1 to 78.1, barely moving, showing the bottleneck's extra constraint -- not alignment itself -- is what matters. Swapping MT-RoPE for standard RoPE is the single biggest change: F1 collapses to 64.4 (-14 points, about 18% relative) and FAD_clap worsens to 0.251 (nearly double). The authors conclude that semantic representation constraints (BREPA) cannot substitute for explicit cross-modal temporal alignment (MT-RoPE) -- each addresses a different problem, and removing either clearly hurts condition-following.
